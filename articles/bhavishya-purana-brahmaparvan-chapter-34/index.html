Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 34 — Signs of Venom, Vital Points, and Serpent Hours

BP 1.34.1

कश्यप उवाच
अतः परं प्रवक्ष्यामि कालदष्टस्य लक्षणम् ।
शृणु गौतम तत्त्वेन यादृशो भवते नरः ॥

Kaśyapa said. Next I shall state the signs of one bitten by the serpent of Time. Gautama, hear truly what such a person becomes like.

BP 1.34.2

जिह्राभङ्गोऽथ हृच्छूलं चक्षुर्भ्यां च न पश्यति ।
दंशं च दग्धसंकाशं पक्वजम्बूफलोपमम् ॥

The tongue is impaired, there is pain in the heart, and he does not see with his eyes. The bite looks burned and resembles a ripe Java plum.

BP 1.34.3

वैवर्ण्यं चैव दन्तानां श्यावो भवति वर्णतः ।
सर्वेष्वङ्गेषु शैथिल्यं पुरीषस्य च भेदनम् ॥

The teeth become discolored and the complexion becomes dark. All the limbs grow slack, and the bowels are loosened.

BP 1.34.4

भग्नस्कन्धकटिग्रीव ऊर्ध्वदृष्टिरधोमुखः ।
दह्यते वेपते चैव स्वपते च मुहुर्मुहुः ॥

The shoulders, hips, and neck are broken. His gaze is upward and his face downward. He burns, trembles, and repeatedly falls asleep.

BP 1.34.5

शस्त्रेण च्छिद्यमानस्य रुधिरं न प्रवर्तते ।
दण्डेन ताड्यमानस्य दण्डराजी न जायते ॥

When he is cut with a weapon, blood does not flow. When he is struck with a staff, no stripe appears.

BP 1.34.6

दंशे काकपदं सुनीलमसकृज्जम्बूफलाभं घनमुच्छूनं रुधिरार्द्रसेकबहुलं कृच्छ्रान्निरोधो भवेत् ।
हिक्काश्वासगलग्रहश्च सुमहान्पाण्डुस्त्वचा दृश्यते शुष्काङ्गः प्रवदन्ति शास्त्रनिपुणास्तत्कालदष्टं विदुः ॥

When the bite bears a crow foot mark, very dark blue, repeatedly like a ripe Java plum, dense and swollen, drenched with much blood, and difficult to stop; when hiccups, breathlessness, and severe constriction of the throat occur, the skin appears pale and the limbs dry. Those skilled in the shastras know this as a bite of Time.

BP 1.34.7

दंशे यस्याथ शोथः प्रचलितवलितं मण्डलं वा सुनीलं प्रस्वेदो गात्र भेदः स्रवति च रुधिरं सानुनासं च जल्पेत् ।
दन्तोष्ठाभ्यां वियोगो भ्रमति च हृदयं सन्निरोधश्च तीव्रो दिव्यानामेष दंशः स्थलविपुलमयो विद्धि तं कालदष्टम् ॥

When the bite has swelling, a moving and curling dark blue circle, sweating, splitting pain in the limbs, flowing blood, and nasal speech; when the teeth and lips draw apart, the heart reels, and there is severe obstruction; this is the bite of celestial serpents, spreading broadly on the surface. Know him as bitten by Time.

BP 1.34.8

दन्तैर्दन्तान्स्पृशति बहुशो दृष्टिरायासखिन्ना स्थूलो दंशः स्रवति रुधिरं केकरं चक्षुरेकम् ॥
प्रत्यादिष्टः श्वसिति सततं सानुनासं च भाषेत्पापं ब्रूते सकलगदितं कालदण्डं तमाहुः ॥

He repeatedly touches tooth to tooth, his sight is wearied by strain, the large bite flows with blood, and one eye squints. When addressed, he breathes continually and speaks nasally. He speaks evil of everything said. They call him the Staff of Time.

BP 1.34.9

वेपते वेदना तीव्रा रक्तनेत्रश्च जायते ।
ग्रीवाभङ्गश्चला नाभिः कालदष्टं विनिर्दिशेत् ॥

He trembles, has severe pain, and becomes red eyed. His neck bends and his navel moves. One should declare him bitten by Time.

BP 1.34.10

दर्पणे सलिले वापि आत्मच्छायां न पश्यति ।
मन्दरश्मिं तथा तीव्रं तेजोहीनं दिवाकरम् ॥

He does not see his own reflection in a mirror or in water. He sees the sun as dim rayed, though intense, and devoid of brilliance.

BP 1.34.11

वेपते वेदनात्रस्तो रक्तनेत्रश्च जायते ।
स याति निधनं जन्तुः कालदण्डं विनिर्दिशेत् ॥

He trembles, distressed by pain, and becomes red eyed. That creature goes to death. One should declare him struck by the Staff of Time.

BP 1.34.12

अष्टम्यां च नवम्यां च कृष्णपक्षे चतुर्दशीम् ।
नागपञ्चमीदष्टानां जीवितस्य च संशयः ॥

On the eighth and ninth, and on the fourteenth of the dark fortnight, and for those bitten on the fifth day of the Serpents, life is in doubt.

BP 1.34.13

आर्द्राश्लेषामघाभरणीकृत्तिकासु विशेषतः ।
विशाखां त्रिषु पूर्वासु मूलस्वातीशतात्मके ॥
सर्पदष्टा न जीवन्ति विषं पीतं च यैस्तथा ॥

Especially under Ardra, Ashlesha, Magha, Bharani, and Krittika; under Vishakha, the three Purvas, Mula, Svati, and Shatabhisha, those bitten by a serpent and those who have drunk poison do not live.

BP 1.34.14

शून्यागारे श्मशाने च शुष्कवृक्षे तथैव च ।
न जीवन्ति नरा दष्टा नक्षत्रे तिथिसंयुते ॥

In an empty house, at a cremation ground, and likewise at a dry tree, those bitten do not live when a constellation and lunar day coincide.

BP 1.34.15

अष्टोत्तरं मर्म शतं प्राणिनां समुदाहृतम् ।
तेषां मध्ये तु मर्माणि दश द्वे चापि कीर्तिते ॥

There are said to be one hundred and eight vital points in living beings. Among them, twelve vital points are named.

BP 1.34.16

शङ्खे नेत्रे भ्रुवोर्मध्ये बस्तिभ्यां वृषणोत्तरे ।
कक्षे स्कन्धे हदि मध्ये तालुके चिबुके गुदे ॥

At the temples, eyes, between the brows, the bladder, above the testicles, the armpits, shoulders, middle of the heart, palate, chin, and anus.

BP 1.34.17

एषु द्वादशमर्मेषु दंशैः शस्त्रेण वा हतः ।
न जीवति नरो लोके कालदण्डं विनिर्दिशेत् ॥

A person struck by a bite or weapon at these twelve vital points does not live in this world. One should declare the Staff of Time.

BP 1.34.18

अकचटतपयशां वदन्ति प्रोक्ता जीवन्ति न तत्र हि ।
गतं ब्रूयाद्यदि स्खलति शिरस्तस्य सम्प्राप्तकालः ॥

They utter the sounds a, ka, ca, ṭa, ta, pa, ya, and sha. Those named do not live there. If one says he is gone and his head falters, his time has arrived.

BP 1.34.19

भवति च यदि दूतो ह्युत्तमस्याधमो वा यदि भवति च दूत उत्तमो वाधमस्य ।
आदौ दष्टस्य नाम यदि वदति क्वचिद्वक्ति तस्याथ पश्चात्तं वर्णभेदो यदि भवति समः प्राप्त कालस्य दूतः ॥

If the messenger of a high person is low, or the messenger of a low person is high; if he speaks the bitten person's name first and the matter afterward; if their complexion is alike, he is a messenger of one whose time has arrived.

BP 1.34.20

दूतो वा दण्डहस्तो भवति च युगलं पाशहस्तस्तथा वा रक्तवस्त्रं च कृष्णं मुखशिरसि गतमेकवस्त्रश्च दूतः ॥
तैलाभ्यक्तश्च तद्वद्यदि त्वरितगतिर्मुक्तकेशश्च याति यः कुर्याद्घोरशब्दं करचरणयुगैः प्राप्तकालस्य दूतः ॥

The messenger may hold a staff, a pair, or a noose. He wears red or black cloth upon his face or head, or only one garment. If oil anointed, swift moving, loose haired, and making a dreadful sound with his hands and feet, he is a messenger of one whose time has arrived.

BP 1.34.21

नागोदयं प्रवक्ष्यामि ईशानेन तु भाषितम् ।
ब्रह्मणा तु पुरा सृष्टा ग्रहा नागास्त्वनेकशः ॥

I shall explain the arising of the Serpents, as spoken by Ishana. Brahma created long ago the planets and many Serpents.

BP 1.34.22

अनन्तं भास्करं विद्यात्सोमं विद्यात्तु वासुकिम् ।
तक्षकं भूमिपुत्रं तु कर्कोटं च बुधं विदुः ॥

Know Ananta as the Sun, Vasuki as the Moon, Takshaka as Mars, the son of Earth, and Karkota as Mercury.

BP 1.34.23

पद्मं बृहस्पतिं विद्यान्महापद्मं च भार्गवम् ।
कुलिकः शंखपालश्च द्वावेतौ तु शनैश्चरः ॥

Know Padma as Jupiter and Mahapadma as Venus, the descendant of Bhrigu. Kulika and Shankhapala are both Saturn.

BP 1.34.24

पूर्वपादः शंखपालो द्वितीयः कुलिकस्तथा ।
नित्यं भागे यथोद्दिष्टे दिनरात्री तथैव च ॥

Shankhapala is in the first quarter, and Kulika likewise in the second, continually in the allotted division, in both day and night.

BP 1.34.25

शुक्रसोमौ च मध्याह्ने उदये तु क्षमासुतः ।
शनिः प्रागष्टमे भागे दिवारात्रे त्विहोच्यते ॥

Venus and the Moon are at midday, Mars, the son of Earth, at rising, and Saturn in the preceding eighth division. This is stated here for day and night.

BP 1.34.26

ग्रहाश्च भुञ्जते चैव शेषं भागस्य लक्षणम् ।
रविवारे सदा ज्ञेयौ पादौ दश चतुर्दश ॥

The planets occupy their portions, and the remaining marks of the divisions are as follows. On Sunday the tenth and fourteenth quarters are always to be known.

BP 1.34.27

अष्ट द्वादश वै चन्द्रे दश षष्ठे कुजे तथा ।
बुधस्य नवमे पादे राहौ च दिवसस्य च ॥

For the Moon, the eighth and twelfth; for Mars, the tenth and sixth; for Mercury, the ninth quarter; and for Rahu, the division of the day.

BP 1.34.28

गुरोर्द्वितीयः षष्ठश्च षोडशस्य तु वर्जयेत् ।
भास्करस्य दिने प्रोक्ते चतुर्थे दशमेष्टमे ॥

For Jupiter, the second, sixth, and sixteenth are to be avoided. On the Sun's day the fourth, tenth, and eighth are stated.

BP 1.34.29

शनैश्चरदिने पादं त्यजेच्चैव सुदारुणम् ।
द्वितीयं द्वादशं चैव षोडशस्य तु वर्जयेत् ॥

On Saturn's day, one should avoid the very dreadful quarter, and also avoid the second, twelfth, and sixteenth.

BP 1.34.30

मुहूर्तघटिकादूर्द्ध्वं घटिका चतुर्थं भागं विंशतिश्च ।
कुहूसुतं बुद्बुदं निमेषमेतत्कालस्य लक्षणम् ॥

After a muhurta and a ghatika, a ghatika, a fourth part, and twenty; Kuhu's son, a bubble, and a blink. These are the measures of Time.
