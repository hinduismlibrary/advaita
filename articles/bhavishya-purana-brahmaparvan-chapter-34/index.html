Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 34 — Traditional Signs of Severe Snakebite and Nāga–Planet Correspondences

BP 1.34.1

कश्यप उवाच
अतः परं प्रवक्ष्यामि कालदष्टस्य लक्षणम् ।
शृणु गौतम तत्त्वेन यादृशो भवते नरः ॥

Kaśyapa said: Next I shall explain the signs of one bitten under the power of Time. Gautama, hear accurately what such a person becomes like.

Word-for-word
अतः परम् प्रवक्ष्यामि (ataḥ param pravakṣyāmi) — next I shall explain;
कालदष्टस्य लक्षणम् (kāla-daṣṭasya lakṣaṇam) — signs of one bitten under Time;
शृणु गौतम तत्त्वेन (śṛṇu gautama tattvena) — Gautama hear accurately;
यादृशः भवते नरः (yādṛśaḥ bhavate naraḥ) — what the person becomes like
Transliteration
kaśyapa uvāca
ataḥ paraṃ pravakṣyāmi kāladaṣṭasya lakṣaṇam |
śṛṇu gautama tattvena yādṛśo bhavate naraḥ | | 1

BP 1.34.2

जिह्राभङ्गोऽथ हृच्छूलं चक्षुर्भ्यां च न पश्यति ।
दंशं च दग्धसंकाशं पक्वजम्बूफलोपमम् ॥

The tongue becomes impaired, there is heart-pain, and the eyes cannot see. The bite looks burned and resembles a ripe jambu fruit.

Word-for-word
जिह्वाभङ्गः (jihvā-bhaṅgaḥ) — impairment of tongue;
हृच्छूलम् (hṛc-chūlam) — heart-pain;
चक्षुर्भ्याम् न पश्यति (cakṣurbhyām na paśyati) — cannot see with eyes;
दंशम् दग्धसंकाशम् (daṃśam dagdha-saṅkāśam) — bite looks burned;
पक्वजम्बूफलोपमम् (pakva-jambū-phalopamam) — like ripe jambu fruit
Transliteration
jihrābhaṅgo'tha hṛcchūlaṃ cakṣurbhyāṃ ca na paśyati |
daṃśaṃ ca dagdhasaṃkāśaṃ pakvajambūphalopamam | | 2

BP 1.34.3

वैवर्ण्यं चैव दन्तानां श्यावो भवति वर्णतः ।
सर्वेष्वङ्गेषु शैथिल्यं पुरीषस्य च भेदनम् ॥

The teeth become discoloured and the complexion dusky; all the limbs grow slack, and there is discharge of stool.

Word-for-word
वैवर्ण्यम् दन्तानाम् (vaivarṇyam dantānām) — discolouration of teeth;
श्यावः वर्णतः (śyāvaḥ varṇataḥ) — dusky complexion;
सर्वेषु अङ्गेषु शैथिल्यम् (sarveṣu aṅgeṣu śaithilyam) — slackness in all limbs;
पुरीषस्य भेदनम् (purīṣasya bhedanam) — discharge of stool
Transliteration
vaivarṇyaṃ caiva dantānāṃ śyāvo bhavati varṇataḥ |
sarveṣvaṅgeṣu śaithilyaṃ purīṣasya ca bhedanam | | 3

BP 1.34.4

भग्नस्कन्धकटिग्रीव ऊर्ध्वदृष्टिरधोमुखः ।
दह्यते वेपते चैव स्वपते च मुहुर्मुहुः ॥

The shoulders, waist, and neck seem broken; the gaze turns upward while the face points down. The victim burns, trembles, and repeatedly falls asleep.

Word-for-word
भग्नस्कन्धकटिग्रीवः (bhagna-skandha-kaṭi-grīvaḥ) — shoulders waist neck seeming broken;
ऊर्ध्वदृष्टिः अधोमुखः (ūrdhva-dṛṣṭiḥ adho-mukhaḥ) — upward gaze downturned face;
दह्यते वेपते (dahyate vepate) — burns and trembles;
स्वपते मुहुः मुहुः (svapate muhuḥ muhuḥ) — repeatedly sleeps
Transliteration
bhagnaskandhakaṭigrīva ūrdhvadṛṣṭiradhomukhaḥ |
dahyate vepate caiva svapate ca muhurmuhuḥ | | 4

BP 1.34.5

शस्त्रेण च्छिद्यमानस्य रुधिरं न प्रवर्तते ।
दण्डेन ताड्यमानस्य दण्डराजी न जायते ॥

When cut with a blade, blood does not flow; when struck with a staff, no line from the blow appears.

Word-for-word
शस्त्रेण छिद्यमानस्य (śastreṇa chidyamānasya) — when cut with blade;
रुधिरम् न प्रवर्तते (rudhiram na pravartate) — blood does not flow;
दण्डेन ताड्यमानस्य (daṇḍena tāḍyamānasya) — when struck with staff;
दण्डराजी न जायते (daṇḍa-rājī na jāyate) — no staff-line appears
Transliteration
śastreṇa cchidyamānasya rudhiraṃ na pravartate |
daṇḍena tāḍyamānasya daṇḍarājī na jāyate | | 5

BP 1.34.6

दंशे काकपदं सुनीलमसकृज्जम्बूफलाभं घनमुच्छूनं रुधिरार्द्रसेकबहुलं कृच्छ्रान्निरोधो भवेत् ।
हिक्काश्वासगलग्रहश्च सुमहान्पाण्डुस्त्वचा दृश्यते शुष्काङ्गः प्रवदन्ति शास्त्रनिपुणास्तत्कालदष्टं विदुः ॥

Experts in the treatises identify as a fatal bite one whose wound bears a very dark crows-foot mark, repeatedly resembles a jambu fruit, is dense and swollen, and is soaked with abundant blood; suppression is difficult, severe hiccup, breathlessness, and constriction of the throat occur, the skin appears pale, and the limbs become dry.

Word-for-word
दंशे काकपदम् सुनीलम् (daṃśe kāka-padam su-nīlam) — dark crow-foot at bite;
जम्बूफलाभम् (jambū-phalābham) — like jambu fruit;
घनम् उच्छूनम् (ghanam ucchūnam) — dense and swollen;
रुधिरार्द्रसेकबहुलम् (rudhirārdra-seka-bahulam) — abundantly soaked with blood;
कृच्छ्रात् निरोधः (kṛcchrāt nirodhaḥ) — difficult suppression;
हिक्काश्वासगलग्रहः (hikkā-śvāsa-gala-grahaḥ) — hiccup breathlessness throat constriction;
पाण्डुः त्वचा (pāṇḍuḥ tvacā) — pale skin;
शुष्काङ्गः (śuṣkāṅgaḥ) — dry-limbed;
कालदष्टम् विदुः (kāla-daṣṭam viduḥ) — know as fatal bite
Transliteration
daṃśe kākapadaṃ sunīlamasakṛjjambūphalābhaṃ ghanamucchūnaṃ rudhirārdrasekabahulaṃ kṛcchrānnirodho bhavet |
hikkāśvāsagalagrahaśca sumahānpāṇḍustvacā dṛśyate śuṣkāṅgaḥ pravadanti śāstranipuṇāstatkāladaṣṭaṃ viduḥ | | 6

BP 1.34.7

दंशे यस्याथ शोथः प्रचलितवलितं मण्डलं वा सुनीलं प्रस्वेदो गात्र भेदः स्रवति च रुधिरं सानुनासं च जल्पेत् ।
दन्तोष्ठाभ्यां वियोगो भ्रमति च हृदयं सन्निरोधश्च तीव्रो दिव्यानामेष दंशः स्थलविपुलमयो विद्धि तं कालदष्टम् ॥

Know as a fatal bite one whose wound swells, whose dark circular mark shifts and folds, who sweats and suffers splitting pain in the limbs, bleeds, speaks nasally, loses control of teeth and lips, feels the heart reel, and experiences intense obstruction; the verse calls this a broad terrestrial bite of divine snakes.

Word-for-word
शोथः (śothaḥ) — swelling;
प्रचलितवलितम् मण्डलम् सुनीलम् (pracalita-valitam maṇḍalam su-nīlam) — shifting folded dark circle;
प्रस्वेदः गात्रभेदः (prasvedaḥ gātra-bhedaḥ) — sweating and splitting limb-pain;
रुधिरम् स्रवति (rudhiram sravati) — blood flows;
सानुनासम् जल्पेत् (sānunāsam jalpet) — speaks nasally;
दन्तोष्ठाभ्याम् वियोगः (dantoṣṭhābhyām viyogaḥ) — loss of control of teeth and lips;
हृदयम् भ्रमति (hṛdayam bhramati) — heart reels;
सन्निरोधः तीव्रः (sannirodhaḥ tīvraḥ) — intense obstruction;
कालदष्टम् (kāla-daṣṭam) — fatal bite
Transliteration
daṃśe yasyātha śothaḥ pracalitavalitaṃ maṇḍalaṃ vā sunīlaṃ prasvedo gātra bhedaḥ sravati ca rudhiraṃ sānunāsaṃ ca jalpet |
dantoṣṭhābhyāṃ viyogo bhramati ca hṛdayaṃ sannirodhaśca tīvro divyānāmeṣa daṃśaḥ sthalavipulamayo viddhi taṃ kāladaṣṭam | | 7

BP 1.34.8

दन्तैर्दन्तान्स्पृशति बहुशो दृष्टिरायासखिन्ना स्थूलो दंशः स्रवति रुधिरं केकरं चक्षुरेकम् ॥
प्रत्यादिष्टः श्वसिति सततं सानुनासं च भाषेत्पापं ब्रूते सकलगदितं कालदण्डं तमाहुः ॥

They call it the Staff of Time when the victim repeatedly grinds the teeth, the gaze is exhausted by strain, the bite is large and bleeding, one eye squints, breathing is constant despite being addressed, speech is nasal, and everything said is inauspicious.

Word-for-word
दन्तैः दन्तान् स्पृशति (dantaiḥ dantān spṛśati) — grinds teeth;
दृष्टिः आयासखिन्ना (dṛṣṭiḥ āyāsa-khinnā) — gaze exhausted by strain;
स्थूलः दंशः रुधिरम् स्रवति (sthūlaḥ daṃśaḥ rudhiram sravati) — large bleeding bite;
केकरम् चक्षुः एकम् (kekaram cakṣuḥ ekam) — one squinting eye;
श्वसिति सततम् (śvasiti satatam) — breathes constantly;
सानुनासम् भाषेत् (sānunāsam bhāṣet) — speaks nasally;
पापम् ब्रूते (pāpam brūte) — speaks inauspiciously;
कालदण्डम् आहुः (kāla-daṇḍam āhuḥ) — call Staff of Time
Transliteration
dantairdantānspṛśati bahuśo dṛṣṭirāyāsakhinnā sthūlo daṃśaḥ sravati rudhiraṃ kekaraṃ cakṣurekam | |
pratyādiṣṭaḥ śvasiti satataṃ sānunāsaṃ ca bhāṣetpāpaṃ brūte sakalagaditaṃ kāladaṇḍaṃ tamāhuḥ | | 8

BP 1.34.9

वेपते वेदना तीव्रा रक्तनेत्रश्च जायते ।
ग्रीवाभङ्गश्चला नाभिः कालदष्टं विनिर्दिशेत् ॥

Trembling, intense pain, reddened eyes, a bent neck, and movement at the navel are designated signs of a fatal bite.

Word-for-word
वेपते (vepate) — trembles;
वेदना तीव्रा (vedanā tīvrā) — intense pain;
रक्तनेत्रः (rakta-netraḥ) — red-eyed;
ग्रीवाभङ्गः (grīvā-bhaṅgaḥ) — bent neck;
चला नाभिः (calā nābhiḥ) — moving navel;
कालदष्टम् विनिर्दिशेत् (kāla-daṣṭam vinirdiśet) — designate fatal bite
Transliteration
vepate vedanā tīvrā raktanetraśca jāyate |
grīvābhaṅgaścalā nābhiḥ kāladaṣṭaṃ vinirdiśet | | 9

BP 1.34.10

दर्पणे सलिले वापि आत्मच्छायां न पश्यति ।
मन्दरश्मिं तथा तीव्रं तेजोहीनं दिवाकरम् ॥

The victim does not see a reflection in a mirror or water and perceives the blazing sun as dim-rayed and lacking brilliance.

मन्दरश्मिम् दिवाकरम् (manda-raśmim divākaram) — dim-rayed sun;
तेजोहीनम् (tejo-hīnam) — lacking brilliance

BP 1.34.11

वेपते वेदनात्रस्तो रक्तनेत्रश्च जायते ।

The victim trembles, terrified by pain, and becomes red-eyed; that creature goes to death—this is designated the Staff of Time.

BP 1.34.12

On the eighth and ninth, and on the fourteenth of the dark fortnight, the life of those bitten on Nāga-pañcamī is in doubt.

BP 1.34.13

आर्द्राश्लेषामघाभरणीकृत्तिकासु विशेषतः ।
विशाखां त्रिषु पूर्वासु मूलस्वातीशतात्मके ॥
सर्पदष्टा न जीवन्ति विषं पीतं च यैस्तथा ॥

Especially under Ārdrā, Āśleṣā, Maghā, Bharaṇī, and Kṛttikā, and under Viśākhā, the three Pūrvās, Mūla, Svātī, and Śatabhiṣaj, those bitten by a snake and those who have drunk poison do not live.

Word-for-word
आर्द्राश्लेषामघाभरणीकृत्तिकासु (ārdrā-āśleṣā-maghā-bharaṇī-kṛttikāsu) — under listed constellations;
विशाखाम् त्रिषु पूर्वासु (viśākhām triṣu pūrvāsu) — Viśākhā and three Pūrvās;
मूलस्वातीशतात्मके (mūla-svātī-śatātmake) — Mūla Svātī Śatabhiṣaj;
सर्पदष्टाः न जीवन्ति (sarpa-daṣṭāḥ na jīvanti) — snake-bitten do not survive;
विषम् पीतम् (viṣam pītam) — venom swallowed
Transliteration

sarpadaṣṭā na jīvanti viṣaṃ pītaṃ ca yaistathā | | 13

BP 1.34.14

शून्यागारे श्मशाने च शुष्कवृक्षे तथैव च ।

Those bitten in an empty house, cremation ground, or by a dry tree, under the specified conjunction of constellation and lunar date, are said not to survive.

अष्टोत्तरं मर्म शतं प्राणिनां समुदाहृतम् ।

BP 1.34.16

शङ्खे नेत्रे भ्रुवोर्मध्ये बस्तिभ्यां वृषणोत्तरे ।
कक्षे स्कन्धे हदि मध्ये तालुके चिबुके गुदे ॥

They are the temples, eyes, space between the brows, bladder region, above the testicles, armpits, shoulders, middle of the heart, palate, chin, and anus.

हृदि मध्ये (hṛdi madhye) — middle of heart;
तालुके चिबुके गुदे (tāluke cibuke gude) — palate chin anus

BP 1.34.17

A person struck by a bite or weapon in these twelve vital points does not survive in this world; it is designated the Staff of Time.
