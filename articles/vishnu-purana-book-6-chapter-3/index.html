VP 6.3.1

सर्वेषामेव भूतानां त्रिविधः प्रतिसंचरः।
नैमित्तिकः प्राकृतिकस् तथैवात्यन्तिको लयः

Sri Paraasharar - For all the beings, there are three types of pralaya, dissolution. One is naimittika pralaya, another is praakrutika pralaya, another is aatyantika pralaya.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sarveṣāmeva bhūtānāṃ trividhaḥ pratisaṃcaraḥ . naimittikaḥ prākṛtikas tathaivātyantiko layaḥ

Śrīdhara

prākṛtodviparārdhaka iti jalpanāt parārdha-dvayātmake brahmāyuṣi pūrṇe prakṛtau tat-kāryasya layaḥ prākṛtaḥ ||१|| śrī-maitreya uvāca— parārdha-saṅkhyāṃ bhagavan mamācakṣva yayā tu saḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

प्राकृतोद्विपरार्धक इति जल्पनात् परार्ध-द्वयात्मके ब्रह्मायुषि पूर्णे प्रकृतौ तत्-कार्यस्य लयः प्राकृतः ॥१॥ श्री-मैत्रेय उवाच— परार्ध-सङ्ख्यां भगवन् ममाचक्ष्व यया तु सः ।

Śrīdhara commentary.

Sri Paraasharar - For all the beings, there are three types of pralaya, dissolution. One is naimittika pralaya, another is praakrutika pralaya, another is aatyantika pralaya.

VP 6.3.2

ब्राह्मो नैमित्तिकस्तेषां कल्पान्ते प्रतिसंचरः।
आत्यन्तिकस्तु मोक्षाख्यः प्राकृतो द्विपरार्धकः

Paraardha is one half of Chaturmukha Brahma's life. Two paraardhas is the two halves together, the complete hundred years of Chaturmukha Brahma. At that time, the entire prakruti mandala, with all the effects will get merged in Paramaatman.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

brāhmo naimittikasteṣāṃ kalpānte pratisaṃcaraḥ . ātyantikastu mokṣākhyaḥ prākṛto dviparārdhakaḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 6.3.3

परार्ध-सङ्ख्यां भगवन् ममाचक्ष्व यया तु सः।
द्विगुणीकृतया ज्ञेयः प्राकृतः प्रतिसञ्चरः

Maitreyar - O Bhagavan, please do tell me what is the measure of this Paraardha. By doubling it, by dviparaardha, we have to understand that time duration when the praakruta pralaya happens.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

parārdhasaṃkhyāṃ bhagavanmamācakṣva yayā tu saḥ . dviguṇīkṛtayā jñeyaḥ prākṛtaḥ pratisaṃcaraḥ

Śrīdhara

prathamāṃśe parārdha-mānam uktam eva . tathāpi kiṃ tad eva mānaṃ parārdhasya uta prakārāntaram apy astīty āśayena punaḥ pṛcchati—parārdheti ||३|| śrī-parāśara uvāca— sthānāt sthānaṃ daśa-guṇam ekasmād gaṇyate dvija .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

प्रथमांशे परार्ध-मानम् उक्तम् एव । तथापि किं तद् एव मानं परार्धस्य उत प्रकारान्तरम् अप्य् अस्तीत्य् आशयेन पुनः पृच्छति—परार्धेति ॥३॥

श्री-पराशर उवाच—

Śrīdhara commentary.

Maitreyar - O Bhagavan, please do tell me what is the measure of this Paraardha. By doubling it, by dviparaardha, we have to understand that time duration when the praakruta pralaya happens.

VP 6.3.4

स्थानात्स्थानं दशगुणमेकस्माद्रण्यते द्विज।
ततोऽष्टादशमे भागे परार्धमभिधीयते

Sri Paraasharar - From one position to another, as in units, tens, hundreds, each one will be ten times the previous. If we go on counting like that, ten times, and ten times, etc., eighteen times, whatever number is got, that is said to be the paraardha. First is one, then ten, then hundred, then thousand, then ten thousand, then a lakh, then keep multiplying by ten, we get prayuta, koti, arbuda, abja, kharva, nikharva, then mahaasaroja, then shanku, then samudra, then madhya. The last

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sthānātsthānaṃ daśaguṇamekasmādraṇyate dvija . tato’ṣṭādaśame bhāge parārdhamabhidhīyate

Śrīdhara

prakārāntaram apy astīty āha—sthānāt sthānam iti . yathāha vāyuḥ— koṭi-koṭi-sahasrāṇi parārdham iti kīrtyate . parārdha-dviguṇaṃ cāpi param āhur manīṣiṇaḥ || sthānaṃ daśa-guṇaṃ vidyād ekaṃ daśa-śataṃ tataḥ . sahasram ayutaṃ tasmān niyutaṃ prayutaṃ tataḥ || arbudaṃ nyarbudaṃ caiva vṛndaṃ caiva tataḥ param . kharvaṃ caiva nikharvaṃ ca śaṅkhaṃ padmaṃ tathaiva ca || samudro madhyam antaś ca parārdhaṃ param eva ca . evam aṣṭādaśaitāni padāni gaṇanā-vidhau || kalpa-saṅkhyā-pravṛttas tu parārdho brahmaṇaḥ smṛtaḥ . tāvac ceṣo’sya kālasya tad-ante pratisṛjyate || iti . ekaṃ daśa-guṇaṃ daśa, daśa daśa-guṇaṃ śataṃ, śataṃ daśa-guṇaṃ sahasram ity evam eka-sthānādīnāṃ mānato vṛddhyā aṣṭādaśa-sthāne parārdhaṃ bhavati kalpāś cātra sāṅkhyeyāḥ . kalpa-saṅkhyā-pravṛttas tu parārdho brahmaṇaḥ smṛtaḥ iti vāyūkteḥ . tataś ca kalpānāṃ koṭi-koṭi-sahasrāṇi parārdham . tad-dviguṇaṃ paraṃ brahmāyur ity uktaṃ bhavati . yat tūktaṃ prathamāṃśe— nijena tasya mānena āyur varṣa-śataṃ smṛtam . tat-parākhyaṃ tad-ardhaṃ ca parārdham abhidhīyate || [१.३.५] evaṃ tu brahmaṇo varṣam evaṃ varṣa-śataṃ smṛtam . [१.३.२६] ity ādi . tasmin pakṣe kalpānāṃ ṣaṭ-triṃśat-sahasrāṇi brahmaṇa āyur ity uktaṃ syāt . anayoś ca pakṣayor mahā-kalpa-bhedena vyavasthā . yad vā, yathokta-kalpa-śabdena lakṣaṇayā tad-aṃśa-bhūta-divya-mānuṣa-varṣādibhiḥ parārdha-mānena virodha iti sampādanīyam ||४|| parārdha-dviguṇaṃ yat tu prākṛtaḥ sa layo dvija .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

प्रकारान्तरम् अप्य् अस्तीत्य् आह—स्थानात् स्थानम् इति । यथाह वायुः—
कोटि-कोटि-सहस्राणि परार्धम् इति कीर्त्यते ।
परार्ध-द्विगुणं चापि परम् आहुर् मनीषिणः ॥
स्थानं दश-गुणं विद्याद् एकं दश-शतं ततः ।
सहस्रम् अयुतं तस्मान् नियुतं प्रयुतं ततः ॥
अर्बुदं न्यर्बुदं चैव वृन्दं चैव ततः परम् ।
खर्वं चैव निखर्वं च शङ्खं पद्मं तथैव च ॥
समुद्रो मध्यम् अन्तश् च परार्धं परम् एव च ।
एवम् अष्टादशैतानि पदानि गणना-विधौ ॥
कल्प-सङ्ख्या-प्रवृत्तस् तु परार्धो ब्रह्मणः स्मृतः ।
तावच् चेषोऽस्य कालस्य तद्-अन्ते प्रतिसृज्यते ॥ इति ।
एकं दश-गुणं दश, दश दश-गुणं शतं, शतं दश-गुणं सहस्रम् इत्य् एवम् एक-स्थानादीनां मानतो वृद्ध्या अष्टादश-स्थाने परार्धं भवति कल्पाश् चात्र साङ्ख्येयाः । कल्प-सङ्ख्या-प्रवृत्तस् तु परार्धो ब्रह्मणः स्मृतः इति वायूक्तेः ।
ततश् च कल्पानां कोटि-कोटि-सहस्राणि परार्धम् । तद्-द्विगुणं परं ब्रह्मायुर् इत्य् उक्तं भवति । यत् तूक्तं प्रथमांशे—
निजेन तस्य मानेन आयुर् वर्ष-शतं स्मृतम् ।
तत्-पराख्यं तद्-अर्धं च परार्धम् अभिधीयते ॥ [१.३.५]
एवं तु ब्रह्मणो वर्षम् एवं वर्ष-शतं स्मृतम् । [१.३.२६] इत्य् आदि ।
तस्मिन् पक्षे कल्पानां षट्-त्रिंशत्-सहस्राणि ब्रह्मण आयुर् इत्य् उक्तं स्यात् । अनयोश् च पक्षयोर् महा-कल्प-भेदेन व्यवस्था । यद् वा, यथोक्त-कल्प-शब्देन लक्षणया तद्-अंश-भूत-दिव्य-मानुष-वर्षादिभिः परार्ध-मानेन विरोध इति सम्पादनीयम् ॥४॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Sri Paraasharar - From one position to another, as in units, tens, hundreds, each one will be ten times the previous. If we go on counting like that, ten times, and ten times, etc., eighteen times, whatever number is got, that is said to be the paraardha. First is one, then ten, then hundred, then thousand, then ten thousand, then a lakh, then keep multiplying by ten, we get prayuta, koti, arbuda, abja, kharva, nikharva, then mahaasaroja, then shanku, then samudra, then madhya. VP 6.3.5

परार्धद्विगुणं यत्तु प्राकृतःस लयो द्विज।
तदाऽव्यक्तेऽखिलं व्यक्तं स्वहेतौ लयमेति वै

Double of the paraardha, whatever happens in the praakruta pralaya, all the vyakta, whatever is manifested here, goes and merges into the unmanifest, which is the cause of all the manifest.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

parārdhadviguṇaṃ yattu prākṛtaḥsa layo dvija . tadā’vyakte’khilaṃ vyaktaṃ svahetau layameti vai

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 6.3.6

निमेषो मानुषो योऽसौ मात्रामात्राप्रमाणतः।
तैः पञ्चदशभिः काष्ठा त्रिंशत्काष्ठ कला स्मृता

Now, the measure of time is told. The human nimesha, the measure of that is maatraa. The nimesha is said to be maatraa, as it is of the measure of a maatraa. The laghu akshara, like a, ka, is one maatraa time measure, this is called nimesha, in human measure. Fifteen of that is called kaashtha, and thirty kaashthaas form one kalaa.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nimeṣo mānuṣo yo’sau mātrāmātrāpramāṇataḥ . taiḥ pañcadaśabhiḥ kāṣṭhā triṃśatkāṣṭha kalā smṛtā

Śrīdhara

naimittikaṃ layaṃ prapañcayiṣyan nimeṣādi-krameṇa prathamāṃśoktam eva kalpa-pramāṇam anusārayati—nimeṣa ity ādi saptabhiḥ . mātraiva mātrā saiva pramāṇaṃ yasya saḥ eka-mātra-laghv-akṣaroccāraṇa-mātra-kāla-sammito nimeṣaḥ . nimeṣa-kāla-tulyo hi mātrā laghv akṣaraṃ ca yat iti brahmāṇḍokteḥ ||६|| nāḍikā tu pramāṇena sā kalā daśa paṃ ca ca .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

नैमित्तिकं लयं प्रपञ्चयिष्यन् निमेषादि-क्रमेण प्रथमांशोक्तम् एव कल्प-प्रमाणम् अनुसारयति—निमेष इत्य् आदि सप्तभिः । मात्रैव मात्रा सैव प्रमाणं यस्य सः एक-मात्र-लघ्व्-अक्षरोच्चारण-मात्र-काल-सम्मितो निमेषः । निमेष-काल-तुल्यो हि मात्रा लघ्व् अक्षरं च यत् इति ब्रह्माण्डोक्तेः ॥६॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Now, the measure of time is told. The human nimesha, the measure of that is maatraa. The nimesha is said to be maatraa, as it is of the measure of a maatraa. The laghu akshara, like a, ka, is one maatraa time measure, this is called nimesha, in human measure. Fifteen of that is called kaashtha, and thirty kaashthaas form one kalaa.

VP 6.3.8

मागधेन तु मानेन जलप्रस्थस्तु स स्मृतः।
हेममाषैः कृतच्छिद्रचतुर्भिश्चतुरङ्गुलैः

How a naadikaa is measured, is told with the measure of a water, 12.5 the measure of a fluid. In Maagadha raajya, it is called one jalaprastha. 12.5 is the measure of fluid, such a measure of water. From this, the duration of a naadikaa is found out. Five gulaganji seeds measure is said to be one maasha. There is a vessel called prastha, which is used in Maagadha desha. This vessel is filled with 12.5 measure of water. When it is four times, it is said to be naadi. There is a Naadikaa ghata, which is a pot of Naadikaa, which is four times this jalaprastha. 1.5 measures of water, with a measure called pala, when this is made four times, it becomes 50. Fifty measures of water, is taken in a pot. Taking four maasha equivalent of gold, and making a pipe out of that, which is four inches long. The pipe is put to that vessel. And 50 measure of water is poured into that. Whatever time is taken for the water to go out, to empty fully, is said to be one naadi. Or, there is a pot which holds 50 measure of that water, where pala is one measure. 50 measures of that is filled into that pot. In the bottom of that vessel, a hole is made, and a small tube made of gold, which is four inches. This empty vessel is placed, in stable water. The time taken for the water to fill this vessel completely, when the vessel dips completely inside water, that time is said to be naadi. So, the measures are either water filling into this pot, or the time taken for the water to go out. This is how a Naadikaa is measured.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

māgadhena tu mānena jalaprasthastu sa smṛtaḥ . hemamāṣaiḥ kṛtacchidracaturbhiścaturaṅgulaiḥ

Śrīdhara

nāḍikājñānopāyam āha unmānenety-ādinārdhena . ambhasaḥ unmānena unmīyate anenety unmānaṃ pātram . ardhonāni trayodaśa sārdha-dvādaśety arthaḥ . unmāna-rūpeṇa ghaṭitāni sārdha-dvādaśa-palāni sā nāḍikā . sārdha dvādaśa-pala-tāmra-nirmita-pātrān nāḍikā jñātety arthaḥ . kiṃ-pramāṇaṃ tat pātraṃ kāryam ? tatrāha—māgadheneti . māgadhena pramāṇena jala-prasthas tu sa smṛtaḥ iti . sārdha-dvādaśa-pala-jalena hi magadha-deśyaḥ prasthaḥ pūryate, tāvat pramāṇaṃ pātraṃ kāryam ity arthāt siddham . nanu tathāpi pātreṇa kathaṃ nāḍikājñānaṃ kriyā-paricchedyatvāt kālasyety āśaṅkya kriyā-siddhaye prasthaṃ viśinaṣṭi—hemeti . māṣaḥ pañca-guñjaḥ hema-māṣaiś caturbhiḥ caturaṅgula-śalākā-rūpeṇa kṛtaiḥ kṛta-cchidraḥ . tad uktaṃ bhavati—sārdha-dvādaśa-pala-tāmra-mayaṃ māgadha-prastha-sammitam ūrdhvāyanaṃ pātraṃ catur-māṣa-catur-aṅgula-hema-śalākayā kṛta-cchidraṃ jale sthāpitaṃ tena cchidreṇa yāvatā kālena pūryate tāvat-kālo nāḍiketi . tathā ca śukaḥ— dvādaśārdha-palonmānaṃ caturbhiś catur-aṅgulaiḥ . svarṇa-māṣaiḥ kṛta-cchidraṃ yāvat prastha-jala-plutam || [bhā.pu. ३.११.९] iti ||८|| nāḍikābhyām atha dvābhyām muhūrto dvija-sattama .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

नाडिकाज्ञानोपायम् आह उन्मानेनेत्य्-आदिनार्धेन । अम्भसः उन्मानेन उन्मीयते अनेनेत्य् उन्मानं पात्रम् । अर्धोनानि त्रयोदश सार्ध-द्वादशेत्य् अर्थः । उन्मान-रूपेण घटितानि सार्ध-द्वादश-पलानि सा नाडिका । सार्ध द्वादश-पल-ताम्र-निर्मित-पात्रान् नाडिका ज्ञातेत्य् अर्थः ।

किं-प्रमाणं तत् पात्रं कार्यम् ? तत्राह—मागधेनेति । मागधेन प्रमाणेन जल-प्रस्थस् तु स स्मृतः इति । सार्ध-द्वादश-पल-जलेन हि मगध-देश्यः प्रस्थः पूर्यते, तावत् प्रमाणं पात्रं कार्यम् इत्य् अर्थात् सिद्धम् ।

ननु तथापि पात्रेण कथं नाडिकाज्ञानं क्रिया-परिच्छेद्यत्वात् कालस्येत्य् आशङ्क्य क्रिया-सिद्धये प्रस्थं विशिनष्टि—हेमेति । माषः पञ्च-गुञ्जः हेम-माषैश् चतुर्भिः चतुरङ्गुल-शलाका-रूपेण कृतैः कृत-च्छिद्रः । तद् उक्तं भवति—सार्ध-द्वादश-पल-ताम्र-मयं मागध-प्रस्थ-सम्मितम् ऊर्ध्वायनं पात्रं चतुर्-माष-चतुर्-अङ्गुल-हेम-शलाकया कृत-च्छिद्रं जले स्थापितं तेन च्छिद्रेण यावता कालेन पूर्यते तावत्-कालो नाडिकेति । तथा च शुकः—

द्वादशार्ध-पलोन्मानं चतुर्भिश् चतुर्-अङ्गुलैः ।

स्वर्ण-माषैः कृत-च्छिद्रं यावत् प्रस्थ-जल-प्लुतम् ॥ [भा.पु. ३.११.९] इति ॥८॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

How a naadikaa is measured, is told with the measure of a water, 12.5 the measure of a fluid. In Maagadha raajya, it is called one jalaprastha. 12.5 is the measure of fluid, such a measure of water. From this, the duration of a naadikaa is found out. Five gulaganji seeds measure is said to be one maasha. There is a vessel called prastha, which is used in Maagadha desha. This vessel is filled with 12.5 measure of water. When it is four times, it is said to be naadi. There is a Naadikaa ghata, which is a pot of Naadikaa, which is four times this jalaprastha. 1.5 measures of water, with a measure called pala, when this is made four times, it becomes 50. Fifty measures of water, is taken in a pot. Taking four maasha equivalent of gold, and making a pipe out of that, which is four inches long. The pipe is put to that vessel. And 50 measure of water is poured into that. Whatever time is taken for the water to go out, to empty fully, is said to be one naadi. Or, there is a pot which holds 50 measure of that water, where pala is one measure. 50 measures of that is filled into that pot. In the bottom of that vessel, a hole is made, and a small tube made of gold, which is four inches. This empty vessel is placed, in stable water. The time taken for the water to fill this vessel completely, when the vessel dips completely inside water, that time is said to be naadi. So, the measures are either water filling into this pot, or the time taken for the water to go out. This is how a Naadikaa is measured.

ततस्तापपरीतास्तु लोकद्वयनिवासिनः।
कृताधिकारागच्छन्ति महर्लोकं महामुने

Being bothered by the heat, those who are living in Bhuvarloka and Suvarloka, those who have still time left, to stay for some more time, because of their adhikaara, powers, position, they go to Maharloka.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tatastāpaparītāstu lokadvayanivāsinaḥ . kṛtādhikārāgacchanti maharlokaṃ mahāmune

Śrīdhara

tata ity ādi śloka-dvayasyaikānvayaḥ . daśāvṛttyā gabhasti-gata-svīya-niyoga-daśāyāḥ tatratya-phala-bhogāvasthāyāḥ . āvṛttyā parivṛttyā . paraiṣiṇaḥ ūrdhva-lokaiṣiṇaḥ . anye tv āhuḥ—trailokye’pi ye kṛtādhikārā manv-ādyāḥ, te hṛtādhikārā bhoga-prakṣīṇa-karmāṇaḥ santaḥ mahar-lokaṃ prāpya tasmād abhitaptāḥ tad-vāsibhiḥ saha sa-gaṇaṃ janaṃ yānti . teṣāṃ manv-ādīnāṃ madhye ye pariṣiṇaḥ para-vastu prāptum icchantaḥ, te daśa-vāram āvṛttyā ākramya tad-vyāpti-dvārā tataḥ param īśvaraṃ viśanti . anye tu trailokyasthā jīvāḥ ātmani saṃlīyante . yathāha vāyuḥ—[*viṣṇucitta-ṭīkā draṣṭavyā*] ... ||२८|| tasmād api mahā-tāpa-taptā lokāt tataḥ param .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तत इत्य् आदि श्लोक-द्वयस्यैकान्वयः । दशावृत्त्या गभस्ति-गत-स्वीय-नियोग-दशायाः तत्रत्य-फल-भोगावस्थायाः । आवृत्त्या परिवृत्त्या । परैषिणः ऊर्ध्व-लोकैषिणः । अन्ये त्व् आहुः—त्रैलोक्येऽपि ये कृताधिकारा मन्व्-आद्याः, ते हृताधिकारा भोग-प्रक्षीण-कर्माणः सन्तः महर्-लोकं प्राप्य तस्माद् अभितप्ताः तद्-वासिभिः सह स-गणं जनं यान्ति । तेषां मन्व्-आदीनां मध्ये ये परिषिणः पर-वस्तु प्राप्तुम् इच्छन्तः, ते दश-वारम् आवृत्त्या आक्रम्य तद्-व्याप्ति-द्वारा ततः परम् ईश्वरं विशन्ति । अन्ये तु त्रैलोक्यस्था जीवाः आत्मनि संलीयन्ते । यथाह वायुः—[*विष्णुचित्त-टीका द्रष्टव्या*] ... ॥२८॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

( ) Being bothered by the heat, those who are living in Bhuvarloka and Suvarloka, those who have still time left, to stay for some more time, because of their adhikaara, powers, position, they go to Maharloka.

VP 6.3.29

तस्माद् अपि महा-ताप-तप्ता लोकात् ततः परम्।
गच्छन्ति जन-लोकं ते दशावृत्त्या परैषिणः

Even in the Maharloka, there is so much of heat because of this huge fire, there also they feel the heat and are getting burnt. They leave that also, and go to Janaloka, which is the next loka. Manu and others who have got still longer life, will go up to Maharloka at that time. Manu and others, who are called Krutaadhikaaraa, as their position and power are taken away, as everything is burnt and there is nothing for them to rule over. There are seven Paataala lokaas which are below. And three - Bhooh, Bhuvah, Suvah - totally it becomes ten. They cannot stay there. Or, ten times, having come and gone, repeating. They desire to attain the superior place. Or, those who are in the three worlds, who still have the position, adhikaara, Manu and others, their powers being taken away, there will be nothing to enjoy there, as everything is lost, will go to Maharloka, and they also feel the heat along with the people living there, and go to Janaloka. Among them, whoever is there, who want to attain the Supreme, in order, they attain the other lokaas, which are Tapoloka, Satyaloka, and then they attain Paramaatman. The other jeevaas, who are living in the three lokaas, go and merge into Chaturmukha Brahma.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tasmādapi mahātāpataptā lokāttataḥ param . gacchanti janalokaṃ te daśāvṛttyā paraiṣiṇaḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.
