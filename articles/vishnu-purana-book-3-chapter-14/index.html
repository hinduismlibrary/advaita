VP 3.14.1

और्व उवाच— ब्रह्मेन्द्र-रुद्र-नासत्य-सूर्याग्नि-वसु-मारुताम्।
विश्वेदेवान् पितृ-गणान्वयांसि मनुजान् पशून्

A person who performs śrāddha with faith pleases the whole world: Brahmā, Indra, Rudra, the Nāsatyas, the Sun, Fire, the Vasus, the Maruts, the Viśvedevas, the ancestors, birds, humans, and animals.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
ब्रह्मेन्द्र-रुद्र-नासत्य-सूर्याग्नि-वसु-मारुतान् (brahmendra-rudra-nāsatya-sūryāgni-vasu-mārutān) — Brahmā, Indra, Rudra, the Nāsatyas, the Sun, Fire, the Vasus, and the Maruts
विश्वेदेवान्-पितृ-गणान्-वयांसि-मनुजान्-पशून् (viśvedevān-pitṛ-gaṇān-vayāṃsi-manujān-paśūn) — the Viśvedevas, the hosts of ancestors, birds, humans, and animals
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

brahmendra-rudra-nāsatya-sūryāgni-vasu-mārutām|viśvedevān pitṛ-gaṇānvayāṃsi manujān paśūn

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

श्राद्धं तत्फलकालांश्च नित्यकाम्यादिभेदतः । चतुर्दशे तु कल्पांश्च तत्फलान्यन्ववर्णयत् । तत्र तावदुत्तरक्रियाणां कालान् वर्णयिष्यन् श्राद्धप्रशंसाम् आह—ब्रह्म इति

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

He describes śrāddha, its times, and its fruits according to distinctions such as obligatory and desired rites. In the fourteenth chapter he has described the procedures and their fruits. Now, while about to describe the times for the subsequent rites, he praises śrāddha, beginning with “Brahma.”

VP 3.14.2

सरीसृपान् ऋषि-गणान् यच् चान्यद् भूत-सञ्ज्ञितम्।
श्राद्धं श्रद्धान्वितः कुर्वन् प्रीणयत्य् अखिलं जगत्

Reptiles, hosts of sages, and every other being are pleased. A person who performs śrāddha with faith thereby pleases the entire world.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
सरीसृपान्-ऋषि-गणान्-यच्-च-अन्यद्-भूत-संज्ञितम् (sarīsṛpān-ṛṣi-gaṇān-yac-ca-anyad-bhūta-saṃjñitam) — reptiles, hosts of ṛṣis, and whatever else is designated a being
श्राद्धं श्रद्धान्वितः कुर्वन्-प्रीणयति अखिलं जगत् (śrāddhaṃ śraddhānvitaḥ kurvan-prīṇayati akhilaṃ jagat) — performing śrāddha with faith, he pleases the entire world
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sarīsṛpān ṛṣi-gaṇān yac cānyad bhūta-sañjñitam|śrāddhaṃ śraddhānvitaḥ kurvan prīṇayaty akhilaṃ jagat

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

No commentary.

VP 3.14.3

मासि मास्य् असिते पक्षे पञ्चदश्यां नरेश्वर।
तथाष्टकासु कुर्वीत काम्यान् कालान् शृणुष्व मे

O king, one should perform desired śrāddha every month on the fifteenth day of the dark fortnight and likewise on the Aṣṭakās. Hear from me the appropriate times.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
मासि मासि असिते पक्षे पञ्चदश्यां-नरेश्वर (māsi māsi asite pakṣe pañcadaśyāṃ-nareśvara) — each month, in the dark fortnight, on the fifteenth day, O king
तथा अष्टकासु कुर्वीत काम्यान्-कालान् शृणुष्व मे (tathā aṣṭakāsu kurvīta kāmyān-kālān śṛṇuṣva me) — and likewise on the Aṣṭakās he should perform [it]; hear from me the desired times
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

māsi māsy asite pakṣe pañcadaśyāṃ nareśvara|tathāṣṭakāsu kurvīta kāmyān kālān śṛṇuṣva me

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

प्रतिमासं कृष्णपक्षे पञ्चदश्यां दर्शश्राद्धं कुर्वीतेत्यर्थः । अष्टका आग्रहायण्यादूर्ध्वं तिस्रः कृष्णाष्टम्यस्तासु

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Śrāddha is to be performed every month on the fifteenth day of the dark fortnight. The Aṣṭakās are the three dark-fortnight eighth days beginning after Āgrahāyaṇa.

VP 3.14.4

श्राद्धार्हम् आगतं द्रव्यं विशिष्टम् अथ वा द्विजम्।
श्राद्धं कुर्वीत विज्ञाय व्यतीपातेऽयने तथा

Having recognized suitable material that has arrived, or a distinguished Brāhmaṇa who has arrived, one should perform śrāddha at Vyatīpāta and likewise at the ayanas.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
श्राद्धार्हम्-आगतं-द्रव्यं-विशिष्टम् अथ वा द्विजम् (śrāddhārham-āgataṃ-dravyaṃ-viśiṣṭam atha vā dvijam) — material suitable for śrāddha that has arrived, or a distinguished Brāhmaṇa
श्राद्धं कुर्वीत विज्ञाय व्यतीपाते अयने तथा (śrāddhaṃ kurvīta vijñāya vyatīpāte ayane tathā) — having recognized [such a circumstance], he should perform śrāddha at Vyatīpāta and likewise at an ayana
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

śrāddhārham āgataṃ dravyaṃ viśiṣṭam atha vā dvijam|śrāddhaṃ kurvīta vijñāya vyatīpāte'yane tathā

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

श्राद्धार्हं द्रव्यं विशिष्टञ्च द्विजमागतं विज्ञायेत्यन्वयः

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The syntactical connection is: having recognized suitable material for śrāddha and a distinguished Brāhmaṇa who has arrived.

VP 3.14.5

विषुवे चापि सम्प्राप्ते ग्रहणे शशि-सूर्ययोः।
समस्तेष्व् एवे भूपाल राशिष्व् अर्के च गच्छति

One should also perform it when an equinox arrives, during an eclipse of the Moon or Sun, and whenever the Sun enters any of the zodiacal signs.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
विषुवे च अपि संप्राप्ते (viṣuve ca api saṃprāpte) — when an equinox too has arrived
ग्रहणे शशि-सूर्ययोः (grahaṇe śaśi-sūryayoḥ) — at an eclipse of the Moon or Sun
समस्तेषु एव राशिषु अर्के च गच्छति (samasteṣu eva rāśiṣu arke ca gacchati) — when the Sun enters all the zodiacal signs
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

viṣuve cāpi samprāpte grahaṇe śaśi-sūryayoḥ|samasteṣv eve bhūpāla rāśiṣv arke ca gacchati

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

समस्तेषु राशिषु अर्के गच्छति सति इति सर्वसङ्क्रान्तीनां श्राद्धकालत्वस्योक्तत्वात् अयने विषुवे चेति पृथगुक्तिः फलातिरेकार्था

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Since the Sun’s entry into all the zodiacal signs has already been stated as a time for śrāddha, the separate mention of the ayanas and the equinox is intended to indicate an additional fruit.

VP 3.14.6

नक्षत्र-ग्रह-पीडासु दुष्ट-स्वप्नावलोकने।
इच्छा-श्राद्धानि कुर्वीत नव-सस्यागमे तथा

One should perform desired śrāddhas when the stars or planets are afflicted, when a bad dream is seen, and likewise when new grain arrives.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
नक्षत्र-ग्रह-पीडासु (nakṣatra-graha-pīḍāsu) — when the stars and planets are afflicted
दुष्ट-स्वप्न-अवलोकने (duṣṭa-svapna-avalokane) — when a bad dream is seen
इच्छा-श्राद्धानि कुर्वीत (icchā-śrāddhāni kurvīta) — he should perform desired śrāddhas
नव-सस्य-आगमे तथा (nava-sasya-āgame tathā) — and likewise when new grain arrives
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nakṣatra-graha-pīḍāsu duṣṭa-svapnāvalokane|icchā-śrāddhāni kurvīta nava-sasyāgame tathā

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

इच्छाश्राद्धानि काम्यानि । नवशस्यागम इति—एतच्च व्रीहियवव्यतिरिक्तविषयम्, तयोर्नित्यश्राद्धानिमित्तत्वात्

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Desired śrāddhas are optional rites. “When new grain arrives” applies to grain other than rice and barley, since those two are causes for the obligatory śrāddha.

VP 3.14.7

अमावास्या यदा मैत्र-विशाखास्व् आस्वातियोगिनी।
श्राद्धैः पितृ-गणस् तृप्तिं तथाप्नोत्य् अष्ट-वार्षिकीम्

When the new-moon day is joined with Maitra, Viśākhā, or Svāti, the ancestral hosts obtain through śrāddha satisfaction lasting eight years.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
अमावास्या यदा मैत्र-विशाखा-स्वाति-योगिनी (amāvāsyā yadā maitra-viśākhā-svāti-yoginī) — when the new-moon day is joined with Maitra, Viśākhā, or Svāti
श्राद्धैः पितृ-गणः तृप्तिं तथा आप्नोति अष्ट-वार्षिकीम् (śrāddhaiḥ pitṛ-gaṇaḥ tṛptiṃ tathā āpnoti aṣṭa-vārṣikīm) — by śrāddhas the ancestral host then obtains satisfaction lasting eight years
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

amāvāsyā yadā maitra-viśākhāsv āsvātiyoginī|śrāddhaiḥ pitṛ-gaṇas tṛptiṃ tathāpnoty aṣṭa-vārṣikīm

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

मैत्रमनुराधा

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Maitra means Anurādhā.

VP 3.14.8

अमावास्या यदा पुष्पे रौद्रे च र्क्षे पुनर् वसौ।
द्वादशाब्दं तदा तृप्तिं प्रयान्ति पितरोऽर्चिताः

When the new-moon day is in Puṣya, Raudra, or Punarvasu, the ancestors, being worshipped, attain satisfaction lasting twelve years.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
अमावास्या यदा पुष्ये रौद्रे अथ ऋक्षे पुनर्वसौ (amāvāsyā yadā puṣye raudre atha ṛkṣe punarvasau) — when the new-moon day is in Puṣya, Raudra, or the asterism Punarvasu
द्वादशाब्दं तदा तृप्तिं प्रयान्ति पितरः अर्चिताः (dvādaśābdaṃ tadā tṛptiṃ prayānti pitaraḥ arcitāḥ) — then, being worshipped, the ancestors attain satisfaction for twelve years
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

amāvāsyā yadā puṣpe raudre ca rkṣe punar vasau|dvādaśābdaṃ tadā tṛptiṃ prayānti pitaro'rcitāḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

रोद्रमार्द्रा

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Raudra means Ārdrā.

VP 3.14.9

वासवाजैक-पादर्क्षे पितॄणां तृप्तिम् इच्छताम्।
वारुणे वाप्य् अमावास्या देवानाम् अपि दुर्लभा

When the new moon is in Vāsava, Ajaikapād, or Vāruṇa, it is difficult even for the gods to obtain for those who desire the satisfaction of the ancestors.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
वासव-अजै-क-पाद्-ऋक्षे (vāsava-ajai-ka-pād-ṛkṣe) — when the asterism is Vāsava or Ajaikapād
पितृणां तृप्तिम् इच्छताम् (pitṛṇāṃ tṛptim icchatām) — for those desiring the ancestors’ satisfaction
वारुणे वा अपि अमावास्या देवानाम् अपि दुर्लभा (vāruṇe vā api amāvāsyā devānām api durlabhā) — or, when it is Vāruṇa, the new-moon day is difficult even for the gods to obtain
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vāsavājaika-pādarkṣe pitṝṇāṃ tṛptim icchatām|vāruṇe vāpy amāvāsyā devānām api durlabhā

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

वासवं ज्येष्ठा, अजैकपात् पूर्वभाद्रपदा, वारुणं शतभिषा, एतेषु नक्षत्रेष्वमावास्या पितॄणां देवतानाञ्च तृप्तिं कर्तुमिच्छतां पुंसा दुर्लभा । इच्छतेति पाठे स एवार्थः

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Vāsava means Jyeṣṭhā; Ajaikapāt means Pūrvabhādrapadā; Vāruṇa means Śatabhiṣā. A new-moon day joined with these asterisms is difficult for a person wishing to bring satisfaction to the ancestors and the deities. With the reading “icchate,” the meaning is the same.

VP 3.14.10

नवस्व् ऋक्षेष्व् अमावास्या यदैतेष्व् अवनी-पते।
तदा हि तृप्तिदं श्राद्धं पितॄणां शृणु चापरम्

When the new moon occurs in these nine asterisms, the śrāddha gives satisfaction to the ancestors. Hear also another [such occasion], O king.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
नवसु ऋक्षेषु अमावास्या यदा एतेषु अवनीपते (navasu ṛkṣeṣu amāvāsyā yadā eteṣu avanīpate) — when the new moon is in these nine asterisms, O king
तदा हि तृप्तिदं श्राद्धं पितृणां (tadā hi tṛptidaṃ śrāddhaṃ pitṛṇāṃ) — then the śrāddha is indeed satisfaction-giving for the ancestors
शृणु च अपरम् (śṛṇu ca aparam) — and hear another [case]
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

navasv ṛkṣeṣv amāvāsyā yadaiteṣv avanī-pate|tadā hi tṛptidaṃ śrāddhaṃ pitṝṇāṃ śṛṇu cāparam

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

नन्वमावस्याश्राद्धत्वान्नित्यतैव, तत् कथमेतेषु फलश्रुतिरित्यत आह—नवस्व् इति । गोदोहेनापः प्रणयेत पशुकामस्य इतिवत् नक्षत्रगुणयोगाद् गुणफलविधिरित्यर्थः । नित्यात् पृथगेतानि श्रद्धानीति वा

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Since the new-moon śrāddha is obligatory, one may ask why a statement of fruit is given for these occasions. The answer is “among the nine”: a fruit is prescribed through the quality of the asterism, as in the rule, “Let him draw water by milking for one desiring cattle”; alternatively, these śrāddhas are distinct from the obligatory one.

VP 3.14.11

गीतं सनत्कुमारेण यथैलाय महात्मने।
पृच्छते पितृ-भक्ताय प्रश्रयावनताय च

This was sung by Sanatkumāra to the great Aila, who asked him as one devoted to the ancestors and bowed in humility.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
गीतं सनत्-कुमारेण यथा ऐलाय महात्मने (gītaṃ sanat-kumāreṇa yathā ailāya mahātmane) — sung by Sanatkumāra to the great Aila
पृच्छते पितृ-भक्ताय प्रश्रय-अवनताय च (pṛcchate pitṛ-bhaktāya praśraya-avanatāya ca) — who was asking, devoted to the ancestors and bowed in humility
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

gītaṃ sanatkumāreṇa yathailāya mahātmane|pṛcchate pitṛ-bhaktāya praśrayāvanatāya ca

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

ऐलाय पुरूरवसे

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Aila means Purūravas.

VP 3.14.12

श्री-सनत्कुमार उवाच— वैशाख-मासस्य च या तृतीया नवम्य् असौ कार्तिक-शुक्ल-पक्षे।
नभस्य् अमासस्य च कृष्ण-पक्षे त्रयोदशी पञ्चदशी च माघे

The third day of Vaiśākha, the ninth day in the bright fortnight of Kārttika, and the thirteenth and fifteenth days in the dark fortnight of Nabhasya and Māgha [are the auspicious dates].

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
वैशाख-मासस्य च या तृतीया (vaiśākha-māsasya ca yā tṛtīyā) — the third day of the month Vaiśākha
नवमी असौ कार्तिक-शुक्ल-पक्षे (navamī asau kārtika-śukla-pakṣe) — this ninth day in the bright fortnight of Kārttika
नभस्य-मासस्य च कृष्ण-पक्षे (nabhasya-māsasya ca kṛṣṇa-pakṣe) — and, in the dark fortnight of the month Nabhasya
त्रयोदशी पञ्चदशी च माघे (trayodaśī pañcadaśī ca māghe) — the thirteenth and fifteenth days in Māgha
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vaiśākha-māsasya ca yā tṛtīyā navamy asau kārtika-śukla-pakṣe|nabhasy amāsasya ca kṛṣṇa-pakṣe trayodaśī pañcadaśī ca māghe

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

पञ्चदशी च माघ इत्यत्र कृष्णपक्ष इत्यनुषङ्गः

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

In the phrase “the fifteenth day in Māgha,” the dark fortnight is to be supplied.

VP 3.14.13

एता युगाद्याः कथिताः पुराणेष्व् अनन्त-पुण्यास् तिथयश् चतस्रः।
उपप्लवे चन्द्रमसो रवेश् च त्रिष्व् अष्टकास्व् अप्य् अयन-द्वये च

These four dates are called yugādis in the Purāṇas and possess limitless merit: eclipses of the Moon and Sun, the three Aṣṭakās, and the two ayanas.

Word-for-word

VP 3.14.27

तिलैः सप्ताष्टभिर् वापि समवेतं जलाञ्जलिम्।
भक्ति-नम्रः समुद्दिश्य भुव्य् अस्माकं प्रदास्यति

Or, with seven or eight sesame seeds and a handful of water combined with them, he will offer it on the ground for our sake with devotion and a bowed mind.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
तिलैः सप्त-अष्टभिः वा अपि (tilaiḥ sapta-aṣṭabhiḥ vā api) — with seven or eight sesame seeds, or even so
समवेतं जल-अञ्जलिम् (samavetaṃ jala-añjalim) — a handful of water combined with them
भक्ति-नम्रः समुद्दिश्य भुवि अस्माकम् प्रदास्यति (bhakti-namraḥ samuddiśya bhuvi asmākam pradāsyati) — with devotion and bowed mind, he will offer it on the ground for our sake
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tilaiḥ saptāṣṭabhir vāpi samavetaṃ jalāñjalim|bhakti-namraḥ samuddiśya bhuvy asmākaṃ pradāsyati

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

No commentary.

VP 3.14.28

यतः कुतश्चित् सम्प्राप्य गोभ्यो वापि गवाह्निकम्।
अभावे प्रीणयन्न् अस्मान् श्रद्धा-युक्तः प्रदास्यति

Having obtained from somewhere or other, or even for the cows a one-day portion of food, he will give it with faith, intending to please us when other means are absent.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
यतः कुतश्चित् संप्राप्य (yataḥ kutaścit saṃprāpya) — having obtained from somewhere or other
गोभ्यः वा अपि गो-आह्निकम् (gobhyaḥ vā api go-āhnikam) — or even a one-day portion of food for cows
अभावे प्रीणयन् अस्मान् श्रद्धा-युक्तः प्रदास्यति (abhāve prīṇayan asmān śraddhā-yuktaḥ pradāsyati) — in the absence of [other means], intent on pleasing us, he will give it with faith
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yataḥ kutaścit samprāpya gobhyo vāpi gavāhnikam|abhāve prīṇayann asmān śraddhā-yuktaḥ pradāsyati

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

गवाह्निकम् एकस्या गोरेकाहतृप्तिजनकं तृणादि

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Go-āhnikam means grass or the like that produces satisfaction for one cow for one day.

VP 3.14.29

सर्वाभावे वनं गत्वा कक्ष-मूल-प्रदर्शकः।
सूर्यादि-लोक-पालानाम् इदम् उच्चैर् वदिष्यति

When everything is absent, having gone to the forest and shown the roots of his armpits, he will loudly say this to the Sun and the other guardians of the worlds:

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
सर्व-अभावे वनं गत्वा (sarva-abhāve vanaṃ gatvā) — when everything is absent, having gone to the forest
कक्ष-मूल-प्रदर्शकः (kakṣa-mūla-pradarśakaḥ) — showing the roots of his armpits
सूर्य-आदि-लोक-पालानाम् इदम् उच्चैः वदिष्यति (sūrya-ādi-loka-pālānām idam uccaiḥ vadiṣyati) — he will loudly say this to the Sun and the other guardians of the worlds
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sarvābhāve vanaṃ gatvā kakṣa-mūla-pradarśakaḥ|sūryādi-loka-pālānām idam uccair vadiṣyati

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

कक्षामूलप्रदर्शकः निर्धनत्वख्यापनार्थमूर्ध्वबाहुः

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Kakṣāmūla-pradarśakaḥ means one who raises his arms to make his poverty known.

VP 3.14.30

न मेष्ति वित्तं न धनं च नान्यच् छ्राद्धोपयोग्यं स्व-पितॄन् नतोऽस्मि।
तृप्यन्तु भक्त्या पितरो मयैतौ कृतौ भुजौ वर्त्मनि मारुतस्य

“I have no wealth, no treasure, and nothing else suitable for śrāddha. I bow to my ancestors. May the ancestors be satisfied through devotion; I have placed these two arms on the path of the wind.”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
न मे अस्ति वित्तम् न धनम् च न अन्यत् श्राद्ध-उपयोग्यम् (na me asti vittam na dhanam ca na anyat śrāddha-upayogyam) — I have no wealth, no treasure, and nothing else suitable for śrāddha
स्व-पितॄन् नतः अस्मि (sva-pitṝn nataḥ asmi) — I bow to my ancestors
तृप्यन्तु भक्त्या पितरो (tṛpyantu bhaktyā pitaro) — may the ancestors be satisfied through devotion
मया एतौ कृतौ भुजौ वर्त्मनि मारुतस्य (mayā etau kṛtau bhujau vartmani mārutasya) — by me these two arms have been placed on the path of the wind
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

na meṣti vittaṃ na dhanaṃ ca nānyac chrāddhopayogyaṃ sva-pitṝn nato'smi|tṛpyantu bhaktyā pitaro mayaitau kṛtau bhujau vartmani mārutasya

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

वित्तं स्वर्णरजतादि इतरद् धनञ्च नास्ति । तत्प्रत्ययार्थं मया भुजौ मारुतस्य वर्त्मनि आकाशे क्षिप्तौ

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

There is no wealth such as gold or silver, nor any other money. To make that evident, I have raised my arms into the sky, the path of the wind.

VP 3.14.31

और्व उवाच— इत्य् एतत् पितृभिर् गीतं भावाभाव-प्रयोजनम्।
यः करोति कृतं तेन श्राद्धं भवति पार्थिव

This is what the ancestors have sung concerning the proper action when means are present and when they are absent. Whoever does it thereby performs śrāddha, O king.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
इति एतत् पितृभिः गीतम् (iti etat pitṛbhiḥ gītam) — thus this has been sung by the ancestors
भाव-अभाव-प्रयोजनम् (bhāva-abhāva-prayojanam) — the application when there is means and when there is no means
यः करोति कृतम् तेन श्राद्धं भवति पार्थिव (yaḥ karoti kṛtam tena śrāddhaṃ bhavati pārthiva) — whoever does it, by that it becomes śrāddha performed, O king
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ity etat pitṛbhir gītaṃ bhāvābhāva-prayojanam|yaḥ karoti kṛtaṃ tena śrāddhaṃ bhavati pārthiva

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

भावाभावप्रयोजनं वित्तस्य भावेऽभावे च प्रयोजनं पितृतृप्तिहेतुप्रयोगम्

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Bhāva-abhāva-prayojanam means the application concerning the presence and absence of wealth—the procedure used as a cause of satisfaction for the ancestors.
