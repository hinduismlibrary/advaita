VP 3.14.1

ब्रह्मेन्द्ररुद्रनासत्यसूर्याग्निवसुमारुताम् । विश्वेदेवान् पितृगणान्वयांसि मनुजान् पशून् ॥१॥

A person who performs śrāddha with faith pleases the whole world: Brahmā, Indra, Rudra, the Nāsatyas, the Sun, Fire, the Vasus, the Maruts, the Viśvedevas, the ancestors, birds, humans, and animals.

Śrīdhara translation

He describes śrāddha, its times, and its fruits according to distinctions such as obligatory and desired rites. In the fourteenth chapter he has described the procedures and their fruits. Now, while about to describe the times for the subsequent rites, he praises śrāddha, beginning with “Brahma.”

VP 3.14.3

मासि मास्य् असिते पक्षे पञ्चदश्यां नरेश्वर । तथाष्टकासु कुर्वीत काम्यान् कालान् शृणुष्व मे ॥३॥

O king, one should perform desired śrāddha every month on the fifteenth day of the dark fortnight and likewise on the Aṣṭakās. Hear from me the appropriate times.

Śrīdhara translation

Śrāddha is to be performed every month on the fifteenth day of the dark fortnight. The Aṣṭakās are the three dark-fortnight eighth days beginning after Āgrahāyaṇa.

VP 3.14.5

विषुवे चापि सम्प्राप्ते ग्रहणे शशिसूर्ययोः । समस्तेष्व् एवे भूपाल राशिष्व् अर्के च गच्छति ॥५॥

One should also perform it when an equinox arrives, during an eclipse of the Moon or Sun, and whenever the Sun enters any of the zodiacal signs.

Śrīdhara translation

Since the Sun’s entry into all the zodiacal signs has already been stated as a time for śrāddha, the separate mention of the ayanas and the equinox is intended to indicate an additional fruit.

VP 3.14.6

नक्षत्रग्रहपीडासु दुष्टस्वप्नावलोकने । इच्छाश्राद्धानि कुर्वीत नवसस्यागमे तथा ॥६॥

One should perform desired śrāddhas when the stars or planets are afflicted, when a bad dream is seen, and likewise when new grain arrives.

Śrīdhara translation

Desired śrāddhas are optional rites. “When new grain arrives” applies to grain other than rice and barley, since those two are causes for the obligatory śrāddha.

VP 3.14.30

न मेष्ति वित्तं न धनं च नान्यच् छ्राद्धोपयोग्यं स्वपितॄन् नतोऽस्मि । तृप्यन्तु भक्त्या पितरो मयैतौ कृतौ भुजौ वर्त्मनि मारुतस्य ॥३०॥

“I have no wealth, no treasure, and nothing else suitable for śrāddha. I bow to my ancestors. May the ancestors be satisfied through devotion; I have placed these two arms on the path of the wind.”

Śrīdhara translation

There is no wealth such as gold or silver, nor any other money. To make that evident, I have raised my arms into the sky, the path of the wind.

VP 3.14.31

इत्य् एतत् पितृभिर् गीतं भावाभावप्रयोजनम् । यः करोति कृतं तेन श्राद्धं भवति पार्थिव ॥३१॥

This is what the ancestors have sung concerning the proper action when means are present and when they are absent. Whoever does it thereby performs śrāddha, O king.

Śrīdhara translation

Bhāva-abhāva-prayojanam means the application concerning the presence and absence of wealth—the procedure used as a cause of satisfaction for the ancestors.
