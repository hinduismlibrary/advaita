VP 4.2.1

यावच्च ब्रह्मलोकात्स ककुद्मी रैवतो नाभ्येति तावत्पुण्यजनसंज्ञा राक्षसास्तामस्य पुरीं कुशस्थलीं नजघ्नुः

Sri Paraasharah - Yaavat cha brahma lokaat sa kakudmee raivato naabhyeti taavat Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4 punyajana samnjyaa raakshasaah taam asya pureem kushasthaleem nijaghnuh | Sri Paraasharar - When this Kakudmi Raivata went to Brahma loka, Kushasthalee was without a proper ruler; till he returned, the raakshasaas by name Punyajana, destroyed the city of Kushasthalee.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yāvacca brahmalokātsa kakudmī raivato nābhyeti tāvatpuṇyajanasaṃjñā rākṣasāstāmasya purīṃ kuśasthalīṃ najaghnuḥ

Śrīdhara

dhṛṣṭādimanuputrāṇāṃ vaṃśāṃstaccaritāni ca . dvitīye tat prasaṅgena saubharyākhyānamuktavān . raivatasya bhrātṛśatamadhye kintāvat ko’pi rājā nābhavadityāśaṅkyāha—yāvacca iti . puṇyajanasañjñā iti viśeṣaṇaṃ sopālambhaṃ rākṣasajāti viśeṣaparaṃ vā ||१||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

धृष्टादिमनुपुत्राणां वंशांस्तच्चरितानि च । द्वितीये तत् प्रसङ्गेन सौभर्याख्यानमुक्तवान् । रैवतस्य भ्रातृशतमध्ये किन्तावत् कोऽपि राजा नाभवदित्याशङ्क्याह—यावच्च इति । पुण्यजनसञ्ज्ञा इति विशेषणं सोपालम्भं राक्षसजाति विशेषपरं वा ॥१॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Sri Paraasharah - Yaavat cha brahma lokaat sa kakudmee raivato naabhyeti taavat Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4 punyajana samnjyaa raakshasaah taam asya pureem kushasthaleem nijaghnuh | Sri Paraasharar - When this Kakudmi Raivata went to Brahma loka, Kushasthalee was without a proper ruler; till he returned, the raakshasaas by name Punyajana, destroyed the city of Kushasthalee.

VP 4.2.10

एते क्षत्रप्रसूता वै पुनश्चांगिरसाः स्मृताः । रथीतराणां प्रवराः क्षत्त्रोपेता द्विजातयः इति

iti | Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4 There is a well known shloka with respect to Ratheetara - Though Ratheetaraas are of Kshatriya vamsha, they somehow got connected with Aangeerasa gotra, and they became called as Aangirasaas. From Kshatriya vamsha, they became Brahmins.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ete kṣatraprasūtā vai punaścāṃgirasāḥ smṛtāḥ | rathītarāṇāṃ pravarāḥ kṣattropetā dvijātayaḥ iti

Śrīdhara

ete kṣatraprasūtā iti . ete rathītarasya pravara-gotrajāḥ kṣatraprasūtāḥ kṣatriyā aprajasya rathītarasya bhāryāyāmaṅgirasā jātatvāt . tathāpi tayoryogāt punaraṅgiraso brāhmaṇāḥ smṛtāḥ, ataḥ kṣatropetā dvijātayaḥ ityanvaḥ ||१०||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

एते क्षत्रप्रसूता इति । एते रथीतरस्य प्रवर-गोत्रजाः क्षत्रप्रसूताः क्षत्रिया अप्रजस्य रथीतरस्य भार्यायामङ्गिरसा जातत्वात् । तथापि तयोर्योगात् पुनरङ्गिरसो ब्राह्मणाः स्मृताः, अतः क्षत्रोपेता द्विजातयः इत्यन्वः ॥१०॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

iti | Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4 There is a well known shloka with respect to Ratheetara - Though Ratheetaraas are of Kshatriya vamsha, they somehow got connected with Aangeerasa gotra, and they became called as Aangirasaas. From Kshatriya vamsha, they became Brahmins.

VP 4.2.15

स चेक्ष्वाकुरष्टकायामुत्पाद्य श्राद्धार्हं मांसमानयेति विकुक्षिमाज्ञापयामास

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

Śrīdhara commentary.

Sa cha ikshvaakuh ashtakaayaam utpaadya shraaddha arham maamsamaanayeti vikukshim aajnyaapayaamaasa | Ikshvaaku, once started to perform Ashtaka Shraaddha, and ordered his son Vikukshi to go and get maamsa which is fit for offering to the pitrus in that Ashtaka Shraaddha.

VP 4.2.16

शेषं च मांसमानीय पित्रे निवेदयामास

Tathaa iti gruheeta aajnyo vidhruta sharaasano vanam abhyetya anekasho mrugaan hatvaa shraanto ati kshut pareeto vikukshirekam shasham abhakshat, shesham cha maamsamaaneeya pitre nivedayaamaasa | Vikukshi said that he would do it, and accepted. Taking his bow and arrows, having gone to the forest, he killed many animals, and was extremely tired, and became very hungry; he ate one hare from the animals he had killed and collected. The remaining, he brought and gave to his father. Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4

Transliteration

VP 4.2.17

अलमनेनामेध्येनामिषेण दुरात्मनानेन तव पुत्रेणैतन्मांसमुपहतं यतोऽनेन शशो भक्षितः

Ikshvaakunaa ikshvaaku kula aachaaryo vasishthah tat prokshanaaya coditah praaha, alam anena amedhyena aamishena, duraatmanaa anena tava putrena etat maamsam upahatam yato anena shasho bhakshitah | Ikshvaaku took it to his aachaarya, Vasishtha, to make it shuddha by doing prokshana. Vasishtha tells him &quot;What is this you have brought? This is not fit for the shraaddha&quot;. Your bad son has spoilt this maamsa which you have brought for the purpose of Ashtaka Shraaddha, because he has eaten one hare out of that, and this is bhukta shesha, and is not fit to be offered in the Ashtaka Shraaddha. This is not sacred anymore, and is impure.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

alamanenāmedhyenāmiṣeṇa durātmanānena tava putreṇaitanmāṃsamupahataṃ yato'nena śaśo bhakṣitaḥ
