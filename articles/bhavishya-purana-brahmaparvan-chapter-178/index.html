Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 178 — Solar Worship

BP 1.178.1

।। अरुण उवाच ।। अबलो बालरूपेण खट्वांगशिखिवाहनः ।। पूर्वेण वदनः श्रीमांस्त्रिशिखः शक्तिसंयुतः ।। १ ।।

Aruṇa said: The child-like one, holding a skull-staff, riding a peacock, facing east, glorious, three-crested, bearing the spear.

BP 1.178.2

कृत्तिकायाश्च रुद्रस्य चांगोद्भूतः सुरार्चितः ।। कार्तिकेयो महातेजा आदित्यवरदर्पितः ।। शांतिं करोतु ते नित्यं बलं सौख्यं च तेजसा ।। २ ।।

Born from the limbs of Kṛttikā and Rudra, worshipped by the gods, the great-radiant Kārttikeya, made proud by Āditya’s boon, may he always bring you peace, strength, and happiness through his splendor.

BP 1.178.3

आत्रेयीबलवान्देव आरोग्यं च खगाधिप ।। श्वेतवस्त्रपरीधानस्त्र्यक्षः कनकसुप्रभः ।। ३ ।।

The powerful god Ātreya, health to you, O lord of birds, clad in white, three-eyed, shining like gold.

BP 1.178.4

शूलहस्तो महाप्राज्ञो नदीशो रविभावितः ।। शांतिं करोतु ते शान्तो धर्मे च मतिमुत्तमाम् ।। ४ ।।

Holding a trident, greatly wise, lord of rivers, filled with Ravi; peaceful, may he bring you peace and the highest understanding in dharma.

BP 1.178.5

धर्मेतरावुभौ नित्यमचलः संप्रयच्छतु ।। महोदरो महाकायः स्निग्धांजनसमप्रभः ।। ५ ।।

May the immovable one always grant both dharma and what is beyond dharma; great-bellied, great-bodied, shining like smooth collyrium.

BP 1.178.6

एकदंष्ट्रोत्कटो देवो गजवक्त्रो महाबलः ।। नागयज्ञोपवीतेन नानाभरणभूषितः ।।६।।

The god, one-tusked, formidable, elephant-faced, of great strength, adorned with a serpent sacred-thread and various ornaments.

BP 1.178.7

सर्वार्थसंपदोद्धारो गणाध्यक्षो वरप्रदः ।। भीमस्य तनयो देवो नायकोथ विनायकः ।। करोतु ते महाशांतिं भास्करार्चनतत्परः ।। ७ ।।

Uplifter of all prosperity and success, head of the hosts, giver of boons, the god, son of Bhīma, leader, Vināyaka, intent on worship of Bhāskara, may he bring you great peace.

BP 1.178.8

इंद्रनीलनिभस्त्र्यक्षो दीप्तशूलायुधोद्यतः ।। रक्तांबरधरः श्रीमान्कृष्णांगो नागभूषणः ।। ८ ।।

Like sapphire, three-eyed, rising with a shining trident, wearing red garments, glorious, dark-bodied, adorned with serpents.

BP 1.178.9

पापापनोदमतुलमलक्ष्यो मलनाशनः ।। करोतु ते महाशांतिं प्रीतः प्रीतेन चेतसा ।। ९ ।।

Unequalled in removing sin, unseen, destroyer of stain; pleased, may he bring you great peace with a pleased mind.

BP 1.178.10

वरांबरधरा कन्या नानालंकारभूषिता ।। त्रिदशानां च जननी पुण्या लोकनमस्कृता ।। १० ।।

The maiden wearing fine garments, adorned with many ornaments, mother of the thirty gods, meritorious, bowed to by the worlds.

BP 1.178.11

सर्वसिद्धिकरा देवी प्रसादपरमास्पदा ।। शांतिं करोतु ते माता भुवनस्य खगाधिप ।। ११ ।।

The goddess who brings all attainments, the highest abode of favor, may the mother of the world bring you peace, O lord of birds.

BP 1.178.12

स्निग्धश्यामेन वर्णेन महामहिषमर्दनी ।। धनुश्चक्रप्रहरणा खङ्गपट्टिशधारिणी ।। १२ ।।

With a smooth dark complexion, great buffalo-slayer, armed with bow and discus, bearing sword and spear.

BP 1.178.13

आतर्जन्यायतकरा सर्वोपद्रवनाशिनी ।। शांतिं करोतु ते दुर्गा भवानी च शिवा तथा ।। १३ ।।

With a raised pointing hand, destroyer of all troubles, may Durgā, Bhavānī, and likewise Śivā bring you peace.

BP 1.178.14

अतिसूक्ष्मो ह्यतिक्रोधस्त्र्यक्षो भृंगिरिटिर्महान् ।। सूर्यात्मको महावीरः सूर्यैकगतमानसः ।। सूर्यभक्तिकरो नित्यं शिवं ते संप्रयच्छतु ।। १४ ।।

Exceedingly subtle and exceedingly wrathful, three-eyed, the great Bhṛṅgiriṭi, with the Sun as his self, great hero, his mind fixed only on the Sun, ever devoted to Sun-worship, may he grant you auspiciousness.

BP 1.178.15

प्रचंडगणसैन्येशो महाघंटाक्षधारकः ।। अक्षमालार्पितकरश्चाक्षचंडेश्वरो वरः ।। १५ ।।

Lord of the army of fierce attendants, holding a great bell and an axe, a rosary in his hand, the excellent Akṣacaṇḍeśvara.

BP 1.178.16

चंडपापहरो नित्यं ब्रह्महत्या विनाशनः ।। शांतिं करोतु ते नित्यमादित्याराधने रतः ।। करोति च महायोगी कल्याणानां परंपराम् ।। 1.178.१६ ।।

Ever removing fierce sin, destroyer of the killing of a Brahmin, devoted to worship of Āditya, may he always bring you peace; the great ascetic makes a succession of auspicious things.

BP 1.178.17

आकाशमातरो दिव्यास्तथान्या देवमातरः ।। सूर्यायणपरा देव्यो जगद्व्याप्य व्यवस्थिताः ।। शांतिं कुर्वंतु मे नित्यं मातरः सुरपूजिताः ।। १७ ।।

The divine mothers of the sky, and the other mothers of the gods, goddesses devoted to the Sun’s path, established throughout the world, worshipped by the gods, may the mothers always bring me peace.

BP 1.178.18

ये रुद्रा रौद्रकर्माणो रौद्र स्थाननिवासिनः ।। मातरो रुद्ररूपाश्च गणानामधिपाश्च ये ।। १८ ।।

Those who are Rudras, of fierce deeds, dwelling in fierce places, mothers in Rudra forms, and lords of the hosts;

BP 1.178.19

विघ्नभूतास्तथा चान्ये दिग्विदिक्षु समाश्रिताः ।। सर्वे ते प्रीतमनसः प्रतिगृह्णंतु मे बलिम् ।। सिद्धिं कुर्वंतु ते नित्यं भयेभ्यः पांतु सर्वतः ।। १९ ।।

the others who have become obstacles and are stationed in the directions and intermediate directions: may they all accept my offering with pleased minds, always accomplish success, and protect from every fear.

BP 1.178.20

ऐंद्रादयो गणा ये तु वज्रहस्ता महाबलाः ।। हिमकुन्देंदुसदृशा नीलकृष्णांगलोहिताः ।। २० ।।

The hosts beginning with Indra, thunderbolts in their hands, of great strength, like snow, jasmine, and the moon, blue, black-bodied, and red;

BP 1.178.21

दिव्यांतरिक्षा भौमाश्च पातालतलवासिनः ।। ऐंद्राः शांतिं प्रकुर्वंतु भद्राणि च पुनः पुनः ।। २१ ।।

those in heaven, the atmosphere, and on earth, and those dwelling in the netherworld: may Indra’s hosts repeatedly bring peace and good things.

BP 1.178.22

आग्नेय्यां ये भृताः सर्वे ध्रुवहत्यनुषंगिणः ।। सूर्यानुरक्ता रक्ताभा जपासुमनिभास्तथा ।। २२ ।।

All those assembled in the southeast, associated with Dhruva’s slaying, devoted to the Sun, red-hued and resembling hibiscus flowers;

BP 1.178.23

विरक्तलोहिता दिव्या आग्नेय्यां भास्करादयः ।। आदित्याराधनपरा आदित्यगतमानसाः ।। २३ ।।

the divine ones, pale-red, in the southeast, beginning with Bhāskara, devoted to worship of Āditya, their minds fixed on Āditya,

BP 1.178.24

शांतिं कुर्वंतु ते नित्यं प्रयच्छंतु बलिं मम ।। भयाऽऽदित्यसमा ये तु सततं दण्डपाणयः ।। आदित्याराधनपराः कं प्रयच्छंतु ते सदा ।। २४ ।।

may they always bring peace and accept my offering. Those like fear and the Sun, continually holding staves, devoted to Sun-worship—to whom should they give?

BP 1.178.25

ऐशान्यां संस्थिता ये तु प्रशांताः शूलपाणयः ।। भस्मोद्धूलितदेहाश्च नीलकण्ठा विलोहिताः ।। २५ ।।

Those stationed in the northeast, tranquil, holding tridents, their bodies dusted with ash, blue-throated and reddish;

BP 1.178.26

दिव्यांतरिक्षा भौमाश्च पातालतलवासिनः ।। सूर्यपूजाकरा नित्यं पूजयित्वांशुमालिनम् ।। २६ ।।

those in heaven, the atmosphere, and on earth, and those dwelling in the netherworld, always performing Sun-worship, having worshipped the garland-rayed one;

BP 1.178.27

ततः सुप्रीतमनसो लोकपालैः समन्विताः ।। शांतिं कुर्वंतु मे नित्यं कं प्रयच्छंतु पूजिताः ।। २७ ।।

then, with pleased minds and joined with the guardians of the worlds, worshipped, may they always bring me peace; to whom should they give?

BP 1.178.28

अमरावती पुरी नाम पूर्वभागे व्यवस्थिता ।। विद्याधरगणाकीर्णा सिद्धगन्धर्वसेविता ।।२८।।

The city named Amarāvatī is situated in the eastern quarter, crowded with hosts of knowledge-bearing spirits, served by perfected beings and heavenly musicians.

BP 1.178.29

रत्नप्राकाररुचिरा महारत्नोपशोभिता ।। तत्र देवपतिः श्रीमान्वज्रपाणिर्महाबलः ।। गोपतिर्गोसहस्रेण शोभमानेन शोभते ।।२९।।

Beautiful with jeweled ramparts, adorned with great jewels; there the splendid lord of the gods, thunderbolt in hand and mighty, Gopati, shines with a thousand shining cows.

BP 1.178.30

ऐरावतगजारूढो गैरिकाभो महाद्युतिः ।। देवेंद्रः सततं हृष्ट आदित्याराधने रतः ।। ३० ।।

Mounted on the elephant Airāvata, ochre-colored, of great splendor, the lord of the gods is always delighted, devoted to worship of Āditya.

BP 1.178.31

सूर्यज्ञानैकपरमः सूर्यभक्तिसमन्वितः ।। सूर्यप्रणामः परमां शांतिं तेऽद्य प्रयच्छतु ।। ३१ ।।

Supremely devoted to knowledge of the Sun alone, joined with devotion to the Sun, may reverence to the Sun give you supreme peace today.

BP 1.178.32

आग्नेयदिग्विभागे तु पुरी तेजस्वती शुभा ।। नानादेवगणाकीर्णा नानारत्नोपशोभिता ।। ३२ ।।

In the division of the southeast is a radiant, auspicious city, crowded with hosts of various gods, adorned with various jewels.

BP 1.178.33

तत्र ज्वाला समाकीर्णो दीप्तांगारसमद्युतिः ।। पुरगो दहनो देवो ज्वलनः पापनाशनः ।। ३३ ।।

There, surrounded by flames, shining like blazing coals, the city-going god Dahana, Jvalana, destroyer of sin, goes about.

BP 1.178.34

आदित्याराधनरत आदित्यगतमानसः ।। शांतिं करोतु ते देवस्तथा पापपरिक्षयम् ।। ३४ ।।

Devoted to worship of Āditya, his mind fixed on Āditya, may the god bring you peace and likewise the destruction of sin.

BP 1.178.35

वैवस्वती पुरी रम्या दक्षिणेन महात्मनः ।। सुरासुरशताकीर्णा नानारत्नोपशोभिता ।।३५।।

The delightful city Vaivasvatī is south of the great-souled one, crowded with hundreds of gods and demons, adorned with various jewels.

BP 1.178.36

तत्र कुन्देंदुसंकाशो हरिपिंगललोचनः ।। महामहिषमारूढः कृष्णस्रग्वस्त्रभूषणः ।। ३६ ।।

There, like jasmine and the moon, tawny-eyed, mounted on a great buffalo, adorned with a dark garland, garments, and ornaments,

BP 1.178.37

अन्तकोऽथ महातेजाः सूर्यधर्मपरायणः ।। आदित्याराधनपरः क्षेमारोग्ये ददातु ते ।। ३७ ।।

then Antaka, of great splendor, devoted to the Sun’s dharma, intent on worship of Āditya, may he give you safety and health.

BP 1.178.38

नैर्ऋते दिग्विभागे तु पुरी कृष्णेति विश्रुता ।। मोहरक्षोगणाशौचपिशाचप्रेतसंकुला ।। ३८ ।।

In the division of the southwest is the city renowned as Kṛṣṇā, crowded with delusion, hosts of demons, impurity, flesh-eating spirits, and spirits of the dead.

BP 1.178.39

तत्र कुन्दनिभो देवो रक्तस्रग्वस्त्र भूषणः ।। खड्गपाणिर्महातेजाः करालवदनोज्ज्वलः ।। ३९ ।।

There the god, like jasmine, wearing a red garland, garments, and ornaments, sword in hand, of great splendor, shines with a terrible face.

BP 1.178.40

रक्षेंद्रो वसते नित्यमादित्याराधने रतः ।। करोतु मे सदा शांतिं धनं धान्यं प्रयच्छतु ।। ४० ।।

The lord of demons dwells there always, devoted to worship of Āditya; may he always bring me peace and give wealth and grain.

BP 1.178.41

पश्चिमे तु दिशो भागे पुरी शुद्धवती सदा ।। नानाभोगिसमाकीर्णा नानाकिन्नरसेविता ।। ४१ ।।

In the western division of the direction is the city named Śuddhavatī, always crowded with various serpents and served by various horse-headed spirits.

BP 1.178.42

तत्र कुन्देदुसंकाशो हरिपिंगल लोचनः ।। शांतिं करोतु मे प्रीतः शांतः शांतेन चेतसा ।। ४२ ।।

There, like jasmine and the moon, tawny-eyed, pleased and peaceful, may he bring me peace with a peaceful mind.

BP 1.178.43

यशोवती पुरी रम्या ऐशानीं दिशमाश्रिता ।। नानागणसमाकीर्णा नानाकृत शुभालया ।। तेजःप्राकारपर्यंता अनौपम्या सदोज्वला ।। ४३ ।।

The delightful city Yaśovatī is situated in the northeast, crowded with many hosts, with auspicious houses of various forms, surrounded by a rampart of splendor, incomparable, ever shining.

BP 1.178.44

तत्र कुंदेंदुसंकाशश्चांबुजाक्षो विभूषितः ।। त्रिनेत्रः शांतरूपात्मा अक्षमाला धराधरः ।। ईशानः परमो देवः सदा शांतिं प्रयच्छतु ।। ४४ ।।

There, adorned, like jasmine and the moon, lotus-eyed, three-eyed, peaceful in form, bearing a rosary and the earth, may the highest god Īśāna always grant peace.

BP 1.178.45

भूलोके तु भुवर्लोके निवसंति च ये सदा ।। देवादेवाः शुभायुक्ताः शांतिं कुर्वंतु ते सदा ।। ४५ ।।

Those who always dwell in the earthly and middle realms, gods and non-gods joined with auspiciousness, may they always bring peace.

BP 1.178.46

महर्लोके जनोलोके परलोके गताश्च ये ।। ते सर्वे मुदिता देवाः शांतिं कुर्वंतु ते सदा ।। ४६ ।।

Those who have gone to Maharloka, Janaloka, and the other worlds, all those pleased gods, may they always bring peace.

BP 1.178.47

सरस्वती सूर्यभक्ता शांतिदा विदधातु मे ।। चारुचामीकरस्था या सरोजकरपल्लवा ।। सूर्यभक्त्याश्रिता देवी विभूतिं ते प्रयच्छतु ।। ४७ ।।

Sarasvatī, devotee of the Sun and giver of peace, may she establish peace for me; she who is seated on beautiful gold, lotus-petaled in hand, the goddess who has taken refuge in devotion to the Sun, may she grant you prosperity.

BP 1.178.48

हारेण सुविचित्रेण भास्वत्कनकमेखला ।। अपराजिता सूर्यभक्ता करोतु विजयं तव ।। ४८ ।।

With a very beautiful garland and a shining golden girdle, Aparājitā, devotee of the Sun, may she bring you victory.
