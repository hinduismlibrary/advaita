Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 96 — The Seventh Day of Victory

BP 1.96.1

दिण्डिरुवाच। यच्चैताः सप्त सप्तम्यो भवता कथिता मम। तासां या प्रथमा देव कथिता सा सविस्तरा ॥

Diṇḍi spoke. Of these seven seventh day observances that you have told me, O Lord, you told the first in detail.

BP 1.96.2

यास्त्वन्या देवशार्दूल ताः सर्वाः कथयस्व मे। येनोपोष्य ततस्तास्तु व्रजेऽहं हेलिसद्म वै ॥

O tiger among the gods, tell me all the others also, so that after fasting according to them I may go to the abode of the Sun.

BP 1.96.3

ब्रह्मोवाच। शुक्लपक्षस्य सप्तम्यां नक्षत्रं पञ्चतारकम्। यदा स्यात्सा तदा ज्ञेया जया नामेति सप्तमी ॥

When the five star constellation is present on the seventh day of the bright fortnight, that seventh day is to be known by the name Jaya.

BP 1.96.4

तस्यां दत्तां हुतं जापस्तर्पणं देवपूजनम्। सर्वं शतगुणं प्रोक्तं पूजा चापि दिवाकरे ॥

On that day every gift, oblation, recitation, satisfaction offering, and worship of the gods is said to be a hundredfold. Worship of the Sun too.

BP 1.96.5

भास्करस्य प्रिया ह्येषा सप्तमी पापनाशिनी। धन्या यशस्या पुत्र्या च कामदा कञ्जजावहा ॥

This seventh day is indeed dear to Bhāskara and destroys sins. It bestows prosperity, fame, children, and desires, and leads to the Lotus Born.

BP 1.96.6

विधिनानेन कर्तव्या तिथिर्या मम विद्यते। तं शृणुष्व विधिं मत्तो येन कृत्वार्थमश्नुमते ॥

That lunar day of mine is to be observed by this procedure. Hear that procedure from me, by performing which one attains the aim.

BP 1.96.7

हंसे हंसमाक्ये शुक्लेयं सप्तमी पुरा। समुपोष्य च कर्तव्या विधिनानेन शङ्कर ॥

Formerly this seventh day was called Haṃsa in the bright fortnight. It is to be performed with complete fasting by this procedure, O Śaṅkara.

BP 1.96.8

पारणा तृतीयाऽहे स्यात्कथितं गोवृषावहम्। प्रथमं चतुरो मासान्पारणं कथितं बुधैः ॥

The fast breaking should be on the third day, said to bring cows and bulls. The wise have declared the first fast breaking for four months.

BP 1.96.9

कथितान्यत्र पुष्पाणि करवीरस्य सुव्रत। चन्दनं च तथा रक्तं धूपार्थं गुग्गुलं परम् ॥

O steadfast one, oleander flowers are prescribed here, with red sandalwood, and excellent guggul resin for incense.

BP 1.96.10

कासारं तु सुपक्वं च नैवेद्यं भास्कराय वै। अनेन विधिना पूज्य मार्तण्डं विबुधाधिपम् ॥

A thoroughly cooked dish is the offering to Bhāskara. One should worship Mārtaṇḍa, lord of the gods, by this procedure.

BP 1.96.11

पूजयेद्ब्राह्मणान्भीम भक्ष्यभोज्यैर्यथाविधि। कासारं भोजयेद्विप्रान्पारणेऽस्मिन्विचक्षणः। स्वयमेव तथाश्नीयात्प्रयतो मौनमाश्रितः ॥

One should honor the Brahmins with foods and dishes according to the rule, O Bhīma. At this fast breaking, the discerning one should feed the Brahmins the cooked dish and eat it oneself, self controlled and observing silence.

BP 1.96.12

पञ्चम्यामेकभक्तं तु षष्ठ्यां नक्तं प्रवर्तते। कृत्वोपवासं सप्तम्यामष्टम्यां पारणं भवेत् ॥

One meal is prescribed on the fifth day. The observance proceeds at night on the sixth. After fasting on the seventh, fast breaking should be on the eighth.

BP 1.96.13

षष्ठ्या समेता कर्तव्या नाष्टम्येयं कदाचन। यस्योपवासनायैव षष्ठ्यामाहुरुपोषितम् ॥

This is to be observed together with the sixth day, never with the eighth. They say that one fasts on the sixth for this observance.

BP 1.96.14

यथैकादश्यां कुर्वन्ति उपवासं मनीषिणः। उपवासनाय द्वादश्यां तथेयं परिकीर्तिता ॥

Just as the wise fast on Ekādaśī, so this observance is proclaimed for fasting on Dvādaśī.

BP 1.96.15

सिद्धार्थकैः स्नानमन्त्रः प्राशनं गोमयस्य तु। भानुर्मे प्रीयतामत्र दन्तकाष्ठं तथार्कजम् ॥

The bathing mantra is with mustard seeds. One should taste cow dung and say, may Bhānu be pleased with me here. The tooth stick should likewise be from the milkweed plant.

BP 1.96.16

इत्येष कथितस्तात प्रथमे पारणे विधिः। द्वितीयं श्रूयतां भीम पारणं गदतो मम ॥

Thus, dear one, the procedure for the first fast breaking has been told. Hear from me the second fast breaking, O Bhīma.

BP 1.96.17

मालतीकुसुमानीह श्रीखण्डं चन्दनं तथा। नैवेद्यं पायसं भानोर्धूपं विजयमादिशेत् ॥

Jasmine flowers and sandalwood paste are here. Milk pudding is the offering to Bhānu, and Victory should be prescribed as incense.

BP 1.96.18

ब्राह्मणान्भोजयेद्वापि तथाश्नीयात्स्वयं विभो। रविर्मे प्रीयतामत्र नाम देवस्य कीर्तयेत् ॥

One should feed the Brahmins and eat oneself as well, O Lord. One should recite the deity name, may Ravi be pleased with me here.

BP 1.96.19

प्राशयेत्पञ्चगव्यं तु खदिरं दन्तधावने। द्वितीये पारणे वीर विधिरुक्तो मयाधुना ॥

One should partake of the five cow products and use catechu for cleaning the teeth. O hero, this is the procedure for the second fast breaking that I have now declared.

BP 1.96.20

तृतीयं पारणं चापि कथ्यमानं निबोध मे। अगस्तिकुसुमैरत्र भास्करं पूजयेद्बुधः ॥

Understand from me the third fast breaking as it is being described. Here the wise person should worship Bhāskara with agati flowers.

BP 1.96.21

समालम्भनमत्रोक्तं श्रीखण्डं कुसुमं तथा। सिह्लको धूप उद्दिष्टो भानोः प्रीतिकरः परः ॥

Anointing is prescribed here with sandal paste and flowers. Olibanum is prescribed as incense, highly pleasing to Bhānu.

BP 1.96.22

शाल्योदनं तु नैवेद्यं रसालोपरिसंयुतम्। ब्राह्मणानां तु दातव्यं भक्षयेत तथात्मना ॥

The offering should be cooked fine rice accompanied by sauce. It should be given to the Brahmins, and one should eat it oneself.

BP 1.96.23

कुशोदकप्राशनं तु बदर्या दन्तधावनम्। विकर्तनः प्रीयतां मे नाम देवस्य कीर्तयेत् ॥

One should partake of water with kusha grass and clean the teeth with a jujube twig. One should recite the deity name, may Vikartana be pleased with me.

BP 1.96.24

वर्षासु देवदेवस्य पूजा कार्या विधानतः। गन्धपुष्पोपहारैस्तु नानाप्रेक्षणकैस्तथा ॥

During the rainy season, worship of the God of gods is to be performed according to the rule, with offerings of fragrance and flowers and with various spectacles.

BP 1.96.25

गोदानैर्भूमिदानैर्वा ब्राह्मणानां च तर्पणैः। इत्थं सम्पूज्य देवेशं देवस्य पुरतः स्थितः ॥

With gifts of cows or land, and with offerings that satisfy the Brahmins, having thus worshipped the Lord of the gods, one should stand before the deity.

BP 1.96.26

कारयेत्परमं पुण्यं धन्यं पुस्तकवाचनम्। वस्त्रैर्गन्धैस्तथा धूपैर्वाचकं पूजयेत्ततः ॥

One should arrange a highly meritorious and blessed reading of a book. Then one should honor the reciter with garments, fragrance, and incense.

BP 1.96.27

देवस्य पुरतः स्थित्वा ततो मन्त्रमुदीरयेत्। देवदेव जगन्नाथ सर्वरोगार्तिनाशन। ग्रहेश लोकनयन विकर्तन तमोऽपह ॥

Standing before the deity, one should then utter the mantra. O God of gods, Lord of the universe, destroyer of all disease and pain, lord of the planets, eye of the world, Vikartana, dispeller of darkness.

BP 1.96.28

कृतेयं देवदेवेश जया नामेति सप्तमी। मया तव प्रसादेन धन्या पापहरा शिवा ॥

This seventh day called Jaya has been performed by me, O Lord of gods, through your grace. It is blessed, sin removing, and auspicious.

BP 1.96.29

अनेन विधिना वीर यः कुर्यात्सप्तमीमिमाम्। तस्य स्नानादिकं सर्वं भवेच्छतगुणं विभो ॥

O hero, whoever performs this seventh day observance by this procedure has everything beginning with bathing become a hundredfold, O Lord.

BP 1.96.30

कृत्वेमां सप्तमीं वीर पुरुषः प्राप्नुयाद्यशः। धनं धान्यं सुवर्णं च पुत्रमार्यबलं श्रियम् ॥

Having performed this seventh day observance, O hero, a person obtains fame, wealth, grain, gold, a son, noble strength, and prosperity.

BP 1.96.31

प्राप्येह देवशार्दुल सूर्यलोकं स गच्छति। तस्मादेत्य पुनर्भूमौ राजराजो भवेद्बुधः ॥

Obtaining these here, O tiger among the gods, he goes to the world of the Sun. Coming from there again to earth, the wise one becomes a king of kings.

BP 1.96.32

इत्येषा कथिता वीर जया नामेति सप्तमी। कृता स्मृता श्रुता सा तु हेलिलोकप्रदायिनी ॥

Thus, O hero, this seventh day called Jaya has been described. Performed, remembered, or heard, it bestows the world of the Sun.
