Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 45 — The Account of Kārttikeya

BP 1.45.1

ब्रह्मोवाच
इदं श्रुणु मयाख्यातं तर्कपूर्वमिदं वचः ।
युष्माकं संशये जाते कृते वै जातिकर्मणोः ॥ ॥

Brahmā said, Hear this statement explained by me with reasoning, since doubt has arisen among you about birth and action.

BP 1.45.2

पुनर्वच्मि निबोधध्वं समासान्न तु विस्तरात् ।
संसिद्धिं यान्ति मनुजा जातिकर्मसमुच्चयात् ॥ ॥

I shall speak again, understand it in brief and not at length. Human beings attain complete success through the combination of birth and action.

BP 1.45.3

सिद्धिं गच्छेद्यथा कार्यं दैवकर्मसमुच्चयात् ।
एवं संसिद्धिमायाति पुरुषो जातिकर्मणोः ॥ ॥

As an undertaking reaches success through the combination of destiny and action, so a man attains complete success through birth and action.

BP 1.45.4

इत्येवमुक्तवान्पूर्वं शिष्याणां बोधने पुरा ।
योगीश्वरो महातेजाः समासान्न तु विस्तरात् ॥ ॥

Thus, long ago, the greatly radiant Lord of Yogis spoke this way while instructing his disciples, briefly and not at length.

BP 1.45.5

सुमन्तुरुवाच
इति पृष्टः पुरा ब्रह्मा ऋषीन्प्रोवाच भारत ।
सवितर्कमिदं वाक्यं विप्रर्षे जातिकर्मणोः ॥ ॥

Sumantu said, Being asked in this way long ago, Brahmā spoke this reasoned statement about birth and action to the sages, O Bharata, O Brahmin seer.

BP 1.45.6

तस्मात्त्वया महाबाहो न कार्यो विस्मयो नृप ।
कार्तिकेयं प्रति सदा देवानां दुर्विदा गतिः ॥ ॥

Therefore, mighty armed king, you should not be astonished. The course of the gods concerning Kārttikeya is always difficult to understand.
