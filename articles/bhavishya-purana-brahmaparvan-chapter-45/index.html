Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 45 — The Account of Kārttikeya

BP 1.45.1

ब्रह्मोवाच
इदं श्रुणु मयाख्यातं तर्कपूर्वमिदं वचः ।
युष्माकं संशये जाते कृते वै जातिकर्मणोः ॥ ॥

Brahmā said: Hear this reasoned statement as explained by me, since doubt has arisen among you concerning birth and action.

Word-for-word
ब्रह्मा उवाच (brahmā uvāca) — Brahmā said; इदम् शृणु (idam śṛṇu) — hear this; मया आख्यातम् (mayā ākhyātam) — explained by me; तर्कपूर्वम् वचः (tarka-pūrvam vacaḥ) — a reasoned statement; युष्माकम् संशये जाते (yuṣmākam saṃśaye jāte) — when doubt has arisen among you; जातिकर्मणोः (jāti-karmaṇoḥ) — concerning birth and action
Transliteration
brahmovāca
idaṃ śruṇu mayākhyātaṃ tarkapūrvamidaṃ vacaḥ |
yuṣmākaṃ saṃśaye jāte kṛte vai jātikarmaṇoḥ || 1 ||

BP 1.45.2

पुनर्वच्मि निबोधध्वं समासान्न तु विस्तरात् ।
संसिद्धिं यान्ति मनुजा जातिकर्मसमुच्चयात् ॥ ॥

I shall state it again; understand it in summary rather than at length. Human beings attain complete success through the conjunction of birth and action.

Word-for-word
पुनः वच्मि (punaḥ vacmi) — I state again; निबोधध्वम् (nibodhadhvam) — understand; समासात् (samāsāt) — in summary; न तु विस्तरात् (na tu vistarāt) — not at length; संसिद्धिम् यान्ति (saṃsiddhim yānti) — attain complete success; मनुजाः (manujāḥ) — human beings; जातिकर्मसमुच्चयात् (jāti-karma-samuccayāt) — through the conjunction of birth and action
Transliteration
punarvacmi nibodhadhvaṃ samāsānna tu vistarāt |
saṃsiddhiṃ yānti manujā jātikarmasamuccayāt || 2 ||

BP 1.45.3

सिद्धिं गच्छेद्यथा कार्यं दैवकर्मसमुच्चयात् ।
एवं संसिद्धिमायाति पुरुषो जातिकर्मणोः ॥ ॥

Just as an undertaking reaches fulfilment through the conjunction of destiny and action, so a person attains complete success through birth and action.

Word-for-word
सिद्धिम् गच्छेत् (siddhim gacchet) — should reach fulfilment; यथा कार्यम् (yathā kāryam) — just as an undertaking; दैवकर्मसमुच्चयात् (daiva-karma-samuccayāt) — through the conjunction of destiny and action; एवम् (evam) — so; संसिद्धिम् आयाति (saṃsiddhim āyāti) — attains complete success; पुरुषः (puruṣaḥ) — a person; जातिकर्मणोः (jāti-karmaṇoḥ) — through birth and action
Transliteration
siddhiṃ gacchedyathā kāryaṃ daivakarmasamuccayāt |
evaṃ saṃsiddhimāyāti puruṣo jātikarmaṇoḥ || 3 ||

BP 1.45.4

इत्येवमुक्तवान्पूर्वं शिष्याणां बोधने पुरा ।
योगीश्वरो महातेजाः समासान्न तु विस्तरात् ॥ ॥

Thus, long ago, the greatly radiant lord of yogins spoke in this way when instructing his disciples—briefly and not at length.

Word-for-word
इति एवम् उक्तवान् (iti evam uktavān) — thus spoke in this way; पूर्वम् पुरा (pūrvam purā) — formerly, long ago; शिष्याणाम् बोधने (śiṣyāṇām bodhane) — in instructing the disciples; योगीश्वरः (yogīśvaraḥ) — lord of yogins; महातेजाः (mahātejāḥ) — greatly radiant; समासात् (samāsāt) — briefly; न तु विस्तरात् (na tu vistarāt) — not at length
Transliteration
ityevamuktavānpūrvaṃ śiṣyāṇāṃ bodhane purā |
yogīśvaro mahātejāḥ samāsānna tu vistarāt || 4 ||

BP 1.45.5

सुमन्तुरुवाच
इति पृष्टः पुरा ब्रह्मा ऋषीन्प्रोवाच भारत ।
सवितर्कमिदं वाक्यं विप्रर्षे जातिकर्मणोः ॥ ॥

Sumantu said: Questioned in this way long ago, Brahmā spoke this reasoned statement about birth and action to the sages, O descendant of Bharata, O brahmin seer.

Word-for-word
सुमन्तुः उवाच (sumantuḥ uvāca) — Sumantu said; इति पृष्टः (iti pṛṣṭaḥ) — questioned in this way; पुरा (purā) — long ago; ब्रह्मा ऋषीन् प्रोवाच (brahmā ṛṣīn provāca) — Brahmā spoke to the sages; भारत (bhārata) — O descendant of Bharata; सवितर्कम् इदम् वाक्यम् (sa-vitarkam idam vākyam) — this reasoned statement; विप्रर्षे (viprarṣe) — O brahmin seer; जातिकर्मणोः (jāti-karmaṇoḥ) — concerning birth and action
Transliteration
sumanturuvāca
iti pṛṣṭaḥ purā brahmā ṛṣīnprovāca bhārata |
savitarkamidaṃ vākyaṃ viprarṣe jātikarmaṇoḥ || 5 ||

BP 1.45.6

तस्मात्त्वया महाबाहो न कार्यो विस्मयो नृप ।
कार्तिकेयं प्रति सदा देवानां दुर्विदा गतिः ॥ ॥

Therefore, mighty-armed king, you should not be astonished. The course of the gods concerning Kārttikeya is always difficult to comprehend.

Word-for-word
तस्मात् त्वया (tasmāt tvayā) — therefore by you; महाबाहो (mahābāho) — O mighty-armed one; न कार्यः विस्मयः (na kāryaḥ vismayaḥ) — astonishment should not be entertained; नृप (nṛpa) — O king; कार्तिकेयम् प्रति (kārtikeyam prati) — concerning Kārttikeya; सदा (sadā) — always; देवानाम् गतिः (devānām gatiḥ) — the course of the gods; दुर्विदा (durvidā) — difficult to comprehend
Transliteration
tasmāttvayā mahābāho na kāryo vismayo nṛpa |
kārtikeyaṃ prati sadā devānāṃ durvidā gatiḥ || 6 ||
