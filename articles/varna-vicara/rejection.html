Rejection of Jāti

Rejection of Jāti as a universal prerequisite of Varṇa

In order to refute the PurvaPaksha position we must make use of the maxim:

One should accept a guru’s statement when it is reasonable or established by scripture; one should not accept it merely because it was spoken by a guru.”

Let us begin with Scriptural Pramanas against Jati as a universal prerequisite of Varna.

Mlecchas and Śūdras becoming Brāhmaṇa like Viśvāmitra

Bhavisya Purana 1.16.56-58

Show Sanskrit
विश्वामित्रस्तु राजेन्द्र ब्राह्मणत्वजिगीषया । तपश्चचार विपुलं सन्तापाय दिवौकसाम् । । ब्राह्मणत्वं न लेभेऽसौ लेभे विघ्नाननेकशः । । ५६
ततस्तु नियमात्तेषां तिथीनां प्रवरा तिथिः । उपोषिता बहुविधा ज्ञात्वा ब्रह्मप्रियां तिथिम् । । ५७
ततो ददौ ब्रह्मा विश्वामित्राय धीमते । इहैव तेन देहेन ब्राह्मणत्वं सुदुर्लभम् । । ५८
तिथीनां प्रवरा ह्येषा तिथीनामुत्तमा तिथिः । क्षत्रियो वैश्यशूद्रौ वा ब्राह्मणत्वमवाप्नुयुः । । ५९

‘O king of kings, Viśvāmitra, desiring to attain brāhmaṇa status, performed vast austerity that caused distress among the heavenly beings; yet he did not obtain that status and encountered many obstacles.’

‘Then, learning which date was dear to Brahmā, he observed in many ways the foremost of lunar dates according to its discipline and fasted upon it.’

‘Thereupon Brahmā granted the wise Viśvāmitra the exceedingly difficult attainment of brāhmaṇa status here in this very body.’

This is indeed the foremost and highest of lunar dates. The source says that through it a kṣatriya, vaiśya, or śūdra may attain brāhmaṇa status.’

Bhavisya Purana 1.16.61

Show Sanskrit
हैहयैस्तालजङ्घैश्च तुरुष्कैर्यवनैः शकैः । उपोषिता इहात्रैव ब्राह्मणत्वमभीप्सुभिः १ । । ६१

‘The source says that Haihayas, Tālajaṅghas, Turks, Yavanas, and Śakas who desired brāhmaṇa status also fasted upon it here.’

Remark 4

Vishvamitra was not born a Brahmin or Brahmin-Jati. In some stories he does a long period of time worth of Tapas to attain brahmin-hood. But this is not so in this story. A simple fast lasting 1 year was what gave him his brahmin-hood. A basic understanding of the vishvamitra story tells us that he became a true brahmin due to the attainment of ritual rights etc. The purana states that Mlecchas and Shudras can also do this fast to attain brahmin-hood. We should understand it to be in the same sense as Vishvamitra.

Rejection of Jāti-distinctions in every sense

We shall now examine statements from the Bhavisya Purana , Brahma Parva which explicitly states that in every sense and every regard there exists no such distinction of Jatis based on birth or family/lineage. We may also find two verses that can be interpreted as calling the PurvaPakshin position as illogical and not grounded by reason and shashtras.

Let us now read the Pramanas:

जीवोऽपि ब्राह्मणः प्रोक्तो यैरतत्त्वज्ञमानवैः । प्रभ्रष्टब्राह्मणत्वास्ते जायन्ते विप्रसङ्गतः । । २२

‘Just as experts clearly distinguish a horse that enters a herd of cattle, so—since all share human nature—a twice-born person is not perceptually distinct from a group of śūdras.’ ‘There is no further distinction beyond the human species that accompanies every person. Consecratory rites and distinctive actions are what provide the basis for differentiating the twice-born from śūdras.’ Some people ignorant of reality have even called the individual soul a brāhmaṇa; through association with such brāhmaṇas they themselves become fallen from brāhmaṇa status.’
