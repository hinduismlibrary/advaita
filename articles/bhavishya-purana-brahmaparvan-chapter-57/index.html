Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 57 — The Solar Chariot Offerings and Ten-Day Worship

BP 1.57.1

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
क्षीरं यवागूर्ब्रह्मणे स्यात्परमान्नं त्रिलोचन ।
फलानि कार्तिकेयस्य दद्याद्भूतेशप्रीतये ।
विवस्वते मधु मांसं तथा मद्यं च सुव्रत ॥ १ ॥

Brahmā spoke. Milk, barley gruel, and excellent food should be given to Brahmā, O three eyed one. Fruits should be given to Kārttikeya to please the lord of beings. Honey, meat, and wine should also be given to Vivasvat, O steadfast one.

BP 1.57.2

पुरुहूताय भक्ष्याणि सानुगाय निवेदयेत् ।
हविरन्नमग्नये स्यादग्रान्नं विष्णवे तथा ॥ २ ॥

Foods should be offered to Indra together with his attendants. Sacrificial food should be offered to Agni, and choice food to Viṣṇu.

BP 1.57.3

राक्षसेभ्यः समैरेयं दद्यान्मांसौदनं हर ।
संस्कृतं पिशितान्नं च रेवताय नivedयेत् ॥ ३ ॥

Liquor should be given to the demons. Rice cooked with meat should be given to Hara, O Hara, and a prepared meat dish should be offered to Revata.

BP 1.57.4

तिलान्नं पितृराजाय दद्यात्त्रिपुरसूदन ।
आश्विनाभ्यामपूपांस्तु वसुभ्यो मांसमोदनम् ॥ ४ ॥

Sesame rice should be given to Yama, king of the ancestors, O destroyer of Tripura. Cakes should be given to the Aśvins, and rice with meat to the Vasus.

BP 1.57.5

पितृभ्यः पायसं दद्याद्घृताक्तं मधुना सह ।
कात्यायन्यै यवागूं च श्रियै दद्यात्तथा दधि ॥ ५ ॥

Milk pudding mixed with clarified butter and honey should be offered to the ancestors, barley gruel to Kātyāyanī, and curd to the goddess of prosperity.

BP 1.57.6

सरस्वत्यै त्रिमधुरं वरुणायेषुरसौदनम् ।
खाडवान्नं धनपतावेवं मित्रे त्रिलोचन ॥ ६ ॥

Three sweet ingredients should be given to Sarasvatī, rice cooked in sugarcane juice to Varuṇa, a cooked rice dish to the lord of wealth, and likewise to Mitra, O three eyed one.

BP 1.57.7

सस्नेहेन तु तक्रेण मरुद्भ्यस्तर्पणं स्मृतम् ।
मांसान्नभक्तसूपांश्च मातृभ्यो वै निवेदयेत् ॥ ७ ॥

A libation of buttermilk enriched with clarified butter is prescribed for the wind gods. One should offer meat, rice, and soup to the Mothers.

BP 1.57.8

उल्लेपिकाश्च भूतेभ्यो जलं सूर्याय वै हर ।
दद्याद्गणाधिपतये मोदकांस्त्रिपुरान्तक ॥ ८ ॥

One should offer rice gruel to the spirits, water to the Sun, O Hara, and sweet dumplings to the lord of hosts, O destroyer of Tripura.

BP 1.57.9

शष्कुल्यस्तु नैर्ऋताय देयाः स्युर्गणनायक ।
सर्वभक्ष्याणि विश्वेभ्यो दातव्यानि समन्ततः ॥ ९ ॥

Fried cakes should be given to Nirṛti, O leader of hosts. All kinds of food should be given on every side to all the gods.

BP 1.57.10

क्षीरौदनमृषिभ्यस्तु क्षीरं नागेभ्य एव हि ।
सूर्यरथाय बलिं दद्यात्कुर्याद्वै सार्वभौतिकम् ॥ १० ॥

Milk rice should be offered to the sages and milk alone to the serpent deities. One should give an offering to the solar chariot and make a universal offering.

BP 1.57.11

उद्वर्तनं सुरा मांसं तद्वाहेभ्यश्च भारत ।
आज्यं च ब्रह्मणे दद्यात्त्र्यम्बकाय तिलांस्तथा ॥ ११ ॥

Massage powder, liquor, and meat should be given to its horses, O Bharata. Clarified butter should be given to Brahmā and sesame to the three eyed one.

BP 1.57.12

स्वाहातनये वै लाजा दातव्यास्त्रिपुरान्तक ।
भास्कराय सदा दद्यात्कोविदारं त्रिलोचन ॥ १२ ॥

Puffed rice should be given to the son of Svāhā, O destroyer of Tripura. The Sun should always receive orchid tree blossoms, O three eyed one.

BP 1.57.13

राजवृक्षं तथेन्द्राय हविष्यं पावकाय च ।
चक्रिणे सप्तधान्यं च गरुडे मत्स्यमोदनम् ॥ १३ ॥

The golden shower tree should be offered to Indra and sacrificial food to Fire. Seven grains should be offered to Viṣṇu, and rice with fish to Garuḍa.

BP 1.57.14

यक्षेभ्यो विविधान्नानि निर्यासं रेवते त्यजेत् ।
वैकंकतस्रजो रुद्र यमाय परिकीर्तिताः ॥ १४ ॥

Various foods should be given to the guardian spirits, and resin to Revata. Garlands of Indian plum flowers are prescribed for Yama, O Rudra.

BP 1.57.15

देयं स्यात्कर्णिकारं त्वश्विभ्यां वृषभध्वज ।
श्रियै पद्मानि देयानि चण्डिकाय सुचन्दनम् ॥ १५ ॥

Dinner plate tree flowers should be given to the Aśvins, O bull banner. Lotuses should be given to the goddess of prosperity, and fine sandalwood to Caṇḍikā.

BP 1.57.16

नवनीतं सरस्वत्यै विनतायै तथामिषम् ।
पुष्पाण्यप्सरसां रुद्र मालत्याः परिकीर्त्तितम् ॥ १६ ॥

Fresh butter should be given to Sarasvatī and meat to Vinatā. Jasmine flowers are prescribed for the celestial nymphs, O Rudra.

BP 1.57.17

वरुणायाग्निमन्थं तु फलं मूलं निरृतये ।
बिल्वं दद्यात्कुबेराय कपित्थं मरुतां तथा ॥ १७ ॥

The fire drill tree should be given to Varuṇa, fruit and roots to Nirṛti. Bael should be given to Kubera and wood apple to the wind gods.

BP 1.57.18

गन्धर्वेभ्यस्त्वारग्वधं दद्यात्त्रिपुरसूदन ।
वासवेभ्यस्तु कर्पूरं दद्याद्दारु गणाधिपे ॥ १८ ॥

The golden shower tree should be given to the heavenly musicians, O destroyer of Tripura. Camphor should be given to the Vasus, and wood to the lord of hosts.

BP 1.57.19

पितृभ्यः पिण्डमूलानि भूतेभ्यश्च बिभीतकम् ।
गोभ्यो यवान्प्रदद्याद्वै मातृभ्यश्चाक्षतांन्हर ॥ १९ ॥

Root vegetables formed into balls should be given to the ancestors, the beleric fruit to the spirits, barley to the cows, and unbroken rice to the Mothers, O Hara.

BP 1.57.20

गुग्गुलं विघ्नपतये विश्वेभ्यो देयमोदनम् ।
ऋषिभ्यो ब्रह्मवृक्षं तु नागेभ्यो विषमुत्तमम् ॥ २० ॥

Fragrant gum resin should be given to the lord of obstacles, rice to all the gods, the sacred tree to the sages, and the best poison to the serpent deities.

BP 1.57.21

भास्करस्येह देयानि सकलानि गणाधिप ।
मधुसर्पिस्तथोक्तानि गैरिकस्य त्रिलोचन ॥ २१ ॥

All of these should be offered to the Sun here, O lord of hosts. Honey, clarified butter, and red ochre are also prescribed, O three eyed one.

BP 1.57.22

न्यग्रोधं तस्य वाहेभ्यो भक्त्या रुद्र निवेदयेत् ।
सायं प्रातस्तु मध्याह्ने सदैकाग्रमना हर ॥ २२ ॥

One should offer the banyan tree to his horses with devotion, O Rudra. In the evening, morning, and at midday, one should always remain attentive, O Hara.

BP 1.57.23

सर्वेषां शक्तितो भक्त्या दहेद्धूपं विचक्षणः ।
मन्त्रतो देवशार्दूल यो यस्येह प्रकीर्तितः ॥ २३ ॥

The wise one should burn incense for each according to his ability and with devotion, using the sacred formula stated for each, O tiger among gods.

BP 1.57.24

शान्त्यर्थं ब्राह्मणेभ्यस्तु तिलान्दद्याद्विचक्षणः ।
वैश्वानरे वा जुहुयाद्घृतेन सहितान्हर ॥ २४ ॥

For peace, the wise one should give sesame to the Brahmins, or offer it with clarified butter into the Fire, O Hara.

BP 1.57.25

देवानाममृतं ह्येते पितॄणां हि स्वधामृतम् ।
शरणं ब्राह्मणानां च सदा ह्येतान्विदुर्बुधाः ॥ २५ ॥

The wise always know that these are nectar for the gods, the sacred offering for the ancestors, and a refuge for the Brahmins.

BP 1.57.26

कस्यपस्याङ्गजा ह्येते पवित्राश्च तथा हर ।
स्नाने दाने तथा होमे तर्पणेह्यशने पराः ॥ २६ ॥

These are born from the limbs of Kaśyapa and are pure, O Hara. They are foremost in bathing, gifts, fire offerings, libations, and eating.

BP 1.57.27

इत्थं देवान्ग्रहांश्चैव पूजयित्वा प्रयत्नतः ।
अवतार्य रथाच्चैनं मण्डले स्थापयेत्पुनः ॥ २७ ॥

Having carefully worshiped the gods and planets in this way, one should bring the god down from the chariot and place him again on the ritual circle.

BP 1.57.28

कृत्वा त्वारार्तिकं यत्नाद्दीपतोययवाक्षतैः ।
कार्पासबीजलवणतुषैर्दुर्दृष्टिशान्तये ॥ २८ ॥

One should carefully wave the lamps, water, barley, and unbroken rice, and use cotton seed, salt, and husks to calm the evil eye.

BP 1.57.29

वेदीमारोपयेत्पश्चात्पत्नीभ्यां सह सुव्रत ।
तत्रस्थं पूजयेद्देवं दिनानि दश सुव्रत ॥ २९ ॥

Then, O steadfast one, he should place him on an altar with his two wives. He should worship the god there for ten days, O steadfast one.

BP 1.57.30

दशाहिकेति विख्याता या पूजा भूतले हर ।
तया सम्पूजयेद्देवं चतुर्थेऽह्नि तथा हर ॥ ३० ॥

This worship is known on earth as the ten day rite, O Hara. With it, one should worship the god also on the fourth day, O Hara.

BP 1.57.31

चतुर्थेऽहनि कर्तव्यं यत्नाद्धि स्नपनं रवेः ।
अभ्यङ्गभोजनाद्यैस्तु पूजासत्कारमण्डलैः ॥ ३१ ॥

On the fourth day, the bathing of the Sun should be carefully performed, together with anointing, food, and the other acts of worship and honor.

BP 1.57.32

अनेन विधिना पूज्य दशाहानि दिवाकरम् ।
ततो नयेत्परं स्थानं यत्तत्पूर्वमथालयम् ॥ ३२ ॥

Having worshiped the Sun for ten days by this procedure, one should then lead him to the highest place, his former temple.
