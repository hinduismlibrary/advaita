Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 65 — Saptamī Observance and the Monthly Forms of Sūrya

BP 1.65.1

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
अतः परं प्रवक्ष्यामि रहस्यां नाम सप्तमीम् ।
सप्तमी कृतमात्रेयं नरांस्तारयते भवात् ॥ ॥

Brahmā said: From this point I shall speak of the secret Saptamī by name. When this Saptamī is merely performed, it carries people across becoming.

Word-for-word
brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said; ataḥ param — from this point onward; pravakṣyāmi — I shall speak; rahasyām — secret; nāma — by name; saptamīm — Saptamī; kṛta-mātrā iyam — this merely performed; narān — people; tārayate — carries across; bhavāt — from becoming
Transliteration
brahmovāca |
ataḥ paraṃ pravakṣyāmi rahasyāṃ nāma saptamīm |
saptamī kṛtamātreyaṃ narāṃs tārayate bhavāt || 1 ||

BP 1.65.2

सप्तापरान् सप्त पूर्वान् पितॄंश्चापि न संशयः ।
रोगांश्छिनत्ति दुश्छेद्यान् दुर्जयाञ्जयते ह्यरीन् ॥ ॥

It carries across seven descendants and seven ancestors, and also the fathers—of this there is no doubt. It cuts away difficult diseases and conquers enemies who are hard to defeat.

Word-for-word
sapta aparān — seven descendants; sapta pūrvān — seven ancestors; pitṝn ca api — and the fathers; na saṃśayaḥ — there is no doubt; rogān — diseases; chinatti — cuts away; duḥ-chedyān — difficult to cut away; durjayān arīn — hard-to-conquer enemies; jayate — conquers
Transliteration
saptāparān sapta pūrvān pitṝṃś cāpi na saṃśayaḥ |
rogāṃś chinatti duśchedyān durjayāñ jayate hy arīn || 2 ||

BP 1.65.3

अर्थान् प्राप्नोति दुष्प्रापान् यः कुर्यान्नाम सप्तमीम् ।
कन्यार्थी लभते कन्यां धनार्थी लभते धनम् ॥ ॥

Whoever performs this Saptamī obtains hard-to-obtain wealth. One who desires a daughter obtains a daughter; one who desires wealth obtains wealth.

Word-for-word
arthān — wealth; prāpnoti — obtains; duṣprāpān — difficult to obtain; yaḥ — whoever; kuryāt — performs; nāma saptamīm — the Saptamī by name; kanyā-arthī — one desiring a daughter; labhate — obtains; kanyām — daughter; dhana-arthī — one desiring wealth; dhanam — wealth
Transliteration
arthān prāpnoti duṣprāpān yaḥ kuryān nāma saptamīm |
kanyārthī labhate kanyāṃ dhanārthī labhate dhanam || 3 ||

BP 1.65.4

पुत्रार्थी लभते पुत्रान् धर्मार्थी धर्ममाप्नुयात् ।
समयान् पालयन् सर्वान् कुर्याच्चेमां विचक्षणः ॥ ॥

One who desires sons obtains sons; one who desires dharma attains dharma. The discerning person should perform this while observing all the rules.

Word-for-word
putra-arthī — one desiring sons; labhate — obtains; putrān — sons; dharma-arthī — one desiring dharma; dharmam āpnuyāt — attains dharma; samayān — rules; pālayan — observing; sarvān — all; kuryāt — should perform; imām — this; vicakṣaṇaḥ — discerning
Transliteration
putrārthī labhate putrān dharmārthī dharmam āpnuyāt |
samayān pālayan sarvān kuryāc cemāṃ vicakṣaṇaḥ || 4 ||

BP 1.65.5

समयाञ्छृणु भूतेश श्रेयसे गदतो मम ।
आदित्यभक्तः पुरुषः सप्तम्यां गणनायक ॥ ॥

Hear the rules I speak for your welfare, O lord of beings. On Saptamī, a person devoted to Āditya, O lord of the hosts—

Word-for-word
samayān — rules; śṛṇu — hear; bhūta-īśa — O lord of beings; śreyase — for welfare; gadato mama — from me speaking; āditya-bhaktaḥ — devoted to Āditya; puruṣaḥ — a person; saptamyām — on Saptamī; gaṇa-nāyaka — O lord of the hosts
Transliteration
samayāñ śṛṇu bhūteśa śreyase gadato mama |
ādityabhaktaḥ puruṣaḥ saptamyāṃ gaṇanāyaka || 5 ||

BP 1.65.6

मैत्रीं सर्वत्र वै कुर्याद् भास्करं वापि चिन्तयेत् ।
सप्तम्यां न स्पृशेत्तैलं नीलं वस्त्रं न धारयेत् ।
न चाप्यामालकैः स्नानं न कुर्यात्कलहं क्वचित् ॥ ॥

should show friendliness everywhere and think of Bhāskara. On Saptamī he should not touch oil, wear blue clothing, bathe with āmalaka fruits, or engage in quarrel anywhere.

Word-for-word
maitrīm — friendliness; sarvatra — everywhere; kuryāt — should make/show; bhāskaram cintayet — should think of Bhāskara; saptamyām — on Saptamī; na spṛśet — should not touch; tailam — oil; nīlam vastram — blue cloth; na dhārayet — should not wear; na snānam — no bathing; āmalakaiḥ — with āmalakas; na kuryāt kalaham — should not make quarrel; kvacit — anywhere
Transliteration
maitrīṃ sarvatra vai kuryād bhāskaraṃ vāpi cintayet |
saptamyāṃ na spṛśet tailaṃ nīlaṃ vastraṃ na dhārayet |
na cāpy āmalakaiḥ snānaṃ na kuryāt kalahaṃ kvacit || 6 ||

BP 1.65.7

दिण्डिरुवाच ।
किमर्थं न स्पृशेत्तैलं सप्तम्यां पद्मसम्भव ॥ ॥

Diṇḍi said: Why should one not touch oil on Saptamī, O Lotus-born?

Word-for-word
diṇḍiḥ uvāca — Diṇḍi said; kimartham — for what reason; na spṛśet — should not touch; tailam — oil; saptamyām — on Saptamī; padma-saṃbhava — O Lotus-born
Transliteration
diṇḍir uvāca |
kimarthaṃ na spṛśet tailaṃ saptamyāṃ padmasaṃbhava || 7 ||

BP 1.65.8

कश्च दोषो भवेद्देव नीलवस्त्रस्य धारणात् ।
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
शृणु दिण्डे महाबाहो नीलवस्त्रस्य धारणे ॥ ॥

And what fault arises, O god, from wearing blue clothing? Brahmā said: Hear, O mighty-armed Diṇḍi, the fault in wearing blue clothing.

Word-for-word
kaḥ ca doṣaḥ — and what fault; bhavet — may arise; deva — O god; nīla-vastrasya dhāraṇāt — from wearing blue clothing; brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said; śṛṇu — hear; diṇḍe — O Diṇḍi; mahā-bāho — O mighty-armed; nīla-vastrasya dhāraṇe — in the wearing of blue clothing
Transliteration
kaś ca doṣo bhaved deva nīlavastrasya dhāraṇāt |
brahmovāca |
śṛṇu diṇḍe mahābāho nīlavastrasya dhāraṇe || 8 ||

BP 1.65.9

दूषणं गणशार्दूल गदतो मम कृत्स्नशः ।
पालनं विक्रयश्चैव सद्वृत्तिरुपजीवनम् ॥ ॥

O tiger among the hosts, hear from me fully its defilement: maintaining it, selling it, and making ones livelihood through such conduct.

Word-for-word
dūṣaṇam — defilement/fault; gaṇa-śārdūla — O tiger among the hosts; gadataḥ mama — from me speaking; kṛtsnaśaḥ — completely; pālanam — maintaining; vikrayaḥ — selling; ca eva — and indeed; sad-vṛttiḥ upajīvanam — living by such conduct
Transliteration
dūṣaṇaṃ gaṇaśārdūla gadato mama kṛtsnaśaḥ |
pālanaṃ vikrayaś caiva sadvṛttir upajīvanam || 9 ||

BP 1.65.10

पतितस्तु भवेद्विप्रस्त्रिभिः कृच्छ्रैर्विशुद्ध्यति ।
नीलीरक्तेन वस्त्रेण यत्कर्म कुरुते द्विजः ॥ ॥

A Brāhmaṇa becomes fallen and is purified by three Kṛcchra rites for whatever act he performs while wearing an indigo-red garment.

Word-for-word
patitaḥ bhavet — becomes fallen; vipraḥ — a Brāhmaṇa; tribhiḥ kṛcchraiḥ — by three Kṛcchra rites; viśuddhyati — is purified; nīlī-raktena vastreṇa — with an indigo-red garment; yat karma — whatever act; kurute — performs; dvijaḥ — twice-born
Transliteration
patitas tu bhaved vipras tribhiḥ kṛcchrair viśuddhyati |
nīlīraktena vastreṇa yat karma kurute dvijaḥ || 10 ||

BP 1.65.11

स्नानं दानं जपो होमः स्वाध्यायः पितृतर्पणम् ।
वृथा तस्य महायज्ञा नीलसूत्रस्य धारणात् ॥ ॥

Bathing, giving, recitation, offering into fire, Vedic study, and satisfaction of the ancestors—all his great sacrifices are fruitless from wearing a blue thread.

Word-for-word
snānam — bathing; dānam — giving; japaḥ — recitation; homaḥ — fire offering; svādhyāyaḥ — Vedic study; pitṛ-tarpaṇam — satisfaction of ancestors; vṛthā — fruitless; tasya — his; mahā-yajñāḥ — great sacrifices; nīla-sūtrasya dhāraṇāt — from wearing a blue thread
Transliteration
snānaṃ dānaṃ japo homaḥ svādhyāyaḥ pitṛtarpaṇam |
vṛthā tasya mahāyajñā nīlasūtrasya dhāraṇāt || 11 ||

BP 1.65.12

नीलीरक्तं यदा वस्त्रं विप्रस्त्वङ्गेषु धारयेत् ।
अहोरात्रोषितो भूत्वा पञ्चगव्येन शुद्ध्यति ॥ ॥

When a Brāhmaṇa wears an indigo-red garment on his limbs, he fasts for a day and night and is purified with Pañcagavya.

Word-for-word
yadā — when; nīlī-raktam vastram — indigo-red garment; vipraḥ — Brāhmaṇa; tvaṅgeṣu — on the limbs; dhārayet — wears; ahorātra-uṣitaḥ bhūtvā — having fasted a day and night; pañca-gavyena — with Pañcagavya; śuddhyati — is purified
Transliteration
nīlīraktaṃ yadā vastraṃ vipras tvaṅgeṣu dhārayet |
ahorātroṣito bhūtvā pañcagavyena śuddhyati || 12 ||

BP 1.65.13

रोमकूपे यदा गच्छेद्रक्तं नीलस्य कस्यचित् ।
पतितस्तु भवेद्विप्रस्त्रिभिः कृच्छ्रैर्विशुद्ध्यति ॥ ॥

When the dye of some blue substance enters the pores of the skin, the Brāhmaṇa becomes fallen and is purified by three Kṛcchra rites.

Word-for-word
romakūpe — into a pore; yadā — when; gacchet — goes; raktam — dye; nīlasya kasyacit — of some blue substance; patitaḥ bhavet — becomes fallen; vipraḥ — Brāhmaṇa; tribhiḥ kṛcchraiḥ — by three Kṛcchra rites; viśuddhyati — is purified
Transliteration
romakūpe yadā gacched raktaṃ nīlasya kasyacit |
patitas tu bhaved vipras tribhiḥ kṛcchrair viśuddhyati || 13 ||

BP 1.65.14

नीलीमध्यं यदा गच्छेत्प्रमादाद्ब्राह्मणः क्वचित् ।
अहोरात्रोषितो भूत्वा पञ्चगव्येन शुद्ध्यति ॥ ॥

When a Brāhmaṇa accidentally enters the midst of indigo, he fasts for a day and night and is purified with Pañcagavya.

Transliteration

ahorātroṣito bhūtvā pañcagavyena śuddhyati || 14 ||

BP 1.65.15

नीलीदारु यदा भिन्द्याद्ब्राह्मणानां शरीरके ।
शोणितं दृश्यते यत्र द्विजश्चान्द्रायणं चरेत् ॥ ॥

When an indigo-wood splinter pierces a Brāhmaṇas body and blood is seen there, the twice-born should perform a Cāndrāyaṇa.

Word-for-word
nīlī-dāru — indigo wood; yadā — when; bhindyāt — pierces; brāhmaṇānām śarīrake — a Brāhmaṇa’s body; śoṇitam — blood; dṛśyate — is seen; yatra — where; dvijaḥ — twice-born; cāndrāyaṇam caret — should perform Cāndrāyaṇa
Transliteration

śoṇitaṃ dṛśyate yatra dvijaś cāndrāyaṇaṃ caret || 15 ||

BP 1.65.16

कुर्यादज्ञानतो यस्तु नीलेर्वा दन्तधावनम् ।
कृत्वा कृच्छ्रद्वयं दिण्डे विशुद्धः स्यान्न संशयः ॥ ॥

Whoever unknowingly cleans his teeth with indigo should perform two Kṛcchra rites and will be purified, O Diṇḍi—of this there is no doubt.

Transliteration
kuryād ajñānato yas tu nīler vā dantadhāvanam |
kṛtvā kṛcchradvayaṃ diṇḍe viśuddhaḥ syān na saṃśayaḥ || 16 ||

BP 1.65.17

वापयेद्यत्र नीलीं तु भवेत् तत्राशुचिर्मही ।
प्रमाणद्वादशाब्दानि तत ऊर्ध्वं शुचिर्भवेत् ॥ ॥

Where indigo is planted, the earth there is impure for a period of twelve years; after that it becomes pure.

Word-for-word

Transliteration

pramāṇadvādaśābdāni tata ūrdhvaṃ śucir bhavet || 17 ||

BP 1.65.18

सप्तम्यां स्पृशतस्तैलमिष्टा भार्या विनश्यति ।

BP 1.65.19

न चैव खादेन्मांसानि मद्यानि न पिबेद्बुधः ।

नावभाषेत चाण्डालं स्त्रियं नैव रजस्वलाम् ।

आकृषेन्न शिरोयूका न वृथा वादमाचरेत् ।

रथ्यायामटनं वापि यत्नतः परिवर्जयेत् ।

BP 1.65.26

चैत्रात्प्रभृति कर्तव्या सर्वदा नाम सप्तमी ।
धातेति चैत्रमासे तु पूजनीयो दिवाकरः ॥ ॥

Beginning with Caitra, the Saptamī by this name is to be performed always. In the month of Caitra, Divākara should be worshiped as Dhātā.

Word-for-word
caitrāt prabhṛti — beginning with Caitra; kartavyā — to be performed; sarvadā — always; nāma saptamī — the Saptamī by name; dhātā iti — as Dhātā; caitra-māse — in Caitra month; pūjanīyaḥ — to be worshiped; divākaraḥ — Divākara
Transliteration
caitrāt prabhṛti kartavyā sarvadā nāma saptamī |
dhāteti caitramāse tu pūjanīyo divākaraḥ || 26 ||

BP 1.65.27

अर्यमेति च वैशाखे ज्येष्ठे मित्रः प्रकीर्तितः ।
आषाढे वरुणो ज्ञेय इन्द्रो नभसि कथ्यते ॥ ॥

In Vaiśākha he is called Aryaman; in Jyeṣṭha, Mitra; in Āṣāḍha, he is known as Varuṇa; and in Nabhas he is called Indra.

Word-for-word
aryaman iti — as Aryaman; vaiśākhe — in Vaiśākha; jyeṣṭhe — in Jyeṣṭha; mitraḥ — Mitra; prakīrtitaḥ — proclaimed; āṣāḍhe — in Āṣāḍha; varuṇaḥ — Varuṇa; jñeyaḥ — to be known; indraḥ — Indra; nabhasi — in Nabhas; kathyate — is called
Transliteration
aryameti ca vaiśākhe jyeṣṭhe mitraḥ prakīrtitaḥ |
āṣāḍhe varuṇo jñeya indro nabhasi kathyate || 27 ||

BP 1.65.28

विवस्वांश्च नभस्येऽथ पर्जन्योऽश्वयुजि स्मृतः ।
पूषा कार्त्तिकमासे तु मार्गशीर्षेषु रुच्यते ॥ ॥

In Nabhasya he is Vivasvān; in Āśvayuja he is remembered as Parjanya; in Kārttika he is Pūṣā; and in Mārgaśīrṣa he is spoken of by the [corresponding] name.

Word-for-word
vivasvān — Vivasvān; nabhasye — in Nabhasya; atha — then; parjanyaḥ — Parjanya; aśvayuji — in Āśvayuja; smṛtaḥ — remembered; pūṣā — Pūṣā; kārttika-māse — in Kārttika month; tu — and; mārgaśīrṣeṣu — in Mārgaśīrṣa; rucyate — is spoken of/named
Transliteration
vivasvāṃś ca nabhasye ’tha parjanyo ’śvayuji smṛtaḥ |
pūṣā kārttikamāse tu mārgaśīrṣeṣu rucyate || 28 ||

BP 1.65.29

भगः पौषे भवेत्पूज्यस्त्वष्टा माघे तु शस्यते ।
विष्णुश्च फाल्गुने मासि पूज्यो वन्द्यश्च भास्करः ॥ ॥

In Pauṣa he is Bhaga and should be worshiped; in Māgha he is praised as Tvaṣṭṛ; in Phālguna he is Viṣṇu, and Bhāskara is to be worshiped and honored.

Word-for-word
bhagaḥ — Bhaga; pauṣe — in Pauṣa; bhavet pūjyaḥ — should be worshiped; tvaṣṭā — Tvaṣṭṛ; māghe — in Māgha; śasyate — is praised; viṣṇuḥ — Viṣṇu; phālgune māsi — in Phālguna month; pūjyaḥ — worshipful; vandyaḥ — venerable; bhāskaraḥ — Bhāskara
Transliteration
bhagaḥ pauṣe bhavet pūjyas tvaṣṭā māghe tu śasyate |
viṣṇuś ca phālgune māsi pūjyo vandyaś ca bhāskaraḥ || 29 ||

BP 1.65.30

सप्तम्यां चैव सप्तम्यां भोजयेद्भोजकान् बुधः ।
सघृतं भोजनं देयं भोजयित्वा विधानतः ॥ ॥

On every Saptamī the wise person should feed the Bhojakas. After feeding them according to the rule, food with ghee should be given.

Word-for-word
saptamyām ca eva — on every Saptamī; bhojayet — should feed; bhojakān — the Bhojakas; budhaḥ — the wise; sa-ghṛtam bhojanam — food with ghee; deyam — should be given; bhojayitvā — having fed; vidhānataḥ — according to the rule
Transliteration
saptamyāṃ caiva saptamyāṃ bhojayed bhojakān budhaḥ |
saghṛtaṃ bhojanaṃ deyaṃ bhojayitvā vidhānataḥ || 30 ||

BP 1.65.31

भोजकायैव विप्राय दक्षिणां स्वर्णमाषकम् ।
सघृतं भोजनं देयं रक्तवस्त्राणि चैव हि ॥ ॥

To the Bhojaka Brāhmaṇa a gold māṣaka should be given as dakṣiṇā; food with ghee and red garments should also be given.

Word-for-word
bhojakāya eva viprāya — to the Bhojaka Brāhmaṇa; dakṣiṇām — gift; svarṇa-māṣakam — a gold māṣaka; sa-ghṛtam bhojanam — food with ghee; deyam — should be given; rakta-vastrāṇi — red garments; ca eva hi — and indeed
Transliteration
bhojakāyaiva viprāya dakṣiṇāṃ svarṇamāṣakam |
saghṛtaṃ bhojanaṃ deyaṃ raktavastrāṇi caiva hi || 31 ||

BP 1.65.32

अभावे भोजकानां तु दक्षिणीया द्विजोत्तमाः ।
तथैव भोजनीयाश्च श्रद्धया परया विभो ॥ ॥

If Bhojakas are unavailable, excellent Brāhmaṇas should receive the gift and should likewise be fed, with supreme faith, O all-pervading one.

Word-for-word

Transliteration
abhāve bhojakānāṃ tu dakṣiṇīyā dvijottamāḥ |
tathaiva bhojanīyāś ca śraddhayā parayā vibho || 32 ||

BP 1.65.33

विशेषतो वाचकश्च ब्राह्मणः कल्पवित्सदा ।
इत्येषा कथिता तुभ्यं सप्तमी गणनायक ॥ ॥

Especially, the Brāhmaṇa reciter who always knows the Kalpa should be honored. Thus this Saptamī has been explained to you, O lord of the hosts.

Word-for-word

Transliteration
viśeṣato vācakaś ca brāhmaṇaḥ kalpavit sadā |
ity eṣā kathitā tubhyaṃ saptamī gaṇanāyaka || 33 ||

BP 1.65.34

श्रुता सती पापहरा सूर्यलोकप्रदायिनी ॥ ॥

When heard, it is truly the remover of sin and the giver of the world of the Sun.

Word-for-word
śrutā — heard; satī — being true; pāpa-harā — remover of sin; sūrya-loka-pradāyinī — giver of the world of the Sun
Transliteration
śrutā satī pāpaharā sūryalokapradāyinī || 34 ||
