Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 65 — The Seventh Day Observance and the Monthly Forms of the Sun

BP 1.65.1

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
अतः परं प्रवक्ष्यामि रहस्यां नाम सप्तमीम् ।
सप्तमी कृतमात्रेयं नरांस्तारयते भवात् ॥ १ ॥

Brahmā said. From now on I shall explain the secret observance called the seventh day. When this observance is performed, it carries people across existence.

BP 1.65.2

सप्तापरान् सप्त पूर्वान् पितॄंश्चापि न संशयः ।
रोगांश्छिनत्ति दुश्छेद्यान् दुर्जयाञ्जयते ह्यरीन् ॥ २ ॥

Without doubt, it carries seven descendants, seven ancestors, and also the forefathers. It cuts through difficult diseases and defeats enemies who are hard to conquer.

BP 1.65.3

अर्थान् प्राप्नोति दुष्प्रापान् यः कुर्यान्नाम सप्तमीम् ।
कन्यार्थी लभते कन्यां धनार्थी लभते धनम् ॥ ३ ॥

Whoever performs the seventh day observance obtains things difficult to obtain. One who desires a daughter obtains a daughter, and one who desires wealth obtains wealth.

BP 1.65.4

पुत्रार्थी लभते पुत्रान् धर्मार्थी धर्ममाप्नुयात् ।
समयान् पालयन् सर्वान् कुर्याच्चेमां विचक्षणः ॥ ४ ॥

One who desires sons obtains sons, and one who desires righteousness attains righteousness. The discerning person should perform this while observing all the appointed times.

BP 1.65.5

समयाञ्छृणु भूतेश श्रेयसे गदतो मम ।
आदित्यभक्तः पुरुषः सप्तम्यां गणनायक ॥ ५ ॥

Hear the rules I tell for your welfare, O Lord of beings. The person devoted to the Sun should observe these on the seventh day, O leader of the hosts.

BP 1.65.6

मैत्रीं सर्वत्र वै कुर्याद् भास्करं वापि चिन्तयेत् ।
सप्तम्यां न स्पृशेत्तैलं नीलं वस्त्रं न धारयेत् ।
न चाप्यामालकैः स्नानं न कुर्यात्कलहं क्वचित् ॥ ६ ॥

He should show friendliness everywhere and think of the Sun. On the seventh day he should not touch oil, wear a blue garment, bathe with Indian gooseberries, or engage in quarrel at any time.

BP 1.65.7

दिण्डिरुवाच ।
किमर्थं न स्पृशेत्तैलं सप्तम्यां पद्मसम्भव ॥ ७ ॥

Diṇḍi asked the reason one should not touch oil on the seventh day, O Lotus born.

BP 1.65.8

कश्च दोषो भवेद्देव नीलवस्त्रस्य धारणात् ।
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
शृणु दिण्डे महाबाहो नीलवस्त्रस्य धारणे ॥ ८ ॥

He asked what fault arises, O god, from wearing blue clothing. Brahmā said. Hear the whole fault of wearing blue clothing, O mighty armed Diṇḍi.

BP 1.65.9

दूषणं गणशार्दूल गदतो मम कृत्स्नशः ।
पालनं विक्रयश्चैव सद्वृत्तिरुपजीवनम् ॥ ९ ॥

Listen while I tell every fault, O tiger among the hosts. Keeping indigo, selling it, and earning a livelihood from it.

BP 1.65.10

पतितस्तु भवेद्विप्रस्त्रिभिः कृच्छ्रैर्विशुद्ध्यति ।
नीलीरक्तेन वस्त्रेण यत्कर्म कुरुते द्विजः ॥ १० ॥

A Brahmin who performs a deed while wearing a garment dyed with indigo becomes fallen, and is purified by three difficult penances.

BP 1.65.11

स्नानं दानं जपो होमः स्वाध्यायः पितृतर्पणम् ।
वृथा तस्य महायज्ञा नीलसूत्रस्य धारणात् ॥ ११ ॥

Bathing, giving, recitation, fire offerings, Vedic study, and offerings to the ancestors, all his great rites are fruitless from wearing a blue thread.

BP 1.65.12

नीलीरक्तं यदा वस्त्रं विप्रस्त्वङ्गेषु धारयेत् ।
अहोरात्रोषितो भूत्वा पञ्चगव्येन शुद्ध्यति ॥ १२ ॥

When a Brahmin wears an indigo dyed garment on his body, he is purified with a mixture of five cow products after fasting for a day and a night.

BP 1.65.13

रोमकूपे यदा गच्छेद्रक्तं नीलस्य कस्यचित् ।
पतितस्तु भवेद्विप्रस्त्रिभिः कृच्छ्रैर्विशुद्ध्यति ॥ १३ ॥

When blue dye of any kind enters the pores of the body, the Brahmin becomes fallen and is purified by three difficult penances.

BP 1.65.14

नीलीमध्यं यदा गच्छेत्प्रमादाद्ब्राह्मणः क्वचित् ।
अहोरात्रोषितो भूत्वा पञ्चगव्येन शुद्ध्यति ॥ १४ ॥

When a Brahmin accidentally enters the indigo field, he is purified with a mixture of five cow products after fasting for a day and a night.

BP 1.65.15

नीलीदारु यदा भिन्द्याद्ब्राह्मणानां शरीरके ।
शोणितं दृश्यते यत्र द्विजश्चान्द्रायणं चरेत् ॥ १५ ॥

When a piece of indigo wood pierces the body of a Brahmin and blood appears, the twice born should perform a penance of the lunar cycle.

BP 1.65.16

कुर्यादज्ञानतो यस्तु नीलेर्वा दन्तधावनम् ।
कृत्वा कृच्छ्रद्वयं दिण्डे विशुद्धः स्यान्न संशयः ॥ १६ ॥

Whoever unknowingly cleans the teeth with indigo, O Diṇḍi, is purified after performing two difficult penances, of this there is no doubt.

BP 1.65.17

वापयेद्यत्र नीलीं तु भवेत् तत्राशुचिर्मही ।
प्रमाणद्वादशाब्दानि तत ऊर्ध्वं शुचिर्भवेत् ॥ १७ ॥

Wherever indigo is planted, the earth there is impure for twelve years. After that, it becomes pure.

BP 1.65.18

सप्तम्यां स्पृशतस्तैलमिष्टा भार्या विनश्यति ।
इत्येष नीलीतैलस्य दोषस्ते कथितो मया ॥ १८ ॥

The beloved wife of one who touches oil on the seventh day perishes. Thus I have told you the fault of indigo oil.

BP 1.65.19

न चैव खादेन्मांसानि मद्यानि न पिबेद्बुधः ।
न द्रोहं कस्यचित्कुर्यान्न पारुष्यं समाचरेत् ॥ १९ ॥

The wise person should eat no meat, drink no liquor, harm no one, and speak no harsh words.

BP 1.65.20

नावभाषेत चाण्डालं स्त्रियं नैव रजस्वलाम् ।
न वापि संस्पृशेद्धीनं मृतकं नावलोकयेत् ॥ २० ॥

He should not speak to a Caṇḍāla or to a menstruating woman. He should not touch a person of low status or look at a corpse.

BP 1.65.21

नास्फोटयेन्नातिहसेद्गायेच्चापि न गीतकम् ।
न नृत्येदतिरागेण न च वाद्यानि वादयेत् ॥ २१ ॥

He should not make a snapping sound, laugh excessively, or sing a song. He should not dance with excessive passion or play instruments.

BP 1.65.22

न शयीत स्त्रिया सार्धं न सेवेत दुरोदरम् ।
न रुद्यादश्रुपातेन न च वाच्यं च शौकिकम् ॥ २२ ॥

He should not lie down with a woman or play dice. He should not weep with falling tears or speak of worldly affairs.

BP 1.65.23

आकृषेन्न शिरोयूका न वृथा वादमाचरेत् ।
परस्यानिष्टकथनमतिशोकं च वर्जयेत् ॥ २३ ॥

He should not pull out head lice, engage in vain argument, speak of another persons misfortune, or indulge in excessive grief.

BP 1.65.24

न कञ्चित्ताडयेज्जन्तुं न कुर्यादतिभोजनम् ।
न चैव हि दिवा स्वप्नं दम्भं शाठ्यं च वर्जयेत् ॥ २४ ॥

He should strike no creature, should not overeat, should not sleep by day, and should avoid hypocrisy and deceit.

BP 1.65.25

रथ्यायामटनं वापि यत्नतः परिवर्जयेत् ।
अथापरो विधिश्चात्र श्रूयतां त्रिपुरान्तक ॥ २५ ॥

He should carefully avoid wandering in the streets. Now hear another rule in this matter, O destroyer of Tripura.

BP 1.65.26

चैत्रात्प्रभृति कर्तव्या सर्वदा नाम सप्तमी ।
धातेति चैत्रमासे तु पूजनीयो दिवाकरः ॥ २६ ॥

From Caitra onward, this seventh day observance should always be performed. In Caitra, the Sun should be worshiped as Dhātā.

BP 1.65.27

अर्यमेति च वैशाखे ज्येष्ठे मित्रः प्रकीर्तितः ।
आषाढे वरुणो ज्ञेय इन्द्रो नभसि कथ्यते ॥ २७ ॥

In Vaiśākha he is called Aryaman, and in Jyeṣṭha he is declared to be Mitra. In Āṣāḍha he is to be known as Varuṇa, and in Nabhas he is called Indra.

BP 1.65.28

विवस्वांश्च नभस्येऽथ पर्जन्योऽश्वयुजि स्मृतः ।
पूषा कार्त्तिकमासे तु मार्गशीर्षेषु रुच्यते ॥ २८ ॥

In Nabhasya he is Vivasvān, and in Āśvayuja he is remembered as Parjanya. In Kārttika he is Pūṣan, and in Mārgaśīrṣa he is called Iṣa.

BP 1.65.29

भगः पौषे भवेत्पूज्यस्त्वष्टा माघे तु शस्यते ।
विष्णुश्च फाल्गुने मासि पूज्यो वन्द्यश्च भास्करः ॥ २९ ॥

In Pauṣa, Bhaga should be worshiped, and in Māgha, Tvaṣṭṛ is praised. In Phālguna, Viṣṇu and the venerable Sun are to be worshiped.

BP 1.65.30

सप्तम्यां चैव सप्तम्यां भोजयेद्भोजकान् बुधः ।
सघृतं भोजनं देयं भोजयित्वा विधानतः ॥ ३० ॥

On every seventh day, the wise person should feed the Sun-priests. After feeding them according to the rule, food with ghee should be given.

BP 1.65.31

भोजकायैव विप्राय दक्षिणां स्वर्णमाषकम् ।
सघृतं भोजनं देयं रक्तवस्त्राणि चैव हि ॥ ३१ ॥

To the Sun-priest Brahmin, a gold coin of one māṣaka should be given as a gift. Food with ghee and red garments should also be given.

BP 1.65.32

अभावे भोजकानां तु दक्षिणीया द्विजोत्तमाः ।
तथैव भोजनीयाश्च श्रद्धया परया विभो ॥ ३२ ॥

If no Sun-priests are available, the best of the twice born should receive the gift and also be fed, with supreme faith, O Lord.

BP 1.65.33

विशेषतो वाचकश्च ब्राह्मणः कल्पवित्सदा ।
इत्येषा कथिता तुभ्यं सप्तमी गणनायक ॥ ३३ ॥

Especially, the Brahmin reciter who always knows the ritual instructions should be honored. Thus this seventh day observance has been explained to you, O leader of the hosts.

BP 1.65.34

श्रुता सती पापहरा सूर्यलोकप्रदायिनी ॥ ३४ ॥

When heard, it removes sin and grants the realm of the Sun.
