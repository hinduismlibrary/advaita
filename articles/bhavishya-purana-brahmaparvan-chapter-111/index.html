Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 111 — The Seventh Day Anointing

BP 1.111.1

ब्रह्मोवाच। श्रावणे मासि देवाग्र्यं सप्तम्यां सप्तवाहनम्। शुक्लपक्षे समभ्यर्च्य पुष्पधूपादिभिः शुचिः ॥

Brahmā said. In the month of Śrāvaṇa, on the seventh day of the bright fortnight, having worshipped the foremost of the gods, the Seven Horsed One, with flowers, incense, and the like, the pure person,

BP 1.111.2

पाखण्डादिभिरालापमकुर्वन्नियतात्मवान्। विप्राय दक्षिणां दत्त्वा नक्तं भुञ्जीत वाग्यतः ॥

Not conversing with heretics and the like, self controlled, having given a gift to a Brahmin, should eat at night, restrained in speech.

BP 1.111.3

अभ्यङ्गं देवदेवस्य वर्षे वर्षे नियोजयेत्। सप्तम्यामन्नमेवाग्र्यं शुभं शुक्लं नवं तथा ॥

Year after year, he should perform the anointing of the God of gods. On the seventh day, the food should be excellent, auspicious, pure, and fresh.

BP 1.111.4

विभवेषु तथान्येषु वादित्राण्येव वै विदुः। तथा देवस्य मासेऽस्मिन्नभ्यङ्गः परिगीयते ॥

When there are other means, musical instruments too are employed. In this month, the anointing of the god is likewise praised.

BP 1.111.5

यस्तु चाराधयेद्भक्त्या भास्करस्य नरोऽच्युत। अभ्यङ्गं विधिवच्छक्त्या कृत्वा ब्राह्मणभोजनम् ॥

Whoever worships Bhāskara with devotion, O Acyuta, having performed the anointing according to the rule and as able, and having fed Brahmins,

BP 1.111.6

शङ्खतूर्यनिनादैश्च ब्रह्मघोषैश्च पुष्कलैः। स दिव्यं यानमारूढो लोकमायाति हेलिनः ॥

He mounts a divine vehicle amid abundant sounds of conches and trumpets, and Vedic recitations, and reaches the world of the Sun.

BP 1.111.7

अनेनैव विधानेन मासान्द्वादश वै क्रमात्। उपोष्य पारणे पूर्णे दद्याद्विप्राय दक्षिणाम् ॥

By this very procedure, in due order through twelve months, having fasted, when the fast is complete, he should give a gift to a Brahmin.

BP 1.111.8

व्रतं यः पुरुषः कुर्यादादित्याराधनं शुचिः। स गच्छेत्परमं लोकं दिव्यं वै वनमालिनः ॥

The pure person who performs the vow and the worship of Āditya goes indeed to the supreme divine world of Vanamālin.
