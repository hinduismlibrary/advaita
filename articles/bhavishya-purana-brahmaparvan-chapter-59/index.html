Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 59 — The Greatness of the Seventh Day Chariot Observance

BP 1.59.1

माघे मासि तथा देव सिते पक्षे जितेन्द्रियः ।
षष्ठ्यामुपोषितो भूत्वा गन्धपुष्पोपहारतः ॥ १ ॥

Brahmā spoke. In the month of Māgha, in the bright fortnight, O god, with the senses controlled, one should fast on the sixth day and offer fragrances and flowers.

BP 1.59.2

पूजयित्वा दिनकरं रात्रौ तस्याग्रतः स्वपेत् ।
विबुद्धस्त्वथ सप्तम्यां भक्त्या भानुं समर्चयेत् ॥ २ ॥

Having worshiped the Sun, one should sleep at night before him. On awakening on the seventh day, one should worship the Sun with devotion.

BP 1.59.3

ब्राह्मणान्भोजयेत्पश्चाद्वित्तशाठ्यं विवर्जयेत् ।
खण्डवेष्टैर्मोदकैश्च तथेक्षुगुडपूपकैः ॥ ३ ॥

Afterward one should feed the Brahmins with sugar covered sweets, sweet dumplings, and cakes of sugarcane and jaggery, and give up stinginess with wealth.

BP 1.59.4

अथ संवत्सरे पूर्णे सप्तम्यां कारयेद्बुधः ।
देवदेवस्य वै यात्रां पूर्वोक्तविधिना हर ॥ ४ ॥

Then, when the year is complete, the wise one should arrange the procession of the God of gods on the seventh day according to the previously stated procedure, O Hara.

BP 1.59.5

कृष्णपक्षे तु यः कृत्वा रथमारोहितं रविम् ।
पश्येद्भक्त्या जगन्नाथं स याति परमां गतिम् ॥ ५ ॥

In the dark fortnight, whoever places the Sun on the chariot and sees the Lord of the universe with devotion attains the highest state.

BP 1.59.6

तृतीयायामैकभक्तं चतुर्थ्यां नक्तमुच्यते ।
पञ्चम्यामयाचितं स्यात्षष्ठ्यां चैवमुपोषणम् ॥ ६ ॥

One meal is prescribed on the third day, food at night on the fourth. On the fifth day, food obtained without asking, and on the sixth day, fasting likewise.

BP 1.59.7

सप्तम्यां पारणं कुर्याद्दृष्ट्वा देवं रथे स्थितम् ।
पूजयित्वा च विधिना शक्त्या भक्त्या त्रिलोचन ॥ ७ ॥

On the seventh day one should break the fast after seeing the god standing on the chariot, then worship according to the rite, according to ability, with devotion, O three eyed one.

BP 1.59.8

सौवर्णं तु रथं कृत्वा ताम्रपात्रोपरि स्थितम् ।
रथमध्ये न्यसेद्व्योम पूजितं मणिभिर्हर ॥ ८ ॥

One should make a golden chariot set above a copper vessel. In the middle of the chariot one should place the sky and worship it with jewels, O Hara.

BP 1.59.9

पद्मरागं न्यसेन्मध्ये मौक्तिकं पूर्वतो न्यसेत् ।
इंद्रनीलमथो याम्यां वारुण्यां मरकतं हर ॥ ९ ॥

One should place a ruby in the middle and a pearl in the east. Then a sapphire in the south and an emerald in the west, O Hara.

BP 1.59.10

प्रवालमुत्तरे रुद्र सवज्रं विन्यसेद्बुधः ।
श्वेतं पीतासितं चापि रक्तं चान्धकसूदन ॥ १० ॥

The wise one should place coral in the north together with a diamond, O Rudra. The garments are white, yellow, black, and red, O destroyer of Andhaka.

BP 1.59.11

एतानि तात वस्त्राणि दिक्षु सर्वासु विन्यसेत् ।
पताकाकारसंस्थानं घण्टाभरणभूषितम् ॥ ११ ॥

These garments, dear one, should be placed in every direction, arranged in the form of banners and adorned with bells.

BP 1.59.12

पुष्पैर्दामैरलंकृत्य रथं रुद्र समन्ततः ।
यथान्यायं पूजयित्वा भास्कराय निवेदयेत् ॥ १२ ॥

Having decorated the chariot all around with flowers and garlands, O Rudra, one should worship it according to the rite and offer it to the Sun.

BP 1.59.13

भोजयित्वाथ वा विप्रानाचार्याय निवेदयेत् ।
योऽधीते सप्तमीकल्पं सोपाख्यानं च भारत ॥ १३ ॥

Then, having fed the Brahmins, one should present it to the teacher who studies the seventh day rite together with its narrative, O Bhārata.

BP 1.59.14

आचार्यः स द्विजो ज्ञेयो वर्णानामनुपूर्वशः ।
सौराणां वैष्णवानां तु शैवानां पार्वतीप्रिय ॥ १४ ॥

The teacher, a twice born man, should be recognized in the order of the varṇas among the Sun worshipers, the Viṣṇu worshipers, and the Śiva worshipers, O beloved of Pārvatī.

BP 1.59.15

अलाभे तु सुवर्णस्य रथं राजतमादिशेत् ।
तदलाभे ताम्रमयं रथं व्योम च कारयेत् ॥ १५ ॥

If gold is unavailable, he should prescribe a silver chariot. If that is unavailable, he should have a copper chariot and the sky made.

BP 1.59.16

अभावे चापि ताम्रस्य रथः पिष्टमयः स्मृतः ।
सहिरण्यो महादेव ताम्रभाजनमाश्रितः ॥ १६ ॥

If copper is also absent, a chariot made of flour is prescribed, with gold, O great god, set upon a copper vessel.

BP 1.59.17

कौशेययुग्मसहितं ब्राह्मणाय निवेदयेत् ।
पूर्वोक्ताय महादेव वाचकाय महात्मने ॥ १७ ॥

He should offer it, together with a pair of silk garments, to the previously mentioned Brahmin, the great souled reciter, O great god.

BP 1.59.18

पञ्चरत्नसमायुक्तं गुभगन्धाधिवासितम् ।
स्वशक्त्या तु विरूपाक्ष वित्तशाठ्यं विवर्जयेत् ॥ १८ ॥

With five gems and perfumed with fragrant substances, it should be offered according to ability, O Virūpākṣa, without stinginess with wealth.

BP 1.59.19

एषा पुण्या पापहरा रथाह्वा सप्तमी हर ।
कथिता ते मया रुद्र महतीयं प्रकीर्तिता ॥ १९ ॥

This meritorious seventh day called the Chariot, which removes sins, has been told to you, O Hara. This great rite has been proclaimed, O Rudra.

BP 1.59.20

स्नानं दानमथो होमः पूजा ग्रहपतेर्हर ।
शतसाहस्रं भवेदस्यां कृतं भूधरविद्यते ॥ २० ॥

Bathing, gifts, fire offerings, and worship of the lord of the planets, O Hara. What is done on this day is known, O bearer of the earth, to become a hundred thousandfold.

BP 1.59.21

एवमेषा पुण्यतमा माघे प्रोक्ता तु सप्तमी ।
यामुपोष्य नरो भक्त्या सूर्यस्यानुचरो भवेत् ॥ २१ ॥

Thus this most meritorious seventh day is declared in Māgha. The person who fasts on it with devotion becomes an attendant of the Sun.

BP 1.59.22

ब्राह्मणो याति देवत्वं क्षत्रियो विप्रतां व्रजेत् ।
वैश्यः क्षत्रियतां याति शूद्रो वैश्यत्वमेति च ॥ २२ ॥

A Brahmin attains divinity, a Kṣatriya becomes a Brahmin, a Vaiśya attains Kṣatriya status, and a Śūdra attains Vaiśya status.

BP 1.59.23

विद्याविनयसम्पन्नं भर्त्तारं कन्यका लभेत् ।
अपुत्रा स्त्री सुतं विन्देत्सौमाग्यं च गणाधिप ॥ २३ ॥

A maiden obtains a husband endowed with learning and humility. A childless woman gains a son and good fortune, O lord of the hosts.

BP 1.59.24

विधवा चाप्युपोष्येमां सप्तमीं त्रिपुरान्तक ।
नान्यजन्मसु वैधव्यं प्राप्नुयात्पार्वतीप्रिय ॥ २४ ॥

A widow too, by fasting on this seventh day, will not encounter widowhood in other births, O destroyer of Tripura, O beloved of Pārvatī.

BP 1.59.25

बहुपुत्रा बहुधना पत्युर्वल्लभतां व्रजेत् ।
यावद्वै सप्त जन्मानि स्त्रियस्तु पुरुषास्तथा ॥ २५ ॥

She obtains many sons, much wealth, and the affection of her husband. Women and men likewise, for seven births.

BP 1.59.26

एवंविधा सप्तमी ते कथिता वृषभध्वज ।
यां श्रुत्वा मानवो भक्त्या मुच्यते ब्रह्महत्यया ॥ २६ ॥

Thus this kind of seventh day has been told to you, O bull banner. By hearing it with devotion, a person is freed from the sin of killing a Brahmin.
