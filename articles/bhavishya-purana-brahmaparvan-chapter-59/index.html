Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 59 — Rathasaptamī and Its Merits

BP 1.59.1

माघे मासि तथा देव सिते पक्षे जितेन्द्रियः ।
षष्ठ्यामुपोषितो भूत्वा गन्धपुष्पोपहारतः ॥

Brahmā said: In the month of Māgha, in the bright fortnight, O god, having controlled his senses, he should fast on the sixth day and offer fragrance and flowers.

Word-for-word
māghe māsi — in Māgha month; site pakṣe — in the bright fortnight; jitendriyaḥ — one who has conquered the senses; ṣaṣṭhyām — on the sixth day; upoṣitaḥ bhūtvā — having fasted; gandha-puṣpa-upahārataḥ — with offerings of fragrance and flowers
Transliteration
māghe māsi tathā deva site pakṣe jitendriyaḥ |
ṣaṣṭhyām upoṣito bhūtvā gandha-puṣpopahārataḥ || 1 ||

BP 1.59.2

पूजयित्वा दिनकरं रात्रौ तस्याग्रतः स्वपेत् ।
विबुद्धस्त्वथ सप्तम्यां भक्त्या भानुं समर्चयेत् ॥

Having worshiped the Sun, he should sleep at night before him; then, awakening on the seventh day, he should worship Bhānu with devotion.

Word-for-word
pūjayitvā — having worshiped; dinakaram — the Sun; rātrau — at night; tasya agrataḥ — before him; svapet — should sleep; vibuddhaḥ — awakened; atha — then; saptamyām — on the seventh; bhaktyā — with devotion; bhānum samarcayet — should worship Bhānu
Transliteration
pūjayitvā dinakaraṃ rātrau tasyāgrataḥ svapet |
vibuddhas tv atha saptamyāṃ bhaktyā bhānuṃ samarcayet || 2 ||

BP 1.59.3

ब्राह्मणान्भोजयेत्पश्चाद्वित्तशाठ्यं विवर्जयेत् ।
खण्डवेष्टैर्मोदकैश्च तथेक्षुगुडपूपकैः ॥

Afterwards he should feed Brāhmaṇas and abandon stinginess with wealth, [offering] khāṇḍaveṣṭa sweets, modakas, and likewise cakes of sugarcane and jaggery.

Word-for-word
brāhmaṇān bhojayet — should feed Brāhmaṇas; paścāt — afterwards; vitta-śāṭhyam vivarjayet — should abandon stinginess with wealth; khaṇḍaveṣṭaiḥ modakaiḥ — with khāṇḍaveṣṭa sweets and modakas; ikṣu-guḍa-pūpakaiḥ — with sugarcane-jaggery cakes
Transliteration
brāhmaṇān bhojayet paścād vittaśāṭhyaṃ vivarjayet |
khaṇḍaveṣṭair modakaiś ca tathekṣu-guḍapūpakaiḥ || 3 ||

BP 1.59.4

अथ संवत्सरे पूर्णे सप्तम्यां कारयेद्बुधः ।
देवदेवस्य वै यात्रां पूर्वोक्तविधिना हर ॥

After a full year, the wise man should arrange the procession of the God of gods on the seventh day by the procedure previously stated, O Hara.

Word-for-word
atha — then; saṃvatsare pūrṇe — when the year is complete; saptamyām — on the seventh; kārayet — should arrange; budhaḥ — the wise one; devadevasya yātrām — the procession of the God of gods; pūrvokta-vidhinā — by the previously stated procedure; hara — O Hara
Transliteration
atha saṃvatsare pūrṇe saptamyāṃ kārayed budhaḥ |
devadevasya vai yātrāṃ pūrvokta-vidhinā hara || 4 ||

BP 1.59.5

कृष्णपक्षे तु यः कृत्वा रथमारोहितं रविम् ।
पश्येद्भक्त्या जगन्नाथं स याति परमां गतिम् ॥

Whoever, having placed Ravi on the chariot in the dark fortnight, sees the Lord of the world with devotion, attains the supreme state.

Word-for-word
kṛṣṇa-pakṣe — in the dark fortnight; yaḥ — whoever; kṛtvā — having made; ratham ārohitaṃ ravim — Ravi mounted on the chariot; paśyet — sees; bhaktyā — with devotion; jagannātham — Lord of the world; saḥ yāti — he attains; paramām gatim — the supreme state
Transliteration
kṛṣṇapakṣe tu yaḥ kṛtvā ratham ārohitaṃ ravim |
paśyed bhaktyā jagannāthaṃ sa yāti paramāṃ gatim || 5 ||

BP 1.59.6

तृतीयायामैकभक्तं चतुर्थ्यां नक्तमुच्यते ।
पञ्चम्यामयाचितं स्यात्षष्ठ्यां चैवमुपोषणम् ॥

On the third day, one meal is prescribed; on the fourth, food at night; on the fifth, food obtained without asking; and on the sixth, fasting likewise.

Word-for-word
tṛtīyāyām — on the third; eka-bhaktam — one meal; caturthyām — on the fourth; naktam — food at night; pañcamyām — on the fifth; ayācitam — unasked-for food; ṣaṣṭhyām — on the sixth; upoṣaṇam — fasting
Transliteration
tṛtīyāyām aikabhaktaṃ caturthyāṃ naktam ucyate |
pañcamyām ayācitaṃ syāt ṣaṣṭhyāṃ caivam upoṣaṇam || 6 ||

BP 1.59.7

सप्तम्यां पारणं कुर्याद्दृष्ट्वा देवं रथे स्थितम् ।
पूजयित्वा च विधिना शक्त्या भक्त्या त्रिलोचन ॥

On the seventh he should break the fast after seeing the god standing on the chariot, and worship him according to the rite, according to his ability, with devotion, O three-eyed one.

Word-for-word
saptamyām — on the seventh; pāraṇam kuryāt — should break the fast; dṛṣṭvā — having seen; devam rathe sthitam — the god standing on the chariot; pūjayitvā — having worshiped; vidhinā — by the rite; śaktyā — according to ability; bhaktyā — with devotion; trilocana — O three-eyed one
Transliteration
saptamyāṃ pāraṇaṃ kuryād dṛṣṭvā devaṃ rathe sthitam |
pūjayitvā ca vidhinā śaktyā bhaktyā trilocana || 7 ||

BP 1.59.8

सौवर्णं तु रथं कृत्वा ताम्रपात्रोपरि स्थितम् ।
रथमध्ये न्यसेद्व्योम पूजितं मणिभिर्हर ॥

Having made a golden chariot situated upon a copper vessel, he should place the sky in the middle of the chariot, worshiped with jewels, O Hara.

Word-for-word
sauvarṇam ratham — golden chariot; kṛtvā — having made; tāmra-pātra-upari sthitam — situated upon a copper vessel; ratha-madhye — in the middle of the chariot; nyaset — should place; vyoma — the sky; pūjitam maṇibhiḥ — worshiped with jewels; hara — O Hara
Transliteration
sauvarṇaṃ tu rathaṃ kṛtvā tāmrapātropari sthitam |
rathamadhye nyased vyoma pūjitaṃ maṇibhir hara || 8 ||

BP 1.59.9

पद्मरागं न्यसेन्मध्ये मौक्तिकं पूर्वतो न्यसेत् ।
इंद्रनीलमथो याम्यां वारुण्यां मरकतं हर ॥

He should place a ruby in the middle and a pearl in the east; then a sapphire in the south and an emerald in the west, O Hara.

Word-for-word
padmarāgam — ruby; madhye — in the middle; nyaset — should place; mauktikam — pearl; pūrvataḥ — in the east; indranīlam — sapphire; yāmyām — in the south; vāruṇyām — in the west; marakatam — emerald
Transliteration
padmarāgaṃ nyasen madhye mauktikaṃ pūrvato nyaset |
iṃdranīlam atho yāmyāṃ vāruṇyāṃ marakataṃ hara || 9 ||

BP 1.59.10

प्रवालमुत्तरे रुद्र सवज्रं विन्यसेद्बुधः ।
श्वेतं पीतासितं चापि रक्तं चान्धकसूदन ॥

He should place coral in the north and a diamond, O Rudra; the wise one should arrange white, yellow, black, and red [cloths], O destroyer of Andhaka.

Word-for-word
pravālam uttare — coral in the north; rudra — O Rudra; savajram — together with a diamond; vinyaset — should arrange; śvetam pīta-asitam raktam — white, yellow, black, red; andhakasūdana — O destroyer of Andhaka
Transliteration
pravālam uttare rudra savajraṃ vinyased budhaḥ |
śvetaṃ pītāsitaṃ cāpi raktaṃ cāndhakasūdana || 10 ||

BP 1.59.11

एतानि तात वस्त्राणि दिक्षु सर्वासु विन्यसेत् ।
पताकाकारसंस्थानं घण्टाभरणभूषितम् ॥

These cloths, dear one, he should arrange in all the directions, formed like banners and adorned with bell ornaments.

Word-for-word
etāni vastrāṇi — these cloths; tāta — dear one; dikṣu sarvāsu — in all directions; vinyaset — should arrange; patākākāra-saṃsthānam — formed like banners; ghaṇṭābharaṇa-bhūṣitam — adorned with bell ornaments
Transliteration
etāni tāta vastrāṇi dikṣu sarvāsu vinyaset |
patākākāra-saṃsthānaṃ ghaṇṭābharaṇa-bhūṣitam || 11 ||

BP 1.59.12

पुष्पैर्दामैरलंकृत्य रथं रुद्र समन्ततः ।
यथान्यायं पूजयित्वा भास्कराय निवेदयेत् ॥

Having adorned the chariot all around with flowers and garlands, he should worship it according to rule and offer it to Bhāskara, O Rudra.

Word-for-word
puṣpaiḥ dāmaiḥ — with flowers and garlands; alaṃkṛtya — having adorned; ratham samantataḥ — the chariot all around; yathānyāyam — according to rule; pūjayitvā — having worshiped; bhāskarāya nivedayet — should offer to Bhāskara
Transliteration
puṣpair dāmair alaṃkṛtya rathaṃ rudra samantataḥ |
yathānyāyaṃ pūjayitvā bhāskarāya nivedayet || 12 ||

BP 1.59.13

भोजयित्वाथ वा विप्रानाचार्याय निवेदयेत् ।
योऽधीते सप्तमीकल्पं सोपाख्यानं च भारत ॥

Or, having fed Brāhmaṇas, he should offer it to the teacher, O Bhārata; whoever studies the Saptamī-kalpa together with its narrative,

Word-for-word
bhojayitvā — having fed; viprān — Brāhmaṇas; ācāryāya nivedayet — should offer to the teacher; yaḥ adhīte — whoever studies; saptamī-kalpam — the Saptamī-kalpa; sopākhyānam — together with the narrative; bhārata — O Bhārata
Transliteration
bhojayitvātha vā viprān ācāryāya nivedayet |
yo'dhīte saptamīkalpaṃ sopākhyānaṃ ca bhārata || 13 ||

BP 1.59.14

आचार्यः स द्विजो ज्ञेयो वर्णानामनुपूर्वशः ।
सौराणां वैष्णवानां तु शैवानां पार्वतीप्रिय ॥

That twice-born teacher is to be known according to the order of the classes, beloved of the Sauras, Vaiṣṇavas, and Śaivas, O beloved of Pārvatī.

Word-for-word
ācāryaḥ — teacher; saḥ dvijaḥ — that twice-born; jñeyaḥ — is to be known; varṇānām anupūrvaśaḥ — according to the order of the classes; saurāṇām vaiṣṇavānām śaivānām — of Sauras, Vaiṣṇavas, Śaivas; pārvatīpriya — O beloved of Pārvatī
Transliteration
ācāryaḥ sa dvijo jñeyo varṇānām anupūrvaśaḥ |
saurāṇāṃ vaiṣṇavānāṃ tu śaivānāṃ pārvatīpriya || 14 ||

BP 1.59.15

अलाभे तु सुवर्णस्य रथं राजतमादिशेत् ।
तदलाभे ताम्रमयं रथं व्योम च कारयेत् ॥

If gold is unavailable, he should prescribe a silver chariot; if that too is unavailable, he should have a copper chariot and sky made.

Word-for-word
alābhe suvarṇasya — when gold is unavailable; ratham rājatam — a silver chariot; ādiśet — should prescribe; tad-alābhe — when that is unavailable; tāmramayam ratham — a copper chariot; vyoma kārayet — should have the sky made
Transliteration
alābhe tu suvarṇasya rathaṃ rājatam ādiśet |
tad-alābhe tāmramayaṃ rathaṃ vyoma ca kārayet || 15 ||

BP 1.59.16

अभावे चापि ताम्रस्य रथः पिष्टमयः स्मृतः ।
सहिरण्यो महादेव ताम्रभाजनमाश्रितः ॥

If even copper is absent, a chariot made of flour is remembered; together with gold, O Mahādeva, it rests upon a copper vessel.

Word-for-word
abhāve tāmrasya — when copper is absent; rathaḥ piṣṭamayaḥ — a flour-made chariot; smṛtaḥ — is prescribed; sa-hiraṇyaḥ — together with gold; mahādeva — O Mahādeva; tāmrabhājanam āśritaḥ — resting upon a copper vessel
Transliteration
abhāve cāpi tāmrasya rathaḥ piṣṭamayaḥ smṛtaḥ |
sahiraṇyo mahādeva tāmrabhājanam āśritaḥ || 16 ||

BP 1.59.17

कौशेययुग्मसहितं ब्राह्मणाय निवेदयेत् ।
पूर्वोक्ताय महादेव वाचकाय महात्मने ॥

Together with a pair of silk garments, he should offer it to the Brāhmaṇa, the great-souled reciter previously mentioned, O Mahādeva.

Word-for-word
kauśeya-yugma-sahitam — furnished with a pair of silk garments; brāhmaṇāya — to a Brāhmaṇa; nivedayet — should offer; pūrvoktāya — previously mentioned; vācakāya — to the reciter; mahātmane — great-souled
Transliteration
kauśeya-yugma-sahitaṃ brāhmaṇāya nivedayet |
pūrvoktāya mahādeva vācakāya mahātmane || 17 ||

BP 1.59.18

पञ्चरत्नसमायुक्तं गुभगन्धाधिवासितम् ।
स्वशक्त्या तु विरूपाक्ष वित्तशाठ्यं विवर्जयेत् ॥

Joined with five jewels and perfumed with a fragrant scent, it should be offered according to ones ability, O Virūpākṣa, abandoning stinginess with wealth.

Word-for-word
pañca-ratna-samāyuktam — joined with five jewels; gubhagandha-adhivāsitam — perfumed with fragrance; svaśaktyā — according to one’s ability; virūpākṣa — O Virūpākṣa; vitta-śāṭhyam vivarjayet — should abandon stinginess with wealth
Transliteration
pañcaratna-samāyuktaṃ gubhagandhādhivāsitam |
svaśaktyā tu virūpākṣa vittaśāṭhyaṃ vivarjayet || 18 ||

BP 1.59.19

एषा पुण्या पापहरा रथाह्वा सप्तमी हर ।
कथिता ते मया रुद्र महतीयं प्रकीर्तिता ॥

This auspicious sin-destroying Saptamī called Rathāhvā has been told to you by me, O Hara, O Rudra; it has been proclaimed as great.

Word-for-word
eṣā — this; puṇyā — auspicious; pāpa-harā — removing sin; rathāhvā saptamī — Saptamī called Rathāhvā; kathitā — told; te mayā — to you by me; rudra — O Rudra; mahatī — great; prakīrtitā — proclaimed
Transliteration
eṣā puṇyā pāpaharā rathāhvā saptamī hara |
kathitā te mayā rudra mahatīyaṃ prakīrtitā || 19 ||

BP 1.59.20

स्नानं दानमथो होमः पूजा ग्रहपतेर्हर ।
शतसाहस्रं भवेदस्यां कृतं भूधरविद्यते ॥

Bathing, giving, oblation, and worship of the lord of the planets, O Hara—whatever is done on this day becomes a hundred thousandfold, as is known on earth.

Word-for-word
snānam — bathing; dānam — giving; homaḥ — oblation; pūjā — worship; grahapateḥ — of the lord of the planets; śata-sāhasram — a hundred thousand; bhavet — becomes; asyām kṛtam — what is done on this; bhūdharavidyate — is known on earth
Transliteration
snānaṃ dānam atho homaḥ pūjā grahapater hara |
śatasāhasraṃ bhaved asyāṃ kṛtaṃ bhūdharavidyate || 20 ||

BP 1.59.21

एवमेषा पुण्यतमा माघे प्रोक्ता तु सप्तमी ।
यामुपोष्य नरो भक्त्या सूर्यस्यानुचरो भवेत् ॥

Thus this most meritorious Saptamī has been proclaimed for Māgha; having fasted on it with devotion, a man becomes an attendant of the Sun.

Word-for-word
evam — thus; eṣā — this; puṇyatamā — most meritorious; māghe proktā — proclaimed in Māgha; saptamī — Saptamī; yām upoṣya — having fasted on which; naraḥ — a man; bhaktyā — with devotion; sūryasya anucaraḥ bhavet — becomes an attendant of the Sun
Transliteration
evam eṣā puṇyatamā māghe proktā tu saptamī |
yām upoṣya naro bhaktyā sūryasyānucaro bhavet || 21 ||

BP 1.59.22

ब्राह्मणो याति देवत्वं क्षत्रियो विप्रतां व्रजेत् ।
वैश्यः क्षत्रियतां याति शूद्रो वैश्यत्वमेति च ॥

A Brāhmaṇa attains divine status; a Kṣatriya goes to Brāhmaṇa-status; a Vaiśya attains Kṣatriya-status; and a Śūdra reaches Vaiśya-status.

Word-for-word
brāhmaṇaḥ yāti — a Brāhmaṇa goes; devatvam — to divinity; kṣatriyaḥ vrajet — a Kṣatriya goes; vipratām — to Brāhmaṇa-status; vaiśyaḥ yāti — a Vaiśya goes; kṣatriyatām — to Kṣatriya-status; śūdraḥ eti — a Śūdra reaches; vaiśyatvam — Vaiśya-status
Transliteration
brāhmaṇo yāti devatvaṃ kṣatriyo vipratāṃ vrajet |
vaiśyaḥ kṣatriyatāṃ yāti śūdro vaiśyatvam eti ca || 22 ||

BP 1.59.23

विद्याविनयसम्पन्नं भर्त्तारं कन्यका लभेत् ।
अपुत्रा स्त्री सुतं विन्देत्सौमाग्यं च गणाधिप ॥

A maiden obtains a husband endowed with learning and humility; a childless woman obtains a son and good fortune, O Lord of Hosts.

Word-for-word
vidyā-vinaya-sampannam — endowed with learning and humility; bharttāram — husband; kanyakā labhet — a maiden obtains; aputrā strī — a childless woman; sutam vindet — obtains a son; saumāgyam — good fortune; gaṇādhipa — O Lord of Hosts
Transliteration
vidyāvinaya-sampannaṃ bharttāraṃ kanyakā labhet |
aputrā strī sutaṃ vindet saumāgyaṃ ca gaṇādhipa || 23 ||

BP 1.59.24

विधवा चाप्युपोष्येमां सप्तमीं त्रिपुरान्तक ।
नान्यजन्मसु वैधव्यं प्राप्नुयात्पार्वतीप्रिय ॥

A widow too, having fasted on this Saptamī, O destroyer of Tripura, does not meet widowhood in other births, O beloved of Pārvatī.

Word-for-word
vidhavā — a widow; ca api — also; upoṣya — having fasted; imām saptamīm — this Saptamī; tripurāntaka — O destroyer of Tripura; na anyajanmasu — not in other births; vaidhavyam prāpnuyāt — would obtain widowhood; pārvatīpriya — O beloved of Pārvatī
Transliteration
vidhavā cāpy upoṣyemāṃ saptamīṃ tripurāntaka |
nānyajanmasu vaidhavyaṃ prāpnuyāt pārvatīpriya || 24 ||

BP 1.59.25

बहुपुत्रा बहुधना पत्युर्वल्लभतां व्रजेत् ।
यावद्वै सप्त जन्मानि स्त्रियस्तु पुरुषास्तथा ॥

A woman with many sons and much wealth attains her husbands affection for as many as seven births; so likewise do men and women.

Word-for-word
bahu-putrā — having many sons; bahu-dhanā — having much wealth; patyuḥ vallabhatām vrajet — attains the husband’s affection; yāvat — for as many as; sapta janmāni — seven births; striyaḥ tu puruṣāḥ tathā — women and men likewise
Transliteration
bahuputrā bahudhanā patyur vallabhatāṃ vrajet |
yāvad vai sapta janmāni striyas tu puruṣās tathā || 25 ||

BP 1.59.26

एवंविधा सप्तमी ते कथिता वृषभध्वज ।
यां श्रुत्वा मानवो भक्त्या मुच्यते ब्रह्महत्यया ॥

Thus has this kind of Saptamī been told to you, O Bull-bannered one; hearing it with devotion, a man is freed from the sin of killing a Brāhmaṇa.

Word-for-word
evaṃ-vidhā saptamī — Saptamī of this kind; te kathitā — told to you; vṛṣabhadhvaja — O Bull-bannered one; yām śrutvā — hearing which; mānavaḥ — a man; bhaktyā — with devotion; mucyate — is freed; brahma-hatyayā — from Brahminicide
Transliteration
evaṃvidhā saptamī te kathitā vṛṣabhadhvaja |
yāṃ śrutvā mānavo bhaktyā mucyate brahmahatyayā || 26 ||
