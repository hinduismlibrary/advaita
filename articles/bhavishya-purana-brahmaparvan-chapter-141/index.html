Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 141 — Bhojaka Community

BP 1.141.1

साम्ब उवाच । भोजकानां यत्त्वयोक्तमव्यङ्गो देहशोधकः । व्रतबन्धस्त्वसौ प्रोक्तस्तेषां जातिश्च का स्मृता ॥

Sāmba said: What is remembered as the jāti of the Bhojakas, whose body-purifying Avyaṅga you described as their vratabandha?
