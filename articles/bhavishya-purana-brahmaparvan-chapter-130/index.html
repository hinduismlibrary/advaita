Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 130 — The Design of Sūrya’s Temple

BP 1.130.1

शतानीक उवाच।
कथं साम्बेन विप्रेन्द्र प्रतिष्ठा कारिता रवेः।
कस्य वा वचनात्तेन प्रासादः कारितो रवेः ॥

Śatānīka said: O foremost of Brahmins, how did Sāmba arrange the consecration of the Sun? At whose word did he have the temple of the Sun built?

BP 1.130.2

सुमन्तुरुवाच।
अत्र ते वच्मि राजेन्द्र यथा साम्बेन धीमता।
प्रतिष्ठा कारिता भानोः प्रसादश्च महीपते ॥

Sumantu said: Here I shall tell you, O king, how the wise Sāmba arranged the consecration of the Sun and built the temple, O lord of the earth.

BP 1.130.3

सुलब्ध्वा प्रतिमां भानोश्चिन्तयामास नारदम्।
स चापि चिन्तितश्चागाद्यत्र जाम्बवतीसुतः ॥

Having obtained the image of the Sun, he thought of Nārada; and Nārada, being thought of, came where Jāmbavatī’s son was.

BP 1.130.4

तमागतमभिप्रेक्ष्य नारदं मुनिसत्तमम्।
सम्पूज्य विधिवत्सांबो नारदं वाक्यमब्रवीत् ॥

Seeing Nārada, the foremost of sages, arrive, Sāmba worshipped him according to rule and spoke these words to him.

BP 1.130.5

प्रासादं कारयेद्यस्तु भास्करस्य नरो द्विज।
किं फलं तस्य देवर्षे प्रतिष्ठां यश्च कारयेत् ॥

O twice-born one, what reward comes to a man who has a temple built for Bhāskara and has the consecration performed, O divine seer?

BP 1.130.6

नारद उवाच।
प्रासादं शोभने देशे यस्तु कारयते रवेः।
स याति नरशार्दूल सूर्यलोकं न संशयः ॥

Nārada said: Whoever has a temple of the Sun built in a beautiful place goes to the world of the Sun, O tiger among men; there is no doubt.

BP 1.130.7

साम्ब उवाच।
कथं कुर्यादायतनं कस्मिन्देशे द्विजोत्तम।
कीदृक्छस्तं चायतनं देवदेवस्य वै द्विज ॥

Sāmba said: How should one make the shrine, and in what place, O best of Brahmins? What kind of shrine is prescribed for the God of gods, O Brahmin?

BP 1.130.8

नारद उवाच।
यत्र प्रभूतं सलिलमागमे च विनाशने।
देवतायतनं कुर्याद्यशोधर्मविवृद्धये ॥

Nārada said: Where water is abundant, even at times of its increase and decrease, one should build a temple of the gods for the growth of fame and righteousness.

BP 1.130.9

इष्टापूर्तेन लभते लोकांस्तांश्च विभूषितान्।
देवानामालयं कार्यं द्वयं यत्र च दृश्यते ॥

Through sacrificial rites and charitable works one gains those splendid worlds. A divine abode should be built where both are seen.

BP 1.130.10

सलिलाद्यं च आरामः कृतेष्वायतनेषु च।
स्थानेष्वेतेषु सान्निध्यमुपगच्छन्ति देवताः ॥

Where water and gardens are present, and temples have been built, the gods come into presence at those places.

BP 1.130.11

सरःसु नलिनीच्छन्ननिरस्तरविरश्मिषु।
हंससंक्षिप्तकह्लारवीथीविमलवारिषु ॥

In lakes covered with lotuses, where the Sun’s rays are screened, and the clear waters contain rows of white water lilies gathered by swans;

BP 1.130.12

हंसकारण्डवक्रौञ्चचक्रवाकविराविषु।
पर्यन्तविमलच्छायाविश्रान्तजनचारिषु ॥

where the calls of swans, ducks, cranes, and ruddy geese resound, and people walk and rest in clear shade along the banks;

BP 1.130.13

क्रौञ्चकाञ्चीसुलापाश्च कलहंसकलस्वनाः।
नद्यस्तोयांशुका यत्र शफरीकृतमेखलाः ॥

where cranes are their girdles and soft-voiced royal swans, where rivers wear water as their garment and little fish as their belts;

BP 1.130.14

फुल्लद्रुमोत्तमावासाः सङ्गमश्रोणिमण्डलाः।
पुलिनाद्युन्नतोरस्का रसहासाश्च निम्नगाः ॥

where rivers dwell among the finest flowering trees, their confluences forming rounded hips, their sandbanks raised like breasts, and their ripples like smiles;

BP 1.130.15

वनोपान्तनदीशैलसंस्करोपान्तभूमिषु।
रमन्ते देवता नित्यं पुरेषूद्यानवत्सु च ॥

The gods always delight in lands beside forests, rivers, and hills, on prepared ground at their edges, and in cities furnished with gardens.

BP 1.130.16

भूमयो ब्राह्मणादीनां याः प्रोक्ता वास्तुकर्मणि।
ता एवं तेषां शस्यन्ते देवतायतनेष्वपि ॥

The ground types prescribed in architectural practice for Brahmins and the other varnas are likewise approved for temples.

BP 1.130.17

चतुःषष्टिपदं कुर्याद्देवतायतनं सदा।
द्वारं च मध्यमं तस्मिन्समदिक्सम्प्रशस्यते ॥

One should always lay out a divine temple on a sixty-four-square plan. Its doorway should be central; equal alignment with the directions is praised.

BP 1.130.18

यो विस्तारो भवेत्तस्य द्विगुणा तत्समुन्नतिः।
उच्छ्रायस्तु तृतीयोऽथ तेन तुल्या कटिर्भवेत् ॥

Whatever its width, its height should be twice that. Its rise should be a third of the measure, and its middle section equal to that.

BP 1.130.19

विस्तारार्धो भवेद्गर्भो भिन्नयोन्याः समन्ततः।
गर्भपादोनविस्तीर्णं द्वारं द्विगुणमुच्छ्रितम् ॥

The inner sanctum should be half the width, with its distinct forms all around. The doorway should be one quarter less than the sanctum’s width and twice as high as it is wide.

BP 1.130.20

उच्छ्रयात्पादविस्तीर्णा शाखा तद्वदुदुम्बरी।
विस्तारात्पादप्रतिमाद्बाहुल्यं शेषयोः स्मृतम् ॥

The doorpost should be one quarter of the height in width, and the lintel the same. The remaining measures are set from a quarter of the width and the breadth of the image.

BP 1.130.21

नृपं सप्तनवभिः शाखाभिस्तत्प्रशस्यते।
अथ शाखाचतुर्भागे प्रतिहारौ निवेशयेत् ॥

For the royal shrine, seven or nine doorframes are praised. Then one should place the two door guardians in the four parts of the frames.

BP 1.130.22

शैलमङ्गल्यविहगः श्रीवृक्षः स्वस्तिकैर्घटैः।
मानाष्टमेन भागेन प्रतिमा स्यात्सपिण्डिका ॥

The auspicious emblems are a mountain, an auspicious bird, the lucky tree, swastikas, and water pots. The image with its pedestal should occupy one eighth of the measure.

BP 1.130.23

द्विभागा प्रतिमा तत्र तृतीयो भागपिण्डिका।
पूर्वे मेरुर्महाबाहो कैलासश्च तथापरे ॥

There the image is two parts and the pedestal one third. Mount Meru is in the east, O mighty-armed one, and Mount Kailāsa in the west.

BP 1.130.24

भवन्ति चापरे वीर विमानच्छदनं तथा।
समुद्रपद्मगरुडनन्दिवर्द्धनकुञ्जराः ॥

Other forms are the Sky Pavilion and its roof, O hero, as well as Ocean, Lotus, Garuḍa, Joy-Increaser, and Elephant.

BP 1.130.25

गृहराजो वृषो हंसः सर्वतोभद्रको घटः।
सिंहो वृषश्चतुष्कोणः षोडशाष्टाश्रयस्तथा ॥

King’s House, Bull, Swan, All-Auspicious, Pot, Lion, Bull, Four-Cornered, and Sixteen-and-Eight-Supported.

BP 1.130.26

इत्येते विंशतिः प्रोक्ताः प्रासादा यदुनन्दन।
यथोक्तानुक्रमेणैव लक्षणानि वदामि ते ॥

These twenty temples have been named, O descendant of Yadu. I shall tell you their characteristics in the order stated.

BP 1.130.27

नवत्रिंशदुच्छ्रितमेरुर्द्वादशभौमो विविधकुहरश्च।
द्वारैर्युतश्चतुभिर्द्वात्रिंशद्धस्तविस्तीर्णः ॥

Meru is thirty-nine cubits high, has twelve storeys and many recesses, is furnished with four doorways, and is thirty-two cubits wide.

BP 1.130.28

त्रिंशद्धस्तायामो दशभौमः सप्त मन्दरः।
शिखरयुतः कैलासोऽपि शिखरवानष्टाविंशोष्टभौमश्च ॥

Mandara is thirty cubits long and ten-storeyed; Kailāsa also has a spire. The spired temple is twenty-eight cubits high and eight-storeyed.

BP 1.130.29

जालगवाक्षैर्युक्तो विमानसंज्ञस्त्रिसप्तकायामः।
नन्दन इति वै भौमो द्वात्रिंशत्षोडशाङ्गयुतः ॥

The form called Sky Pavilion is furnished with lattice windows and is twenty-one cubits long. Delightful Garden has storeys and thirty-two and sixteen members.

BP 1.130.30

वृत्तः समुद्गनामा पद्माकृतिरयं चाष्टौ।
शृङ्गेणैकेन भवेदेकेन च भूमिका तस्य ॥

The round temple is called the Casket; the Lotus is eight-sided, with one spire and one storey.

BP 1.130.31

गरुडाकृतिश्च गरुडो नन्दी वै षष्टिविस्तीर्णः।
कायश्च सप्तभौमो विभूषितोंऽगैश्च सप्तविंशतिभिः ॥

The Garuḍa temple is shaped like Garuḍa; the Bull is sixty cubits wide. Its body has seven storeys and is adorned with twenty-seven members.

BP 1.130.32

कुञ्जर इति गजपृष्ठः षोडशाहस्तोच्छ्रितो मध्ये।
गृहराजः षोडशकस्त्रिचक्रशाला भवेद्वलभी ॥

The Elephant is elephant-backed and sixteen cubits high at the middle. King’s House is sixteen cubits; the Verandah has a three-part hall.

BP 1.130.33

वृष एवं मृमिशृङ्गो द्वावशहस्तः समुन्नतो वृत्तः।
हंसो हंसाकारो घटोऽष्टसहस्रकलशरूपः ॥

The Bull has a horn, is twelve cubits high, raised and round. The Swan is swan-shaped; the Pot has the form of eight thousand water pots.

BP 1.130.34

द्वारैर्युतश्चतुर्भिर्बहुशिखरो भवति सर्वतोभद्रः।
बहुरुचिरचन्द्रशालः सप्तविंशद्भागभूमिश्च ॥

All-Auspicious has four doorways and many spires; it has many beautiful moon-windowed halls and a ground plan of twenty-seven divisions.

BP 1.130.35

सिंहः सिंहाकारो द्वादशकोणोऽष्टहस्तश्च ॥

The Lion is lion-shaped, has twelve corners, and measures eight cubits.

BP 1.130.36

सहस्रत्रितयं चैव कथितं विश्वकर्मणा।
प्राहुः स्थापयतश्चात्र मतमेकं विपश्चितः ॥

Viśvakarman has also declared the measure of three thousand; the wise state one rule here for the person who installs it.

BP 1.130.37

कपोतपालिनीयुक्तमतो गच्छति तुल्यताम् ॥

Furnished with a pigeon parapet, it thereby attains equality.

BP 1.130.38

साम्ब उवाच।
य एते कथिता विप्र प्रासादा विंशतिस्त्वया।
तेषां सूर्यस्य कः कार्यः प्रासादो भास्करस्य तु ॥

Sāmba said: O Brahmin, of the twenty temples you have described, which one should be built for the Sun, for Bhāskara?

BP 1.130.39

स्थानानि यानि चोक्तानि प्रासादस्य द्विजोत्तम।
तेषां त्वयोक्तं हि पुरं व्ययवद्भिर्नरैर्युतम् ॥

O best of Brahmins, you have described the sites for the temple along with a city furnished with people of means.

BP 1.130.40

तस्मिन्प्रदेशे वै कार्यं भानोर्मन्दिरमुत्तमम्।
दिशां भागे च कतमे ब्रूहि शेषं द्विजोत्तम ॥

In that region the excellent temple of the Sun should be built. Tell me, O best of Brahmins, in which direction it should stand, and the rest.

BP 1.130.41

नारद उवाच।
पुरमध्यं समाश्रित्य कुर्यादायतनं रवेः।
दिशां भागेऽथ वा पूर्वे पूर्वद्वारसमीपतः ॥

Nārada said: One should build the Sun’s shrine in the center of a city, or in its eastern quarter near the eastern gate.

BP 1.130.42

भूमिं परीक्ष्य पूर्वं तु कुर्यादायतनं ततः।
इष्टगन्धरसोपेता निम्ना भूमिः प्रशस्यते ॥

First examine the ground; then build the shrine. Low-lying ground endowed with pleasant smell and taste is praised.

BP 1.130.43

शर्करातुषकेशास्थिक्षाराङ्गारविवर्जिता।
मेघदुन्दुभिनिर्घोषा सर्वबीजप्ररोहिणी ॥

It should be free of gravel, husks, hair, bones, alkali, and charcoal; it should resound like a thundercloud and cause every seed to sprout.

BP 1.130.44

शुक्ला रक्ता तथा पीता कृष्णा च कथिता क्षितिः।
द्विजराजन्यवंश्यानां शूद्राणां च यथाक्रमम् ॥

The ground is declared white, red, yellow, and black, in order for Brahmins, Kshatriyas, Vaishyas, and Shudras.

BP 1.130.45

परीक्षितायां तस्यां तु मध्ये तस्याः प्रमाणतः।
उपलिप्य चतुर्हस्तं चतुरस्रं च समन्ततः ॥

After examining it, one should plaster, at its center and according to its measure, a square area four cubits on every side.

BP 1.130.46

हस्तमात्रमधः कृत्वा मध्ये तस्या दशाङ्गुलम्।
गर्तमुत्कीर्य तेनैव पांसुना प्रतिपूरयेत् ॥

Having dug down one cubit, one should excavate a pit ten fingerbreadths deep in its middle and refill it with that same soil.

BP 1.130.47

समे समगुणा ज्ञेया हीने हीनगुणा भवेत्।
वर्धमाने तु वै पांसौ भवेद्वृद्धिकरी क्षितिः ॥

If the soil is level with the ground, it should be known to be of equal quality; if lower, of inferior quality; if it rises, the ground brings increase.

BP 1.130.48

नित्यं सम्मुखमर्कस्य कदाचित्पश्चिमामुखम्।
स्थापनीयं गृहं सम्यक्प्राङ्मुखस्थानकल्पनात् ॥

The temple should always face the Sun, though sometimes it may face west; it should be properly set out according to an east-facing plan.

BP 1.130.49

भवनाद्दक्षिणे पार्श्वे रवेः स्नानगृहं भवेत्।
अग्निहोत्र गृहं कार्यं रवेरुत्तरतः शुभम्।
उदङ्मुखं भवेच्छम्भोर्मातॄणां गृहमेव च ॥

The Sun’s bathing house should be on the south side of the temple; the auspicious fire-offering house should be north of the Sun. The houses of Śambhu and the Mothers should face north.

BP 1.130.50

ब्रह्मा पश्चिमतः स्थाप्यो विष्णुरुत्तरतस्तथा।
निम्बस्तु दक्षिणे पार्श्वे वामे राज्ञी प्रकीर्तिता ॥

Brahmā should be placed in the west and Viṣṇu in the north; the neem tree is on the south side, and the queen is declared to be on the left.

BP 1.130.51

पिंगलो पक्षिणे भानोर्वामतो दण्डनायकः।
श्रीमहाश्वेतयोः स्थानं पुरतस्त्वंशुमालिनः ॥

Piṅgala is on the right side of the Sun, and the staff-bearing attendant on his left. Śrī and Mahāśvetā are placed in front of the Ray-Crowned One.

BP 1.130.52

ततःस्थाप्याश्विनोः स्थानं पूर्वदेवगृहाद्वहिः।
द्वितीयायां तु कक्षायां राज्ञास्रौषौ व्यवस्थितौ ॥

Next, the place of the Aśvins should be set outside the first divine house. In the second enclosure, Rājñā and Asrauṣau are situated.

BP 1.130.53

तृतीयायां तु कक्षायां स्थितौ कल्माषपक्षिणौ।
जण्डकामचरौ स्थाप्यौ दक्षिणां दिशमाश्रितौ ॥

In the third enclosure stand the two dark-speckled birds; Jaṇḍaka and Macara should be placed facing south.

BP 1.130.54

उदीच्यां स्थापनीयस्तु कुबेरः लोकपूजितः।
उत्तरेण ततस्तस्य रेवतः सविनायकः ॥

Kubera, worshipped by the world, should be placed in the north; farther north of him should be Revata together with Vināyaka.

BP 1.130.55

यत्र वा विद्यते स्थानं दिक्षु सर्वा गुहादयः।
द्वे मण्डलेऽर्घ्यदानार्थं कार्ये सव्यापसव्यतः ॥

Wherever there is space, caves and other features may be arranged in every direction. Two ritual diagrams should be made to the right and left for water offerings.

BP 1.130.56

दद्यादुदयवेलायामर्घं सूर्याय दक्षिणे।
उत्तरे मण्डले दद्यादर्घ्यमस्तमने रवेः ॥

At sunrise one should offer water to the Sun in the southern diagram; in the northern diagram one should offer water to the Sun at sunset.

BP 1.130.57

चक्राकृतां तथान्यस्मिन्देवस्य प्रतिमां रवेः।
स्थापयेद्विधिवद्वीर चतुर्भिः कलशैः शुभैः ॥

O hero, according to rule one should install the Sun’s image, shaped like a wheel, in another diagram, together with four auspicious water pots.

BP 1.130.58

नानातूर्यनिनादैश्च शङ्खशब्दैश्च पुष्कलैः।
तृतीये मण्डले ह्येव पूजनीयो दिवाकरः ॥

With the sounds of many instruments and abundant conch-blasts, the Sun should indeed be worshipped in the third ritual diagram.

BP 1.130.59

चतुरस्रं चतुःशृङ्गं व्योम देवगृहाग्रतः।
प्रतिमायास्तु सूत्रेण कार्यं मध्येऽस्य मण्डलम् ॥

A square, four-peaked canopy should be made before the divine temple; its ritual diagram should be laid out at the center by a line drawn from the image.

BP 1.130.60

दिण्डी स्थाप्यः पुरस्तस्मादादित्याभिमुखः स्थितः।
यदेतत्कथितं व्योम सर्वदेवमयं मया ॥

Diṇḍī should be placed in front, standing facing the Sun. This canopy, as I have described it, consists of all the gods.

BP 1.130.61

मध्याह्ने तस्य दातव्यमर्घ्यमत्र यदूत्तम।
अथ वा मण्डलं चान्यत्तृतीयं चक्रसम्मितम् ॥

At midday, O best of the Yadus, water should be offered to him there; or another third diagram, round like a wheel, may be made.

BP 1.130.62

स्थापयित्वा तु देवेशं दातव्योऽर्घः सुपण्डितैः।
देवस्य पुरतः कार्यं व्योमस्थानं समीपतः।
पुस्तकवाचनस्थानमथ वा यत्र रोचते ॥

After installing the Lord of the gods, the wise should offer water. In front of the deity, nearby, one should make a canopy-space or a place for reading books, wherever desired.

BP 1.130.63

एष स्थानविधिः प्रोक्तो देवतानां यथाक्रमम्।
गृहराज्ञोऽथ रुद्रस्तु द्वावेतौ भास्करप्रियौ ॥

This is the prescribed arrangement of the gods in due order. King of the House and Rudra, these two are dear to Bhāskara.
