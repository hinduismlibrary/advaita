Book 2, Chapter 9

Planetary system, under the type of a Śiśumára or porpoise. The earth nourished by the sun. Of rain whilst the sun shines. Of rain from clouds. Rain the support, of vegetation, and thence of animal life. Náráyańa the support of all beings.

VP 2.9.1

तारामयं भगवतः शिशुमाराकृति प्रभोः । दिवि रूपं हरेर् यत् तु तस्य पुच्छे स्थितो ध्रुवः ॥१॥

Sri Paraasharar - The entire nakshatra mandala is in the form of an animal called Shimshumaara, which is a kind of whale, mammal which lives in water. This is a form of Bhagavaan Himself, filled with nakshatraas. That form of Hari, Dhruva is located at the tail portion of that. Or also, that Dhruva is supported by Shimshumaara.

Śrīdhara translation

Atha dhruva prasangaat tat dvaaraa sarvaadhaaram taaraa punja kalpita avayavam shimshumaara aakruti bhagavato roopam aaha - taaraamayam iti | While telling about Dhruva loka, through that, the part which is consisting of all the stars, constellations, in the form of Shimshumaara, form of Bhagavaan Himself, is told here.

VP 2.9.2

सैष भ्रमन् भ्रामयति चन्द्रादित्य्आदिकान् ग्रहान् । भ्रमन्तम् अनु तं यान्ति नक्षत्राणि च चक्रवत् ॥२॥

Soorya, Chandra, and the stars, are all held together by Dhruva, with reins made of Vaayu.

Śrīdhara translation

The commentary explains that Dhruva, situated in the form of Śiśumāra, causes the planets to revolve, while the stars follow his motion. Nārāyaṇa supports that cosmic form from within.

VP 2.9.3

सूराचन्द्रमसौ तारा नक्षत्राणि ग्रहैः सह । वातानीकमयैर् बन्धैर् ध्रुवे बद्धानि तानि वै ॥३॥

the celestial luminaries are in fact bound to the polar-star by aerial cords. The porpoise-like figure of the celestial sphere is

Śrīdhara translation

The sun, moon, stars beginning with Aśvinī, and the other planets are bound to Dhruva by bonds formed of the winds; they follow Dhruva. The seven winds are Āvaha, Pravaha, Anuvaha, Saṃvaha, Vivaha, Parāvaha, and Parivaha, and they carry the three worlds. Their stations lie successively between earth and clouds, clouds and sun and moon, moon and the stars, the stars and the planets, the planets and the Seven Sages, and the Seven Sages and Dhruva.
