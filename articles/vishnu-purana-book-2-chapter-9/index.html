Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Book 2, Chapter 9

Planetary system, under the type of a Śiśumára or porpoise. The earth nourished by the sun. Of rain whilst the sun shines. Of rain from clouds. Rain the support, of vegetation, and thence of animal life. Náráyańa the support of all beings.

VP 2.9.1

तारामयं भगवतः शिशुमाराकृति प्रभोः।
दिवि रूपं हरेर् यत् तु तस्य पुच्छे स्थितो ध्रुवः

Sri Paraasharar - The entire nakshatra mandala is in the form of an animal called Shimshumaara, which is a kind of whale, mammal which lives in water. This is a form of Bhagavaan Himself, filled with nakshatraas. That form of Hari, Dhruva is located at the tail portion of that. Or also, that Dhruva is supported by Shimshumaara.

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

विष्णुपदं ध्रुवादीनां सर्वेषामाश्रयो विष्ण्वादिकारणञ्चेत्य् उक्तम्—यस्मिन् प्रतिष्ठितो भास्वान् मेधीभूतः स्वयं ध्रुव इत्यादिना । तद् एव केन प्रकारेणेत्यपेक्षायां तत्प्रकारविशेषनिरूपणार्थमध्यायारम्भः । तारामयम् इति । नक्षत्रपुञ्जात्मकं शिशुमाराकृति, शिशुमारः सरट-गोधाद्याकारो जलचरसत्त्वविशेषः । तस्याकृतिरिवाकृतिर्यस्य तद्भगवतो रूपं तस्य प्रदक्षिणावर्तकुण्डलीभूतदेहस्योत्तम्भितपुच्छस्य पुच्छाग्रे ध्रुवः स्थित इत्यर्थः

VP 2.9.2

सैष भ्रमन् भ्रामयति चन्द्रादित्य्-आदिकान् ग्रहान्।
भ्रमन्तम् अनु तं यान्ति नक्षत्राणि च चक्रवत्

Soorya, Chandra, and the stars, are all held together by Dhruva, with reins made of Vaayu.

Viṣṇu Purāṇa
सैष भ्रमन् भ्रामयति चन्द्रादित्यादिकान् ग्रहान् (saiṣa bhraman bhrāmayati candrādityādikān grahān) — Soorya;
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

saiṣa bhraman bhrāmayati candrādity-ādikān grahān | bhramantam anu taṃ yānti nakṣatrāṇi ca cakravat

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

सैष इति । सोऽचि लोपे चेत्पादपूरणम् इति सुलोपः । स एष ध्रुवः शिशुमारवशेन भ्रमन् ग्रहान् भ्रामयति । नक्षत्राणि च तं भ्रमन्तं ध्रुवमनुयान्ति

Śrīdhara commentary.

The commentary explains that Dhruva, situated in the form of Śiśumāra, causes the planets to revolve, while the stars follow his motion. Nārāyaṇa supports that cosmic form from within.

VP 2.9.3

सूराचन्द्रमसौ तारा नक्षत्राणि ग्रहैः सह।
वातानीकमयैर् बन्धैर् ध्रुवे बद्धानि तानि वै

the celestial luminaries are in fact bound to the polar-star by aerial cords. The porpoise-like figure of the celestial sphere is

Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sūrācandramasau tārā nakṣatrāṇi grahaiḥ saha | vātānīkamayair bandhair dhruve baddhāni tāni vai

tatra hetum āha—sūryācandramasāv iti । nakṣatrāṇyaśvinyādīni itarāstārāḥ budhādibhiḥ grahaiḥ saha vātasamūhamayairbandhaiḥ badhyante ebhiritibandhāḥ pāśāstairdhruve baddhāni vai yatastamanuyāntītyarthaḥ । vātāśca kūrmoktaḥ— āvahaḥ pravahaścaiva tathaivānuvahaḥ paraḥ । saṃvaho vivahaścaiva tadūrdhvaṃ syāt parāvahaḥ । tathā parivahaścorddhvaṃ vāyavaḥ sapta nemayaḥ iti ॥ sthānāni ca teṣāmuktāni— madhye bhū-meghayor vāyurāvahaḥ pravahastataḥ । meghabhāskarayoḥ sūryaśaśinorudvahaḥ sthitaḥ । candra-nakṣatragaṇayoḥ saṃvaho vivahastataḥ । nakṣatragṛhamadhyaratho grahasaptarṣimadhyagaḥ । parāvahaḥ parivahaḥ saptarṣi-dhruvamadhyagaḥ । ityete vāyavaḥ sapta vahanti bhuvanatrayam ॥ iti

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तत्र हेतुम् आह—सूर्याचन्द्रमसाव् इति । नक्षत्राण्यश्विन्यादीनि इतरास्ताराः बुधादिभिः ग्रहैः सह वातसमूहमयैर्बन्धैः बध्यन्ते एभिरितिबन्धाः पाशास्तैर्ध्रुवे बद्धानि वै यतस्तमनुयान्तीत्यर्थः । वाताश्च कूर्मोक्तः— आवहः प्रवहश्चैव तथैवानुवहः परः । संवहो विवहश्चैव तदूर्ध्वं स्यात् परावहः । तथा परिवहश्चोर्द्ध्वं वायवः सप्त नेमयः इति ॥ स्थानानि च तेषामुक्तानि— मध्ये भू-मेघयोर् वायुरावहः प्रवहस्ततः । मेघभास्करयोः सूर्यशशिनोरुद्वहः स्थितः । चन्द्र-नक्षत्रगणयोः संवहो विवहस्ततः । नक्षत्रगृहमध्यरथो ग्रहसप्तर्षिमध्यगः । परावहः परिवहः सप्तर्षि-ध्रुवमध्यगः । इत्येते वायवः सप्त वहन्ति भुवनत्रयम् ॥ इति

Śrīdhara commentary.

The sun, moon, stars beginning with Aśvinī, and the other planets are bound to Dhruva by bonds formed of the winds; they follow Dhruva. The seven winds are Āvaha, Pravaha, Anuvaha, Saṃvaha, Vivaha, Parāvaha, and Parivaha, and they carry the three worlds. Their stations lie successively between earth and clouds, clouds and sun and moon, moon and the stars, the stars and the planets, the planets and the Seven Sages, and the Seven Sages and Dhruva.

VP 2.9.8

Sun is supporting the whole world with the rains, which is the source of water, this will be told now. How he is supporting, I will tell you, O Maitreya. Because of rains, food is grown, and food is the basic support for the living beings in the world.

VP 2.9.9

विवस्वान् अंशुभिस् तीक्ष्णैर् आदाय जगतो जलम्।
सोमे मुञ्चत्य् अथेन्दुश् च वायु-नाडी-मयैर् दिवि।
नालैर् विक्षिपतेऽभ्रेषु धूमाग्न्य्-अनिल-मूर्तिषु

With his rays, which are very powerful, he sucks all the waters. He nurtures the moon and moon through the Vaayu naadis, through that path, he places all the waters in the clouds (one stage before it becomes rain), and has got three stages - like smoke or vapours, agni and anila. With his fierce rays, Sun collects waters from the world, and pours it on Soma. The Moon pours it in the sky, through the channel of Vaayu. He places it in the clouds. It has got three forms - dhooma, agni and anila; it is called abhra (clouds). Rain bearing clouds are called abhra here.

VP 2.9.10

VP 2.9.24

हृदि नारायणस् तस्य शिशुमारस्य संस्थितः।
बिभर्ता सर्व-भूतानाम् आदिभूतः सनातनः

Naaraayana is located at the heart position of the Shimshumaara chakra; heart is the support of the body. Naaraayana is present as antaryaami in everything. He controls everything. He is Sanaatana, Aadibhoota, Jagat kaarana, He is the primeval cause of the entire creation. He supports everything.
