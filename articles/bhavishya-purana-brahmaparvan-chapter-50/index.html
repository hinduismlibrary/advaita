Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 50 — Sun Worship, Offerings, and the Chariot Rite

BP 1.50.1

वासुदेव उवाच
नैमित्तिकं ततो वक्ष्ये यज्ज्ञात्वा च समासतः ।
सप्तम्यां ग्रहणे चैव संक्रान्तिषु विशेषतः ॥

Vāsudeva spoke. I shall now tell the occasional rite briefly, knowing which one may perform it on the seventh day, especially during eclipses and solar transitions.

BP 1.50.2

शुक्लपक्षस्य सप्तम्यां हविर्भुक्त्वैकदा दिवा ।
सम्यगाचम्य सन्ध्यायां वारुणं प्रणिपत्य च ॥

On the seventh day of the bright fortnight, one should eat sacrificial food once during the day, perform the purification correctly at twilight, and bow to Varuṇa.

BP 1.50.3

इन्द्रियाणि च संयम्य कृतं ध्यात्वा स्वपेदधः ।
दर्भशय्यागतो रात्रौ प्रातः स्नातः सुसंयतः ॥

Restraining the senses and reflecting on the deed, one should sleep on the ground on a bed of sacred grass at night, then bathe in the morning, well controlled.

BP 1.50.4

ततः सन्ध्यामुपास्याथ पूर्वोक्तं च मनुं जपेत् ।
जुहुयाच्च तदा वह्निं सूर्याग्नी परिकल्प्य च ॥

Then one should worship at twilight, recite the previously stated formula, and offer into the fire, setting up the Sun fire.

BP 1.50.5

सूर्याग्निकरणं वक्ष्ये तर्पणं च समासतः ।
अर्चनागारमुल्लिख्य प्रविश्यार्च्य जनैर्जनम् ॥

I shall briefly tell the kindling of the Sun fire and the water offering. Having marked out the worship place, one should enter and worship the source of beings with the people.

BP 1.50.6

प्रक्षिप्यास्तीर्य दर्भश्च पात्राद्यालभ्य च क्रमात् ।
पवित्रं द्विकुशं कृत्वा साग्रं प्रादेशसम्मितम् ॥

Having put down and spread the sacred grass, and having taken the vessels in order, one should make a purifier from two stalks of sacred grass, measuring one span with the tips intact.

BP 1.50.7

तेन पात्राणि सम्प्रोक्ष्य संशोध्याध विलोक्य च ।
उदगग्रे स्थिते पात्रे प्रज्वाल्याथोल्मुकेन च ॥

Having sprinkled the vessels with it, one should clean and inspect them. In a vessel placed facing north, one should then kindle the fire with a firebrand.

BP 1.50.8

पर्यग्निकरणं कृत्वा तथाज्योत्यवनं त्रिधा ।
परिमृज्य स्रुवादींश्र दर्भैः सम्प्रोक्षयेत्ततः ॥

Having made the fire enclosure and offered clarified butter three times, one should wipe the ladles and other things, then sprinkle them with sacred grass.

BP 1.50.9

जुहुयात्प्रोक्ष्य तान्वह्नौ तत्रार्कं पूर्ववद् व्रजेत् ।
अभूमौ स्थितपात्रेण विष्टरेण तु पाणिना ॥
दानेन यदुशार्दूल नान्तरिक्षे स्थले क्वचित् ॥

Having sprinkled them, one should offer them into the fire, then proceed to the Sun as before. The vessel should be set on a seat, not on bare ground, and the offering should never be made in open space.

BP 1.50.10

दक्षिणेन स्रुवं गृह्य जुहुयात्पावकं बुधः ।
हदयेन क्रियाः सर्वाः कर्तव्याः पूर्वचोदिताः ॥

The wise one should take the ladle in the right hand and offer to the fire. All the previously prescribed acts should be performed with the heart.

BP 1.50.11

अर्कादारभ्य संज्ञार्थं दद्यात्तूष्णीं हुतिं स्थितः ।
वरुणाय शतैर्माघे सप्तम्यां वरुणं यजेत् ॥

Beginning with the Sun, one should silently make an offering for the deity names. In Māgha, one should offer hundreds to Varuṇa and worship Varuṇa on the seventh day.

BP 1.50.12

यथाशक्त्या तु विप्रेभ्यः प्रदद्यात्खण्डवेष्टकान् ।
दद्याच्च दक्षिणां शक्त्या प्राप्नोति याचितं फलम् ॥

According to ability, one should give sugar covered cakes to the Brahmins and make a gift as one is able. One obtains the desired fruit.

BP 1.50.13

एवं वै फाल्गुने सूर्यं चैत्रे वैशाख एव च ।
वैशाखे मासि धातारमिन्द्रं ज्येष्ठे यजेद्रविम् ॥

Thus one should worship the Sun in Phālguna, Caitra, and Vaiśākha. In Vaiśākha one should worship Dhātā and Indra, and in Jyeṣṭha one should worship Ravi.

BP 1.50.14

आषाढे श्रावणे मासि नभं भाद्रपदे यमम् ।
तथाश्वयुजि पर्जन्यं त्वष्टारं कार्तिके यजेत् ॥

In Āṣāḍha one should worship Nabha, in Bhādrapada Yama, in Āśvayuja Parjanya, and in Kārttika Tvaṣṭṛ.

BP 1.50.15

मार्गशीर्षे च मित्रं च पौषे विष्णुं यजेद्यदि ।
संवत्सरेण यत्प्रोक्तं फलमिष्टं दिनेदिने ॥
तत्सर्वमाप्नुयात्क्षिप्रं भक्त्या श्रद्धान्वितो व्रती ॥

If one worships Mitra in Mārgaśīrṣa and Viṣṇu in Pauṣa, the desired fruit declared for the year is obtained each day. The vow keeper, filled with devotion and faith, quickly obtains it all.

BP 1.50.16

एवं संवत्सरे पूर्णे कृत्वा वै काञ्चनं रथम् ।
सप्तभिर्वाजिभिर्युक्तं नानारत्नोपशोभितम् ॥

When a full year has passed, one should make a golden chariot joined to seven horses and adorned with various jewels.

BP 1.50.17

आदित्यप्रतिमां मध्ये शुद्धहेम्ना कृतां शुभाम् ।
रत्नैरलङ्कृतां कृत्वा हेमपद्मोपरिस्थिताम् ॥

In its center one should place an auspicious image of the Sun made of pure gold, adorned with jewels and set upon a golden lotus.

BP 1.50.18

तस्मिन् रथवरे कृत्वा सारथिं चाग्रतः स्थितम् ।
वृतं द्वादशभिर्विप्रैः क्रमान्मासाधिपात्मभिः ॥

Having placed the charioteer at the front of that excellent chariot, one should surround it with twelve Brahmins, embodied as the lords of the months in order.

BP 1.50.19

मध्ये कृत्वा स्वमाचार्यं पूजयित्वा यथाश्रुतिः ।
सञ्चिन्त्यादित्यवर्णं वै वस्त्ररत्नादिनार्हयेत् ॥

Having placed ones own teacher in the middle and worshipped according to the teaching, one should contemplate the color of the Sun and honor him with garments, jewels, and the like.

BP 1.50.20

एवं मासाधिपान्विप्रान्सम्पूज्याथ निवेदयेत् ।
आचार्याय रथं छत्रं ग्रामं गाश्च महीं शुभाम् ॥

Having thus fully worshipped the Brahmins who are lords of the months, one should offer to the teacher a chariot, an umbrella, a village, cows, and good land.

BP 1.50.21

अश्वान्मासाधिपेभ्यस्तु द्वादशभ्यो निवेदयेत् ।
एवं भक्त्या यथाशक्त्या हेमरत्नादिभूषणम् ॥

One should offer horses to the twelve lords of the months. With devotion and according to ability, one should also give ornaments of gold, jewels, and the like.

BP 1.50.22

दत्त्वा तस्य नमस्कृत्य व्रतं पूर्णं निवेदयेत् ।
अत ऊर्ध्वं न दोषोऽत्र व्रतस्याकरणेष्वपि ॥

Having given these and bowed to him, one should declare the vow complete. After this, there is no fault even if the vow is not performed.

BP 1.50.23

एवमस्त्विति विप्रेन्द्रैः सहाचार्यः पुनः पुनः ।
बह्वीश्चैवाशिषो दत्त्वा प्रवदेत्प्रीयतामिति ॥

Together with the foremost Brahmins, the teacher should say again and again, So be it, and give many blessings, saying, May he be pleased.

BP 1.50.24

आदित्यो येन कामेन त्वया आराधितो व्रतैः ।
तुभ्यं ददातु तं कामं सम्पूर्णं भवतु व्रतम् ॥

May the Sun grant you the desire for which you worshipped him with vows. May that desire be fulfilled for you, and may your vow be complete.

BP 1.50.25

आचार्यान्विप्ररूपैस्तु प्रविष्टो भास्करः स्वयम् ।
दास्यत्येव परं कर्तुमित्युक्तं भानुना स्वयम् ॥

Bhāskara himself, having entered in the form of teachers who are Brahmins, will grant the highest result. Thus the Sun himself has spoken.

BP 1.50.26

विप्रेभ्यो गुणवद्भ्यश्च निस्वेभ्यश्च विशेषतः ।
दीनान्धकृपणेभ्यश्च शक्त्या दत्त्वा च दक्षिणाम् ॥
ब्राह्मणान्भोजयित्वा च व्रतमेतत्समापयेत् ॥

Having given gifts according to ability to virtuous Brahmins, especially to the poor, the blind, and the needy, and having fed the Brahmins, one should complete this vow.

BP 1.50.27

कृत्वैवं सप्तमीमब्दं राजा भवति धार्मिकः ।
पुरुषः स्त्री भवेद्राज्ञां तादृशामथ वल्लभा ॥

Having performed the seventh day rite for a year, one becomes a righteous king. A man or woman becomes a favorite of kings of that kind.

BP 1.50.28

शतयोजनविस्तीर्णं निःसपत्नमकण्टकम् ।
निष्पन्नमण्डलं भुङ्क्ते साग्रं वर्षशतं सुखी ॥

Happy, one enjoys a completed realm one hundred distance units wide, without enemies and without thorns, for a full hundred years.

BP 1.50.29

वित्तहीनोऽपि यो भक्त्या कृत्त्वा ताम्रमयं रथम् ।
दद्याद्व्रतावसाने तु कृत्वा सर्वं यथोदितम् ॥
सोऽशीतियोजनं भुङ्क्ते विस्तीर्णं मण्डलं नृपः ॥

Even one who is poor in wealth, if devoted, should make a copper chariot and give it at the end of the vow, doing everything as stated. As a king, he enjoys a realm eighty distance units wide.

BP 1.50.30

एवं पिष्टमयं योऽपि वित्तहीनः करोति ना ।
आषष्टियोजनं भुङ्क्ते दीर्घायुर्नीरुजः सुखी ॥
सूर्यलोके च कल्पान्तं यावत्स्थित्वेदमाप्नुयात् ॥

Likewise, a poor person who makes one from flour enjoys a realm sixty distance units wide, long lived, free from illness, and happy. He obtains this and remains in the Sun world until the end of the age.

BP 1.50.31

मनसापि च यो भक्त्या यजेदर्कमतन्द्रितः ।
सर्वावस्थासु सोऽप्यत्र व्याधिभिर्मुच्यते भृशम् ॥

Even one who worships the Sun with devotion in the mind, without negligence, is greatly freed from diseases in every condition.

BP 1.50.32

आपदो न स्पृशन्त्येनं नीहारा इव भास्करम् ।
किं पुनर्व्रतसम्पन्नं भक्तं मन्त्रैश्च रक्षितम् ॥

Afflictions do not touch him, just as mist does not touch the Sun. How much less one endowed with the vow and protected by ritual formulas.

BP 1.50.33

यत एवं ततो ज्ञात्वा विधानं कल्पचोदितम् ।
सुखेन फलसिद्ध्यर्थं कुर्यात्सर्वमशेषतः ॥

Knowing that the procedure is prescribed in the ritual texts, one should do everything completely for the easy attainment of the fruit.

BP 1.50.34

इत्येतत्कथितं साम्ब पुरा सूर्येण मे शुभम् ।
कल्पोऽयं प्रथमे कल्पे सर्वदा गोपितो मया ॥

Thus this auspicious ritual was told to me long ago by the Sun, O Sāmba. I have always kept this procedure protected from the first age.

BP 1.50.35

अनेन विधिना वत्स विशुद्धेनान्तरात्मना ।
भानुमाराधयेत्क्षिप्रं यदीच्छेत्फलमुत्तमम् ॥

By this procedure, O child, with a purified inner self, one should quickly worship the Sun if one desires the highest fruit.

BP 1.50.36

मयास्यैव प्रसादेन प्राप्ताः पुत्राः सहस्रशः ।
असुरा निर्जिताश्चैव सुराः सर्वे वशीकृताः ॥

By his favor I obtained thousands of sons, the demons were defeated, and all the gods were brought under control.

BP 1.50.37

त्वयाप्ययं गोपितव्यः कल्पः सूर्यस्य सम्मतः ।
प्रसादादस्य कल्पस्य सदा सन्निहितो रविः ॥
चक्रेऽस्मिन्निर्जिता येन सुरा सुरनरोरगाः ॥

You too should preserve this rule approved by the Sun. By its favor Ravi is always present, and by this wheel he conquered gods, humans, and serpents.

BP 1.50.38

यदिनाधिष्ठितं चक्रमिदं सूर्यांशुभिः स्वयम् ।
सततं स्यात्प्रभायुक्तं कथमध्याहतं भवेत् ॥

If this wheel were not set in motion by the rays of the Sun himself, how would it remain constantly radiant, and how could it be struck in the middle.

BP 1.50.39

अहमेतं जपन्नित्यं यजन्ध्यायंश्च शक्तितः ।
जातोऽस्मि सर्वकामानां पूज्यः श्रेष्ठश्च तेजसा ।

Chanting this always, sacrificing and meditating according to my ability, I became worthy of worship and foremost in splendor, fulfilling all desires.

BP 1.50.40

त्वमभ्यस्यैव मनसा वाचा वा कर्मणापि वा ।
कुरु भक्तिमनेनैव विधिना फलसिद्धये ॥

You too, by mind, speech, or action, should practice devotion by this very procedure for the attainment of the fruit.

BP 1.50.41

शृणुयाद्भक्तियुक्तो यो नरः श्रद्धासमन्वितः ।
विधानमादितः पुत्र सप्तमीं कुरुते च यः ॥

Whoever hears this with devotion and faith, O child, and performs the seventh day rite from the beginning.

BP 1.50.42

सेह प्राप्याऽखिलं काममारोग्यं च जयं तथा ।
भार्गव्या परया युक्तो गच्छेद्वैरोचनं सदः ॥

He obtains every desire, health, and victory here, and joined with the supreme Bhārgavī, he goes to the assembly of Vairocana.
