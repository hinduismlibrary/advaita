Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 50 — Sun Worship, Offerings, and the Chariot Rite

BP 1.50.1

वासुदेव उवाच
नैमित्तिकं ततो वक्ष्ये यज्ज्ञात्वा च समासतः ।
सप्तम्यां ग्रहणे चैव संक्रान्तिषु विशेषतः ॥

Vāsudeva spoke. I shall now tell the occasional rite briefly, knowing which one may perform it on the seventh day, especially during eclipses and solar transitions.

वासुदेव उवाच (vāsudeva uvāca) — Vāsudeva said;
नैमित्तिकं ततो वक्ष्ये यज्ज्ञात्वा च समासतः (naimittikaṃ tato vakṣye yajjñātvā ca samāsataḥ) — I shall then describe the occasional, and having known it in brief;
सप्तम्यां ग्रहणे चैव संक्रान्तिषु विशेषतः (saptamyāṃ grahaṇe caiva saṃkrāntiṣu viśeṣataḥ) — Especially on the seventh day of the eclipse and on the conjunctions

BP 1.50.2

शुक्लपक्षस्य सप्तम्यां हविर्भुक्त्वैकदा दिवा ।
सम्यगाचम्य सन्ध्यायां वारुणं प्रणिपत्य च ॥

On the seventh day of the bright fortnight, one should eat sacrificial food once during the day, perform the purification correctly at twilight, and bow to Varuṇa.

शुक्लपक्षस्य सप्तम्यां हविर्भुक्त्वैकदा दिवा (śuklapakṣasya saptamyāṃ havirbhuktvaikadā divā) — On the seventh day of the white fortnight he offered oblations once during the day;
सम्यगाचम्य सन्ध्यायां वारुणं प्रणिपत्य च (samyagācamya sandhyāyāṃ vāruṇaṃ praṇipatya ca) — After taking a thorough bath in the evening he offered obeisances to Varuna

BP 1.50.3

इन्द्रियाणि च संयम्य कृतं ध्यात्वा स्वपेदधः ।
दर्भशय्यागतो रात्रौ प्रातः स्नातः सुसंयतः ॥

Restraining the senses and reflecting on the deed, one should sleep on the ground on a bed of sacred grass at night, then bathe in the morning, well controlled.

इन्द्रियाणि च संयम्य कृतं ध्यात्वा स्वपेदधः (indriyāṇi ca saṃyamya kṛtaṃ dhyātvā svapedadhaḥ) — And restraining the senses, he meditated on what he had done and slept down;
दर्भशय्यागतो रात्रौ प्रातः स्नातः सुसंयतः (darbhaśayyāgato rātrau prātaḥ snātaḥ susaṃyataḥ) — He lay on a bed of straw at night and took a bath in the morning with great self-control

BP 1.50.4

ततः सन्ध्यामुपास्याथ पूर्वोक्तं च मनुं जपेत् ।
जुहुयाच्च तदा वह्निं सूर्याग्नी परिकल्प्य च ॥

Then one should worship at twilight, recite the previously stated formula, and offer into the fire, setting up the Sun fire.

ततः सन्ध्यामुपास्याथ पूर्वोक्तं च मनुं जपेत् (tataḥ sandhyāmupāsyātha pūrvoktaṃ ca manuṃ japet) — Then one should worship the evening and chant the Manu mentioned earlier;
जुहुयाच्च तदा वह्निं सूर्याग्नी परिकल्प्य च (juhuyācca tadā vahniṃ sūryāgnī parikalpya ca) — Then he should offer sacrifice to the fire and arrange the fire of the sun

BP 1.50.5

सूर्याग्निकरणं वक्ष्ये तर्पणं च समासतः ।
अर्चनागारमुल्लिख्य प्रविश्यार्च्य जनैर्जनम् ॥

I shall briefly tell the kindling of the Sun fire and the water offering. Having marked out the worship place, one should enter and worship the source of beings with the people.

सूर्याग्निकरणं वक्ष्ये तर्पणं च समासतः (sūryāgnikaraṇaṃ vakṣye tarpaṇaṃ ca samāsataḥ) — I will tell you about the sun-burning and the tarpan in brief;
अर्चनागारमुल्लिख्य प्रविश्यार्च्य जनैर्जनम् (arcanāgāramullikhya praviśyārcya janairjanam) — He entered the worship hall and worshiped the people

BP 1.50.6

प्रक्षिप्यास्तीर्य दर्भश्च पात्राद्यालभ्य च क्रमात् ।
पवित्रं द्विकुशं कृत्वा साग्रं प्रादेशसम्मितम् ॥

Having put down and spread the sacred grass, and having taken the vessels in order, one should make a purifier from two stalks of sacred grass, measuring one span with the tips intact.

प्रक्षिप्यास्तीर्य दर्भश्च पात्राद्यालभ्य च क्रमात् (prakṣipyāstīrya darbhaśca pātrādyālabhya ca kramāt) — Throwing and spreading the straw and getting the vessels and other things in order;
पवित्रं द्विकुशं कृत्वा साग्रं प्रादेशसम्मितम् (pavitraṃ dvikuśaṃ kṛtvā sāgraṃ prādeśasammitam) — Make a sacred dvikusha and measure the whole area

BP 1.50.7

तेन पात्राणि सम्प्रोक्ष्य संशोध्याध विलोक्य च ।
उदगग्रे स्थिते पात्रे प्रज्वाल्याथोल्मुकेन च ॥

Having sprinkled the vessels with it, one should clean and inspect them. In a vessel placed facing north, one should then kindle the fire with a firebrand.

तेन पात्राणि सम्प्रोक्ष्य संशोध्याध विलोक्य च (tena pātrāṇi samprokṣya saṃśodhyādha vilokya ca) — Sprinkle the vessels with it and clean it and look down;
उदगग्रे स्थिते पात्रे प्रज्वाल्याथोल्मुकेन च (udagagre sthite pātre prajvālyātholmukena ca) — Light a torch in a vessel placed at the east end

BP 1.50.8

पर्यग्निकरणं कृत्वा तथाज्योत्यवनं त्रिधा ।
परिमृज्य स्रुवादींश्र दर्भैः सम्प्रोक्षयेत्ततः ॥

Having made the fire enclosure and offered clarified butter three times, one should wipe the ladles and other things, then sprinkle them with sacred grass.

पर्यग्निकरणं कृत्वा तथाज्योत्यवनं त्रिधा (paryagnikaraṇaṃ kṛtvā tathājyotyavanaṃ tridhā) — After setting fire to the forest of lights he divided it into three parts;
परिमृज्य स्रुवादींश्र दर्भैः सम्प्रोक्षयेत्ततः (parimṛjya sruvādīṃśra darbhaiḥ samprokṣayettataḥ) — Wiping the bowls and other objects, one should then sprinkle them with the darbha grass

BP 1.50.9

जुहुयात्प्रोक्ष्य तान्वह्नौ तत्रार्कं पूर्ववद् व्रजेत् ।
अभूमौ स्थितपात्रेण विष्टरेण तु पाणिना ॥
दानेन यदुशार्दूल नान्तरिक्षे स्थले क्वचित् ॥

Having sprinkled them, one should offer them into the fire, then proceed to the Sun as before. The vessel should be set on a seat, not on bare ground, and the offering should never be made in open space.

जुहुयात्प्रोक्ष्य तान्वह्नौ तत्रार्कं पूर्ववद् व्रजेत् (juhuyātprokṣya tānvahnau tatrārkaṃ pūrvavad vrajet) — One should offer oblations and sprinkle them on the fire and there the sun should go as before;
अभूमौ स्थितपात्रेण विष्टरेण तु पाणिना (abhūmau sthitapātreṇa viṣṭareṇa tu pāṇinā) — With a vessel standing on the ground and a mattress in his hand;
दानेन यदुशार्दूल नान्तरिक्षे स्थले क्वचित् (dānena yaduśārdūla nāntarikṣe sthale kvacit) — By charity, O tiger of the Yadus, nowhere in space or on earth

BP 1.50.10

दक्षिणेन स्रुवं गृह्य जुहुयात्पावकं बुधः ।
हदयेन क्रियाः सर्वाः कर्तव्याः पूर्वचोदिताः ॥

The wise one should take the ladle in the right hand and offer to the fire. All the previously prescribed acts should be performed with the heart.

दक्षिणेन स्रुवं गृह्य जुहुयात्पावकं बुधः (dakṣiṇena sruvaṃ gṛhya juhuyātpāvakaṃ budhaḥ) — Mercury should take the ladle with his right hand and offer the fire;
हदयेन क्रियाः सर्वाः कर्तव्याः पूर्वचोदिताः (hadayena kriyāḥ sarvāḥ kartavyāḥ pūrvacoditāḥ) — All actions should be done with the heart, pre-inspired

BP 1.50.11

अर्कादारभ्य संज्ञार्थं दद्यात्तूष्णीं हुतिं स्थितः ।
वरुणाय शतैर्माघे सप्तम्यां वरुणं यजेत् ॥

Beginning with the Sun, one should silently make an offering for the deity names. In Māgha, one should offer hundreds to Varuṇa and worship Varuṇa on the seventh day.

अर्कादारभ्य संज्ञार्थं दद्यात्तूष्णीं हुतिं स्थितः (arkādārabhya saṃjñārthaṃ dadyāttūṣṇīṃ hutiṃ sthitaḥ) — Starting from the sun, he should offer silent offerings for the sake of notion;
वरुणाय शतैर्माघे सप्तम्यां वरुणं यजेत् (varuṇāya śatairmāghe saptamyāṃ varuṇaṃ yajet) — One should offer hundreds of offerings to Varuna on the seventh day of Magha

BP 1.50.12

यथाशक्त्या तु विप्रेभ्यः प्रदद्यात्खण्डवेष्टकान् ।
दद्याच्च दक्षिणां शक्त्या प्राप्नोति याचितं फलम् ॥

According to ability, one should give sugar covered cakes to the Brahmins and make a gift as one is able. One obtains the desired fruit.

यथाशक्त्या तु विप्रेभ्यः प्रदद्यात्खण्डवेष्टकान् (yathāśaktyā tu viprebhyaḥ pradadyātkhaṇḍaveṣṭakān) — He should give pieces of wrappers to the brahmins according to his ability;
दद्याच्च दक्षिणां शक्त्या प्राप्नोति याचितं फलम् (dadyācca dakṣiṇāṃ śaktyā prāpnoti yācitaṃ phalam) — He who gives alms according to his ability obtains the requested fruit

BP 1.50.13

एवं वै फाल्गुने सूर्यं चैत्रे वैशाख एव च ।
वैशाखे मासि धातारमिन्द्रं ज्येष्ठे यजेद्रविम् ॥

Thus one should worship the Sun in Phālguna, Caitra, and Vaiśākha. In Vaiśākha one should worship Dhātā and Indra, and in Jyeṣṭha one should worship Ravi.

एवं वै फाल्गुने सूर्यं चैत्रे वैशाख एव च (evaṃ vai phālgune sūryaṃ caitre vaiśākha eva ca) — Thus the Sun in Phalguna, Chaitra and Vaishakha;
वैशाखे मासि धातारमिन्द्रं ज्येष्ठे यजेद्रविम् (vaiśākhe māsi dhātāramindraṃ jyeṣṭhe yajedravim) — In the month of Vaishakha one should worship Indra, the creator, and in the month of Jyeshta, the sun

BP 1.50.14

आषाढे श्रावणे मासि नभं भाद्रपदे यमम् ।
तथाश्वयुजि पर्जन्यं त्वष्टारं कार्तिके यजेत् ॥

In Āṣāḍha one should worship Nabha, in Bhādrapada Yama, in Āśvayuja Parjanya, and in Kārttika Tvaṣṭṛ.

आषाढे श्रावणे मासि नभं भाद्रपदे यमम् (āṣāḍhe śrāvaṇe māsi nabhaṃ bhādrapade yamam) — Nabha in the month of Ashadha in the month of Sravana and Yama in the month of Bhādrapada;
तथाश्वयुजि पर्जन्यं त्वष्टारं कार्तिके यजेत् (tathāśvayuji parjanyaṃ tvaṣṭāraṃ kārtike yajet) — Similarly, in the Ashwa Yuga, one should worship Parjanya and Tvashta in Kartika

BP 1.50.15

मार्गशीर्षे च मित्रं च पौषे विष्णुं यजेद्यदि ।
संवत्सरेण यत्प्रोक्तं फलमिष्टं दिनेदिने ॥
तत्सर्वमाप्नुयात्क्षिप्रं भक्त्या श्रद्धान्वितो व्रती ॥

If one worships Mitra in Mārgaśīrṣa and Viṣṇu in Pauṣa, the desired fruit declared for the year is obtained each day. The vow keeper, filled with devotion and faith, quickly obtains it all.

मार्गशीर्षे च मित्रं च पौषे विष्णुं यजेद्यदि (mārgaśīrṣe ca mitraṃ ca pauṣe viṣṇuṃ yajedyadi) — If one worships Vishnu in the month of Margashirsa and Mitra in the month of Pausa;
संवत्सरेण यत्प्रोक्तं फलमिष्टं दिनेदिने (saṃvatsareṇa yatproktaṃ phalamiṣṭaṃ dinedine) — The fruits mentioned by the year are desired day by day;
तत्सर्वमाप्नुयात्क्षिप्रं भक्त्या श्रद्धान्वितो व्रती (tatsarvamāpnuyātkṣipraṃ bhaktyā śraddhānvito vratī) — A vow-keeper with devotion and faith will soon attain all that

BP 1.50.16

एवं संवत्सरे पूर्णे कृत्वा वै काञ्चनं रथम् ।
सप्तभिर्वाजिभिर्युक्तं नानारत्नोपशोभितम् ॥

When a full year has passed, one should make a golden chariot joined to seven horses and adorned with various jewels.

एवं संवत्सरे पूर्णे कृत्वा वै काञ्चनं रथम् (evaṃ saṃvatsare pūrṇe kṛtvā vai kāñcanaṃ ratham) — Thus, after a full year, he made a golden chariot;
सप्तभिर्वाजिभिर्युक्तं नानारत्नोपशोभितम् (saptabhirvājibhiryuktaṃ nānāratnopaśobhitam) — It was adorned with seven horses and various gems

BP 1.50.17

आदित्यप्रतिमां मध्ये शुद्धहेम्ना कृतां शुभाम् ।
रत्नैरलङ्कृतां कृत्वा हेमपद्मोपरिस्थिताम् ॥

In its center one should place an auspicious image of the Sun made of pure gold, adorned with jewels and set upon a golden lotus.

आदित्यप्रतिमां मध्ये शुद्धहेम्ना कृतां शुभाम् (ādityapratimāṃ madhye śuddhahemnā kṛtāṃ śubhām) — It was auspicious in the middle of an image of the sun made of pure gold;
रत्नैरलङ्कृतां कृत्वा हेमपद्मोपरिस्थिताम् (ratnairalaṅkṛtāṃ kṛtvā hemapadmoparisthitām) — She was adorned with gems and surrounded by golden lotuses

BP 1.50.18

तस्मिन् रथवरे कृत्वा सारथिं चाग्रतः स्थितम् ।
वृतं द्वादशभिर्विप्रैः क्रमान्मासाधिपात्मभिः ॥

Having placed the charioteer at the front of that excellent chariot, one should surround it with twelve Brahmins, embodied as the lords of the months in order.

तस्मिन् रथवरे कृत्वा सारथिं चाग्रतः स्थितम् (tasmin rathavare kṛtvā sārathiṃ cāgrataḥ sthitam) — He placed the charioteer in the best of the chariots and stood in front of him;
वृतं द्वादशभिर्विप्रैः क्रमान्मासाधिपात्मभिः (vṛtaṃ dvādaśabhirvipraiḥ kramānmāsādhipātmabhiḥ) — It was surrounded by twelve brahmins who were the masters of the months in order

BP 1.50.19

मध्ये कृत्वा स्वमाचार्यं पूजयित्वा यथाश्रुतिः ।
सञ्चिन्त्यादित्यवर्णं वै वस्त्ररत्नादिनार्हयेत् ॥

Having placed ones own teacher in the middle and worshipped according to the teaching, one should contemplate the color of the Sun and honor him with garments, jewels, and the like.

मध्ये कृत्वा स्वमाचार्यं पूजयित्वा यथाश्रुतिः (madhye kṛtvā svamācāryaṃ pūjayitvā yathāśrutiḥ) — Having placed him in the middle he worshiped his teacher according to the scriptures;
सञ्चिन्त्यादित्यवर्णं वै वस्त्ररत्नादिनार्हयेत् (sañcintyādityavarṇaṃ vai vastraratnādinārhayet) — One should think of the color of the sun and adorn it with clothes and gems

BP 1.50.20

एवं मासाधिपान्विप्रान्सम्पूज्याथ निवेदयेत् ।
आचार्याय रथं छत्रं ग्रामं गाश्च महीं शुभाम् ॥

Having thus fully worshipped the Brahmins who are lords of the months, one should offer to the teacher a chariot, an umbrella, a village, cows, and good land.

एवं मासाधिपान्विप्रान्सम्पूज्याथ निवेदयेत् (evaṃ māsādhipānviprānsampūjyātha nivedayet) — Thus one should worship the brahmins who are the masters of the months and offer them;
आचार्याय रथं छत्रं ग्रामं गाश्च महीं शुभाम् (ācāryāya rathaṃ chatraṃ grāmaṃ gāśca mahīṃ śubhām) — A chariot an umbrella a village cows and auspicious land for the teacher

BP 1.50.21

अश्वान्मासाधिपेभ्यस्तु द्वादशभ्यो निवेदयेत् ।
एवं भक्त्या यथाशक्त्या हेमरत्नादिभूषणम् ॥

One should offer horses to the twelve lords of the months. With devotion and according to ability, one should also give ornaments of gold, jewels, and the like.

अश्वान्मासाधिपेभ्यस्तु द्वादशभ्यो निवेदयेत् (aśvānmāsādhipebhyastu dvādaśabhyo nivedayet) — The horses should be offered to the twelve governors of the month;
एवं भक्त्या यथाशक्त्या हेमरत्नादिभूषणम् (evaṃ bhaktyā yathāśaktyā hemaratnādibhūṣaṇam) — Thus with devotion as much as possible, gold, gems and other ornaments

BP 1.50.22

दत्त्वा तस्य नमस्कृत्य व्रतं पूर्णं निवेदयेत् ।
अत ऊर्ध्वं न दोषोऽत्र व्रतस्याकरणेष्वपि ॥

Having given these and bowed to him, one should declare the vow complete. After this, there is no fault even if the vow is not performed.

दत्त्वा तस्य नमस्कृत्य व्रतं पूर्णं निवेदयेत् (dattvā tasya namaskṛtya vrataṃ pūrṇaṃ nivedayet) — Giving it, one should offer obeisances to him and offer him the complete vow;
अत ऊर्ध्वं न दोषोऽत्र व्रतस्याकरणेष्वपि (ata ūrdhvaṃ na doṣo'tra vratasyākaraṇeṣvapi) — From this point onwards there is nothing wrong with observing the vow

BP 1.50.23

एवमस्त्विति विप्रेन्द्रैः सहाचार्यः पुनः पुनः ।
बह्वीश्चैवाशिषो दत्त्वा प्रवदेत्प्रीयतामिति ॥

Together with the foremost Brahmins, the teacher should say again and again, So be it, and give many blessings, saying, May he be pleased.

एवमस्त्विति विप्रेन्द्रैः सहाचार्यः पुनः पुनः (evamastviti viprendraiḥ sahācāryaḥ punaḥ punaḥ) — The teacher along with the great brahmins repeatedly said 'Let it be so';
बह्वीश्चैवाशिषो दत्त्वा प्रवदेत्प्रीयतामिति (bahvīścaivāśiṣo dattvā pravadetprīyatāmiti) — He should also give many blessings and say, 'Please be pleased

BP 1.50.24

आदित्यो येन कामेन त्वया आराधितो व्रतैः ।
तुभ्यं ददातु तं कामं सम्पूर्णं भवतु व्रतम् ॥

May the Sun grant you the desire for which you worshipped him with vows. May that desire be fulfilled for you, and may your vow be complete.

आदित्यो येन कामेन त्वया आराधितो व्रतैः (ādityo yena kāmena tvayā ārādhito vrataiḥ) — The desire with which you have worshiped the sun by your vows;
तुभ्यं ददातु तं कामं सम्पूर्णं भवतु व्रतम् (tubhyaṃ dadātu taṃ kāmaṃ sampūrṇaṃ bhavatu vratam) — May he grant you that desire and may your vow be fulfilled

BP 1.50.25

आचार्यान्विप्ररूपैस्तु प्रविष्टो भास्करः स्वयम् ।
दास्यत्येव परं कर्तुमित्युक्तं भानुना स्वयम् ॥

Bhāskara himself, having entered in the form of teachers who are Brahmins, will grant the highest result. Thus the Sun himself has spoken.

आचार्यान्विप्ररूपैस्तु प्रविष्टो भास्करः स्वयम् (ācāryānviprarūpaistu praviṣṭo bhāskaraḥ svayam) — The Sun himself entered the teachers in the form of brahmins;
दास्यत्येव परं कर्तुमित्युक्तं भानुना स्वयम् (dāsyatyeva paraṃ kartumityuktaṃ bhānunā svayam) — Bhanu himself said that he would give it to him to do the next

BP 1.50.26

विप्रेभ्यो गुणवद्भ्यश्च निस्वेभ्यश्च विशेषतः ।
दीनान्धकृपणेभ्यश्च शक्त्या दत्त्वा च दक्षिणाम् ॥
ब्राह्मणान्भोजयित्वा च व्रतमेतत्समापयेत् ॥

Having given gifts according to ability to virtuous Brahmins, especially to the poor, the blind, and the needy, and having fed the Brahmins, one should complete this vow.

विप्रेभ्यो गुणवद्भ्यश्च निस्वेभ्यश्च विशेषतः (viprebhyo guṇavadbhyaśca nisvebhyaśca viśeṣataḥ) — Especially to the virtuous and selfless brahmins;
दीनान्धकृपणेभ्यश्च शक्त्या दत्त्वा च दक्षिणाम् (dīnāndhakṛpaṇebhyaśca śaktyā dattvā ca dakṣiṇām) — Giving alms according to one's ability to the poor blind and miserable;
ब्राह्मणान्भोजयित्वा च व्रतमेतत्समापयेत् (brāhmaṇānbhojayitvā ca vratametatsamāpayet) — After feeding the brahmins one should complete this vow

BP 1.50.27

कृत्वैवं सप्तमीमब्दं राजा भवति धार्मिकः ।
पुरुषः स्त्री भवेद्राज्ञां तादृशामथ वल्लभा ॥

Having performed the seventh day rite for a year, one becomes a righteous king. A man or woman becomes a favorite of kings of that kind.

कृत्वैवं सप्तमीमब्दं राजा भवति धार्मिकः (kṛtvaivaṃ saptamīmabdaṃ rājā bhavati dhārmikaḥ) — Having done this for the seventh year the king becomes righteous;
पुरुषः स्त्री भवेद्राज्ञां तादृशामथ वल्लभा (puruṣaḥ strī bhavedrājñāṃ tādṛśāmatha vallabhā) — A man and a woman should be the beloved of kings

BP 1.50.28

शतयोजनविस्तीर्णं निःसपत्नमकण्टकम् ।
निष्पन्नमण्डलं भुङ्क्ते साग्रं वर्षशतं सुखी ॥

Happy, one enjoys a completed realm one hundred distance units wide, without enemies and without thorns, for a full hundred years.

शतयोजनविस्तीर्णं निःसपत्नमकण्टकम् (śatayojanavistīrṇaṃ niḥsapatnamakaṇṭakam) — It was a hundred yojanas wide without rivals and without thorns;
निष्पन्नमण्डलं भुङ्क्ते साग्रं वर्षशतं सुखी (niṣpannamaṇḍalaṃ bhuṅkte sāgraṃ varṣaśataṃ sukhī) — He enjoys the resulting circle for a whole hundred years happily

BP 1.50.29

वित्तहीनोऽपि यो भक्त्या कृत्त्वा ताम्रमयं रथम् ।
दद्याद्व्रतावसाने तु कृत्वा सर्वं यथोदितम् ॥
सोऽशीतियोजनं भुङ्क्ते विस्तीर्णं मण्डलं नृपः ॥

Even one who is poor in wealth, if devoted, should make a copper chariot and give it at the end of the vow, doing everything as stated. As a king, he enjoys a realm eighty distance units wide.

वित्तहीनोऽपि यो भक्त्या कृत्त्वा ताम्रमयं रथम् (vittahīno'pi yo bhaktyā kṛttvā tāmramayaṃ ratham) — He who, though poor in wealth, made a copper chariot with devotion;
दद्याद्व्रतावसाने तु कृत्वा सर्वं यथोदितम् (dadyādvratāvasāne tu kṛtvā sarvaṃ yathoditam) — At the end of the vow one should give charity after performing everything as mentioned above;
सोऽशीतियोजनं भुङ्क्ते विस्तीर्णं मण्डलं नृपः (so'śītiyojanaṃ bhuṅkte vistīrṇaṃ maṇḍalaṃ nṛpaḥ) — That king enjoys a vast circle eighty yojanas in length

BP 1.50.30

एवं पिष्टमयं योऽपि वित्तहीनः करोति ना ।
आषष्टियोजनं भुङ्क्ते दीर्घायुर्नीरुजः सुखी ॥
सूर्यलोके च कल्पान्तं यावत्स्थित्वेदमाप्नुयात् ॥

Likewise, a poor person who makes one from flour enjoys a realm sixty distance units wide, long lived, free from illness, and happy. He obtains this and remains in the Sun world until the end of the age.

एवं पिष्टमयं योऽपि वित्तहीनः करोति ना (evaṃ piṣṭamayaṃ yo'pi vittahīnaḥ karoti nā) — Thus, no one who is poor makes dough;
आषष्टियोजनं भुङ्क्ते दीर्घायुर्नीरुजः सुखी (āṣaṣṭiyojanaṃ bhuṅkte dīrghāyurnīrujaḥ sukhī) — He enjoys sixty yojanas, lives a long life, is sick and happy;
सूर्यलोके च कल्पान्तं यावत्स्थित्वेदमाप्नुयात् (sūryaloke ca kalpāntaṃ yāvatsthitvedamāpnuyāt) — And he shall remain in the world of the sun till the end of the Kalpa and attain this

BP 1.50.31

मनसापि च यो भक्त्या यजेदर्कमतन्द्रितः ।
सर्वावस्थासु सोऽप्यत्र व्याधिभिर्मुच्यते भृशम् ॥

Even one who worships the Sun with devotion in the mind, without negligence, is greatly freed from diseases in every condition.

मनसापि च यो भक्त्या यजेदर्कमतन्द्रितः (manasāpi ca yo bhaktyā yajedarkamatandritaḥ) — And he who worships the sun with devotion even with his mind without laziness;
सर्वावस्थासु सोऽप्यत्र व्याधिभिर्मुच्यते भृशम् (sarvāvasthāsu so'pyatra vyādhibhirmucyate bhṛśam) — In all circumstances he too is greatly freed from diseases here

BP 1.50.32

आपदो न स्पृशन्त्येनं नीहारा इव भास्करम् ।
किं पुनर्व्रतसम्पन्नं भक्तं मन्त्रैश्च रक्षितम् ॥

Afflictions do not touch him, just as mist does not touch the Sun. How much less one endowed with the vow and protected by ritual formulas.

आपदो न स्पृशन्त्येनं नीहारा इव भास्करम् (āpado na spṛśantyenaṃ nīhārā iva bhāskaram) — Disasters do not touch him like fog does not touch the sun;
किं पुनर्व्रतसम्पन्नं भक्तं मन्त्रैश्च रक्षितम् (kiṃ punarvratasampannaṃ bhaktaṃ mantraiśca rakṣitam) — What to speak of a devotee endowed with vows and protected by mantras

BP 1.50.33

यत एवं ततो ज्ञात्वा विधानं कल्पचोदितम् ।
सुखेन फलसिद्ध्यर्थं कुर्यात्सर्वमशेषतः ॥

Knowing that the procedure is prescribed in the ritual texts, one should do everything completely for the easy attainment of the fruit.

यत एवं ततो ज्ञात्वा विधानं कल्पचोदितम् (yata evaṃ tato jñātvā vidhānaṃ kalpacoditam) — Because it is so, then knowing the prescription is inspired by the Kalpa;
सुखेन फलसिद्ध्यर्थं कुर्यात्सर्वमशेषतः (sukhena phalasiddhyarthaṃ kuryātsarvamaśeṣataḥ) — One should do everything in full for the attainment of the fruits happily

BP 1.50.34

इत्येतत्कथितं साम्ब पुरा सूर्येण मे शुभम् ।
कल्पोऽयं प्रथमे कल्पे सर्वदा गोपितो मया ॥

Thus this auspicious ritual was told to me long ago by the Sun, O Sāmba. I have always kept this procedure protected from the first age.

इत्येतत्कथितं साम्ब पुरा सूर्येण मे शुभम् (ityetatkathitaṃ sāmba purā sūryeṇa me śubham) — O Sāmba this auspicious thing was told to me by the Sun in the past;
कल्पोऽयं प्रथमे कल्पे सर्वदा गोपितो मया (kalpo'yaṃ prathame kalpe sarvadā gopito mayā) — This Kalpa has always been kept secret by me in the first Kalpa

BP 1.50.35

अनेन विधिना वत्स विशुद्धेनान्तरात्मना ।
भानुमाराधयेत्क्षिप्रं यदीच्छेत्फलमुत्तमम् ॥

By this procedure, O child, with a purified inner self, one should quickly worship the Sun if one desires the highest fruit.

अनेन विधिना वत्स विशुद्धेनान्तरात्मना (anena vidhinā vatsa viśuddhenāntarātmanā) — By this method, my dear, with a pure inner self;
भानुमाराधयेत्क्षिप्रं यदीच्छेत्फलमुत्तमम् (bhānumārādhayetkṣipraṃ yadīcchetphalamuttamam) — If one desires excellent fruits one should worship the Sun at once

BP 1.50.36

मयास्यैव प्रसादेन प्राप्ताः पुत्राः सहस्रशः ।
असुरा निर्जिताश्चैव सुराः सर्वे वशीकृताः ॥

By his favor I obtained thousands of sons, the demons were defeated, and all the gods were brought under control.

मयास्यैव प्रसादेन प्राप्ताः पुत्राः सहस्रशः (mayāsyaiva prasādena prāptāḥ putrāḥ sahasraśaḥ) — By his grace I have had thousands of sons;
असुरा निर्जिताश्चैव सुराः सर्वे वशीकृताः (asurā nirjitāścaiva surāḥ sarve vaśīkṛtāḥ) — The demons were defeated and all the gods were subdued

BP 1.50.37

त्वयाप्ययं गोपितव्यः कल्पः सूर्यस्य सम्मतः ।
प्रसादादस्य कल्पस्य सदा सन्निहितो रविः ॥
चक्रेऽस्मिन्निर्जिता येन सुरा सुरनरोरगाः ॥

You too should preserve this rule approved by the Sun. By its favor Ravi is always present, and by this wheel he conquered gods, humans, and serpents.

त्वयाप्ययं गोपितव्यः कल्पः सूर्यस्य सम्मतः (tvayāpyayaṃ gopitavyaḥ kalpaḥ sūryasya sammataḥ) — You too should keep this Kalpa approved by the Sun;
प्रसादादस्य कल्पस्य सदा सन्निहितो रविः (prasādādasya kalpasya sadā sannihito raviḥ) — By the grace of this Kalpa the Sun is always present;
चक्रेऽस्मिन्निर्जिता येन सुरा सुरनरोरगाः (cakre'sminnirjitā yena surā suranaroragāḥ) — He conquered the gods men and serpents in this wheel

BP 1.50.38

यदिनाधिष्ठितं चक्रमिदं सूर्यांशुभिः स्वयम् ।
सततं स्यात्प्रभायुक्तं कथमध्याहतं भवेत् ॥

If this wheel were not set in motion by the rays of the Sun himself, how would it remain constantly radiant, and how could it be struck in the middle.

यदिनाधिष्ठितं चक्रमिदं सूर्यांशुभिः स्वयम् (yadinādhiṣṭhitaṃ cakramidaṃ sūryāṃśubhiḥ svayam) — If this wheel is occupied by the rays of the sun itself;
सततं स्यात्प्रभायुक्तं कथमध्याहतं भवेत् (satataṃ syātprabhāyuktaṃ kathamadhyāhataṃ bhavet) — It should be constantly bright and how can it be hit in the middle

BP 1.50.39

अहमेतं जपन्नित्यं यजन्ध्यायंश्च शक्तितः ।
जातोऽस्मि सर्वकामानां पूज्यः श्रेष्ठश्च तेजसा ।

Chanting this always, sacrificing and meditating according to my ability, I became worthy of worship and foremost in splendor, fulfilling all desires.

अहमेतं जपन्नित्यं यजन्ध्यायंश्च शक्तितः (ahametaṃ japannityaṃ yajandhyāyaṃśca śaktitaḥ) — I chant this daily and perform sacrifices and meditate with all my might;
जातोऽस्मि सर्वकामानां पूज्यः श्रेष्ठश्च तेजसा (jāto'smi sarvakāmānāṃ pūjyaḥ śreṣṭhaśca tejasā) — I was born worshipful and excellent in splendor of all desires

BP 1.50.40

त्वमभ्यस्यैव मनसा वाचा वा कर्मणापि वा ।
कुरु भक्तिमनेनैव विधिना फलसिद्धये ॥

You too, by mind, speech, or action, should practice devotion by this very procedure for the attainment of the fruit.

त्वमभ्यस्यैव मनसा वाचा वा कर्मणापि वा (tvamabhyasyaiva manasā vācā vā karmaṇāpi vā) — You practice it with your mind, words or actions;
कुरु भक्तिमनेनैव विधिना फलसिद्धये (kuru bhaktimanenaiva vidhinā phalasiddhaye) — Do devotion by the same method for the attainment of fruits

BP 1.50.41

शृणुयाद्भक्तियुक्तो यो नरः श्रद्धासमन्वितः ।
विधानमादितः पुत्र सप्तमीं कुरुते च यः ॥

Whoever hears this with devotion and faith, O child, and performs the seventh day rite from the beginning.

शृणुयाद्भक्तियुक्तो यो नरः श्रद्धासमन्वितः (śṛṇuyādbhaktiyukto yo naraḥ śraddhāsamanvitaḥ) — A man who is endowed with devotion and faith should listen;
विधानमादितः पुत्र सप्तमीं कुरुते च यः (vidhānamāditaḥ putra saptamīṃ kurute ca yaḥ) — My son whoever performs the seventh ritual from the beginning

BP 1.50.42

सेह प्राप्याऽखिलं काममारोग्यं च जयं तथा ।
भार्गव्या परया युक्तो गच्छेद्वैरोचनं सदः ॥

He obtains every desire, health, and victory here, and joined with the supreme Bhārgavī, he goes to the assembly of Vairocana.

सेह प्राप्याऽखिलं काममारोग्यं च जयं तथा (seha prāpyā'khilaṃ kāmamārogyaṃ ca jayaṃ tathā) — Having attained here all desire, health and victory;
भार्गव्या परया युक्तो गच्छेद्वैरोचनं सदः (bhārgavyā parayā yukto gacchedvairocanaṃ sadaḥ) — The assembly should go to Vairocana accompanied by the supreme Bhrigu
