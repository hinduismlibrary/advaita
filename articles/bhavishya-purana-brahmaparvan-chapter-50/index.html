Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 50 — Solar Mantras and the Consecration of Offerings

BP 1.50.1

वासुदेव उवाच
नैमित्तिकं ततो वक्ष्ये यज्ज्ञात्वा च समासतः ।
सप्तम्यां ग्रहणे चैव संक्रान्तिषु विशेषतः ॥

Vāsudeva said I shall then describe the occasional, and having known it in brief Especially on the seventh day of the eclipse and on the conjunctions

Word-for-word
वासुदेव उवाच (vāsudeva uvāca) — Vāsudeva said;
नैमित्तिकं ततो वक्ष्ये यज्ज्ञात्वा च समासतः (naimittikaṃ tato vakṣye yajjñātvā ca samāsataḥ) — I shall then describe the occasional, and having known it in brief;
सप्तम्यां ग्रहणे चैव संक्रान्तिषु विशेषतः (saptamyāṃ grahaṇe caiva saṃkrāntiṣu viśeṣataḥ) — Especially on the seventh day of the eclipse and on the conjunctions
Transliteration
vāsudeva uvāca
naimittikaṃ tato vakṣye yajjñātvā ca samāsataḥ |
saptamyāṃ grahaṇe caiva saṃkrāntiṣu viśeṣataḥ | | 1

BP 1.50.2

BP 1.50.3

दर्भशय्यागतो रात्रौ प्रातः स्नातः सुसंयतः (darbhaśayyāgato rātrau prātaḥ snātaḥ susaṃyataḥ) — He lay on a bed of straw at night and took a bath in the morning with great self-control

BP 1.50.4

जुहुयाच्च तदा वह्निं सूर्याग्नी परिकल्प्य च ॥

जुहुयाच्च तदा वह्निं सूर्याग्नी परिकल्प्य च (juhuyācca tadā vahniṃ sūryāgnī parikalpya ca) — Then he should offer sacrifice to the fire and arrange the fire of the sun

BP 1.50.9

जुहुयात्प्रोक्ष्य तान्वह्नौ तत्रार्कं पूर्ववद् व्रजेत् ।
अभूमौ स्थितपात्रेण विष्टरेण तु पाणिना ॥
दानेन यदुशार्दूल नान्तरिक्षे स्थले क्वचित् ॥

One should offer oblations and sprinkle them on the fire and there the sun should go as before With a vessel standing on the ground and a mattress in his hand By charity, O tiger of the Yadus, nowhere in space or on earth

Word-for-word

अभूमौ स्थितपात्रेण विष्टरेण तु पाणिना (abhūmau sthitapātreṇa viṣṭareṇa tu pāṇinā) — With a vessel standing on the ground and a mattress in his hand;
दानेन यदुशार्दूल नान्तरिक्षे स्थले क्वचित् (dānena yaduśārdūla nāntarikṣe sthale kvacit) — By charity, O tiger of the Yadus, nowhere in space or on earth
