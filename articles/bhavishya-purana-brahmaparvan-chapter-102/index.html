Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 102 — The Worship of the Lunar Mansions

BP 1.102.1

सुमन्तुरुवाच। एवं या देवदेवस्य सप्तमी भास्करस्य तु। यथा बहूनां भार्याणां भर्तुः काचित्प्रिया भवेत् ॥

Sumantu said. Just as one among many wives may be dear to her husband, so one seventh day is dear to Bhāskara, the God of gods.

BP 1.102.2

सर्वाश्च तिथयो ह्यस्य प्रियाः सूर्यस्य भारत। तस्मादस्यां नरेणेह पूजनीयो दिवाकरः ॥

All the lunar days are dear to the Sun, O Bhārata. Therefore, a person should worship Divākara on this day.

BP 1.102.3

शतानीक उवाच। तिथीनामधिपः सूर्यः सर्वासां कथितो यदि। सप्तम्यामेव यागोऽस्य किमर्थं क्रियते बुधैः ॥

Śatānīka said. If the Sun is declared the lord of all lunar days, why is his sacrifice performed by the wise on the seventh day alone.

BP 1.102.4

सुमन्तुरुवाच। इदमर्थं पुरा पृष्टः सुरज्येष्ठो दिवि स्थितः। विष्णुना कुरुशार्दूल तेनोक्तं हरये यथा। तथा ते सर्वमाख्यास्ये शृणुष्वैकमना विभो ॥

Sumantu said. Viṣṇu once asked the eldest of the gods, who stood in heaven, about this matter. O tiger of the Kurus, I shall tell you all that he told Hari. Listen with an attentive mind, O Lord.

BP 1.102.5

सुखासीनं सुरज्येष्ठं पुरा देवं पितामहम्। प्रणम्य शिरसा देव कृष्णो वचनमब्रवीत् ॥

In former times, Kṛṣṇa bowed his head and spoke to the divine Grandsire, eldest of the gods, who was seated comfortably.

BP 1.102.6

यद्येष भानुमान्देवस्तिथीनामधिपः स्मृतः। किमर्थं पूज्यते ब्रह्मन्सप्तम्यां ब्रूहि मे विभो ॥

If this shining god is remembered as lord of all lunar days, O Brahmā, tell me, O Lord, why he is worshipped on the seventh day.

BP 1.102.7

एवमुक्तः सुरज्येष्ठो विष्णुना प्रभविष्णुना। प्रहस्य भगवान्देव इदं वचनमब्रवीत् ॥

Thus addressed by Viṣṇu, the powerful eldest of the gods, the blessed deity laughed and spoke these words.

BP 1.102.8

ब्रह्मोवाच। देवेभ्यस्तिथयो दत्ता भास्करेण महात्मना। मुक्त्वैकां सप्तमीं सर्वां सम्यगाराधनेन वै ॥

Brahmā said. The great Bhāskara gave the lunar days to the gods, except one, the seventh, wholly assigned to his proper worship.

BP 1.102.9

यस्यैव यद्दिनं दत्तं स तस्यैवाधिपः स्मृतः। स्वदिने पूजितस्तस्मात्स्वमन्त्रैर्वरदो भवेत् ॥

Whichever day was assigned to a deity, that deity is remembered as its lord. Therefore, worshipped on its own day with its own formulas, that deity grants boons.

BP 1.102.10

विष्णुरुवाच। अर्केण कतरत्कस्मै दिनं दत्तं महात्मना। स्वदिने पूजितेऽस्मिन्वै स्वमन्त्रैर्जायते ध्रुवम् ॥

Viṣṇu said. Tell me which day the great Sun assigned to each deity and what surely happens when he is worshipped on his own day with his own formulas.

BP 1.102.11

ब्रह्मोवाच। अग्नये प्रतिपद्दत्ता द्वितीया ब्रह्मणे तथा। तृतीया यक्षराजाय गणेशाय चतुर्थ्यपि ॥

Brahmā said. The first day was given to Agni, the second to Brahmā, the third to the king of the Yakṣas, and the fourth to Gaṇeśa.

BP 1.102.12

पञ्चमी नागराजाय कार्तिकेयाय षष्ठ्यपि। सप्तमी स्थापितात्मार्थं दत्ता रुद्राय चाष्टमी ॥

The fifth day was given to the king of serpents, the sixth also to Kārttikeya. The seventh was established for the Self, and the eighth was given to Rudra.

BP 1.102.13

दुर्गायै नवमी दत्ता यमाय दशमी स्वयम्। विश्वेभ्यश्चाथ देवेभ्यो दत्ता चैकादशी सदा ॥

The ninth day was given to Durgā, the tenth to Yama himself, and the eleventh always to all the gods.

BP 1.102.14

द्वादशी विष्णवे दत्ता मदनाय त्रयोदशी। चतुर्दशी शङ्कराय दत्ता सोमाय पूर्णिमा ॥

The twelfth day was given to Viṣṇu, the thirteenth to Madana, the fourteenth to Śaṅkara, and the full moon to Soma.

BP 1.102.15

पितॄणां भानुना दत्ता पुण्या पञ्चदशी सदा। तिथ्यः पञ्चदशैतास्तु सोमस्य परिकीर्तिताः ॥

The holy fifteenth day was always given by Bhānu to the ancestors. These fifteen lunar days are proclaimed to belong to Soma.

BP 1.102.16

पीयते कृष्णपक्षे तु सुरैरेभिर्यथोदितैः। शुक्लपक्षे प्रपूर्यन्ते षोडश्या कलया सह ॥

In the dark fortnight, the gods drink them according to the order stated. In the bright fortnight, they are filled together with the sixteenth part.

BP 1.102.17

अक्षया सा सदैकैका तत्र साक्षात्स्थितो रविः। क्षयवृद्धिकरो ह्येवं तेनासौ तत्पतिः स्मृतः ॥

Each of them is ever imperishable, and Ravi himself stands there directly. Because he causes waning and waxing, he is remembered as their lord.

BP 1.102.18

ददाति गतिमक्षीणा ध्यानमात्रस्थितो रविः। अन्येपीष्टान्यथाकामान्प्रयच्छन्ति सुखेन वै ॥

Ravi, abiding in meditation alone, grants an inexhaustible destination. The others too easily grant desired things according to desire.

BP 1.102.19

तथा सर्वं प्रवक्ष्यामि कृष्ण संक्षेपतः शृणु। अग्निमिष्ट्वा च हुत्वा च प्रतिपद्यमृतं घृतम्। हविषा सर्वधान्यानि प्राप्नुयादमितं धनम् ॥

Now I shall explain everything briefly, O Kṛṣṇa, listen. Having worshipped Agni and offered clarified butter on the first day, one obtains all grains through the offering, and immeasurable wealth.

BP 1.102.20

ब्रह्माणं च द्वितीयायां सम्पूज्य ब्रह्मचारिणम्। भोजयित्वा च विद्यानां सर्वासां पारगो भवेत् ॥

Having fully worshipped Brahmā on the second day and fed a celibate, one becomes learned in all branches of knowledge.

BP 1.102.21

तृतीयायां च वित्तेशं वित्ताढ्यो जायते ध्रुवम्। क्रयादिव्यवहोरेषु लाभो बहुगुणो भवेत् ॥

On the third day, one should worship the Lord of wealth. One surely becomes rich, and profit in buying and other transactions increases manyfold.

BP 1.102.22

गणेशपूजनं कुर्याच्चतुर्थ्यां सर्वकर्मसु। अविघ्नं विद्विषां विघ्नं कुर्याच्चास्य न संशयः ॥

One should worship Gaṇeśa on the fourth day for all undertakings. He removes obstacles and creates obstacles for hostile people, without doubt.

BP 1.102.23

नागानिष्ट्वा च पञ्चम्यां न विषैरभिभूयते। स्त्रियं च लभते पुत्रान्परां च श्रियमाप्नुयात् ॥

Having worshipped the serpents on the fifth day, one is not overcome by poison. One obtains a wife and sons, and attains supreme prosperity.

BP 1.102.24

सम्पूज्य कार्तिकेयं तु षष्ठ्यां श्रेष्ठः प्रजायते। मेधावी रूपसम्पन्नो दीर्घायुः कीर्तिवर्धनः ॥

Having worshipped Kārttikeya on the sixth day, one is born excellent, intelligent, handsome, long lived, and increasing in fame.

BP 1.102.25

सप्तम्यां पूज्य रक्षेशं चित्रभानुं दिवाकरम्। अष्टम्यां पूजितो देवो गोवृषाभरणो हरः ॥

Having worshipped the protector, Citrabhānu the Sun, on the seventh day, and the god Hara, adorned with the bull, on the eighth day,

BP 1.102.26

ज्ञानं ददाति विपुलं कान्तिं च विपुलां तथा। मृत्युहा ज्ञानदश्चैव पाशहा च प्रपूजितः ॥

he gives abundant knowledge and abundant radiance. Worshipped as destroyer of death, giver of knowledge, and remover of bonds, he bestows these.

BP 1.102.27

दुर्गां सम्पूज्य दुर्गाणि नवम्यां तरतीच्छया। सङ्ग्रामे व्यवहारे च सदा विजयमश्नुते ॥

Having worshipped Durgā on the ninth day, one crosses difficulties as desired. In battle and in dealings, one always meets with victory.

BP 1.102.28

दशम्यां यममातिष्ठेत्सर्वव्याधिहरो ध्रुवम्। नरकादथ मृत्योश्च समुद्धरति मानवम् ॥

One should worship Yama on the tenth day. He surely removes every disease and rescues a person from hell and from death.

BP 1.102.29

एकादश्यां यथोद्दिष्टा विश्वेदेवाः प्रपूजिताः। प्रजां पशुं धनं धान्यं प्रयच्छन्ति महीं तथा ॥

When the All Gods are worshipped on the eleventh day as prescribed, they grant offspring, cattle, wealth, grain, and land as well.

BP 1.102.30

द्वादश्यां विष्णुमिष्ट्वेह सर्वदा विजयी भवेत्। पूज्यश्च सर्वलोकानां यथा गोपतिगोकरः ॥

Having worshipped Viṣṇu on the twelfth day, one is always victorious here and is honored by all the worlds, like the Lord of cows among cattle.

BP 1.102.31

कामदेवं त्रयोदश्यां सुरूपो जायते ध्रुवम्। इष्टां रूपवतीं भार्यां लभेत्कामांश्च पुष्कलान् ॥

By worshipping Kāma on the thirteenth day, one surely becomes handsome. One obtains a beloved beautiful wife and abundant pleasures.

BP 1.102.32

दृष्ट्वेश्वरं चतुर्दश्यां सर्वैश्वर्यसमन्वितः। बहुपुत्रो बहुधनस्तथा स्यान्नात्र संशयः ॥

Having worshipped Īśvara on the fourteenth day, one is endowed with all sovereignty, many sons, and great wealth, without doubt.

BP 1.102.33

पौर्णमास्यां तु यः सोमं पूजयेद्भक्तिमान्नरः। स्वाधिपत्यं भवेत्तस्य सम्पूर्णं न च हीयते ॥

A devoted person who worships Soma on the full moon obtains complete sovereignty, which does not decline.

BP 1.102.34

पितरः स्वदिने दिण्डे दृष्टाः कुर्वन्ति सर्वदा। प्रजावृद्धिं धनं रक्षां चायुष्यं बलमेव च ॥

The ancestors, seen on their own day and on the appointed day, always grant increase of offspring, wealth, protection, long life, and strength.

BP 1.102.35

उपवासं विनाप्येते भवन्त्युक्तफलप्रदाः। पूजया जपहोमैश्च तोषिता भक्तितः सदा ॥

Even without fasting, they become givers of the stated fruits when always pleased by devotion through worship, recitation, and fire offerings.

BP 1.102.36

मूलमन्त्रैश्च संज्ञाभिरंशमन्त्रैश्च कीर्तिताः। पूर्ववत्पद्ममध्यस्थाः कर्त्तव्याश्च तिथीश्वराः ॥

The lords of the lunar days are praised with root formulas, names, and subsidiary formulas. As before, they are to be placed in the center of the lotus.

BP 1.102.37

गन्धपुष्पोपहारैश्च यथा शक्त्या विधीयते। पूजा बाह्येन विधिना कृतापि च फलप्रदा ॥

Worship is offered with fragrances, flowers, and other gifts according to ability. Even worship carried out by external rites gives fruit.

BP 1.102.38

आज्यधारासमिद्भिश्च दधिक्षीरान्नमाक्षिकैः। यथोक्तफलदो होमो जपः शान्तेन चेतसा ॥

With streams of clarified butter, kindling sticks, curd, milk, food, and honey, the fire offering gives the stated fruit, and recitation is done with a tranquil mind.

BP 1.102.39

मूलमन्त्राश्च संज्ञाभिरङ्गमन्त्राश्च कीर्तिताः। कृत्वा यज्ञान्दश द्वौ च फलान्येतानि भक्तितः ॥

The root formulas, names, and subsidiary formulas are proclaimed. By performing twelve sacrifices, one obtains these fruits through devotion.

BP 1.102.40

यथोक्तानि तथोक्तानि लभेतेहाधिकान्यपि। इह यस्माद्यथान्यस्मिन्यो वसेद्यः सुखी सदा ॥

He obtains here the stated fruits and greater ones too. In this world and another, one who dwells in that way is always happy.

BP 1.102.41

तेषां लोकेषु मन्त्रज्ञो यावत्तेषां तिथिः स्थिता। दहेत्तस्मात्तथारिष्टं तद्रूपो जायते नरः ॥

In those worlds, while their lunar days remain, a knower of the formulas burns away misfortune. A person is born in their likeness.

BP 1.102.42

सुरूपो धर्मसम्पन्नो क्षपितारिर्महीपतिः। स्त्री वा नपुंसको वापि जायते पुरुषोत्तमः ॥

He is born handsome, endowed with righteousness, a king who has destroyed his enemies. A woman or a eunuch too is born as an excellent person.

BP 1.102.43

इत्येताः कथिताः कृष्ण तिथयो या मया तव। नक्षत्रदेवताः सर्वा नक्षत्रेषु व्यवस्थिताः ॥

Thus, O Kṛṣṇa, these are the lunar days that I have told you. All the deities of the lunar mansions are established in the lunar mansions.

BP 1.102.44

इष्टान्कामान्प्रयच्छन्ति यास्ता वक्ष्ये महीधर। चन्द्रमा यत्र नक्षत्रे महावृद्ध्या स्थितः सदा ॥

I shall tell you those deities who grant desired wishes, O bearer of the earth, when the Moon is always placed in a lunar mansion with great increase.

BP 1.102.45

उक्तस्तु देवतायज्ञस्तदा सा फलदा भवेत्। देवताश्च प्रवक्ष्यामि नक्षत्राणां यथाक्रमम् ॥

When a sacrifice to the deity is performed as taught, it becomes fruitful. I shall also explain the deities of the lunar mansions in due order.

BP 1.102.46

नक्षत्राणि च सर्वाणि यज्ञाश्चैव पृथक्पृथक्। अश्विन्यामश्विनाविष्ट्वा दीर्घायुर्जायते नरः ॥

All the lunar mansions and their sacrifices are distinct. Having worshipped the two Aśvins in Aśvinī, a person becomes long lived.

BP 1.102.47

व्याधिभिर्मुच्यते क्षिप्रमत्यर्थं व्याधिपीडितः। भरण्यां यम उद्दिष्टः कुसुमैरसितैः शुभैः ॥

A person afflicted by disease is quickly freed from it. Yama is prescribed in Bharaṇī, with beautiful dark flowers.

BP 1.102.48

तथा गन्धादिभिः शुभ्रैरपमृत्योर्विमोचयेत् ॥

Likewise, with pure fragrances and other offerings, he should be freed from untimely death.

BP 1.102.49

अनलः कृत्तिकायां तु इह सम्पूजितः परः। रक्तमाल्यादिभिर् दद्यात्फलं होमेन च ध्रुवम् ॥

In Kṛttikā, the supreme Fire is worshipped here. With red garlands and other offerings, and with a fire offering, he surely gives fruit.

BP 1.102.50

पूज्यः प्रजापतिः प्रीत इष्टो दद्यात्पशूंस्तथा। रोहिण्यां देवशार्दूल पूजनादिह गोपते। मृगशीर्षे सदा सोमो ज्ञानमारोग्यमेव च ॥

Prajāpati, worshipped and pleased, grants cattle. In Rohiṇī, O tiger among the gods, worship the Lord of cows here. In Mṛgaśīrṣa, Soma always grants knowledge and health.

BP 1.102.51

आर्द्रायां तु शिवं पूज्य पश्चाद्द्विज यमाप्नुयात्। पद्मादिभिः स दिव्यैश्च पूजितः शं प्रयच्छति ॥

Having worshipped Śiva in Ārdrā, a twice born person should afterward attain self restraint. Worshipped with lotuses and other divine flowers, he grants peace.

BP 1.102.52

तथा पुनर्वस्वदितिः सदा सम्पूज्यते दिवि। गुरूणां तर्पिता चैव मातेव परिरक्षति ॥

Likewise, Aditi is always worshipped in Punarvasu in heaven. Being satisfied, she protects like a mother.

BP 1.102.53

पुष्ये बृहस्पतिर्बुद्धिं ददाति विपुलां शुभाम्। गीतैर्गन्धादिभिर्नागा आश्लेषायां प्रपूजिताः ॥

In Puṣya, Bṛhaspati gives abundant auspicious intelligence. In Āśleṣā, the serpents are worshipped with songs, fragrances, and other offerings.

BP 1.102.54

तर्पिताश्च यथान्यायं भक्ष्याद्यैर्मधुरैः सितैः। रक्षामिषादिभिस्तैस्तैः प्रीतिं कुर्वन्ति मानद ॥

Satisfied according to rule with sweet, white foods and the like, they show favor through those protective offerings and others, O giver of honor.

BP 1.102.55

मघासु पितरः सर्वे हव्यैः कव्यैश्च पूजिताः। प्रयच्छन्ति धनं धान्यं भृत्यान्पुत्रान्पशूंस्तथा ॥

In Maghā, all the ancestors, worshipped with offerings to the gods and the ancestors, grant wealth, grain, servants, sons, and cattle.

BP 1.102.56

फाल्गुन्यामथ वै पूषा इष्टः पुष्पादिभिः शुभैः। पूर्वायां विजयं दद्यादुत्तरायां भगं तथा ॥

In Phalgunī, Pūṣan, worshipped with beautiful flowers and other offerings, grants victory in the former mansion and good fortune in the latter.

BP 1.102.57

भर्तारमीप्सितं दद्यात्कन्यायै पुरुषाय ताम्। इह जन्मनि युज्येत रूपद्रविणसम्पदा ॥

He grants a desired husband to a maiden and gives her to a man. In this life, one is joined with beauty and wealth.

BP 1.102.58

पूजितः सविता हस्ते विश्वतेजोनिधिः सदा। गन्धपुष्पादिभिः सर्वं ददाति विपुलं धनम् ॥

When worshipped in Hasta with fragrant flowers and the like, Savitṛ, the treasure of all radiance, always gives abundant wealth.

BP 1.102.59

राज्यं तु त्वाष्टा चित्रायां निःसपत्नं प्रयच्छति। इष्टः सन्तर्पितः प्रीतः स्वात्यां वायुर्बलं परम् ॥

In Citrā, Tvaṣṭṛ grants a kingdom without rivals. Worshipped and pleased in Svātī, Vāyu grants supreme strength.

BP 1.102.60

इन्द्राग्नी च विशाखायां जातरक्तैः प्रपूज्य च। धनधान्यानि लब्ध्वेह तेजस्वी निवसेद्सदा ॥

Having worshipped Indra and Agni in Viśākhā with red offerings, one obtains wealth and grain and lives here radiant.

BP 1.102.61

रक्तैर्मित्रमनूराधास्व् एवं सम्पूज्य भक्तितः। श्रियो भजन्ति सर्वेषां चिरं जीवन्ति सर्वदा ॥

Having worshipped Mitra in Anurādhā with red offerings and devotion, people enjoy prosperity among all and live long.

BP 1.102.62

ज्येष्ठायां पूर्ववच्छक्रमिष्ट्वा पुष्टिमवाप्नुयात्। गुणैर्ज्येष्ठश्च सर्वेषां कर्मणा च धनेन च ॥

In Jyeṣṭhā, having worshipped Śakra as before, one obtains nourishment and becomes foremost of all in virtues, action, and wealth.

BP 1.102.63

मूले देवपितॄन्सर्वान् भक्त्या सम्पूज्य पूर्ववत्। पूर्ववत्फलमाप्नोति स्वर्गस्थाने ध्रुवो भवेत् ॥

In Mūla, having worshipped all the gods and ancestors with devotion as before, one obtains the same fruit and becomes firmly established in a heavenly place.

BP 1.102.64

पूर्वाषाढे ह्यपः पूज्य हुत्वा तत्रैव पूर्ववत्। सन्तापान्मुच्यते क्षिप्रं शारीरान् मानसात्तथा ॥

In Pūrvāṣāḍhā, having worshipped the Waters and offered oblations there as before, one is quickly freed from bodily and mental afflictions.

BP 1.102.65

आषाढासु तथा विश्वानुत्तराषाढयोगतः। विश्वेशं पूज्य पुष्पाद्यैः सर्वमाप्नोति मानवः ॥

In Uttarāṣāḍhā, having worshipped the Lord of All with flowers and the like, a person obtains everything.

BP 1.102.66

श्रवणे तु सितैर्विष्णुं पीतैर्नीलैश्च भक्तितः। सम्पूज्य श्रियमाप्नोति परं विजयमेव च ॥

In Śravaṇa, having worshipped Viṣṇu with white, yellow, and blue offerings and devotion, one obtains prosperity and supreme victory.

BP 1.102.67

धनिष्ठासु वसूनिष्ट्वा न भयं भजते क्वचित्। महतोऽपि भयात्त्वेतैर्गन्धपुष्पादिभिः शुभैः ॥

Having worshipped the Vasus in Dhaniṣṭhā with auspicious fragrances, flowers, and the like, one never experiences fear, even great fear.

BP 1.102.68

इन्द्रं शतभिषायां च व्याधिभिर्मुच्यते ध्रुवम्। आतुरः पुष्टिमाप्नोति स्वास्थ्यंश्वर्यमेव च ॥

Having worshipped Indra in Śatabhiṣā, one is surely freed from diseases. The afflicted person obtains nourishment, health, and prosperity.

BP 1.102.69

अजं भाद्रपदायां तु शुद्धस्फटिकसन्निभम्। सम्पूज्य भक्तिमाप्नोति परं विजयमेव च ॥

Having worshipped Aja in Bhādrapadā with an image resembling pure crystal, one obtains devotion and supreme victory.

BP 1.102.70

उत्तरायामहिर्बुध्न्यं परां शान्तिमवाप्नुयात्। रेवत्यां पूजितः पूषा ददाति सततं शुभम्। सितैः पुष्पैः स्थितिं चैव धृतिं विजयमेव च ॥

In Uttarā Bhādrapadā, one obtains supreme peace by worshipping Ahirbudhnya. In Revatī, Pūṣan worshipped with white flowers continually grants auspiciousness, stability, firmness, and victory.

BP 1.102.71

तवैवैते समाख्याता यज्ञाः संक्षेपतो मया। नक्षत्रदेवतानां च साधकानां हिताय वै॥ भक्त्या वित्तानुसारेण भवन्ति फलदाः सदा ॥

These sacrifices have been explained to you in brief for the benefit of the deities of the lunar mansions and their practitioners. With devotion and according to their means, they always give fruit.

BP 1.102.72

गन्तुमिच्छेदनन्त्यं वा क्रियां प्रारब्धमेव च। नक्षत्रदेवतायज्ञं कृत्वा तत्सर्वमाचरेत् ॥

Whoever wishes to go somewhere or begin an undertaking should perform the sacrifice to the lunar mansion deity and then carry out all of it.

BP 1.102.73

एवं कृते हि तत्सर्वं यात्राफलमवाप्नुयात्। क्रियाफलं च सम्पूर्णमित्युक्तं भानुना स्वयम् ॥

When this is done, one obtains the full result of the journey and the complete result of the undertaking. This was said by Bhānu himself.

BP 1.102.74

यज्ञात्स विजयं कुर्यात्क्रियां कुर्याद्यथेप्सिताम्। कालचक्रेऽथ वा सूर्यं राशिचक्रे कलात्मना ॥

Through the sacrifice one should achieve victory and perform the action as desired. One should contemplate the Sun as Time, in the wheel of time or in the zodiacal wheel, as the soul of time.

BP 1.102.75

विश्वतेजोनिधिं ध्यात्वा सर्वं कुर्याद्यथेप्सितम्। विभूतिरेषा चोद्दिष्टा क्रियाभिः साध्यते ध्रुवम् ॥

Having meditated on the Sun, the treasure of all radiance, one should do everything as desired. This glory is declared and is surely accomplished through action.

BP 1.102.76

उद्दिष्टाभिः प्रयत्नेन मुक्तियोगेन साध्यते। भानोराराधनाद्वापि प्राप्यते मुक्तिरेव हि। तस्मादाराधय रविं भक्त्या त्वं मधुसूदन ॥

Through the prescribed practices and effort, it is accomplished by the discipline of liberation. Indeed, liberation is also obtained through worship of Bhānu. Therefore, worship Ravi with devotion, O Madhusūdana.

BP 1.102.77

इज्या पूजा नमस्कारशुश्रूषाभिरहर्निशम्। व्रतोपवासैर्विविधैब्राह्मणानां च तर्पणैः ॥

By sacrifice, worship, prostration, and service day and night, through various vows and fasts, and by offerings to Brahmins,

BP 1.102.78

यः कारयति देवार्चिं हृदयालम्बनं रवेः। स नरो भानुसालोक्यमुपैति गतकल्मषः ॥

the person who causes worship of the deity with Ravi as the support of his heart attains the same world as Bhānu, freed from impurity.
