वसुमाहात्म्ये त्रिलोचनवैश्योत्पत्तिवर्णनम् — The account of the greatness of wealth and the origin of the merchant Trilocana.

।। सूत उवाच ।। — Sūta said.

BP 3.4.15.1

भृगुवर्य महाभाग शृणु त्वं जीववर्णितम् ।
पवित्रं वसुमाहात्म्यं सर्ववस्तुसुखप्रदम् ।। १ ।।

O excellent Bhṛgu, great one, hear what Jīva related; the sacred greatness of wealth, which grants happiness in all things.

Transliteration (IAST)

bhṛguvarya mahābhāga śṛṇu tvaṃ jīvavarṇitam || pavitraṃ vasumāhātmyaṃ sarvavastusukhapradam || 1 ||
