VP 2.16.1

ब्राह्मण उवाच— ऋभुर् वर्ष-सहस्रे तु समतीते नरेश्वर।
निदाघ-ज्ञान-दानाय तद् एव नगरं ययौ

After a thousand years had passed, Ribhu went to that same city, O king, to give Nidāgha knowledge.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
ऋभुः वर्ष-सहस्रे (ṛbhuḥ varṣa-sahasre) — Ribhu, after a thousand years;
तु समतीते (tu samatīte) — when they had passed;
नरेश्वर (nareśvara) — O king;
निदाघ-ज्ञान-दानाय (nidāgha-jñāna-dānāya) — for giving knowledge to Nidāgha;
तत् एव नगरम् ययौ (tat eva nagaram yayau) — went to that same city
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

brāhmaṇa uvāca— ṛbhur varṣa-sahasre tu samatīte nareśvara | nidāgha-jñāna-dānāya tad eva nagaraṃ yayau

Śrīdhara

vaṣasahasre samatīte nidāghajñānadānāya yayāviti vadannātmaikyajñānasya cirakālabahūpadeśāpekṣitayā durlabhatāṃ darśayati ||1||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

वषसहस्रे समतीते निदाघज्ञानदानाय ययाविति वदन्नात्मैक्यज्ञानस्य चिरकालबहूपदेशापेक्षितया दुर्लभतां दर्शयति ॥
१॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

After a thousand years have passed, when it is said that he went to give Nidāgha knowledge, the difficulty of knowledge of the unity of the self is shown: it requires instruction over a long time.

VP 2.16.2

नगरस्य बहिः सोऽथ निदाघं ददृशे मुनिः।
महा-बल-परीवारे पुरं विशति पार्थिवे

Outside the city the sage saw Nidāgha, while the king, accompanied by a mighty retinue, was entering the city.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
नगरस्य बहिः (nagarasya bahiḥ) — outside the city;
सः अथ (saḥ atha) — then he;
निदाघम् ददृशे मुनिः (nidāgham dadṛśe muniḥ) — the sage saw Nidāgha;
महा-बल-परिवारे (mahā-bala-parivāre) — with a mighty retinue;
पुरम् विशति पार्थिवे (puram viśati pārthive) — while the king entered the city
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nagarasya bahiḥ so’tha nidāghaṃ dadṛśe muniḥ | mahā-bala-parīvāre puraṃ viśati pārthive

Śrīdhara commentary.

No Śrīdhara commentary in the pinned source.

VP 2.16.3

दूरे स्थितं महाभागं जन-सम्मर्द-वर्जकम्।
क्षुत्-क्षाम-कण्ठम् आयान्तम् अरण्यात् स-समित्-कुशम्

Seeing Nidāgha standing far away, avoiding the crowd, his throat shrivelled by hunger, coming from the forest with fuel and kuśa grass.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
दूरे स्थितम् महा-भागम् (dūre sthitam mahā-bhāgam) — standing far away, the great one;
जन-सम्मर्द-वर्ज्जकम् (jana-sammarda-varjjakam) — avoiding the crowd;
क्षुत्-क्षाम-कण्ठम् (kṣut-kṣāma-kaṇṭham) — his throat emaciated by hunger;
आयान्तम् अरण्यात् (āyāntam araṇyāt) — coming from the forest;
स-समित्-कुशम् (sa-samit-kuśam) — with fuel and kuśa grass
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dūre sthitaṃ mahābhāgaṃ jana-sammarda-varjakam | kṣut-kṣāma-kaṇṭham āyāntam araṇyāt sa-samit-kuśam

Śrīdhara

aspṛśyasparśādiśaṅkayā janasammardavarjjakam | kṣudhā kṣāma.h kṛśaḥ kaṇṭho yasya tam ||3||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अस्पृश्यस्पर्शादिशङ्कया जनसम्मर्दवर्ज्जकम् ।
क्षुधा क्षाम.ह् कृशः कण्ठो यस्य तम् ॥
३॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Because of fear of contact with untouchables and the like, [he is] avoiding the crowd. His throat is shrivelled by hunger: him whose throat is emaciated.

VP 2.16.4

दृष्ट्वा निदाघं स ऋभुर् उपगम्याभिवाद्य च।
उवाच कस्माद् एकान्ते स्थीयते भवता द्विज

Seeing Nidāgha, Ribhu approached, saluted him, and said, “O Brahman, why are you staying in solitude?”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
दृष्ट्वा निदाघम् (dṛṣṭvā nidāgham) — seeing Nidāgha;
सः ऋभुः उपगम्य अभिवाद्य च (saḥ ṛbhuḥ upagamya abhivādya ca) — Ribhu approached and saluted;
उवाच (uvāca) — he said;
कस्मात् एकान्ते स्थीयते (kasmāt ekānte sthīyate) — why is [he] staying in solitude;
भवता द्विज (bhavatā dvija) — by you, O Brahman
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dṛṣṭvā nidāghaṃ sa ṛbhur upagamyābhivādya ca | uvāca kasmād ekānte sthīyate bhavatā dvija

Śrīdhara

ācāryatvaṃ svīyaṃ pracchādayitumabhivādya ca ||4-6||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

आचार्यत्वं स्वीयं प्रच्छादयितुमभिवाद्य च ॥
४-६॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Having saluted, in order to conceal his own status as teacher.

VP 2.16.6

VP 2.16.13

उपर्य् अहं यथा राजा त्वम् अधः कुञ्जरो यथा।
अवबोधाय ते ब्रह्मन् दृष्टान्तो दर्शितो मया

“I am above as the king is; you are below as the elephant is. For your understanding, O Brahman, I have shown an example.”

Word-for-word

उपरि अहम् यथा राजा (upari aham yathā rājā) — I am above as the king;

अवबोधाय ते ब्रह्मन् (avabodhāya te brahman) — for your understanding, O Brahman;
दृष्टान्तः दर्शितः मया (dṛṣṭāntaḥ darśitaḥ mayā) — an example has been shown by me
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

upary ahaṃ yathā rājā tvam adhaḥ kuñjaro yathā | avabodhāya te brahman dṛṣṭānto darśito mayā

Śrīdhara

tadupari ārohāparādhaṃ pariharati dṛṣṭānto darśita iti ||13||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तदुपरि आरोहापराधं परिहरति दृष्टान्तो दर्शित इति ॥
१३॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

By saying ‘the example is shown,’ the fault of taking ‘above’ from the action of climbing is removed.

VP 2.16.14

ऋभुर् उवाच— त्वं राजेव द्विज-श्रेष्ठ स्थितोऽहं गजवद् यदि।
तद् एतत् त्वं समाचक्ष्व कतमस् त्वम् अहं तथा

“If you are situated as the king and I am as the elephant, then tell me exactly which you are and which I am.”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

अहम् गज-वत् यदि (aham gaja-vat yadi) — I as the elephant, if;
तत् एव त्वम् मम आचक्ष्व (tat eva tvam mama ācakṣva) — tell me exactly which you are;
कतमः त्वम् अहम् तथा (katamaḥ tvam aham tathā) — which are you and which am I
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ṛbhur uvāca— tvaṃ rājeva dvija-śreṣṭha sthito’haṃ gajavad yadi | tad etat tvaṃ samācakṣva katamas tvam ahaṃ tathā

Śrīdhara

tadeva tvaṃ samācakṣva katamastvamahaṃ tatha iti sākṣepoktayā na tvaṃ tvaṃ, na cānye’nye, nāhamapyaham iti ||14||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तदेव त्वं समाचक्ष्व कतमस्त्वमहं तथ इति साक्षेपोक्तया न त्वं त्वं, न चान्येऽन्ये, नाहमप्यहम् इति ॥
१४॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

‘Tell me precisely which you are and which I am’: by this terse statement, the earlier teaching ‘you are not you, others are not others, nor am I even I’ is recalled.
