VP 2.16.1

ऋभुर् वर्षसहस्रे तु समतीते नरेश्वर । निदाघज्ञानदानाय तद् एव नगरं ययौ ॥१॥

After a thousand years had passed, Ribhu went to that same city, O king, to give Nidāgha knowledge.

Śrīdhara translation

After a thousand years have passed, when it is said that he went to give Nidāgha knowledge, the difficulty of knowledge of the unity of the self is shown: it requires instruction over a long time.

VP 2.16.3

दूरे स्थितं महाभागं जनसम्मर्दवर्जकम् । क्षुत्क्षामकण्ठम् आयान्तम् अरण्यात् ससमित्कुशम् ॥३॥

Seeing Nidāgha standing far away, avoiding the crowd, his throat shrivelled by hunger, coming from the forest with fuel and kuśa grass.

Śrīdhara translation

Because of fear of contact with untouchables and the like, [he is] avoiding the crowd. His throat is shrivelled by hunger: him whose throat is emaciated.

VP 2.16.23

एकः समस्तं यद् इहास्ति किञ्चित् तद् अच्युतो नास्ति परं ततोऽन्यत् । सोऽहं स च त्वं स च सर्वम् एतद् आत्मस्वरूपं त्यज भेदमोहम् ॥२३॥

Whatever exists here in its entirety is Acyuta; there is nothing higher or other than that. I am he, and you are he, and all this is the nature of the self; abandon delusion of difference.

Śrīdhara translation

Even though the self is not directly perceived, the delusion of difference perceived directly is made possible by the example of space; the taught meaning is concluded with ‘white and blue,’ in these two verses.

VP 2.16.24

इतीरितस् तेन स राजवर्यस् तत्याज भेदं परमार्थदृष्टिः । स चापि जातिस्मरणाप्तबोधस् तत्रैव जन्मन्य् अपवर्गम् आप ॥२४॥

When this was said by him, that best of kings abandoned difference, with vision of ultimate reality; and he too, having obtained knowledge through remembrance of births, attained liberation in that very birth.

Śrīdhara translation

The knowledge obtained through remembrance of births is possessed by him. For at that time, the knowledge taught by Ṛṣabhadeva through remembrance of Bharata’s birth became manifest in this Brahman birth, when the operative karma that caused the fall from yoga had ended. Therefore he attained liberation in that very birth.
