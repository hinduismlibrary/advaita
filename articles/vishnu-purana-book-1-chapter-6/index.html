VP 1.6.5

VP 1.6.8

यज्ञैर् आप्यायिता देवा वृष्ट्य्-उत्सर्गेण वै प्रजाः।
आप्याययन्ते धर्मज्ञ यज्ञाः कल्याण-हेतवः

By sacrifices, O thou who knowest the truth, the gods are nourished; and by the rain which they bestow, mankind are supported:

Śrīdhara translation

The benefit of sacrifice is this: the gods are nourished and release rain; through the rain, the people who perform sacrifice are nourished. The remembered sequence is: oblation placed in fire reaches the sun; rain arises from the sun; food from rain; and beings from food. Therefore sacrifices are causes of welfare.
