VP 1.6.1

VP 1.6.5

पद्भ्याम् अन्याः प्रजा ब्रह्मा ससर्ज द्विज-सत्तम।

These are the four classes of beings created from Brahmā’s mouth and other limbs. The later enumeration connects the feet, thighs, breast, and mouth with the castes in reverse order.

VP 1.6.7

यज्ञ-निष्पत्तये सर्वम् एतद् ब्रह्मा चकार वै।
चातुर्वर्ण्यं महाभाग यज्ञ-साधनम् उत्तमम्

These he created for the performance of sacrifices, the four castes being the fit instruments of their celebration.

Śrīdhara translation

Brahmā created the four castes as the superior instruments for accomplishing sacrifice. He notes that Śūdras participate in sacrificial purposes through service to the twice-born and in forms of sacrifice permitted by sacred tradition, so there is no contradiction.

VP 1.6.8

यज्ञैर् आप्यायिता देवा वृष्ट्य्-उत्सर्गेण वै प्रजाः।
आप्याययन्ते धर्मज्ञ यज्ञाः कल्याण-हेतवः

By sacrifices, O thou who knowest the truth, the gods are nourished; and by the rain which they bestow, mankind are supported:

Śrīdhara translation

The benefit of sacrifice is this: the gods are nourished and release rain; through the rain, the people who perform sacrifice are nourished. The remembered sequence is: oblation placed in fire reaches the sun; rain arises from the sun; food from rain; and beings from food. Therefore sacrifices are causes of welfare.

VP 1.6.9

निष्पाद्यन्ते नरैस् तैस् तु स्व-धर्माभिरतैः सदा।
विशुद्धाचरणोपेतैः सद्भिः सन्-मार्ग-गामिभिः

Śrīdhara translation

Those best qualified to perform sacrifice are people devoted to their Vedic duties, following purity prescribed by sacred tradition, learned, and walking the good path.

विनिन्दकानां वेदस्य यज्ञ-व्याघात-कारिणाम्।
स्थानम् एतत् समाख्यातं स्व-धर्म-त्यागिनश् च ये

the fearful hell of sharp swords, the hell of scourges and of a waveless sea.

Śrīdhara translation

Those who abandon their own duties also go to these hellish places.
