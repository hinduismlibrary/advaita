VP 1.17.1

मैत्रेय श्रूयतां सम्यक् चरितं तस्य धीमतः।
प्रह्लादस्य सदोदार-चरितस्य महात्मनः

Listen, Maitreya, to the story of the wise and magnanimous Prahlāda, whose adventures are ever interesting and instructive.

Śrīdhara translation

Since hatred of Bhagavān is itself the cause of hatred even toward his own son, who is a devotee, he introduces Hiraṇyakaśipu’s story with the words “Diti’s son,” and so on.
