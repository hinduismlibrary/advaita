BP 3.2.19.1

चित्रकूटे च नृपती रूपदत्त इति श्रुतः ।
वने मृगप्रसंगेन वनान्तरमुपाययौ ॥ 1 ॥

At Citrakūṭa there lived a king named Rūpadatta. While hunting deer, he went deeper into the forest.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
चित्रकूटे citrakūṭe at Citrakūṭa
च ca and
नृपती nṛpatī a king
रूपदत्त rūpadatta Rūpadatta
इति iti thus
श्रुतः śrutaḥ was known
वने vane in the forest
मृगप्रसंगेन mṛgaprasaṃgena while hunting deer
वनान्तरमुपाययौ vanāntaramupāyayau went farther into the forest
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

citrakūṭe ca nṛpatī rūpadatta iti śrutaḥ | vane mṛgaprasaṃgena vanāntaramupāyayau || 1 ||

BP 3.2.19.2

मध्याह्ने सरसस्तीरे मुनिपुत्रो ददर्श सः ।
चिन्वतीं पद्मकुसुमं रूपयौवनशालिनीम् ॥ 2 ॥

At midday beside a lake, a sage's son saw a beautiful young woman gathering lotus flowers.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
मध्याह्ने madhyāhne at midday
सरसस्तीरे sarasastīre on the lake's shore
मुनिपुत्रो muniputro a sage's son
ददर्श dadarśa saw
सः saḥ he
चिन्वतीं cinvatīṃ gathering
पद्मकुसुमं padmakusumaṃ lotus flower
रूपयौवनशालिनीम् rūpayauvanaśālinīm beautiful and youthful
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

madhyāhne sarasastīre muniputro dadarśa saḥ | cinvatīṃ padmakusumaṃ rūpayauvanaśālinīm || 2 ||

BP 3.2.19.3

तस्या नेत्रे स्वयं नेत्रे चैकीभूते समागते ।
एतस्मिन्नंतरे विप्रस्तत्र प्राप्तो ददर्श तौ ॥ 3 ॥

Their eyes met and seemed to become one. Just then a Brahmin arrived and saw the pair.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
तस्या tasyā of her
नेत्रे netre eyes
स्वयं svayaṃ himself
नेत्रे netre eyes
चैकीभूते caikībhūte having become one
समागते samāgate met; united
एतस्मिन्नंतरे etasminnaṃtare in the meantime
विप्रस्तत्र viprastatra the Brahmin there
प्राप्तो prāpto arrived
ददर्श dadarśa saw
तौ tau the two
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

tasyā netre svayaṃ netre caikībhūte samāgate | etasminnaṃtare viprastatra prāpto dadarśa tau || 3 ||

BP 3.2.19.4

तस्य दर्शनमात्रेण नृपतेर्ज्ञानमागतम् ।
विनयावनतो राजा धर्मं पप्रच्छ चोत्तमम् ॥ 4 ॥

At the sight of them, insight came to the king. Bowing humbly, he asked about the highest dharma.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
तस्य tasya of him
दर्शनमात्रेण darśanamātreṇa at the mere sight
नृपतेर्ज्ञानमागतम् nṛpaterjñānamāgatam insight came to the king
विनयावनतो vinayāvanato humbly bowing
राजा rājā king
धर्मं dharmaṃ dharma
पप्रच्छ papraccha asked
चोत्तमम् cottamam and the highest
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

tasya darśanamātreṇa nṛpaterjñānamāgatam | vinayāvanato rājā dharmaṃ papraccha cottamam || 4 ||

BP 3.2.19.5

तमुवाच मुनिर्द्धीमान्दयाधर्मप्रपोषणम् ।
निर्भयस्य समं दानं न भूतं न भविष्यति ॥ 5 ॥

The wise sage told him: Compassion nurtures dharma. There has never been, nor will there be, a gift equal to granting fearlessness.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
तमुवाच tamuvāca said to him
मुनिर्द्धीमान्दयाधर्मप्रपोषणम् munirddhīmāndayādharmaprapoṣaṇam the wise sage said compassion nurtures dharma
निर्भयस्य nirbhayasya of the fearless
समं samaṃ together
दानं dānaṃ gift
न na not
भूतं bhūtaṃ has existed
न na not
भविष्यति bhaviṣyati will be born
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

tamuvāca munirddhīmāndayādharmaprapoṣaṇam || | nirbhayasya samaṃ dānaṃ na bhūtaṃ na bhaviṣyati || 5 ||

BP 3.2.19.6

अनर्हान्दण्डमादद्यादर्हपूजाफलं भजेत् ।
मित्रता गोद्विजे नित्यं समता दंडनिग्रहे ॥ 6 ॥

Punish those who deserve punishment, and enjoy the fruit of honoring the worthy. Be ever friendly toward cows and Brahmins, and impartial in administering punishment.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
अनर्हान्दण्डमादद्यादर्हपूजाफलं anarhāndaṇḍamādadyādarhapūjāphalaṃ punish the unworthy, reap the fruit of honoring the worthy
भजेत् bhajet should partake
मित्रता mitratā friendship
गोद्विजे godvije toward cows and Brahmins
नित्यं nityaṃ always
समता samatā impartiality
दंडनिग्रहे daṃḍanigrahe in administering punishment
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

anarhāndaṇḍamādadyādarhapūjāphalaṃ bhajet | mitratā godvije nityaṃ samatā daṃḍanigrahe || 6 ||

BP 3.2.19.7

सत्यता सुरपूजायां दमता गुरुपूजने ।
मृदुता दानसमये संतुष्टिर्निंद्यकर्मणि ॥ 7 ॥

Truthfulness in worship of the gods, self-control in honoring the teacher, gentleness when giving, and contentment regarding blameworthy action.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

सुरपूजायां surapūjāyāṃ in worship of the gods
दमता damatā self-control
गुरुपूजने gurupūjane in honoring the teacher
मृदुता mṛdutā gentleness
दानसमये dānasamaye at the time of giving
संतुष्टिर्निंद्यकर्मणि saṃtuṣṭirniṃdyakarmaṇi contentment regarding blameworthy action
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

satyatā surapūjāyāṃ damatā gurupūjane | mṛdutā dānasamaye saṃtuṣṭirniṃdyakarmaṇi || 7 ||

BP 3.2.19.8

इत्युक्त्वा स मुनिः पुत्रीं तस्मै दत्त्वा गृहं ययौ ।
राजापि च तया सार्द्धं वटमूलेऽशयिष्ट वै ॥ 8 ॥

Having spoken thus, the sage gave his daughter to the king and went home. The king too rested with her at the foot of a banyan tree.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

पुत्रीं putrīṃ the daughter
तस्मै tasmai for him
दत्त्वा dattvā having given
गृहं gṛhaṃ home
ययौ yayau went
राजापि rājāpi the king too

तया tayā by her
सार्द्धं sārddhaṃ together with
वटमूलेऽशयिष्ट vaṭamūle'śayiṣṭa slept at the foot of a banyan tree
वै vai indeed
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

ityuktvā sa muniḥ putrīṃ tasmai dattvā gṛhaṃ yayau | rājāpi ca tayā sārddhaṃ vaṭamūle'śayiṣṭa vai || 8 ||

BP 3.2.19.9

तदा तु राक्षसः कश्चित्तत्पत्नीभक्षणोत्सुकः ।
बोधयामास नृपतिं बलिं तस्मै स भूपतिः ॥ 9 ॥

Then a certain demon, eager to eat his wife, woke the king. The ruler offered a sacrificial gift to the demon.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
तदा tadā then
तु tu but
राक्षसः rākṣasaḥ a demon
कश्चित्तत्पत्नीभक्षणोत्सुकः kaścittatpatnībhakṣaṇotsukaḥ a certain demon eager to eat his wife
बोधयामास bodhayāmāsa awakened
नृपतिं nṛpatiṃ the king
बलिं baliṃ a sacrificial offering
तस्मै tasmai for him

भूपतिः bhūpatiḥ the king
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

tadā tu rākṣasaḥ kaścittatpatnībhakṣaṇotsukaḥ | bodhayāmāsa nṛpatiṃ baliṃ tasmai sa bhūpatiḥ || 9 ||

BP 3.2.19.10

दानार्थं चैव क्रव्यादे सप्तवर्षात्मकं द्विजम् ।
समयं कृतवान्राजा सत्येन स्वगृहं ययौ ॥ 10 ॥

The king promised to offer a seven-year-old Brahmin boy to the flesh-eater, then returned home to fulfill his word.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
दानार्थं dānārthaṃ for the purpose of an offering
चैव caiva and also
क्रव्यादे kravyāde to the flesh-eater
सप्तवर्षात्मकं saptavarṣātmakaṃ seven years old
द्विजम् dvijam a Brahmin
समयं samayaṃ the matter; the question
कृतवान्राजा kṛtavānrājā the king made
सत्येन satyena truthfully; to fulfill his promise
स्वगृहं svagṛhaṃ his own home
ययौ yayau went
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

dānārthaṃ caiva kravyāde saptavarṣātmakaṃ dvijam | samayaṃ kṛtavānrājā satyena svagṛhaṃ yayau || 10 ||

BP 3.2.19.11

अमात्यैः संमतं कृत्वा स्वर्णलक्षं ददौ द्विजे ।
मध्यं बालं पुरस्कृत्य राक्षसाय बलिं ददौ ॥ 11 ॥

With his ministers' consent, he gave a lakh of gold to a Brahmin. Placing the middle son before him, he offered the boy to the demon.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
अमात्यैः amātyaiḥ with the ministers
संमतं saṃmataṃ agreement
कृत्वा kṛtvā having done
स्वर्णलक्षं svarṇalakṣaṃ one lakh of gold
ददौ dadau gave
द्विजे dvije to a Brahmin
मध्यं madhyaṃ the middle
बालं bālaṃ boy
पुरस्कृत्य puraskṛtya having placed in front
राक्षसाय rākṣasāya to the demon
बलिं baliṃ a sacrificial offering
ददौ dadau gave
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

amātyaiḥ saṃmataṃ kṛtvā svarṇalakṣaṃ dadau dvije | madhyaṃ bālaṃ puraskṛtya rākṣasāya baliṃ dadau || 11 ||

BP 3.2.19.12

मृत्युकाले द्विजसुतो विहस्योच्चै रुरोद ह ।
कथं हास्यं कृतं तेन तत्पश्चाद्रोदनं कथम् ॥ 12 ॥

At the moment of death, the Brahmin's son laughed aloud and then wept. Vetāla asked why he laughed and why he wept afterward.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
मृत्युकाले mṛtyukāle at the time of death
द्विजसुतो dvijasuto a Brahmin's son
विहस्योच्चै vihasyoccai laughing aloud
रुरोद ruroda wept
ह ha indeed
कथं kathaṃ how
हास्यं hāsyaṃ laughter
कृतं kṛtaṃ done
तेन tena by him
तत्पश्चाद्रोदनं tatpaścādrodanaṃ weeping afterward
कथम् katham how; why
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

mṛtyukāle dvijasuto vihasyoccai ruroda ha | kathaṃ hāsyaṃ kṛtaṃ tena tatpaścādrodanaṃ katham || 12 ||

BP 3.2.19.13

इति श्रुत्वा नृपः प्राह शृणु वैतालिक द्विज ।
ज्येष्ठपुत्रं पितुर्हृद्यं मातृहृद्यमवर्यकम् ॥ 13 ॥

Hearing this, the king said: Listen, O Vetāla, O Brahmin. The eldest son is dear to his father, and the youngest is dear to his mother.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
इति iti thus
श्रुत्वा śrutvā having heard
नृपः nṛpaḥ king
प्राह prāha said
शृणु śṛṇu hear
वैतालिक vaitālika O Vetāla
द्विज dvija Brahmin
ज्येष्ठपुत्रं jyeṣṭhaputraṃ the eldest son
पितुर्हृद्यं piturhṛdyaṃ dear to the father
मातृहृद्यमवर्यकम् mātṛhṛdyamavaryakam dearest to the mother, the youngest
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

iti śrutvā nṛpaḥ prāha śṛṇu vaitālika dvija | jyeṣṭhaputraṃ piturhṛdyaṃ mātṛhṛdyamavaryakam || 13 ||

BP 3.2.19.14

ज्ञात्वा स मध्यमः पुत्रो राजानं शरणं ययौ ।
निर्दयी रूपसेनश्च पत्नीकल्याणभिक्षुकः ॥ 14 ॥

Knowing his place, the middle son sought refuge with the king. Yet Rūpasena was merciless, seeking his wife's welfare.

Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

मध्यमः madhyamaḥ intermediate
पुत्रो putro a son
राजानं rājānaṃ the king
शरणं śaraṇaṃ refuge
ययौ yayau went
निर्दयी nirdayī merciless
रूपसेनश्च rūpasenaśca and Rūpasena
पत्नीकल्याणभिक्षुकः patnīkalyāṇabhikṣukaḥ seeking his wife's welfare
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

jñātvā sa madhyamaḥ putro rājānaṃ śaraṇaṃ yayau | nirdayī rūpasenaśca patnīkalyāṇabhikṣukaḥ || 14 ||

BP 3.2.19.15

खङ्गहस्तं नृपं ज्ञात्वा जहास शिवतत्परः ।
राक्षसाय शरीरं मे प्राप्तमस्माद्रुरोद ह ॥ 15 ॥
