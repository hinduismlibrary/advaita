Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 161 — Question about the Fruits of Sun Worship

BP 1.161.1

शतानीक उवाच ।। एवमेतद्यथात्थ त्वं भास्करो दैवतं परम् ।। नास्त्यादित्यसमो देवो नास्त्यादित्यसमा गतिः ।। १ ।।

Śatānīka said: It is as you say: Bhāskara is the supreme deity. No god equals Āditya, and no destination equals Āditya’s.

BP 1.161.2

आदित्यमूलमखिलं त्रैलोक्यं नात्र संशयः ।। भवत्यस्माज्जगत्सर्वं सदेवासुरमानुषम् ।। २ ।।

The whole threefold world has Āditya as its root; there is no doubt. From him this entire world arises, with gods, demons, and humans.

BP 1.161.3

रुद्रेन्द्रोपेन्द्रकेन्द्राणां विप्रेंद्र त्रिदिवौकसाम् ।। द्युतिर्द्युतिमतां कृत्स्ना तेजो यत्सार्वलौकिकम् ।। ३ ।।

The splendor of Rudra, Indra, Upendra, and Kendra, O best of Brahmins, is the full radiance of the shining ones, the light belonging to all the worlds of heaven.

BP 1.161.4

सर्वात्मा सर्वलोकेशो महादेवः प्रजापतिः ।। सूर्य एव त्रिलोकस्य मूलं परमदैवतम् ।। ४ ।।

The self of all, lord of all worlds, great god, and lord of creatures: the Sun alone is the root of the three worlds, the supreme deity.

BP 1.161.5

ततः संजायते सर्वं तत्रैव प्रविलीयते ।। भावाभावौ हि लोकनामादित्यान्निःसृतौ पुरा ।। ५ ।।

From him all things arise, and into him alone they dissolve. The worlds’ existence and nonexistence issued forth from Āditya long ago.

BP 1.161.6

जगज्ज्येष्ठो ग्रहो विप्र प्रदीप्तः प्रभवो रविः ।। तत्र गच्छंति निधनं जायंते च पुनः पुनः ।। ६ ।।

The eldest planet of the world, O Brahmin, shining, the source, is Ravi; beings go to their end in him and are born again and again.

BP 1.161.7

क्षणा मुहूर्ता दिवसा रात्रिपक्षाश्च कृत्स्नशः ।। मासाः संवत्सराश्चैव ऋतवश्च युगानि च ।। ७ ।।

Moments, forty-eight-minute periods, days, nights and fortnights in their entirety, months, years, seasons, and world-ages—

BP 1.161.8

स एष कालश्चाग्निश्च द्वादशात्मा प्रजापतिः ।। प्रभासयति विप्रेंद्र त्रैलोक्यं सचराचरम् ।। ८ ।।

he is time and fire, the twelve-formed lord of creatures; he illumines, O best of Brahmins, the three worlds with all that moves and does not move.

BP 1.161.9

तस्मादस्य द्विजश्रेष्ठ पूजने यत्फलं भवेत् ।। तन्मे ब्रूहि प्रयत्नेन प्रसादप्रवणो भव ।। ९ ।।

Therefore, O best of Brahmins, tell me carefully what fruit comes from his worship; be inclined to show me favor.
