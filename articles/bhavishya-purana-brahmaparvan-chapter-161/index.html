Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 161 — Question about the Fruits of Sun Worship

BP 1.161.1

शतानीक उवाच ।। एवमेतद्यथात्थ त्वं भास्करो दैवतं परम् ।। नास्त्यादित्यसमो देवो नास्त्यादित्यसमा गतिः ।। १ ।।

Śatānīka said: It is as you say: Bhāskara is the supreme deity. No god equals Āditya, and no destination equals Āditya’s.
