VP 5.28.1

चारुदेष्णं सुदेष्णं च चारुदेहं च वीर्यवान् ।
सुषेणं चारुगुप्तं च भद्रचारुं तथा परम्

Rukmiṇī bore the mighty Cāru­deṣṇa, Sudeṣṇa, Cārudeha, Suṣeṇa, Cārugupta, and Bhadracāru.

Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

cārudeṣṇaṃ sudeṣṇaṃ ca cārudehaṃ ca vīryavān | suṣeṇaṃ cāruguptaṃ ca bhadracāruṃ tathā param

Śrīdhara

cārudeṣṇam iti | vīryavān iti vīryavantam ity arthaḥ || 1 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

चारुदेष्णम् इति । वीर्यवान् इति वीर्यवन्तम् इत्य् अर्थः ॥१॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

“Cārudeṣṇa” means: “mighty” means mighty.

VP 5.28.4

देवी जाम्बवती चापि रोहिणी कामरूपिणी ।
मद्रराजसुता चान्या सुशीला शीलमण्डना

There were also the goddess Jāmbavatī, Rohiṇī of beautiful forms, and another, the daughter of the king of Madra, named Suśīlā, adorned with good character.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
देवी (devī) — goddess
जाम्बवती (jāmbavatī) — Jāmbavatī
च अपि (ca api) — also
रोहिणी (rohiṇī) — Rohiṇī
काम-रूपिणी (kāma-rūpiṇī) — beautiful-formed
मद्र-राज-सुता (madra-rāja-sutā) — daughter of the king of Madra
च अन्या (ca anyā) — and another
सुशीला (suśīlā) — Suśīlā
शील-मण्डना (śīla-maṇḍanā) — adorned with good character.
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

devī jāmbavatī cāpi rohiṇī kāmarūpiṇī | madrarājasutā cānyā suśīlā śīlamaṇḍanā

Śrīdhara

devīti | jāmbavaty eva rohiṇī-sañjñā | ato na saṅkhyādhikyam | ata eva tasyā ṛkṣa-jātitvāt kāma-rūpiṇīti viśeṣaṇam | kecid rohiṇī nāma paṭṭa-mahiṣī-tulyānyā iti prāhuḥ | tathā na upariṣṭhāt tayoḥ santāna-bodhoktir upapatsyate || 4 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

देवीति । जाम्बवत्य् एव रोहिणी-सञ्ज्ञा । अतो न सङ्ख्याधिक्यम् । अत एव तस्या ऋक्ष-जातित्वात् काम-रूपिणीति विशेषणम् । केचिद् रोहिणी नाम पट्ट-महिषी-तुल्यान्या इति प्राहुः । तथा न उपरिष्ठात् तयोः सन्तान-बोधोक्तिर् उपपत्स्यते ॥४॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

“Goddess” means: Jāmbavatī herself is named Rohiṇī, so there is no increase in the number [of wives]. For this very reason, because she is of the bear-kind, she is described as having a desirable form. Some say that Rohiṇī is another woman equal to a chief queen; otherwise, the later statement concerning the offspring of the two would not be possible.

द्वारकामाजगामाथ यदुचक्रं च केशवः

Then Keśava took Aniruddha, who was married, and returned to Dvārakā; the Yadu host came with him, O best of the twice-born.
