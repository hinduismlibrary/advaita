Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 137 — Pratiṣṭhāpana Vidhi

BP 1.137.1

नारद उवाच । यः प्रासादं रचयति पुमान्देवतानां प्रयत्नात्तत्र प्रीत्या सपदि कुरुते स्थापनां भानुभक्तः । दिव्यान्भोगाँल्लभति च सदा कामतश्चाप्रमेयांस्तान्भुक्त्वासौ पुनरपि भवेच्चक्रवर्ती पृथिव्याम् ॥

Nārada said: The man who, with effort, builds a temple for the deities and promptly performs its installation there with devotion, devoted to Bhānu, always obtains divine enjoyments, immeasurable and according to his desire; having enjoyed them, he again becomes a cakravartin on earth.

BP 1.137.2

ये मानवास्त्रिदशमूर्तिनिकेतनानि कुर्वन्ति साधुजनदृष्टिमनोहराणि । तेषां मृतेऽप्यपरमार्थमये शरीरे लोके परिभ्रमति कीर्तिमयं शरीरम् ॥

Those humans who make abodes for the images of the gods, pleasing to the sight of good people: even after they have died, when the body is of no lasting reality, a body made of fame wanders in the world.

BP 1.137.4

स्नातो भुक्तो वस्त्रालङ्कृतकुसुमैर्गन्धैः प्रतिमाया आस्तीर्णायां शय्यायां स्थापनं कुर्यात् । सुप्तायां तु स नृत्यगीतैर्जागरणैः सम्यगेवाधिवास्य दैवज्ञेन प्रतिदिष्टकाले संस्थापनं कुर्यात् । अभ्यर्च्य कुसुमगन्धानुलेपनैः शङ्खतूर्यनिर्घोषैः प्रादक्षिण्येन नयेदायतनस्य प्रयत्नेन कृत्वा बलिं प्रतिमामभ्यर्च्य ब्राह्मणांश्च साधून्दत्त्वा हिरण्यकलशं विधिना निक्षिपेत्पिंण्डिकामध्ये सुश्वभ्रे स्थापकदैवज्ञद्विजान्सम्यग्विशेषतोऽभर्च्याकल्पान्तं भोगी भवतीह परत्र सुखी ॥

Having bathed, eaten, and been adorned with garments, ornaments, flowers and scents, one should place the image on a spread bed. When it has slept, one should properly keep vigil with dances, songs and wakeful rites; then, at the time indicated by the astrologer, one should perform the installation. Having worshipped it with flowers and scented unguents, amid the sounds of conches and instruments, one should lead it by circumambulation to the temple with care. Having made the bali offering, worshipped the image, and given to Brāhmaṇas and sādhus, one should place a golden vessel according to rule in a good pit within the pedestal. Having properly and especially worshipped the installer, astrologer and twice-born, one becomes an enjoyer until the end of a kalpa, happy here and hereafter.

BP 1.137.5

विष्णोर्भागवता मताश्च सवितुः शम्भोः सभस्मद्विजा मातॄणामपि मातृमण्डलविदो विप्रा विदुर्ब्राह्मणाः । सर्वे यस्य विमुक्तशुक्लवसना बुद्धस्य रक्ताम्बरा ये यं देवमुपाश्रिताः सुविधिना तैस्तस्य कार्या क्रिया ॥

For Viṣṇu, the Bhāgavatas are approved; for Savitṛ and Śambhu, the twice-born with ash; for the Mothers, Brāhmaṇas who know the Mothers’ circle. Those who have laid aside white garments wear red for Buddha. Those who have taken refuge in whichever deity should perform that deity’s rite with proper procedure.
