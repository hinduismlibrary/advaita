Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 137 — Installation of the Deities

BP 1.137.1

नारद उवाच । यः प्रासादं रचयति पुमान्देवतानां प्रयत्नात्तत्र प्रीत्या सपदि कुरुते स्थापनां भानुभक्तः । दिव्यान्भोगाँल्लभति च सदा कामतश्चाप्रमेयांस्तान्भुक्त्वासौ पुनरपि भवेच्चक्रवर्ती पृथिव्याम् ॥

Nārada said: “The man who, with effort, builds a temple for the deities and promptly performs its installation there with devotion, devoted to Bhānu, always obtains immeasurable heavenly pleasures as he desires; having enjoyed them, he again becomes an emperor on earth.”

BP 1.137.2

ये मानवास्त्रिदशमूर्तिनिकेतनानि कुर्वन्ति साधुजनदृष्टिमनोहराणि । तेषां मृतेऽप्यपरमार्थमये शरीरे लोके परिभ्रमति कीर्तिमयं शरीरम् ॥

“Those people who build abodes for the images of the gods, pleasing to the sight of good people—even after they die, while the body is without lasting substance, a body made of fame wanders through the world.”

BP 1.137.3

इति ते कथितमिदं देवपूज्यस्य सवितुः स्थापनमिवाधानम् । साधारणं विधानं शृणु देवानां प्रतिष्ठापने वीर ॥

“Thus I have told you of the setting up of Savitṛ, worshipped by the gods. Hear the general procedure for installing deities, O hero.”

BP 1.137.4

स्नातो भुक्तो वस्त्रालङ्कृतकुसुमैर्गन्धैः प्रतिमाया आस्तीर्णायां शय्यायां स्थापनं कुर्यात् । सुप्तायां तु स नृत्यगीतैर्जागरणैः सम्यगेवाधिवास्य दैवज्ञेन प्रतिदिष्टकाले संस्थापनं कुर्यात् । अभ्यर्च्य कुसुमगन्धानुलेपनैः शङ्खतूर्यनिर्घोषैः प्रादक्षिण्येन नयेदायतनस्य प्रयत्नेन कृत्वा बलिं प्रतिमामभ्यर्च्य ब्राह्मणांश्च साधून्दत्त्वा हिरण्यकलशं विधिना निक्षिपेत्पिंण्डिकामध्ये सुश्वभ्रे स्थापकदैवज्ञद्विजान्सम्यग्विशेषतोऽभ्यर्च्याकल्पान्तं भोगी भवतीह परत्र सुखी ॥

“After bathing and eating, one should carry out the installation, setting the image on a bed spread with garments, ornaments, flowers, and perfumes. When it has been laid to rest, one should keep a proper vigil with dances, songs, and wakeful rites; then one should perform the installation at the time indicated by the astrologer. Having worshipped it with flowers and scented ointments amid the sounds of conches and trumpets, one should carefully lead it around the shrine. Having made the food offering, worshipped the image, and given gifts to Brahmins and good men, one should place a golden vessel according to rule in a fine pit at the center of the pedestal. Having especially and properly worshipped the installer, astrologer, and twice-born men, one enjoys here until the end of an age and is happy hereafter.”

BP 1.137.5

विष्णोर्भागवता मताश्च सवितुः शम्भोः सभस्मद्विजा मातॄणामपि मातृमण्डलविदो विप्रा विदुर्ब्राह्मणाः । सर्वे यस्य विमुक्तशुक्लवसना बुद्धस्य रक्ताम्बरा ये यं देवमुपाश्रिताः सुविधिना तैस्तस्य कार्या क्रिया ॥

“For Viṣṇu, devotees of Viṣṇu are prescribed; for Savitṛ and Śambhu, twice-born men bearing ashes; for the Mothers, Brahmins who know the circle of the Mothers. Those who have set aside white garments wear red for Buddha. Those who have taken refuge in whichever deity should perform that deity’s rite according to the proper rule.”

BP 1.137.6

सामान्यमिदं देवानामधिवासनं भवति मया कथितम् । क्रियमाणमिदं दृष्ट्वा देवानां प्रतिष्ठापनम् । नरो भक्त्या इह कामानवाप्य स्वर्गभाजनं भवति ॥

“This common preliminary consecration of the deities has been told by me. Seeing this installation of the deities being performed, a man, through devotion, obtains his desires here and becomes a partaker of heaven.”

BP 1.137.7

इदं ते कथितं राजन्प्रतिष्ठापनमादितः । यत्कृत्वा सवितुः स्नानं नरो याति मनोगतिम् ॥

“This installation has been told to you from the beginning, O king. By performing the bathing of Savitṛ, a man reaches the goal he desires.”

BP 1.137.8

इत्थं कुर्यान्नरो भक्त्या सवितुः स्थापनं बुधः । कारयेत्पुरतो भक्तया सवितुः स्थापनं बुधः ॥

“Thus the wise man should establish Savitṛ with devotion. With devotion, he should have Savitṛ’s installation performed in front.”

BP 1.137.9

इतिहासपुराणस्य श्रवणं पापनाशनम् । ताभ्यां हि श्रवणाद्वीर सान्निध्यं याति भास्करः ॥

“Listening to the epics and Purāṇas destroys sin. Indeed, through listening to these two, Bhāskara draws near, O hero.”

BP 1.137.10

कृते त्वायतने तस्मिन्ये चान्ये चापि देवताः । तस्मात्कार्यं बुधैर्नित्यं धर्मश्रवणमादितः ॥

“When that shrine has been made, and the other deities as well, the wise should always arrange for the hearing of dharma first.”

BP 1.137.11

वाचकं पूजयित्वा तु ब्राह्मणानुपपूज्य च । कारयेद्वाचनं वीर पुस्तकस्याग्रतो रवेः ॥

“Having worshipped the reciter and also honored the Brahmins, one should have the book recited before Ravi, O hero.”

BP 1.137.12

सर्वस्वं स्थापके दद्याद्यत्किञ्जिद्गृहमागतम् । गोदानमथवा दद्यात्तस्य चित्तं प्रसादयेत् ॥

“One should give the installer everything that has come into the house; or one should give the gift of a cow and gladden his heart.”

BP 1.137.13

इत्येष कथितो वीर प्रतिष्ठाकल्प आदितः । कृत्वा दृष्ट्वा च श्रुत्वा च यं नरोऽर्कमवाप्नुयात् ॥

“Thus, O hero, this procedure for installation has been told from the beginning. By performing, seeing, and hearing it, a man attains Arka.”
