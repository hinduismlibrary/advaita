BP 1.137.1

BP 1.137.4

स्नातो भुक्तो वस्त्रालङ्कृतकुसुमैर्गन्धैः प्रतिमाया आस्तीर्णायां शय्यायां स्थापनं कुर्यात् । सुप्तायां तु स नृत्यगीतैर्जागरणैः सम्यगेवाधिवास्य दैवज्ञेन प्रतिदिष्टकाले संस्थापनं कुर्यात् । अभ्यर्च्य कुसुमगन्धानुलेपनैः शङ्खतूर्यनिर्घोषैः प्रादक्षिण्येन नयेदायतनस्य प्रयत्नेन कृत्वा बलिं प्रतिमामभ्यर्च्य ब्राह्मणांश्च साधून्दत्त्वा हिरण्यकलशं विधिना निक्षिपेत्पिंण्डिकामध्ये सुश्वभ्रे स्थापकदैवज्ञद्विजान्सम्यग्विशेषतोऽभ्यर्च्याकल्पान्तं भोगी भवतीह परत्र सुखी ॥

“After bathing and eating, one should carry out the installation, setting the image on a bed spread with garments, ornaments, flowers, and perfumes. When it has been laid to rest, one should keep a proper vigil with dances, songs, and wakeful rites; then one should perform the installation at the time indicated by the astrologer. Having worshipped it with flowers and scented ointments amid the sounds of conches and trumpets, one should carefully lead it around the shrine. Having made the food offering, worshipped the image, and given gifts to Brahmins and good men, one should place a golden vessel according to rule in a fine pit at the center of the pedestal. Having especially and properly worshipped the installer, astrologer, and twice-born men, one enjoys here until the end of an age and is happy hereafter.”
