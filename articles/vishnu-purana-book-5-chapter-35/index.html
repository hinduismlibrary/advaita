VP 5.35.1

श्रीमैत्रेय उवाच

भूय एवाहमिच्छामि बलभद्रस्य धीमतः ।
श्रोतुं पराक्रमं ब्रह्मन् तन्ममाख्यातुमर्हसि

Maitreya said: I wish once more to hear of the wise Balabhadra’s prowess. O brāhmaṇa, you should tell me about it.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
भूयः एव अहम् इच्छामि (bhūyaḥ eva aham icchāmi) — I wish again
बलभद्रस्य धीमतः (balabhadrasya dhīmataḥ) — of wise Balabhadra
श्रोतुम् (śrotum) — to hear
पराक्रमम् (parākramam) — the prowess
तन् मम आख्यातुम् अर्हसि (tan mama ākhyātum arhasi) — you should tell that to me
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bhūya evāhamicchāmi balabhadrasya dhīmataḥ | śrotuṃ parākramaṃ brahman tanmamākhyātumarhasi

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.35.2

यमुनाकर्षणादीनि श्रुतानि भगवन्मया ।
तत्कथ्यतां महाभाग यदन्यत्कृतवान्बलः

I have heard of his drawing the Yamunā and such deeds. O greatly blessed one, tell me what else Baladeva did.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
यमुना-आकर्षण-आदीनि (yamunā-ākarṣaṇa-ādīni) — drawing the Yamunā and such deeds
श्रुतानि (śrutāni) — heard
भगवन् मया (bhagavan mayā) — O blessed one, by me
तत् कथ्यताम् (tat kathyatām) — let that be told
यत् अन्यत् कृतवान् बलः (yat anyat kṛtavān balaḥ) — what else Bala did
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yamunākarṣaṇādīni śrutāni bhagavanmayā | tatkathyatāṃ mahābhāga yadanyatkṛtavānabalaḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.35.3

श्रीपराशर उवाच

मैत्रेय श्रूयतां कर्म यद्रामेणाभवत्कृतम् ।
अनन्तेनाप्रमेयेन शेषेण धरणीधृता

Parāśara said: Maitreya, hear what was done by Rāma, the immeasurable Ananta, the Śeṣa who supports the earth.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
मैत्रेय (maitreya) — O Maitreya
श्रूयताम् (śrūyatām) — let it be heard
कर्म रामेण कृतम् (karma rāmeṇa kṛtam) — the deed done by Rāma
अनन्तेन अप्रमेयेन (anantena aprameyena) — by immeasurable Ananta
शेषेण धरणी-धृता (śeṣeṇa dharaṇī-dhṛtā) — Śeṣa who supports the earth
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

maitreya śrūyatāṃ karma yadrāmeṇābhavatkṛtam | anantenāprameyena śeṣeṇa dharaṇīdhṛtā

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.35.4

सुयोधनस्य तनयां स्वयंवरकृतक्षणाम् ।
बलादादत्तवान्वीरस्साम्बो जाम्बवतीसुतः

The heroic Sāmba, son of Jāmbavatī, forcibly took the daughter of Suyodhana when the occasion for her svayaṃvara had arrived.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
सुयोधनस्य तनयाम् (suyodhanasya tanayām) — Suyodhana’s daughter
स्वयंवर-कृत-क्षणाम् (svayaṃvara-kṛta-kṣaṇām) — for whom the svayaṃvara occasion had arrived
बलात् (balāt) — by force
आदत्तवान् (ādattavān) — took
वीरः साम्बः (vīraḥ sāmbaḥ) — the hero Sāmba
जाम्बवती-सुतः (jāmbavatī-sutaḥ) — son of Jāmbavatī
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

suyodhanasya tanayāṃ svayaṃvarakṛtakṣaṇām | balādādattavānvīrassāmbo jāmbavatīsutaḥ

Śrīdhara

yathā sakala-bala-janakośādi-sahitāṃ vāraṇāsīṃ viṣṇu-cakraṃ dadāha, tathaiva sarva-sahitaṃ hastināpuraṃ balabhadro gaṅgāyāṃ prakṣeputm udyata iti vaktuṃ tat-prastāvam āha—suyodhanasyety-ādinā viviśur gaja-sāhvayām ity antena | suyodhanasyeti | svayaṃvare kṛtakṣaṇāṃ svayaṃvara-kṛta-kṣaṇāṃ labdhāvasarām || 4 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

यथा सकल-बल-जनकोशादि-सहितां वारणासीं विष्णु-चक्रं ददाह, तथैव सर्व-सहितं हस्तिनापुरं बलभद्रो गङ्गायां प्रक्षेपुत्म् उद्यत इति वक्तुं तत्-प्रस्तावम् आह—सुयोधनस्येत्य्-आदिना विविशुर् गज-साह्वयाम् इत्य् अन्तेन । सुयोधनस्येति । स्वयंवरे कृतक्षणां स्वयंवर-कृत-क्षणां लब्धावसराम् ॥४ ॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Śrīdhara explains that the svayaṃvara occasion had arrived; Sāmba took her by force.

VP 5.35.6

VP 5.35.7

तान्निवार्य बलः प्राह मदलोलकलाक्षरम् ।
मोक्ष्यन्ति ते मद्वचनाद्यास्याम्येको हि कौरवान्

Baladeva restrained them and said in a voice unsteady with intoxication: “They will release Sāmba at my word. I alone will go; who are the Kauravas?”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
तान् निवार्य (tān nivārya) — restraining them
बलः प्राह (balaḥ prāha) — Bala said
मद-लोल-कल-अक्षरम् (mada-lola-kala-akṣaram) — in a voice unsteady with intoxication
मोक्ष्यन्ति ते (mokṣyanti te) — they will release
मत्-वचनात् (mat-vacanāt) — at my word
यास्यामि एकः (yāsyāmi ekaḥ) — I alone will go
कः हि कौरवान् (kaḥ hi kauravān) — who indeed are the Kauravas
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tānnivārya balaḥ prāha madalolakalākṣaram | mokṣyanti te madvacanādyāsyāmye ko hi kauravān

Śrīdhara

tān iti | madena lolaṃ capalaṃ kalākṣaraṃ yathā bhavaty evaṃ prāha || 7 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तान् इति । मदेन लोलं चपलं कलाक्षरं यथा भवत्य् एवं प्राह ॥७ ॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Bala’s voice was made unsteady and playful by intoxication.

VP 5.35.8

श्रीपराशर उवाच

बलदेवस्ततो गत्वा नगरं नागसाह्वयम् ।
बाह्योपवनमध्येऽभून्न विवेश च तत्पुरम्

Then Baladeva went to the city called Nāgasāhvaya. He stayed in the outer garden and did not enter the city.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
बलदेवः ततः गत्वा (baladevaḥ tataḥ gatvā) — then Baladeva went
नगरम् नाग-साह्वयम् (nagaram nāga-sāhvayam) — to the city Nāgasāhvaya
बाह्य-उपवन-मध्ये (bāhya-upavana-madhye) — in the outer garden
अभूत् (abhūt) — he stayed
न विवेश (na viveśa) — did not enter
तत् पुरम् (tat puram) — that city
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

baladevastato gatvā nagaraṃ nāgasāhvayam | bāhyopavanamadhye’bhūnna viveśa ca tatpuram

Śrīdhara

baladeva iti | tat-puraṃ na viveśa svokty-anaṅgīkāre raṇe tair yoddhuṃ tat-puraṃ ca gaṅgāyāṃ parkṣeptum icchan || 8 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

बलदेव इति । तत्-पुरं न विवेश स्वोक्त्य्-अनङ्गीकारे रणे तैर् योद्धुं तत्-पुरं च गङ्गायां पर्क्षेप्तुम् इच्छन् ॥८ ॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Baladeva did not enter the city because, if the Kauravas rejected his words, he intended to fight them and throw the city into the Gaṅgā.

VP 5.35.9

बलमागतमाज्ञाय भूपा दुर्योधनादयः ।

गाम् (gām) — a cow
अर्घ्यम् उदकम् च (arghyam udakam ca) — arghya and water

Śrīdhara

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

They offered Rāma water and pādya, the water for washing the feet.

VP 5.35.14

उग्रसेनोऽपि यद्याज्ञां कौरवाणां प्रदास्यति ।
तदलं पाण्डुरैश्छत्रैर्नृपयोग्यैर्विडम्बनैः

“Even if Ugrasena issues an order to the Kauravas, then enough of these pale royal parasols and other royal pretensions—what a mockery!”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
उग्रसेनः अपि (ugrasenaḥ api) — even Ugrasena
यदि आज्ञाम् प्रदास्यति (yadi ājñām pradāsyati) — if he gives an order

Transliteration (IAST)

ugraseno’pi yadyājñāṃ kauravāṇāṃ pradāsyati | tadalaṃ pāṇḍuraiśchatrairnṛpayogyairviḍambanaiḥ

Śrīdhara

ugrasena iti | viḍambitaiḥ asmad-anusaraṇena dhṛtair yamānāṃ nṛpārhaiś chatrair alaṃ tāny apahariṣyāma iti bhāvaḥ || 14 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

उग्रसेन इति । विडम्बितैः अस्मद्-अनुसरणेन धृतैर् यमानां नृपार्हैश् छत्रैर् अलं तान्य् अपहरिष्याम इति भावः ॥१४ ॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Their royal parasols were held in imitation of the insignia of kings; he means that the Yādavas would take them away.

VP 5.35.15

तद्गच्छ बल मा वा त्वं साम्बमन्यायचेष्टितम् ।
विमोक्ष्यामो न भवतश्चोग्रसेनस्य शासनात्

“Go, Baladeva, or do not go; we shall not release Sāmba because of your command or Ugrasena’s order, for his conduct was improper.”

Word-for-word
