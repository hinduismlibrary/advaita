Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 131 — The Examination of Sacred Timber

BP 1.131.1

नारद उवाच
अथ ते सम्प्रवक्ष्यामि प्रतिमाविधिविस्तरम्।
सर्वेषामेव देवानामादित्यस्य विशेषतः ॥

Nārada said: Now I shall explain fully to you the procedure for images of all the gods, especially of the Sun.

नारद उवाच(nārada uvāca) — Nārada said;
अथ(atha) — now;
ते(te) — to you;
सम्प्रवक्ष्यामि(sampravakṣyāmi) — I shall explain fully;
प्रतिमा(pratimā) — image;
विधि(vidhi) — rule;
विस्तरम्(vistaram) — in detail;
सर्वेषाम्(sarveṣām) — of all;
देवानाम्(devānām) — gods;
आदित्यस्य(ādityasya) — of Āditya;
विशेषतः(viśeṣataḥ) — especially;

BP 1.131.2

अर्चा सप्तविधा प्रोक्ता भक्तानां शुभवृद्धये।
काञ्चनी राजती ताम्री पार्थिवी शैलजा स्मृता ॥

Images are declared to be seven kinds, for increasing the good fortune of devotees: golden, silver, copper, earthen, and stone.

अर्चा(arcā) — image;
सप्तविधा(saptavidhā) — sevenfold;
प्रोक्ता(proktā) — is declared;
भक्तानाम्(bhaktānām) — for devotees;
शुभ(śubha) — good fortune;
वृद्धये(vṛddhaye) — for increase;
काञ्चनी(kāñcanī) — golden;
राजती(rājatī) — silver;
ताम्री(tāmrī) — copper;
पार्थिवी(pārthivī) — earthen;
शैलजा(śailajā) — made of stone;
स्मृता(smṛtā) — is remembered;

BP 1.131.3

वार्क्षी चालेख्यका चेति मूर्तिस्थानानि सप्त वै।
वार्क्षीविधानं ते वीर वर्णयिष्याम्यशेषतः ॥

Wooden and painted images make seven kinds in all. O hero, I shall describe fully to you the procedure for wooden images.

वार्क्षी(vārkṣī) — wooden;
च(ca) — and;

इति(iti) — thus;

स्थानानि(sthānāni) — materials;
सप्त(sapta) — seven;
वै(vai) — indeed;
वार्क्षी(vārkṣī) — wooden-image;
विधानम्(vidhānam) — procedure;
ते(te) — to you;
वीर(vīra) — O hero;
वर्णयिष्यामि(varṇayiṣyāmi) — I shall describe;
अशेषतः(aśeṣataḥ) — fully;

BP 1.131.4

कर्त्रनुकूले दिवसे संवत्सरविशोधिते।
शुभैर्निमित्तैः शकुनैः प्रस्थानैश्च वनं विशेत् ॥

On a day favorable to the undertaking, after examining the year, one should enter the forest amid good omens, auspicious signs, and favorable departures.

कर्तृ(kartṛ) — maker;
अनुकूले(anukūle) — favorable;
दिवसे(divase) — on a day;
संवत्सर(saṃvatsara) — year;
विशोधिते(viśodhite) — properly examined;
शुभैः(śubhaiḥ) — auspicious;
निमित्तैः(nimittaiḥ) — omens;
शकुनैः(śakunaiḥ) — bird-signs;
प्रस्थानैः(prasthānaiḥ) — departure signs;
च(ca) — and;
वनम्(vanam) — forest;
विशेत्(viśet) — one should enter;

BP 1.131.5

क्षीरिणो वर्जिताः सर्वे दुर्बलास्ते स्वभावतः।
चतुष्पथेषु न ग्राह्या ये च पुत्रकवृक्षकाः ॥

All milky trees are to be avoided, as are those naturally weak; trees at crossroads and young trees are not to be taken.

क्षीरिणः(kṣīriṇaḥ) — milky trees;
वर्जिताः(varjitāḥ) — are to be avoided;
सर्वे(sarve) — all;
दुर्बलाः(durbalāḥ) — weak;
ते(te) — they;
स्वभावतः(svabhāvataḥ) — by nature;
चतुष्पथेषु(catuṣpatheṣu) — at crossroads;
न(na) — not;
ग्राह्याः(grāhyāḥ) — to be selected;
ये(ye) — those which;
च(ca) — and;
पुत्रक(putraka) — young;
वृक्षकाः(vṛkṣakāḥ) — trees;

BP 1.131.6

देवतायतनस्था ये तथा वल्मीकसम्भवा।
उत्कीर्णा देवता येषु चैत्यवृक्षाश्च ये स्मृताः ॥

Trees standing at shrines or growing from anthills, those carved with deities, and those known as sacred-shrine trees are to be avoided.

वल्मीक(valmīka) — anthill;
सम्भवाः(sambhavāḥ) — grown from;
उत्कीर्णाः(utkīrṇāḥ) — carved;
देवताः(devatāḥ) — deities;
येषु(yeṣu) — in which;
चैत्यवृक्षाः(caityavṛkṣāḥ) — sacred shrine-trees;
च(ca) — and;
ये(ye) — which;
स्मृताः(smṛtāḥ) — are remembered;

BP 1.131.7

श्मशानभूमिजा ये च पक्षिणां निलयाश्च ये।
सकोटराश्च ये वृक्षाः शुष्काग्रा ये च पादपाः ॥

Trees growing in cremation grounds or serving as birds’ nests, hollow trees, and trees with dried-out tops are to be avoided.

श्मशान(śmaśāna) — cremation ground;
भूमिजाः(bhūmijāḥ) — grown from the ground;
ये(ye) — which;
च(ca) — and;
पक्षिणाम्(pakṣiṇām) — of birds;
निलयाः(nilayāḥ) — abodes;
सकोटराः(sakoṭarāḥ) — having hollows;
वृक्षाः(vṛkṣāḥ) — trees;
शुष्क(śuṣka) — dry;
अग्राः(agrāḥ) — tops;
पादपाः(pādapāḥ) — trees;

BP 1.131.8

शस्त्रेण निहता ये च कुञ्जराश्च तथा कृताः।
सामाद्याः सरुजोऽधश्च व्याधिनश्च तथैव च ॥

Trees struck by weapons or damaged by elephants, diseased at the top or below, and otherwise afflicted are to be avoided.

शस्त्रेण(śastreṇa) — by a weapon;
निहताः(nihatāḥ) — struck down;
ये(ye) — which;
कुञ्जरैः(kuñjaraiḥ) — by elephants;
च(ca) — and;
तथा(tathā) — likewise;
कृताः(kṛtāḥ) — damaged;
सरुजः(sarujaḥ) — diseased;
अधः(adhaḥ) — decayed;
च(ca) — and;
व्याधिनः(vyādhinaḥ) — ill;
तथा एव(tathā eva) — likewise;

BP 1.131.9

अकाले पुष्पिता ये च काले ते च विवर्जिताः।
शीर्णपर्णाश्च तरवो रक्षोध्वांक्षनिषेविताः।
एकशाखातिशाखाश्च त्रिशाखाश्च तथाधमाः ॥

Trees that flower out of season, and those that flower in season, are to be avoided; likewise trees with withered leaves, frequented by demons and crows, with one branch, too many branches, or three branches, and other inferior trees.

अकाले(akāle) — out of season;
पुष्पिताः(puṣpitāḥ) — flowering;
ये(ye) — which;
काले(kāle) — in season;
ते(te) — those;
विवर्जिताः(vivarjitāḥ) — are avoided;
शीर्ण(śīrṇa) — withered;
पर्णाः(parṇāḥ) — leaves;
तरवः(taravaḥ) — trees;
रक्षः(rakṣaḥ) — demons;
ध्वाङ्क्ष(dhvāṅkṣa) — crows;
निषेविताः(niṣevitāḥ) — frequented;
एक(eka) — one;
शाखाः(śākhāḥ) — branches;
अति(ati) — excessive;
च(ca) — and;
त्रि(tri) — three;
तथा(tathā) — likewise;
अधमाः(adhamāḥ) — inferior;

BP 1.131.10

मधूको देवदारुश्च वृक्षराजश्च चन्दनः।
बिल्वश्चाम्रातकश्चैव खदिरोथाञ्जनस्तथा ॥

BP 1.131.11

निम्बः श्रीपर्णवृक्षश्च पनसः सरलार्जुनः।
रक्तचन्दनपर्यन्ताः श्रेष्ठाः स्युः प्रतिमाद्रुमाः ॥

Neem, white teak, jackfruit, chir pine, Arjun tree, and trees up to red sandalwood are excellent for images.

द्रुमाः(drumāḥ) — trees;

BP 1.131.12

वर्णानामानुपूर्व्येण द्वौ द्वौ वृक्षौ प्रकीर्तितौ।

Two trees each are declared in order for the varnas; trees beginning with neem are remembered as common to all varnas.

वर्णानाम्(varṇānām) — for the classes;

BP 1.131.19

BP 1.131.21
