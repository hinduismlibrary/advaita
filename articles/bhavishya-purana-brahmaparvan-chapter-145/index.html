BP 1.145.1

वासुदेव उवाच । ज्ञानोपलब्धिं विप्रेन्द्र भोजकानां महामुने । ब्रूहि तत्त्वं द्विजश्रेष्ठ कौतुकं परमं मम ॥

Vāsudeva said: Tell the attainment of knowledge of the Bhojakas, O lord of brāhmins, great sage. Tell the truth, O best of the twice-born; my curiosity is supreme.

This, equal to nectar, is to be known as the supreme; the immortal thousand-rayed one is beneficial to people. Having understood the essence of truth in the sages' conduct, those whose minds' delusion is gone attain liberation.

BP 1.145.26
