Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 92 — The Mahāśvetā Observance

BP 1.92.1

ब्रह्मोवाच । यस्त्वादित्यग्रहस्यास्य वारो देवस्य सुव्रत । शस्यः प्रोक्तो प्रियो लोके ख्यातो गोश्रुतिभूषणः ॥ ॥

Brahmā said: O well-disciplined one, the day of this Āditya observance is proclaimed auspicious and dear in the world, famed as Gośruti-bhūṣaṇa.

Word-for-word
brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said;
yaḥ tu — whoever, indeed;
āditya-grahasya asya — of this Āditya observance;
vāraḥ — day;
devasya — of the deity;
suvrata — O well-disciplined one;
śasyaḥ — auspicious, praiseworthy;
proktaḥ — proclaimed;
priyaḥ — dear;
loke — in the world;
khyātaḥ — famed;
gośruti-bhūṣaṇaḥ — Gośruti-bhūṣaṇa.
Transliteration
brahmovāca | yastvādityagrahasyāsya vāro devasya suvrata | śasyaḥ prokto priyo loke khyāto gośrutibhūṣaṇaḥ || 1 ||

BP 1.92.2

यस्तु पूजयते तस्मिन् पतङ्गं पतगप्रियम् । गन्धपुष्पोपहारैस्तु सूर्यलोकं स गच्छति ॥ ॥

Whoever worships there Pataṅga, the Sun beloved of birds, with fragrances, flowers, and offerings, goes to the world of Sūrya.

Word-for-word
yaḥ tu — whoever;
pūjayate — worships;
tasmin — on that [day];
pataṅgam — Pataṅga, the Sun;
pataga-priyam — beloved of birds;
gandha-puṣpa-upahāraiḥ — with fragrances, flowers, and offerings;
tu — indeed;
sūrya-lokam — to the world of Sūrya;
saḥ — he;
gacchati — goes.
Transliteration
yastu pūjayate tasmin pataṅgaṃ patagapriyam | gandhapuṣpopahāraistu sūryalokaṃ sa gacchati || 2 ||

BP 1.92.3

सोपवासो गणश्रेष्ठ आदित्यग्रहणे शुचिः । जपमानो महाश्वेतां खपोषमथ वा शिवम् ॥ ॥

O best among the hosts, pure and fasting at the Āditya observance, he should recite Mahāśvetā, or else Khapoṣa or Śiva.

Word-for-word
sa-upavāsaḥ — with fasting;
gaṇa-śreṣṭha — O best among the hosts;
āditya-grahaṇe — at the Āditya observance;
śuciḥ — pure;
japamānaḥ — reciting;
mahāśvetām — Mahāśvetā;
khapoṣam — Khapoṣa;
atha vā — or else;
śivam — Śiva.
Transliteration
sopavāso gaṇaśreṣṭha ādityagrahaṇe śuciḥ | japamāno mahāśvetāṃ khapoṣamatha vā śivam || 3 ||

BP 1.92.4

पूजयेज्जगतामीशं तमोनाशनमाशुगम् । पूजयित्वा खपोषं तु महाश्वेतां ततो जपेत् ॥ ॥

He should worship the Lord of the worlds, the swift destroyer of darkness; having worshiped Khapoṣa, he should then recite Mahāśvetā.

Word-for-word
pūjayet — should worship;
jagatām īśam — Lord of the worlds;
tamaḥ-nāśanam — destroyer of darkness;
āśu-gam — swift-going;
pūjayitvā — having worshiped;
khapoṣam — Khapoṣa;
tu — indeed;
mahāśvetām — Mahāśvetā;
tataḥ — then;
japet — should recite.
Transliteration
pūjayejjagatāmīśaṃ tamonāśanamāśugam | pūjayitvā khapoṣaṃ tu mahāśvetāṃ tato japet || 4 ||

BP 1.92.5

पूजयित्वा महाश्वेतां रविं देवं समर्चयेत् । महाश्वेतां प्रतिष्ठाप्य गन्धपुष्पैः सुपूजिताम् ॥ ॥

Having worshiped Mahāśvetā, he should fully worship the deity Ravi; having installed Mahāśvetā, well worshiped with fragrances and flowers,

Word-for-word
pūjayitvā — having worshiped;
mahāśvetām — Mahāśvetā;
ravim — Ravi;
devam — the deity;
samarcayet — should worship fully;
mahāśvetām — Mahāśvetā;
pratiṣṭhāpya — having installed;
gandha-puṣpaiḥ — with fragrances and flowers;
su-pūjitām — well worshiped.
Transliteration
pūjayitvā mahāśvetāṃ raviṃ devaṃ samarcayet | mahāśvetāṃ pratiṣṭhāpya gandhapuṣpaiḥ supūjitām || 5 ||

BP 1.92.6

तस्या एव बहिः कार्यं स्थण्डिलं सुसमाहितः । शुचौ भूमिविभागे तु वीरं संस्थाप्य यत्नतः ॥ ॥

he should carefully make an altar outside it, with concentrated mind, and should establish the hero upon a clean portion of ground.

Word-for-word
tasyāḥ eva — of that very one;
bahiḥ — outside;
kāryam — to be made;
sthaṇḍilam — an altar;
su-samāhitaḥ — with a well-collected mind;
śucau — in a clean;
bhūmi-vibhāge — portion of ground;
tu — indeed;
vīram — the hero;
saṃsthāpya — having established;
yatnataḥ — carefully.
Transliteration
tasyā eva bahiḥ kāryaṃ sthaṇḍilaṃ susamāhitaḥ | śucau bhūmivibhāge tu vīraṃ saṃsthāpya yatnataḥ || 6 ||

BP 1.92.7

कुर्याद्धोमं तिलैः स्नातः सर्पिषा च विशेषतः । आदित्यग्रहवेलायां जपेञ्छ्वेतां महामते ॥ ॥

Having bathed, he should perform a fire offering with sesame, especially with ghee; at the time of the Āditya observance, O great-minded one, he should recite Śvetā.

Word-for-word
kuryāt — should perform;
homam — fire offering;
tilaiḥ — with sesame;
snātaḥ — having bathed;
sarpiṣā — with ghee;
ca — and;
viśeṣataḥ — especially;
āditya-graha-velāyām — at the time of the Āditya observance;
japet — should recite;
śvetām — Śvetā;
mahāmate — O great-minded one.
Transliteration
kuryāddhomaṃ tilaiḥ snātaḥ sarpiṣā ca viśeṣataḥ | ādityagrahavelāyāṃ japeñchvetāṃ mahāmate || 7 ||

BP 1.92.8

मुक्ते दिनकरे पश्चात्स्नानं कृत्वा समाहितः । पूजयित्वा महाश्वेतां खगोल्कं च ग्रहाधिपम् ॥ ॥

After Dīnakara has been released, having bathed and composed himself, he should worship Mahāśvetā, Khagolka, and the lord of the planets.

Word-for-word
mukte — when released;
dinakare — Dīnakara, the Sun;
paścāt — afterward;
snānam — bathing;
kṛtvā — having performed;
samāhitaḥ — composed, concentrated;
pūjayitvā — having worshiped;
mahāśvetām — Mahāśvetā;
khagolkam — Khagolka;
ca — and;
graha-adhipam — lord of the planets.
Transliteration
mukte dinakare paścātsnānaṃ kṛtvā samāhitaḥ | pūjayitvā mahāśvetāṃ khagolkaṃ ca grahādhipam || 8 ||

BP 1.92.9

ब्राह्मणान् वाचयित्वा च ततो भुञ्जीत वाग्यतः । आदित्यग्रहयुक्तेऽस्मिन् वारे त्रिपुरसूदन ॥ ॥

Having caused the Brāhmaṇas to recite, he should then eat with his speech restrained, on this day joined with the Āditya observance, O destroyer of Tripura.

Word-for-word
brāhmaṇān — the Brāhmaṇas;
vācayitvā — having caused [them] to recite;
ca — and;
tataḥ — then;
bhuñjīta — should eat;
vāk-yataḥ — with speech restrained;
āditya-graha-yukte — joined with the Āditya observance;
asmin vāre — on this day;
tripura-sūdana — O destroyer of Tripura.
Transliteration
brāhmaṇān vācayitvā ca tato bhuñjīta vāgyataḥ | ādityagrahayukte'smin vāre tripurasūdana || 9 ||

BP 1.92.10

यत्कर्म क्रियते पुण्यं तत्सर्वं शुभदं भवेत् । स्नानदानजपादीनां कर्मणां गोवृषध्वज ॥ ॥

Whatever meritorious act is performed becomes auspicious—bathing, giving, recitation, and the other acts, O bull-bannered one.

Word-for-word
yat — whatever;
karma — act;
kriyate — is performed;
puṇyam — meritorious;
tat sarvam — all that;
śubha-dam — giving auspiciousness;
bhavet — becomes;
snāna-dāna-japa-ādīnām — of bathing, giving, recitation, and other acts;
karmaṇām — of actions;
go-vṛṣa-dhvaja — O bull-bannered one.
Transliteration
yatkarma kriyate puṇyaṃ tatsarvaṃ śubhadaṃ bhavet | snānadānajapādīnāṃ karmaṇāṃ govṛṣadhvaja || 10 ||

BP 1.92.11

अनन्तं हि फलं तेषां भवत्यस्मिन्न संशयः । कृतानां तु गणश्रेष्ठ भास्करस्य वचो यथा ॥ ॥

The fruit of those acts becomes endless here, without doubt, when they are performed, O best among the hosts, according to Bhāskaras word.

Word-for-word

tu — indeed;
gaṇa-śreṣṭha — O best among the hosts;
bhāskarasya vacaḥ — Bhāskara’s word;
yathā — according to.
Transliteration
anantaṃ hi phalaṃ teṣāṃ bhavatyasminna saṃśayaḥ | kṛtānāṃ tu gaṇaśreṣṭha bhāskarasya vaco yathā || 11 ||

BP 1.92.12

तस्माद्द्विजगणैः कार्यं पुण्यकर्मविचक्षणैः । एकभक्तं च नक्तं च उपवासं गणाधिप ॥ ॥

BP 1.92.13

ये वादित्यदिने कुर्युस् ते यान्ति परमं पदम् । धर्म्यं पुण्यं यशस्यं च पुत्रीयं कामदं तथा ॥ ॥

BP 1.92.14

तस्मिन्दानमपूपस्य गोदानेन समं भवेत् । द्वादशैते महाबाहो वीरभानोर्महात्मनः ॥ ॥

In this observance, the gift of an apūpa becomes equal to the gift of a cow. These twelve, O mighty-armed one, belong to the great-souled Vīrabhānu.

BP 1.92.15

तुष्टिदाः कथितास् तुभ्यं सर्वपापभयापहाः । पठतां शृण्वतां तात कुर्वतां च विशेषतः ॥ ॥

BP 1.92.16

कृत्वैकमेषां विधिवद् वारं वृषभवाहन । वृषादित्रितयं प्राप्य चात्रिजामचलां तथा ॥ ॥

BP 1.92.17
