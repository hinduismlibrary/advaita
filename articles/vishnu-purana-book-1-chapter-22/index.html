VP 1.22.1

यदाभिषिक्तः स पृथुः पूर्वं राज्ये महर्षिभिः।
ततः क्रमेण राज्यानि ददौ लोक-पितामहः

When Prithu was installed in the government of the earth, the great father of the spheres established sovereignties in other parts of the creation.

Śrīdhara translation

The creation of the gods and others has now been stated. He now explains Viṣṇu’s manifestations by describing the lords appointed over each class, beginning “whatever.”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
यदाभिषिक्तः स पृथुः पूर्वं (yadābhiṣiktaḥ sa pṛthuḥ pūrvaṃ) — when that Pṛthu was consecrated
राज्ये महर्षिभिः (rājye maharṣibhiḥ) — earlier as king
ततः क्रमेण (tataḥ krameṇa) — thereafter in due order
राज्यानि ददौ (rājyāni dadau) — he assigned the kingdoms
लोक-पितामहः (loka-pitāmahaḥ) — the grandsire of the world
Śrīdhara Bhāṣya · verse 1.22.1
एवं तावद् देवादीनां सृष्टिर् उक्ता — The creation of the gods and others has now been stated.
इदानीं तत्तद्अधिपकथनमुखेन विष्णोर् विभूतीर् वक्तुम् आह—यदेत्य्आदिना यावत्समाप्तिः — He now explains Viṣṇu’s manifestations by describing the lords appointed over each class, beginning “whatever.”
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa · transliteration

yadābhiṣiktaḥ sa pṛthuḥ pūrvaṃ rājye maharṣibhiḥ| tataḥ krameṇa rājyāni dadau loka-pitāmahaḥ

Śrīdhara Bhāṣya · Sanskrit

एवं तावद् देवादीनां सृष्टिर् उक्ता। इदानीं तत्तद्अधिपकथनमुखेन विष्णोर् विभूतीर् वक्तुम् आह—यदेत्य्आदिना यावत्समाप्तिः

Śrīdhara Bhāṣya · transliteration

evaṃ tāvad devādīnāṃ sṛṣṭir uktā| idānīṃ tattadadhipakathanamukhena viṣṇor vibhūtīr vaktum āha—yadetyādinā yāvatsamāptiḥ

VP 1.22.2

नक्षत्र-ग्रह-विप्राणां वीरुधां चाप्य् अशेषतः।
सोमं राज्येऽददाद् ब्रह्मा यज्ञानां तपसाम् अपि

Soma was appointed monarch of the stars and planets, of Brahmans and of plants, of sacrifices and of penance.

Śrīdhara translation

He appointed Soma to rule the stars and the other bodies, through the sphere of ascetic practice.

VP 1.22.3
