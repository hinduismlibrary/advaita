VP 3.18.1

तपस्य् अभिरतान् सोऽथ मायामोहो महासुरान्।
मैत्रेय ददृशे गत्वा नर्मदा-तीर-संश्रितान्

Māyāmoha went to the great asuras, who were devoted to austerity, and saw them dwelling on the bank of the Narmadā, O Maitreya.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
तपस्य्-अभिरतान् (tapasy-abhiratān) — devoted to austerity
सोऽथ मायामोहो महासुरान् (so'tha māyāmoho mahāsurān) — then Māyāmoha [the] great asuras
मैत्रेय ददृशे (maitreya dadṛśe) — O Maitreya, he saw
गत्वा नर्मदा-तीर-संश्रितान् (gatvā narmadā-tīra-saṃśritān) — having gone [to those] dwelling on the bank of the Narmadā.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tapasy abhiratān so'tha māyāmoho mahāsurān|maitreya dadṛśe gatvā narmadā-tīra-saṃśritān

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

इहोच्यन्ते हता दैत्या माया-मोह-विमोहिताः । नग्न-संसर्ग-दोषोक्त्यै शैव्या-शतधनुः-कथाः

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Here are described the Daityas slain after being deluded by Māyāmoha; the stories of Śaivyā and Śatadhanu explain the fault of association with the naked.

VP 3.18.2

ततो दिगम्बरो मुण्डो बर्हि-पिच्छ-धरो द्विज।
मायामोहोऽसुरान् श्लक्ष्णम् इदं वचनम् अब्रवीत्

Then Māyāmoha, naked, shaven-headed, and wearing a peacock feather, addressed the asuras in smooth, pleasing words, O twice-born.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
ततो दिगंबरो मुण्डो बर्हिपिच्छधरो द्विज (tato digaṃbaro muṇḍo barhipicchadharo dvija) — then naked, shaven-headed, wearing a peacock feather, O twice-born
मायामोहोऽसुरान् श्लक्ष्णमिदं वचनमब्रवीत् (māyāmoho'surān ślakṣṇamidaṃ vacanamabravīt) — Māyāmoha addressed the asuras in these smooth words
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tato digambaro muṇḍo barhi-piccha-dharo dvija|māyāmoho'surān ślakṣṇam idaṃ vacanam abravīt

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

श्लक्ष्णं मनोहरम्

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Ślakṣṇa means smooth and pleasing.

VP 3.18.3

मायामोह उवाच— हे दैत्य-पतयो ब्रूत यद्-अर्थं तप्यते तपः।
ऐहिकं वाथ पारतन्त्र्यं तपसः फलम् इच्छथ

O lords of the Daityas, tell me: for what purpose is this austerity being performed? Do you seek a worldly result or one beyond this world?

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
हे दैत्यपतयो ब्रूत यदर्थं तप्यते तपः (he daityapatayo brūta yadarthaṃ tapyate tapaḥ) — O lords of the Daityas, tell [me] for what purpose austerity is performed
ऐहिकार्थं नु पारत्र्यं तपसः फलमिच्छथ (aihikārthaṃ nu pāratryaṃ tapasaḥ phalamicchatha) — do you seek a worldly result or an other-worldly result from austerity
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

he daitya-patayo brūta yad-arthaṃ tapyate tapaḥ|aihikaṃ vātha pāratantryaṃ tapasaḥ phalam icchatha

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

ऐहिकम् इह-लोक-भोग्यं, पारत्र्यं पर-लोक-भोग्यम्

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Aihika means enjoyed in this world; pāratrya means enjoyed in the other world.

VP 3.18.4

असुरा ऊचुः— पारत्र्य-फल-लाभाय तपश्चर्या महामते।
अस्माभिर् इयम् आरब्धा किं वा तेऽत्र विवक्षितम्

For the sake of attaining a result beyond this world, O great-minded one, we have begun this austerity. What do you mean by this?

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
पारत्र्यफललाभाय तपश्चर्या महामते (pāratryaphalalābhāya tapaścaryā mahāmate) — for attaining an other-worldly result, O great-minded one, [this] austerity [is] undertaken by us
अस्माभिरियमारब्धा किं वा तेऽत्र विवक्षितम् (asmābhiriyamārabdhā kiṃ vā te'tra vivakṣitam) — what is intended by you here
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

asurā ūcuḥ— pāratrya-phala-lābhāya tapaścaryā mahāmate|asmābhir iyam ārabdhā kiṃ vā te'tra vivakṣitam

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

No commentary.

VP 3.18.5

मायामोह उवाच— कुरुध्वं मम वाक्यानि यदि मुक्तिम् अभीप्सथ।
अर्हध्वम् एनं धर्मं च मुक्ति-द्वारम् असंवृतम्

Follow my words if you desire liberation. Make yourselves worthy of this dharma; its gate to liberation is open.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
कुरुध्वं मम वाक्यानि यदि मुक्तिमभीप्सथ (kurudhvaṃ mama vākyāni yadi muktimabhīpsatha) — perform my words if you desire liberation
अर्हध्वमेनं धर्मं च मुक्तिद्वारमसंवृतम् (arhadhvamenaṃ dharmaṃ ca muktidvāramasaṃvṛtam) — make yourselves worthy of this dharma; the gate of liberation [is] open
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kurudhvaṃ mama vākyāni yadi muktim abhīpsatha|arhadhvam enaṃ dharmaṃ ca mukti-dvāram asaṃvṛtam

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

एवं धर्ममग्रे वक्ष्यमाणम् । असंवृतमुद्घाटितम्

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The dharma that will be explained next; asaṃvṛta means uncovered or opened.

VP 3.18.6

धर्मो विमुक्तेर् अर्होऽयं नैत्स्माद् अपरो वरः।
अत्रैव संस्थिताः स्वर्गं विमुक्तिं वा गमिष्यथ

This dharma is worthy of liberation; nothing is higher than it. Established in it, you will attain heaven or liberation.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
धर्मो विमुक्तेरर्होऽयं नैतस्मादपरो वरः (dharmo vimukterarho'yaṃ naitasmādaparo varaḥ) — this dharma is worthy of liberation; no other [is] higher than it;
अत्रैव संस्थिताः स्वर्गं विमुक्तिं वा गमिष्यथ (atraiva saṃsthitāḥ svargaṃ vimuktiṃ vā gamiṣyatha) — established in this alone, you will attain heaven or liberation
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dharmo vimukter arho'yaṃ naitsmād aparo varaḥ|atraiva saṃsthitāḥ svargaṃ vimuktiṃ vā gamiṣyatha

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

No commentary.

VP 3.18.7

अर्हध्वं धर्मम् एतं च सर्वे यूयं महाबलाः

All of you, O mighty ones, become worthy of this dharma.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
अर्हध्वम् (arhadhvam) — become worthy
धर्मम् एतम् च (dharmam etam ca) — of this dharma also
सर्वे यूयम् महा-बलाः (sarve yūyam mahā-balāḥ) — all of you, O mighty ones
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

arhadhvaṃ dharmam etaṃ ca sarve yūyaṃ mahābalāḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अर्हः योग्यः । एतस्मादपरो धर्मः न परो न श्रेष्ठः

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Arhat means worthy; no dharma is higher or superior than this.

VP 3.18.8

एवं-प्रकारैर् बहुभिर् युक्ति-दर्शन-चर्चितैः।
मायामोहेन ते दैत्या वेद-मार्गाद् अपाकृताः

Through many such arguments and discussions of reasoning, Māyāmoha turned the Daityas away from the Vedic path.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
एवंप्रकारैर्वहुभिर्युक्ति(चोदन) दर्शनचर्चितैः (evaṃprakārairvahubhiryukti(codana) darśanacarcitaiḥ) — with many such methods, discussed through reasoning and doctrine
मायामोहेन ते दैत्या वेदमार्गादपाकृताः (māyāmohena te daityā vedamārgādapākṛtāḥ) — by Māyāmoha, those Daityas were turned away from the Vedic path
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

evaṃ-prakārair bahubhir yukti-darśana-carcitaiḥ|māyāmohena te daityā veda-mārgād apākṛtāḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

एवम्-प्रकारैर् वाक्यैः युक्ति-दर्शनं शुष्क-तर्क-वादः तेन वर्द्धितैः । चर्चितैर् इति वा पाठः । अपाकृता दूरीभूताः

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

He explains 'in these ways' as statements elaborated by claims such as 'this does not withstand reasoning'; 'discussed by reasoning' means examined through inference and scripture. In the alternate reading, yukti means fitness, darśana means direct perception, and tarkita means inference. In another reading, the words are discussed through injunction and scripture, using reasoning; thus Māyāmoha explains and overpowers them until they leave the Vedic path.

VP 3.18.9

धर्मायैतद् अधर्माय सद् एतन् न सद् इत्य् अपि।
विमुक्तये त्व् इदं नैतद् विमुक्तिं सम्प्रयच्छति

This is for dharma and for adharma; it is existent and non-existent. This is for liberation, yet it does not grant liberation.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
धर्मायैतदधर्माय सदेतन्न सदित्यपि (dharmāyaitadadharmāya sadetanna sadityapi) — for dharma, for adharma; existent, not existent also;
विमुक्तये त्विदं नैतद्विमुक्तिं संप्रयच्छति (vimuktaye tvidaṃ naitadvimuktiṃ saṃprayacchati) — for liberation, yet this does not grant liberation
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dharmāyaitad adharmāya sad etan na sad ity api|vimuktaye tv idaṃ naitad vimuktiṃ samprayacchati

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

वेद-मार्गापाकरणम् एव सप्तधा दर्शयति सार्ध-द्वाभ्यां—धर्माय इति

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Beginning with 'for dharma' and ending with 'in one way,' he gives seven examples of the Jaina doctrine of conditional predication (saptabhaṅgī): it may exist, not exist, both exist and not exist, be indescribable, exist and be indescribable, not exist and be indescribable, or both exist and not exist and be indescribable. This is the Ārhata/Jaina anekāntavāda.

VP 3.18.10

परमार्थोऽयम् अत्य् अर्थं परमार्थो न चाप्ययम्।
कार्यम् एतद् अकार्यं च नैतद् एवं स्फुटं त्व् इदम्

This is the highest truth, and it is not the highest truth; it should be done, and it should not be done; this is not so, yet this is clearly so.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
परमार्थोऽयमत्यर्थं परमार्थो न चाप्ययम् (paramārtho'yamatyarthaṃ paramārtho na cāpyayam) — this [is] the highest truth, very much; [it is] not the highest truth also;
कार्यमेतदकार्यं च नैतदेवं स्फुटं त्विदम् (kāryametadakāryaṃ ca naitadevaṃ sphuṭaṃ tvidam) — this is to be done, this is not to be done; this is not so, yet this is clearly so
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

paramārtho'yam aty arthaṃ paramārtho na cāpyayam|kāryam etad akāryaṃ ca naitad evaṃ sphuṭaṃ tv idam

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

No commentary.

VP 3.18.11

धर्मः अयम् बहु-वाससाम् (dharmaḥ ayam bahu-vāsasām) — this is the dharma of those with many garments
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dig-vāsasā-mayaṃ dharmo dharmo'yaṃ bahu-vāsasām

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

No commentary.

VP 3.18.12

इत्य् अनेकान्तवादं च माया-मोहेन नैकधा।
तेन दर्शयता दैत्याः स्व-धर्मं त्याजिता द्विज

By presenting anekāntavāda in many forms, Māyāmoha made the Daityas abandon their own dharma, O twice-born.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
इत्यनेकान्तवादं च मायामोहेन नैकधा (ityanekāntavādaṃ ca māyāmohena naikadhā) — and this anekāntavāda, in many ways, by Māyāmoha
तेन दर्शयता दैत्यास्स्वधर्मं त्याजिता द्विज (tena darśayatā daityāssvadharmaṃ tyājitā dvija) — by displaying it, the Daityas were made to abandon their own dharma, O twice-born
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ity anekāntavādaṃ ca māyā-mohena naikadhā|tena darśayatā daityāḥ sva-dharmaṃ tyājitā dvija

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अनैकान्तवादं फलव्यभिचारिकारणवादं भेदाभेदवादं वा दर्शयता इत्येवं स्वधर्मान् त्याजिता इत्युपसंहारः

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

By displaying anekāntavāda—whether the doctrine of causes whose fruits vary or the doctrine of difference and non-difference—he made them abandon their own dharma; this is the conclusion.

VP 3.18.13

अर्हतैतं महा-धर्मं माया-मोहेन ते यतः।
प्रोक्तास् तम् आश्रिता धर्मम् आर्हतास् तेन तेऽभवन्

Because Māyāmoha taught them this great dharma, they became Ārhatas by taking refuge in it.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
अर्हतैतं महाधर्मं मायामोहेन ते यतः (arhataitaṃ mahādharmaṃ māyāmohena te yataḥ) — because of Māyāmoha, [they embraced] this great dharma, the Ārhata [doctrine]
प्रोक्तास्तमाश्रिता धर्ममर्ह(न्त)तास्तेन तेऽभवन् (proktāstamāśritā dharmamarha(nta)tāstena te'bhavan) — having been taught it and taking refuge in that dharma, they became Ārhatas
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

arhataitaṃ mahā-dharmaṃ māyā-mohena te yataḥ|proktās tam āśritā dharmam ārhatās tena te'bhavan

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

आर्हतानां नामनिरुक्तिम् आह—अर्हत इति

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

He gives the derivation of the name Ārhata: 'arhata' means worthy.

VP 3.18.14

त्रयी-धर्म-समुत्सर्गं माया-मोहेन तेऽसुराः।
कारितास् तन् मया ह्य् आसंस् ततोऽन्ये तत्-प्रचोदिताः

Māyāmoha caused those asuras to abandon the threefold Vedic dharma; influenced by them, others were impelled in the same way.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
त्रयीधर्मसमुत्सर्गं मायामोहेन तेऽसुराः (trayīdharmasamutsargaṃ māyāmohena te'surāḥ) — the abandonment of the threefold dharma, by Māyāmoha, those asuras
कारितास्तन्मया ह्यासंस्ततोऽन्ये तत्प्रचोदिताः (kāritāstanmayā hyāsaṃstato'nye tatpracoditāḥ) — made [to do] this by me, they became so; thereafter others [were] impelled by them
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

trayī-dharma-samutsargaṃ māyā-mohena te'surāḥ|kāritās tan mayā hy āsaṃs tato'nye tat-pracoditāḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

सम्प्रदायप्रवृत्तिम् आह—त्रयि इति

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

He explains the arising of the religious tradition: 'trayi' means the threefold Veda.

VP 3.18.15

तैर् अप्य् अन्ये परे तैश् च तैर् अप्य् अन्ये परे च तैः।
अल्पैर् अहोभिः सन्त्यक्ता तैर् दैत्यैः प्रायशस् त्रयी

Those taught by them taught others, and those others taught still others; within a few days the Daityas had mostly abandoned the threefold Veda.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
तैरप्यन्ये परे तैश्च तैरप्यन्ये परे च तैः (tairapyanye pare taiśca tairapyanye pare ca taiḥ) — by them others, then by those others still others
अल्पैरहोभिस्संत्यक्ता तैर्दैत्यैः प्रायशस्त्रयी (alpairahobhissaṃtyaktā tairdaityaiḥ prāyaśastrayī) — in a few days, the threefold Veda was mostly abandoned by those Daityas
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tair apy anye pare taiś ca tair apy anye pare ca taiḥ|alpair ahobhiḥ santyaktā tair daityaiḥ prāyaśas trayī

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

पाषण्डादयः सर्वे वेदविरुद्धा एव यदाहुः— भ्रष्टः स्वधर्मात् पाषण्डो विकर्मस्थो निषिद्धकृत् । यस्य धर्मध्वजो नित्यं सराध्वज इवोच्छ्रितः । प्रच्छन्नानि च पापानि वैडालं नाम तद्व्रतम् । प्रियं वक्ति पुरोऽन्यत्र विप्रियं कुरुते भृशम् । त्यक्तोपरोधचेष्टश्च शठोऽयं कथितो बुधैः । सन्देहकृधेतुभिश्च सत्कर्मसु सहेतुक । अर्वाग्दृष्टिर्नैकृतिकः स्वार्थसाधनतत्परः । शठो मिथ्याविनीतश्च वकवृत्तिरुदाहृतः । इति । वाङ्मात्रेणापि नार्चयेत् । तैः सम्भाषणमपि न कुर्यात्

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The various classes beginning with the pāṣaṇḍa are all opposed to the Veda. A pāṣaṇḍa is one who has fallen from his own dharma; a vikarmastha performs prohibited acts; a dharmadhvaja carries the signs of dharma without following it; a vaiḍāla-vrata person conceals sins while maintaining a hypocritical observance; a śaṭha speaks pleasantly to someone's face but acts harmfully elsewhere and is known for deceit; a haituka uses false reasons to cast doubt on good actions; a person with a low, crooked outlook is selfish and intent on his own ends; and one with the conduct of a heron displays false dignity. Even with words alone one should not honor such people or converse with them.
