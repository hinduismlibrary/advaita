VP 3.18.1

तपस्य् अभिरतान् सोऽथ मायामोहो महासुरान् । मैत्रेय ददृशे गत्वा नर्मदातीरसंश्रितान् ॥१॥

Māyāmoha went to the great asuras, who were devoted to austerity, and saw them dwelling on the bank of the Narmadā, O Maitreya.

Śrīdhara translation

Here are described the Daityas slain after being deluded by Māyāmoha; the stories of Śaivyā and Śatadhanu explain the fault of association with the naked.

VP 3.18.8

एवंप्रकारैर् बहुभिर् युक्तिदर्शनचर्चितैः । मायामोहेन ते दैत्या वेदमार्गाद् अपाकृताः ॥८॥

Śrīdhara translation

He explains 'in these ways' as statements elaborated by claims such as 'this does not withstand reasoning'; 'discussed by reasoning' means examined through inference and scripture. In the alternate reading, yukti means fitness, darśana means direct perception, and tarkita means inference. In another reading, the words are discussed through injunction and scripture, using reasoning; thus Māyāmoha explains and overpowers them until they leave the Vedic path.

VP 3.18.9

धर्मायैतद् अधर्माय सद् एतन् न सद् इत्य् अपि । विमुक्तये त्व् इदं नैतद् विमुक्तिं सम्प्रयच्छति ॥९॥

This is for dharma and for adharma; it is existent and non-existent. This is for liberation, yet it does not grant liberation.

Śrīdhara translation

Beginning with 'for dharma' and ending with 'in one way,' he gives seven examples of the Jaina doctrine of conditional predication (saptabhaṅgī): it may exist, not exist, both exist and not exist, be indescribable, exist and be indescribable, not exist and be indescribable, or both exist and not exist and be indescribable. This is the Ārhata/Jaina anekāntavāda.

पाषण्डादयः सर्वे वेदविरुद्धा एव यदाहुः— भ्रष्टः स्वधर्मात् पाषण्डो विकर्मस्थो निषिद्धकृत् । यस्य धर्मध्वजो नित्यं सराध्वज इवोच्छ्रितः । प्रच्छन्नानि च पापानि वैडालं नाम तद्व्रतम् । प्रियं वक्ति पुरोऽन्यत्र विप्रियं कुरुते भृशम् । त्यक्तोपरोधचेष्टश्च शठोऽयं कथितो बुधैः । सन्देहकृधेतुभिश्च सत्कर्मसु सहेतुक । अर्वाग्दृष्टिर्नैकृतिकः स्वार्थसाधनतत्परः । शठो मिथ्याविनीतश्च वकवृत्तिरुदाहृतः । इति । वाङ्मात्रेणापि नार्चयेत् । तैः सम्भाषणमपि न कुर्यात् ॥१००॥ इति श्रीधरस्वामिकृतायाम् आत्मप्रकाशाभिधायां स्वप्रकाशाख्यायां वा श्रीविष्णुपुराणटीकायां तृतीयाऽंशे सप्तदशोऽध्यायः ॥
