Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 110 — Anantara Saptamī

BP 1.110.1

ब्रह्मोवाच। शुक्लपक्षे तु सप्तम्यां मासि भाद्रपदेऽच्युत। प्रणम्य शिरसादित्यं पूजयेत्सप्तवाहनम् ॥

Brahmā said: On the seventh day of the bright fortnight in the month of Bhādrapada, O Acyuta, one should bow one's head to Āditya and worship the Seven-horsed One.

Word-for-word
brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said;
śukla-pakṣe — in the bright fortnight;

saptamyām — on the seventh day;
māsi — in the month;
bhādrapade — in Bhādrapada;
acyuta — O Acyuta;
praṇamya — having bowed;
śirasā — with the head;
ādityam — Āditya;
pūjayet — should worship;
sapta-vāhanam — the Seven-horsed One
Transliteration
brahmovāca | śuklapakṣe tu saptamyāṃ māsi bhādrapade'cyuta | praṇamya śirasādityaṃ pūjayetsaptavāhanam || 1 ||

BP 1.110.2

पुष्पधूपादिभिर्वीर कुतपानां च तर्पणैः। पाषण्डादिभिरालापमकुर्वन्नियतात्मवान् ॥

With flowers, incense, and the like, and with offerings that satisfy the Kutapas, O hero, the self-controlled person should not converse with heretics and the like.

Transliteration
puṣpadhūpādibhirvīra kutapānāṃ ca tarpaṇaiḥ | pāṣaṇḍādibhirālāpamakurvanniyatātmavān || 2 ||

BP 1.110.3

विप्राय दक्षिणां दत्त्वा नक्तं भुञ्जीत वाग्यतः। तिष्ठन्व्रजन्प्रस्थितश्च क्षुतप्रस्खलितादिषु ॥

Having given a gift to a Brahmin, he should eat at night, restrained in speech; while standing, walking, setting out, sneezing, stumbling, and in the like situations,

BP 1.110.4

आदित्यनामस्मरणं कुर्वन्नुच्चारणं तथा। अनेनैव विधानेन मासान्द्वादश वै क्रमात् ॥

he should remember and utter the name of Āditya. In this very manner, gradually, for twelve months,

Transliteration

BP 1.110.5

उपोष्य पारणे पूर्णे समभ्यर्च्य जगद्गुरुम्। पुण्येन श्रावणेनेह प्रीणयन्पुष्टिमश्नुते ॥

Having fasted and, at the completion of the breaking of the fast, fully worshipped the Teacher of the world, he pleases Him with meritorious Śrāvaṇa and enjoys nourishment.

Transliteration
upoṣya pāraṇe pūrṇe samabhyarcya jagadgurum | puṇyena śrāvaṇeneha prīṇayanpuṣṭimaśnute || 5 ||

BP 1.110.6

अनन्तं श्रावणेनेह यतः फलमुदाहृतम्। तेनादित्यं समभ्यर्च्य तदेव लभते फलम् ॥

Since the fruit of Ananta has been declared through Śrāvaṇa here, by fully worshipping Āditya with that same observance, one obtains that very fruit.

Transliteration

BP 1.110.7

एवं यः पुरुषः कुर्यादादित्याराधनं शुचिः। प्राप्येह विपुलं भोगं धर्ममर्थं तथाव्ययम् ॥

BP 1.110.8

अमुत्र लोकमायाति दिव्ये खे गीतसंयुते। नारी वा स्वर्गमभ्येत्य ह्यनन्तं फलमश्नुते ॥
