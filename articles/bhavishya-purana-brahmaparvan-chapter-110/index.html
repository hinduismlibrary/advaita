Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 110 — Observance on Consecutive Seventh Days

BP 1.110.1

ब्रह्मोवाच। शुक्लपक्षे तु सप्तम्यां मासि भाद्रपदेऽच्युत। प्रणम्य शिरसादित्यं पूजयेत्सप्तवाहनम् ॥

Brahmā said. In the bright fortnight, on the seventh day of the month of Bhādrapada, O Acyuta, having bowed his head to Āditya, one should worship the Seven Horsed One.

BP 1.110.2

पुष्पधूपादिभिर्वीर कुतपानां च तर्पणैः। पाषण्डादिभिरालापमकुर्वन्नियतात्मवान् ॥

With flowers, incense, and the like, and with libations at the sacred noon hour, O hero, not conversing with heretics and the like, self controlled.

BP 1.110.3

विप्राय दक्षिणां दत्त्वा नक्तं भुञ्जीत वाग्यतः। तिष्ठन्व्रजन्प्रस्थितश्च क्षुतप्रस्खलितादिषु ॥

Having given a gift to a Brahmin, he should eat at night, restrained in speech. While standing, walking, setting out, sneezing, stumbling, and so on,

BP 1.110.4

आदित्यनामस्मरणं कुर्वन्नुच्चारणं तथा। अनेनैव विधानेन मासान्द्वादश वै क्रमात् ॥

He should remember and pronounce the name of Āditya, by this very method, for twelve months in due order.

BP 1.110.5

उपोष्य पारणे पूर्णे समभ्यर्च्य जगद्गुरुम्। पुण्येन श्रावणेनेह प्रीणयन्पुष्टिमश्नुते ॥

Having fasted and, when the fast is complete, having fully worshipped the Teacher of the world, pleasing him here through meritorious listening, he obtains nourishment.

BP 1.110.6

अनन्तं श्रावणेनेह यतः फलमुदाहृतम्। तेनादित्यं समभ्यर्च्य तदेव लभते फलम् ॥

Since endless fruit is declared here through listening, having fully worshipped Āditya by that means, he obtains that very fruit.

BP 1.110.7

BP 1.110.8

अमुत्र लोकमायाति दिव्ये खे गीतसंयुते। नारी वा स्वर्गमभ्येत्य ह्यनन्तं फलमश्नुते ॥
