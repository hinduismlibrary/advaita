Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 10: Women’s Conduct Among Kin and in Public

BP 1.10.1

ब्रह्मोवाच
आराध्यानां हि सर्वेषामयमाराधने विधिः ।
चित्त१ ज्ञानानुवृत्तिश्च हितैषित्वं च सर्वदा ॥ १

Brahmā said: This is the procedure for honouring all who are worthy of honour: understanding their minds, following their wishes, and always seeking their welfare.

BP 1.10.2

कन्या पुनर्भूर्वेश्या च त्रिविधा एव योषितः ।
प्रिया मध्याप्रिया चैव योग्या मध्येतरा तथा ॥ २

Women are three kinds: kanyā, punarbhū and veśyā. They are also beloved, moderately beloved or not beloved; suitable, intermediate or otherwise.

BP 1.10.3

समा श्रेष्ठा च नीचा च भूयोपि त्रिविधाः पुनः ।
पूर्ववत्परयोर्वृत्तिरिष्टानुक्त्या प्रियाप्रिये ॥ ३

‘Again they may be equal, superior, or inferior. Conduct toward the latter two follows the principles already stated, with affection or dislike conveyed through what is desired or left unsaid.’

BP 1.10.4

अधमाप्रिययोरत्र पतिपत्न्यादिका मता ।
निषिद्धानां तु भक्ष्यादि तद्धि यत्नाद्विधीयते ॥ ४

Here the inferior and the unliked are considered in relations such as husband and wife. Prohibited foods and the like are also regulated carefully.

BP 1.10.5

एकद्वित्वबहुत्वाद्या२ ये भेदाः समुदाहृताः ।
ज्येष्ठादिवृत्ते वक्ष्यामस्तानशेषान्द्विजोत्तमाः ॥ ५

‘O best of the twice-born, all the distinctions described—having one, two, or many wives and the rest—will be explained without omission when treating conduct toward the eldest and others.’

BP 1.10.6

वृत्तं च द्विविधं स्त्रीणां बाह्यमाभ्यन्तरं तथा ।
भर्तुरन्यजने बाह्यं तस्याः शारीरमान्तरम् ॥ ६

‘Women’s conduct is twofold, external and internal. External conduct concerns the husband and other people; internal conduct concerns her own embodied and intimate life.’

BP 1.10.7

ज्ञातीतरविभागेन तद्बाह्यं द्विविधं पुनः ।
पूज्यं तुल्यं कनिष्ठं च तत्प्रत्येकं पुनस्त्रिधा ॥ ७

‘External conduct is again divided between relatives and non-relatives; in each division, people are further classed in three ways as venerable, equal, or junior.’

BP 1.10.8

रहोरतं प्रकाशं च शारीरमपि तत्त्रिधा ।
भर्तुश्चित्तानुकूल्येन प्रयोक्तव्यं यथोचितम् ॥ ८

‘The intimate or bodily sphere is also threefold: private affection, public conduct, and physical relations. Each should be applied appropriately in harmony with the husband’s state of mind.’

BP 1.10.9

माता पिता स्वसा भ्राता पितृव्याचार्यमातुलाः ।
सभार्या भगिनी भर्ता भर्तृमातृपितृष्वसा ॥ ९

‘Mother, father, sister, brother, paternal uncle, teacher, maternal uncle together with their wives, the husband, and the husband’s mother, father, and paternal aunt—’

BP 1.10.10

धात्री वृद्धाङ्गनादिश्च यस्तत्रास्ता समो जनः ।
प्रथमोढा सपत्नी च स्त्रीणां मान्यतमो गणः ॥.10.१०

‘—as well as a nurse, elderly women, and others of comparable standing; a co-wife married earlier is also included among the group most deserving a woman’s respect.’

BP 1.10.11

एषामेव त्वपत्यादिभगिनीभ्रातरस्तथा ।
कनिष्ठा भर्तुरित्यादिभार्याश्चाप्तसमो मतः ॥१

‘Their children and younger siblings, and likewise the younger relatives of the husband and the wives of trusted equals, are regarded as belonging to the junior group.’

BP 1.10.12

हीनोऽन्यः शासनीयस्तु तत्र तावन्न विद्यते ।
योग्यता सुतसौभाग्यैर्न यावत्स्यात्प्रतिष्ठिता ॥२

‘A person of lower standing may be instructed, but that status should not be treated as fixed until competence, the bearing of a worthy son, and good fortune have become established.’

BP 1.10.13

यत्रापि गुरु भर्तॄणामानुकूल्येन सर्वदा ।
वृत्तिः प्रशस्यते स्त्रीणां पूजाचाराविरोधिनी५ ॥३

‘Even where someone is a superior of the husband, women’s conduct is always praised when it accords with the husband’s wishes without conflicting with the honour and observance due to that superior.’

BP 1.10.14

देवरैः पतिमित्रैश्च परिहासक्रियोचितैः ।
विविक्तदेशावस्थानं वर्जयेदिति नर्म च ॥४

One should avoid being alone in a secluded place and joking with brothers-in-law or the husband’s friends, even when they are fit for joking.

BP 1.10.15

प्रायशो हि कुलस्त्रीणां शीलविध्वंसहेतवः ।
दुष्टयोगो रहो नित्यं स्वातन्त्र्यमतिनर्मता ॥५

For women of good families, bad company, continual privacy, freedom and excessive joking are usually causes of the destruction of conduct.

BP 1.10.16

दुष्टसङ्गे त्वरा स्त्रीणां युवभिर्नर्म नोचितम् ।
निर्भेषता स्वतन्त्राणां साफल्यं रहसि यजेत् ॥६

‘It advises women against hastening into corrupt association or joking with young men, and warns that fearlessness combined with unrestricted freedom may find its opportunity in seclusion.’

BP 1.10.17

पुंसो दुष्टेङ्गिताकारान्दुष्टभावप्रयोजितान् ।
भ्रातृवत्पितृवच्चैतान्पश्यती परिवर्जयेत् ॥७

‘When a woman observes corrupt gestures and appearances in men that reveal improper intention, she should avoid them even if she ordinarily regards those men like brothers or fathers.’

BP 1.10.18

पुंसोऽ न्याग्रहमालापस्मितविप्रेक्षितानि च ।
करान्तरेण द्रव्याणां निबन्धं ग्रहणार्पणम् ॥८

‘She should also avoid a man’s persistent demands, private conversation, smiles and suggestive glances, and the binding obligation created by taking or giving objects directly from hand to hand.’

BP 1.10.19

द्वारप्रदेशावस्थानं राजमार्गावलोकनम् ।
प्रेक्षोद्यानादिशीलत्वं निरुष्याद्देशमालयम् ॥९

‘A residence should be chosen so as to prevent habitual lingering at the doorway, watching the royal road, and frequenting spectacles, gardens, and similar places.’

BP 1.10.20

बहूनां दर्शने स्थानं दृष्टिवाक्कायचापलम् ।
ष्ठीवनत्वं ससीत्कारमुच्चैर्हसितजल्पितम् ॥.10.२०

Being where many can see; restlessness of eyes, speech and body; spitting; hissing; loud laughter and chatter.

BP 1.10.21

साङ्गत्यं लिङ्गिदुष्टस्त्रीभिघृणीक्षणिकादिभिः ।
मन्त्रमण्डलदीक्षायां सक्तिः संवसनेषु च ॥१

‘It also lists association with sectarian mendicants, corrupt women, spiteful people, fortune-tellers and others; attachment to ritual circles and initiations; and repeated shared residence.’

BP 1.10.22

इत्येवमादिदुर्वृत्तं प्रायोदुष्टजनोचितम् ।
वर्जयेत्परिरक्षन्ती कुलत्रितयवाच्यताम् ॥२

One should avoid such bad conduct, usually proper to wicked people, while protecting the good name of the three families.
