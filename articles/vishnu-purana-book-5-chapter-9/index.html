VP 5.9.1

तस्मिन्रासभदैतेये सानुगे विनिपातिते ।
सौम्यं तद्गोपगोपीनां रम्यं तालवनं बभौ

Sri Paraasharar - When the demon in the form of the donkey, along with his followers, were all killed, and that palm grove became beautiful, the gopaas and gopis started to go there and pluck the flowers.

Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tasmin rāsabhadaiteye sānuge vinipātite | saumyaṃ tadagopagopīnāṃ ramyaṃ tālavanaṃ babhau

Śrīdhara commentary.

VP 5.9.2

ततस्तौ जातहर्षौ तु वसुदेवसुतावुभौ ।
हत्वा धेनुकदैतेयं भाण्डीरं वटमागतौ

Feeling extremely happy, Vasudeva's children Krishna and Balaraama, having killed the demon Dhenuka, came near a fig tree which was called as Bhaandeera.

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

VP 5.9.4

निर्योगपाशस्कन्धौ तौ वनमालाविभूषितौ ।
शुशुभाते महात्मानौ बलाशृङ्गाविवर्षभौ

They were looking so beautiful and the sight was so grand, the rope with which they control the cows - they had put this rope on their shoulders, and they were wearing the garlands of all the beautiful flowers. They were looking so grand and nice, looking like two young bulls with their horns just coming up.

Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

niryogapāśaskandhau tau vanamālāvibhūṣitau | śuśubhāte mahātmānau balāśṛṅgāvivarṣabhau

Śrīdhara

kṣvelamānāv iti | kṣvelamānau siṃhavan nādantau | kṣvelā tu siṃhanādaḥ syāt ity abhidhānāt | vicinvantau vṛkṣārohaṇa-krīḍārtham anvicchantau | dūre sthitau santau cārayantau gāḥ vyāharantau nāmabhiḥ āhvayantau || 3 || śuśubhāte mahātmānau bāla-śṛṅgāv iva rṣabhau || 4 | viṣṇucittaḥ — niryogeti | niryoga-pāśo niyamana-rajjuḥ || 4 || śrīdharaḥ — niryogeti | niryujyate niṣiddhyante duhyamānā gāvaḥ ebhir iti niryogaḥ pāda-bandhana-rajjavaḥ adṛśya-gavāṃ karṣaṇārthā vā tāś ca skandhe yayos tau niryoga-pāśa-skandhau bāla-śṛṅgāv iva vayo-viśeṣopalakṣaṇau || 4 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

क्ष्वेलमानाव् इति । क्ष्वेलमानौ सिंहवन् नादन्तौ । क्ष्वेला तु सिंहनादः स्यात् इत्य् अभिधानात् । विचिन्वन्तौ वृक्षारोहण-क्रीडार्थम् अन्विच्छन्तौ । दूरे स्थितौ सन्तौ चारयन्तौ गाः व्याहरन्तौ नामभिः आह्वयन्तौ ॥३॥
शुशुभाते महात्मानौ बाल-शृङ्गाव् इव र्षभौ ॥४।
विष्णुचित्तः — निर्योगेति । निर्योग-पाशो नियमन-रज्जुः ॥४॥
श्रीधरः — निर्योगेति । निर्युज्यते निषिद्ध्यन्ते दुह्यमाना गावः एभिर् इति निर्योगः पाद-बन्धन-रज्जवः अदृश्य-गवां कर्षणार्था वा ताश् च स्कन्धे ययोस् तौ निर्योग-पाश-स्कन्धौ बाल-शृङ्गाव् इव वयो-विशेषोपलक्षणौ ॥४॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

No separate English commentary is supplied.

VP 5.9.5

सुवर्णाञ्जनचूर्णाभ्यां तौ तदा रूषितांबरौ ।
महेन्द्रायुधसंयुक्तौ श्वेतकृष्णाविवांबुदौ

Golden and blackish blue paste coloured clothes - they were wearing. Like the bow of Indra, white and black clouds, which are decorated with bow of Indra, just like that, Balaraama and Krishna were shining forth - Balarama was fair and Krishna was dark. They are compared to the clouds which are looking grand with the Indra's bow.

Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

suvarṇāñjanacūrṇābhyāṃ tau tadā rūṣitāṃbarau | mahendrāyudhasaṃyuktau śvetakṛṣṇāvivāṃbudau

Śrīdhara

suvarṇeti | rūṣitāmbarau rañjita-vastrau | mahendrāyudham indra-dhanuḥ, tat-saṃyuktāv ambudāv iti || 5 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

सुवर्णेति । रूषिताम्बरौ रञ्जित-वस्त्रौ । महेन्द्रायुधम् इन्द्र-धनुः, तत्-संयुक्ताव् अम्बुदाव् इति ॥५॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

No separate English commentary is supplied.

Being interested in exhibiting all the qualities of a normal human, They were behaving as though they are normal humans. Just like humans engage in play and other things, They were also engaged in the same things, and wandering about in that forest.

VP 5.9.8

ततस् त्व् आन्दोलिकाभिश् च नियुद्धैश् च महा-बलौ ।
व्यायामं चक्रतुस् तत्र क्षेपणीयैस् तथाश्मभिः

A swing made of two gopa's hands, with hands locked together, in between Krishna will sit there, and they will swing Him around, back and forth. Like this, They were playing. They were wrestling, boxing. Throwing stones at each other, They were doing all the acts that normal humans will do. They are the protectors of the entire world.

Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

VP 5.9.9

One raakshasa named Pralamba came there, wanting to kill and eat both of Them, who were playing around happily, having taken the form of a normal gopa. He disguised himself just like a normal gopa.

VP 5.9.10

That Pralamba demon came and mixed himself, without any doubt, along with the other people there, taking the form of a human, just like a gopa. He mixed with all the other gopaas so that nobody can notice that he is a demon. He looked like any other gopa.

VP 5.9.11

He came with the desire to kill Them. He was looking for a moment when they will not be careful or cautious, so that he can attack them. He knew that Krishna cannot be won over. He first decided to kill Balaraama.

VP 5.9.12

हरिणाक्रीडनं नाम बालक्रीडनकं ततः ।
प्रकुर्वन्तो हि ते सर्वे द्वौद्वौ युगपदुत्थितौ

They were playing a particular game, called Harinaakreedaa. Two people, in pairs, they play. Starting from one point, they have to go up to another point. They have to hop, leap like a deer. When two people are going like this, whoever goes fast wins, and the other one loses. The loser has to carry the winner, on his shoulders, and has to run up to the Bhaandeera fig tree and come back again to the starting point. This is the game they were playing. This is called Harinaakreedaa.

Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara

hariṇeti | hariṇākrīḍanaṃ hariṇavad ākrīḍanam utplutyotplutya gamanaṃ yatra dvayoḥ, yas tvarato yāti sa jetā, itaras tu jitaḥ | atra ca jito jetāraṃ vahan bhāṇḍīrakaṃ vaṭaṃ nītvā punar utpluti-sthānaṃ nayed iti paṇa-bandhaḥ || 12 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

हरिणेति । हरिणाक्रीडनं हरिणवद् आक्रीडनम् उत्प्लुत्योत्प्लुत्य गमनं यत्र द्वयोः, यस् त्वरतो याति स जेता, इतरस् तु जितः । अत्र च जितो जेतारं वहन् भाण्डीरकं वटं नीत्वा पुनर् उत्प्लुति-स्थानं नयेद् इति पण-बन्धः ॥१२॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

VP 5.9.13

VP 5.9.33

तत्स्मर्यताममेयात्मंस्त्वयाऽऽत्मा जहि दानवम् ।
मानुष्यमेवावलंब्य बन्धूनां क्रियतां हितम्

O incomparable divine Balaraama, do kill the demon. Thinking that You have incarnated here just like a human, do good to all Your relatives. Though I have reminded You of Your divine form, and divine powers, just acting as though You are a human only, You can kill the demon.
