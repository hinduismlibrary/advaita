Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 108 — The Seventh Day Observance for Attaining All Aims

BP 1.108.1

ब्रह्मोवाच। कृष्णपक्षे तु माघस्य सर्वाप्तिं सप्तमीं शृणु। यामुपोष्य समाप्नोति सर्वान्कामांस्तथा परान् ॥

Brahmā said. Hear of the seventh day observance for attaining all aims in the dark fortnight of Māgha. By fasting on it, one obtains all desires and the higher ones as well.

BP 1.108.2

पाखण्डादिभिरालापं न कुर्याद्भानुतत्परः। पूजयेत्प्रणतो देवमेकाग्रमनसा शुभम् ॥

One devoted to Bhānu should not converse with heretics and the like. He should bow and worship the auspicious deity with his mind fixed on one point.

BP 1.108.3

माघाद्यैः पारणं मासैः षड्भिः सांक्रान्तिकं स्मृतम्। मार्तण्डः प्रथमं नाम द्वितीयं कः प्रकीर्तितम् ॥

The breaking of the fast through six months beginning with Māgha is remembered as the observance at the solar transition. Mārtaṇḍa is the first name, and Ka is proclaimed as the second.

BP 1.108.4

तृतीयं चित्रभानुश्च विभावसुरतः परम्। भगेति पञ्चमं ज्ञेयं षष्ठं हंसः स उच्यते ॥

The third is Citrabhānu, and next is Vibhāvasu. The fifth is to be known as Bhaga, and the sixth is called Hamsa.

BP 1.108.5

पूर्णेषु षट्सु मासेषु पञ्चगव्यमुदाहृतम्। स्नाने च प्राशने चैव प्रशस्तं पापनाशनम् ॥

When six months are complete, the five products of the cow are prescribed. In bathing and drinking, they are praised as destroyers of sin.

BP 1.108.6

प्रणामं देवदेवस्य कृत्वा पूजां यथाविधि। विप्राय दक्षिणां दद्याच्छ्रद्दधानश्च शक्तितः ॥

Having bowed to the God of gods and worshipped according to the rule, one should give a gift to a Brahmin with faith and according to ability.

BP 1.108.7

पारणान्ते च देवस्य प्रीणनं भक्तिपूर्वकम्। कुर्वीत भक्त्या विधिवद्रविभक्त्या तु गृह्यते ॥

At the end of breaking the fast, one should please the deity with devotion. It is accepted through devotion to Ravi.

BP 1.108.8

नक्तभोजी तथा विष्णो तैलक्षारविवर्जितः। कृष्ण जागरणं रात्रौ सप्तम्याम् अथ वा दिने ॥

Eating only at night and abstaining from oil and salt, O Viṣṇu, one should keep vigil on the night of the seventh day, or during the day, O Kṛṣṇa.

BP 1.108.9

एतामुषित्या धर्मज्ञो हंसप्रीणनतत्परः। सर्वान्कामानवाप्नोति सर्वपापैः प्रमुच्यते ॥

Having observed this, the knower of righteousness, intent on pleasing Hamsa, obtains all desires and is freed from every sin.

BP 1.108.10

यतः सर्वमवाप्नोति यद्यदिच्छति चेतसा। अतो लोकेषु विख्याता सर्वार्थावाप्तिसप्तमी ॥

Because one obtains everything that one desires in the mind, it is therefore famous among the worlds as the seventh day observance for attaining all aims.

BP 1.108.11

कृताभिलषिता ह्येषा प्रारब्धा धर्मतत्परैः। पूरयत्यखिलान्कामान्संश्रुता च दिनेदिने ॥

Undertaken by those devoted to righteousness and heard day after day, this fulfills every desire.

BP 1.108.12

तमाराधयस्व रविं तथाथ गरुडध्वज। यथाराधितवान्भानुं भगणाधिपतिः पुरा ॥

Therefore, O one whose banner bears Garuḍa, worship Ravi, as the Lord of the host of planets formerly worshipped Bhānu.
