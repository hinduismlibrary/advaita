Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 104 — The Seventh Day Vow of the Three Aims

BP 1.104.1

शृणुष्व संयतः काम्यानुपवासांस्तथापरान्। तांस्तानाश्रित्य यान्कामान्कुरुतेप्सितमानसः ॥

Hear, self controlled one, the desired fasts and the other fasts as well. Taking refuge in each of those, a person fulfills the desires held in mind.

BP 1.104.2

सप्तम्यां शुक्लपक्षे तु फाल्गुनस्येह मानवः। जपन्हेलीति देवस्य नाम भक्त्या पुनःपुनः ॥

In the month of Phālguna, on the seventh day of the bright fortnight, a person should repeat the name of the Sun God again and again with devotion.

BP 1.104.3

देवार्चने चाष्टशतं कृत्वैतच्च जपेच्छुचिः। स्नातः प्रस्थानकाले तु उत्थाने स्खलिते क्षुते ॥

During the worship of the deity, the pure person should repeat this eight hundred times. Having bathed, at the time of setting out, on rising, after stumbling, and after sneezing,

BP 1.104.4

पाखण्डान्पतितांश्चैव तथैवान्यायशालिनः। नालपेत तथा भानुमर्चयेच्छ्रद्धयान्वितः। इदं चोदाहरेद्भानौ मनः संधाय तत्परः ॥

He should not converse with heretics, fallen persons, or those who act unjustly. He should worship Bhānu with faith and, fixing his mind on him, recite this to him.

BP 1.104.5

हंसहंस कृपालुस्त्वमगतीनां गतिर्भव। संसारार्णवमग्नानां त्राता भव दिवाकर ॥

O Swan, O Swan, compassionate one, be the refuge of those without refuge. Be the rescuer of those sunk in the ocean of worldly existence, O Sun.

BP 1.104.6

एवं प्रसाद्योपवासं कृत्वा नियतमानसः। पूर्वाह्ण एव च सकृत्प्राश्याच्चाचमनीयकम् ॥

Having thus pleased him and fasted with a controlled mind, one should take a sip of water only once in the forenoon.

BP 1.104.7

स्नात्वार्चयित्वा हंसेति पुनर्नाम प्रकीर्तयेत्। वज्रधारात्रयं चैव क्षिपेत्त्रिर्देवपादयोः ॥

Having bathed and worshipped, he should again utter the name Swan. He should cast the three thunderbolt streams at the feet of the deity three times.

BP 1.104.8

चैत्रवैशाखयोश्चैव तद्वज्ज्येष्ठे तु पूजयन्। मर्त्यलोके गतिं श्रेष्ठां कृष्ण प्राप्नोति वै नरः ॥

Worshipping in the same way in Caitra, Vaiśākha, and Jyeṣṭha, a person obtains the highest destination in the human world, O Kṛṣṇa.

BP 1.104.9

उत्क्रांतस्तु व्रजेत्कृष्ण दिव्यं हंसालयं शुभम्। वृषध्वजप्रसादाद्वै संक्रन्दनश्रिया वृतः ॥

Upon departure, O Kṛṣṇa, he goes to the auspicious divine abode of the Swan. Through the grace of the Bull Bannered One, he is surrounded by the splendor of Indra.

BP 1.104.10

आषाढे श्रावणे चैव मासि भाद्रपदे तथा। तथैवाश्वयुजे चैव अनेन विधिना नरः ॥

In Āṣāḍha, Śrāvaṇa, Bhādrapada, and likewise Āśvayuja, a person should follow this procedure.

BP 1.104.11

उपोष्य सम्पूज्य तथा मार्तण्डेति च कीर्तयेत्। गोमूत्रप्राशनोत्पूतो धनी धनपुरं व्रजेत् ॥

Having fasted and fully worshipped, he should also utter the name Mārtaṇḍa. Purified by drinking cow urine, the wealthy person goes to the city of wealth.

BP 1.104.12

आराधितस्य जगतामीश्वरस्याव्ययात्मनः। उत्क्रांतिकाले स्मरणं भास्करस्य तथाप्नुयात् ॥

He should also attain remembrance of Bhāskara at the time of departure, the imperishable Lord of the worlds whom he has worshipped.

BP 1.104.13

क्षीरस्य प्राशनं कृष्ण विधिं चैव यथोदितम्। कार्त्तिकादि यथान्यायं कुर्यान्मासचतुष्टयम् ॥

O Kṛṣṇa, one should drink milk and follow the stated procedure for four months beginning with Kārttika, in due order.

BP 1.104.14

तेनैव विधिना कृष्ण भास्करेति च कीर्तयेत्। स याति भानुसालोक्यं भास्करं स्मरति क्षये ॥

By that same procedure, O Kṛṣṇa, he should utter the name Bhāskara. He goes to the same world as Bhānu, remembering Bhāskara at the end.

BP 1.104.15

प्रतिमासं द्विजातिभ्यो दद्याद्दानं यथेप्सितम्। चातुर्मास्ये तु सम्पूर्णे कृत्वा पुस्तकवाचनम् ॥

Each month, one should give the twice born a desired gift. When the four months are complete, one should arrange a reading of the book.

BP 1.104.16

कथां तु भास्करस्येह सङ्गीतकमथापि वा। धर्मश्रवणमभीष्टं सदा धर्मध्वजस्य तु ॥

A story of Bhāskara here, or music, and hearing about righteousness are always desired by one whose banner is righteousness.

BP 1.104.17

वाचकं पूजयित्वा तु तस्मात्कार्यं विपश्चिता। श्राद्धमन्येन पक्वेन वाचकेन द्विजेन तु। दिव्येन च यथायुक्तमभीष्टं भास्करस्य हि ॥

After honoring the reciter, the learned person should perform the ancestral rite with another cooked offering, through a Brahmin reciter, and with a suitable divine offering, for this is dear to Bhāskara.

BP 1.104.18

एवमेव गतिं श्रेष्ठां देवानामनुकीर्तनात्। प्राप्नुयात्त्रिविधां कृष्ण त्रिलोकाख्यां नरः सदा ॥

In this same way, through praise of the gods, a person should always attain the highest threefold destination called the three worlds, O Kṛṣṇa.

BP 1.104.19

कथितं पारणं यत्ते प्रथमं गोधराधनम्। आधिपत्यं तथा भोगांस्ततः प्राप्नोति मानुषः ॥

The first fast breaking I have described to you is worship of the cow. From that, a person obtains sovereignty and enjoyments.

BP 1.104.20

द्वितीयेन तथा भोगान्गोपतेः प्राप्नुयान्नरः। सूर्यलोकं तृतीयेन पारणेन तथाप्नुयात् ॥

By the second, a person likewise obtains the enjoyments of the Lord of cows. By the third fast breaking, he obtains the world of the Sun.

BP 1.104.21

एवमेतत्समाख्यातं गतिप्रापकमुत्तमम्। विधानं देवाशार्दूल यदुक्तं सप्तमीव्रते ॥

Thus, O tiger among the gods, the excellent procedure for reaching the highest destination, taught in the seventh day vow, has been described.

BP 1.104.22

यः श्वेतां सप्तमीं कुर्यात्सुगतिं श्रद्धया नरः। तथा भक्त्या च वै नारी प्राप्नोति त्रिविधां गतिम् ॥

A person who observes the white seventh day with faith obtains a good destination. A woman also, with devotion, obtains a threefold destination.

BP 1.104.23

एषा धन्या पापहरा तिथिर्नित्यमुपासिता। आराधनाय यस्तेषां यदा भानोर्धराधर ॥

This blessed lunar day, which removes sin, is always to be observed for the worship of Bhānu, O bearer of the earth.

BP 1.104.24

पठतां शृण्वतां चापि सर्वपापभयापहा। तथा धन्या च पुण्या च त्रिवर्गादीष्टदा सदा ॥

For those who read and hear it, it removes the fear of every sin. It is always blessed and meritorious, granting the desired fruits beginning with the three aims.
