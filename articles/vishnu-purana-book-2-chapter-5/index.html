VP 2.5.3

शुक्ला कृष्णारुणा पीता शर्करा शैलकाञ्चनाः । भूमयो यत्र मैत्रेय वरप्रासादमण्डिताः ॥३॥

Their soils are white, black, red, yellow, sandy, stony, and golden; in them, O Maitreya, the regions are adorned with magnificent palaces.

Śrīdhara translation

“Ten thousand” means that each floor rises one thousand yojanas; each Pātāla is therefore a subterranean cavity nine thousand yojanas high. The intervals are explained through the traditional measure; Mahākhyā is Mahātala.
