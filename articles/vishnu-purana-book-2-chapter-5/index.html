VP 2.5.1

विस्तार एष कथितः पृथिव्या भवतो मया।
सप्ततिस् तु सहस्राणि द्विजोच्छ्रायोऽपि कथ्यते

I have described to you the extent of the earth; its depth too, O twice-born, is said to be seventy thousand yojanas.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
विस्तारः एषः कथितः (vistāraḥ eṣaḥ kathitaḥ) — this extent has been described;
पृथिव्याः भवतः मया (pṛthivyāḥ bhavataḥ mayā) — of the earth, to you, by me;
सप्ततिः तु सहस्राणि (saptatiḥ tu sahasrāṇi) — seventy thousand;
द्विज-उच्छ्रायः अपि कथ्यते (dvija-ucchrāyaḥ api kathyate) — the height also is said, O twice-born
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vistāra eṣa kathitaḥ pṛthivyā bhavato mayā | saptatis tu sahasrāṇi dvijocchrāyo’pi kathyate

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 2.5.2

दश-साहस्रम् एकैकं पातालं मुनि-सत्तम।
अतलं वितलं चैव नितलं च गभस्तिमत्।
महाख्यं सुतलं चाग्र्यं पातालं चापि सप्तमम्

Each Pātāla is ten thousand yojanas, O best of sages. They are Atala, Vitala, Nitala, Gabhastimat, Mahātala, Sutala, and the seventh, Pātāla.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
दश-साहस्रम् एकैकम् (daśa-sāhasram ekaikam) — ten thousand each;
पातालम् मुनि-सत्तम (pātālam muni-sattama) — [for] each Pātāla, O best of sages;
अतलम् वितलम् च एव नितलम् च गभस्तिमत् (atalam vitalam ca eva nitalam ca gabhastimat) — Atala, Vitala, Nitala, and Gabhastimat;
महाख्यम् सुतलम् च अग्र्यम् (mahākhyam sutalam ca agryam) — Mahātala, Sutala, and Agrya;
पातालम् च अपि सप्तमम् (pātālam ca api saptamam) — and Pātāla, the seventh
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

daśa-sāhasram ekaikaṃ pātālaṃ muni-sattama | atalaṃ vitalaṃ caiva nitalaṃ ca gabhastimat | mahākhyaṃ sutalaṃ cāgryaṃ pātālaṃ cāpi saptamam

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 2.5.3

शुक्ला कृष्णारुणा पीता शर्करा शैल-काञ्चनाः।
भूमयो यत्र मैत्रेय वर-प्रासाद-मण्डिताः

Their soils are white, black, red, yellow, sandy, stony, and golden; in them, O Maitreya, the regions are adorned with magnificent palaces.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
शुक्ला कृष्णारुणा पीता शर्करा शैल-काञ्चनाः (śuklā kṛṣṇāruṇā pītā śarkarā śaila-kāñcanāḥ) — white, black, red, yellow, sandy, stony, and golden;
भूमयः यत्र मैत्रेय (bhūmayaḥ yatra maitreya) — the soils where, O Maitreya;
वर-प्रासाद-मण्ड् आः (vara-prāsāda-maṇḍ āḥ) — are adorned with excellent palaces
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

śuklā kṛṣṇāruṇā pītā śarkarā śaila-kāñcanāḥ | bhūmayo yatra maitreya vara-prāsāda-maṇḍitāḥ

daśa-sāhasram iti । pratyekaṃ sahasra-yojanocchritā bhūmikāḥ । tato nava-sahasrocchritam ekaikaṃ pātālaṃ bhū-vivaram ity arthaḥ । sahasra-yojanāny eṣāṃ jalāny-antara-nemayaḥ । pratyekaśo’ntarāṇy eṣāṃ sahasrāṇi navādhvānam ॥ iti śiva-rahasyokter mahākhyaṃ mahātalam

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

दश-साहस्रम् इति । प्रत्येकं सहस्र-योजनोच्छ्रिता भूमिकाः । ततो नव-सहस्रोच्छ्रितम् एकैकं पातालं भू-विवरम् इत्य् अर्थः । सहस्र-योजनान्य् एषां जलान्य्-अन्तर-नेमयः । प्रत्येकशोऽन्तराण्य् एषां सहस्राणि नवाध्वानम् ॥ इति शिव-रहस्योक्तेर् महाख्यं महातलम्

Śrīdhara commentary.

“Ten thousand” means that each floor rises one thousand yojanas; each Pātāla is therefore a subterranean cavity nine thousand yojanas high. The intervals are explained through the traditional measure; Mahākhyā is Mahātala.

VP 2.5.5

स्वर्-लोकाद् अपि रम्याणि पातालानीति नारदः।
प्राह स्वर्गसदां मध्ये पातालेभ्यो गतो दिवि

Nārada, having come from the Pātālas to heaven, declared among the dwellers in heaven that the Pātālas are more delightful even than heaven.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
स्वर्ग-सदां मध्ये (svarga-sadāṃ madhye) — among the dwellers in heaven;
पातालेभ्यः गतः दिवि (pātālebhyaḥ gataḥ divi) — having gone from the Pātālas to heaven
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

svar-lokād api ramyāṇi pātālānīti nāradaḥ | prāha svargasadāṃ madhye pātālebhyo gato divi

pātālebhyo divam abhyāgataḥ san svargasadāṃ devānāṃ madhye prāha

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

पातालेभ्यो दिवम् अभ्यागतः सन् स्वर्गसदां देवानां मध्ये प्राह

Śrīdhara commentary.

The phrase means: having come from the Pātālas to heaven, Nārada spoke among the gods who dwell in heaven.

VP 2.5.6

आह्लादकारिणः शुभ्राः मणयो यत्र सुप्रभाः।
नागैर् आभ्रियमाणासु पातालं केन तत् समम्

Where brilliant, white, pleasure-giving jewels are borne by the serpents, what can compare with that Pātāla?

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
आह्लाद-कारिणः शुभ्राः मणयः यत्र सुप्रभाः (āhlāda-kāriṇaḥ śubhrāḥ maṇayaḥ yatra suprabhāḥ) — where brilliant white, pleasure-giving jewels;
नागैः आभ्रियमणासु (nāgaiḥ ābhriyamaṇāsu) — are borne by the serpents;
पातालम् केन तत् समम् (pātālam kena tat samam) — what can compare with that Pātāla
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

āhlādakāriṇaḥ śubhrāḥ maṇayo yatra suprabhāḥ | nāgair ābhriyamāṇāsu pātālaṃ kena tat samam

śubhrāḥ śuddhā maṇayo nāgair ābhriyamāṇāsu dhāryamāṇāsu bhūṣāsu yatra tat pātālaṃ kena samam ? na kenāpi, nirupamam ity arthaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

शुभ्राः शुद्धा मणयो नागैर् आभ्रियमाणासु धार्यमाणासु भूषासु यत्र तत् पातालं केन समम् ? न केनापि, निरुपमम् इत्य् अर्थः

Śrīdhara commentary.

Aahlaadakaarinah iti | Naaga aabharana iti | Aabhriyata iti aabharanam, naagaih bhriyamaanah tu bhooshaasu | Yat va bhooshaah choodaadi alamkaaraah nityaah phanaa mani prabhrutayo vaa | Naaga aabharana bhooshaasu iti cha paathah | That which is worn is an ornament. On the hood, it is said to be a gem, mani, which is very bright and shining, with a lot of lustre, and they are all decorated with these. Paataala loka is extremely beautiful. There is no other world equal to this.

VP 2.5.7

दैत्य-दानव-कन्याभिर् इतश् चेतश् च शोभिते।
पाताले कस्य न प्रीतिर् विमुक्तस्यापि जायते

Adorned here and there by the daughters of Daityas and Dānavas, who would not feel delight in Pātāla, even one who is liberated?

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
दैत्य-दानव-कन्याभिः इतः चेतः च शोभिते (daitya-dānava-kanyābhiḥ itaḥ cetaḥ ca śobhite) — adorned here and there by the daughters of Daityas and Dānavas;
पाताले कस्य न प्रीतिः जायते (pātāle kasya na prītiḥ jāyate) — who would not feel delight in Pātāla;
विमुक्तस्य अपि (vimuktasya api) — even for one who is liberated
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

daitya-dānava-kanyābhir itaś cetaś ca śobhite | pātāle kasya na prītir vimuktasyāpi jāyate

vimuktasya vīta-rāgasyāpi

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

विमुक्तस्य वीत-रागस्यापि

Śrīdhara commentary.

“Liberated” means freed from attachment to sense objects; even such a person might delight in the beauty described here.

VP 2.5.8

दिवार्क-रश्मयो यत्र प्रभां तन्वन्ति नातपम्।

divārka-raśmayo yatra prabhāṃ tanvanti nātapam | śaśinaś ca na śītāya niśi dyotāya kevalam

No commentary.

No commentary.

VP 2.5.10

VP 2.5.11

भूषणान्य् अतिशुभ्राणि गन्धाढ्यं चानुलेपनम्।
वीणा-वेणु-मृदङ्गानां स्वनास् तूर्याणि च द्विज॥११॥

There are very bright ornaments, fragrant unguent, and the sounds of vīṇās, flutes, mṛdaṅgas, and other instruments, O twice-born.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
भूषणानि अति-शुभ्राणि (bhūṣaṇāni ati-śubhrāṇi) — very bright ornaments;

वीणा-वेणु-मृदङ्गानां स्वनाः (vīṇā-veṇu-mṛdaṅgānāṃ svanāḥ) — the sounds of vīṇās, flutes, and mṛdaṅgas;

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तूर्याणि नृत्यगीतवादित्राणि

उदारैरुत्कृष्टैर्भाग्यैः पुण्यैर्भोग्यानि । पातालानामन्तरं मध्यं गोचरो विषयो येषां तैः

Śrīdhara commentary.

“Instruments” means dance, song, and instrumental music. The enjoyments of the Pātālas are enjoyed through excellent merit by those whose sphere and object is within their middle region.

VP 2.5.13

पातालानाम् अधश् चास्ते विष्णोर् या तामसी तनुः।
शेषाख्या यद् गुणान् वक्तुं न शक्ता दैत्य-दानवाः

Below the Pātālas lies the tamasic form of Viṣṇu called Śeṣa, whose qualities the Daityas and Dānavas cannot describe.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
पातालानाम् अधः च आस्ते (pātālānām adhaḥ ca āste) — below the Pātālas lies;
विष्णोः या तामसी तनुः (viṣṇoḥ yā tāmasī tanuḥ) — the tamasic form of Viṣṇu;
शेष-आख्या (śeṣa-ākhyā) — called Śeṣa;

लगितं चर्चितम् अनुलिप्तं, यस्य श्वासानिलैरपास्तं, विक्षिप्तं दिक्षु उदवासतां जलसुगन्धीकरणचूर्णतां यातीत्यर्थः । दिक्क्षोदवासताम् इति पाठे दिशां क्षोदवासताम् अधिवासनचूर्णत्वम् । यद्वा क्षोदाः क्रीडापांशवस्तेषां वासतां स्थानतां दिक्कन्यानां क्रीडार्थे हरिद्रादि रजःस्थानीयं भवतीत्यर्थः । दिक्पटवासताम् इति पाठः

Śrīdhara commentary.

“Applied” means smeared and anointed. In the reading “lambhitam,” it means applied and spread over the body. “Scattered in the quarters” means that the paste, carried by the breath, becomes perfume or perfuming powder in the quarters; the source also records alternative readings.

VP 2.5.26

यम् आराध्य पुराण-र्षिर् गर्गो ज्योतींषि तत्त्वतः।
ज्ञातवान् सकलं चैव निमित्त-पठितं फलम्

Having worshipped him, the ancient sage Garga learned the stars in truth, and all the results read from their indications.

VP 2.5.27

तेनेयं नाग-वर्येण शिरसा विधृता मही।
बिभर्ति मालां लोकानां स-देवासुर-मानुषाम्

This earth, upheld on the head of that excellent serpent, in turn bears the garland of the worlds together with gods, demons, and humans.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
तेन इयं नाग-वर्येण शिरसा विधृता मही (tena iyaṃ nāga-varyeṇa śirasā vidhṛtā mahī) — this earth, upheld on the head of that excellent serpent;
बिभर्ति मालाम् लोकानाम् (bibharti mālām lokānām) — bears the garland of the worlds;
स-देव-असुर-मानुषाम् (sa-deva-asura-mānuṣām) — together with gods, demons, and humans
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

teneyaṃ nāga-varyeṇa śirasā vidhṛtā mahī | bibharti mālāṃ lokānāṃ sa-devāsura-mānuṣām

devāsuramānuṣaiḥ sahitāṃ lokānāṃ pātālādīnāṃ mālāṃ śreṇīṃ tena vidhṛtā satīyaṃ mahī bibharti । lokādhārabhūtāyā bhūmerapi sa ādhāra ityarthaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

देवासुरमानुषैः सहितां लोकानां पातालादीनां मालां श्रेणीं तेन विधृता सतीयं मही बिभर्ति । लोकाधारभूताया भूमेरपि स आधार इत्यर्थः

Śrīdhara commentary.

The earth bears the garland, the series of worlds beginning with Pātāla, together with gods, demons, and humans; hence Śeṣa is the support even of the earth, which supports the worlds.
