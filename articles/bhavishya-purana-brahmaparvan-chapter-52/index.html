Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 52 — The Solar Chariot and Worship of Sūrya

BP 1.52.1

सुमन्तुरुवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा भगवान्देवः शङ्खचक्रगदाधरः ।
अन्तर्धानं गतो वीरं शाम्बस्येह प्रपश्यतः ॥

Sumantu said: Having thus spoken, the blessed Lord, bearer of conch, discus, and mace, disappeared as Śāmba, the hero, was looking on here.

Word-for-word
सुमन्तुः उवाच (sumantuḥ uvāca) — Sumantu said;
इति उक्त्वा (ity uktvā) — having thus spoken; भगवान् देवः (bhagavān devaḥ) — the blessed Lord; शङ्ख-चक्र-गदा-धरः (śaṅkha-cakra-gadā-dharaḥ) — bearer of conch, discus, and mace;
अन्तर्धानं गतः (antardhānaṃ gataḥ) — went into disappearance; वीरं (vīraṃ) — the hero; शाम्बस्य इह प्रपश्यतः (śāmbasya iha prapaśyataḥ) — of Śāmba looking on here
Transliteration
sumanturuvāca |
ityuktvā bhagavāndevaḥ śaṅkhacakragadādharaḥ |
antardhānaṃ gato vīraṃ śāmbasyeha prapaśyataḥ || 1

BP 1.52.6

Word-for-word
श्रीरुद्रः उवाच (śrīrudraḥ uvāca) — Śrī Rudra said;

BP 1.52.7

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
यथा दिवि भ्रमेत्तात रथारूढो रविः सदा ।
तथा ते वर्तयिष्येऽहं रथं चास्य त्रिलोचन ॥

Brahmā said: As Ravi, mounted on the chariot, always roams in heaven, O dear one, so I shall make his chariot move, O three-eyed one.

Word-for-word
ब्रह्मा उवाच (brahmā uvāca) — Brahmā said;
यथा (yathā) — as; दिवि (divi) — in heaven; भ्रमेत् (bhramet) — roams; तात (tāta) — O dear one; रथारूढः (rathārūḍhaḥ) — mounted on the chariot; रविः (raviḥ) — Ravi; सदा (sadā) — always;
तथा (tathā) — so; ते (te) — his; वर्तयिष्ये (vartayiṣye) — I shall make move; अहम् (aham) — I; रथम् च अस्य (ratham ca asya) — the chariot of this one; त्रिलोचन (trilocana) — O three-eyed one
