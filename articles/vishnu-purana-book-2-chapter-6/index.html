VP 2.6.1

ततश् च नरका विप्र भुवोऽधः सलिलस्य च।
पापिनो येषु पात्य् अन्ते ताञ् छृणुष्व महा-मुने

Parāśara said—From this point, O Brahman, hear of the hells below the earth and the waters, where sinners are cast.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
ततः च (tataḥ ca) — and then;
नरकाः (narakāḥ) — hells;
भूवः अधः (bhūvaḥ adhaḥ) — below the earth;
सलिलस्य च (salilasya ca) — and below the waters;
पापिनः (pāpinaḥ) — sinners;
येषु (yeṣu) — in which;
पात्यन्ते (pātyante) — are cast;
तान् शृणु (tān śṛṇu) — hear them.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tataś ca narakā vipra bhuvo’dhaḥ salilasya ca | pāpino yeṣu pāty ante tāñ chṛṇuṣva mahā-mune

bhārate kṛtapuṇyānāmuktāḥ svargapradā bhuvaḥ | tatraiva kṛtapāpānāṃ yātanā ca tathocyate | salilasya ceti tamogarttodakasyādhaḥ brahmāṇḍagatagarbhodakādūrddham eva | diśi dakṣiṇasyāmadhastād bhūmerupariṣṭājjalād iti śukokteḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

भारते कृतपुण्यानामुक्ताः स्वर्गप्रदा भुवः । तत्रैव कृतपापानां यातना च तथोच्यते । सलिलस्य चेति तमोगर्त्तोदकस्याधः ब्रह्माण्डगतगर्भोदकादूर्द्धम् एव । दिशि दक्षिणस्यामधस्ताद् भूमेरुपरिष्टाज्जलाद् इति शुकोक्तेः

Śrīdhara commentary.

Parāśara said—From this point, O Brahman, hear of the hells below the earth and the waters, where sinners are cast.

VP 2.6.7

कूट-साक्षी यथासम्यक्-पक्षपातेन यो वदेत्।
यश् चान्यद् अनृतं वक्ति स नरो याति रौरवम्

A false witness who, knowing the truth, speaks otherwise or with partiality, and one who speaks any other falsehood, goes to Raurava.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
कूट-साक्षी (kūṭa-sākṣī) — false witness;
यथा-सम्यक्-पक्षपातेन (yathā-samyak-pakṣapātena) — with improper partiality;
यः वदेत् (yaḥ vadet) — who speaks;
अन्यः अनृतम् (anyaḥ anṛtam) — another falsehood;
सः नरः (saḥ naraḥ) — that man;
रौरवम् याति (rauravam yāti) — goes to Raurava.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kūṭa-sākṣī yathāsamyak-pakṣapātena yo vadet | yaś cānyad anṛtaṃ vakti sa naro yāti rauravam

jānannapyavadan anyathā vā vadan kūṭasākṣī | yaśca dharmādhikṛtaḥ san asamyag anyāyaṃ vadet | yaścānyadapi anṛtaṃ vakti

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

जानन्नप्यवदन् अन्यथा वा वदन् कूटसाक्षी । यश्च धर्माधिकृतः सन् असम्यग् अन्यायं वदेत् । यश्चान्यदपि अनृतं वक्ति

Śrīdhara commentary.

A false witness is one who, even knowing the truth, speaks otherwise.

VP 2.6.8

भ्रूणहा पुर-हन्ता च गोघ्नश् च मुनि-सत्तम।
यान्ति ते नरकं रोधं यश् चोच्छ्वास-निरोधकः

The slayer of a fetus, the destroyer or plunderer of a city, and the killer of a cow go to Rodha, as does one who stops another’s breath.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
भ्रूण-हा (bhrūṇa-hā) — fetus-killer;
पुर-हन्ता (pura-hantā) — city-destroyer;
गो-घ्नः (go-ghnaḥ) — cow-killer;
रोधम् यान्ति (rodham yānti) — go to Rodha;
उच्छ्वास-निरोधकः (ucchvāsa-nirodhakaḥ) — one who stops the breath.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bhrūṇahā pura-hantā ca goghnaś ca muni-sattama | yānti te narakaṃ rodhaṃ yaś cocchvāsa-nirodhakaḥ

bhrūṇahā garbhahantā purahartā puravilumpakaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

भ्रूणहा गर्भहन्ता पुरहर्ता पुरविलुम्पकः

Śrīdhara commentary.

‘Fetus-killer’ means one who kills a fetus; ‘city-killer’ means one who destroys or plunders a city.

VP 2.6.9

सुरापो ब्रह्महा हर्ता सुवर्णस्य च सूकरे।
प्रयान्ति नरके यश् च तैः संसर्गम् उपैति वै

The drinker of liquor, the killer of a Brahman, the taker of a Brahman’s gold, and one who associates with them go to Śūkara.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
सुरापः (surāpaḥ) — liquor-drinker;
ब्रह्म-हा (brahma-hā) — Brahman-killer;
हर्त (harta) — taker;
सुवर्णस्य (suvarṇasya) — of gold;
शूकरे (śūkare) — to Śūkara;
तैः संसर्गम् (taiḥ saṃsargam) — association with them;
उपैति (upaiti) — approaches.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

surāpo brahmahā hartā suvarṇasya ca sūkare | prayānti narake yaś ca taiḥ saṃsargam upaiti vai

taiḥ saṃsargam iti— saṃvatsareṇa patita saha śayyāsanāśanaiḥ | yājanādhyāpanād yaunāt sadyaḥ patati tatsamaḥ | iti | taiḥ saha saṃsargam

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तैः संसर्गम् इति— संवत्सरेण पतित सह शय्यासनाशनैः । याजनाध्यापनाद् यौनात् सद्यः पतति तत्समः । इति । तैः सह संसर्गम्

Śrīdhara commentary.

‘Association with them’ is explained by the cited rule: by sharing bed, seat, or food with an outcaste for a year, or through officiating for him, teaching him, or sexual relation, one immediately becomes equal to him.

‘A virtuous wife’ means a wife; her seller is the seller of a wife. A bandha-pāla is a prison guard, and a kesari-vikrayī is a seller of horses.

VP 2.6.13
