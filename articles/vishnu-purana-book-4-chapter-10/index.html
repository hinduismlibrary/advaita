VP 4.10.1

यतिययातिसंयात्यायातिवियातिकृतिसंज्ञा नहुषस्य षट्पुत्रा महाबलपराक्रमा बभूवुः

Nahusha's vamsha is going to be told now. Yati, Yayaati, Samyaati, Ayaati, Viyaati, Kruti are Nahusha's sons. They were very powerful valiant sons.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yatiyayātisaṃyātyāyātiviyātikṛtisaṃjñā nahuṣasya ṣaṭputrā mahābalaparākramā babhūvuḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.10.4

उशनसश्च दुहितरं देवयानीं वार्षपर्वणीं च शर्मिष्ठामुपयेमे

Yayaati marries the daughter of Ushanas, Shukraachaarya - Devayaani, and also Vrushaparva's daughter Sharmishthaa. Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

uśanasaśca duhitaraṃ devayānīṃ vārṣaparvaṇīṃ ca śarmiṣṭhāmupayeme

Śrīdhara

daśame nāhuṣasyātha yayātervaṃśa uccate . udgīteneha sarveṣā vaitṛṣṇyamupajāyate . upayeme devayānīṃ brāhmaṇa śarmiṣṭhāṃ gāndharveṇetyavadheyam ||४||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

दशमे नाहुषस्याथ ययातेर्वंश उच्चते । उद्गीतेनेह सर्वेषा वैतृष्ण्यमुपजायते । उपयेमे देवयानीं ब्राह्मण शर्मिष्ठां गान्धर्वेणेत्यवधेयम् ॥४॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Yayaati marries the daughter of Ushanas, Shukraachaarya - Devayaani, and also Vrushaparva's daughter Sharmishthaa. Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4

VP 4.10.6

यदुं च दुर्वसुं चैव देवयानी व्यजायत।
द्रुह्युं चानुं च पूरुं च शर्मिष्ठा वार्षपर्वणी

Devayaani had two sons - Yadu and Turvasu. Sharmishthaa, who was daughter of Vrushaparva, had three sons Druhyu, Anu and Puru.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yaduṃ ca durvasuṃ caiva devayānī vyajāyata | druhyuṃ cānuṃ ca pūruṃ ca śarmiṣṭhā vārṣaparvaṇī

Śrīdhara

anuvaṃśo vaṃśamanugataḥ śloka ity arthaḥ ||६||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अनुवंशो वंशमनुगतः श्लोक इत्य् अर्थः ॥६॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Devayaani had two sons - Yadu and Turvasu. Sharmishthaa, who was daughter of Vrushaparva, had three sons Druhyu, Anu and Puru.

VP 4.10.7

काव्यशापाच्चाकालेनैव ययातिर्जरामवाप

Shukraachaarya gives him a shaapa and Yayaati gets old age very early. Then he goes and requests Shukraachaarya that he should not do like this, and asks his pardon.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kāvyaśāpāccākālenaiva yayātirjarāmavāpa

Śrīdhara

kāvyaśāpāditi . śarmiṣṭhāyāṃ dāsyāṃ yayātinā putrotpādanaṃ devayānyā kathitaṃ śrutvā kruddhasya śukrasya śāpādakāle jarāṃ yayātiravāpa ||७||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

काव्यशापादिति । शर्मिष्ठायां दास्यां ययातिना पुत्रोत्पादनं देवयान्या कथितं श्रुत्वा क्रुद्धस्य शुक्रस्य शापादकाले जरां ययातिरवाप ॥७॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Shukraachaarya gives him a shaapa and Yayaati gets old age very early. Then he goes and requests Shukraachaarya that he should not do like this, and asks his pardon.

VP 4.10.8

प्रसन्नशुक्रवचनाच्च स्वजरां संक्रामयितुं ज्येष्ठं पुत्रं यदुमुवाच

Shukraachaarya being pleased, and said that he cannot make Yayaati get rid of this, but can exchange the old age with someone else. With someone's youth, he can exchange his old age. So, Yayaati goes to his eldest son Yadu, and asks him - &quot;Can you take my old age and give me your youth&quot;.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

prasannaśukravacanācca svajarāṃ saṃkrāmayituṃ jyeṣṭhaṃ putraṃ yadumuvāca

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.10.9

वत्स त्वन्मातामहशापादियमकालेनैव जरा ममोपस्थिता तामहं तस्यैवानुग्रहाद्भवतःसंचारयामि

O son, because of your maternal grandfather's shaapa, I have got akaala jaraa, when I am young itself, I have got this old age problem, with his grace, I can give my old age to you |

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vatsa tvanmātāmahaśāpādiyamakālenaiva jarā mamopasthitā tāmahaṃ tasyaivānugrahādbhavataḥsaṃcārayāmi

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.10.10

एकं वर्षसहस्रमतृप्तोऽस्मि विषयेषु त्वद्वयसा विषयानहं भोक्तुमिच्छमि

I am not satisfied with my sensual pleasures, and for one thousand years, I want to enjoy with your youth. Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ekaṃ varṣasahasramatṛpto'smi viṣayeṣu tvadvayasā viṣayānahaṃ bhoktumicchami

Śrīdhara

prasannaśukravacanāditi—punaḥ prasannaśukrasya yadvacanaṃ yasmin jarāṃ saṅkrāmayiṣyasi, tasmin sthāsyati, tadīyaṃ yauvanaṃ ca āpsyasīti . tasmājjarāṃ saṅkrāmayitumarthād yauvanañcādātuṃ yadumuvāca . vayasā yauvanena ||१०||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

प्रसन्नशुक्रवचनादिति—पुनः प्रसन्नशुक्रस्य यद्वचनं यस्मिन् जरां सङ्क्रामयिष्यसि, तस्मिन् स्थास्यति, तदीयं यौवनं च आप्स्यसीति । तस्माज्जरां सङ्क्रामयितुमर्थाद् यौवनञ्चादातुं यदुमुवाच । वयसा यौवनेन ॥१०॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

I am not satisfied with my sensual pleasures, and for one thousand years, I want to enjoy with your youth. Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4

VP 4.10.11

नात्र भवता प्रत्याख्यानं कर्तव्यमित्युक्तःस यदुर्नैच्छत्तां जरामादातुम्

VP 4.10.20

विश्वाच्या देवयान्या च सहोपभोगं भुक्त्वा कामानामन्तं प्राप्स्यामीत्यनुदिनं तन्मनस्को बभूव

VP 4.10.21

अनुदिनम् उपभोगतः कामान् अतिरम्यान् मेने

As he started enjoying day by day, he started enjoying more and more, and thought it was most wonderful.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

anudinam upabhogataḥ kāmān atiramyān mene

Śrīdhara

upabhogatastadvāsanātaḥ . atiramyān viṣayān atīvamene atiśayenābhilaṣitavānity arthaḥ ||२१||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

उपभोगतस्तद्वासनातः । अतिरम्यान् विषयान् अतीवमेने अतिशयेनाभिलषितवानित्य् अर्थः ॥२१॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

As he started enjoying day by day, he started enjoying more and more, and thought it was most wonderful.

VP 4.10.23

न जातु कामः कामानामुपभोगेन शाम्यति।
हविषा कृष्णवर्त्मेव भूय एवाभिवर्धते

This is a very often quoted shloka. By enjoying the pleasures of the senses, the desire will never end. As the fire keeps on increasing as we offer havis, it keeps on growing more and more, the flames become brighter and bigger, like that, the desires for sensual pleasures keep on increasing only, the more and more one enjoys.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

na jātu kāmaḥ kāmānāmupabhogena śāmyati | haviṣā kṛṣṇavartmeva bhūya evābhivardhate

Śrīdhara

tataśca nirvedāt kāmanivṛttiprakāramāha—na jātv ity-ādinā yāvat samāptiḥ . kṛṣṇavartmā agniḥ ||२३|| yat pṛthivyāṃ vrīhi-yavaṃ hiraṇyaṃ paśavaḥ striyaḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

ततश्च निर्वेदात् कामनिवृत्तिप्रकारमाह—न जात्व् इत्य्-आदिना यावत् समाप्तिः । कृष्णवर्त्मा अग्निः ॥२३॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

This is a very often quoted shloka. By enjoying the pleasures of the senses, the desire will never end. As the fire keeps on increasing as we offer havis, it keeps on growing more and more, the flames become brighter and bigger, like that, the desires for sensual pleasures keep on increasing only, the more and more one enjoys.

VP 4.10.24

यत्पृथिव्यां व्रीहियवं हिरण्यं पशवः स्त्रियः।
एकस्यापि न पर्याप्तं तस्मात्तृष्णां परित्यजेत्

Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4 All the rice, grains, gold, all the cows, all the women, the entire thing is not sufficient for one person; so desire is something which one has to give up. Desire can never be satisfied.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yatpṛthivyāṃ vrīhiyavaṃ hiraṇyaṃ paśavaḥ striyaḥ | ekasyāpi na paryāptaṃ tasmāttṛṣṇāṃ parityajet

Śrīdhara

yaditi tṛṣṇāpūrteraśakyatayā duḥkhahetutvād doṣadṛṣṭiṃ kurvan tāṃ tyajediti bhāvaḥ ||२४|| yadā na kurute bhāvaṃ sarva-bhūteṣu pāpakam .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

यदिति तृष्णापूर्तेरशक्यतया दुःखहेतुत्वाद् दोषदृष्टिं कुर्वन् तां त्यजेदिति भावः ॥२४॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4 All the rice, grains, gold, all the cows, all the women, the entire thing is not sufficient for one person; so desire is something which one has to give up. Desire can never be satisfied.

VP 4.10.25

यदा न कुरुते भावं सर्वभूतेषु पापकम्।
समदृष्टेस्तदा पुंसः सर्वाःसुखमया दिशः

When, in all the beings, one does not have a bad feeling, he will have samadrushti, he will be equally disposed towards everyone, and at that time, he will have all round happiness.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yadā na kurute bhāvaṃ sarvabhūteṣu pāpakam | samadṛṣṭestadā puṃsaḥ sarvāḥsukhamayā diśaḥ

Śrīdhara

tarhi viṣayatyāge kathaṃ sukhaprāptistatrāha—yadā sarvabhūteṣu viṣayeṣu bhāvaṃ guṇādhyāsaṃ pāpakaṃ rāgādijanakaṃ na kurute, kintu teṣu bhautikatvena samadṛṣṭistadā īpsitālābhajanyaduḥkābhāvāt sarvā diśaḥ sukhamayaḥ duḥkhābhāve sukhatvopacārāt ||२५|| yā dustyajā durmatibhir yā na jīryati jīryataḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तर्हि विषयत्यागे कथं सुखप्राप्तिस्तत्राह—यदा सर्वभूतेषु विषयेषु भावं गुणाध्यासं पापकं रागादिजनकं न कुरुते, किन्तु तेषु भौतिकत्वेन समदृष्टिस्तदा ईप्सितालाभजन्यदुःकाभावात् सर्वा दिशः सुखमयः दुःखाभावे सुखत्वोपचारात् ॥२५॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

When, in all the beings, one does not have a bad feeling, he will have samadrushti, he will be equally disposed towards everyone, and at that time, he will have all round happiness.

VP 4.10.26

या दुस्त्यजा दुर्मतिभिर्या न जीर्यति जीर्यतः।
तां तृष्णां संत्यजेत्प्राज्ञःसुखेनैवाभिपूर्यते

That which cannot be given up by bad people, wicked people, and even when you become old, that which does not become old, (as a person becomes old also, the desire does not get old, it remains young), that desire one has to give up completely. Who should give up? One who is wise. Then he becomes a complete person very easily. If he gives up desire, without any difficulty he will become a complete person.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yā dustyajā durmatibhiryā na jīryati jīryataḥ | tāṃ tṛṣṇāṃ saṃtyajetprājñaḥsukhenaivābhipūryate

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.10.27

जीर्यन्ति जीर्यतः केशा दन्ता जीर्यन्ति जीर्यतः।
धनाशा जीविताशा च जीर्यतोऽपि न जीर्यतः

As one gets old, hairs become old and they fall; teeth also become weak and old. But what does not get old is the desire for money, and desire to live. These never get old even if one gets very old.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

jīryanti jīryataḥ keśā dantā jīryanti jīryataḥ | dhanāśā jīvitāśā ca jīryato'pi na jīryataḥ

Śrīdhara

doṣadṛṣṭiṃ darśayan tṛṣṇāyāḥ kālato’pi duṣpūratāmāha—jīryanti iti dvābhyām ||२७|| pūrṇaṃ varṣa-sahasraṃ me viṣayāsakta-cetasaḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

दोषदृष्टिं दर्शयन् तृष्णायाः कालतोऽपि दुष्पूरतामाह—जीर्यन्ति इति द्वाभ्याम् ॥२७॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

As one gets old, hairs become old and they fall; teeth also become weak and old. But what does not get old is the desire for money, and desire to live. These never get old even if one gets very old.

VP 4.10.28

पूर्णं वर्षसहस्रं मे विषयासक्तचेतसः।
तथा प्यनुदिनं तृष्णा मम तेषूपजायते

I spent one thousand years being totally interested in enjoying the pleasures of the senses, in spite of that, every single day, my desire is only increasing.

Transliteration

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.10.29

तस्माद् एताम् अहं त्यक्त्वा ब्रह्मण्य् आधाय मानसम्।
निर्द्वन्द्वो निर्ममो भूत्वा चरिष्यामि मृगैः सह

Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4 I am now going to give up this desire completely, and am going to meditate upon Brahman. I will be unaffected by the dualities of life, I will not have anything which is mine, I will be with the idea that nothing is belonging to me, with the idea of non possession, I will spend my time in the forest with the animals.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

VP 4.10.30
