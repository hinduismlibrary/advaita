Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 105 — The Seventh Day Vow That Grants Desires

BP 1.105.1

ब्रह्मोवाच। फाल्गुनामलपक्षस्य सप्तम्यां क्ष्माधराव्यय। उपोषितो नरो नारी समभ्यर्च्य तमोऽपहम् ॥

Brahmā said. On the seventh day of the bright fortnight of Phālguna, O imperishable bearer of the earth, a man or a woman should fast and fully worship the dispeller of darkness.
