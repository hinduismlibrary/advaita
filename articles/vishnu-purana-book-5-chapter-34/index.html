VP 5.34.1

श्रीमैत्रेय उवाच

चक्रे कर्म महच् छौरिर् बिभ्रणोमानुषीं तनुम् ।
जिगाय शक्रं शर्वं च सर्व-देवांश् च लीलया ॥१॥

Maitreya said: Śauri, assuming a human body, performed great deeds; he conquered Indra, Śarva, and all the gods with ease.

Śrīdhara translation

“The divine activities” means the prowess of the gods dwelling in heaven; this [deed] obstructs that prowess.

Pauṇḍraka Vāsudeva became [a claimant to being] Vāsudeva on earth, called by people deluded by ignorance, “You have incarnated.”

“Pauṇḍraka” means one born in the Pauṇḍra country. Deluded by ignorance, ordinary people told him, “You are Vāsudeva incarnate.”
