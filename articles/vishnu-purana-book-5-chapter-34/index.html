VP 5.34.1

श्रीमैत्रेय उवाच

चक्रे कर्म महच्छौरिर्बिभ्राणो मानुषीं तनुम् ।
जिगाय शक्रं शर्वं च सर्वान्देवांश्च लीलया

Maitreya said: Śauri, assuming a human body, performed great deeds; he conquered Indra, Śarva, and all the gods with ease.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
चक्रे (cakre) — performed
कर्म महत् (karma mahat) — great deeds
शौरिः (śauriḥ) — Śauri
बिभ्-राणः मानुषीम् तनुम् (bibh-rāṇaḥ mānuṣīm tanum) — assuming a human body
जिगाय (jigāya) — conquered
शक्रम् शर्वम् च (śakram śarvam ca) — Indra and Śarva
सर्वान् देवान् (sarvān devān) — all the gods
लीलया (līlayā) — playfully / with ease
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

cakre karma mahacchaurirbibhrāṇo mānuṣīṃ tanum | jigāya śakraṃ śarvaṃ ca sarvāndevāṃśca līlayā

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.34.2

यच्चान्यदकरोत्कर्म दिव्यचेष्टाविघातकृत् ।
तत्कथ्यतां महाभाग परं कौतूहलं हि मे

What other deed did he perform that obstructed the divine activities? O greatly blessed one, tell me; I am exceedingly eager to hear it.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
यत् च अन्यत् (yat ca anyat) — what other
अकरोत् कर्म (akarot karma) — deed he performed
दिव्य-चेष्टा-विघात-कृत् (divya-ceṣṭā-vighāta-kṛt) — obstructing divine activities
तत् कथ्यताम् (tat kathyatām) — let that be told
महा-भाग (mahā-bhāga) — O greatly blessed one
परम् कौतूहलम् हि मे (param kautūhalam hi me) — I am very eager
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yaccānyadakarotkarma divyaceṣṭāvighātakṛt | tatkathyatāṃ mahābhāga paraṃ kautūhalaṃ hi me

Śrīdhara

yac cānyad iti | divyānāṃ divi bhavānāṃ devānāṃ ceṣṭā parākramaḥ, tad-vighātakam || 1 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

यच् चान्यद् इति । दिव्यानां दिवि भवानां देवानां चेष्टा पराक्रमः, तद्-विघातकम् ॥१ ॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

“Divine activities” means the prowess of the gods who dwell in heaven; this deed obstructs that prowess.

VP 5.34.4

पौण्ड्रको वासुदेवस्तु वासुदेवोऽभवद्भुवि ।
अवतीर्णस्त्वमित्युक्तो जनैरज्ञानमोहितैः

Pauṇḍraka Vāsudeva became [a claimant to being] Vāsudeva on earth, called by people deluded by ignorance, “You have incarnated.”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
पौण्ड्रकः वासुदेवः (pauṇḍrakaḥ vāsudevaḥ) — Pauṇḍraka Vāsudeva
वासुदेवः अभवत् (vāsudevaḥ abhavat) — became Vāsudeva
भुवि (bhuvi) — on earth
अवतीर्णः त्वम् इति उक्तः (avatīrṇaḥ tvam iti uktaḥ) — called ‘you have incarnated’
जनैः (janaiḥ) — by people
अज्ञान-मोहितैः (ajñāna-mohitaiḥ) — deluded by ignorance
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

pauṇḍrako vāsudevastu vāsudevo’bhavadbhuvi | avatīrṇastvamityukto janairajñānamohitaiḥ

Śrīdhara

pauṇḍraka iti | pauṇḍrakaḥ pauṇḍra-deśajaḥ | ajñāna-mohitaiḥ prākṛtair janair vāsudevo’vatīrṇas tvam ity uktaḥ || 4 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

पौण्ड्रक इति । पौण्ड्रकः पौण्ड्र-देशजः । अज्ञान-मोहितैः प्राकृतैर् जनैर् वासुदेवोऽवतीर्णस् त्वम् इत्य् उक्तः ॥४ ॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Pauṇḍraka means one born in the Pauṇḍra country. Ordinary people deluded by ignorance told him, “You are Vāsudeva incarnate.”

VP 5.34.5

He came to believe literally, “I alone am Vāsudeva.”

VP 5.34.8

इत्युक्तस्सम्प्रहस्यैनं दूतं प्राह जनार्दनः ।
निजचिह्नमहं चक्रं समुत्स्रक्ष्ये त्वयीति वै

Having heard this, Janārdana laughed and said to the messenger, “I shall cast my own emblem, the discus, at you.”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
इति उक्तः (iti uktaḥ) — thus addressed
सम्-प्रहास्य (sam-prahāsya) — laughing
एनम् दूतम् (enam dūtam) — at that messenger
प्राह (prāha) — said
जनार्दनः (janārdanaḥ) — Janārdana
निज-चिह्नम् अहम् चक्रम् (nija-cihnam aham cakram) — my own emblem, the discus
समुत्स्रक्ष्ये (samutsrakṣye) — I shall cast
त्वयि इति (tvayi iti) — at you, thus
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ityuktassamprahasyainaṃ dūtaṃ prāha janārdanaḥ | nijacihnamahaṃ cakraṃ samutsrakṣye tvayīti vai

Śrīdhara

ity ukta iti | samutsrakṣye tvayīti buddhi-sannihitaṃ pauṇḍrakam abhisandhāyoktaṃ cakraṃ kṣiptvāhaṃ hariṣyāmīti bhāvaḥ || 8 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

इत्य् उक्त इति । समुत्स्रक्ष्ये त्वयीति बुद्धि-सन्निहितं पौण्ड्रकम् अभिसन्धायोक्तं चक्रं क्षिप्त्वाहं हरिष्यामीति भावः ॥८ ॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

By saying “I shall cast the discus at you,” Kṛṣṇa had Pauṇḍraka mentally in view: “I shall throw the discus and destroy him.”

VP 5.34.9

वाच्यश्च पौण्ड्रको गत्वा त्वया दूत वचो मम ।
ज्ञातस्त्वद्वाक्यसद्भावो यत्कार्यं तद्विधीयताम्

“Go to Pauṇḍraka and tell him these words from me: I have understood the real import of your message; now do whatever you have to do.”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
वाच्यः च (vācyaḥ ca) — and he is to be told

गत्वा (gatvā) — having gone
त्वया दूत (tvayā dūta) — by you, O messenger

ज्ञातः (jñātaḥ) — understood
त्वद्-वाक्य-सद्-भावः (tvad-vākya-sad-bhāvaḥ) — the real meaning of your words
यत् कार्यम् (yat kāryam) — whatever is to be done
तत् विधीयताम् (tat vidhīyatām) — let that be done
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vācyaśca pauṇḍrako gatvā tvayā dūta vaco mama | jñātastvadvākyasadbhāvo yatkāryaṃ tadvidhīyatām

Śrīdhara

etat spaṣṭayann āha—vācyaś cety -ādi caturbhiḥ | vācyaś ceti | tvad-vākyasya sad-bhāvo yathā syāt tatham || 9 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

एतत् स्पष्टयन्न् आह—वाच्यश् चेत्य् -आदि चतुर्भिः । वाच्यश् चेति । त्वद्-वाक्यस्य सद्-भावो यथा स्यात् तथम् ॥९ ॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

The next four verses explain this: the messenger is to tell Pauṇḍraka that Kṛṣṇa has understood the real meaning of his words.

Śrīdhara commentary.

“Having come to your city, I shall act so that I will have no further fear from you”—that is, “I shall kill you.”

विष्णोः करम् अभ्याययौ (viṣṇoḥ karam abhyāyayau) — returned to Viṣṇu’s hand
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

akṣīṇāmarṣamatyugrasādhyasādhanasaspṛham | taccakraṃ prasphuraddīpti viṣṇorabhyāyayau karam

Śrīdhara

akṣīṇeti | akṣīṇāmarṣam agata-krodham ata evātyalpasya kṛtyā vārāṇasī-dāhād duḥsādhyasya sādhanena sa-spṛhaṃ punaḥ kāryottara-sādhanāpekṣam ity arthaḥ || 44 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अक्षीणेति । अक्षीणामर्षम् अगत-क्रोधम् अत एवात्यल्पस्य कृत्या वाराणसी-दाहाद् दुःसाध्यस्य साधनेन स-स्पृहं पुनः कार्योत्तर-साधनापेक्षम् इत्य् अर्थः ॥४४ ॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

The discus had unexhausted anger and was eager to accomplish the difficult task of burning Vārāṇasī; having done so, it returned to its further task.
