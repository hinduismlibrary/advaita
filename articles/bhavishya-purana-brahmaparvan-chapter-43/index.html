Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 43 — Birth, Conduct and Human Equality

BP 1.43.1

ब्रह्मोवाच
किं चान्यदपरं यूयं वेदमन्त्रविदो जनाः ।
प्रष्टव्याः कस्य संस्कारे विशेषमुपगच्छत ॥

Brahmā said, What else should you be asked, people who know the Veda and its mantras. Tell me whose rites you consider distinct.

किम् च अन्यत् अपरम् (kim ca anyat aparam) — what further matter;
वेदमन्त्रविदः जनाः (veda-mantra-vidaḥ janāḥ) — people knowing Vedic mantras;
प्रष्टव्याः (praṣṭavyāḥ) — should be asked;
कस्य संस्कारे (kasya saṃskāre) — in whose consecration;
विशेषम् उपगच्छत (viśeṣam upagacchata) — do you perceive distinction

BP 1.43.2

किं देहस्योत येनासौ निसर्गमलिनः स्थितः ।
शुक्रशोणितसम्भूतः शमलोद्भवकीटवत् ॥

What distinction is there in the body, which remains impure by nature, born from semen and blood like a worm arising from filth.

किम् देहस्य (kim dehasya) — is it of the body;
निसर्गमलिनः (nisarga-malinaḥ) — naturally impure;
शुक्रशोणितसम्भूतः (śukra-śoṇita-sambhūtaḥ) — arisen from semen and blood;
शमलोद्भवकीटवत् (śamala-udbhava-kīṭavat) — like a worm born of filth

BP 1.43.3

निषेकादिश्मशानान्तैर्विविधैर्विधिविस्तरैः ।
देहिनोऽतिशयं केचिदुपगच्छन्ति मानवाः ॥

Some people claim that embodied humans attain superiority through elaborate rites of many kinds, from conception to cremation.

निषेकादिश्मशानान्तैः (niṣeka-ādi-śmaśāna-antaiḥ) — from conception through cremation;
विविधैः विधिविस्तरैः (vividhaiḥ vidhi-vistaraiḥ) — by varied elaborate rites;
देहिनः मानवाः (dehinaḥ mānavāḥ) — embodied humans;
अतिशयम् उपगच्छन्ति (atiśayam upagacchanti) — attain excellence

BP 1.43.4

तेषां गूढमनः कायवाग्विदुष्टैः सुचेष्टितैः ।
असंयतमनुष्याणां पक्षोऽयं दूष्यते मया ॥

Despite their outwardly proper acts, their hidden minds, bodies, and speech are corrupt. I reject this position of uncontrolled people.

तेषाम् गूढमनःकायवाक् (teṣām gūḍha-manaḥ-kāya-vāk) — their concealed mind body and speech;
विदुष्टैः (viduṣṭaiḥ) — corrupted;
सुचेष्टितैः (su-ceṣṭitaiḥ) — despite proper gestures;
असंयतमनुष्याणाम् (asaṃyata-manuṣyāṇām) — of uncontrolled people;
पक्षः अयम् दूष्यते मया (pakṣaḥ ayam dūṣyate mayā) — this position is rejected by me

BP 1.43.5

वैदिकाखिलसंस्कारसारभूता द्विजातयः ।
सर्वकार्यकरान्सर्वान्वृषलानतिशेरते ॥

The twice born, who embody the essence of all Vedic rites, surpass all low men who perform every kind of work.

वैदिकाखिलसंस्कारसारभूताः (vaidika-akhila-saṃskāra-sāra-bhūtāḥ) — embodying all Vedic rites;
द्विजातयः (dvijātayaḥ) — twice-born groups;
सर्वकार्यकरान् (sarva-kārya-karān) — performing every work;
वृषलान् अतिशेरते (vṛṣalān atiśerate) — surpass vṛṣalas

BP 1.43.6

चण्डकर्मा विकर्मस्थो ब्रह्महा गुरुतल्पगः ।
स्तेनो गोघ्नः सुरापाणः परस्त्रीरमणप्रियः ॥

One may be cruel in action, engaged in forbidden acts, a killer of Brahmins, a violator of the bed of a teacher, a thief, a killer of cows, a drinker of liquor, and fond of the wife of another man.

चण्डकर्मा (caṇḍa-karmā) — cruel-doing;
विकर्मस्थः (vikarma-sthaḥ) — engaged in forbidden action;
ब्रह्महा (brahma-hā) — brahmin-killer;
गुरुतल्पगः (guru-talpa-gaḥ) — violator of teacher’s bed;
स्तेनः (stenaḥ) — thief;
गोघ्नः (go-ghnaḥ) — cow-killer;
सुरापाणः (surā-pāṇaḥ) — liquor-drinker;
परस्त्रीरमणप्रियः (para-strī-ramaṇa-priyaḥ) — fond of others’ wives

BP 1.43.7

मिथ्यावादी मदोन्मत्तो नास्तिको वेदनिन्दकः ।
ग्रामयाजकनिर्ग्रन्थौ बहुदोषो दुरासदः ॥

A liar, intoxicated with pride, atheist, reviler of the Veda, village priest, unclothed ascetic, full of faults, and difficult to approach.

मिथ्यावादी (mithyā-vādī) — liar;
मदोन्मत्तः (mada-unmattaḥ) — maddened by pride;
नास्तिकः (nāstikaḥ) — atheist;
वेदनिन्दकः (veda-nindakaḥ) — Veda-reviler;
ग्रामयाजकः (grāma-yājakaḥ) — village priest;
निर्ग्रन्थः (nirgranthaḥ) — unclad mendicant;
बहुदोषः (bahu-doṣaḥ) — of many faults;
दुरासदः (durāsadaḥ) — difficult to approach

BP 1.43.8

निषिद्धाचारसंसेवी चोरश्चाटो मदोद्धतः ।
धूर्तो नटः शठः पापी सर्वाशी सर्वविक्रयी ॥

One who follows forbidden conduct, a thief, flatterer, arrogant, rogue, actor, cheat, sinner, eater of everything, and seller of everything.

निषिद्धाचारसंसेवी (niṣiddha-ācāra-saṃsevī) — following prohibited conduct;
चोरः (coraḥ) — thief;
चाटः (cāṭaḥ) — flatterer;
मदोद्धतः (mada-uddhataḥ) — arrogant;
धूर्तः (dhūrtaḥ) — rogue;
नटः (naṭaḥ) — actor;
शठः पापी (śaṭhaḥ pāpī) — deceitful sinner;
सर्वाशी (sarvāśī) — eater of everything;
सर्वविक्रयी (sarva-vikrayī) — seller of everything

BP 1.43.9

वाङ्मनः कायजैर्दुष्टैर्हता ये ब्राह्मणाधमाः ।
ते न शुद्धिं व्रजन्तीह अपि यज्ञशतैरपि ॥

Those lowest Brahmins, ruined by evils born of speech, mind, and body, do not attain purity here even through a hundred sacrifices.

वाङ्मनःकायजैः दुष्टैः (vāṅ-manaḥ-kāya-jaiḥ duṣṭaiḥ) — by corrupt speech mind and body;
हताः ब्राह्मणाधमाः (hatāḥ brāhmaṇa-adhamāḥ) — ruined lowest brāhmaṇas;
न शुद्धिम् व्रजन्ति (na śuddhim vrajanti) — do not attain purity;
यज्ञशतैः अपि (yajña-śataiḥ api) — even by a hundred sacrifices

BP 1.43.10

शूद्राणां यान्यनिष्टानि सम्पद्यन्ते स्वभावतः ।
विप्राणामपि तान्येव निर्विघ्नानि भवन्ति न ॥

The same misfortunes that naturally befall Shudras also befall Brahmins. They are not free from them.

शूद्राणाम् यानि अनिष्टानि (śūdrāṇām yāni aniṣṭāni) — misfortunes of śūdras;
स्वभावतः सम्पद्यन्ते (svabhāvataḥ sampadyante) — arise naturally;
विप्राणाम् अपि तानि एव (viprāṇām api tāni eva) — the same also for brāhmaṇas;
निर्विघ्नानि न भवन्ति (nirvighnāni na bhavanti) — do not become harmless

BP 1.43.11

तस्मान्मन्त्रोग्निहोत्रं वा वेद्यां पशुवधोऽपि वा ।
हेतवो न हि विप्रत्वे शूद्रैः शक्या क्रिया यथा ॥

Therefore mantras, the fire offering, or even the killing of an animal at the altar are not causes of Brahminhood, since Shudras can perform such rites.

तस्मात् मन्त्रः अग्निहोत्रम् वा (tasmāt mantraḥ agnihotram vā) — therefore mantra or fire-sacrifice;
वेद्याम् पशुवधः अपि (vedyām paśu-vadhaḥ api) — even animal slaughter at altar;
हेतवः न हि विप्रत्वे (hetavaḥ na hi vipratve) — are not causes of brāhmaṇa status;
शूद्रैः शक्या क्रिया यथा (śūdraiḥ śakyā kriyā yathā) — since the rite is possible for śūdras

BP 1.43.12

ये चापि कर्मबन्धेन बद्धाः सीदन्ति जन्तवः ।
संसारानलसन्तापविक्लवीकृतमानसाः ॥

Creatures bound by the bonds of action languish, their minds distressed by the burning heat of the cycle of existence.

कर्मबन्धेन बद्धाः (karma-bandhena baddhāḥ) — bound by action;
सीदन्ति जन्तवः (sīdanti jantavaḥ) — creatures languish;
संसारानलसन्ताप (saṃsāra-anala-santāpa) — heat of saṃsāra’s fire;
विक्लवीकृतमानसाः (viklavī-kṛta-mānasāḥ) — minds made distressed

BP 1.43.13

ते जन्ममरणाटव्यां सुखामृतपिपासवः ।
कृपणस्याश्रयेऽटन्तो लभन्ते नैव निर्वृतिम् ॥

Thirsting for the nectar of happiness in the wilderness of birth and death, wandering in a wretched refuge, they never attain peace.

जन्ममरणाटव्याम् (janma-maraṇa-aṭavyām) — in wilderness of birth and death;
सुखामृतपिपासवः (sukha-amṛta-pipāsavaḥ) — thirsting for happiness-nectar;
कृपणस्य आश्रये अटन्तः (kṛpaṇasya āśraye aṭantaḥ) — wandering under wretched refuge;
न एव निर्वृतिम् लभन्ते (na eva nirvṛtim labhante) — never obtain peace

BP 1.43.14

चतुर्वर्णा नरा ये तु तत्तद्वीर्ये नराधमाः ।
तेषां सर्वात्मना सर्वैर्धर्मैः साङ्कर्यमीक्ष्यते ॥

Among men of the four varnas, those lowest in their respective qualities are seen to have a mixture of all duties in every respect.

चतुर्वर्णाः नराः (catur-varṇāḥ narāḥ) — people of four varṇas;
तत्तद्वीर्ये नराधमाः (tat-tad-vīrye nara-adhamāḥ) — lowest men with respective powers;
सर्वात्मना (sarvātmanā) — in every respect;
सर्वैः धर्मैः साङ्कर्यम् (sarvaiḥ dharmaiḥ sāṅkaryam) — mingling of all qualities and duties;
ईक्ष्यते (īkṣyate) — is seen

BP 1.43.15

शूद्रविप्रादयो योनौ न भिद्यन्ते परस्परम् ।
सर्वधर्मसमानत्वात्संस्कारादि निरर्थकम् ॥

Shudras, Brahmins, and others do not differ from one another in birth. Since their qualities and duties are alike, rites and the like are pointless as distinctions.

शूद्रविप्रादयः (śūdra-vipra-ādayaḥ) — śūdras brāhmaṇas and others;
योनौ न भिद्यन्ते (yonau na bhidyante) — do not differ in birth;
परस्परम् (parasparam) — from one another;
सर्वधर्मसमानत्वात् (sarva-dharma-samānatvāt) — because all qualities are alike;
संस्कारादि निरर्थकम् (saṃskāra-ādi nirarthakam) — rites and such are ineffectual

BP 1.43.16

तदनुष्ठानवैधर्म्यवियोगमरणादिभिः ।
असेव्यसेवनैरन्यैः शूद्रविप्रादयः समाः ॥

Through differences of observance, separation, death, service of those unfit to be served, and other conditions, Shudras and Brahmins are alike.

तदनुष्ठानवैधर्म्य (tad-anuṣṭhāna-vaidharmya) — difference or nonperformance of observance;
वियोगमरणादिभिः (viyoga-maraṇa-ādibhiḥ) — separation death and so forth;
असेव्यसेवनैः अन्यैः (asevya-sevanaiḥ anyaiḥ) — serving what should not be served and other acts;
शूद्रविप्रादयः समाः (śūdra-vipra-ādayaḥ samāḥ) — śūdras brāhmaṇas and others are alike

BP 1.43.17

बुद्ध्या शक्त्या स्वभावेन धर्मैर्जात्यादिभिः श्रिया ।
कर्तव्यैः पुण्यपापाभ्यां शनैः सर्वशरीरगैः ॥

They are alike in intelligence, strength, nature, qualities, birth and the like, prosperity, duties, merit and sin, and conditions that gradually pervade every body.

बुद्ध्या शक्त्या स्वभावेन (buddhyā śaktyā svabhāvena) — in intelligence power and nature;
धर्मैः जात्यादिभिः श्रिया (dharmaiḥ jāti-ādibhiḥ śriyā) — qualities birth and prosperity;
कर्तव्यैः (kartavyaiḥ) — duties;
पुण्यपापाभ्याम् (puṇya-pāpābhyām) — merit and sin;
सर्वशरीरगैः (sarva-śarīra-gaiḥ) — pervading every body

BP 1.43.18

बन्धनै रोधनैर्नानायातनोपायपीडनैः ।
दण्डैरदण्डकरणैर्विषादपरिवेदनैः ॥

They are alike in bonds, restraints, many means of affliction, punishments and freedom from punishment, grief, and lamentation.

बन्धनैः रोधनैः (bandhanaiḥ rodhanaiḥ) — by bonds and confinement;
नानायातनोपायपीडनैः (nānā-yātana-upāya-pīḍanaiḥ) — many instruments of torment;
दण्डैः अदण्डकरणैः (daṇḍaiḥ adaṇḍa-karaṇaiḥ) — punishments and exemptions;
विषादपरिवेदनैः (viṣāda-parivedanaiḥ) — grief and lamentation

BP 1.43.19

सात्त्विकः प्रतिधर्माद्यै राजसैश्चित्रवेष्टितैः ।
तामसैस्तापमोहाद्यैर्दूयमानाः पुनः पुनः ॥

They are afflicted again and again by qualities of goodness, by varied passionate states, and by dullness, torment, delusion, and the like.

सात्त्विकैः प्रतिधर्माद्यैः (sāttvikaiḥ prati-dharma-ādyaiḥ) — by sāttvic conditions;
राजसैः चित्रवेष्टितैः (rājasaiḥ citra-veṣṭitaiḥ) — by manifold rājasika states;
तामसैः तापमोहाद्यैः (tāmasaiḥ tāpa-moha-ādyaiḥ) — by tāmasika heat and delusion;
दूयमानाः पुनः पुनः (dūyamānāḥ punaḥ punaḥ) — repeatedly afflicted

BP 1.43.20

श्लेश्ममारुतपित्ताद्यैर्महाबीभत्सदर्शनैः ।
क्वचिद्वृत्तिनिवृत्तिभ्याममृतानृतहिताहितैः ॥

They are afflicted by phlegm, wind, bile, and other humors, by dreadful appearances, and at times by activity and inactivity, truth and falsehood, benefit and harm.

श्लेष्ममारुतपित्ताद्यैः (śleṣma-māruta-pitta-ādyaiḥ) — by phlegm wind bile and so forth;
महाबीभत्सदर्शनैः (mahā-bībhatsa-darśanaiḥ) — most repulsive to see;
वृत्तिनिवृत्तिभ्याम् (vṛtti-nivṛttibhyām) — by activity and cessation;
अमृतानृतहिताहितैः (amṛta-anṛta-hita-ahitaiḥ) — truth falsehood benefit and harm

BP 1.43.21

अलङ्कारोपयोगेन मन्मथाद्यैर्विचेष्टितैः ।
धनलाभाशयानैकजन्तुसङ्घातपातनैः ॥

They are afflicted by adornment, the actions of desire, hopes of gaining wealth, and the destruction of multitudes of living beings.

अलङ्कारोपयोगेन (alaṅkāra-upayogena) — through adornment;
मन्मथाद्यैः विचेष्टितैः (manmatha-ādyaiḥ viceṣṭitaiḥ) — erotic and other agitations;
धनलाभाशया (dhana-lābha-āśayā) — hope of wealth;
अनेकजन्तुसङ्घातपातनैः (aneka-jantu-saṅghāta-pātanaiḥ) — destruction of many beings

BP 1.43.22

अधिसिद्धिगतिं याति नानाविधमनोरथैः ।
आत्मस्नेहपरद्वेषस्वीकृतद्रवरक्षणैः ॥

They proceed toward attainments through many desires, self love, hatred of others, and the taking and guarding of possessions.

अधिसिद्धिगतिम् याति (adhi-siddhi-gatim yāti) — proceeds toward attainments;
नानाविधमनोरथैः (nānā-vidha-manorathaiḥ) — through many desires;
आत्मस्नेहपरद्वेष (ātma-sneha-para-dveṣa) — self-love and hatred of others;
स्वीकृतद्रवरक्षणैः (svīkṛta-drava-rakṣaṇaiḥ) — appropriation and protection of property

BP 1.43.23

अतिक्षीबत्वसंक्षोभक्षुतक्षामक्षमामयैः ।
यातनोपायपैशुन्यशून्यत्वोपशमैस्तथा ॥

They are afflicted by excessive drunkenness, agitation, hunger, emaciation, weakness, disease, means of torment, slander, emptiness, and appeasement.

अतिक्षीबत्वसंक्षोभ (ati-kṣībatva-saṃkṣobha) — extreme intoxication and agitation;
क्षुतक्षामक्षमामयैः (kṣut-kṣāma-kṣamāmayaiḥ) — hunger emaciation and disease;
यातनोपायपैशुन्य (yātana-upāya-paiśunya) — torment methods and slander;
शून्यत्वोपशमैः (śūnyatva-upaśamaiḥ) — emptiness and pacification

BP 1.43.24

अप्रशस्तैरनुष्ठानैः समीपस्थापदः समाः ।
हिंसकाः प्राणिनः पापवितथालापभाषिणः ॥

They are alike through disreputable practices and dangers near at hand. They are violent toward living beings, sinful, and speakers of falsehood.

अप्रशस्तैः अनुष्ठानैः (apraśastaiḥ anuṣṭhānaiḥ) — by disreputable practices;
समीपस्थापदः समाः (samīpa-stha-āpadaḥ samāḥ) — alike in nearby dangers;
हिंसकाः प्राणिनः (hiṃsakāḥ prāṇinaḥ) — injurers of beings;
पापवितथालापभाषिणः (pāpa-vitatha-ālāpa-bhāṣiṇaḥ) — speakers of sinful false talk

BP 1.43.25

साधूनांभाषकाः स्तेना निर्दयाः पारदारिकाः ।
नीचकर्मसमाचाराः सर्वभक्षाः पिशाचवत् ॥

They speak against good people, steal, show no compassion, commit adultery, follow base conduct, and eat everything like demons.

साधूनाम् भाषकाः (sādhūnām bhāṣakāḥ) — revilers of good people;
स्तेनाः (stenāḥ) — thieves;
निर्दयाः (nirdayāḥ) — merciless;
पारदारिकाः (pāradārikāḥ) — adulterers;
नीचकर्मसमाचाराः (nīca-karma-samācārāḥ) — practising base deeds;
सर्वभक्षाः पिशाचवत् (sarva-bhakṣāḥ piśācavat) — eating everything like piśācas

BP 1.43.26

दुष्कुलीना दुराचारा नृपाणामुपजीविनः ।
विप्रकार्या विकर्मस्था धनिनो दुष्टचेतसः ॥

They are of low birth, ill conducted, dependent on kings, hostile to Brahmin duties, engaged in forbidden actions, wealthy, and corrupt minded.

दुष्कुलीनाः (duṣkulīnāḥ) — low-born;
दुराचाराः (durācārāḥ) — ill-conducted;
नृपाणाम् उपजीविनः (nṛpāṇām upajīvinaḥ) — dependent upon kings;
विप्रकार्याः (vipra-kāryāḥ) — hostile to brāhmaṇa duty;
विकर्मस्थाः (vikarma-sthāḥ) — engaged in wrong acts;
धनिनः दुष्टचेतसः (dhaninaḥ duṣṭa-cetasaḥ) — wealthy and corrupt-minded

BP 1.43.27

लुब्धका हरिणान्हत्वा वासं कृत्वा यथा वने ।
तथा खादन्ति पिशुना बहवश्च क्रियावशात् ॥

As hunters kill deer and make their dwelling in the forest, so many slanderers consume through the force of their actions.

लुब्धकाः हरिणान् हत्वा (lubdhakāḥ hariṇān hatvā) — hunters killing deer;
वासम् कृत्वा वने (vāsam kṛtvā vane) — dwelling in forest;
तथा खादन्ति पिशुनाः (tathā khādanti piśunāḥ) — so malicious people consume;
क्रियावशात् (kriyā-vaśāt) — through force of practice

BP 1.43.28

वेदवादमधीयानाः प्राणिघाताभिशंसिनः ।
पुष्णन्ति कपटैरर्थान्वेदविक्रयिणोऽधमाः ॥

Studying arguments about the Veda while praising the killing of living beings, the lowest sellers of the Veda gain wealth through deceit.

वेदवादम् अधीयानाः (veda-vādam adhīyānāḥ) — studying claims about Veda;
प्राणिघाताभिशंसिनः (prāṇi-ghāta-abhiśaṃsinaḥ) — approving killing of beings;
पुष्णन्ति कपटैः अर्थान् (puṣṇanti kapaṭaiḥ arthān) — accumulate wealth by deceit;
वेदविक्रयिणः अधमाः (veda-vikrayiṇaḥ adhamāḥ) — lowest sellers of Veda

BP 1.43.29

मायिनो मत्सरग्रस्ता लुब्धा मुग्धा मदोद्धताः ।
चाटाः कार्पटिकाः कूराः कदर्याः कलहप्रियाः ॥

Deceitful, consumed by envy, greedy, deluded, arrogant, flattering, hypocritical, cruel, miserly, and fond of quarrels.

मायिनः (māyinaḥ) — deceitful;
मत्सरग्रस्ताः (matsara-grastāḥ) — consumed by envy;
लुब्धाः मुग्धाः (lubdhāḥ mugdhāḥ) — greedy and deluded;
मदोद्धताः (mada-uddhatāḥ) — arrogant;
चाटाः कार्पटिकाः (cāṭāḥ kārpaṭikāḥ) — flatterers and hypocrites;
क्रूराः कदर्याः (krūrāḥ kadaryāḥ) — cruel and miserly;
कलहप्रियाः (kalaha-priyāḥ) — fond of quarrels

BP 1.43.30

वाचाटदुष्टकुलटा अटन्तो भाटकैः सह ।
भण्डमान्या भटाटोपैः संक्रुद्धाः सुविलुण्ठकाः ॥

Garrulous, corrupt, and wanton, wandering with hired men, honored as buffoons, displaying a swagger like a warrior, enraged, and given to plunder.

वाचाटदुष्टकुलटाः (vācāṭa-duṣṭa-kulaṭāḥ) — garrulous corrupt wantons;
अटन्तः भाटकैः सह (aṭantaḥ bhāṭakaiḥ saha) — wandering with hirelings;
भण्डमान्याः (bhaṇḍa-mānyāḥ) — honoured as buffoons;
भटाटोपैः (bhaṭa-āṭopaiḥ) — with warrior swagger;
संक्रुद्धाः सुविलुण्ठकाः (saṃkruddhāḥ su-viluṇṭhakāḥ) — enraged plunderers

BP 1.43.31

पर्यटा भाटका जीवाः कण्ठकश्लोकभाषिणः ।
विक्रीणते ह्यविक्रेयमभक्ष्यद्रव्यभक्षिणः ॥

They are wanderers and hired reciters, speaking verses by heart. They sell what must not be sold and eat what must not be eaten.

पर्यटाः भाटकाः जीवाः (paryaṭāḥ bhāṭakāḥ jīvāḥ) — wandering hirelings;
कण्ठकश्लोकभाषिणः (kaṇṭhaka-śloka-bhāṣiṇaḥ) — reciting memorized verses;
विक्रीणते अविक्रेयम् (vikrīṇate avikreyam) — sell what must not be sold;
अभक्ष्यद्रव्यभक्षिणः (abhakṣya-dravya-bhakṣiṇaḥ) — eat inedible substances

BP 1.43.32

शूद्रकर्मानुतिष्ठन्तो निस्तपास्ते नराधमाः ।
सेवाध्यापनवाणिज्यकृष्याद्यारम्भलम्भिताः ॥
गृह्णन्तः सम्पदो बाहयाद्द्रव्यधान्यधनादिकाः ॥

Performing the work of Shudras and lacking austerity, those lowest men gain wealth by undertaking service, teaching, trade, agriculture, and other work. They receive possessions, grain, wealth, and other goods from outside.

शूद्रकर्म अनुतिष्ठन्तः (śūdra-karma anutiṣṭhantaḥ) — performing śūdra work;
निस्तपाः नराधमाः (nistapāḥ nara-adhamāḥ) — austerity-less lowest men;
सेवाध्यापनवाणिज्यकृष्यादि (sevādhyāpana-vāṇijya-kṛṣi-ādi) — service teaching trade agriculture and such;
आरम्भलम्भिताः (ārambha-lambhitāḥ) — undertaking occupations;
गृह्णन्तः सम्पदः (gṛhṇantaḥ sampadaḥ) — accepting property;
द्रव्यधान्यधनादिकाः (dravya-dhānya-dhana-ādikāḥ) — goods grain wealth and so forth

BP 1.43.33

क्रोधादाभ्यन्तरान्दोषांस्तथा दुष्टमनोरथान् ।
अत्यजन्तो विशिष्टानां श्रेष्ठास्ते कचमर्दिनः ॥

Without abandoning inner faults such as anger and evil desires, they claim to be superior and torment distinguished people.

क्रोधात् आभ्यन्तरान् दोषान् (krodhāt ābhyantarān doṣān) — inner faults such as anger;
दुष्टमनोरथान् (duṣṭa-manorathān) — corrupt desires;
अत्यजन्तः (atyajantaḥ) — not abandoning;
विशिष्टानाम् श्रेष्ठाः (viśiṣṭānām śreṣṭhāḥ) — claiming superiority over distinguished;
कचमर्दिनः (kaca-mardinaḥ) — hair-pullers

BP 1.43.34

नोपदेयानि वस्त्राणि नित्यमाददते द्विजाः ।
हापयन्ति न हेयानि कथं ते गुरवः क्षितौ ॥

The twice born continually accept garments that should not be accepted and do not abandon what should be abandoned. How are they teachers on earth.

न उपदेयानि वस्त्राणि (na upadeyāni vastrāṇi) — garments not to be accepted;
नित्यम् आददते द्विजाः (nityam ādadate dvijāḥ) — twice-born continually accept;
न हेयानि हापयन्ति (na heyāni hāpayanti) — do not abandon what should be abandoned;
कथम् ते गुरवः क्षितौ (katham te guravaḥ kṣitau) — how are they teachers on earth

BP 1.43.35

दन्तिका दिण्डिका भण्डाश्चण्डाश्चण्डालचेष्टिता ।
वैतण्डकास्ते निघ्नन्ति यथा सिंहो मृगान्पशून् ॥

Staff bearers, mendicants, buffoons, fierce men with outcaste conduct, and wrangling debaters strike others as a lion strikes deer and beasts.

दन्तिकाः दिण्डिकाः भण्डाः (dantikāḥ diṇḍikāḥ bhaṇḍāḥ) — brawlers impostors buffoons;
चण्डाः चण्डालचेष्टिताः (caṇḍāḥ caṇḍāla-ceṣṭitāḥ) — fierce and acting like caṇḍālas;
वैतण्डिकाः (vaitaṇḍikāḥ) — contentious debaters;
निघ्नन्ति (nighnanti) — strike;
सिंहः मृगान् पशून् यथा (siṃhaḥ mṛgān paśūn yathā) — as lion strikes deer and cattle

BP 1.43.36

निर्ग्रन्थं मुनिमालोक्य मन्यमानाः समुन्नतम् ।
परिभूयावतिष्ठन्ते धिक्तान्रिक्तान्सवैरिणः ॥

Seeing an unclothed sage and considering him exalted, they insult him and stand aloof. Shame on those empty men who are filled with enmity.

निर्ग्रन्थम् मुनिम् आलोक्य (nirgrantham munim ālokya) — seeing an unclad sage;
समुन्नतम् मन्यमानाः (samunnatam manyamānāḥ) — regarding him exalted;
परिभूय अवतिष्ठन्ते (paribhūya avatiṣṭhante) — insult and stand aloof;
धिक् तान् रिक्तान् सवैरिणः (dhik tān riktān savairiṇaḥ) — shame on those empty hostile men

BP 1.43.37

तस्मात्संसारिकाः सत्त्वाश्चित्तक्लेशकलङ्किताः ।
दौःशील्यदौर्मनस्याद्यैस्तुल्यजातीयबन्धनात् ॥

Therefore beings in the cycle of existence are stained by mental afflictions and bound together by common birth, bad conduct, dejection, and the like.

तस्मात् सांसारिकाः सत्त्वाः (tasmāt sāṃsārikāḥ sattvāḥ) — therefore beings in saṃsāra;
चित्तक्लेशकलङ्किताः (citta-kleśa-kalaṅkitāḥ) — stained by mental affliction;
दौःशील्यदौर्मनस्याद्यैः (dauḥśīlya-daurmanasya-ādyaiḥ) — by bad character dejection and such;
तुल्यजातीयबन्धनात् (tulya-jātīya-bandhanāt) — through bondage within one common class

BP 1.43.38

शूद्रां प्ररोचते विप्रो रागिणीं मैथुनं प्रति ।
सा कामदुःखविगमे गर्भं धत्ते समागमे ॥

A Brahmin desires a passionate Shudra woman for sexual union. In their union, she conceives when the distress of desire is relieved.

शूद्राम् प्ररोचते विप्रः (śūdrām prarocate vipraḥ) — a brāhmaṇa desires a śūdra woman;
रागिणीम् मैथुनम् प्रति (rāgiṇīm maithunam prati) — passionate for intercourse;
कामदुःखविगमे (kāma-duḥkha-vigame) — when desire’s distress departs;
गर्भम् धत्ते समागमे (garbham dhatte samāgame) — she conceives in union

BP 1.43.39

कामकामातुराभ्यस्तु रोचन्ते शूद्रमानवाः ।
मैथुनं प्रति ब्राह्मण्ये तेऽपि तासां सुखावहाः ॥

Lustful Shudra men are also pleasing to Brahmin women for sexual union, and they too bring those women pleasure.

कामकामातुराभ्यः (kāma-kāma-āturābhyaḥ) — to lust-afflicted women;
रोचन्ते शूद्रमानवाः (rocante śūdra-mānavāḥ) — śūdra men appeal;
मैथुनम् प्रति ब्राह्मण्ये (maithunam prati brāhmaṇye) — to brāhmaṇa women for intercourse;
ते अपि तासाम् सुखावहाः (te api tāsām sukhāvahāḥ) — they too bring them pleasure

BP 1.43.40

ये तु जात्यादिभिर्भिन्ना गवाश्वोष्ट्मतङ्गजाः ।
ते विजातिषु नो गर्भं कुर्वतेऽपि सुखार्थिनः ॥

Those animals that are distinguished by species, cows, horses, camels, and elephants, do not produce offspring among different species even when seeking pleasure.

जात्यादिभिः भिन्नाः (jāti-ādibhiḥ bhinnāḥ) — distinguished by species;
गवाश्वोष्ट्रमतङ्गजाः (go-aśva-uṣṭra-mataṅgajāḥ) — cows horses camels elephants;
विजातिषु न गर्भम् कुर्वते (vijātiṣu na garbham kurvate) — do not cause pregnancy across species;
सुखार्थिनः अपि (sukhārthinaḥ api) — even seeking pleasure

BP 1.43.41

अनड्वानेव गोरेव कामं पुष्णाति सङ्गमे ।
घोटकाश्च रतिं सम्यक्कुर्वते वडवासु च ॥

A bull satisfies a cow in union, and stallions properly mate with mares.

अनड्वान् एव गोः (anaḍvān eva goḥ) — only bull for cow;
कामम् पुष्णाति सङ्गमे (kāmam puṣṇāti saṅgame) — satisfies desire in union;
घोटकाः (ghoṭakāḥ) — stallions;
रतिम् कुर्वते वडवासु (ratim kurvate vaḍavāsu) — take pleasure with mares

BP 1.43.42

पतिं करभमेवाप्य करभी रमते मुदा ।
गजमेव पतिं लब्ध्वा सुखं तिष्ठति हस्तिनी ॥

A female camel rejoices upon obtaining a male camel as mate, and a female elephant lives happily upon obtaining a male elephant.

पतिम् करभम् एव आप्य (patim karabham eva āpya) — obtaining male camel as mate;
करभी रमते मुदा (karabhī ramate mudā) — she-camel delights;
गजम् एव पतिम् लब्ध्वा (gajam eva patim labdhvā) — obtaining elephant bull as mate;
सुखम् तिष्ठति हस्तिनी (sukham tiṣṭhati hastinī) — elephant-cow lives happily

BP 1.43.43

तिर्यग्जातिः स्त्रिया साकं कुर्वाणाऽपि हि मैथुनम् ।
न तस्याः कुरुते गर्भं नरो नापि सुखासिकाम् ॥

Even when a man has intercourse with a female of another species, he causes no pregnancy in her and gives no fitting pleasure.

तिर्यग्जातिः स्त्रिया साकम् (tiryag-jātiḥ striyā sākam) — man with female animal;
कुर्वाणः अपि मैथुनम् (kurvāṇaḥ api maithunam) — even performing intercourse;
न तस्याः कुरुते गर्भम् (na tasyāḥ kurute garbham) — does not cause her pregnancy;
न अपि सुखासिकाम् (na api sukhāsikām) — nor fitting pleasure

BP 1.43.44

तिरश्चा सह कुर्वाणा मैथुनं मनुजाङ्गना ।
नाधत्ते तत्कृतं गर्भं न युक्तं मैथुनं तयोः ॥

A human woman having intercourse with an animal does not conceive a pregnancy from it. Union between the two is not proper.

तिरश्चा सह (tiraścā saha) — with an animal;
मनुजाङ्गना (manuja-aṅganā) — human woman;
कुर्वाणा मैथुनम् (kurvāṇā maithunam) — performing intercourse;
न आधत्ते तत्कृतम् गर्भम् (na ādhatte tat-kṛtam garbham) — does not conceive its offspring;
न युक्तम् मैथुनम् तयोः (na yuktam maithunam tayoḥ) — their union is not fitting

BP 1.43.45

नैवं कश्चिद्विभागोस्ति मैथुने स्त्रीमनुष्ययोः ।
येन संक्षीयते भेदः प्रस्फुटं द्विजशूद्रयोः ॥

There is no division of this kind in intercourse between human men and women by which the clear distinction between Brahmin and Shudra is diminished.

न एवम् कश्चित् विभागः अस्ति (na evam kaścit vibhāgaḥ asti) — no such division exists;
मैथुने स्त्रीमनुष्ययोः (maithune strī-manuṣyayoḥ) — in human male-female intercourse;
येन संक्षीयते भेदः (yena saṃkṣīyate bhedaḥ) — whereby distinction diminishes;
द्विजशूद्रयोः प्रस्फुटम् (dvija-śūdrayoḥ prasphuṭam) — plainly between twice-born and śūdra

BP 1.43.46

वेदपाठच्छलेनायं न क्रियाभिः प्रपद्यते ।
बहुभिर्जडसङ्घातैरविशिष्टे पदेऽहनि ॥

This status is not attained by the pretext of Vedic recitation or by rites, amid the many dull bodies in an undifferentiated state.

वेदपाठच्छलेन (veda-pāṭha-chalena) — by pretext of Vedic recitation;
अयम् न क्रियाभिः प्रपद्यते (ayam na kriyābhiḥ prapadyate) — this is not attained by rites;
बहुभिः जडसङ्घातैः (bahubhiḥ jaḍa-saṅghātaiḥ) — amid many inert aggregates;
अविशिष्टे पदे (aviśiṣṭe pade) — in an undifferentiated condition

BP 1.43.47

देहे देहिनि चामुष्मिन्नशुचावनवस्थिते ।
रागद्वेषादिभिर्दोषैरधिकं परिपीडिते ॥

In this impure, unstable body and embodied being, greatly afflicted by faults such as attachment and hatred,

देहे देहिनि च अमुष्मिन् (dehe dehini ca amuṣmin) — in this body and embodied being;
अशुचौ अनवस्थिते (aśucau anavasthite) — impure and unstable;
रागद्वेषादिभिः दोषैः (rāga-dveṣa-ādibhiḥ doṣaiḥ) — by faults such as attachment and hatred;
अधिकम् परिपीडिते (adhikam paripīḍite) — greatly afflicted

BP 1.43.48

कुलालचक्रवद्भ्रान्तमानसे विषयार्णवे ।
घोरदुःखभयाक्रान्ते समाजेऽनीश्वरात्मनि ॥

with the mind turning like a potters wheel in an ocean of sense objects, overcome by dreadful suffering and fear, in a society without mastery over itself,

कुलालचक्रवत् भ्रान्तमानसे (kulāla-cakravat bhrānta-mānase) — mind whirling like potter’s wheel;
विषयार्णवे (viṣaya-arṇave) — in ocean of sense objects;
घोरदुःखभयाक्रान्ते (ghora-duḥkha-bhaya-ākrānte) — overcome by dreadful suffering and fear;
समाजे अनीश्वरात्मनि (samāje anīśvara-ātmani) — in society without self-mastery

BP 1.43.49

जन्ममृत्युजराशोकानिष्टयोगाग्निपीडिते ।
हीनसत्त्वशरीरादौ न विशेषो विभाव्यते ॥

tormented by birth, death, old age, grief, unwanted conjunction, and the fire of suffering, no distinction is perceived in bodies of diminished vitality.

जन्ममृत्युजराशोक (janma-mṛtyu-jarā-śoka) — birth death old age grief;
अनिष्टयोगाग्निपीडिते (aniṣṭa-yoga-agni-pīḍite) — tormented by unwanted conjunction and fire;
हीनसत्त्वशरीरादौ (hīna-sattva-śarīra-ādau) — in body of diminished vitality;
न विशेषः विभाव्यते (na viśeṣaḥ vibhāvyate) — no distinction is perceived

BP 1.43.50

तस्मान्मनुष्यभेदोऽयं सङ्केतबलनिर्मितः ।
ब्राह्मण्यं ब्राह्मणासङ्गाद्ब्राह्मणी चोपसेवते ॥

Therefore this division among human beings is made by the force of convention. A woman likewise maintains Brahminhood through association with Brahmins.

तस्मात् मनुष्यभेदः अयम् (tasmāt manuṣya-bhedaḥ ayam) — therefore this human division;
सङ्केतबलनिर्मितः (saṅketa-bala-nirmitaḥ) — constructed by convention;
ब्राह्मण्यम् ब्राह्मणासङ्गात् (brāhmaṇyam brāhmaṇa-āsaṅgāt) — brāhmaṇa status through association with brāhmaṇas;
ब्राह्मणी च उपसेवते (brāhmaṇī ca upasevate) — a brāhmaṇa woman likewise observes it

BP 1.43.51

पतिं त्यक्त्वा मुखस्वादलालसैर्मदलालसैः ।
आसेव्यते विटं गत्वा बन्धकी चेटकैरपि ॥

Abandoning her husband, eager for the taste of the mouth and for intoxication, a wanton woman goes to a lover and is attended even by servants.

पतिम् त्यक्त्वा (patim tyaktvā) — abandoning husband;
मुखस्वादलालसैः मदलालसैः (mukha-svāda-lālasaiḥ mada-lālasaiḥ) — eager for sensual taste and passion;
आसेव्यते विटम् गत्वा (āsevyate viṭam gatvā) — goes to and attends a paramour;
बन्धकी चेटकैः अपि (bandhakī ceṭakaiḥ api) — wanton woman even with servants

BP 1.43.52

ब्राह्मण्यात्प्रच्यवन्तेऽन्ये महापातकसेविताः ।
व्यलीककल्पनैवैषा तस्माज्जात्यादिकल्पना ॥

Some fall from Brahminhood by engaging in great sins. Therefore the conception of birth and the like is a false construction.

ब्राह्मण्यात् प्रच्यवन्ते अन्ये (brāhmaṇyāt pracyavante anye) — others fall from brāhmaṇa status;
महापातकसेविताः (mahā-pātaka-sevitāḥ) — committing great sins;
व्यलीककल्पना एव एषा (vyalīka-kalpanā eva eṣā) — this is false construction;
तस्मात् जात्यादिकल्पना (tasmāt jāti-ādi-kalpanā) — therefore birth-status is imagined
