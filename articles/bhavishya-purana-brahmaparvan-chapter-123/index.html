Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 123 — Sūrya’s Radiance and the Divine Weapons

BP 1.123.1

शतानीक उवाच। भूयोऽपि कथयस्वेमां कथां सूर्यसमाश्रिताम्। न तृप्तिमधिगच्छामि शृण्वन्नेतां कथां मुने ॥

Śatānīka said. Tell me again this story centered on the Sun. I do not become satisfied while hearing this story, O sage.

BP 1.123.2

सुमन्तुरुवाच। भास्करस्य कथां पुण्यां सर्वपापप्रणाशिनीम्। वक्ष्यामि कथितां पूर्वं ब्रह्मणा लोककर्तृणा ॥

Sumantu said. I shall tell the sacred story of the Sun, the destroyer of all sins, previously told by Brahmā, the creator of the worlds.

BP 1.123.3

ऋषयः परिपृच्छन्ति ब्रह्मलोके पितामहम्। तापिताः सूर्यकिरणैस्तेजसा सम्प्रमोहिताः ॥

The sages questioned the Grandfather in the world of Brahmā. Burned by the rays of the Sun and bewildered by his radiance, they asked.

BP 1.123.4

ऋषय ऊचुः। कोऽयं दीप्तो महातेजा हवीराशिसमप्रभः। एतद्वेदितुमिच्छामः प्रभावोऽस्य कुतः प्रभो ॥

The sages said. Who is this blazing one of immense brilliance, shining like a mass of sacrificial offerings. We wish to know this, from where comes his power, O lord.

BP 1.123.5

ब्रह्मोवाच। तमोभूतेषु लोकेषु नष्टे स्थावरजङ्गमे। प्रवृत्ते गुणहेतुत्वे पूर्वं बुद्धिरजायत ॥

Brahmā said. When the worlds had become darkness and all moving and unmoving beings had vanished, and the activity of the qualities had begun, understanding first arose.

BP 1.123.6

अहंकारस्ततो जातो महाभूतप्रवर्तकः। वाय्वग्निरापः खं भूमिस्ततस्त्वण्डमजायत ॥

Then ego arose, the source of the great elements. From it came wind, fire, water, space, and earth. Thereafter the cosmic egg arose.

BP 1.123.7

तस्मिन्नण्ड इमे लोकाः सप्त वै संप्रतिष्ठिताः। पृथ्वी च सप्तभिर्द्वीपैः समुद्रैश्चापि सप्तभिः ॥

In that egg these seven worlds were established, together with the earth, its seven islands, and its seven oceans.

BP 1.123.8

तत्रैवावस्थितो ह्यासमहं विष्णुर्महेश्वरः। प्रमूढास्तमसा सर्वे प्रध्याता ईश्वरं परम् ॥

There I, Viṣṇu, and Maheśvara were present. All, bewildered by darkness, meditated upon the Supreme Lord.

BP 1.123.9

ततो भिद्य महातेजः प्रादुर्भूतं तमोनुदम्। ध्यानयोगेन चास्माभिर्विज्ञातं सवितुस्तथा ॥

Then the great radiance broke forth, dispelling darkness. Through the discipline of meditation we recognized it as the Sun.

BP 1.123.10

ज्ञात्वा च परमात्मानं सर्व एव पृथक्पृथक्। दिव्याभिः स्तुतिभिर्देवं संस्तोतुमुपचक्रमुः ॥

Having known the Supreme Self, each of us began to praise the deity with divine hymns.

BP 1.123.11

आदिदेवोऽसि देवानामीश्वराणां त्वमीश्वरः। आदिकर्तासि भूतानां देवदेव सनातन ॥

You are the first deity of the gods and the Lord of lords. You are the first creator of beings, O eternal God of gods.

BP 1.123.12

जीवनं सर्वसत्त्वानां देवगन्धर्वरक्षसाम्। मुनिकिन्नरसिद्धानां तथैवोरगपक्षिणाम् ॥

You are the life of all beings, gods, celestial musicians, demons, sages, celestial beings, perfected beings, serpents, and birds.

BP 1.123.13

त्वं ब्रह्मा त्वं महादेवस्त्वं विष्णुस्त्वं प्रजापतिः। वायुरिन्द्रश्च सोमश्च विवस्वान्वरुणस्तथा ॥

You are Brahmā, Mahādeva, Viṣṇu, and Prajāpati. You are Vāyu, Indra, the Moon, the Sun, and Varuṇa.

BP 1.123.14

त्वं कालः सृष्टिकर्ता च हर्ता त्राता प्रभुस्तथा। सरितः सागराः शैला विद्युदिन्द्रधनूंषि च। प्रलयः प्रभवश्चैव व्यक्ताव्यक्तः सनातनः ॥

You are Time, the creator, destroyer, protector, and Lord. You are rivers, oceans, mountains, lightning, and rainbows. You are dissolution and origin, the eternal manifest and unmanifest.

BP 1.123.15

ईश्वरात्परतो विद्या विद्यायाः परतः शिवः। शिवात्परतरो देवस्त्वमेव परमेश्वर ॥

Beyond Īśvara is knowledge, beyond knowledge is Śiva. Beyond Śiva is the deity, you alone are the Supreme Lord.

BP 1.123.16

सर्वतः पाणिपादस्त्वं सर्वतोऽक्षिशिरोमुखः। सहस्रांशुस्त्वं तु देव सहस्रकिरणस्तथा ॥

You have hands and feet everywhere, eyes, heads, and faces everywhere. O deity, you are the thousand rayed one, possessing a thousand beams.

BP 1.123.17

भूरादिभूर्भुवः स्वश्च महर्जनस्तपस्तथा। प्रदीप्तं दीप्तिमन्नित्यं सर्वलोकप्रकाशकम्। दुर्निरीक्ष्यं सुरेन्द्राणां यद्रूपं तस्य ते नमः ॥

You are the Earth, the intermediate world, heaven, the Great World, the World of People, and the World of Austerity. Ever blazing and radiant, illuminating all worlds, we bow to that form of yours which even the lords of the gods cannot behold.

BP 1.123.18

सुरसिद्धगणैर्जुष्टं भृग्वत्रिपुलहादिभिः। शुभं परममव्यग्रं यद्रूपं तस्य ते नमः ॥

We bow to that form of yours which is cherished by hosts of gods and perfected beings, by Bhṛgu, Atri, Pulaha, and the others, auspicious, supreme, and undisturbed.

BP 1.123.19

पञ्चातीतस्थितं तद्वै दशैकादश एव च। अर्धमासमतिक्रम्य स्थितं तत्सूर्यमण्डले। तस्मै रूपाय ते देव प्रणताः सर्वदेवताः ॥

We bow to that form of yours abiding beyond the five, and as the tenth and eleventh, remaining in the solar orb beyond half a month. To that form all the gods bow.

BP 1.123.20

विश्वकृद्विश्वभूतं च विश्वानरसुरार्चितम्। विश्वस्थितमचिन्त्यं च यद्रूपं तस्य ते नमः ॥

We bow to that form of yours which creates the universe, has become all beings, is worshiped by the universal fire and the gods, abides throughout the universe, and is inconceivable.

BP 1.123.21

परं यज्ञात्परं देवात्परं लोकात्परं दिवः। दुरतिक्रमेतियः ख्यातस्तस्मादपि परं परात्। परमात्मेति विख्यातं यद्रूपं तस्य ते नमः ॥

We bow to that form renowned as the Supreme Self, beyond sacrifice, deity, worlds, heaven, and even that which is beyond the beyond.

BP 1.123.22

अविज्ञेयमचिन्त्यं च अध्यात्मगतमव्ययम्। अनादिनिधनं देवं यद्रूपं तस्य ते नमः ॥

We bow to that deity, unknowable, inconceivable, abiding in the inner Self, imperishable, without beginning or end.

BP 1.123.23

नमोनमः कारणकारणाय नमोनमः पापविनाशनाय। नमोनमो वन्दितवन्दनाय नमोनमो रोगविनाशनाय ॥

Homage again and again to the cause of causes, homage to the destroyer of sin, homage to the one honored by those who offer homage, homage to the destroyer of disease.

BP 1.123.24

नमोनमः सर्ववरप्रदाय नमोनमः सर्वबलप्रदाय। नमोनमो ज्ञाननिधे सदैव नमोनमः पञ्चदशात्मकाय ॥

Homage to the giver of every blessing and every strength, homage always to the treasure of knowledge, homage to the one formed of fifteen parts.

BP 1.123.25

स्तुतः स भगवानेवं तेजसां रूपमास्थितः। उवाच वाचं कल्याणीं को वरो वः प्रदीयताम् ॥

Thus praised, the Blessed One took the form of the radiances and spoke auspicious words. What boon should be granted to you.

BP 1.123.26

तवातितेजसा रूपं न कश्चित्सहते विभो। सहनीयं भवत्वेतद्धिताय जगतः प्रभो ॥

The beings said. No one can endure your excessively radiant form, O Lord. May it become bearable for the welfare of the world.

BP 1.123.27

एवमस्त्विति गां उक्त्वा भगवान्सर्वकृत्स्वयम्। लोकानां कार्यसिद्ध्यर्थं घर्मवर्षाहिमप्रदः ॥

Having said, So be it, the Blessed One, maker of all, became the giver of heat, rain, and snow for the accomplishment of the worlds' purposes.

BP 1.123.28

अतः सांख्याश्च योगाश्च ये चान्ये मोक्षकाङ्क्षिणः। ध्यायन्ति ध्यानिनो नित्यं हृदयस्थं दिवाकरम् ॥

Therefore the followers of analysis, practitioners of meditation, and other seekers of liberation constantly meditate upon the Sun abiding in the heart.

BP 1.123.29

सर्वलक्षणहीनोऽपि युक्तो वा सर्वपातकैः। सर्वं तरति वै पापं देवकर्मसमाश्रितः ॥

Even one without every auspicious mark, or one joined to every sin, crosses beyond all sin by taking refuge in the divine act.

BP 1.123.30

अग्निहोत्रं च वेदाश्च यज्ञाश्च बहुदक्षिणाः। भानोर्भक्त्या नमस्कारकलां नार्हन्ति षोडशीम् ॥

Fire offerings, the Vedas, and richly endowed sacrifices do not equal even a sixteenth part of a salutation to the Sun made with devotion.

BP 1.123.31

तीर्थानां परमं तीर्थं मङ्गलानां च मङ्गलम्। पवित्रं च पवित्राणां तं प्रपद्ये दिवाकरम् ॥

I take refuge in the Sun, the highest of sacred fords, the auspiciousness of auspicious things, and the purity of the pure.

BP 1.123.32

ब्रह्माद्यैः संस्तुतं देवैर्ये प्रपद्यन्ति भास्करम्। निर्मुक्ताः किल्बिषैः सर्वैस्ते यान्ति रविमन्दिरम् ॥

Those who take refuge in the Sun, praised by Brahmā and the other gods, are freed from every impurity and go to the abode of the Sun.

BP 1.123.33

उपचर्यादिभिः साध्यो यथा वेदे दिवस्पतिः। लोकानामिह सर्वेषां तथा देवो दिवाकरः ॥

As the lord of day is reached through offerings and other rites in the Veda, so the Sun, the deity of all beings in this world, is reached through worship.

BP 1.123.34

शतानीक उवाच। शरीरलेखनं सूर्ये कथं वै प्रतिपादितम्। देवैः सऋषिभिर्वापि तन्ममाचक्ष्व सुव्रत ॥

Śatānīka said. How was the depiction of the body of the Sun established, by the gods or by the sages. Tell me this, O steadfast one.

BP 1.123.35

सुमन्तुरुवाच। ब्रह्मलोके सुखासीनं ब्रह्माणं ते सुरासुराः। ऋषयः समुपागम्य इदमूचुः समाहिताः ॥

Sumantu said. The gods and demons approached Brahmā, seated happily in the world of Brahmā, and spoke these words with collected minds.

BP 1.123.36

भगवन्देवतापुत्रो य एष दिवि राजते। तेनान्धकारो निकृत्तः सोऽयं जाज्वलितीति हि ॥

Lord, this son of the gods shines in heaven. By him darkness has been cut away, and he blazes forth.

BP 1.123.37

अस्य तेजोभिरखिलं जगत्स्थावरजङ्गमम्। नाशमायाति देवेश यथा क्लिष्टं नदीतटम् ॥

By his radiances the entire moving and unmoving world is brought toward destruction, O Lord of gods, as a riverbank is worn away.

BP 1.123.38

वयं च पीडिता सर्वे तेजसा तस्य मोहिताः। पद्मश्चायं यथा म्लानो योऽयं योनिस्तव प्रभो ॥

We are all afflicted and bewildered by his radiance. This lotus, your source, has become withered.

BP 1.123.39

दिवि भुव्यन्तरिक्षे च शर्म नोपलभामहे। तथा कुरु सुरज्येष्ठ यथा तेजः प्रशाम्यति ॥

In heaven, on earth, and in the atmosphere we find no comfort. Act, O eldest of the gods, so that his radiance may be calmed.

BP 1.123.40

एवमुक्तः स भगवान्पद्मयोनिः प्रजापतिः। उवाच भगवान्ब्रह्मा देवान्विष्णुपुरोगमान् ॥

Thus addressed, the Lord, the lotus born Prajāpati Brahmā, spoke to the gods led by Viṣṇu.

BP 1.123.41

महादेवेन सहिता इन्द्रेण च महात्मना। तमेव शरणं देवं गच्छामः सहिता वयम् ॥

Together with Mahādeva and the mighty Indra, let us all go together for refuge to that very deity.

BP 1.123.42

ततस्ते सहिताः सर्वे ब्रह्मविष्ण्वादयः सुराः। गत्वा ते शरणं सर्वे भास्करं लोकभास्करम् ॥

Then all the gods, Brahmā, Viṣṇu, and the others, went for refuge to the Sun, the illuminator of the worlds.

BP 1.123.43

स्तोतुं प्रचक्रमुः सर्वे भक्तिनम्रा समन्ततः। केशादिदेवताः सर्वा भक्तिभावसमन्विताः ॥

All of them, with devotion and bowed bodies, began to praise him from every side. All the deities, from the hair onward, were filled with devotion.

BP 1.123.44

ब्रह्मविष्ण्वीशा ऊचुः। नमोनमः सुरवर तिग्मतेजसे नमोनमः सुरवर संस्तुताय वै। जडान्धमूकान्बधिरान्सकुष्ठान्सश्वित्रिणोऽन्धांविविधव्रणावृतान्। करोषि तानेव पुनर्नवान्सदा अतो महाकारुणिकाय ते नमः ॥

Brahmā, Viṣṇu, and Īśa said. Homage again and again to the best of the gods, to the one of fierce radiance, praised by the best of the gods. You make the dull, blind, mute, deaf, leprous, white spotted, blind, and those covered with various wounds become renewed again and again. Homage to you, greatly compassionate one.

BP 1.123.45

यदौदरं ज्योतिरतित्वरन्महद्यदल्पतेजो यदपीह चक्षुषाम्। यदत्र यज्ञेष्वपनीतमाहितं तवैव तद्रूपमनेकतः स्थितम् ॥

Whatever great inner light hastens forth, whatever small light is present in the eyes, and whatever is placed and offered in sacrifices, all that is your form, abiding in many ways.

BP 1.123.46

सुरद्विषः सागरतोयवासिनः प्रचण्डपाशासिपरश्वधायुधाः। समुच्छ्रितास्ते भुवि पापचेतसः प्रयान्ति नाशं तव देव दर्शनात् ॥

The enemies of the gods who dwell in ocean waters, armed with terrible nooses, swords, and axes, who rise upon the earth with sinful minds, go to destruction at the sight of you, O Lord.

BP 1.123.47

यतो भवान्तीर्थफलं समस्तं यज्ञेषु नित्यं भगवानवस्थितः। नमो भवन्नत्र विचारणास्ति सदा समः शान्तिकरो नराणाम्। यच्चापि लोके तप उच्यते बुधैस्तत्ते महातेज उशन्ति पण्डिताः ॥

Because you are the whole fruit of pilgrimage and are eternally present in sacrifices, homage to you. There is no question here, you are always equal and bring peace to human beings. Whatever the wise call austerity in the world, learned men call that your great radiance.

BP 1.123.48

स्तुतः स भगवानेवं प्रजापतिमुखैः सुरैः। अवधानं ततश्चक्रे श्रवणाभ्यां महीपते ॥

Thus praised by the gods led by Prajāpati, the Lord then gave attention, listening with both ears, O king.

BP 1.123.49

स्तुवन्ति ते ततो भूयः शिवविष्णुपुरोगमाः। कृत्वा मां पुरतः सर्वे भक्तिनम्राः समन्ततः ॥

Then Śiva, Viṣṇu, and the others praised him again, placing me before them, all bowed in devotion from every side.

BP 1.123.50

नमोनमस्त्रिभुवनभूतिदायिने क्रतुक्रियासत्फलसम्प्रदायिने। नमोनमः प्रतिदिनकर्मसाक्षिणे सहस्रसन्दीधितये नमोनमः ॥

Homage to the giver of prosperity in the three worlds and the giver of the true fruit of sacrificial rites, homage to the witness of daily acts and to the thousand rayed one.

BP 1.123.51

प्रसक्तसप्ताश्वयुजे क्षयाय ध्रुवैकरश्मिग्रथिने नमोनमः। सवालखिल्याप्सरःकिन्नरोरगैः संसिद्धगन्धर्वपिशाचमानुषैः। सयक्षरक्षोगणगुह्यकोत्तमैः स्तुतः सदा देव नमोनमस्ते ॥

Homage to the one yoked to seven horses, to the destroyer, and to the one bound by the single fixed ray. O deity, praised always by Vālakhilya sages, heavenly nymphs, celestial beings, serpents, perfected beings, ghouls, humans, spirits, demons, and foremost hidden spirits, homage to you.

BP 1.123.52

यतो रसान्संक्षिपसे शरीरिणां गभस्तिभिर्हिमजलघर्मनिस्रवैः। जगच्च संशोषयसे सदैव अतोऽसि लोके जगतो विशोषणम् ॥

Because with your rays, through the outpourings of cold, water, and heat, you draw in the fluids of embodied beings and always dry up the world, you are called the drying force of the world.

BP 1.123.53

ब्रह्मोवाच। ज्ञात्वा तेषामभिप्रायमुवाच भगवान्वचः। लब्ध्वानुज्ञां ततः सर्वे सुराः संहृष्टचेतसः ॥

Brahmā said. Knowing their intention, the Lord spoke. Having obtained permission, all the gods, their minds delighted.

BP 1.123.54

त्वष्टारं पूजयामासुर्मनोवाक्कायकर्मभिः। विश्वकर्मा तवादेशात्करोतु तव सौम्यताम् ॥

They worshiped Tvaṣṭṛ with the actions of mind, speech, and body. At your command, may Viśvakarman make your form gentle.

BP 1.123.55

ततस्तु तेजसो राशिं सर्वकर्मविधानवित्। भ्रमिमारोपयामास विश्वकर्मा विभावसुम् ॥

Then Viśvakarman, who knew the proper performance of every act, placed the mass of radiance, the Sun, upon a wheel.

BP 1.123.56

अमृतेनाभिषिक्तस्य तदा सूर्यस्य वै विभोः। तेजसः शातनं चक्रे विश्वकर्मा शनैः शनैः ॥

After the Sun had been consecrated with nectar, Viśvakarman slowly and gradually cut down his radiance, O mighty Lord.

BP 1.123.57

आजानुलिखितश्चासौ सुरासुरपूजितः। नाभ्यनन्दत्ततो देव उल्लेखनमतः परम् ॥

His radiance was marked down to the knees, and he was worshiped by gods and demons. Thereafter the deity did not approve of further cutting.

BP 1.123.58

ततः प्रभृति देवस्य चरणौ नित्यसंवृतौ। तापयन्ग्लापयंश्चैव युक्ततेजोऽभवत्तदा ॥

From that time onward the god's feet were always covered, and, heating and weakening, he then possessed moderated radiance.

BP 1.123.59

यच्चास्य शातितं तेजस्तेन चक्रं विनिर्मितम्। येन विष्णुर्जघानोग्रान्सदा वै दैत्यदानवान् ॥

From the radiance that was cut away his discus was fashioned, by which Viṣṇu always slew the fierce Daityas and demons.

BP 1.123.60

शूलशक्तिगदावज्रशरासनपरश्वधान्। देवतानां ददौ कृत्वा विश्वकर्मा महामतिः ॥

Viśvakarman, the greatly wise, fashioned tridents, spears, clubs, thunderbolts, bows, and axes, and gave them to the gods.

BP 1.123.61

त्रिदेवनिर्मितं स्तोत्रं सन्ध्ययोरुभयोर्जपन्। कुलं पुनाति पुरुषो व्याधिभिर्न च पीड्यते ॥

The man who recites at both twilights this hymn made by the three gods purifies his lineage and is not afflicted by diseases.

BP 1.123.62

प्रजावान्सिद्धकर्मा च जीवेत्साग्रं शरच्छतम्। पुत्रवान्धनवांश्चैव सर्वत्र चापराजितः। हित्वा पुरं भूतमयं गच्छेत्सूर्यमयं पुरम् ॥

He becomes prosperous in children, successful in his undertakings, lives a full hundred autumns, has sons and wealth, is unconquered everywhere, and, leaving the city made of beings, goes to the solar city.

BP 1.123.63

भूयोऽपि तुष्टुवुर्देवास्तथा देवर्षयो रविम्। वाग्भिरित्थमशेषस्य त्रैलोक्यस्य समागताः ॥

Again the gods and divine sages praised the Sun, assembled with words for the whole of the three worlds.

BP 1.123.64

देवा ऊचुः। नमस्ते रविरूपाय सोमरूपाय ते नमः। नमो यजुःस्वरूपायाथर्वायाङ्गिरसे नमः ॥

The gods said. Homage to you in the form of the Sun, homage to you in the form of the Moon. Homage to you in the form of the Yajus, and homage to the Atharvan and Aṅgiras.

BP 1.123.65

ज्ञानैकधामभूताय निर्धूततमसे नमः। शुद्धज्योतिःस्वरूपाय निस्तत्त्वायामलात्मने ॥

Homage to the sole abode of knowledge, to the one who has removed darkness. Homage to the form of pure light, to the one whose self is stainless and beyond categories.

BP 1.123.66

नमोऽखिलजगद्व्याप्तिस्वरूपायात्ममूर्तये। सर्वकारणभूताय निष्ठायै ज्ञानचेतसाम् ॥

Homage to the form that pervades the entire universe and to the embodied Self. Homage to the cause of everything and to the foundation of those whose minds are knowledge.

BP 1.123.67

नमोऽस्तु ज्ञेयरूपाय प्रकाशे लक्षरूपिणे। भास्कराय नमस्तुभ्यं तथा शब्दकृते नमः ॥

Homage to the form of what is to be known, to the light that bears the marks. Homage to the Sun and to the maker of sound.

BP 1.123.68

संसारहेतवे चैव सन्ध्याज्योत्स्नाकृते नमः। त्वं सर्वमेतद्भगवाञ्जगद्वै भ्रमति त्वया ॥

Homage to the cause of worldly existence and to the maker of twilight's radiance. O Blessed One, all this universe moves through you.

BP 1.123.69

भ्रमत्वाविद्धमखिलं ब्रह्माण्डं सचराचरम्। त्वदंशुभिरिदं सर्वं संसृष्टं जायते शुचि ॥

The entire moving and unmoving universe is set in motion by you. By your rays all that is created becomes pure.

BP 1.123.70

क्रियते त्वत्करस्पर्शाज्जलादीनां पवित्रता। होमदानादिको धर्मो नोपकाराय जायते ॥

By the touch of your hand the waters and other things are made pure. Without you, duties such as offering and giving do not become fruitful.

BP 1.123.71

तावद्यावन्न संयोगी जगत्यत्र भवाञ्छुचिः। प्रातर्होमं प्रशस्तं हि उदिते त्वयि जायते ॥

As long as you, the pure one, are not joined to the world here, the morning offering is not declared auspicious. It becomes so when you have risen.

BP 1.123.72

ऋचोऽथ सकला ह्येता यजूंषि त्वं जगत्पते। सकलानि च सामानि तपत्येवं जगत्सदा ॥

O Lord of the world, you are all the Ṛg, all the Yajur, and all the Sāma. Thus you heat the entire world forever.

BP 1.123.73

ऋङ्मयस्त्वं जगन्नाथ त्वमेव च यजुर्मयः। तथा साममयश्चैव ततो नाथ त्रयीमयः ॥

You are made of the Ṛg, O Lord of the world. You alone are made of the Yajur, and likewise of the Sāma. Therefore, O Lord, you are made of the three Vedas.

BP 1.123.74

त्वमेव ब्रह्मणो रूपं परं चापरमेव च। मूर्तोऽमूर्तस्तथा सूक्ष्मः स्थूलरूपतया स्थितः ॥

You alone are the form of Brahman, both higher and lower, embodied and unembodied, subtle and present in gross form.

BP 1.123.75

निमेषकाष्ठादिमयः कालरूपः क्षयात्मकः। प्रसीद स्वेच्छया रूपं स्वतेजोमयमादिश ॥

You are the form of time made of instants and kāṣṭhās, whose nature is decay. Be gracious, and by your own will reveal your form made of your own radiance.

BP 1.123.76

इत्थं संस्तूयमानस्तु देवैर्देवर्षिभिस्तथा। मुमोच स्वं तदा तेजस्तेजसां राशिरव्ययः ॥

Thus praised by the gods and divine sages, the imperishable mass of radiances released its own radiance.

BP 1.123.77

यत्तस्य ऋङ्मयं तेजो भविता तेन मेदिनी। यजुर्मयेनापि दिवं स्वयं साममयो रविः ॥

By his radiance made of the Ṛg the earth shall be, by the Yajur heaven, and the Sun himself is made of the Sāma.

BP 1.123.78

शातितास्तेजसो भागा ये च स्युर्दश पञ्च च। तस्यैव तेन शर्वस्य कृतं शूलं महात्मना ॥

The fifteen portions of radiance that were cut away were used by the great one to make the trident of Śarva.

BP 1.123.79

चक्रं विष्णोर्वसूनां च शंकरस्य च दारुणम्। षण्मुखस्य तथा शक्तिः शिबिका धनदस्य च ॥

Viśvakarman made the discus of Viṣṇu, the terrible weapon of Śaṅkara, the spear of the Six Faced One, and the palanquin of the Wealth Giver.

BP 1.123.80

अन्येषां चासुरारीणां शस्त्राण्युग्राणि यानि वै। यक्षविद्याधराणां च तानि चक्रे स विश्वकृत् ॥

He also made the fierce weapons of the other enemies of the demons, and the weapons of spirits and Vidyādharas.

BP 1.123.81

ततश्च षोडशं भागं बिभर्ति भगवान्रविः। तत्तेजसः पञ्चदश शातिता विश्वकर्मणा ॥

Then the Blessed Sun retained one sixteenth part. Viśvakarman had cut away fifteen parts of his radiance.

BP 1.123.82

ततः सुरूपदृग्भानुरुत्तरानगमत्कुरून्। ददर्श तत्र संज्ञां च वडवारूपधारिणीम् ॥

Then the Sun of beautiful vision went north to the Kurus and saw Saṃjñā there, assuming the form of a mare.

BP 1.123.83

इत्येतन्निखिलं भानोः कथितं मुनिसत्तमाः। शृणुयाद्यो नरो भक्त्या अश्वमेधफलं लभेत् ॥

Thus the entire account of the Sun has been told. Whoever hears it with devotion obtains the fruit of an horse sacrifice sacrifice.
