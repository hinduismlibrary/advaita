Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 151 — Description of Solar Dharma

BP 1.151.1

Sūta uvāca । अथ राजा महातेजाः शतानीको द्विजोत्तमम् । प्रणम्य शिरसा भक्त्या सुमन्तुं वाक्यमब्रवीत् ॥ १ ॥

Sūta said: Then King Śatānīka of great splendor bowed his head in devotion to Sumanthu, best of the Brahmins, and spoke these words.

BP 1.151.2

अहो देवस्य माहात्म्यं भास्करस्यामितौजसः । कीर्तितं भवता मह्यं सर्वपापप्रणाशनम् ॥ २ ॥

Ah, the greatness of the immeasurably radiant god Bhāskara, which you have described to me, destroys all sins.

BP 1.151.3

तस्मान्नार्कसमं देवं लोके पश्यामि सुव्रत । न चाप्यस्य स्थिता विप्र गतिर्लोकेषु विद्यते ॥ ३ ॥

Therefore I see no god equal to the Sun in the world, O steadfast one; O Brahmin, no fixed course of his is found among the worlds.

BP 1.151.4

प्रवर्तते जगद्विप्र सर्गकाले दिवाकरात् । स्थितौ पालयते चापि कल्पान्ते संहरेत्पुनः ॥ ४ ॥

The world proceeds from the Day-maker at the time of creation, O Brahmin; during its continuance he protects it, and at the end of the aeon he dissolves it again.

BP 1.151.5

श्रुत्वैवं देवमाहात्म्यं भास्करस्यामितौजसः । कीर्तितं भवता मह्यमश्वमेधशताद्वरम् ॥ ५ ॥

Hearing the greatness of the immeasurably radiant god Bhāskara, as you have described it to me, is better than a hundred horse sacrifices.

BP 1.151.6

किं तु मे संशयो ब्रह्मन्सुमहान्हृदि वर्तते । केनोपायेन विप्रेन्द्र मुच्यते सम्भवार्णवात् ॥ ६ ॥

But a very great doubt remains in my heart, O Brahmin: by what means, O best of Brahmins, is one released from the ocean of becoming?

BP 1.151.7

दिवाकरप्रसादाद्वै सुप्रसन्नाद्वृषध्वजात् । कथं तुष्येत्सदा देवो धर्मेण कतरेण तु ॥ ७ ॥

By the favor of the Day-maker, when the Bull-bannered One is well pleased, how is the god always pleased, and by which sacred duty?

BP 1.151.8

श्रुता मे बहवो धर्माः श्रुतिस्मृत्युदितास्तथा । वैष्णवाः शैवधर्माश्च तथा पौराणिकाः श्रुताः ॥ ८ ॥

I have heard many sacred duties declared in revelation and tradition, the Vaiṣṇava and Śaiva duties, and the Purāṇic duties as well.

BP 1.151.9

श्रोतुकामो ह्यहं विप्र सौरं धर्ममनौपमम् । भगवन्सर्वधन्यास्ते सौरधर्मपरायणाः ॥ ९ ॥

I wish to hear, O Brahmin, the incomparable duties of Sun-worship. O Blessed One, those devoted to Sun-worship are wholly blessed.

BP 1.151.10

ब्रूहि मे देवदेवस्य भानोर्धर्ममनौपमम् । शृण्वतो नास्ति मे तृप्तिरमृतस्यैवमेव च ॥ १० ॥

Tell me the incomparable sacred duty of Bhānu, god of gods. As I listen, I find no satiety, just as with nectar.

BP 1.151.11

अश्वमेधादयो यज्ञा बहुसम्भारविस्तराः । न शक्यास्ते यतः कर्तुमल्पवित्तद्विजातिभिः ॥ ११ ॥

Sacrifices such as the horse sacrifice require many extensive requisites; Brahmins of little wealth cannot perform them.

BP 1.151.12

सुखोपायमतो ब्रूहि धर्मकामार्थसाधकम् । हिताय सर्वमर्त्यानां सर्वपापभयापहम् ॥ १२ ॥

Tell me therefore an easy means that accomplishes duty, desire, and wealth, benefiting all mortals and removing the fear of all sins.

BP 1.151.13

सौरधर्मपरं पुण्यं पवित्रं पापनाशनम् । श्रुत्वा तु वचनं राज्ञो व्यासशिष्यो महामुनिः । प्रणम्य शिरसा व्यासमिदं वचनमब्रवीत् ॥ १३ ॥

Sun-worship is supreme, meritorious, purifying, and destroys sin. Having heard the king’s words, the great sage, Vyāsa’s disciple, bowed his head to Vyāsa and spoke these words.

BP 1.151.14

सुमन्तुरुवाच । श्रूयतामभिधास्यामि सुखोपायं महाफलम् । परमं सर्वधर्माणां सर्वधर्ममनौपमम् ॥ १४ ॥

Sumanthu said: Let it be heard. I shall declare an easy way of great fruit, supreme among all sacred duties, incomparable among them.

BP 1.151.15

रविणा कथितं पूर्वमरुणस्य विशांपते । कृष्णस्य ब्रह्मणो वीर शङ्करस्य न विद्यते ॥ १५ ॥

Ravi told this previously to Aruṇa, O lord of people; it is not found with Kṛṣṇa, Brahmā, or Śaṅkara, O hero.

BP 1.151.16

संसारार्णवमग्नानां सर्वेषां प्राणिनामयम् । सौरधर्मतमः श्रीमान्हिताय जगतोदितः ॥ १६ ॥

This glorious, highest Sun-worship has arisen for the welfare of the world and of all beings drowned in the ocean of worldly existence.

BP 1.151.17

यैरयं शान्तहृदयैः सूर्यभक्तैर्भगार्थिभिः । संसेव्यते परो धर्मस्ते सौरा नात्र संशयः ॥ १७ ॥

Those calm-hearted Sun-devotees seeking good fortune who practice this highest duty are followers of the Sun; there is no doubt.

BP 1.151.18

एककालं द्विकालं वा त्रिकालं नित्यमेव च । ये स्मरन्ति रविं भक्त्या सकृदेवापि भारत । सर्वपापैर्विमुच्यन्ते सप्तजन्मकृतैरपि ॥ १८ ॥

Those who remember Ravi with devotion once, twice, thrice, or continually are freed from all sins, even those done in seven births, O Bharata.

BP 1.151.19

स्तुवन्ति ये सदा भानुं न ते प्रकृतिमानुषाः । स्वर्गलोकात्परिभ्रष्टास्ते ज्ञेया भास्करा भुवि ॥ १९ ॥

Those who always praise Bhānu are not ordinary mortals by nature; having fallen from the heavenly world, they are known as Bhāskaras on earth.

BP 1.151.20

नानर्कः स्मरतेऽर्कं वै नानर्कोऽर्कं समर्चयेत् । नानर्कः कीर्तयेदर्कं नानर्कोऽर्कमवाप्नुयात् ॥ २० ॥

A non-worshipper does not remember the Sun; a non-worshipper does not worship the Sun; a non-worshipper does not praise the Sun; a non-worshipper does not attain the Sun.

BP 1.151.21

सौरधर्मस्य सारोऽयं सूर्यभक्तिः सुनिश्चला । षोडशाङ्गा च सा प्रोक्ता रविणेह दिवौकसाम् ॥ २१ ॥

This unwavering devotion to the Sun is the essence of Sun-worship. Ravi declared it here to have sixteen parts for the dwellers in heaven.

BP 1.151.22

प्रातः स्नानं जपो होमस्तथा देवार्चनं नृप । द्विजानां पूजनं भक्त्या पूजा गोश्वत्थयोस्तथा ॥ २२ ॥

Morning bathing, prayer recitation, fire offering, and worship of the gods, O king; worship of Brahmins with devotion, and worship of the cow and the sacred fig tree as well.

BP 1.151.23

इतिहासपुराणेभ्यो भक्तिश्रद्धापुरस्कृतम् । श्रवणं राजशार्दूल वेदाभ्यासस्तथैव च ॥ २३ ॥

Listening to the epics and Purāṇas, with devotion and faith foremost, O tiger among kings; and likewise studying the Veda.

BP 1.151.24

मद्भक्त्या जनवात्सल्यं पूजायां चानुमोदनम् । स्वयमभ्यर्चयेद्भक्त्या ममाग्रे वाचकं परम् ॥ २४ ॥

Love for people through devotion to me, and rejoicing in worship; one should oneself worship with devotion the excellent reciter who stands before me.

BP 1.151.25

पुस्तकस्य सदा श्रेष्ठ ममातीव प्रियं सुराः । मत्कथाश्रवणं नित्यं स्वरनेत्राङ्गविक्रिया ॥ २५ ॥

The book is always most excellent, exceedingly dear to me, O gods; constant hearing of my stories, and expressive movements of voice, eyes, and limbs.

BP 1.151.26

ममानुस्मरणं नित्यं भक्त्या श्रद्धापुरस्कृतम् । षोडशाड्गा भक्तिरियं यस्मिन्म्लेच्छेऽपि वर्तते । विप्रेन्द्रः स मुनिः श्रीमान्सजात्यः स च पण्डितः ॥ २६ ॥

Constant remembrance of me, with devotion and faith foremost: this devotion has sixteen parts. Even if it dwells in an outsider, he is a glorious sage, foremost among Brahmins, of good birth, and learned.

BP 1.151.27

न मे पृथक्चतुर्वेदा मद्भक्तः श्वपचोऽपि यः । तस्मै देयं ततो ग्राह्यं स च पूज्यो यथा ह्यहम् ॥ २७ ॥

Even a dog-cooker who is my devotee is not separate from the four Vedas. One should give to him and receive from him; he is to be worshipped just as I am.

BP 1.151.28

पत्रं पुष्पं फलं तोयं यो मे भक्त्या प्रयच्छति । तस्याहं न प्रणश्यामि स च मे न प्रणश्यति ॥ २८ ॥

Whoever offers me with devotion a leaf, a flower, a fruit, or water, I am not lost to him, nor is he lost to me.

BP 1.151.29

यो मां सर्वगतं पश्येत्सर्वं च मयि संस्थितम् । तस्याहमास्थितो नित्यं स च नित्यं मयि स्थितः ॥ २९ ॥

Whoever sees me as pervading all and everything as established in me, I always abide in him, and he always abides in me.

BP 1.151.30

अष्टादशार्धकक्षायाः परं चाष्टभिरुद्भवैः । रोधयित्वा महाबाहो तथा ज्ञानतरेण तु ॥ ३० ॥

Going beyond the eighteen-and-a-half enclosures and the eight origins, having restrained them by the boat of knowledge, O mighty-armed,

BP 1.151.31

दुर्गपालं विजित्याशु भास्करार्धं तु दुर्जयम् । जित्वा च पुरराजानां महातेजमनौपमम् ॥ ३१ ॥

and swiftly conquering the fortress-guardian, the unconquerable half of Bhāskara, and the city-kings of great, incomparable splendor,

BP 1.151.32

मनसाचलया भक्त्या यो मां ध्यायति मानवः । अहं तमेव चिंतामि आत्मवत्सततं नरम् ॥ ३२ ॥

whoever meditates on me, a human, with an unwavering mind and devotion, I always regard that very man as myself.
