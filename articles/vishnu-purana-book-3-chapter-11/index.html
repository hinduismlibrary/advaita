VP 3.11.5

ब्राह्मे मुहूर्ते चोत्थाय मनसा मतिमान् नृप । प्रबुद्धश् चिन्तयेद् धर्मम् अर्थं च्प्य् अविरोधिनम् ॥५॥

One should wake up in Braahma muhoorta, and should think at that time, about dharma and artha which are not opposed to each other.

Śrīdhara translation

Śrīdhara glosses garbhādhāna-ādinā puṃsaḥ saṃskṛtasya kṛtim bruvan as “speaking of the deeds of a man consecrated by rites beginning with conception”. He glosses ekādaśe sad-ācārān prāha as “on the eleventh he teaches proper conduct”. He glosses śrī-hari-toṣaṇān as “pleasing to Śrī Hari”. He glosses brāhme muhūrte as “in the Brahma-muhūrta”. He glosses sūryodayāt pūrvam tṛtīye muhūrte as “in the third period before sunrise”. He glosses arthaṃ ca asya dharmasya avirodhinam as “and its meaning not contrary to dharma”.

VP 3.11.6

अपीडया तयोः कामम् उभयोर् अपि चिन्तयेत् । दृष्टादृष्टविनाशाय त्रिवर्गे समदर्शिता ॥६॥

And kaama which is also a purushaartha, which is not opposed to dharma and artha. He should only be thinking about such kaama. All the drushta and adrushta phalaas, those which he can directly see the effects after doing some work, or something which he does not get here, but gets at a later point in time, in order to gain all these fruits, He should dharma, artha, kaama. Artha and kaama should be in line with dharma. He should be equally disposed towards the trivarga. He should not be overdoing some of them.

Śrīdhara translation

Śrīdhara glosses tayoḥ dharma-arthayoḥ dṛṣṭādṛṣṭa-vināśāya as “for preventing visible and invisible harm to dharma and artha”. He glosses dṛṣṭādṛṣṭa-vināśa-nivṛttaye as “for stopping the destruction of what is seen and unseen”. He glosses tādarthye caturthī as “the dative is used in the sense of purpose”. He glosses artha-śabdasya nivṛtter api vācakatvāt as “although the word artha is omitted, the meaning is expressed”. He glosses yadvā vināśam apanētum as “or, to remove the destruction”. He glosses kriyārtha-upapadasya ca karmaṇi sthāninaḥ iti caturthī as “the dative stands for the object of a word denoting purpose”. He glosses dṛṣṭādṛṣṭa-vināśāya iti pāṭhaḥ sugamaḥ as “the reading “for the destruction of the seen and unseen” is straightforward”. He glosses trivarge samadarśitā paraspara-avirodhena bhavatu as “equal regard for the three aims should exist without mutual conflict”. He glosses iti samadṛṣṭiḥ as “this is equal vision”.

यथोक्तदोषहीनेषु सकामेष्वनृतावपि (yathoktadoṣahīneṣu sakāmeṣvanṛtāvapi) — if desire arises, he may approach her even outside the season.
