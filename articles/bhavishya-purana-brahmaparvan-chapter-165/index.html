Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 165 — The Two Seventh-Lunar-Day Observances

BP 1.165.1

सुमंतुरुवाच ।। अहं ते संप्रवक्ष्यामि सूर्यस्य व्रतमुत्तमम् ।। धर्मकामार्थमोक्षाणां प्रतिपादनमुत्तमम् ।। १ ।।

Sumantu said: I shall tell you the excellent vow of the Sun, the excellent exposition of dharma, desire, wealth, and liberation.

BP 1.165.2

पौषमासे तु संप्राप्ते यः कुर्यान्नक्तभोजनम् ।। जितेन्द्रियः सत्यवादी शालिगोधूमगोरसैः ।। २ ।।

When the month of Pauṣa has arrived, whoever observes eating at night should be self-controlled and truthful, taking fine rice, wheat, and cow’s milk.

BP 1.165.3

पक्षयोः सप्तमीं यत्नादुपवासेन यापयेत् ।। त्रिसन्ध्यमर्चयेद्भानुं शाण्डिलेयं च सुव्रत ।। ३ ।।

One should carefully fast on the seventh lunar day in both fortnights; one should worship the Sun at the three twilight periods, and Śāṇḍileya, O keeper of a good vow.

BP 1.165.4

अधःशायी भवेन्नित्यं सर्वभोगविवर्जितः ।। मासि पूर्णे तु सप्तम्यां घृतादिभिररिंदम ।। ४ ।।

He should always sleep on the ground, abstaining from all pleasures. When the month is complete, on the seventh lunar day, with ghee and the like, O subduer of enemies,

BP 1.165.5

कृत्वा स्नानं महापूजां सूर्यदेवस्य भास्कर ।। नैवेद्यं मोदकप्रस्थं क्षीरं सिद्धं निवेदयेत् ।। ५ ।।

after bathing and performing great worship of the Sun, one should offer a measure of sweet dumplings and cooked milk.

BP 1.165.6

भोजयेच्च द्विजानष्टौ भगार्चा शुभलक्षणाम् ।। गां च दत्त्वा महाराज कपिलां भास्कराय तु ।। ६ ।।

One should feed eight Brahmins, perform auspicious worship of Bhaga, and give a tawny cow to the Sun, O great king.

BP 1.165.7

य एवं कुरुते पुण्यं सूर्यस्य व्रतमुत्तमम् ।। तस्य पुण्यफलं वच्मि सर्वकामसमन्वितम् ।। ७ ।।

Whoever thus performs this meritorious, excellent vow of the Sun, I tell the fruit of his merit, accompanied by every desire.

BP 1.165.8

सूर्यकोटिप्रतीकाशैर्विमानैः सार्वकामिकैः ।। अप्सरोगणसङ्कीर्णैर्महाविभवसंयुतैः ।। ८ ।।

In vehicles resembling ten million Suns, granting every desire, filled with hosts of celestial nymphs, and endowed with great splendor,

BP 1.165.9

सङ्गीतनृत्यवाद्याद्यैर्गन्धर्वगणशोभितैः ।। दोधूयमानश्चमरैः स्तूयमानः सुरासुरैः ।। ९ ।।

adorned with hosts of celestial musicians, with singing, dancing, instrumental music, and the like, fanned by fly-whisks, and praised by gods and demons,

BP 1.165.10

सहस्रकिरणाभासः सौरैः सूर्यसमन्वितैः ।। स याति परमं स्थानं यत्रास्ते रविरंशुमान् ।। १० ।।

radiant as a thousand rays, with the solar beings and accompanied by the Sun, he goes to the highest abode where the radiant Sun dwells.

BP 1.165.11

रोमसंख्या तु या तस्यास्तत्प्रसूतिः कुलेषु च ।। तावद्युगसहस्राणि सूरलोके महीयते ।। ११ ।।

For as many as are the hairs upon him and those born in his families, for that many thousand world-ages he is honored in the Sun’s world.

BP 1.165.12

त्रिःसप्तकुलजैः सार्धं भोगान्भुक्त्वा यथेप्सितान् ।। ज्ञानयोगं समासाद्य पुनरेव प्रमुच्यते ।। १२ ।।

Having enjoyed desired pleasures together with his descendants for twenty-one generations, and having attained the yoga of knowledge, he is released again.

BP 1.165.13

योगाद्दुःखान्तमाप्नोति ज्ञानयोगं प्रवर्तते ।। सौरधर्माद्भवेज्ज्ञानं सौरधर्मो भगार्चनात् ।। १३ ।।

Through yoga he reaches the end of sorrow; the yoga of knowledge is set in motion. Knowledge arises from the Sun’s dharma; the Sun’s dharma arises from worship of Bhaga.

BP 1.165.14

इत्येवं ते समाख्यातं भवाम्भोधिव्यपोहने ।। सौरमोक्षक्रमोपायं सूराश्रयनिषेवणम् ।। १४ ।।

Thus I have told you the means for the course of liberation through the Sun, service to the Sun as refuge, for dispelling the ocean of worldly existence.

BP 1.165.15

माघमासे तु संप्राप्ते यः कुर्यान्नक्तभोजनम् ।। पिण्याकं घृतसंयुक्तं भुञ्जानः स जितेन्द्रियः ।। १५ ।।

When the month of Māgha has arrived, whoever eats at night, taking sesame cake mixed with ghee, is self-controlled.

BP 1.165.16

सोपवासश्च सप्तम्यां भवेदुभयपक्षयोः ।। घृताभिषेकमष्टम्यां कुर्याद्भानोर्नराधिप ।। गां च दद्याद्दिनेशाय तरुणीं नीलसंनिभाम् ।। १६ ।।

He should fast on the seventh lunar day in both fortnights and perform a ghee-anointing on the eighth, O lord of men. He should give the Sun a young blue cow.

BP 1.165.17

इन्द्रनीलप्रतीकाशैर्विमानैः शिखिसंयुतैः ।। गत्वादित्यपुरं रम्यं भोगान्भुङ्क्ते यथेप्सितान् ।। १७ ।।

In sapphire-like vehicles accompanied by peacocks, he goes to the lovely city of the Sun and enjoys pleasures as desired.

BP 1.165.18

राजेन्द्र फाल्गुने मासि यः कुर्यान्नक्तभोजनम् ।। श्यामाकक्षीरनीवारैर्जितक्रोधो जितेन्द्रियः ।। १८ ।।

O king of kings, in the month of Phālguna whoever eats at night, taking millet, milk, and wild rice, has subdued anger and restrained the senses.

BP 1.165.19

षष्ठ्यां वाप्यथ सप्तम्यामुपवासपरो नरः ।। अष्टम्यां तु महास्नानं पञ्चगव्यघृतादिभिः ।। १९ ।।

A man intent on fasting on the sixth or seventh lunar day should perform a great bath on the eighth with the five products of the cow, ghee, and the like.

BP 1.165.20

वल्मीकजादिमृद्भिश्च गोमूत्रशकृदादिभिः ।। त्वग्भिश्च क्षीरवृक्षाणां स्नापयित्वा प्रमार्जयेत् ।। २० ।।

With earth from anthills and the like, cow’s urine and dung, and bark from milk-bearing trees, having bathed, one should wipe oneself.

BP 1.165.21

सौरभेयीं ततो दद्याद्रक्ताभां रक्तमालिने ।। पद्मरागप्रतीकाशैर्विमानैर्हस्तिसंयुतैः ।। गत्वादित्यपुरं रम्यं मोदते शाश्वतीः समाः ।। २१ ।।

Then one should give a red cow to the one garlanded in red. In vehicles resembling rubies and accompanied by elephants, one goes to the lovely city of the Sun and delights for everlasting years.

BP 1.165.22

मासि चैत्रे तु संप्राप्ते यः कुर्यान्नक्तभोजनम् ।। शाल्यन्नं पायसैर्युक्तं भुञ्जानश्च जितेन्द्रियः ।। भानवे पाटलां दद्याद्वैष्णवीं तरुणीं नृप ।। २२ ।।

When the month of Caitra has arrived, whoever eats at night, taking fine rice with rice pudding and restraining the senses, should give the Sun a young pink cow belonging to Viṣṇu, O king.

BP 1.165.23

पुष्परागप्रभैर्यानैर्नानाहंसादियायिभिः ।। गच्छेत्सूर्यपुरं रम्यं मोदते शाश्वतीः समाः ।। २३ ।।

In vehicles bright as topaz, drawn by various swans and the like, he goes to the lovely city of the Sun and delights for everlasting years.

BP 1.165.24

वैशाखे वीरमासे तु यः कुर्यान्नक्तभोजनम् ।। सूर्ये खण्डाज्यसंमिश्रं सकृद्दद्यान्निवेदनम् ।। २४ ।।

In the month of Vaiśākha, O hero, whoever eats at night should once make an offering to the Sun mixed with sugar and ghee.

BP 1.165.25

गां च दद्यान्महाराज भास्कराय शुभानन ।। सामान्यं च विधिं कुर्यात्प्रयुक्तो यो मया तव ।। २५ ।।

He should also give a cow to the Sun, O great king, O auspicious-faced one, and perform the general rite that I have taught you.

BP 1.165.26

शुद्धस्फटिकसङ्काशैर्यानैर्बर्हिणवाहनैः ।। अणिमादिगुणैर्युक्तः सूर्यवद्विचरेद्दिवि ।। २६ ।।

In vehicles resembling pure crystal and borne by peacocks, endowed with powers such as becoming minute, he roams in heaven like the Sun.

BP 1.165.27

सम्प्राप्ते श्रावणे मासि यः कुर्यान्नक्तभोजनम् ।। क्षीरषष्टिकभक्तेन सर्वसत्त्वहिते रतः ।। २७ ।।

When the month of Śrāvaṇa has arrived, whoever eats at night, taking milk and sixty-day rice, is devoted to the welfare of all beings.

BP 1.165.28

पीतवर्णां च गां दद्याद्भास्कराय महात्मने ।। सामान्यमखिलं कुर्याद्विधानं यत्प्रकीर्तितम् ।। २८ ।।

He should give a yellow cow to the great-souled Sun and perform the entire general rite that has been proclaimed.

BP 1.165.29

स विचित्रैर्महायानैर्हंससारसगामिभिः ।। गत्वादित्यपुरं श्रीमान्पूर्वोक्तं लभते फलम् ।। २९ ।।

In varied great vehicles moving with swans and cranes, the fortunate one goes to the city of the Sun and obtains the fruit stated before.

BP 1.165.30

वीर भाद्रपदे मासि यः कुर्यान्नक्तभोजनम् ।। हुतशेषहविष्याशी वृक्षमूलमुपाश्रितः ।। ३० ।।

O hero, in the month of Bhādrapada whoever eats at night, taking the food left from the fire offering and dwelling at the foot of a tree,

BP 1.165.31

स्वप्यादायतने रात्रौ सर्वभूतानुकम्पकः ।। दद्याद्गां रोहिणीं श्रेष्ठां भास्कराय महात्मने ।। ३१ ।।

should sleep at night in a temple, compassionate toward all beings, and give an excellent red cow to the great-souled Sun.

BP 1.165.32

निशाकरकरप्रख्यैर्वज्रवैदूर्यसन्निभैः ।। चक्रवाकसमायुक्तैर्विमानैः सार्वकामिकैः ।। ३२ ।।

In vehicles like moonbeams, resembling diamonds and cat’s-eye gems, accompanied by ruddy geese and granting every desire,

BP 1.165.33

गत्वादित्यपुरं रम्यं सुरासुरसुवन्दितम् ।। मोदते स महाभागो यावदाभूतसंप्लवम् ।। ३३ ।।

having gone to the lovely city of the Sun, praised by gods and demons, that very fortunate one delights until the dissolution of all beings.

BP 1.165.34

श्रीमानाश्वयुजे मासि यः कुर्यान्नक्तभोजनम् ।। मिताशनं प्रभुञ्जानो जितक्रोधो जितेन्द्रियः ।। ३४ ।।

In the month of Āśvayuja, the fortunate one who eats at night in moderation, with anger subdued and the senses restrained,

BP 1.165.35

दद्याद्गां पद्मवर्णाभां भानवेऽमिततेजसे ।। दिव्याभरणसम्पन्नां तरुणीं च पयस्विनीम् ।। ३५ ।।

should give the Sun of immeasurable splendor a young, milk-bearing cow whose color resembles a lotus and which is furnished with divine ornaments.

BP 1.165.36

स्वस्तिमौक्तिकसङ्काशैरिन्द्रनीलोपशोभितैः ।। जीवो जीवकसंयुक्तविमानैः सार्वकामिकैः ।। गच्छेद्भानुसलोकत्वं भुञ्जानः स जितेन्द्रियः ।। ३६ ।।

In vehicles resembling auspicious pearls and adorned with sapphires, accompanied by partridges and granting every desire, he goes to the world of the Sun and enjoys, with senses restrained.

BP 1.165.37

दिवाकराय गां दद्याज्ज्वलनार्कसमप्रभाम् ।। पूर्वोक्तं च विधिं कुर्यात्सूर्यतुल्यो भवेन्नरः ।। ३७ ।।

He should give to the Sun a cow shining like fire and the Sun; performing the rite stated before, a man becomes equal to the Sun.

BP 1.165.38

कालानलशिखप्रख्यैर्महायानैर्नगोपमैः ।। महासिंहकृतारोपैः सूर्यवन्मोदते सुखी ।। ३८ ।।

Happy, he delights like the Sun in great vehicles resembling mountains and the flames of the final fire, borne by great lions.

BP 1.165.39

मार्गशीर्षे शुभे मासि यः कुर्यान्नक्तभोजनम् ।। यच्चान्नं पयसा युक्तं भुञ्जानः स जितेन्द्रियः ।। ३९ ।।

In the auspicious month of Mārgaśīrṣa, whoever eats at night, taking food mixed with milk, is self-controlled.

BP 1.165.40

प्रयच्छेद्गां तथा रक्तां नानालङ्कारभूषिताम् ।। सूर्याय कुरुशार्दूल विधिं चापि समाचरेत् ।। ४० ।।

He should give a red cow adorned with various ornaments to the Sun and also perform the rite, O tiger among the Kurus.

BP 1.165.41

सितपद्मनिभैर्यानैः श्वेताश्वरथसंयुतैः ।। गत्वा तत्र पुरे रम्ये प्रभया परयान्वितः ।। ४१ ।।

In vehicles resembling white lotuses and joined with chariots drawn by white horses, he goes to that lovely city, endowed with supreme splendor.

BP 1.165.42

अहिंसासत्यवचनमस्तेयं क्षान्तिरार्जवम् ।। त्रिषवणाग्निहवनं भूशय्या नक्तभोजनम् ।। ४२ ।।

Nonviolence, truthful speech, non-stealing, patience, uprightness, fire offering three times a day, sleeping on the ground, and eating at night;

BP 1.165.43

पक्षयोरुभयोर्मार्गे सप्तम्यां कुरुनन्दन ।। एतान्गुणान्समाश्रित्य कुर्वाणो व्रतमुत्तमम् ।। ४३ ।।

on the seventh lunar day in each of the two fortnights, O delight of the Kurus, one performs the excellent vow by taking up these qualities.

BP 1.165.44

सप्तम्योभयविख्यातं सर्वपापभयापहम् ।। सर्वरोगप्रशमनं सर्वकामफलप्रदम् ।। ४४ ।।

These two seventh-day observances are renowned: they remove all sin and fear, quiet every disease, and grant the fruit of every desire.

BP 1.165.45

इत्येवमादीन्नियमांश्चरेत्सूर्यव्रती सदा ।। य इच्छेद्विपुलं स्थानं भानोरमिततेजसः ।। ४५ ।।

The observer of the Sun’s vow should always practice these and similar observances, if he desires the vast abode of the Sun of immeasurable splendor.
