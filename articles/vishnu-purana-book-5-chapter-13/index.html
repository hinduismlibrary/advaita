VP 5.13.1

गते शक्रे तु गोपालाः कृष्णमक्लिष्टकारिणम् ।
ऊचुः प्रीत्या धृतं दृष्ट्वा तेन गोवर्धनाचलम्

Sri Paraasharar - When Indra returned back, all the gopaas see Krishna and tell Him with great love, having seen the great act of lifting the entire Govardhana giri itself in one hand. They tell Krishna thus -

Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

gate śakre tu gopālāḥ kṛṣṇamakliṣṭakāriṇam | ūcuḥ prītyā dhṛtaṃ dṛṣṭvā tena govardhanācalam

Śrīdhara

gopībhiḥ saha kṛṣṇasya rāsa-kelir mahātmanaḥ | antardhānaṃ punas tasya darśanaṃ cātra varṇyate || 1 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

गोपीभिः सह कृष्णस्य रास-केलिर् महात्मनः ।

अन्तर्धानं पुनस् तस्य दर्शनं चात्र वर्ण्यते ॥१॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

No separate English commentary is supplied.

VP 5.13.6

All the gopaas, gopis, all the children, everyone in Vraja love You so dearly. The whole Vraja is loving You so dearly, without any exception. That also shows that You are not an ordinary human. All the gods put together also cannot do these kinds of wonderful acts like lifting Govardhana and holding in one hand, what You did just now. If all gods collect together, it is impossible to do the wonderful acts You did. So, we are doubting whether You are an ordinary human, who are You?

VP 5.13.16

He was without Balaraama at that time. That which is avyakta, unmanifest, but it was so sweet and unmanifest thing, the song, he sang that song, which is very dear to ladies. This song had Taaraa, Mandra, and all the kramaas combined. It was most wonderful. Taara, Mandra are loud sounds, without opening the lips - it was a mix of both. There is also another paatha - naanaamaatrakrutakramam, and also naanaatantrikutakramam. These mean the sound of the mixture of all the wonderful instruments, whose beats are very slow, or medium paced. The words of that song were not loud enough to be heard by everyone, but they were avyakta, unmanifest. It was so sweet and loving to all the women. Krishna started to sing that kind of a song.

VP 5.13.62

यथा समस्तभूतेषु नभोऽग्निः पृथिवी जलम् ।
वायुश्चात्मा तथैवासौ व्याप्य सर्वमवस्थितः

In all the bhootaas, the elements, aakaasha, agni, pruthivi, jala, vaayu, are there. In the same way, He is pervading everything inside and outside. He is present everywhere. This completes Chapter Thirteen.
