Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 98 — Aparājitā Saptamī

BP 1.98.1

ब्रह्मोवाच। मासि भाद्रपदे शुक्ला सप्तमी या गणाधिप। अपराजितेति विख्याता महापातकनाशिनी ॥

Brahmā said: O lord of the Gaṇas, the bright Saptamī in the month of Bhādrapada is famous as Aparājitā, the destroyer of the great sins.

Word-for-word
brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said;
māsi — in the month;
bhādrapade — of Bhādrapada;
śuklā — bright;
saptamī — Saptamī;
yā — which;
gaṇa-adhipa — O lord of the Gaṇas;
aparājitā iti — as Aparājitā;
vikhyātā — renowned;
mahā-pātaka-nāśinī — destroyer of great sins.
Transliteration
brahmovāca | māsi bhādrapade śuklā saptamī yā gaṇādhipa | aparājiteti vikhyātā mahāpātakanāśinī || 1 ||

BP 1.98.2

चतुर्थ्यामेकभक्तं तु पञ्चम्यां नक्तमादिशेत्। उपवासं तथा षष्ठ्यां सप्तम्यां पारणं स्मृतम् ॥

On the fourth day one meal is prescribed; on the fifth, the observance is at night; likewise there is fasting on the sixth, and the breaking of the fast is remembered for the seventh.

Word-for-word
caturthyām — on the fourth day;
eka-bhaktam — one meal;
tu — indeed;
pañcamyām — on the fifth day;
naktam — at night;
ādiśet — one should prescribe;
upavāsam — fasting;
tathā — likewise;
ṣaṣṭhyām — on the sixth day;
saptamyām — on the seventh day;
pāraṇam — breaking the fast;
smṛtam — is remembered.
Transliteration
caturthyāmekabhaktaṃ tu pañcamyāṃ naktamādiśet | upavāsaṃ tathā ṣaṣṭhyāṃ saptamyāṃ pāraṇaṃ smṛtam || 2 ||

BP 1.98.3

पारणान्यत्र चत्वारि कथितानि मनीषिभिः। पुष्पाणि करवीरस्य तथा रक्तं च चन्दनम् ॥

Here the wise have taught four breakings of the fast. The flowers are oleander, and red sandalwood is used as well.

atra — here;

manīṣibhiḥ — by the wise;
puṣpāṇi — flowers;
karavīrasya — of oleander;
tathā — likewise;
raktam — red;
ca — and;
candanam — sandalwood.
Transliteration
pāraṇānyatra catvāri kathitāni manīṣibhiḥ | puṣpāṇi karavīrasya tathā raktaṃ ca candanam || 3 ||

BP 1.98.4

धूपक्रिया गुग्गुलेन नैवेद्यं गुडपूपकाः। भाद्रपदादिमासेषु विधिरेष प्रकीर्तितः ॥

The incense rite is performed with guggulu, and the offering is guḍapūpaka. This is the procedure prescribed beginning with the month of Bhādrapada.

Word-for-word

guggulena — with guggulu;
naivedyam — offering;
guḍapūpakāḥ — guḍapūpakas;
bhādrapada-ādi-māseṣu — in the months beginning with Bhādrapada;
vidhiḥ — procedure;
eṣaḥ — this;
prakīrtitaḥ — is prescribed.
Transliteration
dhūpakriyā guggulena naivedyaṃ guḍapūpakāḥ | bhādrapadādimāseṣu vidhireṣa prakīrtitaḥ || 4 ||

BP 1.98.5

श्वेतानि भीमपुष्पाणि तथा श्वेतं च चन्दनम्। धूपमाज्यमिहाख्यातं नैवेद्यं पायसं रवेः ॥

The flowers are white, O Bhīma, and white sandalwood as well. Here ghee is declared to be the incense, and payasa is the offering for Ravi.

Word-for-word
śvetāni — white;
bhīma — O Bhīma;
puṣpāṇi — flowers;
tathā — likewise;
śvetam — white;
ca — and;
candanam — sandalwood;

iha — here;
ākhyātam — is declared;
naivedyam — offering;
pāyasam — milk pudding;
raveḥ — for Ravi.
Transliteration
śvetāni bhīmapuṣpāṇi tathā śvetaṃ ca candanam | dhūpamājyamihākhyātaṃ naivedyaṃ pāyasaṃ raveḥ || 5 ||

BP 1.98.6

मार्गशीर्षादिमासेषु विधिरेष प्रकीर्तितः। ततोऽगस्त्यस्य पुष्पाणि कुङ्कुमं च विलेपनम् ॥

This is the procedure prescribed in the months beginning with Mārgaśīrṣa. Thereafter, agastya flowers are used, and saffron is the anointing.

vidhiḥ — procedure;
eṣaḥ — this;

Transliteration
mārgaśīrṣādimāseṣu vidhireṣa prakīrtitaḥ | tato'gastyasya puṣpāṇi kuṅkumaṃ ca vilepanam || 6 ||

BP 1.98.7

धूपार्थं सिह्लकं प्रोक्तमथ वा रविवर्णकम्। शाल्योदनं च नैवेद्यं सरसं फाल्गुनादिषु ॥

Sihlaka, or a substance of the color of Ravi, is prescribed for incense; cooked śāli rice with relish is the offering in the months beginning with Phālguna.

Transliteration
dhūpārthaṃ sihlakaṃ proktamatha vā ravivarṇakam | śālyodanaṃ ca naivedyaṃ sarasaṃ phālgunādiṣu || 7 ||

BP 1.98.8

रक्तोत्पलानि भूतेश सागुरुं चन्दनं तथा। अनन्तो धूप उद्दिष्टो नैवेद्यं खण्डपूपकाः ॥

Red lotuses, O Lord of beings, and sandalwood together with agaru are used. Ananta is prescribed as incense, and khaṇḍapūpaka is the offering.

Word-for-word

dhūpaḥ — incense;

Transliteration

BP 1.98.9

श्रीखण्डं ग्रन्थिसहितमगुरुः सिह्लकं तथा। मुस्ता तथेन्द्रं भूतेश शर्करा गृह्यते त्र्यहम् ॥

Śrīkhaṇḍa with granthi, agaru, and sihlaka, together with musta and indra, are used, O Lord of beings; sugar is taken for three days.

Transliteration

BP 1.98.10

इत्येष धूपोऽनन्तस्तु कथितो देवसत्तम। ज्येष्ठादिमासेषु तथा विधिरुक्तो मनीषिभिः ॥

BP 1.98.11

शृणु नामानि देवस्य प्राशनानि च सुव्रत। सुधांशुरर्यमा चैव सविता त्रिपुरान्तकः ॥

Transliteration

BP 1.98.12

पारणेष्वेव सर्वेषु प्रीयतामिति कीर्तयेत्। गोमूत्रं पञ्चगव्यं तु घृतं चोष्णं पयो दधि ॥

At every breaking of the fast one should recite, “May he be pleased.” The substances are cow urine, pañcagavya, warm ghee, milk, and curd.

Transliteration

BP 1.98.13

यस्त्वेतां सप्तमीं कुर्यादनेन विधिना नरः। अपराजितो भवेत्सोऽसौ सदा शत्रुभिराहवे ॥

The person who performs this Saptamī according to this procedure becomes unconquered, always, in battle with enemies.

Transliteration

BP 1.98.14

जित्वा शत्रुं लभेतापि त्रिवर्गं नात्र संशयः। त्रिवर्गमथ सम्प्राप्य स्वर्भानोः पुरमश्नुते ॥
