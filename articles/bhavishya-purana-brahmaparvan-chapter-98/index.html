Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 98 — The Unconquered Seventh Day Observance

BP 1.98.1

ब्रह्मोवाच। मासि भाद्रपदे शुक्ला सप्तमी या गणाधिप। अपराजितेति विख्याता महापातकनाशिनी ॥

Brahma spoke. In the bright fortnight of Bhādrapada, the seventh day, O lord of the hosts, is known as the Unconquered, the destroyer of great sins.

brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said;
māsi — in the month;
bhādrapade — of Bhādrapada;
śuklā — bright;
saptamī — Saptamī;
yā — which;
gaṇa-adhipa — O lord of the Gaṇas;
aparājitā iti — as Aparājitā;
vikhyātā — renowned;
mahā-pātaka-nāśinī — destroyer of great sins.

BP 1.98.2

चतुर्थ्यामेकभक्तं तु पञ्चम्यां नक्तमादिशेत्। उपवासं तथा षष्ठ्यां सप्तम्यां पारणं स्मृतम् ॥

One meal is prescribed on the fourth day. Eating at night is prescribed on the fifth. Fasting on the sixth and fast breaking on the seventh are remembered.

caturthyām — on the fourth day;
eka-bhaktam — one meal;
tu — indeed;
pañcamyām — on the fifth day;
naktam — at night;
ādiśet — one should prescribe;
upavāsam — fasting;
tathā — likewise;
ṣaṣṭhyām — on the sixth day;
saptamyām — on the seventh day;
pāraṇam — breaking the fast;
smṛtam — is remembered.

BP 1.98.3

पारणान्यत्र चत्वारि कथितानि मनीषिभिः। पुष्पाणि करवीरस्य तथा रक्तं च चन्दनम् ॥

The wise have declared four fast breakings here. Oleander flowers and red sandalwood likewise.

pāraṇāni — breakings of the fast;
atra — here;
catvāri — four;
kathitāni — are taught;
manīṣibhiḥ — by the wise;
puṣpāṇi — flowers;
karavīrasya — of oleander;
tathā — likewise;
raktam — red;
ca — and;
candanam — sandalwood.

BP 1.98.4

धूपक्रिया गुग्गुलेन नैवेद्यं गुडपूपकाः। भाद्रपदादिमासेषु विधिरेष प्रकीर्तितः ॥

The incense rite is with guggul resin resin. Jaggery cakes are the offering. This procedure is proclaimed for the months beginning with Bhādrapada.

dhūpa-kriyā — incense rite;
guggulena — with guggulu;
naivedyam — offering;
guḍapūpakāḥ — guḍapūpakas;
bhādrapada-ādi-māseṣu — in the months beginning with Bhādrapada;
vidhiḥ — procedure;
eṣaḥ — this;
prakīrtitaḥ — is prescribed.

BP 1.98.5

श्वेतानि भीमपुष्पाणि तथा श्वेतं च चन्दनम्। धूपमाज्यमिहाख्यातं नैवेद्यं पायसं रवेः ॥

White Bhīma flowers and white sandalwood likewise. Ghee is declared here as incense. Milk pudding is the offering for the Sun.

śvetāni — white;
bhīma — O Bhīma;
puṣpāṇi — flowers;
tathā — likewise;
śvetam — white;
ca — and;
candanam — sandalwood;
dhūpam — incense;
ājyam — ghee;
iha — here;
ākhyātam — is declared;
naivedyam — offering;
pāyasam — milk pudding;
raveḥ — for Ravi.

BP 1.98.6

मार्गशीर्षादिमासेषु विधिरेष प्रकीर्तितः। ततोऽगस्त्यस्य पुष्पाणि कुङ्कुमं च विलेपनम् ॥

In the months beginning with Mārgaśīrṣa, this procedure is proclaimed. Then come agati flowers, and saffron for anointing.

mārgaśīrṣa-ādi-māseṣu — in the months beginning with Mārgaśīrṣa;
vidhiḥ — procedure;
eṣaḥ — this;
prakīrtitaḥ — is prescribed;
tataḥ — thereafter;
agastyasya — of agastya;
puṣpāṇi — flowers;
kuṅkumam — saffron;
ca — and;
vilepanam — anointing.

BP 1.98.7

धूपार्थं सिह्लकं प्रोक्तमथ वा रविवर्णकम्। शाल्योदनं च नैवेद्यं सरसं फाल्गुनादिषु ॥

Olibanum is prescribed for incense, or a sun colored substance. Cooked fine rice with a savory dish is the offering in the months beginning with Phālguna.

dhūpa-artham — for incense;
sihlakam — sihlaka;
proktam — is prescribed;
atha vā — or;
ravi-varṇakam — of Ravi's color;
śāli-odanam — cooked śāli rice;
ca — and;
naivedyam — offering;
sa-rasam — with relish;
phālguna-ādiṣu — in the months beginning with Phālguna.

BP 1.98.8

रक्तोत्पलानि भूतेश सागुरुं चन्दनं तथा। अनन्तो धूप उद्दिष्टो नैवेद्यं खण्डपूपकाः ॥

Red lotuses, O Lord of Beings, sandalwood together with agarwood likewise. Ananta is prescribed as incense, and sugar cakes are the offering.

rakta-utpalāni — red lotuses;
bhūta-īśa — O Lord of beings;
sa-agarum — together with agaru;
candanam — sandalwood;
tathā — likewise;
anantaḥ — Ananta;
dhūpaḥ — incense;
uddiṣṭaḥ — is prescribed;
naivedyam — offering;
khaṇḍa-pūpakāḥ — khaṇḍapūpakas.

BP 1.98.9

श्रीखण्डं ग्रन्थिसहितमगुरुः सिह्लकं तथा। मुस्ता तथेन्द्रं भूतेश शर्करा गृह्यते त्र्यहम् ॥

Sandalwood paste with a knot, agarwood and olibanum likewise, nutgrass and Indra, O Lord of Beings. Sugar is taken for three days.

śrīkhaṇḍam — śrīkhaṇḍa;
granthi-sahitam — together with granthi;
aguruḥ — agaru;
sihlakam — sihlaka;
tathā — likewise;
mustā — musta;
tathā — and;
indram — indra;
bhūteśa — O Lord of beings;
śarkarā — sugar;
gṛhyate — is taken;
tri-aham — for three days.

BP 1.98.10

इत्येष धूपोऽनन्तस्तु कथितो देवसत्तम। ज्येष्ठादिमासेषु तथा विधिरुक्तो मनीषिभिः ॥

Thus this Ananta incense has been described, O best of the gods. The wise have likewise stated the procedure for the months beginning with Jyeṣṭha.

iti — thus;
eṣaḥ — this;
dhūpaḥ — incense;
anantaḥ — Ananta;
tu — indeed;
kathitaḥ — has been described;
deva-sattama — O best of the gods;
jyeṣṭha-ādi-māseṣu — in the months beginning with Jyeṣṭha;
tathā — likewise;
vidhiḥ — procedure;
uktaḥ — taught;
manīṣibhiḥ — by the wise.

BP 1.98.11

शृणु नामानि देवस्य प्राशनानि च सुव्रत। सुधांशुरर्यमा चैव सविता त्रिपुरान्तकः ॥

Hear the names of the deity and the substances to be eaten, O steadfast one. Sudhāṃśu, Aryaman, Savitṛ, and the destroyer of the Three Cities.

śṛṇu — hear;
nāmāni — names;
devasya — of the deity;
prāśanāni — things to be partaken;
ca — and;
suvrata — O steadfast one;
sudhāṃśuḥ — Sudhāṃśu;
aryamā — Aryaman;
ca eva — and indeed;
savitā — Savitā;
tripurāntakaḥ — Tripurāntaka.

BP 1.98.12

पारणेष्वेव सर्वेषु प्रीयतामिति कीर्तयेत्। गोमूत्रं पञ्चगव्यं तु घृतं चोष्णं पयो दधि ॥

At every fast breaking one should recite, may he be pleased. Cow urine, the five cow products, clarified butter, warm milk, and curd.

pāraṇeṣu — at the breakings of the fast;
eva — indeed;
sarveṣu — all;
prīyatām iti — “may he be pleased,” thus;
kīrtayet — one should recite;
go-mūtram — cow urine;
pañca-gavyam — the five products of the cow;
tu — and;
ghṛtam — ghee;
ca uṣṇam — and warm;
payaḥ — milk;
dadhi — curd.

BP 1.98.13

यस्त्वेतां सप्तमीं कुर्यादनेन विधिना नरः। अपराजितो भवेत्सोऽसौ सदा शत्रुभिराहवे ॥

The man who performs this seventh day observance by this procedure becomes unconquered always in battle against enemies.

yaḥ tu — whoever;
etām — this;
saptamīm — Saptamī;
kuryāt — performs;
anena vidhinā — by this procedure;
naraḥ — person;
aparājitaḥ — unconquered;
bhavet — becomes;
saḥ asau — he indeed;
sadā — always;
śatrubhiḥ — with enemies;
āhave — in battle.

BP 1.98.14

जित्वा शत्रुं लभेतापि त्रिवर्गं नात्र संशयः। त्रिवर्गमथ सम्प्राप्य स्वर्भानोः पुरमश्नुते ॥

Having conquered the enemy, he may obtain the three aims, there is no doubt here. Having attained the three aims, he enjoys the city of Svarbhānu.

jitvā — having conquered;
śatrum — the enemy;
labheta — obtains;
api — also;
tri-vargam — the three aims of life;
na atra saṃśayaḥ — there is no doubt here;
tri-vargam — the three aims;
atha — then;
samprāpya — having attained;
svarbhānoḥ puram — the city of Svarbhānu;
aśnute — enjoys.

BP 1.98.15

ततः पूर्णेषु मासेषु पूजयेच्छक्तितः खगम्। गन्धपुष्पोपहारैस्तु पुराणश्रवणेन च ॥

Then, when the months are complete, one should worship the sky goer according to ability with offerings of fragrance and flowers and by hearing a Purāṇa.

tataḥ — thereafter;
pūrṇeṣu māseṣu — when the months are complete;
pūjayet — should worship;
śaktitaḥ — according to ability;
khagam — the Bird;
gandha-puṣpa-upahāraiḥ — with offerings of fragrances and flowers;
tu — and;
purāṇa-śravaṇena — with hearing of a Purāṇa;
ca — also.

BP 1.98.16

अश्वदानेन च विभोर्ब्राह्मणानां च तर्पणैः। वाचकं पूजयित्वा च भास्करस्य प्रियं सदा ॥

And by giving a horse, O Lord, by satisfying the Brahmins, and by honoring the reciter, one should always do what is dear to Bhāskara.

aśva-dānena — with the gift of a horse;
ca — and;
vibhoḥ — of the Lord;
brāhmaṇānām — of the Brāhmaṇas;
ca — and;
tarpaṇaiḥ — with acts of satisfaction;
vācakam — the reciter;
pūjayitvā — having honored;
ca — and;
bhāskarasya — of Bhāskara;
priyam — the dear act;
sadā — always.

BP 1.98.17

भास्कराय ध्वजान्दद्यान्नानारत्नविभूषितान्। य इत्थं कुरुते वीर सप्तमीं यत्नतः सदा ॥

One should give Bhāskara banners adorned with many jewels. Whoever always carefully performs the seventh day observance in this way, O hero,

bhāskarāya — to Bhāskara;
dhvajān — banners;
dadyāt — should give;
nānā-ratna-vibhūṣitān — adorned with various jewels;
yaḥ — whoever;
ittham — thus;
kurute — performs;
vīra — O hero;
saptamīm — the Saptamī;
yatnataḥ — carefully;
sadā — always.

BP 1.98.18

स पराजित्य वै शत्रुं याति हंससलोकताम् ॥

He, having defeated the enemy, indeed goes to the world shared with the Swan.

saḥ — he;
parājitya — having defeated;
vai — indeed;
śatrum — the enemy;
yāti — goes;
haṃsa-salokataḥ — to the world shared with Haṃsa.

BP 1.98.19

शुक्लाश्वोद्भवयानेन आपगेन पताकिना। आपगाधिपसंकाश आपगानुचरो भवेत् ॥

With a vehicle drawn by white horses, riverlike and bearing a banner, he becomes like the lord of rivers, a follower of the river.

śukla-aśva-udbhava-yānena — by a vehicle born from a white horse;
āpagena — river-like;
patākinā — bearing a banner;
āpagādhipa-saṃkāśaḥ — resembling the lord of rivers;
āpagā-anucaraḥ — attendant of the river;
bhavet — he becomes.
