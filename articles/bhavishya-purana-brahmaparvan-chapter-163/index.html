Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 163 — Flower Worship in Solar Dharma

BP 1.163.1

सुमंतुरुवाच ।। भास्करस्य महाबाहो भानोरमिततेजसः ।। स्नानकाले प्रकुर्वीत जयशब्दादिमंगलम् ।। १ ।।

Sumantu said: O mighty-armed one, when bathing Bhāskara, the immeasurably radiant Sun, one should perform auspicious acts beginning with cries of victory.

BP 1.163.2

पद्मस्वस्तिकशंखं तु श्रीवत्सं द्विजसत्तम ।। हेमरूपादिपात्रेषु कल्पितं गोमयादिभिः ।। २ ।।

O best of Brahmins, form the lotus, svastika, conch, and Śrīvatsa marks in vessels of gold and the like, using cow dung and the like.

BP 1.163.3

नानावर्णकसंयुक्तैरक्षतैस्तिलतंदुलैः ।। स्वच्छैश्च दधिसंमिश्रैर्यथाशोभं प्रपूरितैः ।। ३ ।।

Fill them suitably with pigments of many colors, unbroken rice, sesame, and rice grains, clean and mixed with curds.

BP 1.163.4

द्रव्यपीठप्रदीपाश्च भूताश्वत्थादिपल्लवैः ।। औषधीभिश्च मेध्याभिः सर्वबीजैर्यवादिभिः ।। ४ ।।

Prepare offering stands and lamps, with leaves of bhūta, sacred fig, and the like, with pure medicinal herbs and all seeds, beginning with barley.

BP 1.163.5

सप्तम्यादिषु सर्वेषु षष्ठ्यादिषु विशेषतः ।। शंखभेर्यादिभिः कुर्याद्वाद्यघोषं सुशोभनम् ।। ५ ।।

On all days beginning with the seventh lunar day, especially on those beginning with the sixth, one should make beautiful music with conches, drums, and the like.

BP 1.163.6

त्रिसन्ध्यं वेदनिर्घोषं कुर्वीत फलमुत्तमम् ।। कुर्यान्नीराजनं चैव शंखवादित्रमंगलैः ।। ६ ।।

One should sound the Vedas at the three daily worship times, which brings excellent merit; one should also perform the lamp offering with auspicious conches and instruments.

BP 1.163.7

यावन्नीराजनं कुर्यात्पर्वाणि विधिवद्रवौ ।। तावद्युगसहस्राणि सूरलोके महीयते ।। ७ ।।

For as long as one properly offers the lamp to Ravi on festival days, for that many thousand world-ages one is honored in the Sun’s world.

BP 1.163.8

कपिलापञ्चगव्येन कुशवारियुतेन वै ।। स्नापयेन्मन्त्रपूतेन ब्रह्मस्नानं हि तत्स्मृतम् ।। ८ ।।

With the five products of the tawny cow mixed with sacred grass water, one should bathe him with water purified by mantra; this is remembered as Brahmā’s bath.

BP 1.163.9

यस्त्वेकमपि सर्वेब्दे ब्रह्मस्नानं प्रयच्छति ।। स मुक्तः सर्वपापैस्तु सूर्यलोके महीयते ।। ९ ।।

Whoever performs the Brahmā-bath even once during the whole year is freed from all sins and honored in the Sun’s world.

BP 1.163.10

कपिलापञ्चगव्येन दधिक्षीरयुतेन च ।। स्नानं दशगुणं ज्ञेयं महत्पुण्यं नराधिप ।। १० ।।

A bath with the five products of the tawny cow, together with curds and milk, is known to yield tenfold merit, great merit, O lord of men.

BP 1.163.11

ऋभवो वीरमुद्दिश्य देहशुद्धिं च शाश्वतीम् ।। कपिलामाहरेन्नित्यं मुनिदेवाग्निनिर्मिताम् ।। ११ ।।

For Vīra and lasting purification of the body, the Ṛbhus should daily bring the tawny cow, made by sage, gods, and fire.

BP 1.163.12

कापिलं यः पिबेच्छूद्रो देवकार्यार्थनिर्मितम् ।। स पच्यते महाघोरे सुचिरं नरकार्णवे ।। १२ ।।

A Śūdra who drinks the tawny-cow mixture made for the gods’ work is cooked for a long time in the very dreadful ocean of hell.

BP 1.163.13

वर्षकोटिसहस्रेण यत्पापं समुपार्जितम् ।। घृताभ्यंगेन सूर्यस्य दहेत्सर्वं न संशयः ।। १३ ।।

Whatever sin was amassed over ten billion years is all burned by anointing the Sun with ghee; there is no doubt.

BP 1.163.14

कल्पकोटिसहस्रैस्तु यत्पापं समुपार्जितम् ।। वज्रस्नानेन तत्सर्वं दहत्यग्निरिवेंधनम् ।। १४ ।।

Whatever sin was amassed over ten billion aeons is all burned by the thunderbolt bath, as fire burns fuel.

BP 1.163.15

सप्तम्यां च कृतस्नानो यजेत्सूर्यं सकृन्नरः ।। कुलान्युद्धृत्य सप्तेह सूर्यलोके महीयते ।। वसुहेमादियुक्तं च क्षीरस्नानस्य तत्समम् ।। १५ ।।

A man who bathes on the seventh lunar day and worships the Sun once raises seven families and is honored in the Sun’s world; a milk-bath joined with wealth, gold, and the like is equal to it.

BP 1.163.16

सकृदाढकेन पयसा यो भानुं स्नापयेन्नरः ।। राजतेन विमानेन सोऽर्कलोके महीयते ।। १६ ।।

A man who bathes Bhānu once with a measure of milk is honored in Arka’s world in a silver vehicle.

BP 1.163.17

स्नाप्य दध्ना सकृद्भानुं स त्रिलोके महीयते ।। मधुना स्नपयित्वा तं शुक्रलोके महीयते ।। १७ ।।

Having bathed Bhānu once with curds, he is honored in the three worlds; having bathed him with honey, he is honored in Śukra’s world.

BP 1.163.18

उद्धृत्य शालिपिष्टेन वायुलोकेषु पूज्यते ।। स्नानमिक्षुरसेनेह यः सूर्ये सकृदाचरेत् ।। स गोपतिपुरं गच्छेत्सर्वकामसमन्वितः ।। १८ ।।

Anointing with rice flour, one is honored in Vāyu’s worlds. Whoever bathes the Sun once here with sugarcane juice goes to Gopati’s city, endowed with every desire.

BP 1.163.19

फलोदकेन यो भानुं सकृत्स्नापयते नरः ।। उत्सृज्य पापकलिलं पितृलोके महीयते ।। १९ ।।

A man who bathes Bhānu once with fruit-infused water, abandoning the mire of sin, is honored in the world of the ancestors.

BP 1.163.20

श्रीखंडवारिणा स्नाप्य सकृद्भानुं नराधिप ।। चंद्रांशुनिर्मलः श्रीमांश्चरेदात्रेयमंदिरे ।। २० ।।

Having bathed Bhānu once with sandalwood water, O lord of men, one becomes pure as moonbeams, glorious, and moves in Ātreya’s abode.

BP 1.163.21

वस्त्रपूतेन तोयेन यद्यर्कं स्नापयेत्सकृत् ।। स सर्वकामतृप्तात्मा राकाधिपपुरं व्रजेत् ।। २१ ।।

If one bathes Arka once with water strained through cloth, one, satisfied with every desire, goes to the Moon’s city.

BP 1.163.22

आपो हिष्ठेति जप्येन गंगातोयेन भारत ।। गैरिकेण विमानेन ब्रह्मलोके महीयते ।। २२ ।।

With Ganges water and recitation of the Water-mantra, O Bharata, one is honored in Brahmā’s world in a red-ochre vehicle.

BP 1.163.23

कर्पूरागुरुतोयेन योऽर्कं स्नापयते सकृत् ।। स्नाप्य भानुं सकृन्मन्त्रैः सप्तम्यां समुपोषितः ।। स कुलानेकविंशतिमुत्तार्य रविमाव्रजेत् ।। २३ ।।

Whoever bathes Arka once with camphor-and-agarwood water, and bathes the Sun with mantras on the seventh lunar day after fasting, raises twenty-one families and goes to Ravi.

BP 1.163.24

पितृनुद्दिश्य यो भानुं स्नापयेच्छीतवारिणा ।। तृप्ताः स्वर्गं व्रजन्त्याशु पितरो नरकादपि ।। २४ ।।

Whoever bathes Bhānu with cool water for the ancestors: the satisfied ancestors quickly go to heaven, even from hell.

BP 1.163.25

भानुं शीताम्बुना स्नाप्य धारोष्णपयसा सह ।। स्नाप्य पश्चाद्व्रतेनेशमग्निलोके महीयते ।। २५ ।।

Having bathed Bhānu with cold water and freshly warm milk, O king of men, one then bathes the Lord according to the vow and is honored in Agni’s world.

BP 1.163.26

एतत्स्नानत्रयं कृत्वा पूजयित्वा तु भारत ।। अश्वमेधसहस्रस्य फलं प्राप्नोति मानवः ।। २६ ।।

Having performed these three baths and worship, O Bharata, a man obtains the fruit of a thousand Horse Sacrifices.

BP 1.163.27

मृत्कुम्भात्ताम्रकुम्भैस्तु स्नानं शतगुणं मतम् ।। रौप्यैः पादोत्तरं पुण्यं दर्शनात्स्पर्शनं परम् ।। स्पर्शनादर्चनं श्रेष्ठं घृतस्नानमतः परम् ।। २७ ।।

Compared with an earthen vessel, bathing with copper vessels is held to yield a hundredfold merit; with silver vessels, the merit is a quarter greater. Touching is greater than seeing, worship greater than touching, and the ghee-bath greater still.

BP 1.163.28

इहामुत्र कृतं पापं घृतस्नानेन नश्यति ।। सप्तजन्मकृतं पापं पुराणश्रवणेन तु ।। २८ ।।

Sin committed here and hereafter is destroyed by the ghee-bath; sin committed in seven births is destroyed by listening to the Purāṇa.

BP 1.163.29

दशापराधांस्तोयेन क्षीरेण तु शतं क्षमेत् ।। सहस्रं क्षमते दध्ना घृतेनाप्ययुतं क्षमेत् ।। २९ ।।

Water absolves ten offenses, milk a hundred, curds a thousand, and ghee ten thousand.

BP 1.163.30

नैरन्तर्येण यो मासं घृतस्नानं समाचरेत् ।। दशैकादश कुलानीह नयेत्सूर्यस्य मन्दिरम् ।। ३० ।।

Whoever performs the ghee-bath continuously for a month leads twenty-one generations to the Sun’s temple.

BP 1.163.31

स्नानं पलशतं ज्ञेयमभ्यंगः पञ्चविंशतिः ।। पलानां द्विसहस्रेण महास्नानमिति श्रुतिः ।। ३१ ।।

The bath is to be measured as one hundred palas, the anointing as twenty-five; a bath of two thousand palas is called the great bath.

BP 1.163.32

घृताभ्यंगं घृतस्नानं भानोः कुर्याद्द्विजोत्तम ।। यश्च गोधूमचूर्णैस्तु कषायैर्दर्भसंमितैः ।। ३२ ।।

O best of Brahmins, one should anoint Bhānu with ghee and bathe him with ghee; one should also use wheat flour, herbal decoctions, and sacred grass grass.

BP 1.163.33

दशधेनुसहस्राणि यद्दत्त्वा लभते फलम् ।। तत्फलं लभते सर्वमर्कस्योद्वर्तने कृते ।। ३३ ।।

The fruit gained by giving ten thousand cows is all gained by rubbing Arka with powder.

BP 1.163.34

अर्घ्यं पुष्पफलोपेतं यस्त्वर्काय निवेदयेत् ।। स पूज्यः सर्वलोकेष्वर्कवन्मोदते दिवि ।। ३४ ।।

Whoever offers the Sun a water-offering with flowers and fruits is honored in all worlds and delights in heaven like the Sun.

BP 1.163.35

गन्धतोयेन संमिश्रमुदकाद्द्वादशोत्तरम् ।। पञ्चगव्यसमायुक्तमर्घ्यं शतगुणं नृप ।। ३५ ।।

A water-offering mixed with fragrant water yields twelve times the merit of plain water; joined with the five products of the cow, it yields a hundredfold, O king.

BP 1.163.36

योऽष्टाङ्गगर्भमापूर्य भानोर्मूर्ध्नि निवेदयेत् ।। दशवर्षसहस्राणि रमते चार्कमन्दिरे ।। ३६ ।।

Whoever fills it with the eight ingredients and offers it on Bhānu’s head delights for ten thousand years in Arka’s temple.

BP 1.163.37

आपः क्षीरं कुशाग्राणि घृतं दधि तथा मधु ।। रक्तानि करवीराणि तथा रक्तं च चन्दनम् ।। ३७ ।।

Water, milk, tips of sacred grass grass, ghee, curds, and honey; red oleanders and red sandalwood.

BP 1.163.38

अष्टाङ्ग एष अर्घो वै ब्रह्मणा परिकीर्तितः ।। सततं प्रीतिजननो भास्करस्य नराधिप ।। ३८ ।।

This is the eight-part water-offering declared by Brahmā, continually bringing delight to Bhāskara, O lord of men.

BP 1.163.39

दातुर्वैणवपात्रेण दत्तेऽर्घ्ये यत्फलं भवेत् ।। तस्माच्छतगुणं पुण्यं मृत्पात्रेण नराधिप ।। ३९ ।।

Whatever fruit comes from offering a water-offering in a bamboo vessel, the merit with an earthen vessel is a hundredfold greater, O lord of men.

BP 1.163.40

ताम्रार्घ्यपात्रदानेन पुण्यं शतगुणं मतम् ।। पालाशपद्मपत्राभ्यां ताम्रपात्रे फलं लभेत् ।। ४० ।।

By giving a copper vessel for the water-offering, merit is held to be a hundredfold; one obtains the fruit by using palāśa and lotus leaves in a copper vessel.

BP 1.163.41

रौप्यपात्रेण विज्ञेयं लक्षार्घ्यं नात्र संशयः ।। सुवर्णपात्रविन्यस्तमर्घ्यं कोटिगुणं भवेत् ।। ४१ ।।

A silver vessel is understood to yield a hundred thousand water-offerings, without doubt; a water-offering set in a golden vessel yields ten millionfold merit.

BP 1.163.42

एवं स्नानार्घ्यनैवेद्यबलिधूपादिषु क्रमात् ।। पात्रान्तरविशेषेण तत्फलं तूत्तरोत्तरम् ।। ४२ ।।

Thus, in bathing, water-offerings, food offerings, gifts, incense, and the rest, the fruit increases successively according to the kind of vessel.

BP 1.163.43

रौप्यपात्रप्रदानेन यत्पुण्यं वेदपारगे ।। ताम्रपात्रप्रदानेन तस्माच्छतगुणं रवौ ।। ४३ ।।

The merit of giving a silver vessel to one versed in the Veda is surpassed a hundredfold by giving a copper vessel to Ravi.

BP 1.163.44

फलं कोटिसुवर्णस्य यो दद्याद्वेदपारगे ।। सूर्याय रूप्यपात्रे तु भवेत्पुण्यं ततोऽधिकम् ।। ४४ ।।

Whoever gives a Veda-knower a gift equal to ten million gold coins gains less merit than by offering the Sun a water-offering in a silver vessel.

BP 1.163.45

सुवर्णपात्रं यो दद्याद्भास्कराय महीपते ।। न शक्यं तस्य तद्वक्तुं पुण्यं पात्रविशेषतः ।। ४५ ।।

Whoever gives a golden vessel to Bhāskara, O king, the merit of that, according to the vessel’s distinction, cannot be told.

BP 1.163.46

तुल्यमेव फलं प्रोक्तं सर्वमाढ्यदरिद्रयोः ।। तयोरभ्यधिकं तस्य यस्त्वर्के भावनाधिकः ।। ४६ ।।

The fruit is declared equal for everyone, rich or poor; between the two, it is greater for the one whose devotion to Arka is greater.

BP 1.163.47

विभवे सति यो मोहान्न कुर्याद्विधिविस्तरम् ।। नैव तत्फलमाप्नोति प्रलोभाक्रान्तमानसः ।। ४७ ।।

When he has the means, whoever through delusion does not perform the full rite, his mind seized by greed, does not obtain that fruit.

BP 1.163.48

तस्मान्मन्त्रैः फलैस्तोयैश्चन्दनाद्यैश्च यत्नतः ।। तदनन्तफलं ज्ञेयं भक्तिरेवात्र कारणम् ।। ४८ ।।

Therefore one should carefully use mantras, fruits, water, sandalwood, and the like; the fruit is to be known as endless, for devotion alone is the cause here.

BP 1.163.49

वर्षकोटिशतं दिव्यं सूर्यलोके महीयते ।। गन्धानुलेपनं पुण्यं द्विगुणं चन्दनस्य तु ।। ४९ ।।

For one billion divine years one is honored in the Sun’s world. Anointing with fragrance is meritorious; sandalwood yields twice as much.

BP 1.163.50

गन्धाच्चतुर्गुणं ज्ञेयं पुष्पमष्टगुणं नृप ।। कृष्णागुरु विशेषेण द्विगुणं फलमादिशेत् ।। तस्माच्छतगुणं पुण्यं कुङ्कुमस्य विधीयते ।। ५० ।।

Fragrance is fourfold, flowers eightfold, O king; black agarwood is declared especially to yield twice the fruit, and saffron’s merit is prescribed as a hundredfold of that.

BP 1.163.51

चन्दनागुरुकर्पूरैः श्लक्ष्णपिष्टैः सकुङ्कुमैः ।। भानुं पर्याप्तमालिप्य कल्पकोटिं वसेद्दिवि ।। ५१ ।।

Having thoroughly anointed Bhānu with finely ground sandalwood, agarwood, camphor, and saffron, one dwells in heaven for ten million aeons.

BP 1.163.52

स दीव्येत्सुरवृन्देन पुण्यगन्धैः प्रलेपितः ।। दशवर्षसहस्राणि वीर मित्रपुरे वसेत् ।। ५२ ।।

Anointed with meritorious fragrances, he shines with the assembly of gods; O hero, he dwells in Mitra’s city for ten thousand years.

BP 1.163.53

भक्त्या निवेद्य अर्काय तालवृन्तं नराधिप ।। दशवर्षसहस्राणि वीरलोकं महीयते ।। ५३ ।।

Having offered a palm-leaf fan to Arka with devotion, O lord of men, one is honored in the world of heroes for ten thousand years.

BP 1.163.54

मायूरं व्यजनं दत्त्वा सूर्यायातिविशोभनम् ।। वर्षकोटिशतं पूर्णं प्रभञ्जनपुरे वसेत् ।। ५४ ।।

Having given the Sun a very beautiful peacock fan, one dwells in Prabhañjana’s city for one billion years.

BP 1.163.55

पुष्पैररण्यसम्भूतैः पत्रैर्वा गिरिसम्भवैः ।। अपर्युषितनिश्छिद्रैः प्रोषितैर्जन्तुवर्जितैः ।। ५५ ।।

Use flowers born in the forest or leaves grown on mountains, fresh, unbroken, recently gathered, and free of living creatures.

BP 1.163.56

आत्मारामभवैश्चैव पुष्पैः सम्पूजयेद्रविम् ।। पुष्पजातिविशेषेण भवेत्पुण्यं ततोऽधिकम् ।। ५६ ।।

One should also worship Ravi with flowers grown in one’s own garden; the merit is greater according to the kind of flower.

BP 1.163.57

तपःशीलगुणोपेत इति हासविदि द्विजे ।। दत्त्वा दश सुवर्णस्य निष्कान्यल्लभते फलम् ।। ५७ ।।

To a Brahmin endowed with austerity, good conduct, and virtues, and learned in sacred histories, one should give ten gold coins; one obtains that fruit.

BP 1.163.58

करवीरस्य कुसुममर्काय विनिवेदयेत् ।। लभते तत्फलं वीर यथाह भगवान्रविः ।। एवं पुष्पविशेषेण फलं तदधिकं भवेत् ।। ज्ञेयं पुण्यं रसज्ञेन यथा स्यात्तन्निबोध मे ।। ५८ ।।

One should offer an oleander flower to Arka. One obtains that fruit, O hero, as the venerable Ravi has declared. Thus, according to the flower’s kind, that fruit is greater; listen as I explain how the wise understand the merit.

BP 1.163.59

सदा पुष्पसहस्रेभ्यः करवीरं विशिष्यते ।। बिल्वपत्रसहस्रेभ्यः पद्ममेकं नराधिप ।। ५९ ।।

Oleander is always superior to a thousand flowers; one lotus is superior to a thousand bael leaves, O lord of men.

BP 1.163.60

पद्मपुष्पसहस्रेभ्यो बकपुष्पं विशिष्यते ।। बकपुष्पसहस्रेभ्यो मुद्गरं परमुच्यते ।। ६० ।।

A baka flower is superior to a thousand lotuses; the mudgara flower is said to be supreme over a thousand baka flowers.

BP 1.163.61

कुशपुष्पसहस्रेभ्यः शमीपत्रं विशिष्यते ।। शमीपुष्पसहस्रेभ्यो नृप नीलोत्पलं परम् ।। सर्वासां पुष्पजातीनां प्रवरं नीलमुत्पलम् ।। ६१ ।।

A śamī leaf is superior to a thousand sacred grass flowers; above a thousand śamī flowers, O king, the blue lotus is supreme. The blue lotus is foremost among all kinds of flowers.

BP 1.163.62

रक्तोत्पलसहस्रेण नीलोत्पलशतेन च ।। रक्तैश्च करवीरैश्च यस्तु पूजयते रविम् ।। ६२ ।।

Whoever worships Ravi with a thousand red lotuses, a hundred blue lotuses, and red oleanders

BP 1.163.63

कल्पकोटिसहस्राणि कल्पकोटिशतानि च ।। वसेदर्कपुरे श्रीमान्सूर्यतुल्यपराक्रमः ।। ६३ ।।

dwells in Arka’s city for eleven billion aeons, glorious and equal to the Sun in might.

BP 1.163.64

शेषाणां पुष्पजातीनां यत्फलं परिकीर्तितम् ।। तत्फलस्यानुसारेण सूर्यलोके महीयते ।। ६४ ।।

In accordance with the fruit declared for the remaining kinds of flowers, one is honored in the Sun’s world.

BP 1.163.65

शमीपुष्पं बृहत्याश्च कुसुमं तुल्यमुच्यते ।। करवीरसमा ज्ञेया जातीविजयपाटलाः ।। ६५ ।।

The śamī flower and the bṛhatī flower are said to be equal; jasmine, vijayā, and pāṭalā are to be known as equal to oleander.

BP 1.163.66

श्वेतमन्दारकुसुमं सितपुष्पं च तत्समम् ।। नलचम्पकपुन्नागमुद्गराणां समाः स्मृताः ।। ६६ ।।

The white mandāra flower and white blossoms, as well as nala, champak, puṇṇāga, and mudgara, are held equal.

BP 1.163.67

गन्धवन्त्यपवित्राणि कुसुमानि विवर्जयेत् ।। गन्धहीनमपि ग्राह्यं पवित्रं यत्कुशादिकम् ।। ६७ ।।

One should reject flowers that are fragrant but impure; even a scentless flower may be accepted if pure, such as sacred grass grass and the like.

BP 1.163.68

सात्त्विकं तद्धि कुसुममपवित्रं च तामसम् ।। मुद्गराणि कदम्बानि रात्रौ देयानि सूरये ।। ६८ ।।

That flower is pure; the impure is tāmasic. Mudgara and kadamba flowers are to be offered to the Sun at night.

BP 1.163.69

दिवाशेषाणि पुष्पाणि त्यजेदुपहतानि च ।। मुकुलैर्मार्चयेद्भानुमपक्वं न निवेदयेत् ।। ६९ ।।

One should reject flowers left over from the day and those that are damaged; one should worship Bhānu with buds and not offer unripe fruit.

BP 1.163.70

फलं क्वथितविद्धं च यत्नात्पक्वमपि त्यजेत् ।। अलाभे बत पुष्पाणां पत्राण्यपि निवेदयेत् ।। ७० ।।

One should carefully reject fruit that is boiled or pierced, even if ripe; when flowers are unavailable, one should offer leaves.

BP 1.163.71

पत्राणामप्यलाभे तु फलान्यपि निवेदयेत् ।। फलानामप्यलाभेन तृणगुल्मौषधीरपि ।। ७१ ।।

When leaves too are unavailable, one should offer fruits; when fruits too are unavailable, even grass, shrubs, and herbs.

BP 1.163.72

ओषधीनामभावे तु भक्त्या भवति पूजितः ।। प्रत्येकं मुक्तपुष्पेण दशसौवर्णिकं फलम् ।। ७२ ।।

When herbs are unavailable, he is worshipped with devotion; each freshly gathered flower yields the fruit of ten gold coins.

BP 1.163.73

यः सुगन्धैर्मुक्तपुष्पैः सम्यग्भानुं प्रपूजयेत् ।। माघासितेऽपि सुमनाः सोऽनन्तफलमश्नुते ।। ७३ ।।

Whoever properly worships Bhānu with fragrant, freshly gathered flowers, even in Māgha’s dark fortnight, that clear-minded one obtains endless fruit.

BP 1.163.74

करवीरैर्महाराज संयतो भानुमर्चयेत् ।। सर्वपापविनिर्मुक्तः सूर्यलोके महीयते ।। ७४ ।।

O great king, one should worship Bhānu with oleanders, self-restrained; freed from all sins, one is honored in the Sun’s world.

BP 1.163.75

अगस्त्यकुसुमैर्भक्त्या यः सकृद्भानुमर्चयेत् ।। गवां प्रयुतदानस्य फलं प्राप्य दिवं व्रजेत् ।। ७५ ।।

Whoever worships Bhānu once with devotion and agastya flowers obtains the fruit of giving one million cows and goes to heaven.

BP 1.163.76

मल्लिकोत्पलपद्मैश्च जातीपुन्नागचम्पकैः ।। अशोकश्वेतमन्दारकर्णिकारान्धुकैस्तथा ।। ७६ ।।

With jasmine, water-lilies, lotus, and also jasmine, puṇṇāga, and champak; likewise with aśoka, white mandāra, karṇikāra, and āndhuka;

BP 1.163.77

करवीरार्ककह्लारशमीतगरकेशरैः ।। अगस्तिबकपुष्पैस्तु शतपत्रैर्नराधिप ।। ७७ ।।

with oleander, arka, kahlāra, śamī, tagara, and saffron; also with agasti, baka flowers, and hundred-petalled lotuses, O lord of men.

BP 1.163.78

पुष्पैरेतैर्यथालाभं यो नरः पूजयेद्रविम् ।। स तत्फलमवाप्नोति तदेकाग्रमनाः शृणु ।। ७८ ।।

Whoever worships Ravi with these flowers, according to their availability, obtains that fruit. Listen with a one-pointed mind.

BP 1.163.79

सूर्यकोटिप्रतीकाशैर्विमानैः सर्वकामिभिः ।। पुष्पमालापरिक्षिप्तैर्गीतवादित्रनादितैः ।। ७९ ।।

With vehicles shining like ten million Suns, granting every desire, covered with flower garlands, resounding with songs and instruments,

BP 1.163.80

तन्त्रीमधुरवाद्यैश्च स्वच्छन्दगमनैर्नृप ।। सूर्यकन्यासमाकीर्णैर्देवानां च सुदुर्लभैः ।। ८० ।।

with sweet stringed instruments and movement at will, O king, crowded with the Sun’s daughters, very difficult for the gods to obtain,

BP 1.163.81

दोधूयमानश्चमरैः स्तूयमानः सुरासुरैः ।। गच्छेदर्कपुरीं दिव्यां तत्र सम्पूजितो भवेत् ।। ८१ ।।

fanned by fly-whisks and praised by gods and demons, one goes to Arka’s divine city and is honored there.

BP 1.163.82

यैस्तैश्च वापिकुसुमैर्जलजैः स्थलजैर्नृप ।। सम्पूज्य श्रद्धया भानुमर्कलोके महीयते ।। ८२ ।।

With whatever pond-flowers, water-born or land-grown, O king, one worships Bhānu with faith, one is honored in Arka’s world.

BP 1.163.83

सूर्यस्योपरि यः कुर्याच्छोभनं पुष्पमण्डलम् ।। शोभितं पुष्पस्रग्दामैरापीठान्तं प्रलम्बितैः ।। ८३ ।।

Whoever makes a beautiful circle of flowers above the Sun, adorned with garlands hanging down to the pedestal,

BP 1.163.84

अत्याश्चर्यमहायानैर्दिव्यपुष्पोपशोभितः ।। सर्वेषामुपरिष्टाच्च वसेदर्कपुरे सुखी ।। ८४ ।।

adorned with wondrous great vehicles and divine flowers, he happily dwells above all in Arka’s city.

BP 1.163.85

अनेकरागविन्यस्तैः सुगन्धैः कुसुमैर्गृहम् ।। यः कुर्यात्पर्वकाले तु विचित्रकुसुमोज्ज्वलम् ।। ८५ ।।

Whoever at festival time makes a house bright with fragrant flowers arranged in many colors and varied blossoms

BP 1.163.86

स पुष्पकविमानेन पुष्पमालाकुलेन तु ।। पुष्पेतरपुरं दिव्यं श्रयते नात्र संशयः ।। ८६ ।।

reaches the divine city of Puṣpetara in a Puṣpaka vehicle crowded with flower garlands; there is no doubt.

BP 1.163.87

अक्षयं मोदते कालमतिरस्कृतशासनः ।। सौरादिसर्वलोकेषु यत्रेष्टं तत्र याति सः ।। ८७ ।।

He rejoices for an imperishable time, with no command of time able to overpower him; among all the solar and other worlds he goes wherever he desires.
