Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 113 — Ādityālaya Vandana and Mārjana

BP 1.113.1

विष्णुरुवाच।
सुरज्येष्ठ पुनर्ब्रूहि यत्पृच्छाम्यहमादितः।
यत्फलं समवाप्नोति कारयित्वा रवेर्गृहम् ॥

Viṣṇu said: O eldest among the gods, tell me again what I ask at the outset: what fruit does a person obtain by having a house made for Ravi?

Word-for-word
विष्णुः(viṣṇur) — Viṣṇu said;
सुर-ज्येष्ठ(sura-jyeṣṭha) — O eldest among the gods;
पुनः ब्रूहि(punar brūhi) — tell again;
यत् पृच्छामि अहम् आदितः(yat pṛcchāmy aham āditaḥ) — what I ask at the outset;
यत् फलम् समवाप्नोति(yat phalaṃ samavāpnoti) — what fruit one obtains;
कारयित्वा(kārayitvā) — having caused to be made;
रवेः गृहम्(raver gṛham) — a house for Ravi;
Transliteration
viṣṇuruvāca | surajyeṣṭha punarbrūhi yatpṛcchāmyahamāditaḥ | yatphalaṃ samavāpnoti kārayitvā ravergṛham || 1 ||

BP 1.113.2

देवार्चां कारयित्वा तु यत्पुण्यं पुरुषोऽश्नुते।
पूजयित्वा च विधिवदनुलिप्य च यत्फलम् ॥

What merit does a person enjoy by having an image of the god made, by worshipping it according to rule, and by plastering or anointing it?

Word-for-word
देव-अर्चाम्(devārcām) — the divine image;
कारयित्वा(kārayitvā) — having caused to be made;
तु(tu) — indeed;
यत् पुण्यम् पुरुषः अश्नुते(yat puṇyaṃ puruṣo'śnute) — what merit a person enjoys;
पूजयित्वा च(pūjayitvā ca) — having worshipped, and;
विधिवत्(vidhivat) — according to rule;
अनुलिप्य च(anulipya ca) — having plastered or anointed, and;
यत् फलम्(yat phalam) — what fruit;
Transliteration
devārcāṃ kārayitvā tu yatpuṇyaṃ puruṣo'śnute | pūjayitvā ca vidhivadanulipya ca yatphalam || 2 ||

BP 1.113.3

कानि माल्यानि शस्तानि कानि नार्हति भास्करः।
के धूपा भानुदयिताः के वर्ज्याश्च जगत्पतेः ॥

Which garlands are auspicious for Bhāskara, and which are not fit for him? Which incenses are dear to Bhānu, and which are to be avoided for the Lord of the world?

Word-for-word
कानि माल्यानि(kāni mālyāni) — which garlands;
शस्तानि(śastāni) — auspicious;
कानि न अर्हति(kāni nārhati) — which are not fit;
भास्करः(bhāskaraḥ) — Bhāskara;
के धूपाः(ke dhūpāḥ) — which incenses;
भानु-दयिताः(bhānu-dayitāḥ) — dear to Bhānu;
के वर्ज्याः च(ke varjyāś ca) — which are to be avoided, and;
जगत्-पतेः(jagat-pateḥ) — for the Lord of the world;
Transliteration
kāni mālyāni śastāni kāni nārhati bhāskaraḥ | ke dhūpā bhānudayitāḥ ke varjyāśca jagatpateḥ || 3 ||

BP 1.113.4

उपचारफलं किं स्यात्किं फलं गीतवादिते।
घृतक्षीरादिना यत्तु स्नापिते भास्करे फलम् ॥

What is the fruit of the offerings, and what is the fruit of singing and instrumental music? What fruit comes when Bhāskara is bathed with ghee, milk, and the like?

Word-for-word
उपचार-फलम्(upacāra-phalam) — the fruit of offerings;
किम् स्यात्(kiṃ syāt) — what may it be;
किम् फलम्(kiṃ phalam) — what fruit;
गीत-वादिते(gīta-vādite) — in singing and instrumental music;
घृत-क्षीर-आदिना(ghṛta-kṣīrādinā) — with ghee, milk, and the like;
यत् तु(yat tu) — that which indeed;
स्नापिते भास्करे(snāpite bhāskare) — when Bhāskara is bathed;
फलम्(phalam) — the fruit;
Transliteration
upacāraphalaṃ kiṃ syātkiṃ phalaṃ gītavādite | ghṛtakṣīrādinā yattu snāpite bhāskare phalam || 4 ||

BP 1.113.5

यथोपलेपनादौ च फलमभ्युक्षितेन तु।
दिवाकरगृहे तात तदशेषं वदस्व मे ॥

And what is the fruit of plastering and the other acts, and of sprinkling? O dear one, tell me all of that without remainder concerning the house of Divākara.

Word-for-word
यथा उपलेपन-आदौ च(yathopalepanādau ca) — and concerning plastering and the like;
फलम्(phalam) — the fruit;
अभ्युक्षितेन तु(abhyukṣitena tu) — and of sprinkling;
दिवाकर-गृहे(divākara-gṛhe) — in the house of Divākara;
तात(tāta) — O dear one;
तत् अशेषम्(tad aśeṣam) — that entirely;
वदस्व मे(vadasva me) — tell me;
Transliteration
yathopalepanādau ca phalamabhyukṣitena tu | divākaragṛhe tāta tadaśeṣaṃ vadasva me || 5 ||

BP 1.113.6

ब्रह्मोवाच।
साधु वत्स यदेतत्त्वं मार्तण्डस्येह पृच्छसि।
शुश्रूषणे विधिं पुण्यं तदिहैकमनाः शृणु ॥

Brahmā said: Good, dear child. Since you ask here about Mārtaṇḍa, hear with one-pointed attention the meritorious rule for his service.

Word-for-word
ब्रह्मा उवाच(brahmovāca) — Brahmā said;
साधु(sādhu) — good;
वत्स(vatsa) — dear child;
यत् एतत् त्वम्(yad etat tvam) — since you this;
मार्तण्डस्य इह पृच्छसि(mārtaṇḍasya iha pṛcchasi) — ask here about Mārtaṇḍa;
शुश्रूषणे विधिम् पुण्यम्(śuśrūṣaṇe vidhiṃ puṇyam) — the meritorious rule in service;
तत् इह एक-मनाः शृणु(tad iha eka-manāḥ śṛṇu) — hear that here with one-pointed mind;
Transliteration
brahmovāca | sādhu vatsa yadetattvaṃ mārtaṇḍasyeha pṛcchasi | śuśrūṣaṇe vidhiṃ puṇyaṃ tadihaikamanāḥ śṛṇu || 6 ||

BP 1.113.7

यस्तु देवालयं भानोर्दार्वं शैलमथापि वा।
कारयेन्मृन्मयं चापि तस्य पुण्यफलं शृणु ॥

Hear the meritorious fruit of the person who causes a temple of Bhānu to be made of wood, stone, or even clay.

Word-for-word
यः तु(yaḥ tu) — who indeed;
देवालयम्(devālayam) — a temple;
भानोः(bhānoḥ) — of Bhānu;
दार्वम्(dārvaṃ) — wooden;
शैलम् अथ अपि वा(śailam athāpi vā) — or stone;
कारयेत्(kārayet) — causes to be made;
मृन्मयम् च अपि(mṛnmayaṃ cāpi) — also of clay;
तस्य पुण्य-फलम् शृणु(tasya puṇya-phalaṃ śṛṇu) — hear his meritorious fruit;
Transliteration
yastu devālayaṃ bhānor dārvaṃ śailamathāpi vā | kārayenmṛnmayaṃ cāpi tasya puṇyaphalaṃ śṛṇu || 7 ||

BP 1.113.8

अहन्यहनि यज्ञेन यजतो यन्महाफलम्।
प्राप्नोति तत्फलं भानोर्यः कारयति मन्दिरम् ॥

The person who causes a temple to be built for Bhānu obtains the great fruit that one who worships day after day through sacrifice obtains.

Word-for-word
अहनि अहनि(ahany ahani) — day after day;
यज्ञेन यजतः(yajñena yajataḥ) — of one who worships with sacrifice;
यत् महा-फलम्(yan mahā-phalam) — whatever great fruit;
प्राप्नोति(prāpnoti) — he obtains;
तत् फलम्(tat phalam) — that fruit;
भानोः(bhānoḥ) — of Bhānu;
यः कारयति(yaḥ kārayati) — who causes to be made;
मन्दिरम्(mandiram) — a temple;
Transliteration
ahanyahani yajñena yajato yanmahāphalam | prāpnoti tatphalaṃ bhānoryaḥ kārayati mandiram || 8 ||

BP 1.113.9

कुलानां शतमागामि समतीतं कुलं शतम्।
कारयेद्भगवद्धाम स नयेदर्कलोकताम् ॥

Having caused the divine abode to be made, he leads one hundred future families and one hundred families already gone to the world of the Sun.

Word-for-word
कुलानाम् शतम् आगामि(kulānāṃ śatam āgāmi) — one hundred future families;
समतीतम् कुलम् शतम्(samatītaṃ kulaṃ śatam) — one hundred families already gone;
कारयेत् भगवत्-धाम(kārayet bhagavad-dhāma) — having caused the divine abode to be made;
सः नयेत्(sa nayet) — he leads;
अर्क-लोकताम्(arka-lokatām) — to the state of the Sun's world;
Transliteration
kulānāṃ śatamāgāmi samatītaṃ kulaṃ śatam | kārayedbhagavaddhāma sa nayedarkalokatām || 9 ||

BP 1.113.10

सप्तजन्मकृतं पापं स्वल्पं वा यदि वा बहु।
भानोरालयविन्यासप्रारम्भादेव नश्यति ॥

Sin committed over seven births, whether little or much, perishes from the very beginning of laying out Bhānu's temple.

Word-for-word
सप्त-जन्म-कृतम् पापम्(sapta-janma-kṛtaṃ pāpam) — sin committed in seven births;
स्वल्पम् वा यदि वा बहु(svalpaṃ vā yadi vā bahu) — little or much;
भानोः आलय-विन्यास-प्रारम्भात् एव(bhānor ālaya-vinyāsa-prārambhād eva) — from the very beginning of arranging Bhānu's temple;
नश्यति(naśyati) — perishes;
Transliteration
saptajanmakṛtaṃ pāpaṃ svalpaṃ vā yadi vā bahu | bhānorālayavinyāsaprārambhādeva naśyati || 10 ||

BP 1.113.11

सप्तलोकमयो भानुस्तस्य यः कुरुते गृहम्।
प्रतिष्ठां समवाप्नोति स नरः साप्तलौकिकीम् ॥

Bhānu consists of the seven worlds. The person who makes his house obtains the seven-world status and establishment.

Word-for-word
सप्त-लोक-मयः(sapta-loka-mayaḥ) — consisting of the seven worlds;
भानुः(bhānuḥ) — Bhānu;
तस्य यः कुरुते गृहम्(tasya yaḥ kurute gṛham) — who makes his house;
प्रतिष्ठाम् समवाप्नोति(pratiṣṭhāṃ samavāpnoti) — obtains establishment;
सः नरः(sa naraḥ) — that man;
साप्त-लौकिकीम्(sāpta-laukikīm) — the seven-world status;
Transliteration
saptalokamayo bhānustasya yaḥ kurute gṛham | pratiṣṭhāṃ samavāpnoti sa naraḥ sāptalaukikīm || 11 ||

BP 1.113.12

प्रशस्तदेशभूमिभागे प्रशस्तं भवनं रवेः।
कारयेदक्षयांल्लोकान्स नरः प्रतिपद्यते ॥

A person who causes an excellent dwelling for Ravi to be made on a fine tract of land attains imperishable worlds.

Word-for-word
प्रशस्त-देश-भूमि-भागे(praśasta-deśa-bhūmi-bhāge) — in an excellent region of land;
प्रशस्तम् भवनम्(praśastaṃ bhavanam) — an excellent dwelling;
रवेः(raveḥ) — of Ravi;
कारयेत्(kārayet) — causes to be made;
अक्षयान् लोकान्(akṣayāṃl lokān) — imperishable worlds;
सः नरः प्रतिपद्यते(sa naraḥ pratipadyate) — that man attains;
Transliteration
praśastadeśabhūbhāge praśastaṃ bhavanaṃ raveḥ | kārayedakṣayāṃllokāṃsa naraḥ pratipadyate || 12 ||

BP 1.113.13

इष्टकाचयविन्यासो यावद्वर्षाणि तिष्ठति।
तावद्वर्षसहस्राणि तत्कर्तुर्दिवि संस्थितिः ॥

For as many years as the arrangement of its bricks endures, for so many thousands of years the maker remains in heaven.

Word-for-word
इष्टका-चय-विन्यासः(iṣṭakā-caya-vinyāsaḥ) — the arrangement of a pile of bricks;
यावत् वर्षाणि तिष्ठति(yāvad varṣāṇi tiṣṭhati) — for as many years as it endures;
तावत् वर्ष-सहस्राणि(tāvad varṣa-sahasrāṇi) — so many thousand years;
तत् कर्तुः(tat kartuḥ) — of its maker;
दिवि संस्थितिः(divi saṃsthitiḥ) — abiding in heaven;
Transliteration
iṣṭakācayavinyāso yāvadvarṣāṇi tiṣṭhati | tāvadvarṣasahasrāṇi tatkarturd divi saṃsthitiḥ || 13 ||

BP 1.113.14

प्रतिमां लक्षणवतीं यः कारयति मानवः।
दिवाकरस्य तल्लोकमक्षयं प्रतिपद्यते ॥

The person who causes an image bearing the proper marks to be made attains the imperishable world of Divākara.

Word-for-word
प्रतिमाम् लक्षण-वतीम्(pratimāṃ lakṣaṇa-vatīm) — an image possessing the proper marks;
यः कारयति(yaḥ kārayati) — who causes to be made;
मानवः(mānavaḥ) — a person;
दिवाकरस्य तत् लोकम्(divākarasya tal lokam) — that world of Divākara;
अक्षयम्(akṣayam) — imperishable;
प्रतिपद्यते(pratipadyate) — attains;
Transliteration
pratimāṃ lakṣaṇavatīṃ yaḥ kārayati mānavaḥ | divākarasya tallokamakṣayaṃ pratipadyate || 14 ||

BP 1.113.15

षष्टिर्वर्षसहस्राणां सहस्राणि स मोदते।
लोके सुमनसां वीर प्रत्येकं मधुसूदन ॥

He rejoices for sixty thousand thousand years in the world of the benevolent, O hero, O Madhusūdana.

Word-for-word
षष्टिः वर्ष-सहस्राणाम्(ṣaṣṭir varṣa-sahasrāṇām) — sixty thousand years;
सहस्राणि(sahasrāṇi) — thousands;
सः मोदते(sa modate) — he rejoices;
लोके(loke) — in the world;
सुमनसाम्(sumanasām) — of the benevolent;
वीर(vīra) — O hero;
प्रत्येकम्(pratyekam) — each / fully;
मधुसूदन(madhusūdana) — O Madhusūdana;
Transliteration
ṣaṣṭirvarṣasahasrāṇāṃ sahasrāṇi sa modate | loke sumanasāṃ vīra pratyekaṃ madhusūdana || 15 ||

BP 1.113.16

प्रतिष्ठाप्य रवेर्चां सुप्रशस्ते निवेशने।
पुरुषः कृतकृत्योऽस्ति न दोषफलमश्नुते ॥

Having installed Ravi's image in an excellent dwelling, a person has fulfilled his duty and does not suffer the fruit of any fault.

Word-for-word
प्रतिष्ठाप्य(pratiṣṭhāpya) — having installed;
रवेः अर्चाम्(raver arcām) — Ravi's image;
सुप्रशस्ते निवेशने(supraśaste niveśane) — in an excellent dwelling;
पुरुषः(puruṣaḥ) — a person;
कृत-कृत्यः अस्ति(kṛta-kṛtyo'sti) — has fulfilled his duty;
न दोष-फलम् अश्नुते(na doṣa-phalam aśnute) — does not suffer the fruit of fault;
Transliteration
pratiṣṭhāpya raverarcāṃ supraśaste niveśane | puruṣaḥ kṛtakṛtyo'sti na doṣaphalamaśnute || 16 ||

BP 1.113.17

ये भविष्यन्ति येऽतीता आकल्पं पुरुषाः कुले।
तांस्तारयति संस्थाप्य देवस्य प्रतिमां रवेः ॥

Having installed the image of the god Ravi, he saves all the people who will be born and those who have passed away in his family, for the duration of the age.

Word-for-word
ये भविष्यन्ति(ye bhaviṣyanti) — who will be;
ये अतीताः(ye'tītāḥ) — who have passed away;
आकल्पम्(ākalpam) — until the end of the age;
पुरुषाः कुले(puruṣāḥ kule) — the persons in the family;
तान् तारयति(tāṃs tārayati) — he saves them;
संस्थाप्य(saṃsthāpya) — having installed;
देवस्य प्रतिमाम् रवेः(devasya pratimāṃ raveḥ) — the image of the god Ravi;
Transliteration
ye bhaviṣyanti ye'tītā ākalpaṃ puruṣāḥ kule | tāṃstārayati saṃsthāpya devasya pratimāṃ raveḥ || 17 ||

BP 1.113.18

अनुशिष्टाः किल पुरा यमेन यमकिङ्कराः।
पाशदण्डकराः कृष्ण प्रजासंयमनोद्यताः ॥

The servants of Yama, bearing nooses and staffs and intent on restraining beings, had formerly been instructed by Yama, O Kṛṣṇa.

Word-for-word
अनुशिष्टाः किल पुरा(anuśiṣṭāḥ kila purā) — had formerly been instructed;
यमेन(yamena) — by Yama;
यम-किङ्कराः(yama-kiṅkarāḥ) — the servants of Yama;
पाश-दण्ड-कराः(pāśa-daṇḍa-karāḥ) — holding nooses and staffs;
कृष्ण(kṛṣṇa) — O Kṛṣṇa;
प्रजा-संयमन-उद्यताः(prajā-saṃyamana-udyatāḥ) — intent on restraining beings;
Transliteration
anuśiṣṭāḥ kila purā yamena yamakiṅkarāḥ | pāśadaṇḍakarāḥ kṛṣṇa prajāsaṃyamanodyatāḥ || 18 ||

BP 1.113.19

यम उवाच।
विहरन्तु यथान्यायं नियोगो मेऽनुपाल्यताम्।
नाज्ञाभङ्गं करिष्यन्ति भवतां जन्तवः क्वचित् ॥

Yama said: Let them move according to the proper rule; let my command be carried out. Your beings shall never disobey the command.

Word-for-word
यमः उवाच(yama uvāca) — Yama said;
विहरन्तु(viharantu) — let them move;
यथा-न्यायम्(yathā-nyāyam) — according to the proper rule;
नियोगः मे अनुपाल्यताम्(niyogo me'nupālyatām) — let my command be carried out;
न आज्ञा-भङ्गम् करिष्यन्ति(nājñā-bhaṅgaṃ kariṣyanti) — they shall not disobey the command;
भवताम् जन्तवः क्वचित्(bhavatāṃ jantavaḥ kvacit) — your beings ever;
Transliteration
yama uvāca | viharantu yathānyāyaṃ niyogo me'nupālyatām | nājñābhaṅgaṃ kariṣyanti bhavatāṃ jantavaḥ kvacit || 19 ||

BP 1.113.20

केवलं ये जगन्मूलं विवस्वन्तमुपाश्रिताः।
भवद्भिः परिहर्तव्यास्तेषां नैवेह संस्थितिः ॥

Only those who have taken refuge in Vivasvant, the root of the world, must be avoided by you; they have no place here.
