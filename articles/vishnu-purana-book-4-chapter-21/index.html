VP 4.21.1

अतः परं भविष्यानहं भूपालान्कीर्तयिष्यामि

Sri Paraasharar - I will start to tell about kings who are going to come in future.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
अतः परम् (ataḥ param) — from this point onward
भविष्यान् भूपालान् (bhaviṣyān bhūpālān) — future kings
अहम् कीर्तयिष्यामि (aham kīrtayiṣyāmi) — I will recount.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ataḥ paraṃ bhaviṣyānahaṃ bhūpālānkīrtayiṣyāmi

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.21.2

योऽयं सांप्रतमवनीपतिः परीक्षित्तस्यापि जनमेजयश्रुतसेनोग्रसेनभीमसेनाश्चत्वारः पुत्रा भविष्यन्ति

Pareekshit is going to have four sons - Janamejaya, Shrutasena, Ugrasena and Bheemasena.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
यः अयम् साम्प्रतम् अवनीपतिः परीक्षित् (yaḥ ayam sāmpratam avanīpatiḥ parīkṣit) — this present king Parīkṣit
तस्य अपि चत्वारः पुत्राः भविष्यन्ति (tasya api catvāraḥ putrāḥ bhaviṣyanti) — will also have four sons
जनमेजय-श्रुत-सेन-उग्र-सेन-भीम-सेनाः (janamejaya-śruta-sena-ugra-sena-bhīma-senāḥ) — Janamejaya, Śrutasena, Ugrasena, and Bhīmasena.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yo'yaṃ sāṃpratamavanīpatiḥ parīkṣittasyāpi janamejayaśrutasenograsenabhīmasenāścatvāraḥ putrā bhaviṣyanti

Śrīdhara

ekaviṃśe purovaṃśo bhaviṣya upavarṇyate . kṣemakaṃ prāpya yaḥ saṃsthāṃ kalāvupagamiṣyati . kuruputra-parikṣita ivāsyāpi janamejayādisañjñā eva catvāraḥ putrāḥ ||२||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

एकविंशे पुरोवंशो भविष्य उपवर्ण्यते । क्षेमकं प्राप्य यः संस्थां कलावुपगमिष्यति । कुरुपुत्र-परिक्षित इवास्यापि जनमेजयादिसञ्ज्ञा एव चत्वारः पुत्राः ॥२॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Pareekshit is going to have four sons - Janamejaya, Shrutasena, Ugrasena and Bheemasena.

VP 4.21.4

योऽसौ याज्ञवल्क्याद्वेदमधीत्य कृपादस्त्राण्यवाप्य विषमविषयविरक्तचित्तवृत्तिश्च शौनकोपदेशादात्मज्ञानप्रवीणः परं निर्वाणमवाप्स्यति

Shataaneeka will become a great jnyaani. He will learn the Vedaas from Yaajnyavalkya, he will learn the astra vidyaa from Krupa, his mind will be completely free from all sensual pleasures, he will learn the bhakti jnyaana vidh i from Shounaka, having realized the self, he is going to get moksha. He is going to realize aatma jnyaana, Paramaatma jnyaana and will get mukti.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
यः असौ (yaḥ asau) — he who
याज्ञवल्क्यात् वेदम् अधीत्य (yājñavalkyāt vedam adhītya) — having studied the Veda from Yājñavalkya
कृपात् अस्त्राणि अवाप्य (kṛpāt astrāṇi avāpya) — having obtained weapons from Kṛpa
विषम-विषय-विरक्त-चित्त-वृत्तिः (viṣama-viṣaya-virakta-citta-vṛttiḥ) — whose mind is detached from the varied sense-objects
शौनक-उपदेशात् आत्म-ज्ञान-प्रवीणः (śaunaka-upadeśāt ātma-jñāna-pravīṇaḥ) — skilled in knowledge of the Self through Śaunaka’s instruction
परम् निर्वाणम् अवाप्स्यति (param nirvāṇam avāpsyati) — will attain supreme liberation.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yo'sau yājñavalkyādvedamadhītya kṛpādastrāṇyavāpya viṣamaviṣayaviraktacittavṛttiśca śaunakopadeśādātmajñānapravīṇaḥ paraṃ nirvāṇamavāpsyati

Śrīdhara

tasya janamejayasya . ātmavijñānapravaṇastatparaḥ ||४||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तस्य जनमेजयस्य । आत्मविज्ञानप्रवणस्तत्परः ॥४॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Shataaneeka will become a great jnyaani. He will learn the Vedaas from Yaajnyavalkya, he will learn the astra vidyaa from Krupa, his mind will be completely free from all sensual pleasures, he will learn the bhakti jnyaana vidh i from Shounaka, having realized the self, he is going to get moksha. He is going to realize aatma jnyaana, Paramaatma jnyaana and will get mukti.

VP 4.21.12

VP 4.21.18

ब्रह्मक्षत्रस्य यो योनिर्वंशो देवर्षिसत्कृतः ।
क्षेमकं प्राप्य राजानं संस्थानं प्राप्यते कलौ

The vamsha which is well honoured by even the Devarshis, and which is the origin of the Brahma and Kshatriya race, that will end in Kaliyuga with Kshemaka as the last king.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
ब्रह्म-क्षत्रस्य यः योनिः (brahma-kṣatrasya yaḥ yoniḥ) — which is the source of Brahmin and Kshatriya
वंशः देवर्षि-सत्कृतः (vaṃśaḥ devarṣi-satkṛtaḥ) — the lineage honoured by divine sages
क्षेमकम् प्राप्य राजानम् (kṣemakam prāpya rājānam) — having reached King Kṣemaka
संस्थानम् प्राप्यते कलौ (saṃsthānam prāpyate kalau) — its end is reached in the Kali age.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

brahmakṣatrasya yo yonirvaṃśo devarṣisatkṛtaḥ | kṣemakaṃ prāpya rājānaṃ saṃsthānaṃ prāpyate kalau

Śrīdhara

brahmaṇaḥ brāhmaṇasya kṣatrasya kṣatriyasya ca yoniḥ kāraṇaṃ kṣatriyair eva kaiścit tapo-viśeṣāt brāhmaṇyaṃ labdham iti pūrvaṃ tathoktatvāt . saṃsthāṃ samāptim ||१८||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

ब्रह्मणः ब्राह्मणस्य क्षत्रस्य क्षत्रियस्य च योनिः कारणं क्षत्रियैर् एव कैश्चित् तपो-विशेषात् ब्राह्मण्यं लब्धम् इति पूर्वं तथोक्तत्वात् । संस्थां समाप्तिम् ॥१८॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

The vamsha which is well honoured by even the Devarshis, and which is the origin of the Brahma and Kshatriya race, that will end in Kaliyuga with Kshemaka as the last king.
