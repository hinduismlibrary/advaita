Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 11 — Household Duties and Stores

BP 1.11.1

ब्रह्मोवाच
या पतिं दैवतं पश्येन्मनोवाक्कायकर्मभिः ।
तच्छरीरार्धजातेव सर्वदा हितमाचरेत् ॥

A wife who regards her husband as a deity in thought, speech, and bodily acts should always act for his good, as if born as half of his body.

BP 1.11.2

तत्प्रियां प्रियवत्पश्येत्तद्द्वेष्या द्वेष्यवत्सदा ।
अधर्मानर्थयुक्तेभ्योऽयुक्ता चास्य निवर्तते ॥

She should regard what is dear to him as dear, and what he hates as hateful; she should turn him away from wrongdoing, harm, and what is improper.

BP 1.11.3

प्रियं किमस्य किं पथ्यं साम्यं चास्य कथं भवेत् ।
ज्ञात्वैवं सर्वभृत्येषु न प्रमाद्येत वै द्विजाः ॥

Knowing what pleases him, what is wholesome for him, and how harmony may be kept, O twice-born, she should not be heedless regarding any of the servants.

BP 1.11.4

देवतापितृकार्येषु भर्तुः स्नानाशनादिषु ।
सत्कारेऽभ्यागतानां च यथौचित्यं न हापयेत् ॥

In rites for the gods and ancestors, in her husband's bathing and eating and the like, and in honoring arriving guests, she should not fail to act as is fitting.

BP 1.11.5

वेश्मात्मा च शरीरं हि गृहिणीनां द्विधा कृतम् ।
संस्कर्तव्यं प्रयत्नेन प्रथमं पश्चिमादपि ॥

For housewives, the house and the body are indeed made into a twofold self. She should tend both with effort, the first before the latter.

BP 1.11.6

कृत्वा वेश्म सुसंमृष्टं त्रिकालविहितार्चनम् ।
वृत्तकर्मोपभोगानां संस्कर्तव्यं यथोचितम् ॥

Having thoroughly swept the house and arranged the worship prescribed for the three times, she should properly prepare what is needed for daily duties and enjoyment.

BP 1.11.7

प्रातर्मध्यापराह्णेषु बहिर्मध्यान्तरेषु च ।
गृहसम्मार्जनं कृत्वा निष्कारान्न निशि क्षिपेत् ॥

Having swept the outer, middle, and inner parts of the house in the morning, midday, and afternoon, she should not throw refuse outside at night.

BP 1.11.8

गोमहिष्यादिशालानां तत्पुरीषादिमात्रकम् ।
व्यपनेयं तु यत्नेन सम्मार्जन्या प्रसाधनम् ॥

She should carefully remove dung and the like from the sheds of cows, buffaloes, and other animals, and sweep them clean.

BP 1.11.9

दासकर्मकरादीनां बाह्याभ्यन्तरचारिणाम् ।
पोषणादिविधिं विद्यादनुष्ठानं च कर्मसु ॥

She should know the provisions for servants, laborers, and others who move within and outside, and their performance of their work.

BP 1.11.10

शाकमूलफलादीनां वल्लीनामौषधस्य च ।
सङ्ग्रहः सर्वबीजानां यथाकालं यथाबलम् ॥

She should gather vegetables, roots, fruits, creepers, medicinal plants, and all kinds of seeds at the proper time and according to her means.

BP 1.11.11

ताम्रकांस्यायसादीनां काष्ठवेणुमयस्य च ।
मृन्मयानां च भाण्डानां विविधानां च सङ्ग्रहम् ॥

She should gather various vessels made of copper, bronze, iron, and other metals, of wood and bamboo, and of clay.

BP 1.11.12

कुण्डकादिजलद्रोण्या कलशोदञ्चतालुकाः ।
शाकपात्राण्यनेकानि स्नेहानां गोरसस्य च ॥

She should have water tubs, pitchers, water-drawing vessels and ladles, many vegetable dishes, and vessels for oils and dairy products.

BP 1.11.13

मुसलं कुण्डनीयं तु यन्त्रकं२ चूर्णचालनी ।
दोहन्यो नेत्रकं मन्था मण्डन्यः शृङ्खलानि च ॥

She should have a pestle, mortar, grinder, flour sieve, milking vessels, churning cord and churn, mixing vessels, and chains.

BP 1.11.14

संदंशः कुण्डिका शूलाः पट्टपिप्पलको दृषत् ।
डाविका हस्तको दर्वी भ्राष्टस्फुटलकानि च ॥

She should have tongs, small pots, spits, a board, a grinding stone, a scoop, hand tools, a ladle, and implements for roasting and frying.

BP 1.11.15

तुलाप्रस्थादिमानानि मार्जन्यः पिटकानि च ।
सर्वमेतत्प्रकुर्वीत प्रयत्नेन च सर्वदा ॥

She should carefully provide and always keep scales, standard measures, brooms, and baskets.

BP 1.11.16

हिंग्वादिकमथो जाजी पिपल्यो मारिचानि च ।
राजिका धान्यकं शुण्ठी त्रिचतुर्जातकानि च ॥

She should have asafoetida and the like, cumin, long pepper, black pepper, mustard, coriander, dried ginger, and three-spice and four-spice mixtures.

BP 1.11.17

लवणं क्षारवर्गाश्च सौवीरकपरूषकौ ।
द्विदलामलकं चिंचा सर्वाश्च स्नेहजातयः ॥

She should have salt, alkaline substances, fermented foods and dried fruits, split pulses, Indian gooseberry, tamarind, and all kinds of oils.

BP 1.11.18

शुष्ककाष्ठानि वल्लूरमरिष्टा पिष्टमाषयोः ।
विकाराः पयसश्चापि विविधाः कन्दजातयः ॥

She should have dry firewood, dried meat, fermented preparations, preparations of flour and black gram, various milk products, and kinds of tubers.

BP 1.11.19

नित्यनैमित्तिकानां हि कार्याणामुपयोगतः ।
सर्वमित्यादि संग्राह्यं यथावद्विभवोचितम् ॥

For the sake of daily and occasional duties, she should properly gather all these and similar things according to her means.

BP 1.11.20

यत्कार्याणां समुत्पत्तावुपाहर्तुं न दृश्यते ।
तत्प्रागेव यथायोगं सङ्गृह्णीयात्प्रयत्नतः ॥

Whatever cannot be procured when the need arises, she should carefully gather beforehand, as appropriate.

BP 1.11.21

धान्यानां घृष्टपिष्टानां क्षुण्णोपहतयोरपि ।
भृशं शुष्कार्द्रसिद्धानां क्षयवृद्धी निरूपयेत् ॥

She should carefully determine the decrease and increase of grains that are pounded, ground, crushed, or damaged, and those that are dry, moist, or cooked.
