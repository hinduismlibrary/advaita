Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 154 — The Threefold Account

BP 1.154.1

सुमन्तुरुवाच । श्रुत्वा तु वचनं भानुर्विष्णोरमिततेजसः । उवाच कुरुशार्दूल आदित्यः कृपयान्वितः ॥ १ ॥

Sumantu said: Having heard the words of Viṣṇu of immeasurable splendor, Āditya, moved by compassion, spoke, O tiger among the Kurus.

BP 1.154.2

आदित्य उवाच । कृष्ण कृष्ण महाबाहो शृणु मे परमं वचः । यदर्थं प्रार्थितः कृष्ण तत्सर्वं ते भविष्यति ॥ २ ॥

Āditya said: Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa, mighty-armed, hear my supreme word. All that you prayed for, O Kṛṣṇa, shall come to pass for you.

BP 1.154.3

देवदानवयक्षाणां गन्धर्वोरगरक्षसाम् । अजेयस्त्वं महाबाहो भविष्यसि न संशयः ॥ ३ ॥

You shall be unconquerable by gods, demons, Yakṣas, Gandharvas, serpents, and demons, O mighty-armed; there is no doubt.

BP 1.154.4

जगत्पालयितुं सर्वं समर्थश्च भविष्यसि । अचला तव भक्तिश्च भविष्यति ममोपरि ॥ ४ ॥

You shall be able to protect the whole world, and your devotion toward me shall be unwavering.

BP 1.154.5

ब्रह्मापि सततं शक्तो जगत्स्रष्टुं भविष्यति । संहर्तुं शङ्करश्चापि मत्प्रसादाद्भविष्यति ॥ ५ ॥

Brahmā too shall always be able to create the world, and Śaṅkara too shall be able to dissolve it by my favor.

BP 1.154.6

भवन्तो मत्प्रसादेन ज्ञानिनामुत्तमं पदम् । गमिष्यन्ति न सन्देहो मत्पूजाप्रसादतः ॥ ६ ॥

By my favor, you shall attain the highest state among the wise, no doubt, through the favor of worshipping me.

BP 1.154.7

रवेर्वचनमाकर्ण्य गोश्रुताभरणो विभो । उवाच गोपतिर्गोगो गोपतिं गोवृषध्वजः ॥ ७ ॥

Hearing Ravi’s words, the bull-bannered lord of cattle, adorned with Gośruta, spoke to the lord of cattle: O Gogo, lord of cattle, O all-pervading one.

BP 1.154.8

त्वामाराध्य भविष्यामो वयं सर्वे सुरोत्तमाः । कथमाराधयामो हि भवन्तं श्रद्धयान्विताः । श्रेयसे सततं देव ब्रूहि नस्तत्त्वमात्मनः ॥ ८ ॥

Worshipping you, we shall all become best among the gods, O best of the gods. How shall we worship you with faith? For our welfare always, O god, tell us your own truth.

BP 1.154.9

भवतो हि न पश्यामो मूर्तिं परमपूजिताम् । पश्यामः केवलं तेजो ह्यब्धेस्तोयमिवोज्झितम् ॥ ९ ॥

We do not see your supremely worshipped form; we see only radiance, like water cast forth from the ocean.

BP 1.154.10

ज्वालामालाकुलं सर्वमनेकाकृति चाद्भुतम् । न चाकारविहीनं तु चेतसो लम्बनं भवेत् ॥ १० ॥

All of it is crowded with garlands of flames, many-formed and wondrous; yet what is without form cannot be a support for the mind.

BP 1.154.11

आलम्बनादृते देव न चित्तरमणं क्वचित् । चेतसोऽरमणे भक्तिर्न पुंसां जायते क्वचित् ॥ ११ ॥

Without a support, O god, the mind has no delight anywhere; when the mind has no delight, devotion never arises among people.

BP 1.154.12

भक्तिं विना पूजयितुं न शक्यन्ते दिवौकसः । त्वत्पूजने हि प्राप्यन्ते देव धर्मादयो नरैः ॥ १२ ॥

Without devotion, the dwellers in heaven cannot worship; through your worship, O god, righteousness and the rest are attained by people.

BP 1.154.13

तस्माद्दर्शय तां मूर्तिमात्मनो या परा मता । येन त्वां पूजयित्वा तु वयं सिद्धा भवामहे ॥ १३ ॥

Therefore show that form of yourself which is held to be supreme, so that, having worshipped you, we may attain success.

BP 1.154.14

सूर्य उवाच । साधु साधु महादेव साधु पृष्टोऽस्मि सुव्रत । शृणु चैकमनाः कृत्स्नं गदतो मम मानद ॥ १४ ॥

Sūrya said: Well, well, Mahādeva; well have I been asked, O steadfast one. Listen with one mind to all that I speak, O giver of honor.

BP 1.154.15

चतुर्मूर्तिरहं देव जगद्व्याप्य व्यवस्थितः । श्रेयसे सर्वलोकानामादिमध्यान्तकृत्सदा ॥ १५ ॥

I am four-formed, O god, pervading the world and abiding; I always make the beginning, middle, and end for the welfare of all worlds.

BP 1.154.16

एका मे राजसी मूर्तिर्ब्रह्मेति परिकीर्तिता । सृष्टिं करोति सा नित्यं कल्पादौ जगतां विभो ॥ १६ ॥

One form of mine, of the quality of passion, is called Brahmā; it always creates the worlds at the beginning of the aeon, O lord.

BP 1.154.17

द्वितीया सात्त्विकी प्रोक्ता या परा परिकीर्तिता । जगत्सा पालयेन्नित्यं दुष्टदैत्यविनाशिनी ॥ १७ ॥

The second is said to be of the quality of goodness, the supreme one; it always protects the world, destroying wicked Daityas.

BP 1.154.18

तृतीया तामसी ज्ञेया ईशेति षरिकीर्तिता । त्रैलोक्यं संहरेत्सा तु कल्पान्ते शूलपाणिनी ॥ १८ ॥

The third is known to be of the quality of darkness, called Īśa. Bearing the trident, it dissolves the three worlds at the end of the aeon.

BP 1.154.19

चतुर्थी तु गुणैर्हीना सत्यादिभिरनुत्तमा । सा चाशक्या क्वचिद्द्रष्टुं स्थिता सा चाभवत्सदा ॥ १९ ॥

The fourth is without qualities, unsurpassed among Satya and the rest; it cannot be seen anywhere, yet it always abides.

BP 1.154.20

तया ततमिदं सर्वं यच्चोद्गीथं तु मे गतिः । निष्कला सकला सा तु सुरूपा रूपवर्जिता ॥ २० ॥

By her this whole is pervaded, and the sacred chant is my course. She is partless and with parts, beautiful and devoid of form.

BP 1.154.21

अन्तर्गता च लोकानां न च कर्मफलं गता । तिष्ठमानाप्यलिप्ता सा पद्मपत्रमिवाम्भसा ॥ २१ ॥

She has entered within the worlds, but has not entered the fruit of action. Though standing, she is unattached, like a lotus leaf in water.

BP 1.154.22

अनष्टा च सदा षड्भिः सप्तातीत्य व्यवस्थिता । चतुस्तना च सा षड्भ्यस्तुरीयाख्या सुपूजिता ॥ २२ ॥

She is never destroyed by the six and always stands beyond the seven. She is four-breasted, beyond the six, called the Fourth, and greatly worshipped.

BP 1.154.23

न सा स्प्रष्टुं त्वया शक्या हरिणा ब्रह्मणा न च । मामनाराध्य भूतेश व्योमरूपं कदाचन ॥ २३ ॥

You, Hari, and Brahmā cannot reach her at any time without worshipping me, O lord of beings, whose form is the sky.

BP 1.154.24

यदेतद्भवतां देव प्रबोधार्थमुपस्थितम् । अहंकारविमूढानां तमसा च त्रिलोचन ॥ २४ ॥

This has appeared, O gods, for your awakening, you who are deluded by ego and darkness, O three-eyed one.

BP 1.154.25

प्रकाशाय च लोकानां ज्वालामालासमाकुलम् । कर्णिकेव स्थितं देव भूपद्मस्याखिलस्य च ॥ २५ ॥

For the illumination of the worlds, it is crowded with garlands of flames; it stands like the pericarp of the entire lotus of the earth, O god.

BP 1.154.26

यस्य संदर्शनादेव यूयं सर्वे प्रबोधिताः । प्रकाशमभवद्वापि जगत्सर्वमथार्चिभिः ॥ २६ ॥

By the mere sight of it, you were all awakened, and then the whole world became illuminated by its rays.

BP 1.154.27

तस्मादाराधयस्वैनमस्पृष्टं गमनोपमम् । मन्मूर्तिं येन तां दिव्यां द्रक्ष्यसि त्वं त्रिलोचन ॥ २७ ॥

Therefore worship this untouched one, resembling motion; through my form you will see that divine one, O three-eyed one.

BP 1.154.28

यत्त्वाद्यमीश्वरं जज्ञे तद्व्योम परिकीर्तितम् । कल्पान्ते ह्यत्र वै व्योम्नि लीयन्ते सर्वदेवताः ॥ २८ ॥

That from which the primordial Lord was born is called the sky. At the end of the aeon, all the gods dissolve here in the sky.

BP 1.154.29

दक्षिणे लीयते ब्रह्मा वामे तस्य जनार्दनः । त्वं सदा कचदेशे तु लीयसे त्रिपुरान्तक ॥ २९ ॥

Brahmā dissolves on its right side, Janārdana on its left; you always dissolve in its hair-region, O destroyer of Tripura.

BP 1.154.30

गायत्री लीयते तस्य हृदये लोकमातरः । लीयन्ते मूर्द्ध्नि वै वेदः सषडङ्गपदक्रमः ॥ ३० ॥

Gāyatrī and the mothers of the worlds dissolve in its heart; the Vedas, with their six limbs and word-by-word and sequential recitations, dissolve in its head.

BP 1.154.31

जठरे लीयते सर्वं जगत्स्थावरजङ्गमम् । पुनरुत्पद्यते ह्यस्माद्ब्रह्माद्यं सचराचरम् ॥ ३१ ॥

All the world, moving and unmoving, dissolves in its belly; from this, Brahmā and the rest, moving and unmoving, arise again.

BP 1.154.32

आकाशं व्योम इत्याहुः पृथिवी निक्षुभा मता । भूतश्रेयोऽहमाकाशो निक्षुभा दयिता मम ॥ ३२ ॥

They call space “sky”; the earth is considered Nikṣubhā. I, the sky, am the welfare of beings; Nikṣubhā is my beloved.

BP 1.154.33

मया निक्षुभया सर्वं जगद्व्याप्तं त्रिलोचन । तस्मादाराधय व्योम त्वं ब्रह्मा केशवस्तथा ॥ ३३ ॥

By me and Nikṣubhā the whole world is pervaded, O three-eyed one. Therefore worship the sky—you, Brahmā, and Keśava too.

BP 1.154.34

तन्मे रूपं महद्व्योम पूजयित्वा त्रिलोचन । दिव्यं वर्षसहस्रं हि गिरौ त्वं गन्धमादने । ततो यास्यसि संसिद्धिं षडङ्गां परमां शुभाम् ॥ ३४ ॥

Having worshipped that great sky, my form, O three-eyed one, on Mount Gandhamādana for a thousand divine years, you will then attain supreme, auspicious, sixfold success.

BP 1.154.35

कलापग्राममाश्रित्य शङ्खचक्रगदाधरः । आराधयतु मां भक्त्या व्योमरूपं जनार्दनः ॥ ३५ ॥

Taking refuge in Kalāpa village, Janārdana, bearer of conch, discus, and mace, should worship me with devotion in sky-form.

BP 1.154.36

अन्तरिक्षगतं तीर्थं पुष्करं लोकपावनम् । तत्र गत्वा विरिञ्चो मे व्योमरूपं सदार्चतु ॥ ३६ ॥

Puṣkara, a sacred place in the atmosphere, purifies the world. Having gone there, Viriñca should always worship me in sky-form.

BP 1.154.37

एवं मां सततं यूयं समाराध्य जगत्पतिम् । समानां च सुदिव्यानां सहस्रत्रयमादरात् ॥ ३७ ॥

Thus, having worshipped me constantly, the lord of the world, with reverence for three thousand supremely divine years,

BP 1.154.38

ततो द्रक्ष्यथ मे मूर्तिं परमां यां विदुर्बुधाः । कदम्बगोलकाकारां रश्मिमालाकुलां पराम् ॥ ३८ ॥

Then you will see my supreme form, known to the wise, round like a Kadamba blossom, supreme, crowded with garlands of rays.

BP 1.154.39

अथ नारायणो देवः प्रणम्य शिरसा रविम् । कृताञ्जलिपुटो भूत्वा इदं वचनमब्रवीत् ॥ ३९ ॥

Then the god Nārāyaṇa, bowing his head to Ravi and joining his palms, spoke these words.

BP 1.154.40

विष्णुरुवाच । यदि ते परमं रूपं मतं व्योम ह्यनौपमम् । तमराध्य वयं सर्वे यास्यामः सिद्धिमुत्तमाम् ॥ ४० ॥

Viṣṇu said: If your supreme form is held to be the incomparable sky, then, worshipping it, we shall all attain the highest success.

BP 1.154.41

कीदृग्व्योम त्वहं ब्रह्मा हरश्च त्रिपुरान्तकः । आराधयामहे देव भक्त्या श्रेयोऽर्थमात्मनः ॥ ४१ ॥

What kind of sky shall I, Brahmā, and Hara, the destroyer of Tripura, worship with devotion, O god, for our own welfare?

BP 1.154.42

येन सिद्धिं गमिष्यामस्तमाराध्य दिवाकरम् । तस्मान्नो लक्षणं ब्रूहि व्योम्नः परमपूजित ॥ ४२ ॥

By worshipping Divākara we shall attain success; therefore tell us the defining mark of the sky, O supremely worshipped one.
