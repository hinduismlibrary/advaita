VP 1.18.1

तस्यैवं दानवाश् चेष्टां दृष्ट्वा दैत्यपतेर् भयात् । आचचक्षुः स चोवाच सूदान् आहूय सत्वरः ॥१॥

The Dānavas, observing the conduct of Prahlāda, reported it to the king, lest they should incur his displeasure.

VP 1.18.4

ते तथैव ततश् चक्रुः प्रह्लादस्य महात्मने । विषदानं यथाज्ञप्तं पित्रा तस्य महात्मनः ॥४॥

Accordingly they did so, and administered poison to the virtuous Prahlāda, as his father had commanded them.

Having previously seen weapons, snakes, elephants, and the like, a countermeasure such as a mantra was used. Therefore, poison should be placed in every kind of food, such as sugar candy and sweet cakes, so that it remains unrecognized and he is killed.
