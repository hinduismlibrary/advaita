Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 71 — The Names of Sūrya Taught by Brahmā

BP 1.71.1

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
नामभिः संस्तुतो देवो यैरर्कः परितुष्यति ।
तानि ते कीर्तयाम्येष यथावदनुपूर्वशः ॥ ॥

Brahmā said: I shall proclaim to you, in due order and exactly as they are, the names by which the Deity Arka is pleased.

Word-for-word
brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said; nāmabhiḥ — by names; saṃstutaḥ — praised; devaḥ — Deity; yaiḥ — by which; arkaḥ — Arka; parituṣyati — is pleased; tāni — those; te — to you; kīrtayāmi — I proclaim; eṣaḥ — these; yathāvat — exactly; anupūrvaśaḥ — in due order
Transliteration
brahmovāca |
nāmabhiḥ saṃstuto devo yair arkaḥ parituṣyati |
tāni te kīrtayāmyeṣa yathāvad anupūrvaśaḥ || 1 ||

BP 1.71.2

नमः सूर्याय नित्याय रवये कार्यभानवे ।
भास्कराय मतङ्गाय मार्तण्डाय विवस्वते ॥ ॥

Salutation to Sūrya, the eternal; to Ravi, the radiant performer of deeds; to Bhāskara, Mataṅga, Mārtaṇḍa, and Vivasvat.

Word-for-word
namaḥ — salutation; sūryāya — to Sūrya; nityāya — eternal; ravaye — to Ravi; kārya-bhānave — radiant performer of deeds; bhāskarāya — to Bhāskara; mataṅgāya — to Mataṅga; mārtaṇḍāya — to Mārtaṇḍa; vivasvate — to Vivasvat
Transliteration
namaḥ sūryāya nityāya ravaye kāryabhānave |
bhāskarāya mataṅgāya mārtaṇḍāya vivasvate || 2 ||

BP 1.71.3

आदित्यायादिदेवाय नमस्ते रश्मिमालिने ।
दिवाकराय दीप्ताय अग्नये मिहिराय च ॥ ॥

Salutation to Āditya, the primordial Deity, and to you who wear a garland of rays; salutation to Divākara, the radiant one, to Agni, and to Mihira.

Word-for-word
ādityāya — to Āditya; ādi-devāya — to the primordial Deity; namaḥ — salutation; te — to you; raśmi-māline — wearing a garland of rays; divākarāya — to Divākara; dīptāya — radiant; agnaye — to Agni; mihirāya — to Mihira; ca — and
Transliteration
ādityāyādidevāya namas te raśmimāline |
divākarāya dīptāya agnaye mihirāya ca || 3 ||

BP 1.71.4

प्रभाकराय मित्राय नमस्तेऽदितिसम्भव ।
नमो गोपतये नित्यं दिशां च पतये नमः ॥ ॥

Salutation to Prabhākara and Mitra, to you born of Aditi; salutation always to the lord of cows, and salutation to the lord of the directions.

Word-for-word
prabhākarāya — to Prabhākara; mitrāya — to Mitra; namaḥ — salutation; te — to you; aditi-sambhava — born of Aditi; namaḥ — salutation; go-pataye — to lord of cows; nityam — always; diśām pataye — to lord of directions
Transliteration
prabhākarāya mitrāya namas te ’ditisambhava |
namo gopataye nityaṃ diśāṃ ca pataye namaḥ || 4 ||

BP 1.71.5

नमो धात्रे विधात्रे च अर्यम्णे वरुणाय च ।
पूष्णे भगाय मित्राय पर्जन्यायांशवे नमः ॥ ॥

Salutation to Dhātṛ and Vidhātṛ, to Aryaman and Varuṇa; salutation to Pūṣan, Bhaga, Mitra, Parjanya, and Aṃśu.

Word-for-word
namaḥ — salutation; dhātre — to Dhātṛ; vidhātre — to Vidhātṛ; ca — and; aryamṇe — to Aryaman; varuṇāya — to Varuṇa; pūṣṇe — to Pūṣan; bhagāya — to Bhaga; mitrāya — to Mitra; parjanyāya — to Parjanya; aṃśave — to Aṃśu
Transliteration
namo dhātre vidhātre cāryamṇe varuṇāya ca |
pūṣṇe bhagāya mitrāya parjanyāyāṃśave namaḥ || 5 ||

BP 1.71.6

नमो हितकृते नित्यं धर्माय तपनाय च ।
हरये हरिताश्वाय विश्वस्य पतये नमः ॥ ॥

Salutation always to the benefactor, to Dharma, and to Tapana; salutation to Hari, to the one with green horses, and to the lord of the universe.

Word-for-word
namaḥ — salutation; hita-kṛte — to benefactor; nityam — always; dharmāya — to Dharma; tapanāya — to Tapana; haraye — to Hari; harita-aśvāya — to one with green horses; viśvasya pataye — to lord of universe
Transliteration
namo hitakṛte nityaṃ dharmāya tapanāya ca |
haraye haritāśvāya viśvasya pataye namaḥ || 6 ||

BP 1.71.7

विष्णवे ब्रह्मणे नित्यं त्र्यम्बकाय तथात्मने ।
नमस्ते सप्तलोकेश नमस्ते सप्तसप्तये ॥ ॥

Salutation always to Viṣṇu, to Brahmā, to Tryambaka, and to the Self; salutation to you, lord of the seven worlds, and lord of the seven horses.

Word-for-word
viṣṇave — to Viṣṇu; brahmaṇe — to Brahmā; nityam — always; tryambakāya — to Tryambaka; tathā — likewise; ātmane — to the Self; namaḥ te — salutation to you; sapta-loka-īśa — lord of seven worlds; sapta-saptaye — lord of seven horses
Transliteration
viṣṇave brahmaṇe nityaṃ tryambakāya tathātmane |
namas te saptalokeśa namas te saptasaptaye || 7 ||

BP 1.71.8

एकस्मै हि नमस्तुभ्यमेकचक्ररथाय च ।
ज्योतिषां पतये नित्यं सर्वप्राणभृते नमः ॥ ॥

Salutation to you, the One; salutation to the chariot with a single wheel; salutation always to the lord of lights and to the sustainer of all living beings.

Word-for-word
ekasmai — to the One; namaḥ tubhyam — salutation to you; eka-cakra-rathāya — to the chariot with one wheel; jyotiṣām pataye — to lord of lights; nityam — always; sarva-prāṇa-bhṛte — to sustainer of all living beings
Transliteration
ekasmai hi namas tubhyam ekacakrarathāya ca |
jyotiṣāṃ pataye nityaṃ sarvaprāṇabhṛte namaḥ || 8 ||

BP 1.71.9

हिताय सर्वभूतानां शिवायार्तिहराय च ।
नमः पद्मप्रबोधाय नमो वेदादिमूर्तये ॥ ॥

Salutation to the welfare of all beings, to Śiva, and to the remover of affliction; salutation to the awakener of lotuses and to the original form of the Veda.

Word-for-word
hitāya — to welfare; sarva-bhūtānām — of all beings; śivāya — to Śiva; ārti-harāya — to remover of affliction; namaḥ — salutation; padma-prabodhāya — to awakener of lotuses; veda-ādi-mūrtaye — to original form of the Veda
Transliteration
hitāya sarvabhūtānāṃ śivāyārtiharāya ca |
namaḥ padmaprabodhāya namo vedādimūrtaye || 9 ||

BP 1.71.10

काधिजाय नमस्तुभ्यं नमस्तारासुताय च ।
भीमजाय नमस्तुभ्यं पावकाय च वै नमः ॥ ॥

Salutation to Kādhija and to the son of Tārā; salutation to Bhīmaja and likewise to Pāvaka.

Word-for-word
kādhijāya — to Kādhija; namaḥ tubhyam — salutation to you; tārā-sutāya — to son of Tārā; ca — and; bhīma-jāya — to Bhīmaja; namaḥ — salutation; pāvakāya — to Pāvaka; vai — indeed
Transliteration
kādhijāya namas tubhyaṃ namas tārāsutāya ca |
bhīmajāya namas tubhyaṃ pāvakāya ca vai namaḥ || 10 ||

BP 1.71.11

धिषणाय नमो नित्यं नमः कृष्णाय नित्यदा ।
नमोऽस्त्वदितिपुत्राय नमो लक्ष्याय नित्यशः ॥ ॥

Salutation always to Dhiṣaṇā; salutation ever to Kṛṣṇa; salutation to the son of Aditi and always to Lakṣya.

Word-for-word
dhiṣaṇāya — to Dhiṣaṇā; namaḥ — salutation; nityam — always; kṛṣṇāya — to Kṛṣṇa; nityadā — always; aditi-putrāya — to son of Aditi; namaḥ astu — let there be salutation; lakṣyāya — to Lakṣya; nityaśaḥ — always
Transliteration
dhiṣaṇāya namo nityaṃ namas kṛṣṇāya nityadā |
namo ’stv aditiputrāya namo lakṣyāya nityaśaḥ || 11 ||

BP 1.71.12

एतान्यादित्यनामानि मया प्रोक्तानि वै पुरा ।
आराधनाय देवस्य सर्वकामेन सुव्रत ॥ ॥

These names of Āditya were formerly proclaimed by me for the worship of the Deity, with every desire, O faithful one.

Word-for-word
etāni — these; āditya-nāmāni — names of Āditya; mayā — by me; proktāni — proclaimed; vai — indeed; purā — formerly; ārādhanāya — for worship; devasya — of the Deity; sarva-kāmena — with every desire; suvrata — O faithful one
Transliteration
etāny ādityanāmāni mayā proktāni vai purā |
ārādhanāya devasya sarvakāmena suvrata || 12 ||

BP 1.71.13

सायं प्रातः शुचिर्भूत्वा यः पठेत्सुसमाहितः ।
स प्राप्नोत्यखिलान्कामान्यथाहं प्राप्तवान्पुरा ॥ ॥

Whoever, becoming pure, recites them morning and evening with full concentration obtains all desires, just as I formerly obtained them.

Word-for-word
sāyam — in the evening; prātaḥ — in the morning; śuciḥ bhūtvā — becoming pure; yaḥ — who; paṭhet — recites; susamāhitaḥ — fully concentrated; saḥ — he; prāpnoti — obtains; akhilān kāmān — all desires; yathā — as; aham — I; prāptavān — obtained; purā — formerly
Transliteration
sāyaṃ prātaḥ śucir bhūtvā yaḥ paṭhet susamāhitaḥ |
sa prāpnoty akhilān kāmān yathāhaṃ prāptavān purā || 13 ||

BP 1.71.14

प्रसादात्तस्य देवस्य भास्करस्य महात्मनः ।
श्रीकामः श्रियमाप्नोति धर्मार्थी धर्ममाप्नुयात् ॥ ॥

By the grace of that great-souled Deity Bhāskara, one desiring prosperity obtains prosperity, and one desiring dharma attains dharma.

Word-for-word
prasādāt — by grace; tasya devasya — of that Deity; bhāskarasya — of Bhāskara; mahātmanaḥ — great-souled; śrī-kāmaḥ — desiring prosperity; śriyam āpnoti — obtains prosperity; dharma-arthī — desiring dharma; dharmam āpnuyāt — attains dharma
Transliteration
prasādāt tasya devasya bhāskarasya mahātmanaḥ |
śrīkāmaḥ śriyam āpnoti dharmārthī dharmam āpnuyāt || 14 ||

BP 1.71.15

आतुरो मुच्यते रोगाद्बद्धो मुच्येत बन्धनात् ।
राज्यार्थी राज्यमाप्नोति कामार्थी काममाप्नुयात् ॥ ॥

The afflicted is freed from disease; the bound person is freed from bondage; one desiring a kingdom obtains a kingdom, and one desiring an object of desire obtains that desire.

Word-for-word
āturaḥ — afflicted; mucyate — is freed; rogāt — from disease; baddhaḥ — bound; mucyeta — may be freed; bandhanāt — from bondage; rājya-arthī — desiring a kingdom; rājyam āpnoti — obtains a kingdom; kāma-arthī — desiring an object of desire; kāmam āpnuyāt — obtains desire
Transliteration
āturo mucyate rogād baddho mucyeta bandhanāt |
rājyārthī rājyam āpnoti kāmārthī kāmam āpnuyāt || 15 ||

BP 1.71.16

एतज्जप्यं रहस्यं च सन्ध्योपासनमेव च ।
एतेन जपमात्रेण नरः पापात्प्रमुच्यते ॥ ॥

This is a secret recitation and also a twilight worship. By the mere recitation of it, a person is freed from sin.

Word-for-word
etat — this; japyam — recitation; rahasyam — secret; ca — and; sandhyā-upāsanam — twilight worship; eva — indeed; etena — by this; japa-mātreṇa — by mere recitation; naraḥ — person; pāpāt — from sin; pramucyate — is freed
Transliteration
etaj japyam rahasyaṃ ca saṃdhyopāsanam eva ca |
etena japamātreṇa naraḥ pāpāt pramucyate || 16 ||
