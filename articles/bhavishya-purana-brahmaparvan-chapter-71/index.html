Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 71 — The Names of the Sun Taught by Brahmā

BP 1.71.1

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
नामभिः संस्तुतो देवो यैरर्कः परितुष्यति ।
तानि ते कीर्तयाम्येष यथावदनुपूर्वशः ॥ ॥

Brahmā said. I shall recite to you in proper order the names by which the god Arka, when praised, is pleased.

BP 1.71.2

नमः सूर्याय नित्याय रवये कार्यभानवे ।
भास्कराय मतङ्गाय मार्तण्डाय विवस्वते ॥ ॥

Salutation to the eternal Sun, to Ravi, to Kāryabhānu, to Bhāskara, to Mataṅga, to Mārtaṇḍa, and to Vivasvat.

BP 1.71.3

आदित्यायादिदेवाय नमस्ते रश्मिमालिने ।
दिवाकराय दीप्ताय अग्नये मिहिराय च ॥ ॥

Salutation to Āditya, the first god. Salutation to you, garlanded with rays, to the day-maker, the shining one, to Agni, and to Mihira.

BP 1.71.4

प्रभाकराय मित्राय नमस्तेऽदितिसम्भव ।
नमो गोपतये नित्यं दिशां च पतये नमः ॥ ॥

Salutation to Prabhākara and Mitra. Salutation to you, offspring of Aditi. Salutation always to the lord of cows, and salutation to the lord of the directions.

BP 1.71.5

नमो धात्रे विधात्रे च अर्यम्णे वरुणाय च ।
पूष्णे भगाय मित्राय पर्जन्यायांशवे नमः ॥ ॥

Salutation to Dhātṛ and Vidhātṛ, to Aryaman and Varuṇa. Salutation to Pūṣan, Bhaga, Mitra, Parjanya, and Aṃśu.

BP 1.71.6

नमो हितकृते नित्यं धर्माय तपनाय च ।
हरये हरिताश्वाय विश्वस्य पतये नमः ॥ ॥

Salutation always to the benefactor, to righteousness, and to the burner. Salutation to Hari, the green-horsed one, and to the lord of the universe.

BP 1.71.7

विष्णवे ब्रह्मणे नित्यं त्र्यम्बकाय तथात्मने ।
नमस्ते सप्तलोकेश नमस्ते सप्तसप्तये ॥ ॥

Salutation always to Viṣṇu, to Brahmā, to the three-eyed one, and to the Self. Salutation to you, lord of the seven worlds, and lord of the seven horses.

BP 1.71.8

एकस्मै हि नमस्तुभ्यमेकचक्ररथाय च ।
ज्योतिषां पतये नित्यं सर्वप्राणभृते नमः ॥ ॥

Salutation indeed to you, the One, and to the chariot with a single wheel. Salutation always to the lord of lights, and salutation to the sustainer of all living beings.

BP 1.71.9

हिताय सर्वभूतानां शिवायार्तिहराय च ।
नमः पद्मप्रबोधाय नमो वेदादिमूर्तये ॥ ॥

Salutation to the benefactor of all beings, to Śiva, and to the remover of suffering. Salutation to the awakener of lotuses, and salutation to the form that is the beginning of the Vedas.

BP 1.71.10

काधिजाय नमस्तुभ्यं नमस्तारासुताय च ।
भीमजाय नमस्तुभ्यं पावकाय च वै नमः ॥ ॥

Salutation to Kādhija, salutation also to the son of Tārā. Salutation to Bhīmaja, and likewise salutation to the purifier.

BP 1.71.11

धिषणाय नमो नित्यं नमः कृष्णाय नित्यदा ।
नमोऽस्त्वदितिपुत्राय नमो लक्ष्याय नित्यशः ॥ ॥

Salutation always to Dhiṣaṇā, salutation ever to Kṛṣṇa. Salutation to the son of Aditi, and salutation always to the goal.

BP 1.71.12

एतान्यादित्यनामानि मया प्रोक्तानि वै पुरा ।
आराधनाय देवस्य सर्वकामेन सुव्रत ॥ ॥

These names of the Sun were formerly recited by me for the worship of the god and the fulfillment of every desire, O keeper of good vows.

BP 1.71.13

सायं प्रातः शुचिर्भूत्वा यः पठेत्सुसमाहितः ।
स प्राप्नोत्यखिलान्कामान्यथाहं प्राप्तवान्पुरा ॥ ॥

Whoever, having become pure, recites them with full concentration in the evening and morning obtains all desires, as I obtained them formerly.

BP 1.71.14

प्रसादात्तस्य देवस्य भास्करस्य महात्मनः ।
श्रीकामः श्रियमाप्नोति धर्मार्थी धर्ममाप्नुयात् ॥ ॥

By the grace of that great-souled Sun, one who desires prosperity obtains prosperity, and one who seeks righteousness obtains righteousness.

BP 1.71.15

आतुरो मुच्यते रोगाद्बद्धो मुच्येत बन्धनात् ।
राज्यार्थी राज्यमाप्नोति कामार्थी काममाप्नुयात् ॥ ॥

The sick person is freed from illness, the bound person is released from bondage. One who desires a kingdom obtains a kingdom, and one who desires an object of desire obtains it.

BP 1.71.16

एतज्जप्यं रहस्यं च सन्ध्योपासनमेव च ।
एतेन जपमात्रेण नरः पापात्प्रमुच्यते ॥ ॥

This is a secret recitation and also worship at twilight. By the mere recitation of this, a person is released from sin.
