BP 3.2.23.1

व्यास उवाच ।
इति श्रुत्वा तु मुनयो विशालानगरीस्थिताः ।
स्नात्वा केदारकुण्डे ते मनसा पूजयञ्छिवम् ॥ 1 ॥

Vyāsa said: Having heard this, the sages who lived in Viśālā bathed at Kedāra's pool and worshipped Śiva in their minds.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
व्यास vyāsa Vyāsa
उवाच uvāca said
इति iti thus
श्रुत्वा śrutvā having heard
तु tu but
मुनयो munayo the sages
विशालानगरीस्थिताः viśālānagarīsthitāḥ staying in the city of Viśālā
स्नात्वा snātvā having bathed
केदारकुण्डे kedārakuṇḍe at Kedāra's pool
ते te they
मनसा manasā in her mind
पूजयञ्छिवम् pūjayañchivam worshipped Śiva
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

vyāsa uvāca | iti śrutvā tu munayo viśālānagarīsthitāḥ | snātvā kedārakuṇḍe te manasā pūjayañchivam || 1 ||

व्यतीतयन् vyatītayan they passed
Transliteration

BP 3.2.23.3

एतस्मिन्नंतरे राजा विक्रमादित्यभूपतिः ।
नत्वा मुनीन्समाधिस्थांस्तुष्टाव परया गिरा ॥ 3 ॥

At that time King Vikramāditya arrived. He bowed to the sages absorbed in meditation and praised them in a reverent voice.

BP 3.2.23.5

वाजिमेधं च नृपतेः कारयामस्त्वदाज्ञया ।

At your command, we shall arrange a Vājimedha for the king. You should remain at Cakratīrtha, devoted to meditation.

BP 3.2.23.6

विधिना कारयामास हयमेधं महामखम् ॥ 6 ॥

Sūta agreed and returned with them. In due form he arranged the great horse sacrifice.

महामखम् mahāmakham a great rite

Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

BP 3.2.23.7

पूर्वे तु कपिलस्थानं दक्षिणे सेतुबन्धनम् ।
पश्चिमे सिंधुनद्यन्तं चोत्तरे बदरीवनम् ॥ 7 ॥

The horse traveled east to Kapila's place, south to the bridge at Setu, west to the Sindhu River's end, and north to the Badarī forest.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

सेतुबन्धनम् setubandhanam the bridge at Setu
पश्चिमे paścime in the west
सिंधुनद्यन्तं siṃdhunadyantaṃ the end of the Sindhu River
चोत्तरे cottare and in the north
बदरीवनम् badarīvanam the Badarī forest
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

BP 3.2.23.8

हयो जगाम तरसा ततः क्षिप्रां नदीं गतः ।
त्यक्त्वा कलेवरं वह्नौ स्वर्गलोकमतो ययौ ॥ 8 ॥

The horse sped on to the Kṣiprā River; there it gave up its body in the fire and went from there to heaven.

BP 3.2.23.9

नृपयज्ञे सुराः सर्वे सपत्नीकाः समागताः ।

All the gods, with their wives, assembled at the king's sacrifice. The Moon did not come, and the king became troubled.

BP 3.2.23.10

At the end of the sacrifice, the king gave many gifts and, accompanied by the Vetāla, went to the Moon's realm. The Moon was pleased.

BP 3.2.23.11

भोभो राजन्महाभाग कलौ प्राप्ते भयंकरे ।
मद्गतिर्भूतले नास्ति तस्मान्नायामि तेऽन्तिकम् ॥ 11 ॥

The Moon said: O great king, when the terrible Kali age arrives, I will have no way to come to earth; therefore I cannot come near you.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

कलौ kalau in the Kali age
प्राप्ते prāpte having arrived
भयंकरे bhayaṃkare dreadful
मद्गतिर्भूतले madgatirbhūtale my path on earth
नास्ति nāsti there is not
तस्मान्नायामि tasmānnāyāmi therefore I do not come
तेऽन्तिकम् te'ntikam near you
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

bhobho rājanmahābhāga kalau prāpte bhayaṃkare | madgatirbhūtale nāsti tasmānnāyāmi te'ntikam || 11 ||

BP 3.2.23.12

दत्त्वा सुधामयं तोयं चंद्रश्चान्तर्दधे पुनः ।
ज्ञात्वेन्द्रस्तत्र संप्राप्य द्विजरूपी ह्ययाचयत् ॥ 12 ॥

Then the Moon gave him nectar-like water and disappeared. Indra learned of it, arrived there in the form of a Brahmin, and begged for it.

BP 3.2.23.13

The king gave that nectar to Śakra, who went to heaven. As a result, the king's lifespan became equal to a hundred thousand years.

स्वर्गमुमुपागतः svargamumupāgataḥ went to heaven
तेन tena by him
तस्य tasya of him
फलं phalaṃ the reward
जातमायुर्लक्ष jātamāyurlakṣa his lifespan became a hundred thousand
समं samaṃ together
ह्यभूत् hyabhūt took place
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

BP 3.2.23.14
