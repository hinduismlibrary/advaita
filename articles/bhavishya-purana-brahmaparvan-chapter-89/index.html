Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 89 — The Sādityābhimukha Observance

BP 1.89.1

ब्रह्मोवाच । कृष्णपक्षस्य सप्तम्यां माघमासे भवेत्तु यः । सादित्याभिमुखो ज्ञेयः शृणु चास्य विधिं परम् ॥ ॥

Brahmā said: Whoever is [engaged] on the seventh day of the dark fortnight in the month of Māgha, facing the Sun, is to be understood [as observing this rite]. Hear its supreme procedure.

Word-for-word
brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said;
kṛṣṇa-pakṣasya — of the dark fortnight;
saptamyām — on the seventh day;
māgha-māse — in the month of Māgha;
bhavet — may be, occurs;
tu — indeed;
yaḥ — whoever;
sa-āditya-abhimukhaḥ — facing the Sun;
jñeyaḥ — to be understood;
śṛṇu — hear;
ca — and;
asya — of this;
vidhim — procedure;
param — supreme.
Transliteration
brahmovāca | kṛṣṇapakṣasya saptamyāṃ māghamāse bhavettu yaḥ | sādityābhimukho jñeyaḥ śṛṇu cāsya vidhiṃ param || 1 ||

BP 1.89.2

कृत्वैकभक्तं कृष्णस्य वारे त्रिपुरसूदन । प्रातः कृत्वा ततः स्नानं पूजयित्वा दिवाकरम् ॥ ॥

Having taken a single meal on the day of Kṛṣṇa, O destroyer of Tripura, one should then bathe in the morning and worship Divākara.

Word-for-word
kṛtvā — having performed;
eka-bhaktam — one meal;
kṛṣṇasya — of Kṛṣṇa, the dark [fortnight];
vāre — on the day;
tripura-sūdana — O destroyer of Tripura;
prātaḥ — in the morning;
kṛtvā — having performed;
tataḥ — then;
snānam — bathing;
pūjayitvā — having worshiped;
divākaram — Divākara, the Sun.
Transliteration
kṛtvaikabhaktaṃ kṛṣṇasya vāre tripurasūdana | prātaḥ kṛtvā tataḥ snānaṃ pūjayitvā divākaram || 2 ||

BP 1.89.3

आदित्याभिमुखस्तिष्ठेद्यावदस्तमनं रवेः । जपमानो महाश्वेतां लाभमाश्रित्य सुव्रत ॥ ॥

O well-disciplined one, he should stand facing Āditya until the setting of Ravi, reciting Mahāśvetā and taking refuge in [its] attainment.

Word-for-word
āditya-abhimukhaḥ — facing Āditya;
tiṣṭhet — should stand;
yāvat — until;
astamanam — setting;
raveḥ — of Ravi;
japamānaḥ — reciting;
mahāśvetām — Mahāśvetā;
lābham — attainment, gain;
āśritya — taking refuge in;
suvrata — O well-disciplined one.
Transliteration
ādityābhimukhastiṣṭhedyāvadastamanaṃ raveḥ | japamāno mahāśvetāṃ lābhamāśritya suvrata || 3 ||

BP 1.89.4

चतुर्हस्तमृजं श्लक्ष्णमव्रणं सुसमं दृढम् । रक्तचन्दनवृक्षस्य स्तम्भं कृत्वा गणाधिप ॥ ॥

O lord of hosts, he should make a four-cubit, straight, smooth, flawless, even, and firm post from a red-sandalwood tree.

Word-for-word
catuḥ-hastam — four cubits [in measure];
ṛjam — straight;
ślakṣṇam — smooth;
avraṇam — without blemish;
su-samam — very even;
dṛḍham — firm;
rakta-candana-vṛkṣasya — of a red-sandalwood tree;
stambham — a post;
kṛtvā — having made;
gaṇādhipa — O lord of hosts.
Transliteration
caturhastamṛjaṃ ślakṣṇamavraṇaṃ susamaṃ dṛḍham | raktacandanavṛkṣasya stambhaṃ kṛtvā gaṇādhipa || 4 ||

BP 1.89.5

तमाश्रित्य महाभक्त्या देवदेवं दिवाकरम् । पश्यमानो जपञ्श्वेतां तिष्ठेदस्तमनाद्रवेः ॥ ॥

Leaning upon it with great devotion, looking at the Sun, the God of gods, and reciting Śvetā, he should stand until Ravi sets.

Word-for-word
tam — it;
āśritya — having leaned upon, taking support from;
mahā-bhaktyā — with great devotion;
deva-devam — God of gods;
divākaram — the Sun;
paśyamānaḥ — looking at;
japan — reciting;
śvetām — Śvetā, the white [formula];
tiṣṭhet — should stand;
astamanāt — until setting;
raveḥ — of Ravi.
Transliteration
tamāśritya mahābhaktyā devadevaṃ divākaram | paśyamāno japañśvetāṃ tiṣṭhedastamanādraveḥ || 5 ||

BP 1.89.6

गन्धपुष्पोपहारैस्तु पूजयित्वा दिवाकरम् । ब्राह्मणे दक्षिणां दत्त्वा ततो भुञ्जीत वाग्यतः ॥ ॥

Having worshiped Divākara with fragrances, flowers, and offerings, and having given a gift to a Brāhmaṇa, he should then eat with his speech restrained.

Word-for-word
gandha-puṣpa-upahāraiḥ — with fragrances, flowers, and offerings;
tu — indeed;
pūjayitvā — having worshiped;
divākaram — Divākara;
brāhmaṇe — to a Brāhmaṇa;
dakṣiṇām — a gift, sacrificial fee;
dattvā — having given;
tataḥ — then;
bhuñjīta — should eat;
vāg-yataḥ — with speech restrained.
Transliteration
gandhapuṣpopahāraistu pūjayitvā divākaram | brāhmaṇe dakṣiṇāṃ dattvā tato bhuñjīta vāgyataḥ || 6 ||

BP 1.89.7

इत्थमेतं तु यः कुर्यादादित्यप्रीतये नरः । भानुमांस्तस्य प्रीतः स्यात्सर्वं प्रीतो ददादि हि ॥ ॥

BP 1.89.8

धनं धान्यं तथा पुत्रमारोग्यं भार्गवो यशः । तस्मात्सम्पूजयेदत्र गीर्वाणाधिपतिं हर ॥ ॥

He grants wealth, grain, a son, health, and fame from Bhārgava. Therefore worship here the Lord of the gods, O Hara.
