Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 89 — The Observance of Facing the Sun

BP 1.89.1

ब्रह्मोवाच । कृष्णपक्षस्य सप्तम्यां माघमासे भवेत्तु यः । सादित्याभिमुखो ज्ञेयः शृणु चास्य विधिं परम् ॥ ॥

Brahmā said. Whoever faces the Sun on the seventh day of the dark fortnight in the month of Māgha is known as the Sun Facing one. Hear its supreme procedure.

brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said;
kṛṣṇa-pakṣasya — of the dark fortnight;
saptamyām — on the seventh day;
māgha-māse — in the month of Māgha;
bhavet — may be, occurs;
tu — indeed;
yaḥ — whoever;
sa-āditya-abhimukhaḥ — facing the Sun;
jñeyaḥ — to be understood;
śṛṇu — hear;
ca — and;
asya — of this;
vidhim — procedure;
param — supreme.

BP 1.89.2

कृत्वैकभक्तं कृष्णस्य वारे त्रिपुरसूदन । प्रातः कृत्वा ततः स्नानं पूजयित्वा दिवाकरम् ॥ ॥

Having eaten one meal on the day of Kṛṣṇa, O destroyer of Tripura, he should bathe in the morning and then worship the Sun.

kṛtvā — having performed;
eka-bhaktam — one meal;
kṛṣṇasya — of Kṛṣṇa, the dark [fortnight];
vāre — on the day;
tripura-sūdana — O destroyer of Tripura;
prātaḥ — in the morning;
kṛtvā — having performed;
tataḥ — then;
snānam — bathing;
pūjayitvā — having worshiped;
divākaram — Divākara, the Sun.

BP 1.89.3

आदित्याभिमुखस्तिष्ठेद्यावदस्तमनं रवेः । जपमानो महाश्वेतां लाभमाश्रित्य सुव्रत ॥ ॥

Facing the Sun, he should stand until sunset, reciting the Great White One and seeking benefit, O well vowed one.

āditya-abhimukhaḥ — facing Āditya;
tiṣṭhet — should stand;
yāvat — until;
astamanam — setting;
raveḥ — of Ravi;
japamānaḥ — reciting;
mahāśvetām — Mahāśvetā;
lābham — attainment, gain;
āśritya — taking refuge in;
suvrata — O well-disciplined one.

BP 1.89.4

चतुर्हस्तमृजं श्लक्ष्णमव्रणं सुसमं दृढम् । रक्तचन्दनवृक्षस्य स्तम्भं कृत्वा गणाधिप ॥ ॥

He should make a post of red sandalwood, four cubits long, straight, smooth, flawless, even, and firm, O chief of hosts.

catuḥ-hastam — four cubits [in measure];
ṛjam — straight;
ślakṣṇam — smooth;
avraṇam — without blemish;
su-samam — very even;
dṛḍham — firm;
rakta-candana-vṛkṣasya — of a red-sandalwood tree;
stambham — a post;
kṛtvā — having made;
gaṇādhipa — O lord of hosts.

BP 1.89.5

तमाश्रित्य महाभक्त्या देवदेवं दिवाकरम् । पश्यमानो जपञ्श्वेतां तिष्ठेदस्तमनाद्रवेः ॥ ॥

Leaning on it with great devotion, looking at the Sun, god of gods, and reciting the White One, he should stand until sunset.

tam — it;
āśritya — having leaned upon, taking support from;
mahā-bhaktyā — with great devotion;
deva-devam — God of gods;
divākaram — the Sun;
paśyamānaḥ — looking at;
japan — reciting;
śvetām — Śvetā, the white [formula];
tiṣṭhet — should stand;
astamanāt — until setting;
raveḥ — of Ravi.

BP 1.89.6

गन्धपुष्पोपहारैस्तु पूजयित्वा दिवाकरम् । ब्राह्मणे दक्षिणां दत्त्वा ततो भुञ्जीत वाग्यतः ॥ ॥

Having worshipped the Sun with perfumes, flowers, and offerings, and given a gift to a Brahmin, he should then eat with speech restrained.

gandha-puṣpa-upahāraiḥ — with fragrances, flowers, and offerings;
tu — indeed;
pūjayitvā — having worshiped;
divākaram — Divākara;
brāhmaṇe — to a Brāhmaṇa;
dakṣiṇām — a gift, sacrificial fee;
dattvā — having given;
tataḥ — then;
bhuñjīta — should eat;
vāg-yataḥ — with speech restrained.

BP 1.89.7

इत्थमेतं तु यः कुर्यादादित्यप्रीतये नरः । भानुमांस्तस्य प्रीतः स्यात्सर्वं प्रीतो ददादि हि ॥ ॥

Whoever performs this for the pleasure of the Sun, the Sun is pleased with him and, pleased, indeed grants everything.

ittham — thus;
etam — this;
tu — indeed;
yaḥ — whoever;
kuryāt — should perform;
āditya-prītaye — for the pleasure of Āditya;
naraḥ — a person;
bhānumān — Bhānumān;
tasya — with him, toward him;
prītaḥ — pleased;
syāt — becomes;
sarvam — everything;
prītaḥ — being pleased;
dadāti — gives;
hi — indeed.

BP 1.89.8

धनं धान्यं तथा पुत्रमारोग्यं भार्गवो यशः । तस्मात्सम्पूजयेदत्र गीर्वाणाधिपतिं हर ॥ ॥

He gives wealth, grain, a son, health, and fame, O Bhārgava. Therefore one should worship here the Lord of the gods, O Hara.

dhanam — wealth;
dhānyam — grain;
tathā — likewise;
putram — a son;
ārogyam — health;
bhārgavaḥ — Bhārgava, Venus;
yaśaḥ — fame;
tasmāt — therefore;
sampūjayet — should fully worship;
atra — here;
gīrvāṇa-adhipatim — Lord of the gods;
hara — O Hara.
