ŚB 2.10.45

नास्य कर्मणि जन्मादौ परस्यानुविधीयते ।
कर्तृत्वप्रतिषेधार्थं माययारोपितं हि तत् ॥ ४५ ॥

In the birth and other transformations of the universe, material agency does not truly belong to the Supreme. Such agency is provisionally attributed through māyā precisely so that, in the final understanding, material doership may be denied of Him.

Word-for-word

na — not; asya karmaṇi — in this cosmic action; janma-ādau — beginning with creation/birth; parasya — of the Supreme; anuvidhīyate — is ultimately prescribed/asserted; kartṛtva-pratiṣedha-artham — for denying material agency; māyayā — through māyā; āropitam — attributed; hi — indeed; tat — that.

Transliteration
nāsya karmaṇi janmādau
parasyānuvidhīyate
kartṛtva-pratiṣedhārthaṁ
māyayāropitaṁ hi tat
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तत् किम् ? यतः अस्य विश्वस्य जन्मादौ कर्मणि परस्येश्वरस्येत्थम्-भावः कर्तृत्वं नास्ति । श्रुत्यापि तात्पर्येण न प्रतिपाद्यते । किन्त्व् अनुविधीयतेऽनूद्यते । किम्-अर्थम् ? कर्तृत्व-प्रतिषेधार्थम् । हि यतो माययारोपितं प्रकाशितम् ॥४५॥

tat kim? yataḥ asya viśvasya janmādau karmaṇi parasyeśvarasyettham-bhāvaḥ kartṛtvaṁ nāsti | śrutyāpi tātparyeṇa na pratipādyate | kintv anuvidhīyate ’nūdyate | kim-artham? kartṛtva-pratiṣedhārtham | hi yato māyayāropitaṁ prakāśitam ||45||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. kartṛtvam nāsti — material doership does not belong to Him; tātparyeṇa na pratipādyate — it is not the final purport; anūdyate — provisionally restated; kartṛtva-pratiṣedhārtham — so material agency can ultimately be denied; māyayā āropitam — attributed through māyā.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. Scripture can speak of the Lord as cosmic agent without reducing Him to material doership. The attribution functions pedagogically and is finally qualified by His transcendence.

ŚB 2.10.46

अयं तु ब्रह्मणः कल्पः सविकल्प उदाहृतः ।
विधिः साधारणो यत्र सर्गाः प्राकृतवैकृताः ॥ ४६ ॥

This account has presented Brahmā’s great kalpa together with its subsidiary divisions. The procedure described here is general: it includes the primary creations of material nature and the subsequent, secondary creations.

Word-for-word

ayam — this; brahmaṇaḥ kalpaḥ — Brahmā’s kalpa; sa-vikalpaḥ — together with subsidiary divisions; udāhṛtaḥ — has been described; vidhiḥ — procedure; sādhāraṇaḥ — generally applicable; yatra — in which; sargāḥ — creations; prākṛta — primary/material; vaikṛtāḥ — secondary/modified.

Transliteration
ayaṁ tu brahmaṇaḥ kalpaḥ
sa-vikalpa udāhṛtaḥ
vidhiḥ sādhāraṇo yatra
sargāḥ prākṛta-vaikṛtāḥ
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

उक्तम् अर्थम् उपसंहरति—अयं त्व् इति । ब्रह्मणः सम्बन्धी कल्पो महा-कल्पो विकल्पोऽवान्तरस् तत्-सहित उदाहरणत्वेन संक्षेपत उक्तः । कथम्भूतः ? यत्र महा-कल्पे प्राकृता महद्-आदि-सर्गा अवान्तर-कल्पे च वैकृताः । स्थावरा विसर्गा इत्य् अयं विधिः प्रकारोऽन्यैर् महा-कल्पादिभिः साधारणः ॥४६॥

uktam artham upasaṁharati—ayaṁ tv iti | brahmaṇaḥ sambandhī kalpo mahā-kalpo vikalpo ’vāntaras tat-sahita udāharaṇatvena saṅkṣepata uktaḥ | kathambhūtaḥ? yatra mahā-kalpe prākṛtā mahad-ādi-sargā avāntara-kalpe ca vaikṛtāḥ | sthāvarā visargā ity ayaṁ vidhiḥ prakāro ’nyair mahā-kalpādibhiḥ sādhāraṇaḥ ||46||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. mahā-kalpaḥ — great cosmic cycle; vikalpaḥ avāntaraḥ — subsidiary cycle; prākṛtāḥ — primary creations beginning with mahat; vaikṛtāḥ — subsequent creations; sādhāraṇaḥ — a procedure shared by other kalpas.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. Śrīdhara closes the cosmological exposition by distinguishing the great kalpa from subsidiary cycles and primary creation from later secondary manifestation.

ŚB 2.10.47

परिमाणं च कालस्य कल्पलक्षणविग्रहम् ।
यथा पुरस्ताद् व्याख्यास्ये पाद्मं कल्पमथो शृणु ॥ ४७ ॥

Later I shall explain in detail the measures of time, the defining structure of a kalpa and its subsidiary divisions. Now hear the account of the Pādma Kalpa.

Word-for-word

parimāṇam — measure; kālasya — of time; kalpa-lakṣaṇa-vigraham — the defining structure and divisions of a kalpa; yathā — properly/in detail; purastāt — later, ahead; vyākhyāsye — I shall explain; pādmam kalpam — Pādma Kalpa; athaḥ śṛṇu — now hear.

Transliteration
parimāṇaṁ ca kālasya
kalpa-lakṣaṇa-vigraham
yathā purastād vyākhyāsye
pādmaṁ kalpam atho śṛṇu
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

वक्ष्यमाणं विस्तरं प्रतिजानीते । परिमाणं स्थूलं सूक्ष्मं च कल्पस्य लक्षणम् इयान् एवं-रूप इति तद्-विग्रहम् अवान्तर-कल्पं मन्वन्तरादि-रूपं विभागं च यथावद् विस्तरेण पुरस्तात् तृतीय-स्कन्धे व्याख्यास्यामि । तत्र पाद्मं कल्पम् अथो इति कार्त्स्न्येन व्याख्यायमानं शृणु ॥४७॥

vakṣyamāṇaṁ vistaraṁ pratijānīte | parimāṇaṁ sthūlaṁ sūkṣmaṁ ca kalpasya lakṣaṇam iyān evaṁ-rūpa iti tad-vigraham avāntara-kalpaṁ manvantarādi-rūpaṁ vibhāgaṁ ca yathāvad vistareṇa purastāt tṛtīya-skandhe vyākhyāsyāmi | tatra pādmaṁ kalpam atho iti kārtsnyena vyākhyāyamānaṁ śṛṇu ||47||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. parimāṇam — gross and subtle measures of time; kalpasya lakṣaṇam — definition of the kalpa; avāntara-kalpam — subsidiary kalpas; manvantara-ādi-rūpam vibhāgam — divisions such as manvantaras; tṛtīya-skandhe — to be explained in the Third Canto.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. The Second Canto points forward: the detailed reckoning of cosmic time and the Pādma Kalpa will be unfolded in the Third Canto.
