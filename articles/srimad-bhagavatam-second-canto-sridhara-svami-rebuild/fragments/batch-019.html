ŚB 2.9.43

तुष्टं निशाम्य पितरं लोकानां प्रपितामहम् ।
देवर्षिः परिपप्रच्छ भवान् यन्मानुपृच्छति ॥ ४३ ॥

Seeing and understanding that his father Brahmā—the great-grandfather of the worlds—was pleased, the divine sage Nārada questioned him thoroughly concerning the same matters that you are now asking from me.

Word-for-word

tuṣṭam — satisfied, pleased; niśāmya — seeing and understanding; pitaram — his father; lokānām prapitāmaham — the great-grandfather of the worlds; devarṣiḥ — the divine sage Nārada; paripapraccha — questioned thoroughly; bhavān — you; yat — what; mā — me; anupṛcchati — are asking in succession.

Transliteration
tuṣṭaṁ niśāmya pitaraṁ
lokānāṁ prapitāmaham
devarṣiḥ paripapraccha
bhavān yan mānupṛcchati
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

निशाम्य दृष्ट्वा ज्ञात्वेत्य् अर्थः । मा माम् ॥४२॥

niśāmya dṛṣṭvā jñātvety arthaḥ | mā mām ||42||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. niśāmya — having observed; dṛṣṭvā — having seen; jñātvā — having understood; mā — the form here means mām, “me.”

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. Nārada waits until Brahmā is pleased and receptive, then asks fully. Śukadeva explicitly links Nārada’s inquiry with Parīkṣit’s present questions, establishing the continuity of the teaching.

ŚB 2.9.44

तस्मा इदं भागवतं पुराणं दशलक्षणम् ।
प्रोक्तं भगवता प्राह प्रीतः पुत्राय भूतकृत् ॥ ४४ ॥

Then Brahmā, creator of beings, joyfully taught his son this Bhāgavata Purāṇa with its ten defining subjects—the teaching that Bhagavān Himself had first spoken in condensed form.

Word-for-word

tasmai — to him, Nārada; idam — this; bhāgavatam purāṇam — the Bhāgavata Purāṇa; daśa-lakṣaṇam — possessing ten characteristics or topics; proktam — spoken; bhagavatā — by Bhagavān; prāha — he taught; prītaḥ — pleased; putrāya — to his son; bhūta-kṛt — the creator of beings, Brahmā.

Transliteration
tasmā idaṁ bhāgavataṁ
purāṇaṁ daśa-lakṣaṇam
proktaṁ bhagavatā prāha
prītaḥ putrāya bhūta-kṛt
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

भगवता चतुः-श्लोक्या संक्षेपेण प्रोक्तं विस्तरेण प्राह । दश-लक्षणानि लक्षणीया अर्था विद्यन्ते यस्मिंस् तत् ॥४३॥

bhagavatā catuḥ-ślokyā saṅkṣepeṇa proktaṁ vistareṇa prāha | daśa-lakṣaṇāni lakṣaṇīyā arthā vidyante yasmiṁs tat ||43||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. bhagavatā catuḥ-ślokyā — by Bhagavān in the four-śloka teaching; saṅkṣepeṇa proktam — spoken in condensed form; vistareṇa prāha — Brahmā explained it expansively; daśa-lakṣaṇāni — ten defining topics; lakṣaṇīyā arthāḥ — the subjects to be characterized; vidyante yasmin — which are contained within it.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. The four verses are the condensed seed; Brahmā unfolds that seed expansively as the Bhāgavata. Its “ten characteristics” are not merely ten labels but ten great subjects whose meaning the Purāṇa develops.

ŚB 2.9.45

नारदः प्राह मुनये सरस्वत्यास्तटे नृप ।
ध्यायते ब्रह्म परमं व्यासायामिततेजसे ॥ ४५ ॥

O King, Nārada in turn spoke this teaching to the immeasurably powerful sage Vyāsa on the bank of the Sarasvatī, while Vyāsa was meditating upon the Supreme Absolute Reality.

Word-for-word

nāradaḥ — Nārada; prāha — spoke, taught; munaye — to the sage; sarasvatyāḥ taṭe — on the bank of the Sarasvatī; nṛpa — O King; dhyāyate — to one meditating; brahma paramam — the Supreme Absolute Reality; vyāsāya — to Vyāsa; amita-tejase — of immeasurable spiritual power.

Transliteration
nāradaḥ prāha munaye
sarasvatyās taṭe nṛpa
dhyāyate brahma paramaṁ
vyāsāyāmita-tejase
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तत् सम्प्रदायतो भागवतं मया ज्ञातम् इत्य् आशयेनाह—नारद इति ॥४४॥

tat sampradāyato bhāgavataṁ mayā jñātam ity āśayenāha—nārada iti ||44||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. sampradāyataḥ — through disciplic transmission; bhāgavatam — the Bhāgavata; mayā jñātam — has been known by me, Śukadeva; ity āśayena — with this intention; āha — he states the lineage beginning here with Nārada’s transmission to Vyāsa.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. The point of naming the transmission is epistemic: Śukadeva is explaining how the Bhāgavata reached him through a living sampradāya—Bhagavān to Brahmā, Brahmā to Nārada, and Nārada to Vyāsa.

ŚB 2.9.46

यदुताहं त्वया पृष्टो वैराजात् पुरुषादिदम् ।
यथासीत्तदुपाख्यास्ते प्रश्नानन्यांश्च कृत्स्नशः ॥ ४६ ॥

You asked me how this universe arose in relation to the virāṭ-puruṣa. I shall now explain that fully and in its proper manner, together with all your other questions.

Word-for-word

yat uta — now, concerning what; aham — I; tvayā pṛṣṭaḥ — was asked by you; vairājāt puruṣāt — from or in relation to the cosmic Puruṣa; idam — this universe; yathā āsīt — how it came to be; tat — that; upākhyāsye — I shall explain; te — to you; praśnān anyān ca — and the other questions; kṛtsnaśaḥ — completely, in full detail.

Transliteration
yad utāhaṁ tvayā pṛṣṭo
vairājāt puruṣād idam
yathāsīt tad upākhyāste
praśnān anyāṁś ca kṛtsnaśaḥ
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

भागवत-व्याख्यानेनैव त्वत्-प्रश्नानाम् उत्तरं दास्यामीत्य् आह—यद् उतेति । पुरुषावयवैर्लोकाः स-पालाः पूर्व-कल्पिताः इत्य्-आदिना वैराजात् पुरुषाद् इदं विश्वं कथम् आसीद् इति यद् अहं त्वया पृष्टः तद् यथावद् उपाख्यास्यामि शृण्व् इति ॥४५॥

bhāgavata-vyākhyānenaiva tvat-praśnānām uttaraṁ dāsyāmīty āha—yad uteti | puruṣāvayavair lokāḥ sa-pālāḥ pūrva-kalpitāḥ ity-ādinā vairājāt puruṣād idaṁ viśvaṁ katham āsīd iti yad ahaṁ tvayā pṛṣṭaḥ tad yathāvad upākhyāsyāmi śṛṇv iti ||45||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. bhāgavata-vyākhyānena eva — precisely by explaining the Bhāgavata; tvat-praśnānām uttaram dāsyāmi — I shall answer your questions; vairājāt puruṣāt — concerning the cosmic Person; idaṁ viśvaṁ katham āsīt — how this universe came to be; yathāvat upākhyāsyāmi — I shall explain it exactly as it is; śṛṇu — therefore hear.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. Chapter Nine closes by returning directly to Parīkṣit’s question in 2.8.11. Śukadeva will answer it—and the remaining questions—not by leaving the Bhāgavata framework, but by expanding the Bhāgavata itself.
