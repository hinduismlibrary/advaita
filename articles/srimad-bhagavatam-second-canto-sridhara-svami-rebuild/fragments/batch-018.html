ŚB 2.9.33

अहमेवासमेवाग्रे नान्यद् यत् सदसत् परम् ।
पश्चादहं यदेतच्च योऽवशिष्येत सोऽस्म्यहम् ॥ ३३ ॥

I alone existed before creation; apart from Me there was nothing—neither the manifest effect, nor the unmanifest cause, nor that which stands beyond them. After creation I remain; this entire universe also rests in Me, and when all is withdrawn, I alone remain.

Word-for-word

aham eva — I alone; āsam eva — certainly existed; agre — before creation; na anyat — nothing else; yat sat — what is manifest or gross; asat — the unmanifest or subtle cause; param — that which is beyond or causal to both; paścāt — afterward, after creation; aham — I; yat etat ca — and whatever this universe is; yaḥ avaśiṣyeta — that which remains after dissolution; saḥ asmi aham — that too am I.

Transliteration
aham evāsam evāgre
nānyad yat sad-asat param
paścād ahaṁ yad etac ca
yo ’vaśiṣyeta so ’smy aham
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

एतद् एव सम्यग् उपदिशन् यावान् इत्य् अस्यार्थं स्फुटयति—अहम् इति । अहम् एवाग्रे सृष्टेः पूर्वम् आसं स्थितः, नान्यत् किञ्चित्, यत्सत् स्थूलम् असत् सूक्ष्मं परं तयोः कारणं प्रधानम्, तस्याप्य् अन्तर्मुखतया तदा मय्य् एव लीनत्वात् । अहं च तदा आसम् एव केवलं न चान्यद् अकरवम् । पश्चात् सृष्टेर् अनन्तरम् अप्य् अहम् एवास्मि । यद् एतद् विश्वं तद् अप्य् अहम् अस्मि । प्रलये योऽवशिष्येत सोऽप्य् अहम् एव । अनेन चानाद्य्-अनन्तत्वाद् अद्वितीयत्वाच् च परिपूर्णोऽहम् इत्य् उक्तं भवति ॥३२॥

etad eva samyag upadiśan yāvān ity asyārthaṁ sphuṭayati—aham iti | aham evāgre sṛṣṭeḥ pūrvam āsaṁ sthitaḥ, nānyat kiñcit, yat sat sthūlam asat sūkṣmaṁ paraṁ tayoḥ kāraṇaṁ pradhānam, tasyāpy antarmukhatayā tadā mayy eva līnatvāt | ahaṁ ca tadā āsam eva kevalaṁ na cānyad akaravam | paścāt sṛṣṭer anantaram apy aham evāsmi | yad etad viśvaṁ tad apy aham asmi | pralaye yo ’vaśiṣyeta so ’py aham eva | anena cānādy-anantatvād advitīyatvāc ca paripūrṇo ’ham ity uktaṁ bhavati ||32||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. agre sṛṣṭeḥ pūrvam — before creation; sat sthūlam — the manifest, gross effect; asat sūkṣmam — the unmanifest, subtle state; param ... pradhānam — the primal cause beyond them; mayy eva līnatvāt — because all was then dissolved within Me; sṛṣṭer anantaram api aham eva asmi — even after creation I remain; pralaye — at dissolution; anādi-anantatvāt — because I am beginningless and endless; advitīyatvāt — because I am without a second; paripūrṇaḥ — complete in fullness.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. Śrīdhara reads the verse as a declaration of Bhagavān’s unbroken reality through creation, maintenance and dissolution. Gross effect, subtle cause and primordial matter were latent in Him before manifestation; He remains after manifestation and remains alone when it is withdrawn. Thus His fullness is beginningless, endless and nondependent.

ŚB 2.9.34

ऋतेऽर्थं यत् प्रतीयेत न प्रतीयेत चात्मनि ।
तद्विद्यादात्मनो मायां यथाभासो यथा तमः ॥ ३४ ॥

Whatever appears to possess reality apart from its true foundation in Me, or whatever truly exists yet is not perceived in Me, should be understood as My Māyā—like an illusory appearance, or like darkness that conceals what is present.

Word-for-word

ṛte artham — apart from the real object or true basis; yat pratīyeta — whatever appears; na pratīyeta ca — and whatever is not perceived; ātmani — in the Self, the supreme foundation; tat vidyāt — one should know that; ātmanaḥ māyām — as the Māyā of the Supreme Self; yathā ābhāsaḥ — like an illusory appearance or reflected semblance; yathā tamaḥ — like darkness.

Transliteration
ṛte ’rthaṁ yat pratīyeta
na pratīyeta cātmani
tad vidyād ātmano māyāṁ
yathābhāso yathā tamaḥ
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

यथात्म-माया-योगेनेत्य् अनेन मायाया अपि पृष्टत्वाद् वक्ष्यमाणोपयोगित्वाच् च मायां निरूपयति । ऋते अर्थं विनापि वास्तवम् अर्थं यद् यतः किम् अप्य् अनिरुक्तम् आत्मन्य् अधिष्ठाने प्रतीयेत सद् अपि च न प्रतीयेत, तद् आत्मनो मम मायां विद्यात् । यथा आभासो द्वि-चन्द्रादिर् इत्य् अर्थं विना प्रतीतौ दृष्टान्तः । यथा तम इति सतोऽप्रतीतौ । तमो राहुर् यथा ग्रह-मण्डले स्थितोऽपि न दृश्यते तथा ॥३३॥

yathātma-māyā-yogenety anena māyāyā api pṛṣṭatvād vakṣyamāṇopayogitvāc ca māyāṁ nirūpayati | ṛte arthaṁ vināpi vāstavam arthaṁ yad yataḥ kim apy aniruktam ātmany adhiṣṭhāne pratīyeta sad api ca na pratīyeta, tad ātmano mama māyāṁ vidyāt | yathā ābhāso dvi-candrādir ity arthaṁ vinā pratītau dṛṣṭāntaḥ | yathā tama iti sato ’pratītau | tamo rāhur yathā graha-maṇḍale sthito ’pi na dṛśyate tathā ||33||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. māyāṁ nirūpayati — He defines Māyā; vāstavam artham vinā — apart from a real object; ātmany adhiṣṭhāne — in the Self as the underlying ground; sad api na pratīyeta — something existing may nevertheless not be perceived; dvi-candra-ādiḥ — the illusion of two moons and similar appearances; sataḥ apratītau — non-perception of what truly exists; rāhuḥ — Rāhu, present yet unseen.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. Māyā has two complementary effects: it can project an appearance lacking the reality attributed to it, and it can conceal what actually exists. Śrīdhara illustrates these with the visual illusion of a second moon and with a real but hidden object.

ŚB 2.9.35

यथा महान्ति भूतानि भूतेषूच्चावचेष्वनु ।
प्रविष्टान्यप्रविष्टानि तथा तेषु न तेष्वहम् ॥ ३५ ॥

Just as the great material elements are present within all higher and lower composite bodies and yet remain independently themselves, so I am present within all beings and things, while at the same time not being confined within them.

Word-for-word

yathā — just as; mahānti bhūtāni — the great elements; bhūteṣu — in created beings and material compounds; uccāvaceṣu — high and low; anu — subsequently, throughout; praviṣṭāni — entered, present within; apraviṣṭāni — not entered, remaining distinct; tathā — so; teṣu — in them; na teṣu — and not limited to them; aham — I.

Transliteration
yathā mahānti bhūtāni
bhūteṣūccāvaceṣv anu
praviṣṭāny apraviṣṭāni
tathā teṣu na teṣv aham
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

यथाभाव इत्य् एतत् स्पष्टयति । यथा महाभूतानि भौतिकेष्व् अनु सृष्टेर् अनन्तरं प्रविष्टानि तेषूपलभ्यमानत्वात् । अप्रविष्टानि च प्राग् एव कारणतया तेषु विद्यमानत्वात् । तथा तेषु भूतभौतिकेष्व् अहं न च तेष्व् अहम् । एवंभूता मम सत्तेत्य् अर्थः ॥३४॥

yathābhāva ity etat spaṣṭayati | yathā mahābhūtāni bhautikeṣv anu sṛṣṭer anantaraṁ praviṣṭāni teṣūpalabhyamānatvāt | apraviṣṭāni ca prāg eva kāraṇatayā teṣu vidyamānatvāt | tathā teṣu bhūta-bhautikeṣv ahaṁ na ca teṣv aham | evaṁbhūtā mama sattety arthaḥ ||34||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. mahābhūtāni — the great elements; bhautikeṣu — in material products composed from them; sṛṣṭer anantaram praviṣṭāni — entered after creation in the sense of being perceptible there; prāk eva kāraṇatayā — already present beforehand as causes; bhūta-bhautikeṣu — in elements and their products; ahaṁ na ca teṣu — I am in them and yet not confined to them; evaṁbhūtā mama sattā — such is the mode of My existence.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. “Entered” does not mean the cause begins to exist only after the effect appears. The elements are discernible in their products while also possessing causal existence beyond any one product. Likewise, Bhagavān pervades the cosmos without being enclosed or exhausted by it.

एतावदेव जिज्ञास्यं तत्त्वजिज्ञासुनात्मनः ।
अन्वयव्यतिरेकाभ्यां यत् स्यात् सर्वत्र सर्वदा ॥ ३६ ॥

One who seeks the truth of the Self should inquire into that reality which, by the reasoning of presence and absence, is found to persist everywhere and at all times.

Word-for-word

etāvat eva — precisely this much, this alone; jijñāsyam — is to be inquired into; tattva-jijñāsunā — by one who seeks reality; ātmanaḥ — of the Self; anvaya-vyatirekābhyām — by positive concomitance and negative distinction, presence and absence; yat syāt — that which exists; sarvatra — everywhere; sarvadā — at all times.

Transliteration

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

साधनम् आह । आत्मनस् तत्त्व-जिज्ञासुना एतावत्त्वम् एव जिज्ञास्यं विचार्यम् । तद् एवाह । अन्वयः कार्येषु कारणत्वेनानुवृत्तिः । कारणावस्थायां च तेभ्यो व्यतिरेकः । तथा जाग्रद्-आद्य्-अवस्थासु तत्-तत्-साक्षितयान्वयः । व्यतिरेकश् च समाध्य्-आदौ । एवम् अन्वय-व्यतिरेकाभ्यां यत् स्यात् सर्वत्र सर्वदा च तद् एवात्मेति ॥३५॥

sādhanam āha | ātmanas tattva-jijñāsunā etāvattvam eva jijñāsyaṁ vicāryam | tad evāha | anvayaḥ kāryeṣu kāraṇatvenānuvṛttiḥ | kāraṇāvasthāyāṁ ca tebhyo vyatirekaḥ | tathā jāgrad-ādy-avasthāsu tat-tat-sākṣitayānvayaḥ | vyatirekaś ca samādhy-ādau | evam anvaya-vyatirekābhyāṁ yat syāt sarvatra sarvadā ca tad evātmeti ||35||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. sādhanam āha — He states the method; jijñāsyam vicāryam — to be inquired into and examined; anvayaḥ — positive persistence; kāryeṣu kāraṇatvena anuvṛttiḥ — the cause continuing through its effects; vyatirekaḥ — distinctness or nondependence; jāgrad-ādi-avasthāsu — through waking and other states; sākṣitayā — as their witness; sarvatra sarvadā — everywhere and always; tad eva ātmā — that alone is the Self.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. The concluding method of the four-śloka teaching is inquiry by continuity and distinction. The cause persists through its effects while not being reducible to them; similarly, the witness persists through waking, dream and other states. What withstands this examination everywhere and always is the reality to be known.

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

रिक्थादानां पुत्राणां मध्ये प्रियतमस् तं पितरं सेवमानः शीलादिना परितोषयामासेत्य् उत्तरेणान्वयः ॥४०॥

rikthādānāṁ putrāṇāṁ madhye priyatamas taṁ pitaraṁ sevamānaḥ śīlādinā paritoṣayāmāsety uttareṇānvayaḥ ||40||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. rikthādānām putrāṇām madhye — among the sons entitled to inheritance; priyatamaḥ — most beloved; pitaraṁ sevamānaḥ — serving his father; śīla-ādinā — through good conduct and the other virtues; paritoṣayāmāsa — he thoroughly pleased him; uttareṇa anvayaḥ — the sentence is completed by the following verse.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. Nārada’s qualification for receiving the teaching is relational as well as intellectual: among Brahmā’s sons he is especially dear, and he approaches his father through service, character, humility and restraint.

ŚB 2.9.42

मायां विविदिषन् विष्णोर्मायेशस्य महामुनिः ।
महाभागवतो राजन् पितरं पर्यतोषयत् ॥ ४२ ॥

O King, the great sage Nārada, foremost among devotees, wished to understand the Māyā of Viṣṇu, the Lord and controller of Māyā. By his service he therefore completely pleased his father.

Word-for-word

māyām — Māyā, the Lord’s potency; vividiṣan — desiring to know; viṣṇoḥ — of Viṣṇu; māyā-īśasya — of the Lord of Māyā; mahā-muniḥ — the great sage; mahā-bhāgavataḥ — the great devotee; rājan — O King; pitaram — his father; paryatoṣayat — thoroughly pleased.

Transliteration
māyāṁ vividiṣan viṣṇor
māyeśasya mahā-muniḥ
mahā-bhāgavato rājan
pitaraṁ paryatoṣayat
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

किम् इच्छन् विष्णोर् मायां विविदिषन् ॥४१॥

kim icchan viṣṇor māyāṁ vividiṣan ||41||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. kim icchan — desiring what?; viṣṇoḥ — of Viṣṇu; māyām — Māyā, His power; vividiṣan — wishing to know and understand.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. The purpose of Nārada’s exemplary service is now stated: he wishes to understand Viṣṇu’s Māyā from the one who has just received the Lord’s teaching directly.
