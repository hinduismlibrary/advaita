ŚB 2.10.51

सूत उवाच
राज्ञा परीक्षिता पृष्टो यदवोचन्महामुनिः ।
तद्वोऽभिधास्ये शृणुत राज्ञः प्रश्नानुसारतः ॥ ५१ ॥

Sūta said: I shall now relate to you what the great sage spoke when questioned by King Parīkṣit. Please hear it as the narration follows the sequence of the King’s inquiries.

Word-for-word

sūtaḥ uvāca — Sūta said; rājñā parīkṣitā — by King Parīkṣit; pṛṣṭaḥ — questioned; yat — what; avocat — spoke; mahā-muniḥ — the great sage; tat — that; vaḥ — to you; abhidhāsye — I shall explain; śṛṇuta — please hear; rājñaḥ — of the King; praśna-anusārataḥ — according to the sequence of his questions.

Transliteration
sūta uvāca
rājñā parīkṣitā pṛṣṭo
yad avocan mahā-muniḥ
tad vo ’bhidhāsye śṛṇuta
rājñaḥ praśnānusārataḥ
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

राज्ञा पृष्ट इति । अयम् अर्थः—यद् यूयं पृच्छथ इदम् एव राजापि शुकं पृष्टवान् । शुकोऽपि विदुर-मैत्रेय-संवादं पुरस्कृत्य ये पूर्वं राज्ञा कृताः प्रश्नास् तद्-अनुसारेण सर्वं पुराणार्थम् अवोचन् । तद् एवाहं वोऽभिधास्यामि तथैव शृणुतेति ॥५१॥

rājñā pṛṣṭa iti | ayam arthaḥ—yad yūyaṁ pṛcchatha idam eva rājāpi śukaṁ pṛṣṭavān | śuko ’pi vidura-maitreya-saṁvādaṁ puraskṛtya ye pūrvaṁ rājñā kṛtāḥ praśnās tad-anusāreṇa sarvaṁ purāṇārtham avocan | tad evāhaṁ vo ’bhidhāsyāmi tathaiva śṛṇuteti ||51||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. yad yūyam pṛcchatha — what you are asking; rājā api śukam pṛṣṭavān — the King likewise asked Śuka; vidura-maitreya-saṁvādam puraskṛtya — presenting the Vidura–Maitreya dialogue; praśna-anusāreṇa — according to the questions; purāṇa-artham — the meaning/content of the Purāṇa; abhidhāsyāmi — I shall relate it.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. The questions of Śaunaka mirror the questions previously asked by Parīkṣit. Sūta therefore announces that the Purāṇic teaching will continue through Śuka’s answer, especially by way of the Vidura–Maitreya dialogue.

End of Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, Canto 2 — The Cosmic Manifestation.
