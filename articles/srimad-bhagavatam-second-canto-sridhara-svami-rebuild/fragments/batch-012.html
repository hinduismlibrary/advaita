ŚB 2.8.2

एतद् वेदितुमिच्छामि तत्त्वं तत्त्वविदां वर ।
हरेरद्भुतवीर्यस्य कथा लोकसुमङ्गलाः ॥ २ ॥

O best among knowers of truth, I wish to understand this reality. Narrations of Hari, whose powers are wondrous, bring the highest auspiciousness to the worlds.

Word-for-word

etat — this; veditum — to understand; icchāmi — I desire; tattvam — the truth, reality; tattva-vidām vara — O best of those who know truth; hareḥ — of Hari; adbhuta-vīryasya — whose potency is wonderful; kathāḥ — narrations; loka-su-maṅgalāḥ — supremely auspicious for the worlds.

Transliteration
etad veditum icchāmi
tattvaṁ tattva-vidāṁ vara
harer adbhuta-vīryasya
kathā loka-sumaṅgalāḥ
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

त्वम् एतद् विपुलीकुर्व् इत्य् उक्तं तद् एव विपुलीकरणं पृच्छति—ब्रह्मणेति त्रिभिः । अगुणस्य गुणातीतस्यापि । देववद् दर्शनं यस्य सः ॥१-२॥

tvam etad vipulīkurv ity uktaṁ tad eva vipulīkaraṇaṁ pṛcchati—brahmaṇeti tribhiḥ | aguṇasya guṇātītasyāpi | devavad darśanaṁ yasya saḥ ||1-2||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. tvam etat vipulīkuru — “you expand this”; uktam — having been said; tad eva vipulīkaraṇam pṛcchati — he asks precisely for that expansion; brahmaṇā iti tribhiḥ — by the three verses beginning with brahmaṇā; aguṇasya — of Him who is without material qualities; guṇātītasya api — though beyond the guṇas; devavat darśanam yasya — one whose vision is divine.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. Parīkṣit is asking Śukadeva to expand what was previously stated. Śrīdhara stresses that the Lord is beyond material qualities, and that Nārada is described as one possessed of divine vision.

शृण्वतः श्रद्धया नित्यं गृणतश्च स्वचेष्टितम् ।
कालेन नातिदीर्घेण भगवान् विशते हृदि ॥ ४ ॥

For one who constantly hears with faith and takes up the Lord’s own activities, Bhagavān enters the heart within no very long time.

śṛṇvataḥ — of one who hears; śraddhayā — with faith; nityam — constantly; gṛṇataḥ — of one who recites or takes up; ca — and; sva-ceṣṭitam — His own activities; kālena — in time; na ati-dīrgheṇa — not very long; bhagavān — Bhagavān; viśate — enters; hṛdi — the heart.

Śrīdhara word-for-word. saḥ api — even that; śraddhayā śṛṇvataḥ — for one hearing with faith; na āvaśyakaḥ — is not indispensable; sva-prayatnam vinā api — even without one’s own separate effort; bhagavān svayam eva — Bhagavān Himself; hṛdi viśati — enters the heart.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. Śrīdhara now qualifies the previous emphasis on personal effort: for one who faithfully hears, a separate exertion to force remembrance is unnecessary, because Bhagavān Himself enters the heart.

ŚB 2.8.5

प्रविष्टः कर्णरन्ध्रेण स्वानां भावसरोरुहम् ।
धुनोति शमलं कृष्णः सलिलस्य यथा शरत् ॥ ५ ॥

Entering through the opening of the ear into the lotus-heart of His own devotee, Kṛṣṇa shakes away its impurity, just as autumn clears the impurity from water.

Word-for-word

praviṣṭaḥ — having entered; karṇa-randhreṇa — through the opening of the ear; svānām — of His own devotees; bhāva-saroruham — the lotus of loving disposition, the heart; dhunoti — shakes off, cleanses; śamalam — impurity; kṛṣṇaḥ — Kṛṣṇa; salilasya — of water; yathā śarat — as autumn does.

Transliteration
praviṣṭaḥ karṇa-randhreṇa
svānāṁ bhāva-saroruham
dhunoti śamalaṁ kṛṣṇaḥ
salilasya yathā śarat
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

भाव-सरोरुहं हृदय-कमलं प्रविष्टश् च तद्-गतं सर्वं मलं धुनोति। सलिलस्येति । द्रव्यान्तर-व्यामिश्रणादिना कुम्भ-स्थे जले शोधिते तद् एव केवलं शुध्यति न तु नदी-तडागादि-गतम्, स च मलः कुम्भस्यान्तस् तिष्ठत्य् एव न तु सर्वथा विलीयते । अत एव किंचिच् चलनेन पुनः क्षुभ्यति च । एवं तपोदानादि प्रायश्चित्तं न सर्वथा सर्वेषां सर्वं पापं धुनोति, किन्तु सावशेषम्, तच् च कस्यचिद् एव, किंचिद् एव, हृदि प्रविष्ट-मात्रस् तु श्री-कृष्णः सर्वेषां सर्वं पापं निःशेषं हरतीत्य् अनेन दृष्टान्तेनोक्तं—सलिलस्य मलं यथा शरद् इति ॥५॥

bhāva-saroruhaṁ hṛdaya-kamalaṁ praviṣṭaś ca tad-gataṁ sarvaṁ malaṁ dhunoti | salilasyeti | dravyāntara-vyāmiśraṇādinā kumbha-sthe jale śodhite tad eva kevalaṁ śudhyati na tu nadī-taḍāgādi-gatam, sa ca malaḥ kumbhasyāntas tiṣṭhaty eva na tu sarvathā vilīyate | ata eva kiñcic calanena punaḥ kṣubhyati ca | evaṁ tapo-dānādi prāyaścittaṁ na sarvathā sarveṣāṁ sarvaṁ pāpaṁ dhunoti, kintu sāvaśeṣam, tac ca kasyacid eva, kiñcid eva, hṛdi praviṣṭa-mātras tu śrī-kṛṣṇaḥ sarveṣāṁ sarvaṁ pāpaṁ niḥśeṣaṁ haratīty anena dṛṣṭāntenoktaṁ—salilasya malaṁ yathā śarad iti ||5||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. bhāva-saroruham — the lotus of feeling, the heart-lotus; tad-gatam sarvam malam dhunoti — He removes every impurity situated there; kumbha-sthe jale — water held in a pot; śodhite — when externally purified; malaḥ ... tiṣṭhati — sediment still remains; kiñcit calanena punaḥ kṣubhyati — with a little movement it becomes disturbed again; tapo-dānādi prāyaścittam — expiation such as austerity and charity; sāvaśeṣam — leaves a remainder; śrī-kṛṣṇaḥ — Śrī Kṛṣṇa; niḥśeṣam harati — removes it without remainder.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. The autumn-water comparison is exact: ordinary expiations may settle impurity without wholly destroying it, so disturbance can make it rise again. When Kṛṣṇa enters the heart through hearing, He removes sin at the root and without remainder.

ŚB 2.8.6

धौतात्मा पुरुषः कृष्णपादमूलं न मुञ्चति ।

A person whose heart has been washed clean does not leave the shelter at Kṛṣṇa’s feet. Freed from every affliction, he is like a traveler who has returned to his own home and will not abandon it.

mukta-sarva-parikleśaḥ
pānthaḥ sva-śaraṇaṁ yathā
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

ततश् च कृतार्थो भवतीत्य् आह । धौतात्मा निष्पापः । मुक्ताः सर्वे राग-द्वेषादयः परिक्लेशा येन । पान्थः प्रवासाद् आगतः स्वस्य शरणं गृहं यथा न मुञ्चति, तद्वत् ॥६॥

Śrīdhara word-for-word. kṛtārthaḥ bhavati — he becomes fulfilled; dhautātmā — cleansed within; niṣpāpaḥ — sinless; rāga-dveṣādayaḥ parikleśāḥ — afflictions such as attachment and aversion; muktāḥ — released; pānthaḥ — a traveler; pravāsāt āgataḥ — returned from abroad; svasya śaraṇam gṛham — his own refuge, his home; na muñcati — does not leave.

ŚB 2.8.7

यदधातुमतो ब्रह्मन् देहारम्भोऽस्य धातुभिः ।
यदृच्छया हेतुना वा भवन्तो जानते यथा ॥ ७ ॥

O brāhmaṇa, the self is not composed of material elements, yet its body begins from those elements. Does this occur without a cause, or from some definite cause? Please explain it as you know it.

Word-for-word

yat — that fact which; adhātu-mataḥ — of one not constituted of material elements; brahman — O brāhmaṇa; deha-ārambhaḥ — beginning of embodiment; asya — of this self; dhātubhiḥ — by the material elements; yadṛcchayā — causelessly, accidentally; hetunā vā — or by a cause; bhavantaḥ jānate — you know; yathā — how, as it is.

Transliteration
yad adhātu-mato brahman

yadṛcchayā hetunā vā
bhavanto jānate yathā
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तद् एवं श्रवणौत्सुक्यम् आविष्कृत्य सन्दिग्धान् अर्थान् पृच्छति । अधातुमतः धातवो महा-भूतानि तत्-संबन्ध-शून्यस्यास्य लौकिकस्यात्मनो जीवस्य धातुभिर् देहारम्भ इति यत् । एतत् किम् ? यदृच्छया निर्निमित्तं हेतुना वा कर्मादिना भवन्तो यथावज् जानते । अतः कथयन्त्व् इति शेषः ॥७॥

tad evaṁ śravaṇautsukyam āviṣkṛtya sandigdhān arthān pṛcchati | adhātumataḥ dhātavo mahā-bhūtāni tat-sambandha-śūnyasyāsya laukikasyātmano jīvasya dhātubhir dehārambha iti yat | etat kim? yadṛcchayā nirnimittaṁ hetunā vā karmādinā bhavanto yathāvaj jānate | ataḥ kathayantv iti śeṣaḥ ||7||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. śravaṇa-autsukyam āviṣkṛtya — having displayed eagerness to hear; sandigdhān arthān pṛcchati — he asks about doubtful points; dhātavaḥ mahā-bhūtāni — the elements are the great material elements; tat-sambandha-śūnyasya — of one devoid of intrinsic relation to them; jīvasya — of the living self; dehārambhaḥ — the arising of embodiment; yadṛcchayā nirnimittam — causelessly; hetunā vā karmādinā — or by a cause such as karma; kathayantu — therefore let it be explained.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. Having shown his eagerness for hearing, Parīkṣit turns to difficult philosophical questions. Since the jīva is not itself one of the great material elements, what accounts for its embodiment in them—mere accident, or karma and another definite cause?

ŚB 2.8.8

Transliteration
āsīd yad-udarāt padmaṁ
loka-saṁsthāna-lakṣaṇam
yāvān ayaṁ vai puruṣa
iyattāvayavaiḥ pṛthak
tāvān asāv iti proktaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

यश् चासाव् ईश्वरः, सोऽप्य् एतत्-तुल्य-देहवान् प्रोक्तः, अतस् तस्य को विशेषः ? इत्य् आशयेन पृच्छति—आसीद् इति सार्धेन । लोकानां संस्थानं रचना, तद् एव लक्षणं स्वरूपं यस्य, तत् त्रैलोक्यात्मकं पद्मं यस्योदराद् आसीत् । असाव् ईश्वर इयत्ता-युक्तैः स्व-परिमितैर् अवयवैर् अयं लौकिकः पुरुषो यावान् यत्-सङ्ख्याकावयव-युक्तः, तावान् प्रोक्तः संस्थावान्, अवयववान् इव च प्रोक्तः । अतः को विशेषस् तस्य ? इति ॥८॥

Śrīdhara word-for-word. etat-tulya-dehavān proktaḥ — He too is described as having a body comparable to this; ko viśeṣaḥ — what is the distinction?; lokānāṁ saṁsthānam racanā — the arrangement of the worlds means their structure; trailokya-ātmakam padmam — the lotus comprising the three worlds; yasyodarāt āsīt — which arose from His abdomen; iyattā-yuktaiḥ — endowed with measured extent; sva-parimitaiḥ avayavaiḥ — with individually delimited limbs; avayavavān iva — described as though possessing limbs.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. Parīkṣit presses the apparent similarity between the Lord’s cosmic form and an embodied human form. Śrīdhara identifies the lotus as the structured three-world cosmos and frames the question sharply: if the Lord too is spoken of with measured limbs, what is His decisive difference from ordinary embodiment?

ŚB 2.8.9

अजः सृजति भूतानि भूतात्मा यदनुग्रहात् ।
ददृशे येन तद्रूपं नाभिपद्मसमुद्भवः ॥ ९ ॥

Brahmā, unborn from any material womb and ruler of created beings, creates only by His grace. Born from the lotus of His navel, Brahmā was also enabled by that same Lord to behold His form.

Word-for-word

ajaḥ — the unborn Brahmā; sṛjati — creates; bhūtāni — living beings; bhūta-ātmā — ruler or collective self of beings; yat-anugrahāt — by whose grace; dadṛśe — saw, was enabled to see; yena — by whom; tat-rūpam — that form of His; nābhi-padma-samudbhavaḥ — he who arose from the navel-lotus.

Transliteration

bhūtātmā yad-anugrahāt

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अवश्यं च विशेषो वाच्य इत्य् आह । अजो ब्रह्मा भूतानां व्यष्ट्य्-उपाधीनाम् आत्मा नियन्ता, समष्ट्य्-उपाधित्वात् । येन चानुगृह्णता तस्य रूपं ददृशे दृष्टवान् । एतच् च— “तस्यापि द्रष्टुर् ईशस्य कूटस्थस्याखिलात्मनः । सृज्यं सृजामि सृष्टोऽहम् ईक्षयैवाभिचोदितः ॥” इत्य् अनेन पूर्वम् उक्तम् ॥९॥

avaśyaṁ ca viśeṣo vācya ity āha | ajo brahmā bhūtānāṁ vyaṣṭy-upādhīnām ātmā niyantā, samaṣṭy-upādhitvāt | yena cānugṛhṇatā tasya rūpaṁ dadṛśe dṛṣṭavān | etac ca—“tasyāpi draṣṭur īśasya kūṭasthasyākhilātmanaḥ | sṛjyaṁ sṛjāmi sṛṣṭo ’ham īkṣayaivābhicoditaḥ ||” ity anena pūrvam uktam ||9||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. avaśyam viśeṣaḥ vācyaḥ — a real distinction must indeed be stated; ajaḥ brahmā — the unborn Brahmā; bhūtānām ... niyantā — controller of individual embodied beings; samaṣṭi-upādhitvāt — because he is the collective conditioning principle; anugṛhṇatā — by the One showing grace; tasya rūpam dadṛśe — he saw His form; īkṣayā eva abhicoditaḥ — impelled by His glance alone.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. A genuine distinction between Brahmā and the Lord must be given. Brahmā rules and creates only as a dependent instrument, and even his vision of the Lord’s form occurs by the Lord’s grace. Śrīdhara connects this with Brahmā’s earlier confession that creation is carried out under the Lord’s directing glance.

ŚB 2.8.10

स चापि यत्र पुरुषो विश्वस्थित्युद्भवाप्ययः ।
मुक्त्वात्ममायां मायेशः शेते सर्वगुहाशयः ॥ १० ॥

Please explain also where that Puruṣa—the source of the universe’s maintenance, creation and dissolution, the Lord of Māyā who dwells in every heart—reposes while remaining untouched by His own external potency.

Word-for-word

saḥ ca api — and that very One also; yatra — where; puruṣaḥ — the Puruṣa; viśva-sthiti-udbhava-apyayaḥ — source of the universe’s maintenance, arising and dissolution; muktvā — leaving aside, untouched by; ātma-māyām — His own Māyā; māyā-īśaḥ — Lord of Māyā; śete — lies, reposes; sarva-guhā-śayaḥ — dwelling in every heart.

Transliteration
sa cāpi yatra puruṣo
viśva-sthity-udbhavāpyayaḥ
muktvātma-māyāṁ māyeśaḥ
śete sarva-guhāśayaḥ
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

प्रश्नान्तरम् आह—स चापीति । यत्र शेते येन रूपेणावतिष्ठते । विश्वस्य स्थित्य्-आदयो यस्मात् । एवम्-आदि-प्रश्नानां तत्त्वतोऽर्हस्य् उदाहर्तुम् इति सर्वान्ते क्रियया सम्बन्धः ॥१०॥

praśnāntaram āha—sa cāpīti | yatra śete yena rūpeṇāvatiṣṭhate | viśvasya sthity-ādayo yasmāt | evam-ādi-praśnānāṁ tattvato ’rhasy udāhartum iti sarvānte kriyayā sambandhaḥ ||10||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. praśnāntaram āha — he raises another question; yatra śete — where He lies; yena rūpeṇa avatiṣṭhate — in what form He abides; viśvasya sthity-ādayaḥ yasmāt — from whom maintenance and the other cosmic functions arise; tattvataḥ udāhartum — to explain according to truth; sarvānte kriyayā sambandhaḥ — the governing verb is supplied at the end for all these questions.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. This begins another question: where and in what form does the cosmic Puruṣa abide, He from whom maintenance, creation and dissolution proceed? Śrīdhara notes that the request “please explain in truth” at verse 24 governs this whole series of questions.

ŚB 2.8.11

पुरुषावयवैर्लोकाः सपालाः पूर्वकल्पिताः ।
लोकैरमुष्यावयवाः सपालैरिति शुश्रुम ॥ ११ ॥

We have previously heard that the worlds together with their rulers are assigned to the limbs of the cosmic Person, and conversely that His limbs are described through those worlds and their presiding rulers. Please explain how this correspondence is truly situated.

Word-for-word

puruṣa-avayavaiḥ — through the limbs of the cosmic Person; lokāḥ — the worlds; sa-pālāḥ — together with their rulers; pūrva-kalpitāḥ — previously described or arranged; lokaiḥ — through the worlds; amuṣya avayavāḥ — His limbs; sa-pālaiḥ — together with the rulers; iti — thus; śuśruma — we have heard.

Transliteration
puruṣāvayavair lokāḥ
sapālāḥ pūrva-kalpitāḥ
lokair amuṣyāvayavāḥ
sa-pālair iti śuśruma
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

[विष्वास-प्रस्तुतौ अस्मिन् श्लोके पृथक् श्रीधर-स्वामी-व्याख्या न प्रदत्ता।]

[viśvāsa-prastutau asmin śloke pṛthak śrīdhara-svāmī-vyākhyā na pradattā.]

Śrīdhara word-for-word. No separate Śrīdhara gloss is supplied for this verse in the Vishvasa Gauḍīya-prastuti source used for the rebuild; the verse is retained without inventing a commentary.

Textual note. This card deliberately does not fabricate a Śrīdhara gloss where the source does not provide one. The verse continues Parīkṣit’s inquiry into the reciprocal mapping between the virāṭ-puruṣa’s limbs, the worlds and their presiding deities.
