ŚB 2.10.33

एतद्भगवतो रूपं स्थूलं ते व्याहृतं मया ।
मह्यादिभिश्चावरणैरष्टभिर्बहिरावृतम् ॥ ३३ ॥

I have now described to you this gross cosmic form attributed to the Lord, externally enclosed by the eight successive coverings beginning with earth.

Word-for-word

etat — this; bhagavataḥ rūpam — form of the Lord; sthūlam — gross; te — to you; vyāhṛtam mayā — described by me; mahy-ādibhiḥ — beginning with earth; āvaraṇaiḥ — by coverings; aṣṭabhiḥ — eight; bahiḥ āvṛtam — externally enclosed.

Transliteration
etad bhagavato rūpaṁ
sthūlaṁ te vyāhṛtaṁ mayā
mahy-ādibhiś cāvaraṇair
aṣṭabhir bahir āvṛtam
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

उपसंहरति—एतद् इति । प्रकृत्या सहाष्टभिः ॥३३॥

upasaṁharati—etad iti | prakṛtyā sahāṣṭabhiḥ ||33||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. upasaṁharati — he sums up; prakṛtyā saha aṣṭabhiḥ — eight when prakṛti is included with the other coverings.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. This verse closes the account of the gross cosmic form by identifying its eightfold external enclosure.

ŚB 2.10.34

अतः परं सूक्ष्मतममव्यक्तं निर्विशेषणम् ।
अनादिमध्यनिधनं नित्यं वाङ्मनसः परम् ॥ ३४ ॥

Beyond this is the exceedingly subtle unmanifest condition, without distinguishing material characteristics, without beginning, middle or end, enduring and beyond the reach of ordinary speech and mind.

Word-for-word

ataḥ param — beyond this; sūkṣmatamam — most subtle; avyaktam — unmanifest; nirviśeṣaṇam — without distinguishing characteristics; anādi-madhya-nidhanam — without beginning, middle or end; nityam — enduring; vāk-manasaḥ param — beyond speech and mind.

Transliteration
ataḥ paraṁ sūkṣmatamam
avyaktaṁ nirviśeṣaṇam
anādi-madhya-nidhanaṁ
nityaṁ vāṅ-manasaḥ param
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

स्थूलम् उक्त्वा सूक्ष्मं समष्टि-लिङ्ग-शरीरम् आह । अतः परमस्य कारण-भूतं सूक्ष्मम् अतीन्द्रियम् । यतोऽव्यक्तम् । तत् कुतः ? यतो निर्विशेषणम् अनादि-मध्य-निधनम् उत्पत्ति-स्थिति-लय-शून्यम् । नित्यं सदैक-रूपम्, अपक्षयादि-शून्यम् इत्य् अर्थः । अत एव वाक् च मनश् चेति द्वन्द्वैक्यम् । तस्मात् परम् ॥३४॥

sthūlam uktvā sūkṣmaṁ samaṣṭi-liṅga-śarīram āha | ataḥ param asya kāraṇa-bhūtaṁ sūkṣmam atīndriyam | yato ’vyaktam | tat kutaḥ? yato nirviśeṣaṇam anādi-madhya-nidhanam utpatti-sthiti-laya-śūnyam | nityaṁ sadaika-rūpam, apakṣayādi-śūnyam ity arthaḥ | ata eva vāk ca manaś ceti dvandvaikyam | tasmāt param ||34||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. samaṣṭi-liṅga-śarīram — the subtle aggregate body; atīndriyam — beyond the senses; avyaktam — unmanifest; utpatti-sthiti-laya-śūnyam — free from manifest arising, duration and dissolution; sadaika-rūpam — ever of one condition.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. Having described the gross aggregate, Śrīdhara identifies its subtler causal condition, inaccessible to ordinary sensory cognition and described as unmanifest.

ŚB 2.10.35

अमुनी भगवद्रूपे मया ते ह्यनुवर्णिते ।
उभे अपि न गृह्णन्ति मायासृष्टे विपश्चितः ॥ ३५ ॥

The discerning do not accept either of these two forms just described as the Lord’s own essential form, for both are presentations fashioned within māyā for the purpose of contemplation.

Word-for-word

amunī — these two; bhagavad-rūpe — forms attributed to the Lord; mayā te anuvarṇite — described by me to you; ubhe api — both; na gṛhṇanti — do not accept as ultimate; māyā-sṛṣṭe — produced within māyā; vipaścitaḥ — the discerning.

Transliteration
amunī bhagavad-rūpe
mayā te hy anuvarṇite
ubhe api na gṛhṇanti
māyā-sṛṣṭe vipaścitaḥ
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

उपासनार्थं भगवत्य् आरोपितं रूप-द्वयम् अपवदति—अमुनी इति । न गृह्णन्ति वस्तुतो नाङ्गीकुर्वन्ति । यतो माया-सृष्टे ॥३५॥

upāsanārthaṁ bhagavaty āropitaṁ rūpa-dvayam apavadati—amunī iti | na gṛhṇanti vastuto nāṅgīkurvanti | yato māyā-sṛṣṭe ||35||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. upāsanārtham — for contemplative worship; āropitam — superimposed/attributed; rūpa-dvayam — the two forms; vastutaḥ na aṅgīkurvanti — they do not accept them as the essential reality; māyā-sṛṣṭe — created within māyā.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. The gross and subtle cosmic forms serve an upāsanā function, but the discerning do not identify either one with the Lord’s essential transcendent form.
