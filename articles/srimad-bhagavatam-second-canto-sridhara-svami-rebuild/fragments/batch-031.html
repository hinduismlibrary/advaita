ŚB 2.10.48

शौनक उवाच
यदाह नो भवान् सूत क्षत्ता भागवतोत्तमः ।
चचार तीर्थानि भुवस्त्यक्त्वा बन्धून् सुदुस्त्यजान् ॥ ४८ ॥

Śaunaka said: O Sūta, you told us that Vidura, that foremost devotee, left relatives exceedingly difficult to abandon and traveled among the holy places of the earth.

Word-for-word

śaunakaḥ uvāca — Śaunaka said; yat āha — as you said; naḥ — to us; bhavān — you; sūta — O Sūta; kṣattā — Vidura; bhāgavata-uttamaḥ — foremost devotee; cacāra — traveled; tīrthāni — holy places; bhuvaḥ — of the earth; tyaktvā — leaving; bandhūn — relatives; su-dustyajān — very difficult to abandon.

Transliteration
śaunaka uvāca
yad āha no bhavān sūta
kṣattā bhāgavatottamaḥ
cacāra tīrthāni bhuvas
tyaktvā bandhūn sudustyajān
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

यद् अर्थं सृष्ट्य्-आदि-निरूपणं तद् एव साक्षाच् छ्रोतु-कामः कथान्तरं पृच्छति—यद् आहेति । क्षत्ता विदुरः । भुवः सम्बन्धीनि तीर्थानि । यद् वा, भुवः क्षेत्राणि चेति ॥४८॥

yad arthaṁ sṛṣṭy-ādi-nirūpaṇaṁ tad eva sākṣāc chrotu-kāmaḥ kathāntaraṁ pṛcchati—yad āheti | kṣattā viduraḥ | bhuvaḥ sambandhīni tīrthāni | yad vā, bhuvaḥ kṣetrāṇi ceti ||48||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. śrotu-kāmaḥ — desiring to hear directly; kathāntaram pṛcchati — asks for the next narrative; kṣattā — Vidura; bhuvaḥ tīrthāni — holy places throughout the earth.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. The cosmological account has reached the point where Śaunaka asks to hear its teaching through the concrete narrative of Vidura and Maitreya.

ŚB 2.10.49

क्षत्तुः कौशारवेस्तस्य संवादोऽध्यात्मसंश्रितः ।
यद्वा स भगवांस्तस्मै पृष्टस्तत्त्वमुवाच ह ॥ ४९ ॥

Tell us of the dialogue between Vidura and Maitreya, son of Kuṣāru, which was centered upon spiritual truth, and of the reality that the venerable sage explained when Vidura questioned him.

Word-for-word

kṣattuḥ — of Vidura; kauśāraveḥ — of Maitreya, descendant/son of Kuṣāru; tasya — their; saṁvādaḥ — dialogue; adhyātma-saṁśritaḥ — centered on spiritual knowledge; yat vā — and whatever; saḥ bhagavān — that venerable sage; tasmai — to him; pṛṣṭaḥ — being questioned; tattvam — truth/reality; uvāca — spoke.

Transliteration
kṣattuḥ kauśāraves tasya
saṁvādo ’dhyātma-saṁśritaḥ
yad vā sa bhagavāṁs tasmai
pṛṣṭas tattvam uvāca ha
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

कौषारवेर् मैत्रेयस्य तस्य क्षत्तुश् चाध्यात्म-ज्ञान-संश्रितः संवादः ॥४९॥

kauṣāraver maitreyasya tasya kṣattuś cādhyātma-jñāna-saṁśritaḥ saṁvādaḥ ||49||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. kauṣāraveḥ maitreyasya — of Maitreya, the Kauṣārava; kṣattuḥ — of Vidura; adhyātma-jñāna-saṁśritaḥ — founded upon spiritual knowledge; saṁvādaḥ — their dialogue.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. The requested subject is specifically the spiritually centered conversation between Vidura and Maitreya.

ŚB 2.10.50

ब्रूहि नस्तदिदं सौम्य विदुरस्य विचेष्टितम् ।
बन्धुत्यागनिमित्तं च यथैवागतवान् पुनः ॥ ५० ॥

O gentle Sūta, please tell us all of Vidura’s activities: why he left his relatives and how, in due course, he returned again.

Word-for-word

brūhi — please tell; naḥ — us; tat idam — all this; saumya — O gentle one; vidurasya — of Vidura; viceṣṭitam — activities; bandhu-tyāga-nimittam — cause of leaving his relatives; ca — and; yathā eva — how indeed; āgatavān — returned; punaḥ — again.

Transliteration
brūhi nas tad idaṁ saumya
vidurasya viceṣṭitam
bandhu-tyāga-nimittaṁ ca
yathaivāgatavān punaḥ
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

पुनर् आगतवान् तत्र च निमित्तं ब्रूहि ॥५०॥

punar āgatavān tatra ca nimittaṁ brūhi ||50||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. punar āgatavān — he returned again; nimittam — the cause; brūhi — tell us.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. Śaunaka asks for both sides of Vidura’s journey: the cause of his departure and the circumstance of his return.
