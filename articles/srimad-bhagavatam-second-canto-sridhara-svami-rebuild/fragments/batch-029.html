ŚB 2.10.42

स एवेदं जगद्धाता भगवान् धर्मरूपधृक् ।
पुष्णाति स्थापयन् विश्वं तिर्यङ्नरसुरादिभिः ॥ ४२ ॥

The very same Supreme Lord, sustaining the universe and assuming forms that uphold dharma, maintains and nourishes the world through His manifestations among animals, human beings, devas and others.

Word-for-word

saḥ eva — that very Lord; idam jagat-dhātā — sustainer of this world; bhagavān — the Supreme Lord; dharma-rūpa-dhṛk — assuming forms that uphold dharma; puṣṇāti — nourishes; sthāpayan — maintaining; viśvam — the universe; tiryak-nara-sura-ādibhiḥ — through animal, human, divine and other forms.

Transliteration
sa evedaṁ jagad-dhātā
bhagavān dharma-rūpa-dhṛk
puṣṇāti sthāpayan viśvaṁ
tiryaṅ-nara-surādibhiḥ
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

ब्रह्म-रूपेण स्रष्टृत्वम् उक्त्वा विष्णु-रूपेण पालकत्वम् आह । स एव भगवांस् तिर्यग्-आद्य्-अवतारैर् इदं विश्वं स्थापयन् पालयन् धर्म-रूपेण पुष्णाति भोगैः संवर्धयति ॥४२॥

brahma-rūpeṇa sraṣṭṛtvam uktvā viṣṇu-rūpeṇa pālakatvam āha | sa eva bhagavāṁs tiryag-ādy-avatārair idaṁ viśvaṁ sthāpayan pālayan dharma-rūpeṇa puṣṇāti bhogaiḥ saṁvardhayati ||42||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. brahma-rūpeṇa sraṣṭṛtvam — creation through Brahmā’s form; viṣṇu-rūpeṇa pālakatvam — preservation through Viṣṇu’s form; tiryag-ādi-avatāraiḥ — incarnations among various classes of beings; sthāpayan pālayan — establishing and protecting; puṣṇāti — nourishes.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. After describing creation through Brahmā, Śrīdhara turns to preservation through Viṣṇu: the Lord’s various avatāras establish, protect and nourish the cosmic order.

ŚB 2.10.43

ततः कालाग्निरुद्रात्मा यत्सृष्टमिदमात्मनः ।
सन्नियच्छति कालेन घनानीकमिवानिलः ॥ ४३ ॥

Then, assuming the form of Rudra as the fire of time, He draws back what has issued from Himself, as the wind in due course gathers and disperses a mass of clouds.

Word-for-word

tataḥ — thereafter; kāla-agni-rudra-ātmā — assuming Rudra, the fire of time; yat sṛṣṭam idam — this which has been created; ātmanaḥ — from Himself; sanniyacchati — withdraws/restrains; kālena — by time; ghana-anīkam — a mass of clouds; iva — like; anilaḥ — wind.

Transliteration
tataḥ kālāgni-rudrātmā
yat sṛṣṭam idam ātmanaḥ
sanniyacchati kālena
ghanānīkam ivānilaḥ
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

आत्मनः सकाशाद् यद् इदं सृष्टं तत् सन्नियच्छति संहरति । घनानीकं मेघ-समूहम् ॥४३॥

ātmanaḥ sakāśād yad idaṁ sṛṣṭaṁ tat sanniyacchati saṁharati | ghanānīkaṁ megha-samūham ||43||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. ātmanaḥ sakāśāt — from Himself; sṛṣṭam — created; sanniyacchati — draws together/withdraws; saṁharati — dissolves; ghanānīkam — cloud mass; megha-samūham — collection of clouds.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. The same Lord who creates and preserves also withdraws the cosmos. The cloud image stresses that dissolution is the gathering back of what had previously emerged.

ŚB 2.10.44

इत्थम्भावेन कथितो भगवान् भगवत्तमः ।
नेत्थम्भावेन हि परं द्रष्टुमर्हन्ति सूरयः ॥ ४४ ॥

Thus the Supreme Lord has been spoken of through these modes of cosmic function. Yet the realized seers do not regard His highest reality as exhausted by such descriptions alone.

Word-for-word

ittham-bhāvena — in this manner/mode; kathitaḥ — described; bhagavān — the Lord; bhagavattamaḥ — supremely endowed with divine majesty; na — not; ittham-bhāvena — merely in this mode; param — the Supreme; draṣṭum arhanti — ought to behold/understand; sūrayaḥ — the enlightened sages.

Transliteration
ittham-bhāvena kathito
bhagavān bhagavattamaḥ
nettham-bhāvena hi paraṁ
draṣṭum arhanti sūrayaḥ
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

कर्तृत्वाद्य्-अपवादेन दशमस्य शुद्धिम् आह । इत्थम्-भावेन स्रष्टृत्वादि-रूपेण श्रुत्या कथितः । सूरयस् तु परं केवलम् एवं-रूपेणैव द्रष्टुं नार्हन्ति ॥४४॥

kartṛtvādy-apavādena daśamasya śuddhim āha | ittham-bhāvena sraṣṭṛtvādi-rūpeṇa śrutyā kathitaḥ | sūrayas tu paraṁ kevalam evaṁ-rūpeṇaiva draṣṭuṁ nārhanti ||44||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. kartṛtva-ādi-apavādena — by ultimately qualifying/removing literal material agency; daśamasya śuddhim — purification of the tenth topic, the shelter; sraṣṭṛtva-ādi-rūpeṇa — in modes such as creator; param — the Supreme; na arhanti — should not be limited to that alone.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. Creation, maintenance and dissolution are valid scriptural descriptions, but they do not delimit the Lord’s ultimate nature. This prepares the explicit denial of material agency in the next verse.
