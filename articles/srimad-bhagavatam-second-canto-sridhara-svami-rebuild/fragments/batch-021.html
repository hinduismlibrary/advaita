ŚB 2.10.11

तास्ववात्सीत् स्वसृष्टासु सहस्रं परिवत्सरान् ।
तेन नारायणो नाम यदापः पुरुषोद्भवाः ॥ ११ ॥

He dwelt for a thousand years of His own measure in the waters He had created. Because those waters arose from the Supreme Person, they are called nāra, and because they became His resting place, He is known as Nārāyaṇa.

Word-for-word

tāsu — in those waters; avātsīt — He dwelt; sva-sṛṣṭāsu — created by Himself; sahasram parivatsarān — for a thousand years; tena — for that reason; nārāyaṇaḥ nāma — He is named Nārāyaṇa; yat — because; āpaḥ — the waters; puruṣa-udbhavāḥ — arose from the Supreme Person.

Transliteration
tāsv avātsīt sva-sṛṣṭāsu
sahasraṁ parivatsarān
tena nārāyaṇo nāma
yad āpaḥ puruṣodbhavāḥ
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अप्सु वासं नारायण-नाम-निरुक्त्या स्पष्टयति । तेन अप्सु वासेन । यद् यस्मात् पुरुषो नरः, तस्माद् उद्भवो यासां, ता नारा आपोऽयनम् अस्येति नारायण इत्य् अर्थः । तद् उक्तम्— “आपो नारा इति प्रोक्ता आपो वै नर-सूनवः । अयनं तस्य ताः पूर्वं तेन नारायणः स्मृतः ॥” इति ॥११॥

apsu vāsaṁ nārāyaṇa-nāma-niruktyā spaṣṭayati | tena apsu vāsena | yad yasmāt puruṣo naraḥ, tasmād udbhavo yāsāṁ, tā nārā āpo ’yanam asyeti nārāyaṇa ity arthaḥ | tad uktam—“āpo nārā iti proktā āpo vai nara-sūnavaḥ | ayanaṁ tasya tāḥ pūrvaṁ tena nārāyaṇaḥ smṛtaḥ ||” iti ||11||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. apsu vāsaḥ — dwelling in the waters; nārāyaṇa-nāma-niruktyā — by explaining the derivation of the name Nārāyaṇa; puruṣaḥ naraḥ — the Supreme Person is nara; tasmāt udbhavaḥ — arising from Him; nārāḥ āpaḥ — the waters are called nāra; ayanam asya — they are His resting place; nārāyaṇaḥ — therefore Nārāyaṇa.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. Śrīdhara takes the verse as an etymological explanation of Nārāyaṇa: the waters issue from Nara and then become His ayana, His place of repose.

Transliteration

Śrīdhara word-for-word. tasya prabhāvam āha — he states the Lord’s power; dravyam upādānam — substance is the material cause; karma-ādīni nimittāni — action and the others are instrumental causes; jīvaḥ bhoktā — the jīva is the experiencer; kārya-kṣamā bhavanti — they become capable of carrying out their functions only by His favor.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. The verse does not deny these factors their respective roles; it denies them independence. Matter, instrumental causes and the experiencer become effective only within the Lord’s sustaining power.

ŚB 2.10.15

ओजः सहो बलं जज्ञे ततः प्राणो महानसु: ॥ १५ ॥
