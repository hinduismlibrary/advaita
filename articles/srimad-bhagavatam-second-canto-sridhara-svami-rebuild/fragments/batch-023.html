ŚB 2.10.24

हस्तौ रुरुहतुस्तस्य नानाकर्मचिकीर्षया ।
तयोस्तु बलवानिन्द्र आदानमुभयाश्रयम् ॥ २४ ॥

When He desired to perform many kinds of action, His two hands became manifest. In them appeared the power of action with mighty Indra as presiding deity, and grasping became the activity dependent upon both.

Word-for-word

hastau — two hands; ruruhatuḥ — became manifest; nānā-karma — many actions; cikīrṣayā — from the desire to perform; balavān indraḥ — powerful Indra; ādānam — taking or grasping; ubhaya-āśrayam — dependent on both.

Transliteration
hastau ruruhatus tasya
nānā-karma-cikīrṣayā
tayos tu balavān indra
ādānam ubhayāśrayam
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

रुरुहतुर् निर्भिन्नौ । बलम् इन्द्रियम्, इन्द्रो देवता, तद्-उभयाश्रयम् आदानं कर्म ॥२४॥

ruruhatur nirbhinnau | balam indriyam, indro devatā, tad-ubhayāśrayam ādānaṁ karma ||24||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. ruruhatuḥ — became differentiated; balam — operative power; indraḥ — presiding deity; ādānam — grasping; karma — the action.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. The hands, their operative strength and Indra are correlated; grasping is the action dependent on the faculty and its deity.

ŚB 2.10.25

गतिं जिगीषतः पादौ रुरुहातेऽभिकामिकाम् ।
पद्भ्यां यज्ञः स्वयं हव्यं कर्मभिः क्रियते नृभिः ॥ २५ ॥

When He desired movement toward a chosen destination, His feet appeared. With the feet is associated Yajña, the Lord of sacrifice; through the power of locomotion people reach the places and materials required for sacrificial work.

Word-for-word

gatim — movement; jigīṣataḥ — desiring to go or attain; pādau — the feet; abhikāmikām — desired; yajñaḥ — Yajña; havyam — sacrificial offering; karmabhiḥ — by action; nṛbhiḥ — by people.

Transliteration
gatiṁ jigīṣataḥ pādau
ruruhāte ’bhikāmikām
padbhyāṁ yajñaḥ svayaṁ havyaṁ
karmabhiḥ kriyate nṛbhiḥ
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अभिकामिकाम् अभीष्टां हिताम् इत्य् अर्थः । पद्भ्यां सह यज्ञो विष्णुर् एव स्वयं तद्-अधिष्ठातृ-रूपेण स्थितः । कर्मभिर् इति गत्य्-आख्या कर्म-शक्तिर् इन्द्रियम् उक्तम् । हव्यं क्रियत इति गति-प्राप्यं यज्ञार्थं द्रव्यं विषय इत्य् उक्तम् ॥२५॥

abhikāmikām abhīṣṭāṁ hitām ity arthaḥ | padbhyāṁ saha yajño viṣṇur eva svayaṁ tad-adhiṣṭhātṛ-rūpeṇa sthitaḥ | karmabhir iti gaty-ākhyā karma-śaktir indriyam uktam | havyaṁ kriyata iti gati-prāpyaṁ yajñārthaṁ dravyaṁ viṣaya ity uktam ||25||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. abhikāmikām — desired and beneficial; yajñaḥ viṣṇuḥ — Yajña, Viṣṇu; gati-ākhyā karma-śaktiḥ — power of locomotion; yajñārtham dravyam — material reached for sacrifice.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. Śrīdhara reads the feet as the organ of locomotion and Yajña/Viṣṇu as its presiding deity; the object is what one reaches by movement for purposeful action.

ŚB 2.10.26

निरभिद्यत शिश्नो वै प्रजानन्दामृतार्थिनः ।
उपस्थ आसीत्कामानां प्रियं तदुभयाश्रयम् ॥ २६ ॥

When He desired offspring, pleasure and the nectar-like enjoyment associated with generation, the generative organ became manifest. The reproductive faculty and its presiding power appeared, and the pleasure of desire depends upon both.

Word-for-word

nirabhidyata — became manifest; śiśnaḥ — genitals; prajā — offspring; ānanda — pleasure; amṛta-arthinaḥ — desiring nectar-like enjoyment; upasthaḥ — reproductive faculty; kāmānām — of desires; priyam — pleasure; ubhaya-āśrayam — dependent on both.

Transliteration
nirabhidyata śiśno vai
prajānandāmṛtārthinaḥ
upastha āsīt kāmānāṁ
priyaṁ tad-ubhayāśrayam
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

प्रजा अपत्यम् आनन्दो रतिर् अमृतं स्वर्गादि तद्-अर्थिनः । शिश्नोऽधिष्ठानम् उपस्थम् इन्द्रियं प्रजापतिश् चासीद् इति ज्ञेयम् । तद्-उभयाश्रयं कामानां सम्बन्धि प्रियं सुखम् ॥२६॥

prajā apatyam ānando ratir amṛtaṁ svargādi tad-arthinaḥ | śiśno ’dhiṣṭhānam upastham indriyaṁ prajāpatiś cāsīd iti jñeyam | tad-ubhayāśrayaṁ kāmānāṁ sambandhi priyaṁ sukham ||26||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. prajā — offspring; ānandaḥ — delight; amṛtam — heavenly enjoyment; upastham — reproductive faculty; prajāpatiḥ — presiding deity; priyam sukham — desired pleasure.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. Śrīdhara distinguishes the physical seat, the reproductive faculty and Prajāpati as deity, with associated pleasure as their object.
