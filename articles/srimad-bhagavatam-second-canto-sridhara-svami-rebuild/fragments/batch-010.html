ŚB 2.7.35

ये वा मृधे समितिशालिन आत्तचापाः
काम्बोजमत्स्यकुरुसृञ्जयकैकयाद्याः ।
यास्यन्त्यदर्शनमलं बलपार्थभीम-
व्याजाह्वयेन हरिणा निलयं तदीयम् ॥ ३५ ॥

Likewise the renowned warriors who entered battle with bows drawn—the Kāmbojas, Matsyas, Kurus, Sṛñjayas, Kekayas and others—would disappear from mortal sight through Hari acting under names such as Bala, Pārtha and Bhīma, and would reach the destination appropriate to Him.

Word-for-word

mṛdhe — in battle; samiti-śālinaḥ — renowned in combat; ātta-cāpāḥ — with bows taken up; kāmboja-matsya-kuru-sṛñjaya-kaikaya-ādyāḥ — Kāmbojas, Matsyas, Kurus, Sṛñjayas, Kekayas and others; yāsyanti — will go; adarśanam — beyond mortal sight; balapārthabhīma-vyāja-āhvayena — by Hari under names such as Bala, Pārtha and Bhīma; hariṇā — by Hari; nilayam tadīyam — His destination/abode.

Transliteration
ye vā mṛdhe samiti-śālina ātta-cāpāḥ
kāmboja-matsya-kuru-sṛñjaya-kaikayādyāḥ
yāsyanty adarśanam alaṁ bala-pārtha-bhīma-
vyājāhvayena hariṇā nilayaṁ tadīyam
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

समितौ सङ्ग्रामे शालन्ते श्लाघन्ते ते समिति-शालिनः । ननु प्रलम्ब-खर-कपि-बल्वल-रुक्मि-प्रमुखा बलभद्रेण निहताः, काम्बोजादयश् च भीमार्जुनादिभिः, शम्बरः प्रद्युम्नेन, यवनो मुचुकुन्देन, न तु हरिणा, तत्राह । बल-पार्थ-भीमा इत्य्-आदयो व्याजेनैवाह्वया नामानि यस्य तेन । सप्तोक्षाणस् तु तेन दमिताः कालान्तरे यास्यन्तीति भावः । अत्र प्रलम्ब-खरादयः सायुज्यं, पौण्ड्रक-दन्तवक्रादयो वैकुण्ठं यास्यन्तीति विवेचनीयम् अग्रे व्याख्यास्यमान-युक्तेः ॥३४-३५॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. This completes the sentence begun in 2.7.34. Śrīdhara explains that although several enemies were outwardly slain by Balarāma, Arjuna, Bhīma, Pradyumna or Mucukunda, Hari was acting through those names and powers. He also distinguishes different final attainments among the slain.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. This is Vyāsa. The Lord sees that people’s intelligence and lifespan diminish while the Vedic ocean remains difficult to cross, so appearing through Satyavatī He divides it branch by branch.

अन्तर्-ज्ञानाभावेऽपि मायेयम् इति ज्ञानं तत्-कृपया बहूनाम् अस्तीत्य् आह—वेदाहम् इति त्रिभिः । यूयम् इति सनकादीन् अन्तर्भाव्य बहुत्वम् । दैत्य-वर्यः प्रह्लादः । मनुः स्वायम्भुवस् तस्य पत्नी शतरूपा च । तद्-आत्मजाः प्रिय-व्रतोत्तानपादौ पुत्रौ कन्याश् च । प्राचीनबर्हिषो विसर्ग-लोपश् छान्दसः । अङ्गो वेन-पिता ॥४३॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. Verses 43–45 list persons who, by divine grace, understand something of the Lord’s Yogamāyā even without knowing its absolute limit. Śrīdhara identifies the “foremost Daitya” as Prahlāda, Manu as Svāyambhuva, his wife as Śatarūpā, and Aṅga as Vena’s father.

ŚB 2.7.44

इक्ष्वाकुरैलमुचुकुन्दविदेहगाधि-
रघ्वम्बरीषसगरा गयनाहुषाद्याः ।
मान्धात्रलर्कशतधन्वनुरन्तिदेवा
देवव्रतो बलिरमूर्त्तरयो दिलीपः ॥ ४४ ॥

The list also includes Ikṣvāku, Aila, Mucukunda, the king of Videha, Gādhi, Raghu, Ambarīṣa, Sagara, Gaya, Nahuṣa and others; Māndhātā, Alarka, Śatadhanu, Anu, Rantideva, Devavrata, Bali, Amūrtaraya and Dilīpa.

Word-for-word

ikṣvākuḥ — Ikṣvāku; ailaḥ — Aila; mucukundaḥ — Mucukunda; videhaḥ — king of Videha; gādhiḥ — Gādhi; raghu — Raghu; ambarīṣa — Ambarīṣa; sagara — Sagara; gaya — Gaya; nāhuṣa — Nahuṣa; māndhātṛ — Māndhātā; alarka — Alarka; śatadhanu — Śatadhanu; anu — Anu; rantideva — Rantideva; devavrata — Bhīṣma; bali — Bali; amūrtaraya — Amūrtaraya; dilīpa — Dilīpa.

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

शतधनुः अनुश् च । सन्धिर् आर्षः । रन्तिदेवा इति पाठः सुगमः । एक-पद्ये एतैः सहितो देवव्रत इत्य् अर्थः ॥४४॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. Śrīdhara parses Śatadhanu and Anu as separate names, notes the archaic sandhi, and records that the reading Rantidevāḥ is straightforward. The list continues into verse 45.
