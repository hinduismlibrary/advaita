Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 135 — Bathing the Sun Image

BP 1.135.1

नारद उवाच । अतः परं प्रवक्ष्यामि स्नानकर्मविधिं तव । स्नापकस्तु महाप्राज्ञो ब्राह्मणो वेदपारगः ॥

Nārada said: I shall now explain to you the procedure for the bathing rite. The bath officiant should be a very wise Brahmin, versed in the Vedas.

BP 1.135.2

अभिज्ञः सौरशास्त्राणामरुणो यदुसत्तम । भोजको भोजकैश्चान्यैर्ब्राह्मणैश्च तथा वृतः ॥

Learned in the scriptures of the Sun, Aruṇa, O best of the Yadus, should be accompanied by Sun-priests and other Brahmins.

BP 1.135.3

दिशाभागे मण्डलस्य ईशाने वै यथाक्रमम् । हस्तमात्रप्रमाणं तु भद्रपीठं तु विन्यसेत् ॥

In the northeast part of the ritual diagram, in due order, one should place an auspicious seat measuring one cubit.

BP 1.135.4

हस्तिना शकटेनापि भक्त्या ब्रह्मरथेन च । मंगलैर्ब्रह्मघोषैश्च देवं प्रासादमानयेत् ॥

One should bring the deity to the temple by elephant, by cart, or by Brahmā’s chariot, with devotion, amid auspicious observances and Vedic recitations.

BP 1.135.5

भद्रपीठं समादाय भद्रं कर्णेति मन्त्रतः । सूत्रधारस्तथा प्रोक्तः शुक्लाम्बरधरः शुचिः ॥

Taking the auspicious seat with the formula “May we hear what is good,” the ritual carpenter is described as pure and clad in white garments.

BP 1.135.6

स्नापयेत्कलशं गृह्य देवदेवं विभावसुम् । सामुद्रं तोयमाहृत्य जाह्नवं यामुनं तथा ॥

Taking a pitcher, one should bathe the god of gods, Vibhāvasu, with ocean water, and likewise with Gaṅgā and Yamunā water.

BP 1.135.7

सारस्वतं जलं पुण्यं चान्द्रभागं ससैन्धवम् । पुष्करस्य जलं श्रेष्ठं गिरिप्रस्रवणोदकम् ॥

Sacred Sarasvatī water, Cāndrabhāgā and Sindhu water, the excellent water of Puṣkara, and water from mountain springs;

BP 1.135.8

अन्यद्वापि शुचि तोयं नदीनदतडागजम् । यथाशक्त्या उपाहृत्य कलशैः काञ्चनादिभिः ॥

or any other pure water from rivers, streams, or ponds, collected as one is able in pitchers of gold and other materials.

BP 1.135.9

भोजकाश्चाष्टभिः सूर्यं कलशैः स्नापयन्ति वै । ततस्तु मणिरत्नानि सर्वबीजौषधीस्तथा ॥

The Sun-priests bathe the Sun with eight pitchers. Then gems and jewels, all seeds, and medicinal herbs;

BP 1.135.10

सुगन्धीनि च माल्यानि स्थलजान्यम्बुजानि च । चन्दनानि च मुख्यानि गन्धाश्च विविधास्तथा ॥

fragrant garlands, flowers grown on land and in water, fine sandalwoods, and various fragrances;

BP 1.135.11

ब्राह्मी सुवर्चला मुस्ता विष्णुक्रान्ता शतावरी । दूर्वा च शिबिपुष्पी च प्रियङ्गू रजनी वचा ॥

water hyssop, Suvarcalā, nutgrass, dwarf morning glory, asparagus, Bermuda grass, Śibipuṣpī, Priyaṅgu, turmeric, and sweet flag.

BP 1.135.12

सम्भृत्यैतांस्तु सम्भारान्स्नानकर्मविभागवित् । बलाश्वत्थशिरीषाणां पल्लवैः कुशसंयुतैः ॥

The person who knows the divisions of the bathing rite should gather these materials with sprigs of country mallow, sacred fig, and siris, together with sacred grass.

BP 1.135.13

कलशोपरि विन्यस्य दद्यादर्घ्यं रवेः सदा । काञ्चनै राजतैस्ताम्रैर्मृण्मयैः कलशैस्तथा ॥

Having placed them above the pitchers, one should always offer water to the Sun, with pitchers of gold, silver, copper, and clay.

BP 1.135.14

साक्षतैः सहिरण्यैश्च सर्वौषधिसमन्वितैः । गायत्र्या परिपूतैस्तु षोडशैः स्नापयेद्रविम् ॥

With unbroken rice, gold, and all medicinal herbs, purified by the Gāyatrī, one should bathe the Sun with sixteen pitchers.

BP 1.135.15

कुशोत्तरां ततः कृत्वा वेदिं पक्वेष्टकामयीम् । तस्यां वेद्यां समारोप्य परिधाप्य च वाससी ॥

Then one should make an altar of baked bricks, topped with sacred grass; placing the image on that altar, one should clothe it with two garments.

BP 1.135.16

प्रतिमामभिषिञ्चेच्च सोपवासः प्रयत्नतः । मूर्ध्नि सर्वौषधीः कृत्वा तथैवामलकानि च ॥

Fasting, one should carefully bathe the image, placing all the medicinal herbs on its head, and likewise the āmalaka fruits.

BP 1.135.17

मन्त्रेण मृत्तिकां चापि मन्त्रतश्च जलं तथा । त्वं देवी वन्दिता देवैः सकलैर्दैत्यदानवैः ॥

With a formula for the clay and likewise for the water: “You, Goddess, are worshipped by all the gods, Daityas, and demons.”

BP 1.135.18

तेन संस्थापिता मूर्ध्नि मया देवस्य शुद्धये । आदिस्त्वं सर्वभूतानां देवतानां च सर्वथा ॥

“By that, you are placed by me upon the head for the purification of the deity. You are indeed the origin of all beings and all deities.”

BP 1.135.19

रसानां पतये तुभ्यमाह्वानं च कृतं मया । इत्थं पौराणिकैर्मंत्रैवैदिकैश्च विशेषतः ॥

“I have made an invocation to you, lord of the essences.” In this way, especially with Purāṇic and Vedic formulas,

BP 1.135.20

कार्यं हि वारुणं स्नानं देवस्य यदुनंदन । इत्थमुच्चारयेद्वाचं कुर्यात्स्नानं विचक्षणः ॥

the water-bathing of the deity must be performed, O descendant of Yadu. The discerning one should speak thus and perform the bathing.

BP 1.135.21

देवास्त्वामभिषिञ्चन्तु ब्रह्मविष्णुशिवादयः । व्योमगङ्गा च पूर्णेन द्वितीयकलशेन तु ॥

May the gods, beginning with Brahmā, Viṣṇu, and Śiva, bathe you; and may heavenly Gaṅgā do so with the full second pitcher.

BP 1.135.22

सारस्वतस्य पूर्णेन कलशेन सुरोत्तम । शक्रादयोभिषिंचन्तु लोकपालाः सुरोत्तमाः ॥

With a full pitcher of Sarasvatī water, O best of the gods, may Indra and the excellent guardians of the worlds bathe you.

BP 1.135.23

सागरोदकपूर्णेन चतुर्थकलशेन तु । वारिणा परिपूर्णेन पद्मपत्रसुगन्धिना ॥

“With a fourth pitcher filled with ocean water, and with water abundant and fragrant with lotus leaves.”

BP 1.135.24

पञ्चमेनाभिषिञ्चन्तु नागाश्च कलशेन तु । हिमवद्धेमकूटाद्याश्चाभिषिञ्चन्तु वारिणा ॥

May the Nāgas bathe you with the fifth pitcher; may Himavat, Hemakūṭa, and the other mountains bathe you with water.

BP 1.135.25

नैरृतोदकपूर्णेन षष्ठेन कलशेन तु । सर्वतीर्थाम्बुपूर्णेन पद्मरेणुसुवासिना ॥

“With the sixth pitcher filled with water from the southwest, and filled with the waters of all sacred bathing places, fragrant with lotus pollen.”

BP 1.135.26

सप्तमेनाभिषिञ्चन्तु ऋषयः सप्त ये वराः । वसवश्चाभिषिञ्चन्तु कलशेनाष्टमेन वै ॥

May the seven excellent sages bathe you with the seventh pitcher; may the Vasus also bathe you with the eighth.

BP 1.135.27

अष्टमङ्गलयुक्तेन देवदेव नमोऽस्तु ते । ततो वै कलशैर्दिव्यैः स्नानकर्म समारभेत् ॥

“O god of gods, homage to you, joined with the eight auspicious things.” Then one should begin the bathing rite with divine pitchers.

BP 1.135.28

समुद्रं गच्छ यः प्रोक्तो मन्त्रमेतमुदीरयेत् । हिरण्यगर्भेति च यो मन्त्रस्तं समुदीरयेत् ॥

One should utter the formula “Go to the ocean,” and one should also utter the formula “Golden Embryo.”

BP 1.135.29

समुद्रज्येष्ठेति मन्त्रेण क्षालयेन्मृत्तिकान्वितम् । सिनीवालीति मन्त्रेण दद्याद्वल्मीकमृत्तिकाम् ॥

With the formula “Eldest of the ocean,” one should wash it together with clay; with the formula to Sinīvālī, one should offer anthill clay.

BP 1.135.30

शम्युदुम्बरमश्वत्थं न्यग्रोधं च पलाशकम् । यज्ञं यज्ञेति मन्त्रेण दद्यात्पञ्चकषायिकम् ॥

With the formula “Sacrifice, O sacrifice,” one should offer the five astringent plants: desert bean tree, cluster fig, sacred fig, banyan, and flame-of-the-forest.

BP 1.135.31

पञ्चगव्यं पवित्रं च आहरेत्ताम्रभाजने । गायत्र्या चैव गोमूत्रं गन्धद्वारेति गोमयम् ॥

One should bring the purifying five products of the cow in a copper vessel: cow urine with the Gāyatrī, and cow dung with the formula “Fragrant Gateway.”

BP 1.135.32

आप्यायस्वेति च क्षीरं दधिक्राव्णेति वै दधि । तेजोऽसीति घृतं तद्वद्देवस्य त्वा कुशोदकम् ॥

Milk with the formula “Grow Full,” curd with the formula to Dadhikrāvan, ghee with “You are Splendor,” and sacred-grass water with “You, belonging to the god.”

BP 1.135.33

एवमादिविधियुतं पञ्चगव्यं प्रकीर्तितम् । या ओषधीति मंत्रेण स्नानमोषधिभिः क्रमात् ॥

The five cow products, joined with these and the other procedures, are thus prescribed. Then, in order, one should bathe with herbs using the formula “O plants.”

BP 1.135.34

द्रुपदाभिः पुनस्तस्य कुर्याच्चोद्वर्तनं बुधः । शिरः स्नानं ततो दद्यान्मानस्तोकाभिमन्त्रितम् ॥

The wise one should then rub the image with the “wooden post” formulas. Then one should give a head-bath with the formula “Do not harm our offspring.”

BP 1.135.35

विष्णोरराटमन्त्रेण दद्याद्गन्धोदकं शुभम् । ततो नद्युद्भवेनैव क्षालयेच्छुद्धवारिणा ॥

With the formula to Viṣṇu, one should offer auspicious scented water; thereafter one should rinse the image with pure river water.

BP 1.135.36

जातवेदसमुच्चार्य वस्त्रपूतेन वारिणा । तत आवाहयेद्देवं रक्तमाल्याम्बरं शुभम् ॥

Having uttered the formula “All-knowing Fire,” one should use water filtered through cloth, then invoke the deity with an auspicious red garland and garment.

BP 1.135.37

एह्येहि भगवन्भानो लोकानुग्रहकारक । यज्ञभागं गृहाणार्घ्यमर्कदेव नमोऽस्तु ते ॥

“Come, come, O blessed Sun, benefactor of the worlds. Accept the sacrificial share and water offering. O Sun, homage to you.”

BP 1.135.38

हिरण्येन तु पात्रेण देवायार्घ्यं प्रदापयेत् । इदं विष्णुर्विचक्रमे मन्त्रेणार्घ्यं समर्पयेत् ॥

One should have water offered to the deity in a golden vessel; with the formula “Viṣṇu strode across this,” one should present the water offering.

BP 1.135.39

पार्थिवैः प्रथमं कलशैः स्नापयेद्भास्करं बुधः । ततस्त्वौदुम्बरैर्वीर राजतैस्तदनन्तरम् ॥

The wise one should first bathe the Sun with earthen pitchers; then, O hero, with pitchers of cluster-fig wood, and thereafter with silver ones.

BP 1.135.40

ततस्तु काञ्चनैर्देवं स्नापयेद्यदुनन्दन । सर्वतीर्थजलैर्युक्तं सर्वौषधिसमन्वितम् ॥

Then, O descendant of Yadu, one should bathe the deity with golden pitchers, joined with waters from all sacred bathing places and all medicinal herbs.

BP 1.135.41

शङ्खमादाय देवस्य ततो मूर्धनि शङ्कर । दत्त्वा पुष्पाणि देवस्य मूर्ध्नि यत्नाद्विचक्षणः ॥

Then, O Śaṅkara, taking a conch, the discerning one should carefully place flowers upon the deity’s head.

BP 1.135.42

तोयमुत्क्षिप्य यत्नेन ततः स्नपनमाचरेत् । प्रथमं स्नापयेद्देवं वारिणा यदुनन्दन ॥

Having carefully lifted up the water, one should perform the bathing. First one should bathe the deity with water, O descendant of Yadu.

BP 1.135.43

ततस्तु पयसा राजन्पायसेन ततस्तु वै । घृतेन मधुना वापि तथा इक्षुरसेन च ॥

Then with milk, O king, and then with rice pudding; also with ghee, honey, and sugarcane juice.

BP 1.135.44

अग्निष्टोमस्य यज्ञस्य गोमेधस्य च सुव्रत । ज्योतिष्टोमस्य राजेन्द्र वाजपेयस्य वै विभो ॥

O firm-vowed one, of the fire-praise sacrifice and cow sacrifices, of the Jyotiṣṭoma, O king, of the strength-drink sacrifice, O lord,

BP 1.135.45

राजसूयाश्वमेधाभ्यां घृताद्यैर्लभते फलम् । यस्तु कारयते स्नानं यस्तु भक्त्या प्रपश्यति क्रियमाणं तु देवस्य स्नानं यदुकुलोद्वह ॥

and of the royal-consecration sacrifice and horse sacrifice, one obtains the fruit through ghee and the other substances. Whoever has the bathing performed, whoever devoutly watches the deity’s bathing as it is performed, O descendant of Yadu;

BP 1.135.46

य एते कथिता यज्ञा एतेषां क्रमशः फलम् । अर्चां च कुरुशार्दूल दृष्ट्वा वै लभते फलम् ॥

by seeing the image, O tiger among the Kurus, receives in due order the fruit of these sacrifices.

BP 1.135.47

स्नानं तु यत्नतः कार्यं देवदेवस्य सुव्रत । यथा न लङ्घयेत्कश्चिद्देवस्य स्नपनं विभोः ॥

The bathing of the god of gods must be performed carefully, O firm-vowed one, so that no one neglects the Lord’s bathing.

BP 1.135.48

न प्राश्नन्ति यथा काकास्तीर्थं लोकविगर्हिताः । स्नानोदकं तु देवस्य अथवा पय एव हि ॥

Just as crows, despised by people, do not partake of a sacred bathing place, so one should not partake of the deity’s bathing water or milk.

BP 1.135.49

भूमौ गतं यथा चैव प्राश्नाति यदुनंदन । रोगं प्राप्नोति कर्ता वै दुःखं कारयिता तथा ॥

Whoever partakes of it after it has fallen on the ground, O descendant of Yadu, becomes ill; the one who causes this also suffers.

BP 1.135.50

तस्माद्यत्नेन कर्तव्यं देवस्य स्नपनं विभोः ॥

Therefore the bathing of the Lord must be performed carefully.

BP 1.135.51

स्नापयित्वा क्रमेणेत्थं स्नानकर्म विधानवित् । ततो वर्धनिकां गृह्य वारिधारां समुत्सृजेत् ॥

Having thus bathed the deity in due order, the one who knows the procedure should take the water pitcher and release a stream.

BP 1.135.52

त्रिवारान्पुरतोऽर्कस्य आचमस्वेति च ब्रुवन् । वेदोसीति च मन्त्रेण उपवीतं प्रवापयेत् ॥

Saying “Sip” three times before the Sun, and with the formula “You are the Veda,” one should offer the sacred thread.

BP 1.135.53

बृहस्पतेति मन्त्रेण वस्त्रयुग्मं प्रदापयेत् । यत्नक्रमं प्रकुर्वाणः पुष्पमालां प्रदापयेत् ॥

With the formula to Bṛhaspati, one should offer a pair of garments; performing the rite carefully, one should offer a flower garland.

BP 1.135.54

धूरसीति च मन्त्रेण धूपं दद्यात्सगुग्गलम् । समिद्धोञ्जनमन्त्रेण अञ्जनं तु प्रदापयेत् ॥

With the formula “You are the bearer,” one should offer incense with guggul resin; with the formula “Well-kindled,” one should offer collyrium.

BP 1.135.55

युञ्जानीति च मन्त्रेण रोचनां तस्य दापयेत् । आरार्तिकं च वै कुर्याद्दीर्घायुष्ट्वाय वर्चसे ॥

With the formula “I yoke,” one should offer yellow pigment to him, and one should wave the lamp for long life and radiance.

BP 1.135.56

स्नानकर्म त्विदं प्रोक्तं भास्करस्य महात्मनः । भोजका ब्राह्मणाश्चैव क्रियां कुर्युः प्रयत्नतः ॥

This bathing rite of the great Sun has been declared. The Sun-priests and Brahmins should carefully perform the rite.

BP 1.135.57

बहवृचोऽथर्वणश्चैव छन्दोगोध्वर्युरेव च । स्नापकस्य च चिह्नानि ये च मूर्तिधरास्तथा ॥

The Rig reciters, Atharva reciters, Sāma chanters, and ritual priests, the marks of the bath officiant, and those who bear the image,

BP 1.135.58

तेषां प्रवक्ष्यामि विभो शृणु चैकमनाः किल । सम्पूर्णगात्रो मतिमाञ्छास्त्रज्ञः प्रियदर्शनः ॥

I shall tell their characteristics, O Lord; listen with a focused mind: complete in body, intelligent, learned in scripture, and pleasing to behold;

BP 1.135.59

कुलीनः श्रद्दधानश्च आर्यदेशसमुद्भवः । न स्थूलो न कृशो दीर्घः सौरशास्त्रविशारदः ॥

of good family, faithful, born in a noble land, neither stout nor thin, tall, and skilled in the scriptures of the Sun.

BP 1.135.60

यश्च युक्तो जितात्मा च गुरुभक्तो जितेन्द्रियः । पञ्चविंशतितत्त्वज्ञः स्थापकः समुदाहृतः ॥

Disciplined, self-controlled, devoted to the teacher, with senses conquered, and knowing the twenty-five principles: such is called an image-installer.

BP 1.135.61

वर्जनीयांश्च वक्ष्यामि यैस्तु कर्म न कारयेत् । हीनाङ्गश्चाधिकाङ्गश्च वामनो विकटस्तथा ॥

I shall tell those to be avoided, by whom the rite should not be performed: one deficient in limbs, excessive in limbs, a dwarf, or deformed;

BP 1.135.62

नातिगौरो न कृष्णश्च स्नापनाय प्रयोजयेत् । चार्वाको याजकश्चैव नित्यं गोमुखदम्भकः ॥

One very fair or dark should not be employed for the bathing; nor a Cārvāka officiant, always hypocritical and cow-faced;

BP 1.135.63

अशुचिव्रतसंयुक्तः श्यामदन्तोऽथ मत्सरी । कोपनो दुष्टशीलश्च युवा वा वृद्ध एव च ॥

one joined to impure observances, dark-toothed, envious, angry, of bad conduct, young or old;

BP 1.135.64

श्वित्री कुष्ठी च रोगी च काणो दुर्मतिरेव च । संकीर्णो जातिहीनश्च तथा न वृषलीपतिः ॥

one with white patches, a leper, ill, one-eyed, foolish, of mixed birth, low-born, or the husband of a Śūdra woman;

BP 1.135.65

कुब्जश्चाधस्तथा व्यंगः खल्वाटो विकलेन्द्रियः । अविनीतो दुरात्मा च विकलः पङ्गुरेव च ॥

a hunchback, short in the lower body, deformed, bald, defective in the senses, undisciplined, evil-minded, impaired, or lame;

BP 1.135.66

तिथिनक्षत्रयोगानां वाराणां च तथा विभो । सूचको जीविकार्थं हि यश्च मूल्येन पाठयेत् ॥

one who reveals lunar days, constellations, conjunctions, and weekdays for a livelihood, and who recites for a fee, O Lord;

BP 1.135.67

ईदृशान्स्नापकान्सर्वान्वर्जयेत प्रयत्नतः । तस्मात्सर्वप्रयत्नेन परीक्ष्याः स्नापका बुधैः ॥

all such bath officiants should be carefully avoided. Therefore the wise should examine bath officiants with every effort.
