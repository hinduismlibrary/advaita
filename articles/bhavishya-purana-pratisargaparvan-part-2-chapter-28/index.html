BP 3.2.28.1

सूत उवाच ।
अथ ते वर्णयिष्यामि कथां साधूपचारिताम् ।
नृपोपदेशतः साधुः कृतार्थोऽभूद्वणिग्यथा ॥ 1 ॥

Sūta said: Now I will tell a story, praised by the virtuous, of how a merchant prospered through a king's advice.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
सूत sūta Sūta
उवाच uvāca said
अथ atha then
ते te they
वर्णयिष्यामि varṇayiṣyāmi I shall describe
कथां kathāṃ the story
साधूपचारिताम् sādhūpacāritām recounted by the virtuous
नृपोपदेशतः nṛpopadeśataḥ by the king's instruction
साधुः sādhuḥ the merchant
कृतार्थोऽभूद्वणिग्यथा kṛtārtho'bhūdvaṇigyathā how the merchant became successful
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

sūta uvāca | atha te varṇayiṣyāmi kathāṃ sādhūpacāritām | nṛpopadeśataḥ sādhuḥ kṛtārtho'bhūdvaṇigyathā || 1 ||

BP 3.2.28.2

मणिपूरपती राजा चन्द्रचूडो महायशाः ।
सह प्रजाभिरानर्च सत्यनारायणं प्रभुम् ॥ 2 ॥

In Maṇipūra ruled the renowned King Candracūḍa. Together with his subjects he worshipped Lord Satyanārāyaṇa.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
मणिपूरपती maṇipūrapatī the king of Maṇipūra
राजा rājā king
चन्द्रचूडो candracūḍo Candracūḍa
महायशाः mahāyaśāḥ greatly renowned
सह saha with
प्रजाभिरानर्च prajābhirānarca honored by his subjects
सत्यनारायणं satyanārāyaṇaṃ Satyanārāyaṇa
प्रभुम् prabhum the Lord
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

maṇipūrapatī rājā candracūḍo mahāyaśāḥ | saha prajābhirānarca satyanārāyaṇaṃ prabhum || 2 ||

BP 3.2.28.3

धनैरापूर्य तरणीः सह गच्छन्नदीतटे ॥ 3 ॥

A virtuous merchant worth a hundred thousand lived in Ratnapura. He filled boats with wealth and traveled along the riverbank.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

साधुर्लक्षपतिर्वणिक् sādhurlakṣapatirvaṇik a virtuous merchant worth a hundred thousand
धनैरापूर्य dhanairāpūrya having filled with wealth
तरणीः taraṇīḥ boats
सह saha with
गच्छन्नदीतटे gacchannadītaṭe going to a riverbank
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

atha ratnapurasthāyī sādhurlakṣapatirvaṇik | dhanairāpūrya taraṇīḥ saha gacchannadītaṭe || 3 ||

भर्त्रादिष्टस्तथा bhartrādiṣṭastathā thus instructed by his master
चक्रे cakre made
कर्णधारः karṇadhāraḥ the helmsman
सभृत्यकैः sabhṛtyakaiḥ with his servants

BP 3.2.28.7

कर्णधारा नगा वीरा युयुधुर्मल्ललीलया ॥ 7 ॥

BP 3.2.28.8

स्वयमुत्तीर्य सामात्यो लोकान्पप्रच्छ सादरम् ।
यज्ञस्थानं समालोक्य प्रशस्तं समुदो ययौ ॥ 8 ॥

The merchant himself came ashore with his minister, respectfully questioned the people, saw the excellent sacrificial place, and went to the assembly.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

सादरम् sādaram respectfully
यज्ञस्थानं yajñasthānaṃ the sacrificial place
समालोक्य samālokya having seen
प्रशस्तं praśastaṃ praised; excellent
समुदो samudo to the assembly
ययौ yayau went
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

svayamuttīrya sāmātyo lokānpapraccha sādaram | yajñasthānaṃ samālokya praśastaṃ samudo yayau || 8 ||

BP 3.2.28.9

किमत्र क्रियते सभ्या भवद्भिर्लोकपूजितैः ।
सभ्या ऊचुश्च ते सर्वे सत्यनारायणो विभुः ॥ 9 ॥

What is being done here by you, honored by the world? The assembly members replied:

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

सभ्या sabhyā O assembly members
भवद्भिर्लोकपूजितैः bhavadbhirlokapūjitaiḥ by you, honored by the world
सभ्या sabhyā O assembly members
ऊचुश्च ūcuśca and they said
ते te they

सत्यनारायणो satyanārāyaṇo Satyanārāyaṇa
विभुः vibhuḥ the all-pervading Lord
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

kimatra kriyate sabhyā bhavadbhirlokapūjitaiḥ | sabhyā ūcuśca te sarve satyanārāyaṇo vibhuḥ || 9 ||

BP 3.2.28.10

पूज्यते बंधुभिः सार्धं राज्ञा लोकानुकंपिना ।
प्राप्तं निष्कटकं राज्यं सत्यनारायणार्चनात् ।
3.2.28.१० । ॥ 10 ॥

Compassionate King Candracūḍa worships Satyanārāyaṇa with his relatives. Through this worship he gained a kingdom free from enemies.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
पूज्यते pūjyate is worshipped
बंधुभिः baṃdhubhiḥ with relatives
सार्धं sārdhaṃ together with
राज्ञा rājñā by the king
लोकानुकंपिना lokānukaṃpinā by the compassionate king
प्राप्तं prāptaṃ obtained
निष्कटकं niṣkaṭakaṃ free from obstacles
राज्यं rājyaṃ kingdom
सत्यनारायणार्चनात् satyanārāyaṇārcanāt through worship of Satyanārāyaṇa
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

pūjyate baṃdhubhiḥ sārdhaṃ rājñā lokānukaṃpinā | prāptaṃ niṣkaṭakaṃ rājyaṃ satyanārāyaṇārcanāt || 10 ||

BP 3.2.28.11

धनार्थी लभते द्रव्यं पुत्रार्थी सुतमुत्तमम् ।
ज्ञानार्थी लभते चक्षुर्निर्भयः स्याद्भयातुरः ॥ 11 ॥

The seeker of wealth gains riches, the seeker of a son gains an excellent child, the seeker of knowledge gains wisdom, and the fearful become fearless.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
धनार्थी dhanārthī seeking wealth
लभते labhate obtains
द्रव्यं dravyaṃ wealth; goods
पुत्रार्थी putrārthī seeking a son
सुतमुत्तमम् sutamuttamam an excellent child
ज्ञानार्थी jñānārthī seeking knowledge
लभते labhate obtains
चक्षुर्निर्भयः cakṣurnirbhayaḥ eyesight and fearlessness
स्याद्भयातुरः syādbhayāturaḥ the one distressed by fear may become fearless
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

dhanārthī labhate dravyaṃ putrārthī sutamuttamam | jñānārthī labhate cakṣurnirbhayaḥ syādbhayāturaḥ || 11 ||

BP 3.2.28.12

सर्वान्कामानवाप्नोति नरः सत्यसुरार्च नात् ।
विधानं तु ततः श्रुत्वा चैलं बद्ध्वा गलेऽसकृत् ॥ 12 ॥

A person obtains all desires by worshipping the God of Truth. Hearing this procedure, he tied a cloth around his neck and bowed.

श्रुत्वा śrutvā having heard
चैलं cailaṃ a cloth

गलेऽसकृत् gale'sakṛt around his neck once more
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

sarvānkāmānavāpnoti naraḥ satyasurārca nāt | vidhānaṃ tu tataḥ śrutvā cailaṃ baddhvā gale'sakṛt || 12 ||

BP 3.2.28.13

वृथोद्यमः vṛthodyamaḥ my efforts are in vain
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

daṃḍavatpraṇipatyāha kāmaṃ sabhyānamodayat | anapatyo'smi bhagavanvṛthaiśvaryo vṛthodyamaḥ || 13 ||
