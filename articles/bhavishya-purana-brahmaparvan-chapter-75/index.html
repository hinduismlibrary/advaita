Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 75 — Sāmba’s Question and the Sun as Healer

BP 1.75.1

शतानीक उवाच । कथं साम्बः प्रपन्नो ऽर्कं केन वा प्रतिपादितः । उग्रं शापं च तं प्राप्य पितरं स किम् उक्तवान् ॥ ॥

Śatānīka said: How did Sāmba turn to Arka, and by whom was he instructed? Having received that terrible curse, what did he say to his father?

Word-for-word
śatānīkaḥ uvāca — Śatānīka said;
katham — how;
sāmbaḥ — Sāmba;
prapannaḥ — approached, resorted to;
arkam — Arka, the Sun;
kena vā — by whom, or;
pratipāditaḥ — instructed, taught;
ugram śāpam — terrible curse;
tam prāpya — having received that;
pitaram — to his father;
saḥ — he;
kim uktavān — what did he say.
Transliteration
śatānīka uvāca | kathaṃ sāmbaḥ prapanno ’rkaṃ kena vā pratipāditaḥ | ugraṃ śāpaṃ ca taṃ prāpya pitaraṃ sa kim uktavān || 1 ||

BP 1.75.2

सुमन्तुर् उवाच । उक्तम् एव पुरा वीर यथा शप्तः स यादवः । पित्रा साम्बो महाराज हरिणाम्बुजधारिणा ॥ ॥

Sumantu said: I have already told you, O hero, how that Yādava was cursed by his father, O great king, by Hari who bears the lotus.

Word-for-word
sumantuḥ uvāca — Sumantu said;
uktam eva purā — indeed told before;
vīra — O hero;
yathā — how;
śaptaḥ — cursed;
saḥ yādavaḥ — that Yādava;
pitrā — by the father;
sāmbaḥ — Sāmba;
mahārāja — O great king;
hariṇā — by Hari;
ambuja-dhāriṇā — bearing the lotus.
Transliteration
sumantur uvāca | uktam eva purā vīra yathā śaptaḥ sa yādavaḥ | pitrā sāmbo mahārāja hariṇāmbujadhāriṇā || 2 ||

BP 1.75.3

अथ शापाभिभूतस् तु साम्बः पितरम् अब्रवीत् । विनयावनतो भूत्वा प्राञ्जलिः शिरसा गतः ॥ ॥

Then, overwhelmed by the curse, Sāmba spoke to his father, having bowed humbly with joined palms and lowered his head.

Word-for-word
atha — then;
śāpa-abhibhūtaḥ — overwhelmed by the curse;
tu — but;
sāmbaḥ — Sāmba;
pitaram — to his father;
abravīt — spoke;
vinaya-avanataḥ bhūtvā — having bowed humbly;
prāñjaliḥ — with joined palms;
śirasā gataḥ — with head lowered.
Transliteration
atha śāpābhibhūtas tu sāmbaḥ pitaram abravīt | vinayāvanato bhūtvā prāñjaliḥ śirasā gataḥ || 3 ||

BP 1.75.4

किं मयापकृतं देव येन शप्तो ऽस्म्य् अहं त्वया । अहं त्वदाज्ञया देव त्वरमाणो ऽत्र आगतः ॥ ॥

What wrong have I done, Lord, that I have been cursed by you? At your command, Lord, I came here in haste.

Word-for-word
kim mayā apakṛtam — what wrong was done by me;
deva — O Lord;
yena — whereby;
śaptaḥ asmi — I have been cursed;
aham — I;
tvayā — by you;
aham — I;
tvat-ājñayā — by your command;
deva — O Lord;
tvaramāṇaḥ atra āgataḥ — came here hastening.
Transliteration
kiṃ mayāpakṛtaṃ deva yena śapto ’smy ahaṃ tvayā | ahaṃ tvadājñayā deva tvaramāṇo ’tra āgataḥ || 4 ||

BP 1.75.5

कस्मान् निपातितः शापो मयि ते ऽनपकारिणि । न वै जानाम्य् अहं किञ्चित् प्रसीद जगतः पते ॥ ॥

Why was the curse cast upon me, who did you no harm? I truly know nothing; be gracious, Lord of the world.

Word-for-word
kasmāt nipātitaḥ śāpaḥ — why was the curse cast;
mayi — upon me;
te anapakāriṇi — who did no harm to you;
na vai jānāmi aham kiñcit — I truly know nothing;
prasīda — be gracious;
jagataḥ pate — Lord of the world.
Transliteration
kasmān nipātitaḥ śāpo mayi te ’napakāriṇi | na vai jānāmy ahaṃ kiñcit prasīda jagataḥ pate || 5 ||

BP 1.75.6

शापं नियच्छ मे देव प्रसादं कुरु मे प्रभो । कश्मलेनाभिभूतो ऽहं येन मुच्येय किल्बिषात् ॥ ॥

Withdraw the curse from me, Lord; show me grace, my master. I am overcome by anguish; grant me a way by which I may be freed from sin.

Word-for-word
śāpam niyaccha — withdraw the curse;
me — from me;
deva — O Lord;
prasādam kuru — show grace;
me — to me;
prabho — O master;
kaśmalena abhibhūtaḥ aham — I am overcome by anguish;
yena — whereby;
mucyeya — may I be freed;
kilbiṣāt — from sin.
Transliteration
śāpaṃ niyaccha me deva prasādaṃ kuru me prabho | kaśmalenābhibhūto ’haṃ yena mucyeya kilbiṣāt || 6 ||

BP 1.75.7

तम् उवाच ततः कृष्णः साम्बं बुद्ध्वा ह्य् अनागसम् । नाहं पुत्र पुनः शक्तो रोगस्यास्य व्यपोहने ॥ ॥

Then Kṛṣṇa said to him, knowing Sāmba to be innocent: My son, I am no longer able to remove this disease.

Word-for-word
tam uvāca tataḥ kṛṣṇaḥ — then Kṛṣṇa said to him;
sāmbam — Sāmba;
buddhvā — knowing, recognizing;
hi anāgasam — truly innocent;
na aham — I am not;
putra — O son;
punar — again, any longer;
śaktaḥ — able;
rogasya asya vyapohane — to remove this disease.
Transliteration
tam uvāca tataḥ kṛṣṇaḥ sāmbaṃ buddhvā hy anāgasam | nāhaṃ putra punaḥ śakto rogasyāsya vyapohane || 7 ||

BP 1.75.8

अस्यायं जगतो नाथो द्वादशात्मा दिवाकरः । सहस्ररश्मिर् आदित्यः शक्तः पुत्र व्यपोहितुम् ॥ ॥

This Lord of the world, the twelve-souled Divākara, the thousand-rayed Āditya, is able to remove it, my son.

Word-for-word
asya ayam — this is;
jagataḥ nāthaḥ — Lord of the world;
dvādaśātmā — twelve-souled;
divākaraḥ — the Sun;
sahasra-raśmiḥ — thousand-rayed;
ādityaḥ — Āditya;
śaktaḥ — able;
putra — O son;
vyapohitum — to remove.
Transliteration
asyāyaṃ jagato nātho dvādaśātmā divākaraḥ | sahasraraśmir ādityaḥ śaktaḥ putra vyapohitum || 8 ||

BP 1.75.9

ज्ञातं मयाधुना चैव यथा त्वं नारदेन तु । रोषाद् विसर्जितः पुत्र मत्सकाशं महात्मना ॥ ॥

I now know that you were sent away from my presence by Nārada, the great-souled one, out of anger, my son.

Word-for-word
jñātam mayā adhunā ca eva — now indeed known by me;
yathā — that;
tvam — you;
nāradena tu — by Nārada;
roṣāt — out of anger;
visarjitaḥ — sent away;
putra — O son;
mat-sakāśam — from my presence;
mahātmanā — by the great-souled one.
Transliteration
jñātaṃ mayādhunā caiva yathā tvaṃ nāradena tu | roṣād visarjitaḥ putra matsakāśaṃ mahātmanā || 9 ||

BP 1.75.10

तस्मात् तम् एव पृच्छ त्वं प्रसाद्य ऋषिसत्तमम् । आख्यास्यति स ते देवं शापं यस् ते ऽपनेष्यति ॥ ॥

Therefore ask him alone. Having propitiated that best of sages, he will tell you of the Deity who will remove the curse you have received.

Word-for-word
tasmāt — therefore;
tam eva pṛccha — ask him alone;
tvam — you;
prasādya — having propitiated;
ṛṣi-sattamam — the best of sages;
ākhyāsyati — he will tell;
saḥ — he;
te — to you;
devam — the Deity;
śāpam — the curse;
yaḥ te apaneṣyati — who will remove for you.
Transliteration
tasmāt tam eva pṛccha tvaṃ prasādya ṛṣisattamam | ākhyāsyati sa te devaṃ śāpaṃ yas te ’paneṣyati || 10 ||

BP 1.75.11

अथ एतत् स पितुर् वाक्यं श्रुत्वा जाम्बवतीसुतः । दीनः शोकपरीतात्मा ततः सञ्चिन्त्य भारत ॥ ॥

Having heard these words of his father, Jāmbavatīs son, distressed and his mind filled with sorrow, then reflected, O Bhārata.

Word-for-word
atha etat — then this;
saḥ pituḥ vākyam — his father’s words;
śrutvā — having heard;
jāmbavatī-sutaḥ — Jāmbavatī’s son;
dīnaḥ — distressed;
śoka-parīta-ātmā — whose self was filled with sorrow;
tataḥ sañcintya — then reflecting;
bhārata — O Bhārata.
Transliteration
atha etat sa pitur vākyaṃ śrutvā jāmbavatīsutaḥ | dīnaḥ śokaparītātmā tataḥ sañcintya bhārata || 11 ||

BP 1.75.12

द्वारवत्यां स्थितं विष्णुं कदाचिद् द्रष्टुम् आगतम् । विनयाद् उपसङ्गम्य साम्बः पप्रच्छ नारदम् ॥ ॥

He went to see Viṣṇu, who was staying in Dvārakā, and approaching with humility, Sāmba asked Nārada.

Word-for-word
dvāravatyām sthitam — staying in Dvārakā;
viṣṇum — Viṣṇu;
kadācit draṣṭum āgatam — once came to see;
vinayāt upasaṅgamya — approaching humbly;
sāmbaḥ — Sāmba;
papraccha — asked;
nāradam — Nārada.
Transliteration
dvāravatyāṃ sthitaṃ viṣṇuṃ kadācid draṣṭum āgatam | vinayād upasaṅgamya sāmbaḥ papraccha nāradam || 12 ||

BP 1.75.13

भगवन् वेधसः पुत्र सर्वलोकज्ञ सुव्रत । प्रसादं कुरु मे विप्र प्रणतस्य महामते ॥ ॥

O venerable sage, son of the Creator, knower of all worlds and steadfast in vows, show grace to me, O wise one, who bow before you.

Word-for-word
bhagavan — O venerable one;
vedhasaḥ putra — son of the Creator;
sarva-loka-jña — knower of all worlds;
su-vrata — steadfast in vows;
prasādam kuru — show grace;
me — to me;
vipra — O sage;
praṇatasya — of one who bows;
mahā-mate — O wise one.
Transliteration
bhagavan vedhasaḥ putra sarvalokajña suvrata | prasādaṃ kuru me vipra praṇatasya mahāmate || 13 ||

BP 1.75.14

ये मे नीरुजं कायं कश्मलं च प्रणश्यति । तं योगम् इहि मे विप्र प्रणतस्यास्य सुव्रत ॥ ॥

O sage of good vows, give me that discipline by which my body may become free of disease and my affliction may perish; I bow before you.

Word-for-word
ye — by which;
me — my;
nīrujam kāyam — disease-free body;
kaśmalam ca — affliction too;
praṇaśyati — perishes;
tam yogam — that discipline;
iha me — give me here;
vipra — O sage;
praṇatasya asya — of this one who bows;
su-vrata — O steadfast in vows.
Transliteration
ye me nīrujaṃ kāyaṃ kaśmalaṃ ca praṇaśyati | taṃ yogam ihi me vipra praṇatasyāsya suvrata || 14 ||

BP 1.75.15

नारद उवाच । यः स्तुत्यः सर्वदेवानां नमस्यः पूज्य एव च । पूजयित्वाशु तं देवं ततो व्याधिं प्रहास्यसि ॥ ॥

Nārada said: Worship at once that Deity whom all the gods praise, bow to, and worship; then you will cast off disease.

Word-for-word
nāradaḥ uvāca — Nārada said;
yaḥ — who;
stutyaḥ — praised;
sarva-devānām — by all the gods;
namasyaḥ — worthy of homage;
pūjyaḥ — worthy of worship;
eva ca — indeed and;
pūjayitvā āśu — having worshipped at once;
tam devam — that Deity;
tataḥ — then;
vyādhim — disease;
prahāsyasi — you will cast off.
Transliteration
nārada uvāca | yaḥ stutyaḥ sarvadevānāṃ namasyaḥ pūjya eva ca | pūjayitvāśu taṃ devaṃ tato vyādhiṃ prahāsyasi || 15 ||

BP 1.75.16

साम्ब उवाच । कः स्तुत्यः सर्वदेवानां नमस्यः पूज्य एव च । कः सर्वगश् च सर्वत्र शरणं यं व्रजाम्य् अहम् ॥ ॥

Sāmba said: Who is praised, bowed to, and worshipped by all the gods? Who is all-pervading and everywhere, to whom should I go for refuge?

Word-for-word
sāmbaḥ uvāca — Sāmba said;
kaḥ — who;
stutyaḥ — praised;
sarva-devānām — by all the gods;
namasyaḥ — worthy of homage;
pūjyaḥ — worthy of worship;
kaḥ — who;
sarva-gaḥ — all-pervading;
ca sarvatra — and everywhere;
śaraṇam — refuge;
yam — to whom;
vrajāmi aham — should I go, I.
Transliteration
sāmba uvāca | kaḥ stutyaḥ sarvadevānāṃ namasyaḥ pūjya eva ca | kaḥ sarvagaś ca sarvatra śaraṇaṃ yaṃ vrajāmy aham || 16 ||

BP 1.75.17

पितृशापानलेनाहं दह्यमानो महामुने । शान्त्यर्थम् अस्य कं देवं शरणं च व्रजाम्य् अहम् ॥ ॥

O great sage, burned by the fire of my fathers curse, to which Deity should I go for refuge for the sake of its pacification?

Word-for-word
pitṛ-śāpa-anala-ena — by the fire of the father’s curse;
aham — I;
dahyamānaḥ — being burned;
mahā-mune — O great sage;
śānti-artham asya — for the pacification of this;
kam devam — which Deity;
śaraṇam ca — refuge too;
vrajāmi aham — should I go, I.
Transliteration
pitṛśāpānalenāhaṃ dahyamāno mahāmune | śāntyartham asya kaṃ devaṃ śaraṇaṃ ca vrajāmy aham || 17 ||

BP 1.75.18

एतच् छ्रुत्वा तु साम्बस्य वचनं करुणावहम् । हित्वा तु कामजं वीर नारदो वाक्यम् अब्रवीत् ॥ ॥

sāmbasya — of Sāmba;
vacanam — words;
karuṇā-āvaham — bringing compassion;
hitvā — having set aside;
tu — but;
kāma-jam — desire-born;
vīra — O hero;
nāradaḥ — Nārada;
vākyam abravīt — spoke words.
Transliteration
etac chrutvā tu sāmbasya vacanaṃ karuṇāvaham | hitvā tu kāmajaṃ vīra nārado vākyam abravīt || 18 ||

BP 1.75.19

स्तुत्यो वन्द्यश् च पूज्यश् च नमस्य ईड्य एव च । भास्करो यदुशार्दूल ब्रह्मादीनां सदानघ ॥ ॥

Nārada said: Bhāskara is praised, revered, worshipped, worthy of homage, and worthy of adoration by Brahmā and the other gods, O tiger among the Yadus, O sinless one.

pūjyaḥ — worshipped;
