Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 183 — Procedure for Ancestral Rites

BP 1.183.1

भास्कर उवाच।। कुर्यात्पंचमहायज्ञानधिकारो द्विजस्य सः।। भूतपित्रमरब्रह्ममनुष्याणां यथाविधि।।१।।

Bhāskara said: A twice-born man is entitled to perform the five great sacrifices for beings, ancestors, gods, Brahman, and humans, according to rule.

BP 1.183.2

सदा सदान कृत्यानां फलार्थमपरे स्थिताः।। नित्यानित्य मिति प्राहुरनुषंगात्फलं परे ।।२।।

Some hold that rites performed every day are done for their fruit. Others call them constant and nonconstant; still others say that the fruit follows incidentally.

BP 1.183.3

अतिथेः परितोषाय परिचर्या विधीयते ।। अदृष्टनियमादृष्टमारोग्यान्तं च वर्जनम् ।।३।।

Service is prescribed for the satisfaction of a guest. Abstinence follows an unseen rule and continues until recovery.

BP 1.183.4

तिस्रोष्टकास्तु कर्तव्या मध्यावर्ती चतुर्थिका।। शाकपायसपूपैस्तु मांसेन तु चतुर्थिका।।४।।

Three eighth-day rites should be performed, with a fourth in the middle. The fourth is offered with greens, rice pudding, cakes, and meat.

BP 1.183.5

प्रतिपदि क्रियते यत्तु चतुष्पार्वणमुच्यते ।। स्वगृह्योक्तविधानेन तत्तु पक्षादि कीर्त्यते।।५।।

What is performed on the first day is called the four-part lunar rite. According to the procedure stated in one’s own domestic ritual text, it is called the fortnightly rite and the rest.

BP 1.183.6

नित्यं नैमित्तिकं काम्यं वृद्धिश्राद्धं सपिंडनम्।। पार्वणं चेति विज्ञेयं गोष्ठशुद्ध्यर्थमुत्तमम्।। कर्मांगं नवमं प्रोक्तं वैदिकं दशमं स्मृतम् ।।६।।

The daily, occasional, desire-based, prosperity, joining-with-ancestors, and lunar rites are to be known, along with a rite for purification of the assembly, which is excellent. The ninth is called an adjunct of the rite; the tenth is remembered as Vedic.

BP 1.183.7

।। अनूरुरुवाच ।। यदेतद्भवता प्रोक्तं श्राद्धं द्वादशधा विभो ।। तस्य सर्वस्य मा ब्रूहि लक्षणं वै पृथक्पृथक् ।।७।।

Anūru said: You have declared the ancestral rite to be twelvefold, O lord. Tell me separately the characteristic of each one.

BP 1.183.8

नित्यं किमुच्यते श्राद्धं किं वा नैमित्तिकं भवेत् ।। काम्यादि देवदेवेश एतेषां लक्षणं वद ।।८।।

What is called the daily ancestral rite, and what is occasional? Tell the characteristics of the desire-based rite and the rest, O god of gods.

BP 1.183.9

।। आदित्य उवाच ।। अहन्यहनि यच्छ्राद्धं तन्नित्यं खग कीर्तितम् ।। वैश्वदेवविहीनं तु अशक्तावुदकेन तु ।।९।।

An ancestral rite performed every day is called daily, O bird. If one is unable to perform the offering to all gods, it is done with water.

BP 1.183.10

एकोद्दिष्टं तु यच्छ्राद्धं तन्नैमित्तिकमुच्यते ।। तत्सदैव प्रकर्तव्यमयुग्मान्भोजयेद्द्विजान् ।। १० ।।

The rite dedicated to one deceased person is called occasional. It should always be performed, and one should feed an odd number of twice-born men.

BP 1.183.11

कामयुक्तं हि तत्काम्यमभिप्रेतार्थसिद्धये ।। पार्वणेन विधानेन तदप्युक्तं खगाधिप ।।११।।

That which is joined with desire is desire-based, for the fulfillment of the intended object. That too is prescribed according to the lunar rite, O lord of birds.

BP 1.183.12

वृद्धौ यत्क्रियते श्राद्धं वृद्धिश्राद्धं तदुच्यते ।। सर्वं प्रदक्षिणं कार्यं पूर्वाह्णे तूपवीतिना ।।१२।।

An ancestral rite performed for increase is called the prosperity rite. Everything should be done clockwise in the forenoon, with the sacred thread worn over the left shoulder.

BP 1.183.13

गंधोदकतिलैर्युक्तं कुर्यात्पात्रचतुष्टयम्।। अर्घार्थं पितृपात्रेषु प्रेतपात्रं प्रमोचयेत् ।।१३।।

Make four vessels containing scented water and sesame. For the water offering to the ancestors, place the vessel for the departed among their vessels.

BP 1.183.14

ये समाना इति द्वाभ्यामेतज्ज्ञेयं सपिंडनम् ।। नित्येन तुल्यं शेषं स्यादेकोद्दिष्टं स्त्रिया अपि।।१४।।

By the two verses beginning “They are the same,” this is known as joining with the ancestors. The rest is like the daily rite; the rite for one deceased person is also for a woman.

BP 1.183.15

दर्शे वै क्रियते यत्तु तत्पार्वणमुदाहृतम् ।। पर्वणि क्रियते यच्च तत्पार्वणमिति स्थितिः ।। गोभ्यश्च क्रियते श्राद्धं तद्गोष्ठश्राद्धमुच्यते ।। १५ ।।

What is performed on the new moon is called the lunar rite; what is performed on the lunar day is likewise held to be the lunar rite. The ancestral rite performed for cows is called the cowshed rite.

BP 1.183.16

बहूनां विदुषां संपत्सुखार्थं पितृतृप्तये ।। क्रियते शुद्धये यद्वै ब्राह्मणानां च भोजनम् ।। शुद्यर्थमिति तत्प्रोक्तं वैनतेय मनीषिभिः ।।१६।।

For the prosperity and happiness of many learned men, for the satisfaction of the ancestors, and for purification, Brahmins are fed. The wise call this the rite for purification, O son of Vinatā.

BP 1.183.17

निषेककाले सोमे च सीमतोन्नयने तथा ।। ज्ञेयं पुंसवनं श्राद्धं तच्च कर्मांगमेव च ।। १७ ।।

At conception, at the Soma rite, and at the parting of the hair, the ancestral rite is known as the rite for securing a male child; it is also an adjunct of the ritual.

BP 1.183.18

क्रियते देवमुद्दिश्य सप्तम्यादिषु यत्नतः ।। गच्छेद्देशांतरे यस्तु श्राद्धं कुर्यात्तु सर्पिषा ।। तद्यत्नार्थमिति प्रोक्तं प्रदिशेच्च न संशयः ।।१८।।

It is carefully performed for a deity on the seventh day and the following days. Whoever goes to another region should perform the ancestral rite with ghee. This is called the rite for a purpose and should certainly be prescribed.

BP 1.183.19

शरीरोपचये श्राद्धमश्ववृद्ध्यर्थमेव च ।। पुष्ट्यर्थमेतद्विज्ञेयमौपचारिकमुच्यते ।।१९।।

The ancestral rite for bodily growth and for the increase of horses is to be known as a rite for nourishment; it is called the customary rite.

BP 1.183.20

सर्वेषामेव श्राद्धानां श्रेष्ठं सांवत्सरं मतम् ।। कियते यत्खगश्रेष्ठ मृतेहनि बुधैः सह ।। २० ।।

Of all ancestral rites, the annual one is held to be best, O best of birds; the wise perform it on the day of death.

BP 1.183.21

मृतेऽहनि पुनर्यस्तु न कुर्याच्छ्राद्धमादरात् ।। मातुश्च खगशार्दूल वत्सरांते मृतेऽहनि ।। २१ ।।

Whoever does not carefully perform the ancestral rite on the day of death, and at year’s end on his mother’s day of death, O tiger among birds—

BP 1.183.22

नाहं तस्य खगश्रेष्ठ पूजां गृह्णामि नो हरिः ।। न ब्रह्मा न च वै रुद्रो न चान्ये देवतागणाः ।। २२ ।।

I do not accept worship from him, nor does Hari; neither Brahmā nor Rudra nor the other hosts of gods accept it, O best of birds.

BP 1.183.23

तस्माद्यत्नेन कर्तव्यं वर्षेवर्षे मृतेऽहनि ।। नरेण खगशार्दूल भोजकेन विशेषतः ।। २३ ।।

Therefore a man should perform it carefully every year on the day of death, O tiger among birds, especially the one who gives the feast.

BP 1.183.24

भोजको यस्तु वै श्राद्धं न करोति खगाधिप ।। मातापितृभ्यां सततं वर्षेवर्षे मृतेऽहनि ।।२४।।

But a feast-giver who does not perform the ancestral rite for his mother and father every year on the day of their death, O lord of birds—

BP 1.183.25

स याति नरकं घोरं तामिस्रं नाम नामतः ।। ततो भवति दुष्टात्मा नगरे सूकरः खग ।।२५।।

goes to the terrible hell called Darkness. Then he becomes an evil-minded pig in a city, O bird.

BP 1.183.26

अनूरुरुवाच ।। न जानाति दिनं यस्तु न मासं विबुधाधिप ।। मृतौ यत्र महाप्राज्ञ पितरौ स कथं नरः ।। श्राद्धं करोतु वै ताभ्यां विधिवद्वत्सरात्मकम् ।।२६।।

Anūru said: If a man does not know the day or month when his parents died, O lord of the wise, O greatly wise one, how should he perform their annual ancestral rite according to rule?

BP 1.183.27

।। आदित्य उवाच ।। न जानाति नरो यस्तु मृतानां विनतात्मज ।। मासं दिनं मृतानां तु पितॄणां खगसत्तम ।। २७ ।।

Āditya said: The man who does not know the month and day of his ancestors’ deaths, O son of Vinatā, O best of birds—

BP 1.183.28

यथा कुर्यात्खगश्रेष्ठ शृणु कृत्स्नं समासतः ।। मृताहं यो न जानाति मानवो विनतात्मज ।।२८।।

hear fully and briefly how he should perform it, O best of birds. A man who does not know the day of death, O son of Vinatā—

BP 1.183.29

तेन कार्यममायां च श्राद्धं सांवत्सरं खग ।। मासे मार्गशिरे वीर माघे वा विधिवत्खग ।। २९ ।।

should perform the annual ancestral rite at new moon, in the month of Mārgaśīrṣa or Māgha, according to rule, O bird, O hero.

BP 1.183.30

विशेषतो भोजनेन यो मां पूज्यते सदा ।। प्रीतये मम वै तेन संपूज्याः पितरः सदा ।। ३० ।।

He who always worships me especially by feeding others should, for my pleasure, always have the ancestors worshipped through him.

BP 1.183.31

ममेष्टाः पितरो नित्यं गावो विप्राश्च सुव्रत ।। तस्माच्च ते सदा पूज्या मद्भक्तेन विशेषतः ।।३१।।

The ancestors, cows, and Brahmins are always dear to me, O man of good vows. Therefore they should always be worshipped, especially by my devotee.
