VP 4.5.1

इक्ष्वाकुतनयो योऽसौ निमिर्नाम्ना, स तु सहस्रं वत्सरं सत्रमारेभ

Sri Paraasharar - Ikshvaaku's son Nimi, started a Satra which goes for 1000 years. Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ikṣvākutanayo yo'sau nimirnāmnā, sa tu sahasraṃ vatsaraṃ satramārebha

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.5.2

वसिष्ठं च होतारं वरयामास

He invited Vasishtha to be the hotru for that 1000 year Satra.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vasiṣṭhaṃ ca hotāraṃ varayāmāsa

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.5.3

तम् आह वसिष्ठोऽहम् इन्द्रेण पञ्च-वर्ष-शत-यागार्थं प्रथमं वृतः

Vasishtha said that Indra has already invited me to perform a yaaga which will go for 500 years.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tam āha vasiṣṭho'ham indreṇa pañca-varṣa-śata-yāgārthaṃ prathamaṃ vṛtaḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.5.4

तद्-अनन्तरं प्रतिपाल्यताम् आगतस् तवापि ऋत्विग् भविष्यामीत्य् उक्ते स पृथिवी-पतिर् न किञ्चिद् उक्तवान्

I will come after 500 years, will finish Indra's yaaga, and then I will perform your yaaga, being a ritwik for your Satra. King Nimi did not say anything, he just kept quiet.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tad-anantaraṃ pratipālyatām āgatas tavāpi ṛtvig bhaviṣyāmīty ukte sa pṛthivī-patir na kiñcid uktavān

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.5.5

वसिष्ठोऽप्य् अनेन समन्विप्सितम् इत्य् अमर-पतेर् यागम् अकरोत्

Vasishta thought that he has agreed to what I said, that he will wait for 500 years, and went to Indra's place and performed his yaaga.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vasiṣṭho'py anena samanvipsitam ity amara-pater yāgam akarot

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.5.6

सोऽपि तत्काल एवान्यैर्गौतमादिभिर्यागमकरोत्

Nimi, meanwhile, invited others like Gautama, and other rishis and started his Satra.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

so'pi tatkāla evānyairgautamādibhiryāgamakarot

Śrīdhara

maunaṃ sammatilakṣaṇamiti yuktyaiva samanvīpsimanumatamiti matvā tatra gatvā yāgamakarot ||६||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

मौनं सम्मतिलक्षणमिति युक्त्यैव समन्वीप्सिमनुमतमिति मत्वा तत्र गत्वा यागमकरोत् ॥६॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Nimi, meanwhile, invited others like Gautama, and other rishis and started his Satra.

VP 4.5.7

समाप्ते चामरपतेर्यागे त्वरमाणो वशिष्ठो निमियज्ञं करिष्यामीत्याजगाम

As Vasishta had told Nimi, having completed Indra's yaaga for 500 years, he immediately rushed to Nimi's yajnya, that he will perform Nimi's yajnya.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

samāpte cāmarapateryāge tvaramāṇo vaśiṣṭho nimiyajñaṃ kariṣyāmītyājagāma

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.5.8

तत्-कर्म-कर्तृत्वं च गौतमस्य दृष्ट्वा स्व-पते तस्मै राज्ञे मां प्रत्याख्यायैतद् अनेन गौतमाय कर्मान्तरं समर्पितंयस्मात् तस्माद् अयं विदेहो भविष्यतीति शापं ददौ

Vasishta comes here and sees that the responsibility to perform that Satra, is with Gautama, who is performing it, and King Nimi was sleeping at that time; that without even informing me, the karma, opportunity to Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4 perform this Satra is given to Gautama, without even informing me, and because of this, he will be without a body, he gave a curse.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tat-karma-kartṛtvaṃ ca gautamasya dṛṣṭvā sva-pate tasmai rājñe māṃ pratyākhyāyaitad anena gautamāya karmāntaraṃ samarpitaṃyasmāt tasmād ayaṃ videho bhaviṣyatīti śāpaṃ dadau

Śrīdhara

māmapratyāvakhyāyeti pūrva maduttare pratyuramadarttvaṃva yadvā indrayāgāt māmanivāryaiva, etat karmāntaraṃ nimermanuṣṭitapūrva pūrvakarmavat etat karmāntaraṃ madanuṣṭheyaṃ gautamāya samarpitaṃ karmaṇi antaramalpasyāvakāśo datta iti vārthaḥ . śīghradehapātabhayādidaṃ karma ārabdhamityato videha eva bhavatu iti śāpaṃ dadau ||८||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

मामप्रत्यावख्यायेति पूर्व मदुत्तरे प्रत्युरमदर्त्त्वंव यद्वा इन्द्रयागात् मामनिवार्यैव, एतत् कर्मान्तरं निमेर्मनुष्टितपूर्व पूर्वकर्मवत् एतत् कर्मान्तरं मदनुष्ठेयं गौतमाय समर्पितं कर्मणि अन्तरमल्पस्यावकाशो दत्त इति वार्थः । शीघ्रदेहपातभयादिदं कर्म आरब्धमित्यतो विदेह एव भवतु इति शापं ददौ ॥८॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Vasishta comes here and sees that the responsibility to perform that Satra, is with Gautama, who is performing it, and King Nimi was sleeping at that time; that without even informing me, the karma, opportunity to Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4 perform this Satra is given to Gautama, without even informing me, and because of this, he will be without a body, he gave a curse.

VP 4.5.9

प्रबुद्धश्वासाववनीपतिरपि प्राह

When king Nimi woke up, he came to know about this, and says thus -

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

prabuddhaśvāsāvavanīpatirapi prāha

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.5.10

यस्मान्मामसम्भाष्याजानत एव शयानस्य शापोत्सर्गमसौ दुष्टगुरुश्वकार, तस्मात् तस्यापि देहः पतिष्यतीति प्रतिशापं दत्त्वा देहमत्यजत्

This bad guru, without even talking to me and without my knowledge, when I was sleeping, gave me a curse, and he also cursed Vasishta that his body also will fall, and then he gave up his body.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yasmānmāmasambhāṣyājānata eva śayānasya śāpotsargamasau duṣṭaguruśvakāra, tasmāt tasyāpi dehaḥ patiṣyatīti pratiśāpaṃ dattvā dehamatyajat

Śrīdhara

mām asambhāṣyeti māṃ vinaiva gautamadvārā kimiti karma kṛtamiti māṃ pratyanuktvety arthaḥ ||१०||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

माम् असम्भाष्येति मां विनैव गौतमद्वारा किमिति कर्म कृतमिति मां प्रत्यनुक्त्वेत्य् अर्थः ॥१०॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

This bad guru, without even talking to me and without my knowledge, when I was sleeping, gave me a curse, and he also cursed Vasishta that his body also will fall, and then he gave up his body.

VP 4.5.11

तच्छापाच्च मित्रावरुणयोस्तेजसि वशिष्ठतेजः प्रविष्टम्

Vasishta's tejas, his linga shareera entered into Mitra and Varuna's veerya, because of the shaapa.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tacchāpācca mitrāvaruṇayostejasi vaśiṣṭhatejaḥ praviṣṭam

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.5.12

उर्वशी-दर्शनाद् उद्भूत-बीज-प्रपातयोस् तयोः सकाशाद् वसिष्ठो देहम् अपरं लेभे

When Mitra and Varuna saw Oorvashi, they got excited, and their veerya skalana happened, and because of this, Vasishta got another body, he was born with another body.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

urvaśī-darśanād udbhūta-bīja-prapātayos tayoḥ sakāśād vasiṣṭho deham aparaṃ lebhe

Śrīdhara

tejasi vīrye tejo liṅgaśarīram, ceta iti pāṭhe sa evārthaḥ . paścādudbhūto vīryaprapāto vīryacyutiryayostādṛśayoḥ ||१२||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तेजसि वीर्ये तेजो लिङ्गशरीरम्, चेत इति पाठे स एवार्थः । पश्चादुद्भूतो वीर्यप्रपातो वीर्यच्युतिर्ययोस्तादृशयोः ॥१२॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

When Mitra and Varuna saw Oorvashi, they got excited, and their veerya skalana happened, and because of this, Vasishta got another body, he was born with another body.

VP 4.5.13

निमेर् अपि तच्-छरीरम् अतिमनोहर-गन्ध-तैलादिभिर् उपसंस्क्रियमाणं नैव क्लेदादिकं दोषम् अवाप सद्यो मृत इव तस्थौ

Nimi's body was also protected, with wonderful fragrant oil, and was maintained very well, and it never deteriorated in any way. It was as good as when he was there. The body remained as though he was just dead.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nimer api tac-charīram atimanohara-gandha-tailādibhir upasaṃskriyamāṇaṃ naiva kledādikaṃ doṣam avāpa sadyo mṛta iva tasthau

Śrīdhara

mṛtasya dehadhāraṇaṃ yajñasamāptiṃ vaṃśapravṛtti coddiśyeti vaktuṃ dehadhāraṇaprakāramāha—nimer iti ||१३||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

मृतस्य देहधारणं यज्ञसमाप्तिं वंशप्रवृत्ति चोद्दिश्येति वक्तुं देहधारणप्रकारमाह—निमेर् इति ॥१३॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Nimi's body was also protected, with wonderful fragrant oil, and was maintained very well, and it never deteriorated in any way. It was as good as when he was there. The body remained as though he was just dead.

VP 4.5.14

यज्ञसमाप्तौ च भागग्रहणाय देवानागतान् ऋत्विज ऊचुः, यजमानाय वरो दीयतामिति

Nimi's yaaga got over after 1000 years, and after that, in order to receive their havis bhaaga, all the devataas arrived there, and the ritwiks there told the devataas, please do give the yajamaana a boon.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yajñasamāptau ca bhāgagrahaṇāya devānāgatān ṛtvija ūcuḥ, yajamānāya varo dīyatāmiti

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.5.15

देवैश् च छन्दितोऽसौ निमिर् आह

All the devataas told that you can ask for a boon.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

devaiś ca chandito'sau nimir āha

Śrīdhara

chanditaḥ varārthamicchāṃ kāritaḥ ||१५||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

छन्दितः वरार्थमिच्छां कारितः ॥१५॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

All the devataas told that you can ask for a boon.

VP 4.5.16

भगवन्तोऽखिलसंसारदुः खहन्तारः

You are the ones who destroy all the grief of the samsaara.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bhagavanto'khilasaṃsāraduḥ khahantāraḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.5.17

न ह्य् एतादृग् अन्यद् दुःखम् अस्ति यच् छरीरात्मनोर् वियोगे भवति

There is no greater grief than losing one's body, death. When the aatman leaves the body, that is the most sorrowful thing for a person. Everyone dreads marana.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

na hy etādṛg anyad duḥkham asti yac charīrātmanor viyoge bhavati

No commentary.

VP 4.5.18

तद् अहम् इच्छामि सकल-लोक-लोचनेषु वस्तुं न पुनः शरीर-ग्रहणं कर्तुम् इत्य् एवम् उक्तैर् देवैर् असाव् अशेष-भूतानां नेत्रेष्व् अवतारितः

I don't want to take another body, but I want to live in the eyes of all the beings in this world. Then the devaas said let it be so, and he got into the eyes of all the beings.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tad aham icchāmi sakala-loka-locaneṣu vastuṃ na punaḥ śarīra-grahaṇaṃ kartum ity evam uktair devair asāv aśeṣa-bhūtānāṃ netreṣv avatāritaḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.5.19

ततो भूतान्युन्मेषनिमेषं चक्रुः

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tato bhūtānyunmeṣanimeṣaṃ cakruḥ

Śrīdhara

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

आसाम् अवस्थितिं कारितः ॥१९॥

From that time onwards, the blinking of the eyes, closing and opening of the eyes, started happening, because Nimi is living in the eyes. Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4

VP 4.5.20

अपुत्रस्य च तस्य भूभुजः शरीरमराजकभीरवस्ते मुनयोऽरण्यां ममन्थुः

Because Nimi was without a son, using his body, the rishis did mathana with arani.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तत्पुत्रस्य सञ्ज्ञात्रयं तद् वंशप्रवृत्तिं च वक्तुमाह—अपुत्रस्य इति ॥२०॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Because Nimi was without a son, using his body, the rishis did mathana with arani.

VP 4.5.27

धृष्टकेतोर्हर्य्यश्वस्तस्य च मरुः मरोः प्रतिकः तस्मात्कृतरथः तस्य देवमीढः तस्य च विबुधः विबुधस्य महाधृतिस्तस्य कृतरातः ततो महारोमा तस्य स्वर्णरोमा तत्पुत्रो ह्रस्वरोमा ह्रस्वरोम्णस्सीरध्वजोऽभवत्

Dhrushtaketu had a son by name Haryashva. His son was Maru. Maru's son was Pratika. Pratika's son was Krutaratha. His son was Devameedha. His son was Vibudha. Then Mahaarathi, Krutaraatha and then Mahaaroma. His son was Svarnaroma, his son was Hasvaroma. Hasvaroma's son was Seeradhvaja.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dhṛṣṭaketorharyyaśvastasya ca maruḥ maroḥ pratikaḥ tasmātkṛtarathaḥ tasya devamīḍhaḥ tasya ca vibudhaḥ vibudhasya mahādhṛtistasya kṛtarātaḥ tato mahāromā tasya svarṇaromā tatputro hrasvaromā hrasvaromṇassīradhvajo'bhavat

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.5.28

Viṣṇu Purāṇa

VP 4.5.30

सीरध्वजस्यापत्यं भानुमान् बानुमतः शतद्युम्नः तस्य तु शुचिः तस्माच्चोर्ज्जनामा पुत्रो जज्ञे

Seeradhvaja's son was Bhaanumaan. His son was Shatadyumna. He had a son Shuchi. He had a son by name Oorjanaama.

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.5.31

तस्यापि शतध्वजः ततः कुतिः कुतेरञ्जनः तत्पुत्रः पुरुजित् ततोऽरिष्टनेमिः तस्मात् श्रुतायुः श्रुतायुषः सुपार्श्वः तस्मात्सृञ्जयः ततः क्षेमावी
क्षेमाविनोनेनाःतस्माद्भौमरथः तस्य सत्यरथः तस्मादुपगुः उपगोरुपगुप्तः तत्पुत्रः स्वागतस्तस्य च स्वानंद तस्य च स्वापनः तस्माच्च सुवर्च्चाः तस्य च सुपार्श्वः तस्यापि सुभाषः तस्य सुश्रुतः तस्मात्सुश्रुज्जयः तस्य पुत्रो विजयः विजयस्य ऋतः ऋतात्सुनयः सुनयाद्वीतहव्यः, तस्मात् धृतिः धृतेर्बहुलाश्वः तस्य पुत्रः कृतिः

His son was Shatadhvaja, his son was Kruti. Kruti had a son Anjana. His son was Purujit, his son was Arishtanemi. He had a son Shrutaayu, then Supaarshva, then Srunjaya; Srunjaya had a son Kshemaavi, he had a son Anenas, he had a son Bhoumaratha, then Satyaratha, then Upagu, Upagu had a son Upagupta, then Svaagata, then Svaananda, then Svaapana, Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4 then Suvarchaa, then Supaarshva, then Subhaasha, then Sushruta, then Jaya, then Vijaya, then Ruta, then Sunaya, then Veetahavyas, then Dhruti, then Bahulaashva, then Kruti.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tasyāpi śatadhvajaḥ tataḥ kutiḥ kuterañjanaḥ tatputraḥ purujit tato'riṣṭanemiḥ tasmāt śrutāyuḥ śrutāyuṣaḥ supārśvaḥ tasmātsṛñjayaḥ tataḥ kṣemāvī kṣemāvinonenāḥtasmādbhaumarathaḥ tasya satyarathaḥ tasmādupaguḥ upagorupaguptaḥ tatputraḥ svāgatastasya ca svānaṃda tasya ca svāpanaḥ tasmācca suvarccāḥ tasya ca supārśvaḥ tasyāpi subhāṣaḥ tasya suśrutaḥ tasmātsuśrujjayaḥ tasya putro vijayaḥ vijayasya ṛtaḥ ṛtātsunayaḥ sunayādvītahavyaḥ, tasmāt dhṛtiḥ dhṛterbahulāśvaḥ tasya putraḥ kṛtiḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.5.34

प्रायेणैते आत्मविद्याश्रयिणो भूपाला भवन्ति

Perhaps all these kings will be knowledgeable about aatma vidyaa. They will be kings who are brahmavits. This completes Chapter 5 of Amsha 4. Soma Vamsha, Pururavas and Urvashi Now, the Chapter 6.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

prāyeṇaite ātmavidyāśrayiṇo bhūpālā bhavanti

Śrīdhara

ātmavidyāśrayiṇaḥ ātmavidaḥ ||३४||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

आत्मविद्याश्रयिणः आत्मविदः ॥३४॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Perhaps all these kings will be knowledgeable about aatma vidyaa. They will be kings who are brahmavits. This completes Chapter 5 of Amsha 4. Soma Vamsha, Pururavas and Urvashi Now, the Chapter 6.
