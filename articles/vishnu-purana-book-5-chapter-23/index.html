VP 5.23.1

गार्ग्यं गोष्ठ्यान्द्विजंश्यालः षण्ढ इत्य् उक्तवान् द्विज ।
यदूनां सन्निधौ सर्वे जहसुर् यादवास् तदा ॥१॥

His brother-in-law called Gārgya, a twice-born man, a eunuch in the assembly, O twice-born one; all the Yādavas laughed in the presence of the Yadus.

Śrīdhara translation

To introduce the account of Kālayavana’s origin, in connection with the statement that someone sometimes flees, he narrates the story beginning with “Gārgya” and ending with “he went to Mathurā.” “Gārgya”: because Gārgya had no son, his brother-in-law called him a eunuch in mockery.

Deluded by your māyā, wholly engrossed in sense-objects, I wander within the pit of possessiveness and pride, O Supreme Lord.

Śrīdhara translation

“I”: the sense of “mine and yours,” and the pride arising from it—that itself is the pit; I wander within it.
