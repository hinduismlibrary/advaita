VP 5.23.1

गार्ग्यं गोष्ठ्यां द्विजं श्यालष्षण्ड इत्युक्तवान्द्विज ।
यदूनां सन्निधौ सर्वे जहसुर्यादवास्तदा

Sri Paraasharar - Once what happened was that Gaargya Rishi, in a huge assembly of Yaadavaas, his brother-in-law called him a shanda, a napumsaka. And everyone laughed at Gaargya Rishi.

Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

gārgyaṃ goṣṭhyāṃ dvijaṃ śyālaṣṣaṇḍa ityuktavāndvija | yadūnāṃ sannidhau sarve jahasuryādavāstadā

Śrīdhara

kvacit palāyanaṃ karotīty uktasya palāyanasya prastāvāya kālayavanotpatty-ādi-prakāram ākhyāyikayā varṇayati—gārgyam ity ādinā | mathurāṃ yayāv ity antena | gārgyam iti | gārgyasyānapatyatvāt taṃ ṣaṇḍam ity uktavān śyālopahasan || 1 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

क्वचित् पलायनं करोतीत्य् उक्तस्य पलायनस्य प्रस्तावाय कालयवनोत्पत्त्य्-आदि-प्रकारम् आख्यायिकया वर्णयति—गार्ग्यम् इत्य् आदिना । मथुरां ययाव् इत्य् अन्तेन । गार्ग्यम् इति । गार्ग्यस्यानपत्यत्वात् तं षण्डम् इत्य् उक्तवान् श्यालोपहसन् ॥१॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

No separate English commentary is supplied.

VP 5.23.4

सन्तोषयामासच तं यवनेशो ह्य् अनात्मजः ।
तद्-योषित्-सङ्गमाच् चास्य पुत्रोऽभूद् अलि-सन्निभः

He went and pleased, and did all service for a king of Yavanaas, who did not have any children. And with the Yaavana's wife, he had a son who was black like a bee.

* na vyākhyātam.* ārādhayan mahā-devaṃloha-cūrṇam510abhakṣayat | dadau varaṃ ca tuṣṭo’smai511varṣetu dvādaśe512haraḥ || 3 || viṣṇu-cittaḥ : * na vyākhyātam.* śrīdharaḥ — ārādhayann iti | sa gārgyo mahā-devam ārādhayann āyasa-cūrṇaṃ vrata-rūpeṇābhakṣayat || 3 || tad-yoṣit-saṅgamāc cāsya putro’bhūd ali-sannibhaḥ515 || 4 || viṣṇu-cittaḥ : * na vyākhyātam.* śrīdharaḥ — santoṣayāmāseti | taṃ caivaṃ labdha-varam āgataṃ jñātvā anātmajo putro yavaneśaḥ sva-bhāryāyāṃ putrotpādanāya prārthayiṣyan taṃ santoṣayāmāsa | ali-sannibhaḥ bhṛṅgavat kṛṣṇa-varṇaḥ * || *4 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

* न व्याख्यातम्.*
आराधयन् महा-देवंलोह-चूर्णम्५१०अभक्षयत् ।
ददौ वरं च तुष्टोऽस्मै५११वर्षेतु द्वादशे५१२हरः ॥३॥
विष्णु-चित्तः : * न व्याख्यातम्.*
श्रीधरः — आराधयन्न् इति । स गार्ग्यो महा-देवम् आराधयन्न् आयस-चूर्णं व्रत-रूपेणाभक्षयत् ॥३॥
तद्-योषित्-सङ्गमाच् चास्य पुत्रोऽभूद् अलि-सन्निभः५१५॥४॥
विष्णु-चित्तः : * न व्याख्यातम्.*
श्रीधरः — सन्तोषयामासेति । तं चैवं लब्ध-वरम् आगतं ज्ञात्वा अनात्मजो पुत्रो यवनेशः स्व-भार्यायां पुत्रोत्पादनाय प्रार्थयिष्यन् तं सन्तोषयामास । अलि-सन्निभः भृङ्गवत् कृष्ण-वर्णः *॥*४॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

VP 5.23.5

VP 5.23.8

प्रययौ स व्यवच्छिन्नं छिन्नयानो दिनेदिने ।

As he travelled with his huge army to Mathuraa, on the way, many horses and elephants died because they were extremely tired. He just left them behind, and everyday, his army got reduced like this. He was extremely angry, and marched with his army towards Mathuraa.

Transliteration (IAST)

Śrīdhara commentary.

VP 5.23.9

कृष्णोऽपि चिन्तयामास क्षपितं यादवं बलम् ।
यवनेन रणे गम्यं मागधस्य भविष्यति

Krishna thought that if this Kaalayavana comes and destroys the Yaadavaas here, then Jaraasandha will easily come and attack us, and win over us. When Jaraasandha attacks from one side, and Kaalayavana from another side, anyone then can win over the Yaadavaas, Krishna thought. The one who wins will come and attack Mathuraa. When Kaalayavana comes and destroys the Yaadavaas, then Jaraasandha will easily come and defeat us. If by chance, Jaraasandha destroys us, then Kaalayavana will attack and win over Mathuraa. Krishna thought like this.

Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

VP 5.23.44

त्वन्मायामूढमनसो जन्ममृत्युजरादिकान् ।

Those who are covered by the veil of maayaa, prakruti and their mind is totally deluded, with maaya, moha, and they attain only birth, death, old age. They attain only sufferings everywhere. After that, they go to Yama loka to see Yama.

ततो निजक्रियासूतिनरकेष्वतिदारुणम् ।

That which is obtained from their own karmaas, the acts which they do, they go to various hells, and they suffer great sufferings there. Because they do not know their real nature, and they don't know Your nature properly. They do not have the knowledge about Your nature.
