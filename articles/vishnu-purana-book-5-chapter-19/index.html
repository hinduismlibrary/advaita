VP 5.19.1

एवम् अन्तर् जले विष्णुम् अभिष्टूय स यादवः ।
अर्चयामास सर्वेशं धूप-पुष्पैर् मनो-मयैः

Sri Paraasharar - Having eulogized Bhagavaan under the water like this, Akroora started to worship Him with all the dhoopa, pushpa, which were all in the mind.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
एवम् अन्तर्-जले विष्णुम् (evam antar-jale viṣṇum) — thus Viṣṇu in the water
अभिष्टूय स यादवः (abhiṣṭūya sa yādavaḥ) — having praised, that Yādava
अर्चयाम् आस सर्वेशम् (arcayām āsa sarveśam) — worshipped the Lord of all
धूप-पुष्पैः मनोमयैः (dhūpa-puṣpaiḥ manomayaiḥ) — with incense and mental flowers
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

evam antar jale viṣṇum abhiṣṭūya sa yādavaḥ | arcayāmāsa sarveśaṃ dhūpa-puṣpair mano-mayaiḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

VP 5.19.2

परित्यक्तान्यविषयो मनस्तत्र निवेश्य सः ।
ब्रह्मभूते चिरं स्थित्वा विरराम समाधितः

Having given up the thought about anything else, focussing his mind completely on the Supreme Form, and the wonderful experience that he was having there, he was meditating and entered into the Samaadhi state and was there for a long time.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ब्रह्म-भूते चिरम् स्थित्वा (brahma-bhūte ciram sthitvā) — remaining long in Brahman
विरराम समाधितः (virarāma samādhitaḥ) — rested in meditation
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

parityaktānyaviṣayo manastatra niveśya saḥ | brahmabhūte ciraṃ sthitvā virarāma samādhitaḥ

After a long time, he thought that "I have done what I ought to have done", and again, he got up and came back to the chariot having got up from the waters of Yamunaa river.

VP 5.19.5

श्रीकृष्ण उवाच

विस्मयोत्फुल्लनयनो भवान्संलक्ष्यते यतः

Akroora, when he came back again and saw Balaraama and Krishna who were sitting there in the chariot as before, was completely filled with surprise, and his eyes widened. Seeing him, Krishna told thus. Sri Krishna - I can see the joy in your face. Your eyes are broad, and you are full of surprise; you must have seen some wonderful sight inside the river water.

विस्मय-उत्फुल्ल-नयनः (vismaya-utphulla-nayanaḥ) — eyes opened in amazement
भवान् संलक्ष्यते यतः (bhavān saṃlakṣyate yataḥ) — you are seen to be such
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nūnaṃ te dṛṣṭamāścaryamakrūra yamunājale | vismayotphullanayano bhavānasaṃlakṣyate yataḥ

VP 5.19.6

अक्रूर उवाच

अन्तर्जले यदाश्चर्यं दृष्टं तत्र मयाच्युत ।
तदत्रापि हि पश्यामि मूर्तिमत्पुरतः स्थितम्

Akroora - O Krishna, Achyuta. Whatever the wonderful sight that I saw, that wonderful vision of Yours under the water, the same thing, I am seeing here also. And it has taken a real form here. I am seeing in front of my eyes.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
अन्तर्-जले यत् आश्चर्यम् (antar-jale yat āścaryam) — the wonder in the water
दृष्टम् तत्र मया अच्युत (dṛṣṭam tatra mayā acyuta) — seen there by me, O Acyuta
तत् अत्र अपि हि पश्यामि (tat atra api hi paśyāmi) — that here too I see
मूर्ति-मत् पुरतः स्थितम् (mūrti-mat purataḥ sthitam) — embodied, standing before me
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

antarjale yadāścaryaṃ dṛṣṭaṃ tatra mayācyuta | tadatrāpi hi paśyāmi mūrtimatpurataḥ sthitam

Śrīdhara commentary.

VP 5.19.7

जगद् एतन् महाश्चर्य-रूपं यस्य महात्मनः ।
तेनाश्चर्य-परेणाहं भवता कृष्ण सङ्गतः

This whole world is whose most wonderful form, of such a Supreme Being, of that most wonderful person, O Krishna. You who are such a most wonderful person, I am again united with You.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
जगत् एतत् महा-आश्चर्य-रूपम् (jagat etat mahā-āścarya-rūpam) — this world, of great-wonder form
यस्य महात्मनः (yasya mahātmanaḥ) — of the great Self
तेन आश्चर्य-परेण अहम् (tena āścarya-pareṇa aham) — with that wonder-filled one I
भवता कृष्ण सङ्गतः (bhavatā kṛṣṇa saṅgataḥ) — have met you, Kṛṣṇa
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

jagad etan mahāścarya-rūpaṃ yasya mahātmanaḥ | tenāścarya-pareṇāhaṃ bhavatā kṛṣṇa saṅgataḥ

Śrīdhara

jagad iti | tenāścarya-pareṇa bhagavataiva aham antar-jale cātra ca saṅgato’smi | ato jala eva viśeṣeṇāścaryaṃ kiṃ pṛcchāmīty arthaḥ || 7 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

जगद् इति । तेनाश्चर्य-परेण भगवतैव अहम् अन्तर्-जले चात्र च सङ्गतोऽस्मि । अतो जल एव विशेषेणाश्चर्यं किं पृच्छामीत्य् अर्थः ॥७॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

VP 5.19.8

तत् किम् एतेन मथुरां यास्यामो मधुसूदन ।
बिभेमि कंसाद् धिग् जन्म पर-पिण्डोपजीविनाम्

Those who live doing service to others, I hate such a life, and there should be never people like that; they are of no use. I am scared of Kamsa, as he is waiting for me, and I cannot delay any more. What to do with all these things, let us go fast to Mathura. This is not the right place to discuss about tattva, etc. Because I am really delayed and scared of Kamsa to whom I am subordinate. If I am delayed, he will be very angry. Though I perceived You directly with my eyes, I am not able to speak to You, I have to rush and get back to Mathura, being scared of Kamsa.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
तत् किम् एतेन मथुरां यास्यामः (tat kim etena mathurāṃ yāsyāmaḥ) — why with this [delay] shall we go to Mathurā
मधुसूदन (madhusūdana) — O Madhusūdana
बिभेमि कंसात् (bibhemi kaṃsāt) — I fear Kamsa
धिक् जन्म पर-पिण्ड-उपजीविनाम् (dhik janma para-piṇḍa-upajīvinām) — fie on the birth of those living on another’s food
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tat kim etena mathurāṃ yāsyāmo madhusūdana | bibhemi kaṃsād dhig janma para-piṇḍopajīvinām

Śrīdhara

rathe sthitaṃ māṃ jale sthitaḥ tvaṃ kathaṃ dṛṣṭavān ? iti mohayantam evāha—tat kim etena ? iti | parameśvare sākṣād dṛṣṭe’pi, vilambe sati kaṃsaḥ kupyed iti kaṃsād bibhemi | ataḥ para-piṇḍam anyair dattam annam upajīvanti ye, teṣāṃ janma dhik || 8 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

रथे स्थितं मां जले स्थितः त्वं कथं दृष्टवान् ? इति मोहयन्तम् एवाह—तत् किम् एतेन ? इति । परमेश्वरे साक्षाद् दृष्टेऽपि, विलम्बे सति कंसः कुप्येद् इति कंसाद् बिभेमि । अतः पर-पिण्डम् अन्यैर् दत्तम् अन्नम् उपजीवन्ति ये, तेषां जन्म धिक् ॥८॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

No separate English commentary is supplied.

VP 5.19.14
