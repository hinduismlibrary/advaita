VP 5.19.8

तत् किम् एतेन मथुरां यास्यामो मधुसूदन ।
बिभेमि कंसाद् धिग् जन्म पर-पिण्डोपजीविनाम् ॥८॥

“What is the use of this, O Madhusūdana? Shall we go to Mathurā? I fear Kaṃsa. Cursed is the birth of those who live on another’s food.”

Śrīdhara translation

“How did you see me, who was in the chariot, while you were in the water?” Thus, Kṛṣṇa speaks to Akrūra as though deluding him: “What is the use of this?” Though he has directly seen the Supreme Lord, he fears Kaṃsa, thinking that Kaṃsa will become angry if he delays. Therefore, cursed is the birth of those who live on food given by others.
