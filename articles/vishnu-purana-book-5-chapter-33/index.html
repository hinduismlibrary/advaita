VP 5.33.1

श्रीपराशर उवाच

बाणोऽपि प्रणिपत्याग्रे मैत्रेयाह त्रिलोचनम् ।
देव बाहु-सहस्रेण निर्विण्णोऽस्मि आहवं विना ॥१॥

Parāśara said: Bāṇa, too, bowed before the Three-eyed Lord and said to Maitreya, “O Lord, with a thousand arms I am weary when there is no battle.”

Śrīdhara translation

This battle and the cutting off of Bāṇa’s arms are now to be told, showing that the deed was approved even by Śambhu. “Before” means before Aniruddha’s arrival.

VP 5.33.3

श्रीशङ्कर उवाच

मयूर-ध्वज-भङ्गस् ते यदा बाण भविष्यति ।
पिशिताशि-जनानन्दं प्राप्स्यसि त्वं तदा रणम् ॥३॥

Śaṅkara said: “O Bāṇa, when your peacock-banner is broken, then you will obtain a battle that delights the flesh-eating beings.”

“Flesh-eaters” are those who eat flesh, such as Piśācas; the battle delights them.

देवीभिः सततं विप्र भू-भार-तरणेच्छया ॥५३॥

There Janārdana, surrounded by sons and grandsons, dwelt happily with the goddesses, O brāhmaṇa, desiring to remove the burden of the earth.
