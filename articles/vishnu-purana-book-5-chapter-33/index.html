VP 5.33.1

श्रीपराशर उवाच

बाणोऽपि प्रणिपत्याग्रे मैत्रेयाह त्रिलोचनम् ।
देव बाहुसहस्रेण निर्विण्णोऽस्म्याहवं विना

Parāśara said: Bāṇa, too, bowed before the Three-eyed Lord and said to Maitreya, “O Lord, with a thousand arms I am weary when there is no battle.”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
बाणोऽपि (bāṇo’pi) — Bāṇa, too
प्रणिपत्य (praṇipatya) — having bowed
अग्रे (agre) — before
मैत्रेयाह (maitreyāha) — he said to Maitreya
त्रिलोचनम् (trilocanam) — to the Three-eyed Lord
देव (deva) — O Lord
बाहु-सहस्रेण (bāhu-sahasreṇa) — with a thousand arms
निर्विण्णोऽस्मि (nirviṇṇo’smi) — I am weary
आहवम् विना (āhavam vinā) — without battle
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bāṇo’pi praṇipatyāgre maitreyāha trilocanam | deva bāhusahasreṇa nirviṇṇo’smyāhavaṃ vinā

Śrīdhara

atha yuddhaṃ bāṇa-bāhu-cchedaṃ vakṣyan śambhor api tat-sammatam iti darśayann āha—bāṇo’pīti | agre aniruddhāgamanāt prāk || 1 || .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अथ युद्धं बाण-बाहु-च्छेदं वक्ष्यन् शम्भोर् अपि तत्-सम्मतम् इति दर्शयन्न् आह—बाणोऽपीति । अग्रे अनिरुद्धागमनात् प्राक् ॥१ ॥.

Śrīdhara commentary.

Śrīdhara introduces the coming account of the battle and the cutting off of Bāṇa’s arms, noting that it was approved by Śambhu; “before” means before Aniruddha’s arrival.

No commentary.

VP 5.33.3

श्रीशङ्कर उवाच

मयूरध्वजभङ्गस्ते यदा बाण भविष्यति ।
पिशिताशिजनानन्दं प्राप्स्यसे त्वं तदा रणम्

Śaṅkara said: “O Bāṇa, when your peacock-banner is broken, then you will obtain a battle that delights the flesh-eating beings.”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
मयूर-ध्वज-भङ्गः ते (mayūra-dhvaja-bhaṅgaḥ te) — breaking of your peacock-banner
यदा (yadā) — when
बाण (bāṇa) — O Bāṇa
भविष्यति (bhaviṣyati) — will occur
पिशिताशि-जन-आनन्दम् (piśitāśi-jana-ānandam) — delight of flesh-eating beings
प्राप्स्यसि (prāpsyasi) — you will obtain
त्वम् (tvam) — you
तदा रणम् (tadā raṇam) — then, a battle
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

mayūradhvajabhaṅgaste yadā bāṇa bhaviṣyati | piśitāśijanānandaṃ prāpsyase tvaṃ tadā raṇam

Śrīdhara

mayūra-dhvajeti | piśitāśino māṃsa-bhujo janān piśācādīn ānandayatīti tathā || 3 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

मयूर-ध्वजेति । पिशिताशिनो मांस-भुजो जनान् पिशाचादीन् आनन्दयतीति तथा ॥३ ॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

“Flesh-eaters” means those who eat flesh—such as Piśācas—and whom the battle delights.

tata iti | śambhor vākyaṃ śrutvā hṛṣṭo gṛham āgataḥ san mayūra-cihna-dhvaja-bhaṅga-sambhavam ālokya punar harṣaṃ yayau || 4 ||

Śrīdhara commentary.

After hearing Śambhu’s words, Bāṇa returned home delighted; seeing the breaking of the peacock-marked banner, he became joyful again.

VP 5.33.10

Transliteration (IAST)

The compound means, “as they were saying, ‘Where has Aniruddha gone?’”

VP 5.33.11

Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तम् इति । नारद-वाक्याद् एव योग-विद्या-निपुणया चित्रलेखया नीतं शोणितपुरे वर्तमानं श्रुत्वा अमरैर् न नीत इति प्रत्ययं निश्चयं जग्मुः । पारिजात-हरणे पराजितैर् अमरैः प्रायशो नीतः स्याद् इत्य् आशङ्कां जहुर् इत्य् अर्थः ॥११ ॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

On Nārada’s word they became certain that Citrale khā, skilled in yogic knowledge, had taken Aniruddha to Śoṇitapura—not the immortals. They abandoned the suspicion that the defeated immortals had taken him after the Pārijāta episode.
