VP 6.2.1

व्यासश्चाह महाबुद्धिर्यदत्रैव हि वस्तुनि।
तच्छ्रूयतां महाभाग गदतो मम तत्त्वतः

Sri Paraasharar - In spite of so many bad things which are going to happen in Kali yuga, where dharma is going to be destroyed, and all kinds of adharma will ensue, which were described, he continues about what Vyaasar has told about this. Now, he is going to tell something good about Kali yuga. With very little effort, one can get punya. In the last shloka of the previous adhyaaya, it was concluded that whatever can be achieved with great tapas in Kruta yuga, can be achieved with little effort in Kali yuga. This continues now. I am going to tell you about that, listen to it. Paraasharar is telling what Vyaasar has told.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vyāsaścāha mahābuddhiryadatraiva hi vastuni . tacchrūyatāṃ mahābhāga gadato mama tattvataḥ

Śrīdhara

nikṛṣṭo’pi kalit atyāyāsena mahā-phala-prada iti prasaṅgān nikṛṣṭayoḥ strī-śūdrayor api guṇādhikyaṃ vakutm itihāsaṃ prastauti vyāsa ity ādinā . vyāsaś ceti . vastuni arthe ||१|| kasmin kāle’lpako dharmo dadāti sumahat phalam .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

निकृष्टोऽपि कलित् अत्यायासेन महा-फल-प्रद इति प्रसङ्गान् निकृष्टयोः स्त्री-शूद्रयोर् अपि गुणाधिक्यं वकुत्म् इतिहासं प्रस्तौति व्यास इत्य् आदिना । व्यासश् चेति । वस्तुनि अर्थे ॥१॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Sri Paraasharar - In spite of so many bad things which are going to happen in Kali yuga, where dharma is going to be destroyed, and all kinds of adharma will ensue, which were described, he continues about what Vyaasar has told about this. Now, he is going to tell something good about Kali yuga. With very little effort, one can get punya. In the last shloka of the previous adhyaaya, it was concluded that whatever can be achieved with great tapas in Kruta yuga, can be achieved with little effort in Kali yuga. This continues now. I am going to tell you about that, listen to it. Paraasharar is telling what Vyaasar has told.

VP 6.2.2

कस्मिन्कालेऽल्पको धर्मो ददानि सुमहत्फलम्।
मुनीनां पुण्यवादोऽभूत्कैश्चासौ क्रियते सुखम्

All the great munis had gathered, and were discussing about punya vishaya. About what is going to give us punya. At what time very little dharma will also give us great fruits, great benefits. They were discussing like this. They were also discussing about what has to be done.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kasminkāle’lpako dharmo dadāni sumahatphalam . munīnāṃ puṇyavādo’bhūtkaiścāsau kriyate sukham

Śrīdhara

vādo vicāraḥ . kaiś cāpi jātibhiḥ . asau dharmaḥ . sukham anāyāsena kriyate ||२|| sandeha-nirṇayārthāya veda-vyāsaṃ mahāmunim .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

वादो विचारः । कैश् चापि जातिभिः । असौ धर्मः । सुखम् अनायासेन क्रियते ॥२॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

All the great munis had gathered, and were discussing about punya vishaya. About what is going to give us punya. At what time very little dharma will also give us great fruits, great benefits. They were discussing like this. They were also discussing about what has to be done.

VP 6.2.3

सन्देह-निर्णयार्थाय वेद-व्यासं महामुनिम्।
ययुस् ते संशयं प्रष्टुं मैत्रेय मुनि-पुङ्गवाः

In the first shloka, Paraasharar told about what Vyaasar has told to the rishis, about that very little effort within which great benefit can be gained. Paraasharar is continuing. All the great sages had gathered and were discussing about punya vishaya. They were discussing about what is to be done, and at what time it will happen. In order to get their doubts clarified, they approached Mahaamuni Vedavyaasa, and wanted to ask all these doubts, about the punya vishaya which they were discussing.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

saṃśaya-viṣaya-bhūtam arthaṃ praṣṭuṃ yayuḥ ||३|| dadṛśus te muniṃ tatra jāhnavī-salile dvija .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

संशय-विषय-भूतम् अर्थं प्रष्टुं ययुः ॥३॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

In the first shloka, Paraasharar told about what Vyaasar has told to the rishis, about that very little effort within which great benefit can be gained. Paraasharar is continuing. All the great sages had gathered and were discussing about punya vishaya. They were discussing about what is to be done, and at what time it will happen. In order to get their doubts clarified, they approached Mahaamuni Vedavyaasa, and wanted to ask all these doubts, about the punya vishaya which they were discussing.

VP 6.2.5

snānāvasānaṃ snāna-samāpti-pratīkṣamāṇāḥ . tarūṇāṃ ṣaṇḍaṃ samūham . upāśritāḥ tasthuḥ sthitāḥ ||५|| magno’tha jāhnavī-toyād utthāyāha suto mama .

Śrīdhara commentary.

They were waiting for him to complete his bath there. Waiting for him, they went and stood under the shade of a tree near the banks of that great river.

VP 6.2.8

निमग्नश्च समुत्थाय पुनः प्राह महामुनिः।
योषितः साधु धन्यास्तास्ताभ्यो धन्यतरोऽस्तिकः

He took a dip again, and again got up. He said that women are all very fortunate. He said - "Who is more fortunate than them?". Kaliyuga is good. Shoodraas are fortunate. Women are more fortunate.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nimagnaśca samutthāya punaḥ prāha mahāmuniḥ . yoṣitaḥ sādhu dhanyāstāstābhyo dhanyataro’stikaḥ

Śrīdhara

tābhyo yoṣidbhyaḥ . dhanyataraḥ kaḥ prāṇy asti ? na ko’pīty arthaḥ . atas tā eva dhanyatarāḥ . atra ca kaleḥ sādhutvam . śūdrasya sādhutvaṃ dhanyatvam . yoṣitāṃ dhanyataratvaṃ ceti . yathottaraṃ guṇādhikyam . tatra gaṅgāyām avamarṣaṇe bhagavad-dhyāna-pūte citte pratītam iti . unmajjyonmajjya pada-trayoktyā darśitam . unmajjyeti ca pāṭhaḥ ||८|| tataḥ snātvā yathā-nyāyam ācāntaṃ ca kṛta-kriyam .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

ताभ्यो योषिद्भ्यः । धन्यतरः कः प्राण्य् अस्ति ? न कोऽपीत्य् अर्थः । अतस् ता एव धन्यतराः । अत्र च कलेः साधुत्वम् । शूद्रस्य साधुत्वं धन्यत्वम् । योषितां धन्यतरत्वं चेति । यथोत्तरं गुणाधिक्यम् । तत्र गङ्गायाम् अवमर्षणे भगवद्-ध्यान-पूते चित्ते प्रतीतम् इति । उन्मज्ज्योन्मज्ज्य पद-त्रयोक्त्या दर्शितम् । उन्मज्ज्येति च पाठः ॥८॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

He took a dip again, and again got up. He said that women are all very fortunate. He said - "Who is more fortunate than them?". Kaliyuga is good. Shoodraas are fortunate. Women are more fortunate.

VP 6.2.15

यत्कृते दशभिर्वर्षैस्त्रेतायां हायनेन तत्।

Vyaasa - Whatever is done in ten years in Kruta yuga, it can be achieved in Tretaa yuga in one year. In Dvaapara yuga, it can be achieved within one month. In Kali yuga, it can be achieved in one day and night.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तद् एव स्फुटयति यत् कृते दशभिर् इत्य् आदि द्वाभ्याम् । कृते कृत-युगे हायनेन वर्षेण ॥१५॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Vyaasa - Whatever is done in ten years in Kruta yuga, it can be achieved in Tretaa yuga in one year. In Dvaapara yuga, it can be achieved within one month. In Kali yuga, it can be achieved in one day and night.

VP 6.2.16

And in Kali yuga, it can be achieved in one day itself. Whatever is done by Tapas, Brahmacharya, Japa, it can be achieved in Kali yuga in one day. That is why I said that Kali is saadhu.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

VP 6.2.18

धर्मोत्कर्षमतीवात्र प्राप्नोति पुरुषः कलौ।
अल्पायासेन धर्मज्ञास्तेन तुष्टोऽस्म्यहं कलेः

With very little effort, they achieve great dharma, they get all the fruits. And they become dhaarmikaas, dharmajnyaas in Kali yuga. That is why I am very happy with Kali yuga. By mere Bhagavannaama sankeertana.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dharmotkarṣamatīvātra prāpnoti puruṣaḥ kalau . alpāyāsena dharmajñāstena tuṣṭo’smyahaṃ kaleḥ

alpāyāsena saṅkīrtanādineti kaleḥ sādhutvam uktam ||१८|| vrata-caryā-parair grāhyā vedāḥ pūrvaṃ dvijātibhiḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit
