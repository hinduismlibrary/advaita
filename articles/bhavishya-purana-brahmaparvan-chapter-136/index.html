Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 136 — Consecrating the Sun Image

BP 1.136.1

नारद उवाच । अतः परं प्रवक्ष्यामि अधिवासनमुत्तमम् । सहस्रशीर्षा पुरुषो मण्डपं यत्नतो विशेत् ॥

Nārada said: “Hereafter I shall explain the excellent consecration. The thousand-headed Person should carefully enter the pavilion.”

BP 1.136.2

ततोऽन्ये च शुचौ देशे असंस्पृष्टोपलेपने । मण्डलं पञ्चवर्णैस्तु आलिखेच्चतुरस्रकम् ॥

“Then, in another pure place, untouched and unsmeared, one should draw a square design with five colors.”

BP 1.136.3

पताकातोरणच्छत्रध्वजमाल्याद्यलंकृतम् । विचित्रसुवितानाढ्यं प्रकीर्णकुसुमोत्करैः ॥

“It should be adorned with banners, gateways, parasols, flags, garlands, and the rest, rich in varied canopies, with heaps of flowers strewn about.”

BP 1.136.4

तस्य मध्ये कुशास्तीर्णे मूर्तिः स्थाप्या विवस्वतः । तत्रास्यावाहनं कृत्वा दद्यादर्घ्यं विवस्वते ॥

“In its center, on sacrificial grass that has been spread, the image of Vivasvat should be placed. Having invoked him there, one should offer water to Vivasvat.”

BP 1.136.5

सुवर्णमधुपर्कादि कृत्वा तत्र विधानतः । देवस्य दर्शयेद्गां च सवत्सां रोहिणीं शुभाम् ॥

“Having prepared the golden honey-mixture and the rest according to rule, one should show the deity the auspicious red cow with her calf.”

BP 1.136.6

नमो गोपतये तुभ्यं सहस्रांशो प्रसीद मे । एवमर्घ्येण सम्मन्त्र्य परिधाय च वाससी ॥

“Homage to you, lord of cows, O thousand-rayed one; be gracious to me. Having invoked him with this water offering, one should also put on two garments.”

BP 1.136.7

यज्ञोपवीतमातिथ्यं तथाभ्यङ्गं तथैव च । वत्सरे वत्सरे तस्य नवमव्यङ्गमाहरेत् ॥

“The sacred thread, hospitality, and anointing likewise: each year one should bring him a new sacred girdle.”

BP 1.136.8

श्रावणे मासि राजेन्द्र पवित्रं तस्य तद्धि वै । ब्राह्मणान्भोजयित्वा तु वर्षेवर्षे प्रयोजयेत् ॥

“In the month of Śrāvaṇa, that indeed is his sacred-thread offering. After feeding the Brahmins, one should offer it every year.”

BP 1.136.9

अव्यङ्गं यदुशार्दूल श्रावणे मासि भास्करम् । सर्वगन्धैः समालभ्य चन्दनागुरुकुङ्कुमैः ॥

“O best of the Yadus, in Śrāvaṇa place the unblemished girdle on Bhāskara, anointing him with all fragrances—sandalwood, aloe wood, and saffron.”

BP 1.136.10

अलङ्कारैरलङ्कृत्य कुसुमैश्च सुगन्धिभिः । मालाभिश्च विचित्राभिराबद्धाभिरनेकशः ॥

“Adorn him with ornaments, fragrant flowers, and many kinds of garlands tied in different ways.”

BP 1.136.11

ततो धूपं निवेद्याशु प्रतिमाग्रे प्रयत्नतः । सहस्रशीर्षा पुरुषो मण्डपं च प्रवेशयेत् ॥

“Then, having carefully offered incense before the image, one should bring the thousand-headed Person into the pavilion.”

BP 1.136.12

नमः शम्भवेति मन्त्रेण शय्यायां विनिवेशयेत् । विश्वतश्चक्षुरित्येव कुर्यात्कमलनिष्कलम् ॥

“With the sacred formula ‘Homage to Śambhu,’ one should place him on the bed; with ‘The eye is everywhere,’ one should make the lotus complete.”

BP 1.136.13

पुनरेव च वक्ष्यामि सङ्कलीकरणं शुभम् । स्नापने तु यथाकार्यः स्वदेहे न्यास उत्तमः ॥

“I shall again explain the auspicious joining rite. During the bathing, the excellent placement of syllables should be made on one’s own body in the prescribed way.”

BP 1.136.14

प्रतिमायां तथा कार्यो यथा चालम्भनं बुधः । ॐ हुं खषोल्काय नमो मूलमन्त्रः प्रकीर्तितः ॥

“The wise one should do the same on the image as in the touching rite. The root formula is proclaimed: ‘Om, huṃ, homage to Khaṣolka.’”

BP 1.136.15

आदित्योऽयं स्वयं देवो ह्यक्षरेणोपबृंहितः । ॐकारं विन्यसेन्मूर्ध्नि हुंकारं नासिकोपरि ॥

“This Āditya is himself the deity, strengthened by the syllable. One should place Om on the head and huṃ above the nose.”

BP 1.136.16

खकारं च ललाटे तु षकारं वदने न्यसेत् । लकारं चैव कंठे तु ककारं हृदये न्यसेत् ॥

“One should place the sound kha on the forehead, ṣa on the face, la on the throat, and ka on the heart.”

BP 1.136.17

यकारं तु भुजे वामे नकारं दक्षिणे भुजे । मकारं वामकुक्षौ च विसर्गं दक्षिणे न्यसेत् ॥

“One should place ya on the left arm and na on the right arm; ma on the left side and the breath-release sound on the right.”

BP 1.136.18

ॐकारं तु सदा ध्यायेज्ज्वालामालासमाकुलम् । हुङ्कारं शुद्धवर्णाभं प्रसुवन्तमलं शुभम् ॥

“One should always meditate on Om, surrounded by a garland of flames; on huṃ, shining with pure color and bringing forth what is spotless and auspicious.”

BP 1.136.19

खकारं चिन्तयेत्प्राज्ञो भिन्नाञ्जनसमप्रभम् । तरुणादित्यवर्णाभं खकारं चिन्तयेद्बुधः ॥

“The wise one should contemplate kha, shining like split collyrium; the learned one should contemplate kha, having the color of the young Sun.”

BP 1.136.20

षोकारं तु महाबाहो हेमवर्णं विचिन्तयेत् । शुक्लपद्मनिभाकारमकारं चिंतयेद्बुधः ॥

“O mighty-armed one, one should contemplate ṣa as gold-colored; the wise one should contemplate ma as having the form of a white lotus.”

BP 1.136.21

जातीकुसुमसंकाशं ह्रींकारं सर्ववर्णकम् । क्षीरवर्णं सकारं तु चिन्तयेत्सततं बुधः ॥

“One should contemplate hrīṃ as resembling a jasmine flower and containing all colors; the wise one should always contemplate sa as milk-colored.”

BP 1.136.22

नकारं हिमकुन्दाभं मकारममृताक्षरम् । ह्रींकारं विद्युत्संकाशं ह्रींकारं सर्ववर्णकम् ॥

“One should contemplate na as resembling snow and jasmine, ma as the deathless syllable, and hrīṃ as resembling lightning and containing all colors.”

BP 1.136.23

क्षीरवर्णं सकारं तु चिन्तयेत्सततं बुधः । नकारं स्वर्णवर्णाभं मकारं कनकप्रभम् ॥

“The wise one should always contemplate sa as milk-colored, na as resembling gold in color, and ma as having golden splendor.”

BP 1.136.24

ततो देवं महात्मानं सहस्रकिरणं रविम् । प्रसादाभिमुखं देवं शयनीये निवेशयेत् ॥

“Then one should place the great deity Ravi, of a thousand rays, upon the bed, facing graciously.”

BP 1.136.25

अग्निकार्यं ततः कुर्युरग्निकुण्डेषु वै द्विजाः । ततोऽरण्यां समुत्थाप्य अग्निं लौकिकमेव वा ॥

“Then the twice-born should perform the fire rites in the fire pits. Next they should kindle fire from the fire sticks or use an ordinary fire.”

BP 1.136.26

प्रज्वाल्याग्निं विधानेन कुर्याद्धोमं विचक्षणः । बह्वृचः पूर्वकाण्डेषु याम्यां मध्यन्दिनस्तथा ॥

“The discerning one should kindle the fire according to rule and make the fire offerings. The Ṛgveda reciters should be in the east and the Mādhyandina reciters in the south.”

BP 1.136.27

पश्चिमे चैव च्छन्दोग उत्तरेऽथर्वणो मतः । मध्ये च भोजकः कुर्याद्धोमं यज्ञे यदुत्तम ॥

“The Sāma Veda chanters are in the west, and the Atharva Veda chanters are held to be in the north. In the middle the Sun-priest should perform the finest sacrifice-offering.”

BP 1.136.28

शमीपालाशोदुम्बराणि ह्यपामार्गस्तथैव च । द्वादश तु सहस्राणि अष्टौ चत्वारि एव च ॥

“Use Prosopis, flame-of-the-forest, cluster fig, and prickly chaff flower; twelve thousand, eight thousand, and four thousand,”

BP 1.136.29

द्वे त्रीणि च सहस्राणि अथ वा एकमेव हि । अग्निर्मूर्धेति मन्त्रेण कुण्डस्यालम्भनं भवेत् ॥

“two thousand, three thousand, or even one thousand. With the formula ‘Fire is the head,’ the preparation of the fire pit is performed.”

BP 1.136.30

उल्लिख्याभ्युक्ष्य तेनैव अग्निं दूतमिति स्मृताः । सम्बुध्यस्वाग्ने मन्त्रेण गर्भाधानं तु कारयेत् ॥

“Having marked it out and sprinkled it, one should call the fire ‘the messenger’; with the formula ‘Awaken, O Fire,’ one should perform the conception rite.”

BP 1.136.31

सीमन्तेति पुनस्तत्र महामन्त्रेण होमयेत् । जातकर्म तथा प्रोक्तं प्राणायामं विदुर्बुधाः ॥

“One should again offer oblations there with the great formula for parting the hair. The birth rite is likewise prescribed, and the wise know breath control.”

BP 1.136.32

नमः स्वाहेति मन्त्रेण नामकरणमेव च । अन्नप्राशनमन्त्रेण अन्नप्राशनमादिशेत् ॥

“With the formula ‘Homage, svāhā,’ one should perform the naming rite; with the formula for first feeding, one should direct the first feeding.”

BP 1.136.33

ज्येष्ठमग्रेति मन्त्रेण तेन चूडोपकर्मणि । व्रतबन्धस्य मन्त्रेण व्रतबन्धं समादिशेत् ॥

“With the formula ‘Foremost among the eldest,’ one should perform the tonsure rite; with the formula for the vow’s binding, one should direct the vow’s binding.”

BP 1.136.34

समावर्तनमित्येव आकृष्णेति च होमयेत् । पत्नीसंयोजनं चैव स्वयमेव प्रकल्पयेत् ॥

“One should offer oblations with the formula for the homecoming rite and the formula ‘Toward the dark one’; one should also arrange the marriage.”

BP 1.136.35

अग्निहोत्रादिकं कर्म यज्ञकर्माणि यानि च । महाव्याहृतिमन्त्रेण होतव्यानि समन्ततः ॥

“The rites beginning with the daily fire offering, and all sacrificial rites, should be offered on every side with the formula of the great utterances.”

BP 1.136.36

मातॄणां यज्ञभूतानां बलिकर्म प्रदापयेत् । सर्वकामसमृद्ध्यर्थं कारयेदधिवासनम् ॥

“One should make food offerings to the Mothers, who are forms of the sacrifice; one should perform the consecration for the fulfillment of all desires.”

BP 1.136.37

त्रिरात्रं पञ्चरात्रं च अहोरात्रमथापि वा । ततः त्वलङ्कृतां स्नातां मणिरत्नैर्विभूषिताम् ॥

“The rite may last three nights, five nights, or even one day and night. Then the image should be bathed, adorned, and decorated with gems and jewels.”

BP 1.136.38

कृतरक्षां प्रयत्नेन प्रतिमामधिवासयेत् । देवागारादथैशाने दिग्भागे दिव्यमन्दिरम् ॥

“Having carefully protected the image, one should consecrate it in a divine shrine in the northeast quarter of the deity’s temple.”

BP 1.136.39

कृत्वा कुशपरिस्तीर्णे वरास्तरणसंवृते । पूर्वशीर्षां तथा शय्यां शुक्लां शुक्लाम्बरोत्तराम् ॥

“Having spread sacrificial grass and covered it with excellent bedding, one should prepare a bed with its head to the east, white and covered above with white cloth.”

BP 1.136.40

तस्यामावेशयेत्सम्यङ्महाश्वेतमुपाहरेत् । निक्षुभा दक्षिणे पार्श्वे वामे राज्ञी च कीर्तिता ॥

“One should properly place the great white image there and bring it near. Nikṣubhā is named on the right side, and Rājñī on the left.”

BP 1.136.41

दण्डपिङ्गलकौ चास्य स्थितौ पादप्रवेशितौ । तस्यां संवेशितायां तु शर्वर्यां प्रतिमां रवेः ॥

“Daṇḍa and Piṅgala stand at its feet. When the image of Ravi has been laid there for that night,”

BP 1.136.42

वसेत्तां रजनीं तत्र स्तूयमानश्चतुदिशम् । ब्राह्मणैर्बन्दिभिश्चापि गीतज्ञैश्चारणैस्तथा ॥

“it should remain there, praised on every side by Brahmins, court praisers, skilled singers, and bards.”

BP 1.136.43

कुर्याज्जागरणं तत्र सूर्यभक्तिसमन्वितैः । प्रभातायां तु शर्वर्यां बोधयेदृग्विधानतः ॥

“Those endowed with devotion to the Sun should keep vigil there. When that night has passed into dawn, one should awaken the image according to the Rigvedic procedure.”

BP 1.136.44

हविष्यं भोक्तुकामांस्तु ब्राह्मणान्भोजकांस्तथा । दक्षिणाभिश्च सम्पूज्य तैः कृतस्वस्तिवाचनः ॥

“The Brahmins and Sun-priests who wish to eat the sacrificial food should be honored with gifts and fed; then they should recite blessings.”

BP 1.136.45

ततो गर्भगृहस्थाय मध्ये कृत्वा तु पिण्डिकाम् । विधिवत्तत्र सौवर्णं न्यसेत्सप्तहयं रथम् ॥

“Then, having made a pedestal in the middle of the inner shrine, one should duly place there the golden chariot with seven horses.”

BP 1.136.46

सर्वबीजौषधैश्चैव तत्र धृत्वा विधानवित् । दत्त्वार्घ्यं स्थापयेत्तत्र यजमानः सहायवान् ॥

“The patron who knows the rite should place all seeds and medicinal herbs there, make the water offering, and set the image in place with his assistants.”

BP 1.136.47

शङ्खदुन्दुभिनिर्घोषैर्जलधारासहाक्षतैः । कृत्या पुण्याहशब्दं तु आलयस्य प्रदक्षिणाम् ॥

“With the sounds of conches and kettle drums, streams of water, and unbroken rice, one should utter the auspicious-day formula and circumambulate the shrine.”

BP 1.136.48

शुभलग्ने दिने ऋक्षे पूर्वाह्णे भानवे क्षणे । मुहूर्ते च शुभे भानोः प्रतिमां स्थापयेद्बुधः ॥

“At an auspicious ascendant, day, and lunar mansion, in the forenoon, at the Sun’s appointed moment and at a favorable interval, the wise one should install Bhānu’s image.”

BP 1.136.49

नाधोमुखीं नोर्ध्वमुखीं न पार्श्वावनतां तथा । समामभिमुखीं चेमां प्रतिमां तु निवेशयेत् ॥

“One should install this image neither facing downward nor upward nor bent to the side, but level and facing forward.”

BP 1.136.50

पत्न्यौ चास्य ततः सम्यक्पार्श्वयोर्विनिवेशयेत् । निक्षुभा दक्षिणे पार्श्वे रवे राज्ञी तु वामतः ॥

“Then one should properly place his two consorts at the sides: Nikṣubhā at Ravi’s right and Rājñī at his left.”

BP 1.136.51

ततस्तदुपहारार्थं सम्भारं प्राक्तमाहृतैः । मोदकायूषिकापूपशष्कुलीभूतशीर्षकैः ॥

“Then, for that offering, use the materials brought earlier: sweet dumplings, longevity cakes, sacrificial cakes, sesame cakes, and spirit cakes.”

BP 1.136.52

कृशरैः पायसोन्मिश्रैः सर्वदिक्षु क्षिपेद्बलिम् । इन्द्राय देवपतये बलिने वज्रपाणये ॥

“With sesame-and-rice dishes mixed with milk pudding, one should scatter food offerings in all directions for Indra, lord of the gods, the mighty one, thunderbolt in hand.”

BP 1.136.53

शतयज्ञाधिपतये तस्मै इन्द्राय ते नमः । त्रातारमिन्द्रमन्त्रेण इन्द्रस्यावाहनं भवेत् ॥

“Salutation to you, Indra, lord of a hundred sacrifices. Indra should be invoked with the formula ‘Indra, the protector.’”

BP 1.136.54

अग्नये रक्तनेत्राय ज्वालामालार्चिताय वै । शक्तिहस्ताय तीव्राय तथा चैवाजवाहिने । आग्रेय्यामग्रिमन्त्रेण वह्नेरावाहनं स्मृतम् ॥

“For Agni, red-eyed, worshipped with a garland of flames, spear in hand, fierce, and goat-riding: in the southeast the invocation of Fire is made with the fire formula.”

BP 1.136.55

दण्डहस्ताय कृष्णाय महिषोत्तमवाहिने । सूर्यपुत्राय देवाय धर्मराजाय वै नमः ॥

“Salutation to the dark one, staff in hand, riding an excellent buffalo, the god Dharmarāja, son of the Sun.”

BP 1.136.56

यमाय त्विति मन्त्रेण मुद्रास्तस्यैव कीर्तिताः । नैर्ऋते खड्गहस्ताय नीललोहितकाय च ॥

“His hand gestures are proclaimed with the formula ‘To Yama, to you.’ In the southwest [place] the sword-bearing, blue-red one.”

BP 1.136.57

सर्वबाह्याधिपतये विरूपाख्याय वै नमः । आयं गौरिति मन्त्रेण नैर्ऋत्यां तु प्रकल्पयेत् ॥

“Salutation to the lord of all outer regions, named Virūpākṣa. One should establish him in the southwest with the formula ‘This cow has come.’”

BP 1.136.58

वारुण्यां पाशहस्ताय वरुणायेति कल्पयेत् । मन्त्रेणावाहनं विद्यात्पञ्चनद्यः सरस्वतीम् ॥

“In the west one should place Varuṇa, holding a noose, with the formula ‘To Varuṇa.’ One should know the invocation with the formula ‘The five rivers and Sarasvatī.’”

BP 1.136.59

प्राणात्मकाय धूपाय अव्यङ्गायानिलाय च । ध्वजहस्ताय भीमाय नमो गन्धवहाय च ॥

“Salutation to the one whose nature is breath, to the smoke, to the unblemished one, to the Wind; to the banner-holder, the terrible one, and the scent-bearer.”

BP 1.136.60

तस्याप्यावाहनं विद्याद्यद्देवादेवहेडनम् । गदाहस्ताय सोमाय शुष्मिणे नृगताय च ॥

“One should know his invocation by the formula ‘Whatever offense against the gods [we have done].’ Soma is mace-handed, powerful, and moving among people.”

BP 1.136.61

गदापट्टिशहस्ताय सोमराजाय वै नमः । ईशावास्यं च गुह्या वै सोममन्त्रः प्रकीर्तितः ॥

“Salutation to King Soma, holding a mace and halberd. ‘All this is pervaded by the Lord’ is proclaimed as the secret formula of Soma.”

BP 1.136.62

चतुर्मुखाय देवाय पद्मासनगताय च । कृष्णाजिननिषण्णाय नमो लम्बोदराय च ॥

“Salutation to the four-faced god, seated on a lotus seat and sitting on a black antelope skin, and to the pot-bellied one.”

BP 1.136.63

गणाधिपतये देव नीलकण्ठाय शूलिने । विरूपाक्षाय रुद्राय त्रैलोक्याधिपते नमः । अभि त्वा शूर नो मन्त्र ईशानाय प्रकल्पयेत् ॥

“Salutation to the god, lord of the attendants, blue-throated, trident-bearing, odd-eyed Rudra, lord of the three worlds. One should establish Īśāna with the formula ‘Toward you, O hero, we …’”

BP 1.136.64

सर्वनागाधिराजाय श्वेतवर्णाय भोगिने । सहस्रफणिने नित्यमनन्ताय नमोनमः ॥

“Repeated salutation to Ananta, king of all serpents, white in color, serpent-bodied, and always thousand-hooded.”

BP 1.136.65

नमोऽस्तु सर्पेभ्य इति मन्त्रश्चैव प्रकीर्तितः । पञ्चरात्रादिभिर्न्यासो ह्यंगन्यासः प्रयुज्यते ॥

“The formula ‘Homage be to the serpents’ is also proclaimed. In the five-night rites and the like, the placement of syllables on the body and limbs is performed.”

BP 1.136.66

तथोपक्षीरपानैश्च स्तुतिस्तोत्रैश्च भास्करम् । विप्रेभ्यो भोजकेभ्यश्च ततो दद्याच्च दक्षिणाम् ॥

“Then, with milk offerings, praises, and hymns to Bhāskara, one should give ritual fees to the Brahmins and Sun-priests.”

BP 1.136.67

सूर्यक्रतुं महापुण्यं नैव कुर्याददक्षिणम् । स्थाप्यतेऽनेन विधिना तद्भक्तैः प्रतिमा च या ॥

“One should never perform the greatly meritorious Sun sacrifice without a ritual fee. The image that is installed by his devotees according to this procedure,”

BP 1.136.68

सा तु वृद्धिकरा नित्यं सान्निध्याच्च सदा भवेत् । सप्तजन्मसु तेषां तु न रोगाः सम्भवन्ति हि ॥

“continually brings increase and remains near. For seven births no illness arises for them.”

BP 1.136.69

उपासते त्रिरात्रं ये भानोर्यात्राभिवासने । गन्धमाल्योपहारैस्तु ते यान्ति भुवनं रवेः ॥

“Those who worship for three nights during the preparation of Bhānu’s festival procession, with fragrances, garlands, and offerings, go to Ravi’s world.”

BP 1.136.70

आत्मीयं परकीय वा प्रतिमास्थापनं रवेः । यः पश्यति पुमान्भक्त्या स स्वर्लोकमवाप्नुयात् ॥

“Whoever devotedly sees the installation of Ravi’s image, whether his own or another’s, that man attains the heavenly world.”

BP 1.136.71

दशानामश्वमेधानां वाजपेयशतस्य च । फलं प्राप्नोति पुरुषः प्रतिष्ठाप्य दिवाकरम् ॥

“A man obtains the fruit of ten horse sacrifices and a hundred strength-offering rites by installing Divākara.”

BP 1.136.72

यावत्कीर्तिः पुण्यकृता भानोः स्थाने निवेशिते । तावत्स तु यदुश्रेष्ठ सूर्यलोके महीयते ॥

“For as long as the meritorious fame of Bhānu remains established in that place, for so long he is honored in the world of the Sun, O best of the Yadus.”

BP 1.136.73

स्थापने चास्य वै मन्त्रः प्रोक्तो लोकेषु पूजितः । ध्रुवा द्यौश्च ध्रुवा पृथ्वी ध्रुवं विश्वमिदं जगत् । श्रेयसे यजमानस्य तथा त्वं ध्रुवतां व्रज ॥

“The revered formula for his installation is proclaimed: ‘Firm is heaven, firm is earth, firm is this whole world. For the patron’s welfare, may you likewise become firm.’”

BP 1.136.74

स्थापयित्वा रविं भक्त्या विधिदृष्टेन कर्मणा । मासे मासे ऋतुफलं लभन्ते नात्र संशयः ॥

“Having installed Ravi with devotion by the rite prescribed in the rule, one obtains month by month the fruit of the seasons; of this there is no doubt.”

BP 1.136.75

एकाहेनापि यद्भानोः पूजया प्राप्यते फलम् । न तु क्रतुशतैर्वीर प्राप्यते मानवैर्भुवि ॥

“The fruit obtained by worshipping Bhānu even for one day is not obtained by mortals on earth through a hundred sacrifices, O hero.”

BP 1.136.76

कृत्वापि सुमहत्पापं यः पश्चात्सेवते रविम् । स याति सूर्यलोकं तु नरो विगतकल्मषः ॥

“Even after committing a very great sin, whoever afterward serves Ravi goes to the world of the Sun, free from stain.”

BP 1.136.77

न भवेदिष्टकानां च द्रवणं भूमिसंमिति । स्वर्गे महीयते तावत्कारको देववेश्मनः ॥

“So long as the bricks do not crumble and the ground does not sink, the maker of the deity’s temple is honored in heaven.”

BP 1.136.78

खण्डस्फुटितसंस्कारं कृत्वा यत्फलमाप्यते । न तु क्रतुसहस्रैस्तु प्राप्यते फलमुत्तमम् ॥

“The excellent fruit obtained by repairing what is broken and cracked is not obtained even by a thousand sacrifices.”

BP 1.136.79

सिकतायामपि गृहं यस्तु कुर्याद्विभावसोः । गोपतेः स प्रियसदः प्रगच्छेद्गोपतेर्वरम् ॥

“Whoever builds a house for the Sun even in sand goes to the Sun’s cherished abode, the highest realm of the Sun.”

BP 1.136.80

इत्येवं सुरवरस्य तस्य भानोर्भूतानां स्थितिनिलयप्रसूतिहेतोः । श्रीभागी भवति नरो निकेतकारी कल्पानां वसति शतं स सूर्यलोके ॥

“Thus, the man who builds a dwelling for that best of gods, Bhānu, cause of beings’ existence, abode, and birth, becomes prosperous and dwells for a hundred ages in the world of the Sun.”
