Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 172 — The Dharma of the Sun

BP 1.172.1

सुमंतुरुवाच ।। पुनः शृणु महाराज धर्ममादित्यसंमतम् ।। सौरप्रियं सदा सौरं पवित्रं पापनाशनम् ।। १ ।।

Sumantu said: Hear again, O great king, the dharma approved by the Sun, always dear to Sun-worshippers, solar, purifying, destroyer of sin.

BP

क्वचिद्गच्छन्यदा पश्येत्सूर्यार्चासंप्रपूजनम् ।। यत्र पूजा ततो गच्छन्त्स सूर्यो नात्र संशयः ।। २ ।।

Whenever, while going somewhere, one sees full worship of the Sun, where the worship is, there the Sun goes; of this there is no doubt.

BP 1.172.3

स्नाननैवेद्यवस्त्रैश्च नानालंकारभूषणैः ।। यथाविभवमाश्रित्य नमस्कारादिसंस्तवैः ।। ३ ।।

With bathing, food-offerings, garments, various ornaments and adornments, according to one’s means, with salutations and praises and the like.

BP

दृष्ट्वायतनमाक्रम्य नमस्कृत्य रविं व्रजेत् ।। क्वचित्पथि नदीं शैलं गच्छमानं च भोजकम् ।। ४ ।।

Having seen the shrine, entered it, and bowed to the Sun, one should go; on the road one may see a river, a mountain, and a travelling Sun-priest.

BP 1.172.5

उपश्रुत्यावनिं गत्वा भोजकं पूजयेद्बुधः ।। रथाश्वगजयानेभ्यो ह्यवतीर्य मगान्नृप ।। मगानां भोजनं भक्त्या शक्त्या दानं प्रकल्पयेत् ।। ५ ।।

Having heard and gone to the ground, the wise one should honor a Sun-priest; having dismounted from chariots, horses, elephants, and vehicles, O king, one should arrange food and gifts for the Magas with devotion and according to one’s ability.

BP

दशपूर्वान्दश परानात्मना सह भारत ।। समादाय व्रजेत्स्थानं रवेरमिततेजसः ।। ६ ।।

Taking ten ancestors and ten descendants, together with oneself, O Bharata, one should go to the abode of the Sun of immeasurable splendor.

BP 1.172.7

दैवपर्वोत्सवे श्राद्धे पुण्येषु दिवसेषु च ।। भानुं संपूज्य विधिवद्भोजकान्भोजयेत्ततः ।। ७ ।।

At divine festivals, observances, ancestral rites, and sacred days, one should worship the Sun properly and then feed the Sun-priests.

BP

पितरः सर्वदेवानां सूर्यमाश्रित्य संस्थिताः ।। प्रीते सूर्ये तु ते सर्वे प्रीताः स्युर्नात्र संशयः ।। ८ ।।

The ancestors of all the gods abide relying on the Sun; when the Sun is pleased, they are all pleased; of this there is no doubt.

BP 1.172.9

यदा च श्रद्धया युक्तं प्रसक्तं रविपूजनम् ।। भोजयेद्भोजकं भक्त्या श्राद्धेषु विधिवन्नृप ।। ९ ।।

When worship of the Sun is joined with faith and undertaken, one should feed a Sun-priest with devotion at ancestral rites, according to rule, O king.

BP

भोजकस्य महाराज दिवसेनापि यत्फलम् ।। न तच्छक्यमिदं तेन प्राप्तुं वर्षशतैरपि ।। ।।

O great king, the merit of a Sun-priest even in one day cannot be attained by him even in hundreds of years.

BP 1.172.11

यः पश्यति प्रसन्नात्मा यो न द्वेष्टि न कांक्षति ।। शब्दादीनां तु संभोगं स विज्ञेयो जितेंद्रियः ।। ११ ।।

He who sees with a clear mind, who neither hates nor desires enjoyment of sounds and the like, is known as one who has conquered the senses.

BP

ज्ञानविज्ञानसम्पन्नः सूर्यभक्त्या समन्वितः ।। पाषंडयोगमुक्तश्च स वै भोजक उच्यते।। १२ ।।

Endowed with knowledge and discernment, joined with devotion to the Sun, and freed from association with heretical doctrines, he indeed is called a Sun-priest.

BP 1.172.13

सूर्यधर्माद्भवेज्ज्ञानं ज्ञानाद्वैराग्यसंभवः ।। ज्ञानवैराग्ययुक्तस्य सूर्ययोगः प्रवर्तते ।। १३ ।।

From the Sun’s dharma arises knowledge; from knowledge arises detachment; for one possessing knowledge and detachment, union with the Sun arises.

BP

सूर्ययोगाच्च सर्वज्ञः परिपूर्णः सुनिर्वृतः ।। आत्मन्यवस्थितः शुद्धः सूर्यवद्दि वि मोदते ।। १४ ।।

Through union with the Sun, one is all-knowing, complete, fully tranquil, abiding in the self, pure, and rejoices in heaven like the Sun.

BP 1.172.15

सर्वेषामेव भूतानामुत्तमः पुरुषः स्मृतः ।। पुरुषेभ्यो द्विजः श्रेष्ठो द्विजेभ्यो ग्रंथपारगः ।। १५ ।।

Among all beings the human person is held highest; among persons the Brahmin is superior; among Brahmins, one who has mastered the texts.

BP

ग्रन्थिभ्यो वेदविद्वांसस्तेभ्यस्त त्त्वार्थचिंतकाः ।। अर्थविद्भ्यश्च ज्ञानार्थप्रतिबुद्धो विशिष्यते ।। १६ ।।

Among those learned in texts, Veda-knowers are higher; above them are thinkers on the meaning of reality; above those who know meaning is one awakened to the meaning of knowledge.

BP 1.172.17

ज्ञानार्थकोटिकोटिभ्यो वरिष्ठा योगिनो मताः ।। योगिनां कोटिकोटिभ्यो भोजकश्चोत्तमो भवेत् ।। १७ ।।

Among crores upon crores of seekers of knowledge, yogis are considered superior; among crores upon crores of yogis, the Sun-priest is highest.

BP

योगज्ञा योगनिष्ठाश्च पितरो योगसंभवाः ।। भोजिते भोजके सर्वे प्रीताः स्युस्ते न संशयः ।। १८ ।।

The ancestors know yoga, are steadfast in yoga, and are born of yoga; when a Sun-priest is fed, they are all pleased, of this there is no doubt.

BP 1.172.19

सर्वज्ञानतपोदानैः कृतैर्दत्तैश्च यत्फलम् ।। तत्फलं लभते सर्वं विधिवद्भोज्यभोजकम् ।। १९ ।।

All the fruit of knowledge, austerities, and gifts performed and given is obtained by duly feeding a Sun-priest.

BP

यश्च द्रव्यकलापात्मा दक्षिणा हविर्ऋत्विजः ।। ऋग्यजुःसाम योगैश्च देवयज्ञः प्रकीर्तितः ।। ।।

A sacrifice to the gods is declared to consist of collected materials, priestly fee, oblation, officiating priests, and the conjunctions of Ṛg, Yajur, and Sāma.

BP 1.172.21

ब्रह्मचर्यं तपो मौनं क्षांतिराहारलाघवम् ।। इत्येतत्तपसां रूपं सुधीरं पंचलक्षणम् ।। २१ ।।

Celibacy, austerity, silence, patience, and moderation in food: this is the fivefold form of austerities for the wise.

BP

यच्च दिष्टं विशिष्टं च न्यायप्राप्तं च यद्भवेत् ।। तत्तद्गुणवते देयमित्येतद्दानलक्षणम् ।। २२ ।।

Whatever is prescribed, distinguished, and rightly obtained should be given to one of good qualities; this is the mark of giving.

BP 1.172.23

विवर्धनीं सहस्राणां सर्वसस्यप्ररोहिणीम् ।। दद्याद्भूमिं जलोपेतां भूमिदानं तदुच्यते ।। २३ ।।

One should give land that increases by thousands, makes every crop sprout, and is supplied with water; this is called the gift of land.

BP

एकच्छत्रां महीं कृत्वा द्विजेभ्यः प्रतिपादयेत् ।। संपूर्णां पर्वतारण्यैर्भूमिदानं तदुच्यते।। २४ ।।

Having brought the whole earth under one parasol, one should present it to Brahmins, complete with mountains and forests; this is called the gift of land.

BP 1.172.25

कन्यामलंकृतां दद्यादधनाय नराधिप ।। द्विजाय वेदविदुषे कन्यादानं तदुच्यते ।। २५ ।।

O ruler of men, one should give an adorned maiden to a poor Brahmin learned in the Veda; this is called the gift of a maiden.

BP

सर्वदोषविनिर्मुक्तां कुलयोग्यामलंकृताम् ।। मध्यमोत्तमवस्त्राणां यो दद्यादहतानि च ।। एतत्समासतो ज्ञेयं वस्त्रदानस्य लक्षणम् ।। २६ ।।

Free from all faults, suitable to the family, and adorned: whoever gives unworn garments of medium and excellent quality—this, in brief, is known as the mark of giving garments.

BP 1.172.27

ब्रह्मविष्णुसमाधिक्यकांतिशीलपरायणः ।। अहोरात्रं न भुञ्जीत ह्युपवासस्य लक्षणम् ।। २७ ।।

Devoted to the splendor and conduct of Brahmā and Viṣṇu, one should eat nothing by day or night; this is the mark of fasting.

BP

चत्वारिंशत्समायुक्तं पिंडानां हि शतद्वयम् ।। मासे ह्यद्यायथाकाममिदं चाद्रायणं स्मृतम् ।। २८ ।।

Two hundred mouthfuls joined with forty in a month, according to one’s desire: this is remembered as the lunar vow.

BP 1.172.29

ऋषिभिः सर्वशास्त्रज्ञैस्तपोनिष्ठैर्जितेंद्रियैः ।। देवैश्च सेवितं तोयं क्षितौ तत्तीर्थमु च्यते ।। २९ ।।

Water served by sages who know all the śāstras, devoted to austerity and self-controlled, and by the gods, is called a sacred ford on earth.

BP

सूर्यावांतरस्थानानि पुण्यक्षेत्राणि निर्दिशेत् ।। मृतानां तेषु सूर्यत्वं सौरक्षेत्रेषु देहिनाम् ।। ।।

One should declare the Sun’s intermediate stations to be sacred regions; embodied beings who die there attain the state of the Sun in the solar regions.

BP 1.172.31

दानान्यावसथं कुर्यादुद्यानं देवतागृहम् ।। आवसथ तीर्थेष्वेतानि यः कुर्यात्सोऽक्षयं लभते फलम् ।। ३१ ।।

One should make gifts and build a rest-house, a garden, and a shrine to the gods; whoever makes these at rest-houses and sacred fords obtains imperishable fruit.

BP

क्षांतिः स्पृहा तथा सत्यं दानं शीलं तपः श्रुतम् ।। एतदष्टांगमुद्दिष्टं परं पात्रस्य लक्षणम् ।। ३२ ।।

Patience, longing, truth, giving, conduct, austerity, and sacred learning: this is declared the eightfold mark of the highest recipient.

BP 1.172.33

यज्ञोपवासदानानि तपस्तीर्थफलानि च ।। संपूर्णं लभते भक्त्या भोजयित्वा तु भोजकान् ।। ३३ ।।

By feeding Sun-priests with devotion, one gains in full the fruits of sacrifices, fasts, gifts, austerity, and sacred fords.

BP

सूरे भक्तिः क्षमा सत्यं दशेंद्रियविनिग्रहः ।। सुखितेषु च मैत्री च सूर्यधर्मस्य लक्षणम् ।। ३४ ।।

Devotion to the Sun, patience, truth, restraint of the ten senses, and friendliness toward the joyful are the marks of the Sun’s dharma.

BP 1.172.35

सूर्यभक्तं द्विजं भक्त्या यः श्राद्धेषु च भोजयेत् ।। कुलसप्तकमुद्धृत्य सूर्यलोके महीयते ।। ३५ ।।

Whoever with devotion feeds at ancestral rites a Brahmin devoted to the Sun, having raised seven families, is honored in the Sun’s world.

BP

बहुनात्र किमुक्तेन सूर्यभक्तं तु भोजयेत् ।। सूर्यभक्तेन यद्भुक्तं भानुनामाश्रयं नृप ।। ३६ ।।

What need is there to say much? One should feed a devotee of the Sun; what a Sun-devotee eats has the Sun for its refuge, O king.

BP 1.172.37

न वेदविदुषां कोट्या लभ्यते चेह तत्फलम् ।। तत्फलं लभते राजन्भोजं भाज्य विधानतः ।। ३७ ।।

That fruit cannot be attained here by crores of Veda-knowers; O king, one obtains that fruit by feeding a Sun-priest according to rule.

BP

तस्माच्छ्राद्धे विशेषेण पुण्येषु दिवसेषु च ।। सूर्यमुद्दिश्य विप्रेंद्र भोजं संभोजयेन्नृप ।। ३८ ।।

Therefore, especially at ancestral rites and on sacred days, one should offer a meal for the Sun to the foremost Brahmin, O king.

BP 1.172.39

असंयतः संयतो वा सर्वावस्थां गतोपि वा ।। यश्चासौ रविभक्तः स्यात्सूर्यवत्पूज्य एव हि ।। ३९ ।।

Whether uncontrolled or controlled, or in any condition, whoever is a devotee of the Sun is indeed to be worshipped like the Sun.

BP

संसर्गाद्वापि वा लोभाद्भोजकं यस्तु भोजयेत् ।। सोऽपि यां गतिमाप्नोति न तां यज्ञशतैरपि ।। ।।

Whether through association or greed, whoever feeds a Sun-priest attains a state that even hundreds of sacrifices cannot attain.

BP 1.172.41

तस्मान्मान्यश्च पूज्यश्च रक्षणीयश्च सर्वदा ।। भोजकः कुरुशार्दूल सौरेण गतिमिच्छता ।। ४१ ।।

Therefore, a Sun-priest is always to be honored, worshipped, and protected by one who desires the solar path, O tiger of the Kurus.

BP

नाममात्रप्रयत्नोऽपि यदि स्याद्भोजको रवेः ।। सूर्यवत्स हि द्रष्टव्यः पूजनीयश्च भारत ।। ४२ ।।

Even if someone is a Sun-priest of the Sun only in name and by mere effort, he is to be regarded like the Sun and worshipped, O Bharata.

BP 1.172.43

गृहे श्राद्धस्य यत्पुण्यमरण्ये तच्छताधिकम् ।। सौराश्रमेषु विज्ञेयं तत्पुण्यमयुताधिकम् ।। ४३ ।।

The merit of ancestral rites at home is a hundredfold in the forest; in Sun-hermitages that merit is ten thousandfold.

BP

दत्त्वा तु भोजके सौम्यं ह्यासनं सपरिच्छदम् ।। धातुदंतमयं चापि राजा भवति भूतले ।। ४४ ।।

Having given a pleasant seat, supplied with fittings, and made of metal and ivory, one becomes king on earth.

BP 1.172.45

विमले वाससी दत्त्वा भोजकाय महीपते ।। उद्धृत्य शतसाहस्रं सूर्यलोके महीयते ।। ४५ ।।

Having given two spotless garments to a Sun-priest, O lord of the earth, having uplifted a hundred thousand, one is honored in the Sun’s world.

BP

दत्त्वा तु लोमशां राजन्भोजकाय शुभां बृहत् ।। रोम्णिरोम्णि सुवर्णस्य दत्तस्य फलमाप्नुयात् ।। ४६ ।।

Having given a large, auspicious woollen garment to a Sun-priest, O king, one obtains the fruit of giving gold for every hair.

BP 1.172.47

शंखं ददाति यो भक्त्या तथा दिव्ये च पादुके ।। सूर्यलोकमवाप्नोति तेजसा रविसन्निभः ।। ४७ ।।

Whoever gives a conch and also divine sandals with devotion reaches the Sun’s world, with splendor resembling the Sun.

BP

लिखापयति यो भक्त्या पुराणेन तु पुस्तकम् ।। युगकोटिशतं दिव्यं सूर्यलोके महीयते ।। ४८ ।।

Whoever has a book of the Purāṇa written with devotion is honored in the Sun’s world for a divine billion ages.

BP 1.172.49

भवेदिहागतः श्रीमान्सुखाढ्यो वेदपारगः ।। यः करोति गृहं भानोस्तत्स्थानं चोत्तमं भवेत् ।। ४९ ।।

Whoever builds a house for the Sun becomes prosperous here, rich in happiness, and versed in the Veda; that place, too, becomes highest.

BP

तत्सूर्यो भोजकः सोत्र भोजकः सूर्य एव हि ।। तेन भोजकविप्रेषु दानमक्षय्यमित्यपि ।। ।।

The Sun is the Sun-priest, and the Sun-priest here is indeed the Sun; therefore a gift to Sun-priest Brahmins is imperishable.

BP 1.172.51

यद्यद्यस्योपयुज्येत देयं तत्तस्य यत्नतः ।। उपयोगपरो नित्यं सूर्यस्तदुभयोरपि ।। ५१ ।।

Whatever is useful to him should be given to him with care; the Sun is always intent on the benefit of both.

BP

व्याख्याने सौरधर्मस्य कृत्वा आमलकं महत् ।। शोभितं पुष्पपत्राद्यैर्न्यसेत्तत्रासने सुराः ।। ५२ ।।

For the exposition of the Sun’s dharma, having made a great gooseberry adorned with flowers, leaves, and the like, one should place the gods there on a seat.

BP 1.172.53

शोभितं माल्यगन्धैस्तु सूर्यस्य साधनं महत् ।। पुरस्तात्तस्य संस्थाप्य आचार्यं पूजयेत्सदा ।। सूर्यवत्सौरधर्मे च तुल्यमेतद्द्वयं वचः ।। ५३ ।।

The great image of the Sun is adorned with garlands and fragrances; having set it before him, one should always worship the teacher. In the Sun’s dharma, these two injunctions are equal to the Sun.

BP

य एवं न्यायतो वक्ति सौरधर्मं शृणोति च ।। आयुर्विद्यां यशः कीर्तिमुपलभ्य रविं जपेत् ।। वदंत्यन्ये पिबंत्यन्ये सर्वे ते फलभागिनः ।। ५४ ।।

Whoever speaks and hears the Sun’s dharma according to rule, having obtained long life, knowledge, fame, and renown, should chant the Sun’s name; some speak, others drink, all share in the fruit.

BP 1.172.55

तस्मादेवंविधो धर्मो वाचकैश्च विदुर्बुधाः ।। तस्यान्ते पूजयेद्भक्त्या य इच्छेद्विपुलं यशः ।। ५५ ।।

Thus the wise know this kind of dharma through reciters; at its end, whoever desires abundant fame should worship with devotion.
