Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 185 — Procedure for the Maternal Ancestral Rite

BP 1.185.1

।। आदित्य उवाच ।। रात्रौ श्राद्धं न कुर्वीत राक्षसी कीर्तिता हि सा ।। सन्ध्ययोरुभयोर्वीर सूर्ये चैव तिरोहिते ।।१।।

The Sun said: One should not perform the ancestral rite at night, for it is called demonic; nor at either twilight, O hero, nor when the Sun has set.

BP 1.185.2

अकृत्वा मातृयज्ञं तु यः श्राद्धं परिवेषयेत् ।। तातस्य क्रोधसंयुक्तां हिंसामिच्छंति दारुणाम् ।।२।।

Whoever serves the ancestral rite without performing the maternal offering desires terrible violence joined with the father’s anger.

BP 1.185.3

।। अनूरुरुवाच ।। मातृश्राद्धं कथं कार्यं काश्च ता मातरः स्मृताः।। नांदीमुखाश्च पितरः कथं पूजामवाप्नुयुः ।। ३ ।।

Anūru said: How should the ancestral rite for mothers be performed, and which mothers are remembered? How do the auspicious-faced ancestors receive worship?

BP 1.185.4

।। आदित्य उवाच ।। हन्त ते सम्प्रवक्ष्यामि मातृश्राद्धविधिं खग ।। शृणु त्वं खगशार्दूल गदतो मम कृत्स्नशः ।।४।।

The Sun said: I shall fully tell you the procedure for the ancestral rite for mothers, O bird. Listen as I speak completely, O tiger among birds.

BP 1.185.5

पूर्वाह्णे भोजयेद्विप्रानष्टौ सर्वान्प्रदक्षिणान् ।। तथान्यं नवमं विप्रं चतुरश्च खगाधिप ।।५।।

In the forenoon, one should feed eight Brahmins, all in clockwise order, and likewise a ninth Brahmin and four more, O lord of birds.

BP 1.185.6

ऋजून्वै कृतपान्दत्त्वा सत्येन विधिवत्खग ।। कृत्वा यवैस्तिलार्थं तु दधिमिश्रं क्रमेण च ।।६।।

Having duly offered the properly prepared portions with truth, prepare barley and sesame mixed with curds in due order, O bird.

BP 1.185.7

गन्धपुष्पादिकं सर्वं कुर्याद्विप्रप्रदक्षिणम् ।। ब्राह्मणेभ्यस्ततो दद्यान्मधुरं भोजनं खग ।। ७ ।।

One should offer fragrances, flowers, and everything else while circumambulating the Brahmins; then one should give sweet food to the Brahmins, O bird.

BP 1.185.8

गुडमिश्रं खगश्रेष्ठ सवस्त्रमोदनं परम् ।। रसानां मोदकांश्चैव न च तान्कटुकांस्तथा ।। ८ ।।

O best of birds, give excellent cooked rice mixed with molasses, accompanied by a garment, and sweet cakes of various flavors, but not pungent ones.

BP 1.185.9

एवं भुक्तेषु विप्रेषु दद्यात्पिंडान्समाहितः ।। दध्यक्षतविमिश्रांस्तु बदरैश्च खगाधिप ।। ९ ।।

When the Brahmins have eaten, one should attentively give rice-ball offerings mixed with curds and unbroken rice, with jujubes, O lord of birds.

BP 1.185.10

कृत्वा तु मंडपं वीर चतुरस्रं प्रदक्षिणम् ।। पूर्वाग्रांश्च कुशान्दत्त्वा पुष्पाणां प्रकरं तथा ।। 1.185.१० ।।

Having made a square pavilion in clockwise order, O hero, place the sacred grass with its tips facing east, and likewise a heap of flowers.

BP 1.185.11

सव्येन पाणिना वीर विधिवत्खगसत्तम ।। मात्रे प्रमात्रे तन्मात्रे निर्वपेत्पूर्वतोमुखः ।। ११ ।।

With the left hand, according to rule, facing east, one should offer to the mother, the maternal grandmother, and the great-grandmother, O best of birds.

BP 1.185.12

पितुर्मात्रे तु तन्मात्रे निर्वपेद्विधिवत्खग ।। वृद्धायै प्रपितामह्यै तथान्यं निर्वपेद्बुधः ।।१२।।

One should offer according to rule to the father’s mother and to her mother, O bird; the wise one should also offer another to the aged great-grandmother.

BP 1.185.13

एवमुद्दिश्य वै मातॄः षट् पिंडान्निर्वपेत्खग ।। अष्टाशयेद्द्विजान्वीर मातृश्राद्धे खगाधिप ।। नवमं सर्वदैवत्यं भोजयेद्विधिवत्खग ।।१३।।

Having thus designated the six mothers, one should offer six rice-ball offerings. In the ancestral rite for mothers, one should feed eight Brahmins and properly feed a ninth for all the gods.

BP 1.185.14

नांदीमुखांस्तानुद्दिश्य पितॄन्पंच द्विजोत्तमान् ।। भोजयेद्विधिच्छ्राद्धे वृद्धिश्राद्धे प्रदक्षिणम् ।।१४।।

Having designated the five auspicious-faced ancestors, one should feed the best Brahmins according to the prescribed ancestral rite, in the rite for increase, proceeding clockwise.

BP 1.185.15

इत्थं श्राद्धद्वयं कुयाद्वृद्धौ कश्यपनंदन ।। तथान्यमपि ते वच्मि परं श्राद्धविधिं तव ।।१५।।

Thus one should perform the two ancestral rites during a rite for increase, O son of Kaśyapa; I shall also tell you another, higher procedure for the ancestral rite.

BP 1.185.16

अथैवं भोजयेच्छ्राद्धे तत्पूर्वं तु प्रवर्तयेत्।। अन्यथा तत्र लुंपंति सदेवासुरमानुषाः ।।१६।।

One should conduct the feeding at the ancestral rite in this way, beginning it beforehand; otherwise the gods, demons, and humans together plunder it.

BP 1.185.17

अग्न्यभावे तु विप्रस्य पाणावेवोपपादयेत्।। यो ह्यग्निः स द्विजो वीन्द मंत्रदर्शिभिरुच्यते।। ।।१७।।

When there is no fire, one should place the offering in a Brahmin’s hand. The twice-born is that fire, the seers of mantras say.

BP 1.185.18

पूर्व पात्रे यदन्नं च यच्चान्नमुपकल्पितम् ।। तेनैव सह भोक्तव्यं पृथग्भावो न विद्यते ।।१८।।

Whatever food is in the first vessel and whatever food has been prepared should be eaten together with it; there is no separation.

BP 1.185.19

द्वौ दैवेऽथर्वणौ विप्रौ प्राङ्मुखावुपवेशयेत्।। पित्र्ये त्रीनुदगास्यांश्च वृद्धौ चाध्वर्युसंगमान् ।।१९।।

For the rite to the gods, one should seat two Atharva-Veda Brahmins facing east; for the ancestors, three facing north; and for the rite of increase, those associated with the officiating priest.

BP 1.185.20

त्रीणि श्राद्धे पवित्राणि दौहित्रः कुतुपास्तिलाः ।। त्रीणि चात्र प्रशंसंति शौचमक्रोधमत्वरम् ।। 1.185.२० ।।

Three things purify an ancestral rite: a daughter’s son, the midday interval, and sesame. Three things are praised here: cleanliness, freedom from anger, and lack of haste.

BP 1.185.21

दौहित्रं खंडमित्युक्तं ललाटाय प्रजापते ।। तत्र शृंगस्य यत्पात्रं तद्दौहित्रमिति स्मृतम् ।।२१।।

The daughter’s son is said to be a fragment at Prajāpati’s forehead; the vessel made from a horn there is remembered as the daughter’s son.

BP 1.185.22

सव्यादंसात्परिभ्रष्टं नाभिदेशे व्यवस्थितम् ।। एकवस्त्रं तु तं विद्याद्दैवे पित्र्ये च वर्जयेत् ।।२२।।

A garment slipped from the left shoulder and resting at the navel is to be recognized as a single garment; one should avoid it in rites to the gods and ancestors.

BP 1.185.23

पितृदेवमनुष्याणां पूजनं भोजनं तथा ।। नोत्तरीयं विना कार्यं कृतं स्यान्निष्फलं यतः ।।२३।।

Worship and feeding for ancestors, gods, and humans should not be done without an upper garment, since, if done so, it would be fruitless.

BP 1.185.24

परिधानकृते स्कंधे गृहस्थो योऽर्चयेत्पितॄन् ।। न स तत्फलमाप्नोति यथा योगपटावृतः ।।२४।।

A householder who worships the ancestors with a garment resting on his shoulder does not obtain that fruit, as though covered by a meditation strap.

BP 1.185.25

वनस्थानां खगश्रेष्ठ यतीनां च महामते ।। सिद्धये कर्मणां वीर योगपट्टकमुच्यते ।।२५।।

For forest-dwellers and ascetics, O best of birds and greatly wise one, the meditation strap is prescribed for the accomplishment of rites, O hero.

BP 1.185.26

हस्तौ प्रक्षाल्य गंडूषं यः पिबेदविचक्षणः ।। स तु दैवं च पित्र्यं च आत्मानं चोपघातयेत् ।।२६।।

Whoever, without discernment, swallows a mouthful of water after washing his hands harms the rite to the gods, the ancestral rite, and himself.

BP 1.185.27

भोजनेष्वेव तिष्ठंति स्वस्ति कुर्वंति ये द्विजाः ।। आसुरं तद्भवेच्छ्राद्धं पितॄणां नोपतिष्ठते ।। २७ ।।

Those Brahmins who remain only for the meals and give blessings make the rite demonic; it does not reach the ancestors.

BP 1.185.28

दातारो नोऽभिवर्धंता वेदाः संततिरेव च ।। श्रद्धा च नो मा व्यगमद्बहुदेयं च नोऽस्त्विति ।।२८।।

May our givers increase, and the Vedas and our descendants continue; may faith not leave us, and may we have much to give.
