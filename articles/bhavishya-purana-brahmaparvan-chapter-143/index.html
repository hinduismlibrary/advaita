Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 143 — Incense and Related Procedures

BP 1.143.1

सुमन्तुरुवाच । श्रुत्वैवमेव साम्बेन व्यासात्सत्यवतीसुतात् । अव्यङ्गस्य च उत्पत्तिं पुनरागान्महामतिः ॥

Sumantu said: Having heard from Vyāsa, son of Satyavatī, about the origin of Avyaṅga, the great-minded Sāmba returned again.

BP 1.143.2

अथागत्य महातेजाः साम्बो गत्वाश्रमं पुनः । नारदस्य महाबाहोर्नारदं वाक्यमब्रवीत् ॥

Then the greatly radiant Sāmba went again to the hermitage of mighty-armed Nārada and spoke these words to Nārada.

BP 1.143.3

कथमुत्क्षिप्य वै धूपं भोजकैः सवितुर्मुने । स्नानमाचमनं चैवमर्घ्यदानं महात्मने ॥

How do the Sun-priests raise incense to Savitṛ, O sage? How are bathing, ritual sipping, and the water offering to the great one performed?

BP 1.143.4

साम्बस्य वचनं श्रुत्वा नारदो मुनिसत्तमः । उवाच कुरुशार्दूल साम्बं जाम्बवतीसुतम् ॥

Hearing Sāmba’s words, Nārada, the best of sages, spoke to Sāmba, son of Jāmbavatī: “O tiger among the Kurus.”

BP 1.143.5

हन्त ते कथयिष्यामि रवेर्धूपविधिक्रमम् । स्नानमाचमनं चैव स्वर्णदानं तथैव च ॥

Well, I shall tell you the order of the incense-offering procedure for the Sun, and likewise bathing, ritual sipping, and the giving of gold.

BP 1.143.6

आचान्तस्त्रिस्ततः स्नात्वा वाससी निर्मले शुभे । अनार्द्रे संवसीतैव पवित्रे परिधाय च ॥

Having sipped water three times and bathed, one should put on two clean, auspicious garments, pure and dry.

BP 1.143.7

उदङ्मुखः प्राङ्मुखो वाप्याचामेच्च प्रयत्नतः । जले जलस्थो नाचामेज्जलादुत्तीर्य यत्नतः ॥

Facing north or east, one should carefully sip water. One should not sip while standing in water, but only after carefully coming out of the water.

BP 1.143.8

अप्सु सूर्यस्तथाग्निश्च माता देवी सरस्वती । तस्मादुत्तीर्य चाचामेन्नाचामेत्तु जलाशये ॥

The Sun, Fire, Mother Goddess, and Sarasvatī are in the waters. Therefore, having come out, one should sip water; one should not sip in a reservoir.

BP 1.143.9

उपविश्य शुचौ देशे प्रयतः प्रागुदङ्मुखः । पादौ प्रक्षाल्य हस्तौ च अन्तर्जानुस्तथाचमेत् ॥

Having sat in a clean place, attentive, facing east or north, one should wash the feet and hands and sip water with the hands between the knees.

BP 1.143.10

प्रसन्नास्त्रिः पिबेत्त्वापः प्रयतः सुसमाहितः । सम्मार्जनं तु द्विः कुर्यात्त्रिभिरभ्युक्षणं पुनः ॥

Serene, restrained, and well-composed, one should sip water three times. One should wipe twice and then sprinkle three times.

BP 1.143.11

मूर्धानं खानि चात्मानमुपस्पृश्यानु पूर्वशः । आचान्तोऽर्कं नमस्कृत्य शौचेषु शुचितामियात् ॥

Having touched the head, the sense openings, and oneself in order, having sipped water and bowed to Arka, one should attain purity in the acts of purification.

BP 1.143.12

क्रियां यः कुरुते मोहादनाचम्येह नास्तिकः । भवन्तीह क्रियाः सर्वा वृथा तस्य न संशयः ॥

A nonbeliever who through delusion performs a rite here without sipping water—every rite of his becomes fruitless, without doubt.

BP 1.143.13

शुचिकामा हि वै देवा वेदैरेवमुदाहृताः । इनोपासाकृतश्चैव सर्वे देवाः प्रयत्नतः ॥

The gods indeed desire purity; this is declared in the Vedas. All the gods are made through worship of the Sun, with care.

BP 1.143.14

शौचमेव प्रशंसन्ति शौचाङ्गैर्हि विधीयते । आचान्तो मौनमास्थाय देवागारं ततो व्रजेत् ॥

They praise purity alone; it is prescribed through the acts of purification. Having sipped water and observed silence, one should then go to the temple.

BP 1.143.15

श्वासरोधनिमित्तं तु प्राणमाच्छाद्य वाससा । शिरः प्रावृत्य यत्नेन केशोदकनिवृत्तये ॥

For the purpose of restraining the breath, one should cover the vital breath with a cloth, carefully covering the head to prevent water from the hair.

BP 1.143.16

ततः पूजां रवेः कुर्यात्पुष्पैर्नानाविधैः शुभैः । गायत्रीं सशिरस्कां च यजमानः प्रयत्नतः ॥

Then one should worship the Sun with many kinds of auspicious flowers. The ritual patron should carefully recite the Gāyatrī together with its head.

BP 1.143.17

धूपं ततोऽग्नये दद्यात्प्रथमं गुग्गुलाहुतम् । पुष्पाञ्जलिं ततो गृह्य तच्छिखायां प्रयत्नतः ॥

Then one should offer incense to the fire, first an offering of guggul resin. Then, carefully taking a handful of flowers, one should hold it at the tip of that flame.

BP 1.143.18

रवेर्मूर्धनि तं दद्याद्देवमन्त्रमुदाहरन् ॥

One should place it on the Sun’s head while uttering the god’s mantra.

BP 1.143.19

ॐ व्रतेन यद्व्रतिनो वर्जयन्ति देवा मनुष्याः पितरश्च सर्वे । तस्यादित्यं प्रसरं च मनामहे यस्तेजसा प्रथमं नाविभाति ॥

Om. By the vow that all vow-keepers—gods, humans, and ancestors—set aside, we honor the course of that Āditya, who does not shine forth first through his splendor.

BP 1.143.20

धूपवेलाः स्मृताः पञ्च धूपेष्वेव तु पञ्चसु । हवनाद्याः क्रियाः पञ्च रक्षिष्येऽहं तथा पुनः ॥

Five times for incense are prescribed among the five incense offerings. I shall again protect the five rites beginning with the fire-offering.

BP 1.143.21

दण्डनायकवेला तु प्रत्यूषे ऋक्षदर्शनात् । नाज्ञावेला प्रदोषस्तु तत्त्वकार्यं विजानता ॥

The time of Daṇḍanāyaka is at dawn, from the sighting of the constellations; the appointed time is twilight for one who understands the rite.

BP 1.143.22

त्रिकालं तु रवेः पूजा कर्तव्या सूर्यदर्शनात् । अर्धोदितस्तु पूर्वाह्णे ततोऽर्द्धस्तु रविर्विभुः ॥

Worship of the Sun is to be performed three times, when the Sun is seen. In the forenoon the mighty Sun is half-risen; then he is half—

BP 1.143.23

हेलयेति च पूर्वाह्णे मध्याह्ने ज्वलनाय च । तथैव मण्डले देयं नीचाह्ने ज्वलनाय च ॥

In the forenoon, with ease; at midday, to the Burning One; likewise, it should be offered in the solar orb in the late afternoon to the Burning One.

BP 1.143.24

चन्दनोदकमिश्राणि गन्धोदकयुतानि च । पद्मानि करवीराणि तथा रक्तोत्पलानि च ॥

Lotuses mixed with sandalwood water and joined with fragrant water, oleanders, and red lotuses;

BP 1.143.25

कुसुमोदकमिश्राणि कुरुंटकुसुमं तथा । गन्धादीनि च दिव्यानि कृत्वा वै ताम्रभाजने ॥

Having placed flower-water mixtures, yellow barleria flowers, and divine fragrances and the like in a copper vessel,

BP 1.143.26

धूपं दत्त्वाग्नये वीर प्रयत्नाद्गुग्गुलाहुतिम् । अर्घ्यपात्रं तदा गृह्य कुर्यादावाहनं रवेः ॥

Having offered incense to the fire, O hero, carefully make an offering of guggul resin. Then, taking the water-offering vessel, one should invite the Sun.

BP 1.143.27

एहि सूर्य सहस्रांशो तेजोराशे जगत्पते । अनुकम्प्य मां देव गृहाणार्घ्यं दिवाकर ॥

“Come, O Sun, thousand-rayed, mass of radiance, lord of the world. Have compassion on me, O god; accept the water offering, O day-maker.”

BP 1.143.28

अनेनावाहनं कृत्वा जानुभ्यामवनीं गतः । रवेर्निवेदयेदर्घ्यमादित्यहृदये गतः ॥

Having made the invocation thus, one should go down to the ground on both knees and offer the water offering to the Sun, having reached the heart of Āditya.

BP 1.143.29

ॐ नमो भगवते आदित्याय विश्वाय खेशाय ब्रह्मणेलोककर्तृणे। ईशानाय पुराणाय सहस्राक्षाय ते नमः ॥

Om, salutation to the blessed Āditya, the All, the Lord of Space, Brahman, maker of the worlds, the Ruler, the Ancient One, the Thousand-eyed. Salutation to you.

BP 1.143.30

सोमाय ऋग्यजुरथर्वाय। ॐ भूर्भुवःस्वःॐमहः ॐजनः ॐतपः ॐ सत्यं ब्रह्मणे मुण्डे मध्ये पुरतः । आदित्याय नमः ॥

To Soma, to the Ṛg, Yajur, and Atharva Vedas. Om: Earth, atmosphere, heaven, vastness, people, austerity, truth. Salutation to Brahman on the head, in the middle, and in front; salutation to Āditya.

BP 1.143.31

नारद उवाच । सावित्र्याश्च परे तन्त्रे त्रैलोक्यत्राणकारिणे । परितः परिगृह्याथ धूपभाजनमुत्क्षिपेत् ॥

Nārada said: In the supreme rite of Sāvitrī, the protector of the three worlds, having taken up the incense vessel all around, one should lift it.

BP 1.143.32

निवेदयेत्ततो धूपं वाचमेतामुदीरयेत् । त्वमेक एव रुद्राणां वसूनां च पुरातनः ॥

Then one should offer the incense and utter these words: “You alone are the ancient one among the Rudras and the Vasus.”

BP 1.143.33

देवानां गीर्भिरभितः संस्तुतः शाश्वतो दिवि । पूर्वाह्णे च त्वया तेन मध्याह्ने चापरेण तु ॥

“Praised on every side by the words of the gods, eternal in heaven; by you in the forenoon, and by the other at midday.”

BP 1.143.34

ॐ नमो भगवते ज्ञानात्मने त्वां च । विष्णोस्तत्परमं पदं सदा पश्यंति सूरयः ॥

Om, salutation to the blessed one whose nature is knowledge, and to you. The seers always behold that highest station of Viṣṇu.

BP 1.143.35

दिवाकरस्तु सायाह्ने मन्त्रेणार्घ्यं निवेदयेत् । ॐ नमो वरुणाय शम्भवे ॥

At evening, one should present the water offering to the day-maker with the mantra: “Om, salutation to Varuṇa and Śambhu.”

BP 1.143.36

ॐ आकृष्णेन रजसा वर्तमानो निवेशयन्नमृतं मर्त्यं च । हिरण्ययेन सविता रथेनादेवो याति भुवनानि पश्यन् ॥

“Moving through the dark firmament, placing the immortal and the mortal, the god Savitṛ goes in a golden chariot, looking upon the worlds.”

BP 1.143.37

अनेन विधिना दत्त्वा धूपं सूर्याय भोजकः । उत्क्षिपेच्चैव धूपेन विशेद्गर्भगृहं ततः ॥

Having offered incense to the Sun according to this procedure, the Sun-priest should lift the incense and then enter the inner chamber.

BP 1.143.38

ततः प्रविश्य धूपं तु प्रतिमार्थं निवेदयेत् । मन्त्रेण मिहिरायेति निक्षुभायेति नित्यशः ॥

Then, entering, one should offer incense to the image with the mantras “To Mihira” and “to Nikṣubhā,” at all times.

BP 1.143.39

ततो राज्ञै नमश्चेति निक्षुभायै ततो नमः । दण्डनायकसंज्ञाय पिङ्गलाय च वै नमः ॥

Then, “salutation to the king,” and then salutation to Nikṣubhā; salutation to the one named Daṇḍanāyaka and indeed to Piṅgala.

BP 1.143.40

तथा राज्ञाय स्रौषाय तथेशाय गरुत्मते । ततः प्रदक्षिणं कुर्वन्दिग्देवेभ्यो निवेदयेत् ॥

Likewise, salutation to the king, to Srauṣa, to the Lord, and to Garutmān. Then, while circumambulating, one should make offerings to the deities of the directions.

BP 1.143.41

दिण्डिने तु ततो दद्याद्धेमन्ताय यदूत्तम । महेश्वराय दद्यात्तु तथा व्योमाय यादव ॥

Then one should offer to Diṇḍin and to Hemanta, O best of the Yadus; one should also offer to Maheśvara and to Vyoma, O Yādava.

BP 1.143.42

(विश्वेभ्यो देवेभ्यो नमः । रुद्रेभ्यो नमः) । ॐ ब्रह्मणे मुण्डपतये आदित्याय पुरुषेश्वराय सूर्याय नमोनमः ॥

Salutation to all the gods; salutation to the Rudras. Om, repeated salutation to Brahman, lord of Muṇḍa, to Āditya, lord of beings, and to the Sun.

BP 1.143.43

ॐ अनेककान्तये नत्वा शेषाय वासुकितक्षककर्कोटकाय पद्मशङ्खकुलिकेभ्यो नागराजेभ्यो नमः ॥

Om, having bowed to the many-rayed one, salutation to Śeṣa, Vāsuki, Takṣaka, Karkoṭaka, Padma, conch, Kulika, and the serpent kings.

BP 1.143.44

तलसुतलपातालातलवितलरसातलादिवासिभ्यो दैत्यादानवपिशाचेभ्यो नमः । ततः प्रदक्षिणं कुर्यान्मातृकाभ्यो नमोनमः ( ॐ ग्रहेभ्यो नमः ॥

Salutation to the demons, demons, and flesh-eating spirits dwelling in Tala, Sutala, Pātāla, Atala, Vitala, Rasātala, and the other [regions]. Then one should circumambulate the Mothers; repeated salutation to the Mothers. Om, salutation to the planets.

BP 1.143.45

ॐ दण्डनायकाय नमः । ॐ मार्तंडाय नमः । ॐ विनायकाय नमः । ) ॥

Om, salutation to Daṇḍanāyaka; Om, salutation to Mārtaṇḍa; Om, salutation to Vināyaka.

BP 1.143.46

एवमुद्दिश्य नामानि धूपं दत्त्वा वरानन । उत्क्षिप्तो यत्र वै धूपो मुक्त्वा तत्रैव तं पुनः ॥

Having thus named them and offered incense, O fair-faced one, wherever the incense has been lifted, one should leave it there again.

BP 1.143.47

सूर्यगुप्तैरभिष्टूय एवं विज्ञाय ते ततः । अर्चितस्त्वं यथा शक्त्या मया भक्त्या विभावसो । ऐहिकामुष्मिकीं नाथ कार्यसिद्धिं वदस्व मे ॥

Having praised those guarded by the Sun and understood thus, then say: “You have been worshipped by me, O Vibhāvasu, with devotion according to my ability. O lord, tell me the success of my work in this world and the next.”

BP 1.143.48

एवं त्रिषवणं स्नात्वा योऽर्चयेत्प्रणतो रविम् । विधिना तु यथोक्तेन सोऽश्वमेधफलं लभेत् ॥

Whoever, having bathed at the three ritual bathing times, worships the Sun with bowed head according to the stated procedure obtains the fruit of the horse sacrifice.

BP 1.143.49

यश्चैवं कुरुते नित्यं यथोक्तं धूपविस्तरम् । स पुत्रवानरोगी च मृतः संलीयते रवौ ॥

Whoever always performs this extensive incense rite as stated becomes a father and is free from disease; when dead, he merges with the Sun.

BP 1.143.50

विधिना तु यथोक्तेन क्रियमाणानि यत्नतः । सर्वकार्याणि सिद्ध्यन्ति सफलानि भवन्ति च ॥

When the rites are performed carefully according to the stated procedure, all undertakings succeed and bear fruit.

BP 1.143.51

पुष्पं श्रेष्ठं यदा न स्यात्पत्राणि समुपाहरेत् । पत्रं न स्यात्ततो धूपं धूपो न स्यात्ततो जलम् ॥

When the best flower is unavailable, one should gather leaves; if leaves are unavailable, then incense; if incense is unavailable, then water.

BP 1.143.52

सर्वं न स्याद्यदा चैव प्रणिपातेन पूजयेत् । अशक्तः प्रणिपातस्य मनसा पूजयेद्रविम् ॥

When all are unavailable, one should worship by prostration; one unable to prostrate should worship the Sun with the mind.

BP 1.143.53

असम्भवे तु द्रव्याणां विधिरेष प्रकीर्तितः । द्रव्याणां सम्भवे चैव सर्वमेवोपहारयेत् ॥

When the materials are unavailable, this procedure is prescribed; when the materials are available, one should offer them all.

BP 1.143.54

मन्त्रैः कर्मयुतो यस्तु मित्रे धूपं निवेदयेत् । उच्चारणाच्च वै तेषां धूपप्रीतो भवेद्रविः ॥

Whoever offers incense to Mitra with the rites accompanied by mantras, by their recitation makes the Sun pleased with the incense.

BP 1.143.55

शिरो नासामुखं चैव भृशमावृत्य यत्नतः । पूजयेद्भास्करं वीर शिथिलं तु न कारयेत् ॥

Carefully covering the head, nose, and mouth well, one should worship Bhāskara, O hero; one should not leave the covering loose.

BP 1.143.56

नलिनेन तु राजेन्द्र नरो याति दिवाकरम् । तस्माद्युक्तं सदा कार्यं पूज्यते च दिवाकरः ॥

By a lotus, O king, a person reaches the Sun. Therefore the proper act should always be done, and the Sun is worshipped.

BP 1.143.57

तेऽश्वमेधफलं प्राप्य सूर्यलोकं व्रजन्ति हि । धूपेन पूज्यमानं तु नराः पश्यन्ति यादव ॥

They indeed obtain the fruit of the horse sacrifice and go to the world of the Sun. People see the Sun being worshipped with incense, O Yādava.

BP 1.143.58

यान्ति ते परमं स्थानं यन्न पश्यन्ति सूरयः । क्रियमाणं तथार्कं च भक्त्या पश्यन्ति ये नराः । सर्वान्कामानिह प्राप्य ते यान्ति परमं पदम् ॥

They go to the highest station, which the seers do not see. Those who see Arka being worshipped with devotion obtain all desires here and then go to the highest state.
