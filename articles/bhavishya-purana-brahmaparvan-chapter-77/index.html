Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 77 — The Cosmic Person and the Origin of the Sun

BP 1.77.1

नारद उवाच । विस्तरेणानुपूर्व्य च सूर्यं निगदतः शृणु । ततः शेषान् प्रवक्ष्ये ऽहं नमस्कृत्य विवस्वते ॥ ॥

Nārada said: Hear as I describe the Sun in detail and in due order. Then, after bowing to Vivasvat, I shall explain the remaining matters.

Word-for-word
nāradaḥ uvāca — Nārada said;
vistareṇa — in detail;
anupūrvyā — in due order;
ca — and;
sūryam — the Sun;
nigadataḥ — while describing;
śṛṇu — hear;
tataḥ — then;
śeṣān — the remaining matters;
pravakṣye aham — I shall explain;
namaskṛtya — having bowed;
vivasvate — to Vivasvat.
Transliteration
nārada uvāca | vistareṇānupūrvya ca sūryaṃ nigadataḥ śṛṇu | tataḥ śeṣān pravakṣye ’haṃ namaskṛtya vivasvate || 1 ||

BP 1.77.2

अव्यक्तं कारणं यत् तन् नित्यं सदसदात्मकम् । प्रधानं प्रकृतिश् चेति यम् आहुस् तत्त्वचिन्तकाः ॥ ॥

That unmanifest cause, eternal and consisting of being and non-being, is what thinkers call Pradhāna and Prakṛti.

Word-for-word
avyaktam — unmanifest;
kāraṇam — cause;
yat tat — which is that;
nityam — eternal;
sat-asat-ātmakam — consisting of being and non-being;
pradhānam — Pradhāna;
prakṛtiḥ ca iti — and Prakṛti, thus;
yam — which;
āhuḥ — they call;
tattva-cintakāḥ — thinkers on reality.
Transliteration
avyaktaṃ kāraṇaṃ yat tan nityaṃ sadasadātmakam | pradhānaṃ prakṛtiś ceti yam āhus tattvacintakāḥ || 2 ||

BP 1.77.3

गन्धैर् वर्णैर् असैर् हीनं शब्दस्पर्शविवर्जितम् । जगद्योनिं महद्भूतं परं ब्रह्म सनातनम् ॥ ॥

Devoid of smell, color, and taste, free from sound and touch, it is the great being, the womb of the universe, the supreme eternal Brahman.

Word-for-word
gandhaiḥ — of smell;
varṇaiḥ — of color;
asaiḥ — of taste;
hīnam — devoid;
śabda-sparśa-vivarjitam — free from sound and touch;
jagat-yonim — womb of the universe;
mahat-bhūtam — great being;
param brahma — supreme Brahman;
sanātanam — eternal.
Transliteration
gandhair varṇair asair hīnaṃ śabdasparśavivarjitam | jagadyoniṃ mahadbhūtaṃ paraṃ brahma sanātanam || 3 ||

BP 1.77.4

निग्रहं सर्वभूतानाम् अव्यक्तं अभवत् किल । अनाद्यन्तम् अजं सूक्ष्मं त्रिगुणं प्रभवाप्ययम् ॥ ॥

The unmanifest indeed became the restraint of all beings: without beginning or end, unborn, subtle, threefold in qualities, and the source and dissolution of all.

Word-for-word
nigraham — restraint;
sarva-bhūtānām — of all beings;
avyaktam — the unmanifest;
abhavat kila — indeed became;
anādi-antam — without beginning or end;
ajam — unborn;
sūkṣmam — subtle;
tri-guṇam — threefold in qualities;
prabhava-apyayam — source and dissolution.
Transliteration
nigrahaṃ sarvabhūtānām avyaktaṃ abhavat kila | anādyantam ajaṃ sūkṣmaṃ triguṇaṃ prabhavāpyayam || 4 ||

BP 1.77.5

अनाकारम् अविज्ञेयं तम् आहुः पुरुषं परम् । तस्यात्मना सर्वम् इदं जगद्व्याप्तं महात्मनः ॥ ॥

Formless and unknowable, that they call the supreme Puruṣa. By the Self of that great-souled one, this entire universe is pervaded.

Word-for-word
anākāram — formless;
avijñeyam — unknowable;
tam — that;
āhuḥ — they call;
puruṣam param — the supreme Puruṣa;
tasya ātmanā — by his Self;
sarvam idam jagat — this entire universe;
vyāptam — pervaded;
mahātmanaḥ — of the great-souled one.
Transliteration
anākāram avijñeyaṃ tam āhuḥ puruṣaṃ param | tasyātmanā sarvam idaṃ jagadvyāptaṃ mahātmanaḥ || 5 ||

BP 1.77.6

तस्य् एश्वरस्य प्रतिमा ज्ञानवैराग्यलक्षणा । धर्मेश्वर्यकृता बुद्धिर् ब्राह्मी तस्याभिमानिनः ॥ ॥

The image of that Lord is marked by knowledge and detachment. The intellect of that self-identifying one, fashioned by dharma and sovereignty, is Brāhmī.

Word-for-word
tasya īśvarasya — of that Lord;
pratimā — image, manifestation;
jñāna-vairāgya-lakṣaṇā — marked by knowledge and detachment;
dharma-īśvarya-kṛtā — fashioned by dharma and sovereignty;
buddhiḥ — intellect;
brāhmī — Brāhmī;
tasya abhimāninaḥ — of that self-identifying one.
Transliteration
tasy eśvarasya pratimā jñānavairāgyalakṣaṇā | dharmeśvaryakṛtā buddhir brāhmī tasyābhimāninaḥ || 6 ||

BP 1.77.7

अव्यक्ताज् जायते तस्य मनसा यद्यद् इच्छति । चतुर्मुखस्य ब्रह्मत्वे कालत्वे चान्तकृद् भवेत् ॥ ॥

From the Unmanifest arises whatever he desires through the mind. In the state of four-faced Brahmā he creates, and in the form of Time he becomes the maker of the end.

Word-for-word
avyaktāt — from the Unmanifest;
jāyate — arises;
tasya — for him, of him;
manasā — through the mind;
yad yad icchati — whatever he desires;
catur-mukhasya — of the four-faced one;
brahmatve — in Brahmāhood;
kālatve — in the form of Time;
ca — and;
anta-kṛt bhavet — becomes the maker of the end.
Transliteration
avyaktāj jāyate tasya manasā yadyad icchati | caturmukhasya brahmatve kālatve cāntakṛd bhavet || 7 ||

BP 1.77.8

सहस्रमूर्धा पुरुषस् तिस्रो ऽवस्थाः स्वयम्भुवः । सत्त्वं रजश् च ब्रह्मत्वे कालत्वे च रजस् तमः ॥ ॥

The Puruṣa has a thousand heads, and Svayambhū has three states. Sattva is in Brahmāhood, while in the form of Time there are rajas and tamas.

Word-for-word
sahasra-mūrdhā — thousand-headed;
puruṣaḥ — the Puruṣa;
tisraḥ avasthāḥ — three states;
svayambhuvaḥ — of Svayambhū;
sattvam — sattva;
rajaḥ ca — and rajas;
brahmatve — in Brahmāhood;
kālatve — in the form of Time;
rajaḥ tamaḥ — rajas and tamas.
Transliteration
sahasramūrdhā puruṣas tisro ’vasthāḥ svayambhuvaḥ | sattvaṃ rajaś ca brahmatve kālatve ca rajas tamaḥ || 8 ||

BP 1.77.9

सात्त्विकं पुरुषत्वे च गुणवृत्तं स्वयंभुवः । ब्रह्मत्वे सृजते लोकान् कालत्वे चापि संक्षिपेत् ॥ ॥

In Puruṣahood there is the sattvic mode of the qualities of Svayambhū. In Brahmāhood he creates the worlds, and in the form of Time he also contracts them.

Word-for-word
sāttvikam — sattvic;
puruṣatve — in Puruṣahood;
ca — and;
guṇa-vṛttam — mode of the qualities;
svayaṃbhuvaḥ — of Svayambhū;
brahmatve — in Brahmāhood;
sṛjate — creates;
lokān — worlds;
kālatve — in the form of Time;
ca api — and also;
saṃkṣipet — contracts, withdraws.
Transliteration
sāttvikaṃ puruṣatve ca guṇavṛttaṃ svayaṃbhuvaḥ | brahmatve sṛjate lokān kālatve cāpi saṃkṣipet || 9 ||

BP 1.77.10

पुरुषत्वे उदासीनस् तिस्रो ऽवस्थाः प्रजापतेः । त्रिधा विभज्य चात्मानं त्रिकालं सम्प्रवर्तते ॥ ॥

In Puruṣahood he is detached; these are the three states of Prajāpati. Dividing himself threefold, he sets the three times in motion.

Word-for-word
puruṣatve — in Puruṣahood;
udāsīnaḥ — detached, indifferent;
tisraḥ avasthāḥ — three states;
prajāpateḥ — of Prajāpati;
tridhā — threefold;
vibhajya — having divided;
ca — and;
ātmānam — himself;
tri-kālam — the three times;
sampravartate — sets in motion.
Transliteration
puruṣatve udāsīnas tisro ’vasthāḥ prajāpateḥ | tridhā vibhajya cātmānaṃ trikālaṃ sampravartate || 10 ||

BP 1.77.11

सृजते ग्रसते चैव वीक्षते च त्रिभिः स्वयम् । अग्रे हिरण्यगर्भस् तु प्रादुर्भूतः स्वयम्भुवः ॥ ॥

By the three states he himself creates, devours, and observes. In the beginning Hiraṇyagarbha arose, the self-born one.

Word-for-word
sṛjate — creates;
grasate — devours;
ca eva — and indeed;
vīkṣate — observes;
ca — and;
tribhiḥ — by the three;
svayam — himself;
agre — in the beginning;
hiraṇya-garbhaḥ — Hiraṇyagarbha;
tu — indeed;
prādurbhūtaḥ — arose, became manifest;
svayambhuvaḥ — the self-born.
Transliteration
sṛjate grasate caiva vīkṣate ca tribhiḥ svayam | agre hiraṇyagarbhas tu prādurbhūtaḥ svayambhuvaḥ || 11 ||

BP 1.77.12

आदित्यस्यादिदेवत्वाद् अजातत्वाद् अजः स्मृतः । देवेषु स महान् देवो महादेवः स्मृतस् ततः ॥ ॥

Because he is the first divinity of Āditya and unborn, he is remembered as Aja. Among the gods he is the great god, and therefore is remembered as Mahādeva.

Word-for-word
ādityasya ādi-devatvāt — because of being the first divinity of Āditya;
ajātatvāt — because of being unborn;
ajaḥ smṛtaḥ — remembered as Aja;
deveṣu — among the gods;
saḥ — he;
mahān devaḥ — great god;
mahādevaḥ smṛtaḥ — remembered as Mahādeva;
tataḥ — therefore.
Transliteration
ādityasyādidevatvād ajātatvād ajaḥ smṛtaḥ | deveṣu sa mahān devo mahādevaḥ smṛtas tataḥ || 12 ||

BP 1.77.13

सर्वेशत्वाच् च लोकस्याधीशत्वाच् च ईश्वरः । बृहत्त्वाच् च स्मृतो ब्रह्मा भवत्वाद् भव उच्यते ॥ ॥

Because he is lord of all and ruler of the world, he is Īśvara. Because of his greatness he is remembered as Brahmā; because of his becoming, he is called Bhava.

Word-for-word
sarva-īśatvāt — because of lordship over all;
ca — and;
lokasya — of the world;
adhīśatvāt — because of being the supreme ruler;
ca — and;
īśvaraḥ — Īśvara;
bṛhattvāt — because of greatness;
smṛtaḥ brahmā — remembered as Brahmā;
bhavatvāt — because of becoming;
bhavaḥ ucyate — is called Bhava.
Transliteration
sarveśatvāc ca lokasyādhīśatvāc ca īśvaraḥ | bṛhattvāc ca smṛto brahmā bhavatvād bhava ucyate || 13 ||

BP 1.77.14

पाति यस्मात् प्रजाः सर्वाः प्रजापति रतः स्मृतः । पुरे शेते च वै यस्मात् तस्मात् पुरुष उच्यते ॥ ॥

Since he protects all beings, he is remembered as Prajāpati. Since he lies in the city, he is therefore called Puruṣa.

Word-for-word

yasmāt — because;
prajāḥ sarvāḥ — all beings;

yasmāt — because;
tasmāt — therefore;
puruṣaḥ ucyate — is called Puruṣa.
Transliteration
pāti yasmāt prajāḥ sarvāḥ prajāpati rataḥ smṛtaḥ | pure śete ca vai yasmāt tasmāt puruṣa ucyate || 14 ||

BP 1.77.15

नोत्पाद्यत्वाद् अपूर्वत्वात् स्वयंभूर् इति विश्रुतः ॥ ॥

Because he is not produced and has no prior origin, he is renowned as Svayambhū.

Word-for-word

BP 1.77.16

हिरण्याण्डगतो यस्माद् ग्रहेशो वै दिवस्पतिः । तस्माद् हिरण्यगर्भो ऽसौ देवदेवो दिवाकरः ॥ ॥

Because he entered the Golden Egg, he is the lord of the planets and lord of heaven. Therefore he is Hiraṇyagarbha, the god of gods, Divākara.

Transliteration

BP 1.77.17

आपो नारा इति प्रोक्ता ऋषिभिस् तत्त्वदर्शिभिः । अयनं तस्य ता आपस् तेन नारायणः स्मृतः ॥ ॥

The waters are called Nārā by the seers who perceive reality. Since those waters are his resting place, he is remembered as Nārāyaṇa.

Transliteration

BP 1.77.18

अर इत्य् एष शीघ्रार्थो निपातः कविभिः स्मृतः । आप एवार्णवा भूत्वा न शीघ्रास् तेन ता नराः ॥ ॥

Transliteration

BP 1.77.19

एकार्णवे पुरा तस्मिन् नष्टे स्थावरजङ्गमे । नारायणाख्यः पुरुषः सुष्वाप सलिले तदा । सहस्रशीर्षा सुमनाः सहस्राक्षः सहस्रपात् ॥ ॥

In that ancient single ocean, when moving and unmoving beings had perished, the Person called Nārāyaṇa slept in the waters. He was thousand-headed, benevolent, thousand-eyed, and thousand-footed.

Transliteration

BP 1.77.20

सहस्रबाहुः प्रथमः प्रजापतिस् त्रयीपथे यः पुरुषो निगद्यते । आदित्यवर्णो भुवनस्य गोप्ता अपूर्व एकः पुरुषः पुराणः ॥ ॥

BP 1.77.21
