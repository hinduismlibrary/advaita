Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 77 — The Cosmic Person and the Origin of the Sun

BP 1.77.1

नारद उवाच । विस्तरेणानुपूर्व्य च सूर्यं निगदतः शृणु । ततः शेषान् प्रवक्ष्ये ऽहं नमस्कृत्य विवस्वते ॥ ॥

Nārada said. Hear as I describe the Sun in detail and in order. Then, after bowing to the Sun, I shall tell the rest.

BP 1.77.2

अव्यक्तं कारणं यत् तन् नित्यं सदसदात्मकम् । प्रधानं प्रकृतिश् चेति यम् आहुस् तत्त्वचिन्तकाः ॥ ॥

That unmanifest cause, eternal and having the nature of being and nonbeing, is what thinkers call the First Principle and Nature.

BP 1.77.3

गन्धैर् वर्णैर् असैर् हीनं शब्दस्पर्शविवर्जितम् । जगद्योनिं महद्भूतं परं ब्रह्म सनातनम् ॥ ॥

Devoid of smell, color, and taste, free from sound and touch, it is the great being, the womb of the universe, the supreme eternal Brahman.

BP 1.77.4

निग्रहं सर्वभूतानाम् अव्यक्तं अभवत् किल । अनाद्यन्तम् अजं सूक्ष्मं त्रिगुणं प्रभवाप्ययम् ॥ ॥

The unmanifest became the restraint of all beings, they say, without beginning or end, unborn and subtle, threefold in qualities, and the source and dissolution.

BP 1.77.5

अनाकारम् अविज्ञेयं तम् आहुः पुरुषं परम् । तस्यात्मना सर्वम् इदं जगद्व्याप्तं महात्मनः ॥ ॥

They call that formless and unknowable one the supreme Person. This entire universe is pervaded by the Self of that great souled one.

BP 1.77.6

तस्य् एश्वरस्य प्रतिमा ज्ञानवैराग्यलक्षणा । धर्मेश्वर्यकृता बुद्धिर् ब्राह्मी तस्याभिमानिनः ॥ ॥

The form of that Lord is marked by knowledge and detachment. The intellect of that self identifying one, shaped by righteousness and sovereignty, is the intellect of Brahmā.

BP 1.77.7

अव्यक्ताज् जायते तस्य मनसा यद्यद् इच्छति । चतुर्मुखस्य ब्रह्मत्वे कालत्वे चान्तकृद् भवेत् ॥ ॥

From the Unmanifest, whatever he desires in mind is born. In the state of four faced Brahmā he creates, and in the form of Time he becomes the maker of the end.

BP 1.77.8

सहस्रमूर्धा पुरुषस् तिस्रो ऽवस्थाः स्वयम्भुवः । सत्त्वं रजश् च ब्रह्मत्वे कालत्वे च रजस् तमः ॥ ॥

The Person has a thousand heads, and the Self Born has three states. Goodness is in the state of Brahmā, while passion and darkness are in the state of Time.

BP 1.77.9

सात्त्विकं पुरुषत्वे च गुणवृत्तं स्वयंभुवः । ब्रह्मत्वे सृजते लोकान् कालत्वे चापि संक्षिपेत् ॥ ॥

In the state of the Person, goodness is the quality functioning in the Self Born. In Brahmāhood he creates the worlds, and in Time he contracts them.

BP 1.77.10

पुरुषत्वे उदासीनस् तिस्रो ऽवस्थाः प्रजापतेः । त्रिधा विभज्य चात्मानं त्रिकालं सम्प्रवर्तते ॥ ॥

In the state of the Person he is detached. These are the three states of the Lord of creatures. Dividing himself threefold, he sets the three times in motion.

BP 1.77.11

सृजते ग्रसते चैव वीक्षते च त्रिभिः स्वयम् । अग्रे हिरण्यगर्भस् तु प्रादुर्भूतः स्वयम्भुवः ॥ ॥

By himself, through the three states, he creates, devours, and observes. At the beginning, the Golden Embryo, the Self Born, appeared.

BP 1.77.12

आदित्यस्यादिदेवत्वाद् अजातत्वाद् अजः स्मृतः । देवेषु स महान् देवो महादेवः स्मृतस् ततः ॥ ॥

Because he is the first god of the Sun and unborn, he is remembered as the Unborn. Among the gods he is the great god, and therefore is remembered as the Great God.

BP 1.77.13

सर्वेशत्वाच् च लोकस्याधीशत्वाच् च ईश्वरः । बृहत्त्वाच् च स्मृतो ब्रह्मा भवत्वाद् भव उच्यते ॥ ॥

Because he is lord of all and ruler of the world, he is called the Lord. Because he is great, he is remembered as Brahmā, and because he becomes, he is called Being.

BP 1.77.14

पाति यस्मात् प्रजाः सर्वाः प्रजापति रतः स्मृतः । पुरे शेते च वै यस्मात् तस्मात् पुरुष उच्यते ॥ ॥

Because he protects all beings, he is remembered as the Lord of Creatures. Because he lies in the city, he is called the Person.

BP 1.77.15

नोत्पाद्यत्वाद् अपूर्वत्वात् स्वयंभूर् इति विश्रुतः ॥ ॥

Because he cannot be produced and has no prior origin, he is renowned as the Self Born.

BP 1.77.16

हिरण्याण्डगतो यस्माद् ग्रहेशो वै दिवस्पतिः । तस्माद् हिरण्यगर्भो ऽसौ देवदेवो दिवाकरः ॥ ॥

Because he entered the Golden Egg, he is the lord of planets and lord of the day. Therefore he is the Golden Embryo, the god of gods, the Day Maker.

BP 1.77.17

आपो नारा इति प्रोक्ता ऋषिभिस् तत्त्वदर्शिभिः । अयनं तस्य ता आपस् तेन नारायणः स्मृतः ॥ ॥

The seers who perceive truth call the waters Nārā. Since the waters are his resting place, he is remembered as Nārāyaṇa.

BP 1.77.18

अर इत्य् एष शीघ्रार्थो निपातः कविभिः स्मृतः । आप एवार्णवा भूत्वा न शीघ्रास् तेन ता नराः ॥ ॥

The word ara is remembered by poets as meaning swift. Since the waters became oceans and were not swift, they are therefore called Nara.

BP 1.77.19

एकार्णवे पुरा तस्मिन् नष्टे स्थावरजङ्गमे । नारायणाख्यः पुरुषः सुष्वाप सलिले तदा । सहस्रशीर्षा सुमनाः सहस्राक्षः सहस्रपात् ॥ ॥

In that ancient single ocean, when moving and unmoving beings had perished, the Person called Nārāyaṇa slept in the waters. He had a thousand heads, was gracious, thousand eyed, and thousand footed.

BP 1.77.20

सहस्रबाहुः प्रथमः प्रजापतिस् त्रयीपथे यः पुरुषो निगद्यते । आदित्यवर्णो भुवनस्य गोप्ता अपूर्व एकः पुरुषः पुराणः ॥ ॥

Thousand armed, the first Lord of Creatures, he is declared the Person on the path of the three Vedas, sun colored, guardian of the universe, without predecessor, one, and ancient.

BP 1.77.21

हिरण्यगर्भः पुरुषो महात्मा सम्पद्यते वै तमसः परस्तात् ॥ ॥

Beyond darkness, the great souled Golden Embryo, the Person comes into being.
