Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 140 — Origin of the Bhojakas

BP 1.140.1

सुमन्तुरुवाच । एवं स आनयित्वा तु मगान्साम्बो महीपते । स महात्मा पुरा साम्बश्चन्द्रभागासरित्तटे ॥

पञ्चधूपाः प्रदातव्याः सिद्धिरस्येह सर्वदा । दण्डनायकवेले द्वे त्रिसन्ध्यं भास्करस्य तु ॥

“Five incenses are to be offered; its accomplishment is always here. At the time of Daṇḍanāyaka, twice, at the three sandhyās, for Bhāskara.”

Word-for-word
pañca-dhūpāḥ — five incenses
pradātavyāḥ — are to be offered
siddhiḥ — accomplishment
asya — of this
iha — here
sarvadā — always
daṇḍanāyaka-velā — at Daṇḍanāyaka’s time
dve — twice
tri-sandhyam — at the three sandhyās
bhāskarasya — for Bhāskara
tu — indeed
Transliteration
pañcadhūpāḥ pradātavyāḥ siddhirasyeha sarvadā | daṇḍanāyakavele dve trisandhyaṃ bhāskarasya tu || 50 ||
