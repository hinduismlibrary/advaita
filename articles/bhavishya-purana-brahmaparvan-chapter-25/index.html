Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 25 — Signs of the Male Body and Their Results

BP 1.25.1

ब्रह्मोवाच
दक्षिणावर्तलिङ्गश्च नरो वै पुत्रमान्भवेत् ।
वामावर्ते तथा लिङ्गे नरः कन्यां प्रसूयते ॥

Brahmā said: A man whose penis curves to the right will have sons; when the penis curves to the left, a man begets daughters.

BP 1.25.2

स्थूलैः शिरालैर्विषमैर्लिङ्गैर्दारिद्र्यमादिशेत् ।
ऋजुभिर्वर्तुलाकारैः पुरुषा पुत्रभागिनः ॥

One should declare poverty from thick, veined, uneven penises; men with straight, round penises have sons.

BP 1.25.3

निम्नपादोपविष्टस्य भूमिं स्पृशति मेहनः ।
दुःखितं तं विजानीयात्पुरुषं नात्र संशयः ॥

When a man sits with his feet lowered and his penis touches the ground, know him to be sorrowful, no doubt.

BP 1.25.4

भूमौ पादोपविष्टस्य गुल्फौ स्पृशति मेहनः ।
ईश्वरं तं विजानीयात्प्रमदानां च वल्लभम् ॥

When a man sits with his feet on the ground and his penis touches his ankles, know him as a lord and beloved of women.

BP 1.25.5

सिंहव्याघ्रसमो यस्य ह्रस्वौ भवति मेहनः ।
भोगवान्स तु विज्ञेयोऽशेषभोगसमन्वितः ॥

If a man's penis is short, like a lion's or tiger's, he should be known as an enjoyer, endowed with every pleasure.

BP 1.25.6

रेखाकृतिर्मणिर्यस्य मेहने हि विराजते ।
पार्थिवः स तु विज्ञेयः समुद्रवचनं यथा ॥

If a line-shaped jewel shines on a man's penis, he is to be known as a ruler, according to the Ocean's statement.

BP 1.25.7

सुवर्णरजतप्रख्यैर्मणिमुक्तासमप्रभैः ।
प्रवालसदृशैः स्निग्धैर्मणिभिः पार्थिवो भवेत् ॥

A smooth glans like gold or silver, shining like jewels and pearls and resembling coral, makes a man a ruler.

BP 1.25.8

पाण्डुरैर्मलिनै रूक्षैर्दीर्घव्यासैर्दिशो व्रजेत् ।
समैस्तथोन्नतैश्चापि सुस्निग्धैर्मणिभिर्गृही ॥

A pale, dirty, rough, long, broad glans makes a man travel to the directions; an even, raised, very smooth glans marks a householder.

BP 1.25.9

धनरक्षास्तथा स्त्रीणां भोक्तारस्ते भवन्ति हि ।
मणिभिर्मध्यनिम्नैस्तु पितरस्ते भवन्ति हि ॥

They become guardians of wealth and enjoyers of women; those with a glans depressed in the middle become fathers.

BP 1.25.10

युवतीनां महाबाहो निःस्वाश्चापि भवन्ति ते ।
नोल्बणैश्चापि धनिनो नरा वीरा भवन्ति हि ॥

O mighty-armed one, they are also poor among young women; men with non-prominent glans become wealthy and brave.

BP 1.25.11

मूत्रधारा पतेदेका वलिता दक्षिणा यदि ।
स भवेत्पार्थिवः पृथ्व्याः समुद्रवचनं यथा ॥

If urine falls in a single stream, curved to the right, the man becomes a ruler of the earth, according to the Ocean's statement.

BP 1.25.12

द्वे धारे च तथा स्निग्धे धनवान्भोगवान्स्मृतः ।
बहुधारास्तथा रूक्षाः सशब्दाः पुरुषाधमाः ॥

Two smooth streams mark a wealthy man and an enjoyer. Many rough, noisy streams mark the lowest of men.

BP 1.25.13

मीनगन्धि भवेद्रेतो धनवान्पुत्रवान्भवेत् ।
हविगन्धि भवेद्यस्य धनाढ्यः श्रोत्रियः स्मृतः ॥

Semen smelling like fish makes a man wealthy and a father of sons. He whose semen smells like a sacrificial offering is remembered as wealthy and learned in the Veda.

BP 1.25.14

मूषगन्धिर्भवेत्पुत्री पद्मगन्धिर्नृपः स्मृतः ।
लाक्षागन्धिर्भवेद्यश्च बहुकन्यः प्रजायते ॥
मद्यगन्धिर्भवेद्योद्धा क्षारगन्धिर्दरिद्रकः ॥

Mouse-smelling semen begets daughters; lotus-smelling semen marks a king. From lac-scented semen many daughters are born; wine-smelling semen marks a warrior, alkali-smelling semen a poor man.

BP 1.25.15

शीघ्रमैथुनगामी यः स दीर्घायुरतोऽन्यथा ।
अल्पायुर्देवशार्दूल विज्ञेयो नात्र संशयः ॥

He who reaches sexual union quickly lives long; otherwise he is short-lived, O tiger among the gods. There is no doubt.

BP 1.25.16

तनुशुक्रः स्त्रीजनको मांसगन्धी च भोगवान् ।
पद्मवर्णं भवेद्रक्तं स नरो धनवान्भवेत् ॥

Thin semen begets daughters; flesh-smelling semen marks an enjoyer. A man whose blood is lotus-colored becomes wealthy.

BP 1.25.17

किञ्चिद्रक्तं तथा कृष्णं भवेद्यस्य तु शोणितम् ।
अधमः स तु विज्ञेयः सदा दुःखैकभाजनम् ॥

Whose blood is slightly red and also black, he should be known as inferior, always a vessel of sorrow alone.

BP 1.25.18

प्रवालसदृशं स्निग्धं भवेद्यस्य च शोणितम् ।
राजानं तं विजानीयात्सप्तद्वीपाधिपं गुह ॥

Whose blood is smooth and resembles coral, know him as a king, lord of the seven islands, O Guha.

BP 1.25.19

विस्तीर्णा मांसला स्निग्धा वस्तिः पुंसां प्रशस्यते ।
निर्मांसा विकटा रूक्षा बस्तिर्येषां न ते शुभाः ॥

A broad, fleshy, smooth bladder is praised among men. Those whose bladders are fleshless, misshapen, and rough are inauspicious.

BP 1.25.20

गोमायुसदृशी यस्य श्वानोष्ट्रमहिषस्य च ।
स भवेद्दुःखितो नित्यं पुरुषो नात्र संशयः ॥

One whose bladder resembles a jackal's, a dog's, a camel's, or a buffalo's is always miserable, no doubt.

BP 1.25.21

यश्चैकवृषणस्तात जले प्राणान्विमुञ्जति ।
स्त्रीचञ्चलस्तु विषमैः समै राज्यं प्रचक्षते ॥

One who has a single testicle, dear one, gives up his life in water. Unequal testicles mark fickleness toward women; equal ones are said to mark kingship.

BP 1.25.22

ऊर्ध्वगैश्चापि ह्रस्वायुः शतञ्जीवी प्रलम्बधृक् ।
मानवाश्चापि रक्तैस्तु धनवन्तो भवन्ति वै ॥

Testicles that rise upward indicate a short life; one who bears them hanging lives a hundred years. Red ones make men wealthy.

BP 1.25.23

स्थूलस्फिग्भवति क्षेमी द्रव्ययुक्तः समांसधृक् ।
व्याघ्रस्फिङ्मण्डलो राजा मण्डूकस्फिङ्नराधिपः ॥
द्विमण्डलो महाबाहो सिंहस्फिक्सार्वभौमता ॥

A man with large, evenly fleshed buttocks is secure and possesses wealth. Round tiger-like buttocks mark a king, frog-like buttocks a ruler, and two rounded lion-like buttocks universal sovereignty, O mighty-armed one.

BP 1.25.24

उष्ट्रवानरयोर्यस्तु धारयेत्स्फिङ्महामते ।
धनधान्यविहीनोऽसौ विज्ञेयो भीमनन्दन ॥

Whoever bears buttocks like those of a camel or monkey, O great-minded one, should be known as lacking wealth and grain, O delight of Bhīma.

BP 1.25.25

पुमान्मृगोदरो धन्यो मयूरोदर एव च ।
व्याघ्रोदरो नरपती राजा सिंहोदरो भवेत् ॥

A man with a deer's belly is fortunate, and so is one with a peacock's belly. A tiger-bellied man is a ruler; one with a lion's belly becomes a king.

BP 1.25.26

मण्डूकसदृशं यस्य पुरुषस्योदरं भवेत् ।
स भवेत्पार्थिवः पुष्ट्या समुद्रवचनं यथा ॥

A man's belly resembling a frog's makes him a ruler through its fullness, according to the Ocean's statement.

BP 1.25.27

मांसलैर्ऋजुभिर्वृत्तैः पार्श्वैर्नृपतयः स्मृताः ।
ईश्वरो व्याघ्रपृष्ठस्तु सेनायाश्चैव नायकः ॥

Kings are said to have fleshy, straight, rounded sides. One with a tiger's back is a lord and the leader of an army.

BP 1.25.28

सिंहपृष्ठो नरो यस्तु बन्धनं तस्य निर्दिशेत् ।
कूर्मपृष्ठास्तु राजानो धनसौभाग्यभागिनः ॥

For a man with a lion's back, bondage is declared. Kings with tortoise backs partake of wealth and good fortune.

BP 1.25.29

विस्तीर्णं हदयं येषां मांसलोमचितं समम् ।
शतायुषो विजानीयाद्भोगभाजो महाधनान् ॥

Those whose chest is broad, even, fleshy, and covered with hair should be known as living a hundred years, enjoying pleasures, and possessing great wealth.

BP 1.25.30

विरलाः शुष्कास्तथा रूक्षा दृश्यन्तेऽङ्गुलयः करे ।
स भवेद् दुःखितो नित्यं नरो दारिद्र्यभाजनम् ॥

Fingers in the hand that appear widely separated, dry, and rough mark a man who is always sorrowful and an abode of poverty.

BP 1.25.31

यस्य मीनसमा रेखा कर्मसिद्धिभ्र तस्य तु ।
धनवान्स तु विज्ञेयो बहुपुत्रश्च मानवः ॥

He whose palm has a fish-like line carries success in action; he should be known as wealthy and having many sons.

BP 1.25.32

तुला यस्य तु वेदी वा करमध्ये तु दृश्यते ।
तस्य सिध्यति वाणिज्यं पुरुषस्य न संशयः ॥

If a balance or sacrificial altar is seen in the middle of a man's hand, his trade succeeds, no doubt.

BP 1.25.33

सौम्ये पाणितले यस्य द्विजस्य तु विशेषतः ।
यज्ञयाजी भवेन्नित्यं बहुवित्तश्च मानवः ॥

A Brahmin with an auspicious palm continually performs sacrifices and becomes a man of much wealth.

BP 1.25.34

शैलं वाप्यथ वा वृक्षः करमध्ये तु दृश्यते ।
अचलां श्रियमाप्नोति बहुभृत्यसमन्वितः ॥

If a mountain or a tree is seen in the middle of the hand, he attains unshakable prosperity, accompanied by many servants.

BP 1.25.35

शक्तितोमरबाणासिरेखा चापोपमा तथा ।
यस्य हस्ते महाबाहो स जयेद्विग्रहे रिपून् ॥

O mighty-armed one, a man whose hand has lines shaped like a spear, javelin, arrow, sword, or bow conquers enemies in battle.

BP 1.25.36

ध्वजभ्राप्यथ वा शंखः करमध्ये तु दृश्यते ।
समुद्रयायी स भवेद्धनी च सततं गुह ॥

If a banner, a bee, or a conch is seen in the middle of the hand, he becomes a sea-traveler and is always wealthy, O Guha.

BP 1.25.37

श्रीवत्समथ वा पद्मं वज्रं वा चकमेव च ।
रथो वाप्यथ वा कुम्भो यस्य हस्ते प्रकाशते ॥
राजानं तं विजानीयात्परसैन्यविदारणम् ॥

One whose hand displays Śrīvatsa, a lotus, a thunderbolt, a wheel, a chariot, or a pot should be known as a king who breaks enemy armies.

BP 1.25.38

दक्षिणे तु कराङ्गुष्ठे यवो यस्य तु दृश्यते ।
सर्वविद्याप्रवक्ता च भवेद्वै नात्र संशयः ॥

If a barley grain is seen on the right thumb, he becomes a speaker on all branches of knowledge, no doubt.

BP 1.25.39

यस्य पाणितले रेखा कनिष्ठामूलमुत्थिता ।
गता मध्यं प्रदेशिन्याः स जीवेच्छरदः शतम् ॥

A man whose palm has a line rising from the root of the little finger to the middle of the index finger lives a hundred autumns.
