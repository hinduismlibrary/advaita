VP 4.3.1

व्रतश्च मांधातुः पुत्रसंततिरभिधीयते

Atah cha maandaatuh putra santatih abhidheeyate | Maandhaatru's putra santati is going to be told now.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vrataśca māṃdhātuḥ putrasaṃtatirabhidhīyate

Śrīdhara

tṛtīye saurabheḥ siddhirmāndhādṛtanayānvayaḥ . varṇyate sagarasyāpi caritaṃ ripughātinaḥ . paripakvā rāgādihīnā manovṛttiryasya saḥ bhikṣuryatirabhavat || bhagavatyaśeṣakarmakalāpamāsajya samarpya acyutapadāmavāpeti sambandhaḥ . paravatāṃ param . indriyebhyaḥ parā hyārthā arthebhyaśca paraṃ manaḥ . manāstu parā buddhirbuddherātmā mahān paraḥ . mahataḥ paramavyaktamavyaktāt puruṣaḥ paraḥ puraṣānna paraṃ kiñcit sā kāṣṭhā sā parā gatiḥ . ityuktyā paravatāmindriyādīnāṃ paramāntaramity arthaḥ || rājavaṃśaprastāve uktasaubharicaritasyāsaṅgatim apākaroti—ityetad iti || ataḥ ataḥ param ||१||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तृतीये सौरभेः सिद्धिर्मान्धादृतनयान्वयः । वर्ण्यते सगरस्यापि चरितं रिपुघातिनः । परिपक्वा रागादिहीना मनोवृत्तिर्यस्य सः भिक्षुर्यतिरभवत् ॥ भगवत्यशेषकर्मकलापमासज्य समर्प्य अच्युतपदामवापेति सम्बन्धः । परवतां परम् । इन्द्रियेभ्यः परा ह्यार्था अर्थेभ्यश्च परं मनः । मनास्तु परा बुद्धिर्बुद्धेरात्मा महान् परः । महतः परमव्यक्तमव्यक्तात् पुरुषः परः पुरषान्न परं किञ्चित् सा काष्ठा सा परा गतिः । इत्युक्त्या परवतामिन्द्रियादीनां परमान्तरमित्य् अर्थः ॥ राजवंशप्रस्तावे उक्तसौभरिचरितस्यासङ्गतिम् अपाकरोति—इत्येतद् इति ॥ अतः अतः परम् ॥१॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Atah cha maandaatuh putra santatih abhidheeyate | Maandhaatru's putra santati is going to be told now.

VP 4.3.2

अंबरीषस्य मांधातृतनयस्य युवनाश्वः पुत्रोऽभूत्

Ambareeshasya maandhaatru tanayasya yuvanaashvah putro abhoot | Maandhaatru's son Ambareesha had a son by name Yuvanaashva.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

aṃbarīṣasya māṃdhātṛtanayasya yuvanāśvaḥ putro'bhūt

Śrīdhara

ambarīṣasya yuvanāśvaḥ prapitāmahasanāmā yato hāritāddhāritā aṅgiraso dvijā haritagotrapravarāḥ . atha haritānāmārṣeya āṅgirasāmbarīṣa-yuvanāśvāśceti pravarapāṭhāt ||२||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अम्बरीषस्य युवनाश्वः प्रपितामहसनामा यतो हारिताद्धारिता अङ्गिरसो द्विजा हरितगोत्रप्रवराः । अथ हरितानामार्षेय आङ्गिरसाम्बरीष-युवनाश्वाश्चेति प्रवरपाठात् ॥२॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Ambareeshasya maandhaatru tanayasya yuvanaashvah putro abhoot | Maandhaatru's son Ambareesha had a son by name Yuvanaashva.

VP 4.3.4

रसातले मौनेया नाम गंधर्वा बभूवुष्षट्कोटिसंख्यातास्तैरशेषाणि नागकुलान्यपहृतप्रधानरत्नाधिपत्यान्यक्रियंत

Rasaatale mouneyaa naama gandharvaa babhoovuh shatkoti sankhyaataah taih asheshaani naaga kulaani apahruta pradhaana ratna adhipatnyaani akriyanta | In Rasaatala, there were Gandharvaas called Mouneyaas. They were 6 crores in number. They stole all the Naaga kulaa's ratnaas and also their rulership.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

rasātale mauneyā nāma gaṃdharvā babhūvuṣṣaṭkoṭisaṃkhyātāstairaśeṣāṇi nāgakulānyapahṛtapradhānaratnādhipatyānyakriyaṃta

Śrīdhara

idānī māndhātṛtanayasya purukutsasya vaṃśaṃ vaktuṃ prastauti—rasātala ity-ādinā varaṃ dadur ityantena . muneḥ kaśyapapatnayāḥ putrāścitrasenādyāḥ . apahṛtāni pradhānaratnāni ādhipatyaṃ ca yeṣāṃ tānyakriyanta ||४||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

इदानी मान्धातृतनयस्य पुरुकुत्सस्य वंशं वक्तुं प्रस्तौति—रसातल इत्य्-आदिना वरं ददुर् इत्यन्तेन । मुनेः कश्यपपत्नयाः पुत्राश्चित्रसेनाद्याः । अपहृतानि प्रधानरत्नानि आधिपत्यं च येषां तान्यक्रियन्त ॥४॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Rasaatale mouneyaa naama gandharvaa babhoovuh shatkoti sankhyaataah taih asheshaani naaga kulaani apahruta pradhaana ratna adhipatnyaani akriyanta | In Rasaatala, there were Gandharvaas called Mouneyaas. They were 6 crores in number. They stole all the Naaga kulaa's ratnaas and also their rulership.

VP 4.3.5

तैश्च गंधर्ववीर्यावधूतैरुरगेश्वरैः तूयमानो भगवानशेषदेवेशः तवश्रवणोन्मीलितोन्निद्रपुंडरीकनयनो जलशयनो निद्रावसानप्रबुद्धः प्रणिपत्याभिहितः, भगवन्नस्माकमेतेभ्यो गंधर्वेभ्यो भयमुत्पन्नं कथमुपशममेष्यतीति

Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4 Taih cha gandharva veerya avadhootaih urageshvaraih stooyamaano bhagavaan ashesha deveshah stava shravana unmeelita unnidra pundareeka nayanah nidraa avasaana prabuddhah panipatya abhihitah, bhagavan asmaakam etebhyo gandharvebhyo bhayam utpannam katham upashamam eshyati iti | The serpent lords were insulted by the Gandharva veeraas. Bhagavaan, the Lord of all the Gods, is praised, and having heard the stuti, He opened his eyes which were like the just bloomed lotus. He was sleeping in the Ksheera saagara. He woke up at the end of His yoga nidraa. They paid obeisance to Bhagavaan Vishnu, and all the serpent lords prayed to Him like this - Now, we have got this fear due to these Gandharvaas. How can we get rid of this fear from the Gandharvaas. They seek His help. Bhagavaan is stava priya.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

taiśca gaṃdharvavīryāvadhūtairurageśvaraiḥ tūyamāno bhagavānaśeṣadeveśaḥ tavaśravaṇonmīlitonnidrapuṃḍarīkanayano jalaśayano nidrāvasānaprabuddhaḥ praṇipatyābhihitaḥ, bhagavannasmākametebhyo gaṃdharvebhyo bhayamutpannaṃ kathamupaśamameṣyatīti

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.3.6

आह च भगवाननादिनिधनः पुरुषोत्तमो योऽसौ यौवनाश्वस्य मांधातुः पुरुकुत्सनामा पुत्रस्तमहमनुप्रविश्य तानशेषान् दुष्टगंधर्वानुपशमं नयिष्यामीति

Aaha cha bhagavaan anaadi nidhanah purushottamo yo asou youvanaashvasya maandhaatuh purukutsa naamaa putrah tam aham anupravishya taan asheshaan dushta gandharvaan upashamam nayishyaami iti | Bhagavaan, One who is without a beginning or end, says - Maandhaatu's son Youvanaashva, his son Purukutsa, I will enter into him, and kill all the bad gandharvaas. Don't worry, he gives the assurance.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

āha ca bhagavānanādinidhanaḥ puruṣottamo yo'sau yauvanāśvasya māṃdhātuḥ purukutsanāmā putrastamahamanupraviśya tānaśeṣān duṣṭagaṃdharvānupaśamaṃ nayiṣyāmīti

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.3.7

तदाकर्ण्य भगवते जलशायिने कृतप्रणामाः पुनर्नागलोकमागताः पन्नगाधिपतयो नर्मदां च पुरुकुत्सानयनाय चोदयामासुः

Tat aakarnya bhagavate jalashaayine kruta pranaamaah ounah naaga lokam aagataah pannaga adhipatayo narmadaam cha purukutsa aanayanaaya chodayaamaasuh | Having heard this, all the serpent lords pay obeisance to Bhagavaan who is lying down on the Ksheera Samudra, they came back to the Naaga Loka, and they requested Narmadaa to go and bring Purukutsa there.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tadākarṇya bhagavate jalaśāyine kṛtapraṇāmāḥ punarnāgalokamāgatāḥ pannagādhipatayo narmadāṃ ca purukutsānayanāya codayāmāsuḥ

Śrīdhara

narmadāṃ svabhaginīṃ purukutsasya bhāryām . nārmadā bhātṛbhirdattā purukutsāya yoragaiḥ . tayā rasātalaṃ nīto bhujagendraprayuktvā . iti śukokteḥ ||७||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

नर्मदां स्वभगिनीं पुरुकुत्सस्य भार्याम् । नार्मदा भातृभिर्दत्ता पुरुकुत्साय योरगैः । तया रसातलं नीतो भुजगेन्द्रप्रयुक्त्वा । इति शुकोक्तेः ॥७॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Tat aakarnya bhagavate jalashaayine kruta pranaamaah ounah naaga lokam aagataah pannaga adhipatayo narmadaam cha purukutsa aanayanaaya chodayaamaasuh | Having heard this, all the serpent lords pay obeisance to Bhagavaan who is lying down on the Ksheera Samudra, they came back to the Naaga Loka, and they requested Narmadaa to go and bring Purukutsa there.

VP 4.3.11

यस्तेऽनुस्मरणसमवेतं नामग्रहणं करिष्यति न तस्य सर्पविषभयं भविष्यतीति

Sakala pannaga adhipatayah cha narmadaayai varam daduh, yah te anusmarana samavetam naamagrahanam karishyati na tasya sarpa visha bhayam bhavishyati iti | Then all the serpent lords gave a vara to Narmadaa. VP 4.3.14

इत्युच्चार्याहर्निशमंधकारप्रवेशे वा सर्पैर्न दश्यते, न चापि कृतानुस्मरणभुजो विषमपि भुक्तमुपघाताय भवति

Even if they think of this Narmadaa and tell this shloka, even if they eat food mixed with poison, they will not get any harm because of this.

VP 4.3.20

ततश्च सुमनास्तस्यापि त्रिधन्वा त्रिधन्वनस्त्रय्यारुणिः

Tatah cha vasumanaah tasya api tridhanvaa tridhanvanah trayyaarunih | Hasta had a son by name. He had a son by name Tridhanvaa. He had a son by name Trayyaaruni.

Transliteration

VP 4.3.47

यवनान्मुंडितशिरसोऽर्द्धमुंडिताञ्च्छकान् प्रलंबकेशान् पारदान् पप्लवाञ्श्मश्रुधरान् निस्स्वाध्यायवषट्कारानेतानन्यांश्च क्षत्रियांश्चकार

Yavanaan mundita shiraso ardha munditaan shakaan pralamba keshaan paaradaan paplavaan cha shmashhrudharaan nissvaadhyaaya vashat kaaraan etaan anyaan cha kshatriyaan chakaara | For Yavanaas, he shaved their head. For Shakaas, he made them with half Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4 shaven head. Paradaas had long hair. Paplavaas were made to wear the beard and moustache. All of them and others also, he made them without any study of Vedaas, vashat kaara, and such things.

VP 4.3.49

सगरोऽपि स्वमधिष्ठानमागम्य अस्खलितचक्रस्सप्तद्वीपवतीमिमामुर्वीं प्रशशास

Sagaro api svam adhisthaanam aagamya askhalita chakrah sapta dveepavateem iva urveem prashasaasa Sagara came back to his place, and without any obstructions, problems, he ruled over all the Sapta dveepa, the whole world containing Sapta dveepaas, he had an unopposed rule. He had the rulership of this entire bhoo mandala, the Sapta dveepaas, having got back to his place. This is the story of Sagara Chakravarti. Sagara iti Chakram aajnyaa, balam vaa This concludes the Chapter 3. Sagara's vamsha is told next. Iti Sri Vishnu Puraane Chaturthe Amshe Truteeyo Adhyaayah Iti Sri Vishnu Puraana Vyaakhyaane Vishnu Chitteeye Truteeyo Adhyaayah Atha Chaturtho Adhyaayah Mitrasaha and Khatvaanga, Story of Raama Avataara We just concluded the Chapter 3 of Amsha 4 of the Vishnu Puraana. We will start with Chapter 4 now, in which the stories of Sagara, his vamsha, and how Bhageeratha brings Gangaa to earth, Kalmaashapaadaa's story, Khatvaanga, story of Iskvaaku vamsha will be told, and also Sri Raamachandra's story will come. Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4
