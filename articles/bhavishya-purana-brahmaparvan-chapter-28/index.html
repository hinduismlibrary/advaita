Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 28 — Traditional Marks of Women

BP 1.28.1

ब्रह्मोवाच
शृण्विदानीं महाबाहो स्त्रीलक्षणमनुत्तमम् ।
यन्मयोक्तं पुरा वीर नारदस्य महात्मनः ॥

Brahmā said: Mighty-armed one, now hear the unsurpassed marks of women that I formerly explained to the great-souled Nārada.

Word-for-word
ब्रह्मोवाच (brahmovāca) — Brahmā said;
शृणु इदानीम् (śṛṇu idānīm) — hear now;
स्त्रीलक्षणम् अनुत्तमम् (strī-lakṣaṇam anuttamam) — unsurpassed marks of women;
मया उक्तम् पुरा (mayā uktam purā) — formerly explained by me;
नारदस्य महात्मनः (nāradasya mahātmanaḥ) — to great-souled Nārada
Transliteration
brahmovāca
śṛṇvidānīṃ mahābāho strīlakṣaṇamanuttamam |
yanmayoktaṃ purā vīra nāradasya mahātmanaḥ | | 1

BP 1.28.2

तत्त्वं विज्ञायते येन शुभाशुभमवस्थितम् ।
निन्दितं च प्रशस्तं च स्त्रीणां वक्ष्यामि लक्षणम् ॥

By these the truth of what is auspicious or inauspicious is known. I shall explain the censured and praised marks of women.

Word-for-word
तत्त्वम् विज्ञायते (tattvam vijñāyate) — truth is known;
येन (yena) — by which;
शुभाशुभम् अवस्थितम् (śubha-aśubham avasthitam) — established auspicious and inauspicious;
निन्दितम् च प्रशस्तम् च (ninditam ca praśastam ca) — censured and praised;
स्त्रीणाम् लक्षणम् वक्ष्यामि (strīṇām lakṣaṇam vakṣyāmi) — I shall explain women’s marks
Transliteration
tattvaṃ vijñāyate yena śubhāśubhamavasthitam |
ninditaṃ ca praśastaṃ ca strīṇāṃ vakṣyāmi lakṣaṇam | | 2

BP 1.28.3

मातरं पितरं चैव भ्रातरं मातुलं तथा ।
द्वौ तु बिम्बौ परीक्षेत समुद्रस्य वचो यथा ॥

One should examine her mother, father, brother, and maternal uncle, and the two family lines, according to the Oceans teaching.

Word-for-word
मातरम् पितरम् च (mātaram pitaram ca) — mother and father;
भ्रातरम् मातुलम् (bhrātaram mātulam) — brother and maternal uncle;
द्वौ बिम्बौ परीक्षेत (dvau bimbau parīkṣeta) — should examine the two lines;
समुद्रस्य वचः यथा (samudrasya vacaḥ yathā) — according to the Ocean’s teaching
Transliteration
mātaraṃ pitaraṃ caiva bhrātaraṃ mātulaṃ tathā |
dvau tu bimbau parīkṣeta samudrasya vaco yathā | | 3

BP 1.28.4

मुहूर्ते तिथिसम्पन्ने नक्षत्रे चाभिपूजिते ।
द्विजैस्तु सह वागम्य कन्यां वीक्षेत शास्त्रवित् ॥

At an auspicious moment furnished with a proper lunar date and honoured constellation, one learned in scripture should approach with brāhmaṇas and inspect the maiden.

Word-for-word
मुहूर्ते तिथिसम्पन्ने (muhūrte tithi-sampanne) — at a moment with proper lunar date;
नक्षत्रे अभिपूजिते (nakṣatre abhipūjite) — under honoured constellation;
द्विजैः सह आगम्य (dvijaiḥ saha āgamya) — approaching with brāhmaṇas;
कन्याम् वीक्षेत (kanyām vīkṣeta) — should inspect maiden;
शास्त्रवित् (śāstra-vit) — scripture-knower
Transliteration
muhūrte tithisampanne nakṣatre cābhipūjite |
dvijaistu saha vāgamya kanyāṃ vīkṣeta śāstravit | | 4

BP 1.28.5

हस्तौ पादौ परीक्षेत अङ्गुलीर्नखमेव च ।
पाणिमेव च जङ्घे च कटिनासोरु एव च ॥

He should examine the hands, feet, fingers, nails, palms, shanks, waist, nose, and thighs.

Word-for-word
हस्तौ पादौ परीक्षेत (hastau pādau parīkṣeta) — examine hands and feet;
अङ्गुलीः नखम् (aṅgulīḥ nakham) — fingers and nails;
पाणिम् (pāṇim) — palm;
जङ्घे (jaṅghe) — shanks;
कटिनासोरु (kaṭi-nāsa-ūru) — waist nose and thighs
Transliteration
hastau pādau parīkṣeta aṅgulīrnakhameva ca |
1pāṇimeva ca jaṅghe ca kaṭināsoru eva ca | | 5

BP 1.28.6

जघनोदरपृष्ठं च स्तनौ कर्णौ भुजौ तथा ।
जिह्वां चोष्ठौ च दन्ताश्च कपोलं गलकं तथा ॥

He should also examine the hips, abdomen, back, breasts, ears, arms, tongue, lips, teeth, cheeks, and throat.

Word-for-word
जघन-उदर-पृष्ठम् (jaghana-udara-pṛṣṭham) — hips abdomen and back;
स्तनौ कर्णौ भुजौ (stanau karṇau bhujau) — breasts ears and arms;
जिह्वाम् ओष्ठौ दन्तान् (jihvām oṣṭhau dantān) — tongue lips and teeth;
कपोलम् गलकम् (kapolam galakam) — cheeks and throat
Transliteration
jaghanodarapṛṣṭhaṃ ca stanau karṇau bhujau tathā |

BP 1.28.7

चक्षुर्नासा ललाटं च शिरः केशांस्तथैव च ।
रोमराजिं स्वरं वर्णमावर्तानि तु वा पुनः ॥

And the eyes, nose, forehead, head, hair, line of body hair, voice, complexion, and bodily whorls.

Word-for-word
चक्षुः नासा ललाटम् (cakṣuḥ nāsā lalāṭam) — eyes nose forehead;
शिरः केशान् (śiraḥ keśān) — head and hair;
रोमराजिम् (roma-rājim) — line of body hair;
स्वरम् वर्णम् (svaram varṇam) — voice and complexion;
आवर्तानि (āvartāni) — bodily whorls
Transliteration
cakṣurnāsā lalāṭaṃ ca śiraḥ keśāṃstathaiva ca |
romarājiṃ1 svaraṃ varṇamāvartāni tu vā punaḥ | | 7

BP 1.28.8

यस्यास्तु रेखाग्रीवायां या च रक्तान्तलोचना ।
यस्य सा गृहमागच्छेत्तद्गृहं सुखमेधते ॥

A house prospers in happiness when entered by a woman who has lines upon her neck and eyes red at their corners.

Word-for-word
यस्याः रेखा ग्रीवायाम् (yasyāḥ rekhā grīvāyām) — whose neck has lines;
रक्तान्तलोचना (rakta-anta-locanā) — eyes red at corners;
सा गृहम् आगच्छेत् (sā gṛham āgacchet) — she enters a house;
तत् गृहम् सुखम् एधते (tat gṛham sukham edhate) — that house prospers happily
Transliteration
yasyāstu rekhāgrīvāyāṃ yā2 ca raktāntalocanā |
yasya sā gṛhamāgacchettadgṛhaṃ sukhamedhate | | 8

BP 1.28.9

ललाटे दृश्यते यस्यास्त्रिशूलं देवनिर्मितम् ।
बहूनां स्त्रीसहस्राणां स्वामिनीं तां विनिर्दिशेत् ॥

One upon whose forehead a divinely formed trident is seen should be identified as mistress of many thousands of women.

Word-for-word

त्रिशूलम् देवनिर्मितम् (triśūlam deva-nirmitam) — divinely formed trident;
बहूनाम् स्त्रीसहस्राणाम् (bahūnām strī-sahasrāṇām) — of many thousands of women;
स्वामिनीम् ताम् विनिर्दिशेत् (svāminīm tām vinirdiśet) — identify her as mistress
Transliteration
lalāṭe dṛśyate yasyāstriśūlaṃ devanirmitam |

BP 1.28.10

राजहंसगतिर्यस्या मृगाक्षी मृगवर्णिका ।
समशुक्लाग्रदन्ता च कन्यां तामुत्तमां विदुः ॥

The wise know as excellent a maiden whose gait is like a royal haṃsa, whose eyes and complexion resemble a deers, and whose teeth are even, white, and fine-tipped.

Word-for-word
राजहंसगतिः (rāja-haṃsa-gatiḥ) — gait like royal haṃsa;
मृगाक्षी (mṛgākṣī) — deer-eyed;
मृगवर्णिका (mṛga-varṇikā) — deer-complexioned;
समशुक्लाग्रदन्ता (sama-śukla-agra-dantā) — even white fine-tipped teeth;
कन्याम् उत्तमाम् विदुः (kanyām uttamām viduḥ) — know maiden as excellent
Transliteration
rājahaṃsagatiryasyā mṛgākṣī mṛgavarṇikā |
samaśuklāgradantā ca kanyāṃ tāmuttamāṃ viduḥ | | 1.28.10

BP 1.28.11

मण्डूककुक्षी या कन्या न्यग्रोधपरिमण्डला ।
एकं जनयते पुत्रं सोऽपि राजा भविष्यति ॥

A maiden with a frog-like abdomen and a banyan-like rounded form bears one son, and he will become a king.

सः अपि राजा भविष्यति (saḥ api rājā bhaviṣyati) — he will become king

BP 1.28.13

आयतौ श्रवणौ यस्याः सुरूपा चापि नासिका ।

BP 1.28.14
