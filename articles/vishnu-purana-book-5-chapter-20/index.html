VP 5.20.1

राज-मार्गे ततः कृष्णः सानुलेपन-भाजनाम् ।
ददर्श कुब्जाम् आयान्तीं नव-यौवन-गोचराम् ॥१॥

Then, on the royal road, Kṛṣṇa saw Kubjā coming, carrying a vessel of unguent and in the bloom of youth.

Śrīdhara translation

“On the royal road” is explained. “Carrying a vessel of unguent” means Kubjā, accompanied by a vessel for unguent.

VP 5.20.5

VP 5.20.8

भक्ति-च्छेदानुलिप्ताङ्गौ ततस् तौ पुरुषर्षभौ ।

Then those two best of men, their limbs anointed with ornamental designs, shone like white and dark clouds accompanied by rainbows.

Śrīdhara translation

“With ornamental designs.” The design of leaf-patterns on the cheeks, chest, arms, and other parts, in colors such as saffron, is called ornamental marking. Their limbs were anointed with such designs; therefore, through the colored unguent and the radiance of their bodies, the two resembled white and dark clouds accompanied by rainbows.

मायाविमोहितेन मया त्वय्य् एतावन्तं कालं पुत्रबुद्धिः कृता, अधुना तु सा बुद्धिर् मिथ्यैवेत्य् आह—मायाविमोहितदृशेति ॥१०४॥

VP 5.20.105

कर्माणि रुद्र-मरुद्-अश्वि-शतक्रतूनां
साध्यानि यानि न भवन्ति निरीक्षितानि ।
त्वं विष्णुर् ईशजगतामुपकार-हेतोः
प्राप्तोऽसि नः परिगतो विगतो हि मोहः ॥१०५॥

“Your deeds are not even seen by Rudra, the Maruts, the Aśvins, Śatakratu, and the Sādhyas. You, Viṣṇu, Lord of the worlds, have come for the world’s welfare, and have come to us; my delusion has indeed vanished.”

Śrīdhara translation

The reason for the absence of possessiveness is given. Your deeds are not even possible for Rudra and the others, nor are they seen directly, because your deeds are inconceivable and wondrous. Or, “seen” means directly perceived. Therefore you are Viṣṇu himself, come to us as a son for the welfare of the worlds; this has been rightly understood. Thus delusion has vanished.
