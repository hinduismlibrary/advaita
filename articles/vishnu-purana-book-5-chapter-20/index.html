VP 5.20.1

राजमार्गे ततः कृष्णःसानुलेपनभाजनाम् ।
ददर्श कुब्जामायान्तीं नवयौवनगोचराम्

Sri Paraasharar - Krishna comes back to the Raajamaarga, the path which leads to the palace, and sees a young woman, who is coming there, who has a crooked body, and humpback, Kubjaa, and she is carrying all kinds of fragrant paste to be smeared on the body.

VP 5.20.3

सकामेनेव सा प्रोक्ता सानुरागा हरिं प्रति ।
प्राह सा ललितं कुब्जा तद्दर्शनबलात्कृता

That Kubjaa, when Krishna addressed her like that, she, full of love towards Him, told Him with great love, because of the sight that she had, seeing Krishna directly there.

VP 5.20.4

कान्त कस्मान्न जानासि कंसेन विनियोजिताम् ।
नैकवक्रेति विख्यातामनुलेपनकर्मणि

O dear, who don't you know me as Naikavaktraa, which is my name, and I am being deployed by Kamsa and I prepare this paste for him everyday and take it to him. All these fragrant pastes that I am grinding and bringing, are for Kamsa, to be applied on his body.

Transliteration (IAST)

कान्तेति । अनुलेपन-कर्मणि गन्ध-वस्तु-साधने कंसेन नियुक्ताम् ॥४॥

He does not like the fragrant pastes which are smeared on the body, to be ground by anyone else. When I go and deliver this to him, and serve him, he will be very pleased and will grant me all kinds of rewards.

VP 5.20.8

भक्तिच्छेदानुलिप्ताङ्गौ ततस्तौ पुरुषर्षभौ ।
सेंद्रचापो व्यराजेतां सितकृष्णाविवांबुदौ

She had a style of smearing, where the paste is smeared in such a way that all the Vaishnava chihnaas are marked on the body, like on the forehead, on the arms - this is called Patra rachanaa vidhi, or Bhakticcheda. She decorated Them with all these things, which were most fragrant, with the wonderful paste which she was carrying for Kamsa. With this, Balaraama and Krishna were radiant like two clouds - white and black clouds, which were decorated by the Indradhanush, the multi coloured bow of Indra.

Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bhakticchedānuliptāṅgau tatastau puruṣarṣabhau | seṃdracāpo vyarājetāṃ sitakṛṣṇāvivāṃbudau

Śrīdhara

bhakti-cchedeti | kuṅkumādi-varṇaiḥ kapola-vakṣasthala-bhujādiṣu patra-bhaṅga-racanā-bhakti-cchedaḥ | bhakti-ccheda-vidhinānuliptāṅgau, ata eva citra-varṇānulepanena svāṅga-kāntyā ca tayor indracāpa-sahita-sita-kṛṣṇāmbuda-sādṛśyam || 8 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

भक्ति-च्छेदेति । कुङ्कुमादि-वर्णैः कपोल-वक्षस्थल-भुजादिषु पत्र-भङ्ग-रचना-भक्ति-च्छेदः । भक्ति-च्छेद-विधिनानुलिप्ताङ्गौ, अत एव चित्र-वर्णानुलेपनेन स्वाङ्ग-कान्त्या च तयोर् इन्द्रचाप-सहित-सित-कृष्णाम्बुद-सादृश्यम् ॥८॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

No separate English commentary is supplied.

VP 5.20.9

ततस्तां चुबुके शौरिरुल्लाधनविधानवित् ।
उत्पाट्य तोलयामास व्द्यङ्गुलेनाग्रपाणिना

Then, Krishna, who was an expert in treating such people, with such deformities, about how to straighten them. He placed two fingers under her chin, and pulled her up.

Transliteration (IAST)

anulepanārpaṇa-phalaṃ sadya eva darśitam ity āha—tata ity-ādi-sārdhena | tata iti | ullāpanam ṛjūkaraṇam | pāṭhāntare ullāghanam arogīkaraṇaṃ tat-prakārajñaḥ śauriḥ madhyama-tarjanī-rūpa-dvy-aṅguly-upalakṣitena agra-pāṇinā pāṇy-agreṇa cubue mukhasyādhastād utpāṭyoddhṛtya tolayāmāsa uccālitavān || 9 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अनुलेपनार्पण-फलं सद्य एव दर्शितम् इत्य् आह—तत इत्य्-आदि-सार्धेन । तत इति । उल्लापनम् ऋजूकरणम् । पाठान्तरे उल्लाघनम् अरोगीकरणं तत्-प्रकारज्ञः शौरिः मध्यम-तर्जनी-रूप-द्व्य्-अङ्गुल्य्-उपलक्षितेन अग्र-पाणिना पाण्य्-अग्रेण चुबुए मुखस्याधस्ताद् उत्पाट्योद्धृत्य तोलयामास उच्चालितवान् ॥९॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

No separate English commentary is supplied.

VP 5.20.10

चकर्ष पद्भ्यां च तदा ऋजुत्वं केशवोऽनयत् ।
ततःसा ऋजुतां प्राप्ता योषितामभवद्वरा

Pressing her feet with His foot, and two fingers under her chin, He pulled her up, such that all her deformity vanished and she became straight. All her deformity immediately went away. Having lost all her deformity, she became a beautiful woman.

Śrīdhara commentary.

No separate English commentary is supplied.

VP 5.20.11

Immediately, she was filled with great love for Krishna, and told with very sweet voice, and with great love, held Krishna's clothes and said "Please do come to my house", she pleaded.

Transliteration (IAST)

Śrīdhara commentary.

Śrīdhara commentary.

VP 5.20.15

VP 5.20.18

Kamsa came to know that Akroora has reached Mathura, and also heard that loud sound of that bow breaking, and immediately called Chaanoora and Mushtika, and told them thus. Having heard that Akroora has come, and also having heard the noise of the huge bow breaking, Kamsa immediately calls Chaanoora and Mushtika, and gives them orders. We next see what Kamsa told Chaanoora and Mushtika.
