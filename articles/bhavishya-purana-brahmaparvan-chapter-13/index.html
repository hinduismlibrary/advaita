Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 13 — Household Conduct and Family Reputation

BP 1.13.1

ब्रह्मोवाच
प्रथमं प्रतिबुध्येत प्रवर्तेत स्वकर्मसु ।
पश्चाद्भृत्यजनस्यापि भुञ्जीत च शयीत च ॥

Brahmā said: ‘She should be the first to awaken and begin her own duties; only after attending to the servants and other household members should she eat and sleep.

BP 1.13.2

भर्त्रा विरहिता स्त्री च श्वशुराभ्यां विशेषतः ।
देहलीं नातिवर्तेत प्रतीकारे महत्यपि ॥

A woman separated from her husband, especially while with her parents-in-law, should not cross the threshold, even in a great emergency.

BP 1.13.3

उत्थाय प्रथमं भर्तुरविज्ञाता न निष्क्रमेत् ।
क्षपायां सावशेषायां रात्रौ वा वासरादिषु ॥

After rising first, she should not leave without her husband’s knowledge while any part of the night remains, during the night itself, or at the beginning of the day.

BP 1.13.4

तद्वासभवनस्यैव शनैराहूय कार्मिकान् ।
स्वव्यापारेषु तान्सर्वांस्तत्रतत्र नियोजयेत् ॥

From within that residence she should quietly summon the workers and assign every one of them to the appropriate place for his or her particular occupation.

BP 1.13.5

विबुद्धस्य ततो भर्तुर्निर्वर्त्यावश्यकं विधिम् ।
गृहकार्याणि सर्वाणि विदधीताप्रमादतः ॥

Once her husband has awakened and the necessary morning observances have been completed, she should arrange all household duties without negligence.

BP 1.13.6

मुक्त्वावासकनेपथ्यं कर्मयोग्यं विधाय च ।
तत्कालोचितकर्तव्यमनुतिष्ठेद्यथाक्रमम् ॥

Having put aside the clothing and adornment used for rest and made herself ready for work, she should perform in due order whatever duties are appropriate to that time.

BP 1.13.7

महानसं सुसम्मृष्टं चुल्ल्यादिविहितार्चनम् ।
सर्वोपकरणोपेतमसम्बाधमनाविलम् ॥

The kitchen should be thoroughly swept, the prescribed reverence paid to the hearth and related places, and the room kept fully equipped, spacious, and free from impurity.

BP 1.13.8

न चातिगुह्यं प्रकटं प्रविभक्तक्रियाश्रयम् ।
भर्तुराप्तजनाकीर्णं गूढं कक्षादिवर्जितम् ॥

It should be neither excessively concealed nor exposed, but divided into appropriate work areas, occupied by people trusted by the husband, secure, and free from unnecessary private recesses.

BP 1.13.9

तत्र पाकादिभाण्डानि बहिरन्तश्च कारयेत् ।
निणिक्तमलपङ्कानि शुक्तिवल्कादिचूर्णकैः ॥

Cooking vessels and other utensils, both inside and outside, should be cleaned of grease, dirt, and mud with powders made from shell, bark, and similar materials.

BP 1.13.10

निशि कुर्वीत धूमार्चिः शोधितानि दिवातपः ।
दधिपात्राणि कुर्वीत सदैवान्तरितानि च ॥

At night the utensils should be purified by smoke and flame, and by day with sunlight. Vessels used for curds should always be kept separate.

BP 1.13.11

साधुकारितदुग्धेषु शोधितेषु दिवातपे ।
ईषद्गृह्योक्तपात्रेषु स्वच्छं येन भवेद्दधि ॥

Curds become clear and wholesome when placed in suitable vessels containing properly prepared milk, the vessels having been purified by sunlight and treated as prescribed in domestic practice.

BP 1.13.12

स्नेहगोरसपाकादि कृत्वा सुप्रत्ययेक्षितम् ।
कुर्यात्स्वयमधिष्ठाय भर्तुः पाकविधिक्रियाम् ॥

After ensuring trustworthy oversight of oils, dairy products, cooking, and related operations, she should personally supervise the procedure for preparing her husband’s food.

BP 1.13.13

किं प्रियं च किमाग्नेयं षड्रसाभ्यन्तरेषु च ।
किं पथ्यं किमपथ्यं च स्वास्थ्यं वास्य कथं भवेत् ॥
इति यत्नाद्विजानीयादनुष्ठेयं च तत्तथा ॥ ।

She should carefully learn what he likes, what is warming or stimulating, which combinations of the six tastes suit him, what is wholesome or unwholesome, and how his health may be maintained; she should act accordingly.

BP 1.13.14

नित्यानुरागं सत्कारमाहारं सुपरीक्षितम् ।
महानसादौ कुर्वीत जनमाप्तं क्रमागतम् ॥

A trusted person of long-standing loyalty should be stationed in the kitchen and related areas, treated with continual affection and respect, and given food that has been carefully inspected.

BP 1.13.15

शत्रु दायादसम्बन्धं क्रुद्धभीतावमानितम् ।
अवाच्योपगृहीतं वा नैवमादीनि योजयेत् ॥

She should not appoint there anyone connected with an enemy or rival heir, anyone angry, frightened, or humiliated, or anyone bound by a grievance too shameful to state, nor others of comparable risk.

BP 1.13.16

पुनः पुनः प्रतिष्ठाप्य गुप्तं स्वयमधिष्ठितम् ।
भर्तुराहारपानादि विदध्यादप्रमादतः ॥

Repeatedly checking that it is secure and personally supervised, she should arrange her husband’s food, drink, and related provisions without negligence.

BP 1.13.17

पाकं निर्वर्त्य मात्राणां कृत्वा स्वेदप्रमार्जनम् ।
गन्धताम्बूलमाल्यादि किञ्चिदादाय मात्रया ॥

After completing the cooking and setting the portions, she should wipe away perspiration and take a moderate amount of fragrance, betel, garlands, and similar adornment.

BP 1.13.18

यथौचित्यादितत्काले भर्तुर्विनयसम्भ्रमैः ।
तत्कालानुगतात्यर्थमाहारमुपपादयेत् ॥

At the appropriate time, with humility and attentive eagerness toward her husband, she should provide a meal fully suited to the requirements of that particular occasion.

BP 1.13.19

स्वभावामयकालानां वैपरीत्येन सर्वदा ।
सर्वमाहारपानादि प्रयोज्यं तद्विदो जगुः ॥

Experts say that food and drink should always be adjusted by counterbalancing a person’s constitution, illness, and the season.

BP 1.13.20

हीनतुल्याधिकत्वेन भर्ता पश्यति यं यथा ।
तं तथैवाधिकं पश्येन्न्यायतः प्रतिपत्तिषु ॥

Whomever her husband regards as inferior, equal, or superior, she should regard in the corresponding way and, according to justice, show even greater care in her courteous response.

BP 1.13.21

सापत्नकान्यपत्यानि पश्येत्स्वेभ्यो विशेषतः ।
भगिनीवत्सपत्नीश्च तद्वधून्निजबन्धुवत् ॥

She should regard the children of co-wives with particular care, even beyond her own; she should treat co-wives like sisters and their daughters-in-law like her own relatives.

BP 1.13.22

ग्रासाच्छादशिरोभ्यङ्गस्नानमण्डनकादिकम् ।
सपत्नीनामकृत्वा तु आत्मनोऽपि न कारयेत् ॥

She should not arrange food, clothing, oiling of the head, bathing, adornment, or similar comforts for herself before providing them for her co-wives.

BP 1.13.23

व्याधितानां चिकित्सार्थमौषधादिकमादरात् ।
विदध्यादात्मनस्तासां सर्वाश्रितजनस्य च ॥

For those who are ill, she should respectfully arrange medicine and whatever else is needed for treatment—for herself, for the co-wives, and for every dependent member of the household.

BP 1.13.24

तच्छोके शुचमादद्यात्तत्तुष्टौ मुदमावहेत् ।
भृत्यबन्धुसपत्नीनां तुल्यदुःखसुखा भवेत् ॥

She should take on their grief when they grieve and bring joy when they are content, sharing alike the happiness and sorrow of servants, relatives, and co-wives.

BP 1.13.25

लग्धावकाशा स्वप्याच्य निशि सुप्तोत्थिता क्रमात् ।
अन्यत्र व्ययकर्तारं पतिं रहसि बोधयेत् ॥

Having found a suitable private opportunity—before sleep or after waking in the night—she should quietly inform her husband about expenditure or activity occurring elsewhere in the household.

BP 1.13.26

यदवद्यं सपत्नीनां स्वयमस्मै न तद्वदेत् ।
दौःशील्यादि तु सापायं गूढमस्मै निवेदयेत् ॥

She should not herself report ordinary faults of co-wives to him; but misconduct and similar matters that pose genuine danger should be disclosed to him privately.

BP 1.13.27

दुर्भगामनपत्यां वा भर्त्रा चातितिरस्कृताम् ।
अदुष्टां सम्यमाश्वास्य तेनैतामनुकूलयेत् ॥

A co-wife who is unfortunate, childless, or severely rejected by the husband, yet is not corrupt, should be properly comforted and reconciled with him.

BP 1.13.28

तथा वाग्दण्डपारुष्यैर्जनं भर्त्रा विपीडितम् ।
कुर्याद्विधेयमाश्वास्य न चेद्दोषाय तद्भवेत् ॥

Likewise, a household member who has been harshly afflicted by the husband through words or punishment should be comforted and made cooperative, provided doing so does not produce further fault.

BP 1.13.29

मत्वात्मनोनपत्यत्वं कालं चापि गतं बहुम् ।
सन्तानादिकमुद्दिश्य कार्यमात्मनिवेदनम् ॥

Recognizing that she herself has no child and that much time has passed, she should make a personal appeal concerning the need for offspring and continuation of the family.

BP 1.13.30

यच्चान्यदपि जानीयात्किञ्चिदस्य चिकीर्षितम् ।
तत्किलाजानतीवास्य सिद्धमेव प्रदर्शयेत् ॥

Whatever else she learns that her husband intends to do, she should, while appearing not to know it, present the matter to him as already accomplished.

BP 1.13.31

वैवाहिकं विधिं भर्तुः सर्वं कृत्वा ससम्भ्रमम् ।
परिणीता च तां पश्येन्नित्यं भगिनिकामिव ॥

After respectfully carrying out every part of her husband’s marriage ceremony, she should continually regard the newly married woman like a younger sister.

BP 1.13.32

पूजां सम्बन्धिवर्गस्य मङ्गलं मङ्गलानि च ।
कुर्यादभिनवोढायाः सुप्रहष्टेन चेतसा ॥

With a genuinely joyful heart she should arrange the honouring of the new bride’s relatives and perform the auspicious observance and all accompanying rites for her.

BP 1.13.33

मातृवच्छिक्षयेदेनां गृहकृत्येष्वमत्सरा ।
प्रदेशिकविधिं वास्या विदध्याद्यत्नतः स्वयम् ॥

Without jealousy, she should instruct the new wife like a mother in household duties and should personally and carefully establish for her the procedures particular to the locality.

BP 1.13.34

एवं भर्तुरभिप्राय सर्वमित्यादिकारयेत् ।
सुखार्थं वापि सन्त्यज्य स्त्रीणां भर्ताधिदेवता ॥

She should carry out all these matters according to her husband’s intention. Even if she relinquishes comfort, her husband is the presiding deity of women.

BP 1.13.35

भर्ताधिदेवता नार्या वर्णा ब्राह्मदेवताः ।
ब्राह्मणा ह्यग्निदेवास्तु प्रजा राजन्यदेवताः ॥

A husband is a woman’s presiding deity; Brahman is the deity of the social classes; Agni is the deity of brāhmaṇas; and the king is the deity of his subjects.

BP 1.13.36

तासां त्रिवर्गसंसिद्धौ प्रदिष्टं कारणद्वयम् ।
भर्तुर्यदनुकूलत्वं यच्च शीलमविप्लुतम् ॥

For women’s accomplishment of the three aims of life, two causes are prescribed: harmony with the husband and character that does not fall away from integrity.

BP 1.13.37

न तथा यौवनं लोके नापि रूपं न भूषणम् ।
यथा प्रियानुकूलत्वं सिद्धं शश्वदनौषधम् ॥

Neither youth, beauty, nor ornament is as effective in the world as harmony with the beloved; the source calls such harmony a perpetually successful medicine requiring no drug.

BP 1.13.38

वयोरूपादिहारिण्यो दृश्यन्ते दुर्भगाः स्त्रियः ।
वल्लभा मन्दरूपाश्च बह्व्यो गलितयौवनाः ॥

Women who possess youth, beauty, and other attractions are nevertheless seen to be unfortunate in love, while many of modest appearance and fading youth remain deeply beloved.

BP 1.13.39

तस्मात्प्रियत्वं लोकानां निदानं योग्यतापरम् ।
तां विनान्ये गुणा वन्ध्याः सर्वेऽनर्थकृतोऽपि वा ॥

Therefore the highest basis for being loved by people is suitability of character. Without it, all other virtues are fruitless or may even produce harm.

BP 1.13.40

तस्मात्सर्वप्रयत्नेन विदध्यादात्मयोग्यताम् ।
परचित्तज्ञता चास्या मूलं सर्वक्रियास्विह ॥

Therefore she should cultivate her own suitability with every effort. Here, the ability to understand another person’s mind is the root of all her undertakings.

BP 1.13.41

बहिरागच्छतो ज्ञात्वा कालं संमृज्य भूमिकाम् ।
सज्जीकृतासना तिष्ठेत्तस्याज्ञां प्रतितत्परा ॥

Knowing the time when her husband will return from outside, she should sweep the floor, have a seat prepared, and remain ready and intent upon receiving his instructions.

BP 1.13.42

स्वयं प्रक्षालयेत्पादावुत्थाप्य परिचारिकाम् ।
तालवृन्तादिकैः कुर्याच्छमस्वेदापनोदनम् ॥

She should herself wash his feet, having an attendant rise to assist, and use a palm-leaf fan or similar implement to relieve fatigue and remove perspiration.

BP 1.13.43

आहारस्नानपानादौ सस्पृहं यत्र लक्षयेत् ।
तदिंगितज्ञा तत्त्वेन सिद्धमस्मै निवेदयेत् ॥

When she observes that he desires food, bathing, drink, or anything similar, she should understand the indication accurately and present what has already been prepared for him.

BP 1.13.44

सपन्तीपतिबन्धूनां भर्तृचित्तानुकूल्यतः ।
प्रतिपत्तिं प्रयुञ्जीत स्वबन्धूनां न वै तथा ॥

She should show proper conduct toward co-wives and her husband’s relatives in accordance with his inclination, but not so toward her own relatives.

BP 1.13.45

तेषु चात्मनि च ज्ञात्वा भर्तृचित्तं प्रसादयेत् ।
प्रतिपत्तिं तथाप्येषां नाद्रियेत स्वबन्धुषु ॥

After discerning her husband’s feelings toward them and toward herself, she should seek to please him; even then, however, her treatment of his relatives should not simply be redirected toward favouring her own kin.

BP 1.13.46

अपि भर्तुरभिप्रेतं नारी तत्कुलवासिनी ।
सत्कारैर्निजबन्धूनां तेन नोपैति वाच्यताम् ॥

Even with her husband’s approval, a woman residing in his family should honour her own relatives in a measured way, so that such attentions do not expose her to reproach.

BP 1.13.47

पूज्य एव हि सम्बन्धः सर्वावस्थासु योषिताम् ।
कस्ततोऽप्युपकारांशं लिप्सेत कुलजः पुमान् ॥

A relationship created by marriage is indeed worthy of honour for women in every circumstance. What well-born man would seek to extract a share of material benefit from it?

BP 1.13.48

सम्पूज्य स्वसुतां तस्मै विधिवत्प्रतिपाद्यते ।
ततोऽस्या लिप्सते नाम किमकार्यमतः परम् ॥

Having honoured his daughter and duly given her to another family, if a father then seeks gain from her, what improper act could lie beyond that?

BP 1.13.49

कन्यां प्रदाय यैर्वृत्तिरात्मनः परिकल्प्यते ।
दासभण्डनटादीनां मार्गोऽयं न महात्मनाम् ॥

Those who give a daughter and then arrange their own livelihood from her marriage follow the path of servants, hired performers, and actors—not that of noble people.

BP 1.13.50

तस्मात्स्त्रीबांधवा नित्यं प्रीतिमात्रैकसाधिनीम् ।
प्रतिपत्तिं समादद्युः सम्बन्धिभ्यः प्रसंगिनीम् ।

Therefore a wife’s relatives should continually accept from her relations by marriage only those courtesies that create mutual affection, not material dependence.

BP 1.13.51

तस्या भर्तरि रक्षेत प्रीतिं लोके च वाच्यताम् ।
आत्मनोऽसत्प्रवादं च चेष्टेरन्साधुवृत्तयः ॥

People of good conduct should act so as to preserve her affection for her husband, protect her reputation in the world, and prevent false scandal against their own family.

BP 1.13.52

एवं विज्ञाय सद्वृत्तं स्त्री वर्तेत तथा सदा ।
येन तत्परिवर्गस्य भवेद्भर्तुश्च सम्मता ॥

Understanding good conduct in this way, a woman should always behave so that she is accepted and respected both by her husband and by his entire circle.

BP 1.13.53

प्रियापि साधुवृत्तापि विख्याताभिजनापि च ।
जनापवादात्सम्प्राप सीतानर्थं सुदारुणम् ॥

Though beloved, virtuous in conduct, and born from a renowned family, Sītā incurred terrible suffering through public accusation.

BP 1.13.54

सर्वस्यामिषभूतत्वाद्गुणदोषानभिज्ञतः ।
प्रायेणाविनयौचित्यात्स्त्रीणां वृत्तं हि दुष्करम् ॥

Because women are objects of desire for all, because [people] do not discern virtue and fault, and because misconduct is common, women’s conduct is indeed difficult.

BP 1.13.55

अगृह्यत्वान्मनोवृत्तेः प्रायः कपटदर्शनात् ।
निरङ्कुशत्वाल्लोकस्य निर्वाच्या विरलाः स्त्रियः ॥

Because movements of the mind cannot be grasped, appearances are often deceptive, and public speech is uncontrolled, few women—however blameless—remain entirely free from accusation.

BP 1.13.56

दैवयोगादयोगत्वाद्व्यवहारानभिज्ञतः ।
वाच्यतापत्तयो दृष्टाः स्त्रीणां शुद्धेऽपि चेतसि ॥

Through the working of fate, unsuitable circumstances, and ignorance of social dealings, women have been seen to fall under accusation even when their minds are pure.

BP 1.13.57

तासां दैवप्रतीकारो नोपभोगादृते भवेत् ।
चारित्रं लोकवृत्तं च एतयोर्विदुरौषधम् ॥

There may be no remedy for the workings of fate except enduring their result; for the other two dangers, the wise recognize personal character and careful public conduct as the medicine.

BP 1.13.58

हिंदोलकादिक्रीडायां प्रसक्तां तरुणीं निशि ।
रममाणां विटैः सार्धं विधवां स्वैरचारिणीम् ॥

Consider a young widow roaming freely at night, absorbed in swing-play and similar amusements, and sporting in the company of libertines—

BP 1.13.59

वृद्धादिभार्यां सज्जायां यानगेयादिसंगिनीम् ।
कः श्रद्दध्यात्सतीत्येवं साध्वीमपि हि योषितम् ॥

—or the wife of an elderly man, elaborately dressed and continually attending travel, music, and similar entertainments: who would believe such a woman chaste, even if she were in fact virtuous?

BP 1.13.60

यौ चासामिङ्गिताकारौ सन्दिग्धार्थप्रसाधकौ ।
तयोस्तत्त्वपरिज्ञानं विषयो योगिनां यदि ॥

If the true interpretation of women’s gestures and appearances, which can support ambiguous meanings, is a subject fit only for yogins, ordinary judgment remains uncertain.

BP 1.13.61

तस्माद्यथोक्तमाचारमनुतिष्ठेत्सुसंयता ।
मिथ्यालग्नोप्यसद्वादः कम्पयत्येव तत्कुलम् ॥

Therefore, fully self-controlled, she should follow the conduct already described. Even a false accusation, once attached, can shake her family.

BP 1.13.62

त्रिकुल्या वाच्यता रक्ष्या प्रतिष्ठाप्यथ सन्ततिः ।
भर्तुस्त्रिवर्गसिद्धिश्च साध्यं तत्कुलयोषिताम् ॥

The aims of women in established families are to guard the reputation of all three connected families, establish the continuation of the lineage, and help accomplish the husband’s three aims of life.

BP 1.13.63

पातयन्त्येव दौःशील्यादात्मानं सकुलत्रयम् ।
उद्धरन्ति तदैवैताः स्त्रियश्चारित्रभूषणाः ॥

Through corrupt conduct women bring down themselves together with all three families; women adorned by character lift those same families up.

BP 1.13.64

भर्तृचित्तानुकूलत्वं यासां शीलमविच्युतम् ।
तासां रत्नसुवर्णादि भार एव न मण्डनम् ॥

For women whose disposition remains harmonious with the husband’s mind and whose character never falls away, jewels, gold, and similar ornaments are merely weight, not their true adornment.

BP 1.13.65

लोकज्ञाने परा कोटिः पत्यौ भक्तिश्च शाश्वती ।
शुद्धान्वयानां नारीणां विद्यादेतत्कुलव्रतम् ॥

The family vow of women born from pure lineages should be known as the highest degree of worldly discernment together with enduring devotion toward the husband.

BP 1.13.66

तस्माल्लोकश्च भर्ता च सम्यगाराधितो यया ।
धर्ममर्थं च कामं च सैवाप्नोति निरत्यया ॥

Therefore the woman who properly honours both society and her husband obtains, without falling away, dharma, wealth, and desire.
