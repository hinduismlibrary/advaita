Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 13 — Household Conduct and Family Reputation

BP 1.13.1

ब्रह्मोवाच
प्रथमं प्रतिबुध्येत प्रवर्तेत स्वकर्मसु ।
पश्चाद्भृत्यजनस्यापि भुञ्जीत च शयीत च ॥

Brahmā spoke: She should awaken first and engage in her own duties; after the servants and household members, she should eat and sleep.

ब्रह्मा (brahmā) — Brahmā; उवाच (uvāca) — spoke; प्रथमम् (prathamam) — first; प्रतिबुध्येत (pratibudhyeta) — should awaken; प्रवर्तेत (pravarteta) — should engage; स्वकर्मसु (svakarmasu) — in her own duties; पश्चात् (paścāt) — afterward; भृत्यजनस्य (bhṛtyajanasya) — of the servants; अपि (api) — also; भुञ्जीत (bhuñjīta) — should eat; च (ca) — and; शयीत (śayīta) — should sleep; च (ca) — and

BP 1.13.2

भर्त्रा विरहिता स्त्री च श्वशुराभ्यां विशेषतः ।
देहलीं नातिवर्तेत प्रतीकारे महत्यपि ॥

A woman separated from her husband, especially while with her parents-in-law, should not cross the threshold, even in a great emergency.

भर्त्रा (bhartrā) — from her husband; विरहिता (virahitā) — separated; स्त्री (strī) — woman; च (ca) — and; श्वशुराभ्याम् (śvaśurābhyām) — from her parents-in-law; विशेषतः (viśeṣataḥ) — especially; देहलीम् (dehalīm) — threshold; न (na) — not; अतिवर्तेत (ativarteta) — should cross; प्रतीकारे (pratīkāre) — in an emergency; महति (mahati) — great; अपि (api) — even

BP 1.13.3

उत्थाय प्रथमं भर्तुरविज्ञाता न निष्क्रमेत् ।
क्षपायां सावशेषायां रात्रौ वा वासरादिषु ॥

Having risen first, she should not go out without her husband’s knowledge while part of the night remains, at night, or during the day.

उत्थाय (utthāya) — having risen; प्रथमम् (prathamam) — first; भर्तुः (bhartuḥ) — of her husband; अविज्ञाता (avijñātā) — without his knowing; न (na) — not; निष्क्रमेत् (niṣkramet) — should go out; क्षपायाम् (kṣapāyām) — during the night; सावशेषायाम् (sāvaśeṣāyām) — while a portion remains; रात्रौ (rātrau) — at night; वा (vā) — or; वासरादिषु (vāsarādiṣu) — during the day and the like

BP 1.13.4

तद्वासभवनस्यैव शनैराहूय कार्मिकान् ।
स्वव्यापारेषु तान्सर्वांस्तत्रतत्र नियोजयेत् ॥

From that very dwelling, she should quietly summon the workers and assign them all to their respective tasks.

तत् (tat) — that; वासभवनस्य (vāsabhavanasya) — of the dwelling; एव (eva) — indeed; शनैः (śanaiḥ) — quietly; आहूय (āhūya) — having summoned; कार्मिकान् (kārmikān) — workers; स्वव्यापारेषु (svavyāpāreṣu) — in their respective tasks; तान् (tān) — them; सर्वान् (sarvān) — all; तत्रतत्र (tatratatra) — here and there; नियोजयेत् (niyojayet) — she should assign

BP 1.13.5

विबुद्धस्य ततो भर्तुर्निर्वर्त्यावश्यकं विधिम् ।
गृहकार्याणि सर्वाणि विदधीताप्रमादतः ॥

Then, after her husband has awakened and completed the necessary rite, she should perform all the household tasks without negligence.

विबुद्धस्य (vibuddhasya) — of the awakened; ततः (tataḥ) — then; भर्तुः (bhartuḥ) — husband; निर्वर्त्य (nirvartya) — having completed; आवश्यकम् (āvaśyakam) — necessary; विधिम् (vidhim) — rite; गृहकार्याणि (gṛhakāryāṇi) — household tasks; सर्वाणि (sarvāṇi) — all; विदधीत (vidadhīta) — she should perform; अप्रमादतः (apramādataḥ) — without negligence

BP 1.13.6

मुक्त्वावासकनेपथ्यं कर्मयोग्यं विधाय च ।
तत्कालोचितकर्तव्यमनुतिष्ठेद्यथाक्रमम् ॥

Having put aside her sleeping garment and apparel and made herself fit for work, she should carry out in order the task proper to that time.

मुक्त्वा (muktvā) — having put aside; आवासक (āvāsaka) — sleeping garment; नेपथ्यम् (nepathyam) — apparel; कर्मयोग्यम् (karmayogyam) — fit for work; विधाय (vidhāya) — having made; च (ca) — and; तत्कालोचितकर्तव्यम् (tatkālocitakartavyam) — the task proper to that time; अनुतिष्ठेत् (anutiṣṭhet) — she should carry out; यथाक्रमम् (yathākramam) — in order

BP 1.13.7

महानसं सुसम्मृष्टं चुल्ल्यादिविहितार्चनम् ।
सर्वोपकरणोपेतमसम्बाधमनाविलम् ॥

The kitchen should be well swept, the hearth and the like properly attended, equipped with every utensil, unobstructed, and clean.

महानसम् (mahānasam) — kitchen; सुसम्मृष्टम् (susammṛṣṭam) — well swept; चुल्ल्यादि (cullyādi) — hearth and the like; विहितार्चनम् (vihitārcanam) — properly attended; सर्वोपकरणोपेतम् (sarvopakaraṇopetam) — equipped with every utensil; असम्बाधम् (asambādham) — unobstructed; अनाविलम् (anāvilam) — clean

BP 1.13.8

न चातिगुह्यं प्रकटं प्रविभक्तक्रियाश्रयम् ।
भर्तुराप्तजनाकीर्णं गूढं कक्षादिवर्जितम् ॥

It should be neither too hidden nor exposed, with separate areas for different tasks, filled with the husband’s trusted people, private, and without rooms and the like.

न (na) — not; च (ca) — and; अतिगुह्यम् (atiguhyam) — too hidden; प्रकटम् (prakaṭam) — exposed; प्रविभक्तक्रियाश्रयम् (pravibhaktakriyāśrayam) — with separate areas for different tasks; भर्तुः (bhartuḥ) — of the husband; आप्तजनाकीर्णम् (āptajanākīrṇam) — filled with trusted people; गूढम् (gūḍham) — private; कक्षादिवर्जितम् (kakṣādivarjitam) — without rooms and the like

BP 1.13.9

तत्र पाकादिभाण्डानि बहिरन्तश्च कारयेत् ।
निणिक्तमलपङ्कानि शुक्तिवल्कादिचूर्णकैः ॥

There she should have the cooking vessels and the like cleaned inside and out, removing grease, dirt, and mud with powders of shell, bark, and the like.

तत्र (tatra) — there; पाकादिभाण्डानि (pākādibhāṇḍāni) — cooking vessels and the like; बहिः (bahiḥ) — outside; अन्तः (antaḥ) — inside; च (ca) — and; कारयेत् (kārayet) — she should have [them] cleaned; निणिक्तमलपङ्कानि (niṇiktamalapaṅkāni) — smeared with grease, dirt, and mud; शुक्तिवल्कादिचूर्णकैः (śuktivalkādicūrṇakaiḥ) — with powders of shell, bark, and the like

BP 1.13.10

निशि कुर्वीत धूमार्चिः शोधितानि दिवातपः ।
दधिपात्राणि कुर्वीत सदैवान्तरितानि च ॥

At night she should cleanse the vessels with smoke and flame; by day, with sunlight. She should always keep the curd vessels separate.

निशि (niśi) — at night; कुर्वीत (kurvīta) — she should cleanse; धूमार्चिः (dhūmārciḥ) — smoke and flame; शोधितानि (śodhitāni) — the cleansed [vessels]; दिवा (divā) — by day; आतपः (ātapaḥ) — sunlight; दधिपात्राणि (dadhipātrāṇi) — curd vessels; कुर्वीत (kurvīta) — she should keep; सदा (sadā) — always; एव (eva) — indeed; अन्तरितानि (antaritāni) — separate; च (ca) — and

BP 1.13.11

साधुकारितदुग्धेषु शोधितेषु दिवातपे ।
ईषद्गृह्योक्तपात्रेषु स्वच्छं येन भवेद्दधि ॥

In milk properly prepared, [and] in vessels cleansed in sunlight and prescribed for slight heating, curd may become clear.

साधुकारितदुग्धेषु (sādhukāritadugdheṣu) — in milk properly prepared; शोधितेषु (śodhiteṣu) — in [vessels] cleansed; दिवातपे (divātape) — in sunlight; ईषत् (īṣat) — slightly; गृह्य (gṛhya) — prescribed in household rites; उक्त (ukta) — stated; पात्रेषु (pātreṣu) — in vessels; स्वच्छम् (svaccham) — clear; येन (yena) — by which; भवेत् (bhavet) — may become; दधि (dadhi) — curd

BP 1.13.12

स्नेहगोरसपाकादि कृत्वा सुप्रत्ययेक्षितम् ।
कुर्यात्स्वयमधिष्ठाय भर्तुः पाकविधिक्रियाम् ॥

Having prepared oil, milk products, cooking, and the like, and having carefully inspected them, she should herself oversee and perform her husband’s cooking procedures.

स्नेह (sneha) — oil; गोरस (gorasa) — milk products; पाक (pāka) — cooking; आदि (ādi) — and the like; कृत्वा (kṛtvā) — having prepared; सु (su) — well; प्रत्यय (pratyaya) — inspection; ईक्षितम् (īkṣitam) — examined; कुर्यात् (kuryāt) — she should do; स्वयम् (svayam) — herself; अधिष्ठाय (adhiṣṭhāya) — having overseen; भर्तुः (bhartuḥ) — of her husband; पाकविधि (pākavidhi) — cooking method; क्रियाम् (kriyām) — the act

BP 1.13.13

किं प्रियं च किमाग्नेयं षड्रसाभ्यन्तरेषु च ।
किं पथ्यं किमपथ्यं च स्वास्थ्यं वास्य कथं भवेत् ॥
इति यत्नाद्विजानीयादनुष्ठेयं च तत्तथा ॥ ।

She should carefully learn: What is pleasing? What is heating among the six tastes? What is wholesome, and what is unwholesome? How may his health be? She should then carry out what is to be done accordingly.

किम् (kim) — what; प्रियम् (priyam) — is pleasing; च (ca) — and; किम् (kim) — what; आग्नेयम् (āgneyam) — is heating; षड्रसाभ्यन्तरेषु (ṣaḍrasābhyantareṣu) — among the six tastes; च (ca) — and; किम् (kim) — what; पथ्यम् (pathyam) — is wholesome; किम् (kim) — what; अपथ्यम् (apathyam) — is unwholesome; च (ca) — and; स्वास्थ्यम् (svāsthyam) — health; वा (vā) — or; अस्य (asya) — of him; कथम् (katham) — how; भवेत् (bhavet) — may be; इति (iti) — thus; यत्नात् (yatnāt) — carefully; विजानीयात् (vijānīyāt) — she should learn; अनुष्ठेयम् (anuṣṭheyam) — to be carried out; च (ca) — and; तत् (tat) — that; तथा (tathā) — accordingly

BP 1.13.14

नित्यानुरागं सत्कारमाहारं सुपरीक्षितम् ।
महानसादौ कुर्वीत जनमाप्तं क्रमागतम् ॥

In the kitchen and elsewhere, she should provide constant affection, respectful treatment, and carefully examined food to trusted personnel long established [in service].

नित्य (nitya) — constant; अनुरागम् (anurāgam) — affection; सत्कारम् (satkāram) — respectful treatment; आहारम् (āhāram) — food; सु (su) — well; परीक्षितम् (parīkṣitam) — examined; महानसादौ (mahānasādau) — in the kitchen and elsewhere; कुर्वीत (kurvīta) — she should provide; जनम् (janam) — personnel; आप्तम् (āptam) — trusted; क्रमागतम् (kramāgatam) — long established

BP 1.13.15

शत्रु दायादसम्बन्धं क्रुद्धभीतावमानितम् ।
अवाच्योपगृहीतं वा नैवमादीनि योजयेत् ॥

She should not appoint anyone connected with an enemy or rival heir, angry, afraid, insulted, improperly received, or of such kinds.

शत्रु (śatru) — enemy; दायाद (dāyāda) — rival heir; सम्बन्धम् (sambandham) — connected with; क्रुद्ध (kruddha) — angry; भीत (bhīta) — afraid; अवमानितम् (avamānitam) — insulted; अवाच्य (avācya) — improper; उपगृहीतम् (upagṛhītam) — taken in; वा (vā) — or; न (na) — not; एवम् (evam) — such; आदीनि (ādīni) — and the like; योजयेत् (yojayet) — she should appoint

BP 1.13.16

पुनः पुनः प्रतिष्ठाप्य गुप्तं स्वयमधिष्ठितम् ।
भर्तुराहारपानादि विदध्यादप्रमादतः ॥

Having repeatedly secured [the arrangements] and personally overseen them, she should arrange her husband’s food, drink, and the like without negligence.

पुनः (punaḥ) — again; पुनः (punaḥ) — again; प्रतिष्ठाप्य (pratiṣṭhāpya) — having secured; गुप्तम् (guptam) — securely; स्वयम् (svayam) — herself; अधिष्ठितम् (adhiṣṭhitam) — having overseen; भर्तुः (bhartuḥ) — of her husband; आहार (āhāra) — food; पान (pāna) — drink; आदि (ādi) — and the like; विदध्यात् (vidadhyāt) — she should arrange; अप्रमादतः (apramādataḥ) — without negligence

BP 1.13.17

पाकं निर्वर्त्य मात्राणां कृत्वा स्वेदप्रमार्जनम् ।
गन्धताम्बूलमाल्यादि किञ्चिदादाय मात्रया ॥

Having completed the cooking and set the portions, she should wipe away perspiration and take a little fragrance, betel, garlands, and the like in moderation.

पाकम् (pākam) — cooking; निर्वर्त्य (nirvartya) — having completed; मात्राणाम् (mātrāṇām) — of the portions; कृत्वा (kṛtvā) — having made; स्वेद (sveda) — sweat; प्रमार्जनम् (pramārjanam) — wiping away; गन्ध (gandha) — fragrance; ताम्बूल (tāmbūla) — betel; माल्य (mālya) — garland; आदि (ādi) — and the like; किञ्चित् (kiñcit) — a little; आदाय (ādāya) — having taken; मात्रया (mātrayā) — in moderation

BP 1.13.18

यथौचित्यादितत्काले भर्तुर्विनयसम्भ्रमैः ।
तत्कालानुगतात्यर्थमाहारमुपपादयेत् ॥

With the deference and attentiveness appropriate to the time and her husband, she should offer food greatly suited to that time.

यथौचित्यादि (yathaucityādi) — according to propriety and the like; तत्काले (tatkāle) — at that time; भर्तुः (bhartuḥ) — toward her husband; विनय (vinaya) — deference; सम्भ्रमैः (sambhramaiḥ) — with attentiveness; तत्कालानुगत (tatkālānugata) — suited to that time; अत्यर्थम् (atyartham) — greatly; आहारम् (āhāram) — food; उपपादयेत् (upapādayet) — she should offer

BP 1.13.19

स्वभावामयकालानां वैपरीत्येन सर्वदा ।
सर्वमाहारपानादि प्रयोज्यं तद्विदो जगुः ॥

Those who know this have said that food, drink, and the like should always be used contrary to [one’s] constitution, illness, and season.

स्वभाव (svabhāva) — constitution; आमय (āmaya) — illness; कालानाम् (kālānām) — of seasons; वैपरीत्येन (vaiparītyena) — in opposition; सर्वदा (sarvadā) — always; सर्वम् (sarvam) — all; आहार (āhāra) — food; पान (pāna) — drink; आदि (ādi) — and the like; प्रयोज्यम् (prayojyam) — should be used; तद्विदः (tadvidaḥ) — those who know this; जगुः (jaguḥ) — have said

BP 1.13.20

हीनतुल्याधिकत्वेन भर्ता पश्यति यं यथा ।
तं तथैवाधिकं पश्येन्न्यायतः प्रतिपत्तिषु ॥

Whomever the husband regards as inferior, equal, or superior, she should regard in the same way—and with greater regard—in her conduct, appropriately.

हीन (hīna) — inferior; तुल्य (tulya) — equal; अधिकत्वेन (adhikatvena) — as superior; भर्ता (bhartā) — the husband; पश्यति (paśyati) — regards; यम् (yam) — whom; यथा (yathā) — in whichever way; तम् (tam) — that person; तथा (tathā) — in that way; एव (eva) — indeed; अधिकम् (adhikam) — more; पश्येत् (paśyet) — she should regard; न्यायतः (nyāyataḥ) — appropriately; प्रतिपत्तिषु (pratipattiṣu) — in her conduct

BP 1.13.21

सापत्नकान्यपत्यानि पश्येत्स्वेभ्यो विशेषतः ।
भगिनीवत्सपत्नीश्च तद्वधून्निजबन्धुवत् ॥

She should regard the children of co-wives especially as [dearer] than her own; she should regard co-wives as sisters and their daughters-in-law as her own kin.

सापत्नकानि (sāpatnakāni) — of co-wives; अपत्यानि (apatyāni) — children; पश्येत् (paśyet) — she should regard; स्वेभ्यः (svebhyaḥ) — than her own; विशेषतः (viśeṣataḥ) — especially; भगिनीवत् (bhaginīvat) — like sisters; सपत्नीन् (sapatnīn) — co-wives; च (ca) — and; तद् (tad) — their; वधून् (vadhūn) — daughters-in-law; निजबन्धुवत् (nijabandhuvat) — like her own kin

BP 1.13.22

ग्रासाच्छादशिरोभ्यङ्गस्नानमण्डनकादिकम् ।
सपत्नीनामकृत्वा तु आत्मनोऽपि न कारयेत् ॥

Without first arranging food, clothing, head-oiling, bathing, adornment, and the like for her co-wives, she should not arrange them even for herself.

ग्रास (grāsa) — food; आच्छाद (ācchāda) — clothing; शिरोभ्यङ्ग (śirobhyaṅga) — head-oiling; स्नान (snāna) — bathing; मण्डनक (maṇḍanaka) — adornment; आदिकम् (ādikam) — and the like; सपत्नीनाम् (sapatnīnām) — for co-wives; अकृत्वा (akṛtvā) — without doing; तु (tu) — but; आत्मनः (ātmanaḥ) — for herself; अपि (api) — even; न (na) — not; कारयेत् (kārayet) — she should arrange

BP 1.13.23

व्याधितानां चिकित्सार्थमौषधादिकमादरात् ।

BP 1.13.47

कस्ततोऽप्युपकारांशं लिप्सेत कुलजः पुमान् ॥

A relationship is indeed worthy of honor for women in all circumstances. What well-born man would seek even a share of benefit from it?

BP 1.13.48

BP 1.13.49

कन्यां प्रदाय यैर्वृत्तिरात्मनः परिकल्प्यते ।

BP 1.13.50

तस्मात्स्त्रीबांधवा नित्यं प्रीतिमात्रैकसाधिनीम् ।
प्रतिपत्तिं समादद्युः सम्बन्धिभ्यः प्रसंगिनीम् ।

BP 1.13.51

तस्या भर्तरि रक्षेत प्रीतिं लोके च वाच्यताम् ।
आत्मनोऽसत्प्रवादं च चेष्टेरन्साधुवृत्तयः ॥

BP 1.13.52

एवं विज्ञाय सद्वृत्तं स्त्री वर्तेत तथा सदा ।
येन तत्परिवर्गस्य भवेद्भर्तुश्च सम्मता ॥

एवम् (evam) — thus; विज्ञाय (vijñāya) — having understood; सद्वृत्तम् (sadvṛttam) — good conduct; स्त्री (strī) — a woman; वर्तेत (varteta) — she should behave; तथा (tathā) — so; सदा (sadā) — always; येन (yena) — by which; तत्परिवर्गस्य (tatparivargasya) — of her whole circle; भवेत् (bhavet) — she may be; भर्तुः (bhartuḥ) — of her husband; च (ca) — and; सम्मता (sammatā) — approved

BP 1.13.53

प्रियापि साधुवृत्तापि विख्याताभिजनापि च ।
जनापवादात्सम्प्राप सीतानर्थं सुदारुणम् ॥

BP 1.13.54

सर्वस्यामिषभूतत्वाद्गुणदोषानभिज्ञतः ।
प्रायेणाविनयौचित्यात्स्त्रीणां वृत्तं हि दुष्करम् ॥

BP 1.13.55

अगृह्यत्वान्मनोवृत्तेः प्रायः कपटदर्शनात् ।

BP 1.13.56

BP 1.13.57

BP 1.13.58

हिण्डोलकादिक्रीडायां प्रसक्तां तरुणीं निशि ।

BP 1.13.61
