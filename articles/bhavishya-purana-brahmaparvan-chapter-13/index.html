Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 13 — Household Conduct and Family Reputation

BP 1.13.1

ब्रह्मोवाच
प्रथमं प्रतिबुध्येत प्रवर्तेत स्वकर्मसु ।
पश्चाद्भृत्यजनस्यापि भुञ्जीत च शयीत च ॥

Brahmā said: She should awaken first and begin her own duties. After the servants and household members, she should eat and sleep.

BP 1.13.2

भर्त्रा विरहिता स्त्री च श्वशुराभ्यां विशेषतः ।
देहलीं नातिवर्तेत प्रतीकारे महत्यपि ॥

A woman separated from her husband, especially while with her parents-in-law, should not cross the threshold, even in a great need.

BP 1.13.3

उत्थाय प्रथमं भर्तुरविज्ञाता न निष्क्रमेत् ।
क्षपायां सावशेषायां रात्रौ वा वासरादिषु ॥

Having risen first, she should not leave without her husband’s knowledge while night remains, during the night, or at daybreak.

BP 1.13.4

तद्वासभवनस्यैव शनैराहूय कार्मिकान् ।
स्वव्यापारेषु तान्सर्वांस्तत्रतत्र नियोजयेत् ॥

From that residence she should quietly summon the workers and assign each to the proper work.

BP 1.13.5

विबुद्धस्य ततो भर्तुर्निर्वर्त्यावश्यकं विधिम् ।
गृहकार्याणि सर्वाणि विदधीताप्रमादतः ॥

After her husband has awakened and completed the necessary rites, she should perform all the household work without negligence.

BP 1.13.6

मुक्त्वावासकनेपथ्यं कर्मयोग्यं विधाय च ।
तत्कालोचितकर्तव्यमनुतिष्ठेद्यथाक्रमम् ॥

Having put aside her sleeping clothes and made herself fit for work, she should perform in order the duties proper to that time.

BP 1.13.7

महानसं सुसम्मृष्टं चुल्ल्यादिविहितार्चनम् ।
सर्वोपकरणोपेतमसम्बाधमनाविलम् ॥

The kitchen should be well swept, the hearth and the like duly honored, and it should be supplied with all utensils, spacious, and clean.

BP 1.13.8

न चातिगुह्यं प्रकटं प्रविभक्तक्रियाश्रयम् ।
भर्तुराप्तजनाकीर्णं गूढं कक्षादिवर्जितम् ॥

It should be neither too hidden nor too exposed, divided according to its functions, occupied by people trusted by her husband, secure, and without secluded rooms and the like.

BP 1.13.9

तत्र पाकादिभाण्डानि बहिरन्तश्च कारयेत् ।
निणिक्तमलपङ्कानि शुक्तिवल्कादिचूर्णकैः ॥

She should have the cooking vessels and the like cleaned inside and out, removing grease, dirt, and mud with powders made from shell, bark, and the like.

BP 1.13.10

निशि कुर्वीत धूमार्चिः शोधितानि दिवातपः ।
दधिपात्राणि कुर्वीत सदैवान्तरितानि च ॥

At night she should purify them with smoke and flame, and by day with sunlight. She should always keep the curd vessels separate.

BP 1.13.11

साधुकारितदुग्धेषु शोधितेषु दिवातपे ।
ईषद्गृह्योक्तपात्रेषु स्वच्छं येन भवेद्दधि ॥

To make clear curd, she should use well-prepared milk and vessels cleansed in sunlight and warmed slightly as prescribed.

BP 1.13.12

स्नेहगोरसपाकादि कृत्वा सुप्रत्ययेक्षितम् ।
कुर्यात्स्वयमधिष्ठाय भर्तुः पाकविधिक्रियाम् ॥

Having prepared oil, dairy products, cooking, and the like, and having carefully inspected them, she should herself oversee the preparation of her husband’s food.

BP 1.13.13

किं प्रियं च किमाग्नेयं षड्रसाभ्यन्तरेषु च ।
किं पथ्यं किमपथ्यं च स्वास्थ्यं वास्य कथं भवेत् ॥
इति यत्नाद्विजानीयादनुष्ठेयं च तत्तथा ॥ ।

She should carefully learn what he likes, what is warming, which of the six tastes suit him, what is wholesome and unwholesome, and how his health may be maintained; she should act accordingly.

BP 1.13.14

नित्यानुरागं सत्कारमाहारं सुपरीक्षितम् ।
महानसादौ कुर्वीत जनमाप्तं क्रमागतम् ॥

She should place a trusted, long-standing attendant in the kitchen and the like, show continual affection and respect, and give carefully inspected food.

BP 1.13.15

शत्रु दायादसम्बन्धं क्रुद्धभीतावमानितम् ।
अवाच्योपगृहीतं वा नैवमादीनि योजयेत् ॥

She should not appoint a person connected with an enemy or rival heir, angry, frightened, insulted, or one who has been taken in for an unspeakable reason, or the like.

BP 1.13.16

पुनः पुनः प्रतिष्ठाप्य गुप्तं स्वयमधिष्ठितम् ।
भर्तुराहारपानादि विदध्यादप्रमादतः ॥

Having repeatedly made [the arrangements] secure and personally overseen them, she should prepare her husband’s food, drink, and the like without negligence.

BP 1.13.17

पाकं निर्वर्त्य मात्राणां कृत्वा स्वेदप्रमार्जनम् ।
गन्धताम्बूलमाल्यादि किञ्चिदादाय मात्रया ॥

After finishing the cooking and setting the portions, she should wipe away perspiration and take a little fragrance, betel, garlands, and the like.

BP 1.13.18

यथौचित्यादितत्काले भर्तुर्विनयसम्भ्रमैः ।
तत्कालानुगतात्यर्थमाहारमुपपादयेत् ॥

At the proper time, with modesty and attentive eagerness toward her husband, she should provide food especially suited to that occasion.

BP 1.13.19

स्वभावामयकालानां वैपरीत्येन सर्वदा ।
सर्वमाहारपानादि प्रयोज्यं तद्विदो जगुः ॥

Those who know say that food and drink should always be prescribed in the opposite way to one’s constitution, illness, and season.

BP 1.13.20

हीनतुल्याधिकत्वेन भर्ता पश्यति यं यथा ।
तं तथैवाधिकं पश्येन्न्यायतः प्रतिपत्तिषु ॥

Whomever her husband regards as inferior, equal, or superior, she should regard in the same way and show him greater attention with due regard.

BP 1.13.21

सापत्नकान्यपत्यानि पश्येत्स्वेभ्यो विशेषतः ।
भगिनीवत्सपत्नीश्च तद्वधून्निजबन्धुवत् ॥

She should care for the children of co-wives even more than for her own; she should regard co-wives as sisters and their daughters-in-law as her own relatives.

BP 1.13.22

ग्रासाच्छादशिरोभ्यङ्गस्नानमण्डनकादिकम् ।
सपत्नीनामकृत्वा तु आत्मनोऽपि न कारयेत् ॥

Without first providing for her co-wives, she should not arrange food, clothing, oiling of the head, bathing, adornment, and the like even for herself.

BP 1.13.23

व्याधितानां चिकित्सार्थमौषधादिकमादरात् ।
विदध्यादात्मनस्तासां सर्वाश्रितजनस्य च ॥

She should respectfully arrange medicines and the like for the treatment of the sick—herself, the co-wives, and all dependent people.

BP 1.13.24

तच्छोके शुचमादद्यात्तत्तुष्टौ मुदमावहेत् ।
भृत्यबन्धुसपत्नीनां तुल्यदुःखसुखा भवेत् ॥

When they grieve, she should grieve; when they are pleased, she should bring joy. She should share equally the happiness and sorrow of servants, relatives, and co-wives.

BP 1.13.25

लग्धावकाशा स्वप्याच्य निशि सुप्तोत्थिता क्रमात् ।
अन्यत्र व्ययकर्तारं पतिं रहसि बोधयेत् ॥

Having found an opportunity, before sleep or after waking at night, she should privately inform her husband about the person making expenditures elsewhere.

BP 1.13.26

यदवद्यं सपत्नीनां स्वयमस्मै न तद्वदेत् ।
दौःशील्यादि तु सापायं गूढमस्मै निवेदयेत् ॥

She should not tell him the faults of co-wives herself; but she should privately tell him of misconduct and the like that is dangerous.

BP 1.13.27

दुर्भगामनपत्यां वा भर्त्रा चातितिरस्कृताम् ।
अदुष्टां सम्यमाश्वास्य तेनैतामनुकूलयेत् ॥

She should comfort and reconcile with him a co-wife who is unfortunate, childless, or greatly rejected by her husband, if she is not corrupt.

BP 1.13.28

तथा वाग्दण्डपारुष्यैर्जनं भर्त्रा विपीडितम् ।
कुर्याद्विधेयमाश्वास्य न चेद्दोषाय तद्भवेत् ॥

Likewise, she should comfort and make obedient a person afflicted by the husband’s harsh words or punishment, if that does not cause harm.

BP 1.13.29

मत्वात्मनोनपत्यत्वं कालं चापि गतं बहुम् ।
सन्तानादिकमुद्दिश्य कार्यमात्मनिवेदनम् ॥

Thinking that she has no child and that much time has passed, she should make a personal appeal concerning children and the like.

BP 1.13.30

यच्चान्यदपि जानीयात्किञ्चिदस्य चिकीर्षितम् ।
तत्किलाजानतीवास्य सिद्धमेव प्रदर्शयेत् ॥

Whatever else she knows he intends to do, she should, as if not knowing it, present it to him as already accomplished.

BP 1.13.31

वैवाहिकं विधिं भर्तुः सर्वं कृत्वा ससम्भ्रमम् ।
परिणीता च तां पश्येन्नित्यं भगिनिकामिव ॥

Having carefully carried out all the rites of her husband’s marriage, she should always regard the newly married woman as a younger sister.

BP 1.13.32

पूजां सम्बन्धिवर्गस्य मङ्गलं मङ्गलानि च ।
कुर्यादभिनवोढायाः सुप्रहष्टेन चेतसा ॥

With a very joyful mind, she should perform the honoring of the new bride’s relatives and the auspicious rites and observances.

BP 1.13.33

मातृवच्छिक्षयेदेनां गृहकृत्येष्वमत्सरा ।
प्रदेशिकविधिं वास्या विदध्याद्यत्नतः स्वयम् ॥

Without jealousy, she should teach her household duties like a mother and carefully establish for her the customs of the region.

BP 1.13.34

एवं भर्तुरभिप्राय सर्वमित्यादिकारयेत् ।
सुखार्थं वापि सन्त्यज्य स्त्रीणां भर्ताधिदेवता ॥

She should arrange everything according to her husband’s intention; even giving up comfort, a woman’s husband is her deity.

BP 1.13.35

भर्ताधिदेवता नार्या वर्णा ब्राह्मदेवताः ।
ब्राह्मणा ह्यग्निदेवास्तु प्रजा राजन्यदेवताः ॥

A husband is a woman’s deity; Brahmā is the deity of the classes; Agni is the deity of Brahmins; and the king is the deity of his subjects.

BP 1.13.36

तासां त्रिवर्गसंसिद्धौ प्रदिष्टं कारणद्वयम् ।
भर्तुर्यदनुकूलत्वं यच्च शीलमविप्लुतम् ॥

For women’s attainment of the three aims, two causes are prescribed: harmony with the husband and unwavering good conduct.

BP 1.13.37

न तथा यौवनं लोके नापि रूपं न भूषणम् ।
यथा प्रियानुकूलत्वं सिद्धं शश्वदनौषधम् ॥

In the world, neither youth, beauty, nor ornament is as [effective] as harmony with the beloved; it is a medicine that always succeeds.

BP 1.13.38

वयोरूपादिहारिण्यो दृश्यन्ते दुर्भगाः स्त्रियः ।
वल्लभा मन्दरूपाश्च बह्व्यो गलितयौवनाः ॥

Women who have youth, beauty, and the like are seen to be unfortunate; many who are less beautiful and past their youth are beloved.

BP 1.13.39

तस्मात्प्रियत्वं लोकानां निदानं योग्यतापरम् ।
तां विनान्ये गुणा वन्ध्याः सर्वेऽनर्थकृतोऽपि वा ॥

Therefore suitability is the highest cause of being loved by people. Without it, all other virtues are fruitless or even harmful.

BP 1.13.40

तस्मात्सर्वप्रयत्नेन विदध्यादात्मयोग्यताम् ।
परचित्तज्ञता चास्या मूलं सर्वक्रियास्विह ॥

Therefore she should strive in every way to make herself suitable; understanding another’s mind is the root of all her actions here.

BP 1.13.41

बहिरागच्छतो ज्ञात्वा कालं संमृज्य भूमिकाम् ।
सज्जीकृतासना तिष्ठेत्तस्याज्ञां प्रतितत्परा ॥

Knowing the time her husband will return from outside, she should sweep the floor, have a seat prepared, and wait, intent on his command.

BP 1.13.42

स्वयं प्रक्षालयेत्पादावुत्थाप्य परिचारिकाम् ।
तालवृन्तादिकैः कुर्याच्छमस्वेदापनोदनम् ॥

She should wash his feet herself, having called the attendant, and use a palm-leaf fan and the like to remove fatigue and perspiration.

BP 1.13.43

आहारस्नानपानादौ सस्पृहं यत्र लक्षयेत् ।
तदिंगितज्ञा तत्त्वेन सिद्धमस्मै निवेदयेत् ॥

When she notices his desire for food, bathing, drink, and the like, knowing his sign, she should give him what is prepared.

BP 1.13.44

सपन्तीपतिबन्धूनां भर्तृचित्तानुकूल्यतः ।
प्रतिपत्तिं प्रयुञ्जीत स्वबन्धूनां न वै तथा ॥

According to her husband’s inclination, she should show proper conduct toward co-wives and his relatives, but not in the same way toward her own relatives.

BP 1.13.45

तेषु चात्मनि च ज्ञात्वा भर्तृचित्तं प्रसादयेत् ।
प्रतिपत्तिं तथाप्येषां नाद्रियेत स्वबन्धुषु ॥

Having understood his feelings toward them and herself, she should please him; even so, she should not favor her own relatives over his.

BP 1.13.46

अपि भर्तुरभिप्रेतं नारी तत्कुलवासिनी ।
सत्कारैर्निजबन्धूनां तेन नोपैति वाच्यताम् ॥

Even if approved by her husband, a woman living in his family should honor her own relatives in such a way that she does not incur blame.

BP 1.13.47

पूज्य एव हि सम्बन्धः सर्वावस्थासु योषिताम् ।
कस्ततोऽप्युपकारांशं लिप्सेत कुलजः पुमान् ॥

A relationship by marriage is always worthy of honor for women. What well-born man would seek even a share of benefit from it?

BP 1.13.48

सम्पूज्य स्वसुतां तस्मै विधिवत्प्रतिपाद्यते ।
ततोऽस्या लिप्सते नाम किमकार्यमतः परम् ॥

Having honored his daughter and duly given her to that man, if he then seeks gain from her, what wrong could be greater than this?

BP 1.13.49

कन्यां प्रदाय यैर्वृत्तिरात्मनः परिकल्प्यते ।
दासभण्डनटादीनां मार्गोऽयं न महात्मनाम् ॥

Those who give a daughter and then arrange their livelihood through her follow the way of servants, hired entertainers, and actors, not of noble people.

BP 1.13.50

तस्मात्स्त्रीबांधवा नित्यं प्रीतिमात्रैकसाधिनीम् ।
प्रतिपत्तिं समादद्युः सम्बन्धिभ्यः प्रसंगिनीम् ।

Therefore the woman’s relatives should always accept from her marriage relations only courtesies that bring affection.

BP 1.13.51

तस्या भर्तरि रक्षेत प्रीतिं लोके च वाच्यताम् ।
आत्मनोऽसत्प्रवादं च चेष्टेरन्साधुवृत्तयः ॥

People of good conduct should strive to preserve her affection for her husband, her good name in the world, and freedom from false blame upon their own family.

BP 1.13.52

एवं विज्ञाय सद्वृत्तं स्त्री वर्तेत तथा सदा ।
येन तत्परिवर्गस्य भवेद्भर्तुश्च सम्मता ॥

Knowing good conduct thus, a woman should always behave so that she is approved by her husband and his whole circle.

BP 1.13.53

प्रियापि साधुवृत्तापि विख्याताभिजनापि च ।
जनापवादात्सम्प्राप सीतानर्थं सुदारुणम् ॥

Though beloved, of good conduct, and of a renowned family, Sītā suffered terrible misfortune through public accusation.

BP 1.13.54

सर्वस्यामिषभूतत्वाद्गुणदोषानभिज्ञतः ।
प्रायेणाविनयौचित्यात्स्त्रीणां वृत्तं हि दुष्करम् ॥

Because women are objects of desire for everyone, because people do not know virtue and fault, and because improper conduct is common, women’s conduct is difficult.

BP 1.13.55

अगृह्यत्वान्मनोवृत्तेः प्रायः कपटदर्शनात् ।
निरङ्कुशत्वाल्लोकस्य निर्वाच्या विरलाः स्त्रियः ॥

Because the mind’s course cannot be grasped, appearances are often deceptive, and the world is uncontrolled, women who escape blame are few.

BP 1.13.56

दैवयोगादयोगत्वाद्व्यवहारानभिज्ञतः ।
वाच्यतापत्तयो दृष्टाः स्त्रीणां शुद्धेऽपि चेतसि ॥

Through fate, unsuitable circumstances, and ignorance of social dealings, women are blamed even when their minds are pure.

BP 1.13.57

तासां दैवप्रतीकारो नोपभोगादृते भवेत् ।
चारित्रं लोकवृत्तं च एतयोर्विदुरौषधम् ॥

For them, there is no remedy for fate except experiencing it; character and conduct in the world are known to the wise as remedies for these two.

BP 1.13.58

हिंदोलकादिक्रीडायां प्रसक्तां तरुणीं निशि ।
रममाणां विटैः सार्धं विधवां स्वैरचारिणीम् ॥

A young widow, roaming freely at night, absorbed in swinging and the like, sporting with libertines—

BP 1.13.59

वृद्धादिभार्यां सज्जायां यानगेयादिसंगिनीम् ।
कः श्रद्दध्यात्सतीत्येवं साध्वीमपि हि योषितम् ॥

—or the wife of an old man, dressed up and given to travel, singing, and the like: who would believe such a woman chaste, even if she were virtuous?

BP 1.13.60

यौ चासामिङ्गिताकारौ सन्दिग्धार्थप्रसाधकौ ।
तयोस्तत्त्वपरिज्ञानं विषयो योगिनां यदि ॥

If the truth of women’s gestures and appearances, which can be ambiguous, is a subject for yogins—

BP 1.13.61

तस्माद्यथोक्तमाचारमनुतिष्ठेत्सुसंयता ।
मिथ्यालग्नोप्यसद्वादः कम्पयत्येव तत्कुलम् ॥

—therefore, fully self-controlled, she should follow the conduct already stated. Even a false accusation, once attached, shakes her family.

BP 1.13.62

त्रिकुल्या वाच्यता रक्ष्या प्रतिष्ठाप्यथ सन्ततिः ।
भर्तुस्त्रिवर्गसिद्धिश्च साध्यं तत्कुलयोषिताम् ॥

Women of good families should guard the reputation of three families, establish offspring, and accomplish the three aims of their husband.

BP 1.13.63

पातयन्त्येव दौःशील्यादात्मानं सकुलत्रयम् ।
उद्धरन्ति तदैवैताः स्त्रियश्चारित्रभूषणाः ॥

Through bad conduct women bring themselves and their three families down; adorned with character, they raise them up.

BP 1.13.64

भर्तृचित्तानुकूलत्वं यासां शीलमविच्युतम् ।
तासां रत्नसुवर्णादि भार एव न मण्डनम् ॥

For those whose character never departs from harmony with their husband’s mind, jewels, gold, and the like are only a burden, not an adornment.

BP 1.13.65

लोकज्ञाने परा कोटिः पत्यौ भक्तिश्च शाश्वती ।
शुद्धान्वयानां नारीणां विद्यादेतत्कुलव्रतम् ॥

For women of pure lineage, the highest worldly wisdom and everlasting devotion to the husband are to be known as the family vow.

BP 1.13.66

तस्माल्लोकश्च भर्ता च सम्यगाराधितो यया ।
धर्ममर्थं च कामं च सैवाप्नोति निरत्यया ॥

Therefore, she who rightly honors both society and her husband attains without failure righteousness, wealth, and desire.
