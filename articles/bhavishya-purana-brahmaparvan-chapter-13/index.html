Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 13 — Household Conduct and Family Reputation

BP 1.13.1

ब्रह्मोवाच
प्रथमं प्रतिबुध्येत प्रवर्तेत स्वकर्मसु ।
पश्चाद्भृत्यजनस्यापि भुञ्जीत च शयीत च ॥

Brahmā said: ‘She should be the first to awaken and begin her own duties; only after attending to the servants and other household members should she eat and sleep.

Word-for-word
ब्रह्मा उवाच (brahmā uvāca) — Brahmā said;
प्रथमम् प्रतिबुध्येत (prathamam pratibudhyeta) — should awaken first;
प्रवर्तेत स्व-कर्मसु (pravarteta sva-karmasu) — begin own duties;
पश्चात् भृत्य-जनस्य अपि (paścāt bhṛtya-janasya api) — afterward concerning servants also;
भुञ्जीत च शयीत च (bhuñjīta ca śayīta ca) — should eat and sleep
Transliteration

BP 1.13.2

A woman separated from her husband, especially while with her parents-in-law, should not cross the threshold, even in a great emergency.

BP 1.13.3

उत्थाय प्रथमं भर्तुरविज्ञाता न निष्क्रमेत् ।
क्षपायां सावशेषायां रात्रौ वा वासरादिषु ॥

After rising first, she should not leave without her husband’s knowledge while any part of the night remains, during the night itself, or at the beginning of the day.

क्षपायाम् स-अवशेषायाम् (kṣapāyām sa-avaśeṣāyām) — while night remains;
रात्रौ वा (rātrau vā) — or at night;
वासर-आदिषु (vāsara-ādiṣu) — at beginning of day
Transliteration
