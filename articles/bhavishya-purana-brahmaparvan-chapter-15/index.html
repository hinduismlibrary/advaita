Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 15 — Conduct, Care and Traditional Massage

BP 1.15.1

ब्रह्मोवाच
दुर्भगा च पुनर्नित्यमुपवासादितत्परा ।
बाह्येषु पतिकृत्येषु स्याद्विशेषाभियोगिनी ॥

Brahmā said: ‘A wife who lacks her husband’s favour should remain continually intent upon fasts and similar observances and apply herself especially to his outward duties and affairs.

Word-for-word
ब्रह्मा उवाच (brahmā uvāca) — Brahmā said;
दुर्भगा (durbhagā) — wife lacking favour;
पुनः नित्यम् (punaḥ nityam) — continually;
उपवास-आदि-तत्परा (upavāsa-ādi-tatparā) — intent on fasts and others;
बाह्येषु पति-कृत्येषु (bāhyeṣu pati-kṛtyeṣu) — in husband’s outward duties;
स्यात् विशेष-अभियोगिनी (syāt viśeṣa-abhiyoginī) — should apply herself especially
Transliteration
brahmovāca
durbhagā ca punarnityamupavāsāditatparā |
bāhyeṣu patikṛtyeṣu syādviśeṣābhiyoginī | | 1

BP 1.15.2

न प्रशंसां सपत्नीषु निंदां चापि तथात्मनि ।
असूयां भर्तुरीर्ष्यां वा प्रणयं वापि दर्शयेत् ॥

She should not display praise of co-wives, self-condemnation, resentment toward her husband, jealousy, or possessive familiarity.

Word-for-word
न प्रशंसाम् सपत्नीषु (na praśaṃsām sapatnīṣu) — not praise regarding co-wives;
निन्दाम् च अपि तथा आत्मनि (nindām ca api tathā ātmani) — nor condemnation of herself;
असूयाम् भर्तुः (asūyām bhartuḥ) — resentment toward husband;
ईर्ष्याम् वा (īrṣyām vā) — or jealousy;
प्रणयम् वा अपि (praṇayam vā api) — or possessive familiarity;
दर्शयेत् (darśayet) — should display
Transliteration
na praśaṃsāṃ sapatnīṣu niṃdāṃ cāpi tathātmani |
asūyāṃ bharturīrṣyāṃ vā praṇayaṃ vāpi darśayet | | 2

BP 1.15.3

मद्विधा या हि बह्वेतत्तच्चात्त्यंतिकमश्नुते ।
यदस्या युष्मतो यायाद्भार्याशब्दाभिधेयताम् ॥

A woman who endures much in this manner attains the highest result expressed here: that, in relation to you, she continues to deserve the designation “wife.”

Word-for-word
मद्-विधा या हि (mad-vidhā yā hi) — woman of this kind;
बहु एतत् (bahu etat) — much of this;
तत् च आत्यन्तिकम् अश्नुते (tat ca ātyantikam aśnute) — attains that ultimate result;
यत् अस्याः (yat asyāḥ) — whereby for her;
युष्मतः (yuṣmataḥ) — in relation to you;
यायात् (yāyāt) — may come;
भार्या-शब्द-अभिधेयताम् (bhāryā-śabda-abhidheyatām) — designation by word wife
Transliteration
madvidhā yā hi bahvetattaccāttyaṃtikamaśnute |
yadasyā yuṣmato yāyādbhāryāśabdābhidheyatām | | 3

BP 1.15.4

न च निर्भूषणा तिष्ठेन्न चाप्युद्धतभूषणा ।
नान्यदा गंधमाल्यादि ग्राह्यं पत्युपचारतः ॥

She should remain neither without ornament nor excessively adorned. Fragrance, garlands, and similar things should be taken at other times only in connection with attending upon her husband.

Word-for-word
न च निर्भूषणा तिष्ठेत् (na ca nirbhūṣaṇā tiṣṭhet) — should not remain unadorned;
न च अपि उद्धत-भूषणा (na ca api uddhata-bhūṣaṇā) — nor excessively adorned;
न अन्यदा (na anyadā) — not at other time;
गन्ध-माल्य-आदि ग्राह्यम् (gandha-mālya-ādi grāhyam) — fragrance, garlands and others to be taken;
पति-उपचारतः (pati-upacārataḥ) — for service of husband
Transliteration
na ca nirbhūṣaṇā tiṣṭhenna cāpyuddhatabhūṣaṇā |
nānyadā gaṃdhamālyādi grāhyaṃ patyupacārataḥ | | 4

BP 1.15.5

तन्न्यूनं सर्वशो ग्राह्यं वल्लभाया विशेषतः ।
भूषणं गन्धमाल्यं तु तावत्कालमलक्षितम् ॥

In every respect she should accept less than the favoured wife, especially regarding ornaments, fragrance, and garlands, and should keep these unobtrusive for the appropriate period.

Word-for-word
तत्-न्यूनम् सर्वशः ग्राह्यम् (tat-nyūnam sarvaśaḥ grāhyam) — should take less than that in every respect;
वल्लभायाः विशेषतः (vallabhāyāḥ viśeṣataḥ) — especially than favoured wife;
भूषणम् (bhūṣaṇam) — ornament;
गन्ध-माल्यम् तु (gandha-mālyam tu) — fragrance and garland;
तावत्-कालम् अलक्षितम् (tāvat-kālam alakṣitam) — unobtrusive for that time
Transliteration
tannyūnaṃ sarvaśo grāhyaṃ vallabhāyā viśeṣataḥ |
bhūṣaṇaṃ gandhamālyaṃ tu tāvatkālamalakṣitam | | 5

BP 1.15.6

सम्बाधानां प्रदेशानां नित्यं स्वेदादिमार्जनम् ।
दन्तनासादिपङ्कानां विगन्धस्य च शोधनम् ॥

She should continually cleanse perspiration from confined parts of the body, remove deposits from the teeth, nose, and similar places, and eliminate unpleasant odour.

Word-for-word
सम्बाधानाम् प्रदेशानाम् (sambādhānām pradeśānām) — of confined body regions;
नित्यम् स्वेद-आदि-मार्जनम् (nityam sveda-ādi-mārjanam) — continual cleansing of perspiration and others;
दन्त-नास-आदि-पङ्कानाम् (danta-nāsa-ādi-paṅkānām) — deposits of teeth, nose and others;
विगन्धस्य च (vigandhasya ca) — and unpleasant odour;
शोधनम् (śodhanam) — removal
Transliteration
sambādhānāṃ pradeśānāṃ nityaṃ svedādimārjanam |
dantanāsādipaṅkānāṃ vigandhasya ca śodhanam | | 6

BP 1.15.7

निमित्तं भर्तुरेतासां यत्किंचिदभिलक्षयेत् ।
नानेन वा तयोर्यत्नं विदध्यादङ्गमार्जने ॥

Whatever indication she notices that her husband regards as a reason for these attentions, she should apply effort accordingly to cleansing and grooming her body.

Word-for-word
निमित्तम् भर्तुः (nimittam bhartuḥ) — indication from husband;
एतासाम् (etāsām) — concerning these;
यत् किञ्चित् अभिलक्षयेत् (yat kiñcit abhilakṣayet) — whatever she notices;
अनेन वा (anena vā) — by this;
तयोः यत्नम् विदध्यात् (tayoḥ yatnam vidadhyāt) — should make effort concerning them;
अङ्ग-मार्जने (aṅga-mārjane) — in body cleansing
Transliteration
nimittaṃ bharturetāsāṃ yatkiṃcidabhilakṣayet |
nānena vā tayoryatnaṃ vidadhyādaṅgamārjane | | 7

BP 1.15.8

सर्वासां च सपत्नीनां सर्वत्रानुगता भवेत् ।
वैतसीं वृत्तिमास्थाय वल्लभाया विशेषतः ॥

She should accommodate all her co-wives in every matter, adopting the yielding manner of a reed, especially toward the favoured wife.

Word-for-word
सर्वासाम् च सपत्नीनाम् (sarvāsām ca sapatnīnām) — of all co-wives;
सर्वत्र अनुगता भवेत् (sarvatra anugatā bhavet) — should be accommodating everywhere;
वैतसीम् वृत्तिम् आस्थाय (vaitasīm vṛttim āsthāya) — adopting conduct of a reed;
वल्लभायाः विशेषतः (vallabhāyāḥ viśeṣataḥ) — especially toward favoured wife
Transliteration
sarvāsāṃ ca sapatnīnāṃ sarvatrānugatā bhavet |
vaitasīṃ vṛttimāsthāya vallabhāyā viśeṣataḥ | | 8

BP 1.15.9

अन्यस्या यदनुष्ठेयं यन्न सीदेत्समर्पितम् ।
भर्तुश्चाविदितं यत्नात्तत्कुर्यादविरोधि चेत् ॥

If a task assigned to another wife would otherwise fail, she should carefully perform it—even without her husband’s knowledge—provided it creates no conflict.

Word-for-word
अन्यस्याः यत् अनुष्ठेयम् (anyasyāḥ yat anuṣṭheyam) — what should be performed by another;
यत् न सीदेत् समर्पितम् (yat na sīdet samarpitam) — so assigned task does not fail;
भर्तुः च अविदितम् (bhartuḥ ca aviditam) — unknown to husband;
यत्नात् तत् कुर्यात् (yatnāt tat kuryāt) — should carefully do it;
अविरोधि चेत् (avirodhi cet) — if not conflicting
Transliteration
anyasyā yadanuṣṭheyaṃ yanna sīdetsamarpitam |
bhartuścāviditaṃ yatnāttatkuryādavirodhi cet | | 9

BP 1.15.10

कोशवस्त्रान्नताम्बूलगन्धापानौषधादिकम् ।
तत्सर्वमनियुक्तानां दोषवत्वाद्विरुध्यते ॥

The treasury, clothing, food, betel, perfumes, drink, medicine, and similar stores should not be handled by persons who have not been appointed, because unauthorized handling is considered faulty.

Word-for-word
कोश (kośa) — treasury;
वस्त्र (vastra) — clothing;
अन्न (anna) — food;
ताम्बूल (tāmbūla) — betel;
गन्ध (gandha) — perfume;
पान (pāna) — drink;
औषध-आदिकम् (auṣadha-ādikam) — medicine and others;
तत् सर्वम् (tat sarvam) — all that;
अनियुक्तानाम् (aniyuktānām) — of unauthorized persons;
दोषवत् (doṣavat) — faulty;
विरुध्यते (virudhyate) — is prohibited
Transliteration
kośavastrānnatāmbūlagandhāpānauṣadhādikam |
tatsarvamaniyuktānāṃ doṣavatvādvirudhyate | | 1.15.10

BP 1.15.11

यत्तु मुक्तमनुष्ठेयं गहसम्मार्जनादिकम् ।
स्त्रीणामनधिकारेऽपि प्रायस्तद्विधिरुच्यते ॥

Yet unassigned duties that plainly need doing, such as sweeping the house, are generally prescribed for women even where they possess no separate authority over stores.

Word-for-word
यत् तु मुक्तम् अनुष्ठेयम् (yat tu muktam anuṣṭheyam) — what is left free to perform;
गृह-संमार्जन-आदिकम् (gṛha-saṃmārjana-ādikam) — house sweeping and others;
स्त्रीणाम् अनधिकारे अपि (strīṇām anadhikāre api) — even without women’s authority;
प्रायः तत्-विधिः उच्यते (prāyaḥ tat-vidhiḥ ucyate) — that rule is generally stated
Transliteration
yattu muktamanuṣṭheyaṃ gahasammārjanādikam |
strīṇāmanadhikāre'pi prāyastadvidhirucyate | | 11

BP 1.15.12

अभ्यङ्गोद्वर्तनं स्नानं भोजनं मण्डनानि च ।
कुर्याद्भर्तुरपत्यानां धात्रीकर्माणि सादरम् ॥

She should respectfully perform the duties of a nurse for her husband’s children: oiling, rubbing with cleansing paste, bathing, feeding, adorning, and similar care.

Word-for-word
अभ्यङ्ग (abhyaṅga) — oiling;
उद्वर्तनम् (udvartanam) — rubbing with paste;
स्नानम् (snānam) — bathing;
भोजनम् (bhojanam) — feeding;
मण्डनानि च (maṇḍanāni ca) — and adornment;
कुर्यात् (kuryāt) — should perform;
भर्तुः अपत्यानाम् (bhartuḥ apatyānām) — for husband’s children;
धात्री-कर्माणि (dhātrī-karmāṇi) — nurse’s duties;
स-आदरम् (sa-ādaram) — respectfully
Transliteration
abhyaṅgodvartanaṃ snānaṃ bhojanaṃ maṇḍanāni ca |
kuryādbharturapatyānāṃ dhātrīkarmāṇi sādaram | | 12

BP 1.15.13

आत्मवत्तान्यपत्यानि साधयत्यनुयोगतः ।
स्वेनाप्यमीषां वित्तेन विदध्यान्मण्डनादिकम् ॥

When entrusted with them, she should care for those children as her own and may use even her personal property to provide their adornment and other needs.

Word-for-word
आत्म-वत् तानि अपत्यानि (ātma-vat tāni apatyāni) — those children like own;
साधयति अनुयोगतः (sādhayati anuyogataḥ) — should care for when entrusted;
स्वेन अपि (svena api) — even with her own;
अमीषाम् वित्तेन (amīṣām vittena) — property for them;
विदध्यात् मण्डन-आदिकम् (vidadhyāt maṇḍana-ādikam) — should provide adornment and others
Transliteration
ātmavattānyapatyāni sādhayatyanuyogataḥ |
svenāpyamīṣāṃ vittena vidadhyānmaṇḍanādikam | | 13

BP 1.15.14

भोगः स्वयमपत्यैर्वा स्त्रीवित्तस्य पतिर्विधा ।
पूर्वे वयस्यभिनन्द्य पश्चिमे चोपयोजनम् ॥

The husband directs the use of a wife’s property, whether for himself or the children. Its possession is welcomed in the earlier stage of life and its practical use belongs to the later stage.

Word-for-word
भोगः स्वयम् अपत्यैः वा (bhogaḥ svayam apatyaiḥ vā) — use by himself or children;
स्त्री-वित्तस्य (strī-vittasya) — of wife’s property;
पतिः विधा (patiḥ vidhā) — husband directs;
पूर्वे वयसि अभिनन्द्यम् (pūrve vayasi abhinandyam) — welcomed in earlier age;
पश्चिमे च उपयोजनम् (paścime ca upayojanam) — and use in later age
Transliteration
bhogaḥ svayamapatyairvā strīvittasya patirvidhā |
pūrve vayasyabhinandya paścime copayojanam | | 14

BP 1.15.15

उभयोगस्तु वा मा वा कर्मजः पृथगेव सः ।
सद्वृत्ते त्वधिकां ख्यातिं कुर्वीत क्रियया पुनः ॥

Whether the two spouses are united in enjoyment or not is separately shaped by past action; through renewed conduct, however, she should create a greater reputation for good character.

Word-for-word
उभयोः योगः तु (ubhayoḥ yogaḥ tu) — union of both;
वा मा वा (vā mā vā) — whether or not;
कर्म-जः (karma-jaḥ) — born of action;
पृथक् एव सः (pṛthak eva saḥ) — that is separate;
सद्-वृत्ते तु (sad-vṛtte tu) — in good conduct;
अधिकाम् ख्यातिम् कुर्वीत (adhikām khyātim kurvīta) — should create greater reputation;
क्रियया पुनः (kriyayā punaḥ) — through action again
Transliteration
ubhayogastu vā mā vā karmajaḥ pṛthageva saḥ |
sadvṛtte tvadhikāṃ khyātiṃ kurvīta kriyayā punaḥ | | 15

BP 1.15.16

न कापि दुर्भगा नाम सुभगा नाम जातितः ।
व्यवहाराद्भवत्येष निर्देशो रिपुमित्रवत् ॥

No woman is by birth inherently “unfortunate” or “fortunate” in love. Such descriptions arise from conduct and relationship, just like the terms enemy and friend.

Word-for-word
न का अपि दुर्भगा नाम (na kā api durbhagā nāma) — no one inherently unfortunate;
सुभगा नाम जातितः (subhagā nāma jātitaḥ) — nor fortunate by birth;
व्यवहारात् भवति (vyavahārāt bhavati) — arises from conduct;
एष निर्देशः (eṣa nirdeśaḥ) — this designation;
रिपु-मित्र-वत् (ripu-mitra-vat) — like enemy and friend
Transliteration
na kāpi durbhagā nāma subhagā nāma jātitaḥ |
vyavahārādbhavatyeṣa nirdeśo ripumitravat | | 16

BP 1.15.17

भर्तृचित्तापरिज्ञानादननुष्ठानतोऽपि वा।
वृत्तैर्लोकविरुद्धैश्च यान्ति दुर्भगतां स्त्रियः ॥

Women become unfavoured through failing to understand the husband’s mind, failing to act accordingly, or adopting patterns of conduct opposed to accepted social practice.

Word-for-word
भर्तृ-चित्त-अपरिज्ञानात् (bhartṛ-citta-aparijñānāt) — from not understanding husband’s mind;
अननुष्ठानतः अपि वा (ananuṣṭhānataḥ api vā) — or failure to act;
वृत्तैः लोक-विरुद्धैः च (vṛttaiḥ loka-viruddhaiḥ ca) — by conduct opposed to society;
यान्ति दुर्भगताम् स्त्रियः (yānti durbhagatām striyaḥ) — women become unfavoured
Transliteration
bhartṛcittāparijñānādananuṣṭhānato'pi vā|
vṛttairlokaviruddhaiśca yānti durbhagatāṃ striyaḥ | | 17

BP 1.15.18

प्रानुकूल्यान्मनोवृत्तैः परोऽपि प्रियतां व्रजेत् ।
प्रातिकूल्यान्निजोप्याशु प्रियः प्रद्वेषतामियात् ॥

Through harmony of mind and conduct even an outsider becomes dear; through opposition, even one’s own beloved quickly becomes an object of aversion.

Word-for-word
प्रानुकूल्यात् मनः-वृत्तैः (prānukūlyāt manaḥ-vṛttaiḥ) — through harmony in mind and conduct;
परः अपि प्रियताम् व्रजेत् (paraḥ api priyatām vrajet) — even outsider becomes dear;
प्रातिकूल्यात् (prātikūlyāt) — through opposition;
निजः अपि आशु प्रियः (nijaḥ api āśu priyaḥ) — even own beloved quickly;
प्रद्वेषताम् इयात् (pradveṣatām iyāt) — becomes hated
Transliteration
prānukūlyānmanovṛttaiḥ paro'pi priyatāṃ vrajet |
prātikūlyānnijopyāśu priyaḥ pradveṣatāmiyāt | | 18

BP 1.15.19

तस्मात्सर्वास्ववस्थासु मनोवाक्कायकर्मभिः ।
प्रियं समाचरेन्नित्यं तच्चितानुविधायिनी ॥

Therefore in every circumstance she should continually do what is pleasing through mind, speech, body, and action, conforming herself to his state of mind.

Word-for-word
तस्मात् (tasmāt) — therefore;
सर्वासु अवस्थासु (sarvāsu avasthāsu) — in every circumstance;
मनः-वाक्-काय-कर्मभिः (manaḥ-vāk-kāya-karmabhiḥ) — through mind, speech, body and action;
प्रियम् समाचरेत् नित्यम् (priyam samācaret nityam) — should always do what is pleasing;
तत्-चित्त-अनुविधायिनी (tat-citta-anuvidhāyinī) — conforming to his mind
Transliteration
tasmātsarvāsvavasthāsu manovākkāyakarmabhiḥ |

BP 1.15.20
