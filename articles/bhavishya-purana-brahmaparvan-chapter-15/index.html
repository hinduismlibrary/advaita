Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 15 — Conduct, Care and Traditional Massage

BP 1.15.1

ब्रह्मोवाच
दुर्भगा च पुनर्नित्यमुपवासादितत्परा ।
बाह्येषु पतिकृत्येषु स्याद्विशेषाभियोगिनी ॥

Brahmā said: ‘A wife who lacks her husband’s favour should remain continually intent upon fasts and similar observances and apply herself especially to his outward duties and affairs.

BP 1.15.2

न प्रशंसां सपत्नीषु निंदां चापि तथात्मनि ।
असूयां भर्तुरीर्ष्यां वा प्रणयं वापि दर्शयेत् ॥

She should not display praise of co-wives, self-condemnation, resentment toward her husband, jealousy, or possessive familiarity.

BP 1.15.3

मद्विधा या हि बह्वेतत्तच्चात्त्यंतिकमश्नुते ।
यदस्या युष्मतो यायाद्भार्याशब्दाभिधेयताम् ॥

A woman who endures much in this manner attains the highest result expressed here: that, in relation to you, she continues to deserve the designation “wife.”

BP 1.15.4

न च निर्भूषणा तिष्ठेन्न चाप्युद्धतभूषणा ।
नान्यदा गंधमाल्यादि ग्राह्यं पत्युपचारतः ॥

She should remain neither without ornament nor excessively adorned. Fragrance, garlands, and similar things should be taken at other times only in connection with attending upon her husband.

BP 1.15.5

तन्न्यूनं सर्वशो ग्राह्यं वल्लभाया विशेषतः ।
भूषणं गन्धमाल्यं तु तावत्कालमलक्षितम् ॥

In every respect she should accept less than the favoured wife, especially regarding ornaments, fragrance, and garlands, and should keep these unobtrusive for the appropriate period.

BP 1.15.6

सम्बाधानां प्रदेशानां नित्यं स्वेदादिमार्जनम् ।
दन्तनासादिपङ्कानां विगन्धस्य च शोधनम् ॥

She should continually cleanse perspiration from confined parts of the body, remove deposits from the teeth, nose, and similar places, and eliminate unpleasant odour.

BP 1.15.7

निमित्तं भर्तुरेतासां यत्किंचिदभिलक्षयेत् ।
नानेन वा तयोर्यत्नं विदध्यादङ्गमार्जने ॥

Whatever indication she notices that her husband regards as a reason for these attentions, she should apply effort accordingly to cleansing and grooming her body.

BP 1.15.8

सर्वासां च सपत्नीनां सर्वत्रानुगता भवेत् ।
वैतसीं वृत्तिमास्थाय वल्लभाया विशेषतः ॥

She should accommodate all her co-wives in every matter, adopting the yielding manner of a reed, especially toward the favoured wife.

BP 1.15.9

अन्यस्या यदनुष्ठेयं यन्न सीदेत्समर्पितम् ।
भर्तुश्चाविदितं यत्नात्तत्कुर्यादविरोधि चेत् ॥

If a task assigned to another wife would otherwise fail, she should carefully perform it—even without her husband’s knowledge—provided it creates no conflict.

BP 1.15.10

कोशवस्त्रान्नताम्बूलगन्धापानौषधादिकम् ।
तत्सर्वमनियुक्तानां दोषवत्वाद्विरुध्यते ॥

The treasury, clothing, food, betel, perfumes, drink, medicine, and similar stores should not be handled by persons who have not been appointed, because unauthorized handling is considered faulty.

BP 1.15.11

यत्तु मुक्तमनुष्ठेयं गहसम्मार्जनादिकम् ।
स्त्रीणामनधिकारेऽपि प्रायस्तद्विधिरुच्यते ॥

Yet unassigned duties that plainly need doing, such as sweeping the house, are generally prescribed for women even where they possess no separate authority over stores.

BP 1.15.12

अभ्यङ्गोद्वर्तनं स्नानं भोजनं मण्डनानि च ।
कुर्याद्भर्तुरपत्यानां धात्रीकर्माणि सादरम् ॥

She should respectfully perform the duties of a nurse for her husband’s children: oiling, rubbing with cleansing paste, bathing, feeding, adorning, and similar care.

BP 1.15.13

आत्मवत्तान्यपत्यानि साधयत्यनुयोगतः ।
स्वेनाप्यमीषां वित्तेन विदध्यान्मण्डनादिकम् ॥

When entrusted with them, she should care for those children as her own and may use even her personal property to provide their adornment and other needs.

BP 1.15.14

भोगः स्वयमपत्यैर्वा स्त्रीवित्तस्य पतिर्विधा ।
पूर्वे वयस्यभिनन्द्य पश्चिमे चोपयोजनम् ॥

The husband directs the use of a wife’s property, whether for himself or the children. Its possession is welcomed in the earlier stage of life and its practical use belongs to the later stage.

BP 1.15.15

उभयोगस्तु वा मा वा कर्मजः पृथगेव सः ।
सद्वृत्ते त्वधिकां ख्यातिं कुर्वीत क्रियया पुनः ॥

Whether the two spouses are united in enjoyment or not is separately shaped by past action; through renewed conduct, however, she should create a greater reputation for good character.

BP 1.15.16

न कापि दुर्भगा नाम सुभगा नाम जातितः ।
व्यवहाराद्भवत्येष निर्देशो रिपुमित्रवत् ॥

No woman is by birth inherently “unfortunate” or “fortunate” in love. Such descriptions arise from conduct and relationship, just like the terms enemy and friend.

BP 1.15.17

भर्तृचित्तापरिज्ञानादननुष्ठानतोऽपि वा।
वृत्तैर्लोकविरुद्धैश्च यान्ति दुर्भगतां स्त्रियः ॥

Women become unfavoured through failing to understand the husband’s mind, failing to act accordingly, or adopting patterns of conduct opposed to accepted social practice.

BP 1.15.18

प्रानुकूल्यान्मनोवृत्तैः परोऽपि प्रियतां व्रजेत् ।
प्रातिकूल्यान्निजोप्याशु प्रियः प्रद्वेषतामियात् ॥

Through harmony of mind and conduct even an outsider becomes dear; through opposition, even one’s own beloved quickly becomes an object of aversion.

BP 1.15.19

तस्मात्सर्वास्ववस्थासु मनोवाक्कायकर्मभिः ।
प्रियं समाचरेन्नित्यं तच्चितानुविधायिनी ॥

Therefore in every circumstance she should continually do what is pleasing through mind, speech, body, and action, conforming herself to his state of mind.

BP 1.15.20

यामन्यां कामयेत्तासां तं तया संप्रयोजयेत् ।
कुपितां च प्रियां काञ्चिद्यत्नादस्मै प्रसादयेत् ॥

If her husband desires another woman among them, she should help bring the two together; and if some beloved wife is angry, she should carefully reconcile her with him.

BP 1.15.21

तत्पादपरिचर्यायां गोत्रसंवाहने तथा ।
पीडने शिरसश्चैव परं कौशलमभ्यसेत् ॥

She should cultivate exceptional skill in attending to his feet, massaging the limbs, and applying pressure to the head.

BP 1.15.22

पीडनं मृदु मध्यं च गात्रावस्थाविशेषतः ।
मुखगात्रादिभिर्लिङ्गैः प्रयोज्यं तत्सुखावहम् ॥

Pressure may be gentle or medium according to the particular condition of the body; signs from the face, limbs, and other responses should guide what produces comfort.

BP 1.15.23

बाहूरुकटिपृष्ठेषु स्कंधे शिरसि पादयोः ।
गाढमर्दनमिच्छन्ति प्रायोन्यत्रापि मध्यमम् ॥

Firm pressure is generally desired upon the arms, thighs, waist, back, shoulders, head, and feet; elsewhere medium pressure is usually preferred.

BP 1.15.24

निर्मांसेषु प्रदेशेषु नाभिमूलेषु मर्मसु ।
हृद्गण्डककपोलादाविच्छन्ति मृदुमर्दनम् ॥

Gentle massage is desired over fleshless or delicate regions, around the navel and vital points, and in areas such as the heart, jaw, and cheeks.

BP 1.15.25

गाढं जाग्रदवस्थायामर्धसुप्तस्य मध्यमम् ।
किञ्चित्सपरिघातं च मृदुसुप्तस्य नेति वा ॥

Firm pressure is suitable while he is awake, medium pressure when half asleep, and only a little light contact when gently sleeping—or none at all if it disturbs him.

BP 1.15.26

विरुद्धं सर्वगात्रेषु लोमवस्तु विशेषतः ।
उत्कण्डूयत्यु सोद्धर्षं स्नेहाक्तेषु च मर्द नम् ॥

Rubbing contrary to the direction of body hair is generally avoided, especially over delicate hair; scratching may be vigorous where there is itching, while oiled limbs may be massaged.

BP 1.15.27

स्पर्शाद्रोमाञ्चजननं सनखच्छुरितं शनैः ।
पुलकोल्लेखनोपेतं शिरःकंडूश्च पार्श्वयोः ॥

Touch may be used slowly to raise gooseflesh, together with light tracing by the nails, delicate stroking over the raised hairs, and scratching of the head and sides.

BP 1.15.28

तेषु तेषु च गात्रेषु तत्प्रयोज्यं तथातथा ।
निद्रागमाय तत्काले रागसंधुक्षणाय च ॥

These techniques should be applied to the various limbs in the appropriate ways, either to bring on sleep at that time or to kindle desire.

BP 1.15.29

तिष्ठतश्चोपविष्टस्य जाग्रतः स्वपतोऽपि वा ।
संवाहनं प्रशंसंति यदत्यर्थं सुखावहम् ॥

Massage is praised as exceedingly comforting whether a person is standing, seated, awake, or asleep.

BP 1.15.30

नैष्पन्द्यं पुलकोद्भेदो गात्राणामक्षिमीलनम् ।
तत्प्रदेशार्पणं किञ्चिद्बोधेद्विकृतिदर्शनम् ॥

Stillness, the arising of gooseflesh, relaxation of the limbs, closing of the eyes, and presenting a particular body region indicate through these changes where attention is desired.

BP 1.15.31

ऊरू मूलादिदेशे च तत्पाणिप्रतिपीडनम् ।
लक्षयेन्निपुणा यत्र तत्रैवाधिकमाचरेत् ॥

A skilled woman should observe where he presses her hand—such as at the roots of the thighs and related regions—and should give greater attention precisely there.

BP 1.15.32

एवमेव यथोद्दिष्टं स्त्रीवृत्तं यानुतिष्ठति ।
पतिमाराध्य सम्पूर्ण त्रिवर्गं साधिगच्छति ॥

A woman who follows the conduct exactly as set forth here, having pleased her husband, attains in full the three aims of dharma, wealth, and desire.
