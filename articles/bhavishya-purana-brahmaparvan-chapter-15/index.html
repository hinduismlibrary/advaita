Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 15 — Conduct, Care and Traditional Massage

BP 1.15.1

ब्रह्मोवाच
दुर्भगा च पुनर्नित्यमुपवासादितत्परा ।
बाह्येषु पतिकृत्येषु स्याद्विशेषाभियोगिनी ॥

Brahmā said: An unfavoured wife should always be intent on fasting and the like, and should be especially devoted to her husband’s outward affairs.

BP 1.15.2

न प्रशंसां सपत्नीषु निंदां चापि तथात्मनि ।
असूयां भर्तुरीर्ष्यां वा प्रणयं वापि दर्शयेत् ॥

She should show neither praise of her co-wives, nor self-blame, nor resentment, jealousy toward her husband, or excessive affection.

BP 1.15.3

मद्विधा या हि बह्वेतत्तच्चात्त्यंतिकमश्नुते ।
यदस्या युष्मतो यायाद्भार्याशब्दाभिधेयताम् ॥

A woman who is thus devoted attains the highest end, when she comes to be called your wife.

BP 1.15.4

न च निर्भूषणा तिष्ठेन्न चाप्युद्धतभूषणा ।
नान्यदा गंधमाल्यादि ग्राह्यं पत्युपचारतः ॥

She should be neither without ornaments nor adorned excessively; she should take fragrances, garlands, and the like only in serving her husband.

BP 1.15.5

तन्न्यूनं सर्वशो ग्राह्यं वल्लभाया विशेषतः ।
भूषणं गन्धमाल्यं तु तावत्कालमलक्षितम् ॥

Her ornaments, fragrances, garlands, and the like should in every respect be less than those of the favoured wife, and remain unnoticed for that time.

BP 1.15.6

सम्बाधानां प्रदेशानां नित्यं स्वेदादिमार्जनम् ।
दन्तनासादिपङ्कानां विगन्धस्य च शोधनम् ॥

She should always wipe perspiration from the concealed parts and clean deposits from the teeth, nose, and the like, and remove bad odour.

BP 1.15.7

निमित्तं भर्तुरेतासां यत्किंचिदभिलक्षयेत् ।
नानेन वा तयोर्यत्नं विदध्यादङ्गमार्जने ॥

Whatever sign of her husband’s need for these things she notices, she should accordingly make the effort to cleanse and groom her body.

BP 1.15.8

सर्वासां च सपत्नीनां सर्वत्रानुगता भवेत् ।
वैतसीं वृत्तिमास्थाय वल्लभाया विशेषतः ॥

She should follow all her co-wives in every matter, adopting the yielding manner of a reed, especially toward the favoured wife.

BP 1.15.9

अन्यस्या यदनुष्ठेयं यन्न सीदेत्समर्पितम् ।
भर्तुश्चाविदितं यत्नात्तत्कुर्यादविरोधि चेत् ॥

Whatever another wife ought to do, and whatever would otherwise be left undone, she should carefully do, if it does not cause conflict, without her husband’s knowledge.

BP 1.15.10

कोशवस्त्रान्नताम्बूलगन्धापानौषधादिकम् ।
तत्सर्वमनियुक्तानां दोषवत्वाद्विरुध्यते ॥

The treasury, clothes, food, betel, perfumes, drink, medicines, and the like are improper for those not assigned to them to handle.

BP 1.15.11

यत्तु मुक्तमनुष्ठेयं गहसम्मार्जनादिकम् ।
स्त्रीणामनधिकारेऽपि प्रायस्तद्विधिरुच्यते ॥

But what is left to be done, such as sweeping the house, is generally prescribed for women, even without authority.

BP 1.15.12

अभ्यङ्गोद्वर्तनं स्नानं भोजनं मण्डनानि च ।
कुर्याद्भर्तुरपत्यानां धात्रीकर्माणि सादरम् ॥

She should respectfully perform the nurse’s duties for her husband’s children: anointing, rubbing with cleansing paste, bathing, feeding, and adorning them.

BP 1.15.13

आत्मवत्तान्यपत्यानि साधयत्यनुयोगतः ।
स्वेनाप्यमीषां वित्तेन विदध्यान्मण्डनादिकम् ॥

She should care for the children as her own when entrusted with them; with her own wealth also, she should provide their adornments and the like.

BP 1.15.14

भोगः स्वयमपत्यैर्वा स्त्रीवित्तस्य पतिर्विधा ।
पूर्वे वयस्यभिनन्द्य पश्चिमे चोपयोजनम् ॥

The husband directs the use of a woman’s wealth, whether for himself or her children; it is to be valued in the earlier years and used in the later years.

BP 1.15.15

उभयोगस्तु वा मा वा कर्मजः पृथगेव सः ।
सद्वृत्ते त्वधिकां ख्यातिं कुर्वीत क्रियया पुनः ॥

Whether the two spouses are joined or not, that result is separately due to past action; through good conduct she should again gain greater renown by her actions.

BP 1.15.16

न कापि दुर्भगा नाम सुभगा नाम जातितः ।
व्यवहाराद्भवत्येष निर्देशो रिपुमित्रवत् ॥

No woman is by birth called unfortunate or fortunate; this designation comes from conduct, as do the designations enemy and friend.

BP 1.15.17

भर्तृचित्तापरिज्ञानादननुष्ठानतोऽपि वा।
वृत्तैर्लोकविरुद्धैश्च यान्ति दुर्भगतां स्त्रियः ॥

Women become unfortunate through not understanding their husband’s mind, through not acting accordingly, or through conduct opposed to people’s ways.

BP 1.15.18

प्रानुकूल्यान्मनोवृत्तैः परोऽपि प्रियतां व्रजेत् ।
प्रातिकूल्यान्निजोप्याशु प्रियः प्रद्वेषतामियात् ॥

Through a favourable disposition, even a stranger becomes dear; through opposition, even one’s own beloved quickly becomes an object of hatred.

BP 1.15.19

तस्मात्सर्वास्ववस्थासु मनोवाक्कायकर्मभिः ।
प्रियं समाचरेन्नित्यं तच्चितानुविधायिनी ॥

Therefore, in every circumstance, she should always act pleasingly through mind, speech, body, and deeds, following his state of mind.

BP 1.15.20

यामन्यां कामयेत्तासां तं तया संप्रयोजयेत् ।
कुपितां च प्रियां काञ्चिद्यत्नादस्मै प्रसादयेत् ॥

If he desires another of them, she should unite him with that woman; and if any beloved wife is angry, she should carefully reconcile her to him.

BP 1.15.21

तत्पादपरिचर्यायां गोत्रसंवाहने तथा ।
पीडने शिरसश्चैव परं कौशलमभ्यसेत् ॥

She should cultivate great skill in attending to his feet, rubbing his limbs, and pressing his head.

BP 1.15.22

पीडनं मृदु मध्यं च गात्रावस्थाविशेषतः ।
मुखगात्रादिभिर्लिङ्गैः प्रयोज्यं तत्सुखावहम् ॥

Pressure should be gentle or medium according to the state of the body; signs from the face, limbs, and the like should guide the pressure that gives comfort.

BP 1.15.23

बाहूरुकटिपृष्ठेषु स्कंधे शिरसि पादयोः ।
गाढमर्दनमिच्छन्ति प्रायोन्यत्रापि मध्यमम् ॥

Firm massage is generally desired on the arms, thighs, waist, back, shoulders, head, and feet; elsewhere, medium pressure is usually desired.

BP 1.15.24

निर्मांसेषु प्रदेशेषु नाभिमूलेषु मर्मसु ।
हृद्गण्डककपोलादाविच्छन्ति मृदुमर्दनम् ॥

Gentle massage is desired on fleshless parts, around the navel and vital points, and from the heart, jaw, and cheeks onward.

BP 1.15.25

गाढं जाग्रदवस्थायामर्धसुप्तस्य मध्यमम् ।
किञ्चित्सपरिघातं च मृदुसुप्तस्य नेति वा ॥

Massage should be firm while he is awake, medium while he is half-asleep, and light while he is lightly asleep, or not at all.

BP 1.15.26

विरुद्धं सर्वगात्रेषु लोमवस्तु विशेषतः ।
उत्कण्डूयत्यु सोद्धर्षं स्नेहाक्तेषु च मर्द नम् ॥

Rubbing against the hair is improper over the body, especially where there is body hair; scratch vigorously where itching arises, and massage the oiled parts.

BP 1.15.27

स्पर्शाद्रोमाञ्चजननं सनखच्छुरितं शनैः ।
पुलकोल्लेखनोपेतं शिरःकंडूश्च पार्श्वयोः ॥

Touch should slowly raise gooseflesh; scratch lightly with the nails, raise the hairs, and scratch the head and sides.

BP 1.15.28

तेषु तेषु च गात्रेषु तत्प्रयोज्यं तथातथा ।
निद्रागमाय तत्काले रागसंधुक्षणाय च ॥

On those and those limbs she should apply this in the fitting way, at the time, for bringing sleep and for kindling desire.

BP 1.15.29

तिष्ठतश्चोपविष्टस्य जाग्रतः स्वपतोऽपि वा ।
संवाहनं प्रशंसंति यदत्यर्थं सुखावहम् ॥

Massage is praised as very comforting for one standing, seated, awake, or asleep.

BP 1.15.30

नैष्पन्द्यं पुलकोद्भेदो गात्राणामक्षिमीलनम् ।
तत्प्रदेशार्पणं किञ्चिद्बोधेद्विकृतिदर्शनम् ॥

Stillness, gooseflesh, closing of the eyes, slight presentation of a body part, and signs of change should be understood.

BP 1.15.31

ऊरू मूलादिदेशे च तत्पाणिप्रतिपीडनम् ।
लक्षयेन्निपुणा यत्र तत्रैवाधिकमाचरेत् ॥

At the roots of the thighs and other places, she should notice where he presses her hand; there she should apply greater pressure.

BP 1.15.32

एवमेव यथोद्दिष्टं स्त्रीवृत्तं यानुतिष्ठति ।
पतिमाराध्य सम्पूर्ण त्रिवर्गं साधिगच्छति ॥

A woman who follows this conduct as stated, having pleased her husband, fully attains the three aims: righteousness, wealth, and desire.
