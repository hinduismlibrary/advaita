Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 129 — Sāmba Establishes the Solar Image

BP 1.129.1

सुमन्तुरुवाच। अथ लब्धवरः साम्बो वरं प्राप्य पुरातनम्। मन्यमानस्तदाश्चर्यं प्रहृष्टेनान्तरात्मना ॥

Sumantu said: “Then Sāmba, having obtained the ancient boon, regarded it as a wonder and rejoiced inwardly.”

Word-for-word
सुमन्तुरुवाच (sumanturuvāca) — Sumantu said
अथ (atha) — then
लब्धवरः (labdhavaraḥ) — having obtained the boon
साम्बः (sāmbaḥ) — Sāmba
वरम् (varam) — the boon
प्राप्य (prāpya) — having attained
पुरातनम् (purātanam) — ancient
मन्यमानः (manyamānaḥ) — regarding
तत् आश्चर्यम् (tad āścaryam) — that as a wonder
प्रहृष्टेन अन्तरात्मना (prahṛṣṭena antarātmanā) — with a delighted inner self
Transliteration
sumanturuvāca | atha labdhavaraḥ sāmbo varaṃ prāpya purātanam | manyamānastadāścaryaṃ prahṛṣṭenāntarātmanā || 1 ||

BP 1.129.2

पूर्वाभ्यासेन तेनैव सार्धमन्यैस्तपस्विभिः। स्नापनार्थं नातिदूरं चन्द्रभागां नदीं ययौ ॥

“Together with the other ascetics, and following that same former practice, he went not far away to the river Candrabhāgā for bathing.”

Word-for-word

Transliteration
pūrvābhyāsena tenaiva sārdhamanyais tapasvibhiḥ | snāpanārthaṃ nātidūraṃ candrabhāgāṃ nadīṃ yayau || 2 ||

BP 1.129.3

कृत्वात्ममण्डलाकारं श्रद्दधानो दिने दिने। सस्नौ सञ्चिन्तयामास किं रूपं स्थापयाम्यहम् ॥

“Making the form of his own solar orb, faithful day after day he bathed and pondered: ‘What form shall I establish?’”

Word-for-word

Transliteration
kṛtvātmamaṇḍalākāraṃ śraddadhāno dine dine | sasnau sañcintayāmāsa kiṃ rūpaṃ sthāpayāmyaham || 3 ||

BP 1.129.4

स स्नातः सहसैवाथ प्रणम्य तु प्रभावतीम्। उह्यमानां जलौघेन प्रतिमां सम्मुखीं रवेः ॥

“Having bathed, he suddenly bowed to Prabhāvatī, the image of Ravi being carried toward him by the flood of water.”

Word-for-word

Transliteration
sa snātaḥ sahasaivātha praṇamya tu prabhāvatīm | uhyamānāṃ jalaughena pratimāṃ sammukhīṃ raveḥ || 4 ||

BP 1.129.5

तां दृष्ट्वा तस्य वीरस्य समुत्पन्नमिदं यथा। देवेन यत्तदाज्ञप्तं तदिदं नात्र संशयः ॥

“Seeing it, this thought arose in that hero: ‘What the god commanded—this is it; there is no doubt here.’”

Word-for-word
ताम् दृष्ट्वा (tām dṛṣṭvā) — having seen it
तस्य वीरस्य (tasya vīrasya) — of that hero
समुत्पन्नम् (samutpannam) — arose

तत् इदम् (tat idam) — that is this
न अत्र संशयः (na atra saṃśayaḥ) — there is no doubt here
Transliteration
tāṃ dṛṣṭvā tasya vīrasya samutpannamidaṃ yathā | devena yattadājñaptaṃ tadidaṃ nātra saṃśayaḥ || 5 ||

BP 1.129.6

स तामुत्तार्य सलिलादानीय च महीपते। तस्मिन्मित्रवनोद्देशे स्थापयामास तां तदा ॥

Transliteration
sa tāmuttārya salilādānīya ca mahīpate | tasminmitravanoddeśe sthāpayāmāsa tāṃ tadā || 6 ||

BP 1.129.7

निधाय प्रतिमां लोके साम्बस्तस्य महात्मनः। मित्रं मित्रवने रम्ये स्थापयित्वा विधानतः ॥

“Having installed the image in the world, Sāmba established Mitra, the image of that great-souled Lord, in beautiful Mitravan according to the prescribed rule.”

Word-for-word

Transliteration

BP 1.129.8

ततस्तामेव पप्रच्छ प्रणम्य प्रतिमां रवेः। केनेयं निर्मिता नाथ भवतो ह्याकृतिः शुभा ॥

“Then, bowing to the image of Ravi, he asked it: ‘By whom was this auspicious image, O Lord, made in your form?’”

Word-for-word

Transliteration

BP 1.129.9

प्रतिमा तमुवाचाथ शृणु साम्ब ब्रुवे स्वयम्। निर्मिता येन चाप्येषा मदीया पुरुषाकृतिः ॥

“The image then spoke to him: ‘Listen, Sāmba; I myself shall tell you by whom this image of my human form was made.’”

Word-for-word

BP 1.129.10

ममातितेजसाविष्टं रूपमासीत्पुरातनम्। असह्यं सर्वभूतानां ततोऽस्म्यभ्यर्चितः सुरैः। सह्यं भवतु ते रूपं सर्वप्राणिभृतामिति ॥

BP 1.129.11
