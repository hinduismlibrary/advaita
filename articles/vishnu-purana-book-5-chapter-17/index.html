VP 5.17.1

अक्रूरोऽपि विनिष्क्रम्य स्यन्दनेनाशु-गामिना ।
कृष्ण-सन्दर्शनाकाङ्क्षी प्रययौ नन्द-गोकुलम् ॥१॥

Akrūra too, longing to see Kṛṣṇa, set out in a swift-going chariot and went to Nandagokula.

Śrīdhara translation

“Akrūra,” thus. When Śrī Kṛṣṇa entered Gokula, Akrūra too then set out from Mathurā and went to Gokula.

VP 5.17.2

चिन्तयामास चाक्रूरो नास्ति धन्यतरो मया ।
योऽहम् अंशावतीर्णस्य मुखं द्रक्ष्यामि चक्रिणः ॥२॥

Akrūra thought: “There is no one more fortunate than I, who shall see the face of the discus-bearer, descended as an aṃśa.”

Śrīdhara translation

The wishes of Akrūra as he travels are stated from “he thought” through “he will say to me, ‘Akrūra’” (verse 12). “He thought”: “There is none more fortunate than I.”

VP 5.17.5

विनिर्जग्मुर् यतो वेदा वेदाङ्गान्य् अखिलानि च ।
द्रक्ष्यामि तत् परं धाम धाम्नां भगवतो मुखम् ॥५॥

From whom the Vedas and all the Vedāṅgas issued, I shall see that supreme dhāma of Bhagavān, His face.

Śrīdhara translation

“Issued,” thus. “Of the abodes” means of the luminous ones. The “supreme abode” is their support, since the Sun, Moon, and Fire depend on the features of the face—the eyes and mouth.

Thus anticipating the all-knowing Lord’s possible contempt: although the jīva is knowledge-natured, it is not all-knowing because covered by the impurity of rajas and tamas; the Lord is free of that. “A mass of stainless sattva” means composed of pure sattva.
