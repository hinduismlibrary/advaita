Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 153 — Description of the Sun’s Radiance

BP 1.153.1

सुमन्तुरुवाच । सुमतिश्च रवेर्भक्तः पाण्डवेय महामते । अतस्ते निखिलं वच्मि शृणुष्वैकमना नृप ॥ १ ॥

Sumantu said: Sumati too is a devotee of Ravi, O son of Pāṇḍu, O great-minded king. Therefore I shall tell you all; listen with one mind.

BP 1.153.2

कल्पादौ सृजतो वीर ब्रह्मणो विविधाः प्रजाः । अहंकारो महानासीन्नास्ति लोके मदुत्तमः ॥ २ ॥

At the beginning of the aeon, O hero, as Brahmā created various beings, great ego arose in him: “There is no one in the world superior to me.”

BP 1.153.3

तथा पालयतो वीर केशवस्य धरापते । तथा संहरतो जज्ञेऽहङ्कारस्त्र्यम्बकस्य च ॥ ३ ॥

Likewise, ego arose in Keśava as he protected the world, and in Tryambaka as he dissolved it, O hero, O lord of earth.

BP 1.153.4

चिंतयन्तोथ ते देवाः केशवश्च नराधिप । मिथस्ते स्पर्धया युक्ताः परस्परविरोधिनः ॥ ४ ॥

Then those gods and Keśava pondered, O king; bound by rivalry, they opposed one another.

BP 1.153.5

विवादस्तु महानासीत्कञ्जाम्बुनगौकसाम् । परस्परं महाबाहो मनसाश्रित्य केवलम् ॥ ५ ॥

A great dispute arose among the lotus-born, the water-dweller, and the mountain-dweller, O mighty-armed, each relying only on his own mind.

BP 1.153.6

अहं कर्ता विकर्ताऽहं पालकोऽहं जगत्प्रभुः । इत्याह भगवान्ब्रह्मा कृष्णभीमौ समर्चितौ ॥ ६ ॥

“I am the doer; I am the maker; I am the protector; I am the lord of the world,” said the Blessed Brahmā, worshipped by Kṛṣṇa and Bhīma.

BP 1.153.7

तथैत्य शंकरः क्रुद्धः कः शक्तो मदृते भुवि । संहर्तुं जगदेतद्धि स्रष्टुं पालयितुं तथा ॥ ७ ॥

Angry, Śaṅkara said: Who on earth is able, except me, to dissolve this whole world, and likewise to create and protect it?

BP 1.153.8

नारायणोऽप्येवमेव मनाक् क्रोधसमन्वितः । न वा शक्तो जगत्स्रष्टुं संहर्तुं रक्षितुं तथा ॥ ८ ॥

Nārāyaṇa too was likewise somewhat filled with anger; he was unable to create the world, dissolve it, and protect it.

BP 1.153.9

एवं तेषां प्रवदतां क्रुद्धानां च परस्परम् । समाविशत्तदाऽज्ञानं तमो मोहात्मकं विभो ॥ ९ ॥

While they were speaking thus, angry with one another, ignorance entered them then, O lord—darkness consisting of delusion.

BP 1.153.10

तेन क्रान्तधियः सर्वे न पश्यन्ति परस्परम् । अत्यर्थं मोहमापन्ना न जानन्तीह किञ्चन ॥ १० ॥

Their minds overcome by it, they did not see one another; fallen into extreme delusion, they knew nothing at all here.

BP 1.153.11

अपश्यन्तो मिथस्ते तु निषण्णाः क्ष्मातले विभो । आरमन्ति हि ये चान्ये ते दिवाकरमास्थिताः ॥ ११ ॥

Not seeing one another, they sat upon the earth, O lord; the others who took delight had resorted to Divākara.

BP 1.153.12

तमसा मोहिताः सर्वे निद्रावत्क्रान्तचेतसः । मनाग्ज्ञानेन चाक्रान्ताः किं कुर्यामेति मोहिताः ॥ १२ ॥

All were deluded by darkness, their minds overcome as if by sleep; touched only slightly by knowledge, they were bewildered: What shall we do?

BP 1.153.13

अथ भूताधिपो देवो गोश्रुताभरणोज्ज्वलः । चन्द्रार्धकृतशोभस्तु शीतलांशुविशोधितः ॥ १३ ॥

Then the god, lord of beings, radiant with the Gośruta ornament, adorned by the half-moon and brightened by cool moonlight,

BP 1.153.14

आर्तिमेत्य परां वीर मोहितस्तमसा विभो । अपश्यन्नब्रवीद्देवं माधवं भूधरं हरिम् ॥ १४ ॥

He came into great distress, O hero, deluded by darkness, O lord; he saw and spoke to the god Mādhava, the earth-bearer, Hari.

BP 1.153.15

महादेव उवाच । कृष्ण कृष्ण महाबाहो क्व गतस्त्वं महामते । ब्रह्मा च क्व गतो वीर नाहं पश्यामि वां क्वचित् ॥ १५ ॥

Mahādeva said: Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa, mighty-armed, where have you gone, O great-minded one? Where has Brahmā gone, O hero? I do not see you two anywhere.

BP 1.153.16

मोहेन महताहं वै तमसा च विमोहितः । किं करोमि क्व गच्छामि क्व चाहमधुना स्थितः ॥ १६ ॥

I am greatly deluded by delusion and darkness. What shall I do? Where shall I go? Where am I standing now?

BP 1.153.17

क्ष्माधरं पृथिवीं वृक्षान् देवगन्धर्वदानवान् । विपुलं सागरं सिन्धून्नहि पश्यामि किञ्चन ॥ १७ ॥

I do not see anything at all—not the mountain that bears the earth, the earth, trees, gods, Gandharvas, demons, the vast ocean, or rivers.

BP 1.153.18

केनोपायेन पश्येयं जगत्स्थावरजङ्गमम् । ब्रूहि मे देवशार्दूल व्रीडा मेऽतीव जायते ॥ १८ ॥

By what means could I see the whole world, moving and unmoving? Tell me, tiger among the gods; great shame arises in me.

BP 1.153.19

शङ्करस्य वचः श्रुत्वा हरिर्वचनमब्रवीत् । शोकगद्गदया वाचा तमसा मोहितो नृप ॥ १९ ॥

Having heard Śaṅkara’s words, Hari spoke in a voice choked with grief, deluded by darkness, O king.

BP 1.153.20

विष्णुरुवाच । भीम भीम न जानेऽहं क्व भवान्वर्ततेऽधुना । ममापि मोहितं चेतस्तमसातीव शङ्कर ॥ २० ॥

Viṣṇu said: Bhīma, Bhīma, I do not know where you are now. My mind too is deeply deluded by darkness, O Śaṅkara.

BP 1.153.21

क्व गच्छामि क्व तिष्ठामि कथं तत्स्वस्थतां व्रजेत् । तमसा पूरितं सर्वं जगद्धि परमेश्वर ॥ २१ ॥

Where shall I go, where shall I stand, and how shall this return to a sound state? The whole world is indeed filled with darkness, O Supreme Lord.

BP 1.153.22

यद्यसौ दृश्यते देवः सुरज्येष्ठोऽम्बुजोद्भवः । पृच्छावस्तं महात्मानं यदि ते रोचते हर ॥ २२ ॥

If the lotus-born, eldest of the gods, is found, let us ask that great-souled one, if it pleases you, O Hari.

BP 1.153.23

हित्वा दर्पमहङ्कारं सममास्थाय केवलम् । पद्माननं पद्मयोनिं पद्मपत्रनिभेक्षणम् ॥ २३ ॥

Having abandoned pride and ego and adopted an impartial stance, they stood before the lotus-faced, lotus-born one, whose eyes resembled lotus petals.

BP 1.153.24

इत्येवं गदतो वाक्यं विष्णोरमिततेजसः । श्रुत्वोवाच विभुर्ब्रह्मा गङ्गाधरमहीधरौ ॥ २४ ॥

Having heard these words spoken by Viṣṇu of immeasurable splendor, the all-pervading Brahmā spoke to Gaṅgādhara and the earth-bearer.

BP 1.153.25

कृष्ण कृष्ण महाबाहो भीम भीम महामते । क्व भवन्तौ ब्रूत किं च किं युवामूचथुर्मिथः ॥ २५ ॥

Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa, mighty-armed; Bhīma, Bhīma, great-minded; where are you two? Speak: what did you say to one another?

BP 1.153.26

ममातीव मनोबुद्धी तमसा वशमागते । न शृणोमि न पश्यामि निद्रामोहवशं गतः ॥ २६ ॥

My mind and intellect have come greatly under the power of darkness. I do not hear; I do not see; I have come under the power of sleep and delusion.

BP 1.153.27

अहो बत जगत्सर्वं सदेवासुरमानुषम् । तमसा व्याष्टतं देवौ न जाने क्व गतं महः ॥ २७ ॥

Ah, all this world, with gods, demons, and humans, is enveloped by darkness. O gods, I do not know where its splendor has gone.

BP 1.153.28

अथ तेषां प्रवदतां ब्रह्मादीनां दिवौकसाम् । दर्पक्रोधभयार्तानां तमसाक्रान्तचेतसाम् ॥ २८ ॥

Then, while Brahmā and the other gods were speaking, afflicted by pride, anger, and fear, their minds overcome by darkness,

BP 1.153.29

तेषां दर्पापहाराय प्रबोधार्थं च गोपतेः । तेजोरूपं समुद्भूतमष्टशृङ्गमनौपमम् ॥ २९ ॥

An incomparable, eight-peaked form of radiance arose to remove their pride and awaken the Lord of cattle.

BP 1.153.30

अलक्ष्यं पापतमसा महद्व्योम नराधिप । ज्वालामालावृतं वीर बहुरूपं च भासते ॥ ३० ॥

Hidden by sinful darkness, the great sky shines, O king, covered with garlands of flames, O hero, and in many forms.

BP 1.153.31

शतयोजनविस्तीर्णं गतमूर्ध्वं भ्रमत्तथा । गोमध्यतो महाराज कर्णिकेवाम्बुजस्य तु ॥ ३१ ॥

It spanned a hundred yojanas, stretched upward, and revolved; in the midst of the rays, O great king, like the pericarp of a lotus.

BP 1.153.32

प्रकाशं तेजसा तस्य जगत्सर्वमिदं नृप । पुरेष्वन्तर्यथा वीर अम्बुजस्यार्चिभिः सदा ॥ ३२ ॥

By its radiance all this world became bright, O king, like the inside of cities always lit by the rays of a lotus, O hero.

BP 1.153.33

दृष्ट्वा परस्परं सर्वे हुङ्कारादिविकारिणः । तेजसा मोहितास्तस्य जगत्सर्वमिदं नृप ॥ ३३ ॥

All looked at one another, making cries such as Huṃ; they were bewildered by its radiance, O king, and this whole world was bewildered.

BP 1.153.34

तेजसा मोहितं तस्य महद्व्योम नराधिप । ततो विस्मयमासीनः दृष्टगोपतयो नृप ॥ ३४ ॥

The great sky bewildered them by its radiance, O king; then, having seen the Lord of cattle, they sat in wonder, O king.

BP 1.153.35

पश्यमाना महो व्योम्नि मिथो वचनमब्रुवन् । अहो तेजः समुद्धूतमस्माकं श्रेयसे नृप ॥ ३५ ॥

Gazing at the great wonder in the sky, they spoke to one another: Ah, this radiance has arisen for our welfare, O king.

BP 1.153.36

प्रकाशाय च लोकानां सर्वे पश्याम किं न्विदम् । ज्ञानायोर्ध्वं गतो ब्रह्मा चाधस्तात्त्रिपुरान्तक ॥ ३६ ॥

For the illumination of the worlds, what is this that we all see? Brahmā went upward in search of knowledge, and the destroyer of Tripura went downward.

BP 1.153.37

तिर्यग्जगाम देवेशश्चक्राम्बुजगदाधरः । अलब्ध्वा तस्य ते सर्वे प्रमाणं गैरिकाधिपाः ॥ ३७ ॥

The lord of the gods, bearer of the discus, lotus, and mace, went across. All of them, the mountain-lords, failed to find its measure.

BP 1.153.38

विस्मयोत्फुल्लनयना समागम्य परस्परम् । सर्वे कञ्जादिका देवा इदं वचनमब्रुवन् ॥ ३८ ॥

Their eyes opened wide with wonder; having assembled together, all the gods beginning with the lotus-born spoke these words.

BP 1.153.39

कोऽयं किमात्मकश्चायं किमिदं तेजसां निधिः । अहोऽस्य दर्शनात्सर्वे सञ्जाता ज्ञानिनो वयम् ॥ ३९ ॥

Who is this? What is his nature? What is this treasure of splendors? Ah, by seeing him we have all become wise.

BP 1.153.40

तस्मात्सर्वे प्रणम्यैनं स्तुवीमोऽद्भुतदर्शनम् । कृताञ्जलिपुटाः सर्वे चास्तुवंस्त्रिदिवौकसः ॥ ४० ॥

Therefore let us all bow to him and praise him of wondrous appearance. All the dwellers in the three heavens praised him with joined palms.

BP 1.153.41

स्तुवतामप्यथैतेषां सहस्रकिरणो रविः । आत्मानं दर्शयामास कृपया परया वृतः ॥ ४१ ॥

While they were praising, Ravi of a thousand rays showed himself, surrounded by supreme compassion.

BP 1.153.42

ज्ञात्वा भक्तिं महाबाहो ब्रह्मादीनां महोपमाम् । अथ ते व्योम्नि देवेशं ददृशुः परमेश्वरम् ॥ ४२ ॥

Knowing the incomparable devotion of Brahmā and the others, they then saw the Lord of gods, the Supreme Lord, in the sky, O mighty-armed.

BP 1.153.43

खषोल्कलोकनाथेशं सहस्रकिरणोज्ज्वलम् । कृत्तिकाभिरसंस्पृष्टं यद्वा तत्कार्तिकास्थितम् ॥ ४३ ॥

They saw the sky-flame, lord of the worlds, shining with a thousand rays, untouched by the Kṛttikās—or else dwelling in Kārtika.

BP 1.153.44

दुर्जयं कृत्तिकानां तु तथैकेन विवर्जितम् । तथा हस्तविहीनं च सप्तर्षिरहितं तथा ॥ ४४ ॥

Hard to conquer by the Kṛttikās, yet deprived of one; likewise without hands and without the seven sages.

BP 1.153.45

वर्षाब्दरहितं देवं सप्तस्वरविवर्जितम् । सकलं निष्कलं चैव सदैकाकाररूपिणम् ॥ ४५ ॥

The god without rain and clouds, devoid of the seven notes, whole and partless, always of a single form.

BP 1.153.46

तद्दृष्ट्वानेकशिरसमनेकचरणं तथा । अनेकोदरबाह्वंसमनेकाभरणान्वितम् ॥ ४६ ॥

Seeing him with many heads and likewise many feet, many bellies, arms, and shoulders, furnished with many ornaments,

BP 1.153.47

अनेकाननमक्षीबं सहस्राक्षमनौपमम् । अनेकवर्णरूपं च अनेकमुकुटोज्ज्वलम् ॥ ४७ ॥

Many-faced, wakeful, thousand-eyed, incomparable, of many-colored forms, and shining with many crowns.

BP 1.153.48

दृष्ट्वैवं देवदेवस्य रूपं भानोर्महात्मनः । विस्मयोत्फुल्लनयनास्तुष्टवुस्ते दिवाकरम् ॥ ४८ ॥

Having thus seen the form of the great-souled Bhānu, the Lord of gods, their eyes opened wide with wonder; they praised the Day-maker.

BP 1.153.49

कृताञ्जलिपुटो भूत्वा ब्रह्मा स्तोतुं प्रचक्रमे । प्रणम्य शिरसा भानुमिदं वचनमब्रवीत् ॥ ४९ ॥

Having joined his palms, Brahmā began to praise. Bowing his head to Bhānu, he spoke these words.

BP 1.153.50

ब्रह्मोवाच । नमस्ते देवदेवेश सहस्रकिरणोज्ज्वल । लोकदीप नमस्तेऽस्तु नमस्ते कोणवल्लभ ॥ ५० ॥

Brahmā said: Salutation to you, lord of the gods, shining with a thousand rays. O lamp of the worlds, salutation to you; salutation to you, beloved of the quarters.

BP 1.153.51

भास्कराय नमो नित्यं खषोल्काय नमोनमः । विष्णवे कालचक्राय सोमायामिततेजसे ॥ ५१ ॥

Salutation always to Bhāskara, salutation again and again to the sky-flame; to Viṣṇu, the wheel of time, and to Soma of immeasurable radiance.

BP 1.153.52

नमस्ते पञ्चकालाय इन्द्राय वसुरेतसे । खगाय लोकनाथाय एकचक्ररथाय च ॥ ५२ ॥

Salutation to you of five periods, to Indra, rich in seed; to the sky-goer, lord of the worlds, and rider of the one-wheeled chariot.

BP 1.153.53

जगद्धिताय देवाय शिवायामिततेजसे । तमोघ्नाय सुरूपाय तेजसां निधये नमः ॥ ५३ ॥

Salutation to the god who benefits the world, the auspicious one of immeasurable radiance; to the destroyer of darkness, beautiful in form, treasure of splendors.

BP 1.153.54

अर्थाय कामरूपाय धर्मायामिततेजसे । मोक्षाय मोक्षरूपाय सूर्याय च नमोनमः ॥ ५४ ॥

Salutation again and again to the aim, the embodiment of desire, righteousness of immeasurable radiance, liberation, its embodiment, and the Sun.

BP 1.153.55

क्रोधलोभविहीनाय लोकानां स्थितिहेतवे । शुभाय शुभरूपाय शुभदाय शुभात्मने ॥ ५५ ॥

Salutation to the one free from anger and greed, cause of the worlds’ stability; to the auspicious one, of auspicious form, giver of auspiciousness, whose self is auspicious.

BP 1.153.56

शान्ताय शान्तरूपाय शान्तयेऽस्मासु वै नमः । नमस्ते ब्रह्मरूपाय ब्राह्मणाय नमोनमः ॥ ५६ ॥

Salutation to the peaceful one, to the one of peaceful form, to peace among us; salutation to you whose form is Brahman, and to the Brahmin, again and again.

BP 1.153.57

ब्रह्मदेवाय ब्रह्मरूपाय ब्रह्मणे परमात्मने । ब्रह्मणे च प्रसादं वै कुरु देव जगत्पते ॥ ५७ ॥

To the god Brahmā, to the one whose form is Brahman, to Brahman, the supreme Self; grant favor to Brahman too, O god, lord of the world.

BP 1.153.58

एवं स्तुत्वा रविं ब्रह्मा श्रद्धया परया विभो । तूष्णीमासीन्महाभाग प्रहृष्टेनान्तरात्मना ॥ ५८ ॥

Having thus praised Ravi with supreme faith, Brahmā fell silent, his inner self greatly delighted, O lord.

BP 1.153.59

ब्रह्मणोऽनन्तरं रुद्रः स्तोत्रं चक्रे विभावसोः । त्रिपुरारिर्महातेजाः प्रणम्य शिरसा रविम् ॥ ५९ ॥

After Brahmā, Rudra composed a hymn to Vibhāvasu. The great-rayed destroyer of Tripura bowed his head to Ravi.

BP 1.153.60

महादेव उवाच । जय भाव जयाजेय जय हंस दिवाकर । जय शम्भो महाबाहो खग गोचर भूधर ॥ ६० ॥

Mahādeva said: Hail, O Being; hail, O unconquerable one; hail, O Swan, O Day-maker. Hail, O source of well-being, mighty-armed, sky-goer, mountain-bearer.

BP 1.153.61

जय लोकप्रदीपेन जय भानो जगत्पते । जय काल जयानन्त संवत्सर शुभानन ॥ ६१ ॥

Hail, lamp of the worlds; hail, Bhānu, lord of the world. Hail, Time; hail, Endless One; hail, Year, auspicious-faced one.

BP 1.153.62

जय देवादितेः पुत्र कश्यपानन्दवर्धन । तमोघ्न जय सप्तेश जय सप्ताश्ववाहन ॥ ६२ ॥

Hail, son of Aditi, increaser of Kaśyapa’s joy; destroyer of darkness, hail; lord of seven, hail; rider of seven horses.

BP 1.153.63

महेश जय कान्तीश जय कालेश शङ्कर । अर्थकामेश धर्मेश जय मोक्षेश शर्मद ॥ ६३ ॥

Great Lord, hail; lord of radiance, hail; lord of time, O Śaṅkara; lord of wealth and desire, lord of righteousness, lord of liberation, giver of happiness, hail.

BP 1.153.64

जय वेदाङ्गरूपाय ग्रहरूपाय वै नमः । सत्याय सत्यरूपाय सुरूपाय शुभाय च ॥ ६४ ॥

Hail to the one whose form is the limbs of the Veda and whose form is the planets; to Truth, of true form, beautiful and auspicious.

BP 1.153.65

क्रोधलोभविनाशाय कामनाशाय वै जय । कल्माषपक्षिरूपाय यतिरूपाय शम्भवे ॥ ६५ ॥

Hail to the destroyer of anger and greed, to the destroyer of desire; to the auspicious one in the form of a mottled bird, in the form of an ascetic.

BP 1.153.66

विश्वाय विश्वरूपाय विश्वकर्माय वै जय । जयोङ्कार वषट्कार स्वाहाकार स्वधामय ॥ ६६ ॥

Hail to the universe, the universal form, the maker of the universe; hail to the syllable Om, the Vaṣaṭ call, the Svāhā call, and the Svadhā offering.

BP 1.153.67

जयाश्वमेधरूपाय चाग्निरूपार्यमाय च । संसारार्णवपीताय मोक्षद्वारप्रदाय च ॥ ६७ ॥

Hail to him whose form is the horse sacrifice, the form of fire, and Aryaman; to him who drank the ocean of worldly existence and gives the gate of liberation.

BP 1.153.68

संसारार्णवमग्नस्य मम देव जगत्पते । हस्तावलम्बनो देव भव त्वं गोपतेऽद्भुत ॥ ६८ ॥

For me, sunk in the ocean of worldly existence, O god, lord of the world, be a handhold, O wondrous lord of cattle.

BP 1.153.69

ईशोऽप्येवमहीनाङ्गं स्तुत्वा भानुं प्रयत्नतः । विरराम महाराज प्रणम्य शिरसा रविम् ॥ ६९ ॥

Īśa too praised Bhānu, complete-limbed, with care, then stopped, bowing his head to Ravi, O great king.

BP 1.153.70

अथ विष्णुर्महातेजाः कृताञ्जलिपुटो रविम् । उवाच राजशार्दूल भक्त्या श्रद्धासमन्वितः ॥ ७० ॥

Then Viṣṇu of great splendor, with palms joined, spoke to Ravi with devotion and faith, O tiger among kings.

BP 1.153.71

विष्णुरुवाच । नमामि देवदेवेशं भूतभावनमव्ययम् । दिवाकरं रविं भानुं मार्तण्डं भास्करं भगम् ॥ ७१ ॥

Viṣṇu said: I bow to the Lord of gods, creator of beings, the imperishable one: Day-maker, Ravi, Bhānu, Mārtāṇḍa, Light-maker, Bhaga.

BP 1.153.72

इन्द्रं विष्णुं हरिं हंसमर्कं लोकगुरुं विभुम् । त्रिनेत्रं त्र्यक्षरं त्र्यङ्गं त्रिमूर्तिं त्रिगतिं शुभम् ॥ ७२ ॥

Indra, Viṣṇu, Hari, the Swan, the Sun, the world-teacher, the all-pervading one; three-eyed, three-syllabled, three-limbed, three-formed, threefold in course, auspicious.

BP 1.153.73

षण्मुखाय नमो नित्यं त्रिनेत्राय नमोनमः । चतुर्विंशतिपादाय नमो द्वादशपाणये ॥ ७३ ॥

Salutation always to the six-faced one, salutation again and again to the three-eyed one; salutation to the twenty-four-footed, to the twelve-handed.

BP 1.153.74

नमस्ते भूतपतये लोकानां पतये नमः । देवानां पतये नित्यं वर्णानां पतये नमः ॥ ७४ ॥

Salutation to you, lord of beings; salutation to the lord of the worlds; salutation always to the lord of the gods, and to the lord of the varṇas.

BP 1.153.75

त्वं ब्रह्मा त्वं जगन्नाथो रुद्रस्त्वं च प्रजापतिः । त्वं सोमस्त्वं तथादित्यस्त्वमोंकारक एव हि ॥ ७५ ॥

You are Brahmā, you are the Lord of the world, Rudra and Prajāpati; you are Soma and likewise Āditya; indeed, you are the maker of Om.

BP 1.153.76

बृहस्पतिर्बुधस्त्वं हि त्वं शुक्रस्त्वं विभावसुः । यमस्त्वं वरुणस्त्वं हि नमस्ते कश्यपात्मज ॥ ७६ ॥

You indeed are Bṛhaspati and Budha; you are Śukra, you are Vibhāvasu; you are Yama, you are Varuṇa. Salutation to you, son of Kaśyapa.

BP 1.153.77

त्वया ततमिदं सर्वं जगत्स्थावरजङ्गमम् । त्वत्त एव समुत्पन्नं सदेवासुरमानुषम् ॥ ७७ ॥

By you this whole world, moving and unmoving, is pervaded; from you alone it arose, together with gods, demons, and humans.

BP 1.153.78

ब्रह्मा चाहं च रुद्रश्च समुत्पन्ना जगत्पते । कल्पादौ तु पुरा देव स्थितये जगतोऽनघ ॥ ७८ ॥

Brahmā, I, and Rudra were produced, O lord of the world, at the beginning of the aeon of old, O god, for the world’s continuance, O sinless one.

BP 1.153.79

नमस्ते वेदरूपाय अहोरूपाय वै नमः । नमस्ते ज्ञानरूपाय यज्ञाय च नमोनमः ॥ ७९ ॥

Salutation to you whose form is the Veda, salutation to you whose form is day; salutation to you whose form is knowledge, and salutation again and again to sacrifice.

BP 1.153.80

प्रसीदास्मासु देवेश भूतेश किरणोज्ज्वल । संसारार्णवमग्नानां प्रसादं कुरु गोपते ॥ ८० ॥ वेदान्ताय नमो नित्यं नमो यज्ञकलाय च ॥

Be gracious to us, lord of the gods, lord of beings, radiant with rays; grant favor to us sunk in the ocean of worldly existence, O lord of cattle. Salutation always to Vedānta and to the part of sacrifice.

BP 1.153.81

सुमन्तुरुवाच । स्तुत्वैवं भास्करं भक्त्या विष्णुर्भरतसत्तम । प्रदध्यौ नृपशार्दूल रविं तद्गतमानसः ॥ ८१ ॥

Sumantu said: Having thus praised Bhāskara with devotion, Viṣṇu meditated on Ravi with his mind fixed on him, O best of Bharatas, O tiger among kings.

BP 1.153.82

एवं ते नरशार्दूल देवा ब्रह्मादयोऽनघ । स्तुवन्ति तं महात्मानं सहस्रकिरणं रविम् ॥ ८२ ॥

Thus, O tiger among men, the gods led by Brahmā praise him, the great-souled Ravi of a thousand rays, O sinless one.

BP 1.153.83

इत्येवं स्तुवतां तेषां रविं भक्त्या महात्मनाम् । अथ तुष्टो रविस्तेषां ब्रह्मदीनां जगत्पतिः ॥ ८३ ॥

As the great-souled ones praised Ravi with devotion in this way, Ravi, lord of the world, was pleased with Brahmā and the others.

BP 1.153.84

विज्ञाय भक्तिं परमां श्रद्धां च परमां विभुः । उवाच स महातेजाः प्रहृष्टेनान्तरात्मना ॥ ८४ ॥

Knowing their supreme devotion and supreme faith, the all-pervading one of great splendor spoke, his inner self delighted.

BP 1.153.85

ग्रहेशो व्योम चारूढस्तेजसा प्रज्वलन्दिशः । ब्रह्माणं विष्णुमीशानमामन्त्र्यैतान्विशांपते ॥ ८५ ॥

The lord of planets mounted the sky, blazing the directions with radiance, and addressed Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Īśāna, and these, O lord of people.

BP 1.153.86

दृष्ट्वा तान्प्रणतान्त्सर्वाञ्छिरोभिरवनिं गतान् । तुष्टोऽस्मि ते सुरज्येष्ठ चतुर्मुख जगत्पते । वरं वरय भद्रं ते मनसा त्वं यदिच्छसि ॥ ८६ ॥

Seeing them all bowed, their heads touching the earth, he said: I am pleased with you, eldest of the gods, four-faced lord of the world. Choose a boon; good fortune to you, whatever you desire in your mind.

BP 1.153.87

कृत्वा तु वचनं भानोर्ब्रह्मा लोकगुरुर्नृप । जगाम शिरसा भूमावुवाच स कृताञ्जलिः ॥ ८७ ॥

Then Brahmā, teacher of the world, acted on Bhānu’s words, bowed his head to the earth, and spoke with palms joined, O king.

BP 1.153.88

ब्रह्मोवाच । कृतकृत्योऽस्मि देवेश पूतश्चास्मि खगाधिप । धन्योऽस्म्यनुगृहीतोऽस्मि गतोऽस्मि परमां गतिम् ॥ ८८ ॥

Brahmā said: I am fulfilled, O lord of the gods; I am purified, O lord of the sky-goers. I am blessed, favored, and have reached the highest state.

BP 1.153.89

सहस्रकिरणैर्यन्मे भवान्दर्शनमागतः ॥ ८९ ॥

Since you, with a thousand rays, have come into my sight.

BP 1.153.90

अपश्यतश्च देवेश मूढमासीन्मनो मम । भगवन्सम्प्रसीद त्वं ममोपरि विभावसो ॥ ९० ॥

When I could not see you, O lord of the gods, my mind was bewildered. O Blessed One, be gracious to me, Vibhāvasu.

BP 1.153.91

प्रयच्छ त्वं बलं भक्तिमात्मनो मम गोपते । गत्वा शिरोभिरवनिमष्टाङ्गैः पतितस्य च । भक्त्या विज्ञप्तिमाकर्ण्य प्रसादं कुरु गोपते ॥ ९१ ॥

Grant me strength and devotion, O lord of cattle. Hear my petition made with devotion as I fall to the earth with eight limbs; show favor to me, O lord of cattle.

BP 1.153.92

ब्रह्मणो वचनं श्रुत्वा पूषा देवो जगत्पतिः । तथेत्याह महाराज विराट् प्रश्रयान्वितम् ॥ ९२ ॥

Having heard Brahmā’s words, the god Pūṣā, lord of the world, said: So be it, with humility, O great king.

BP 1.153.93

ब्रह्मणे च वरं दत्त्वा राजन्देवो दिवाकरः । उवाच त्र्यम्बकं देवं शशाङ्ककृतशेखरम् ॥ ९३ ॥

Having granted a boon to Brahmā, the god Day-maker spoke to the three-eyed god, whose crest is adorned by the moon, O king.

BP 1.153.94

वरं वरय भूतेश भूभृज्जादयितानघ । यमिच्छसि महादेव ददेऽहं तदशेषतः ॥ ९४ ॥

Choose a boon, O lord of beings, beloved of the Mountain-born One, O sinless one. Whatever you desire, O great god, I shall give completely.

BP 1.153.95

भास्करस्य वचः श्रुत्वा ईश्वरस्त्रिपुरान्तक । गत्वा तु शिरसा भूमौ प्रणम्योवाच भास्करम् ॥ ९५ ॥

Having heard Bhāskara’s words, Īśvara, the destroyer of Tripura, bowed his head to the earth and spoke to Bhāskara.

BP 1.153.96

महादेव उवाच । पुण्योऽहं पुण्यकर्माहं नास्ति धन्यतरो मम । गतोऽहं परमां सिद्धिं गतश्च परमां गतिम् ॥ ९६ ॥

Mahādeva said: I am meritorious, I do meritorious deeds; there is none more fortunate than I. I have attained the highest success and the highest state.

BP 1.153.97

नाप्राप्यमस्ति देवेश नासाध्यं मम किंचन । यस्य मे भगवान्देवः प्रसादप्रवणः स्थितः ॥ ९७ ॥

Nothing is unattainable, nothing impossible for me, since the Blessed God stands inclined to favor me, O lord of the gods.

BP 1.153.98

त्वया ततमिदं सर्वं जगत्स्थावरजङ्गमम् । त्वत्त एव समुत्पन्नं लयं च त्वयि यास्यति ॥ ९८ ॥

By you this whole world, moving and unmoving, is pervaded; from you alone it arose, and into you it will go at dissolution.

BP 1.153.99

यदि तुष्टो मम विभो अनुग्राह्योऽस्मि ते यदि । अचलां देहि मे भक्तिमात्मनश्चरणं नय ॥ ९९ ॥

If you are pleased with me, O all-pervading one, if I am to be favored by you, give me unwavering devotion and lead me to your feet.

BP 1.153.100

व्योमकेशवचः श्रुत्वा पूषा देवो दिवाकरः । तथेत्याह हरं वीर ततो हरिमुवाच ॥ १०० ॥

Having heard the words of the Sky-haired One, the god Pūṣā, the Day-maker, said: So be it, to Hara, O hero; then he spoke to Hari.

BP 1.153.101

नारायण महाबाहो वरं वरय गोधर । परितुष्टोऽस्मि ते देव यमिच्छसि महाबल ॥ १०१ ॥

Nārāyaṇa, mighty-armed, choose a boon, O bearer of the earth. I am wholly pleased with you, O god; ask for whatever you desire, O mighty one.

BP 1.153.102

श्रुत्वा तु भास्करवचः कीलालजनको हरिः । उवाच परया भक्त्या नत्वा च शिरसा रविम् ॥ १०२ ॥

Having heard Bhāskara’s words, Hari, source of water, spoke with supreme devotion, bowing his head to Ravi.

BP 1.153.103

नारायण उवाच । जय देव जगन्नाथ जय देव गुरो रवे । प्रसीद मम देवेश भक्तिं यच्छात्मनो रवे ॥ १०३ ॥

Nārāyaṇa said: Hail, O god, lord of the world; hail, O god, teacher, O Ravi. Be gracious to me, lord of the gods; give me devotion to yourself, O Ravi.

BP 1.153.104

येनाहं सर्वदेवानामुत्तमः स्यां जगत्पते । अजेयश्च तथा देव दैत्यदानवरक्षसाम् ॥ १०४ ॥

By this I may be highest among all the gods, O lord of the world, and likewise unconquered by Daityas, demons, and demons, O god.

BP 1.153.105

त्वद्भक्त्या बृंहितबलस्तेजसा महतान्वितः । ततो मया महत्कर्म कर्तव्यं तव शासनात् ॥ १०५ ॥

Strengthened by devotion to you and endowed with great splendor, I shall then perform a great task at your command.

BP 1.153.106

प्रजानां पालनं देव देवानां च ग्रहाधिप । वर्णानामाश्रमाणां च वर्णधर्मस्य वा विभो ॥ १०६ ॥

Protection of the people, O god, and of the gods, O lord of planets; protection of varṇas, life-stages, and the duties of varṇas, O all-pervading one.

BP 1.153.107

दुष्टदैत्यविनाशाय लोकानां पालनाय च । सृष्टोऽहं भवता पूर्वं कल्पादौ च कृतोऽनघ ॥ १०७ ॥

For the destruction of wicked Daityas and the protection of the worlds, I was formerly created by you at the beginning of the aeon, O sinless one.

BP 1.153.108

यस्य रुष्टो भवान्स्याद्वै कथञ्चित्पुरुषस्य तु । व्याधिर्दुःखं मनोरोगं दारिद्र्यं सन्ततिक्षयः ॥ १०८ ॥

For any man with whom you may somehow be angry, there are illness, grief, mental affliction, poverty, and loss of offspring.

BP 1.153.109

तस्यैतानि भवन्तीह आधयो विविधास्तथा । तस्मात्त्वं च ततो देव संस्तव्यः सततं बुधैः ॥ १०९ ॥

These and various afflictions likewise come upon him here. Therefore, O god, you are always to be praised by the wise.

BP 1.153.110

एवं त्वां गोपते देव भक्त्या श्रद्धासमन्वितः । अहमर्चितुमिच्छामि तस्मान्मयि कृपां कुरु ॥ ११० ॥

Thus, O god, lord of cattle, endowed with devotion and faith, I wish to worship you; therefore show favor to me.
