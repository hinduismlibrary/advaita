Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 54 — The Glory and Signs of the Sun

BP 1.54.1

रुद्र उवाच ।
अहो हंसस्य माहात्म्यं वर्णितं भवतेदृशम् ।
कथ्यतां पुनरेवेदं माहात्म्यं भास्करस्य तु ॥

Rudra spoke. You have described the greatness of the swan so well. Tell me again the greatness of Bhāskara.

BP 1.54.2

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
आदित्यमन्त्रमखिलं त्रैलोक्यं सचराचरम् ।
भवत्यस्माज्जगत्सर्वं सदेवासुरमानुषम् ॥

Brahmā spoke. The entire three worlds, moving and unmoving, become the complete formula of the Sun. From him arises the whole world, with gods, demons, and humans.

BP 1.54.3

रुद्रेन्द्रोपेन्द्रचन्द्राणां विप्रेन्द्रत्रिदिवौकसाम् ।
महाद्युतिमतां कृत्स्नं तेजो यत्सार्वलौकिकम् ॥

The entire universal brilliance of Rudra, Indra, Upendra, the Moon, the foremost Brahmins, and the dwellers in heaven, all possessing great splendor, is his.

BP 1.54.4

सर्वात्मा सर्वलोकेशो देवदेवः प्रजापतिः ।
सूर्य एष त्रिलोकस्य मूलं परमदैवतम् ॥

He is the self of all, lord of all worlds, god of gods, and lord of beings. This Sun is the root of the three worlds, the highest deity.

BP 1.54.5

अग्नौ प्रास्ताहुतिः सम्यगादित्यमुपतिष्ठति ।
आदित्याज्जायते वृष्टिर्वृष्टेरन्नं ततः प्रजाः ॥

The oblation properly offered into Fire reaches the Sun. From the Sun rain is born, from rain food, and from food beings.

BP 1.54.6

सूर्यात्प्रसूयते सर्वं तत्र चैव प्रलीयते ।
भावाभावौ हि लोकानामादित्यान्निःसृतौ पुरा ॥

Everything is produced from the Sun and dissolves in him. The existence and nonexistence of the worlds issued from the Sun in ancient times.

BP 1.54.7

एतत्तु ध्यानिनां ध्यानं मोक्षं चाप्येष मोक्षिणाम् ।
अत्र गच्छन्ति निर्वाणं जायन्तेऽस्मात्पुनः प्रजाः ॥

This is the meditation of meditators, and the liberation of those seeking release. Here they attain final release, and from him beings are born again.

BP 1.54.8

क्षणा मुहूर्ता दिवसा निशाः पक्षाश्च नित्यशः ।
मासाः संवत्सराश्चैव ऋतवोऽथ युगानि च ॥

Moments, time divisions, days, nights, fortnights, months, years, seasons, and ages.

BP 1.54.9

सदादित्यादृते ह्येषा कालसङ्ख्या न विद्यते ।
कालादृते न नियमो नाग्निर्न हवनक्रिया ॥

Apart from the Sun, this reckoning of time does not exist. Without time there is no rule, no fire, and no act of offering.

BP 1.54.10

ऋतूनामविभागाच्च, पुष्पमूलफलं कुतः ।
कुतः सस्यविनिष्पत्तिस्तृणौषधिगणाः कुतः ॥

Without division of the seasons, whence flowers, roots, and fruits. Whence the growth of crops, and the groups of grasses and herbs.

BP 1.54.11

अभावो व्यवहाराणां जन्तूनां दिवि चेह च ।
जगत्प्रतपनमृते भास्करं वारितस्करम् ॥

There would be no activity among creatures in heaven or here without Bhāskara, who heats the world and draws up water.

BP 1.54.12

नावृष्ट्या तपते सूर्यो नावृष्ट्या परिविश्यते ।
नावृष्ट्या विकृतिं धत्ते वारिणा दीप्यते रविः ॥

The Sun does not burn in a drought, nor is he enveloped in a drought. He does not undergo change in a drought, and Ravi shines by means of water.

BP 1.54.13

वसन्ते कपिलः सूर्यो ग्रीष्मे काञ्चनसप्रभः ।
श्वेतो वर्णेन वर्षासु पाण्डुः शरदि भास्करः ॥

In spring the Sun is tawny, in summer he is bright like gold. In the rainy season he is white in color, and in autumn Bhāskara is pale.

BP 1.54.14

हेमन्ते ताम्रवर्णस्तु शिशिरे लोहितो रविः ।
इति वर्णाः समाख्याताः शृणु वर्णफलं हर ॥

In the cold season he is copper colored, and in winter Ravi is red. These colors have been declared. Hear the results of the colors, O Hara.

BP 1.54.15

कृष्णोभयाय जगतस्ताम्रः सेनापतिं विनाशयति ।
पीतो नरेन्द्रपुत्रं श्वेतस्तु पुरोहितं हन्ति ॥

Black brings fear to the world, copper destroys the commander, yellow destroys the son of the king, and white kills the priest.

BP 1.54.16

चित्रोऽथ वापि धूम्रो रवी रश्मिव्याकुलं करोत्युच्चैः ।
तस्करशस्त्रनिपातैर्यदि न सलिलमाशु पातयति ॥

When the Sun is mottled or smoky, his rays are greatly scattered. If he does not soon bring rain, blows from thieves and weapons will fall.
