Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 106 — Pāpanāśinī Saptamī Vrata

BP 1.106.1

ब्रह्मोवाच। पुनश्चैतन्महाभाग श्रूयतां गदतो मम। प्रोक्तं खगेन देवानां तिथिमाहात्म्यमुत्तमम् ॥

Brahmā said: Again, O greatly fortunate one, listen to me as I speak of the excellent greatness of the lunar day that was taught by the Bird-bannered Lord to the gods.

BP 1.106.2

विष्णुरुवाच। विजयातिजया चैव जयन्ती च महातिथिः। त्वत्तः श्रुता सुरथेष्ठ ब्रूहि मे पापनाशिनीम् ॥

Viṣṇu said: Vijayā, Atijayā, and Jayantī are great lunar days, as I have heard from you, O best of the Suras. Tell me of the sin-destroying one.

pāpa-nāśinīm — the sin-destroying one
Transliteration
viṣṇuruvāca | vijayātijayā caiva jayantī ca mahātithiḥ | tvattaḥ śrutā suratheṣṭha brūhi me pāpanāśinīm || 2 ||

BP 1.106.3

तथोत्तरायणं ब्रूहि शस्तं यद्भास्करार्चने। यत्र सम्पूजितो भानुर्भवेत्सर्वाघनाशनः। तन्मे कथय यत्नेन भक्त्या पृष्टोऽक्षयं फलम् ॥

Tell me also about the auspicious Uttarāyaṇa for the worship of Bhāskara, where, when Bhānu is fully worshipped, he becomes the destroyer of every sin. Asked with devotion, explain that to me carefully, together with its imperishable fruit.

BP 1.106.4

Brahmā said: In the bright fortnight, whichever constellation belongs to Ravi on the seventh day, then that Saptamī is greatly meritorious and sin-destroying.

BP 1.106.5

तस्यां सम्पूज्य देवेशं चित्रभानुं जगद्गुरुम्। सप्तजन्मकृतात्पापान्मुच्यते नात्र संशयः ॥

Having fully worshipped the Lord of the gods, Citrabhānu, the teacher of the world, on that day, one is freed from sins committed over seven births—of this there is no doubt.

BP 1.106.6

यश्चोपवासं कुरुते तस्यां नियतमानसः। सर्वपापविमुक्तात्मा सूर्यलोके महीयते ॥

BP 1.106.7

BP 1.106.9

सामवेदफलं साम यजुर्वेदफलं यजुः। अथर्वा अथर्वांगिरसो निखिलं यच्छते रविः ॥
