Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 106 — The Vow of the Seventh Day That Destroys Sin

BP 1.106.1

ब्रह्मोवाच। पुनश्चैतन्महाभाग श्रूयतां गदतो मम। प्रोक्तं खगेन देवानां तिथिमाहात्म्यमुत्तमम् ॥

Brahmā said. Again, O greatly fortunate one, hear from me as I speak of the excellent greatness of the lunar day declared by the bird to the gods.

brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said;
punaḥ — again;
ca — and;
etat — this;
mahā-bhāga — O greatly fortunate one;
śrūyatām — let it be heard;
gaditaḥ — spoken;
mama — by me;
proktam — taught;
khagena — by the Bird-bannered Lord;
devānām — to the gods;
tithi-māhātmyam — greatness of the lunar day;
uttamam — excellent

BP 1.106.2

विष्णुरुवाच। विजयातिजया चैव जयन्ती च महातिथिः। त्वत्तः श्रुता सुरथेष्ठ ब्रूहि मे पापनाशिनीम् ॥

Viṣṇu said. Vijayā, Atijayā, and Jayantī are great lunar days. I have heard of them from you, O best of the warriors. Tell me of the day that destroys sin.

viṣṇuḥ uvāca — Viṣṇu said;
vijayā — Vijayā;
atijayā — Atijayā;
ca eva — and indeed;
jayantī — Jayantī;
ca — and;
mahā-tithiḥ — great lunar day;
tvattaḥ — from you;
śrutā — heard;
suratha-iṣṭha — O best of the Suras;
brūhi — tell;
me — to me;
pāpa-nāśinīm — the sin-destroying one

BP 1.106.3

तथोत्तरायणं ब्रूहि शस्तं यद्भास्करार्चने। यत्र सम्पूजितो भानुर्भवेत्सर्वाघनाशनः। तन्मे कथय यत्नेन भक्त्या पृष्टोऽक्षयं फलम् ॥

Tell me also about the northern course, which is approved for the worship of Bhāskara, where fully worshipped, Bhānu becomes the destroyer of every sin. Tell me carefully its imperishable fruit, as I ask with devotion.

tathā — likewise;
uttarāyaṇam — Uttarāyaṇa;
brūhi — tell;
śastam — auspicious;
yat — which;
bhāskara-arcane — in the worship of Bhāskara;
yatra — where;
sampūjitaḥ — fully worshipped;
bhānuḥ — Bhānu;
bhavet — becomes;
sarva-aghana-āśanaḥ — destroyer of every sin;
tat — that;
me — to me;
kathaya — explain;
yatnena — carefully;
bhaktyā — with devotion;
pṛṣṭaḥ — asked;
akṣayam — imperishable;
phalam — fruit

BP 1.106.4

ब्रह्मोवाच। शुक्लपक्षे तु सप्तम्यां यदर्क्षं तु रवेर्भवेत्। तदा स्यात्सा महापुण्या सप्तमी पापनाशिनी ॥

Brahmā said. On the seventh day of the bright fortnight, when the constellation of Ravi prevails, that seventh day is greatly meritorious and destroys sin.

brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said;
śukla-pakṣe — in the bright fortnight;
tu — indeed;
saptamyām — on the seventh day;
yat — whichever;
ṛkṣam — constellation;
tu — indeed;
raveḥ — of Ravi;
bhavet — may be;
tadā — then;
syāt — becomes;
sā — that;
mahā-puṇyā — greatly meritorious;
saptamī — Saptamī;
pāpa-nāśinī — sin-destroying

BP 1.106.5

तस्यां सम्पूज्य देवेशं चित्रभानुं जगद्गुरुम्। सप्तजन्मकृतात्पापान्मुच्यते नात्र संशयः ॥

On that day, having fully worshipped the Lord of the gods, Citrabhānu, the Teacher of the world, one is freed from sins made over seven births, there is no doubt.

tasyām — on that;
sampūjya — having fully worshipped;
deva-īśam — Lord of the gods;
citra-bhānum — Citrabhānu;
jagat-gurum — teacher of the world;
sapta-janma-kṛtāt — committed over seven births;
pāpāt — from sin;
mucyate — is freed;
na atra saṃśayaḥ — there is no doubt here

BP 1.106.6

यश्चोपवासं कुरुते तस्यां नियतमानसः। सर्वपापविमुक्तात्मा सूर्यलोके महीयते ॥

Whoever fasts on that day with a controlled mind, his self freed from all sins, is honored in the world of the Sun.

yaḥ ca — and whoever;
upavāsam — fast;
kurute — performs;
tasyām — on that;
niyata-mānasaḥ — with controlled mind;
sarva-pāpa-vimukta-ātmā — whose self is freed from every sin;
sūrya-loke — in the world of Sūrya;
mahīyate — is honored

BP 1.106.7

दानं यद्दीयते किञ्चित्तमुद्दिश्य दिवाकरम्। होमो वा क्रियते तत्र तत्सर्वं चाक्षयं भवेत् ॥

Whatever gift is given to Divākara, or whatever fire offering is made there, all of it becomes imperishable.

divākaram — Divākara;

tatra — there;

ca — and;
akṣayam — imperishable;
bhavet — becomes

BP 1.106.8

एक ऋग्वेदः पुरतो जप्तः श्रद्धापरेण तु। ऋग्वेदस्य समस्तस्य यच्छते सत्फलं ध्रुवम् ॥

A single recitation of the Ṛgveda before him by one devoted to faith surely gives the good fruit of the entire Ṛgveda.

ṛg-vedaḥ — Ṛgveda;

BP 1.106.9

सामवेदफलं साम यजुर्वेदफलं यजुः। अथर्वा अथर्वांगिरसो निखिलं यच्छते रविः ॥

A song gives the fruit of the Sāmaveda, and a sacrificial formula the fruit of the Yajurveda. Ravi grants the whole of the Atharva and Aṅgiras collection.

sāma — Sāman;
sāmaveda-phalam — fruit of the Sāmaveda;
yajuḥ — Yajus;
yajurveda-phalam — fruit of the Yajurveda;
atharvā — Atharva;
atharvāṅgirasaḥ — of the Atharva-Aṅgiras;
nikhilam — entire;
yacchate — grants;
raviḥ — Ravi

BP 1.106.10

तारका इव राजन्ते द्योतमाना दिवानिशम्। समभ्यर्च्य च सप्तम्यां देवदेवं दिवाकरम् ॥

Having fully worshipped the God of gods, Divākara, on the seventh day, they shine like stars, radiant day and night.

samabhyarcya — having fully worshipped;

saptamyām — on the seventh day;
deva-devam — God of gods;
divākaram — Divākara

BP 1.106.11

यत्र पापमशेषं वै नाशयत्यत्र भास्करः। कर्तव्या सप्तमी कृष्ण तेनोक्ता पापनाशिनी ॥

Where Bhāskara destroys every sin without remainder, there the seventh day must be observed, O Kṛṣṇa. For this reason it is called the day that destroys sin.

BP 1.106.12

अस्यां समभ्यर्च्य रविं याति सौरपुरं नरः। विमानवरमारुह्य कर्पूरोद्भवमुत्तमम् ॥

Having fully worshipped Ravi on this day, a person goes to the solar city, ascending an excellent aerial vehicle born of camphor.

BP 1.106.13

तेजसा कविसंकाशः प्रभया सूर्यसन्निभः। कान्त्यात्रेयसमः कृष्ण शौर्ये हरिसमः सदा ॥

Radiant like Kavi in splendor, like the Sun in brilliance, equal to the son of Atri in beauty, always equal to Hari in valor, O Kṛṣṇa,

BP 1.106.14

मोदते तत्र सुचिरं वृन्दारकगणैः सह। पुनरेत्य भुवं कृष्ण भवेद्वै क्ष्माधिपाधिपः ॥

He rejoices there for a long time with the hosts of the gods. Returning again to earth, O Kṛṣṇa, he becomes the lord of earthly lords.
