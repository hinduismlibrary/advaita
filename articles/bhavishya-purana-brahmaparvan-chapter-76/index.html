Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 76 — Nārada’s Vision of the Solar Retinue

BP 1.76.1

नारद उवाच । कदाचित् पर्यटंल् लोकान् सूर्यलोकं अहं गतः । तत्र दृष्टो मया सूर्यः सर्वदेवगंऐर् वृतः ॥ ॥

Nārada said: Once, while wandering through the worlds, I went to the world of the Sun. There I saw the Sun surrounded by all the hosts of the gods.

Word-for-word
nāradaḥ uvāca — Nārada said;
kadācit — once;
paryaṭan lokān — wandering through the worlds;
sūrya-lokam — to the world of the Sun;
aham gataḥ — I went;
tatra — there;
dṛṣṭaḥ mayā — seen by me;
sūryaḥ — the Sun;
sarva-deva-gaṇaiḥ — by all the hosts of gods;
vṛtaḥ — surrounded.
Transliteration
nārada uvāca | kadācit paryaṭaṃl lokān sūryalokaṃ ahaṃ gataḥ | tatra dṛṣṭo mayā sūryaḥ sarvadevagaṃair vṛtaḥ || 1 ||

BP 1.76.2

गन्धर्वैर् अप्सरोभिश् च नागैर् यक्षैश् च राक्षसैः । तत्र गायन्ति गन्धर्वा नृत्यन्त्य् अप्सरसस् तथा ॥ ॥

Gandharvas, Apsarases, Nāgas, Yakṣas, and Rākṣasas were there; the Gandharvas sang and the Apsarases danced.

Word-for-word
gandharvaiḥ — by Gandharvas;
apsarobhiḥ ca — and by Apsarases;
nāgaiḥ — by Nāgas;
yakṣaiḥ ca — and by Yakṣas;
rākṣasaiḥ — by Rākṣasas;
tatra — there;
gāyanti — sing;
gandharvāḥ — Gandharvas;
nṛtyanti — dance;
apsarasaḥ tathā — the Apsarases likewise.
Transliteration
gandharvair apsarobhiś ca nāgair yakṣaiś ca rākṣasaiḥ | tatra gāyanti gandharvā nṛtyanty apsarasas tathā || 2 ||

BP 1.76.3

रक्षन्त्य् उद्गतशस्त्रास् तं यक्षराक्षसपन्नगाः । ऋचो यजूंषि सामानि मूर्तिमन्तीह सर्वशः । तत्कृतैर् विविधैः स्तोत्रैः स्तुवन्ति ऋषयो रविम् ॥ ॥

The Yakṣas, Rākṣasas, and Nāgas guarded him with weapons raised. The Ṛks, Yajus formulas, and Sāmans were present there in embodied form; with hymns of many kinds fashioned from them, the Ṛṣis praised the Sun.

Word-for-word
rakṣanti — protect;
udgata-śastrāḥ — with weapons raised;
tam — him;
yakṣa-rākṣasa-pannagāḥ — Yakṣas, Rākṣasas, and Nāgas;
ṛcaḥ — Ṛk verses;
yajūṃṣi — Yajus formulas;
sāmāni — Sāmans;
mūrtimanti iha sarvaśaḥ — embodied here in every way;
tat-kṛtaiḥ — made from them;
vividhaiḥ stotraiḥ — with various hymns;
stuvanti — praise;
ṛṣayaḥ — the Ṛṣis;
ravim — the Sun.
Transliteration
rakṣanty udgataśastrās taṃ yakṣarākṣasapannagāḥ | ṛco yajūṃṣi sāmāni mūrtimantīha sarvaśaḥ | tatkṛtair vividhaiḥ stotraiḥ stuvanti ṛṣayo ravim || 3 ||

BP 1.76.4

मूर्तिमत्यः स्थितास् तत्र तिस्रः संध्याः शुभाननाः । गृहीतवज्रनाराचाः परिवार्य रविं स्थिताः ॥ ॥

There stood the three Sandhyās in embodied form, beautiful-faced, holding thunderbolts and iron arrows; surrounding the Sun, they remained stationed there.

Word-for-word
mūrtimatyaḥ — embodied;
sthitāḥ tatra — standing there;
tisraḥ saṃdhyāḥ — the three Sandhyās;
śubhānanāḥ — beautiful-faced;
gṛhīta-vajra-nārācāḥ — holding thunderbolts and iron arrows;
parivārya — surrounding;
ravim — the Sun;
sthitāḥ — standing.
Transliteration
mūrtimatyaḥ sthitās tatra tisraḥ saṃdhyāḥ śubhānanāḥ | gṛhītavajranārācāḥ parivārya raviṃ sthitāḥ || 4 ||

BP 1.76.5

अरुणा वर्णतः पूर्वा मध्यमा चेन्दुसन्निभा । तृतीया क्ष्माजसंकाशा संध्या चैव प्रकीर्तिता ॥ ॥

The first was red in color, the middle one resembled the moon, and the third Sandhyā was said to have the appearance of the earth.

Word-for-word
aruṇā — red;
varṇataḥ — in color;
pūrvā — the first;
madhyamā — the middle one;
ca indu-sannibhā — and resembling the moon;
tṛtīyā — the third;
kṣmāja-saṃkāśā — resembling the earth;
saṃdhyā — Sandhyā;
ca eva prakīrtitā — was indeed proclaimed.
Transliteration
aruṇā varṇataḥ pūrvā madhyamā cendusannibhā | tṛtīyā kṣmājasaṃkāśā saṃdhyā caiva prakīrtitā || 5 ||

BP 1.76.6

आदित्या वसवो रुद्रा मरुतो ऽथाश्विनौ तथा । त्रिसंध्यं पूजयन्त्य् अर्कं तथान्ये च दिवौकसः ॥ ॥

The Ādityas, Vasus, Rudras, Maruts, and the Aśvins, together with other gods of heaven, worshipped Arka at the three Sandhyās.

Word-for-word
ādityāḥ — Ādityas;
vasavaḥ — Vasus;
rudrāḥ — Rudras;
marutaḥ — Maruts;
atha aśvinau tathā — and likewise the two Aśvins;
tri-saṃdhyam — at the three Sandhyās;
pūjayanti — worship;
arkam — Arka, the Sun;
tathā anye — likewise others;
divaukasaḥ — gods of heaven.
Transliteration
ādityā vasavo rudrā maruto ’thāśvinau tathā | trisaṃdhyaṃ pūjayanty arkaṃ tathānye ca divaukasaḥ || 6 ||

BP 1.76.7

ईरयञ् जयशब्दं त्व् इन्द्रस् तत्रैव तिष्ठति । कविश् च त्र्यम्बको देवस् त्रिसंध्यं पूजयन्ति वै ॥ ॥

Indra stood there, uttering cries of victory. Kavi and the god Tryambaka worshipped at the three Sandhyās.

Word-for-word
īrayan — uttering;
jaya-śabdam — the sound of victory;
tu — indeed;
indraḥ — Indra;
tatra eva tiṣṭhati — stands there;
kaviḥ ca — Kavi and;
tryambakaḥ devaḥ — the god Tryambaka;
tri-saṃdhyam — at the three Sandhyās;
pūjayanti vai — indeed worship.
Transliteration
īrayañ jayaśabdaṃ tv indras tatraiva tiṣṭhati | kaviś ca tryambako devas trisaṃdhyaṃ pūjayanti vai || 7 ||

BP 1.76.8

दिनादाव् अम्बुजाकारं पूजयेद् अम्बुजासनम् । चक्ररूपं तु मध्याह्ने घृतार्चिः पूजयेत् सदा ॥ ॥

At the beginning of the day one should worship him seated upon a lotus, in lotus form; at midday one should always worship him in the form of a discus, as a flame of ghee.

Word-for-word
dinādau — at the beginning of the day;
ambuja-ākāram — in lotus form;
pūjayet — one should worship;
ambuja-āsanam — seated upon a lotus;
cakra-rūpam — in the form of a discus;
tu — but;
madhyāhne — at midday;
ghṛta-arciḥ — as a ghee-flame;
pūjayet sadā — one should always worship.
Transliteration
dinādāv ambujākāraṃ pūjayed ambujāsanam | cakrarūpaṃ tu madhyāhne ghṛtārciḥ pūjayet sadā || 8 ||

BP 1.76.9

पूजयेत् सगणं रात्रौ विपुलाज्यस्वरूपिणम् । रविं भक्त्या सदा देवं कंजार्धकृतशेखरः ॥ ॥

At night one should worship the god Ravi with his retinue, in the form of abundant clarified butter, with devotion always; he bears a crest made of half a lotus.

Word-for-word
pūjayet — one should worship;
sagaṇam — with the retinue;
rātrau — at night;
vipula-ajya-svarūpiṇam — in the form of abundant clarified butter;
ravim — Ravi;
bhaktyā — with devotion;
sadā — always;
devam — the god;
kañja-ardha-kṛta-śekharaḥ — whose crest is made of half a lotus.
Transliteration
pūjayet sagaṇaṃ rātrau vipulājyasvarūpiṇam | raviṃ bhaktyā sadā devaṃ kaṃjārdhakṛtaśekharaḥ || 9 ||

BP 1.76.10

सारथ्यं कुरुते तस्य पतगस्याग्रजः सदा । वहमानो रथं दिव्यं कालावयवनिर्मितम् ॥ ॥

pataga-asya agrajaḥ — the elder brother of the Bird;
sadā — always;

ratham divyam — the divine chariot;
kāla-avayava-nirmitam — made from the limbs of Time.
Transliteration
sārathyaṃ kurute tasya patagasyāgrajaḥ sadā | vahamāno rathaṃ divyaṃ kālāvayavanirmitam || 10 ||

BP 1.76.11

हरितैः सप्तभिर् युक्तं छन्दोभिर् वाजिरूपिभिः ॥ ॥

It is yoked with seven green steeds in the form of the metres.

Word-for-word

chandobhiḥ — with metres;
vāji-rūpibhiḥ — having the form of horses.
Transliteration

BP 1.76.12

द्वे भार्ये पार्श्वयोस् तस्य राज्ञीनिक्षुभसंज्ञिते । तथान्यैर् नामभिर् देवाः परिवार्य रविं स्थिताः ॥ ॥

He has two wives at his sides, known as Rājñī and Nikṣubhā. The gods, under other names as well, stood surrounding Ravi.

BP 1.76.13

पिङ्गलो लेखकस् तत्र तथान्यो दण्डनायकः । राजाश्रोषौ च द्वौ द्वारे स्थितौ कल्माषपक्षिणौ ॥ ॥

Piṅgala was the scribe there, and another was the officer of punishment. Two speckled birds, Rājāśroṣa and his companion, stood at the doorway.

Transliteration

BP 1.76.14

ततो व्योम चतुःशृङ्गं मेरोः सदृशलक्षणम् । दिण्डिस् तथाग्रतस् तस्य दिक्षु चान्ये स्थिताः सुराः ॥ ॥

Then there was the four-horned firmament, possessing features resembling Meru. Diṇḍi stood before it, and other gods stood in the directions.

meroḥ sadṛśa-lakṣaṇam — having features resembling Meru;
diṇḍiḥ — Diṇḍi;

Transliteration

BP 1.76.15

एवं सर्वगमं देवं प्रदीप्तं जगति द्विज । ब्रह्माद्यैः संस्तुतं देवं गीर्वाणैर् ऋषभोत्तमम् । ग्रहेशं भुवनेशानम् आदित्यं शरणं व्रज ॥ ॥

Thus, O twice-born one, take refuge in Āditya, the all-pervading and radiant god in the universe, praised by Brahmā and the others, the supreme bull among the gods, lord of the planets and lord of the worlds.

Word-for-word

gīrvāṇaiḥ — by the gods;
ṛṣabha-uttamam — supreme bull;
graheśam — lord of the planets;

Transliteration
evaṃ sarvagamaṃ devaṃ pradīptaṃ jagati dvija | brahmādyaiḥ saṃstutaṃ devaṃ gīrvāṇair ṛṣabhottamam | graheśaṃ bhuvaneśānam ādityaṃ śaraṇaṃ vraja || 15 ||

BP 1.76.16

BP 1.76.17

BP 1.76.18

पिङ्गलश् चापि कस् तत्र किं चासौ लिखते सदा । राजाश्रोषौ च कौ तत्र कौ च कल्माषपक्षिणौ ॥ ॥
