Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 76 — Nārada’s Vision of the Solar Retinue

BP 1.76.1

नारद उवाच । कदाचित् पर्यटंल् लोकान् सूर्यलोकं अहं गतः । तत्र दृष्टो मया सूर्यः सर्वदेवगंऐर् वृतः ॥ ॥

Nārada said. Once, while roaming among the worlds, I went to the world of the Sun. There I saw the Sun surrounded by all the hosts of gods.

nāradaḥ uvāca — Nārada said;
kadācit — once;
paryaṭan lokān — wandering through the worlds;
sūrya-lokam — to the world of the Sun;
aham gataḥ — I went;
tatra — there;
dṛṣṭaḥ mayā — seen by me;
sūryaḥ — the Sun;
sarva-deva-gaṇaiḥ — by all the hosts of gods;
vṛtaḥ — surrounded.

BP 1.76.2

गन्धर्वैर् अप्सरोभिश् च नागैर् यक्षैश् च राक्षसैः । तत्र गायन्ति गन्धर्वा नृत्यन्त्य् अप्सरसस् तथा ॥ ॥

The Gandharvas, Apsarases, Nāgas, Yakṣas, and demons were there. The Gandharvas sang there, and the Apsarases danced.

gandharvaiḥ — by Gandharvas;
apsarobhiḥ ca — and by Apsarases;
nāgaiḥ — by Nāgas;
yakṣaiḥ ca — and by Yakṣas;
rākṣasaiḥ — by Rākṣasas;
tatra — there;
gāyanti — sing;
gandharvāḥ — Gandharvas;
nṛtyanti — dance;
apsarasaḥ tathā — the Apsarases likewise.

BP 1.76.3

रक्षन्त्य् उद्गतशस्त्रास् तं यक्षराक्षसपन्नगाः । ऋचो यजूंषि सामानि मूर्तिमन्तीह सर्वशः । तत्कृतैर् विविधैः स्तोत्रैः स्तुवन्ति ऋषयो रविम् ॥ ॥

The Yakṣas, demons, and Nāgas guarded him with weapons raised. The hymns, sacrificial formulas, and chants stood embodied there in every form. With various praises made from them, the sages praised the Sun.

rakṣanti — protect;
udgata-śastrāḥ — with weapons raised;
tam — him;
yakṣa-rākṣasa-pannagāḥ — Yakṣas, Rākṣasas, and Nāgas;
ṛcaḥ — Ṛk verses;
yajūṃṣi — Yajus formulas;
sāmāni — Sāmans;
mūrtimanti iha sarvaśaḥ — embodied here in every way;
tat-kṛtaiḥ — made from them;
vividhaiḥ stotraiḥ — with various hymns;
stuvanti — praise;
ṛṣayaḥ — the Ṛṣis;
ravim — the Sun.

BP 1.76.4

मूर्तिमत्यः स्थितास् तत्र तिस्रः संध्याः शुभाननाः । गृहीतवज्रनाराचाः परिवार्य रविं स्थिताः ॥ ॥

There stood the three Twilights embodied, fair of face, holding thunderbolts and iron arrows, surrounding the Sun.

mūrtimatyaḥ — embodied;
sthitāḥ tatra — standing there;
tisraḥ saṃdhyāḥ — the three Sandhyās;
śubhānanāḥ — beautiful-faced;
gṛhīta-vajra-nārācāḥ — holding thunderbolts and iron arrows;
parivārya — surrounding;
ravim — the Sun;
sthitāḥ — standing.

BP 1.76.5

अरुणा वर्णतः पूर्वा मध्यमा चेन्दुसन्निभा । तृतीया क्ष्माजसंकाशा संध्या चैव प्रकीर्तिता ॥ ॥

The first was red in color, the middle one resembled the Moon, and the third Twilight was said to be like the Earth.

aruṇā — red;
varṇataḥ — in color;
pūrvā — the first;
madhyamā — the middle one;
ca indu-sannibhā — and resembling the moon;
tṛtīyā — the third;
kṣmāja-saṃkāśā — resembling the earth;
saṃdhyā — Sandhyā;
ca eva prakīrtitā — was indeed proclaimed.

BP 1.76.6

आदित्या वसवो रुद्रा मरुतो ऽथाश्विनौ तथा । त्रिसंध्यं पूजयन्त्य् अर्कं तथान्ये च दिवौकसः ॥ ॥

The Ādityas, Vasus, Rudras, Maruts, and Aśvins worship the Sun at the three Twilights, and the other dwellers of heaven also.

ādityāḥ — Ādityas;
vasavaḥ — Vasus;
rudrāḥ — Rudras;
marutaḥ — Maruts;
atha aśvinau tathā — and likewise the two Aśvins;
tri-saṃdhyam — at the three Sandhyās;
pūjayanti — worship;
arkam — Arka, the Sun;
tathā anye — likewise others;
divaukasaḥ — gods of heaven.

BP 1.76.7

ईरयञ् जयशब्दं त्व् इन्द्रस् तत्रैव तिष्ठति । कविश् च त्र्यम्बको देवस् त्रिसंध्यं पूजयन्ति वै ॥ ॥

Indra stands there uttering the word victory. The Sage and the Three Eyed God worship at the three Twilights.

īrayan — uttering;
jaya-śabdam — the sound of victory;
tu — indeed;
indraḥ — Indra;
tatra eva tiṣṭhati — stands there;
kaviḥ ca — Kavi and;
tryambakaḥ devaḥ — the god Tryambaka;
tri-saṃdhyam — at the three Sandhyās;
pūjayanti vai — indeed worship.

BP 1.76.8

दिनादाव् अम्बुजाकारं पूजयेद् अम्बुजासनम् । चक्ररूपं तु मध्याह्ने घृतार्चिः पूजयेत् सदा ॥ ॥

At the beginning of the day one should worship the lotus shaped one seated on a lotus. At midday one should always worship him in the form of a discus, the flame of ghee.

dinādau — at the beginning of the day;
ambuja-ākāram — in lotus form;
pūjayet — one should worship;
ambuja-āsanam — seated upon a lotus;
cakra-rūpam — in the form of a discus;
tu — but;
madhyāhne — at midday;
ghṛta-arciḥ — as a ghee-flame;
pūjayet sadā — one should always worship.

BP 1.76.9

पूजयेत् सगणं रात्रौ विपुलाज्यस्वरूपिणम् । रविं भक्त्या सदा देवं कंजार्धकृतशेखरः ॥ ॥

At night one should worship the god Sun with his attendants, in the form of abundant ghee. He is always worshipped with devotion, with a crest made from half a lotus.

pūjayet — one should worship;
sagaṇam — with the retinue;
rātrau — at night;
vipula-ajya-svarūpiṇam — in the form of abundant clarified butter;
ravim — Ravi;
bhaktyā — with devotion;
sadā — always;
devam — the god;
kañja-ardha-kṛta-śekharaḥ — whose crest is made of half a lotus.

BP 1.76.10

सारथ्यं कुरुते तस्य पतगस्याग्रजः सदा । वहमानो रथं दिव्यं कालावयवनिर्मितम् ॥ ॥

The elder brother of the bird always serves as his charioteer, bearing the divine chariot formed from the parts of Time.

sārathyam kurute — performs the office of charioteer;
tasya — of him;
pataga-asya agrajaḥ — the elder brother of the Bird;
sadā — always;
vahamānaḥ — carrying;
ratham divyam — the divine chariot;
kāla-avayava-nirmitam — made from the limbs of Time.

BP 1.76.11

हरितैः सप्तभिर् युक्तं छन्दोभिर् वाजिरूपिभिः ॥ ॥

It is joined to seven green steeds shaped as meters.

haritaiḥ — green;
saptabhiḥ — seven;
yuktam — yoked;
chandobhiḥ — with metres;
vāji-rūpibhiḥ — having the form of horses.

BP 1.76.12

द्वे भार्ये पार्श्वयोस् तस्य राज्ञीनिक्षुभसंज्ञिते । तथान्यैर् नामभिर् देवाः परिवार्य रविं स्थिताः ॥ ॥

At his sides are two wives, named Rājñī and Nikṣubhā. Gods under other names also stand surrounding the Sun.

dve bhārye — two wives;
pārśvayoḥ tasya — at his sides;
rājñī-nikṣubha-saṃjñite — known as Rājñī and Nikṣubhā;
tathā anyaiḥ nāmabhiḥ — likewise by other names;
devāḥ — the gods;
parivārya — surrounding;
ravim — Ravi;
sthitāḥ — stood.

BP 1.76.13

पिङ्गलो लेखकस् तत्र तथान्यो दण्डनायकः । राजाश्रोषौ च द्वौ द्वारे स्थितौ कल्माषपक्षिणौ ॥ ॥

Piṅgala is the scribe there, and another is the officer of punishment. Two speckled birds, Rājā and Śroṣa, stand at the doorway.

piṅgalaḥ — Piṅgala;
lekhakaḥ — the scribe;
tatra — there;
tathā anyaḥ — and another;
daṇḍa-nāyakaḥ — officer of punishment;
rājāśroṣau — Rājāśroṣa and his companion;
ca — and;
dvau — two;
dvāre sthitau — standing at the doorway;
kalmāṣa-pakṣiṇau — speckled birds.

BP 1.76.14

ततो व्योम चतुःशृङ्गं मेरोः सदृशलक्षणम् । दिण्डिस् तथाग्रतस् तस्य दिक्षु चान्ये स्थिताः सुराः ॥ ॥

Then the four horned sky, with features resembling Meru, is there. Diṇḍi stands before him, and other gods stand in the directions.

tataḥ — then;
vyoma — the firmament;
catuḥ-śṛṅgam — four-horned;
meroḥ sadṛśa-lakṣaṇam — having features resembling Meru;
diṇḍiḥ — Diṇḍi;
tathā agrataḥ tasya — likewise before it;
dikṣu — in the directions;
ca anye surāḥ — and other gods;
sthitāḥ — stood.

BP 1.76.15

एवं सर्वगमं देवं प्रदीप्तं जगति द्विज । ब्रह्माद्यैः संस्तुतं देवं गीर्वाणैर् ऋषभोत्तमम् । ग्रहेशं भुवनेशानम् आदित्यं शरणं व्रज ॥ ॥

Thus, O Brahmin, take refuge in the Sun, the all pervading radiant god in the world, praised by Brahmā and the others, the chief of the gods, lord of planets, and ruler of worlds.

evam — thus;
sarva-gamam — all-pervading;
devam — the god;
pradīptam — radiant;
jagati — in the universe;
dvija — O twice-born one;
brahmādyaiḥ — by Brahmā and the others;
saṃstutam — praised;
devam — the god;
gīrvāṇaiḥ — by the gods;
ṛṣabha-uttamam — supreme bull;
graheśam — lord of the planets;
bhuvaneśānam — lord of the worlds;
ādityam — Āditya;
śaraṇam vraja — take refuge.

BP 1.76.16

साम्ब उवाच । तत्त्वतः श्रोतुम् इच्छामि कथं सर्वगतो रविः ॥ ॥

Sāmba said. I wish to hear truly how the Sun pervades all.

sāmbaḥ uvāca — Sāmba said;
tattvataḥ — truly, in reality;
śrotum icchāmi — I wish to hear;
katham — how;
sarva-gataḥ — all-pervading;
raviḥ — Ravi.

BP 1.76.17

कति वा रश्मयस् तस्य मूर्तयश् च कति स्मृताः । का राज्ञी निक्षुभा का च कश् चायं दण्डनायकः ॥ ॥

How many rays does he have, and how many forms are remembered. Who are Rājñī and Nikṣubhā, and who is this officer of punishment.

kati vā — how many, indeed;
raśmayaḥ tasya — his rays;
mūrtayaḥ ca — and forms;
kati smṛtāḥ — how many are remembered;
kā — who;
rājñī — Rājñī;
nikṣubhā — Nikṣubhā;
kā ca — and who;
kaḥ ca ayam — and who is this;
daṇḍa-nāyakaḥ — officer of punishment.

BP 1.76.18

पिङ्गलश् चापि कस् तत्र किं चासौ लिखते सदा । राजाश्रोषौ च कौ तत्र कौ च कल्माषपक्षिणौ ॥ ॥

Who is Piṅgala there, and what does he always write. Who are Rājā and Śroṣa there, and who are the two speckled birds.

piṅgalaḥ ca api — and also Piṅgala;
kaḥ tatra — who there;
kim ca asau likhate sadā — and what does he always write;
rājāśroṣau ca — and Rājāśroṣa and his companion;
kau tatra — who are the two there;
kau ca — and who are the two;
kalmāṣa-pakṣiṇau — speckled birds.

BP 1.76.19

किं दैवत्यं च तद् व्योम मेरोः सदृशलक्षणम् । को दिण्डिर् अग्रतस् तत्र के देवा दिक्षु ये स्थिताः ॥ ॥

What deity belongs to that sky, whose features resemble Meru. Who is Diṇḍi standing before him, and who are the gods standing in the directions.

kim daivatyaṃ — what deity;
ca — and;
tat vyoma — that firmament;
meroḥ sadṛśa-lakṣaṇam — having features resembling Meru;
kaḥ diṇḍiḥ — who is Diṇḍi;
agrataḥ tatra — before it there;
ke devāḥ — which gods;
dikṣu — in the directions;
ye sthitāḥ — who stand.

BP 1.76.20

तत्त्वतो निगमैश् चैव विस्तरेण वदस्व माम । येनाहं तत्त्वतो ज्ञात्वा व्रजामि शरणं द्विज ॥ ॥

Tell me truly and at length through the Vedas, so that, knowing truly, I may take refuge, O Brahmin.

tattvataḥ — truly;
nigamaiḥ ca eva — and indeed through the Vedas;
vistareṇa — in detail;
vadasva — tell;
mām — me;
yena — so that;
aham — I;
tattvataḥ jñātvā — knowing truly;
vrajāmi — I may go, take refuge;
śaraṇam — to refuge;
dvija — O twice-born one.
