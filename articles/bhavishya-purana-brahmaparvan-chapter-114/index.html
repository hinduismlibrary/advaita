Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 114 — Bathing the Sun Image with Ghee and Milk

BP 1.114.1

ब्रह्मोवाच।
स्थापितां प्रतिमां भानोः सम्यक्सम्पूज्य मानवः।
यं यं प्रार्थयते कामं तं तं प्राप्नोत्यसंशयम् ॥

Brahmā said. A person who properly worships the installed image of the Sun obtains every desire that he seeks, without doubt.

ब्रह्मा उवाच(brahmovāca) — Brahmā said;
स्थापिताम् प्रतिमाम् भानोः(sthāpitāṃ pratimāṃ bhānoḥ) — the installed image of Bhānu;
सम्यक् सम्पूज्य(samyak sampūjya) — having properly worshipped;
मानवः(mānavaḥ) — a person;
यम् यम् प्रार्थयते कामम्(yaṃ yaṃ prārthayate kāmam) — whatever desire he seeks;
तम् तम् प्राप्नोति(taṃ taṃ prāpnoti) — he obtains each one;
असंशयम्(asaṃśayam) — without doubt;

BP 1.114.2

यः स्नापयति देवस्य घृतेन प्रतिमां रवेः।
प्रस्थेप्रस्थे द्विजाग्र्याणां स ददाति गवां शतम् ॥

Whoever bathes the image of the Sun with ghee gives one hundred cows for every measure of ghee to the foremost Brahmins.

यः स्नापयति(yaḥ snāpayati) — who bathes;
देवस्य प्रतिमाम्(devasya pratimām) — the image of the god;
घृतेन(ghṛtena) — with ghee;
रवेः(raveḥ) — of Ravi;
प्रस्थे प्रस्थे(prasthe prasthe) — at each prastha;
द्विज-अग्र्याणाम्(dvijāgryāṇām) — to the foremost of the twice-born;
सः ददाति(sa dadāti) — he gives;
गवाम् शतम्(gavāṃ śatam) — one hundred cows;

BP 1.114.3

गवां शतस्य विप्रेभ्यो यद्वत्तस्य भवेत्फलम्।
घृतप्रस्थेन तद्भानोर् भवेत्स्नातकयोगिनाम् ॥

The fruit obtained by giving one hundred cows to Brahmins is the same fruit obtained by the contemplatives who bathe the Sun with a measure of ghee.

गवाम् शतस्य(gavāṃ śatasya) — of one hundred cows;
विप्रेभ्यः(viprebhyo) — to Brahmins;
यद्वत् तस्य भवेत् फलम्(yadvat tasya bhavet phalam) — whatever fruit would be for that;
घृत-प्रस्थेन(ghṛta-prasthena) — by a prastha of ghee;
तत् भानोः(tad bhānoḥ) — that of Bhānu;
भवेत् स्नातक-योगिनाम्(bhavet snātaka-yoginām) — comes for the bathing yogins;

BP 1.114.4

भूरिद्युम्नेन सम्प्राप्ता सप्तद्वीपा वसुन्धरा।
घटोदकेन मार्तण्डप्रतिमा स्नापिता किल ॥

Bhūridyumna obtained the earth with its seven islands when the image of the Sun was bathed with water from a pot.

भूरिद्युम्नेन(bhūridyumnena) — by Bhūridyumna;
सम्प्राप्ता(samprāptā) — was obtained;
सप्त-द्वीपा वसुन्धरा(sapta-dvīpā vasundharā) — the earth with seven continents;
घट-उदकेन(ghaṭodakena) — with water from a pot;
मार्तण्ड-प्रतिमा(mārtaṇḍa-pratimā) — the image of Mārtaṇḍa;
स्नापिता किल(snāpitā kila) — was indeed bathed;

BP 1.114.5

प्रतिमामसिताष्टम्यां घृतेन जगतीपतेः।
स्नापयित्वा समस्तेभ्यः पापेभ्यः कृष्ण मुच्यते ॥

Having bathed the image of the Lord of the world with ghee on the eighth day of the dark fortnight, one is freed from all sins, O Kṛṣṇa.

प्रतिमाम्(pratimām) — the image;
असित-अष्टम्याम्(asita-aṣṭamyām) — on the dark eighth day;
घृतेन(ghṛtena) — with ghee;
जगती-पतेः(jagatī-pateḥ) — of the Lord of the world;
स्नापयित्वा(snāpayitvā) — having bathed;
समस्तेभ्यः पापेभ्यः(samastebhyaḥ pāpebhyaḥ) — from all sins;
कृष्ण(kṛṣṇa) — O Kṛṣṇa;
मुच्यते(mucyate) — one is freed;

BP 1.114.6

सप्तम्यामथ षष्ठ्यां वा गव्येन हविषा रवेः।
स्नपनं तु भवेच्छ्रेष्ठं महापातकनाशनम् ॥

Bathing the Sun on the seventh or sixth day with an offering prepared from cow products is best and destroys great sins.

सप्तम्याम् अथ षष्ठ्याम् वा(saptamyām atha ṣaṣṭhyāṃ vā) — on the seventh or the sixth day;
गव्येन हविषा(gavyena haviṣā) — with an oblation of cow's milk;
रवेः(raveḥ) — of Ravi;
स्नपनम् तु भवेत् श्रेष्ठम्(snapanaṃ tu bhavec chreṣṭham) — bathing indeed is supreme;
महा-पातक-नाशनम्(mahā-pātaka-nāśanam) — destroying the great sins;

BP 1.114.7

ज्ञानतोऽज्ञानतो वापि यत्पापं कुरुते नरः।
तत्क्षालयति सन्ध्यायां घृतेन स्नपनं रवेः ॥

Whatever sin a person commits knowingly or unknowingly, bathing the Sun with ghee at twilight washes it away.

ज्ञानतः अज्ञानतः वा अपि(jñānato'jñānato vāpi) — knowingly or unknowingly;
यत् पापम्(yat pāpam) — whatever sin;
कुरुते नरः(kurute naraḥ) — a person commits;
तत् क्षालयति(tat kṣālayati) — washes that away;
सन्ध्यायाम्(sandhyāyām) — at twilight;
घृतेन स्नपनम् रवेः(ghṛtena snapanaṃ raveḥ) — bathing Ravi with ghee;

BP 1.114.8

सर्वयज्ञमयो भानुर्हव्यानां परमं घृतम्।
तयोरशेषपापानां क्षालकः सङ्गमो भवेत् ॥

The Sun is formed of all sacrifices, and ghee is supreme among offerings. Their union becomes the cleanser of all sins.

सर्व-यज्ञ-मयः भानुः(sarva-yajña-mayo bhānuḥ) — Bhānu formed of every sacrifice;
हव्यानाम् परमम् घृतम्(havyānāṃ paramaṃ ghṛtam) — ghee supreme among oblations;
तयोः सङ्गमः(tayor saṅgamaḥ) — the coming together of those two;
भवेत्(bhavet) — becomes;
अशेष-पापानाम् क्षालकः(aśeṣa-pāpānāṃ kṣālakaḥ) — a cleanser of all sins;

BP 1.114.9

येषु क्षीरवहा नद्यो ह्रदाः पायसकर्दमाः।
मोदते तेषु लोकेषु क्षीरस्नानकरो रवेः ॥

The person who bathes the Sun with milk rejoices in those worlds where rivers flow with milk and lakes have mud made of sweet rice pudding.

येषु(yeṣu) — in which;
क्षीर-वहाः नद्यः(kṣīra-vahā nadyo) — rivers flowing with milk;
ह्रदाः(hradāḥ) — lakes;
पायस-कर्दमाः(pāyasa-kardamāḥ) — having sweet porridge as mud;
मोदते तेषु लोकेषु(modate teṣu lokeṣu) — he rejoices in those worlds;
क्षीर-स्नान-करः रवेः(kṣīra-snāna-karo raveḥ) — the one who bathes Ravi with milk;

BP 1.114.10

आह्लादं निर्वृतिस्थानमारोग्यं चारुरूपताम्।
सप्तजन्मान्यवाप्नोति क्षीरस्नानपरो रवेः ॥

For seven births, one who delights in bathing the Sun with milk obtains joy, a place of peace, health, and beautiful form.

आह्लादम्(āhlādam) — delight;
निर्वृति-स्थानम्(nirvṛti-sthānam) — a place of peace;
आरोग्यम् च अरु-रूपताम्(ārogyaṃ cārurūpatām) — health and beautiful form;
सप्त-जन्मानि(sapta-janmāni) — for seven births;
अवाप्नोति(avāpnoti) — obtains;
क्षीर-स्नान-परः रवेः(kṣīra-snāna-paro raveḥ) — devoted to bathing Ravi with milk;

BP 1.114.11

दध्यादीनां विकाराणां क्षीरतः सम्भवो यथा।
यथा च विमलं क्षीरं यथा निर्वृतिकरकम्।
तथा च निर्मलं ज्ञानं भवत्यपि न संशयः ॥

Just as curd and other transformations arise from milk, and just as pure milk brings peace, so knowledge too becomes pure, without doubt.

दधि-आदीनाम् विकाराणाम्(dadhi-ādīnāṃ vikārāṇām) — of transformations such as curd;
क्षीरतः सम्भवः यथा(kṣīrataḥ sambhavo yathā) — just as the arising is from milk;
यथा च विमलम् क्षीरम्(yathā ca vimalaṃ kṣīram) — just as milk is pure;
यथा निर्वृति-करकम्(yathā nirvṛti-karakam) — just as it brings peace;
तथा च निर्मलम् ज्ञानम्(tathā ca nirmalaṃ jñānam) — so knowledge too is pure;
भवति अपि न संशयः(bhavaty api na saṃśayaḥ) — it becomes, there is no doubt;

BP 1.114.12

ग्रहानुकूलतां पुष्टिं प्रियत्वमखिले जने।
करोति भगवान्भानुः क्षीरस्नपनतोषितः ॥

The blessed Sun, pleased by bathing with milk, brings favorable planets, nourishment, and affection among all people.

ग्रह-अनुकूलताम्(grahānukūlatām) — favourableness of the planets;
पुष्टिम्(puṣṭim) — nourishment;
प्रियत्वम् अखिले जने(priyatvam akhile jane) — affection among all people;
करोति(karoti) — brings about;
भगवान् भानुः(bhagavān bhānuḥ) — the blessed Bhānu;
क्षीर-स्नपन-तोषितः(kṣīra-snapana-toṣitaḥ) — pleased by bathing with milk;

BP 1.114.13

सर्वस्य स्निग्धतामेति दृष्टमात्रे प्रसीदति।
घृतक्षीरेण देवेश स्नापिते तिमिरापहे ॥

When the Lord of gods, remover of darkness, is bathed with ghee and milk, everyone becomes tender, and he is pleased at the mere sight.

सर्वस्य स्निग्धताम् एति(sarvasya snigdhatām eti) — everyone attains tenderness;
दृष्ट-मात्रे(dṛṣṭa-mātre) — at the mere sight;
प्रसीदति(prasīdati) — he becomes gracious;
घृत-क्षीरेण(ghṛta-kṣīreṇa) — with ghee and milk;
देवेश(deveśa) — O Lord of gods;
स्नापिते(snāpite) — when bathed;
तिमिर-अपहे(timirāpahe) — O remover of darkness;
