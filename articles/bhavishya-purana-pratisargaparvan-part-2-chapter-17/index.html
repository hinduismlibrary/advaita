BP 3.2.17.1

सूत उवाच ।
पुनः प्रोवाच वेतालस्तदा ब्राह्मणरूपवान् ।
शृणु भूप महाभाग कथां तव मनोरमाम् ॥ 1 ॥

Sūta said: Then Vetāla, appearing in the form of a Brahmin, spoke again: Listen, fortunate king, to this delightful story.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

भूप bhūpa king
महाभाग mahābhāga O greatly fortunate one
कथां kathāṃ the story
तव tava your
मनोरमाम् manoramām delightful

Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

sūta uvāca | punaḥ provāca vetālastadā brāhmaṇarūpavān | śṛṇu bhūpa mahābhāga kathāṃ tava manoramām || 1 ||

साध्विति sādhviti saying “well done”
तं taṃ him
प्रोच्य procya having praised
सद्वाक्यैः sadvākyaiḥ with good words
समपूजयत् samapūjayat honored
इतिहासं itihāsaṃ a tale
पुनः punaḥ again
प्राह prāha said
परीक्षार्थे parīkṣārthe for the sake of testing
नृपाय nṛpāya to the king
सः saḥ he
