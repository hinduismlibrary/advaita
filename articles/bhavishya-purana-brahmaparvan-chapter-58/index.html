Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 58 — The Chariot Procession of the Sun

BP 1.58.1

अनेन विधिना यस्तु कुर्याद वा कारयेत वा ।
यात्रां भगवतो भक्त्या भास्करस्यामितौजसः ॥ १ ॥

Brahmā spoke. Whoever performs or arranges, with devotion and according to this procedure, the procession of the glorious Sun of immeasurable splendor.

anena vidhinā — by this procedure; yaḥ — whoever; kuryāt vā kārayeta vā — performs or causes to be performed; yātrām — the procession; bhagavataḥ — of the Blessed Lord; bhaktyā — with devotion; bhāskarasyāmitaujasaḥ — of Bhāskara, of immeasurable vigor

BP 1.58.2

स परार्धं तु वर्षाणां सूर्यलोके महीयते ।
कुले न जायते तस्य दरिद्रो व्याधितोऽपि वा ॥ २ ॥

He is honored in the world of the Sun for half of the lifetime of Brahmā. No poor or sick person is born in his family.

saḥ — he; parārdham varṣāṇām — half a parārdha of years; sūryaloke — in the solar world; mahīyate — is honored; kule — in the family; na jāyate — is not born; daridraḥ — poor; vyādhitaḥ api — even diseased

BP 1.58.3

अभ्यङ्गाय घृतं यस्तु भास्कराय प्रयच्छति ।
कृते तु वर्णतिलके स गच्छेत सुरभी पुरम ॥ ३ ॥

Whoever offers ghee to the Sun for anointing and applies a colored mark reaches the city of Surabhi.

abhyaṅgāya — for anointing; ghṛtam — ghee; yaḥ prayacchati — whoever offers; bhāskarāya — to Bhāskara; kṛte varṇatilake — when the colored mark has been made; saḥ gacchet — he goes; surabhī puram — to Surabhī’s city

BP 1.58.4

तीर्थोदकं तु यो भक्त्या गङ्गायाश च तथोदकम ।
स्नानार्थम आनयेद यस्तु भास्करस्य त्रिलोचन ॥ ४ ॥

Whoever brings, with devotion, water from sacred fords and likewise water of the Ganges for bathing the Sun, O three eyed one.

tīrtha-udakam — sacred water; yaḥ ānayet — whoever brings; bhaktyā — with devotion; gaṅgāyāḥ udakam — water of the Gaṅgā; snānārtham — for bathing; bhāskarasya — of Bhāskara; trilocana — O three-eyed one

BP 1.58.5

भक्त्या वर्णत्रयं दद्याद भास्करस्य त्रिलोचन ।
समाप्येहाखिलान कामान प्राप्नुयाद वरुणालयम ॥ ५ ॥

Whoever offers three colors to the Sun, O three eyed one, having fulfilled all desires here, attains the abode of Varuṇa.

bhaktyā — with devotion; varṇa-trayam — the three colors; dadyāt — should give; bhāskarasya — for Bhāskara; samāpya iha akhilān kāmān — having fulfilled all desires here; prāpnuyāt — would attain; varuṇālayam — Varuṇa’s abode

BP 1.58.6

रक्तवर्णं तु यो दद्याद धविष्यान्नं गुडौदनम ।
स गच्छेद दीप्तिमान रुद्र सूर्यलोकं पुरं वरम ॥ ६ ॥

Whoever offers red food, food fit for sacrifice, and rice cooked with jaggery, radiant, O Rudra, reaches the excellent city in the world of the Sun.

rakta-varṇam — red-colored; yaḥ dadyāt — whoever gives; haviṣyānnam — sacrificial food; guḍaudanam — jaggery rice; saḥ gacchet — he goes; dīptimān — radiant; rudra — O Rudra; sūryaloka-puram — city of the solar world; varam — excellent

BP 1.58.7

गच्छेत पुरवरे रुद्र यत्र देवः प्रजापतिः ।
स्नापयेद्यस तु वा भक्त्या भास्करं पूजयेत तथा ॥ ७ ॥

He reaches the excellent city, O Rudra, where the Lord of creatures dwells, whoever bathes the Sun with devotion and worships him.

gacchet — he goes; puravare — to the excellent city; yatra — where; devaḥ prajāpatiḥ — the god Prajāpati; snāpayet — should bathe; bhaktyā — with devotion; pūjayet tathā — should worship likewise

BP 1.58.8

स गच्छेद दीप्तिमान रुद्र सूर्यलोकं न संशयः ।
रथम आरोपयेद यस तु रथमार्गं प्रमार्जति ॥ ८ ॥

Whoever sets the Sun on a chariot and sweeps the chariot road reaches the world of the Sun, radiant, O Rudra, without doubt.

saḥ gacchet — he goes; dīptimān — radiant; na saṃśayaḥ — without doubt; ratham āropayet — should mount the chariot; rathamārgam — the chariot-road; pramārjati — cleans

BP 1.58.9

स याति वातसालोक्यं वाततुल्यपराक्रमः ।
रथस्य गच्छतो यस्तु मार्गे कुर्यात सुमण्डलम ॥ ९ ॥

Whoever makes a beautiful ritual design on the road as the chariot proceeds, attains the realm of the wind, with strength equal to the wind.

saḥ yāti — he goes; vāta-sālokyam — companionship with Vāta; vāta-tulya-parākramaḥ — having valor equal to wind; rathasya gacchataḥ — of the moving chariot; mārge — on the road; kuryāt — should make; sumaṇḍalam — a beautiful mandala

BP 1.58.10

स लोकं प्राप्नुयात्पुण्यं मारुतं नात्र संशयः ।
सूर्यस्य गच्छतो यस्तु मार्गं कुर्यात्सुमण्डलम ॥ १० ॥

Whoever makes a beautiful ritual design on the road along which the Sun proceeds attains the blessed realm of the wind, without doubt.

saḥ prāpnuyāt — he attains; lokam — the world; puṇyam mārutam — blessed world of the wind; na atra saṃśayaḥ — there is no doubt; sūryasya gacchataḥ — of the traveling Sun; mārgaṃ kuryāt — should make a road; sumaṇḍalam — beautiful

BP 1.58.11

स लोकं प्राप्नुयात्पुण्यं यः कुर्यान्मार्गमादरात ।
पुष्पप्रकरशोभाढ्यं शुभतोरणमण्डितम ॥ ११ ॥

Whoever reverently makes the road, rich with heaps of flowers and adorned with auspicious arches, attains the blessed realm.

saḥ prāpnuyāt — he attains; yaḥ kuryāt — whoever makes; mārgam — a road; ādarāt — respectfully; puṣpa-prakara-śobhā-āḍhyam — rich in the beauty of masses of flowers; śubha-toraṇa-maṇḍitam — adorned with auspicious gateways

BP 1.58.12

शंखतूर्यनिनादाढ्यं तथा प्रेक्षणकान्वितम ।
स याति परमं स्थानं यत्र देवो विभावसुः ॥ १२ ॥

The road is rich with the sound of conches and trumpets and furnished with performances. He reaches the highest place where the Sun dwells.

śaṅkha-tūrya-nināda-āḍhyam — rich in conch-and-trumpet sound; prekṣaṇaka-anvitam — furnished with spectacles; saḥ yāti — he goes; paramam sthānam — the supreme place; yatra devaḥ vibhāvasuḥ — where the god Vibhāvasu is

BP 1.58.13

देवेन सहितो यस्तु नृत्यन्गायंस्तथार्चयन ।
कुर्यान्महोत्सवं भक्त्या स याति परमं पदम ॥ १३ ॥

Whoever dances, sings, and worships together with the god, and makes a great festival with devotion, reaches the highest state.

devena sahitaḥ — accompanied by the god; nṛtyan gāyan arcayan — dancing, singing, worshiping; kuryāt mahotsavam — makes a great festival; bhaktyā — with devotion; saḥ yāti — he reaches; paramam padam — the supreme state

BP 1.58.14

प्रजागरं यस्तु कुर्याद्देवे रथगते रवौ ।
स सुखी पुण्यवान्नित्यं मोदते शाश्वतीः समाः ॥ १४ ॥

Whoever keeps vigil when the Sun is on the chariot remains happy and meritorious, rejoicing for eternal years.

prajāgaram kuryāt — should keep vigil; deve — when the god; rathagate ravau — Ravi has gone to the chariot; saḥ — he; sukhī — happy; puṇyavān — meritorious; nityam — always; modate — rejoices; śāśvatīḥ samāḥ — eternal years

BP 1.58.15

भक्तदासादिकं सर्वं यो ददाति रवेर नरः ।
सम्प्राप्येहाखिलान्कामान्सूर्यलोकमवाप्नुयात ॥ १५ ॥

Whoever gives the Sun everything beginning with food, servants, and the like, having obtained all desires here, reaches the world of the Sun.

bhakta-dāsa-ādikam sarvam — everything beginning with food and servants; yaḥ dadāti — whoever gives; raveḥ — to Ravi; naraḥ — the man; samprāpya iha akhilān kāmān — having attained all desires here; sūryalokam avāpnuyāt — would attain the solar world

BP 1.58.16

रथारूढस्य सूर्यस्य भ्रमतो दर्शनं हर ।
दुर्लभं देवशार्दूल विशेषात पुरतो व्रजन ॥ १६ ॥

Seeing the Sun mounted on the chariot as he travels is difficult, O Hara, especially when going before him, O tiger among gods.

rathārūḍhasya sūryasya — of the Sun mounted on the chariot; bhramataḥ — traveling; darśanam — sight; hara — O Hara; durlabham — difficult; devaśārdūla — O tiger among gods; viśeṣāt — especially; purataḥ vrajan — going in front

BP 1.58.17

उत्तराभिमुखं यान्तं तथा वै दक्षिणामुखम ।
धन्यः पश्यति देवेशं भास्करं भक्तवत्सलम ॥ १७ ॥

Fortunate is the person who sees the lord of the gods, the Sun who loves his devotees, moving northward and likewise facing southward.

uttarābhimukham yāntam — going northward; tathā — likewise; dakṣiṇāmukham — facing south; dhanyaḥ — fortunate; paśyati — sees; deveśam bhāskaram — lord of the gods, Bhāskara; bhaktavatsalam — loving toward devotees

BP 1.58.18

अथ संवत्सरे प्राप्ये भानोर यात्रादिने यदि ।
रथप्रकमणं तत्र न कथञ्चित्कृतं भवेत ॥ १८ ॥

If, in the year, the chariot procession has not been performed on the day of the Sun procession.

atha — now; saṃvatsare prāpye — when the year has arrived; bhānoḥ yātrādine — on the Sun’s procession-day; yadi — if; ratha-prakramaṇam — chariot movement; na kathañcit kṛtam bhavet — has not been done in any way

BP 1.58.19

ततो वै द्वादशे वर्षे कर्तव्यं भूतिमिच्छता ।
इन्द्रध्वजस्य चाप्येवं यदि नोत्थापनं कृतम ॥ १९ ॥

Then one who desires prosperity should perform it in the twelfth year, and likewise if the banner of Indra has not been raised.

tataḥ — then; dvādaśe varṣe — in the twelfth year; kartavyam — it must be done; bhūtim icchatā — by one desiring prosperity; indradhvajasya — of Indra’s banner; ca api evam — and likewise; na utthāpanam kṛtam — raising has not been done

BP 1.58.20

ततो वै द्वादशे वर्षे कर्तव्यं नान्तरा पुनः ।
यात्रायाश्चापि ये भङ्गं कुर्वन्ति वृषभध्वज ॥ २० ॥

It should again be performed in the twelfth year without an intervening period. Those who interrupt the procession, O bull banner.

tataḥ — then; dvādaśe varṣe — in the twelfth year; kartavyam — must be done; na antarā punaḥ — again without an interval; yātrāyāḥ ca api — and of the procession; ye kurvanti bhaṅgam — those who cause interruption; vṛṣabhadhvaja — O Bull-bannered one

BP 1.58.21

मन्देहा नाम ते ज्ञेया राक्षसा नात्र संशयः ।
ये कुर्वन्ति तथा यात्रां नरा धर्मध्वजस्य तु ॥ २१ ॥

They are to be known as demons called Mandehas, without doubt, those who obstruct the procession of the banner of righteousness.

mandehā nāma — named Mandehā; te jñeyāḥ — they are to be known; rākṣasāḥ — demons; na atra saṃśayaḥ — without doubt; ye narāḥ — those men; kurvanti tathā yātrām — cause such a procession; dharmadhvajasya — of Dharma’s banner

BP 1.58.22

इन्द्रादिदेवास्ते ज्ञेया गताश्च परमं पदम ।
पुनर्यात्राविधिं चेमं समासात्कथयामि ते ॥ २२ ॥

Those who attain the highest state are to be known as Indra and the other gods. I shall again tell you this procedure for the procession in brief.

indra-ādi-devāḥ — Indra and the other gods; te jñeyāḥ — they are to be known; gatāḥ — gone; paramam padam — to the supreme state; punaḥ — again; yātrā-vidhim imam — this procession-procedure; samāsāt — briefly; kathayāmi te — I tell you

BP 1.58.23

यं श्रुत्वा सर्वपापेभ्यो मुच्यते नात्र संशयः ।
वर्तमाने तु वै माघे रथे देवगणाश्रिते ॥ २३ ॥

Hearing this frees one from all sins, without doubt, when the month of Māgha is current and the chariot is attended by hosts of gods.

yam śrutvā — having heard which; sarva-pāpebhyaḥ mucyate — is freed from all sins; na atra saṃśayaḥ — without doubt; vartamāne māghe — when Māgha is current; rathe — in the chariot; deva-gaṇa-āśrite — attended by hosts of gods

BP 1.58.24

स तस्मिन्नेव मनसा स्थापनीयो रथोपरि ।
द्यौर्मही च द्विमूर्तिस्थे यथापूर्वं प्रतिष्ठिते ॥ २४ ॥

He should mentally establish him on that very chariot. Heaven and Earth are to be set there in their two forms as before.

saḥ — he; tasmin eva — in that very one; manasā — mentally; sthāpanīyaḥ — should be established; rathopari — upon the chariot; dyaus mahī — Heaven and Earth; dvimūrtī — two-formed; yathāpūrvam pratiṣṭhite — established as before

BP 1.58.25

तथैव राज्ञी द्यौर्ज्ञेया निक्षुभा पृथिवी स्मृता ।
एताभ्यामपि देवीभ्यां यथैव सवितुस्तथा ॥ २५ ॥

Likewise Heaven is to be known as the queen, and Nikṣubhā is remembered as Earth. The rite for these two goddesses is just as for the Sun.

tathā eva — likewise; rājñī — queen; dyaus — Dyaus; jñeyā — is to be known; nikṣubhā — Nikṣubhā; pṛthivī smṛtā — remembered as Earth; etābhyām devībhyām — with these two goddesses; yathā eva savituḥ — just as of Savitṛ

BP 1.58.26

दिण्डिनः पिंगलादीनां पृथुः कार्यो रथक्रमः ।
मनसा चिन्तयेदन्यां यथास्थानेषु देवताम ॥ २६ ॥

The entire arrangement for the chariot of Diṇḍin, Piṅgala, and the others should be made. One should mentally consider the other deities in their proper places.

diṇḍinaḥ piṅgalādīnām — of Diṇḍin, Piṅgala, and the others; pṛthuḥ — broad; kāryaḥ — should be made; rathakramaḥ — chariot arrangement; manasā — mentally; cintayet — should contemplate; anyām devatām — the other deity; yathāsthāneṣu — in the proper places

BP 1.58.27

दिक्पालांल्लोकपालांश्च कल्पयेत्मनसैव तु ।
देवो वेदमयश्चायं सर्वदेवमयस्तथा ॥ २७ ॥

One should mentally form the guardians of the directions and the guardians of the worlds. This god is made of the Vedas and likewise of all the gods.

dikpālān — guardians of directions; lokapālān ca — and guardians of worlds; kalpayet — should assign; manasā eva — only mentally; devaḥ vedamayaḥ — the god is of the nature of the Veda; sarvadevamayaḥ — made of all the gods

BP 1.58.28

मंडलमृङ्मयंचैव छन्दांस्यास्यं प्रकीर्तितम ।
गायत्रीचैव त्रिष्टुप्च जगत्यनुष्टुबेव च ॥ २८ ॥

The ritual circle is made of verses of praise, and its mouth is said to be the metres, Gāyatrī, Triṣṭubh, Jagatī, and Anuṣṭubh.

maṇḍalam — mandala; ṛṅmayam — made of Ṛg-verses; chandāṃsi — meters; āsyam — mouth; prakīrtitam — proclaimed; gāyatrī, triṣṭup, jagatī, anuṣṭup — the named meters

BP 1.58.29

पंक्तिश्च बृहतीचैव उष्णिगेव च सप्तमी ।
ततो देवमयत्वाच्च च्छन्दसांचैव कल्पनात ॥ २९ ॥

The seventh is Paṅkti, and likewise Bṛhatī and Uṣṇih. By the arrangement of the metres, it becomes divine.

paṅktiḥ — Paṅkti; bṛhatī — Bṛhatī; uṣṇik — Uṣṇih; saptamī — the seventh; tataḥ — then; deva-mayatvāt — because of being divine; chandasaḥ kalpanāt — because of the arrangement of the meters

BP 1.58.30

ततो वेदमयत्वाच्च तरणिर्लोकपूजितः ।
रथप्रकमणात्सूर्यो वोढव्यो ब्रह्मवादिभिः ॥ ३० ॥

Then, because it is made of the Vedas and honored by the worlds, the Sun should be borne in the chariot procession by those who speak of Brahman.

tataḥ — then; vedamayatvāt — because of being made of the Veda; taraṇiḥ — the Sun; lokapūjitaḥ — honored by the worlds; rathaprakramaṇāt — through the chariot-procession; sūryaḥ — the Sun; voḍhavyaḥ — should be carried; brahmavādibhiḥ — by Brahman-knowers

BP 1.58.31

उपवासपरैर्युक्तैर्वेदवेदांगपारगैः ।
रथंतु नारुहेच्छूद्रो भास्करस्य त्रिलोचन ॥ ३१ ॥

The chariot of the Sun should not be mounted by a Śūdra, O three eyed one, but by those devoted to fasting and learned in the Vedas and their branches.

upavāsa-paraiḥ yuktaiḥ — joined to devotion to fasting; veda-vedāṅga-pāragaiḥ — learned in the Veda and Vedāṅgas; ratham na āruhet — should not mount the chariot; śūdraḥ — a Śūdra; bhāskarasya — of Bhāskara; trilocana — O three-eyed one

BP 1.58.32

आरुह्य तरणेर्यानं व्रजेच्छूद्रो ह्यधोगतिम ।
यथोक्तकरणाद्रुद्र सदा शान्तिर्भवेन्नृणाम ॥ ३२ ॥

Having mounted the vehicle of the Sun, a Śūdra would go to a lower state. By doing what is prescribed, O Rudra, there is always peace for people.

āruhya — having mounted; taraṇeḥ yānam — the vehicle of the Sun; vrajet — would go; śūdraḥ — a Śūdra; adhogatim — a lower state; yathokta-karaṇāt — from doing what is prescribed; rudra — O Rudra; śāntiḥ bhavet — peace would be; nṛṇām — for people

BP 1.58.33

नायकश्चापि सर्वेषां देवानां तु दिवाकरः ।
विन्यसेत्तु रथानां तु देवतायतनेषु च ॥ ३३ ॥

The Sun is the leader of all the gods. One should place the chariots in the shrines of the deities.

nāyakaḥ — leader; sarveṣām devānām — of all the gods; divākaraḥ — Divākara; vinyaset — should arrange; rathānām — the chariots; devatā-āyataneṣu — in the temples of the deities

BP 1.58.34

ततो धूपोपहारैस्तु पूजयेत्प्रथमं रविम ।
दिग्देवानुचरांश्चैव पूजयेत्पूज्यते श्रिया ॥ ३४ ॥

Then one should first worship the Sun with incense offerings. One should also worship the attendants of the gods of the directions, and one is honored with prosperity.

tataḥ — then; dhūpa-upahāraiḥ — with incense offerings; pūjayet prathamam — should worship first; ravim — Ravi; dik-devānucarān — attendants of the directional gods; pūjayet — should worship; pūjyate śriyā — is honored with prosperity

BP 1.58.35

अपूज्य प्रथमं सूर्यम अपरांस्तु पूजयेत ।
तत्तद्भूतकृतं पाद्यं न प्रगृह्णन्ति देवताः ॥ ३५ ॥

Whoever worships the other gods without first worshiping the Sun, the gods do not accept the water offering made from the respective elements.

apūjya — having worshiped; prathamam — first; sūryam — the Sun; aparān — the others; pūjayet — should worship; tat-tad-bhūta-kṛtam pādyam — foot-water made for each element; na pragṛhṇanti — do not accept; devatāḥ — the deities

BP 1.58.36

यात्राकाले तु सम्प्राप्ते सवितुर दीक्षितां तनुम ।
ये द्रक्ष्यंति नरा भक्त्या ते भविष्यन्त्य अकल्मषाः ॥ ३६ ॥

When the time of the procession of the Sun has arrived, those who see his consecrated form with devotion will become free from sin.

yātrā-kāle samprāpte — when procession-time has arrived; savituḥ dīkṣitām tanum — Savitṛ’s consecrated body; ye narāḥ drakṣyanti — the men who will see; bhaktyā — with devotion; te bhaviṣyanti — they will become; akalmaṣāḥ — sinless

BP 1.58.37

पौर्णमास्याममायांच दर्शनं पुण्यदं स्मृतम ।
सप्तम्यांच तथा षष्ठ्यां दिने तस्य रवेस तथा ॥ ३७ ॥

Seeing him on the full moon and new moon is held to bestow merit, and likewise on the seventh and sixth days and on the day of the Sun.

paurṇamāsyām — on the full moon; amāyām — on the new moon; darśanam — seeing; puṇya-dam — giving merit; saptamyām — on the seventh; ṣaṣṭhyām — on the sixth; dine tasya raveḥ — on Ravi’s day

BP 1.58.38

आषाढी कार्त्तिकी माघी तिथ्यः पुण्यतमाः स्मृताः ।
महाभाग्यं तिथे पुण्यं यथा शास्त्रेषु गीयते ॥ ३८ ॥

The seventh days of Āṣāḍha, Kārttika, and Māgha are remembered as most meritorious. Their great fortune and merit are sung in the shastras.

āṣāḍhī, kārttikī, māghī — the named lunar days; tithyaḥ — lunar days; puṇyatamāḥ — most meritorious; mahābhāgyam — great fortune; puṇyam — merit; yathā śāstreṣu gīyate — as sung in the scriptures

BP 1.58.39

कार्त्तिक्यांतु विशेषेण महाकार्त्तिक्युदाहृता ।
एवं कालसमायोगाद्यात्राकालो विशिष्यते ॥ ३९ ॥

In particular, the seventh day of Kārttika is called the Great Kārttikī. Thus the time of the procession is distinguished by the conjunction of times.

kārttikyām — in Kārttikī; viśeṣeṇa — especially; mahā-kārttikī udāhṛtā — called the great Kārttikī; kāla-samāyogāt — from the conjunction of time; yātrā-kālaḥ — procession-time; viśiṣyate — is distinguished

BP 1.58.40

दर्शनंच महापुण्यं सर्वपापहरं भवेत ।
उपवासपरो यस्तु तस्मिन काले यतव्रतः ॥ ४० ॥

The sight is greatly meritorious and removes all sins. Whoever is devoted to fasting at that time should observe the vow.

darśanam — sight; mahāpuṇyam — greatly meritorious; sarva-pāpa-haram — removing all sins; bhavet — may be; upavāsa-paraḥ — devoted to fasting; yas tu — whoever; tasmin kāle — at that time; yata-vrataḥ — disciplined by a vow

BP 1.58.41

पूजयेत्तु रविं भक्त्या स गच्छेत परमां गतिम ।
देवोऽयं यज्ञपुरुषो लोकानुग्रहकांक्षया ॥ ४१ ॥

He should worship the Sun with devotion and attain the highest state. This god is the Sacrificial Person, desiring the welfare of the worlds.

pūjayet — should worship; ravim — Ravi; bhaktyā — with devotion; saḥ gacchet — he would attain; paramām gatim — supreme state; devaḥ ayam — this god; yajña-puruṣaḥ — Sacrificial Person; loka-anugraha-kāṃkṣayā — desiring the welfare of the worlds

BP 1.58.42

प्रतिमावस्थितो भूत्वा पूजां गृह्णात्यनुग्रहात ।
स्नानाद्दानाज्जपाद्धोमात्संयोगाद्देवकर्मणः ॥ ४२ ॥

Having taken a place in an image, he graciously accepts worship through bathing, gifts, recitation, fire offerings, and divine rites.

pratimā-avasthitaḥ bhūtvā — having become established in an image; pūjām gṛhṇāti — accepts worship; anugrahāt — through grace; snānāt dānāt japāt homāt — through bathing, gifts, recitation, and oblation; saṃyogāt deva-karmaṇaḥ — through divine acts

BP 1.58.43

कूर्चानां वपनाच्चैव दीक्षितः पुरुषो भवेत ।
कचानां वापनं कार्यं सूर्यभक्तैः सदा नरैः ॥ ४३ ॥

By shaving the tuft of hair, a person should become consecrated. Men devoted to the Sun should always shave their hair.

kūrcānām vapanāt — from shaving the tufts; dīkṣitaḥ puruṣaḥ bhavet — a man becomes consecrated; kacānām vapanam — shaving of the hair; kāryam — should be done; sūrya-bhaktaiḥ naraiḥ — by men devoted to the Sun

BP 1.58.44

सूर्यक्रतौ शुचिस त्वेवं दीक्षितः पुरुषो भवेत ।
चतुर्णामपि वर्णानां भक्त्या सूर्यस्य नित्यदा ॥ ४४ ॥

In the rite of the Sun, a person should thus be pure and consecrated. People of all four varṇas should do this daily with devotion to the Sun.

sūrya-kratau — in the solar rite; śuciḥ — pure; dīkṣitaḥ puruṣaḥ bhavet — a man should become consecrated; caturṇām varṇānām — of the four classes; bhaktyā — with devotion; sūryasya — to the Sun; nityadā — always

BP 1.58.45

एवं येऽत्र करिष्यन्ति ते नरा नित्यदीक्षिताः ।
चीर्णव्रता महात्मानस ते यास्यन्ति परां गतिम ॥ ४५ ॥

Those who do this here, ever consecrated and having fulfilled their vows, those great souls will reach the highest state.

ye atra kariṣyanti — those who perform here; te narāḥ — those men; nitya-dīkṣitāḥ — constantly consecrated; cīrṇa-vratāḥ — having fulfilled vows; mahātmānaḥ — great souls; yāsyanti — will go; parām gatim — to the supreme state

BP 1.58.46

इत्येषा कथिता रुद्र रथयात्रा दिवस्पतेः ।
यां श्रुत्वा वाचयित्वा सर्वरोगैर्विमुच्यते ॥ ४६ ॥

Thus, O Rudra, the chariot procession of the lord of the day has been told. By hearing and reciting it, one is freed from all diseases.

iti eṣā — thus this; kathitā — has been told; rudra — O Rudra; ratha-yātrā — chariot-procession; divaspateḥ — of the Lord of the Day; yām śrutvā vācayitvā — having heard and recited which; sarva-rogaiḥ vimucyate — is freed from all diseases

BP 1.58.47

कृत्वाच विधिवद्भक्त्या याति सूर्यसदो नरः ।
रथाह्वा कथिता रुद्र समासात्सप्तमी शुभा ॥ ४७ ॥

By performing it properly and with devotion, a person reaches the abode of the Sun. Thus, O Rudra, the auspicious seventh day called the Chariot has been told in brief.

kṛtvā vidhivat — having performed properly; bhaktyā — with devotion; yāti — goes; sūrya-sadaḥ — Sun’s abode; naraḥ — a man; rathāhvā — called Rathāhvā; kathitā — told; samāsāt — in brief; saptamī śubhā — auspicious Saptamī

BP 1.58.48

भूयोऽपि श्रूयतां रुद्र सप्तमी गदतो मम ॥ ४८ ॥

Hear again, O Rudra, the seventh day as I speak of it.

bhūyaḥ api — again; śrūyatām — let it be heard; rudra — O Rudra; saptamī — the Saptamī; gadataḥ mama — from me speaking
