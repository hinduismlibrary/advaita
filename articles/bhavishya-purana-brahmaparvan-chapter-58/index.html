Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 58 — Ratha-yātrā, the Solar Chariot Procession

BP 1.58.1

अनेन विधिना यस्तु कुर्याद वा कारयेत वा ।
यात्रां भगवतो भक्त्या भास्करस्यामितौजसः ॥

Brahmā said: Whoever, by this procedure, performs or causes to be performed with devotion the procession of the Blessed Bhāskara, whose splendor is immeasurable,

Word-for-word
anena vidhinā — by this procedure; yaḥ — whoever; kuryāt vā kārayeta vā — performs or causes to be performed; yātrām — the procession; bhagavataḥ — of the Blessed Lord; bhaktyā — with devotion; bhāskarasyāmitaujasaḥ — of Bhāskara, of immeasurable vigor
Transliteration
anena vidhinā yastu kuryād vā kārayeta vā |
yātrāṃ bhagavato bhaktyā bhāskarasyāmitaujasaḥ || 1 ||

BP 1.58.2

स परार्धं तु वर्षाणां सूर्यलोके महीयते ।
कुले न जायते तस्य दरिद्रो व्याधितोऽपि वा ॥

is honored in the world of the Sun for half a parārdha of years. In his family there is not born one who is poor or diseased.

Word-for-word
saḥ — he; parārdham varṣāṇām — half a parārdha of years; sūryaloke — in the solar world; mahīyate — is honored; kule — in the family; na jāyate — is not born; daridraḥ — poor; vyādhitaḥ api — even diseased
Transliteration
sa parārdhaṃ tu varṣāṇāṃ sūryaloke mahīyate |
kule na jāyate tasya daridro vyādhito'pi vā || 2 ||

BP 1.58.3

अभ्यङ्गाय घृतं यस्तु भास्कराय प्रयच्छति ।
कृते तु वर्णतिलके स गच्छेत सुरभी पुरम ॥

Whoever offers ghee to Bhāskara for anointing, after making the colored mark, goes to the city of Surabhī.

Word-for-word
abhyaṅgāya — for anointing; ghṛtam — ghee; yaḥ prayacchati — whoever offers; bhāskarāya — to Bhāskara; kṛte varṇatilake — when the colored mark has been made; saḥ gacchet — he goes; surabhī puram — to Surabhī’s city
Transliteration
abhyaṅgāya ghṛtaṃ yastu bhāskarāya prayacchati |
kṛte tu varṇatilake sa gacchet surabhī puram || 3 ||

BP 1.58.4

तीर्थोदकं तु यो भक्त्या गङ्गायाश च तथोदकम ।
स्नानार्थम आनयेद यस्तु भास्करस्य त्रिलोचन ॥

Whoever, with devotion, brings sacred water and also the water of the Gaṅgā for the bathing of Bhāskara, O three-eyed one,

Word-for-word
tīrtha-udakam — sacred water; yaḥ ānayet — whoever brings; bhaktyā — with devotion; gaṅgāyāḥ udakam — water of the Gaṅgā; snānārtham — for bathing; bhāskarasya — of Bhāskara; trilocana — O three-eyed one
Transliteration
tīrthodakaṃ tu yo bhaktyā gaṅgāyāś ca tathodakam |
snānārtham ānayed yastu bhāskarasya trilocana || 4 ||

BP 1.58.5

भक्त्या वर्णत्रयं दद्याद भास्करस्य त्रिलोचन ।
समाप्येहाखिलान कामान प्राप्नुयाद वरुणालयम ॥

Whoever gives the three colors to Bhāskara, O three-eyed one, having fulfilled all desires here, would attain the abode of Varuṇa.

Word-for-word
bhaktyā — with devotion; varṇa-trayam — the three colors; dadyāt — should give; bhāskarasya — for Bhāskara; samāpya iha akhilān kāmān — having fulfilled all desires here; prāpnuyāt — would attain; varuṇālayam — Varuṇa’s abode
Transliteration
bhaktyā varṇatrayaṃ dadyād bhāskarasya trilocana |
samāpyehākhilān kāmān prāpnuyād varuṇālayam || 5 ||

BP 1.58.6

रक्तवर्णं तु यो दद्याद धविष्यान्नं गुडौदनम ।
स गच्छेद दीप्तिमान रुद्र सूर्यलोकं पुरं वरम ॥

Whoever gives red-colored food, havis-food, and jaggery rice goes, O Rudra, radiant, to the excellent city of the Suns world.

Word-for-word
rakta-varṇam — red-colored; yaḥ dadyāt — whoever gives; haviṣyānnam — sacrificial food; guḍaudanam — jaggery rice; saḥ gacchet — he goes; dīptimān — radiant; rudra — O Rudra; sūryaloka-puram — city of the solar world; varam — excellent
Transliteration
raktavarṇaṃ tu yo dadyād dhaviṣyānnaṃ guḍaudanam |
sa gacched dīptimān rudra sūryalokaṃ puraṃ varam || 6 ||

BP 1.58.7

गच्छेत पुरवरे रुद्र यत्र देवः प्रजापतिः ।
स्नापयेद्यस तु वा भक्त्या भास्करं पूजयेत तथा ॥

He goes to the excellent city, O Rudra, where the god Prajāpati is, if with devotion he bathes Bhāskara and worships him likewise.

Word-for-word
gacchet — he goes; puravare — to the excellent city; yatra — where; devaḥ prajāpatiḥ — the god Prajāpati; snāpayet — should bathe; bhaktyā — with devotion; pūjayet tathā — should worship likewise
Transliteration
gacchet puravare rudra yatra devaḥ prajāpatiḥ |
snāpayedyas tu vā bhaktyā bhāskaraṃ pūjayet tathā || 7 ||

BP 1.58.8

स गच्छेद दीप्तिमान रुद्र सूर्यलोकं न संशयः ।
रथम आरोपयेद यस तु रथमार्गं प्रमार्जति ॥

He goes, radiant, to the Suns world, O Rudra, without doubt, whoever mounts the chariot and cleans the chariot-road.

Word-for-word
saḥ gacchet — he goes; dīptimān — radiant; na saṃśayaḥ — without doubt; ratham āropayet — should mount the chariot; rathamārgam — the chariot-road; pramārjati — cleans
Transliteration
sa gacched dīptimān rudra sūryalokaṃ na saṃśayaḥ |
ratham āropayed yas tu rathamārgaṃ pramārjati || 8 ||

BP 1.58.9

स याति वातसालोक्यं वाततुल्यपराक्रमः ।
रथस्य गच्छतो यस्तु मार्गे कुर्यात सुमण्डलम ॥

He attains companionship with Vāta and has valor equal to the wind, whoever makes a beautiful mandala on the road along which the chariot travels.

Word-for-word
saḥ yāti — he goes; vāta-sālokyam — companionship with Vāta; vāta-tulya-parākramaḥ — having valor equal to wind; rathasya gacchataḥ — of the moving chariot; mārge — on the road; kuryāt — should make; sumaṇḍalam — a beautiful mandala
Transliteration
sa yāti vātasālokyaṃ vātatulyaparākramaḥ |
rathasya gacchato yastu mārge kuryāt sumaṇḍalam || 9 ||

BP 1.58.10

स लोकं प्राप्नुयात्पुण्यं मारुतं नात्र संशयः ।
सूर्यस्य गच्छतो यस्तु मार्गं कुर्यात्सुमण्डलम ॥

He attains the blessed world of the wind, without doubt, whoever makes a beautiful road for the Sun as he travels.

Word-for-word
saḥ prāpnuyāt — he attains; lokam — the world; puṇyam mārutam — blessed world of the wind; na atra saṃśayaḥ — there is no doubt; sūryasya gacchataḥ — of the traveling Sun; mārgaṃ kuryāt — should make a road; sumaṇḍalam — beautiful
Transliteration
sa lokaṃ prāpnuyātpuṇyaṃ mārutaṃ nātra saṃśayaḥ |
sūryasya gacchato yastu mārgaṃ kuryātsumaṇḍalam || 10 ||

BP 1.58.11

स लोकं प्राप्नुयात्पुण्यं यः कुर्यान्मार्गमादरात ।
पुष्पप्रकरशोभाढ्यं शुभतोरणमण्डितम ॥

He attains the blessed world who respectfully makes a road rich with the beauty of masses of flowers and adorned with auspicious gateways.

Word-for-word
saḥ prāpnuyāt — he attains; yaḥ kuryāt — whoever makes; mārgam — a road; ādarāt — respectfully; puṣpa-prakara-śobhā-āḍhyam — rich in the beauty of masses of flowers; śubha-toraṇa-maṇḍitam — adorned with auspicious gateways
Transliteration
sa lokaṃ prāpnuyātpuṇyaṃ yaḥ kuryānmārgamādarāt |
puṣpaprakaraśobhāḍhyaṃ śubhatoraṇamaṇḍitam || 11 ||

BP 1.58.12

शंखतूर्यनिनादाढ्यं तथा प्रेक्षणकान्वितम ।
स याति परमं स्थानं यत्र देवो विभावसुः ॥

Rich in the sound of conches and trumpets and furnished with spectacles, he reaches the supreme place where the god Vibhāvasu dwells.

Word-for-word
śaṅkha-tūrya-nināda-āḍhyam — rich in conch-and-trumpet sound; prekṣaṇaka-anvitam — furnished with spectacles; saḥ yāti — he goes; paramam sthānam — the supreme place; yatra devaḥ vibhāvasuḥ — where the god Vibhāvasu is
Transliteration
śaṃkhatūryaninādāḍhyaṃ tathā prekṣaṇakānvitam |
sa yāti paramaṃ sthānaṃ yatra devo vibhāvasuḥ || 12 ||

BP 1.58.13

देवेन सहितो यस्तु नृत्यन्गायंस्तथार्चयन ।
कुर्यान्महोत्सवं भक्त्या स याति परमं पदम ॥

Whoever, accompanied by the god, dances, sings, and worships in this way, and makes a great festival with devotion, reaches the supreme state.

Word-for-word
devena sahitaḥ — accompanied by the god; nṛtyan gāyan arcayan — dancing, singing, worshiping; kuryāt mahotsavam — makes a great festival; bhaktyā — with devotion; saḥ yāti — he reaches; paramam padam — the supreme state
Transliteration
devena sahito yastu nṛtyangāyaṃstathārcayan |
kuryānmahotsavaṃ bhaktyā sa yāti paramaṃ padam || 13 ||

BP 1.58.14

प्रजागरं यस्तु कुर्याद्देवे रथगते रवौ ।
स सुखी पुण्यवान्नित्यं मोदते शाश्वतीः समाः ॥

Whoever keeps vigil when Ravi, the god, has mounted the chariot, is happy and meritorious and rejoices forever for eternal years.

Word-for-word
prajāgaram kuryāt — should keep vigil; deve — when the god; rathagate ravau — Ravi has gone to the chariot; saḥ — he; sukhī — happy; puṇyavān — meritorious; nityam — always; modate — rejoices; śāśvatīḥ samāḥ — eternal years
Transliteration
prajāgaraṃ yastu kuryāddeve rathagate ravau |
sa sukhī puṇyavānnityaṃ modate śāśvatīḥ samāḥ || 14 ||

BP 1.58.15

भक्तदासादिकं सर्वं यो ददाति रवेर नरः ।
सम्प्राप्येहाखिलान्कामान्सूर्यलोकमवाप्नुयात ॥

The man who gives all things beginning with food, servants, and the like to Ravi, having attained all desires here, would attain the Suns world.

Word-for-word
bhakta-dāsa-ādikam sarvam — everything beginning with food and servants; yaḥ dadāti — whoever gives; raveḥ — to Ravi; naraḥ — the man; samprāpya iha akhilān kāmān — having attained all desires here; sūryalokam avāpnuyāt — would attain the solar world
Transliteration
bhaktadāsādikaṃ sarvaṃ yo dadāti raver naraḥ |
samprāpyehākhilānkāmānsūryalokamavāpnuyāt || 15 ||

BP 1.58.16

रथारूढस्य सूर्यस्य भ्रमतो दर्शनं हर ।
दुर्लभं देवशार्दूल विशेषात पुरतो व्रजन ॥

Word-for-word

durlabhaṃ devaśārdūla viśeṣāt purato vrajan || 16 ||

BP 1.58.17

उत्तराभिमुखं यान्तं तथा वै दक्षिणामुखम ।
धन्यः पश्यति देवेशं भास्करं भक्तवत्सलम ॥

BP 1.58.18

अथ संवत्सरे प्राप्ये भानोर यात्रादिने यदि ।
रथप्रकमणं तत्र न कथञ्चित्कृतं भवेत ॥

Word-for-word
tataḥ — then; dvādaśe varṣe — in the twelfth year; kartavyam — it must be done; bhūtim icchatā — by one desiring prosperity; indradhvajasya — of Indra’s banner; ca api evam — and likewise; na utthāpanam kṛtam — raising has not been done

मन्देहा नाम ते ज्ञेया राक्षसा नात्र संशयः ।

BP 1.58.28

मंडलमृङ्मयंचैव छन्दांस्यास्यं प्रकीर्तितम ।
गायत्रीचैव त्रिष्टुप्च जगत्यनुष्टुबेव च ॥

The mandala is declared to be made of Ṛg-verses, and the meters are proclaimed as its mouth: Gāyatrī, Triṣṭubh, Jagatī, and Anuṣṭubh.

Word-for-word
maṇḍalam — mandala; ṛṅmayam — made of Ṛg-verses; chandāṃsi — meters; āsyam — mouth; prakīrtitam — proclaimed; gāyatrī, triṣṭup, jagatī, anuṣṭup — the named meters
Transliteration
maṃḍalamṛṅmayaṃcaiva chandāṃsyāsyaṃ prakīrtitam |
gāyatrīcaiva triṣṭupca jagatyanuṣṭubeva ca || 28 ||

BP 1.58.31

उपवासपरैर्युक्तैर्वेदवेदांगपारगैः ।

BP 1.58.32

आरुह्य तरणेर्यानं व्रजेच्छूद्रो ह्यधोगतिम ।

BP 1.58.34

BP 1.58.36

BP 1.58.37

पौर्णमास्याममायांच दर्शनं पुण्यदं स्मृतम ।
