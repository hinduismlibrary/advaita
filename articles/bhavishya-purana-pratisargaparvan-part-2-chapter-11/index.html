BP 3.2.11.1

सूत उवाच ।
भोः शौनक महाभाग स वैतालो हि देवता ।
राजानमब्रवीद्गाथां धर्मप्रश्नमयीं शुभाम् ॥ 1 ॥

Sūta said: O great Śaunaka, the Vetāla, a divine being, spoke this auspicious verse of questions about dharma to the king.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
सूत (sūta) — Sūta (the narrator);
उवाच (uvāca) — said;
भोः (bhoḥ) — O;
शौनक (śaunaka) — Śaunaka; O Śaunaka;
महाभाग (mahābhāga) — O greatly fortunate one;
स (sa) — he;
वैतालो (vaitālo) — Vetāla;
हि (hi) — indeed;
देवता (devatā) — deity;
राजानमब्रवीद्गाथां (rājānamabravīdgāthāṃ) — addressed the king with a verse;
धर्मप्रश्नमयीं (dharmapraśnamayīṃ) — containing a question about dharma;
शुभाम् (śubhām) — beautiful;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

sūta uvāca | bhoḥ śaunaka mahābhāga sa vaitālo hi devatā || | rājānamabravīdgāthāṃ dharmapraśnamayīṃ śubhām || 1 ||

BP 3.2.11.2

राजन्पुण्यपुरे रम्ये नानाजननिषेविते ।
धर्मवल्लभभूपालस्तत्र राज्यं पुराकरोत् ॥ 2 ॥

O king, in the lovely city of Puṇyapura there once ruled a righteous king devoted to dharma.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
राजन्पुण्यपुरे (rājanpuṇyapure) — O king, in the holy city;
रम्ये (ramye) — lovely;
नानाजननिषेविते (nānājananiṣevite) — served by many people;
धर्मवल्लभभूपालस्तत्र (dharmavallabhabhūpālastatra) — there the king was devoted to dharma;
राज्यं (rājyaṃ) — a reign;
पुराकरोत् (purākarot) — formerly ruled;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

rājanpuṇyapure ramye nānājananiṣevite | dharmavallabhabhūpālastatra rājyaṃ purākarot || 2 ||

BP 3.2.11.3

सत्यप्रकाशस्तन्मंत्री लक्ष्मीश्चामात्यकामिनी ।
कदाचित्स तु भूपालो मंत्रिणं प्राह धर्मवित् ॥ 3 ॥

His minister was Satyaprakāśa, and Lakṣmī was the minister’s beloved. One day the king, wise in dharma, spoke to his minister.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
सत्यप्रकाशस्तन्मंत्री (satyaprakāśastanmaṃtrī) — his minister Satyaprakāśa;
लक्ष्मीश्चामात्यकामिनी (lakṣmīścāmātyakāminī) — and Lakṣmī, the minister’s beloved;
कदाचित्स (kadācitsa) — once he;
तु (tu) — indeed; then;
भूपालो (bhūpālo) — the king;
मंत्रिणं (maṃtriṇaṃ) — the minister;
प्राह (prāha) — said;
धर्मवित् (dharmavit) — knowing dharma;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

satyaprakāśastanmaṃtrī lakṣmīścāmātyakāminī | kadācitsa tu bhūpālo maṃtriṇaṃ prāha dharmavit || 3 ||

BP 3.2.11.4

आनंदः कतिधा लोके तन्ममाचक्ष्व सत्तम ।
स होवाच महाराज सुखं चैव चतुर्विधम् ॥ 4 ॥

“How many kinds of bliss are there in the world? Tell me, excellent one.” He replied: “Happiness is fourfold, great king.”

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
आनंदः (ānaṃdaḥ) — bliss;
कतिधा (katidhā) — in how many ways;
लोके (loke) — in the world;
तन्ममाचक्ष्व (tanmamācakṣva) — tell me that;
सत्तम (sattama) — O excellent man;
स (sa) — he;
होवाच (hovāca) — he said;
महाराज (mahārāja) — O great king;
सुखं (sukhaṃ) — happiness;
चैव (caiva) — and also;
चतुर्विधम् (caturvidham) — fourfold;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

ānaṃdaḥ katidhā loke tanmamācakṣva sattama | sa hovāca mahārāja sukhaṃ caiva caturvidham || 4 ||

BP 3.2.11.5

ब्रह्मचर्याश्रमे यो वै ब्रह्मानंदो महोत्तमः ।
गार्हस्थ्ये विषयानंदो मध्यमः कथितो बुधैः ॥ 5 ॥

“In the student stage there is the highest bliss of Brahman; in household life, the wise call the pleasure of the senses intermediate.”

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
ब्रह्मचर्याश्रमे (brahmacaryāśrame) — in the student stage;
यो (yo) — whoever;
वै (vai) — indeed;
ब्रह्मानंदो (brahmānaṃdo) — the bliss of Brahman;
महोत्तमः (mahottamaḥ) — most excellent;
गार्हस्थ्ये (gārhasthye) — in household life;
विषयानंदो (viṣayānaṃdo) — sensual pleasure;
मध्यमः (madhyamaḥ) — intermediate;
कथितो (kathito) — is described;
बुधैः (budhaiḥ) — by the wise;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

brahmacaryāśrame yo vai brahmānaṃdo mahottamaḥ | gārhasthye viṣayānaṃdo madhyamaḥ kathito budhaiḥ || 5 ||

BP 3.2.11.6

वानप्रस्थे महाराज स धर्मानंदकोऽधमः ।
कर्मकांडेन चानंदः सत्यधर्मः स वै स्मृतः ॥ 6 ॥

“In the forest-dweller stage, O king, the bliss of dharma is lesser; the bliss of ritual action is called true dharma.”

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
वानप्रस्थे (vānaprasthe) — in the forest-dweller stage;
महाराज (mahārāja) — O great king;
स (sa) — he;
धर्मानंदकोऽधमः (dharmānaṃdako'dhamaḥ) — the bliss of dharma is inferior;
कर्मकांडेन (karmakāṃḍena) — through ritual action;
चानंदः (cānaṃdaḥ) — and bliss;
सत्यधर्मः (satyadharmaḥ) — true dharma;
स (sa) — he;
वै (vai) — indeed;
स्मृतः (smṛtaḥ) — remembered;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

vānaprasthe mahārāja sa dharmānaṃdako'dhamaḥ | karmakāṃḍena cānaṃdaḥ satyadharmaḥ sa vai smṛtaḥ || 6 ||

BP 3.2.11.7

संन्यस्ते तु शिवानंदस्स हि सर्वोत्तमोत्तमः ।
विषयानंदको राजन्स्त्रीप्रधानः प्रकीर्तितः ॥ 7 ॥

“In renunciation there is the bliss of Śiva, the highest of all. O king, sensual pleasure is said to depend chiefly on women.”

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
संन्यस्ते (saṃnyaste) — in renunciation;
तु (tu) — indeed; then;
शिवानंदस्स (śivānaṃdassa) — the bliss of Śiva is;
हि (hi) — indeed;
सर्वोत्तमोत्तमः (sarvottamottamaḥ) — the highest of all;
विषयानंदको (viṣayānaṃdako) — sensual pleasure;
राजन्स्त्रीप्रधानः (rājanstrīpradhānaḥ) — O king, chiefly dependent on women;
प्रकीर्तितः (prakīrtitaḥ) — is proclaimed;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

saṃnyaste tu śivānaṃdassa hi sarvottamottamaḥ | viṣayānaṃdako rājanstrīpradhānaḥ prakīrtitaḥ || 7 ||

BP 3.2.11.8

स्त्रियं विना सुखं नास्ति गृहस्थाश्रमके नृप ।
इति श्रुत्वा स भूपालो देशांतरमुपाययौ ॥ 8 ॥

“There is no happiness in household life without a woman, O king.” Hearing this, the king went to another country.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
स्त्रियं (striyaṃ) — a woman;
विना (vinā) — without;
सुखं (sukhaṃ) — happiness;
नास्ति (nāsti) — there is not;
गृहस्थाश्रमके (gṛhasthāśramake) — in the householder stage;
नृप (nṛpa) — O king;
इति (iti) — thus;
श्रुत्वा (śrutvā) — having heard;
स (sa) — he;
भूपालो (bhūpālo) — the king;
देशांतरमुपाययौ (deśāṃtaramupāyayau) — went to another country;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

striyaṃ vinā sukhaṃ nāsti gṛhasthāśramake nṛpa | iti śrutvā sa bhūpālo deśāṃtaramupāyayau || 8 ||

BP 3.2.11.9

पत्नीमन्वेषयामास स्वयोग्यां धर्मतत्पराम् ।
प्राप्तवान्न तु वामांगीं मनोवृत्त्यनुसारिणीम् ॥ 9 ॥

He searched for a wife suited to him and devoted to dharma, but found no lovely woman whose disposition matched his own.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
पत्नीमन्वेषयामास (patnīmanveṣayāmāsa) — he sought a wife;
स्वयोग्यां (svayogyāṃ) — suited to himself;
धर्मतत्पराम् (dharmatatparām) — devoted to dharma;
प्राप्तवान्न (prāptavānna) — he did not find;
तु (tu) — indeed; then;
वामांगीं (vāmāṃgīṃ) — a lovely woman;
मनोवृत्त्यनुसारिणीम् (manovṛttyanusāriṇīm) — one matching his disposition;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

patnīmanveṣayāmāsa svayogyāṃ dharmatatparām | prāptavānna tu vāmāṃgīṃ manovṛttyanusāriṇīm || 9 ||

BP 3.2.11.10

स भूपो मंत्रिणं प्राह नारीमन्वेषयाद्य भोः ।
नो चेत्प्राणानहं त्यक्ष्ये सत्यं वाक्यं ब्रवीम्यहम् ॥ 10 ॥

The king said to his minister: “Find me a woman today, or I will give up my life. I speak the truth.”

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
स (sa) — he;
भूपो (bhūpo) — the king;
मंत्रिणं (maṃtriṇaṃ) — the minister;
प्राह (prāha) — said;
नारीमन्वेषयाद्य (nārīmanveṣayādya) — find a woman today;
भोः (bhoḥ) — O;
नो (no) — otherwise;
चेत्प्राणानहं (cetprāṇānahaṃ) — if so, I my life;
त्यक्ष्ये (tyakṣye) — will give up;
सत्यं (satyaṃ) — the truth;
वाक्यं (vākyaṃ) — words;
ब्रवीम्यहम् (bravīmyaham) — I declare;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

sa bhūpo maṃtriṇaṃ prāha nārīmanveṣayādya bhoḥ | no cetprāṇānahaṃ tyakṣye satyaṃ vākyaṃ bravīmyaham || 10 ||

BP 3.2.11.11

इति श्रुत्वा ययौ मंत्री देशाद्देशांतरं प्रति ।
सिंधुदेशे च संप्राप्य समुद्रं प्रति सोऽगमत् ।
तुष्टाव मनसा सिंधुं सर्वतीर्थपतिं शुभम् ॥ 11 ॥

Hearing this, the minister travelled from country to country. Reaching Sindhu, he went to the sea and mentally praised Sindhu, lord of all sacred waters.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
इति (iti) — thus;
श्रुत्वा (śrutvā) — having heard;
ययौ (yayau) — went;
मंत्री (maṃtrī) — the minister;
देशाद्देशांतरं (deśāddeśāṃtaraṃ) — from country to country;
प्रति (prati) — toward;
सिंधुदेशे (siṃdhudeśe) — in the land of Sindhu;
च (ca) — and;
संप्राप्य (saṃprāpya) — having approached;
समुद्रं (samudraṃ) — the sea;
प्रति (prati) — toward;
सोऽगमत् (so'gamat) — he went;
तुष्टाव (tuṣṭāva) — praised; worshipped;
मनसा (manasā) — in her mind;
सिंधुं (siṃdhuṃ) — the Sindhu;
सर्वतीर्थपतिं (sarvatīrthapatiṃ) — lord of all sacred waters;
शुभम् (śubham) — auspicious;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

iti śrutvā yayau maṃtrī deśāddeśāṃtaraṃ prati | siṃdhudeśe ca saṃprāpya samudraṃ prati so'gamat | tuṣṭāva manasā siṃdhuṃ sarvatīrthapatiṃ śubham || 11 ||

BP 3.2.11.12

बुद्धिप्रकाश उवाच ।
सिंधुदेव नमस्तुभ्यं सर्वरत्नालय प्रभो ॥ 12 ॥

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
बुद्धिप्रकाश (buddhiprakāśa) — Buddhiprakāśa (the minister’s name);
उवाच (uvāca) — said;
सिंधुदेव (siṃdhudeva) — O god of the Sindhu;
नमस्तुभ्यं (namastubhyaṃ) — homage to you;
सर्वरत्नालय (sarvaratnālaya) — O abode of every jewel;
प्रभो (prabho) — O lord;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

buddhiprakāśa uvāca | siṃdhudeva namastubhyaṃ sarvaratnālaya prabho || 12 ||

BP 3.2.11.13

अहं ते शरणं प्राप्तः शरणागतवत्सल ।
त्वां नमामि जलाधीशं गंगादिसरितां पतिम् ॥ 13 ॥

Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

शरणं (śaraṇaṃ) — refuge;
प्राप्तः (prāptaḥ) — met; suffered;

जलाधीशं (jalādhīśaṃ) — the lord of waters;

Transliteration

BP 3.2.11.14

स्त्रीरत्नं देहि राज्ञोऽर्थे नो चेत्प्राणांस्त्यजाम्यहम् ।
इति श्रुत्वा प्रसन्नात्मा सागरः सरितां पतिः ॥ 14 ॥

“Give a jewel among women for the king; otherwise I shall give up my life.” Hearing this, the sea, lord of rivers, was pleased.

Word-for-word

स्त्रीरत्नं (strīratnaṃ) — a jewel among women;

श्रुत्वा (śrutvā) — having heard;
प्रसन्नात्मा (prasannātmā) — with a serene heart;

पतिः (patiḥ) — the lord;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

strīratnaṃ dehi rājño'rthe no cetprāṇāṃstyajāmyaham | iti śrutvā prasannātmā sāgaraḥ saritāṃ patiḥ || 14 ||

BP 3.2.11.15

जले वृक्षं सुवर्णांगं पत्रविद्रुमकं महत् ।
मुक्ता फलान्वितं दिव्यं मंत्रिणे समदर्शयत् ॥ 15 ॥

In the water it showed him a great tree with a golden trunk, coral leaves, and divine pearls for fruit.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
जले (jale) — in the water;
वृक्षं (vṛkṣaṃ) — a tree;
सुवर्णांगं (suvarṇāṃgaṃ) — golden-bodied;

महत् (mahat) — great;
मुक्ता (muktā) — pearls;
फलान्वितं (phalānvitaṃ) — bearing as fruit;

Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

jale vṛkṣaṃ suvarṇāṃgaṃ patravidrumakaṃ mahat | muktā phalānvitaṃ divyaṃ maṃtriṇe samadarśayat || 15 ||

BP 3.2.11.16

तस्योपरि स्थिता बाला सुकुमारी मनोरमा ।
तत्रैव सा लये जाता वृक्षेण सह भूपते ॥ 16 ॥

A lovely, delicate maiden stood upon it. She had appeared there together with the tree, O king.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
तस्योपरि (tasyopari) — upon it;

बाला (bālā) — the maiden;
सुकुमारी (sukumārī) — the delicate one;
मनोरमा (manoramā) — delightful;
तत्रैव (tatraiva) — right there;
सा (sā) — she;
लये (laye) — in union;
जाता (jātā) — born;
वृक्षेण (vṛkṣeṇa) — with the tree;
सह (saha) — with;
भूपते (bhūpate) — O king;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

tasyopari sthitā bālā sukumārī manoramā | tatraiva sā laye jātā vṛkṣeṇa saha bhūpate || 16 ||

BP 3.2.11.17

इति दृष्ट्वा महाश्चर्यं नृपांतिकमुपाययौ ।
वर्णयित्वा तु तत्सर्वं राज्ञा सार्धं समाप्तवान् ॥ 17 ॥

Seeing this great wonder, the minister returned to the king. He described it all and finished his account.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
इति (iti) — thus;
दृष्ट्वा (dṛṣṭvā) — having seen;
महाश्चर्यं (mahāścaryaṃ) — a great wonder;
नृपांतिकमुपाययौ (nṛpāṃtikamupāyayau) — came near the king;
वर्णयित्वा (varṇayitvā) — having described;
तु (tu) — indeed; then;
तत्सर्वं (tatsarvaṃ) — all that;

viṣayī yo hi bhūpālastasya rājyavināśanam | strīmadaṃ prāpya rājyasya sadā hānimavāpnuyāt || 34 ||

Textual basis

Devanāgarī follows Sanskrit Wikisource, Bhaviṣya Purāṇa, Pratisargaparvan, Part 2, Chapter 11, cross-checked with the Wisdomlib Sanskrit edition. IAST follows the linked Sanskrit transcription. The English translation is prepared for this reader; the word-for-word layer pairs source Devanāgarī words with IAST tokens. Verses 4–7 give the minister’s ranking of happiness; verses 24–28 contain an obscure curse and sexual coercion; verses 31–34 state the text’s gendered claim about kingship and women. These are rendered as narrative claims. The Wikisource transcription is adapted under CC BY-SA 4.0.
