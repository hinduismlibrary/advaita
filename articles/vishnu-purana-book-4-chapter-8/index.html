VP 4.8.1

पुरूरवसो ज्येष्ठः पुत्रो यस्त्वायुर्नामा स राहोदुहितरमुपयेमे

Pururava's eldest son's name was Aayu. He married Raahu's daughter.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

VP 4.8.6

गृत्समदस्य शौनकश्चातुर्वर्ण्यप्रवर्तयिताऽभूत्

Grutsamada had a son Shounaka, who was responsible for promulgation of the Chaaturvarnya.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

gṛtsamadasya śaunakaścāturvarṇyapravartayitā'bhūt

āyuṣaḥ pañcaputreṣu kṣatravṛddhasya santatiḥ . alpā proktāṣṭameyatra jātau dhanvantarirhariḥ . cāturvarṇyapravarttayitā-tadvaṃśe catvāro varṇā abhavannity arthaḥ ||६||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

आयुषः पञ्चपुत्रेषु क्षत्रवृद्धस्य सन्ततिः । अल्पा प्रोक्ताष्टमेयत्र जातौ धन्वन्तरिर्हरिः । चातुर्वर्ण्यप्रवर्त्तयिता-तद्वंशे चत्वारो वर्णा अभवन्नित्य् अर्थः ॥६॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Grutsamada had a son Shounaka, who was responsible for promulgation of the Chaaturvarnya. Sri Engal Aalwaan’s Commentary | |

VP 4.8.9

स हि संसिद्धकार्यकरणस्सकलसम्भूतिष्वशेषज्ञानविदा भगवता नारायणेन चातीतसम्भूतौ तस्मै वरो दत्तः

He had under control the prakruti, and because of that, his body and indriyaas never became old. They were always young. He had mastered that. In all his births, he was born from Ksheerasaagara, and at that time, was given a vara by none other than Naaraayana.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sa hi saṃsiddhakāryakaraṇassakalasambhūtiṣvaśeṣajñānavidā bhagavatā nārāyaṇena cātītasambhūtau tasmai varo dattaḥ

Śrīdhara

saṃsiddhāni matdharmarahitāni kāryakaraṇāni dehendriyāṇi yasya saḥ . sakalasambhūtiṣu sarveṣu janmasu aśeṣajñānavit sakalaśāstrajñaḥ || atītasambhūtau kṣīrābdherjanmāni ||९||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

संसिद्धानि मत्धर्मरहितानि कार्यकरणानि देहेन्द्रियाणि यस्य सः । सकलसम्भूतिषु सर्वेषु जन्मसु अशेषज्ञानवित् सकलशास्त्रज्ञः ॥ अतीतसम्भूतौ क्षीराब्धेर्जन्मानि ॥९॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

He had under control the prakruti, and because of that, his body and indriyaas never became old. They were always young. He had mastered that. In all his births, he was born from Ksheerasaagara, and at that time, was given a vara by none other than Naaraayana. Sri Engal Aalwaan’s Commentary | | |

VP 4.8.10

काशिराजगोत्रेऽवतीर्य त्वमष्टधा सम्यगायुर्वेदं करिष्यसि यज्ञभागभुग्भविष्यसीति

Naaraayana gave him a vara that during the Ksheera Saagara mathana, when he is born, he will be born in Kaashi raaja vamsha, and will create 8 angaas for Aayurveda, and will also get havirbhaaga from all the yajnyaas. \ Dhanvantari was responsible for the 8 angaas in Aayurveda. Kaaya, Baala, Graha, Oordhvaanga, Shalya, Damshtra, Jaraa, Visha are the 8 angaas. Kaaya is general, internal medicine. Baala is paediatric. Graha is psychology. Oordhvaanga is the E, N, T - Ear, Nose, Throat. Shalya is surgery. Damshtra is toxicology. Jaraa is that branch of Aayurveda treating old age related problems. Visha is aphrodisiacs. These are the 8 angaas of Aayurveda created by Dhanvantari. He also got a vara that he will receive havis from yajnya.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kāśirājagotre'vatīrya tvamaṣṭadhā samyagāyurvedaṃ kariṣyasi yajñabhāgabhugbhaviṣyasīti

Śrīdhara

aṣṭadheti . aṣṭaprakāramāyurvedaṃ kariṣyasi . aṣṭāṅgamiti vā pāṭhaḥ . taduktaṃ— kāyabālagrahoddhā ca śalyaṃ draṣṭuṃ jarā viṣam . aṣṭāvaṅgani tasyāhuścikitsā yeṣa saṃsthitā || yaddhā śalyaṃ śalākā bhūtavidyā kāyaśuddhiśca aṅgaṃ, tantraṃ, rasāyanaṃ, bājīkaraṇaṃ kumāratantram . ityaṣṭadhā ||१०||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अष्टधेति । अष्टप्रकारमायुर्वेदं करिष्यसि । अष्टाङ्गमिति वा पाठः । तदुक्तं— कायबालग्रहोद्धा च शल्यं द्रष्टुं जरा विषम् । अष्टावङ्गनि तस्याहुश्चिकित्सा येष संस्थिता ॥ यद्धा शल्यं शलाका भूतविद्या कायशुद्धिश्च अङ्गं, तन्त्रं, रसायनं, बाजीकरणं कुमारतन्त्रम् । इत्यष्टधा ॥१०॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Naaraayana gave him a vara that during the Ksheera Saagara mathana, when he is born, he will be born in Kaashi raaja vamsha, and will create 8 angaas for Aayurveda, and will also get havirbhaaga from all the yajnyaas. Kaaya is general, internal medicine. Baala is paediatric. Graha is psychology. Oordhvaanga is the E, N, T - Ear, Nose, Throat. Shalya is surgery. Damshtra is toxicology. He also got a vara that he will receive havis from yajnya.

VP 4.8.11

तस्य च धन्वन्तरेः पुत्रः केतुमान् केतुमतो भीमरथस्तस्यापि दिवोदासस्तस्यापि प्रतर्दनः

VP 4.8.17

षष्टिवर्षसहस्राणि षष्टिवर्षशतानि च ।
अलर्कादपरो नान्यो बुभुजे मेदिनीं युवा

For sixty thousand years and sixty hundreds of years, no one other than Alarka, while young, enjoyed the earth.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
षष्टि-वर्ष-सहस्राणि (ṣaṣṭi-varṣa-sahasrāṇi) — sixty thousand years
षष्टि-वर्ष-शतानि च (ṣaṣṭi-varṣa-śatāni ca) — and sixty hundreds of years
अलर्कात् (alarkāt) — than Alarka
अपरः (aparaḥ) — another
न अन्यः (na anyaḥ) — no other
बुभुजे (bubhuje) — enjoyed/ruled
मेदिनीम् (medinīm) — the earth
युवा (yuvā) — while youthful.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ṣaṣṭivarṣasahasrāṇi ṣaṣṭivarṣaśatāni ca | alarkādaparo nānyo bubhuje medinīṃ yuvā

Śrīdhara

ṣaṭ-ṣaṣṭi-sahasra-vatsaraṃ yāvad alarkād aparo na yuvā, tāvat kālaṃ cālarkād anyo na ca bubhuje medinīm ity anvayaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

षट्षष्टिसहस्रवत्सरं यावद् अलर्काद् अपरो न युवा, तावत् कालं चालर्काद् अन्यो न च बुभुजे मेदिनीम् इत्य् अन्वयः ॥१७॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

The construction is: for sixty-six thousand years, no one other than Alarka, remaining youthful, ruled the earth.

VP 4.8.20

ततश्च सत्यकेतुः तस्माद्विभुः तत्तनयस्सुविभुः तश्च सुकुमारस्तस्यापि धृष्टकेतुः ततश्च वीतिबोत्रः तस्माद्भार्गः भार्गस्य भार्गभूमिः ततश्चातुर्वर्ण्यप्रवृत्तिरित्येते काश्यप भूभृतः कथिताः

Dharmaketu had a son Satyaketu. His son was Vibhu. His son was Suvibhu. Then Sukumaara, then Drushtaketu, then Veetihotra, then Bhaarga. Bhaarga's son was Bhaargabhoomi. Bhaargabhoomi was responsible for establishing Chaaturvarnya. This is the story of all the kings of Kaashya vamsha.

VP 4.8.21

रजेस्तु सन्ततिः श्रूयताम्

Now, I will tell you about Raji's vamsha, who is another son of Pururavas. This completes Chapter 8. We just completed Chapter 8, of Amsha 4. Where the vamsha anucharita of Pururavas is being told. His eldest son is Aayu, whose vamsha is being told. Aayu had five sons, Nahusha, Kshatravruddha, Rambha, Raji, Anena. In this, Kshatravruddha had a son by name Kaashya, and Kaashya's vamsha was told. In which, a very famous king Alarka, who ruled over the earth for 66000 years, whose greatness is being sung even today, was told. Another son of Pururavas is Raji. Raji's vamsha is going to be told. Raji’s Vamsha Now, Chapter 9.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

rajestu santatiḥ śrūyatām
