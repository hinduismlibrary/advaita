Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 177 — Description of the Fire-Rite Procedure

BP 1.177.1

।। अरुण उवाच ।। पद्मरागप्रभा देवी चतुर्वदनपंकजा ।। अक्षमालार्पितकरा कमंडलुधरा शुभा ।। १ ।।

Aruṇa said: The auspicious goddess, ruby-radiant, with four lotus faces, a rosary in her hand, and a water-pot.

BP 1.177.2

ब्रह्माणी सौम्यवदना आदित्याराधने रता ।। शांतिं करोतु सुप्रीता आशीर्वादपरा खग ।। २ ।।

Brahmāṇī, gentle-faced, intent on worship of the Sun, greatly pleased, intent on giving blessings, may she bring peace, O bird.

BP 1.177.3

महाश्वेतेति विख्याता आदित्यदयिता सदा ।। हिमकुंदेंदुसदृशा महावृषभवाहिनी ।। ३ ।।

Known as Mahāśvetā, ever dear to the Sun, resembling snow, jasmine, and the moon, riding a great bull.

BP 1.177.4

त्रिशूलहस्तावरणा विश्रुताभरणा सती ।। चतुर्भुजा चतुर्वक्त्रा त्रिनेत्रा पापनाशिनी ।। वृषध्वजार्चनरता रुद्राणी शांतिदा भवेत् ।। ४ ।।

With a trident in hand, adorned with famous ornaments, virtuous, four-armed, four-faced, three-eyed, destroyer of sins, intent on worship of the Bull-bannered One, may Rudrāṇī become a giver of peace.

BP 1.177.5

मयूरवाहना देवी सिंदूरारुणविग्रहा ।। शक्तिहस्ता महाकाया सर्वालंकारभूषिता ।।५।।

The goddess whose mount is a peacock, whose body is vermilion-red, holding a spear, large-bodied, adorned with every ornament.

BP 1.177.6

सूर्यभक्ता महावीर्या सूर्यार्चनरता सदा ।। कौमारी वरदा देवी शांतिमाशु करोतु ते ।। ६ ।।

Devoted to the Sun, of great valor, always intent on worship of the Sun, may the boon-giving goddess Kaumārī quickly bring you peace.

BP 1.177.7

गदाचक्रधरा श्यामा पीतांबरधरा खग ।। चतुर्भुजा हि सा देवी वैष्णवी सुरपूजिता ।। ७ ।।

Bearing a mace and discus, dark, wearing a yellow garment, O bird, that goddess Vaiṣṇavī indeed is four-armed and worshipped by the gods.

BP 1.177.8

सूर्यार्चनपरा नित्यं सूर्यैकगतमानसा ।। शांतिं करोतु ते नित्यं सर्वासुरविमर्दिनी ।।८।।

Always devoted to worship of the Sun, with her mind fixed on the Sun alone, may the crusher of all demons always bring you peace.

BP 1.177.9

ऐरावतगजारूढा वज्रहस्ता महा बला ।। सर्वत्रलोचना देवी वर्णतः कर्बुरारुणा ।। ९ ।।

Mounted on the elephant Airāvata, thunderbolt in hand, of great strength, the goddess whose eyes are everywhere, mottled red in color.

BP 1.177.10

सिद्धगंधर्वनमिता सर्वालंकारभूषिता ।। इंद्राणी ते सदा देवी शांतिमाशु करोतु वै ।। १.१७७.१० ।।

Bowed to by perfected beings and celestial musicians, adorned with every ornament, may the goddess Indrāṇī always quickly bring you peace indeed.

BP 1.177.11

वराहघोणा विकटा वराहवरवाहिनी ।। श्यामावदाता या देवी शंखचक्रगदाधरा ।। ११ ।।

Boar-snouted, formidable, riding the excellent boar; the goddess who is dark and fair, bearing conch, discus, and mace.

BP 1.177.12

तेजयंतीति निमिषान्पूजयंती सदा रविम् ।। वाराही वरदा देवी तव शांतिं करोतु वै ।। १२ ।।

Called Tejayantī, ever worshipping the Sun at every moment, may the boon-giving goddess Vārāhī bring peace to you indeed.

BP 1.177.13

अर्धकोशा कटीक्षामा निर्मांसा स्नायुबंधनात् ।। करालवदना घोरा खड्गघंटोद्गता सती ।। १३ ।।

Half-cup-shaped, slender at the waist, fleshless, bound by sinews, terrible-faced and dreadful, the virtuous one, raising sword and bell.

BP 1.177.14

कपालमालिनी क्रूरा खट्वांगवरधारिणी ।। आरक्ता पिंगनयना गजचर्मावगुंठिता ।। १४ ।।

Garlanded with skulls, fierce, bearing the excellent skull-staff, red, tawny-eyed, covered with elephant hide.

BP 1.177.15

गोश्रुताभरणा देवी प्रेतस्थाननिवासिनी ।। शिवारूपेण घोरेण शिवरूपभयंकरी ।। चामुंडा चंडरूपेण सदा शांतिं करोतु ते ।। १५ ।।

The goddess adorned with Gośruta, dwelling in the place of the dead, terrible in Śiva’s fierce form, frightening in Śiva’s form; may Cāmuṇḍā, in her fierce form, always bring you peace.

BP 1.177.16

चंडमुंडकरा देवी मुंडदेहगता सती ।। कपालमालिनी क्रूरा खट्वांगवरधारिणी ।। १६ ।।

The goddess who slew Caṇḍa and Muṇḍa, the virtuous one who has entered Muṇḍa’s body, garlanded with skulls, fierce, bearing the excellent skull-staff.

BP 1.177.17

आकाशमातरो देव्यस्तथान्या लोकमातरः ।। भूतानां मातरः सर्वास्तथान्याः पितृमातरः ।। १७ ।।

Goddesses, mothers of the sky and other mothers of the worlds, all mothers of beings and other mothers of ancestors.

BP 1.177.18

वृद्धिश्राद्धेषु पूज्यंते यास्तु देव्यो मनीषिभिः ।। मात्रे प्रमात्रे तन्मात्रे इति मातृमुखास्तथा ।। १८ ।।

Those goddesses worshipped by the wise at rites for prosperity—Mother, Grandmother, Great-grandmother—are foremost among the Mothers.

BP 1.177.19

पितामही तु तन्माता वृद्धा या च पितामही ।। इत्येतास्तु पितामह्यः शांतिं ते पितृमातरः ।। १९ ।।

The paternal grandmother, that mother, and the aged paternal grandmother: these are the paternal grandmothers, mothers of the ancestors; may they bring you peace.

BP 1.177.20

सर्वा मातृमहादेव्यः स्वायुधाव्यग्रपाणयः ।। जगद्व्याप्य प्रतिष्ठंत्यो बलिकामा महोदयाः ।। २० ।।

All the great mother-goddesses, with hands intent on their own weapons, pervading the world and abiding, desiring offerings, of great glory.

BP 1.177.21

शांतिं कुर्वंतु ता नित्यमादित्याराधने रताः ।। शांतेन चेतसा शांत्यः शांतये तव शांतिदा ।। २१ ।।

May they always make peace, devoted to worship of the Sun; tranquil in mind, peace-bringing, may they grant peace for you.

BP 1.177.22

सर्वावयवमुख्येन गात्रेण च सुमध्यमा ।। पीतश्यामाति सौम्येन स्निग्धवर्णेन शोभना ।। २२ ।।

With every limb excellent and the body slender-waisted, yellow-dusky, exceedingly gentle, beautiful with a lustrous complexion.

BP 1.177.23

ललाटतिलकोपेता चंद्ररेखार्धधारिणी ।। चित्रांबरधरा देवी सर्वाभरणभूषिता ।। २३ ।।

Marked with a forehead mark, bearing half the moon’s crescent, wearing a variegated garment, the goddess adorned with every ornament.

BP 1.177.24

वरा स्त्रीमयरूपाणां शोभा गुणसुसम्पदा ।। भावनामात्रसंतुष्टा उमा देवी वरप्रदा ।।२४।।

Best in beauty among female forms, endowed with excellent qualities; goddess Umā, satisfied by thought alone, giver of boons.

BP 1.177.25

साक्षादागत्य रूपेण शांतेनामिततेजसा ।। शांतिं करोतु ते प्रीता आदित्याराधने रता ।। २५ ।।

Having come in person in a peaceful form of immeasurable splendor, may she, pleased and intent on worship of the Sun, make peace for you.
