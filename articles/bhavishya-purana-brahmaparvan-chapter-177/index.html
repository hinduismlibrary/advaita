BP 1.177.25

साक्षादागत्य रूपेण शांतेनामिततेजसा ।। शांतिं करोतु ते प्रीता आदित्याराधने रता ।। २५ ।।

Having come directly in a peaceful form of immeasurable splendor, pleased and devoted to the worship of Āditya, may she make peace for you.
