VP 5.27.1

शम्बरेण हृतो वीरः प्रद्युम्नः स कथं मुने ।
शम्बरः स महावीर्यः प्रद्युम्नेन कथं हतः

Maitreya asked: “How was the heroic Pradyumna carried away by Śambara, and how was the greatly powerful Śambara killed by Pradyumna?”

VP 5.27.3

षष्ठेऽह्नि जातमात्रं तु प्रद्युम्नं सूतिकागृहात् ।
ममैष हन्तेति मुने हृतवान् कालशम्बरः

On the sixth day after his birth, Pradyumna was carried from the birth chamber by Kāla-Śambara, who thought, “This is the one who will kill me.”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

तु (tu) — indeed
प्रद्युम्नम् (pradyumnam) — Pradyumna
सूतिका-गृहात् (sūtikā-gṛhāt) — from the birth chamber

इति (iti) — thus
मुने (mune) — O sage
हृतवान् (hṛtavān) — carried away
काल-शम्बरः (kāla-śambaraḥ) — Kāla-Śambara.
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ṣaṣṭhe’hni jātamātraṃ tu pradyumnaṃ sūtikāgṛhāt | mamaiṣa hanteti mune hṛtavānakālaśambaraḥ

Śrīdhara

ṣaṣṭhe’hnīti | kāla-śambaraḥ mṛtyu-tulya-śambaraḥ || 3 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

षष्ठेऽह्नीति । काल-शम्बरः मृत्यु-तुल्य-शम्बरः ॥३॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

“On the sixth day” means: Kāla-Śambara, Śambara who was equal to death.

VP 5.27.5

पातितं तत्र चैवैकः मत्स्यो जग्राह बालकम् ।
न ममार च तस्यापि जठराग्निप्रदीपितः

There a fish swallowed the child that had been cast in; yet the child did not die, though the fish’s digestive fire was burning.

Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

pātitaṃ tatra caivaikaḥ matsyo jagrāha bālakam | na mamāra ca tasyāpi jaṭharāgnipradīpitaḥ

Śrīdhara

pātitam iti | na mamāra bhagavad-vīrya-jātatvāt kāmāṃśatvāc ca | na hi kāmo jalādibhiḥ saṃhrīyate || 5 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

पातितम् इति । न ममार भगवद्-वीर्य-जातत्वात् कामांशत्वाच् च । न हि कामो जलादिभिः संह्रीयते ॥५॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

“Cast down” means: he did not die because he was born from the Lord’s power and because he was a portion of Kāma; Kāma is not destroyed by water and the like.

Śrīdhara commentary.

“By the fish-catchers” means: by fish-catchers, by those who kill fish.

VP 5.27.7

तस्य मायावती नाम पत्नी सर्वगृहेश्वरी ।
कारयामास सूदानामाधिपत्यमनिन्दिता

His wife named Māyāvatī, mistress of the entire household, appointed the blameless one over the cooks.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
तस्य (tasya) — his
मायावती (māyāvatī) — Māyāvatī
नाम (nāma) — named
पत्नी (patnī) — wife
सर्व-गृह-ईश्वरी (sarva-gṛha-īśvarī) — mistress of the whole household
कारयामास (kārayāmāsa) — appointed
सूदानाम् (sūdānām) — of the cooks
आधिपत्यम् (ādhipatyam) — authority
अनिन्दिता (aninditā) — blameless.
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tasya māyāvatī nāmapatnī sarvagṛheśvarī | kārayāmāsa sūdānāmādhipatyamaninditā

Śrīdhara

tasya iti | māyāvatī nāma patnī māyā-rūpeṇa tasya patnī ity evaṃ mohotpādanataḥ tat-patnīti vyavahāraḥ | yad vā, nāma-patnī nāma-mātreṇa patnīm ucyate, vastutas tu aninditā pativratā kāmasya bhāryety arthaḥ | etac cāgre sphuṭībhaviṣyati || 7 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तस्य इति । मायावती नाम पत्नी माया-रूपेण तस्य पत्नी इत्य् एवं मोहोत्पादनतः तत्-पत्नीति व्यवहारः । यद् वा, नाम-पत्नी नाम-मात्रेण पत्नीम् उच्यते, वस्तुतस् तु अनिन्दिता पतिव्रता कामस्य भार्येत्य् अर्थः । एतच् चाग्रे स्फुटीभविष्यति ॥७॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

“His” means: Māyāvatī is called his wife because, in the form of illusion, she produced delusion and was conventionally treated as his wife. Or, “wife” may mean only a nominal wife, called so merely by name; in reality she was the blameless, devoted wife of Kāma. This will become clear later.
