Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 29 — Worship and Mantras of Gaṇeśa

BP 1.29.1

शतानीक उवाच
गकाराक्षरदेवस्य गणेशस्य महात्मनः ।
आराधनविधिं ब्रूहि साङ्गं मन्त्रसमन्वितम् ॥

Śatānīka said: Tell me the complete method, accompanied by mantras, for worshipping the great god Gaṇeśa, whose sacred syllable is ga.

Word-for-word
शतानीक उवाच (śatānīka uvāca) — Śatānīka said;
गकाराक्षरदेवस्य (gakārākṣara-devasya) — of the god whose syllable is ga;
गणेशस्य महात्मनः (gaṇeśasya mahātmanaḥ) — of great Gaṇeśa;
आराधनविधिम् (ārādhana-vidhim) — method of worship;
ब्रूहि (brūhi) — tell;
साङ्गम् मन्त्रसमन्वितम् (sāṅgam mantra-samanvitam) — complete and accompanied by mantras
Transliteration
śatānīka uvāca
gakārākṣaradevasya gaṇeśasya mahātmanaḥ |
ārādhanavidhiṃ brūhi sāṅgaṃ mantrasamanvitam | | 1

BP 1.29.2

सुमन्तुरुवाच
न तिथिर्न च नक्षत्रं नोपवासो विधीयते ।
यथेष्टं चेष्टतः सिद्धिः सदा भवति कामिका ॥

Sumantu said: No lunar date, constellation, or fast is prescribed. Whenever one chooses to undertake it, the desired accomplishment always arises.

Word-for-word
सुमन्तुः उवाच (sumantuḥ uvāca) — Sumantu said;
न तिथिः न नक्षत्रम् (na tithiḥ na nakṣatram) — neither lunar date nor constellation;
न उपवासः विधीयते (na upavāsaḥ vidhīyate) — no fast is prescribed;
यथेष्टम् चेष्टतः (yatheṣṭam ceṣṭataḥ) — acting whenever desired;
सिद्धिः कामिका (siddhiḥ kāmikā) — desired accomplishment;
सदा भवति (sadā bhavati) — always occurs
Transliteration
sumanturuvāca
na tithirna ca nakṣatraṃ nopavāso vidhīyate |
yatheṣṭaṃ1 ceṣṭataḥ siddhiḥ sadā bhavati kāmikā | | 2

BP 1.29.3

श्वेतार्कमूलं सङ्गृह्य कुर्याद्गणपतिं बुधः ।
अङ्गुष्ठपर्वमात्रं तु पद्मासनगतं तथा ॥

A wise person should gather the root of a white arka plant and fashion Gaṇapati from it, one thumb-joint in size and seated in the lotus posture.

Word-for-word

कुर्यात् गणपतिम् (kuryāt gaṇapatim) — should fashion Gaṇapati;
बुधः (budhaḥ) — wise person;
अङ्गुष्ठपर्वमात्रम् (aṅguṣṭha-parva-mātram) — one thumb-joint in size;
पद्मासनगतम् (padmāsana-gatam) — seated in lotus posture
Transliteration
śvetārkamūlaṃ saṅgṛhya kuryādgaṇapatiṃ budhaḥ |
aṅguṣṭhaparvamātraṃ tu padmāsanagataṃ tathā | | 3

BP 1.29.4

चतुर्भुजं त्रिनेत्रं च सर्वाभरणभूषितम् ।
नागयज्ञोपवीताङ्गं शशाङ्ककृतशेखरम् ॥

He should have four arms and three eyes, be adorned with every ornament, wear a serpent as his sacred thread, and bear the moon upon his crest.

शशाङ्ककृतशेखरम् (śaśāṅka-kṛta-śekharam) — moon made his crest

BP 1.29.5

दन्तं सव्ये करे दद्याद्द्वितीये चाक्षसूत्रकम् ।
तृतीये परशुं दद्याच्चतुर्थे मोदकं न्यसेत् ॥

तृतीये परशुम् (tṛtīye paraśum) — axe in third;
चतुर्थे मोदकम् (caturthe modakam) — sweetmeat in fourth;

Transliteration

BP 1.29.6

कुङ्कुमं चन्दनं चापि समालम्भनमुच्यते ।

Saffron and sandalwood are prescribed for anointing. One should worship Gaṇa with red garments, ornaments, and garlands.

माल्यैः (mālyaiḥ) — with garlands;
आराधयेत् गणम् (ārādhayet gaṇam) — should worship Gaṇa
Transliteration

BP 1.29.7

धूपेन च सुगन्धेन मोदकैश्चापि पूजयेत् ।
एवं पूज्याग्रतस्तस्य भोजयेद्ब्राह्मणं बुधः ॥

One should worship him with fragrant incense and sweetmeats. After worshipping him in this way, the wise worshipper should feed a brāhmaṇa before him.

Word-for-word
धूपेन सुगन्धेन (dhūpena sugandhena) — with fragrant incense;
मोदकैः च (modakaiḥ ca) — and sweetmeats;
पूजयेत् (pūjayet) — should worship;
एवम् पूज्य (evam pūjya) — worshipping thus;
अग्रतः तस्य (agrataḥ tasya) — before him;
भोजयेत् ब्राह्मणम् (bhojayet brāhmaṇam) — should feed a brāhmaṇa
Transliteration
dhūpena ca sugandhena modakaiścāpi pūjayet |

BP 1.29.8

वामनं कुब्जकं चापि भोजयेत्पुरतो द्विजम् ।
आशीर्वादं ततस्तस्मात्प्राप्य सिद्धिमवाप्नुयात् ॥

He should also feed before the deity a short or hunchbacked brāhmaṇa. Then, receiving his blessing, he attains success.

आशीर्वादम् प्राप्य (āśīrvādam prāpya) — receiving blessing;
सिद्धिम् अवाप्नुयात् (siddhim avāpnuyāt) — should attain success
Transliteration

BP 1.29.9

भक्त्या कुरुकुलश्रेष्ठ शृणु मन्त्रपदानि वै ।

मन्त्रपदानि (mantra-padāni) — mantra syllables;
गं स्वाहा (gaṃ svāhā) — gaṃ svāhā;
मूलमन्त्रः अयम् (mūla-mantraḥ ayam) — this root mantra;
प्रणवेन समन्वितः (praṇavena samanvitaḥ) — joined with oṃ
Transliteration
bhaktyā kurukulaśreṣṭha 1śṛṇu mantrapadāni vai |

BP 1.29.10

गां नमो हदयं ज्ञेयं गीं शिरः परिकीर्तितम् ।
शिखा च गूं नमो ज्ञेयो गैं नमः कवचं स्मृतम् ॥

Gāṃ namaḥ is known as the heart, gīṃ as the head, gūṃ namaḥ as the topknot, and gaiṃ namaḥ as the armour.

Word-for-word
गां नमः हृदयम् (gāṃ namaḥ hṛdayam) — gāṃ namaḥ is the heart;
गीं शिरः (gīṃ śiraḥ) — gīṃ is the head;
गूं नमः शिखा (gūṃ namaḥ śikhā) — gūṃ namaḥ is the topknot;
गैं नमः कवचम् (gaiṃ namaḥ kavacam) — gaiṃ namaḥ is the armour
Transliteration
gāṃ namo hadayaṃ jñeyaṃ gīṃ śiraḥ parikīrtitam |

BP 1.29.11

गौं नमो नेत्रमुद्दिष्टं गः फट् कामास्त्रमुच्यते ।

BP 1.29.12

गं गणेशाय नमो गन्धमन्त्रः प्रकीर्तितः ।
पुष्पोल्काय नमः पुष्पमन्त्र एष प्रकीर्तितः ॥

Gaṃ, homage to Gaṇeśa is declared the mantra for fragrance. Homage to Puṣpolka is declared the flower mantra.

पुष्पोल्काय नमः (puṣpolkāya namaḥ) — homage to Puṣpolka;
पुष्पमन्त्रः (puṣpa-mantraḥ) — flower mantra
Transliteration
gaṃ gaṇeśāya namo gandhamantraḥ prakīrtitaḥ |
puṣpolkāya namaḥ puṣpamantra eṣa prakīrtitaḥ | | 12

BP 1.29.16

BP 1.29.19

BP 1.29.20

तिलयवहोमेन सर्वे जनपदा वश्या भवन्ति ।
