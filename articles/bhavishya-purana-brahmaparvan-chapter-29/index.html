Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 29 — Gaṇeśa Worship and Its Mantras

BP 1.29.1

शतानीक उवाच
गकाराक्षरदेवस्य गणेशस्य महात्मनः ।
आराधनविधिं ब्रूहि साङ्गं मन्त्रसमन्वितम् ॥

Śatānīka said. Tell me the full procedure with its parts and formulas for worshipping the great Gaṇeśa, the god of the syllable ga.

BP 1.29.2

सुमन्तुरुवाच
न तिथिर्न च नक्षत्रं नोपवासो विधीयते ।
यथेष्टं चेष्टतः सिद्धिः सदा भवति कामिका ॥

Sumantu said. No lunar day, constellation, or fast is prescribed. Accomplishment in accordance with one's desire always comes from acting as one wishes.

BP 1.29.3

श्वेतार्कमूलं सङ्गृह्य कुर्याद्गणपतिं बुधः ।
अङ्गुष्ठपर्वमात्रं तु पद्मासनगतं तथा ॥

A wise person should gather the root of the white crown flower and make Gaṇeśa from it, the size of a thumb joint and seated in the lotus posture.

BP 1.29.4

चतुर्भुजं त्रिनेत्रं च सर्वाभरणभूषितम् ।
नागयज्ञोपवीताङ्गं शशाङ्ककृतशेखरम् ॥

He should have four arms and three eyes, be adorned with every ornament, wear a serpent as his sacred thread, and bear the moon upon his crest.

BP 1.29.5

दन्तं सव्ये करे दद्याद्द्वितीये चाक्षसूत्रकम् ।
तृतीये परशुं दद्याच्चतुर्थे मोदकं न्यसेत् ॥

Place a tusk in his left hand, a rosary in the second, an axe in the third, and a sweet cake in the fourth.

BP 1.29.6

कुङ्कुमं चन्दनं चापि समालम्भनमुच्यते ।
वासोभिर्भूषणै रक्तैर्माल्यैश्चाराधयेद्गणम् ॥

Saffron and sandalwood are prescribed for anointing. Worship the Lord of Hosts with red garments, ornaments, and garlands.

BP 1.29.7

धूपेन च सुगन्धेन मोदकैश्चापि पूजयेत् ।
एवं पूज्याग्रतस्तस्य भोजयेद्ब्राह्मणं बुधः ॥

Worship him with fragrant incense and sweet cakes. Having worshipped him in this way, the wise person should feed a Brahmin before him.

BP 1.29.8

वामनं कुब्जकं चापि भोजयेत्पुरतो द्विजम् ।
आशीर्वादं ततस्तस्मात्प्राप्य सिद्धिमवाप्नुयात् ॥

One should also feed a short or hunchbacked Brahmin before the deity. Then, receiving his blessing, one attains accomplishment.

BP 1.29.9

भक्त्या कुरुकुलश्रेष्ठ शृणु मन्त्रपदानि वै ।
गं स्वाहा मूलमन्त्रोऽयं प्रणवेन समन्वितः ॥

Listen with devotion, best of the Kuru line, to the formula words. The seed sound gaṃ, with svāhā and Om, is the root formula.

BP 1.29.10

गां नमो हदयं ज्ञेयं गीं शिरः परिकीर्तितम् ।
शिखा च गूं नमो ज्ञेयो गैं नमः कवचं स्मृतम् ॥

The seed sound gāṃ with salutation is the heart, gīṃ is declared the head, gūṃ with salutation is the crown, and gaiṃ with salutation is the armor.

BP 1.29.11

गौं नमो नेत्रमुद्दिष्टं गः फट् कामास्त्रमुच्यते ।
आगच्छोल्कामुखायेति मन्त्र आवाहने ह्ययम् ॥

Gauṃ with salutation is assigned to the eyes, and gaḥ phaṭ is called the weapon of desire. Come, O Flame Faced One: this is the formula for invocation.

BP 1.29.12

गं गणेशाय नमो गन्धमन्त्रः प्रकीर्तितः ।
पुष्पोल्काय नमः पुष्पमन्त्र एष प्रकीर्तितः ॥

Gaṃ, homage to Gaṇeśa, is declared the fragrance formula. Homage to the Flower Flame is declared the flower formula.

BP 1.29.13

धूपोल्काय नमो धूपमन्त्र एष प्रकीर्तितः ।
दीपोल्काय नमो दीपमन्त्र एष प्रकीर्तितः ॥

Homage to the Incense Flame is the incense formula. Homage to the Lamp Flame is the lamp formula.

BP 1.29.14

ॐ गं महोल्काय नमो बलिमन्त्रः प्रकीर्तितः ।
ओं संसिद्धोल्काय नमो मन्त्रश्चायं विसर्जने ॥

Om gaṃ, homage to the Great Flame, is declared the offering formula. Om, homage to the Perfected Flame, is the formula for dismissal.

BP 1.29.15

ओं महाकर्णाय विद्महे वक्रतुण्डाय धीमहि ।
तन्नो दन्तिः प्रचोदयात् गायत्री जपः पूर्वतः ॥

We know the Great Eared One. We meditate upon the Curved Trunked One. May the Tusked One inspire us. This Gāyatrī prayer is to be recited first.

BP 1.29.16

महागणपतये वीर स्वाहा दक्षिणतः सदा ।
महोल्काय पश्चिमतः कूश्माण्डायोत्तरेण तु ॥
एकदन्तत्रिपुरान्तकाय आग्नेय्यां वीर निर्दिशेत् ॥

Svāhā to the Great Lord of Hosts in the south. To the Great Flame in the west, to Kūṣmāṇḍa in the north, and to the One Tusked Destroyer of Tripura in the southeast, O hero.

BP 1.29.17

ओं शिवदत्त विकटहरहास प्राणाय स्वा नैर्ऋत्याम् ।
तुलम्बनात्यचलदन्तकाय स्वाहा वायव्याम् ॥

In the southwest, Om svāhā to Śivadatta, the Formidable One, the Laughing One, the Life. In the northwest, svāhā to the Long Hanging, Unmoving Tusked One.

BP 1.29.18

पद्मदंष्ट्राय नरायेति ऐशान्यां होमयेद्बुधः ।
हुं फट् हुँ फट् हस्ततालध्वनिर्हसनकूर्दनः ॥

The wise should offer in the northeast to the Lotus Tusked One and to Nara. Huṃ phaṭ, huṃ phaṭ. The sound of clapping hands, laughing, and leaping is the gesture of the god.

BP 1.29.19

मृदनर्तनगणपतिर्देवस्य मुद्रां ततो होमं समाचरेत् ।
न यदा वश्या भवति ॥
कृष्णतिलाहुतिमष्टसहस्रं जुहुयात्त्रिरात्रेण राजा वश्यो भवति ॥

Then show the gesture of the Dancing Gaṇapati and perform the fire offering. If the person does not come under control, pour eight thousand oblations of black sesame over three nights, and a king comes under control.

BP 1.29.20

तिलयवहोमेन सर्वे जनपदा वश्या भवन्ति ।
अति रूपवती कन्या गच्छन्तमनु(?)च्छति ॥

By offering sesame and barley, all the inhabitants of a region come under control. An exceedingly beautiful maiden follows the one who departs.

BP 1.29.21

चणतन्दुलहोमेनाजितो भवेत् ।
निम्बपत्रसमैस्तैलैर्विद्वेषणं करोति ॥
सोमग्रहणे उदकमध्ये अवतीर्य अष्टसहस्रं जपेत् ।
सङ्ग्रामे अपराजितो भवति ॥

By an offering of chickpeas and rice, one becomes unconquered. Oils mixed with neem leaves produce enmity. During a lunar eclipse, enter the water and recite eight thousand times. One becomes undefeated in battle.

BP 1.29.22


आदित्याभिमुखो भूत्वा अष्टसहस्रं जपेत् ।
आदित्यो वरदो भवति ॥

Facing the Sun, recite eight thousand times. The Sun becomes a giver of boons.

BP 1.29.23

शुक्लचतुर्थ्यामुपोष्य गन्धपुष्पादिभिरर्चनं कृत्वा तिलतन्दुलाञ्जुहुयात् ।
शिरसा धारयंस्तैरपराजितो भवति ॥

After fasting on the bright fourth day and worshipping with fragrance, flowers, and the rest, offer sesame and rice. Carrying them upon the head, one becomes undefeated.

BP 1.29.24

अपामार्गसमिद्भिरग्निं प्रज्वाल्य एकविंशत्याहुतीर्यो जुहुयात् ।
त्रिरात्राच्छत्रुं व्यापादयति ॥

Having kindled a fire with prickly chaff flower sticks, whoever offers twenty one oblations destroys an enemy within three nights.

BP 1.29.25

अथोत्तरेण मन्त्रं व्याख्यास्ये ।
वृक्षमूले कज्जलं सङ्गृह्य सप्तभिर्मन्त्रितं कृत्वा नेत्राण्यञ्जयेद्यं पश्यति स वशी भवति ॥

Now I shall explain the next formula. Gather lampblack at the root of a tree, consecrate it with seven recitations, and apply it to the eyes. Whoever one sees comes under one's control.

BP 1.29.26

पुष्पं फलं मूलं चाष्टसहस्राभिमन्त्रितं कृत्वा यस्मै ददाति स वश्यो भवति ॥

Having consecrated a flower, fruit, or root with eight thousand recitations, give it to someone. That person comes under one's control.

BP 1.29.27

यत्किञ्चिन्मूलमन्त्रेण करोति तत्सिध्यति ।
सर्वे ग्रहाः सुप्रीता भवन्ति ॥
नगरद्वारं गत्वा अष्टसहस्रं द्वारं निरूपयेत् ॥

Whatever one does with the root formula succeeds. All the planets become pleased. Having gone to the city gate, one should direct eight thousand recitations toward the gate.

BP 1.29.28

पुरं द्वारेण गृह्यते प्राङ्मुखो यजति स उच्चाटयति ।
सम्मुखो जपति चोरान्विद्रावयति ॥

The city is taken through its gate. Worshipping while facing east drives a person away. Reciting face to face drives thieves away.

BP 1.29.29

तृणानि लूनयति । काष्ठानि च्छेदयति ॥

He plucks grass. He cuts wood.

BP 1.29.30

गजराजेन युद्धयति । जलमध्ये सप्तरात्रं जपेत् । अकाले वर्षयति । कूपतडागाञ्छोषयति ॥
प्रतिमां नृत्ययति । आकर्षयति । स्तम्भयति । योजनशतात्स्त्रीपुरुषानाकर्षयति ॥

He makes the king of elephants fight. Reciting for seven nights in water, he makes rain fall out of season and dries up wells and ponds. He makes an image dance, draws things toward himself, and makes them stand still. He draws women and men from a hundred leagues away.

BP 1.29.31

गोरोचनां च सहस्राभिमन्त्रितां कृत्वा हस्ते बद्ध्वा योजनशतसहस्रं गत्वा पुनरागच्छति ॥

Having consecrated cow's yellow pigment with a thousand recitations and tied it to the hand, one travels a hundred thousand leagues and returns.

BP 1.29.32

अथ मारयितुकामः खदिरकीलकं कृत्वा स्त्रीपुरुषं विचिन्त्य हदये निखनयेत् । क्षणादेव म्रियते ॥

If one desires to kill, make a peg of catechu wood, think upon the woman or man, and drive it into the heart. The person dies at once.

BP 1.29.33

सर्वपातकविमुक्तो भवति । अग्नितेजाः सर्वेभ्योऽपराजितो भवति ॥

One becomes free of every great sin, radiant with the power of fire, and undefeated by all.

BP 1.29.34

ॐ वकतुण्डाय स्वाहा । ॐ एकदंष्ट्राय स्वाहा । ॐ कृतकृष्णाय स्वाहा । ॐ गजकर्णाय स्वाहा ॥
ॐ लम्बोदराय स्वाहा । ॐ विकटाय स्वाहा । ॐ धूम्रवर्णाय स्वाहा । ॐ गगनकूजाय स्वाहा ॥
ॐ विनायकाय स्वाहा । ॐ गणपतये स्वाहा । ॐ हस्तिमुखाय स्वाहा ॥

Om, offering to the Curved Trunked One. Om, offering to the One Tusked One. Om, offering to Kṛtakṛṣṇa. Om, offering to the Elephant Eared One. Om, offering to the Big Bellied One. Om, offering to the Formidable One. Om, offering to the Smoke Colored One. Om, offering to the Sky Sounding One. Om, offering to Vināyaka. Om, offering to the Lord of Hosts. Om, offering to the Elephant Faced One.
