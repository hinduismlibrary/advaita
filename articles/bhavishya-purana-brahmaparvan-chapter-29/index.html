Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 29 — Gaṇeśa Worship and Its Mantras

BP 1.29.1

शतानीक उवाच
गकाराक्षरदेवस्य गणेशस्य महात्मनः ।
आराधनविधिं ब्रूहि साङ्गं मन्त्रसमन्वितम् ॥

Śatānīka said. Tell me the full procedure with its parts and formulas for worshipping the great Gaṇeśa, the god of the syllable ga.

शतानीकः (śatānīkaḥ) — Śatānīka; उवाच (uvāca) — said; गकाराक्षरदेवस्य (gakārākṣaradevasya) — of the god of the syllable ga; गणेशस्य (gaṇeśasya) — of Gaṇeśa; महात्मनः (mahātmanaḥ) — the great-souled; आराधनविधिम् (ārādhanavidhim) — the worship procedure; ब्रूहि (brūhi) — tell; साङ्गम् (sāṅgam) — with its parts; मन्त्रसमन्वितम् (mantrasamanvitam) — provided with mantras

BP 1.29.2

सुमन्तुरुवाच
न तिथिर्न च नक्षत्रं नोपवासो विधीयते ।
यथेष्टं चेष्टतः सिद्धिः सदा भवति कामिका ॥

Sumantu said. No lunar day, constellation, or fast is prescribed. Accomplishment in accordance with one's desire always comes from acting as one wishes.

सुमन्तुः (sumantuḥ) — Sumantu; उवाच (uvāca) — said; न (na) — not; तिथिः (tithiḥ) — a lunar day; न (na) — not; च (ca) — and; नक्षत्रम् (nakṣatram) — a constellation; न (na) — not; उपवासः (upavāsaḥ) — a fast; विधीयते (vidhīyate) — is prescribed; यथेष्टम् (yatheṣṭam) — as desired; चेष्टतः (ceṣṭataḥ) — from acting; सिद्धिः (siddhiḥ) — accomplishment; सदा (sadā) — always; भवति (bhavati) — comes; कामिका (kāmikā) — according to desire

BP 1.29.3

श्वेतार्कमूलं सङ्गृह्य कुर्याद्गणपतिं बुधः ।
अङ्गुष्ठपर्वमात्रं तु पद्मासनगतं तथा ॥

A wise person should gather the root of the white crown flower and make Gaṇeśa from it, the size of a thumb joint and seated in the lotus posture.

श्वेतार्कमूलम् (śvetārkamūlam) — the root of white arka; सङ्गृह्य (saṅgṛhya) — having gathered; कुर्यात् (kuryāt) — one should make; गणपतिम् (gaṇapatim) — Gaṇapati; बुधः (budhaḥ) — a wise person; अङ्गुष्ठपर्वमात्रम् (aṅguṣṭhaparvamātram) — the size of a thumb-joint; तु (tu) — indeed; पद्मासनगतम् (padmāsanagatam) — seated in the lotus posture; तथा (tathā) — thus

BP 1.29.4

चतुर्भुजं त्रिनेत्रं च सर्वाभरणभूषितम् ।
नागयज्ञोपवीताङ्गं शशाङ्ककृतशेखरम् ॥

He should have four arms and three eyes, be adorned with every ornament, wear a serpent as his sacred thread, and bear the moon upon his crest.

चतुर्भुजम् (caturbhujam) — four-armed; त्रिनेत्रम् (trinetram) — three-eyed; च (ca) — and; सर्वाभरणभूषितम् (sarvābharaṇabhūṣitam) — adorned with every ornament; नागयज्ञोपवीताङ्गम् (nāgayajñopavītāṅgam) — having a serpent as the sacred thread; शशाङ्ककृतशेखरम् (śaśāṅkakṛtaśekharam) — with the moon made his crest

BP 1.29.5

दन्तं सव्ये करे दद्याद्द्वितीये चाक्षसूत्रकम् ।
तृतीये परशुं दद्याच्चतुर्थे मोदकं न्यसेत् ॥

Place a tusk in his left hand, a rosary in the second, an axe in the third, and a sweet cake in the fourth.

दन्तम् (dantam) — a tusk; सव्ये (savye) — in the left; करे (kare) — hand; दद्यात् (dadyāt) — one should place; द्वितीये (dvitīye) — in the second; च (ca) — and; अक्षसूत्रकम् (akṣasūtrakam) — a rosary; तृतीये (tṛtīye) — in the third; परशुम् (paraśum) — an axe; दद्यात् (dadyāt) — one should place; चतुर्थे (caturthe) — in the fourth; मोदकम् (modakam) — a sweet cake; न्यसेत् (nyaset) — one should put

BP 1.29.6

कुङ्कुमं चन्दनं चापि समालम्भनमुच्यते ।
वासोभिर्भूषणै रक्तैर्माल्यैश्चाराधयेद्गणम् ॥

Saffron and sandalwood are prescribed for anointing. Worship the Lord of Hosts with red garments, ornaments, and garlands.

कुङ्कुमम् (kuṅkumam) — saffron; चन्दनम् (candanam) — sandalwood; च (ca) — and; अपि (api) — also; समालम्भनम् (samālambhanam) — anointing; उच्यते (ucyate) — is called; वासोभिः (vāsobhiḥ) — with garments; भूषणैः (bhūṣaṇaiḥ) — with ornaments; रक्तैः (raktaiḥ) — red; माल्यैः (mālyaiḥ) — with garlands; च (ca) — and; आराधयेत् (ārādhayet) — one should worship; गणम् (gaṇam) — the Gaṇa

BP 1.29.7

धूपेन च सुगन्धेन मोदकैश्चापि पूजयेत् ।
एवं पूज्याग्रतस्तस्य भोजयेद्ब्राह्मणं बुधः ॥

Worship him with fragrant incense and sweet cakes. Having worshipped him in this way, the wise person should feed a Brahmin before him.

धूपेन (dhūpena) — with incense; च (ca) — and; सुगन्धेन (sugandhena) — fragrant; मोदकैः (modakaiḥ) — with sweet cakes; च (ca) — and; अपि (api) — also; पूजयेत् (pūjayet) — one should worship; एवम् (evam) — thus; पूज्य (pūjya) — having worshipped; अग्रतः (agrataḥ) — in front; तस्य (tasya) — of him; भोजयेत् (bhojayet) — one should feed; ब्राह्मणम् (brāhmaṇam) — a Brahmin; बुधः (budhaḥ) — the wise one

BP 1.29.8

वामनं कुब्जकं चापि भोजयेत्पुरतो द्विजम् ।
आशीर्वादं ततस्तस्मात्प्राप्य सिद्धिमवाप्नुयात् ॥

One should also feed a short or hunchbacked Brahmin before the deity. Then, receiving his blessing, one attains accomplishment.

वामनम् (vāmanam) — a dwarf; कुब्जकम् (kubjakam) — a hunchback; च (ca) — and; अपि (api) — also; भोजयेत् (bhojayet) — one should feed; पुरतः (purataḥ) — in front; द्विजम् (dvijam) — a Brahmin; आशीर्वादम् (āśīrvādam) — a blessing; ततः (tataḥ) — then; तस्मात् (tasmāt) — from him; प्राप्य (prāpya) — having received; सिद्धिम् (siddhim) — accomplishment; अवाप्नुयात् (avāpnuyāt) — one may attain

BP 1.29.9

भक्त्या कुरुकुलश्रेष्ठ शृणु मन्त्रपदानि वै ।
गं स्वाहा मूलमन्त्रोऽयं प्रणवेन समन्वितः ॥

Listen with devotion, best of the Kuru line, to the formula words. The seed sound gaṃ, with svāhā and Om, is the root formula.

भक्त्या (bhaktyā) — with devotion; कुरुकुलश्रेष्ठ (kurukulaśreṣṭha) — O best of the Kuru line; शृणु (śṛṇu) — listen; मन्त्रपदानि (mantrapadāni) — the mantra-words; वै (vai) — indeed; गं (gaṃ) — the seed-syllable gaṃ; स्वाहा (svāhā) — svāhā; मूलमन्त्रः (mūlamantraḥ) — the root mantra; अयम् (ayam) — this; प्रणवेन (praṇavena) — with the syllable Om; समन्वितः (samanvitaḥ) — joined

BP 1.29.10

गां नमो हृदयम् ज्ञेयं गीं शिरः परिकीर्तितम् ।
शिखा च गूं नमो ज्ञेयो गैं नमः कवचं स्मृतम् ॥

The seed sound gāṃ with salutation is the heart, gīṃ is declared the head, gūṃ with salutation is the crown, and gaiṃ with salutation is the armor.

गां (gāṃ) — the seed-syllable gāṃ; नमः (namaḥ) — salutation; हृदयम् (hṛdayam) — the heart; ज्ञेयम् (jñeyam) — is to be known; गीं (gīṃ) — the seed-syllable gīṃ; शिरः (śiraḥ) — the head; परिकीर्तितम् (parikīrtitam) — is declared; शिखा (śikhā) — the crown; च (ca) — and; गूं (gūṃ) — the seed-syllable gūṃ; नमः (namaḥ) — salutation; ज्ञेयः (jñeyaḥ) — is to be known; गैं (gaiṃ) — the seed-syllable gaiṃ; नमः (namaḥ) — salutation; कवचम् (kavacam) — the armor; स्मृतम् (smṛtam) — is remembered

BP 1.29.11

गौं नमो नेत्रमुद्दिष्टं गः फट् कामास्त्रमुच्यते ।
आगच्छोल्कामुखायेति मन्त्र आवाहने ह्ययम् ॥

Gauṃ with salutation is assigned to the eyes, and gaḥ phaṭ is called the weapon of desire. Come, O Flame Faced One: this is the formula for invocation.

गौं (gauṃ) — the seed-syllable gauṃ; नमः (namaḥ) — salutation; नेत्रम् (netram) — the eyes; उद्दिष्टम् (uddiṣṭam) — is assigned; गः (gaḥ) — the seed-syllable gaḥ; फट् (phaṭ) — phaṭ; कामास्त्रम् (kāmāstram) — the weapon of desire; उच्यते (ucyate) — is called; आगच्छ (āgaccha) — come; उल्कामुखाय (ulkāmukhāya) — to Ulkāmukha; इति (iti) — thus; मन्त्रः (mantraḥ) — the mantra; आवाहने (āvāhane) — for invocation; हि (hi) — indeed; अयम् (ayam) — this

BP 1.29.12

गं गणेशाय नमो गन्धमन्त्रः प्रकीर्तितः ।
पुष्पोल्काय नमः पुष्पमन्त्र एष प्रकीर्तितः ॥

Gaṃ, homage to Gaṇeśa, is declared the fragrance formula. Homage to the Flower Flame is declared the flower formula.

गं (gaṃ) — the seed-syllable gaṃ; गणेशाय (gaṇeśāya) — to Gaṇeśa; नमः (namaḥ) — salutation; गन्धमन्त्रः (gandhamantraḥ) — the fragrance-mantra; प्रकीर्तितः (prakīrtitaḥ) — is declared; पुष्पोल्काय (puṣpolkāya) — to Puṣpolka; नमः (namaḥ) — salutation; पुष्पमन्त्रः (puṣpamantraḥ) — the flower-mantra; एषः (eṣaḥ) — this; प्रकीर्तितः (prakīrtitaḥ) — is declared

BP 1.29.13

धूपोल्काय नमो धूपमन्त्र एष प्रकीर्तितः ।
दीपोल्काय नमो दीपमन्त्र एष प्रकीर्तितः ॥

Homage to the Incense Flame is the incense formula. Homage to the Lamp Flame is the lamp formula.

धूपोल्काय (dhūpolkāya) — to Dhūpolka; नमः (namaḥ) — salutation; धूपमन्त्रः (dhūpamantraḥ) — the incense-mantra; एषः (eṣaḥ) — this; प्रकीर्तितः (prakīrtitaḥ) — is declared; दीपोल्काय (dīpolkāya) — to Dīpolka; नमः (namaḥ) — salutation; दीपमन्त्रः (dīpamantraḥ) — the lamp-mantra; एषः (eṣaḥ) — this; प्रकीर्तितः (prakīrtitaḥ) — is declared

BP 1.29.14

ॐ गं महोल्काय नमो बलिमन्त्रः प्रकीर्तितः ।
ओं संसिद्धोल्काय नमो मन्त्रश्चायं विसर्जने ॥

Om gaṃ, homage to the Great Flame, is declared the offering formula. Om, homage to the Perfected Flame, is the formula for dismissal.

ॐ (ॐ) — Om; गं (gaṃ) — the seed-syllable gaṃ; महोल्काय (maholkāya) — to Maholka; नमः (namaḥ) — salutation; बलिमन्त्रः (balimantraḥ) — the offering-mantra; प्रकीर्तितः (prakīrtitaḥ) — is declared; ॐ (ॐ) — Om; संसिद्धोल्काय (saṃsiddholkāya) — to Saṃsiddholka; नमः (namaḥ) — salutation; मन्त्रः (mantraḥ) — the mantra; च (ca) — and; अयम् (ayam) — this; विसर्जने (visarjane) — for dismissal

BP 1.29.15

ओं महाकर्णाय विद्महे वक्रतुण्डाय धीमहि ।
तन्नो दन्तिः प्रचोदयात् गायत्री जपः पूर्वतः ॥

We know the Great Eared One. We meditate upon the Curved Trunked One. May the Tusked One inspire us. This Gāyatrī prayer is to be recited first.

ॐ (ॐ) — Om; महाकर्णाय (mahākarṇāya) — to the Great-Eared One; विद्महे (vidmahe) — we know; वक्रतुण्डाय (vakratuṇḍāya) — to the Curved-Trunked One; धीमहि (dhīmahi) — we meditate; तत् (tat) — that; नः (naḥ) — us; दन्तिः (dantiḥ) — the Tusked One; प्रचोदयात् (pracodayāt) — may inspire; गायत्री (gāyatrī) — the Gāyatrī; जपः (japaḥ) — recitation; पूर्वतः (pūrvataḥ) — first

BP 1.29.16

महागणपतये वीर स्वाहा दक्षिणतः सदा ।
महोल्काय पश्चिमतः कूश्माण्डायोत्तरेण तु ॥
एकदन्तत्रिपुरान्तकाय आग्नेय्यां वीर निर्दिशेत् ॥

Svāhā to the Great Lord of Hosts in the south. To the Great Flame in the west, to Kūṣmāṇḍa in the north, and to the One Tusked Destroyer of Tripura in the southeast, O hero.

महागणपतये (mahāgaṇapataye) — to the Great Gaṇapati; वीर (vīra) — O hero; स्वाहा (svāhā) — svāhā; दक्षिणतः (dakṣiṇataḥ) — in the south; सदा (sadā) — always; महोल्काय (maholkāya) — to Maholka; पश्चिमतः (paścimataḥ) — in the west; कूश्माण्डाय (kūśmāṇḍāya) — to Kūṣmāṇḍa; उत्तरेण (uttareṇa) — in the north; तु (tu) — indeed; एकदन्तत्रिपुरान्तकाय (ekadantatripurāntakāya) — to the One-Tusked Destroyer of Tripura; आग्नेय्याम् (āgneyyām) — in the southeast; वीर (vīra) — O hero; निर्दिशेत् (nirdiśet) — one should assign

BP 1.29.17

ओं शिवदत्त विकटहरहास प्राणाय स्वाहा नैर्ऋत्याम् ।
तुलम्बनात्यचलदन्तकाय स्वाहा वायव्याम् ॥

In the southwest, Om svāhā to Śivadatta, the Formidable One, the Laughing One, the Life. In the northwest, svāhā to the Long Hanging, Unmoving Tusked One.

ॐ (ॐ) — Om; शिवदत्त (śivadatta) — Śivadatta; विकट (vikaṭa) — Vikaṭa; हरहास (harahāsa) — Harahāsa; प्राणाय (prāṇāya) — to life; स्वाहा (svāhā) — svāhā; नैर्ऋत्याम् (nairṛtyām) — in the southwest; तुलम्बनात्यचलदन्तकाय (tulambanātyacaladantakāya) — to Tulambana, the Unmoving-Tusked One; स्वाहा (svāhā) — svāhā; वायव्याम् (vāyavyām) — in the northwest

BP 1.29.18

पद्मदंष्ट्राय नरायेति ऐशान्यां होमयेद्बुधः ।
हुं फट् हुँ फट् हस्ततालध्वनिर्हसनकूर्दनः ॥

The wise should offer in the northeast to the Lotus Tusked One and to Nara. Huṃ phaṭ, huṃ phaṭ. The sound of clapping hands, laughing, and leaping is the gesture of the god.

पद्मदंष्ट्राय (padmadaṃṣṭrāya) — to Padmadaṃṣṭra; नराय (narāya) — to Nara; इति (iti) — thus; ऐशान्याम् (aiśānyām) — in the northeast; होमयेत् (homayet) — one should make the offering; बुधः (budhaḥ) — the wise one; हुं (huṃ) — huṃ; फट् (phaṭ) — phaṭ; हुं (huṃ) — huṃ; फट् (phaṭ) — phaṭ; हस्ततालध्वनिः (hastatāladhvaniḥ) — the sound of hand-clapping; हसन (hasana) — laughter; कूर्दनः (kūrdanaḥ) — leaping

BP 1.29.19

मृदनर्तनगणपतिर्देवस्य मुद्रां ततो होमं समाचरेत् ।
न यदा वश्या भवति ॥
कृष्णतिलाहुतिमष्टसहस्रं जुहुयात्त्रिरात्रेण राजा वश्यो भवति ॥

Then show the gesture of the Dancing Gaṇapati and perform the fire offering. If the person does not come under control, pour eight thousand oblations of black sesame over three nights, and a king comes under control.

मृदनर्तनगणपतिः (mṛdanartanagaṇapatiḥ) — Mṛdanartana Gaṇapati; देवस्य (devasya) — of the god; मुद्राम् (mudrām) — the gesture; ततः (tataḥ) — then; होमम् (homam) — the fire-offering; समाचरेत् (samācaret) — one should perform; न (na) — not; यदा (yadā) — when; वश्या (vaśyā) — under control; भवति (bhavati) — she becomes; कृष्णतिलाहुतिम् (kṛṣṇatilāhutim) — an oblation of black sesame; अष्टसहस्रम् (aṣṭasahasram) — eight thousand; जुहुयात् (juhuyāt) — one should offer; त्रिरात्रेण (trirātreṇa) — over three nights; राजा (rājā) — a king; वश्यः (vaśyaḥ) — under control; भवति (bhavati) — becomes

BP 1.29.20

तिलयवहोमेन सर्वे जनपदा वश्या भवन्ति ।
अति रूपवती कन्या गच्छन्तमनुच्छति ॥

By offering sesame and barley, all the inhabitants of a region come under control. An exceedingly beautiful maiden follows the one who departs.

तिलयवहोमेन (tilayavahomena) — by an offering of sesame and barley; सर्वे (sarve) — all; जनपदाः (janapadāḥ) — the people of the land; वश्याः (vaśyāḥ) — under control; भवन्ति (bhavanti) — become; अति (ati) — exceedingly; रूपवती (rūpavatī) — beautiful; कन्या (kanyā) — a maiden; गच्छन्तम् (gacchantam) — the one going away; अनुच्छति (anucchati) — follows

BP 1.29.21

चणतन्दुलहोमेनाजितो भवेत् ।
निम्बपत्रसमैस्तैलैर्विद्वेषणं करोति ॥
सोमग्रहणे उदकमध्ये अवतीर्य अष्टसहस्रं जपेत् ।
सङ्ग्रामे अपराजितो भवति ॥

By an offering of chickpeas and rice, one becomes unconquered. Oils mixed with neem leaves produce enmity. During a lunar eclipse, enter the water and recite eight thousand times. One becomes undefeated in battle.

चणतन्दुलहोमेन (caṇatandulahomena) — by an offering of chickpeas and rice; अजितः (ajitaḥ) — unconquered; भवेत् (bhavet) — one becomes; निम्बपत्रसमैः (nimbapatrasamaiḥ) — mixed with neem leaves; तैलैः (tailaiḥ) — with oils; विद्वेषणम् (vidveṣaṇam) — enmity; करोति (karoti) — one produces; सोमग्रहणे (somagrahaṇe) — during a lunar eclipse; उदकमध्ये (udakamadhye) — in the water; अवतीर्य (avatīrya) — having entered; अष्टसहस्रम् (aṣṭasahasram) — eight thousand; जपेत् (japet) — one should recite; सङ्ग्रामे (saṅgrāme) — in battle; अपराजितः (aparājitaḥ) — unconquered; भवति (bhavati) — one becomes

BP 1.29.22


आदित्याभिमुखो भूत्वा अष्टसहस्रं जपेत् ।
आदित्यो वरदो भवति ॥

Facing the Sun, recite eight thousand times. The Sun becomes a giver of boons.

आदित्याभिमुखः (ādityābhimukhaḥ) — facing the Sun; भूत्वा (bhūtvā) — having become; अष्टसहस्रम् (aṣṭasahasram) — eight thousand; जपेत् (japet) — one should recite; आदित्यः (ādityaḥ) — the Sun; वरदः (varadaḥ) — a boon-giver; भवति (bhavati) — becomes

BP 1.29.23

शुक्लचतुर्थ्यामुपोष्य गन्धपुष्पादिभिरर्चनं कृत्वा तिलतन्दुलाञ्जुहुयात् ।
शिरसा धारयंस्तैरपराजितो भवति ॥

After fasting on the bright fourth day and worshipping with fragrance, flowers, and the rest, offer sesame and rice. Carrying them upon the head, one becomes undefeated.

शुक्लचतुर्थ्याम् (śuklacaturthyām) — on the bright fourth lunar day; उपोष्य (upoṣya) — having fasted; गन्धपुष्पादिभिः (gandhapuṣpādibhiḥ) — with fragrances, flowers, and the rest; अर्चनम् (arcanam) — worship; कृत्वा (kṛtvā) — having performed; तिलतन्दुलान् (tilatandulān) — sesame and rice grains; जुहुयात् (juhuyāt) — one should offer; शिरसा (śirasā) — on the head; धारयन् (dhārayan) — wearing; तैः (taiḥ) — those; अपराजितः (aparājitaḥ) — unconquered; भवति (bhavati) — one becomes

BP 1.29.24

अपामार्गसमिद्भिरग्निं प्रज्वाल्य एकविंशत्याहुतीर्यो जुहुयात् ।
त्रिरात्राच्छत्रुं व्यापादयति ॥

Having kindled a fire with prickly chaff flower sticks, whoever offers twenty one oblations destroys an enemy within three nights.

अपामार्गसमिद्भिः (apāmārgasamidbhiḥ) — with apāmārga firewood; अग्निम् (agnim) — the fire; प्रज्वाल्य (prajvālya) — having kindled; एकविंशतिआहुतीः (ekaviṃśatiāhutīḥ) — twenty-one oblations; यः (yaḥ) — who; जुहुयात् (juhuyāt) — offers; त्रिरात्रात् (trirātrāt) — within three nights; शत्रुम् (śatrum) — an enemy; व्यापादयति (vyāpādayati) — destroys

BP 1.29.25

अथोत्तरेण मन्त्रं व्याख्यास्ये ।
वृक्षमूले कज्जलं सङ्गृह्य सप्तभिर्मन्त्रितं कृत्वा नेत्राण्यञ्जयेद्यं पश्यति स वशी भवति ॥

Now I shall explain the next formula. Gather lampblack at the root of a tree, consecrate it with seven recitations, and apply it to the eyes. Whoever one sees comes under one's control.

अथ (atha) — now; उत्तरेण (uttareṇa) — the next; मन्त्रम् (mantram) — mantra; व्याख्यास्ये (vyākhyāsye) — I shall explain; वृक्षमूले (vṛkṣamūle) — at the root of a tree; कज्जलम् (kajjalam) — lampblack; सङ्गृह्य (saṅgṛhya) — having gathered; सप्तभिः (saptabhiḥ) — with seven; मन्त्रितम् (mantritam) — consecrated by mantras; कृत्वा (kṛtvā) — having made; नेत्राणि (netrāṇi) — the eyes; अञ्जयेत् (añjayet) — one should anoint; यम् (yam) — whom; पश्यति (paśyati) — one sees; सः (saḥ) — he; वशी (vaśī) — under control; भवति (bhavati) — becomes

BP 1.29.26

पुष्पं फलं मूलं चाष्टसहस्राभिमन्त्रितं कृत्वा यस्मै ददाति स वश्यो भवति ॥

Having consecrated a flower, fruit, or root with eight thousand recitations, give it to someone. That person comes under one's control.

पुष्पम् (puṣpam) — a flower; फलम् (phalam) — a fruit; मूलम् (mūlam) — a root; च (ca) — and; अष्टसहस्राभिमन्त्रितम् (aṣṭasahasrābhimantritam) — consecrated by eight thousand recitations; कृत्वा (kṛtvā) — having made; यस्मै (yasmai) — to whom; ददाति (dadāti) — one gives; सः (saḥ) — he; वश्यः (vaśyaḥ) — under control; भवति (bhavati) — becomes

BP 1.29.27

यत्किञ्चिन्मूलमन्त्रेण करोति तत्सिध्यति ।
सर्वे ग्रहाः सुप्रीता भवन्ति ॥
नगरद्वारं गत्वा अष्टसहस्रं द्वारं निरूपयेत् ॥

Whatever one does with the root formula succeeds. All the planets become pleased. Having gone to the city gate, one should direct eight thousand recitations toward the gate.

यत्किञ्चित् (yatkiñcit) — whatever; मूलमन्त्रेण (mūlamantreṇa) — with the root mantra; करोति (karoti) — one does; तत् (tat) — that; सिध्यति (sidhyati) — succeeds; सर्वे (sarve) — all; ग्रहाः (grahāḥ) — the planets; सुप्रीताः (suprītāḥ) — well-pleased; भवन्ति (bhavanti) — become; नगरद्वारम् (nagaradvāram) — the city-gate; गत्वा (gatvā) — having gone; अष्टसहस्रम् (aṣṭasahasram) — eight thousand; द्वारम् (dvāram) — the gate; निरूपयेत् (nirūpayet) — one should direct

BP 1.29.28

पुरं द्वारेण गृह्यते प्राङ्मुखो यजति स उच्चाटयति ।
सम्मुखो जपति चोरान्विद्रावयति ॥

The city is taken through its gate. Worshipping while facing east drives a person away. Reciting face to face drives thieves away.

पुरम् (puram) — the city; द्वारेण (dvāreṇa) — through the gate; गृह्यते (gṛhyate) — is seized; प्राङ्मुखः (prāṅmukhaḥ) — facing east; यजति (yajati) — one worships; सः (saḥ) — he; उच्चाटयति (uccāṭayati) — drives away; सम्मुखः (sammukhaḥ) — facing directly; जपति (japati) — one recites; चोरान् (corān) — thieves; विद्रावयति (vidrāvayati) — puts to flight

BP 1.29.29

तृणानि लूनयति । काष्ठानि च्छेदयति ॥

He plucks grass. He cuts wood.

तृणानि (tṛṇāni) — grasses; लूनयति (lūnayati) — he plucks; काष्ठानि (kāṣṭhāni) — wood; छेदयति (chedayati) — he cuts

BP 1.29.30

गजराजेन युद्धयति । जलमध्ये सप्तरात्रं जपेत् । अकाले वर्षयति । कूपतडागाञ्छोषयति ॥
प्रतिमां नृत्ययति । आकर्षयति । स्तम्भयति । योजनशतात्स्त्रीपुरुषानाकर्षयति ॥

He makes the king of elephants fight. Reciting for seven nights in water, he makes rain fall out of season and dries up wells and ponds. He makes an image dance, draws things toward himself, and makes them stand still. He draws women and men from a hundred leagues away.

गजराजेन (gajarājena) — with the king of elephants; युद्धयति (yuddhayati) — he causes to fight; जलमध्ये (jalamadhye) — in the water; सप्तरात्रम् (saptarātram) — for seven nights; जपेत् (japet) — one should recite; अकाले (akāle) — out of season; वर्षयति (varṣayati) — he causes rain; कूपतडागान् (kūpataḍāgān) — wells and ponds; शोषयति (śoṣayati) — he dries; प्रतिमाम् (pratimām) — an image; नृत्ययति (nṛtyayati) — he makes dance; आकर्षयति (ākarṣayati) — he attracts; स्तम्भयति (stambhayati) — he immobilizes; योजनशतात् (yojanaśatāt) — from a hundred yojanas; स्त्रीपुरुषान् (strīpuruṣān) — women and men; आकर्षयति (ākarṣayati) — he attracts

BP 1.29.31

गोरोचनां च सहस्राभिमन्त्रितां कृत्वा हस्ते बद्ध्वा योजनशतसहस्रं गत्वा पुनरागच्छति ॥

Having consecrated cow's yellow pigment with a thousand recitations and tied it to the hand, one travels a hundred thousand leagues and returns.

गोरोचनाम् (gorocanām) — yellow cow-pigment; च (ca) — and; सहस्राभिमन्त्रिताम् (sahasrābhimantritām) — consecrated by a thousand recitations; कृत्वा (kṛtvā) — having made; हस्ते (haste) — on the hand; बद्ध्वा (baddhvā) — having tied; योजनशतसहस्रम् (yojanaśatasahasram) — a hundred thousand yojanas; गत्वा (gatvā) — having gone; पुनः (punaḥ) — again; आगच्छति (āgacchati) — one returns

BP 1.29.32

अथ मारयितुकामः खदिरकीलकं कृत्वा स्त्रीपुरुषं विचिन्त्य हृदये निखनयेत् । क्षणादेव म्रियते ॥

If one desires to kill, make a peg of catechu wood, think upon the woman or man, and drive it into the heart. The person dies at once.

अथ (atha) — then; मारयितुकामः (mārayitukāmaḥ) — wishing to kill; खदिरकीलकम् (khadirakīlakam) — a peg of khadira wood; कृत्वा (kṛtvā) — having made; स्त्रीपुरुषम् (strīpuruṣam) — a woman or man; विचिन्त्य (vicintya) — having visualized; हृदये (hṛdaye) — in the heart; निखनयेत् (nikhanayet) — one should drive in; क्षणात् (kṣaṇāt) — in a moment; एव (eva) — indeed; म्रियते (mriyate) — one dies

BP 1.29.33

सर्वपातकविमुक्तो भवति । अग्नितेजाः सर्वेभ्योऽपराजितो भवति ॥

One becomes free of every great sin, radiant with the power of fire, and undefeated by all.

सर्वपातकविमुक्तः (sarvapātakavimuktaḥ) — freed from all sins; भवति (bhavati) — one becomes; अग्नितेजाः (agnitejāḥ) — having the splendor of fire; सर्वेभ्यः (sarvebhyaḥ) — by all; अपराजितः (aparājitaḥ) — unconquered; भवति (bhavati) — one becomes
