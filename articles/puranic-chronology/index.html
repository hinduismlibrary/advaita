Reading the dates. Intervals describe the named passage or group, not the date of every verse in its received chapter. T and D in the chronology refer to the printed chronological table and discussion. Commentary coverage and later quotations are recorded separately in each chapter.

Sources and scope

Chronological table, discussion, and appendices in Studies in the Purāṇic Records on Hindu Rites and Customs (1940), pp. 8–188 and 265–339. Consult the scan ↗. The quotation appendix is selective; an unindexed chapter has no negative attestation claim. Named works and loci are transcribed from its tables, with some damaged OCR rows left unresolved.

Commentary editions: Bhāgavata Purāṇa; Viṣṇu Purāṇa I–III and IV–VI. The Viṣṇu edition records Śrīdhara’s complete commentary and Viṣṇucitta’s coverage of I, II, and VI. Full Bhāgavata chapter extents follow the received 335-chapter text; full Viṣṇu extents cover 22, 16, 18, 24, 38, and 8 chapters.
