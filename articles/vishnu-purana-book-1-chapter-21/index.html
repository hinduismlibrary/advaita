VP 1.21.1

संह्रादपुत्र आयुष्मान् शिबिर् बाष्कल एव च । विरोचनस् तु प्राह्लादिर् बलिर् जज्ञे विरोचनात् ॥१॥

The sons of Saṃhlāda were Āyuṣmān, Śibi, and Bāṣkala; Virocana was Prahlāda’s son, and Bali was born from Virocana.

VP 1.21.40

मरुतो नाम देवास्ते बभूवुरतिवेगिनः । यदुक्तं वै मघवता तेनैव मरुतोऽभवन् ॥४०॥

Indra split each one into seven with the thunderbolt that cleaves mountains. They became the very swift gods called the Maruts.

Śrīdhara translation

“Being afflicted” and “being cut” explain the wordplay: because of the command “do not cry,” they became the Maruts. “Vajra” indicates the thunderbolt; the hand holding it is Vajrapāṇi, and one who possesses that hand is Vajrapāṇī.
