Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 146 — Description of the Sun-Priests

BP 1.146.1

शतानीक उवाच । य एते भोजकाः प्रोक्ता देवदेवस्य पूजकाः । नान्यं भोज्यमथैतेषां ब्राह्मणैश्च कदाचन ॥

Śatānīka said: These Sun-priests are declared to be worshippers of the God of gods. Do Brahmins never eat any other food than theirs?

BP 1.146.2

भास्करस्य प्रिया ह्येते पूज्यत्वं च तथा गताः । दिव्याश्चैते स्मृता विप्रा आदित्याम्भः समुद्भवाः ॥

They are dear to Bhāskara and have attained worshipfulness. These Brahmins are remembered as divine, born from the waters of Āditya.

BP 1.146.3

अभोज्यत्वं कथं याता भोजकास्तद्वदस्व मे । किं कुर्वाणास्तथा कर्म भोज्यतां यान्ति मे वद ॥

How did the Sun-priests become unfit to eat with? Tell me that. By doing what do they become fit to eat with? Tell me.

BP 1.146.4

सुमन्तुरुवाच । इममर्थं पुरा पृष्टो वासुदेवो महीपते । कृतवर्मणा पुरा राजंस्तथा साम्बो महाबलः ॥

Sumantu said: Formerly Vasudeva was asked this matter by Kṛtavarman, O king, and likewise by mighty Sāmba.

BP 1.146.5

गतौ साम्बपुरीं वीर तथा नारदपर्वतौ । भुक्तवन्तो गृहे सर्वे भोजकस्य महात्मनः ॥

Nārada and Parvata both went to Sāmbapurī, O hero. All of them ate in the house of the great-souled Sun-priest.

BP 1.146.6

आदित्यकर्मणो लोके देवान्नख्यातिमागताः । तेन ते पूजिताः सर्वे भक्त्या भोज्यैरनेकशः ॥

Through the Sun-rite they gained renown in the world as food fit for the gods. Therefore all were worshipped with many foods, in devotion.

BP 1.146.7

आगतास्ते पुरीं वीर पुण्यां द्वारवतीं विभोः । तावृषी दिवमारुढौ राजन्नारदपर्वतौ ॥

They came to holy Dvāravatī, the city of the Lord. Those two sages, Nārada and Parvata, ascended to heaven, O king.

BP 1.146.8

वासुदेवं महातेजा हार्दिक्यो वाक्यमब्रवीत् । य एते भोजका विप्र पूजका भास्करस्य तु ॥

The great-radiant Hārdikya spoke these words to Vāsudeva: These Sun-priests, O Brahmin, are worshippers of Bhāskara.

BP 1.146.9

अन्नमेषां कथं विप्रौ भुक्तवन्तौ जनार्दन । तावृषी दिव्यमाख्यातौ यौ तौ नारदपर्वतौ । अभोज्याः किल एते वै ब्राह्मणानां जनार्दन ॥

How did you two Brahmins eat their food, Janārdana? Those two sages, Nārada and Parvata, are declared divine. These Sun-priests are indeed unfit for Brahmins to eat with, Janārdana.

BP 1.146.10

वासुदेव उवाच । न ते भोज्या महाबाहो भोज्या भोजाश्च सर्वदा । अभिवाद्यां प्रयत्नेन यथादित्यो महामते ॥

Vāsudeva said: They are not fit to eat with, O mighty-armed one; the Bhojas are always fit to eat with and should be respectfully saluted with effort, like the Sun, O great-minded one.

BP 1.146.11

आचरन्तश्र तत्कर्म भोज्यत्वं प्रवजन्ति ते । तच्छ्रूयतां यदुश्रेष्ठ यत्कार्यं चापि तैर्विभो । यतमानैर्महाबाहो तदिहैकमनाः शृणु ॥

By practicing that rite, they become fit to eat with. Hear what they and those who strive must do, O best of the Yadus, O lord. Hear that here with a one-pointed mind, O mighty-armed one.

BP 1.146.12

वृषली यस्य वै भार्या यश्चाव्यङ्गं न धारयेत् । अभोज्यः स तु विज्ञेयो भोजको नात्र संशयः ॥

A Sun-priest whose wife is a Śūdra woman and who does not wear the sacred girdle is known to be unfit to eat with; there is no doubt.

BP 1.146.13

अस्नातः पूजयेद्यस्तु तथाभ्यङ्गविवर्जितः । आदित्यं यदुशार्दूल तथा च विधिना विभो ॥

He who worships the Sun unbathed and likewise without the sacred girdle, though following the prescribed rite, O lord,

BP 1.146.14

सेवको भोजको यस्तु शूद्रान्नं येन भुज्यते । कृषिं च कुरुते यस्तु देवार्चामपि वर्जयेत् ॥

the Sun-priest who is a servant, who eats food prepared by Śūdras, practices farming, and abandons the worship of the gods,

BP 1.146.15

जातकर्मादयो यस्य न संस्काराः कृता विभो । आरुणेयैश्च मन्त्रैश्च सावित्रीं न च वै पठेत् । तस्य गेहे द्विजो भुक्त्वा कृच्छ्रपादेन शुध्यति ॥

for whom the rites beginning with birth-ceremony have not been performed, O lord, and who does not recite the Sāvitrī with the formulas of Aruṇa—if a Brahmin eats in his house, he is purified by a quarter of an austerity.

BP 1.146.16

पितृदेवमनुष्याणां भूतानां भास्करस्य तु । अकृत्वा विधिवत्पूजां यस्तु भुङ्क्ते स धर्महा ॥

He who eats without duly worshipping the ancestors, gods, people, beings, and Bhāskara is a destroyer of sacred duty.

BP 1.146.17

अभ्यङ्गेन विहीनो यः शंखहीनस्तथैव च । शिरसा धारयेत्केशान्स ज्ञेयो भोजकाधमः ॥

He who lacks the sacred girdle and likewise the conch, and wears his hair long, is to be known as the lowest Sun-priest.

BP 1.146.18

देवार्चनं तथा होमं स्नानं तर्पणमेव च । दानं ब्राह्मणपूजां च कुर्वतो भोजकस्य तु । अभ्यङ्गेन विहीनस्य सर्वं भवति निष्फलम् ॥

For a Sun-priest who performs worship of the gods, fire-offerings, bathing, ancestral libations, giving, and worship of Brahmins, everything becomes fruitless if he lacks the sacred girdle.

BP 1.146.19

सर्वदेवमयो ह्येष सर्ववेदमयस्तथा । अभ्यङ्गो यदुशार्दूल पवित्रः परमः स्मृतः ॥

The sacred girdle is made of all the gods and likewise of all the Vedas. It is remembered as pure and supreme, O best of the Yadus.

BP 1.146.20

भोजकानां यदुश्रेष्ठ तस्य मूले स्थितो हरिः । मध्ये ब्रह्मा महातेजा अग्रे गोश्रुतिभूषणः ॥

For the Sun-priests, O best of the Yadus, Hari is situated at its root; in the middle is the great-radiant Brahmā; at the tip is the one adorned with sacred revelation.

BP 1.146.21

ऋग्वेदो यस्य मूलस्थो मध्ये सामानि कृत्स्नशः । यजुर्वेदस्तथा श्रेष्ठश्चाथर्वसहितः स्थितः ॥

At its root is the Ṛgveda; in the middle are all the Sāma hymns. The Yajurveda, the best, stands together with the Atharvaveda.

BP 1.146.22

त्रयोऽग्नयस्तथा राजंस्त्रयो लोकाः स्थिताः क्रमात् । एवमेव पवित्रस्तु अभ्यङ्गो भोजकस्य तु ॥

Three fires, O king, and three worlds are situated in order. Even so, the sacred girdle of a Sun-priest is pure.

BP 1.146.23

यस्त्वनेन विहीनस्तु भोजको भोजकाधमः । अभोज्यः स तु विज्ञेयः सोऽशुचिर्नात्र संशयः ॥

The Sun-priest who lacks this is the lowest Sun-priest. He is to be known as unfit to eat with and impure; there is no doubt.

BP 1.146.24

निर्माल्यमथ नैवेद्यं कुङ्कुमं देवहेलिनाम् । ये प्रयच्छन्ति शूद्राणां विक्रीणन्ति च भोजकाः । तेऽधमा भोजका ज्ञेया ये च देवस्वहारिणः ॥

The lowest Sun-priests are those who give the worship-remnants, food first offered to the gods, and red powder to Śūdras, sell them, or steal the property of the gods.

BP 1.146.25

न पूजयन्ति देवेशं देवस्वं क्षपयन्ति च । न ते देव प्रियास्तात विज्ञेया भोजकाधमाः ॥

They do not worship the Lord of gods and squander the property of the gods. They are not dear to the gods, dear one; they are to be known as the lowest Sun-priests.

BP 1.146.26

यस्मिन्न भुक्ते नैवेद्यं भोजकोऽश्नाति मानद । तदन्नं भुङ्जतस्तस्य नरकाय न शान्तये ॥

When the offering to Bhāskara has not yet been eaten, if a Sun-priest eats it, that food brings hell, not peace, to the one who eats it.

BP 1.146.27

नैवेद्यं भोजयेत्तस्माद्भास्करस्य नरः सदा । प्रथमं यदुशार्दूल तच्च देहविशोधनम् ॥

Therefore a person should always offer the food to Bhāskara first. That, O best of the Yadus, is bodily purification.

BP 1.146.28

ब्राह्मणानां पुरोडाशो यथा कायविशोधनः । भोजकानां तथा वीर नैवेद्यं कायशोधनम् ॥

As the baked offering of Brahmins purifies the body, so, O hero, the food first offered by Sun-priests purifies the body.
