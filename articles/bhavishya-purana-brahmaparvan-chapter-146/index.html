ब्राह्मणानां पुरोडाशो यथा कायविशोधनः । भोजकानां तथा वीर नैवेद्यं कायशोधनम् ॥

As the baked offering of Brahmins purifies the body, so, O hero, the food first offered by Sun-priests purifies the body.

brāhmaṇānām — of brāhmins
puroḍāśaḥ — puroḍāśa
yathā — as
kāya-viśodhanaḥ — purification of the body
bhojakānām — of Bhojakas
tathā — likewise
vīra — O hero
naivedyam — naivedya
kāya-śodhanam — purification of the body
