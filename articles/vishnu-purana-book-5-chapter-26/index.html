VP 5.26.1

भीष्मकः कुण्डिने राजा विदर्भ-विषयेऽभवत् ।

निर्जितः पातितश् चोर्व्यां लीलयैव स चक्रिणा ॥१०॥

After slaying the army, crowded with elephants, horses, foot-soldiers, and chariots, the discus-bearer defeated him and cast him to the ground with ease.

VP 5.26.11

निर्जित्य रुक्मिणं सम्यग् उपयेमे च रुक्मिणीम् ।
राक्षसेन विवाहेन सम्प्राप्तां मधुसूदनः ॥११॥

Having thoroughly defeated Rukmī, Madhusūdana married Rukmiṇī, obtained through a Rākṣasa marriage.

Śrīdhara translation

“Rākṣasa” means the Rākṣasa form of marriage, known in Smṛti as abduction in battle.

VP 5.26.12

तस्यां जज्ञे च प्रद्युम्नो मदनांशः स वीर्यवान् ।
जहार शम्बरो यं वै यो जघान च शम्बरम् ॥१२॥

From her was born the mighty Pradyumna, a portion of Madana. Śambara carried him away, and Pradyumna killed Śambara.

Śrīdhara translation

“In her” means: “A portion of Madana” is a portion of Kāma. Remembering the former enmity, Śambara carried him away; Pradyumna killed the Śambara who had carried him away.
