VP 2.12.1

रथस् त्रिचक्रः सोमस्य कुन्दाभास् तस्य वाजिनः । वामदक्षिणतो युक्ता दश तेन चरत्य् असौ ॥१॥

The chariot of Soma has three wheels; its steeds are white like jasmine, yoked on the left and right, and with them he travels.

Śrīdhara translation

The Sun’s position was stated in four chapters and eight months. With the account of Soma and the rest, that exposition is concluded. The Moon’s horses are white, unlike those of the Sun, and ten are yoked on the left and right of his chariot.

VP 2.12.2

वीथ्याश्रयाणि ऋक्षाणि ध्रुवाधारेण वेगिना । ह्रासवृद्धिक्रमस् तस्य रश्मीनां सवितुर् यथा ॥२॥

The lunar mansions are placed in the paths supported by Dhruva, the swift one; their waxing and waning proceeds as does the waxing and waning of the Sun’s rays.

Śrīdhara translation

The mansions beginning with Aśvinī rest in paths such as Nāga-vīthī and are traversed eastward. The Moon’s waxing and waning proceed like the stated increase and decrease of the Sun’s rays.

VP 2.12.4

The radiant Sun replenishes the waning Moon, drunk by the gods, when he remains with one digit, with a single ray.

The waning Soma becomes full again and satisfies the gods and others. At new moon, because the Fathers have drunk the fifteenth digit, a remainder of the sixteenth remains as one digit; it is restored by the single ray called Suṣumṇā.

VP 2.12.5

By the same sequence in which the gods drink him, the Sun, the thief of water, replenishes the Moon day by day.

Śrīdhara translation

By the same order in which the gods beginning with Fire drink Soma from the first lunar day onward, the Sun replenishes the Moon each day. He is called a water-thief because he takes water invisibly and uses it to fill Soma.

तेषु वैराग्योत्पादनेन स्वकर्मानुष्ठानद्वारा श्रीवासुदेवप्राप्त्यर्थम् इत्य् आह—यच्चैतद् इति । कर्मवश्यो जीवो नाना योनीर्ब्रजतीति ज्ञात्वा विरक्तः सन् ध्रुवं नित्यमचलं परिपूर्णम् अतः सर्वदैकरूपं वासुदेवं येनोपायेन स्वकर्मादिना तद्भजनात्तत्प्रसादाल्लब्धज्ञानेन विशति तद् एव कुर्यादित्यर्थः ॥४६॥ इति श्रीधरकृतायामात्मप्रकाशाभिधायां स्वप्रकाशाख्यायां वा श्रीविष्णुपुराणटीकायां द्वितीयांशे द्वादशोऽध्यायः ॥ इति श्रीविष्णुपुराणे द्वितीयांशे द्वादशोऽध्यायः ॥ —ओ)०(ओ—
