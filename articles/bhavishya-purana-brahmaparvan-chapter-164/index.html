Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 164 — The Sun’s Sixth-Lunar-Day Vow

BP 1.164.1

शतानीक उवाच ।। पुनस्त्वं देवदेवस्य भास्करस्य महौजसः ।। पूजने यत्फलं प्रोक्तं तन्मे ब्रूहि द्विजोत्तम ।। १ ।।

Śatānīka said: O best of Brahmins, tell me again the fruit declared for worship of the mighty Bhāskara, god of gods.

BP 1.164.2

सुमंतुरुवाच ।। शृणु त्वं हि महाराज सर्वदं लोकपूजितम् ।। ब्रह्मेशोपेंद्रदेवानां त्रयाणामपि भारत ।। २ ।।

Sumantu said: Hear, O great king, O Bharata, of the giver of all, worshipped by the worlds and by the three gods Brahmā, Īśa, and Upendra.

BP 1.164.3

सुखासीनं सुरज्येष्ठं मनोवत्यां चतुर्मुखम् ।। प्रणम्य शिरसा भूमौ विष्ण्वीशौ वाक्यमूचतुः ।। ३ ।।

Having bowed with their heads to the ground before the eldest of the gods, four-faced and comfortably seated in Manovatī, Viṣṇu and Īśa spoke these words.

BP 1.164.4

य एष भगवान्देवः सहस्रकिरणो रविः ।। अस्य यत्पूजने पुण्यं प्राप्यते तद्वदस्व नौ ।। ४ ।।

This blessed god, Ravi of a thousand rays: tell us what merit is gained by worshipping him.

BP 1.164.5

ब्रह्मोवाच ।। साधु साधु जगन्नाथ साधु पृष्टोऽस्मि वामिह ।। तस्माच्छृणुतमेकाग्रौ गदतो निखिलं मम ।। ५ ।।

Brahmā said: Good, good, O lord of the world; you two have asked well here. Therefore, attentive, hear all that I say.

BP 1.164.6

स्वयमुत्पाद्य पुष्पाणि यः सूर्यं पूजयेत्स्वयम् ।। तानि साक्षात्प्रगृह्णाति तद्भक्त्या सततं रविः ।। ६ ।।

Whoever himself gathers flowers and himself worships the Sun, Ravi always receives those flowers directly, through devotion to him.

BP 1.164.7

यस्त्वारामं रवेः कुर्यादाम्रबिल्वादिशोभितम् ।। जातीविजयराजार्ककरवीरैः सकुङ्कुमैः ।। ७ ।।

Whoever makes a garden for the Sun, adorned with mango, bel, and the like, jasmine, vijaya, rājārka, and oleander, together with saffron;

BP 1.164.8

पुन्नागनागबकुलैरशोकतिलचंपकैः ।। अगस्तिकदलीखण्डैस्तस्य पुण्यफलं शृणु ।। ८ ।।

with puṇṇāga, nāga, bakula, aśoka, sesame, champak, agastya, and banana groves—hear the fruit of his merit.

BP 1.164.9

यावद्धि पत्रं कुसुमं बीजं सूतफलानि च ।। तावद्वर्षसहस्राणि सूरलोके महीयते ।। ९ ।।

For as many leaves, flowers, seeds, and fruits as there are, for that many thousand years he is honored in the Sun’s world.

BP 1.164.10

सघृतं गुग्गुलं दद्याद्राजन् वा कन्दुरुं तथा ।। चतुर्वेदिगृहे जन्म प्राप्नोति सततं सुखी ।। १० ।।

Offering guggul resin with ghee, O king, or frankincense likewise, one is born in the house of a man versed in the four Vedas and is always happy.

BP 1.164.11

कृष्णागुरुं च कर्पूरधूपं दद्याद्दिवाकरे ।। नैरन्तर्येण यस्तस्य राजन्पुण्यफलं शृणु ।। ११ ।।

One should offer black agarwood and camphor incense to the Sun. O king, hear the fruit of whoever does this without interruption.

BP 1.164.12

कल्पकोटिसहस्राणि कल्पकोटिशतानि च ।। भुक्त्वा सूर्यपुरे भोगांस्तस्यान्ते क्ष्माधिपो भवेत् ।। १२ ।।

Having enjoyed pleasures in the Sun’s city for eleven billion aeons, at their end he becomes lord of the earth.

BP 1.164.13

गुग्गुलुं घृतसंयुक्तं यक्षो गृह्णाति शब्दकृत् ।। यक्षाह्वयस्य दानेन तस्य लोके महीयते ।। १३ ।।

A yakṣa receives guggul resin mixed with ghee, making a sound. By this offering to the yakṣa, one is honored in his world.

BP 1.164.14

कृष्णांशौ कृष्णसप्तम्यां यः साज्यं गुग्गुलुं दहेत् ।। स चासौ सौरमासाद्य वर्षाणां च दशार्बुदम् ।। १४ ।।

Whoever burns guggul resin with ghee on the dark lunar half and its seventh day reaches the Sun’s world and remains there for one billion years.

BP 1.164.15

देवदारुं नमेरुं च श्रीवासं कुन्दुरुं तथा ।। श्रीफलं चाज्यसंयुक्तं दग्ध्वाश्रयमवाप्नुयात् ।। १५ ।।

Having burned deodar, nameru, śrīvāsa, and frankincense, and likewise bel fruit mixed with ghee, one obtains refuge.

BP 1.164.16

एवं सौगन्धिकं रूपं षट्सहस्रगुणोत्तरम् ।। अगुरुं दशसाहस्रं सघृतं द्विगुणं भवेत् ।। १६ ।।

Thus the fragrant substance is six thousandfold; agarwood is ten thousandfold, and with ghee it is doubled.

BP 1.164.17

अनन्तफलदं देवं सदा कुन्दुरुकामुकम् ।। द्विसहस्रपलानां तु महिषाक्षस्य गुग्गुलोः ।। १७ ।।

The god gives endless fruit and always desires frankincense. Of the buffalo-eyed guggul resin, two thousand palas—

BP 1.164.18

दग्ध्वार्धमविमिश्रस्य सूर्यतुल्यः प्रजायते ।। शोधयेत्पापसंयुक्तं पुरुषं नात्र संशयः ।। १८ ।।

having burned half of the unmixed guggul resin, one becomes equal to the Sun. One purifies a man joined with sin; there is no doubt here.

BP 1.164.19

कृष्णागुरुभवं धूपं तुषाग्निरिव काञ्चनम् ।। योंतःपुरगृहं गन्धैः सुगन्धैः प्रविलेपयेत् ।। १९ ।।

Black-agarwood incense is like gold in a fire of chaff. Whoever perfumes the inner chambers with fragrant substances,

BP 1.164.20

कपाटद्वारकुड्यादितिर्यगूर्ध्वं सवेदिकम् ।। वासयेत्पुष्पमालाभिर्धूपैश्चापि सुगन्धिभिः ।। २० ।।

the doors, doorways, walls, and the like, across and above, together with the altar, should perfume with flower garlands and fragrant incense.

BP 1.164.21

तस्य पुण्यं यथावत्तु युवयोर्वच्मि कृत्स्नशः ।। आपूरयन्दिशः सर्वा नानागन्धसमन्वितैः ।। २१ ।।

I tell you two his merit completely as it is, while filling all directions with various fragrances.

BP 1.164.22

कल्पकोटिशतं दिव्यं तेजसा वह्निसन्निभः ।। शक्रवत्प्रज्वलन्देवः सूर्यलोके महीयते ।। २२ ।।

For one billion divine aeons, blazing with splendor like fire and shining like Indra, he is honored in the Sun’s world.

BP 1.164.23

तस्यान्ते धर्मशेषेण त्रैलोक्याधिपतिर्भवेत् ।। शतावृतं तु यः कुर्यादेवं गन्धैर्भगालयम् ।। २३ ।।

At their end, through the remainder of his merit, he becomes lord of the three worlds. Whoever makes Bhaga’s temple fragrant in this way a hundred times,

BP 1.164.24

स सर्वशर्मसंयुक्तः सूर्यतुल्यपराक्रमः ।। सूर्यलोके वसेद्देवो युवाभ्यां संप्रपूजितः ।। २४ ।।

he, endowed with every happiness and with might equal to the Sun, dwells as a god in the Sun’s world, worshipped by you two.

BP 1.164.25

तद्वच्छुक्लैश्च संवीतं पट्टसूत्रैर्विनिर्मितम् ।। दत्त्वोपवीतं सूर्याय भवेद्वेदाङ्गपारगः ।। २५ ।।

Likewise, having given the Sun a sacred thread wrapped in white and made of silk threads, one becomes versed in the limbs of the Veda.

BP 1.164.26

वासांसि सुविचित्राणि सूरलोके महीयते ।। त्रुटिमात्रं तु यो दद्यादूर्णावस्त्रं सपङ्कजम् ।। २६ ।।

One is honored in the Sun’s world for garments of varied beauty. Whoever gives even a tiny piece of woolen cloth together with a lotus

BP 1.164.27

भास्करस्योत्तमाङ्गेषु तस्य पुण्यं ब्रवीम्यहम् ।। इन्द्रस्यार्धासने तिष्ठेद्यावदिन्द्राश्चतुर्दश ।। २७ ।।

I tell the merit of that offering placed on the crown of Bhāskara: he remains on half of Indra’s seat for as long as fourteen Indras.

BP 1.164.28

एवं वित्तानुसारेण सर्वं ज्ञेयं समासतः ।। सर्वेषां हेमपात्राणां मुकुटानां च सर्वशः ।। २८ ।।

Thus everything should be understood briefly according to one’s means, including all golden vessels and crowns.

BP 1.164.29

अर्कपत्रपुटं चूर्णं मधुपर्णसमन्वितम् ।। यो निवेदयतेऽर्काय सोऽश्वमेधफलं लभेत् ।। २९ ।।

Whoever offers to the Sun powder wrapped in calotropis leaves and joined with madhuka leaves obtains the fruit of the Horse Sacrifice.

BP 1.164.30

शालितण्डुलप्रस्थस्य कुर्यादन्नं सुसंस्कृतम् ।। सूर्याय च चरुं दत्त्वा सप्तम्यां तु विशेषतः ।। ३० ।।

One should prepare well-cooked food from a measure of fine rice and give the Sun an oblation, especially on the seventh lunar day.

BP 1.164.31

संयावं कृशरं पूपं पायसं यावकं तथा ।। दध्योदनरसालान्नमोदकान्गुडपूपकान् ।। ३१ ।।

Sweet cakes, rice-and-pulse porridge, fried cakes, milk pudding, barley porridge, curd-rice, sweet curd, dumplings, and jaggery cakes;

BP 1.164.32

यावन्तस्तण्डुलास्तस्मिन्नैवेद्ये परिसङ्ख्यया ।। तावद्वर्षसहस्राणि सूरलोके महीयते ।। ३२ ।।

for as many grains of rice as are counted in that offering, for that many thousand years one is honored in the Sun’s world.

BP 1.164.33

गुडखण्डकृतानां च भक्ष्याणां विनिवेदने ।। घृतेन प्लावितानां च फलं शतगुणं लभेत् ।। ३३ ।।

By offering eatables made of jaggery pieces and flooded with ghee, one obtains a hundredfold fruit.

BP 1.164.34

रसाल खाद्यकाद्यानां भक्ष्याणां फलमिष्यते ।। तदर्धं सलिलस्यापि वासितस्य निवेदयेत् ।। ३४ ।।

The fruit of sweet curd and other foods and eatables is desired; one should also offer half that amount of scented water.

BP 1.164.35

यथाकालोपलब्धानि भक्ष्याणि विविधानि च ।। निवेद्यार्काय परमं स्थानं प्राप्नोति पूजनात् ।। ३५ ।।

Having offered the various foods available according to the season to the Sun, one reaches the highest place through worship.

BP 1.164.36

प्रज्वाल्य घृतदीपं तु भास्करस्यालये शुभम् ।। आग्नेयं यानमारुह्य गच्छेत्सौमनसं पुरम् ।। ३६ ।।

Having lit an auspicious ghee lamp in Bhāskara’s temple, one mounts a fiery vehicle and goes to the city of Saumanasa.

BP 1.164.37

यः कुर्यात्कार्तिके मासि शोभनां दीपमालिकाम् ।। सप्तम्यामथ षष्ठ्यां वा अमावास्यायामथापि वा ।। ३७ ।।

Whoever makes a beautiful row of lamps in the month of Kārttika, on the seventh or sixth lunar day, or also on the new-moon day,

BP 1.164.38

भास्करायुतसङ्काशस्तेजसा भासयन्दिशः ।। दिव्याभरणसम्पन्नः कुलमुद्धृत्य सर्वशः ।। ३८ ।।

resembling ten thousand Bhāskaras, illuminating the directions with splendor, furnished with divine ornaments, and having raised his whole family,

BP 1.164.39

यावत्प्रदीपसङ्ख्यानं घृतेनापूर्य बोधितम् ।। तावद्वर्षसहस्राणि सूर्यलोके महीयते ।। ३९ ।।

for as many lamps as are counted, filled with ghee and lit, for that many thousand years he is honored in the Sun’s world.

BP 1.164.40

दीपवृक्षमथोद्बोध्य पर्वस्वायतनेषु वै ।। पूर्वस्माद्द्विगुणं पुण्यं लभते नात्र संशयः ।। ४० ।।

Then, having lit a tree of lamps in the temples at festivals, one obtains twice the merit of before; there is no doubt here.

BP 1.164.41

दीपवृक्षं समुद्बोध्य भास्करायतनेषु भोः ।। सर्वलोहमयं वीर रविलोके महीयते ।। ४१ ।।

Having lit a tree of lamps in Bhāskara’s temples, O hero, made entirely of metal, one is honored in Ravi’s world.

BP 1.164.42

शिरसा धारयेद्दीपं भास्करस्याग्रतो निशि ।। ललाटे चैव हस्ताभ्यां समुद्युक्तस्तथोरसि ।। ४२ ।।

At night, one should hold a lamp on the head before Bhāskara, on the forehead, in both hands, and likewise on the chest.

BP 1.164.43

भास्करायुतसङ्काशो विमानैरर्कसन्निभैः ।। कल्पायुतशतं चैव सूर्यलोके महीयते ।। ४३ ।।

Resembling ten thousand Bhāskaras, in vehicles resembling the Sun, one is honored in the Sun’s world for one million aeons.

BP 1.164.44

अन्नदाता तु यो वीर वीरलोके महीयते ।। भास्करस्याग्रतो दत्त्वा दर्पणं निर्मलं शुभम् ।। ४४ ।।

Whoever gives food, O hero, is honored in the world of heroes. Having given a clear, auspicious mirror before Bhāskara,

BP 1.164.45

पर्यङ्के शोभितं कृत्वा श्वेतमाल्यैः सचन्दनैः ।। वृकार्कनिर्मलः श्रीमान्दिव्याभरणरूपधृक् ।। कल्पायुतसहस्राणि सूर्यलोके महीयते ।। ४५ ।।

having placed it on a couch, adorned with white garlands and sandalwood, pure as Vṛkārka, glorious, bearing divine ornaments, one is honored in the Sun’s world for ten million aeons.

BP 1.164.46

कृत्वा प्रदक्षिणं भक्त्या श्रद्धधानो रवेर्नरः ।। अश्वमेधसहस्रस्य सुखेन लभते फलम् ।। ४६ ।।

A man who faithfully circumambulates Ravi with devotion easily obtains the fruit of a thousand Horse Sacrifices.

BP 1.164.47

कृत्वा प्रदक्षिणं यस्तु नमस्कारं प्रयोजयेत् ।। राजसूयाश्वमेधाभ्यां सकलं विन्दते फलम् ।। ४७ ।।

Whoever circumambulates and makes a bow obtains the entire fruit of the Royal Consecration and Horse Sacrifices.

BP 1.164.48

नमस्कारः स्मृतो यज्ञः सर्वयज्ञोत्तमोत्तमः ।। नमस्कृत्वा सहस्रांशुमश्वमेधफलं लभेत् ।। ४८ ।।

A bow is held to be a sacrifice, the best of all sacrifices; having bowed to the thousand-rayed Sun, one obtains the fruit of the Horse Sacrifice.

BP 1.164.49

प्रणम्य दण्डवद्भूमौ नमस्कारेण योऽर्चयेत् ।। स यां गतिमवाप्नोति न तां क्रतुशतैरपि ।। ४९ ।।

For one billion divine years one is honored in the Sun’s world. Anointing with fragrance is merit; sandalwood is twice that.

BP 1.164.50

सर्वयज्ञोपवासेषु सर्वतीर्थेषु यत्फलम् ।। अभिज्ञाप्योपहारेण पूजया फलमश्नुते ।। ५० ।।

The fruit found in all sacrifices, fasts, and sacred fords, one obtains through worship with an offering made in reverence.

BP 1.164.51

श्वेतं महाध्वजं कृत्वा कृत्वा चापं च रङ्गकम् ।। किङ्किणीजालनिर्घोषं मयूरच्छत्रभूषितम् ।। यस्त्वर्यम्णे नरो दद्याच्छ्रद्धया परयान्वितः ।। ५१ ।।

A man who, with supreme faith, gives Aryaman a great white banner, a bow, and a painted emblem, sounding with a net of small bells and adorned with a peacock parasol,

BP 1.164.52

स शतेन विमानानां सर्वदेवनमस्कृतः ।। मन्वन्तरशतं देव मोदते दिवि देववत् ।। ५२ ।।

being honored by all the gods, delights in heaven like a god for a hundred periods of Manu, in a hundred vehicles.

BP 1.164.53

ध्वजमालाकुलं कुर्याद्यः प्रान्तेषु भगालयम् ।। महाध्वजाष्टकं चापि दिग्विदिक्षु निवेदयेत् ।। ५३ ।।

Whoever fills the edges of Bhaga’s temple with banner garlands and offers eight great banners in the directions and intermediate directions,

BP 1.164.54

स विमानसहस्रेण ध्वजमालाकुलेन तु ।। कल्पायुतशतं दिव्यं मोदते दिवि सूरवत् ।। ५४ ।।

with a thousand vehicles crowded with banner garlands, he delights in heaven like the Sun for a million divine aeons.

BP 1.164.55

शतचन्द्रांशुविमलं मुक्तादामोपशोभितम् ।। मणिदण्डमयं छत्रं दद्याद्वा काञ्चनादिकम् ।। ५५ ।।

One should give a parasol, clear as the rays of a hundred moons, adorned with pearl strings, made with a jeweled staff, or made of gold and the like.

BP 1.164.56

स धार्यमाणच्छत्रेण हेमदण्डोपशोभिना ।। मोदते सूर्यलोके तु विमानवरमास्थितः ।। ५६ ।।

With the parasol being borne, adorned with a golden staff, seated on an excellent vehicle, he rejoices in the Sun’s world.

BP 1.164.57

ततस्तस्माच्च्युतो लोकान्निसर्गाद्भुवमागतः ।। भुङ्क्ते समुद्रपर्यन्तामेकच्छत्रां वसुन्धराम् ।। ५७ ।।

Then, having fallen from that world and come to earth by the natural course, he enjoys the earth bounded by the ocean under one parasol.

BP 1.164.58

यः शृङ्खलासमायुक्तां महाघण्टां महास्वनाम् ।। कांस्यलोहमयीं वापि निबध्नीयाद्भगालये ।। ५८ ।।

Whoever fastens in Bhaga’s temple a great, loud-sounding bell joined with a chain, made of bronze or iron,

BP 1.164.59

शोभनः स्यान्नरः श्रीमान्भगस्यातिवल्लभः ।। सूर्यतुल्यबलो भूत्वा सूर्यलोके महीयते ।। ५९ ।।

becomes handsome and glorious, very dear to Bhaga; having become equal to the Sun in strength, he is honored in the Sun’s world.

BP 1.164.60

भेरीमृदङ्गपटहझर्झरीमर्दलादिकम् ।। दशकांस्यादिवादित्रं यो भगाय निवेदयेत् ।। ६० ।।

Whoever offers Bhaga instruments such as drums, kettledrums, large drums, cymbals, and mardalas, and ten bronze and other instruments,

BP 1.164.61

स विमानैर्महाभागैर्वंशवीणायुतस्वनैः ।। युगान्तकशतं दिव्यं भगलोके महीयते ।। ६१ ।।

with splendid vehicles sounding with bamboo flutes and lutes, one is honored in Bhaga’s world for a hundred divine age-endings.

BP 1.164.62

सुसङ्गीतकदानेन सवाद्येन विशेषतः ।। यथेष्टं भास्करे लोके मोदते कालमक्षयम् ।। ६२ ।।

By giving good music, especially with instruments, one delights as desired in Bhāskara’s world for unending time.

BP 1.164.63

महामहास्वनं दत्त्वा शङ्खयुग्मं भगालये ।। युगकोटिशतं दिव्यं भगलोके महीयते ।। ६३ ।।

Having given a pair of very loud conches in Bhaga’s temple, one is honored in Bhaga’s world for one billion divine world-ages.

BP 1.164.64

विमानं बहुवर्णाभं मध्ये पङ्कजभूषितम् ।। विचित्रमेकवर्णं वासनवस्त्रोपकल्पितम् ।। ६४ ।।

A vehicle of many colors, adorned with a lotus in the middle, varied yet of one color, furnished with a covering cloth,

BP 1.164.65

किङ्किणीजालसम्पन्नं वर्णकैश्चोपशोभितम् ।। पुष्पमालाप्रभं वापि घण्टाचामरभूषितम् ।। ६५ ।।

equipped with a net of small bells, adorned with pigments, bright as flower garlands, and adorned with a bell and fly-whisk;

BP 1.164.66

भगस्योपरि यो दद्यात्सर्वरत्नोपशोभितम् ।। दुकूलपट्टदेवाङ्गैर्वस्त्रैर्वा वर्णकान्वितैः ।। ६६ ।।

whoever gives it above Bhaga, adorned with every jewel or furnished with garments of fine silk and colored cloth,

BP 1.164.67

पट्टादिवस्त्रतन्तूनां परिसङ्ख्या तु या भवेत् ।। तावद्युगसहस्राणि सूरलोके महीयते ।। ६७ ।।

for whatever number there may be of threads in silk and other garments, for that many thousand world-ages one is honored in the Sun’s world.

BP 1.164.68

भगाहुत्या जगत्सर्वं सृष्टिद्वारेण धार्यते ।। अग्निवर्त्मा वचस्युक्तो ह्यग्निरस्यात्मजः सदा ।। ६८ ।।

Through offerings to Bhaga the whole world is sustained by way of creation. Agni is said in speech to be his path, for Agni is always his son.

BP 1.164.69

यस्त्वग्निकार्यं विधिवन्नित्यं भगालये ।। भगमुद्दिश्य राजेन्द्र स याति परमां गतिम् ।। ६९ ।।

Whoever properly performs the daily fire rite in Bhaga’s temple, directing it toward Bhaga, O king of kings, goes to the highest destination.

BP 1.164.70

सर्वान्नं यावकोपेतं यस्तु नित्यविधिं हरेत् ।। पुष्पधूपजलोपेतं काले काले विशेषतः ।। ७० ।।

Whoever performs the daily rite with all foods and barley, furnished with flowers, incense, and water at each proper time,

BP 1.164.71

महाश्वेतादिमातॄणां त्रिकल्पानां च सर्वशः ।। यः कृत्वा सकृदप्येवं सर्वदिक्षु बलिं हरेत् ।। स नरश्च सहस्राणि शाण्डिलेयपुरे वसेत् ।। ७१ ।।

having once performed this for the Mothers beginning with Mahāśvetā, for the three cycles, and offered gifts in all directions, that man dwells in Śāṇḍileya’s city for thousands of years.

BP 1.164.72

सौरसन्ध्याबलिं कृत्वा दिनान्ते सततं रवेः ।। वर्षायुतशतं साग्रं भगलोके महीयते ।। ७२ ।।

Having continually made the Sun’s twilight offering to Ravi at day’s end, one is honored in Bhaga’s world for a full million years.

BP 1.164.73

दध्योदनपयोभिर्यः पूरितं पात्रमावृतम् ।। पुष्पधूपार्चितं चैव वितानोपरि शोभितम् ।। ७३ ।।

A vessel filled with curd-rice and milk, covered, worshipped with flowers and incense, and adorned above with a canopy,

BP 1.164.74

शिरसा धारयेत्पात्रं शनैर्गच्छेत्प्रदक्षिणम् ।। रवायतनपर्यन्ते शङ्खवीणादिनिस्वनैः ।। ७४ ।।

one should carry the vessel on one’s head and slowly circumambulate the boundary of Ravi’s temple, with the sounds of conches, lutes, and the like.

BP 1.164.75

दर्पणैर्धूपमालाभिर्गेयनृत्यादिशोभितम् ।। भानोर्हि स्मृतिशीलश्च तस्य पुण्यफलं शृणु ।। ७५ ।।

Adorned with mirrors, incense, garlands, songs, dances, and the like—hear the merit of the one constant in remembrance of the Sun.

BP 1.164.76

दिव्यं वर्षसहस्रं तु दिव्यं वर्षशतं तथा ।। तपस्तप्तं महत्तेन भवेदेवं न संशयः ।। ७६ ।।

A divine thousand years and also a divine hundred years: thus great austerity is performed by him; there is no doubt.

BP 1.164.77

भगभक्तिप्रसन्नात्मा यद्यपि स्यात्स पापकृत् ।। भगलोके वसेन्नित्यं भगानुचरतां गतः ।। ७७ ।।

Even if he is a sinner, one whose heart is gladdened by devotion to Bhaga dwells always in Bhaga’s world, having attained the state of Bhaga’s attendant.

BP 1.164.78

कृष्णां तु षष्ठीं नक्तेन यश्च कृष्णां च सप्तमीम् ।। इह भोगानवाप्नोति परत्र च शुभां गतिम् ।। ७८ ।।

Whoever observes the dark sixth lunar day by eating at night and likewise the dark seventh obtains enjoyments here and a good destination hereafter.

BP 1.164.79

योब्दमेकं तु कुर्वीत नक्तं भगदिने नरः ।। ब्रह्मचारी जितक्रोधो भगार्चनपरो नरः ।। अयाचितात्परं नक्तं तस्मान्नक्तेन वर्तयेत् ।। ७९ ।।

A man should observe eating at night for one year on Bhaga’s day, celibate, with anger subdued and devoted to worship of Bhaga. Therefore he should eat at night from food obtained without asking.

BP 1.164.80

देवैस्तु भुक्तं मध्याह्ने पूर्वाह्णे ऋषिभिस्तथा ।। अपराह्णे तु पितृभिः सन्ध्यायां गुह्यकादिभिः ।। ८० ।।

Food is eaten by the gods at midday, by the seers in the forenoon, by the ancestors in the afternoon, and by the Guhyakas and others at twilight.

BP 1.164.81

सर्वा वेला ह्यतिक्रम्य सौराणां भोजनं परम् ।। भुञ्जानो नक्तकाले तु सूर्यभक्तिपरायणः ।। ८१ ।।

Passing over all the other times, the night meal is best for the Sun’s devotees; whoever eats at night is devoted to worship of the Sun.

BP 1.164.82

भगलोकमवाप्नोति सुमनाः सुमनोव्रतः ।। भुक्त्वा सौमनसाँल्लोकान्राजा भवति भूतले ।। ८२ ।।

Clear-minded and of a good vow, he reaches Bhaga’s world; having enjoyed the pleasant worlds, he becomes a king on earth.

BP 1.164.83

हविष्यभोजनं स्नानमाहारस्य च लाघवम् ।। अग्निकार्यमधःशय्यां नक्तभोजी समाचरेत् ।। ८३ ।।

The night-eater should perform a meal of sacrificial food, bathing, moderation in food, the fire rite, and sleeping on the ground.

BP 1.164.84

कृष्णषष्ठ्यां प्रयत्नेन कृत्वा नक्तं विधानतः ।। नरो मार्गशिरे मासि अंशुमानिति पूजयेत् ।। ८४ ।।

Having carefully observed the night meal according to rule on the dark sixth lunar day in Mārgaśīrṣa, a man should worship the Sun as Aṃśumān.

BP 1.164.85

विधिवत्प्राश्य गोमूत्रमनाहारो निशि स्वपेत् ।। अतिरात्रस्य यज्ञस्य फलमाप्नोति मानवः ।। ८५ ।।

Having properly drunk cow’s urine, one should sleep at night without food; a man obtains the fruit of the Atirātra sacrifice.

BP 1.164.86

पुष्येऽप्येवं सहस्रांशुं भानुमन्तमुषन्ति च ।। वाजपेयफलं प्राप्य घृतं प्राश्य लभेन्नरः ।। ८६ ।।

Likewise in Puṣya one should worship the thousand-rayed Sun as Bhānumant; by drinking ghee a man obtains the fruit of the strength-drink sacrifice.

BP 1.164.87

माघे दिवाकरं नाम कृष्णषष्ठ्यां नरोत्तम ।। निशि पीत्वा तु गोक्षीरं गोमेधफलमाप्नुयात् ।। ८७ ।।

In Māgha, on the dark sixth lunar day, O best of men, having drunk cow’s milk at night, one obtains the fruit of the cow sacrifice.

BP 1.164.88

मार्तण्डं फाल्गुने मासि पूजयित्वा गवां पयः ।। पिबेत्ततः सूर्यलोके मोदते सोऽयुतायुतम् ।। ८८ ।।

Having worshipped Mārtāṇḍa in the month of Phālguna and drunk the cows’ milk, one then delights in the Sun’s world for a hundred million years.

BP 1.164.89

चैत्रे मासि विवस्वन्तं पूजयित्वा सुभक्तिमान् ।। हविष्याशी सूर्यलोकेऽप्सरोभिः सह मोदते ।। ८९ ।।

Having worshipped Vivasvant in the month of Caitra, the very devoted one, eating sacrificial food, delights in the Sun’s world with celestial nymphs.

BP 1.164.90

वैशाखे चण्डकिरणं पूजयेच्च पयोव्रतः ।। वर्षाणामयुतं साग्रं मोदते सूर्यसन्निधौ ।। ९० ।।

In Vaiśākha one should worship the sharp-rayed Sun, observing a milk vow; one delights in the Sun’s presence for a full ten thousand years.

BP 1.164.91

ज्येष्ठे दिवस्पतिं पूज्य गवां शृङ्गोदकं पिबेत् ।। गवां कोटिप्रदानस्य निखिलं फलमाप्नुयात् ।। ९१ ।।

Having worshipped the lord of the day in Jyeṣṭha, one should drink water from cows’ horns; one obtains the entire fruit of giving ten million cows.

BP 1.164.92

आषाढे त्वर्कनामानमिष्ट्वा प्राश्य च गोमयम् ।। प्रयात्यर्कसलोकं तु वर्षाणां च शतं शतम् ।। ९२ ।।

In Āṣāḍha, having worshipped the one named Arka and eaten cow dung, one goes to Arka’s world for ten thousand years.

BP 1.164.93

श्रावणेऽर्यमणं नाम पूजयित्वा पयः पिबेत् ।। वर्षाणामयुतं साग्रं मोदते भास्करालये ।। ९३ ।।

In Śrāvaṇa, having worshipped the one named Aryaman, one should drink milk; one delights in Bhāskara’s abode for a full ten thousand years.

BP 1.164.94

मासि भाद्रपदे षष्ठ्यां भास्करं नाम पूजयेत् ।। भास्करं पञ्चगव्यस्य सर्वमेधफलं लभेत् ।। ९४ ।।

In Bhādrapada, on the sixth lunar day, one should worship the one named Bhāskara; by worshipping him with the five products of the cow, one obtains the fruit of all sacrifices.

BP 1.164.95

मासि चाश्वयुजे षष्ठ्यां भगाख्यं नाम पूजयेत् ।। पलगोमूत्रभुक्चैव अश्वमेधफलं लभेत् ।। ९५ ।।

In Āśvayuja, on the sixth lunar day, one should worship the one named Bhaga; eating a pala of cow’s urine, one obtains the fruit of the Horse Sacrifice.

BP 1.164.96

मासे तु कार्तिके षष्ठ्यां शक्राख्यं नाम पूजयेत् ।। दूर्वाङ्कुरं सकृत्प्राश्य राजसूयफलं लभेत् ।। ९६ ।।

In Kārttika, on the sixth lunar day, one should worship the one named Śakra; having eaten a durvā sprout once, one obtains the fruit of the Royal Consecration.

BP 1.164.97

वर्षान्ते भोजयेद्विप्रान्सूर्यभक्तिपरायणान् ।। पायसं मधुसंयुक्तं वज्रेण च परिप्लुतम् ।। ९७ ।।

At the year’s end, one should feed Brahmins devoted to worship of the Sun with rice pudding mixed with honey and covered with vajra.

BP 1.164.98

शक्त्या हिरण्यवासांसि भक्त्या तेभ्यो निवेदयेत् ।। निवेदयेच्च सूर्याय कृष्णां गां च पयस्विनीम् ।। ९८ ।।

According to one’s means, one should offer gold and garments to them with devotion; one should also offer the Sun a black, milk-bearing cow.

BP 1.164.99

वर्षमेकं च देवे वै नैरन्तर्येण यो नयेत् ।। कृष्णषष्ठीव्रतं भक्त्या तस्य पुण्यफलं शृणु ।। ९९ ।।

Whoever completes a whole year without interruption in devotion to the god through the dark sixth-day vow—hear the fruit of his merit.

BP 1.164.100

सर्वपापविनिर्मुक्तः सर्वकामसमन्वितः ।। मोदते सूर्यलोके तु स नरः शाश्वतीः समाः ।। १०० ।।

Freed from all sins and endowed with every desire, that man rejoices in the Sun’s world for everlasting years.

BP 1.164.101

पुण्येष्वहःसु सर्वेषु विषुवद्ग्रहणादिषु ।। दानोपवासहोमाद्यैरक्षयं खगो जायते ।। १०१ ।।

On all holy days, at the equinoxes, eclipses, and the like, through gifts, fasts, fire offerings, and the like, one becomes imperishable with the Sun.

BP 1.164.102

सुमंतुरुवाच ।। इत्युक्तवान्पुरा भानुररुणाय विशांपते ।। कृष्णषष्ठीव्रतं पुण्यं सर्वपापभयापहम् ।। १०२ ।।

Sumantu said: Thus Bhānu spoke long ago to Aruṇa, O lord of the people, of the meritorious dark sixth-day vow that removes all sin and fear.

BP 1.164.103

कृत्वेदं पुरुषो भक्त्या भास्करस्य महात्मनः ।। प्रयाति परमं स्थानं भानोरमिततेजसः ।। १०३ ।।

Having performed this with devotion, a man goes to the highest abode of the great-souled Bhāskara, the immeasurably radiant Sun.
