Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 157 — Introduction to the Sun’s Incarnation

BP 1.157.1

शतानीक उवाच । एतन्मे कौतुकं ब्रह्मन्यद्वरं ब्रह्मणे रविः । दत्तवांस्तव पुत्रत्वमन्वये कश्यपस्य तु ॥ १ ॥

Śatānīka said: This is my wonder, O Brahmin: the boon Ravi gave Brahmā was birth as a son in Kaśyapa’s line.

BP 1.157.2

यास्यामि द्विजशार्दूल प्रपन्नतिमिरापहः । एतन्मे महदाश्चर्यं शंस भूमिं कथं व्रजेत् ॥ २ ॥

I shall go to him who removes darkness for those who take refuge, O tiger among Brahmins. This is my great wonder; tell me how he comes to earth.

BP 1.157.3

देवादीनां प्रणेता यो यो भुवि प्रसवो विभुः । स कथं भूतले व्योम जन्मभावं गमिष्यति ॥ ३ ॥

He who leads the gods and others, the all-pervading source on earth—how will he pass from the sky to birth on the ground?

BP 1.157.4

किमर्थं दिव्यमात्मानं जन्मने स नियोक्ष्यति । यश्चक्रं वर्तयत्येको ब्रह्मादीनां मनोरमम् ॥ ४ ॥

For what purpose will he direct his divine self toward birth, he who alone turns the delightful wheel of Brahmā and the others?

BP 1.157.5

स जन्मनि कथं पुण्यां बुद्धिं चक्रे विदांवर । गोपायनं यत्कुरुते जगतः सर्वलौकिकम् ॥ ५ ॥

How did he form his sacred thought concerning birth, O best of the learned, he who protects the whole world?

BP 1.157.6

गोभिः पालयते कृत्स्नमात्मनो यः स्वयं रविः । महाभूतानि भूतात्मा यश्चकार दधार च ॥ ६ ॥

Ravi himself protects the whole world with his rays; the self of beings made and sustained the great elements.

BP 1.157.7

अगर्भः स कथं गर्भमुदरे याचते विभुः । येन गोभिः समाक्रांता विप्र लोकाश्चतुर्दश ॥ ७ ॥

How does the unborn, all-pervading one seek a womb in the belly, he by whose rays the fourteen worlds are fully pervaded, O Brahmin?

BP 1.157.8

स्थापिता जगतो मार्गास्त्रिवर्गप्रवरास्त्रयः । योऽन्तकाले जगत्पीत्वा कृत्वा वज्रमयं वपुः ॥ ८ ॥

He established the three paths of the world, foremost among the three aims; at the end he drinks up the world, having made his body like a thunderbolt.

BP 1.157.9

लोकमेकार्णवं चक्रे दृश्यते स्वेन कर्मणा । यः पुराणे पुराणात्मा तेजसां रूपमास्थितः ॥ ९ ॥

He made the world a single ocean, as is seen through his own act; in the Purāṇa, the ancient Self assumed the form of the lights.

BP 1.157.10

अपि सृष्ट्वा द्विजश्रेष्ठ यः ससर्ज वसुंधराम् । चकार च पुरा यश्च त्रैलोक्यमिदमव्ययः ॥ १० ॥

Having created, O best of Brahmins, he created the earth; formerly the imperishable one also made this threefold world.

BP 1.157.11

ददौ कृत्वा वसुमतीं सुराणां सुरसत्तमः । यः स्थितो ह्यनलं पीत्वा संवत्सरमयश्च यः । पातालस्थोर्णवरसं मध्यतोयमयं हविः ॥ ११ ॥

The best of gods made the earth and gave it to the gods. He stands having drunk fire; he is made of the year, the essence of the sea in the underworld, and the oblation of the central waters.

BP 1.157.12

सहस्रशिरसं देवं सहस्राक्षं सहस्रशः । सहस्रचरणं ब्रह्मन्यमाहुर्वै युगे युगे ॥ १२ ॥

They call him in every age the thousand-headed god, thousand-eyed, thousandfold, and thousand-footed, O Brahmin.

BP 1.157.13

मुखाद्यस्य समुत्पन्नो वेधा लोकपितामहः । हरिश्च वक्षसो यस्य ललाटाद्यस्य शंकरः ॥ १३ ॥

From whose mouth the creator, grandsire of the world, was born; Hari from whose chest, and Śaṅkara from whose forehead.

BP 1.157.14

येन ते निहता दैत्या मंदेहा नाम नामतः । ब्रह्मादीनां दुराधर्षो यः सदा विघ्ननाशनः ॥ १४ ॥

He slew the Daityas named Mandehas; he is ever unassailable even to Brahmā and the others, the all-pervading destroyer of obstacles.

BP 1.157.15

सर्वदेवमयं कृत्वा सर्वायुधधरं वपुः । एकचक्ररथारूढो गरुडाग्रजसारथिः ॥ १५ ॥

Having made his body consist of all the gods and bear every weapon, he rides a one-wheeled chariot, with Garuḍa’s elder brother as charioteer.

BP 1.157.16

करांते यो जगत्सर्वं सह दानवराक्षसम् । प्रकाशतममस्पृष्टं वपुर्यस्य सदा द्विज ॥ १६ ॥

At the end of his rays is the whole world, together with demons and demons; his body is ever most luminous and untouched, O Brahmin.

BP 1.157.17

पूर्वा दिशं गतो नित्यमुदयाचलमक्रमम् । नाशयेद्यस्तु सततं तमो लोकस्य शांतये ॥ १७ ॥

He always goes eastward to the uninterrupted mountain of sunrise; he constantly destroys the world’s darkness for its peace.

BP 1.157.18

नाशयित्वा तमो यस्तु क्रियाः सर्वाः प्रवर्तयेत् । योक्त्राणि दक्षिणादीक्ष्य मुसलोलूखलानि च ॥ १८ ॥

Having destroyed darkness, he sets all actions in motion; he looks southward at the yokes, mortars, and pestles.

BP 1.157.19

गार्हपत्येन विधिना तद्वद्धार्येण कर्मणा । अग्निमाहवनीं चैव वेदिं चैव कुशं स्रुचम् । प्रोक्षणीयव्रतं चैव अवभृथं तथैव च ॥ १९ ॥

By the household-fire rite and the sustaining rite: the offering fire, the altar, sacred grass grass, the ladle, sprinkling vessel, vow, and concluding bath.

BP 1.157.20

सर्वानिमांश्च यश्चक्रे हव्यभागप्रदान्मुखे । हव्यादांश्च सुरान्यज्ञे कव्यादांश्च पितॄनपि ॥ २० ॥

He made all these, placing the shares of offerings at the front: the gods who eat oblations in sacrifice and the ancestors who eat ancestral offerings.

BP 1.157.21

भागार्थे मधुधानाय चक्रे यो यज्ञकर्मणि । पूषणं च सुतं सोमं पवित्रामरणीमपि ॥ २१ ॥

For the sake of portions and honey-offerings in the sacrificial rite, he made Pūṣan, pressed Soma, the purifier, and the fire-kindling sticks.

BP 1.157.22

यज्ञानि च द्रव्याणि यज्ञांश्चापि सऋत्विजः । सदस्यान्यजमानांश्च मेधाविनस्तथोत्तमान् ॥ २२ ॥

He made the sacrifices and their materials, the rites with their officiating priests, assembly members, sacrificers, the wise, and the excellent.

BP 1.157.23

विबभाज पुरा सर्वं पारमेष्ठयेन कर्मणा । युगानुरूपो यः कृत्वा लोकाननुवरं क्रमात् ॥ २३ ॥

Formerly he divided all through Brahmā’s work; forming the worlds one after another in order according to the ages.

BP 1.157.24

क्षणान्कलाश्च काष्ठाश्च कालवैकल्यमेव च । मुहूर्तास्तिथयो मासाः पक्षाः संवत्सरास्तथा ॥ २४ ॥

Moments, measures, and short time-units, and the variations of time; forty-eight-minute periods, lunar days, months, fortnights, and years likewise.

BP 1.157.25

ऋतवः कालयोगाश्च प्रमाणं विविधं नृषु । आयुः क्षेत्राण्यपचयोपचयांश्चैव योऽकरोत् ॥ २५ ॥

Seasons, conjunctions of time, diverse measures among people, lifespan, fields, decline, and increase—he made them.

BP 1.157.26

सृष्टा लोकास्त्रयोनंता येन ज्ञानेन वर्त्मना । सर्व ,भूतगणाः सृष्टाः सर्वभूतात्मना सदा ॥ २६ ॥

The threefold, endless worlds were created by him through the path of knowledge; all hosts of beings were always created by the self of all beings.

BP 1.157.27

प्रणामत्रयपूर्वेण योगेन रमते च यः । यो गतागतिपोतेन त्रातास्ति जगदीश्वरः ॥ २७ ॥

He delights in the discipline of yoga preceded by three prostrations; the Lord of the world is the savior, the boat across the cycle of coming and going.

BP 1.157.28

यो गतिर्वृषयुक्तानां गतिर्योऽपापकर्मणाम् । चातुर्वर्ण्यप्रभावश्च वपुर्होत्रस्य रक्षिता ॥ २८ ॥

He is the course of those yoked to dharma and the course of those whose deeds are sinless; the source of the four varṇas and protector of the sacrificial rite.

BP 1.157.29

धातुवैद्यस्य यो वेत्ता चतुराश्रमसंश्रयः । दिगंबरानुभूतश्च वायुर्वायुविभावनः ॥ २९ ॥

He knows the treatment of the bodily elements, supports the four stages of life, and is experienced by the sky-clad ascetic; he is wind and the source of wind.

BP 1.157.30

अग्नीषोमात्मकं ज्योतिर्योगीशः क्षणदांतकः । यः परं श्रूयते ज्योतिर्यः परं श्रूयते तपः ॥ ३० ॥

Light consisting of Fire and Soma, lord of yogis, destroyer of night: he is heard as the supreme light, he is heard as the supreme austerity.

BP 1.157.31

यं परं परमं प्राहुः परमात्मानमच्युतम् । ब्रह्मादिभिः स्तुतो देवो यश्च दैत्यांतकृद्विभुः ॥ ३१ ॥

He whom they call the supreme, highest, immutable Supreme Self, the god praised by Brahmā and the others, the all-pervading slayer of Daityas.

BP 1.157.32

युगांतेष्वतंको यस्तु यश्च लोकांतकोत्तमः । सेतुर्यो लोकसेतूनां मध्ये यो मध्यकर्मणाम् ॥ ३२ ॥

He is the end at the ends of the ages and the highest end of the worlds, the bridge among the bridges of worlds, the middle among the actions in between.

BP 1.157.33

वेत्ता यो वेदविदुषां प्रभुर्यः प्रभविष्णुनाम् । सौम्यभूतस्तु सौम्यानामग्निभूतोग्निवर्चसाम् ॥ ३३ ॥

He is the knower among knowers of the Veda, lord among the powerful, gentle among the gentle, fire among those of fiery brilliance.

BP 1.157.34

मानुषाणां मनोभूतस्तपोभूतस्तपस्विनाम् । विनयो नयवृत्तीनां तेजस्तेजस्विनामपि ॥ ३४ ॥

He is mind among humans, austerity among ascetics, humility among those who practice discipline, and radiance among the radiant.

BP 1.157.35

विग्रहो विग्रहाणां च गतिर्गतिमतामपि । आकाशप्रसवो वायुर्वायुः प्राणो हुताशनः । देवाहुतिप्रदानोद्यत्प्राणाग्निस्तमनाशनः ॥ ३५ ॥

He is form among forms and the course among those who move; space-born wind, wind as breath, fire as the oblation-eater, the life-fire striving to offer to the gods, destroyer of darkness.

BP 1.157.36

रसाद्धि शोणितं भवति शोणितान्मांसमुच्यते । मांसान्मज्जावसोर्जन्म मज्जनोस्थीनि जन्मतः ॥ ३६ ॥

From the vital essence, blood arises; from blood, flesh is said to arise; from flesh, marrow and fat are born; from marrow, bones are born.

BP 1.157.37

अस्थिमज्ज्ञः समभवत्ततो वै शुक्रंमादिशेत् । शुक्राद्गर्भः समभवद्रसमूलेन कर्मणा । तत्रापः प्रथमो भागः स सौम्यो राशिरुच्यते ॥ ३७ ॥

From the marrow of the bones, semen arose; from semen the embryo arose through an act rooted in vital essence. There water is the first part, called the lunar element.

BP 1.157.38

ततः क्ष्मासंभवो ज्ञेयो द्वितीयो राशिरुच्यते । शुक्रं सोमात्मकं विद्यादात्मरूपं यदात्मकम् ॥ ३८ ॥

Then the arising of earth is known as the second element. Semen is to be known as consisting of the Moon, of the nature of the self.

BP 1.157.39

भवो रसात्मकस्तेषां वीर्यं च शशिपावकम् । कफवर्गे भवेच्छुक्रं पित्तवर्गे च शोणितम् ॥ ३९ ॥

Their becoming is of the nature of vital essence, and their vigor is Moon and fire. Semen belongs to the phlegm class, and blood to the bile class.

BP 1.157.40

कफस्य पृथिवी स्थानं पित्तं नाभौ प्रतिष्ठितम् । देवस्य मध्यहृदयं स्थानं तु मनसः स्मृतम् । नाभिकोष्ठांतरस्थं तु तत्र देवो दिवाकरः ॥ ४० ॥

Earth is the place of phlegm; bile is established at the navel. The middle heart of the god is remembered as the place of mind; there, in the space between navel and belly, is Divākara.

BP 1.157.41

मनः प्रजापतिर्ज्ञेयः कफः सोमो विभाव्यते । पित्तमग्निः स्मृतो यस्मादग्नीषोमात्मकं जगत् ॥ ४१ ॥

Mind is to be known as the lord of creatures; phlegm is regarded as the Moon; bile is remembered as fire, since the world consists of Fire and Soma.

BP 1.157.42

एवं प्रवर्तिते गर्भे वर्धितेंऽबुदसन्निभे । वायुः प्रवेशं संचक्रे संगतः परमात्मना ॥ ४२ ॥

When the embryo thus began and grew, resembling a cloud, Wind entered, joined with the Supreme Self.

BP 1.157.43

ततोंऽगानि विसृजते बिभर्ति परिवर्तयन् । प्राणोऽपानः समानश्च उदानो व्यान एव च ॥ ४३ ॥

Then, turning, he sends forth and sustains the limbs: the breath, downward breath, equalizing breath, upward breath, and diffused breath.

BP 1.157.44

प्राणोस्य प्रथमं स्थानं वर्धयन्परिवर्तते । अपानं पश्चिमे काय उदानोर्ध्वं शरीरगः । व्यानोऽथ व्यापको देहे समानः सन्निवर्तते ॥ ४४ ॥

The breath moves, increasing, in its first place; downward breath is in the lower body, upward breath moves above, diffused breath spreads through the body, and equalizing breath remains.

BP 1.157.45

भूतावाप्तिस्ततस्तस्य जायतींद्रियगोचरा । पृथिवी वायुराकाश आपो ज्योतिश्च पंचमः ॥ ४५ ॥

Then the elements arise for him within the range of the senses: earth, wind, space, water, and light as the fifth.

BP 1.157.46

तस्येंद्रियाणि पिष्टानि स्वंस्वं योगं प्रचक्रमुः । पार्थिवं देहमाहुस्तु प्राणात्मानं च मारुतम् ॥ ४६ ॥

His senses, formed, began their respective functions. They call the body earthly and the life-self wind.

BP 1.157.47

निद्रा ह्याकाशयोनिश्च जलाश्रये प्रवर्तते । ज्योतिश्चक्षुषि तज्जन्म तदंशस्तामसः स्मृतः ॥ ४७ ॥

Sleep indeed has space as its source and proceeds resting on water; light is born in the eye, and its portion is remembered as dark.

BP 1.157.48

ग्रामाश्च विषयाश्चैव यस्य वीर्यं प्रवर्तितम् । एवं यः सृजते लोकान्सदेवासुरमानवान् ॥ ४८ ॥

Villages and sense-objects are set in motion by his power. Thus he creates the worlds with gods, demons, and humans.

BP 1.157.49

स कथं देवदेवेशो गर्भमेष्यति चांशुमान् । यथोदरमदित्यास्तु यः स्वयं चाविशत्पुरा ॥ ४९ ॥

How will the radiant Lord of gods enter a womb, he who himself formerly entered the belly of Aditi?

BP 1.157.50

एवं मे संशयो ब्रह्मन्नेष मे विस्मयो महान् । कथं रविर्ययौ गर्भभावं द्विजवरेति मे ॥ ५० ॥

Thus is my doubt, O Brahmin; this is my great wonder: how did Ravi enter the state of the womb, O best of Brahmins?

BP 1.157.51

आश्चर्यं परमं पृच्छे त्वामहं भास्करस्य वै । भानोरुत्पत्तिमाश्चर्यं हृदि मे परिवर्तते ॥ ५१ ॥

I ask you about the supreme wonder of Bhāskara; the wonder of Bhānu’s birth turns in my heart.

BP 1.157.52

एतदाश्चर्यमाख्यानं कथयस्व महामुने । समाख्याहि बलं वीर्य भानोरमिततेजसः ॥ ५२ ॥

Tell me this wondrous account, O great sage; explain the strength and might of Bhānu, of immeasurable splendor.
