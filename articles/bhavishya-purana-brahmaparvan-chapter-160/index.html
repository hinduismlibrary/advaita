Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 160 — Brahmā and Others See the Sun’s Form

BP 1.160.1

शतानीक उवाच ।। अहो देवस्य चरितं भास्करस्य त्वयोदितम् ।। ब्रह्मादयोऽपि यं नित्यं पूजयंति विधानतः ।। १ ।।

Śatānīka said: Ah, you have told the account of Bhāskara, whom Brahmā and the others always worship according to rule.

BP 1.160.2

ब्रह्मा विष्णुः सुरा ब्रह्मंस्तमाराध्य दिवाकरम् ।। ददृशुस्तस्य किंभूतं रूपं यत्तन्महाद्भुतम् ।। २ ।।

Brahmā, Viṣṇu, and the gods, O Brahmin, having worshipped Divākara, saw his form, what it was: exceedingly wondrous.

BP 1.160.3

सुमंतुरुवाच ।। आराध्य देवमीशानं भास्करं सूतिवाचकम् ।। कविष्णू कुरुशार्दूल जग्मतुस्तौ हिमाचलम् ।। ३ ।।

Sumantu said: Having worshipped the Lord, Īśāna, Bhāskara, the announcer of creation, Kavi and Viṣṇu, O tiger among the Kurus, those two went to the Himalaya.

BP 1.160.4

गोपतेरंतिकं वीर प्रहृष्टौ विभुदर्शने ।। कुंदेंदुसंनिभं द्रष्टुं कंजजश्चाच्युतश्च तौ ।। ४ ।।

The two, delighted by the sight of the god, went near Gopati, O hero, to see him, resembling jasmine and the Moon, the lotus-born and Acyuta.

BP 1.160.5

ददृशतुर्महात्मानं चंद्रार्धकृतशेखरम् ।। पूजयंतं विवस्वंतं भास्करं वीरवन्नृप ।। ५ ।।

They saw the great-souled one, wearing the crescent Moon as a crest, worshipping Vivasvat, Bhāskara, O king, in heroic fashion.

BP 1.160.6

आर्च्योचतुर्महात्मानं कविष्णू तं त्रिलोचनम् ।। भोभो भीम सुरश्रेष्ठ पश्यावामिह चागतौ ।। ६ ।।

Having worshipped the great-souled three-eyed one, Kavi and Viṣṇu said: Ho, Bhīma, best of the gods, behold, we two have come here.

BP 1.160.7

श्रुत्वोवाच तयोर्वाक्यं कंजजस्याच्युतस्य च ।। प्रणम्य शिरसा भूमौ कृत्वा पूजां विधानतः ।। ७ ।।

Having heard the words of the lotus-born and Acyuta, he bowed his head to the ground and performed worship according to rule.

BP 1.160.8

उवाच मधुरं वाक्यं शिक्षाक्षरसमन्वितम् ।। हर्ष गद्गदया वाचा दिशः सन्नादयन्निव ।। ८ ।।

He spoke sweet words, well-articulated, with a voice choked by joy, as if making the directions resound.

BP 1.160.9

किमाराध्य रविं प्राप्तौ सर्वदेववरं विभुम् ।। कथ्यतां निखिलं देवौ परमं कौतुकं मम ।। ९ ।।

What have you two gained by worshipping Ravi, the all-pervading best of all gods? Tell me all of it, O gods; I greatly wonder.

BP 1.160.10

दृष्टवंतौ परं किंचिद्रूपं देवस्य शंकरम् ।। अव्ययस्याप्रमेयस्य भानोरमिततेजसः ।। १० ।।

Did you two see any supreme form of the imperishable, immeasurable Bhānu of immeasurable splendor, the auspicious god?

BP 1.160.11

निशम्य वचनं वीर शंकरस्य महात्मनः ।। ऊचतुस्तौ महात्मानौ कविष्णू देवसत्तमौ ।। ११ ।।

Having heard the words of the great-souled Śaṅkara, those two great souls, Kavi and Viṣṇu, the best of the gods, spoke.

BP 1.160.12

न तत्पश्यावहे रूपं यत्तत्परममद्भुतम् ।। आराधयितुमेवापि ह्यागतौ तेंतिकं च तम् ।। १२ ।।

We did not see that supreme, wondrous form. We came near him indeed only to worship.

BP 1.160.13

तस्मादाराधयामो हि एकीभूय विभावसुम् ।। गत्वोदयगिरिं पुण्यं पर्वतं कनकोज्ज्वलम् ।। १३ ।।

Therefore let us worship Vibhāvasu together, having gone to the sacred mountain Udayagiri, bright with gold.

BP 1.160.14

श्रुत्वा तु वचनं वीर कंजजाच्युतयोर्हरः ।। तथेत्याह महाबाहो हर्षादुत्फुल्ललोचनः ।। १४ ।।

Having heard the words of the lotus-born and Acyuta, Hara said, So be it, O mighty-armed one, his eyes blossoming with joy.

BP 1.160.15

अथ ते राजशार्दूल विविगोगतयो नृप ।। जग्मुस्तं पर्वतश्रेष्ठमुदयाचलमाशु वै ।। १५ ।।

Then they, O tiger among kings, quickly went to that best of mountains, Udayācala.

BP 1.160.16

तमासाद्य नगं पुण्यं शृंगैस्त्रिभिरलंकृतम् ।। नानाधातुपिनद्धाङ्गं नानाधातुविभूषितम् ।। १६ ।।

Having reached that sacred mountain, adorned with three peaks, its body encrusted with various minerals and adorned with various minerals,

BP 1.160.17

आराधनाय विधिवद्यत्नं चक्रुर्विभावसोः ।। स्तुवन्तस्ते समर्चंतो ध्यायंतश्च विभावसुम् ।। १७ ।।

they strove according to rule in the worship of Vibhāvasu, praising him, making offerings to him, and meditating on him.

BP 1.160.18

दिव्यवर्षसहस्रांते तपंतः संस्थिता नगे ।। पद्मासनगतो ब्रह्मा ध्यायमानो दिवाकरम् ।। १८ ।।

At the end of a thousand divine years, they remained on the mountain in austerity; Brahmā, seated in the lotus posture, meditated on Divākara.

BP 1.160.19

स्थाणुवत्संस्थितो भूमावूर्ध्वबाहुस्त्रिलोचनः ।। पंचाग्निं भजमानस्तु स्थितो विष्णुरवाक्छिराः ।। १९ ।।

The three-eyed one stood on the ground like a post, with arms raised; Viṣṇu stood with head lowered, practicing the five fires.

BP 1.160.20

एवं वर्षसहस्रांते तपश्चक्रुः सुदारुणम् ।। आराधयंतो विधिवद्गोपतिं पुत्रमालिनम् ।। २० ।।

Thus, at the end of a thousand years, they performed very severe austerity, worshipping according to rule Gopati, garlanded with sons.

BP 1.160.21

अथ ब्रह्मेशविष्णूनां कुर्वतां तप उत्तमम् ।। तुतोष भगवान्भानुरुवाच च महीपते ।। २१ ।।

Then, while Brahmā, Īśa, and Viṣṇu were performing excellent austerity, the blessed Bhānu was pleased and spoke, O lord of the earth.

BP 1.160.22

ब्रह्मञ्छंभो हरे ब्रूत मत्तः किमभिवांछथ ।। तुष्टोहं भवतां दातु मिहायातो वरं स्वयम् ।। २२ ।।

O Brahmā, Śambhu, Hari, speak: what do you desire from me? I am pleased with you; I have come here myself to give a boon.

BP 1.160.23

सुमंतुरुवाच ।। निशम्य वचनं भानोः शांतं हृद्यं मनोरमम् ।। प्रणम्य शिरसा केशव इदं वचमब्रुवन् ।। २३ ।।

Sumantu said: Having heard Bhānu’s peaceful, heart-pleasing, delightful words, they bowed their heads, and Keśava spoke these words.

BP 1.160.24

कृतकृत्या वयं सर्वे प्रसादात्तव गोपते ।। त्वामाराध्य पुरा देव त्वत्तः प्राप्य वरं शुभम् ।। २४ ।।

We are all fulfilled by your favor, O Gopati. Having worshipped you formerly, O god, we obtained an auspicious boon from you.

BP 1.160.25

उत्पत्तिस्थितिनाशानां वयं सर्वे दिवाकर ।। संभूयेह समर्था वै त्वत्प्रसादान्न संशयः ।। २५ ।।

For creation, preservation, and destruction, we are all capable together here, O Divākara, through your favor; there is no doubt.

BP 1.160.26

किं त्वेकं देवदेवेश वरमिच्छामहे विभो ।। यत्ते परमकं रूपं दुर्लभं दुर्दृशं तथा ।। २६ ।।

But we desire one boon, O Lord of gods, all-pervading one: that supreme form of yours, difficult to attain and difficult to see.

BP 1.160.27

तस्मादस्माज्जगन्नाथ रूपं दर्शय तेऽच्युतम् ।। सर्वदेवमयं यत्ते यत्त्वयोक्तं पुरानघ ।। २७ ।।

Therefore, O Lord of the world, show us your imperishable form, consisting of all the gods, which you spoke of long ago, O sinless one.

BP 1.160.28

तेषां तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा ब्रह्मविष्ण्वीशभाषितम् ।। दर्शयामास तद्रूपमद्भुतं लोकपूजितम् ।। २८ ।।

Having heard their words, spoken by Brahmā, Viṣṇu, and Īśa, he showed that wondrous form, worshipped by the world.

BP 1.160.29

अनेकवक्त्रशिरसमनेकाद्भुतदर्शनम् ।। सर्वदेवमयं दिव्यं सर्वलोकमयं तथा ।। २९ ।।

Many-faced and many-headed, with many wondrous appearances, divine, consisting of all the gods and likewise of all the worlds.

BP 1.160.30

भूः पादौ द्यौः शिरश्चापि तत्राग्नी लोचने मते ।। पादांगुल्यः पिशाचाश्च हस्तांगुल्यश्च गुह्यकाः ।। ३० ।।

Earth was his feet and heaven his head; the fires were held to be his eyes. The toes were demons, and the fingers were nature spirits.

BP 1.160.31

विश्वे देवाः स्मृतास्तस्य जंघासंघाः सुरोत्तमाः ।। यक्षाः कुक्षिषु संलीना केशाश्चाप्सरसां गणाः ।। ३१ ।।

The collective gods were remembered as his thighs, O best of the gods; nature spirits were gathered in his belly, and hosts of celestial nymphs were his hair.

BP 1.160.32

दृष्टिधृष्ट्यश्च विपुलाः केशा वीरांशवः स्मृताः ।। तारका रोमरूपाणि रोमाणि च महर्षयः ।। ३२ ।।

His sights and glances were vast; his hair was remembered as the rays of heroes. The stars were the forms of his body hairs, and great seers were his hairs.

BP 1.160.33

बाहवो विदिशस्तस्य दिशः श्रोत्रे नराधिप ।। अश्विनौ श्रवणे चास्य नासा वायुर्महाबलः ।। ३३ ।।

His arms were the intermediate directions; the directions were his ears, O lord of men. The two Aśvins were his hearing, and mighty Vāyu was his nose.

BP 1.160.34

प्रसादश्च क्षमा चैव मनो धर्मस्तथैव च ।। सत्यमस्याभवद्वाणी जिह्वा देवी सरस्वती ।। ३४ ।।

Grace and forbearance were his; his mind was dharma itself. Truth was his speech, and the goddess Sarasvatī was his tongue.

BP 1.160.35

ग्रीवादितिर्महादेवी तालू रुद्रश्च वीर्यवान् ।। द्वारं स्वर्गस्य नाभिर्वै मित्रस्त्वष्टा पिचंडकः ।। ३५ ।।

The great goddess Aditi was his neck; mighty Rudra was his palate. His navel was the gate of heaven; Mitra, Tvaṣṭṛ, and Picaṇḍaka were there.

BP 1.160.36

मुखं वैश्वानरश्चास्य वृषणौ च भगस्तदा ।। हृदयं भगवान्ब्रह्मा ह्युदरं कश्यपो मुनिः ।। ३६ ।।

Vaiśvānara was his mouth, Bhaga his two testicles, the blessed Brahmā his heart, and the sage Kaśyapa his belly.

BP 1.160.37

पृष्ठेऽस्य वसवो देवा मरुतः सर्वसंधिषु ।। सर्वच्छंदांसि दशना ज्योतींषि विमला प्रभा ।। ३७ ।।

The gods called Vasus were on his back, the Maruts in all his joints; all the metres were his teeth, and the heavenly lights his pure radiance.

BP 1.160.38

प्राणो रुद्रो महादेवः कुक्षौ चास्य महार्णवाः ।। उदरे चास्य गंधर्वा भुजगाश्च महाबलाः ।। ३८ ।।

His breath was the great god Rudra; the great oceans were in his belly, and Gandharvas and mighty serpents were in his abdomen.

BP 1.160.39

लक्ष्मीर्मेधा धृतिः कांतिः सर्वा विद्याश्च वै कटौ ।। ललाटमस्य परमं वयःस्थानं परात्मनः ।। ३९ ।।

Lakṣmī, intelligence, steadfastness, radiance, and all forms of knowledge were at his hips; his forehead was the seat of life of the Supreme Self.

BP 1.160.40

सर्वज्योतींषि जानीहि तपश्चक्रश्च देवराट् ।। तदेतदादिदेवस्य तनौ ह्याहुर्महात्मनः ।। ४० ।।

Know that all the heavenly lights, the wheel of austerity, and the king of the gods are said to be in the body of the great-souled Primal God.

BP 1.160.41

स्तनौ कुक्षौ च वेदाश्च तेष्टौ चास्य मखाः स्मृताः ।। यष्टव्यपशुबंधाश्च द्विजानां वेष्टि तानि च ।। ४१ ।।

The Vedas were his breasts and belly, and his sacrificial rites are remembered there; the twice-born’s sacrificial animal bonds and enclosures were also in him.

BP 1.160.42

सर्वदेवमयं दृष्ट्वा रूपमर्कस्य ते नृप ।। ब्रह्मा हरो हरिर्देवाः परं विस्मयमागताः ।। ४२ ।।

Seeing the form of Arka, consisting of all the gods, O king, Brahmā, Hara, Hari, and the gods were greatly astonished.

BP 1.160.43

प्रणम्य शिरसा देवं वेपमाना धरां गताः ।। भयगद्गदया वाचा इदं वचनमब्रुवन् ।। ४३ ।।

Bowing their heads to the god, trembling, they fell to the ground; with voices choked by fear, they spoke these words.

BP 1.160.44

समीक्ष्य रूपं ते देव भीमं ज्वालासमाकुलम् ।। अनेकमुखबाहूरुचरणं चकिता वयम् ।। ४४ ।।

Seeing your fearsome form, O god, crowded with flames, with many faces, arms, thighs, and feet, we are frightened.

BP 1.160.45

दिग्ज्ञानं हृतमस्माकं तत्प्रसीद जगत्पते ।। उपसंहर विश्वात्मन्द्रष्टुं शक्ता न ते वयम् ।। ४५ ।।

Our knowledge of the directions has been taken away; therefore be gracious, O Lord of the world. Withdraw, O Self of the universe; we cannot look at you.

BP 1.160.46

इति तेषां वचः श्रुत्वा देवदेवो दिवाकरः ।। प्रसन्नो भगवानाह वचस्तान्प्रहसन्निदम् ।। ४६ ।।

Having heard their words, Divākara, the god of gods, was pleased; the blessed one spoke these words to them, smiling.

BP 1.160.47

आदित्य उवाच ।। यदेतत्परमं पुण्यमद्भुतं लोकभावनम् ।। दृष्टं भवद्भिर्देवेंद्रा मम सर्वजगन्मयम् ।। ४७ ।।

Āditya said: This supreme, sacred, wondrous, world-sustaining form seen by you, O lords of the gods, consists of all the worlds and is mine.

BP 1.160.48

एतन्मया प्रसन्नेन युष्माकं श्रेयसेऽनघाः ।। दर्शितं पूजितेनेह योगिनां यन्महालयम् ।। ४८ ।।

This great abode of the yogis, worshipped here, has been shown by me, pleased, for your welfare, O sinless ones.

BP 1.160.49

ब्रह्मेशाच्युता ऊचुः ।। एवमेतन्न संदेहो यथात्थ त्वं दिवस्पते ।। योगिनामपि देवेश दर्शनं ह्यस्य दुर्लभम् ।। ४९ ।।

Brahmā, Īśa, and Acyuta said: It is so indeed, without doubt, as you have said, O Lord of the Day. Even for yogis, O Lord of gods, the sight of this is difficult.

BP 1.160.50

त्वामाराध्य जगन्नाथं नाप्राप्यमिह विद्यते ।। तस्मात्पूज्यतमो लोके नान्यो देवेषु विद्यते ।। ५० ।।

Worshipping you, O Lord of the world, nothing here is unattainable; therefore no other among the gods is more worthy of worship in the world.

BP 1.160.51

एवमुक्त्वाऽदितेः पुत्रो जगामादर्शनं रविः ।। ब्रह्मादयोपि ते हर्षं प्रापुर्देवस्य दर्शनात् ।। ५१ ।।

Having spoken thus, Ravi, the son of Aditi, went out of sight. Brahmā and the others too rejoiced at seeing the god.

BP 1.160.52

एवं ब्रह्मादयो देवाः पूजयित्वा दिवाकरम् ।। गतास्ते परमां सिद्धिं गंधर्वा ऋषयस्तथा ।। ५२ ।।

Thus, having worshipped Divākara, the gods beginning with Brahmā, the celestial singers, and the sages attained the highest perfection.
