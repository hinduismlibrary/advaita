VP 4.18.1

ययातेश्चतुर्थपुत्रस्यानोःसभानलचक्षुः परमेषुसंज्ञास्त्रयः पुत्राः बभूवुः

VP 4.18.13

यस्य क्षेत्रे दीर्घतमसांगवङ्गकलिङ्गसुह्यपौण्ड्राख्यं बालेयं क्षत्रमजन्यत

They are called Baaleya Kshatriyaas, as they were born in Bali's land. Deerghatamas had all these people there - Anga, Vanga, Kalinga, Suhma and Poundra.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yasya kṣetre dīrghatamasāṃgavaṅgakaliṅgasuhyapauṇḍrākhyaṃ bāleyaṃ kṣatramajanyata

Śrīdhara

aṣṭādaśe’nujasyānovaṃśaḥ samanuvarṇitaḥ . prātilomyāt samutpatteryo’sau sūtatvāmāgataḥ . bale kṣetre bhāryāyāṃ jātatvād bāleyam ||१३||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अष्टादशेऽनुजस्यानोवंशः समनुवर्णितः । प्रातिलोम्यात् समुत्पत्तेर्योऽसौ सूतत्वामागतः । बले क्षेत्रे भार्यायां जातत्वाद् बालेयम् ॥१३॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

They are called Baaleya Kshatriyaas, as they were born in Bali's land. Deerghatamas had all these people there - Anga, Vanga, Kalinga, Suhma and Poundra.

VP 4.18.14

तन्नामसंततिसंज्ञाश्च पञ्चविषयाः बभूवुः

Five countries were named after them - these five children.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tannāmasaṃtatisaṃjñāśca pañcaviṣayāḥ babhūvuḥ

Śrīdhara

tannāmeti—teṣāmaṅgādīnāṃ nāmāni yāsāṃ santatīnāṃ tāsāṃ sañjñā yeṣāṃ te viṣayā deśā babhūvuḥ ||१४||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तन्नामेति—तेषामङ्गादीनां नामानि यासां सन्ततीनां तासां सञ्ज्ञा येषां ते विषया देशा बभूवुः ॥१४॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Five countries were named after them - these five children.

VP 4.18.18

यस्याजपुत्रो दशरथः शान्तां नाम कन्यामनपत्यस्य दुहितृत्वे युयोज

Dasharatha who was son of Aja, had a daughter by name Shaantaa. Romapaada did not have children, so he gave his daughter Shaantaa as adopted daughter of Romapaada.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yasyājaputro daśarathaḥ śāntāṃ nāma kanyāmanapatyasya duhitṛtve yuyoja

Śrīdhara

tataścitraratha iti—yasya putro romapādasañjño daśaratho jajñe, yasmai ajaputro daśarathaḥ svakanyāṃ duhitṛtve yuyoja dattavānity arthaḥ ||१८||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

ततश्चित्ररथ इति—यस्य पुत्रो रोमपादसञ्ज्ञो दशरथो जज्ञे, यस्मै अजपुत्रो दशरथः स्वकन्यां दुहितृत्वे युयोज दत्तवानित्य् अर्थः ॥१८॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Dasharatha who was son of Aja, had a daughter by name Shaantaa. Romapaada did not have children, so he gave his daughter Shaantaa as adopted daughter of Romapaada.

VP 4.18.23

जयद्रथो ब्रह्मक्षत्रान्तरालसंभूत्यां पत्न्यां विजयं नाम पुत्रमजीजनत्

Jayadratha's wife was born to a Brahmin and Kshatriya parents. In that wife, Jayadratha had a son by name Vijaya. Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4 Braahmana kanyaa and Kshatriya purusha - their son is called as Soota. Therefore, Vijaya and others are Sootaas.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

jayadratho brahmakṣatrāntarālasaṃbhūtyāṃ patnyāṃ vijayaṃ nāma putramajījanat

Śrīdhara

prātilomyena brahmakṣatrayorantarāle saṅkare sambhūtirjanma yasyāstasyāṃ sūtāyāmity arthaḥ . brāhmaṇyāṃ kṣatriyājjātaḥ sūtaḥ iti smṛteḥ, ato mātṛvadāvarṇasaṅkarā iti vacanād vijayaḥ sūta eva, ataśca karṇo’pi tadvaṃśyatvāt sūtatvena khyātaḥ ||२३||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

प्रातिलोम्येन ब्रह्मक्षत्रयोरन्तराले सङ्करे सम्भूतिर्जन्म यस्यास्तस्यां सूतायामित्य् अर्थः । ब्राह्मण्यां क्षत्रियाज्जातः सूतः इति स्मृतेः, अतो मातृवदावर्णसङ्करा इति वचनाद् विजयः सूत एव, अतश्च कर्णोऽपि तद्वंश्यत्वात् सूतत्वेन ख्यातः ॥२३॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Jayadratha's wife was born to a Brahmin and Kshatriya parents. In that wife, Jayadratha had a son by name Vijaya. Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4 Sri Engal Aalwaan’s Commentary | | | Braahmana kanyaa and Kshatriya purusha - their son is called as Soota. Therefore, Vijaya and others are Sootaas.
