VP 5.14.1

प्रदोषाग्रे कदाचित्तु रासासक्ते जनार्दने ।
त्रासयन्समदो गोष्ठमरिष्टस्समुपागमत्

Sri Paraasharar - In the Pradosha kaala, when Krishna was enjoying in sporting with the gopis in the Raasamandala, Arishta, a ferocious demon in the form of a bull, causing terror to everyone. He came near all the cows.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
प्रदोषाग्रे (pradoṣāgre) — at the beginning of evening twilight
कदाचित् तु (kadācit tu) — once indeed
रासासक्ते जनार्दने (rāsāsakte janārdane) — while Janārdana was absorbed in the Rāsa [dance]
त्रासयन् (trāsayan) — terrifying
समदः (samadaḥ) — arrogant
गोष्ठम् (goṣṭham) — the cowherd settlement
अरिष्टः समुपागमत् (ariṣṭaḥ samupāgamat) — Ariṣṭa came near
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

pradoṣāgre kadācittu rāsāsakte janārdane | trāsayansamado goṣṭhamariṣṭassamupāgamat

Śrīdhara

pradoṣāgra iti | pradoṣāgre sandhyāvasāne | ariṣṭo nāma vṛṣabhākir daityaḥ samado goṣṭhaṃ trasayan samupāgataḥ || 1 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

प्रदोषाग्र इति । प्रदोषाग्रे सन्ध्यावसाने । अरिष्टो नाम वृषभाकिर् दैत्यः समदो गोष्ठं त्रसयन् समुपागतः ॥१॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

No separate English commentary is supplied.

All the gopaas were extremely pleased, by this wonderful act of Krishna, and they started praising Krishna, just like, when Indra killed the demon Jambha, all the gods were so happy and they praised Indra. In the same way, all the gopaas were feeling so happy and joyous that they praised Krishna for this wonderful act of killing Arishta demon, who came in the form of a ferocious bull. This completes Chapter Fourteen. We just concluded Chapter 14 of Amsha 5. Where the killing of demon Arishta, who came in the form of a ferocious bull, was narrated by Sri Paraasharar. Kamsa instructs Akroora Now, Chapter Fifteen. All the leelaa cheshtitaas of Sri Krishna are summarized in the next two shlokaas.
