VP 1.9.1

इदं च शृणु मैत्रेय यत् पृष्टोऽहम् इह त्वया ।
श्री-सम्बद्धं मया ह्य् एतच् छ्रुतम् आसीन् मरीचितः

Parāśara said: Hear this also, O Maitreya, concerning Śrī, which I heard from Marīci in answer to your question.

Śrīdhara translation

Marīci is the father of Kāśyapa. From Marīci, this account was heard in the same way.

VP 1.9.2

दुर्वासाः शङ्करस्यांशश् चचार पृथिवीम् इमाम् ।
स ददर्श स्रजं द्वियाम् ऋषिर् विद्याधरी-करे

Durvāsas, a portion of Śaṅkara, wandered over this earth. The sage saw a divine garland in the hand of a Vidyādhara woman.

Śrīdhara translation

Durvāsas is described as observing the vow of madness; yogins may appear like inert, mad, or possessed beings. His being a portion of Śaṅkara explains his power to curse Śakra and his intolerance of offences. Santānaka is a celestial tree whose flowers made the forest fragrant and delightful.

VP 1.9.149

इति सकलविभूत्यवाप्तिहेतुः
स्तुतिर् इयम् इन्द्रमुखोद्गता हि लक्ष्म्याः ।
अनुदिनम् इह पठ्यते नृभिर् यैर्
वसति न तेषु कदाचिद् अप्यलक्ष्मीः

This hymn of Lakṣmī, uttered by Indra and the cause of attainment of every prosperity, is recited daily by men; Alakṣmī never dwells in their homes.

No commentary.
