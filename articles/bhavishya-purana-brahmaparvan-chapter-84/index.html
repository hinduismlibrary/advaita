Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 84 — The Gentle Day Observance

BP 1.84.1

ब्रह्मोवाच । नक्षत्रं रोहिणी वीर यदा वारे ऽस्य वै भवेत् । यात्य् असौ सौम्यतां वीर स सौम्यः परिकीर्तितः ॥ ॥

Brahmā said. O hero, when the star Rohiṇī occurs on this day, it becomes gentle, and that day is proclaimed the Gentle Day, O hero.

brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said;
nakṣatram — lunar mansion;
rohiṇī — Rohiṇī;
vīra — O hero;
yadā — when;
vāre — on the day;
asya — of this;
vai — indeed;
bhavet — occurs;
yāti — goes, becomes;
asau — this;
saumyatām — gentleness;
vīra — O hero;
saḥ — that;
saumyaḥ — Saumya;
parikīrtitaḥ — proclaimed.

BP 1.84.2

स्नानं दानं जपो होमः पितृ-देवादि-तर्पणम् । अक्षयं स्यान् न सन्देहस् त्व् अत्र वारे महात्मनः ॥ ॥

Bathing, giving, recitation, fire offering, and offerings that satisfy ancestors, gods, and others become imperishable on this day, without doubt, O great one.

snānam — bathing;
dānam — giving;
japaḥ — recitation;
homaḥ — fire offering;
pitṛ-deva-ādi-tarpaṇam — offerings to ancestors, gods, and others;
akṣayam — inexhaustible;
syāt — becomes;
na sandehaḥ — no doubt;
tu — indeed;
atra — here;
vāre — on the day;
mahātman — O great one.

BP 1.84.3

नक्तं समाश्रितो यो ऽत्र पूजयेद् भास्करं नरः । याति लोकं स देवस्य भास्करस्य न संशयः ॥ ॥

A person who undertakes the night observance here and worships the Sun goes to the world of the Sun, without doubt.

naktam — night observance;
samāśritaḥ — undertaken;
yaḥ — whoever;
atra — here;
pūjayet — should worship;
bhāskaram — Bhāskara;
naraḥ — person;
yāti — goes;
lokam — world;
saḥ — he;
devasya — of the god;
bhāskarasya — of Bhāskara;
na saṃśayaḥ — without doubt.

BP 1.84.4

रक्तोत्पलानि वै तत्र तथा रक्तं च चन्दनम् । सु-गन्धश् चापि धूपस् तु नैवेद्यं पायसं तथा । ब्राह्मणाय च दातव्यं भोक्तव्यं चात्मना तथा ॥ ॥

There, red lotuses and red sandalwood, fragrant incense, and rice pudding as a food offering are prescribed. It should also be given to a Brahmin and likewise eaten by oneself.

dātavyam — should be given;

BP 1.84.5

य एवं पूजयेत् सौम्ये चित्रभानुं गवां पतिम् । स विमुक्तः तु पापेभ्यः त्वाष्ट्रीं कान्तिम् अवाप्नुयात् ॥ ॥

Whoever worships the radiant Sun, Lord of Cows, in this way on the Gentle Day is freed from sins and attains the beauty of the daughter of Tvaṣṭṛ.

yaḥ — whoever;
evam — thus;
pūjayet — should worship;

tvāṣṭrīm — belonging to Tvaṣṭṛ’s daughter;
kāntim — radiance;
avāpnuyāt — may attain.
