VP 3.8.1

भगवन् भगवान् देवः संसारविजिगीषुभिः । समाख्याहि जगन्नाथो विष्णुर् आराध्यते यथा ॥१॥

O Lord. O divine Lord. Explain how Viṣṇu the Lord of the universe is worshipped by those who seek to overcome saṃsāra.

Śrīdhara translation

The means of worshipping Viṣṇu is presented in the dharma proper to a person. The dialogue between Sagara and Aurva is explained across nine chapters. It explains how Viṣṇu is worshipped through the various forms of dharma.

VP 3.8.9

वर्णाश्रमाचरवता पुरुषेण परः पुमान् । विष्णुर् आराध्यते पन्था नान्यत् तत्तोषकारणम् ॥९॥

The Supreme Person Viṣṇu is worshipped by a person who follows the conduct of varṇa and āśrama. No other path pleases Viṣṇu.

Śrīdhara translation

The description of a person as one who follows varṇa and āśrama conduct identifies who is eligible. Only a person whose conduct follows the Veda and the Purāṇas and Āgamas that do not conflict with it is eligible. A person of censured conduct is not eligible. Taking up vows and hearing or reciting while abandoning the dharma taught in Śruti is not a valid path.

VP 3.8.10

दिग्दर्शिनी वर्णाश्रमाचारः तद्वता पुरुषेण विष्णुर् आराध्यत न चान्यत् परः तस्य तोषकारणं यजन् यज्ञान् यजत्य् एनं जपत्य् एनं जपन् नृप । निघ्नन्न् अन्यान् हिनस्त्य् एनं सर्वभूतो यतो हरिः ॥१०॥

A person who offers sacrifices to Indra and the other deities worships Viṣṇu. A person who recites any mantra recites Viṣṇu. A person who harms another harms Viṣṇu because Hari is in all beings.

Śrīdhara translation

The sacrifices are those offered to Indra and the other deities. Offering them is worshipping Viṣṇu. Reciting any mantra is reciting Viṣṇu alone. Harming another is harming Viṣṇu alone.

Śrīdhara translation

A stone is called grāva. Pārakya means a jewel belonging to another person. Union with a wife during the fertile season is praised by Śruti but is not imposed as an exclusive rule. The commentary distinguishes a new injunction when an act is otherwise unavailable from a rule that selects one means when several are partly available and from parisaṅkhyā which excludes alternatives. Jyotiṣṭoma illustrates an injunction. Five clawed animals and the instruction to approach a wife during her fertile season illustrate parisaṅkhyā. These acts are already known from ordinary experience so the scriptural statement excludes competing alternatives. The quoted penalty for failing to approach a wife in season is not contradictory. It applies when desire exists but aversion keeps the man away. Śuka likewise says sexual union meat and wine are natural to living beings and are not generally enjoined.

इत्य् एते कथिता राजन् वर्णधर्मा मया तव । धर्मान् आश्रमिणां सम्यग् ब्रुवतो मे निशामय ॥४१॥

Thus I have told you the duties of the varṇas O king. Listen while I properly explain the duties of the āśramas.

Śrīdhara translation

After restating what was taught Aurva undertakes to explain the duties of the āśramas.
