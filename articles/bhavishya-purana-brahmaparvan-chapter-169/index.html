Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 169 — The Sun Vow and Chariot Offering

BP 1.169.1

सुमंतुरुवाच ।। मृन्मयं दारुजं शैलं पक्वेष्टकमथापि वा ।। कृत्वा मठं गृहं वापि यथा विभवसंभवात् ।। १ ।।

Sumantu said: One should make a monastery or house from clay, wood, stone, or baked brick, according to one’s means.

BP 1.169.2

सर्वोपकरणोपेतं सर्वधान्यसमन्वितम् ।। सूर्यायेत्थं गृहं दद्यात्सर्वान्कामानवाप्नुयात् ।। २ ।।

Equipped with every implement and supplied with every grain, one should give such a house to the Sun and obtain all desires.

BP 1.169.3

कृत्वैकभक्तं हेमन्ते माघमासमतन्द्रितः ।। मासान्तेन रथं कुर्याच्चित्रवस्त्रोपशोभितम् ।। ३ ।।

While observing a one-meal fast through winter and the month of Māgha, without negligence, one should make at the month’s end a chariot adorned with colorful garments.

BP 1.169.4

श्वेतैश्चतुर्भिः संयुक्तं तुरगैः समलंकृतम् ।। श्वेतध्वजपताकाभिश्छत्रचामरदर्पणैः ।। ४ ।।

Joined with four white horses, it should be adorned with white banners, pennants, parasols, fly-whisks, and mirrors.

BP 1.169.5

तण्डुलाढकपिष्टेन कृत्वा भानुं नराधिप ।। विन्यस्य तं रथोपस्थे संज्ञया सह भूपते ।। ५ ।।

Having fashioned the Sun from flour made of a measure of rice, O ruler of men, one should place him on the chariot’s platform together with Saṃjñā, O lord of the earth.

BP 1.169.6

तं रात्रौ राजमार्गेण शङ्खभेर्यादिनिस्वनैः ।। भ्रमयित्वा शनैः पश्चात्सूर्यायतनमाविशेत् ।। ६ ।।

At night, having led it along the royal road to the sounds of conches, drums, and the like, one should then slowly enter the Sun’s shrine.

BP 1.169.7

तत्र जागरपूजाभिः प्रदीपावलिशोभितैः ।। प्रेक्षणीयैः प्रदानैश्च क्षपयित्वा शनैः क्षपाम् ।। ७ ।।

There, with vigils and worship, rows of lamps, and splendid offerings, one should slowly pass the night.

BP 1.169.8

प्रभाते स्नपनं कृत्वा मधुक्षीरघृतेन च ।। दीनान्धकृपणेभ्योऽन्नं यथाशक्त्या च दक्षिणाम् ।। ८ ।।

At dawn, having bathed with honey, milk, and ghee, one should give food to the poor, blind, and helpless, and a gift according to one’s means.

BP 1.169.9

रथं संवाहनोपेतं भास्कराय निवेदयेत् ।। भुक्त्वा च बान्धवैः सार्धं प्रणम्यार्कगृहं व्रजेत् ।। ९ ।।

One should present to the Sun a fully equipped chariot; after eating with one’s relatives and bowing, one should go to the Sun’s shrine.

BP 1.169.10

सर्वव्रतानां प्रवरं मन्त्रधर्मान्वितः सदा ।। व्रतं सूर्यव्रतं नाम सर्वकामार्थसिद्धये ।। १० ।।

This vow, named the Sun-vow, always joined with mantra and dharma, is foremost among all vows for accomplishing all desires and aims.

BP 1.169.11

सर्वव्रतेषु यत्पुण्यं सर्वयज्ञेषु यत्फलम् ।। सर्वं सूर्यरथेनेह तत्पुण्यं लभते नृप ।। ११ ।।

Whatever merit there is in all vows, whatever fruit in all sacrifices, all that merit one gains here through the Sun’s chariot, O king.

BP 1.169.12

सूर्यायुतप्रतीकाशैर्विमानैः सार्वकामिकैः ।। त्रिसप्तकुलजैः सार्धं सूर्यलोके महीयते ।। १२ ।।

Accompanied by vehicles resembling ten thousand Suns and granting every desire, one is honored in the Sun’s world together with those born in twenty-one families.

BP 1.169.13

भुक्त्वा तु विपुलान्भोगान्सर्वलोकेष्वनुक्रमात् ।। कल्पायुतशतं साग्रं ततो राजा भवेत्क्षितौ ।। १३ ।।

Having enjoyed abundant pleasures successively in all worlds for a full million cosmic cycles, thereafter one becomes a king on earth.

BP 1.169.14

पञ्चबलिसमायुक्तं मृदुषड्वास्तुकल्पितम् ।। सर्वोपकरणोपेतं सूर्यं संज्ञां प्रकल्पयेत् ।। १४ ।।

Joined with five offerings, arranged in a soft sixfold design and equipped with every implement, one should fashion the Sun and Saṃjñā.

BP 1.169.15

संज्ञादेवीसमायुक्तं पैष्टांशाढ्यं निवेदयेत् ।। सौरज्ञानार्थतत्त्वज्ञमाचार्यमुदयान्वितम् ।। १५ ।।

One should offer the flour-rich food, joined with goddess Saṃjñā, to a teacher who knows the truth and meaning of solar knowledge and is endowed with prosperity.

BP 1.169.16

सम्पूज्य गन्धपुष्पाद्यैर्वस्त्रालङ्कारचामरैः ।। भक्ष्यभोज्यैरशेषैश्च ततः शय्यां निवेदयेत् ।। १६ ।।

Having worshipped fully with fragrances, flowers, and the like, garments, ornaments, fly-whisks, and all eatables and foods, one should then offer a bed.

BP 1.169.17

तदूर्णातूलवस्त्राणां परिसङ्ख्या तु यावती ।। तावद्वर्षसहस्राणि सूर्यलोके महीयते ।। १७ ।।

For as many threads as there are in the wool and cotton garments, for that many thousands of years one is honored in the Sun’s world.

BP 1.169.18

सुरादिसर्वलोकेषु भुक्त्वा भोगानशेषतः ।। कामादागत्य लोकेऽस्मिन्राजा भवति धार्मिकः ।। १८ ।।

After enjoying all pleasures in all the worlds beginning with those of the gods, returning to this world through desire, one becomes a righteous king.

BP 1.169.19

दश गोभिः सह वृषं ता वृषैकादशाः स्मृताः ।। सूर्याय विनिवेद्येह यत्फलं लभते शृणु ।। १९ ।।

Hear the fruit obtained here by offering ten cows and a bull to the Sun; together with the bull, they are counted as eleven cattle.

BP 1.169.20

द्वादशादित्यतुल्यात्मा अणिमादिगुणैर्युतः ।। सर्वत्र मोदते राजन्सूर्यस्यानुचरो भवेत् ।। २० ।।

Equal in essence to the twelve Suns and endowed with powers beginning with becoming minute, one rejoices everywhere, O king, and becomes an attendant of the Sun.
