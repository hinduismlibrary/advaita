Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 70 — The Seventh-Day Observance and the Fruits of Sun Worship

BP 1.70.1

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
ततो मध्याह्नसमये स्नातः प्रयतमानसः ।
तथैव देवान्विधिवत्पूजयित्वा यथासुखम् ॥ ॥

Brahmā said. Then, at midday, having bathed with a disciplined mind and having duly worshipped the gods, he should do as is fitting.

BP 1.70.2

सम्यक्कृतजपो मौनी नरो हुतहुताशनः ।
निष्क्रम्य देवायतनाद्भोजकान्भोजयेत्ततः ॥ ॥

Having completed the recitation properly, remained silent, and made offerings into the fire, a man should leave the temple and then feed the Sun-priests.

BP 1.70.3

तथा पुराणविदुष इतिहासविदो द्विजान् ।
तथा वेदविदश्चैव दिव्यांभौमांश्च सुव्रत ॥ ॥

Likewise, he should feed the twice-born learned in the Purāṇas and histories, and those learned in the Vedas, both celestial and earthly, O good-vowed one.

BP 1.70.4

रक्तानि वस्त्राणि तथा च गावः सुगन्धमाल्यादि हविष्यमन्नम् ।
पयस्विनी चाप्यथ भोजकाय देया तथान्यत्प्रियमात्मनो यत् ॥ ॥

Red garments, cows, fragrant garlands and the like, sacrificial food, and a milk-bearing cow should be given to the Sun-priest, as well as whatever else is dear to oneself.

BP 1.70.5

भवेदलाभो यदि भोजकानां विप्रास्तदार्हन्ति जयॊपजीविनः ।
ये मन्त्रविद्ब्राह्मणपाठकाश्च ये येऽपि सामाध्ययनेषु युक्ताः ॥ ॥

If Sun-priests are unavailable, Brahmins who live by reciting the Jaya are worthy of the offering, as are those who know mantras, reciters of the Brahmins, and those engaged in studying the Vedic chant.

BP 1.70.6

प्रथमं भोजका भोज्याः पुराणविदुषैः सह ।
तेषामृते मन्त्रविदस्तथा वेदविदो द्विजाः ॥ ॥

First the Sun-priests should be fed together with those learned in the Purāṇas. In their absence, twice-born men who know mantras and those who know the Vedas should be fed.

BP 1.70.7

कृत्वैवं सप्तमीः सप्त नरो भक्त्या समन्वितः ।
श्रद्दधानोऽनसूयश्च अनन्तं प्राप्नुयात्सुखम् ॥ ॥

Having performed seven observances of the seventh lunar day in this way, a person endowed with devotion, faithful and free from envy, obtains endless happiness.

BP 1.70.8

दशानामश्वमेधानां कृतानां यत्फलं लभेत् ।
तत्फलं सप्तमीः सप्त कृत्वा प्राप्नोति मानवः ॥ ॥

The fruit obtained from performing ten horse sacrifices is obtained by a person who performs seven observances of the seventh lunar day.

BP 1.70.9

दुष्प्रापं नास्ति तल्लोके अनया यन्न लभ्यते ।
न च रोगोऽस्त्यसौ लोकेऽनया यो न शाम्यति ॥ ॥

Nothing difficult to obtain in that world is unattainable through this, and no disease in the world exists that is not pacified by it.

BP 1.70.10

कुष्ठानि चापि सर्वाणि दुरुच्छेद्यान्यपि ध्रुवम् ।
अपयान्ति यथा नागा गरुडस्य भयार्दिताः ॥ ॥

All forms of leprosy, even those difficult to eradicate, certainly depart like serpents stricken with fear of Garuḍa.

BP 1.70.11

व्रतनियमतपोभिः सप्तमीः सप्तैवं विधिवदिह हि कृत्वा मानवो धर्मशीलः ।
श्रुतधनसुतभाग्यारोग्यपुण्यैरुपेतो व्रजति तदनुलोकं शाश्वतं तिग्मरश्मेः ॥ ॥

A righteous person who properly performs seven observances of the seventh lunar day here through vows, rules, and austerities, endowed with learning, wealth, sons, fortune, health, and merit, goes to the eternal world of the Fierce-Rayed One.

BP 1.70.12

इमं विधिं द्विजश्रेष्ठ श्रुत्वा कृत्वा च मानवः ।
सहस्ररश्मिं स विशेषान्नात्र कार्या विचारणा ॥ ॥

Having heard and performed this rite, O best of the twice-born, a man should especially worship the Thousand-Rayed One. No question should be raised about this.

BP 1.70.13

सुरैर्वा मुनिभिर्वापि पुराणज्ञैरिदं श्रुतम् ।
सर्वे ते परमात्मानं पूजयन्ति दिवाकरम् ॥ ॥

This was heard formerly by gods, sages, and knowers of the Purāṇas. All of them worship the Supreme Self, the Sun.

BP 1.70.14

इदमाख्यानमार्षेयं यन्मयाभिहितं तव ।
सूर्यभक्ताय दातव्यं नेतरााय कदाचन ॥ ॥

This account of the seers, which I have told you, should be given to a devotee of the Sun, never to another.

BP 1.70.15

यश्चैतच्छ्रावयेन्नित्यं यश्चैतच्छृणुयन्नरः ।
स सहस्रार्चिषं देवं प्रविशेन्नात्र संशयः ॥ ॥

Whoever has this recited daily and whoever hears it enters the god with a thousand rays. There is no doubt about this.

BP 1.70.16

मुच्येदार्तस्तथा रोगाच्छ्रुत्वेमामादितः कथाम् ।
जिज्ञासुर्लभते कामान्भक्तः सूर्यगतिं लभेत् ॥ ॥

An afflicted person is freed from illness by hearing this account from the beginning. The seeker of knowledge obtains his desires, and the devotee attains the path of the Sun.

BP 1.70.17

क्षेमेण गच्छतेऽध्वानं यस्त्विदं पठतेऽध्वनि ।
यो यं प्रार्थयते कामं स तं प्राप्नोति च ध्रुवम् ॥ ॥

Whoever recites this on the road travels safely. Whatever desire a person prays for, he surely obtains it.

BP 1.70.18

एकान्तभावोपगत एकान्ते सुसमाहितः ।
प्राप्यैतत्परमं गुह्यं भूत्वा सूर्यव्रतो नरः ॥ ॥

A man becomes a Sun-votary after entering solitude, being well concentrated there, and obtaining this supreme secret.

BP 1.70.19

प्राप्नोति परमं स्थानं भास्करस्य महात्मनः ।
लग्नगर्भा प्रमुच्येत गर्भिणी जनयेत्सुतम् ॥ ॥

He attains the supreme station of the great-souled Sun. A woman with an obstructed pregnancy is delivered, and a pregnant woman gives birth to a son.

BP 1.70.20

वन्ध्या प्रसवमाप्नोति पुत्रपौत्रसमन्विता ।
एवमेतन्ममाख्यातं भास्करेणामितौजसा ।
मयापि तव माख्यातं भक्त्या भानोः इदं द्विज ॥ ॥

A barren woman obtains childbirth and is endowed with sons and grandsons. Thus this was told to me by the Sun of immeasurable radiance, and I too have told this to you, O Brahmin, out of devotion to the Sun.

BP 1.70.21

पूजनीयस्त्वया भानुः सर्वपापोपशान्तये ।
स हि धाता विधाता च सर्वस्य जगतो गुरुः ॥ ॥

The Sun should be worshipped by you for the pacification of all sins. He is indeed the sustainer, ordainer, and teacher of the whole world.

BP 1.70.22

उद्यन्न्यः कुरुते नित्यं जगद्वितिमिरं करैः ।
द्वादशात्मा स देवेशः प्रीयतां तेऽदितेः सुतः ॥ ॥

May the son of Aditi, the Lord of gods with twelve forms, who rises and always makes the world free from darkness with his rays, be pleased with you.
