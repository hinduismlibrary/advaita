Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 70 — Sarṣapa-saptamī and the Fruits of Solar Worship

BP 1.70.1

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
ततो मध्याह्नसमये स्नातः प्रयतमानसः ।
तथैव देवान्विधिवत्पूजयित्वा यथासुखम् ॥ ॥

Brahmā said: Then, at midday, having bathed with a disciplined mind, he should likewise worship the gods properly, according to his means.

Word-for-word
brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said; tataḥ — then; madhyāhna-samaye — at midday; snātaḥ — having bathed; prayata-mānasaḥ — with disciplined mind; tathā eva — likewise; devān — the gods; vidhivat — properly; pūjayitvā — having worshiped; yathā-sukham — according to means
Transliteration
brahmovāca |
tato madhyāhnasamaye snātaḥ prayatamānasaḥ |
tathaiva devān vidhivat pūjayitvā yathāsukham || 1 ||

BP 1.70.2

सम्यक्कृतजपो मौनी नरो हुतहुताशनः ।
निष्क्रम्य देवायतनाद्भोजकान्भोजयेत्ततः ॥ ॥

Having properly completed his recitation, remaining silent, and offered into the fire, the person should leave the temple of the Deity and then feed the Bhojakas.

Word-for-word
samyak-kṛta-japaḥ — having properly performed recitation; maunī — silent; naraḥ — person; huta-hutāśanaḥ — having offered into the fire; niṣkramya — having left; deva-āyatanāt — from the temple of the Deity; bhojakān — the Bhojakas; bhojayet — should feed; tataḥ — then
Transliteration
samyakkṛtajapo maunī naro hutahutāśanaḥ |
niṣkramya devāyatanād bhojakān bhojayet tataḥ || 2 ||

BP 1.70.3

तथा पुराणविदुष इतिहासविदो द्विजान् ।
तथा वेदविदश्चैव दिव्यांभौमांश्च सुव्रत ॥ ॥

Likewise, [he should feed] twice-born men learned in the Purāṇas and histories, and also those learned in the Vedas, O faithful one—both the divine and earthly [reciters].

Word-for-word
tathā — likewise; purāṇa-viduṣaḥ — learned in Purāṇas; itihāsa-vidaḥ — learned in histories; dvijān — twice-born; tathā — likewise; veda-vidaḥ — learned in the Vedas; ca eva — and indeed; divyān — divine; bhaumān — earthly; ca — and; suvrata — O faithful one
Transliteration
tathā purāṇaviduṣo ’tihāsavido dvijān |
tathā vedavidaś caiva divyān bhaumāṃś ca suvrata || 3 ||

BP 1.70.4

रक्तानि वस्त्राणि तथा च गावः सुगन्धमाल्यादि हविष्यमन्नम् ।
पयस्विनी चाप्यथ भोजकाय देया तथान्यत्प्रियमात्मनो यत् ॥ ॥

Red garments, cows, fragrant garlands and the like, sacrificial food, and a milk-giving cow should be given to the Bhojaka, as well as whatever else is dear to oneself.

Word-for-word
raktāni vastrāṇi — red garments; tathā — likewise; gāvaḥ — cows; sugandha-mālya-ādi — fragrant garlands and the rest; haviṣya-annam — sacrificial food; payasvinī — milk-giving cow; ca api — and also; bhojakāya deyā — should be given to the Bhojaka; tathā anyat — likewise other; priyam ātmanaḥ — dear to oneself
Transliteration
raktāni vastrāṇi tathā ca gāvaḥ sugandhamālyādi haviṣyam annam |
payasvinī cāpy atha bhojakāya deyā tathānyat priyam ātmano yat || 4 ||

BP 1.70.5

भवेदलाभो यदि भोजकानां विप्रास्तदार्हन्ति जयॊपजीविनः ।
ये मन्त्रविद्ब्राह्मणपाठकाश्च ये येऽपि सामाध्ययनेषु युक्ताः ॥ ॥

If Bhojakas are unavailable, Brāhmaṇas who are worthy and live by victory should receive [the offering], as should those who know mantras, Brāhmaṇa reciters, and those engaged in studying the Sāman.

Word-for-word
bhavet alābhaḥ — there should be unavailability; yadi — if; bhojakānām — of Bhojakas; viprāḥ — Brāhmaṇas; tat arhanti — they deserve that; jaya-upajīvinaḥ — living by victory; mantra-vidaḥ — knowing mantras; brāhmaṇa-pāṭhakāḥ — Brāhmaṇa reciters; ye ye — whoever; sāma-adhyayaneṣu yuktāḥ — engaged in studying Sāman
Transliteration
bhaved alābho yadi bhojakānāṃ viprās tad arhanti jayopajīvinaḥ |
ye mantravid brāhmaṇapāṭhakāś ca ye ye ’pi sāmādhyayaneṣu yuktāḥ || 5 ||

BP 1.70.6

प्रथमं भोजका भोज्याः पुराणविदुषैः सह ।
तेषामृते मन्त्रविदस्तथा वेदविदो द्विजाः ॥ ॥

First the Bhojakas should be fed together with those learned in the Purāṇas; in their absence, twice-born men who know mantras and likewise those who know the Vedas [should be fed].

Word-for-word
prathamam — first; bhojakāḥ — Bhojakas; bhojyāḥ — should be fed; purāṇa-viduṣaiḥ saha — together with those learned in Purāṇas; teṣām ṛte — in their absence; mantra-vidaḥ — those knowing mantras; tathā — likewise; veda-vidaḥ — those knowing Vedas; dvijāḥ — twice-born
Transliteration
prathamaṃ bhojakā bhojyāḥ purāṇaviduṣaiḥ saha |
teṣām ṛte mantravid as tathā vedavido dvijāḥ || 6 ||

BP 1.70.7

कृत्वैवं सप्तमीः सप्त नरो भक्त्या समन्वितः ।
श्रद्दधानोऽनसूयश्च अनन्तं प्राप्नुयात्सुखम् ॥ ॥

A person who performs seven Saptamīs in this way, endowed with devotion, faithful and free from envy, obtains endless happiness.

Word-for-word
kṛtvā — having performed; evam — thus; saptamīḥ sapta — seven Saptamīs; naraḥ — a person; bhaktyā samanvitaḥ — endowed with devotion; śraddadhānaḥ — faithful; anasūyaḥ — free from envy; anantam sukham — endless happiness; prāpnuyāt — obtains
Transliteration
kṛtvaivaṃ saptamīḥ sapta naro bhaktyā samanvitaḥ |
śraddadhāno ’nasūyaś cānantam prāpnuyāt sukham || 7 ||

BP 1.70.8

दशानामश्वमेधानां कृतानां यत्फलं लभेत् ।
तत्फलं सप्तमीः सप्त कृत्वा प्राप्नोति मानवः ॥ ॥

The fruit obtained by performing ten Aśvamedhas is obtained by a person who performs seven Saptamīs.

Word-for-word
daśānām — of ten; aśvamedhānām — Aśvamedhas; kṛtānām — performed; yat phalam — whatever fruit; labhet — one obtains; tat phalam — that fruit; saptamīḥ sapta kṛtvā — having performed seven Saptamīs; prāpnoti — obtains; mānavaḥ — a person
Transliteration
daśānām aśvamedhānāṃ kṛtānāṃ yat phalaṃ labhet |
tat phalaṃ saptamīḥ sapta kṛtvā prāpnoti mānavaḥ || 8 ||

BP 1.70.9

दुष्प्रापं नास्ति तल्लोके अनया यन्न लभ्यते ।
न च रोगोऽस्त्यसौ लोकेऽनया यो न शाम्यति ॥ ॥

There is nothing difficult to obtain in the world that is not obtained through this; nor is there a disease in the world that is not quieted by it.

Word-for-word
duṣprāpam — difficult to obtain; na asti — there is not; tat loke — that in the world; anayā — by this; yat na labhyate — which is not obtained; na ca — nor; rogaḥ — disease; asti — exists; asau loke — that in the world; anayā — by this; yaḥ na śāmyati — which is not quieted
Transliteration
duṣprāpaṃ nāsti tal loke anayā yan na labhyate |
na ca rogo ’sty asau loke ’nayā yo na śāmyati || 9 ||

BP 1.70.10

कुष्ठानि चापि सर्वाणि दुरुच्छेद्यान्यपि ध्रुवम् ।
अपयान्ति यथा नागा गरुडस्य भयार्दिताः ॥ ॥

All forms of leprosy, even those difficult to remove, certainly depart, just as serpents flee in fear of Garuḍa.

Word-for-word
kuṣṭhāni — forms of leprosy; ca api — and also; sarvāṇi — all; dur-ucchedyāni — difficult to remove; api — even; dhruvam — certainly; apayānti — depart; yathā — as; nāgāḥ — serpents; garuḍasya — of Garuḍa; bhaya-arditāḥ — afflicted by fear
Transliteration
kuṣṭhāni cāpi sarvāṇi durucchadyāny api dhruvam |
apayānti yathā nāgā garuḍasya bhayārditāḥ || 10 ||

BP 1.70.11

व्रतनियमतपोभिः सप्तमीः सप्तैवं विधिवदिह हि कृत्वा मानवो धर्मशीलः ।
श्रुतधनसुतभाग्यारोग्यपुण्यैरुपेतो व्रजति तदनुलोकं शाश्वतं तिग्मरश्मेः ॥ ॥

A righteous person who properly performs seven Saptamīs here through vows, observances, and austerities, endowed with learning, wealth, sons, fortune, health, and merit, goes to the eternal world of the Fierce-Rayed One.

Word-for-word
vrata-niyama-tapobhiḥ — through vows, observances, and austerities; saptamīḥ sapta — seven Saptamīs; evam — thus; vidhivat — properly; kṛtvā — having performed; mānavaḥ — person; dharma-śīlaḥ — righteous; śruta — learning; dhana — wealth; suta — sons; bhāgya — fortune; ārogya — health; puṇyaiḥ — merits; upetaḥ — endowed; vrajati — goes; tat-anu-lokam — that world; śāśvatam — eternal; tigma-raśmeḥ — of the Fierce-Rayed One
Transliteration
vrataniyamatapobhiḥ saptamīḥ saptaivaṃ vidhivad iha hi kṛtvā mānavo dharmaśīlaḥ |
śrutadhanasutabhāgyārogyapuṇyair upeto vrajati tad anulokaṃ śāśvataṃ tigmaraśmeḥ || 11 ||

BP 1.70.12

इमं विधिं द्विजश्रेष्ठ श्रुत्वा कृत्वा च मानवः ।
सहस्ररश्मिं स विशेषान्नात्र कार्या विचारणा ॥ ॥

Having heard and performed this rite, O best of twice-born, a person especially worships the Thousand-Rayed One; there should be no doubt here.

Word-for-word
imam vidhim — this rite; dvija-śreṣṭha — O best of twice-born; śrutvā — having heard; kṛtvā — having performed; ca — and; mānavaḥ — a person; sahasra-raśmim — Thousand-Rayed One; saḥ — he; viśeṣāt — especially; na atra kāryā vicāraṇā — doubt should not be made here
Transliteration
imaṃ vidhiṃ dvijaśreṣṭha śrutvā kṛtvā ca mānavaḥ |
sahasraraśmiṃ sa viśeṣān nātra kāryā vicāraṇā || 12 ||

BP 1.70.13

सुरैर्वा मुनिभिर्वापि पुराणज्ञैरिदं श्रुतम् ।
सर्वे ते परमात्मानं पूजयन्ति दिवाकरम् ॥ ॥

This has been heard by gods, sages, and those who know the Purāṇas; all of them worship Divākara, the Supreme Self.

Word-for-word
suraiḥ — by gods; vā — or; munibhiḥ — by sages; vā api — or also; purāṇa-jñaiḥ — by knowers of Purāṇas; idam — this; śrutam — heard; sarve te — all of them; paramātmānam — Supreme Self; pūjayanti — worship; divākaram — Divākara
Transliteration
surair vā munibhir vāpi purāṇajñair idaṃ śrutam |
sarve te paramātmānaṃ pūjayanti divākaram || 13 ||

BP 1.70.14

इदमाख्यानमार्षेयं यन्मयाभिहितं तव ।
सूर्यभक्ताय दातव्यं नेतरााय कदाचन ॥ ॥

This is a ṛṣi-born account that I have told you; it should be given to a devotee of the Sun, never to another.

Word-for-word
idam ākhyānam — this account; ārṣeyam — born of sages; yat — which; mayā abhihitam — spoken by me; tava — to you; sūrya-bhaktāya — to a devotee of the Sun; dātavyam — should be given; na itarāya — not to another; kadācana — ever
Transliteration
idam ākhyānam ārṣeyaṃ yan mayābhihitaṃ tava |
sūryabhaktāya dātavyaṃ netarāya kadācana || 14 ||

BP 1.70.15

यश्चैतच्छ्रावयेन्नित्यं यश्चैतच्छृणुयन्नरः ।
स सहस्रार्चिषं देवं प्रविशेन्नात्र संशयः ॥ ॥

Whoever causes this to be heard daily, and whoever hears it, enters the Thousand-Rayed Deity; of this there is no doubt.

Word-for-word
yaḥ — who; etat śrāvayet — causes this to be heard; nityam — daily; yaḥ — who; etat śṛṇuyāt — hears this; naraḥ — person; saḥ — he; sahasra-arciṣam devam — Thousand-Rayed Deity; praviśet — enters; na atra saṃśayaḥ — there is no doubt
Transliteration
yaś caitac chrāvayen nityaṃ yaś caitac chṛṇuyān naraḥ |
sa sahasrārciṣaṃ devaṃ praviśen nātra saṃśayaḥ || 15 ||

BP 1.70.16

मुच्येदार्तस्तथा रोगाच्छ्रुत्वेमामादितः कथाम् ।
जिज्ञासुर्लभते कामान्भक्तः सूर्यगतिं लभेत् ॥ ॥

The afflicted is freed from disease by hearing this account from the beginning; the inquisitive obtains desires, and the devotee obtains the path of the Sun.

Word-for-word
mucyeta — is freed; ārtaḥ — afflicted person; tathā — likewise; rogāt — from disease; śrutvā — having heard; imām kathām — this account; āditaḥ — from the beginning; jijñāsuḥ — inquisitive person; labhate — obtains; kāmān — desires; bhaktaḥ — devotee; sūrya-gatim — path of the Sun; labhet — obtains
Transliteration
mucyed ārtaḥ tathā rogāc chrutv emām āditaḥ kathām |
jijñāsur labhate kāmān bhaktaḥ sūryagatiṃ labhet || 16 ||

BP 1.70.17

क्षेमेण गच्छतेऽध्वानं यस्त्विदं पठतेऽध्वनि ।
यो यं प्रार्थयते कामं स तं प्राप्नोति च ध्रुवम् ॥ ॥

Whoever recites this while on a journey travels safely. Whatever desire a person prays for, he surely obtains that desire.

Word-for-word

Transliteration

yo yaṃ prārthayate kāmaṃ sa taṃ prāpnoti ca dhruvam || 17 ||

BP 1.70.18

एकान्तभावोपगत एकान्ते सुसमाहितः ।
प्राप्यैतत्परमं गुह्यं भूत्वा सूर्यव्रतो नरः ॥ ॥

BP 1.70.19

प्राप्नोति परमं स्थानं भास्करस्य महात्मनः ।
लग्नगर्भा प्रमुच्येत गर्भिणी जनयेत्सुतम् ॥ ॥

BP 1.70.20

वन्ध्या प्रसवमाप्नोति पुत्रपौत्रसमन्विता ।
एवमेतन्ममाख्यातं भास्करेणामितौजसा ।
मयापि तव माख्यातं भक्त्या भानोः इदं द्विज ॥ ॥

A barren woman obtains childbirth and is endowed with sons and grandsons. Thus this was told to me by Bhāskara of immeasurable splendor; I too have told it to you, O Brāhmaṇa, out of devotion to Bhānu.

BP 1.70.21

पूजनीयस्त्वया भानुः सर्वपापोपशान्तये ।

BP 1.70.22

उद्यन्न्यः कुरुते नित्यं जगद्वितिमिरं करैः ।
द्वादशात्मा स देवेशः प्रीयतां तेऽदितेः सुतः ॥ ॥
