Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 21 — Gaurī’s Vow and the Imperishable Third Lunar Day

BP 1.21.1

सुमन्तरुवाच
पतिव्रता पतिप्राणा पतिशुश्रूषणे रता ।
एवंविधापि या प्रोक्ता शुचिः संशोभना सती ॥

Sumantu said: A chaste wife, devoted to her husband as to her life, delighting in serving him, is described as pure, radiant, and faithful.

BP 1.21.2

सोपवासा तृतीयां तु लवणं परिवर्जयेत् ।
सा गृह्णाति च वै भक्त्या व्रतमामरणान्तिकम् ॥

She should fast on the third lunar day and abstain from salt. With devotion she undertakes this vow until the end of her life.

BP 1.21.3

गौरी ददाति सन्तुष्टा रूपं सौभाग्यमेव च ।
लावण्यं ललितं हृद्यं श्लाघ्यं पुंसां मनोरमम् ॥

When pleased, Gaurī grants beauty and good fortune, grace, charm, loveliness, praiseworthiness, and delightfulness to men.

BP 1.21.4

पुंसो मनोरमा नारी भर्त्ता नार्या मनोरमः ।
गौरीव्रतेन भवति राजँल्लवणवर्जनात् ॥

Through Gaurī's vow and abstaining from salt, O king, a woman becomes pleasing to her husband, and her husband pleasing to her.

BP 1.21.5

इदं व्रतं प्रति विभो धर्मराजस्य शृण्वतः ।
उमया च पुरा प्रोक्तं यद्वाक्यं तन्निबोध मे ॥

O mighty one, concerning this vow, hear from me the words that Umā once spoke while Dharmarāja listened.

BP 1.21.6

मया व्रतमिदं सृष्टं सौभाग्यकरणं नृणाम् ।
मर्त्ये तु नियता नारी व्रतमेतच्चरिष्यति ॥
सह भर्त्रा स मोदेत यथा भर्ता हरो मम ॥

“I created this vow to bring good fortune to people. A self-controlled mortal woman who observes this vow will rejoice with her husband, just as Hara is my husband.”

BP 1.21.7

या च कन्या न भर्तारं विन्दते शोभना सती ।
सा त्विदं व्रतमुद्दिश्य भवेदक्षारभोजना ॥
मच्चित्ता मन्मनाः कुर्यान्मद्भक्ता मत्परिग्रहा ॥

“A beautiful, chaste maiden who does not find a husband should undertake this vow and eat food without salt. With her mind fixed on me, devoted to me, and given to me, she should perform it.”

BP 1.21.8

गौरीं संस्थाप्य सौवर्णीं गन्धालङ्कारभूषिताम् ।
वस्त्रालङ्कारसंवीतां पुष्पमण्डलमण्डिताम् ॥

She should install a golden image of Gaurī, adorned with scents and ornaments, clothed and adorned, and decorated with a circle of flowers.

BP 1.21.9

लवणं गुडं घृतं तैलं देव्यै शक्त्या निवेदयेत् ।
कटुखण्डं जीरकं च पत्रशाकं च भारत ॥

She should offer the goddess salt, molasses, ghee, and oil according to her means, and also pieces of pungent spice, cumin, and leafy greens, O Bharata.

BP 1.21.10

गुडघृष्टांस्तथापूपान्खडवेष्टांस्तथा नृप ।
ब्राह्मणे व्रतसम्पन्ने प्रदद्यात्सुबहुश्रुते ॥

O king, she should give molasses-coated cakes and wrapped cakes to a Brahmin who has completed vows and is deeply learned.

BP 1.21.11

शुक्लपक्षे सदा देया यथा शक्त्या हिरण्मयी ।
धनहीने तु भक्त्या च मधुवृक्षमयी नृप ॥

During the bright fortnight, a golden image should always be given according to one's means. One without wealth may, with devotion, give one made of mahua wood, O king.

BP 1.21.12

अर्च्या नित्यं संनिधानात्तत्र गौरी न संशयः ।
अक्षारलवणं रात्रौ भुंक्ते चैव सुवाग्यता ॥

It should be worshipped daily, for Gaurī is undoubtedly present there. At night the fortunate woman eats food without alkali or salt.

BP 1.21.13

गौरी सन्निहिता नित्यं भूमौ प्रस्तरशायिनी ।
एवं नियमयुक्तस्य देव्या यत्समुदाहृतम् ॥

Gaurī is always present where she lies on a stone upon the ground. Hear the great result declared by the goddess for one who follows this discipline,

BP 1.21.14

तच्छृणुष्व महाबाहो कथ्यमानं महाफलम् ।
भर्तारं तु लभेत्कन्या यं वाच्छति मनोऽनुगम् ॥

O mighty-armed one: a maiden obtains the husband she desires, the one who follows her heart.

BP 1.21.15

सुचिरं सह वै भर्त्रा क्रीडयित्वा इहैव सा ।
सन्ततिं च प्रतिष्ठाप्य सह तेनैव गच्छति ॥

After sporting with that husband here for a long time and establishing offspring, she goes with him.

BP 1.21.16

हेलिलोकं चन्द्रलोकं लोकं चित्रशिखण्डिनः ।
गत्वा याति सदो राजन्वामदेवस्य भारत ॥

Having gone to Heli's world, the Moon's world, and the world of Citraśikhaṇḍin, she goes to the assembly of Vāmadeva, O king, O Bharata.

BP 1.21.17

विधवा तु यदा राजन्देव्या व्रतपरायणा ।
भर्त्तारं नियता नित्यं सदार्चनपरायणा ॥

When a widow is devoted to the goddess's vow, O king, disciplined concerning her husband and always intent upon worship—

BP 1.21.18

इह चोत्सृज्य देहं स्वं दृष्ट्वा हरिपुरे प्रियम् ।
आक्षिप्य यमदूतेभ्यः सह भर्त्रा रमेद्दिवि ॥

—leaving her body here, she sees her beloved in Hari's city, draws him away from Yama's messengers, and rejoices in heaven with her husband.

BP 1.21.19

वर्षकोटिं दशगुणां रमित्वा सा इहागता ।
भर्त्रा सहैव पूर्वोक्तं लभते फलमीप्सितम् ॥

After enjoying there for ten crores of years, she returns here and, together with her husband, obtains the desired result already described.

BP 1.21.20

इन्द्राण्यापि व्रतमिदं पुत्रार्थिन्या नराधिप ।
लब्धः पुत्रो व्रतस्यान्ते जयन्तो नाम नामतः ॥

Indrāṇī also undertook this vow, desiring a son, O king; at the vow's end a son named Jayanta was obtained.

BP 1.21.21

अरुन्धत्या तथा चीर्णं वशिष्ठं प्रति कामतः ।
दृश्यते दिवि चाद्यापि वशिष्ठस्य समीपतः ॥

Arundhatī also observed it out of love for Vasiṣṭha; even today she is seen in heaven beside Vasiṣṭha.

BP 1.21.22

रोहिण्या लवणत्यागात्सपन्नीगणमर्दनम् ।
लब्धं देव्याः प्रसादेन सौभाग्यमचलं दिवि ॥

By giving up salt, Rohiṇī, through the goddess's favor, obtained the defeat of her co-wives and unshakable good fortune in heaven.

BP 1.21.23

इत्येषा तिथिरित्येव तृतीया लोकपूजिता ।
सदा विशेषतः पुण्या वैशाखे मासि या भवेत् ॥

Thus this third lunar day is honored by the world. It is always especially sacred when it occurs in the month of Vaiśākha.

BP 1.21.24

पुण्या भाद्रपदे मासि माघेप्येवं न संशयः ।
माघे भाद्रपदे चापि स्त्रीणां धन्या प्रचक्षते ॥

It is sacred in Bhādrapada and likewise in Māgha, no doubt. The third day in Māgha and Bhādrapada is declared fortunate for women.

BP 1.21.25

साधारणी तु वैशाखे सर्वलोकस्य भारत ।
माघमासे तृतीयायां गुडस्य लवणस्य च ॥
दानं श्रेयस्करं राजन्स्त्रीणां च पुरुषस्य च ॥

O Bharata, in Vaiśākha it is common to all the world. On the third day of Māgha, giving molasses and salt brings welfare to women and men.

BP 1.21.26

गुडेन तुष्यते दत्तो लवणेन तु विश्वभूः ।
गुडपूपास्तु दातव्या मासि भाद्रपदे तथा ॥

The recipient is pleased by molasses, and the creator of the universe by salt. In Bhādrapada, molasses cakes should also be given.

BP 1.21.27

तृतीयायां तु माघस्य वामदेवस्य प्रीतये ।
वारिदानं प्रशस्तं स्यान्मोदकानां च भारत ॥

On Māgha's third day, giving water and sweet dumplings is praised for the pleasure of Vāmadeva, O Bharata.

BP 1.21.28

वैशाखे मासि राजेन्द्र तृतीया चन्दनस्य च ।
वारिणा तुष्यते वेधा मोदकैर्भीम एव हि ॥
दानात्तु चन्दनस्येह कञ्जजो नात्र संशयः ॥

O king of kings, on Vaiśākha's third day the Creator is pleased by water, Bhīma by sweet dumplings, and the lotus-born by a gift of sandalwood, no doubt.

BP 1.21.29

यात्वेषा कुरुशार्दूल वैशाखे मासि वै तिथिः ।
तृतीया साऽक्षया लोके गीर्वाणैरभिनन्दिता ॥

O tiger among the Kurus, this third lunar day in Vaiśākha is called Akṣayā in the world and is praised by the gods.

BP 1.21.30

आगतेयं महाबाहो भूरि चन्द्रं वसुव्रता ।
कलधौतं तथान्नं च घृतं चापि विशेषतः ॥
यद्यद्दत्तं त्वक्षयं स्यात्तेनेयमक्षया स्मृता ॥

When it arrives, O mighty-armed one, abundant silver, food, and especially ghee should be given. Whatever is given becomes imperishable; therefore it is remembered as Akṣayā.

BP 1.21.31

यत्किञ्चिद्दीयते दानं स्वल्पं वा यदि वा बहु ।
तत्सर्वमक्षयं स्याद्वै तेनेयमक्षया स्मृता ॥

Whatever gift is given on this day, whether little or much, all of it becomes imperishable; therefore it is remembered as Akṣayā.

BP 1.21.32

योऽस्यां ददाति करकन्वारिबीजसमन्वितान् ।
स याति पुरुषो वीर लोकं वै हेममालिनः ॥

O hero, a man who gives water-jars supplied with water and seeds on this day goes to the world of the golden-garlanded one.

BP 1.21.33

इत्येषा कथिता वीर तृतीया तिथिरुत्तमा ।
यामुपोष्य नरो राजन्नृद्धिं वृद्धिं श्रियं भजेत् ॥

Thus, O hero, the excellent third lunar day is described. By fasting on it, O king, a man partakes of prosperity, increase, and good fortune.
