VP 5.38.1

श्रीपराशर उवाच

अर्जुनोऽपि तदान्विष्य रामकृष्णकलेवरे ।
संस्कारं लम्भयामास तथान्येषामनुक्रमात्

Parāśara said: Arjuna saw the bodies of Krishna and Balaraama, and he wanted to do samskaara, and as well as for others also.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
अर्जुनः अपि (arjunaḥ api) — Arjuna too
तदा अन्वीक्ष्य (tadā anvīkṣya) — then seeing
राम-कृष्ण-कलेवरे (rāma-kṛṣṇa-kalevare) — the bodies of Rāma and Kṛṣṇa
संस्कारम् लम्भयाम् आस (saṃskāram lambhayām āsa) — caused the funeral rites
तथा अन्येषाम् (tathā anyeṣām) — and of the others
अनुक्रमात् (anukramāt) — in order
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

arjuno’pi tadānviṣya rāmakṛṣṇakalevare | saṃskāraṃ lambhayāmāsa tathānyeṣāmanukramāt

VP 5.38.3

रेवती चापि रामस्य देहमाश्लिष्य सत्तमा ।
विवेश ज्वलितं वह्निं तत्सङ्गाह्लादशीतलम्

Revati also embraced Balaraama's deha, and entered into the burning fire and it became very cool because of the happiness which she attained, because of sanga of Balaraama.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
रेवती च अपि (revatī ca api) — and Revati too
रामस्य देहम् आश्लिष्य (rāmasya deham āśliṣya) — embracing Rāma’s body
सत्तमा (sattamā) — the excellent woman
विविश (viviśa) — entered
ज्वलितम् वह्निम् (jvalitam vahnim) — the blazing fire
तत्-सङ्ग-आह्लाद-शीतलम् (tat-saṅga-āhlāda-śītalam) — cooled by his association
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

revatī cāpi rāmasya dehamāśliṣya sattamā | viveśa jvalitaṃ vahniṃ tatsaṅgāhlādaśītalam

Śrīdhara

revatīti | tat-saṅgena ya āhlādaḥ tena śītala-prāyam

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

रेवतीति । तत्-सङ्गेन य आह्लादः तेन शीतल-प्रायम्

Śrīdhara commentary.

Śrīdhara explains that Rāma’s association made the fire seem almost cool.

VP 5.38.8

यस्मिन्दिने हरिर्यातो दिवं सन्त्यज्य मेदिनीम् ।
तस्मिन्नेवावतीर्णोऽयं कालकायो बली कलिः

On the same day which Krishna completed His avataara and left His maanusha roopa which He had taken here, and left the earth and went back, on the same day, Kali entered. Kali, who was full of dust and dirt, and who was waiting for the time when Krishna would complete His avataara, so that he can become more powerful.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
यस्मिन् दिने (yasmin dine) — on the day when
हरिः यातः दिवम् (hariḥ yātaḥ divam) — Hari went to heaven
सन्त्यज्य मेदिनीम् (santyajya medinīm) — abandoning the earth
तस्मिन् दिने एव (tasmin dine eva) — on that very day
अवतीर्णः अयम् (avatīrṇaḥ ayam) — this one appeared
काल-कायः (kāla-kāyaḥ) — whose body is time
बली कलिः (balī kaliḥ) — the powerful Kali
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yasmindine hariryāto divaṃ santyajya medinīm | tasminnevāvatīrṇo’yaṃ kālakāyo balī kaliḥ

Śrīdhara

yasminn iti | avatīrṇa āvirbhūtaḥ | kāla-kāyaḥ kalir nāma

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

यस्मिन्न् इति । अवतीर्ण आविर्भूतः । काल-कायः कलिर् नाम

Śrīdhara commentary.

Śrīdhara explains that avatīrṇa means manifested and Kāla-kāya is the name Kali.
