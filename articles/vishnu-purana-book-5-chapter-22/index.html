VP 5.22.1

जरासन्ध-सुते कंस उपयेमे महा-बलः ।
अस्तिम्प्राप्तिञ्चमैत्रेयतयोर् भर्तृ-हणं हरिम् ॥१॥

The mighty Kaṃsa married Asti and Prāpti, the daughters of Jarāsandha, O Maitreya; Hari slew their husband.

Śrīdhara translation

“Asti and Prāpti” are the names of the two daughters of Jarāsandha; Hari slew their husband, Kaṃsa.

VP 5.22.2

महा-बल-परीवारो मगधाधिपतिर् बली ।

The mighty lord of Magadha, Jarāsandha, came in anger with a great army to kill the Yādavas.

“The lord of Magadha” is Jarāsandha. “Together with the Yādavas” means accompanied by the Yādavas.

VP 5.22.9

जिते तस्मिन् सुदुर्वृत्ते जरासन्धेमहामुने।
जीवमाने गते तत्र कृष्णस् तेनामन्यत नाजितम् ॥९॥

When that very wicked Jarāsandha had been defeated but had gone away alive, O great sage, Kṛṣṇa did not consider him conquered.

Śrīdhara translation

He did not consider him defeated, since his return was inevitable. In another reading: he considered himself defeated by that very one.
