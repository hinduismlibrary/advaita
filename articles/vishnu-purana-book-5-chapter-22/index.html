VP 5.22.1

जरासंधसुते कंस उपयेमे महाबलः ।
अस्तिं प्राप्तिं च मैत्रेय तयोर्भर्तृहणं हरिम्

Jarāsandha’s two daughters, Asti and Prāpti, were married by the mighty Kaṃsa, O Maitreya. Hari was the slayer of their husband.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
जरासंध-सुते (jarāsaṃdha-sute) — the daughters of Jarāsandha
कंसः (kaṃsaḥ) — Kaṃsa
उपयेमे (upayeme) — married
महा-बलः (mahā-balaḥ) — the very powerful one
अस्तिम् (astim) — Asti
प्राप्तिं (prāptiṃ) — Prāpti
च (ca) — and
मैत्रेय (maitreya) — O Maitreya
तयोः (tayoḥ) — of those two
भर्तृ-हणम् (bhartṛ-haṇam) — the slayer of the husband
हरिम् (harim) — Hari.
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

jarāsaṃdhasute kaṃsa upayeme mahābalaḥ | astiṃ prāptiṃ ca maitreya tayorbhartṛhaṇaṃ harim

Śrīdhara

jarāsandheti | asti-prāpti-sañjñe jarāsandha-sute tayor bhartṛ-haṇaṃ kaṃsa-hantāram ||1||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

जरासन्धेति । अस्ति-प्राप्ति-सञ्ज्ञे जरासन्ध-सुते तयोर् भर्तृ-हणं कंस-हन्तारम् ॥१॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

On “Jarāsandha”: the daughters of Jarāsandha were named Asti and Prāpti; Hari was the slayer of their husband, Kaṃsa.

On “Great army”: the lord of Magadha is Jarāsandha; “together with the Yādavas” means accompanied by the Yādavas.

On “When defeated”: he did not consider him conquered because his return was certain. In another reading, the meaning is that he considered himself defeated by that very man.

VP 5.22.15

तस्यापिपक्षक्षपणे कियानुद्यमविस्तरः

He who by mind alone creates and dissolves the universe—what exertion is required of him to destroy the hosts of his enemies?

Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Transliteration (IAST)

manasaiva jagatsṛṣṭiṃ saṃhāraṃ ca karoti yaḥ | tasyāpipakṣakṣapaṇe kiyānudyamavistaraḥ

Śrīdhara

līlātvam eva prapañcayati—manasaiveti yāvat samāpti ||15||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

लीलात्वम् एव प्रपञ्चयति—मनसैवेति यावत् समाप्ति ॥१५॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

On “By mind alone”: he elaborates the fact that this is sport, up to the completion of the phrase “by mind alone.”

VP 5.22.18

मनुष्यदेहिनां चेष्टामित्येवमनुवर्तते ।
लीला जगत्पतेस्तस्य च्छन्दतः परिवर्तते

Thus the sport of the Lord of the world follows the conduct of embodied human beings; it unfolds according to his will.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

चेष्टाम् (ceṣṭām) — the conduct

तस्य (tasya) — his

Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

manuṣyadehināṃ ceṣṭāmityevamanuvartate | līlā jagatpatestasya cchandataḥ parivartate

Śrīdhara

manuṣyeti | chandataḥ icchātaḥ līlā parivartate ||18||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

मनुष्येति । छन्दतः इच्छातः लीला परिवर्तते ॥१८॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

On “Of embodied human beings”: the sport unfolds according to his will.
