Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 41 — Vedic Study, Conduct, and Brahmin Status

BP 1.41.1

ब्रह्मोवाच
वेदाध्ययनमप्येतद्ब्राह्मण्यं प्रतिपद्यते ।
विप्रवद्वैश्यराजन्यौ राक्षसा रावणादयः ॥

Brahmā said, Vedic study too is taken as Brahminhood, yet Vaishyas, Kshatriyas, and Rakshasas like Rāvaṇa study like Brahmins.

BP 1.41.2

श्वादचाण्डालदासाश्च लुब्धकाभीरधीवराः ।
येन्येऽपि वृषलाः केचित्तेऽपि वेदानधीयते ॥

Dog eaters, outcastes, servants, hunters, cowherds, fishermen, and others considered low also study the Vedas.

BP 1.41.3

यदा देशान्तरं गत्वा ब्राह्मण्यं क्षत्रियं श्रिताः ।
व्यापाराकारभावाद्यैर्विप्रतुल्यैः प्रकल्पितैः ॥

Having gone to another land, they assume Brahmin or Kshatriya status through occupations, appearance, manner, and other features made to resemble Brahmins.

BP 1.41.4

वेदानधीत्य वेदौ वा वेदं वापि यथाक्रमम् ।
प्रोद्वहन्ति शुभां कन्यां शुद्धब्राह्मणजा नराः ॥

Having studied the Vedas, two Vedas, or even one Veda, as appropriate, men born from pure Brahmin families marry an auspicious maiden.

BP 1.41.5

अथवाधीत्य वेदांस्तु क्षत्रवैश्यैस्तु वा नराः ।
गौडपूर्वां कृतामेयुर्जातिं वा दाक्षिणात्यजाम् ॥

Or, having studied the Vedas with Kshatriyas or Vaishyas, people may enter a community from Gauḍa or a birth group from the south.

BP 1.41.6

अपरिज्ञातशूद्रत्वाद्ब्राह्मण्यं यान्ति कामतः ।
तस्मान्न ज्ञायते भेदो वेदाध्यायक्रियाकृतः ॥

Because their Shudra origin is unknown, they attain Brahminhood as they wish. Therefore no distinction produced by Vedic study and ritual action can be discerned.

BP 1.41.7

शास्त्रकारैस्तथा चोक्तं न्यायमार्गानुसारिभिः ।
ते साधु मतमाकर्ण्य सन्तः सन्ति विमत्सराः ॥

The authors of the shastras who follow the path of reason have also said this. Hearing that sound view, good people are free from envy.

BP 1.41.8

आचारहीनान्न पुनंति वेदा यद्यप्यधीताः सह षद्भिरङ्गैः ।
शिल्पं हि वेदाध्ययनं द्विजानां वृत्तं स्मृतं ब्राह्मणलक्षणं तु ॥

The Vedas do not purify one without good conduct, even if studied with their six limbs. Vedic study is a skill of the twice born, but conduct is remembered as the mark of a Brahmin.

BP 1.41.9

अधीत्य चतुरो वेदान्यदि वृत्ते न तिष्ठति ।
न तेन क्रियते कार्यं स्त्रीरत्नेनेव षण्ढकः ॥

After studying the four Vedas, if one does not abide in conduct, no purpose is accomplished by him, just as a eunuch gains nothing from a jewel among women.

BP 1.41.10

शिखाप्रणवसंस्कारसन्ध्योपासनमेखलाः ।
दण्डाजिनपवित्राद्याः शूद्रेष्वपि निरङ्कुशाः ॥

The topknot, Om, consecratory rites, twilight worship, girdle, staff, deerskin, purifying ring, and the like may be adopted without restraint even by Shudras.

BP 1.41.11

प्रसङ्गोऽपि हि शूद्राणां न शक्यो विनिवारितुम् ।
देवोत्तमत्रयेणापि निवर्तन्ते नराः स्वयम् ॥

The participation of Shudras cannot be prevented. Even the three highest gods withdraw, and people withdraw of their own accord.

BP 1.41.12

तस्मान्नैतेऽपि लक्ष्यन्ते विलक्षणतया नृणाम् ।
यज्ञोपवीतसंस्कारमेखलाचूलिकादयः ॥

Therefore the sacred thread, consecratory rites, girdle, topknot, and the rest are not recognized as distinctive marks among people.

BP 1.41.13

आभिचारिकमन्त्राद्यैर्दुर्लभत्वादिभाषणैः ।
ब्राह्मणस्यैव शक्तिश्चेत्केनास्य विनिहन्यते ॥

If power belongs only to a Brahmin through spells, rare utterances, and the like, by whom can that power be destroyed.

BP 1.41.14

तपः सत्यादिमाहात्म्याद्देवतासमयस्मृतिः ।
मन्त्रशक्तिर्नृणामेषां सर्वेषामपि विद्यते ॥

Through the greatness of austerity, truth, and other virtues, and through remembered agreements with the gods, the power of mantras is present in all people.

BP 1.41.15

वञ्चनं दुर्वचस्यापि क्रियते सर्वमानवैः ।
शूद्रब्राह्मणयोस्तस्मान्नास्ति भेदः कथञ्चन ॥

Deception and harsh speech are practised by all people. Therefore there is no difference whatever between a Shudra and a Brahmin.

BP 1.41.16

शापानुग्रहकारित्वं शक्तिभेदो न विद्यते ।
चौरचाटादिराजन्यदुर्जनाभिहते नृणाम् ॥

There is no difference in the power to curse or bless among people struck by thieves, flatterers, rulers, and other wicked persons.

BP 1.41.17

आत्मादुःखोदयापायं स्वेषु जन्तुषु रक्षणम् ।
कर्तुं न प्रभवेच्छूद्रो ब्राह्मणस्तद्वदेव हि ॥

A Shudra is not able to cause or remove his own suffering or protect the living beings among his own people. A Brahmin is just the same.

BP 1.41.18

मा भूद्युगे कलावेतद्देशे चाकार्यकृद्द्विजे ।
स्यादन्यदेशकालादौ द्विजानामतिशायिनाम् ॥

Let this not be confined to the Kali age, this land, or a twice born person who acts wrongly. The same may occur among eminent twice born people in other lands and times.

BP 1.41.19

शापानुग्रहसामर्थ्यमन्यद्वाध्यात्मगोचरम् ।
ब्रह्मसाधनमेतद्धि लिङ्गं केचित्प्रचक्षते ॥

Some proclaim the power to curse or bless, or another spiritual capacity, as a sign of the means to Brahman.

BP 1.41.20

संसारारक्तचेतस्का मोहान्धतमसावृताः ।
पतन्त्युन्मार्गगर्तेषु प्रत्यग्नि शलभा यथा ॥

With minds attached to the cycle of existence and covered by the blind darkness of delusion, they fall into pits off the path like moths into fire.

BP 1.41.21

जातिधर्मः स्वयं किञ्जिद्विशेषः श्रुतिसङ्गमात् ।
असिद्धः शूद्रजातीनां प्रसिद्धो विप्रजातिषु ॥

Is birth duty itself some special quality arising from union with sacred learning, unestablished among Shudras but recognized among Brahmins.

BP 1.41.22

संस्कारो योनिसाध्यो वा सामग्री प्रभवोऽथ वा ।
शूद्रेभ्योऽतिशयं धत्ते यः साधारणतागुणः ॥

Is it a consecration achieved through birth, an effect produced by the assembly of materials, or some common quality that bestows superiority over Shudras.

BP 1.41.23

विप्राणां पञ्चधा भेदः कल्पनीयस्तु पण्डितैः ।
न जातिजस्त्रयीजो वा विशेषो युक्तिबाधकात् ॥
क्रमाक्रमक्रियाः सन्ति न सनातनवस्तुनः ॥

The wise would have to imagine five divisions among Brahmins. No distinction born from birth or from the three Vedas withstands reason. Ordered and unordered rites concern things that are not eternal.

BP 1.41.24

नित्यो न हेतुर्विगतक्रियत्वाद्धेतुर्भवेद्वेदविशेषतः सः ।
स तत्समस्तत्प्रतिसन्निधानात्कालात्ययेक्षित्वमयुक्तमेव ॥

The eternal is not a cause, since it is without action. If a particular Vedic rite were the cause, its presence with all the rest would make the result appear at the wrong time, which is unreasonable.

BP 1.41.25

स्वान्तः शरीरवृत्तिस्थः श्रुतियोगादुदेति यः ।
सोऽनन्यवेदविज्ञातस्वभावोऽन्यैर्न गम्यते ।

That which arises through union with sacred learning, abiding within and established in the activity of the body, has a nature known by no other Veda and is not reached by others.

BP 1.41.26

विशिष्टाधीतिधर्मत्वे कृत्रिमा ब्रह्मसङ्गतिः ।
यस्यास्यतिशयस्तस्य नान्यो नाश्रयते यदि ॥

If connection with Brahman is produced artificially by special learning, that superiority would belong to whoever possesses it, if it rests on no other basis.

BP 1.41.27

दृश्यस्वभावं किमभीष्टमेतद्ब्राह्मण्यमाहोस्विददृष्टरूपम् ।
सर्वैः प्रतीयेत हि दृश्यरूपं ततोऽन्यथावद्गतिरेव न स्यात् ॥

Is the desired Brahminhood something with a visible nature, or is it of unseen form. Whatever is visible would be perceived by all. Otherwise no determinate understanding of it could arise.

BP 1.41.28

सामग्र्यभावात्परमं विशेषं भूदेवगात्रस्थमभूमिदेवाः ।
स्मरन्ति तेनात्मनि पुण्यपापं यथा तथेत्येतदयुक्तमुक्तम् ॥

Non Brahmins claim that a supreme distinction resides in the body of Brahmins because the necessary materials are absent in themselves. They say merit and sin are therefore in the self. This is unreasonable.

BP 1.41.29

सामग्र्यनुष्ठानगुणैः समग्राः शूद्रा यतः सन्ति समा द्विजानाम् ।
तस्माद्विशेषो द्विजशूद्रनाम्नोर्नाध्यात्मिको बाह्यनिमित्तको वा ॥

Since Shudras may be complete in the materials, observances, and qualities of the twice born, the distinction between the names twice born and Shudra is neither spiritual nor caused by anything external.

BP 1.41.30

संस्कारतः सोऽतिशयो यदि स्यात्सर्वस्य पुंसोऽस्त्यतिसंस्कृतस्य ।
यः संस्कृतो विप्रगणप्रधानो व्यासादिकैस्तेन न तस्य साम्यम् ॥

If superiority comes from consecration, it would belong to every highly consecrated person. If one consecrated man is foremost among Brahmins, then Vyāsa and the others would have no equal.

BP 1.41.31

हेतुत्वं घटते नैषां जात्यादीनामसम्भवात् ।
जातेरकृतकत्वाच्च अधीते न विशेषतः ॥

These things cannot be causes, since birth and the like are not produced. Birth is not made, and study does not create a particular distinction.

BP 1.41.32

संस्कारातिशयाभावादन्तरस्यागते परैः ।
भौतिकत्वाच्छरीरस्य समस्तानामसंहतैः ॥

No inward distinction is attained through consecration, and the bodies of all people are material and composed of the same elements.

BP 1.41.33

किं चान्यनास्तिकम्लेच्छ यवनादिजनेष्वलम् ॥

What more need be said about atheists, Mlecchas, Yavanas, and other peoples.

BP 1.41.34

वेदोदितबहिर्दुष्टचरितेषु दुरात्मसु ।
धर्मादतिशयो दृष्टः क्रूरसाहसिकादिषु ॥
तस्माद्विप्रेषु जात्यादिसामग्रीप्रभवो न सः ॥

Excellence in righteousness is seen among wicked people whose outward conduct is contrary to the Veda, among cruel and violent people. Therefore such excellence among Brahmins does not arise from birth and its materials.

BP 1.41.35

तस्मान्न च विभेदोऽस्ति न बहिर्नान्तरात्मनि ।
सुखादौ न चैश्वर्ये नाज्ञायां नाभयेष्वपि ॥

Therefore there is no distinction outside or within the self, in happiness, power, ignorance, or fearlessness.

BP 1.41.36

न वीर्ये नाकृतौ नाक्षे न व्यापारे न चायुषि ।
नाङ्गे पुष्टे न दौर्बल्ये न स्थैर्ये नापि चापले ॥

There is no distinction in strength, form, the senses, occupation, lifespan, well nourished limbs, weakness, stability, or restlessness.

BP 1.41.37

न प्रज्ञायां न वैराग्ये न धर्मे न पराक्रमे ।
न त्रिवर्गे न नैपुण्ये न रूपादौ न भेषजे ॥

There is no distinction in intelligence, dispassion, righteousness, courage, the three aims of life, skill, beauty, appearance, or medicine.

BP 1.41.38

न स्त्रीगर्भे न गमने न देहमलसम्प्लवे ।
नास्थिरन्ध्रे न च प्रेम्णि न प्रमाणेषु लोमसु ॥

There is no distinction in the womb of a woman, movement, bodily impurity, openings in the bones, affection, measurements, or hair.

BP 1.41.39

शूद्रब्राह्मण्योर्भेदो मृग्यमाणोऽपि यत्नतः ।
नेक्ष्यते सर्वधर्मेषु संहतैस्त्रिदशैरपि ॥

Even when carefully sought, a distinction between Shudra and Brahmin is not seen in any quality, even by all the gods assembled together.

BP 1.41.40

उक्तमात्रा विसम्भूतिर्विचारक्रमकारिभिः ।
वृद्धवृन्दारकाधीशैरप्रधृष्यमिदं वचः ॥

This unassailable statement was spoken by the ancient lords of the gods, who proceed through ordered inquiry.

BP 1.41.41

न ब्राह्मणाश्चन्द्रमरीचिशुभ्रा न क्षत्रियाः किंशुकपुष्पवर्णाः ।
न चेह वैश्या हरितालतुल्याः शूद्रा न चाङ्गारसमानवर्णाः ॥

Brahmins are not white like moonbeams, Kshatriyas are not colored like the flowers of the flame tree, Vaishyas are not yellow green like mineral pigment, and Shudras are not the color of embers.

BP 1.41.42

पादप्रचारैस्तनुवर्णकेशैः सुखेन दुःखेन च शोणितेन ।
त्वङ्मांसमेदोस्थिरसैः समानाश्चतुष्प्रभेदा हि कथं भवन्ति ॥

Since they are alike in gait, body, color, hair, pleasure, pain, blood, skin, flesh, fat, bones, and bodily fluids, how do they become four distinct kinds.

BP 1.41.43

वर्णप्रमाणाकृतिगर्भवासवाग्बुद्धिकर्मेन्द्रियजीवितेषु ।
बलत्रिवर्गामयभेषजेषु न विद्यते जातिकृतो विशेषः ॥

There is no distinction made by birth in color, measure, form, the time in the womb, speech, intelligence, action, senses, life, strength, the three aims of life, illness, or medicine.

BP 1.41.44

स एक एवात्र पतिः प्रजानां कथं पुनर्जातिकृतः प्रभेदः ।
प्रमाणदृष्टान्तनयप्रवादैः परीक्ष्यमाणो विघटत्वमेति ॥

There is one and the same lord of creatures here. How then is there a division made by birth. Examined by valid proofs, examples, reasoning, and established teaching, it falls apart.

BP 1.41.45

चत्वार एकस्य पितुः सुताश्च तेषां सुतानां खलु जातिरेका ।
एवं प्रजानां हि पितैक एवं पित्रैकभावान्न च जातिभेदः ॥

Four sons of one father have one birth, and their sons likewise have one birth. All creatures have one father, and from their shared father there is no difference of birth.

BP 1.41.46

फलान्यथोदुम्बरवृक्षजातेर्यथाग्रमध्यान्तभवानि यानि ।
वर्णाकृतिस्पर्शरसैः समानि तथैकतो जातिरतिप्रचिन्त्या ॥

The fruits born at the top, middle, and bottom of a cluster fig tree are alike in color, form, touch, and taste. Birth should likewise be carefully understood as one.

BP 1.41.47

ये कौशिकाः काश्यपगौतमाश्च कौंडिन्यमाण्डव्यवशिष्ठगोत्राः ।
आत्रेयकौत्साङ्गिरसः सगर्गा मौद्गल्यकात्यायनभार्गवाश्च ॥

There are Kauśikas, Kāśyapas, Gautamas, Kauṇḍinyas, Māṇḍavyas, and Vasiṣṭhas, as well as Ātreyas, Kautsas, Āṅgirases, Gargas, Maudgalyas, Kātyāyanas, and Bhārgavas.

BP 1.41.48

गोत्राणि नानाविधजातयश्च भ्रातृस्नुषामैथुनपुत्रभावाः ।
वैवाहिकं कर्म न वर्णभेदः सर्वाणि शिल्पानि भवन्ति तेषाम् ॥

Their lineages and kinds of birth are various, as are unions between brothers and their sisters in law, and the children born from them. Marriage is an act, not a distinction of varna. All crafts are found among them.

BP 1.41.49

ये चान्ये पण्डिताः प्राहुर्देहब्राह्मणतां नराः ।
तेषां दुर्दृष्टितिमिरमपनीयानुकम्प्य च ॥

Other scholars who say that the body makes a man a Brahmin should have the darkness of their faulty vision removed, with compassion.

BP 1.41.50

न्यायाञ्जनौषधैर्दिव्यैः परिणामसुखावहैः ।
उपनीतः प्रयत्नेन सुदृष्टिं संविदद्महे ॥

With the divine medicine of reason, an eye ointment that brings happiness in its result, we carefully lead them to clear sight and understanding.

BP 1.41.51

मृतिमत्त्वाच्च नाशित्वं नाशित्वाच्छेषभूतवत् ।
देहाधारनिविष्टानां ब्राह्मण्यं न प्रकल्प्यते ॥

Because it is mortal, the body is perishable, and because it is perishable, it is like other leftover matter. Brahminhood is not posited in those lodged within and supported by the body.

BP 1.41.52

एकैकोवयवस्तेषां न ब्राह्मण्यं समश्नुते ।
न चानेकसमूहेऽपि सर्वथातिप्रसङ्गतः ॥

No single bodily part of theirs possesses Brahminhood, nor does a collection of many parts possess it, since that would lead to an excessive conclusion.

BP 1.41.53

पृथिव्युदकवाय्यग्निपरिणामविशेषतः ।
देहतः सर्वभूतानां ब्राह्मणत्वप्रसङ्गतः ॥

Since bodies are particular transformations of earth, water, air, and fire, Brahminhood would follow for all embodied beings if it arose from the body.

BP 1.41.54

देहस्य ब्राह्मणत्वं यैरतत्त्वज्ञैः प्रकल्प्यते ।
संस्कर्तॄणां शरीरस्य तेषां न ब्रह्मता भवेत् ॥

Those who, ignorant of reality, imagine Brahminhood to belong to the body would themselves have no Brahminhood, as makers of the body.

BP 1.41.55

मृग्यमाणे प्रयत्नेन देहे तन्नोपलभ्यते ।
तस्मान्न देहे ब्राह्मण्यं नापि देहात्मकं भवेत् ॥

When the body is carefully examined, that status is not found in it. Therefore Brahminhood is neither in the body nor constituted by the body.

BP 1.41.56

वर्णापसदचांडालश्वादादीनां प्रसज्यते ।
यदि देहस्य विप्रत्वं भवद्भिरुपगम्यते ॥

If you accept that the body is Brahmin, the same would apply to degraded varnas, outcastes, dog eaters, and others.

BP 1.41.57

देहशक्तिगुणैः क्षीणैः कायभस्मादिरूपवत् ।
तस्माद्देहात्मकेनैतद्ब्राह्मण्यं नापि कर्मजम् ॥

The powers and qualities of the body diminish, and it becomes ash and the like. Therefore this Brahminhood is neither constituted by the body nor produced by action.
