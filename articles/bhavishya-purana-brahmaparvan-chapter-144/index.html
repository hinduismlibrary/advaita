धूपमाल्यैर्युतश्चापि उपहारैस्तथैव च । भोजयन्ति सहस्रांशुं तेन ते भोजकाः स्मृताः ॥

Joined with incense and garlands, and likewise with offerings, they feed Sahasrāṃśu; therefore they are remembered as Bhojakas.
