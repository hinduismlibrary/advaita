VP 5.16.1

केशी चापि बलोदग्रः कंस-दूतः प्रचोदितः ।
कृष्णस्य निधनाकाङ्क्षी वृन्दावनम् उपागमत् ॥१॥

Keśin, also mighty in strength, impelled by Kaṃsa’s messenger and desiring Kṛṣṇa’s death, came to Vṛndāvana.

Śrīdhara translation

“Keśin,” thus. “Mighty in strength” means arrogant through strength. He was impelled by Kaṃsa’s messenger from his own place; he was not summoned by Kaṃsa and sent personally. He could not tolerate delay in pursuing his own aim and the demon’s task; this is the meaning.

अलं त्रासेन गोपालाः केशिनः किं भयातुरैः ।

“Enough of fear, cowherds! Why be frightened of Keśin? Is the valor of heroes being diminished by you, who are of the cowherd kind?”

“Enough,” thus. “Is the heroes’ valor being lost?” Or: even when the master is present, if a retainer is afraid, others will think the master has little strength. Therefore, through your fear, my own men, is my valor as a hero being lost? Why is it being lost? This is the meaning.

किम् अनेनाल्प-सारेण ह्रेषिताटोप-कारिणा।

“What can this horse of little strength do, making a display with its neighing, devoid of Daitya strength, and prancing?”

Śrīdhara translation

“What with this?” thus. Its neighing alone is a display. This horse, deprived of Daitya strength, is mounted by the Daityas after they forcibly seize it; what can it do for us, the slayers of Daityas? “Prancing” means going with the back bent; this is the meaning of “prancing.”
