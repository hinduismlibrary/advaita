Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 8: Householder’s Conduct and the Protection of Wives

BP 1.8.1

ब्रह्मोवाच
कर्तव्यं यद्गृहस्थेन तदिदानीं निबोधत ।
गदतो द्विजशार्दूल विस्तराच्छास्त्रतस्तथा ॥ १

Brahmā spoke: Now hear at length from śāstra what a householder should do, O tiger among the twice-born.

BP 1.8.2

वैवाहिकेऽग्नौ कुर्वीत गृह्यं कर्म यथाविधि ।
शुभदेशाश्रयश्चैव पत्नी वैवाहिकी गृहे ॥ २

He should perform the household rite according to rule in the marriage fire, dwell in an auspicious place, and keep his wedded wife in the house.

BP 1.8.3

स्वाश्रयेण विना शक्यं न यस्माद्रक्षणादिकम् ।
वित्तानामिव दाराणामतस्तद्विधिरुच्यते ॥ ३

Since protection and the like are not possible without a refuge of one's own, for wives as for wealth, its rule is stated.

BP 1.8.4

हेतवो हि त्रिवर्गस्य विपरीतास्तु१ मानद ।
अरक्षणाद्भवन्त्यस्मादमीषां रक्षणं मतम् ॥ ४

The causes of the three aims become opposed when unprotected, O giver of honor; therefore their protection is held necessary.

BP 1.8.5

निसर्गात्पुंस्यसन्तोषाद्गुणदोषविमर्षतः ।
दुष्टानां चापि संसर्गाद्रक्ष्या एव च योषितः ॥ ५

By nature, from men's dissatisfaction, from discernment of qualities and faults, and from association with the wicked, women indeed are to be guarded.

BP 1.8.6

पुरुषस्थानवेश्मानि त्रिविधं प्राहुराश्रयम् ।
वित्तानां रक्षणाद्यर्थमपूर्वाधिगमाय च ॥ ६

A man, a place, and a house are said to be a threefold refuge for protecting wealth and acquiring what has not yet been obtained.

BP 1.8.7

कुलीनो नीतिमान्प्राज्ञः सत्यसन्धो दृढव्रतः ।
विनीतो धार्मिकस्त्यागी२ विज्ञेयः पुरुषाश्रयः ॥ ७

The human refuge is known as well-born, skilled in policy, wise, true to his word, firm in vows, disciplined, righteous, and generous.

BP 1.8.8

नगरे खर्वटे खेटे ग्रामे चापि क्रमागते ।
यात्रावशाद्वा निवसेद्धार्मिकाद्यजनान्विते ॥ ८

He may live in a city, market town, town, or village; while traveling, he may live in a place inhabited by righteous and similar people.

BP 1.8.9

गुरुणानुमतस्तत्र ग्रामण्यादिजनेन वा ।
प्रतिवेश्माद्यबाधेन शुद्धं कुर्यान्निवेशनम् ॥ ९

With the permission of a teacher or village head and other local people, he should establish a clean dwelling without obstructing neighboring houses.

BP 1.8.10

द्वारचत्वरशालानां सर्वकारुकवेश्मनाम् ।
द्यूतसूनासुरावेशनटराजानुजीविनाम् ॥.8.१०

He should dwell far from doorways, squares, halls, houses of artisans, gambling houses, slaughterhouses, liquor shops, performers, and those who serve the king—

BP 1.8.11

पाखण्डदेववीथीनां राजमार्गकुलस्य च ।
दूरात्सुगुप्तं कर्तव्या जीविका विभवोचिता ॥१

—and from heretical establishments, temple roads, royal roads, and crowded family compounds; a livelihood suited to his means should be set up far away and well protected.

BP 1.8.12

सापिधानैकनिष्काशं शुद्धपृष्टं समन्ततः ।
सद्वृत्ताप्तजनाकीर्णमदुष्टप्रातिवेशिकम् ॥२

The house should have a covered entrance and a single exit, be clean all around, be filled with trustworthy people of good conduct, and have neighbors of uncorrupted character.

BP 1.8.13

प्रागुदक्प्रवणे देशे वास्तुविद्याविधानतः ।
प्रविभक्तक्रियाकाञ्क्षं सर्वर्तुकमनोहरम् ॥३

On ground sloping east or north, it should be laid out according to building science, its spaces divided by their functions, and made pleasant in every season.

BP 1.8.14

अर्चास्नानोदकागारगोष्ठागारमहानसैः ।
युक्तं गोवाजिशालाभिः सदासीभृत्यकाश्रयैः ॥४

It should have rooms for worship, bathing, water, cattle, storage, and cooking, together with stalls for cows and horses and quarters for maidservants and attendants.

BP 1.8.15

बहिरन्तः पुरस्त्रीकं सर्वोपकरणैर्युतम् ।
विभक्तशयनोद्देशमाप्तवृद्धैरधिष्ठितम् ॥५

It should have outer and inner women's quarters, all necessary implements, separate sleeping places, and the supervision of trustworthy elders.

BP 1.8.16

अरक्षणाद्धि दाराणां वर्णसङ्करजादयः ।
दृष्टा हि बहवो दोषास्तस्माद्रक्ष्याः सदा स्त्रियः ॥६

From lack of protection of wives many faults, such as mixture of varṇas, are seen; therefore women should always be protected.

BP 1.8.17

न ह्यासां प्रमदं दद्यान्न स्वातन्त्र्यं न विश्वसेत् ।
विश्वस्तवच्च चेष्टेत न्याय्यं भर्त्सनमाचरेत् ॥७

He should allow them neither negligence nor independence, nor trust them; he should act as if trusting and administer fitting reproof.

BP 1.8.18

नाधिकारं क्वचिद्दद्यादृते पाकादिकर्मणः ।
स्त्रीणां ग्रामीणवत्ता हि भोगायालं सुशासिता ॥८

He should give them no authority in any matter except cooking and the like; their village condition, when well governed, is enough for enjoyment.

BP 1.8.19

नित्यं तत्कर्मयोगेन ताः कर्तव्या निरन्तराः ।
इत्येवं सर्वदा व्याप्तेः स्यादविद्यनिराश्रया ॥९

He should keep them continually engaged in those tasks; thus, when occupied at all times, ignorance will have no support.

BP 1.8.20

दौर्गत्यमतिरूपं१ चाप्यसत्सङ्गः स्वतन्त्रता ।
पानाशनकथागोष्ठीप्रियत्वाकर्मशीलता२ ॥.8.२०

Poverty, excessive beauty, bad company, independence, fondness for drinking, eating and conversation, and idleness.

BP 1.8.21

कुहकेक्षणिकामुण्डाभिक्षुकीसूतिकादिभिः ।
गोप्रसङ्गैस्तथा३ सद्भिर्लिङ्गियाचकशिल्पिभिः ॥१

Deceivers, fortune-tellers, shaven-headed ascetics, mendicant women, midwives and the like; frequent dealings with herdsmen, religious mendicants, petitioners, and artisans.

BP 1.8.22

संवाहोद्यानयात्रासूद्यानेष्वामन्त्रणादिषु ।
प्रसङ्गस्तीर्थयात्रार्थं धर्मेषु प्रकटेषु च ॥२

Massage, gardens, journeys, invitations, pilgrimages, and public religious observances.

BP 1.8.23

विप्रयोगः सदा भर्त्रा तज्ज्ञातिकुलनिःस्वता ।
अमाधुर्यकदर्यत्वे भृशं पुंसां च वाच्यता ॥३

Constant separation from the husband, poverty among his family and relatives, lack of tenderness, miserliness, and severe blame from men.

BP 1.8.24

अतिक्रौर्यमतिक्षान्तिरत्यन्ताभीतिपातनम् ।
स्त्रीभिर्जितत्वमत्यर्थं सत्यं तास्ताः सदोषताः ॥४

Excessive cruelty, excessive forbearance, causing extreme fear, and being greatly overcome by women—these are faults.

BP 1.8.25

स्त्रीणां४ पत्युरधीनत्वात्पुमानेव हि निन्द्यते ।
भर्तुरेव हि तज्जाड्यं यद्भृत्यानामयोग्यता ॥५

Because women are dependent on the husband, the man himself is blamed; the unfitness of servants is the master's own incompetence.

BP 1.8.26

तस्माद्यथोदितास्वेता रक्ष्याः शासनताडनैः ।
ताडनैश्च यथाकालं यथावत्समुपाचरेत् ॥६

Therefore such women as described are to be protected through discipline and chastisement; one should chastise properly and at the proper time.

BP 1.8.27

परिगृह्य बहून्दारानुपचारैः समो भवेत् ।
यथाक्रमोचितैः कर्म दानसत्कारवासनैः ॥७

Having taken many wives, a man should treat them equally through services, giving each in order the duties, gifts, honors, and garments suited to her.

BP 1.8.28

प्रथमोऽभिजनो धर्मो योग्यत्वं च सुपुत्रता ।
पक्षे वित्तं विशेत्स्त्रीणां मानस्तत्कारणं तथा ॥८

Among wives, precedence is from family standing, duty, fitness, and bearing a worthy son; wealth also enters into consideration, and honor should rest on these causes.

BP 1.8.29

तस्मान्मानो न कर्तव्यो हेयश्चापि न तत्कृतः ।
गुरुत्वे लाघवे वापि सतां कार्यं निबन्धनम् ॥९

Therefore honor should not be given without cause, nor withheld when it is due; among good people, greater or lesser standing should have a basis.

BP 1.8.30

आकस्मिके प्रयुञ्जानः प्रेक्षावान्मानलाघवे ।
स यत्किञ्चनकारित्वाच्चायमेवैति लाघवम् ॥.8.३०

A discerning person who assigns honor or slight without reason himself becomes slighted through arbitrary conduct.

BP 1.8.31

यथा मानापमानौ हि प्रयुज्येतानिमित्ततः ।
तन्निमित्ता जनत्यागे प्रयतन्ते तदाश्रिताः ॥१

When honor and dishonor are applied without cause, those who depend on them strive to leave them for that reason.

BP 1.8.32

एतदेव ह्यपत्यानां१ ज्ञेयं माननकारणम् ।
यत्स्वापत्यनिमित्तेषु२ प्रधाने कुलयोग्यते ॥२

This same principle is to be known as a cause of honor for children; among matters concerning one's offspring, suitability to the family is primary.

BP 1.8.33

तत्संयोगात्सुखं पुंसां महद्दुःखं वियोगतः ।
तत्प्राप्तिः प्रति हातव्या स्वार्थायैव प्रियाण्यपि ॥३

From union with them men have great happiness, and from separation great sorrow; for one's own interest, even what is dear is to be relinquished.

BP 1.8.34

अतः स्वार्थैकनिष्ठोऽयं लोकः सर्वोऽवसीयते ।
तत्प्रसिद्धिर्भवेदस्तमानाद् भ्रान्तिविधायकः ॥४

Thus it is concluded that the whole world is fixed upon self-interest alone; when this is not recognized, delusion arises.

BP 1.8.35

ततो दारादिका भृत्या नियन्तव्यास्तथा द्विजाः ।
यथेहामुत्र वा श्रेयः प्राप्नुयादुत्तरोत्तरम् ॥५

Therefore wives, servants, and the rest should be guided so that they may attain increasing good here and hereafter, O twice-born.

BP 1.8.36

स्त्रीणां धर्मार्थकामेषु नातिसन्धानमाचरेत् ।
तासां तेष्वभिसन्धानाद्भवेदात्माभिसंहितः ॥६

He should not deceive women concerning duty, wealth, and pleasure; through their pursuit of these, his own aim is fulfilled.

BP 1.8.37

जाया त्वर्धं शरीरस्य नृणां धर्मादिसाधने ।
नातस्तासु व्यथां काञ्चित्प्रतिकूलं समाचरेत् ॥७

A wife is half the body of a man in accomplishing duty and the rest; therefore he should cause her no pain and act nothing contrary to her.

BP 1.8.38

यज्ञोत्सवादौ नाकस्मात्काञ्चिदासां विशेषयेत् ।
वस्त्रताम्बूलदानादौ प्रतिपत्तौ समो भवेत् ॥८

At sacrifices, festivals, and the like, he should not arbitrarily favor any one of them; in giving clothes, betel, and the like, and in treatment, he should be equal.

BP 1.8.39

प्रियाप्रियत्वं भेदो हि कामतस्तु रहोगतः ।
उपचारैः पुनर्वाक्यैस्तुल्यवृत्तिः प्रशस्यते ॥९

Differences of affection or dislike arising from desire belong in private; in courtesies and words, equal conduct is praised.

BP 1.8.40

आर्तवे तु पुनः सर्वा उपगम्याः प्रिया इव ।
पूर्वाभिजातधर्मार्था पुत्रिणी चोत्तरोत्तरम् ॥.8.४०

During the fertile season, all should be approached as beloved; precedence is successively by earlier marriage, family standing, duty, and bearing a son.

BP 1.8.41

उदग्गच्छेदनेनैव विधिना नित्यमार्तवे ।
तुल्यवृत्तिर्यथाकालं स्वं स्वं वासमखण्डयन् ॥१

By this rule he should regularly go to each in her season, maintaining equal conduct at the proper time and not interrupting each one's allotted residence.

BP 1.8.42

नित्यपर्यायवासानामपादानमसून्विदुः ।
ऋतुदुःखं प्रमोदश्च तथा पूर्वं समागतः ॥२

For those whose residence is always in rotation, taking away their turn is understood as taking their life; the pain of the season and the joy that follows have been stated before.

BP 1.8.43

अन्यया सह यद्दुःखं सदसद्वा रहोगतम् ।
उत्कण्ठितं वा यत्किञ्चित्सपत्नीषु न तद्वसेत् ॥३

Whatever sorrow, good or bad, occurs in private with another wife, and whatever longing there is, should not be told among co-wives.

BP 1.8.44

यत्किञ्चिदन्यसम्बद्धमन्यथा कथितं मिथः ।
तस्य कुर्यादनिर्वेदमात्मनैव विचिन्तयेत् ॥४

Whatever concerning one is told differently to another between them, he should not be troubled by it; he should reflect on it himself.

BP 1.8.45

अन्योऽन्यमत्सराख्यानैर्न ता वाचापि भर्त्सयेत् ।
गुणदोषौ च विज्ञाय स्वयं कुर्यान्न निष्कलौ ॥५

He should not rebuke them by repeating accounts of one another's jealousy; he should discern their merits and faults himself and not regard either as without fault.

BP 1.8.46

वस्त्रालङ्कारभोज्यादौ तदपत्येष्वनुक्रमात् ।
मातृदोषाननादृत्य तुल्यदृष्टिः पिता भवेत् ॥६

In clothing, ornaments, food, and the like for their children, a father should follow the proper order and regard them equally, disregarding faults attributed to the mothers.

BP 1.8.47

अन्यस्यान्यगतैर्दोषैर्दूषणं न हि नीतिमत् ।
यत्तु तेषामपत्यं तु तत्तुल्यमुभयोरपि ॥७

It is not proper conduct to blame one for another's faults; their child belongs equally to both.

BP 1.8.48

प्रीतिं द्वेषमभिप्रायं शौचाशौचगतागमान् ।
बहिरन्तश्च जानीयाद्दास गूढचरैः सदा ॥८

He should always know through servants and secret observers their affections, aversions, intentions, and movements concerning purity and impurity, both within and without.

BP 1.8.49

आत्मानमपि विज्ञाय चित्तवृत्तेरनीश्वरम् ।
विश्वसेत कथं स्त्रीषु सर्वाविनयधामसु ॥९

Knowing himself to have no mastery over the movements of his mind, how could he trust women, all abodes of indiscipline?

BP 1.8.50

वृद्धदास्यः क्रमायाता धात्र्यश्च परिचारिकाः ।
तन्मातृपितृकाद्याश्च षण्डवृद्धाश्चरा मताः ॥.8.५०

Long-serving elderly maidservants, nurses, attendants, the wife's parents and other relatives, and elderly eunuchs are considered spies.

BP 1.8.51

विविधैस्तत्कथाख्यानैस्तुल्यशीलदयान्वितैः ।
प्रविश्यान्तरभिप्रायं विद्यात्काले प्रयोजितैः ॥१

Through various accounts introduced at suitable times by people of like character and compassion, he should learn their inner intentions.

BP 1.8.52

तेषु तेषु कथार्थेषु कथ्यमानेषु लक्षयेत् ।
मुखाकारादिभिर्लिङ्गैरभिप्रायं मनोगतम् ॥२

As those different stories are being told, he should discern the thought in the mind from signs such as the face.

BP 1.8.53

सीतारुन्धतिसम्बन्धैस्तथा शाकुन्तलादिभिः ।
सदसच्चरिताख्यानैर्भावं विद्यात्प्रवृत्तितः ॥३

Through stories concerning Sītā and Arundhatī, and Śakuntalā and others, recounting good and bad conduct, he should know disposition from the response.

BP 1.8.54

तद्दुष्टानामदुष्टेषु साधूनामितरेषु च ।
प्रीतिः कथाप्रबन्धेषु स्यात्सख्यं पुरुषेष्वितः ॥४

In stories, the wicked take delight in accounts of the virtuous, and the virtuous in accounts of others; from this, friendship with men is known.

BP 1.8.55

एवमागमदुष्टाभ्यामनुमित्या च तत्त्वतः ।
स्त्रीणां विदित्वाभिप्रायं वर्तेताशु यथोचितम् ॥५

Thus, truly knowing women's intentions through sacred tradition and inference, he should promptly act as is proper.

BP 1.8.56

स्त्रीभ्यो विप्रतिपन्नाभ्यः प्राणैरपि वियोजनम् ।
दृष्टं हि च यथा राज्ञामतो रक्षेत्प्रयत्नतः ॥६

Men have indeed been deprived of life by women who turned against them, as has been seen among kings; therefore he should guard diligently.

BP 1.8.57

वेण्या गूढेन शस्त्रेण हतो राजा शुभध्वजः ।
मेखलामणिना देव्या सौवीरश्च नराधिपः ॥७

King Śubhadhvaja was killed by a weapon hidden in a braid, and the ruler of Sauvīra by a jewel worn by his queen.

BP 1.8.58

भ्रात्रा देवीप्रयुक्तेन भद्रसेनो निपातितः ।
तथा पुत्रेण कारूषो घातितो दर्पणासिना ॥८

Bhadrasena was struck down by his brother at the queen's instigation; likewise, the ruler of Karūṣa was killed by his son with a mirror sword.

BP 1.8.59

द्वौ काशिराजौ वै वन्द्यौ चानन्दापुरयोषिता ।
विषं प्रयुज्य पञ्चत्वमानीतौ पूजितात्मकौ ॥९

Two honored kings of Kāśī were brought to death by a woman of Ānandapura who used poison against them.

BP 1.8.60

एवमादि महाभागा राजानो ब्राह्मणाश्च ह ।
स्त्रीभिर्यत्र निपात्यन्ते तत्रान्येष्विह का कथा ॥.8.६०

When great kings and Brahmins have been struck down by women in such cases, what need is there to speak of other people?

BP 1.8.61

तस्मान्नित्याप्रमत्तेन जाया रक्ष्याश्च नित्यशः ।
यथावदुपचर्याश्च गुणदोषानुरूपतः ॥१

Therefore, a wife should always be protected by one who is ever vigilant, and properly treated according to her qualities and faults.

BP 1.8.62

वैषम्यादुपचाराणां विकारैश्चानिमित्तजैः ।
विशेषेण सपत्नीकैरकस्माच्चापि वेदनैः ॥२

Unequal treatment, changes without cause, special distinctions among co-wives, and sudden distress;

BP 1.8.63

असम्भागे च वाग्दण्डपारुष्यादप्रसङ्गतः ।
प्रद्वेषो भर्तरि स्त्रीणां प्रकोपश्चापि जायते ॥३

when sharing is unequal and words of chastisement and harshness are used without cause, hatred of the husband and anger arise among women.

BP 1.8.64

ततश्चायाति वार्धक्यमुद्वोढुश्चापि शत्रुताम् ।
तस्मान्न तान्प्रयुञ्जीत दोषान्दारविनाशकान् ॥४

Then old age and hostility toward the husband also come; therefore he should not use the faults that destroy wives.

BP 1.8.65

न चैताः स्वकुलाचारमधर्मं वापि चाञ्जसा ।
न गुणांश्चाप्युपेक्षन्ते प्रकृत्या किमु पीडिताः ॥५

These women do not readily disregard family custom, wrongdoing, or virtues; how much less when they are distressed?

BP 1.8.66

सतीत्वे प्रायशः स्त्रीणां प्रदृष्टं कारणत्रयम् ।
परपुंसामसम्प्रीतिः प्रिये प्रीतिः स्वरक्षणे ॥६

Three causes of fidelity among women are generally seen: lack of affection for other men, affection for the beloved husband, and self-protection.

BP 1.8.67

तस्मात्सुरक्षिता नित्यमुपचारैर्यथोचितैः ।
सुभृता१ नित्यकर्माणः कर्तव्या योषितः सदा ॥७

Therefore women should always be securely protected with fitting attentions, well provided for, and kept engaged in their daily duties.

BP 1.8.68

उत्तमां सामदानाभ्यां मध्यमाभ्यां तु मध्यमाम् ।
पश्चिमाभ्यामुभाम्यां च अधमां सम्प्रसाधयेत् ॥८

A woman of superior standing should be won by conciliation and gifts, a middling one by the two middle means, and an inferior one by the last two.

BP 1.8.69

भेददण्डौ प्रयुज्यापि प्रागपत्याद्यपेक्षया ।
तच्छिष्टानां तदा पश्चात्सामदानप्रसाधने ॥९

Even when division and punishment are used, children and the like should be considered first; afterward, the remaining ones should be won by conciliation and gifts.

BP 1.8.70

यास्तु विध्वस्तचारित्रा भर्तुश्चाहितकारिकाः ।
त्याज्या एवं स्त्रियः सद्भिः२ कालकूटविषोपमाः ॥ ७०

Women whose character is ruined and who act against their husbands' welfare should be abandoned by good men, like deadly poison.

BP 1.8.71

इष्टाः कुलोद्गताः साध्व्यो विनीता भर्तृवत्सलाः ।
सर्वदा साधनीयास्ताः सम्प्रदायोत्तरोत्तरैः॥ ७१

The beloved women of good lineage, virtuous, disciplined, and affectionate toward their husbands should always be supported through ever-greater observances.

BP 1.8.72

एवमेव यथोद्दिष्टं स्त्रीवृत्तं योऽनुतिष्ठति ।
प्राप्नोत्येव स सम्पूर्णं त्रिवर्गं लोकसम्भवम् ॥ ७२

Whoever follows the conduct concerning women exactly as set forth here obtains fully the three aims of worldly life.
