Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 8: Householder’s Conduct and the Protection of Wives

BP 1.8.1

ब्रह्मोवाच
कर्तव्यं यद्गृहस्थेन तदिदानीं निबोधत ।
गदतो द्विजशार्दूल विस्तराच्छास्त्रतस्तथा ॥ १

Brahmā said: Now learn what a householder should do, O tiger among the twice-born, as I tell it at length according to the śāstras.

Word-for-word
ब्रह्मा उवाच (brahmā uvāca) — Brahmā said;
कर्तव्यम् यत् गृहस्थेन (kartavyam yat gṛhasthena) — what should be done by householder;
तत् इदानीम् निबोधत (tat idānīm nibodhata) — understand that now;
गदतः (gadataḥ) — as I explain;
द्विज-शार्दूल (dvija-śārdūla) — O tiger among twice-born;
विस्तरात् (vistarāt) — in detail;
शास्त्रतः तथा (śāstrataḥ tathā) — according to scripture
Transliteration
brahmovāca
kartavyaṃ yadgṛhasthena tadidānīṃ nibodhata |

BP 1.8.4

अमीषाम् रक्षणम् मतम् (amīṣām rakṣaṇam matam) — their protection is prescribed
