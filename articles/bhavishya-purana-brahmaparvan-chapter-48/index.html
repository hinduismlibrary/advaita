Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 48 — Sāmba’s Inquiry into Solar Worship

BP 1.48.1

शतानीक उवाच
विस्तराद्वद विप्रेन्द्र सप्तमीकल्पमुत्तमम् ।
महाभाग्यं च देवस्य भास्करस्य महात्मनः ॥

Śatānīka said, Tell me in detail, O best of Brahmins, the excellent seventh day rite and the great fortune of the great god Bhāskara.

शतानीक उवाच (śatānīka uvāca) — The devil said;
विस्तराद्वद विप्रेन्द्र सप्तमीकल्पमुत्तमम् (vistarādvada viprendra saptamīkalpamuttamam) — O best of brahmins tell me in detail the best of the seventh kalpa;
महाभाग्यं च देवस्य भास्करस्य महात्मनः (mahābhāgyaṃ ca devasya bhāskarasya mahātmanaḥ) — And great fortune to the great god Sun

BP 1.48.2

सुमन्तुरुवाच
अत्रैवाहुर्महात्मानः सम्वादं पुण्यमुत्तमम् ।
कृष्णेन सह सत्त्वेन स्वपुत्रेण महीपते ॥

Sumantu said, Here the great ones relate the sacred and excellent dialogue of Krishna with his virtuous son, O king.

सुमन्तुरुवाच (sumanturuvāca) — Sumantu said;
अत्रैवाहुर्महात्मानः सम्वादं पुण्यमुत्तमम् (atraivāhurmahātmānaḥ samvādaṃ puṇyamuttamam) — It is here that the great souls say that dialogue is the most sacred;
कृष्णेन सह सत्त्वेन स्वपुत्रेण महीपते (kṛṣṇena saha sattvena svaputreṇa mahīpate) — O lord of the earth with Krishna and his son Sattva

BP 1.48.3

भक्त्या प्रणम्य विधिवद्वासुदेवं जगद्गुरुम् ।
इहामुत्र हितं शांबः पप्रच्छ ज्ञानमुत्तमम् ॥

Having bowed duly with devotion to Vāsudeva, the teacher of the world, Sāmba asked the highest knowledge for welfare here and hereafter.

भक्त्या प्रणम्य विधिवद्वासुदेवं जगद्गुरुम् (bhaktyā praṇamya vidhivadvāsudevaṃ jagadgurum) — Having bowed down with devotion to Vāsudeva, the spiritual master of the universe, in accordance with rituals;
इहामुत्र हितं शांबः पप्रच्छ ज्ञानमुत्तमम् (ihāmutra hitaṃ śāṃbaḥ papraccha jñānamuttamam) — Sāmba inquired of the best of knowledge for the welfare of here and there

BP 1.48.4

जातो जन्तुः कथं दुःखैर्जन्मनीह न बाध्यते ।
प्राप्नोति विविधान्कामान्कथं च मधुसूदन ॥

Tell me how a being born here can be free from the sufferings of birth, and how he attains various desires, O Madhusūdana.

जातो जन्तुः कथं दुःखैर्जन्मनीह न बाध्यते (jāto jantuḥ kathaṃ duḥkhairjanmanīha na bādhyate) — How can a being born not be bound by sufferings in this world?;
प्राप्नोति विविधान्कामान्कथं च मधुसूदन (prāpnoti vividhānkāmānkathaṃ ca madhusūdana) — And how, O Madhusoodana, does he attain various desires?

BP 1.48.5

परत्र स्वर्गमाप्नोति सुखानि विविधानि च ।
अनुभूयोचितं कालं कथं मुक्तिमवाप्नुते ॥

Tell me how one attains heaven in the next world and various pleasures, and after experiencing them for the proper time, attains liberation.

परत्र स्वर्गमाप्नोति सुखानि विविधानि च (paratra svargamāpnoti sukhāni vividhāni ca) — In the next world he attains heaven and various pleasures;
अनुभूयोचितं कालं कथं मुक्तिमवाप्नुते (anubhūyocitaṃ kālaṃ kathaṃ muktimavāpnute) — How does one attain liberation at the right time to experience?

BP 1.48.6

दृष्ट्वैवं मम निर्वेदो जातो व्याधिर्जनार्दन ।
दृष्ट्वेमं जीविताशापि रोचते न हि मे क्षणम् ॥

Seeing this, dispassion has arisen in me, O Janārdana. Seeing this, even the hope of life does not please me for a moment.

दृष्ट्वैवं मम निर्वेदो जातो व्याधिर्जनार्दन (dṛṣṭvaivaṃ mama nirvedo jāto vyādhirjanārdana) — Seeing thus, I became detached from the disease, O Janardana;
दृष्ट्वेमं जीविताशापि रोचते न हि मे क्षणम् (dṛṣṭvemaṃ jīvitāśāpi rocate na hi me kṣaṇam) — I don't like the hope of survival when I see her for a moment

BP 1.48.7

किं त्वेवमकृतार्थोऽस्मि यन्मे प्राणा न यान्ति हि ।
संसारे न पतिष्यामि जराव्याधिसमन्विते ॥

Yet I am unfulfilled in this way, since my life does not depart. I shall not fall into the cycle of existence beset by old age and disease.

किं त्वेवमकृतार्थोऽस्मि यन्मे प्राणा न यान्ति हि (kiṃ tvevamakṛtārtho'smi yanme prāṇā na yānti hi) — Why am I so unfulfilled that my life is not going away;
संसारे न पतिष्यामि जराव्याधिसमन्विते (saṃsāre na patiṣyāmi jarāvyādhisamanvite) — I will not fall into the world of old age and disease

BP 1.48.8

येनोपायेन तन्मेऽद्य प्रसादं कुरु सुव्रत ।
आधिव्याधिविनिर्मुक्तो यथाहं स्यां तथा वद ॥

By whatever means, show me your favor today, O steadfast one. Tell me how I may be freed from afflictions and disease.

येनोपायेन तन्मेऽद्य प्रसादं कुरु सुव्रत (yenopāyena tanme'dya prasādaṃ kuru suvrata) — O virtuous one please do me a favor today by whatever means you can;
आधिव्याधिविनिर्मुक्तो यथाहं स्यां तथा वद (ādhivyādhivinirmukto yathāhaṃ syāṃ tathā vada) — Tell me how I can be free from mental illness

BP 1.48.9

वासुदेव उवाच
देवतायाः प्रसादोऽन्यः सर्वस्य परमो मतः ।
उपायः शाश्वतो नित्य इति मे निश्चिता मतिः ॥

Vāsudeva said, The favor of a deity is considered the highest thing for all. My settled understanding is that the means is eternal and perpetual.

वासुदेव उवाच (vāsudeva uvāca) — Vāsudeva said;
देवतायाः प्रसादोऽन्यः सर्वस्य परमो मतः (devatāyāḥ prasādo'nyaḥ sarvasya paramo mataḥ) — The grace of the goddess is considered to be the supreme of all;
उपायः शाश्वतो नित्य इति मे निश्चिता मतिः (upāyaḥ śāśvato nitya iti me niścitā matiḥ) — I am convinced that the remedy is eternal and everlasting

BP 1.48.10

अनुमानागमाद्यैश्च सम्यगुत्पादिता मया ।
कदाचिदन्यथा कर्तुं धीयते केनचित्क्वचित् ॥

I have properly established this through inference, scripture, and the like. Sometimes someone somewhere is inclined to make it otherwise.

अनुमानागमाद्यैश्च सम्यगुत्पादिता मया (anumānāgamādyaiśca samyagutpāditā mayā) — I have properly produced it by inference and inference;
कदाचिदन्यथा कर्तुं धीयते केनचित्क्वचित् (kadācidanyathā kartuṃ dhīyate kenacitkvacit) — Sometimes someone somewhere thinks of doing something else

BP 1.48.11

प्रसादो जायते तस्य सम्यगाराधनक्रिया ।
यदा तां च समुद्दिश्य कृता तद्वेदिना तथा ॥

His favor arises from the act of proper worship, when it is performed for that deity by one who knows the rite.

प्रसादो जायते तस्य सम्यगाराधनक्रिया (prasādo jāyate tasya samyagārādhanakriyā) — Grace is born of his act of right worship;
यदा तां च समुद्दिश्य कृता तद्वेदिना तथा (yadā tāṃ ca samuddiśya kṛtā tadvedinā tathā) — And when it was done with her in mind, so did the altar

BP 1.48.12

विशिष्टा देवता सम्यग्विशिष्टेनैव देहिना ।
आराधिता विशिष्टं च ददाति फलमीहितम् ॥

A particular deity, properly worshipped by a particular embodied being, indeed grants the particular desired fruit.

विशिष्टा देवता सम्यग्विशिष्टेनैव देहिना (viśiṣṭā devatā samyagviśiṣṭenaiva dehinā) — A distinct goddess with a perfectly distinct body;
आराधिता विशिष्टं च ददाति फलमीहितम् (ārādhitā viśiṣṭaṃ ca dadāti phalamīhitam) — Worshiped and distinguished, it gives the fruit of good

BP 1.48.13

शाम्ब उवाच
अस्तित्वे न च सन्देहः केषाञ्चिद्देवतां प्रति ।
नास्तीति निश्चयोऽन्येषां विशिष्टास्त्वं कथाः कुरुः ॥

Sāmba said, Some have no doubt about the existence of deities, while others are certain that they do not exist. You, however, tell me about them in detail.

शाम्ब उवाच (śāmba uvāca) — Sāmba said;
अस्तित्वे न च सन्देहः केषाञ्चिद्देवतां प्रति (astitve na ca sandehaḥ keṣāñciddevatāṃ prati) — There is no doubt about the existence of some gods;
नास्तीति निश्चयोऽन्येषां विशिष्टास्त्वं कथाः कुरुः (nāstīti niścayo'nyeṣāṃ viśiṣṭāstvaṃ kathāḥ kuruḥ) — You tell stories that are unique to others that are certainly not there

BP 1.48.14

वासुदेव उवाच
सिद्धं तु देवतास्तित्वमागमेषु बहुष्वथ ।
प्रमाणमागमो यस्य तस्यास्तित्वं च विद्यते ॥

Vāsudeva said, The existence of deities is established in many authoritative texts. For that whose authority is scripture, its existence is established.

वासुदेव उवाच (vāsudeva uvāca) — Vāsudeva said;
सिद्धं तु देवतास्तित्वमागमेषु बहुष्वथ (siddhaṃ tu devatāstitvamāgameṣu bahuṣvatha) — But the existence of the gods is proved in many comings;
प्रमाणमागमो यस्य तस्यास्तित्वं च विद्यते (pramāṇamāgamo yasya tasyāstitvaṃ ca vidyate) — and the existence of that which has the coming of proof

BP 1.48.15

अनुमानेन वाप्यद्य तदस्तित्वं प्रसाध्यते ।
प्रमाणमस्ति यस्येदं सिद्धा यस्येह चास्तिता ॥

Their existence is also established by inference. When there is evidence for a thing, its existence here is established.

अनुमानेन वाप्यद्य तदस्तित्वं प्रसाध्यते (anumānena vāpyadya tadastitvaṃ prasādhyate) — Even by inference, its existence is proved today;
प्रमाणमस्ति यस्येदं सिद्धा यस्येह चास्तिता (pramāṇamasti yasyedaṃ siddhā yasyeha cāstitā) — There is proof that this is perfect and that it exists here

BP 1.48.16

प्रत्यक्षेणापि चास्तित्वं देवतायां प्रसाध्यते ।
तच्चावश्यं प्रमाणं च दृष्टं सर्वशरीरिणाम् ॥

The existence of a deity is also established by direct perception. That indeed is a means of knowledge, seen by all embodied beings.

प्रत्यक्षेणापि चास्तित्वं देवतायां प्रसाध्यते (pratyakṣeṇāpi cāstitvaṃ devatāyāṃ prasādhyate) — And even by direct existence is attained in the deity;
तच्चावश्यं प्रमाणं च दृष्टं सर्वशरीरिणाम् (taccāvaśyaṃ pramāṇaṃ ca dṛṣṭaṃ sarvaśarīriṇām) — That is certainly the proof seen by all embodied beings

BP 1.48.17

यदि नामा विविक्तास्तु तिर्यग्योनिगता अपि ।
नोत्पद्यते तथा ह्यस्ति व्यवहारो यथा स्थितः ॥

Even among those born in animal forms, distinct names occur. The established practice of using them does not arise anew.

यदि नामा विविक्तास्तु तिर्यग्योनिगता अपि (yadi nāmā viviktāstu tiryagyonigatā api) — If the names are separate, even if they are in the animal world;
नोत्पद्यते तथा ह्यस्ति व्यवहारो यथा स्थितः (notpadyate tathā hyasti vyavahāro yathā sthitaḥ) — It does not arise, for there is behavior as it stands

BP 1.48.18

शाम्ब उवाच
प्रत्येक्षेणोपलभ्यन्ते सम्यग्वै यदि देवताः ।
अनुमानागमाभ्यां च तदर्थं .न प्रयोजनम् ॥

Sāmba said, If the deities are properly perceived individually, there is no need for inference and scripture for that purpose.

शाम्ब उवाच (śāmba uvāca) — Sāmba said;
प्रत्येक्षेणोपलभ्यन्ते सम्यग्वै यदि देवताः (pratyekṣeṇopalabhyante samyagvai yadi devatāḥ) — If the gods are properly perceived by each sight;
अनुमानागमाभ्यां च तदर्थं .न प्रयोजनम् (anumānāgamābhyāṃ ca tadarthaṃ .na prayojanam) — and by inference and inference for that purpose .no purpose

BP 1.48.19

वासुदेव उवाच
प्रत्यक्षेणोपलभ्यन्ते न सर्वा देवताः क्वचित् ।
अनुमानागमैर्गम्याः सन्ति चान्याः सहस्रशः ॥

Vāsudeva said, Not all deities are perceived directly everywhere. Thousands of others are known through inference and scripture.

वासुदेव उवाच (vāsudeva uvāca) — Vāsudeva said;
प्रत्यक्षेणोपलभ्यन्ते न सर्वा देवताः क्वचित् (pratyakṣeṇopalabhyante na sarvā devatāḥ kvacit) — Not all gods are directly perceived anywhere;
अनुमानागमैर्गम्याः सन्ति चान्याः सहस्रशः (anumānāgamairgamyāḥ santi cānyāḥ sahasraśaḥ) — There are thousands of others accessible by inference

BP 1.48.20

शाम्ब उवाच
या चाक्षगोचरा काचिद्विशिष्टेष्टफलप्रदा ।
तामेवादौ ममाचक्ष्व कथयिष्यस्यथापराम् ॥

Sāmba said, Tell me first of whichever one is perceptible to the senses and grants a particular desired result. Then you may tell me of another.

शाम्ब उवाच (śāmba uvāca) — Sāmba said;
या चाक्षगोचरा काचिद्विशिष्टेष्टफलप्रदा (yā cākṣagocarā kācidviśiṣṭeṣṭaphalapradā) — which is visible to the eye and gives some distinct desired results;
तामेवादौ ममाचक्ष्व कथयिष्यस्यथापराम् (tāmevādau mamācakṣva kathayiṣyasyathāparām) — Tell me about that one and you will tell me about the other

BP 1.48.21

वासुदेव उवाच
प्रत्यक्षं देवता सूर्यो जगच्चक्षुर्दिवाकरः ।
तस्मादभ्यधिका काचिद्देवता नास्ति शाश्वती ॥

Vāsudeva said, The Sun is the manifest deity, the eye of the world, the maker of the day. Therefore no eternal deity is superior to him.

वासुदेव उवाच (vāsudeva uvāca) — Vāsudeva said;
प्रत्यक्षं देवता सूर्यो जगच्चक्षुर्दिवाकरः (pratyakṣaṃ devatā sūryo jagaccakṣurdivākaraḥ) — The sun is the direct deity and the sun is the eye of the universe;
तस्मादभ्यधिका काचिद्देवता नास्ति शाश्वती (tasmādabhyadhikā kāciddevatā nāsti śāśvatī) — There is no god greater than that eternal

BP 1.48.22

यस्मादिदं जगज्जातं लयं यास्यति यत्र च ।
कृतादिलक्षणः कालः स्मृतः साक्षाद्दिवाकरः ॥

From whom this world has arisen and into whom it will pass at dissolution, time marked by the Kṛta and other ages is known as the visible Sun.

यस्मादिदं जगज्जातं लयं यास्यति यत्र च (yasmādidaṃ jagajjātaṃ layaṃ yāsyati yatra ca) — From whom this world was born and where it will go to dissolution;
कृतादिलक्षणः कालः स्मृतः साक्षाद्दिवाकरः (kṛtādilakṣaṇaḥ kālaḥ smṛtaḥ sākṣāddivākaraḥ) — The time characterized by the beginning of the deed is remembered as the sun itself

BP 1.48.23

ग्रहनक्षत्रयोगाश्च राशयः करणानि च ।
आदित्या वसवो रुद्रा अश्विनौ वायवोऽनलः ॥

Planetary conjunctions, constellations, zodiac signs and lunar days, the Ādityas, Vasus, Rudras, the two Aśvins, the winds and fire.

ग्रहनक्षत्रयोगाश्च राशयः करणानि च (grahanakṣatrayogāśca rāśayaḥ karaṇāni ca) — The conjunctions of the planets and stars and the signs of the zodiac;
आदित्या वसवो रुद्रा अश्विनौ वायवोऽनलः (ādityā vasavo rudrā aśvinau vāyavo'nalaḥ) — The suns, the Vasus, the Rudras, the Ashvins, the winds and the fire

BP 1.48.24

शक्रः प्रजापतिः सर्वे भूर्भुवःस्वस्तथैव च ।
लोकाः सर्वे नगा नागाः सरितः सागरास्तथा ॥
भूतग्रामस्य सर्वस्य स्वयं हेतुर्दिवाकरः ॥

Indra, Prajāpati, all the earth, atmosphere, and heaven, all worlds, mountains, serpents, rivers, and oceans. The Sun himself is the cause of the whole multitude of beings.

शक्रः प्रजापतिः सर्वे भूर्भुवःस्वस्तथैव च (śakraḥ prajāpatiḥ sarve bhūrbhuvaḥsvastathaiva ca) — Indra, the Creator, all the earth, the earth, the heavens and so on;
लोकाः सर्वे नगा नागाः सरितः सागरास्तथा (lokāḥ sarve nagā nāgāḥ saritaḥ sāgarāstathā) — All the worlds, mountains, serpents, rivers and seas;
भूतग्रामस्य सर्वस्य स्वयं हेतुर्दिवाकरः (bhūtagrāmasya sarvasya svayaṃ heturdivākaraḥ) — The Sun itself is the cause of the whole world of ghosts

BP 1.48.25

अस्येच्छया जगत्सर्वमुत्पन्नं सचराचरम् ।
स्थितं प्रवर्तते चैव स्वार्थे चानुप्रवर्तते ॥

By his will the whole world, moving and unmoving, is produced, stands, and operates, and proceeds according to its own purpose.

अस्येच्छया जगत्सर्वमुत्पन्नं सचराचरम् (asyecchayā jagatsarvamutpannaṃ sacarācaram) — By His will the whole universe, moving and unmoving, was created;
स्थितं प्रवर्तते चैव स्वार्थे चानुप्रवर्तते (sthitaṃ pravartate caiva svārthe cānupravartate) — It is established and acts and acts in selfishness

BP 1.48.26

प्रसादादस्य लोकोऽयं चेष्टमानः प्रदृश्यते ।
अस्मिन्नभ्युदिते सर्वमुदेदस्तमिते सति ॥
अस्तं यातीत्यदृश्येन किमेतत्कथ्यते मया ॥

By his favor this world is seen in motion. When he rises, everything rises, and when he sets, everything sets. He goes unseen at setting. What need is there for me to say this.

प्रसादादस्य लोकोऽयं चेष्टमानः प्रदृश्यते (prasādādasya loko'yaṃ ceṣṭamānaḥ pradṛśyate) — By his grace this world appears to be moving;
अस्मिन्नभ्युदिते सर्वमुदेदस्तमिते सति (asminnabhyudite sarvamudedastamite sati) — In this rising, everything rises and sets;
अस्तं यातीत्यदृश्येन किमेतत्कथ्यते मया । ।२ (astaṃ yātītyadṛśyena kimetatkathyate mayā | |2) — What is this I’m talking about when I can see that it’s going to set?

BP 1.48.27

तस्मादतः परं नास्ति न भूतं न भविष्यति ।
यो वै वेदेषु सर्वेषु परमात्मेति गीयते ॥

Therefore there is none beyond him, none that has been or will be. He is sung in all the Vedas as the Supreme Self.

तस्मादतः परं नास्ति न भूतं न भविष्यति (tasmādataḥ paraṃ nāsti na bhūtaṃ na bhaviṣyati) — There is nothing beyond that, neither past nor future;
यो वै वेदेषु सर्वेषु परमात्मेति गीयते (yo vai vedeṣu sarveṣu paramātmeti gīyate) — He who is sung in all the Vedas as the Supreme Self

BP 1.48.28

इतिहासपुराणेषु अन्तरात्मेति गीयते ।
बाह्यात्मैति सुषुम्णास्थः स्वप्नस्थो जाग्रतः स्थितः ॥

In histories and sacred accounts, he is praised as the inner self. He is called the outer self, abiding in the central channel, present in dream and established in waking.

इतिहासपुराणेषु अन्तरात्मेति गीयते (itihāsapurāṇeṣu antarātmeti gīyate) — In the Puranas of history it is sung as the inner self;
बाह्यात्मैति सुषुम्णास्थः स्वप्नस्थो जाग्रतः स्थितः (bāhyātmaiti suṣumṇāsthaḥ svapnastho jāgrataḥ sthitaḥ) — The external self is in the Sushumna, in the dream, in the awake state

BP 1.48.29

अस्तं यातीत्यदृष्टेन किमेतत्कथ्यते मया ।
तस्मादतः परं नास्ति न भूतं न भविष्यति ॥

When he sets, he is unseen. I need say nothing more about this. Therefore there is none beyond him, nothing past or future.

अस्तं यातीत्यदृष्टेन किमेतत्कथ्यते मया (astaṃ yātītyadṛṣṭena kimetatkathyate mayā) — What am I saying when I have not seen the setting sun;
तस्मादतः परं नास्ति न भूतं न भविष्यति (tasmādataḥ paraṃ nāsti na bhūtaṃ na bhaviṣyati) — There is nothing beyond that, neither past nor future

BP 1.48.30

यन्न वाह इति ख्यातः प्रेरकः सर्वदेहिनाम् ।
नानेन रहितं किञ्चिद्भूतमस्ति चराचरम् ॥

He is known as the Bearer, the impeller of all embodied beings. There is no moving or unmoving being that exists without him.

यन्न वाह इति ख्यातः प्रेरकः सर्वदेहिनाम् (yanna vāha iti khyātaḥ prerakaḥ sarvadehinām) — That which is not known as Vaha is the motivator of all embodied beings;
नानेन रहितं किञ्चिद्भूतमस्ति चराचरम् (nānena rahitaṃ kiñcidbhūtamasti carācaram) — There is nothing moving or non-moving without various beings

BP 1.48.31

यो वेदैर्वेदविद्भिश्च विस्तरेणेह शक्यते ।
वक्तुं वर्षशतैर्नासौ शक्यः संक्षेपतो मया ॥

The Vedas and those who know the Vedas can speak of him at length for hundreds of years. I cannot describe him briefly.

यो वेदैर्वेदविद्भिश्च विस्तरेणेह शक्यते (yo vedairvedavidbhiśca vistareṇeha śakyate) — which can be explained here in detail by the Vedas and the Vedic scholars;
वक्तुं वर्षशतैर्नासौ शक्यः संक्षेपतो मया (vaktuṃ varṣaśatairnāsau śakyaḥ saṃkṣepato mayā) — I can't tell you that in a nutshell in hundreds of years

BP 1.48.32

तस्माद्गुणाकरः ख्यातः सर्वत्रायं दिवाकरः ।
सर्वेशः सर्वकर्तायं सर्वभर्तायमव्ययः ॥

Therefore this Sun is known everywhere as a mine of qualities. He is lord of all, doer of all, supporter of all, and imperishable.

१तस्माद्गुणाकरः ख्यातः सर्वत्रायं दिवाकरः (1tasmādguṇākaraḥ khyātaḥ sarvatrāyaṃ divākaraḥ) — 1Therefore this sun is known everywhere as the source of virtue;
सर्वेशः सर्वकर्तायं सर्वभर्तायमव्ययः (sarveśaḥ sarvakartāyaṃ sarvabhartāyamavyayaḥ) — The Lord of all, the doer of all, the sustainer of all, is inexhaustible

BP 1.48.33

जाता मत्स्यादयः सम्यग्गतिमन्तो महेश्वरात् ।
मण्डलव्यतिरिक्तं च जानामि परमार्थतः ॥

Fish and other creatures, endowed with movement, are born from the Great Lord. In the highest truth, I know him to be beyond the solar disk.

जाता मत्स्यादयः सम्यग्गतिमन्तो महेश्वरात् (jātā matsyādayaḥ samyaggatimanto maheśvarāt) — Fish and others were born from the Lord, moving perfectly;
मण्डलव्यतिरिक्तं च जानामि परमार्थतः (maṇḍalavyatiriktaṃ ca jānāmi paramārthataḥ) — And I know it's beyond the circle in the ultimate sense

BP 1.48.34

तथास्य मण्डलं कृत्वा यो ह्येनमुपतिष्ठते ।
प्रातः सायं च मध्याह्ने स याति परमां गतिम् ॥

Whoever constructs his solar disk and worships him morning, evening, and at midday attains the highest goal.

तथास्य मण्डलं कृत्वा२ यो ह्येनमुपतिष्ठते (tathāsya maṇḍalaṃ kṛtvā2 yo hyenamupatiṣṭhate) — and whoever makes a circle of it2 and offers it;
प्रातः सायं च मध्याह्ने स याति परमां गतिम् (prātaḥ sāyaṃ ca madhyāhne sa yāti paramāṃ gatim) — In the morning in the evening and at noon he attains the supreme destination

BP 1.48.35

किं पुनर्मण्डलस्थं यो जपते परमार्थतः ।
विविधाः सिद्धयस्तस्य भवन्ति न तदद्भुतम् ॥

As for one who recites in the solar disk in the highest truth, various attainments arise for him, and that is no wonder.

किं पुनर्मण्डलस्थं यो जपते परमार्थतः (kiṃ punarmaṇḍalasthaṃ yo japate paramārthataḥ) — What about the one who chants in the circle in the supreme sense?;
विविधाः सिद्धयस्तस्य भवन्ति न तदद्भुतम् (vividhāḥ siddhayastasya bhavanti na tadadbhutam) — It is not surprising that he attains various perfections

BP 1.48.36

मण्डले च स्थितं देवं देहे चैनं व्यवस्थितम् ।
स्वबुद्ध्यैवमसम्मूढो य- पश्यति स पश्यति ॥

The one who, undeluded by his own understanding, sees the deity situated in the solar disk and established in the body, truly sees.

मण्डले च स्थितं देवं देहे चैनं व्यवस्थितम् (maṇḍale ca sthitaṃ devaṃ dehe cainaṃ vyavasthitam) — And the god is situated in the circle and He is situated in the body;
स्वबुद्ध्यैवमसम्मूढो य- पश्यति स पश्यति (svabuddhyaivamasammūḍho ya- paśyati sa paśyati) — He who sees, thus undeluded by his own intellect, sees

BP 1.48.37

ध्यात्वैवं पूजयेद्यस्तु जपेद्यो जुहुयाच्च यः ।
स सर्वान्प्राप्नुयात्कामान्गच्छेद्धर्मध्वजं तथा ॥

Whoever worships after meditating thus, whoever recites, and whoever offers oblations should attain all desired aims and go to the Banner of Righteousness.

ध्यात्वैवं पूजयेद्यस्तु जपेद्यो जुहुयाच्च यः (dhyātvaivaṃ pūjayedyastu japedyo juhuyācca yaḥ) — He who meditates and worships in this way, he who chants and he who offers oblations;
स सर्वान्प्राप्नुयात्कामान्गच्छेद्धर्मध्वजं तथा (sa sarvānprāpnuyātkāmāngaccheddharmadhvajaṃ tathā) — He will attain all his desires and go to the banner of righteousness

BP 1.48.38

तस्मात्त्वमिह दुःखानामन्तं कर्तुं यदीच्छसि ।
इहामुत्र च भोगानां भुक्तिं मुक्तिं च शाश्वतीम् ॥

Therefore, if you wish to end suffering here and attain enjoyment here and hereafter, as well as lasting liberation, worship the Sun.

तस्मात्त्वमिह दुःखानामन्तं कर्तुं यदीच्छसि (tasmāttvamiha duḥkhānāmantaṃ kartuṃ yadīcchasi) — Therefore if you wish to put an end to suffering here;
इहामुत्र च भोगानां भुक्तिं मुक्तिं च शाश्वतीम् (ihāmutra ca bhogānāṃ bhuktiṃ muktiṃ ca śāśvatīm) — Enjoyment of pleasures here and hereafter and eternal liberation

BP 1.48.39

आराधयार्कमर्कस्थो मन्त्रैरिह तदात्मनि ।
अङ्गैर्वृतं वृते चैव स्थाने शास्त्रेण शोधिते ॥

Worship him with mantras, established in the solar disk and in his own self, surrounded by his attendant forms, in an enclosure purified by scripture.

आराधयार्कमर्कस्थो मन्त्रैरिह तदात्मनि (ārādhayārkamarkastho mantrairiha tadātmani) — Worship the sun in the sun with mantras here in that Self;
अङ्गैर्वृतं वृते चैव स्थाने शास्त्रेण शोधिते (aṅgairvṛtaṃ vṛte caiva sthāne śāstreṇa śodhite) — It is covered with limbs and covered in a place purified by the scriptures

BP 1.48.40

कवचेन च सङ्गुस्ते सर्वतोऽस्त्रेण रक्षिते ।
एवं प्राप्स्यसि यत्नेन सर्वदा फलमीप्सितम् ॥

Joined with the armor and protected all around by weapons, you will always obtain the desired result through effort.

कवचेन च सङ्गुस्ते सर्वतोऽस्त्रेण रक्षिते (kavacena ca saṅguste sarvato'streṇa rakṣite) — They were protected by armor and weapons from all sides;
एवं प्राप्स्यसि यत्नेन सर्वदा फलमीप्सितम् (evaṃ prāpsyasi yatnena sarvadā phalamīpsitam) — Thus you will always get the desired results with your efforts

BP 1.48.41

दुःखमाध्यात्मिकं नेह तथा चैवाधिभौतिकम् ।
आधिदैविकमत्युग्रं न भविष्यति ते सदा ॥

Affliction arising within oneself, affliction arising from other beings, and the extremely fierce affliction arising from divine causes will never befall you.

दुःखमाध्यात्मिकं नेह तथा चैवाधिभौतिकम् (duḥkhamādhyātmikaṃ neha tathā caivādhibhautikam) — Suffering is not spiritual here, nor is it superphysical;
आधिदैविकमत्युग्रं न भविष्यति ते सदा (ādhidaivikamatyugraṃ na bhaviṣyati te sadā) — They will not always be supernaturally fierce

BP 1.48.42

न भयं विद्यते तेषां प्रपन्ना ये दिवाकरम् ।
इहामुत्र सुखं तेषामच्छिद्रं जायते सुखम् ॥

There is no fear for those who take refuge in the Sun. Happiness here and hereafter is theirs, happiness without interruption arises.

न भयं विद्यते तेषां प्रपन्ना ये दिवाकरम् (na bhayaṃ vidyate teṣāṃ prapannā ye divākaram) — There is no fear for those who take refuge in the Sun;
इहामुत्र सुखं तेषामच्छिद्रं जायते सुखम् (ihāmutra sukhaṃ teṣāmacchidraṃ jāyate sukham) — Happiness here and there is born of their flawless happiness

BP 1.48.43

सूर्येणेदं ममोद्दिष्टं साक्षाद्यज्ज्ञानमुत्तमम् ।
आराधितेन विधिवत्कालेन बहुना तथा ॥

This highest knowledge was declared to me directly by the Sun, after he had been worshipped properly for a long time.

सूर्येणेदं ममोद्दिष्टं साक्षाद्यज्ज्ञानमुत्तमम् (sūryeṇedaṃ mamoddiṣṭaṃ sākṣādyajjñānamuttamam) — This is the best knowledge directly taught to me by the Sun;
आराधितेन विधिवत्कालेन बहुना तथा (ārādhitena vidhivatkālena bahunā tathā) — and by much of the worshiped ritual time

BP 1.48.44

प्राप्यते परमं स्थानं यत्र धर्मध्वजः स्थितः ।
एतत्संक्षिप्तमुद्दिष्टं क्षिप्रसिद्धिकरं परम् ॥
यथा नान्यदतोऽस्तीति स्वयं तूर्येण भाषितम् ॥

The highest state is attained, where the Banner of Righteousness stands. This has been briefly declared, supreme and bringing swift attainment. Tūrya himself said that nothing else exists beyond this.

प्राप्यते परमं स्थानं यत्र धर्मध्वजः स्थितः (prāpyate paramaṃ sthānaṃ yatra dharmadhvajaḥ sthitaḥ) — The supreme place where the banner of righteousness stands is reached;
एतत्संक्षिप्तमुद्दिष्टं क्षिप्रसिद्धिकरं परम् (etatsaṃkṣiptamuddiṣṭaṃ kṣiprasiddhikaraṃ param) — This is briefly described and supremely quick to attain;
यथा नान्यदतोऽस्तीति स्वयं तूर्येण भाषितम् (yathā nānyadato'stīti svayaṃ tūryeṇa bhāṣitam) — As the trumpet itself has spoken, there is no other

BP 1.48.45

उपायोऽयं समाख्यातस्तव संक्षेपतस्त्विह ।
यस्मात्परतरो नास्ति हितोपायः शरीरिणाम् ॥

This method has been briefly explained to you here. There is no better means of welfare for embodied beings than this.

उपायोऽयं समाख्यातस्तव संक्षेपतस्त्विह (upāyo'yaṃ samākhyātastava saṃkṣepatastviha) — This remedy has been briefly explained to you here;
यस्मात्परतरो नास्ति हितोपायः शरीरिणाम् (yasmātparataro nāsti hitopāyaḥ śarīriṇām) — There is no other means of welfare for embodied beings than this
