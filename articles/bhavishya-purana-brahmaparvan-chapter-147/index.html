Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 147 — Characteristics of the Bhojakas

BP 1.147.1

Vāsudeva said: Here, O mighty-armed one, the god Bhāskara, worshipped by the gods, was asked by Aruṇa: Who are the dear Bhojakas?
