Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 127 — Sāmba’s Healing and Solar Grace

BP 1.127.1

शतानीक उवाच। कथमाराधितः सूर्यः साम्बेनामिततेजसा। विमुक्तस्तु कथं रोगैर्ब्रूहि मां द्विजसत्तम ॥

Śatānīka said. How was the Sun worshipped by Sāmba of immeasurable radiance, and how was he freed from disease. Tell me, best of the twice born.

BP 1.127.2

सुमंतुरुवाच। साधु पृष्टो'स्मि राजेन्द्र शृणु साम्बकथां पुरा। विस्ताराद्वच्मि ते सर्वां कथां पापविमोचिनीम ॥

Sumantu said. You have asked well, O king. Hear the ancient story of Sāmba. I shall tell you the entire story in detail, the story that frees from sin.

BP 1.127.3

पुरा संश्रुत्य माहात्म्यं भास्करस्य स नारदात। विनयाद उपसङ्गम्य वचः पितरमब्रवीत ॥

Having formerly heard of the greatness of Bhāskara from Nārada, he respectfully approached his father and spoke.

BP 1.127.4

कश्मलेनाभिभूतो'स्मि मलेन व्याधिनाच्युत। वैद्यैरोषधिभिश्चापि न शान्तिर्मम विद्यते ॥

I am overcome by impurity, defilement, and disease, O Acyuta. I find no relief from physicians or medicines.

BP 1.127.5

वनं गच्छामि भगवन्ननुज्ञां दातुमर्हसि। शिवेन पुण्डरीकाक्ष ध्याय मां पुरुषोत्तम ॥

I am going to the forest, Lord. You should grant me permission. Think of me kindly, O lotus eyed one, O Supreme Person.

BP 1.127.6

अनुज्ञातः स कृष्णेन सिन्धोरुत्तरकूलतः। गत्वा सन्तारयामास चन्द्रभागां महानदीम ॥

Having received permission from Kṛṣṇa, he went from the northern bank of the Sindhu and crossed the great river Candrabhāgā.

BP 1.127.7

ततो मित्रवनम गत्वा तीर्थं त्रैलोक्यविश्रुतम। उपवासपरः साम्बः शुष्को धमनिसन्ततः ॥

Then he went to Mitravana, a sacred ford renowned in the three worlds. Sāmba devoted himself to fasting, emaciated, with every vein visible.

BP 1.127.8

आराधनार्थं सूर्यस्य गुह्यं स्तोत्रं जजाप ह। वेदैश्चतुर्भिः समितं पुराणाश्रयबृंहितम ॥

For worship of the Sun, he recited a secret hymn, joined with the four Vedas and magnified by support from the Purāṇas.

BP 1.127.9

यदेत्न्मअण्डलं शुक्लं दिव्यं ह्यजरमव्ययम। युक्तं मनोजवैरश्वैर्हारीतैर्ब्रह्मवादिभिः ॥

This bright, divine, ageless, imperishable orb is yoked to horses swift as thought, to the Hārītas, speakers of Brahman.

BP 1.127.10

आदिरेष हि भूतानामादित्य इति संज्ञितः। त्रैलोक्यचक्षुरेवात्र परमात्मा प्रजापतिः ॥

He is the beginning of beings and is named Āditya. Here he is the eye of the three worlds, the Supreme Self, and the lord of creatures.

BP 1.127.11

एष वै मण्डले ह्यस्मिन्पुरुषो दीप्यते महान। एष विष्णुरचिन्त्यात्मा ब्रह्मा चैष पितामहः ॥

This great Person shines in this orb. He is Viṣṇu, whose nature is inconceivable, and he is Brahmā, the Grandfather.

BP 1.127.12

रुद्रो महेन्द्रो वरुण आकाशं पृथिवी जलम। वायुः शशाङ्कः पर्जन्यो धनाध्यक्षो विभावसुः ॥

He is Rudra, great Indra, Varuṇa, space, earth, water, wind, the Moon, rain, the lord of wealth, and Fire.

BP 1.127.13

य एष मण्डले ह्यस्मिन्पुरुषो दीप्यते महान। एकः साक्षान्महादेवो वृत्रमण्डनिभः सदा ॥

This great Person shines in this orb. He alone is directly the Great God, always like the circle of Vṛtra.

BP 1.127.14

कालो ह्येष महाबाहुर निबोधोत्पत्तिलक्षणः। य एष मण्डले ह्यस्मिंस्तेजोभिः पूरयन्म अहीम ॥

He is Time, O mighty armed one. Understand the sign of his origin, as he fills the earth with his radiances in this orb.

BP 1.127.15

भ्राम्यते ह्यव्यवच्छिन्नो वातैर्यो'मृतलक्षणः। नातः परतरं किञ्चित्तेजसा विद्यते क्वचित ॥

He revolves without interruption, borne by the winds, marked by immortality. Nothing in radiance exists anywhere higher than he.

BP 1.127.16

पुष्णाति सर्वभूतानि एष एव सुधामृतैः। अन्तस्थान्म्लेच्छजातीयांस्तिर्यग्योनिगतानपि ॥

He alone nourishes all beings with nectar, even those within, those among foreign peoples, and those born in animal wombs.

BP 1.127.17

कारुण्यात्सर्वभूतानि पासि त्वं च विभावसो। श्वित्­रकुष्ठ्यन्धबधिरान्पङ्गूंश्चापि तथा विभो ॥

Through compassion you protect all beings, O Vibhāvasu, those with white leprosy, those with leprosy, the blind, the deaf, and the lame, O all pervading one.

BP 1.127.18

प्रपन्नवत्सलो देव कुरुते नीरुजो भवान। चक्रमण्डलमग्रांश्च निर्धनाल्­पायुषस्तथा ॥

O god, affectionate toward those who seek refuge, you make those with circular skin patches, the poor, and the short lived free from disease.

BP 1.127.19

प्रत्यक्षदर्शी त्वं देव समुद्धरसि लीलया। का मे शक्तिः स्तवैस्तो­तु­मार्तो'हं रोगपीडितः ॥

You are the visible witness, O god, and raise beings up in play. What strength have I, suffering and afflicted by disease, to praise you with hymns.

BP 1.127.20

स्तूयसे त्वं सदा देवैर्ब्रह्मविष्णुशिवादिभिः। महेन्द्रसिद्धगन्धर्वैरप्सरोभिः सगुह्यकैः ॥

You are always praised by the gods, by Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Śiva, and others, by great Indra, perfected beings, celestial musicians, heavenly nymphs, and hidden spirits.

BP 1.127.21

स्तुतिभिः किं पवित्रैर्वा तव देव समीरितैः। यस्य ते ऋग्यजुः साम्नां त्रितयं मण्डलस्थितम ॥

What need have you of pure praises spoken by the gods, O god, when the triad of the Ṛg, Yajus, and Sāma Vedas is situated in your orb.

BP 1.127.22

ध्यानिनां त्वं परं ध्यानं मोक्षद्वारं च मोक्षिणाम। अनन्ततेजसाक्षोभ्योह्य अचिन्त्याव्यक्तनीष्कलः ॥

You are the highest meditation for meditators and the gateway of liberation for those seeking release, of endless radiance, unshaken, inconceivable, unmanifest, and without parts.

BP 1.127.23

यदयम व्याहतः किञ्चित्स्तोत्रे'स्मिञ्जगतः पतिः। आर्तिं भक्तिं च विज्ञाय तत्सर्वं ज्ञातुमर्हसि ॥

If anything has been spoken wrongly in this hymn, O Lord of the world, knowing my distress and devotion, you should know all of it.

BP 1.127.24

तमुवाच ततः सूर्यः प्रीत्या जाम्बवतीसुतम। प्रीतो'स्मि तपसा वत्से ब्रूहि तन्मां यदिच्छसि ॥

Then the Sun spoke affectionately to the son of Jāmbavatī. I am pleased with your penance, child. Tell me what you wish.

BP 1.127.25

साम्ब उवाच। यदि प्रसन्नो भगवानेष एव वरो मम। भक्तिर्भवतु मे'त्यर्थं त्वयि देव सनातन ॥

Sāmba said. If the Blessed Lord is pleased, this alone is my boon, may my devotion to you be exceedingly great, O eternal god.

BP 1.127.26

श्रीसूर्य उवाच। भूयस्तुष्टो'स्मि भद्रं ते वरं वरय सुव्रत। स द्वितीयं वरं वव्रे तदैव वरदं विभुम ॥

The Sun said. I am even more pleased, may good be yours. Ask for a boon, O steadfast one. He then asked the great boon giver for a second boon.

BP 1.127.27

मलः शरीरसंस्थो मे त्वत्प्रसादात्प्रणश्यतु। येन मे शुद्धमखिलं वपुर्भवतु गोपते ॥

May the defilement lodged in my body perish through your grace, so that my entire body may become pure, O lord of cows.

BP 1.127.28

सुमंतुरुवाच। स तथास्त्विति तेनोक्तो भास्करेण महात्मना। तां मुमोच रुजं साम्बो देहात्त्वचमिवोरगः ॥

Sumantu said. When the great souled Bhāskara said, So be it, Sāmba cast off that disease from his body as a serpent casts its skin.

BP 1.127.29

ततो रूपेण दिव्येन रूपवानभवत्पुनः। प्रणम्य शिरसा देवं पुरतो'वस्थितो'भवत ॥

Then he became beautiful again in divine form, bowed his head to the god, and stood before him.

BP 1.127.30

श्रीसूर्य उवाच। भूयश्च शृणु मे साम्बं तुष्टो'हं यद्ब्रवीमि ते। अद्य प्रभृति त्वन्नाम्ना मम स्थानानि सुव्रत। क्षितौ ये स्थापयिष्यन्ति तेषां लोकाः सनातनाः ॥

The Sun said. Hear again what I tell you, Sāmba, because I am pleased with you. From this day onward, O steadfast one, those who establish my shrines on earth in your name shall attain eternal worlds.

BP 1.127.31

स्थापयस्वैव मामस्मिंश्चन्द्रभागातटे शुभे। तव नाम्ना च साम्बेदं परां ख्यातिं गमिष्यति ॥

Establish me on this auspicious bank of the Candrabhāgā. By your name this place shall attain supreme fame, O Sāmba.

BP 1.127.32

कीर्तिस्तवाक्षया लोके ख्यातिं यास्यति सुव्रत। भूयश्च ते प्रदास्यामि प्रत्यहं स्वप्नदर्शनम ॥

Your fame shall be imperishable in the world and shall become renowned, O steadfast one. I shall also grant you a vision of me in dreams every day.

BP 1.127.33

सुमंतुरुवाच। एवं दत्त्वा वरं तस्मै वृष्णिसिंहाय चापरम। प्रत्यक्षदर्शनं दत्त्वा तत्रैवान्तरधाद्धरिः ॥

Sumantu said. Having thus given that boon and another to that lion among the Vṛṣṇis, and having granted him direct vision, Hari disappeared there.

BP 1.127.34

य इदं पठते स्तोत्रं त्रिकालं भक्तिमान्नरः। त्रिसप्ततम आवर्त्यहोमं वा सप्तरात्रकम ॥

A devout man who recites this hymn three times a day, repeats it twenty one hundred times, or performs a fire offering for seven nights.

BP 1.127.35

राज्यकामो लभेद्राज्यं धनकामो लभेद्धनम। रोगार्तो मुच्यते रोगाद्यथा साम बस्तथैव सः ॥

The one who desires kingship obtains kingship, the one who desires wealth obtains wealth, and the one afflicted by disease is freed from disease, just as Sāmba was.

BP 1.127.36

सूर्यलोकं व्रजेच्चापि भक्त्या पूज्य दिवाकरम। रमते च तथा तस्मिन देवैश्च परिवारितः ॥

He too goes to the world of the Sun, worshipping the Day Maker with devotion, and delights there, surrounded by the gods.
