रङ्कणवैश्योत्पत्तिवर्णनम् — The account of the origin of the merchant Rankaṇa.

।। बृहस्पतिरुवाच ।। — Bṛhaspati said.

BP 3.4.16.1

स्वायम्भुवेऽन्तरे पूर्वं ध्रुववंशसमुद्भवः
राजा प्राचीनबर्हिश्च बभूव मखकारकः ।। १ ।।

In the Svāyambhuva interval of old, King Prācīnabarhis, born in Dhruva’s line, became a performer of sacrifice.

Transliteration (IAST)

svāyambhuve’ntare pūrvaṃ dhruvavaṃśasamudbhavaḥ || rājā prācīnabarhiśca babhūva makhakārakaḥ || 1 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa · literal pāda gloss
svāyambhuve’ntare pūrvaṃ dhruvavaṃśasamudbhavaḥ — In the Svāyambhuva interval of old, born from Dhruva’s lineage;
rājā prācīnabarhiśca babhūva makhakārakaḥ — King Prācīnabarhis became a maker of sacrifices.

BP 3.4.16.2

नारदस्योपदेशेन त्यक्त्वा हिंसामयं मखम्
ज्ञानवान् वैष्णवो भूत्वा दशपुत्रानजीजनत् ।। २ ।।

Instructed by Nārada, he abandoned the violent sacrifice, became wise and devoted to Viṣṇu, and fathered ten sons.

Transliteration (IAST)

nāradasyopadeśena tyaktvā hiṃsāmayaṃ makham || jñānavān vaiṣṇavo bhūtvā daśaputrānajījanat || 2 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa · literal pāda gloss
nāradasyopadeśena tyaktvā hiṃsāmayaṃ makham — By Nārada’s instruction, abandoning the sacrifice filled with violence;
jñānavān vaiṣṇavo bhūtvā daśaputrānajījanat — becoming wise and a Vaiṣṇava, he begot ten sons.

BP 3.4.16.3

प्रचेतानाम तेषां वै जातं ते चैकरूपिणः
पितुराज्ञां पुरस्कृत्य जलमध्ये तपोऽर्थिनः
रत्नाकरस्य सिन्धोश्च मग्नभूता बभूविरे ।। ३ ।।

They were called the Pracetases and were all alike. Obeying their father, they entered the waters, seeking austerity; they remained submerged in the ocean.

Transliteration (IAST)

pracetānāma teṣāṃ vai jātaṃ te caikarūpiṇaḥ || piturājñāṃ puraskṛtya jalamadhye tapo’rthinaḥ || ratnākarasya sindhośca magnabhūtā babhūvire || 3 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa · literal pāda gloss
pracetānāma teṣāṃ vai jātaṃ te caikarūpiṇaḥ — They were named Pracetas, all alike;
piturājñāṃ puraskṛtya jalamadhye tapo’rthinaḥ — placing their father’s command first, desiring austerity in the water;
ratnākarasya sindhośca magnabhūtā babhūvire — they became submerged in the ocean.

BP 3.4.16.4

तेषां तु तपसा तुष्टः स्वयम्भूश्चतुराननः
सप्ताब्धिषु स तान्सप्त सुतान्संस्थाप्य लोकराट् ।। ४ ।।

Pleased by their austerities, the self-born four-faced Lord of the Worlds placed seven of the sons in the seven oceans.

Transliteration (IAST)

teṣāṃ tu tapasā tuṣṭaḥ svayambhūścaturānanaḥ || saptābdhiṣu sa tānsapta sutānsaṃsthāpya lokarāṭ || 4 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa · literal pāda gloss
teṣāṃ tu tapasā tuṣṭaḥ svayambhūścaturānanaḥ — Pleased by their austerities, the self-born four-faced one;
saptābdhiṣu sa tānsapta sutānsaṃsthāpya lokarāṭ — the Lord of the worlds placed those seven sons in the seven oceans.

BP 3.4.16.5

रत्नाकरेऽष्टमं पुत्रं नवमं मानसोत्तरे
दशमं मेरुशाखायां सुतं कृत्वा मुमोद ह ।। ५ ।।

He placed the eighth son in the Ocean of Jewels, the ninth on Mānasottara, and the tenth on a branch of Meru, and rejoiced.

Transliteration (IAST)

ratnākare’ṣṭamaṃ putraṃ navamaṃ mānasottare || daśamaṃ meruśākhāyāṃ sutaṃ kṛtvā mumoda ha || 5 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa · literal pāda gloss
ratnākare’ṣṭamaṃ putraṃ navamaṃ mānasottare — The eighth son in the Ocean of Jewels, the ninth on Mānasottara;
daśamaṃ meruśākhāyāṃ sutaṃ kṛtvā mumoda ha — having placed the tenth son on a branch of Meru, he rejoiced.

BP 3.4.16.6

आपो वहति यो लोके स आपव इति स्मृतः
द्वितीयो वरुणो नाम यादसां पतिरप्पतिः ।। ६ ।।

He who bears the waters in the world is remembered as Āpava; the second, named Varuṇa, is lord of aquatic beings and of the waters.

Transliteration (IAST)

āpo vahati yo loke sa āpava iti smṛtaḥ || dvitīyo varuṇo nāma yādasāṃ patirappatiḥ || 6 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa · literal pāda gloss
āpo vahati yo loke sa āpava iti smṛtaḥ — He who carries water in the world is remembered as Āpava;
dvitīyo varuṇo nāma yādasāṃ patirappatiḥ — the second, named Varuṇa, is lord of the aquatic beings and lord of waters.

BP 3.4.16.7

ददौ पाशं तदा ब्रह्मा दैत्यबन्धनहेतवे
पाशी नाम ततो जातं वरुणस्य महात्मनः ।। ७ ।।

Then Brahmā gave him a noose for binding the Daityas; therefore the great Varuṇa became known as Pāśī, the noose-bearer.

Transliteration (IAST)

dadau pāśaṃ tadā brahmā daityabandhanahetave || pāśī nāma tato jātaṃ varuṇasya mahātmanaḥ || 7 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa · literal pāda gloss
dadau pāśaṃ tadā brahmā daityabandhanahetave — Then Brahmā gave a noose, for the purpose of binding the Daityas;
pāśī nāma tato jātaṃ varuṇasya mahātmanaḥ — from that, the great Varuṇa became named Pāśī.

BP 3.4.16.8

स तु पूर्वभवे चासीद्ब्राह्मणः शक्तिपूजकः.
आपवो नाम विख्यातो वारुणीपानतत्परः ।। ८ ।।

In a former birth he was a Brāhmaṇa who worshipped Śakti. Famous as Āpava, he was devoted to drinking Vāruṇī.

Transliteration (IAST)

sa tu pūrvabhave cāsīdbrāhmaṇaḥ śaktipūjakaḥ. || āpavo nāma vikhyāto vāruṇīpānatatparaḥ || 8 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa · literal pāda gloss
sa tu pūrvabhave cāsīdbrāhmaṇaḥ śaktipūjakaḥ. — In a former birth he was a Brāhmaṇa, a worshipper of Śakti;
āpavo nāma vikhyāto vāruṇīpānatatparaḥ — famous by the name Āpava, devoted to drinking Vāruṇī.

BP 3.4.16.9

भद्रकाल्याः प्रियो भक्तो नित्यं पूजनतत्परः
नानारक्तमयैः पुष्पैर्गुण्ठितां रक्तमालिकाम् ।। ९ ।।

He was Bhadrakālī’s beloved devotee, always intent on worship, and wove a red garland from many crimson flowers.

Transliteration (IAST)

bhadrakālyāḥ priyo bhakto nityaṃ pūjanatatparaḥ || nānāraktamayaiḥ puṣpairguṇṭhitāṃ raktamālikām || 9 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa · literal pāda gloss
bhadrakālyāḥ priyo bhakto nityaṃ pūjanatatparaḥ — A beloved devotee of Bhadrakālī, always intent on worship;
nānāraktamayaiḥ puṣpairguṇṭhitāṃ raktamālikām — a red garland woven from flowers of many kinds of red.

BP 3.4.16.10

रक्तचन्दनसंयुक्तां गृहीत्वा मन्त्रसंयुतः
भद्रकाल्यै निवेद्याशु नवार्णवपरोऽभवत् ।। १० ।।

Taking it with red sandalwood and accompanied by a mantra, he quickly offered it to Bhadrakālī and devoted himself to the nine-syllabled mantra.

Transliteration (IAST)

raktacandanasaṃyuktāṃ gṛhītvā mantrasaṃyutaḥ || bhadrakālyai nivedyāśu navārṇavaparo’bhavat || 10 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa · literal pāda gloss
raktacandanasaṃyuktāṃ gṛhītvā mantrasaṃyutaḥ — Taking it, joined with red sandalwood and accompanied by a mantra;
bhadrakālyai nivedyāśu navārṇavaparo’bhavat — quickly offering it to Bhadrakālī, he became devoted to the nine-syllabled mantra.

BP 3.4.16.11

धूपैर्दीपैश्च नैवेद्यैस्ताम्बूलैर्ऋतुजैः फलैः
पूजयित्वा महालक्ष्मीं भद्रकालीं सनातनीम् ।। ११ ।।

With incense, lamps, offerings, betel, and seasonal fruits, he worshipped eternal Mahālakṣmī, Bhadrakālī.

Transliteration (IAST)

dhūpairdīpaiśca naivedyaistāmbūlairṛtujaiḥ phalaiḥ || pūjayitvā mahālakṣmīṃ bhadrakālīṃ sanātanīm || 11 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa · literal pāda gloss
dhūpairdīpaiśca naivedyaistāmbūlairṛtujaiḥ phalaiḥ — With incense, lamps, offerings, betel, and fruits of the season;
pūjayitvā mahālakṣmīṃ bhadrakālīṃ sanātanīm — having worshipped Mahālakṣmī, the eternal Bhadrakālī.

BP 3.4.16.12

तिलैः शर्करया युक्तं मधुना च हविः स्वयम्
वह्निद्वारेण संहृत्य तुष्टाव जगदम्बिकाम् ।। १२ ।।

He prepared an offering with sesame, sugar, and honey, presented it through the fire, and praised the Mother of the Universe.

Transliteration (IAST)

tilaiḥ śarkarayā yuktaṃ madhunā ca haviḥ svayam || vahnidvāreṇa saṃhṛtya tuṣṭāva jagadambikām || 12 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa · literal pāda gloss
tilaiḥ śarkarayā yuktaṃ madhunā ca haviḥ svayam — An offering joined with sesame, sugar, and honey, himself;
vahnidvāreṇa saṃhṛtya tuṣṭāva jagadambikām — having presented it through fire, he praised the Mother of the Universe.

BP 3.4.16.13

चरित्रं मध्यमं देव्या विष्णुदेवेन निर्मितम्
नवार्णवेन तेनैव प्रत्यहं जाप्यतत्परः ।। १३ ।।

The Goddess’s Middle Carita was composed by Viṣṇudeva; with that and the nine-syllabled mantra he was intent on daily recitation.

Transliteration (IAST)

caritraṃ madhyamaṃ devyā viṣṇudevena nirmitam || navārṇavena tenaiva pratyahaṃ jāpyatatparaḥ || 13 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa · literal pāda gloss
caritraṃ madhyamaṃ devyā viṣṇudevena nirmitam — The Goddess’s Middle Carita, made by Viṣṇudeva;
navārṇavena tenaiva pratyahaṃ jāpyatatparaḥ — with that very nine-syllabled mantra, intent on reciting each day.

BP 3.4.16.14

एवं वर्षत्रयं जातं तस्य पूजां प्रकुर्वतः
प्रसन्नाभूत्तदा देवी वरदा सर्वमङ्गला
वरं ब्रूहीति वचनं तमाह द्विजसत्तमम् ।। १४ ।।

As he worshipped her in this way for three years, the all-auspicious, boon-granting Goddess became pleased. She said to the excellent Brāhmaṇa, “Ask for a boon.”

Transliteration (IAST)

evaṃ varṣatrayaṃ jātaṃ tasya pūjāṃ prakurvataḥ || prasannābhūttadā devī varadā sarvamaṅgalā || varaṃ brūhīti vacanaṃ tamāha dvijasattamam || 14 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa · literal pāda gloss
evaṃ varṣatrayaṃ jātaṃ tasya pūjāṃ prakurvataḥ — Thus three years passed as he performed her worship;
prasannābhūttadā devī varadā sarvamaṅgalā — then the Goddess, pleased, boon-giving, all-auspicious;
varaṃ brūhīti vacanaṃ tamāha dvijasattamam — she spoke to that excellent twice-born one: “Ask for a boon.”

BP 3.4.16.15

इति वाक्यं प्रियं श्रुत्वा द्विज आपव नम्रधीः
तुष्टाव दण्डवद् भूत्वा भद्रकालीं सनातनीम् ।। १५ ।।

Hearing these dear words, the humble-minded Brāhmaṇa Āpava praised eternal Bhadrakālī, bowing down before her.

Transliteration (IAST)

iti vākyaṃ priyaṃ śrutvā dvija āpava namradhīḥ || tuṣṭāva daṇḍavad bhūtvā bhadrakālīṃ sanātanīm || 15 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa · literal pāda gloss
iti vākyaṃ priyaṃ śrutvā dvija āpava namradhīḥ — Hearing this pleasing speech, the humble-minded Brāhmaṇa Āpava;
tuṣṭāva daṇḍavad bhūtvā bhadrakālīṃ sanātanīm — having bowed down, he praised eternal Bhadrakālī.

।। आपव उवाच ।। — Āpava said.

BP 3.4.16.16

विष्णुकल्पे पुरा चासीद्दानवो महिषासुरः
कोटिकोटिसहस्रैस्तु रथैर्वाजिगजैर्युतः ।। १६ ।।

Transliteration (IAST)

viṣṇukalpe purā cāsīddānavo mahiṣāsuraḥ || koṭikoṭisahasraistu rathairvājigajairyutaḥ || 16 ||

BP 3.4.16.17

त्रैलोक्यं स्ववशे कृत्वा महेन्द्रस्स तदाभवत्
स्वारोचिषान्तरः कालो गतो राज्यं प्रकुर्वतः ।। १७ ।।

Having brought the three worlds under his control, he became a great Indra; the age of Svārociṣa passed while he ruled.

Transliteration (IAST)

trailokyaṃ svavaśe kṛtvā mahendrassa tadābhavat || svārociṣāntaraḥ kālo gato rājyaṃ prakurvataḥ || 17 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa · literal pāda gloss

svārociṣāntaraḥ kālo gato rājyaṃ prakurvataḥ — the Svārociṣa interval passed as he ruled.

BP 3.4.16.18

ततस्स भगवान्विष्णुस्सर्वदेवसमन्वितः
समुत्पाद्य मुखात्तेजो ज्वालामाली बभूव ह ।। १८ ।।

Then Bhagavān Viṣṇu, together with all the gods, brought forth radiance from his mouth, and it became a wreath of flames.

Transliteration (IAST)

tatassa bhagavānviṣṇussarvadevasamanvitaḥ || samutpādya mukhāttejo jvālāmālī babhūva ha || 18 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa · literal pāda gloss

BP 3.4.16.19

ज्योतिर्लिङ्गात्तदा देवी भवती स्वेच्छया भुवि
सम्भूय महिषं हन्त्री तस्यै देव्यै नमोनमः ।। १९ ।।

Then the Goddess Bhavatī appeared on earth from the radiant liṅga by her own will; arising there, she slew Mahiṣa. Homage to that Goddess.

Transliteration (IAST)

jyotirliṅgāttadā devī bhavatī svecchayā bhuvi || sambhūya mahiṣaṃ hantrī tasyai devyai namonamaḥ || 19 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa · literal pāda gloss
jyotirliṅgāttadā devī bhavatī svecchayā bhuvi — Then the Goddess Bhavatī, by her own will, on earth from the radiant liṅga;
sambhūya mahiṣaṃ hantrī tasyai devyai namonamaḥ — arising, she was the slayer of Mahiṣa; homage, homage to that Goddess.

BP 3.4.16.20

रूद्रकल्पे पुरा चासीद्रुदच्छम्भुमुखाद्दिवि
रावणश्च सहस्रास्यो जातो ब्रह्माण्डरावणः ।। २० ।।

In the age of Rudra, in heaven, from the face of Rudra-Śambhu was born the thousand-headed Rāvaṇa, Brahmāṇḍarāvaṇa.

Transliteration (IAST)

rūdrakalpe purā cāsīdrudacchambhumukhāddivi || rāvaṇaśca sahasrāsyo jāto brahmāṇḍarāvaṇaḥ || 20 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa · literal pāda gloss
rūdrakalpe purā cāsīdrudacchambhumukhāddivi — In the age of Rudra, formerly, in heaven, from Rudra-Śambhu’s face;
rāvaṇaśca sahasrāsyo jāto brahmāṇḍarāvaṇaḥ — Rāvaṇa, thousand-headed, was born as Brahmāṇḍarāvaṇa.

BP 3.4.16.21

राक्षसो बलवान्घोरो लोकालोकगिरेरधः
न्यवसद्देवदैत्यानां मनुष्याणां च भक्षकः
षष्ठे मन्वन्तरे तेन ब्रह्माण्डं राज्यसात्कृतम् ।। २१ ।।
