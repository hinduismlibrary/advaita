Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 80 — The Fruits of Sun Worship and the Seventh Day Rite

BP 1.80.1

सुमन्तुर् उवाच । इत्थं श्रुत्वा कथां दिव्यां हेलि-माहात्म्यम् आश्रिताम् । साम्बः पप्रच्छ भूयो ऽपि नारदं मुनि-सत्तमम् ॥ ॥

Sumantu said. Having heard this divine story concerning the greatness of the Sun, Sāmba again questioned Nārada, the best of sages.

BP 1.80.2

साम्ब उवाच । सूर्य-पूजा-फलं यच् च यच् च दान-फलं महत् । प्रणिपाते फलं यच् च गीत-वाद्ये च यत् फलम् ॥ ॥

Sāmba said. Whatever is the fruit of worshiping the Sun, whatever great fruit comes from giving, whatever fruit comes from bowing, and whatever fruit comes from song and music,

BP 1.80.3

भास्करस्य द्विज-श्रेष्ठ तन् मे ब्रूहि समन्ततः । येन सम्पूजयाम्य् एष भानुं देवैः सदार्चितम् ॥ ॥

O best of Brahmins, tell me all of this fully, by which I may worship the Sun, who is always worshiped by the gods.

BP 1.80.4

नारद उवाच । इमम् अर्थं पुरा पृष्टो ब्रह्मा लोक-पितामहः । दिण्डिना यदु-शार्दूल शृणुष्व एकाग्र-मानसः ॥ ॥

Nārada said. Formerly Brahmā, the Grandfather of the worlds, was asked this very matter by Diṇḍi. O tiger among the Yadus, listen with full concentration.

BP 1.80.5

सुखासीनं तथा देवं सुर-ज्येष्ठं पितामहम् । प्रणम्य शिरसा दिण्डिर् इदं वचनम् अब्रवीत् ॥ ॥

Having bowed his head to the Grandfather, the god seated at ease and foremost among the gods, Diṇḍi spoke these words.

BP 1.80.6

दिण्डिर् उवाच । सूर्य-पूजा-फलं ब्रूहि ब्रूहि दान-फलं तथा । प्रणामे यत् फलं देव यच् चोक्तं तौर्यकत्रये ॥ ॥

Diṇḍi said. Tell me the fruit of worshiping the Sun, tell me likewise the fruit of giving, the fruit of bowing, O Lord, and the fruit of the three kinds of music.

BP 1.80.7

इतिहास-पुराणाभ्यां कारिते श्रवणे तथा । पुरतो देव-देवस्य यत् फलं स्यात् तद् उच्यताम् ॥ ॥

Tell what fruit there is from arranging the recitation of epics and ancient histories before the Lord of gods.

BP 1.80.8

मार्जने लेपने यच् च देव-देवस्य मन्दिरे । भास्करस्य कृते ब्रूहि मम लोक-पितामह ॥ ॥

Tell me, Grandfather of the worlds, the fruit of sweeping and plastering the temple of the Lord of gods for the sake of the Sun.

BP 1.80.9

ब्रह्मोवाच । स्तुति-जप्य-उपहारेण पूजया न नरो रवेः । उपवासेन षष्ठ्यां च सर्व-पापः प्रमुच्यते ॥ ॥

Brahmā said. A person is freed from all sins by praise, recitation, offerings, and worship of the Sun, and by fasting on the sixth day.

BP 1.80.10

प्रणिधाय शिरो भूमौ नमस्कार-परो रवेः । तत्-क्षणात् सर्व-पापेभ्यो मुच्यते नात्र संशयः ॥ ॥

Having laid his head on the ground, devoted to bowing to the Sun, he is freed at once from all sins. There is no doubt about this.

BP 1.80.11

भक्ति-युक्तो नरो यस् तु रवेः कुर्यात् प्रदक्षिणाम् । प्रदक्षिणी-कृता तेन सप्त-द्वीपा भवेन् मही ॥ ॥

A person who devoutly circumambulates the Sun thereby circumambulates the earth with its seven continents.

BP 1.80.12

सूर्य-लोकं व्रजेच् चापि इह रोगैश् च मुच्यते । उपानहौ परित्यज्य अन्यथा नरकं व्रजेत् ॥ ॥

He reaches the world of the Sun and is freed from diseases here. He must leave his shoes aside, or else he goes to hell.

BP 1.80.13

सोपानत्को नरो यस् त्व् आरोहेत् सूर्य-मन्दिरम् । स याति नरकं घोरम् असिपत्रवनं विभो ॥ ॥

A man who climbs the temple of the Sun while wearing shoes goes to the terrible hell, the Forest of Sword Leaves, O Lord.

BP 1.80.14

सूर्यं मनसि यः कृत्वा कुर्याद् व्योम-प्रदक्षिणाम् । प्रदक्षिणी-कृतास् तेन सर्वे देवा भवन्ति हि ॥ ॥

Whoever keeps the Sun in mind and circumambulates the sky thereby indeed circumambulates all the gods.

BP 1.80.15

परितुष्टाश् च ते सर्वे प्रयच्छन्ति गतिं शुभाम् । सर्वे देवा महा-बाहो ह्य् अभीष्टं तु परन्तप ॥ ॥

All those gods, being pleased, grant an auspicious destination. All the gods grant what is desired, O mighty armed one, O scorcher of enemies.

BP 1.80.16

एकाहारो नरो भूत्वा षष्ठ्यां यो ऽर्चयते रविम् । सप्तम्यां वा महा-बाहो सूर्य-लोकं स गच्छति ॥ ॥

A person who eats only once and worships the Sun on the sixth day, or on the seventh day, O mighty armed one, goes to the world of the Sun.

BP 1.80.17

अहोरात्र-उपवासी स पूजयेद् यस् तु भास्करम् । सप्तम्यां वा अथ षष्ठ्यां वा स गच्छेत् परमां गतिम् ॥ ॥

Whoever fasts for a day and night and worships the Sun on the seventh or sixth day reaches the highest destination.

BP 1.80.18

कृष्ण-पक्षस्य सप्तम्यां सोपवासो जितेन्द्रियः । सर्व-रक्त-उपहारेण पूजयेद् यस् तु भास्करम् ॥ ॥

Whoever fasts and controls the senses on the seventh day of the dark fortnight should worship the Sun with all red offerings,

BP 1.80.19

पङ्कजैः करवीरैर् वा कुङ्कुम-उदक-चन्दनैः । मोदकैश् च गण-श्रेष्ठ सूर्य-लोकं स गच्छति ॥ ॥

With lotuses, oleander flowers, saffron water, sandalwood, and sweet cakes, O best of the hosts, he goes to the world of the Sun.

BP 1.80.20

शुक्ल-पक्षस्य सप्तम्याम् उपवास-रतः सदा । सर्व-शुक्ल-उपहारेण पूजयेद् यस् तु भास्करम् ॥ ॥

Whoever is always devoted to fasting on the seventh day of the bright fortnight should worship the Sun with all white offerings,

BP 1.80.21

जाती-मुद्गरकैश् चैव श्वेत-उत्पल-कदम्बकैः । पायसेन तथा देवं स-वज्रेणार्चयेद् रविम् ॥ ॥

With jasmine, Arabian jasmine, white lotuses, and burflower blossoms, one should worship the Sun with rice pudding and the thunderbolt, O Lord.

BP 1.80.22

सर्व-पाप-विशुद्धात्मा विधुः कान्त्या न संशयः । हंस-युक्तेन यानेन हंस-लोकम् अवाप्नुते ॥ ॥

He, with his soul purified from all sins, shines like the moon, without doubt. By a vehicle yoked with swans, he attains the world of swans.

BP 1.80.23

दिण्डिर् उवाच । ब्रूहि मे विस्तराद् देव सप्तमी-कल्पम् उत्तमम् । उपोष्य सप्तमीं येन गमिष्ये शरणं रवेः ॥ ॥

Diṇḍi said. Tell me in detail, O Lord, the excellent seventh day observance, by which, having fasted on the seventh day, I may take refuge in the Sun.

BP 1.80.24

ब्रह्मोवाच । साधु पृष्टो ऽस्मि भवता सप्तमी-कल्पम् उत्तमम् । यथा सहस्र-किरणः पुरा पृष्टो ऽरुणेन वै ॥ ॥

Brahmā said. You have asked well about the excellent seventh day observance, as the thousand rayed Sun was formerly asked by Aruṇa.

BP 1.80.25

कथिताः सप्त सप्तम्यो भानुना श्रेयसे नृणाम् । अरुणस्य गण-श्रेष्ठ पृच्छतः कारणान्तरे ॥ ॥

The Sun described seven seventh day observances for the welfare of people, when Aruṇa asked on another occasion, O best of the hosts.

BP 1.80.26

कस्यचित् त्व् अथ कालस्य देव-देवं दिवाकरम् । ध्यानम् आश्रित्य तिष्ठन्तम् अरुणो वाक्यम् अब्रवीत् ॥ ॥

At some time, Aruṇa spoke these words to the Lord of gods, the Sun, who stood absorbed in meditation.

BP 1.80.27

किम् अर्थं देव-देवेश ध्यानम् आश्रित्य तिष्ठसि । दिनं न याति देवेश कारणं मम कथ्यताम् ॥ ॥

Why do you stand absorbed in meditation, O Lord of gods. The day does not pass, O Lord of gods. Tell me the reason.

BP 1.80.28

कुरु चङ्क्रमणं देव वहमानो दिवस्-पते । इत्य् एवं भगवान् पृष्ट इदं वचनम् अब्रवीत् ॥ ॥

Move onward, O god, bearing the day, O Lord of the day. Thus questioned, the blessed one spoke these words.

BP 1.80.29

शृणु त्वं द्विज-शार्दूल यद्-अर्थं ध्यानम् आश्रितः । अर्वावसुर् द्विज-श्रेष्ठः स चापुत्रः खगोत्तम ॥ ॥

Listen, O tiger among Brahmins, to the reason I am absorbed in meditation. Arvāvasu, the best of Brahmins, is childless, O best of birds.

BP 1.80.30

आराधयति मां नित्यं गन्ध-पुष्प-उपहारकैः । पुत्र-कामः खग-श्रेष्ठ न च जानात्य् अयं यथा ॥ ॥

Desiring a son, he worships me always with offerings of fragrance and flowers, O best of birds, but he does not know the way.

BP 1.80.31

पुत्र-दो ऽहं भवे येन विधिना पूजितः खग । श्रूयतां च विधिः सर्वे येन प्रीतो भवे नृणाम् ॥ ॥

Hear the rite, O bird, by which, when worshiped, I become a giver of sons and become pleased with people.

BP 1.80.32

सप्तमी-कल्प-संज्ञो वै विधीनाम् उत्तमो विधिः । यस् तु मां पूजयेद् नित्यं तस्य पुत्रान् ददाम्य् अहम् ॥ ॥

The rite known as the Seventh Day Observance is the best of rites. I give sons to the one who worships me daily.

BP 1.80.33

गृह्णीष्व सप्तमी-कल्पं गत्वा ब्रूहि द्विज-उत्तमम् । येनाहं बहु-पुत्रत्वं दद्यां तस्य तथा खग ॥ ॥

Receive the Seventh Day Observance and go tell that best Brahmin, by which, O bird, I may grant him many sons.

BP 1.80.34

श्रुत्वा भानोः क्षणाद् एव जगाम स खगोत्तमः । कथयामास तत् सर्वं भानोर् वचनम् आदितः ॥ ॥

Having heard the words of the Sun, the best of birds went at once. He related everything, beginning with the words of the Sun.

BP 1.80.35

ब्राह्मणस्य खग-श्रेष्ठ स च श्रुत्वा द्विज-उत्तमः । चकार सप्तमी-कल्पं यथाख्यातं खगेन तु ॥ ॥

O best of birds, that best Brahmin, having heard it, performed the Seventh Day Observance just as the bird had described it.

BP 1.80.36

ऋद्धिं वृद्धिं तथारोग्यं प्राप्य पुत्रांश् च पुष्कलान् । गतो ऽसौ सूर्य-लोकं च तेजसा तत्-समो ऽभवत् ॥ ॥

Having attained prosperity, growth, health, and many sons, he went to the world of the Sun and became equal to him in radiance.
