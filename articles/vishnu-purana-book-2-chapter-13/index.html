VP 2.13.1

भगवन् सम्यग् आख्यातं यत् पृष्टोऽसि मया किल।
भू-समुद्रादि-सरितां संस्थानं ग्रह-संस्थितिः

Maitreyar asks Paraasharar - O Bhagavan, you have told me completely everything that I wanted to know, whatever I requested you. About the earth, oceans, mountains, their positions, the planetary positions, etc.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
भगवन् (bhagavan) — O Bhagavan
सम्यग् (samyag) — well, completely
आख्यातं (ākhyātaṃ) — have explained
यत् (yat) — what
पृष्टोऽसि (pṛṣṭo’si) — you were asked
मया (mayā) — by me
किल (kila) — indeed
भू-समुद्रादि-सरितां (bhū-samudrādi-saritāṃ) — of the earth, oceans, and rivers
संस्थानं (saṃsthānaṃ) — the arrangement
ग्रह-संस्थितिः (graha-saṃsthitiḥ) — the arrangement of the planets
एवं तावत् हरेः स्थूले विश्व-रूपे निरूपिते (evaṃ tāvat hareḥ sthūle viśva-rūpe nirūpite) — thus, after Hari's gross cosmic form has been described
तेन शुद्ध-मतिः तस्य सूक्ष्म-रूपं बुभुत्सते (tena śuddha-matiḥ tasya sūkṣma-rūpaṃ bubhutsate) — with a purified mind, he wishes to know his subtle form
तत् कुर्यात् विशति हि येन वासुदेवम् (tat kuryāt viśati hi yena vāsudevam) — that he should do by which he enters Vāsudeva
तत्-प्रवेश-उपाय-भूत-ज्ञान-योग-आश्रयम् (tat-praveśa-upāya-bhūta-jñāna-yoga-āśrayam) — resting on knowledge and yoga, the means of entry
प्रथम-अध्याय-उपक्षिप्तम् एव भरत-चरितम् (prathama-adhyāya-upakṣiptam eva bharata-caritam) — the account of Bharata introduced in the first chapter
उक्त-अनुवाद-पूर्वकम् पृच्छति (ukta-anuvāda-pūrvakam pṛcchati) — he asks after repeating what was said
भगवन् इति षड्भिः (bhagavan iti ṣaḍbhiḥ) — with six verses beginning “O Blessed Lord”
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bhagavan samyag ākhyātaṃ yat pṛṣṭo’si mayā kila | bhū-samudrādi-saritāṃ saṃsthānaṃ graha-saṃsthitiḥ

evaṃ tāvaddhareḥ sthūle viśvarūpe nirūpite | tena śuddhamatistasya sūkṣmarūpaṃ bubhutsate | tat kuryādviśati hi yena vāsudevam ityukte tatpraveśopāyabhūtajñānayogāśrayaṃ prathamādhyādhyopakṣiptameva bharatacaritamuktānuvādapūrvakaṃ pṛcchati—bhagavann iti ṣaḍbhiḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

एवं तावद्धरेः स्थूले विश्वरूपे निरूपिते । तेन शुद्धमतिस्तस्य सूक्ष्मरूपं बुभुत्सते । तत् कुर्याद्विशति हि येन वासुदेवम् इत्युक्ते तत्प्रवेशोपायभूतज्ञानयोगाश्रयं प्रथमाध्याध्योपक्षिप्तमेव भरतचरितमुक्तानुवादपूर्वकं पृच्छति—भगवन्न् इति षड्भिः

Śrīdhara commentary.

Maitreyar asks Paraasharar - O Bhagavan, you have told me completely everything that I wanted to know, whatever I requested you. About the earth, oceans, mountains, their positions, the planetary positions, etc.

VP 2.13.2

विष्ण्व्-आधारं यथा चैतत् त्रैलोक्यं समवस्थितम्।
परमार्थस् तु ते प्रोक्तो यथा ज्ञानं प्रधानतः

You also told me how this entire world consisting of sentient and non-sentient, is having Sri Vishnu as its inner self, Vishnu being the Inner Controller of everything, how everything is existing like that, being His shareera, He is aatma for everything. In that, most prominently, you have told me what is Paramaartha, that is, the jeevaatman, their jnyaana svaroopa, that is eka roopa. How jeevaatman is also Paramaartha, that they never change, also you tole me prominently.
