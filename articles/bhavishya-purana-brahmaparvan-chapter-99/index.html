Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 99 — The Great Victory Seventh Day Observance

BP 1.99.1

ब्रह्मोवाच। शुक्लपक्षे तु सप्तम्यां यदा संक्रमते रविः। महाजया तदा सा वै सप्तमी भास्करप्रिया ॥

Brahma spoke. When the Sun enters a sign on the seventh day of the bright fortnight, that is indeed the Great Victory seventh day, dear to Bhāskara.

BP 1.99.2

स्नानं दानं जपो होमः पितृदेवाभिपूजनम्। सर्वं कोटिगुणं प्रोक्तं भास्करस्य वचो यथा ॥

Bathing, giving, recitation, fire offering, and worship of the ancestors and gods are all declared ten millionfold, according to the word of Bhāskara.

BP 1.99.3

यस्तस्यां मानवो भक्त्या घृतेन स्नापयेद्रविम्। सोऽश्वमेधफलं प्राप्य स्वर्गलोकमवाप्नुयात् ॥

A person who bathes the Sun with ghee on that day with devotion obtains the fruit of the horse sacrifice and reaches the heavenly world.

BP 1.99.4

पयसा स्नापयेद्यस्तु भास्करं भक्तिमान्नरः। विमुक्तः सर्वपापेभ्यो याति सूर्यसलोकताम् ॥

A devoted person who bathes Bhāskara with milk is freed from all sins and goes to the world shared with the Sun.

BP 1.99.5

कार्पूरेण विमानेन किङ्किणीजालमालिनी। तेजसा हरिसंकाशः कान्त्या सूर्यसमस्तथा ॥

In a camphor vehicle decked with a net of small bells, he is like Hari in splendor and equal to the Sun in radiance.

BP 1.99.6

स्थित्वा तत्र चिरं कालं राजा भवति चाञ्जसा। महाजयैषा कथिता सप्तमी त्रिपुरान्तक ॥

Having stayed there for a long time, he quickly becomes a king. O destroyer of the Three Cities, this Great Victory seventh day has been described.

BP 1.99.7

यामुपोष्य नरो भक्त्या भवते सूर्यलोकगः। ततो याति परं ब्रह्म यत्र गत्वा न शोचति ॥

A man who fasts on it with devotion becomes one who goes to the world of the Sun. Then he goes to the highest Brahman, where he does not grieve after going.
