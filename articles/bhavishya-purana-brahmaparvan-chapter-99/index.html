Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 99 — Mahājayā Saptamī

BP 1.99.1

ब्रह्मोवाच। शुक्लपक्षे तु सप्तम्यां यदा संक्रमते रविः। महाजया तदा सा वै सप्तमी भास्करप्रिया ॥

Brahmā said: When Ravi enters a new sign on the seventh day of the bright fortnight, that Saptamī is Mahājayā, dear to Bhāskara.

Word-for-word
brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said;
śukla-pakṣe — in the bright fortnight;
tu — indeed;
saptamyām — on the seventh day;
yadā — when;
saṃkramate — enters;
raviḥ — Ravi;
mahājayā — Mahājayā;
tadā — then;
sā — that;
vai — indeed;
saptamī — Saptamī;
bhāskara-priyā — dear to Bhāskara.
Transliteration
brahmovāca | śuklapakṣe tu saptamyāṃ yadā saṃkramate raviḥ | mahājayā tadā sā vai saptamī bhāskarapriyā || 1 ||

BP 1.99.2

स्नानं दानं जपो होमः पितृदेवाभिपूजनम्। सर्वं कोटिगुणं प्रोक्तं भास्करस्य वचो यथा ॥

Bathing, giving, recitation, oblation, and the worship of ancestors and gods—all are declared to become ten million times greater, according to Bhāskara's word.

Word-for-word
snānam — bathing;
dānam — giving;
japaḥ — recitation;
homaḥ — oblation;
pitṛ-deva-abhipūjanam — worship of ancestors and gods;
sarvam — everything;
koṭi-guṇam — ten million times;
proktam — is declared;
bhāskarasya vacaḥ yathā — according to Bhāskara's word.
Transliteration
snānaṃ dānaṃ japo homaḥ pitṛdevābhipūjanam | sarvaṃ koṭiguṇaṃ proktaṃ bhāskarasya vaco yathā || 2 ||

BP 1.99.3

यस्तस्यां मानवो भक्त्या घृतेन स्नापयेद्रविम्। सोऽश्वमेधफलं प्राप्य स्वर्गलोकमवाप्नुयात् ॥

A person who devotedly bathes Ravi with ghee on that day obtains the fruit of the Aśvamedha and reaches the heavenly world.

Word-for-word
yaḥ — whoever;
tasyām — on that;
mānavaḥ — person;
bhaktyā — with devotion;
ghṛtena — with ghee;
snāpayet — bathes;
ravim — Ravi;
saḥ — he;
aśvamedha-phalam — fruit of the Aśvamedha;
prāpya — obtaining;
svarga-lokam — heavenly world;
avāpnuyāt — may attain.
Transliteration
yastasyāṃ mānavo bhaktyā ghṛtena snāpayedravim | so'śvamedhaphalaṃ prāpya svargalokamavāpnuyāt || 3 ||

BP 1.99.4

पयसा स्नापयेद्यस्तु भास्करं भक्तिमान्नरः। विमुक्तः सर्वपापेभ्यो याति सूर्यसलोकताम् ॥

The devoted person who bathes Bhāskara with milk is freed from every sin and goes to the world shared with the Sun.

Word-for-word
payasā — with milk;
snāpayet — bathes;
yaḥ tu — whoever;
bhāskaram — Bhāskara;
bhaktimān — devoted;
naraḥ — person;
vimuktaḥ — freed;
sarva-pāpebhyaḥ — from every sin;
yāti — goes;
sūrya-salokataḥ — to the world shared with the Sun.
Transliteration
payasā snāpayedyastu bhāskaraṃ bhaktimānnaraḥ | vimuktaḥ sarvapāpebhyo yāti sūryasalokatām || 4 ||

BP 1.99.5

कार्पूरेण विमानेन किङ्किणीजालमालिनी। तेजसा हरिसंकाशः कान्त्या सूर्यसमस्तथा ॥

With a celestial vehicle made of camphor and adorned with a network of little bells, he becomes equal to Hari in splendor and equal to the Sun in radiance.

Word-for-word
kārpūreṇa — with camphor;
vimānena — with a celestial vehicle;
kiṅkiṇī-jāla-mālinī — adorned with a network of small bells;
tejasā — in splendor;
hari-saṃkāśaḥ — equal to Hari;
kāntyā — in radiance;
sūrya-samaḥ — equal to the Sun;
tathā — likewise.
Transliteration
kārpūreṇa vimānena kiṅkiṇījālamālinī | tejasā harisaṃkāśaḥ kāntyā sūryasamastathā || 5 ||

BP 1.99.6

स्थित्वा तत्र चिरं कालं राजा भवति चाञ्जसा। महाजयैषा कथिता सप्तमी त्रिपुरान्तक ॥

Having remained there for a long time, he readily becomes a king. This Saptamī called Mahājayā has been taught, O Tripurāntaka.

Word-for-word
sthitvā — having remained;
tatra — there;
ciraṃ kālam — for a long time;
rājā — a king;
bhavati — becomes;
ca añjasā — and readily;
mahājayā — Mahājayā;
eṣā — this;
kathitā — has been taught;
saptamī — Saptamī;
tripurāntaka — O Tripurāntaka.
Transliteration
sthitvā tatra ciraṃ kālaṃ rājā bhavati cāñjasā | mahājayaiṣā kathitā saptamī tripurāntaka || 6 ||

BP 1.99.7

यामुपोष्य नरो भक्त्या भवते सूर्यलोकगः। ततो याति परं ब्रह्म यत्र गत्वा न शोचति ॥

Having fasted for it with devotion, a person becomes one who goes to the world of the Sun; from there he proceeds to the supreme Brahman, where, having gone, he does not grieve.

Word-for-word
yām — for which;
upoṣya — having fasted;
naraḥ — a person;
bhaktyā — with devotion;
bhavate — becomes;
sūrya-loka-gaḥ — one who goes to the world of the Sun;
tataḥ — from there;
yāti — goes;
param brahma — to supreme Brahman;
yatra — where;
gatvā — having gone;
na śocati — he does not grieve.
Transliteration
yāmupoṣya naro bhaktyā bhavate sūryalokagaḥ | tato yāti paraṃ brahma yatra gatvā na śocati || 7 ||
