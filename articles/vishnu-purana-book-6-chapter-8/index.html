VP 6.8.1

इत्येष कथितः सम्यक् तृतीयः प्रतिसञ्चरः।
आत्यन्तिको विमुक्तिर्या लयो ब्रह्मणि शाश्वते ॥१॥

Thus I have correctly described the third dissolution, the final liberation: absorption into the eternal Brahman.

Śrīdhara

anantarādhyāya-trayoktātyantika-layopasaṃhāra-pūrvakam āditaḥ sa-praśaṃsaṃ sarva-purāṇārtham upasaṃharann āha—itīty-ādiś caturbhiḥ

अनन्तराध्याय-त्रयोक्तात्यन्तिक-लयोपसंहार-पूर्वकम् आदितः स-प्रशंसं सर्व-पुराणार्थम् उपसंहरन्न् आह—इतीत्य्-आदिश् चतुर्भिः

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

After summarizing the ultimate dissolution taught in the preceding three chapters, he begins by praising and concludes the purport of the whole Purāṇa in four verses.

VP 6.8.7

ज्ञातश्च त्रिविधो राशिः शक्तिश्च त्रिविधा गुरो।
विज्ञाता सा च कार्त्स्न्येन त्रिविधा भावभावना ॥७॥

By your grace, O teacher, I have learned the threefold class and the threefold power, and have fully understood the three kinds of contemplation of reality.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
ज्ञातः (jñātaḥ) — known
च (ca) — and
त्रिविधः (trividhaḥ) — threefold
राशिः (rāśiḥ) — class; group
शक्तिः (śaktiḥ) — power
च (ca) — and
त्रिविधा (trividhā) — threefold

विज्ञाता (vijñātā) — understood
सा (sā) — that
च (ca) — and
कार्त्स्न्येन (kārtsnyena) — fully
त्रिविधा (trividhā) — threefold
भाव-भावना (bhāva-bhāvanā) — contemplation of states/reality
Śrīdhara
चतुर्विधो राशिः (caturvidho rāśiḥ) — the class is fourfold;
चतुर्-विभागः संसृष्टौ (catur-vibhāgaḥ saṃsṛṣṭau) — fourfold in division at creation;
चतुर्धा संस्थितः स्थितौ (caturdhā saṃsthitaḥ sthitau) — established fourfold at maintenance;
प्रलयं च करोत्य् अन्ते (pralayaṃ ca karoty ante) — and performs dissolution at the end;
चतुर्भेदो जनार्दनः (caturbhedo janārdanaḥ) — Janārdana is of four kinds;
पूर्वाध्यायोक्ता हि परतत्त्वेश्वरतदवतारजगद्रूपा त्रिविधा शक्तिः (pūrvādhyāyoktā hi paratattveśvaratadavatārajagadrūpā trividhā śaktiḥ) — the preceding chapter describes a threefold power: the Supreme Lord, his incarnations, and the universe;
विष्णु-शक्तिः परा प्रोक्ता क्षेत्रज्ञाख्या तथापरा (viṣṇu-śaktiḥ parā proktā kṣetrajñākhyā tathāparā) — the supreme power is Viṣṇu’s; another is called the knower of the field;
अविद्या-कर्म-सञ्ज्ञा अन्या तृतीया शक्तिः (avidyā-karma-sañjñā anyā tṛtīyā śaktiḥ) — a third power is called ignorance and action;
ब्रह्म-भावना कर्म-भावना उभय-भावना (brahma-bhāvanā karma-bhāvanā ubhaya-bhāvanā) — contemplation of Brahman, of action, and of both;
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

jñātaśca trividho rāśiḥ śaktiśca trividhā guro। vijñātā sā ca kārtsnyena trividhā bhāvabhāvanā

Śrīdhara

caturvidho rāśiḥ, catur-vibhāgaḥ saṃsṛṣṭau caturdhā saṃsthitaḥ sthitau । pralayaṃ ca karoty ante caturbhedo janārdanaḥ ॥ [vi.pu. 1.22.22] iti prathamāṃśe uktaḥ । pūrvādhyāyoktā hi para-tattveśvara-tad-avatāra-jagad-rūpā trividhā śaktiḥ, viṣṇu-śaktiḥ parā proktā kṣetra-jñākhyā tathāparā । avidyā-karma-sañjñānyā tṛtīyā śaktir iṣyate ॥ [vi.pu. 6.5.61] ity uktvā trividhā bhāva-bhāvanā brahma-bhāvanā karma-bhāvanā ubhaya-bhāvanā ceti

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

चतुर्विधो राशिः, चतुर्-विभागः संसृष्टौ चतुर्धा संस्थितः स्थितौ । प्रलयं च करोत्य् अन्ते चतुर्भेदो जनार्दनः ॥ [वि.पु. १.२२.२२] इति प्रथमांशे उक्तः । पूर्वाध्यायोक्ता हि पर-तत्त्वेश्वर-तद्-अवतार-जगद्-रूपा त्रिविधा शक्तिः, विष्णु-शक्तिः परा प्रोक्ता क्षेत्र-ज्ञाख्या तथापरा । अविद्या-कर्म-सञ्ज्ञान्या तृतीया शक्तिर् इष्यते ॥ [वि.पु. ६.५.६१] इत्य् उक्त्वा त्रिविधा भाव-भावना ब्रह्म-भावना कर्म-भावना उभय-भावना चेति

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

“Fourfold class”: in the First Aṃśa it is said, “Janārdana is fourfold in division, is established in four forms at creation and sustenance, and performs dissolution at the end.” The three powers described in the preceding chapter are the Supreme Lord, his incarnations, and the universe; another passage names the supreme power of Viṣṇu, the power called the knower of the field, and a third power called ignorance and action. The three contemplations are contemplation of Brahman, of action, and of both.
