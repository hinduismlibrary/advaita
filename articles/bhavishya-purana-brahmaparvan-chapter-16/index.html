Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 16 — The Five Great Sacrifices and the Pratipad Vow

BP 1.16.1

सुमन्तुरुवाच
इत्युक्त्वा भगवान्ब्रह्मा स्त्रीलक्षणमशेषतः ।
सद्वृत्तं च तथा स्त्रीणां जगाम स निजालयम् ॥

Sumantu said: ‘Having thus explained without omission the marks of women and likewise women’s good conduct, the Blessed Brahmā returned to his own abode.

BP 1.16.2

ऋषयश्च तथा जग्मुः स्वानि धिष्ण्यान्यशेषतः ।
स्त्रीलक्षणं तथा वृत्तं श्रुत्वा कृत्स्नं महीपते ॥

The seers likewise returned to all their respective seats after hearing in full the marks and conduct of women, O ruler of the earth.

BP 1.16.3

इत्थं लक्षणसम्पन्नां भार्यां प्राप्य महीपते ।
कर्तव्यं यद्गृहस्थेन तदिदानीं निबोध मे ॥

Having obtained a wife endowed with the characteristics described, O ruler of the earth, now understand from me what a householder should do.

BP 1.16.4

बैवाहिकाग्नौ कुर्वीत गृह्यं कर्म यथाविधि ।
पञ्चयज्ञविधानं तु पक्तिं कुर्यात्सदा गृही ॥

He should perform the domestic rites according to rule in the fire kindled at marriage, and the householder should continually carry out the prescribed five great sacrifices with prepared food.

BP 1.16.5

पञ्च सूना गृहस्थस्य तेन स्वर्गं न गच्छति ।
कण्डनी पेषणी चुल्ली उदकुम्भीः प्रमार्जनी ॥

A householder has five unavoidable places of injury, through which he would not reach heaven without expiation: the mortar, grinding stone, hearth, water-pot, and broom.

BP 1.16.6

आसां क्रमेण सर्वासां विशुद्ध्यर्थं मनीषिभिः ।
पञ्चोद्दिष्टा महायज्ञाः प्रत्यहं गृहमेधिनाम् ॥

For purification from all these in due order, the wise prescribe five great sacrifices to householders every day.

BP 1.16.7

अध्यापनं ब्रह्मयज्ञः पितृयज्ञश्च तर्पणम् ।
होमो दैवो बलिर्भौमस्तथान्योऽतिथिपूजनम् ॥

Teaching and sacred study are the Brahma-sacrifice; offering water is the ancestor-sacrifice; fire-offering is the divine sacrifice; bali placed upon the earth is the elemental sacrifice; and the other is honouring a guest.

BP 1.16.8

पञ्चैतान्यो महायज्ञान्न हापयति शक्तितः ।
स गृहेऽपि वसन्नित्यं सूनोदोर्षैर्न लिप्यते ॥

One who, according to his ability, does not omit these five great sacrifices remains unstained by the faults of the five places of injury even while living continually at home.

BP 1.16.9

देवतातिथिभृत्यानां पितॄणामात्मनश्च यः ।
न निर्वपति पञ्चानामुच्छ्वसन्न च जीवति ॥

One who does not apportion offerings to the five—to deities, guests, dependants, ancestors, and oneself—merely breathes but does not truly live.

BP 1.16.10

शतानीक उवाच
यस्य नास्ति गृहे त्वग्निः स मृतो नात्र संशयः ।
न स पूजयितुं शक्तो देवादीन्ब्राह्मणोत्तमः ॥

Śatānīka said: ‘One whose house has no sacred fire is as though dead—of this there is no doubt. O best of brāhmaṇas, he is unable to worship the gods and others.

BP 1.16.11

निरग्निकस्य विप्रस्य कथं देवादयो द्विज. ।
प्रीताः स्युः शान्तये तस्य परं कौतूहलं मम ॥

O twice-born sage, how may the gods and others become pleased with a brāhmaṇa who maintains no ritual fire, so that he may obtain peace? My curiosity is very great.

BP 1.16.12

सुमन्तुरुवाच
साधु पृष्टोऽस्मि राजेन्द्र श्रूयतां परमं वचः।
अनग्नयस्तु ये विप्रास्तेषां श्रेयोऽभिधीयते ॥

Sumantu said: ‘You have asked well, O king of kings. Hear the supreme teaching: I shall explain the welfare of those brāhmaṇas who have no sacred fire.

BP 1.16.13

व्रतोपवासनियमैर्नानादानैस्तथा नृप ।
देवादयो भवन्त्येव प्रीतास्तेषां न संशयः ॥

Through vows, fasts, disciplines, and many kinds of gifts, O king, the gods and others certainly become pleased with them; of this there is no doubt.

BP 1.16.14

विशेषादुपवासेन तिथौ किल महीपते ।
प्रीता देवादयस्तेषां भवन्ति कुरुनन्दन ॥

Especially through fasting upon the prescribed lunar date, O ruler of the earth and delight of the Kurus, the gods and others become pleased with them.

BP 1.16.15

शतानीक उवाच
भगवंस्त्वं तिथीन्ब्रूहि तिथीनां च विधिं हि मे ।
प्राशनं गृह्यधर्मांश्च उपवासविधीनपि ॥

Śatānīka said: ‘Blessed sage, tell me the lunar dates and their procedures, the foods to be taken, the domestic duties, and the rules for fasting.

BP 1.16.16

मुच्येम येन पापौघात्त्वत्प्रसाद्द्विजोत्तम ।
संसाराच्चापि विप्रेन्द्र श्रेयसे जगतस्तथा ॥

O best of the twice-born, by your grace may we be released from the mass of sins and from saṃsāra, and may the teaching likewise promote the welfare of the world.

BP 1.16.17

सुमन्तुरुवाच
शृणु कौरव कर्माणि तिथिगुह्याश्रितानि तु ।
श्रुतानि घ्नन्ति पापानि उपोषितफलानि च ॥

Sumantu said: ‘Hear, O Kaurava, the rites founded upon the secret teaching of the lunar dates. Merely heard, they destroy sins; hear also the fruits gained by fasting.

BP 1.16.18

प्रतिपदि क्षीरप्राशनं द्वितीयायां लवणवर्जनम् ।
तृतीयायां तिलान्नं प्राश्नीयाच्चतुर्थ्यां क्षीराशनश्च पञ्चम्याम् ॥
फलाशनः सदा षष्ठ्यां शाकाशनः सप्तम्यां बिल्वाहारोष्टम्यां तु ॥

On Pratipad one takes milk; on the second, food without salt; on the third, sesame-food. The text next prescribes milk across the fourth and fifth sequence, fruit on the sixth, greens on the seventh, and bilva fruit on the eighth.

BP 1.16.19

पिष्टाशनो नवम्यामनग्निपाकाहारो दशम्यामेकादश्यां घृताहारो द्वादश्यां पायसाहारः ।
त्रयोदश्यां गोमूत्राहारश्चतुर्दश्यां यवान्नाहारः ॥

On the ninth one eats flour-preparations; on the tenth, food not cooked by fire; on the eleventh, clarified butter; on the twelfth, milk-rice; on the thirteenth, cow’s urine; and on the fourteenth, barley-food.

BP 1.16.20

कुशोदकप्राशनः पौर्णमास्यां हविष्याहारोऽमावास्यायाम् ।
एष प्राशनविधिस्तिथीनामेव चानेन विधिना पक्षमेकं यो वर्तयति ॥

On the full moon one drinks water touched by kuśa grass, and on the new moon eats sacrificial food. This is the dietary procedure for the lunar dates; whoever observes one fortnight by this rule—

BP 1.16.21

सोऽश्वमेधफलं दशगुणफलमवाप्नोति ।
स्वर्गे मन्वन्तराणि यावत्प्रतिवसति ॥

—obtains fruit ten times that of an Aśvamedha sacrifice and dwells in heaven for the span of many Manvantaras.

BP 1.16.22

उपगीयमानोप्सरोगन्धर्वैर्मासत्रयचतुष्टयम् ।
सोश्वमेधराजसूयानां शतगुणमवाप्नोति ॥

Celebrated in song by Apsarases and Gandharvas, the practitioner who continues for three or four months obtains a hundredfold fruit of the Aśvamedha and Rājasūya sacrifices.

BP 1.16.23

स्वर्गे उपगीयमानोप्सरोगन्धर्वैश्चतुर्युगानां दशशतीर्यावत्प्रतिवसति ।
तथाष्टमासपारणे राजसूयाश्वमेधाभ्यां सहस्रगुणफलमवाप्नोति ॥

Celebrated by Apsarases and Gandharvas, he remains in heaven for a thousand cycles of four ages; upon completing eight months, he obtains a thousandfold fruit of the Rājasūya and Aśvamedha.

BP 1.16.24

स्वर्गे चतुर्दश मन्वतराणि यावत्प्रतिवसति ।
उपगीयमानोप्सरोगन्धर्वैर्य एवं नियममास्थाय वर्षमेकं वर्तयति ॥

He remains in heaven for fourteen Manvantaras, celebrated by Apsarases and Gandharvas. Whoever adopts this discipline and maintains it for one year—

BP 1.16.25

स सवितुर्लोके कालं मन्वन्तरं प्रतिवसति ॥

—dwells in the world of Savitṛ for the duration of a Manvantara.

BP 1.16.26

य एवं नियमान्राजन्नाश्वयुजनवम्यां माघमासस्य सप्तम्यां वैशाखतृतीयायां कार्तिकपौर्णमास्यां तिथिव्रतानि गृदृणाति ब्रह्मचारी गृहस्थो वनस्थो नारी नरो वा शूद्रः प्रयतमानसः दीर्घायुष्यं सवितुः सालोक्यं व्रजति ॥

O king, whoever undertakes these lunar-date vows on the ninth of Āśvayuja, the seventh of Māgha, the third of Vaiśākha, or the full moon of Kārttika—whether celibate student, householder, forest-dweller, woman, man, or śūdra—with a disciplined mind attains long life and residence in Savitṛ’s world.

BP 1.16.27

यैश्चापि पुरा राजन्ननेन विधिना एतासु तिथिष्वन्यजन्मान्तरे उपवासविधिः कृतः दानानि दत्तानि विविधप्रकाराणि ब्राह्मणानां तपस्विजनेषु वा ॥

O king, those who in a former birth fasted according to this rule on these lunar dates and gave many kinds of gifts to brāhmaṇas or ascetics—

BP 1.16.28

त्रिरात्रोपवासिनां तीर्थयात्रातपोगुरुमातापितृशुश्रूशानिरतानां तेषां स्वर्गादिभोगवासनादिहागतानां फलनिष्पत्तिचिह्नानि मनुष्यलोके प्रत्यक्षत एव दृश्यन्ते ॥

—and those devoted to three-night fasts, pilgrimage, austerity, and service to teacher, mother, and father: after enjoying heaven and returning here, the visible signs of their resulting merit are directly observed in the human world.

BP 1.16.29

हस्त्यश्वयानयुग्यधनरत्नकनकहिरण्यकटककेयूरग्रैवेयककटिसूत्रकर्णालङ्कारमुकुटवरवस्त्रवरनारी-वरविलेपनसुरूपगुणदीर्घायुषो विगताधिव्याधयो दानोपवासरतानां फलान्येतानि नृत्यगीत-वादित्रमङ्गलपाठकशब्दैरिहाद्यापि पुण्यकृतो बोध्यमाना दृश्यन्त इति ॥

Elephants, horses, vehicles, draught animals, wealth, jewels, gold, bracelets, armlets, necklaces, girdles, earrings, crowns, fine garments, excellent companions, choice perfumes, beauty, virtue, long life, and freedom from mental and physical illness are declared signs of devotion to gifts and fasting; even now, meritorious people are seen awakened by sounds of dance, song, instruments, and auspicious recitation.

BP 1.16.30

तथाकृतोपवासा अपि हि दृश्यन्ते ॥

Thus people who have performed the prescribed fasts are visibly seen.

BP 1.16.31

तथा अदत्तदाना अकृतपुण्याश्च प्रत्यक्षत एव दृश्यन्ते ॥

Likewise, people who formerly gave no gifts and performed no meritorious acts are also said to be directly observable.

BP 1.16.32

तद्यथा काणकुष्ठिबधिरजडमूकव्यङ्गा रोगदारिद्र्योपसर्गव्याधिहतायुषश्च दृश्यंतेऽद्यापि मानवाः ॥

The source gives as examples people who are one-eyed, afflicted by skin disease, deaf, cognitively impaired, mute, physically disabled, burdened by disease and poverty, or whose lives are shortened by disorders, and claims such people remain visible even today.

BP 1.16.33

शतानीक उवाच
द्विजेन्द्र तिथयः प्रोक्ताः समासेन त्वया बुध ।
विस्तरेणैव मे भूयः प्रब्रूहि द्विजसत्तम ॥

Śatānīka said: ‘O lord of the twice-born, wise one, you have described the lunar dates briefly. O best of the twice-born, explain them to me again in full detail.

BP 1.16.34

रहस्यं यत्तिथीनां तु देवानां च विचेष्टितम् ।
यानीष्टानि च देवानां भोज्यानि नियमास्तथा ॥

Tell me the secret of the lunar dates, the activities of their deities, the foods those gods prefer, and the corresponding disciplines.

BP 1.16.35

तानि मे वद धर्मज्ञ येन पूतो भवेन्वहम् ।
निर्द्वन्द्वो हि यथा विप्र लभे यागफलानि तु ॥

Tell me these, O knower of dharma, so that I may become pure day after day and, free from inner conflict, obtain the fruits of sacrifice.

BP 1.16.36

सुमन्तुरुवाच
रहस्यं यत्तिथीनां च भोजनं फलमेव तु ।
यावच्च येन नियमो विशेषात्स्त्रीजनस्य च ॥

Sumantu said: ‘I shall explain the secrets of the lunar dates, their foods and fruits, and how long each discipline lasts—especially the rules applying to women.

BP 1.16.37

एतत्ते सर्वमाख्यामो रहस्यं तन्निबोध मे ।
यन्मया नोक्तपूर्वं हि कस्यचित्सुप्रियस्य हि ॥

I shall relate all of this to you. Understand that secret from me, which I have never previously told even to anyone especially dear.

BP 1.16.38

तत्तेऽहं सम्प्रवक्ष्यामि यस्य देवस्य या तिथिः ।
देवतानां रहस्यानि व्रतानि नियमास्तथा ॥

I shall now explain which lunar date belongs to which deity, together with the gods’ secrets, vows, and disciplines.

BP 1.16.39

ताञ्छृणुष्व महाबाहो गदतो मम नारद ।
सृष्टिं पूर्वं वदिष्यामि संक्षेपेण तिथिं प्रति ॥

Hear them as I speak, O mighty-armed one. First I shall describe in summary the creation associated with each lunar date.

BP 1.16.40

तमोभूतमिदं त्वासीदलभ्यमवितर्कितम् ।
जगद्ब्रह्मा समागत्यासृजदात्मानमात्मना ॥

This universe was darkness, inaccessible and beyond reasoning. Brahmā came and, through himself, brought his own manifested being into existence.

BP 1.16.41

संभूतात्मैव आत्मासावण्डमध्याद्विनिःसृतः ।
आत्मनैवात्मनो ह्यण्डं सृष्ट्वा स विभुरादितः ॥

That self-born being emerged from within the cosmic egg, having first created the egg of himself by his own power.

BP 1.16.42

ब्रह्मा नारायणाख्योऽसौ सृष्टिं कर्तुं समुद्यतः ।
ताभ्यां सोण्डकपालाभ्यां दिवं भूमिं च निर्ममे ॥

That Brahmā, called Nārāyaṇa, set about creating the cosmos. From the two halves of the egg he fashioned heaven and earth.

BP 1.16.43

दिशश्चैवोपदिशश्चैव देवादीन्दानवांस्तथा ।
तिथिं पूर्वामिमां राजंश्चकाराथ विभुः स्वयम् ॥

He then made the directions and intermediate directions, the gods, the Dānavas, and the rest. O king, the mighty one himself also created this first lunar date.

BP 1.16.44

तिथीनां प्रवरा यस्माद्ब्रह्मणा समुदाहृता ।
प्रतिपादिता परे पूर्वे प्रतिपत्तेन तूच्यते ॥

Because it was declared by Brahmā to be foremost among lunar dates and was established first, it is called Pratipad—the date of first attainment or establishment.

BP 1.16.45

अस्मात्पदात्तु तिथयो यस्मात्त्वन्याः प्रकीर्तिताः ।
अस्यान्ते कथयिष्यामि उपवासविधिं परम् ॥

Because the other lunar dates are proclaimed as proceeding from this first step, I shall explain at the conclusion of its account the supreme procedure for its fast.

BP 1.16.46

कार्त्तिक्यां माघसप्तम्यां वैशाखस्य युगादिषु ।
नियमोपवासं प्रथमं ग्राहयेत विधानवित् ॥

One who knows the procedure should first undertake this disciplined fast on Kārttikī, the seventh of Māgha, the third of Vaiśākha marking the beginning of the age, or the other prescribed commencement dates.

BP 1.16.47

यां तिथिं नियमं कर्तुं भक्त्या समनुगच्छति ।
तस्यां तिथौ विधानं यत्तन्निबोध जनाधिप ॥

O ruler of people, understand the procedure to be followed on whichever lunar date a person approaches with devotion in order to undertake the discipline.

BP 1.16.48

यदा तु प्रतिपद्यां वै गृह्णीयान्नियमं नृप ।
चतुर्दश्यां कृताहारः संकल्पं परिकल्पयेत् ॥

When one undertakes the discipline of Pratipad, O king, one should eat on the fourteenth and then formally establish the resolve.

BP 1.16.49

अमावास्यां न भुञ्जीत त्रिकालं स्नानमाचरेत् ।
पवित्रो हि जपेन्नित्यं गायत्रीं शिरसा सह ॥

On the new-moon day one should not eat and should bathe at all three daily times. Remaining ritually pure, one should continually recite the Gāyatrī together with its śiras formula.

BP 1.16.50

अर्चयित्वा प्रभाते तु गन्धमाल्यैर्द्विजोत्तमान् ।
शक्त्या क्षीरं प्रदद्यात्तु ब्रह्मा मे प्रीयतां प्रभुः ॥

In the morning, after honouring foremost brāhmaṇas with fragrances and garlands, one should give milk according to one’s ability, saying, “May Lord Brahmā be pleased with me.”

BP 1.16.51

ततो भुञ्जीत गोक्षीरमनेन विधिना नृप ।
एष एव विधिर्दृष्टः सर्वासु तिथिषु नृप ॥

Then one should drink cow’s milk, O king. This same general procedure is prescribed for all the lunar-date vows.

BP 1.16.52

संवत्सरगते काले व्रतमेतत्समाप्यते ।
व्रतांते यत्फलं तस्य तन्निबोध नराधिप ॥

When the period of one year has elapsed, this vow is completed. O ruler of people, now understand the fruit obtained at the conclusion of the vow.

BP 1.16.53

विमुक्तपापः शुद्धात्मा दिव्यदेहस्य देहिनः ।
ब्रह्मा ददाति संतुष्टो विमानमतितेजसम् ॥
अव्याहतगतिं दिव्यं किन्नराप्सरसैर्युतम् ॥

Pleased with the embodied person who is freed from sin, purified in self, and possessed of a celestial body, Brahmā grants a brilliantly radiant divine aerial vehicle of unobstructed motion, accompanied by Kinnaras and Apsarases.

BP 1.16.54

रमित्वा सुचिरं तत्र दैवतैः सह देववत् ।
इह चागत्य विप्रत्वं दश जन्मान्यसौ लभेत् ॥

After enjoying that realm for a very long time among the deities like a god, he returns here and obtains birth as a brāhmaṇa for ten successive lives.

BP 1.16.55

वेदवेदांगविद्यश्च दीर्घायुश्चैव सुप्रभः ।
भोगी धनपतिर्दाता जायतेऽसौ कृते युगे ॥

In the Kṛta age he is born learned in the Vedas and Vedāṅgas, long-lived, radiant, prosperous in enjoyments, a lord of wealth, and generous in giving.

BP 1.16.56

विश्वामित्रस्तु राजेन्द्र ब्राह्मणत्वजिगीषया ।
तपश्चचार विपुलं सन्तापाय दिवौकसाम् ॥
ब्राह्मणत्वं न लेभेऽसौ लेभे विघ्नाननेकशः ॥

O king of kings, Viśvāmitra, desiring to attain brāhmaṇa status, performed vast austerity that caused distress among the heavenly beings; yet he did not obtain that status and encountered many obstacles.

BP 1.16.57

ततस्तु नियमात्तेषां तिथीनां प्रवरा तिथिः ।
उपोषिता बहुविधा ज्ञात्वा ब्रह्मप्रियां तिथिम् ॥

Then, learning which date was dear to Brahmā, he observed in many ways the foremost of lunar dates according to its discipline and fasted upon it.

BP 1.16.58

ततो ददौ ब्रह्मा विश्वामित्राय धीमते ।
इहैव तेन देहेन ब्राह्मणत्वं सुदुर्लभम् ॥

Thereupon Brahmā granted the wise Viśvāmitra the exceedingly difficult attainment of brāhmaṇa status here in this very body.

BP 1.16.59

तिथीनां प्रवरा ह्येषा तिथीनामुत्तमा तिथिः ।
क्षत्रियो वैश्यशूद्रौ वा ब्राह्मणत्वमवाप्नुयुः ॥

This is the foremost of lunar days, the best lunar day. By it, a Kṣatriya, Vaiśya, or Śūdra may attain Brāhmaṇa-hood.

BP 1.16.60

एवं तिथिरियं राजन्कामदा कञ्जजप्रिया ।
सरहस्या मया प्रोक्ता या नोक्ता यस्य कस्यचित् ॥

Thus, O king, this lunar date is a granter of desires and dear to the lotus-born Brahmā. I have explained it together with its secret teaching, which is not disclosed indiscriminately.

BP 1.16.61

हैहयैस्तालजङ्घैश्च तुरुष्कैर्यवनैः शकैः ।
उपोषिता इहात्रैव ब्राह्मणत्वमभीप्सुभिः ॥

Haihaya, Tālajaṅgha, Turuṣka, Yavana, and Śaka, desiring Brāhmaṇa-hood, fasted here on this day.

BP 1.16.62

इत्येषा परमा पुण्या शिवा पापहरा तथा ।
पठितोपासिता राजञ्छ्रद्धया च श्रुता तथा ॥

This vow is therefore supremely sacred, auspicious, and destructive of sin when recited, observed, or heard with faith, O king.

BP 1.16.63

माहात्म्यं चापि योप्यस्याः शृणुयान्मानवो नृप ।
ऋद्धिं वृद्धिं तथा कीर्तिं शिवं चाप्य दिवं व्रजेत् ॥

O king, any person who hears the greatness of this vow attains prosperity, increase, fame, auspicious welfare, and finally the celestial realm.
