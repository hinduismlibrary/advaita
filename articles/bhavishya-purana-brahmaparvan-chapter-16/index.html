Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 16 — The Five Great Sacrifices and the Pratipad Vow

BP 1.16.1

सुमन्तुरुवाच
इत्युक्त्वा भगवान्ब्रह्मा स्त्रीलक्षणमशेषतः ।
सद्वृत्तं च तथा स्त्रीणां जगाम स निजालयम् ॥

Sumantu said: ‘Having thus explained without omission the marks of women and likewise women’s good conduct, the Blessed Brahmā returned to his own abode.

Word-for-word
सुमन्तुः उवाच (sumantuḥ uvāca) — Sumantu said;
इति उक्त्वा (iti uktvā) — having spoken thus;
भगवान् ब्रह्मा (bhagavān brahmā) — Blessed Brahmā;
स्त्री-लक्षणम् अशेषतः (strī-lakṣaṇam aśeṣataḥ) — women’s marks without omission;
सद्-वृत्तम् च तथा स्त्रीणाम् (sad-vṛttam ca tathā strīṇām) — and women’s good conduct;
जगाम सः निज-आलयम् (jagāma saḥ nija-ālayam) — returned to his abode
Transliteration
sumanturuvāca
ityuktvā bhagavānbrahmā strīlakṣaṇamaśeṣataḥ |
sadvṛttaṃ ca tathā strīṇāṃ jagāma sa nijālayam | | 1

BP 1.16.2

स्त्री-लक्षणम् तथा वृत्तम् (strī-lakṣaṇam tathā vṛttam) — women’s marks and conduct;
श्रुत्वा कृत्स्नम् (śrutvā kṛtsnam) — hearing completely;

BP 1.16.5

BP 1.16.6

For purification from all these in due order, the wise prescribe five great sacrifices to householders every day.

BP 1.16.7

अध्यापनं ब्रह्मयज्ञः पितृयज्ञश्च तर्पणम् ।
होमो दैवो बलिर्भौमस्तथान्योऽतिथिपूजनम् ॥

Teaching and sacred study are the Brahma-sacrifice; offering water is the ancestor-sacrifice; fire-offering is the divine sacrifice; bali placed upon the earth is the elemental sacrifice; and the other is honouring a guest.

Word-for-word
अध्यापनम् (adhyāpanam) — teaching and sacred study;
ब्रह्म-यज्ञः (brahma-yajñaḥ) — Brahma-sacrifice;
पितृ-यज्ञः च तर्पणम् (pitṛ-yajñaḥ ca tarpaṇam) — ancestor-sacrifice is water offering;
होमः दैवः (homaḥ daivaḥ) — fire-offering is divine;
बलिः भौमः (baliḥ bhaumaḥ) — bali on earth;
तथा अन्यः अतिथि-पूजनम् (tathā anyaḥ atithi-pūjanam) — other is guest honouring
Transliteration
adhyāpanaṃ brahmayajñaḥ pitṛyajñaśca tarpaṇam |
homo daivo balirbhaumastathānyo'tithipūjanam | |7

BP 1.16.8

पञ्चैतान्यो महायज्ञान्न हापयति शक्तितः ।
स गृहेऽपि वसन्नित्यं सूनोदोर्षैर्न लिप्यते ॥

One who, according to his ability, does not omit these five great sacrifices remains unstained by the faults of the five places of injury even while living continually at home.

Word-for-word
पञ्च एतान् महा-यज्ञान् (pañca etān mahā-yajñān) — these five great sacrifices;
न हापयति शक्तितः (na hāpayati śaktitaḥ) — does not omit according to ability;
सः गृहे अपि वसन् नित्यम् (saḥ gṛhe api vasan nityam) — living continually at home;
सूना-दोषैः न लिप्यते (sūnā-doṣaiḥ na lipyate) — not stained by faults of injury
Transliteration
pañcaitānyo mahāyajñānna hāpayati śaktitaḥ |
sa gṛhe'pi vasannityaṃ sūnodorṣairna lipyate | | 8

BP 1.16.9

देवतातिथिभृत्यानां पितॄणामात्मनश्च यः ।
न निर्वपति पञ्चानामुच्छ्वसन्न च जीवति ॥

One who does not apportion offerings to the five—to deities, guests, dependants, ancestors, and oneself—merely breathes but does not truly live.

Word-for-word
देवता (devatā) — deities;
अतिथि (atithi) — guests;
भृत्यानाम् (bhṛtyānām) — dependants;
पितॄणाम् (pitṝṇām) — ancestors;
आत्मनः च (ātmanaḥ ca) — and oneself;
यः न निर्वपति पञ्चानाम् (yaḥ na nirvapati pañcānām) — one who does not apportion to five;
उच्छ्वसन् (ucchvasan) — breathing;
न च जीवति (na ca jīvati) — does not live
Transliteration
devatātithibhṛtyānāṃ pitṝṇāmātmanaśca yaḥ |

BP 1.16.10

शतानीक उवाच
यस्य नास्ति गृहे त्वग्निः स मृतो नात्र संशयः ।
न स पूजयितुं शक्तो देवादीन्ब्राह्मणोत्तमः ॥

Śatānīka said: ‘One whose house has no sacred fire is as though dead—of this there is no doubt. O best of brāhmaṇas, he is unable to worship the gods and others.

Word-for-word
शतानीकः उवाच (śatānīkaḥ uvāca) — Śatānīka said;
यस्य न अस्ति गृहे अग्निः (yasya na asti gṛhe agniḥ) — whose house has no fire;
सः मृतः (saḥ mṛtaḥ) — he is dead;
न अत्र संशयः (na atra saṃśayaḥ) — no doubt;
न सः पूजयितुम् शक्तः (na saḥ pūjayitum śaktaḥ) — unable to worship;
देव-आदीन् (deva-ādīn) — gods and others;
ब्राह्मण-उत्तम (brāhmaṇa-uttama) — O best brāhmaṇa
Transliteration
śatānīka uvāca
yasya nāsti gṛhe tvagniḥ sa mṛto nātra saṃśayaḥ |
na sa pūjayituṃ śakto devādīnbrāhmaṇottamaḥ | | 1.16.10

BP 1.16.18

प्रतिपदि क्षीरप्राशनं द्वितीयायां लवणवर्जनम् ।
तृतीयायां तिलान्नं प्राश्नीयाच्चतुर्थ्यां क्षीराशनश्च पञ्चम्याम् ॥
फलाशनः सदा षष्ठ्यां शाकाशनः सप्तम्यां बिल्वाहारोष्टम्यां तु ॥

On Pratipad one takes milk; on the second, food without salt; on the third, sesame-food. The text next prescribes milk across the fourth and fifth sequence, fruit on the sixth, greens on the seventh, and bilva fruit on the eighth.

Word-for-word

तृतीयायाम् (tṛtīyāyām) — on third;
तिल-अन्नम् प्राश्नीयात् (tila-annam prāśnīyāt) — should eat sesame-food;
चतुर्थ्याम् (caturthyām) — on fourth;
