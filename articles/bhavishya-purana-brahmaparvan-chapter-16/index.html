Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 16 — The Five Great Sacrifices and the First Lunar Day Vow

BP 1.16.1

सुमन्तुरुवाच
इत्युक्त्वा भगवान्ब्रह्मा स्त्रीलक्षणमशेषतः ।
सद्वृत्तं च तथा स्त्रीणां जगाम स निजालयम् ॥

Sumantu said: Having thus explained without omission the marks of women and the good conduct of women, the Blessed Brahmā went to his own abode.

BP 1.16.2

ऋषयश्च तथा जग्मुः स्वानि धिष्ण्यान्यशेषतः ।
स्त्रीलक्षणं तथा वृत्तं श्रुत्वा कृत्स्नं महीपते ॥

The seers likewise went to their own abodes after hearing fully about the marks and conduct of women, O king.

BP 1.16.3

इत्थं लक्षणसम्पन्नां भार्यां प्राप्य महीपते ।
कर्तव्यं यद्गृहस्थेन तदिदानीं निबोध मे ॥

Having obtained a wife endowed with these qualities, O king, now learn from me what a householder should do.

BP 1.16.4

बैवाहिकाग्नौ कुर्वीत गृह्यं कर्म यथाविधि ।
पञ्चयज्ञविधानं तु पक्तिं कुर्यात्सदा गृही ॥

One should perform the domestic rites in the marriage fire according to rule; the householder should always prepare the five sacrifices.

BP 1.16.5

पञ्च सूना गृहस्थस्य तेन स्वर्गं न गच्छति ।
कण्डनी पेषणी चुल्ली उदकुम्भीः प्रमार्जनी ॥

A householder has five slaughter-places; because of these, he does not go to heaven: the mortar, the grinding stone, the hearth, the water-pot, and the broom.

BP 1.16.6

आसां क्रमेण सर्वासां विशुद्ध्यर्थं मनीषिभिः ।
पञ्चोद्दिष्टा महायज्ञाः प्रत्यहं गृहमेधिनाम् ॥

For the purification of all these, the wise have prescribed five great sacrifices every day for householders.

BP 1.16.7

अध्यापनं ब्रह्मयज्ञः पितृयज्ञश्च तर्पणम् ।
होमो दैवो बलिर्भौमस्तथान्योऽतिथिपूजनम् ॥

Teaching is the Brahma sacrifice; the ancestor sacrifice is the libation; the fire-offering is the divine sacrifice; the offering on earth is the offering to beings; and the other is the honoring of a guest.

BP 1.16.8

पञ्चैतान्यो महायज्ञान्न हापयति शक्तितः ।
स गृहेऽपि वसन्नित्यं सूनोदोर्षैर्न लिप्यते ॥

Whoever, according to his ability, does not omit these five great sacrifices is not stained, even while living at home, by the faults of the five slaughter-places.

BP 1.16.9

देवतातिथिभृत्यानां पितॄणामात्मनश्च यः ।
न निर्वपति पञ्चानामुच्छ्वसन्न च जीवति ॥

Whoever does not offer a share to the five—gods, guests, dependants, ancestors, and himself—merely breathes; he does not truly live.

BP 1.16.10

शतानीक उवाच
यस्य नास्ति गृहे त्वग्निः स मृतो नात्र संशयः ।
न स पूजयितुं शक्तो देवादीन्ब्राह्मणोत्तमः ॥

Śatānīka said: One whose home has no fire is dead; of this there is no doubt. O best of Brahmins, he is unable to worship the gods and the others.

BP 1.16.11

निरग्निकस्य विप्रस्य कथं देवादयो द्विज. ।
प्रीताः स्युः शान्तये तस्य परं कौतूहलं मम ॥

O twice-born one, how can the gods and the others be pleased for the welfare of a Brahmin who has no fire? My curiosity about this is great.

BP 1.16.12

सुमन्तुरुवाच
साधु पृष्टोऽस्मि राजेन्द्र श्रूयतां परमं वचः।
अनग्नयस्तु ये विप्रास्तेषां श्रेयोऽभिधीयते ॥

Sumantu said: You have asked well, O king of kings. Hear the highest teaching: I shall tell the welfare of those Brahmins who have no fire.

BP 1.16.13

व्रतोपवासनियमैर्नानादानैस्तथा नृप ।
देवादयो भवन्त्येव प्रीतास्तेषां न संशयः ॥

Through vows, fasts, disciplines, and various gifts, the gods and the others certainly become pleased with them; there is no doubt.

BP 1.16.14

विशेषादुपवासेन तिथौ किल महीपते ।
प्रीता देवादयस्तेषां भवन्ति कुरुनन्दन ॥

Especially through fasting on a lunar date, the gods and the others become pleased with them, O delight of the Kurus.

BP 1.16.15

शतानीक उवाच
भगवंस्त्वं तिथीन्ब्रूहि तिथीनां च विधिं हि मे ।
प्राशनं गृह्यधर्मांश्च उपवासविधीनपि ॥

Śatānīka said: O venerable one, tell me the lunar dates and their rules, the foods to be eaten, the duties of household life, and the rules for fasting—

BP 1.16.16

मुच्येम येन पापौघात्त्वत्प्रसाद्द्विजोत्तम ।
संसाराच्चापि विप्रेन्द्र श्रेयसे जगतस्तथा ॥

—so that by your grace we may be freed from the mass of sins and from the cycle of rebirth, O best of Brahmins, and so that the world may prosper.

BP 1.16.17

सुमन्तुरुवाच
शृणु कौरव कर्माणि तिथिगुह्याश्रितानि तु ।
श्रुतानि घ्नन्ति पापानि उपोषितफलानि च ॥

Sumantu said: Hear, O Kaurava, the rites attached to the secret observances of the lunar dates. Hearing them destroys sins; hear also the fruits of fasting.

BP 1.16.18

प्रतिपदि क्षीरप्राशनं द्वितीयायां लवणवर्जनम् ।
तृतीयायां तिलान्नं प्राश्नीयाच्चतुर्थ्यां क्षीराशनश्च पञ्चम्याम् ॥
फलाशनः सदा षष्ठ्यां शाकाशनः सप्तम्यां बिल्वाहारोष्टम्यां तु ॥

On the first lunar day one drinks milk; on the second, one abstains from salt; on the third, one eats sesame and rice; on the fourth and fifth, one takes milk; on the sixth, fruit; on the seventh, greens; on the eighth, bilva fruit.

BP 1.16.19

पिष्टाशनो नवम्यामनग्निपाकाहारो दशम्यामेकादश्यां घृताहारो द्वादश्यां पायसाहारः ।
त्रयोदश्यां गोमूत्राहारश्चतुर्दश्यां यवान्नाहारः ॥

On the ninth one eats flour preparations; on the tenth, food not cooked by fire; on the eleventh, ghee; on the twelfth, milk pudding; on the thirteenth, cow’s urine; and on the fourteenth, barley food.

BP 1.16.20

कुशोदकप्राशनः पौर्णमास्यां हविष्याहारोऽमावास्यायाम् ।
एष प्राशनविधिस्तिथीनामेव चानेन विधिना पक्षमेकं यो वर्तयति ॥

On the full moon one drinks water mixed with sacred grass; on the new moon one eats food fit for an offering. This is the food rule for the lunar dates. Whoever observes one fortnight by this rule—

BP 1.16.21

सोऽश्वमेधफलं दशगुणफलमवाप्नोति ।
स्वर्गे मन्वन्तराणि यावत्प्रतिवसति ॥

—obtains ten times the fruit of a horse sacrifice and lives in heaven for the duration of the Manu ages.

BP 1.16.22

उपगीयमानोप्सरोगन्धर्वैर्मासत्रयचतुष्टयम् ।
सोश्वमेधराजसूयानां शतगुणमवाप्नोति ॥

Sung to by heavenly nymphs and celestial musicians for three or four months, he obtains a hundredfold the fruit of horse sacrifices and royal consecrations.

BP 1.16.23

स्वर्गे उपगीयमानोप्सरोगन्धर्वैश्चतुर्युगानां दशशतीर्यावत्प्रतिवसति ।
तथाष्टमासपारणे राजसूयाश्वमेधाभ्यां सहस्रगुणफलमवाप्नोति ॥

Sung to by heavenly nymphs and celestial musicians, he dwells in heaven for a thousand cycles of four ages; after completing eight months, he obtains a thousandfold fruit from royal consecrations and horse sacrifices.

BP 1.16.24

स्वर्गे चतुर्दश मन्वतराणि यावत्प्रतिवसति ।
उपगीयमानोप्सरोगन्धर्वैर्य एवं नियममास्थाय वर्षमेकं वर्तयति ॥

He dwells in heaven for fourteen Manu ages, sung to by heavenly nymphs and celestial musicians. Whoever undertakes this discipline and observes it for one year—

BP 1.16.25

स सवितुर्लोके कालं मन्वन्तरं प्रतिवसति ॥

—dwells for a Manu age in the world of the Sun.

BP 1.16.26

य एवं नियमान्राजन्नाश्वयुजनवम्यां माघमासस्य सप्तम्यां वैशाखतृतीयायां कार्तिकपौर्णमास्यां तिथिव्रतानि गृदृणाति ब्रह्मचारी गृहस्थो वनस्थो नारी नरो वा शूद्रः प्रयतमानसः दीर्घायुष्यं सवितुः सालोक्यं व्रजति ॥

Whoever observes these rules on the ninth of Āśvayuja, the seventh of Māgha, the third of Vaiśākha, or the full moon of Kārttika—whether a celibate student, householder, forest-dweller, woman, man, or Śūdra, with a disciplined mind—attains long life and the world of the Sun.

BP 1.16.27

यैश्चापि पुरा राजन्ननेन विधिना एतासु तिथिष्वन्यजन्मान्तरे उपवासविधिः कृतः दानानि दत्तानि विविधप्रकाराणि ब्राह्मणानां तपस्विजनेषु वा ॥

Those who formerly, in another birth, observed the fast on these lunar dates according to this rule and gave various gifts to Brahmins or ascetics—

BP 1.16.28

त्रिरात्रोपवासिनां तीर्थयात्रातपोगुरुमातापितृशुश्रूशानिरतानां तेषां स्वर्गादिभोगवासनादिहागतानां फलनिष्पत्तिचिह्नानि मनुष्यलोके प्रत्यक्षत एव दृश्यन्ते ॥

—and those devoted to three-night fasts, pilgrimages, austerities, and service to teachers, mothers, and fathers: when they return here after desiring pleasures such as those of heaven, the signs of their attained fruit are directly seen in the human world.

BP 1.16.29

हस्त्यश्वयानयुग्यधनरत्नकनकहिरण्यकटककेयूरग्रैवेयककटिसूत्रकर्णालङ्कारमुकुटवरवस्त्रवरनारी-वरविलेपनसुरूपगुणदीर्घायुषो विगताधिव्याधयो दानोपवासरतानां फलान्येतानि नृत्यगीत-वादित्रमङ्गलपाठकशब्दैरिहाद्यापि पुण्यकृतो बोध्यमाना दृश्यन्त इति ॥

Elephants, horses, vehicles, draft animals, wealth, jewels, gold, bracelets, armlets, necklaces, waist-cords, ear ornaments, crowns, fine garments, excellent women, unguents, beauty, virtue, long life, and freedom from mental and bodily illness are the fruits of giving and fasting. Even today, the meritorious are seen and recognized by sounds of dancing, singing, instruments, and auspicious recitation.

BP 1.16.30

तथाकृतोपवासा अपि हि दृश्यन्ते ॥

People who have observed the fasts are likewise seen.

BP 1.16.31

तथा अदत्तदाना अकृतपुण्याश्च प्रत्यक्षत एव दृश्यन्ते ॥

Likewise, people who gave no gifts and did no meritorious acts are directly seen.

BP 1.16.32

तद्यथा काणकुष्ठिबधिरजडमूकव्यङ्गा रोगदारिद्र्योपसर्गव्याधिहतायुषश्च दृश्यंतेऽद्यापि मानवाः ॥

Even today people are seen who are blind in one eye, afflicted with skin disease, deaf, dull, mute, deformed, oppressed by disease and poverty, or whose lives are cut short by illness.

BP 1.16.33

शतानीक उवाच
द्विजेन्द्र तिथयः प्रोक्ताः समासेन त्वया बुध ।
विस्तरेणैव मे भूयः प्रब्रूहि द्विजसत्तम ॥

Śatānīka said: O lord of the twice-born, wise one, you have told me the lunar dates briefly. O best of the twice-born, tell me about them again in detail.

BP 1.16.34

रहस्यं यत्तिथीनां तु देवानां च विचेष्टितम् ।
यानीष्टानि च देवानां भोज्यानि नियमास्तथा ॥

Tell me the secret of the lunar dates, the acts of their gods, the foods dear to the gods, and their rules.

BP 1.16.35

तानि मे वद धर्मज्ञ येन पूतो भवेन्वहम् ।
निर्द्वन्द्वो हि यथा विप्र लभे यागफलानि तु ॥

Tell me these, O knower of duty, so that I may be purified and, free from opposites, obtain the fruits of sacrifice.

BP 1.16.36

सुमन्तुरुवाच
रहस्यं यत्तिथीनां च भोजनं फलमेव तु ।
यावच्च येन नियमो विशेषात्स्त्रीजनस्य च ॥

Sumantu said: I shall tell you the secret of the lunar dates, their foods, their fruits, and the duration of their rules, especially those for women.

BP 1.16.37

एतत्ते सर्वमाख्यामो रहस्यं तन्निबोध मे ।
यन्मया नोक्तपूर्वं हि कस्यचित्सुप्रियस्य हि ॥

I shall tell you all this; learn that secret from me, which I have never before told to anyone dear to me.

BP 1.16.38

तत्तेऽहं सम्प्रवक्ष्यामि यस्य देवस्य या तिथिः ।
देवतानां रहस्यानि व्रतानि नियमास्तथा ॥

I shall explain to you which lunar date belongs to which god, the gods’ secrets, vows, and rules.

BP 1.16.39

ताञ्छृणुष्व महाबाहो गदतो मम नारद ।
सृष्टिं पूर्वं वदिष्यामि संक्षेपेण तिथिं प्रति ॥

Hear me speak of them, O mighty-armed one. First I shall briefly describe the creation connected with each lunar date.

BP 1.16.40

तमोभूतमिदं त्वासीदलभ्यमवितर्कितम् ।
जगद्ब्रह्मा समागत्यासृजदात्मानमात्मना ॥

This universe was darkness, inaccessible and inconceivable. Brahmā came and created the world from himself, by himself.

BP 1.16.41

संभूतात्मैव आत्मासावण्डमध्याद्विनिःसृतः ।
आत्मनैवात्मनो ह्यण्डं सृष्ट्वा स विभुरादितः ॥

That self-born being emerged from the middle of the egg. At the beginning, he created the egg from himself, by himself.

BP 1.16.42

ब्रह्मा नारायणाख्योऽसौ सृष्टिं कर्तुं समुद्यतः ।
ताभ्यां सोण्डकपालाभ्यां दिवं भूमिं च निर्ममे ॥

Brahmā, called Nārāyaṇa, set about creating. From the two halves of the egg he made heaven and earth.

BP 1.16.43

दिशश्चैवोपदिशश्चैव देवादीन्दानवांस्तथा ।
तिथिं पूर्वामिमां राजंश्चकाराथ विभुः स्वयम् ॥

He also made the directions and intermediate directions, the gods and the Dānavas. Then the mighty one himself made this first lunar date, O king.

BP 1.16.44

तिथीनां प्रवरा यस्माद्ब्रह्मणा समुदाहृता ।
प्रतिपादिता परे पूर्वे प्रतिपत्तेन तूच्यते ॥

Because Brahmā declared it foremost among the lunar dates and established it first, it is called the first lunar day.

BP 1.16.45

अस्मात्पदात्तु तिथयो यस्मात्त्वन्याः प्रकीर्तिताः ।
अस्यान्ते कथयिष्यामि उपवासविधिं परम् ॥

Since the other lunar dates are declared from this one, at the end of its account I shall tell the supreme rule for fasting.

BP 1.16.46

कार्त्तिक्यां माघसप्तम्यां वैशाखस्य युगादिषु ।
नियमोपवासं प्रथमं ग्राहयेत विधानवित् ॥

One who knows the rule should first undertake the disciplined fast on Kārttikī, the seventh of Māgha, and the age-beginning day in Vaiśākha.

BP 1.16.47

यां तिथिं नियमं कर्तुं भक्त्या समनुगच्छति ।
तस्यां तिथौ विधानं यत्तन्निबोध जनाधिप ॥

Whatever lunar date a person devoutly undertakes a discipline upon, O ruler of people, learn the rule to be observed on that date.

BP 1.16.48

यदा तु प्रतिपद्यां वै गृह्णीयान्नियमं नृप ।
चतुर्दश्यां कृताहारः संकल्पं परिकल्पयेत् ॥

When one undertakes the discipline of the first lunar day, O king, one should eat on the fourteenth and make the resolve.

BP 1.16.49

अमावास्यां न भुञ्जीत त्रिकालं स्नानमाचरेत् ।
पवित्रो हि जपेन्नित्यं गायत्रीं शिरसा सह ॥

On the new-moon day one should not eat and should bathe at the three daily times. Being pure, one should always recite the Gāyatrī together with its head formula.

BP 1.16.50

अर्चयित्वा प्रभाते तु गन्धमाल्यैर्द्विजोत्तमान् ।
शक्त्या क्षीरं प्रदद्यात्तु ब्रह्मा मे प्रीयतां प्रभुः ॥

In the morning, after honoring the foremost Brahmins with fragrances and garlands, one should give milk according to one’s ability, saying, “May Lord Brahmā be pleased with me.”

BP 1.16.51

ततो भुञ्जीत गोक्षीरमनेन विधिना नृप ।
एष एव विधिर्दृष्टः सर्वासु तिथिषु नृप ॥

Then one should drink cow’s milk in this way, O king. This same rule is prescribed for all the lunar dates, O king.

BP 1.16.52

संवत्सरगते काले व्रतमेतत्समाप्यते ।
व्रतांते यत्फलं तस्य तन्निबोध नराधिप ॥

When a year has passed, this vow is completed. Hear, O ruler of people, the fruit of the vow at its end.

BP 1.16.53

विमुक्तपापः शुद्धात्मा दिव्यदेहस्य देहिनः ।
ब्रह्मा ददाति संतुष्टो विमानमतितेजसम् ॥
अव्याहतगतिं दिव्यं किन्नराप्सरसैर्युतम् ॥

Pleased with the person who is freed from sin, pure in self, and embodied in a divine body, Brahmā grants a very radiant divine aerial car, with unobstructed movement, accompanied by celestial musicians and nymphs.

BP 1.16.54

रमित्वा सुचिरं तत्र दैवतैः सह देववत् ।
इह चागत्य विप्रत्वं दश जन्मान्यसौ लभेत् ॥

Having enjoyed there for a long time with the gods, like a god, he returns here and obtains birth as a Brahmin for ten lives.

BP 1.16.55

वेदवेदांगविद्यश्च दीर्घायुश्चैव सुप्रभः ।
भोगी धनपतिर्दाता जायतेऽसौ कृते युगे ॥

In the Kṛta age he is born learned in the Vedas and their auxiliary disciplines, long-lived, radiant, rich in enjoyments, lord of wealth, and generous.

BP 1.16.56

विश्वामित्रस्तु राजेन्द्र ब्राह्मणत्वजिगीषया ।
तपश्चचार विपुलं सन्तापाय दिवौकसाम् ॥
ब्राह्मणत्वं न लेभेऽसौ लेभे विघ्नाननेकशः ॥

O king of kings, Viśvāmitra, desiring to attain Brahminhood, performed great austerity to distress the dwellers in heaven; he did not attain Brahminhood and encountered many obstacles.

BP 1.16.57

ततस्तु नियमात्तेषां तिथीनां प्रवरा तिथिः ।
उपोषिता बहुविधा ज्ञात्वा ब्रह्मप्रियां तिथिम् ॥

Then, learning that the foremost lunar date was dear to Brahmā, he fasted on it in many ways according to its discipline.

BP 1.16.58

ततो ददौ ब्रह्मा विश्वामित्राय धीमते ।
इहैव तेन देहेन ब्राह्मणत्वं सुदुर्लभम् ॥

Then Brahmā granted wise Viśvāmitra the very difficult attainment of Brahminhood here in this very body.

BP 1.16.59

तिथीनां प्रवरा ह्येषा तिथीनामुत्तमा तिथिः ।
क्षत्रियो वैश्यशूद्रौ वा ब्राह्मणत्वमवाप्नुयुः ॥

This is the foremost, the best of lunar dates. By it a Kṣatriya, a Vaiśya, or a Śūdra may attain Brahminhood.

BP 1.16.60

एवं तिथिरियं राजन्कामदा कञ्जजप्रिया ।
सरहस्या मया प्रोक्ता या नोक्ता यस्य कस्यचित् ॥

Thus, O king, this lunar date grants desires and is dear to the lotus-born one. I have told it to you with its secret, which I had never told anyone.

BP 1.16.61

हैहयैस्तालजङ्घैश्च तुरुष्कैर्यवनैः शकैः ।
उपोषिता इहात्रैव ब्राह्मणत्वमभीप्सुभिः ॥

The Haihayas, Tālajaṅghas, Turuṣkas, Yavanas, and Śakas fasted here, desiring to attain Brahminhood.

BP 1.16.62

इत्येषा परमा पुण्या शिवा पापहरा तथा ।
पठितोपासिता राजञ्छ्रद्धया च श्रुता तथा ॥

This is the supremely meritorious and auspicious observance, which destroys sin when recited, observed, and heard with faith, O king.

BP 1.16.63

माहात्म्यं चापि योप्यस्याः शृणुयान्मानवो नृप ।
ऋद्धिं वृद्धिं तथा कीर्तिं शिवं चाप्य दिवं व्रजेत् ॥

Whoever hears the greatness of this observance, O king, attains prosperity, increase, fame, auspiciousness, and goes to heaven.
