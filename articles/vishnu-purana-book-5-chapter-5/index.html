VP 5.5.1

विमुक्तो वसुदेवोऽपि नन्दस्य शकटं गतः ।
प्रहृष्टं दृष्टवान् नन्दं पुत्रो जातो ममैव वै ॥१॥

Vasudeva too, released, went to Nanda’s cart; he saw Nanda delighted, saying, “A son has indeed been born to me.”

Śrīdhara translation

Knowing that all the demons had been sent by Kaṃsa to kill children, Vasudeva too went to Nanda’s cart station to send Nanda and the others out for Gokula’s protection. “The cart” means the place where the cart was unyoked.

VP 5.5.3

दत्तो हि वार्षिकः सर्वो भवद्भिर् नृपतेः करः ।

The king’s entire annual tax has indeed been paid by you; you came for this purpose, therefore the wealthy should not remain here.

Śrīdhara translation

Wealthy people whose business is complete should not remain near a wicked king; this instruction is expressed in the two verses beginning “The annual tax indeed…”

VP 5.5.5

“Born of Rohiṇī” means he whose birth was in Rohiṇī. “His own” means regarded as belonging to him; this is the hidden intention.

VP 5.5.19

मुखं बाहू प्रबाहू च मनः सर्वेन्द्रियाणि च ।
रक्षत्व् अव्याहतैश्वर्यस् तव नारायणोऽव्ययः
विष्नुचित्तः : मुखम् इति । प्रबाहू कूर्पराद् अधो-भागौ ॥१९॥

May the imperishable Nārāyaṇa, whose sovereignty is unobstructed, protect your face, arms, forearms, mind, and all the senses.

Śrīdhara translation

“Forearms” means the two parts below the elbows.

VP 5.5.22

एवं कृत-स्वस्त्ययनो नन्द-गोपेन बालकः ।
शायितः शकटस्याधो बाल-पर्यङ्किका-तले
विष्नुचित्तः :एवम् इति । स्वस्त्ययनं क्षेम-प्रापणं कर्म बाल-पर्यङ्किका बालार्थोऽल्प-पर्यङ्क-तलम् उपरि-भागः ॥२२॥

Thus, after Nandagopa performed the auspicious rite, the child was laid beneath the cart on a small child’s bed.

Śrīdhara translation

“Thus”: he upon whom the auspicious rite was performed. The rite brings well-being. A child’s bed is a small couch suited to a child; he was laid on its surface beneath the cart. This introduces the cart-overturning episode.
