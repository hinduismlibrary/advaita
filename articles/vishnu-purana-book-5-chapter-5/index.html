VP 5.5.1

विमुक्तो वसुदेवोऽपि नन्दस्य शकटं गतः ।
प्रहृष्टं दृष्टवान्नन्दं पुत्रो जातो ममेति वै

Sri Paraasharar - Vasudeva approaches Nanda, and having seen him, is very happy. Nanda is also very happy, and says that a son is born to him. Vasudeva also tells him that "It is your good luck, good fortune that you have got a son".

VP 5.5.3

दत्तो हि वार्षिकःसर्वो भवद्भिर्नृपतेः करः ।
यदर्थमागतास्तस्मान्नात्र स्थेयं महाधनैः

Vasudeva says - "You came to pay the tax and other things to Kamsa, and now that you have finished your work, you should not stay any longer. Those who have money should not stay longer than needed". The yearly taxes and others that you have paid. When the king comes to know that someone is very rich, they may tax more money, and it is not a good thing. That was not the real intention of Vasudeva. Why he said that is that Krishna is there, and Nandagopa has to go there, and look after him, so that he should not stay here longer, because Kamsa may start thinking of doing some harm. That was the real intention of Vasudeva.
