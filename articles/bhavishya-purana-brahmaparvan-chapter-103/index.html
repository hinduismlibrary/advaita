Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 103 — The Greatness of Sun Worship

BP 1.103.1

ब्रह्मोवाच। यश्च देवालयं भक्त्या भानोः कारयते स्थिरम्। स सप्त पुरुषांल्लोकान्भानोर्नयति मानवः ॥

Whoever builds a lasting temple of Bhānu with devotion leads seven generations of people to the worlds of Bhānu.

BP 1.103.2

यावन्त्यब्दानि देवार्चा रवेस्तिष्ठति मन्दिरे। तावद्वर्षसहस्राणि सूर्यलोके स मोदते ॥

For as many years as the image of Ravi remains in the temple, for that many thousands of years he rejoices in the world of the Sun.

BP 1.103.3

देवार्चा लक्षणोपेता यद्गृहे सन्ततो विधिः। निष्कामं वा मनो यस्य स याति रविसाम्यताम् ॥

A person in whose house the image of the deity bears the prescribed marks and worship is always performed, whose mind is without desire, attains equality with Ravi.

BP 1.103.4

पुष्पाण्यतिसुगन्धीनि मनोज्ञानि च यः पुमान्। प्रयच्छति हि देवेशं तद्भावगतमानसः ॥

Whoever offers very fragrant and pleasing flowers to the Lord of the gods, his mind absorbed in him,

BP 1.103.5

धूपांश्च विविधांस्तांस्तान्गन्धाढ्यं चानुलेपनम्। दीपबल्युपहाराश्च यच्चाभीष्टमथात्मनः ॥

and varied incenses, richly perfumed unguents, lamps, offerings, and whatever else he desires for himself,

BP 1.103.6

नरः सोऽनुदिनं यज्ञात्प्राप्नोत्याराधनाद्रवे। यज्ञेशो भगवान्भानुर्मखैरपि च तोष्यते ॥

obtains merit day by day through sacrifice and worship of Ravi. Bhānu, the blessed lord of sacrifices, is also pleased by sacrificial rites.

BP 1.103.7

बहूपकरणा यज्ञा नानासम्भारविस्तराः। प्राप्यन्ते तैर्धनयुतैर्मनुष्यैर्लोकसञ्चयैः ॥

Sacrifices require many implements and plentiful preparations of many kinds. They are performed by wealthy people with gathered multitudes.

BP 1.103.8

भक्त्या तु पुरुषैः पूजा कृता दूर्वांकुरैरपि। रवेर्ददाति हि फलं सर्वयज्ञैः सुदुर्लभम् ॥

But worship performed by people with even shoots of Bermuda grass gives Ravi a reward very difficult to obtain through all sacrifices.

BP 1.103.9

यानि पुष्पाणि भक्ष्याणि धूपगन्धानुलेपनम्। दयितं भूषणं यच्च रक्तके चैव वाससी ॥

Whatever flowers, foods, incense, fragrant unguents, beloved ornaments, and red garments there are,

BP 1.103.10

यानि चाभ्युपहाराणि भक्ष्याणि च फलानि च। प्रयच्छ तानि देवेश भवेथाश्चैव तन्मनाः ॥

whatever offerings, foods, and fruits there are, offer them to the Lord of the gods and keep your mind fixed on him.

BP 1.103.11

आद्यं तं यज्ञपुरुषं यथाशक्त्या प्रसादय। आराध्य स्थापितं देवं तस्मिन्नेव नरालये ॥

Please the primordial Sacrifice Person according to your ability, worshipping the deity installed in that very human temple.

BP 1.103.12

पुष्पैस्तीर्थोदकैर्गन्धैर्मधुना सर्पिषा तथा। क्षीरेण स्नापयेद्देवं चित्रभानुं दिवाकरम् ॥

With flowers, sacred waters, fragrances, honey, clarified butter, and milk, one should bathe the deity, Citrabhānu, the maker of day.

BP 1.103.13

दधिक्षीरह्रदान्याति स्वर्गलोकान्मधुच्युतान्। प्रयास्यति यदुश्रेष्ठ निर्वृतिं वापि शाश्वतीम् ॥

He reaches heaven worlds with lakes of curds and milk flowing with honey, O best of the Yadus, and attains eternal bliss.

BP 1.103.14

स्तोत्रैर्गीतैस्तथा वाद्यैर्ब्राह्मणानां च तर्पणैः। मनसश्चैकतायोगादाराधय विभावसुम् ॥

With hymns, songs, instruments, offerings to Brahmins, and concentration of mind, worship Vibhāvasu.

BP 1.103.15

आराध्य तं विदेहानां पुरुषाः सप्तसप्ततिः। हैहयानां च पञ्चाशदमृतत्वं समागताः ॥

By worshipping him, seventy seven men of Videha and fifty of the Haihayas attained immortality.

BP 1.103.16

स त्वमेभिः प्रकारैस्तमुपवासैस्तु भास्करम्। सन्तोषय हि तुष्टोऽसौ भानुर्भवति शान्तिदः ॥

Thus, satisfy Bhāskara by these methods and by fasting. When pleased, Bhānu becomes the giver of peace.

BP 1.103.17

कृष्ण उवाच। उपवासैश्चित्रभानुः कथं तुष्टः प्रजायते। परिचर्या कथं कार्या या कार्या चोपवासिना ॥

Kṛṣṇa said. Explain how fasting pleases Citrabhānu and how the service required of a person who fasts should be performed.

BP 1.103.18

यद्यत्कार्यं यदा चैव भानोराराधनं नरैः। तत्सर्वं विस्तराद्ब्रह्मन्यथावद्वक्तुमर्हसि ॥

Whatever people should do for the worship of Bhānu, and whenever it should be done, explain all of it fully and properly, O Brahmā.

BP 1.103.19

ब्रह्मोवाच। स्मृतः सम्पूजितो धूपपुष्पाद्यैः स सदा रविः। भोगिनामुपकाराय किं पुनश्चोपवासिनाम् ॥

Ravi, remembered and fully worshipped with incense, flowers, and the like, is always beneficial to those who enjoy. How much more to those who fast.

BP 1.103.20

उपावृत्तस्तु पापेभ्यो यस्तु वासोगुणैः सह। उपवासः स विज्ञेयः सर्वभोगविवर्जितः ॥

Fasting is understood as turning away from sins, keeping good conduct, and abstaining from all enjoyments.

BP 1.103.21

एकरात्रं द्विरात्रं वा त्रिरात्रमथ वा हरे। उपवासी रविं यस्तु भक्त्या ध्यायति मानवः ॥

A person who fasts for one night, two nights, or three nights, O Hari, and meditates on Ravi with devotion,

BP 1.103.22

तन्नामयाजी तत्कर्मरतस्तद्गतमानसः। निष्कामः पूजयित्वा तं परं ब्रह्माधिगच्छति ॥

worshipping his name, intent on his acts, with mind fixed on him, having worshipped him without desire, reaches the supreme reality.

BP 1.103.23

यश्च काममभिध्याय भास्करार्पितमानसः। उपोषति तमाप्नोति प्रसन्ने तु वृषध्वजे ॥

Whoever fasts while contemplating a desire, with the mind offered to Bhāskara, obtains it when the Bull Bannered Lord is pleased.

BP 1.103.24

श्रीकृष्ण उवाच। ब्राह्मणैः क्षत्रियैर्वैश्यैः शूद्रैः स्त्रीभिस्तथा विभो। संसारगर्तपङ्कस्थैः सुगतिः प्राप्यते कथम् ॥

Kṛṣṇa said. How Brahmins, Kṣatriyas, Vaiśyas, Śūdras, and women sunk in the mire of worldly existence attain a good destination, O Lord.

BP 1.103.25

ब्रह्मोवाच। अथाराध्य जगन्नाथं भास्करं तिमिरापहम्। निर्व्यलीकेन चित्तेन प्रयास्यति च सद्गतिम् ॥

Brahmā said. Having worshipped Bhāskara, the Lord of the world and dispeller of darkness, with a guileless mind, one goes to a good destination.

BP 1.103.26

विषयाग्राहवैषम्यं न चित्तं भास्करार्पणम्। स कथं पाप कर्ता वै नरो यास्यति सद्गतिम् ॥

When the mind is not disturbed by grasping at sense objects but is offered to Bhāskara, how can a person who commits sin fail to reach a good destination.

BP 1.103.27

यदि संसारदुःखार्तः सुगतिं गन्तुमिच्छसि। तदाराधय सर्वेशं ग्रहेशं लोकपूजितम् ॥

If you are afflicted by the suffering of worldly existence and wish to go to a good destination, then worship the Lord of all, the Lord of planets, honored by the world.

BP 1.103.28

पुष्पैः सुगन्धैर्हद्यैश्च धूपैः सागुरुचन्दनैः। वासोविभूषणैर्भक्ष्यैरुपवासपरायणः ॥

One intent on fasting should worship with fragrant and pleasing flowers, incense with agarwood and sandalwood, garments, ornaments, and foods.

BP 1.103.29

यदि संसारनिर्वेदादभिवाच्छसि सद्गतिम्। तदाराधय कालेशं यच्चेष्टं तव चेतसा ॥

If, from weariness with worldly existence, you desire a good destination, then worship the Lord of Time, whatever your mind desires.

BP 1.103.30

पुष्पाणि यदि तेन स्युः शस्तं पादपपल्लवैः। दूर्वांकुरैरपि कृष्ण तदभावेऽर्चयेद्रविम् ॥

If flowers are available, use them. If not, the leaves of trees are praised. If those are absent, O Kṛṣṇa, one should worship Ravi even with shoots of Bermuda grass.

BP 1.103.31

पुष्पपत्राम्बुभिर्धूपैर्यथाविभवमात्सनः। पूजितस्तुष्टिमतुलां भक्त्या यात्येकचेतसाम् ॥

With flowers, leaves, water, and incense according to ability, he becomes incomparably pleased when worshipped with devotion and a single mind.

BP 1.103.32

यः सदायतने भानोः कुर्यात्सम्मार्जनं नरः। स पांसुदेहसंयोगात्सर्वपापैः प्रमुच्यते ॥

Whoever always sweeps the temple of Bhānu is freed from every sin through contact with the temple dust.

BP 1.103.33

यावत्यः पांसुकणिका मार्ज्यन्ते भास्करालये। दिनानि दिवि दिव्यानि तावन्ति मोदते नरः ॥

For as many grains of dust are swept from the temple of Bhāskara, for that many divine days the person rejoices in heaven.

BP 1.103.34

सबाह्याभ्यन्तरं वेश्म मार्जते भास्करस्य यः। स बाह्याभ्यन्तरस्तस्य कायो निष्कल्मषो भवेत् ॥

Whoever sweeps the house of Bhāskara both outside and inside, his body becomes free of defilement both outside and inside.

BP 1.103.35

यश्चानुलेपनं कुर्याद्भानोरायतने नरः। स हेलिलोकमासाद्य मोदते गोगते हरौ ॥

A person who applies unguent in the shrine of Bhānu reaches the world of the Sun and rejoices in the world of cows with Hari.

BP 1.103.36

मृदा वा मृद्विकारैर्वा वर्णकैर्गोमयेन वा। अनुलेपनकृद्भक्त्या नरो गोपतिमाप्नुयात् ॥

Whether with soil, products of soil, pigments, or cow dung, a person who makes an anointing with devotion attains the Lord of cows.

BP 1.103.37

उदकाभ्युक्षणं भानोर्यः करोति तथाक्षये। स गच्छति नरः कृष्ण यत्रास्ते गोपतिः सदा ॥

Whoever sprinkles water for Bhānu also on the imperishable day goes, O Kṛṣṇa, to where the Lord of cows always dwells.

BP 1.103.38

पुष्पप्रकरमत्यर्थं सुगन्धं भास्करालये। अनुलिप्ते नरो दद्यात्पूषोत्तरगृहं व्रजेत् ॥

A person who gives an exceedingly fragrant heap of flowers in the temple of Bhāskara after anointing it goes to the northern abode of Pūṣan.

BP 1.103.39

विमानवरमभ्येति सर्वरत्नमयं दिवि। सम्प्राप्नोति नरो दत्त्वा दीपकं भास्करालये ॥

A person who gives a lamp in the temple of Bhāskara obtains in heaven an excellent vehicle made entirely of jewels.

BP 1.103.40

यस्तु सम्वत्सरं पूर्णं तिलपात्रप्रदो नरः। ध्वजं च भास्करे दद्यात्सममत्र फलं लभेत् ॥

A person who gives a vessel of sesame for a full year and also gives a banner to Bhāskara obtains an equal reward here.

BP 1.103.41

विधुनोत्यतिवातेन दातुरज्ञानतः कृतम्। पापं दातुर्गृहे भानुर्दिवारात्रौ न संशयः ॥

Bhānu shakes away by a mighty wind the sin committed unknowingly in the house of the giver, by day and by night, without doubt.

BP 1.103.42

गीतवाद्यादिर्भिर्देवं य उपास्ते विभावसुम्। गन्धर्वनृत्यैर्वाद्यैश्च विमानस्थो निषेव्यते ॥

Whoever worships the deity Vibhāvasu with songs, instruments, and the like is attended by celestial dances and music while seated in an aerial vehicle.

BP 1.103.43

जातिस्मरत्वं वृद्धिं च ततस्तु परमां गतिम्। प्राप्नोति हेलेरायतने पुण्याख्यानकथाकरः ॥

The teller of sacred stories in the shrine of the Sun obtains remembrance of former births, prosperity, and then the highest destination.

BP 1.103.44

तस्मात्कुर्यात्प्रयत्नेन पूजयेद्वापि वाचकम्। नान्यत्प्रीतिकरं भानोः पुण्याख्यानादृते क्वचित् ॥

Therefore, one should make an effort and also honor the reciter, for nothing anywhere pleases Bhānu more than a sacred account.

BP 1.103.45

एकोऽपि हेलेः सुकृतः प्रणामो दशाश्वमेधावभृथेन तुल्यः। दशाश्वमेधी पुनरेति जन्म हेलिप्रणामी न पुनर्भवाय ॥

Even one proper bow to the Sun equals the concluding bath of ten horse sacrifices. The performer of ten horse sacrifices is born again, but one who bows to the Sun does not return for rebirth.

BP 1.103.46

एवं देवेश्वरो भक्त्या येन भानुरुपासितः। स प्राप्नोति गतिं श्लाघ्यां यामिच्छति च चेतसा ॥

Thus, whoever worships Bhānu, Lord of the gods, with devotion obtains the praiseworthy destination he desires in his heart.

BP 1.103.47

तमाराध्य मया प्राप्तं ब्रह्मत्वं लोकपूजितम्। सौरेर्यथेप्सितं प्राप्तं त्वया तस्मात्पुरानघ ॥

By worshipping him, I attained the state of Brahmā, honored by the worlds. You too obtained from the Sun what you desired long ago, O sinless one.

BP 1.103.48

ब्रह्महत्याभिभूतस्तु गोश्रुताभरणो हरः। तमाराध्य रविं भक्त्या मुक्तोऽसौ ब्रह्महत्यया ॥

Hara, afflicted by the sin of killing a Brahmin and wearing the cow ear ornament, was freed from that sin by worshipping Ravi with devotion.

BP 1.103.49

देवत्वं मनुजैः कैश्चिद्गन्धर्वत्वं तथा परैः। विद्याधरत्वमपरैरेवाप्तं हि दिवाकरात् ॥

Some people attained godhood from Divākara, others the state of celestial musicians, and others the state of knowledge bearers.

BP 1.103.50

लेखः क्रतुशतेनेशमाराध्यैनं दिवाकरम्। इन्द्रत्वमगमत्तस्मान्नान्यः पूज्यो दिवाकरात् ॥

Lekha, having worshipped this lord Divākara with a hundred sacrifices, attained the state of Indra. Therefore, none is more worthy of worship than Divākara.

BP 1.103.51

देवेभ्योऽप्यतिपूज्यस्तु स्वगुरुर्ब्रह्मचारिणा। तस्मात्स यज्ञपुरुषो विवस्वान्पूज्य एव हि ॥

For a celibate, his own teacher is more worthy of worship than even the gods. Therefore, Vivasvān, the Sacrifice Person, is indeed worthy of worship.

BP 1.103.52

स्त्रियाश्च भर्तारमृते पूज्योऽत्यन्तं विभावसुः। भर्तुर्गृहस्थस्य सतः पूज्यो गोपतिरंशुमान् ॥

For a woman, apart from her husband, Vibhāvasu is supremely worthy of worship. For a married householder, the Lord of cows, Aṃśumān, is worthy of worship.

BP 1.103.53

वैश्यानामपि चाराध्यस्तपोभिस्तमनाशनः। ध्येयः परिव्राजकानां सदा देवो विभावसुः ॥

For Vaiśyas too, he is to be worshipped through austerities and fasting. For wandering ascetics, the god Vibhāvasu is always to be meditated upon.

BP 1.103.54

एवं सर्वाश्रमाणां हि चित्रभानुः परायणम्। सर्वेषां चैव वर्णानां तमाराध्याप्नुयाद्गतिम् ॥

Thus Citrabhānu is the refuge of all stages of life. By worshipping him, all varnas attain their destination.
