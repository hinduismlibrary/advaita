Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 168 — The Vow for a Desired Husband

BP 1.168.1

सुमंतुरुवाच ।। एकभक्तेन या नारी कार्त्तिकं क्षपयेन्नृप ।। क्षमाहिंसादिनियमैः संयता ब्रह्मचारिणी ।। १ ।।

Sumantu said: O king, a woman who passes the month of Kārttika taking one meal a day, restrained by observances beginning with patience and non-violence, should be celibate.

BP 1.168.2

गुडाज्यमिश्रं शाल्यन्नं भास्कराय निवेदयेत् ।। पक्षयोरुभयोस्तात श्रद्धया परयान्विता ।। २ ।।

With great faith, O dear one, she should offer rice mixed with jaggery and ghee to the Sun in both fortnights.

BP 1.168.3

पुष्पाणां करवीराणां गुग्गुलं साज्यमादिशेत् ।। सप्तम्यां तात षष्ठ्यां वै उपवासरतिर्भवेत् ।। ३ ।।

She should offer oleander flowers and guggul resin with ghee; on the seventh and sixth lunar days, O dear one, she should delight in fasting.

BP 1.168.4

इन्द्रनीलप्रतीकाशैर्विमानैः सार्वकामिकैः ।। वर्षायुतशतं साग्रं सूरलोके महीयते ।। ४ ।।

With vehicles resembling sapphires and granting every desire, she is honored in the Sun’s world for a full million years.

BP 1.168.5

तथा च सर्वलोकेषु भोगमासाद्य यत्नतः ।। क्रमादागत्य लोकेऽस्मिन्यथेष्टं विन्दते पतिम् ।। ५ ।।

Likewise, having carefully obtained enjoyments in all worlds, she gradually returns to this world and obtains a husband as she desires.

BP 1.168.6

इत्येवं सर्वयज्ञेषु विधिस्तुल्यः प्रकीर्तितः ।। एकभक्तोपवासस्य फलं च सदृशं भवेत् ।। ६ ।।

Thus this observance is declared equal to all sacrifices; the fruit of taking one meal and fasting is likewise equal.

BP 1.168.7

क्षमा सत्यं दया दानं शौचमिन्द्रियनिग्रहः ।। सूर्यपूजाग्निहवनं सन्तोषः स्तेयवर्जनम् ।। ७ ।।

Patience, truth, compassion, giving, purity, restraint of the senses, worship of the Sun, fire-offering, contentment, and abstaining from theft.

BP 1.168.8

सर्वव्रतेष्वयं धर्मः सामान्यो दशधा स्मृतः ।। निःशेषमहं वक्ष्यामि मासान्मासव्रतं प्रति ।। ८ ।।

This dharma is remembered as common to all vows, tenfold; I shall tell it completely, the monthly vow for each month.

BP 1.168.9

मार्गशीर्षे शुभे मासि व्योमपृष्ठे विनिर्मितम् ।। गन्धमाल्यैरलंकृत्य शुभाननमनौपमम् ।। ९ ।।

In the auspicious month of Mārgaśīrṣa, one should make the incomparable, beautiful-faced form upon the sky’s surface, adorning it with fragrances and garlands.

BP 1.168.10

ताम्रपात्रादिकैश्चैवाप्यप्सरोगणसेवितैः ।। सुमेरौ दशसाहस्रे सूर्यलोके महीयते ।। १० ।।

With copper vessels and the like, served by hosts of celestial nymphs, she is honored on Mount Meru in the Sun’s world for ten thousand years.

BP 1.168.11

सर्वदेवकदम्बेषु संप्राप्य विमलां श्रियम् ।। क्रमादागत्य लोकेऽस्मिन्राजानं पतिमाप्नुयात् ।। पुष्पैरमुमलंकृत्य भानवे विनिवेदयेत् ।। ११ ।।

Having gained pure prosperity among all groups of gods, she gradually returns to this world and obtains a king as husband; one should adorn this with flowers and offer it to the Sun.

BP 1.168.12

गन्धमाल्यैरलंकृत्य शुभाननमनौपमम् ।। ताम्रपात्रादिकांस्यं वा कृत्वा तत्र निवेदयेत् ।। १२ ।।

Having adorned the incomparable, beautiful-faced form with fragrances and garlands, one should make a copper or bronze vessel and the like and offer it there.

BP 1.168.13

महापुष्पकयानेन दिव्यगन्धप्रवाहिना ।। सुमेरौ दशसाहस्रं सूर्यलोके महीयते ।। १३ ।।

With a great celestial chariot streaming divine fragrance, she is honored on Mount Meru in the Sun’s world for ten thousand years.

BP 1.168.14

भुक्त्वा तु विपुलान्भोगान्सर्वलोकेषु भारत ।। संप्राप्यैतं क्रमाल्लोकं यथेष्टं विन्दते पतिम् ।। १४ ।।

Having enjoyed abundant pleasures in all worlds, O Bharata, gradually returning to this world, she obtains a husband as she desires.

BP 1.168.15

माघे रथमश्वयुजं दीपमाल्यविभूषितम् ।। पिष्टसानुसमायुक्तं कृत्वायतनमानयेत् ।। १५ ।।

In Māgha, one should make a horse-yoked chariot adorned with rows of lamps and garlands, join it with a mound of flour, and bring it to the shrine.

BP 1.168.16

महारथोपमैर्यानैः श्वेताश्ववरसंयुतैः ।। वर्षायुतशतं साग्रं सूर्यलोके महीयते ।। १६ ।।

With vehicles resembling great chariots and joined with the finest white horses, she is honored in the Sun’s world for a full million years.

BP 1.168.17

सर्वामराणां लोकेषु प्राप्य भोगान्यथेप्सितान् ।। क्रमादागत्य लोकेऽस्मिन्यथेष्टं पतिमाप्नुयात् ।। १७ ।।

Having obtained desired pleasures in all the worlds of the immortals, she gradually returns to this world and obtains a husband as she desires.

BP 1.168.18

प्रतिमां फाल्गुने मासि कृत्वा पिष्टमयीं रवेः ।। गन्धमाल्यैरलंकृत्य स्थापयेद्भास्करालये ।। १८ ।।

In the month of Phālguna, one should make a flour image of the Sun, adorn it with fragrances and garlands, and place it in the Sun’s shrine.

BP 1.168.19

यानैरप्रतिमैर्दिव्यैर्गीतनादसमाकुलैः ।। सुमेरौ दशसाहस्रं सूर्यलोके महीयते ।। १९ ।।

With unequalled divine vehicles filled with the sound of songs, she is honored on Mount Meru in the Sun’s world for ten thousand years.

BP 1.168.20

सर्वाभिमतलोकेऽस्मिन्प्राप्य भोगान्यथेप्सितान् ।। पुनरेत्य इमं लोकं यथेष्टं विन्दते पतिम् ।। २० ।।

Having obtained desired pleasures in this world of all desires, returning again to this world, she obtains a husband as she desires.

BP 1.168.21

कृत्वारुणं तथा चैत्रे गन्धमाल्योपशोभितम् ।। स्थाप्य पात्रे यथोक्ते तु भास्कराय निवेदयेत् ।। २१ ।।

Likewise, in Caitra, having made Aruṇa adorned with fragrances and garlands, and placed him in the prescribed vessel, one should offer him to the Sun.

BP 1.168.22

शरदिन्दुप्रतीकाशैर्विमानैः सार्वकामिकैः ।। वर्षायुतशतं साग्रं सूर्यलोके महीयते ।। २२ ।।

With vehicles resembling the autumn moon and granting every desire, she is honored in the Sun’s world for a full million years.

BP 1.168.23

कर्मक्षयादिहागत्य पुत्रपौत्रसमन्वितम् ।। अभीष्टं पतिमासाद्य लभेद्भोगान्सुदुर्लभान् ।। २३ ।।

When her karma is exhausted and she comes here, joined with sons and grandsons and having obtained the desired husband, she gains pleasures difficult to obtain.

BP 1.168.24

तण्डुलाढकपिष्टेन कृत्वा वै मेरुपर्वतम् ।। निक्षुभार्कसमायुक्तं सर्वधातुविभूषितम् ।। २४ ।।

Having made Mount Meru from flour of a measure of rice, joined with Nikṣubhā and the Sun, and adorned with every kind of metal,

BP 1.168.25

नानालङ्कारसम्पन्नं नानामाल्यविभूषितम् ।। सर्वरत्नसमायुक्तं स्थापयेद्भास्करालये ।। २५ ।।

furnished with various ornaments, adorned with various garlands, and joined with every jewel, one should place it in the Sun’s shrine.

BP 1.168.26

महद्व्योमव्रतं ह्येतद्वैशाखे यः समाचरेत् ।। नानाविधैश्च यानैस्तु सूर्यलोके महीयते ।। २६ ।।

Whoever performs this great sky-vow in Vaiśākha is honored in the Sun’s world with vehicles of many kinds.

BP 1.168.27

सौरादिसर्वलोकेषु भुक्त्वा भोगानशेषतः ।। क्रमादागत्य लोकेऽस्मिन्राजानं पतिमाप्नुयात् ।। २७ ।।

Having enjoyed all pleasures completely in the worlds beginning with the solar world, she gradually returns to this world and obtains a king as husband.

BP 1.168.28

द्वितीयं च तथा पद्ममाषाढे पिष्टमुत्तमम् ।। सर्वबीजरसैः पूर्णं कृत्वा तु शुभ्रलक्षणम् ।। नानाकेशरगन्धाढ्यं सर्वरत्नविभूषितम् ।। २८ ।।

Likewise, in Āṣāḍha, one should make a second, excellent lotus of flour, filled with the essences of all seeds, auspiciously marked and white, rich in many filaments and fragrances, and adorned with every jewel.

BP 1.168.29

एतैर्वा हैमभिर्यानैः सर्वभोगान्वितैर्नृप ।। वर्षकोटिशतं साग्रं सूर्यलोके महीयते ।। २९ ।।

With these golden vehicles endowed with all pleasures, O king, she is honored in the Sun’s world for a full billion years.

BP 1.168.30

भुक्त्वा तु विपुलान्भोगान्सर्वलोकेष्वनुक्रमात् ।। प्राप्ता तु सर्वभोगाढ्यं तरुणं विन्दते पतिम् ।। ३० ।।

Having enjoyed abundant pleasures successively in all worlds, she arrives and obtains a young husband rich in every enjoyment.

BP 1.168.31

सर्वधातुसमाकीर्णं विचित्रध्वजशोभितम् ।। निवेदयेत्सूर्याय श्रावणे तिलपर्वतम् ।। ३१ ।।

In Śrāvaṇa, one should offer to the Sun a mountain of sesame, heaped with all metals and adorned with variegated banners.

BP 1.168.32

स्वच्छन्दगामिभिर्यानैर्नानावर्णविभूषितैः ।। वर्षकोटिशतं साग्रं सूर्यलोके महीयते ।। ३२ ।।

With freely moving vehicles adorned in many colors, she is honored in the Sun’s world for a full billion years.

BP 1.168.33

संप्राप्य विविधान्भोगान्बह्वाश्चर्यसमन्वितान् ।। क्रमाल्लोकमिमं प्राप्य राजानं विन्दते पतिम् ।। ३३ ।।

Having obtained various pleasures accompanied by many wonders, gradually reaching this world, she obtains a king as husband.

BP 1.168.34

कृत्वा भाद्रपदे मासि व्योम शालिमयं नृप ।। वितानध्वजच्छत्राढ्यं नानामालाविभूषितम् ।। ३४ ।।

In Bhādrapada, O king, one should make a sky-shaped rice offering, rich in canopies, banners, and parasols, adorned with many garlands.

BP 1.168.35

तरुणार्ककरप्रख्यैर्महायानैः सुशोभनैः ।। वर्षकोटिसहस्राणि सूर्यलोके महीयते ।। ३५ ।।

With beautiful great vehicles resembling the rays of the young Sun, she is honored in the Sun’s world for ten billion years.

BP 1.168.36

संप्राप्य विविधान्भोगान्त्सर्वान्निमिषसंभवान् ।। क्रमादागत्य लोकेऽस्मिन्राजानं विन्दते पतिम् ।। ३६ ।।

Having obtained all kinds of pleasures arising in the blink of an eye, she gradually returns to this world and obtains a king as husband.

BP 1.168.37

कृत्वा चाश्वयुजे मासि विपुलं धान्यपर्वतम् ।। सुवर्णवस्त्रगन्धाढ्यं भास्कराय निवेदयेत् ।। ३७ ।।

In the month of Āśvayuja, one should make a vast mountain of grain, rich in gold, garments, and fragrances, and offer it to the Sun.

BP 1.168.38

सावित्रैश्च महायानैर्वरभोगसमन्वितैः ।। वर्षकोटिसहस्राणि सूर्यलोके महीयते ।। ३८ ।।

With great vehicles of Savitṛ joined with excellent pleasures, she is honored in the Sun’s world for ten billion years.

BP 1.168.39

सूर्यलोकादिलोकेषु भुक्त्वा भोगान्यथेप्सितान् ।। अस्मिँल्लोके च संप्राप्ता राजानं विन्दते पतिम् ।। चन्द्राग्निभास्कराणां तु कान्तितेजःप्रभान्वितम् ।। ३९ ।।

Having enjoyed desired pleasures in the worlds beginning with the Sun’s world, when she arrives in this world she obtains a king as husband, endowed with the beauty, splendor, and radiance of the Moon, fire, and the Sun.

BP 1.168.40

यं यं कामं समुद्दिश्य नरनारीनपुंसकाः ।। पूजयन्ति रविं भक्त्या तत्सर्वं प्राप्नुवन्ति हि ।। ४० ।।

Whatever desire men, women, and eunuchs direct themselves toward, worshipping the Sun with devotion, they indeed obtain all of it.
