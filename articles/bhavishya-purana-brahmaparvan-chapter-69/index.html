Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 69 — Dream Omens on the Seventh Lunar Day

BP 1.69.1

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
सप्तम्यामुषितो विप्रः स्वप्नदर्शनमुच्यते ।
स्वप्ने दृष्टे च सप्तम्यां पुरुषो नियतव्रतः ॥ ॥

Brahmā said. The dream-visions of a Brahmin who has observed the seventh lunar day are described. A man of fixed vows sees a dream on the seventh lunar day.

brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said; saptamyām uṣitaḥ — having fasted on Saptamī; vipraḥ — a Brāhmaṇa; svapna-darśanam — dream-vision; ucyate — is described; svapne dṛṣṭe — when a dream is seen; ca — and; saptamyām — on Saptamī; puruṣaḥ — a person; niyata-vrataḥ — disciplined in the vow

BP 1.69.2

समाप्य विधिवत्सर्वं जपहोमादिकं क्रमात् ।
पूजयित्वा दिनेशं तु यथाविभवमात्मनः ॥ ॥

He should complete all rites in proper order, including recitation, fire offerings, and the rest, and worship the Sun according to his means.

samāpya — having completed; vidhivat — properly; sarvam — everything; japa-homa-ādikam — recitation, fire-offering, and the rest; kramāt — in sequence; pūjayitvā — having worshiped; dineśam — Dineśa; yathā-vibhavam — according to means; ātmanaḥ — of himself

BP 1.69.3

ततः शयीत शयने देवदेवं विचिन्तयन् ।
सम्प्रसुप्तो यदा पश्येदुदयन्तं दिवाकरम् ॥ ॥

Then he should lie on his bed, contemplating the god of gods. When fully asleep, he sees the rising Sun.

tataḥ — then; śayīta — he should lie down; śayane — on the bed; deva-devam — God of gods; vicintayan — contemplating; samprasuptaḥ — fully asleep; yadā — when; paśyet — he sees; udayantam — rising; divākaram — the Sun

BP 1.69.4

शक्रध्वजं तथा चन्द्रं तस्य सर्वाः समृद्धयः ।
दृश्यं जनं तथा शक्तिं स्रग्विगोवेणुनिस्वनाः ॥ ॥

Seeing the banner of Indra and the Moon brings him all prosperity. The sight of people, power, garlands, cows, and flute sounds is also auspicious.

śakra-dhvajam — Indra’s banner; tathā — likewise; candram — the Moon; tasya — for him; sarvāḥ samṛddhayaḥ — all prosperities; dṛśyam janam — people seen; tathā — likewise; śaktim — power; srak — garland; vigaḥ — cow; veṇu-nisvanāḥ — sounds of flutes

BP 1.69.5

श्वेताब्जचामरादर्शकनकासिसुतोद्भवम् ।
रुधिरस्य स्रुतिं सेकं पानं चैश्वर्यकारकम् ॥ ॥

A white lotus, a fly-whisk, a mirror, gold, a sword, the birth of a son, and the flow, sprinkling, and drinking of blood are causes of prosperity.

śveta-abja — white lotus; cāmara — fly-whisk; ādarśa — mirror; kanaka — gold; asi — sword; suta-udbhavam — birth of a son; rudhirasya srutim — flow of blood; sekam — sprinkling; pānam — drinking; aiśvarya-kārakam — causing prosperity

BP 1.69.6

श्वेतायाः पञ्चपूताया दर्शनं वृद्धिकारकम् ।
प्रजापतेर्घृताक्तस्य दर्शनं पुत्रदं स्मृतम् ॥ ॥

The sight of a white one purified five times brings increase. The sight of Prajāpati smeared with ghee is said to grant a son.

śvetāyāḥ — of the white one; pañca-pūtāyāḥ — purified fivefold; darśanam — seeing; vṛddhi-kārakam — causing increase; prajāpateḥ — of Prajāpati; ghṛta-aktasya — anointed with ghee; darśanam — seeing; putra-dam — granting a son; smṛtam — remembered

BP 1.69.7

शस्तवृक्षाभिरोहश्च क्षिप्रमैश्वर्यकारकः ।
दोहने महिषीसिंहीगोधेनूनां स्वके मुखे ॥ ॥

Climbing auspicious trees quickly brings prosperity. A person milks buffaloes, lionesses, cows, and milch cows into the mouth.

śasta-vṛkṣa-abhirohaḥ — climbing auspicious trees; ca — and; kṣipram — quickly; aiśvarya-kārakaḥ — causing prosperity; dohanam — milking; mahiṣī — buffalo; siṃhī — lioness; go-dhenūnām — cows and cattle; svake mukhe — with one’s own mouth

BP 1.69.8

धनुषां च शराणां च नाभौ च द्रुतनिर्गतिः ।
अभिहन्यात्स्वयं खादेत्सिंहान्गा भुजगांस्तथा ॥ ॥

Bows and arrows swiftly emerge from the navel. One strikes and eats lions, cows, and serpents.

dhanuṣām — of bows; ca — and; śarāṇām — of arrows; ca — and; nābhau — at the navel; druta-nirgatiḥ — swift emergence; abhihanyāt — should strike; svayam — oneself; khādet — should eat; siṃhān — lions; gāḥ — cattle; bhujagān — serpents; tathā — likewise

BP 1.69.9

स्वाङ्गशीर्षं हुतवहे तस्य श्रीरग्रतः स्थिता ।
राजते हैमने पात्रे यो भुङ्क्ते पायसं द्विजः ॥ ॥

Seeing the head and limbs of the body in the fire, fortune stands before him. The Brahmin who eats rice pudding in a golden vessel shines.

sva-aṅga-śīrṣam — one’s own head; hutavahe — in the fire; tasya — for him; śrīḥ — fortune; agrataḥ sthitā — standing before; rājate — shines; haimane pātre — in a golden vessel; yaḥ — who; bhuṅkte — eats; pāyasam — rice-milk pudding; dvijaḥ — Brāhmaṇa

BP 1.69.10

पद्मपत्रे यथा विप्रस्तस्य जन्तोर्बलं भवेत् ।
द्यूते वादेऽथ वा युद्धे विजयॊ हि सुखावहः ॥ ॥

The strength of that person becomes like that of a lotus leaf, O Brahmin. Victory in gambling, debate, or battle brings happiness.

padma-patre — on a lotus leaf; yathā — as; vipra — O Brāhmaṇa; tasya jantoḥ — of that person; balam — strength; bhavet — becomes; dyūte — in gambling; vāde — in debate; atha vā — or in; yuddhe — battle; vijayaḥ — victory; sukhāvahaḥ — bringing happiness

BP 1.69.11

अग्नेस्तु ग्रसनं विप्र आग्नेयं वृद्धिकारकम् ।
गात्रस्य ज्वलनं विप्र शिरोवेधश्च भूतये ॥ ॥

O Brahmin, swallowing fire is an omen of growth. The burning of the body and piercing of the head, O Brahmin, bring prosperity.

agneḥ — of fire; tu — indeed; grasanam — swallowing; vipra — O Brāhmaṇa; āgneyam — igneous; vṛddhi-kārakam — causing increase; gātrasya — of the body; jvalanam — burning; śiraḥ-vedhaḥ — piercing of the head; ca — and; bhūtaye — for prosperity

BP 1.69.12

माल्याम्बराणां युक्तानां शस्तानां शुक्लपक्षिणाम् ।
सदा लाभं प्रशंसन्ति तथा विष्ठानुलेपनम् ॥ ॥

The constant gain of suitable garlands, garments, auspicious white birds, and anointing with excrement is praised.

mālya-ambarāṇām — of garlands and garments; yuktānām — furnished with; śastānām — auspicious; śukla-pakṣiṇām — white birds; sadā — always; lābham — gain; praśaṃsanti — they praise; tathā — likewise; viṣṭhā-anulepanam — anointing with excrement

BP 1.69.13

स्वाङ्गस्य कर्तने क्षेपे रथयाने प्रजागमः ।
नानाशिरोबाहुता च हस्तानां कुरुते श्रियम् ॥ ॥

The cutting and casting away of the bodily limb, riding in a chariot, the arrival of people, and having many heads and arms bring wealth.

sva-aṅgasya — of one’s own limb; kartane — in cutting; kṣepe — in casting away; ratha-yāne — in chariot travel; prajā-gamaḥ — arrival of people; nānā-śiro-bāhutā — many heads and arms; ca — and; hastānām — of hands; kurute — makes; śriyam — prosperity

BP 1.69.14

अगम्यागमनं चैव शोकमध्ययनं तथा ।
देवद्विजजनाचार्यगुरुवृद्धतपस्विनः ॥ ॥

One may dream of going to forbidden places, of grief and study, and of gods, Brahmins, people, teachers, preceptors, elders, and ascetics.

agamya-āgamanam — going to an inaccessible place; ca eva — and indeed; śokam — sorrow; adhyayanam — study; tathā — likewise; deva — gods; dvija-jana — Brāhmaṇa people; ācārya — teachers; guru — gurus; vṛddha — elders; tapasvin — ascetics

BP 1.69.15

यद्यद्वदन्ति तत्सर्वं सत्यमेव हि निर्दिशेत् ।
प्रशस्तदर्शनं चैव अभिषेको नृपश्रियाः ॥ ॥

Whatever they say should all indeed be declared true. An auspicious sight and an anointing are signs of royal fortune.

yat yat — whatever; vadanti — they say; tat sarvam — all that; satyam eva — truly; hi — indeed; nirdiśet — should declare; praśasta-darśanam — auspicious vision; ca eva — and indeed; abhiṣekaḥ — anointing; nṛpa-śriyāḥ — of royal prosperity

BP 1.69.16

स्याद्राज्यं शिरश्छेदेन बहुधा स्फुटितेन तु ।
रुदितं हर्षसम्प्राप्त्यै राज्यं निगडबन्धने ॥ ॥

Kingship may follow decapitation and being split open in many ways. Weeping is for the attainment of joy, and bondage in fetters is for a kingdom.

syāt — may be; rājyam — kingdom; śiraḥ-chedana — decapitation; bahudhā — in many ways; sphuṭita — split open; tu — indeed; ruditaṃ — weeping; harṣa-samprāptyai — for attainment of joy; rājyam — kingdom; nigaḍa-bandhane — in bondage with fetters

BP 1.69.17

तुरङ्गं वृषभं पद्मं राजानां श्वेतकुञ्जरम् ।
महदैश्वर्यमाप्नोति योऽभीकश्चाधिरोहति ॥ ॥

Whoever fearlessly mounts a horse, a bull, a lotus, or the white elephant of kings obtains great prosperity.

turaṅgam — horse; vṛṣabham — bull; padmam — lotus; rājñām — of kings; śveta-kuñjaram — white elephant; mahat aiśvaryam — great prosperity; āpnoti — obtains; yaḥ — who; abhīkaḥ — fearless; ca adhirohati — mounts

BP 1.69.18

ग्रसमानो ग्रहांस्तारामहीं च परिवर्तयन् ।
उन्मूलयन्पर्वतांश्च राज्यलाभमवाप्नुयात् ॥ ॥

Swallowing planets and stars, turning the earth, and uprooting mountains, one may obtain a kingdom.

grasamānaḥ — swallowing; grahān — planets; tārāḥ — stars; mahīm — earth; ca — and; parivartayan — turning; unmūlayan — uprooting; parvatān — mountains; ca — and; rājya-lābham — gain of kingdom; avāpnuyāt — may obtain

BP 1.69.19

देहान्निष्क्रान्तिरन्त्राणां तैर्वा वृक्षस्य वेष्टनम् ।
पातः समुद्रसरितामैश्वर्याणि सुखानि च ॥ ॥

The entrails leaving the body, or wrapping them around a tree, and the fall of oceans and rivers bring riches and happiness.

dehāt — from the body; niṣkrāntiḥ — departure; antrāṇām — of the entrails; taiḥ — with them; vā — or; vṛkṣasya veṣṭanam — wrapping of a tree; pātaḥ — falling; samudra-saritām — of oceans and rivers; aiśvaryāṇi — prosperities; sukhāni — happiness; ca — and

BP 1.69.20

उदधिं सरितं वापि तीर्त्वा पारं प्रयाति च ।
अद्रिं लङ्घयतेऽपि च भवन्त्यर्थजयायुषः ॥ ॥

Crossing a sea or river and reaching the far shore, or leaping over a mountain, brings wealth, victory, and long life.

udadhiṃ — ocean; saritam — river; vā api — or; tīrtvā — having crossed; pāram — the farther shore; prayāti — goes; ca — and; adrim — mountain; laṅghayate — leaps over; api — also; bhavanti — become; artha-jaya-āyuṣaḥ — wealth, victory, and long life

BP 1.69.21

उज्ज्वला स्त्री विशेदङ्कमाशीर्वादपराः स्त्रियः ।
भवत्यर्थागमः शीघ्रं कृमिभिर्यदि भक्ष्यते ॥ ॥

A radiant woman enters the lap. Women give blessings. Wealth comes quickly if a person is eaten by worms.

ujjvalā strī — radiant woman; viśet — enters; aṅkam — lap; āśīrvāda-parāḥ striyaḥ — women devoted to blessing; bhavati — becomes; artha-āgamaḥ — arrival of wealth; śīghram — quickly; kṛmibhiḥ — by worms; yadi — if; bhakṣyate — is eaten

BP 1.69.22

स्वप्ने स्वप्न इति ज्ञातं दृष्टप्रकथनं तथा ।
मङ्गलानां च सर्वेषां शुभं दर्शनमेव च ॥ ॥

Knowing in a dream that it is a dream, telling what was seen, and seeing all auspicious things are themselves auspicious.

svapne — in a dream; svapnaḥ iti jñātam — known as a dream; dṛṣṭa-prakathanam — telling what was seen; tathā — likewise; maṅgalānām — of auspicious things; ca — and; sarveṣām — all; śubham darśanam — auspicious seeing; eva — indeed

BP 1.69.23

संयोगश्चैव माङ्गल्यैरारोग्यधनकारकः ।
ऐश्वर्यराज्यलाभाय यस्मिन्स्वप्न उदाहृतः ॥ ॥

Connection with auspicious things brings health and wealth. The dream in which this is declared leads to prosperity and kingship.

saṃyogaḥ — union; ca eva — and indeed; māṅgalyaiḥ — with auspicious things; ārogya-dhana-kārakaḥ — causing health and wealth; aiśvarya-rājya-lābhāya — for gain of prosperity and kingdom; yasmin — in which; svapnaḥ — dream; udāhṛtaḥ — is declared

BP 1.69.24

तैर्दृष्टै रोगिणो रोगान्मुच्यन्ते नात्र संशयः ।
न स्वप्नं शोभनं दृष्ट्वा स्वप्यात्प्रातश्च कीर्तयेत् ।
राजभोजकविप्रेभ्यः शुचिभ्यश्च शुचिर्नरः ॥ ॥

When these are seen, the sick are freed from illness, there is no doubt. Having seen a good dream, a pure person should not sleep again, but should recount it at dawn to kings, Sun-priests, Brahmins, and the pure.

taiḥ dṛṣṭaiḥ — by those seen; rogiṇaḥ — the sick; rogāt — from diseases; mucyante — are freed; na atra saṃśayaḥ — there is no doubt; na svapyāt — should not sleep; prātaḥ — at dawn; kīrtayet — should recount; rāja-bhojaka-viprebhyaḥ — to kings, Bhojakas, and Brāhmaṇas; śucibhyaḥ — to the pure; śuciḥ naraḥ — a pure person
