VP 5.11.3

VP 5.11.8

विद्युल्लता-कषाघात-त्रस्तैर् इव घनैर् घनम् ।
नादापूरित-दिक्-चक्रैर् धारा-सारम् अपात्यत

Dense clouds, as if frightened by blows from lightning-whips, poured a mass of torrents, filling the circle of directions with their roar.

Śrīdhara translation

“Lightning,” thus. The lightning-vine itself is the whip; as if frightened by its blow, the clouds whose circle of directions was filled by the roar of thunder poured the essence of streams, excellent rain, densely. Or, it means that the stream was poured as a mass, spreading all around. Just as in the world all the goods in an enclosure and the like are thrown down by punished men, frightened by a whip and shouting, so is the comparison.
