VP 2.10.1

साशीतिमण्डलशतं काष्ठयोर् अन्तरं द्वयोः । आरोहणावरोहाभ्याम् भानोर् अब्देन या गतिः ॥१॥

Sri Paraasharar - Between the two ends - southern and northern, there are 183 mandalaas. He goes up and down. In one year, every day, he moves one mandala at a time; up 183 and down 183.

Śrīdhara translation

The commentary explains the Sun’s monthly movement through the northern and southern courses. Dhruva draws the reins inward during the northward course and releases them outward during the southward course, producing the chariot’s ascent and descent.

VP 2.10.2

स रथोऽधिष्ठितो देवैर् आदित्यैर् ऋषिभिस् तथा । गन्धर्वैर् अप्सरोभिश् च ग्रामणीसर्पराक्षसैः ॥२॥

His car is presided over by divine Ádityas, Rishis, heavenly singers and nymphs, Yakshas, serpents, and Rákshasas (one of each being placed in it in every month).

Śrīdhara translation

The deities are the Ādityas themselves, and the grāmaṇī is a Yakṣa.
