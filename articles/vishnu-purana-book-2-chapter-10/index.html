VP 2.10.1

साशीति-मण्डल-शतं काष्ठयोर् अन्तरं द्वयोः।
आरोहणावरोहाभ्याम् भानोर् अब्देन या गतिः

Sri Paraasharar - Between the two ends - southern and northern, there are 183 mandalaas. He goes up and down. In one year, every day, he moves one mandala at a time; up 183 and down 183.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
साशीति-मण्डल-शतं (sāśīti-maṇḍala-śataṃ) — one hundred and eighty solar circles
काष्ठयोर् (kāṣṭhayor) — of the two limits
अन्तरं (antaraṃ) — interval
द्वयोः (dvayoḥ) — of the two
आरोहणावरोहाभ्याम् (ārohaṇāvarohābhyām) — by ascent and descent
भानोर् (bhānor) — of the Sun
अब्देन (abdena) — in a year
या (yā) — which
गतिः (gatiḥ) — course
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sāśīti-maṇḍala-śataṃ kāṣṭhayor antaraṃ dvayoḥ | ārohaṇāvarohābhyām bhānor abdena yā gatiḥ

atha pratimāsaṃ sūryarathaparibhramaṇādhikārigaṇān vakṣyannuttarāyaṇadakṣiṇāyanayor anulomapratilomagantavyāni krāntivṛttānyāha—sāśīti iti | uttaradakṣiṇakāṣṭhayor madhye aśītyuttaraṃ maṇḍalaśataṃ bhānorgatirgantavyaṃ vartma ārohaṇāvarohābhyām iti yugākṣakaṭibandhau vāyupāśau dhruvo yadākarṣati tadottarāyaṇe pratyahamabhyantaraikaikamaṇḍalapraveśe rathasyārohaṇaṃ bhavati, yadā tu tau pāśau dhruvaḥ prasārayati dakṣiṇāyane teṣveva maṇḍaleṣu pratilomaṃ bahirekaikamaṇḍalaṃ praviśato rathasyāvarohaṇaṃ bhavati | taduktaṃ mātsye— ākṛṣyate yadā tau tu dhruveṇa samadhiṣṭhitau | tadā so'bhyantaraṃ sūryo bhramate maṇḍalāni tu | dhruveṇa mucyamāne tu punā raśmiyugena tu | tathaiva vākṣataḥ sūryo maṇḍalāni ca iti

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अथ प्रतिमासं सूर्यरथपरिभ्रमणाधिकारिगणान् वक्ष्यन्नुत्तरायणदक्षिणायनयोर् अनुलोमप्रतिलोमगन्तव्यानि क्रान्तिवृत्तान्याह—साशीति इति । उत्तरदक्षिणकाष्ठयोर् मध्ये अशीत्युत्तरं मण्डलशतं भानोर्गतिर्गन्तव्यं वर्त्म आरोहणावरोहाभ्याम् इति युगाक्षकटिबन्धौ वायुपाशौ ध्रुवो यदाकर्षति तदोत्तरायणे प्रत्यहमभ्यन्तरैकैकमण्डलप्रवेशे रथस्यारोहणं भवति, यदा तु तौ पाशौ ध्रुवः प्रसारयति दक्षिणायने तेष्वेव मण्डलेषु प्रतिलोमं बहिरेकैकमण्डलं प्रविशतो रथस्यावरोहणं भवति । तदुक्तं मात्स्ये— आकृष्यते यदा तौ तु ध्रुवेण समधिष्ठितौ । तदा सोऽभ्यन्तरं सूर्यो भ्रमते मण्डलानि तु । ध्रुवेण मुच्यमाने तु पुना रश्मियुगेन तु । तथैव वाक्षतः सूर्यो मण्डलानि च इति

Śrīdhara commentary.

The commentary explains the Sun’s monthly movement through the northern and southern courses. Dhruva draws the reins inward during the northward course and releases them outward during the southward course, producing the chariot’s ascent and descent.

VP 2.10.2

स रथोऽधिष्ठितो देवैर् आदित्यैर् ऋषिभिस् तथा।
गन्धर्वैर् अप्सरोभिश् च ग्रामणी-सर्प-राक्षसैः

His car is presided over by divine Ádityas, Rishis, heavenly singers and nymphs, Yakshas, serpents, and Rákshasas (one of each being placed in it in every month).

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

देवैरादित्यैरादित्या एव देवास्तैः, ग्रामणीर्यक्षः

The deities are the Ādityas themselves, and the grāmaṇī is a Yakṣa.

VP 2.10.3

धाता क्रतु-स्थला चैव पुलस्त्यो वासुकिस् तथा।
रथ-भृद् ग्रामणीर् हेतिस् तुम्बुरुश् चैव सप्तमः

This starts with Chaitra maasa, also called Madhu maasa. In this month, Dhaataa is Aadityaa, Ratusthalaa is the Apsaraa, Pulastya is the Rishi, Vaasuki is the Sarpa, Rathabhrut is the Yaksha, Heti is the Raakshasa gana, and Tumburu is the Gandharva.

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

Śrīdhara commentary.

Dhaataa iti | Rathabhrut graamaneeh rathabhrut naama yakshah | Hetih raakshasah | Rathakrut iti cha paathah |

VP 2.10.4

वालिखिल्यास् तथैवैनं परिवार्य समासते

Vaalakhilya Rishis are surrounding, and go along with him.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
वालिखिल्यास् (vālikhilyās) — the Vālakhilyas
तथैवैनं (tathaivainaṃ) — likewise
परिवार्य (parivārya) — indeed, him
समासते (samāsate) — having surrounded, remain
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vālikhilyās tathaivainaṃ parivārya samāsate

vahanti vahanānukūlatayā rathaṃ sannahyantīty arthaḥ, sannahyanti rathaṃ nāgāḥ iti śukokteḥ | abhīṣusaṅgrahaḥ raśmisaṃyojanam | nityasevakān āha—bālakhilyā iti

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

वहन्ति वहनानुकूलतया रथं सन्नह्यन्तीत्यर्थः, सन्नह्यन्ति रथं नागाः इति शुकोक्तेः । अभीषुसङ्ग्रहः रश्मिसंयोजनम् । नित्यसेवकानाह—बालखिल्या इति

Śrīdhara commentary.

“They carry” means that they harness the chariot in a way suited to its movement; Śuka says that the serpents harness the chariot. “Gathering the reins” means joining the reins or rays. He names the constant attendants with “the Bālakhilyas.”

VP 2.10.23

सोऽयं सप्त-गणः सूर्य-मण्डले मुनि-सत्तम।
हिमोष्ण-वारि-वृष्टीनां हेतुः स्वसमयं गतः

O Maitreyar, all these categories in the orb of the Sun, they are the cause of the snow, warmth during summer, rains, at the right time.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
सोऽयं (so’yaṃ) — this
सप्त-गणः (sapta-gaṇaḥ) — the sevenfold group
सूर्य-मण्डले (sūrya-maṇḍale) — in the Sun’s orb
मुनि-सत्तम (muni-sattama) — O best of sages
हिमोष्ण-वारि-वृष्टीनां (himoṣṇa-vāri-vṛṣṭīnāṃ) — of cold, heat, water, and rain
हेतुः (hetuḥ) — cause
स्वसमयं (svasamayaṃ) — at their proper season
गतः (gataḥ) — has come
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

so’yaṃ sapta-gaṇaḥ sūrya-maṇḍale muni-sattama | himoṣṇa-vāri-vṛṣṭīnāṃ hetuḥ svasamayaṃ gataḥ

saptānāṃ sādhāraṇaṃ karm āha—so'yam iti | svayam āgata iti pāṭhe svasvakāle hemantagrīṣmādāvāgataḥ san himādivṛṣṭīnāṃ heturityarthaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

सप्तानां साधारणं कर्म् आह—सोऽयम् इति । स्वयम् आगत इति पाठे स्वस्वकाले हेमन्तग्रीष्मादावागतः सन् हिमादिवृष्टीनां हेतुरित्यर्थः

Śrīdhara commentary.

Vishnu Shakti in the Sun Now, Chapter 11. Here, the various acts of the Sun are going to be told here by Sri Paraasharar.
