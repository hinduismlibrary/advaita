Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 95 — The Greatness of Sun Worship

BP 1.95.1

ब्रह्मोवाच । त्रिभिः प्रदक्षिणां कृत्वा यो नमस्कुरुते रविम् । भूमौ गतेन शिरसा स याति परमां गतिम् ॥ ॥

Brahma spoke. Whoever circumambulates the Sun three times and bows with his head touching the ground goes to the highest state.

BP 1.95.2

सोपानत्को देवगृहमारोहेद्यस्तु मानवः । स याति नरकं घोरं तामिस्रं नाम नामतः ॥ ॥

A person who enters the temple of the deity wearing footwear goes to the terrible hell named Tāmisra.

BP 1.95.3

श्लेष्ममूत्रपुरीषाणि समुत्सृजति यस्तु वै । देवस्यायतने भानोः स गच्छेन्नरकं क्रमात् ॥ ॥

Whoever releases phlegm, urine, and feces in the temple of the Sun goes in due course to hell.

BP 1.95.4

घृतं मधु पयस्तोयं तथेक्षुरसमुत्तमम् । स्नपनार्थं तु देवस्य ये ददतीह मानवाः । सर्वकामानवाप्येह ते यान्ति हेलिमण्डलम् ॥ ॥

Those people who give ghee, honey, milk, water, and excellent sugarcane juice for the bathing of the deity obtain all desires here and go to the solar sphere.

BP 1.95.5

स्नाप्यमानं रविं भक्त्या ये पश्यन्ति वृषध्वज । तेऽश्वमेधफलं प्राप्य लयं यान्ति वृषध्वजे ॥ ॥

Those who devotedly behold the Sun while he is being bathed, O Bull Bannered One, obtain the fruit of the horse sacrifice and merge into the Bull Bannered Lord.

BP 1.95.6

स्नपनं ये च कुर्वंति भानोर्भक्तिसमन्विताः । लभन्ते तत्फलं भीम राजसूयाश्वमेधयोः ॥ ॥

Those who perform the bathing of Bhānu with devotion obtain the fruit of the royal consecration and horse sacrifice, O Bhīma.

BP 1.95.7

यथा न लङ्घयेत्कश्चित्स्नपनं भास्करस्य तु । तथा कार्यं प्रयत्नेन लङ्घितं ह्यसुखावहम् ॥ ॥

One should take care that no one neglects the bathing of Bhāskara, for neglecting it indeed brings unhappiness.

BP 1.95.8

तामिस्रं नरकं याति लङ्घयेच्च स रौरवान् । तस्मात्सर्वप्रयत्नेन कार्यं स्नपनमादितः ॥ ॥

If he neglects it, he goes to Tāmisra hell and to Raurava. Therefore the bathing should be performed from the beginning with every effort.

BP 1.95.9

घृतेन स्नापयेद्देवं कञ्जमाप्नोति मानवः । मधुना प्रियमायाति तोयेनापि घृतौकसम् ॥ ॥

Bathing the deity with ghee, a person attains the Lotus Born. With honey he reaches the beloved one, and with water the one whose abode is ghee.

BP 1.95.10

इक्षुरसेन संस्नाप्य पयसा कञ्जशध्वजम् । एवमेभिः स्नापयेद्वै रविमीहितमाप्नुयात् ॥ ॥

Having bathed the Lotus Bannered One with sugarcane juice and milk, one should bathe the Sun with these substances and attain the desired result.
