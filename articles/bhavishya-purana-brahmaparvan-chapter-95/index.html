Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 95 — The Greatness of Sun Worship

BP 1.95.1

ब्रह्मोवाच । त्रिभिः प्रदक्षिणां कृत्वा यो नमस्कुरुते रविम् । भूमौ गतेन शिरसा स याति परमां गतिम् ॥ ॥

Brahma spoke. Whoever circumambulates the Sun three times and bows with his head touching the ground goes to the highest state.

brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said;
tribhiḥ — three times;
pradakṣiṇām kṛtvā — having circumambulated;
yaḥ — whoever;
namaskurute — bows;
ravim — to Ravi;
bhūmau — on the ground;
gatena śirasā — with the head lowered;
saḥ yāti — he goes;
paramām gatim — to the highest destination.

BP 1.95.2

सोपानत्को देवगृहमारोहेद्यस्तु मानवः । स याति नरकं घोरं तामिस्रं नाम नामतः ॥ ॥

A person who enters the temple of the deity wearing footwear goes to the terrible hell named Tāmisra.

sa-upān-atkaḥ — wearing sandals;
deva-gṛham — the temple of the deity;
ārohet — should climb;
yaḥ tu — whoever;
mānavaḥ — person;
saḥ yāti — he goes;
narakam — to hell;
ghoram — terrible;
tāmisram — Tāmisra;
nāma — by name.

BP 1.95.3

श्लेष्ममूत्रपुरीषाणि समुत्सृजति यस्तु वै । देवस्यायतने भानोः स गच्छेन्नरकं क्रमात् ॥ ॥

Whoever releases phlegm, urine, and feces in the temple of the Sun goes in due course to hell.

śleṣma — phlegm;
mūtra — urine;
purīṣāṇi — feces;
samutsṛjati — releases;
yaḥ tu — whoever;
vai — indeed;
devasya āyatane — in the temple of the deity;
bhānoḥ — of Bhānu;
saḥ gacchet — he goes;
narakam — to hell;
kramāt — in due course.

BP 1.95.4

घृतं मधु पयस्तोयं तथेक्षुरसमुत्तमम् । स्नपनार्थं तु देवस्य ये ददतीह मानवाः । सर्वकामानवाप्येह ते यान्ति हेलिमण्डलम् ॥ ॥

Those people who give ghee, honey, milk, water, and excellent sugarcane juice for the bathing of the deity obtain all desires here and go to the solar sphere.

ghṛtam — ghee;
madhu — honey;
payaḥ — milk;
toyam — water;
tathā — likewise;
ikṣu-rasa-uttamam — excellent sugarcane juice;
snāpana-artham — for bathing;
devasya — of the deity;
ye dadati — those who give;
sarva-kāmān — every desire;
avāpya — obtaining;
helimaṇḍalam — the orb of Heli;
yānti — they go.

BP 1.95.5

स्नाप्यमानं रविं भक्त्या ये पश्यन्ति वृषध्वज । तेऽश्वमेधफलं प्राप्य लयं यान्ति वृषध्वजे ॥ ॥

Those who devotedly behold the Sun while he is being bathed, O Bull Bannered One, obtain the fruit of the horse sacrifice and merge into the Bull Bannered Lord.

snāpyamānam — being bathed;
ravim — Ravi;
bhaktyā — with devotion;
ye paśyanti — those who see;
vṛṣadhvaja — O bull-bannered one;
te — they;
aśvamedha-phalam — fruit of the Aśvamedha;
prāpya — obtaining;
layam yānti — go to absorption;
vṛṣadhvaje — in the bull-bannered one.

BP 1.95.6

स्नपनं ये च कुर्वंति भानोर्भक्तिसमन्विताः । लभन्ते तत्फलं भीम राजसूयाश्वमेधयोः ॥ ॥

Those who perform the bathing of Bhānu with devotion obtain the fruit of the royal consecration and horse sacrifice, O Bhīma.

snapanam — bathing;
ye kurvanti — those who perform;
bhānoḥ — of Bhānu;
bhakti-samanvitāḥ — endowed with devotion;
labhante — obtain;
tat-phalam — that fruit;
bhīma — O mighty one;
rājasūya-aśvamedhayoḥ — of the Rājasūya and Aśvamedha.

BP 1.95.7

यथा न लङ्घयेत्कश्चित्स्नपनं भास्करस्य तु । तथा कार्यं प्रयत्नेन लङ्घितं ह्यसुखावहम् ॥ ॥

One should take care that no one neglects the bathing of Bhāskara, for neglecting it indeed brings unhappiness.

yathā — so that;
na laṅghayet — should not pass over;
kaścit — anyone;
snapanaṃ — bathing;
bhāskarasya — of Bhāskara;
tathā — thus;
kāryam — it should be done;
prayatnena — with effort;
laṅghitam — passed over;
asukhāvaham — bringing unhappiness.

BP 1.95.8

तामिस्रं नरकं याति लङ्घयेच्च स रौरवान् । तस्मात्सर्वप्रयत्नेन कार्यं स्नपनमादितः ॥ ॥

If he neglects it, he goes to Tāmisra hell and to Raurava. Therefore the bathing should be performed from the beginning with every effort.

tāmisram narakam — Tāmisra hell;
yāti — goes;
laṅghayet — should pass over;
ca — and;
saḥ — he;
rauravān — to Raurava [hells];
tasmāt — therefore;
sarva-prayatnena — with every effort;
kāryam — should be performed;
snapanaṃ — bathing;
āditaḥ — from the beginning.

BP 1.95.9

घृतेन स्नापयेद्देवं कञ्जमाप्नोति मानवः । मधुना प्रियमायाति तोयेनापि घृतौकसम् ॥ ॥

Bathing the deity with ghee, a person attains the Lotus Born. With honey he reaches the beloved one, and with water the one whose abode is ghee.

ghṛtena — with ghee;
snāpayet — should bathe;
devam — the deity;
kañjam — the Lotus-born;
āpnoti — attains;
mānavaḥ — person;
madhunā — with honey;
priyam — the beloved;
āyāti — reaches;
to yena api — even with water;
ghṛtaukasam — the ghee-dwelling one.

BP 1.95.10

इक्षुरसेन संस्नाप्य पयसा कञ्जशध्वजम् । एवमेभिः स्नापयेद्वै रविमीहितमाप्नुयात् ॥ ॥

Having bathed the Lotus Bannered One with sugarcane juice and milk, one should bathe the Sun with these substances and attain the desired result.

ikṣu-rasena — with sugarcane juice;
saṃsnāpya — having bathed;
payasā — with milk;
kañja-śadhvajam — the lotus-bannered one;
evam ebhiḥ — thus with these;
snāpayet — should bathe;
vai — indeed;
ravim — Ravi;
īhitam — desired result;
āp­nuyāt — should obtain.
