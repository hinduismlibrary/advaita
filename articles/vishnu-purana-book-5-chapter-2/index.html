VP 5.2.1

यथोक्तं सा जगद्-धात्रा देव-देवेन वै तथा ।
षड्-गर्भ-गर्भ-विन्यासं चक्रे चान्यस्य कर्षणम् ॥१॥

As the God of gods, bearer of the world, had said, she placed the six embryos in the womb and transferred the other [embryo].

Śrīdhara translation

‘As stated’ etc.: the placing of the six embryos in Devakī’s womb in sequence, and the drawing of the other, seventh embryo from Devakī’s womb toward Rohiṇī’s womb—she did this.

VP 5.2.7

देवता ऊचुः—

VP 5.2.19

रूप-कर्म-स्वरूपाणि न परिच्छेद-गोचरे ।
यस्याखिल-प्रमाणानि स विष्णुर् गर्भगस् तव ॥१९॥

His forms, actions, and essential nature are beyond delimitation; all his measures are beyond definition. That Viṣṇu has entered your womb.

Śrīdhara translation

‘Forms’ etc.: ‘Form’ is each particular play of action; ‘essential form’ is the body. ‘Name and action’ is also a reading. All the measures of his forms and so forth, such as childhood and greatness, do not fall within the range of delimitation. In another reading, his names and the like cannot be delimited by any measures. That Viṣṇu has entered your womb.
