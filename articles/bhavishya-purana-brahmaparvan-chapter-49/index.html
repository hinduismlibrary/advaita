Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 49 — The Method of Sun Worship

BP 1.49.1

वासुदेव उवाच
अथार्चनविधिं वक्ष्ये धर्मकेतोरनुत्तमम् ।
सर्वकामप्रदं पुण्यं विघ्नघ्नं दुरितापहम् ॥

Vāsudeva said, I shall now describe the excellent rite of worship of Dharmaketu, meritorious, granting every desire, destroying obstacles and removing evil.

वासुदेव उवाच (vāsudeva uvāca) — Vāsudeva said;
अथार्चनविधिं वक्ष्ये धर्मकेतोरनुत्तमम् (athārcanavidhiṃ vakṣye dharmaketoranuttamam) — I shall now describe the excellent method of worship of Dharmaketu;
सर्वकामप्रदं पुण्यं विघ्नघ्नं दुरितापहम् (sarvakāmapradaṃ puṇyaṃ vighnaghnaṃ duritāpaham) — It bestows all desires, is holy, destroys obstacles and removes evils

BP 1.49.2

सूर्यमन्त्रैः पुरः स्नातो यजेत्तेनैव भास्करम् ।
यतस्ततः प्रवक्ष्यामि स्नानमादौ समासतः ॥

Having bathed before the Sun with the Sun mantras, one should worship Bhāskara with those same mantras. I shall briefly explain the bath first.

सूर्यमन्त्रैः पुरः स्नातो यजेत्तेनैव भास्करम् (sūryamantraiḥ puraḥ snāto yajettenaiva bhāskaram) — One who has bathed in front of him with the mantras of the sun should worship the sun with the same;
यतस्ततः प्रवक्ष्यामि स्नानमादौ समासतः (yatastataḥ pravakṣyāmi snānamādau samāsataḥ) — From there I will tell you in brief at the beginning of the bath

BP 1.49.3

आचान्तस्तमुपालभ्य मुद्रया शुचिशुद्धया ।
कृत्वा नीराजनं पुत्र संशोध्य च जलं ततः ॥

Having sipped water and approached him with a purified gesture, perform the waving of lights, my son, and then purify the water.

आचान्तस्तमुपालभ्य मुद्रया शुचिशुद्धया (ācāntastamupālabhya mudrayā śuciśuddhayā) — Achantha worshiped him with a pure and pure seal;
कृत्वा नीराजनं पुत्र संशोध्य च जलं ततः (kṛtvā nīrājanaṃ putra saṃśodhya ca jalaṃ tataḥ) — My son after making the arrangement of water and then purifying the water

BP 1.49.4

स्नानाद्धृदयपूतेन मन्त्रेण मत्कुलोद्वह ।
उत्थायाचम्य तेनैव वाससी परिधाय च ॥

O upholder of my lineage, bathe with the mantra purified in the heart. Then rise, sip water with that same mantra, and put on two garments.

स्नानाद्धृदयपूतेन मन्त्रेण मत्कुलोद्वह (snānāddhṛdayapūtena mantreṇa matkulodvaha) — O chief of my family by bathing with a mantra purified from the heart;
उत्थायाचम्य तेनैव वाससी परिधाय च (utthāyācamya tenaiva vāsasī paridhāya ca) — He got up and washed himself and put on his clothes

BP 1.49.5

द्विराचम्याथ सम्प्रोक्ष्य तनुं सप्ताक्षरेण च ।
उत्थायाचम्य तेनैवं रवेः कृत्वार्घ्यमेव च ॥

Then sip water twice and sprinkle the body with the seven syllable mantra. Rise, sip water with that same mantra, and offer water to Ravi.

द्विराचम्याथ सम्प्रोक्ष्य तनुं सप्ताक्षरेण च (dvirācamyātha samprokṣya tanuṃ saptākṣareṇa ca) — Then he washed himself twice and sprinkled his body with the seven syllables;
उत्थायाचम्य तेनैवं रवेः कृत्वार्घ्यमेव च (utthāyācamya tenaivaṃ raveḥ kṛtvārghyameva ca) — I got up and washed myself with it and offered arghya to the sun

BP 1.49.6

दत्त्वा तेन जपित्वा तं स्वकं ध्यात्वार्कवद्धृदि ।
गत्वा चायतनं शुभ्रमार्कमार्की तनुं यजेत् ॥

Having made the offering, recite that mantra and meditate on him as the Sun in the heart of the worshipper. Then go to the bright shrine and worship the Sun in his solar form.

दत्त्वा तेन जपित्वा तं स्वकं ध्यात्वार्कवद्धृदि (dattvā tena japitvā taṃ svakaṃ dhyātvārkavaddhṛdi) — Giving it, chanting it, meditating on it in your heart like the sun;
गत्वा चायतनं शुभ्रमार्कमार्की तनुं यजेत् (gatvā cāyatanaṃ śubhramārkamārkī tanuṃ yajet) — Going to the tea temple, one should sacrifice the body of Shubramarkarki

BP 1.49.7

पूरकं कुम्भकं कृत्वा रेचकं च समाहितः ।
कृत्वोङ्कारेण दोषांस्तु हन्यात्कायादिसम्भवान् ॥

Being composed, perform inhalation, retention, and exhalation. With Om, destroy the faults arising from the body and the rest.

पूरकं कुम्भकं कृत्वा रेचकं च समाहितः (pūrakaṃ kumbhakaṃ kṛtvā recakaṃ ca samāhitaḥ) — The supplement is potted and the laxative is concentrated;
कृत्वोङ्कारेण दोषांस्तु हन्यात्कायादिसम्भवान् (kṛtvoṅkāreṇa doṣāṃstu hanyātkāyādisambhavān) — But by doing the oṁkāra he should kill the evils arising from the body and other things

BP 1.49.8

वायव्याग्नेयमाहेन्द्रवारुणीभिर्यथाक्रमम् ।
किल्विषं वारुणाद्भिश्च हन्यात्सिद्ध्यर्थमात्मनः ॥

Using the Air, Fire, Indra, and Varuna rites in order, one should destroy sin with the waters of Varuna for attainment of the worshipper.

वायव्याग्नेयमाहेन्द्रवारुणीभिर्यथाक्रमम् (vāyavyāgneyamāhendravāruṇībhiryathākramam) — Indra and Varuni in order from the wind and the south;
किल्विषं वारुणाद्भिश्च हन्यात्सिद्ध्यर्थमात्मनः (kilviṣaṃ vāruṇādbhiśca hanyātsiddhyarthamātmanaḥ) — He should kill Kilvis and Varuna for the sake of his own perfection

BP 1.49.9

शोषणं दहनं स्तम्भं प्लावनं च यथाक्रमात् ।
वाय्वग्नीन्द्रजनाख्याभिर्धारणाभिः कृते सति ॥

The drying, burning, stilling, and flooding are performed in order by the practices called Air, Fire, Indra, and Water.

शोषणं दहनं स्तम्भं प्लावनं च यथाक्रमात् (śoṣaṇaṃ dahanaṃ stambhaṃ plāvanaṃ ca yathākramāt) — drying, burning, stacking and floating, respectively;
वाय्वग्नीन्द्रजनाख्याभिर्धारणाभिः कृते सति (vāyvagnīndrajanākhyābhirdhāraṇābhiḥ kṛte sati) — When done by the holdings called Vayu, Agni and Indrajana

BP 1.49.10

ध्यात्वा विशुद्धमात्मानं प्रणमेदर्कमास्थितम् ।
देहं तेनैव सञ्चिन्त्य पञ्चभूतमयं परम् ॥

Having meditated on the purified self, one should bow to the Sun established there. One should conceive the body as that supreme form made of the five elements.

ध्यात्वा विशुद्धमात्मानं प्रणमेदर्कमास्थितम् (dhyātvā viśuddhamātmānaṃ praṇamedarkamāsthitam) — One should meditate on the pure Self and bow down to the sun;
देहं तेनैव सञ्चिन्त्य पञ्चभूतमयं परम् (dehaṃ tenaiva sañcintya pañcabhūtamayaṃ param) — Thinking of the body by that very thing, the Supreme Being consisting of the five elements

BP 1.49.11

सूक्ष्मं स्थूलं तथाक्षाणि स्वस्थानेषु प्रकम्य च ।
विन्यस्याङ्गानि खादीनि हृदाद्यानि हृद्यादिषु ॥

Having placed the subtle, the gross, and the senses in their proper places, one should arrange the limbs beginning with space, and those beginning with the heart, in their respective places.

सूक्ष्मं स्थूलं तथाक्षाणि स्वस्थानेषु प्रकम्य च (sūkṣmaṃ sthūlaṃ tathākṣāṇi svasthāneṣu prakamya ca) — The subtle and the gross and the eyes moving in their places;
विन्यस्याङ्गानि खादीनि हृदाद्यानि हृद्यादिषु (vinyasyāṅgāni khādīni hṛdādyāni hṛdyādiṣu) — Placing the limbs in the ditches, the heart and the others in the heart and the others

BP 1.49.12

खस्वाहा हदयं भानोः खमर्काय शिरस्तथा ।
उल्का स्वाहा शिखार्कस्य यै च हुं कवचं परम् ॥
खां फडस्त्रं च संहाराश्चादितः प्रणवः कृतः ॥

Kha Svāhā is the heart of Bhānu, Kham is the head of Arka, Ulkā Svāhā is the crest of Arka, and Yai Huṃ is the supreme armor. Khāṃ Phaṭ is the weapon. The dissolution mantras are preceded by Om.

खस्वाहा हदयं भानोः खमर्काय शिरस्तथा (khasvāhā hadayaṃ bhānoḥ khamarkāya śirastathā) — Khasvaha is the heart of the sun and Khamarka is the head;
उल्का स्वाहा शिखार्कस्य यै च हुं कवचं परम् (ulkā svāhā śikhārkasya yai ca huṃ kavacaṃ param) — Meteor Svaha is the peak of the sun and Yai is the supreme shield;
खां फडस्त्रं च संहाराश्चादितः प्रणवः कृतः (khāṃ phaḍastraṃ ca saṃhārāścāditaḥ praṇavaḥ kṛtaḥ) — The oṁkāra was chanted from Khan, Phadastra and Samharas

BP 1.49.13

स पूर्वे प्रणवस्याथो मन्त्रकर्मप्रसिद्धये ।
एभिर्जलं त्रिधा जप्त्वा स्नानद्रव्याणि तेन च ॥

For the success of the mantra rite, Om is placed before these. Having recited these over the water three times, sprinkle the bathing substances with it.

स पूर्वे प्रणवस्याथो मन्त्रकर्मप्रसिद्धये (sa pūrve praṇavasyātho mantrakarmaprasiddhaye) — He is the former of the oṁkāra for the purpose of making the mantras and rituals famous;
एभिर्जलं त्रिधा जप्त्वा स्नानद्रव्याणि तेन च (ebhirjalaṃ tridhā japtvā snānadravyāṇi tena ca) — With these chant water in three parts and with it the bathing materials

BP 1.49.14

सम्प्रोक्ष्य पूजयेत्सूर्यं गन्धपुष्पादिभिः शुभैः ।
ततो मूर्तिषु सर्वासु रात्रावग्नौ प्रपूजयेत् ॥

After sprinkling, one should worship the Sun with auspicious perfumes, flowers, and the rest. Then one should worship all the images in the fire at night.

सम्प्रोक्ष्य पूजयेत्सूर्यं गन्धपुष्पादिभिः शुभैः (samprokṣya pūjayetsūryaṃ gandhapuṣpādibhiḥ śubhaiḥ) — Sprinkle and worship the sun with auspicious offerings of fragrant flowers and other offerings;
ततो मूर्तिषु सर्वासु रात्रावग्नौ प्रपूजयेत् (tato mūrtiṣu sarvāsu rātrāvagnau prapūjayet) — Then one should worship all the idols in the fire at night

BP 1.49.15

प्राक्पश्चिमोदगभ्यग्रां प्रातः सायं निशासु वै ।
सप्ताक्षरेण तन्मन्त्रं ध्यात्वा च पद्मकर्णिकाम् ॥

Facing east, west, or north in the morning, evening, and at night, one should meditate on the seven syllable mantra and the lotus pericarp.

प्राक्पश्चिमोदगभ्यग्रां प्रातः सायं निशासु वै (prākpaścimodagabhyagrāṃ prātaḥ sāyaṃ niśāsu vai) — From the east to the west to the north in the morning in the evening and at night;
सप्ताक्षरेण तन्मन्त्रं ध्यात्वा च पद्मकर्णिकाम् (saptākṣareṇa tanmantraṃ dhyātvā ca padmakarṇikām) — And meditate on that mantra in seven syllables with lotus ears

BP 1.49.16

आदित्यमण्डलान्तस्थं तत्र देहं प्रकल्पयेत् ।
प्रभामण्डलमध्यस्थं ध्यात्वा देहं यथा पुरा ॥
सर्वलक्षणसम्पूर्णं सहस्रकिरणोज्ज्वलम् ॥

One should imagine the body placed there within the solar disk. Meditate as before on the body placed in the middle of the circle of radiance, complete with all marks and shining with a thousand rays.

आदित्यमण्डलान्तस्थं तत्र देहं प्रकल्पयेत् (ādityamaṇḍalāntasthaṃ tatra dehaṃ prakalpayet) — The body should be conceived there at the end of the solar system;
प्रभामण्डलमध्यस्थं ध्यात्वा देहं यथा पुरा (prabhāmaṇḍalamadhyasthaṃ dhyātvā dehaṃ yathā purā) — Meditating on the body in the midst of the sphere of radiance as before;
सर्वलक्षणसम्पूर्णं सहस्रकिरणोज्ज्वलम् (sarvalakṣaṇasampūrṇaṃ sahasrakiraṇojjvalam) — Full of all characteristics and radiant with a thousand rays

BP 1.49.17

रक्तैर्गन्धैश्च पुष्पैश्च चरुभिर्बलिभिस्तथा ।
रक्तचन्दनमिश्रैर्वा वस्त्रैरावरणैः शुभैः ॥

With red perfumes, flowers, cooked offerings, and gifts, or with garments and auspicious coverings mixed with red sandalwood.

रक्तैर्गन्धैश्च पुष्पैश्च चरुभिर्बलिभिस्तथा (raktairgandhaiśca puṣpaiśca carubhirbalibhistathā) — red fragrant flowers and charms and offerings;
रक्तचन्दनमिश्रैर्वा वस्त्रैरावरणैः शुभैः (raktacandanamiśrairvā vastrairāvaraṇaiḥ śubhaiḥ) — or covered with auspicious clothes mixed with red sandalwood

BP 1.49.18

आवाहनादिकर्माणि रक्षां तु हृदयेन च ।
तच्चित्तश्च सदा कुर्याज्ज्ञात्वा कर्मक्रमं बुधः ॥

A wise person, knowing the order of the rites, should always perform the acts beginning with invocation and protection by the heart mantra, keeping the mind on him.

आवाहनादिकर्माणि रक्षां तु हृदयेन च (āvāhanādikarmāṇi rakṣāṃ tu hṛdayena ca) — Invocations and other rituals and protection with the heart;
तच्चित्तश्च सदा कुर्याज्ज्ञात्वा कर्मक्रमं बुधः (taccittaśca sadā kuryājjñātvā karmakramaṃ budhaḥ) — And that mind should always be fixed by the wise, knowing the order of action

BP 1.49.19

कृत्वा चावाहनं मन्त्रैरेकत्र स्थापनं ततः ।
यावद्यागावसानं तु सान्निध्यं तत्र कल्प्य च ॥

Having invoked him with mantras, one should then establish him in one place and maintain his presence there until the worship is complete.

कृत्वा चावाहनं मन्त्रैरेकत्र स्थापनं ततः (kṛtvā cāvāhanaṃ mantrairekatra sthāpanaṃ tataḥ) — After making the invocation and placing them together with mantras;
यावद्यागावसानं तु सान्निध्यं तत्र कल्प्य च (yāvadyāgāvasānaṃ tu sānnidhyaṃ tatra kalpya ca) — and imagining the presence there until the end of the sacrifice

BP 1.49.20

दत्त्वा पाद्यादिकां पूजां शक्त्या वार्घ्यं निवेद्य च ।
जपित्वा विधिवद्ध्यात्वा ततो देवीं विसर्जयेत् ॥

Having offered worship beginning with water for the feet, one should offer water for welcome according to ability. After reciting as prescribed and meditating, one should then dismiss the Goddess.

दत्त्वा पाद्यादिकां पूजां शक्त्या वार्घ्यं निवेद्य च (dattvā pādyādikāṃ pūjāṃ śaktyā vārghyaṃ nivedya ca) — Having offered water for washing feet and other offerings and offered arghya according to one's ability;
जपित्वा विधिवद्ध्यात्वा ततो देवीं विसर्जयेत् (japitvā vidhivaddhyātvā tato devīṃ visarjayet) — After chanting the mantra in accordance with the ritual, one should then release the goddess

BP 1.49.21

एष कर्म क्रमः प्रोक्तः सर्वेषां यजनक्रमात् ।
प्रवक्ष्यामि जपस्थानं पद्मेशावरणे तथा ॥

This is declared to be the order of the rite for the worship of all. I shall also explain the place for recitation and the enclosure of the Lotus Lord.

एष कर्म क्रमः प्रोक्तः सर्वेषां यजनक्रमात् (eṣa karma kramaḥ proktaḥ sarveṣāṃ yajanakramāt) — This order of action is said to be the order of all sacrifices;
प्रवक्ष्यामि जपस्थानं पद्मेशावरणे तथा (pravakṣyāmi japasthānaṃ padmeśāvaraṇe tathā) — I shall describe the place of chanting and the lotus covering

BP 1.49.22

आदित्यं कर्णिकासंस्थं दलेष्वङ्गानि पूर्वशः ।
सोमादीन्राहुपर्यंतान्ग्रहांश्चैवोदगादितः ॥

Place Āditya in the pericarp and the limbs first on the petals. Place the planets from Soma to Rāhu, beginning from the north.

आदित्यं कर्णिकासंस्थं दलेष्वङ्गानि पूर्वशः (ādityaṃ karṇikāsaṃsthaṃ daleṣvaṅgāni pūrvaśaḥ) — The sun is seated in the ears and the limbs are in the clusters in the former;
सोमादीन्राहुपर्यंतान्ग्रहांश्चैवोदगादितः (somādīnrāhuparyaṃtāngrahāṃścaivodagāditaḥ) — From the rising of the moon and other planets up to Rahu

BP 1.49.23

मूर्तिमल्लोकपालांश्च क्रमादावरणेष्वथ ।
तदस्त्राणि च रक्षार्थं स्वमन्त्रैः पूजयेत्क्रमात् ॥

Then in the enclosures, one should worship the embodied guardians of the worlds in order, and their weapons for protection, each in turn with its own mantra.

मूर्तिमल्लोकपालांश्च क्रमादावरणेष्वथ (mūrtimallokapālāṃśca kramādāvaraṇeṣvatha) — The idol and the guardians of the worlds were placed in the covers in order;
तदस्त्राणि च रक्षार्थं स्वमन्त्रैः पूजयेत्क्रमात् (tadastrāṇi ca rakṣārthaṃ svamantraiḥ pūjayetkramāt) — And to protect those weapons, one should worship them with their own mantras in order

BP 1.49.24

प्रणवैश्चाभिधानैश्च चतुर्थ्यां ह्यभियोजितैः ।
सर्वेषां कथिता मन्त्रा मुद्राश्च कथयाम्यतः ॥

The mantras for all are formed by joining Om and the names in the fourth case. I shall now explain the hand gestures.

प्रणवैश्चाभिधानैश्च चतुर्थ्यां ह्यभियोजितैः (praṇavaiścābhidhānaiśca caturthyāṃ hyabhiyojitaiḥ) — The oṁkāras and the abhidhanas are invoked on the fourth day;
सर्वेषां कथिता मन्त्रा मुद्राश्च कथयाम्यतः (sarveṣāṃ kathitā mantrā mudrāśca kathayāmyataḥ) — I shall relate the mantras and mudras recited by all

BP 1.49.25

व्योममुद्रा रतिः पद्मा महाश्वेतास्त्रमेव च ।
पञ्चमुद्राः समाख्याताः सर्वकर्मप्रसिद्धये ॥

Sky, Love, Lotus, Great White, and Weapon are declared as the five hand gestures for success in all rites.

व्योममुद्रा रतिः पद्मा महाश्वेतास्त्रमेव च (vyomamudrā ratiḥ padmā mahāśvetāstrameva ca) — Vyomamudra Rati, Padma and the great white weapon;
पञ्चमुद्राः समाख्याताः सर्वकर्मप्रसिद्धये (pañcamudrāḥ samākhyātāḥ sarvakarmaprasiddhaye) — The five seals are described for the perfection of all actions

BP 1.49.26

उत्तानौ तु करौ कृत्या अङ्गुल्यो ग्रथिताः क्रमात् ।
तर्जनीं यन्ति यावत्ताः समे वाधोमुखे स्थिते ॥

Hold both hands open and turned upward, with the fingers interlaced in order until the index fingers meet, with the hands level or turned downward.

उत्तानौ तु करौ कृत्या अङ्गुल्यो ग्रथिताः क्रमात् (uttānau tu karau kṛtyā aṅgulyo grathitāḥ kramāt) — The hands were raised and the fingers were tied together in order;
तर्जनीं यन्ति यावत्ताः समे वाधोमुखे स्थिते (tarjanīṃ yanti yāvattāḥ same vādhomukhe sthite) — They go to the index finger as long as they are standing on the same west face

BP 1.49.27

तर्जन्यो मध्यमस्यैव ज्येष्ठाग्रे वानुगोपरि ।
मुद्रेयं सर्वमुद्राणां व्योम मुद्रेति कीर्तिता ॥
सर्वकर्मसु योगोऽयं तथा स्थानं प्रकल्पते ॥

The index fingers are placed on the middle fingers, or above them at the tips of the ring fingers. This is called the Sky hand gesture among all gestures. This joining is used in all rites, and its position is arranged accordingly.

तर्जन्यो मध्यमस्यैव ज्येष्ठाग्रे वानुगोपरि (tarjanyo madhyamasyaiva jyeṣṭhāgre vānugopari) — The index finger of the middle one is at the tip of the eldest or above the left;
मुद्रेयं सर्वमुद्राणां व्योम मुद्रेति कीर्तिता (mudreyaṃ sarvamudrāṇāṃ vyoma mudreti kīrtitā) — This seal is called the sky seal of all seals;
सर्वकर्मसु योगोऽयं तथा स्थानं प्रकल्पते (sarvakarmasu yogo'yaṃ tathā sthānaṃ prakalpate) — This yoga assumes such a place in all actions

BP 1.49.28

पद्मवत्प्रसृताः सर्वा महाश्वेता रवेः स्मृता ।
जवसंनिहितो नित्यं रथारूढो रविः स्मृतः ॥

All the fingers are extended like a lotus, and this is remembered as the Great White for Ravi. Ravi is always described as attended by speed and mounted on a chariot.

पद्मवत्प्रसृताः सर्वा महाश्वेता रवेः स्मृता (padmavatprasṛtāḥ sarvā mahāśvetā raveḥ smṛtā) — They are all spread out like lotuses and are considered to be great white by the Sun;
जवसंनिहितो नित्यं रथारूढो रविः स्मृतः (javasaṃnihito nityaṃ rathārūḍho raviḥ smṛtaḥ) — The Sun is said to be always riding on a chariot associated with speed

BP 1.49.29

हस्तावूर्ध्वमुखौ कृत्वा वामाङ्गुष्ठेन योजितौ ।
द्रव्याणां शोधने योज्या रक्षार्थं च विशेषतः ॥

Having turned both hands upward and joined them with the left thumb, this gesture is used for purifying substances and especially for protection.

हस्तावूर्ध्वमुखौ कृत्वा वामाङ्गुष्ठेन योजितौ (hastāvūrdhvamukhau kṛtvā vāmāṅguṣṭhena yojitau) — With the hands facing upwards, joined with the left thumb;
द्रव्याणां शोधने योज्या रक्षार्थं च विशेषतः (dravyāṇāṃ śodhane yojyā rakṣārthaṃ ca viśeṣataḥ) — It should be used in the purification of materials and especially for protection

BP 1.49.30

अनया मुद्रया सर्वं रक्षितं शोधितं भवेत् ।
अर्घ्यं दत्वा प्रयोक्तव्या पूजान्ते च विशेषतः ॥

By this gesture everything becomes protected and purified. It should especially be used after offering water, at the end of worship.

अनया मुद्रया सर्वं रक्षितं शोधितं भवेत् (anayā mudrayā sarvaṃ rakṣitaṃ śodhitaṃ bhavet) — With this seal everything should be protected and purified;
अर्घ्यं दत्वा प्रयोक्तव्या पूजान्ते च विशेषतः (arghyaṃ datvā prayoktavyā pūjānte ca viśeṣataḥ) — It should be used after offering arghya and especially at the end of worship

BP 1.49.31

जपध्यानावसाने च यदीच्छेत्सिद्धिमात्मनः ।
अनेन विधिना नित्यं जपेदब्दमतन्द्रितः ॥

At the end of recitation and meditation, if one desires accomplishment for oneself, one should recite daily by this method for a year without weariness.

जपध्यानावसाने च यदीच्छेत्सिद्धिमात्मनः (japadhyānāvasāne ca yadīcchetsiddhimātmanaḥ) — And at the end of chanting and meditating, if he desires perfection of himself;
अनेन विधिना नित्यं जपेदब्दमतन्द्रितः (anena vidhinā nityaṃ japedabdamatandritaḥ) — One should chant this mantra daily for a year without laziness

BP 1.49.32

स लभेतेप्सितान्कामानिहामुत्र न संशयः ।
रोगार्तो मुच्यते रोगाद्धनहीनो धनं लभेत् ॥

He obtains the desired aims here and hereafter, without doubt. One afflicted with disease is freed from disease, and one without wealth obtains wealth.

स लभेतेप्सितान्कामानिहामुत्र न संशयः (sa labhetepsitānkāmānihāmutra na saṃśayaḥ) — He will undoubtedly obtain the desired desires here and hereafter;
रोगार्तो मुच्यते रोगाद्धनहीनो धनं लभेत् (rogārto mucyate rogāddhanahīno dhanaṃ labhet) — He who is sick is freed from disease and he who has no wealth gets wealth

BP 1.49.33

राज्यभ्रष्टो लभेद्राज्यमपुत्रः पुत्रमाप्नुयात् ।
प्रज्ञामेधासमृद्धीश्च चिरं जीवति मानवः ॥
सुरूपां लभते कन्यां कुलीना पुरुषो ध्रुवम् ॥

One deprived of a kingdom obtains a kingdom, and one without a son obtains a son. A person gains abundance of insight and intelligence and lives long. A man certainly obtains a beautiful girl of noble family.

राज्यभ्रष्टो लभेद्राज्यमपुत्रः पुत्रमाप्नुयात् (rājyabhraṣṭo labhedrājyamaputraḥ putramāpnuyāt) — He who has lost the kingdom will get the kingdom and he who has no son will get a son;
प्रज्ञामेधासमृद्धीश्च चिरं जीवति मानवः (prajñāmedhāsamṛddhīśca ciraṃ jīvati mānavaḥ) — A man lives long with wisdom, intelligence and prosperity;
सुरूपां लभते कन्यां कुलीना पुरुषो ध्रुवम् (surūpāṃ labhate kanyāṃ kulīnā puruṣo dhruvam) — A noble man will surely get a beautiful girl

BP 1.49.34

सौभाग्यं स्त्री कुलीनापि कन्या च पुरुषोत्तमम् ।
अविद्यो लभते विद्यामित्युक्तं भानुना पुरा ॥

A woman obtains good fortune, and a well born maiden obtains an excellent husband. The unlearned obtains knowledge, so Bhānu said formerly.

सौभाग्यं स्त्री कुलीनापि कन्या च पुरुषोत्तमम् (saubhāgyaṃ strī kulīnāpi kanyā ca puruṣottamam) — Fortunately a woman of noble birth and a daughter are the best of men;
अविद्यो लभते विद्यामित्युक्तं भानुना पुरा (avidyo labhate vidyāmityuktaṃ bhānunā purā) — It was said by Bhanu in the past that ignorance attains knowledge

BP 1.49.35

नित्ययागः स्मृतो ह्येष धनधान्यसुखावहः ।
प्रजापशुविवृद्धिश्च निष्कामस्यापि जायते ॥

This daily sacrifice brings wealth, grain, and happiness. Growth of offspring and cattle also comes even to one without desire.

नित्ययागः स्मृतो ह्येष धनधान्यसुखावहः (nityayāgaḥ smṛto hyeṣa dhanadhānyasukhāvahaḥ) — This daily sacrifice is said to bring wealth and grain and happiness;
प्रजापशुविवृद्धिश्च निष्कामस्यापि जायते (prajāpaśuvivṛddhiśca niṣkāmasyāpi jāyate) — and the increase of the people and the cattle is born even of the unwilling

BP 1.49.36

तदैकः स्तूयते स्वर्गे शब्द्यते च नरोत्तमः ।
भक्त्या तं पूजयेद्यस्तु नरः पुण्यतरः सदा ॥

Then he alone is praised in heaven and acclaimed as the best of men. Whoever worships him with devotion is always more meritorious.

तदैकः स्तूयते स्वर्गे शब्द्यते च नरोत्तमः (tadaikaḥ stūyate svarge śabdyate ca narottamaḥ) — Then one is praised in heaven and sounded like the best of men;
भक्त्या तं पूजयेद्यस्तु नरः पुण्यतरः सदा (bhaktyā taṃ pūjayedyastu naraḥ puṇyataraḥ sadā) — But he who worships Him with devotion is always more holy

BP 1.49.37

इह वै कामिकं प्राप्य ततो गच्छेन्मनोः पदम् ।
द्विजस्तस्य प्रसादेन तेजसा बुधसन्निभः ॥

Having gained the desired reward here, he then goes to the abode of Manu. By his favor, the twice born man shines like Budha.

इह वै कामिकं प्राप्य ततो गच्छेन्मनोः पदम् (iha vai kāmikaṃ prāpya tato gacchenmanoḥ padam) — Here, having attained the lustful, one should then go to the abode of the mind;
द्विजस्तस्य प्रसादेन तेजसा बुधसन्निभः (dvijastasya prasādena tejasā budhasannibhaḥ) — By his grace the brahmin resembled Mercury in splendor
