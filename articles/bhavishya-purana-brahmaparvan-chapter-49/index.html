Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 49 — The Method of Sun Worship

BP 1.49.1

वासुदेव उवाच
अथार्चनविधिं वक्ष्ये धर्मकेतोरनुत्तमम् ।
सर्वकामप्रदं पुण्यं विघ्नघ्नं दुरितापहम् ॥

Vāsudeva said, I shall now describe the excellent rite of worship of Dharmaketu, meritorious, granting every desire, destroying obstacles and removing evil.

BP 1.49.2

सूर्यमन्त्रैः पुरः स्नातो यजेत्तेनैव भास्करम् ।
यतस्ततः प्रवक्ष्यामि स्नानमादौ समासतः ॥

Having bathed before the Sun with the Sun mantras, one should worship Bhāskara with those same mantras. I shall briefly explain the bath first.

BP 1.49.3

आचान्तस्तमुपालभ्य मुद्रया शुचिशुद्धया ।
कृत्वा नीराजनं पुत्र संशोध्य च जलं ततः ॥

Having sipped water and approached him with a purified gesture, perform the waving of lights, my son, and then purify the water.

BP 1.49.4

स्नानाद्धृदयपूतेन मन्त्रेण मत्कुलोद्वह ।
उत्थायाचम्य तेनैव वाससी परिधाय च ॥

O upholder of my lineage, bathe with the mantra purified in the heart. Then rise, sip water with that same mantra, and put on two garments.

BP 1.49.5

द्विराचम्याथ सम्प्रोक्ष्य तनुं सप्ताक्षरेण च ।
उत्थायाचम्य तेनैवं रवेः कृत्वार्घ्यमेव च ॥

Then sip water twice and sprinkle the body with the seven syllable mantra. Rise, sip water with that same mantra, and offer water to Ravi.

BP 1.49.6

दत्त्वा तेन जपित्वा तं स्वकं ध्यात्वार्कवद्धृदि ।
गत्वा चायतनं शुभ्रमार्कमार्की तनुं यजेत् ॥

Having made the offering, recite that mantra and meditate on him as the Sun in the heart of the worshipper. Then go to the bright shrine and worship the Sun in his solar form.

BP 1.49.7

पूरकं कुम्भकं कृत्वा रेचकं च समाहितः ।
कृत्वोङ्कारेण दोषांस्तु हन्यात्कायादिसम्भवान् ॥

Being composed, perform inhalation, retention, and exhalation. With Om, destroy the faults arising from the body and the rest.

BP 1.49.8

वायव्याग्नेयमाहेन्द्रवारुणीभिर्यथाक्रमम् ।
किल्विषं वारुणाद्भिश्च हन्यात्सिद्ध्यर्थमात्मनः ॥

Using the Air, Fire, Indra, and Varuna rites in order, one should destroy sin with the waters of Varuna for attainment of the worshipper.

BP 1.49.9

शोषणं दहनं स्तम्भं प्लावनं च यथाक्रमात् ।
वाय्वग्नीन्द्रजनाख्याभिर्धारणाभिः कृते सति ॥

The drying, burning, stilling, and flooding are performed in order by the practices called Air, Fire, Indra, and Water.

BP 1.49.10

ध्यात्वा विशुद्धमात्मानं प्रणमेदर्कमास्थितम् ।
देहं तेनैव सञ्चिन्त्य पञ्चभूतमयं परम् ॥

Having meditated on the purified self, one should bow to the Sun established there. One should conceive the body as that supreme form made of the five elements.

BP 1.49.11

सूक्ष्मं स्थूलं तथाक्षाणि स्वस्थानेषु प्रकम्य च ।
विन्यस्याङ्गानि खादीनि हृदाद्यानि हृद्यादिषु ॥

Having placed the subtle, the gross, and the senses in their proper places, one should arrange the limbs beginning with space, and those beginning with the heart, in their respective places.

BP 1.49.12

खस्वाहा हदयं भानोः खमर्काय शिरस्तथा ।
उल्का स्वाहा शिखार्कस्य यै च हुं कवचं परम् ॥
खां फडस्त्रं च संहाराश्चादितः प्रणवः कृतः ॥

Kha Svāhā is the heart of Bhānu, Kham is the head of Arka, Ulkā Svāhā is the crest of Arka, and Yai Huṃ is the supreme armor. Khāṃ Phaṭ is the weapon. The dissolution mantras are preceded by Om.

BP 1.49.13

स पूर्वे प्रणवस्याथो मन्त्रकर्मप्रसिद्धये ।
एभिर्जलं त्रिधा जप्त्वा स्नानद्रव्याणि तेन च ॥

For the success of the mantra rite, Om is placed before these. Having recited these over the water three times, sprinkle the bathing substances with it.

BP 1.49.14

सम्प्रोक्ष्य पूजयेत्सूर्यं गन्धपुष्पादिभिः शुभैः ।
ततो मूर्तिषु सर्वासु रात्रावग्नौ प्रपूजयेत् ॥

After sprinkling, one should worship the Sun with auspicious perfumes, flowers, and the rest. Then one should worship all the images in the fire at night.

BP 1.49.15

प्राक्पश्चिमोदगभ्यग्रां प्रातः सायं निशासु वै ।
सप्ताक्षरेण तन्मन्त्रं ध्यात्वा च पद्मकर्णिकाम् ॥

Facing east, west, or north in the morning, evening, and at night, one should meditate on the seven syllable mantra and the lotus pericarp.

BP 1.49.16

आदित्यमण्डलान्तस्थं तत्र देहं प्रकल्पयेत् ।
प्रभामण्डलमध्यस्थं ध्यात्वा देहं यथा पुरा ॥
सर्वलक्षणसम्पूर्णं सहस्रकिरणोज्ज्वलम् ॥

One should imagine the body placed there within the solar disk. Meditate as before on the body placed in the middle of the circle of radiance, complete with all marks and shining with a thousand rays.

BP 1.49.17

रक्तैर्गन्धैश्च पुष्पैश्च चरुभिर्बलिभिस्तथा ।
रक्तचन्दनमिश्रैर्वा वस्त्रैरावरणैः शुभैः ॥

With red perfumes, flowers, cooked offerings, and gifts, or with garments and auspicious coverings mixed with red sandalwood.

BP 1.49.18

आवाहनादिकर्माणि रक्षां तु हृदयेन च ।
तच्चित्तश्च सदा कुर्याज्ज्ञात्वा कर्मक्रमं बुधः ॥

A wise person, knowing the order of the rites, should always perform the acts beginning with invocation and protection by the heart mantra, keeping the mind on him.

BP 1.49.19

कृत्वा चावाहनं मन्त्रैरेकत्र स्थापनं ततः ।
यावद्यागावसानं तु सान्निध्यं तत्र कल्प्य च ॥

Having invoked him with mantras, one should then establish him in one place and maintain his presence there until the worship is complete.

BP 1.49.20

दत्त्वा पाद्यादिकां पूजां शक्त्या वार्घ्यं निवेद्य च ।
जपित्वा विधिवद्ध्यात्वा ततो देवीं विसर्जयेत् ॥

Having offered worship beginning with water for the feet, one should offer water for welcome according to ability. After reciting as prescribed and meditating, one should then dismiss the Goddess.

BP 1.49.21

एष कर्म क्रमः प्रोक्तः सर्वेषां यजनक्रमात् ।
प्रवक्ष्यामि जपस्थानं पद्मेशावरणे तथा ॥

This is declared to be the order of the rite for the worship of all. I shall also explain the place for recitation and the enclosure of the Lotus Lord.

BP 1.49.22

आदित्यं कर्णिकासंस्थं दलेष्वङ्गानि पूर्वशः ।
सोमादीन्राहुपर्यंतान्ग्रहांश्चैवोदगादितः ॥

Place Āditya in the pericarp and the limbs first on the petals. Place the planets from Soma to Rāhu, beginning from the north.

BP 1.49.23

मूर्तिमल्लोकपालांश्च क्रमादावरणेष्वथ ।
तदस्त्राणि च रक्षार्थं स्वमन्त्रैः पूजयेत्क्रमात् ॥

Then in the enclosures, one should worship the embodied guardians of the worlds in order, and their weapons for protection, each in turn with its own mantra.

BP 1.49.24

प्रणवैश्चाभिधानैश्च चतुर्थ्यां ह्यभियोजितैः ।
सर्वेषां कथिता मन्त्रा मुद्राश्च कथयाम्यतः ॥

The mantras for all are formed by joining Om and the names in the fourth case. I shall now explain the hand gestures.

BP 1.49.25

व्योममुद्रा रतिः पद्मा महाश्वेतास्त्रमेव च ।
पञ्चमुद्राः समाख्याताः सर्वकर्मप्रसिद्धये ॥

Sky, Love, Lotus, Great White, and Weapon are declared as the five hand gestures for success in all rites.

BP 1.49.26

उत्तानौ तु करौ कृत्या अङ्गुल्यो ग्रथिताः क्रमात् ।
तर्जनीं यन्ति यावत्ताः समे वाधोमुखे स्थिते ॥

Hold both hands open and turned upward, with the fingers interlaced in order until the index fingers meet, with the hands level or turned downward.

BP 1.49.27

तर्जन्यो मध्यमस्यैव ज्येष्ठाग्रे वानुगोपरि ।
मुद्रेयं सर्वमुद्राणां व्योम मुद्रेति कीर्तिता ॥
सर्वकर्मसु योगोऽयं तथा स्थानं प्रकल्पते ॥

The index fingers are placed on the middle fingers, or above them at the tips of the ring fingers. This is called the Sky hand gesture among all gestures. This joining is used in all rites, and its position is arranged accordingly.

BP 1.49.28

पद्मवत्प्रसृताः सर्वा महाश्वेता रवेः स्मृता ।
जवसंनिहितो नित्यं रथारूढो रविः स्मृतः ॥

All the fingers are extended like a lotus, and this is remembered as the Great White for Ravi. Ravi is always described as attended by speed and mounted on a chariot.

BP 1.49.29

हस्तावूर्ध्वमुखौ कृत्वा वामाङ्गुष्ठेन योजितौ ।
द्रव्याणां शोधने योज्या रक्षार्थं च विशेषतः ॥

Having turned both hands upward and joined them with the left thumb, this gesture is used for purifying substances and especially for protection.

BP 1.49.30

अनया मुद्रया सर्वं रक्षितं शोधितं भवेत् ।
अर्घ्यं दत्वा प्रयोक्तव्या पूजान्ते च विशेषतः ॥

By this gesture everything becomes protected and purified. It should especially be used after offering water, at the end of worship.

BP 1.49.31

जपध्यानावसाने च यदीच्छेत्सिद्धिमात्मनः ।
अनेन विधिना नित्यं जपेदब्दमतन्द्रितः ॥

At the end of recitation and meditation, if one desires accomplishment for oneself, one should recite daily by this method for a year without weariness.

BP 1.49.32

स लभेतेप्सितान्कामानिहामुत्र न संशयः ।
रोगार्तो मुच्यते रोगाद्धनहीनो धनं लभेत् ॥

He obtains the desired aims here and hereafter, without doubt. One afflicted with disease is freed from disease, and one without wealth obtains wealth.

BP 1.49.33

राज्यभ्रष्टो लभेद्राज्यमपुत्रः पुत्रमाप्नुयात् ।
प्रज्ञामेधासमृद्धीश्च चिरं जीवति मानवः ॥
सुरूपां लभते कन्यां कुलीना पुरुषो ध्रुवम् ॥

One deprived of a kingdom obtains a kingdom, and one without a son obtains a son. A person gains abundance of insight and intelligence and lives long. A man certainly obtains a beautiful girl of noble family.

BP 1.49.34

सौभाग्यं स्त्री कुलीनापि कन्या च पुरुषोत्तमम् ।
अविद्यो लभते विद्यामित्युक्तं भानुना पुरा ॥

A woman obtains good fortune, and a well born maiden obtains an excellent husband. The unlearned obtains knowledge, so Bhānu said formerly.

BP 1.49.35

नित्ययागः स्मृतो ह्येष धनधान्यसुखावहः ।
प्रजापशुविवृद्धिश्च निष्कामस्यापि जायते ॥

This daily sacrifice brings wealth, grain, and happiness. Growth of offspring and cattle also comes even to one without desire.

BP 1.49.36

तदैकः स्तूयते स्वर्गे शब्द्यते च नरोत्तमः ।
भक्त्या तं पूजयेद्यस्तु नरः पुण्यतरः सदा ॥

Then he alone is praised in heaven and acclaimed as the best of men. Whoever worships him with devotion is always more meritorious.

BP 1.49.37

इह वै कामिकं प्राप्य ततो गच्छेन्मनोः पदम् ।
द्विजस्तस्य प्रसादेन तेजसा बुधसन्निभः ॥

Having gained the desired reward here, he then goes to the abode of Manu. By his favor, the twice born man shines like Budha.
