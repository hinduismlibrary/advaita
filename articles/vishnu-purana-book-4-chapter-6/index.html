VP 4.6.1

सूर्यस्य वंश्या भगवन्कथिता भवता मम । सोमस्याप्यखिलान्वंश्याञ्छ्रोतुमिच्छामि पार्थिवान्

Maitreyar - O Paraasharar, you told me about Soorya vamsha. The kings who were in the Soma vamsha, I would like to know about all of them also.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sūryasya vaṃśyā bhagavankathitā bhavatā mama | somasyāpyakhilānvaṃśyāñchrotumicchāmi pārthivān

Śrīdhara

sūrya-vaṃśa-prasaṅgena soma-vaṃśas tad-anvaye . elo’pi varṇyate ṣaṣṭhe tretāgnir yena nirmitaḥ ||१|| kīrtyate sthira-kīrtīnāṃ yeṣām adyāpi santatiḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

सूर्य-वंश-प्रसङ्गेन सोम-वंशस् तद्-अन्वये ।

एलोऽपि वर्ण्यते षष्ठे त्रेताग्निर् येन निर्मितः ॥१॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Maitreyar - O Paraasharar, you told me about Soorya vamsha. The kings who were in the Soma vamsha, I would like to know about all of them also.

VP 4.6.2

They are all very great kings. Their fame is sung about all over, even today. Be pleased to narrate to me the story of all the kings of the Soma vamsha.

VP 4.6.4

अयं हि वंशोऽतिबलपराक्रमद्युतिशीलचेष्टावद्भिरतिगुणान्वितैर्नहुषययातिकार्तवीर्यार्जुनादिभिर्भूपालैरलङ्कृः तमहं कथयामि श्रूयताम्

The kings of this vamsha were very valiant, very powerful, they had good character, conduct, endowed with great good qualities; it was decorated by such great kings, Nahusha, Yayaati, Kaartaveeryaarjuna and others, I am going to tell you their story, listen to me.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ayaṃ hi vaṃśo'tibalaparākramadyutiśīlaceṣṭāvadbhiratiguṇānvitairnahuṣayayātikārtavīryārjunādibhirbhūpālairalaṅkṛḥ tamahaṃ kathayāmi śrūyatām

Śrīdhara

atiśayitaṃ balaṃ sāmarthyaṃ, parākramaḥ śauryaṃ, dyutiḥ kāntiḥ, śīlam ācāraḥ, ceṣṭā dāna-bhogādi-līlā, atibalādibhir yuktaiḥ guṇā gāmbhīryādayaḥ atiguṇānvitaiḥ, ca alaṅkṛtaḥ ||४||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अतिशयितं बलं सामर्थ्यं, पराक्रमः शौर्यं, द्युतिः कान्तिः, शीलम् आचारः, चेष्टा दान-भोगादि-लीला, अतिबलादिभिर् युक्तैः गुणा गाम्भीर्यादयः अतिगुणान्वितैः, च अलङ्कृतः ॥४॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

The kings of this vamsha were very valiant, very powerful, they had good character, conduct, endowed with great good qualities; it was decorated by such great kings, Nahusha, Yayaati, Kaartaveeryaarjuna and others, I am going to tell you their story, listen to me.

आम्नायानुसारी वेदविधौ दत्तचित्तः गार्हपत्याहवनीयदक्षिणाग्निरूपेणाग्निं त्रिधा कृत्वा मनोरथमुद्दिश्य कृत्वा ॥ शमीगर्भाश्वत्थमथनादुत्थित एवाग्निरग्निहोत्रोपयुक्त इति दर्शयन्नाहस—अन्तरटव्याम् इति । यद्यप्यग्निं त्रिधा कृत्वा उर्वशीलोकमुद्दिश्य यजेथा इत्येवं गन्धर्वैरुक्तं नत्वियमुर्वशीति तथापि मोहवशात् पुरूरवा उर्वशीयमित्यवगत्य इदानीमचिन्तयद् इत्य् अर्थः ॥७८॥ अन्तर् अटव्याम् अचिन्तयत् अहो मेऽतीव मूढता किम् अहम् अकरवम् ? ७९॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

They told him that according to the injunctions of the Vedaas, make this Agni into three, perform yaagaas with the sankalpa that you want to get saalokya with Urvashi; desiring that you want to be in the same world as Urvashi. You will definitely get Urvashi saalokya with this. Then he took that Agnisthaalee and went away. Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4
