Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 186 — Description of Purification

BP 1.186.1

भास्कर उवाच ।। श्रावण्यां तु बलिः कार्यः सर्पाणां मंत्रपूर्वकः ।। शयनारोहणे चैव कार्ये सुखमभीप्सता ।। १ ।।

Bhāskara said: In Śrāvaṇa, an offering to the serpents should be made with mantras; it should also be made when one goes to bed, by a person desiring comfort.

BP 1.186.2

कार्यः प्रत्यवरोहस्तु मार्गशीर्ष्यां न संशयः ।। फलं विना त्वनुष्ठानं नित्यानामिष्यते स्फुटम् ।। २ ।।

The descent should be performed in Mārgaśīrṣa, without doubt; performance of obligatory rites without expectation of fruit is clearly desired.

BP 1.186.3

काम्यानां सफलार्थं तु दोषप्राप्त्यर्थमेव च ।। नैमित्तिकानां करणं त्रिविधं कर्मणां फलम् ।। ३ ।।

Desired rites are performed to obtain their fruit, and occasional rites to avert fault; the fruit of actions is threefold.

BP 1.186.4

फलं केचिदुपात्तस्य दुरितस्य प्रचक्षते ।। अनुत्पत्तिं तथा चान्ये प्रत्येत्याभ्युपमंत्र्य च ।। ४ ।।

Some declare the result to be removal of the sin incurred; others, having considered and agreed, say it is the non-arising of sin.

BP 1.186.5

नित्यक्रियं तथा चान्ये अनुषंगात्फलं श्रुतिः ।। अग्न्याधेयं तथा दर्शं पौर्णमासं द्वितीयकम्।।५।।

Others say the result of obligatory action follows from its performance; the Vedas prescribe fire-establishment, the new-moon rite, and the second full-moon rite.

BP 1.186.6

चातुर्मास्यमग्निहोत्रं पशुबंधो निरूढकाः ।। सौत्रामणी संस्थाः स्युर्हविषः श्रुतिनोदिताः ।। ६ ।।

The four-month rites, fire-offerings, animal sacrifice, the tethered-animal rites, the Sautrāmaṇī, and the forms of food oblation are enjoined by the Vedas.

BP 1.186.7

अग्निष्टोमोऽत्यग्निष्टोम उक्थ्यः संषोडशी तथा ।। वाजपेयातिरात्रश्च आप्योर्यामः श्रुतौ श्रुतः ।।७।।

The fire hymn, the greater fire hymn, the hymn rite, the sixteenfold rite, the strength-offering, the overnight rite, and Āpyoryāma are mentioned in the Vedas.

BP 1.186.8

दया स्यात्सर्वभूतेषु अनसूयाथ मंगलम् ।। क्षांतिर्दया त्वनायासः शौचमस्पृहता व्रतम् ।। ८ ।।

There should be compassion toward all beings, freedom from envy, and auspiciousness; patience, compassion, effortlessness, purity, and lack of covetousness are vows.

BP 1.186.9

सम्यगुक्तास्तु संस्कारा ब्रह्मप्राप्तिनिमित्तकाः ।। अनंतरं प्रवक्ष्यामि विप्राणां वृत्तयः शुभाः ।। ९ ।।

The purificatory rites have been rightly declared as means of attaining Brahman; next, I will tell the good occupations of Brahmins.

BP 1.186.10

ऋतामृते च विप्राणामृतं प्रमृतमेव च ।। प्रतिग्रहवणिज्यादि श्रेयसी नोत्तरोत्तरा ।। 1.186.१० ।।

For Brahmins, gleaning, an unasked gift, begging, and cultivation, followed by accepting gifts, trade, and the rest, are successively less worthy.

BP 1.186.11

आजीविकावृत्तयस्तु इत्याद्याः संप्रवर्तिताः ।। तासां कयापि जीवेत्तु अनुतिष्ठेद्यथाविधि ।। ११ ।।

These ways of livelihood have been set forth; one should live by any one of them and act according to its rule.

BP 1.186.12

नित्यं शुचिः सुगंधश्च स्नानशीलः प्रियंवदः ।। पूज्यश्च पूजयेद्देवान्कार्याणि स्वयमाचरेत् ।। १२ ।।

One should be clean and fragrant every day, given to bathing and pleasant speech; being worthy of worship, one should worship the gods and perform one’s tasks oneself.

BP 1.186.13

नेक्षेतार्कं न नग्नां स्त्रीं न च संस्पृष्ट मैथुनम् ।। नाप्सु मूत्रं पुरीषं वा नाशुची रात्रितारकाः ।। १३ ।।

One should not look at the Sun, a naked woman, or sexual intercourse; one should not urinate or defecate in water, nor look at inauspicious stars at night.

BP 1.186.14

शास्त्रोक्ता यंत्रणा या तु नानुक्तानि व्रतानि च ।। स्वर्गार्थं साधयेत्तैश्च शक्तिमान्मनसा तथा ।।१४।।

The restraints prescribed in scripture and the vows not otherwise stated should be practiced for heaven’s sake by one able to do so, with strength and with the mind.

BP 1.186.15

नित्यानि केचिदिच्छंति काम्यानि च तथापरे ।। काम्याप्रवृत्तौ संगे च प्रायश्चित्तं विधीयते ।।१५।।

Some desire obligatory rites, others rites for a desired end. When one undertakes the latter and becomes attached, atonement is prescribed.

BP 1.186.16

व्रतानि मनसा त्विष्टसंकल्प इति मानसः ।। अंतरानुफलं यत्तदृषिवादाः प्रकीर्तिताः ।।१६।।

Vows undertaken in the mind with a desired resolve are mental vows; their inner result is proclaimed in the sayings of seers.

BP 1.186.17

अनध्यायं स्वयं सम्यग्वर्जयेत्फलसाधनम् ।। आत्मा शुद्धस्तथा देशो ह्यसुहारः प्रजापदः ।। १७ ।।

One should properly avoid days on which study is forbidden, as a means to the result. The self, place, food, and people should be pure.

BP 1.186.18

अशुभानि निमित्तानि उत्पातो विकृतं तथा ।। पर्वाणि मनसोऽशुद्धिरनध्याय इति स्मृतः ।।१८।।

Inauspicious signs, portents, abnormalities, sacred days, and impurity of mind are remembered as days on which study is forbidden.

BP 1.186.19

अनध्यायाश्च दृष्टार्था अदृष्टार्थास्तथापरे ।। वेदाध्ययनमेवेति त्रिधा मद्ध्यानदर्शनम् ।।१९।।

Days on which study is forbidden are said to have visible purposes, while others have unseen purposes; Vedic study itself, according to my meditative vision, is threefold.

BP 1.186.20

अभक्ष्यं सर्ववर्णानां शावाशौचं खगाधिप ।। द्रव्यशुद्धिस्तथैव स्यादन्यथा त्वसमंजसम् ।।1.186.२०।।

O lord of birds, forbidden foods for all varṇas, impurity from a corpse, and the purification of substances are to be understood; otherwise it is improper.

BP 1.186.21

जातिदुष्टं क्रियादुष्टं कालदुष्टं विभूषितम्।। संसर्गाश्रयदुष्टं च सहृल्लेखं स्वभावतः ।।२१।।

Food is tainted by kind, preparation, time, ornamentation, contact, source, a mark in the heart, and its nature.

BP 1.186.22

लशुनं गृंजनं चैव पलांडुं कवकानि च ।। वार्ताकं नालिकेरं तु मूलकं जातिदुष्टकम्।।२२।।

Garlic, a small red kind of garlic or onion, onion, mushrooms, eggplant, coconut, and radish are tainted by kind.

BP 1.186.23

नो भुञ्जीत क्रियादुष्टं दुष्टं च पतितैः पृथक्।। कालदुष्टं तु विज्ञेयं हानिदं चिरसंस्थितम् ।।२३।।

One should not eat food tainted by preparation, nor food tainted through contact with fallen persons. Food spoiled by time is harmful when it has stood for long.

BP 1.186.24

दधिभक्ष्यविकाराश्च मधुवर्ज्यास्तदिष्यते ।।२४।।

Curd, foods, and their preparations are permitted, excluding honey.

BP 1.186.25

सुरालशुनसंस्पृष्टं पेयूषादिसमन्वितम् ।। संसर्गदुष्टमेतद्धि शुनोच्छिष्टं खगेश्वर।।२५।।

Food touched by liquor and garlic, combined with whey and the like, is tainted by contact; so too is what a dog has left, O lord of birds.

BP 1.186.26

शूद्रसक्तं खंडसक्तं ज्ञेयमाश्रयदूषितम्।। विचिकित्सा तु हृदये भक्ष्ये यस्मिन्त्सुजायते।।२६।।

Food attached to a Śūdra or a fragment is tainted by its source; doubt arises in the heart about food.

BP 1.186.27

सहृल्लेखं तु तज्ज्ञेयं पुरीषं तु स्वभावतः ।। रसदुष्टे विकारोऽपि रसस्येति प्रदर्शितः।।२७।।

That is known as marked by the heart. Feces is impure by nature; in food tainted in taste, a change of taste is also shown.

BP 1.186.28

पायसं क्षीरपाकादि तस्मिन्नेव दिने तथा।। यथाशास्त्रं खगश्रेष्ठ भक्ष्याभक्ष्ये निरूपयेत्।।२८।।

Rice pudding, milk-cooked foods, and the like are for that very day; according to scripture, O best of birds, one should determine what is edible and inedible.

BP 1.186.29

प्राणात्यये प्रोक्षितं च श्राद्धे च द्विजकाम्यया ।। पितॄन्देवांश्चार्पयित्वा भुंजन्मांसं न दोषभाक्।।२९।।

Meat sprinkled in a life-threatening emergency, or at an ancestral rite at a Brahmin’s request, may be eaten after it has been offered to the ancestors and gods, without incurring fault.

BP 1.186.30

प्रेताशुद्धिः सपिंडानां तस्मिन्नेव मृते सति।। दशाहं द्वादशाहं वा पक्षं मासं त्वशुद्धता ।। 1.186.३० ।।

When one dies, the impurity of close kin lasts ten days, twelve days, a fortnight, or a month.

BP 1.186.31

दशाहादित्रिके भागे वर्णशो न भवंति हि ।। दशाहेन तु भोज्याः स्युः सूतकाशौचयोस्तथा ।।३१।।

In the three divisions beginning with ten days, the periods are not divided by varṇa; they may receive food after ten days during both birth and death impurity.

BP 1.186.32

ऊर्ध्वं दशाहादेकाहश्रवणे सति ।। संवत्सरे व्यतीते तु स्नानादेव विशुध्यति ।।३२।।

If one hears of a death after ten days have passed, impurity lasts one day; once a year has passed, one is purified by bathing alone.

BP 1.186.33

समानोदकता प्रोक्ता जन्मनाम्नोरपर्यये ।। सपिंडाः सप्तपुरुषाः श्रुतावेतन्निदर्शनम् ।।३३।।

The relation of shared water offerings is declared when births and names are no longer traced. Close kin extend for seven generations; this is the Vedic example.

BP 1.186.34

आ दंतजन्मनः सद्य आचूडान्नैष्ठिकी स्मृता ।। त्रिरात्रमाव्रतादेशात्सपिंडेषु मृतेषु च ।।३४।।

Until a child’s teeth appear, purification is immediate; until the tonsure, a fixed rule is remembered. Three nights are prescribed for a vow observance and when close kin die.

BP 1.186.35

तेषामपि तदेकं स्याद्वयोवस्थाप्यपेक्ष्यते ।। समानोदकात्त्रिरात्रेण शुध्येद्वै मृत्युजन्मनोः ।।३५।।

For them too, the period is one, depending on age. Those sharing water are purified in three nights after birth or death.

BP 1.186.36

गर्भस्रावे त्रिरात्रेण उदक्या शुध्यते तथा ।। अनंतजन्म मरणे तच्छेषेण विशुध्यति ।।३६।।

In miscarriage, a woman in impurity is purified after three nights; likewise, after birth and death, one is purified at the end of the remaining period.

BP 1.186.37

द्विजानां त्वेवमेव स्यात्पित्रर्थं मातुरेव वा ।। अग्निहोत्रार्थं विज्ञेयं सद्यः शौचमिति स्थितिः ।।३७।।

For Brahmins, it is the same in regard to father or mother; for the fire offering, immediate purity is the rule.

BP 1.186.38

असपिंडे तु निर्हारात्त्रिरात्रमपि मानवः ।। तस्यैवानुगतौ ज्ञेयं सद्यः शौचं खगाधिप ।।३८।।

For a person who is not close kin, impurity lasts three nights from removal; for one following that same person, purity is immediate, O lord of birds.

BP 1.186.39

शुध्येद्द्विजो दशाहेन जन्महानौ द्वियोनिषु ।। षङ्भिस्त्रिभिरहै केन क्षत्रविट्शूद्रयोनिषु ।।३९।।

A Brahmin is purified in ten days after birth or death; for Kṣatriya, Vaiśya, and Śūdra, the periods are six, three, and one day.

BP 1.186.40

उक्तशौचं यथान्यायं शारीरं तत्त्वदर्शिभिः ।। द्रव्यशुद्धिविधानं तु यथावदभिधीयते ।। 1.186.४० ।।

Bodily purification has been correctly stated by those who see the truth; now the procedure for purifying objects is properly described.

BP 1.186.41

तैजसी मार्तिकी वीर वारिशुद्धिः स्मृता तथा ।। निर्लेपक्षालनेनैव स्पर्शे तु प्रोक्षणेन वै ।।४१।।

Purification by fire, earth, and water is also declared, O hero; by wiping and washing, and by sprinkling after contact.

BP 1.186.42

अशुद्धं नैव किंचिद्धि द्रव्यमस्तीति खेचर ।। वचनाच्छुद्ध्यशुद्धी तु द्रव्याणामिह खेचर ।।४२।।

No substance is inherently impure, O sky-goer; the purity and impurity of substances here arise from prescription, O sky-goer.

BP 1.186.43

स्नानं शौचं च कर्तव्यं द्रव्यशौचादनंतरम् ।। प्रातःस्नानं तु नित्यं स्याद्ग्रहणे काम्यमेव च ।।४३।।

After purifying objects, one should bathe and perform purification. Morning bathing is obligatory; bathing at an eclipse is for a desired purpose.

BP 1.186.44

नैमित्तिकं क्षुराशौचं तेन पापाद्विशुध्यति ।। उक्तं तु शौचं विज्ञेयं दोषक्षयकरं खग ।।४४।।

Purification after shaving is occasional; through it one is purified of sin. The purification described should be understood as removing faults, O bird.

BP 1.186.45

कर्मांगं चेति विज्ञेयं षट्प्रकारा समासतः ।। एवमाचमनं विद्याद्विशिष्टं तु द्विजन्मनाम् ।।४५।।

It is to be understood briefly as sixfold and as a part of ritual; water-sipping is distinctively prescribed for Brahmins.

BP 1.186.46

तदा मृतानां तद्वत्स्यादन्येषां तु यथासुखम् ।। कन्यानिवृत्तिं पुत्रैस्तु यथान्यायं समाचरेत्।। ४६ ।।

Then it applies likewise to the dead; for others, as they please. Sons should arrange a daughter’s marriage according to rule.

BP 1.186.47

कलाशिल्पानिसर्वाणि गृह्रीयात्परितुष्टये ।। ४७ ।।

One should acquire all arts and crafts for contentment.

BP 1.186.48

शुश्रूषेत पतिं भार्या परितोषं यथा व्रजेत् ।। गुरूणां परितोषश्च धर्मः स्त्रीणां सनातनः।।४८।।

A wife should serve her husband so that he may be pleased; pleasing her elders is the eternal duty of women.

BP 1.186.49

वृद्धाऽपुत्रा यदि मृता तदभावे नृपस्य तु ।। मृतापत्याप्यगर्भा च वृद्धापत्या पतिव्रता ।। ४९ ।।

If an old woman dies without a son, in the absence of descendants it belongs to the king. A woman whose child has died, who is not pregnant, or who is old with children is devoted to her husband.

BP 1.186.50

कुर्यादनिर्वृतं भर्तुर्गते सन्निहितेपि वा ।। एतां धर्म समां निष्ठां भर्तृलोकमवाप्नुयात् ।। 1.186.५० ।।

She should fulfill her husband’s wishes, whether he has departed or is nearby; by keeping to this same steadfast duty, she attains her husband’s world.

BP 1.186.51

स्त्री धर्मचारिणी साध्वी मृता दाह्या तथाग्निभिः ।। विपरीताददाह्या तु पुनर्दारक्रिया तथा ।।५१।।

A virtuous woman who follows righteousness should be cremated with fire after death; a woman who is otherwise should not be burned, but should undergo another marriage rite.

BP 1.186.52

स्त्रीणां नियोगो विहितो मरणाद्ब्रह्मचारकम् ।। प्राप्तव्यश्च यथाधर्मो दृष्टादृष्टफलप्रदः ।। ५२ ।।

For women, an appointed union after the husband’s death is prescribed, as is a life of celibacy; righteous conduct should be followed, for it gives visible and unseen fruits.

BP 1.186.53

तस्माद्धर्मं सदा कुर्यात्कुर्वती स्वर्गमाप्नुयात् ।।५३।।

Therefore one should always practice righteousness; a woman who practices it attains heaven.
