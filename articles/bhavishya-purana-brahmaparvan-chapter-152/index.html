Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 152 — Questions on Solar Dharma

BP 1.152.1

सुमन्तुरुवाच । सूरे च दुर्लभा भक्तिर्दुर्लभं सुरपूजनम् । सूराय दुर्लभं दानं सूरहोमश्च दुर्लभः ॥ १ ॥

Sumantu said: Devotion to the Sun is hard to attain; worship of the gods is hard to attain. Offering to the Sun is hard to attain, and oblation to the Sun is hard to attain.

BP 1.152.2

सुदुर्लभं रवेर्ज्ञानं तदभ्यासोऽपि दुर्लभः । सुदुर्लभतरं ज्ञेयं खषोल्कज्ञानमुत्तमम् ॥ २ ॥

Knowledge of Ravi is very hard to attain, and its practice too is hard to attain. The excellent knowledge of the sky-flame is harder still to know.

BP 1.152.3

सुदुर्लभतरं ज्ञानं सदा वै भास्करस्य तु । प्रदक्षिणां चक्रतुर्वै पादौ भक्त्यार्कमन्दिरे ॥ ३ ॥

The knowledge of Bhāskara is always very hard to attain; the two feet performed a reverent circumambulation at the Sun’s temple.

BP 1.152.4

तौ करौ श्लाघ्यतां प्राप्तौ यौ पूजां चक्रतू रवेः । सैवैका रसना धन्या स्तोत्रं या कुरुते रवेः ॥ ४ ॥

Those two hands are praiseworthy which performed worship of Ravi; that one tongue is blessed which sings a hymn to Ravi.

BP 1.152.5

तन्मनः पुण्यतां प्राप्तं यद्धित्वा विषयं नृप । निश्चला च रवेर्लीला निर्भीका क्रोधवर्जिता ॥ ५ ॥

That mind attains purity which abandons sense-objects, O king; Ravi’s play is unwavering, fearless, and free from anger.

BP 1.152.6

शतानीक उवाच । सूर्यार्चनविधिं कुर्वञ्छ्रोतुमिच्छामि तत्त्वतः । त्वत्प्रसादाद्द्विजश्रेष्ठ कौतूहलमतीव मे ॥ ६ ॥

Śatānīka said: I wish to hear the rite of Sun-worship as it truly is, O best of Brahmins; through your favor my curiosity is very great.

BP 1.152.7

यत्पुण्यं स्थापिते सूर्ये कृते सूर्यालये च यत् । सम्मार्जने च यत्पुण्यं यत्पुण्यमुपलेपने ॥ ७ ॥

What merit is there in installing the Sun and in building a temple to the Sun? What merit is there in sweeping, and what merit in plastering?

BP 1.152.8

स्थाने कृते च यत्पुण्यं तथा नीराजने कृते । नीलौषधिप्रवापेन नृत्यमङ्गलवादितैः ॥ ८ ॥

What merit is there in preparing the place, and likewise in waving the lamps; in sprinkling blue herbs, in dancing, and in auspicious instrumental music?

BP 1.152.9

अर्घ्यदानेन यत्पुण्यं तोयस्नानेन यद्भवेत् । पञ्चामृतमयस्नाने दधिस्नाने च यत्फलम् ॥ ९ ॥

What merit comes from offering water, and what comes from bathing with water? What fruit is there in a bath with the five nectars and in a bath with curds?

BP 1.152.10

चक्राभ्यङ्गे च यत्प्रोक्तं वज्रस्नाने च यत्फलम् । मधुस्नाने पयःस्नाने स्नान इक्षुरसस्य तु ॥ १० ॥

What is said of anointing the discus, and what fruit is there in a thunderbolt bath, a honey bath, a milk bath, and a bath with sugarcane juice?

BP 1.152.11

उद्वर्तनं शुचिस्थाने कुशपुष्पोदकेन तु । सुवर्णरत्नतोयैश्च गन्धचन्दनवारिभिः ॥ ११ ॥

What fruit comes from rubbing in a clean place with water containing sacred grass grass and flowers, with water containing gold and gems, and with fragrant sandal-water?

BP 1.152.12

कर्पूरागुरुतोयेन स्वच्छतो येन यत्फलम् । विलेपनैश्च गन्धाढ्यैर्विलेपनफलं लभेत् ॥ १२ ॥

What fruit comes from water with camphor and agarwood, and from clear water? By richly scented unguents one obtains the fruit of anointing.

BP 1.152.13

तालपत्रप्रदाने तु प्रदाने चामरस्य तु । रक्तपुष्पार्चने यच्च दामभिः पूजनेन च ॥ १३ ॥

What merit comes from giving palm-leaves and a fly-whisk, and from worship with red flowers and worship with garlands?

BP 1.152.14

सुमानां मण्डपे यच्च पुष्पमालावलम्बनात् । पूजाभक्तिविशेषैश्च गृहमालावलम्बने ॥ १४ ॥

What merit comes from hanging flower-garlands in a flower pavilion, from special acts of devotion in worship, and from hanging garlands on a house?

BP 1.152.15

पुष्पदानविशेषेण धूपदीपैश्च यत्फलम् । वस्त्रालङ्कारदाने तु पुण्यश्रवणकीर्तने ॥ १५ ॥

What fruit comes from special offerings of flowers, incense, and lamps; what merit comes from giving clothes and ornaments, and from hearing and praising?

BP 1.152.16

ब्रह्मश्रवस्य दाने तु अव्यङ्गस्य च गोपते । मगानां मत्प्रसादेन अभिवादनपूजने ॥ १६ ॥

What merit comes from giving the Brahman-recitation and the Maga girdle, O lord of cattle, and from greeting and worshipping the Magas through your favor?

BP 1.152.17

व्योमपूजाफलं यच्च अरुणस्य च पूजनम् । तथान्यदपि यत्प्रोक्तमज्ञानाद्ब्राह्मणोत्तम ॥ १७ ॥

What is the fruit of worshipping the sky and of worshipping Aruṇa, and of whatever else has been prescribed but is unknown to me, O best of Brahmins?

BP 1.152.18

तत्सर्वं ब्रूहि मे ब्रह्मन्भक्तानामनुकम्पया ॥ १८ ॥

Tell me all of that, O Brahmin, out of compassion for the devotees.
