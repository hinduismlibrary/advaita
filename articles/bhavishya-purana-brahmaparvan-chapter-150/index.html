Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 150 — Āditya Worship Procedure

BP 1.150.1

वासुदेव उवाच । अतः परं प्रवक्ष्यामि यथा पूज्यो दिवाकरः । स्थण्डिले यदुशार्दूल निबोधैकाग्रमानसः ॥

Vāsudeva said: I shall explain further how the Day-maker is to be worshipped on the altar, O tiger among the Yadus. Listen with a single-pointed mind.

BP 1.150.2

मण्डलैरष्टभिः कार्यं चक्रं कालात्मकं शुभम् । मध्ये पद्माकृतं चक्रमरैर्द्वादशभिर्युतम् ॥

One should make an auspicious wheel, the nature of time, with eight circles; in the middle, a lotus-shaped wheel joined with twelve spokes.

BP 1.150.3

तन्मध्ये कमलं प्रोक्तं पत्राष्टकसमन्वितम् । सर्वात्मा सकलो देवः खषोल्कः किरणोज्ज्वलः ॥

In its middle is said to be a lotus endowed with eight petals. The all-souled, complete god, the sky-flame, radiant with rays,

BP 1.150.4

पूजनीयः सदा मध्ये सहस्रकिरणायुधः । प्रणवेन महाबाहो चतुर्बाहुसमन्वितः ॥

is always to be worshipped in the middle, armed with a thousand rays. With Om, O mighty-armed one, he is represented with four arms.

BP 1.150.5

अरुणं पूजयेत्प्राज्ञः सदा देवाग्रजं शुभम् । दक्षिणे पूजयेद्देवीं निक्षुभां भास्करस्य तु ॥

The wise one should always worship auspicious Aruṇa, the elder brother of the god. On the right, one should worship Bhāskara’s goddess Nikṣubhā.

BP 1.150.6

रेवतं दक्षिणे पार्श्वे उत्तरे पिङ्गलं सदा । संज्ञां च यदुशार्दूल श्रेयसे सततं बुधः ॥

Revata on the right side, and Pingala always in the north; and Saṃjñā, O tiger among the Yadus, the wise one should always worship for welfare.

BP 1.150.7

आग्नेय्यां लेखकं वीर नैर्ऋत्यामश्विनौ तथा । वायव्यां पूजयेद्देवं मनुं वैवस्वतं विभुम् ॥

One should worship the Scribe in the southeast, O hero, and the Aśvins in the southwest. In the northwest one should worship the mighty Manu Vaivasvata.

BP 1.150.8

ऐशान्यां पूजयेद्देवीं यमुनां लोकपावनीम् । द्वितीयावरणे वीर पूर्वतः पूजयेद्वियत् ॥

In the northeast one should worship the goddess Yamunā, purifier of the world. In the second enclosure, O hero, one should worship the sky in the east.

BP 1.150.9

दक्षिणे च ततो देवीं पश्चिमे गरुडं तथा । उत्तरे नागराजानं पुत्रमैरावतं शुभम् ॥

In the south, the goddess; in the west, Garuḍa; in the north, the auspicious serpent-king and his son Airāvata.

BP 1.150.10

आग्नेय्यां पूजयेद्धेलिं प्रहेलिं नैर्ऋते तथा । वायव्यामुर्वशीं देवीमीशाने विनतां तथा ॥

In the southeast one should worship Heli, and Praheli in the southwest; in the northwest the goddess Urvaśī, and in the northeast Vinatā.

BP 1.150.11

तृतीयावरणे पूर्वे पूजयेद्गुरुमादरात् । पश्चिमे त्वर्कपुत्रं तु उत्तरे धिषणं तथा ॥

In the third enclosure, one should respectfully worship the Teacher in the east; in the west the Sun’s son, and in the north Dhīṣaṇa.

BP 1.150.12

ऐशाने शशिपुत्रं तु सोममाग्नेयमण्डले । पूजयेद्दक्षिणे कोणे नैर्ऋते राहुमादरात् ॥

In the northeast, the Moon’s son; in the southeastern circle, Soma. One should respectfully worship Rāhu in the southwestern corner.

BP 1.150.13

वायव्ये विकचं वीर पूजयेत्सततं बुधः । चतुर्थावरणे देवं पूजयेल्लेखमादरात् ॥

In the northwest, O hero, the wise one should always worship Vikaca. In the fourth enclosure, one should respectfully worship the god Lekha.

BP 1.150.14

आग्नेये शाण्डिलीपुत्रं दक्षिणे दक्षिणाधिपम् । विरूपाक्षं नैर्ऋते देवं जलेशं पश्चिमे तथा ॥

In the southeast, the son of Śāṇḍilī; in the south, the lord of the south; in the southwest, the odd-eyed god; and Jaleśa in the west.

BP 1.150.15

वायुपुत्रं च वायव्यां सततं पूजयेन्नरः । ईशाने देवमीशानं पूजयेत्सततं बुधः ॥

A man should always worship the son of the Wind in the northwest; in the northeast the wise one should always worship the god Īśāna.

BP 1.150.16

उत्तरे यक्षराजानां कुबेरं पूजयेद्बुधः । पञ्चमे पूजयेेद्वीर सदा स्वावरणे द्विजाः ॥

In the north the wise one should worship Kubera, king of the Yakṣas. In the fifth enclosure, O hero, the twice-born should always worship in their own enclosure.

BP 1.150.17

पूर्वतः परमां देवीं महाश्वेतां महामतिः । श्रियमृद्धिं विभूतिं च धृतिं चैवोन्नतिं तथा ॥

In the east, the wise one should worship the supreme goddess Mahāśvetā, prosperity, abundance, power, steadfastness, and elevation,

BP 1.150.18

पृथिवीं यदुशार्दूल महाकीर्तिं तथैव च । इन्द्रं विष्णुं चार्यमणं भगं पर्जन्यमेव च ॥

Earth, O tiger among the Yadus, and likewise great fame; Indra, Viṣṇu, Aryaman, Bhaga, and Parjanya,

BP 1.150.19

विवस्वन्तं तथार्कं च त्वष्टारं किरणोज्ज्वलम् । पूजयेद्वरुणं षष्ठे चैवमेतान्दिवाकरान् ॥

Vivasvant, Arka, and Tvaṣṭṛ, radiant with rays; in the sixth enclosure one should worship Varuṇa. These are the Day-makers.

BP 1.150.20

शिरो नेत्रे तथा वर्म अस्त्रं च यदुसत्तम । अरुणं सरथं वीर सप्तमे पूजयेद्बुधः ॥

The head, eyes, armor, and weapon, O best of the Yadus, and Aruṇa with his chariot, O hero, one should worship in the seventh enclosure.

BP 1.150.21

तथाश्वान्यदुशार्दूल सदा चावरणे बुधः । यक्षरक्षांसि गन्धर्वान्मासान्पक्षानहानि तु । संवत्सरं तथा पुत्र ह्येतान्संपूजयेत्पुरा ॥

Likewise the horses, O tiger among the Yadus, always in the enclosure; then the Yakṣas, demons, Gandharvas, months, fortnights, days, and the year, O son—these one should worship first.

BP 1.150.22

य एवं पूजयेद्देवं भास्करं सततं नरः । स गच्छेत्परमं स्थानं यत्र गत्वा न शोचति ॥

Whoever thus always worships the god Bhāskara goes to the highest place, where, having gone, he does not grieve.

BP 1.150.23

ॐ खषोल्काय नमः । मूलमन्त्राक्षराणीह चाङ्गानि परिचक्षते । अनेन विधिना यस्तु पूजयेत्सततं रविम् ॥

Om, salutation to the sky-flame. Here the syllables of the root mantra are declared to be its limbs. Whoever always worships Ravi by this procedure

BP 1.150.24

नित्यमुभयसप्तम्यां स गच्छेत्परमं पदम् । इत्युक्त्वा भगवान्देवो जगामाशु गृहं रविः ॥

always on both seventh lunar days goes to the highest state. Having spoken thus, the Blessed god Ravi quickly went to his home.
