Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 150 — Āditya Worship Procedure

BP 1.150.1

वासुदेव उवाच । अतः परं प्रवक्ष्यामि यथा पूज्यो दिवाकरः । स्थण्डिले यदुशार्दूल निबोधैकाग्रमानसः ॥

Vāsudeva said: I shall explain further how the Day-maker is to be worshipped on the altar, O tiger among the Yadus. Listen with a single-pointed mind.

BP 1.150.2

मण्डलैरष्टभिः कार्यं चक्रं कालात्मकं शुभम् । मध्ये पद्माकृतं चक्रमरैर्द्वादशभिर्युतम् ॥

One should make an auspicious wheel, the nature of time, with eight circles; in the middle, a lotus-shaped wheel joined with twelve spokes.

In its middle is said to be a lotus endowed with eight petals. The all-souled, complete god, the sky-flame, radiant with rays,

BP 1.150.5

अरुणं पूजयेत्प्राज्ञः सदा देवाग्रजं शुभम् । दक्षिणे पूजयेद्देवीं निक्षुभां भास्करस्य तु ॥

The wise one should always worship auspicious Aruṇa, the elder brother of the god. On the right, one should worship Bhāskara’s goddess Nikṣubhā.

BP 1.150.6

रेवतं दक्षिणे पार्श्वे उत्तरे पिङ्गलं सदा । संज्ञां च यदुशार्दूल श्रेयसे सततं बुधः ॥

Revata on the right side, and Pingala always in the north; and Saṃjñā, O tiger among the Yadus, the wise one should always worship for welfare.

BP 1.150.7

आग्नेय्यां लेखकं वीर नैर्ऋत्यामश्विनौ तथा । वायव्यां पूजयेद्देवं मनुं वैवस्वतं विभुम् ॥

One should worship the Scribe in the southeast, O hero, and the Aśvins in the southwest. In the northwest one should worship the mighty Manu Vaivasvata.

ऐशान्यां पूजयेद्देवीं यमुनां लोकपावनीम् । द्वितीयावरणे वीर पूर्वतः पूजयेद्वियत् ॥

In the northeast one should worship the goddess Yamunā, purifier of the world. In the second enclosure, O hero, one should worship the sky in the east.

आग्नेय्यां पूजयेद्धेलिं प्रहेलिं नैर्ऋते तथा । वायव्यामुर्वशीं देवीमीशाने विनतां तथा ॥
