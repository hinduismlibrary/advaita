Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 88 — The Vijayā Observance

BP 1.88.1

ब्रह्मोवाच । शुक्लपक्षस्य सप्तम्यां प्राजापत्यर्क्षसंयुतः । स ज्ञेयो विजयो नाम सर्वपापभयापहः ॥ ॥

Brahmā said: The seventh day of the bright fortnight, joined with the asterism of Prajāpati, is known as Vijaya, the remover of all sin and fear.

Word-for-word
brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said;
śukla-pakṣasya — of the bright fortnight;
saptamyām — on the seventh day;
prājāpati-ṛkṣa-saṃyutaḥ — joined with the asterism of Prajāpati;
saḥ — that;
jñeyaḥ — is to be known;
vijayaḥ — Vijaya;
nāma — by name;
sarva-pāpa-bhaya-apahaḥ — remover of all sin and fear.
Transliteration
brahmovāca | śuklapakṣasya saptamyāṃ prājāpatyarkṣasaṃyutaḥ | sa jñeyo vijayo nāma sarvapāpabhayāpahaḥ || 1 ||

BP 1.88.2

तत्र कोटिगुणं सर्वफलं पुण्यस्य कर्मणः । ददाति भगवान्देवः पूजितश्चन्दनाधिपः ॥ ॥

There the blessed Lord, worshiped as the Lord of sandalwood, grants the entire fruit of meritorious action multiplied ten million-fold.

Word-for-word
tatra — there;
koṭi-guṇam — multiplied ten million-fold;
sarva-phalam — the entire fruit;
puṇyasya — of meritorious;
karmaṇaḥ — action;
dadāti — gives;
bhagavān — the blessed Lord;
devaḥ — the deity;
pūjitaḥ — worshiped;
candana-adhipaḥ — Lord of sandalwood.
Transliteration
tatra koṭiguṇaṃ sarvaphalaṃ puṇyasya karmaṇaḥ | dadāti bhagavāndevaḥ pūjitaścandanādhipaḥ || 2 ||

BP 1.88.3

स्नानं दानं जपो होमः पितृदेवादिपूजनम् । नक्तं चाप्युपवासस्तु सर्वमत्र दिवाकरः ॥ ॥

Bathing, giving, recitation, fire offerings, worship of ancestors and deities, the night observance, and fasting—all these, here, are for Divākara.

Word-for-word
snānam — bathing;
dānam — giving;
japaḥ — recitation;
homaḥ — fire offering;
pitṛ-deva-ādi-pūjanam — worship of ancestors, deities, and others;
naktam — the night observance;
ca api — and also;
upavāsaḥ — fasting;
tu — indeed;
sarvam — all;
atra — here;
divākara — O Divākara, Sun.
Transliteration
snānaṃ dānaṃ japo homaḥ pitṛdevādipūjanam | naktaṃ cāpyupavāsastu sarvamatra divākaraḥ || 3 ||

BP 1.88.4

कुर्यात्कोटिगुणं सर्वं पूजितो ह्यत्र गोपतिः । तस्मादत्र सदा देवं पूजयेद्भक्तिमान्नरः ॥ ॥

When the Lord of cows is worshiped here, all these acts yield ten million times their fruit. Therefore a devoted person should always worship the deity here.

Word-for-word
kuryāt — should make, should perform;
koṭi-guṇam — ten million-fold;
sarvam — all;
pūjitaḥ — worshiped;
hi — indeed;
atra — here;
gopatiḥ — the Lord of cows;
tasmāt — therefore;
atra — here;
sadā — always;
devam — the deity;
pūjayet — should worship;
bhaktimān — devoted;
naraḥ — person.
Transliteration
kuryātkoṭiguṇaṃ sarvaṃ pūjito hyatra gopatiḥ | tasmādatra sadā devaṃ pūjayedbhaktimānnaraḥ || 4 ||

BP 1.88.5

सर्वेशं सप्तद्वीपेशं सप्तसैन्धववाहनम् । सप्तम्यां तु समाराध्य सप्तप्रकृतिसम्भवम् ॥ ॥

Having worshiped on the seventh day the Lord of all, the lord of the seven continents, whose vehicle is the seven Sindhu rivers, and who arises from the seven primordial principles,

Word-for-word
sarva-īśam — Lord of all;
sapta-dvīpa-īśam — lord of the seven continents;
sapta-saindhava-vāhanam — whose vehicle is the seven Sindhu rivers;
saptamyām — on the seventh day;
tu — indeed;
samārādhya — having worshiped fully;
sapta-prakṛti-sambhavam — arising from the seven primordial principles.
Transliteration
sarveśaṃ saptadvīpeśaṃ saptasaindhavavāhanam | saptamyāṃ tu samārādhya saptaprakṛtisambhavam || 5 ||

BP 1.88.6

सप्तलोकाधिपत्यं तु प्राप्नुते सप्तरश्मिभिः ॥ ॥

he attains sovereignty over the seven worlds through the seven-rayed Lord.

Word-for-word
sapta-loka-adhipatyam — sovereignty over the seven worlds;
tu — indeed;
prāpnute — he attains;
sapta-raśmibhiḥ — through the seven rays, the seven-rayed Lord.
Transliteration
saptalokādhipatyaṃ tu prāpnute saptaraśmibhiḥ || 6 ||
