Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 88 — The Victory Observance

BP 1.88.1

ब्रह्मोवाच । शुक्लपक्षस्य सप्तम्यां प्राजापत्यर्क्षसंयुतः । स ज्ञेयो विजयो नाम सर्वपापभयापहः ॥ ॥

Brahmā said. The seventh day of the bright fortnight joined with the constellation of Prajāpati is known as Victory, removing all sin and fear.

BP 1.88.2

तत्र कोटिगुणं सर्वफलं पुण्यस्य कर्मणः । ददाति भगवान्देवः पूजितश्चन्दनाधिपः ॥ ॥

Worshipped as the Lord of Sandalwood, the divine Lord grants the entire fruit of meritorious acts ten million times over.

BP 1.88.3

स्नानं दानं जपो होमः पितृदेवादिपूजनम् । नक्तं चाप्युपवासस्तु सर्वमत्र दिवाकरः ॥ ॥

Bathing, giving, recitation, fire offering, worship of ancestors and gods, a night observance, and fasting, all this here belongs to the Sun.

BP 1.88.4

कुर्यात्कोटिगुणं सर्वं पूजितो ह्यत्र गोपतिः । तस्मादत्र सदा देवं पूजयेद्भक्तिमान्नरः ॥ ॥

When the Lord of Cows is worshipped here, he makes everything ten millionfold. Therefore a devoted person should always worship the god here.

BP 1.88.5

सर्वेशं सप्तद्वीपेशं सप्तसैन्धववाहनम् । सप्तम्यां तु समाराध्य सप्तप्रकृतिसम्भवम् ॥ ॥

Having worshipped on the seventh day the Lord of all, lord of the seven continents, whose vehicle is the seven rivers of the Sindhu, and who is born from the seven basic elements.

BP 1.88.6

सप्तलोकाधिपत्यं तु प्राप्नुते सप्तरश्मिभिः ॥ ॥

He attains sovereignty over the seven worlds through the seven rays.
