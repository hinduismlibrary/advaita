Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 94 — The Greatness of Sacred Hearing

BP 1.94.1

ब्रह्मोवाच । अत्राख्यानमुशन्तीह संवादं गणपुङ्गव । पितामहकुमाराभ्यां पुण्यं पापहरं शिवम् ॥ ॥

Brahmā said. Here they desire an account, O chief of the hosts, the meritorious dialogue between the Grandfather and Kumāra, which is auspicious and removes sin.

atra — here; ākhyānam — account; uśanti — they declare; saṃvādam — dialogue; pitāmaha-kumārābhyām — between Pitāmaha and Kumāra; puṇyam — holy; pāpa-haram — removing sin; śivam — auspicious.

BP 1.94.2

स्रष्टारं सर्वलोकानां सुखासीनं पितामहम् । प्रणम्य शिरसा देवं श्रद्धाभक्तिसमन्वितः ॥ ॥

Endowed with faith and devotion, he bowed his head to the deity, the Grandfather, creator of all worlds, seated comfortably.

sraṣṭāram — creator; sarva-lokānām — of all worlds; sukhāsīnam — seated comfortably; pitāmaham — Pitāmaha; praṇamya śirasā — having bowed with the head; śraddhā-bhakti-samanvitaḥ — endowed with faith and devotion.

BP 1.94.3

कुमारो देवशार्दूल इदं वचनमब्रवीत् । गतोऽहमद्य भगवन्द्रष्टुं देवं दिवाकरम् ॥ ॥

Kumāra spoke these words, O tiger among the gods. Today I went to see the god Sun, O Blessed One.

kumāraḥ — Kumāra; deva-śārdūla — O tiger among the gods; idam vacanam — these words; abravīt — spoke; gataḥ aham — I went; adya — today; bhagavan — O Blessed One; draṣṭum — to see; devam — the deity; divākaram — Divākara.

BP 1.94.4

कृत्वा प्रदक्षिणं देवः स मया पूजितो रविः । प्रणम्य शिरसा भक्त्या परया श्रद्धया विभो ॥ ॥

Having circumambulated the deity, I worshipped the Sun. With supreme devotion and faith, I bowed my head, O Lord.

kṛtvā pradakṣiṇam — having circumambulated; devaḥ — the deity; saḥ — that; mayā — by me; pūjitaḥ — worshiped; raviḥ — Ravi; praṇamya śirasā — having bowed with the head; parayā bhaktyā — with supreme devotion; śraddhayā — with faith; vibho — O Lord.

BP 1.94.5

अनुज्ञातस्ततस्तेन सुखासीनो ह्यहं स्थितः । आसीनेन मया तत्र दृष्टमाश्चर्यमद्भुतम् ॥ ॥

Then, having been permitted by him, I sat comfortably. As I sat there, I saw a wondrous marvel.

anujñātaḥ — permitted; tataḥ — then; tena — by him; sukhāsīnaḥ — seated comfortably; aham — I; sthitaḥ — remained; āsīnena mayā — by me while seated; tatra — there; dṛṣṭam — seen; āścaryam adbhutam — wondrous and astonishing.

BP 1.94.6

काञ्चनेन विमानेन किङ्किणीजालमालिना । मणिमुक्ताविचित्रेण वैदूर्यवरवेदिना ॥ ॥

In a golden aerial car adorned with a net of bells, varied with gems and pearls, and inlaid with excellent beryl.

kāñcanena vimānena — in a golden aerial car; kiṅkiṇī-jāla-mālinā — adorned with a network of bells; maṇi-muktā-vicitreṇa — adorned with jewels and pearls; vaidūrya-vara-vedinā — furnished with excellent vaidūrya gems.

BP 1.94.7

आगतं पुरुषं तत्र दृष्ट्वा देवो दिवाकरः । ससम्भ्रमं समुत्थाय आसनादेव सत्तम ॥ ॥

Seeing the man arrive there, the Sun rose in haste from his seat, O excellent one.

āgatam puruṣam — the man who had arrived; tatra — there; dṛṣṭvā — seeing; devaḥ — the deity; divākaraḥ — Divākara; sa-sambhramam — with haste; samutthāya — rising; āsanāt — from the seat; sattama — O excellent one.

BP 1.94.8

गृहीत्वा दक्षिणे पाणौ पुरतः प्राप्य तं नरम् । शिरस्याघ्राय देवेश पूजयामास वै रविः ॥ ॥

Taking him by the right hand and bringing the man before him, the Sun honored him, smelling his head, O Lord of gods.

gṛhītvā — taking; dakṣiṇe pāṇau — by the right hand; purataḥ prāpya — bringing before; tam naram — that man; śirasi āghrāya — smelling the head; deveśaḥ — Lord of gods; pūjayāmāsa — honored; raviḥ — Ravi.

BP 1.94.9

उपविष्टं तु तं भानुरिदं वचनमब्रवीत् । सुस्वागतं भद्र सुखकृता प्रीतास्त्वया वयम् ॥ ॥

When the man sat, the Sun said these words. Welcome, fortunate one. We are pleased by your good act.

upaviṣṭam — seated; tam — him; bhānuḥ — Bhānu; idam vacanam — these words; abravīt — said; su-svāgatam — welcome; bhadra — O blessed one; sukha-kṛtā — by the maker of happiness; prītāḥ — pleased; tvayā — by you; vayam — we.

BP 1.94.10

समीपे मम तिष्ठ त्वं यावदाभूतसंप्लवम् । पुनर्यास्यसि तत्स्थानं यत्र ब्रह्मा स्वयं स्थितः ॥ ॥

Stay near me until the dissolution of beings. Again you will go to that place where Brahmā himself is established.

samīpe mama — near me; tiṣṭha — remain; tvam — you; yāvat — until; ābhūta-saṃplavam — the dissolution of beings; punaḥ yāsyasi — you will go again; tat sthānam — that place; yatra — where; brahmā — Brahmā; svayam — himself; sthitaḥ — abides.

BP 1.94.11

एतस्मिन्नन्तरे चान्यो विमानवरमास्थितः । आगतः पुरुषो देवो यत्र तिष्ठति भास्करः ॥ ॥

Meanwhile another man arrived in an excellent aerial car at the place where the Sun stands.

etasmin antare — meanwhile; ca anyaḥ — and another; vimāna-varam āsthitaḥ — mounted on an excellent aerial car; āgataḥ — arrived; puruṣaḥ — man; yatra — where; tiṣṭhati — abides; bhāskaraḥ — Bhāskara.

BP 1.94.12

स चाप्येवं नरो देव पूजितो भानुना तदा । सामपूर्वं तथोक्तश्च प्रश्रयावनतः स्थितः ॥ ॥

That man too was then honored by the Sun, addressed first with a Vedic chant, and stood bowing with humility.

saḥ ca api — and he too; evam — thus; naraḥ — man; deva — O deity; pūjitaḥ — worshiped; bhānunā — by Bhānu; tadā — then; sāma-pūrvam — preceded by a Sāman chant; tathā uktaḥ — addressed thus; praśraya-avanataḥ — bowed with humility; sthitaḥ — stood.

BP 1.94.13

तत्र मे कौतुकं जातं दृष्ट्वा पूजां कृतां तयोः । भानुना देवशार्दूल पृष्टो भानुर्मया ततः ॥ ॥

Seeing the worship performed for the two of them, curiosity arose in me. Then I asked the Sun, O tiger among the gods.

tatra — there; me — to me; kautukam — curiosity; jātam — arose; dṛṣṭvā — seeing; pūjām — worship; kṛtām — performed; tayoḥ — of the two; pṛṣṭaḥ — asked; bhānuḥ — Bhānu; mayā — by me; tataḥ — then.

BP 1.94.14

किमनेन कृतं देव योऽयं पूर्वमिहागतः । नरस्तत्र सकाशं वै यस्य तुष्टो भवादृशाम् ॥ ॥

O god, what deed did this man who came here first perform, that you were pleased with him.

kim anena kṛtam — what was done by this one; deva — O deity; yaḥ ayam — this one who; pūrvam — earlier; iha āgataḥ — came here; naraḥ — man; tatra sakāśam — to that place; yasya — with whom; tuṣṭaḥ — pleased; bhavān — you; adṛśām — such as you.

BP 1.94.15

यदस्य भवता पूजा कृता हि स्वयमेव तु । अत्र मे कौतुकं जातं विस्मयश्च विशेषतः ॥ ॥

You yourself performed worship for him. Curiosity arose in me here, and especially wonder.

yat — because; asya — for him; bhavatā — by you; pūjā — worship; kṛtā — performed; hi — indeed; svayam eva — yourself; atra — here; me — my; kautukam — curiosity; jātam — arose; vismayaḥ — wonder; ca — and; viśeṣataḥ — especially.

BP 1.94.16

तथैवास्य कृता पूजा द्वितीयस्य नरस्य च । सर्वथा पुण्यकर्माणाव् इमौ नरवरोत्तमौ ॥ ॥

The second man too received worship in the same way. These two foremost men are surely doers of meritorious deeds in every way.

tathā eva — likewise; asya — for him; kṛtā pūjā — worship was performed; dvitīyasya narasya — of the second man; ca — and; sarvathā — in every way; puṇya-karmāṇau — performers of meritorious deeds; imau — these two; naravarottamau — excellent among men.

BP 1.94.17

ब्रह्मविष्णुशिवाद्यैस्तु पूज्यते भगवान् सदा । यस्त्वमाभ्यां परं पूजां कृतवान्देवसत्तम ॥ ॥

The Blessed One is always worshipped by Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Śiva, and the others. Yet you performed greater worship for these two, O best of gods.

brahma-viṣṇu-śiva-ādyaiḥ — by Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Śiva, and others; pūjyate — is worshiped; bhagavān — the Blessed One; sadā — always; yaḥ — who; tvam — you; ābhyām — to these two; param pūjām — supreme worship; kṛtavān — performed; deva-sattama — O best of deities.

BP 1.94.18

कथ्यतां मम देवेश किमेतौ कर्म चक्रतुः । यस्येदृक्परमं पुण्यं फलं दिव्यमवापतुः । श्रुत्वा तद्वचनं देव इदं वचनमब्रवीत् ॥ ॥

Tell me, Lord of gods, what acts did these two perform, by which they obtained such supreme, divine fruit of merit. Hearing his words, the god spoke these words.

kathyatām — let it be told; mama — to me; deveśa — O Lord of gods; kim — what; etau — these two; karma cakratuḥ — performed as acts; yasya — of which; īdṛśam — such; paramam — supreme; puṇyam — merit; phalam — fruit; divyam — divine; avāpatuḥ — they obtained; śrutvā — hearing; tat vacanam — those words; devaḥ — the deity; abravīt — spoke.

BP 1.94.19

सूर्य उवाच । साधु पृष्टोऽस्मि भवता कर्मणो निर्णयं परम् ॥ ॥

The Sun said. You have rightly asked me about the supreme result of the deed.

sūryaḥ uvāca — Sūrya said; sādhu — well; pṛṣṭaḥ asmi — I have been asked; bhavatā — by you; karmaṇaḥ — of the act; nirṇayam param — the supreme determination.

BP 1.94.20

यदनेन कृतं कर्म नरेण मुनिसत्तम । योऽसौ सूर्यमिहायातस्तच्छृणुष्व महामते ॥ ॥

Hear, O best of sages, the deed performed by that man, the one who came here to the Sun, O great minded one.

yat — what; anena — by this; kṛtam karma — act was performed; nareṇa — by the man; muni-sattama — O best of sages; yaḥ asau — that one who; sūryam iha āyātaḥ — came here to Sūrya; tat śṛṇuṣva — hear that; mahāmate — O great-minded one.

BP 1.94.21

येयं मदंशसम्भूतैः पार्थिवैः पालिता सदा । अयोध्या नाम नगरी प्रख्याता पृथिवीतले ॥ ॥

A city named Ayodhyā, famous on earth, is always protected by earthly kings born from my portion.

iyam — this; madaṃśa-sambhūtaiḥ — born from my portions; pārthivaiḥ — by earthly kings; pālitā — protected; sadā — always; ayodhyā nāma nagarī — the city named Ayodhyā; prakhyātā — famous; pṛthivī-tale — on earth.

BP 1.94.22

तत्रासौ वैश्यजातीयो धनपाल इति स्मृतः । तस्याः पुर्यां द्विजश्रेष्ठ दिव्यमायतनं व्यधात् ॥ ॥

There, O best of Brahmins, a man of Vaiśya birth named Dhanapāla built a divine sanctuary in that city.

tatra — there; asau — this one; vaiśya-jātīyaḥ — of Vaiśya birth; dhanapālaḥ — Dhanapāla; iti smṛtaḥ — remembered as; tasyāḥ puryām — in that city; dvija-śreṣṭha — O best of twice-born; divyam āyatanam — divine temple; vyadhāt — built.

BP 1.94.23

तस्मिन्नायतने दिव्ये ह्याम्नायार्थं तथाश्रितः । ब्राह्मणानां विशिष्टानां पूजयित्वा कदम्बकम् ॥ ॥

In that divine sanctuary, for the purpose of Vedic recitation, he worshipped an assembly of distinguished Brahmins.

tasmin āyatane — in that temple; divye — divine; āmnāya-artham — for the sake of Vedic tradition; tathā āśritaḥ — likewise supported; brāhmaṇānām — of Brāhmaṇas; viśiṣṭānām — distinguished; kadambakam — an assembly; pūjayitvā — having worshiped.

BP 1.94.24

इतिहासपुराणाभ्यां वाचकं च विशेषतः । पूजयित्वा द्विजश्रेष्ठं मुनिश्रेष्ठं महामुनिम् ॥ ॥

He especially honored the reciter of the histories and Purāṇas, a foremost Brahmin, foremost among sages, a great sage.

itihāsa-purāṇābhyām — of histories and Purāṇas; vācakam — reciter; ca — and; viśeṣataḥ — especially; pūjayitvā — having worshiped; dvija-śreṣṭham — best of Brāhmaṇas; muni-śreṣṭham — best of sages; mahā-munim — great sage.

BP 1.94.25

पुस्तकं चापि सम्पूज्य गन्धपुष्पोपहारतः । तस्य विप्रकदम्बस्य व्यासस्य च यथाग्रतः ॥ ॥

He also worshipped the book with offerings of perfumes and flowers, before that assembly of Brahmins and Vyāsa.

pustakam — book; ca api — also; sampūjya — having fully worshiped; gandha-puṣpa-upahārataḥ — with fragrances, flowers, and offerings; tasya vipra-kadambasya — of that assembly of Brāhmaṇas; vyāsasya ca — and of Vyāsa; yathā agrataḥ — before.

BP 1.94.26

प्रकल्प्योक्तो द्विजोऽनेन पाठको वाचकोत्तमः । एष तिष्ठति देवेशः सहस्रकिरणो रविः ॥ ॥

Having appointed this Brahmin as the reader, the foremost reciter, he said. The Sun with a thousand rays, Lord of the gods, stands here.

prakalpya — appointing; uktaḥ — said; dvijaḥ — Brāhmaṇa; anena — by him; pāṭhakaḥ — reader; vācaka-uttamaḥ — excellent reciter; eṣaḥ — this; tiṣṭhati — stands; deveśaḥ — Lord of gods; sahasra-kiraṇaḥ — thousand-rayed; raviḥ — Ravi.

BP 1.94.27

चातुर्वर्ण्यमिदं वापि श्रोतुकामं कदम्बकम् । तिष्ठ चेह द्विजश्रेष्ठ कुरु पुस्तकवाचनम् ॥ ॥

This assembly of four varṇas wishes to hear. Stay here, O best of Brahmins, and conduct the reading of the book.

cātur-varṇyam — the four classes; idam — this; vā api — indeed; śrotu-kāmam — wishing to hear; kadambakam — assembly; tiṣṭha — remain; ca iha — here; dvija-śreṣṭha — O best of twice-born; kuru — perform; pustaka-vācanam — book reading.

BP 1.94.28

येन मे वरदो भानुः सप्त जन्मानि वै भवेत् । यावत्संवत्सरं विप्र प्रगृह्य वृत्तिमुत्तमाम् ॥ ॥

May the Sun be a giver of boons to me for seven births. O Brahmin, accept an excellent livelihood for one year.

yena — by which; me — for me; varadaḥ — benefactor; bhānuḥ — Bhānu; sapta janmāni — for seven births; bhavet — may become; yāvat saṃvatsaram — for one year; vipra — O Brāhmaṇa; pragṛhya — accepting; vṛttim uttamām — excellent livelihood.

BP 1.94.29

स्वर्णनिष्कशतं विप्र ततो दास्ये तथापरम् । पूर्णे वर्षे द्विजश्रेष्ठ श्रेयोऽर्थम् अहमात्मनः ॥ ॥

At the end of the year, O best of Brahmins, I will give you a hundred gold coins and something more, for my own welfare.

svarṇa-niṣka-śatam — a hundred gold coins; vipra — O Brāhmaṇa; tataḥ — then; dāsye — I shall give; tathā aparam — likewise something further; pūrṇe varṣe — when the year is complete; dvija-śreṣṭha — O best of twice-born; śreyaḥ-artham — for the sake of welfare; aham-ātmanaḥ — of myself.

BP 1.94.30

एवं प्रवर्तिते तस्मिन् पुण्ये पुस्तकवाचने । षण्मासागतमात्रे तु काले सुरवरोत्तम । तथैवान्तरतश्चायं कालधर्ममुपेयिवान् ॥ ॥

When that meritorious book reading had thus begun, and only six months had passed, O best of gods, he too met death.

evam — thus; pravartite — having begun; tasmin — that; puṇye — holy; pustaka-vācane — book reading; ṣaṇ-māsa-āgate mātre — when only six months had come; kāle — in time; sura-vara-uttama — O best of gods; tathā eva — likewise; antaraḥ — within; ayam — this one; kāla-dharmam upeyivān — met death.

BP 1.94.31

मया चास्य विमानं तु प्रेषितं कुर्वतो व्रतम् । इत्येषा कर्मणस्तुष्टिः पुण्याख्यानकजार्चिता ॥ ॥

I sent an aerial car for him while he was observing the vow. This is the pleasure from the deed, worship arising from a meritorious narrative.

mayā — by me; asya — for him; vimānam — aerial car; preṣitam — sent; kurvataḥ — while performing; vratam — vow; iti — thus; eṣā — this; karmaṇaḥ tuṣṭiḥ — satisfaction from the act; puṇya-ākhyāna-ja — born from sacred narratives; arcitā — worshiped.

BP 1.94.32

गन्धपुष्पोपहारैस्तु न तथा जायते मम । प्रीतिर् देववरश्रेष्ठ पुराणश्रवणे यथा ॥ ॥

My pleasure does not arise so much from offerings of perfume, flowers, and the like, O best of gods, as from hearing the Purāṇas.

gandha-puṣpa-upahāraiḥ — by fragrances, flowers, and offerings; na tathā — not so; jāyate — arises; mama prītiḥ — my pleasure; devavara-śreṣṭha — O best of gods; purāṇa-śravaṇe — in hearing the Purāṇas; yathā — as.

BP 1.94.33

गोसुवर्णहिरण्यानां वस्त्राणां चापि कृत्स्नशः । ग्रामाणां नगराणां च दानं प्रीतिकरं मम ॥ ॥

The giving of cows, gold and money, all kinds of garments, villages, and cities pleases me.

go-suvarṇa-hiraṇyānām — of cows and gold; vastrāṇām — of garments; ca api — and also; kṛtsnaśaḥ — entirely, in full; grāmāṇām — of villages; nagarāṇām — of cities; dānam — giving; prīti-karam — pleasing; mama — to me.

BP 1.94.34

न तथा स्यात् सुरश्रेष्ठ यथा प्रीतिकरं गुह । इतिहासपुराणाभ्यां श्रवणं सुरसैन्यप ॥ ॥

That is not so pleasing, O best of gods, as hearing the histories and Purāṇas, O Guha, commander of the host of gods.

na tathā — not so; syāt — would be; sura-śreṣṭha — O best of gods; yathā — as; prīti-karam — pleasing; guha — O Guha; itihāsa-purāṇābhyām — of histories and Purāṇas; śravaṇam — hearing; sura-sainya-pa — O lord of the divine army.

BP 1.94.35

श्राद्धं कुर्वन्ति ये मह्यं भक्ष्यभोज्यैर् अनेकशः । न करोति तथा प्रीतिर् यथा पुस्तकवाचनम् ॥ ॥

Those who perform ancestral rites for me with many kinds of food do not give me as much pleasure as the reading of a book.

śrāddham — śrāddha; kurvanti — perform; ye — those who; mahyam — for me; bhakṣya-bhojyaiḥ — with foods to eat and enjoy; anekaśaḥ — many kinds; na karoti — does not make; tathā prītiḥ — such pleasure; yathā — as; pustaka-vācanam — book reading.

BP 1.94.36

कर्णश्राद्धे यथा प्रीतिर् मम स्यात् सुरसत्तम । न तथा जायते प्रीतिर् भोज्यश्राद्धे तथैव च ॥ ॥

The pleasure from an offering to the ear, O excellent god, does not arise in the same way from an offering of food.

karṇa-śrāddhe — in ear-heard śrāddha; yathā — as; prītiḥ — pleasure; mama — my; syāt — may be; sura-sattama — O excellent god; na tathā — not so; jāyate — arises; prītiḥ — pleasure; bhojya-śrāddhe — in food śrāddha; tathā eva — likewise.

BP 1.94.37

अथ किं बहुनोक्तेन नान्यत्प्रीतिकरं मम । पुण्याख्यानाद् ऋते देव गुह्यम् एतत् प्रकीर्तितम् ॥ ॥

What more need be said. Nothing pleases me except the meritorious narrative, O god. This secret has been declared.

atha kim — then why; bahunā uktena — with much being said; na anyat — nothing else; prīti-karam — pleasing; mama — to me; puṇya-ākhyānāt ṛte — apart from sacred narratives; deva — O deity; guhyam — secret; etat — this; prakīrtitam — proclaimed.

BP 1.94.38

यश्चायमपरो विप्र इहायातो नरोत्तमः । अयमासीद् द्विजश्रेष्ठस् तस्मिन्नेव पुरोत्तमे ॥ ॥

This other excellent man who has come here was the foremost Brahmin in that same excellent city.

yaḥ ca ayam — and this one; aparaḥ — other; vipraḥ — Brāhmaṇa; iha āyātaḥ — has come here; nara-uttamaḥ — excellent man; ayam āsīt — he was; dvija-śreṣṭhaḥ — foremost Brāhmaṇa; tasmin eva — in that very; pura-uttame — excellent city.

BP 1.94.39

एकदा तु गतश्चायं धर्मश्रवणमुत्तमम् । श्रोतुं भक्त्या द्विजश्रेष्ठ श्रद्धया परया वृतः ॥ ॥

Once he went to hear the supreme teaching of dharma, O best of Brahmins, filled with devotion and supreme faith.

ekadā — once; gataḥ — went; ayam — this one; dharma-śravaṇam uttamam — supreme hearing of dharma; śrotum — to hear; bhaktyā — with devotion; dvija-śreṣṭha — O best of twice-born; śraddhayā parayā — with supreme faith; vṛtaḥ — covered, endowed.

BP 1.94.40

श्रुत्वा तत्र ततो भक्त्या पुण्याख्यानम् अनुत्तमम् । कृत्वा प्रदक्षिणं तस्य वाचकस्य महात्मनः । एष विप्रोऽमरश्रेष्ठ दत्तवान् स्वर्णमाषकम् ॥ ॥

Having heard there the unsurpassed meritorious narrative with devotion, he circumambulated that great reciter and gave him a gold coin, O best of immortals.

śrutvā — having heard; tatra — there; puṇya-ākhyānam anuttamam — unsurpassed sacred narrative; kṛtvā pradakṣiṇam — having circumambulated; tasya vācakasya — that reciter; mahātmanaḥ — great; eṣaḥ vipraḥ — this Brāhmaṇa; dattavān — gave; svarṇa-māṣakam — a gold coin.

BP 1.94.41

दत्त्वा तु दक्षिणां तस्मै वाचकायामितौजसे । आनन्दमगमद् विप्रः प्राप्तवान् काञ्चनं यथा ॥ ॥

Having given a gift to that exceedingly radiant reciter, the Brahmin rejoiced as if he had obtained gold.

dattvā — having given; dakṣiṇām — gift; tasmai — to him; vācakāya — to the reciter; amita-aujase — of immeasurable splendor; ānandam agamat — went to joy; vipraḥ — Brāhmaṇa; prāptavān — obtained; kāñcanam — gold; yathā — as if.

BP 1.94.42

एतद्धि सफलं चास्य न चान्यत् कृतवान् अयम् । यदनेन कृता पूजा वाचकस्य महात्मनः । फलं हि कर्मणस् तस्य यन् मया पूजितः स्वयम् ॥ ॥

This alone bore fruit for him. He performed no other deed. His worship of the great reciter bore the fruit of the deed by which I myself worshipped him.

etat hi — this indeed; saphalam — fruitful; asya — for him; na ca anyat — and nothing else; kṛtavān — performed; ayam — he; yā pūjā — the worship; anena kṛtā — performed by him; vācakasya — of the reciter; phalam — fruit; karmaṇaḥ tasya — of that act; yat — which; mayā — by me; pūjitaḥ — worshiped; svayam — myself.

BP 1.94.43

वाचकं पूजयेद्यस्तु श्रद्धाभक्तिसमन्वितः । तेनाहं पूजितः स्यां वै को विष्णुः शङ्करस् तथा ॥ ॥

Whoever worships the reciter, endowed with faith and devotion, worships me by that. Viṣṇu and Śaṅkara, what are they in comparison.

vācakam — reciter; pūjayet — should worship; yaḥ — whoever; śraddhā-bhakti-samanvitaḥ — endowed with faith and devotion; tena — by him; aham — I; pūjitaḥ — worshiped; syām — become; vai — indeed; kaḥ — what; viṣṇuḥ — Viṣṇu; śaṅkaraḥ — Śaṅkara; tathā — likewise.

BP 1.94.44

वाचकं भोजयेद्यस्तु भक्त्या भोज्यैर् अनुत्तमैः । तेनाहं पूजितः स्यां वै दश वर्षाणि पञ्च च ॥ ॥

Whoever feeds the reciter devotedly with the finest foods thereby worships me for fifteen years.

vācakam — reciter; bhojayet — should feed; yaḥ — whoever; bhaktyā — with devotion; bhojyaiḥ anuttamaiḥ — with excellent foods; tena — by him; aham — I; pūjitaḥ — worshiped; syām — become; daśa varṣāṇi pañca — fifteen years.

BP 1.94.45

न यमो न यमी चापि न मन्दो न मनुस्तथा । तपती न तथान्विष्टा यथेष्टो वाचको मम ॥ ॥

Neither Yama, Yamī, Manda, Manu, nor Tapatī is as dear to me as my reciter.

na — not; yamaḥ — Yama; na yamī — nor Yamī; na mandaḥ — nor Manda; na manuḥ — nor Manu; na tapatī — nor Tapatī; na tathā — not so; anviṣṭā — any other; yathā iṣṭaḥ — as dear; vācakaḥ — reciter; mama — to me.

BP 1.94.46

वाचके सत्कृते देव भोजिते सुरसैन्यप । तृप्तिर् भवति मे देव संवत्सरशतद्वयम् ॥ ॥

When the reciter is honored and fed, O commander of the host of gods, I am satisfied for two hundred years.

vācake — when the reciter; satkṛte — honored; deva — O deity; bhojite — fed; sura-sainya-pa — O lord of the divine army; tṛptiḥ — satisfaction; bhavati — becomes; me — for me; saṃvatsara-śata-dvayam — two hundred years.

BP 1.94.47

न केवलं मम प्रीतिर् वाचके भोजिते भवेत् । कृत्स्नशो देवतानां च इन्द्रादीनां तथा भवेत् ॥ ॥

Not only is my pleasure produced when the reciter is fed. The pleasure of all the gods, including Indra, is produced likewise.

na kevalam — not only; mama prītiḥ — my pleasure; vācake bhojite — when the reciter is fed; bhavet — arises; kṛtsnaśaḥ — wholly; devatānām — of the deities; indrādīnām — of Indra and the others; tathā — likewise.

BP 1.94.48

ब्रह्मविष्णुशिवादीनां स चेष्ठो वाचको मम । प्रीते तस्मिन् देवताः स्युः सर्वाः प्रीता न संशयः ॥ ॥

That reciter is dear to Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Śiva, and the others, and to me. When he is pleased, all the gods are pleased, without doubt.

brahma-viṣṇu-śiva-ādīnām — of Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Śiva, and others; saḥ — he; iṣṭaḥ — dear; vācakaḥ — reciter; mama — my; prīte tasmin — when he is pleased; devatāḥ — deities; syuḥ — become; sarvāḥ prītāḥ — all pleased; na saṃśayaḥ — no doubt.

BP 1.94.49

इत्य् एतत् कथितं सर्वम् आभ्यां कर्म महाबल ॥ ॥

This is all the deed performed by these two, O mighty one.

iti — thus; etat sarvam — all this; kathitam — told; ābhyām — by these two; karma — action; mahā-bala — O mighty one.

BP 1.94.50

न चान्यच् चक्रतुः कर्म किम् अन्यच् छ्रोतुम् इच्छसि । एतद् दृष्ट्वाहम् आश्चर्यं तवाभ्याशम् इहागतः । किम् अत्र तथ्यं देवेश कथ्यतां कौतुकं मम ॥ ॥

They performed no other deed. What else do you wish to hear. Seeing this wonder, I came here near you. Tell me what is true here, Lord of gods. This is my curiosity.

na ca anyat — and nothing else; cakratuḥ — the two performed; karma — act; kim anyat — what else; śrotum icchasi — do you wish to hear; etat dṛṣṭvā — seeing this; aham — I; āścaryam — wonder; tava abhyāśam āgataḥ — came near you; kim atra tathyam — what is the truth here; kathyatām — let it be told; kautukam mama — my curiosity.

BP 1.94.51

श्रुत्वा कुमारवचनं सर्वलोकपितामह ॥ ॥

The Grandfather of all worlds heard the words of Kumāra.

śrutvā — having heard; kumāra-vacanam — Kumāra’s words; sarva-loka-pitāmaha — grandfather of all worlds.

BP 1.94.52

ब्रह्मोवाच । हन्त भोः साधु पुण्योऽसि नास्ति तुल्यस् त्वयापरः । यद् दृष्टौ भवता तौ हि सुपुण्यौ पुण्यकारिणौ ॥ ॥

Brahmā said. Well done. You are meritorious. No one else is equal to you, since you have seen those two, so meritorious and performing meritorious deeds.

hanta bhoḥ — well then; sādhu — excellent; puṇyaḥ asi — you are holy; na asti — there is not; tulyaḥ — equal; tvayā — to you; aparaḥ — another; yat — because; dṛṣṭau — seen; bhavatā — by you; tau — those two; su-puṇyau — highly meritorious; puṇya-kāriṇau — performers of merit.

BP 1.94.53

यद् उक्तं भानुना वत्स तत् तथा नान्यथा भवेत् । यदासीन् मे मुखं पुत्र प्रथमं लोकपूजितम् ॥ ॥

What the Sun said, dear child, is true and not otherwise. My face was first worshipped by the worlds, O son.

yat uktam — what was said; bhānunā — by Bhānu; vatsa — dear one; tat tathā — that is so; na anyathā — not otherwise; yadā āsīt — when it became; me mukham — my face; putra — O son; prathamam — first; loka-pūjitam — worshiped by the worlds.

BP 1.94.54

तस्माद् एतानि सर्वाणि निर्गतानि समन्ततः । इतिहासपुराणानि लोकानां हितकाम्यया ॥ ॥

Therefore all these histories and Purāṇas issued forth everywhere, for the welfare of the worlds.

tasmāt — therefore; etāni sarvāṇi — all these; nirgatāni — emerged; samantataḥ — everywhere; itihāsa-purāṇāni — histories and Purāṇas; lokānām — of the worlds; hita-kāmyayā — from desire for welfare.

BP 1.94.55

यथैतानि ममेष्टानि पुराणानि महामते । न तथा वै चतुर्वेदी न चाङ्गिनि महामते ॥ ॥

These Purāṇas are dear to me. No knower of the four Vedas, nor of their limbs, is as dear to me, O great minded one.

yathā — as; etāni — these; mama iṣṭāni — dear to me; purāṇāni — Purāṇas; mahāmate — O great-minded one; na tathā — not so; catur-vedī — knower of four Vedas; na ca aṅgini — nor the Vedic limbs; mahāmate — O great-minded one.

BP 1.94.56

शृण्वन्ति एतानि ये भक्त्या नित्यं श्रद्धासमन्विताः । दत्त्वा तु वाचके वृत्तिं ते गच्छन्ति परं पदम् ॥ ॥

Those who always hear these with devotion and faith, and give the reciter a livelihood, go to the highest state.

śṛṇvanti — hear; etāni — these; ye — those who; bhaktyā — with devotion; nityam — daily; śraddhā-samanvitāḥ — endowed with faith; dattvā — having given; vācake — to the reciter; vṛttim — livelihood; te gacchanti — they go; param padam — supreme state.

BP 1.94.57

धर्मार्थकाममोक्षाणां स्पष्टीकरणम् उत्तमम् । इतिहासपुराणानि मया सृष्टानि सुव्रत ॥ ॥

I created the histories and Purāṇas, O steadfast one, as the highest explanation of dharma, wealth, desire, and liberation.

dharma-artha-kāma-mokṣāṇām — of dharma, wealth, desire, and liberation; spaṣṭīkaraṇam uttamam — supreme clarification; itihāsa-purāṇāni — histories and Purāṇas; mayā — by me; sṛṣṭāni — created; suvrata — O well-disciplined one.

BP 1.94.58

चत्वारो य इमे वेदा गूढार्थाः सततं स्मृताः । अतस्त्वेतानि सृष्टानि बोधायैषां महामते ॥ ॥

These four Vedas are always considered to have hidden meanings. Therefore these were created for their understanding, O great minded one.

catvāraḥ — four; ime vedāḥ — these Vedas; gūḍha-arthāḥ — having hidden meanings; satatam — always; smṛtāḥ — remembered; ataḥ — therefore; etāni — these; sṛṣṭāni — created; bodhāya — for understanding; eṣām — of these; mahāmate — O great-minded one.

BP 1.94.59

यस्तु कारयते नित्यं धर्मश्रवणमुत्तमम् । आदित्याद् भास्करं प्राप्य याति तत्परमं पदम् ॥ ॥

Whoever continually causes the supreme hearing of dharma, reaches Bhāskara from Āditya and goes to that highest state.

yaḥ tu — whoever; kārayate — causes to be performed; nityam — continually; dharma-śravaṇam uttamam — supreme hearing of dharma; ādityāt — from Āditya; bhāskaram prāpya — attaining Bhāskara; yāti — goes; tat paramam padam — to that highest state.

BP 1.94.60

दत्त्वा तु दक्षिणां तत्र आदित्यस्य पुरं व्रजेत् । किमाश्चर्यं सुरश्रेष्ठ दानपात्रं हि तत्परम् ॥ ॥

Having given the fee there, one goes to the city of Āditya. What wonder is this, O best of gods. For he is the supreme recipient of gifts.

dattvā — having given; dakṣiṇām — gift; tatra — there; ādityasya puram — Āditya’s city; vrajet — goes; kim āścaryam — what wonder; sura-śreṣṭha — O best of gods; dāna-pātram — recipient of gifts; hi — indeed; tat param — that supreme.

BP 1.94.61

यथा देववरो लेखो यथा हेतिः परं पविः । ब्राह्मणानां तथा श्रेष्ठो वाचको नात्र संशयः ॥ ॥

As Lekha is foremost among the gods, and the thunderbolt is supreme among weapons, so the reciter is foremost among Brahmins, without doubt.

yathā — as; deva-varaḥ — best of gods; lekhaḥ — Lekha; yathā — as; hetiḥ — weapon; param paviḥ — supreme sharp thing; brāhmaṇānām — among Brāhmaṇas; tathā — so; śreṣṭhaḥ — best; vācakaḥ — reciter; na atra saṃśayaḥ — no doubt here.

BP 1.94.62

हेतिर् यथा तेजसां तु सरसां सागरो यथा । तथा सर्वद्विजेभ्यस् तु वाचकः प्रवरः स्मृतः ॥ ॥

As a weapon is foremost among shining things, and the ocean among lakes, so the reciter is remembered as foremost among all Brahmins.

hetiḥ — weapon; yathā — as; tejasām — among radiant things; tu — indeed; sarasām — among lakes; sāgaraḥ — ocean; yathā — as; tathā — so; sarva-dvijebhyaḥ — among all twice-born; vācakaḥ — reciter; pravaraḥ — foremost; smṛtaḥ — remembered.

BP 1.94.63

वाचकं पूजयेद्यस्तु नरो भक्तिपुरःसरम् । पूजितं सकलं तेन जगत्यान् नात्र संशयः ॥ ॥

Whoever worships the reciter with devotion foremost thereby worships the whole world, without doubt.

vācakam — reciter; pūjayet — should worship; yaḥ — whoever; naraḥ — person; bhakti-puraḥsaram — with devotion foremost; pūjitam — worshiped; sakalam — all; tena — by him; jagati — in the world; na atra saṃśayaḥ — no doubt here.

BP 1.94.64

सत्यमुक्तं न सन्देहो भानुना मत्कुलोद्वह । वाचकेन समं पात्रं न जात्वन्यद् भवेत् क्वचित् ॥ ॥

The Sun spoke the truth, without doubt, O scion of my lineage. There is never anywhere another recipient equal to the reciter.

satyam uktam — truth has been spoken; na sandehaḥ — no doubt; bhānunā — by Bhānu; mat-kula-udvaha — O joy of my lineage; vācakena samam — equal to the reciter; pātram — recipient; na jātu — never; anyat — another; bhavet — has been; kvacit — anywhere.

BP 1.94.65

तच् छ्रुत्वा ब्रह्मणो वाक्यं कुमारो वाक्यमब्रवीत् ॥ ॥

Having heard the words of Brahmā, Kumāra spoke these words.

tat śrutvā — having heard that; brahmaṇaḥ vākyam — Brahmā’s words; kumāraḥ — Kumāra; vākyam abravīt — spoke words.

BP 1.94.66

अहो हि धन्यता तस्य पुण्यश्रवणकारिणः । दानं च ददतोऽत्यर्थं पुण्यता वाचकाय वै ॥ ॥

Ah, how great is the blessedness of the one who causes meritorious hearing, and the merit of the one who gives abundantly to the reciter.

aho — oh; hi — indeed; dhanyatā — blessedness; tasya — of him; puṇya-śravaṇa-kāriṇaḥ — who causes sacred hearing; dānam — gift; ca — and; dadataḥ — of the giver; atyartham — abundantly; puṇyatā — merit; vācakāya — for the reciter; vai — indeed.

BP 1.94.67

ब्रह्मोवाच । इत्थं दिण्डे सदा यस्तु देवदेवस्य मन्दिरे । कुर्यात् तु धर्मश्रवणं स याति परमां गतिम् ॥ ॥

Brahmā said. Whoever thus always causes the hearing of dharma in the temple of the God of gods at Diṇḍi goes to the highest state.

ittham — thus; diṇḍe — at Diṇḍi; sadā — always; yaḥ tu — whoever; deva-devasya mandire — in the temple of the Lord of gods; kuryāt — should perform; dharma-śravaṇam — hearing of dharma; saḥ yāti — he goes; paramām gatim — to the highest destination.
