Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 138 — Raising the Banner

BP 1.138.1

हन्त ते कथयिष्यामि ध्वजारोपणमुत्तमम् । यदुक्तं ब्रह्मणा पूर्वमृषभाधिपते पुरः ॥

Nārada said: “Now I shall tell you the excellent raising of the banner, formerly spoken by Brahmā before you, O lord of bulls.”

BP 1.138.2

पुरा देवासुरे युद्धे यानि देवैर्जयेप्सुभिः । कृतान्युपरि चिह्नानि वाहनानि ध्वजानि तु ॥

Formerly, in the battle between the gods and Asuras, the gods desiring victory made the signs, vehicles, and banners above.

BP 1.138.3

लक्ष्मचिह्नध्वजं केतुरिति पर्यायनामभिः । कीर्तितः स च तस्येह प्रमाणं गदतो शृणु ॥

It is known by the synonymous names mark, emblem, banner, and standard. Hear me state its measure here.

BP 1.138.4

ध्वजो वंशस्य कर्तव्यस्त्वविद्ध ऋजुरव्रणः । प्रासादव्यास तुल्यस्य ध्वजवंशप्रमाणतः ॥

The banner pole should be unpierced, straight, and without a blemish. Its measure should equal the temple’s breadth.

BP 1.138.5

देवागारस्य ये प्रोक्ता मञ्जरीकलशादयः । अथ वा तत्प्रमाणस्तु ध्वजदण्डः प्रकीर्तितः ॥

The temple’s finials, pitchers, and the like are prescribed; or the flagstaff is declared to have their measure.

BP 1.138.6

अन्तर्गृहस्य या वेदी सूत्रतः परिकल्पिता । तस्या व्यासो भवेद्वंशः प्रसादस्य यदूत्तम ॥

The pole’s measure should be the width of the inner shrine’s altar, laid out by cord, O best of the Yadus.

BP 1.138.7

अथ वा मूलसूत्रेण यो व्यासोऽन्तर्गृहस्य तु । प्रासादव्यास इति ते प्रोक्तश्चेह न संशयः ॥

Or the width of the inner shrine measured by the base cord is here called the temple’s breadth; of this there is no doubt.

BP 1.138.8

केतुर्भवेद्वरो वंशो न निम्नो न ऋजुस्तथा । पत्रं ध्वजे युगं चैव नलिकापुरुषस्तथा ॥

The standard’s pole should be excellent, neither low nor crooked. The banner also has a leaf, a yoke, a tube, and a figure.

BP 1.138.9

चतुर्हस्ता भवन्त्येते प्रशस्ताः कृष्णनन्दन । अष्टहस्तप्रमाणस्तु विंशार्धस्य प्रमाणतः ॥

These are praised as four cubits, O delight of Kṛṣṇa; the measure is eight cubits, according to half of twenty.

BP 1.138.10

सामान्यो ध्वजदण्डस्तु सर्वसाधारणो मतः । दण्डपाणिध्वजो यस्तु स्मृतः षोडशहस्तवान् ॥

The ordinary flagstaff is considered common to all. The banner of Staff-in-Hand is remembered as sixteen cubits.

BP 1.138.11

विंशद्धस्तात्परो दण्डो न कार्यः सर्वथा रवेः । युग्महस्तस्तु कर्तव्यो ध्वजदण्डो मनीषिभिः ॥

For Ravi, a staff longer than twenty cubits should never be made. The wise should make the flagstaff an even number of cubits.

BP 1.138.12

चतुरङ्गुलविस्तीर्णः सुवृत्तो द्व्यङ्गुलोपरि । नातिसूक्ष्मो न च स्थूलो न कार्यो नतपर्वकः ॥

It should be four fingers wide, well-rounded, and measure two fingers at the top; it should be neither too fine nor too thick, and should have no bent joints.

BP 1.138.13

समपर्वातु कर्तव्यः सुदृढः सूक्ष्म एव हि । वक्रः पुत्रविनाशाय सव्रणोऽर्थविनाशनः ॥

It should be made with even joints, very firm and fine. A crooked one brings the loss of a son; one with a blemish brings the loss of wealth.

BP 1.138.14

रोगदो युग्महस्तस्तु भिन्नो दुःखमनन्तकम् । करोति हानि धर्मस्य हीनो यस्तु प्रमाणतः ॥

A two-cubit staff brings illness; a broken one causes endless grief. One deficient in measure brings loss of dharma.

BP 1.138.15

वैषम्यमसमपर्वा दद्यात्कृच्छ्रमधोन्नतः ।

Unevenness and unequal joints bring distress; a staff raised too high or too low brings hardship.

BP 1.138.16

जयो जयन्तो जैत्रेयः शत्रुहन्ता जयावहः । नन्दोपनन्दनौ चैवेन्द्रोपेन्द्रौ गदितौ तथा । दशैते कीर्तिता भेदा ध्वजस्यानन्दसम्मिताः ॥

Victory, Victor, Triumphant, Enemy-Slayer, Victory-Bringer, Delight, Greater Delight, Indra, and Upendra are named. These ten kinds of banner are declared, sized according to the Ānanda measure.

BP 1.138.17

द्विजहस्तस्तु जयो दण्डो जयन्तो द्विगुणो मतः । द्वादशहस्तस्तु जैत्रेयः शत्रुहन्ता कलान्वितः ॥

Victory’s staff is two cubits; Victor is considered twice that. Triumphant is twelve cubits; Enemy-Slayer is furnished with its divisions.

BP 1.138.18

जयावहस्तु विंशार्धो नन्द आदित्यसन्निभः । चतुर्दशोपनन्दस्तु इन्द्रः षोडश उच्यते ॥

Victory-Bringer is half of twenty; Delight is like the Sun. Greater Delight is fourteen; Indra is said to be sixteen.

BP 1.138.19

उपेन्द्रोऽष्टादशः प्रोक्तस्तथेन्द्रो विंशतिः स्मृतः । भिन्नो वक्रोऽसाधितश्च न कार्यो दण्ड एव हि ॥

Upendra is said to be eighteen, and Indra is remembered as twenty. A broken, crooked, or misshapen staff should not be made.

BP 1.138.20

मूलमन्त्रेण कर्तव्यो व्यासतोऽन्तर्गृहस्य तु । ध्वजदण्डो महाबाहो अथ वा वास्तुमानतः ॥

The flagstaff should be made by the base measure and according to the width of the inner shrine, O mighty-armed one, or according to the measure of the building site.

BP 1.138.21

मञ्जरीमानतो वापि तदर्धेनाथवा विभो । पताका वै शुभा कार्या ध्वजवंशावलम्बिनी ॥

Or by the measure of the finial, or by half of it, O lord. A fine banner, hanging from the flagstaff, should be made.

BP 1.138.22

देवागारस्य शिखरात्त्रिभागपरिमार्जनी । सा प्रोक्ता दशधा वीर मानतोमानतस्तथा ॥

Its measure is one third of the temple’s summit. O hero, it is said to have ten forms according to the main and lesser measures.

BP 1.138.23

अङ्कुरः पल्लवश्चैव स्कन्धः शाखा तथैव च । पताका कदली वीर केतुर्लक्ष्म जयस्तथा ॥

Bud, leaf, stem, branch, banner, plantain, standard, mark, and victory, O hero—

BP 1.138.24

ध्वजश्च दशमः प्रोक्तः सर्वदेवमयोऽव्ययः । अङ्कुरो द्व्यङ्गुलः प्रोक्तः पल्लवश्चतुरङ्गुलः ॥

and the flag is declared the tenth, made of all the gods and imperishable. The bud is said to be two fingers, the leaf four fingers.

BP 1.138.25

स्कन्धः षडङ्गुलः प्रोक्तः शाखा चाष्टाङ्गुला मता । एकादशपताका तु कदली च चतुर्दश ॥

The stem is said to be six fingers; the branch is held to be eight fingers. The banner is eleven, and the plantain fourteen.

BP 1.138.26

केतुस्तु षोडशः प्रोक्तो लक्ष्माष्टादशमुच्यते । जया विंशति वै प्रोक्ता एतावत्त्वङ्गुलानि तु ॥

The standard is said to be sixteen, the mark eighteen, and Victory is declared to be twenty fingers; these are the measures in fingers.

BP 1.138.27

देवागारस्य कुम्भस्य प्रसन्ना सा प्रमार्जनी । अङ्कुरेति पताका सा विज्ञेया यदुनन्दन ॥

The clear measure from the temple’s finial is to be known as the Bud banner, O descendant of Yadu.

BP 1.138.28

पल्लवेति द्वितीयस्य मार्जनी परिकीर्तिता । त्रिभागमार्जनीस्कन्धः शाखा वै पञ्चमस्य तु ॥

The second measure is called the Leaf; the Stem is measured by three parts, and the Branch is of the fifth measure.

BP 1.138.29

अष्टमस्य तथा केतुर्लक्ष्म च नवमस्य तु ॥

The sixth is called the Banner, the Plantain the seventh, the Standard the eighth, and the Mark the ninth.

BP 1.138.30

ततस्तु दशमः प्रोक्तो जयन्तो यदुनन्दन । वृषस्थानावमार्गी तु ध्वजस्तु परिकीर्तितः ॥

Then the tenth is declared to be Victor, O descendant of Yadu. The banner is said to be placed at the bull’s station.

BP 1.138.31

गजो मेषोऽथ महिषः कबन्धस्तु वृषस्तथा । हरिणोऽथ नरश्चैव नरश्च नरसत्तम ॥

Elephant, ram, buffalo, headless trunk, bull, deer, man, and man, O best of men—

BP 1.138.32

स्थानान्येतानि भूयोऽथ प्रयुक्तस्य ध्वजस्य तु । दिशभागे तु पूर्वात्तु क्रमेण परिकल्पयेत् ॥

these are the stations for the banner when raised. One should assign them in order to the directional quarters, beginning with the east.

BP 1.138.33

एवं दशविधा प्रोक्ता पताका तत्त्वदर्शिभिः । कर्तव्या सा यथापूर्वं तच्छृणु त्वं नराधिप ॥

Thus the banner is declared tenfold by those who know the truth. It should be made as before; hear of it, O king.

BP 1.138.34

शुक्लवस्त्रमयी चित्रा सघण्टा सुमनोहरा । नानाचामरसम्पन्ना किङ्किणीजालमण्डिता ॥

It should be made of white cloth, variegated, very lovely, furnished with many fly-whisks and adorned with a net of small bells.

BP 1.138.35

ध्वजाग्रे चैव कर्तव्यो देवतालिङ्गसूचकः । काञ्चनो वाथ रौप्यो वा मणिरत्नमयोऽपि वा ॥

At the top of the banner one should make a sign indicating the deity, either golden, silver, or made of jewels and gems.

BP 1.138.36

रङ्गकैर्लिख्यते वापि तद्वाहनसमाकृतिः । ध्वजदण्डोऽत्र विन्यस्तः कर्तव्यो यदुनन्दन ॥

Or the shape of its mount may be drawn in colors. The flagstaff should be set there and made, O descendant of Yadu.

BP 1.138.37

गरुत्मान्स्तु ध्वजो विष्णोरीश्वरस्य ध्वजो वृषः । ब्रह्मणः पङ्कजं कार्यं रवेर्धर्मः स्मृतो ध्वजः ॥

Garuḍa is Viṣṇu’s banner; the bull is Īśvara’s banner. A lotus should be made for Brahmā; Dharma is remembered as the Sun’s banner.

BP 1.138.38

हंसो जलाधिपस्योक्तः सोमस्य तु नरो ध्वजः । बलदेवस्य कालस्तु कामस्य मकरध्वजः ॥

The swan is said to be the banner of the lord of waters; a man is Soma’s banner. Time is Baladeva’s, and the sea-monster is Kāma’s banner.

BP 1.138.39

सिंहो ध्वजस्तु दुर्गायाः कीर्तितो यदुनन्दन । गोधा चापि उमादेव्या रैवतस्य हयः स्मृतः ॥

The lion is declared to be Durgā’s banner, O descendant of Yadu; the lizard is Umā’s, and the horse is remembered as Raivata’s.

BP 1.138.40

कच्छपो वरुणस्योक्तो वातस्य हरिणो मतः । पावकस्य तथा मेष आखुर्गणपतेर्मतः ॥

The tortoise is said to be Varuṇa’s; the deer is held to be the Wind’s. The ram is likewise Fire’s, and the mouse is Gaṇapati’s.

BP 1.138.41

ब्रह्मर्षीणां कुशः प्रोक्त इत्येषा ध्वजकल्पना । यस्य यद्वाहनं प्रोक्तं तत्तस्य ध्वज उच्यते ॥

Sacrificial grass is named for the Brahmin sages; this is the arrangement of banners. Whatever mount is prescribed for each deity, that is called its banner.

BP 1.138.42

विष्णोर्ध्वजे तु सौवर्णं दण्डं कुर्याद्विचक्षणः । पताका चापि पीता स्याद्गरुडस्य समीपगा ॥

For Viṣṇu’s banner the discerning one should make a golden staff; the banner should also be yellow and placed near Garuḍa.

BP 1.138.43

ईश्वरस्य ध्वजे दण्डो राजतो यदुनन्दनः । पताका चापि शुक्ला स्याद्वृषभस्य समीपगा ॥

The staff for Īśvara’s banner is silver, O descendant of Yadu; the banner should also be white and placed near the bull.

BP 1.138.44

पितामहध्वजे दण्डः स्मृतस्ताम्रमयो बुधैः । पद्मवर्णा पताका स्यात्पङ्कजस्य समीपगा ॥

The wise remember the Grandfather’s banner pole as made of copper. Its banner should be lotus-colored and placed near the lotus.

BP 1.138.45

आदित्यस्य च सौवर्णो ध्वजे दण्डः प्रकीर्तितः । पञ्चवर्णा पताका स्याद्धर्मस्याधोगता नृप ॥

Āditya’s banner pole is declared to be golden; the banner should be five-colored and placed below Dharma, O king.

BP 1.138.46

किङ्किणीजालसम्पन्ना नानाबुद्बुदसन्निभा । पुष्पमालोपसम्पन्ना नानावादिभिरावृता ॥

It should be furnished with a net of small bells, resemble many bubbles, bear flower garlands, and be surrounded by many instruments.

BP 1.138.47

दण्ड इन्द्रध्वजस्योक्तः काञ्चनो यदुनन्दन । पताका बहुवर्णा स्यात्कुञ्जरस्य समीपगा ॥

Indra’s banner pole is said to be golden, O descendant of Yadu; the banner should be many-colored and placed near the elephant.

BP 1.138.48

आयसश्चापि दण्डोक्तो यमचिह्नं विचक्षणैः । पताका वर्णतः कृष्णा महिषस्य समीपगा ॥

The discerning say the staff is made of iron, the emblem of Yama; the banner should be black in color and placed near the buffalo.

BP 1.138.49

जलाधिपध्वजो दण्डो राजतः परिकीर्तितः । पताका सर्वतः श्वेता विचित्रा सा च कथ्यते ॥

The banner pole of the lord of waters is proclaimed to be silver; the banner is white on every side and is said to be variegated.

BP 1.138.50

ध्वजे चापि कुबेरस्य दण्डो मणिमयः स्मृतः । पताका चापि रक्ता स्यान्नरपादसमीपगा ॥

For Kubera’s banner the pole is remembered as made of jewels; the banner should also be red and placed near a man’s foot.

BP 1.138.51

बलदेवध्वजे दण्डो राजतो यदुनन्दन । पताका वर्णतः शुक्ला तालस्याधोगता स्मृता ॥

Baladeva’s banner pole is silver, O descendant of Yadu; the banner is remembered as white in color and placed below the palm tree.

BP 1.138.52

कामध्वजे त्रिलोहः स्याद्दण्डो यदुकुलोद्वह । पताका रोहिणी तत्र मकरस्य समीपगा ॥

The pole of Kāma’s banner should be of three metals, O scion of the Yadu line; its banner is Rohiṇī, placed near the sea-monster.

BP 1.138.53

मायूरं कार्त्तिकेयस्य चिह्नं लोकेषु गीयते । त्रिलोहदण्डमारूढं बहुरत्नविभूषितम् ॥

The peacock is celebrated in the worlds as Kārttikeya’s emblem, mounted on a three-metal pole and adorned with many jewels.

BP 1.138.54

बहुवर्णकचित्रा तु पताका कथिता बुधैः । हस्तिदन्तभवं दण्डं कुर्याद्गणपतेर्नृप ॥

The wise declare the banner to be variegated and many-colored. One should make Gaṇapati’s pole from ivory, O king.

BP 1.138.55

ताम्रदण्डं समारुढं संशुद्धं सम्प्रतिष्ठितम् । शुक्ला पताका कर्तव्या सुप्रमाणा महीपते ॥

It should be mounted on a copper pole, purified, and properly set up. The banner should be white and of the proper measure, O king.

BP 1.138.56

मातॄणां चापि कर्तव्यो नैकरूपो ध्वजो बुधैः । पताकाभिरनेकाभिर्बहुरत्नाभिरन्वितः ॥

The wise should also make a many-formed banner for the Mothers, furnished with many pennants and many jewels.

BP 1.138.57

रेवतस्यापि कर्तव्यो ध्वजो वाजी नराधिप । रक्ता पताका तत्रापि कर्तव्या यदुनन्दन ॥

A banner with a horse should also be made for Revata, O king; its pennant should likewise be red, O descendant of Yadu.

BP 1.138.58

चामुण्डामन्दिरे कार्यः शिरोमालाकुलो ध्वजः । नीला पताका कर्तव्या दण्डो लोहमयस्तथा ॥

In Cāmuṇḍā’s temple a banner crowded with a garland of heads should be made. The pennant should be blue and the pole made of iron.

BP 1.138.59

रीतीमयश्च मातॄणां रेवतस्य च कारयेत् । गौर्या ध्वजस्ताम्रमयः पताका गोपसन्निभा ॥

The pole of the Mothers’ and Revata’s banner should be made of brass. Gaurī’s pole is copper, and her pennant resembles a cow.

BP 1.138.60

स्वर्णदण्डस्तु वीरस्य ध्वजो मेषसमन्वितः । पताका बहुरत्नाढ्या कर्तव्या यदुनन्दन ॥

The pole of the hero’s banner is golden and bears a ram; its pennant should be rich with many jewels, O descendant of Yadu.

BP 1.138.61

अश्मसारमयो दण्डो ध्वजो वातस्य उच्यते । पताका कृष्णवर्णा तु हरिणस्य समीपगा ॥

The pole of the Wind’s banner is said to be made of hard stone; its pennant should be black and placed near the deer.

BP 1.138.62

भगवत्या ध्वजो दण्डः सर्वरत्नमयः स्मृतः । पताका तु त्रिवर्णा स्यात्सिंहस्याधोगता नृप ॥

The pole of the Goddess’s banner is remembered as made of every kind of jewel; the pennant should be three-colored and placed below the lion, O king.

BP 1.138.63

एवंविधमिमं कृत्वा ध्वजं लक्षणलक्षितम् । अधिवास्य ततो राजंस्तत आरोपयेद्बुधः ॥

Having made this kind of banner, marked with its proper features, and ritually prepared it, the wise one should then raise it, O king.

BP 1.138.64

ततः सर्वौषधीभिश्च स्नापयित्वा प्रयत्नतः । समालभ्य च बध्नीयान्मध्ये प्रतिसरान्नृप ॥

Then one should carefully bathe it with all the herbs, anoint it, and tie a protective thread around its middle, O king.

BP 1.138.65

कल्पयित्वा शुभां वेदिं कलशैरुपशोभिताम् । तस्यां वेद्यां समारोप्य तां रात्रिमधिवासयेत् ॥

Having prepared an auspicious altar adorned with pitchers, one should place it on that altar and consecrate it for that night.

BP 1.138.66

नानाकुसुमचित्रां च स्रजं तस्यानुलम्बयेत् । समभ्यर्च्य प्रयत्नेन धूपमस्य निवेदयेत् ॥

One should hang from it a garland variegated with many flowers. Having carefully worshipped it, one should offer incense to it.

BP 1.138.67

बलिकर्म ततः कृत्वा कृशरापूपकादिभिः । पलालपूपिकाभिश्च दधिपायससूपकैः ॥

Then one should make food offerings with rice-and-sesame dish, cakes, and the like, millet cakes, curds, milk-rice, and soups.

BP 1.138.68

उद्दिश्य लोकपालेभ्यो बलिं दद्याच्च वायसैः । ब्राह्मणान्स्वस्ति वाच्याथ कृत्वा पुण्याहमङ्गलम् ॥

One should offer portions to the guardians of the directions and to the crows, then have the Brahmins pronounce blessings and declare the day auspicious.

BP 1.138.69

वादित्रकृतनिर्घोषं जलं संस्कारसंयुतम् । नानाबुद्बुदसम्पन्नं वेष्टितं नववाससा ॥

The water, accompanied by the sound of instruments, should be ritually prepared, filled with many bubbles, and wrapped in new cloth.

BP 1.138.70

शुभे लग्ने दिने ऋक्षे ध्वजं चारोपयेद्बुधः । विन्यस्य स्वर्णकलशं श्वभ्रराजं ध्वजस्य तु ॥

At an auspicious ascendant, day, and lunar mansion, the wise one should raise the banner, placing a golden pitcher in the principal pit for the banner.

BP 1.138.71

एवमारोपयेद्यस्तु ध्वजं देवालयोपरि । स श्रिया वर्धते नित्यं प्राप्नोति परमां गतिम् ॥

Whoever thus raises the banner above the temple grows constantly in prosperity and reaches the highest goal.

BP 1.138.72

असुरा वासमिच्छन्ति ध्वजहीने सुरालये । तस्माद्देवालयं प्राज्ञो ध्वजहीनं न कारयेत् ॥

Demons wish to dwell in a gods’ temple without a banner; therefore a wise man should not leave a temple without a banner.

BP 1.138.73

मन्त्रश्च स्थापने प्रोक्तो विधानज्ञैर्ध्वजस्य तु । एह्येहि भगवन्देव देववाहन वै खग ॥

The sacred formula for installing the banner, declared by those who know the rite, is: “Come, come, O blessed deity, vehicle of the gods, O bird!”

BP 1.138.74

श्रीकरः श्रीनिवासश्च जय जैत्रोपशोभित । व्योमरूप महारूप धर्मात्मंस्त्वं च वै गतेः ॥

“Giver of prosperity, abode of prosperity, adorned with victory and triumph, form of the sky, great form, righteous one, you indeed are the goal.”

BP 1.138.75

सान्निध्यं कुरु दण्डेऽस्मिन्साक्षी च ध्रुवतां व्रज । कुरु वृद्धिं सदा कर्तुः प्रासादस्यार्कवल्लभ ॥

“Be present in this pole and become its steadfast witness. Always cause the maker’s temple to prosper, O beloved of the Sun.”

BP 1.138.76

ॐ एह्येहि भगवन्नीश्वरविनिर्मित उपरिचरवायुमार्गानुसारिञ्छ्रीनिवास रिपुध्वंस यक्षनिलय सर्वदेवप्रियं कुरु सान्निध्यं शान्ति स्वस्त्ययनं च मेभयं सर्वविघ्ना व्यपसरन्तु ॥

“Om. Come, come, O blessed one, made by Īśvara, moving above and following the wind’s path, abode of prosperity, destroyer of enemies, dwelling of Yakṣas, beloved of all gods. Be present; bring me peace, well-being, and safety. Let all obstacles depart.”

BP 1.138.77

मन्त्रेणानेन राजेन्द्र श्वभ्रे दण्डे निवेशयेत् । पताकां पूर्वमन्त्रेण स्थित्या पूर्वमुखो नृप ॥

With this sacred formula, O king of kings, one should set the pole in the pit; with the earlier formula one should set the pennant firmly in place, facing east, O king.

BP 1.138.78

क्षिपेदूर्ध्वमथाकाशं प्रासादशिखराद्विभोः । यजमानस्ततः पश्येत्पताकां यदुनन्दन ॥

One should raise it upward into the sky from the summit of the temple, O lord; then the patron should look upon the pennant, O descendant of Yadu.

BP 1.138.79

प्रासादपुरतो वापि पताकां पातयेद्यदि । इन्द्रलोकं तदा कर्ता विशेद्वै यदुनन्दन ॥

If one lets the pennant fall in front of the temple, then its maker enters Indra’s world, O descendant of Yadu.

BP 1.138.80

आग्नेय्यामग्निलोकं तु याम्यां यमसदो भजेत् । नैऋत्यां नैऋतं लोकं वारुण्यां वारुणं व्रजेत् ॥

At the southeast he reaches the world of Fire; at the south, Yama’s abode; at the southwest, Nirṛti’s world; at the west, Varuṇa’s realm.

BP 1.138.81

यस्य देवस्य यद्वेश्म कृतं यदुकुलोद्वह । तस्य लोकमवाप्नोति वृषस्थानगतो यदि ॥

For whichever deity a dwelling is made, O scion of the Yadu line, its maker reaches that deity’s world if he goes to the bull’s station.

BP 1.138.82

वायव्ये वायुमाप्नोति सौम्यायां सोममाप्नुयात् । ऐशान्यामीशमाप्नोति कर्ता वै देववेश्मनः ॥

At the northwest he reaches the Wind; at the north he should reach Soma; at the northeast the maker of the deity’s dwelling reaches Īśa.

BP 1.138.83

य एवं कारयेद्भक्त्या ध्वजस्यारोपणं रवेः । स हि भुक्त्वा परान्भोगान्सूर्यलोके महीयते ॥

Whoever devoutly causes the raising of Ravi’s banner enjoys the highest pleasures and is honored in the world of the Sun.

BP 1.138.84

तेजसाम्बुजसंकाशः कान्त्या चाम्बुजसन्निभः । द्विजातितुल्यः प्रभया विक्रमेण च गोपतेः ॥

His radiance is like a lotus and his beauty like a lotus; in splendor he is equal to a twice-born man, and in prowess to the Lord of Cows.
