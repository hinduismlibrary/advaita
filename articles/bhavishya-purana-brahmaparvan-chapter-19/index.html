Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 19 — Sukanyā, Cyavana, the Aśvins and Puṣpa-dvitīyā

BP 1.19.1

सुमन्तुरुवाच
द्वितीयायां तु राजेन्द्र अश्विनौ सोमपीतिनौ ।
च्यवनेन कृतौ यज्ञे मिषतो मघवस्य च ॥

Sumantu said: ‘O king, on the second lunar day the Aśvins, drinkers of Soma, were admitted to the sacrifice by Cyavana even as Maghavan looked on.

BP 1.19.2

शतानीक उवाच
कथमिन्द्रस्य मिषतः कृतौ तौ सोमपीतिनौ ।
च्यवनेन हि देवानां पश्यतां तद्वदस्व मे ॥

Śatānīka said: ‘How did Cyavana make those two drinkers of Soma while Indra and the gods watched? Tell me this.

BP 1.19.3

अहो महत्तपस्तस्य च्यवनस्य महात्मनः ।
यदिन्द्रस्य बलादेव देवत्वं प्रापितावुभौ ॥

How extraordinary was the austerity of the great-souled Cyavana, by whose sheer power both attained divine status despite Indra!

BP 1.19.4

सुमन्तुरुवाच
पुरातनयुगे सन्धौ पश्चिमेऽथ नराधिप ।
च्यवनो योगमास्थाय गंगाकूलेऽवसच्चिरम् ॥

Sumantu said: ‘At the close of an ancient age, O king, Cyavana entered yogic absorption and dwelt long upon the bank of the Gaṅgā.

BP 1.19.5

तत्र शर्यातिरायातः स्नातुमन्तः पुरैः सह ।
स्नात्वाभ्यर्च्य पितॄन्देवान्गमनायोपचक्रमे ॥

King Śaryāti came there to bathe with the women of his household. After bathing and honouring the ancestors and gods, he prepared to depart.

BP 1.19.6

तत्र मूढं जनपदमपश्यत्पथि चेष्टनम् ।
विण्मूत्रोत्सर्गसंरुद्धं ज्योतिराक्षिप्तनिष्क्रियम् ॥
भ्रमन्तं तत्रतत्रैव समीक्ष्य स बलं नृपः ॥

On the road he saw his bewildered people writhing, their excretion obstructed and their bodily functions arrested by a radiant force; the king examined his army wandering everywhere.

BP 1.19.7

उवाच दुर्मना राजा अमात्यान्स्वान्पुरोगमान् ।
च्यवनस्याश्रमोऽयं हि नापराद्धं तु केनचित् ॥

Distressed, the king addressed his ministers and leaders: “This is Cyavana’s hermitage. Has someone committed an offence here?”

BP 1.19.8

न चोवाच यदा कश्चित्तस्य राज्ञस्तु पृच्छतः ।
तदा सुता सुकन्यास्य प्रोवाच पितरं वचः ॥

When no one answered the questioning king, his daughter Sukanyā spoke to her father.

BP 1.19.9

मया दृष्टं तु यत्तात सखिभिः सह कौतुकम् ।
तत्ते वच्मि निबोध त्वं शृणु तात महाद्भुतम् ॥

“Father, I saw something curious while with my companions. I shall tell you; attend and hear this great wonder.”

BP 1.19.10

शिञ्जितारावबहुलाः काञ्चीनूपुरमेखलाः ।
गायन्त्यो विलपन्त्यश्च कीडन्त्यश्चात्र कानने ॥

“While we wandered here in the forest, singing, lamenting, and playing, our girdles, anklets, and waist-bands made abundant tinkling sounds.”

BP 1.19.11

कोकिलध्वनिमश्रौषं व्यक्ताव्यक्ताक्षरं कृशम् ।
सुकन्ये ह्येहिह्येहीति वल्मीकाद्वचमुद्गिरन् ॥

“I heard a faint, cuckoo-like voice of partly distinct syllables emerging from an anthill: ‘Sukanyā, come here, come here!’”

BP 1.19.12

तत्र गत्वाद्भुतं तात पश्यामः किल पावकौ ।
दीपाविवाचलशिखौ भूयः कन्या उवाच ह ॥

“Going there, father, we saw what appeared to be two wondrous fires, like two lamps with steady flames,” the maiden continued.

BP 1.19.13

मया च कौतुकात्तात किमेतदित्यबुद्धितः ।
सूदितौ दर्भसूच्यग्रैस्तत्तेजः समुपारमत् ॥

“Out of curiosity and ignorance, father, I pierced them with the points of blades of darbha grass, asking, ‘What is this?’ Their light then subsided.”

BP 1.19.14

तच्छ्रुत्वा नृपतिस्त्रस्तस्तूर्णं तद्वनमागमत् ।
यत्रास्ते भार्गवः कष्टं वल्मीकान्तर्गतो मुनिः ॥

Hearing this, the terrified king hurried to the forest where the suffering Bhārgava sage sat enclosed within the anthill.

BP 1.19.15

गत्वा स तत्र प्रोवाच प्रणिपत्य द्विजोत्तमम् ।
अपराद्धं मया देव तत्क्षमस्व नमोऽस्तु ते ॥

He went there, bowed, and said to the foremost brāhmaṇa: “Revered one, I have offended you. Forgive it; homage to you.”

BP 1.19.16

स तं प्रोवाच नृपतिं मया ज्ञातं नराधिप ।
सुकन्यां मे प्रयच्छस्व निवेशार्थी ह्यहं नृप ॥
अनुक्रमन्सुकन्यां तु दत्त्वा राजन्सुखी भव ॥

Cyavana replied: “I understand, O king. Give me Sukanyā in marriage, for I seek a household. Hand her over and depart happily.”

BP 1.19.17

इत्युक्तः प्रददौ राजा सुकन्यामविचारयन् ।
ततः स्वपुरमागम्य अवसत्सुचिरं सुखी ॥

Thus addressed, the king gave Sukanyā without hesitation. He then returned to his city and lived happily for a long time.

BP 1.19.18

सुकन्यापि पतिं लब्ध्वा सुप्रीताराधयत्तदा ।
राज्यश्रियं परित्यज्य वल्कलाजिनधारिणी ॥

Having obtained her husband, Sukanyā served him with great affection, abandoning royal splendour and wearing bark and antelope skin.

BP 1.19.19

गते बहुतिथे काले वसन्ते समुपस्थिते ।
तपोद्योतितसर्वाङ्गीं रूपोदार्यगुणान्विताम् ॥
स्नातां स्वभार्यां च्यवन उवाच मधुराक्षरम् ॥

After much time had passed and spring arrived, Cyavana addressed his freshly bathed wife—whose whole body shone through austerity and who possessed beauty, generosity, and virtue—in gentle words.

BP 1.19.20

एह्येहि भद्रे भद्रं ते शयनीयं समाश्रय ।
अपत्यं जनयस्याद्य कुलद्वयविवर्धनम् ॥

“Come, blessed lady—may good attend you. Approach the bed and today conceive a child who will increase both our families.”

BP 1.19.21

एवमुक्ता तु सा कन्या प्राञ्जलिः पतिमब्रवीत् ।
नार्हस्यद्य सुकल्याण सङ्गमं स्थण्डिलेऽसमे ॥

So addressed, the maiden joined her palms and said to her husband: “Handsome one, it is not fitting today to unite upon this uneven bare ground.”

BP 1.19.22

मम प्रियं कुरुष्वाद्य ततो मामाह्वयस्व च ।
पितृगेहे यथातिष्ठं शयनीये सुसंस्कृते ॥

“Do what is pleasing to me today and then summon me: provide a well-appointed bed such as the one upon which I stayed in my father’s house.”

BP 1.19.23

बहुगैरिकवर्णाद्यैः श्वेतपीतारुणाकुले ।
वस्त्रालङ्कारगन्धाद्यैस्तथा त्वमपि तत्कुरु ॥

“Make it rich with many mineral colours—white, yellow, and red—and likewise furnished with garments, ornaments, fragrances, and the rest.”

BP 1.19.24

आत्मानं वयसोपेतं रूपवन्तं सुवर्चसम् ।
वस्त्रालङ्कारगन्धाढ्यं पश्येयं येन सादरम् ॥

“And let me respectfully behold you youthful, handsome, radiant, and adorned with fine garments, ornaments, and fragrances.”

BP 1.19.25

सुकन्याया वचः श्रुत्वा च्यवनः प्राह दुर्मनाः ।
न मेऽस्ति वित्तं कल्याणि पितुस्तेऽस्ति यथा वने ॥

Hearing Sukanyā’s words, the downcast Cyavana said: “Lovely one, I do not possess wealth here in the forest such as exists in your father’s house.”

BP 1.19.26

स कथं भूषयाम्यद्य सुरूपश्च कथं वद ।
प्रोवाच सा पतिं भूयः प्रहसन्ती कृताञ्जलिः ॥
वित्तं ददावैलविलो रूपं वैरोचनोऽददत् ॥

“How then can I adorn myself today, and how can I become handsome? Tell me.” Smiling with joined palms, she again said to her husband: “Aila’s son gave wealth, and Virocana’s son gave beauty.”

BP 1.19.27

च्यवनः प्राह भार्यां तां न करिष्ये तपोव्ययम् ।
एवमुक्त्वा तपश्चोग्रं तताप सुचिरं तदा ॥

Cyavana told his wife, “I shall not expend my accumulated austerity.” Having said this, he then practised fierce asceticism for a long time.

BP 1.19.28

अथ तत्रागतौ वीरावश्विनौ कालपर्ययात् ।
दृष्टवन्तौ सुकन्यां तौ दीप्त्या वै देवतामिव ॥

In due course the two heroic Aśvins came there. They saw Sukanyā shining like a goddess.

BP 1.19.29

उपगम्योचतुस्तां तौ का त्वं सुन्दरि रूपिणी ।
किमर्थमिह एका त्वं तिष्ठसे कस्तवाश्रयः ॥

Approaching her, the two asked: “Who are you, beautiful and lovely woman? Why do you stand here alone, and who is your protector?”

BP 1.19.30

सा तावुवाच तन्वङ्गी शर्यातिदुहिता ह्यहम् ।
भर्ता च च्यवनो मह्यं कौ च वां मे तथोच्यताम् ॥

The slender woman replied: “I am King Śaryāti’s daughter, and Cyavana is my husband. Now tell me who you two are.”

BP 1.19.31

ऊचतुश्चाश्विनौ देवावावां विद्धि नृपात्मजे ।
किं करिष्यसि तेन त्वं जीर्णेन च कृशेन च ॥
आवयोर्वृणु भर्तारमेकमेव यमिच्छसि ॥

The divine Aśvins said: “Know us to be the Aśvins, princess. What will you do with that aged and emaciated man? Choose whichever one of us you desire as husband.”

BP 1.19.32

सा त्वब्रवीच्च मा मैवं वक्तुमर्हौ दिवौकसौ ।
भर्तारमनुरक्ताङ्गी यथा स्वाहा विभावसोः ॥

She replied: “Divine ones, you must not speak so. I am devoted to my husband just as Svāhā is devoted to Vibhāvasu, the Fire-god.”

BP 1.19.33

अश्विनावूचतुः
आयातु च विशत्वद्य च्यवनो वैष्णवीजलम् ।
ततो नौ मध्यगं ह्येकं वृणीष्वान्यं यमिच्छति ॥

The Aśvins said: “Let Cyavana come and enter the sacred water today. Then choose as you wish one man standing between us.”

BP 1.19.34

तावब्रूतां सुकन्यां तु गत्वा पृच्छ स्वकं पतिम् ।
तं च पृष्ट्वा पुनश्चात्रागच्छ नौ सन्निधौ पुनः ॥ .

The two told Sukanyā: “Go and ask your own husband. After asking him, return here again to our presence.”

BP 1.19.35

आवामत्रैव तिष्ठावो यावदागमनं तव ।
सा गत्वा प्राह भर्तारमश्विनावेवसूचतुः ॥

“We shall remain here until you return.” She went and told her husband what the Aśvins had said.

BP 1.19.36

रूपवन्तं च भर्तारं करिष्यावो यमिच्छसि ।
अथ मध्यगतं ह्येकं भर्तृत्वेन वरिष्यसि ॥

“We shall make whichever man you desire handsome; then you must choose one from among the three as your husband.”

BP 1.19.37

एवमस्त्विति तां प्राह भार्यां च्यवनस्त्वरन् ।
सा तं गृह्य जगामाशु यत्र तौ भिषजावुभौ ॥

Cyavana quickly told his wife, “So be it.” Taking him with her, she hastened to where the two divine physicians stood.

BP 1.19.38

सा तावुवाच च्यवनो यथोक्तं भवतोर्वचः ।
कुरुतं ह्यश्विनौ क्षिप्रं सुकन्या चेप्सितं वृणोत् ॥

She told them: “Cyavana accepts your proposal. O Aśvins, act quickly, and Sukanyā will choose the one she desires.”

BP 1.19.39

तौ तं सङ्गृह्य गङ्गायां प्रविष्टौ मुनिना सह ।
मुहूर्तात्तु समुत्तिष्ठन्रूपतश्च श्रिया वृताः ॥

Taking hold of him, the two entered the Gaṅgā together with the sage. After a moment all three rose, endowed with beauty and splendour.

BP 1.19.40

शोभन्ते स्म महाबाहौ कमुद्भिद्य तपोयुताः ।
कल्पादौ कलशे यद्वत्कञ्जाक्ष व्योम वेधसः ॥
उदकादुत्थितास्तस्मात्सर्वे ते समरूपकाः ॥

O mighty-armed king, they shone alike, emerging from the water through ascetic power, like lotus-eyed Brahmās arising from the cosmic vessel at creation’s beginning.

BP 1.19.41

सुकन्या तु ततो दृष्ट्वा भर्त्तारं देवरूपिणम् ।
हर्षेण महताविष्टा न च तं वेद भारत ॥

Seeing her husband in divine form, Sukanyā was filled with great joy, yet, O descendant of Bharata, she could not identify him.

BP 1.19.42

समकायाः समवयः समरूपाः समश्रियः ।
वस्त्रालङ्कारसदृशान्दृष्ट्वा चिन्तां गता चिरम् ॥

Their bodies, ages, appearance, and splendour were equal. Seeing them alike in garments and ornaments, she remained long in thought.

BP 1.19.43

सा चिन्तयित्वा सुचिरं वैद्यदेवावुवाच ह ।
बीभत्सोऽपि मया भर्ता परित्यक्तो न कर्हिचित् ॥

After prolonged reflection, she addressed the divine physicians: “I have never abandoned my husband, even when he was repulsive.”

BP 1.19.44

भवद्भिरात्मसंदृशं कथं त्यक्त्वा वृणे परम् ।
तस्मात्तमेव भर्तारं प्रयच्छध्वं दिवौकसः ॥

“How could I forsake him and choose another resembling you? Therefore, gods, return that very husband to me.”

BP 1.19.45

तया सबहुमानं तौ प्राञ्जल्या प्रार्थितौ तदा ।
देवचिह्नानि स्वान्येव धारयन्तौ सुपूजितौ ॥

Thus respectfully entreated by her with joined palms, the two honoured gods displayed their own divine marks.

BP 1.19.46

सुकन्या निपुणं तौ तु सुनिरीक्ष्य च विह्वला ।
न रजो न निमेषो वै न स्पृशेते धरां पदे ॥

Sukanyā carefully examined the two while bewildered: neither dust nor blinking affected them, and their feet did not touch the earth.

BP 1.19.47

अयं च सरजा म्लानो भूमिमाश्रित्य तिष्ठति ।
निमेषं चैव तस्यैवं ज्ञात्वा वै च्यवनो वृतः ॥

The other man bore dust, appeared slightly faded, stood upon the earth, and blinked. Recognizing these signs, she chose Cyavana.

BP 1.19.48

च्यवने वृते सुकन्यया पुष्पवृष्टिः पपात ह ।
देवदुन्दुभयश्चैव प्रावाद्यन्त अनेकशः ॥

When Sukanyā chose Cyavana, a rain of flowers fell and divine drums sounded repeatedly.

BP 1.19.49

ततस्तु च्यवनस्तुष्टो दिव्यरूपधरस्तदा ।
उवाच तौ तु सुप्रीत अश्विनौ किं करोमि वाम् ॥

Then the delighted Cyavana, bearing a divine form, affectionately asked the Aśvins: “What may I do for you two?”

BP 1.19.50

भार्या दत्ता कृतं रूपं देवानामपि दुर्लभम् ।
उपकारं वरिष्ठं यो न करोत्युपकारिणः ॥

“You restored my wife to me and bestowed a beauty difficult even for gods to obtain. A person who does not render the highest service to benefactors—”

BP 1.19.51

एकविंशत्स गच्छेच्च नरकाणि क्रमेण वै ।
तस्मादहं वरिष्ठं वै करिष्येऽहममानुषम् ॥

“—goes successively to twenty-one hells. Therefore I shall perform for you a supreme deed beyond ordinary human power.”

BP 1.19.52

उपकारं भवद्भ्यां तु प्रीतः कुर्यां सुनिश्चितम् ।
यज्ञभागफलं दद्यां यद्देवेष्वपि दुर्लभम् ॥

“Pleased with you both, I shall certainly repay your kindness by giving you a share in the sacrifice, a privilege difficult even for the gods to obtain.”

BP 1.19.53

एवमुक्त्वा तु देवेशौ विससर्ज महामुनिः ।
आजगामाश्रमं पुण्यं सहभार्यो मुदान्वितः ॥

Having spoken thus, the great sage dismissed the two divine lords. Filled with joy, he returned with his wife to his holy hermitage.

BP 1.19.54

अथ शुश्राव शर्यातिश्च्यवनं देवरूपिणम् ।
जगाम च महातेजा द्रष्टुं मुनिवरं वशी ॥

Then Śaryāti heard that Cyavana had acquired a divine form. The self-controlled, radiant king went to see the excellent sage.

BP 1.19.55

तं दृष्ट्वा प्रणिपत्यादौ प्रतिपूज्य यथार्हतः ।
सुकन्यां तु ततो दृष्ट्वा प्रणिपत्याभिनन्द्य च ॥

Seeing him, the king first bowed and honoured him fittingly. Then, seeing Sukanyā, he greeted her after she bowed.

BP 1.19.56

सस्वजे मूर्ध्नि आघ्राय ततोत्सङ्गं समानयत् ।
सा तस्याः सस्वजे प्रेम्णा आनन्दाश्रुपरिप्लुता ॥
संस्थाप्य तां मुदा युक्तो नृपतिः सह भार्यया ॥

He embraced her, smelled her head affectionately, and drew her onto his lap. Overwhelmed by tears of joy, her mother also embraced her lovingly; the king and queen then settled her happily.

BP 1.19.57

भूयोऽब्रवीत्सुसंतुष्टं च्यवनस्तं नराधिपम् ।
संभारं कुरु यज्ञार्थं याजयिष्ये नराधिप ॥

Highly pleased, Cyavana again addressed the king: “Prepare the requisites for a sacrifice, O ruler; I shall officiate for you.”

BP 1.19.58

एवमुक्तः स नृपतिः प्रणिपत्य महामुनिम् ।
जगाम स्वपुरं हृष्टो यज्ञार्थं यत्नमाचरत् ॥

Thus addressed, the king bowed to the great sage and returned joyfully to his city, where he diligently prepared for the sacrifice.

BP 1.19.59

सप्रेष्यान्प्रेषयन्क्षिप्रं यज्ञार्थे द्रव्यमाहरत् ।
मंत्रिपुरोहिताचार्यानानयामास सत्वरम् ॥

He quickly dispatched servants and gathered the sacrificial materials, then promptly summoned ministers, priests, and teachers.

BP 1.19.60

समानीतेषु सर्वेषु तेषु द्रव्येषु भारत ।
आजगाम विशुद्धात्मा च्यवनः सह भार्यया ॥

When all the materials had been assembled, O Bharata, the pure-souled Cyavana arrived with his wife.

BP 1.19.61

सम्पूजितश्च शुश्राव महान्तं त्यागमौजसम् ।
अन्यैश्च बहुभिः सार्द्धमत्र्यङ्गिरसभार्गवैः ॥

He was duly honoured and displayed magnificent generosity and vigour, accompanied by many descendants of Atri, Aṅgiras, and Bhṛgu.

BP 1.19.62

प्रवर्तिते महायज्ञे यजमाने नृपोत्तमे ।
ऋत्विक्त्वकर्मनिरते हूयमाने हुताशने ॥
आहूताः स्वागताः सर्वे भागार्थं त्रिदिवालयाः ॥

When the great sacrifice began with the excellent king as patron, the officiants attended to their duties and offerings were poured into the fire; all the heavenly gods were invited, welcomed, and summoned to receive their portions.

BP 1.19.63

यज्ञभागे प्रवृत्ते तु शास्त्रोक्तेन विधानतः ।
आगतावश्विनौ तत्र आहूतो च्यवनेन तु ॥

When distribution of the sacrificial portions commenced according to scriptural rule, the two Aśvins arrived, having been summoned by Cyavana.

BP 1.19.64

आह्वाने क्रियमाणे तु अश्विभ्यां तु तदा नृप ।
प्रोवाचेन्द्रोऽथ च्यवनं नैतौ भागान्वितौ कुरु ॥
देवानां भिषजावेतौ न भागार्हौ न दैवतौ ॥

O king, as an offering was being prepared for the Aśvins, Indra told Cyavana: “Do not give these two a share. They are the gods’ physicians; they are neither entitled to a portion nor true deities.”

BP 1.19.65

च्यवनस्त्विंद्रमाहेदं देवौ ह्येतावुभावपि ।
ममोपकारिणावेतौ दद्मि भागं न संशयः ॥

Cyavana replied to Indra: “These two are indeed both gods and my benefactors. I shall give them a share; of this there is no doubt.”

BP 1.19.66

ततो ह्युवाच सक्रोधः स शक्रश्च्यवनं रुषा ।
विप्रर्षे प्रहरिष्यामि यदि भागं प्रयच्छसि ॥

Then Śakra angrily and wrathfully told Cyavana: “Brāhmaṇa-seer, I shall strike you if you grant them a share.”

BP 1.19.67

एवमुक्तस्तु विप्रर्षिर्न चोवाचापि किञ्चन ।
भागौ ददौ च सोऽश्विभ्यां स्रुवमुद्यम्य मन्त्रतः ॥

Though addressed in this way, the brāhmaṇa-seer said nothing. Raising the ladle and reciting the mantra, he gave the two portions to the Aśvins.

BP 1.19.68

अथ उद्यम्य भिदुरं मोक्तुकामो दिवस्पतिः ।
स्तम्भितश्च्यवनेनाथ सवज्रः स नराधिप ॥

Then the lord of heaven raised his destructive thunderbolt to release it, but Cyavana immobilized him together with the weapon, O king.

BP 1.19.69

स स्तम्भयित्वात्विन्द्रं तु भागं दत्त्वाश्विनोर्वशी ।
समापयामास तदा यज्ञकर्म यथार्थवत् ॥

Having immobilized Indra and given the Aśvins their portion, the self-controlled sage completed the sacrificial rite exactly as prescribed.

BP 1.19.70

कञ्जजोऽथाजगामाशु आह च च्यवनं तदा ।
उत्तंभ्यतामयं लेखो भागश्चास्त्वश्विनोरिह ॥

Then the lotus-born Brahmā swiftly arrived and told Cyavana: “Release this god; let the Aśvins’ share stand here.”

BP 1.19.71

तथेन्द्रस्तमुवाचेदं च्यवनं प्रीतमानसः ।
जानामि शक्तिं तपसश्च्यवनेह तवोत्तमाम् ॥

Indra likewise spoke to Cyavana with a pleased heart: “Cyavana, I recognize the supreme power of your austerity here.”

BP 1.19.72

ख्यापनार्थं हि तपसस्तव एतत्कृतं मया ।
अद्यप्रभृति भागोऽस्तु देवत्वं चाश्विनोस्तथा ॥

“I did this only to make your ascetic power renowned. From today onward let the Aśvins possess both a sacrificial share and divine status.”

BP 1.19.73

यस्त्विमां तपसः ख्यातिं त्वदीयां वै पठिष्यति ।
शृणुयाद्वापि शुद्धात्मा तस्य पुण्यफलं शृणु ॥

“Hear the meritorious result obtained by any pure-hearted person who recites or listens to this account of the fame of your austerity.”

BP 1.19.74

विरोचनसदो गत्वा गत्वा पुष्पसदस्तथा ।
कालेऽथ वामदेवस्य मुञ्जकेशसदस्तथा ॥
यौवनयुक्तः स क्रीडास्तिष्ठतीति न संशयः ॥

“Having gone to Virocana’s assembly, the assembly of flowers, Vāmadeva’s realm, and the assembly of Muñjakeśa in due time, he remains there youthful and enjoying delights—of this there is no doubt.”

BP 1.19.75

एवमुक्त्वा जगामाशु देवः स्वभवनं वशी ।
च्यवनोऽपि सभार्यो वै शयार्तिश्चाश्रमं गतः ॥

Having spoken thus, the self-controlled god swiftly returned to his abode. Cyavana, his wife, and Śaryāti likewise went to the hermitage.

BP 1.19.76

अथापश्यद्विमानाभं भवनं देवनिर्मितम् ।
शय्यासनवरैर्जुष्टं सर्वकामसमृद्धिमत् ॥

There Cyavana beheld a mansion resembling a celestial aerial car, fashioned by the gods, furnished with excellent beds and seats, and endowed with every desired enjoyment.

BP 1.19.77

उद्यानवापिभिर्जुष्टं देवेन्द्रेण समाहृतम् ।
गोखण्डसन्निभं रेजे गृहं तद्भुवि दुर्लभम् ॥

Equipped with gardens and reservoirs and brought forth by the lord of gods, that mansion—resembling a great celestial enclosure—shone as something rare upon earth.

BP 1.19.78

सुभूषणानि दिव्यानि रत्नवन्ति महान्ति च ।
अरजान्सि च वस्त्राणि दिव्यप्रावरणानि च ॥

It contained splendid divine ornaments set with jewels, spotless garments, and celestial coverings.

BP 1.19.79

तत्सर्वमखिलं सह पत्न्या महामुनिः ।
मुदं परमिकां लेभे शक्रं च प्रशशंस ह ॥

The great sage, together with his wife, obtained all of it in full, experienced supreme delight, and praised Śakra.

BP 1.19.80

एवमिष्टा तिथिरियं द्वितीया अश्विनोर्नृप ।
देवत्वं यज्ञभागं च सम्प्राप्ताविह भारत ॥

Thus, O king and descendant of Bharata, this second lunar day became dear to the Aśvins, for upon it they obtained divine status and a share in sacrifice.

BP 1.19.81

उपोष्या विधिना येन तं शृणुष्व नराधिप ।
रूपं सुरूपं यो वाञ्छेद्द्वितीयायां नराधिप ॥

Now hear, O king, the rule by which one should fast upon it. A person desiring a beautiful form should observe the second lunar day.

BP 1.19.82

कार्तिके शुक्लपक्षस्य द्वितीयायां नराधिप ।
पुष्पाहारो वर्षमेकं भवेत्स नियतात्मवान् ॥

On the bright fortnight’s second day in Kārttika, O king, a self-controlled person should subsist upon flowers for one year.

BP 1.19.83

कालप्राप्तानि यानि स्युर्हविष्यं कुसुमानि तु ।
भुञ्जीयात्तानि दत्त्वा तु ब्राह्मणेभ्यो नराधिप ॥

O king, whatever seasonable edible flowers are available should first be given to brāhmaṇas and then eaten by the observer.

BP 1.19.84

सौवर्णरौप्यपुष्पाणि अथ वा जलजानि च ।
व्रतान्ते तस्य सन्तुष्टौ देवौ त्रिभुवनेऽश्विनौ ॥

At the completion of the vow, the two Aśvin gods who range the three worlds are pleased by flowers of gold and silver, or alternatively by water-born lotuses.

BP 1.19.85

ददतुः कामगं दिव्यं विमानमतितेजसम् ।
सुचिरं दिवि नारीभिर्लोकेऽसौ रमतेऽश्विनोः ॥

They grant a brilliant divine aerial car that travels at will. For a long time the observer enjoys himself with celestial women in the Aśvins’ realm.

BP 1.19.86

इह चागत्य कल्पान्ते जातो विप्रः पुरस्कृतः ।
वेदवेदांगविदुषः सप्तजन्मान्तराण्यसौ ॥

Returning here at the end of the cosmic age, he is born as an eminent brāhmaṇa, learned in the Vedas and their auxiliaries, throughout seven successive lives.

BP 1.19.87

जातो जातो भवेद्विद्वान्ब्राह्मणोऽसौ कृते युगे ।
दाता यज्ञपतिर्वाग्मी आधिव्याधिविवर्जितः ॥

In every birth during the Kṛta age he becomes a learned brāhmaṇa: generous, a patron of sacrifice, eloquent, and free from mental and bodily affliction.

BP 1.19.88

पुत्रपौत्रैः परिवृतः सह पन्त्याऽवसच्चिरम् ।
मध्यदेशे सुनगरे धर्मिष्ठो राज्यभाग्भवेत् ॥

Surrounded by sons and grandsons, he lives long with his wife; righteous and residing in an excellent city of the central country, he obtains royal sovereignty.

BP 1.19.89

इत्येषा कथिता तुभ्यं द्वितीया पुष्पसंज्ञिता ।
फलसंज्ञा तथान्या स्यात्सुते वै मुञ्जकेशिनि ॥

Thus I have related to you the second lunar day called Puṣpa. Another is known as Phala and concerns Muñjakeśin’s son.

BP 1.19.90

सुष्ठु पुण्या पापहरा विष्टरश्रवसः प्रिया ।
अशून्यशयना लोके प्रख्याता कुरुनन्दन ॥

O delight of the Kurus, it is exceedingly meritorious, destroys sin, and is dear to Viṣṭaraśravas; in the world it is renowned as the Aśūnya-śayana observance.

BP 1.19.91

तामुपोष्य नरो राजञ्छ्रद्धाभक्तिपुरस्कृतः ।
ऋद्धिं वृद्धिं श्रियं वाथ भार्यया सह मोदते ॥

O king, a person who fasts upon it with faith and devotion enjoys prosperity, increase, and splendour together with his wife.
