Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 5: The Householder’s Duties and the Marks of Women

BP 1.5.1

सुमन्तुरुवाच
षट्त्रिंशदाब्दिकं चर्यं गुरौ त्रैवेदिकं व्रतम् ।
तदर्धिकं पादिकं वा ग्रहणान्तिकमेव च ॥ १

Sumantu said: The three-Veda vow of study with a guru is to be observed for thirty-six years, half that, a quarter, or until learning is complete.

Word-for-word
सुमन्तुः उवाच (sumantuḥ uvāca) — Sumantu said;
षट्-त्रिंशत्-आब्दिकम् (ṣaṭ-triṃśat-ābdikam) — lasting thirty-six years;
चर्यम् (caryam) — discipline;
गुरौ (gurau) — with teacher;
त्रै-वेदिकम् व्रतम् (trai-vedikam vratam) — vow for three Vedas;
तत्-अर्धिकम् (tat-ardhikam) — half of that;
पादिकम् वा (pādikam vā) — or a quarter;
ग्रहण-अन्तिकम् (grahaṇa-antikam) — until learning is complete
Transliteration
