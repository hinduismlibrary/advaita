Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 5: The Householder’s Duties and the Marks of Women

BP 1.5.1

सुमन्तुरुवाच
षट्त्रिंशदाब्दिकं चर्यं गुरौ त्रैवेदिकं व्रतम् ।
तदर्धिकं पादिकं वा ग्रहणान्तिकमेव च ॥ १

Sumantu said: The three-Veda vow of study with a guru is to be observed for thirty-six years, half that, a quarter, or until learning is complete.

BP 1.5.2

वेदानधीत्य वेदौ वा वेदं वापि नृपोत्तम ।
अविप्लुतब्रह्मचर्यो गृहस्थाश्रममावसेत् ॥ २

After studying three Vedas, two Vedas, or even one Veda, O best of kings, one whose brahmacarya is unbroken should enter the gṛhastha āśrama.

BP 1.5.3

तं प्रतीतं स्वधर्मेण ब्रह्मदायहरं पितुः ।
स्रग्विणं तल्प आसीनमर्हयेत्प्रथमं गवा ॥ ३

‘The father should first honour with a cow that graduate who is firmly established in his own dharma, inherits the treasury of sacred knowledge, wears a garland, and is seated upon a couch.’

BP 1.5.4

गुरुणा समनुज्ञातः समावृत्तौ यथाविधि ।
उद्वहेत द्विजो भार्यां सवर्णां लक्षणान्विताम् ॥ ४

Permitted by the guru at samāvartana, in due form, a twice-born should marry a wife of the same varṇa, endowed with auspicious marks.

BP 1.5.5

शतानीक उवाच
लक्षणं द्विजशार्दूल स्त्रीणां वद महामुने ।
कीदृग्लक्षणसंयुक्ता कन्या स्यात्सुखदा नृप ॥ ५

Śatānīka said: Tell me, O tiger among the twice-born, the marks of women, great sage. What marks make a maiden bring happiness, O king?

BP 1.5.6

सुमन्तुरुवाच
यदुक्तं ब्राह्मणा पूर्वं स्त्रीलक्षणमनुत्तमम् ।
श्रेयसे सर्वलोकानां शुभाशुभफलप्रदम् ॥ ६

Sumantu said: ‘I shall relate the unsurpassed teaching on women’s characteristics that Brahmā formerly proclaimed, which promotes the welfare of all the worlds and indicates auspicious and inauspicious results.’

BP 1.5.7

तत्ते वच्मि महाबाहो शृणुष्वैकमना नृप ।
श्रुतेन येन जानीषे कन्यां शोभनलक्षणाम् ॥ ७

That I tell you; listen with a single mind, O king, by hearing which you will know a maiden with auspicious marks.

BP 1.5.8

सुखासीनं सुरश्रेष्ठमभिगम्य महर्षयः ।
पप्रच्छुर्लक्षणं स्त्रीणां यत्पृष्टोऽहं त्वयाधुना ॥ ८

‘Long ago, the great seers approached the foremost of the gods while he was seated at ease and asked him about the characteristics of women—the very matter about which you have now questioned me.’

BP 1.5.9

प्रणम्य शिरसा देवमिदं वचनमब्रुवन् ।
भगवन्ब्रूहि नः सर्वं स्त्रीणां लक्षणमुत्तमम् ॥ ९

‘Bowing their heads to the god, they spoke these words: “Blessed Lord, tell us in full the supreme teaching concerning the characteristics of women—”’

BP 1.5.10

श्रेयसे सर्वलोकानां शुभाशुभफलप्रदम् ।
प्रशस्तामप्रशास्तां च जानीमो येन कन्यकाम् ॥.5.१०

‘“—which promotes the welfare of all the worlds and indicates auspicious and inauspicious results, so that we may distinguish a maiden who is commended from one who is not commended.”’

BP 1.5.11

तेषां तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा विरिञ्चो वाक्यमब्रवीत् ।
शृणुध्वं द्विजशार्दूला वच्मि युष्मास्वशेषतः ॥१

‘Hearing their words, Viriñci replied: “Listen, O tigers among the twice-born; I shall explain the matter to you without omission.”’

BP 1.5.12

प्रतिष्ठिततलौ सम्यग्रताम्भोजसमप्रभौ ।
ईदृशौ चरणौ धन्यौ योषितां भोगवर्धनौ ॥२

‘Feet whose soles are properly formed and shine like red lotuses are considered fortunate in women and are said to increase worldly enjoyment.’

BP 1.5.13

करालैरतिनिर्मासै रूक्षैरर्धशिरान्वितैः ।
दारिद्र्यं दुर्भगत्वं च प्राप्नुवन्ति न संशयः ॥३

‘Feet or toes that are misshapen, excessively fleshless, rough, and marked by prominent veins are said to bring poverty and misfortune; of this there is no doubt.’

BP 1.5.14

अङ्गुल्यः संहता वृत्ताः स्निग्(?)धाः सूक्ष्मनखास्तथा ।
कुर्वन्त्यत्यन्तमैश्वर्यं राजभावं च योषितः ॥४

‘Toes that are close together and rounded, with a smooth appearance and fine nails, are said to produce great prosperity and the condition of royalty for women.’

BP 1.5.15

ह्रस्वाः सुजीवितं ह्रस्वा विरला वित्तहानये ।
दारिद्र्यं मूलमग्नासु प्रेष्यं च पृथुलासु च ॥५

‘Short toes are associated with a comfortable life, while sparse or widely separated toes indicate loss of wealth. Toes sunken at the roots indicate poverty, and very broad ones servitude.’

BP 1.5.16

परस्परसमारूढैस्तनुभिर्वृत्तपर्वभिः ।
बहूनपि पतीन्हत्वा दासी भवति वै द्विजाः ॥६

‘The traditional teaching says that a woman whose toes overlap one another and are thin with rounded joints may lose several husbands and eventually become a servant, O twice-born sages.’

BP 1.5.17

अङ्गुष्ठोन्नतपर्वाणस्तुङ्गाग्राः कोमलान्विताः ।
रत्नकाञ्चनलाभाय विपरीता विपत्तये ॥७

‘Toes with raised joints, elevated tips, and softness are held to indicate the acquisition of jewels and gold; the opposite characteristics indicate adversity.’

BP 1.5.18

सुभगत्वं नखैः स्निग्धैराताम्रैश्च धनाद् यता ।
पुत्राः स्युरुन्नतैरेभिः सुसूक्ष्मैश्चापि राजता ॥८

‘Smooth nails of a coppery-red colour indicate good fortune and abundance of wealth. Raised and very fine nails are associated with sons and royal prosperity.’

BP 1.5.19

पाण्डुरैः स्फुटितै रूक्षैर्नीलैर्धूम्रैस्तथा खरैः ।
निःस्वता भवति स्त्रीणां पीतैश्चाभक्ष्यभक्षणम् ॥९

‘Pale, cracked, rough, blue, smoky, or coarse nails are said to indicate poverty in women; yellow nails are associated with eating what ought not to be eaten.’

BP 1.5.20

गुल्फाः स्निग्धाश्च वृत्ताश्च समारूढशिरास्तथा ।
यदि स्युर्नूपुरान्दध्युर्बान्धवाद्यैः समाप्नुयुः ॥.5.२०

‘If the ankles are smooth, rounded, and their veins well covered, women are said to wear anklets and receive prosperity through relatives and others.’

BP 1.5.21

अशिरा शरकाण्डाभाः सुवृत्ताल्पतनूरुहाः ।
जङ्घा कुर्वन्ति सौभाग्यं यानं च गजवाजिभिः ॥१

‘Calves without visible veins, resembling reed-stalks, well rounded, and bearing little hair are said to bring good fortune and travel by elephants and horses.’

BP 1.5.22

क्लिश्यते रोमजङ्घा स्त्री भ्रमत्युद्धतपिण्डिका ।
काकजङ्घा पतिं हन्ति वाचाटा कपिला च याः ॥२

A woman with hairy shanks suffers; one with raised calves wanders; a crow-legged, talkative or tawny woman kills her husband.

BP 1.5.23

जानुभिश्चैव मार्जारसिंहजान्वनुकारिभिः ।
श्रियमाप्य सुभाग्यत्वं प्राप्नुवन्ति सुतांस्तथा ॥३

‘Women whose knees resemble the knees of a cat or lion are said to attain prosperity and good fortune, and likewise to obtain sons.’

BP 1.5.24

घटाभैरध्वगा नार्यो निर्मांसैः कुलटा स्त्रियः ।
शिरालैरपि हिंस्राः स्युर्विश्लिष्टैर्धनवर्जिताः ॥४

‘Women with pot-like knees are described as travellers; those with fleshless knees as unchaste; those with prominent veins as violent; and those with separated or loose joints as deprived of wealth.’

BP 1.5.25

अत्यन्तकुटिलै रूक्षैः स्फुटिताग्रैर्गुडप्रभैः ।
अनेकजैस्तथा रोमैः केशैश्चापि तथाविधैः ॥५

‘Body hair that is extremely curled, rough, split at the tips, brown like jaggery, and grows several hairs from a single follicle—and head hair of the same kind—’

BP 1.5.26

अत्यन्तपिङ्गला नारी विषतुल्येति निश्चितम् ।
सप्ताहाभ्यन्तरे पापा पतिं हन्यान्न संशयः ॥६

‘—is said to mark a woman who is extremely tawny and as dangerous as poison. The traditional text claims that within seven days she may bring ruin upon her husband; of this it expresses no doubt.’

BP 1.5.27

हस्तिहस्तनिभैर्वृतै रम्भाभैः करभोपमैः ।
प्राप्नुवन्त्यूरुभिः शश्वत्स्त्रियः सुखमनङ्गजम् ॥७

‘Women whose thighs resemble elephant trunks, are rounded, resemble plantain stems, or are like the limbs of a young elephant are said continually to obtain the pleasures arising from love.’

BP 1.5.28

दौर्भाग्यं बद्धमांसैश्च बन्धनं रोमशोरुभिः ।
तनुभिर्वधमित्याहुर्मध्यच्छिद्रेष्वनीशता ॥८

Tightly fleshed thighs bring misfortune; hairy thighs, bondage; thin thighs, death; a gap between the thighs, lack of authority.

BP 1.5.29

सन्ध्यावर्णं समं चारु सूक्ष्मरोमान्वितं पृथु ।
जघनं शस्यते स्त्रीणां रतिसौख्यकरं द्विज ॥९

‘A woman’s hip and pelvic region is praised when it has an even, pleasing colour, is symmetrical and beautiful, bears fine hair, and is broad; such a form is said to promote sexual happiness, O twice-born sage.’

BP 1.5.30

अरोमको भगो यस्याः समः सुश्लिष्टसंस्थितः ।
अपि नीचकुलोत्पन्न राजपत्नी भवत्यसौ ॥.5.३०

‘A woman whose vulva is hairless, even, and closely formed is said to become the wife of a king even if she was born in a family of low standing.’

BP 1.5.31

अश्वत्थपत्रसदृशः कूर्मपृष्ठोन्नतस्तथा ।
शशिबिम्बनिभश्चापि तथैव कलशाकृतिः ॥
भगः शस्ततमः स्त्रीणां रतिसौभाग्यवर्धनः ॥१

‘A vulva resembling an aśvattha leaf, raised like a tortoise’s back, shaped like the disc of the moon, or formed like a fine vessel is most highly praised for women and is said to increase sexual pleasure and good fortune.’

BP 1.5.32

तिलपुष्पनिभो यश्च यद्यग्रे खुरसन्निभः ।
द्वावप्येतौ परप्रेष्यं कुर्वाते च दरिद्रताम् ॥२

‘One resembling a sesame flower, or one whose front resembles a hoof, is traditionally said to bring servitude to others and poverty.’

BP 1.5.33

उलूखलनिभैः शोकं मरणं विवृताननैः ।
विरूपैः पूतिनिर्मांसैर्गजसन्निभरोमभिः ॥
दौःशील्यं दुर्भगत्वं च दारिद्र्यमधिगच्छति ॥३

‘A mortar-like form is associated with grief, and a widely open mouth with death. A deformed, foul, fleshless form with hair like that of an elephant is said to lead to bad conduct, misfortune, and poverty.’

BP 1.5.34

कपित्थफलसंकाशः पीनो वलिविवर्जितः ।
स्फीताः प्रशस्यते स्त्रीणां निन्दितभ्रान्यथा द्विजाः ॥४

‘A pelvic or buttock region resembling the kapittha fruit, full, free from folds, and well developed is praised in women, O twice-born sages; a form otherwise constituted is censured.’

BP 1.5.35

पयोधरभरानम्रप्रचलत्त्रिवलीगुरुः ।
मध्यः शुभावहः स्त्रीणां रोमराजीविभूषितः ॥५

‘A woman’s waist that bends beneath the weight of the breasts, is graced by three moving folds, and is adorned by a central line of hair is said to bring auspiciousness.’

BP 1.5.36

पणवाभैर्मृदङ्गाभैस्तथा मध्ये यवोपमैः ।
प्राप्नुवन्ति भयावासक्लेशदौःशील्यमीदृशैः ॥६

‘Waists shaped like a paṇava drum or mṛdaṅga, or narrowed in the middle like a barley grain, are said to bring fear, insecure dwelling, suffering, and bad conduct.’

BP 1.5.37

अवक्रानुल्बणं पृष्ठमरोमशमगर्हितम् ।
नानास्तरणपर्यङ्करतिसौख्यकरं परम् ॥७

‘A back that is straight, not excessively raised, hairless, and free from defects is said to bring the highest enjoyment of varied coverings, couches, and love.’

BP 1.5.38

कुब्जमद्रोणिकं पृष्ठं रोमशं यदि योषितः ।
स्वप्नान्तरे सुखं तस्या नास्ति हन्यात्पतिं च सा ॥८

If a woman’s back is hunched, hollowed or hairy, she has no happiness even in dreams; she kills her husband.

BP 1.5.39

विपुलैःसुकुमारैश्च कुक्षिभिः सुबहुप्रजाः ।
मण्डूककुक्षिर्या नारी राजानं सा प्रसूयते ॥९

‘Women with broad and delicate abdomens are said to have many children. A woman whose abdomen resembles that of a frog is said to give birth to a king.’

BP 1.5.40

उन्नतैर्बलिभिर्वध्याः सुवृत्तैः कुलटा स्त्रियः ।
जारकर्मरतास्ताः स्युः प्रव्रज्यां च समाप्नुयुः ॥.5.४०

Raised abdominal folds bring death; rounded folds mark an unchaste woman. Such women engage in illicit unions and attain renunciation.

BP 1.5.41

उन्नता च नतैः क्षुद्रा विषमैर्विषमाशया ।
आयुरैश्वर्यसम्पन्ना वनिता हृदयैः समैः ॥१

A raised heart-region indicates pride, a sunken one lowliness, and an uneven one an uneven disposition; a woman with an even heart-region is endowed with longevity and prosperity.

BP 1.5.42

सुवृत्तमुन्नतं पीनमदूरोन्नतमायतम् ।
स्तनयुग्ममिदं शस्तमतोऽन्यदसुखावहम् ॥२

‘A pair of breasts that is well rounded, raised, full, not excessively prominent, and extended in proper proportion is praised; a form different from this is said to bring unhappiness.’

BP 1.5.43

उन्नतिः प्रथमे गर्भे द्वयोरेकस्य भूयसी ।
वामे तु जायते कन्या दक्षिणे तु भवेत्सुतः ॥३

‘During a first pregnancy, if one of the two breasts becomes more elevated, the traditional teaching predicts a daughter when the left is higher and a son when the right is higher.’

BP 1.5.44

दीर्घे तुचूचुके यस्याः सा स्त्री धूर्ता रतिप्रिया ।
सुवृत्ते तु पुनर्यस्या द्वेष्टि सा पुरुषं सदा ॥४

‘A woman with long nipples is described as cunning and fond of sexual pleasure, while one whose nipples are very round is said continually to dislike men.’

BP 1.5.45

स्तनैः सर्पफणाकारैः श्वजिह्वाकृतिभिस्तथा ।
दारिद्र्यमधिगच्छन्ति स्त्रियः पुरुषचेष्टिताः ॥
अवष्टब्धघटीतुल्या भवन्ति हि तथा द्विजाः ॥५

Women with breasts shaped like serpent hoods or dog tongues attain poverty and display manly conduct; those like supported jars are likewise described, O twice-born.

BP 1.5.46

सुसमं मांसलं चारु शिरो रोमविवर्जितम् ।
वक्षो यस्या भवेन्नार्या भोगान्भुक्ते यथेप्सितान् ॥६

‘A woman whose chest is symmetrical, fleshy, beautiful, free of prominent veins, and hairless is said to enjoy whatever pleasures she desires.’

BP 1.5.47

हिंस्रा भवति वक्रेण दौःशील्यं रोमशेन तु ।
निर्मांसेन तु वैधव्यं विस्तीर्णे कलहप्रिया ॥७

A crooked chest makes [her] violent; a hairy one brings bad conduct; a fleshless one, widowhood; a broad one, fondness for quarrels.

BP 1.5.48

चतस्रो रक्तगम्भीरा रेखाः स्निग्धाः करे स्त्रियाः ।
यदि स्युः सुखमाप्नोति विच्छिन्नाभिरनीशता ॥८

If a woman’s palm has four red, deep and smooth lines, she obtains happiness; with broken lines, she lacks control.

BP 1.5.49

रेखाः कनिष्ठिकामूलाद्यस्याः प्राप्ताः प्रदेशिनीम् ।
शतमायुर्भवेत्तस्यास्त्रयाणामुन्नतौ क्रमात् ॥९

‘When lines extend from the base of the little finger to the forefinger, the woman is said to have a lifespan of one hundred years; three progressively rising lines are likewise treated as auspicious.’

BP 1.5.50

संवृत्ताः समपर्वाणस्तीक्ष्णाग्राः कोमलत्वचः ।
समा ह्यंगुलयो यस्याः सा नारी भोगवर्धिनी ॥.5.५०

‘A woman whose fingers are rounded, have even joints, sharp tips, soft skin, and equal proportions is said to increase enjoyment.’

BP 1.5.51

बन्धुजीवारुणैस्तुंगैर्नखैरैश्वर्यमाप्नुयात् ।
खरैर्वक्रैर्विवर्णाभैः श्वेतप्रीतैरनीशता ॥१

‘Raised nails red like the bandhujīva flower are said to bring prosperity. Coarse, crooked, discoloured, white, or yellowish nails are associated with powerlessness.’

BP 1.5.52

रक्तैर्मृदुभिरैश्वर्यं निश्छिद्राङ्गुलिभिर्द्विजाः ।
स्फुटितैर्विषमै रूक्षैः क्लेशं पाणिभिराप्नुयुः ॥२

‘Red and soft palms with fingers that close without gaps are said to indicate prosperity, O twice-born sages; cracked, uneven, and rough hands indicate suffering.’

BP 1.5.53

समरेखा यवा यासाङ्गुष्ठाङ्गुलिपर्वसु ।
तासां हि विपुलं सौख्यं धनं धान्यं तथाऽक्षयम् ॥३

‘Women whose thumb and finger joints bear well-aligned barley-shaped marks are said to possess abundant happiness and inexhaustible wealth and grain.’

BP 1.5.54

मणिबन्धोऽव्यवच्छिन्नो रेखात्रयविभूषितः ।
ददाति न चिरादेव भोगमायुस्तथाक्षयम् ॥४

‘A wrist that is unbroken in contour and adorned by three lines is said quickly to bestow enjoyment and imperishable longevity.’

BP 1.5.55

श्रीवत्सध्वजपद्माक्षगजवाजिनिवेशनैः ।
चक्रस्वस्तिकवज्रासिपूर्णकुम्भनिभाङ्कुशैः ॥५

‘Marks resembling the Śrīvatsa, banner, lotus-eye, elephant, horse, dwelling, discus, svastika, thunderbolt, sword, full vessel, and elephant-goad—’

BP 1.5.56

प्रासादच्छत्रमुकुटैर्हारकेयूरकुण्डलैः ।
शङ्खतोरणनिर्व्यूहैर्हस्तन्यस्तैर्नृपस्त्रियः ॥६

‘—as well as palace, parasol, crown, necklace, armlet, earrings, conch, gateway, and architectural projections, when found upon the hands, are said to mark women who become queens.’

BP 1.5.57

यस्याः पाणितले रक्ता यूपकुम्भाश्च कुण्डिकाः ।
दृश्यन्ते चरणे यस्या यज्ञपली भवत्यसौ ॥७

‘A woman upon whose red palm are seen signs of a sacrificial post, vessel, and water-pot—and upon whose feet such signs appear—is said to become the wife of a sacrificer.’

BP 1.5.58

वीथ्यापणतुलामानैस्तथा मुद्रादिभिः स्त्रियः ।
भवन्ति वणिजां पत्न्यो रत्नकाञ्चनशालिनाम् ॥८

‘Women whose hands bear signs of streets, shops, balances, measures, seals, and similar objects are said to become wives of merchants who possess jewels and gold.’

BP 1.5.59

दात्रयोक्त्रयुगाबन्धफलोलूखललाङ्गलैः ।
भवन्ति धनधान्याढ्या कृषीवलजनाङ्गनाः ।९

‘Signs of sickles, ropes, yokes, bindings, fruit, mortars, and ploughs are said to mark women of agricultural households who become rich in wealth and grain.’

BP 1.5.60

अनुन्नतशिरासन्धि पीनं रोमविवर्जितम् ।
गोपुच्छाकृति नारीणां भुजयोर्युगुलं शुभम् ॥.5.६०

‘A pair of arms without prominent veins or joints, full, hairless, and shaped like a cow’s tail is considered auspicious for women.’

BP 1.5.61

निगूढग्रन्थयो यस्याः कूर्परौ रोमवर्जितौ ।
बाहू वै ललितौ यस्याः प्रशस्तौ वृत्तकोमलौ ॥१

‘A woman whose joints are well concealed and whose elbows are hairless, and whose arms are graceful, rounded, and soft, is described as possessing praiseworthy limbs.’

BP 1.5.62

उन्नतावनतौ चैव नातिस्थूलौ न रोमशौ ।
सुखदौ तु सदा स्त्रीणां सौभाग्यारोग्यवर्धनौ ॥२

‘Arms or shoulders that are neither raised nor drooping, neither excessively thick nor hairy, are said always to bring women happiness and to increase good fortune and health.’

BP 1.5.63

स्थूले स्कन्धे वहेद्भारं रोमशे व्याधिता भवेत् ।
वक्रस्कन्धे भवेद्वन्ध्या कुलटा चोन्नतानने ॥३

With heavy shoulders she bears burdens; with hairy shoulders she is ill; with crooked shoulders she is barren; a woman with an uplifted face is unchaste.

BP 1.5.64

स्पष्टं रेखात्रयं यस्या ग्रीवायां चतुरङ्गुलम् ।
मणिकाञ्चनमुक्ताढ्यं सा दधाति विभूषणम् ॥४

‘A woman whose neck clearly bears three lines across a span of four fingers is said to wear ornaments rich in jewels, gold, and pearls.’

BP 1.5.65

अधना स्त्री कृशग्रीवा दीर्घग्रीवा च .बन्धकी ।
हस्वग्रीवा मृतापत्या स्थूलग्रीवा च दुःखिता ॥५

‘A thin-necked woman is said to be poor, a long-necked one unchaste, a short-necked one bereft of children, and a thick-necked one afflicted by sorrow.’

BP 1.5.66

अनुन्नता समांसा च समा यस्याः कृकाटिका ।
सुदीर्घमायुस्त्वस्यास्तु चिरं भर्ता च जीवति ॥६

‘A woman whose nape is not raised, is evenly fleshed, and symmetrical is said to have a very long life, while her husband also lives for a long time.’

BP 1.5.67

निर्मांसा बहुमांसा च शिराला रोमशा तथा ।
कुटिला विकटा चैव विस्तीर्णा न च शस्यते ॥७

‘A neck or nape that is fleshless, excessively fleshy, veiny, hairy, crooked, misshapen, or very broad is not praised.’

BP 1.5.68

न स्थूलो न कृशोऽत्यर्थं न वक्रो न च रोमशः ।
हनुरेवंविधः श्रेयांस्ततोऽन्यो न प्रशस्यते ॥८

‘A chin that is neither thick nor excessively thin, neither crooked nor hairy, is considered excellent; a form different from this is not praised.’

BP 1.5.69

चतुरस्रमुखी धूर्ता मण्डलास्या शिवा भवेत् ।
अप्रजा वाजिवक्रा स्त्री महावक्रा च दुर्भगा ॥९

‘A square-faced woman is described as cunning, while a round-faced one is considered auspicious. A horse-faced woman is said to be childless, and one with a very large face unfortunate.’

BP 1.5.70

श्ववराहवृकोलूकमर्कटास्याश्च याः स्त्रियः ।
कूरास्ताः पापकर्मिण्यः प्रजाबान्धववर्जिताः ॥.5.७०

Women whose faces are like those of dogs, boars, wolves, owls or monkeys are cruel, sinful, and without children or relatives.

BP 1.5.71

मालतीबकुलाम्भोजनीलोत्पलसुगन्धि यत् ।
वदनं मुच्यते नैतत्पानताम्बूलभोजनैः ॥ ७१

‘A mouth naturally fragrant like mālatī, bakula, lotus, or blue lotus is praised when that fragrance is not lost through drinking, chewing betel, or eating.’

BP 1.5.72

ताम्राभः किञ्चिदालम्भः स्थौल्यकार्श्यविवर्जितः ।
अधरो यदि तुङ्गश्च नारीणां भोजदः सदा ॥ ७२

‘A lower lip that is coppery, slightly pendant, neither thick nor thin, and gently raised is said always to confer food and enjoyment upon a woman.’

BP 1.5.73

स्थूले कलहशीला स्याद्विवर्णे चातिदुःखिता ।
उत्तरोष्ठेन तीक्ष्णेन वनिता चातिकोपना ॥ ७३

‘A thick lip is associated with a quarrelsome disposition, and a discoloured one with great suffering. A woman with a sharply formed upper lip is said to be exceedingly irascible.’

BP 1.5.74

जिह्वा तनुतरा वक्रा ताम्रा दीर्घा च शस्यते ।
स्थूला ह्रस्वा विवर्णा या वक्रा भिन्ना च निन्दिता ॥ ७४

‘A tongue that is slender, gently curved, coppery, and long is praised; one that is thick, short, discoloured, crooked, or split is censured.’

BP 1.5.75

शङ्खकुन्देन्दुधवलं स्निग्धैस्तुङ्गैरसन्धिभिः ।
मिष्टान्नपानमाप्नोति दन्तैरेभिरनुन्नतैः ॥ ७५

‘Teeth white like a conch, jasmine, or the moon, smooth, well raised, close-set, and even are said to bring sweet foods and drinks.’

BP 1.5.76

सूक्ष्मैरतिकृशैर्ह्रस्वैः स्फुटितैर्विरलैस्तथा ।
रूक्षश्च दुःखिता नित्यं विकटैर्भामिनी भवेत् ॥ ७६

‘A woman whose teeth are excessively fine or thin, short, cracked, sparse, rough, or irregular is said continually to experience unhappiness.’

BP 1.5.77

सुमृष्टदर्पणाम्भोजपूर्णबिम्बेन्दुसन्निभम् ।
वदनं वरनारीणामभीष्टफलदं स्मृतम् ॥ ७७

‘The face of an excellent woman, resembling a polished mirror, a lotus, or the complete disc of the full moon, is remembered as bestowing desired results.’

BP 1.5.78

न स्थूला न कृशा वक्रा नातिदीर्घा समुन्नता ।
ईदृशी नासिका यस्याः सा धन्या तु शुभङ्करी ॥ ७८

‘A woman whose nose is neither thick, thin, crooked, nor excessively long, but is evenly raised, is considered fortunate and auspicious.’

BP 1.5.79

उन्नता मृदुला या च रेखा शुद्धा न सङ्गन्ता ।
भ्रूर्वक्रतुल्या सूक्ष्मा च योषितां सा सुखावहा ॥ ७९

‘An eyebrow forming a raised, soft, clean, unjoined line, finely curved like a bow, is said to bring happiness to women.’

BP 1.5.80

धनुस्तुल्याभिः सौभाग्यं वन्ध्या स्याद्दीर्घरोमभिः ।
पिङ्गलासङ्गता ह्रस्वा दारिद्र्याय न संशयः ॥.5.८०

Bow-shaped eyebrows bring good fortune; long eyebrow hairs make [a woman] barren; tawny, joined or short eyebrows bring poverty, without doubt.

BP 1.5.81

नीलोत्पलदलप्रख्यैराताम्रैश्चारुपक्ष्मभिः ।
वनिता नयनैरेभिर्भोगसौभाग्यभागिनी ॥ ८१

‘A woman whose eyes resemble blue-lotus petals, are slightly coppery, and possess beautiful eyelashes is said to share in enjoyment and good fortune.’

BP 1.5.82

खञ्जनाक्षी मृगाक्षी च वराहाक्षी वराङ्गना ।
यत्रयत्र समुत्पन्ना महान्तं भोगमश्नुते ॥ ८२

‘A beautiful woman with eyes like the khañjana bird, a deer, or a boar is said to experience great enjoyment wherever she is born.’

BP 1.5.83

अगम्भीरैरसंश्लिष्टैर्बहुरेखाविभूषितैः ।
राजपन्त्यो भवन्तीह नयनैर्मधुपिङ्गलैः ॥ ८३

‘Women with shallow, well-separated eyes adorned by many fine lines and coloured like honey are said here to become royal wives.’

BP 1.5.84

वायसाकृतिनेत्राणि दीर्घापाङ्गानि योषिताम् ।
अनाविलानि चारूणि भवन्ति हि विभूतये ॥ ८४

‘Eyes shaped like those of a crow, with long outer corners, clear and beautiful, are said to bring prosperity to women.’

BP 1.5.85

गम्भीरैः पिङ्गलैश्चैव दुःखिताः स्युश्चिरायुषः ।
वयोमध्ये त्यजेत्प्राणानुन्नताक्षी तु२ याङ्गना ॥ ८५

‘Women with deep, tawny eyes are said to experience sorrow yet live long; a woman with protruding or raised eyes is said to die in middle age.’

BP 1.5.86

रक्ताक्षी विषमाक्षी चधूम्राक्षी प्रेतलोचना ।
वर्जनीया सदा नारी श्वनेत्रा चैव दूरतः ॥ ८६

A red-eyed, uneven-eyed, smoke-eyed, corpse-eyed or dog-eyed woman should always be avoided from afar.

BP 1.5.87

उद्भ्रान्तकैः करैश्चित्रैर्नयनस्त्वंगनास्त्विह ।
मद्यमांसप्रिया नित्यं चपलाश्चैव सर्वतः ॥ ८७

‘Women whose eyes are restless, varied in colour, and constantly wandering are described as continually fond of alcohol and meat and as fickle in every respect.’

BP 1.5.88

करालाकृतयः कर्णा नभःशब्दास्तु संस्थिताः ।
वहन्ति विकसत्कान्तिं हेमरत्नविभूषणम् ॥ ८८

‘Ears of a striking or formidable shape, with their inner formation open like space, are said to carry expanding beauty and ornaments of gold and jewels.’

BP 1.5.89

खरोष्ट्रनकुलोलूककपिलश्रवणाः स्त्रियः ।
प्राप्नुवन्ति महद्दुःखं प्रायशः प्रव्रजन्ति च ॥ ८९

‘Women whose ears resemble those of an ass, camel, mongoose, or owl, or are tawny, are said to undergo great sorrow and often to wander or enter a life of renunciation.’

BP 1.5.90

ईषदापाण्डुगण्डा या सुवृत्ता पर्वणि त्विह ।
प्रशस्ता निन्दिता त्वन्या रोमकूपकदूषिता ॥.5.९०

‘A woman whose cheeks are slightly pale, smoothly rounded, and properly formed at their contours is praised; another whose cheeks are marred by conspicuous hair follicles is censured.’

BP 1.5.91

अर्धेन्दुप्रतिमाभोगमरोम तु समाहितम ।
भोगारोग्यकरं श्रेष्ठं ललाटं वरयोषिताम् ॥ ९१

‘A forehead with an expanse resembling a half moon, hairless and evenly composed, is considered excellent for beautiful women and is said to bring enjoyment and health.’

BP 1.5.92

द्विगुणं परिणाहेन ललाटं विहितं च यत् ।
शिरः प्रशस्तं नारीणामधन्या हस्तिमस्तका ॥ ९२

‘A forehead whose breadth and circumference possess the prescribed twofold proportion, together with a well-formed head, is praised for women; a woman with an elephant-like head is considered unfortunate.’

BP 1.5.93

सूक्ष्माः कृष्णा मृदुस्निग्धाः कुञ्जिताग्राः शिरोरुहाः ।
भवन्ति श्रेयसे स्त्रीणामन्ये स्युः क्लेशशोकदाः ॥ ९३

‘Fine, black, soft, glossy hair whose tips curl is said to promote women’s welfare; hair of contrary qualities is said to bring suffering and sorrow.’

BP 1.5.94

हंसकोकिलवीणालिशिखिवेणुस्वराः स्त्रियः ।
प्राप्नुवन्ति बहून्भोगान्भृत्यानाज्ञापयन्ति च ॥ ९४

‘Women whose voices resemble a swan, cuckoo, vīṇā, bee, peacock, or flute are said to obtain many enjoyments and to command servants.’

BP 1.5.95

भिन्नकांस्यस्वरा नारी खरकाकस्वरा च या ।
रोगं व्याधिं भयं शोकं दारिद्र्यं चाधिगच्छति ॥ ९५

‘A woman whose voice resembles cracked bronze, an ass, or a crow is said to encounter illness, disease, fear, sorrow, and poverty.’

BP 1.5.96

हंसगोवृषचक्राह्वमत्तमातङ्गगामिनी ।
स्वकुलं द्योतयेन्नारी महिषी पार्थिवस्य च ॥ ९६

‘A woman whose gait resembles a swan, cow, bull, cakrāhva bird, or intoxicated elephant is said to illuminate her lineage and to become the chief queen of a ruler.’

BP 1.5.97

श्वशृगालगतिर्निन्द्या या च वायसवद्व्रजेत् ।
दासी मृगगतिर्नारी द्रुतगामी च बन्धकी ॥ ९७

A gait like a dog’s or jackal’s is censured, as is walking like a crow. A woman with a deer’s gait is a servant; one who walks quickly is unchaste.

BP 1.5.98

फलिनी रोचना हेमकुङ्कुमप्रभ एव च ।
वर्णः शुभकरः स्त्रीणां यश्च दूर्वाङ्कुरोपमः ॥ ९८

‘A complexion resembling the pigment rocanā, gold, saffron, a ripened fruit, or the fresh shoot of dūrvā grass is considered auspicious for women.’

BP 1.5.99

मृदूनि मृदुरोमाणि नात्यन्तस्वेदकानि च ।
सुरभीणि च गात्राणि यासां ताः पूजिताः स्त्रियः ॥ ९९

‘Women whose limbs are soft, whose body hair is delicate, whose bodies do not perspire excessively, and who are naturally fragrant are said to be honoured.’

BP 1.5.100

नोद्वहेत्कपिलां कन्यां नाधिकाङ्गीं न रोगिणीम् ।
नालोमिकां नातिह्रस्वां न वाचाटां न पिङ्गलाम् ॥.5.१००

One should not marry a tawny maiden, one with an extra limb, a sick woman, one without body hair, one excessively short, talkative or tawny.

BP 1.5.101

नर्क्षवृक्षनदीनाम्नीं नान्त्यपर्वतनामिकाम् ।
न पक्ष्यहिप्रेष्यनाम्नीं न च भीषणनामिकाम् ॥०१

One should not marry a maiden named after a constellation, tree, river, distant mountain, bird, serpent or servant, or with a frightening name.

BP 1.5.102

अव्यङ्गाङ्गीं सौम्यनाम्नीं हंसवारणगामिनीम् ।
तनुलोमकेशदशनां मृदङ्गीमुद्वहेत्स्त्रियम् ॥०२

‘One should marry a woman whose limbs are without defect, whose name is gentle, whose gait resembles a swan or elephant, whose body hair, head hair, and teeth are fine, and whose limbs are soft.’

BP 1.5.103

महान्त्यपि समृद्धानि गोजाविधनधान्यतः ।
स्त्रीसम्बन्धे दशैतानि कुलानि परिवर्जयेत् ॥०३

‘Even if they are great and prosperous in cattle, goats, sheep, wealth, and grain, these ten families should be avoided when forming a marriage alliance.’

BP 1.5.104

हीनक्रियं निष्पुरुषं निश्छन्दोरोमशार्शसम् ।
क्षयामयाव्यपस्मारिश्वित्रकुष्ठिकुलानि च ॥०४

‘They are families deficient in sacred observance, lacking male descendants, without Vedic recitation, excessively hairy or afflicted by piles, and families marked by wasting disease, chronic illness, epilepsy, leucoderma, or leprosy.’

BP 1.5.105

पादौ सुगुल्फौ प्रथमं प्रतिष्ठौ जङ्घे द्वितीयं च सुजानुचक्रे ।
मेढ्रोरुगुह्यं च ततस्तृतीयं नाभिः कटिश्चेति चतुर्थमाहुः ॥०५

‘The feet with shapely ankles constitute the first division; the shanks with well-formed knees the second; the generative organ, thighs, and private region the third; and the navel and waist are called the fourth.’

BP 1.5.106

उदरं कथयन्ति पञ्चमं हृदयं षष्ठमथ स्तनान्वितम् ।
अथ सप्तममंसजत्रुणी कथयन्त्यष्टममोष्ठकन्धरे ॥०६

‘They call the abdomen the fifth division and the heart together with the breasts the sixth; the shoulders and collarbones are said to be the seventh, and the lips and neck the eighth.’

BP 1.5.107

नवमं नयने च सभ्रुणी सललाटं दशमं शिरस्तथा ।
अशुभेष्वशुभं दशाफलं चरणं चरणाद्यशुभेषु शोभनम् ॥०७

‘The eyes together with the eyebrows form the ninth, and the forehead together with the head the tenth. Among inauspicious features the result of the ten divisions is inauspicious, while among auspicious features the result beginning with the feet is auspicious.’

BP 1.5.108

इदं महात्मा स महानुभावः शचीनिमित्तं गुरुरब्रवीद्द्विजाः ।
शक्रेण पृष्टः सविशेषमुत्तमं संलक्ष्यमुक्तं वरयोषलक्षणम् ॥०८

‘O twice-born sages, the great and illustrious preceptor related this excellent and detailed teaching concerning Śacī; questioned by Śakra, he explained the marks of an excellent woman.’

BP 1.5.109

मत्सकाशात्पुनः श्रुत्वा लक्षणं पुरुषस्य च ।
यथाधुना भवद्भिस्तु श्रुतं मत्तो द्विजोत्तमाः ॥०९

‘Having then heard from me the marks of a man, O best of the twice-born, you have now heard them just as they were related by me.’

BP 1.5.110

लक्षणेभ्यः प्रशस्तं तु स्त्रीणां सद्वृत्तमुच्यते ।
सद्वृत्तमुक्त्वा या स्त्री सा प्रशस्ता न च लक्षणैः ॥.5.११०

‘For women, good conduct is declared more praiseworthy than bodily marks. A woman endowed with good conduct is praised for that, and not merely for her marks.’

BP 1.5.111

ईदृग्लक्षणसम्पन्नां सुकन्यामुद्वहेत्तु यः ।
ऋद्धिर्वृद्धिस्तथा कीर्तिस्तत्र तिष्ठति नित्यशः ॥११

‘For the man who marries a good maiden endowed with such qualities, prosperity, increase, and fame remain there perpetually.’
