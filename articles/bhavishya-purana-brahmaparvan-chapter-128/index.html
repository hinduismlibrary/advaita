Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 128 — The Sun’s Chief Hymn

BP 1.128.1

सुमन्तुरुवाच। अस्तावीच्च तत साम्बः कृशो धमनिसन्ततः। राजन्नामसहस्रेण सहस्रांशुं दिवाकरम् ॥

Sumantu said: Then Sāmba, thin and with his veins showing, praised the thousand-rayed Sun, the maker of day, with a thousand names, O king.

सुमन्तुरुवाच (sumanturuvāca) — Sumantu said
अस्तावीच्च (astāvīcca) — then praised
तत (tata) — then
साम्बः (sāmbaḥ) — Sāmba
कृशः (kṛśaḥ) — emaciated
धमनिसन्ततः (dhamanisantataḥ) — with veins extended
राजन् (rājan) — O king
नामसहस्रेण (nāmasahasreṇa) — with a thousand names
सहस्रांशुम् (sahasrāṃśum) — the thousand-rayed one
दिवाकरम् (divākaram) — the maker of day

BP 1.128.2

खिद्यमानं ततो दृष्ट्वा सूर्यः कृष्णात्मजं तदा। स्वप्नेऽस्मै दर्शनं दत्त्वा पुनर्वचनमब्रवीत् ॥

Seeing Kṛṣṇa’s son distressed, the Sun then appeared to him in a dream and spoke to him again.

खिद्यमानम् (khidyamānam) — distressed
ततः (tataḥ) — then
दृष्ट्वा (dṛṣṭvā) — having seen
सूर्यः (sūryaḥ) — the Sun
कृष्णात्मजम् (kṛṣṇātmajam) — Kṛṣṇa’s son
तदा (tadā) — at that time
स्वप्ने (svapne) — in a dream
अस्मै (asmai) — to him
दर्शनम् (darśanam) — appearance
दत्त्वा (dattvā) — having given
पुनः (punaḥ) — again
वचनम् (vacanam) — words
अब्रवीत् (abravīt) — spoke

BP 1.128.3

श्रीसूर्य उवाच। साम्ब साम्ब महावाहो शृणु जाम्बवतीसुत। अलं नामसहस्रेण पठ चेमं शुभं स्तवम् ॥

The Sun said: “Sāmba, Sāmba, mighty-armed one, listen, son of Jāmbavatī. Enough with the thousand names; recite this auspicious hymn.”

श्रीसूर्य उवाच (śrīsūrya uvāca) — Śrī Sūrya said
साम्ब (sāmba) — O Sāmba
महावाहो (mahāvāho) — O mighty-armed one
शृणु (śṛṇu) — listen
जाम्बवतीसुत (jāmbavatīsuta) — son of Jāmbavatī
अलम् (alam) — enough
नामसहस्रेण (nāmasahasreṇa) — with the thousand names
पठ (paṭha) — recite
च (ca) — and
इमम् (imam) — this
शुभम् (śubham) — auspicious
स्तवम् (stavam) — hymn

BP 1.128.4

यानि गुह्यानि नामानि पवित्राणि शुभानि च। तानि ते कीर्तयिष्यामि प्रयत्नादवधारय ॥

“I shall proclaim to you those names that are secret, pure, and auspicious. Attend to them carefully.”

यानि (yāni) — which
गुह्यानि (guhyāni) — secret
नामानि (nāmāni) — names
पवित्राणि (pavitrāṇi) — pure
शुभानि (śubhāni) — auspicious
च (ca) — and
तानि (tāni) — those
ते (te) — to you
कीर्तयिष्यामि (kīrtayiṣyāmi) — I shall proclaim
प्रयत्नात् (prayatnāt) — with care
अवधारय (avadhāraya) — attend

BP 1.128.5

वैकर्तनो विवस्वांश्च मार्तण्डो भास्करो रविः। लोकप्रकाशकः श्रीमांल्लोकचक्षुर्ग्रहेश्वरः ॥

“He is Vaikartana, Vivasvat, Mārtaṇḍa, Bhāskara, and the Sun; the illuminator of the worlds, glorious, the eye of the worlds, and lord of the planets.”

वैकर्तनः (vaikartanaḥ) — Vaikartana
विवस्वान् (vivasvān) — Vivasvat
च (ca) — and
मार्तण्डः (mārtaṇḍaḥ) — Mārtaṇḍa
भास्करः (bhāskaraḥ) — Bhāskara
रविः (raviḥ) — Ravi
लोकप्रकाशकः (lokaprakāśakaḥ) — illuminator of the worlds
श्रीमान् (śrīmān) — glorious
लोकचक्षुः (lokacakṣuḥ) — eye of the worlds
ग्रहेश्वरः (graheśvaraḥ) — lord of the planets

BP 1.128.6

लोकसाक्षी त्रिलोकेशः कर्ता हर्ता तमिस्रहा। तपनस्तापनश्चैव शुचिः सप्ताश्ववाहनः ॥

“He is the witness of the worlds, lord of the three worlds, maker and withdrawer, dispeller of darkness, heater and warmer, pure, and rider of seven horses.”

लोकसाक्षी (lokasākṣī) — witness of the worlds
त्रिलोकेशः (trilokeśaḥ) — lord of the three worlds
कर्ता (kartā) — creator
हर्ता (hartā) — withdrawer
तमिस्रहा (tamisrahā) — destroyer of darkness
तपनः (tapanaḥ) — heat-giver
तापनः (tāpanaḥ) — heater
च (ca) — and
शुचिः (śuciḥ) — pure
सप्ताश्ववाहनः (saptāśvavāhanaḥ) — rider of seven horses

BP 1.128.7

गभस्तिहस्तो ब्रह्मा च सर्वदेवनमस्कृतः। एकर्विंशतिरित्येष स्तव इष्टः सदा मम ॥

“He has rays for hands, is Brahmā, and is honored by all the gods. This hymn of twenty-one names is always dear to me.”

गभस्तिहस्तः (gabhastihastaḥ) — having rays for hands
ब्रह्मा (brahmā) — Brahmā
च (ca) — and
सर्वदेवनमस्कृतः (sarvadevanamaskṛtaḥ) — honored by all the gods
एकर्विंशतिः (ekarviṃśatiḥ) — twenty-one
इति (iti) — thus
एषः (eṣaḥ) — this
स्तवः (stavaḥ) — hymn
इष्टः (iṣṭaḥ) — dear
सदा (sadā) — always
मम (mama) — to me

BP 1.128.8

शरीरारोग्यदश्चैव धनवृद्धियशस्करः। स्तवराज इति ख्यातस्त्रिषु लोकेषु विश्रुतः ॥

“It gives bodily health, increase of wealth, and fame. It is known as the king of hymns and renowned in the three worlds.”

शरीरारोगदः (śarīrārogadaḥ) — giver of bodily health
च (ca) — and
एव (eva) — indeed
धनवृद्धियशस्करः (dhanavṛddhiyaśkaraḥ) — giver of wealth’s increase and fame
स्तवराजः (stavarājaḥ) — king of hymns
इति (iti) — thus
ख्यातः (khyātaḥ) — known
त्रिषु (triṣu) — in the three
लोकेषु (lokeṣu) — worlds
विश्रुतः (viśrutaḥ) — renowned

BP 1.128.9

य एतेन महाबाहो द्वे सन्ध्येऽस्तमनोदये। स्तौति मां प्रणतो भूत्वा सर्वपापैः प्रमुच्यते ॥

“Whoever, O mighty-armed one, praises me with this at the two twilights, sunset and sunrise, having bowed down, is freed from all sins.”

यः (yaḥ) — whoever
एतेन (etena) — with this
महाबाहो (mahābāho) — O mighty-armed one
द्वे (dve) — two
सन्ध्ये (sandhye) — twilights
अस्तमनोदये (astamanodaye) — at sunset and sunrise
स्तौति (stauti) — praises
माम् (mām) — me
प्रणतः (praṇataḥ) — bowed down
भूत्वा (bhūtvā) — having become
सर्वपापैः (sarvapāpaiḥ) — from all sins
प्रमुच्यते (pramucyate) — is freed

BP 1.128.10

मानसं वाचिकं वापि कायिकं यच्च दुष्कृतम्। एकजाप्येन तत्सर्वं प्रणश्यति ममाग्रतः ॥

“Whatever wrongdoing there is, mental, verbal, or bodily, all of it perishes before me through a single recitation.”

मानसम् (mānasam) — mental
वाचिकम् (vācikam) — verbal
वा (vā) — or
अपि (api) — also
कायिकम् (kāyikam) — bodily
यत् (yat) — whatever
च (ca) — and
दुष्कृतम् (duṣkṛtam) — wrongdoing
एकजाप्येन (ekajāpyena) — by one recitation
तत् (tat) — that
सर्वम् (sarvam) — all
प्रणश्यति (praṇaśyati) — is destroyed
ममाग्रतः (mamāgrataḥ) — before me

BP 1.128.11

एष जप्यश्च होमश्च सन्ध्योपासनमेव च। बलिमन्त्रोऽर्घ्यमन्त्रोऽथ धूपमन्त्रस्तथैव च ॥

“This is to be recited, offered in the fire, and used in twilight worship; it is the formula for an offering, the formula for water-offering, and likewise the formula for incense.”

एषः (eṣaḥ) — this
जप्यः (japyaḥ) — to be recited
च (ca) — and
होमः (homaḥ) — fire offering
सन्ध्योपासनम् (sandhyopāsanam) — twilight worship
एव (eva) — indeed
बलिमन्त्रः (balimantraḥ) — mantra for bali
अर्घ्यमन्त्रः (arghyamantraḥ) — mantra for arghya
अथ (atha) — then
धूपमन्त्रः (dhūpamantraḥ) — mantra for incense
तथा (tathā) — likewise
एव (eva) — indeed
च (ca) — and

BP 1.128.12

अन्नप्रदाने स्नाने च प्रणिपाते प्रदक्षिणे। पूजितोऽयं महामन्त्रः सर्वपापहरः शुभः ॥

“In giving food, bathing, bowing down, and walking around, this great auspicious formula is worshipped as the remover of all sins.”

अन्नप्रदाने (annapradāne) — in giving food
स्नाने (snāne) — in bathing
च (ca) — and
प्रणिपाते (praṇipāte) — in prostration
प्रदक्षिणे (pradakṣiṇe) — in circumambulation
पूजितः (pūjitaḥ) — worshiped
अयम् (ayam) — this
महामन्त्रः (mahāmantraḥ) — great mantra
सर्वपापहरः (sarvapāpaharaḥ) — remover of all sins
शुभः (śubhaḥ) — auspicious

BP 1.128.13

एवमुक्त्वा स भगवान्भास्करो जगतां पतिः। आमन्त्र्य कृष्णतनयं तत्रैवान्तर्हितोऽभवत् ॥

Having spoken thus, the blessed Bhāskara, lord of the worlds, took leave of Kṛṣṇa’s son and disappeared there.

एवम् (evam) — thus
उक्त्वा (uktvā) — having spoken
सः (saḥ) — he
भगवान् (bhagavān) — Blessed Lord
भास्करः (bhāskaraḥ) — Bhāskara
जगताम् (jagatām) — of the worlds
पतिः (patiḥ) — lord
आमन्त्र्य (āmantrya) — having taken leave
कृष्णतनयम् (kṛṣṇatanayam) — Kṛṣṇa’s son
तत्र (tatra) — there
एव (eva) — indeed
अन्तर्हितः (antarhitaḥ) — disappeared
अभवत् (abhavat) — became

BP 1.128.14

साम्बोऽपि स्तवराजेन स्तुत्वा सप्ताश्ववाहनम्। प्रीतात्मा नीरुजः श्रीमांस्तस्माद्रोगाद्विमुक्तवान् ॥

Sāmba too, having praised the seven-horse-borne Sun with the king of hymns, became joyful in heart, free from illness, glorious, and freed from that disease.

साम्बः (sāmbaḥ) — Sāmba
अपि (api) — also
स्तवराजेन (stavarājena) — with the king of hymns
स्तुत्वा (stutvā) — having praised
सप्ताश्ववाहनम् (saptāśvavāhanam) — the seven-horse vehicle
प्रीतात्मा (prītātmā) — joyful in heart
नीरुजः (nīrujaḥ) — free from illness
श्रीमान् (śrīmān) — glorious
तस्मात् (tasmāt) — from that
रोगात् (rogāt) — disease
विमुक्तवान् (vimuktavān) — became free
