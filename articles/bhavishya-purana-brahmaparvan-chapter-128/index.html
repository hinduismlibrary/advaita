Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 128 — The Sun’s Chief Hymn

BP 1.128.1

सुमन्तुरुवाच। अस्तावीच्च तत साम्बः कृशो धमनिसन्ततः। राजन्नामसहस्रेण सहस्रांशुं दिवाकरम् ॥

Sumantu said: Then Sāmba, thin and with his veins showing, praised the thousand-rayed Sun, the maker of day, with a thousand names, O king.

BP 1.128.2

खिद्यमानं ततो दृष्ट्वा सूर्यः कृष्णात्मजं तदा। स्वप्नेऽस्मै दर्शनं दत्त्वा पुनर्वचनमब्रवीत् ॥

Seeing Kṛṣṇa’s son distressed, the Sun then appeared to him in a dream and spoke to him again.

BP 1.128.3

श्रीसूर्य उवाच। साम्ब साम्ब महावाहो शृणु जाम्बवतीसुत। अलं नामसहस्रेण पठ चेमं शुभं स्तवम् ॥

The Sun said: “Sāmba, Sāmba, mighty-armed one, listen, son of Jāmbavatī. Enough with the thousand names; recite this auspicious hymn.”

BP 1.128.4

यानि गुह्यानि नामानि पवित्राणि शुभानि च। तानि ते कीर्तयिष्यामि प्रयत्नादवधारय ॥

“I shall proclaim to you those names that are secret, pure, and auspicious. Attend to them carefully.”

BP 1.128.5

वैकर्तनो विवस्वांश्च मार्तण्डो भास्करो रविः। लोकप्रकाशकः श्रीमांल्लोकचक्षुर्ग्रहेश्वरः ॥

“He is Vaikartana, Vivasvat, Mārtaṇḍa, Bhāskara, and the Sun; the illuminator of the worlds, glorious, the eye of the worlds, and lord of the planets.”

BP 1.128.6

लोकसाक्षी त्रिलोकेशः कर्ता हर्ता तमिस्रहा। तपनस्तापनश्चैव शुचिः सप्ताश्ववाहनः ॥

“He is the witness of the worlds, lord of the three worlds, maker and withdrawer, dispeller of darkness, heater and warmer, pure, and rider of seven horses.”

BP 1.128.7

गभस्तिहस्तो ब्रह्मा च सर्वदेवनमस्कृतः। एकर्विंशतिरित्येष स्तव इष्टः सदा मम ॥

“He has rays for hands, is Brahmā, and is honored by all the gods. This hymn of twenty-one names is always dear to me.”

BP 1.128.8

शरीरारोग्यदश्चैव धनवृद्धियशस्करः। स्तवराज इति ख्यातस्त्रिषु लोकेषु विश्रुतः ॥

“It gives bodily health, increase of wealth, and fame. It is known as the king of hymns and renowned in the three worlds.”

BP 1.128.9

य एतेन महाबाहो द्वे सन्ध्येऽस्तमनोदये। स्तौति मां प्रणतो भूत्वा सर्वपापैः प्रमुच्यते ॥

“Whoever, O mighty-armed one, praises me with this at the two twilights, sunset and sunrise, having bowed down, is freed from all sins.”

BP 1.128.10

मानसं वाचिकं वापि कायिकं यच्च दुष्कृतम्। एकजाप्येन तत्सर्वं प्रणश्यति ममाग्रतः ॥

“Whatever wrongdoing there is, mental, verbal, or bodily, all of it perishes before me through a single recitation.”

BP 1.128.11

एष जप्यश्च होमश्च सन्ध्योपासनमेव च। बलिमन्त्रोऽर्घ्यमन्त्रोऽथ धूपमन्त्रस्तथैव च ॥

“This is to be recited, offered in the fire, and used in twilight worship; it is the formula for an offering, the formula for water-offering, and likewise the formula for incense.”

BP 1.128.12

अन्नप्रदाने स्नाने च प्रणिपाते प्रदक्षिणे। पूजितोऽयं महामन्त्रः सर्वपापहरः शुभः ॥

“In giving food, bathing, bowing down, and walking around, this great auspicious formula is worshipped as the remover of all sins.”

BP 1.128.13

एवमुक्त्वा स भगवान्भास्करो जगतां पतिः। आमन्त्र्य कृष्णतनयं तत्रैवान्तर्हितोऽभवत् ॥

Having spoken thus, the blessed Bhāskara, lord of the worlds, took leave of Kṛṣṇa’s son and disappeared there.

BP 1.128.14

साम्बोऽपि स्तवराजेन स्तुत्वा सप्ताश्ववाहनम्। प्रीतात्मा नीरुजः श्रीमांस्तस्माद्रोगाद्विमुक्तवान् ॥

Sāmba too, having praised the seven-horse-borne Sun with the king of hymns, became joyful in heart, free from illness, glorious, and freed from that disease.
