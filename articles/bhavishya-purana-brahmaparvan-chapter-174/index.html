Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 174 — Praise of the Sun

BP 1.174.1

।। अरुण उवाच ।। पूजयित्वा रविं भक्त्या ब्रह्मा ब्रह्मत्वमागतः ।। विष्णुत्वं चापि देवेशो विष्णुराप तदर्चनात् ।। १ ।।

Aruṇa said: Having worshipped Ravi with devotion, Brahmā attained the state of Brahmā; and the lord of the gods, Viṣṇu, attained Viṣṇuhood through that worship.

BP 1.174.2

शंकरोऽपि जगन्नाथः पूजयित्वा दिवाकरम् ।। महादेवत्वमगमत्तत्प्रसादात्खगाधिप ।। २ ।।

Śaṅkara too, Lord of the World, having worshipped the Day-maker, attained the state of Mahādeva through his favor, O lord of birds.

BP 1.174.3

सहस्राक्षोपि देवेश इन्द्रो भानुं तमोपहम् ।। इन्द्रत्वमगमद्देवं पूजयित्वा दिवाकरम् ।। ३ ।।

Indra, the thousand-eyed lord of the gods, attained the state of Indra by worshipping Bhānu, the remover of darkness, the Day-maker.

BP 1.174.4

मातरो देवगंधर्वाः पिशाचोरगराक्षसाः ।। पूजयंति सदा भानुमीशानं सुरनायकम् ।। ४ ।।

Mothers, gods, celestial musicians, spirits, serpents, and demons always worship Bhānu, Īśāna, leader of the gods.

BP 1.174.5

सर्वमेतज्जगन्नित्यं भानौ देवे प्रतिष्ठितम् ।। तस्मात्संपूजयेद्भानुं य इच्छेत्स्वर्गमक्षयम् ।। ५ ।।

All this world is always established in the god Bhānu; therefore one who desires imperishable heaven should worship Bhānu completely.

BP 1.174.6

यो न पूजयते सूर्यं भास्करं तमसूदनम् ।। धर्मार्थकाममोक्षाणां न नरो भाजनं भवेत् ।। ६ ।।

A man who does not worship the Sun, Bhāskara, destroyer of darkness, is not a vessel for righteousness, wealth, desire, or liberation.

BP 1.174.7

तस्मात्कार्यं हि तद्ध्यानं यावज्जीवं प्रतिज्ञया ।। अर्चयेत सदा भानुमापन्नोऽपि सदा खग ।। ७ ।।

Therefore that meditation should indeed be undertaken by vow for as long as life lasts; one should always worship Bhānu, even when afflicted, O bird.

BP 1.174.8

यस्तु संतिष्ठते नित्यं विना सूर्यस्य पूजनात् ।। वरं प्राणपरित्यागः शिरसो वाथ च्छेदनम् ।। ८ ।।

One who always remains without worship of the Sun: better is giving up life, or even cutting off the head.

BP 1.174.9

सूर्यं संपूज्य भुञ्जीत त्रिदशेशं दिवाकरम् ।। इत्थं निर्वहते यस्य यावज्जीवं तदर्चनम् ।। मनुष्यचर्मणा नद्धः स रविर्नात्र संशयः ।। ९ ।।

Having worshipped the Sun, lord of the thirty gods, the Day-maker, one should eat. Whoever carries out that worship throughout life, enclosed in human skin, is Ravi himself; of this there is no doubt.

BP 1.174.10

न हि अर्कार्चनादन्यत्पुण्यमप्यधिकं भवेत् ।। इति विज्ञाय यत्नेन पूजयस्व दिवाकरम् ।। १० ।।

There is no greater merit than worship of Arka; knowing this, worship the Day-maker diligently.

BP 1.174.11

सूर्यभक्तागमाश्चैव सूर्यार्चनपरायणाः ।। संयता धर्मसंपन्ना धर्मादीन्साधयन्ति ते ।। ११ ।।

Those who follow the teachings of devotion to the Sun, intent on worship of the Sun, disciplined and endowed with righteousness, accomplish righteousness and the rest.

BP 1.174.12

सर्वद्वन्द्वसहा वीरा नीतिविध्युक्तचेतसः ।। परोपकारनिरता गुरुशुश्रूषणे रताः ।। १२ ।।

They are heroes, enduring all opposites, their minds guided by rules of conduct, devoted to helping others, intent on serving the teacher.

BP 1.174.13

अमानिनो बुद्धिमन्तोऽव्यक्तस्पर्धा गतस्पृहाः ।। शांता स्वांतगता भद्रा नित्यं स्वागतवादिनः ।। १३ ।।

Without conceit, intelligent, free from hidden rivalry, free of desire, peaceful, inward-minded, good, always speaking words of welcome.

BP 1.174.14

स्वल्पवाचः सुमनसः शूराः शास्त्रविशारदाः ।। शौचाचारसुसंपन्ना दयादाक्षिण्यगोचराः ।। १४ ।।

Few of speech, good-minded, brave, versed in śāstras, well endowed with purity and conduct, given to compassion and generosity.

BP 1.174.15

दंभमत्सरनिर्मुक्तास्तृष्णालोभविवर्जिताः ।। संविभागपराः प्रोक्ता न शठाश्चाप्यकुत्सिताः ।। १५ ।।

Freed from hypocrisy and envy, free from craving and greed, devoted to sharing, they are said to be neither deceitful nor blameworthy.

BP 1.174.16

विषयेष्वपि निर्लेपाः पद्मपत्रमिवांभसा ।। न दीना मानिनश्चैव न च रोगवशानुगाः ।। १६ ।।

Unattached even among sense-objects, like a lotus leaf by water; neither lowly nor conceited, and not subject to illness.

BP 1.174.17

भवंति भावितात्मानः सुस्निग्धाः साधुसेविताः ।। न पाणिपादवाक्चक्षुःश्रोत्रशिश्नोदरे रताः ।। १७ ।।

They are self-disciplined, very affectionate, served by the virtuous; they do not indulge the hands, feet, speech, eyes, ears, genitals, or belly.

BP 1.174.18

चपलानि न कुर्वंति सर्वव्यासंगवर्जिताः ।। सूर्यासनरताः शांताः षडक्षरमनोगताः ।। १८ ।।

They do not act restlessly, are free from all distractions, devoted to the Sun’s seat, peaceful, their minds fixed on the six syllables.

BP 1.174.19

इत्याचारसमायुक्ता भवंति भुवि मानवाः ।। एकांतभक्तिमास्थाय धर्मकामार्थसिद्धये ।। १९ ।।

Thus human beings on earth are endowed with conduct, having adopted one-pointed devotion for the fulfillment of righteousness, desire, and wealth.

BP 1.174.20

पूजनीयो रविर्नित्यं गुणेष्वेतेषु वर्तते ।। सर्वेषामेव पात्राणामतिपात्रं दिवाकरः ।। पतन्तं त्रायते यस्मादतीव नरकार्णवात् ।। २० ।।

Ravi, who abides in these qualities, should always be worshipped; among all recipients, the Day-maker is the most worthy, for he saves one falling into the great ocean of hell.

BP 1.174.21

तस्य पात्रातिपात्रस्य माहात्म्यं दानमण्वपि ।। अनेन फलमादिष्टमिहलोके परत्र च ।। २१ ।।

Such is the greatness of a gift, even a small one, to that most worthy recipient; by this the fruit is declared in this world and the next.

BP 1.174.22

द्रव्येणापि हि यः कुर्यान्नरः कर्म तदालये ।। सोऽपि देहक्षये ज्ञानं प्राप्य शांतिमवाप्नुयात् ।। २२ ।।

Whoever performs an act in that temple even with material means, at the end of the body obtains knowledge and attains peace.

BP 1.174.23

सर्वद्विजकदंबेषु कश्चिज्ज्ञानमवाप्नुयात् ।। कश्चिदेतत्तु मे दिव्यं लब्ध्वा ज्ञानं विमुञ्चति ।। २३ ।।

Among all assemblies of the twice-born, one may attain knowledge; another, having obtained this divine knowledge from me, is liberated.

BP 1.174.24

तावद्भ्रमंति संसारे दुःखशोकपरिप्लुताः ।। न भवंति रवेर्भक्ता यावत्सर्वेपि देहिनः ।। २४ ।।

They wander in the cycle of rebirth, filled with sorrow and grief, so long as all embodied beings do not become devotees of Ravi.

BP 1.174.25

सूर्यस्यालेपनं पुण्यं द्विगुणं चन्दनस्य तु ।। चन्दनादगुरौ ज्ञेयं पुण्यमष्टगुणोत्तरम् ।। २५ ।।

Anointing the Sun is meritorious; sandalwood is twice as meritorious; compared with sandalwood, the merit of aloeswood is known to be eightfold greater.

BP 1.174.26

कृष्णागुरौ विशेषेण द्विगुणं फलमिष्यते ।। तस्माच्छतगुण पुण्यं कुंकुमस्य विधीयते ।। २६ ।।

For black aloeswood especially, double fruit is desired; therefore a hundredfold merit is prescribed for saffron.

BP 1.174.27

सूर्ययज्ञोपकरणं कृत्वाल्पं यदि वा बहु ।। भावाद्वित्तानुसारेण सूर्यलोके महीयते ।। २७ ।।

Having made the implements for a Sun-sacrifice, whether little or much, according to devotion and one's means, one is honored in the Sun's world.

BP 1.174.28

यदपीष्टमनिष्टं च न्यायेनोभयमागतम् ।। तत्सूर्याय निवेद्यं सद्भक्त्यानन्तफलार्थिना ।। २८ ।।

Whatever is desired or undesired, both having come by right means, should be offered to the Sun with true devotion by one seeking endless fruit.

BP 1.174.29

कर्मशाठ्येन यः कुर्याद्दुःखेनापि तदर्चनम् ।। सोऽपि द्विजो दिवं याति कर्मणा पापवर्जितः ।। २९ ।।

Whoever performs that worship even with difficulty and guile in the act, that twice-born man too goes to heaven, freed from sin through the act.

BP 1.174.30

सर्वमन्यत्परित्यज्य सूर्ये चैकमना सदा ।। सूर्यपूजाविधिं कुर्याद्य इच्छेच्छ्रेय आत्मनः ।। ३० ।।

Abandoning everything else, always single-minded toward the Sun, one who desires his own welfare should perform the rite of Sun-worship.

BP 1.174.31

त्वरितं जीवितं याति त्वरितं यौवनं तथा ।। त्वरितं व्याधिरप्येति तस्मान्नित्यं रविं व्रजेत् ।। ३१ ।।

Life passes swiftly, youth too swiftly, illness too swiftly comes; therefore one should always turn to Ravi.

BP 1.174.32

यावन्नाभ्येति मरणं यावन्नाक्रमते जरा ।। यावन्नेंद्रिय वैकल्यं तावदर्चेद्दिवाकरम् ।। ३२ ।।

As long as death does not come, old age does not approach, and the senses are not impaired, so long should one worship the Day-maker.

BP 1.174.33

न सूर्यार्चनतुल्योपि न धर्मोन्यो जगत्त्रये ।। इत्थं विज्ञाय देवेशं पूजयस्व दिवाकरम् ।। ३३ ।।

There is no other righteousness in the three worlds equal to worship of the Sun; knowing this, worship the Day-maker, lord of the gods.

BP 1.174.34

ये भक्त्या देवदेवेशं सूर्यं शांतमजं प्रभुम् ।। इह लोके सुखं प्राप्य ते गताः परमं पदम् ।। ३४ ।।

Those who worship with devotion the Sun, Lord of the gods, peaceful, unborn, supreme, having attained happiness in this world, have gone to the highest state.

BP 1.174.35

गोपतिं पूजयित्वा तु प्रहृष्टेनांतरात्मना ।। कृतांजलिपुटो भूत्वा पुरा ब्रह्माब्रवीदिदम् ।। ३५ ।।

Having worshipped the Lord of Cows with a delighted inner self and palms joined, Brahmā formerly spoke this.

BP 1.174.36

।। ब्रह्मोवाच ।। भगवन्तं भगकरं शांतचित्तमनुत्तमम् ।। देवमार्गप्रणेतारं प्रणतोऽस्मि रविं सदा ।। ३६ ।।

Brahmā said: I always bow to Ravi, the giver of fortune, tranquil-minded, unsurpassed, leader of the path of the gods.

BP 1.174.37

शाश्वतं शोभनं शुद्धं चित्रभानुं दिवस्पतिम् ।। देवदेवेशमीशेशं प्रणतोऽस्मि दिवाकरम् ।। ३७ ।।

I bow to the Day-maker, eternal, beautiful, pure, many-hued in radiance, lord of the day, lord of the gods, lord of lords.

BP 1.174.38

सर्वदुःखहरं देवं सर्वदुःखहरं रविम् ।। वराननं वरांगं च वरस्थानं वरप्रदम्।। ३८ ।।

I bow to the god who removes all sorrow, to Ravi who removes all sorrow, of excellent face and body, of excellent abode, giver of boons.

BP 1.174.39

वरेण्यं वरदं नित्यं प्रणतोऽस्मि विभावसुम् ।। अर्कमर्यमणं चेन्द्रं विष्णुमीशं दिवाकरम् ।। ३९ ।।

I always bow to Vibhāvasu, most worthy and giver of boons; to Arka, Aryaman, Indra, Viṣṇu, the Lord, and the Day-maker.

BP 1.174.40

देवेश्वरं देवरतं प्रणतोऽस्मि विभावसुम् ।। य इदं शृणुयान्नित्यं ब्रह्मणोक्तं स्तवं परम् ।। स हि कीर्तिं परां प्राप्य पुनः सूर्यपुरं व्रजेत् ।। ४० ।।

I bow to Vibhāvasu, lord of the gods, devoted to the gods. Whoever always hears this supreme hymn spoken by Brahmā, having gained supreme fame, will go again to the city of the Sun.
