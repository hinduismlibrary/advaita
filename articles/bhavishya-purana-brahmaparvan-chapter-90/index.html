Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 90 — The Hṛdaya Observance

BP 1.90.1

ब्रह्मोवाच । रविसङ्क्रमणे यः स्याद्रवेर्वारो गणाधिप । स ज्ञेयो हृदयो नाम आदित्यहृदयप्रियः ॥ ॥

Brahmā said: The day of Ravi that occurs at the Suns transition, O lord of hosts, is known as Hṛdaya, dear to Ādityas heart.

Word-for-word
brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said;
ravi-saṅkramaṇe — at the Sun’s transition;
yaḥ — which;
syāt — may occur;
raveḥ — of Ravi;
vāraḥ — day;
gaṇādhipa — O lord of hosts;
saḥ — that;
jñeyaḥ — is to be known;
hṛdayaḥ — Hṛdaya;
nāma — by name;
āditya-hṛdaya-priyaḥ — dear to Āditya’s heart.
Transliteration
brahmovāca | ravisaṅkramaṇe yaḥ syādravervāro gaṇādhipa | sa jñeyo hṛdayo nāma ādityahṛdayapriyaḥ || 1 ||

BP 1.90.2

तत्र नक्तं समाश्रित्य देवं सम्पूज्य भक्तितः । गत्वा च सदने भानोरादित्याभिमुखस्थितः ॥ ॥

There, undertaking the night observance and worshiping the deity with devotion, he should go to the abode of Bhānu and stand facing Āditya.

Word-for-word
tatra — there;
naktam — the night observance;
samāśritya — undertaking, resorting to;
devam — the deity;
sampūjya — having fully worshiped;
bhaktitaḥ — with devotion;
gatvā — having gone;
ca — and;
sadane — at the abode;
bhānoḥ — of Bhānu;
āditya-abhimukha-sthitaḥ — standing facing Āditya.
Transliteration
tatra naktaṃ samāśritya devaṃ sampūjya bhaktitaḥ | gatvā ca sadane bhānorādityābhimukhasthitaḥ || 2 ||

BP 1.90.3

जपेदादित्यहृदयं सङ्ख्ययाष्टशतं बुधः । अथ वास्तमनं यावद्भास्करं चिंतयेद्धृदि ॥ ॥

The wise person should recite the Āditya-hṛdaya one hundred and eight times; or, until sunset, he should contemplate Bhāskara in his heart.

Word-for-word
japet — should recite;
āditya-hṛdayam — the Āditya-hṛdaya;
saṅkhyayā — in number;
aṣṭa-śatam — eight hundred;
budhaḥ — the wise person;
atha vā — or else;
astamanam — sunset;
yāvat — until;
bhāskaram — Bhāskara;
cintayet — should contemplate;
hṛdi — in the heart.
Transliteration
japedādityahṛdayaṃ saṅkhyayāṣṭaśataṃ budhaḥ | atha vāstamanaṃ yāvadbhāskaraṃ ciṃtayeddhṛdi || 3 ||

BP 1.90.4

गृहमेत्य ततो विप्रान्भोजयेच्छक्तितः शिव । भुक्त्वा तु पायसं वीर ततो भूमौ स्वपेद्बुधः ॥ ॥

Then, returning home, he should feed Brāhmaṇas according to his ability, O auspicious one; having eaten rice cooked in milk, O hero, the wise person should then sleep upon the ground.

Word-for-word
gṛham etya — having come home;
tataḥ — then;
viprān — Brāhmaṇas;
bhojayet — should feed;
śaktitaḥ — according to ability;
śiva — O auspicious one;
bhuktvā — having eaten;
tu — indeed;
pāyasam — milk-rice, payasa;
vīra — O hero;
tataḥ — then;
bhūmau — upon the ground;
svapet — should sleep;
budhaḥ — the wise person.
Transliteration
gṛhametya tato viprānbhojayecchaktitaḥ śiva | bhuktvā tu pāyasaṃ vīra tato bhūmau svapedbudhaḥ || 4 ||

BP 1.90.5

योऽत्र सम्पूजयेत्भानुं भक्त्या श्रद्धासमन्वितः । स कामांल्लभते सर्वान्भास्कराद्धृदयस्थितान् ॥ ॥

Whoever worships Bhānu here with devotion and faith obtains all desires that abide in the heart of Bhāskara.

Word-for-word

sampūjayet — should fully worship;

śraddhā-samanvitaḥ — endowed with faith;
saḥ — he;
kāmān — desires;

hṛdaya-sthitān — abiding in the heart.
Transliteration
yo'tra sampūjayetbhānuṃ bhaktyā śraddhāsamanvitaḥ | sa kāmāṃllabhate sarvānbhāskarāddhṛdayasthitān || 5 ||

BP 1.90.6

तेजसा यशसा तुल्यः प्रभयैषां महात्मनः । शक्रगोपाण्डजानां तु गोपतेर्गोवृषेक्षण ॥ ॥

He becomes equal in radiance, fame, and splendor to these great beings—to Śakra, to gopa, and to the egg-born—O Lord of cows whose eyes are like a bulls.

Word-for-word

Transliteration
tejasā yaśasā tulyaḥ prabhayaiṣāṃ mahātmanaḥ | śakragopāṇḍajānāṃ tu gopatergovṛṣekṣaṇa || 6 ||
