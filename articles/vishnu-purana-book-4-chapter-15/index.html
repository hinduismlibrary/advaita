VP 4.15.1

हिरण्यकशिपुत्वे च रावणत्वे च विष्णुना । अवाप निहतो भोगानप्राप्यानमरैरपि

Maitreyar - When he was killed by saakshaat Vishnu only, when he was Hiranyakashipu, and also when he was born as Raavana, he attained all the enjoyments which are not even attainable by the gods.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

hiraṇyakaśiputve ca rāvaṇatve ca viṣṇunā | avāpa nihato bhogānaprāpyānamarairapi

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.15.2

न लयं तत्र तेनैव निहतः स कथं पुनः । संप्राप्तः शिशुपालत्वे सायुज्यं शाश्वते हरौ

But, he never attained Saayujya in those two births as Hiranyakashipu and Raavana, even though he was killed by Bhagavaan directly. When he was born as Shishupaala, he attained saakshaat moksha itself.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

na layaṃ tatra tenaiva nihataḥ sa kathaṃ punaḥ | saṃprāptaḥ śiśupālatve sāyujyaṃ śāśvate harau

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.15.3

एतदिच्छाम्यहं श्रोतुं सर्वधर्मभृत्तां वर । कौतूहलपरेणैतत्पृष्टो मे वक्तुमर्हसि

I would like to know why such a thing happened. In all the three births, he was hating Bhagavaan only. You who are knowledgeable about all the dharmaas, please do tell me. I am very curious to know this.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

etadicchāmyahaṃ śrotuṃ sarvadharmabhṛttāṃ vara | kautūhalapareṇaitatpṛṣṭo me vaktumarhasi

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.15.4

दैत्येश्वरस्य वधायाखिललोकोत्पत्तिस्थितिविनाशकारिणा पूर्वं तनुग्रहणं कुर्वता नृसिंह रूपमाविष्कृतम्

Sri Paraasharar - In order to kill Hiranyakashipu, One who is responsible for the creation, sustenance and also dissolution of all the worlds, Bhagavaan incarnated taking the form of Nrisimha.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

daityeśvarasya vadhāyākhilalokotpattisthitivināśakāriṇā pūrvaṃ tanugrahaṇaṃ kurvatā nṛsiṃha rūpamāviṣkṛtam

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.15.5

तत्र च हिरण्यकशिपोर्विष्णुरयमित्येतन्न मनस्यभूत्

During that birth, however, Hiranyakashipu did not recognize Nrisimha as saakshaat Bhagavaan.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tatra ca hiraṇyakaśiporviṣṇurayamityetanna manasyabhūt

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.15.6

निरतिशयपुण्यसमुद्भूतमेतत्सत्त्वजातमिति

He thought that this is some strange creation, who had a combination of nara and simha, man-lion, and due to some great punya, this kind of creature is born. He did not recognize saakshaat Vishnu in that form.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

niratiśayapuṇyasamudbhūtametatsattvajātamiti

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.15.7

रजोद्रेकप्रेरितैकाग्रमतिस्तद्भावनायोगात्ततोऽवाप्तवधहैतुकीं निरतिशयामेवाखिलत्रैलोक्याधिक्यकारीणीं दशाननत्वे भोगसंपदमवाप

Because his rajas became extremely high, because of thinking about Him only, he developed so much of hatred, and he was killed by saakshaat Bhagavaan Himself, in the form of Nrisimha, as a result of that, he obtained unparalleled wealth and rulership of all the three worlds as Dashaanana.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

rajodrekapreritaikāgramatistadbhāvanāyogāttato'vāptavadhahaitukīṃ niratiśayāmevākhilatrailokyādhikyakārīṇīṃ daśānanatve bhogasaṃpadamavāpa

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.15.8

न तु स तस्मिन्ननादिनिधने परब्रह्मभूते भगवत्यनालंबिनि कृते मनसस्तल्लयमवाप

But he never fixed his mind thinking that He is Bhagavaan only, One who is beginningless and endless, that He is saakshaat Parabrahma as praised in the Shrutis, that He is the aadhaara for everyone, He does not exist within the aadhaara of anyone else. Dashaanana never fixed his mind in Bhagavaan.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

na tu sa tasminnanādinidhane parabrahmabhūte bhagavatyanālaṃbini kṛte manasastallayamavāpa

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.15.9

एवं दशाननत्वेऽप्यनङ्गपराधीनतया जानकीसमासक्तचेतसा भगवता दाशरथिरूपधारिणा हतस्य तद्रूपदर्शनमेवासीत् नायमच्युत इत्यासक्तिर्विपद्यतोऽन्तःकरणे मानुषबुद्धिरेव केवलमस्याभूत्

Even when he was born as Raavana, he was under the sway of kaama, he was interested in Jaanaki under the passion of kaama, and Bhagavaan Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4 incarnated as Daasharathi, Dasharatha's son Raama and killed him. Raavana thought that Raama was only a man, an ordinary human, and never recognized Him as Achyuta. He thought Him as just a manushya.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

evaṃ daśānanatve'pyanaṅgaparādhīnatayā jānakīsamāsaktacetasā bhagavatā dāśarathirūpadhāriṇā hatasya tadrūpadarśanamevāsīt nāyamacyuta ityāsaktirvipadyato'ntaḥkaraṇe mānuṣabuddhireva kevalamasyābhūt

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.15.10

पुनरप्यच्युतविनिपातमात्रफलमखिलभूमण्डलश्लाघ्यचेदिराजकुले जन्म अव्याहतैश्वर्यं शिशुपालत्वेऽप्यवाप

Because he was killed by saakshaat Bhagavaan, the Chedi kingdom was a great one, the Chedi raaja's vamsha was praised in the entire earth, he was born in that vamsha, with unending wealth, he obtained all of this, born as Shishupaala.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

punarapyacyutavinipātamātraphalamakhilabhūmaṇḍalaślāghyacedirājakule janma avyāhataiśvaryaṃ śiśupālatve'pyavāpa

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.15.11

तत्र त्वखिलानामेव स भगवन्नाम्नां त्वङ्कारकारणमभवत्

Shishupaala kept blaming Him taking all these names - &quot; Tvam, tvam&quot;, pointing fingers at Him, as &quot;You are like this, You are like that&quot;, developing great hatred towards Him. He took every name of Bhagavaan.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tatra tvakhilānāmeva sa bhagavannāmnāṃ tvaṅkārakāraṇamabhavat

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.15.12

ततश्च तत्कालकृतानां तेषामशेषणामेवाच्युतनाम्नामनवरतमनेकजन्मसु वर्धितविद्वेषानुबन्धिचित्तो विनिन्दनसंतर्जनादिषूच्चारणमकरोत्

During that avataara kaala, Bhagavaan had taken all the names. Shishupaala kept on repeating all those names. Shishupaala had developed so much of hatred over many births that he kept on repeating those names without any break. Shishupaala kept on blaming and ridiculing, and threatening Him. But he kept on repeating the various names of Bhagavaan.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tataśca tatkālakṛtānāṃ teṣāmaśeṣaṇāmevācyutanāmnāmanavaratamanekajanmasu vardhitavidveṣānubandhicitto vinindanasaṃtarjanādiṣūccāraṇamakarot

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.15.13

तच्च रूपमुत्फुल्लपद्मदलामलाक्षमत्युज्ज्वलपीतवस्त्रधार्यमखिलकिरिटकेयूरहारकटकादिशोभितमुदारचतुर्बाहुशङ्खचक्रगदाधरमतिप्ररूढवैरानुभावादटनभोजनस्नानासनशयनादिष्वशेषावस्थान्तरेषु नान्यत्रोपययावस्य चेतसः

Bhagavaan's form was coming to him at every stage, when he was roaming around, when he was eating, when he was sitting, when he was sleeping, lying down, Shishupaala was always thinking of Bhagavaan's wonderful form. Bhagavaan's form was with eyes like just bloomed lotus petals, very bright and shiny peetaambara, He was radiant with all the great aabharanaas, keyoora, kataka, haara. He had four arms wearing the wonderful discus, conch and aayudhaas. Because of the hatred developed, all the time, he was thinking of Bhagavaan's form only.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tacca rūpamutphullapadmadalāmalākṣamatyujjvalapītavastradhāryamakhilakiriṭakeyūrahārakaṭakādiśobhitamudāracaturbāhuśaṅkhacakragadādharamatiprarūḍhavairānubhāvādaṭanabhojanasnānāsanaśayanādiṣvaśeṣāvasthāntareṣu nānyatropayayāvasya cetasaḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.15.14

ततस्तमेवाक्रोशेषूच्चारयंस्तमेव हृदयेन धारयन्नात्मवधाय यावद्भगवद्धस्तचक्रांशुमालोज्ज्वालमक्षयतेजः स्वरूपं ब्रह्मभूतमपगतद्वेषादिदोषं भगवन्तमद्राक्षीत्

When he was blaming Him, Shishupaala kept on repeating His names only, and was also thinking in his mind of Bhagavaan's wonderful form only, till the Sudarshana Chakra of Bhagavaan was shining forth very radiantly, like a burning fire, with unparalleled radiance, at that time, when he saw the discus of Bhagavaan coming, all his hatred went away, and he saw saakshaat Bhagavaan at that moment.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tatastamevākrośeṣūccārayaṃstameva hṛdayena dhārayannātmavadhāya yāvadbhagavaddhastacakrāṃśumālojjvālamakṣayatejaḥ svarūpaṃ brahmabhūtamapagatadveṣādidoṣaṃ bhagavantamadrākṣīt

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.15.15

तावच्च भगवच्चक्रेणाशु व्यापादितस्तत्स्मरणदग्धाखिलाघसंचयो भगवताऽन्तमुपनीतस्तस्मिन्नेव लयमुपययौ

Immediately, Shishupaala was killed by the Sudarshana Chakra of Bhagavaan, and because of remembering Him, he had all his sins destroyed completely. He was killed by Bhagavaan and went and united with Bhagavaan Himself.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tāvacca bhagavaccakreṇāśu vyāpāditastatsmaraṇadagdhākhilāghasaṃcayo bhagavatā'ntamupanītastasminneva layamupayayau

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.15.16

एतत्तवाखिलं मयानभिहितम्

Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4 O Maitreyar, you asked the doubt about why during Shishupaala's birth itself, he went and attained Saayujya with Bhagavaan. I have told that.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

etattavākhilaṃ mayānabhihitam

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.15.17

अयं हि भगवान् कीर्तितश्च संस्मृतश्च द्वेषानुबन्धेनापि अखिलसुरासुरादिर्दुल्लभं फलं प्रयच्छति, किमुत सम्यग्भक्तिमतामिति

Paraasharar says - When someone does the keertana, or samsmruti of Bhagavaan, chanting His name, remembrance of Bhagavaan, or even developing hatred, He gives the fruits which cannot be attained by all the suraas and asuraas by any other means. Those who are highly devoted to Him, what to say about them?

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ayaṃ hi bhagavān kīrtitaśca saṃsmṛtaśca dveṣānubandhenāpi akhilasurāsurādirdullabhaṃ phalaṃ prayacchati, kimuta samyagbhaktimatāmiti

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.15.18

VP 4.15.30

ततश्च सकलजगन्महातरुमूलभूतोभूतभविष्यदादिसकलसुरासुरमुनिजनमनसामप्यगोचरोऽब्जभवप्रमुखैरनलमुखैः प्रणम्यावनिभारहरणाय प्रसादितो भगवाननादिमध्यनिधनो देवकीगर्भमवततार वासुदेवः

Now, Paraasharar is telling about Krishna avataara. He is like the source tree of the entire world. All the suraas, asuraas, munis, those who are there earlier, or those in the future, none of them can know Him completely through their minds. Chaturmukha Brahma and all the devaas, Agni, Vaayu and others - they all prostrated to Him, and requested Him to come, to incarnate in this world to destroy the evil. Bhagavaan who is Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4 without beginning, middle or end, He was born as Vaasudeva, Sri Krishna, in Devaki.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tataśca sakalajaganmahātarumūlabhūtobhūtabhaviṣyadādisakalasurāsuramunijanamanasāmapyagocaro'bjabhavapramukhairanalamukhaiḥ praṇamyāvanibhāraharaṇāya prasādito bhagavānanādimadhyanidhano devakīgarbhamavatatāra vāsudevaḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.15.32
