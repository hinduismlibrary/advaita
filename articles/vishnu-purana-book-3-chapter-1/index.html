VP 3.1.1

कथिता गुरुणा सम्यग्भूसमुद्रादिसंस्थितिः । सूर्यादीनां च संस्थानं ज्योतिषां चातिविस्तरात्

The arrangement of the earth, oceans, and the rest was fully described by you, my teacher. You also described the arrangement of the sun and other luminaries, and of the stars in great detail.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
कथिता गुरुणा सम्यक् (kathitā guruṇā samyak) — fully described by you, my teacher
भू-समुद्र-आदि-संस्थितिः (bhū-samudra-ādi-saṃsthitiḥ) — arrangement of earth, oceans, and the rest
सूर्य-आदीनाम् च संस्थानम् (sūrya-ādīnām ca saṃsthānam) — arrangement of the sun and other luminaries
ज्योतिषाम् च अति-विस्तरात् (jyotiṣām ca ati-vistarāt) — and of the stars in great detail
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kathitā guruṇā samyagbhūsamudrādisaṃsthitiḥ | sūryādīnāṃ ca saṃsthānaṃ jyotiṣāṃ cātivistarāt

Śrīdhara

parānandapadāmbhojaśrīdharaḥ śrīdharo yatiḥ | purāṇe vaiṣṇave vyākhyāt tṛtīyāṃśe yathāmati || aṃśadvayena sṛṣṭyādidvisaptabhuvanoktibhiḥ | adhyāropya niṣiddhaṃ tad rājadvijasaduktibhiḥ || tṛtīyāṃśe manuvyāsadharmādyāḥ sthitihetavaḥ | varṇyante'tra niṣedhāya kauravyapṛtanā yathā || atrāpi prathame'dhyāye saptamanvantarāṇi tu | bhavadbhūtāni kathitānyavatārā harer api || vṛttakīrtanapūrvakaṃ manvantarāṇāṃ svarūpaṃ pṛcchati—kathitā iti caturbhiḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

परानन्दपदाम्भोज-श्रीधरः श्रीधरो यतिः । पुराणे वैष्णवे व्याख्यात् तृतीयांशे यथामति ॥ अंशद्वयेन सृष्ट्यादिद्विसप्तभुवनोक्तिभिः । अध्यारोप्य निषिद्धं तद् राजद्विजसदुक्तिभिः ॥ तृतीयांशे मनुव्यासधर्माद्याः स्थितिहेतवः । वर्ण्यन्तेऽत्र निषेधाय कौरव्यपृतना यथा ॥ अत्रापि प्रथमेऽध्याये सप्तमन्वन्तराणि तु । भवद्भूतानि कथितान्यवतारा हरेरपि ॥ वृत्तकीर्तनपूर्वकं मन्वन्तराणां स्वरूपं पृच्छति—कथिता इति चतुर्भिः

Commentary in the Śrīdhara recension.

The opening metrical signature names Śrīdhara in the third person. It identifies Śrīdhara as the ascetic who commented on the third part of the Viṣṇu Purāṇa according to Śrīdhara’s understanding. The first two parts discuss creation and the fourteen worlds, first presenting them and then rejecting that account through the true words of kings and twice-born teachers. The third part describes Manu, Vyāsa, dharma, and other causes of preservation. It also refers briefly to the Kaurava army in connection with rejection, though the wording is compressed. This first chapter recounts the seven Manvantaras and Hari’s descents. Maitreya recalls the earlier account and asks about the Manvantaras in four verses beginning with kathitā, meaning described.

VP 3.1.5

I will properly narrate to you, in sequence, the Manvantaras that have passed and those yet to come.

Word-for-word

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

atītānāgatānīha yāni manvantarāṇi vai | tānyahaṃ bhavataḥ samyakkathayāmi yathākramam

Śrīdhara

iha vārāhakalpe | yathākramamityanena kramaprāptaṃ vartamānamapi manvantaraṃ jñeyam

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

इह वाराहकल्पे । यथाक्रममित्यनेन क्रमप्राप्तं वर्तमानमपि मन्वन्तरं ज्ञेयम्

Commentary in the Śrīdhara recension.

In the Varāha Kalpa. By the expression in sequence, the present Manvantara, which has arrived in the sequence, is also to be understood.

Commentary in the Śrīdhara recension.

An objection may be raised. If Manu, Manu’s sons, the gods, Indra, and the sages protect the world, what need is there for Viṣṇu as protector? The passage beginning with Viṣṇu’s power, through the chapter’s end, shows that Viṣṇu presides over preservation in every Manvantara. Viṣṇu’s essential power appears in deities such as Yajña. As a portion of Viṣṇu, Yajña was born to Ākūti in the Svāyambhuva Manvantara. Alternatively, in the first interval, Yajña was born to Ruci, the mind-born son of Brahmā.
