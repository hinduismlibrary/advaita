VP 3.1.1

कथिता गुरुणा सम्यग्भूसमुद्रादिसंस्थितिः । सूर्यादीनां च संस्थानं ज्योतिषां चातिविस्तरात् ॥१॥

The arrangement of the earth, oceans, and the rest was fully described by you, my teacher. You also described the arrangement of the sun and other luminaries, and of the stars in great detail.

This first chapter recounts the seven Manvantaras and Hari’s descents. Maitreya recalls the earlier account and asks about the Manvantaras in four verses beginning with kathitā, meaning described.

I will properly narrate to you, in sequence, the Manvantaras that have passed and those yet to come.
