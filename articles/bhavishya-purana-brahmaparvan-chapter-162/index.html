Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 162 — Solar Dharma and Temple Worship

BP 1.162.1

सुमंतुरुवाच ।। भानुं प्रतिष्ठाप्य नरः सर्वदेवमयं विभुम् ।। प्राप्नोत्यमरतां वीर तेजसा रविसन्निभः ।। १ ।।

Sumantu said: Having installed Bhānu, the all-pervading one consisting of all the gods, a man attains immortality, O hero, radiant like Ravi.

BP 1.162.2

यो भानुं द्वेष्टि संमोहात्सर्वदेवनमस्कृतम् ।। नरो नरकगामी स्यात्तस्य संभाषणादपि ।। २ ।।

A man who through delusion hates Bhānu, bowed to by all the gods, is headed for hell even from speaking with him.

BP 1.162.3

भानुमिष्टं प्रतिष्ठाप्य सर्वयत्नैर्विधानतः ।। यत्पुण्यफलमाप्नोति तदेकाग्रमनाः शृणु ।। ३ ।।

Having installed Bhānu, the desired one, with every effort and according to rule, hear with a focused mind what fruit of merit one obtains.

BP 1.162.4

सर्वयज्ञतपोदानतीर्थदेवेषु यत्फलम् ।। तत्फलं कोटिगुणितं स्थाप्य भानुं लभेन्नरः ।। ४ ।।

Whatever fruit belongs to all sacrifices, austerities, gifts, sacred fords, and gods, a man obtains it multiplied ten millionfold by installing Bhānu.

BP 1.162.5

यो भानुं स्थापयेद्भक्त्या विधिपूर्वं नराधिप ।। सर्वांगमुदितं पुण्यं लभेत्कोटिगुणं नरः ।। ५ ।।

Whoever installs Bhānu with devotion and according to rule, O lord of men, obtains merit that gladdens the whole being, multiplied ten millionfold.

BP 1.162.6

मातृजान्पितृजांश्चैव यत्र चोद्वहते स्त्रियम् ।। कुलत्रयं समुद्धृत्य शक्रलोके महीयते ।। ६ ।।

Whoever marries a woman born in the maternal and paternal lines raises up three families and is honored in Indra’s world.

BP 1.162.7

भुक्त्वा विपुलान्भोगान्प्रलये समुपस्थिते ।। ज्ञानयोगं समासाद्य तत्रैव प्रविमुच्यते ।। ७ ।।

Having enjoyed abundant pleasures, when dissolution arrives, he attains the yoga of knowledge and is released there.

BP 1.162.8

अथ वा राज्यमाकांक्षेज्जायते संभवांतरे ।। सप्तद्वीपसमुद्रायाः क्षितेरधिपतिर्भवेत् ।। ८ ।।

Or, if he desires a kingdom, he is born in another existence and becomes lord of the earth with its seven islands and oceans.

BP 1.162.9

यत्कृत्वा पार्थिवं व्योम्नि अर्चयेत्सर्वदेवकम् ।। समूलमखिलं तेन त्रैलोक्यं पूजितं भवेत् ।। ९ ।।

Having made an earthen image and worshipped the all-divine one in the sky, by that the whole threefold world is worshipped down to its root.

BP 1.162.10

इहैव धनवाञ्छ्रीमान्सोऽन्तेऽर्कत्वमवाप्नुयात् ।। त्रिसंध्यं कीर्तयेद्व्योम कृत्वा बिंबेन पार्थिवम् ।। १० ।।

Here itself he becomes wealthy and glorious and at the end attains the state of the Sun. Having made an earthen image, he should praise the Sun at the three daily junctions.

BP 1.162.11

शतैकादशकं यावत्तस्य पुण्यफलं शृणु ।। अनेन सह देहेन भानुः संतिष्ठते क्षितौ ।। ११ ।।

Hear the fruit of his merit lasting for one hundred and eleven; Bhānu remains on earth together with this body.

BP 1.162.12

पापहा सर्वमर्त्यानां दर्शनात्स्पर्शनादपि ।। उद्धारयेच्च संस्थाप्य कुलानामेकविंशतिम् ।। १२ ।।

He destroys the sins of all mortals through sight and even through touch; having installed him, one raises up twenty-one families.

BP 1.162.13

गीर्वाणैः सहितो नित्यं मोदते दिवि सूरवत् ।। योऽपि पिष्टमयं व्योम सर्वगंधोपशोभितम् ।। १३ ।।

He rejoices always in heaven with the gods, like the Sun. Whoever also makes a sky-image from flour, adorned with every fragrance,

BP 1.162.14

कुसुमैः सुसुगंधैश्च फलैश्च विविधैर्नृप ।। भक्ष्यलेह्यरसैश्चैव कृतदीपैरलंकृतैः ।। १४ ।।

with very fragrant flowers and varied fruits, O king, with foods to eat, lick, and taste, and adorned with lamps that have been made,

BP 1.162.15

नानारसमायुक्तं नानागंधसमन्वितम् ।। तस्य दक्षिणपार्श्वे तु विन्यसेदगुरुं बुधः ।। १५ ।।

endowed with varied tastes and accompanied by varied fragrances, the wise man should place agarwood on its right side.

BP 1.162.16

दद्याद्वै पश्चिमे भागे श्रीखंडं चंदनं शुभम् ।। उत्तरे चंदनं दद्याद्रक्तं दद्याच्च पूर्वतः ।। १६ ।।

One should place auspicious sandalwood on the western side; sandalwood should be placed in the north, and red sandalwood in the east.

BP 1.162.17

एवं वित्तानुसारेण कृत्वा विभवविस्तरम् ।। कृष्णपक्षे तु सप्तम्यां भास्करस्य निवेदयेत् ।। १७ ।।

Thus, according to one’s means, having prepared the full array of resources, one should offer it to Bhāskara on the seventh lunar day of the dark fortnight.

BP 1.162.18

सकृदेव तु यः कुर्याद्व्योम भरतसत्तम ।। यत्फलं हि भवेत्तस्य तन्मे निगदतः शृणु ।। १८ ।।

Whoever makes the sky-image just once, O best of the Bharatas, hear from me the fruit that would be his.

BP 1.162.19

सर्वपापविनिर्मुक्तः सर्वदुःखविवर्जितः ।। निष्कलः सर्वगो भूत्वा प्रविशेत्परमव्ययम् ।। तेजसा रविसंकाशः प्रभयार्कसमप्रभः ।। १९ ।।

Freed from all sins and devoid of all sorrow, without parts and having become all-pervading, he enters the highest imperishable; radiant like Ravi, bright like the Sun.

BP 1.162.20

पांसुना क्रीडमानो यः कुर्याद्व्योम ह्यकार्यतः ।। स राजन्भवते राजा पर्वतेषु समंततः ।। २० ।।

Whoever, playing with dust, makes the sky-image without a rite, O king, becomes a king over all the mountains around.

BP 1.162.21

सर्वेषामेव पात्राणां परं पात्रं विभावसुः ।। एतत्संतारयेद्यस्मादतीव नरकार्णवात् ।। २१ ।।

Among all recipients, the Sun is the supreme recipient, since he carries one across the very ocean of hell.

BP 1.162.22

तस्य पात्रस्य माहात्म्यं ध्रुवमक्षयमादिशेत् ।। तस्मात्तस्मै सदा देयमप्रमेयफलार्थिभिः ।। २२ ।।

One should declare the greatness of that recipient to be firm and imperishable; therefore those seeking immeasurable fruit should always give to him.

BP 1.162.23

रवौ दत्तं हुतं जप्तं बलिं पूजां निवेदयेत् ।। अनंतफलमादिष्टं ब्रह्मादिसुरसत्तमैः ।। २३ ।।

Whatever is given, offered into fire, recited, presented as an offering, or worshipped for Ravi is declared to have endless fruit by the best of gods beginning with Brahmā.

BP 1.162.24

भक्त्या वित्तानुसारेण यः कुर्यादालयं रवेः ।। सोग्नेयं यानमारुह्य मोदते सह भानुना ।। २४ ।।

Whoever makes a temple for Ravi with devotion and according to his means mounts a fiery vehicle and rejoices with Bhānu.

BP 1.162.25

महाविभवसारोपि यः कुर्याद्भक्तिवर्जितम् ।। अल्पे महति वा तुल्यं फलमाढ्यदरिद्रयोः ।। २५ ।।

Even a man of great wealth who acts without devotion: whether the work is small or great, the fruit is equal for rich and poor.

BP 1.162.26

वित्तशाठ्येन यः कुर्याद्वित्तवानपि मानवः ।। न स फलमवाप्नोति प्रलोभाक्रांतमानसः ।। २६ ।।

Even a wealthy man who acts stingily with his wealth does not obtain the fruit, his mind seized by greed.

BP 1.162.27

तस्मात्त्रिभागं वित्तस्य जीवनाय प्रकल्पयेत् ।। भागद्वयं च धर्मार्थे अनित्यं जीवनं यतः ।। २७ ।।

Therefore one should set aside a third of one’s wealth for living and two parts for dharma, since life is impermanent.

BP 1.162.28

भक्त्या प्रचोदितं कुर्यादल्पवित्तोऽपि यो नरः ।। महाविभवसारोऽपि न कुर्याद्भक्तिवर्जितः ।। २८ ।।

A man of little wealth, impelled by devotion, should do it; even a man of great wealth should not do it without devotion.

BP 1.162.29

सर्वस्वमपि यो दद्यादर्के भक्तिविवर्जितः ।। न तेन धर्मभागी स्याद्भक्तिरेवात्र कारणम् ।। २९ ।।

Whoever gives all his wealth to Arka without devotion does not thereby share in dharma; devotion alone is the cause here.

BP 1.162.30

न तपोभिर्विभोरुग्रैर्न च सर्वैर्महामखैः ।। गच्छेदेकं पुरं दिव्यमर्के भक्तियुतो नृप ।। ३० ।।

One does not reach the one divine city through fierce austerities or all the great sacrifices; devotion to Arka takes one there, O king.

BP 1.162.31

रुचिरं शुभशैलोत्थं कुर्यायस्तु रवेर्गृहम् ।। त्रिसप्तकुलसंयुक्तः सूर्यलोकमवाप्नुयात् ।। ३१ ।।

Whoever makes a beautiful, auspicious temple of Ravi from stone born of the mountain obtains the Sun’s world together with twenty-one families.

BP 1.162.32

यन्मया कोटिगुणितं कृतं स्यादिष्टकाम्यया ।। द्विपरार्धगुणं पुण्यं शैलजेऽपि विदुर्बुधाः ।। ३२ ।।

The merit I may have made for a desired aim, multiplied ten millionfold, the wise know as twice a parārdha of merit even in a stone image.

BP 1.162.33

मृच्छैलेन समं ज्ञेयं पुण्यमाढ्यदरिद्रयोः ।। यत्र तत्र गतः कुर्याद्भक्त्या पुण्यं भगालयम् ।। ३३ ।।

The merit is to be understood as equal for the rich and poor, whether it is made of clay or stone; wherever he goes, he should make a temple of Bhaga with devotion.

BP 1.162.34

शैलोत्थमिष्टकाभिर्वा दृढं दारुमयं शुभम् ।। स गच्छेत्परमं स्थानं भानोरमिततेजसः ।। गैरिकं यानमारुह्य यः कुर्याद्धृतभूषणः ।। ३४ ।।

Whether made strong and beautiful with mountain stone bricks or of wood, he reaches the highest abode of the immeasurably radiant Bhānu; whoever makes it, adorned, mounts a red-ochre vehicle.

BP 1.162.35

क्रीडमानोऽपि यः कुर्याद्बालभावेऽर्कमंदिरम् ।। सोऽर्कलोकमवाप्नोति विमानवरमास्थितः ।। ३५ ।।

Whoever, even while playing as a child, makes a temple of Arka attains Arka’s world, seated on an excellent vehicle.

BP 1.162.36

पुष्पमालाकुलं दिव्यं धूपगंधादिवासितम् ।। अप्सरोगणसंकीर्णं सर्वकाममुखप्रदम् ।। ३६ ।।

Divine, crowded with flower garlands, perfumed with incense and fragrances, filled with hosts of celestial nymphs, granting the fulfillment of every desire.

BP 1.162.37

तत्र रूढो महाराज वत्सरं वृंदमुत्तमम् ।। उषित्वा भास्करपुरे पूज्यमानस्तु दैवतैः ।। ३७ ।।

Mounted there, O great king, having dwelt for an excellent span of years in Bhāskara’s city, worshipped by the gods,

BP 1.162.38

क्रमादागत्य लोकेऽस्मिन्राजा भवति धार्मिकः ।। धर्मार्थकामसंपन्नो यशसा च नराधिप ।। ३८ ।।

in due course he comes to this world and becomes a righteous king, endowed with dharma, wealth, desire, and fame, O lord of men.

BP 1.162.39

पश्यन्परिहरञ्जंतून्मार्जन्या मृदुसूक्ष्मया ।। शनैः संमार्जनं कुर्याच्चांद्रायणफलं भवेत् ।। ३९ ।।

Seeing and avoiding living beings, one should slowly sweep with a soft, fine broom; one thereby obtains the fruit of the lunar-cycle fast.

BP 1.162.40

पुत्रार्थं देहजीर्णाया वन्ध्यायाश्च विशेषतः ।। रोगार्तानां च भूतानामारोग्यार्थं प्रपूजयेत् ।। ४० ।।

One should worship for the sake of a son, for a woman whose body is worn, especially for a barren woman, and for the health of sick beings.

BP 1.162.41

गृहीत्वा गोमयं स्वच्छं स्थाने च पतितं शुभे ।। उपर्युपरि संत्यज्य प्रत्यग्रं जन्तुवर्जितम् ।। ४१ ।।

Having taken clean cow dung fallen in an auspicious place, one should lay it in layers, fresh and free of living beings.

BP 1.162.42

वस्त्रपूतगोमयेन यः कुर्यादुपलेपनम् ।। पश्येत्तु सुखिताञ्जंतूंश्चांद्रायणशतं लभेत् ।। ४२ ।।

Whoever plasters with cow dung strained through cloth and makes the living beings glad obtains the fruit of a hundred lunar-cycle fasts.

BP 1.162.43

यः कुर्यात्सर्वकार्याणि वस्त्रपूतेन वारिणा ।। स मुनिः स महासाधुः स गच्छेत्परमां गतिम् ।। ४३ ।।

Whoever does all actions with water strained through cloth is a sage, a great holy one, and goes to the highest destination.

BP 1.162.44

क्षरंति सर्वदानानि यज्ञहोमबलिक्रियाः ।। अक्षरं तु महादानं सुखदं सर्वदेहिनाम् ।। ४४ ।।

All gifts, sacrifices, fire offerings, and offering rites perish; the great gift is imperishable and brings happiness to all embodied beings.

BP 1.162.45

नैरंतर्येण यः कुर्यात्पक्षं संमार्जनार्चनम् ।। वर्षमेकं शतं दिव्यं सुरलोके महीयते ।। ४५ ।।

Whoever continuously performs sweeping and worship for a fortnight is honored for one hundred divine years in the world of the gods.

BP 1.162.46

तस्यांते च चतुर्वेद सुरूपः प्रियदर्शनः ।। आद्यः सर्वगुणोपेतो राजा भवति धार्मिकः ।। ४६ ।।

At its end he is versed in the four Vedas, beautiful and pleasing to behold; foremost and endowed with every quality, he becomes a righteous king.

BP 1.162.47

संपर्केणापि यः कुर्यान्नरः कर्म भगालये ।। सोपि सौमनसं गत्वा पुरं क्रीडति नित्यशः ।। ४७ ।।

Even a man who has any part in work at Bhaga’s temple goes to the city of Saumanasa and delights there always.

BP 1.162.48

तावद्भ्रमंति संसारे दुःखशोकपरिप्लुताः ।। न भजंति रविं भक्त्या यावत्सर्वेऽपि देहिनः ।। ४८ ।।

So long as embodied beings do not worship Ravi with devotion, they wander in the cycle of rebirth, overwhelmed by pain and grief.

BP 1.162.49

समासक्तं तथा चित्तं जन्तोर्विषयगोचरे ।। यद्यर्को न भवद्देवः को मुञ्चेदेव बन्धनात् ।। ४९ ।।

The mind of a living being is attached to the range of sense-objects; if Arka were not a god, who would release it from bondage?

BP 1.162.50

यः कुर्यात्कुट्टिमां भूमिं दर्पणोदरसन्निभाम् ।। नानावर्णविचित्रां च विचित्रकुसुमोज्ज्वलाम् ।। ५० ।।

Whoever makes a floor resembling the inside of a mirror, varied with many colors and bright with diverse flowers,

BP 1.162.51

क्वचित्कलशविन्यस्तां पंकजैरुपशोभिताम् ।। रम्यां मनोरमां सौम्यामर्कायतनसंसदि ।। ५१ ।।

with pitchers set here and there, adorned with lotuses, lovely, delightful, and gentle, in the assembly hall of the Sun’s temple,

BP 1.162.52

यावद्दंडा भवेद्भूमिः समंताच्च सुशोभना ।। तावद्युगसहस्राणि सूरलोके महीयते ।। ५२ ।।

for as many measuring-rod lengths as the ground is beautifully adorned on every side, for that many thousand world-ages he is honored in the Sun’s world.

BP 1.162.53

कारयेच्चित्रशास्त्रज्ञैश्चित्रकर्मार्कमंदिरे ।। विचित्रं यानमारुह्य चित्रभानोर्गृहं व्रजेत् ।। ५३ ।।

One should have painting work made in Arka’s temple by those skilled in painting; mounting a varied vehicle, one goes to the abode of Citrabhānu.

BP 1.162.54

यावत्स देवरूपाणि ग्रहरूपाणि लेखयेत् ।। तावद्युगसहस्राणि स्वर्गलोके महीयते ।। ५४ ।।

For as many forms of gods and forms of planets as he paints, for that many thousand world-ages he is honored in heaven.

BP 1.162.55

भवेद्भूमिः समंताच्च यः कुर्यादर्कमंदिरम् ।। आरामावसथादीनां लभेदामूल्यकं फलम् ।। ५५ ।।

Whoever makes a temple of Arka with beautiful ground all around obtains the priceless fruit of gardens, dwellings, and the like.
