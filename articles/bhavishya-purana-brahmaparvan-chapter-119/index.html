Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 119 — The Merit of Offering a Lamp and the Sin of Taking One

BP 1.119.1

ब्रह्मोवाच। अन्धे तमसि दुष्पारे नरके पतितात्किल। संक्रोशमानान्संक्षुब्धानुवाच यमकिङ्करः ॥

Brahmā said. In the hell of impenetrable darkness, the servants of Yama addressed those who had fallen there, crying out and agitated.

BP 1.119.2

विलापैरलमत्रेति किं वो विलपिते फलम्। यत्प्रमादादिभिः पूर्वमात्मायं समुपेक्षितः ॥

Enough of wailing. What fruit is there in your lament. Through negligence and the like you formerly neglected the Self.

BP 1.119.3

पूर्वमालोचितं नैतत्कथमन्ते भविष्यति। इदानीं यातनां भुङ्ध्वं किं विलापं करिष्यथ ॥

What you considered before has not turned out this way in the end. Now endure the torment, why will you lament.

BP 1.119.4

देहो दिनानि स्वल्पानि विषयाश्चातिदुर्बलाः। एतत्को न विजानाति येन यूयं प्रमादिनः ॥

The body lasts only a few days, and the objects of the senses are exceedingly weak. Who does not know this, by which you became negligent.

BP 1.119.5

जन्तुर्जन्मसहस्रेभ्य एकस्मिन्मानुषो यदि। स तत्राप्यतिमूढात्मा किं भोगानभिधावति ॥

Among thousands of births, if a living being obtains human birth once, even then, with a deluded self, why does he run after pleasures.

BP 1.119.6

पुत्रदारगृहक्षेत्रहिताय सततोद्यताः। न जानन्ति ततो मूढाः स्वल्पमप्यात्मनो हितम् ॥

The deluded, constantly striving for the welfare of sons, wives, houses, and fields, do not know even the slightest welfare of their own Self.

BP 1.119.7

वञ्चितोऽहं मया लब्धमिदमस्मादुपागतम्। न वेत्ति मोहितः कश्चित्प्रक्रान्तनरको नरः ॥

I have been cheated, I have gained this, this has come to me. The deluded person does not know that he has entered hell.

BP 1.119.8

न वेत्ति सूर्यचन्द्रादीन्कालमात्मानमेव च। साक्षिभूतानशेषस्य शुभस्येहाशुभस्य च ॥

He does not know the Sun, Moon, and the rest, nor time, nor the Self itself, which is the witness of all that is good and evil here.

BP 1.119.9

जन्मान्यन्यानि जायन्ते पुत्रदारादिदेहिनाम्। यदर्थं यत्कृतं कर्म तस्य जन्मशतानि तु ॥

Other births arise for embodied beings through sons, wives, and the rest. The action done for their sake brings hundreds of births.

BP 1.119.10

अहो मोहस्य माहात्म्यं ममत्वं नरकेष्वपि। क्रन्दते मातरं तातं पीड्यमानोऽपि यत्स्वयम् ॥

Ah, the power of delusion. Even in the hells there is possessiveness, and though suffering, one cries for mother and father.

BP 1.119.11

एवमाकृष्टचित्तानां विषयैः स्वादुतर्पणैः। नृणां न जायते बुद्धिः परमार्थविलोकिनी ॥

When people's minds are drawn by the objects of the senses that give sweet satisfaction, understanding that sees the highest truth does not arise.

BP 1.119.12

तथा च विषयासङ्गे करोत्यविरतं मनः। को हि भारो रवेर्नाम्नि जिह्वायाः परिकीर्तने ॥

Thus the mind remains continually attached to the objects of the senses. What burden is there for the tongue in reciting the name of the Sun.

BP 1.119.13

वर्तितैलेऽल्पमूल्ये च यद्वर्त्तिर्लभ्यते सुधा। अतो वै कतरो लाभः का तश्चिन्ता भवेत्तदा ॥

When oil and a wick are available for little cost, what greater gain is there, and what concern should remain.

BP 1.119.14

येनायतेषु हस्तेषु स्वातंत्र्ये सति दीपकः। महाफलो भानुगृहे न दत्तो नरकापहाः ॥

When the hands are free, why is a lamp not offered in the house of the Sun. It gives great fruit and removes hell.

BP 1.119.15

नरो विलपते किञ्चिदिदानीं दृश्यते फलम्। अस्वातंत्र्ये विलपतां स्वातंत्र्ये सति मानिनाम् ॥

A person laments now over a little, and the result is seen. Those who lamented when they lacked freedom now lament when they possess freedom.

BP 1.119.16

अवश्यं पातिनः प्राणा भोक्ता जीवोऽप्यहर्निशम्। दत्तं च लभते भोक्तं कामयन्विषयानपि ॥

Life is certain to fall, and the living being enjoys day and night. One receives the enjoyment one has given, even while desiring the objects of the senses.

BP 1.119.17

एतत्स्थानं दुष्कृतैर्वा युक्तं चाद्य मयेक्षितम्। इदानीं किं विलापेन सहध्वं यदुपागतम् ॥

This condition joined to evil deeds is what I now see. Why lament now, endure what has come.

BP 1.119.18

यद्येतदनभीष्टं वो यद्दुःखं समुपस्थितम्। तदद्भुतमतिः पापे न कर्तव्या कदाचन ॥

If this is not desired by you, if this suffering has arisen, such sin must never be committed by a foolish mind.

BP 1.119.19

कृतेऽपि पापके कर्मण्यज्ञानादघनाशनम्। कर्तव्यमनवच्छिन्नं पूजनं सवितुः सदा ॥

Even when a sinful deed has been done through ignorance, the uninterrupted worship of the Sun, the destroyer of sin, should always be performed.

BP 1.119.20

ब्रह्मोवाच। नारकास्तद्वचः श्रुत्वा तमूचुरतिदुःखिताः। क्षुत्क्षामकण्ठास्तृट्तापविसंस्फुटिततालुकाः ॥

Brahmā said. Having heard those words, the hell dwellers, greatly distressed, spoke to him, their throats dried by hunger and their palates cracked by the heat of thirst.

BP 1.119.21

भो भोः साधो कृतं कर्म यदस्माभिस्तदुच्यताम्। नरकस्थैर्विपाकोऽयं भुज्यते यत्सुदारुणः ॥

O good sir, tell us what deed we performed, whose terrible result is now being suffered by us who dwell in hell.

BP 1.119.22

किङ्कर उवाच। युष्माभियौंवनोन्मादान्मुदितैरविवेकिभिः। घृतलोभेन मार्तण्डगृहाद्दीपः पुरा हृतः ॥

The servant of Yama said. In the madness of youth, delighted and without discrimination, you once stole a lamp from the house of the Sun out of greed for ghee.

BP 1.119.23

तेनास्मिन्नरके घोरे क्षुत्तृष्णापरिपीडिताः। भवन्तः पतितास्तीव्रे शीतवातविदारिताः ॥

For that reason you have fallen into this terrible hell, afflicted by hunger and thirst, torn by the fierce cold wind.

BP 1.119.24

ब्रह्मोवाच। एतत्ते दीपदानस्य प्रदीपहरणस्य च। पुण्यं पापं च कथितं भास्करायतनेऽच्युत ॥

Brahmā said. Thus, O Acyuta, the merit of offering a lamp and the sin of taking away a lamp have been told in the temple of the Sun.

BP 1.119.25

सर्वत्रैव हि दीपस्य प्रदानं कृष्ण शस्यते। विशेषेण जगद्धातुर्भास्करस्य निवेशने ॥

The offering of a lamp is praised everywhere, O Kṛṣṇa, and especially in the abode of the Sun, the sustainer of the world.

BP 1.119.26

येऽन्धा मूका बधिरा निर्विवेका हीनास्तैस्तैर्दानसाधनैर्वृष्णिवीर। तैस्तैर्दीपाः साधुलोकप्रदत्ता देवागारादन्यतः कृष्ण नीताः ॥

O hero of the Vṛṣṇis, lamps offered by virtuous people are taken from the temple elsewhere by those who are blind, mute, deaf, without discernment, or deprived of the means of giving, O Kṛṣṇa.
