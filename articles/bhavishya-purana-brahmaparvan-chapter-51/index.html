Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 51 — The Great Seventh Day Vow

BP 1.51.1

वासुदेव उवाच
माघस्य शुक्लपक्षे तु पञ्चम्यां मत्कुलोद्वह ।
एकभक्तं सदाख्यातं षष्ट्यां नक्तमुदाहृतम् ॥

Vāsudeva spoke. On the fifth day of the bright fortnight of Māgha, O descendant of my family, one meal is prescribed. On the sixth day, eating at night is stated.

BP 1.51.2

सप्तम्यामुपवासं तु केचिदिच्छन्ति सुव्रत ।
षष्ठ्यां केचिद्वदन्तीह सप्तम्यां पारणं किल ॥

Some desire fasting on the seventh day, O steadfast one. Some say that the fast is on the sixth day and the breaking of fast on the seventh.

BP 1.51.3

कृतोपवासः षष्ठ्यां तु पूजयेद्भास्करं बुधः ।
रक्तचन्दनमिश्रैस्तु करवीरैश्च सुव्रत ॥

Having fasted on the sixth day, the wise one should worship Bhāskara with red sandalwood mixed with oleander flowers, O steadfast one.

BP 1.51.4

गुग्गुलेन महाबाहो संयावेन च सुव्रत ।
पूजयेद्देवदेवेशं शङ्करं भास्करं रविम् ॥

With fragrant gum incense and sweet flour cakes, O mighty armed one, O steadfast one, one should worship the lord of gods, Śaṅkara, Bhāskara, the Sun.

BP 1.51.5

एवं हि चतुरो मासान्माघादीन्पूजयेद्रविम् ।
आत्मनश्चापि शुध्यर्थं प्राशनं गोमयस्य च ॥

Thus one should worship the Sun for four months beginning with Māgha, and consume cow dung for purification of oneself.

BP 1.51.6

स्नानं च गोमयेनेह कर्तव्यं चात्मशुद्धये ।
ब्राह्मणान्दिव्यभौमांश्च भोजयेच्चापि शक्तितः ॥

Bathing with cow dung should also be done here for purification of oneself. One should feed heavenly and earthly Brahmins according to one ability.

BP 1.51.7

ज्येष्ठादिष्वथ मासेषु श्वेतचन्दनमुच्यते ।
श्वेतानि चापि पुष्पाणि शुभगन्धान्वितानि वै ॥

In the months beginning with Jyeṣṭha, white sandalwood is prescribed, and white flowers accompanied by good fragrance.

BP 1.51.8

कृष्णागरुस्तथा धूपो नैवेद्यं पायसं स्मृतम् ।
तेनैव ब्राह्मणांस्तुष्टान्भोजयेच्च महामते ॥

Black aloe wood is prescribed as incense, and rice pudding as the offering. With that, O great minded one, one should satisfy the Brahmins and feed them.

BP 1.51.9

प्राशयेत्पञ्चगव्यं तु स्नानं तेनैव पुत्रक ।
कार्तिकादिषु मासेषु अगस्तिकुसुमैः स्मृतम् ॥

One should partake of the mixture of five cow products and bathe with it, O child. In the months beginning with Kārttika, Agati flowers are prescribed.

BP 1.51.10

पूजयेन्नरशार्दूल धूपैश्चैवापराजितैः ।
नैवेद्यं गुडपूपास्तु तथा चेक्षुरसं स्मृतम् ॥

O tiger among men, one should worship with incense and butterfly pea flowers. Jaggery cakes are prescribed as the offering, and likewise sugarcane juice.

BP 1.51.11

तेनैव ब्राह्मणांस्तात भोजयस्व स्वशक्तितः ।
कुशोदकं प्राशयेथाः स्नानं च कुरु शुद्धये ॥

Dear child, one should feed the Brahmins with those same things according to one ability. One should drink water infused with sacrificial grass and bathe for purification.

BP 1.51.12

तृतीये पारणास्यान्ते माघे मासि महामते ।
भोजनं तत्र दानं च द्विगुणं समुदाहृतम् ॥

At the end of the third breaking of fast in the month of Māgha, food and gifts are prescribed in double amount, O great minded one.

BP 1.51.13

देवदेवस्य पूजा च कर्तव्या शक्तितो बुधैः ।
रथस्य चापि दानं तु रथयात्रा तु सुव्रत ॥

The wise should worship the lord of gods according to their ability. The gift of a chariot and the chariot procession should also be made, O steadfast one.

BP 1.51.14

व्रतस्य प्राप्तिहेतोर्वै कर्तव्या विभवे सति ।
दानं स्वर्णरथस्येह यथोक्तं विभवे सति ।
इत्येषा कथिता पुत्र रथाह्वा सप्तमी शुभा ॥

For attainment of the vow, one should perform this when able. The gift of a golden chariot here is as stated, when one is able. Thus this auspicious seventh day rite called the chariot has been told, O child.

BP 1.51.15

महासप्तमी विख्याता महापुण्या महोदया ।
यामुपोष्य धनं पुत्रान्कीर्तिं विद्यामवाप्नुयात् ॥

The Great Seventh Day is renowned, greatly meritorious, and greatly elevating. By fasting on it, one may obtain wealth, sons, fame, and knowledge.

BP 1.51.16

तथाखिलं कुवलयं चन्द्रेण च समोर्चिषा ॥

Likewise, the entire world together with the Moon of equal radiance.
