Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 51 — Mahāsaptamī Vow

BP 1.51.1

वासुदेव उवाच
माघस्य शुक्लपक्षे तु पञ्चम्यां मत्कुलोद्वह ।
एकभक्तं सदाख्यातं षष्ट्यां नक्तमुदाहृतम् ॥

Vāsudeva said: In Māghas bright fortnight, on the fifth, one meal is always declared, O lifter of my lineage; on the sixth, night-eating is stated.

Word-for-word
वासुदेव उवाच (vāsudeva uvāca) — Vāsudeva said;
माघस्य शुक्लपक्षे (māghasya śuklapakṣe) — of Māgha, in the bright fortnight; तु (tu) — but; पञ्चम्यां (pañcamyāṃ) — on the fifth; मत्कुलोद्वह (matkulodvaha) — O lifter of my lineage;
एकभक्तं (ekabhaktaṃ) — one meal; सदाख्यातं (sadākhyātaṃ) — always declared; षष्ट्यां (ṣaṣṭyāṃ) — on the sixth; नक्तमुदाहृतम् (naktamudāhṛtam) — night-eating is stated
Transliteration
vāsudeva uvāca
māghasya śuklapakṣe tu pañcamyāṃ matkulodvaha |
ekabhaktaṃ sadākhyātaṃ ṣaṣṭyāṃ naktamudāhṛtam || 1

BP 1.51.2

सप्तम्यामुपवासं तु केचिदिच्छन्ति सुव्रत ।
षष्ठ्यां केचिद्वदन्तीह सप्तम्यां पारणं किल ॥

Some desire a fast on the seventh, O one of good vows; some say here that the fast is on the sixth and the breaking of the fast, indeed, on the seventh.

Word-for-word
सप्तम्याम् (saptamyām) — on the seventh; उपवासम् (upavāsam) — fast; तु (tu) — but; केचित् (kecit) — some; इच्छन्ति (icchanti) — desire; सुव्रत (suvrata) — O good-vowed one;
षष्ठ्यां (ṣaṣṭhyāṃ) — on the sixth; केचित् (kecit) — some; वदन्ति (vadanti) — say; इह (iha) — here; सप्तम्यां (saptamyāṃ) — on the seventh; पारणं (pāraṇaṃ) — breaking of the fast; किल (kila) — indeed
Transliteration
saptamyāmupavāsaṃ tu kecidicchanti suvrata |
ṣaṣṭhyāṃ kecidvadantīha saptamyāṃ pāraṇaṃ kila || 2

BP 1.51.3

कृतोपवासः षष्ठ्यां तु पूजयेद्भास्करं बुधः ।
रक्तचन्दनमिश्रैस्तु करवीरैश्च सुव्रत ॥

Having fasted on the sixth, the wise person should worship Bhāskara, O one of good vows, with karavīras mixed with red sandalwood.

Word-for-word
कृतोपवासः (kṛtopavāsaḥ) — one who has performed a fast; षष्ठ्यां (ṣaṣṭhyāṃ) — on the sixth; तु (tu) — but; पूजयेत् (pūjayet) — should worship; भास्करं (bhāskaraṃ) — Bhāskara; बुधः (budhaḥ) — the wise one;
रक्तचन्दनमिश्रैः (raktacandanamiśraiḥ) — mixed with red sandalwood; करवीरैः (karavīraiḥ) — with karavīras; च (ca) — and; सुव्रत (suvrata) — O good-vowed one
Transliteration
kṛtopavāsaḥ ṣaṣṭyāṃ tu pūjayedbhāskaraṃ budhaḥ |
raktacandanamiśraistu karavīraiśca suvrata || 3

BP 1.51.4

गुग्गुलेन महाबाहो संयावेन च सुव्रत ।
पूजयेद्देवदेवेशं शङ्करं भास्करं रविम् ॥

With guggulu, O mighty-armed one, and with saṃyāva, O one of good vows, he should worship the lord of the gods, Śaṅkara, Bhāskara, Ravi.

Word-for-word
गुग्गुलेन (guggulena) — with guggulu; महाबाहो (mahābāho) — O mighty-armed one; संयावेन (saṃyāvena) — with saṃyāva; च (ca) — and; सुव्रत (suvrata) — O good-vowed one;
पूजयेत् (pūjayet) — should worship; देवदेवेशं (devadeveśaṃ) — the lord of the gods; शङ्करं (śaṅkaraṃ) — Śaṅkara; २ — editorial numeral retained from the source transcription; भास्करं (bhāskaraṃ) — Bhāskara; रविम् (ravim) — Ravi
Transliteration
guggulena mahābāho saṃyāvena ca suvrata |
pūjayeddevadeveśaṃ śaṅkaraṃ 2 bhāskaraṃ ravim || 4

BP 1.51.5

एवं हि चतुरो मासान्माघादीन्पूजयेद्रविम् ।
आत्मनश्चापि शुध्यर्थं प्राशनं गोमयस्य च ॥

Thus indeed, for four months beginning with Māgha, one should worship Ravi; also, the ingestion of cow dung is for ones own purification.

Word-for-word
एवं (evaṃ) — thus; हि (hi) — indeed; चतुरः मासान् (caturaḥ māsān) — four months; माघादीन् (māghādīn) — beginning with Māgha; पूजयेत् (pūjayet) — one should worship; रविम् (ravim) — Ravi;
आत्मनः (ātmanaḥ) — of oneself; च अपि (ca api) — and also; शुध्यर्थम् (śudhyartham) — for purification; प्राशनम् (prāśanam) — ingestion; गोमयस्य (gomayasya) — of cow dung; च (ca) — and
Transliteration
evaṃ hi caturo māsānmāghādīnpūjayedravim |
ātmanaścāpi śudhyarthaṃ prāśanaṃ gomayasya ca || 5

BP 1.51.6

स्नानं च गोमयेनेह कर्तव्यं चात्मशुद्धये ।
ब्राह्मणान्दिव्यभौमांश्च भोजयेच्चापि शक्तितः ॥

Here too, for self-purification, a bath with cow dung must be performed; one should also feed divine and earthly Brāhmaṇas according to ability.

Word-for-word
स्नानं (snānaṃ) — bath; च (ca) — and; गोमयेन (gomayena) — with cow dung; इह (iha) — here; कर्तव्यम् (kartavyam) — must be performed; च (ca) — and; आत्मशुद्धये (ātmaśuddhaye) — for self-purification;
ब्राह्मणान् (brāhmaṇān) — Brāhmaṇas; दिव्यभौमान् (divyabhaumān) — divine and earthly; च (ca) — and; भोजयेत् (bhojayet) — one should feed; च अपि (ca api) — and also; शक्तितः (śaktitaḥ) — according to ability
Transliteration
snānaṃ ca gomayeneha kartavyaṃ cātmaśuddhaye |
brāhmaṇāndivyabhaumāṃśca bhojayeccāpi śaktitaḥ || 6

BP 1.51.7

ज्येष्ठादिष्वथ मासेषु श्वेतचन्दनमुच्यते ।
श्वेतानि चापि पुष्पाणि शुभगन्धान्वितानि वै ॥

Then, in the months beginning with Jyeṣṭha, white sandalwood is prescribed, and also white flowers furnished with good fragrance.

Word-for-word
ज्येष्ठादिषु (jyeṣṭhādiṣu) — in those beginning with Jyeṣṭha; अथ (atha) — then; मासेषु (māseṣu) — in the months; श्वेतचन्दनम् (śvetacandanam) — white sandalwood; उच्यते (ucyate) — is prescribed;
श्वेतानि (śvetāni) — white; च अपि (ca api) — and also; पुष्पाणि (puṣpāṇi) — flowers; शुभगन्धान्वितानि (śubhagandhānvitāni) — furnished with good fragrance; वै (vai) — indeed
Transliteration
jyeṣṭhādiṣvatha māseṣu śvetacandanamucyate |
śvetāni cāpi puṣpāṇi śubhagandhānvitāni vai || 7

BP 1.51.8

कृष्णागरुस्तथा धूपो नैवेद्यं पायसं स्मृतम् ।
तेनैव ब्राह्मणांस्तुष्टान्भोजयेच्च महामते ॥

Black aguru is likewise incense; pāyasa is remembered as the food offering; with that same [offering] he should feed the pleased Brāhmaṇas, O great-minded one.

Word-for-word
कृष्णागरुः (kṛṣṇāguruḥ) — black aguru; तथा (tathā) — likewise; धूपः (dhūpaḥ) — incense; नैवेद्यम् (naivedyam) — food offering; पायसम् (pāyasam) — pāyasa; स्मृतम् (smṛtam) — is remembered;
तेन एव (tena eva) — with that same; ब्राह्मणान् (brāhmaṇān) — Brāhmaṇas; तुष्टान् (tuṣṭān) — pleased; भोजयेत् (bhojayet) — he should feed; च (ca) — and; महामते (mahāmate) — O great-minded one
Transliteration
kṛṣṇāgarustathā dhūpo naivedyaṃ pāyasaṃ smṛtam |
tenaiva brāhmaṇāṃstuṣṭānbhojayecca mahāmate || 8

BP 1.51.9

प्राशयेत्पञ्चगव्यं तु स्नानं तेनैव पुत्रक ।
कार्तिकादिषु मासेषु अगस्तिकुसुमैः स्मृतम् ॥

One should consume pañcagavya and bathe with that same, O son; in the months beginning with Kārttika, [the worship] is remembered with Agastya flowers.

Word-for-word
प्राशयेत् (prāśayet) — one should consume; पञ्चगव्यम् (pañcagavyam) — pañcagavya; तु (tu) — but; स्नानम् (snānam) — bathing; तेन एव (tena eva) — with that same; पुत्रक (putraka) — O son;
कार्तिकादिषु (kārtikādiṣu) — in those beginning with Kārttika; मासेषु (māseṣu) — in the months; अगस्तिकुसुमैः (agastikusumaiḥ) — with Agastya flowers; स्मृतम् (smṛtam) — is remembered
Transliteration
prāśayetpañcagavyaṃ tu snānaṃ tenaiva putraka |
kārtikādiṣu māseṣu agastikusumaiḥ smṛtam || 9

BP 1.51.10

पूजयेन्नरशार्दूल धूपैश्चैवापराजितैः ।
नैवेद्यं गुडपूपास्तु तथा चेक्षुरसं स्मृतम् ॥

One should worship, O tiger among men, with dhūpas and aparājitas; jaggery cakes are the food offering, and likewise sugarcane juice is remembered.

Word-for-word
पूजयेत् (pūjayet) — one should worship; नरशार्दूल (naraśārdūla) — O tiger among men; धूपैः (dhūpaiḥ) — with dhūpas; च एव (ca eva) — and indeed; अपराजितैः (aparājitaiḥ) — with aparājitas;
नैवेद्यम् (naivedyam) — food offering; गुडपूपाः (guḍapūpāḥ) — jaggery cakes; तु (tu) — but; तथा (tathā) — likewise; च (ca) — and; इक्षुरसम् (ikṣurasam) — sugarcane juice; स्मृतम् (smṛtam) — is remembered
Transliteration
pūjayennaraśārdūla dhūpaiścaivāparājitaiḥ |
naivedyaṃ guḍapūpāstu tathā cekṣurasaṃ smṛtam || 10

BP 1.51.11

तेनैव ब्राह्मणांस्तात भोजयस्व स्वशक्तितः ।
कुशोदकं प्राशयेथाः स्नानं च कुरु शुद्धये ॥

With that same [offering], feed Brāhmaṇas, dear one, according to your ability; drink kuśa-water and bathe for purification.

Word-for-word
तेन एव (tena eva) — with that same; ब्राह्मणान् (brāhmaṇān) — Brāhmaṇas; तात (tāta) — dear one; भोजयस्व (bhojayasva) — feed; स्वशक्तितः (svaśaktitaḥ) — according to your ability;
कुशोदकम् (kuśodakam) — kuśa-water; प्राशयेथाः (prāśayethāḥ) — you should drink; स्नानम् (snānam) — bathing; च (ca) — and; कुरु (kuru) — do; शुद्धये (śuddhaye) — for purification
Transliteration
tenaiva brāhmaṇāṃstāta bhojayasva svaśaktitaḥ |
kuśodakaṃ prāśayethāḥ snānaṃ ca kuru śuddhaye || 11

BP 1.51.12

तृतीये पारणास्यान्ते माघे मासि महामते ।
भोजनं तत्र दानं च द्विगुणं समुदाहृतम् ॥

At the end of the third pāraṇa, in the month of Māgha, O great-minded one, food and giving there are declared to be doubled.

Word-for-word
तृतीये (tṛtīye) — at the third; पारणस्य अन्ते (pāraṇasya ante) — at the end of the breaking of the fast; माघे मासि (māghe māsi) — in the month of Māgha; महामते (mahāmate) — O great-minded one;
भोजनम् (bhojanam) — food; तत्र (tatra) — there; दानम् (dānam) — giving; च (ca) — and; द्विगुणम् (dviguṇam) — doubled; समुदाहृतम् (samudāhṛtam) — is declared
Transliteration
tṛtīye pāraṇāsyānte māghe māsi mahāmate |
bhojanaṃ tatra dānaṃ ca dviguṇaṃ samudāhṛtam || 12

BP 1.51.13

देवदेवस्य पूजा च कर्तव्या शक्तितो बुधैः ।
रथस्य चापि दानं तु रथयात्रा तु सुव्रत ॥

The worship of the god of gods must be performed by the wise according to ability; a chariot also is to be given, and a chariot procession, O one of good vows.

Word-for-word
देवदेवस्य (devadevasya) — of the god of gods; पूजा (pūjā) — worship; च (ca) — and; कर्तव्या (kartavyā) — must be performed; शक्तितः (śaktitaḥ) — according to ability; बुधैः (budhaiḥ) — by the wise;
रथस्य (rathasya) — of a chariot; च अपि (ca api) — and also; दानम् (dānam) — giving; तु (tu) — but; रथयात्रा (rathayātrā) — chariot procession; तु (tu) — but; सुव्रत (suvrata) — O good-vowed one
Transliteration
devadevasya pūjā ca kartavyā śaktito budhaiḥ |
rathasya cāpi dānaṃ tu rathayātrā tu suvrata || 13

BP 1.51.14

व्रतस्य प्राप्तिहेतोर्वै कर्तव्या विभवे सति ।
दानं स्वर्णरथस्येह यथोक्तं विभवे सति ।
इत्येषा कथिता पुत्र रथाह्वा सप्तमी शुभा ॥

For obtaining the vows fruit, it is to be performed when there is wealth; the giving here of a golden chariot is as stated, when there is wealth; thus this auspicious seventh day called Rathā is described, O son.

Word-for-word
व्रतस्य प्राप्तिहेतोः (vratasya prāptihetor) — for the purpose of obtaining the vow; वै (vai) — indeed; कर्तव्या (kartavyā) — it is to be performed; विभवे सति (vibhave sati) — when there is wealth;
दानम् (dānam) — giving; स्वर्णरथस्य (svarṇarathasya) — of a golden chariot; इह (iha) — here; यथोक्तम् (yathoktam) — as stated; विभवे सति (vibhave sati) — when there is wealth;
इति (iti) — thus; एषा (eṣā) — this; कथिता (kathitā) — described; पुत्र (putra) — O son; रथाह्वा (rathāhvā) — called Rathā; सप्तमी (saptamī) — seventh; शुभा (śubhā) — auspicious
Transliteration
vratasya prāptihetor vai kartavyā vibhave sati |
dānaṃ svarṇarathasyeha yathoktaṃ vibhave sati |
ityeṣā kathitā putra rathāhvā saptamī śubhā || 14

BP 1.51.15

महासप्तमी विख्याता महापुण्या महोदया ।
यामुपोष्य धनं पुत्रान्कीर्तिं विद्यामवाप्नुयात् ॥

Mahāsaptamī is renowned, greatly meritorious, greatly elevating; by fasting on it one may obtain wealth, sons, fame, and knowledge.

Word-for-word
महासप्तमी (mahāsaptamī) — Mahāsaptamī; विख्याता (vikhyātā) — renowned; महापुण्या (mahāpuṇyā) — greatly meritorious; महोदया (mahodaya) — greatly elevating;
याम् (yām) — which; उपोष्य (upoṣya) — having fasted; धनम् (dhanam) — wealth; पुत्रान् (putrān) — sons; कीर्तिम् (kīrtim) — fame; विद्याम् (vidyām) — knowledge; अवाप्नुयात् (avāpnuyāt) — one may obtain
Transliteration
mahāsaptamī vikhyātā mahāpuṇyā mahodayā |
yāmupoṣya dhanaṃ putrānkīrtiṃ vidyāmavāpnuyāt || 15

BP 1.51.16

तथाखिलं कुवलयं चन्द्रेण च समोर्चिषा ॥

And likewise the entire world, together with the moon of equal radiance.

Word-for-word
तथा (tathā) — likewise; अखिलम् (akhilam) — entire; कुवलयम् (kuvalayam) — world; चन्द्रेण (candreṇa) — with the moon; च (ca) — and; समोर्चिषा (samorciṣā) — with equal radiance
Transliteration
tathākhilaṃ kuvalayaṃ candreṇa ca samorciṣā || 16
