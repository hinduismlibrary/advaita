MMK 11.4

If birth were after and aging / death before, how could there be an uncaused aging / death which has no birth?

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

paścājjātiryadi bhavejjarāmaraṇamāditaḥ | ahetukamajātasya syājjaraṃ maraṇaṃ katham || 4 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

Suppose instead that aging and death come first and birth comes later. Aging and death would then occur without birth as their cause. But the Buddha teaches that aging and death arise in dependence on birth. Just as a falling object depends on being lifted and thrown, there is no uncaused destruction that precedes the birth of the thing said to perish.
