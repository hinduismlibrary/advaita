MMK 5.2

A thing without characteristics does not exist anywhere at all. If a thing without characteristics does not exist, to what do characteristics extend?

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

alakṣaṇo na kaścicca bhāvaḥ saṃvidyate kvacit | asatyalakṣaṇe bhāve kramatāṃ kuha lakṣaṇam || 2 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

An opponent might say that the characteristic can be applied to the object because the object exists. Candrakīrti replies that no entity without a characteristic is found anywhere. If no unmarked space or other thing is established, where could the characteristic be applied?

The point is not that a characteristic cannot be named. It is that no independent object is first available for the characteristic to mark. A thing cannot be established as the basis for a mark before the relation between the marked thing and its mark is established.
