MMK 5.4

If characteristics do not extend [to something] , something characterized would be impossible. If something characterized is impossible, characteristics too would not exist.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

lakṣaṇāsaṃpravṛttau ca na lakṣyamupapadyate | lakṣyasyānupapattau ca lakṣaṇasyāpyasaṃbhavaḥ || 4 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

If a characteristic does not apply, the characterized object cannot be established. One might reply that the characteristic still exists even if its application has been rejected, and that it establishes the object. Candrakīrti answers that the object itself has not been established.

When the characterized object is not established, the characteristic has no support and cannot be established either. This is why the earlier argument concludes that neither the object marked nor the mark is found.
