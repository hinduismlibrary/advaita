MMK 5.7

Therefore, space is not a thing; it is not a non-thing; it is not something characterized; it is not a characteristic. The other five elements too are similar to space.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

tasmānna bhāvo nābhāvo na lakṣyaṃ nāpi lakṣaṇam | ākāśasamā dhātavaḥ pañca ye pare || 7 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

The conclusion is that space is neither a thing nor a non-thing, neither the characterized object nor its characteristic. The five other elements—earth, water, fire, air, and consciousness—are like space: they too are to be understood as free from intrinsically established being, non-being, object, characteristic, or conceptual construction.
