MMK 2.23

[A going] which is other than the going by which a goer is made evident does not [enable a goer to] go. Because it is impossible for going to be twofold within a single goer.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

gatyā yayocyate gantā tato 'nyāṃ sa na gacchati | gatī dve nopapadyete yasmād eke pragacchati || 23 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

The movement by which someone is described as a goer cannot be separated from the movement that this person is then said to perform. If the person performs another movement as well, there are two movements in one goer: the one that qualifies him as a goer and the one by which he goes. This double movement has already been rejected. The familiar examples “a speaker speaks” and “an agent acts” are therefore no counterexample.
