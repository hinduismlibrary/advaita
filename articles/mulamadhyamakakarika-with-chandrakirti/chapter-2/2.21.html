MMK 2.21

If things are not established as the same and as different, how can they be established?

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

ekībhāvena vā siddhir nānābhāvena vā yayoḥ | na vidyate tayoḥ siddhiḥ kathaṃ nu khalu vidyate || 21 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

The goer and the action of going have not been established either as one or as different. What other mode of existence could establish them? Candrakīrti’s conclusion is that neither is established in its own nature.
