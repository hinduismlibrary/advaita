MMK 2.3

How can going be possible within motion? Because motion that is not going is impossible.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

gamyamānasya gamanaṃ kathaṃ nāmopapatsyate | gamyamāne dvigamanaṃ yadā naivopapadyate || 3 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

You call a stretch “being gone over” because it is joined with the action of going, and then say that this same stretch “is gone over.” If one action is already used to account for the designation “being gone over,” the further claim that it is gone over requires a second action. But a second act of going in the same stretch cannot be established. The complete statement “what is being gone over is gone over” therefore fails: only the designation “being gone over” is available, not a further going.
