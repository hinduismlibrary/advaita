MMK 16.5

na badhyante na mucyante udayavyayadharmiṇaḥ
saṃskārāḥ pūrvavat sattvo badhyate na na mucyate || 5 ||

Formations, whose nature is arising and ceasing, are not bound and are not released. A being, as before, is neither bound nor released.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

na badhyante na mucyante udayavyayadharmiṇaḥ | saṃskārāḥ pūrvavat sattvo badhyate na na mucyate || 5 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

Here it is said: These afflictions beginning with passion, which are called bondage in making the bound not independent, and through which bound ordinary persons are established as not passing beyond the three realms, when this passion and the rest are conceived as bondage, are not possible for formations whose nature is arising and ceasing, which are momentary, which perish immediately after arising, and which, having perished, do not exist.

Release, whose characteristic is the severing of bondage such as passion, also cannot be possible, because impermanent formations do not exist.

“As before” means by the method stated before. And just as, by the method stated before, bondage and release of formations are not possible, so, just as before, a being too is neither bound nor released. Thus bondage and release do not exist.
