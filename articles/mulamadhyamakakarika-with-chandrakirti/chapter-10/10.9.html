MMK 10.9

yadīndhanamapekṣyāgniragneḥ siddhasya sādhanam
evaṃ satīndhanaṃ cāpi bhaviṣyati niragnikam || 9 ||

If, depending on fuel, fire comes to be, this would be proof of an already established fire. In this way, fuel too will be without fire.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

yadīndhanamapekṣyāgniragneḥ siddhasya sādhanam | evaṃ satīndhanaṃ cāpi bhaviṣyati niragnikam || 9 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

Even if you think: Fuel is first, fire afterward; even so, if fire comes to be depending on fuel, then there would again be proof of fire already established. Dependence is proper only for a thing that exists, whose established form is existent. For the nonexistent Devadatta does not depend on anyone in a house. Likewise, if fire were not existent, it would not depend on fuel. Therefore the existence of fire must be accepted. And then what is to be done again by depending on this fuel? For an established fire is not to be made again by fuel, so that dependence on fuel might be effective for that purpose. Therefore it is not reasonable to say that fire comes to be depending on fuel.

Moreover, if it is supposed that fire comes to be depending on fuel:

If fuel were unestablished, it would not at all be depended upon by fire, because dependence on what is unestablished is impossible. Therefore the establishment of fuel without fire would have to be accepted. But this is not so. Therefore this is not reasonable.
