MMK 10.12

apekṣyendhanamagnirna nānapekṣyāgnirindhanam
apekṣyendhanamagniṃ na nānapekṣyāgnimindhanam || 12 ||

Fire does not exist depending on fuel; nor does fire exist without depending on fuel. Fuel does not exist depending on fire, nor without depending on fire.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

apekṣyendhanamagnirna nānapekṣyāgnirindhanam | apekṣyendhanamagniṃ na nānapekṣyāgnimindhanam || 12 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

Since this is so, therefore:

If it should be said: Then surely fire will exist without dependence. He says that this too is not reasonable:

Because otherness is denied and because the consequence of being without a cause would follow. And just as fuel does not exist either depending on fire or without depending on fire, so too he says:

This has already been explained in the immediately preceding discussion, so it is not said again.
