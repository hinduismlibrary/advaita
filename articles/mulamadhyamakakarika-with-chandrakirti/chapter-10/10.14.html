MMK 10.14

Wood itself is not fire; fire is also not something other than wood. Fire does not possess wood; wood does not exist in fire; that (fire) does not exist in it.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

indhanaṃ punaragnirna nāgniranyatra cendhanāt | nāgnirindhanavānnāgnāvindhanāni na teṣu saḥ || 14 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

Candrakīrti gathers the five alternatives. Fire is not identical with fuel, nor is it something other than fuel. Fire does not possess fuel as an independently established thing; fuel is not located inside an independently established fire; and fire is not located inside independently established fuel. The analysis leaves no self-existing fire-fuel relation.
