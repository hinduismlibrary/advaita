MMK 10.1

If firewood were fire, actor and act would be one. If fire were other than wood, it would occur even without wood.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

yadindhanaṃ sa cedagnirekatvaṃ kartṛkarmaṇoḥ | anyaścedindhanādagnirindhanādapyṛte bhavet || 1 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

An opponent says that fire and fuel depend on one another yet still have their own natures: fire has heat and burning as its nature, while fuel has its own material nature. In the same way, they argue, an appropriator and what is appropriated may depend on one another and still exist intrinsically. Candrakīrti asks whether fire and fuel are one or different. If the fuel itself is fire, actor and act collapse into one thing. If fire is different from fuel, it should exist even without fuel.
