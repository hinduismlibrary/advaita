MMK 10.11

How can a thing (A) which is established dependently (on B) be dependent (on B) when it (A) is not established? If one asks, "how can establishment be dependent?" It is not reasonable for it (A) to be dependent.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

yo 'pekṣya sidhyate bhāvaḥ so 'siddho 'pekṣate katham | athāpyapekṣate siddhastvapekṣāsya na yujyate || 11 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

A thing established in dependence on another is either not yet established, or already established. If it is not established, how can it depend on anything? If it is already established, why does it need dependence? The same applies to fuel. Since neither an unestablished nor an already established fire can depend on fuel in the proposed way, fire and fuel are not established through mutual dependence.
