MMK 10.11

yo 'pekṣya sidhyate bhāvaḥ so 'siddho 'pekṣate katham
athāpyapekṣate siddhastvapekṣāsya na yujyate || 11 ||

A thing that is established depending on another: how does that unestablished thing depend? Or, if it depends, it is established; but its dependence is not reasonable.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

yo 'pekṣya sidhyate bhāvaḥ so 'siddho 'pekṣate katham | athāpyapekṣate siddhastvapekṣāsya na yujyate || 11 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

Moreover, he says that establishment of fire and fuel even through mutual dependence is absent, because there is no dependence of what is established and what is unestablished:

Indeed, the entity called fire, which is established depending on the entity called fuel, does it depend on fuel as unestablished or as established? If unestablished, then because it is unestablished it would not depend on fuel, just as a hare’s horn does not. But if established, then, because it is established, what need has it for dependence on fuel? For what is established is not established again, because that would be pointless. The same is to be said of fuel. Therefore there is no establishment of fire and fuel either through mutual dependence or simultaneously.
