MMK 8.5

If activity etc. did not appear, dharma and adharma would not be evident. If dharma and adharma did not exist, there would be no fruit that comes from them.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

dharmādharmau na vidyete kriyādīnāmasaṃbhave | dharme cāsatyadharme ca phalaṃ tajjaṃ na vidyate || 5 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

Merit and demerit depend on actions, agents and means. When Devadatta freely refrains from taking life, his action is virtuous; the same analysis applies to other wholesome deeds, such as honoring the Three Jewels, parents and other worthy people. Harmful actions work in the opposite way. If actions, agents and means cannot be established, merit and demerit cannot be established either, and no karmic result can arise from them.
