MMK 9.7

sarvebhyo darśanādibhyo yadi pūrvo na vidyate
ekaikasmātkathaṃ pūrvo darśanādeḥ sa vidyate || 7 ||

If one is not prior to all seeing and the rest, how can he exist prior to each one of seeing and the rest?

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

sarvebhyo darśanādibhyo yadi pūrvo na vidyate | ekaikasmātkathaṃ pūrvo darśanādeḥ sa vidyate || 7 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

Reply: This too is not reasonable, because it is impossible for a person without seeing and the rest, without appropriation, without a cause, and without defilement to exist.

“If one is not prior to all seeing and the rest, how can he exist prior to each one of seeing and the rest?”

One who is not prior to all is not prior to each one either. For example, if a forest does not exist before all trees, it does not exist before each individual tree either. And when no oil is produced from all the grains of sand, there is no oil from any one grain of sand. Further, if one is prior to each one individually, it is accepted that he is indeed prior to all, since all exist only by excluding each individual one. Therefore, it is not reasonable that anyone exists prior to each one separately.
