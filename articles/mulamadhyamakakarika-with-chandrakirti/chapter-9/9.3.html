MMK 9.3

What configures / makes known that thing which is present before seeing, hearing, and feeling, etc.?

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

darśanaśravaṇādibhyo vedanādibhya eva ca | yaḥ prāgvyavasthito bhāvaḥ kena prajñapyate 'tha saḥ || 3 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

What could designate or make known a person supposedly established before seeing, hearing, feeling and the other experiences? Those experiences are the basis on which a person is designated. If the person preceded them, the person would be independent of those causes, like cloth independent of its material. Anything wholly independent of its causes would be causeless; no such prior person is established.
