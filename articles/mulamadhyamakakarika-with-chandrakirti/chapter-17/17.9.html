MMK 17.9

yastasmāc cittasaṃtānaś cetaso 'bhipravartate
tataḥ phalam ṛte cittāt sa ca nābhipravartate || 9 ||

From that mind a continuum of mind proceeds; fruit arises from it; it does not proceed apart from that mind.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

yastasmāc cittasaṃtānaś cetaso 'bhipravartate | tataḥ phalam ṛte cittāt sa ca nābhipravartate || 9 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

Just as the sequence concerning the seed has been explained, so:

“From that mind a continuum of mind proceeds; from it a fruit [arises], and that continuum does not proceed apart from that mind.”

Therefore, from a mind associated with the particular differences of wholesome and unwholesome volition, a continuum of mind proceeds, having that as its cause. From that continuum of mind, developed by wholesome and unwholesome volition, when the cooperating causes are present without deficiency, a desired or undesired fruit arises in good and bad destinies. But apart from that mind, independently of that mind, that continuum does not proceed.
