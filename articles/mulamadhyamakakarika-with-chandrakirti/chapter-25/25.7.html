MMK 25.7

If nirvana were not a thing, how could it possibly be nothing? The one for whom nirvana is not a thing, for him it is not nothing.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

bhāvo yadi na nirvāṇam abhāvaḥ kiṃ bhaviṣyati | nirvāṇaṃ yatra bhāvo na nābhāvas tatra vidyate || 7 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

The opponent may say that nirvāṇa is not an entity but the absence of afflictions and birth. Candrakīrti rejects this as well: absence cannot be made into a thing called nirvāṇa.

If nirvāṇa were merely the absence of birth and affliction, it would be indistinguishable from their impermanence or temporary nonappearance. That would not explain liberation.
