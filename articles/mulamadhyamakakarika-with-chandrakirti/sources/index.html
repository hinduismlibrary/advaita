Candrakīrti’s original Sanskrit and English translations

Chapter 1 contains the English introduction and the current English translation of all fourteen Candrakīrti commentary passages. Each root verse has its own page, and the translated commentary is split into separately numbered passage pages such as 1.1.1 and 1.1.2. The English commentary translation is currently available for Chapters 1–21; Chapters 22–27 still need translation from the Sanskrit. Chapters 2–21 include commentary passages for every root verse, each on its own numbered page. Chapter 7 has 37 numbered commentary pages for its 34 verses, Chapter 8 has 14 numbered pages for its 13 verses, Chapter 9 has 14 numbered pages for its 12 verses, Chapter 10 has 19 numbered pages for its 16 verses, and Chapter 11 has 10 numbered pages for its 8 verses, while Chapter 12 has 13 numbered pages for its 10 verses, and Chapter 13 has 12 numbered pages for its 8 verses, and Chapter 14 has 10 numbered pages for its 8 verses, and Chapter 15 has 16 numbered pages for its 11 verses, and Chapter 16 has 15 numbered pages for its 10 verses, and Chapter 17 has 33 numbered pages for its 33 verses, and Chapter 18 has 12 numbered pages for its 12 verses, and Chapter 19 has 6 numbered pages for its 6 verses, and Chapter 20 has 24 numbered pages for its 24 verses, and Chapter 21 has 21 numbered pages for its 21 verses. The GRETIL Sanskrit transcription is distributed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; the English commentary translation is shared under the same license. It is not the published translation of Anne MacDonald or Mervyn Sprung.
