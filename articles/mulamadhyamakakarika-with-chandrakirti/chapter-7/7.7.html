MMK 7.7

If that which has not been born is able to give birth to that, that of yours which is being born should be able to give birth to that.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

ayamutpadyamānaste kāmamutpādayedimam | yadīmamutpādayitumajātaḥ śaknuyādayam || 7 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

The opponent says the root arising, while coming into being, produces the arising-of-arising, and that this second arising then produces the root. Candrakīrti asks how an event that is itself still unborn could produce another unborn event. If an unarisen thing could produce something, anything could arise without a cause. The same difficulty applies to the proposed arising-of-arising.
