MMK 7.33

Because birth and remaining and perishing are not established, there is no conditioned. Because the conditioned is utterly unestablished, how can the unconditioned be established?

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

utpādasthitibhaṅgānāmasiddhernāsti saṃskṛtam | saṃskṛtasyāprasiddhau ca kathaṃ setsyatyasaṃskṛtam || 33 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

If arising, abiding and perishing are not established, conditioned things are not established either, because those are their proposed marks. Some posit unconditioned things such as space, non-analytical cessation and nirvana; others posit emptiness or suchness as unconditioned. But if the conditioned is not established, there is no basis for establishing its opposite as a separate unconditioned reality.
