MMK 7.12

pradīpaḥ svaparātmānau saṃprakāśayate yadi
tamo 'pi svaparātmānau chādayiṣyatyasaṃśayam || 12 ||

If a lamp illuminates itself and others, then surely darkness too will cover itself and others.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

pradīpaḥ svaparātmānau saṃprakāśayate yadi | tamo 'pi svaparātmānau chādayiṣyatyasaṃśayam || 12 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

And if you conceive that a lamp illuminates itself and others, then darkness too, which is its opposite, should be conceived to cover itself and others, he says:

“If a lamp illuminates itself and others, then surely darkness too will cover itself and others.”

Because it is an opponent, darkness too will perform an activity directed at itself and another, like a lamp, and thus it will cover itself as well as another. And if darkness covers itself, it itself would not be perceived, because it would be concealed by darkness, like a pot. For this very reason it is said in the Noble Upāli’s Questions:

“Here, in this lovely Teaching, having gone forth, abandoning the marks of a householder, you will be fruitful, excellent among those instructed by the compassionate one.

“Having gone forth, abandoning the marks of a householder, there will be attainment of every fruit. Again, having weighed the nature of the dharma, there will be attainment of every fruit and its fruits.

“They obtained the fruit and that attainment; wonder arose again in them. Ah, the greatly compassionate Lion of Men! The Victor has taught reasoning well.”

And in the Noble Ratnakūṭa-sūtra:

“Just as a lamp, placed long ago in a house by some person, leaves no darkness there; so, having remained there a long time, I will not go from here.

“Darkness has no power to go away when a lamp has been made. Depending on the lamp, darkness perishes; both are empty, and one does not conceive anything.

“Likewise, Noble One, depending on knowledge without outflows, ignorance accumulated with afflictions disappears. Contact between knowledge and affliction never exists at any time.

“Knowledge does not conceptualize; ignorance does not exist. Depending on knowledge, darkness itself perishes. Fear too is ungraspable, like a sky-flower; likewise knowledge and ignorance are both empty.”
