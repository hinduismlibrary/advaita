MMK 7.14

Born and the unborn - being born does not in any way give birth. That has been explained by the gone, not gone and going.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

notpadyamānaṃ notpannaṃ nānutpannaṃ kathaṃcana | utpadyate tathākhyātaṃ gamyamānagatāgataiḥ || 14 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

Nor does arising produce another thing. Nothing arises as what is presently arising, already arisen, or not yet arisen. This parallels the earlier analysis of going: what has been gone over is not being gone over now, what has not yet been gone over is not being gone over, and no separate “being gone over” can be found. Likewise, the presently arising cannot be separated from what is already or not yet arisen; the already arisen belongs to the past, while the not-yet-arisen belongs to the future. No arising is established in any of the three times.
