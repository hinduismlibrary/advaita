MMK 22.6

If there does not exist a tathagata [who is] not depending on the aggregates, how does he grasp [depend on? them]?

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

skandhāṃścāpyanupādāya nāsti kaścittathāgataḥ | yaśca nāstyanupādāya sa upādāsyate katham || 6 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

If no Tathāgata exists apart from the aggregates, there is no prior appropriator who could later take them up. To say that what does not exist appropriates them would make the event causeless.

The argument closes the alternative left by the previous verse: an intrinsically existing Tathāgata cannot be established either before or through appropriation of the aggregates.
