MMK 22.7

[Since] there is nothing to be grasped / dependent on, there can be no grasping / depending. There is no tathagata at all who is without grasping / depending.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

na bhavatyanupādattamupādānaṃ ca kiṃcana | na cāsti nirupādānaḥ kathaṃcana tathāgataḥ || 7 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

Unappropriated aggregates cannot serve as an independently established act of appropriation. If nothing takes them up, they cannot be established as “appropriated” from their own side.

Nor can an appropriator exist independently of what is appropriated. Candrakīrti rejects both a self-existing person without aggregates and self-existing appropriation without a person.
