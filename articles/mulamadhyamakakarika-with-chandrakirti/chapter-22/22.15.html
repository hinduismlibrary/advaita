MMK 22.15

Those who make fixations about Buddha who is beyond fixations and without deterioration -- all those who are damaged by fixations do not see the tathagata.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

prapañcayanti ye buddhaṃ prapañcātītamavyayam | te prapañcahatāḥ sarve na paśyanti tathāgatam || 15 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

Those who proliferate concepts about the Buddha who is beyond conceptual proliferation—casting him as permanent or impermanent, existent or nonexistent, and so on—are overcome by those very concepts.

Because they take their own fabrications as reality, they do not see the Tathāgata. The Buddha is beyond such views, not a hidden entity that could be captured by a more elaborate description.
