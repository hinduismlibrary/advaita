MMK 22.4

If self-nature does not exist, how can there be the existence of other-nature? What is a Tathagata apart from own-nature and other-nature?

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

svabhāvaparabhāvābhyāmṛte kaḥ sa tathāgataḥ || 4 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

An entity that exists would have to be established through its own nature or through another nature. Once both are excluded, what independently established entity could remain to be called the Tathāgata?

Candrakīrti’s point is about intrinsic existence. The name “Tathāgata” can be used in ordinary discourse, but analysis finds no self-grounded person behind the designation.
