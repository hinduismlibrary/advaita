Candrakīrti’s original Sanskrit and English translations

Chapter 1 includes its English introduction and verse-by-verse commentary. Throughout the book, each Nāgārjuna verse has its own page, with Candrakīrti’s complete commentary directly beneath it; use the next-verse link to continue. The English commentary covers all 27 chapters. The Sanskrit source is the Prasannapadā text edited by P. L. Vaidya and served by GRETIL; the English text on this site is translated from that Sanskrit. The GRETIL Sanskrit transcription is distributed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; the English commentary translation is shared under the same license. It is not the published translation of Anne MacDonald or Mervyn Sprung.

For comparison, an independently published full English translation (open-access, copyrighted): Anne MacDonald, In Clear Words: The Prasannapadā, Chapter One, Vol. II (2015). The published translation is linked for reference, not reproduced. Primary Sanskrit text: GRETIL, Candrakīrti’s Prasannapadā, based on P. L. Vaidya’s 1960 edition (electronic transcription distributed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). For a scholarly critical edition and annotated English translation of Chapter 1, see Anne MacDonald, In Clear Words: The Prasannapadā, Chapter One (2015).

Further English reference: Mervyn Sprung, Lucid Exposition of the Middle Way: The Essential Chapters from the Prasannapadā of Candrakīrti (1979), translated with T. R. V. Murti and U. S. Vyas. This is a copyrighted, selectively translated volume; it is a reference for comparison rather than the text reproduced on this site.
