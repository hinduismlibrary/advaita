MMK 24.19

apratītya samutpanno dharmaḥ kaścin na vidyate
yasmāt tasmād aśūnyo hi dharmaḥ kaścin na vidyate || 19 ||

No dharma whatsoever exists that has arisen without dependence. Therefore no dharma whatsoever that is nonempty exists.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

apratītya samutpanno dharmaḥ kaścin na vidyate | yasmāt tasmād aśūnyo hi dharmaḥ kaścin na vidyate || 19 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

And, when examined in every way:

No dharma that has arisen without dependence exists. Since that is so, no dharma that is nonempty exists.

That which has arisen without dependence is not found. As it is said in the Śataka:

“At no time, for anyone, anywhere, is there existence without dependence. Therefore no eternal thing exists anywhere, at any time. Ordinary people imagine things such as space to be eternal; even the discerning do not see an object in them.”

And it was said by the Blessed One:

“The wise one realizes dharmas through dependence and does not take a stand upon a view of extremes. He knows dharmas with causes and conditions; no dharma has no cause and no condition.”

Thus: no dharma whatsoever exists that has arisen without dependence. And that which has arisen without dependence is empty. Therefore no dharma that is nonempty exists. Since this is so, all dharmas are empty for us, and the fault asserted by the other does not follow.
