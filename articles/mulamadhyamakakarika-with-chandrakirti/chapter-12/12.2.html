MMK 12.2

If it were made by self, therefore it would not be contingently arising, because those aggregates arise contingently on these aggregates.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

svayaṃ kṛtaṃ yadi bhavet pratītya na tato bhavet | skandhān imān amī skandhāḥ saṃbhavanti pratītya hi || 2 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

If suffering were made by itself, it could not arise dependently. Yet the aggregates that arise in a new birth arise in dependence on the aggregates that ended with death. Because this continuity is dependent, suffering is not produced by an independently existing self.
