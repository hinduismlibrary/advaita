MMK 18.8

Everything is real, not real; both real and not real; neither not real nor real: this is the teaching of the Buddha.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

sarvaṃ tathyaṃ na vā tathyaṃ tathyaṃ cātathyameva ca | naivātathyaṃ naiva tathyaṃ etadbuddhānuśāsanam || 8 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

An opponent asks how people can learn a truth that cannot be expressed. Candrakīrti answers that the Buddha teaches progressively, meeting people through what they can first understand. Some are first taught conventional appearances as true and later shown their lack of ultimate truth; others are taught both, while the most prepared are guided beyond both fixed positions.

These instructions follow a therapeutic sequence. Like medicine matched to an illness, each teaching leads listeners from confused views toward reality; the Buddha does not offer one formula to every audience.
