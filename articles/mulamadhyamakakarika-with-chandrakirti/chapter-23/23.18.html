MMK 23.18

confusions do not occur for those who are being confused. For whom do confusions occur? Examine this by yourself!

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

na viparyasyamānasya saṃbhavanti viparyayāḥ | vimṛśasva svayaṃ kasya saṃbhavanti viparyayāḥ || 18 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

Nor can an independently existing person in the process of becoming mistaken be found. A supposed “partly mistaken” person would already be mistaken in one respect and not mistaken in another, so the analysis still finds no separate bearer of error.

Candrakīrti asks the reader to examine for themselves who could possess an intrinsically real inversion. No such subject is established.
