MMK 23.24

If the afflictions of some existed by their own nature, how could they be let go of? Who can let go of what exists by nature?

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

yadi bhūtāḥ svabhāvena kleśāḥ kecid dhi kasyacit | kathaṃ nāma prahīyeran kaḥ svabhāvaṃ prahāsyati || 24 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

If afflictions existed by their own nature, they could not be abandoned. A thing’s intrinsic nature would be unalterable, as the hardness of earth does not disappear.

Thus an inherently existent affliction could never be removed by a path. The possibility of liberation itself shows that afflictions are not fixed, self-existing entities.
