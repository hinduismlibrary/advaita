MMK 23.21

If self and purity and permanence and happiness were existent, self and purity and permanence and happiness would not be confusions.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

ātmā ca śuci nityaṃ ca sukhaṃ ca yadi vidyate | ātmā ca śuci nityaṃ ca sukhaṃ ca na viparyayaḥ || 21 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

If self, purity, permanence, and happiness existed as fixed realities, they would not be mistaken perceptions; they would simply be true of things by their own nature.

The conditional exposes the problem in treating these four notions as intrinsically real. The analysis does not establish them as independent qualities.
