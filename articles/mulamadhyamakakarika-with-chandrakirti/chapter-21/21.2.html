MMK 21.2

bhaviṣyati kathaṃ nāma vibhavaḥ saṃbhavaṃ vinā
vinaiva janma maraṇaṃ vibhavo nodbhavaṃ vinā || 2 ||

How could cessation exist without arising? There would be death without birth; cessation does not exist without arising.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

bhaviṣyati kathaṃ nāma vibhavaḥ saṃbhavaṃ vinā | vinaiva janma maraṇaṃ vibhavo nodbhavaṃ vinā || 2 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

“How could cessation exist without arising? There would be death without birth; cessation does not exist without arising.” || 2 ||

How indeed will passing away, destruction, exist without arising, production? “How indeed?” shows that it is utterly impossible, as is commonly known. The meaning is: How indeed will it exist? It certainly does not come about. If, on the other hand, passing away existed without arising, what fault would there be? It is replied: There would be death without birth; there would be death of what was not born. But death of what was not born is not seen. Therefore passing away cannot exist without arising. Here, the first half-verse is to be understood as the proposition, the middle pāda as stating the consequence, and the last as the conclusion. || 2 ||
