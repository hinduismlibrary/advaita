MMK 21.13

Things are not created from themselves, nor are they created from something else; they are not created from [both] themselves and something else. How are they created?

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

na svato jāyate bhāvaḥ parato naiva jāyate | na svataḥ parataścaiva jāyate jāyate kutaḥ || 13 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

A thing does not arise from itself or from something wholly other. It does not arise from both, and it does not arise without a cause.

Candrakīrti refers back to the first chapter’s analysis of production. Since none of these accounts establishes arising, no thing is produced by its own nature.
