MMK 21.1

vinā vā saha vā nāsti vibhavaḥ saṃbhavena vai
vinā vā saha vā nāsti saṃbhavo vibhavena vai || 1 ||

Cessation does not exist either without arising or together with arising; arising does not exist either without cessation or together with cessation.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

vinā vā saha vā nāsti vibhavaḥ saṃbhavena vai | vinā vā saha vā nāsti saṃbhavo vibhavena vai || 1 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

The twenty-first chapter, called “Examination of Arising and Passing Away.” An opponent says: “Time indeed exists by its own-nature, because it is the cause of arising and passing away. Here, depending on some particular time, the arising of a sprout, the production of entities, takes place; depending on some particular time, passing away, destruction, takes place, and not at all times, even when the collection of causes and conditions exists. Therefore time indeed exists, because it is the cause of arising and passing away.” It is replied: Time would be a cause of arising and passing away if arising and passing away themselves existed. But they do not. Showing how they do not exist, he says:

“Cessation does not exist either without arising or together with arising; arising does not exist either without cessation or together with cessation.” || 1 ||
