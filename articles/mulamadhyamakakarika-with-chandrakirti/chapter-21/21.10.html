MMK 21.10

Rising and passing cannot possibly be one; rising and passing also cannot possibly be other.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

saṃbhavo vibhavaścaiva naika ityupapadyate | saṃbhavo vibhavaścaiva na nānetyupapadyate || 10 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

Arising and passing cannot be one and the same, since they are opposed, like light and darkness. They cannot be wholly different either, because passing is never found without arising and arising is never found without passing.

Candrakīrti concludes that neither identity nor absolute difference gives these events an intrinsic existence.
