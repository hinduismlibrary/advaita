MMK 15.8

If [things] existed essentially, they would not come to non-existence. It is never the case that an essence could become something else.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

yady astitvaṃ prakṛtyā syān na bhaved asya nāstitā | prakṛter anyathābhāvo na hi jātūpapadyate || 8 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

If a thing existed by its own nature, it could never become non-existent or change. A nature that is truly intrinsic would remain what it is, like space’s unobstructed character. Yet things change and cease. Their transformation, like heat that depends on conditions, shows that they do not possess an unchanging own-nature.
