MMK 15.6

Those who view essence, thingness of the other, things and non-things do not see the suchness in the teaching of the awakened.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

svabhāvaṃ parabhāvaṃ ca bhāvaṃ cābhāvam eva ca | ye paśyanti na paśyanti te tattvaṃ buddhaśāsane || 6 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

Those who see own-nature, other-nature, things, or non-things do not see reality as taught by the Buddha. Such views mistake conventional labels for intrinsic truth. The Buddha’s teaching is authoritative because it accords with reasoning and reveals things as they are; teachings that contradict analysis cannot be treated as the final path. The dependent arising of things is deeper than any fixed claim that they truly exist or do not exist.
