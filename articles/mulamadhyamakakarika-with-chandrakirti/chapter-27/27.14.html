MMK 27.14

"I will occur at another time in the future," "I will not occur:" all those views are similar to [those of] the past.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

adhvany anāgate kiṃ nu bhaviṣyāmīti darśanam | na bhaviṣyāmi cety etad atītenādhvanā samam || 14 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

What has been shown for the past applies in the same way to the future. “Will I exist in the future?” and “Will I not exist?” are not established; the same reasoning rejects “I will both exist and not exist” and “I will neither exist nor not exist.” The wording of the earlier arguments is simply shifted from the past to the future.
