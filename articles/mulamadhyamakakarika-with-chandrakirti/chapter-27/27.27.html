MMK 27.27

How can one part of that-which-is-clung-to perish while one part does not perish? Likewise, that is unreasonable.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

upādānaikadeśaś ca kathaṃ nāma vinaṅkṣyate | na naṅkṣyate caikadeśo naitad apy upapadyate || 27 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

The same question applies to the appropriated aggregates: how could one part of that continuum perish while another part did not? No reasoning establishes such a division. Candrakīrti says to explain it just as for the appropriator. Neither the self nor the aggregates provide a basis for saying that one part of the world ends and another is endless.
