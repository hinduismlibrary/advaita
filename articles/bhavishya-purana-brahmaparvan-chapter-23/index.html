Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 23 — Vināyaka’s Obstacle-Removal Rite

BP 1.23.1

शतानीक उवाच
केनायं भीमजो विप्र प्रमथाधिपतिः कृतः ।
भर्तृत्वे चापि विघ्नानामधिकारी कथं बभौ ॥

Śatānīka said: Brāhmaṇa, by whom was this son of Bhīma made lord of the Pramathas, and how did he become the authority presiding over obstacles?

Word-for-word
शतानीकः उवाच (śatānīkaḥ uvāca) — Śatānīka said;
केन (kena) — by whom;
अयम् भीम-जः (ayam bhīma-jaḥ) — this son of Bhīma;
प्रमथ-अधिपतिः कृतः (pramatha-adhipatiḥ kṛtaḥ) — made lord of Pramathas;
विघ्नानाम् भर्तृत्वे (vighnānām bhartṛtve) — as ruler of obstacles;
अधिकारी कथम् बभौ (adhikārī katham babhau) — how became authority
Transliteration
śatānīka uvāca
kenāyaṃ bhīmajo vipra pramathādhipatiḥ kṛtaḥ |
bhartṛtve cāpi vighnānāmadhikārī kathaṃ babhau | | 1

BP 1.23.2

सुमन्तुरुवाच
साधु पृष्टोऽस्मि राजेन्द्र यदयं विघ्नकारकः ।
यैर्वापि विघ्नकरणैर्निर्युक्तोऽपि विनायकः ॥
तत्ते वच्मि महाबाहो शृणुष्वैकमनाधुना ॥

Sumantu said: You have asked well, O king. I shall tell you how he came to create obstacles and by what obstruction-producing functions Vināyaka was appointed. Mighty-armed one, listen now with one-pointed attention.

Word-for-word
सुमन्तुः उवाच (sumantuḥ uvāca) — Sumantu said;
साधु पृष्टः अस्मि (sādhu pṛṣṭaḥ asmi) — I am well asked;
यत् अयम् विघ्न-कारकः (yat ayam vighna-kārakaḥ) — how he creates obstacles;
विघ्न-करणैः (vighna-karaṇaiḥ) — by obstruction functions;
निर्युक्तः विनायकः (niryuktaḥ vināyakaḥ) — Vināyaka appointed;
तत् ते वच्मि (tat te vacmi) — I tell you;
शृणु एक-मनाः (śṛṇu eka-manāḥ) — listen single-mindedly
Transliteration
sumanturuvāca
sādhu pṛṣṭo'smi rājendra yadayaṃ vighnakārakaḥ |
yairvāpi vighnakaraṇairniryukto'pi vināyakaḥ | |
tatte vacmi mahābāho śṛṇuṣvaikamanādhunā | | 2

BP 1.23.3

आद्ये कृतयुगे वीर प्रजासर्गमवाप ह ।
दृष्ट्वा कर्माणि सिद्धानि विना विघ्नेन भारत ॥

At the beginning of the Kṛta age, when creation had arisen, O hero and descendant of Bharata, deeds were seen to succeed without obstacles.

कर्माणि सिद्धानि (karmāṇi siddhāni) — deeds accomplished;
विना विघ्नेन (vinā vighnena) — without obstacle
Transliteration

BP 1.23.4

अगतक्लेशां प्रजां दृष्ट्वा गर्वितां कृत्स्नशो नृप ।
बहुशश्चिन्तयित्या तु इदं कर्म महीपते ॥

Seeing the entire population free from hardship and consequently arrogant, O king, the Lord reflected repeatedly upon this matter.

गर्विताम् कृत्स्नशः (garvitām kṛtsnaśaḥ) — wholly arrogant;
दृष्ट्वा (dṛṣṭvā) — seeing;
बहुशः चिन्तयित्वा (bahuśaḥ cintayitvā) — reflecting repeatedly;
इदम् कर्म (idam karma) — this matter
Transliteration

BP 1.23.5

विनायकः समृद्ध्यर्थं प्रजानां विनियोजितः ।
गणानां चाधिपत्ये च भीमः कञ्जजसात्त्वतैः ॥
ततोपसृष्टो यस्तस्य लक्षणानि निबोध मे ॥

For the proper prosperity of creatures, Vināyaka was assigned his function, while Bhīma was appointed lord over the Gaṇas by the lotus-born Brahmā and the Sātvata Lord. Now understand the signs of one afflicted by him.

Word-for-word
विनायकः विनियोजितः (vināyakaḥ viniyojitaḥ) — Vināyaka assigned;
समृद्धि-अर्थम् प्रजानाम् (samṛddhi-artham prajānām) — for creatures’ prosperity;
गणानाम् आधिपत्ये (gaṇānām ādhipatye) — lordship over Gaṇas;
भीमः (bhīmaḥ) — Bhīma;
कञ्ज-ज-सात्वतैः (kañja-ja-sātvataiḥ) — by lotus-born and Sātvata;
तेन उपसृष्टः (tena upasṛṣṭaḥ) — afflicted by him;
लक्षणानि निबोध (lakṣaṇāni nibodha) — understand signs
Transliteration

BP 1.23.6

BP 1.23.7

BP 1.23.10

His mind assumes a distorted form adorned with oleander. One afflicted by Vināyaka does not obtain the kingdom inherited from his ancestors.

BP 1.23.11

कुमारी न च भर्तारमपत्यं गर्भिणी तथा ।
आचार्यत्वं श्रोत्रियश्च शिष्याश्चाध्ययनं तथा ॥
वणिग्लाभं च नाप्नोति कृषिं चैव कृषीवलः ॥

A maiden does not obtain a husband, nor a pregnant woman a child; a Vedic scholar does not gain a teachers position, nor students their learning; a merchant obtains no profit, nor a farmer his crop.

शिष्याः न अध्ययनम् (śiṣyāḥ na adhyayanam) — students not study;
वणिक् न लाभम् (vaṇik na lābham) — merchant not profit;
कृषीवलः न कृषिम् (kṛṣīvalaḥ na kṛṣim) — farmer not crop
Transliteration
