Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 23 — Vināyaka’s Obstacle-Removal Rite

BP 1.23.1

शतानीक उवाच
केनायं भीमजो विप्र प्रमथाधिपतिः कृतः ।
भर्तृत्वे चापि विघ्नानामधिकारी कथं बभौ ॥

Śatānīka said: O Brahmin, who made this son of Bhīma lord of the attendants, and how did he become the authority over obstacles?

BP 1.23.2

सुमन्तुरुवाच
साधु पृष्टोऽस्मि राजेन्द्र यदयं विघ्नकारकः ।
यैर्वापि विघ्नकरणैर्निर्युक्तोऽपि विनायकः ॥
तत्ते वच्मि महाबाहो शृणुष्वैकमनाधुना ॥

Sumantu said: You have asked well, O king. I shall tell how Vināyaka became an obstacle-maker and what kinds of obstacles he was appointed to cause. O mighty-armed one, listen now with a focused mind.

BP 1.23.3

आद्ये कृतयुगे वीर प्रजासर्गमवाप ह ।
दृष्ट्वा कर्माणि सिद्धानि विना विघ्नेन भारत ॥

At the beginning of the Kṛta age, creation of beings arose, O hero. O descendant of Bharata, deeds were seen to succeed without obstacles.

BP 1.23.4

अगतक्लेशां प्रजां दृष्ट्वा गर्वितां कृत्स्नशो नृप ।
बहुशश्चिन्तयित्या तु इदं कर्म महीपते ॥

Seeing the people free from hardship and wholly proud, O king, he thought repeatedly about this action, O ruler of the earth.

BP 1.23.5

विनायकः समृद्ध्यर्थं प्रजानां विनियोजितः ।
गणानां चाधिपत्ये च भीमः कञ्जजसात्त्वतैः ॥
ततोपसृष्टो यस्तस्य लक्षणानि निबोध मे ॥

The terrible Vināyaka was appointed by Brahmā and Kṛṣṇa for the prosperity of creatures and lordship over the hosts. Learn from me the signs of one afflicted by him.

BP 1.23.6

स्वप्नेवगाहतेऽत्यर्थं जलं मुण्डांश्च पश्यति ।
काषायवाससश्चैव क्रव्यादांश्चाधिरोहति ॥

In dreams, he plunges deeply into water, sees shaven-headed men and men wearing saffron garments, and mounts flesh-eating beasts.

BP 1.23.7

अन्त्यजैर्गर्दभैरुष्ट्रैः सहैकत्रावतिष्ठते ।
व्रजमानस्तथात्मानं मन्यतेऽनुगतं परैः ॥

He stays together with outcastes, donkeys, and camels; while walking, he thinks that others are following him.

BP 1.23.8

विमना विफलारम्भः संसीदत्यनिमित्ततः ।
करटारूढमात्मानमम्भसोन्तरगं तथा ॥

He is dejected, his undertakings fail, and he sinks without cause. He sees himself mounted on a donkey and also submerged in water.

BP 1.23.9

पत्तिभिश्चावृतं यान्तं सङ्गमनान्तिकं नृप ।
पश्यते कुरुशार्दूल स्वप्नान्ते नात्र संशयः ॥

O king, at the end of a dream, he sees himself going toward a confluence, surrounded by foot soldiers, O tiger among the Kurus; there is no doubt.

BP 1.23.10

चित्तं च विकृताकारं करवीरविभूषितम् ।
तेनोपसृष्टो लभते न राज्यं पौर्वसंभवम् ॥

His mind takes a distorted form, adorned with oleander. Afflicted by him, a man does not obtain the kingdom inherited from his ancestors.

BP 1.23.11

कुमारी न च भर्तारमपत्यं गर्भिणी तथा ।
आचार्यत्वं श्रोत्रियश्च शिष्याश्चाध्ययनं तथा ॥
वणिग्लाभं च नाप्नोति कृषिं चैव कृषीवलः ॥

A maiden does not obtain a husband, nor a pregnant woman a child. A teacher does not obtain teacherhood, a Vedic scholar students, or students their studies; a merchant does not gain profit, nor a farmer cultivation.

BP 1.23.12

स्नपनं तस्य कर्तव्यं पुण्येऽहनि ' महीपते ।
गौरसर्षपकल्केन साज्येनोत्सादितेन तु ॥

His ritual bathing should be performed on an auspicious day, O king, after he is rubbed with a paste of white mustard mixed with ghee.

BP 1.23.13

शुक्लपक्षे चतुर्थ्यां तु वासरे धिषणस्य च ।
तिष्ये च वीरनक्षत्रे तस्यैव पुरतो नृप ॥

On the fourth day of the bright fortnight, on Thursday, under the Tiṣya star, in his presence, O hero, O king.

BP 1.23.14

सर्वौषधैः सर्वगन्धैर्विलिप्तशिरसस्तथा ।
भद्रासनोपविष्टस्य स्वस्ति वाच्य द्विजाञ्छुभान् ॥

His head should be smeared with all medicinal herbs and fragrances. Seated on an auspicious seat, he should have the auspicious Brahmins speak blessings.

BP 1.23.15

व्योमकेशं तु सम्पूज्य पार्वतीं भीमजं तथा ।
कृष्णं सपितरं तात पवमानं सितं तथा ॥

After worshipping Vyomakeśa, Pārvatī, the son of Bhīma, Kṛṣṇa together with his father, Pavamāna, and Śita,

BP 1.23.16

धिषणं चेन्दुपुत्रं च कोणं केतुं च भारत ।
विधुन्तुदं बाहुलेयं नन्दकस्य च धारिणम् ॥

—also Jupiter, the Moon's son, Saturn, Ketu, Rāhu, Bāhuleya, and the wielder of Nandaka, O descendant of Bharata.

BP 1.23.17

अश्वस्थानाद्गजस्थानाद्वल्मीकात्सङ्गमाद्ह्रदात् ।
मृत्तिकां रोचनां गन्धान्गुग्गुलं चाप्सु निक्षिपेत् ॥

One should put into the water earth from a horse-stall, an elephant-stall, an anthill, a confluence, and a lake, yellow pigment, fragrances, and guggul resin.

BP 1.23.18

यदाहृतं ह्येकवर्णैश्चतुर्भिः कलशैर्ह्रदात् ।
चर्मण्यानडुहे रक्ते स्थाप्यं भद्रासनं तथा ॥

The water brought from the lake in four pots of the same color should be placed on a red oxhide; the auspicious seat should be placed there as well.

BP 1.23.19

सहस्राक्षं शतधारमृषिभिः पावनं कृतम् ।
तेन त्वामभिषिञ्चामि पावमान्यः पुनन्तु ते ॥

With that purifying water, thousand-eyed and hundred-streamed, made pure by the sages, I consecrate you. May the purifying hymns cleanse you.

BP 1.23.20

भगं ते वरुणो राजा भगं सूर्यो बृहस्पतिः ।
भगमिन्द्रश्च वायुश्च भगं सप्तर्षयो ददुः ॥

May King Varuṇa give you fortune, and the Sun and Bṛhaspati; may Indra and Vāyu give you fortune, and the Seven Seers.

BP 1.23.21

यत्ते केशेषु दौर्भाग्यं सीमन्ते यच्च मूर्धनि ।
ललाटे कर्णयोरक्ष्णोरापस्तद् घ्नन्तु ते सदा ॥

Whatever misfortune is in your hair, in its parting, on your head, forehead, ears, and eyes, may the waters always destroy it.

BP 1.23.22

स्नातस्य सार्षपं तैलं सुवेणौदुम्बरेण तु ।
जुहुयान्मूर्धनि कुशान्सव्येन परिगृह्य तु ॥

After he has bathed, one should offer mustard oil upon his head with a figwood ladle, holding sacrificial grass in the left hand.

BP 1.23.23

मितश्च सम्मितश्चैव तथा च शालकंटकः ।
कूष्माण्डो राजश्रेष्ठास्तेऽग्नयः स्वाहासमन्विताः ॥

Mita, Sammita, Śālakaṇṭaka, and Kūṣmāṇḍa, O best of kings—these are the fires; [invoke them] with the utterance svāhā.

BP 1.23.24

नामभिर्बलिमन्त्रैश्च नमस्कारसमन्वितैः ।
दद्याच्चतुष्पथे शूर्पे कुशानास्तीर्य सर्वतः ॥

With their names, offering formulas, and salutations, one should spread grass all around in a winnowing basket and give the offering at a crossroads.

BP 1.23.25

कृताकृतांस्तण्डुलांश्च पललौदनमेव च ।
मत्स्यान्पक्वांस्तथैवामान्मांसमेतावदेव तु ॥

Cooked and uncooked rice grains, rice with sesame paste, cooked and raw fish, and the same amount of meat.

BP 1.23.26

पुष्पं चित्रं सगन्धं च सुरां च त्रिविधामपि ।
मूलकं पूरिकाः पूपांस्तथैवोण्डेरिकास्रजम् ॥
दधिपायसमन्नं च गुडवेष्टान्समोदकान् ॥

Variegated fragrant flowers, three kinds of liquor, radish, fried breads, cakes, a garland of small pastries, curd, milk-rice, food, jaggery cakes, and sweet dumplings.

BP 1.23.27

विनायकस्य जननीमुपतिष्ठेत्ततोऽम्बिकाम् ।
दूर्वासर्षपपुष्पाणां दत्त्वा पुष्पाञ्जलित्रयम् ॥

Then one should approach Ambikā, Vināyaka's mother, and offer three handfuls of sacred grass, mustard, and flowers.

BP 1.23.28

रूपं देहि यशो देहि भगं भगवति देहि मे ।
पुत्रान्देहि धनं देहि सर्वान्कामांश्च देहि मे ॥
अचलां बुद्धिं मे देहि धरायां ख्यातिमेव च ॥

Give me beauty; give me fame; give me fortune, O Goddess. Give me sons and wealth, and give me every desire. Give me unwavering understanding and renown on earth.

BP 1.23.29

ततः शुक्लाम्बरधरः शुक्लमाल्यानुलेपनः ।
भोजयेद् ब्राह्मणान्दद्याद्वस्त्रयुग्मं गुरोरपि ॥

Then, wearing white garments, white garlands, and unguents, he should feed Brahmins and give the teacher a pair of garments.

BP 1.23.30

एवं विनायकं पूज्य ग्रहांश्चैव विधानतः ।
कर्मणां फलमाप्नोति श्रियं प्राप्नोत्यनुत्तमाम् ॥

Thus worshipping Vināyaka and the planets according to rule, one obtains the fruits of actions and attains unsurpassed prosperity.

BP 1.23.31

आदित्यस्य सदा पूजां तिलकं स्वामिनस्तथा ।
विनायकपतेश्चैव सर्वसिद्धिमवाप्नुयात् ॥

By constant worship of the Sun, Svāmin's mark, and Lord Vināyaka, one obtains every accomplishment.
