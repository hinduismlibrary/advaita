Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 82 — Nanda Saptamī and Its Merits

BP 1.82.1

दिण्डिर् उवाच । ये त्व् आदित्य-दिने ब्रह्मन् पूजयन्ति दिवाकरम् । स्नान-दानादिकं तेषां किं फलं स्याद् ब्रवीतु मे ॥ ॥

Diṇḍin said: O Brahman, what fruit belongs to those who worship Divākara on the day of Āditya, with bathing, giving, and the other rites? Tell me.

Transliteration

BP 1.82.24

पठतां शृण्वतां चेदं विधानं त्रिपुरान्तक । कं ददात्य् अचलं दिव्यम् अम्बुजाम् अचलां तथा ॥ ॥

For those who read and hear this rite, O destroyer of Tripura, it grants an unshakable divine [attainment] and likewise an unshakable lotus [of fortune].

paṭhatāṃ śṛṇvatāṃ cedaṃ vidhānaṃ tripurāntaka | kaṃ dadāty acalaṃ divyam ambujām acalāṃ tathā || 24 ||
