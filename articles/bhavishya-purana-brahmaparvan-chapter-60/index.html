Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 60 — The Chariot Journey and Sūrya Worship

BP 1.60.1

इत्युक्त्वा स जगामाशु सुरज्येष्ठं त्रिलोचनम् ।
रथयात्रा महाबाहो सूर्यस्येत्यमितौजसः ॥

Sumantu said: Having said this, he quickly went to the eldest of the gods, the three-eyed one. O mighty-armed one, the chariot-procession of the Sun of immeasurable splendor.

Word-for-word
iti uktvā — having said this; saḥ jagāma āśu — he went quickly; sura-jyeṣṭham — the eldest of the gods; trilocanam — the three-eyed one; ratha-yātrā — chariot-procession; mahābāho — O mighty-armed one; sūryasya — of the Sun; amita-ojas — of immeasurable splendor
Transliteration
ity uktvā sa jagāmāśu surajyeṣṭhaṃ trilocanam |
rathayātrā mahābāho sūryasyety amitaujasaḥ || 1 ||

BP 1.60.2

यमाराध्य जगन्नाथं मम पूर्वपितामहाः ।
तुष्ट्यर्थं ब्राह्मणानां त्वन्नमापुश्चतुर्विधम् ॥

Śatānīka said: Having worshiped the Lord of the world, my forefathers obtained four kinds of food for the satisfaction of the Brāhmaṇas.

Word-for-word
yam ārādhya — having worshiped whom; jagannātham — Lord of the world; mama pūrva-pitāmahāḥ — my forefathers; tuṣṭyartham — for satisfaction; brāhmaṇānām — of Brāhmaṇas; annam āpuḥ caturvidham — obtained four kinds of food
Transliteration
yam ārādhya jagannāthaṃ mama pūrvapitāmahāḥ |
tuṣṭyarthaṃ brāhmaṇānāṃ tv annam āpuś caturvidham || 2 ||

BP 1.60.3

तस्य देवस्य माहात्म्यं श्रुतं च बहुशो मया ।
देवर्षिसिद्धमनुजैः स्तुतस्य हि दिने दिने ॥

I have heard many times the greatness of that god, who is praised day after day by divine sages, Siddhas, and men.

Word-for-word
tasya devasya — of that god; māhātmyam — greatness; śrutam — heard; bahuśaḥ — many times; mayā — by me; deva-ṛṣi-siddha-manujaiḥ — by divine sages, Siddhas, and men; stutasya — of the praised one; dine dine — day after day
Transliteration
tasya devasya māhātmyaṃ śrutaṃ ca bahuśo mayā |
devarṣi-siddha-manujaiḥ stutasya hi dine dine || 3 ||

BP 1.60.4

कः स्तोतुमीशस्तमजं यस्यैतत्सचराचरम् ।
अव्ययस्याप्रमेयस्य विबुध्येतोदयाज्जगत् ॥

Who is able to praise that unborn one, whose moving and unmoving creation is this—the imperishable, immeasurable one, by whose rising the world awakens?

Word-for-word
kaḥ — who; stotum īśaḥ — able to praise; tam ajam — that unborn one; yasya etat sacarācaram — whose is this moving and unmoving; avyayasya aprameyasya — of the imperishable, immeasurable one; udayāt jagat vibudhyeta — the world awakens from whose rising
Transliteration
kaḥ stotum īśas tam ajaṃ yasyaitat sacarācaram |
avyayasyāprameyasya vibudhyetodayāj jagat || 4 ||

BP 1.60.5

कराभ्यां यस्य देवेशौ कविष्णू लोकपूजितौ ।
उत्पन्नौ द्विजशार्दूल ललाटात्त्रिपुरान्तकः ॥

From whose two hands, O tiger among Brāhmaṇas, arose the two lords of the gods, Brahmā and Viṣṇu, worshiped by the world; and from whose forehead came the destroyer of Tripura.

Word-for-word
karābhyām — from the two hands; yasya — of whom; deveśau kaviṣṇū — the two divine lords, Brahmā and Viṣṇu; lokapūjitau — worshiped by the world; utpannau — arose; dvijaśārdūla — O tiger among Brāhmaṇas; lalāṭāt — from the forehead; tripurāntakaḥ — the destroyer of Tripura
Transliteration
karābhyāṃ yasya deveśau kaviṣṇū lokapūjitau |
utpannau dvijaśārdūla lalāṭāt tripurāntakaḥ || 5 ||

BP 1.60.6

तस्य देवस्य कैः शक्या वक्तुं सर्वा विभूतयः ।
सोऽहमिच्छामि देवस्य तस्य सर्वात्मना द्विज ॥

By whom can all the glories of that god be told? Therefore, O twice-born one, I wish with all my being to hear of that god.

Word-for-word
tasya devasya — of that god; kaiḥ śakyāḥ — by whom are possible; vaktum — to tell; sarvāḥ vibhūtayaḥ — all glories; saḥ aham — therefore I; icchāmi — wish; sarvātmanā — with all my being; dvija — O twice-born one
Transliteration
tasya devasya kaiḥ śakyā vaktuṃ sarvā vibhūtayaḥ |
so'ham icchāmi devasya tasya sarvātmanā dvija || 6 ||

BP 1.60.7

श्रोतुमाराधनं येन निस्तरेयं भवार्णवम् ।
केनोपायेन मन्त्रैर्वा रहस्यैः परिचर्यया ॥

I wish to hear the worship by which I might cross the ocean of becoming: by what means, by mantras, by secrets, or by service?

Word-for-word
śrotum — to hear; ārādhanam — worship; yena — by which; nistareyam — I might cross; bhava-arṇavam — the ocean of worldly existence; kena upāyena — by what means; mantraiḥ vā — by mantras or; rahasyaiḥ — by secret teachings; paricaryayā — by service
Transliteration
śrotum ārādhanaṃ yena nistareyaṃ bhavārṇavam |
kenopāyena mantrair vā rahasyaiḥ paricaryayā || 7 ||

BP 1.60.8

दानैर्वृतोपवासैर्वा होमैर्जाप्यैरथापि वा ।
आराधितः समस्तानां क्लेशानां हानिदो भवेत् ॥

Or, worshiped by gifts, vows, fasts, oblations, recitations, or any of these, may he become the remover of all afflictions?

Word-for-word
dānaiḥ — by gifts; vrata-upavāsaiḥ — by vows and fasts; homaiḥ — by oblations; jāpyaiḥ — by recitations; atha api vā — or by any; ārādhitaḥ — worshiped; samastānām kleśānām — of all afflictions; hānidaḥ bhavet — may become the remover
Transliteration
dānair vratopavāsair vā homair jāpyair athāpi vā |
ārādhitaḥ samastānāṃ kleśānāṃ hānido bhavet || 8 ||

BP 1.60.9

सैका विद्या हि विद्यानां यया तुष्यति सर्वकृत् ।
श्रुतानामपि तत्पुण्यं यत्र भानोः प्रकीर्तनम् ॥

That alone is the knowledge among knowledges by which the Doer of all is pleased; even the merit of what is heard is there where Bhānus praise is proclaimed.

Word-for-word
sā ekā vidyā — that one knowledge; vidyānām — among knowledges; yayā — by which; tuṣyati — is pleased; sarva-kṛt — the doer of all; śrutānām api — even of what is heard; tat puṇyam — that merit; yatra — where; bhānoḥ prakīrtanam — praise of Bhānu
Transliteration
sāikā vidyā hi vidyānāṃ yayā tuṣyati sarvakṛt |
śrutānām api tat puṇyaṃ yatra bhānoḥ prakīrtanam || 9 ||

BP 1.60.10

रहस्यानां रहस्यं तद्येन हंसः प्रसीदति ।
एकः श्रेष्ठतमो मंत्रस्तदेकं परमं व्रतम् ॥

That is the secret of secrets by which Haṃsa is pleased; that one is the most excellent mantra, that one the supreme vow.

Word-for-word
rahasyānām rahasyam — secret of secrets; yena — by which; haṃsaḥ prasīdati — Haṃsa is pleased; ekaḥ śreṣṭhatamaḥ mantraḥ — one most excellent mantra; tat ekam paramam vratam — that one supreme vow
Transliteration
rahasyānāṃ rahasyaṃ tad yena haṃsaḥ prasīdati |
ekaḥ śreṣṭhatamo mantraḥ tad ekaṃ paramaṃ vratam || 10 ||

BP 1.60.11

उपोषितं च तच्छ्रेष्ठं येन भानुः प्रसीदति ।
सा चैका रसना धन्या मार्तण्डं स्तौति या सदा ॥

That fast is the most excellent by which Bhānu is pleased; that tongue alone is blessed which always praises Mārtaṇḍa.

Word-for-word
upoṣitam — fasted; tat śreṣṭham — that most excellent; yena — by which; bhānuḥ prasīdati — Bhānu is pleased; sā ekā rasanā — that one tongue; dhanyā — blessed; yā stauti — which praises; sadā — always
Transliteration
upoṣitaṃ ca tac chreṣṭhaṃ yena bhānuḥ prasīdati |
sā caikā rasanā dhanyā mārtaṇḍaṃ stauti yā sadā || 11 ||

BP 1.60.12

तदेकं निर्मलं चित्तं यद्गतं सततं रवौ ।
श्लाघ्यानामपि तौ श्लाघ्याविह लोके परत्र च ॥

That alone is the pure mind which is always directed to Ravi; those two are praiseworthy among the praiseworthy, here in this world and beyond.

Word-for-word
tat ekam nirmalam cittam — that one pure mind; yat gatam — which has gone; satatam ravau — always to Ravi; ślāghyānām api — even among the praiseworthy; tau ślāghyau — those two praiseworthy; iha loke paratra ca — here and in the next world
Transliteration
tad ekaṃ nirmalaṃ cittaṃ yad gataṃ satataṃ ravau |
ślāghyānām api tau ślāghyāv iha loke paratra ca || 12 ||

BP 1.60.13

यो सदा द्विजशार्दूल भानोः पूजाकरौ करौ ।
तदेकं केवलं धन्यं शरीरं सर्वजन्तुषु ॥

That body alone is blessed among all creatures whose two hands always perform the worship of Bhānu, O tiger among Brāhmaṇas.

Word-for-word
yaḥ — whoever; sadā — always; dvijaśārdūla — O tiger among Brāhmaṇas; bhānoḥ pūjā-karau karau — two hands making worship of Bhānu; tat ekam kevalam — that alone; dhanyam śarīram — blessed body; sarva-jantuṣu — among all creatures
Transliteration
yo sadā dvijaśārdūla bhānoḥ pūjākarau karau |
tad ekaṃ kevalaṃ dhanyaṃ śarīraṃ sarvajantuṣu || 13 ||

BP 1.60.14

यदेव पुलकोद्भासि भानोर्नामानुकीर्तने ।
सा जिह्वा कण्ठतालूकमथ वा प्रतिजिह्विका ॥

That which shines with horripilation during the recitation of Bhānus name is the tongue, the throat-palate, or the secondary tongue.

Word-for-word
yat eva — that indeed; pulaka-udbhāsi — shining with horripilation; bhānoḥ nāma-anukīrtane — during recitation of Bhānu’s name; sā jihvā — that is the tongue; kaṇṭha-tālūkam — throat-palate; atha vā — or; prati-jihvikā — secondary tongue
Transliteration
yad eva pulakodbhāsi bhānor nāmānukīrtane |
sā jihvā kaṇṭhatālūkam atha vā pratijihvikā || 14 ||

BP 1.60.15

अथ वा सापरो रोगो या न वक्ति रवेर्गुणम् ।
नवद्वाराणि सन्त्यस्मिन्पुरे पुरुषसत्तम ॥

Or else that is another disease which does not speak of Ravis virtues. There are nine gates in this city, O best of men.

Word-for-word
atha vā — or else; sā — that; aparaḥ rogaḥ — another disease; yā na vakti — which does not speak; raveḥ guṇam — Ravi’s virtues; nava-dvārāṇi — nine gates; santi asmin pure — are in this city; puruṣasattama — O best of men
Transliteration
atha vā sā paro rogo yā na vakti raver guṇam |
nava-dvārāṇi santy asmin pure puruṣasattama || 15 ||

BP 1.60.16

प्राकारैस्त्वावृते विष्वग्वृथा तानि विदुर्बुधाः ।
दत्त्वावधानं यच्छब्दे विनैव रविसंस्तुतिम् ॥

The wise know those gates, surrounded on every side by ramparts, to be useless when attention is given to sound without the praise of Ravi.

Word-for-word
prākāraiḥ āvṛte viṣvak — surrounded everywhere by ramparts; vṛthā tāni viduḥ budhāḥ — the wise know them useless; dattvā avadhānam — giving attention; yat śabde — to sound; vinā eva — without; ravi-saṃstutim — praise of Ravi
Transliteration
prākārais tv āvṛte viṣvag vṛthā tāni vidur budhāḥ |
dattvāvadhānaṃ yac chabde vinaiva ravisaṃstutim || 16 ||

BP 1.60.17

श्रेयसां न हि सम्प्राप्तौ पुरुषाणां विचेष्टितम् ।
जन्मन्यविफला सेवा कृता याश्रित्य भास्करम् ॥

There is no human effort in the attainment of blessedness that is fruitless; service performed in a birth by taking refuge in Bhāskara is never fruitless.

Word-for-word
śreyasām samprāptau — in attaining blessedness; na hi — indeed not; puruṣāṇām viceṣṭitam — human effort; janmani — in a birth; aviphalā sevā — fruitless service; kṛtā — performed; āśritya bhāskaram — having taken refuge in Bhāskara
Transliteration
śreyasāṃ na hi samprāptau puruṣāṇāṃ viceṣṭitam |
janmany aviphalā sevā kṛtā yāśritya bhāskaram || 17 ||

BP 1.60.18

दुर्गसंसारकान्तारमपारमभिधावताम् ।
एको भानुनमस्कारः संसारार्णवतारकः ॥

For those running through the impassable wilderness of saṃsāra, a single salutation to Bhānu is the ferryman across the ocean of worldly existence.

Word-for-word
durga-saṃsāra-kāntāram — difficult wilderness of saṃsāra; apāram — boundless; abhidhāvatām — for those running through; ekaḥ — one; bhānu-namaskāraḥ — salutation to Bhānu; saṃsāra-arṇava-tārakaḥ — ferry across the ocean of saṃsāra
Transliteration
durga-saṃsāra-kāntāram apāram abhidhāvatām |
eko bhānu-namaskāraḥ saṃsārārṇava-tārakaḥ || 18 ||

BP 1.60.19

रत्नानामाकरो मेरुः सर्वाश्चर्यमयं नभः ।
तीर्थानामाश्रयो गङ्गा देवानामाश्रयो रविः ॥

Meru is the mine of jewels, the sky is made of every wonder; Gaṅgā is the refuge of sacred fords, and Ravi is the refuge of the gods.

Word-for-word
ratnānām ākaraḥ — mine of jewels; meruḥ — Meru; sarva-āścarya-mayam nabhaḥ — sky made of every wonder; tīrthānām āśrayaḥ — refuge of sacred fords; gaṅgā — Gaṅgā; devānām āśrayaḥ — refuge of the gods; raviḥ — Ravi
Transliteration
ratnānām ākaro meruḥ sarvāścaryamayaṃ nabhaḥ |
tīrthānām āśrayo gaṅgā devānām āśrayo raviḥ || 19 ||

BP 1.60.20

एवमादिगुणो भोगो भानोरमिततेजसः ।
श्रुतो मे बहुशः सिद्धैर्गीयमानैस्तथामरैः ॥

Such and other qualities and enjoyments of Bhānu, whose radiance is immeasurable, have been heard by me many times from Siddhas and likewise from singing immortals.

Word-for-word
evam-ādi-guṇaḥ bhogaḥ — such and other qualities and enjoyments; bhānoḥ amita-tejasaḥ — of Bhānu of immeasurable radiance; śrutaḥ me — heard by me; bahuśaḥ — many times; siddhaiḥ — from Siddhas; gīyamānaiḥ amaraiḥ — from singing immortals
Transliteration
evamādi-guṇo bhogo bhānor amitatejasaḥ |
śruto me bahuśaḥ siddhair gīyamānais tathāmaraiḥ || 20 ||

BP 1.60.21

सोऽहमिच्छामि तं देवं सप्तलोकपरायणम् ।
दिवाकरमशेषस्य जगतो हृद्यवस्थितम् ॥

Therefore I wish to hear of that god, the refuge of the seven worlds, Divākara, abiding in the heart of the entire universe.

Word-for-word
saḥ aham — therefore I; icchāmi — wish; tam devam — that god; sapta-loka-parāyaṇam — refuge of the seven worlds; divākaram — Divākara; aśeṣasya jagataḥ — of the entire universe; hṛdi avasthitam — abiding in the heart
Transliteration
so'ham icchāmi taṃ devaṃ sapta-loka-parāyaṇam |
divākaram aśeṣasya jagato hṛdy avasthitam || 21 ||

BP 1.60.22

आराधयितुमीशेशं भास्करं चामितौजसम् ।
मार्तण्डं भुवनाधारं स्मृतमात्राघदारिणम् ॥

I wish to worship Bhāskara, the lord of lords, of immeasurable splendor, Mārtaṇḍa, the support of the worlds, who destroys sins at the mere thought of him.

Word-for-word
ārādhayitum — to worship; īśeśam — lord of lords; bhāskaram — Bhāskara; amita-ojasam — of immeasurable splendor; mārtaṇḍam — Mārtaṇḍa; bhuvana-ādhāram — support of the worlds; smṛta-mātra-aghadāriṇam — destroying sin by mere remembrance
Transliteration
ārādhayitum īśeśaṃ bhāskaraṃ cāmitaujasam |
mārtaṇḍaṃ bhuvanādhāraṃ smṛtamātrāghadāriṇam || 22 ||
