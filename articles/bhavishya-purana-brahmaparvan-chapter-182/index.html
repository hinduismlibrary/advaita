Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 182 — Marriage Procedure

BP 1.182.1

।। आदित्य उवाच ।। उक्तं धर्मस्य रूपं तु साधिकारं सनातनम् ।। अतः परं प्रवक्ष्यामि धर्ममाश्रमिणां खग ।। १ ।।

Āditya said: The eternal form of dharma, together with its qualifications, has been told. From here I shall tell the dharma of those in the four stages of life, O bird.

BP 1.182.2

ब्रह्मचारी गृहस्थश्च वनस्थो भिक्षुरेव च ।। चत्वार आश्रमाः प्रोक्ता एक एव चतुर्विधः ।। २ ।।

Vedic student, householder, forest-dweller, and mendicant: the four stages of life are declared, one indeed in four forms.

BP 1.182.3

गायत्रो ब्रह्मचारी तु प्राजापत्यो द्वितीयकः ।। देवव्रतस्तृतीयस्तु नैष्ठिकस्तु चतुर्थकः ।। ३ ।।

The Gāyatrī student is first, the student of Prajāpati second, the student of the divine vow third, and the lifelong student fourth.

BP 1.182.4

चत्वार आश्रमाः प्रोक्ताः सवेदाः समधर्मकाः ।। अतः परं प्रवक्ष्यामि संस्कारं धर्मसिद्धये ।। ४ ।।

The four stages of life are declared, with their Vedas, equal in dharma. From here I shall tell the life-rites for the fulfillment of dharma.

BP 1.182.5

गर्भाधानमृतौ कार्यं हृष्टयोस्तु समन्त्रकम् ।। कार्यं पुंसवनं मातुस्तृतीये मासि संयुतैः ।। ५ ।।

The conception rite should be performed in the fertile season by a joyful couple, with mantras. The rite for securing a male child should be performed for the mother in the third month, with the proper observances.

BP 1.182.6

सीमन्तः सप्तमे गर्भे षष्ठे वा सप्तमेऽपि वा ।। पात्रसंस्कारका इष्टा गर्भाधानादयस्त्रयः ।। ६ ।।

The parting of the hair is in the seventh month of pregnancy, or in the sixth or seventh. The three rites beginning with conception are prescribed for the purification of the womb.

BP 1.182.7

जातकर्मादयः सर्वे संस्काराः पुरुषस्य तु ।। जातस्य प्राशनं यत्र स्वर्णा दीनां समन्त्रकम् ।। ७ ।।

All a man’s life-rites begin with the birth rite. At the birth, the newborn is fed gold and the like, with mantras.

BP 1.182.8

जातकर्मेति तत्प्रोक्तं गुह्यं नाम तदैव तु ।। प्रकाशो नाम कर्तव्यं दिने त्वेकादशेर्थवत् ।। ८ ।।

That is called the birth rite. A secret name is given at that time; a public name, meaningful, is to be given on the eleventh day.

BP 1.182.9

धर्मशास्त्रादिसंयुक्तं षष्ठेऽन्नप्राशनं खग ।। प्रथमेऽब्दे तृतीये वा चूडाकर्म विधीयते ।। ९ ।।

Connected with the treatises on dharma and the rest, the first feeding of solid food is in the sixth month, O bird. Hair-cutting is prescribed in the first or third year.

BP 1.182.10

अष्टमे दशमे वापि ब्राह्मणस्योपनायनम् ।। पुरुषस्य तथा चान्यजातीयानां विशेषतः ।। १० ।।

The initiation of a Brahmin is in the eighth or tenth year; likewise that of men of other castes, in particular.

BP 1.182.11

एकादशे द्वादशे वा कार्य क्षत्रियवैश्ययोः ।। वेदसंस्कारकं वच्मि मन्यन्ते त्वौपनायनम् ।। ११ ।।

For a Kṣatriya and Vaiśya it should be performed in the eleventh or twelfth year. I shall tell the rite that consecrates one for the Veda; they consider it to be initiation.

BP 1.182.12

पुरुषस्य तथा चान्य उभयोश्च ब्रवीम्यहम् ।। सावित्रं वैदिकं चैव महानाम्नीमहाव्रतम् ।। १२ ।।

For a man, for others, and for both, I speak of the Gāyatrī vow, the Vedic vow, the Great Name, and the Great Vow;

BP 1.182.13

तथौपनिषदं चाब्दं गोदानं च सुवर्णकम् ।। व्रतानि ग्रहणार्थानि वेदस्येति मनोर्मतम् ।। १३ ।।

likewise the Upaniṣad vow, the year-long vow, the cow-gift, and the gold-gift. These vows are for receiving the Veda, according to Manu’s view.

BP 1.182.14

वेदैकदेशपाठस्य उक्तं गृह्ये प्रपञ्चकम् ।। उक्ता गुरोस्तु शुश्रूषा दृष्टादृष्टार्थसाधनम् ।। १४ ।।

The detailed procedure for reciting one part of the Veda is stated in the household ritual text. Service to the teacher is said to bring seen and unseen results.

BP 1.182.15

उभयोर्वा तथा चान्या यथान्यायं यथाश्रुतम् ।। गुरोरप्येव तं विद्यात्तद्ध्यानं त्रिविधं स्मृतम् ।। १५ ।।

Or another of both, according to rule and what has been heard. One should also know that meditation on the teacher is remembered as threefold.

BP 1.182.16

तोषः परस्परस्येति एतावान्धर्मसंग्रहः ।। कृत्स्नो वेदोऽधिगंतव्यः स्वधर्ममनुतिष्ठता ।। १६ ।।

Mutual satisfaction is the whole collection of dharma. The entire Veda is to be learned by one who follows his own dharma.

BP 1.182.17

ज्ञात्वा वेदं ब्रह्मचारी ग्रंथार्थान्यान्यथाविधि ।। नैष्ठिकश्च विधानं तु यावत्क्लीबं विधीयते ।। विद्यान्तेऽभीष्टदानं च अनुज्ञातो गृही भवेत् ।। १७ ।।

Having learned the Veda, the Vedic student should learn the meanings of texts according to rule. The lifelong student should follow the prescribed rule for as long as it is laid down. At the end of study, having given the desired gift and received permission, he should become a householder.

BP 1.182.18

निष्कासनं गुरुगृहाद्गृहस्थस्य यथाभवेत् ।। नैष्ठिकश्च तथा स्नानं कुर्यात्सम्यग्यथाविधि ।। १८ ।।

The departure from the teacher’s house should be as is fitting for a householder. The lifelong student too should bathe properly according to rule.

BP 1.182.19

उद्वहेद्वै ततो भार्यां सवर्णां लक्षणान्विताम् ।। अविप्लुतब्रह्मचर्यश्चाधिकारी खगोत्तम ।। १९ ।।

Then he should marry a wife of the same varṇa, endowed with the marks. One whose celibacy has not been broken is eligible, O best of birds.

BP 1.182.20

स्वतन्त्रमन्ये चेच्छन्ति ह्यधिकारं द्विजोत्तमाः ।। सप्तमीं पञ्चमीं चैव कन्यकां पितृमातृतः ।। २० ।।

Some require that a maiden be outside the forbidden kinship, O best of the twice-born: seventh from the father and fifth from the mother.

BP 1.182.21

उद्वहेत द्विजो भार्यामसमानार्षगोत्रजाम् ।। संख्याविधिविवाहेषु गोत्रार्थं विधिवर्जितम् ।। २१ ।।

A twice-born should marry a wife who is not of the same sage-lineage or gotra. In marriages governed by the prescribed degrees, the gotra rule is set aside.

BP 1.182.22

विकल्पेनैव मन्तव्यमृषीणां विविधा श्रुतिः ।। अष्टौ विवाहा वर्णानां संस्काराख्या इति श्रुतिः ।। २२ ।।

The varied Vedic revelation of the sages is to be understood by alternatives. The eight marriages of the varṇas are called life-rites; thus is the Vedic revelation.

BP 1.182.23

यस्तु दोषवतीं कन्यामनाख्याय प्रय च्छति ।। तस्य कुर्यान्नृपो दंडं स्वयं षण्णवतिं पणान् ।।२३।।

Whoever gives a defective maiden without disclosing it, the king himself should impose a fine of ninety-six coins.

BP 1.182.24

पितुर्गृहे तु या कन्या रजः पश्यत्यसंस्कृता ।। पतंति पितरस्तस्य कन्या च वृषली भवेत् ।। २४ ।।

A maiden who menstruates in her father’s house before the rites are performed causes her ancestors to fall, and she becomes a low-status woman.

BP 1.182.25

यस्तु तां वरयेत्कन्यां ब्राह्मणो ज्ञानदुर्बलः ।। अश्राद्धेयमपाङ्क्तेयं तं विद्याद्वृषलीपतिम् ।। २५ ।।

A Brahmin weak in knowledge who chooses that maiden is to be known as unfit for ancestral rites, excluded from the row, and the husband of a low-status woman.

BP 1.182.26

सर्वदोषान्हि विख्याप्य स्त्रिया वा पुरुषस्य वा ।। उभयोरपि विख्याप्य ततः संबन्धमाचरेत् ।। २६ ।।

Having fully disclosed all faults, whether of the woman or the man, and having disclosed them to both, one should then form the marriage bond.

BP 1.182.27

गौरी कन्या प्रधाना वै मध्यमा कन्यका स्मृता ।। रोहिणी तत्समा ज्ञेया अधमा तु रजस्वला ।। २७ ।।

The Gaurī maiden is indeed foremost; the middle maiden is remembered as intermediate; Rohiṇī is to be known as equal to her, while the menstruating maiden is lowest.

BP 1.182.28

।। अनूरुरुवाच ।। गौरी तु का मता कन्या रोहिणी च जगत्पते ।। रजस्वला नग्निका च देवकन्या च का भवेत् ।। २८ ।।

Anūru said: Which maiden is considered Gaurī, and which Rohiṇī, O lord of the world? What are the menstruating maiden, the bare maiden, and the divine maiden?

BP 1.182.29

।। भास्कर उवाच ।। असंप्राप्तरजा गौरी प्राप्ते रजसि रोहिणी ।। अव्यञ्जनयुता कन्या कुचहीना च नग्निका ।। २९ ।।

Bhāskara said: Before menstruation begins, she is Gaurī; when menstruation has begun, she is Rohiṇī. A maiden without sexual marks and without breasts is the bare maiden.

BP 1.182.30

सप्तवर्षा भवेद्गौरी दशवर्षा तु नग्निका ।। द्वादशे तु भवेत्कन्या अत ऊर्ध्वं रजस्वला ।। ३० ।।

At seven years she is Gaurī; at ten years she is the bare maiden; at twelve she is a maiden; beyond that she is menstruating.

BP 1.182.31

व्यञ्जनेन समोपेतां सोमो भुङ्क्ते हि कन्यकाम् ।। पयोधरेषु गंधर्वा रजस्यग्निः प्रकीर्तितः ।। ३१ ।।

Soma enjoys the maiden endowed with sexual marks; the Gandharvas are in the breasts, and Agni is declared to be in menstruation.

BP 1.182.32

हिनस्ति व्यञ्जनैः पुत्रान्कुलं हन्यात्पयोधरैः ।। गतिमिष्टां तथा लोकान्हन्यात्तु रजसा पितुः ।। ३२ ।।

With sexual marks she harms sons; with breasts she destroys the family; by menstruation she destroys the father’s desired path and worlds.

BP 1.182.33

तस्मादव्यञ्जनोपेतामरजस्कपयोधराम् ।। नान्योपभुक्तां सोमाद्यैर्दद्याद्दुहितरं पिता ।। ३३ ।।

Therefore a father should give his daughter without sexual marks, not menstruating, with breasts, and not previously enjoyed by Soma and the others.

BP 1.182.34

अन्नं तस्य न भोक्तव्यं वृथा पाको हि स स्मृतः ।। वृथा पाकस्य भुक्त्वान्नं प्रायश्चित्तं समाचरेत् ।।३४।।

Her food is not to be eaten, for that cooking is remembered as fruitless. Having eaten food from fruitless cooking, one should perform expiation.

BP 1.182.35

प्राणायामं त्रिरभ्यस्य घृतं प्राश्य विशुद्ध्यति ।। विवाहयेदेकगोत्रां समानप्रवरां खग ।। कृत्वा तस्यां समुत्सर्गमतिकृच्छ्रो विशोधनम् ।। ३५ ।।

By practicing breath-control three times and drinking ghee, one is purified. One should marry a woman of the same gotra and lineage, O bird; after the act of release with her, a very hard penance is the purification.

BP 1.182.36

उद्वाहयेत्सगोत्रां च तनयां मातुलस्य च ।। ऋषिभिश्चैव तुल्यो यो द्विजश्चांद्रायणं चरेत् ।। ३६ ।।

One should marry a woman of the same gotra, and the daughter of the maternal uncle. A twice-born who is equal to the sages should perform the moon-cycle penance.

BP 1.182.37

असपिण्डा तु या मातुरसगोत्रा च या पितुः ।। सा प्रशस्ता द्विजातीनां दारकर्मणि मैथुने ।। ३७ ।।

She who is not within the mother’s prohibited kinship and is not of the father’s gotra is praised for twice-born men in marriage and sexual union.

BP 1.182.38

।। अरुण उवाच ।। दारकर्म किमुक्तं वै यदुक्तं भवता इदम् ।। सा प्रशस्ता द्विजातीनां दारकर्मणि मैथुने ।। ३८ ।।

Aruṇa said: What is called the marriage rite that you have spoken of here? She is praised for twice-born men in marriage and sexual union.

BP 1.182.39

।। आदित्य उवाच ।। अग्निहोत्रादि यत्कर्म वैदिकं विनतात्मज ।। तदुक्तं दारकर्मेति द्वाभ्यां योगात्तु मैथुने ।।३९।।

Āditya said: The Vedic act beginning with the fire offering, O son of Vinatā, is called the marriage rite, because of the union of two in sexual intercourse.

BP 1.182.40

नोद्वहेत्कपिलां कन्यां नाधिकांगीं न रोगिणीम् ।। नालोमिकां नातिलोमां न चाकूटां न पिङ्गलाम् ।।४०।।

One should not marry a tawny maiden, one with excessive limbs, or one who is ill; neither one without hair nor one with excessive hair, nor a hunchback, nor one with tawny eyes.

BP 1.182.41

ऋक्षवृक्षनदीनाम्नीं नान्यपर्वतनामिकाम् ।। न यक्षाहिप्रेष्यनाम्नीं चातिभीषणनामिकाम्।।४१।।

One should not marry a maiden named after a lunar mansion, tree, or river, nor one named after another mountain, a spirit, a serpent, a servant, or something exceedingly terrifying.

BP 1.182.42

यस्यास्तु न भवेद्भ्राता न विज्ञायेत वै पिता ।। नोपगच्छेद्धि तां प्राज्ञः पुत्रिकाधर्मशंकया ।।४२।।

The wise man should not approach one who has no brother and whose father is unknown, for fear of the rule that makes a daughter an heir.

BP 1.182.43

हंसस्वरामेकवर्णां मधुपिंगललोचनाम् ।। तादृशीं वरयेत्कन्यां गृहार्थी खगसत्तम ।। ४३ ।।

A man seeking a household should choose a maiden with a swan-like voice, an even complexion, and honey-brown eyes, O best of birds.

BP 1.182.44

दाराग्निहोत्रसंयोगं कुरुते योऽग्रजे स्थिते ।। परिवेत्ता स विज्ञेयः परिवित्तिस्तु पूर्वजः ।।४४।।

Whoever performs the marriage and fire rite while the elder brother is present is to be known as the younger who marries first; the elder is the one passed over.

BP 1.182.45

परिवित्तिः परिवेत्ता च यया स परिविद्यते ।। सर्वे ते नरकं यांति दातृयाजकपंचमाः ।। ४५ ।।

The passed-over elder, the younger who marries before him, the woman through whom it happens, the giver, and the officiating priest—all five go to hell.

BP 1.182.46

क्लीबे देशांतरस्थे वा पतिते व्रजिते तथा ।। योगशास्त्राभियुक्ते च न दोषः परिवेदने ।।४६।।

When the elder is impotent, lives in another country, has fallen, has renounced the world, or is engaged in the teaching on Yoga, there is no fault in marrying before him.

BP 1.182.47

खंजवामनकुब्जेषु गद्गदेषु जडेषु च ।। जात्यंधे बधिरे मूके न दोषः परिवेदने ।। ४७ ।।

For the lame, dwarfs, hunchbacks, stammerers, and dull-witted; for those blind from birth, deaf, and mute, there is no fault in marrying before the elder.

BP 1.182.48

न श्राद्धं तु कनिष्ठस्य विकुलाय च कन्यका ।। वरश्च कुलशीलाभ्यां न शुद्ध्येत कदाचन ।। न मंत्राः कारणं तत्र न च कन्या वृता भवेत् ।। ४८ ।।

There is no ancestral rite for the younger brother, nor is a maiden to be given to a bad family. The groom is never purified by family and character; mantras are not the cause there, nor is the maiden to be chosen.

BP 1.182.49

उद्वाहिता तु याकन्या न च प्राप्ता तु मैथुनम् ।। पुनरभ्येति भर्तारं यथा कन्या तथैव सा ।। ४९ ।।

A maiden who has been married but has not entered sexual union returns again to her husband; she is just as a maiden.

BP 1.182.50

समाक्षिप्य मतां कन्यां पिता त्वक्षतयोनिकाम् ।। कुलशीलवते दद्यान्न स्याद्दोषः खगाधिप ।। ५० ।।

Having examined and approved the maiden, whose womb is uninjured, the father should give her to a man of good family and character; there is no fault, O lord of birds.

BP 1.182.51

।। अनूरुरुवाच ।। एतेऽष्टौ प्रभवाः प्रोक्ता विवाहा ये जगत्पते ।। लक्षणं ब्रूहि चैतेषां समासात्तिमिरापह ।। ५१ ।।

Anūru said: These eight marriages are said to be the sources, O lord of the world. Tell me their characteristics briefly, O remover of darkness.

BP 1.182.52

।। आदित्य उवाच ।। शुभां लक्षणसंपन्नां कुलशीलगुणान्विताम् ।। अलंकृत्यार्हते दानं विवाहो ब्राह्म उच्यते ।। ५२ ।।

Āditya said: The giving of an auspicious maiden, endowed with marks, family, character, and qualities, adorned and given to a worthy man, is called the Brahma marriage.

BP 1.182.53

सह धर्मक्रियाहेतोर्दानं समयबंधनात् ।। अलंकृत्यैव कन्यायाः प्राजापत्यः स उच्यते ।। ५३ ।।

Giving an adorned maiden for the joint performance of dharma rites, bound by agreement, is called the Prajāpati marriage.

BP 1.182.54

प्रदानं यत्र कन्यायाः सहगोमिथुनेन तु ।। सवर्णायाः सगोत्रायास्तमार्षमृषयो विदुः।। ५४ ।।

Where the maiden is given together with a pair of cows to a man of the same varṇa and gotra, the sages know it as the Sage marriage.

BP 1.182.55

अंतर्वेद्यां समानीय कन्यां कनकमंडिताम् ।। ऋत्विजे चैव यद्दानं विवाहो दैवसंज्ञकः ।। ५५ ।।

Bringing the maiden, adorned with gold, within the altar enclosure and giving her to the officiating priest is the marriage called the Divine marriage.

BP 1.182.56

एते विवाहाश्चत्वारो धर्मकामार्थदायकाः ।। अशुल्का ब्रह्मणा प्रोक्तास्तारयंति कुलद्वयम् ।। ५६ ।।

These four marriages bestow dharma, desire, and wealth. Declared by Brahmā to be without bride-price, they carry across two families.

BP 1.182.57

चतुर्ष्वेतेषु दत्तायामुत्पन्नो यः सुतः स्त्रियाम् ।। दातुः प्रतिग्रहीतुश्च पुनात्यासप्तमान्पितॄन् ।। ५७ ।।

The son born from a woman given in these four marriages purifies the ancestors of the giver and receiver through the seventh generation.

BP 1.182.58

विविक्ते स्वयमन्योऽन्यं स्त्रीपुंसोर्यः समागमः ।। प्रीतिहेतुः स गांधर्वो विवाहः पंचमो मतः ।।५८।।

The mutual union of a woman and man by themselves in a secluded place, for the sake of affection, is regarded as the fifth, the Celestial marriage.

BP 1.182.59

हत्वा च्छित्त्वा च भित्त्वा च क्रोशंतीं रुदतीं गृहात्।। प्रसह्य कन्याहरणं राक्षसोद्वाह उच्यते।।५९।।

Killing, cutting, and breaking, then forcibly carrying a maiden from her house while she cries and weeps, is called the Demon marriage.

BP 1.182.60

शुल्कं प्रदाय कन्याया हरणं व्यसनादपि।। प्रसाद हेतुरुक्तोयमासुरः सप्तमस्तथा ।। ६० ।।

Taking a maiden after paying a bride-price, even from desire, is said to be for favor; this is the Asura marriage, the seventh.

BP 1.182.61

सुप्तां मत्तां प्रमत्तां वा रहो यत्रोपगच्छति ।। स पापिष्ठो विवाहानां पैशाचश्चाष्टमोधमः ।। ६१ ।।

Where one secretly approaches a sleeping, intoxicated, or heedless woman, that most sinful marriage is the eighth and lowest, the Spirit marriage.

BP 1.182.62

एतान्सशुल्कान्सामान्यान्विवाहांश्चतुरो विदुः ।। केवलं क्षत्रियस्यैव वीर्यं छित्त्वा हि राक्षसः ।। ६२ ।।

These four marriages with bride-price are known as common. The forceful Demon marriage is for a Kṣatriya alone, after breaking resistance.

BP 1.182.63

प्राप्ते पूर्वविवाहे तु विधिर्वैवाहिकः शिवः ।। कर्तव्यस्तु त्रिभिर्वर्णैः समयेनाग्निसाक्षिकः ।। ६३ ।।

When the preceding marriage has taken place, the auspicious marriage rite should be performed by the three varṇas at the proper time, with fire as witness.

BP 1.182.64

दोषवत्याः प्रदाने तु दातुः षण्णवतिर्दमः ।। स्यात्तु शुल्कप्रदाने च कन्यायाश्चापवर्जने ।।६४।।

For giving a defective maiden, the giver’s fine is ninety-six; likewise for taking a bride-price and for rejecting the maiden.

BP 1.182.65

मोक्षोपवर्तनं द्वेषः स्त्रीधनस्य निवर्तनम् ।। आकांक्षा तीर्थसंरोधस्त्यागहेतुश्च वक्ष्यते ।। ६५ ।।

Release and return, hatred, the return of a woman’s wealth, desire, obstruction of pilgrimage, and the cause of abandonment will be told.

BP 1.182.66

परस्परस्य संबंधान्मोक्षः स्त्रीपुंसयोः स्मृतः ।। न स्यादन्यतरः प्रीतो रोषात्सांप्रतिकादपि ।। ६६ ।।

Release of a woman and man is remembered from their mutual connection. If either is not pleased, there is no release, even from present anger.

BP 1.182.67

बाधते चेत्पतिर्भार्यां स तु द्वेष इति स्मृतः ।। वृत्तिराभरणं शुल्कं लाभश्च स्त्रीधनं भवेत्।।६७।।

If a husband oppresses his wife, that is called hatred. Livelihood, ornaments, bride-price, and gain become the woman’s wealth.

BP 1.182.68

भोक्तृस्तु स्वयमेवेदं प्रतिज्ञाहननं भवेत् ।। वृथा मोक्षेण भोगेन स्त्रियै दद्यात्सवृद्धिकम् ।।६८।।

If the man who enjoys her consumes this wealth himself, it is breaking the promise. If he releases her without cause after enjoying her, he should give it to the woman with increase.

BP 1.182.69

आपत्तिसमये जाते स्त्रीधनं भोक्तुमर्हति ।। आकांक्षेताष्टवर्षाणि भर्तापि प्रसवं स्त्रियाः ।। ६९ ।।

When distress has arisen, he may use the woman’s wealth. The husband too should wait eight years for the woman to bear a child.

BP 1.182.70

जायन्ते यदि नो पुत्रास्तस्यां यत्ने महत्यपि ।। ततो विंदेत पुत्रार्थी धर्मतः कुलजां स्त्रियम्।। ७० ।।

If sons are not born from her despite great effort, then one who desires a son may lawfully obtain a woman of a proper family.

BP 1.182.71

पुत्रलाभात्परं लोके नास्ति हि प्रसवार्थिनः ।। एतां शुल्कस्य तां भुक्त्वा अन्यां लब्धुं यदीच्छति ।। समस्तास्तोषयित्वार्थैः सूर्योढां परमां वरेत्।।७१।।

For one seeking offspring, there is nothing in the world beyond obtaining a son. Having taken this woman for a bride-price, if he wishes to obtain another, after satisfying everyone with wealth, he should choose an excellent bride given in marriage.

BP 1.182.72

एका शूद्रस्य वैश्यस्य द्वे तिस्रः क्षत्रियस्य तु ।। चतस्रो ब्राह्मणस्य स्युर्भार्या राज्ञो यथेष्टतः ।। ७२ ।।

One wife for a Śūdra, two for a Vaiśya, three for a Kṣatriya, and four for a Brahmin; for a king, as he desires.

BP 1.182.73

अतीर्थगमनात्पुंसस्तीर्थे संगाहनात्स्रियाः ।। उभयोर्धर्मलोपः स्यात्स्वेष्वेव तु विशेषतः ।। ७३ ।।

From a man’s going to a place that is not a pilgrimage, and a woman’s bathing at a pilgrimage, there is loss of dharma for both, especially in their own cases.

BP 1.182.74

यौगपद्ये तु तीर्थानां विवाहक्रमशो व्रजेत् ।। तत्साम्यं जीवपुत्रा वा ग्रहणक्रमशोपि वा।।७४।।

When the pilgrimages coincide, one should go in the order of marriage. If they are equal, then by living sons, or by the order of taking.

BP 1.182.75

ब्राह्मादिभिर्विवाहैस्तु संस्कृतौ तौ खगाधिप ।। अष्टौ विवाहा वर्णानां वैनतेय उशंति वै ।। ७५ ।।

The two are consecrated by marriages beginning with the Brahma marriage, O lord of birds. The eight marriages of the varṇas are declared, O son of Vinatā.

BP 1.182.76

ब्राह्मो दैवस्तथार्षश्च प्राजापत्यः खगा धिप ।। गांधर्वश्चासुरो रक्षः पैशाचस्त्वष्टमोऽधमः ।। ७६ ।।

The Brahma, Divine, and Sage marriages, and the Prajāpati marriage, O lord of birds; the Celestial, Asura, Demon, and Spirit marriages—the eighth and lowest.

BP 1.182.77

प्रशस्ताः क्षत्रियादीनां विप्रादीनां तु मानतः ।। प्रतिग्रहादयो बद्धा विवाहा ब्राह्मणस्य तु ।।७७।।

They are praised for Kṣatriyas and the other varṇas according to rank; marriages beginning with acceptance are prescribed for a Brahmin.

BP 1.182.78

क्षत्रियस्यापि देया तु प्रतिग्रहविवर्जिता ।। प्रवृत्तिं केचिदिच्छंति दानमित्यपरे स्त्रियाः ।। पावनं पुरुषाणां तु विवाहं परिचक्षते ।।७८।।

For a Kṣatriya too, the maiden is to be given, but without acceptance. Some desire a betrothal; others call it the giving of women; they declare marriage to be purification for men.
