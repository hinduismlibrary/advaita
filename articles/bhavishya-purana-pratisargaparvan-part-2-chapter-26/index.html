BP 3.2.26.1

सूत उवाच ।
राजासीद्धार्मिकः कश्चित्केदारमणिपूरके ।
चन्द्रचूड इति ख्यातः प्रजापालनतत्परः ॥ 1 ॥

Sūta said: Once there was a righteous king named Candracūḍa, who ruled in Kedāramaṇipūraka and was devoted to protecting his people.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
सूत sūta Sūta
उवाच uvāca said
राजासीद्धार्मिकः rājāsīddhārmikaḥ a righteous king
कश्चित्केदारमणिपूरके kaścitkedāramaṇipūrake a certain one in Kedāramaṇipūraka
चन्द्रचूड candracūḍa Candracūḍa
इति iti thus
ख्यातः khyātaḥ famous
प्रजापालनतत्परः prajāpālanatatparaḥ devoted to protecting his subjects
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

sūta uvāca | rājāsīddhārmikaḥ kaścitkedāramaṇipūrake | candracūḍa iti khyātaḥ prajāpālanatatparaḥ || 1 ||
