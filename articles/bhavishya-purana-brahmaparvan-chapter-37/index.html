Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 37 — The Bhādrapada Nāga-pañcamī Vow

BP 1.37.1

॥ सुमंतुरुवाच ॥ ॥
तथा भाद्रपदे मासि पंचम्यां श्रद्धयान्वितः ॥
अथालेख्य नरो नागान्कृष्णवर्णादिवर्णकैः ॥ ॥

Sumantu said: Likewise, in the month of Bhādrapada, on Pañcamī, a person filled with faith should draw Nāgas using black and other coloured pigments.

Word-for-word
सुमन्तुः उवाच (sumantuḥ uvāca) — Sumantu said;
भाद्रपदे मासि पञ्चम्याम् (bhādrapade māsi pañcamyām) — on Pañcamī in Bhādrapada;
श्रद्धयान्वितः (śraddhayānvitaḥ) — filled with faith;
आलेख्य नागान् (ālekhya nāgān) — should draw Nāgas;
कृष्णवर्णादिवर्णकैः (kṛṣṇa-varṇādi-varṇakaiḥ) — with black and other pigments
Transliteration
|| sumaṃturuvāca || ||
tathā bhādrapade māsi paṃcamyāṃ śraddhayānvitaḥ ||
athālekhya naro nāgānkṛṣṇavarṇādivarṇakaiḥ || 1 ||

BP 1.37.2

पूजयेद्गंधपुष्पैश्च सर्पिः पायसगुग्गुलैः ॥
तस्य तुष्टिं समायांति पन्नगास्तक्षकादयः ॥ ॥

One should worship them with fragrances, flowers, ghee, milk-rice, and guggulu; Takṣaka and the other serpents become pleased with that worshipper.

Word-for-word
पूजयेत् गन्धपुष्पैः (pūjayet gandha-puṣpaiḥ) — worship with fragrances and flowers;
सर्पिः पायसगुग्गुलैः (sarpiḥ pāyasa-guggulaiḥ) — with ghee milk-rice and guggulu;
तस्य तुष्टिम् समायान्ति (tasya tuṣṭim samāyānti) — become pleased with that person;
पन्नगाः तक्षकादयः (pannagāḥ takṣakādayaḥ) — serpents beginning with Takṣaka
Transliteration
pūjayedgaṃdhapuṣpaiśca sarpiḥ pāyasaguggulaiḥ ||
tasya tuṣṭiṃ samāyāṃti pannagāstakṣakādayaḥ||2||

BP 1.37.3

आसप्तमात्कुलात्तस्य न भयं नागतो भवेत् ॥
तस्मात्सर्वप्रयत्नेन नागान्संपूजयेद्बुधः ॥ ॥

No fear from Nāgas arises in that persons lineage through the seventh generation. Therefore the wise should worship the Nāgas with every effort.

Word-for-word
आसप्तमात् कुलात् (ā-saptamāt kulāt) — through seventh generation of family;
न भयम् नागतः (na bhayam nāgataḥ) — no fear from Nāgas;
तस्मात् सर्वप्रयत्नेन (tasmāt sarva-prayatnena) — therefore with every effort;
नागान् सम्पूजयेत् बुधः (nāgān sampūjayet budhaḥ) — wise should worship Nāgas
Transliteration
āsaptamātkulāttasya na bhayaṃ nāgato bhavet||
tasmātsarvaprayatnena nāgānsaṃpūjayedbudhaḥ||3||
