Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 37 — The Bhādrapada Fifth Day Serpent Vow

BP 1.37.1

॥ सुमन्तुरुवाच ॥ ॥
तथा भाद्रपदे मासि पञ्चम्याम् श्रद्धयान्वितः ॥
अथालेख्य नरो नागान् कृष्णवर्णादिवर्णकैः ॥ ॥

Sumantu said. Likewise, in the month of Bhādrapada, on the fifth lunar day, a person filled with faith should draw serpents using black and other colored pigments.

सुमन्तुः (sumantuḥ) — Sumantu; उवाच (uvāca) — said; तथा (tathā) — likewise; भाद्रपदे (bhādrapade) — in Bhādrapada; मासि (māsi) — in the month; पञ्चम्याम् (pañcamyām) — on the fifth lunar day; श्रद्धयान्वितः (śraddhayānvitaḥ) — endowed with faith; अथ (atha) — then; आलेख्य (ālekhya) — having drawn; नरः (naraḥ) — a person; नागान् (nāgān) — Nāgas; कृष्णवर्णादिवर्णकैः (kṛṣṇavarṇādivarṇakaiḥ) — with black and other-colored pigments

BP 1.37.2

पूजयेद्गन्धपुष्पैश्च सर्पिः पायसगुग्गुलैः ॥
तस्य तुष्टिं समायांति पन्नगास्तक्षकादयः ॥ ॥

One should worship them with fragrances, flowers, ghee, milk pudding, and fragrant resin. Takṣaka and the other serpents become pleased with that person.

पूजयेत् (pūjayet) — one should worship; गन्धपुष्पैः (gandhapuṣpaiḥ) — with fragrances and flowers; च (ca) — and; सर्पिः (sarpiḥ) — ghee; पायस (pāyasa) — milk-rice; गुग्गुलैः (guggulaiḥ) — with guggulu; तस्य (tasya) — of him; तुष्टिम् (tuṣṭim) — to satisfaction; समायान्ति (samāyānti) — come; पन्नगाः (pannagāḥ) — serpents; तक्षकादयः (takṣakādayaḥ) — Takṣaka and the rest

BP 1.37.3

आसप्तमात्कुलात्तस्य न भयं नागतो भवेत् ॥
तस्मात्सर्वप्रयत्नेन नागान् सम्पूजयेद्बुधः ॥ ॥

No fear of serpents will arise in the family of that person through seven generations. Therefore the wise person should worship serpents with every effort.

आसप्तमात् (āsaptamāt) — through seven; कुलात् (kulāt) — generations of the family; तस्य (tasya) — of him; न (na) — not; भयम् (bhayam) — fear; नागतः (nāgataḥ) — from a Nāga; भवेत् (bhavet) — would arise; तस्मात् (tasmāt) — therefore; सर्वप्रयत्नेन (sarvaprayatnena) — with every effort; नागान् (nāgān) — Nāgas; सम्पूजयेत् (sampūjayet) — one should worship; बुधः (budhaḥ) — the wise one
