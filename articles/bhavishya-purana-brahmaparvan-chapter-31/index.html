Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 31 — The Three Fourth Day Vows and the Mars Observance

BP 1.31.1

सुमन्तुरुवाच
शिवा शान्ता सुखा राजंश्चतुर्थी त्रिविधा स्मृता ।
मासि भाद्रपदे शुक्ला शिवा लोकेषु पूजिता ॥

Sumantu said. O king, the fourth day is remembered in three forms: Auspicious, Peaceful, and Happy. The bright fourth day in Bhādrapada is Auspicious, worshipped in the worlds.

सुमन्तुः (sumantuḥ) — Sumantu; उवाच (uvāca) — said; शिवा (śivā) — Śivā; शान्ता (śāntā) — Śāntā; सुखा (sukhā) — Sukhā; राजन् (rājan) — O king; चतुर्थी (caturthī) — fourth lunar day; त्रिविधा (trividhā) — threefold; स्मृता (smṛtā) — remembered; मासि (māsi) — in the month; भाद्रपदे (bhādrapade) — in Bhādrapada; शुक्ला (śuklā) — bright; शिवा (śivā) — Śivā; लोकेषु (lokeṣu) — in the worlds; पूजिता (pūjitā) — worshipped

BP 1.31.2

तस्यां स्नानं तथा दानमुपवासो जपस्तथा ।
क्रियमाणं शतगुणं प्रसादाद्दन्तिनो नृप ॥

Bathing, giving, fasting, and recitation performed on that day become a hundredfold through the favor of the elephant-faced Lord, O king.

तस्याम् (tasyām) — on that; स्नानम् (snānam) — bathing; तथा (tathā) — and; दानम् (dānam) — giving; उपवासः (upavāsaḥ) — fasting; जपः (japaḥ) — recitation; तथा (tathā) — and; क्रियमाणम् (kriyamāṇam) — being performed; शतगुणम् (śataguṇam) — hundredfold; प्रसादात् (prasādāt) — through the favor; दन्तिनः (dantinaḥ) — of the Tusked One; नृप (nṛpa) — O king

BP 1.31.3

गुडलवणघृतानां तु दानं शुभकरं स्मृतम् ।
गुडापूपैस्तथा वीर पुण्यं ब्राह्मणभोजनम् ॥

The gift of jaggery, salt, and ghee is remembered as auspicious. Feeding Brahmins with jaggery cakes is meritorious, O hero.

गुडलवणघृतानाम् (guḍalavaṇaghṛtānām) — of jaggery, salt, and ghee; तु (tu) — indeed; दानम् (dānam) — gift; शुभकरम् (śubhakaram) — auspicious-making; स्मृतम् (smṛtam) — remembered; गुडापूपैः (guḍāpūpaiḥ) — with jaggery cakes; तथा (tathā) — and; वीर (vīra) — O hero; पुण्यम् (puṇyam) — merit; ब्राह्मणभोजनम् (brāhmaṇabhojanam) — feeding Brahmins

BP 1.31.4

यास्तस्यां नरशार्दूल पूजयन्ति सदा स्त्रियः ।
गुडलवणपूपैश्च श्वश्रूं श्वसुरमेव च ॥

Tiger among men, the women who always worship their mothers-in-law and fathers-in-law on that day with jaggery, salt, and cakes—

याः (yāḥ) — those who; तस्याम् (tasyām) — on that day; नरशार्दूल (naraśārdūla) — O tiger among men; पूजयन्ति (pūjayanti) — worship; सदा (sadā) — always; स्त्रियः (striyaḥ) — women; गुडलवणपूपैः (guḍalavaṇapūpaiḥ) — with jaggery, salt, and cakes; च (ca) — and; श्वश्रूम् (śvaśrūm) — mother-in-law; श्वसुरम् (śvasuram) — father-in-law; एव (eva) — indeed; च (ca) — and

BP 1.31.5

ताः सर्वाः सुभगाः स्युर्वे विघ्रेशस्यानुमोदनात् ।
कन्यका तु विशेषेण विधिनानेन पूजयेत् ॥

—all become fortunate through the approval of the Lord of Obstacles. A maiden in particular should worship by this procedure.

ताः (tāḥ) — they; सर्वाः (sarvāḥ) — all; सुभगाः (subhagāḥ) — fortunate; स्युः (syuḥ) — may become; वै (vai) — indeed; विघ्नेशस्य (vighneśasya) — of Vighneśa; अनुमोदनात् (anumodanāt) — through approval; कन्यका (kanyakā) — maiden; तु (tu) — indeed; विशेषेण (viśeṣeṇa) — especially; विधिना (vidhinā) — by the procedure; अनेन (anena) — this; पूजयेत् (pūjayet) — should worship

BP 1.31.6

सुमन्तुरुवाच
माघे मासि तथा शुक्ला या चतुर्थी महीपते ।
सा शान्ता शान्तिदा नित्यं शान्तिं कुर्यात्सदैव हि ॥

Sumantu said. O king, the bright fourth day in Māgha is Peaceful. It always gives peace and brings peace.

सुमन्तुः (sumantuḥ) — Sumantu; उवाच (uvāca) — said; माघे (māghe) — in Māgha; मासि (māsi) — in the month; तथा (tathā) — also; शुक्ला (śuklā) — bright; या (yā) — which; चतुर्थी (caturthī) — fourth lunar day; महीपते (mahīpate) — O lord of the earth; सा (sā) — that; शान्ता (śāntā) — Śāntā; शान्तिदा (śāntidā) — peace-giving; नित्यम् (nityam) — always; शान्तिम् (śāntim) — peace; कुर्यात् (kuryāt) — may bring; सदा (sadā) — always; एव (eva) — indeed; हि (hi) — surely

BP 1.31.7

स्नानदानादिकं कर्म सर्वमस्यां कृतं विभो ।
भवेत्सहस्रगुणितं प्रसादात्तस्य दन्तिनः ॥

Every act of bathing, giving, and the like performed on this day becomes a thousandfold through the favor of the elephant-faced Lord.

स्नानदानादिकम् (snānadānādikam) — bathing, giving, and the like; कर्म (karma) — act; सर्वम् (sarvam) — all; अस्याम् (asyām) — on this; कृतम् (kṛtam) — done; विभो (vibho) — O lord; भवेत् (bhavet) — becomes; सहस्रगुणितम् (sahasraguṇitam) — thousandfold; प्रसादात् (prasādāt) — through the favor; तस्य (tasya) — of that; दन्तिनः (dantinaḥ) — of the Tusked One

BP 1.31.8

कृतोपवासो यस्तस्यां पूजयेद्वि घ्ननायकम् ।
तस्य होमादिकं कर्म भवेत्साहस्रिकं नृप ॥

Whoever fasts on that day and worships the Remover of Obstacles, the fire offering and other acts become a thousandfold, O king.

कृतोपवासः (kṛtopavāsaḥ) — one who has fasted; यः (yaḥ) — who; तस्याम् (tasyām) — on that day; पूजयेत् (pūjayet) — should worship; विघ्ननायकम् (vighnanāyakam) — the Lord of Obstacles; तस्य (tasya) — his; होमादिकम् (homādikam) — fire-offering and the like; कर्म (karma) — rite; भवेत् (bhavet) — becomes; साहस्रिकम् (sāhasrikam) — thousandfold; नृप (nṛpa) — O king

BP 1.31.9

लवणं च गुडं शाकं गुडपूपांश्च भारत ।
दत्त्वा भक्त्या तु विप्रेभ्यः फलं साहस्रिकं भजेत् ॥

Descendant of Bharata, one who devoutly gives salt, jaggery, greens, and jaggery cakes to Brahmins receives a thousandfold fruit.

लवणम् (lavaṇam) — salt; च (ca) — and; गुडम् (guḍam) — jaggery; शाकम् (śākam) — greens; गुडपूपान् (guḍapūpān) — jaggery cakes; च (ca) — and; भारत (bhārata) — O Bhārata; दत्त्वा (dattvā) — having given; भक्त्या (bhaktyā) — with devotion; तु (tu) — indeed; विप्रेभ्यः (viprebhyaḥ) — to Brahmins; फलम् (phalam) — fruit; साहस्रिकम् (sāhasrikam) — thousandfold; भजेत् (bhajet) — one obtains

BP 1.31.10

विशेषतः स्त्रियो राजन्पूजयन्तो गुरुं नृप ।
गुडलवणघृतैर्वीर सदा स्युर्भाग्यसंयुताः ॥

Especially women, O king, who worship their teacher with jaggery, salt, and ghee will always be joined with good fortune, O hero.

विशेषतः (viśeṣataḥ) — especially; स्त्रियः (striyaḥ) — women; राजन् (rājan) — O king; पूजयन्तः (pūjayantaḥ) — worshipping; गुरुम् (gurum) — the teacher; नृप (nṛpa) — O king; गुडलवणघृतैः (guḍalavaṇaghṛtaiḥ) — with jaggery, salt, and ghee; वीर (vīra) — O hero; सदा (sadā) — always; स्युः (syuḥ) — they become; भाग्यसंयुताः (bhāgyasaṃyutāḥ) — endowed with good fortune

BP 1.31.11

सुमन्तुरुवाच
सुखावहा च सुसुखा सौभाग्यकरणी परम् ॥

Sumantu said. It brings happiness, is very pleasant, and makes the highest good fortune.

सुमन्तुः (sumantuḥ) — Sumantu; उवाच (uvāca) — said; सुखावहा (sukhāvahā) — happiness-bringing; च (ca) — and; सुसुखा (susukhā) — very pleasant; सौभाग्यकरणी (saubhāgyakaraṇī) — fortune-producing; परम् (param) — supreme

BP 1.31.12

चतुर्थी कुरुशार्दूल रूपसौभाग्यदा शुभा ।
सुखाव्रतं महापुण्यं रूपदं भाग्यदं तथा ॥

Tiger of the Kuru line, this auspicious fourth day gives beauty and good fortune. The Happy Vow is greatly meritorious and gives beauty and fortune.

चतुर्थी (caturthī) — fourth lunar day; कुरुशार्दूल (kuruśārdūla) — O tiger of the Kurus; रूपसौभाग्यदा (rūpasaubhāgyadā) — giving beauty and good fortune; शुभा (śubhā) — auspicious; सुखाव्रतम् (sukhāvratam) — the Sukhā vow; महापुण्यम् (mahāpuṇyam) — greatly meritorious; रूपदम् (rūpadam) — beauty-giving; भाग्यदम् (bhāgyadam) — fortune-giving; तथा (tathā) — also

BP 1.31.13

सुसूक्ष्मं सुकरं धन्यमिह पुण्यसुखावहम् ।
परत्र फलदं वीर दिव्यरूपप्रदायकम् ॥

It is very subtle, easy to perform, and blessed. Here it brings merit and happiness, and hereafter it gives fruit and a divine form, O hero.

सुसूक्ष्मम् (susūkṣmam) — very subtle; सुकरम् (sukaram) — easy to perform; धन्यम् (dhanyam) — blessed; इह (iha) — here; पुण्यसुखावहम् (puṇyasukhāvaham) — bringing merit and happiness; परत्र (paratra) — hereafter; फलदम् (phaladam) — fruit-giving; वीर (vīra) — O hero; दिव्यरूपप्रदायकम् (divyarūpapradāyakam) — bestowing a divine form

BP 1.31.14

हसितं ललितं चोक्तं चेष्टितं च सुखावहम् ।
सविलासभुजक्षेपश्चंक्रमश्चेष्टितं शुभम् ॥

Smiling, graceful speech, and movement bring happiness. A playful swing of the arms and walking about are auspicious conduct.

हसितम् (hasitam) — smiling; ललितम् (lalitam) — graceful; च (ca) — and; उक्तम् (uktam) — speech; च (ca) — and; चेष्टितम् (ceṣṭitam) — conduct; च (ca) — and; सुखावहम् (sukhāvaham) — bringing happiness; सविलासभुजक्षेपः (savilāsabhujakṣepaḥ) — a playful swinging of the arms; चंक्रमः (caṃkramaḥ) — walking about; चेष्टितम् (ceṣṭitam) — conduct; शुभम् (śubham) — auspicious

BP 1.31.15

सुखाव्रतेन सर्वेषां सुखं कुरुकुलोद्वह ।
कृत्येन पूजिते चेशे विघ्रेशे शिवयोः सुते ॥

Scion of the Kuru line, the Happy Vow brings happiness to all when the Lord of Obstacles, the son of Śiva and Umā, is duly worshipped.

सुखाव्रतेन (sukhāvratena) — through the Sukhā vow; सर्वेषाम् (sarveṣām) — for all; सुखम् (sukham) — happiness; कुरुकुलोद्वह (kurukulodvaha) — O upholder of the Kuru line; कृत्येन (kṛtyena) — by due performance; पूजिते (pūjite) — when worshipped; ईशे (īśe) — in the Lord; विघ्नेशे (vighneśe) — in Vighneśa; शिवयोः (śivayoḥ) — of Śiva and Śivā; सुते (sute) — son

BP 1.31.16

यदा शुक्लचतुर्थ्यां तु वारो भौमस्य वै भवेत् ।
तदा सा सुखदा ज्ञेया चतुर्थी वै सुखेति च ॥

When the bright fourth day falls on Tuesday, it is to be known as the happiness-giving fourth day, called Happy.

यदा (yadā) — when; शुक्लचतुर्थ्याम् (śuklacaturthyām) — on the bright fourth day; तु (tu) — indeed; वारः (vāraḥ) — weekday; भौमस्य (bhaumasya) — of Bhauma; वै (vai) — indeed; भवेत् (bhavet) — occurs; तदा (tadā) — then; सा (sā) — that; सुखदा (sukhadā) — happiness-giving; ज्ञेया (jñeyā) — to be known; चतुर्थी (caturthī) — fourth day; वै (vai) — indeed; सुखा (sukhā) — Sukhā; इति (iti) — thus; च (ca) — and

BP 1.31.17

पुरा मैथुनमाश्रित्य स्थिताभ्यां तु हिमाचले ।
भीमोमाभ्यां महाबाहो रक्तबिन्दुश्च्युतः क्षितौ ॥

Long ago, while Bhīma and Umā were united on the Himalaya, a drop of blood fell to the earth, O mighty armed one.

पुरा (purā) — formerly; मैथुनम् (maithunam) — sexual union; आश्रित्य (āśritya) — having undertaken; स्थिताभ्याम् (sthitābhyām) — by the two abiding; तु (tu) — indeed; हिमाचले (himācale) — on the Himalaya; भीमोमाभ्याम् (bhīmomābhyām) — by Bhīma and Umā; महाबाहो (mahābāho) — O mighty-armed one; रक्तबिन्दुः (raktabinduḥ) — drop of blood; च्युतः (cyutaḥ) — fell; क्षितौ (kṣitau) — upon the earth

BP 1.31.18

मेदिन्यां स प्रयत्नेन सुखे विधृतोऽनया ।
जातोस्याः स कुजो वीर रक्तो रक्तसमुद्भवः ॥

The earth carefully held it in happiness. From her was born Mars, red and born from blood, O hero.

मेदिन्याम् (medinyām) — on the earth; सः (saḥ) — he; प्रयत्नेन (prayatnena) — carefully; सुखे (sukhe) — happily; विधृतः (vidhṛtaḥ) — held; अनया (anayā) — by her; जातः (jātaḥ) — was born; अस्याः (asyāḥ) — from her; सः (saḥ) — he; कुजः (kujaḥ) — Kuja; वीर (vīra) — O hero; रक्तः (raktaḥ) — red; रक्तसमुद्भवः (raktasamudbhavaḥ) — born from blood

BP 1.31.19

ममाङ्गतो यथोत्पन्नस्तस्मादङ्गारको ह्ययम् ।
अङ्गदोङ्गोपकारश्च अङ्गानां तु प्रदो नृणाम् ॥

Because he was born from my body, he is Mars. He gives armlets, benefits the limbs, and gives limbs to people.

मम (mama) — from me; अङ्गतः (aṅgataḥ) — from the body; यथा (yathā) — as; उत्पन्नः (utpannaḥ) — born; तस्मात् (tasmāt) — therefore; अङ्गारकः (aṅgārakaḥ) — Aṅgāraka; हि (hi) — indeed; अयम् (ayam) — this one; अङ्गदः (aṅgadaḥ) — giver of armlets; अङ्गोपकारः (aṅgopakāraḥ) — beneficial to the limbs; च (ca) — and; अङ्गानाम् (aṅgānām) — of the limbs; तु (tu) — indeed; प्रदः (pradaḥ) — giver; नृणाम् (nṛṇām) — of people

BP 1.31.20

सौभाग्यादिकरो यस्मात्तस्मादङ्गारको मतः ।
भक्त्या चतुर्थ्यां नक्तेन यो वै श्रद्धासमन्वितः ॥

Because he brings good fortune and the like, he is considered Mars. Whoever, filled with faith, observes the fourth day with a night meal—

सौभाग्यादिकरः (saubhāgyādikaraḥ) — producer of good fortune and the like; यस्मात् (yasmāt) — because; तस्मात् (tasmāt) — therefore; अङ्गारकः (aṅgārakaḥ) — Aṅgāraka; मतः (mataḥ) — is considered; भक्त्या (bhaktyā) — with devotion; च (ca) — and; चतुर्थ्याम् (caturthyām) — on the fourth day; नक्तेन (naktena) — by a night-fast; यः (yaḥ) — whoever; वै (vai) — indeed; श्रद्धासमन्वितः (śraddhāsamanvitaḥ) — endowed with faith

BP 1.31.21

उपवत्स्यति ना राजन्नारी वा नान्यमानसा ।
पूजयेच्च कुजं भक्तया रक्तपुष्पविलेपनैः ॥

—whether man or woman, O king, should fast with a mind on no other thing and worship Mars with devotion, red flowers, and unguents.

उपवत्स्यति (upavatsyati) — will fast; नरः (naraḥ) — a man; राजन् (rājan) — O king; नारी (nārī) — a woman; वा (vā) — or; न (na) — not; अन्यमानसा (anyamānasā) — with the mind on another; पूजयेत् (pūjayet) — should worship; च (ca) — and; कुजम् (kujam) — Kuja; भक्त्या (bhaktyā) — with devotion; रक्तपुष्पविलेपनैः (raktapuṣpavilepanaiḥ) — with red flowers and unguents

BP 1.31.22

गणेशं प्रथमं भक्त्या योजयेच्छद्धयान्वितः ।
यस्य तुष्टः प्रयच्छेत्स सौभाग्यं रूपसम्पदम् ॥

First, with devotion and faith, one should worship Gaṇeśa. When pleased, he grants good fortune and beauty.

गणेशम् (gaṇeśam) — Gaṇeśa; प्रथमम् (prathamam) — first; भक्त्या (bhaktyā) — with devotion; योजयेत् (yojayet) — should worship; श्रद्धयान्वितः (śraddhayānvitaḥ) — endowed with faith; यस्य (yasya) — whose; तुष्टः (tuṣṭaḥ) — being pleased; प्रयच्छेत् (prayacchet) — may grant; सः (saḥ) — he; सौभाग्यम् (saubhāgyam) — good fortune; रूपसम्पदम् (rūpasampadam) — beauty and prosperity

BP 1.31.23

पूर्वं च कृतसङ्कल्पः स्नानं कृत्वा यथाविधि ।
