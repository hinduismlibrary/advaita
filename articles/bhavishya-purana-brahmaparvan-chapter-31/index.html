Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 31 — The Three Forms of Caturthī and the Aṅgāraka Vow

BP 1.31.1

सुमन्तुरुवाच
शिवा शान्ता सुखा राजंश्चतुर्थी त्रिविधा स्मृता ।
मासि भाद्रपदे शुक्ला शिवा लोकेषु पूजिता ॥

Sumantu said: O king, Caturthī is remembered in three forms—Śivā, Śāntā, and Sukhā. The bright fourth of Bhādrapada, honoured throughout the worlds, is Śivā.

Word-for-word
शिवा शान्ता सुखा (śivā śāntā sukhā) — Śivā Śāntā and Sukhā;
चतुर्थी त्रिविधा (caturthī trividhā) — Caturthī is threefold;
भाद्रपदे शुक्ला (bhādrapade śuklā) — bright fourth in Bhādrapada;
लोकेषु पूजिता (lokeṣu pūjitā) — honoured in the worlds
Transliteration
sumanturuvāca
śivā śāntā sukhā rājaṃścaturthī trividhā smṛtā |
māsi bhādrapade śuklā śivā lokeṣu pūjitā | | 1
