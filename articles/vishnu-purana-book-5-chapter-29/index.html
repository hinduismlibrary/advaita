VP 5.29.1

द्वारवत्यां स्थिते कृष्णे शक्रस्त्रिभुवनेश्वरः ।
आजगामाथ मैत्रेय मत्तैरावतपृष्ठगः

Indra, lord of the three worlds, came to Kṛṣṇa in Dvārakā, riding on the back of the intoxicated Airāvata.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
द्वारवत्याम् (dvāravatyām) — in Dvārakā
स्थिते कृष्णे (sthite kṛṣṇe) — when Kṛṣṇa was staying
शक्रः (śakraḥ) — Indra
त्रि-भुवन-ईश्वरः (tri-bhuvana-īśvaraḥ) — lord of the three worlds
आजगाम (ājagāma) — came
अथ (atha) — then
मैत्रेय (maitreya) — O Maitreya
मत्त-ऐरावत-पृष्ठगः (matta-airāvata-pṛṣṭhagaḥ) — riding Airāvata's back
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dvāravatyāṃ sthite kṛṣṇe śakrastribhuvaneśvaraḥ | ājagāmātha maitreya mattairāvatapṛṣṭhagaḥ

Śrīdhara

idānīṃ bhauma-vadha-prasaṅgena hareḥ sahasraśo bhāryā-lābhaṃ vaktum indrāgamanādikam āha—dvāravatyām ity ādinā, parimṛśyatām (12) ity antena || 1 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

इदानीं भौम-वध-प्रसङ्गेन हरेः सहस्रशो भार्या-लाभं वक्तुम् इन्द्रागमनादिकम् आह—द्वारवत्याम् इत्य् आदिना, परिमृश्यताम् (१२) इत्य् अन्तेन ॥१॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Now, on the occasion of Bhauma’s killing, the arrival of Indra and the rest is discussed in order to speak of Hari’s gaining thousands of wives, beginning with “in Dvārakā” and ending with “let it be considered” (12).

VP 5.29.6

Word-for-word

दिवौकसः (divaukasaḥ) — the gods
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yuṣmaddordaṇḍasambhūtiparitrāte jagattraye | yajvayajñāṃśasamprāptyā tṛptiṃ yānti divaukasaḥ

Śrīdhara

yuṣmad iti | yajvanāṃ yajñāṃśā yajña-bhāgāḥ teṣāṃ samprāptyā || 6 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

Śrīdhara commentary.

“Your” means the sacrificial portions of the sacrificers—the shares of the sacrifice; through obtaining those, [the gods are satisfied].

VP 5.29.14

Viṣṇu Purāṇa

आरुह्य (āruhya) — mounting

गगन-चरम् (gagana-caram) — the sky-goer

प्राग्ज्योतिषम् पुरम् (prāgjyotiṣam puram) — to Prāgjyotiṣa city
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sañcityāgatamāruhya garuḍaṃ gaganecaram | satyabhāmāṃ samāropya yayau prāgjyotiṣaṃ puram

Śrīdhara

sañcintyeti | sañcintyāgatam smṛti-mātreṇopagataṃ satyabhāmāṃ samāropyety atra harivaṃśoktaṃ kāraṇaṃ draṣṭavyam | nāradānīte pārijāta-kusume rukmiṇyai datte tad viditvā prakupitāṃ satyabhāmāṃ sāntvayatā bhagavatoktam || 14 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

सञ्चिन्त्येति । सञ्चिन्त्यागतम् स्मृति-मात्रेणोपगतं सत्यभामां समारोप्येत्य् अत्र हरिवंशोक्तं कारणं द्रष्टव्यम् । नारदानीते पारिजात-कुसुमे रुक्मिण्यै दत्ते तद् विदित्वा प्रकुपितां सत्यभामां सान्त्वयता भगवतोक्‍तम् ॥१४॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

“Having thought” means: having thought of the one who came by mere recollection. The reason stated in the Harivaṃśa for placing Satyabhāmā [on Garuḍa] should be understood. When the Pārijāta flower brought by Nārada was given to Rukmiṇī, the Lord spoke this while pacifying the angered Satyabhāmā.

प्रसीदेति । किं च अदोषाय दोष-निवृत्त्य्-अर्थं त्वत्-पुत्रोऽपि त्वयैव निपातितः । वध-दण्डेन निष्पापः कृतः । किम् अत्र मया प्रार्थ्यम् इति भावः ॥२९॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

“Be gracious” means: for removal of fault, your son too was struck down by you alone. He was made free from sin by the punishment of death. What is there for me to request here? This is the purport.

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

चतुर्-दंष्ट्रान् इति । षट्-सहस्रान् षट्-सहस्राणि सङ्ख्या एषां तान् नियुतानि लक्षाणि ॥३२॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

“Four-tusked” means six thousand. “Niyuta” means ten-thousands; their number is counted in lakhs.
