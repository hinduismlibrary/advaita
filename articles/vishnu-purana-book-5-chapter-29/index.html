VP 5.29.1

द्वारवत्यांस्थिते कृष्णेशक्रस् त्रिभुवनेश्वरः ।
आजगामाथ मैत्रेय मत्तैरावत-पृष्ठगः ॥१॥

While Kṛṣṇa was staying in Dvārakā, Śakra, lord of the three worlds, came, O Maitreya, riding on intoxicated Airāvata.

Śrīdhara translation

Now, to speak of Hari’s gaining thousands of wives in connection with Bhauma’s slaying, he relates Indra’s arrival and the rest, beginning with “in Dvārakā” and ending with “let it be considered.”
