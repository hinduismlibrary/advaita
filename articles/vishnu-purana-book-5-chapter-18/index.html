VP 5.18.1

चिन्तयन्न् इति गोविन्दम् उपगम्य स यादवः ।
अक्रूरोऽस्मीति चरणौ ननाम शिरसा हरेः ॥१॥

Thinking thus, that Yādava approached Govinda; saying, “I am Akrūra,” he bowed his head at Hari’s feet.

Śrīdhara translation

“Thinking thus.” By saying, “I am Akrūra,” he made known through his Yādava identity that he was connected with them; he bowed after saying his own name.

“Meaning-laden,” etc. The definition of bhāva and the rest is: “A mental modification supported by an external object is called bhāva by the wise; its heightening is regarded as rasa.
