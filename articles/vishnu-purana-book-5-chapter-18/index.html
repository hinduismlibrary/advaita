VP 5.18.1

चिन्तयन्निति गोविन्दमुपगम्य स यादवः ।
अक्रुरोस्मीति चरणौ ननाम शिरसा हरेः

Sri Paraasharar - All the time thinking like this, that He is sarvajnya, He knows that I have not committed any sin, and He will definitely accept me, with that hope, Akroora went there and bowed down to Krishna and paid obeisance at His lotus feet, and also told his name, that he is Akroora.

Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

cintayanniti govindamupagamya sa yādavaḥ | akrurosmīti caraṇau nanāma śirasā hareḥ

Śrīdhara

cintayann iti | yādavo’krūro’smīti yādavatvoktyā svasya tat-sambanditāṃ vijñāpayan sva-nāmokti-pūrvakaṃ praṇanāma || 1 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

चिन्तयन्न् इति । यादवोऽक्रूरोऽस्मीति यादवत्वोक्त्या स्वस्य तत्-सम्बन्दितां विज्ञापयन् स्व-नामोक्ति-पूर्वकं प्रणनाम ॥१॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

No separate English commentary is supplied.

VP 5.18.2

VP 5.18.4

VP 5.18.13

दृष्ट्वा गोपीजनःसास्रः श्लथद्वलयबाहुकः ।
निशश्वासातिदुःखार्तः प्राह चेदं परस्परम्

All the gopis see that Krishna is leaving Gokula and going to Mathura. Immediately, all their hands became thin, and all the rings, bangles they were wearing in their hands, started to become loose and fall down. They started deep breaths, because of feeling very dejected, that Krishna is leaving Gokula and going to Mathura, and they started to talk to each other like this.

VP 5.18.14

मथुरां प्राप्य गोविन्दः कथं गोकुलमेष्यति ।
नगरस्त्रीकलालापमधु श्रोत्रेण पास्यति

This Krishna is going to Mathura, how will He come back to Gokula. He will be totally captured and will be enjoying with His ears all the wonderful talk of the women folk of the city Mathura.

Transliteration (IAST)

Śrīdhara commentary.

No separate English commentary is supplied.

VP 5.18.15

विलासेति ॥ विलासवाक्यपानेषु ग्राम्यगोपीषु – अविदग्धगोपीषु

The gopi janaas are not immersed in sweet talk and all that; the city women folk are all very good in that, and He will be captivated by that. How can He smile even and turn towards all of us, we are all graamya gopis living in the village. We are unaware of all those things which the city women do.

Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vilāseti || vilāsavākyapāneṣu grāmyagopīṣu – avidagdhagopīṣu

Śrīdhara

vilāsa-vākyeti | vilāsa-vākyāni nayanābhinna-rūpa-vilāsa-yuktāni vākyāni teṣāṃ pāneṣu śṛṅgārādi-rasavattvāt amṛta-mādhuryavattvāt vakyānām atyādareṇa śrotra-dvārā peyatvaṃ siddham | cittasya tad-viṣaye kṛtāspadatvāt pātṛtvaṃ ca siddham | atas tac-cittaṃ bhūyo grāmya-gopīṣu kathaṃ yāsyati ? na katham api yāsyatīty arthaḥ || 15 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

विलास-वाक्येति । विलास-वाक्यानि नयनाभिन्न-रूप-विलास-युक्तानि वाक्यानि तेषां पानेषु शृङ्गारादि-रसवत्त्वात् अमृत-माधुर्यवत्त्वात् वक्यानाम् अत्यादरेण श्रोत्र-द्वारा पेयत्वं सिद्धम् । चित्तस्य तद्-विषये कृतास्पदत्वात् पातृत्वं च सिद्धम् । अतस् तच्-चित्तं भूयो ग्राम्य-गोपीषु कथं यास्यति ? न कथम् अपि यास्यतीत्य् अर्थः ॥१५॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

No separate English commentary is supplied.

VP 5.18.17

Their talk is always with meaningful smiles; they speak very sweetly, which is very attractive. They have very attractive walk. The city folk have all this, it is well known. Even their glances are all like that. Their ways are very good, and everybody gets attracted by that. Our speech is not like that, it can never exceed the way that they speak attractively, with a lot of meaning, smiles and glances; we can never do such things.

विलासो नेत्रजो ज्ञेयो विलासो नयनान्तजः ॥ इति ।

नागरीणाम् एतद्-वाक्यादिकम् अतीवानन्द-जनकम् ॥१७॥

VP 5.18.18

ग्राम्यो हरिरयं तासां विलासनिगडैर्युतः ।
भवतीनांपुनः पार्श्वं कया युक्त्या समेष्यति

One gopi is telling another gopi like this. Hari, who lived in Gokula, all the way here, when He goes and sees their wonderful attractive ways there, how can you even think of He coming back, to you people? It is not a village thing. It is something new to Him. He is only used to the talk of the Gokula women folk only, the gopis. Now, He goes and sees something which is very new and different; it will be very attractive to Him, and by what logic will He come back here, there is no way that He will even come back to you people. This is what one gopi is telling another.

Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Krishna told here, also includes the names of all the avataaraas like Varaaha, Raama, Narasimha. In Moksha Dharma, it is said that Chaturmukha Brahma does Aniruddha Upaasanaa. In this puraana also, this is being told.
