BP 3.2.4.1

सूत उवाच ।
इति श्रुत्वा स वैतालो राजानमिदमब्रवीत् ।
काचिद्भोगावती नाम्ना नगरी परमाद्भुता ॥ 1 ॥

Sūta said: Hearing this, the Vetāla spoke to the king: “There is a wondrous city called Bhogavatī…”

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
सूत (sūta) — Sūta;
उवाच (uvāca) — said;
इति (iti) — thus;
श्रुत्वा (śrutvā) — having heard;
स (sa) — he;
वैतालो (vaitālo) — Vetāla;
राजानमिदमब्रवीत् (rājānamidamabravīt) — said this to the king;
काचिद्भोगावती (kācidbhogāvatī) — a certain city called Bhogavatī;
नाम्ना (nāmnā) — by name;
नगरी (nagarī) — city;
परमाद्भुता (paramādbhutā) — wondrous;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

sūta uvāca | iti śrutvā sa vaitālo rājānamidamabravīt | kācidbhogāvatī nāmnā nagarī paramādbhutā || 1 ||

BP 3.2.4.2

रूपवर्मा च नृपतिस्तत्र राज्यं करोति वै ।
चूडामणिरिति ख्यातः शुको बुद्धिविशारदः ॥ 2 ॥

“…where King Rūpavarman rules. In his palace lives a wise parrot known as Cūḍāmaṇi.”

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
रूपवर्मा (rūpavarmā) — Rūpavarman;
च (ca) — and;
नृपतिस्तत्र (nṛpatistatra) — the king there;
राज्यं (rājyaṃ) — a reign;
करोति (karoti) — does;
वै (vai) — indeed;
चूडामणिरिति (cūḍāmaṇiriti) — known as Cūḍāmaṇi;
ख्यातः (khyātaḥ) — famous;
शुको (śuko) — the parrot Śuka;
बुद्धिविशारदः (buddhiviśāradaḥ) — wise in understanding;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

rūpavarmā ca nṛpatistatra rājyaṃ karoti vai | cūḍāmaṇiriti khyātaḥ śuko buddhiviśāradaḥ || 2 ||

BP 3.2.4.3

तस्य भूपस्य गेहे च निवसनञ्च्छुभपंजरे ।
कदाचिद्रूपवर्मा च त्रिंशदब्द उरूर्जितः ॥ 3 ॥

The parrot lived in an auspicious cage in the king’s palace. One day, when Rūpavarman was about thirty years old…

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
तस्य (tasya) — of him;
भूपस्य (bhūpasya) — of the king;
गेहे (gehe) — in the house;
च (ca) — and;
निवसनञ्च्छुभपंजरे (nivasanañcchubhapaṃjare) — dwelling in an auspicious cage;
कदाचिद्रूपवर्मा (kadācidrūpavarmā) — once Rūpavarman;
च (ca) — and;
त्रिंशदब्द (triṃśadabda) — thirty years;
उरूर्जितः (urūrjitaḥ) — mighty;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

tasya bhūpasya gehe ca nivasanañcchubhapaṃjare | kadācidrūpavarmā ca triṃśadabda urūrjitaḥ || 3 ||

BP 3.2.4.4

पप्रच्छ मम योग्या वै शुक काचिद्वरांगना ।
चेदस्ति तर्हि मे ब्रूहि श्रुत्वा तं चाब्रवीच्छुकः ॥ 4 ॥

…he asked, “Tell me, parrot, if there is a beautiful woman who would be a suitable wife for me.” The parrot replied:

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
पप्रच्छ (papraccha) — asked;
मम (mama) — my;
योग्या (yogyā) — suited; worthy;
वै (vai) — indeed;
शुक (śuka) — O parrot;
काचिद्वरांगना (kācidvarāṃganā) — a beautiful woman;
चेदस्ति (cedasti) — if there is;
तर्हि (tarhi) — then;
मे (me) — to me;
ब्रूहि (brūhi) — tell;
श्रुत्वा (śrutvā) — having heard;
तं (taṃ) — him;
चाब्रवीच्छुकः (cābravīcchukaḥ) — and the parrot said;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

papraccha mama yogyā vai śuka kācidvarāṃganā | cedasti tarhi me brūhi śrutvā taṃ cābravīcchukaḥ || 4 ||

BP 3.2.4.5

मगधेश्वरभूपस्य कन्या चन्द्रवती शुभा ।
तव योग्या हि भो राजन्सांप्रतं तां गृहाण वै ॥ 5 ॥

“The lovely Candravatī, daughter of the king of Magadha, is indeed suited to you. Take her now, O king.”

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
मगधेश्वरभूपस्य (magadheśvarabhūpasya) — of the king of Magadha;
कन्या (kanyā) — the maiden;
चन्द्रवती (candravatī) — Candravatī;
शुभा (śubhā) — auspicious;
तव (tava) — your;
योग्या (yogyā) — suited; worthy;
हि (hi) — indeed;
भो (bho) — O friend;
राजन्सांप्रतं (rājansāṃprataṃ) — O king, now;
तां (tāṃ) — her;
गृहाण (gṛhāṇa) — take; marry;
वै (vai) — indeed;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

magadheśvarabhūpasya kanyā candravatī śubhā | tava yogyā hi bho rājansāṃprataṃ tāṃ gṛhāṇa vai || 5 ||

BP 3.2.4.6

इति श्रुत्वा स नृपतिर्गणेशं द्विजसत्तमम् ।
प्रेषयित्वा ददौ द्रव्यं यथोद्वाह्या तथा कुरु ॥ 6 ॥

Hearing this, the king summoned the excellent Brahmin Gaṇeśa, gave him wealth, and said, “Arrange the marriage as it should be.”

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
इति (iti) — thus;
श्रुत्वा (śrutvā) — having heard;
स (sa) — he;
नृपतिर्गणेशं (nṛpatirgaṇeśaṃ) — the king [sent] Gaṇeśa;
द्विजसत्तमम् (dvijasattamam) — the excellent Brahmin;
प्रेषयित्वा (preṣayitvā) — having sent;
ददौ (dadau) — gave;
द्रव्यं (dravyaṃ) — wealth; goods;
यथोद्वाह्या (yathodvāhyā) — as she should be married;
तथा (tathā) — so;
कुरु (kuru) — do; make;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

iti śrutvā sa nṛpatirgaṇeśaṃ dvijasattamam | preṣayitvā dadau dravyaṃ yathodvāhyā tathā kuru || 6 ||

BP 3.2.4.7

गणेशोपि गतस्तूर्णं देशे मागधके शुभे ।
महादेवं च संपूज्य चकार स्तवनं मुदा ॥ 7 ॥

Gaṇeśa quickly went to the auspicious land of Magadha. Worshipping Mahādeva, he gladly offered this hymn:

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
गणेशोपि (gaṇeśopi) — Gaṇeśa too;
गतस्तूर्णं (gatastūrṇaṃ) — went quickly;
देशे (deśe) — in the land;
मागधके (māgadhake) — in Magadha;
शुभे (śubhe) — auspicious;

संपूज्य (saṃpūjya) — having worshipped;
चकार (cakāra) — ruled; made;
स्तवनं (stavanaṃ) — a hymn;
मुदा (mudā) — gladly;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

gaṇeśopi gatastūrṇaṃ deśe māgadhake śubhe | mahādevaṃ ca saṃpūjya cakāra stavanaṃ mudā || 7 ||

BP 3.2.4.8

नमः शिवाय शांताय सर्वाभीष्टप्रदायिने ।
भवाय शंकरायैव रुद्राय सततं नमः ॥ 8 ॥

“Homage constantly to Śiva, the peaceful one, giver of every desired good; to Bhava, Śaṅkara, and Rudra.”

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
नमः (namaḥ) — salutation;
शिवाय (śivāya) — to Śiva;
शांताय (śāṃtāya) — the peaceful one;
सर्वाभीष्टप्रदायिने (sarvābhīṣṭapradāyine) — giver of every desired boon;
भवाय (bhavāya) — to Bhava;
शंकरायैव (śaṃkarāyaiva) — to Śaṅkara indeed;
रुद्राय (rudrāya) — to Rudra;
सततं (satataṃ) — constantly;
नमः (namaḥ) — salutation;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

namaḥ śivāya śāṃtāya sarvābhīṣṭapradāyine | bhavāya śaṃkarāyaiva rudrāya satataṃ namaḥ || 8 ||

BP 3.2.4.9

मृडायानंदरूपाय सर्वदुःखहराय च ॥ 9 ॥

“Homage to Mṛḍa, whose nature is bliss and who removes all suffering.”

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
मृडायानंदरूपाय (mṛḍāyānaṃdarūpāya) — to Mṛḍa, whose nature is bliss;

Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

mṛḍāyānaṃdarūpāya sarvaduḥkhaharāya ca || 9 ||

BP 3.2.4.10

इत्युक्तवति विप्रे च तदा चन्द्रवती शुभा ।
कामातुराब्रवीच्चैनां नाम्ना मदनमञ्जरीम् ॥ 10 ॥

While the Brahmin was speaking, the lovely Candravatī, overcome by desire, spoke to her companion named Madanamañjarī:

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
इत्युक्तवति (ityuktavati) — when he had spoken thus;
विप्रे (vipre) — the Brahmin;
च (ca) — and;
तदा (tadā) — then;
चन्द्रवती (candravatī) — Candravatī;
शुभा (śubhā) — auspicious;
कामातुराब्रवीच्चैनां (kāmāturābravīccaināṃ) — lovesick, she spoke to her;
नाम्ना (nāmnā) — by name;
मदनमञ्जरीम् (madanamañjarīm) — Madanamañjarī;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

ityuktavati vipre ca tadā candravatī śubhā | kāmāturābravīccaināṃ nāmnā madanamañjarīm || 10 ||

BP 3.2.4.11

मम योग्यश्च पुरुषः कश्चिदस्ति महीतले ।
साह भो रूपवर्मा च योग्यो भोगापुरीपतिः ॥ 11 ॥

“Is there a man on earth who would be suitable for me?” She answered, “Rūpavarman, king of Bhogavatī, is suitable.”

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
मम (mama) — my;
योग्यश्च (yogyaśca) — suitable;
पुरुषः (puruṣaḥ) — man;
कश्चिदस्ति (kaścidasti) — is there someone;
महीतले (mahītale) — on earth;
साह (sāha) — she said;
भो (bho) — O friend;
रूपवर्मा (rūpavarmā) — Rūpavarman;
च (ca) — and;
योग्यो (yogyo) — suitable;
भोगापुरीपतिः (bhogāpurīpatiḥ) — the lord of Bhogavatī;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

mama yogyaśca puruṣaḥ kaścidasti mahītale | sāha bho rūpavarmā ca yogyo bhogāpurīpatiḥ || 11 ||

BP 3.2.4.12

इति श्रुत्वा तु सा देवी दुर्गां वाञ्छितदायिनीम् ।
तुष्टाव मनसा सुभ्रूर्यया जातमिदं जगत् ॥ 12 ॥

Hearing this, the beautiful woman worshipped in her mind Durgā, who grants wishes and from whom the world was born.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
इति (iti) — thus;
श्रुत्वा (śrutvā) — having heard;
तु (tu) — indeed; then;
सा (sā) — she;
देवी (devī) — Goddess;
दुर्गां (durgāṃ) — Durgā;
वाञ्छितदायिनीम् (vāñchitadāyinīm) — granter of wishes;
तुष्टाव (tuṣṭāva) — praised; worshipped;
मनसा (manasā) — in her mind;
सुभ्रूर्यया (subhrūryayā) — the lovely-browed one, by whom;
जातमिदं (jātamidaṃ) — this world was born;
जगत् (jagat) — the world;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

iti śrutvā tu sā devī durgāṃ vāñchitadāyinīm | tuṣṭāva manasā subhrūryayā jātamidaṃ jagat || 12 ||

BP 3.2.4.13

नमोनमो जगन्मातर्मम कार्यप्रदायिनि ।
त्रिलिंगजननी त्वं वै वर्णमूर्तिः सनातनी ॥ 13 ॥

“Homage again and again to you, Mother of the world, who brings my wish to fulfillment. You are the eternal mother of the three worlds and the embodiment of the varṇas.”

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

जगन्मातर्मम (jaganmātarmama) — O Mother of the world, my;
कार्यप्रदायिनि (kāryapradāyini) — giver of what I seek;
त्रिलिंगजननी (triliṃgajananī) — mother of the three worlds;
त्वं (tvaṃ) — you;
वै (vai) — indeed;
वर्णमूर्तिः (varṇamūrtiḥ) — embodiment of the varṇas;
सनातनी (sanātanī) — eternal;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

namonamo jaganmātarmama kāryapradāyini | triliṃgajananī tvaṃ vai varṇamūrtiḥ sanātanī || 13 ||

BP 3.2.4.14

त्वं स्वाहा त्वं स्वधा संध्या नमस्तस्यै नमोनमः ।
नृपतिं रूपवर्माणं मत्पतिं कुरु भोः शिवे ॥ 14 ॥

कुरु (kuru) — do; make;
भोः (bhoḥ) — O;

Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

tvaṃ svāhā tvaṃ svadhā saṃdhyā namastasyai namonamaḥ | nṛpatiṃ rūpavarmāṇaṃ matpatiṃ kuru bhoḥ śive || 14 ||

BP 3.2.4.15

इति स्तुत्या प्रसन्नाभूज्जगदंबा जगन्मयी ।
पितरं मगधेशं च मोहयित्वा च मातरम् ॥ 15 ॥

Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

iti stutyā prasannābhūjjagadaṃbā jaganmayī | pitaraṃ magadheśaṃ ca mohayitvā ca mātaram || 15 ||

BP 3.2.4.16

विवाहं कारयामास मासान्ते सिद्धरूपिणी ।
रूपवर्मा चंद्रवती बुभुजाते परं सुखम् ॥ 16 ॥

बुभुजाते (bubhujāte) — the two enjoyed;

सुखम् (sukham) — happiness;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

vivāhaṃ kārayāmāsa māsānte siddharūpiṇī | rūpavarmā caṃdravatī bubhujāte paraṃ sukham || 16 ||

BP 3.2.4.17

एकस्मिन्दिवसे राजन्मेनां मदनमंजरीम् ।
नृपः प्राह विवाहं त्वं सुशुकेन कुरुष्व भोः ॥ 17 ॥

Word-for-word

विवाहं (vivāhaṃ) — marriage;
त्वं (tvaṃ) — you;
सुशुकेन (suśukena) — with the parrot Śuka;
कुरुष्व (kuruṣva) — do;
भोः (bhoḥ) — O;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
