VP 4.4.1

काश्यपदुहिता सुमतिर्विदर्भराजतनया केशिनी च द्वे भार्ये सगरस्यास्ताम्

Sagara had two wives - Sumathi, daughter of Kaashyapa, and Keshini, daughter of Vidarbha raajaa.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kāśyapaduhitā sumatirvidarbharājatanayā keśinī ca dve bhārye sagarasyāstām

Śrīdhara

kapilaḥ sāgarān ṣaṣṭi-sahasrāṇi yatādahat . saudāsarakṣobhavaśca tathā turye’nurṇyate . kaśyapaduhita iti . kaśyapasya marīciputrasya vinatātanayāyā ityagre vakṣyamāṇatvāt ||१||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

कपिलः सागरान् षष्टि-सहस्राणि यतादहत् । सौदासरक्षोभवश्च तथा तुर्येऽनुर्ण्यते । कश्यपदुहित इति । कश्यपस्य मरीचिपुत्रस्य विनतातनयाया इत्यग्रे वक्ष्यमाणत्वात् ॥१॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Sagara had two wives - Sumathi, daughter of Kaashyapa, and Keshini, daughter of Vidarbha raajaa.

They worshipped Ourva Rishi, and wanted to have children, as they did not have children. They worshipped with great devotion, and he was very happy. He gave them a boon.

VP 4.4.10

अथ तत्रापि च वयस्यतीते असच्चरितमेवैनं पिता तत्याज

Even when he grew up, Asamanjasa was only involved in bad things, and so, his father Sagara deserted him, left him. He sent him out of the country.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अपवृत्तो दुर्वृत्तः, अतीतवाल्यो गतबालत्वः । तत्र बाल्ये अतीतेऽपि तदेव दुष्टं चरितं यस्य तम्। ॥१०॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Even when he grew up, Asamanjasa was only involved in bad things, and so, his father Sagara deserted him, left him. He sent him out of the country.

Even the 60000 sons, of Sumati, also followed Asamanjasa's ways only, all bad ways.

VP 4.4.12

ततश्चासमंजसचरितानुकारिभिस्सागरैरपध्वस्तयज्ञादिसन्मार्गे जगति देवास्सकलविद्यामयमसंस्पृष्टमशेषदोषैर्भगवतः पुरुषोत्तमस्यांशभूतं कपिलं प्रणम्य तदर्थमूचुः

When all the 60000 sons also followed Asamanjasa's bad ways only, all the devaas got very worried that 60000 people are going in bad ways like this, that all the yajnya anusthaana will stop, there will be nobody following the right path, the path of dharma, in this whole world, no one will follow the good conduct taught in the Vedaas. The devaas approached Kapila Rishi, who had mastered all the branches of knowledge, and was never touched by any defect. He was an amsha of Bhagavaan Purushottama Himself. The devaas went and did pranaama to Kapila Rishi, and told him thus.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tataścāsamaṃjasacaritānukāribhissāgarairapadhvastayajñādisanmārge jagati devāssakalavidyāmayamasaṃspṛṣṭamaśeṣadoṣairbhagavataḥ puruṣottamasyāṃśabhūtaṃ kapilaṃ praṇamya tadarthamūcuḥ

Śrīdhara

apadhyastā nirākṛtāḥ yajñādayaḥ sanmārgāḥ yasmin tathābhūte jagati sati tadarthaṃ yajñādilokarūpamartham ||१२||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अपध्यस्ता निराकृताः यज्ञादयः सन्मार्गाः यस्मिन् तथाभूते जगति सति तदर्थं यज्ञादिलोकरूपमर्थम् ॥१२॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

When all the 60000 sons also followed Asamanjasa's bad ways only, all the devaas got very worried that 60000 people are going in bad ways like this, that all the yajnya anusthaana will stop, there will be nobody following the right path, the path of dharma, in this whole world, no one will follow the good conduct taught in the Vedaas. The devaas approached Kapila Rishi, who had mastered all the branches of knowledge, and was never touched by any defect. He was an amsha of Bhagavaan Purushottama Himself. The devaas went and did pranaama to Kapila Rishi, and told him thus.

VP 4.4.15

अत्यार्त्त जगत्परित्राणाय च भगवतोऽत्र शरीरग्रहणमित्याकर्ण्य भगवानाहाल्पैरैव दिनैर्विनंक्ष्यन्तीति

You have taken this birth, where Bhagavaan Purushottama has Himself manifested. In this world which is suffering a lot, in order to protect this world, you have taken this birth, this shareera. He said - &quot;Don't worry, in a short time, they will all get destroyed&quot;.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

You have taken this birth, where Bhagavaan Purushottama has Himself manifested. In this world which is suffering a lot, in order to protect this world, you have taken this birth, this shareera. He said - "Don't worry, in a short time, they will all get destroyed".

VP 4.4.18

ततस्तत्तनयाश्चाश्वखुरगतिनिर्वंधेन वसुधातलमेकैकं योजनमवनेश्च निचख्नुः

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tatastattanayāścāśvakhuragatinirvaṃdhena vasudhātalamekaikaṃ yojanamavaneśca nicakhnuḥ

Śrīdhara

tato’nantaraṃ tattanayāḥ sagaratanayāḥ . atinirbandhena khuracihnānusāreṇa vasudhātalaṃ viviśuriti pūrvoktasyaiva viśatervacanavipariṇāmenānvayaḥ . teṣāñcaikaiko yojanaṃ yojanamekaikaṃ cakhāna garttañcakāra ity arthaḥ cakhanuritti pāṭhe tattanayā bhuvastalaṃ cakhanurityekaṃ vākyaṃ tatreyattāmāha—ekaikaḥ arthāt teṣāmeva, yojanaṃ yojanaṃ cakhānetyanyadvākyam ||१८||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

ततोऽनन्तरं तत्तनयाः सगरतनयाः । अतिनिर्बन्धेन खुरचिह्नानुसारेण वसुधातलं विविशुरिति पूर्वोक्तस्यैव विशतेर्वचनविपरिणामेनान्वयः । तेषाञ्चैकैको योजनं योजनमेकैकं चखान गर्त्तञ्चकार इत्य् अर्थः चखनुरित्ति पाठे तत्तनया भुवस्तलं चखनुरित्येकं वाक्यं तत्रेयत्तामाह—एकैकः अर्थात् तेषामेव, योजनं योजनं चखानेत्यन्यद्वाक्यम् ॥१८॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

These 60000 sons, to search for that horse and bring it back, so that he can complete the Ashvamedha yaaga, followed the marks of the hoofs of the horse, and dug one yojana below the earth.

VP 4.4.21

सगरोऽप्यवगम्याश्वानुसारि तत्पुत्रबलमशेष परमर्षिणा कपिलेन तेजसा दग्धं ततोंऽशुमन्तमसमंजस पुत्र माहूयाश्वानयनाय युयोज
