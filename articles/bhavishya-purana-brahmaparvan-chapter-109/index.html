Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 109 — The Seventh Day Worship of Mārtaṇḍa

BP 1.109.1

ब्रह्मोवाच। मार्तण्डसप्तमीं कृष्ण यथान्यां वक्ष्यामि तेऽनघ। शृणुष्वैकमना वीर गदतो मे शुभप्रदाम् ॥

Brahmā said. O Kṛṣṇa, O sinless one, I shall tell you of another worship of Mārtaṇḍa on the seventh day, which brings good fortune. Listen with an attentive mind, O hero, as I speak.

BP 1.109.2

यस्याः सम्यगनुष्ठानात्प्राप्नोत्यभिमतं फलम्। पौषे मासे सिते पक्षे सप्तम्यां समुपोषितः ॥

By its proper performance, one obtains the desired result. One should fast on the seventh day of the bright fortnight in the month of Pauṣa.

BP 1.109.3

सम्यक्सम्पूज्य मार्तण्डं मार्तण्ड इति वै जपेत्। पूजयेत्कुतपं भक्त्या श्रद्धया परयान्वितः ॥

Having fully worshipped Mārtaṇḍa, one should indeed repeat the name Mārtaṇḍa. One should worship the Sun with devotion, endowed with supreme faith.

BP 1.109.4

धूपपुष्पोपहाराद्यैरुपवासैः समाहितः। मार्तण्डेति जपंन्नाम पुनस्तद्गतमानसः ॥

With incense, flowers, offerings, and the like, and with fasting, composed, one should repeat the name Mārtaṇḍa, the mind again fixed on him.

BP 1.109.5

विप्राय दक्षिणां दद्याद्यथाशक्त्या खगध्वज। स्वपन्विबोधन्स्खलितो मार्तण्डेति च कीर्तयेत् ॥

He should give a gift to a Brahmin according to his ability, O Bird Bannered One. While sleeping, waking, or stumbling, he should also utter Mārtaṇḍa.

BP 1.109.6

पाषण्डादीविकर्मस्थैरालापं च विवर्जयेत्। गोमूत्रं गोपयो वापि दधि क्षीरमथापि वा ॥

He should avoid conversation with heretics and those engaged in forbidden acts. For purification of himself, he should drink cow urine, cow milk, curd, or milk.

BP 1.109.7

गोदेहतः समुद्भूतं प्राश्नीयादात्मशुद्धये। द्वितीयेऽह्नि पुनः स्नातस्तथैवाभ्यर्चनं रवेः ॥

He should consume what arises from the body of the cow for purification of himself. On the second day, having bathed again, he should likewise worship Ravi.

BP 1.109.8

तेनैव नाम्ना सम्भूय दत्त्वा विप्राय दक्षिणाम्। ततो भुञ्जीत गोदोहसम्भूतेन समन्वितम् ॥

Having joined with that same name and given a gift to a Brahmin, he should then eat together with what is obtained from milking a cow.

BP 1.109.9

एवमेवाखिलान्मासानुपोष्य प्रयतः शुचिः। दद्याद्गवादिकं विप्रान्प्रतिमासं स्वशक्तितः ॥

In this very way, having fasted through all the months, pure and self controlled, he should give cows and the like to Brahmins each month according to his ability.

BP 1.109.10

धारिता चेत्पुनर्वर्षे यथाशक्त्या गवादिकम्। दत्त्वा परं रवेर्भूयः शृणु यत्फलमश्नुते ॥

If the observance is continued for another year, and one gives a cow and the like according to ability, hear again what fruit he obtains from the Sun.

BP 1.109.11

स्वर्णशृंगीं च पञ्चम्यां षष्ठ्यां च वृषभं नरः। प्रतिमासं द्विजातिभ्यो यद्दत्त्वा फलमश्नुते ॥

The fruit a person enjoys by giving a golden horned cow on the fifth day and a bull on the sixth day to the twice born each month,

BP 1.109.12

तत्प्राप्नोत्यखिलं सम्यग्व्रतमेतदुपोषितः। तं च लोकमवाप्नोति मार्तण्डो यत्र तिष्ठति ॥

Having fasted on this vow, he obtains all that completely and reaches the world where Mārtaṇḍa dwells.

BP 1.109.13

शाण्डेलेयसमः कृष्ण तेजसा नात्र संशयः। मार्तण्डसप्तमीमेतामुपोष्यैते गणा दिवि ॥

He becomes equal to Śāṇḍileya in radiance, O Kṛṣṇa, of this there is no doubt. These hosts in heaven, having fasted on this worship of Mārtaṇḍa on the seventh day,

BP 1.109.14

विद्योतमाना दृश्यन्ते लोकैरद्यापि भूधर। तस्मात्त्वमादिदेवेशं ग्रहेशं भास्करं रविम्। अनयार्चय गोविन्द गोपतिं गोलसन्निभम् ॥

They shine and are seen by people even today, O bearer of the earth. Therefore, O Govinda, worship in this way the first Lord of gods, the Lord of planets, Bhāskara Ravi, the Lord of cows, resembling the solar orb.
