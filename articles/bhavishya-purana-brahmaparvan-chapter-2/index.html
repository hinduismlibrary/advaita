Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 2: Creation, Cosmic Time, and Social Duties

BP 1.2.1

सुमन्तुरुवाच
शृणुष्वेदं महाबाहो पुराणं पञ्चलक्षणम् ।
यच्छ्रुत्वा मुच्यते राजन्पुरुषो ब्रह्महत्यया ॥ १

Sumantu said: Hear this Purāṇa of five marks, O mighty-armed one. By hearing it, a person is freed from killing a Brahmin, O king.

BP 1.2.2

पर्वाणि चात्र वै पञ्च कीर्तितानि स्वयम्भुवा ।
प्रथमं कथ्यते ब्राह्मं द्वितीयं वैष्णवं स्मृतम् ॥ २

The Self-born proclaimed five sections here. The first is called the Brahma section; the second, the Vishnu section.

BP 1.2.3

तृतीयं शैवमाख्यातं चतुर्थं त्वाष्ट्रमुच्यते ।
पञ्चमं प्रतिसर्गाख्यं सर्वलोकैः सुपूजितम् ॥ ३

The third is called the Shiva section; the fourth, the Maker's section; and the fifth, the Re-creation section, greatly honoured by all the worlds.

BP 1.2.4

एतानि तात पर्वाणि लक्षणानि निबोध मे ।
सर्गश्च प्रतिसर्गश्च वंशो मन्वन्तराणि च ॥ ४

Learn from me the marks of these sections, my child: creation, re-creation, lineage, the reigns of Manus,

BP 1.2.5

वंशानुचरितं चैव पुराणं पञ्चलक्षणम् ।
चतुर्दशभिर्विद्याभिर्भूषितं कुरुनन्दन ॥ ५

and the histories of lineages. A Purāṇa has five marks and is adorned with fourteen branches of knowledge, O delight of the Kurus.

BP 1.2.6

अङ्गानि चतुरो वेदा मीमांसा न्यायविस्तरः ।
पुराणं धर्मशास्त्रं च विद्या ह्येताश्चतुर्दश ॥ ६

The six limbs of the Veda, the four Vedas, ritual inquiry, logic, the Purāṇa, and the law treatise: these are the fourteen branches of knowledge.

BP 1.2.7

आयुर्वेदो धनुर्वेदो गान्धर्वश्चैव ते त्रयः ।
अर्थशास्त्रं चतुर्थं तु विद्या ह्यष्टादशैव ताः ॥ ७

The science of life, the knowledge of weapons, and the art of music are the three; political economy is the fourth. These are the eighteen branches of knowledge.

BP 1.2.8

प्रथमं कथ्यते सर्गो भूतानामिह सर्वशः ।
यच्छ्रुत्वा पापनिर्मुक्तो याति शान्तिमनुत्तमाम् ॥ ८

First, the creation of all beings here is told. By hearing it, one is freed from sin and goes to unsurpassed peace.

BP 1.2.9

जगदासीत्पुरा तात तमोभूतमलक्षणम् ।
अविज्ञेयमतर्क्यं च प्रसुप्तमिव सर्वशः ॥ ९

Formerly, my child, the universe was darkness, without distinguishing marks, unknowable, beyond reasoning, and as though entirely asleep.

BP 1.2.10

ततः स भगवानीशो ह्यव्यक्तो व्यञ्जयन्निदम् ।
महाभूतानि वृत्तौजाः प्रोत्थितस्तमनाशनः ॥.2.१०

Then the blessed, sovereign, unmanifest Lord, making this manifest, arose with great power over the elements, destroying darkness.

BP 1.2.11

योऽसावतीन्द्रियोऽग्राह्यः सूक्ष्मोऽव्यक्तः सनातनः ।
सर्वभूतमयोऽचिन्त्यः स एष स्वयमुत्थितः ॥१

He is beyond the senses, imperceptible, subtle, unmanifest, eternal, formed of all beings, and inconceivable; he arose by himself.

BP 1.2.12

योऽसौ षड्विंशको लोके तथा यः पुरुषोत्तमः ।
भास्करश्च महाबाहो परं ब्रह्म च कथ्यते ॥२

He is called the twenty-sixth principle in the world and the Supreme Person; he is also called Bhāskara and supreme Brahman, O mighty-armed one.

BP 1.2.13

सोऽभिध्याय शरीरात्स्वात्सिसृक्षुर्विविधाः प्रजाः ।
अत एव ससर्जादौ तासु वीर्यमवासृजत् ॥३

Desiring to create diverse beings from his own body, he contemplated. At the beginning he created the waters and cast his seed into them.

BP 1.2.14

यस्मादुत्पद्यते सर्वं सदेवासुरमानुषम् ।
बीजं शुक्रं तथा रेत उग्रं वीर्यं च कथ्यते ॥४

From him all arises, consisting of gods, demons, and humans. It is called seed, semen, generative fluid, fierce potency, and force.

BP 1.2.15

वीर्यस्यैतानि नामानि कथितानि स्वयम्भुवा ।
तदण्डमभवद्धैमं ज्वालामालाकुलं विभो ॥५

The Self-born declared these names of force. From it came the golden egg, covered with a mass of flames, O lord.

BP 1.2.16

यस्मिञ्जज्ञे स्वयं ब्रह्मा सर्वलोकपितामहः ।
सुरज्येष्ठश्चतुर्वक्त्रः परमेष्ठी पितामहः ॥६

Within it Brahmā himself was born, the grandsire of all worlds, eldest of the gods, four-faced, supreme, and Grandsire.

BP 1.2.17

क्षेत्रज्ञः पुरुषो वेधाः शम्भुर्नारायणस्तथा ।
पर्यायवाचकैः शब्दैरेव ब्रह्मा प्रकीर्त्यते ॥७

Brahmā is proclaimed by synonymous names: Knower of the Field, Person, Maker, Auspicious One, and Nārāyaṇa.

BP 1.2.18

सदा मनीषिभिस्तात विरञ्चिः कञ्जजस्तथा२ ।
आपो नारा इति प्रोक्ता आपो वै नरसूनवः ॥८

The wise always call him Virañci and Lotus-born, my child. The waters are called nārā, for they are said to be the offspring of Nara.

BP 1.2.19

ता यदस्यायनं पूर्वं तेन नारायणः स्मृतः ।
अरमित्येव शीघ्राय नियताः कविभिः कृताः ॥९

Because those waters were formerly his resting place, he is remembered as Nārāyaṇa. The poets established this name in connection with swiftness and settled repose.

BP 1.2.20

आप एवार्णवीभूत्वा सुशीघ्रास्तेन ता नराः ।
यत्तत्कारणमव्यक्तं नित्यं सदसदात्मकम् ॥.2.२०

Those waters quickly became an ocean, and on their account they were called nāras. That unmanifest cause is eternal and has being and non-being as its nature.

BP 1.2.21

तद्विसृष्टः स पुरुषो लोके ब्रह्मेति कीर्त्त्यते ।
एवं स भगवानण्डे तत्त्वमेव निरूप्य वै ॥२ १

Created from that, the Person is celebrated in the world as Brahmā. Thus the blessed Lord set forth the principles within the egg.

BP 1.2.22

ध्यानमास्थाय राजेन्द्र तदण्डमकरोद्द्विधा ।
शकलाभ्यां च राजेन्द्र दिवं भूमिं च निर्ममे ॥२

Entering meditation, O king of kings, he divided that egg in two; from its two halves he fashioned heaven and earth.

BP 1.2.23

अन्तर्व्योम दिशश्चाष्टौ वारुणं स्थानमेव हि ।
ऊर्ध्वं महान्गतो राजन् समन्ताल्लोकभूतये ॥३

Within it he made the space, the eight directions, and the watery realm. The Great One extended above and all around for the coming-to-be of the worlds, O king.

BP 1.2.24

महतश्चाप्यहंकारस्तस्माच्च त्रिगुणा अपि ।
त्रिगुणा अतिसूक्ष्मास्तु बुद्धिगम्या हि भारत ॥४

From the Great Principle came the I-principle, and from that the three qualities. The three qualities are exceedingly subtle and can be grasped by the intellect, O descendant of Bharata.

BP 1.2.25

उत्पत्तिहेतुभूता वै भूतानां महतां नृप ।
तेषामेव गृहीतानि शनैः पञ्चेन्द्रियाणि तु ॥५

They became the causes of the arising of the great elements, O king. From those very principles the five senses were gradually formed.

BP 1.2.26

तथैवावयवाः सूक्ष्माः षण्णामप्यमितौजसाम् ॥६

Likewise were the subtle constituent parts of all six principles of immeasurable power.

BP 1.2.27

संनिवेश्यात्ममात्रासु स राजन्भगवान्विभुः ।
भूतानि निर्ममे तात सर्वाणि विधिपूर्वकम् ॥७

Placing them within their own measures, the all-pervading Lord made all beings in due order, my child.

BP 1.2.28

यन्मूर्त्यवयवाः सूक्ष्मास्तस्येमान्याश्रयाणि षट् ।
तस्माच्छरीरमित्याहुस्तस्य मूर्तिं मनीषिणः ॥८

The six subtle limbs of his form are its supports; therefore the wise call it his body.

BP 1.2.29

महान्ति तानि भूतानि आविशन्ति ततो विभुम् ।
कर्मणा सह राजेन्द्र सगुणाश्चापि वै गुणाः ॥९

Then the great elements enter the all-pervading one together with action, and the qualities enter along with their qualities, O king of kings.

BP 1.2.30

तेषामिदं तु सप्तानां पुरुषाणां महौजसाम् ।
सूक्ष्माभ्यो मूर्तिमात्राभ्यः सम्भवत्यव्ययाद्वयम् ॥.2.३०

From the subtle particles of these seven mighty principles, the perishable world arises from the imperishable.

BP 1.2.31

भूतादिमहतस्तात येन व्याप्तमिदं जगत् ।
तस्मादपि महाबाहो पुरुषाः पञ्च एव हि ॥१

My child, this entire world is pervaded by the elements beginning with the source of beings and the Great Principle; therefore, O mighty-armed one, there are only five persons.

BP 1.2.32

केचिदेवं परां तात सृष्टिमिच्छन्ति पण्डिताः ।
अन्येऽप्येवं महाबाहो प्रवदन्ति मनीषिणः ॥२

Some wise people, my child, hold the highest creation to be thus; other wise people also describe it in this way, O mighty-armed one.

BP 1.2.33

योऽसावात्मा परस्तात कल्पादौ सृजते तनुम् ।
प्रजनश्च महाबाहो सिसृक्षुर्विविधाः प्रजाः ॥३

That supreme Self creates a body at the beginning of a cosmic cycle. Wishing to create diverse beings, he becomes their progenitor, O mighty-armed one.

BP 1.2.34

तेन सृष्टः पुद्गलस्तु प्रधानं विशते नृप ।
प्रधानं क्षोभितं तेन विकारान्युजते बहून् ॥४

The being created by him enters primary matter, O king. Stirred by him, primary matter joins with many transformations.

BP 1.2.35

उत्पद्यते महांस्तस्मात्ततो भूतादिरेव हि ।
उत्पद्यते विशालं च भूतादेः कुरुनन्दन ॥५

From it arises the Great Principle, and from that, the source of beings. From the source of beings the Vast arises, O delight of the Kurus.

BP 1.2.36

विशालाच्च हरिस्तात हरेश्चापि वृकास्तथा ।
वृकैर्मुष्णन्ति च बुधास्तस्मात्सर्वं भवेन्नृप ॥६

From the Vast comes Hari, my child, and from Hari the Vṛkas. Through the Vṛkas the wise discern; from them the whole comes to be, O king.

BP 1.2.37

तथैषामेव राजेन्द्र प्रादुर्भवति वेगतः ।
मात्राणां कुरुशार्दूल विबोधस्तदनन्तरम् ॥७

In this same sequence they quickly become manifest, O king; immediately afterward comes awareness of the subtle measures, O tiger among the Kurus.

BP 1.2.38

तस्मादपि हृषीकाणि विविधानि नृपोत्तम ।
तथेयं सृष्टिराख्याताऽऽराध्यतः कुरुनन्दन ॥८

From that arise the various senses, O best of kings. Thus this creation is described, O delight of the Kurus.

BP 1.2.39

भूयो निबोध राजेन्द्र भूतानामिह विस्तरम् ।
गुणाधिकानि सर्वाणि भूतानि पृथिवीपते ॥९

Now hear further, O king of kings, the detailed nature of the elements here. Each succeeding element possesses more qualities, O lord of the earth.

BP 1.2.40

आकाशमादितः कृत्वा उत्तरोत्तरमेव हि ।
एकं द्वौ च तथा त्रीणि चत्वारश्चापि पञ्च च ॥.2.४०

Beginning with space, each successive element has one, two, three, four, and five qualities.

BP 1.2.41

ततः स भगवान्ब्रह्मा पद्मासनगतः प्रभुः ।
सर्वेषां तु स नामानि कर्माणि च पृथक्पृथक् ॥१

Then the powerful Lord Brahmā, seated on the lotus, separately made the names and functions of them all.

BP 1.2.42

वेदशब्देभ्य एवादौ पृथक्संस्थाश्च निर्ममे ।
कर्मोद्भवानां देवानां सोऽसृजद्देहिनां प्रभुः ॥२

At the beginning he made their distinct forms from the words of the Veda. The Lord created the gods and embodied beings born of action.

BP 1.2.43

तुषितानां गणं राजन्यज्ञं चैव सनातनम् ।
दत्त्वा वीर समानेभ्यो गुह्यं ब्रह्म सनातनम् ॥३

He created the host of the Contented Gods and the eternal sacrifice, O king; and, O hero, he bestowed the secret eternal Brahman on those of equal standing.

BP 1.2.44

दुदोह यज्ञसिद्धयर्थमृग्यजुः सामलक्षणम् ।
कालं कालविभक्तीश्च ग्रहानृतूंस्तथा नृप ॥४

For the accomplishment of sacrifice he drew forth the Rig, Yajur, and Sama Vedas; he also made time and its divisions, the planets, and the seasons, O king.

BP 1.2.45

सरितः सागराञ्छैलान्समानि विषमाणि च ।
कामं क्रोधं तथा वाचं रतिं चापि कुरुद्वह ॥५

He made rivers, oceans, and mountains, level and uneven places, desire, anger, speech, and pleasure, O descendant of Kuru.

BP 1.2.46

सृष्टिं ससर्ज राजेन्द्र सिसृक्षुर्विविधाः प्रजाः ।
धर्माधर्मौ विवेकाय कर्मणां च तथासृजत् ॥४ ६

Desiring to create diverse beings, he created the world, O king; he also created duty and wrongdoing for the discerning of actions.

BP 1.2.47

सुखदुःखादिभिर्द्वन्द्वैः प्रजाश्चेमा न्ययोजयत् ।
अण्व्यो मात्राविनाशिन्यो दशार्धानां तु याः स्मृताः ॥७

He joined these creatures with pairs such as pleasure and pain, and with the subtle, imperishable measures remembered as five.

BP 1.2.48

ताभिः सर्वमिदं वीर सम्भवत्यनुपूर्वशः ।
यत्कृतं तु पुरा कर्म सन्नियुक्तेन वै नृप ॥८

Through them this entire world comes into being in due order, O hero. Whatever action was formerly done by an appointed being, O king—

BP 1.2.49

स तदेव स्वयं भेजे सृज्यमानं पुनः पुनः ।
हिंस्राहिंस्रे मृदुक्रूरे धर्माधर्मे ऋतानृते ॥९

—that very action each being receives again and again when created: violent or nonviolent, gentle or harsh, duty or wrongdoing, truth or falsehood.

BP 1.2.50

यद्यथास्याभवत्सर्गे तत्तस्य स्वयमाविशत् ।
यथा च लिङ्गान्यृतवः स्वयमेवानुपर्यये ॥.2.५०

Whatever each being was at creation entered into it of itself, just as the signs of the seasons follow one another in succession.

BP 1.2.51

स्वानि स्वान्यभिपद्यन्ते तथा कर्माणि देहिनः ।
लोकस्येह विवृद्ध्यर्थं मुखबाहूरुपादतः ॥१

In the same way embodied beings take up their own actions. For the growth of the world, he created the classes from mouth, arms, thighs, and feet.

BP 1.2.52

ब्रह्म क्षत्रं तथा चोभौ वैश्यशूद्रौ नृपोत्तम ।
मुखानि यानि चत्वारि तेभ्यो वेदा विनिःसृताः ॥२

The Brahmin and Kshatriya, and likewise the Vaishya and Śūdra, O best of kings. From his four mouths the Vedas issued forth.

BP 1.2.53

ऋग्वेदसहिता तात वसिष्ठेन महात्मना ।
पूर्वान्मुखान्महाबाहो दक्षिणाच्चापि वै शृणु ॥३

The Rig Veda, my child, together with the great-souled Vasiṣṭha, came from the eastern mouth; hear, O mighty-armed one, what came from the southern mouth.

BP 1.2.54

यजुर्वेदो महाराज याज्ञवल्क्येन वै सह ।
सामानि पश्चिमात्तात गौतमश्च महानृषिः ॥४

The Yajur Veda came from the southern mouth with Yājñavalkya, O great king; the Sama chants and the great sage Gautama came from the western mouth, my child.

BP 1.2.55

अथर्ववेदो राजेन्द्र मुखाच्चाप्युत्तरान्नृप ।
ऋषिश्चापि तथा राजञ्छौनको लोकपूजितः ॥५

The Atharva Veda came from the northern mouth, O king of kings, and with it the sage Śaunaka, honoured throughout the world.

BP 1.2.56

यत्तन्मुखं महाबाहो पञ्चमं लोकविश्रुतम् ।
अष्टादशपुराणानि सेतिहासानि भारत ॥६

From that renowned fifth mouth came the eighteen Purāṇas together with the histories, O descendant of Bharata.

BP 1.2.57

निर्गतानि ततस्तस्मान्मुखात्कुरुकुलोद्वह ।
तथान्याः स्मृतयश्चापि यमाद्या लोकपूजिताः ॥७

They issued from that mouth, O descendant of the Kuru line; so too did the other revered law codes, beginning with those of Yama.

BP 1.2.58

ततः स भगवान्देवो द्विधा देहमकारयत् ।
द्विधा कृत्वात्मनो देहमर्धेन पुरुषोऽभवत् ॥८

Then the blessed god made his body twofold. Dividing his own body in two, he became male in one half.

BP 1.2.59

अर्धेन नारी तस्यां च विराजमसृजत्प्रभुः ।
तपस्तप्त्वासृजद्यं तु स स्वयं पुरुषो विराट् ॥९

With the other half he became female, and in her the Lord created Virāj. Having performed austerity, he created him; he himself was the Virāṭ Person.

BP 1.2.60

स चकार तपो राजन्सिसृक्षुर्विविधाः प्रजाः ।
पतीन्प्रजानामसृजन्महर्षीनादितो दश ॥.2.६०

Desiring to create diverse beings, he performed austerity, O king, and at the beginning created ten great seers as lords of creatures.

BP 1.2.61

नारदं च भृगुं तात कं प्रचेतसमेव हि ।
पुलहं क्रतुं पुलस्त्यं च अत्रिमङ्गिरसं तथा ॥१

These were Nārada, Bhṛgu, Ka, Pracetas, Pulaha, Kratu, Pulastya, Atri, and Aṅgiras.

BP 1.2.62

मरीचिं चापि राजेन्द्र योऽसावाद्यः प्रजापतिः ।
एतांश्चान्यांश्च राजेन्द्र असृजद् भूरितेजसः ॥२

And Marīci, O king of kings, who was the first lord of creatures. He created these and other beings of great radiance.

BP 1.2.63

अथ देवानृषीन्दैत्यान्सोऽसृजत्कुरुनन्दन ।
यक्षरक्षः पिशाचांश्च गन्धर्वाप्सरसोऽसुरान् ॥३

Then he created gods, seers, Titans, nature spirits, demons, ghouls, heavenly musicians, celestial nymphs, and anti-gods, O delight of the Kurus.

BP 1.2.64

मनुष्याणां पितॄणां च सर्पाणां चैव भारत ।
नागानां च महाबाहो ससर्ज विविधान्गणान् ॥४

He created humans, ancestors, serpents, and great serpents in diverse groups, O descendant of Bharata, O mighty-armed one.

BP 1.2.65

क्षणरुचोऽशनिगणान्रोहितेन्द्रधनूंषि च ।
धूमकेतूंस्तथा चोल्का निर्याताञ्ज्योतिषां गणान् ॥५

He created flashes of lightning, heaps of thunderbolts, red rainbows, comets, meteors, and groups of heavenly lights.

BP 1.2.66

मनुष्यान्किन्नरान्मत्स्यान्वराहांश्च विहङ्गमान् ।
गजानश्वानथ पशून्मृगान्व्यालांश्च भारत ॥६

He created humans, celestial half-horse beings, fish, boars, birds, elephants, horses, cattle, deer, and fierce beasts, O descendant of Bharata.

BP 1.2.67

कृमिकीटपतङ्गांश्च यूकालिक्षकमत्कुणान् ।
सर्वं च दंशमशकं स्थावरं१ च पृथग्विधम् ॥७

He created worms, insects, moths, lice, nits, bedbugs, biting insects, mosquitoes, and every kind of immovable being.

BP 1.2.68

एवं स भास्करो देवः ससर्ज भुवनत्रयम् ।
येषां तु यादृशं कर्म भूतानामिह कीर्तितम् ॥८

Thus the god Bhāskara created the three worlds. Here the actions of beings, each according to its kind, are declared.

BP 1.2.69

कथयिष्यामि तत्सर्वं क्रमयोगं च जन्मनि ।
गजा व्याला मृगास्तात पशवश्च पृथग्विधाः ॥९

I shall tell all this and the order of births. Elephants, predators, deer, and various beasts, my child—

BP 1.2.70

पिशाचा मानुषास्तात रक्षांसि च जरायुजाः ।
द्विजास्तु अण्डजाः सर्पा नक्रा मत्स्याः सकच्छपाः ॥.2.७०

—and ghouls, humans, and demons are womb-born. Birds, snakes, crocodiles, fish, and turtles are egg-born.

BP 1.2.71

एवंविधानि यानीह स्थलजान्यौदकानि च ।
स्वेदजं दंशमशकं यूकालिक्षकमत्कुणाः ॥ ७१

Such creatures as these are born on land or in water. Biting insects, mosquitoes, lice, nits, and bedbugs are moisture-born.

BP 1.2.72

ऊष्मणा चोपजायन्ते यच्चान्यत्किञ्चिदीदृशम् ।
उद्भिज्जाः स्थावराः सर्वे बीजकाण्डप्ररोहिणः ॥ ७२

They and whatever else is like them arise from heat. All immovable beings are sprout-born, growing from seed and stalk.

BP 1.2.73

ओषध्यः फलपाकान्ता नानाविधफलोपगाः ।
अपुष्पाः फलवन्तो ये ते वनस्पतयः स्मृताः ॥ ७३

Herbs end with the ripening of their fruit and bear many kinds of fruit. Those that bear fruit without flowers are called forest trees.

BP 1.2.74

पुष्पिणः फलिनश्चैव वृक्षास्तूभयतः स्मृताः ।
गुच्छगुल्मं तु विविधं तथैव तृणजातयः ॥ ७४

Those that bear both flowers and fruit are called trees. There are many kinds of bushes and shrubs, and likewise the kinds of grasses.

BP 1.2.75

बीजकाण्डरुहाण्येव प्रताना वल्ल्य एव च ।
तमसा बहुरूपेण वेष्टिताः कर्महेतुना ॥ ७५

Plants growing from seeds and stalks, spreading plants, and creepers are enveloped in darkness of many forms through the cause of action.

BP 1.2.76

अन्तःसंज्ञा भवन्त्येते सुखदुःखसमन्विताः ।
एतावत्यस्तु गतयः प्रोद्भूताः कुरुनन्दन ॥ ७६

These beings have inward consciousness and are accompanied by pleasure and pain. Such are the courses of existence that have arisen, O delight of the Kurus.

BP 1.2.77

तस्माद्देवाद्दीप्तिमन्तो भास्कराच्य महात्मनः ।
घोरेऽस्मिंस्तात संसारे नित्यं सततयायिनि ॥ ७७

From that radiant god, the great-souled Bhāskara, they arose in this terrible cycle of existence that moves on without ceasing, my child.

BP 1.2.78

एवं सर्वं स सृष्ट्वेदं राजँल्लोकगुरुं परम् ।
तिरोभूतः स भूतात्मा कालं कालेन पीडयन् ॥ ७८

Having thus created all this, O king, the supreme teacher of the worlds, the Self of beings, became hidden, wearing down time by means of time.

BP 1.2.79

यदा स देवो जागर्ति तदेदं चेष्टते जगत् ।
यदा स्वपिति शान्तात्मा तदा सर्वं निमीलति ॥ ७९

When that god awakens, this world moves; when the tranquil one sleeps, everything closes in.

BP 1.2.80

तस्मिन्स्वपिति राजेन्द्र जन्तवः कर्मबन्धनाः ।
स्वकर्मभ्यो निवर्तन्ते मनश्च ग्लानिमृच्छति ॥.2.८०

When he sleeps, O king of kings, creatures bound by action cease from their actions, and the mind comes to exhaustion.

BP 1.2.81

युगपत्तु प्रलीयन्ते यदा तस्मिन्महात्मनि ।
तदायं सर्वभूतात्मा सुखं स्वपिति भारत ॥ ८१

When they all dissolve at once into that great Self, then the Self of all beings sleeps in ease, O descendant of Bharata.

BP 1.2.82

तमो यदा समाश्रित्य चिरं तिष्ठति सेन्द्रियः ।
न नवं कुरुते कर्म तदोत्क्रामति मूर्तितः ॥ ८२

When an embodied being, together with its senses, remains long in darkness, it makes no new action and then departs from its bodily form.

BP 1.2.83

यदाहंमात्रिको भूत्वा बीजं स्थास्नु चरिष्णु च ।
समाविशति संसृष्टस्तदा मूतिं विमुञ्चति ॥ ८३

When it becomes the I-principle and enters, united, the seed of what is stationary and what moves, then it leaves its form.

BP 1.2.84

एवं स जाग्रत्स्वप्नाभ्यामिदं सर्वं जगत्प्रभुः ।
संजीवयति चाजस्रं प्रमापयति चाव्ययः ॥ ८४

Thus the imperishable Lord continually brings this whole world to life through waking and dream, and brings it to an end.

BP 1.2.85

कल्पादौ सृजते तात अन्ते कल्पस्य संहरेत् ।
दिनं तस्येह यत्तात कल्पान्तमिति कथ्यते ॥ ८५

At the beginning of a cosmic cycle he creates, my child; at the end of the cycle he withdraws creation. His day here, my child, is called a cosmic cycle.

BP 1.2.86

कालसंख्यां ततस्तस्य कल्पस्य शृणु भारत ।
निमेषा दश चाष्टौ च अक्ष्णः काष्ठा निगद्यते ॥ ८६

Hear the reckoning of time for that cycle, O descendant of Bharata. Eighteen blinks of the eye are declared to make one small unit of time.

BP 1.2.87

त्रिंशत्काष्ठाः कलामाहुः क्षणस्त्रिंशत्कलाः स्मृताः ।
मुहूर्तमथ मौहूर्ता वदन्ति द्वादश क्षणम् ॥ ८७

Thirty small units are called one larger unit; thirty larger units are remembered as one moment. Those who measure time say twelve moments make one period.

BP 1.2.88

त्रिंशन्मुहूर्तमुद्दिष्टमहोरात्रं मनीषिभिः ।
मासस्त्रिंशदहोरात्रं द्वौ द्वौ मासावृतुः स्मृतः ॥ ८८

The wise declare thirty periods to be one day and night. Thirty days and nights make a month; every two months make a season.

BP 1.2.89

ऋतुत्रयमप्ययनमयने द्वे तु वत्सरः ।
अहोरात्रे विभजते सूर्यो मानुषदैविके ॥ ८९

Three seasons make one course of the sun, and two courses make a year. The sun divides day and night for humans and gods.

BP 1.2.90

रात्रिः स्वप्नाय भूतानां चेष्टायै कर्मणामहः ।
पित्र्ये रात्र्यहनी मासः प्रविभागस्तु पक्षयोः ॥.2.९०

For living beings, night is for sleep and day for action. For the ancestors, a month is a night and day, divided into two fortnights.

BP 1.2.91

कर्म चेष्टास्वहः कृष्णः मुक्तः स्वप्नाय शर्वरी ।
दैवे रात्र्यहनी वर्षं प्रविभागस्तयोः पुनः ॥ ९१

For the ancestors, the bright fortnight is day for action and the dark fortnight night for sleep. For the gods, a year is night and day, divided into two parts.

BP 1.2.92

अहस्तत्रोदगयनं रात्रिः स्याद्दक्षिणायनम् ।
ब्राह्मस्य तु क्षपाहस्य यत्प्रमाणं महीपते ॥ ९२

There the sun's northward course is day, and its southward course is night. Hear, O ruler of the earth, the measure of Brahmā's day and night.

BP 1.2.93

एकैकशो युगानां तु क्रमशस्तन्तिबोध मे ।
चत्वार्याहुः सहस्राणि वर्षाणां तत्कृतं युगम् ॥ ९३

Hear me speak in order of the ages, one by one. They declare four thousand years for the Age of Truth.

BP 1.2.94

तस्य तावच्छती सन्ध्या सन्ध्यांशश्च तथाविधः ।
त्रेता त्रीणि सहस्राणि वर्षाणि च विदुर्बुधाः ॥ ९४

Its dawn is four hundred years, and its twilight is likewise. The wise know that the Age of Three Parts lasts three thousand years.

BP 1.2.95

शतानि षट् च राजेन्द्र सन्ध्यासन्ध्यांशयोः पृथक् ।
वर्षाणां द्वे सहस्रे तु द्वापरे परिकीर्तिते ॥ ९५

The dawn and twilight of that age are six hundred years divided between them, O king of kings. The Age of Two Parts is declared to last two thousand years.

BP 1.2.96

चत्वारि च शतान्याहुः सन्ध्यासन्ध्यांशयोर्बुधः ।
सहस्रं कथितं तिष्ये शतद्वयसमन्वितम् ॥ ९६

The wise declare four hundred years for its dawn and twilight divided between them. In the Age of Strife, a thousand years are stated, along with two hundred more.

BP 1.2.97

एषा चतुर्युगस्यापि संख्या प्रोक्ता नृपोत्तम ।
यदेतत्परिसंख्या तमादावेव चतुर्युगम् ॥ ९७

This is declared to be the reckoning of the four ages, O best of kings. The total reckoning at the beginning is one complete cycle of four ages.

BP 1.2.98

एतद्द्वादशसाहस्रं देवानां युगमुच्यते ।
दैविकानां युगानां तु सहस्रपरिसंख्यया ॥ ९८

This total of twelve thousand is called an age of the gods. A thousand such divine ages are reckoned together.

BP 1.2.99

ब्राह्ममेकमहर्ज्ञेयं तावती रात्रिरुच्यते ।
एतद्युगसहस्रान्तं ब्राह्मं पुण्यमहर्विदुः ॥ ९९

That is to be known as one day of Brahmā, and an equal span is said to be his night. The wise know that his holy day ends after this thousandfold cycle of ages.

BP 1.2.100

रात्रिं च तावतीमेव तेऽहोरात्रविदो जनाः ।
ततोऽसौ युगपर्यन्ते प्रसुप्तः प्रतिबुध्यते ॥.2.१००

Those who know day and night know his night to be just as long. Then, at the end of the cycle of ages, he awakens from sleep.

BP 1.2.101

प्रतिबुद्धस्तु सृजति मनः सदसदात्मकम् ।
मनः सृष्टिं विकुरुते चोद्यमानं सिसृक्षया ॥०१

Awakened, he creates mind, whose nature is being and non-being. Impelled by the desire to create, mind transforms and brings forth creation.

BP 1.2.102

विपुलं जायते तस्मात्तस्य शब्दं गुणं विदुः ।
विपुलात्तु विकुर्वाणात्सर्वगन्धवहः शुचिः ॥०२

From it arises the vast expanse; sound is known as its quality. From the transforming vast expanse comes the pure wind that carries every scent.

BP 1.2.103

बलवाञ्जायते वायुः स वै स्पर्शगुणो मतः ।
वायोरपि विकुर्वाणाद्विरोचिष्णु तमोनुदम् ॥०३

The strong wind arises; touch is held to be its quality. From the transforming wind comes the shining one that dispels darkness.

BP 1.2.104

उत्पद्यते विचित्रांशुस्तस्य रूपं गुणं विदुः ।
तस्मादपि विकुर्वाणादापो जाताः स्मृता बुधैः ॥०४

Radiant fire with varied rays arises; form is known as its quality. From that too, as it transforms, the waters are born, as the wise declare.

BP 1.2.105

तासां गुणो रसो ज्ञेयः सर्वलोकस्य भावनः ।
अद्भ्यो गन्धगुणा भूमिरित्येषा सृष्टिरादितः ॥०५

Taste is to be known as the quality of the waters, which sustain the whole world. From the waters came earth, whose quality is smell. This is creation at the beginning.

BP 1.2.106

यत्प्राग्द्वादशसाहस्रमुक्तं सौमनसं युगम् ।
तदेकसप्ततिगुणं मन्वन्तरमिहोच्यते ॥०६

The harmonious cycle of twelve thousand years mentioned before, multiplied seventy-one times, is here called a reign of a Manu.

BP 1.2.107

मन्वन्तराण्यसंख्यानि सर्गः संहार एव च ।
तथाप्यहे सदा ब्राह्मे मनवस्तु चतुर्दश ॥०७

The reigns of Manus, creations, and dissolutions are without number. Yet in every day of Brahmā there are always fourteen Manus.

BP 1.2.108

कथ्यन्ते कुरुशार्दूल संख्यया पण्डितैः सदा ।
मनोः स्वायम्भुवस्येह षड्वंश्या मनवोऽपरे ॥०८

The learned always count them thus, O tiger among the Kurus. Six other Manus are spoken of here after Svāyambhuva Manu.

BP 1.2.109

सृष्टवन्तः प्रजाः स्वाःस्वाः महात्मानो महौजसः ।
सावर्णेयस्तथा पञ्चभौत्यो रौच्यस्तथापरः ॥०९

Those great-souled and mighty ones created their own respective beings. The future Manus also include Sāvarṇeya, Pāñcabhautya, and another named Raucya.

BP 1.2.110

एते भविष्या मनवः सप्त प्रोक्ता नृपोत्तम ।
स्वस्वेऽन्तरे सर्वमिदं पालयन्ति चराचरम् ॥.2.११०

These seven future Manus are declared, O best of kings. In their own intervals they protect all this, moving and unmoving.

BP 1.2.111

एवंविधं दिनं तस्य विरिञ्चेस्तु महात्मनः ।
तस्यान्ते कुरुते सर्गं यथेदं कथितं तव ॥११

Such is the day of the great-souled Viriñci. At its end he creates as I have related to you.

BP 1.2.112

क्रीडन्निवैतत्कुरुते परमेष्ठी नराधिप ।
चतुष्पात्सकलो धर्मः सत्यं चैव कृते युगे ॥१२

The Supreme Lord does all this as if playing, O ruler of men. In the Age of Truth, duty is complete, standing on four feet, and truth prevails.

BP 1.2.113

नाधर्मेणागमः कश्चिन्मनुष्याणां प्रवर्तते ।
इतरेष्वागमास्तात धर्मश्च कुरुनन्दन ॥१३

No undertaking among humans proceeds through wrongdoing. In the other ages, custom and duty change, my child, O delight of the Kurus.

BP 1.2.114

यादृशाः परिहीयन्ते यथाह भगवान्मनुः ।
चौर्याच्चाप्यनृताद्राजन्मायाभिरमितद्युते ॥१४

They decline in the manner the blessed Manu declared. Through theft, falsehood, and deceit, O radiant king, this decline occurs.

BP 1.2.115

पादेन हीयते धर्मस्त्रेतादिषु युगेषु वै ।
अरोगाः सर्वसिद्धार्थाश्चतुर्वर्षशतायुषः ॥१५

In the ages beginning with the Age of Three Parts, duty is diminished by one foot at a time. In the Age of Truth, people are free of illness, attain every aim, and live four hundred years.

BP 1.2.116

कृतत्रेतादिषु त्वेषां वयो ह्रसति पादशः ।
वेदोक्तमायुराशीश्च मर्त्यानां कुरुनन्दन ॥१६

In the Age of Truth, the Age of Three Parts, and those after them, their lifespan diminishes by a quarter at a time; so do the lifespan and blessings ordained by the Veda for mortals, O delight of the Kurus.

BP 1.2.117

कर्मणां तु फलं तात फलत्यनुयुगं सदा ।
प्रभावश्च तथा लोके फलत्येव शरीरिणाम् ॥१७

The fruit of actions, my child, always ripens according to the age; likewise, the power of embodied beings in the world bears fruit.

BP 1.2.118

अन्ये कृतयुगे धर्मास्त्रेतायां द्वापरे परे ।
अन्ये कलियुगे नृणां युगधर्मानुरूपतः ॥१८

Duties are one kind in the Age of Truth, another in the Age of Three Parts, another in the Age of Two Parts, and another in the Age of Strife, for people according to each age's duty.

BP 1.2.119

तपः परं कृतयुगे त्रेतायां ज्ञानमुच्यते ।
द्वापरे यज्ञमित्याहुर्दानमेकं कलौ युगे ॥१९

Austerity is supreme in the Age of Truth; in the Age of Three Parts, knowledge is declared supreme. In the Age of Two Parts they call sacrifice supreme; in the Age of Strife, giving alone.

BP 1.2.120

सर्वस्य राजन्सर्गस्य गुप्त्यर्थं च महाद्युते ।
मुखबाहूरुपादानां पृथक्कर्माण्यकल्पयत् ॥.2.१२०

For the protection of the whole creation, O radiant king, he assigned distinct duties to those born from the mouth, arms, thighs, and feet.

BP 1.2.121

अध्यापनमध्ययनं यजनं याजनं तथा ।
दानं प्रतिग्रहं चैव ब्राह्मणानामकल्पयत् ॥२१

For Brahmins he ordained teaching, studying, performing sacrifice, officiating at sacrifice, giving, and receiving gifts.

BP 1.2.122

प्रजानां पालनं राजन्दानमध्ययनं तथा ।
विषयेषु प्रसक्तिं च तथेज्यां क्षत्रियस्य तु ॥२२

For the Kshatriya he ordained protecting the people, giving, studying, engagement with his domains, and sacrifice, O king.

BP 1.2.123

पशूनां रक्षणं दानमिज्याध्ययनमेव च ।
वणिक्पथं कुसीदं च वैश्यस्य कृषिरेव च ॥२३

For the Vaishya he ordained tending cattle, giving, sacrifice, study, trade, lending at interest, and agriculture.

BP 1.2.124

एकमेव तु शूद्रस्य कर्म लोके प्रकीर्तितम् ।
एतेषामेव वर्णानां शुश्रूषामनुपूर्वशः ॥२४

Only one duty is proclaimed in the world for the Śūdra: service, in due order, to these other varṇas.

BP 1.2.125

पुरुषस्य सदा श्रेष्ठं नाभेरूर्ध्वं नृपोत्तम ।
तस्मादपि शुचितरं मुखं तात स्वयम्भुवः ॥२५

The part of a person above the navel is always superior, O best of kings; of that, the mouth fashioned by the Self-born is purer still, my child.

BP 1.2.126

तस्मान्मुखाद्द्विजो जात इतीयं वैदिकी श्रुतिः ।
सर्वस्यैवास्य धर्मस्य धर्मतो ब्राह्मणः प्रभुः ॥२६

Therefore the twice-born was born from the mouth: this is the Vedic revelation. By duty, the Brahmin is master of all this duty.

BP 1.2.127

स सृष्टो ब्रह्मणा पूर्वं तपस्तप्त्वा कुरूद्वह ।
हव्यानामिव कव्यानां सर्वस्यापि च गुप्तये ॥२७

Having performed austerity, Brahmā created him first, O bearer of the Kuru line, to guard the offerings to the gods and the ancestors, and to guard everything.

BP 1.2.128

अश्नन्ति च मुखेनास्य हव्यानि त्रिदिवौकसः ।
कव्यानि चैव पितरः किम्भूतमधिकं ततः ॥२८

Through his mouth the dwellers in heaven eat the offerings to the gods, and the ancestors eat the offerings to the ancestors. What being is greater than he?

BP 1.2.129

भूतानां प्राणिनः श्रेष्ठाः प्राणिनां बुद्धिजीविनः ।
बुद्धिमत्सु नराः श्रेष्ठा नरेषु ब्राह्मणाः स्मृताः ॥२९

Among beings, living creatures are best; among living creatures, those sustained by understanding; among those with understanding, humans; among humans, Brahmins are held best.

BP 1.2.130

ब्राह्मणेषु च विद्वांसो विद्वत्सु कृतबुद्धयः ।
कृतबुद्धिषु कर्तारः कर्तृषु ब्रह्मवेदिनः ॥.2.१३०

Among Brahmins, the learned; among the learned, those of firm understanding; among those of firm understanding, those who act; among those who act, the knowers of Brahman.

BP 1.2.131

जन्म विप्रस्य राजेन्द्र धमार्थमिह कथ्यते ।
उत्पन्नः सर्वसिद्धयर्थं४ याति ब्रह्मसदो नृप ॥३१

The birth of a learned Brahmin is declared here to be for the sake of duty, O king of kings. Born for the attainment of all accomplishments, he reaches Brahmā's assembly, O king.

BP 1.2.132

स चापि जायमानस्तु पृथिव्यामिह जायते ।
भूतानां प्रभवायैव धर्मकोशस्य गुप्तये ॥३२

When such a person is born here on earth, he is born for the arising of beings and for guarding the treasury of duty.

BP 1.2.133

सर्वं हि ब्राह्मणस्येदं यत्किञ्चित्पृथिवीगतम् ।
जन्मना चोत्तमेनेदं सर्वं वै ब्राह्मणोऽर्हति ॥३३

Whatever there is upon the earth belongs to the Brahmin. By his highest birth the Brahmin is entitled to it all.

BP 1.2.134

स्वकीयं ब्राह्मणो भुङ्क्ते विदधाति च सुव्रत ।
करुणां कुर्वतस्तस्य भुञ्जन्ती हेतरे जनाः ॥३४

A Brahmin consumes what is his own and distributes it, O keeper of good vows. Other people eat through his kindness.

BP 1.2.135

त्रयाणामिह वर्णानां भावाभावाय वै द्विजः ।
भवेद्राजन्न सन्देहस्तुष्टो भावाय वै द्विजः ॥३५

The twice-born here is the cause of the being and non-being of the other three varṇas, O king; there is no doubt. When pleased, he is the cause of their being.

BP 1.2.136

अभावाय भवेत्क्रुद्धस्तस्मात्पूज्यतमो हि सः ।
ब्राह्मणे सति नान्यस्य प्रभुत्वं विद्यते नृप । .।३६

When angered, he becomes the cause of their non-being; therefore he is most worthy of honour. When a Brahmin is present, no one else has authority, O king.

BP 1.2.137

कामात्करोत्यसौ कर्म कामगश्च नृपोत्तम ।
तस्माद्वृन्दारकपुरी तस्मादपि महः पुनः ॥३७

He acts according to his desire and goes where he wishes, O best of kings. From there he reaches the city of the gods, and then the world of Great.

BP 1.2.138

महर्लोकाज्जनोलोकं ब्रह्मलोकं च गच्छति ।
ब्रह्मत्वं च महाबाहो याति विप्रो न संशयः ॥३८

From the Great World he goes to the World of Progeny, and to Brahmā's world. A Brahmin attains the state of Brahman, O mighty-armed one; there is no doubt.

BP 1.2.139

शतानीक उवाच
ब्रह्मत्वं नाम दुष्प्रापं ब्रह्मलोकेषु सुव्रत ॥३९

Śatānīka said: The state of Brahman is difficult to attain, even in the worlds of Brahmā, O keeper of good vows.

BP 1.2.140

ब्रह्मत्वं कीदृशं विप्रो ब्रह्मलोकं च गच्छति ।
नाममात्रोऽथ किं विप्रो ब्रह्मत्वं ब्रह्मणः सदा ॥
याति ब्रह्मन्गुणाः के स्युर्ब्रह्मप्राप्तौ ममोच्यताम् ॥.2.१४०

What is the state of Brahman, and how does a Brahmin go to Brahmā's world? Is he a Brahmin by name alone, or does he always attain Brahman's state? Tell me, O Brahmin, what qualities lead to Brahman.

BP 1.2.141

सुमन्तुरुवाच
साधुसाधु महाबाहो शृणु मे परमं वचः ॥४१

Sumantu said: Well asked, well asked, O mighty-armed one. Hear my highest word.

BP 1.2.142

ये प्रोक्ता वेदशास्त्रेषु संस्कारा ब्राह्मणस्य तु ।
गर्भाधानादयो ये च संस्कारा यस्य पार्थिव ॥४२

The rites prescribed for a Brahmin in the Vedas and śāstras, those beginning with the conception rite, O ruler of the earth—

BP 1.2.143

चत्वारिंशतथाष्टौ च निर्वृत्ताः शास्त्रतो नृप ।
स याति ब्रह्मणः स्थानं ब्राह्मणत्वं च मानद ॥
संस्काराः सर्वथा हेतुर्ब्रह्मत्वे नात्र संशयः ॥४३

—when all forty-eight are completed according to śāstra, he reaches Brahmā's abode and the state of a Brahmin, O giver of honour. These rites are surely the cause of Brahmanhood; of this there is no doubt.

BP 1.2.144

शतानीक उवाच
संस्काराः के मता ब्रह्मन्ब्रह्मत्वे ब्राह्मणस्य तु ।
शंस मे द्विजशार्दूलं कौतुकं हि महन्मम ॥४४

Śatānīka said: Which rites are held to lead a Brahmin to Brahmanhood? Tell me, O tiger among the twice-born; my curiosity is great.

BP 1.2.145

सुमन्तुरुवाच
साधुसाधु महाबाहो शृणु मे परमं वचः ।
ये प्रोक्ता वेदशास्त्रेषु संस्कारा ब्राह्मणस्य तु ॥
मनीषिभिर्महाबाहो शृणु सर्वानशेषतः ॥४५

Sumantu said: Well asked, well asked, O mighty-armed one. Hear my highest word. Hear all the rites of a Brahmin prescribed in the Vedas and śāstras by the wise, without omission.

BP 1.2.146

गर्भाधानं पुंसवनं सीमन्तोन्नयनं तथा ।
जातकर्मान्नाशनं च चूडोपनयनं नृप ॥४६

The conception rite, the rite for a male child, the parting of a pregnant woman's hair, the birth rite, the first feeding, the tonsure, and initiation into Vedic study, O king.

BP 1.2.147

ब्रह्मव्रतानि चत्वारि स्नानं च तदनन्तरम् ।
सधर्मचारिणीयोगो यज्ञानां कर्म मानद ॥४७

The four vows of sacred study, the bath upon their completion, joining with a wife who shares one's duty, and the performance of the sacrifices, O giver of honour.

BP 1.2.148

पञ्चानां कार्यमित्याहुरात्मनः श्रेयसे नृप ।
देवपितृमनुष्याणां भूतानां ब्राह्मणस्तथा ॥४८

The five sacrifices are to be performed for one's own welfare, O king: to the gods, ancestors, human beings, living beings, and sacred learning.

BP 1.2.149

एतेषां चाष्टकाकर्म पार्वणश्राद्धमेव हि ।
श्रावणी चाग्रहायणी चैत्री चाश्वयुजी तथा ॥४९

The eighth-day rites, the ancestor offering at the new and full moons, and the seasonal rites for the rainy season, early winter, spring, and autumn.

BP 1.2.150

पाकयज्ञास्तथा सप्त अग्न्याधानं च सत्क्रिया ।
अग्निहोत्रं तथा राजन्दर्शं च विधुसञ्क्षये ॥.2.१५०

The seven household sacrifices, the establishment of the sacred fires with proper rites, the daily fire offering, and the new-moon offering, O king.

BP 1.2.151

पौर्णमासं च राजेन्द्र चातुर्मास्यानि चापि हि ।
निरूपणं पशुवधं तथा सौत्रामणीति च ॥५१

The full-moon offering, the four-month rites, the prescribed animal sacrifice, and the restorative rite, O king of kings.

BP 1.2.152

हविर्यज्ञास्तथा सप्त तेषां चापि हि सत्क्रिया ।
अग्निष्टोमोऽत्यग्निष्टोमस्तथोक्थ्यः षोडशीं विदुः ॥१५२

These are the seven sacrifices of oblations, each with its proper rite. The Praise of Agni, Greater Praise of Agni, Hymn Rite, and Sixteenfold are known.

BP 1.2.153

वाजपेयोऽतिरात्रश्च आप्तोर्यामेति वै स्मृतः ।
संस्कारेषु स्थिताः सप्त सोमाः कुरुकुलोद्वह ॥५३

The Strength-Drink, the Overnight rite, and the Completion rite are also known. These seven Soma sacrifices are included among the consecrations, O bearer of the Kuru line.

BP 1.2.154

इत्येते द्विजसंस्काराश्चत्वारिंशन्नृपोत्तम ।
अष्टौ चात्मगुणास्तात शृणु तानपि भारत ॥५४

These are the forty consecrations of a twice-born person, O best of kings. There are also eight qualities of the self; hear them too, my child, O descendant of Bharata.

BP 1.2.155

अनसूया दया क्षान्तिरनायासं च मङ्गलम् ।
अकार्पण्यं तथा शौचमस्पृहा च कुरूद्वह ॥५५

Freedom from envy, compassion, patience, freedom from strain, auspiciousness, generosity, purity, and lack of desire, O bearer of the Kuru line.

BP 1.2.156

य एतेऽष्टगुणास्तात कीर्त्यन्ते वै मनीषिभिः ।
एतेषां लक्षणं वीर शृणु सर्वमशेषतः ॥५६

These eight qualities are praised by the wise, my child. Hear all their marks without omission, O hero.

BP 1.2.157

न गुणान्गुणिनो हन्ति न स्तौत्यात्मगुणानपि ।
प्रहष्यन्ते नान्यदोषैरनसूया प्रकीर्तिता ॥५७

Not attacking the virtues of the virtuous, not praising one's own virtues, and not rejoicing at the faults of others is called freedom from envy.

BP 1.2.158

अपरे बन्धुवर्गे वा मित्रे द्वेष्टरि वा सदा ।
आत्मवद्वर्तनं यत्स्यात्सा दया परिकीर्तिता ॥५८

Acting toward other people, relatives, friends, or enemies as one would toward oneself is called compassion.

BP 1.2.159

वाचा मनसि काये च दुःखेनोत्पादितेन च ।
न कुप्यति न चाप्रीतिः सा क्षमा परिकीर्तिता ॥५९

When pain has been caused through words, mind, or body, not becoming angry or resentful is called patience.

BP 1.2.160

अभक्ष्यपरिहारश्च संसर्गश्चाप्यनिन्दितैः ।
आचारे च व्यवस्थानं शौचमेतत्प्रकीर्तितम् ॥.2.१६०

Avoiding forbidden food, associating with those who are not blameworthy, and abiding in proper conduct: this is called purity.

BP 1.2.161

शरीरं पीड्यते येन शुभेनापि च कर्मणा ।
अत्यन्तं तन्न कुर्वीत अनायासः स उच्यते ॥६१

One should not perform to excess even a good action that causes severe pain to the body. This is called freedom from strain.

BP 1.2.162

प्रशस्ताचरणं नित्यमप्रशस्तविवर्जनम् ।
एतद्धि मङ्गलं प्रोक्तं मुनिभिर्ब्रह्मवादिभिः ॥६२

Always following praiseworthy conduct and avoiding what is blameworthy: this is declared auspiciousness by sages who speak of Brahman.

BP 1.2.163

स्तोकादपि प्रदातव्यमदीनेनान्तरात्मना ।
अहन्यहनि यत्किंचिदकार्पण्यं तदुच्यते ॥६३

Something should be given every day, even if little, with an inward spirit free of meanness. This is called generosity.

BP 1.2.164

यथोत्पन्नेन सन्तुष्टः स्वल्पेनाप्यथ वस्तुना ।
अहिंसया परस्वेषु साऽस्पृहा परिकीर्तिता ॥६४

Being content with whatever comes, even if it is little, and not harming what belongs to another: this is called lack of desire.

BP 1.2.165

वपुर्यस्य तु इत्येतैः संस्कारैः संस्कृतं द्विजः ।
ब्रह्मत्वमिह सम्प्राप्य ब्रह्मलोकं च गच्छति ॥६५

A twice-born person whose body is purified by these rites attains Brahmanhood here and then goes to Brahmā's world.

BP 1.2.166

वैदिकैः कर्मभिः पुण्यैर्निषेकाद्यैर्द्विजन्मनाम् ।
कार्यः शरीरसंस्कारः पावनः प्रेत्य चेह च ॥६६

For the twice-born, bodily purification should be performed through meritorious Vedic rites beginning with conception; it purifies both here and after death.

BP 1.2.167

गर्भशुद्धिं ततः प्राप्य धर्मं चाश्रमलक्षणम् ।
याति मुक्तिं न सन्देहः पुराणेऽस्मिन्नृपोत्तम ॥६७

Having obtained purity from the womb onward and the duty marked by the stages of life, one attains release; of this there is no doubt in this Purāṇa, O best of kings.

BP 1.2.168

उशन्ति कुरुशार्दूल ब्राह्मणा नात्र संशयः ।
१आश्रितानां विशेषेण ये नित्यं स्वस्तिवादिनः ॥६८

Those who depend on Brahmins especially desire those who always speak blessings, O tiger among the Kurus; of this there is no doubt.

BP 1.2.169

निकटस्थान्द्विजान्हित्वा योऽन्यान्पूजयति द्विजान् ।
सिद्धं पापं तदपमानात्तद्वक्तुं नैव शक्यते ॥६९

Whoever passes over nearby twice-born persons and honours other twice-born persons instead incurs sin through that disrespect; it cannot be described.

BP 1.2.170

तस्मात्सदा समीपस्थः सम्पूज्यो विधिवन्नृप ।
पूजयेदतिथींस्तद्वदन्नपानादिदानतः ॥.2.१७०

Therefore one who lives nearby should always be honoured according to rule, O king. Guests too should be honoured by giving food, drink, and the like.

BP 1.2.171

ब्राह्मणः सर्ववर्णानां ज्येष्ठः श्रेष्ठस्तथोत्तमः ।
एवमस्मिन्पुराणे तु संस्कारान्ब्राह्मणस्य तु ॥७१

The Brahmin is eldest, best, and foremost among all varṇas. These are the Brahmin's rites in this Purāṇa.

BP 1.2.172

शृणोति यश्च जानाति यश्चापि पठते सदा ।
ऋद्धिं वृद्धिं तथा कीर्तिं प्राप्येह श्रियमुत्तमाम् ॥७२

Whoever hears these rites, knows them, or continually recites them gains prosperity, increase, fame, and the highest good fortune here.

BP 1.2.173

धनं धान्यं यशश्चापि पुत्रान्बन्धून्सुरूपताम् ।
सावित्रं लोकमासाद्य ब्रह्मलोकमवाप्नुयात् ॥७३

May he gain wealth, grain, fame, children, relatives, and beauty; having reached the world of the Sun, may he attain Brahmā's world.
