Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 68 — The Worship of the Sun and the Fruits of Offerings

BP 1.68.1

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
वच्मि ते परमं देवं सर्वदेवैश्च पूजितम् ।
आराधयन्ति यं देवं ब्रह्मविष्णुमहेश्वराः ॥ ॥

Brahmā said. I shall tell you of the supreme god, worshipped by all the gods, whom Brahmā, Viṣṇu, and Maheśvara worship.

BP 1.68.2

पद्माकृतिं सदा ब्रह्मा नलिनैर्गुग्गुलेन तु ।
व्योमरूपं सदा देवं महादेवोर्चते रविम् ॥ ॥

Brahmā always worships the lotus-formed Sun with lotuses and guggul resin. Mahādeva always worships Ravi, the god whose form is the sky.

BP 1.68.3

जातीपुष्पैर्द्विजश्रेष्ठ धूपेन विजयेण तु ।
वृषणं शिल्हकं विप्र श्रीखण्डमगरुस्तथा ॥ ॥

Jasmine flowers and Vijaya incense are used, O best of the twice-born. Vṛṣaṇa, benzoin, sandalwood, and agarwood are used too, O Brahmin.

BP 1.68.4

कर्पूरं च तथा मुस्ता शर्करा सत्वचा द्विज ।
इत्येष विजयो धूपः स्वयं देवेन निर्मितः ॥ ॥

Camphor, nutgrass, sugar, and bark are also its ingredients, O twice-born. This Vijaya incense was made by the god himself.

BP 1.68.5

केशवश्चक्ररूपं तु सदा सम्पूजयेद्रविम् ।
नीलोत्पलदलश्यामो नीलोत्पलकदम्बकैः ॥ ॥

Keśava should always worship the Sun, who is disc-shaped and dark as a blue-lotus petal, with clusters of blue lotuses.

BP 1.68.6

धूपेनागुरुसंज्ञेन भक्तिश्रद्धासमन्वितः ।
मया स पृष्टो देवेशस्तस्यैवाराधनाय वै ॥ ॥

I, endowed with devotion and faith, asked the Lord of the gods about that very worship, using agarwood incense.

BP 1.68.7

कानि पुष्पाणि चेष्टानि सदा भास्करपूजने ।
तेन चोक्तानि पुष्पाणि स्वयं तानि निबोध मे ॥ ॥

Which flowers are always desired in the worship of the Sun? He himself told me those flowers. Hear them from me.

BP 1.68.8

मल्लिकायास्तु कुसुमैर्भोगवान्जायते नरः ।
सौभाग्यं पुण्डरीकैश्च भजत्येव च शाश्वतम् ॥ ॥

By jasmine flowers, a person becomes rich in enjoyments. By white lotuses, one continually enjoys lasting good fortune.

BP 1.68.9

गन्धकुटजकैः पुष्पैः परमैश्वर्यमश्नुते ।
भवत्यक्षयमत्यन्तं नित्यमर्चयतो रविम् ॥ ॥

By fragrant kurchi flowers, one obtains supreme sovereignty. For the person who constantly worships Ravi, it becomes wholly imperishable.

BP 1.68.10

मन्दारपुष्पैः पूजा तु सर्वकुष्ठविनाशिनी ।
बिल्वपत्रैश्च कुसुमैर्महतीं श्रियमश्नुते ॥ ॥

Worship with coral-tree flowers destroys all leprosy. With bael leaves and flowers, one obtains great prosperity.

BP 1.68.11

अर्कस्रजा भवत्यर्थं सर्वकामफलप्रदः ।
प्रदद्याद्रूपिणीं कन्यामर्चितो बकुलस्रजा ॥ ॥

A garland of crown flowers brings wealth and grants the fruit of every desire. Worshipped with a garland of Spanish cherry flowers, the Sun gives a beautiful maiden.

BP 1.68.12

किंशुकैरर्चितो देवो न पीडयति भास्करः ।
पूजितोऽगस्त्यकुसुमैरानुकूल्यं प्रयच्छति ॥ ॥

Worshipped with flame-of-the-forest flowers, the Sun does not afflict. Worshipped with agathi flowers, he gives favor.

BP 1.68.13

करवीरैस्तु विप्रेन्द्र सूर्यस्यानुचरो भवेत् ।
तथा मुद्गरपुष्पैश्च समभ्यर्च्य दिवाकरम् ॥ ॥

With oleanders, O lord of the twice-born, one becomes an attendant of the Sun. One should likewise worship the Sun with jasmine flowers.

BP 1.68.14

हंसयुक्तेन यानेन रवेः सालोक्यतां व्रजेत् ।
शतपुष्पसहस्रैस्तु पूषसालोक्यतां व्रजेत् ।
बकपुष्पैर्द्विजश्रेष्ठ याति भानुसलोकताम् ॥ ॥

With a vehicle yoked to swans, one reaches the world of Ravi. With a thousand dill flowers, one reaches the world of Pūṣan. With Baka flowers, O best of the twice-born, one goes to the world of Bhānu.

BP 1.68.15

चतुःसमेन गन्धेन समभ्यर्च्य दिवाकरम् ।
पञ्चभूतालयस्थानमाप्नुयात्र न संशयः ॥ ॥

Having worshipped the Sun with fourfold fragrance, one reaches the station of the five elements. There is no doubt.

BP 1.68.16

देवागारं तु सम्मार्ज्य भक्त्या यस्तु प्रलेपयेत् ।
स रोगान्मुच्यते क्षिप्रं द्रव्यलाभं च विन्दति ॥ ॥

Whoever lovingly sweeps and plasters the temple is quickly freed from disease and obtains wealth.

BP 1.68.17

तस्य चायतनं भक्त्या गैरिकेणोपलेपयेत् ।
प्राप्नुयाद्महतीं लक्ष्मीं रोगैश्चापि प्रमुच्यते ॥ ॥

One should lovingly coat that shrine with red ochre. One obtains great prosperity and is also freed from disease.

BP 1.68.18

अष्टादशेह कुष्ठानि ये चान्ये व्याधयो नृणाम् ।
प्रलयं यान्ति ते सर्वे मृदा यद्युपलेपयेत् ॥ ॥

The eighteen kinds of leprosy and all other diseases of people perish if one plasters with earth.

BP 1.68.19

विलेपनानां सर्वेषां रक्तचन्दनमुत्तमम् ।
पुष्पाणां करवीराणि प्रशस्तानि प्रचक्षते ॥ ॥

Among all ointments, red sandalwood is best. Among flowers, oleanders are declared excellent.

BP 1.68.20

नातः परतरं किञ्चिद्भास्वतस्तुष्टिकारकम् ।
किं तस्य न भवेल्लोके यस्त्वेभिः स्वर्चयेद्रविम् ॥ ॥

There is nothing higher than this for pleasing the radiant Sun. What would such a person not have in this world, if he worships Ravi well with these?

BP 1.68.21

करवीरैः पूजयेद्यो भास्करं श्रद्धयान्वितः ।
सर्वकामसमृद्धोऽसौ सूर्यकाममवाप्नुयात् ॥ ॥

Whoever worships Bhāskara with faith and oleanders becomes prosperous in every desire and obtains the desire of the Sun.

BP 1.68.22

विलेप्यायतनं यस्तु कुर्यान्मण्डलकं शुभम् ।
स सूर्यलोकमासाद्य मोदते शाश्वतीः समाः ॥ ॥

Whoever plasters the shrine and makes an auspicious circle reaches the world of the Sun and rejoices for everlasting years.

BP 1.68.23

एकेनास्य भवेदर्थो द्वाभ्यामारोग्यमश्नुते ।
त्रिभिः सन्तत्यविच्छिन्ना चतुर्भिर्भार्गवीं लभेत् ॥ ॥

By one circle he obtains wealth. By two he obtains health. By three he obtains unbroken progeny. By four he obtains Bhārgavī.

BP 1.68.24

पञ्चभिर्विपुलं धान्यं षड्भिरायुर्बलं यशः ।
सप्तमण्डलकारी स्यान्मण्डलाधिपतिर्नरः ॥ ॥

By five he obtains abundant grain. By six, long life, strength, and fame. A man who makes seven circles becomes lord of circles.

BP 1.68.25

आयुर्धनसुतैर्युक्तः सूर्यलोके महीयते ।
घृतप्रदीपदानेन चक्षुष्माञ्जायते नरः ॥ ॥

Endowed with long life, wealth, and sons, he is honored in the world of the Sun. By giving a ghee lamp, a person becomes one who has sight.

BP 1.68.26

कटुतैलप्रदानेन स शत्रूञ्जयते नरः ।
तिलतैलप्रदानेन सूर्यलोके महीयते ॥ ॥

By giving pungent oil, a person conquers enemies. By giving sesame oil, he is honored in the world of the Sun.

BP 1.68.27

मधूकतैलदानेन सौभाग्यं परमं व्रजेत् ।
सम्पूज्य विधिवद्देवं पुष्पधूपादिभिर्बुधः ॥ ॥

By giving mahua oil, one reaches supreme good fortune. The wise man should duly worship the god with flowers, incense, and the like.

BP 1.68.28

यथाशक्त्या ततः पश्चान्नैवेद्यं भक्तितो न्यसेत् ।
पुष्पाणां प्रवरा जाती धूपानां विजयः परः ॥ ॥

Afterward, according to his ability, he should place the offering with devotion. Jasmine is foremost among flowers, and Vijaya is highest among incenses.

BP 1.68.29

गन्धानां कुङ्कुमं श्रेष्ठं लेपानां रक्तचन्दनम् ।
दीपदाने घृतं श्रेष्ठं नैवेद्ये मोदकः परः ॥ ॥

Saffron is best among fragrances, red sandalwood among ointments. Ghee is best for lamp offerings, and modaka is supreme among food offerings.

BP 1.68.30

एतैस्तुष्यति देवेशः सान्निध्यं चाधिगच्छति । एवं संपूज्य विधिवत्कृत्वा चापि प्रदक्षिणम् ॥ ३० ॥

With these the Lord of the gods is pleased, and the worshipper attains his presence. Having worshipped thus according to rule, one should also perform circumambulation.

BP 1.68.31

प्रणम्य शिरसा देवं देवदेवं दिवाकरम् । सुखासीनस्ततः पश्येद्रवेरभिमुखे स्थितः ॥ ३१ ॥

Having bowed his head to Divākara, the god of gods, one should then sit comfortably facing the Sun and look.

BP 1.68.32

एकं सिद्धार्थकं कृत्वा हस्ते पानीयसंयुतम् । कामं यथेष्टं हृदये कृत्वा तं वाञ्छितं नरः ॥ ३२ ॥

Having placed one mustard seed with water in the hand, a person should place the desired wish in the heart.

BP 1.68.33

पिबेत्सतोयं तं विप्र अस्पृष्टं दशनैः सकृत् । द्वितीयायां तु सप्तम्यां द्वौ गृहीत्वा तु सुव्रत ॥ ३३ ॥

He should drink it with water once, without touching it with his teeth, O Brahmin. On the second seventh lunar day, O good-vowed one, he should take two.

BP 1.68.34

तृतीयायां तु सप्तम्यां ग्रहीतव्यास्त्रयोऽपि च । ज्ञेयाश्चतुर्थ्यां चत्वारः पञ्चम्यां पञ्च एव हि ॥ ३४ ॥

On the third seventh lunar day, three are to be taken. Four are to be known on the fourth, and five indeed on the fifth.

BP 1.68.35

षट् पिबेच्चापि षष्ठ्यां तु इतीयं वैदिकी श्रुतिः । सप्तम्यां सप्तमायां तु सप्त चैव पिबेन्नरः ॥ ३५ ॥

He should drink six on the sixth. This is the Vedic revelation. On the seventh seventh lunar day, a man should drink seven.

BP 1.68.36

आदौ प्रभृति विज्ञेयो मन्त्रोऽयमभिमन्त्रणे । सिद्धार्थकस्त्वं हि लोके सर्वत्र श्रूयसे यथा । तथा मामपि सिद्धार्थमर्थतः कुरुतां रविः ॥ ३६ ॥

At the beginning this mantra should be known for consecration. Mustard seed, you are heard everywhere in the world as successful. May the Sun make me successful in my aim too.

BP 1.68.37

ततो हविरुपस्पृश्य जपं कुर्याद्यथेप्सितम् । हुताशनं च जुहुयाद्विधिदृष्टेन कर्मणा ॥ ३७ ॥

Then, having touched the offering, one should recite as desired. One should also offer into the fire according to the prescribed rite.

BP 1.68.38

एवमेव पराः कार्या सप्तम्यः सप्त सर्वदा । एकात्प्रभृति कार्या सा सर्वदोदकसप्तमी ॥ ३८ ॥

The seven following seventh-day rites should always be performed in this way. That water rite on the seventh day is always performed beginning with one.

BP 1.68.39

एकं तोयेन सहितं द्वौ चापि घृतसंयुतौ । त्रींस्तथा मधुना सार्धं दध्ना चतुर एव च ॥ ३९ ॥

One is accompanied by water, two by ghee, three by honey, and four by curds.

BP 1.68.40

युक्तान्नपयसा पञ्च षट् च गोमयसंयुतान् । पञ्चगव्येन वै सप्त पिबेत्सिद्धार्थकान्द्विज ॥ ४० ॥

Five are mixed with food and milk, six with cow dung, and seven with the five cow products. O Brahmin, the mustard seeds should be drunk.

BP 1.68.41

अनेन विधिना यस्तु कुर्यात्सर्षपसप्तमीम् । बहुपुत्रो बहुधनः सिद्धार्थश्चापि सर्वदा ॥ ४१ ॥

Whoever performs the mustard-seed seventh-day rite by this method has many sons, much wealth, and success at all times.

BP 1.68.42

इह लोके नरो विप्र प्रेत्ययाति विभावसुम् । तस्मात्सम्पूजयेद्देवं विधिनानेन भास्करम् ॥ ४२ ॥

In this world, O Brahmin, a person goes to the Sun after death. Therefore one should worship the Sun according to this method.
