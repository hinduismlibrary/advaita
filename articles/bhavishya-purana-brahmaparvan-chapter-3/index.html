Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 3: Saṃskāras and Daily Rites

BP 1.3.1

शतानीक उवाच
जातकर्मादिसंस्कारान्वर्णानामनुपूर्वशः ।
आश्रमाणां च मे धर्मं कथयस्व द्विजोत्तम ॥ १

Śatānīka said: Tell me in due order the saṃskāras beginning with jāta-karma for the varṇas, and the dharma of the āśramas, O best of the twice-born.

BP 1.3.2

सुमन्तुरुवाच
गर्भाधानं पुंसवनं सीमन्तोन्नयनं तथा ।
जातकर्मान्नप्राशश्च चूडामौञ्जीनिबन्धनम् ॥ २

Sumantu said: Garbhādhāna, puṃsavana, sīmantonnayana, jāta-karma, annaprāśana, cūḍā and mauñjī-bandhana.

BP 1.3.3

बैजिकं गार्भिकं चैनो द्विजानामपमृज्यते ।
स्वाध्यायेन व्रतैर्होमैस्त्रैविद्येनेज्यया श्रुतैः ॥ ३

The faults born of seed and womb are removed from the twice-born through svādhyāya, vows, homas, the three Vedas, yajña and Vedic learning.

BP 1.3.4

महायज्ञैश्च ब्राह्मीयं यज्ञैश्च क्रियते तनुः ।
शृणुष्वैकमना राजन्यथा सा क्रियते तनुः ॥ ४

‘Through the great sacrifices and other rites, the body is made fit for brahman. Listen with one-pointed attention, O king, to how that body is thus prepared.’

BP 1.3.5

प्राङ्नाभिकर्तनात्पुंसो जातकर्म विधीयते ।
मन्त्रवत्प्राशनं चास्य हिरण्यमधुसर्पिषाम् ॥ ५

‘Before the cutting of a male infant’s umbilical cord, the birth ceremony is prescribed. With mantras, he is made to taste gold, honey, and clarified butter.’

BP 1.3.6

नामधेयं दशम्यां तु केचिदिच्छन्ति पार्थिव ।
द्वादश्यामपरे राजन्मासि पूर्णे तथा परे ॥ ६

‘Some prescribe the naming ceremony on the tenth day, O ruler of the earth; others on the twelfth day, O king, and still others after a full month.’

BP 1.3.7

अष्टादशेऽहनि तथाऽन्ये वदन्ति मनीषिणः ।
पुण्ये तिथौ मुहूर्ते च नक्षत्रे च गुणान्विते ॥ ७

‘Other wise teachers say it should occur on the eighteenth day, at an auspicious lunar date and moment and under a favourable constellation.’

BP 1.3.8

मङ्गल्यं तात विप्रस्य शिवशर्मेति पार्थिव ।
राजन्यस्य विशिष्टं तु इन्दुवर्मेति कथ्यते ॥। ८ ॥

‘The brāhmaṇa’s name should be auspicious, my child—such as Śivaśarman, O ruler of the earth. A ruler’s name should convey distinction, such as Induvarman.’

BP 1.3.9

वैश्यस्य धनसंयुक्तं शूद्रस्य च जुगुप्सितम् ।
धनवर्धनेति वैश्यस्य सर्वदासेति हीनजे ॥ ९

A vaiśya’s name should be connected with wealth, and a śūdra’s with lowliness: Dhanavardhana for a vaiśya and Sarvadāsa for one born low.

BP 1.3.10

मनुना च तथा प्रोक्तं नाम्नो लक्षणमुत्तमम् ।
शर्मवद्ब्राह्मणस्य स्याद्राज्ञो रक्षासमन्वितम् ॥.3.१०

‘Manu likewise declared the proper characteristics of names: a brāhmaṇa’s should be connected with śarman, while a king’s should be associated with protection.’

BP 1.3.11

वैश्यस्य पुष्टिसंयुक्तं शूद्रस्य प्रेष्यसंयुतम् ।
स्त्रीणां सुखोद्यमक्रूरं विस्पष्टार्थमनोरमम् ॥१

‘A vaiśya’s name should suggest prosperity, and a śūdra’s service. Women’s names should be easy to pronounce, not harsh, clear in meaning, and pleasing.’

BP 1.3.12

मङ्गल्यं दीर्घवर्णान्तमाशीर्वादाभिधानवत् ।
द्वादशेऽहनि राजेन्द्र शिशोर्निष्कमणं गृहात् ॥२

‘A woman’s name should be auspicious, end in a long vowel, and carry the sense of a blessing. The infant’s first departure from the house may be performed on the twelfth day, O king of kings.’

BP 1.3.13

चतुर्थे मासि कर्त्तव्यं तथान्येषां मतं विभो ।
षष्ठेऽन्नप्राशनं मासि यथेष्टं मङ्गलं कुले ॥३

‘According to others, O lord, that rite should be performed in the fourth month. The first feeding of solid food is performed in the sixth month, with whatever auspicious family custom is preferred.’

BP 1.3.14

चूडाकर्म द्विजातीनां सर्वेषामनुपूर्वशः ।
प्रथमेऽब्दे तृतीये वा कर्तव्यं कुरुनन्दन ॥४

‘The tonsure ceremony for all the twice-born classes, each in due order, should be performed in the first or the third year, O delight of the Kurus.’

BP 1.3.15

गर्भाष्टमेऽब्दे कुर्वीत ब्राह्मणस्योपनायनम् ।
गर्भादेकादशे राजन्क्षत्रियस्य विनिर्दिशेत् ॥५

‘A brāhmaṇa’s initiation should be performed in the eighth year from conception; that of a kṣatriya is prescribed in the eleventh year from conception, O king.’

BP 1.3.16

द्वादशेऽब्देऽपि गर्भात्तु वैश्यस्य व्रतमादिशेत् ।
ब्रह्मवर्चसकामेन कार्यं विप्रस्य पञ्चमे ॥६

‘The vow of initiation for a vaiśya is prescribed in the twelfth year from conception. For a brāhmaṇa who desires spiritual brilliance it should be performed in the fifth year.’

BP 1.3.17

बलार्थिना तथा राज्ञः षष्ठे ऽब्दे कार्यमेव हि ।
अर्थकामेन वैश्यस्य अष्टमे कुरुनन्दन ॥७

‘For a ruler who desires strength it should indeed be performed in the sixth year, and for a vaiśya who desires wealth in the eighth, O delight of the Kurus.’

BP 1.3.18

आषोडशाद्ब्राह्मणस्य सावित्री नातिवर्तते ।
द्वाविंशतेः क्षत्रबन्धोराचतुर्विंशतेर्विशः ॥८

‘The Sāvitrī initiation must not be delayed beyond the sixteenth year for a brāhmaṇa, the twenty-second for one of the warrior class, or the twenty-fourth for a vaiśya.’

BP 1.3.19

अत ऊर्ध्वं तु ये राजन्यथाकालमसंस्कृताः ।
सावित्रीपतिता व्रात्या व्रात्यस्तोमादृते क्रतोः ॥९

‘Those who remain unconsecrated beyond the proper time, O king, have fallen from the Sāvitrī and become vrātyas unless they perform the Vrātyastoma sacrifice.’

BP 1.3.20

न चाप्येभिरपूतैस्तु आपद्यपि हि कर्हिचित् ।
ब्राह्मं यौनं च सम्बन्धमाचरेद्ब्राह्मणैः सह ॥.3.२०

‘Even in adversity, brāhmaṇas should never establish a relationship of sacred learning or marriage with these persons while they remain unpurified.’

BP 1.3.21

भवन्ति राजंश्चर्माणि व्रतिनां त्रिविधानि च ।
कार्ष्णरौरववास्तानि ब्रह्मक्षत्रविशां नृप ॥१

‘The skins worn by initiated students are of three kinds, O king: those called Kārṣṇa, Raurava, and Vāsta respectively for the brāhmaṇa, kṣatriya, and vaiśya.’

BP 1.3.22

वशीरंश्चानुपूर्व्येण वस्त्राणि विविधानि तु ।
ब्रह्मक्षत्रविशो राजञ्छाणक्षौमादिकानि च ॥२

‘Their garments likewise differ in proper sequence: for the brāhmaṇa, kṣatriya, and vaiśya they are made respectively of such materials as hemp, linen, and wool, O king.’

BP 1.3.23

मौञ्जी त्रिवृत्समा श्लक्ष्णा कार्या विप्रस्य मेखला ।
क्षत्रियस्य च मौर्वीज्या वैश्यस्य शणतान्तवी ॥३

‘A brāhmaṇa’s girdle should be a smooth, even, three-stranded cord of muñja grass. A kṣatriya’s is made from murvā fibre, and a vaiśya’s from hemp thread.’

BP 1.3.24

मुञ्जालाभे तु कर्तव्या कुशाश्मन्तकबल्वजैः ।
त्रिवृत्ता ग्रन्थिनैकेन त्रिभिः पञ्चभिरेव च ॥४

‘When muñja is unavailable, the girdle may be made from kuśa, aśmantaka, or balvaja fibres, twisted into three strands and furnished with one, three, or five knots.’

BP 1.3.25

कार्पासमुपवीतं स्याद्विप्रस्योर्ध्ववृतं त्रिवृत् ।
शणसूत्रमयं राज्ञो वैश्यस्याविकसौत्रिकम् ॥५

‘A brāhmaṇa’s sacred thread should be of cotton, twisted upward and made in three strands; a ruler’s should be of hemp thread, and a vaiśya’s of woollen yarn.’

BP 1.3.26

पुष्कराणि तथा चैषां भवन्ति त्रिविधानि तु ।
ब्रह्मणो बैल्वपालाशौ तृतीयं प्लक्षजं नृप ॥६

‘The woods for their staffs are likewise of three groups. A brāhmaṇa may use bilva or palāśa, and plakṣa as the third, O king.’

BP 1.3.27

वाटखादिरौ क्षत्रियस्तु तथान्यं वेतसोद्भवम् ।
पैलवोदुम्बरौ वैश्यस्तथाश्वत्थजमेव हि ॥७

‘A kṣatriya may carry one made from vaṭa or khadira, or another produced from vetasa. A vaiśya may use pīlu or udumbara, and likewise one made from aśvattha.’

BP 1.3.28

दण्डानेतान्महाबाहो धर्मतोऽर्हन्ति धारितुम् ।
केशान्तिको ब्राह्मणस्य दण्डः कार्यः प्रमाणतः ॥८

‘These are the staffs that they are entitled to carry according to dharma, O mighty-armed king. The prescribed length of a brāhmaṇa’s staff reaches to the hairline.’

BP 1.3.29

ललाटसम्मितो राज्ञः स्यात्तु नासान्तिको विशः ।
ऋजवस्ते तू सर्वे स्युर्ब्राह्मणाः सौम्यदर्शनाः ॥९

‘A ruler’s staff should reach the forehead and a vaiśya’s the tip of the nose. All the staffs should be straight and pleasing in appearance.’

BP 1.3.30

अनुद्वेगकरा नॄणां सत्वचो नाग्निदूषिताः ।
प्रगृह्य चेप्सितं दण्डमुपस्थाय च भास्करम् ॥.3.३०

‘They should not alarm people, should retain their bark, and should not be damaged by fire. Taking up the desired staff and presenting oneself before Bhāskara—’

BP 1.3.31

सम्यग्गुरुं तथा पूज्य चरेद्भैक्ष्यं यथाविधि ।
भवत्पूर्वं चरेद्भैक्ष्यमुपनीतो द्विजोत्तमः ॥१

‘—and having duly honoured the teacher, the initiated student should beg for alms according to rule. A brāhmaṇa student should begin his request with the word bhavat.’

BP 1.3.32

भवन्मध्यं तु राजन्यो वैश्यस्य भवदुत्तरम् ।
मातरं वा स्वसारं वा मातुर्वा भगिनीं निजाम् ॥२

‘A kṣatriya should place bhavat in the middle of the request, while a vaiśya places it at the end. He should first approach his mother, his own sister, or his mother’s sister—’

BP 1.3.33

भिक्षेत भैक्ष्यं प्रथमं या चैनं नावमानयेत् ।
सुवर्णं रजतं चान्नं सा पात्रेऽस्य विनिर्दिशेत् ॥३

‘—and beg the first alms from a woman who will not treat him with contempt. She should place gold, silver, or food into his bowl as prescribed.’

BP 1.3.34

समाहृत्य ततो भैक्ष्यं यावदर्थममायया ।
निवेद्य गुरवेऽश्नीयादाचम्य प्राङ्मुखः शुचिः ॥४

‘After gathering without deceit as much alms as is needed, he should present it to his teacher. Having sipped water and become pure, he may eat while facing east.’

BP 1.3.35

आयुष्यं प्राङ्मुखो भुङ्क्ते यशस्यं दक्षिणामुखः ।
श्रियं प्रत्यङ्मुखो भुङ्क्ते ऋतं भुङ्क्ते उदङ्मुखः ॥५

‘Eating while facing east promotes longevity; facing south, fame; facing west, prosperity; and eating while facing north promotes truth.’

BP 1.3.36

उपस्पृश्य द्विजो राजन्नन्नमद्यात्समाहितः ।
भुक्त्वा चोपस्पृशेत्सम्यगद्भिः खानि च संस्पृशेत् ॥६

‘After touching water, O king, a twice-born person should eat with a collected mind. Having eaten, he should rinse properly and touch the openings of his senses with water.’

BP 1.3.37

तथान्नं पूजयेन्नित्यमद्याच्चैतदकुत्सयन् ।
दर्शनात्तस्य हष्येद्वै प्रसीदेच्चापि भारत॥७

‘One should always honour food and eat it without reviling it. At the sight of food one should rejoice and become gracious, O descendant of Bharata.’

BP 1.3.38

अभिनन्द्य ततोऽश्नीयादित्येवं मनुरब्रवीत् ।
पूजितं त्वशनं नित्यं बलमोजश्च यच्छति॥८

‘One should praise the food and then eat—thus Manu declared. Food that is continually honoured bestows strength and vigour.’

BP 1.3.39

अपूजितं तु तद्भुक्तमुभयं नाशयेदिदम्।
नोच्छिष्टं कस्यचिद्दद्यान्नाद्याच्चैतत्तथान्तरा ॥९

‘Food eaten without honour destroys both strength and vigour. One should not give one’s leftovers to anyone, nor should one eat between the appointed meals.’

BP 1.3.40

यस्त्वन्नमन्तरा कृत्वा लोभादत्ति नृपोत्तम ।
विनाशं याति स नर इह लोके परत्र च ॥
यथाभवत्पुरा वैश्यो धनवर्द्धनसंज्ञितः ॥.3.४०

‘A person who, driven by greed, eats between meals meets ruin both in this world and the next, O best of kings, just as formerly happened to the vaiśya named Dhanavardhana.’

BP 1.3.41

शतानीक उवाच
स कथमन्तरं पूर्वमन्नस्य द्विजसत्तम ।
किमन्तरं तथान्नस्य कथं वा तत्कृतं भवेत् ॥१

Śatānīka said: ‘How did he formerly interrupt his meal, O best of the twice-born? What constituted that interruption, and how did it come about?’

BP 1.3.42

सुमन्तुरुवाच
पुरा कृतयुगे राजन्वैश्यो वसति पुष्करे ।
धनवर्धननामा वै समृद्धौ धनधान्यतः ॥२

Sumantu said: ‘Long ago in the Kṛta age, O king, there lived at Puṣkara a vaiśya named Dhanavardhana, prosperous in wealth and grain.’

BP 1.3.43

निदाघकाले राजेन्द्र स कृत्वा वैश्वदेविकम् ।
सपुत्रभ्रातृभिः सार्धं तथा वै मित्रबन्धुभिः ॥
आहारं कुरुते राजन्भक्ष्यभोज्यसमन्वितम् ॥३

‘In the hot season, O king of kings, after performing the Vaiśvadeva offering, he took a meal of many foods together with his sons, brothers, friends, and relatives.’

BP 1.3.44

अथ तद्भुञ्जतस्तस्य अन्नं शब्दो महानभूत् ।
करुणः कुरुशार्दूल अथ तं स प्रधावितः ॥४

‘While he was eating that food, a great and pitiful cry arose, O tiger among the Kurus. He immediately ran toward the sound.’

BP 1.3.45

त्यक्त्वा स भोजनं यावन्निष्क्रान्तो गृहबाह्यतः ।
अथ शब्दस्तिरोभूतः स भूयो गृहमागतः ॥५

‘He abandoned his meal and went outside the house; by the time he emerged, the sound had vanished. He therefore returned home again.’

BP 1.3.46

तमेव भाजनं गृह्य आहारं कृतवान्नृप ।
भुक्तशेषं महाबाहो आहारं स तु भुक्तवान् ॥६

‘Taking up the very same dish, O king, he resumed his meal. Thus, O mighty-armed king, he ate food left over from his interrupted eating.’

BP 1.3.47

भुक्त्वा स शतधा जातस्तस्मिन्नेव क्षणे नृप ।
तस्मादन्नं न राजेन्द्र अश्नीयादन्तरा क्वचित् ॥७

‘The instant he had eaten, his body was split into a hundred pieces, O king. Therefore, O king of kings, one should never eat again after interrupting a meal.’

BP 1.3.48

न चैवात्यशनं कुर्यान्न चोच्छिष्टः क्वचिद्व्रजेत् ।
रसो भवत्यत्यशनाद्रसाद्रोगः प्रवर्तते ॥८

‘One should not overeat, nor go anywhere while still ritually impure from food. Excessive eating generates a disordered bodily humour, and from that humour disease proceeds.’

BP 1.3.49

स्नानं दानं जपो होमः पितृदेवाभिपूजनम् ।
न भवन्ति रसे जाते नराणां भरतर्षभ ॥९

‘When such a disorder has arisen in people, O bull among the Bharatas, bathing, generosity, recitation, fire offerings, and worship of ancestors and gods cannot be properly performed.’

BP 1.3.50

अनारोग्यमनायुष्यमस्वर्ग्यं चातिभोजनम् ।
अपुण्यं लोकविद्विष्टं तस्मात्तत्परिवर्जयेत् ॥.3.५०

‘Overeating causes ill health, shortens life, obstructs heaven, brings demerit, and is condemned by people. Therefore one should avoid it.’

BP 1.3.51

यक्षभूतपिशाचानां रक्षसां च नृपोत्तम ।
४गम्यो भवति वै विप्र उच्छिष्टो नात्र संशयः ॥१

‘A brāhmaṇa who remains defiled by leftovers becomes vulnerable to Yakṣas, spirits, Piśācas, and Rākṣasas, O best of kings; of this there is no doubt.’

BP 1.3.52

शुचित्वमाश्रयेत्तस्माच्छुचित्वान्मोदते दिति? ।
सुखेन चेह रमते इतीयं वैदिकी श्रुतिः ॥२

‘Therefore one should resort to purity. Through purity one rejoices in heaven and dwells happily here—such is the Vedic revelation.’

BP 1.3.53

शतानीक उवाच
शुचितामियात्कथं विप्रः कथं चाशुचितामियात् ।
एतन्मे इहि विप्रेन्द्र कौतुकं परमं मम ॥३

Śatānīka said: ‘How does a brāhmaṇa attain purity, and how does he fall into impurity? Tell me this, O lord of brāhmaṇas, for my curiosity is very great.’

BP 1.3.54

सुमन्तुरुवाच
उपस्पृश्य शुचिर्विप्रो भवते भरतर्षभ ।
विधिवत्कुरुशार्दूल भवेद्विधिपरो ह्यतः ॥४

Sumantu said: ‘By touching water according to rule, a brāhmaṇa becomes pure, O bull among the Bharatas. Therefore, O tiger among the Kurus, he should carefully observe the prescribed procedure.’

BP 1.3.55

शतानीक उवाच
उपस्पर्शविधिं विप्र कथय त्वं ममाखिलम् ।
शुचित्वमाप्नुयाद्येन आचान्तो ब्राह्मणो द्विजः ॥५

Śatānīka said: ‘Describe to me in full the procedure for touching water, O brāhmaṇa—the ācamana by which a twice-born brāhmaṇa may attain purity.’

BP 1.3.56

सुमन्तुरुवाच
साधु पृष्टोऽस्मि राजेन्द्र शृणु विप्रो यथा भवेत् ।
शुचिर्भरतशार्दूल विधिना येन वा विभो ॥६

Sumantu said: ‘You have asked well, O king of kings. Hear, O tiger among the Bharatas, the procedure by which a brāhmaṇa becomes pure, O lord.’

BP 1.3.57

प्रक्षाल्य हस्तौ पादौ च प्राङ्मुखोदङ्मुखोऽपि वा ।
उपविश्य शुचौ देशे बाहुं कृत्वा च दक्षिणम् ॥७

‘Having washed his hands and feet, he should sit in a clean place facing east or north and position his right arm properly.’

BP 1.3.58

जान्वन्तरे महाबाहो ब्रह्मसूत्रसमन्वितः ।
सुसमौ चरणौ कृत्वा तथा बद्धशिखो नृप ॥८

‘The right arm should be placed between the knees, O mighty-armed king. He should wear the sacred thread, set both feet evenly, and keep his hair-knot bound.’

BP 1.3.59

न तिष्ठन्न च संजल्पंस्तथा चानवलोकयन् ।
न त्वरन्कुपितो वापि त्यक्त्वा राजन्सुदूरतः ॥९

‘He should not perform it while standing, talking, looking around, hurrying, or angry; these conditions should be kept far away, O king.’

BP 1.3.60

प्रसन्नाभिस्तथाद्भिस्तु आचान्तः शुचितामियात् ।
नोष्णाभिर्न सफेनाभिर्युक्ताभिः कलुषेण च ॥.3.६०

‘By performing ācamana with clear water he attains purity. The water must not be hot, foamy, or mixed with impurity.’

BP 1.3.61

वर्णेन रसगन्धाभ्यां हीनाभिर्न च भारत ।
सबुद्बुदाभिश्च तथा नाचामेत्पण्डितो नृप ॥१

‘A learned person should not perform ācamana, O king, with water deficient in its proper colour, taste, or smell, nor with water containing bubbles, O descendant of Bharata.’

BP 1.3.62

पञ्चतीर्थानि विप्रस्य श्रूयन्ते दक्षिणे करे ।
देवतीर्थं पितृतीर्थं ब्रह्मतीर्थं च मानद ॥२

‘Five sacred places are traditionally recognized on a brāhmaṇa’s right hand: the Deva-tīrtha, Pitṛ-tīrtha, and Brahma-tīrtha, O giver of honour—’

BP 1.3.63

प्राजापत्यं तथा चान्यत्तथान्यत्सौम्यमुच्यते ।
अङ्गुष्ठमूलोत्तरतो येयं रेखा महीपते ॥३

‘—together with the Prājāpatya and another called the Saumya. The line situated just above the root of the thumb, O ruler of the earth—’

BP 1.3.64

ब्राह्मं तीर्थं वदन्त्येतद्वसिष्ठाद्या द्विजोत्तमाः ।
कायं कनिष्ठिकामूले अङ्गुल्यग्रे तु दैवतम् ॥४

‘—is called the Brahma-tīrtha by Vasiṣṭha and other foremost brāhmaṇas. The Kāya or Prājāpatya is at the base of the little finger, while the divine tīrtha is at the fingertips.’

BP 1.3.65

तर्जन्यङ्गुष्ठयोरन्तः पित्र्यं तीर्थमुदाहृतम् ।
करमध्ये स्थितं सौम्यं प्रशस्तं देवकर्मणि ॥५

‘The sacred place of the ancestors is declared to lie between the forefinger and thumb. The Saumya is situated in the centre of the palm and is approved for rites directed to the gods.’

BP 1.3.66

देवार्चाबलिहरणं प्रविक्षपणमेव च ।
एतानि देवतीर्थेन कुर्यात्कुरुकुलोद्वह ॥६

‘Worship of the gods, presentation of bali offerings, and ritual scattering should be performed with the Deva-tīrtha, O bearer of the Kuru lineage.’

BP 1.3.67

अन्ननिर्वपणं राजंस्तथा सपवनं नृप ।
लाजाहोमं तथा सौम्यं प्राजापत्येन कारयेत् ॥७

‘The placement of food offerings, the rite accompanied by purification, and the gentle offering of parched grain should be performed with the Prājāpatya tīrtha, O king.’

BP 1.3.68

कमण्डलूपस्पर्शनं दधिप्राशनमेव च ।
सौम्यतीर्थेन राजेन्द्र सदा कुर्याद्विचक्षणः ॥८

‘A discerning person should always touch the water-pot and taste curds with the Saumya tīrtha, O king of kings.’

BP 1.3.69

पितॄणां तर्पणं कार्यं पितृतीर्थेन धीमता ।
ब्राह्मेण चापि तीर्थेन सदोषस्पर्शनं परम् ॥९

‘A wise person should offer water to the ancestors with the Pitṛ-tīrtha. With the Brahma-tīrtha he performs the supreme purifying touch after contact involving impurity.’

BP 1.3.70

घनाङ्गुलिकरं कृत्वा एकाग्रः सुमना द्विजः ।
त्रिः कृत्वा यः पिबेदापो मुखशब्दविवर्जितः ॥.3.७०

‘A twice-born person should close the fingers together to form the hand properly, remain one-pointed and serene, and sip water three times without making any sound with the mouth.’

BP 1.3.71

शृणु यत्फलमाप्नोति प्रीणाति च यथा सुरान् ।
प्रथमं यत्पिबेदाप ऋग्वेदस्तेन तृप्यति ॥ ७१

‘Hear the fruit he obtains and how he satisfies the gods. By the first sip of water, the Ṛgveda is satisfied.’

BP 1.3.72

यद्द्वितीयं यजुर्वेदस्तेन प्रीणाति भारत ।
यत्तृतीयं सामवेदस्तेन प्रीणाति भारत ॥ ७२

‘By the second sip he satisfies the Yajurveda, O descendant of Bharata; by the third, he satisfies the Sāmaveda.’

BP 1.3.73

प्रथमं यन्मृजेदास्यं दक्षिणाङ्गुष्ठमूलतः ।
अथर्ववेदः प्रीणाति तेन राजन्नसंशयः ॥ ७३

‘When he first wipes his mouth with the base of the right thumb, the Atharvaveda is satisfied thereby, O king; of this there is no doubt.’

BP 1.3.74

इतिहासपुराणानि यद्द्वितीयं प्रमार्जति ।
यन्मूर्धानं हि राजेन्द्र अभिषिञ्चति वै द्विजः ॥ ७४

‘When he wipes it a second time, the Itihāsas and Purāṇas are satisfied. When the twice-born person sprinkles the crown of his head, O king of kings—’

BP 1.3.75

तेन प्रीणाति वै रुद्रं शिखामालभ्य वै ऋषीन् ।
यदक्षिणी चालभते रविः प्रीणाति तेन वै ॥ ७५

‘—he pleases Rudra thereby; by touching the hair-knot, the seers; and when he touches both eyes, he pleases the Sun.’

BP 1.3.76

नासिकालम्भनाद्वायुं प्रीणात्येव न संशयः ।
यच्छ्रोत्रमालभेद्विप्रो दिशः प्रीणाति तेन वै ॥ ७६

‘By touching the nose he surely pleases Vāyu; of this there is no doubt. When a brāhmaṇa touches his ears, he thereby pleases the directions.’

BP 1.3.77

यमं कुबेरं वरुणं वासवं चाग्निमेव च ।
यद्बाहुमन्वालभते एतान्प्रीणाति तेन वै ॥ ७७

‘When he touches his arms, he thereby pleases Yama, Kubera, Varuṇa, Vāsava, and Agni.’

BP 1.3.78

यन्नाभिमन्वालभते प्राणानां ग्रन्थिमेव च ।
तेन प्रीणाति राजेन्द्र इतीयं वैदिकी श्रुतिः ॥ ७८

‘When he touches the navel, the very knot of the vital breaths, he brings satisfaction through that act, O king of kings—such is the Vedic revelation.’

BP 1.3.79

अभिषिञ्चति यत्पादौ विष्णुं प्रीणाति तेन वै ।
यद्भूम्याच्छादकं वारि विसर्जयति मानद ॥ ७९

‘When he sprinkles both feet, he thereby pleases Viṣṇu. When he releases upon the earth the water used to cover or wash them, O giver of honour—’

BP 1.3.80

वासुकिप्रमुखान्नागांस्तेन प्रीणाति भारत ।
यद्बिन्दवोऽन्तरे भूमौ पतन्तीह नराधिप ॥.3.८०

‘—he thereby pleases Vāsuki and the foremost Nāgas, O descendant of Bharata. The drops that fall here upon the earth in between, O ruler of men—’

BP 1.3.81

भूतग्रामं ततस्तस्तु प्रीणन्तीह चतुर्विधम् ।
अङ्गुष्ठेन प्रदेशिन्या लभेत चाक्षिणी नृप ॥ ८१

Thereby he satisfies the fourfold host of beings here. With the thumb and forefinger he should touch both eyes, O king.

BP 1.3.82

अनामिकाङ्गुष्ठिकाभ्यां नासिकामालभेन्नृप ।
मध्यमाङ्गुष्ठाभ्यां मुखं संस्पृशेद्भरतर्षभ ॥ ८२

‘With the ring finger and thumb he should touch the nose, O king; with the middle finger and thumb he should touch the mouth, O bull among the Bharatas.’

BP 1.3.83

कनिष्ठिकाङ्गुष्ठकाभ्यां कर्णमालभते नृप ।
अङ्गुलीभिस्तथा बाहुमङ्गुष्ठेन तु मङ्गलम् ॥ ८३

‘With the little finger and thumb he touches the ears, O king; with the fingers he likewise touches the arms, and with the thumb the auspicious central point.’
