VP 1.14.1

पृथोः पुत्रौ तु धर्मज्ञौ जज्ञातेऽन्तर्द्धि-वादिनौ।
शिखण्डिनी हविर्धानम् अन्तर्धानाद् व्यजायत॥१॥

Pruthu had two sons, who were knowledgeable about dharma - Antardhaana and Vaadi. Antardhaana's wife Shikhandinee, gave birth to Havirdhaana.

Śrīdhara translation

“Disappeared completely” means that he became invisible. Therefore the wife named Śikhaṇḍinī bore Havirdhāna, the son.

VP 1.14.2

हविर्धानाद् षड् आग्नेयी धिषणाजनयत् सुतान्।
प्राचीनबर्हिषं शुक्रं गयं कृष्णं वृजाजिनौ॥२॥

From Havirdhaana, his wife Dhishanaa of the Agni kula, gave birth to six sons. Their names are Praacheenabarhis, Shukra, Gaya, Krushna, Vruja, Ajina.

“Āgneyī” means born in the lineage of Agni; Dhīṣaṇā is her name.

VP 1.14.4

प्राचीनाग्राः कुशास् तस्य पृथिव्यां विस्तृता मुने।
प्राचीनबर्हिर् अभवत् ख्यातो भुवि महा-बलः॥४॥

He was called Praacheenabarhis because all the darbhaas which had praacheena agra, which are used for specific purpose in the yajnyaas, were spread all over the earth. That is why he became famous as Praacheenabarhis. He was of great valour.

Śrīdhara translation

The kuśa grass had its tips facing east everywhere because sacrifices were being performed. The cited verse says that the earth was covered with east-pointing kuśa grass while the sacrificial rites were being extended.

VP 1.14.5

समुद्र-तनयायां तु कृत-दारो मही-पतिः।
महतस् तमसः पारे सवर्णायां महा-मते॥५॥

The king married Savarṇā, daughter of the ocean, who was beyond the great darkness, O wise one.

Śrīdhara translation

After completing austerity beyond Savarṇā, he took her as his wife; that is, he married her after performing austerity. In the reading “beyond darkness,” the meaning is that he produced a son in order to cross the hell called darkness.

VP 1.14.7

अपृथग् धर्म-चरणास् तेऽतप्यन्त महत् तपः।

Following the same dhamaacharane, they started to perform great tapas for 10000 years under the waters of the ocean.

They practised the same dharma and followed the same religious observances. This is stated to suggest that they had one common wife; they lay submerged in the waters of the ocean.

Śrīdhara translation

They bow to him in the form of the Person: pure, appearing to possess qualities because he impels them, as if attached to them, and in the form of the innermost Self.
