VP 1.14.1

पृथोः पुत्रौ तु धर्मज्ञौ जज्ञातेऽन्तर्द्धिवादिनौ । शिखण्डिनी हविर्धानम् अन्तर्धानाद् व्यजायत ॥१॥

Pruthu had two sons, who were knowledgeable about dharma - Antardhaana and Vaadi. Antardhaana's wife Shikhandinee, gave birth to Havirdhaana.

Śrīdhara translation

“Disappeared completely” means that he became invisible. Therefore the wife named Śikhaṇḍinī bore Havirdhāna, the son.

VP 1.14.4

प्राचीनाग्राः कुशास् तस्य पृथिव्यां विश्रुता मुने । प्राचीनबर्हिर् अभवत् ख्यातो भुवि महाबलः ॥४॥

He was called Praacheenabarhis because all the darbhaas which had praacheena agra, which are used for specific purpose in the yajnyaas, were spread all over the earth. That is why he became famous as Praacheenabarhis. He was of great valour.

Śrīdhara translation

The kuśa grass had its tips facing east everywhere because sacrifices were being performed. The cited verse says that the earth was covered with east-pointing kuśa grass while the sacrificial rites were being extended.

The king married Savarṇā, daughter of the ocean, who was beyond the great darkness, O wise one.

Śrīdhara translation

After completing austerity beyond Savarṇā, he took her as his wife; that is, he married her after performing austerity. In the reading “beyond darkness,” the meaning is that he produced a son in order to cross the hell called darkness.
