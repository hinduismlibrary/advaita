Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 30 — Further Worship and Rites of Gaṇeśa

BP 1.30.1

सुमन्तरुवाच
निम्बमयमङ्गुष्ठपर्वमात्रं गणपतिं कृत्वा नित्यधूपगन्धादिभिरर्चयित्वा प्रच्छन्नं शिरसि बद्ध्वा गच्छेत् ॥
सर्वजनप्रियो भवति । श्वेतार्कमूलाङ्गुष्ठमात्रं गणपतिं कृत्वा धूपादिभिरर्चयित्वा सर्वान्वर्णान्वशमानयति ॥
श्वेतचन्दनमङ्गुष्ठमात्रं गणपतिं कृत्वा पुष्पगन्धादिभिरर्चयित्वा शुक्लचतुर्थ्यामष्टम्यां वा बलिं कुर्यादष्टसहस्रं जुहुयाद्दध्ना पायसेन राजानं वशमानयति ।
रक्तचन्दनमयं गणपतिमङ्गुष्ठमात्रं कृत्वा भौतिकं बलिं दद्याद्दधिमधुघृताहुतीनां गणपतिमष्टसहस्रं जुहुयादात्मप्रापिकां प्रजां वशमानयति । रक्तकरवीरमूलाङ्गुष्ठपर्वमात्रं गणपतिं कारयेत् ॥
रक्तपुष्पगन्धोपहारैर्बलिं दद्यात् । तिललवणघृतेनाष्टसहस्रं जुहुयात् । दशग्रामान्वशमानयति ॥
श्वेतकरवीराङ्गुष्ठपर्वमात्रं गणपतिं कृत्वा तिलपिष्टदधिघृतक्षीरहरिद्रामिश्रेणाष्टसहस्रं
जुहुयाद्वेश्यां वशमानयति ॥
अश्वत्थमूलाङ्गुष्ठपर्वमात्रं गणपतिं कृत्वा गन्धपुष्पधूपबलिं दत्त्वा शतं जुहुयाच्छत्रुं वशमानयति ॥
अर्कमूलाङ्गुष्ठपर्वमात्रं गणपतिं कृत्वा गन्धपुष्पधूपबलीन् दद्यात् ।
तिन्दुकाष्टशतं जुहुयाच्छत्रुं वशमानयति ॥
बिल्वमूलमयमङ्गुष्ठपर्वमात्रं गणपतिं कृत्वा गन्धपुष्पधूपार्चितं कृत्वा त्रिमध्वक्तानामष्टसहस्रं जुहुयाद्राजामात्यान्वशमानयति ॥
शिरसि धूपान्धृत्वा गच्छेद्राजद्वारं विग्रहे जयो भवति ।
हस्तिदन्तमृत्तिकामयमङ्गुष्ठपर्वमात्रं गणपतिं कारयेत् ॥
गन्धपुष्पधूपार्चितं कृत्वा. कृष्णचतुर्थ्यां नग्नो भूत्वाभ्यर्चयेत् ।
सप्त वाराञ्जपेन्नित्यं नारीणां सुभगो भवति ॥

Sumantu said: Fashion a thumb-joint-sized Gaṇapati from neem, worship it daily with incense, fragrance, and other offerings, bind it secretly upon the head, and go forth; one becomes dear to everyone. A Gaṇapati of white arka root, similarly worshipped, is said to bring all social groups under ones influence. A thumb-sized image of white sandalwood, worshipped with flowers and fragrances, is offered bali on the bright fourth or eighth lunar day; eight thousand oblations of curds and milk-rice are said to influence a king. A red-sandalwood image is given a material offering and eight thousand oblations of curds, honey, and ghee, said to influence ones subjects. A red-oleander-root image is offered red flowers, fragrances, sesame, salt, and ghee; eight thousand oblations are said to influence ten villages. A white-oleander image receives a mixture of sesame paste, curds, ghee, milk, and turmeric in eight thousand oblations, said to influence a courtesan. A sacred-fig-root image, worshipped with fragrance, flowers, incense, and bali, receives one hundred oblations to influence an enemy. An arka-root image receives the same worship and eight hundred tinduka offerings for that purpose. A bilva-root image, worshipped and offered eight thousand portions anointed with the three sweet substances, is said to influence a king and his ministers. Carrying the incense upon ones head and approaching the royal gate is said to bring victory in a dispute. One should also fashion a thumb-joint-sized Gaṇapati from earth associated with elephant ivory, worship it with fragrance, flowers, and incense, and worship unclothed on the dark fourth; seven daily recitations are said to make one attractive to women.

BP 1.30.2

वृषभशृङ्गमृत्तिकाङ्गुष्ठमात्रं गणपतिं कारयेत् ।
गन्धपुष्पार्चितं कृत्वा गुग्गुलुधूपं दद्याद्घोषपतिं वशमानयति ॥
अथ वा वल्मीकमृत्तिकांगुष्ठपर्वमात्रं गणपतिं कारयेत् ।
कटुकतैलेन प्रतिमां लेपयेत् ॥
उन्मत्तककाष्ठेनाग्निं प्रज्वाल्याहुतीनामष्टसहस्रं जुहुयात्तिलसर्षपमिश्रेण सर्वधूपं दद्यात्त्रिकटुकेन लेपयेत् ॥
अगरुधूपं दद्याद्राजानं वशमानयति । परेषां च वल्लभो भवति । रक्तचन्दनेनात्मानं धूपयेत्सुभगो भवति ॥
ॐ गणपतये वक्रतुण्डाय गजदन्ताय गुलगुलेतिनिनादाय चतुर्भुजाय त्रिनेत्राय मुशलपाशवज्रहस्ताय सर्वभूतदमनाय सर्वलोकवशंकराय सर्वदुष्टोपघातजननाय सर्वशत्रुविमर्दनाय सर्वराज्यसमीहनाय राजानमिह वशमानय हन हन पच पच वज्राङ्कुशेन गणेश फट् स्वाहा ।

One should make a thumb-sized Gaṇapati from earth taken at a bulls horn. Having worshipped him with fragrance and flowers, one should offer guggulu incense and bring the head of the settlement under ones control. Or one should make a thumb-joint-sized Gaṇapati from anthill earth and anoint the image with pungent oil. Kindling a fire with dhattūra wood, one should offer eight thousand oblations mixed with sesame and mustard, give incense throughout, and anoint him with the three pungent spices. Offering agarwood incense brings a king under ones control and makes one beloved by others. Fumigating oneself with red sandalwood makes one fortunate. Om, Gaṇapati, Vakratuṇḍa, elephant-tusked, sounding gulguleti, four-armed, three-eyed, holding pestle, noose, and thunderbolt, subduer of all beings, controller of all worlds, cause of the destruction of all the wicked, crusher of all enemies, fulfiller of every kingdom: bring the king here under my control; strike, strike; cook, cook; with the thunderbolt goad, O Gaṇeśa, phaṭ, svāhā.

BP 1.30.3

ॐ गां गीं गूं गैं गौं गः स्वाहा नमः हदयं मूलमन्त्रस्य ।
ॐ कः शिरः, ॐ खः शिखा, ॐ गः हदयम्, ॐ गुः वक्त्रं, ॐ गैं नेत्रम् । ॐ घः कवचम्, ॐ ङ आवाहनं हदयस्य आवाहनाङ्गानि भवन्ति ॐ नमः हदयं मूलमन्त्रस्य, ॐ गाः शिरः, ॐ गैः नमः शिखा ॐ गौः नमः कवचम्, ॐ गं नमः नेत्रे, ॐ गः फट् अस्त्रम् ॥
ॐ अङ्गुष्ठोल्काय स्वाहा आवाहनं हदयस्य स्वाहा विसर्जनं हदयस्य ॐ गन्धोल्काय स्वाहा ॥
गन्धमन्त्रः ॥ ॐ धर्मभूतोल्काय स्वाहा ॥ पुष्पमन्त्रः ॥ दुर्जयाय पूर्वेण । ॐ धूर्जटये दक्षिणेन ।
ॐ लम्बोदराय पश्चिमतः । ॐ गणपतये उत्तरतः । ॐ गणाधिपतये ऐशान्याम् । ॐ महागणपतये आग्नेय्याम् । ॐ कूश्माण्डाय नैर्ऋत्याम् ।
ॐ एकदन्तत्रिपुरघातिने त्रिनेत्राय वायव्याम् । ॐ महागणपतये विद्महे वक्रतुण्डाय धीमहि ॥
तन्नो दन्तिः प्रचोदयात् ॥ गायत्री ॥
पद्मदंष्ट्रामालाप्रकर्षणीपरश्वंकुशपाशपटहमुद्रा अष्टौ मुद्रा दर्शयित्वा ततः कर्माणि कारयेत् ॥
कृष्णतिलाहुतीनामष्टसहस्रं जुहुयात् । राजानं वशमानयेत् ॥
आवाहनाद्येकादशमुद्रा नैवेद्यान्तं क्रमाद्दर्शयेत् ॥
आराधयेद्येन विधिना त्रिनेत्रं शूलिनं हरम् ।
तेनैवाराधयेद्देवं विघ्नेशं गणपं नृप ॥
तदेव मण्डलं चास्य अङ्गन्यासस्तथैव च ।
ऋते मन्त्रपदानीह समानं सर्वमेव हि ॥
पूजयेद्यस्तु विघ्नेशमेकदन्तमुमासुतम् ।
नश्यन्ति तस्य विघ्नानि न चारिष्टं कदाचन ॥

Om gāṃ gīṃ gūṃ gaiṃ gauṃ gaḥ, svāhā; salutation, the heart of the root mantra. Om kaḥ, the head; om khaḥ, the topknot; om gaḥ, the heart; om guḥ, the mouth; om gaiṃ, the eye; om ghaḥ, the armour; om ṅa, the invocation: these are the invocation limbs of the heart. Om namaḥ, the heart of the root mantra; om gāḥ, the head; om gaiḥ namaḥ, the topknot; om gauḥ namaḥ, the armour; om gaṃ namaḥ, the eyes; om gaḥ phaṭ, the weapon. Om, svāhā to Aṅguṣṭholka; svāhā, invocation of the heart; svāhā, dismissal of the heart; om, svāhā to Gandholka. The fragrance mantra. Om, svāhā to Dharmabhūtolka. The flower mantra. To Durjaya in the east; om, to Dhūrjaṭi in the south; om, to Lambodara in the west; om, to Gaṇapati in the north; om, to Gaṇādhipati in the northeast; om, to Mahāgaṇapati in the southeast; om, to Kūṣmāṇḍa in the southwest; om, to three-eyed Ekadanta Tripuraghātin in the northwest. We know Mahāgaṇapati; we meditate upon Vakratuṇḍa; may the Tusked One inspire us. This is the Gāyatrī. Having shown the eight mudrās—lotus, tusk, rosary, drawing, axe, goad, noose, and drum—one should then perform the rites. One should offer eight thousand oblations of black sesame and bring the king under ones control. One should show the eleven mudrās in order, from invocation through the food offering. O king, worship Vighneśa Gaṇapati by the very method by which three-eyed, trident-bearing Hara is worshipped. His maṇḍala and the placement of the limbs are the same; here everything is the same except the mantra words. Whoever worships Vighneśa, the one-tusked son of Umā, has his obstacles destroyed and never has misfortune.
