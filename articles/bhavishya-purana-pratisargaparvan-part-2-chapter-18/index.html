BP 3.2.18.1

सूत उवाच ।
भोः शौनक महाप्राज्ञ वैतालस्तं हि सोऽब्रवीत् ।
सुदक्षो नाम नृपतिर्वसन्कंबलके पुरे ॥ 1 ॥

Sūta said: O profoundly wise Śaunaka, the Vetāla spoke to him: A king named Sudakṣa lived in the city of Kambalaka.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

महाप्राज्ञ mahāprājña O greatly wise one
वैतालस्तं vaitālastaṃ the Vetāla addressed him
हि hi indeed
सोऽब्रवीत् so'bravīt he said
सुदक्षो sudakṣo Sudakṣa
नाम nāma name
नृपतिर्वसन्कंबलके nṛpatirvasankaṃbalake the king living in Kambalaka
पुरे pure in the city
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

sūta uvāca | bhoḥ śaunaka mahāprājña vaitālastaṃ hi so'bravīt | sudakṣo nāma nṛpatirvasankaṃbalake pure || 1 ||
