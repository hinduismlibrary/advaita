Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 179 — Solar Rites

BP 1.179.1

अरुण उवाच ।। कृत्तिका परमा देवी रोहिणी च वरानना ।। श्रीमन्मृगशिरो भद्रा आर्द्रा चाप्यपरोज्ज्वला ।। १ ।।

Aruṇa said: Kṛttikā, the supreme goddess, and Rohiṇī, fair-faced; glorious Mṛgaśīrṣa, auspicious Ārdrā, and the exceedingly radiant one.

BP 1.179.2

पुनर्वसुस्तथा पुष्य आश्लेषा च तथाधिप ।। सूर्यार्चनरता नित्यं सूर्यभावानुभाविताः ।। २ ।।

Punarvasu likewise, and Puṣya, and Āśleṣā, O lord; ever engaged in Sun-worship, imbued with the Sun’s nature.

BP 1.179.3

अर्चयंति सदा देवमादित्यं सुरते सदा ।। नक्षत्रमातरो ह्येताः प्रभामालाविभूषिताः ।। ३ ।।

They always worship the god Āditya, ever in delight. These are the mothers of the lunar mansions, adorned with a garland of radiance.

BP 1.179.4

मघा सर्वगुणोपेता पूर्वा चैव तु फाल्गुनी ।। स्वाती विशाखा वरदा दक्षिणां दिशमाश्रिताः ।। ४ ।।

Maghā, endowed with every quality, and Pūrvaphālgunī; Svātī and Viśākhā, boon-giving, have resorted to the southern direction.

BP 1.179.5

अर्चयंति सदा देवमादित्यं सुरपूजितम् ।। तवापि शांतिकं द्योतं कुर्वंतु गगनोदिताः ।। ५ ।।

They always worship the god Āditya, honored by the gods. May those arisen in the sky make a pacifying radiance for you.

BP 1.179.6

अनुराधा तथा ज्येष्ठा मूलं सूर्यपुरःसरा ।। पूर्वाषाढा महावीर्या आषाढा चोत्तरा तथा ।। ६ ।।

Anurādhā, likewise Jyeṣṭhā, and Mūla, with the Sun in front; mighty Pūrvāṣāḍhā, and Uttarāṣāḍhā likewise.

BP 1.179.7

अभिजिन्नाम नक्षत्रं श्रवणं च बहुश्रुतम् ।। एताः पश्चिमतो दीप्ता राजंते चानु मूर्तयः ।। ७ ।।

The lunar mansion named Abhijit, and the famed Śravaṇa: these, shining toward the west, shine as forms in succession.

BP 1.179.8

भास्करं पूजयंत्येताः सर्वकालं सुभाविताः ।। शांतिं कुर्वंतु ते नित्यं विभूतिं च महर्द्धिकाम् ।।८।।

These worship Bhāskara at all times, with good intent. May they always bring you peace and great prosperity.

BP 1.179.9

धनिष्ठा शतभिषा तु पूर्वभाद्रपदा तथा ।। ९ ।।

Dhaniṣṭhā, Śatabhiṣā, and Pūrvabhādrapadā likewise.

BP 1.179.10

उत्तरा भाद्ररेवत्यौ चाश्विनी च महामते ।। भरणी च महादेवी नित्यमुत्तरतः स्थिताः ।। १० ।।

Uttarabhādrapadā and Revatī, and Aśvinī, O greatly wise one; Bharaṇī, the great goddess, is always situated in the north.

BP 1.179.11

सूर्यार्चनरता नित्यमादित्यगतमा नसाः ।। शांतिं कुर्वतु ते नित्यं विभूतिं च महर्द्धिकाम् ।। ११ ।।

Ever engaged in Sun-worship, their minds fixed on Āditya, may they always bring you peace and great prosperity.

BP 1.179.12

मेषो मृगाधिपः सिंहो धनुर्दीप्तिमतां वरः ।। पूर्वेण भासयंत्येते सूर्ययोगपराः शुभाः ।। शांतिं कुर्वंतु ते नित्यं भक्त्या सूर्यपदांबुजे ।। १२ ।।

The Ram, the Lion, and Sagittarius, best of the radiant ones, auspicious and devoted to union with the Sun, shine in the east. May they always bring you peace, devoted to the lotus feet of the Sun.

BP 1.179.13

वृषः कन्या च परमा मकरश्चापि बुद्धिमान् ।। एते दक्षिणभागे तु पूजयंति रविं सदा ।। भक्त्या परमया नित्यं शांतिं कुर्वंतु ते सदा ।।१३।।

Taurus, Virgo, exalted Capricorn, and Budha the wise one, always worship Ravi in the southern part. With supreme devotion, may they always bring you peace.

BP 1.179.14

मिथुनं च तुला कुंभः पश्चिमे च व्यवस्थिताः ।। जपंत्येते सदाकालमादित्यं ग्रहनाय कम् ।। १४ ।।

Gemini, Libra, and Aquarius are situated in the west. They constantly recite Āditya, the lord of the planets.

BP 1.179.15

शांतिं कुर्वंतु ते नित्यं खखोल्काज्ञानतत्पराः ।। सतपोदत्तपुष्पाभ्यां ये स्मृता सततं बुधैः ।। १५ ।।

May they always bring you peace, intent on knowledge of the Sun, remembered constantly by the wise with flowers offered through austerity.

BP 1.179.16

ऋषयः सप्त विख्याता ध्रुवांताः प्ररमोज्ज्वलाः ।। भानुप्रसादात्संपन्नाः शांतिं कुर्वंतु ते सदा ।। १६ ।।

The seven famed sages, ending with Dhruva, supremely radiant, prospered by Bhānu’s favor, may they always bring you peace.

BP 1.179.17

कश्यपो गालवो गार्ग्यो विश्वामित्रो महामुनिः ।। मुनिर्दक्षो वशिष्ठश्च मार्कण्डः पुलहः क्रतुः ।। १७ ।।

Kaśyapa, Gālava, Gārgya, the great sage Viśvāmitra; the sage Dakṣa, Vasiṣṭha, Mārkaṇḍa, Pulaha, and Kratu.

BP 1.179.18

नारदो भृगुरात्रेयो भारद्वाजश्च वै मुनिः ।। वाल्मीकिः कौशिको वात्स्यः शाकल्योऽथ पुनर्वसुः ।। १८ ।।

Nārada, Bhṛgu, Ātreya, and the sage Bhāradvāja; Vālmīki, Kauśika, Vātsya, Śākalya, and then Punarvasu.

BP 1.179.19

शालंकायन इत्येते ऋषयोऽथ महातपाः ।। सूर्यध्यानैकपरमाः शांतिं कुर्वंतु ते सदा।। १९ ।।

Śālaṅkāyana and these sages of great ascetic heat, supremely intent on meditation on the Sun, may they always bring you peace.

BP 1.179.20

मुनिकन्या महाभागा ऋषिकन्याः कुमारिकाः ।। सूर्यार्चनरता नित्यं शांतिं कुर्वंतु ते सदा ।। २० ।।

The fortunate daughters of sages, the unmarried daughters of seers, ever engaged in Sun-worship, may they always bring you peace.

BP 1.179.21

सिद्धाः समृद्धतपसो ये चान्ये वै महातपाः ।। विद्याधरा महात्मानो गरुडश्च त्वया सह ।। २१ ।।

The perfected beings, abundant in ascetic practice, and the other great ascetics; the great-souled knowledge-bearing spirits, and Garuḍa together with you.

BP 1.179.22

आदित्यपरमा ह्येते आदित्याराधने रताः ।। सिद्धिं ते संप्रयच्छंतु आशीर्वादपरायणाः ।। २२ ।।

These are wholly devoted to Āditya, engaged in worship of Āditya. Intent on blessing, may they grant you success.

BP 1.179.23

नमुचिर्दैत्यराजेद्रः शंकुकर्णो महाबलः ।। महानाथोऽथ विख्यातो दैत्यः परमवीर्यवान् ।। २३ ।।

Namuci, foremost of demon kings; mighty Śaṅkukarṇa; and the famed Mahānātha, a demon of supreme might.

BP 1.179.24

ग्रहाधिपस्य देवस्य नित्यं पूजापरायणाः ।। बलं वीर्यं च ते ऋद्धिमारोग्यं च ब्रुवंतु ते ।।२४।।

Ever devoted to worship of the god, lord of the planets, may they proclaim strength, vigor, prosperity, and health for you.

BP 1.179.25

महाढ्यो यो हयग्रीवः प्रह्लादः प्रभयान्वितः ।। तानैकाग्निमुखो दैत्यः कालनेमिर्महाबलः ।। २५ ।।

Hayagrīva, greatly rich, and radiant Prahlāda; and the mighty demon Kalanemi, with a single fiery face.

BP 1.179.26

एते दैत्या महात्मानः सूर्यभावेन भाविताः ।।। तुष्टिं बलं तथाऽऽरोग्यं प्रयच्छंतु सुरारयः ।। २६ ।।

These great-souled demons, imbued with the Sun’s nature, enemies of the gods, may they grant satisfaction, strength, and health.

BP 1.179.27

वैरोचनो हिरण्याक्षस्तुर्वसुश्च सुलोचनः ।। मुचकुंदो मुकुंदश्च दैत्यो रैवतकस्तथा ।।२७।।

Vairochana, Hiraṇyākṣa, Turvasu, and Sulocana; Mucukunda, Mukunda, and the demon Raivataka likewise.

BP 1.179.28

भावेन परमेणेमं यजंते सततं रविम् ।। सततं च शुभात्मानः पुष्टिं कुर्वंतु ते सदा ।। २८ ।।

With supreme devotion they constantly worship this Ravi. Ever auspicious in spirit, may they always bring you nourishment.

BP 1.179.29

दैत्यपतयो महाभागा दैत्यानां कन्यकाः शुभाः ।। कुमारा ये च दैत्यानां शांतिं कुर्वंतु ते सदा ।। २९ ।।

The fortunate lords of the demons, the auspicious daughters of the demons, and the demon youths, may they always bring you peace.

BP 1.179.30

आरक्तेन शरीरण रक्तांतायतलोचनाः ।। महाभागाः कृताटोपाः शंखाद्याः कृतलक्षणाः ।। ३० ।।

With a red body, eyes red at the corners and long; greatly fortunate, making a display, marked with the conch and the rest.

BP 1.179.31

अनंतो नागराजेंद्र आदित्याराधने रतः ।। महापापविषं हत्वा शांतिमाशु करोतु ते ।। ३१ ।।

Ananta, foremost king of serpents, engaged in worship of Āditya, having destroyed the poison of great sin, may he quickly bring you peace.

BP 1.179.32

अतिपीतेन देहेन विस्फुरद्भोगसंपदा ।। तेजसा चातिदीप्तेन कृतस्वस्तिकलांछनः ।। ३२ ।।

With a very yellow body, endowed with shining coils, exceedingly blazing in splendor, marked with the swastika.

BP 1.179.33

नगराट् तक्षकः श्रीमान्नामकोट्या समन्वितः ।। करोतु ते महाशांतिं सर्वदोषविषापहाम् ।। ३३ ।।

The glorious serpent king Takṣaka, accompanied by countless names, may he bring you great peace, removing all faults and poison.

BP 1.179.34

अतिकृष्णेन वर्णेन स्फुटाधिकटमस्तकः ।। कंठरेखात्रयोपेतो घोरदंष्ट्रायुधोद्यतः ।। ३४ ।।

Very black in color, with a clearly raised head, furnished with three throat-lines, with terrible fangs raised as weapons.

BP 1.179.35

कर्कोटको महानागो विषदर्पबलान्वितः ।। विषशस्त्राग्निसंतापं हत्वा शांतिं करोतु ते ।। ३५ ।।

Karkoṭaka, the great serpent, endowed with the pride and force of poison, having destroyed the torment of poison, weapons, and fire, may he bring you peace.

BP 1.179.36

पद्मवर्णः पद्मकान्तिः फुल्लपद्मायतेक्षणः ।। ख्यातः पद्मो महानागो नित्यं भास्करपूजकः ।। ३६ ।।

Lotus-colored, with the splendor of a lotus, with eyes long as a blooming lotus; the famed Padma, a great serpent, always worships Bhāskara.

BP 1.179.37

स ते शांति शुभं शीघ्रमचलं संप्रयच्छतु ।। श्यामेन देहभारेण श्रीमत्कमललोचनः ।। ३७ ।।

May he quickly grant you auspicious, steadfast peace. With a dark, massive body, glorious, lotus-eyed;

BP 1.179.38

विषदर्पबलोन्मत्तो ग्रीवायां रेखयान्वितः ।। शंखपालश्रिया दीप्तः सूर्यपादाब्जपूजकः ।। महाविषं गरश्रेष्ठं हत्वा शांतिं करोतु ते ।। ३८ ।।

maddened with the pride and force of poison, marked with lines at the neck, shining with the glory of Śaṅkhapāla, worshipping the Sun’s lotus feet; having destroyed the great, excellent poison, may he bring you peace.

BP 1.179.39

अतिगौरेण देहेन चंद्रार्धकृतशेखरः ।। दीपभागे कृताटोपशुभलक्षणलक्षितः ।। ३९ ।।

With a very fair body, a half-moon made his crest, displaying himself in the lamp’s light, marked with auspicious signs.

BP 1.179.40

कुलिको नाम नागेन्द्रो नित्यं सूर्यपरायणः ।। अपहृत्य विषं घोरं करोतु तव शांतिकम् ।। ४० ।।

Kulika by name, king of serpents, always devoted to the Sun; having removed the terrible poison, may he bring you peace.

BP 1.179.41

अंतरिक्षे च ये नागा ये नागाः स्वर्गसंस्थिताः ।। गिरिकंदरदुर्गेषु ये नागा भुवि संस्थिताः ।। ४१ ।।

Those serpents in the atmosphere and those situated in heaven; those situated on earth in mountain caves and difficult places;

BP 1.179.42

पाताले ये स्थिता नागाः सर्वे यत्र समाहिताः ।। सूर्यपादा र्चनासक्ताः शांतिं कुर्वंतु ते सदा ।।४२।।

those serpents situated in the netherworld, all assembled wherever, devoted to worship of the Sun’s feet, may they always bring you peace.

BP 1.179.43

नागिन्यो नागकन्याश्च तथा नागकुमारकाः ।। सूर्यभक्ताः सुमनसः शांतिं कुर्वंतु ते सदा ।।४३।।

Female serpents, serpent maidens, and serpent youths likewise, devoted to the Sun and benevolent in mind, may they always bring you peace.

BP 1.179.44

य इदं नामसंस्थानं कीर्तयेच्छृणुयात्तथा ।। न तं सर्पा विहिंसति न विषं क्रमते सदा ।। ४४ ।।

Whoever recites or hears this arrangement of names: serpents do not harm him, nor does poison ever enter.
