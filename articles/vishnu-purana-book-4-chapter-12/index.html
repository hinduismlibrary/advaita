VP 4.12.1

क्रोष्टोस्तु यदुपुत्रस्यात्मजो ध्वजिनीवान्

Sri Paraasharah - Yadu's son was Kroshtru, and he had a son by name Dhvajineevaan.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kroṣṭostu yaduputrasyātmajo dhvajinīvān

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.12.13

भार्यावश्यास्तु ये केचिद्भविष्यन्त्यथ वा मृताः । तेषां तु ज्यामघः श्रेष्ठः शैव्यापतिरभून्नृपः

Those who are under the control of their wives, who were there in the past, and who are going to come in the future, among them, Jyaamagha is said to be the leader, he is the best among them. He had a wife by name Shaibyaa.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bhāryāvaśyāstu ye kecidbhaviṣyantyatha vā mṛtāḥ | teṣāṃ tu jyāmaghaḥ śreṣṭhaḥ śaivyāpatirabhūnnṛpaḥ

No commentary.

VP 4.12.14

अपुत्रा तस्य सा पत्नी शैव्या नाम तथाऽप्यसौ । अपत्यकामोऽपि भयान्नान्यां भार्यामविन्दत

He was so scared of his wife Shaibyaa, that, though she did not bear him any children, when he was desirous of having a child, even then, out of fear of her, he never married again.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

No commentary.

VP 4.12.15

Once upon a time, he engaged in a battle, where there were plenty of chariots, horses, elephants, the complete army and forces, and it was a very fierce battle, and he won over all the enemies in that battle.

Viṣṇu Purāṇa

No commentary.

VP 4.12.16

तच्चारिचक्रमपास्तपुत्रकलत्रबन्धुबलकोशं स्वमधिष्ठानं परित्यज्य दिशः प्रति विद्रुतम्

All the enemies and kings ran away in all directions, having lost their wives and children, their relatives, wealth, treasure, etc.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

taccāricakramapāstaputrakalatrabandhubalakośaṃ svamadhiṣṭhānaṃ parityajya diśaḥ prati vidrutam

Śrīdhara

dvādaśe kroṣṭra-sañjñasya yadu-putrasya santatiḥ vaṃśa-kṛd avarṇyate yatra jyāmaghaḥ strī-jitāgraṇīḥ . caturdaśa mahānti ratnāni yasya saḥ . ratnāni cātra sva-sva-jāti-śreṣṭhāni dharma-saṃhitoktāni— cakraṃ ratho maṇiḥ khaḍgaś carma ratnaṃ ca pañcamam . ketur nidhiś ca saptaiva prāṇa-hīnāni cakṣate || bhāryā purohitaś caiva senānī rathakṛc ca yaḥ . patyaśvau kalabhāś ceti prāṇinaḥ sapta kīrtitāḥ || caturdaśeti ratnāni sarveṣāṃ cakravartinām . jyāmaghasya bandhyā-pateḥ aputrasya bhāryā-vaśyatvam eva putra-pradam āsīd iti vakṣyan kathām āha—atrādyāpi iti yāvat-samāpti . adhiṣṭhānaṃ nivāsam ||१६||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

द्वादशे क्रोष्ट्र-सञ्ज्ञस्य यदु-पुत्रस्य सन्ततिः वंश-कृद् अवर्ण्यते यत्र ज्यामघः स्त्री-जिताग्रणीः । चतुर्दश महान्ति रत्नानि यस्य सः । रत्नानि चात्र स्व-स्व-जाति-श्रेष्ठानि धर्म-संहितोक्तानि— चक्रं रथो मणिः खड्गश् चर्म रत्नं च पञ्चमम् । केतुर् निधिश् च सप्तैव प्राण-हीनानि चक्षते ॥ भार्या पुरोहितश् चैव सेनानी रथकृच् च यः । पत्यश्वौ कलभाश् चेति प्राणिनः सप्त कीर्तिताः ॥ चतुर्दशेति रत्नानि सर्वेषां चक्रवर्तिनाम् । ज्यामघस्य बन्ध्या-पतेः अपुत्रस्य भार्या-वश्यत्वम् एव पुत्र-प्रदम् आसीद् इति वक्ष्यन् कथाम् आह—अत्राद्यापि इति यावत्-समाप्ति । अधिष्ठानं निवासम् ॥१६॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

All the enemies and kings ran away in all directions, having lost their wives and children, their relatives, wealth, treasure, etc.

VP 4.12.17

तस्मिंश्च विद्रुतेऽतित्रासलोलायतलोचनयुगलं त्राहित्राहि मां तातांब भ्रतरित्याकुलविलापविधुरं स राजकन्यारत्नमद्राक्षीत्

Jyaamagha saw an excellent princess there, whose eyes were rolling with fear. She was crying loudly - &quot;Save me, O father, O mother, O brother, save me&quot;.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tasmiṃśca vidrute'titrāsalolāyatalocanayugalaṃ trāhitrāhi māṃ tātāṃba bhratarityākulavilāpavidhuraṃ sa rājakanyāratnamadrākṣīt

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.12.18

तद्दर्शनाच्च तस्यामनुरागानुगतान्तरात्मा स नृपोऽचिन्तयत्

When he saw her, he developed good feelings towards her. and he thought like this.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अनुरागस्यानुगतोऽधीनोऽन्तरात्मा मनो यस्य सः ॥१८॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

When he saw her, he developed good feelings towards her. and he thought like this.

VP 4.12.19

साध्विदं ममापत्यरहितस्य वन्ध्याभर्तुः सांप्रतं विधिनाऽपत्यकारणं कन्यारत्नमुपपादितम्

This is a good thing which is happening here, I am without any children. My wife is sterile, has not given me any children. Out of chance, so that I can have children, I have got this princess.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sādhvidaṃ mamāpatyarahitasya vandhyābhartuḥ sāṃprataṃ vidhinā'patyakāraṇaṃ kanyāratnamupapāditam

No commentary.

VP 4.12.23

अथैनां रथमारोप्य स्वनगरमगच्छत्

He took her in his chariot alongside, and came back to return to his city.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

athaināṃ rathamāropya svanagaramagacchat

Śrīdhara

śaivyayāham anujñātaḥ samudvakṣyāmīti niścitatvāt śaivyānujñābhāve kanyā-ratnasya snuṣātve’py adoṣaḥ ||२३||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

शैव्ययाहम् अनुज्ञातः समुद्वक्ष्यामीति निश्चितत्वात् शैव्यानुज्ञाभावे कन्या-रत्नस्य स्नुषात्वेऽप्य् अदोषः ॥२३॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

He took her in his chariot alongside, and came back to return to his city.

VP 4.12.24

विजयिनं च राजानमशेषपौरभृत्वयरिजनामात्यसमेता शैव्या द्रष्टुमधिष्ठानद्वारमागता

Shaibyaa wanted to welcome him. So she came to the entrance of the city itself, to meet the victorious king who had returned, she came with all the people, ministers, servants, etc. She came to welcome the king.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

No commentary.

VP 4.12.25

सा चावलोक्य राज्ञः सव्यपार्श्ववर्त्तिनीं कन्यामीषदुद्भूतामर्षस्फुरदधरपल्लवा राजानमवोचत्

Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4 She immediately saw this princess next to the king, in his chariot, and got so jealous and angry, that her lips were quivering, and she told the king thus.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sā cāvalokya rājñaḥ savyapārśvavarttinīṃ kanyāmīṣadudbhūtāmarṣasphuradadharapallavā rājānamavocat

No commentary.

VP 4.12.27

असावप्यनालोचितोत्तरवचनोऽतिभयात्तामाह स्नुषा ममेयमिति

The king was so scared of his wife, that he had not thought of an answer for such a question. Without thinking, he immediately said that &quot;This is my daughter in law&quot;.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

asāvapyanālocitottaravacano'tibhayāttāmāha snuṣā mameyamiti

Śrīdhara

īṣad udbhūtāmarṣeṇa sphuran tad-adhara-pallavo yasyāḥ . anālocitam avicāritam uttara-vacanaṃ yena saḥ ||२७||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

ईषद् उद्भूतामर्षेण स्फुरन् तद्-अधर-पल्लवो यस्याः । अनालोचितम् अविचारितम् उत्तर-वचनं येन सः ॥२७॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

The king was so scared of his wife, that he had not thought of an answer for such a question. Without thinking, he immediately said that "This is my daughter in law".

VP 4.12.29

नाहं प्रसूता पुत्रेण नान्या पत्न्यभवत्तव । स्नुषासंबन्धता ह्येषा कतमेन सुतेन ते

O King. I do not have children, and you don't have any other wife. How can you have a daughter in law? Who is that son?

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nāhaṃ prasūtā putreṇa nānyā patnyabhavattava | snuṣāsaṃbandhatā hyeṣā katamena sutena te

Śrīdhara

nāhaṃ prasūtā apatyavatī . anyā ca putreṇa viśiṣṭā te patnī nābhavat . katamena sutena nimittena snuṣā-sambadhena upādhinā vācyā, api tu na kenāpīty ākṣepaḥ ||२९|| śrī-parāśara uvāca—

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

नाहं प्रसूता अपत्यवती । अन्या च पुत्रेण विशिष्टा ते पत्नी नाभवत् । कतमेन सुतेन निमित्तेन स्नुषा-सम्बधेन उपाधिना वाच्या, अपि तु न केनापीत्य् आक्षेपः ॥२९॥ श्री-पराशर उवाच—

Śrīdhara commentary.

O King. I do not have children, and you don't have any other wife. How can you have a daughter in law? Who is that son?

VP 4.12.30

इत्यात्मेर्ष्याकोपकलुषितवचनमुषितविवेको भयाद्दुरुक्तपरिहारार्थमिदमवनीपतिराह

Sri Paraasharar - Because of her words filled with jealousy and anger, he lost the right and wrong thinking, he did not know what he was telling, and out of that, what he just muttered, in order to justify that, he thought of some answer. So, the king told thus -

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ityātmerṣyākopakaluṣitavacanamuṣitaviveko bhayādduruktaparihārārthamidamavanīpatirāha

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.12.31

यस्ते जनिष्यत्यात्मजस्तस्येयमनागतस्यैव भार्या निरूपितेत्याकर्ण्योद्भूतमृदुहासा तथेत्याह

You are going to have a son. Even though he is not yet born, I thought that you will have a son, and I have brought this princess as his wife. Hearing that, she was extremely pleased, and said &quot;Very good&quot;. Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yaste janiṣyatyātmajastasyeyamanāgatasyaiva bhāryā nirūpitetyākarṇyodbhūtamṛduhāsā tathetyāha

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

Transliteration

VP 4.12.33

अनन्तरं चातिशुद्धलग्नहोरांशकावयवोक्तकृतपुत्रजन्मालापगुणाद्वयसः परिणाममुपगताऽपि शैव्या स्वल्पैरेवाहोभिर्गर्भमवाप

According to Jyoutisha, the time at which this conversation happened, was a very auspicious time, and was very good for having a son. Because of that auspicious time, though she had passed the age of having children, very soon, she became pregnant.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

anantaraṃ cātiśuddhalagnahorāṃśakāvayavoktakṛtaputrajanmālāpaguṇādvayasaḥ pariṇāmamupagatā'pi śaivyā svalpairevāhobhirgarbhamavāpa

Śrīdhara

anantaraṃ ca śaivyā garbham avāpety anvayaḥ . tatra hetuḥ--atiśuddhā ye lagna-horāṃśakāvayavās teṣūktaṃ tathāstv iti . astu devatayoktam uktis tena sahakṛto yaḥ śaivyā-rājābhyāṃ putra-janmātmakaḥ ālāpas tasya guṇāta sādaguṇyāta . astu devatākhyā kācid devatā hi śubhāśubha-vacanaṃ tathāstv iti anumodate, tatra rāśīnām udayo lagnaṃ rāśer arddhaṃ horāyā navamo bhāgo’ṃśakaḥ avayavaḥ dvādaśādiḥ, eteṣu śubheṣu uktaṃ kṛtaṃ cāvaśyaṃ bhavatīty arthaḥ ||३३||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अनन्तरं च शैव्या गर्भम् अवापेत्य् अन्वयः । तत्र हेतुः--अतिशुद्धा ये लग्न-होरांशकावयवास् तेषूक्तं तथास्त्व् इति । अस्तु देवतयोक्तम् उक्तिस् तेन सहकृतो यः शैव्या-राजाभ्यां पुत्र-जन्मात्मकः आलापस् तस्य गुणात सादगुण्यात । अस्तु देवताख्या काचिद् देवता हि शुभाशुभ-वचनं तथास्त्व् इति अनुमोदते, तत्र राशीनाम् उदयो लग्नं राशेर् अर्द्धं होराया नवमो भागोऽंशकः अवयवः द्वादशादिः, एतेषु शुभेषु उक्तं कृतं चावश्यं भवतीत्य् अर्थः ॥३३॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

According to Jyoutisha, the time at which this conversation happened, was a very auspicious time, and was very good for having a son. Because of that auspicious time, though she had passed the age of having children, very soon, she became pregnant.
