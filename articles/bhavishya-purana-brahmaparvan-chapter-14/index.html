Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 14 — Conduct During Absence and Relations Among Co-Wives

BP 1.14.1

ब्रह्मोवाच
प्रेषिते मण्डनं स्त्रीणां पत्यौ मङ्गलमात्रकम् ।
निष्पादनं च यत्नेन तदारब्धस्य कर्मणः ॥

Brahmā said: ‘When a husband has gone away, a woman’s adornment should be limited to what is auspicious, and she should diligently complete whatever work he began.

BP 1.14.2

शय्यामुपार्जनमर्थानां व्ययानां परिहापणम् ॥

She should maintain the sleeping arrangements, work to acquire or preserve household resources, and reduce unnecessary expenditure.

BP 1.14.3

व्रतोपवासतात्पर्यं तद्वार्तापरिमार्गणम् ।
दैवज्ञेक्षणिकप्रश्नो देवानामुपयाचनम् ॥

She should be intent upon vows and fasts, seek news of him, ask astrologers and diviners, and make supplications to the gods.

BP 1.14.4

नित्यं तस्यागमाशंसा क्षेमार्थं देवपूजनम् ।
न चात्युज्ज्वलवेषत्वं न सदा तैलधारणम् ॥

She should continually hope for his return and worship the gods for his welfare, without wearing excessively brilliant clothing or applying oil every day.

BP 1.14.5

ज्ञातिवेश्म न गन्तव्यं सकामगमनेन च ।
गुरूणामाज्ञया यावद्भर्तुराप्तजनैः सह ॥

She should not go to a relative’s house merely because she wishes to go; she may do so only with the elders’ permission and in the company of people trusted by her husband.

BP 1.14.6

तत्रापि न चिरं तिष्ठेत्स्नानादीन्वापि नाचरेत् ।
यावदर्थं क्षणं स्थित्वा ततः शीघ्रं समाचरेत् ॥

Even there she should not remain long or undertake bathing and similar routines. She should stay only as long as the purpose requires and then return quickly.

BP 1.14.7

आगते प्रकृतिस्थैव कृत्वा तात्कालिकं विधिम् ।
मुक्तप्रवासने पथ्ये स्नाते भुक्तवति प्रिये ॥

When her husband returns she should remain naturally composed and perform the immediate rites appropriate to his arrival; after he has put aside his travelling clothes, taken suitable refreshment, bathed, and eaten—

BP 1.14.8

आत्मानं समलङ्कृत्य सविशेषं मुदान्विता ।
देवपूजोपहारादीन्दद्यात्प्रागुपपादितान् ॥

—she should adorn herself especially and joyfully, and present the offerings for divine worship and other gifts that she had prepared in advance.

BP 1.14.9

कनिष्ठामातृवज्ज्येष्ठां तदपत्यानि चात्मवत् ।
पश्येत्तत्परिवर्गं तु नित्यं स्वपरिवर्गवत् ॥

A younger co-wife should regard the elder like a mother, the elder’s children like her own, and the elder’s whole circle continually like her own people.

BP 1.14.10

तत्पुरोनासने तिष्ठेत्पतिं नामंत्रयेत च ।
तदभिप्रायतः कुर्यात्प्रवृत्तिं सर्वकर्मसु ॥

She should not sit down in the elder wife’s presence before being invited, and in every activity should proceed according to the elder’s intention.

BP 1.14.11

न संसृजेत तद्द्विष्टैः सख्यं कुर्वीत तत्प्रियैः ।
जनमाप्ततमं तस्य सदाभर्तुश्च मानयेत् ॥

She should not associate with those whom the elder wife dislikes, but should form friendship with those dear to her and always honour the person most trusted by both her and the husband.

BP 1.14.12

पैतृकात्समुपानीतं वसुसौगंधिकादिकम् ।
तस्मै निवेद्यात्मतया तदा तदुपयोजयेत् ॥

Whatever property, perfumes, or other articles she brings from her paternal home should first be disclosed to the elder wife and then used from time to time as shared household property.

BP 1.14.13

सोपि तत्प्रीतये किंचिदादद्यादल्पमूल्यकम् ।
संगोप्य मातृवत्स्थेयं तत्तथैवोपयोजयेत् ॥

For affection’s sake, the elder wife may accept some token of small value from it. The younger should preserve the remainder under her maternal care and use it in the same spirit.

BP 1.14.14

तत्प्रीत्यर्थं गृहीतं यद्वैलक्ष्यादिनिवृत्तये ।
सविशेषं प्रसंगेन तस्यैतत्प्रतिपादयेत् ॥

Whatever the younger wife has accepted to please the elder or remove embarrassment should, when an occasion arises, be given back or presented to her with special consideration.

BP 1.14.15

स्त्रीणां यदेतत्सापत्न्यं परं मात्सर्यकारणम् ।
तस्मात्तत्परिहर्तव्यं परमोदारचर्यया ॥

Because co-wifehood is a powerful cause of jealousy among women, that jealousy should be avoided through conduct of exceptional generosity.

BP 1.14.16

तथा कल्पितनेपथ्या भर्तुः पर्यायवासरे ।
ह्रियमादयमानेव पतिं गच्छेद्विसर्जिता ॥

Properly dressed and adorned for her appointed night in the husband’s rotation, the younger wife should approach him when released to do so, while displaying becoming modesty.

BP 1.14.17

गत्वा रहसि भर्तारं तत्कालोचितसंभ्रमैः ।
तद्भावानुगतैस्तैस्तैः सविशेषमुपाचरेत् ॥

Having gone privately to her husband, she should serve him with special attentions suited to that time and responsive to his particular disposition.

BP 1.14.18

प्रतिबुध्य ततः काले सविशेषं त्रपान्विता ।
ज्येष्ठाय वसतिं गच्छेद्विशेषेण तथा पुनः ॥

On awakening at the proper time, she should return to the elder wife’s residence with particular modesty and, on later occasions, behave in the same way.

BP 1.14.19

अप्रातिकूल्यं ज्येष्ठाया हितमन्यत्र योषितः ।
ततः शनैस्त्ववच्छिद्य पतिं स्ववशमानयेत् ॥

Without acting contrary to the elder wife and while promoting the welfare of the other women, she may gradually distinguish herself and win her husband’s affection.

BP 1.14.20

बहिष्पाकादियोगेन चतुःषष्ट्या रहोगतम् ।
ज्येष्ठामतिशयानेव भर्तारमुपरञ्जयेत् ॥

Through food prepared separately and related attentions, and through the traditional sixty-four intimate arts in privacy, she may please her husband beyond the elder wife.

BP 1.14.21

प्रागल्भ्यं रहसि स्त्रीणां लज्जाधिक्यं ततोऽन्यदा ।
चित्तज्ञानानुवृत्तिश्च पत्यौ संसेवनं परम् ॥

Boldness in privacy, greater modesty at other times, understanding and following the husband’s mind, and devoted service to him are held supreme for women.

BP 1.14.22

एवमाराध्य भर्तारं गृहमाक्रम्य च क्रमात् ।
गौरवं प्रतिपत्तिं वा ज्येष्ठादिषु न हापयेत् ॥

Having pleased her husband in this way and then resumed her place in the household, she should not diminish the respect or courteous treatment due to the elder wife and others.

BP 1.14.23

गृहव्यापारदानेषु पतिं गुढं तधा वदेत् ।
अधिकुर्यादनिच्छन्ती ज्येष्ठैवेनां यथा बलात् ॥

In matters of household business and gifts she should speak to her husband discreetly; even if the younger wife is reluctant, the elder should formally delegate appropriate responsibility to her.

BP 1.14.24

सापि विज्ञाय भर्तारं कनिष्ठाकृष्टमानसम् ।
विश्रामं प्रार्थयेदेनामधिकुर्यात्सुतामिव ॥

The elder wife, recognizing that her husband’s mind is drawn toward the younger, should ask her for relief from duties and entrust responsibilities to her as though to a daughter.

BP 1.14.25

मत्वा भर्तुरभिप्रेतं रक्षन्ती निजगौरवम् ।
कृतं भर्त्रनुकूलं स्यात्तदिष्टायानुमोदयेत् ॥

Understanding what her husband desires while preserving her own dignity, the elder wife should approve whatever has been done in harmony with him and what pleases him.

BP 1.14.26

स्थानिनो यदभिप्रेतं भृत्यैः किं क्रियतेऽन्यथा ।
क्लिश्यते तत्र मूढात्मा परतन्त्रो वृथा जनः ॥

What can dependants do contrary to the wish of the one who holds authority? A foolish person who is dependent merely suffers there in vain.

BP 1.14.27

तस्मात्सर्वास्ववस्थासु मनोवाक्कायकर्मभिः ।
हितं स्वाम्यनुकूलत्वं नारीणां तु विशेषतः ॥

Therefore, in every condition, alignment with the master’s welfare through mind, speech, body, and action is held beneficial—especially for women in the source’s household model.

BP 1.14.28

सापि ज्येष्ठापतिं चैव गृहतन्त्रं च सर्वदा ।
समावर्ज्य गुणैर्धीरा प्रागवस्थां न विस्मरेत् ॥

The younger wife, in turn, should continually win over the elder wife, the husband, and the whole household order by her virtues; though composed and intelligent, she should not forget her former position.

BP 1.14.29

न सौभाग्यमदं कुर्यान्न चौद्धत्यादिविक्रियाम् ।
नितरामानतिं गच्छेत्सदानार्यभयादिव ॥

She should not become proud of her good fortune or display haughtiness and similar changes; she should remain especially deferential, as though continually mindful of the elder wife’s authority.

BP 1.14.30

यथा योग्यतया पत्यौ सौभाग्यमभिवर्धते ।
स्पर्धयेच्य कुलस्त्रीणां प्रश्रयोपाधिकं तथा ॥

As favour with the husband increases through suitability, so among women of established families even rivalry should remain qualified by humility and deference.

BP 1.14.31

एवमाराध्य भर्तारं तत्कार्येष्वप्रमादिनी ।
पूज्यानां पूजने नित्यं भृत्यानां भरणेषु च ॥

Having thus pleased her husband, remaining attentive to his affairs, continually honouring those worthy of honour, and providing for servants—

BP 1.14.32

गुणानामर्जने नित्यं शीलवत्परिरक्षणे ।
प्रेत्य चेह च निर्द्वन्द्वं सुखमाप्नोत्यनुत्तमम् ॥

—continually acquiring virtues and carefully protecting good character, she obtains unsurpassed happiness, free from conflict, both here and after death.
