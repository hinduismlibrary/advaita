VP 3.9.1

और्व उवाच— बालः कृतोपनयनो वेदाहरण-तत्परः।
गुरु-गेहे वसेद् भूप ब्रह्मचारी समाहितः॥१॥

After a boy has undergone the upanayana ceremony, he should devote himself to learning the Veda. He should live in his teacher’s house as a focused brahmacārin, O king.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
बालः कृत-उपनयनः (bālaḥ kṛta-upanayanaḥ) — a boy who has undergone initiation
वेद-आहरण-तत्परः (veda-āharaṇa-tatparaḥ) — devoted to learning the Veda
गुरु-गेहे वसेत् (guru-gehe vaset) — should live in the teacher’s house
भूप (bhūpa) — O king
ब्रह्मचारी (brahmacārī) — a brahmacārin
समाहितः (samāhitaḥ) — focused.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bālaḥ kṛtopanayano vedāharaṇa-tatparaḥ|guru-gehe vased bhūpa brahmacārī samāhitaḥ||

Śrīdhara

nirucya haritoṣārthān varṇadharmānaśeṣataḥ । tattadāśramadharmāśca navame’tra nirūpitāḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

निरुच्य हरितोषार्थान् वर्णधर्मानशेषतः । तत्तदाश्रमधर्माश्च नवमेऽत्र निरूपिताः

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Śrīdhara introduces the chapter by saying that, after fully explaining the duties of the varṇas as means of pleasing Hari, the ninth chapter now sets out the duties belonging to the several āśramas.

VP 3.9.2

शौचाचार-व्रतं तत्र कार्यं शुश्रूषणं गुरोः।
व्रतानि चरता ग्राह्यो वेदश् च कृत-बुद्धिना॥२॥

There he should maintain purity and proper conduct and serve his teacher. While observing the vows, he should study the Veda with a disciplined mind.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

तत्र (tatra) — there
कार्यम् (kāryam) — to be performed
शुश्रूषणम् गुरोः (śuśrūṣaṇam guroḥ) — service of the teacher

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

śaucācāra-vrataṃ tatra kāryaṃ śuśrūṣaṇaṃ guroḥ|vratāni caratā grāhyo vedaś ca kṛta-buddhinā||

Śrīdhara

vratāni prājāpatyaṃ tathā saumyamāgneyaṃ vaiśvadaivikam ityādīni madhumāṃsavarjanādīni ca caratā kurvatā vedo grāhyaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

व्रतानि प्राजापत्यं तथा सौम्यमाग्नेयं वैश्वदैविकम् इत्यादीनि मधुमांसवर्जनादीनि च चरता कुर्वता वेदो ग्राह्यः

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Śrīdhara glosses vratāni prājāpatyaṃ tathā saumyamāgneyaṃ vaiśvadaivikam ityādīni madhumāṃsavarjanādīni ca caratā kurvatā vedo grāhyaḥ as “The vows include the Prājāpatya, Saumya, Āgneya, and Vaiśvadeva observances, together with abstinence from honey, meat, and similar things. While practising these, the student should learn the Veda”.

VP 3.9.3

No commentary.

VP 3.9.4

स्थिते तिष्ठेद् व्रजेद् याते नीचैर् आसीत चासति।
संहितो गुरोर् नृप-श्रेष्ठ प्रतिकूलं न सञ्चरेत्

When the teacher stands, the student should stand; when he goes, the student should go; when he sits, the student should sit below him. O best of kings, the student should never act contrary to his teacher.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
स्थिते (sthite) — when he stands
तिष्ठेत् (tiṣṭhet) — should stand
व्रजति याते (vrajati yāte) — when he goes
नीचैः आसीत (nīcaiḥ āsīta) — should sit below
असति (asati) — when he sits
शिष्यः गुरोः (śiṣyaḥ guroḥ) — the teacher’s student
न सञ्चरेत् प्रतिकूलम् (na sañcaret pratikūlam) — should not act contrary.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sthite tiṣṭhed vrajed yāte nīcair āsīta cāsati|saṃhito guror nṛpa-śreṣṭha pratikūlaṃ na sañcaret

Śrīdhara

sthite ūrdhībhūte gurau śiṣyastiṣṭhet ūrdhībhavet, tathā āsati āsīne nīcaiḥ hīnabhāvenāsīta, pratikūlametadviparītaṃ na kuryāt

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

स्थिते ऊर्धीभूते गुरौ शिष्यस्तिष्ठेत् ऊर्धीभवेत्, तथा आसति आसीने नीचैः हीनभावेनासीत, प्रतिकूलमेतद्विपरीतं न कुर्यात्

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Śrīdhara glosses sthite as “when standing”. He glosses ūrdhībhūte gurau as “when the guru is elevated”. He glosses śiṣyas tiṣṭhet as “the student should stand”. He glosses ūrdhībhavet as “should be elevated”. He glosses tathā āsati as “likewise when he is standing”. He glosses āsīne as “when [the guru is] seated”. He glosses nīcaiḥ hīnabhāvena āsīta as “he should sit below, with humble disposition”. He glosses pratikūlam etad viparītaṃ na kuryāt as “he should not do this contrary opposite”.

VP 3.9.5

तेनैवोक्तं पठेद् वेदं नान्य-चित्तः पुरः-स्थितः।
अनुज्ञातश् च भिक्षान्नम् अश्नीयाद् गुरुणा ततः

He should recite the Veda only as instructed, with his attention undivided and seated before the teacher; once the teacher gives permission, he may then eat the food obtained by begging.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
तेन एव उक्तः (tena eva uktaḥ) — instructed by that very teacher
पठेत् वेदम् (paṭhet vedam) — should recite the Veda
न अन्य-चित्तः (na anya-cittaḥ) — with no other thought
पुरस्थितः (purasthitaḥ) — seated before him
अनुज्ञातः (anujñātaḥ) — permitted
भिक्षा-अन्नम् अश्नीयात् (bhikṣā-annam aśnīyāt) — should eat alms-food
गुरुना (gurunā) — by the teacher
ततः (tataḥ) — then.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tenaivoktaṃ paṭhed vedaṃ nānya-cittaḥ puraḥ-sthitaḥ|anujñātaś ca bhikṣānnam aśnīyād guruṇā tataḥ

Śrīdhara

tenaivoktaḥ paṭhet na tu śīghrādhyāpanāya guruṃ niyuñjīta

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तेनैवोक्तः पठेत् न तु शीघ्राध्यापनाय गुरुं नियुञ्जीत

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Śrīdhara glosses tenaivoktaḥ paṭhet na tu śīghrādhyāpanāya guruṃ niyuñjīta as “He should study the Veda exactly as taught, without appointing the teacher merely for rapid instruction”.

VP 3.9.6

VP 3.9.7

गृहीत-ग्राह्य-वेदश् च ततोऽनुज्ञाम् अवाप्य च।
गार्हस्थ्यम् आविशेत् प्राज्ञो निष्पन्न-गुरु-निष्कृतिः

After mastering the Veda he is meant to learn and obtaining his teacher’s permission, the wise student should enter household life, having repaid his debt to his teacher.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
गृहीत-ग्राह्य-वेदः (gṛhīta-grāhya-vedaḥ) — having mastered the Veda to be learned
ततः (tataḥ) — then
अनुज्ञाम् अवाप्य (anujñām avāpya) — having obtained permission
गार्हस्थ्यम् आविशेत् (gārhasthyam āviśet) — should enter household life
प्राज्ञः (prājñaḥ) — the wise
निष्पन्न-गुरु-निष्कृतिः (niṣpanna-guru-niṣkṛtiḥ) — having discharged the debt to the teacher.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

gṛhīta-grāhya-vedaś ca tato'nujñām avāpya ca|gārhasthyam āviśet prājño niṣpanna-guru-niṣkṛtiḥ

Śrīdhara

gṛhīto grāhyo grahaṇārho vedo yena saḥ । niṣpannaguruniṣkṛtiḥ dattagurudakṣiṇaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

गृहीतो ग्राह्यो ग्रहणार्हो वेदो येन सः । निष्पन्नगुरुनिष्कृतिः दत्तगुरुदक्षिणः

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Śrīdhara explains “one who has received the Veda” as one by whom the Veda fit to be learned has actually been learned. “One who has discharged his debt to the guru” means one who has given the guru-dakṣiṇā.

VP 3.9.8

विधिनावाप्त-दारस् तु धनं प्राप्य स्व-कर्मणा।

After taking a wife according to the rule and obtaining wealth through his own work, he should perform all the duties of a householder to the best of his ability, O king.

कुर्यात् (kuryāt) — should perform
शक्तितः (śaktitaḥ) — according to ability.
Transliteration

vidhināvāpta-dāras tu dhanaṃ prāpya sva-karmaṇā|gṛhastha-kāryam akhilaṃ kuryād bhūpāla śaktitaḥ||

VP 3.9.9

निवापेन पितॄन् अर्चन् यज्ञैर् देवांस् तथातिथीन्।
अन्नैर् मुनींश् चस्वाध्यायैर् अपत्येन प्रजापतिम्

By offerings he should honor the ancestors, by sacrifices the gods, by food the guests and sages, by study the seers, and through offspring Prajāpati.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
निवापेन (nivāpena) — by offerings
पितॄन् अर्चयन् (pitṝn arcayan) — honoring the ancestors
यज्ञैः देवान् (yajñaiḥ devān) — the gods through sacrifices
तथा अतिथीन् (tathā atithīn) — likewise guests
अन्नैः मुनीन् (annaiḥ munīn) — sages with food
स्वाध्यायैः (svādhyāyaiḥ) — through study
अपत्येन (apatyena) — through offspring
प्रजापतिं (prajāpatiṃ) — Prajāpati.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nivāpena pitṝn arcan yajñair devāṃs tathātithīn|annair munīṃś casvādhyāyair apatyena prajāpatim

Śrīdhara

nivāpena piṇḍadānādinā, pitṛniti pitṛyajño darśitaḥ, yajñairdevāniti devayajñaḥ, atithīnannairiti manuṣyayajñaḥ, munīṃśca svādhyāyairiti brahmayajñaḥ, apatyena prajāpatim iti prasaṅgād gṛhasthasyāvaśyakaṃ karttavyamityuktam

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

निवापेन पिण्डदानादिना, पितृनिति पितृयज्ञो दर्शितः, यज्ञैर्देवानिति देवयज्ञः, अतिथीनन्नैरिति मनुष्ययज्ञः, मुनींश्च स्वाध्यायैरिति ब्रह्मयज्ञः, अपत्येन प्रजापतिम् इति प्रसङ्गाद् गृहस्थस्यावश्यकं कर्त्तव्यमित्युक्तम्

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Śrīdhara glosses nivāpena piṇḍa-dāna-ādinā pitṝn iti pitṛ-yajñaḥ darśitaḥ as ““the ancestors by offerings” shows the ancestor sacrifice”. He glosses yajñaiḥ devān iti deva-yajñaḥ as ““the gods by sacrifices” is the god sacrifice”. He glosses atithīn annaiḥ iti manuṣya-yajñaḥ as ““guests by food” is the human sacrifice”. He glosses munīn ca svādhyāyaiḥ iti brahma-yajñaḥ as ““sages by study” is the Brahma sacrifice”. He glosses apatyena prajā-patim iti as ““Prajāpati by offspring””. He glosses prasaṅgāt gṛhasthasya āvaśyakam kartavyam iti uktam as “thus, incidentally, the householder's obligatory duties are stated”.

VP 3.9.10

भूतानि बलिभिश् चैव वैराग्य्लक्षणेनाखिलं जगत्।
प्राप्नोति लोकान् पुरुषो निज-कर्म-समर्जितान्॥१०॥

Through offerings to beings and kindness toward the whole world, a person obtains the worlds earned by his own actions.

वात्सल्येन (vātsalyena) — through kindness

Transliteration

bhūtāni balibhiś caiva vairāgylakṣaṇenākhilaṃ jagat|prāpnoti lokān puruṣo nija-karma-samarjitān||

Śrīdhara

balikarmaṇā bhūtānīti bhūtayajñaḥ । vāksatyena satyavacanena । vātsalyeneti pāṭhe dātṛtvenopakartṛtvena vā

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Śrīdhara identifies the offering to beings through bali as the Bhūta-yajña. “By truth of speech” means by speaking truth. On the variant reading vātsalyena, the sense is generosity or helpfulness.

VP 3.9.12

वेदाहरण-कार्याय तीर्थ-स्नानाय च प्रभो।
