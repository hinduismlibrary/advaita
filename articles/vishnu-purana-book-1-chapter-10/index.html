VP 1.10.1

कथितं मे त्वया सर्वं यत् पृष्ठोऽसि मया मुने।
भृगु-सर्गात् प्रभृत्य् एष सर्गो मे कथ्यतां पुनः

Maitreya said: All that I asked you, O sage, you have told me. Now tell me again the creation beginning with the creation of Bhṛgu.

Śrīdhara translation

The request is to hear the Bhṛgu lineage again in detail, so that the connection between the earlier and later accounts is clear.

VP 1.10.2

भृगोः ख्यात्यां समुत्पन्ना लक्ष्मीर् विष्णु-परिग्रहः।
तथा धातृ-विधातारौ ख्यात्यां जातौ सुतौ भृगोः

Parāśara said: Lakṣmī was born from Bhṛgu and Khyāti and became Viṣṇu’s consort. Khyāti also bore Bhṛgu the two sons Dhātṛ and Vidhātṛ.

Śrīdhara translation

The verse resumes the sons of Bhṛgu; viṣṇu-parigraha means “Viṣṇu’s wife.”

VP 1.10.3

आयतिर् नियतिश् चैव मेरोः कन्ये महात्मनः।
भार्ये धातृ-विधात्रोस् ते तयोर् जातौ सुताव् उभौ

Āyati and Niyati were the two daughters of the great Meru; they became the wives of Dhātṛ and Vidhātṛ, and each bore one son.

Śrīdhara translation

The two sons are one each: Prāṇa and Mṛkaṇḍu, born respectively to Āyati and Niyati.
