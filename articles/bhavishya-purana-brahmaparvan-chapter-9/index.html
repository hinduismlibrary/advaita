Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 9: Women’s Scriptural Eligibility and Household Duties

BP 1.9.1

ब्रह्मोवाच
एवं स्त्रीषु मनुष्याणां वृत्तिरुक्ता समासतः ।
साम्प्रतं च मनुष्येषु स्त्रीणां समुपदिश्यते ॥ १

Thus, briefly, the conduct of men toward women has been told. Now the conduct of women toward men is taught.

BP 1.9.2

सम्यगाराधनात्पुंसां रतिर्वृत्तिश्च योषितः ।
पुत्राः स्वर्गाद्यदृष्टं च तस्मादिष्टो हि तद्विधिः ॥ २

Through proper service by men come women's affection and conduct, sons, and unseen results beginning with heaven; therefore that procedure is desired.

BP 1.9.3

कर्तव्यं नाम यत्किञ्चित्सर्वं विधिमपेक्षते ।
व्यक्तिमायाति वैफल्यं तदेवारब्धमन्यथा ॥ ३

Whatever is to be done requires a procedure. The very undertaking begun otherwise comes to failure.

BP 1.9.4

विध्यपेक्षीणि सर्वाणि कार्याण्यविफलान्यपि ।
हेतुभूतास्त्रिवर्गस्य महारम्भा विशेषतः ॥४

All actions depend on procedure, even those that do not fail; especially great undertakings that are causes of the three aims of life.

BP 1.9.5

सर्वसाध्याविधिज्ञानमागमैकनिबन्धनम् ।
साध्यं दृष्टमदृष्टं च द्वयं विधिनिषेधयोः ॥ ५

Knowledge of the procedure for accomplishing every end rests only on sacred tradition. Both visible and unseen results are attained through injunctions and prohibitions.

BP 1.9.6

शास्त्राधिकारो न स्त्रीणां न ग्रन्थानां च धारणे ।
तस्मादिहान्ये मन्यन्ते तच्छासनमनर्थकम् ॥ ६

Women have no entitlement to study śāstra or retain its texts; therefore some consider that instruction meaningless.

BP 1.9.7

आगमैकक्रियायोगे स्त्रीणामध्यधिकारिता ।
मृते भर्तरि साध्वी स्यादित्यादौ स्मृतिभाषितम् ॥ ७

Women have some entitlement in rites grounded in sacred tradition. The remembered tradition states, among other things, that a faithful wife should remain so when her husband has died.

BP 1.9.8

तस्मात्कार्यमकार्यं वा विज्ञाय प्रभुरागमात् ।
गुणदोषेषु ताः सम्यक्छास्ति राजा प्रजा इव ॥ ८

Therefore, having learned from śāstra what should and should not be done, the master instructs them rightly concerning merits and faults, as a king instructs his subjects.

BP 1.9.9

सत्येव प्रमदाः काश्चिद्विशेषाधिगतागमाः ।
यत्तु शास्त्राधिकारित्वं वचनं स्यान्निरर्थकम् ॥ ९

Although some women have indeed specially learned sacred tradition, the statement that they are entitled to śāstra would be meaningless.

BP 1.9.10

केचिद्वेदविदो विप्राः कृत्यैर्वेषक्रियापराः ।
तथापि जातिमात्रेण त एवात्राधिकारिणः ॥.9.१०

Some Brahmins know the Veda and are intent on ritual acts and outward forms; nevertheless, here those same people are entitled by birth alone.

BP 1.9.11

क्रियन्ते वेदशास्त्रज्ञैः प्रयोगाः शास्त्रलौकिकाः ।
स्थितमेषामदूरेऽपि शास्त्रमेव निबन्धनम् ॥१

Those who know Veda and śāstra perform scriptural and customary rites; even when śāstra is not nearby, it remains their basis.

BP 1.9.12

व्याधधीवरगोपालप्रभृतीनां च दृश्यते ।
विष्ट्यं गारकसौर्यादिदिनानां परिवर्जनम् ॥२

Even among hunters, fishermen, cowherds, and the like, avoidance of inauspicious calendar days is seen.

BP 1.9.13

गम्यागम्यादिकार्येयं नियताचारसंस्थितिः ।
लोकानां शास्त्रवाक्यानां प्राणाः स्वेष्टनिबन्धनाः ॥३

The established practice concerning what is permitted or forbidden, and the like, is settled. The life of people and the words of śāstra depend on what each desires.

BP 1.9.14

तस्माच्चतुर्णां वर्णानामाश्रमाणां च सर्वशः ।
मुख्यगौणादिभेदानां ज्ञेया शास्त्राधिकारिता ॥४

Therefore, the authority of śāstra for all four varṇas and all stages of life, with primary, secondary, and other distinctions, is to be understood in every case.

BP 1.9.15

पौर्वापर्यं तु विज्ञातुमशक्यं लोकशास्त्रयोः ।
तच्छास्त्रमेव मन्तव्यं यथा कर्मशरीरवत् ॥५

It is impossible to know the priority between customary practice and śāstra; śāstra alone is to be considered, like action with the body.

BP 1.9.16

आगमे च पुराणे च द्विधैव नास्तिकग्रहम् ।
मार्गं महद्भिराचीर्णं प्रपद्येताविकल्पधीः ॥६

In sacred tradition and Purāṇa, one should not grasp at the twofold denial of the nonbeliever; with an undivided mind, one should follow the path trodden by the great.

BP 1.9.17

मूलं गृहस्थधर्माणां यस्मान्नार्यः पतिव्रताः ।
तस्मादासां प्रवक्ष्यामि भर्तुराराधने विधिम् ॥७

Since devoted wives are the root of household duties, I shall explain for them the procedure for serving the husband.
