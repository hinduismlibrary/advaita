Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 9: Women’s Scriptural Eligibility and Household Duties

BP 1.9.1

ब्रह्मोवाच
एवं स्त्रीषु मनुष्याणां वृत्तिरुक्ता समासतः ।
साम्प्रतं च मनुष्येषु स्त्रीणां समुपदिश्यते ॥ १

Brahmā said: Thus the conduct of men toward women has been stated briefly; now the conduct of women toward men is set forth.

BP 1.9.2

सम्यगाराधनात्पुंसां रतिर्वृत्तिश्च योषितः ।
पुत्राः स्वर्गाद्यदृष्टं च तस्मादिष्टो हि तद्विधिः ॥ २

Through proper service by men come women’s affection and way of life, sons, and the unseen [fruits] beginning with heaven; therefore that procedure is desired.

BP 1.9.3

कर्तव्यं नाम यत्किञ्चित्सर्वं विधिमपेक्षते ।
व्यक्तिमायाति वैफल्यं तदेवारब्धमन्यथा ॥ ३

‘Whatever is to be done depends upon a proper procedure. The very undertaking begun otherwise comes visibly to failure.’

BP 1.9.4

विध्यपेक्षीणि सर्वाणि कार्याण्यविफलान्यपि ।
हेतुभूतास्त्रिवर्गस्य महारम्भा विशेषतः ॥४

‘All undertakings, even those otherwise capable of success, depend upon procedure—especially great undertakings that serve as causes of the three aims of life.’

BP 1.9.5

सर्वसाध्याविधिज्ञानमागमैकनिबन्धनम् ।
साध्यं दृष्टमदृष्टं च द्वयं विधिनिषेधयोः ॥ ५

‘Knowledge of the procedure for accomplishing every end rests solely upon received scripture. Both visible and unseen results are objects of injunctions and prohibitions.’

BP 1.9.6

शास्त्राधिकारो न स्त्रीणां न ग्रन्थानां च धारणे ।
तस्मादिहान्ये मन्यन्ते तच्छासनमनर्थकम् ॥ ६

Women have no adhikāra in the śāstras or in retaining their texts. Therefore some consider that instruction meaningless.

BP 1.9.7

आगमैकक्रियायोगे स्त्रीणामध्यधिकारिता ।
मृते भर्तरि साध्वी स्यादित्यादौ स्मृतिभाषितम् ॥ ७

‘Yet women possess eligibility to perform rites grounded in received tradition; teachings in the Smṛti such as “when her husband has died, she should remain faithful” exemplify this.’

BP 1.9.8

तस्मात्कार्यमकार्यं वा विज्ञाय प्रभुरागमात् ।
गुणदोषेषु ताः सम्यक्छास्ति राजा प्रजा इव ॥ ८

‘Therefore, after learning from scripture what should and should not be done, the household’s responsible authority should instruct them properly concerning merits and faults, as a king instructs his subjects.’

BP 1.9.9

सत्येव प्रमदाः काश्चिद्विशेषाधिगतागमाः ।
यत्तु शास्त्राधिकारित्वं वचनं स्यान्निरर्थकम् ॥ ९

Though some women have indeed acquired special knowledge of āgama, the statement that they have adhikāra in the śāstras would be meaningless.

BP 1.9.10

केचिद्वेदविदो विप्राः कृत्यैर्वेषक्रियापराः ।
तथापि जातिमात्रेण त एवात्राधिकारिणः ॥.9.१०

Some brāhmaṇas know the Veda and are occupied with rites and forms; nevertheless, here they alone have adhikāra by birth alone.

BP 1.9.11

क्रियन्ते वेदशास्त्रज्ञैः प्रयोगाः शास्त्रलौकिकाः ।
स्थितमेषामदूरेऽपि शास्त्रमेव निबन्धनम् ॥१

Those who know the Vedaśāstras perform both prescribed and customary rites; even when the śāstra is not near, it remains their foundation.

BP 1.9.12

व्याधधीवरगोपालप्रभृतीनां च दृश्यते ।
विष्ट्यं गारकसौर्यादिदिनानां परिवर्जनम् ॥२

‘Even among hunters, fishermen, cowherds, and similar communities, one observes avoidance of days marked by Viṣṭi, Gara, Saura, and other calendrical conditions.’

BP 1.9.13

गम्यागम्यादिकार्येयं नियताचारसंस्थितिः ।
लोकानां शास्त्रवाक्यानां प्राणाः स्वेष्टनिबन्धनाः ॥३

‘The established custom governing what relations or actions are permitted and forbidden forms the vital principle of both popular practice and scriptural statements, each directed toward its desired end.’

BP 1.9.14

तस्माच्चतुर्णां वर्णानामाश्रमाणां च सर्वशः ।
मुख्यगौणादिभेदानां ज्ञेया शास्त्राधिकारिता ॥४

Therefore the śāstra-adhikāra of all four varṇas and āśramas, with its primary, secondary and other distinctions, is to be understood in every case.

BP 1.9.15

पौर्वापर्यं तु विज्ञातुमशक्यं लोकशास्त्रयोः ।
तच्छास्त्रमेव मन्तव्यं यथा कर्मशरीरवत् ॥५

‘It is impossible to determine temporal priority between customary life and scripture; scripture should therefore be regarded as inseparably related to practice, like action and the body through which it is done.’

BP 1.9.16

आगमे च पुराणे च द्विधैव नास्तिकग्रहम् ।
मार्गं महद्भिराचीर्णं प्रपद्येताविकल्पधीः ॥६

‘In both sacred tradition and Purāṇa, a twofold denial should not be seized upon as grounds for unbelief. With a mind free from indecision, one should follow the path trodden by the great.’

BP 1.9.17

मूलं गृहस्थधर्माणां यस्मान्नार्यः पतिव्रताः ।
तस्मादासां प्रवक्ष्यामि भर्तुराराधने विधिम् ॥७

Since devoted wives are the root of household dharma, I shall explain the procedure for their service to the husband.
