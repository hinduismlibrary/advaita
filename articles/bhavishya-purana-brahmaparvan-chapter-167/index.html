Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 167 — The Nikṣubhā and Sun Vow

BP 1.167.1

सुमंतुरुवाच ।। षष्ठ्यां चाप्यथ सप्तम्यां नियता ब्रह्मचारिणी ।। वर्षमेकं न भुंक्ते या महाभागजिगीषया ।। १ ।।

Sumantu said: The woman who, on the sixth and seventh lunar days, is disciplined and celibate and does not eat for one year, seeking great fortune.

BP 1.167.2

वर्षान्ते प्रतिमां कृत्वा निक्षुभाङ्केति विश्रुताम् ।। स्नानाद्यं च विधिं कृत्वा पूर्वोक्तं लभते गुणम् ।। २ ।।

At the year’s end, having made the image known as Nikṣubhāṅka and performed the bathing rite and the rest, she obtains the merit stated before.

BP 1.167.3

जाम्बूनदमयैर्यानैश्चतुर्द्वारैरलंकृते ।। गत्वादित्यपुरे रम्ये अशेषं विन्दते फलम् ।। ३ ।।

With vehicles of gold adorned with four doorways, she goes to the lovely city of the Sun and obtains the full fruit.

BP 1.167.4

सौरादिसर्वलोकेषु भोगान्भुक्त्वा यथेप्सितान् ।। क्रमादागत्य लोकेऽस्मिन् राजानं पतिमाप्नुयात् ।। ४ ।।

Having enjoyed desired pleasures in all worlds beginning with the solar worlds, she gradually returns to this world and obtains a king as husband.

BP 1.167.5

या नार्युपवसेदेवं कृष्णामेकां तु सप्तमीम् ।। सा गच्छेत्परमं स्थानं भानोरमिततेजसः ।। ५ ।।

A woman who thus fasts on one seventh lunar day of the dark fortnight goes to the highest abode of the Sun, of immeasurable splendor.

BP 1.167.6

वर्षान्ते प्रतिमां कृत्वा शालिपिष्टमयीं शुभाम् ।। पीतानुलेपनैर्माल्यैः पीतवस्त्रैश्च पूजयेत् ।। पूर्वोक्तमखिलं कृत्वा भास्कराय निवेदयेत् ।। ६ ।।

At the year’s end, having made an auspicious image of rice flour, one should worship it with yellow ointments, garlands, and yellow garments; having done everything stated before, one should offer it to the Sun.

BP 1.167.7

सप्तभीमैर्महायानैर्दन्तिचामीकरप्रभैः ।। वर्षकोटिशतं साग्रं सूर्यलोके महीयते ।। ७ ।।

With seven fearsome great vehicles gleaming like elephant ivory and gold, she is honored in the Sun’s world for a full billion years.

BP 1.167.8

सौरलोकादिलोकेषु भुक्त्वा भोगान्नराधिप ।। क्रमादागत्य लोकेऽस्मिन्यथेष्टं विन्दते पतिम् ।। सर्वलक्षणसम्पन्नं धनधान्यसमन्वितम् ।। ८ ।।

Having enjoyed pleasures in the worlds beginning with the Sun’s world, O ruler of men, she gradually returns to this world and obtains a husband as desired, complete with every mark and endowed with wealth and grain.

BP 1.167.9

कृष्णपक्षे तु सप्तम्यां या नारी तु दृढव्रता ।। ९ ।।

The woman who is steadfast in her vow on the seventh lunar day of the dark fortnight—

BP 1.167.10

वर्षान्ते सर्वगन्धाढ्यं निक्षुभार्कं निवेदयेत् ।। सुवर्णमणिमुक्ताभ्यां भोजयित्वा मगाङ्गनाम् ।। १० ।।

At the year’s end, one should offer the fragrant image of Nikṣubhā and the Sun, having fed a Maga woman and given her gold, gems, and pearls.

BP 1.167.11

सुविचित्रैर्महायानैर्दिव्यगन्धर्वशोभितैः ।। सा वै युगसहस्राणि सूर्यलोके नराधिप ।। ११ ।।

With richly varied great vehicles adorned with divine Gandharvas, she remains in the Sun’s world for a thousand ages, O ruler of men.

BP 1.167.12

यथेष्टं भानवे लोके भोगान्भुक्त्वा तु कृत्स्नशः ।। क्रमादागत्य लोकेऽस्मिन् राजन्तं विन्दते पतिम् ।। सर्वलक्षणसम्पन्नं धनधान्यसमन्वितम् ।। १२ ।।

Having enjoyed completely the desired pleasures in the Sun’s world, she gradually returns to this world and obtains a radiant husband, complete with every mark and endowed with wealth and grain.

BP 1.167.13

एवं या कुरुते राजन्व्रतं पापभयापहम् ।। निक्षुभार्कमिदं पुण्यं सा याति परमं पदम् ।। १३ ।।

Thus, the woman who performs this vow, which removes sin and fear, reaches the highest state through this meritorious Nikṣubhā and Sun observance, O king.

BP 1.167.14

वर्षमेकं महाबाहो श्रद्धया परयान्वितः ।। वर्षान्ते वै भोजयेद्वीर दाम्पत्यं भोजकेषु वै ।। १४ ।।

For one year, O mighty-armed one, with great faith; at the year’s end, O hero, one should feed a married pair among the Sun-priests.

BP 1.167.15

भोजयित्वा तु दाम्पत्यं भोगकानां महाबलैः ।। पूजयेद्गन्धमाल्यैस्तु वासोभिः कुरुनन्दन ।। १५ ।।

Having fed a married pair among the mighty Sun-priests, one should honor them with fragrances, garlands, and garments, O descendant of Kuru.

BP 1.167.16

कृत्वा ताम्रमये पात्रे वज्रपूर्णैरलंकृतम् ।। निक्षुभार्कं तु सौवर्णं दत्त्वा ताभ्यां तु शक्तितः ।। १६ ।।

Having made it in a copper vessel adorned with diamonds, one should give the golden image of Nikṣubhā and the Sun to the two according to one’s ability.

BP 1.167.17

निक्षुभा भोजिका ज्ञेया भोजकोऽर्कः प्रकीर्तितः ।। तस्मात्तौ पूजयेत्सौरीश्वरवच्छ्रद्धयान्वितः ।। १७ ।।

Nikṣubhā is to be known as the Sun-priestess, and the Sun-priest is declared to be the Sun; therefore, endowed with faith, one should worship those two like the lord of the Sun.
