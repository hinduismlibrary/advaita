Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 167 — The Nikṣubhā and Sun Vow

BP 1.167.1

सुमंतुरुवाच ।। षष्ठ्यां चाप्यथ सप्तम्यां नियता ब्रह्मचारिणी ।। वर्षमेकं न भुंक्ते या महाभागजिगीषया ।। १ ।।

Sumantu said: The woman who, on the sixth and seventh lunar days, is disciplined and celibate and does not eat for one year, seeking great fortune.

BP 1.167.6

BP 1.167.7
