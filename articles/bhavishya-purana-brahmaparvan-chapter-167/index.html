Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 167 — The Nikṣubhārka Vow

BP 1.167.1

सुमंतुरुवाच ।। षष्ठ्यां चाप्यथ सप्तम्यां नियता ब्रह्मचारिणी ।। वर्षमेकं न भुंक्ते या महाभागजिगीषया ।। १ ।।

Sumantu said: A woman who, on Ṣaṣṭhī and Saptamī, is disciplined and celibate, and does not eat for one year seeking great fortune.

BP 1.167.4

BP 1.167.6
