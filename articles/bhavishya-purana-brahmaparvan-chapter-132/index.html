Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 132 — The Marks and Proportions of Sūrya’s Image

BP 1.132.1

नारद उवाच
हन्त ते सर्वदेवानां प्रतिमालक्षणं परम्।
वच्मि ते यदुशार्दूल आदित्यस्य विशेषतः ॥

Nārada said: Now I shall tell you the supreme characteristics of images of all the gods, especially of Āditya, O tiger among the Yadus.

नारद उवाच(nārada uvāca) — Nārada said;
हन्त(hanta) — now, indeed;
ते(te) — to you;
सर्व-देवानाम्(sarva-devānām) — of all the gods;
प्रतिमा(pratimā) — image;
लक्षणम्(lakṣaṇam) — characteristics;
परम्(param) — supreme;
वच्मि(vacmi) — I shall tell;
यदुशार्दूल(yaduśārdūla) — O tiger among the Yadus;
आदित्यस्य(ādityasya) — of Āditya;
विशेषतः(viśeṣataḥ) — especially;

BP 1.132.2

एकहस्ता द्विहस्ता वा त्रिहस्ता वा प्रमाणतः।
तथा सार्द्धत्रिहस्ता च सवितुः प्रतिमा शुभा ॥

The image of Savitṛ, in measure, may be one, two, or three cubits, or likewise three and a half cubits; it is auspicious.

एक-हस्ता(eka-hastā) — one cubit;
द्वि-हस्ता(dvi-hastā) — two cubits;
वा(vā) — or;
त्रि-हस्ता(tri-hastā) — three cubits;
प्रमाणतः(pramāṇataḥ) — in measure;
तथा(tathā) — likewise;
सार्द्ध-त्रि-हस्ता(sārddha-tri-hastā) — three and a half cubits;
च(ca) — and;
सवितुः(savituḥ) — of Savitṛ;
प्रतिमा(pratimā) — image;
शुभा(śubhā) — auspicious;

BP 1.132.3

प्रसादाद्द्वारतो वापि प्रमाणं च प्रकल्पितम्।
तद्वत्प्रयाणं कर्तव्यं सततं शुभमिच्छता ॥

Its measure may also be determined from the temple or its doorway. One who always desires good should act accordingly.

प्रसादात्(prasādāt) — from the temple;
द्वारतः(dvārataḥ) — from the doorway;
वा(vā) — or;
अपि(api) — also;
प्रमाणम्(pramāṇam) — measure;
च(ca) — and;
प्रकल्पितम्(prakalpitam) — is determined;
तद्वत्(tadvat) — accordingly;
प्रयाणम्(prayāṇam) — procedure;
कर्तव्यम्(kartavyam) — should be followed;
सततम्(satatam) — always;
शुभम्(śubham) — auspiciousness;
इच्छता(icchatā) — by one desiring;

BP 1.132.4

एकहस्ता भवेत्सौम्या द्विहस्ता धनधान्यदा।
त्रिहस्ता प्रतिमा भानोः सर्वकामप्रदा स्मृता ॥

A one-cubit image is gentle; a two-cubit one gives wealth and grain. A three-cubit image of the Sun is held to grant every desire.

एक-हस्ता(eka-hastā) — one cubit;
भवेत्(bhavet) — may be;
सौम्या(saumyā) — gentle;
द्वि-हस्ता(dvi-hastā) — two cubits;
धन-धान्य-दा(dhana-dhānya-dā) — giver of wealth and grain;
त्रि-हस्ता(tri-hastā) — three cubits;
प्रतिमा(pratimā) — image;
भानोः(bhānoḥ) — of the Sun;
सर्व-काम-प्रदा(sarva-kāma-pradā) — granting every desire;
स्मृता(smṛtā) — is held to be;

BP 1.132.5

सार्धत्रिहस्ता प्रतिमा सुभिक्षक्षेमकारिणी।
अग्रे मध्ये च मूले च प्रतिमा सर्वतः समा।
गान्धर्वी सा तु विज्ञेया धनधान्यावहा स्मृता ॥

A three-and-a-half-cubit image brings abundant food and well-being. Its front, middle, and base are even on every side. It is known as the Gāndharvī and is remembered as bringing wealth and grain.

सार्ध-त्रि-हस्ता(sārdha-tri-hastā) — three and a half cubits;
प्रतिमा(pratimā) — image;
सुभिक्ष-क्षेम-कारिणी(subhikṣa-kṣema-kāriṇī) — bringing abundance and well-being;
अग्रे(agre) — at the front;
मध्ये(madhye) — in the middle;
च(ca) — and;
मूले(mūle) — at the base;
सर्वतः(sarvataḥ) — on every side;
समा(samā) — even;
गान्धर्वी(gāndharvī) — Gāndharvī;
सा(sā) — that;
तु(tu) — indeed;
विज्ञेया(vijñeyā) — is to be known;
धन-धान्य-आवहा(dhana-dhānya-āvahā) — bringing wealth and grain;
स्मृता(smṛtā) — is remembered;

BP 1.132.6

देवागारस्य यद्द्वारं तस्मादष्टांशमुद्यता।
विभागैः पिण्डिकाः कार्या द्वौ भागौ प्रतिमा भवेत् ॥

Its height should be one-eighth of the temple doorway’s measure. The pedestals should be made in divisions, with the image occupying two parts.

देव-आगारस्य(deva-āgārasya) — of the shrine;
यत्(yat) — whatever;
द्वारम्(dvāram) — doorway;
तस्मात्(tasmāt) — from that;
अष्ट-अंशम्(aṣṭa-aṃśam) — one eighth;
उद्यता(udyatā) — raised in height;
विभागैः(vibhāgaiḥ) — in divisions;
पिण्डिकाः(piṇḍikāḥ) — pedestals;
कार्याः(kāryāḥ) — are to be made;
द्वौ(dvau) — two;
भागौ(bhāgau) — parts;
प्रतिमा(pratimā) — image;
भवेत्(bhavet) — should be;

BP 1.132.7

अङ्गुलैश्च तथा मूर्तिश्चतुरशीतिसंमितैः।
विस्तारायामतः कार्या वदनं द्वादशाङ्गुलम् ॥

The image should measure eighty-four fingerbreadths in width and height; the face should be twelve fingerbreadths.

अङ्गुलैः(aṅgulaiḥ) — in fingerbreadths;
च(ca) — and;
तथा(tathā) — likewise;
मूर्तिः(mūrtiḥ) — image;
चतुरशीति-सम्मितैः(caturaśīti-sammitaiḥ) — measuring eighty-four;
विस्तार(vistāra) — in breadth;
आयामतः(āyāmataḥ) — and in height;
कार्या(kāryā) — is to be made;
वदनम्(vadanam) — face;
द्वादश-अङ्गुलम्(dvādaśa-aṅgulam) — twelve fingerbreadths;

BP 1.132.8

मुखात्त्रिभागैश्चिबुकं ललाटं नासिका तथा।
कर्णौ नासिकया तुल्यौ पादौ चानियतौ तयोः ॥

The chin, forehead, and nose are each one-third of the face. The ears are equal to the nose; the two feet are not fixed in relation to them.

मुखात्(mukhāt) — from the face;
त्रि-भागैः(tri-bhāgaiḥ) — in three parts;
चिबुकम्(cibukam) — chin;
ललाटम्(lalāṭam) — forehead;
नासिका(nāsikā) — nose;
तथा(tathā) — likewise;
कर्णौ(karṇau) — two ears;
नासिकया(nāsikayā) — with the nose;
तुल्यौ(tulyau) — equal;
पादौ(pādau) — two feet;
च(ca) — and;
अनियतौ(aniyatau) — not fixed;
तयोः(tayoḥ) — of those two;

BP 1.132.9

नयने द्व्यङ्गुले स्यातां त्रिभागा तारका भवेत्।
तृतीयतारकाभागात्कुर्याद्दृष्टिं विचक्षणः ॥

The eyes should be two fingerbreadths; the iris should be one-third. The discerning maker should fashion the gaze from one-third of the iris.

नयने(nayane) — two eyes;
द्वि-अङ्गुले(dvi-aṅgule) — two fingerbreadths;
स्याताम्(syātām) — should be;
त्रि-भागा(tri-bhāgā) — one third;
तारका(tārakā) — iris;
भवेत्(bhavet) — should be;
तृतीय(tṛtīya) — third;
तारका-भागात्(tārakā-bhāgāt) — from a part of the iris;
कुर्यात्(kuryāt) — should make;
दृष्टिम्(dṛṣṭim) — the gaze;
विचक्षणः(vicakṣaṇaḥ) — the discerning one;

BP 1.132.10

ललाटमस्तकोत्सेधं कुर्यात्तत्सममेव च।
परिणाहस्तु शिरसो भवेद्द्वाविंशदङ्गुलः ॥

The height of the forehead and head should be equal. The circumference of the head should be twenty-two fingerbreadths.

ललाट(lalāṭa) — forehead;
मस्तक(mastaka) — head;
उत्सेधम्(utsedham) — height;
कुर्यात्(kuryāt) — one should make;
तत्(tat) — that;
समम्(samam) — equal;
एव(eva) — indeed;
च(ca) — and;
परिणाहः(pariṇāhaḥ) — circumference;
तु(tu) — indeed;
शिरसः(śirasaḥ) — of the head;
भवेत्(bhavet) — should be;
द्वाविंशत्(dvāviṃśat) — twenty-two;
अङ्गुलः(aṅgulaḥ) — fingerbreadths;

BP 1.132.11

तुल्या नासिकया ग्रीवा मुखेन हृदयांतरम्।
मुखमात्रा भवेन्नाभिस्ततो मेढ्रमनन्तरम्।
मुखविस्तारणमुरस्ततोऽर्द्धं तु कटिः स्मृता ॥

The neck is equal to the nose; the distance from the mouth to the heart is one face-measure. The navel is one face-measure; next is the male organ. The chest is as wide as the face, and half that measure is said to be the waist.

तुल्या(tulyā) — equal;
नासिकया(nāsikayā) — to the nose;
ग्रीवा(grīvā) — neck;
मुखेन(mukhena) — by the face;
हृदय-अन्तरम्(hṛdaya-antaram) — distance to the heart;
मुख-मात्रा(mukha-mātrā) — one face-measure;
भवेत्(bhavet) — should be;
नाभिः(nābhiḥ) — navel;
ततः(tataḥ) — then;
मेढ्रम्(meḍhram) — generative organ;
अनन्तरम्(anantaram) — next;
मुख-विस्तारणम्(mukha-vistāraṇam) — breadth of the face;
उरः(uraḥ) — chest;
ततः(tataḥ) — then;
अर्द्धम्(arddham) — half;
कटिः(kaṭiḥ) — waist;
स्मृता(smṛtā) — is prescribed;

BP 1.132.12

बाहू प्रवाहतुल्यौ तु ऊरू जङ्घे च तत्समे।
गुल्फाधस्तात्तु पादः स्यादुच्छ्रितश्चतुरङ्गुलः ॥

The arms should equal the outstretched span; the thighs and shanks should likewise be equal. Below the ankles, the foot should rise four fingerbreadths.

बाहू(bāhū) — arms;
प्रवाह-तुल्यौ(pravāha-tulyau) — equal to the span;
तु(tu) — indeed;
ऊरू(ūrū) — thighs;
जङ्घे(jaṅghe) — shanks;
च(ca) — and;
तत्-समे(tat-same) — equal to them;
गुल्फ-अधस्तात्(gulpha-adhastāt) — below the ankles;
तु(tu) — indeed;
पादः(pādaḥ) — foot;
स्यात्(syāt) — should be;
उच्छ्रितः(ucchritaḥ) — raised;
चतुर्-अङ्गुलः(catur-aṅgulaḥ) — four fingerbreadths;

BP 1.132.13

षडङ्गुलसुविस्तारस्तस्याङ्गुष्ठाङ्गुलत्रयम्।
प्रदेशिनी च तत्तुल्या हीना शेषा नखैर्युताः ॥

Its breadth should be six fingerbreadths; the great toe, three fingerbreadths. The second toe should equal it; the remaining toes should be shorter and furnished with nails.

षट्-अङ्गुल(ṣaṭ-aṅgula) — six fingerbreadths;
सु-विस्तारः(su-vistāraḥ) — well-proportioned in breadth;
तस्य(tasya) — of it;
अङ्गुष्ठ(aṅguṣṭha) — great toe;
अङ्गुल-त्रयम्(aṅgula-trayam) — three fingerbreadths;
प्रदेशिनी(pradeśinī) — second toe;
च(ca) — and;
तत्-तुल्या(tat-tulyā) — equal to it;
हीना(hīnā) — shorter;
शेषाः(śeṣāḥ) — the remaining ones;
नखैः(nakhaiḥ) — with nails;
युताः(yutāḥ) — provided;

BP 1.132.14

चतुर्दशाङ्गुलः पाद आयामात्परिकीर्तितः।
एवं लक्षणसंयुक्ता प्रतिमार्च्या भवेत्सदा ॥

The foot is declared to be fourteen fingerbreadths in length. An image endowed with these characteristics should always be worshipped.

चतुर्दश-अङ्गुलः(caturdaśa-aṅgulaḥ) — fourteen fingerbreadths;
पादः(pādaḥ) — foot;
आयामात्(āyāmāt) — in length;
परिकीर्तितः(parikīrtitaḥ) — is declared;
एवम्(evam) — thus;
लक्षण-संयुक्ता(lakṣaṇa-saṃyuktā) — endowed with the proper marks;
प्रतिमा(pratimā) — image;
अर्च्या(arcyā) — is to be worshipped;
भवेत्(bhavet) — should be;
सदा(sadā) — always;

BP 1.132.15

अंसौ हरेस्तथैवोरू ललाटं च सनासिकम्।
नियते नयने गण्डौ मूर्तेः कुर्यात्समुन्नते ॥

One should fashion the shoulders, thighs, forehead, and nose of Hari’s image; its eyes should be steady and its cheeks raised.

अंसौ(aṃsau) — two shoulders;
हरेः(hareḥ) — of Hari;
तथा(tathā) — likewise;
ऊरू(ūrū) — thighs;
ललाटम्(lalāṭam) — forehead;
च(ca) — and;
स-नासिकम्(sa-nāsikam) — with the nose;
नियते(niyate) — steady;
नयने(nayane) — two eyes;

मूर्तेः(mūrteḥ) — of the image;
कुर्यात्(kuryāt) — one should fashion;
समुन्नते(samunnate) — raised;

BP 1.132.16

विशालधवलावामपक्ष्मलायतलोचने।
सस्मिताननपद्मस्य चारुबिम्बाधरस्तथा ॥

Its eyes should be large, bright, long-lashed, and extended; its lotus-face should be smiling, and its lower lip lovely like the bimba fruit.

BP 1.132.17

रत्नप्रोद्भासिमुकुटकटकाङ्गदहारवान्।
अव्यङ्गपदमध्यादिसमायोगोऽपि शोभितः ॥

BP 1.132.18

सुप्रभो मण्डलश्चारुर्विचित्रमणिकुण्डलः।
कराभ्यां काञ्चनीं मालां प्रोद्वहन्ससरोरुहाम् ॥

BP 1.132.19

एवं लक्षणसंयुक्तां कारयेदीहितप्रदाम्।
प्रजाभ्यश्च सदा भानुः शिवारोग्याभयप्रदः ॥

One should have it made with these characteristics and granting desired ends. The Sun always gives auspiciousness, health, and freedom from fear to the people.

एवम्(evam) — thus;
लक्षण-संयुक्ताम्(lakṣaṇa-saṃyuktām) — endowed with characteristics;

सदा(sadā) — always;

शिव(śiva) — well-being;
आरोग्य(ārogya) — health;
अभय-प्रदः(abhaya-pradaḥ) — giver of fearlessness;

BP 1.132.20

अल्पाङ्गायां नृपभयं हीनाङ्गायामकल्पता।
खातोदर्यां च क्षुत्पीडा कृशायां तु दरिद्रता ॥

An image with short limbs portends fear from the king; one with deficient limbs, incapacity. A hollow-bellied image brings hunger, while a thin one brings poverty.

अल्प-अङ्गायाम्(alpa-aṅgāyām) — in one with short limbs;
नृप-भयम्(nṛpa-bhayam) — fear from the king;
हीन-अङ्गायाम्(hīna-aṅgāyām) — in one with deficient limbs;
अकल्पता(akalpatā) — incapacity;
खात-उदर्याम्(khāta-udaryām) — in one hollow-bellied;
च(ca) — and;
क्षुत्-पीडा(kṣut-pīḍā) — affliction by hunger;
कृशायाम्(kṛśāyām) — in one thin;
तु(tu) — indeed;
दरिद्रता(daridratā) — poverty;

BP 1.132.21

सक्षतायां भयं शस्त्रात्स्फुटिता मृत्युकारिणी।
दक्षिणावनतायां तु शश्वदायुः क्षयो भवेत् ॥

An image with wounds portends danger from weapons; one split or cracked causes death. If it bends to the right, there will continually be loss of life.

आयुः(āyuḥ) — life span;
क्षयः(kṣayaḥ) — loss;
भवेत्(bhavet) — would occur;

BP 1.132.22

उत्तरावनतायां तु वियोगो भवति ध्रुवम्।

BP 1.132.23

तस्माद्भास्करभक्तेन लोकद्वयहितैषिणा।

BP 1.132.24

BP 1.132.26

कमलोदरकान्तिनिभः कञ्चुकगुप्तः प्रसन्नमुखः।

BP 1.132.27

BP 1.132.29

वामोन्नता तु पत्नीं दक्षिणावनता हिनस्त्यायुः।
