Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 101 — Bhadra Saptamī

BP 1.101.1

ब्रह्मोवाच। शुक्लपक्षे तु सप्तम्यां नक्षत्रं सवितुर्भवेत्। यदा प्रथमता चैव तदा वै भद्रतां व्रजेत् ॥

Brahmā said: When, on the seventh day of the bright fortnight, Savitṛ's constellation is present and it is in its first position, then the day attains the state of Bhadra.

Word-for-word

vai — indeed;
bhadratām — the state of Bhadra;
vrajet — it attains.
Transliteration
brahmovāca | śuklapakṣe tu saptamyāṃ nakṣatraṃ saviturbhavet | yadā prathamatā caiva tadā vai bhadratāṃ vrajet || 1 ||

तस्माद्धारय तद्वीर न त्याज्यं सप्तमीव्रतम्। त्यजमानो भवेद्वीर आरूढपतितो नरः ॥

Therefore maintain it, O hero; the Saptamī observance must not be abandoned. The person who abandons it becomes one who has fallen after ascending, O hero.

BP 1.101.25

श्रावयेद्यस्तु भक्त्या च सप्तमीकल्पमादितः। सोऽश्वमेधफलं प्राप्य ततो याति परं पदम् ॥

Whoever causes the Saptamī observance to be heard with devotion from the beginning obtains the fruit of the Aśvamedha and then goes to the highest state.
