Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 101 — The Auspicious Seventh Day Vow

BP 1.101.1

ब्रह्मोवाच। शुक्लपक्षे तु सप्तम्यां नक्षत्रं सवितुर्भवेत्। यदा प्रथमता चैव तदा वै भद्रतां व्रजेत् ॥

Brahmā said. When, on the seventh day of the bright fortnight, the constellation of Savitṛ holds the first position, the day becomes auspicious.

BP 1.101.2

स्नपनं तत्र देवस्य घृतेन कथितं बुधैः। क्षीरेण च तथा वीर पुनरिक्षुरसेन च ॥

There, the wise declare the bathing of the deity with clarified butter, milk, and likewise with sugarcane juice, O hero.

BP 1.101.3

स्नापयित्वा तु देवेशं चन्दनेन विलेपयेत्। दग्ध्वा तु गुग्गुलं तस्य दद्याद्भद्रं तथाग्रतः ॥

Having bathed the Lord of the gods, one should smear him with sandalwood. Having burned fragrant gum resin, one should place the auspicious offering before him.

BP 1.101.4

गोधूमचूर्णं निवपन्विमलं शशिसन्निभम्। सवज्रं सगुडं चैव रक्तपुष्पोपशोभितम् ॥

One should scatter pure wheat flour, like the moon, with a diamond and jaggery, adorned with red flowers.

BP 1.101.5

यदस्य शृङ्गमीशानं तत्र वै मौक्तिकं न्यसेत्। यदाग्नेयं तत्र माणिक्यं न्यसेद्वा लोहितं मणिम् ॥

In its northeastern corner one should place a pearl. In its southeastern corner one should place a ruby or a red gem.

BP 1.101.6

नैरृत्ये मकरं दद्याद्वायव्ये पद्मरागिणम्। गाङ्गेयमन्ततस्तस्य स्वशक्त्या विन्यसेद्बुधः ॥

In the southwestern corner one should place a sea monster, and in the northwestern corner a red jewel. The wise person should place gold from the Ganges in its center according to ability.

BP 1.101.7

चतुर्थ्यामेकभक्तं तु पञ्चम्यां नक्तमादिशेत्। षष्ठ्यामयाचितं प्रोक्तं उपवासो ह्यतः परः ॥

On the fourth day, one meal is prescribed. On the fifth, food at night. On the sixth, food obtained without asking is prescribed. After that, one should fast.

BP 1.101.8

पाषण्डिनो विकर्मस्थान्बैडालव्रतिकान्त्यजान्। सप्तम्यां पालयेत्प्राज्ञो दिवा स्वापं विवर्जयेत् ॥

On the seventh day, the wise person should avoid heretics, those engaged in forbidden acts, cat vow keepers, and outcasts. One should avoid sleeping during the day.

BP 1.101.9

अनेन विधिना यस्तु कुर्याद्वै भद्रसप्तमीम्। तस्मै भद्राणि सर्वाणि यच्छन्ति ऋभवः सदा ॥

Whoever performs the auspicious seventh day according to this procedure, the Rbhus always grant him every blessing.

BP 1.101.10

भद्रं ददाति यस्त्वस्यां भद्रस्तस्य सुतो भवेत्। भद्रमासाद्य भूतेश सदा भद्रेण तिष्ठति ॥

Whoever gives the auspicious offering on this day has an auspicious son. Having attained good fortune, O Lord of beings, he always remains in good fortune.

BP 1.101.11

दिण्डिरुवाच। कोऽयं भद्र इति प्रोक्तः कथं कार्यं प्रभूषणम्। दत्त्वा च किं फलं विद्याद्विधिना केन दीयते ॥

Diṇḍi said. Explain what this auspicious object is, how the ornament should be made, what fruit comes from giving it, and by what procedure it is given.

BP 1.101.12

ब्रह्मोवाच। व्योम भद्रमिति प्रोक्तं देवचिह्नमनूपमम्। यद्धृत्वेह नरः सूर्यं मुच्यते सर्वकिल्बिषैः ॥

Brahmā said. The sky is called the auspicious object, an incomparable mark of the gods. A person who wears it here is freed from every sin through the Sun.

BP 1.101.13

शालिपिण्डमयं कार्यं चतुष्कोणमनूपमम्। गव्येन सर्पिषा युक्तं खण्डशर्करयान्वितम् ॥

It should be made from a lump of fine rice, four cornered and incomparable, joined with ghee from a cow and sugar candy.

BP 1.101.14

चातुर्जातकपूर्णं तु द्राक्षाभिश्च विशेषतः। नालिकेरफलैश्चैव सुगन्धं च गणाधिप ॥

It should be filled with four spices, especially grapes, and also coconuts, and be fragrant, O lord of the hosts.

BP 1.101.15

मध्येन्द्रनीलं भद्रस्य न्यसेत्प्राज्ञः स्वशक्तितः। पुष्परागं मरकतं पद्मरागं तथैव च ॥

The wise person should place a blue sapphire in the center of the auspicious emblem according to ability, and likewise a yellow sapphire, an emerald, and a red ruby.

BP 1.101.16

अनौपम्यं च माणिक्यं क्रमात्कोणेषु विन्यसेत्। वाचकायाथ वा दद्यादथ वा भोजके स्वयम् ॥

An incomparable ruby should be placed successively in the corners. Then one should give it to the reciter, or to the priest, or eat it oneself.

BP 1.101.17

अनेन विधिना यस्तु कृत्वा भद्रं प्रयच्छति। स हि भद्राणि सम्प्राप्य गच्छेद्गोपतिमन्दिरम् ॥

Whoever makes and gives this auspicious object according to this procedure obtains every blessing and goes to the temple of the Lord of cows.

BP 1.101.18

ब्रह्मलोकं ततो गच्छेद्यानारूढो न संशयः। तेजसा गोजसंकाशः कांत्या गोजसमस्तथा ॥

Thereafter, mounted on a vehicle, he surely goes to the world of Brahmā. In radiance he resembles a ray of light, and in beauty he is likewise like a ray of light.

BP 1.101.19

प्रभया गोपतेस्तुल्य ऊर्जसा गोपरस्य च। तस्मादेत्य पुनर्भूमौ गोपतिः स्यान्न संशयः। प्रसादाद्गोपतेर्वीर सर्वज्ञाधिपपूजितः ॥

He is equal in radiance to the Lord of cows and in strength to the cow protector. Returning to earth, he surely becomes a lord of cows by the grace of the Lord of cows, honored by the ruler of all knowers, O hero.

BP 1.101.20

इत्येषा कथिता भीम भद्रा नामेति सप्तमी। यामुपोष्य नरो भीम ब्रह्मलोकमवाप्नुयात् ॥

Thus, O Bhīma, this seventh day called the Auspicious One has been told. By fasting on it, a person attains the world of Brahmā, O Bhīma.

BP 1.101.21

शृण्वन्ति ये पठन्तीह कुर्वन्ति च गणाधिप। ते सर्वे भद्रमासाद्य यान्ति तद्ब्रह्म शाश्वतम् ॥

Those who hear, read, and perform it here, O lord of the hosts, all attain blessings and go to the eternal Absolute.

BP 1.101.22

सुमन्तुरुवाच। इत्युक्तवान्पुरा ब्रह्मा दिण्डिने सप्तमीव्रतम्। मयाप्युक्तं तव विभो यथाज्ञातं यथाश्रुतम् ॥

Sumantu said. Formerly, Brahmā spoke this seventh day vow to Diṇḍi. I have also told it to you, O Lord, as I have known and heard it.

BP 1.101.23

गृहीत्वा सप्तमीकल्पं मानवो यस्तु भूतले। त्यजेत्कामाद्भयाद्वापि स ज्ञेयः पतितोऽबुधः ॥

A person on earth who, having undertaken this seventh day observance, abandons it from desire or fear is to be known as fallen and foolish.

BP 1.101.24

तस्माद्धारय तद्वीर न त्याज्यं सप्तमीव्रतम्। त्यजमानो भवेद्वीर आरूढपतितो नरः ॥

Therefore, maintain it, O hero. The seventh day vow must not be abandoned. A person who abandons it becomes one who has fallen after ascending, O hero.

BP 1.101.25

श्रावयेद्यस्तु भक्त्या च सप्तमीकल्पमादितः। सोऽश्वमेधफलं प्राप्य ततो याति परं पदम् ॥

Whoever causes this seventh day observance to be heard from the beginning with devotion obtains the fruit of a horse sacrifice and then goes to the highest state.
