VP 5.8.1

गाः पालयन्तौ च पुनः सहितौ बलकेशवौ ।

Sri Paraasharar - Once, when they were wandering around, Balaraama and Keshava, looking after the cows, were wandering around in that forest, went to the beautiful palm grove.
