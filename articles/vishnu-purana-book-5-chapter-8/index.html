VP 5.8.1

गाः पालयन्तौ च पुनः सहितौ बलकेशवौ ।
भ्रममाणौ वने तस्मिन् रम्यं तालवनं गतौ

Sri Paraasharar - Once, when they were wandering around, Balaraama and Keshava, looking after the cows, were wandering around in that forest, went to the beautiful palm grove.

Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

gāḥ pālayantau ca punaḥ sahitau balakeśavau | bhramamāṇau vane tasmin ramyaṃ tālavanaṃ gatau

Śrīdhara commentary.

VP 5.8.2

तत्तु तालवनं दिव्यं धेनुको नाम दानवः ।
मृगमांसकृताहारः सदाऽध्यास्ते खराकृतिः

There was a fierce demon there, called Dhenuka, and he was there, having taken the form of a donkey. He had made that as his place of living. He was eating the flesh of deer and other animals which were there. That wonderful palm grove was completely controlled by him.

Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tattu tālavanaṃ divyaṃ dhenuko nāma dānavaḥ | mṛgamāṃsakṛtāhāraḥ sadā’dhyāste kharākṛtiḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No Śrīdhara commentary text is supplied here.

VP 5.8.3

तत्तु तालवनं पक्वफलसंपत्समन्वितम् ।
दृष्ट्वा स्पृहान्विता गोपाः फलादानेऽब्रुवन्वचः

That palm grove was completely filled with ripe fruits. Having seen that, all the gopa boys wanted to eat those fruits. They told Krishna to please go there and fetch the fruits for them.

Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tattu tālavanaṃ pakvaphalasaṃpatsamanvitam | dṛṣṭvā spṛhānvitā gopāḥ phalādāne’bruvanvacaḥ

Śrīdhara

tat tv iti | phalānām ādāne spṛhānvitāḥ santaḥ abruvan || 3 || gopā ūcuḥ—

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तत् त्व् इति । फलानाम् आदाने स्पृहान्विताः सन्तः अब्रुवन् ॥३॥

गोपा ऊचुः—

Śrīdhara commentary.

No separate English commentary is supplied.

VP 5.8.4

गोपा ऊचुः
हे राम हे कृष्ण सदा धेनुकेनैष रक्ष्यते ।
भूप्रदेशो यतस्तस्मात्पक्वानीमानि संति वै

Gopaas - O Krishna, O Balaraama, Dhenuka raakshasa is protecting this taala vana, palm grove, and that is why these ripe fruits are there. These fruits are so nice, we want to have them.

Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

gopā ūcuḥ he rāma he kṛṣṇa sadā dhenukenaiṣa rakṣyate | bhūpradeśo yatastasmātpakvānīmāni saṃti vai

VP 5.8.5

फलानि पश्य तालानां गन्धामोदितदिंशि वै ।
वयमेतान्यभीप्सामः पात्यन्तां यदि रोचते

Look at all those fruits, O Balaraama, O Krishna. The fragrance of those fruits has spread all over in all directions. We are interested in eating them, please go and do get some fruits for us.

Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

phalāni paśya tālānāṃ gandhāmoditadiṃśi vai | vayametānyabhīpsāmaḥ pātyantāṃ yadi rocate

Śrīdhara

phalānīti | gandhāmodita-diṃśigandhenāmoditāḥ surabhīkṛtā diśo yais tāni phalāni paśya | gandhāmodita-diśānīti pāṭhe ṭāb-anto diśā-śabdaḥ | yeṣāṃ gandhena āmodavatyo diśās tānīty arthaḥ || 5 || śrī-parāśara uvāca—

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

फलानीति । गन्धामोदित-दिंशिगन्धेनामोदिताः सुरभीकृता दिशो यैस् तानि फलानि पश्य । गन्धामोदित-दिशानीति पाठे टाब्-अन्तो दिशा-शब्दः । येषां गन्धेन आमोदवत्यो दिशास् तानीत्य् अर्थः ॥५॥

श्री-पराशर उवाच—

Śrīdhara commentary.

No separate English commentary is supplied.

VP 5.8.6

इति गोपकुमाराणां श्रुत्वा संकर्षणो वचः ।
एतत्कर्तव्यमित्युक्त्वा पातयामास तानि वै ।
कृष्णश्च पातयामास भुवितानि फलानि वै

Sri Paraasharar - Balaraama immediately said - "Yes, yes, we have to do this; all our friends are very desirous of having these fruits, we should get it for them". And he started to bring down the fruits. Even Krishna started plucking the fruits and putting them down on the ground.

Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

iti gopakumārāṇāṃ śrutvā saṃkarṣaṇo vacaḥ | etatkartavyamityuktvā pātayāmāsa tāni vai | kṛṣṇaśca pātayāmāsa bhuvitāni phalāni vai

Śrīdhara

itīti | pātayāmāsa bāhubhyām iti vijñeyaṃ, bāhubhyāṃ tālāṃs tān parikampayan [bhā.pu. 10.15.29] iti śukokteḥ || 6 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

इतीति । पातयामास बाहुभ्याम् इति विज्ञेयं, बाहुभ्यां तालांस् तान् परिकम्पयन् [भा.पु. १०.१५.२९] इति शुकोक्तेः ॥६॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

No separate English commentary is supplied.

VP 5.8.7

फलानां पततां शब्दमाकर्ण्य सुदुरासदः ।
आजगाम स दुष्टात्मा कोपाद्दैतेयगर्दभः

That demon in the form of a donkey, having heard all the sound of the fruits falling down from the tree, felt very angry, and came immediately, that wicked demon.

Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

phalānāṃ patatāṃ śabdamākarṇya sudurāsadaḥ | ājagāma sa duṣṭātmā kopāddaiteyagardabhaḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No Śrīdhara commentary text is supplied here.

VP 5.8.8

पद्भ्यामुभाभ्यां स तदा पश्चिमाभ्यां बलं बली ।
जघानोरसि ताभ्यां च स च तेनाभ्यगृह्यत

He started kicking Them with the hind legs. He kicked Them on Their chest, with lot of force, and then, Balaraama caught hold of both the hind legs.

Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

padbhyāmubhābhyāṃsatadāpaścimābhyāṃbalaṃbalī | jaghānorasi tābhyāṃ ca sa ca tenābhyagṛhyata

Śrīdhara

gṛhīteti | gṛhītvā dhenukaṃ sa rāmaḥ tṛṇa-rājani tāle cikṣepa || 8 || tataḥ phalāny anekāni tālāgrān nipatan kharaḥ |

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

गृहीतेति । गृहीत्वा धेनुकं स रामः तृण-राजनि ताले चिक्षेप ॥८॥ ततः फलान्य् अनेकानि तालाग्रान् निपतन् खरः ।

Śrīdhara commentary.

No separate English commentary is supplied.

VP 5.8.9

गृहीत्वा भ्रामयामास सोंऽबरे गतजीवितम् ।
तस्मिन्नेव स चिक्षेप वेगेन तृणराजनि

When he started kicking Them with his legs, They held his legs, and started to rotate him all around, holding his legs, and threw him in the air, with so much of force. He fell down on the palm grove, and he lost his life. He fell dead.

Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

gṛhītvā bhrāmayāmāsa soṃ’bare gatajīvitam | tasminneva sa cikṣepa vegena tṛṇarājani

Śrīdhara commentary.

VP 5.8.10

ततः फलान्यनेकानि तालाग्रान्निपतन्खरः ।
पृथिव्यां पातयामास महावातो घनानिव

Just as a powerful wind, when it hits the clouds, when the rains come down, just like that, when that huge demon fell on the palm grove, all the fruits fell by themselves, on the ground.

Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tataḥ phalānyanekāni tālāgrānnipatankharaḥ | pṛthivyāṃ pātayāmāsa mahāvāto ghanāniva

Śrīdhara

tata iti | &quot;mahāvātor itāni vā&quot; iti paṭhāntare vā-śabda upamārthaḥ || 10 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तत इति । "महावातोर् इतानि वा" इति पठान्तरे वा-शब्द उपमार्थः ॥१०॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

No separate English commentary is supplied.

VP 5.8.11

अन्यानथ सजातीयानागतान्दैत्यगर्दभान् ।
कृष्णश्चिक्षेप तालाग्रे बलभद्रश्च लीलया

Dhenuka had a lot of his followers, who were also in the form of donkeys. They all came and attacked Krishna and Balaraama. Krishna and Balaraama killed all of them, and threw them in the palm grove.

Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

anyānatha sajātīyānāgatāndaityagardabhān | kṛṣṇaścikṣepa tālāgre balabhadraśca līlayā

VP 5.8.12

क्षणेनालङ्कृता पृथ्वी पक्वैस्तालफलैस्तदा ।
दैत्य गर्दभदेहैश्च मैत्रेय शुशुभेऽधिकम्

In no time, that palm grove was completely filled, as though it was decorated with all the fruits, all the ripe fruits fell down there, and also the dead bodies of all the demons.

Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kṣaṇenālaṅkṛtā pṛthvī pakvaistālaphalaistadā | daityagardabhadehaiśca maitreya śuśubhe’dhikam

Śrīdhara commentary.

VP 5.8.13

ततो गावो निराबाधास्तस्मिंस्तालवने द्विज ।
नवशष्पं सुखं चेरुर्यन्न भुक्तमभूत्पुरा

After that, all the cows, happily came there without any fear, and ate all the fresh grass available there, happily, which they had never touched. Because none of the cows had entered there earlier, they were all scared of this demon and his followers. There was no more any trouble from Dhenuka in that palm grove. This completes Chapter Eight.

Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tato gāvo nirābādhāstasmiṃstālavane dvija | navaśaṣpaṃ sukhaṃ ceruryanna bhuktamabhūtpurā
