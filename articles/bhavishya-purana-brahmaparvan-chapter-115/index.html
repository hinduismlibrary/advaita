Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 115 — The Sun and Its Flowers, Fragrances, and Offerings

BP 1.115.1

ब्रह्मोवाच।
प्रशंसन्ति महात्मानः संवादं भास्कराश्रयम्।
गौतम्या सह कौशल्या सुमनायां सुरालये ॥

Brahmā said. Great souls praise the dialogue about the Sun, spoken by Gautamī and Kauśalyā in the temple of the gods at Sumanā.

BP 1.115.2

स्वर्गेऽतिशोभनां दृष्ट्वा कौशल्यां पतिना सह।
ब्राह्मणी गौतमी नाम पर्यपृच्छत विस्मिता ॥

Seeing Kauśalyā, exceedingly beautiful in heaven, together with her husband, Gautamī, a Brahmin woman, asked her in wonder.

BP 1.115.3

गौतमी उवाच।
शतशः सन्ति कौशल्ये देवाः स्वर्गनिवासिनः।
देवपत्न्यस्तथैवैताः सिद्धाः सिद्धाङ्गनास्तथा ॥

Gautamī said. Hundreds of gods dwell in heaven, O Kauśalyā, as do the wives of the gods, perfected beings, and perfected women.

BP 1.115.4

न तेषामीदृशो गन्धो न कान्तिर्न सुरूपता।
न वाससी शोभने ये यथा ते पतिना सह ॥

None of them has such fragrance, radiance, beauty, or splendid garments as you possess together with your husband.

BP 1.115.5

नैवाभरणजातानि तेषां भ्राजन्ति वै तथा।
यथा तव यथा पत्युर्न च स्वर्गनिवासिनाम् ॥

Their ornaments do not shine as those of you and your husband, nor as the ornaments of the dwellers in heaven.

BP 1.115.6

सुस्नातचैलतश्चैव युवयोरतिरिच्यते।
लेखाद्यानामपीशानां क्षयातिशयवर्जितः ॥

The garments of the two of you, freshly washed, surpass even those of the lords of the celestial hosts, which are free from decay and excess.

BP 1.115.7

तपःप्रभावो दानं वा होमो वा कर्मसंज्ञितम्।
युवयोर्यत्समाचक्ष्व तत्सर्वं वरवर्णिनि।
येन मे विक्रमे बुद्धिर्मनुजा येन संगताः ॥

Tell me all the austerities, gifts, fire offerings, and other rites you two performed, O lovely one, by which I attained this splendor and by which humans attain such association.

BP 1.115.8

कौशल्या उवाच।
यज्ञो यज्ञेश्वरो भानुरावाभ्यां जातु तोषितः।
स्वर्गप्राप्तिरियं तस्य कर्मणः फलमुत्तमम् ॥

Kauśalyā said. The Sun, the Lord of sacrifice, was once pleased by our sacrifice. This attainment of heaven is the highest fruit of that act.

BP 1.115.9

सुरूपता ततः प्रीतिः पश्यतां चारुवेषिता।
यत्पृच्छसि महाभागे तदप्येषां वदामि ते ॥

Then came beauty, affection, and lovely appearance to those who see us. O greatly fortunate one, I shall tell you what you ask.

BP 1.115.10

तीर्थोदकैस्तथा गन्धैः स्नापितो यद्दिवाकरः।
तेन कान्तिरियं नित्यं देवान्त्रिभुवनेश्वरान् ॥

The Sun was bathed with waters from sacred places and fragrant substances. From that came this everlasting radiance among the gods, the lords of the three worlds.

BP 1.115.11

मनःप्रसादः सौम्यत्वं शरीरे ये च निर्वृताः।
यत्प्रियत्वं च सर्वं स्यात्तद्घृतस्नपनात्फलम् ॥

Peace of mind, gentleness of body, and all pleasant comforts arise as the fruit of bathing with ghee.

BP 1.115.12

यान्यभीष्टानि वासांसि यच्चाभीष्टविभूषणम्।
रत्नानि यान्यभीष्टानि यत्प्रियं चानुलेपनम् ॥

All desired garments, desired ornaments, desired jewels, and dear ointments.

BP 1.115.13

ये धृता यानि माल्यानि दयितान्यभवन्सदा।
मम भर्तुस्तदैवास्य तदा राज्यं प्रशासतः ॥

The garlands that my husband had worn and always loved became his then, while he ruled the kingdom.

BP 1.115.14

तानि सर्वाणि सर्वज्ञ सर्वपातरि भानुनि।
दत्तानि तं समुत्थोऽयं गन्धधूपात्मको गुणः ॥

All those things were given to the Sun, O all knowing protector of all. From them arose this quality made of fragrance and incense.

BP 1.115.15

आहारा दयिता ये च पवित्राश्च निवेदिताः।
त्रिलोककर्तुः सवितुस्तृप्तिस्तद्गुणसम्भवा ॥

Desired and pure foods, when offered, produce the satisfaction of the Sun, creator of the three worlds. This satisfaction arises from those qualities.

BP 1.115.16

स्वर्गकामेन मे भर्त्रा मया च शुभदर्शने।
कृतमेतत्कृतेनाभूदावयोर्भवसंक्षयः ॥

My husband and I, wishing for heaven, performed this, O beautiful one. Through this deed, the end of rebirth came for both of us.

BP 1.115.17

ये त्वकामाः नराः सम्यक्तत्कुर्वन्ति च शोभने।
तेषां ददाति विश्वेशो भगवान्मुक्तिमीश्वरः ॥

Those people who do this rightly without desire, O beautiful one, are given liberation by the Blessed Lord of the universe, the supreme Lord.

BP 1.115.18

ब्रह्मोवाच।
एवमभ्यर्च्य मार्तण्डमर्कं देवेश्वरं गुरुम्।
प्राप्तोऽस्म्यभिमतान्कामान्कृष्णाहं शाश्वतीः समाः ॥

Brahmā said. Having thus worshipped the Sun, lord of gods and teacher, I obtained my desired wishes, O Kṛṣṇa, for everlasting years.

BP 1.115.19

चन्दनागुरुकर्पूरकुङ्कुमोशीरपद्मकैः।
अनुलिप्तो नरैर्भक्त्या ददाति सागरोद्भवाम् ॥

Anointed by people with devotion using sandalwood, aloeswood, camphor, saffron, vetiver, and lotuses, he grants treasures from the ocean.

BP 1.115.20

कालेयकं तुरुष्कं च रक्तचन्दनमेव च।
यान्यात्मनि सदेष्टानि तानि शस्यान्यपाकुरु ॥

Dark sandalwood, olibanum, and red sandalwood, all things especially dear to oneself, praise and offer these.

BP 1.115.21

गन्धाश्चापि शुभा ये च धूपा ये विजयादयः।
दिवाकरस्य धर्मज्ञ निवेद्याः सर्वदाच्युत ॥

A knower of righteousness should always offer auspicious fragrances and incenses that bring victory to the Sun, O Acyuta.

BP 1.115.22

न दद्यात्सल्लकीक्षारं नो मुखेन च संहृतम्।
दद्यादर्काय धर्मज्ञ धूपमाराधनोद्यतः ॥

One should not offer salai resin, nor incense gathered by mouth. Intent on worship, O knower of righteousness, one should offer incense to the Sun.

BP 1.115.23

मालती मल्लिका चैव यूथिका चातिमुक्तिकः।
पाटलाः करवीरश्च जपा सेवान्तिरेव च ॥

Jasmine, Arabian jasmine, clustered jasmine, fragrant creeper flowers, trumpet flowers, oleander, hibiscus, and chrysanthemums.

BP 1.115.24

कुङ्कुमस्तगरश्चैव कर्णिकारः सकेशरः।
चम्पकः केतक कुन्दो बाणबर्बरमालिका ॥

Saffron, valerian, yellow laburnum with stamens, champak, screw pine, star jasmine, arrow flowers, and wild jasmine garlands.

BP 1.115.25

अशोकस्तिलको लोध्रस्तथा चैवाटरूषकः।
शतपत्राणि धन्यानि बकाह्वानी विशेषतः ॥

Ashoka, tilak, lodh, Malabar nut, and hundred petaled roses are auspicious, especially the crane call blossoms.

BP 1.115.26

अगस्तिं किंशुकं तद्वत्पूजार्थं भास्करस्य तु।
अमी पुष्पप्रकारास्तु शस्ता भास्करपूजने ॥

Sesbania and flame of the forest are for the worship of the Sun. These kinds of flowers are praised in the worship of the Sun.

BP 1.115.27

बिल्वपत्रं शमीपत्रं पत्रं वा भृङ्गरस्य च।
तमालपत्रं च हरे सदैव भगवत्प्रियम् ॥

Bael leaves, shami leaves, false daisy leaves, and Indian bay leaves are always dear to the blessed Lord.

BP 1.115.28

तुलसी कालतुलसी तथा रक्तं च चन्दनम्।
केतकी पत्रपुष्पं तु सद्यस्तुष्टिकरं रवेः ॥

Holy basil, dark basil, red sandalwood, and screw pine leaves and flowers immediately please the Sun.

BP 1.115.29

पद्मोत्पलसमुत्थानि रक्तं नीलोत्पलं तथा।
सितोत्पलं तु भानोस्तु दयितानि सदाच्युत ॥

Red lotus, blue lotus, and white lotus are always dear to the Sun, O Acyuta.

BP 1.115.30

कृष्णलोकन्मत्तकं कान्तं तथैव गिरिमल्लिका।
न कर्णिकारिकापुष्पं भास्कराय निवेदयेत् ॥

Dark eye flowers, intoxicating flowers, beloved flowers, and mountain jasmine should not be offered to the Sun, nor the flower of yellow laburnum.

BP 1.115.31

कुटजं शाल्मलीपुष्पं तथान्यद्गन्धवर्जितम्।
निवेदितं भयं रोगं निःस्वतां च प्रयच्छति ॥

Kurchi flowers, silk cotton flowers, and anything else without fragrance, when offered, bring fear, disease, and poverty.

BP 1.115.32

येषां न प्रतिषेधोऽस्ति गन्धवर्णान्वितानि च।
तानि पुष्पाणि देयानि मानवे लोकभानवे ॥

Flowers that are not forbidden and possess fragrance and color should be offered to the Sun, the human lord of the world.

BP 1.115.33

सुगन्धैश्च मुरामांसीकर्पूरागरुचन्दनैः।
तथान्यैश्च शुभैर्द्रव्यैरर्चयेद्वनमालिनम् ॥

One should worship the Garlanded Lord with fragrant costus, spikenard, camphor, aloeswood, sandalwood, and other auspicious substances.

BP 1.115.34

दुकूलपट्टकौशेयवार्क्षकार्पासकादिभिः।
वासोभिः पूजयेद्भानुं यानि चात्मप्रियाणि तु ॥

One should worship the Sun with fine cloth, silk, bark cloth, cotton, and other garments, whatever are dear to oneself.

BP 1.115.35

भक्ष्याणि यान्यभीष्टानि भोज्यान्यभिमतानि च।
फलं च वल्लभं यत्स्यात्तत्ते देयं दिवाकरे ॥

Whatever foods are desired, whatever dishes are favored, and whatever fruit is dear should be given to the Sun.

BP 1.115.36

सुवर्णमणिमुक्तानि रजतं च तथाच्युत।
दक्षिणा विविधा चेह यच्चान्यदपि वल्लभम् ॥

Gold, gems, pearls, silver, various gifts here, and whatever else is dear should also be offered, O Acyuta.

BP 1.115.37

आत्मानं भास्करं मत्वा यज्ञं तस्मै निवेदयेत्।
तत्तदव्यक्तरूपाय भास्कराय निवेदयेत् ॥

Considering oneself to be the Sun, one should offer the sacrifice to him. One should offer each thing to the Sun, whose form is unmanifest.
