Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 115 — The Sun and Its Flowers, Fragrances, and Offerings

BP 1.115.1

ब्रह्मोवाच।
प्रशंसन्ति महात्मानः संवादं भास्कराश्रयम्।
गौतम्या सह कौशल्या सुमनायां सुरालये ॥

Brahmā said. Great souls praise the dialogue about the Sun, spoken by Gautamī and Kauśalyā in the temple of the gods at Sumanā.

ब्रह्मा उवाच(brahmovāca) — Brahmā said;
प्रशंसन्ति(praśaṃsanti) — praise;
महात्मानः(mahātmānaḥ) — the great souls;
संवादम्(saṃvādam) — the dialogue;
भास्कर-आश्रयम्(bhāskarāśrayam) — concerning Bhāskara;
गौतम्या सह कौशल्या(gautamyā saha kauśalyā) — between Gautamī and Kauśalyā;
सुमनायाम् सुर-आलये(sumanāyāṃ surālaye) — in Sumanā's temple of the gods;

BP 1.115.2

स्वर्गेऽतिशोभनां दृष्ट्वा कौशल्यां पतिना सह।
ब्राह्मणी गौतमी नाम पर्यपृच्छत विस्मिता ॥

Seeing Kauśalyā, exceedingly beautiful in heaven, together with her husband, Gautamī, a Brahmin woman, asked her in wonder.

स्वर्गे(svarge) — in heaven;
अति-शोभनाम्(atiśobhanāṃ) — exceedingly beautiful;
दृष्ट्वा(dṛṣṭvā) — having seen;
कौशल्याम् पतिना सह(kauśalyāṃ patinā saha) — Kauśalyā together with her husband;
ब्राह्मणी गौतमी नाम(brāhmaṇī gautamī nāma) — the Brāhmaṇī named Gautamī;
पर्यपृच्छत(paryapṛcchata) — asked;
विस्मिता(vismitā) — in wonder;

BP 1.115.3

गौतमी उवाच।
शतशः सन्ति कौशल्ये देवाः स्वर्गनिवासिनः।
देवपत्न्यस्तथैवैताः सिद्धाः सिद्धाङ्गनास्तथा ॥

Gautamī said. Hundreds of gods dwell in heaven, O Kauśalyā, as do the wives of the gods, perfected beings, and perfected women.

गौतमी उवाच(gautamy uvāca) — Gautamī said;
शतशः सन्ति(śataśaḥ santi) — there are hundreds;
कौशल्ये(kauśalye) — O Kauśalyā;
देवाः स्वर्ग-निवासिनः(devāḥ svarga-nivāsinaḥ) — gods dwelling in heaven;
देव-पत्न्यः(deva-patnyaḥ) — wives of the gods;
तथा एव एताः(tathaivaitāḥ) — and likewise these;
सिद्धाः सिद्ध-अङ्गनाः तथा(siddhāḥ siddhāṅganās tathā) — perfected women and their companions;

BP 1.115.4

न तेषामीदृशो गन्धो न कान्तिर्न सुरूपता।
न वाससी शोभने ये यथा ते पतिना सह ॥

None of them has such fragrance, radiance, beauty, or splendid garments as you possess together with your husband.

न तेषाम् ईदृशः गन्धः(na teṣām īdṛśo gandhaḥ) — none of them has such fragrance;
न कान्तिः(na kāntiḥ) — not radiance;
न सुरूपता(na surūpatā) — not beauty;
न वाससी शोभने(na vāsasī śobhane) — not splendid garments;
ये यथा ते पतिना सह(ye yathā te patinā saha) — as you are with your husband;

BP 1.115.5

नैवाभरणजातानि तेषां भ्राजन्ति वै तथा।
यथा तव यथा पत्युर्न च स्वर्गनिवासिनाम् ॥

Their ornaments do not shine as those of you and your husband, nor as the ornaments of the dwellers in heaven.

न एव आभरण-जातानि(naivābharaṇa-jātāni) — not the kinds of ornaments;
तेषाम् भ्राजन्ति(teṣāṃ bhrājanti) — of them shine;
वै तथा(vai tathā) — indeed in that way;
यथा तव(yathā tava) — as yours;
यथा पत्युः(yathā patyuḥ) — as your husband's;
न च स्वर्ग-निवासिनाम्(na ca svarga-nivāsinām) — nor of the dwellers in heaven;

BP 1.115.6

सुस्नातचैलतश्चैव युवयोरतिरिच्यते।
लेखाद्यानामपीशानां क्षयातिशयवर्जितः ॥

The garments of the two of you, freshly washed, surpass even those of the lords of the celestial hosts, which are free from decay and excess.

सु-स्नात-चैलतः च एव(susnāta-cailataś caiva) — even from freshly bathed garments;
युवयोः(yuvayor) — of you two;
अतिरिच्यते(atiricyate) — is surpassed;
लेख-आदीनाम् अपि ईशानाम्(lekhādyānām apīśānām) — even of the lords of the hosts;
क्षय-अतिशय-वर्जितः(kṣayātiśaya-varjitaḥ) — free from decay and excess;

BP 1.115.7

तपःप्रभावो दानं वा होमो वा कर्मसंज्ञितम्।
युवयोर्यत्समाचक्ष्व तत्सर्वं वरवर्णिनि।
येन मे विक्रमे बुद्धिर्मनुजा येन संगताः ॥

Tell me all the austerities, gifts, fire offerings, and other rites you two performed, O lovely one, by which I attained this splendor and by which humans attain such association.

तपः-प्रभावः(tapaḥ-prabhāvo) — the power of austerity;
दानम् वा(dānaṃ vā) — or generosity;
होमः वा(homo vā) — or offering;
कर्म-संज्ञितम्(karma-saṃjñitam) — known as a rite;
युवयोः यत् समाचक्ष्व(yuvayor yat samācakṣva) — tell what was done by you two;
तत् सर्वम्(tat sarvam) — all that;
वर-वर्णिनि(vara-varṇini) — O lovely one;
येन मे विक्रमे बुद्धिः(yena me vikrame buddhiḥ) — by which I have this splendour;
मनुजाः येन सङ्गताः(manujā yena saṅgatāḥ) — by which human beings attain association;

BP 1.115.8

कौशल्या उवाच।
यज्ञो यज्ञेश्वरो भानुरावाभ्यां जातु तोषितः।
स्वर्गप्राप्तिरियं तस्य कर्मणः फलमुत्तमम् ॥

Kauśalyā said. The Sun, the Lord of sacrifice, was once pleased by our sacrifice. This attainment of heaven is the highest fruit of that act.

कौशल्या उवाच(kauśalyovāca) — Kauśalyā said;
यज्ञः(yajñaḥ) — sacrifice;
यज्ञ-ईश्वरः भानुः(yajñeśvaro bhānuḥ) — Bhānu, Lord of sacrifice;
आवाभ्याम्(āvābhyāṃ) — by us two;
जातु तोषितः(jātu toṣitaḥ) — was once pleased;
स्वर्ग-प्राप्तिः इयम्(svarga-prāptir iyam) — this attainment of heaven;
तस्य कर्मणः फलम् उत्तमम्(tasya karmaṇaḥ phalam uttamam) — the supreme fruit of that act;

BP 1.115.9

सुरूपता ततः प्रीतिः पश्यतां चारुवेषिता।
यत्पृच्छसि महाभागे तदप्येषां वदामि ते ॥

Then came beauty, affection, and lovely appearance to those who see us. O greatly fortunate one, I shall tell you what you ask.

सुरूपता(surūpatā) — beauty;
ततः प्रीतिः(tataḥ prītiḥ) — then affection;
पश्यताम्(paśyatām) — of those who behold;
चारु-वेषिता(cāru-veṣitā) — lovely appearance;
यत् पृच्छसि(yat pṛcchasi) — what you ask;
महाभागे(mahābhāge) — O greatly fortunate one;
तत् अपि एषाम् वदामि ते(tad apy eṣāṃ vadāmi te) — that too I tell you;

BP 1.115.10

तीर्थोदकैस्तथा गन्धैः स्नापितो यद्दिवाकरः।
तेन कान्तिरियं नित्यं देवान्त्रिभुवनेश्वरान् ॥

The Sun was bathed with waters from sacred places and fragrant substances. From that came this everlasting radiance among the gods, the lords of the three worlds.

तीर्थ-उदकैः(tīrthodakaiḥ) — with water from sacred places;
तथा गन्धैः(tathā gandhaiḥ) — and with fragrant substances;
स्नापितः(snāpitaḥ) — was bathed;
यत् दिवाकरः(yad divākaraḥ) — when Divākara;
तेन कान्तिः इयम् नित्यम्(tena kāntir iyaṃ nityam) — through that this perpetual radiance;
देवान् त्रि-भुवन-ईश्वरान्(devāṃs tri-bhuvaneśvarān) — among the gods, lords of the three worlds;

BP 1.115.11

मनःप्रसादः सौम्यत्वं शरीरे ये च निर्वृताः।
यत्प्रियत्वं च सर्वं स्यात्तद्घृतस्नपनात्फलम् ॥

Peace of mind, gentleness of body, and all pleasant comforts arise as the fruit of bathing with ghee.

मनः-प्रसादः(manaḥ-prasādaḥ) — peace of mind;
सौम्यत्वम् शरीरे(saumyatvaṃ śarīre) — gentleness in the body;
ये च निर्वृताः(ye ca nirvṛtāḥ) — and the states of peace;
यत् प्रियत्वम् च सर्वम्(yat priyatvaṃ ca sarvam) — every form of pleasantness;
स्यात्(syāt) — may be;
तत् घृत-स्नपनात् फलम्(tad ghṛta-snapanāt phalam) — that is the fruit of bathing with ghee;

BP 1.115.12

यान्यभीष्टानि वासांसि यच्चाभीष्टविभूषणम्।
रत्नानि यान्यभीष्टानि यत्प्रियं चानुलेपनम् ॥

All desired garments, desired ornaments, desired jewels, and dear ointments.

यानि अभीष्टानि वासांसि(yāny abhīṣṭāni vāsāṃsi) — whatever desired garments;
यत् च अभीष्ट-विभूषणम्(yac cābhīṣṭa-vibhūṣaṇam) — whatever desired ornament;
रत्नानि यानि अभीष्टानि(ratnāni yāny abhīṣṭāni) — whatever desired jewels;
यत् प्रियम् च अनुलेपनम्(yat priyaṃ cānulepanam) — whatever anointing is dear;

BP 1.115.13

ये धृता यानि माल्यानि दयितान्यभवन्सदा।
मम भर्तुस्तदैवास्य तदा राज्यं प्रशासतः ॥

The garlands that my husband had worn and always loved became his then, while he ruled the kingdom.

ये धृताः यानि माल्यानि(ye dhṛtā yāni mālyāni) — the garlands that were worn;
दयितानि अभवन् सदा(dayitāny abhavan sadā) — were always beloved;
मम भर्तुः(mama bhartuḥ) — of my husband;
तदा एव अस्य(tadaivāsya) — then indeed his;
तदा राज्यम् प्रशासतः(tadā rājyaṃ praśāsataḥ) — when he was ruling the kingdom;

BP 1.115.14

तानि सर्वाणि सर्वज्ञ सर्वपातरि भानुनि।
दत्तानि तं समुत्थोऽयं गन्धधूपात्मको गुणः ॥

All those things were given to the Sun, O all knowing protector of all. From them arose this quality made of fragrance and incense.

तानि सर्वाणि(tāni sarvāṇi) — all those things;
सर्वज्ञ(sarvajña) — O all-knowing one;
सर्व-पातरि(sarva-pātari) — O protector of all;
भानुनि(bhānuni) — to Bhānu;
दत्तानि(dattāni) — offered;
तम् समुत्थः अयम्(taṃ samuttho'yaṃ) — from them arose this;
गन्ध-धूप-आत्मकः गुणः(gandha-dhūpātmako guṇaḥ) — quality consisting of fragrance and incense;

BP 1.115.15

आहारा दयिता ये च पवित्राश्च निवेदिताः।
त्रिलोककर्तुः सवितुस्तृप्तिस्तद्गुणसम्भवा ॥

Desired and pure foods, when offered, produce the satisfaction of the Sun, creator of the three worlds. This satisfaction arises from those qualities.

आहाराः(āhārāḥ) — foods;
दयिताः ये च(dayitā ye ca) — that are dear and;
पवित्राः च निवेदिताः(pavitrāś ca niveditāḥ) — pure and offered;
त्रि-लोक-कर्तुः सवितुः(tri-loka-kartuḥ savituḥ) — of Savitṛ, maker of the three worlds;
तृप्तिः(tṛptiḥ) — satisfaction;
तत्-गुण-सम्भवा(tad-guṇa-sambhavā) — arising from those qualities;

BP 1.115.16

स्वर्गकामेन मे भर्त्रा मया च शुभदर्शने।
कृतमेतत्कृतेनाभूदावयोर्भवसंक्षयः ॥

My husband and I, wishing for heaven, performed this, O beautiful one. Through this deed, the end of rebirth came for both of us.

स्वर्ग-कामेन(svarga-kāmena) — desiring heaven;
मे भर्त्रा(me bhartrā) — my husband;
मया च(mayā ca) — and by me;
शुभ-दर्शने(śubha-darśane) — O beautiful one;
कृतम् एतत्(kṛtam etat) — this was done;
कृतेन अभूत्(kṛtenābhūt) — through the act there arose;
आवयोः भव-संक्षयः(āvayor bhava-saṃkṣayaḥ) — the end of worldly existence for us two;

BP 1.115.17

ये त्वकामाः नराः सम्यक्तत्कुर्वन्ति च शोभने।
तेषां ददाति विश्वेशो भगवान्मुक्तिमीश्वरः ॥

Those people who do this rightly without desire, O beautiful one, are given liberation by the Blessed Lord of the universe, the supreme Lord.

ये तु अकामाः नराः(ye tv akāmā narāḥ) — those persons without desire;
सम्यक् तत् कुर्वन्ति(samyak tat kurvanti) — properly perform that;
च शोभने(ca śobhane) — O beautiful one;
तेषाम् ददाति(teṣāṃ dadāti) — to them he gives;
विश्वेशः भगवान्(viśveśo bhagavān) — the blessed Lord of the universe;
मुक्तिम् ईश्वरः(muktim īśvaraḥ) — the supreme Lord gives liberation;

BP 1.115.18

ब्रह्मोवाच।
एवमभ्यर्च्य मार्तण्डमर्कं देवेश्वरं गुरुम्।
प्राप्तोऽस्म्यभिमतान्कामान्कृष्णाहं शाश्वतीः समाः ॥

Brahmā said. Having thus worshipped the Sun, lord of gods and teacher, I obtained my desired wishes, O Kṛṣṇa, for everlasting years.

ब्रह्मा उवाच(brahmovāca) — Brahmā said;
एवम् अभ्यर्च्य(evam abhyarcya) — having worshipped thus;
मार्तण्डम् अर्कम्(mārtaṇḍam arkam) — Mārtaṇḍa, Arka;
देव-ईश्वरम् गुरुम्(dev eśvaraṃ gurum) — the Lord and teacher of the gods;
प्राप्तः अस्मि(prāpto'smi) — I attained;
अभिमतान् कामान्(abhimatān kāmān) — desired wishes;
कृष्ण अहम्(kṛṣṇāhaṃ) — O Kṛṣṇa, I;
शाश्वतीः समाः(śāśvatīḥ samāḥ) — everlasting years;

BP 1.115.19

चन्दनागुरुकर्पूरकुङ्कुमोशीरपद्मकैः।
अनुलिप्तो नरैर्भक्त्या ददाति सागरोद्भवाम् ॥

Anointed by people with devotion using sandalwood, aloeswood, camphor, saffron, vetiver, and lotuses, he grants treasures from the ocean.

चन्दन-अगरु-कर्पूर-कुङ्कुम-उशीर-पद्मकैः(candana-aguru-karpūra-kuṅkumośīra-padmakaiḥ) — with sandalwood, aguru, camphor, kuṅkuma, uśīra, and lotuses;
अनुलिप्तः(anulipto) — being anointed;
नरैः भक्त्या(narair bhaktyā) — by people with devotion;
ददाति(dadāti) — he grants;
सागर-उद्भवाम्(sāgarodbhavām) — what arises from the ocean;

BP 1.115.20

कालेयकं तुरुष्कं च रक्तचन्दनमेव च।
यान्यात्मनि सदेष्टानि तानि शस्यान्यपाकुरु ॥

Dark sandalwood, olibanum, and red sandalwood, all things especially dear to oneself, praise and offer these.

कालेयकम्(kāleyakaṃ) — kāleyaka;
तुरुष्कम् च(turuṣkaṃ ca) — turuṣka, and;
रक्त-चन्दनम् एव च(raktacandanam eva ca) — and red sandalwood;
यानि आत्मनि सदा इष्टानि(yāny ātmani sadā iṣṭāni) — whatever is always desired for oneself;
तानि शंस्यानि अपाकुरु(tāni śasyāny apākuru) — praise and offer those;

BP 1.115.21

गन्धाश्चापि शुभा ये च धूपा ये विजयादयः।
दिवाकरस्य धर्मज्ञ निवेद्याः सर्वदाच्युत ॥

A knower of righteousness should always offer auspicious fragrances and incenses that bring victory to the Sun, O Acyuta.

गन्धाः च अपि शुभाः(gandhāś cāpi śubhāḥ) — and auspicious fragrances;
ये च धूपाः(ye ca dhūpāḥ) — and the incenses;
विजय-आदयः(vijayādayaḥ) — beginning with victory;
दिवाकरस्य(divākarasya) — for Divākara;
धर्मज्ञ(dharmajña) — O knower of dharma;
निवेद्याः सर्वदा(nivedyāḥ sarvadā) — should always be offered;
अच्युत(acyuta) — O Acyuta;

BP 1.115.22

न दद्यात्सल्लकीक्षारं नो मुखेन च संहृतम्।
दद्यादर्काय धर्मज्ञ धूपमाराधनोद्यतः ॥

One should not offer salai resin, nor incense gathered by mouth. Intent on worship, O knower of righteousness, one should offer incense to the Sun.

न दद्यात्(na dadyāt) — one should not give;
सल्लकी-क्षारम्(sallakīkṣāraṃ) — sallakī resin;
न उ मुखेन च संहृतम्(no mukhena ca saṃhṛtam) — nor what is gathered by mouth;
दद्यात् अर्काय(dadyād arkāya) — one should give to Arka;
धर्मज्ञ(dharmajña) — O knower of dharma;
धूमम् आराधन-उद्यतः(dhūmam ārādhanodyataḥ) — incense, intent on worship;

BP 1.115.23

मालती मल्लिका चैव यूथिका चातिमुक्तिकः।
पाटलाः करवीरश्च जपा सेवान्तिरेव च ॥

Jasmine, Arabian jasmine, clustered jasmine, fragrant creeper flowers, trumpet flowers, oleander, hibiscus, and chrysanthemums.

मालती मल्लिका च एव(mālatī mallikā caiva) — mālatī and mallikā;
यूथिका च अतिमुक्तिकः(yūthikā cātimuktikaḥ) — yūthikā and atimuktika;
पाटलाः(pāṭalāḥ) — pāṭalā flowers;
करवीरः च(karavīraś ca) — karavīra, and;
जपा सेवन्ती एव च(japā sevantī eva ca) — japā and śevantī also;

BP 1.115.24

कुङ्कुमस्तगरश्चैव कर्णिकारः सकेशरः।
चम्पकः केतक कुन्दो बाणबर्बरमालिका ॥

Saffron, valerian, yellow laburnum with stamens, champak, screw pine, star jasmine, arrow flowers, and wild jasmine garlands.

कुङ्कुमः तगरः च एव(kuṅkumastagaraś caiva) — kuṅkuma and tagara;
कर्णिकारः स-केशरः(karṇikāraḥ sakeśaraḥ) — karṇikāra with filaments;
चम्पकः(campakaḥ) — campaka;
केतक कुन्दः(ketaka kundaḥ) — ketaka and kunda;
बाण-बर्बर-मालिका(bāṇa-barbara-mālikā) — bāṇa and barbaramālikā;

BP 1.115.25

अशोकस्तिलको लोध्रस्तथा चैवाटरूषकः।
शतपत्राणि धन्यानि बकाह्वानी विशेषतः ॥

Ashoka, tilak, lodh, Malabar nut, and hundred petaled roses are auspicious, especially the crane call blossoms.

अशोकः तिलकः(aśokaḥ tilakaḥ) — aśoka and tilaka;
लोध्रः(lodhraḥ) — lodhra;
तथा च एव आटरूषकः(tathā caivāṭarūṣakaḥ) — and also āṭarūṣaka;
शत-पत्राणि(śata-patrāṇi) — hundred-petalled flowers;
धन्यानि(dhanyāni) — auspicious;
बक-आह्वानी विशेषतः(bakāhvānī viśeṣataḥ) — especially those called bakāhvānī;

BP 1.115.26

अगस्तिं किंशुकं तद्वत्पूजार्थं भास्करस्य तु।
अमी पुष्पप्रकारास्तु शस्ता भास्करपूजने ॥

Sesbania and flame of the forest are for the worship of the Sun. These kinds of flowers are praised in the worship of the Sun.

अगस्तिम्(agastiṃ) — agasti;
किंशुकम् तद्वत्(kiṃśukaṃ tadvat) — kiṃśuka likewise;
पूजा-अर्थम् भास्करस्य तु(pūjārthaṃ bhāskarasya tu) — for the worship of Bhāskara;
अमी पुष्प-प्रकाराः तु(amī puṣpa-prakārās tu) — these kinds of flowers indeed;
शस्ताः(śastāḥ) — are auspicious;
भास्कर-पूजने(bhāskara-pūjane) — in Bhāskara worship;

BP 1.115.27

बिल्वपत्रं शमीपत्रं पत्रं वा भृङ्गरस्य च।
तमालपत्रं च हरे सदैव भगवत्प्रियम् ॥

Bael leaves, shami leaves, false daisy leaves, and Indian bay leaves are always dear to the blessed Lord.

बिल्व-पत्रम्(bilvapatraṃ) — bilva leaves;
शमी-पत्रम्(śamīpatraṃ) — śamī leaves;
पत्रम् वा भृङ्गरस्य च(patraṃ vā bhṛṅgarasya ca) — or leaves of bhṛṅgarasa;
तमाल-पत्रम् च(tamālapatraṃ ca) — and tamāla leaves;
हरे(hare) — to the Lord;
सदा एव भगवत्-प्रियम्(sadaiva bhagavatpriyam) — always dear to the blessed Lord;

BP 1.115.28

तुलसी कालतुलसी तथा रक्तं च चन्दनम्।
केतकी पत्रपुष्पं तु सद्यस्तुष्टिकरं रवेः ॥

Holy basil, dark basil, red sandalwood, and screw pine leaves and flowers immediately please the Sun.

तुलसी(tulasī) — tulasī;
काल-तुलसी तथा(kāla-tulasī tathā) — dark tulasī also;
रक्तम् च चन्दनम्(raktaṃ ca candanam) — and red sandalwood;
केतकी-पत्र-पुष्पम् तु(ketakī-patra-puṣpaṃ tu) — the leaves and flowers of ketakī;
सद्यः तुष्टि-करम् रवेः(sadyaḥ tuṣṭi-karaṃ raveḥ) — immediately pleasing to Ravi;

BP 1.115.29

पद्मोत्पलसमुत्थानि रक्तं नीलोत्पलं तथा।
सितोत्पलं तु भानोस्तु दयितानि सदाच्युत ॥

Red lotus, blue lotus, and white lotus are always dear to the Sun, O Acyuta.

पद्म-उत्पल-समुत्थानि(padmotpala-samutthāni) — arising from lotus and water-lily;
रक्तम् नील-उत्पलम् तथा(raktaṃ nīlotpalaṃ tathā) — red and blue water-lily;
सित-उत्पलम् तु(sitotpalam tu) — but white water-lily;
भानोस तु दयितानि(bhānos tu dayitāni) — are dear to Bhānu;
सदा अच्युत(sadācyuta) — always, O Acyuta;

BP 1.115.30

कृष्णलोकन्मत्तकं कान्तं तथैव गिरिमल्लिका।
न कर्णिकारिकापुष्पं भास्कराय निवेदयेत् ॥

Dark eye flowers, intoxicating flowers, beloved flowers, and mountain jasmine should not be offered to the Sun, nor the flower of yellow laburnum.

कृष्ण-लोकन्-मत्तकम्(kṛṣṇa-lokan-mattakaṃ) — kṛṣṇalokan-mattaka;
कान्तम्(kāntaṃ) — kānta;
तथा एव गिरि-मल्लिका(tathaiva giri-mallikā) — and girimallikā;
न निवेदयेत्(na nivedayet) — should not offer;
कर्णिकारिका-पुष्पम्(karṇikārikā-puṣpaṃ) — the flower of karṇikārikā;
भास्कराय(bhāskarāya) — to Bhāskara;

BP 1.115.31

कुटजं शाल्मलीपुष्पं तथान्यद्गन्धवर्जितम्।
निवेदितं भयं रोगं निःस्वतां च प्रयच्छति ॥

Kurchi flowers, silk cotton flowers, and anything else without fragrance, when offered, bring fear, disease, and poverty.

कुटजम्(kuṭajaṃ) — kuṭaja;
शाल्मली-पुष्पम्(śālmalī-puṣpaṃ) — śālmalī flowers;
तथा अन्यत् गन्ध-वर्जितम्(tathānyad gandha-varjitam) — or anything else without fragrance;
निवेदितम्(niveditam) — offered;
भयम् रोगम् च(bhayaṃ rogaṃ ca) — fear and disease;
निःस्वताम् च प्रयच्छति(niḥsvatāṃ ca prayacchati) — and gives poverty;

BP 1.115.32

येषां न प्रतिषेधोऽस्ति गन्धवर्णान्वितानि च।
तानि पुष्पाणि देयानि मानवे लोकभानवे ॥

Flowers that are not forbidden and possess fragrance and color should be offered to the Sun, the human lord of the world.

येषाम् न प्रतिषेधः अस्ति(yeṣāṃ na pratiṣedho'sti) — which are not forbidden;
गन्ध-वर्ण-अन्वितानि च(gandha-varṇānvitāni ca) — and possess fragrance and colour;
तानि पुष्पाणि(tāni puṣpāṇi) — those flowers;
देयानि(deyāni) — should be offered;
मानवे लोक-भानवे(mānave loka-bhānave) — to the human Lord of the world;

BP 1.115.33

सुगन्धैश्च मुरामांसीकर्पूरागरुचन्दनैः।
तथान्यैश्च शुभैर्द्रव्यैरर्चयेद्वनमालिनम् ॥

One should worship the Garlanded Lord with fragrant costus, spikenard, camphor, aloeswood, sandalwood, and other auspicious substances.

सुगन्धैः च(sugandhaiś ca) — with fragrant substances;
मुरा-मांसी-कर्पूर-अगरु-चन्दनैः(murāmāṃsī-karpūrāguru-candanaiḥ) — with murā, māṃsī, camphor, aguru, and sandalwood;
तथा अन्यैः शुभैः द्रव्यैः(tathānyaiś ca śubhair dravyaiḥ) — and with other auspicious materials;
अर्चयेत्(arcayet) — one should worship;
वन-मालिनम्(vana-mālinam) — the Vanamālin;

BP 1.115.34

दुकूलपट्टकौशेयवार्क्षकार्पासकादिभिः।
वासोभिः पूजयेद्भानुं यानि चात्मप्रियाणि तु ॥

One should worship the Sun with fine cloth, silk, bark cloth, cotton, and other garments, whatever are dear to oneself.

दुकूल-पट्ट-कौशेय-वार्क्षक-कार्पासक-आदिभिः(dukūla-paṭṭa-kauśeya-vārkṣaka-kārpāsaka-ādibhiḥ) — with fine cloth, silk, tree-fibre, cotton, and the like;
वासोभिः(vāsobhiḥ) — with garments;
पूजयेत् भानुम्(pūjayed bhānum) — one should worship Bhānu;
यानि च आत्म-प्रियाणि तु(yāni cātma-priyāṇi tu) — whatever are dear to oneself;

BP 1.115.35

भक्ष्याणि यान्यभीष्टानि भोज्यान्यभिमतानि च।
फलं च वल्लभं यत्स्यात्तत्ते देयं दिवाकरे ॥

Whatever foods are desired, whatever dishes are favored, and whatever fruit is dear should be given to the Sun.

भक्ष्याणि यानि अभीष्टानि(bhakṣyāṇi yāny abhīṣṭāni) — whatever desired foods;
भोज्यानि अभिमतानि च(bhojyāny abhimatāni ca) — and favoured prepared dishes;
फलम् च वल्लभम् यत् स्यात्(phalaṃ ca vallabhaṃ yat syāt) — and whatever fruit is dear;
तत् ते देयम्(tat te deyam) — that should be given;
दिवाकरे(divākare) — to Divākara;

BP 1.115.36

सुवर्णमणिमुक्तानि रजतं च तथाच्युत।
दक्षिणा विविधा चेह यच्चान्यदपि वल्लभम् ॥

Gold, gems, pearls, silver, various gifts here, and whatever else is dear should also be offered, O Acyuta.

सुवर्ण-मणि-मुक्तानि(suvarṇa-maṇi-muktāni) — gold, gems, and pearls;
रजतम् च(rajataṃ ca) — and silver;
तथा अच्युत(tathācyuta) — also, O Acyuta;
दक्षिणा विविधा च इह(dakṣiṇā vividhā ceha) — various gifts here;
यत् च अन्यत् अपि वल्लभम्(yac cānyad api vallabham) — and whatever else is dear;

BP 1.115.37

आत्मानं भास्करं मत्वा यज्ञं तस्मै निवेदयेत्।
तत्तदव्यक्तरूपाय भास्कराय निवेदयेत् ॥

Considering oneself to be the Sun, one should offer the sacrifice to him. One should offer each thing to the Sun, whose form is unmanifest.

आत्मानम् भास्करम् मत्वा(ātmānaṃ bhāskaraṃ matvā) — considering oneself to be Bhāskara;
यज्ञम् तस्मै निवेदयेत्(yajñaṃ tasmai nivedayet) — one should offer the sacrifice to him;
तत् तत्(tat tat) — each offering;
अव्यक्त-रूपाय(avyakta-rūpāya) — whose form is unmanifest;
भास्कराय निवेदयेत्(bhāskarāya nivedayet) — one should offer to Bhāskara;
