Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 115 — Bhāskara Worship: Flowers, Incense, and Offerings

BP 1.115.1

ब्रह्मोवाच।
प्रशंसन्ति महात्मानः संवादं भास्कराश्रयम्।
गौतम्या सह कौशल्या सुमनायां सुरालये ॥

Brahmā said: The great souls praise the dialogue concerning Bhāskara, between Gautamī and Kauśalyā, in Sumanā's temple of the gods.

Word-for-word
ब्रह्मा उवाच(brahmovāca) — Brahmā said;
प्रशंसन्ति(praśaṃsanti) — praise;
महात्मानः(mahātmānaḥ) — the great souls;
संवादम्(saṃvādam) — the dialogue;
भास्कर-आश्रयम्(bhāskarāśrayam) — concerning Bhāskara;
गौतम्या सह कौशल्या(gautamyā saha kauśalyā) — between Gautamī and Kauśalyā;
सुमनायाम् सुर-आलये(sumanāyāṃ surālaye) — in Sumanā's temple of the gods;
Transliteration
brahmovāca | praśaṃsanti mahātmānaḥ saṃvādaṃ bhāskarāśrayam | gautamyā saha kauśalyā sumanāyāṃ surālaye || 1 ||

BP 1.115.2

स्वर्गेऽतिशोभनां दृष्ट्वा कौशल्यां पतिना सह।
ब्राह्मणी गौतमी नाम पर्यपृच्छत विस्मिता ॥

Seeing Kauśalyā, exceedingly beautiful in heaven, together with her husband, the Brāhmaṇī named Gautamī asked her in wonder.

Word-for-word
स्वर्गे(svarge) — in heaven;
अति-शोभनाम्(atiśobhanāṃ) — exceedingly beautiful;
दृष्ट्वा(dṛṣṭvā) — having seen;
कौशल्याम् पतिना सह(kauśalyāṃ patinā saha) — Kauśalyā together with her husband;
ब्राह्मणी गौतमी नाम(brāhmaṇī gautamī nāma) — the Brāhmaṇī named Gautamī;
पर्यपृच्छत(paryapṛcchata) — asked;
विस्मिता(vismitā) — in wonder;
Transliteration
svarge'tiśobhanāṃ dṛṣṭvā kauśalyāṃ patinā saha | brāhmaṇī gautamī nāma paryapṛcchata vismitā || 2 ||

BP 1.115.3

गौतमी उवाच।
शतशः सन्ति कौशल्ये देवाः स्वर्गनिवासिनः।
देवपत्न्यस्तथैवैताः सिद्धाः सिद्धाङ्गनास्तथा ॥

Gautamī said: O Kauśalyā, there are hundreds of gods dwelling in heaven, as well as these wives of the gods, and perfected women and their companions.

Word-for-word
गौतमी उवाच(gautamy uvāca) — Gautamī said;
शतशः सन्ति(śataśaḥ santi) — there are hundreds;
कौशल्ये(kauśalye) — O Kauśalyā;
देवाः स्वर्ग-निवासिनः(devāḥ svarga-nivāsinaḥ) — gods dwelling in heaven;
देव-पत्न्यः(deva-patnyaḥ) — wives of the gods;
तथा एव एताः(tathaivaitāḥ) — and likewise these;
सिद्धाः सिद्ध-अङ्गनाः तथा(siddhāḥ siddhāṅganās tathā) — perfected women and their companions;
Transliteration
gautamyuvāca | śataśaḥ santi kauśalye devāḥ svarganivāsinaḥ | devapatnyastathaivaitāḥ siddhāḥ siddhāṅganāstathā || 3 ||

BP 1.115.4

न तेषामीदृशो गन्धो न कान्तिर्न सुरूपता।
न वाससी शोभने ये यथा ते पतिना सह ॥

None of them has such fragrance, radiance, beauty, or splendid garments as you and your husband possess.

Word-for-word
न तेषाम् ईदृशः गन्धः(na teṣām īdṛśo gandhaḥ) — none of them has such fragrance;
न कान्तिः(na kāntiḥ) — not radiance;
न सुरूपता(na surūpatā) — not beauty;
न वाससी शोभने(na vāsasī śobhane) — not splendid garments;
ये यथा ते पतिना सह(ye yathā te patinā saha) — as you are with your husband;
Transliteration
na teṣāmīdṛśo gandho na kāntirna surūpatā | na vāsasī śobhane ye yathā te patinā saha || 4 ||

BP 1.115.5

नैवाभरणजातानि तेषां भ्राजन्ति वै तथा।
यथा तव यथा पत्युर्न च स्वर्गनिवासिनाम् ॥

Their ornaments do not shine as yours and your husband's do, nor as those of the other dwellers in heaven.

Word-for-word
न एव आभरण-जातानि(naivābharaṇa-jātāni) — not the kinds of ornaments;
तेषाम् भ्राजन्ति(teṣāṃ bhrājanti) — of them shine;
वै तथा(vai tathā) — indeed in that way;
यथा तव(yathā tava) — as yours;
यथा पत्युः(yathā patyuḥ) — as your husband's;
न च स्वर्ग-निवासिनाम्(na ca svarga-nivāsinām) — nor of the dwellers in heaven;
Transliteration
naivābharaṇajātāni teṣāṃ bhrājanti vai tathā | yathā tava yathā patyurna ca svarganivāsinām || 5 ||

BP 1.115.6

सुस्नातचैलतश्चैव युवयोरतिरिच्यते।
लेखाद्यानामपीशानां क्षयातिशयवर्जितः ॥

The excellence of your freshly bathed garments surpasses that of the garments of the lords of the heavenly hosts, which are free from decay and excess.

Word-for-word
सु-स्नात-चैलतः च एव(susnāta-cailataś caiva) — even from freshly bathed garments;
युवयोः(yuvayor) — of you two;
अतिरिच्यते(atiricyate) — is surpassed;
लेख-आदीनाम् अपि ईशानाम्(lekhādyānām apīśānām) — even of the lords of the hosts;
क्षय-अतिशय-वर्जितः(kṣayātiśaya-varjitaḥ) — free from decay and excess;
Transliteration
susnātacailataścaiva yuvayoratiricyate | lekhādyānāmapīśānāṃ kṣayātiśayavarjitaḥ || 6 ||

BP 1.115.7

तपःप्रभावो दानं वा होमो वा कर्मसंज्ञितम्।
युवयोर्यत्समाचक्ष्व तत्सर्वं वरवर्णिनि।
येन मे विक्रमे बुद्धिर्मनुजा येन संगताः ॥

O lovely one, tell me all the austerity, generosity, offering, or other rite that you two performed, by which I have attained this splendour and by which human beings attain such association.

Word-for-word
तपः-प्रभावः(tapaḥ-prabhāvo) — the power of austerity;
दानम् वा(dānaṃ vā) — or generosity;
होमः वा(homo vā) — or offering;
कर्म-संज्ञितम्(karma-saṃjñitam) — known as a rite;
युवयोः यत् समाचक्ष्व(yuvayor yat samācakṣva) — tell what was done by you two;
तत् सर्वम्(tat sarvam) — all that;
वर-वर्णिनि(vara-varṇini) — O lovely one;
येन मे विक्रमे बुद्धिः(yena me vikrame buddhiḥ) — by which I have this splendour;
मनुजाः येन सङ्गताः(manujā yena saṅgatāḥ) — by which human beings attain association;
Transliteration
tapaḥprabhāvo dānaṃ vā homo vā karmasaṃjñitam | yuvayoryatsamācakṣva tatsarvaṃ varavarṇini | yena me vikrame buddhirmanujā yena saṅgatāḥ || 7 ||

BP 1.115.8

कौशल्या उवाच।
यज्ञो यज्ञेश्वरो भानुरावाभ्यां जातु तोषितः।
स्वर्गप्राप्तिरियं तस्य कर्मणः फलमुत्तमम् ॥

Kauśalyā said: Bhānu, the Lord of sacrifices, was once pleased by us through sacrifice. This attainment of heaven is the supreme fruit of that act.

Word-for-word
कौशल्या उवाच(kauśalyovāca) — Kauśalyā said;
यज्ञः(yajñaḥ) — sacrifice;
यज्ञ-ईश्वरः भानुः(yajñeśvaro bhānuḥ) — Bhānu, Lord of sacrifice;
आवाभ्याम्(āvābhyāṃ) — by us two;
जातु तोषितः(jātu toṣitaḥ) — was once pleased;
स्वर्ग-प्राप्तिः इयम्(svarga-prāptir iyam) — this attainment of heaven;
तस्य कर्मणः फलम् उत्तमम्(tasya karmaṇaḥ phalam uttamam) — the supreme fruit of that act;
Transliteration
kauśalyovāca | yajño yajñeśvaro bhānurāvābhyāṃ jātu toṣitaḥ | svargaprāptiriyaṃ tasya karmaṇaḥ phalamuttamam || 8 ||

BP 1.115.9

सुरूपता ततः प्रीतिः पश्यतां चारुवेषिता।
यत्पृच्छसि महाभागे तदप्येषां वदामि ते ॥

Then came beauty, affection, and lovely appearance before those who behold us. O greatly fortunate one, I shall also tell you what you ask.

Word-for-word
सुरूपता(surūpatā) — beauty;
ततः प्रीतिः(tataḥ prītiḥ) — then affection;
पश्यताम्(paśyatām) — of those who behold;
चारु-वेषिता(cāru-veṣitā) — lovely appearance;
यत् पृच्छसि(yat pṛcchasi) — what you ask;
महाभागे(mahābhāge) — O greatly fortunate one;
तत् अपि एषाम् वदामि ते(tad apy eṣāṃ vadāmi te) — that too I tell you;
Transliteration
surūpatā tataḥ prītiḥ paśyatāṃ cāruveṣitā | yatpṛcchasi mahābhāge tadapyeṣāṃ vadāmi te || 9 ||

BP 1.115.10

तीर्थोदकैस्तथा गन्धैः स्नापितो यद्दिवाकरः।
तेन कान्तिरियं नित्यं देवान्त्रिभुवनेश्वरान् ॥

Divākara was bathed with water from sacred places and with fragrant substances; through that came this perpetual radiance among the gods, the lords of the three worlds.

Word-for-word
तीर्थ-उदकैः(tīrthodakaiḥ) — with water from sacred places;
तथा गन्धैः(tathā gandhaiḥ) — and with fragrant substances;
स्नापितः(snāpitaḥ) — was bathed;
यत् दिवाकरः(yad divākaraḥ) — when Divākara;
तेन कान्तिः इयम् नित्यम्(tena kāntir iyaṃ nityam) — through that this perpetual radiance;
देवान् त्रि-भुवन-ईश्वरान्(devāṃs tri-bhuvaneśvarān) — among the gods, lords of the three worlds;
Transliteration
tīrthodakaistathā gandhaiḥ snāpito yaddivākaraḥ | tena kāntiriyaṃ nityaṃ devāṃstribhuvaneśvarān || 10 ||

BP 1.115.11

मनःप्रसादः सौम्यत्वं शरीरे ये च निर्वृताः।
यत्प्रियत्वं च सर्वं स्यात्तद्घृतस्नपनात्फलम् ॥

Peace of mind, gentleness in the body, and every form of pleasantness arise as the fruit of bathing with ghee.

Word-for-word
मनः-प्रसादः(manaḥ-prasādaḥ) — peace of mind;
सौम्यत्वम् शरीरे(saumyatvaṃ śarīre) — gentleness in the body;
ये च निर्वृताः(ye ca nirvṛtāḥ) — and the states of peace;
यत् प्रियत्वम् च सर्वम्(yat priyatvaṃ ca sarvam) — every form of pleasantness;
स्यात्(syāt) — may be;
तत् घृत-स्नपनात् फलम्(tad ghṛta-snapanāt phalam) — that is the fruit of bathing with ghee;
Transliteration
manaḥprasādaḥ saumyatvaṃ śarīre ye ca nirvṛtāḥ | yatpriyatvaṃ ca sarvaṃ syāttadghṛtasnapanātphalam || 11 ||

BP 1.115.12

यान्यभीष्टानि वासांसि यच्चाभीष्टविभूषणम्।
रत्नानि यान्यभीष्टानि यत्प्रियं चानुलेपनम् ॥

Whatever garments are desired, whatever ornaments are desired, whatever jewels are desired, and whatever anointing is dear—

Word-for-word
यानि अभीष्टानि वासांसि(yāny abhīṣṭāni vāsāṃsi) — whatever desired garments;
यत् च अभीष्ट-विभूषणम्(yac cābhīṣṭa-vibhūṣaṇam) — whatever desired ornament;
रत्नानि यानि अभीष्टानि(ratnāni yāny abhīṣṭāni) — whatever desired jewels;
यत् प्रियम् च अनुलेपनम्(yat priyaṃ cānulepanam) — whatever anointing is dear;
Transliteration
yānyabhīṣṭāni vāsāṃsi yaccābhīṣṭavibhūṣaṇam | ratnāni yānyabhīṣṭāni yatpriyaṃ cānulepanam || 12 ||

BP 1.115.13

ये धृता यानि माल्यानि दयितान्यभवन्सदा।
मम भर्तुस्तदैवास्य तदा राज्यं प्रशासतः ॥

The garlands that were worn and were always beloved of my husband became his at that time, when he was ruling the kingdom.

Word-for-word
ये धृताः यानि माल्यानि(ye dhṛtā yāni mālyāni) — the garlands that were worn;
दयितानि अभवन् सदा(dayitāny abhavan sadā) — were always beloved;
मम भर्तुः(mama bhartuḥ) — of my husband;
तदा एव अस्य(tadaivāsya) — then indeed his;
तदा राज्यम् प्रशासतः(tadā rājyaṃ praśāsataḥ) — when he was ruling the kingdom;
Transliteration
ye dhṛtā yāni mālyāni dayitānyabhavansadā | mama bhartustadaivāsya tadā rājyaṃ praśāsataḥ || 13 ||

BP 1.115.14

तानि सर्वाणि सर्वज्ञ सर्वपातरि भानुनि।
दत्तानि तं समुत्थोऽयं गन्धधूपात्मको गुणः ॥

All those things, O all-knowing one and protector of all, were offered to Bhānu; from them arose this quality consisting of fragrance and incense.

Word-for-word
तानि सर्वाणि(tāni sarvāṇi) — all those things;
सर्वज्ञ(sarvajña) — O all-knowing one;
सर्व-पातरि(sarva-pātari) — O protector of all;
भानुनि(bhānuni) — to Bhānu;
दत्तानि(dattāni) — offered;
तम् समुत्थः अयम्(taṃ samuttho'yaṃ) — from them arose this;
गन्ध-धूप-आत्मकः गुणः(gandha-dhūpātmako guṇaḥ) — quality consisting of fragrance and incense;
Transliteration
tāni sarvāṇi sarvajña sarvapātari bhānuni | dattāni taṃ samuttho'yaṃ gandhadhūpātmako guṇaḥ || 14 ||

BP 1.115.15

आहारा दयिता ये च पवित्राश्च निवेदिताः।
त्रिलोककर्तुः सवितुस्तृप्तिस्तद्गुणसम्भवा ॥

The foods that are dear and pure and are offered produce the satisfaction of Savitṛ, the maker of the three worlds; it arises from those qualities.

Word-for-word
आहाराः(āhārāḥ) — foods;
दयिताः ये च(dayitā ye ca) — that are dear and;
पवित्राः च निवेदिताः(pavitrāś ca niveditāḥ) — pure and offered;
त्रि-लोक-कर्तुः सवितुः(tri-loka-kartuḥ savituḥ) — of Savitṛ, maker of the three worlds;
तृप्तिः(tṛptiḥ) — satisfaction;
तत्-गुण-सम्भवा(tad-guṇa-sambhavā) — arising from those qualities;
Transliteration
āhārā dayitā ye ca pavitrāśca niveditāḥ | trilokakartuḥ savitustṛptistadguṇasambhavā || 15 ||

BP 1.115.16

स्वर्गकामेन मे भर्त्रा मया च शुभदर्शने।
कृतमेतत्कृतेनाभूदावयोर्भवसंक्षयः ॥

Desiring heaven, my husband and I performed this, O beautiful one; through that act the end of worldly existence came for us both.

Word-for-word
स्वर्ग-कामेन(svarga-kāmena) — desiring heaven;
मे भर्त्रा(me bhartrā) — my husband;
मया च(mayā ca) — and by me;
शुभ-दर्शने(śubha-darśane) — O beautiful one;
कृतम् एतत्(kṛtam etat) — this was done;
कृतेन अभूत्(kṛtenābhūt) — through the act there arose;
आवयोः भव-संक्षयः(āvayor bhava-saṃkṣayaḥ) — the end of worldly existence for us two;
Transliteration
svargakāmena me bhartrā mayā ca śubhadarśane | kṛtametatkṛtenābhūdāvayorbhavasaṃkṣayaḥ || 16 ||

BP 1.115.17

ये त्वकामाः नराः सम्यक्तत्कुर्वन्ति च शोभने।
तेषां ददाति विश्वेशो भगवान्मुक्तिमीश्वरः ॥

Those persons who properly perform this without desire, O beautiful one, receive liberation from the blessed Lord of the universe, the supreme Lord.

Word-for-word
ये तु अकामाः नराः(ye tv akāmā narāḥ) — those persons without desire;
सम्यक् तत् कुर्वन्ति(samyak tat kurvanti) — properly perform that;
च शोभने(ca śobhane) — O beautiful one;
तेषाम् ददाति(teṣāṃ dadāti) — to them he gives;
विश्वेशः भगवान्(viśveśo bhagavān) — the blessed Lord of the universe;
मुक्तिम् ईश्वरः(muktim īśvaraḥ) — the supreme Lord gives liberation;
Transliteration
ye tvakāmā narāḥ samyaktatkurvanti ca śobhane | teṣāṃ dadāti viśveśo bhagavānmuktimīśvaraḥ || 17 ||

BP 1.115.18

ब्रह्मोवाच।
एवमभ्यर्च्य मार्तण्डमर्कं देवेश्वरं गुरुम्।
प्राप्तोऽस्म्यभिमतान्कामान्कृष्णाहं शाश्वतीः समाः ॥

Brahmā said: Thus having worshipped Mārtaṇḍa, Arka, the Lord and teacher of the gods, I attained my desired wishes, O Kṛṣṇa, for everlasting years.

Word-for-word
ब्रह्मा उवाच(brahmovāca) — Brahmā said;
एवम् अभ्यर्च्य(evam abhyarcya) — having worshipped thus;
मार्तण्डम् अर्कम्(mārtaṇḍam arkam) — Mārtaṇḍa, Arka;
देव-ईश्वरम् गुरुम्(dev eśvaraṃ gurum) — the Lord and teacher of the gods;
प्राप्तः अस्मि(prāpto'smi) — I attained;
अभिमतान् कामान्(abhimatān kāmān) — desired wishes;
कृष्ण अहम्(kṛṣṇāhaṃ) — O Kṛṣṇa, I;
शाश्वतीः समाः(śāśvatīḥ samāḥ) — everlasting years;
Transliteration
brahmovāca | evamabhyarcya mārtaṇḍamarkaṃ deveśvaraṃ gurum | prāpto'smyabhimatānkāmānkṛṣṇāhaṃ śāśvatīḥ samāḥ || 18 ||

BP 1.115.19

चन्दनागुरुकर्पूरकुङ्कुमोशीरपद्मकैः।
अनुलिप्तो नरैर्भक्त्या ददाति सागरोद्भवाम् ॥
