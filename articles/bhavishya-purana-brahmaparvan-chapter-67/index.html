Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 67 — The Inner Self of Sūrya and the One Reality

BP 1.67.1

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
प्रभाते संस्तुतो देवो मूर्तिहेतोर्मया पुरा ।
यजन्तं चापरं देवं भक्तिनम्रं महामतिः ॥ ॥

Brahmā said: Formerly, at dawn, I praised the Deity. Then, O great-minded one, I saw another deity worshiping with a devotion-bent posture for the sake of a form.

Word-for-word
brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said; prabhāte — at dawn; saṃstutaḥ — praised; devaḥ — the Deity; mūrti-hetor — for the sake of a form; mayā — by me; purā — formerly; yajantam — worshiping; ca — and; aparam devam — another deity; bhakti-namram — bent through devotion; mahā-matiḥ — great-minded
Transliteration
brahmovāca |
prabhāte saṃstuto devo mūrtihetor mayā purā |
yajantaṃ cāparaṃ devaṃ bhaktinamraṃ mahāmatiḥ || 1 ||

BP 1.67.2

प्रत्यक्षत्वमथो गत्वा रहस्यं प्रोक्तवान्मम ।
अहं च कृतवान्प्रश्नं दृष्ट्वा प्रत्यक्षतो रविम् ॥ ॥

Having become manifest before me, he then told me a secret. Seeing Ravi directly, I put a question to him.

Word-for-word
pratyakṣatvam — manifestness; athaḥ — then; gatvā — having attained; rahasyam — secret; proktavān — spoke; mama — to me; aham — I; ca — and; kṛtavān — made; praśnam — a question; dṛṣṭvā — having seen; pratyakṣataḥ — directly; ravim — Ravi
Transliteration
pratyakṣatvam atho gatvā rahasyaṃ proktavān mama |
ahaṃ ca kṛtavān praśnaṃ dṛṣṭvā pratyakṣato ravim || 2 ||

BP 1.67.3

वेदेषु च पुराणेषु साङ्गोपाङ्गेषु गीयसे ।
त्वमजः शाश्वतो धाता महाभूतमनुत्तमम् ॥ ॥

You are sung in the Vedas and Purāṇas, together with their limbs and subsidiary limbs. You are unborn, eternal, the supporter, and the unsurpassed great element.

Word-for-word
vedeṣu — in the Vedas; ca — and; purāṇeṣu — in the Purāṇas; sa-aṅga-upāṅgeṣu — with limbs and subsidiary limbs; gīyase — you are sung; tvam — you; ajaḥ — unborn; śāśvataḥ — eternal; dhātā — supporter; mahā-bhūtam — great element; anuttamam — unsurpassed
Transliteration
vedeṣu ca purāṇeṣu sāṅgopāṅgeṣu gīyase |
tvam ajaḥ śāśvato dhātā mahābhūtam anuttamam || 3 ||

BP 1.67.4

प्रतिष्ठितं भूतभव्यं त्वयि सर्वमिदं जगत् ।
चत्वारो ह्याश्रमा देव सर्वे गार्हस्थ्यमूलकाः ॥ ॥

All this universe, past and future, is established in you. The four āśramas, O Deity, all have the household life as their root.

Word-for-word
pratiṣṭhitam — established; bhūta-bhavyam — past and future; tvayi — in you; sarvam idam jagat — all this universe; catvāraḥ — four; hi — indeed; āśramāḥ — stages of life; deva — O Deity; sarve — all; gārhasthya-mūlakāḥ — rooted in household life
Transliteration
pratiṣṭhitaṃ bhūtabhavyaṃ tvayi sarvam idaṃ jagat |
catvāro hy āśramā deva sarve gārhasthyamūlakāḥ || 4 ||

BP 1.67.5

यजन्ते त्वामहरहर्नानामूर्तिसमाश्रिताः ।
पिता माता हि सर्वस्य दैवतं त्वं हि शाश्वतम् ॥ ॥

They worship you day after day, taking refuge in many forms. You are the father and mother of all; you are indeed the eternal deity.

Word-for-word
yajante — worship; tvām — you; ahar-ahaḥ — day after day; nānā-mūrti-samāśritāḥ — taking refuge in many forms; pitā — father; mātā — mother; hi — indeed; sarvasya — of all; daivatam — deity; tvam — you; śāśvatam — eternal
Transliteration
yajante tvām ahar ahar nānāmūrti samāśritāḥ |
pitā mātā hi sarvasya daivataṃ tvaṃ hi śāśvatam || 5 ||

BP 1.67.6

यजसे चैव कं देवमेव चापि न विद्महे ।
कथ्यतां मम देवेश परं कौतूहलं हि मे ॥ ॥

And which deity do you worship? We do not know this at all. Tell me, Lord of the gods; I have a great curiosity.

Word-for-word
yajase — you worship; ca eva — and indeed; kam devam — which deity; eva ca api — even; na vidmahe — we do not know; kathyatām — let it be told; mama — to me; deva-īśa — O Lord of the gods; param — great; kautūhalam — curiosity; hi — indeed; me — my
Transliteration
yajase caiva kaṃ devam eva cāpi na vidmahe |
kathyatāṃ mama deveśa paraṃ kautūhalaṃ hi me || 6 ||

BP 1.67.7

इत्थं मयोक्तो भगवानिदं वचनमब्रवीत् ।
अवाच्यमेतद्वक्तव्यमात्मगुह्यं सनातनम् ॥ ॥

Thus addressed by me, the Blessed Lord spoke these words: This is unutterable, yet it must be spoken—the eternal secret of the Self.

Word-for-word
ittham — thus; mayā uktaḥ — addressed by me; bhagavān — Blessed Lord; idam vacanam — these words; abravīt — spoke; avācyam — unutterable; etat — this; vaktavyam — must be spoken; ātma-guhyam — secret of the Self; sanātanam — eternal
Transliteration
itthaṃ mayokto bhagavān idaṃ vacanam abravīt |
avācyam etad vaktavyam ātmaguḥyaṃ sanātanam || 7 ||

BP 1.67.8

तव भक्तिमतो ब्रह्मन्वक्ष्याम्यहं यथातथम् ।
यतः सूक्ष्ममविज्ञेयमव्यक्तमचलंध्रुवम् ॥ ॥

Because you are devoted, O Brahman, I shall speak it exactly as it is: that which is subtle, unknowable, unmanifest, unmoving, and fixed.

Word-for-word
tava — your; bhakti-mataḥ — devoted; brahman — O Brahman; vakṣyāmi — I shall speak; yathā-tatham — exactly as it is; yataḥ — which; sūkṣmam — subtle; avijñeyam — unknowable; avyaktam — unmanifest; acalam — unmoving; dhruvam — fixed
Transliteration
tava bhaktimato brahman vakṣyāmy ahaṃ yathātatham |
yataḥ sūkṣmam avijñeyam avyaktaṃ acalaṃ dhruvam || 8 ||

BP 1.67.9

इन्द्रियैरिन्द्रियार्थैश्च सर्वभूतैश्च वर्जितम् ।
स ह्यन्तरात्मा भूतानां क्षेत्रज्ञश्चेति कथ्यते ॥ ॥

It is free from the senses, the objects of the senses, and all beings. It is called the inner Self of beings and the knower of the field.

Word-for-word
indriyaiḥ — from senses; indriya-arthaiḥ — from sense objects; ca — and; sarva-bhūtaiḥ — from all beings; varjitam — free; saḥ — he; hi — indeed; antar-ātmā — inner Self; bhūtānām — of beings; kṣetrajñaḥ — knower of the field; ca — and; iti — thus; kathyate — is called
Transliteration
indriyair indriyārthaiś ca sarvabhūtaiś ca varjitam |
sa hy antarātmā bhūtānāṃ kṣetrajñaś ceti kathyate || 9 ||

BP 1.67.10

त्रिगुणव्यतिरिक्तोऽसौ पुरुषश्चेति कथ्यते ।
हिरण्यगर्भो भगवान्सैव बुद्धिरिति स्मृता ॥ ॥

It is said to be the Puruṣa beyond the three guṇas. The Blessed Hiraṇyagarbha is that same [principle], remembered as buddhi.

Word-for-word
tri-guṇa-vyatiriktaḥ — beyond the three guṇas; asau — he; puruṣaḥ — Puruṣa; iti — thus; kathyate — is called; hiraṇya-garbhaḥ — Hiraṇyagarbha; bhagavān — Blessed; sa eva — that same; buddhiḥ — intellect; iti — thus; smṛtā — remembered
Transliteration
triguṇavyatirikto ’sau puruṣaś ceti kathyate |
hiraṇyagarbho bhagavān saiva buddhir iti smṛtā || 10 ||

BP 1.67.11

महानिति च योगेषु प्रधानश्चेति कथ्यते ।
साङ्ख्ये च पठ्यते शास्त्रे नामभिर्बहुभिः सदा ॥ ॥

In the teachings of yoga it is called Mahān, and in the Sāṅkhya scripture it is called Pradhāna; it is always read of under many names.

Word-for-word
mahān — the Great; iti — thus; ca — and; yogeṣu — in the teachings of yoga; pradhānaḥ — Pradhāna; ca — and; iti — thus; kathyate — is called; sāṅkhye — in Sāṅkhya; paṭhyate — is read; śāstre — in scripture; nāmabhiḥ — by names; bahubhiḥ — many; sadā — always
Transliteration
mahān iti ca yogeṣu pradhānaś ceti kathyate |
sāṅkhye ca paṭhyate śāstre nāmabhir bahubhiḥ sadā || 11 ||

BP 1.67.12

विश्वगो विश्वभूतश्च विश्वात्मा विश्वसम्भवः ।
धृतं चैवात्मकं येन इदं त्रैलोक्यमात्मना ॥ ॥

He pervades all; he is all beings, the Self of the universe, and the source of the universe. By his own Self this threefold world is held.

Word-for-word
viśvagaḥ — all-pervading; viśva-bhūtaḥ — source of all beings; viśva-ātmā — Self of the universe; viśva-sambhavaḥ — source of the universe; dhṛtam — held; ca eva — and indeed; ātmakam — as Self; yena — by which; idam trailokyam — this threefold world; ātmanā — by the Self
Transliteration
viśvago viśvabhūtaś ca viśvātmā viśvasambhavaḥ |
dhṛtaṃ caivātmakam yena idaṃ trailokyam ātmanā || 12 ||

BP 1.67.13

अशरीरः शरीरेषु लिप्यते न च कर्मभिः ।
मम अन्तरात्मा तव च ये चान्ये देहसंज्ञकाः ॥ ॥

Bodiless among bodies, it is not stained by actions. It is the inner Self of me, of you, and of all others who are known as embodied.

Word-for-word
aśarīraḥ — bodiless; śarīreṣu — among bodies; lipyate — is stained; na — not; ca — and; karmabhiḥ — by actions; mama — my; antar-ātmā — inner Self; tava — your; ca — and; ye anye — and others who; deha-saṃjñakāḥ — known as embodied
Transliteration
aśarīraḥ śarīreṣu lipyate na ca karmabhiḥ |
mamāntarātmā tava ca ye cānye dehasaṃjñakāḥ || 13 ||

BP 1.67.14

सर्वेषां साक्षिभूतोऽसौ न करोति न लिप्यते ।
सगुणो निर्गुणो विष्णुर्ज्ञानगम्यो ह्यसौ स्मृतः ॥ ॥

It is the witness of all; it neither acts nor is stained. It is Viṣṇu, with attributes and without attributes, remembered as attainable through knowledge.

Word-for-word
sarveṣām — of all; sākṣibhūtaḥ — having become the witness; asau — he; na karoti — does not act; na lipyate — is not stained; saguṇaḥ — with attributes; nirguṇaḥ — without attributes; viṣṇuḥ — Viṣṇu; jñāna-gamyaḥ — attainable through knowledge; hi — indeed; asau — he; smṛtaḥ — remembered
Transliteration
sarveṣāṃ sākṣibhūto ’sau na karoti na lipyate |
saguṇo nirguṇo viṣṇur jñānagamyo hy asau smṛtaḥ || 14 ||

BP 1.67.15

सर्वतः पाणिपादोऽसौ सर्वतोऽक्षिशिरोमुखः ।
सर्वतः श्रुतियुक्तोऽसौ सर्वमावृत्य तिष्ठति ॥ ॥

He has hands and feet everywhere, eyes, heads, and faces everywhere; he possesses ears everywhere and stands pervading everything.

Word-for-word
sarvataḥ — everywhere; pāṇi-pādaḥ — hands and feet; asau — he; sarvataḥ — everywhere; akṣi-śiras-mukhaḥ — eyes, heads, and faces; sarvataḥ — everywhere; śruti-yuktaḥ — possessed of ears; sarvam āvṛtya — pervading everything; tiṣṭhati — stands
Transliteration
sarvataḥ pāṇipādo ’sau sarvato ’kṣiśiromukhaḥ |
sarvataḥ śrutiyukto ’sau sarvam āvṛtya tiṣṭhati || 15 ||

BP 1.67.16

विश्वमूर्धा विश्वभुजो विश्वपादाक्षिनासिकः ।
एकश्चरति क्षेत्रेषु स्वैरचारी यथासुखम् ॥ ॥

He has the universe as his head, arms throughout the universe, and feet, eyes, and noses throughout the universe. He moves alone through the fields, freely, as he pleases.

Word-for-word
viśva-mūrdhā — whose head is the universe; viśva-bhujaḥ — whose arms are the universe; viśva-pāda-akṣi-nāsikaḥ — whose feet, eyes, and noses are the universe; ekaḥ — alone; carati — moves; kṣetreṣu — through fields; svaira-cārī — freely moving; yathā-sukham — as he pleases
Transliteration
viśvamūrdhā viśvabhujo viśvapādākṣināsikaḥ |
ekaś carati kṣetreṣu svairacārī yathāsukham || 16 ||

BP 1.67.17

क्षेत्राण्यस्य शरीराणि बीजं चापि शुभाशुभम् ।
तानि वेत्ति स योगात्मा अतः क्षेत्रज्ञ उच्यते ।
अव्यक्तके पुरे शेते तेनासौ पुरुषः स्मृतः ॥ ॥

The fields are his bodies, and their seed is good and evil. The Self joined with yoga knows them; therefore he is called the knower of the field. He lies in the unmanifest city; therefore he is remembered as Puruṣa.

Word-for-word
kṣetrāṇi — fields; asya — his; śarīrāṇi — bodies; bījam — seed; ca api — and also; śubha-aśubham — good and evil; tāni vetti — knows them; saḥ — he; yoga-ātmā — Self joined with yoga; ataḥ — therefore; kṣetrajñaḥ — knower of the field; ucyate — is called; avyakta-ke pure — in the unmanifest city; śete — lies; tena — therefore; asau — he; puruṣaḥ — Puruṣa; smṛtaḥ — remembered
Transliteration
kṣetrāṇy asya śarīrāṇi bījaṃ cāpi śubhāśubham |
tāni vetti sa yogātmā ataḥ kṣetrajña ucyate |
avyaktake pure śete tenāsau puruṣaḥ smṛtaḥ || 17 ||

BP 1.67.18

विश्वं बहुविधं ज्ञेयं स च सर्वत्र विद्यते ।
तस्मात्स बहुरूपत्वाद्विश्वरूप इति स्मृतः ॥ ॥

The universe is to be known as manifold, and he is present everywhere. Therefore, because of his many forms, he is remembered as Viśvarūpa.

Word-for-word
viśvam — universe; bahu-vidham — manifold; jñeyam — to be known; saḥ — he; ca — and; sarvatra — everywhere; vidyate — is present; tasmāt — therefore; bahu-rūpatvāt — because of many forms; viśva-rūpaḥ — universal-formed; iti — thus; smṛtaḥ — remembered
Transliteration
viśvaṃ bahuvidhaṃ jñeyaṃ sa ca sarvatra vidyate |
tasmāt sa bahurūpatvād viśvarūpa iti smṛtaḥ || 18 ||

BP 1.67.19

महापुरुषशब्दं हि बिभर्त्येष सनातनः ।
स तु वै विक्रियापन्नः सृजत्यात्मानमात्मना ॥ ॥

This eternal one bears the designation Mahāpuruṣa. When he undergoes transformation, he creates himself by himself.

BP 1.67.20

आकाशात्पतितं तोयं याति स्वाद्वन्तरं यथा ।
भूमे रसविशेषेण तथा गुणवशात्तु सः ॥ ॥

BP 1.67.21

एक एव यथा वायुर्देहे तिष्ठति पञ्चधा ।
एकत्वं च पृथक्त्वं च तथा तस्य न संशयः ॥ ॥

BP 1.67.22

स्थानान्तरविशेषेण यथाग्निर्लभते पराम् ।
संज्ञां दावाग्निराद्येषु तथा देवो ह्यसौ स्मृतः ॥ ॥

BP 1.67.23

यथा दीपसहस्राणि दीप एकः प्रसूयते ।

BP 1.67.24

स यदा बुध्यतेत्मानं तदा भवति केवलः ।
एकत्वं प्रलये चास्य बहुत्वं स्यात्प्रवर्तने ॥ ॥

When he knows the Self, then he becomes alone. At dissolution there is oneness for him, and at manifestation there is multiplicity.

Word-for-word
