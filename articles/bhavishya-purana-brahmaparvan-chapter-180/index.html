Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 180 — Pacification Rite

BP 1.180.1

गंगा पुण्या महादेवी यमुना नर्मदा नदी ।। गौतमी चापि कावेरी वरुणा देविका तथा ।। १ ।।

The sacred great goddess Gaṅgā, Yamunā, the river Narmadā, Gautamī, Kāverī, Varuṇā, and Devikā likewise.

BP 1.180.2

सर्वग्रहपतिं देवं लोकेशं लोकनायकम् ।। पूजयंति सदा नद्यः सूर्यसद्भावभाविताः ।। शांतिं कुर्वंतु ते नित्यं सूर्यध्यानैकमानसाः ।।२।।

The rivers always worship the god who is lord of all planets, lord of the worlds, and leader of the world, their minds filled with the Sun’s true nature. With minds fixed only on meditation on the Sun, may they always bring you peace.

BP 1.180.3

निरंजना नाम नदी शोणश्चापि महानदः ।। मंदाकिनी च परमा तथा सन्निहिता शुभा।।३।।

The river named Nirañjanā and the great river Śoṇa; Mandākinī, the supreme, and auspicious Sannihitā likewise.

BP 1.180.4

एताश्चान्याश्च बहवो भुवि दिव्यंतरिक्षके।। सूर्यार्चनरता नद्यः कुर्वंतु तव शांतिकम् ।।४।।

These and many other rivers on earth, in heaven, and in the atmosphere, devoted to Sun-worship, may bring you peace.

BP 1.180.5

महावैश्रवणो देवो यक्षराजो महर्षिकः ।। यक्षकोटिपरीवारो यक्षसंख्येयसंयुतः ।।५।।

The god Mahāvaiśravaṇa, king of nature spirits and great seer, surrounded by ten million nature spirits, attended by countless nature spirits.

BP 1.180.6

महाविभवसंपन्नः सूर्यपादार्चने रतः ।। सूर्यध्यानैकपरमः सूर्यभावेन भावितः।।६।।

Possessed of great abundance, devoted to worship of the Sun’s feet, supremely intent on meditation on the Sun, imbued with the Sun’s nature.

BP 1.180.7

शांतिं करोतु ते प्रीतः पद्मपत्रायतेक्षणः ।। माणिभद्रो महायक्षो मणिरत्नविभूषितः ।।७।।

Pleased, with eyes long as lotus petals, may he bring you peace. Māṇibhadra, the great nature spirit, adorned with jewels and gems,

BP 1.180.8

मनोहरेण हारेण कंठलग्नेन राजते ।। यक्षिणीयक्षकन्याभिः परिवारितविग्रहः ।। सूर्यार्चनसमासक्तः करोतु तव शांतिकम्।।८।।

shines with a lovely necklace resting on his neck. Surrounded by female nature spirits and their maidens, wholly intent on Sun-worship, may he bring you peace.

BP 1.180.9

सुचिरो नाम यक्षेंद्रो मणिकुडल भूषितः ।। ललाटे हेमपटलप्रबद्धेन विराजते ।। ९ ।।

The nature-spirit lord named Sucira, adorned with jewel earrings, shines with a golden band fastened on his forehead.

BP 1.180.10

बहुयक्षसमाकीर्णो यक्षैर्नमितविग्रहः ।। सूर्यपूजापरो युक्तः करोतु तव शांतिकम् ।। १०।।

Surrounded by many nature spirits, bowed to by them, intent on Sun-worship and devoted, may he bring you peace.

BP 1.180.11

पांचिको नाम यक्षेंद्रः कंठाभरणभूषितः ।। कुक्कुटेन विचित्रेण बहुरत्नान्वितेन तु ।। ११ ।।

Pāñcika by name, lord of nature spirits, adorned with a neck ornament, and with a variegated cock adorned with many jewels;

BP 1.180.12

यक्षवृन्दसमाकीर्णो यक्षकोटिसमन्वितः ।। सूर्यार्चनकरः श्रीमान्करोतु तव शांतिकम् ।। १२ ।।

surrounded by groups of nature spirits, accompanied by ten million of them, glorious, performing Sun-worship, may he bring you peace.

BP 1.180.13

धृतराष्ट्रो महातेजा नानायक्षाधिपः खग ।। दिव्यपट्टः शुक्लच्छत्रो मणिकांचनभूषितः ।। १३ ।।

Dhṛtarāṣṭra, of great splendor, lord of many nature spirits, O bird, wearing a divine garment and a white parasol, adorned with jewels and gold;

BP 1.180.14

सूर्यभक्तः सूर्यरतः सूर्यपूजापरायणः ।। सूर्यप्रसादसंपन्नः करोतु तव शांतिकम् ।। १४ ।।

a devotee of the Sun, delighting in the Sun, devoted to Sun-worship, endowed with the Sun’s favor, may he bring you peace.

BP 1.180.15

विरूपाक्षश्च यक्षेंद्रः श्वेतवासा महाद्युतिः ।। नानाकांचनमालाभिरुपशोभितकंधरः ।। १५ ।।

Virūpākṣa, lord of nature spirits, clad in white, of great radiance, his shoulders adorned with many golden garlands;

BP 1.180.16

सूर्यपूजापरो भक्तः कंजाक्षः कंजसन्निभः ।। तेजसादित्यसंकाशः करोतु तव शांतिकम् ।। १६ ।।

devoted to Sun-worship, a worshipper, lotus-eyed, lotus-like, resembling the Sun in splendor, may he bring you peace.

BP 1.180.17

अंतीरक्षगता यक्षा ये यक्षाः स्वर्गगामिनः ।। नानारूपधरा यक्षाः सूर्यभक्ता दृढव्रताः ।। १७ ।।

Those nature spirits in the atmosphere and those who go to heaven, spirits bearing many forms, devoted to the Sun, firm in their vows;

BP 1.180.18

तद्भक्तास्तद्गमनसः सूर्यपूजासमुत्सुकाः ।। शांतिं कुर्वंतु ते हृष्टाः शांताः शांतिपरायणाः ।। १८ ।।

devoted to him, their minds turned toward him, eager for Sun-worship, joyful, peaceful, devoted to peace, may they bring you peace.

BP 1.180.19

यक्षिण्यो विविधाकारास्तथा यक्षकुमारकाः ।। यक्षकन्या महाभागाः सूर्याराधनतत्पराः ।। १९ ।।

Female nature spirits of various forms, and spirit youths, fortunate spirit maidens, intent on Sun-worship;

BP 1.180.20

शांतिं स्वस्त्ययनं क्षेमं बलं कल्याणमुत्तमम् ।। सिद्धिं चाशु प्रयच्छंतु नित्यं च सुसमाहिताः ।। २० ।।

ever well-composed, may they quickly grant peace, well-being, safety, strength, excellent good fortune, and success.

BP 1.180.21

पर्वताः सर्वतः सर्वे वृक्षाश्चैव महर्द्धिकाः ।। सूर्यभक्ताः सदा सर्वे शांतिं कुर्वंतु ते सदा ।। २१ ।।

All mountains everywhere, and flourishing trees, all always devoted to the Sun, may they always bring you peace.

BP 1.180.22

सागराः सर्वतः सर्वे गृहारण्यानि कृत्स्नशः ।। सूर्यस्याराधनपराः कुर्वंतु तव शांतिकम् ।। २२ ।।

All oceans everywhere, all houses and forests, intent on worship of the Sun, may they bring you peace.

BP 1.180.23

राक्षसाः सर्वतः सर्वे घोररूपा महाबलाः ।। स्थलजा राक्षसा ये तु अंतरिक्षगताश्च ये ।।२३।।

All demons everywhere, terrible in form and mighty; the demons born on earth and those in the atmosphere;

BP 1.180.24

पाताले राक्षसा ये तु नित्यं सूर्यार्चने रताः ।। शांतिं कुर्वंतु ते सर्वे तेजसा नित्यदीपिताः ।। २४ ।।

the demons in the netherworld, ever engaged in Sun-worship, all blazing with splendor, may they bring you peace.

BP 1.180.25

प्रेताः प्रेतगणाः सर्वे ये प्रेताः सर्वतोमुखाः ।। अतिदीप्ताश्च ये प्रेता ये प्रेता रुधिराशनाः ।।२५।।

All spirits of the dead and their hosts, those facing in every direction, those exceedingly radiant, those that eat blood;

BP 1.180.26

अंतरिक्षे च ये प्रेतास्तथा ये स्वर्गवासिनः ।। पाताले भूतले वापि ये प्रेताः कामरूपिणः ।। २६ ।।

the spirits of the dead in the atmosphere and those dwelling in heaven, in the netherworld or on earth, having forms at will;

BP 1.180.27

एकचक्रो रथो यस्य यस्तु देवो वृषध्वजः ।। तेजसा तस्य देवस्य शांतिं कुर्वंतु ते सदा ।।२७।।

may they always bring peace through the splendor of the god whose chariot has one wheel and whose banner bears a bull.

BP 1.180.28

ये पिशाचा महावीर्या वृद्धिमंतो महाबलाः ।। नानारूपधराः सर्वे सर्वे च गुणवत्तराः ।।२८।।

The mighty demons, prosperous and very strong, all bearing many forms, all endowed with qualities;

BP 1.180.29

अंतरिक्षे पिशाचा ये स्वर्गे ये च महाबलाः ।। पाताले भूतले ये च बहुरूपा मनोजवाः ।। २९ ।।

the demons in the atmosphere and in heaven, of great might; those in the netherworld and on earth, many-formed and swift as the mind;

BP 1.180.30

यस्याहं सारथिर्वीर यस्य त्वं तुरगः सदा ।। तेजसा तस्य देवस्य शांतिं कुर्वंतु तेऽञ्जसा ।। ३० ।।

O hero, I am the charioteer of the one whose horse you always are; by that god’s splendor, may they swiftly bring you peace.

BP 1.180.31

अपस्मारग्रहाः सर्वे सर्वे चापि ज्वरग्रहाः ।। ये च स्वर्गस्थिताः सर्वे भूमिगा ये ग्रहोत्तमाः ।। ।। ३१ ।।

All the seizure spirits and all the fever spirits, those situated in heaven and the excellent spirits that move on earth;

BP 1.180.32

पाताले तु ग्रहा ये च ये ग्रहाः सर्वतो गताः ।। दक्षिणे किरणे यस्य सूर्यस्य च स्थितो हरिः ।। ३२ ।।

the spirits in the netherworld and those that have gone everywhere; Hari is situated in the Sun’s southern ray.

BP 1.180.33

हरो यस्य सदा वामे ललाटे कंजजः स्थितः ।। तेजसा तस्य देवस्य शांतिं कुर्वंतु ते सदा ।। ३३ ।।

Hara is always on his left, and the lotus-born one is situated on his forehead. By that god’s splendor, may they always bring peace.

BP 1.180.34

इति देवादयः सर्वे सूर्ययज्ञविधायिनः ।। कुर्वंतु जगतः शांतिं सूर्यभक्तेषु सर्वदा ।। ३४ ।।

Thus all the gods and the rest, performers of the Sun-sacrifice, may always bring peace to the world among the Sun’s devotees.

BP 1.180.35

जयः सूर्याय देवाय तमोहंत्रे विवस्वते ।। जयप्रदाय सूर्याय भास्कराय नमोस्तु ते ।। ३५ ।।

Victory to the Sun, the god, the destroyer of darkness, Vivasvat. Victory to the victory-giving Sun, Bhāskara; homage to you.

BP 1.180.36

ग्रहॊत्तमाय देवाय जयः कल्याणकारिणे ।। जयः पद्मविकाशाय बुधरूपाय ते नमः ।। ३६ ।।

Victory to the foremost of planets, the god who brings welfare. Victory to the lotus-opener, in the form of Budha; homage to you.

BP 1.180.37

जयः दीप्तिविधानाय जयः शांतिविधायिने ।। तमोघ्नाय जयायैव अजिताय नमोनमः ।।३७।।

Victory to the arranger of radiance; victory to the maker of peace; repeated homage to the victory-giving, darkness-destroying, unconquered one.

BP 1.180.38

जयार्क जयदीप्तीश सहस्रकिरणोज्ज्वल ।। जय निर्मितलोकस्त्वमजिताय नमोनमः ।। ३८ ।।

Victory, O Arka; victory, O lord of radiance, bright with a thousand rays. Victory to you, maker of the worlds; repeated homage to the unconquered one.

BP 1.180.39

गायत्रीदेहरूपाय सावित्रीदयिताय च ।। धराधराय सूर्याय मार्तंडाय नमोनमः ।। ३९ ।।

Repeated homage to the Sun whose body is Gāyatrī, who is dear to Sāvitrī, who bears the earth, and to Mārtaṇḍa.

BP 1.180.40

सुमंतुरुवाच ।। एवं हि कुर्वतः शांतिमरुणस्य महीपते ।। श्रेयसे वैनतेयस्य गरुडस्य महात्मनः ।। ४० ।।

Sumantu said: Thus, O king, Aruṇa’s pacification was performed for the welfare of the great-souled Garuḍa, son of Vinatā;

BP 1.180.41

एतस्मिन्नेव काले तु सुपर्णः पत्रवानभूत् ।। तेजसा बुधसंकाशो वलेन हरिणा समः ।। ४१ ।।

at that very time Suparṇa became feathered, radiant as Budha and equal to Hari in strength.

BP 1.180.42

संपूर्णावयवो राजन्यथापूर्वं तथाभवत् ।। प्रसादाद्देवदेवस्य भास्करस्य महात्मनः ।। ४२ ।।

Complete in all his limbs, O king, he became as he was before, by the favor of the god of gods, the great-souled Bhāskara.

BP 1.180.43

एवमन्येऽपि राजेंद्र मानवा ये च रोगिणः ।। अस्मित्कृतेऽग्निकार्ये तु विरुजास्ते भवंति हि ।। तस्माद्यत्नेन कर्तव्यमग्निकार्यं विधानतः ।। ४३ ।।

Thus other humans too, O king, who are ill become free from illness when this fire rite is performed. Therefore the fire rite should be performed carefully according to rule.

BP 1.180.44

करणीयं च राजेंद्र मानवैश्च प्ररोगिभिः ।। अस्मिन्कृते अग्निकाये विरुजास्ते भवंति हि।। ४४ ।।

It should also be performed, O king, by humans who are greatly ill; when this fire rite is performed, they indeed become free from illness.

BP 1.180.45

ग्रहोपघाते दुर्भिक्ष उत्पातेषु च कृत्स्नशः ।। अवर्षमाणे पर्जन्ये लक्षहोमसमन्वितः ।। ४५ ।।

During affliction by planets, famine, and portents of every kind, when the rain-cloud does not rain, with a hundred thousand fire-offerings;

BP 1.180.46

पूजयित्वा प्रसूक्तं तु ध्यात्वा वीरं प्रयत्नतः ।। वारुणैश्च तथा सूक्तैर्होमं कुर्याद्विचक्षणः ।। ४६ ।।

having worshipped with the excellent hymn and meditated carefully on the hero, the wise one should make offerings with the hymns to Varuṇa likewise.

BP 1.180.47

चेतसा सुप्रसन्नेन सर्पिषा मधुना सह ।। तिलैर्यवैश्च सहितैः पायसं मधुना तथा ।। ४७ ।।

With a very clear mind, with ghee together with honey; with sesame and barley together, and rice pudding with honey likewise.

BP 1.180.48

इदं च शांतिकं कुर्याद्बलिं दयात्प्रयत्नतः ।। एवं कृते श्रियं देवा वर्षंते कामना नृणाम् ।। ४८ ।।

One should perform this pacification rite and carefully give the food-offering. When it is done thus, the gods rain prosperity and people’s desires.

BP 1.180.49

इत्येवं शांतिकाध्यायं यः पठेच्छृणुयादपि ।। विधिना सर्वलोकस्तु ध्यायमानो दिवाकरम्।। ४९ ।।

Whoever reads or hears this chapter of pacification, all people meditating on Divākara according to rule—

BP 1.180.50

स विजित्य रणे शत्रुं मानं च परमं लभेत् ।। अक्षयं मोदते कालमतिरस्कृतशासनः ।। ५० ।।

conquers the enemy in battle and obtains the highest honor; he rejoices through imperishable time, his command unopposed.

BP 1.180.51

व्याधिभिर्नाभिभूयेत पुत्रपौत्रैः प्रतिष्ठितः ।। भवेदादित्यसदृशस्तेजसा प्रभया तथा ।। ५१ ।।

He is not overcome by diseases, established through sons and grandsons; he becomes like Āditya in splendor and radiance.

BP 1.180.52

यानुद्दिश्य पठेद्वीर वाचको मानवो भुवि।। न पीड्यते च तै रोगैर्वातपित्तकफात्मकैः ।। ५२ ।।

O hero, the human reciter on earth, reciting for those whom he intends, is not afflicted by diseases of wind, bile, and phlegm.

BP 1.180.53

नाकाले मरणं तस्य न सर्पैश्चापि दश्यते ।। न विषं क्रमते देहे न जडांध्यं न मूकता ।। ५३ ।।

For him there is no untimely death, nor is he bitten by serpents; poison does not enter the body, nor dullness, blindness, or muteness.

BP 1.180.54

न चोत्पत्ति भयं तस्य नाभिचारकजं भवेत् ।। ये रोगा ये महोत्पाता येऽहयश्च महाविषाः ।। ते सर्वे प्रशमं यांति श्रवणादस्य भारत।।।५४।।

No fear arises for him, nor harm born of sorcery. Whatever diseases, great portents, and highly venomous serpents there are, all are pacified by hearing this, O Bhārata.

BP 1.180.55

यत्पुण्यं सर्व तीर्थानां गंगादीनां विशेषतः ।। तत्पुण्यं कोटिगुणितं प्राप्नोति श्रवणादिभिः ।। ५५ ।।

The merit of all pilgrimage places, especially those beginning with Gaṅgā—that merit, multiplied by ten million, he obtains through hearing and the rest.

BP 1.180.56

दशानां राजसूयानामन्येषां च विशेषतः ।। जीवेद्वर्षशतं साग्रं सर्वव्याधिविवर्जितः ।। ५६ ।।

The merit of ten royal consecration rites and of the other rites in particular; he lives more than a hundred years, free from all diseases.

BP 1.180.57

गोघ्नश्चैव कृतघ्नश्च ब्रह्महा गुरुतल्पगः ।। शरणागतदीनार्तमित्रविश्रभघातकः ।। ५७ ।।

The cow-killer, the ungrateful one, the killer of a Brahmin, the violator of the teacher’s bed; the killer of one who seeks refuge, of the poor, the afflicted, a friend, or one who trusts;

BP 1.180.58

दुष्टः पापसमाचारः पितृहा मातृहा तथा ।। श्रवणादस्य पापेभ्यो मुच्यते नात्र संशयः ।। ५८ ।।

the wicked one of sinful conduct, the killer of his father and likewise of his mother, is released from sins through hearing this; there is no doubt.

BP 1.180.59

इतिहासमिमं पुण्यमग्निकार्यमनुत्तमम् ।। न दद्यात्कस्यचिद्वीर मूर्खस्य कलुषात्मनः ।। ५९ ।।

This sacred history, this excellent fire rite, O hero, should not be given to any fool of impure mind.

BP 1.180.60

सूर्यभक्ते सदा देयं सूर्येण कथितं पुरा ।। अरुणस्य महाबाहो गरुडस्यारुणेन च ।। ६० ।।

It should always be given to a devotee of the Sun. It was told formerly by the Sun concerning Aruṇa, O mighty-armed one, and by Aruṇa concerning Garuḍa.

BP 1.180.61

गरुडेन पुरा प्रोक्तं भोजकानां महात्मनाम् ।। सूर्यशर्मसुखादीनां शाकद्वीपे महीपते ।। ६१ ।।

It was formerly spoken by Garuḍa to the great-souled Sun-priests, O king, to Sūryaśarman and the others in Śākadvīpa.

BP 1.180.62

तैश्चापि कथितं पुण्यं मुनेर्व्यासस्य धीमतः ।। तेनापि कथितं पुण्यं सर्वपापभयापहम् ।। ६२ ।।

By them this sacred account was told to the wise sage Vyāsa; by him too this sacred account, removing fear of all sins, was told.
