Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 36 — Serpent Classification and the Śrāvaṇa Nāga-pañcamī Vow

BP 1.36.1

॥ गौतम उवाच ॥ ॥
कीदृशं सर्पदष्टस्य सर्पिण्याः कीदृशं भवेत् ॥
कुमारदष्टः कीदृक्स्यात्सूतिकादंशितस्य च ॥ ॥

Gautama said: What are the signs when one is bitten by a male snake or a female snake, and what are they when bitten by a young snake or one that has recently given birth?

Word-for-word
कीदृशम् सर्पदष्टस्य (kīdṛśam sarpa-daṣṭasya) — signs of male-snake bite;
सर्पिण्याः कीदृशम् (sarpiṇyāḥ kīdṛśam) — of female-snake bite;
कुमारदष्टः (kumāra-daṣṭaḥ) — bitten by young snake;
सूतिकादंशितस्य (sūtikā-daṃśitasya) — bitten by one recently giving birth
Transliteration
|| gautama uvāca|| ||
kīdṛśaṃ sarpadaṣṭasya sarpiṇyāḥ kīdṛśaṃ bhavet ||
kumāradaṣṭaḥ kīdṛksyātsūtikādaṃśitasya ca || 1 ||

BP 1.36.2

रूपं नपुंसकेनेह व्यंतरेण च कीदृशम् ॥
एतदाख्याहि मे सर्वमेभिर्दष्टस्य लक्षणम् ॥ ॥ ॥

What are the signs here when bitten by a sexless or intermediate snake? Tell me all the characteristics of those bitten by these.

Word-for-word
नपुंसकेन (napuṃsakena) — by sexless snake;
व्यन्तरेण (vyantareṇa) — by intermediate snake;
कीदृशम् रूपम् (kīdṛśam rūpam) — what signs;
एभिः दष्टस्य लक्षणम् (ebhiḥ daṣṭasya lakṣaṇam) — characteristics of one bitten by these;
आख्याहि सर्वम् (ākhyāhi sarvam) — tell all
Transliteration
rūpaṃ napuṃsakeneha vyaṃtareṇa ca kīdṛśam ||
etadākhyāhi me sarvamebhirdaṣṭasya lakṣaṇam || 2 || ||

BP 1.36.3

॥ कश्यप उवाच ॥ ॥
अतः परं प्रवक्ष्यामि नागानां रूपलक्षणम् ॥
सर्पदष्टस्य च तथा समासाद्द्विजपुंगव ॥ ॥

Kaśyapa said: Next, foremost of brāhmaṇas, I shall explain briefly the forms and marks of Nāgas and of those bitten by snakes.

Word-for-word
अतः परम् प्रवक्ष्यामि (ataḥ param pravakṣyāmi) — next I shall explain;
नागानाम् रूपलक्षणम् (nāgānām rūpa-lakṣaṇam) — forms and marks of Nāgas;
सर्पदष्टस्य च (sarpa-daṣṭasya ca) — and snake-bitten person;
समासात् (samāsāt) — briefly
Transliteration
|| kaśyapa uvāca || ||
ataḥ paraṃ pravakṣyāmi nāgānāṃ rūpalakṣaṇam ||
sarpadaṣṭasya ca tathā samāsāddvijapuṃgava || 3 ||

BP 1.36.4

अथ सर्पेण दष्टस्य ऊर्ध्व दृष्टिः प्रजायते ॥
सर्पीदष्टस्य च तथा अधोदृष्टिः प्रजायते ॥ ॥

One bitten by a male snake develops an upward gaze; one bitten by a female snake develops a downward gaze.

Word-for-word
सर्पेण दष्टस्य (sarpeṇa daṣṭasya) — one bitten by male snake;
ऊर्ध्वदृष्टिः (ūrdhva-dṛṣṭiḥ) — upward gaze;
सर्पीदष्टस्य (sarpī-daṣṭasya) — one bitten by female snake;
अधोदृष्टिः (adho-dṛṣṭiḥ) — downward gaze
Transliteration
atha sarpeṇa daṣṭasya ūrdhva dṛṣṭiḥ prajāyate ||
sarpīdaṣṭasya ca tathā adhodṛṣṭiḥ prajāyate || 4 ||

BP 1.36.5

कन्यादष्टस्य वामा स्यादृष्टिर्द्विजवरोत्तम ॥
कुमारेणापि दष्टस्य दक्षिणा एव जायते ॥ ॥

Best of brāhmaṇas, the gaze turns left when bitten by a young female and right when bitten by a young male.

Word-for-word
कन्यादष्टस्य (kanyā-daṣṭasya) — bitten by young female;
वामा दृष्टिः (vāmā dṛṣṭiḥ) — leftward gaze;
कुमारेण दष्टस्य (kumāreṇa daṣṭasya) — bitten by young male;
दक्षिणा जायते (dakṣiṇā jāyate) — rightward arises
Transliteration
kanyādaṣṭasya vāmā syādṛṣṭirdvijavarottama ||
kumāreṇāpi daṣṭasya dakṣiṇā eva jāyate ||5||

BP 1.36.6

गर्भिण्या वाथ दष्टस्य तथा स्वेदश्च जायते ॥
रोमांचः सूतिकायास्तु वेपथुश्चापि जायते ॥
नपुंसकेन दष्टस्य अंगमर्दः प्रजायते ॥ ॥ ॥

A bite from a pregnant snake produces sweating; one from a snake that has recently given birth produces horripilation and trembling; a sexless snakes bite produces aching of the limbs.

Word-for-word
गर्भिण्या दष्टस्य (garbhiṇyā daṣṭasya) — bitten by pregnant snake;
स्वेदः जायते (svedaḥ jāyate) — sweating arises;
सूतिकायाः (sūtikāyāḥ) — from recently delivered snake;
रोमाञ्चः वेपथुः (romāñcaḥ vepathuḥ) — horripilation and trembling;
नपुंसकेन दष्टस्य (napuṃsakena daṣṭasya) — bitten by sexless snake;
अङ्गमर्दः (aṅga-mardaḥ) — limb aching
Transliteration
garbhiṇyā vātha daṣṭasya tathā svedaśca jāyate ||
romāṃcaḥ sūtikāyāstu vepathuścāpi jāyate ||
napuṃsakena daṣṭasya aṃgamardaḥ prajāyate || ||6||

BP 1.36.7

पन्नग्यः प्रभवो रात्रौ दिवा सर्पो विषाधिकः ॥
नपुंसकस्तु संध्यायां कश्यपेन तु भाषितम् ॥ ॥

Female snakes are more potent at night, male snakes by day, and sexless snakes at twilight—so Kaśyapa taught.

Word-for-word
पन्नग्यः प्रभवः रात्रौ (pannagyaḥ prabhavaḥ rātrau) — female snakes potent at night;
दिवा सर्पः विषाधिकः (divā sarpaḥ viṣādhikaḥ) — male more venomous by day;
नपुंसकः सन्ध्यायाम् (napuṃsakaḥ sandhyāyām) — sexless at twilight;
कश्यपेन भाषितम् (kaśyapena bhāṣitam) — taught by Kaśyapa
Transliteration
pannagyaḥ prabhavo rātrau divā sarpo viṣādhikaḥ||
napuṃsakastu saṃdhyāyāṃ kaśyapena tu bhāṣitam||7||

BP 1.36.8

अंधकारे तु दष्टो य उदके गहने वने ॥
सुप्तो वा चेत्प्रमत्तो वा यदि सर्पं न पश्यति ॥
दष्टरूपाण्यजानन्वै कथं वैद्यचिकित्सितम् ॥ ॥

If someone is bitten in darkness, water, dense forest, while asleep or inattentive, and does not see the snake or recognize the bite-signs, how is a physician to treat the case?

Word-for-word
अन्धकारे दष्टः (andhakāre daṣṭaḥ) — bitten in darkness;
उदके गहने वने (udake gahane vane) — in water or dense forest;
सुप्तः प्रमत्तः वा (suptaḥ pramattaḥ vā) — asleep or inattentive;
सर्पम् न पश्यति (sarpam na paśyati) — does not see snake;
दष्टरूपाणि अजानन् (daṣṭa-rūpāṇi ajānan) — not knowing bite-signs;
कथम् वैद्यचिकित्सितम् (katham vaidya-cikitsitam) — how physician treats
Transliteration
aṃdhakāre tu daṣṭo ya udake gahane vane ||
supto vā cetpramatto vā yadi sarpaṃ na paśyati ||
daṣṭarūpāṇyajānanvai kathaṃ vaidyacikitsitam||8||

BP 1.36.9

चतुर्विधा इह प्रोक्ताः पन्नगास्तु महात्मना ॥
दर्वीकरा मंडलिनो राजिला व्यंतरास्तथा ॥ ॥

The great sage described four kinds of snakes: hooded snakes, maṇḍalins, striped rājilas, and intermediate types.

Word-for-word
चतुर्विधाः पन्नगाः (catur-vidhāḥ pannagāḥ) — four kinds of snakes;
दर्वीकराः (darvīkarāḥ) — hooded snakes;
मण्डलिनः (maṇḍalinaḥ) — maṇḍalins;
राजिलाः (rājilāḥ) — striped rājilas;
व्यन्तराः (vyantarāḥ) — intermediate types
Transliteration
caturvidhā iha proktāḥ pannagāstu mahātmanā ||
darvīkarā maṃḍalino rājilā vyaṃtarāstathā || 9 ||

BP 1.36.10

दर्वीकरा वातविषा मंडला पैत्तिकाः स्मृताः ॥
श्लेष्मला राजिल्रा ज्ञेया व्यंतराः सान्निपातिकाः ॥ ॥

Hooded snakes are associated with wind-poison, maṇḍalins with bile, rājilas with phlegm, and intermediate snakes with a compound disorder of all three.

Word-for-word
दर्वीकराः वातविषाः (darvīkarāḥ vāta-viṣāḥ) — hooded snakes wind-poisoned;
मण्डलाः पैत्तिकाः (maṇḍalāḥ paittikāḥ) — maṇḍalins bilious;
राजिलाः श्लेष्मलाः (rājilāḥ śleṣmalāḥ) — rājilas phlegmatic;
व्यन्तराः सान्निपातिकाः (vyantarāḥ sānnipātikāḥ) — intermediate all-three disorder
Transliteration
darvīkarā vātaviṣā maṃḍalā paittikāḥ smṛtāḥ ||
śleṣmalā rājilrā jñeyā vyaṃtarāḥ sānnipātikāḥ || 1.36.10 ||

BP 1.36.11

रक्तं परीक्षयेदेषां सर्पाणां तु पृथक्पृथक् ॥
कृष्णं दर्वीकराणां तु जायते नाल्पमुल्बणम् ॥ ॥

Their blood should be examined separately. That of hooded snakes is black, not scanty, and intense.

Word-for-word
रक्तम् परीक्षयेत् (raktam parīkṣayet) — should examine blood;
सर्पाणाम् पृथक् पृथक् (sarpāṇām pṛthak pṛthak) — of snakes separately;
कृष्णम् दर्वीकराणाम् (kṛṣṇam darvīkarāṇām) — black for hooded snakes;
न अल्पम् उल्बणम् (na alpam ulbaṇam) — not scanty and intense
Transliteration
raktaṃ parīkṣayedeṣāṃ sarpāṇāṃ tu pṛthakpṛthak ||
kṛṣṇaṃ darvīkarāṇāṃ tu jāyate nālpamulbaṇam || 11 ||

BP 1.36.12

रक्तं घनं च बहुशः शोणितं मंडली कृतम् ॥
पिच्छिलं राजिले स्वल्पं तद्वद्व्यंतरके तथा ॥ ॥

The blood of a maṇḍalin is thick and abundant; that of a rājila is slimy and scanty, and the intermediate type is similar.

Word-for-word
रक्तम् घनम् बहु (raktam ghanam bahu) — blood thick and abundant;
मण्डलीकृतम् (maṇḍalī-kṛtam) — of maṇḍalin;
पिच्छिलम् राजिले स्वल्पम् (picchilam rājile svalpam) — slimy and scanty in rājila;
व्यन्तरके तथा (vyantarake tathā) — likewise intermediate
Transliteration
raktaṃ ghanaṃ ca bahuśaḥ śoṇitaṃ maṃḍalī kṛtam ||
picchilaṃ rājile svalpaṃ tadvadvyaṃtarake tathā || 12 ||

BP 1.36.13

सर्पा ज्ञेयास्तु चत्वारः पंचमो नोपलभ्यते ॥
ब्राह्मणः क्षत्रियो वैश्यः शूद्र श्चैव चतुर्थकः ॥ ॥

Four snake categories are recognized, and no fifth is found: brāhmaṇa, kṣatriya, vaiśya, and śūdra as the fourth.

Word-for-word
सर्पाः चत्वारः (sarpāḥ catvāraḥ) — four snake categories;
पञ्चमः न उपलभ्यते (pañcamaḥ na upalabhyate) — no fifth found;
ब्राह्मणः क्षत्रियः वैश्यः (brāhmaṇaḥ kṣatriyaḥ vaiśyaḥ) — brāhmaṇa kṣatriya vaiśya;
शूद्रः चतुर्थकः (śūdraḥ caturthakaḥ) — śūdra fourth
Transliteration
sarpā jñeyāstu catvāraḥ paṃcamo nopalabhyate ||
brāhmaṇaḥ kṣatriyo vaiśyaḥ śūdra ścaiva caturthakaḥ || 13 ||

BP 1.36.14

ब्राह्मणे मधुरं दद्यात्तिक्तं दद्यात्तथोत्तरे ॥
वैश्ये कर्षफलं दद्याच्छूद्रे त्रिस्थूणमेव च ॥ ॥

To a Brāhmaṇa, give something sweet; next, give something bitter. To a Vaiśya, give fruit weighing a karṣa; to a Śūdra, tristhūṇa.

Word-for-word
ब्राह्मणे मधुरम् दद्यात् (brāhmaṇe madhuram dadyāt) — give sweet for brāhmaṇa type;
तिक्तम् उत्तरे (tiktam uttare) — bitter for next;
वैश्ये कर्षफलम् (vaiśye karṣa-phalam) — karṣa measure of fruit for vaiśya;
शूद्रे त्रिस्थूणम् (śūdre tri-sthūṇam) — tristhūṇa for śūdra
Transliteration
brāhmaṇe madhuraṃ dadyāttiktaṃ dadyāttathottare ||
vaiśye karṣaphalaṃ dadyācchūdre tristhūṇameva ca || 14 ||

BP 1.36.15

ब्राह्मणेन तु दष्टस्य दाहो गात्रेषु जायते ॥
मूर्च्छा च प्रबला स्याद्वै नात्मानमभिजानते ॥ ॥

A person bitten by a brāhmaṇa-type snake experiences burning in the limbs and powerful fainting, and does not recognize the self.

Word-for-word
ब्राह्मणेन दष्टस्य (brāhmaṇena daṣṭasya) — bitten by brāhmaṇa-type;
दाहः गात्रेषु (dāhaḥ gātreṣu) — burning in limbs;
मूर्च्छा प्रबला (mūrcchā prabalā) — powerful faint;
न आत्मानम् अभिजानते (na ātmānam abhijānate) — does not recognize self
Transliteration
brāhmaṇena tu daṣṭasya dāho gātreṣu jāyate ||
mūrcchā ca prabalā syādvai nātmānamabhijānate || 15 ||

BP 1.36.16

श्यामवर्णं मुखं च स्यान्मज्जास्तंभश्च जायते ॥
तस्य कुर्यात्प्रतीकारं येन संपद्यते सुखम् ॥ ॥

The face becomes dark, and stiffness arises in the marrow. One should perform the countermeasure for him by which well-being occurs.

Word-for-word
श्यामवर्णम् मुखम् (śyāma-varṇam mukham) — dark-coloured face;
मज्जास्तम्भः (majjā-stambhaḥ) — rigidity of marrow;
प्रतीकारम् कुर्यात् (pratīkāram kuryāt) — perform countermeasure;
येन सम्पद्यते सुखम् (yena sampadyate sukham) — by which relief arises
Transliteration
śyāmavarṇaṃ mukhaṃ ca syānmajjāstaṃbhaśca jāyate ||
tasya kuryātpratīkāraṃ yena saṃpadyate sukham || 16 ||

BP 1.36.17

अश्वगंधाप्यपामार्गः सिंदुवारं सुरामयम् ॥
एतत्सर्पिः समायुक्तं पाने नस्ये च दापयेत् ॥
एतेनैवोपचारेण सुखी भवति मानवः ॥ ॥

Aśvagandhā, apāmārga, sinduvāra, and surāmaya, mixed with ghee, should be given to drink and through the nose. By this very treatment, the man becomes well.

Word-for-word
अश्वगन्धा अपामार्गः (aśvagandhā apāmārgaḥ) — aśvagandhā and apāmārga;
सिन्दुवारम् सुरामयम् (sinduvāram surāmayam) — sinduvāra and surāmaya;
सर्पिः समायुक्तम् (sarpiḥ samāyuktam) — combined with ghee;
पाने नस्ये दापयेत् (pāne nasye dāpayet) — administer orally and nasally;
उपचारेण सुखी (upacāreṇa sukhī) — relieved by procedure
Transliteration
aśvagaṃdhāpyapāmārgaḥ siṃduvāraṃ surāmayam ||
etatsarpiḥ samāyuktaṃ pāne nasye ca dāpayet ||
etenaivopacāreṇa sukhī bhavati mānavaḥ || 17 ||

BP 1.36.18

क्षत्रियेण तु दष्टस्य कंपो गात्रेषु जायते ॥
मूर्छा मोहस्तथा स्याद्वै नात्मानमभिवेत्ति सः ॥ ॥

A person bitten by a kṣatriya-type snake develops trembling in the limbs, fainting, and confusion, and does not recognize the self.

Word-for-word
क्षत्रियेण दष्टस्य (kṣatriyeṇa daṣṭasya) — bitten by kṣatriya-type;
कम्पः गात्रेषु (kampaḥ gātreṣu) — trembling in limbs;
मूर्च्छा मोहः (mūrcchā mohaḥ) — fainting and confusion;
न आत्मानम् अभिवेत्ति (na ātmānam abhivetti) — does not recognize self
Transliteration
kṣatriyeṇa tu daṣṭasya kaṃpo gātreṣu jāyate ||
mūrchā mohastathā syādvai nātmānamabhivetti saḥ || 18 ||

BP 1.36.19

जायते वेदना तस्य ऊर्ध्वं चैव निरीक्षते ॥
तस्य कुर्यात्प्रतीकारं येन संपद्यते सुखम् ॥ ॥

Pain arises in him and he looks upward. One should perform the countermeasure for him by which well-being occurs.

Word-for-word
वेदना जायते (vedanā jāyate) — pain arises;
ऊर्ध्वम् निरीक्षते (ūrdhvam nirīkṣate) — looks upward;
प्रतीकारम् कुर्यात् (pratīkāram kuryāt) — perform countermeasure;
सम्पद्यते सुखम् (sampadyate sukham) — relief arises
Transliteration
jāyate vedanā tasya ūrdhvaṃ caiva nirīkṣate ||
tasya kuryātpratīkāraṃ yena saṃpadyate sukham || 19 ||

BP 1.36.20

अर्कमूलमपामार्गं प्रियंगुमिन्द्रवारुणीम् ॥
एतत्सर्पिः समायुक्तं पानं नस्यं च दापयेत् ॥
एतेनैवोपचारेण सुखी भवति मानवः ॥ ॥

Arka root, apāmārga, priyaṅgu, and indravāruṇī, mixed with ghee, should be given to drink and through the nose. By this very treatment, the man becomes well.

मधूकसारं सहसिंदुवारं हिंगुं च पिष्ट्वा गुटिका च कार्या ॥ ॥

The historical formula says to grind varāhakarṇī, gajapippalī, gāndhārikā, long pepper, deodar, madhūka essence, sinduvāra, and asafoetida into pills.

Word-for-word
वराहकर्णीम् गजपिप्पलीम् (varāha-karṇīm gaja-pippalīm) — varāhakarṇī and gajapippalī;
गान्धारिकाम् पिप्पलदेवदारु (gāndhārikām pippala-deva-dāru) — gāndhārikā long pepper deodar;
मधूकसारम् सिन्दुवारम् (madhūka-sāram sinduvāram) — madhūka essence and sinduvāra;
हिङ्गुम् पिष्ट्वा (hiṅgum piṣṭvā) — grinding asafoetida;
गुटिका कार्या (guṭikā kāryā) — pills to be made
Transliteration
varāhakarṇīṃ gajapippalīṃ ca gāṃdhārikāṃ pippaladevadāru ||
madhūkasāraṃ sahasiṃduvāraṃ hiṃguṃ ca piṣṭvā guṭikā ca kāryā ||59||

BP 1.36.60

॥ सुमंतुरुवाच ॥ ॥
इत्युक्तवान्पुरा वीर गौतमस्य प्रजापतिः॥
लक्षणं सर्वैनागानां रूपवर्णौ विषं तथा ॥ ॥

Sumantu said: Hero, long ago the Lord of Creatures taught Gautama the marks of all Nāgas—their form, colour, and venom.

Word-for-word
सुमन्तुः उवाच (sumantuḥ uvāca) — Sumantu said;

लक्षणम् सर्वनागानाम् (lakṣaṇam sarva-nāgānām) — marks of all Nāgas;
रूपवर्णौ विषम् (rūpa-varṇau viṣam) — form colour and venom
Transliteration
|| sumaṃturuvāca || ||
ityuktavānpurā vīra gautamasya prajāpatiḥ||
lakṣaṇaṃ sarvaināgānāṃ rūpavarṇau viṣaṃ tathā || 1.36.60 ||

BP 1.36.61

तस्मात्संपूजयेन्नागान्सदा भक्त्या समन्वितः ॥
विशेषतस्तु पंचम्यां पयसा पायसेन च ॥ ॥

Therefore one should always worship the Nāgas with devotion, especially on Pañcamī, using milk and milk-rice.

Word-for-word
तस्मात् सम्पूजयेत् नागान् (tasmāt sampūjayet nāgān) — therefore worship Nāgas;
सदा भक्त्या समन्वितः (sadā bhaktyā samanvitaḥ) — always with devotion;
विशेषतः पञ्चम्याम् (viśeṣataḥ pañcamyām) — especially on Pañcamī;
पयसा पायसेन (payasā pāyasena) — with milk and milk-rice
Transliteration
tasmātsaṃpūjayennāgānsadā bhaktyā samanvitaḥ ||
viśeṣatastu paṃcamyāṃ payasā pāyasena ca ||61||

BP 1.36.62

श्रावणे मासि पंचम्यां शुक्लपक्षे नरा धिप ।
द्वारस्योभयतो लेख्या गोमयेन विषोल्बणाः ॥ ।

O ruler of men, on the bright Pañcamī of Śrāvaṇa, highly venomous Nāgas should be drawn with cow dung on both sides of the doorway.

शुक्लपक्षे (śukla-pakṣe) — in bright fortnight;
द्वारस्य उभयतः लेख्याः (dvārasya ubhayataḥ lekhyāḥ) — drawn on both sides of door;
गोमयेन विषोल्बणाः (go-mayena viṣolbaṇāḥ) — highly venomous snakes with cow dung

śrāvaṇe māsi paṃcamyāṃ śuklapakṣe narā dhipa |

BP 1.36.63

पूजयेद्विधिवद्द्वारं दधिदूर्वाक्षतैः कुशैः ।

BP 1.36.64
