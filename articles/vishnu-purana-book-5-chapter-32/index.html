VP 5.32.1

प्रद्युम्नाद्या हरेः पुत्रा रुक्मिण्यां कथितास्तव ।
भानुभौमेरिकाद्यांश्च सत्यभामा व्यजायत

The sons of Hari, beginning with Pradyumna, born from Rukmiṇī, have been described to you; Satyabhāmā gave birth to Bhānu, Bhaumerika, and the others.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
प्रद्युम्नाद्याः (pradyumnādyāḥ) — beginning with Pradyumna
हरेः पुत्राः (hareḥ putrāḥ) — sons of Hari
रुक्मिण्याम् (rukmiṇyām) — from Rukmiṇī
कथिताः तव (kathitāḥ tava) — have been described to you
भानु-भौमेरिकाद्यांश् च (bhānu-bhaumerikādyāṃś ca) — Bhānu, Bhaumerika, and others
सत्यभामा (satyabhāmā) — Satyabhāmā
व्यजायत (vyajāyata) — gave birth
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

pradyumnādyā hareḥ putrā rukmiṇyāṃ kathitāstava | bhānubhaumerikādyāṃśca satyabhāmā vyajāyata

Śrīdhara

pūrvokta-putrānuvāda-pūrvakaṃ hareḥ sarvān putrān āha—pradyumnādyā ity ādi-pañcabhiḥ || 1 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

पूर्वोक्त-पुत्रानुवाद-पूर्वकं हरेः सर्वान् पुत्रान् आह—प्रद्युम्नाद्या इत्य् आदि-पञ्चभिः ॥१॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

After recounting the previously mentioned sons, he speaks of all the sons of Hari in the first five verses beginning with “Pradyumna and the others.”

VP 5.32.3

तनया भद्रविन्दाद्या नाग्नजित्यां महाबलाः ।

VP 5.32.6

Pradyumna, the son of Rukmiṇī, was the first of all of them; Aniruddha was born from Pradyumna, and Vajra was born from him.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

वज्रः तस्मात् अजायत (vajraḥ tasmāt ajāyata) — Vajra was born from him
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

pradyumnaḥ prathamasteṣāṃ sarveṣāṃ rukmiṇīsutaḥ | pradyumnādaniruddho’bhūdvajrastasmādajāyata

Śrīdhara

aniruddhasya vivāhāntaraṃ tāvad pradyumnādy-anuvādaḥ | pradyumna iti || 6 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अनिरुद्धस्य विवाहान्तरं तावद् प्रद्युम्नाद्य्-अनुवादः । प्रद्युम्न इति ॥६॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Until the account of Aniruddha’s marriage, the account of Pradyumna and the others continues; this is the explanation of “Pradyumna.”

VP 5.32.8

यत्र युद्धमभूद्घोरं हरिशङ्करयोर्महत् ।
छिन्नं सहस्रं बाहूनां यत्र बाणस्य चक्रिणा

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Transliteration (IAST)

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

Śrīdhara commentary.

“In which” means: in that marriage in which the battle took place, and in that battle in which the discus-bearer cut off Bāṇa’s thousand arms.
