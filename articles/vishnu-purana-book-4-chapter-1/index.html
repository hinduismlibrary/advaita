VP 4.1.1

भगवन्यन्नरैः कार्यं साधुकर्मण्यवस्थितैः।
तन्मह्यं गुरुणाख्यातं नित्यनैमित्तिकात्मकम्

Maitreyar – Those who are established in saadhu karma, vaidika karmaas, whatever one has to do, that you told me in great detail. Nitya and Naimittika karmaas which are very much required, by those who are following the vaidika dharmaas, you told me about them.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bhagavanyannaraiḥ kāryaṃ sādhukarmaṇyavasthitaiḥ | tanmahyaṃ guruṇākhyātaṃ nityanaimittikātmakam

Śrīdhara

dharmopadharmayoḥ parvam uktayor anuvarṇyate . manu-vaṃśaś caturthāṃśe pravartaka-nivartakaḥ || nediṣṭha-vaṃśaṃ prathame’kathayad yatra raivataḥ . raivatīṃ haline prādāt sva-sutāṃ druhiṇājñayā || uktānuvāda-pūrvakaṃ manu-vaṃśaṃ pṛcchati—bhagavan iti . guruṇā tvayā ākhyātam ||१|| varṇa-dharmās tathākhyātā dharmā ye cāśrameṣu ca .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

धर्मोपधर्मयोः पर्वम् उक्तयोर् अनुवर्ण्यते ।
मनु-वंशश् चतुर्थांशे प्रवर्तक-निवर्तकः ॥
नेदिष्ठ-वंशं प्रथमेऽकथयद् यत्र रैवतः ।
रैवतीं हलिने प्रादात् स्व-सुतां द्रुहिणाज्ञया ॥
उक्तानुवाद-पूर्वकं मनु-वंशं पृच्छति—भगवन् इति । गुरुणा त्वया आख्यातम् ॥१॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Maitreyar – Those who are established in saadhu karma, vaidika karmaas, whatever one has to do, that you told me in great detail. Nitya and Naimittika karmaas which are very much required, by those who are following the vaidika dharmaas, you told me about them.

VP 4.1.5

तद् यथा सकल-जगताम् आदिर् अनादि-भूतः स ऋग्-यजुः-सामादि-मयो भगवान् विष्णुस् तस्य ब्रह्मणो मूर्तं रूपं हिरण्यगर्भो ब्रह्माण्ड-भूतो ब्रह्मा भगवान् प्राग् बभूव

Bhagavaan Vishnu is the sole creator of this whole world. He is beginningless. He is Ringmaya, Yajurmaya, Saamaveda maya, Bhagavaan Vishnu. He is praised by the Vedaas, He is established in the Vedaas. Every word of the Vedaas is telling about Him only. He is that Brahmaa's moorta svaroopa, that which is manifested. He is called Hiranyagarbha, and is the whole Brahmaanda itself. He is Chaturmukha Brahma who was born first. He is the first chetana in the Brahmaanda, called Hiranyagarbha.

Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

सुद्युम्नस्य प्रथमं कन्यात्वाद् राज्यानर्हत्वं दर्शयन्न् आह तत्र, तस्मिन् कर्मणि मनु-पत्न्या कन्यार्थं प्रार्थितस्य होतुर् अपाचारात् कन्या-सङ्कल्प-रूपाद् अपहते विकल्पे जाते सति कन्याभूत् । “अपकृतेर्” इति पाठे अपकृष्टान् होमाद् धेतोर् इत्य् अर्थः ॥९॥

VP 4.1.11

After having given his daughter in marriage to Balaraama, King Raivata went to Himaalaya for doing tapas. This is the story of Raivata and Revati. Raivata and Revati go to Chaturmukha Brahma, and get mesmerized by listening to the music of Gandharvaas there, spends many chaturyugaas there, and comes back and sees that nobody is there; Chaturmukha Brahma says that Krishna has manifested there, and His brother Balaraama is there, who is also His amsha manifested as a human, that He is the right person for his daughter. This is the story of marriage of Revati and Balaraama. This concludes Chapter 1. || Iti Sri Vishnu Puraane Chaturthe Amshe Prathamo Adhyaayah || || Iti Sri Vishnu Chitteeye Sri Vishnu Puraana Vyaakhyaane Chaturthe Amshe Prathamo Adhyaayah || || Atha Dviteeyo Adhyaayah || Raivata’s Vamsha
