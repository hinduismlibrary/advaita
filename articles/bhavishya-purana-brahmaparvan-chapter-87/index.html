Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 87 — The Victory Observance

BP 1.87.1

ब्रह्मोवाच । जयन्तो ह्युत्तरे ज्ञेयश्चायने गणनायक । वारो देवस्य यः स्याद्वै तत्र पूज्यो दिवाकरः ॥ ॥

Brahmā said. Victory is known during the northern course, O chief of hosts. On whichever day it falls, the Sun should be worshipped.

BP 1.87.2

पूजितस्तत्र देवेशः सहस्रगुणितं फलम् । ददाति देवशार्दूल स्नानदानादिकर्मणाम् ॥ ॥

There, worshipped, the Lord of the gods grants the fruit of bathing, giving, and other acts a thousandfold, O tiger among gods.

BP 1.87.3

घृतेन पयसा यत्र स्नानमिक्षुरसेन तु । विलेपनं कुङ्कुमं तु प्रशस्तं भास्करे प्रियम् ॥ ॥

Bathing with ghee, milk, and sugarcane juice, and anointing with saffron, is praised and dear to the Sun.

BP 1.87.4

धूपक्रिया गुग्गुलेन नैवेद्ये मोदकः स्मृतः । इत्थं सम्पूज्य देवेशं कुर्याद्धोमं ततस्तिलैः । ब्राह्मणान्भोजयेत्पश्चान्मोदकांस्तिलशष्कुलीः ॥ ॥

The incense offering is with fragrant resin, and the food offering is known as a sweet dumpling. Having worshipped the Lord of the gods thus, one should make a fire offering with sesame, then feed the Brahmins sweet dumplings and sesame cakes.

BP 1.87.5

इत्थं यः पूजयेद्भानुं मन्त्रेणैव गणाधिप । सहस्रगुणितं तस्य फलं देवो ददाति वै ॥ ॥

Whoever worships the Sun thus with the mantra, O chief of hosts, receives a thousandfold result.

BP 1.87.6

स्नानदानजपादीनामुपवासस्य वै विभो ॥ ॥

For bathing, giving, recitation, and fasting, O Lord.
