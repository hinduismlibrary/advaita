Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 87 — The Victory Observance

BP 1.87.1

ब्रह्मोवाच । जयन्तो ह्युत्तरे ज्ञेयश्चायने गणनायक । वारो देवस्य यः स्याद्वै तत्र पूज्यो दिवाकरः ॥ ॥

Brahmā said. Victory is known during the northern course, O chief of hosts. On whichever day it falls, the Sun should be worshipped.

brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said;
jayantaḥ — Jayanta;
hi — indeed;
uttare — in the northern [course];
jñeyaḥ — to be known;
ca — and;
ayane — in the course, solstice;
gaṇanāyaka — O lord of hosts;
vāraḥ — day;
devasya — of the deity;
yaḥ — which;
syāt — may be;
vai — indeed;
tatra — there;
pūjyaḥ — to be worshiped;
divākaraḥ — Divākara, the Sun.

BP 1.87.2

पूजितस्तत्र देवेशः सहस्रगुणितं फलम् । ददाति देवशार्दूल स्नानदानादिकर्मणाम् ॥ ॥

There, worshipped, the Lord of the gods grants the fruit of bathing, giving, and other acts a thousandfold, O tiger among gods.

pūjitaḥ — worshiped;
tatra — there;
deveśaḥ — Lord of the gods;
sahasra-guṇitam — thousandfold;
phalam — fruit;
dadāti — gives;
deva-śārdūla — O tiger among the gods;
snāna-dāna-ādi-karmaṇām — of acts such as bathing and giving.

BP 1.87.3

घृतेन पयसा यत्र स्नानमिक्षुरसेन तु । विलेपनं कुङ्कुमं तु प्रशस्तं भास्करे प्रियम् ॥ ॥

Bathing with ghee, milk, and sugarcane juice, and anointing with saffron, is praised and dear to the Sun.

ghṛtena — with ghee;
payasā — with milk;
yatra — where, there;
snānam — bathing;
ikṣu-rasena — with sugarcane juice;
tu — and;
vilepanam — anointing;
kuṅkumam — saffron;
tu — indeed;
praśastam — praised, excellent;
bhāskare — for Bhāskara;
priyam — dear.

BP 1.87.4

धूपक्रिया गुग्गुलेन नैवेद्ये मोदकः स्मृतः । इत्थं सम्पूज्य देवेशं कुर्याद्धोमं ततस्तिलैः । ब्राह्मणान्भोजयेत्पश्चान्मोदकांस्तिलशष्कुलीः ॥ ॥

The incense offering is with fragrant resin, and the food offering is known as a sweet dumpling. Having worshipped the Lord of the gods thus, one should make a fire offering with sesame, then feed the Brahmins sweet dumplings and sesame cakes.

dhūpa-kriyā — incense rite;
guggulena — with guggulu;
naivedye — in the food offering;
modakaḥ — modaka;
smṛtaḥ — prescribed, remembered;
ittham — thus;
sampūjya — having fully worshiped;
deveśam — the Lord of the gods;
kuryāt — should perform;
homam — fire offering;
tataḥ — then;
tilaiḥ — with sesame;
brāhmaṇān — the Brāhmaṇas;
bhojayet — should feed;
paścāt — afterward;
modakān — modakas;
tila-śaṣkulīḥ — sesame cakes.

BP 1.87.5

इत्थं यः पूजयेद्भानुं मन्त्रेणैव गणाधिप । सहस्रगुणितं तस्य फलं देवो ददाति वै ॥ ॥

Whoever worships the Sun thus with the mantra, O chief of hosts, receives a thousandfold result.

ittham — thus;
yaḥ — whoever;
pūjayet — should worship;
bhānum — Bhānu;
mantreṇa eva — with the mantra alone;
gaṇādhipa — O lord of hosts;
sahasra-guṇitam — thousandfold;
tasya — his;
phalam — fruit;
devaḥ — the deity;
dadāti — gives;
vai — indeed.

BP 1.87.6

स्नानदानजपादीनामुपवासस्य वै विभो ॥ ॥

For bathing, giving, recitation, and fasting, O Lord.

snāna-dāna-japa-ādīnām — of bathing, giving, recitation, and other acts;
upavāsasya — of fasting;
vai — indeed;
vibho — O Lord.
