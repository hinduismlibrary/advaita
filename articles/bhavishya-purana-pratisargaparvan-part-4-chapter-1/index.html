BP 3.4.1.1

।। श्रीगणेशाय नमः ।। ।। श्रीराधावल्लभो जयति ।। ।। वेदव्यास उवाच ।।।। एवं द्वापरसंध्याया अन्ते सूतेन वर्णितम् ।। सूर्यचंद्रान्वयाख्यानं तन्मया कथितं तव ।।१।

Salutation to Śrī Gaṇeśa; may Śrī Rādhāvallabha be victorious. Vyāsa said: Thus, at the end of the Dvāpara age’s transition, Sūta narrated the account of the solar and lunar lineages; I have related it to you.

Transliteration (IAST)

|| śrīgaṇeśāya namaḥ || || śrīrādhāvallabho jayati || || vedavyāsa uvāca |||| evaṃ dvāparasaṃdhyāyā ante sūtena varṇitam || sūryacaṃdrānvayākhyānaṃ tanmayā kathitaṃ tava ||1|

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
śrī-gaṇeśāya — to Śrī Gaṇeśa
namaḥ — salutation
śrī-rādhā-vallabhaḥ — the beloved Lord of Rādhā
jayati — is victorious
veda-vyāsaḥ uvāca — Vyāsa said
evam — thus
dvāpara-sandhyāyāḥ ante — at the end of the Dvāpara transition
sūtena varṇitam — narrated by Sūta
sūrya-candra-anvaya-ākhyānam — account of the solar and lunar lineages
tat mayā kathitam tava — that was told by me to you

BP 3.4.1.2

विशालायां पुनर्गत्वा वैतालेन विनिर्मितम् ।। कथयिष्यति सूतस्तमितिहाससमुच्चयम् ।।२।

Returning again to Viśālā, Sūta will narrate that compendium of history composed by the Vetāla.

Transliteration (IAST)

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
viśālāyām — in Viśālā
punaḥ gatvā — having returned again
vetālena vinirmitam — composed by the Vetāla
kathayiṣyati — will narrate
sūtaḥ — Sūta
tam itihāsa-samuccayam — that compendium of history

BP 3.4.1.3

तन्मया कथितं सर्वं हृषीकोत्तमपुण्यदम्।। पुनर्विक्रमभूपेन भविष्यति समाह्वयः ।। ३ ।

All this, which bestows excellent merit, was told by me. Again, King Vikrama will be summoned.

Transliteration (IAST)

Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

bhaviṣyati samāhvayaḥ — there will be a summons

BP 3.4.1.4

नैमिषारण्यमासाद्य श्रावयिष्यति वै कथाम्।। पुनरुक्तानि यान्येव पुराणाष्टादशानि वै ।। ४ ।

On reaching Naimiṣāraṇya, he will again cause the story to be heard—the eighteen Purāṇas that are recited there.

Transliteration (IAST)

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

punar-uktāni — repeated again

BP 3.4.1.5

तानि चोपपुराणानि भविष्यंति कलौ युगे ।। तेषां चोपपुराणानां द्वादशाध्यायमुत्तमम् ।। ५ ।

Those and the subsidiary Purāṇas will be present in the Kali age. Among those subsidiary Purāṇas, the excellent one has twelve chapters.

Transliteration (IAST)

tāni copapurāṇāni bhaviṣyaṃti kalau yuge || teṣāṃ copapurāṇānāṃ dvādaśādhyāyamuttamam || 5 |

BP 3.4.1.6

The compendium of history has also been declared to be the essence. That is what I gladly told you, O best of the Hṛṣīkas.

sārabhūtaśca kathitaṃ itihāsasamuccayaḥ || yaste mayā ca kathito hṛṣīkottama te mudā || 6 |

BP 3.4.1.7

BP 3.4.1.8

तत्कथां भगवान्सूतो नैमिषारण्यमास्थितः ।। अष्टाशीतिसहस्राणि श्रावयिष्यति वै मुनीम् ।। ८ ।

The blessed Sūta, dwelling at Naimiṣāraṇya, will recite that story to eighty-eight thousand sages.

tatkathāṃ bhagavānsūto naimiṣāraṇyamāsthitaḥ || aṣṭāśītisahasrāṇi śrāvayiṣyati vai munīm || 8 |

BP 3.4.1.9

BP 3.4.1.10

सूतपार्श्वं गमिष्यंति नैमिषारण्यवासिनः ।। तत्पृष्टेनैव सूतेन यदुक्तं तच्छृणुष्व भोः ।। 3.4.1.१० ।

BP 3.4.1.11

।। ऋषय ऊचुः ।।।। श्रुतं कृष्णस्य चरितं भगवन्भवतोदितम् ।। इदानीं श्रोतुमिच्छामि राज्ञां तेषां क्रमात्कुलम् ।।।।११ ।

BP 3.4.1.12

चतुर्णां वह्निजातानां परं कौतूहलं हि नः ।। स हरिस्त्रियुगी प्रोक्तः कथं जातः कलौ युगे ।। १२ ।

Our curiosity about the four fire-born ones is great. How was Hari, called the Lord of three ages, born in the Kali age?

Transliteration (IAST)

caturṇāṃ vahnijātānāṃ paraṃ kautūhalaṃ hi naḥ || sa haristriyugī proktaḥ kathaṃ jātaḥ kalau yuge || 12 |

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
caturṇām vahni-jātānām — of the four fire-born ones

tri-yugī proktaḥ — called the Lord of three ages
katham jātaḥ — how was born
kalau yuge — in the Kali age

BP 3.4.1.13

|| sūta uvāca |||| kathayāmi muniśreṣṭhā yuṣmākaṃ praśnamuttamam || agnivaṃśanṛpāṇāṃ ca caritraṃ śṛṇu vistarāt || 13 |

BP 3.4.1.14

प्रमरश्च महीपालो दक्षिणां दिशमास्थितः ।। अम्बया रचितां दिव्यां प्रमराय पुरीं शुभाम् ।। १४ ।

BP 3.4.1.15

BP 3.4.1.21
