Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 122 — A Hymn to the Sun

BP 1.122.1

शतानीक उवाच। तस्मिन्काले समारूढो लिख्यमानो दिवस्पतिः। ब्रह्मादिभिः स्तुतो देवैर्यथा वै तद्वदस्व मे ॥

Śatānīka said. At that time, when the Lord of the Day was mounted and being drawn, he was praised by Brahmā and the other gods. Tell me that account.

BP 1.122.2

सुमन्तुरुवाच। शृणुष्वैकमना राजन्यथा देवो दिवस्पतिः। ब्रह्मादिभिः स्तुतो देवैर्ऋषिभिश्च पुरानघ ॥

Sumantu said. Listen with a single mind, O king, to how the Lord of the Day was praised formerly by Brahmā, the gods, and the sages, O sinless one.

BP 1.122.3

प्रयत्नतः प्रणतहितानुकम्पिने स्वरूपतो लोकविभाविने नमः। दिवस्पते कमलकुलावबोधिने नमस्तमः पटसपटावपायिने ॥

Homage, with effort, to the compassionate one who protects those who bow and illumines the worlds by his own form. Homage to the Lord of the Day, who awakens the lotus cluster and makes the veil of darkness fall away.

पटसपटावपायिने (paṭasapaṭāvapāyine) — not (compound/form)

BP 1.122.4

पावनातिशयपुण्यकर्मणे नैककामविभवप्रदायिने। भासुरामलमयूखमालिने सर्वलोकहितकारिणे नमः ॥

Homage to the supremely pure one of exceedingly holy deeds, the giver of abundant desired wealth, the wearer of a garland of bright, stainless rays, the worker for the welfare of all worlds.

पावनातिशयपुण्यकर्मणे (pāvanātiśayapuṇyakarmaṇe) — merit (compound/form)

BP 1.122.5

अजाय लोकत्रयभावनाय भूतात्मने गोपतये प्रियाय। नमो महाकारुणिकोत्तमाय सूर्याय लोकत्रयभावनाय ॥

Homage to the unborn source of the three worlds, the Self of beings, the lord of cows, the beloved. Homage to the Sun, best of the greatly compassionate, the source of the three worlds.

BP 1.122.6

विवस्वते ज्ञानकृतान्तरात्मने जगत्प्रतिष्ठाय जगद्धितैषिणे। स्वयम्भुवे लोकसमस्तचक्षुषे सुरोत्तमायामिततेजसे नमः ॥

Homage to Vivasvat, the inner Self who creates knowledge, the foundation of the world and seeker of the world's welfare, the one born of himself, the eye of all the worlds, supreme among the gods, of immeasurable radiance.

BP 1.122.7

निजोदयाय सुरगणमौलिमणे जगता त्वं महितस्त्वमुरुमयूखसहस्रतपाः। जगति विभो तमसतुद वनतिमिरासवपावन मदाद्भवति विलोहितविग्रहमुतातिमिरविनाशिनमुग्रं सुतरां त्रिभुवनभाप्रकरैः ॥

You are honored by the world as the jewel upon the crowns of the hosts of gods, blazing with a thousand great rays. O Lord, by the splendor of the three worlds you dispel darkness and purify the world.

BP 1.122.9
