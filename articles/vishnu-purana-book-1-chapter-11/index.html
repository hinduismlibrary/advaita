VP 1.11.1

प्रियव्रतोत्तानपादौ मनोः स्वायम्भुवस्य तु।
द्वौ पुत्रौ तु महावीर्यौ धर्मज्ञौ कथितौ तव॥१॥

Parāśara said: I had told you about two sons, who had great valour, who were always following the path of dharma, as two sons of Svāyambhuva Manu, called Priyavrata and Uttānapāda.

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Manu's daughter's vamsha was told so far. Now Parāśara tells about the vamsha of Manu's sons.

VP 1.11.53

बाह्यार्थात् अखिलात् चित्तं त्याजयेत् प्रथमं नरः।
तस्मिन्न् एव जगद्-धाम्नि ततः कुर्वीत निश्चलम्॥५३॥

First, one has to withdraw the mind from all the external sense objects. Mind should be steady, should not wander around here and there. Śrīdhara Sanskrit

ब्राह्यार्थानित्य्-आदिना अष्टाङ्गयोगसाध्यचित्तसमाधानपूर्वकं मन्त्रजपम् उपदिशन्ति । तत्र प्रथमं ब्राह्यार्थान् चितं त्याजयेत् इति त्यागानुकूलप्राथमिकप्रयत्नविधानेन यथार्हं यमनियमासनप्राणायामपूर्वकः प्रत्याहार उक्तः । तस्मिन्नेव निश्चलं कुर्वीतेति धारणोक्ता ॥५३॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

By these three verses, ṛṣis teach Dhruva the Aṣṭāṅga Yoga, which is an accessory to the worship. The nature of that yoga is that it is required in order to attain concentration of mind. in order to do the japa, the accessory required is that one has to develop concentration of mind, and then do the japa, chanting of the mantra. Along with that Ashtaanga yoga, they teach him the mantra which is to be chanted. Aṣṭāṅga Yoga has yama, niyama, aasana, praanaayaama, pratyaahaara, dhaaranaa, dhyaana, samaadhi. First, one has to give up all the external objects, giving up of telling lies, giving up doing violence, this is called yama; mind has to be controlled, one should desist from telling lies, and doing violence; violence by all the tri karanaas. Pratyaahaara is withdrawing the mind from all external sense objects. Achieving steadiness of the mind is possible through aasana and praanaayaama. Mind has to be steadily focussed on something. Aasana and praanaayaama are extremely essential. Focus the mind on the Supreme Brahman. Focus the mind on the divya mangala vigraha of Bhagavaan, this is the shubha aashraya. First, withdrawing the mind from everything external, giving up telling lies anruta, giving up violence, withdrawing the mind from all external sense objects, achieving steadiness of the mind, thinking of the divya mangala vigraha of Paramaatman dhaaranaa.

VP 1.11.55
