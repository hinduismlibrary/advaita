Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 66 — Sūrya Worship, Sāmba’s Healing and the Greatness of Āditya

BP 1.66.1

सूर्यमाराधयद्दिण्डी सूर्यस्यानुचरोऽभवत् ॥ १ ॥

Diṇḍi worshiped the Sun and became his attendant.

sūryam ārādhayat — worshiped the Sun; diṇḍī — Diṇḍi; sūryasya anucaraḥ abhavat — became the attendant of the Sun

BP 1.66.2

शतानीक उवाच ।
भूयः कथय विप्रेन्द्र माहात्म्यं भास्करस्य मे ।
शृण्वतो नास्ति मे तृप्तिरमृतस्येव सुव्रत ॥ २ ॥

Śatānīka said. Tell me again, O best of Brahmins, the greatness of the Sun. As I listen, I find no satisfaction, as with nectar, O steadfast one.

śatānīkaḥ uvāca — Śatānīka said; bhūyaḥ — again; kathaya — tell; vipra-indra — O best of Brāhmaṇas; māhātmyam — greatness; bhāskarasya — of Bhāskara; me — to me; śṛṇvataḥ — of one listening; na asti — there is not; me tṛptiḥ — satisfaction for me; amṛtasya iva — as with nectar; suvrata — O faithful one

BP 1.66.3

सुमन्तुरुवाच ।
शृणुष्वावहितो राजन्संवादं द्विजशङ्खयोः ।
यं श्रुत्वा सर्वपापेभ्यो मुच्यते मानवो नृप ॥ ३ ॥

Sumantu said. Listen attentively, O king, to the dialogue of the Brahmin and conch. A person who hears it is freed from all sins, O king.

BP 1.66.4

आसीनमाश्रमे शङ्खं द्विजो द्रष्टुं जगाम ह ।
फलभारानतच्छाये वृक्षवृन्दसमाकुले ॥ ४ ॥

The twice born went to see conch seated in the hermitage, amid a cluster of trees whose fruit laden branches bent low and cast shade.

āsīnam — seated; āśrame — in the hermitage; śaṅkham — Śaṅkha; dvijaḥ — the Brāhmaṇa; draṣṭum — to see; jagāma — went; phala-bhāra-anata-chāye — whose shade was bent by the weight of fruit; vṛkṣa-vṛnda-samākule — crowded with groups of trees

BP 1.66.5

परस्परमृगशृङ्गकण्डूयितमृगावृते ।
बर्हिर्वनाम्बरानीततीर्थकन्दोपभोगिनि ॥ ५ ॥

It was filled with deer scratching one another with their horns, and peacocks enjoying roots brought from the forest and the sacred waters.

paraspara — one another; mṛga-śṛṅga — with deer horns; kaṇḍūyita — scratched; mṛga-āvṛte — filled with deer; barhiḥ — peacocks; vana-ambara-ānīta — brought from the forest; tīrtha-kanda-upabhogini — enjoying roots from the sacred waters

BP 1.66.6

प्रभूतकुसुमामोदषट्पदोद्गीतशालिनी ।
सिद्धदेवर्षिगन्धर्वतीर्थसेवितवारिणि ॥ ६ ॥

It was rich with the fragrance of abundant flowers and the song of bees, and its waters were frequented by perfected beings, gods, divine seers, celestial musicians, and pilgrims.

prabhūta-kusuma-āmoda — fragrance of abundant flowers; ṣaṭpada-udgīta — sung by bees; śālinī — rich in; siddha-deva-ṛṣi-gandharva-tīrtha-sevita-vāriṇi — whose waters were frequented by Siddhas, gods, seers, Gandharvas, and pilgrims

BP 1.66.7

मुण्डैश्च जटिलैश्चैव तापसैरुपशोभिते ।
आश्रमेतं मुनिश्रेष्ठं शङ्खाह्वं सुखमास्थितम् ॥ ७ ॥

It was adorned with ascetics, both shaven headed and matted haired. There the foremost sage named conch dwelt happily.

muṇḍaiḥ — with shaven-headed ascetics; jaṭilaiḥ — with matted-haired ascetics; tāpasaiḥ — with ascetics; upaśobhite — adorned; āśrame — in the hermitage; tam — that; muni-śreṣṭham — best of sages; śaṅkha-āhvam — named Śaṅkha; sukham āsthitam — dwelling happily

BP 1.66.8

स्तोत्रैः स्तोतुं सहस्रांशुं तद्भक्तं तत्परायणम् ।
ततः संहत्य सहसा तं भोजककुमारकाः ॥ ८ ॥

The Sun-priest boys then gathered suddenly to praise the thousand rayed Sun with hymns. Conch was devoted to him and wholly intent on him.

stotraiḥ — with hymns; stotum — to praise; sahasrāṃśum — the thousand-rayed one; tat-bhaktam — devoted to him; tat-parāyaṇam — wholly dedicated to him; tataḥ — then; saṃhatya — gathering; sahasā — suddenly; bhojaka-kumārakāḥ — Bhojaka boys

BP 1.66.9

विनीताः उपसङ्गम्य यथावदभिवाद्य च ।
आसनेषूपविष्टास्त उपविष्टमथाब्रुवन् ॥ ९ ॥

Having approached humbly and duly saluted him, they sat on the seats and then spoke to him as he sat.

vinītāḥ — humble; upasaṅgamya — having approached; yathāvat — duly; abhivādya — having saluted; ca — and; āsaneṣu — upon seats; upaviṣṭāḥ — seated; te — they; upaviṣṭam — seated; atha — then; abruvan — spoke

BP 1.66.10

भगवन् सर्ववेदेषु छिन्धि नः संशयो महान् ।
विनयेनोपपन्नानां कुमाराणां ततो मुनिः ॥ १० ॥

The boys, endowed with humility, said, O Blessed One, clear away our great doubt concerning all the Vedas.

bhagavan — revered one; sarva-vedeṣu — concerning all the Vedas; chindhi — cut away; naḥ — our; saṃśayaḥ — doubt; mahān — great; vinayena upapannānām — endowed with humility; kumārāṇām — of the boys; tataḥ — then; muniḥ — the sage

BP 1.66.11

अनादींश्चतुरो वेदानुवाच प्रीतमानसः ।
तेषां तु पठतामेव आश्रमं तु यदृच्छया ॥ ११ ॥

Then the sage, with a pleased mind, recited the four beginningless Vedas. While they were reciting, a twice born happened by chance to come to the hermitage.

anādīn — beginningless; caturaḥ — four; vedān — Vedas; uvāca — recited; prīta-mānasaḥ — with delighted mind; teṣām — of them; paṭhatām — reciting; eva — while; āśramam — to the hermitage; yadṛcchayā — by chance

BP 1.66.12

मुनिश्रेष्ठोऽथ तं देशमाजगाम द्विजो नृप ।
यथावदर्चितस्तेन शङ्खेनामिततेजसा ॥ १२ ॥

O king, the foremost sage, the twice born, then came to that place. The immeasurably radiant conch duly honored him.

muni-śreṣṭhaḥ — best of sages; atha — then; tam deśam — that place; ājagāma — came; dvijaḥ — the Brāhmaṇa; nṛpa — O king; yathāvat — duly; arcitaḥ — honored; tena — by him; śaṅkhena — by Śaṅkha; amita-tejasā — immeasurably radiant

BP 1.66.13

वन्दितश्च कुमारैस्तैर्भवतीत्प्रीतमानसः ।
अथैतानब्रवीच्छङ्खस्तान्भोजककुमारकान् ॥ १३ ॥

He was saluted by those boys and became pleased in mind. Then conch addressed those Sun-priest boys.

vanditaḥ — saluted; ca — and; kumāraiḥ — by the boys; taiḥ — by them; abhavat — became; prīta-mānasaḥ — pleased in mind; atha — then; etān — these; abravīt — spoke; śaṅkhaḥ — Śaṅkha; tān — those; bhojaka-kumārakān — Bhojaka boys

BP 1.66.14

शिष्टागमादनध्यायः स च जातो विरम्यताम् ।
यथाज्ञापयसीत्याहुः कुमारास्ते ऋषिं ततः ॥ १४ ॥

A day of rest from study has come according to the rule of the learned, so let us stop. The boys then said to the sage, We shall do as you command.

BP 1.66.15

प्रपच्छ सिद्धिदश्चैतान्के ह्येते किं पठन्ति च ।
शङ्खोवाच महाराज कुमाराः भोजकात्मजाः ॥ १५ ॥

Then the giver of success asked them who these boys were and what they studied. Conch said, O great king, they are sons of the Sun-priests.

prapaccha — he asked; siddhi-daḥ — giver of accomplishment; ca — and; etān — these; ke — who; hi — indeed; ete — these; kim — what; paṭhanti — study; ca — and; śaṅkhaḥ uvāca — Śaṅkha said; mahārāja — O great king; kumārāḥ — the boys; bhojaka-ātmajāḥ — sons of Bhojakas

BP 1.66.16

ससूत्रकल्पांश्चतुरो विप्र वेदानधीयते ।
तथैव सप्तमीकल्पे परिचर्यां च भास्वतः ॥ १६ ॥

O Brahmin, they study the four Vedas together with their aphorisms and ritual manuals, and likewise the service of the radiant Sun according to the seventh day rite.

sa-sūtra-kalpān — together with Sūtras and Kalpas; caturaḥ — four; vipra — O Brāhmaṇa; vedān — Vedas; adhīyate — they study; tathā eva — likewise; saptamī-kalpe — in the Saptamī Kalpa; paricaryām — service; bhāsvataḥ — of the radiant one

BP 1.66.17

अग्निकार्यविधानं च प्रतिष्ठाकल्पमादितः ।
अध्यङ्गलक्षणं ब्रह्मन् रथयात्राविधिं तथा ॥ १७ ॥

They also study the rules for fire rites, the ritual of consecration from the beginning, the characteristics of the subsidiary limbs, O Brahman, and likewise the procedure for the chariot procession.

agni-kārya-vidhānam — rule for the fire rite; pratiṣṭhā-kalpam āditaḥ — beginning with the Pratiṣṭhā Kalpa; adhyaṅga-lakṣaṇam — marks of the body; brahman — O Brahman; ratha-yātrā-vidhim — procedure of the chariot festival; tathā — likewise

BP 1.66.18

द्विज उवाच ।
कथं क्रियेत सप्तम्यां भास्करार्चनविधिक्रमः ।
गन्धपुष्पप्रदीपानां किं फलं रविमन्दिरे ॥ १८ ॥

The Brahmin asked how the rite of worshiping the Sun should be performed on the seventh lunar day, and what fruit perfumes, flowers, and lamps bear in the temple of the Sun.

dvijaḥ uvāca — the Brāhmaṇa said; katham — how; kriyeta — should be performed; saptamyām — on Saptamī; bhāskara-arcana-vidhi-kramaḥ — the procedure of Bhāskara worship; gandha-puṣpa-pradīpānām — of perfumes, flowers, and lamps; kim phalam — what is the fruit; ravi-mandire — in the Sun’s temple

BP 1.66.19

केन तुष्यति दानेन व्रतेन नियमेन च ।
धूपपुष्पोपहारादि किं च देयं विवस्वते ॥ १९ ॥

He asked what gift, vow, and observance pleased the Sun, and what incense, flowers, offerings, and the rest should be given to him.

BP 1.66.20

एतदिच्छाम्यहं श्रोतुं तन्मे ब्रूहि तपोधन ।
विशेषतस्तु माहात्म्यं ब्रूहि मां भास्करस्य हि ॥ २० ॥

BP 1.66.21

शङ्ख उवाच ।
इममर्थं वसिष्ठेन पृष्टः साम्बो यथा पुरा ।

BP 1.66.22

अत्राश्रमे पुण्यतमे तीर्थानामुत्तमे प्रभुः ।
ववन्दे नियतात्मानं वसिष्ठं मुनिसत्तमम् ॥ २२ ॥

In this most sacred hermitage, foremost among holy places, the lord bowed to Vasiṣṭha, the excellent sage who had mastered himself.

atra āśrame — in this hermitage; puṇyatame — most holy; tīrthānām uttame — foremost among sacred places; prabhuḥ — the Lord; vavande — saluted; niyata-ātmānam — self-controlled; vasiṣṭham — Vasiṣṭha; muni-sattamam — excellent sage

BP 1.66.23

विनयेनोपसङ्गम्य ववन्दे चरणौ मुनेः ।
कृतप्रणामं साम्बं तु भक्तिप्रह्वीकृताननम् ॥ २३ ॥

BP 1.66.24

विलोक्य परमप्रीतो मुनिः पप्रच्छ तं तदा ।
सर्वतः स्फुटितं गात्रं कुष्ठेन महता तव ॥ २४ ॥

BP 1.66.25

घोररूपेण तीव्रेण कथं तद्विगतं तव ।

BP 1.66.26

तेजस्विता अतिमहती तथैव सुकुमारता ॥ २६ ॥

How did you gain very great radiance and likewise softness.

BP 1.66.27

साम्ब उवाच ।

BP 1.66.28

वसिष्ठ उवाच ।
कथमारााधितः सूर्यस्त्वया यादवनन्दन ।

BP 1.66.29

साम्ब उवाच ।
शृणुष्वावहितो ब्रह्मन् सर्वमेव मया यथा ।

BP 1.66.30

मोहान्मयोपहसितो दुर्वासाः कोपनो मुनिः ।
ततोऽहं तस्य शापेन महाकुष्ठमवाप्तवान् ॥ ३० ॥

BP 1.66.31

ततोऽहं पितरं गत्वा कुष्ठयोगाभिपीडितः ।

BP 1.66.32

तात सीदति मे गात्रं स्वरश्च परिहीयते ।

BP 1.66.33

अशेषव्याधिराज्ञाहं पीडितः क्रूरकर्मणा ।

BP 1.66.67

ललाटाद्यस्य रुद्रस्तु का तुल्या तेन देवता ।

BP 1.66.68

सोऽहमिच्छामि देवस्य तस्य सर्वात्मनः प्रभो ।

BP 1.66.69

केनोपायेन मन्त्रैर्वा रहस्यैः परिचर्यया ।

BP 1.66.70

आराधितः समस्तानां क्लेशानां हानिदो रविः ।
शक्यः समाराधयितुं कथं शंस प्रजापते ॥ ७० ॥

BP 1.66.71

धर्मार्थकामसम्प्राप्तौ पुरुषाणां विचेष्टताम् ।
जन्मन्यवितथा सैका क्रिया यार्कं समाश्रिता ॥ ७१ ॥

For people striving to gain virtue, wealth, and pleasure, one act in life is unfailing, the act that takes refuge in the Sun.

BP 1.66.72

दुर्गसंसारकान्तारमपारमभिधावताम् ।
एकः सूर्यनमस्कारो मुक्तिमार्गस्य देशकः ॥ ७२ ॥

BP 1.66.73

सोऽहमिच्छामि तं देवं सप्तलोकपरायणम् ।
कालायनमशेषस्य जगतो हृद्यवस्थितम् ॥ ७३ ॥

BP 1.66.74

आराधयितुं गोपालं ग्रहेषममितौजसम् ।
शङ्करं जगतो दीपं स्मृतमात्राघनाशनम् ॥ ७४ ॥

I wish to worship the protector of cows, the lord of the planets, of immeasurable splendor, the beneficent one, the lamp of the universe, the destroyer of sin by mere remembrance.

BP 1.66.75

तमनाद्यं सुरश्रेष्ठं प्रसादयितुमिच्छतः ।

BP 1.66.77

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
यत्पृच्छसि द्विजश्रेष्ठ सूर्यस्याराधनं प्रति ।

BP 1.66.78

अनादि यत्परं ब्रह्म सर्वहेयविवर्जितम् ।

BP 1.66.79

प्रधानपुंसोरनयोर्यतः क्षोभः प्रवर्तते ।

BP 1.66.80

तत्क्षोभकत्वाद्ब्रह्माण्डं सृष्टेर्हेतुरनिरञ्जनः ।

BP 1.66.82

ततः स्थितौ पालयिता विष्णुत्वं जगतः क्षये ।

BP 1.66.83
