VP 6.5.1

आध्यात्मिकादि मैत्रेय ज्ञात्वा तापत्रयं बुधः।
उत्पन्नज्ञानवैराग्यः प्राप्नोत्यात्यन्तिकं लयम्

Sri Paraasharar - Aadhyaatmika, Aadhibhoutika, Aadhidaivika, are the three kinds of taapas told. These are said to be taapa traya, the three sufferings. Having known all of these, one for whom the real knowledge of jnyaana and vairaagya, and who has developed asanga with all these, vairaagya, who has given up all these things. He can only attain aatyantika laya, moksha.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ādhyātmikādi maitreya jñātvā tāpatrayaṃ budhaḥ . utpannajñānavairāgyaḥ prāpnotyātyantikaṃ layam

Śrīdhara

ātyantika-layo nāma sarvātmanaḥ ajñāna-tat-kārya-nāśātmako mokṣaḥ ātmānaṃ deha-sandhānaṃ ca nimittīkṛtya bhavatīty-ādhyātmikaḥ ||१|| ādhyātmiko’pi dvividhaḥ śārīro mānasas tathā .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

आत्यन्तिक-लयो नाम सर्वात्मनः अज्ञान-तत्-कार्य-नाशात्मको मोक्षः आत्मानं देह-सन्धानं च निमित्तीकृत्य भवतीत्य्-आध्यात्मिकः ॥१॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Sri Paraasharar - Aadhyaatmika, Aadhibhoutika, Aadhidaivika, are the three kinds of taapas told. These are said to be taapa traya, the three sufferings. Having known all of these, one for whom the real knowledge of jnyaana and vairaagya, and who has developed asanga with all these, vairaagya, who has given up all these things. He can only attain aatyantika laya, moksha.

VP 6.5.2

आध्यात्मिकोऽपि द्विविधश्शारीरो मानसस्तथा।
शारीरो बहुभिर्भेदैर्भिद्यते श्रुयतां च सः

The aadhyaatmika taapa is explained first. Here, aatma means shareera. In this shareera, there are two parts - shaareera and maanasa - related to the body, and related to the mind. Both are said to be aadhyaatmika taapa. All the various sufferings arising out of the body, are of so many varieties, and I am going to tell you that, listen to me.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ādhyātmiko’pi dvividhaśśārīro mānasastathā . śārīro bahubhirbhedairbhidyate śruyatāṃ ca saḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

Anything other than Bhagavat vishaya, that which is opposed to Bhagavaan, is all bandhaka, avidyaa roopa. That which gets svaroopa vikaara itself, the achit, He is different and distinct from that. He is different and distinct from prakruti samsrushta chetana, baddha chetana. He is always shuddha, not like muktaas, who were at one time associated with prakruti, and were ashuddha. Bhagavaan is different and distinct from muktaas, this is shown by nirmala. Ekaroopam shows that He is different and distinct from nitya sooris also; based on Bhagavaan's sankalpa, they can take many forms. Where is this told, means that this is told in the Shrutis. It is told in the Shrutis that there is no other way. We have to do upaasanaa on Him, it has to be darshana samaanaakaara roopaapanna. Whatever Keshidhvaja taught to Khaandikya Janaka, that Sri Paraasharar is telling here, the Bhagavat svaroopa. This completes Chapter Five.
