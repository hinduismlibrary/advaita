Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 56 — The Solar Chariot Procession and Planetary Pacification

BP 1.56.1

श्रीरुद्र उवाच ।
कथं प्रचालयेद्ब्रह्मन् रथस्थं तमनाशनम् ।
अनुगाश्च कथं चास्य के च ते अनुगा क्रमात् ॥

Śrī Rudra said: O Brahman, how should one set in motion the imperishable one seated on the chariot? And how should his attendants be arranged, and who are those attendants in their order?

Word-for-word
श्रीरुद्रः उवाच (śrīrudraḥ uvāca) — Śrī Rudra said
कथम् (katham) — how; प्रचालयेत् (pracālayet) — should set in motion; ब्रह्मन् (brahman) — O Brahman; रथस्थम् (rathastham) — seated on the chariot; तम् (tam) — him; अनाशनम् (anāśanam) — imperishable
अनुगाः (anugāḥ) — attendants; च (ca) — and; कथम् (katham) — how; च अस्य (ca asya) — and of him; के (ke) — who; च ते (ca te) — and those; अनुगाḥ (anugāḥ) — attendants; क्रमात् (kramāt) — in order
Transliteration
śrīrudra uvāca |
kathaṃ pracālayed brahman rathasthaṃ tam anāśanam |
anugāś ca kathaṃ cāsya ke ca te anugā kramāt || 1 ||

BP 1.56.2

BP 1.56.3

यथा पर्यटनं तु स्याद्विषमे पथि गच्छतः ॥

BP 1.56.4

BP 1.56.5

रक्षको द्वारको यस्माद्रथारूढौ तु पृष्ठतः ॥

BP 1.56.6

BP 1.56.7

शनैःशनैर्नयेद्रुद्र रथं देवस्य यत्नतः ।
युगाक्षचक्रभङ्गो वा यथा न स्यात्त्रिलोचन ॥

Slowly, slowly should one lead the gods chariot with care, O Rudra, so that there may be no breaking of the yoke, axle, or wheel, O three-eyed one.

Word-for-word
शनैः शनैः (śanaiḥ śanaiḥ) — slowly, slowly; नयेत् (nayet) — one should lead; रथम् (ratham) — the chariot; देवस्य (devasya) — of the god; यत्नतः (yatnataḥ) — carefully
युग-अक्ष-चक्र-भङ्गः (yuga-akṣa-cakra-bhaṅgaḥ) — breaking of the yoke, axle, or wheel; वा (vā) — or; यथा न स्यात् (yathā na syāt) — so that it may not be; त्रिलोचन (trilocana) — O three-eyed one
Transliteration

yugākṣacakrabhaṅgo vā yathā na syāt trilocana || 7 ||

BP 1.56.8

ईषाभङ्गे द्विजभयं भग्नेऽक्षे क्षत्रियक्षयः ।
तुलाभङ्गे तु वैश्यानां शय्याशूद्रक्षयो भवेत् ॥

When the īṣā breaks, there is danger for Brāhmaṇas; when the axle breaks, destruction of Kṣatriyas; when the balance beam breaks, the decline of Vaiśyas and of the Śūdra/bed order is said to follow.

Word-for-word
ईषा-भङ्गे (īṣā-bhaṅge) — when the pole/shaft breaks; द्विज-भयम् (dvija-bhayam) — danger for the twice-born
भग्ने अक्षे (bhagne akṣe) — when the axle is broken; क्षत्रिय-क्षयः (kṣatriya-kṣayaḥ) — destruction/decline of Kṣatriyas
तुला-भङ्गे (tulā-bhaṅge) — when the balance beam breaks; तु (tu) — indeed; वैश्यानाम् (vaiśyānām) — of Vaiśyas; शय्या-शूद्र-क्षयः (śayyā-śūdra-kṣayaḥ) — a textually difficult compound, concerning bed/Śūdra decline; भवेत् (bhavet) — may be
Transliteration
īṣābhaṅge dvijabhayaṃ bhagne 'kṣe kṣatriyakṣayaḥ |
tulābhaṅge tu vaiśyānāṃ śayyāśūdrakṣayo bhavet || 8 ||

BP 1.56.9

युगभङ्गे त्वनावृष्टिः पीठभङ्गे प्रजाभयम् ।
परचक्रागमं विद्याच्चक्रभङ्गे रथस्य तु ॥

Word-for-word
युग-भङ्गे (yuga-bhaṅge) — when the yoke breaks; तु (tu) — indeed; अनावृष्टिः (anāvṛṣṭiḥ) — lack of rain/drought
पीठ-भङ्गे (pīṭha-bhaṅge) — when the platform breaks; प्रजा-भयम् (prajā-bhayam) — fear among the people
पर-चक्र-आगमम् (para-cakra-āgamam) — arrival of a hostile army; विद्यात् (vidyāt) — one should know; चक्र-भङ्गे (cakra-bhaṅge) — when the wheel breaks; रथस्य (rathasya) — of the chariot; तु (tu) — but
Transliteration
yugabhaṅge tv anāvṛṣṭiḥ pīṭhabhaṅge prajābhayam |
paracakrāgamaṃ vidyāc cakrabhaṅge rathasya tu || 9 ||

BP 1.56.10

ध्वजस्य पतने चापि नृपभङ्गं विनिर्दिशेत् ।

BP 1.56.11

छत्रभङ्गाद्भयं रुद्र युवराज्ञो विनिर्दिशेत् ।

BP 1.56.18
