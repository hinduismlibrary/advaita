Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 56 — The Solar Chariot Procession and Planetary Pacification

BP 1.56.1

श्रीरुद्र उवाच ।
कथं प्रचालयेद्ब्रह्मन् रथस्थं तमनाशनम् ।
अनुगाश्च कथं चास्य के च ते अनुगा क्रमात् ॥

Rudra spoke, asking how one should set the imperishable one seated on the chariot in motion, O Brahman, how his attendants should follow, and who those attendants are in order.

BP 1.56.2

भूयोभूयः सुरश्रेष्ठ विस्तरान्मम श्रेयसे ।
वद सर्वं जगन्नाथ परं कौतूहलं हि मे ॥

Again and again, O best of gods, tell me fully for my welfare. Tell everything, O lord of the world, for my curiosity is indeed great.

BP 1.56.3

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
शनैर्नयेद्रथं रुद्र वर्त्मना सु समेन तु ।
यथा पर्यटनं तु स्याद्विषमे पथि गच्छतः ॥

Brahmā spoke. One should lead the chariot slowly, O Rudra, along a very level road, so that it may travel on an uneven path.

BP 1.56.4

प्रतीहाररथं पूर्वं नयेन्मार्गविशुद्धये ।
तस्मादनन्तरं रुद्र दण्डनायकमादरात् ॥

First one should lead the chariot of the gatekeeper to clear the road. After that, one should lead the commander with respect, O Rudra.

BP 1.56.5

पिङ्गलं च ततस्तस्य पृष्ठगं चादरान्नयेत् ।
रक्षको द्वारको यस्माद्रथारूढौ तु पृष्ठतः ॥

Then one should respectfully lead Piṅgala, who follows behind it. For the protector and the doorkeeper are mounted on the chariot at the rear.

BP 1.56.6

रथारूढस्तथा दिण्डी देवस्य पुरतः स्थितः ।
तस्मादपि तथा रुद्र लेखको भास्करप्रियः ॥

Likewise, Diṇḍī, mounted on the chariot, stands before the god. Likewise, O Rudra, the writer dear to Bhāskara should be led.

BP 1.56.7

शनैःशनैर्नयेद्रुद्र रथं देवस्य यत्नतः ।
युगाक्षचक्रभङ्गो वा यथा न स्यात्त्रिलोचन ॥

One should lead the chariot of the god slowly and carefully, O Rudra, so that the yoke, axle, or wheel does not break, O three eyed one.

BP 1.56.8

ईषाभङ्गे द्विजभयं भग्नेऽक्षे क्षत्रियक्षयः ।
तुलाभङ्गे तु वैश्यानां शय्याशूद्रक्षयो भवेत् ॥

When the pole breaks, fear arises for the Brahmins. When the axle breaks, the Kṣatriyas are destroyed. When the crossbar breaks, the Vaiśyas are destroyed, and when the bed of the chariot breaks, the Śūdras are destroyed.

BP 1.56.9

युगभङ्गे त्वनावृष्टिः पीठभङ्गे प्रजाभयम् ।
परचक्रागमं विद्याच्चक्रभङ्गे रथस्य तु ॥

When the yoke breaks, drought follows, and when the platform breaks, fear for the people arises. When the chariot wheel breaks, one should know that an enemy army is coming.

BP 1.56.10

ध्वजस्य पतने चापि नृपभङ्गं विनिर्दिशेत् ।
व्यङ्गितप्रतिमायां तु राज्ञो मरणमादिशेत् ॥

When the banner falls, one should declare the downfall of the king. When the image is damaged, one should predict the death of the king.

BP 1.56.11

छत्रभङ्गाद्भयं रुद्र युवराज्ञो विनिर्दिशेत् ।
उत्पन्नेष्वेवमाद्येषु उत्पातेष्वशुभेषु च ॥

When these and other inauspicious omens arise, one should declare danger for the crown prince from the breaking of the umbrella, O Rudra.

BP 1.56.12

बलिकर्म पुनः कुर्याच्छांतिहोमं तथैव च ।
ब्राह्मणान्वाचयेद्भूयो दद्याद्दानानि चैव हि ॥

One should perform the offering rite again, and likewise the peace fire offering. One should again have the Brahmins recite and give gifts as well.

BP 1.56.13

पूर्वोत्तरे च दिग्भागे रथस्याग्निं प्रकल्पयेत् ।
समिद्भिस्तु घृताक्ताभिर्होमयेज्जातवेदसम् ॥

One should set the fire in the northeast part of the chariot. With fuel sticks smeared with clarified butter, one should offer to Fire.

BP 1.56.14

स्वाहाकारान्वदन्सम्यग्दैवतेभ्यस्त्वनुक्रमात् ।
ग्रहेभ्यश्च प्रजाभ्यश्च नामान्युद्दिश्य होमयेत् ॥

One should utter the offering formulas properly to the deities in order. One should make offerings for the planets and the people, naming them.

BP 1.56.15

प्रथमं चाग्नये स्वाहा स्वाहा सोमाय चैव हि ।
स्वाहा प्रजापतये च देया आहुतयः क्रमात् ॥

First, hail to Fire, and likewise hail to Soma. Hail to Prajāpati, and the oblations should be given in order.

BP 1.56.16

स्वस्त्यस्त्विह च विप्रेभ्यः स्वस्ति राज्ञे तथैव च ।
गोभ्यः स्वस्ति प्रजाभ्यश्च जगतः शान्तिरस्तु वै ॥

May there be well being here for the Brahmins, and well being for the king likewise. May there be well being for cows and people, and may there indeed be peace for the world.

BP 1.56.17

शं नोऽस्तु द्विपदे नित्यं शान्तिरस्तु चतुष्पदे ।
शं प्रजाभ्यस्तथैवास्तु शं सदात्मनि चास्तु वै ॥

May there always be well being for the two footed, and peace for the four footed. May there likewise be well being for the people, and may there always be well being in the self.

BP 1.56.18

भूः शान्तिरस्तु देवेश भुवः शान्तिस्तथैव च ।
स्वश्चैवास्तु तथा शान्तिः सर्वत्रास्तु तथा रवेः ॥

May there be peace on earth, O lord of gods, and likewise peace in the atmosphere. May there also be peace in heaven, and may there be peace everywhere for the Sun.

BP 1.56.19

त्वं देव जगतः स्रष्टा पोष्टा चैव त्वमेव हि ।
प्रजापाल ग्रहेषान शान्तिं कुरु दिवस्पते ॥

You alone are the creator and nourisher of the world, O god. O protector of people, lord of planets, make peace, O lord of day.

BP 1.56.20

इदमَन्यच्च वक्ष्यामि शान्त्या परमकारणम् ।
यात्राकारणभूतस्य पुरुषस्य स्वजन्मनः ॥

I shall also tell the highest means of peace for a person undertaking a journey, according to his own birth.

BP 1.56.21

दुःस्थान्ग्रहांश्च विज्ञाय ग्रहशान्तिं समाचरेत् ।
प्रादेशमात्राः कर्त्तव्याः समिधोऽथ प्रमाणतः ॥

Having discerned the planets that are ill placed, one should perform the pacification of the planets. The kindling sticks should be made one handspan in length according to measure.

BP 1.56.22

अर्कमय्यो रवेः कार्या पालाश्यः शशिनः स्मृताः ।
खादिर्यश्चैव भौमाय आपामार्ग्योऽब्जसूनवे ॥

Fuel sticks from the giant milkweed are prescribed for the Sun, and those from the flame of the forest for the Moon. Those from the catechu tree are prescribed for Mars, and those from the prickly chaff flower for the son of the Moon.

BP 1.56.23

आश्वत्थ्यश्चाथ जीवाय औदुम्बर्यः सिताय च ।
असिताय शमीमय्यो दूर्वा कार्यस्तु राहवे ॥

Those from the sacred fig are prescribed for Jupiter, and those from the cluster fig for Venus. Those from the Indian mesquite are prescribed for Saturn, and Bermuda grass should be used for Rāhu.

BP 1.56.24

केतवे तु कुशाः कार्याः दक्षिणाश्चाप्यतः शृणु ।
सूर्याय शोभनां धेनुं शङ्खं दद्यादथेन्दवे ॥

Sacrificial grass should be prepared for Ketu. Now hear the gifts too. One should give a fine cow to the Sun and a conch to the Moon.

BP 1.56.25

रक्तमनड्वाहं भौमाय काञ्चनं सोमसूनवे ।
जीवाय वाससी देये शुक्रायाश्वं सितं हर ॥

One should give a red bull to Mars and gold to the son of the Moon. Two garments should be given to Jupiter, and a white horse to Venus, O Hara.

BP 1.56.26

शनैश्चराय गां नीलां राहवे भाण्डपायसम् ।
छागं तु केतवे दद्याच्छृण्वेषां भोजनान्यपि ॥

One should give a black cow to Saturn and a vessel of rice pudding to Rāhu. One should give a goat to Ketu. Hear their foods also.

BP 1.56.27

गुडोदनं तु सूर्याय सोमाय घृतपायसम् ।
हविष्यमन्नं भौमाय क्षीरान्नं सोमसूनवे ॥

Jaggery rice should be given to the Sun, rice pudding with clarified butter to the Moon, sacrificial rice to Mars, and milk rice to the son of the Moon.

BP 1.56.28

दध्योदनं तु जीवाय शुक्रायाथ घृताशनम् ।
तिलपिष्टांश्च माषांश्च सूर्यपुत्राय दापयेत् ॥

Curd rice should be given to Jupiter and food prepared with clarified butter to Venus. Sesame paste and black gram should be given to the son of the Sun.

BP 1.56.29

राहवे दापयेन्मांसं केतवे चित्रमोदनम् ।
सौवीरमारनालं च स्विन्नबीजं च काञ्जिकम् ॥

One should give meat to Rāhu and varied rice to Ketu, and also sour gruel, fermented rice gruel, boiled seeds, and fermented gruel.

BP 1.56.30

यथा बाणप्रहाराणां वारणं कवचं स्मृतम् ।
तथा दैवोपघातानां शान्तिर्भवति वारणम् ॥

Just as armor is said to ward off arrow strikes, so pacification is a ward against divine afflictions.

BP 1.56.31

अहिंसकस्य दान्तस्य धर्मार्जितधनस्य च ।
नित्यं च नियमस्थस्य सदा सानुग्रहा ग्रहाः ॥

The planets are always gracious to the nonviolent, the self controlled, the person whose wealth is earned through right conduct, and the one always abiding by observance.

BP 1.56.32

ग्रहाः पूज्याः सदा रुद्र इच्छता विपुलं यशः ।
श्रीकामः शान्तिकामो वा ग्रहयज्ञं समाचरेत् ॥

The planets should always be worshipped, O Rudra, by one desiring abundant fame. One desiring prosperity or peace should perform a sacrifice to the planets.

BP 1.56.33

वृष्ट्यायुःपुष्टिकामो वा तथैवाभिचरन्पुनः ।
यानपत्या भवेन्नारी दुष्प्रजाश्चापि या भवेत् ॥

A person desiring rain, long life, or nourishment, a person practicing sorcery, a childless woman, and a woman with poor offspring.

BP 1.56.34

बाला यस्याः प्रम्रियन्ते या च कन्याप्रजा भवेत् ।
राज्यभ्रष्टो नृपो यस्तु दीर्घरोगी च यो भवेत् ॥

A woman whose young children die, one whose children are daughters, a king fallen from his kingdom, and one who is long diseased.

BP 1.56.35

ग्रहयज्ञः स्मृतस्तेषां मानवानां मनीषिभिः ।
तस्मादसौ सदा कार्यः श्रेयोऽर्थं जानता हर ॥

The wise have prescribed the planetary sacrifice for those people. Therefore, one who knows what is beneficial should always perform it for welfare, O Hara.

BP 1.56.36

दत्तपुष्पः क्रूरदृक्च पुष्पजो धिषणस्तथा ।
सितासितौ तथा रुद्र उपरागः शिखी तथा ॥

The one given flowers, the fierce eyed one, the flower born one, the wise one, the white and the dark ones, O Rudra, the eclipsing one, and likewise the crested one.

BP 1.56.37

एते ग्रहा महाबाहो विद्वद्भिः पूजिताः सदा ।
ताम्रकात्स्फाटिकाद्रक्तचन्दनात्स्वर्णकादपि ॥

The one given flowers, the fierce eyed one, the flower born one, the wise one, the white and the dark ones, O Rudra, the eclipsing one, and likewise the crested one.

BP 1.56.38

राजतादायसात्सीसाद्ग्रहाः कार्याः प्रयत्नतः ।
स्वर्णे वाथ पटे लेख्या यथाशास्त्रं महेश्वर ॥

The learned always worship these planets, O mighty armed one, in copper, crystal, red sandalwood, and gold.

BP 1.56.39

यथावर्णं प्रदेयानि वासांसि कुसुमानि च ।
गन्धाश्च बलयश्चैव धूपो देयश्च गुग्गुलः ॥

The planets should be carefully made from silver, iron, and lead, or drawn on gold or cloth according to the shastra, O Maheshvara.

BP 1.56.40

कर्त्तव्या मन्त्रवंतश्च चरवः प्रतिदैवतम् ।
आकृष्णेन इमं देवा अग्निर्मूर्धा दिवः ककुत् ॥

Garments and flowers should be given according to color, and fragrances and bracelets. Gum resin should be given as incense.

BP 1.56.41

उद्बुध्यस्व यथासंख्यमृच एताः प्रकीर्तिताः ।
बृहस्पते अतियदर्यस्तथैवाऽन्नात्परिस्रुतः ॥

One should prepare the cooked offerings accompanied by formulas for each deity, beginning with By the black one, This, O gods, and Fire is the head, the summit of heaven.

BP 1.56.42

शं नो देवी तथा काण्डात्केतुं कृण्वन्निमाः क्रमात् ।
पूर्वोक्ताः समिधस्त्वत्र यथाशास्त्रं प्रहोमयेत् ॥

The verses proclaimed in due order are Awaken, O Bṛhaspati, beyond the noble one, and flowing from food.

BP 1.56.43

एकैकस्याष्टशतकमष्टाविंशतिरेव वा ।
होतव्या मधुसर्पिभ्यां दध्ना चैव समन्विताः ॥

The formulas are May the goddess bring us well being, From a stalk, Making a banner, and these, in order. One should offer here the previously mentioned kindling sticks according to the shastra.

BP 1.56.44

पूर्वोक्तभोजनं यद्धि ब्राह्मणेभ्यो निवेदयेत् ।
शक्तितो वा यथालाभं दक्षिणा तु विधानतः ॥

One should present the food stated before to the Brahmins, with a sacrificial gift according to capacity or according to what is obtained.

BP 1.56.45

यश्च यस्य यदा दुःस्थः स तं यत्नेन पूजयेत् ।
मयैषा हि वरो दत्तः पूजिताः पूजयिष्यथ ॥

When any planet is ill placed for someone, that planet should be worshipped carefully. This boon was indeed given by me, when worshipped, you shall worship in return.

BP 1.56.46

ग्रहाधीना नरेन्द्राणामुच्छ्रयाः पतनानि च ।
भावाभावौ च जगतस्तस्मात्पूज्यतमा ग्रहाः ॥

The rises and falls of kings are under the planets, and the existence and nonexistence of the world. Therefore, the planets are most worthy of worship.

BP 1.56.47

ग्रहा गावो नरेन्द्राश्च गुरवो ब्राह्मणास्तथा ।
पूजिताः पूजयन्त्येते निर्दहन्त्यपमानिताः ॥

Planets, cows, kings, teachers, and Brahmins, when worshipped, worship in return, and when dishonored, they burn.

BP 1.56.48

यथा समुत्थितं यन्त्रं यन्त्रेणैव प्रहन्यते ।
तथा समुत्थितां पीडां ग्रहशान्त्या प्रशामयेत् ॥

As a machine that has arisen is struck down by a machine, so one should calm an arisen affliction by the pacification of the planets.

BP 1.56.49

यज्वनां सत्यवाक्यानां तथा नित्योपवासिनाम् ।
जपहोमपराणां च सर्वं दुष्टं प्रशाम्यति ॥

For those who perform sacrifices, speak truth, always fast, and are intent on recitation and fire offerings, all evil is calmed.

BP 1.56.50

एवं कृत्वा प्रजाशान्तिं कृत्वा च स्वस्तिवाचनम् ।
पुनः सज्जं रथं कृत्वा कुर्यात्प्रक्रमणं हर ॥

Having thus brought peace to the people and performed the recitation of well being, one should make the chariot ready again and set out, O Hara.

BP 1.56.51

मार्गं शेषं नयित्वा तु नयेद्देवालयं रविम् ।
पूजयित्वा ततः पूर्वा याः प्रोक्ता रथदेवताः ॥

Having taken it along the remaining road, one should lead the Sun to the temple and worship the previously mentioned chariot deities.

BP 1.56.52

यथा पूज्या ग्रहाः सर्वे उत्पातेṣu त्रिलोचन ।
रथदेवास्तथा पूज्या याः स्थिता रथमाश्रिताः ॥

Just as all planets are to be worshipped when omens arise, O three eyed one, so too are the chariot deities who dwell on the chariot to be worshipped.
