BP 3.1.7.1

चित्रकूटगिरेर्देशे परिहारो महीपतिः ।
कलिंजरपुरं रम्यमक्रोशायतनं स्मृतम् ॥ 1 ॥

In the region of Mount Citrakūṭa was King Parihāra. The lovely city of Kaliñjara is said to extend one krośa.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
चित्रकूटगिरेर्देशे (citrakūṭagirerdeśe) — in the region of Mount Citrakūṭa;
परिहारो (parihāro) — King Parihāra;
महीपतिः (mahīpatiḥ) — king;
कलिंजरपुरं (kaliṃjarapuraṃ) — the city of Kaliñjara;
रम्यमक्रोशायतनं (ramyamakrośāyatanaṃ) — lovely; one krośa in extent;
स्मृतम् (smṛtam) — is remembered; is said;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

citrakūṭagirerdeśe parihāro mahīpatiḥ |
kaliṃjarapuraṃ ramyamakrośāyatanaṃ smṛtam || 1 ||
