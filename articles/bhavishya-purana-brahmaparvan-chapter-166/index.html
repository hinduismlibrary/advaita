Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 166 — The Nikṣubhā Vow

BP 1.166.1

सुमंतुरुवाच ।। सूर्यभक्ता तु या नारी ध्रुवं सा पुरुषो भवेत् ।। स्त्री पुत्रमुत्तमं सा चेत्कांक्षते शृणु तद्व्रतम् ।। १ ।।

Sumantu said: A woman devoted to the Sun surely becomes a man. If she, as a woman, desires an excellent son, hear that vow.

BP 1.166.2

निक्षुभार्काख्यमाख्यातं सदा प्रीतिविवर्धनम् ।। अवियोगकरं वीर धर्मकामार्थसाधकम् ।। २ ।।

The vow of Nikṣubhā and the Sun is declared: it always increases affection, prevents separation, O hero, and fulfills dharma, desire, and wealth.

BP 1.166.3

सप्तम्यामथ षष्ठ्यां वा सङ्क्रान्तौ च रवेर्दिने ।। हविषा हविर्होमं तु सोपवासः समाचरेत् ।। ३ ।।

On the seventh or sixth lunar day, on a solar transition, or on Sunday, one should perform an offering of sacrificial food into the fire while fasting.

BP 1.166.4

निक्षुभां कांस्यनिष्पन्नां कृत्वा स्वर्णमयीं शुभाम् ।। राजतीं वाथ वा वर्षं स्नापयेच्च घृतादिभिः ।। ४ ।।

Having made an auspicious image of Nikṣubhā in bronze, gold, or silver, one should bathe it for a year with ghee and the like.

BP 1.166.5

गन्धमाल्यैरलंकृत्य वस्त्रयुग्मैश्च शोभनैः ।। भक्ष्यभोज्यैरशेषैश्च वितानध्वजचामरैः ।। ५ ।।

Having adorned it with fragrances and garlands, beautiful pairs of garments, all eatables and cooked foods, canopies, banners, and fly-whisks.

BP 1.166.6

भोजयेत्सूर्यभक्तांश्च शुक्लवस्त्रावगुण्ठितान् ।। कृत्वायतनमध्ये तु प्रतिमामुपकल्पयेत् ।। ६ ।।

One should feed Sun-devotees wrapped in white garments and set up the image in the middle of the shrine.

BP 1.166.7

कृत्वा शिरसि तत्पात्रं वितानच्छत्रशोभितम् ।। ध्वजशंखादिविभवैर्भगस्यायतनं नयेत् ।। ७ ।।

Having placed that vessel on the head, adorned with a canopy and parasol, one should take it to Bhaga’s shrine with the splendors of banners, conches, and the like.

BP 1.166.8

निक्षुभार्कदिनेशस्य व्रतमेतन्निवेदयेत् ।। तत्पिंड्यां स्थापयेत्पात्रमुपशोभासमन्वितम् ।। ८ ।।

One should declare this vow of Nikṣubhā, the Sun, and the Lord of Day; one should place the adorned vessel on the pedestal.

BP 1.166.9

प्रदक्षिणीकृत्य रविं प्रणिपत्य क्षमापयेत् ।। समाप्य तद्व्रतं पुण्यं शृणुयात्फलमश्नुते ।। ९ ।।

Having circumambulated the Sun, bowed down, and asked forgiveness, one should hear of the fruit of the completed meritorious vow and obtain it.

BP 1.166.10

द्वादशादित्यसङ्काशैर्महायानैर्नगोपमैः ।। यथेष्टं भानवे लोके सौरैः सार्धं प्रमोदते ।। १० ।।

With great vehicles resembling the twelve Suns and like mountains, one rejoices as desired in the Sun’s world together with Sun-devotees.

BP 1.166.11

वर्षकोटिसहस्राणि वर्षकोटिशतानि च ।। नन्दतेऽसौ महाभाग विष्णुलोके महीयते ।। ११ ।।

For eleven billion years, that very fortunate person rejoices and is honored in Viṣṇu’s world.

BP 1.166.12

ततः कर्मविशेषेण सर्वकामसमन्वितम् ।। ब्रह्मलोकं समासाद्य परं सुखमवाप्नुयात् ।। १२ ।।

Through a particular deed, having reached Brahmā’s world and endowed with every desire, one obtains supreme happiness.

BP 1.166.13

ब्रह्मलोकात्परिभ्रष्टः श्रीमान्सुरसुपूजितः ।। प्रजापतिमवाप्नोति सुरासुरनमस्कृतः ।। १३ ।।

Having fallen from Brahmā’s world, glorious and greatly honored by the gods, one reaches Prajāpati, bowed to by gods and demons.

BP 1.166.14

लोकानिह चिरं भुक्त्वा सोमलोके महीयते ।। सोमादिन्द्रं पुनर्लोकमासाद्येन्द्रपतिर्भवेत् ।। १४ ।।

Having long enjoyed the worlds here, one is honored in Soma’s world; from Soma, having again reached Indra’s world, one becomes lord of Indra.

BP 1.166.15

इन्द्रलोकाच्च गन्धर्वलोकं प्राप्य स मोदते ।। ततस्तद्धर्मशेषेण भवत्यादित्यभावितः ।। १५ ।।

From Indra’s world, having reached the Gandharva world, one rejoices; then, through the remainder of that dharma, one becomes filled with the Sun.

BP 1.166.16

स्वकर्मभावनोद्योगात्पुनः प्रारभते शुभम् ।। शुभाच्च पुनरेत्येह स यात्यतिसहस्रशः ।। १६ ।।

Through striving to cultivate one’s own deeds, one begins the good anew; from the good, returning here again, one goes forth many thousands of times.

BP 1.166.17

यावन्नाप्नोति मरणं तावद्भ्रमति कर्मणा ।। सुनिर्वेदात्सवैराग्यं वैराग्याज्ज्ञानसंभवः ।। १७ ।।

As long as one does not meet death, so long one wanders through action; from profound disillusionment comes detachment, and from detachment knowledge arises.

BP 1.166.18

ज्ञानात्प्रवर्तते योगो योगाद्दुःखान्तमाप्नुयात् ।। १८ ।।

From knowledge yoga proceeds; through yoga one reaches the end of sorrow.
