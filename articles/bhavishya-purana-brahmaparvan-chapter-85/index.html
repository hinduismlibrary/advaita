Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 85 — The Wish Granting Observance

BP 1.85.1

ब्रह्मोवाच । प्राप्ते मार्गशीर्षे मासि शुक्ल-षष्ठ्यां तु यो भवेत् । स ज्ञेयः कामदो वारः स इष्टो भास्करस्य तु ॥ ॥

Brahmā said. When the sixth day of the bright fortnight falls in Mārgaśīrṣa, it is known as the day that grants desires, dear to the Sun.

brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said;
prāpte — having arrived;
mārgaśīrṣe māsi — in the month of Mārgaśīrṣa;
śukla-ṣaṣṭhyām — on the sixth day of the bright fortnight;
tu — indeed;
yaḥ — which;
bhavet — occurs;
saḥ — that;
jñeyaḥ — to be known;
kāmadaḥ — Kāmadā;
vāraḥ — day;
saḥ — that;
iṣṭaḥ — beloved;
bhāskarasya — of Bhāskara;
tu — indeed.

BP 1.85.2

तत्र यः पूजयेद् भानुं भक्त्या श्रद्धा-समन्वितः । विमुक्तः सर्व-पापैस् तु प्राप्णुते नन्दनाधिपम् ॥ ॥

Whoever worships the Sun there with devotion and faith, freed from every sin, reaches the lord of Nandana Garden.

tatra — there;
yaḥ — whoever;
pūjayet — should worship;
bhānum — Bhānu;
bhaktyā — with devotion;
śraddhā-samanvitaḥ — endowed with faith;
vimuktaḥ — freed;
sarva-pāpaiḥ — from every sin;
tu — indeed;
prāpṇute — attains;
nandanādhipam — Lord of Nandana.

BP 1.85.3

रक्त-चन्दन-मिश्राणि करवीराणि सुव्रत । धूपं घृताहुतिं वीर भास्करस्य प्रयोजयेत् ॥ ॥

O well vowed one, he should offer oleander flowers mixed with red sandalwood. O hero, he should offer incense and ghee oblations to the Sun.

rakta-candana-miśrāṇi — mixed with red sandalwood;
karavīrāṇi — karavīra flowers;
suvrata — O well-disciplined one;
dhūpam — incense;
ghṛta-āhutim — oblation of ghee;
vīra — O hero;
bhāskarasya — for Bhāskara;
prayojayet — should employ, offer.

BP 1.85.4

नैवेद्यं चापि कृशरं सु-गन्धं तीक्ष्णम् एव च । कृत्वोपवासम् अथ वा नक्तं त्रिपुर-सूदन ॥ ॥

He should offer a fragrant, pungent rice and lentil dish as food, and should fast or observe the night rite, O destroyer of Tripura.

naivedyam — food offering;
ca api — and also;
kṛśaram — kṛśara;
su-gandham — fragrant;
tīkṣṇam — sharp, pungent;
eva — indeed;
ca — and;
kṛtvā — having performed;
upavāsam — fasting;
atha vā — or else;
naktam — night observance;
tripura-sūdana — O destroyer of Tripura.

BP 1.85.5

इत्थं प्रपूजितो ह्य् अत्र भास्करो लोक-भास्करः । कामान् ददाति सर्वान् वै अतो ऽयं कामदः स्मृतः ॥ ॥

Worshipped here in this way, the Sun, illuminator of the world, grants every desire. Therefore he is remembered as the Wish Giver.

ittham — thus;
prapūjitaḥ — fully worshiped;
hi — indeed;
atra — here;
bhāskaraḥ — Bhāskara;
loka-bhāskaraḥ — illuminator of the world;
kāmān — desires;
dadāti — gives;
sarvān — all;
vai — indeed;
ataḥ — therefore;
ayam — this one;
kāmadaḥ — Kāmadā, giver of desires;
smṛtaḥ — remembered.

BP 1.85.6

स पुत्रं पुत्र-कामस्य धन-कामस्य वा धनम् । विद्यार्थिने शुभां विद्याम् आरोग्यं रोगिणे विभो ॥ ॥

He gives a son to the one desiring a son, wealth to the one desiring wealth, auspicious knowledge to the one seeking knowledge, and health to the sick, O Lord.

saḥ — he;
putram — son;
putra-kāmasya — of one desiring a son;
dhanam — wealth;
dhana-kāmasya — of one desiring wealth;
vā — or;
vidyārthine — to one seeking knowledge;
śubhām vidyām — auspicious learning;
ārogyam — health;
rogiṇe — to the sick person;
vibho — O Lord.

BP 1.85.7

अन्यांश् च विविधान् कामान् मन्त्रैः सम्पूजितो रविः । ददाति गण-शार्दूल अतो ऽयं कामदः स्मृतः ॥ ॥

Worshipped fully with mantras, the Sun grants other various desires as well, O chief of hosts. Therefore he is remembered as the Wish Giver.

anyān — other;
ca — and;
vividhān — various;
kāmān — desires;
mantraiḥ — with mantras;
sampūjitaḥ — fully worshiped;
raviḥ — Ravi;
dadāti — gives;
gaṇa-śārdūla — O tiger among the hosts;
ataḥ — therefore;
ayam — this one;
kāmadaḥ — Kāmadā;
smṛtaḥ — remembered.

BP 1.85.8

दद्याद् यो मण्डकं चात्र गोपतेर् गोत्र-भूषणः । गोत्रारि-तेजसा तुल्यो गोपतेर् गोपुरं व्रजेत् ॥ ॥

Whoever gives a cake here becomes an ornament of the lineage of the Lord of Cows, equal in splendor to the foe of mountains, and goes to the gateway of the Lord of Cows.

dadyāt — should give;
yaḥ — whoever;
maṇḍakam — cake;
ca — and;
atra — here;
gopateḥ — of the Lord of cows;
gotra-bhūṣaṇaḥ — ornament of the lineage;
gotra-ari-tejasā — with the splendor of the lineage’s foe;
tulyaḥ — equal;
gopateḥ — of the Lord of cows;
gopuram — gateway;
vrajet — goes.
