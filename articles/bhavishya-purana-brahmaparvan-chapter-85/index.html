Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 85 — The Kāmadā Observance

BP 1.85.1

ब्रह्मोवाच । प्राप्ते मार्गशीर्षे मासि शुक्ल-षष्ठ्यां तु यो भवेत् । स ज्ञेयः कामदो वारः स इष्टो भास्करस्य तु ॥ ॥

Brahmā said: The day that occurs on the sixth day of the bright fortnight in the month of Mārgaśīrṣa is to be known as the Kāmadā day, beloved of Bhāskara.

Word-for-word
brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said;
prāpte — having arrived;
mārgaśīrṣe māsi — in the month of Mārgaśīrṣa;
śukla-ṣaṣṭhyām — on the sixth day of the bright fortnight;
tu — indeed;
yaḥ — which;
bhavet — occurs;
saḥ — that;
jñeyaḥ — to be known;
kāmadaḥ — Kāmadā;
vāraḥ — day;
saḥ — that;
iṣṭaḥ — beloved;
bhāskarasya — of Bhāskara;
tu — indeed.
Transliteration
brahmovāca | prāpte mārgaśīrṣe māsi śukla-ṣaṣṭhyāṃ tu yo bhavet | sa jñeyaḥ kāmado vāraḥ sa iṣṭo bhāskarasya tu || 1 ||

BP 1.85.2

तत्र यः पूजयेद् भानुं भक्त्या श्रद्धा-समन्वितः । विमुक्तः सर्व-पापैस् तु प्राप्णुते नन्दनाधिपम् ॥ ॥

Whoever there worships Bhānu with devotion and faith, freed from every sin, attains the Lord of Nandana.

Word-for-word
tatra — there;
yaḥ — whoever;
pūjayet — should worship;
bhānum — Bhānu;
bhaktyā — with devotion;
śraddhā-samanvitaḥ — endowed with faith;
vimuktaḥ — freed;
sarva-pāpaiḥ — from every sin;
tu — indeed;
prāpṇute — attains;
nandanādhipam — Lord of Nandana.
Transliteration
tatra yaḥ pūjayed bhānuṃ bhaktyā śraddhā-samanvitaḥ | vimuktaḥ sarva-pāpais tu prāpṇute nandanādhipam || 2 ||

BP 1.85.3

रक्त-चन्दन-मिश्राणि करवीराणि सुव्रत । धूपं घृताहुतिं वीर भास्करस्य प्रयोजयेत् ॥ ॥

O well-disciplined one, he should use karavīra flowers mixed with red sandalwood, and, O hero, he should offer incense and oblations of ghee to Bhāskara.

Word-for-word
rakta-candana-miśrāṇi — mixed with red sandalwood;
karavīrāṇi — karavīra flowers;
suvrata — O well-disciplined one;
dhūpam — incense;
ghṛta-āhutim — oblation of ghee;
vīra — O hero;
bhāskarasya — for Bhāskara;
prayojayet — should employ, offer.
Transliteration
rakta-candana-miśrāṇi karavīrāṇi suvrata | dhūpaṃ ghṛtāhutiṃ vīra bhāskarasya prayojayet || 3 ||

BP 1.85.4

नैवेद्यं चापि कृशरं सु-गन्धं तीक्ष्णम् एव च । कृत्वोपवासम् अथ वा नक्तं त्रिपुर-सूदन ॥ ॥

He should offer kṛśara as food, fragrant and sharp, and should observe a fast or else the night observance, O destroyer of Tripura.

Word-for-word
naivedyam — food offering;
ca api — and also;
kṛśaram — kṛśara;
su-gandham — fragrant;
tīkṣṇam — sharp, pungent;
eva — indeed;
ca — and;
kṛtvā — having performed;
upavāsam — fasting;
atha vā — or else;
naktam — night observance;
tripura-sūdana — O destroyer of Tripura.
Transliteration
naivedyaṃ cāpi kṛśaraṃ su-gandhaṃ tīkṣṇam eva ca | kṛtvopavāsam atha vā naktaṃ tripura-sūdana || 4 ||

BP 1.85.5

इत्थं प्रपूजितो ह्य् अत्र भास्करो लोक-भास्करः । कामान् ददाति सर्वान् वै अतो ऽयं कामदः स्मृतः ॥ ॥

Bhāskara, the illuminator of the world, being worshiped in this way here, grants every desire; therefore he is remembered as Kāmadā.

Word-for-word
ittham — thus;
prapūjitaḥ — fully worshiped;
hi — indeed;
atra — here;
bhāskaraḥ — Bhāskara;
loka-bhāskaraḥ — illuminator of the world;
kāmān — desires;
dadāti — gives;
sarvān — all;
vai — indeed;
ataḥ — therefore;
ayam — this one;
kāmadaḥ — Kāmadā, giver of desires;
smṛtaḥ — remembered.
Transliteration
itthaṃ prapūjito hy atra bhāskaro loka-bhāskaraḥ | kāmān dadāti sarvān vai ato 'yaṃ kāmadaḥ smṛtaḥ || 5 ||

BP 1.85.6

स पुत्रं पुत्र-कामस्य धन-कामस्य वा धनम् । विद्यार्थिने शुभां विद्याम् आरोग्यं रोगिणे विभो ॥ ॥

He gives a son to one who desires a son, wealth to one who desires wealth, auspicious learning to one who seeks knowledge, and health to the sick, O Lord.

Word-for-word
saḥ — he;
putram — son;
putra-kāmasya — of one desiring a son;
dhanam — wealth;
dhana-kāmasya — of one desiring wealth;
vā — or;
vidyārthine — to one seeking knowledge;
śubhām vidyām — auspicious learning;
ārogyam — health;
rogiṇe — to the sick person;
vibho — O Lord.
Transliteration
sa putraṃ putra-kāmasya dhana-kāmasya vā dhanam | vidyārthine śubhāṃ vidyām ārogyaṃ rogiṇe vibho || 6 ||

BP 1.85.7

अन्यांश् च विविधान् कामान् मन्त्रैः सम्पूजितो रविः । ददाति गण-शार्दूल अतो ऽयं कामदः स्मृतः ॥ ॥

Ravi, being fully worshiped with mantras, grants various other desires as well, O tiger among the hosts; therefore he is remembered as Kāmadā.

kāmadaḥ — Kāmadā;
smṛtaḥ — remembered.
Transliteration
anyāṃś ca vividhān kāmān mantraiḥ sampūjito raviḥ | dadāti gaṇa-śārdūla ato 'yaṃ kāmadaḥ smṛtaḥ || 7 ||

BP 1.85.8

दद्याद् यो मण्डकं चात्र गोपतेर् गोत्र-भूषणः । गोत्रारि-तेजसा तुल्यो गोपतेर् गोपुरं व्रजेत् ॥ ॥

Whoever gives a cake here, the ornament of the Lord of cows lineage, goes to the gateway of the Lord of cows, equal in radiance to the splendor of the lineages foe.

Word-for-word

gotra-ari-tejasā — with the splendor of the lineage’s foe;
tulyaḥ — equal;
gopateḥ — of the Lord of cows;
gopuram — gateway;
vrajet — goes.
Transliteration
dadyād yo maṇḍakaṃ cātra gopater gotra-bhūṣaṇaḥ | gotrāri-tejasā tulyo gopater gopuraṃ vrajet || 8 ||
