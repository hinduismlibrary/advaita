Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 39 — The Sixth Day Vow and Worship of Kārttikeya

BP 1.39.1

॥सुमंतुरुवाच॥ ॥
षष्ठ्यां फलाशनो राजन्विशेषात्कार्तिके नृप ॥
राज्यच्युतो विशेषेण स्वं राज्यं लभतेऽचिरात्॥ ॥

Sumantu said. O king, one who eats fruit on the sixth lunar day, especially in Kārttika, quickly regains his own kingdom, especially if deprived of it.

सुमन्तुः उवाच (sumantuḥ uvāca) — Sumantu said;
षष्ठ्याम् (ṣaṣṭhyām) — on sixth lunar day;
फल-अशनः (phala-aśanaḥ) — eating fruit;
विशेषात् कार्तिके (viśeṣāt kārtike) — especially in Kārttika;
राज्य-च्युतः (rājya-cyutaḥ) — fallen from kingdom;
स्वम् राज्यम् लभते अचिरात् (svam rājyam labhate acirāt) — quickly regains own kingdom

BP 1.39.2

षष्ठी तिथिर्महाराज सर्वदा सर्वकामदा॥
उपोष्या तु प्रयत्नेन सर्वकालं जयार्थिना ॥

O great king, the sixth lunar day always grants all desires. A person seeking victory should fast carefully on it at all times.

षष्ठी तिथिः (ṣaṣṭhī tithiḥ) — sixth lunar day;
सर्वदा सर्व-काम-दा (sarvadā sarva-kāma-dā) — always granting every desire;
उपोष्या प्रयत्नेन (upoṣyā prayatnena) — to be fasted upon carefully;
सर्व-कालम् (sarva-kālam) — at all times;
जय-अर्थिना (jaya-arthinā) — by one seeking victory

BP 1.39.3

कार्त्तिकेयस्य दयिता एषा षष्ठी महातिथिः ।
देवसेनाधिपत्यं हि प्राप्तं तस्यां महात्मना ॥

This great lunar day of the sixth is dear to Kārttikeya. On it that great soul obtained command of the army of the gods.

कार्त्तिकेयस्य दयिता (kārttikeyasya dayitā) — dear to Kārttikeya;
एषा षष्ठी महा-तिथिः (eṣā ṣaṣṭhī mahā-tithiḥ) — this great sixth date;
देव-सेना-अधिपत्यम् (deva-senā-adhipatyam) — command of divine army;
प्राप्तम् तस्याम् महात्मना (prāptam tasyām mahātmanā) — obtained on it by great soul

BP 1.39.4

अस्यां हि श्रेयसा युक्तो यस्मात्स्कन्दो भवाग्रणीः ।
तस्मात्षष्ठ्यां नक्तभोजी प्राप्नुयादीप्सितं सदा ॥

Since Skanda is joined with good fortune on this day and is foremost among beings, one who eats at night on the sixth always obtains what he desires.

अस्याम् श्रेयसा युक्तः (asyām śreyasā yuktaḥ) — joined with excellence on this;
स्कन्दः भव-अग्रणीः (skandaḥ bhava-agraṇīḥ) — Skanda foremost of Bhava’s followers;
तस्मात् षष्ठ्याम् (tasmāt ṣaṣṭhyām) — therefore on sixth;
नक्त-भोजी (nakta-bhojī) — eating at night;
प्राप्नुयात् ईप्सितम् (prāpnuyāt īpsitam) — obtains desired

BP 1.39.5

दत्त्वार्घ्यं कार्तिकेयाय स्थित्वा वै दक्षिणामुखः ।
दध्ना घृतोदकैः पुष्पैर्मन्त्रेणानेन सुव्रत ॥

Having made the ceremonial offering to Kārttikeya, standing facing south with curds, ghee, water, and flowers, using this mantra, O keeper of vows.

दत्त्वा अर्घ्यम् कार्तिकेयाय (dattvā arghyam kārtikeyāya) — offering arghya to Kārttikeya;
स्थित्वा दक्षिणा-मुखः (sthitvā dakṣiṇā-mukhaḥ) — standing south-facing;
दध्ना घृत-उदकैः पुष्पैः (dadhnā ghṛta-udakaiḥ puṣpaiḥ) — with curd ghee water flowers;
मन्त्रेण अनेन (mantreṇa anena) — with this mantra

BP 1.39.6

सप्तर्षिदारज स्कन्द स्वाहापतिसमुद्भव ।
रुद्रार्यमाग्निज विभो गङ्गागर्भ नमोऽस्तु ते ॥
प्रीयतां देवसेनानीः सम्पादयतु हृद्गतम् ॥

O Skanda, born from the wives of the seven sages, born from the husband of Svāhā, born from Rudra, Aryaman, and Agni, O all pervading one, child of Gaṅgā, homage to you. May the commander of the gods be pleased and fulfill what is in my heart.

सप्त-ऋषि-दार-ज स्कन्द (sapta-ṛṣi-dāra-ja skanda) — Skanda born of Seven Seers’ wives;
स्वाहा-पति-समुद्भव (svāhā-pati-samudbhava) — born of Svāhā’s lord;
रुद्र-अर्यम-अग्नि-ज (rudra-aryama-agni-ja) — offspring of Rudra Aryaman Agni;
गङ्गा-गर्भ (gaṅgā-garbha) — Gaṅgā-borne embryo;
नमः ते (namaḥ te) — homage to you;
प्रीयताम् देव-सेनानीः (prīyatām deva-senānīḥ) — may divine commander be pleased;
सम्पादयतु हृद्-गतम् (sampādayatu hṛd-gatam) — may he fulfil heart’s desire

BP 1.39.7

दत्त्वा विप्राय चात्मान्नं यच्चान्यदपि विद्यते ।
पश्चाद्भुङ्क्ते त्वसौ रात्रौ भूमिं कृत्वा तु भाजनम् ॥

After giving his own food and whatever else is available to a Brahmin, he eats later at night, making the ground his vessel.

दत्त्वा विप्राय आत्म-अन्नम् (dattvā viprāya ātma-annam) — giving brāhmaṇa one’s food;
यत् च अन्यत् विद्यते (yat ca anyat vidyate) — whatever else exists;
पश्चात् भुङ्क्ते रात्रौ (paścāt bhuṅkte rātrau) — afterward eats at night;
भूमिम् कृत्वा भाजनम् (bhūmim kṛtvā bhājanam) — making earth the dish

BP 1.39.8

एवं षष्ठ्यां व्रतं स्नेहात्प्रोक्तं स्कन्देन यत्नतः ।
तन्निबोध महाराज प्रोच्यमानं मयाखिलम् ॥

Thus Skanda carefully taught this vow for the sixth day with affection. Hear it in full, O great king, as I explain it.

एवम् षष्ठ्याम् व्रतम् (evam ṣaṣṭhyām vratam) — thus vow on sixth;
स्नेहात् प्रोक्तम् स्कन्देन (snehāt proktam skandena) — lovingly taught by Skanda;
यत्नतः (yatnataḥ) — carefully;
तत् निबोध महाराज (tat nibodha mahārāja) — understand it great king;
प्रोच्यमानम् मया अखिलम् (procyamānam mayā akhilam) — fully explained by me

BP 1.39.9

षष्ठ्यां यस्तु फलाहारो नक्ताहारो भविष्यति ।
शुक्लाकृष्णासु नियतो ब्रह्मचारी समाहितः ॥

Whoever eats fruit and eats at night on the sixth day remains disciplined through both the bright and dark fortnights, celibate, and composed.

षष्ठ्याम् यः फल-आहारः (ṣaṣṭhyām yaḥ phala-āhāraḥ) — one eating fruit on sixth;
नक्त-आहारः (nakta-āhāraḥ) — eating at night;
शुक्ल-कृष्णासु नियतः (śukla-kṛṣṇāsu niyataḥ) — disciplined in bright and dark fortnights;
ब्रह्मचारी (brahmacārī) — celibate;
समाहितः (samāhitaḥ) — concentrated

BP 1.39.10

तस्य सिद्धिं धृतिं तुष्टिं राज्यमायुर्निरामयम् ।
पारत्रिकं चैहिकं च दद्यात्स्कन्दो न संशयः ॥

For him Skanda grants accomplishment, firmness, contentment, kingdom, healthy longevity, and benefits in the next world and this world, without doubt.

तस्य सिद्धिम् धृतिम् तुष्टिम् (tasya siddhim dhṛtim tuṣṭim) — his accomplishment steadfastness satisfaction;
राज्यम् आयुः निरामयम् (rājyam āyuḥ nirāmayam) — kingship and disease-free lifespan;
पारलौकिकम् ऐहिकम् च (pāralaukikam aihikam ca) — otherworldly and worldly;
दद्यात् स्कन्दः (dadyāt skandaḥ) — Skanda grants;
न संशयः (na saṃśayaḥ) — no doubt

BP 1.39.11

यो हि नक्तोपवासः स्यात्स नक्तेन व्रती भवेत् ।
इह वामुत्र सोऽत्यन्तं लभते ख्यातिमुत्तमाम् ॥
स्वर्गे च नियतं वासं लभते नात्र संशयः ॥

One who observes a fast through the night should keep the vow by eating at night. Here or in the other world he gains the highest renown and surely obtains a permanent dwelling in heaven, without doubt.

यः नक्त-उपवासः (yaḥ nakta-upavāsaḥ) — one fasting until night;
नक्तेन व्रती (naktena vratī) — night-eating observer;
इह वा अमुत्र (iha vā amutra) — here or hereafter;
ख्यातिम् उत्तमाम् लभते (khyātim uttamām labhate) — obtains supreme fame;
स्वर्गे नियतम् वासम् (svarge niyatam vāsam) — assured heavenly residence;
न संशयः (na saṃśayaḥ) — no doubt

BP 1.39.12

इह चागत्य कालान्ते यथोक्तफलभाग्भवेत् ।
देवानामपि वन्द्योऽसौ राज्ञां राजा भविष्यति ॥

On coming here at the end of time, he partakes of the fruits as stated. Revered even by the gods, he becomes king of kings.

इह आगत्य काल-अन्ते (iha āgatya kāla-ante) — returning here at period’s end;
यथा-उक्त-फल-भाक् (yathā-ukta-phala-bhāk) — sharing stated fruits;
देवानाम् अपि वन्द्यः (devānām api vandyaḥ) — revered even by gods;
राज्ञाम् राजा भविष्यति (rājñām rājā bhaviṣyati) — becomes king of kings

BP 1.39.13

यश्चापि शृणुयात्कल्पं षष्ठ्याः कुरुकुलोद्वह ।
तस्य सिद्धिस्तथा तुष्टिर्धृतिः स्यात्ख्यातिसम्भवा ॥

Whoever also hears the observance of the sixth day, O descendant of the Kuru line, will have accomplishment, contentment, firmness, and fame arising from it.

यः शृणुयात् कल्पम् षष्ठ्याः (yaḥ śṛṇuyāt kalpam ṣaṣṭhyāḥ) — one hearing ordinance of sixth;
कुरु-कुल-उद्वह (kuru-kula-udvaha) — bearer of Kuru lineage;
तस्य सिद्धिः तुष्टिः धृतिः (tasya siddhiḥ tuṣṭiḥ dhṛtiḥ) — his accomplishment satisfaction steadfastness;
ख्याति-सम्भवा (khyāti-sambhavā) — arising as fame
