VP 4.20.1

परीक्षितश्च जनमेजयश्रुतसेनोग्रसेनभीमसेनाश्चात्वारः पुत्राः

Sri Paraasharar - From Pareekshit, four sons were born - Janamejaya, Shrutasena, Ugrasena and Bheemasena.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

parīkṣitaśca janamejayaśrutasenograsenabhīmasenāścātvāraḥ putrāḥ

Śrīdhara

viṃśe tu kuruputrāṇāṃ vaṃśānāha parāśaraḥ yatra svapurasantāna dhṛtarāṣṭrādisantatiḥ || kuroḥ putrāṇāṃ madhye sudhanuṣo vaṃśa uktaḥ, idānīṃ kuruputrasyaiva parikṣito vaṃśa ucyate . paṃrikṣita iti ||१||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

विंशे तु कुरुपुत्राणां वंशानाह पराशरः यत्र स्वपुरसन्तान धृतराष्ट्रादिसन्ततिः ॥ कुरोः पुत्राणां मध्ये सुधनुषो वंश उक्तः, इदानीं कुरुपुत्रस्यैव परिक्षितो वंश उच्यते । पंरिक्षित इति ॥१॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Sri Paraasharar - From Pareekshit, four sons were born - Janamejaya, Shrutasena, Ugrasena and Bheemasena. Sri Engal Aalwaan’s Commentary | |

VP 4.20.13

यंयं कराभ्यां स्पृशति वीर्णं यौवनमेति सः । शान्तिं चाप्नोति येनाग्र्यां कर्मणा तेन शन्तनुः

Shantanu was so famous that this shloka is being sung in respect of him. Whomever Shantanu touches with his hands, if he is old also, he will become young. That was his power. He gets utmost peace and tranquility, and because of that, he was called Shantanu.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yaṃyaṃ karābhyāṃ spṛśati vīrṇaṃ yauvanameti saḥ | śāntiṃ cāpnoti yenāgryāṃ karmaṇā tena śantanuḥ

Śrīdhara

yaṃ yaṃ jantuṃ spṛśati, sa sa yauvanameti, śāntiñcāpnoti . etāvatā yauvanādirūpaṃ śaṃ kalyāṇaṃ tanotīti śāntanunāmanirukteti jñeyam ||१३||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

यं यं जन्तुं स्पृशति, स स यौवनमेति, शान्तिञ्चाप्नोति । एतावता यौवनादिरूपं शं कल्याणं तनोतीति शान्तनुनामनिरुक्तेति ज्ञेयम् ॥१३॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Shantanu was so famous that this shloka is being sung in respect of him. Whomever Shantanu touches with his hands, if he is old also, he will become young. That was his power. He gets utmost peace and tranquility, and because of that, he was called Shantanu.

Śrīdhara commentary.

For twelve years, there were no rains in Shantanu's country.

VP 4.20.15

The gods are not bringing rains to my country, and the country was in a lot of trouble because there was no water, there was famine. He called all the Brahmins and asked them - what is my aparaadha, what wrong did I do? Why are the gods not bringing rains to my country?

No commentary.

VP 4.20.21

तदलमेतेन तु तस्मै दीयतामित्युक्ते तस्यं मन्त्रिप्रवरेणाश्मराविणा तत्रारण्ये तपस्विनो वेदवादविरोधवक्तारः प्रयुक्ताः

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

Śrīdhara commentary.

VP 4.20.26

असावपि देवापिर्वेदवादविरोधयुक्तिदूषितमनेकप्रकारं तानाह

Devaapi's mind was already disturbed by being in the company of those tapasvis, who were talking against the Vedaas. So, he started to argue with them against the Vedaas. He started to argue against what is told in the Vedaas. He started to prove that all those things were wrong. His arguments were against the Vedaas. Devaapi fell from the path of righteousness only during that time, because in the next Kruta yuga, Devaapi is said to be the restorer of the Kshatriya race. He fell from the path of righteousness only for a brief time. Whatever is told in the mantra as is applicable to another kalpa. Devaapi started to bring out arguments against what the Brahmins said.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

VP 4.20.30

वेदवादविरोधवचनोच्चारणदूषिते च तस्मिन्देवापौ तिष्ठत्यपि ज्येष्ठभ्रातर्यखिलसस्यनिष्पत्तये ववर्ष भगवान्पर्जन्यः

Because Devaapi had gone away from the path of righteousness, Shantanu was eligible to rule over the country.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vedavādavirodhavacanoccāraṇadūṣite ca tasmindevāpau tiṣṭhatyapi jyeṣṭhabhrātaryakhilasasyaniṣpattaye vavarṣa bhagavānparjanyaḥ

Śrīdhara

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

शान्तनोर्वशमतिविस्तरेणाग्रे वक्ष्यन् प्रथमं बाह्लीकवंशमाह—बाह्लीकस्य इति ॥३०॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Because Devaapi had gone away from the path of the Vedaas, though the elder brother Devaapi was there, the God Parjanya brought abundant rains so that there is abundant harvest in his country. Because Devaapi had gone away from the path of righteousness, Shantanu was eligible to rule over the country.
