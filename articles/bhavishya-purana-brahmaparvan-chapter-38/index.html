Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 38 — The Fifth Day Serpent Rite in Āśvayuja

BP 1.38.1

॥ ॥ सुमन्तुरुवाच ॥ ॥
तथा चाश्वयुजे मासि पञ्चम्यां कुरुनन्दन ॥
कृत्वा कुशमयान्नागान्गन्धाद्यैः सम्प्रपूजयेत् ॥ ॥

Sumantu said. Likewise, O descendant of Kuru, in the month of Āśvayuja, on the fifth lunar day, one should make serpents of sacred grass and worship them thoroughly with perfumes and other offerings.

सुमन्तुः (sumantuḥ) — Sumantu; उवाच (uvāca) — said; तथा (tathā) — likewise; आश्वयुजे (āśvayuje) — in Āśvayuja; मासि (māsi) — in the month; पञ्चम्याम् (pañcamyām) — on the fifth lunar day; कुरुनन्दन (kurunandana) — O descendant of Kuru; कृत्वा (kṛtvā) — having made; कुशमयान् (kuśamayān) — made of Kuśa grass; नागान् (nāgān) — Nāgas; गन्धाद्यैः (gandhādyaiḥ) — with fragrance and the rest; सम्प्रपूजयेत् (samprapūjayet) — one should worship thoroughly

BP 1.38.2

घृतोदकाभ्यां पयसा स्नपयित्वा विशाम्पते ॥
गोधूमैः पयसा स्विन्नैर्भक्ष्यैश्च विविधैस्तथा ॥ ॥

Having bathed them with water mixed with ghee and with milk, O lord of the people, one should offer wheat cooked in milk and various foods.

घृतोदकाभ्याम् (ghṛtodakābhyām) — with ghee-water; पयसा (payasā) — with milk; स्नपयित्वा (snapayitvā) — having bathed; विशाम्पते (viśāmpate) — O lord of the people; गोधूमैः (godhūmaiḥ) — with wheat; पयसा (payasā) — with milk; स्विन्नैः (svinnaiḥ) — cooked; भक्ष्यैः (bhakṣyaiḥ) — with foods; च (ca) — and; विविधैः (vividhaiḥ) — various; तथा (tathā) — also

BP 1.38.3

यस्त्वस्यां विधिवन्नागाञ्छुचिर्भक्त्या समन्वितः ॥
पूजयेत्कुरुशार्दूल तस्य शेषादयो नृप ॥ ॥

O tiger of the Kurus, O king, whoever worships the serpents properly on this day, pure and devoted, has Śeṣa and the other serpents.

यः (yaḥ) — who; तु (tu) — indeed; अस्याम् (asyām) — on this; विधिवत् (vidhivat) — according to rule; नागान् (nāgān) — Nāgas; शुचिः (śuciḥ) — pure; भक्त्या (bhaktyā) — with devotion; समन्वितः (samanvitaḥ) — endowed; पूजयेत् (pūjayet) — should worship; कुरुशार्दूल (kuruśārdūla) — O tiger of the Kurus; तस्य (tasya) — for him; शेषादयः (śeṣādayaḥ) — Śeṣa and the others; नृप (nṛpa) — O king

BP 1.38.4

नागाः प्रीता भवन्ति इह शांतिमाप्नोति वा विभो ॥
स शान्तिलोकमासाद्य मोदते शाश्वतीः समाः ॥ ॥

The serpents are pleased here, and he attains peace, O lord. Having reached the world of peace, he rejoices for everlasting years.

नागाः (nāgāḥ) — Nāgas; प्रीताः (prītāḥ) — pleased; भवन्ति (bhavanti) — become; इह (iha) — here; शान्तिम् (śāntim) — peace; आप्नोति (āpnoti) — he obtains; वा (vā) — or; विभो (vibho) — O lord; सः (saḥ) — he; शान्तिलोकम् (śāntilokam) — the world of peace; आसाद्य (āsādya) — having reached; मोदते (modate) — rejoices; शाश्वतीः (śāśvatīḥ) — eternal; समाः (samāḥ) — years

BP 1.38.9

इत्येष कथितो वीर पञ्चमीकल्प उत्तमः ।
यत्रायमुच्यते मन्त्रः सर्वसर्पनिषेधकः ॥
ॐ कुरुकुल्ले फट् स्वाहा ॥

Thus, O hero, this excellent rite of the fifth lunar day has been told, in which the formula that wards off all serpents is recited.

इति (iti) — thus; एषः (eṣaḥ) — this; कथितः (kathitaḥ) — is told; वीर (vīra) — O hero; पञ्चमीकल्पः (pañcamīkalpaḥ) — Pañcamī rite; उत्तमः (uttamaḥ) — excellent; यत्र (yatra) — in which; अयम् (ayam) — this; उच्यते (ucyate) — is spoken; मन्त्रः (mantraḥ) — mantra; सर्वसर्पनिषेधकः (sarvasarpaniṣedhakaḥ) — warding off all serpents; ॐ (oṃ) — Oṃ; कुरुकुल्ले (kurukulle) — Kurukulle; फट् (phaṭ) — Phaṭ; स्वाहा (svāhā) — Svāhā
