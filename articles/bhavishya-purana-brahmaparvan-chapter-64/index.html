Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 64 — The Fruitful Seventh Day Observance and Its Annual Rite

BP 1.64.1

दिण्डिरुवाच ।
उपवासैः सुरश्रेष्ठ कथं तुष्यति भास्करः ।
परिहारांस्तथाचक्ष्व ये त्याज्याश्चोपवासिभिः ॥ १ ॥

Diṇḍi said. O best of the gods, tell me how fasting pleases the Sun, and what those who fast should avoid.

diṇḍiḥ uvāca — Diṇḍi said; upavāsaiḥ — by fasts; sura-śreṣṭha — O best of the gods; katham — how; tuṣyati — is pleased; bhāskaraḥ — Bhāskara; parihārān — avoidances; tathā ācakṣva — explain likewise; ye — which; tyājyāḥ — should be abandoned; upavāsibhiḥ — by those who fast

BP 1.64.2

यद्यत्कार्यं यथा चैव भास्कराराधने नरैः ।
तत्सर्वं विस्तराद्ब्रह्मन् यथावद्वक्तुमर्हसि ॥ २ ॥

You should explain fully and correctly, O Brahman, everything people should do in worship of the Sun and how each thing should be done.

yat yat — whatever; kāryam — must be done; yathā ca eva — and in whatever way; bhāskara-ārādhane — in Bhāskara’s worship; naraiḥ — by people; tat sarvam — all that; vistarāt — in detail; brahman — O Brahman; yathāvat — properly; vaktum arhasi — you ought to speak

BP 1.64.3

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
स्मृतः सम्पूजितो धूपपुष्पान्नैर्भानुरादरात् ।
भोगिनामुपकाराय किं पुनश्चोपवासिनाम् ॥ ३ ॥

Brahmā said. The Sun, remembered and worshiped with reverence, incense, flowers, and food, benefits those who partake, and benefits those who fast even more.

brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said; smṛtaḥ — remembered; sampūjitaḥ — fully worshiped; dhūpa-puṣpa-annaiḥ — with incense, flowers, and food; bhānuḥ — Bhānu; ādarāt — respectfully; bhoginām upakārāya — for the benefit of those who enjoy; kim punaḥ — how much more; upavāsinām — of those who fast

BP 1.64.4

उपावृत्तस्य पापेभ्यो यस्तु वासो गुणैः सह ।
उपवासः स विज्ञेयः सर्वभोगविवर्जितः ॥ ४ ॥

Fasting is known as dwelling with virtue after turning away from sins, and as being free from every enjoyment.

upāvṛttasya — of one who has turned away; pāpebhyaḥ — from sins; yaḥ tu vāsaḥ — that dwelling; guṇaiḥ saha — together with virtues; upavāsaḥ — fasting; saḥ vijñeyaḥ — it should be understood; sarva-bhoga-vivarjitaḥ — free from every enjoyment

BP 1.64.5

एकरात्रं द्विरात्रं वा त्रिरात्रं नक्तमेव च ।
उपवासी रविं यस्तु भक्त्या ध्यायति मानवः ॥ ५ ॥

A person fasts for one night, two nights, three nights, or at night alone, and meditates on the Sun with devotion.

eka-rātram — one night; dvi-rātram — two nights; tri-rātram — three nights; naktam eva — only at night; ca — and; upavāsī — fasting; ravim — upon Ravi; yaḥ tu — whoever; bhaktyā — with devotion; dhyāyati — meditates; mānavaḥ — a person

BP 1.64.6

तन्नामजापी तत्कर्मरतस्तद्गतमानसः ।
निष्कामः पुरुषो दिण्डे स ब्रह्म परमाप्नुयात् ॥ ६ ॥

Repeating his name, intent on his deeds, and fixing his mind on him, the desireless person, O Diṇḍi, attains the highest Brahman.

tat-nāma-jāpī — repeating his name; tat-karma-rataḥ — engaged in his work; tat-gata-mānasaḥ — mind gone to him; niṣkāmaḥ — without desire; puruṣaḥ — a person; diṇḍe — O Diṇḍi; saḥ — he; brahma param — supreme Brahman; āpnuyāt — may attain

BP 1.64.7

यं च काममभिध्याय मार्तण्डार्पितमानसः ।
उपोषति तमाप्नोति प्रसन्ने खगमेऽखिलम् ॥ ७ ॥

Whatever desire he contemplates, keeping his mind offered to the Sun, he obtains it fully when the Sun is pleased.

yam ca kāmam — whatever desire; abhidhyāya — contemplating; mārtaṇḍa-arpita-mānasaḥ — with mind offered to Mārtaṇḍa; upoṣati — fasts; tam āpnoti — obtains that; prasanne — when pleased; khagame — the Sky-goer; akhilam — all

BP 1.64.8

दिण्डिरुवाच ।
ब्राह्मणैः क्षत्रियैर्वैश्यैः शूद्रैः स्त्रीभिश्च कञ्जज ।
संसारगर्ते पङ्कस्थे सुगतिः प्राप्यते कथम् ॥ ८ ॥

Diṇḍi said. O Lotus born, explain how Brahmins, Kṣatriyas, Vaiśyas, Śūdras, and women sunk in the muddy pit of the cycle of existence attain a good state.

diṇḍiḥ uvāca — Diṇḍi said; brāhmaṇaiḥ kṣatriyaiḥ vaiśyaiḥ śūdraiḥ strībhiḥ ca — by Brāhmaṇas, Kṣatriyas, Vaiśyas, Śūdras, and women; kañjaja — O Lotus-born; saṃsāra-garte — in the pit of saṃsāra; paṅka-sthe — standing in mud; su-gatiḥ — good destiny; prāpyate — is attained; katham — how

BP 1.64.9

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
अनाराध्य जगन्नाथं गोपतिं ध्वान्तनाशनम् ।
निर्व्यलीकेन चित्तेन कः प्रयास्यति सद्गतिम् ॥ ९ ॥

Brahmā said. Who can attain a good state with a sincere mind without worshiping the Lord of the universe, the Lord of cows, the destroyer of darkness.

brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said; anārādhya — without worshiping; jagannātham — Lord of the universe; go-patim — Lord of the cows; dhvānta-nāśanam — destroyer of darkness; nirvyalīkena cittena — with a guileless mind; kaḥ — who; prayāsyati — will proceed; sad-gatim — good destiny

BP 1.64.10

विषयग्राहि वै यस्य न चित्तं भास्करार्पितम् ।
स कथं पापपङ्काक्तो नरो यास्यति सद्गतिम् ॥ १० ॥

How can a person whose mind grasps sense objects, and is not fixed on the Sun, reach a good state when stained with the mud of sin.

viṣaya-grāhi — grasping sense-objects; vai — indeed; yasya — whose; na cittam — mind is not; bhāskara-arpitam — offered to Bhāskara; saḥ — he; katham — how; pāpa-paṅka-āktaḥ — smeared with the mud of sin; naraḥ — a person; yāsyati — will go; sad-gatim — good destiny

BP 1.64.11

यदि संसारदुःखार्तः सुगतिं गन्तुमिच्छसि ।
तदाराधय सर्वेशं भास्करं ज्योतिषां पतिम् ॥ ११ ॥

If you are afflicted by the suffering of the cycle of existence and wish to reach a good state, then worship the Lord of all, the Sun, the lord of the lights.

yadi — if; saṃsāra-duḥkha-ārtaḥ — afflicted by saṃsāra’s suffering; su-gatim — good destiny; gantum icchasi — you wish to go; tadā — then; ārādhaya — worship; sarva-īśam — Lord of all; bhāskaram — Bhāskara; jyotiṣām patim — Lord of the lights

BP 1.64.12

पुष्पैः सुगन्धैर्हृद्यैश्च धूपैः सागुरुचन्दनैः ।
वासोभिर्भूषणैर्भक्ष्यैरुपवासपरायणः ॥ १२ ॥

A person devoted to the fast should worship him with fragrant and pleasing flowers, incense of agarwood and sandalwood, garments, ornaments, and foods.

puṣpaiḥ — with flowers; su-gandhaiḥ hṛdyaiḥ — fragrant and pleasing; dhūpaiḥ — with incense; sa-agaru-candanaiḥ — with agaru and sandalwood; vāsobhiḥ — with garments; bhūṣaṇaiḥ — with ornaments; bhakṣyaiḥ — with food; upavāsa-parāyaṇaḥ — devoted to fasting

BP 1.64.13

यदि संसारनिर्वेदादभिवाञ्छसि सद्गतिम् ।
तदाराधय मार्तण्डं भक्तिप्रवणचेतसा ॥ १३ ॥

If, from weariness with worldly existence, you desire a good state, then worship the Sun with a mind inclined toward devotion.

yadi — if; saṃsāra-nirvedāt — through disillusionment with saṃsāra; abhivāñchasi — you long for; sad-gatim — good destiny; tadā — then; ārādhaya — worship; mārtaṇḍam — Mārtaṇḍa; bhakti-pravaṇa-cetasā — with a mind inclined to devotion

BP 1.64.14

पुष्पाणि यदि ते न स्नुस्तच्छस्तपादपपल्लवैः ।
दूर्वाङ्कुरैरपि दिण्डे तदभावेऽर्चयार्यमम् ॥ १४ ॥

If you have no flowers, use tender leaves of approved trees, and also shoots of Bermuda grass. If those too are unavailable, worship Aryaman, O Diṇḍi.

puṣpāṇi — flowers; yadi — if; te — for you; na santu — are not; tat — then; śasta-pādapa-pallavaiḥ — with tender leaves of approved trees; dūrvā-aṅkuraiḥ api — even with shoots of dūrvā; diṇḍe — O Diṇḍi; tat-abhāve — in their absence; arcaya — worship; aryamam — Aryaman

BP 1.64.15

पुष्पपत्राम्बुभिर्धूपैर्यथाविभवमात्मनः ।
पूजितस्तुष्टिमतुलां भक्त्या यात्येकचेतसा ॥ १५ ॥

Worshiped with flowers, leaves, water, and incense according to the means of the worshipper, he attains incomparable satisfaction through devotion and a mind fixed on one thing.

puṣpa-patra-ambubhiḥ — with flowers, leaves, and water; dhūpaiḥ — with incense; yathā-vibhavam ātmanaḥ — according to one’s own means; pūjitaḥ — worshiped; tuṣṭim atulām — incomparable satisfaction; bhaktyā — through devotion; yāti — receives; eka-cetasā — with one-pointed mind

BP 1.64.16

यः सदायतने भानोः कुरुते मार्जनक्रियाम् ।
स यात्युत्तमके स्थाने सर्वपापं व्यपोहति ॥ १६ ॥

Whoever performs the sweeping rite in the temple of the Sun each day goes to the highest place and removes every sin.

yaḥ — whoever; sadā — always; āyatane bhānoḥ — in Bhānu’s temple; kurute — performs; mārjana-kriyām — the act of sweeping; saḥ yāti — he goes; uttamake sthāne — to the highest place; sarva-pāpam — every sin; vyapohati — casts away

BP 1.64.17

यावत्यः पांसुकणिका मार्ज्यन्ते भास्करालये ।
दिनानि दिवि तावन्ति तिष्ठत्यर्कसमो नरः ॥ १७ ॥

For as many grains of dust as are swept from the temple of the Sun, for that many days the person remains in heaven, equal to the Sun.

yāvatyaḥ — as many; pāṃsu-kaṇikāḥ — grains of dust; mārjyante — are swept away; bhāskara-ālayaḥ — temple of Bhāskara; dināni — days; divi — in heaven; tāvanti — that many; tiṣṭhati — remains; arka-samaḥ — equal to the Sun; naraḥ — person

BP 1.64.18

अहन्यहनि यत्पापं कुरुते गणनायक ।
गोचर्ममात्रं सम्मार्ज्य हन्ति तद्भास्करालये ॥ १८ ॥

Whatever sin is committed day after day, O leader of the hosts, he destroys it by sweeping an area the size of a cowhide in the temple of the Sun.

ahani ahani — day after day; yat pāpam — whatever sin; kurute — commits; gaṇa-nāyaka — O lord of the hosts; go-carma-mātram — an area the size of a cowhide; sammārjya — having swept; hanti — destroys; tat — it; bhāskara-ālaya — in Bhāskara’s temple

BP 1.64.19

यश्चानुलेपनं कुर्याद्भानोरायतने नरः ।
सोऽपि लोकं समासाद्य हंसेन सह मोदते ॥ १९ ॥

The person who applies a coating in the temple of the Sun also reaches that world and rejoices with the swan.

yaḥ ca — whoever also; anulepanam kur yāt — performs anointing; bhānoḥ āyatane — in Bhānu’s temple; naraḥ — person; saḥ api — he too; lokam samāsādya — having reached the world; haṃsena saha — together with the haṃsa; modate — rejoices

BP 1.64.20

मृदा धातुविकारैर्वा वर्णकैर्गोमयेन वा ।
उपलेपनकृद्याती मत्पुरं यानमास्थितः ॥ २० ॥

Whoever plasters the temple with clay, mineral substances, pigments, or cow dung goes to my city mounted on a vehicle.

mṛdā — with clay; dhātu-vikāraiḥ — with mineral substances; vā — or; varṇakaiḥ — with pigments; gomayena — with cow dung; vā — or; upalepana-kṛt — one who plasters; yāti — goes; mat-puram — my city; yānam āsthitaḥ — mounted on a vehicle

BP 1.64.21

उदकाभ्युक्षणं भानोर्यः करोति सदा गृहे ।
सोऽपि गच्छति यत्रास्ते भगवान् यादसां पतिः ॥ २१ ॥

Whoever always sprinkles water in the temple of the Sun also goes where the Blessed Lord, lord of aquatic beings, resides.

udaka-abhyukṣaṇam — sprinkling with water; bhānoḥ — of Bhānu; yaḥ karoti — whoever performs; sadā — always; gṛhe — in the house; saḥ api — he too; gacchati — goes; yatra āste — where resides; bhagavān — the Blessed One; yādasām patiḥ — lord of aquatic beings

BP 1.64.22

पुष्पप्रकरमत्यर्थं सुगन्धं भास्करालये ।
अनुलिप्ते नरो दत्त्वा न दुर्गतिमवाप्नुयात् ॥ २२ ॥

A person who gives an abundant mass of fragrant flowers to the coated temple of the Sun does not reach a bad state.

puṣpa-prakaram — heap of flowers; atyartham — exceedingly abundant; su-gandham — fragrant; bhāskara-ālaya — in Bhāskara’s temple; anulipte — when anointed; naraḥ — person; dattvā — having given; na durgatim avāpnuyāt — does not attain a bad destiny

BP 1.64.23

विमानमतिशोभाढ्यं सर्वर्तुसुखभूषितम् ।
समाप्नोति नरो दत्त्वा दीपकं भास्करालये ॥ २३ ॥

A person who gives a lamp in the temple of the Sun obtains a celestial vehicle rich in beauty and adorned with the pleasures of every season.

vimānam — celestial vehicle; ati-śobha-āḍhyam — rich in great beauty; sarva-ṛtu-sukha-bhūṣitam — adorned with the pleasures of every season; samāpnoti — obtains; naraḥ — person; dattvā — having given; dīpakam — lamp; bhāskara-ālaya — in Bhāskara’s temple

BP 1.64.24

यस्तु संवत्सरं पूर्णं तिलपात्रप्रदो नरः ।
ध्वजं च भास्करे दद्यात्सममत्र फलं लभेत् ॥ २४ ॥

A person who gives a vessel of sesame for a full year and also gives a banner to the Sun obtains an equal reward here.

yaḥ tu — whoever; saṃvatsaram pūrṇam — a full year; tila-pātra-pradaḥ — giver of a sesame vessel; naraḥ — person; dhvajam ca — and a flag; bhāskare — to Bhāskara; dadyāt — gives; samam phalam — equal fruit; labhet — obtains; atra — here

BP 1.64.25

विधूतो हन्ति वातेन दातुरज्ञानतः कृतम् ।
पापं कर्तुर्गृहे भानोर्दिवारात्रौ नराधिप ॥ २५ ॥

A flag shaken by the wind removes the sin unknowingly committed by the giver and maker, day and night in the temple of the Sun, O king.

vidhūtaḥ — shaken; hanti — destroys; vātena — by the wind; dātuḥ — of the giver; ajñānataḥ kṛtam — done unknowingly; pāpam — sin; kartuḥ — of the maker; gṛhe bhānoḥ — in Bhānu’s house; divā-rātrau — day and night; nara-adhipa — O lord of men

BP 1.64.26

गीतवाद्यादिभिर्भानुं य उपास्ते तमोपहम् ।
गन्धर्वैर्नृत्यगीतैः स विमानस्थो निषेव्यते ॥ २६ ॥

The person who worships the Sun, destroyer of darkness, with songs, instruments, and the like is served by celestial musicians with dance and song while seated in a celestial vehicle.

gīta-vādya-ādibhiḥ — with songs, instruments, and the like; bhānum — Bhānu; tamaḥ-apaham — remover of darkness; gandharvaiḥ — by Gandharvas; nṛtya-gītaiḥ — with dance and song; saḥ — he; vimāna-sthaḥ — situated in a celestial vehicle; niṣevyate — is attended

BP 1.64.27

जातिस्मरत्वं मेधां च तथैवोपरमे स्मृतिम् ।
प्राप्नोति गणशार्दूल कृत्वा पुस्तकवाचनम् ॥ २७ ॥

By reading books, O tiger among the hosts, a person gains recollection of past births, intelligence, and memory at the end.

jāti-smaratvam — memory of past births; medhām — intelligence; ca — and; tathā eva — likewise; uparame — at the time of passing; smṛtim — memory; prāpnoti — obtains; gaṇa-śārdūla — O tiger among the hosts; kṛtvā — having done; pustaka-vācanam — book reading

BP 1.64.28

एवं खगेश्वरो भक्त्या येन भानुरुपासितः ।
स प्राप्नोति गतिं श्लाघ्यां यां यामिच्छति चेतसा ॥ २८ ॥

Whoever thus worships the Sun with devotion, O lord of birds, obtains the praiseworthy destination he desires in his mind.

evam — thus; khaga-īśvara — O lord of birds; bhaktyā — with devotion; yena — by whom; bhānuḥ upāsitaḥ — Bhānu is worshiped; saḥ — he; prāpnoti — obtains; gatim ślāghyām — praiseworthy destiny; yām yām — whatever; icchati — desires; cetasā — with the mind

BP 1.64.29

देवत्वं मनुजैः कैश्चिद्गन्धर्वत्वं तथा परैः ।
विद्याधरत्वमपरैः संराध्येह दिवाकरम् ॥ २९ ॥

Some people attain the state of gods, others the state of celestial musicians, and others the state of celestial knowledge bearers by worshiping the Sun here.

devatvam — godhood; manujaiḥ kaiścit — by some humans; gandharvatvam — Gandharva-hood; tathā paraiḥ — likewise by others; vidyādharatvam — Vidyādhara-hood; aparaiḥ — by others; saṃrādhy — having worshiped fully; iha — here; divākaram — Divākara

BP 1.64.30

शक्रः क्रतुशतेनेशमाराध्य ज्योतिषां पतिम् ।
देवेन्द्रत्वं गतस्तस्मान्नान्यः पूज्यतमः क्वचित् ॥ ३० ॥

Śakra worshiped the Lord of the lights with a hundred sacrifices and attained lordship of the gods. Therefore, nowhere is another more worthy of worship.

śakraḥ — Śakra; kratu-śatena — with a hundred sacrifices; īśam — the Lord; ārādhya — having worshiped; jyotiṣām patim — Lord of the lights; deva-indratvam gataḥ — attained lordship of the gods; tasmāt — therefore; na anyaḥ — no other; pūjya-tamaḥ — more worshipful; kvacit — anywhere

BP 1.64.31

ब्रह्मचारिगृहस्थानां वनस्थानां गणाधिप ।
नान्यः पूज्यस्तथा स्त्रीणामृते देवं दिवाकरम् ॥ ३१ ॥

For celibate students, householders, forest dwellers, and women, O lord of the hosts, no other deity is to be worshiped except the Sun.

brahmacāri-gṛhasthānām — of students and householders; vanasthānām — of forest-dwellers; gaṇa-adhipa — O lord of the hosts; na anyaḥ pūjyaḥ — no other is worshipful; tathā — likewise; strīṇām — of women; ṛte — except; devam divākaram — the god Divākara

BP 1.64.32

मध्ये परिव्राजकानां सहस्रांशुं महात्मनाम् ।
मोक्षद्वारं विशन्तीह तं रविं विजितात्मनाम् ॥ ३२ ॥

Among mendicants, the great souled ones who have conquered themselves enter here into that Sun, the doorway to liberation.

madhye — among; parivrājakānām — wandering renunciants; sahasra-aṃśum — the Thousand-rayed One; mahātmanām — of the great-souled; mokṣa-dvāram — door of liberation; viśanti — they enter; iha — here; tam ravim — that Ravi; vijita-ātmanām — of those who have conquered themselves

BP 1.64.33

BP 1.64.41

BP 1.64.42
