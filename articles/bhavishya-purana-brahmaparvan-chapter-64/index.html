Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 64 — The Fruitful Seventh Day Observance and Its Annual Rite

BP 1.64.1

दिण्डिरुवाच ।
उपवासैः सुरश्रेष्ठ कथं तुष्यति भास्करः ।
परिहारांस्तथाचक्ष्व ये त्याज्याश्चोपवासिभिः ॥ १ ॥

Diṇḍi said. O best of the gods, tell me how fasting pleases the Sun, and what those who fast should avoid.

BP 1.64.2

यद्यत्कार्यं यथा चैव भास्कराराधने नरैः ।
तत्सर्वं विस्तराद्ब्रह्मन् यथावद्वक्तुमर्हसि ॥ २ ॥

You should explain fully and correctly, O Brahman, everything people should do in worship of the Sun and how each thing should be done.

BP 1.64.3

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
स्मृतः सम्पूजितो धूपपुष्पान्नैर्भानुरादरात् ।
भोगिनामुपकाराय किं पुनश्चोपवासिनाम् ॥ ३ ॥

Brahmā said. The Sun, remembered and worshiped with reverence, incense, flowers, and food, benefits those who partake, and benefits those who fast even more.

BP 1.64.4

उपावृत्तस्य पापेभ्यो यस्तु वासो गुणैः सह ।
उपवासः स विज्ञेयः सर्वभोगविवर्जितः ॥ ४ ॥

Fasting is known as dwelling with virtue after turning away from sins, and as being free from every enjoyment.

BP 1.64.5

एकरात्रं द्विरात्रं वा त्रिरात्रं नक्तमेव च ।
उपवासी रविं यस्तु भक्त्या ध्यायति मानवः ॥ ५ ॥

A person fasts for one night, two nights, three nights, or at night alone, and meditates on the Sun with devotion.

BP 1.64.6

तन्नामजापी तत्कर्मरतस्तद्गतमानसः ।
निष्कामः पुरुषो दिण्डे स ब्रह्म परमाप्नुयात् ॥ ६ ॥

Repeating his name, intent on his deeds, and fixing his mind on him, the desireless person, O Diṇḍi, attains the highest Brahman.

BP 1.64.7

यं च काममभिध्याय मार्तण्डार्पितमानसः ।
उपोषति तमाप्नोति प्रसन्ने खगमेऽखिलम् ॥ ७ ॥

Whatever desire he contemplates, keeping his mind offered to the Sun, he obtains it fully when the Sun is pleased.

BP 1.64.8

दिण्डिरुवाच ।
ब्राह्मणैः क्षत्रियैर्वैश्यैः शूद्रैः स्त्रीभिश्च कञ्जज ।
संसारगर्ते पङ्कस्थे सुगतिः प्राप्यते कथम् ॥ ८ ॥

Diṇḍi said. O Lotus born, explain how Brahmins, Kṣatriyas, Vaiśyas, Śūdras, and women sunk in the muddy pit of the cycle of existence attain a good state.

BP 1.64.9

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
अनाराध्य जगन्नाथं गोपतिं ध्वान्तनाशनम् ।
निर्व्यलीकेन चित्तेन कः प्रयास्यति सद्गतिम् ॥ ९ ॥

Brahmā said. Who can attain a good state with a sincere mind without worshiping the Lord of the universe, the Lord of cows, the destroyer of darkness.

BP 1.64.10

विषयग्राहि वै यस्य न चित्तं भास्करार्पितम् ।
स कथं पापपङ्काक्तो नरो यास्यति सद्गतिम् ॥ १० ॥

How can a person whose mind grasps sense objects, and is not fixed on the Sun, reach a good state when stained with the mud of sin.

BP 1.64.11

यदि संसारदुःखार्तः सुगतिं गन्तुमिच्छसि ।
तदाराधय सर्वेशं भास्करं ज्योतिषां पतिम् ॥ ११ ॥

If you are afflicted by the suffering of the cycle of existence and wish to reach a good state, then worship the Lord of all, the Sun, the lord of the lights.

BP 1.64.12

पुष्पैः सुगन्धैर्हृद्यैश्च धूपैः सागुरुचन्दनैः ।
वासोभिर्भूषणैर्भक्ष्यैरुपवासपरायणः ॥ १२ ॥

A person devoted to the fast should worship him with fragrant and pleasing flowers, incense of agarwood and sandalwood, garments, ornaments, and foods.

BP 1.64.13

यदि संसारनिर्वेदादभिवाञ्छसि सद्गतिम् ।
तदाराधय मार्तण्डं भक्तिप्रवणचेतसा ॥ १३ ॥

If, from weariness with worldly existence, you desire a good state, then worship the Sun with a mind inclined toward devotion.

BP 1.64.14

पुष्पाणि यदि ते न स्नुस्तच्छस्तपादपपल्लवैः ।
दूर्वाङ्कुरैरपि दिण्डे तदभावेऽर्चयार्यमम् ॥ १४ ॥

If you have no flowers, use tender leaves of approved trees, and also shoots of Bermuda grass. If those too are unavailable, worship Aryaman, O Diṇḍi.

BP 1.64.15

पुष्पपत्राम्बुभिर्धूपैर्यथाविभवमात्मनः ।
पूजितस्तुष्टिमतुलां भक्त्या यात्येकचेतसा ॥ १५ ॥

Worshiped with flowers, leaves, water, and incense according to the means of the worshipper, he attains incomparable satisfaction through devotion and a mind fixed on one thing.

BP 1.64.16

यः सदायतने भानोः कुरुते मार्जनक्रियाम् ।
स यात्युत्तमके स्थाने सर्वपापं व्यपोहति ॥ १६ ॥

Whoever performs the sweeping rite in the temple of the Sun each day goes to the highest place and removes every sin.

BP 1.64.17

यावत्यः पांसुकणिका मार्ज्यन्ते भास्करालये ।
दिनानि दिवि तावन्ति तिष्ठत्यर्कसमो नरः ॥ १७ ॥

For as many grains of dust as are swept from the temple of the Sun, for that many days the person remains in heaven, equal to the Sun.

BP 1.64.18

अहन्यहनि यत्पापं कुरुते गणनायक ।
गोचर्ममात्रं सम्मार्ज्य हन्ति तद्भास्करालये ॥ १८ ॥

Whatever sin is committed day after day, O leader of the hosts, he destroys it by sweeping an area the size of a cowhide in the temple of the Sun.

BP 1.64.19

यश्चानुलेपनं कुर्याद्भानोरायतने नरः ।
सोऽपि लोकं समासाद्य हंसेन सह मोदते ॥ १९ ॥

The person who applies a coating in the temple of the Sun also reaches that world and rejoices with the swan.

BP 1.64.20

मृदा धातुविकारैर्वा वर्णकैर्गोमयेन वा ।
उपलेपनकृद्याती मत्पुरं यानमास्थितः ॥ २० ॥

Whoever plasters the temple with clay, mineral substances, pigments, or cow dung goes to my city mounted on a vehicle.

BP 1.64.21

उदकाभ्युक्षणं भानोर्यः करोति सदा गृहे ।
सोऽपि गच्छति यत्रास्ते भगवान् यादसां पतिः ॥ २१ ॥

Whoever always sprinkles water in the temple of the Sun also goes where the Blessed Lord, lord of aquatic beings, resides.

BP 1.64.22

पुष्पप्रकरमत्यर्थं सुगन्धं भास्करालये ।
अनुलिप्ते नरो दत्त्वा न दुर्गतिमवाप्नुयात् ॥ २२ ॥

A person who gives an abundant mass of fragrant flowers to the coated temple of the Sun does not reach a bad state.

BP 1.64.23

विमानमतिशोभाढ्यं सर्वर्तुसुखभूषितम् ।
समाप्नोति नरो दत्त्वा दीपकं भास्करालये ॥ २३ ॥

A person who gives a lamp in the temple of the Sun obtains a celestial vehicle rich in beauty and adorned with the pleasures of every season.

BP 1.64.24

यस्तु संवत्सरं पूर्णं तिलपात्रप्रदो नरः ।
ध्वजं च भास्करे दद्यात्सममत्र फलं लभेत् ॥ २४ ॥

A person who gives a vessel of sesame for a full year and also gives a banner to the Sun obtains an equal reward here.

BP 1.64.25

विधूतो हन्ति वातेन दातुरज्ञानतः कृतम् ।
पापं कर्तुर्गृहे भानोर्दिवारात्रौ नराधिप ॥ २५ ॥

A flag shaken by the wind removes the sin unknowingly committed by the giver and maker, day and night in the temple of the Sun, O king.

BP 1.64.26

गीतवाद्यादिभिर्भानुं य उपास्ते तमोपहम् ।
गन्धर्वैर्नृत्यगीतैः स विमानस्थो निषेव्यते ॥ २६ ॥

The person who worships the Sun, destroyer of darkness, with songs, instruments, and the like is served by celestial musicians with dance and song while seated in a celestial vehicle.

BP 1.64.27

जातिस्मरत्वं मेधां च तथैवोपरमे स्मृतिम् ।
प्राप्नोति गणशार्दूल कृत्वा पुस्तकवाचनम् ॥ २७ ॥

By reading books, O tiger among the hosts, a person gains recollection of past births, intelligence, and memory at the end.

BP 1.64.28

एवं खगेश्वरो भक्त्या येन भानुरुपासितः ।
स प्राप्नोति गतिं श्लाघ्यां यां यामिच्छति चेतसा ॥ २८ ॥

Whoever thus worships the Sun with devotion, O lord of birds, obtains the praiseworthy destination he desires in his mind.

BP 1.64.29

देवत्वं मनुजैः कैश्चिद्गन्धर्वत्वं तथा परैः ।
विद्याधरत्वमपरैः संराध्येह दिवाकरम् ॥ २९ ॥

Some people attain the state of gods, others the state of celestial musicians, and others the state of celestial knowledge bearers by worshiping the Sun here.

BP 1.64.30

शक्रः क्रतुशतेनेशमाराध्य ज्योतिषां पतिम् ।
देवेन्द्रत्वं गतस्तस्मान्नान्यः पूज्यतमः क्वचित् ॥ ३० ॥

Śakra worshiped the Lord of the lights with a hundred sacrifices and attained lordship of the gods. Therefore, nowhere is another more worthy of worship.

BP 1.64.31

ब्रह्मचारिगृहस्थानां वनस्थानां गणाधिप ।
नान्यः पूज्यस्तथा स्त्रीणामृते देवं दिवाकरम् ॥ ३१ ॥

For celibate students, householders, forest dwellers, and women, O lord of the hosts, no other deity is to be worshiped except the Sun.

BP 1.64.32

मध्ये परिव्राजकानां सहस्रांशुं महात्मनाम् ।
मोक्षद्वारं विशन्तीह तं रविं विजितात्मनाम् ॥ ३२ ॥

Among mendicants, the great souled ones who have conquered themselves enter here into that Sun, the doorway to liberation.

BP 1.64.33

एवं सर्वाश्रमाणां हि सहस्रांशुः परायणम् ।
सर्वेषां चैव वर्णानां ग्रहेशो वै गतिः परा ॥ ३३ ॥

Thus, the thousand rayed one is the refuge of all stages of life, and the lord of the planets is the highest goal for all varṇas.

BP 1.64.34

शृणुष्व गदतो काम्यानुपवासांस्तथापरान् ।
शृणु दिण्डे महापुण्यफलिकां सप्तमीं पराम् ॥ ३४ ॥

Listen as I speak of other desire granting fasts. Hear, O Diṇḍi, about the supreme seventh day observance that yields great merit.

BP 1.64.35

आदित्याराधनायैनां सर्वपापहरां शिवाम् ।
यामुपोष्य नरो भक्त्या मुच्यते सर्वपातकैः ॥ ३५ ॥

This auspicious observance is for worship of the Sun and removes all sins. A person who fasts on it with devotion is freed from every transgression.

BP 1.64.36

तथा लोकमवाप्नोति सूर्यस्यामिततेजसः ।
अथ भाद्रपदे मासि शुक्लपक्षे समागते ॥ ३६ ॥

He thus attains the world of the Sun, whose splendor is immeasurable. Next comes the bright fortnight of Bhādrapada.

BP 1.64.37

सोपोष्या प्रथमं तात विधानं शृणु तत्र वै ।
अयाचितं चतुर्थ्यां तु पञ्चम्यामेकभोजनम् ॥ ३७ ॥

First he should fast on it, dear one. Hear the procedure. On the fourth day he should eat food not asked for, and on the fifth he should eat one meal.

BP 1.64.38

उपवासपरः षष्ठ्यां जितक्रोधो जितेन्द्रियः ।
अर्चयित्वा दिनकरं गन्धधूपनिवेदनैः ॥ ३८ ॥

On the sixth day, devoted to fasting and having conquered anger and the senses, he should worship the Sun with fragrances, incense, and offerings.

BP 1.64.39

पुरतः स्थण्डिले रात्रौ स्वप्याद्देवस्य पुत्रक ।
प्रध्यायन्मनसा देवं सर्वभूतार्तिनाशनम् ॥ ३९ ॥

At night he should sleep on the ritual ground before the god, O dear son, meditating in his mind on the god who destroys the distress of all beings.

BP 1.64.40

सर्वदोषप्रशमनं सर्वपातकनाशनम् ।
विबुद्धस्त्वथ सप्तम्यां कुर्याद्ब्राह्मणभोजनम् ॥ ४० ॥

It pacifies every fault and destroys every sin. Having awakened on the seventh day, he should feed Brahmins.

BP 1.64.41

पूजयित्वा दिनकरं पुष्पधूपविलेपनैः ।
नैवेद्यं तात देवस्य फलानि कथयन्ति हि ॥ ४१ ॥

Having worshiped the Sun with flowers, incense, and unguents, he should offer fruits as food for the god, dear one, so the wise declare.

BP 1.64.42

खर्जूरनालिकेराणि तथाम्रफलानि तु ।
मातुलिङ्गफलान्येव कथितानि मनीषिभिः ॥ ४२ ॥

The wise declare dates, coconuts, mangoes, and citrons.

BP 1.64.43

एतैश्च भोजयेद्विप्रानात्मना च प्रभक्षयेत् ।
तथैषामप्यभावेन शृणु चान्यानि सुव्रत ॥ ४३ ॥

With these he should feed Brahmins and eat them himself. If they are unavailable, hear of other things, O good vowed one.

BP 1.64.44

शालिगोधूमपिष्टानि कारयेद्गणनायक ।
गुडगर्भकृतानीह घृतपाकेन पावयेत् ॥ ४४ ॥

O leader of the hosts, he should make flour cakes from fine rice and wheat, filled with jaggery, and cook them in ghee.

BP 1.64.45

चातुर्यावकमिश्राणि आदित्याय निवेदयेत् ।
अग्निकार्यमथो कृत्वा ब्राह्मणान् भोजयेत्ततः ॥ ४५ ॥

He should offer them mixed with the fourfold barley preparation to the Sun. Then, after performing the fire rite, he should feed Brahmins.

BP 1.64.46

इत्थं द्वादश वै मासान्कार्यं व्रतमनुत्तमम् ।
मासि मासि फलाहारः फलदायी फलार्चनः ॥ ४६ ॥

Thus the excellent vow is to be performed for twelve months. Each month the food and offerings are fruits, and it bestows fruit.

BP 1.64.47

वर्षान्ते त्वथ कुर्वीत शक्त्या ब्राह्मणभोजनम् ।
स्नानप्राशनयोश्चापि विधानं शृणु सुव्रत ॥ ४७ ॥

At the end of the year he should feed Brahmins according to his ability. Hear, O good vowed one, the procedure for bathing and drinking.

BP 1.64.48

गोमूत्रं गोमयं क्षीरं दधि सर्पिः कुशोदकम् ।
तिलसर्षपयोः कल्कं श्वेता मृच्चापि सुव्रत ॥ ४८ ॥

For bathing and drinking, use cow urine, cow dung, milk, curds, ghee, water infused with ritual grass, a paste of sesame and mustard, and white clay, O good vowed one.

BP 1.64.49

दूर्वाकल्कं घृतं चापि गोशृङ्गक्षालितं जलम् ।
जातिगुल्मविनिर्यासः प्रशस्तः स्नानकर्मणि ॥ ४९ ॥

A paste of Bermuda grass, ghee, water rinsed through the horn of a cow, and the extract of jasmine shrubs are praised for bathing.

BP 1.64.50

प्राशने चाप्यथैतानि सर्वपापहराणि वै ।
आदौ कृत्वा भाद्रपदं यथासङ्ख्यं विदुर्बुधाः ॥ ५० ॥

These are also sin removing when drunk. The wise know the order, beginning with Bhādrapada.

BP 1.64.51

इत्थं वर्षान्तमासाद्य भोजयित्वा द्विजोत्तमान् ।
दिव्यान् भोगान् महादेव ततस्तेभ्यो निवेदयेत् ॥ ५१ ॥

At the end of the year, after feeding the excellent twice born in this way, he should then offer them heavenly enjoyments, O great god.

BP 1.64.52

फलानि तात हैमानि यथाशक्त्या गणाधिप ।
सवत्सामथ वा धेनुं भूमिं सस्यान्वितामथ ॥ ५२ ॥

Offer golden fruits according to your ability, O leader of the hosts, or a cow with her calf, or land furnished with crops.

BP 1.64.53

प्रासादमथ वा भौमं सर्वधान्यसमन्वितम् ।
दद्यात् शुक्लानि वस्त्राणि ताम्रपात्रं सविद्रुमम् ॥ ५३ ॥

Or he should give an earthly temple supplied with every kind of grain, white garments, and a copper vessel together with coral.

BP 1.64.54

शक्तियुक्तस्य चैतानि दरिद्रस्य तु मे शृणु ।
फलानि पिष्टकान्येषां तिलचूर्णान्वितानि तु ॥ ५४ ॥

These are for a person of means. Hear from me what a poor person may give, fruits and cakes mixed with sesame flour.

BP 1.64.55

भोजयित्वा द्विजान् दद्यार् राजतानि फलानि तु ।
धातुरक्तं वस्त्रयुग्ममाचार्याय निवेदयेत् ॥ ५५ ॥

Having fed the twice born, he should give silver fruits and offer a pair of garments colored with mineral dye to the teacher.

BP 1.64.56

सहिरण्यं महादेव पञ्चरत्नसमन्वितम् ।
इत्थं समाप्यते तात पारणं वार्षिकान्तिकम् ॥ ५६ ॥

Together with gold and five gems, O great god, the annual rite of breaking the fast is completed in this way, dear one.

BP 1.64.57

इत्येषा वै पुण्यतमा सप्तमी दुरितापहा ।
यामुपोष्य नराः सर्वे यान्ति सूर्यसलोकताम् ॥ ५७ ॥

This is the most meritorious seventh day observance, remover of evil. By fasting on it, all people go to the world of the Sun.

BP 1.64.58

पूज्यमानः सदा देवैर्गन्धर्वाप्सरसां गणैः ।
अनया मानवो नित्यं पूजयेद्भास्करं सदा ॥ ५८ ॥

The Sun, always honored by the gods and hosts of celestial musicians and nymphs, should always be worshiped by a person through this observance.

BP 1.64.59

दारिद्र्यदुःखदुरितैर्मुक्तो याति दिवाकरम् ।
ब्राह्मणो मोक्षमायाति क्षत्रियश्चेन्द्रतां व्रजेत् ॥ ५९ ॥

Freed from poverty, suffering, and evil, a person goes to the Sun. A Brahmin attains liberation, and a Kṣatriya reaches the state of Indra.

BP 1.64.60

वैश्यो धनदसालोक्यं शूद्रो विप्रत्वमाप्नुयात् ।
अपुत्रो लभते पुत्रं दुर्भगा सुभगा भवेत् ॥ ६० ॥

A Vaiśya attains the world of the giver of wealth, and a Śūdra attains Brahmin status. A man without a son obtains a son, and an unfortunate woman becomes fortunate.

BP 1.64.61

यामुपोष्य च नारीमां सप्तमीं लोकपूजिताम् ।
विधवा वा सती भक्त्या अनया पूजयेद्रविम् ॥ ६१ ॥

A woman who fasts on this seventh day observance, honored by the world, whether widowed or chaste, should worship the Sun with devotion through it.

BP 1.64.62

नान्यजन्मनि वैधव्यं नारी प्राप्नोति मानद ।
चिन्तामणिसमा ह्येषा विज्ञेया फलसप्तमी ॥ ६२ ॥

A woman does not attain widowhood in another birth, O giver of honor. This fruitful seventh day observance is to be known as equal to the wish fulfilling jewel.

BP 1.64.63

पठतां शृण्वतां दिण्डे सर्वकामप्रदा स्मृता ॥ ६३ ॥

It is remembered as granting every desire to those who read and those who hear, O Diṇḍi.
