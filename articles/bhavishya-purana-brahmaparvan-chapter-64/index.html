Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 64 — Phala-saptamī and Its Annual Observance

BP 1.64.1

दिण्डिरुवाच ।
उपवासैः सुरश्रेष्ठ कथं तुष्यति भास्करः ।
परिहारांस्तथाचक्ष्व ये त्याज्याश्चोपवासिभिः ॥ ॥

Diṇḍi said: O best of the gods, how is Bhāskara pleased by fasts? Explain also the things that those who fast must avoid.

Word-for-word
diṇḍiḥ uvāca — Diṇḍi said; upavāsaiḥ — by fasts; sura-śreṣṭha — O best of the gods; katham — how; tuṣyati — is pleased; bhāskaraḥ — Bhāskara; parihārān — avoidances; tathā ācakṣva — explain likewise; ye — which; tyājyāḥ — should be abandoned; upavāsibhiḥ — by those who fast
Transliteration
diṇḍir uvāca |
upavāsaiḥ suraśreṣṭha kathaṃ tuṣyati bhāskaraḥ |
parihārāṃs tathācakṣva ye tyājyāś copavāsibhiḥ || 1 ||

BP 1.64.2

यद्यत्कार्यं यथा चैव भास्कराराधने नरैः ।
तत्सर्वं विस्तराद्ब्रह्मन् यथावद्वक्तुमर्हसि ॥ ॥

Whatever must be done, and in whatever manner, by people in the worship of Bhāskara—you should explain all of it fully and properly, O Brahman.

Word-for-word
yat yat — whatever; kāryam — must be done; yathā ca eva — and in whatever way; bhāskara-ārādhane — in Bhāskara’s worship; naraiḥ — by people; tat sarvam — all that; vistarāt — in detail; brahman — O Brahman; yathāvat — properly; vaktum arhasi — you ought to speak
Transliteration
yad yat kāryaṃ yathā caiva bhāskarārādhane naraiḥ |
tat sarvaṃ vistarād brahman yathāvad vaktum arhasi || 2 ||

BP 1.64.3

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
स्मृतः सम्पूजितो धूपपुष्पान्नैर्भानुरादरात् ।
भोगिनामुपकाराय किं पुनश्चोपवासिनाम् ॥ ॥

Brahmā said: Bhānu, remembered and worshiped respectfully with incense, flowers, and food, benefits those who enjoy such things; how much more does he benefit those who fast?

Word-for-word
brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said; smṛtaḥ — remembered; sampūjitaḥ — fully worshiped; dhūpa-puṣpa-annaiḥ — with incense, flowers, and food; bhānuḥ — Bhānu; ādarāt — respectfully; bhoginām upakārāya — for the benefit of those who enjoy; kim punaḥ — how much more; upavāsinām — of those who fast
Transliteration
brahmovāca |
smṛtaḥ sampūjito dhūpapuṣpānair bhānur ādarāt |
bhoginām upakārāya kiṃ punaś copavāsinām || 3 ||

BP 1.64.4

उपावृत्तस्य पापेभ्यो यस्तु वासो गुणैः सह ।
उपवासः स विज्ञेयः सर्वभोगविवर्जितः ॥ ॥

That dwelling together with virtues by which one turns away from sins is to be understood as fasting; it is free from every enjoyment.

Word-for-word
upāvṛttasya — of one who has turned away; pāpebhyaḥ — from sins; yaḥ tu vāsaḥ — that dwelling; guṇaiḥ saha — together with virtues; upavāsaḥ — fasting; saḥ vijñeyaḥ — it should be understood; sarva-bhoga-vivarjitaḥ — free from every enjoyment
Transliteration
upāvṛttasya pāpebhyo yas tu vāso guṇaiḥ saha |
upavāsaḥ sa vijñeyaḥ sarvabhogavivarjitaḥ || 4 ||

BP 1.64.5

एकरात्रं द्विरात्रं वा त्रिरात्रं नक्तमेव च ।
उपवासी रविं यस्तु भक्त्या ध्यायति मानवः ॥ ॥

Whether for one night, two nights, three nights, or only by night, the person who fasts and meditates upon Ravi with devotion—

Word-for-word

Transliteration

BP 1.64.6

तन्नामजापी तत्कर्मरतस्तद्गतमानसः ।
निष्कामः पुरुषो दिण्डे स ब्रह्म परमाप्नुयात् ॥ ॥

He who repeats his name, delights in his work, has his mind fixed upon him, and is without desire, O Diṇḍi, attains the supreme Brahman.

Word-for-word
tat-nāma-jāpī — repeating his name; tat-karma-rataḥ — engaged in his work; tat-gata-mānasaḥ — mind gone to him; niṣkāmaḥ — without desire; puruṣaḥ — a person; diṇḍe — O Diṇḍi; saḥ — he; brahma param — supreme Brahman; āpnuyāt — may attain
Transliteration

BP 1.64.7

यं च काममभिध्याय मार्तण्डार्पितमानसः ।
उपोषति तमाप्नोति प्रसन्ने खगमेऽखिलम् ॥ ॥

With his mind offered to Mārtaṇḍa, whatever desire a person fasts while contemplating, he obtains it all when the Sky-goer is pleased.

Word-for-word

Transliteration
yaṃ ca kāmam abhidhyāya mārtaṇḍārpitamānasaḥ |
upoṣati tam āpnoti prasanne khagame ’khilam || 7 ||

BP 1.64.8

दिण्डिरुवाच ।
ब्राह्मणैः क्षत्रियैर्वैश्यैः शूद्रैः स्त्रीभिश्च कञ्जज ।
संसारगर्ते पङ्कस्थे सुगतिः प्राप्यते कथम् ॥ ॥

Diṇḍi said: O Lotus-born, how is a good destiny attained by Brāhmaṇas, Kṣatriyas, Vaiśyas, Śūdras, and women who are stuck in the mud at the bottom of the pit of saṃsāra?

Word-for-word
diṇḍiḥ uvāca — Diṇḍi said; brāhmaṇaiḥ kṣatriyaiḥ vaiśyaiḥ śūdraiḥ strībhiḥ ca — by Brāhmaṇas, Kṣatriyas, Vaiśyas, Śūdras, and women; kañjaja — O Lotus-born; saṃsāra-garte — in the pit of saṃsāra; paṅka-sthe — standing in mud; su-gatiḥ — good destiny; prāpyate — is attained; katham — how
Transliteration
diṇḍir uvāca |
brāhmaṇaiḥ kṣatriyair vaiśyaiḥ śūdraiḥ strībhiś ca kañjaja |
saṃsāragarte paṅkasthe sugatiḥ prāpyate katham || 8 ||

BP 1.64.9

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
अनाराध्य जगन्नाथं गोपतिं ध्वान्तनाशनम् ।
निर्व्यलीकेन चित्तेन कः प्रयास्यति सद्गतिम् ॥ ॥

Brahmā said: Without worshiping the Lord of the universe, the Lord of the cows, the destroyer of darkness, who could proceed to a good destiny with a guileless mind?

Word-for-word
brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said; anārādhya — without worshiping; jagannātham — Lord of the universe; go-patim — Lord of the cows; dhvānta-nāśanam — destroyer of darkness; nirvyalīkena cittena — with a guileless mind; kaḥ — who; prayāsyati — will proceed; sad-gatim — good destiny
Transliteration
brahmovāca |
anārādhya jagannāthaṃ gopatiṃ dhvāntanāśanam |
nirvyalīkena cittena kaḥ prayāsyati sadgatim || 9 ||

BP 1.64.10

विषयग्राहि वै यस्य न चित्तं भास्करार्पितम् ।
स कथं पापपङ्काक्तो नरो यास्यति सद्गतिम् ॥ ॥

How will a person whose mind grasps sense-objects and is not offered to Bhāskara, smeared with the mud of sin, reach a good destiny?

Word-for-word
viṣaya-grāhi — grasping sense-objects; vai — indeed; yasya — whose; na cittam — mind is not; bhāskara-arpitam — offered to Bhāskara; saḥ — he; katham — how; pāpa-paṅka-āktaḥ — smeared with the mud of sin; naraḥ — a person; yāsyati — will go; sad-gatim — good destiny
Transliteration
viṣayagrāhi vai yasya na cittaṃ bhāskarārpitam |
sa kathaṃ pāpapaṅkākto naro yāsyati sadgatim || 10 ||

BP 1.64.11

यदि संसारदुःखार्तः सुगतिं गन्तुमिच्छसि ।
तदाराधय सर्वेशं भास्करं ज्योतिषां पतिम् ॥ ॥

Word-for-word
yadi — if; saṃsāra-duḥkha-ārtaḥ — afflicted by saṃsāra’s suffering; su-gatim — good destiny; gantum icchasi — you wish to go; tadā — then; ārādhaya — worship; sarva-īśam — Lord of all; bhāskaram — Bhāskara; jyotiṣām patim — Lord of the lights
Transliteration
yadi saṃsāraduḥkhārtaḥ sugatiṃ gantum icchasi |
tad ārādhaya sarveśaṃ bhāskaraṃ jyotiṣāṃ patim || 11 ||

BP 1.64.12

पुष्पैः सुगन्धैर्हृद्यैश्च धूपैः सागुरुचन्दनैः ।
वासोभिर्भूषणैर्भक्ष्यैरुपवासपरायणः ॥ ॥

BP 1.64.13

यदि संसारनिर्वेदादभिवाञ्छसि सद्गतिम् ।

BP 1.64.14

पुष्पाणि यदि ते न स्नुस्तच्छस्तपादपपल्लवैः ।

BP 1.64.15

पुष्पपत्राम्बुभिर्धूपैर्यथाविभवमात्मनः ।

BP 1.64.17
