Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 93 — The Greatness of Bhānu

BP 1.93.1

ब्रह्मोवाच । येषां धर्मक्रियाः सर्वाः सदैवोद्दिश्य भास्करम् । न कुले जायते तेषां दरिद्रो व्याधितोऽपि वा ॥ ॥

Brahmā said. No poor person or even sick person is born in the family of those whose every religious act is always directed to Bhāskara.

BP 1.93.2

देवायतनभूमेस्तु गोमयेनोपलेपनम् । यः करोति नरो भक्त्या सद्यः पापात्प्रमुच्यते ॥ ॥

Whoever devoutly plasters the temple ground with cow dung is immediately freed from sin.

BP 1.93.3

श्वेतया रक्तया वापि पीतमृत्तिकयापि वा । उपलेपनकर्ता वै चिन्तितं लभते फलम् ॥ ॥

The one who plasters it with white, red, or yellow earth obtains the desired result.

BP 1.93.4

चित्रभानुं विरञ्च्यैव कुसुमैर्यः सुगन्धिभिः । पूजयेत्सोपवासस्तु स कामानीप्सितांल्लभेत् ॥ ॥

Whoever worships Citrabhānu and Virañci with fragrant flowers and fasts obtains the desired wishes.

BP 1.93.5

घृतेन दीपकं ज्वाल्य तिलतैलेन वा रवेः । प्रयाति सूर्यलोकं स दीपकोटिशतैर्वृतः ॥ ॥

Lighting a lamp for the Sun with ghee or sesame oil, one goes to the world of the Sun, surrounded by a hundred crores of lamps.

BP 1.93.6

दीपतैलप्रदानेन न याति नरकं नरः । दीपतैलं तिलाश्चैव महापातकनाशनाः ॥ ॥

A man does not go to hell by giving lamp oil. Lamp oil and sesame seeds destroy the great sins.

BP 1.93.7

दीपं ददाति यो नित्यं भास्करायतनेषु वै । चतुष्पथेषु तीर्थेषु रूपौजस्वी ह जायते ॥ ॥

Whoever always gives lamps at the shrines of the Sun, at crossroads, and at sacred places is born beautiful and radiant.

BP 1.93.8

यस्तु कारयते दीपं रवेर्भक्तिसमन्वितः । स कामानीप्सितान्प्राप्य वृन्दारकपुरं व्रजेत् ॥ ॥

Whoever has a lamp lit for the Sun with devotion obtains the desired wishes and goes to the city of the gods.

BP 1.93.9

यः समालभते सूर्यं चन्दनागुरुकुङ्कुमैः । कर्पूरेण विमिश्रैश्च तथा कस्तूरिकान्वितैः ॥ ॥

One who anoints the Sun with sandalwood, aloe wood, saffron mixed with camphor, and musk.

BP 1.93.10

शुभं कालं कोटिशतं विहृत्य च भवालये । पुनः सञ्जायते भूमौ राजराजो न संशयः । सर्वकामसमृद्धात्मा सर्वलोकनमस्कृतः ॥ ॥

Having spent a hundred crores of auspicious years in the abode of Bhava, he is surely born again on earth as a king of kings, with every desire fulfilled and honored by all worlds.

BP 1.93.11

चन्दनोदकमिश्रैश्च दत्त्वार्घ्यं कुसुमै रवेः । सपुत्रपौत्रपत्नीकः स्वर्गलोके महीयते ॥ ॥

Offering the Sun a water offering with sandalwood water and flowers, he is honored in heaven together with his sons, grandsons, and wife.

BP 1.93.12

सुगन्धोदकमिश्रैस्तु दत्त्वार्घ्यं कुसुमै रवेः । देवलोके चिरं स्थित्वा राजा भवति भूतले ॥ ॥

Offering the Sun a water offering with fragrant water and flowers, he remains long in the world of the gods and becomes a king on earth.

BP 1.93.13

स हिरण्येन चार्घ्येण रक्तोदकयुतेन वा । कोटीशतं तु वर्षाणां स्वर्गलोके महीयते ॥ ॥

With a golden water offering, or one mixed with red water, he is honored in heaven for a hundred crores of years.

BP 1.93.14

पद्मैरभ्यर्चनं कृत्वा रवेः स्वर्गगतो नरः । पद्मे वसति वर्षाणां स्त्रीपद्मशतसंवृतः ॥ ॥

Having worshipped Ravi with lotuses, the man who goes to heaven dwells in a lotus for years, surrounded by a hundred women like lotuses.

BP 1.93.15

गुग्गुलं सघृतं दत्त्वा रवेर्भक्तिसमन्वितः । तत्क्षणात्सर्वपापेभ्यो मुच्यते नात्र संशयः ॥ ॥

Whoever devotedly offers fragrant resin with ghee to Ravi is immediately freed from all sins, without doubt.

BP 1.93.16

पक्षं तु गुग्गुलं दत्त्वा मुच्यते ब्रह्महत्यया । संवत्सरेण लभते अश्वमेधफलं शिव ॥ ॥

By offering fragrant resin for a fortnight, one is freed from the sin of killing a Brahmin. In one year one obtains the fruit of the horse sacrifice, O Śiva.

BP 1.93.17

धूपेन लभते स्वर्गं तुरुष्केण सुगन्धिना । कर्पूरागुरुधूपेन राजसूयफलं लभेत् ॥ ॥

With fragrant resin incense one obtains heaven. With camphor and aloe wood incense one obtains the fruit of the royal-consecration sacrifice.

BP 1.93.18

पूर्वाह्णे मानवो भक्त्या श्रद्धया योऽर्चयेद्रविम् । स तत्फलमवाप्नोति यद्दत्ते कपिलाशते ॥ ॥

Whoever worships Ravi in the forenoon with devotion and faith obtains the fruit gained by giving a hundred tawny cows.

BP 1.93.19

मध्याह्ने योऽर्चयेत्सूर्यं प्रयतात्मा जितेन्द्रियः । लभते भूमिदानस्य गोशतस्य च तत्फलम् ॥ ॥

Whoever worships the Sun at midday, pure and self controlled, obtains the fruit of giving land and a hundred cows.

BP 1.93.20

पश्चिमायां तु सन्ध्यायां योऽर्चयेद्भास्करं नरः । शुचिः शुक्लाम्बरोष्णीषो गोसहस्रफलं लभेत् ॥ ॥

Whoever worships Bhāskara at the western twilight, pure and wearing white clothes and a turban, obtains the fruit of a thousand cows.

BP 1.93.21

अर्धरात्रे तु यो हेलिं भक्त्या सम्पूजयेन्नरः । जातिस्मरत्वमाप्नोति कुले जातो वृषान्वितः ॥ ॥

Whoever worships the Sun with devotion at midnight gains remembrance of former births and is born in a family with bulls.

BP 1.93.22

प्रदोषरात्रिवेलायां यः पूजयति भास्करम् । स गत्वा सहसा वीर क्रीडेत्सौमनसं क्षयम् ॥ ॥

Whoever worships Bhāskara at the evening time, O hero, quickly goes and plays in the Joyful Abode.

BP 1.93.23

दण्डनायकवेलायां प्रभातसमये पुनः । पूजयित्वा रविं भक्त्या व्रजेदनिमिषालयम् ॥ ॥

Again, at dawn, having worshipped Ravi with devotion during the early morning watch, he goes to the abode of the unblinking gods.

BP 1.93.24

एवं वेलासु सर्वासु अवेलासु च मानवः । भक्त्या पूजयते योऽर्कमर्कपुष्पैः समाहितः । तेजसादित्यसंकाशो ह्यर्कलोके महीयते ॥ ॥

At all times and also at untimely hours, whoever worships the Sun with devotion and a focused mind, offering milkweed flowers, shines like the Sun and is honored in the world of the Sun.

BP 1.93.25

अयने तूत्तरे सूर्यमथ वा दक्षिणायने । पूजयेद्यस्तु वै भक्त्या स गच्छेत्कञ्जजालयम् ॥ ॥

At the northern or southern solstice, whoever worships the Sun with devotion goes to the abode of the Lotus born.

BP 1.93.26

तत्रस्थः पूज्यते केशैः सर्वेः सुमनसस्तथा । गोपतिः पूज्यते यद्वद्गोपतिप्रमुखैः सुरैः ॥ ॥

There he is honored with all flowers and blossoms, just as the Lord of Cows is honored by the gods led by the Lord of Cows.

BP 1.93.27

विषुवेषूपरागेषु षडशीतिमुखेषु च । पूजयित्वा रविं भक्त्या नात्मानं शोचते नरः ॥ ॥

Whoever worships Ravi with devotion at the equinoxes, eclipses, and the eighty six junctures does not grieve for himself.

BP 1.93.28

विबुध्य वा स्वयं वापि यो नमस्कुरुते रविम् । सन्तुष्टो भास्करस्तस्मै गतिमिष्टां प्रयच्छति ॥ ॥

Whoever bows to Ravi on waking or by his own choice is given the desired destination by the pleased Sun.

BP 1.93.29

कृशरापायसापूपपलल्ōन्मिश्रमोदकैः । बलिं कृत्वा तु सूर्याय सर्वकाममवाप्नुयात् ॥ ॥

Offering rice and lentils, milk porridge, cakes, meat, and sweet dumplings together to the Sun, one obtains every desire.

BP 1.93.30

मोदकानां प्रदानेन पायसस्य च सुव्रत । मधुमांसरसैश्चापि प्रीयतेऽतीव भास्करः ॥ ॥

By giving sweet dumplings and milk porridge, and also honey and meat juices, the Sun is greatly pleased, O well disciplined one.

BP 1.93.31

घृतेन तर्पनं कृत्वा सदा स्निग्धो भवेन्नरः । तर्पयित्वा तु मांसेन सद्यः पापात्प्रमुच्यते ॥ ॥

Having made an offering with ghee, a person is always well nourished. Having satisfied the Sun with meat, one is immediately freed from sin.

BP 1.93.32

घृतेन स्नपनं कृत्वा एकाहमुदये रवेः । गवां शतसहस्रस्य दत्तस्य फलमश्नुते ॥ ॥

Having bathed the deity with ghee for one day at sunrise, one obtains the fruit of giving one hundred thousand cows.

BP 1.93.33

गवां क्षीरेण सन्तर्प्य पुण्डरीकफलं लभेत् । रसेन स्नापयेद्देवमश्वमेधफलं लभेत् ॥ ॥

Having offered cow milk to the deity, one obtains the fruit of the Puṇḍarīka rite. Bathing the deity with juice yields the fruit of the horse sacrifice.

BP 1.93.34

सूर्याय तरुणीं धेनुं गामेकां यः प्रयच्छति । कञ्जजाम् अचलां प्राप्य पुनर्लेखपुरं व्रजेत् ॥ ॥

Whoever gives one young cow to the Sun reaches the immovable abode of the Lotus born, then goes again to the city of the gods.

BP 1.93.35

गोशरीरे तु रोमाणि यावन्ति त्रिपुरान्तक । स तावद्वर्षकोटीस्तु लेखालोके महीयते ॥ ॥

For as many hairs as there are on the body of a cow, O destroyer of Tripura, for that many crores of years he is honored in the world of the gods.

BP 1.93.36

गोशतं भानवे दत्त्वा राजसूयफलं लभेत् । अश्वमेधफलं तस्य यः सहस्रं प्रयच्छति ॥ ॥

Giving a hundred cows to Bhānu yields the fruit of the royal-consecration sacrifice. Whoever gives a thousand cows obtains the fruit of the horse sacrifice.

BP 1.93.37

गुग्गुलं देवदारुं च दहे नित्यं घृतस्रवम् । आज्यधूमो हि देवानां प्रकृत्यैव प्रियः सदा ॥ ॥

One should always burn fragrant resin and cedar with ghee flowing. The smoke of clarified butter is always naturally dear to the gods.

BP 1.93.38

भेरीयादीनि च वाद्यानि शङ्खवेण्वादिकानि च । ये प्रयच्छन्ति सूर्याय यान्ति ते हंसमन्दिरम् ॥ ॥

Those who offer drums and other instruments, conches and flutes to the Sun go to the abode of the swan.

BP 1.93.39

वज्रमाहरते यस्तु रवेर्भक्तिसमन्वितः । तीर्थोदकमथै वान्यः स याति विबुधालयम् ॥ ॥

Whoever brings a diamond to Ravi with devotion, or another who brings water from a sacred place, goes to the abode of the gods.

BP 1.93.40

विमानैः स्त्रीशताकीर्णैः क्रीडयित्वा चिरं नरः । मानुषत्वमनुप्राप्य राजा भवति धार्मिकः ॥ ॥

After sporting for a long time in aerial cars crowded with hundreds of women, a person gains human birth and becomes a righteous king.

BP 1.93.41

छत्रं ध्वजं वितानं च पताकाश्चामराणि च । हेमदण्डानि वै दद्याद्रवेर्यो भक्तिमान्नरः ॥ ॥

A devout person gives the Sun umbrellas, banners, canopies, flags, fly whisks, and golden staffs.

BP 1.93.42

विमानेन स दिव्येन किङ्किणीजालमालिना । सूर्यलोकमतो गत्वा भवत्यप्सरसां पतिः ॥ ॥

In a divine aerial car adorned with a net of bells, he goes from there to the world of the Sun and becomes the lord of the celestial nymphs.

BP 1.93.43

तत्रोष्य सुचिरं कालं स्वर्गात्प्रत्यागतः पुनः । मानुष्ये जायते राजा सर्वराजनमस्कृतः ॥ ॥

After dwelling there for a long time, he returns again from heaven and is born among humans as a king honored by all kings.

BP 1.93.44

दत्त्वा वासांसि सूर्याय अलङ्कारांस्तथैव च । क्रीडते जनलोकस्थो यावदाभूतसंप्लवम् ॥ ॥

Having given clothes and ornaments to the Sun, he sports in the Jana world until the dissolution of all beings.

BP 1.93.45

गीतवादित्रनृत्यैश्च कुर्याज्जागरणं रवेः । गन्धर्वाप्सरसां मध्ये क्रीडते सुचिरं नरः ॥ ॥

He should keep vigil for the Sun with songs, instrumental music, and dance. A person plays for a long time among celestial singers and nymphs.

BP 1.93.46

गन्धैः पुष्पैस्तथा पत्रैः स्तोत्रैर्वा विविधैः । ये स्तुवन्ति रविं भक्त्या ते यान्ति पतगालयम् ॥ ॥

Those who praise Ravi with devotion using fragrances, flowers, leaves, or various hymns go to the abode of the winged one.

BP 1.93.47

उषः स्तुवन्ति ये सूर्यमुपगायन्ति ते सदा । पाठकाश्चारणाश्चैव सर्वे ते स्वर्गगामिनः ॥ ॥

Those who praise the Sun at dawn and always sing in accompaniment, the reciters and bards alike, all go to heaven.

BP 1.93.48

अश्वयुक्तं युगैर्युक्तं यो दद्याद्रवये रथम् । काञ्चनं वापि रौप्यं वा मणिरत्नान्वितं शुभम् ॥ ॥

Whoever gives the Sun a beautiful chariot yoked with horses and fitted with yokes, made of gold or silver and adorned with gems.

BP 1.93.49

स यानेनार्कवर्णेन किङ्किणीजालमालिना । स्वर्गलोकमितो गत्वा क्रीडतेऽप्सरसा सह ॥ ॥

In a vehicle the color of the Sun, adorned with a net of bells, he goes from here to heaven and plays with a celestial nymph.

BP 1.93.50

यस्तु दारुमयं कुर्याद्रवे रथमनुत्तमम् । स यात्यर्कसवर्णेन विमानेनार्कमण्डलम् ॥ ॥

Whoever makes an excellent wooden chariot for Ravi goes to the solar orb in an aerial car the color of the Sun.

BP 1.93.51

यात्रां कुर्वन्ति ये भानोर् नराः संवत्सरादपि । षण्मासाद्वा गणश्रेष्ठ तेषां पुण्यफलं शृणु ॥ ॥

Men who conduct a procession for Bhānu for a year or even six months, O chief of the hosts, hear their meritorious result.

BP 1.93.52

ध्यानिनो योगिनश्चैव प्राप्नुवन्तीह यां गतिम् । तां गतिं प्रतिपद्यन्ते सूर्यवर्त्मावगाहिनः ॥ ॥

The state attained here by meditators and yogis is attained by those who enter the path of the Sun.

BP 1.93.53

रथं वहन्ति ये भानोर् नरा भक्तिसमन्विताः । अरोगाश्चादरिद्रश्च जातो जातो भवन्ति ते ॥ ॥

Those who draw the chariot of Bhānu with devotion are free from disease and poverty, birth after birth.

BP 1.93.54

कर्तारो रथयात्राया ये नरा भास्करस्य तु । ते भानुलोकमासाद्य विहरन्ति यथासुखम् ॥ ॥

Those who conduct the chariot procession of Bhāskara, having reached the world of Bhānu, enjoy themselves as they please.

BP 1.93.55

यात्राभङ्गं तु यो मोहात् क्रोधाद्वा कुरुते नरः । मन्देहास्ते नरा ज्ञेया राक्षसाः पापकारिणः ॥ ॥

A man who breaks the procession through delusion or anger is to be known as a Mandeha demon, an evil doer.

BP 1.93.56

धनं धान्यं हिरण्यं वा वासांसि विविधानि च । ये प्रयच्छन्ति सूर्याय ते यान्ति परमां गतिम् ॥ ॥

Those who give wealth, grain, gold, or various garments to the Sun go to the highest state.

BP 1.93.57

गा वाथ महिषीर्वापि गजानश्वांश्च शोभनान् । यः प्रयच्छति सूर्याय तस्य पुण्यफलं शृणु ॥ ॥

Hear the meritorious result for one who gives cows, buffaloes, or beautiful elephants and horses to the Sun.

BP 1.93.58

अक्षयं सर्वकामीयम् अश्वमेधफलं लभेत् । सहस्रगुणितं तच्च दानमस्योपतिष्ठति ॥ ॥

He obtains the imperishable fulfillment of all desires and the fruit of the horse sacrifice. That gift yields a thousandfold result for him.

BP 1.93.59

महीं ददाति योऽर्काय कृष्टां फलवतीं शुभाम् । स तारयति वै वंश्यान् दश पूर्वान् दशापरान् ॥ ॥

Whoever gives the Sun cultivated, fruitful, auspicious land delivers ten ancestors and ten descendants of his lineage.

BP 1.93.60

विमानेन च दिव्येन गोपुरं गोपतेर्व्रजेत् । क्रीडत्यप्सरसां मध्ये करीव करिणीगणे ॥ ॥

In a divine aerial car he goes to the gate of the Lord of cows and plays among the celestial nymphs like an elephant among a herd of female elephants.

BP 1.93.61

ग्रामं ददाति यो भक्त्या सूर्याय मतिमान् नरः । विमानेनार्कवर्णेन स याति परमां गतिम् ॥ ॥

The wise person who gives a village to the Sun with devotion goes to the highest state in an aerial car the color of the Sun.

BP 1.93.62

आरामान् ये प्रयच्छन्ति पत्रपुष्पफलोपगान् । भानवे भक्तियुक्तास्तु ते यान्ति परमां गतिम् ॥ ॥

Those who give gardens furnished with leaves, flowers, and fruit to Bhānu with devotion go to the highest state.

BP 1.93.63

मानसं वाचिकं वापि कर्मजं यच्च दुष्कृतम् । सर्वं सूर्यप्रसादेन अशेषं च प्रणश्यति ॥ ॥

Whatever evil deed is mental, verbal, or born of action perishes completely through the grace of the Sun.

BP 1.93.64

आर्तो वा व्याधितो वापि दरिद्रो दुःखितोऽपि वा । आदित्यं शरणं गत्वा नात्मानं शोचते नरः ॥ ॥

Whether afflicted, sick, poor, or sorrowful, a person who takes refuge in Āditya does not grieve for himself.

BP 1.93.65

एकाहेनापि यद्भानोः पूजायाः प्राप्यते फलम् । तद्वै क्रतुशतैरिष्टैः प्राप्यते फलमुत्तमम् ॥ ॥

The highest fruit obtained from one day of worship of Bhānu is obtained through a hundred sacrifices.

BP 1.93.66

कृत्वा प्रेक्षणकं भानोर् दिव्यम् आयतने शुभम् । अक्षयं सर्वकामीयं राजसूयफलं लभेत् ॥ ॥

Having staged a divine spectacle for Bhānu in a beautiful temple, one obtains inexhaustible fulfillment of every desire and the fruit of the royal-consecration sacrifice.

BP 1.93.67

वेश्याकदम्बकं यस्तु दद्यात्सूर्याय भक्तितः । स गछेत्परमं स्थानं यत्र तिष्ठति भानुमान् ॥ ॥

Whoever devoutly gives a company of courtesans to the Sun goes to the highest place where the Sun stands.

BP 1.93.68

पुस्तकं भानवे दद्याद् भारतस्य गणाधिप । सर्वपापविमुक्तात्मा विष्णुलोके महीयते ॥ ॥

Whoever gives the book of the Bhārata to Bhānu, freed from every sin, is honored in the world of Viṣṇu.

BP 1.93.69

रामायणस्य दत्त्वा तु पुस्तकं त्रिपुरान्तक । वाजपेयफलं प्राप्य गोपतेः पुरम् आव्रजेत् ॥ ॥

Having given the book of the Rāmāyaṇa, O destroyer of Tripura, one obtains the fruit of the strength-drink sacrifice and goes to the city of the Lord of cows.

BP 1.93.70

भविष्यं साम्बसंज्ञं वा दत्त्वा सूर्याय पुस्तकम् । राजसूयाश्वमेधाभ्यां फलं प्राप्नोति मानवः ॥ ॥

Giving the Sun a book called the Bhaviṣya or the Sāmba, a person obtains the fruit of the royal-consecration sacrifice and the horse sacrifice.

BP 1.93.71

सर्वान् कामान् अवाप्नोति याति सूर्यसलोकताम् । सूर्यलोके चिरं स्थित्वा ब्रह्मलोकं व्रजेत् पुनः । स्थित्वा कल्पशतं तत्र राजा भवति भूतले ॥ ॥

He obtains all desires and reaches the same world as the Sun. After dwelling long in the world of the Sun, he goes again to the world of Brahmā. Having stayed there for a hundred cosmic ages, he becomes a king on earth.

BP 1.93.72

भानोर् आयतने यस्तु प्रपां कुर्याद् गणाधिप । स याति परमं स्थानं दिव्यं सौमनसं नरः ॥ ॥

The person who builds a drinking pavilion at the temple of Bhānu, O chief of the hosts, goes to the highest place, the divine Joyful Abode.

BP 1.93.73

शीतकाले घनं दद्यान् नराणां शीतनाशनम् । भानोर् आयतने देव अश्वमेधफलं लभेत् ॥ ॥

In the cold season, whoever gives a thick covering that removes the cold of people at the temple of Bhānu obtains the fruit of the horse sacrifice.

BP 1.93.74

इतिहासपुराणाभ्यां पुण्यं पुस्तकवाचनम् । अश्वमेधसहस्रं यो नित्यं कर्तुं प्रवर्तते । न तत्फलमवाप्नोति यदाप्नोत्यस्य कर्मणः ॥ ॥

Reading the books of histories and Purāṇas is meritorious. One who continually undertakes a thousand horse sacrifices does not obtain the fruit that comes from this deed.

BP 1.93.75

तस्मात् सर्वप्रयत्नेन कार्यं पुस्तकवाचनम् । इतिहासपुराणाभ्यां भानोर् आयतने शुभम् ॥ ॥

Therefore, with every effort, the reading of books should be performed as an auspicious act in the temple of Bhānu through histories and Purāṇas.

BP 1.93.76

नान्यत् पुष्टिकरं भानोस् तथा तुष्टिकरं परम् । पुण्याख्यानकथा यास्तु यथा तुष्यति भास्करः ॥ ॥

Nothing else is as nourishing to Bhānu or as supremely pleasing as the meritorious narrative that pleases Bhāskara.
