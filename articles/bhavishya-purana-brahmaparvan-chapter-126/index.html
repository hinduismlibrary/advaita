Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 126 — The Worlds and Mount Meru

BP 1.126.1

सुमन्तुरुवाच। आकाशं खं दिशो व्योम अन्तरिक्षं नभो'म्बरम। पुष्करं गगनं मेरुर्विपुलं च बिलं तथा ॥

Sumantu said. Space, ether, directions, expanse, midspace, heaven, sky, lotus, firmament, Meru, vastness, and cave are names of the sky, O king.

BP 1.126.2

आपोछिद्रं तथा शून्यं तमो वै रोदसी तथा। नामान्येतानि ते व्योम्नः कीर्तितानि महीपते ॥

The water opening, the void, the darkness, and the two regions are also proclaimed as names of your sky, O king.

BP 1.126.3

लवणक्षीरदध्यम्लघृतमध्विक्षवस्तथा। स्वादूदकश्च सप्तैते समुद्राः परिकीर्तिताः ॥

Salt, milk, sour curds, ghee, honey, sugarcane juice, and sweet water, these seven are proclaimed as oceans.

BP 1.126.4

हिमवान्हेमकूटश्च निषधो नील एव च। श्वेतश्च शृङ्गवांश्चैव षडेते वर्षपर्वताः ॥

Himavān, Golden Peak, Niṣadha, Blue, White, and Horned are the six mountain ranges of the regions.

BP 1.126.5

मध्यसंस्थस्तथैतेषां महाराजतपर्वतः। माहेन्द्री चाप्यथाग्नेयी याम्या च नैरृती तथा ॥

The great silver mountain stands in the midst of these. The cities of Mahendra, Agni, Yama, and Nairṛta are established above.

BP 1.126.6

वारुणी चाथ वायव्या सौम्यैशानी तथैव च। एताः पुर्यस्तु देवानां तथोपरि समाश्रिताः ॥

The cities of Varuṇa, the Wind, Soma, and Īśāna are likewise established above as cities of the gods.

BP 1.126.7

पृथिव्यां तु स्थितो वीर लोकालोकस्तु पर्वतः। ततश्चण्डकपालं तु तस्मात्तत्परतस्तु यः ॥

On earth stands the mountain Lokāloka, O hero. Beyond it is Caṇḍakapāla, and beyond that is what lies farther.

BP 1.126.8

ततो'ग्निर्वायुराकाशं ततो भूतादिरुच्यते। ततो महानहङ्कारः प्रकृतिः पुरुषस्ततः ॥

Beyond that are fire, wind, and space. Then comes the principle of beings, then the Great Principle, ego, Nature, and then the Person.

BP 1.126.9

पुरुषादीश्वरो श्रेय ईश्वरेणाव्रृतं जगत। ईश्वरो भगवन्भानुस्तेनेदं पूरितं जगत ॥

Beyond the Person is the higher Lord, Īśvara, and the universe is covered by Īśvara. The blessed Lord Bhānu has filled this universe.

BP 1.126.10

सहस्रांशुर्महातेजाश्चतुर्बाहुर्महाबलः। ऊर्ध्वमप्यथ लोकास्तु प्राङ्मया ये प्रकीर्तिताः ॥

He is thousand rayed, greatly radiant, four armed, and mighty. Above are the worlds that I previously proclaimed.

BP 1.126.11

भूयस्तान्सम्प्रवक्ष्यामि अण्डावरणकारकान। भूर्लोकस्तुभुवर्लोकस्तृतीयः परिकीर्तितः ॥

I shall explain further the coverings of the egg. The Earth world, the Atmosphere world, and the third is proclaimed.

BP 1.126.12

महर्जनस्तपः सत्यः सप्तलोकाः प्रकीर्तिताः। ततस्तवण्डकपालं तु तस्माच्च परस्तपः ॥

The Great World, the World of People, the World of Austerity, and the World of Truth are proclaimed as the seven worlds. Beyond that is the shell of the egg, and beyond it is the highest austerity.

BP 1.126.13

ततो'ग्निर्वायुराकाशं ततो भूतादिरुच्यते। ततो महान्प्रधानश्च प्रकृतिपुरुषस्ततः ॥

Beyond that are fire, wind, and space. Then comes the principle of beings, then the Great Principle, the Primal Cause, Nature, and then the Person.

BP 1.126.14

पुरुषादीश्वरो ज्ञेय ईश्वरेणावृतं जगत। भूमेरधस्तात्सप्तैव लोकानभिमताञ्छृणु ॥

Beyond the Person, Īśvara is to be known, and the universe is covered by Īśvara. Hear the seven worlds regarded as beneath the earth.

BP 1.126.15

तलं सुतलपाताले तलातलं तथातलम। वितलं च कुरुश्रेष्ठ सप्तमं च रसातलम ॥

Tala, Sutala, Pātāla, Talātala, Atala, and Vitala, and the seventh, Rasātala, O best of the Kurus.

BP 1.126.16

ततो'ग्निर्वायुराकाशं ततो भूतादिरुच्यते। ततो महान्प्रधानश्च प्रकृतिः पुरुषस्ततः ॥

Beyond these are fire, wind, space, the principle of beings, the Great Principle, the Primal Cause, Nature, and the Person.

BP 1.126.17

पुरुषादीश्वरो ज्ञेय ईश्वरेणावृतं जगत। एवं मेरोः प्रमाणं तु सर्वमेतत्प्रकीर्तितम ॥

Beyond the Person is known Īśvara, who covers the universe. Thus the whole measure of Meru has been proclaimed.

BP 1.126.18

चतुरस्रश्चतुःशृंगः स मेरुः काञ्चनः शुभः। पृथिव्यां संस्थितो मध्ये सिद्धगन्धर्वसेवितः ॥

This Meru is square, four horned, golden, and auspicious. It stands in the middle of the earth, served by perfected beings and celestial musicians.

BP 1.126.19

चतुर्भिः काञ्चनैः शृंगैर्दिव्यैर्दिवमिवोल्लिखन। योजनानां सहस्राणि चतुराशीतिरुच्छ्रितः ॥

Scraping heaven with its four divine golden horns, it rises eighty four thousand yojanas.

BP 1.126.20

प्रविष्टः षोडशाधस्तादष्टाविंशतिविस्तृतः। विस्तारस्त्रिगुणश्चास्य परिणाहस्ततः स्मृतः ॥

It extends sixteen thousand below and is twenty eight thousand in breadth. Its circumference is remembered as three times its breadth.

BP 1.126.21

तस्य सौमनसं नाम शृंगमेकं तु काञ्चनम। द्वितीयं पद्मरागाभं ज्योतिष्कं नाम नामतः ॥

One horn is named Saumanasa, and it is golden. The second resembles a ruby and is named Jyotiṣka.

BP 1.126.22

तृतीयं नामतश्चित्रं सर्वदेवमयं शुभम। चतुर्थं राजतं शुक्लं चन्द्रौजस्कमिति स्मृतम ॥

The third is named Citra, auspicious and filled with all the gods. The fourth is silver and white, remembered as Candraujas.

BP 1.126.23

यत्तु सौमनसं नाम शृंगं गाङ्गेयमुच्यते। तदेव चोदयो नाम्ना यत्रोद्यन्दृश्यते रविः ॥

The horn named Saumanasa is called Gāṅgeya. It is also named Udaya, where the rising Sun is seen.

BP 1.126.24

उत्तरेण परिक्रम्य जम्बूद्वीपं दिवाकरः। दृश्यो भवति भूतानां शिखरं च समास्थितः ॥

Having circled north of Jambūdvīpa, the Day Maker becomes visible to beings and stands upon the summit.

BP 1.126.25

काञ्चनस्य च शैलस्य तेजसार्कस्य चाहृते। उभे सन्ध्ये प्रकाशेते आताम्रे पूर्वपश्चिमे ॥

By the radiance of the golden mountain and the Sun, both twilights shine copper red in the east and the west.

BP 1.126.26

शृंगे सौमनसे सूर्य उत्तिष्ठत्युत्तरायणे। ज्योतिषे दक्षिणे चापि विषुवे मध्यतस्तयोः ॥

On the Saumanasa horn the Sun rises in the northern course, and on Jyotiṣka in the south, at the equinox midway between the two.

BP 1.126.27

ईशेन्द्रोद्रिश्च ऐशान्यां तत्राग्निः पूर्वदक्षिणे। नैरृते'पि ततो ज्ञेयो वायव्ये मरुतस्तथा ॥

Īśa and Indra are in the northeast, Agni in the southeast, Nairṛta in the southwest, and the Maruts in the northwest.

BP 1.126.28

मध्ये तु कंजजः साक्षाद्ग्रहा ज्योतींषि चैव हि। आदित्यस्तेन रूपेण तस्मिन्व्योम्नि प्रतिष्ठितः ॥

The Lotus Born himself is in the middle, and the planets and lights too. Āditya is established in that firmament in this form.

BP 1.126.29

इदं देवमयं व्योम तथा लोकमयं स्मृतम। पूर्वकोणस्थिते शृंगे स्थितः शुक्रो महीपते ॥

This firmament is remembered as filled with gods and worlds. On the horn situated at the eastern corner stands Śukra, O king.

BP 1.126.30

हेलिजश्चापरे ज्ञेयो धननाथस्तथापरे। सोमश्चापि चतुर्थे तु स्थितः शृंगे जनाधिप ॥

Helija is to be known on another horn, and the lord of wealth on another. Soma too stands on the fourth horn, O lord of men.

BP 1.126.31

मध्ये केशास्थितो राजन्हुङ्कारश्च पिनाकिनः। शृंगे पूर्वोत्तरे राजन्स्थितो देवो विधुक्षये ॥

In the middle, O king, stand Keśa and the Huṃ sound of the bow bearer. On the northeastern horn, O king, stands the god at the waning of the Moon.

BP 1.126.32

ततः स्थितो महादेवो गोपतिर्लोकपूजितः। पूर्वाग्नेयीस्थिते शृंगे स्थितो वै शाण्डिलः सुतः ॥

Then there stands the great god, lord of cows, worshipped by the world. On the eastern southeastern horn stands the son of Śāṇḍila.

BP 1.126.33

ततः स्थितो महातेजाः कीनाशो हेलिनन्दनः। स्थितो वै नैरृते शृंगे विरूपाक्षो महाबलः ॥

Then the greatly radiant Kīnāśa, son of Heli, stands there. On the southwestern horn stands mighty Virūpākṣa.

BP 1.126.34

तस्मादनन्तरो देवः स्थितो वै यादसां पतिः। ततः स्थितो महातेजा वीर मित्रो महाबलः ॥

Immediately after him stands the god, lord of the aquatic beings. Then stands Mitra, the greatly radiant hero, mighty.

BP 1.126.35

वायव्यं शृंगमाश्रित्य सर्वदेवनमस्कृतम। ततः स्थितो दशबलो नरमारुह्य भारत ॥

Taking refuge on the northwest horn, honored by all the gods, stands the ten strong one, mounted on a man, O Bhārata.

BP 1.126.36

ब्रह्मा मध्ये स्थितो देवो ह्यनन्तश्चाध एव हि। उपेन्द्रशङ्करौ देवौ ब्रह्मणो'न्ते समास्थितौ ॥

The god Brahmā stands in the middle, and Ananta is below. The gods Upendra and Śaṅkara stand established at the end of Brahmā.

BP 1.126.37

एष मेरुस्तथा व्योम एष धर्मश्च पठ्यते। सर्वदेवमयश्चायं मेरुर्व्योम इति स्मृतः ॥

This is Meru and the firmament. This is read as dharma. It is filled with all the gods, and Meru is remembered as the firmament.

BP 1.126.38

तथा वेदमयश्चापि पठ्यते नात्र संशयः। शृंगाणि वेदाश्चत्वारः पूर्वशृंगादयो विदुः ॥

It is also read as consisting of the Vedas, there is no doubt. The four horns are known as the four Vedas, beginning with the eastern horn.
