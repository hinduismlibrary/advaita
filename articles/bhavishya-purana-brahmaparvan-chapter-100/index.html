Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 100 — The Joyful Seventh Day Observance

BP 1.100.1

ब्रह्मोवाच। या तु मार्गशिरे मासि शुक्लपक्षे तु सप्तमी। नन्दा सा कथिता वीर सर्वानन्दकरी शुभा ॥

Brahma spoke. That seventh day in the bright fortnight of Mārgaśīrṣa is called Nandā, O hero, auspicious and bestowing joy on all.

brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said;
yā — which;
tu — indeed;
mārgaśire māsi — in the month of Mārgaśīrṣa;
śukla-pakṣe — in the bright fortnight;
saptamī — Saptamī;
nandā — Nandā;
sā — that;
kathitā — is called;
vīra — O hero;
sarva-ānanda-karī — bestowing joy upon all;
śubhā — auspicious.

BP 1.100.2

पञ्चम्यामेकभक्तं तु षष्ठ्यां नक्तं प्रकीर्तितम्। सप्तम्यामुपवासं तु कीर्तयन्ति मनीषिणः ॥

On the fifth day one meal is prescribed. On the sixth day eating at night is prescribed. The wise proclaim fasting on the seventh day.

pañcamyām — on the fifth day;
eka-bhaktam — one meal;
tu — indeed;
ṣaṣṭhyām — on the sixth day;
naktam — at night;
prakīrtitam — is prescribed;
saptamyām — on the seventh day;
upavāsam — fasting;
tu — indeed;
kīrtayanti — they proclaim;
manīṣiṇaḥ — the wise.

BP 1.100.3

पारणान्यत्र वै त्रीणि शंसन्तीह मनीषिणः। मालतीकुसुमानीह सुगन्धं चन्दनं तथा ॥

The wise declare three fast breakings here. Here too are jasmine flowers and fragrant sandalwood.

pāraṇāni — breakings of the fast;
atra — here;
vai — indeed;
trīṇi — three;
śaṃsanti — declare;
iha — here;
manīṣiṇaḥ — the wise;
mālatī-kusumāni — mālatī flowers;
iha — here;
su-gandham — fragrant;
candanam — sandalwood;
tathā — likewise.

BP 1.100.4

कर्पूरागरुसम्मिश्रं धूपं चात्र विनिर्दिशेत्। दध्योदनं सखण्डं च नैवेद्यं भास्करप्रियम् ॥

One should prescribe incense mixed with camphor and agarwood here. Curd rice with sugar is the offering dear to Bhāskara.

karpūra-aguru-sammiśram — mixed with camphor and agaru;
dhūpam — incense;
ca — and;
atra — here;
vinirdiśet — one should prescribe;
dadhi-odanam — curd rice;
sa-khaṇḍam — together with sugar;
ca — and;
naivedyam — offering;
bhāskara-priyam — dear to Bhāskara.

BP 1.100.5

तमेव दद्याद्विप्रेभ्योऽश्नीयाच्च तदनु स्वयम्। धूपार्थं भास्करस्यैष प्रथमे पारणे विधिः ॥

One should give that very offering to the Brahmins, and then eat it oneself. This is the procedure for the first fast breaking for the incense offering to Bhāskara.

tam eva — that very;
dadyāt — should give;
viprebhyaḥ — to the Brāhmaṇas;
aśnīyāt — should eat;
ca — and;
tadanu — thereafter;
svayam — oneself;
dhūpa-artham — for incense;
bhāskarasya — of Bhāskara;
eṣaḥ — this;
prathame pāraṇe — at the first breaking;
vidhiḥ — procedure.

BP 1.100.6

पलाशपुष्पाणि विभो धूपो यः शक्य एव च। कर्पूरं चन्दनं कुष्ठमगुरुः सिह्लकं तथा ॥

Flame of the forest flowers, O Lord, and incense that is obtainable. Camphor, sandalwood, costus root, agarwood, and olibanum likewise.

palāśa-puṣpāṇi — palāśa flowers;
vibho — O Lord;
dhūpaḥ — incense;
yaḥ — which;
śakyaḥ — śakya;
eva — indeed;
ca — and;
karpūram — camphor;
candanam — sandalwood;
kuṣṭham — kuṣṭha;
aguruḥ — agaru;
sihlakam — sihlaka;
tathā — likewise.

BP 1.100.7

सग्रन्थि वृषणं भीम कुंकुमं गृञ्जनं तथा। हरीतकी तथा भीम एष पक्षक उच्यते ॥

With a knot, a testicle, saffron, and garlic, O Bhīma, and chebulic myrobalan likewise, O Bhīma. This mixture is called Pakṣaka.

sa-granthi — together with granthi;
vṛṣaṇam — vṛṣaṇa;
bhīma — O Bhīma;
kuṃkumam — saffron;
gṛñjanam — gṛñjana;
tathā — likewise;
harītakī — harītakī;
eṣaḥ — this;
pakṣakaḥ — Pakṣaka;
ucyate — is called.

BP 1.100.8

धूपः प्रबोध आदिष्टो नैवेद्यं खण्डमण्डकाः। कृष्णागरुः सितं कञ्जं बालकं वृषणं तथा ॥

Prabodha is prescribed as incense. Sugar cakes are the offering. Black agarwood, white lotus, vetiver root, and the testicle likewise.

dhūpaḥ — incense;
prabodhaḥ — Prabodha;
ādiṣṭaḥ — is prescribed;
naivedyam — offering;
khaṇḍa-maṇḍakāḥ — khaṇḍamaṇḍakas;
kṛṣṇa-aguruḥ — black agaru;
sitam — white;
kañjam — lotus;
bālakam — bālaka;
vṛṣaṇam — vṛṣaṇa;
tathā — likewise.

BP 1.100.9

चन्दनं तगरो मुस्ता प्रबोधशर्करान्विता। भोजयेद्ब्राह्मणांश्चापि खण्डखाद्यैर्गणाधिप। निम्बपत्रं तु सम्प्राश्य ततो भुञ्जीत वाग्यतः ॥

Sandalwood, Indian valerian, and nutgrass with Prabodha sugar. One should also feed the Brahmins with sugar cakes, O lord of the hosts. Having chewed a neem leaf, one should then eat, restrained in speech.

candanam — sandalwood;
tagaram — tagara;
mustā — musta;
prabodha-śarkara-anvitā — combined with Prabodha sugar;
bhojayet — should feed;
brāhmaṇān — the Brāhmaṇas;
ca api — and also;
khaṇḍa-khādyaiḥ — with sugar-made foods;
gaṇa-adhipa — O lord of the Gaṇas;
nimba-patram — neem leaf;
tu — and;
samprāśya — having partaken;
tataḥ — thereafter;
bhuñjīta — should eat;
vāg-yataḥ — restrained in speech.

BP 1.100.10

पारणस्य द्वितीयस्य विधिरेष प्रकीर्तितः ॥

This is the procedure proclaimed for the second fast breaking.

pāraṇasya — of the breaking of the fast;
dvitīyasya — second;
vidhiḥ — procedure;
eṣaḥ — this;
prakīrtitaḥ — is proclaimed.

BP 1.100.11

नीलोत्पलानि शुभ्राणि धूपं गौग्गुलमाहरेत्। नैवेद्यं पायसं देयं प्रीतये भास्करस्य तु ॥

One should bring bright blue lotuses and guggul resin resin as incense. Milk pudding should be given as an offering for the pleasure of Bhāskara.

nīla-utpalāni — blue lotuses;
śubhrāṇi — beautiful;
dhūpam — incense;
guggulam — guggulu;
āharet — should bring;
naivedyam — offering;
pāyasam — milk pudding;
deyam — should be given;
prītaye — for pleasure;
bhāskarasya — of Bhāskara;
tu — indeed.

BP 1.100.12

विलेपनं चन्दनं तु प्राशने विधिरुच्यते। तृतीयस्यापि ते वीर कथितो विधिरुत्तमः ॥

Sandalwood is prescribed for anointing. The excellent procedure for the third partaking has also been told to you, O hero.

vilepanam — anointing;
candanam — sandalwood;
tu — indeed;
prāśane — for partaking;
vidhiḥ — procedure;
ucyate — is prescribed;
tṛtīyasya — of the third;
api — also;
te — to you;
vīra — O hero;
kathitaḥ — has been told;
vidhiḥ uttamaḥ — excellent procedure.

BP 1.100.13

शृणु नामानि देवस्य पावनानि नृणां सदा। विष्णुर्भगस्तथा धाता प्रीयतामुद्गिरेच्च वै ॥

Hear the purifying names of the deity for people always. One should utter, may Viṣṇu, Bhaga, and Dhātā be pleased.

nāmāni — names;
devasya — of the deity;
pāvanāni — purifying;
nṛṇām — for people;
sadā — always;
viṣṇuḥ — Viṣṇu;
bhagaḥ — Bhaga;
tathā — likewise;
dhātā — Dhātā;
prīyatām — may he be pleased;
udgiret — one should utter;
ca — and;
vai — indeed.

BP 1.100.14

अनेन विधिना यस्तु कुर्यात्प्रयतमानसः। सकामानिह सम्प्राप्य नन्दते शाश्वती समाः ॥

Whoever performs this with a disciplined mind, having obtained desired things here, rejoices for everlasting years.

anena vidhinā — by this procedure;
yaḥ tu — whoever;
kuryāt — performs;
prayata-mānasaḥ — with a disciplined mind;
sa-kāmān — desires;
iha — here;
samprāpya — having obtained;
nandate — rejoices;
śāśvatīḥ samāḥ — everlasting years.

BP 1.100.15

ततः सूर्यसदो गत्या नन्दते नन्दवर्धन। एषा तु नन्दजननी तवाख्याता भया शिव ॥

Then, having gone to the abode of the Sun, one rejoices, O increaser of joy. This mother of joy has been described to you, Bhayā, O auspicious one.

sūrya-sadaḥ — the Sun's abode;
gatvā — having gone;
nandate — he rejoices;
nanda-vardhana — O increaser of joy;
eṣā — this;
tu — indeed;
nanda-jananī — mother of joy;
tava — to you;
ākhyātā — has been described;
bhayā — O fearful one;
śiva — O auspicious one.

BP 1.100.16

यामुपोष्य ततो भुक्त्वा नन्दते हंसमाप्य वै ॥

Having fasted for it and then eaten, one rejoices upon attaining the swan.

yām — for which;
upoṣya — having fasted;
tataḥ — thereafter;
bhuktvā — having eaten;
nandate — he rejoices;
haṃsam āpya — having attained Haṃsa;
vai — indeed.
