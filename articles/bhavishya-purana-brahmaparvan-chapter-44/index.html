Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 44 — Brahmin Conduct and Varna Duties

BP 1.44.1

ब्रह्मोवाच
हेयोपादेयतत्त्वज्ञास्त्यक्तान्यायपथागमाः ।
जितेन्द्रियमनोवाचः सदाचारपरायणाः ॥

Brahmā said, They know the truth of what is to be rejected and accepted, have left the path of injustice, have mastered their senses, mind, and speech, and are devoted to good conduct.

हेयोपादेयतत्त्वज्ञाः (heya-upādeya-tattva-jñāḥ) — knowing what to reject and accept;
त्यक्तान्यायपथागमाः (tyakta-anyāya-patha-āgamāḥ) — abandoning unjust paths;
जितेन्द्रियमनोवाचः (jita-indriya-mano-vācaḥ) — mastering senses mind and speech;
सदाचारपरायणाः (sadācāra-parāyaṇāḥ) — devoted to right conduct

BP 1.44.2

नियमाचारवृत्तस्था हितान्वेषणतत्पराः ।
संसाररक्षणोपायक्रियायुक्तमनोरथाः ॥

Those established in discipline and proper conduct, intent on seeking welfare, direct their purpose toward actions that protect beings in the cycle of existence.

नियमाचारवृत्तस्थाः (niyama-ācāra-vṛtta-sthāḥ) — abiding in disciplined conduct;
हितान्वेषणतत्पराः (hita-anveṣaṇa-tatparāḥ) — intent on seeking welfare;
संसाररक्षणोपाय (saṃsāra-rakṣaṇa-upāya) — means protecting beings in saṃsāra;
क्रियायुक्तमनोरथाः (kriyā-yukta-manorathāḥ) — intentions joined to practice

BP 1.44.3

सम्यग्दर्शनसम्पन्नाः समाधिस्था हतक्रुधः ।
स्वाध्यायभक्तहृदयास्त्यक्तसङ्गा विमत्सराः ॥

Those endowed with right vision, established in concentration, free from anger, devoted in heart to recitation, free from attachment, and without envy.

सम्यग्दर्शनसम्पन्नाः (samyag-darśana-sampannāḥ) — endowed with right vision;
समाधिस्थाः (samādhi-sthāḥ) — established in concentration;
हतक्रुधः (hata-krudhaḥ) — having conquered anger;
स्वाध्यायभक्तहृदयाः (svādhyāya-bhakta-hṛdayāḥ) — hearts devoted to self-study;
त्यक्तसङ्गाः (tyakta-saṅgāḥ) — abandoning attachment;
विमत्सराः (vimatsarāḥ) — free from envy

BP 1.44.4

विशोका विमदाः शान्ता सर्वप्राणिहितैषिणः ।
सुखदुःखसमालोका विविक्तस्थानवासिनः ॥

Free from grief and pride, peaceful, seeking the welfare of all beings, regarding pleasure and pain alike, and dwelling in secluded places.

विशोकाः विमदाः शान्ताः (viśokāḥ vimadāḥ śāntāḥ) — free from grief pride and peaceful;
सर्वप्राणिहितैषिणः (sarva-prāṇi-hita-eṣiṇaḥ) — seeking welfare of all beings;
सुखदुःखसमालोकाः (sukha-duḥkha-sama-ālokāḥ) — viewing pleasure and pain equally;
विविक्तस्थानवासिनः (vivikta-sthāna-vāsinaḥ) — dwelling in secluded places

BP 1.44.5

व्रतोपयुक्तसर्वाङ्गा धार्मिकाः पापभीरवः ।
निर्ममा निरहङ्कारा दानशूरा दयापराः ॥

Those whose whole being is devoted to vows, who are righteous, fear sin, are without possessiveness and ego, heroic in giving, and devoted to compassion.

व्रतोपयुक्तसर्वाङ्गाः (vrata-upayukta-sarvāṅgāḥ) — every limb devoted to vows;
धार्मिकाः (dhārmikāḥ) — righteous;
पापभीरवः (pāpa-bhīravaḥ) — fearing sin;
निर्ममाः निरहङ्काराः (nirmamāḥ nirahaṅkārāḥ) — without possessiveness or ego;
दानशूराः (dāna-śūrāḥ) — heroic in generosity;
दयापराः (dayā-parāḥ) — devoted to compassion

BP 1.44.6

सत्यब्रह्मविदः शान्ता सर्वशास्त्रेषु निष्ठिताः ।
सर्वलोकहितोपायप्रवृत्तेन स्वयंभुवा ॥

They know truth and Brahman, are peaceful, and are grounded in all the scriptures. The standard for the welfare of all worlds was made by the Self Born.

सत्यब्रह्मविदः (satya-brahma-vidaḥ) — knowers of truth and Brahman;
शान्ताः (śāntāḥ) — peaceful;
सर्वशास्त्रेषु निष्ठिताः (sarva-śāstreṣu niṣṭhitāḥ) — grounded in all śāstras;
सर्वलोकहितोपायप्रवृत्तेन (sarva-loka-hita-upāya-pravṛttena) — acting for welfare of every world;
स्वयंभुवा (svayambhuvā) — by the Self-born

BP 1.44.7

वागीश्वरेण देवेन नाभेयेन भवच्छिदा ।
ब्रह्मणा कृतमर्यादास्त एवं ब्राह्मणाः स्मृताः ॥

By Brahmā, the divine Lord of Speech, born from the navel, the cutter of worldly existence. Those who follow this standard are remembered as Brahmins.

वागीश्वरेण देवेन (vāgīśvareṇa devena) — by divine lord of speech;
नाभेयेन (nābheyena) — by Nābheya;
भवच्छिदा (bhava-cchidā) — cutter of becoming;
ब्रह्मणा कृतमर्यादाः (brahmaṇā kṛta-maryādāḥ) — bounded by Brahmā’s standard;
ते ब्राह्मणाः स्मृताः (te brāhmaṇāḥ smṛtāḥ) — they are remembered as brāhmaṇas

BP 1.44.8

महातपोधनैरार्यैः सर्वसत्त्वाभयप्रदैः ।
सर्वलोकहितार्थाय निपुणं सुप्रतिष्ठितम् ॥

It was skilfully and firmly established by noble ones rich in austerity, granting fearlessness to all beings, for the welfare of all worlds.

महातपोधनैः आर्यैः (mahā-tapo-dhanaiḥ āryaiḥ) — by noble ones rich in austerity;
सर्वसत्त्वाभयप्रदैः (sarva-sattva-abhaya-pradaiḥ) — giving fearlessness to all beings;
सर्वलोकहितार्थाय (sarva-loka-hita-arthāya) — for welfare of every world;
निपुणम् सुप्रतिष्ठितम् (nipuṇam supratiṣṭhitam) — skilfully and firmly established

BP 1.44.9

वृहत्त्वाद्भगवान्ब्रह्मा नाभेयस्तस्य ये जनाः ।
भक्त्यासक्ताः प्रपन्नाश्च ब्राह्मणास्ते प्रकीर्तिताः ॥

Because of the greatness of the Blessed Brahmā, born from the navel, those people devoted and surrendered to him in faith are proclaimed Brahmins.

बृहत्त्वात् भगवान् ब्रह्मा (bṛhattvāt bhagavān brahmā) — because of greatness the Blessed is Brahmā;
नाभेयः (nābheyaḥ) — Nābheya;
तस्य ये जनाः (tasya ye janāḥ) — those people of his;
भक्त्यासक्ताः प्रपन्नाः च (bhakti-āsaktāḥ prapannāḥ ca) — attached and surrendered through devotion;
ब्राह्मणाः ते प्रकीर्तिताः (brāhmaṇāḥ te prakīrtitāḥ) — they are proclaimed brāhmaṇas

BP 1.44.10

क्षत्रियास्तु क्षतत्राणाद्वैश्या वार्ताप्रवेशनात् ।
ये तु श्रुतेर्द्रुतिं प्राप्ताः शूद्रास्तेनेह कीर्तिताः ॥

Kshatriyas are named for protecting the injured, Vaishyas for entering trade. Those who have reached the decline of sacred study are here called Shudras.

क्षत्रियाः क्षतत्राणात् (kṣatriyāḥ kṣata-trāṇāt) — kṣatriyas from protection against injury;
वैश्याः वार्ताप्रवेशनात् (vaiśyāḥ vārtā-praveśanāt) — vaiśyas from entering livelihood;
श्रुतेः द्रुतिम् प्राप्ताः (śruteḥ drutim prāptāḥ) — reaching weakened sacred learning;
शूद्राः ते इह कीर्तिताः (śūdrāḥ te iha kīrtitāḥ) — they are here called śūdras

BP 1.44.11

ये चाचाररताः प्राहुर्ब्राह्मण्यं ब्रह्मवादिनः ।
ते तु फलं प्रशंसन्ति यत्सदा मनसेप्सितम् ॥

Those speakers of Brahman who say Brahminhood belongs to people devoted to proper conduct praise the fruit always desired by the mind.

ये आचाररताः (ye ācāra-ratāḥ) — those devoted to conduct;
ब्राह्मण्यम् प्राहुः (brāhmaṇyam prāhuḥ) — declare brāhmaṇa status;
ब्रह्मवादिनः (brahma-vādinaḥ) — speakers of Brahman;
फलम् प्रशंसन्ति (phalam praśaṃsanti) — praise the fruit;
यत् सदा मनसा ईप्सितम् (yat sadā manasā īpsitam) — always desired by mind

BP 1.44.12

क्षमा दमो दया दानं सत्यं शौचं धृतिर्घृणा ।
मार्दवार्जवसन्तोषानहङ्कारतपःशमाः. ॥

Forbearance, self restraint, compassion, generosity, truth, purity, firmness, moral shame, gentleness, sincerity, contentment, absence of ego, austerity, and calmness.

क्षमा (kṣamā) — forbearance;
दमः (damaḥ) — restraint;
दया (dayā) — compassion;
दानम् (dānam) — generosity;
सत्यम् (satyam) — truth;
शौचम् (śaucam) — purity;
धृतिः (dhṛtiḥ) — firmness;
घृणा (ghṛṇā) — moral shame;
मार्दवम् (mārdavam) — gentleness;
आर्जवम् (ārjavam) — sincerity;
सन्तोषः (santoṣaḥ) — contentment;
अनहङ्कारः (anahaṅkāraḥ) — humility;
तपः (tapaḥ) — austerity;
शमः (śamaḥ) — tranquillity

BP 1.44.13

धर्मो ज्ञानमपैशुन्यं ब्रह्मचर्यममूढता ।
ध्यानमास्तिक्यमद्वेषो वैराग्यं च शमात्मता ॥

Righteousness, knowledge, absence of slander, celibacy, freedom from delusion, meditation, faith, absence of hatred, dispassion, and serenity of mind.

धर्मः (dharmaḥ) — dharma;
ज्ञानम् (jñānam) — knowledge;
अपैशुन्यम् (apaiśunyam) — absence of slander;
ब्रह्मचर्यम् (brahmacaryam) — celibate discipline;
अमूढता (amūḍhatā) — freedom from delusion;
ध्यानम् (dhyānam) — meditation;
आस्तिक्यम् (āstikyam) — faith;
अद्वेषः (adveṣaḥ) — absence of hatred;
वैराग्यम् (vairāgyam) — dispassion;
शमात्मता (śamātmatā) — tranquil self

BP 1.44.14

पापभीरुत्वमस्तेयममात्सर्यमतृष्णता ।
नैःसङ्ग्यं गुरुशुश्रूषा मनोवाक्काय संयमः ॥

Fear of sin, nonstealing, freedom from envy and craving, detachment, service to the teacher, and restraint of mind, speech, and body.

पापभीरुत्वम् (pāpa-bhīrutvam) — fear of sin;
अस्तेयम् (asteyam) — non-stealing;
अमात्सर्यम् (amātsaryam) — freedom from envy;
अतृष्णता (atṛṣṇatā) — freedom from craving;
नैःसङ्ग्यम् (naiḥsaṅgyam) — nonattachment;
गुरुशुश्रूषा (guru-śuśrūṣā) — service to teacher;
मनोवाक्कायसंयमः (mano-vāk-kāya-saṃyamaḥ) — restraint of mind speech and body

BP 1.44.15

य एवम्भूतमाचारमनुतिष्ठन्ति मानवाः ।
ब्राह्मण्यं पुष्कलं तेषां नित्यमेव प्रवर्धते ॥

The Brahmin character of human beings who follow conduct of this kind continually increases.

ये मानवाः (ye mānavāḥ) — those human beings;
एवम्भूतम् आचारम् (evambhūtam ācāram) — conduct of this kind;
अनुतिष्ठन्ति (anutiṣṭhanti) — practise;
ब्राह्मण्यम् पुष्कलम् तेषाम् (brāhmaṇyam puṣkalam teṣām) — their abundant brāhmaṇa character;
नित्यम् एव प्रवर्धते (nityam eva pravardhate) — continually increases

BP 1.44.16

ते स्वमतास्वादलब्धवर्णाचारा महौजसः ।
सर्वशास्त्राविरोधेन पवित्रीकृतमानसाः ॥

They are mighty, obtaining the conduct of their varna through their own teaching, and their minds are purified without contradiction of any scripture.

स्वमतास्वादलब्धवर्णाचाराः (sva-mata-āsvāda-labdha-varṇa-ācārāḥ) — gaining varṇa conduct through their teaching;
महौजसः (mahaujasaḥ) — mighty;
सर्वशास्त्राविरोधेन (sarva-śāstra-avirodhena) — without contradicting any śāstra;
पवित्रीकृतमानसाः (pavitrī-kṛta-mānasāḥ) — minds made pure

BP 1.44.17

सज्जनाभिमताः प्राज्ञाः पुराणागमपण्डिताः ।
गीतगीतागमाचाराः स्मृतिकाराः पठन्ति च ॥

Approved by good people, wise, learned in sacred histories and teachings, they follow the conduct of the Gita and recite the remembered laws.

सज्जनाभिमताः (sajjana-abhimatāḥ) — approved by good people;
प्राज्ञाः (prājñāḥ) — wise;
पुराणागमपण्डिताः (purāṇa-āgama-paṇḍitāḥ) — learned in Purāṇa and Āgama;
गीतगीतागमाचाराः (gīta-gīta-āgama-ācārāḥ) — practising teachings sung in texts;
स्मृतिकाराः पठन्ति (smṛti-kārāḥ paṭhanti) — recite works of lawgivers

BP 1.44.18

मन्वन्तरेषु सर्वेषु चतुर्युगविभागशः ।
वर्णाश्रमाचारकृतं कर्म सिद्ध्यत्यनुत्तमम् ॥

In all ages of Manu, according to the divisions of the four ages, action performed according to the conduct of varna and the stages of life attains unsurpassed success.

मन्वन्तरेषु सर्वेषु (manvantareṣu sarveṣu) — in every Manvantara;
चतुर्युगविभागशः (catur-yuga-vibhāgaśaḥ) — according to four-yuga division;
वर्णाश्रमाचारकृतम् कर्म (varṇa-āśrama-ācāra-kṛtam karma) — action produced by varṇa-āśrama conduct;
सिद्ध्यति अनुत्तमम् (siddhyati anuttamam) — reaches unsurpassed fulfilment

BP 1.44.19

संसिद्धायां तु वार्तायां ततस्तेषां स्वयं प्रभुः ।
मर्यादां स्थापयामास यथारब्धं परस्परम् ॥

When livelihoods had been established, the Lord himself set their respective boundaries according to how they had begun.

संसिद्धायाम् वार्तायाम् (saṃsiddhāyām vārtāyām) — when livelihood was established;
ततः स्वयं प्रभुः (tataḥ svayaṃ prabhuḥ) — then the Lord himself;
मर्यादाम् स्थापयामास (maryādām sthāpayāmāsa) — fixed boundaries;
यथारब्धम् परस्परम् (yathā-ārabdham parasparam) — according to respective undertakings

BP 1.44.20

ये वै परिगृहीतारस्तेषां सत्त्वबलाधिकाः ।
इतरेषां क्षतत्राणान्स्थापयामास क्षत्रियान् ॥

He established as Kshatriyas those among them who were stronger in force and vigor, to protect the others from injury.

ये परिगृहीतारः (ye parigṛhītāraḥ) — those accepting responsibility;
तेषाम् सत्त्वबलाधिकाः (teṣām sattva-bala-adhikāḥ) — stronger in vitality;
इतरेषाम् क्षतत्राणान् (itareṣām kṣata-trāṇān) — protectors of others from injury;
स्थापयामास क्षत्रियान् (sthāpayāmāsa kṣatriyān) — established as kṣatriyas

BP 1.44.21

उपतिष्ठन्ति ये तान्वै याचन्तो नर्मदाः सदा ।
सत्यब्रह्म सदाभूतं वदन्तो ब्राह्मणास्तु ते ॥

Those who attend to them, continually asking with gentle speech and speaking truth and Brahman, are Brahmins.

उपतिष्ठन्ति ये तान् (upatiṣṭhanti ye tān) — those who attend them;
याचन्तः नर्मदाः सदा (yācantaḥ narmadāḥ sadā) — continually asking and speaking pleasantly;
सत्यब्रह्म सदाभूतम् (satya-brahma sadā-bhūtam) — truth and ever-existent Brahman;
वदन्तः ब्राह्मणाः ते (vadantaḥ brāhmaṇāḥ te) — those speakers are brāhmaṇas

BP 1.44.22

ये चान्येप्यबलास्तेषां वैश्यकर्मणि संस्थिताः ।
कीलानि नाशयन्ति स्म पृथिव्यां प्रागतन्द्रिताः ॥
वैश्यानेव तु तानाह कीनाशान्वृत्तिमाश्रितान् ॥

Those others who were weaker were placed in the work of Vaishyas. Diligently they broke up the clods of earth. He called them Vaishyas, ploughmen who had taken up a livelihood.

अन्ये अपि अबलाः (anye api abalāḥ) — others who were weaker;
वैश्यकर्मणि संस्थिताः (vaiśya-karmaṇi saṃsthitāḥ) — remaining in vaiśya work;
कीलानि नाशयन्ति पृथिव्याम् (kīlāni nāśayanti pṛthivyām) — breaking earth-clods;
प्रागतन्द्रिताः (prāg-atandritāḥ) — tirelessly;
वैश्यान् तान् आह (vaiśyān tān āha) — called them vaiśyas;
कीनाशान् वृत्तिम् आश्रितान् (kīnāśān vṛttim āśritān) — ploughmen dependent on livelihood

BP 1.44.23

शोचन्तश्व द्रवन्तश्च परिचर्यासु ये नराः ।
निस्तेजसोऽल्पवीर्याश्च शूद्रांस्तानब्रवीत्तु सः ॥

Those men who grieved and hurried about in service, lacking splendor and having little strength, he called Shudras.

शोचन्तः द्रवन्तः च (śocantaḥ dravantaḥ ca) — grieving and hurrying;
परिचर्यासु ये नराः (paricaryāsu ye narāḥ) — men in service;
निस्तेजसः (nistejasaḥ) — lacking brilliance;
अल्पवीर्याः (alpa-vīryāḥ) — of little strength;
शूद्रान् तान् अब्रवीत् (śūdrān tān abravīt) — he called them śūdras

BP 1.44.24

ब्राह्मणक्षत्रियविशां शूद्राणां च परस्परम् ।
कर्माणि प्रविभक्तानि स्यभावप्रभवैर्गुणैः ॥

The duties of Brahmins, Kshatriyas, Vaishyas, and Shudras were divided according to the qualities arising from their own nature.

ब्राह्मणक्षत्रियविशाम् शूद्राणाम् (brāhmaṇa-kṣatriya-viśām śūdrāṇām) — of brāhmaṇas kṣatriyas vaiśyas and śūdras;
कर्माणि प्रविभक्तानि (karmāṇi pravibhaktāni) — duties divided;
परस्परम् (parasparam) — from one another;
स्वभावप्रभवैः गुणैः (svabhāva-prabhavaiḥ guṇaiḥ) — by qualities arising from nature

BP 1.44.25

शमस्तपो दमः शौचं क्षांतिरार्जवमेव च ।
ज्ञानं विज्ञानमास्तिक्यं ब्रह्मकर्म स्वभावजम् ॥

Tranquillity, austerity, restraint, purity, patience, straightforwardness, knowledge, realization, and faith are the work of a Brahmin, arising from his nature.

शमः (śamaḥ) — tranquillity;
तपः (tapaḥ) — austerity;
दमः (damaḥ) — restraint;
शौचम् (śaucam) — purity;
क्षान्तिः (kṣāntiḥ) — patience;
आर्जवम् (ārjavam) — sincerity;
ज्ञानम् विज्ञानम् (jñānam vijñānam) — knowledge and realization;
आस्तिक्यम् (āstikyam) — faith;
ब्रह्मकर्म स्वभावजम् (brahma-karma svabhāva-jam) — brāhmaṇa work born of nature

BP 1.44.26

शौर्यं तेजो धृतिर्दाक्ष्यं युद्धे चाप्यपलायनम् ।
दानमीश्वरभावश्च क्षात्रं कर्म स्वभावजम् ॥

Heroism, vigor, firmness, skill, not fleeing in battle, generosity, and a disposition to rule are the work of a Kshatriya, arising from his nature.

शौर्यम् (śauryam) — heroism;
तेजः (tejaḥ) — brilliance;
धृतिः (dhṛtiḥ) — firmness;
दाक्ष्यम् (dākṣyam) — skill;
युद्धे अपलायनम् (yuddhe apalāyanam) — not fleeing in battle;
दानम् (dānam) — generosity;
ईश्वरभावः (īśvara-bhāvaḥ) — governing disposition;
क्षात्रम् कर्म स्वभावजम् (kṣātram karma svabhāva-jam) — kṣatriya work born of nature

BP 1.44.27

कृषिगोरक्षवाणिज्यं वैश्यकर्म स्वभावजम् ।
परिचर्यात्मकं कर्म शूद्रस्यापि स्वभावजम् ॥

Agriculture, protection of cattle, and trade are the work of a Vaishya, arising from his nature. Service is likewise the work of a Shudra, arising from his nature.

कृषिगोरक्षवाणिज्यम् (kṛṣi-go-rakṣa-vāṇijyam) — agriculture cattle protection and trade;
वैश्यकर्म स्वभावजम् (vaiśya-karma svabhāva-jam) — vaiśya work born of nature;
परिचर्यात्मकम् कर्म (paricaryā-ātmakam karma) — work consisting of service;
शूद्रस्य अपि स्वभावजम् (śūdrasya api svabhāva-jam) — also born of śūdra nature

BP 1.44.28

योगस्तपो दया दानं सत्यं धर्मश्रुतिर्घृणा ।
ज्ञानं विज्ञानमास्तिक्यमेतद्ब्राह्मणलक्षणम् ॥

Yoga, austerity, compassion, generosity, truth, learning of righteousness, moral shame, knowledge, realization, and faith are the marks of a Brahmin.

योगः (yogaḥ) — yoga;
तपः (tapaḥ) — austerity;
दया (dayā) — compassion;
दानम् (dānam) — generosity;
सत्यम् (satyam) — truth;
धर्मश्रुतिः (dharma-śrutiḥ) — sacred learning;
घृणा (ghṛṇā) — moral shame;
ज्ञानम् विज्ञानम् (jñānam vijñānam) — knowledge and realization;
आस्तिक्यम् (āstikyam) — faith;
ब्राह्मणलक्षणम् (brāhmaṇa-lakṣaṇam) — mark of a brāhmaṇa

BP 1.44.29

शिखा ज्ञानमयी यस्य पवित्रं च तपोमयम् ।
ब्राह्मण्यं पुष्कलं तस्य मनुः स्वायम्भुवोऽब्रवीत् ॥

He whose topknot is made of knowledge and whose sacred thread is made of austerity has abundant Brahminhood, declared Svāyambhuva Manu.

शिखा ज्ञानमयी यस्य (śikhā jñānamayī yasya) — whose topknot consists of knowledge;
पवित्रम् तपोमयम् (pavitram tapomayam) — sacred thread consists of austerity;
ब्राह्मण्यम् पुष्कलम् तस्य (brāhmaṇyam puṣkalam tasya) — his brāhmaṇa character is abundant;
मनुः स्वायम्भुवः अब्रवीत् (manuḥ svāyambhuvaḥ abravīt) — Svāyambhuva Manu declared

BP 1.44.30

यत्र वा तत्र वा वर्णे उत्तमाधममध्यमाः ।
निवृत्तः पापकर्मेभ्यो ब्राह्मणः स विधीयते ॥

In whatever varna one may be, high, low, or middle, the person who has turned away from sinful actions is declared a Brahmin.

यत्र वा तत्र वा वर्णे (yatra vā tatra vā varṇe) — in whatever varṇa;
उत्तमाधममध्यमाः (uttama-adhama-madhyamāḥ) — highest lowest or middle;
निवृत्तः पापकर्मेभ्यः (nivṛttaḥ pāpa-karmebhyaḥ) — withdrawn from sinful deeds;
ब्राह्मणः सः विधीयते (brāhmaṇaḥ saḥ vidhīyate) — he is prescribed as brāhmaṇa

BP 1.44.31

शूद्रोऽपि शीलसम्पन्नो ब्राह्मणादधिको भवेत् ।
ब्राह्मणो विगताचारः शूद्राद्धीनतरो भवेत् ॥

Even a Shudra endowed with good character may be superior to a Brahmin. A Brahmin devoid of conduct may be lower than a Shudra.

शूद्रः अपि शीलसम्पन्नः (śūdraḥ api śīla-sampannaḥ) — even śūdra endowed with character;
ब्राह्मणात् अधिकः भवेत् (brāhmaṇāt adhikaḥ bhavet) — may be superior to brāhmaṇa;
ब्राह्मणः विगताचारः (brāhmaṇaḥ vigata-ācāraḥ) — brāhmaṇa devoid of conduct;
शूद्रात् हीनतरः भवेत् (śūdrāt hīnataraḥ bhavet) — may be lower than śūdra

BP 1.44.32

न सुरां सन्धयेद्यस्तु आपणेषु गृहेषु च ।
न विक्रीणाति च तथा सच्छूद्रो हि स उच्यते ॥

One who does not prepare liquor in shops or homes, and likewise does not sell it, is called a good Shudra.

न सुराम् सन्धयेत् (na surām sandhayet) — does not prepare liquor;
आपणेषु गृहेषु च (āpaṇeṣu gṛheṣu ca) — in shops or homes;
न विक्रीणाति (na vikrīṇāti) — does not sell;
सच्छूद्रः सः उच्यते (sac-chūdraḥ saḥ ucyate) — he is called a good śūdra

BP 1.44.33

यद्येका स्फुटमेव जातिरपरा कृत्यात्परं भेदिनी ।
यद्वा व्याहृतिरेकतामधिगता यच्चान्यधर्मं ययौ ॥
एकैकाखिलभावभेदनिधनोत्पत्तिस्थितिव्यापिनी ।
किं नासौ प्रतिपत्तिगोचरपथं यायाद्विभक्त्या नृणाम् ॥

If one human birth is plainly single, while another distinction arises from action, if speech has attained unity yet gone to another usage, and if one reality pervades the origin, continuance, and dissolution of every differentiated state, why should it not enter the path of human understanding through distinction.

यदि एका स्फुटम् एव जातिः (yadi ekā sphuṭam eva jātiḥ) — if manifestly one human kind;
अपरा कृत्यात् परम् भेदिनी (aparā kṛtyāt param bhedinī) — another division only from action;
व्याहृतिः एकताम् अधिगता (vyāhṛtiḥ ekatām adhigatā) — speech attained unity;
अन्यधर्मम् ययौ (anya-dharmam yayau) — took other modes;
एकैका अखिलभावभेद (ekaikā akhila-bhāva-bheda) — one reality amid every differentiated state;
निधन-उत्पत्ति-स्थिति-व्यापिनी (nidhana-utpatti-sthiti-vyāpinī) — pervading destruction arising and duration;
किम् न असौ प्रतिपत्तिगोचरपथम् यायात् (kim na asau pratipatti-gocara-patham yāyāt) — why should it not become accessible to understanding;
विभक्त्या नृणाम् (vibhaktyā nṛṇām) — by human analysis
