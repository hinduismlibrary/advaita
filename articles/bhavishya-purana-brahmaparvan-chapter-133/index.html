Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 133 — The Solar Image and Sūrya as All Deities

BP 1.133.1

नारद उवाच।
ततोऽधिवासनं कुर्याद्विधिदृष्टेन कर्मणा।
ऐशान्यां दिशि वै कुर्यादधिवासनमण्डपम् ॥

Nārada said: Then one should perform the preliminary consecration according to the prescribed rite, and make the preparation pavilion in the northeast.

BP 1.133.2

चतुस्तोरणसम्पन्नं सर्वाभरणसंयुतम्।
दिशासु विदिशास्वेव पताकाभिस्तु भूषितम् ॥

It should have four gateways, be furnished with all ornaments, and be decorated with banners in the cardinal and intermediate directions.

BP 1.133.3

आग्नेय्यां दिशि रक्ताः स्युः कृष्णाः स्युर्याम्यनैर्ऋते।
श्वेता दिश्यपरस्यां तु वायव्यामेव पाण्डुरा ॥

In the southeast the banners should be red; in the south and southwest, black. In the west they should be white, and in the northwest pale.

BP 1.133.4

चित्रा चोत्तरपार्श्वे तु पीता पूर्वोत्तरे तथा।
श्रियमायुर्जयं चैव बलं यशो यदूत्तम ॥

In the north they should be variegated, and in the northeast yellow. O best of the Yadus, they give prosperity, long life, victory, strength, and fame.

BP 1.133.5

ददाति सा वीर कृता सम्पदर्धे न संशयः।
हिताय सर्वलोकानां मृण्मयी प्रतिमा भवेत् ॥

Made in this way, it surely gives abundance and increase, O hero. An image of clay should be made for the welfare of all worlds.

BP 1.133.6

सुभिक्षक्षेमदा नित्यं सर्वा मणिमयीकृता।
गाङ्गेया पुष्टिदा रौप्या स्याद्वै कीर्तिप्रवर्तिनी ॥

An image made entirely of jewels always gives abundance and security. A golden one gives nourishment, and a silver one advances fame.

BP 1.133.7

प्रजावृद्धिं ताम्रमयीं कुर्यान्नित्यमसंशयः।
भूमेर्लाभं तु विपुलं कुर्यादश्ममयी सदा ॥

One should surely make a copper image for increase of offspring. A stone image always gives great gain of land.

BP 1.133.8

प्रधानपुरुषं हन्ति त्रपुलोहमयी सदा।
सर्वदेवमयस्यैवमर्चा कुर्यात्प्रयत्नतः ॥

An image of tin always destroys the principal person. One should carefully worship the image as consisting of all the gods.

BP 1.133.9

साम्ब उवाच।
सर्वदेवमयत्वं हि ब्रूहि मे भास्करस्य तु।
सर्वदेवमयो ह्येष कथं नारद कथ्यते ॥

Sāmba said: Tell me how Bhāskara consists of all the gods; how is he said, O Nārada, to be all the gods?

BP 1.133.10

नारद उवाच।
साधु साम्ब महाबाहो शृणु मे परमं वचः।
बुधसोमौ स्मृतौ नेत्रे ललाटे चेश्वरः स्थितः ॥

Nārada said: Well asked, Sāmba of mighty arms; hear my supreme words. Mercury and the Moon are held to be his eyes, and Śiva stands on his forehead.

BP 1.133.11

सुरज्येष्ठः शिरस्तस्य कपालेऽस्य बृहस्पतिः।
एकादशे तथा रुद्राः कण्ठमस्य समाश्रिताः ॥

Indra, chief of the gods, is on his head; Jupiter is in his skull. The eleven Rudras abide in his throat.

BP 1.133.12

नक्षत्राणि ग्रहाश्चैव दशनेषु समाश्रिताः।
धर्माधर्मौ च देवस्य ओष्ठसम्पुटके स्थितौ ॥

The constellations and planets abide in his teeth; righteousness and unrighteousness rest in the two parts of the god’s lips.

BP 1.133.13

सर्वशास्त्रमयी देवी जिह्वायां च सरस्वती।
दिशश्च विदिशश्चैव सर्वाः श्रोत्रेषु संस्थिताः ॥

The goddess Sarasvatī, embodiment of all scriptures, is in his tongue; all the directions and intermediate directions are situated in his ears.

BP 1.133.14

ब्रह्मेन्द्रौ तालुदेशे तु स्थितौ देवैश्च पूजितौ।
आदित्या द्वादश विभोर्भ्रूर्मध्ये समाश्रिताः ॥

Brahmā and Indra, worshipped by the gods, stand at his palate; the twelve Ādityas abide between the brows of the Lord.

BP 1.133.15

ऋषयो रोमकूपेषु समुद्रा जठरे स्थिताः।
यक्षकिन्नरगन्धर्वाः पिशाचा दानवास्तथा ॥

The sages are in his hair pores, and the oceans in his belly; nature spirits, celestial musicians, heavenly singers, ghosts, and demons are there as well.

BP 1.133.16

राक्षसाश्च गणाः सर्वे हृदये स्युः स्थिताः रवेः।
नद्यो बाहुगताश्चैव नगाः कक्षान्तरे स्थिताः ॥

All the hosts of demons abide in the Sun’s heart; rivers extend along his arms, and mountains stand between his sides.

BP 1.133.17

पृष्ठमध्ये स्थितो मेरुः स्तनयोरन्तरे कुजः।
तस्य पुत्रो धर्मराजः स्थितो वै नाभिमण्डले ॥

Mount Meru is situated in the middle of his back; Mars is between his breasts. His son, King Dharma, is situated in the region of his navel.

BP 1.133.18

कटिदेशे पृथिव्याद्या लिङ्गे सृष्टिः समाश्रिता।
जानुनी चाश्विनीदेवावूरू तस्याचला स्मृताः ॥

The Earth and the rest are at his waist; creation rests in his penis. The two Aśvin gods are at his knees, and his thighs are said to be the mountains.

BP 1.133.19

सप्त पाताललोकास्तु नखमध्ये समाश्रिताः।
ससागरवना पृथ्वी पादमध्येऽस्य वर्तते ॥

The seven netherworlds abide within his nails; the Earth, with its oceans and forests, is present at the middle of his feet.

BP 1.133.20

देवः कालाग्निरुद्रो यो दन्तान्तेषु समाश्रितः।
सर्वदेवमयो भानुः सर्वदेवात्मकस्तथा ॥

Rudra, the fire of time, abides at the tips of his teeth. The Sun consists of all the gods and is the very self of every god.

BP 1.133.21

व्यंगेषु वायवश्चैव लोकालोकं चराचरम्।
व्याप्तं कर्मशरीरेण वायुना तस्य वै विभोः ॥

The winds are in his joints; Lokāloka, all that moves and does not move, is pervaded by the wind that is the body of that mighty Lord.

BP 1.133.22

स एष भगवानर्को भूतानुग्रहणे स्थितः।
एतत्ते परमं ज्ञानमेतत्ते परमं पदम् ॥

That blessed Sun stands for the favor of living beings. This is the highest knowledge; this is the highest state.

BP 1.133.23

तस्य स्थानविभागेन प्रतिमास्थापनं यथा।
तत्ते सर्वं प्रवक्ष्यामि यथोक्तं ब्रह्मणा पुरा ॥

I shall tell you all how the image is to be installed according to the divisions of his abodes, as Brahmā declared of old.
