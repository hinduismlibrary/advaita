Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 42 — Conduct, Rites, and Brahmin Status

BP 1.42.1

ब्रह्मोवाच
अपरैश्च सदाचारयोगयुक्तैर्मनीषिभिः ।
यदकारि महासत्त्वैः सुभाषितमिदं शृणु ॥

Brahmā said, Hear this well spoken teaching composed by other thoughtful, great souled people joined with good conduct and yoga.

BP 1.42.2

बहुवनस्पतिशङ्खपिपीलिकाभ्रमरवारणजातिमुदाहरन्।
गतिषु कर्ममितो नटवत्सदा भ्रमति जन्तुरलब्धसुदर्शनः ॥

Citing births among many plants, conches, ants, bees, and elephants, the creature wanders constantly through destinies measured by its actions, like an actor, without attaining true vision.

BP 1.42.3

रूपैश्वर्यज्ञानकुलैर्विभवैर्वर्मितो भूत्वा धर्मपथं चेद्विजहासि ।
न वक्ष्ये व्रजन्भुवनानि त्वमटिष्यंस्तस्मादभिभस्मीभूते मद आत्मनः ॥

Armored with beauty, power, knowledge, lineage, and wealth, if you abandon the path of righteousness, I will not tell how you will wander through the worlds. Therefore let pride in self be burned to ashes.

BP 1.42.4

जातिकुलरूपवयोवर्णानेकश्रुतमदान्धाः क्लीबाः ।
परत्र चेह च हितमप्यर्थं न पश्यन्ति ॥

People blinded by pride in birth, family, beauty, age, varna, and extensive learning are powerless. They do not see what is beneficial here or hereafter.

BP 1.42.5

ज्ञात्वा भवपरिवर्ते जातीनां कोटिशतसहस्रेषु ।
हीनोत्तममध्यत्वं को जातिमदं बुधः कुर्यात् ॥

Knowing that in the cycle of existence there are hundreds of thousands of crores of births, what wise person would take pride in being high, low, or middle by birth.

BP 1.42.6

नैकाञ्जातिविशेषानिन्द्रियनिवृत्तिपूर्वकान्सर्वान् ।
कर्मवशाद्गच्छत्यत्र कस्यैका शाश्वती जातिः ॥

A being passes through every kind of birth, preceded by the loss of one set of senses, under the force of action. Whose birth is eternal and single.

BP 1.42.7

विद्वत्सदसि योऽप्याह संस्काराद्ब्राह्मणो भवन् ।
न्यायज्ञैः स निराकार्यो वाक्यैर्न्यायानुसारिभिः ॥

Even if someone in an assembly of scholars says that one becomes a Brahmin through consecratory rites, those who know logic should refute him with statements that follow reason.

BP 1.42.8

गर्भाधानं पुंसवनं सीमन्तोन्नयनं तथा ।
जातकर्म नामकर्म तथान्नप्राशनं च वै ॥

The rites are conception, the rite for a male child, parting the hair of a pregnant woman, the birth rite, naming, and the first feeding of solid food.

BP 1.42.9

चूडोपनयनं चास्य व्रतादेशस्तथैव च ।
समावर्तनमप्यन्यत्पाणिग्रहणमेव च ॥

They also include tonsure, initiation, instruction in vows, return from studentship, and taking a hand in marriage.

BP 1.42.10

इत्येवमादिसंस्कारविधानैर्येऽतिसंस्कृताः ।
त एव ब्राह्मणा येषां नैरन्तर्येण कामनाः ॥

Those greatly refined by prescriptions for these and similar rites are Brahmins whose desires for them are uninterrupted.

BP 1.42.11

यस्माद्वै ब्राह्मणा जाता ब्राह्मणैः कृतसंस्कृतैः ।
नायुः शक्तिर्हि कान्त्यादिविशेषो विद्यते स्फुटः ॥

Since Brahmins are born from Brahmins who underwent rites, no clear distinction in lifespan, strength, beauty, or the like is found.

BP 1.42.12

तौ वा ब्राह्मणगात्रोत्थौ संस्कृतासंस्कृतौ नरौ ।
इष्टानिष्टाप्त्यनाप्तिभ्यां न भिद्येते परस्परम् ॥

Two men born from Brahmin bodies, one consecrated and one unconsecrated, do not differ from one another in obtaining or failing to obtain what is desired or undesired.

BP 1.42.13

ज्ञानाध्ययनमीमांसा नियमेन्द्रियनिग्रहैः ।
विना संस्कारयोगेऽपि पुंसः शूद्रान्न भिन्नता ॥

Even when joined with rites, a man is not distinct from a Shudra without knowledge, study, inquiry, disciplined observance, and control of the senses.

BP 1.42.14

संस्कारः क्रियमाणश्च न शूद्रे च प्रवर्तते ।
संस्कृताङ्गश्च पापेभ्यो न पश्यति निवर्तते ॥

The rite performed does not operate in a Shudra. Yet a person whose limbs are consecrated is not seen to turn away from sinful acts.

BP 1.42.15

विलासिनीभुजंगादिजनवन्मदविह्वलाः ।
व्यामुह्यन्ति सदाचाराद्ब्राह्मणत्वात्पतन्ति च ॥

Overwhelmed by pride like people infatuated with courtesans and serpents, they become confused, depart from good conduct, and fall from Brahminhood.

BP 1.42.16

संस्कृतोऽपि दुराचारो नरकं याति मानवः ।
निःसंस्कारः सदाचारो भवेद्विप्रोत्तमः सदा ॥

A consecrated person of bad conduct goes to hell. A person without consecration who has good conduct may always be an excellent Brahmin.

BP 1.42.17

मन्त्रपूतात्मसंस्कारयुक्तोऽपि प्लवते न तु ।
ब्राह्मण्यादविकल्पं स पश्चाद्दुश्चरितो नरः ॥

Even one joined with consecrations that purify the self by mantra does not remain afloat. A man of bad conduct afterward is surely removed from Brahminhood.

BP 1.42.18

सामर्थ्यात्पतनं तस्माद्ब्राह्मण्यान्मुच्यते ध्रुवम् ।
दुरनुष्ठानसक्तानां पुंसां पुरुषपुङ्गवैः ॥

Therefore, because of their capacity to fall, men attached to improper observances are certainly released from Brahminhood, as the foremost men have taught.

BP 1.42.19

किं क्वचिद्दृष्टमेवैतत्किं वा स्पर्धाविदत्ययम् ।
तुल्यमुत्सहसे कर्तुमप्यदृष्टं तदा वद ॥

Has this ever been seen anywhere, or is it a distortion born of rivalry. If you venture to assert even what is unseen, then say so.

BP 1.42.20

आचारमनुष्ठिन्तो व्यासादिमुनिसत्तमाः ।
गर्भाधानादिसंस्कारकलापरहिताः स्फुटम् ॥

Vyāsa and other excellent sages practised good conduct while clearly lacking the whole collection of rites beginning with conception.

BP 1.42.21

विप्रोत्तमाः श्रियं प्राप्ताः सर्वलोकनमस्कृताः ।
बहवः कथ्यमाना ये कतिचित्तान्निबोधत ॥

Many became foremost Brahmins, attained wealth, and were honored by all the worlds. Hear a few of them as they are recounted.

BP 1.42.22

जातो व्यासस्तु कैवर्त्याः श्वपाक्याश्च पराशरः ।
शुक्याः शुकः कणादाख्यस्तथोलूक्याः सुतोऽभवत् ॥

Vyāsa was born from a fisherwoman, Parāśara from a woman of dog eating birth, Śuka from a female parrot, and the sage named Kaṇāda became the son of an owl.

BP 1.42.23

मृगीजोथर्षशृङ्गोपि वशिष्ठो गणिकात्मजः ।
मन्दपालो मुनिश्रेष्ठो नाविकापत्यमुच्यते ॥

Ṛṣyaśṛṅga was born from a doe, Vasiṣṭha was the son of a courtesan, and the excellent sage Maṇḍapāla is said to be the offspring of a boatwoman.

BP 1.42.24

माण्डव्यो मुनिराजस्तु मण्डूकीगर्भसम्भवः ।
बहवोऽन्येऽपि विप्रत्वं प्राप्ता ये पूर्ववद्द्विजाः ॥

The king of sages Māṇḍavya was born from the womb of a frog. Many others too attained Brahminhood and became twice born as described before.

BP 1.42.25

यच्चैतच्चारुचरितैरर्च्यमुच्चरितं वचः ।
तद्विचार्याचरन्नुच्चैराचारोपचितद्युतिः ॥

Having considered this excellent statement, honored and spoken by people of good conduct, one should practise it and become radiant through conduct.

BP 1.42.26

हरिणीगर्भसम्भूत ऋष्यशृङ्गो महामुनिः ।
तपसा ब्राह्मणो जातः संस्कारस्तेन कारणम् ॥

The great sage Ṛṣyaśṛṅga, born from the womb of a hind, became a Brahmin through austerity. This was the consecration that caused it.

BP 1.42.27

श्वपाकीगर्भसम्भूतः पिता व्यासस्य पार्थिव ।
तपसा ब्राह्मणो जातः संस्कारस्तेन कारणम् ॥

The father of Vyāsa was born from a woman of dog eating birth, O king. Through austerity he became a Brahmin. That consecration was the cause.

BP 1.42.28

उलूकीगर्भसम्भूतः कणादाख्यो महामुनिः ।
तपसा ब्राह्मणो जातः संस्कारस्तेन कारणम् ॥

The great sage named Kaṇāda, born from the womb of an owl, became a Brahmin through austerity. That consecration was the cause.

BP 1.42.29

गणिकागर्भसम्भूतो वशिष्ठश्च महामुनिः ।
तपसा ब्राह्मणो जातः संस्कारस्तेन कारणम् ॥

The great sage Vasiṣṭha, born from the womb of a courtesan, became a Brahmin through austerity. That consecration was the cause.

BP 1.42.30

नाविकागर्भसम्भूतो मन्दपालो महामुनिः ।
तपसा ब्राह्मणो जातः संस्कारस्तेन कारणम् ॥

The great sage Maṇḍapāla, born from the womb of a boatwoman, became a Brahmin through austerity. That consecration was the cause.

BP 1.42.31

वेदतन्त्रजसंस्कारकलापनिपुणैरपि ।
विद्यातपोधनबलादुत्कृष्टं लभ्यते फलम् ।

Even those skilled in the full array of rites arising from the Veda and sacred teachings obtain the highest fruit through the power of knowledge, austerity, wealth, and strength.

BP 1.42.32

लब्धसंस्कारदेहाश्च महापातकिनो नराः ।
यस्मान्निवर्तते ब्रह्म तस्मात्साङ्केतिकं विदुः ॥

Men whose bodies received the rites may commit great sins. Since Brahminhood departs from them, people know it as a conventional designation.
