Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 42 — Conduct, Rites, and Brahmin Status

BP 1.42.1

ब्रह्मोवाच
अपरैश्च सदाचारयोगयुक्तैर्मनीषिभिः ।
यदकारि महासत्त्वैः सुभाषितमिदं शृणु ॥

Brahmā said, Hear this well spoken teaching composed by other thoughtful, great souled people joined with good conduct and yoga.

ब्रह्मा उवाच (brahmā uvāca) — Brahmā said;
अपरैः मनीषिभिः (aparaiḥ manīṣibhiḥ) — by other thoughtful people;
सदाचार-योग-युक्तैः (sadācāra-yoga-yuktaiḥ) — joined with good conduct and yoga;
महा-सत्त्वैः यत् अकारि (mahā-sattvaiḥ yat akāri) — made by great souls;
सु-भाषितम् इदम् शृणु (su-bhāṣitam idam śṛṇu) — hear this well-spoken teaching

BP 1.42.2

बहुवनस्पतिशङ्खपिपीलिकाभ्रमरवारणजातिमुदाहरन्।
गतिषु कर्ममितो नटवत्सदा भ्रमति जन्तुरलब्धसुदर्शनः ॥

Citing births among many plants, conches, ants, bees, and elephants, the creature wanders constantly through destinies measured by its actions, like an actor, without attaining true vision.

बहु-वनस्पति-शङ्ख-पिपीलिका-भ्रमर-वारण-जातिम् (bahu-vanaspati-śaṅkha-pipīlikā-bhramara-vāraṇa-jātim) — births among plants conches ants bees elephants;
उदाहरन् (udāharan) — citing;
गतिषु कर्म-मितः (gatiṣu karma-mitaḥ) — in destinations measured by karma;
नट-वत् भ्रमति जन्तुः (naṭa-vat bhramati jantuḥ) — creature wanders like actor;
अलब्ध-सु-दर्शनः (alabdha-su-darśanaḥ) — not obtaining true vision

BP 1.42.3

रूपैश्वर्यज्ञानकुलैर्विभवैर्वर्मितो भूत्वा धर्मपथं चेद्विजहासि ।
न वक्ष्ये व्रजन्भुवनानि त्वमटिष्यंस्तस्मादभिभस्मीभूते मद आत्मनः ॥

Armored with beauty, power, knowledge, lineage, and wealth, if you abandon the path of righteousness, I will not tell how you will wander through the worlds. Therefore let pride in self be burned to ashes.

रूप-ऐश्वर्य-ज्ञान-कुलैः विभवैः (rūpa-aiśvarya-jñāna-kulaiḥ vibhavaiḥ) — beauty lordship knowledge lineage wealth;
वर्मितः भूत्वा (varmitaḥ bhūtvā) — becoming armoured;
धर्म-पथम् विजहासि चेत् (dharma-patham vijahāsi cet) — if abandon dharma path;
भुवनानि अटिष्यन् (bhuvanāni aṭiṣyan) — wandering worlds;
तस्मात् मदः आत्मनः भस्मीभूतः (tasmāt madaḥ ātmanaḥ bhasmībhūtaḥ) — therefore self-pride reduced to ash

BP 1.42.4

जातिकुलरूपवयोवर्णानेकश्रुतमदान्धाः क्लीबाः ।
परत्र चेह च हितमप्यर्थं न पश्यन्ति ॥

People blinded by pride in birth, family, beauty, age, varna, and extensive learning are powerless. They do not see what is beneficial here or hereafter.

जाति-कुल-रूप-वयः-वर्ण-अनेक-श्रुत (jāti-kula-rūpa-vayaḥ-varṇa-aneka-śruta) — birth family beauty age colour much learning;
मद-अन्धाः (mada-andhāḥ) — blinded by pride;
क्लीबाः (klībāḥ) — powerless;
परत्र इह च (paratra iha ca) — here and hereafter;
हितम् अर्थम् न पश्यन्ति (hitam artham na paśyanti) — do not see beneficial end

BP 1.42.5

ज्ञात्वा भवपरिवर्ते जातीनां कोटिशतसहस्रेषु ।
हीनोत्तममध्यत्वं को जातिमदं बुधः कुर्यात् ॥

Knowing that in the cycle of existence there are hundreds of thousands of crores of births, what wise person would take pride in being high, low, or middle by birth.

भव-परिवर्ते (bhava-parivarte) — in revolutions of becoming;
जातीनाम् कोटि-शत-सहस्रेषु (jātīnām koṭi-śata-sahasreṣu) — among countless births;
हीन-उत्तम-मध्यत्वम् (hīna-uttama-madhyatvam) — low high middle states;
कः बुधः जाति-मदम् कुर्यात् (kaḥ budhaḥ jāti-madam kuryāt) — what wise person would take birth-pride

BP 1.42.6

नैकाञ्जातिविशेषानिन्द्रियनिवृत्तिपूर्वकान्सर्वान् ।
कर्मवशाद्गच्छत्यत्र कस्यैका शाश्वती जातिः ॥

A being passes through every kind of birth, preceded by the loss of one set of senses, under the force of action. Whose birth is eternal and single.

न एकान् जाति-विशेषान् (na ekān jāti-viśeṣān) — many birth distinctions;
इन्द्रिय-निवृत्ति-पूर्वकान् (indriya-nivṛtti-pūrvakān) — preceded by sensory cessation;
सर्वान् कर्म-वशात् गच्छति (sarvān karma-vaśāt gacchati) — passes through all by karma;
कस्य एका शाश्वती जातिः (kasya ekā śāśvatī jātiḥ) — whose one eternal birth-status

BP 1.42.7

विद्वत्सदसि योऽप्याह संस्काराद्ब्राह्मणो भवन् ।
न्यायज्ञैः स निराकार्यो वाक्यैर्न्यायानुसारिभिः ॥

Even if someone in an assembly of scholars says that one becomes a Brahmin through consecratory rites, those who know logic should refute him with statements that follow reason.

विद्वत्-सदसि यः आह (vidvat-sadasi yaḥ āha) — one who says in learned assembly;
संस्कारात् ब्राह्मणः भवन् (saṃskārāt brāhmaṇaḥ bhavan) — becoming brāhmaṇa through rites;
न्याय-ज्ञैः निराकार्यः (nyāya-jñaiḥ nirākāryaḥ) — to be refuted by logicians;
वाक्यैः न्याय-अनुसारिभिः (vākyaiḥ nyāya-anusāribhiḥ) — with reason-following statements

BP 1.42.8

गर्भाधानं पुंसवनं सीमन्तोन्नयनं तथा ।
जातकर्म नामकर्म तथान्नप्राशनं च वै ॥

The rites are conception, the rite for a male child, parting the hair of a pregnant woman, the birth rite, naming, and the first feeding of solid food.

गर्भाधानम् (garbhādhānam) — impregnation rite;
पुंसवनम् (puṃsavanam) — male-child rite;
सीमन्त-उन्नयनम् (sīmanta-unnayanam) — hair-parting rite;
जात-कर्म (jāta-karma) — birth rite;
नाम-कर्म (nāma-karma) — naming;
अन्न-प्राशनम् (anna-prāśanam) — first feeding

BP 1.42.9

चूडोपनयनं चास्य व्रतादेशस्तथैव च ।
समावर्तनमप्यन्यत्पाणिग्रहणमेव च ॥

They also include tonsure, initiation, instruction in vows, return from studentship, and taking a hand in marriage.

चूडा-उपनयनम् (cūḍā-upanayanam) — tonsure and initiation;
व्रत-आदेशः (vrata-ādeśaḥ) — vow instruction;
समावर्तनम् (samāvartanam) — return from studentship;
पाणि-ग्रहणम् (pāṇi-grahaṇam) — marriage hand-taking

BP 1.42.10

इत्येवमादिसंस्कारविधानैर्येऽतिसंस्कृताः ।
त एव ब्राह्मणा येषां नैरन्तर्येण कामनाः ॥

Those greatly refined by prescriptions for these and similar rites are Brahmins whose desires for them are uninterrupted.

इति-एवम्-आदि-संस्कार-विधानैः (iti-evam-ādi-saṃskāra-vidhānaiḥ) — by these and similar rite prescriptions;
अति-संस्कृताः (ati-saṃskṛtāḥ) — highly refined;
ते एव ब्राह्मणाः (te eva brāhmaṇāḥ) — they are brāhmaṇas;
येषाम् नैरन्तर्येण कामनाः (yeṣām nairantaryeṇa kāmanāḥ) — whose intention is continuous

BP 1.42.11

यस्माद्वै ब्राह्मणा जाता ब्राह्मणैः कृतसंस्कृतैः ।
नायुः शक्तिर्हि कान्त्यादिविशेषो विद्यते स्फुटः ॥

Since Brahmins are born from Brahmins who underwent rites, no clear distinction in lifespan, strength, beauty, or the like is found.

ब्राह्मणाः जाताः ब्राह्मणैः कृत-संस्कृतैः (brāhmaṇāḥ jātāḥ brāhmaṇaiḥ kṛta-saṃskṛtaiḥ) — brāhmaṇas born from ritually refined brāhmaṇas;
न आयुः-शक्ति-कान्ति-आदि-विशेषः (na āyuḥ-śakti-kānti-ādi-viśeṣaḥ) — no distinction in life power beauty etc.;
विद्यते स्फुटः (vidyate sphuṭaḥ) — exists clearly

BP 1.42.12

तौ वा ब्राह्मणगात्रोत्थौ संस्कृतासंस्कृतौ नरौ ।
इष्टानिष्टाप्त्यनाप्तिभ्यां न भिद्येते परस्परम् ॥

Two men born from Brahmin bodies, one consecrated and one unconsecrated, do not differ from one another in obtaining or failing to obtain what is desired or undesired.

तौ ब्राह्मण-गात्र-उत्थौ (tau brāhmaṇa-gātra-utthau) — two born from brāhmaṇa bodies;
संस्कृत-असंस्कृतौ नरौ (saṃskṛta-asaṃskṛtau narau) — consecrated and unconsecrated men;
इष्ट-अनिष्ट-आप्ति-अनाप्तिभ्याम् (iṣṭa-aniṣṭa-āpti-anāptibhyām) — in obtaining or not desired and undesired;
न भिद्येते परस्परम् (na bhidyete parasparam) — do not differ mutually

BP 1.42.13

ज्ञानाध्ययनमीमांसा नियमेन्द्रियनिग्रहैः ।
विना संस्कारयोगेऽपि पुंसः शूद्रान्न भिन्नता ॥

Even when joined with rites, a man is not distinct from a Shudra without knowledge, study, inquiry, disciplined observance, and control of the senses.

ज्ञान-अध्ययन-मीमांसा (jñāna-adhyayana-mīmāṃsā) — knowledge study inquiry;
नियम-इन्द्रिय-निग्रहैः विना (niyama-indriya-nigrahaiḥ vinā) — without discipline and sense control;
संस्कार-योगे अपि (saṃskāra-yoge api) — even joined with rites;
पुंसः शूद्रात् न भिन्नता (puṃsaḥ śūdrāt na bhinnatā) — man not distinct from śūdra

BP 1.42.14

संस्कारः क्रियमाणश्च न शूद्रे च प्रवर्तते ।
संस्कृताङ्गश्च पापेभ्यो न पश्यति निवर्तते ॥

The rite performed does not operate in a Shudra. Yet a person whose limbs are consecrated is not seen to turn away from sinful acts.

संस्कारः क्रियमाणः (saṃskāraḥ kriyamāṇaḥ) — rite being performed;
न शूद्रे प्रवर्तते (na śūdre pravartate) — does not operate in śūdra;
संस्कृत-अङ्गः च (saṃskṛta-aṅgaḥ ca) — one with consecrated body;
पापेभ्यः न निवर्तते (pāpebhyaḥ na nivartate) — does not turn from sins

BP 1.42.15

विलासिनीभुजंगादिजनवन्मदविह्वलाः ।
व्यामुह्यन्ति सदाचाराद्ब्राह्मणत्वात्पतन्ति च ॥

Overwhelmed by pride like people infatuated with courtesans and serpents, they become confused, depart from good conduct, and fall from Brahminhood.

विलासिनी-भुजङ्ग-आदि-जन-वत् (vilāsinī-bhujaṅga-ādi-jana-vat) — like people of courtesans and serpents;
मद-विह्वलाः (mada-vihvalāḥ) — overwhelmed by pride;
व्यामुह्यन्ति (vyāmuhyanti) — become confused;
सदाचारात् (sadācārāt) — from good conduct;
ब्राह्मणत्वात् पतन्ति (brāhmaṇatvāt patanti) — fall from brāhmaṇa status

BP 1.42.16

संस्कृतोऽपि दुराचारो नरकं याति मानवः ।
निःसंस्कारः सदाचारो भवेद्विप्रोत्तमः सदा ॥

A consecrated person of bad conduct goes to hell. A person without consecration who has good conduct may always be an excellent Brahmin.

संस्कृतः अपि दुराचारः (saṃskṛtaḥ api durācāraḥ) — even consecrated bad-conduct person;
नरकम् याति (narakam yāti) — goes to hell;
निःसंस्कारः सदाचारः (niḥsaṃskāraḥ sadācāraḥ) — unconsecrated good-conduct person;
विप्र-उत्तमः भवेत् सदा (vipra-uttamaḥ bhavet sadā) — may always be finest brāhmaṇa

BP 1.42.17

मन्त्रपूतात्मसंस्कारयुक्तोऽपि प्लवते न तु ।
ब्राह्मण्यादविकल्पं स पश्चाद्दुश्चरितो नरः ॥

Even one joined with consecrations that purify the self by mantra does not remain afloat. A man of bad conduct afterward is surely removed from Brahminhood.

मन्त्र-पूत-आत्म-संस्कार-युक्तः अपि (mantra-pūta-ātma-saṃskāra-yuktaḥ api) — even mantra-purified and consecrated;
प्लवते न तु (plavate na tu) — does not stay afloat;
ब्राह्मण्यात् अविकल्पम् (brāhmaṇyāt avikalpam) — securely in brāhmaṇa status;
पश्चात् दुश्चरितः नरः (paścāt duścaritaḥ naraḥ) — afterward bad-conduct man

BP 1.42.18

सामर्थ्यात्पतनं तस्माद्ब्राह्मण्यान्मुच्यते ध्रुवम् ।
दुरनुष्ठानसक्तानां पुंसां पुरुषपुङ्गवैः ॥

Therefore, because of their capacity to fall, men attached to improper observances are certainly released from Brahminhood, as the foremost men have taught.

सामर्थ्यात् पतनम् (sāmarthyāt patanam) — falling is possible;
तस्मात् ब्राह्मण्यात् मुच्यते ध्रुवम् (tasmāt brāhmaṇyāt mucyate dhruvam) — therefore certainly released from brāhmaṇa status;
दुरनुस्थान-सक्तानाम् पुंसाम् (duranusthāna-saktānām puṃsām) — people attached to bad practice;
पुरुष-पुङ्गवैः (puruṣa-puṅgavaiḥ) — by foremost people

BP 1.42.19

किं क्वचिद्दृष्टमेवैतत्किं वा स्पर्धाविदत्ययम् ।
तुल्यमुत्सहसे कर्तुमप्यदृष्टं तदा वद ॥

Has this ever been seen anywhere, or is it a distortion born of rivalry. If you venture to assert even what is unseen, then say so.

किम् क्वचित् दृष्टम् एतत् (kim kvacit dṛṣṭam etat) — has this been seen anywhere;
किम् वा स्पर्धा-विद्-अत्मयम् (kim vā spardhā-vid-atmayam) — or rivalry-distorted knowledge;
तुल्यम् उत्सहसे कर्तुम् (tulyam utsahase kartum) — venture to make equivalent;
अपि अदृष्टम् (api adṛṣṭam) — even unseen;
तदा वद (tadā vada) — then say

BP 1.42.20

आचारमनुष्ठिन्तो व्यासादिमुनिसत्तमाः ।
गर्भाधानादिसंस्कारकलापरहिताः स्फुटम् ॥

Vyāsa and other excellent sages practised good conduct while clearly lacking the whole collection of rites beginning with conception.

आचारम् अनुतिष्ठन्तः (ācāram anutiṣṭhantaḥ) — practising conduct;
व्यास-आदि-मुनि-सत्तमाः (vyāsa-ādi-muni-sattamāḥ) — Vyāsa and excellent sages;
गर्भाधान-आदि-संस्कार-कलाप-रहिताः (garbhādhāna-ādi-saṃskāra-kalāpa-rahitāḥ) — without collection of rites beginning impregnation;
स्फुटम् (sphuṭam) — clearly

BP 1.42.21

विप्रोत्तमाः श्रियं प्राप्ताः सर्वलोकनमस्कृताः ।
बहवः कथ्यमाना ये कतिचित्तान्निबोधत ॥

Many became foremost Brahmins, attained wealth, and were honored by all the worlds. Hear a few of them as they are recounted.

विप्र-उत्तमाः (vipra-uttamāḥ) — finest brāhmaṇas;
श्रियम् प्राप्ताः (śriyam prāptāḥ) — attained splendour;
सर्व-लोक-नमस्कृताः (sarva-loka-namaskṛtāḥ) — honoured by all worlds;
बहवः (bahavaḥ) — many;
कतिचित् तान् निबोधत (katicit tān nibodhata) — understand a few

BP 1.42.22

जातो व्यासस्तु कैवर्त्याः श्वपाक्याश्च पराशरः ।
शुक्याः शुकः कणादाख्यस्तथोलूक्याः सुतोऽभवत् ॥

Vyāsa was born from a fisherwoman, Parāśara from a woman of dog eating birth, Śuka from a female parrot, and the sage named Kaṇāda became the son of an owl.

व्यासः कैवर्त्याः जातः (vyāsaḥ kaivartyāḥ jātaḥ) — Vyāsa born of fisherwoman;
श्वपाक्याः पराशरः (śvapākyāḥ parāśaraḥ) — Parāśara from śvapākī;
शुक्याः शुकः (śukyāḥ śukaḥ) — Śuka from female parrot;
कणादः उलूक्याः सुतः (kaṇādaḥ ulūkyāḥ sutaḥ) — Kaṇāda son of owl

BP 1.42.23

मृगीजोथर्षशृङ्गोपि वशिष्ठो गणिकात्मजः ।
मन्दपालो मुनिश्रेष्ठो नाविकापत्यमुच्यते ॥

Ṛṣyaśṛṅga was born from a doe, Vasiṣṭha was the son of a courtesan, and the excellent sage Maṇḍapāla is said to be the offspring of a boatwoman.

मृगी-जः ऋष्यशृङ्गः (mṛgī-jaḥ ṛṣyaśṛṅgaḥ) — Ṛṣyaśṛṅga born of doe;
वसिष्ठः गणिका-आत्मजः (vasiṣṭhaḥ gaṇikā-ātmajaḥ) — Vasiṣṭha son of courtesan;
मन्दपालः मुनि-श्रेष्ठः (mandapālaḥ muni-śreṣṭhaḥ) — excellent sage Mandapāla;
नाविका-अपत्यम् उच्यते (nāvikā-apatyam ucyate) — said child of boatwoman

BP 1.42.24

माण्डव्यो मुनिराजस्तु मण्डूकीगर्भसम्भवः ।
बहवोऽन्येऽपि विप्रत्वं प्राप्ता ये पूर्ववद्द्विजाः ॥

The king of sages Māṇḍavya was born from the womb of a frog. Many others too attained Brahminhood and became twice born as described before.

माण्डव्यः मुनि-राजः (māṇḍavyaḥ muni-rājaḥ) — royal sage Māṇḍavya;
मण्डूकी-गर्भ-सम्भवः (maṇḍūkī-garbha-sambhavaḥ) — born from frog womb;
बहवः अन्ये अपि द्विजाः (bahavaḥ anye api dvijāḥ) — many other twice-born;
विप्रत्वम् प्राप्ताः (vipratvam prāptāḥ) — attained brāhmaṇa status;
पूर्व-वत् (pūrva-vat) — as before

BP 1.42.25

यच्चैतच्चारुचरितैरर्च्यमुच्चरितं वचः ।
तद्विचार्याचरन्नुच्चैराचारोपचितद्युतिः ॥

Having considered this excellent statement, honored and spoken by people of good conduct, one should practise it and become radiant through conduct.

यत् एतत् चारु-चरितैः अर्च्यम् उच्चरितम् वचः (yat etat cāru-caritaiḥ arcyam uccaritam vacaḥ) — noble statement uttered by beautiful conduct people;
तत् विचार्य आचरन् (tat vicārya ācaran) — reflecting and practising it;
आचार-उपचित-द्युतिः (ācāra-upacita-dyutiḥ) — radiant through accumulated conduct

BP 1.42.26

हरिणीगर्भसम्भूत ऋष्यशृङ्गो महामुनिः ।
तपसा ब्राह्मणो जातः संस्कारस्तेन कारणम् ॥

The great sage Ṛṣyaśṛṅga, born from the womb of a hind, became a Brahmin through austerity. This was the consecration that caused it.

हरिणी-गर्भ-सम्भूतः ऋष्यशृङ्गः (hariṇī-garbha-sambhūtaḥ ṛṣyaśṛṅgaḥ) — Ṛṣyaśṛṅga born from doe;
महा-मुनिः (mahā-muniḥ) — great sage;
तपसा ब्राह्मणः जातः (tapasā brāhmaṇaḥ jātaḥ) — became brāhmaṇa by austerity;
संस्कारः तेन कारणम् (saṃskāraḥ tena kāraṇam) — rite is therefore not cause

BP 1.42.27

श्वपाकीगर्भसम्भूतः पिता व्यासस्य पार्थिव ।
तपसा ब्राह्मणो जातः संस्कारस्तेन कारणम् ॥

The father of Vyāsa was born from a woman of dog eating birth, O king. Through austerity he became a Brahmin. That consecration was the cause.

श्वपाकी-गर्भ-सम्भूतः (śvapākī-garbha-sambhūtaḥ) — born from śvapākī womb;
पिता व्यासस्य (pitā vyāsasya) — father of Vyāsa;
तपसा ब्राह्मणः जातः (tapasā brāhmaṇaḥ jātaḥ) — became brāhmaṇa by austerity;
संस्कारः तेन कारणम् (saṃskāraḥ tena kāraṇam) — rite not the cause

BP 1.42.28

उलूकीगर्भसम्भूतः कणादाख्यो महामुनिः ।
तपसा ब्राह्मणो जातः संस्कारस्तेन कारणम् ॥

The great sage named Kaṇāda, born from the womb of an owl, became a Brahmin through austerity. That consecration was the cause.

उलूकी-गर्भ-सम्भूतः (ulūkī-garbha-sambhūtaḥ) — born from owl womb;
कणाद-आख्यः महा-मुनिः (kaṇāda-ākhyaḥ mahā-muniḥ) — great sage named Kaṇāda;
तपसा ब्राह्मणः जातः (tapasā brāhmaṇaḥ jātaḥ) — became brāhmaṇa by austerity;
संस्कारः तेन कारणम् (saṃskāraḥ tena kāraṇam) — rite not cause

BP 1.42.29

गणिकागर्भसम्भूतो वशिष्ठश्च महामुनिः ।
तपसा ब्राह्मणो जातः संस्कारस्तेन कारणम् ॥

The great sage Vasiṣṭha, born from the womb of a courtesan, became a Brahmin through austerity. That consecration was the cause.

गणिका-गर्भ-सम्भूतः वसिष्ठः (gaṇikā-garbha-sambhūtaḥ vasiṣṭhaḥ) — Vasiṣṭha born from courtesan;
महा-मुनिः (mahā-muniḥ) — great sage;
तपसा ब्राह्मणः जातः (tapasā brāhmaṇaḥ jātaḥ) — became brāhmaṇa through austerity;
संस्कारः तेन कारणम् (saṃskāraḥ tena kāraṇam) — rite not cause

BP 1.42.30

नाविकागर्भसम्भूतो मन्दपालो महामुनिः ।
तपसा ब्राह्मणो जातः संस्कारस्तेन कारणम् ॥

The great sage Maṇḍapāla, born from the womb of a boatwoman, became a Brahmin through austerity. That consecration was the cause.

नाविक-गर्भ-सम्भूतः मन्दपालः (nāvika-garbha-sambhūtaḥ mandapālaḥ) — Mandapāla born from boatwoman;

BP 1.42.31

वेदतन्त्रजसंस्कारकलापनिपुणैरपि ।
विद्यातपोधनबलादुत्कृष्टं लभ्यते फलम् ।

Even those skilled in the full array of rites arising from the Veda and sacred teachings obtain the highest fruit through the power of knowledge, austerity, wealth, and strength.

वेद-तन्त्र-ज-संस्कार-कलाप-निपुणैः अपि (veda-tantra-ja-saṃskāra-kalāpa-nipuṇaiḥ api) — even skilled in Veda-Tantra rite collection;
विद्या-तपः-धन-बलात् (vidyā-tapaḥ-dhana-balāt) — by knowledge austerity wealth strength;
उत्कृष्टम् लभ्यते फलम् (utkṛṣṭam labhyate phalam) — excellent fruit obtained

BP 1.42.32

लब्धसंस्कारदेहाश्च महापातकिनो नराः ।
यस्मान्निवर्तते ब्रह्म तस्मात्साङ्केतिकं विदुः ॥

Men whose bodies received the rites may commit great sins. Since Brahminhood departs from them, people know it as a conventional designation.

लब्ध-संस्कार-देहाः (labdha-saṃskāra-dehāḥ) — bodies having rites;
महा-पातकिनः नराः (mahā-pātakinaḥ narāḥ) — great sinners;
यस्मात् निवर्तते ब्रह्म (yasmāt nivartate brahma) — from whom Brahman withdraws;
तस्मात् साङ्केतिकम् विदुः (tasmāt sāṅketikam viduḥ) — therefore understood conventional
