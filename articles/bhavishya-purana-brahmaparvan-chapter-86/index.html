Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 86 — The Son Giving and Victory Observances

BP 1.86.1

ब्रह्मोवाच । पञ्च-तारं भवेद् यत्र नक्षत्रं ते वृषध्वज । वारे तु देव-देवस्य स वारः पुत्रदः स्मृतः ॥ ॥

Brahmā said. When the five star asterism occurs, O Bull Bannered One, that day of the Lord of gods is remembered as the day that gives sons.

BP 1.86.2

उपवासो भवेत् तत्र श्राद्धं कार्यं तथा भवेत् । प्राशनं चापि पिण्डस्य मध्यमस्य प्रकीर्तितम् ॥ ॥

There should be fasting there, and the ancestral rite should likewise be performed. The eating of the middle rice ball is also prescribed.

BP 1.86.3

स-उपवासः तु यो भक्त्या पूजयेद् अत्र गोपतिम् । धूप-माल्य-उपहारैस् तु दिव्य-गन्ध-समन्वितैः ॥ ॥

One should worship the Lord of Cows there with devotion while fasting, with incense, garlands, and offerings of divine fragrance.

BP 1.86.4

एवं पूज्य विवस्वन्तं तस्यैव पुरतो निशि । भूमौ स्वपिति वै वीर जपन् श्वेतां महा-मते ॥ ॥

Having worshipped the Sun in this way before him at night, O hero, he sleeps on the ground while reciting the White One, O great minded one.

BP 1.86.5

प्रातर् उत्थाय च स्नानं कृत्वा दत्त्वार्घ्यम् उत्तमम् । रक्त-चन्दन-सम्मिश्रैः करवीरैर् गणाधिप ॥ ॥

Rising in the morning and bathing, he should offer an excellent water offering with oleander flowers mixed with red sandalwood, O chief of hosts.

BP 1.86.6

प्रपूज्य ग्रह-भूतेशम् अंशुमन्तं त्रिलोचन । वीरं च पूजयित्वा तु ततः श्राद्धं प्रकल्पयेत् ॥ ॥

Having fully worshipped the Lord of planets and beings, the radiant Sun, O three eyed one, and also worshipped the hero, he should then arrange the ancestral rite.

BP 1.86.7

पञ्चभिर् ब्राह्मणैर् देव दिव्यैः भौमैश् च सुव्रत । मगसंज्ञौ तत्र दिव्यौ ब्राह्मणौ परिकल्पयेत् ॥ ॥

With five Brahmins, O god, both heavenly and earthly, O well vowed one, he should appoint there two heavenly Brahmins known as Magas.

BP 1.86.8

त्रीन् अत्र ब्राह्मणान् भीमान् प्रकल्प्यान्धक-सूदन । कुर्याद् एवं ततः श्राद्धं पार्वणं भास्कर-प्रियम् ॥ ॥

Having appointed three formidable Brahmins here, O slayer of Andhaka, he should then perform the regular ancestral rite dear to the Sun.

BP 1.86.9

श्राद्धे त्व् अथ समाप्ते तु दद्यात् पिण्डं तु मध्यमम् । पुरतो देव-देवस्य स्थित्वा मन्त्रेण सुव्रत ॥ ॥

When the ancestral rite is complete, he should stand before the Lord of gods and give the middle rice ball with the mantra, O well vowed one.

BP 1.86.10

स एष पिण्डो देवेश यो ऽभीष्टस् तव सर्वदा । अश्नामि पश्यते तुभ्यं तेन मे सन्ततिर् भवेत् ॥ ॥

This is the rice ball, O Lord of gods, always desired by you. I eat it as you look on. By it may my progeny arise.

BP 1.86.11

प्रसादात् तव देवेश इति मे भावितं मनः । इत्थं सम्पूजितो ह्य् अत्र भास्करः पुत्रदो भवेत् ॥ ॥

By your grace, O Lord of gods, this is the thought in my mind. Worshipped here in this way, the Sun becomes a giver of sons.

BP 1.86.12

अतो ऽयं पुत्रदो वारो देवस्य परिकीर्तितः । एवम् अत्र सदा यस् तु भास्करं पूजयेद् नरः ॥ ॥

Therefore this day of the god is proclaimed as the son giving day. Whoever always worships the Sun here in this way.

BP 1.86.13

उपवास-परः श्राद्धे स पुत्रं लभते ध्रुवम् । धनं धान्यं हिरण्यं च आरोग्यं सुखदं तथा । सूर्य-लोकं च सम्प्राप्य ततो राजा भवेन् नृषु ॥ ॥

Devoted to fasting and the ancestral rite, he surely obtains a son, wealth, grain, gold, health, and happiness. After reaching the world of the Sun, he then becomes a king among people.

BP 1.86.14

प्रभया द्विज-संकाशः कान्त्या चाम्बुज-सन्निभः । वीर्येण गोपतेस् तुल्यः गाम्भीर्ये सागरोपमः ॥ ॥

Like a Brahmin in radiance, like a lotus in beauty, equal to the Lord of Cows in valor, and like the ocean in depth.

BP 1.86.15

ब्रह्मोवाच । दक्षिणे त्व् अयने यः स्यात् स जयः परिकीर्तितः ॥ ॥

Brahmā said. The one that occurs in the southern course is proclaimed as Victory.

BP 1.86.16

अत्र उपवासो नक्तं च स्नानं दानं जपस् तथा । भवेच् छत-गुणं देव भास्कर-प्रीतये कृतम् ॥ ॥

Here fasting, the night observance, bathing, giving, and recitation performed to please the Sun all become a hundredfold, O god.

BP 1.86.17

तस्मान् नक्तादि कर्तव्यं यत् स्याच् छत-गुणं विभो ॥ ॥

Therefore the night observance and the rest should be performed, O Lord, for they become a hundredfold.
