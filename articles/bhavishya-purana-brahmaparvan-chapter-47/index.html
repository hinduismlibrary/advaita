Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 47 — The Seventh Day Vow of Eating Greens

BP 1.47.1

सुमन्तुरुवाच
सप्तम्यां सोपवासस्तु नक्ताहारोऽपि वा भवेत् ।
सप्तम्यां देवदेवेन लब्धं स्वं रूपमादरात् ॥

Sumantu said, On the seventh day one should fast, or eat at night. On the seventh day the God of gods attained his own form with reverence.

सुमन्तुरुवाच (sumanturuvāca) — Sumantu said;
सप्तम्यां सोपवासस्तु नक्ताहारोऽपि वा भवेत् (saptamyāṃ sopavāsastu naktāhāro'pi vā bhavet) — On the seventh day one should fast with him or eat nothing;
सप्तम्यां देवदेवेन लब्धं स्वं रूपमादरात् (saptamyāṃ devadevena labdhaṃ svaṃ rūpamādarāt) — On the seventh day the god of gods respectfully assumed his form

BP 1.47.2

अण्डेन सह जातो वै अण्डस्थो बुद्धिमाप्तवान् ।
अण्डस्थस्यैव दक्षेण भार्यां दत्त्वा स्वकां सुताम् ॥

He was born with the egg and, abiding in the egg, attained intelligence. Daksha gave his own daughter as wife to him who was in the egg.

अण्डेन सह जातो वै अण्डस्थो बुद्धिमाप्तवान् (aṇḍena saha jāto vai aṇḍastho buddhimāptavān) — Born with an egg, he attained intelligence while in the egg;
अण्डस्थस्यैव दक्षेण भार्यां दत्त्वा स्वकां सुताम् (aṇḍasthasyaiva dakṣeṇa bhāryāṃ dattvā svakāṃ sutām) — Daksha gave his daughter to his wife while he was still in the egg

BP 1.47.3

नाम्ना रूपेति रूपेण नान्या नारी तथा भवेत् ।
अण्डस्थ एव सुचिरं स्थितो मार्तण्ड इत्यतः ॥

Named Rūpā, no other woman was like her in form. Since he remained in the egg for a long time, he was called Mārtāṇḍa.

नाम्ना रूपेति रूपेण नान्या नारी तथा भवेत् (nāmnā rūpeti rūpeṇa nānyā nārī tathā bhavet) — No other woman in name or form should be like that;
अण्डस्थ एव सुचिरं स्थितो मार्तण्ड इत्यतः (aṇḍastha eva suciraṃ sthito mārtaṇḍa ityataḥ) — Because Martanda remained in the egg for a long time

BP 1.47.4

दक्षाज्ञया विश्वकर्मा वपुरस्य प्रकाशयन् ।
प्रकाशतस्ततो नाम तस्य जातं नराधिप ॥
अण्डस्थस्यैव सञ्जातो यमुना यम एव च ॥

By the command of Daksha, Viśvakarma revealed his body. From its radiance his name arose, O king. Yama and Yamunā were born from him while he was in the egg.

दक्षाज्ञया विश्वकर्मा वपुरस्य प्रकाशयन् (dakṣājñayā viśvakarmā vapurasya prakāśayan) — By the command of Daksha Vishwakarma revealed his body;
प्रकाशतस्ततो नाम तस्य जातं नराधिप (prakāśatastato nāma tasya jātaṃ narādhipa) — O lord of men his name was born from the light;
अण्डस्थस्यैव सञ्जातो यमुना यम एव च (aṇḍasthasyaiva sañjāto yamunā yama eva ca) — Yamuna and Yama were born from the egg

BP 1.47.5

दाक्षायणी तस्य भार्या वैराग्यात्तनुमध्यमा ।
चिन्तयामास सा देवी दुःखान्निर्वेदमागता ॥

His wife Dākṣāyaṇī, slender of waist, became detached through sorrow and reflected, the goddess having grown weary of grief.

दाक्षायणी तस्य भार्या वैराग्यात्तनुमध्यमा (dākṣāyaṇī tasya bhāryā vairāgyāttanumadhyamā) — His wife Dakshayani was of slender waist out of renunciation;
चिन्तयामास सा देवी दुःखान्निर्वेदमागता (cintayāmāsa sā devī duḥkhānnirvedamāgatā) — The goddess thought and became detached from her grief

BP 1.47.6

अहो तेजोमयं रूपं कान्तं कान्तस्य कान्तिमत् ।
न चास्य किञ्चित्पश्यामि अङ्गं तेजोविमोहितम् ॥

Ah, his form is filled with radiance, lovely and beautiful with the beauty of the beloved. I see no limb of his, being bewildered by the radiance.

अहो तेजोमयं रूपं कान्तं कान्तस्य कान्तिमत् (aho tejomayaṃ rūpaṃ kāntaṃ kāntasya kāntimat) — Oh, the radiant form, the lovely, the brightness of the lovely;
न चास्य किञ्चित्पश्यामि अङ्गं तेजोविमोहितम् (na cāsya kiñcitpaśyāmi aṅgaṃ tejovimohitam) — And I can see nothing in his body, bewildered by the brightness

BP 1.47.7

शुभं कनकतुल्यं मे रूपं कान्तं सुकान्तिमत् ।
साम्प्रतं श्यामतां यातं दग्धमेतस्य तेजसा ॥

My lovely form, auspicious and like gold, was radiant and beautiful. Now it has become dark, burned by his radiance.

शुभं कनकतुल्यं मे रूपं कान्तं सुकान्तिमत् (śubhaṃ kanakatulyaṃ me rūpaṃ kāntaṃ sukāntimat) — My beauty is auspicious and golden, lovely and radiant;
साम्प्रतं श्यामतां यातं दग्धमेतस्य तेजसा (sāmprataṃ śyāmatāṃ yātaṃ dagdhametasya tejasā) — It has now turned dark and been burnt by the light of this

BP 1.47.8

तस्मात्तप्स्ये तपश्चाहं गत्वा वै उत्तरान्कुरून् ।
स्वां छायामत्र निक्षिप्य भयाच्छापस्य रूपिणी ॥

Therefore I shall perform austerities, going to the Northern Kurus and leaving my own shadow here, taking a form through fear of the curse.

तस्मात्तप्स्ये तपश्चाहं गत्वा वै उत्तरान्कुरून् (tasmāttapsye tapaścāhaṃ gatvā vai uttarānkurūn) — Therefore I shall perform austerities and go to the northern Kurus;
स्वां छायामत्र निक्षिप्य भयाच्छापस्य रूपिणी (svāṃ chāyāmatra nikṣipya bhayācchāpasya rūpiṇī) — She cast her shadow here in the form of a curse out of fear

BP 1.47.9

निक्षिप्योवाच तां बालां मा चास्मै वै वदिष्यसि ।
एवं सा निश्चयं कृत्वा गता वै उत्तरान्कुरून् ॥

Having left her shadow, she said to that young woman, Do not tell him this. Having made this resolve, she went to the Northern Kurus.

निक्षिप्योवाच तां बालां मा चास्मै वै वदिष्यसि (nikṣipyovāca tāṃ bālāṃ mā cāsmai vai vadiṣyasi) — He put the child down and said 'Don't tell him';
एवं सा निश्चयं कृत्वा गता वै उत्तरान्कुरून् (evaṃ sā niścayaṃ kṛtvā gatā vai uttarānkurūn) — Having thus decided she went to the northern Kurus

BP 1.47.10

स्वरूपं तत्र निक्षिप्य वडवारूपधारिणी ।
चचार सा मृगैः सार्धं बहून्वर्षगणान्नृप ॥

Leaving her own form there and assuming the form of a mare, she roamed with the deer for many years, O king.

स्वरूपं तत्र निक्षिप्य वडवारूपधारिणी (svarūpaṃ tatra nikṣipya vaḍavārūpadhāriṇī) — She placed her form there and assumed the form of a vadava;
चचार सा मृगैः सार्धं बहून्वर्षगणान्नृप (cacāra sā mṛgaiḥ sārdhaṃ bahūnvarṣagaṇānnṛpa) — O king she wandered about with the deer for many years

BP 1.47.11

असावपि च मार्तण्डश्छायां भार्याममन्यत् ।
शनिं च तपतीं चैव द्वे अपत्ये च जज्ञिवान् ॥

Mārtāṇḍa too considered Chāyā his wife. He fathered two children, Śani and Tapatī.

असावपि च मार्तण्डश्छायां भार्याममन्यत् (asāvapi ca mārtaṇḍaśchāyāṃ bhāryāmamanyat) — And Asa also, Martanda, took Shaya as his wife;
शनिं च तपतीं चैव द्वे अपत्ये च जज्ञिवान् (śaniṃ ca tapatīṃ caiva dve apatye ca jajñivān) — He had two children, Saturn and Tapati

BP 1.47.12

अथ च्छायात्मापत्यानि स्नेहेन परिपालयेत् ।
नातिस्नेहेन चापश्यद्यमुनां यममेव च ॥

Then Chāyā lovingly cared for her own children, but did not look upon Yamunā and Yama with much affection.

अथ च्छायात्मापत्यानि स्नेहेन परिपालयेत् (atha cchāyātmāpatyāni snehena paripālayet) — Then the shadow should take care of its children with affection;
नातिस्नेहेन चापश्यद्यमुनां यममेव च (nātisnehena cāpaśyadyamunāṃ yamameva ca) — He did not look at Yamuna or Yama with much affection

BP 1.47.13

अथ ताभ्यां विवादोऽभूदादित्यदुहित्रोर्द्वयोः ।
ते उभे विवदन्त्यौ तु परस्परमसम्मतम् ॥
यमुना तपती चोभे निम्नगे सम्बभूवतुः ॥

Then a quarrel arose between the two daughters of the Sun. The two argued, not agreeing with each other. Yamunā and Tapatī both became rivers.

अथ ताभ्यां विवादोऽभूदादित्यदुहित्रोर्द्वयोः (atha tābhyāṃ vivādo'bhūdādityaduhitrordvayoḥ) — Then there arose a quarrel between the two daughters of the Sun;
ते उभे विवदन्त्यौ तु परस्परमसम्मतम् (te ubhe vivadantyau tu parasparamasammatam) — They both argued but disagreed with each other;
यमुना तपती चोभे निम्नगे सम्बभूवतुः (yamunā tapatī cobhe nimnage sambabhūvatuḥ) — The Yamuna and Tapati both appeared in the lower reaches

BP 1.47.14

यमोऽपि यमुनाभ्राता छायया ताडितो भृशम् ।
पादमुद्यम्य तस्या वै तस्थौ सम्मुख एव सः ॥

Yama, the brother of Yamunā, was also struck severely by Chāyā. Raising his foot against her, he stood directly before her.

यमोऽपि यमुनाभ्राता छायया ताडितो भृशम् (yamo'pi yamunābhrātā chāyayā tāḍito bhṛśam) — Yama, the brother of Yamuna, was also severely beaten by the shadow;
पादमुद्यम्य तस्या वै तस्थौ सम्मुख एव सः (pādamudyamya tasyā vai tasthau sammukha eva saḥ) — He raised his foot and stood right in front of her

BP 1.47.15

छाया शशाप तं रोषाद्यस्मात्पादोद्यतो मम।
तस्मात्ते कर्म बीभत्सं प्राणिनां प्राणहिंसनम् ॥

In anger, Chāyā cursed him, saying that because he raised his foot against her, his deed would be dreadful, the taking of the lives of living beings.

छाया शशाप तं रोषाद्यस्मात्पादोद्यतो मम (chāyā śaśāpa taṃ roṣādyasmātpādodyato mama) — The shadow cursed him in anger for raising my feet;
तस्मात्ते कर्म बीभत्सं प्राणिनां प्राणहिंसनम् (tasmātte karma bībhatsaṃ prāṇināṃ prāṇahiṃsanam) — Therefore your action is terrible and it is harmful to the lives of living beings

BP 1.47.16

भविष्यति चिरं मूढ आचन्द्रार्कं न संशयः ।
पादं च यदि भूमौ त्वमिमं संस्थापयिष्यसि ॥

You fool, if you place this foot on the ground, this will happen to you for a long time, for as long as the Sun and Moon endure.

भविष्यति चिरं मूढ आचन्द्रार्कं न संशयः (bhaviṣyati ciraṃ mūḍha ācandrārkaṃ na saṃśayaḥ) — It will be a long time, you fool, and the moon and the sun will no doubt be there;
पादं च यदि भूमौ त्वमिमं संस्थापयिष्यसि (pādaṃ ca yadi bhūmau tvamimaṃ saṃsthāpayiṣyasi) — And if you set this foot on the ground

BP 1.47.17

कृमयो भक्षयिष्यन्ति मच्छापकलुषीकृतम् ॥

Worms will eat the foot made impure by my curse.

कृमयो भक्षयिष्यन्ति मच्छापकलुषीकृतम् (kṛmayo bhakṣayiṣyanti macchāpakaluṣīkṛtam) — Worms will eat what is contaminated by my curse

BP 1.47.18

तेषां विवदमानानां मार्तण्डोऽभ्यागमत्ततः ।
यमोऽप्याह महात्मानं मार्तण्डं लोकपावनम् ॥

While they were arguing, Mārtāṇḍa came to them. Yama then spoke to the great soul Mārtāṇḍa, purifier of the worlds.

तेषां विवदमानानां मार्तण्डोऽभ्यागमत्ततः (teṣāṃ vivadamānānāṃ mārtaṇḍo'bhyāgamattataḥ) — While they were arguing Martanda approached them;
यमोऽप्याह महात्मानं मार्तण्डं लोकपावनम् (yamo'pyāha mahātmānaṃ mārtaṇḍaṃ lokapāvanam) — Yama also addressed the great soul Martanda, the purifier of the worlds

BP 1.47.19

तात नित्यमियं चापि क्रूरभावेन पश्यति ।
न चास्याः सुसमा दृष्टिरस्मास्वस्तीति लक्ष्यते ॥

Father, she always looks at us with cruel intent. It is clear that her gaze toward us is not equal.

तात नित्यमियं चापि क्रूरभावेन पश्यति (tāta nityamiyaṃ cāpi krūrabhāvena paśyati) — My father always looks at her with cruelty;
न चास्याः सुसमा दृष्टिरस्मास्वस्तीति लक्ष्यते (na cāsyāḥ susamā dṛṣṭirasmāsvastīti lakṣyate) — It is not obvious that we have a well-balanced vision

BP 1.47.20

प्रोवाचाथ स तां छायां मार्तण्डो भृशकोपनः ।
समे अपत्ये किं मूढे समत्वं नानुपश्यसि ॥

Then, greatly angered, Mārtāṇḍa spoke to Chāyā, asking why she did not treat her children equally.

प्रोवाचाथ स तां छायां मार्तण्डो भृशकोपनः (provācātha sa tāṃ chāyāṃ mārtaṇḍo bhṛśakopanaḥ) — Then Martanda in a rage spoke to that shadow;
१समे अपत्ये किं मूढे समत्वं नानुपश्यसि (1same apatye kiṃ mūḍhe samatvaṃ nānupaśyasi) — 1 Why don't you see equality in the same child, you fool?

BP 1.47.21

यमः प्रोवाच पितरं नेयं माता पितर्मम ।
मातुश्छाया त्वियं पापा शप्तोऽहमनया पितः ॥
यमुना तपती वृत्तं तत्सर्वं विन्यवेदयत् ॥

Yama said to his father, Father, this is not my mother. This sinful woman is the shadow of my mother. I have been cursed by her, father. Yamunā and Tapatī related all that had happened.

यमः प्रोवाच पितरं नेयं माता पितर्मम (yamaḥ provāca pitaraṃ neyaṃ mātā pitarmama) — Yama said to his father, "This is not my mother, my father;
मातुश्छाया त्वियं पापा शप्तोऽहमनया पितः (mātuśchāyā tviyaṃ pāpā śapto'hamanayā pitaḥ) — I am cursed by this sinful father who is the shadow of my mother;
यमुना तपती वृत्तं तत्सर्वं विन्यवेदयत् (yamunā tapatī vṛttaṃ tatsarvaṃ vinyavedayat) — Yamuna told him all that had happened to Tapati

BP 1.47.22

अथ प्रोवाच मार्तण्डो मा ते पादो महीतले ।
मांसं रुधिरमादाय कृमयो यान्तु भूतलम् ॥

Then Mārtāṇḍa said, May your foot not touch the earth. Let the worms take the flesh and blood and go down to the ground.

अथ प्रोवाच मार्तण्डो मा ते पादो महीतले (atha provāca mārtaṇḍo mā te pādo mahītale) — Then Martanda said, "Do not let your feet fall to the ground;
मांसं रुधिरमादाय कृमयो यान्तु भूतलम् (māṃsaṃ rudhiramādāya kṛmayo yāntu bhūtalam) — Let the worms take the flesh and blood and go to the ground

BP 1.47.23

यमुनायाश्च यत्तोयं गङ्गातुल्यं भविष्यति ।
नर्मदायास्तपत्याश्च समं पुण्येन वै द्विज ॥

The water of Yamunā will become equal to the Ganges. The waters of Narmadā and Tapatī too will be equal in merit, O twice born.

यमुनायाश्च यत्तोयं गङ्गातुल्यं भविष्यति (yamunāyāśca yattoyaṃ gaṅgātulyaṃ bhaviṣyati) — And the water of the Yamuna which will be equal to the Ganges;
नर्मदायास्तपत्याश्च समं पुण्येन वै द्विज (narmadāyāstapatyāśca samaṃ puṇyena vai dvija) — O brahmin it is equally pious for the rivers Narmada and Tapati

BP 1.47.24

विन्ध्यस्य दक्षिणेनेह तपती प्रवहिष्यति ।
तत्सायुज्यतया साग्रं गङ्गा यास्यति शोभना ॥

Here Tapatī will flow south of the Vindhya. By joining with it, she will flow together with the beautiful Ganges.

विन्ध्यस्य दक्षिणेनेह तपती प्रवहिष्यति (vindhyasya dakṣiṇeneha tapatī pravahiṣyati) — The Tapati will flow here south of the Vindhya;
तत्सायुज्यतया साग्रं गङ्गा यास्यति शोभना (tatsāyujyatayā sāgraṃ gaṅgā yāsyati śobhanā) — By the union of that the beautiful Ganga will flow throughout

BP 1.47.25

गङ्गामासाद्य यमुना गङ्गा सैव भविष्यति ।
सौरसौम्ये उभे पुण्ये सर्वपापप्रणाशने ॥

When Yamunā reaches the Ganges, she herself will become the Ganges. Both are sacred, Solar and Lunar, destroyers of all sins.

गङ्गामासाद्य यमुना गङ्गा सैव भविष्यति (gaṅgāmāsādya yamunā gaṅgā saiva bhaviṣyati) — When Yamuna reaches Ganga it will become Ganga;
सौरसौम्ये उभे पुण्ये सर्वपापप्रणाशने (saurasaumye ubhe puṇye sarvapāpapraṇāśane) — Both the solar and gentle are holy and destroy all sins

BP 1.47.26

सौरी च वैष्णवी चोभे महापापभयापहे ।

तस्य श्रीर्विजयश्चैव त्रिवर्गश्चापि वर्धते ॥

A person should observe this vow for as long as he lives. His prosperity and victory, and the three aims of life, increase.

यावज्जीवं नरः कश्चिद्व्रतमेतच्चरेदिति (yāvajjīvaṃ naraḥ kaścidvratametaccarediti) — That a man should observe this vow for the rest of his life;
तस्य श्रीर्विजयश्चैव त्रिवर्गश्चापि वर्धते (tasya śrīrvijayaścaiva trivargaścāpi vardhate) — His prosperity and victory and the three worlds increase

BP 1.47.50

मृतश्च स्वर्गमायाति विमानवरमास्थितः ।
सूर्यलोके स रमते मन्वन्तरगणान्बहून् ॥
इह चागत्य कालान्ते नृपः शान्ति समन्वितः ॥

When he dies, he goes to heaven mounted on an excellent aerial car. He rejoices in the world of the Sun for many periods of Manu. Returning here at the end of time, O king, he is endowed with peace.

मृतश्च स्वर्गमायाति विमानवरमास्थितः (mṛtaśca svargamāyāti vimānavaramāsthitaḥ) — And the dead man comes to heaven in a splendid aerial chariot;
सूर्यलोके स रमते मन्वन्तरगणान्बहून् (sūryaloke sa ramate manvantaragaṇānbahūn) — He enjoys in the sun world for many manvas;
इह चागत्य कालान्ते नृपः शान्ति समन्वितः (iha cāgatya kālānte nṛpaḥ śānti samanvitaḥ) — And here at the end of time the king arrived in peace

BP 1.47.51

पुत्रपौत्रैः परिवृतो दाता स्यान्नृपतिश्चिरम् ।
भुनक्ति हि धरां राजन्विग्रहैश्चाजितः परै ॥

Surrounded by sons and grandsons, he will be generous and a king for a long time. O king, he enjoys the earth, unconquered by his enemies in battle.

पुत्रपौत्रैः परिवृतो दाता स्यान्नृपतिश्चिरम् (putrapautraiḥ parivṛto dātā syānnṛpatiściram) — Surrounded by his sons and grandsons a king will be a giver for a long time;
भुनक्ति हि धरां राजन्विग्रहैश्चाजितः परै (bhunakti hi dharāṃ rājanvigrahaiścājitaḥ parai) — O king he enjoys the earth and is conquered by others in conflicts

BP 1.47.52

ये नरा राजशार्दूल शाकाहारेण सप्तमीम् ।
उपोष्य लब्धं तत्तीर्थं पित्र्यं वै राजसंज्ञिकम् ॥

Those people, O tiger among kings, who fast on the seventh day while eating greens, obtain that ancestral sacred place known as Rājasañjñika.

ये नरा राजशार्दूल शाकाहारेण सप्तमीम् (ye narā rājaśārdūla śākāhāreṇa saptamīm) — O tiger among kings those men who eat vegetables for the seventh day;
उपोष्य लब्धं तत्तीर्थं पित्र्यं वै राजसंज्ञिकम् (upoṣya labdhaṃ tattīrthaṃ pitryaṃ vai rājasaṃjñikam) — After observing a fast I obtained that holy place known as Raja

BP 1.47.53

कुरुणा तव पूर्वेण शाकाहारेण सप्तमीम् ।
धर्मक्षेत्रं कुरुक्षेत्रं कृतं तस्य विवस्वता ॥

Your forefather Kuru observed the seventh day while eating greens. Vivasvat made the holy field Kurukṣetra for him.

कुरुणा तव पूर्वेण शाकाहारेण सप्तमीम् (kuruṇā tava pūrveṇa śākāhāreṇa saptamīm) — Kuruna your former vegetarian seventh;
धर्मक्षेत्रं कुरुक्षेत्रं कृतं तस्य विवस्वता (dharmakṣetraṃ kurukṣetraṃ kṛtaṃ tasya vivasvatā) — Vivasvan made the field of Dharma Kurukshetra for him

BP 1.47.54

सप्तमी नवमी षष्ठी तृतीया पञ्चमी नृप ।
कामदास्तिथयो ह्येता इहैव नरयोषिताम् ॥

The seventh, ninth, sixth, third, and fifth days, O king, are desire granting lunar days for men and women here.

सप्तमी नवमी षष्ठी तृतीया पञ्चमी नृप (saptamī navamī ṣaṣṭhī tṛtīyā pañcamī nṛpa) — The seventh, ninth, sixth, third and fifth, O king;
कामदास्तिथयो ह्येता इहैव नरयोषिताम् (kāmadāstithayo hyetā ihaiva narayoṣitām) — These are the lustful dates for men and women here

BP 1.47.55

सप्तमी माघमासे तु नवम्याश्वयुजे मता ।
षष्ठी भाद्रपदे धन्या वैशाखे तु तृतीयिका ॥

The seventh day in Māgha, the ninth in Āśvayuja, the sixth in Bhādrapada is auspicious, and the third in Vaiśākha.

सप्तमी माघमासे तु नवम्याश्वयुजे मता (saptamī māghamāse tu navamyāśvayuje matā) — The seventh is considered to be in the month of Magha and the ninth in the month of Ashvayuga;
षष्ठी भाद्रपदे धन्या वैशाखे तु तृतीयिका (ṣaṣṭhī bhādrapade dhanyā vaiśākhe tu tṛtīyikā) — The sixth is auspicious in Bhādrapada and the third in Vaishakha

BP 1.47.56

पुण्या भाद्रपदे प्रोक्ता पञ्चमी नागपञ्चमी ।
इत्येतास्तेषु मासेषु विशेषास्तिथयः स्मृताः ॥

The fifth day in Bhādrapada is declared sacred as Nāga Pañcamī. These are remembered as the special lunar days in those months.

पुण्या भाद्रपदे प्रोक्ता पञ्चमी नागपञ्चमी (puṇyā bhādrapade proktā pañcamī nāgapañcamī) — The fifth day of the holy month of Bhādrapada is called Nagapanchami;
इत्येतास्तेषु मासेषु विशेषास्तिथयः स्मृताः (ityetāsteṣu māseṣu viśeṣāstithayaḥ smṛtāḥ) — These are the special dates mentioned in those months

BP 1.47.57

शाकं सुसत्कृतं कृत्वा यश्च भक्त्या समन्वितः ।
दत्त्वा विप्रे यथाशक्त्या पश्चाद्भुङ्क्ते निशि व्रती ॥

Having well prepared the greens, the person endowed with devotion gives to a Brahmin according to his means, then the keeper of the vow eats at night.

शाकं सुसत्कृतं कृत्वा यश्च भक्त्या समन्वितः (śākaṃ susatkṛtaṃ kṛtvā yaśca bhaktyā samanvitaḥ) — And he who is well-prepared with vegetables and who is accompanied by devotion;
दत्त्वा विप्रे यथाशक्त्या पश्चाद्भुङ्क्ते निशि व्रती (dattvā vipre yathāśaktyā paścādbhuṅkte niśi vratī) — The vow-keeper gives to the brahmin as much as he can and then eats at night

BP 1.47.58

कार्तिके शुक्लपक्षस्य ग्राह्येयं कुरुनन्दन ।
चतुभिर्वापि मासैस्तु पारणं प्रथमं स्मृतम् ॥

This should be observed in the bright fortnight of Kārttika, O delight of the Kurus. The first breaking of the fast is prescribed after four months.

कार्तिके शुक्लपक्षस्य ग्राह्येयं कुरुनन्दन (kārtike śuklapakṣasya grāhyeyaṃ kurunandana) — This is acceptable in the white fortnight of Kartika, O son of the Kurus;
चतुभिर्वापि मासैस्तु पारणं प्रथमं स्मृतम् (catubhirvāpi māsaistu pāraṇaṃ prathamaṃ smṛtam) — Parana is said to be the first day after four or four months

BP 1.47.59

अगस्त्यकुसुमैश्चात्र पूजा कार्या विभावसोः ।
विलेपनं कुङ्कुमं तु धूपश्चैवापराजितैः ॥

Here the Sun should be worshipped with Agastya flowers. Saffron is for anointing, and Aparājitā flowers are for incense.

अगस्त्यकुसुमैश्चात्र पूजा कार्या विभावसोः (agastyakusumaiścātra pūjā kāryā vibhāvasoḥ) — The flowers of Agastya should be worshiped here for the fire;
विलेपनं कुङ्कुमं तु धूपश्चैवापराजितैः (vilepanaṃ kuṅkumaṃ tu dhūpaścaivāparājitaiḥ) — Ointment, saffron and incense were offered to the defeated

BP 1.47.60

स्नानं च पञ्चगव्येन तमेव प्राशयेत्ततः ।
नैवेद्यं पायसं चात्र देवदेवस्य कीर्तितम् ॥

The bath is with the five products of the cow, then one should partake of that same mixture. Rice pudding is declared here as an offering to the God of gods.

स्नानं च पञ्चगव्येन तमेव प्राशयेत्ततः (snānaṃ ca pañcagavyena tameva prāśayettataḥ) — Then one should take a bath with five kinds of cow's milk and eat it;
नैवेद्यं पायसं चात्र देवदेवस्य कीर्तितम् (naivedyaṃ pāyasaṃ cātra devadevasya kīrtitam) — Naivedya and payasam are mentioned here for the gods and goddesses

BP 1.47.61

तदेव देयं विप्राणां शाकं भक्ष्यमथात्मना ।
शुभशाकसमायुक्तं भक्ष्यपेयसमन्वितम् ॥

That same vegetable food should be given to Brahmins and then eaten oneself, together with auspicious greens and foods to eat and drink.

तदेव देयं विप्राणां शाकं भक्ष्यमथात्मना (tadeva deyaṃ viprāṇāṃ śākaṃ bhakṣyamathātmanā) — That very vegetable should be given to the brahmins and then eaten by the self;
शुभशाकसमायुक्तं भक्ष्यपेयसमन्वितम् (śubhaśākasamāyuktaṃ bhakṣyapeyasamanvitam) — It was filled with auspicious vegetables and food and drink

BP 1.47.62

द्वितीये पारणे राजञ्छुभगन्धानि यानि वै ।
पुष्पाणि तानि देवस्य तथा श्वेतं च चन्दनम् ॥

At the second breaking of the fast, O king, whatever flowers are fragrant are for the deity, and also white sandalwood.

द्वितीये पारणे राजञ्छुभगन्धानि यानि वै (dvitīye pāraṇe rājañchubhagandhāni yāni vai) — The second parana, O king, is the fragrance of these things;
पुष्पाणि तानि देवस्य तथा श्वेतं च चन्दनम् (puṣpāṇi tāni devasya tathā śvetaṃ ca candanam) — Those flowers of the god and white sandalwood

BP 1.47.63

अगुरुश्चापि धूपोऽत्र नैवेद्यं गुडपूपकाः ।
स्नानं कुशोदकेनात्र प्राशनं गोमयस्य तु ॥

Agarwood is the incense here, and jaggery cakes the offering. The bath is with kuśa water, and the consumption is of cow dung.

अगुरुश्चापि धूपोऽत्र नैवेद्यं गुडपूपकाः (aguruścāpi dhūpo'tra naivedyaṃ guḍapūpakāḥ) — Aguru and incense are offerings here and jaggery cakes;
स्नानं कुशोदकेनात्र प्राशनं गोमयस्य तु (snānaṃ kuśodakenātra prāśanaṃ gomayasya tu) — Bathing in Kushoda water here and eating cow dung

BP 1.47.64

तृतीये करवीराणि तथा रक्तं च चन्दनम् ।
धूपानां गुग्गुलश्चात्र प्रियो देवस्य सर्वदा ॥

In the third, oleander flowers and red sandalwood are used. Guggulu is always dear to the deity as incense.

तृतीये करवीराणि तथा रक्तं च चन्दनम् (tṛtīye karavīrāṇi tathā raktaṃ ca candanam) — In the third, caraway seeds and red sandalwood;
धूपानां गुग्गुलश्चात्र प्रियो देवस्य सर्वदा (dhūpānāṃ guggulaścātra priyo devasya sarvadā) — Guggul is always dear to the gods for incense

BP 1.47.65

शाल्योदनं तु नैवेद्यं दधिमिश्रं महामते ।
तमेव ब्राह्मणानां च भक्ष्यलेह्यसमन्वितम् ॥
कालशाकेन च विभो युक्तं दद्याद्विचक्षणः ॥

Cooked rice mixed with curd is the offering, O wise one. The discerning person should give that same food to the Brahmins, with foods to chew and lick, and mixed with dark greens, O lord.

शाल्योदनं तु नैवेद्यं दधिमिश्रं महामते (śālyodanaṃ tu naivedyaṃ dadhimiśraṃ mahāmate) — O great soul rice food mixed with curd should be offered;
तमेव ब्राह्मणानां च भक्ष्यलेह्यसमन्वितम् (tameva brāhmaṇānāṃ ca bhakṣyalehyasamanvitam) — That very same food and drink offered to the brahmins;
कालशाकेन च विभो युक्तं दद्याद्विचक्षणः (kālaśākena ca vibho yuktaṃ dadyādvicakṣaṇaḥ) — O lord a wise man should give it mixed with kale vegetables

BP 1.47.66

गौरसर्षपकल्केन स्नानं चात्र विदुर्बुधाः ।
तस्यैव प्राशनं धन्यं सर्वपापहरं शुभम् ॥

The wise know that the bath here is with a paste of white mustard. Partaking of that is blessed, auspicious, and removes all sins.

गौरसर्षपकल्केन स्नानं चात्र विदुर्बुधाः (gaurasarṣapakalkena snānaṃ cātra vidurbudhāḥ) — The wise also know how to bathe here with cow's milk and mustard seed lime;
तस्यैव प्राशनं धन्यं सर्वपापहरं शुभम् (tasyaiva prāśanaṃ dhanyaṃ sarvapāpaharaṃ śubham) — Eating it alone is blessed and auspicious and destroys all sins

BP 1.47.67

तृतीये पारणस्यान्ते महद्ब्राह्मणभोजनम् ।
श्रवणं च पुराणस्य वाचनं चापि शस्यते ॥

At the end of the third breaking of the fast, a great meal for Brahmins is prescribed. Listening to and reciting the sacred account is also praised.

तृतीये पारणस्यान्ते महद्ब्राह्मणभोजनम् (tṛtīye pāraṇasyānte mahadbrāhmaṇabhojanam) — At the end of the third parana there is a great brahmin meal;
श्रवणं च पुराणस्य वाचनं चापि शस्यते (śravaṇaṃ ca purāṇasya vācanaṃ cāpi śasyate) — Listening to and reading the Puranas is also recommended

BP 1.47.68

दैवस्य पुरतस्तात ब्राह्मणानां तथाग्रतः ।
ब्राह्मणाद्वाचकाच्छ्राव्यं नान्यवर्णसमुद्भवात् ॥
अथ तान्ब्राह्मणान्सर्वान्भक्त्या शक्त्या च पूजयेत् ॥

In front of the deity and before the Brahmins, it should be heard from a Brahmin reciter, not from one born in another varṇa. Then one should worship all those Brahmins with devotion and according to the means available.

दैवस्य पुरतस्तात ब्राह्मणानां तथाग्रतः (daivasya puratastāta brāhmaṇānāṃ tathāgrataḥ) — O dear father before fate and before the brahmins;
ब्राह्मणाद्वाचकाच्छ्राव्यं नान्यवर्णसमुद्भवात् (brāhmaṇādvācakācchrāvyaṃ nānyavarṇasamudbhavāt) — It should be heard from a Brahmin speaker, not from another caste;
अथ तान्ब्राह्मणान्सर्वान्भक्त्या शक्त्या च पूजयेत् (atha tānbrāhmaṇānsarvānbhaktyā śaktyā ca pūjayet) — Then one should worship all those Brahmins with devotion and power

BP 1.47.69

वाचकस्यामले राजन्वाससी सन्निवेदयेत् ।
वाचके पूजिते देवः सदा तुष्यति भास्करः ॥

One should present clean garments to the reciter, O king. When the reciter is honored, Bhāskara is always pleased.

वाचकस्यामले राजन्वाससी सन्निवेदयेत् (vācakasyāmale rājanvāsasī sannivedayet) — O king one should present the immaculate robe to the reciter;
वाचके पूजिते देवः सदा तुष्यति भास्करः (vācake pūjite devaḥ sadā tuṣyati bhāskaraḥ) — The sun is always pleased when the god is worshiped by the speaker

BP 1.47.70

करवीरं यथेष्टं तु तथा रक्तं च चन्दनम् ।
यथेष्टं गुग्गुलं तस्य यथेष्टं पायसं सदा ॥

Oleander and red sandalwood as desired, guggulu as desired, and always rice pudding as desired.

करवीरं यथेष्टं तु तथा रक्तं च चन्दनम् (karavīraṃ yatheṣṭaṃ tu tathā raktaṃ ca candanam) — Caraway seeds and red sandalwood as desired;
यथेष्टं गुग्गुलं तस्य यथेष्टं पायसं सदा (yatheṣṭaṃ guggulaṃ tasya yatheṣṭaṃ pāyasaṃ sadā) — He always had as much guggulu as he wanted and as much payasam as he wanted

BP 1.47.71

यथेष्टा मोदकास्तस्य यथा वै ताम्रभाजनम् ।
यथेष्टं च घृतं तस्य यथेष्टो वाचकः सदा ॥
पुराणं च यथेष्टं वै सवितुः कुरुनन्दन ॥

Sweet cakes as desired, a copper vessel as desired, and ghee as desired. The reciter should always receive as desired, and the sacred account for Sāvitar as desired, O delight of the Kurus.

यथेष्टा मोदकास्तस्य यथा वै ताम्रभाजनम् (yatheṣṭā modakāstasya yathā vai tāmrabhājanam) — The modakas are as desired as the copper vessels;
यथेष्टं च घृतं तस्य यथेष्टो वाचकः सदा (yatheṣṭaṃ ca ghṛtaṃ tasya yatheṣṭo vācakaḥ sadā) — And the ghee as desired is always the speaker of it as desired;
पुराणं च यथेष्टं वै सवितुः कुरुनन्दन (purāṇaṃ ca yatheṣṭaṃ vai savituḥ kurunandana) — And the Puranas are as desired by the Sun, O son of the Kurus

BP 1.47.72

इत्येषा सप्तमी पुण्या शाकाह्वा गोपतेः सदा ।
यामुपोष्य नरो भक्त्या भाग्यवांश्च प्रजायते ॥

Thus this is the sacred seventh day vow, always called the vow of greens, belonging to the Lord of Cows. By fasting on it with devotion, a person becomes fortunate.

इत्येषा सप्तमी पुण्या शाकाह्वा गोपतेः सदा (ityeṣā saptamī puṇyā śākāhvā gopateḥ sadā) — This is the seventh sacred Shakahva of the cowherd man always;
यामुपोष्य नरो भक्त्या भाग्यवांश्च१ प्रजायते (yāmupoṣya naro bhaktyā bhāgyavāṃśca1 prajāyate) — By fasting it a man is born with devotion and fortunate1
