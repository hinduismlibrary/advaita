Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 18 — Brahmā’s Chariot Festival and Sacred First-Lunar-Day Dates

BP 1.18.1

सुमन्तुरुवाच
पौर्णमास्युपवासं तु कृत्वा भक्त्या नराधिप ।
अनेन विधिना यस्तु विरिञ्चिं पूजयेन्नरः ॥

Sumantu said: O king, having observed the full-moon fast with devotion, whoever worships Viriñci in this way—

BP 1.18.2

प्रतिपद्यां महाबाहो स गच्छेद्ब्रह्मणः पदम् ।
ऋग्भिर्विशेषतो देवी विरिञ्चेर्वास्तुदेवताः ॥

—on Pratipad, O mighty-armed, goes to Brahmā’s abode. Especially with Ṛgvedic hymns, [worship] the goddess and the dwelling deities of Viriñci.

BP 1.18.3

कार्त्तिके मासि देवस्य रथयात्रा प्रकीर्तिता ।
यां कृत्वा मानवो भक्त्या याति ब्रह्मसलोकताम् ॥

In Kārttika, the god’s chariot procession is proclaimed. Whoever performs it with devotion goes to Brahmā’s world.

BP 1.18.4

कार्तिके मासि राजेन्द्र पौर्णमास्यां चतुर्मुखम् ।
मार्गेण चर्मणा सार्धं सावित्र्या च परन्तप ॥

On the full moon in Kārttika, O king, the four-faced one should be led along the road together with a hide and Sāvitrī, O scorcher of foes.

BP 1.18.5

भ्रामयेन्नगरं सर्वं नानावाद्यैः समन्वितम् ।
स्थापयेद् भ्रामयित्वा तु सलोकं नगरं नृप ॥

One should take him around the whole city, accompanied by various instruments. Having led him around, one should install him again with the people of the city, O king.

BP 1.18.6

ब्राह्मणं भोजयित्याग्रे शांडिलेयं प्रपूज्य च ।
आरोपयेद्रथे देवं पुण्यवादित्रनिस्वनैः ॥

Having first fed a brāhmaṇa and worshipped Śāṇḍileya, one should mount the god upon the chariot amid the sound of auspicious instruments.

BP 1.18.7

रथाग्रे शांडिलीपुत्रं पूजयित्वा विधानतः ।
ब्राह्मणान्वाचयित्वा च कृत्वा पुण्याहमंगलम् ॥

Having duly worshipped Śāṇḍilī’s son at the front of the chariot, one should have the brāhmaṇas recite and make the auspicious proclamation.

BP 1.18.8

देवमारोपयित्वा तु रथे कुर्यात्प्रजागरम् ।
नानाविधैः प्रेक्षणकैर्ब्रह्मघोषैश्च पुष्कलैः ॥

Having mounted the god on the chariot, one should keep vigil with various spectacles and abundant recitations of Brahmā.

BP 1.18.9

कृत्वा प्रजागरं ह्येवं प्रभाते ब्राह्मणं नृप ।
भोजयित्वा यथाशक्त्या भक्ष्यभोज्यैरनेकशः ॥

Having kept vigil in this way, at dawn, O king, one should feed a brāhmaṇa according to one’s ability with many kinds of foods to chew and eat.

BP 1.18.10

पूजयित्वा जनं वीर वज्रेण विधिवन्नृप ।
बीजेन च महाबाहो पयसा पायसेन च ॥

Having duly honored the people with a vajra, seeds, milk, and rice pudding, O hero, O mighty-armed king—

BP 1.18.11

ब्राह्मणान्वाचयित्वा च च्छांदेन विधिना नृप ।
कृत्वा पुण्याहशब्दं च रथं च भ्रामयेत्पुरे ॥

—one should have the brāhmaṇas recite according to the prescribed meter, make the auspicious proclamation, and lead the chariot through the city, O king.

BP 1.18.12

चतुर्वेदविदैर्विप्रैर्भ्रामयेद्ब्रह्मणो रथम् ।
बह्वृचाथर्वणोच्चारैश्छन्दोगाध्वर्युभिस्तथा ॥

Brāhmaṇas who know the four Vedas should lead Brahmā’s chariot, with the recitations of the Bahvṛcas, Atharvans, Chāndogas, and Adhvaryus.

BP 1.18.13

भ्रामयेद्देवदेवस्य सुरज्येष्ठस्य तं रथम् ।
प्रदक्षिणं पुरं सर्वं मार्गेण सुसमेन तु ॥

One should lead that chariot of the god of gods, the eldest of the gods, clockwise around the whole city along a smooth road.

BP 1.18.14

न वोढव्यो रथो वीर शूद्रेण शुभमिच्छता ।
नारुहेत रथं प्राज्ञो मुक्त्वैकं भोजकं नृप ॥

The chariot should not be drawn by a Śūdra who desires auspiciousness, O hero. A wise man should not mount the chariot, except for one Bhojaka, O king.

BP 1.18.15

ब्रह्मणो दक्षिणे पार्श्वे सावित्रीं स्थापयेन्नृप ।
भोजको वामपार्श्वे तु पुरतः कञ्जजं न्यसेत् ॥

O king, Sāvitrī should be placed at Brahmā’s right side, the Bhojaka at the left side, and the lotus-born one in front.

BP 1.18.16

एवं तूर्यनिनादैस्तु शंखशब्दैश्च पुष्कलैः ।
भ्रामयित्वा रथं राजन्पुरं सर्वं प्रदक्षिणम् ॥
स्वस्थाने स्थापयेद्भूयः कृत्वा नीराजनं बुधः ॥

Thus, with the sounds of musical instruments and abundant conch-blasts, having led the chariot clockwise around the whole city, the wise one should again place it in its own place and perform the waving of lights.

BP 1.18.17

य एवं कुरुते यात्रां भक्त्या यश्चापि पश्यति ।
रथं चाकर्षते यस्तु स गच्छेद्ब्रह्मणः पदम् ॥

Whoever performs this procession with devotion, whoever sees it, and whoever pulls the chariot goes to Brahmā’s abode.

BP 1.18.18

कार्तिके मास्यमावास्यां यस्तु दीपप्रदीपनम् ।
शालायां ब्रह्मणः कुर्यात्स गच्छेद्ब्रह्मणः पदम् ॥

Whoever lights lamps in Brahmā’s hall on the new-moon day in Kārttika goes to Brahmā’s abode.

BP 1.18.19

प्रतिपदि ब्राह्मणांश्चापि गुडमिश्रैः प्रदीपकैः ।
वासोभिरहतैश्चापि स गच्छेद्ब्रह्मणः पदम् ॥

On Pratipad, whoever honors brāhmaṇas with lamps mixed with molasses and with new garments also goes to Brahmā’s abode.

BP 1.18.20

गंधैपुष्पैर्नवैर्वस्त्रैरात्मानं पूजयेच्च यः ।
तस्यां प्रतिपदायां तु स गच्छेद्ब्रह्मणः पदम् ॥

Whoever worships himself with fresh fragrances, flowers, and garments on that Pratipad goes to Brahmā’s abode.

BP 1.18.21

महापुण्या तिथिरियं बलिराज्यप्रवर्तिनी ।
ब्रह्मणः सुप्रिया नित्यं बालेया परिकीर्तिता ॥

This lunar date is greatly meritorious, inaugurates King Bali’s reign, is always dear to Brahmā, and is known as Bāleyā.

BP 1.18.22

ब्राह्मणान्पूजयित्वास्यामात्मानं च विशेषतः ।
स याति परमं स्थानं विष्णोरमिततेजसः ॥

Having honored the brāhmaṇas on this day, and especially himself, a person goes to the supreme abode of Viṣṇu of immeasurable radiance.

BP 1.18.23

चैत्रे मासि महाबाहो पुण्या प्रतिपदा परा ।
तस्यां यः श्वपचं स्पृष्ट्वा स्नानं कुर्यान्नरोत्तम ।

In the month of Caitra, O mighty-armed, there is a supreme, meritorious Pratipad. The best of men who touches a dog-cooker on that day should bathe.

BP 1.18.24

न तस्य दुरितं किञ्चिन्नाधयो व्याधयो नृप ।
भवन्ति कुरुशार्दूल तस्मात्स्नानं प्रवर्तयेत् ॥

No sin, no mental affliction, and no disease befall him, O king, O tiger among the Kurus; therefore one should undertake the bath.

BP 1.18.25

दिव्यं नीराजनं तद्धि सर्वरोगविनाशनम् ।
गोमहिष्यादि यत्किंचित्तत्सर्वं भूषयेन्नृप ॥

That divine waving of lights destroys all disease. Whatever there is, beginning with cows and buffaloes, one should decorate all of it, O king.

BP 1.18.26

तैलशस्त्रादिभिर्वस्त्रस्तोरणाधस्ततो नयेत् ।
ब्राह्मणानां तथा भोज्यं कुर्यात्कुरुकुलोद्वह ॥

With oil, weapons, and the like, and with garments, one should lead them beneath a festoon; one should also prepare food for the brāhmaṇas, O pride of the Kuru lineage.

BP 1.18.27

तिस्रो ह्येताः पराः प्रोक्तास्तिथयः कुरुनन्दन ।
कार्त्तिकेश्वयुजे मासि चैत्रे मासे च भारत ॥

These three lunar dates are declared supreme, O delight of the Kurus: in Kārttika, in Āśvayuja, and in Caitra, O Bharata.

BP 1.18.28

स्नानं दानं शतगुणं कार्त्तिके या तिथिर्नृप ।
बलिराज्याप्तिसुखदा पांशुलाशुभनाशिनी ॥

On the Kārttika lunar date, O king, bathing and giving are a hundredfold; it gives the happiness of attaining Bali’s kingdom and destroys dust, impurity, and inauspiciousness.
