Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 18 — Brahmā’s Chariot Festival and Sacred Pratipad Dates

BP 1.18.1

सुमन्तुरुवाच
पौर्णमास्युपवासं तु कृत्वा भक्त्या नराधिप ।
अनेन विधिना यस्तु विरिञ्चिं पूजयेन्नरः ॥

Sumantu said: ‘O ruler of people, a person who fasts on the full moon and then worships Viriñci with devotion according to this procedure—

BP 1.18.2

प्रतिपद्यां महाबाहो स गच्छेद्ब्रह्मणः पदम् ।
ऋग्भिर्विशेषतो देवी विरिञ्चेर्वास्तुदेवताः ॥

—on Pratipad, O mighty-armed one, goes to Brahmā’s abode. The goddess and the Vāstu deities of Viriñci should especially be honoured with Ṛgvedic verses.

BP 1.18.3

कार्त्तिके मासि देवस्य रथयात्रा प्रकीर्तिता ।
यां कृत्वा मानवो भक्त्या याति ब्रह्मसलोकताम् ॥

The god’s chariot festival is prescribed in the month of Kārttika. A person who performs it with devotion attains residence in Brahmā’s world.

BP 1.18.4

कार्तिके मासि राजेन्द्र पौर्णमास्यां चतुर्मुखम् ।
मार्गेण चर्मणा सार्धं सावित्र्या च परन्तप ॥

On the full moon in Kārttika, O king of kings and scorcher of foes, the four-faced god should be taken out together with Sāvitrī, using the prescribed route and protective covering.

BP 1.18.5

भ्रामयेन्नगरं सर्वं नानावाद्यैः समन्वितम् ।
स्थापयेद् भ्रामयित्वा तु सलोकं नगरं नृप ॥

The image should be carried around the entire city accompanied by many instruments. After the circuit, O king, it should be reinstalled together with the gathered townspeople.

BP 1.18.6

ब्राह्मणं भोजयित्याग्रे शांडिलेयं प्रपूज्य च ।
आरोपयेद्रथे देवं पुण्यवादित्रनिस्वनैः ॥

First one should feed a brāhmaṇa and worship the Śāṇḍileya officiant, then mount the god upon the chariot amid the sounds of auspicious instruments.

BP 1.18.7

रथाग्रे शांडिलीपुत्रं पूजयित्वा विधानतः ।
ब्राह्मणान्वाचयित्वा च कृत्वा पुण्याहमंगलम् ॥

After worshipping Śāṇḍilī’s son according to rule at the front of the chariot, one should have brāhmaṇas recite and perform the auspicious puṇyāha proclamation.

BP 1.18.8

देवमारोपयित्वा तु रथे कुर्यात्प्रजागरम् ।
नानाविधैः प्रेक्षणकैर्ब्रह्मघोषैश्च पुष्कलैः ॥

Having mounted the deity upon the chariot, one should keep a night vigil with many kinds of spectacles and abundant recitation in praise of Brahmā.

BP 1.18.9

कृत्वा प्रजागरं ह्येवं प्रभाते ब्राह्मणं नृप ।
भोजयित्वा यथाशक्त्या भक्ष्यभोज्यैरनेकशः ॥

After maintaining the vigil in this way, O king, at dawn one should feed a brāhmaṇa according to one’s ability with many kinds of foods to chew and eat.

BP 1.18.10

पूजयित्वा जनं वीर वज्रेण विधिवन्नृप ।
बीजेन च महाबाहो पयसा पायसेन च ॥

O hero and mighty-armed king, the assembled people should then be honoured according to rule with the vajra rite, seed-grain, milk, and milk-rice.

BP 1.18.11

ब्राह्मणान्वाचयित्वा च च्छांदेन विधिना नृप ।
कृत्वा पुण्याहशब्दं च रथं च भ्रामयेत्पुरे ॥

After having the brāhmaṇas recite according to the metrical procedure, O king, and proclaiming puṇyāha, one should draw the chariot through the city.

BP 1.18.12

चतुर्वेदविदैर्विप्रैर्भ्रामयेद्ब्रह्मणो रथम् ।
बह्वृचाथर्वणोच्चारैश्छन्दोगाध्वर्युभिस्तथा ॥

The chariot of Brahmā should be drawn by brāhmaṇas who know the four Vedas, with recitations by Bahvṛcas, Atharvavedins, Chāndogas, and Adhvaryus.

BP 1.18.13

भ्रामयेद्देवदेवस्य सुरज्येष्ठस्य तं रथम् ।
प्रदक्षिणं पुरं सर्वं मार्गेण सुसमेन तु ॥

The chariot of the god of gods, eldest of the deities, should travel clockwise around the whole city upon a smooth and level road.

BP 1.18.14

न वोढव्यो रथो वीर शूद्रेण शुभमिच्छता ।
नारुहेत रथं प्राज्ञो मुक्त्वैकं भोजकं नृप ॥

O hero, a Śūdra desiring auspiciousness should not draw the chariot. A wise man should not mount the chariot, O king, except for the one Bhojaka.

BP 1.18.15

ब्रह्मणो दक्षिणे पार्श्वे सावित्रीं स्थापयेन्नृप ।
भोजको वामपार्श्वे तु पुरतः कञ्जजं न्यसेत् ॥

O king, Sāvitrī should be placed at Brahmā’s right side, the Bhojaka officiant at the left, and the lotus-born god set toward the front.

BP 1.18.16

एवं तूर्यनिनादैस्तु शंखशब्दैश्च पुष्कलैः ।
भ्रामयित्वा रथं राजन्पुरं सर्वं प्रदक्षिणम् ॥
स्वस्थाने स्थापयेद्भूयः कृत्वा नीराजनं बुधः ॥

Thus, amid the sounds of trumpets and abundant conch-blasts, O king, the wise should draw the chariot clockwise through the entire city, return it to its place, and perform the waving-of-lights ceremony.

BP 1.18.17

य एवं कुरुते यात्रां भक्त्या यश्चापि पश्यति ।
रथं चाकर्षते यस्तु स गच्छेद्ब्रह्मणः पदम् ॥

Whoever performs this procession with devotion, whoever sees it, and whoever pulls the chariot goes to Brahmā’s abode.

BP 1.18.18

कार्तिके मास्यमावास्यां यस्तु दीपप्रदीपनम् ।
शालायां ब्रह्मणः कुर्यात्स गच्छेद्ब्रह्मणः पदम् ॥

One who lights lamps in Brahmā’s shrine on the new-moon day of Kārttika goes to Brahmā’s abode.

BP 1.18.19

प्रतिपदि ब्राह्मणांश्चापि गुडमिश्रैः प्रदीपकैः ।
वासोभिरहतैश्चापि स गच्छेद्ब्रह्मणः पदम् ॥

One who on Pratipad honours brāhmaṇas with lamps mixed with molasses and with unworn garments also goes to Brahmā’s abode.

BP 1.18.20

गंधैपुष्पैर्नवैर्वस्त्रैरात्मानं पूजयेच्च यः ।
तस्यां प्रतिपदायां तु स गच्छेद्ब्रह्मणः पदम् ॥

One who on that Pratipad honours and adorns oneself with fragrances, flowers, and new garments goes to Brahmā’s abode.

BP 1.18.21

महापुण्या तिथिरियं बलिराज्यप्रवर्तिनी ।
ब्रह्मणः सुप्रिया नित्यं बालेया परिकीर्तिता ॥

This lunar date is exceedingly sacred and inaugurates the reign of King Bali. It is continually dear to Brahmā and is celebrated as Bāleyā.

BP 1.18.22

ब्राह्मणान्पूजयित्वास्यामात्मानं च विशेषतः ।
स याति परमं स्थानं विष्णोरमिततेजसः ॥

After honouring brāhmaṇas upon it and especially honouring oneself, a person goes to the supreme abode of Viṣṇu of immeasurable radiance.

BP 1.18.23

चैत्रे मासि महाबाहो पुण्या प्रतिपदा परा ।
तस्यां यः श्वपचं स्पृष्ट्वा स्नानं कुर्यान्नरोत्तम ।

O mighty-armed one, another supremely sacred Pratipad occurs in the month of Caitra. The source prescribes that on it a person should bathe after contact with a śvapaca.

BP 1.18.24

न तस्य दुरितं किञ्चिन्नाधयो व्याधयो नृप ।
भवन्ति कुरुशार्दूल तस्मात्स्नानं प्रवर्तयेत् ॥

O king and tiger among the Kurus, no wrongdoing, mental affliction, or bodily disease is said to remain for that person; therefore the bathing rite should be undertaken.

BP 1.18.25

दिव्यं नीराजनं तद्धि सर्वरोगविनाशनम् ।
गोमहिष्यादि यत्किंचित्तत्सर्वं भूषयेन्नृप ॥

That divine waving-of-lights ceremony destroys every disease. O king, all cattle, buffaloes, and other domestic animals should be decorated.

BP 1.18.26

तैलशस्त्रादिभिर्वस्त्रस्तोरणाधस्ततो नयेत् ।
ब्राह्मणानां तथा भोज्यं कुर्यात्कुरुकुलोद्वह ॥

They should be anointed with oil, marked or protected with implements, clothed, and led beneath an ornamental gateway; then food should be prepared for brāhmaṇas, O bearer of the Kuru lineage.

BP 1.18.27

तिस्रो ह्येताः पराः प्रोक्तास्तिथयः कुरुनन्दन ।
कार्त्तिकेश्वयुजे मासि चैत्रे मासे च भारत ॥

O delight of the Kurus and descendant of Bharata, these three lunar dates are declared supreme: those in Kārttika, Āśvayuja, and Caitra.

BP 1.18.28

स्नानं दानं शतगुणं कार्त्तिके या तिथिर्नृप ।
बलिराज्याप्तिसुखदा पांशुलाशुभनाशिनी ॥

On the Kārttika date, O king, bathing and giving produce a hundredfold result; it bestows the happiness associated with Bali’s reign and removes dust, impurity, and inauspiciousness.
