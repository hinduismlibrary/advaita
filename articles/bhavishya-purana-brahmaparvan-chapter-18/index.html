Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 18 — Brahmā’s Chariot Festival and Sacred Pratipad Dates

BP 1.18.1

सुमन्तुरुवाच
पौर्णमास्युपवासं तु कृत्वा भक्त्या नराधिप ।
अनेन विधिना यस्तु विरिञ्चिं पूजयेन्नरः ॥

Sumantu said: ‘O ruler of people, a person who fasts on the full moon and then worships Viriñci with devotion according to this procedure—

Word-for-word
सुमन्तुः उवाच (sumantuḥ uvāca) — Sumantu said;
पौर्णमासी-उपवासम् कृत्वा (paurṇamāsī-upavāsam kṛtvā) — fasting on full moon;
भक्त्या (bhaktyā) — with devotion;
नर-अधिप (nara-adhipa) — O ruler of people;
अनेन विधिना (anena vidhinā) — by this procedure;
यः विरिञ्चिम् पूजयेत् (yaḥ viriñcim pūjayet) — one who worships Viriñci
Transliteration
sumanturuvāca
paurṇamāsyupavāsaṃ tu kṛtvā bhaktyā narādhipa |
anena vidhinā yastu viriñciṃ pūjayennaraḥ | | 1

BP 1.18.2

प्रतिपद्यां महाबाहो स गच्छेद्ब्रह्मणः पदम् ।
ऋग्भिर्विशेषतो देवी विरिञ्चेर्वास्तुदेवताः ॥

—on Pratipad, O mighty-armed one, goes to Brahmā’s abode. The goddess and the Vāstu deities of Viriñci should especially be honoured with Ṛgvedic verses.

Word-for-word
प्रतिपद्याम् (pratipadyām) — on Pratipad;
महा-बाहो (mahā-bāho) — O mighty-armed;
सः गच्छेत् ब्रह्मणः पदम् (saḥ gacchet brahmaṇaḥ padam) — he goes to Brahmā’s abode;
ऋग्भिः विशेषतः (ṛgbhiḥ viśeṣataḥ) — especially with Ṛgvedic verses;
देवी (devī) — goddess;
विरिञ्चेः वास्तु-देवताः (viriñceḥ vāstu-devatāḥ) — Vāstu deities of Viriñci
Transliteration
pratipadyāṃ mahābāho sa gacchedbrahmaṇaḥ padam |
ṛgbhirviśeṣato 1 devī viriñcervāstudevatāḥ | | 2

BP 1.18.3

कार्त्तिके मासि देवस्य रथयात्रा प्रकीर्तिता ।
यां कृत्वा मानवो भक्त्या याति ब्रह्मसलोकताम् ॥

The god’s chariot festival is prescribed in the month of Kārttika. A person who performs it with devotion attains residence in Brahmā’s world.

Word-for-word
कार्त्तिके मासि (kārttike māsi) — in Kārttika month;
देवस्य रथ-यात्रा (devasya ratha-yātrā) — god’s chariot festival;
प्रकीर्तिता (prakīrtitā) — prescribed;
याम् कृत्वा मानवः (yām kṛtvā mānavaḥ) — person performing it;
भक्त्या (bhaktyā) — devotionally;
याति ब्रह्म-सलोकताम् (yāti brahma-salokatām) — reaches Brahmā’s world
Transliteration
kārttike māsi devasya rathayātrā prakīrtitā |
yāṃ kṛtvā mānavo bhaktyā yāti brahmasalokatām | | 3

BP 1.18.4

कार्तिके मासि राजेन्द्र पौर्णमास्यां चतुर्मुखम् ।
मार्गेण चर्मणा सार्धं सावित्र्या च परन्तप ॥

On the full moon in Kārttika, O king of kings and scorcher of foes, the four-faced god should be taken out together with Sāvitrī, using the prescribed route and protective covering.

Word-for-word
कार्त्तिके मासि (kārttike māsi) — in Kārttika month;
राजेन्द्र (rājendra) — O king of kings;
पौर्णमास्याम् (paurṇamāsyām) — on full moon;
चतुर्मुखम् (caturmukham) — four-faced god;
मार्गेण (mārgeṇa) — by prescribed route;
चर्मणा सार्धम् (carmaṇā sārdham) — with protective covering;
सावित्र्या च (sāvitryā ca) — and with Sāvitrī;
परन्तप (parantapa) — O scorcher of foes
Transliteration
kārtike māsi rājendra paurṇamāsyāṃ caturmukham |
mārgeṇa carmaṇā sārdhaṃ sāvitryā ca parantapa | | 4

BP 1.18.5

भ्रामयेन्नगरं सर्वं नानावाद्यैः समन्वितम् ।
स्थापयेद् भ्रामयित्वा तु सलोकं नगरं नृप ॥

The image should be carried around the entire city accompanied by many instruments. After the circuit, O king, it should be reinstalled together with the gathered townspeople.

Word-for-word
भ्रामयेत् नगरम् सर्वम् (bhrāmayet nagaram sarvam) — should carry around whole city;
नाना-वाद्यैः समन्वितम् (nānā-vādyaiḥ samanvitam) — accompanied by varied instruments;
स्थापयेत् भ्रामयित्वा तु (sthāpayet bhrāmayitvā tu) — after circuit should install;
स-लोकम् नगरम् (sa-lokam nagaram) — with city’s people;
नृप (nṛpa) — O king
Transliteration
bhrāmayennagaraṃ sarvaṃ nānāvādyaiḥ samanvitam |
sthāpayed bhrāmayitvā tu salokaṃ nagaraṃ nṛpa | | 5

BP 1.18.6

ब्राह्मणं भोजयित्याग्रे शांडिलेयं प्रपूज्य च ।
आरोपयेद्रथे देवं पुण्यवादित्रनिस्वनैः ॥

First one should feed a brāhmaṇa and worship the Śāṇḍileya officiant, then mount the god upon the chariot amid the sounds of auspicious instruments.

Word-for-word
ब्राह्मणम् भोजयित्वा अग्रे (brāhmaṇam bhojayitvā agre) — first feeding a brāhmaṇa;
शाण्डिलेयम् प्रपूज्य च (śāṇḍileyam prapūjya ca) — worshipping Śāṇḍileya;
आरोपयेत् रथे देवम् (āropayet rathe devam) — should mount god on chariot;
पुण्य-वादित्र-निस्वनैः (puṇya-vāditra-nisvanaiḥ) — with sounds of auspicious instruments
Transliteration
brāhmaṇaṃ bhojayityāgre śāṃḍileyaṃ prapūjya ca |
āropayedrathe devaṃ puṇyavāditranisvanaiḥ | | 6

BP 1.18.7

रथाग्रे शांडिलीपुत्रं पूजयित्वा विधानतः ।
ब्राह्मणान्वाचयित्वा च कृत्वा पुण्याहमंगलम् ॥

After worshipping Śāṇḍilī’s son according to rule at the front of the chariot, one should have brāhmaṇas recite and perform the auspicious puṇyāha proclamation.

Word-for-word
रथ-अग्रे (ratha-agre) — at chariot front;
शाण्डिली-पुत्रम् पूजयित्वा (śāṇḍilī-putram pūjayitvā) — worshipping Śāṇḍilī’s son;
विधानतः (vidhānataḥ) — according to rule;
ब्राह्मणान् वाचयित्वा च (brāhmaṇān vācayitvā ca) — having brāhmaṇas recite;
कृत्वा पुण्याह-मङ्गलम् (kṛtvā puṇyāha-maṅgalam) — performing auspicious puṇyāha
Transliteration
rathāgre śāṃḍilīputraṃ pūjayitvā vidhānataḥ |
brāhmaṇānvācayitvā ca kṛtvā puṇyāhamaṃgalam | | 7

BP 1.18.8

देवमारोपयित्वा तु रथे कुर्यात्प्रजागरम् ।
नानाविधैः प्रेक्षणकैर्ब्रह्मघोषैश्च पुष्कलैः ॥

Having mounted the deity upon the chariot, one should keep a night vigil with many kinds of spectacles and abundant recitation in praise of Brahmā.

Word-for-word
देवम् आरोपयित्वा तु (devam āropayitvā tu) — mounting deity;
रथे (rathe) — on chariot;
कुर्यात् प्रजागरम् (kuryāt prajāgaram) — should keep vigil;
नाना-विधैः प्रेक्षणकैः (nānā-vidhaiḥ prekṣaṇakaiḥ) — with various spectacles;
ब्रह्म-घोषैः च पुष्कलैः (brahma-ghoṣaiḥ ca puṣkalaiḥ) — and abundant Brahmā chants
Transliteration
devamāropayitvā tu rathe kuryātprajāgaram |
nānāvidhaiḥ prekṣaṇakairbrahmaghoṣaiśca puṣkalaiḥ | | 8

BP 1.18.9

कृत्वा प्रजागरं ह्येवं प्रभाते ब्राह्मणं नृप ।
भोजयित्वा यथाशक्त्या भक्ष्यभोज्यैरनेकशः ॥

After maintaining the vigil in this way, O king, at dawn one should feed a brāhmaṇa according to one’s ability with many kinds of foods to chew and eat.

Word-for-word
कृत्वा प्रजागरम् एवम् (kṛtvā prajāgaram evam) — maintaining vigil thus;
प्रभाते (prabhāte) — at dawn;
ब्राह्मणम् नृप (brāhmaṇam nṛpa) — brāhmaṇa, O king;
भोजयित्वा यथा-शक्त्या (bhojayitvā yathā-śaktyā) — feeding according to ability;
भक्ष्य-भोज्यैः अनेकशः (bhakṣya-bhojyaiḥ anekaśaḥ) — with many chewable and prepared foods
Transliteration
kṛtvā prajāgaraṃ hyevaṃ prabhāte brāhmaṇaṃ nṛpa |
bhojayitvā yathāśaktyā bhakṣyabhojyairanekaśaḥ | | 9

BP 1.18.10

पूजयित्वा जनं वीर वज्रेण विधिवन्नृप ।
बीजेन च महाबाहो पयसा पायसेन च ॥

O hero and mighty-armed king, the assembled people should then be honoured according to rule with the vajra rite, seed-grain, milk, and milk-rice.

Word-for-word
पूजयित्वा जनम् (pūjayitvā janam) — honouring the people;
वीर (vīra) — O hero;
वज्रेण विधि-वत् (vajreṇa vidhi-vat) — with vajra rite according to rule;
नृप (nṛpa) — O king;
बीजेन च (bījena ca) — with seed;
महा-बाहो (mahā-bāho) — O mighty-armed;
पयसा (payasā) — with milk;
पायसेन च (pāyasena ca) — and milk-rice
Transliteration
pūjayitvā janaṃ2 vīra vajreṇa vidhivannṛpa |
bījena ca mahābāho payasā pāyasena ca | | 1.18.10

BP 1.18.11

ब्राह्मणान्वाचयित्वा च च्छांदेन विधिना नृप ।
कृत्वा पुण्याहशब्दं च रथं च भ्रामयेत्पुरे ॥

After having the brāhmaṇas recite according to the metrical procedure, O king, and proclaiming puṇyāha, one should draw the chariot through the city.

Word-for-word
ब्राह्मणान् वाचयित्वा च (brāhmaṇān vācayitvā ca) — having brāhmaṇas recite;
छान्देन विधिना (chāndena vidhinā) — by metrical procedure;
नृप (nṛpa) — O king;
कृत्वा पुण्याह-शब्दम् च (kṛtvā puṇyāha-śabdam ca) — proclaiming puṇyāha;
रथम् भ्रामयेत् पुरे (ratham bhrāmayet pure) — should draw chariot through city
Transliteration
brāhmaṇānvācayitvā ca cchāṃdena vidhinā nṛpa |
kṛtvā puṇyāhaśabdaṃ ca rathaṃ ca bhrāmayetpure | | 11

BP 1.18.12

चतुर्वेदविदैर्विप्रैर्भ्रामयेद्ब्रह्मणो रथम् ।
बह्वृचाथर्वणोच्चारैश्छन्दोगाध्वर्युभिस्तथा ॥

The chariot of Brahmā should be drawn by brāhmaṇas who know the four Vedas, with recitations by Bahvṛcas, Atharvavedins, Chāndogas, and Adhvaryus.

Word-for-word
चतुर्-वेद-विदैः विप्रैः (catur-veda-vidaiḥ vipraiḥ) — by brāhmaṇas knowing four Vedas;
भ्रामयेत् ब्रह्मणः रथम् (bhrāmayet brahmaṇaḥ ratham) — should draw Brahmā’s chariot;
बह्वृच (bahvṛca) — Ṛgvedic reciters;
आथर्वण-उच्चारैः (ātharvaṇa-uccāraiḥ) — Atharvavedic utterances;
छान्दोगैः (chāndogaiḥ) — Sāmavedins;
अध्वर्युभिः तथा (adhvaryubhiḥ tathā) — and Yajurvedic officiants
Transliteration
caturvedavidairviprairbhrāmayedbrahmaṇo ratham |
bahvṛcātharvaṇoccāraiśchandogādhvaryubhistathā | | 12

BP 1.18.13

भ्रामयेद्देवदेवस्य सुरज्येष्ठस्य तं रथम् ।
प्रदक्षिणं पुरं सर्वं मार्गेण सुसमेन तु ॥

The chariot of the god of gods, eldest of the deities, should travel clockwise around the whole city upon a smooth and level road.

Word-for-word
भ्रामयेत् (bhrāmayet) — should draw;
देव-देवस्य (deva-devasya) — of god of gods;
सुर-ज्येष्ठस्य (sura-jyeṣṭhasya) — eldest of deities;
तम् रथम् (tam ratham) — that chariot;
प्रदक्षिणम् (pradakṣiṇam) — clockwise;
पुरम् सर्वम् (puram sarvam) — whole city;
मार्गेण सु-समेन (mārgeṇa su-samena) — by smooth level road
Transliteration
bhrāmayeddevadevasya surajyeṣṭhasya taṃ ratham |
pradakṣiṇaṃ puraṃ sarvaṃ mārgeṇa susamena tu | | 13

BP 1.18.14

न वोढव्यो रथो वीर शूद्रेण शुभमिच्छता ।
नारुहेत रथं प्राज्ञो मुक्त्वैकं भोजकं नृप ॥

O hero, a Śūdra desiring auspiciousness should not draw the chariot. A wise man should not mount the chariot, O king, except for the one Bhojaka.

Word-for-word
न वोढव्यः रथः (na voḍhavyaḥ rathaḥ) — chariot not to be drawn;
वीर (vīra) — O hero;
शूद्रेण (śūdreṇa) — by śūdra;
शुभम् इच्छता (śubham icchatā) — by one desiring auspiciousness;
न आरुहेत् रथम् प्राज्ञः (na āruhet ratham prājñaḥ) — discerning person should not mount chariot;
मुक्त्वा एकम् भोजकम् (muktvā ekam bhojakam) — except one Bhojaka;
नृप (nṛpa) — O king
Transliteration
na voḍhavyo ratho vīra śūdreṇa śubhamicchatā |
nāruheta rathaṃ prājño muktvaikaṃ bhojakaṃ nṛpa | | 14

BP 1.18.15

ब्रह्मणो दक्षिणे पार्श्वे सावित्रीं स्थापयेन्नृप ।
भोजको वामपार्श्वे तु पुरतः कञ्जजं न्यसेत् ॥

O king, Sāvitrī should be placed at Brahmā’s right side, the Bhojaka officiant at the left, and the lotus-born god set toward the front.

Word-for-word
ब्रह्मणः दक्षिणे पार्श्वे (brahmaṇaḥ dakṣiṇe pārśve) — at Brahmā’s right side;
सावित्रिं स्थापयेत् (sāvitriṃ sthāpayet) — should place Sāvitrī;
नृप (nṛpa) — O king;
भोजकः वाम-पार्श्वे (bhojakaḥ vāma-pārśve) — Bhojaka on left side;
पुरतः कञ्ज-जम् न्यसेत् (purataḥ kañja-jam nyaset) — place lotus-born toward front
Transliteration
brahmaṇo dakṣiṇe pārśve sāvitrīṃ sthāpayennṛpa |
bhojako vāmapārśve tu purataḥ kañjajaṃ nyaset | | 15

BP 1.18.16

एवं तूर्यनिनादैस्तु शंखशब्दैश्च पुष्कलैः ।
भ्रामयित्वा रथं राजन्पुरं सर्वं प्रदक्षिणम् ॥
स्वस्थाने स्थापयेद्भूयः कृत्वा नीराजनं बुधः ॥

Thus, amid the sounds of trumpets and abundant conch-blasts, O king, the wise should draw the chariot clockwise through the entire city, return it to its place, and perform the waving-of-lights ceremony.

Word-for-word
एवम् (evam) — thus;
तूर्य-निनादैः (tūrya-ninādaiḥ) — with trumpet sounds;
शङ्ख-शब्दैः च पुष्कलैः (śaṅkha-śabdaiḥ ca puṣkalaiḥ) — abundant conch sounds;
भ्रामयित्वा रथम् (bhrāmayitvā ratham) — drawing chariot;
पुरम् सर्वम् प्रदक्षिणम् (puram sarvam pradakṣiṇam) — clockwise through whole city;
स्व-स्थाने स्थापयेत् भूयः (sva-sthāne sthāpayet bhūyaḥ) — return to own place;
कृत्वा नीराजनम् बुधः (kṛtvā nīrājanam budhaḥ) — wise performing waving of lights
Transliteration
evaṃ tūryaninādaistu śaṃkhaśabdaiśca puṣkalaiḥ |
bhrāmayitvā rathaṃ rājanpuraṃ sarvaṃ pradakṣiṇam | |
svasthāne sthāpayedbhūyaḥ kṛtvā nīrājanaṃ budhaḥ | | 16

BP 1.18.17

य एवं कुरुते यात्रां भक्त्या यश्चापि पश्यति ।
रथं चाकर्षते यस्तु स गच्छेद्ब्रह्मणः पदम् ॥

Whoever performs this procession with devotion, whoever sees it, and whoever pulls the chariot goes to Brahmā’s abode.

Word-for-word
यः एवम् कुरुते यात्राम् (yaḥ evam kurute yātrām) — whoever performs procession thus;
भक्त्या (bhaktyā) — devotionally;
यः च अपि पश्यति (yaḥ ca api paśyati) — whoever sees;
रथम् च आकर्षते यः (ratham ca ākarṣate yaḥ) — whoever pulls chariot;
सः गच्छेत् ब्रह्मणः पदम् (saḥ gacchet brahmaṇaḥ padam) — goes to Brahmā’s abode
Transliteration
ya evaṃ kurute yātrāṃ bhaktyā yaścāpi paśyati |
rathaṃ cākarṣate yastu sa gacchedbrahmaṇaḥ padam | | 17

BP 1.18.18

कार्तिके मास्यमावास्यां यस्तु दीपप्रदीपनम् ।
शालायां ब्रह्मणः कुर्यात्स गच्छेद्ब्रह्मणः पदम् ॥

One who lights lamps in Brahmā’s shrine on the new-moon day of Kārttika goes to Brahmā’s abode.

Word-for-word
कार्त्तिके मासि (kārttike māsi) — in Kārttika month;
अमावास्याम् (amāvāsyām) — on new moon;
यः तु दीप-प्रदीपनम् (yaḥ tu dīpa-pradīpanam) — one who lights lamps;
शालायाम् ब्रह्मणः कुर्यात् (śālāyām brahmaṇaḥ kuryāt) — in Brahmā’s shrine;
सः गच्छेत् ब्रह्मणः पदम् (saḥ gacchet brahmaṇaḥ padam) — goes to Brahmā’s abode
Transliteration
kārtike māsyamāvāsyāṃ yastu dīpapradīpanam |
