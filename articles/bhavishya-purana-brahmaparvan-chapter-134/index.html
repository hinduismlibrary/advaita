Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 134 — Auspicious Days and Solar Consecration

BP 1.134.1

नारद उवाच।
प्रतिपच्य द्वितीया च चतुर्थी पञ्चमी तथा।
दशमी त्रयोदशी चैव पौर्णमासी च कीर्तिता ॥

Nārada said: The first, second, fourth, fifth, tenth, and thirteenth lunar days, and the full-moon day, are declared.

BP 1.134.2

सोमो बृहस्पतिश्चैव शुक्रश्चैव बुधस्तथा।
एते सौम्या ग्रहाः प्रोक्ताः प्रतिष्ठायज्ञकर्मणि ॥

The Moon, Jupiter, Venus, and Mercury: these planets are declared auspicious for the consecration rite.

BP 1.134.3

त्रिषूत्तरासु रेवत्यामश्विन्यां ब्राह्मभे तथा।
पुनर्वस्वोस्तथा हस्ते वासवे श्रवणेऽथवा।
भरण्यां चैव नक्षत्रे भानोः स्थापनमुत्तमम् ॥

In the three Uttara constellations, Revatī, Aśvinī, Brāhmabha, Punarvasu, Hasta, Vāsava, Śravaṇa, or Bharaṇī, the installation of the Sun is most excellent.

BP 1.134.4

शोधयित्वा तु वै भूमिं तुषकेशविवर्जिताम्।
वालुकाङ्गारपाषाणास्थिविहीनां विशोध्य तु ॥

The ground should be cleared, free of husks and hair; then it should be purified, free of sand, charcoal, stones, and bones.

BP 1.134.5

चतुर्हस्तसमायुक्ता वेदी विस्तरतो रवेः ॥

The altar for the Sun should be four cubits in width.

BP 1.134.6

मण्डपस्तु प्रमाणेन दशहस्तः समंततः।
मण्डलं वृक्षशाखाभिः कारयेद्विधिपूर्वकम् ॥

The pavilion should be ten cubits on every side; the ritual design should be made properly with branches of trees.

BP 1.134.7

नदीसङ्गमतीरोत्थां मृत्तिकां च समानयेत्।
उपलिप्य ततो भूमिं कारयेत्कुण्डमुत्तमम् ॥

One should bring clay from the bank of a river confluence; after plastering the ground, one should make an excellent fire pit.

BP 1.134.8

चतुरस्रं श्रिया युक्तं पूर्वं कुण्डं तु कारयेत्।
दक्षिणे चार्धचन्द्रं स्याद्वारुण्यां दिशि वर्तुलम् ॥

One should make the eastern fire pit square and prosperous; the southern one should be a half-moon, and the western one circular.

BP 1.134.9

पद्माकारं तु वै कुर्यादुत्तरे च विचक्षणः।
तोरणानि ततः कुर्यात्पञ्चहस्तानि सुव्रत ॥

In the north, the discerning one should make it lotus-shaped; then one should make gateways five cubits in measure, O firm-vowed one.

BP 1.134.10

न्यग्रोधोदुम्बरौ चैव बिल्वपालाशमेव च।
अश्वत्थश्च शमी चैव चन्दनश्चेति कीर्तिताः ॥

The trees named are the banyan and cluster fig, wood apple and flame-of-the-forest, sacred fig, desert bean tree, and sandalwood.

BP 1.134.11

शुक्लवस्त्रसमायुक्तश्चित्रपट्टसमन्वितः।
जपमालान्वितः कुर्यात्तोरणानि विचक्षणः ॥

The discerning one, wearing white cloth and patterned silk and carrying a rosary, should make the gateways.

BP 1.134.12

अग्निमीऌएति मन्त्रेण यजेद्वै पूर्वतोरणम्।
इषेत्वोर्जेति मन्त्रेण यजेद्दक्षिणतोरणम् ॥

With the formula “I praise Fire,” one should worship the eastern gateway; with “For nourishment and strength,” one should worship the southern gateway.

BP 1.134.13

अग्न आयाहीति मन्त्रेण पश्चिमं तु समर्चयेत्।
शं नो देवीति मन्त्रेण यजेदुत्तरतोरणम् ॥

With the formula “Come, Fire,” one should duly honour the western gateway; with “May the goddesses bring us good,” one should worship the northern gateway.

BP 1.134.14

कलशांस्तु समादाय हेमगर्भसमन्वितान्।
श्वेतचन्दनपङ्केन कण्ठस्वस्तिकभूषणान् ॥

Having taken the water pitchers filled with gold and adorned at their necks with swastika marks made of white sandal paste,

BP 1.134.15

यवशालिशरावान्नवस्त्रालङ्कारविग्रहान्।
आजिघ्रेति च मन्त्रेण कलशांस्तु निवेशयेत् ॥

with bowls of barley and rice, new garments, ornaments, and images, one should set the pitchers in place with the formula “I breathe in.”

BP 1.134.16

दुकूलश्चित्रपट्टैश्च वेष्टयेत्स्तम्भमालिकाम्।
ध्वजादर्शपताकाभिश्चामरैस्तु वितानकैः ॥

One should wrap the row of pillars with fine cloth and patterned silk, and adorn them with flags, mirrors, banners, fly-whisks, and canopies.

BP 1.134.17

शङ्खघण्टानिनादैश्च गेयमङ्गलवाचनैः।
तूर्यभेरीनिनादैश्च वेदध्वनिसमन्वितैः ॥

With the sounds of conches and bells, auspicious songs and recitations, horns and drums, and Vedic chant;

BP 1.134.18

पुण्यैश्च जयशब्दैश्च कारयेत महोत्सवम्।
पताकाभिर्विचित्राभिः पूजामाल्योपशोभितम् ॥

One should hold a great festival with auspicious cries of victory, adorned with varied banners and made splendid with worship and garlands.

BP 1.134.19

विचित्रस्रग्वितानाढ्यं प्रकीर्णकुसुमाङ्कुरम्।
तन्मध्ये तु कुशास्तीर्णे देवार्चां स्थापयेद् बुधः ॥

Rich in varied garlands and canopies, strewn with flowers and buds, in its midst, spread with sacred grass, the wise one should install the deity’s image.

BP 1.134.20

पताकां पीतवर्णां तु पूर्वे शक्राय दापयेत्।
आग्नेय्यां रक्तवर्णाभां यमाशायां यमोपमान् ॥

In the east one should give a yellow banner to Indra; in the southeast, one red-hued for Yama, and one like Yama in Yama’s direction.

BP 1.134.21

नीलाञ्जनसमप्रख्यां नैर्ऋत्यां च प्रदापयेत्।
वारुण्यां सितवर्णां च कृष्णां वायव्यगोचरे ॥

One should give a banner dark-blue like collyrium in the southwest, a white one in the west, and a black one in the northwest.

BP 1.134.22

हरितां यक्षराजाय ऐशान्यां सर्ववर्णिकाम्।
श्वेतरक्तकचूर्णेन पद्ममालेखयेत्ततः ॥

One should give a green one to the king of the Yakṣas, and a many-coloured one in the northeast; then one should draw a lotus with white and red powder.

BP 1.134.23

वैद्या वेदीति मन्त्रेण वेद्या आलभनं भवेत्।
पूर्वाग्रानुत्तराग्रांश्च कुशानास्तीर्य यत्नतः ॥

With the formula “You are the altar,” the altar should be touched; one should carefully spread the sacred grass with its tips facing east and north.

BP 1.134.24

योगेयोगेति मन्त्रेण कुशश्चास्तरणं भवेत्।
शय्या तत्रैव कर्तव्या दिव्यास्तरणसंयुता ॥

With the formula “At every joining,” sacred grass should be spread as a covering; there one should prepare a couch furnished with a divine coverlet.

BP 1.134.25

गडुके द्वे विचित्रे तु तन्मध्ये स्थापयेद्बुधः।
विचित्रदीपमालाभिर्भक्ष्यभोज्यान्नपानकैः ॥

The wise one should place two decorated vessels in their midst, with varied rows of lamps, foods to taste and eat, cooked rice, and drinks.

BP 1.134.26

पूपकान्सुविचित्त्रान्वै मोदकांश्च प्रदापयेत्।
पायसं कृशरं चैव दध्योदनसमन्वितम् ॥

One should offer beautifully prepared cakes and sweet dumplings, together with rice pudding, a rice-and-bean dish, and curd rice.

BP 1.134.27

दधि चन्द्रसमप्रख्यं शुभच्छत्रं च विन्यसेत् ॥

One should also set out curd bright as the moon and an auspicious umbrella.
