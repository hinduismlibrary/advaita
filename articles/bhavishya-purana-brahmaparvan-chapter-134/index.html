Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 134 — Auspicious Days and Solar Consecration

BP 1.134.1

नारद उवाच।
प्रतिपच्य द्वितीया च चतुर्थी पञ्चमी तथा।
दशमी त्रयोदशी चैव पौर्णमासी च कीर्तिता ॥

Nārada said: “Pratipad, Dvitīyā, Caturthī, Pañcamī, Daśamī, Trayodaśī, and the full-moon day are declared [suitable].”
