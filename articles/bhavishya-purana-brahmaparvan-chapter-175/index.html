Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 175 — Description of the Sun-and-Fire Rite

BP 1.175.1

।। गरुड उवाच ।। सर्वरोगहता ये तु आधिव्याधिसमन्विताः ।। ग्रहोपघातैर्विविधैरर्दिता ये च मानवाः ।। १ ।।

Garuḍa said: Those struck by every disease, beset by mental and bodily afflictions, and those people tormented by various planetary afflictions;

BP 1.175.2

अरिभिः पीडिता ये च विनायकहताश्च ये ।। कर्तव्यं किं भवेत्तेषामात्मनः श्रेयसेऽनघ ।। २ ।।

Those tormented by enemies, and those struck by Vināyaka—what should they do for their own welfare, O sinless one?

BP 1.175.3

।। अरुण उवाच ।। नानारोगहतानां तु अर्दितानां तथारिभिः ।। आदित्याराधनं मुक्त्वा नान्यच्छ्रेयस्करं परम् ।। ३ ।।

Aruṇa said: For those struck by many diseases and likewise tormented by enemies, apart from worship of the Sun there is no higher good.

BP 1.175.4

तस्मादाराधयेन्नित्यं सर्वरोगविनाशनम् ।। ग्रहोपघातहंतारं राजोपद्रवनाशनम् ।। ४ ।।

Therefore one should always worship the destroyer of all diseases, the slayer of planetary afflictions, the remover of troubles caused by kings.

BP 1.175.5

।। गरुड उवाच ।। सर्वपत्रविहीनं मे सर्वरोगविवर्जितम् ।। शापेन ब्रह्मवादिन्याः पश्यांगं द्विजसत्तम ।। ५ ।।

Garuḍa said: Look at my body, wholly without feathers and free from all disease, through the curse of the woman who speaks of Brahman, O best of the twice-born.

BP 1.175.6

एवं मत्तस्य मे तात किं कार्यमवशिष्यते ।। येनाहं कर्मणा कल्पो भवेयं पत्रवान्पुनः ।। ६ ।।

Thus, dear father, with me in this condition, what action remains, by which deed may I again become able and feathered?

BP 1.175.7

तन्मे ब्रूहि खगश्रेष्ठ प्रपन्नस्य खगाधिप ।। यत्कृत्वा कल्पतां प्राप्य पूजयामि दिवाकरम् ।। ७ ।।

Tell me that, O best of birds, O lord of birds, as I have taken refuge; by doing it and regaining my ability, I may worship the Day-maker.

BP 1.175.8

।। अरुण उवाच ।। पूजयस्व जगन्नाथं भास्करं तिमिरापहम् ।। सूर्याग्निकार्यं सततं शुद्धचित्तः समाचर ।। ८ ।।

Aruṇa said: Worship the Lord of the World, the Light-maker, remover of darkness; perform the Sun-and-fire rite continually, with a pure mind.

BP 1.175.9

महाशांतिकरं ख्यातं सर्वोपद्रवनाशनम् ।। ग्रहोपघातहंतारं शुभकार्यंकरं परम् ।। ९ ।।

It is renowned as bringing great peace, destroying all disturbances, slaying planetary afflictions, and producing the highest auspicious acts.

BP 1.175.10

।। गरुड उवाच ।। नाहं शक्तोस्मि वै कर्तुं पूजां दिनकरस्य च ।। न चाग्निकार्यं शक्नोमि कर्तुं विकलतां गतः ।। १० ।।

Garuḍa said: I am indeed unable to perform worship of the Sun, nor can I perform the fire rite, having become incapacitated.

BP 1.175.11

तस्मान्मे कुरु शांत्यर्थमग्निकार्यं खगाधिप ।। महाशांतिरिति ख्यातं शांतये मम सुव्रत ।। ११ ।।

Therefore perform the fire rite for my peace, O lord of birds; it is known as Great Pacification, for my peace, O man of good vows.

BP 1.175.12

।। अरुण उवाच ।।। एवमेव यदात्थ त्वं वैनतेय खगाधिप ।। अकल्पस्त्वं न शक्नोषि महाव्याधिप्रपीडितः ।। १२ ।।

Aruṇa said: It is just as you say, O son of Vinatā, lord of birds; you are unfit and cannot, afflicted by a great disease.

BP 1.175.13

अहं करोमि ते पुत्र शांतये पावकार्चनम् ।। यत्कृतं मम चार्केण पुरा शांतिदमादरात् ।। १३ ।।

I shall perform the worship of Fire for your peace, my son, which was formerly performed for me by the Sun with reverence, bringing peace.

BP 1.175.14

सर्वपापहरं पुण्यं महाविप्रविनाशनम् ।। महोदयं शातिकरं लक्षहोमविधि स्मृतम् ।। १४ ।।

Meritorious, remover of all sins, destroyer of the great sin of killing a Brahmin, of great power, peace-making—the procedure of a hundred thousand oblations is remembered.

BP 1.175.15

अपमृत्युहरं वीर सर्वव्याधिहरं परम् ।। परचक्रप्रमथनं सदाविजयवर्धनम् ।। १५ ।।

It removes untimely death, O hero, removes all diseases supremely, crushes enemy forces, and ever increases victory.

BP 1.175.16

तृप्तिदं सर्वदेवानां भास्करप्रियमुत्तमम् ।। आग्नेय्यां दिशि लिप्याथ स्थडिलं गोमयेन तु ।।१६।।

It gives satisfaction to all gods and is most dear to the Sun; then one should plaster the altar with cow dung in the southeast direction.

BP 1.175.17

देवालयस्य विधिवत्कुर्यादग्निप्रबोधनम् ।। महाव्याहृतिभिर्वीर लक्षहोमं समाचरेत् ।।१७।।

One should properly kindle the fire in the temple; with the great sacred utterances, O hero, one should perform a hundred thousand oblations.

BP 1.175.18

भूर्भुवःस्वरिति स्वाहा प्रणवेन समन्वितम् ।। आरक्तदेहरूपाय रक्ताक्षाय महात्मने ।। १८ ।।

“Earth, midspace, heaven—svāhā,” joined with Om, to the ruddy-bodied, red-eyed, great-souled one.

BP 1.175.19

धराधराय शान्ताय सहस्राक्षिशिराय च ।। १९ ।।

To the supporter of the earth, the peaceful one, the one with a thousand eyes and a thousand heads.

BP 1.175.20

अधोमुखाय श्वेताय स्वाहा पूर्वाहुतिं सृजेत् ।।। चतुर्मुखाय शांताय पद्मासनगताय च ।। २० ।।

One should offer the first oblation with svāhā to the downward-faced, white one, and to the four-faced, peaceful one seated on a lotus.

BP 1.175.21

पद्मवर्णाय वेधाय कमण्डलुधराय च ।। द्वितीयोर्ध्वमुखायेह स्वाहाकाराहुतिं सृजेत् ।। २१ ।।

To the lotus-colored creator, bearer of a water-pot, here one should offer with svāhā to the second, upward-faced one.

BP 1.175.22

हेमवर्णाय देहाय ऐरावतगताय च ।। सहस्राक्षशरीराय पूर्वदिश्युन्मुखाय च ।। २२ ।।

To the golden-hued one, mounted on Airāvata, thousand-eyed in body, facing the eastern direction.

BP 1.175.23

देवाधिपाय चेन्द्राय विहस्ताय शुभाय च ।। स्वाहाकारं चोत्सृजेदेव तृतीयवदनाय च ।। २३ ।।

To the lord of the gods, to Indra, the armed and auspicious one; one should also offer with svāhā to the third-faced one.

BP 1.175.24

दीप्ताय व्यक्तदेहाय ज्वालामालाकुलाय च ।। इन्द्रनीलाभदेहाय सर्वारोग्यकराय च ।। २४ ।।

To the shining one, manifest-bodied, surrounded by a garland of flames, sapphire-bodied, giver of health to all.

BP 1.175.25

यमाय धर्मराजाय दक्षिणाशामुखाय च ।। कृष्णांबरधरायेह स्वाहाहुतिमनुत्सृजेत् ।।२५।।

To Yama, Dharmarāja, facing south, wearing a black garment; here one should release an oblation with svāhā.

BP 1.175.26

नीलजीमूतवर्णाय रक्तांबरधराय च ।। मुक्ताफलशरीराय पिंगाक्षाय महात्मने ।। २६ ।।

To the blue-cloud-colored one, wearing a red garment, pearl-bodied, tawny-eyed, great-souled one.

BP 1.175.27

शुक्लवस्त्राय पीताय दिव्यपाशधराय च ।। स्वाहाकाराय च तथा पश्चिमाभिमुखाय च ।। २७ ।।

To the white-clad yellow one, bearer of a divine noose, the one of the svāhā-form, and likewise the west-facing one.

BP 1.175.28

कृष्णपिंगलनेत्राय वायव्याभिमुखाय च ।। नीलध्वजाय वीराय तथा चेन्द्राय वेधसे ।। २८ ।।

To the black-and-tawny-eyed one, facing northwest, the blue-bannered hero, and likewise to Indra and the creator.

BP 1.175.29

स्वाहेति पवनायेह आहुतिं चोत्सृजेद्बुधः ।। गदाहस्ताय सूर्याय चित्रस्रग्भूषणाय च ।। २९ ।।

The wise one should offer an oblation here with “svāhā” to the Wind; and to the Sun, mace in hand, adorned with a variegated garland.

BP 1.175.30

महोदराय शांताय स्वाहाधिपतये तथा ।। उत्तराभिमुखायेह महादेवप्रियाय च ।। ३० ।।

To the great-bellied, peaceful one, lord of the svāhā offering, facing north here, dear to Mahādeva.

BP 1.175.31

श्वेताय श्वेतवर्णाय चित्राक्षाय महात्मने ।। शांताय शांतरूपाय पिनाकवरधारिणे ।। ३१ ।।

To the white one, white-hued, many-eyed, great-souled, peaceful, of peaceful form, bearing the excellent bow Pināka.

BP 1.175.32

ईशानाभिमुखायेह दद्यादीशाय चाहुतिम् ।। विसृजेत्खगशार्दूल विधिवच्छ्रेयसेनघ ।। ३२ ।।

Here one should give an oblation to the northeast-facing Lord; one should release it according to rule for welfare, O tiger among birds, O sinless one.

BP 1.175.33

एवं देवं महात्मानं पावकं विधिवत्खग ।। अर्हेदिति तु यत्कार्यं तत्सौरं खगसत्तम ।। ३३ ।।

Thus, O bird, one should honor the great-souled god Fire according to rule; this rite is solar, O best of birds.

BP 1.175.34

लक्षहोमं च विधिवत्कृत्वा शांतिकमाचरेत् ।। भूर्भुवःस्वरिति स्वा हा लक्षहोमविधिः स्मृतः ।। ३४ ।।

Having properly performed a hundred thousand oblations, one should perform the peace rite; “Earth, midspace, heaven—svāhā” is remembered as the procedure for a hundred thousand oblations.

BP 1.175.35

महाहोमे च वै सौर एष एव विधिः परः ।। कृत्वैवमग्निकार्यं तु भोजको भास्कराय वै ।। ३५ ।।

In the great fire offering too, this same procedure is indeed supreme for the solar rite; having thus performed the fire rite, the priest makes the offering to the Light-maker.

BP 1.175.36

शांत्यर्थं सर्वलोकानां ततः शांतिकमाचरेत् ।। सिन्दूरासनरक्ताभः रक्तपद्माभलोचनः ।। ३६ ।।

Then, for the peace of all worlds, one should perform the peace rite. Red as a vermilion seat, with eyes like red lotuses.

BP 1.175.37

सहस्रकिरणो देवः सप्ताश्वरथवाहनः ।। गभस्तिमाली भगवान्सर्वदेवनमस्कृतः ।। ३७ ।।

May the god of a thousand rays, riding a chariot drawn by seven horses, garlanded with rays, the blessed one, bowed to by all the gods,

BP 1.175.38

करोतु ते महाशांतिं ग्रहपीडानिवारिणीम् ।। त्रिचक्ररथमाऱूढो अपां सारमयं तु यः ।। ३८ ।।

Make great peace for you, removing planetary affliction; he who is mounted on a three-wheeled chariot made of the essence of waters.

BP 1.175.39

दशाश्ववाहनो देव आत्रेयश्चामृतस्रवः ।। शीतांशुरमृतात्मा च क्षयवृद्धिसमन्वितः ।। सोमः सौम्येन भावेन ग्रहपीडां व्यपोहतु ।। ३९ ।।

May Soma, the god riding ten horses, descendant of Atri and flowing with nectar, cool-rayed, of immortal essence, endowed with waning and waxing, remove planetary affliction with a gentle nature.

BP 1.175.40

पद्मरागनिभो भौमो मधुपिंगल लोचनः ।। अंगारकोऽग्निसदृशो ग्रहपीडां व्यपोहतु ।। ४० ।।

May Mars, Earth-born, ruby-like, honey-tawny-eyed, resembling fire, remove planetary affliction.

BP 1.175.41

पुष्परागनिभेनेह देहेन परिपिंगलः ।। पीतमाल्यांबरधरो बुधः पीडां व्यपोहतु ।। ४१ ।।

May Mercury, wholly tawny, with a body like topaz and wearing yellow garlands and garments, remove affliction.

BP 1.175.42

तप्तगैरिकसंकाशः सर्वशास्त्रविशारदः ।। सर्वदेवगुरुर्विप्रो ह्यथर्वणवरो मुनिः ।। ४२ ।।

Resembling heated ochre, skilled in all śāstras, the Brahmin, teacher of all gods, the foremost sage of the Atharva Veda,

BP 1.175.43

बृहस्पतिरिति ख्यात अर्थशास्त्रपरश्च यः ।। शांतेन चेतसा सोपि परेण सुसमाहितः ।। ४३ ।।

Known as Bṛhaspati and intent on the science of polity, he too, with a peaceful mind, is fully composed by the Supreme.

BP 1.175.44

ग्रहपीडां विनिर्जित्य करोतु तव शांतिकम् ।। सूर्यार्चनपरो नित्यं प्रसादाद्भास्करस्य तु ।। ४४ ।।

Having conquered planetary affliction, may he bring you peace, ever intent on worship of the Sun through the favor of the Light-maker.

BP 1.175.45

हिमकुंदेंदुवर्णाभो दैत्यदानवपूजितः ।। महेश्वरस्ततो धीमान्महासौरो महामतिः ।। ४५ ।।

Like snow, jasmine, and the moon in hue, worshipped by demons and titans; then the wise Great Lord, great solar devotee, of great mind.

BP 1.175.46

सूर्यार्चनपरो नित्यं शुक्रः शुक्लनिभस्तदा ।। नीतिशास्त्रपरो नित्यं ग्रहपीडां व्यपोहतु ।। ४६ ।।

May Śukra, ever intent on worship of the Sun, shining like white, always intent on the science of conduct, remove planetary affliction.

BP 1.175.47

नानारूपधरोव्यक्त अविज्ञातगतिश्च यः ।। नोत्पत्तिर्जायते यस्य नोदयपीडितैरपि ।। ४७ ।।

He who bears many forms, is unmanifest, and whose course is unknown; for him no origin arises, nor any rising, even when afflicted.

BP 1.175.48

एकचूलो द्विचूलश्च त्रिशिखः पंचचूलकः ।। सहस्रशिररूपस्तु चन्द्रकेतुरिव स्थितः ।। ४८ ।।

One-crested, two-crested, three-tufted, five-tufted, in a thousand-headed form, standing like the banner of the moon.

BP 1.175.49

सूर्यपुत्रोग्निपुत्रस्तु ब्रह्मविष्णुशिवात्मकः ।। अनेकशिखरः केतुः स ते पीडां व्यपोहतु ।। ४९ ।।

Son of the Sun and son of Fire, of the nature of Brahmā, Viṣṇu, and Śiva, many-crested Ketu—may he remove your affliction.

BP 1.175.50

एते ग्रहा महात्मानः सूर्यार्चनपराः सदा ।। शांतिं कुर्वंतु ते हृष्टाः सदा कालं हितेक्षणाः ।। ५० ।।

May these great-souled planets, ever intent on worship of the Sun, pleased and looking favorably upon you, always make peace for you.
