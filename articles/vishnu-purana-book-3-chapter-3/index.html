VP 3.3.1

ज्ञातम् एतन् मया त्वत्तो यथा सर्वम् इदं जगत्।
विष्णुर् विष्णौ विष्णुतश् च न परं विद्यते ततः

I have understood from you how this whole world is Viṣṇu, exists in Viṣṇu, and comes from Viṣṇu. Nothing exists beyond that.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
ज्ञातम् एतत् मया (jñātam etat mayā) — this has been understood by me
त्वत्तः (tvattaḥ) — from you
यथा (yathā) — how
सर्वम् इदम् जगत् (sarvam idam jagat) — this entire world
विष्णुः (viṣṇuḥ) — Viṣṇu
विष्णौ (viṣṇau) — in Viṣṇu
विष्णुतः च (viṣṇutaḥ ca) — and from Viṣṇu
न परम् विद्यते (na param vidyate) — nothing exists beyond that
ततः (tataḥ) — beyond that.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

jñātam etan mayā tvatto yathā sarvam idaṃ jagat|viṣṇur viṣṇau viṣṇutaś ca na paraṃ vidyate tataḥ

Śrīdhara

sthitihetuprasaṅgena muneḥ praśnānusārataḥ । vedavyāsastṛtīye'tra saprapañco'nuvarṇyate । vedavyastiṃ praṣṭuṃ sarvagranthatātparyamanuvadati—jñātam iti । yathā sarvaṃ jagat viṣṇureva, tataḥ paraṃ na vidyate, yathā ca viṣṇo tiṣṭhati, viṣṇauḥ sakāśāt yathā bhavati, tathā tvatto mayā jñātam । anena sarvo'pi kārakādyartho viṣṇāvupalakṣyate ||1||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

स्थितिहेतुप्रसङ्गेन मुनेः प्रश्नानुसारतः । वेदव्यासस्तृतीयेऽत्र सप्रपञ्चोऽनुवर्ण्यते । वेदव्यस्तिं प्रष्टुं सर्वग्रन्थतात्पर्यमनुवदति—ज्ञातम् इति । यथा सर्वं जगत् विष्णुरेव, ततः परं न विद्यते, यथा च विष्णो तिष्ठति, विष्णौः सकाशात् यथा भवति, तथा त्वत्तो मया ज्ञातम् । अनेन सर्वोऽपि कारकाद्यर्थो विष्णावुपलक्ष्यते ॥१॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Śrīdhara says that the context of preservation and Maitreya’s question call for a detailed account of Veda-vyāsa in the Third Book. To ask about the division of the Veda, Maitreya restates the purpose of the whole work. Maitreya has understood from Parāśara how the world is Viṣṇu, abides in Viṣṇu, and arises from Viṣṇu. Thus, every grammatical relation, including the case endings, points to Viṣṇu.

VP 3.3.2

एतत् तु श्रोतुम् इच्छामि व्यस्ता वेदा महात्मना।
वेदव्यास-स्वरूपेण तथा तेन युगे युगे

Now I want to hear how the great one divided the Vedas, age after age, in the form of Veda-vyāsa.

Viṣṇu Purāṇa
एतत् तु श्रोतुम् इच्छामि (etat tu śrotum icchāmi) — this I desire to hear
व्यस्ताः वेदाः (vyastāḥ vedāḥ) — the Vedas divided
महात्मना (mahātmanā) — by the great one
वेद-व्यास-स्वरूपेण (veda-vyāsa-svarūpeṇa) — in the form of Veda-vyāsa
तथा (tathā) — in that way

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

etat tu śrotum icchāmi vyastā vedā mahātmanā|vedavyāsa-svarūpeṇa tathā tena yuge yuge

Śrīdhara

yathā vedā vyastā iti prakārapraśnaḥ ||2||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Śrīdhara identifies this as Maitreya’s question about the manner in which the Vedas were divided.

तं तम् आचक्ष्व भगवन् शाखाभेदांश् च मे वद

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yasmin yasmin yuge vyāso yo ya āsīn mahāmune|taṃ tam ācakṣva bhagavan śākhābhedāṃś ca me vada

Śrīdhara

yasmin yasminnityadhikaraṇapraśnaḥ । yo ya iti kartṛ praśnaḥ । śākhābhedāniti śākhāsaṅkhyānāmnoḥ praśnaḥ ||3||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

यस्मिन् यस्मिन्नित्यधिकरणप्रश्नः । यो य इति कर्तृ प्रश्नः । शाखाभेदानिति शाखासङ्ख्यानाम्नोः प्रश्नः ॥३॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Śrīdhara reads in each age as a question about time and setting, who as a question about the agent, and branch divisions as a question about their number and names.

VP 3.3.4

वेद-द्रुमस्य मैत्रेय शाखा-भेदाः सहस्रशः।
न शक्या विस्तराद् वक्तुं सङ्क्षेपेण शृणुष्व त्वम्

Viṣṇu Purāṇa
वेद-द्रुमस्य (veda-drumasya) — of the Veda-tree

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

veda-drumasya maitreya śākhā-bhedāḥ sahasraśaḥ|na śakyā vistarād vaktuṃ saṅkṣepeṇa śṛṇuṣva tvam

Śrīdhara

tatra tāvat śākhābhedānāṃ saṅkhyāto nāmataśca vistāro vaktumaśakya ityāha—vedadrumasya iti ||4||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तत्र तावत् शाखाभेदानां सङ्ख्यातो नामतश्च विस्तारो वक्तुमशक्य इत्याह—वेदद्रुमस्य इति ॥४॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Śrīdhara explains that the thousands of branch divisions cannot be described in full by their number and their names.

VP 3.3.6

Transliteration

vīryaṃ tejo balaṃ cālpaṃ manuṣyāṇām avekṣya ca|hitāya sarva-bhūtānāṃ veda-bhedān karoti saḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

Śrīdhara glosses vigor as enthusiasm, radiance as prowess, and strength as capacity for retention. Parāśara says the capacities of human beings are small.

VP 3.3.7

ययासौ कुरुते तन्वा वेदम् एकं पृथक् प्रभुः।
वेदव्यासाभिधाना तु सा च मूर्तिर् मधुद्विषः॥७॥

The body by which that Lord divides the one Veda into separate parts is called Veda-vyāsa. That is the form of the slayer of Madhu.

यया (yayā) — by which
असौ (asau) — that Lord
कुरुते (kurute) — divides
तन्वा (tanvā) — with the body

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yayāsau kurute tanvā vedam ekaṃ pṛthak prabhuḥ|vedavyāsābhidhānā tu sā ca mūrtir madhudviṣaḥ||

Śrīdhara

nanu apara eva vedavyāsāḥ, kathaṃ bhagavān vedabhedān karoti ityāśaṅkya—sarve bhagavattanava ityāha—yaya iti ||7||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

ननु अपर एव वेदव्यासाः, कथं भगवान् वेदभेदान् करोति इत्याशङ्क्य—सर्वे भगवत्तनव इत्याह—यय इति ॥७॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Śrīdhara anticipates a question about how Bhagavān divides the Vedas if the Veda-vyāsas are separate persons. Śrīdhara answers that every Vyāsa is a form of Bhagavān.

VP 3.3.8

यस्मिन् मन्वन्तरे व्यासा ये ये स्युस् तान् निबोध मे।

atra tāvat atītānāgatamanvantarāṇāṃ vyāsān ativistaratvena upekṣya vaivasvate manvantare vyatītān vyāsān bhāvinañcaikaṃ tathā śākhābhedāṃśca vaktum āha—yasmin iti ||8||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Śrīdhara explains that the question mentions all Manvantaras, but Parāśara sets aside past and future Manvantaras because the account would be too extensive. Parāśara describes the past Vyāsas of the Vaivasvata Manvantara, one future Vyāsa, and the branch divisions.

Transliteration

Śrīdhara

kiñca ṛgādimayaḥ ṛgvedādirūpaḥ ṛgādisāraḥ praṇavastadātmā ca śarīriṇāṃ vedavibhāgakartṛṇāmātmā ca sa eva ||30||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

किञ्च ऋगादिमयः ऋग्वेदादिरूपः ऋगादिसारः प्रणवस्तदात्मा च शरीरिणां वेदविभागकर्तृणामात्मा च स एव ॥३०॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Śrīdhara explains that Parāśara now describes the identity of the word Praṇava and the Brahman it denotes. The form of Vāsudeva called Praṇava is undivided, yet appears divided as the three sacred utterances and the three worlds. The same Lord is perceived as divided by minds that distinguish differences.

Śrīdhara explains the Lord as formed of Ṛg and the other Vedas, and as their essence. Praṇava is of that nature, and it is also the Self of embodied beings who divide the Vedas.

praṇavākhyavāsudevasya sarvātmatvaṃ nivedayati—sa bhidyate iti । sa eva bhidyate ṛgādirūpeṇa bahubhirbhedaiḥ saśākham anekaprakārakaśākhākaṃ vedamātmānameva tattatpraṇetṛrūpaḥ san sa eva karoti sa eva samastaśākhārūpaśca । tatra hetubhūtaviśeṣaṇatrayam āha—jñānasvarūpo bhagavānananta iti । tena anantaśākhārūpatvaṃ tatpraṇetṛtvañcopapadyate iti ||30||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

प्रणवाख्यवासुदेवस्य सर्वात्मत्वं निवेदयति—स भिद्यते इति । स एव भिद्यते ऋगादिरूपेण बहुभिर्भेदैः सशाखम् अनेकप्रकारकशाखाकं वेदमात्मानमेव तत्तत्प्रणेतृरूपः सन् स एव करोति स एव समस्तशाखारूपश्च । तत्र हेतुभूतविशेषणत्रयम् आह—ज्ञानस्वरूपो भगवाननन्त इति । तेन अनन्तशाखारूपत्वं तत्प्रणेतृत्वञ्चोपपद्यते इति ॥३०॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Śrīdhara explains that Parāśara declares the all-Self nature of Praṇava-Vāsudeva. The Lord is divided into forms beginning with Ṛg, and through many divisions makes the Veda rich with branches. As Vyāsa and the other promulgators, the Lord makes the Veda with its branches, and the Lord is the form of all branches. The Lord is knowledge in nature and endless, which explains the Lord’s being both the branches and their promulgator.
