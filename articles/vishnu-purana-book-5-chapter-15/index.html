VP 5.15.1

ककुद्मति हतेऽरिष्टे धेनुके विनिपातिते ।
प्रलंबे निधनं नीते धृते गोवर्धनाचले

Sri Paraasharar - Demon Arishta, who came in the form of a ferocious bull, was killed. Dhenuka was killed. Pralamba was killed. Krishna held up the Govardhana mountain like an umbrella, in one hand.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
ककुद्मति हते अरिष्टे (kakudmati hate ariṣṭe) — when Ariṣṭa was slain
धेनुके विनिपातिते (dhenuke vinipātite) — when Dhenuka was struck down
प्रलम्बे निधनं नीते (pralambe nidhanaṃ nīte) — when Pralamba was brought to death
धृते गोवर्धनाचले (dhṛte govardhanācale) — when Govardhana mountain was held
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kakudmati hate’riṣṭe dhenuke vinipātite | pralaṃbe nidhanaṃ nīte dhṛte govardhanācale

Śrīdhara

vasato gokule citraṃ karma kṛṣṇasya varṇitam | athāha mathurākṛtyaṃ prastotuṃ nāradāgamam || tad evaṃ gokule rāsa-krīḍā-vinodaiḥ kaṃsa-vadhād-deva-kāryaṃ hariṇā vismṛtam ivālakṣya tat-smaraṇa-prasaṅgāya nāradāgamanam āha—kakudmati iti | kakudmati vṛṣabhākṛtau daiteye || 1 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

वसतो गोकुले चित्रं कर्म कृष्णस्य वर्णितम् ।
अथाह मथुराकृत्यं प्रस्तोतुं नारदागमम् ॥
तद् एवं गोकुले रास-क्रीडा-विनोदैः कंस-वधाद्-देव-कार्यं हरिणा विस्मृतम् इवालक्ष्य तत्-स्मरण-प्रसङ्गाय नारदागमनम् आह—ककुद्मति इति । ककुद्मति वृषभाकृतौ दैतेये ॥१॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

No separate English commentary is supplied.

VP 5.15.2

दमिते कालिये नागे भग्नेतुङ्गद्रुमद्वये ।
हतायां पूतनायां च शकटे परिवर्तिते

The Kaaliya serpent was subdued. Yamalaarjuna, who were in the form of two trees, were liberated from their curse. Pootanaa was killed, and Shakataasura was also killed. All these are the leelaa cheshtitaas of Sri Krishna. All these were done.

हतायां पूतनायां च (hatāyāṃ pūtanāyāṃ ca) — when Pūtanā too was slain
शकटे परिवर्तिते (śakaṭe parivartite) — when the cart was overturned
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

damite kāliye nāge bhagne tuṅgadrumadvaye | hatāyāṃ pūtanāyāṃ ca śakaṭe parivartite

VP 5.15.6

Having come to know all these events, Kamsa thought that before Sri Krishna and Balaraama become powerful, before they attain their youth, he has to get rid of them, kill them. Otherwise, it will be impossible to kill them, once they attain youth, Kamsa thought.

VP 5.15.16

चाणूरमुष्टिकौ मल्लौ नियुद्धकुशलौ मम ।
ताभ्यां सहानयोर्युद्धं सर्वलोकोऽत्र पश्यतु

Chaanoora and Mushtika are experts in wrestling, as duels. Let the entire world witness the wrestling between them - Chaanoora and Mushtika wrestling with Balaraama and Sri Krishna.

Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ताभ्यां सह आनयोः युद्धम् (tābhyāṃ saha ānayoḥ yuddham) — their fight with those two
सर्व-लोको ऽत्र पश्यतु (sarva-loko ’tra paśyatu) — let all people here see
