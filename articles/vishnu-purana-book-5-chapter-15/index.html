VP 5.15.1

ककुद्मति हतेऽरिष्टे धेनुके विनिपातिते ।
प्रलम्बे निधनं नीते धृते गोवर्धनाचले ॥१॥

When Ariṣṭa with a hump was killed, when Dhenuka was struck down, when Pralamba was brought to his end, and when Govardhana mountain was lifted.

Śrīdhara translation

While dwelling in Gokula, Kṛṣṇa’s wondrous deeds have been described. Now he speaks of the affair in Mathurā, to set forth Nārada’s arrival. Then, noticing that Hari seemed to have forgotten the gods’ task of killing Kaṃsa amid the delights of rāsa sports in Gokula, he relates Nārada’s arrival for the occasion of remembering that: “Kakudmati,” thus. “Kakudmati” [means] a Daitya in the form of a bull.
