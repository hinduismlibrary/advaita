Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 170 — The Gift of Cows

BP 1.170.1

सुमन्तुरुवाच ।। सवृषं गोशतं दत्त्वा भास्कराय नराधिप ।। त्रिःसप्तकुलजैः सार्धं शृणु यत्फलमाप्नुयात् ।। १ ।।

Sumantu said: Having given a hundred cows together with a bull to Bhāskara, O lord of men, hear what fruit one obtains together with those born in three-times-seven families.

sārdham — together
śṛṇu — hear
yat-phalam — what fruit
āpnuyāt — one would obtain
Transliteration
sumanturuvāca | | savṛṣaṃ gośataṃ dattvā bhāskarāya narādhipa | | triḥsaptakulajaiḥ sārdhaṃ śṛṇu yatphalamāpnuyāt | | १ | |

BP 1.170.2

वरकोटिप्रतीकाशैः सर्वकामसमन्वितैः ।। महायानैरसंख्येयैरमरासुरपूजितैः ।। २ ।।

With countless great vehicles resembling crores of boons, endowed with all desires and worshipped by immortals and asuras.

Transliteration

BP 1.170.3

द्वादशादित्यसंकाशो दिवाकर इवापरः ।। गत्वादित्यपुरं रम्यं क्रीडते सूर्यमण्डपे ।। ३ ।।

Resembling the twelve Ādityas, another Divākara, having gone to the lovely city of Āditya, he plays in the solar pavilion.

BP 1.170.4

भुक्त्वा तु विपुलान्भोगान्प्रलये सर्वदेहिनाम् ।। मोहकञ्चुकमुत्सृज्य विशत्यादित्यमण्डले ।। ४ ।।

Having enjoyed abundant pleasures at the dissolution of all embodied beings, abandoning the sheath of delusion, one enters the solar orb.

Transliteration

BP 1.170.5

सर्वज्ञः सूरपरमः शुद्धः स्वात्मन्यवस्थितः ।। सर्वगः परिपूर्णत्वात्सूर्यवद्दीप्तिमान्भवेत् ।। ५ ।।

All-knowing, supreme among the gods, pure, established in his own self, all-pervading because complete, one becomes radiant like the Sun.

Word-for-word

Transliteration

BP 1.170.6

यो दद्यादुभयमुखीं सौरभेयीं दिवाकरे ।। सप्तद्वीपां महीं दत्त्वा यत्फलं तदवाप्नुयात् ।। पादद्वयं शिरोऽर्धं च सशैलवनकानना ।। ६ ।।

Whoever gives a two-faced Saurabheyī cow to Divākara obtains the fruit of giving the earth with seven islands, with two feet and half a head, together with mountains, woods, and forests.

Transliteration
