Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 170 — The Gift of Cows

BP 1.170.1

सुमन्तुरुवाच ।। सवृषं गोशतं दत्त्वा भास्कराय नराधिप ।। त्रिःसप्तकुलजैः सार्धं शृणु यत्फलमाप्नुयात् ।। १ ।।

Sumantu said: Having given a hundred cows with a bull to the Sun, O ruler of men, hear the fruit one obtains together with those born in twenty-one families.

sumantuḥ — Sumantu
uvāca — said
sa-vṛṣam — together with a bull
go-śatam — a hundred cows
dattvā — having given
bhāskarāya — to Bhāskara
nara-adhipa — O lord of men
triḥ-sapta-kula-jaiḥ — with those born in three-times-seven families
sārdham — together
śṛṇu — hear
yat-phalam — what fruit
āpnuyāt — one would obtain

BP 1.170.2

वरकोटिप्रतीकाशैः सर्वकामसमन्वितैः ।। महायानैरसंख्येयैरमरासुरपूजितैः ।। २ ।।

With countless great vehicles resembling ten million boons, endowed with every desire, and worshipped by gods and demons.

vara-koṭi-pratīkāśaiḥ — resembling crores of excellent things
sarva-kāma-samanvitaiḥ — endowed with all desires
mahā-yānaiḥ — with great vehicles
asaṃkhyeyaiḥ — countless
amara-asura-pūjitaiḥ — worshipped by immortals and asuras

BP 1.170.3

द्वादशादित्यसंकाशो दिवाकर इवापरः ।। गत्वादित्यपुरं रम्यं क्रीडते सूर्यमण्डपे ।। ३ ।।

Resembling the twelve Suns, he is like another Sun; having gone to the lovely city of the Sun, he sports in the solar pavilion.

dvādaśa-āditya-saṃkāśaḥ — resembling the twelve Ādityas
divākaraḥ — Divākara
iva — like
aparaḥ — another
gatvā — having gone
āditya-puram — Āditya’s city
ramyam — lovely
krīḍate — plays
sūrya-maṇḍape — in the solar pavilion

BP 1.170.4

भुक्त्वा तु विपुलान्भोगान्प्रलये सर्वदेहिनाम् ।। मोहकञ्चुकमुत्सृज्य विशत्यादित्यमण्डले ।। ४ ।।

Having enjoyed abundant pleasures at the dissolution of all embodied beings, he casts off the sheath of delusion and enters the solar orb.

bhuktvā — having enjoyed
tu — indeed
vipulān — abundant
bhogān — pleasures
pralaye — at dissolution
sarva-dehinām — of all embodied beings
moha-kañcukam — sheath of delusion
utsṛjya — abandoning
viśati — enters
āditya-maṇḍale — the solar orb

BP 1.170.5

सर्वज्ञः सूरपरमः शुद्धः स्वात्मन्यवस्थितः ।। सर्वगः परिपूर्णत्वात्सूर्यवद्दीप्तिमान्भवेत् ।। ५ ।।

All-knowing, supreme among the gods, pure, established in his own self, he becomes all-pervading through completeness and radiant like the Sun.

sarva-jñaḥ — all-knowing
sūra-paramaḥ — supreme among the gods
śuddhaḥ — pure
sva-ātmani — in his own self
avasthitaḥ — established
sarva-gaḥ — all-pervading
paripūrṇatvāt — because complete
sūrya-vat — like the Sun
dīpti-mān — radiant
bhavet — would become

BP 1.170.6

यो दद्यादुभयमुखीं सौरभेयीं दिवाकरे ।। सप्तद्वीपां महीं दत्त्वा यत्फलं तदवाप्नुयात् ।। पादद्वयं शिरोऽर्धं च सशैलवनकानना ।। ६ ।।

Whoever gives a two-faced cow to the Sun obtains the fruit of giving the earth with seven islands, with two feet and half a head, with mountains, woods, and forests.

yaḥ — whoever
dadyāt — would give
ubhaya-mukhīm — two-faced
saurabheyīm — Saurabheyī cow
divākare — to Divākara
sapta-dvīpām — with seven islands
mahīm — earth
dattvā — having given
yat-phalam — whatever fruit
tat — that
avāpnuyāt — would obtain
pāda-dvayam — two feet
śiraḥ-ardham — half a head
ca — and
sa-śaila-vana-kānanā — together with mountains, woods, and forests
