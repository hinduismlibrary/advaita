Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 170 — The Gift of Cows

BP 1.170.1

सुमन्तुरुवाच ।। सवृषं गोशतं दत्त्वा भास्कराय नराधिप ।। त्रिःसप्तकुलजैः सार्धं शृणु यत्फलमाप्नुयात् ।। १ ।।

Sumantu said: Having given a hundred cows with a bull to the Sun, O ruler of men, hear the fruit one obtains together with those born in twenty-one families.

BP 1.170.2

वरकोटिप्रतीकाशैः सर्वकामसमन्वितैः ।। महायानैरसंख्येयैरमरासुरपूजितैः ।। २ ।।

With countless great vehicles resembling ten million boons, endowed with every desire, and worshipped by gods and demons.

BP 1.170.3

द्वादशादित्यसंकाशो दिवाकर इवापरः ।। गत्वादित्यपुरं रम्यं क्रीडते सूर्यमण्डपे ।। ३ ।।

Resembling the twelve Suns, he is like another Sun; having gone to the lovely city of the Sun, he sports in the solar pavilion.

BP 1.170.4

भुक्त्वा तु विपुलान्भोगान्प्रलये सर्वदेहिनाम् ।। मोहकञ्चुकमुत्सृज्य विशत्यादित्यमण्डले ।। ४ ।।

Having enjoyed abundant pleasures at the dissolution of all embodied beings, he casts off the sheath of delusion and enters the solar orb.

BP 1.170.5

सर्वज्ञः सूरपरमः शुद्धः स्वात्मन्यवस्थितः ।। सर्वगः परिपूर्णत्वात्सूर्यवद्दीप्तिमान्भवेत् ।। ५ ।।

All-knowing, supreme among the gods, pure, established in his own self, he becomes all-pervading through completeness and radiant like the Sun.

BP 1.170.6

यो दद्यादुभयमुखीं सौरभेयीं दिवाकरे ।। सप्तद्वीपां महीं दत्त्वा यत्फलं तदवाप्नुयात् ।। पादद्वयं शिरोऽर्धं च सशैलवनकानना ।। ६ ।।

Whoever gives a two-faced cow to the Sun obtains the fruit of giving the earth with seven islands, with two feet and half a head, with mountains, woods, and forests.
