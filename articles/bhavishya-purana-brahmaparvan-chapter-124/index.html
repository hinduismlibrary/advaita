Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 124 — Rudra Dindi and the Solar Retinue

BP 1.124.1

ब्रह्मोवाच। वक्ष्याम्यहं ते पुनरेव दिंडे सूर्यस्य सर्वप्रवरप्रधानम। व्योम्नः परं तिष्ठति यस्तु मग्नः स मुच्यते रुद्र इहापि दिंडी ॥

Brahmā said. I shall tell you again, O Dindi, the foremost of all the attendants of the Sun. Rudra Dindi, who stands immersed beyond the firmament, is freed here too.

BP 1.124.2

स्थित्वा पुरा ब्रह्मशिरः किलासौ प्रगृह्य तत्तस्य शिरःकपालम। ततो ह्यसावाश्रममुत्तमं शिवो बहूदकैः पुष्पफलैः समृद्धम ॥

In former times he stood on the head of Brahmā and took the skull as a bowl. Then Śiva went to an excellent hermitage, rich in water, flowers, and fruits.

BP 1.124.3

नग्नो यदा दारुवने मुनीनां दृष्ट्वा च तं भैक्ष्यचरं सुरेशम। योषित्सुताः संक्षुभितास्तु सर्वे जग्मुर्हरं तं मुनयः सुतुष्टाः॥

When naked in the Daru forest of the sages, he saw the Lord of the gods wandering for alms. The women were all stirred, and the sages, greatly pleased, went to Hara.

BP 1.124.4

स हन्यमानो मुनिमुख्यसंघैर्गृहीतलोष्टैरृषिदंडकाष्ठैः। विहाय दिंडिः स तु तान्सुरर्षींस्ततो रवेर्लोकमथाजगाम ॥

Struck by groups of chief sages with clods of earth and wooden staffs, Dindi left those divine sages and then went to the world of the Sun.

BP 1.124.5

आगच्छमानं प्रमथास्तमूचुर्देवेश नित्यं भ्रमसे किमर्थम। स प्राह तान्पापविमोचनार्थमटामि तीर्थानि सुरालयांश्च ॥

As he approached, the attendants said to him, Lord of the gods, why do you wander constantly. He said to them, I wander through sacred fords and the abodes of the gods for the sake of release from sin.

BP 1.124.6

ते भूय ऊचुः प्रमथास्तमेवमत्रैव तिष्ठस्व रवेः पुरस्तात। शुद्धिं तवैष प्रकरिष्यतीति शुद्धस्ततो यास्यसि रुद्रलोकम ॥

Again the attendants said to him, Remain here before the Sun. He will purify you, and once purified you will go to the world of Rudra.

BP 1.124.7

इत्येवमुक्तः प्रमथैस्तु रुद्रस्तत्रैव तस्थौ रवितोषणाय। नग्नो जटी मुष्टिकपालपाणी रूपेण चैवाप्रतिमस्त्रिलोके ॥

Thus addressed by the attendants, Rudra remained there to please the Sun, naked, with matted hair, holding a skull in his fist, and unequalled in form throughout the three worlds.

BP 1.124.8

उक्तः स तुष्टेन ततः सवित्रा प्रीतोस्मि देवागमनात्तवाहम। मद्दर्शनादेव भवान्विशुद्धो दिंडीति नाम्ना भवितासि लोके ॥

Then the pleased Sun said to him, I am pleased by your coming, O god. By seeing me alone you have become purified, and in the world you shall be known by the name Dindi.

BP 1.124.9

अष्टादशैते प्रमथास्तु भानोश्चतुर्दशान्ये तु रवे रथस्थाः। हे देवते द्वौ च ऋषिप्रधानौ गंधर्वसर्पावपि तावदेव ॥

These are eighteen attendants of Bhānu, and fourteen others stand on the chariot of the Sun. O deity, there are also two foremost sages, a celestial musician, and a serpent.

BP 1.124.10

यक्षौ च सिद्धौ च निशाचरौ चादित्यात्मजावप्सरसां प्रधानौ। वसंति ते ह्यस्तमुषश्च सूर्ये तेषाम शीतिश्चतुरोत्तरा सा ॥

Two spirits, two perfected beings, two night wanderers, two sons of Āditya, and two foremost heavenly nymphs dwell with the setting and rising Sun. Their number is eighty four.

BP 1.124.11

इत्यादिदेवप्रवरास्तु सर्वे धात्वर्थशब्दैश्च भवंति सिद्धाः ॥

Thus all these foremost divine beings become perfected through words that give the meanings of roots.

BP 1.124.12

ऋषय ऊचुः। विस्तराद्ब्रूहि मे ब्रह्मन्प्रवरान्धातुशब्दजान। यतश्च कौतुकं ब्रह्मन्नस्माकमिह जायते ॥

The sages said. Tell us in detail, O Brahmā, about the foremost beings known through words derived from roots, for curiosity about this has arisen in us here, O Brahmā.

BP 1.124.13

ब्रह्मोवाच। भूयस्तव प्रवक्ष्यामि दंडनायकपिंगलौ। राज्ञस्रौषादयश्चान्ये दिग्देवा दिंडिना सह ॥

Brahmā said. I shall explain further the Punishment Warden and Piṅgala, Rājña and Srauṣa, and other directional gods together with Dindi.

BP 1.124.14

मया सह समागम्य पुरा देवैर्विचारितम। एष कारुणिकः सूर्यो युध्यते दानवैः सह ॥

Having met with me in former times, the gods deliberated. This compassionate Sun fights with the demons.

BP 1.124.15

ते तु लब्धवरा भूत्वा अमात्याद्या ह्यभीक्ष्णशः। आदित्यं मन्यमानास्ते तपंतं हंतुमुद्यताः ॥

Having obtained boons, they and their ministers continually set out to kill the blazing Āditya, considering themselves mighty.

BP 1.124.16

तस्मात्तेषां विघातार्थं प्रवराश्च भवामहे। अस्माभिः प्रतिरुद्धास्ते न द्रक्ष्यंति दिवाकरम ॥

Therefore we become foremost attendants for their defeat. Checked by us, they shall not see the Sun.

BP 1.124.17

संमंत्र्यैवं ततः स्कंदो वामपार्श्वे रवेः स्थितः। दंडनायकसंज्ञस्तु सर्वलोकस्य स प्रभुः ॥

Having thus deliberated, Skanda stood on the left side of the Sun. He is the lord of all the worlds, called the Punishment Warden.

BP 1.124.18

उक्तश्च स तदार्केण त्वं प्रजादंडनायकः। दंडनीतिकरो यस्मात्तस्मात्तं दंडनायकः ॥

Then the Sun said to him, You are the Punishment Warden of the people. Since you administer discipline, you are called the Punishment Warden.

BP 1.124.19

लिखते यः प्रजानां च सुकृतं यच्च दुष्कृतम। अग्निर्दक्षिणपार्श्वे तु पिंगलत्वात्स पिंगलः ॥

He writes the merit and wrongdoing of the people. Agni stands on the southern side, and because he is tawny he is called Piṅgala.

BP 1.124.20

आश्विनौ चापि सूर्यस्य पार्श्वयोरुभयोः स्थितौ। अश्वरूपात्समुपन्नौ तेन तावश्विनौ सुरौ ॥

The two Aśvins also stand on both sides of the Sun. Since these two gods arose in horse form, they are called the Aśvins.

BP 1.124.21

द्वारपालौ स्मृतौ तस्य राज्ञः श्रेष्ठौ महाबलौ। कार्त्तिकेयः स्मृतो राज्ञः श्रेष्ठश्चापि हरः स्मृतः ॥

Two mighty and excellent gatekeepers of that king are remembered. Kārttikeya is remembered as the foremost of the king, and Hara too is remembered as foremost.

BP 1.124.22

राजृदीप्तौ स्मृतो धातुर्नकारस्तस्य प्रत्ययः। सुरसेनापतित्वेन स यस्माद्दीप्यते सदा। तस्मात्स कार्तिकेयस्तु नाम्ना राज्ञ इति स्मृतः ॥

The root meaning to shine has the letter na as its suffix. Since he always shines as commander of the gods army, Kārttikeya is remembered by the name Rājña.

BP 1.124.23

स्रु गतौ च स्मृतो धातुर्यस्य स प्रत्ययः स्मृतः। गच्छतीति रहस्तस्मात्पर्यायात्स्रौष उच्यते ॥

The root meaning to go is known. Since he goes secretly, by a synonym he is called Srauṣa.

BP 1.124.24

प्रथमं यद्भवेद्द्वारं धर्मार्थाभ्यां समाश्रितम। तत्रैतौ संस्थितौ देवौ लोकपूज्यौ द्विजोत्तमाः ॥

At the first gate, which is supported by righteousness and wealth, these two gods stand, worshipped by the world, O best of the twice born.

BP 1.124.25

द्वितीयायां तु कक्षायामप्रधृष्टौ व्यवस्थितौ। पक्षिप्रेताधिपौ नाम्ना स्मृतौ कल्माषपक्षिणौ ॥

In the second chamber stand two unconquered lords of birds and spirits. They are remembered by the name Speckled Birds.

BP 1.124.26

वर्णस्य शबलत्वाच्च यमः कल्माष उच्यते। पक्षावस्येति यः पक्षी गरुडः परिकीर्तितः ॥

Because of the mottled color of his complexion, Yama is called Speckled. Since he has wings, he is proclaimed as the bird Garuḍa.

BP 1.124.27

स्थितो दक्षिणत तस्य दंडहस्तसमन्वितः। उत्तरेण स्थितो'र्कस्य कुबेरश्च विनायकः ॥

To his south stands one holding a staff in his hand. To the north of the Sun stand Kubera and Vināyaka.

BP 1.124.28

कुबेरो धनदो ज्ञेयो हस्तिरूपो विनायकः। कुत्सया कुप्यता शप्तं कुशरीरमजायत। कुबेरः कुशरीरत्वात्स नाम्ना धनदः स्मृतः ॥

Kubera is to be known as the giver of wealth, and Vināyaka has the form of an elephant. A misshapen body came into being through contempt, anger, and a curse. Because of his misshapen body Kubera is remembered by the name Wealth Giver.

BP 1.124.29

नायकः सर्वसत्त्वानां तेन नायक उच्यते। विविधं नयते यस्मात्स तु तस्माद्विनायकः ॥

He is the leader of all beings, and therefore he is called Leader. Because he leads in various ways, he is therefore Vināyaka.

BP 1.124.30

रैवतश्चैव दिंडिश्च तौ रवेः पूर्वतः स्थितौ। ततो दिंडिः स्मृतो रुद्रो रेवतस्तनयो रवेः ॥

Raivata and Dindi both stand to the east of the Sun. Dindi is remembered as Rudra, and Raivata as the son of the Sun.

BP 1.124.31

प्लुतं गच्छत्यसौ यस्मात्सर्वलोकनमस्कृतः। रेवृप्लवगतौ धातू रेवतस्तेन स स्मृतः ॥

Because he moves in leaps, honored by all the worlds, and because the roots mean leaping and moving, he is remembered as Raivata.

BP 1.124.32

डीङ्गतावस्य वै धातोर्दिंडिशब्दो निपात्यते। डयते'सौ सदा दिंडी तेन दिंडी प्रकीर्तितः ॥

The word Dindi is derived from a root meaning going. Because he is always in motion, he is proclaimed as Dindi.

BP 1.124.33

इत्येते प्रवराः प्रोक्ता धात्वर्था नैगमैः शुभैः। एषां संक्षेपतो भूयः संख्यां वो निगदामि वै ॥

Thus these foremost beings have been described through auspicious Vedic root meanings. I shall now tell you their number again in brief.

BP 1.124.34

अश्विनौ तौ ततो ज्ञेयौ दंडनायकपिंगलौ। तौ सूर्यद्वारपौ ज्ञेयौ राज्ञस्रौषौ ततः स्मृतौ ॥

The two Aśvins are to be known, as well as the Punishment Warden and Piṅgala. Those two are known as the gatekeepers of the Sun, then they are remembered as Rājña and Srauṣa.

BP 1.124.35

रेवतश्चैव दिंडिश्च इत्येते प्रवरा मया। अष्टादश समा ख्याताः संक्षेपात्संख्यया मया ॥

Raivata and Dindi too are among the foremost beings I have declared. In brief, I have stated their number as eighteen.

BP 1.124.36

इत्येभिर्नामभिस्त्वन्ये दानवानां जिघांसया। परिवार्य स्थिताः सूर्यं नानाप्रहरणायुधाः ॥

With these names, and desiring to kill the demons, others stand surrounding the Sun, armed with many kinds of weapons.

BP 1.124.37

सरूपाश्चान्यरूपाश्च विरूपाः कामरूपिणः। परिवार्य स्थिताः सूर्यं गरुडश्च महाबलः ॥

Some have beautiful forms, others different forms, some distorted forms, and some assume forms at will. Mighty Garuḍa too stands surrounding the Sun.

BP 1.124.38

धातुर्दिविति वै प्रोक्तो क्रीडायां स तु उच्यते। क्रीडंते दिवि वै यस्मात्तस्मात्ते दैवताः स्मृताः ॥

The root word meaning play is used. Because they play in heaven, they are remembered as deities.

BP 1.124.39

ऋचो यजूंषि सामानि यान्युक्तानीह वै मया। नानारूपैः स्थितान्येव रवेस्तानि समंततः ॥

The Ṛg, Yajur, and Sāma Vedas that I have named here stand all around the Sun in many forms.

BP 1.124.40

सुमंतुरुवाच। इत्येवमुक्तवान्ब्रह्मा ऋषीणां पृच्छतां पुरा। ते श्रुत्वाराध्य देवेशं संसिद्धा दिवि संस्थिताः ॥

Sumantu said. Thus Brahmā spoke in former times to the sages who questioned him. Hearing this, they worshipped the Lord of the gods, became perfected, and remained established in heaven.
