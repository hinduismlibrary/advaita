Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 117 — The Sun Temple Priests and Their Duties

BP 1.117.1

अरुण उवाच। किमर्थं भोजकस्तुभ्यं प्रियो देवेश कथ्यताम्। नान्ये विप्रादयो वर्णा देवतायतनेषु वै ॥

Aruna said. For what reason is the Sun temple priest dear to you, O Lord of gods. Tell me why the other varnas, beginning with the Brahmins, are not in the temples of the gods.

BP 1.117.2

कश्चायं भोजको देव कस्य पुत्रः किमात्मकः। वर्णतश्चास्य मे ब्रूहि कर्म चास्य समन्ततः ॥

Who is this Sun temple priest, O god, whose son is he and of what nature. Tell me his varna and all his actions.

BP 1.117.3

आदित्य उवाच। साधु पृष्टोऽस्मि भद्रं ते वैनतेय महामते। शृणुष्वैकमनाः सर्वं गदतो मम खेचर ॥

You have asked well, may good be yours, O wise Vainateya. Listen with one pointed mind to all that I say, O sky goer.

BP 1.117.4

विप्रादयस्तु ये त्वन्ये वर्णाः कश्यपनन्दन। ते पूजयन्ति मां नित्यं भक्तिश्रद्धासमन्विताः ॥

Other varnas, beginning with Brahmins, O son of Kaśyapa, worship me daily, endowed with devotion and faith.

BP 1.117.5

देवालयेषु ये विप्राः प्रीत्या मां पूजयन्ति हि। अन्याश्च देवतावृत्त्या ते स्युर्देवलकाः खग। एतस्मात्कारणान्मह्यं भोजको दयितः सदा ॥

Those Brahmins who worship me lovingly in temples, and those who make their livelihood by serving other deities, become temple priests, O bird. For this reason the Sun temple priest is always dear to me.

BP 1.117.6

वर्णतो ब्राह्मणश्चायं स्वानुष्ठानपरो यदि। अनुष्ठानविहीनो हि नरकं यात्यसंशयम् ॥

This man is a Brahmin by varna when devoted to his own observance. Without observance he surely goes to hell.

BP 1.117.7

न त्याज्यं भोजकैस्तस्मात्स्वकं कर्म कदाचन। मयासौ निर्मितः पूर्वं तेजसा स्वेन वै खग ॥

Therefore the Sun temple priest must never abandon his own work. I created him formerly from my own radiance, O bird.

BP 1.117.8

पूजार्थमात्मनो नूनं कर्म चास्य प्रकीर्तितम्। प्रियव्रतसुतो राजा शाकद्वीपे महामतिः ॥

His work was proclaimed for the sake of my worship. The wise king, son of Priyavrata, ruled in Śākadvīpa.

BP 1.117.9

तेन मे कारितं दिव्यं विमानप्रतिमं गृहम्। तस्मिन्द्वीपे तदात्मीये दिव्यं शिलामयं महत् ॥

He had a great divine stone temple built for me on his own island, resembling an aerial car.

BP 1.117.10

स मदर्चां कारयित्वा काञ्चनीं लक्षणान्विताम्। प्रतिष्ठापनाय वै तस्याश्चिन्तयामास सुव्रतः ॥

Having had my golden image made, endowed with auspicious marks, the good king thought about its consecration.

BP 1.117.11

कृतमायतनं श्रेष्ठं तेनेयं प्रतिमा कृता। को वै प्रतिष्ठापयिता देवमर्कं शुभालये ॥

He had made an excellent temple, and this image had been made. Who will consecrate the god Sun in the auspicious shrine.

BP 1.117.12

एवं सञ्चिन्तयित्वा तु जगाम शरणं मम। भक्तिं तस्य च सञ्चिन्त्य खगाहं पार्थिवस्य तु ॥

Having thought thus, he sought refuge in me. Considering the devotion of that earthly king, O bird.

BP 1.117.13

गतोऽहं दर्शनं तस्य उक्तञ्चापि मया खग। किं चिन्तयसि राजेन्द्र कुतश्चिंता समागता ॥

I went to see him and spoke, O king, what are you thinking, and from where has this worry arisen.

BP 1.117.14

ब्रूहि यत्ते हृदि प्रौढं चिन्ताकारणमागतम्। संपादयिष्ये तत्सर्वं विमना भव मा नृप ॥

Tell me the deep cause of worry that has come into your heart. I shall accomplish it all, do not be troubled, O king.

BP 1.117.15

अत्यर्थं दुष्करमपि करिष्ये नात्र संशयः। इत्युक्तः स मया राजा इदं वचनमब्रवीत् ॥

I shall do even what is exceedingly difficult, without doubt. Thus addressed by me, the king spoke these words.

BP 1.117.16

द्वीपेऽस्मिन्देवदेवस्य कृतमायतनं तव। मया भक्त्या जगन्नाथ तथेयं प्रतिमा कृता ॥

In this island I made a temple for you, God of gods, O Lord of the universe. In devotion I made this image too.

BP 1.117.17

प्रतिष्ठां कारयेद्यस्तु तव देवालये खग। यत्र सन्ति त्रयो वर्णा द्वीपेस्मिन्क्षत्रियादयः ॥

Who will perform the consecration in your temple, O bird, where three varnas, beginning with Kshatriyas, dwell on this island.

BP 1.117.18

ते मयोक्ता न कुर्वन्ति प्रतिष्ठां तव कृत्स्नशः। न चाप्यर्चां जगन्नाथ ब्राह्मणश्चात्र विद्यते ॥

I told them, but they do not perform your consecration completely. Nor is there a Brahmin here to worship you, O Lord of the universe.

BP 1.117.19

तेनेयमागता चिन्ता हृदि शल्यं तयार्पितम्। ततो मयोक्तो राजाऽसौ वैनतेय वचः शुभम् ॥

For this reason this worry has arisen, and it is placed like a thorn in my heart. Then I spoke auspicious words to that king, O son of Vinatā.

BP 1.117.20

एवमेतन्न संदेहो यथात्थ त्वं नराधिपः। क्षत्रियादित्रयो वर्णा द्वीपेऽस्मिन्नात्र संशयः ॥

It is just as you say, O king, without doubt. The three varnas beginning with Kshatriyas dwell on this island, without doubt.

BP 1.117.21

ते च नार्हन्ति मे पूजां न प्रतिष्ठां कदाचन। तस्मात्ते श्रेयसे राजन्प्रतिष्ठामात्मनस्तथा ॥

They are not fit for my worship, nor for my consecration at any time. Therefore, O king, for your welfare I shall create one for my consecration.

BP 1.117.22

सृजामि प्रथमं वर्णं भग संज्ञमनौपमम्। इत्युक्त्वा तमहं वीर राजानं खगसत्तम ॥

I shall first create an incomparable varna called Bhaga, I said to that king, O hero and best of birds.

BP 1.117.23

जगाम परमां चिन्तां तस्य कार्यस्य सिद्धये। अथ मे चिन्तयानस्य स्वशरीराद्विनिःसृताः ॥

I entered into deep thought for the accomplishment of that task. While I was thinking, eight mighty beings emerged from my own body.

BP 1.117.24

शशिकुन्देन्दुसंकाशाः संख्ययाष्टौ महाबलाः। पठन्ति चतुरो वेदान्सांगोपनिषदः खग ॥

They shone like the moon, jasmine flowers, and the moon orb, eight in number and mighty. They recited the four Vedas with their limbs and Upanishads.

BP 1.117.25

काषायवाससः सर्वे करण्डाम्बुजधारिणः। ललाटफलकाद्द्वौ तु द्वौ चान्यौ वक्षसस्तथा ॥

All wore ochre garments and carried water pots and lotuses. Two came from the surface of my forehead, and two others from my chest.

BP 1.117.26

चरणाभ्यां तथा द्वौ तु पादाभ्यां द्वौ तथा खग। अथ ते च महात्मानः सर्वे प्रणतकन्धराः ॥

Two likewise came from my feet, and two from my legs, O bird. Then all those great souls bowed their heads.

BP 1.117.27

पितरं मन्यमाना मामिदं वचनमब्रुवन्। तात तात महादेव लोकनाथ जगत्पते ॥

Considering me their father, they spoke these words. Father, father, great god, lord of the worlds, lord of the universe.

BP 1.117.28

किमर्थं भवता सृष्टा वयं देवस्य देहतः। ब्रूहि सर्वं करिष्याम आदेशं भवतोऽखिलम् ॥

For what purpose have you created us from your body. Tell us everything, and we shall carry out your entire command.

BP 1.117.29

पितास्माकं भवान्देवो वयं पुत्रा न संशयः। इत्युक्तवन्तस्ते सर्वे मयोक्ता देवसम्भवाः ॥

You are our father, the god, and we are sons, without doubt. Having spoken thus, all those divine born ones stood before me.

BP 1.117.30

प्रियव्रतसुतो योयमस्य वाक्यं करिष्यथ। स चाप्युक्तो मया राजा शाकद्वीपाधिपः खग ॥

Do what this king, the son of Priyavrata, says. I also spoke to that king, the ruler of Śākadvīpa, O bird.

BP 1.117.31

य एते मत्सुता राजन्नर्घ्या ब्राह्मणसत्तमाः। कारयन्तु प्रतिष्ठां मे सर्वैरेभिर्महीपते ॥

O king, these are my sons, worthy and excellent Brahmins. Let all of them perform my consecration, O ruler of the earth.

BP 1.117.32

कारयित्वा प्रतिष्ठां तु ममार्चायां नराधिप। पश्चादायतनं सर्वमेषामर्पय पूजने ॥

Having caused the consecration of my image, O king, give the whole temple to them for worship.

BP 1.117.33

एते मत्पूजने योग्याः प्रतिष्ठासु च सर्वशः। समाप्य न प्रहर्तव्यं भोजकेभ्यः कदाचन ॥

They are fit in every way for my worship and consecrations. When the consecration is complete, never strike the Sun temple priests.

BP 1.117.34

सर्वमायतनार्थं तु गृहक्षेत्रादिकं च यत्। धनधान्यादिकं राजन्यन्ममायतने भवेत् ॥

Whatever houses, fields, and other things are for the temple, and whatever wealth and grain is in my shrine.

BP 1.117.35

तत्सर्वं भोजकेभ्यस्तु दातव्यं नात्र संशयः। धनधान्यसुवर्णादि गृहक्षेत्रादिकं च यत्। यन्मदीयं भवेत्किञ्जिद्ग्रामे वा नगरे क्वचित् ॥

All that must be given to the Sun temple priests, without doubt. Whatever wealth, grain, gold, houses, fields, and other things belonging to me may be found in a village or city.

BP 1.117.36

तस्य सर्वस्य राजेन्द्र मदीयस्य समन्ततः। अधिपा भोजकाः सर्वे नान्ये विप्रादयो नृप ॥

Of all that belongs to me, the Sun temple priests are the masters, O king, and no others, whether Brahmins or the rest.

BP 1.117.37

यथाधिकारी पुत्रस्तु पितृद्रव्यस्य वै भवेत्। तथा मदीयवित्तस्य भोजकाः स्युर्न संशयः ॥

Just as a son is the rightful heir to the wealth of his father, so the Sun temple priests are heirs to my wealth, without doubt.

BP 1.117.38

इत्युक्तेन मया राज्ञा तथा सर्वं प्रवर्तितम्। कारयित्वा प्रतिष्ठां तु दत्त्वा सर्वस्वमेव हि। भोजकेभ्यः खगश्रेष्ठ ततो हर्षमवाप्तवान् ॥

When I had spoken thus, the king caused everything to proceed accordingly. Having caused the consecration and given all his possessions to the Sun temple priests, O best of birds, he was filled with joy.

BP 1.117.39

एवमेते मया सृष्टा भोजका गरुडाग्रज। अहमात्मा ततो ह्येषां सर्वे सुमनसस्तथा ॥

Thus I created the Sun temple priests, O elder brother of Garuḍa. I am their Self, and all of them are benevolent.

BP 1.117.40

मत्पुत्रेण समा ज्ञेयास्तथा मम हिताः सदा। तस्मात्तेभ्यः प्रदातव्यं न हर्तव्यं कदाचन ॥

They are to be regarded as equal to my son and always dear to me. Therefore things must be given to them, never taken away.

BP 1.117.41

भोजकस्य हरेद्यस्तु लोभाद्द्वेषात्तथापि वा। स याति नरकं घोरं तामिस्रं शाश्वतीः समाः ॥

Whoever takes the property of a Sun temple priest through greed or hatred goes to the terrible hell named Tāmīsra for everlasting years.

BP 1.117.42

तस्माद्ग्रामादिकं द्रव्यं यत्किञ्चिन्मम विद्यते। तत्सर्वं भोजकस्वं हि पितृपर्यागतं मम ॥

Therefore whatever property of mine exists in a village and the like, all of it belongs to the Sun temple priests, having come to me from my forefathers.

BP 1.117.43

भोजकश्च भवेद्यादृक्तत्ते वच्मि खगेश्वर। ममाज्ञां पालेयद्यस्तु स्वानुष्ठानपरः सदा ॥

I shall tell you what kind of Sun temple priest there should be, O lord of birds. He should always preserve my command and be devoted to his own observance.

BP 1.117.44

वेदाधिगमनं पूर्वं दारसंग्रहणं तथा। अभ्यङ्गधारणं नित्यं तथा त्रिषवणं स्मृतम् ॥

First, study of the Vedas, then taking a wife, daily anointing, and bathing at the three times are remembered as his duties.

BP 1.117.45

पञ्चकृत्वः सदा पूज्यो ह्यहं रात्रौ दिने तथा। देवब्राह्मणवेदानां निन्दा कार्या न वै क्वचित् ॥

I must always be worshipped five times, by night and by day. The gods, Brahmins, and Vedas must never be reviled anywhere.

BP 1.117.46

नान्यादेवप्रतिष्ठा तु कार्या वै भोजकेन तु। ममापि च न कर्तव्या तेन एकाकिना क्वचित् ॥

Another deity must not be consecrated by the Sun temple priest. Nor should he perform my consecration alone anywhere.

BP 1.117.47

सर्वमेव निवेद्यान्नं नाश्नीयाद्भोजकः सदा। न भुञ्जीत गृहं गत्वा शूद्रस्य गरुडाग्रज ॥

After all food is offered to me, the Sun temple priest should always eat. He must not eat in the house of a Śūdra, O elder brother of Garuḍa.

BP 1.117.48

शूद्रोच्छिष्टं प्रयत्नेन सदा त्याज्यं हि भोजकैः। येऽश्नन्ति भोजका नित्यं शूद्रान्नं शूद्रवेश्मनि ॥

The Sun temple priests should always carefully reject food left by a Śūdra. Those who regularly eat the food of a Śūdra in a Śūdra dwelling.

BP 1.117.49

ते वै पूजाफलं चात्र कथं प्राप्स्यन्ति खेचर। गत्वा गृहं तु शूद्रस्य न भोक्तव्यं कदाचन ॥

How will they obtain the fruit of worship here, O sky goer. Having gone to the house of a Śūdra, one must never eat.

BP 1.117.50

गृहागतं च शूद्रान्नं तच्च त्याज्यं तथैव च। आध्मातव्योम्बुजो नित्यं भोजकेनाग्रतो मम ॥

Food of a Śūdra brought home should also be rejected. The Sun temple priest should daily blow the conch before me.

BP 1.117.51

सकृत्प्रवादिते शंखे मम प्रीतिर्हि जायते। षण्मासान्नात्र सन्देहः पुराणश्रवणं तथा ॥

When the conch is blown once, my pleasure arises. There is no doubt about this for six months. The Purana should also be heard.

BP 1.117.52

तस्माच्छंखः सदा वाद्यो भोजकेन प्रयत्नतः। तस्येयं परमा वृत्तिर्नैवेद्यं यन्मदीयकम् ॥

Therefore the conch should always be blown carefully by the Sun temple priest. This is his highest livelihood, the food offered to me.

BP 1.117.53

नाभोज्यं भुञ्जते यस्मात्तेनैते भोजका मताः। मगं ध्यायन्ति ते यस्मात्तेन ते मगधाः स्मृताः ॥

Because they do not eat forbidden food, they are called Sun temple priests. Because they meditate on Maga, they are called Magadhas.

BP 1.117.54

भोजयन्ति च मां नित्यं तेन ते भोजका स्मृताः। अभ्यङ्गं च प्रयत्नेन प्रायं शुद्धिकरं परम् ॥

Because they feed me daily, they are called Sun temple priests. Anointing with care is supremely purifying.

BP 1.117.55

अभ्यङ्गहीनो ह्यशुचिर्भोजकः स्यान्न संशयः। यस्तु मां पूजयेद्वीर अभ्यङ्गेन विना खग न तस्य सन्ततिः स्याद्वै न चाहं प्रीतिमान्भवे ॥

Without anointing, the Sun temple priest is impure, without doubt. Whoever worships me without anointing, O hero, O bird, shall have no offspring, and I shall not be pleased with him.

BP 1.117.56

मुण्डनं शिरसा कार्यं शिखा धार्या प्रयत्नतः ॥

The head should be shaved, and the tuft carefully maintained.

BP 1.117.57

नक्तं चादित्यदिवसे तथा षष्ठ्यां प्रवर्तयेत्। सप्तम्यामुपवासस्तु मम संक्रमणे तथा ॥

He should undertake the night fast on Sunday and the sixth day. He should also fast on the seventh day and on my solar transition.

BP 1.117.58

कर्तव्यो भोजकेनैव मत्प्रीत्यै गरुडाग्रज। त्रिकालं चापि गायत्रीं जपेद्वाचा पुरो मम ॥

For my pleasure, O elder brother of Garuḍa, the Sun temple priest must do this, and recite the Gāyatrī aloud before me three times a day.

BP 1.117.59

मुखमावृत्य यत्नेन पूजनीयोऽहमादरात्। मौनं चास्य प्रयत्नेन त्यक्त्वा क्रोधं च दूरतः ॥

He should carefully cover his mouth and worship me reverently. He should observe silence with care and keep anger far away.

BP 1.117.60

शूद्रेभ्यो यस्तु वैश्येभ्यो लोभात्कामात्प्रयच्छति। निर्माल्यं मम वै वत्स स याति नरकं ध्रुवम् ॥

Whoever, out of greed or desire, gives my ritual remnants to Śūdras or Vaiśyas, O child, certainly goes to hell.

BP 1.117.61

लोभाद्वै भोजको यस्तु मत्पुष्पाणि खगाधिप। यच्छतेन्यस्य दुष्टात्मा मय्यनारोप्य खेचर ॥

O lord of birds, a wicked hearted Sun temple priest who, out of greed, gives my flowers to another without first placing them on me.

BP 1.117.62

स ज्ञेयो मे परः शत्रुः स मामर्हो न चार्चितुम्। निर्माल्यं मम देयं स्याद्ब्राह्मणादिषु वै नृषु ॥

He is to be known as my enemy, not worthy of worshipping me. My ritual remnants should be given to Brahmins and other men.

BP 1.117.63

नैवेद्यं यन्मदीयं तु तदश्नीयात्सदैव हि। तेनासौ शुद्ध्यते नित्यं हविष्यान्नसमं तथा ॥

He should always eat the food offered to me. By this he is continually purified, like food prepared for sacrifice.

BP 1.117.64

तत्क्षणादुत्क्षिपेद्यस्तु ममांगात्पुष्पमेव हि। नान्यस्य देयं नैवेद्यं मदीयमुदके क्षिपेत् ॥

Whoever immediately removes a flower from my body must not give it to another. He should cast my offering into water.

BP 1.117.65

पञ्चगव्यसमं तस्य मन्मतं नात्र संशयः। ममाङ्गलग्नं यत्किञ्चिद्गन्धं पुष्पमथापि वा ॥

Whatever scent or flower has touched my limbs is considered by me equal to the five products of the cow, without doubt.

BP 1.117.66

दातव्यं न च वैश्याय न शूद्राय कदाचन। आत्मना तद्ग्रहीतव्यं न विक्रेयं कथञ्चन ॥

It should never be given to a Vaiśya or Śūdra. One should take it oneself and never sell it.

BP 1.117.67

यस्तु नारोप्य पुष्पाणि अव्यङ्गानि ममोपरि। यः कश्चिदाहरेल्लोके स याति नरकं ध्रुवम् ॥

Whoever in this world takes intact flowers without first placing them on me surely goes to hell.

BP 1.117.68

स्नपनं मम निर्माल्यं पावकं यस्तु लङ्घयेत्। स नरो नरकं याति सरौद्रं रौरवं खग ॥

Whoever steps over my bathing remnants, sacred like fire, goes to the terrible Raurava hell, O bird.

BP 1.117.69

भोजकेन सदा कार्यं स्नपनं मे प्रयत्नतः। यथा न लङ्घयेत्कश्चिद्यथा श्वा नापि भक्षयेत् ॥

The Sun temple priest must always perform my ritual bathing carefully, so that no one steps over it and no dog eats it.

BP 1.117.70

यद्ययत्नपरः कुर्याद्भोजकः स्नपनं मम। यथा वै लङ्घितमतिर्भक्ष्यतां च खगाधिप ॥

If a Sun temple priest performs my ritual bathing carelessly, so that it is stepped over and eaten, O lord of birds.

BP 1.117.71

स याति नरकं रौद्रं तामिश्रं नाम नामतः। एकभक्तं सदा कार्यं स्नानं त्रैकालमेव हि ॥

He goes to the fierce hell named Tāmīsra. He should take one meal a day and bathe at the three times.

BP 1.117.72

त्रिचैलं परिवर्तेत भवितव्यं दिनेदिने। पूजाकालेऽर्घकाले च क्रोधस्त्याज्यः प्रयत्नतः ॥

He should change three garments day by day. At the time of worship and the time of water offering, anger must be carefully abandoned.

BP 1.117.73

अमांगल्यं न वक्तव्यं वक्तव्यं च शुभं तदा। ईदृग्भूतो भोजको मे प्रेयान् पूजाकरः सदा ॥

He must not speak what is inauspicious, but should speak what is auspicious then. A Sun temple priest who is thus, always engaged in worship, is dear to me.

BP 1.117.74

सन्मान्यः पूजनीयश्च विप्रादीनां यथास्म्यहम्। यः करोत्यवमानं तु वृत्तिरूपं तु भोजके ॥

He should be honored and worshipped as I am among Brahmins and others. Whoever insults the Sun temple priest and his livelihood.

BP 1.117.75

तस्याहं रोषमेत्याशु कुलं हन्मि समन्ततः। प्रियो मे भोजको नित्यं यथा त्वं विनतासुत ॥

I quickly become angry with him and utterly destroy his family. The Sun temple priest is always dear to me, as you are, O son of Vinatā.

BP 1.117.76

उपलेपनकर्ता च सम्मार्जनपरश्च यः। परावसुरुवाच। इत्युक्त्वा भगवान्भानुर्बभ्राम रथमास्थितः ॥

Whoever applies plaster and is intent on sweeping. Parāvasu said, having spoken thus, the blessed Sun mounted his chariot and went on.

BP 1.117.77

अरुणोऽपि तथा श्रुत्वा मुदया परया नृपः। पूज्यस्तस्मान्महाराज भोजकस्तु महीपते ॥

Aruna too, having heard this, rejoiced with great joy, O king. Therefore, O great king, the Sun temple priest should be honored.

BP 1.117.78

तस्माद्देवं वाचकाय द्वितीयमशनं नरैः ॥

Therefore people should give a second meal to the reciter.

BP 1.117.79

ब्रह्मोवाच। इत्थं श्रुत्वा स राजा तु कर्मणः फलमात्मनः। पुरातनं महाबाहुर्मुदमाप महीपतिः ॥

Brahmā said. Having heard this, that mighty king rejoiced in the ancient fruit of his own actions.

BP 1.117.80

यद्यदायतनं भानोः पृथिव्यां पश्यते नृपः। तस्मिंस्तस्मिन्कारयति उपलेपनमादरात् ॥

Wherever on earth the king saw a temple of the Sun, there he caused the plastering to be done with devotion.

BP 1.117.81

भार्या तस्यापि सुश्रोणि पुण्यश्रवणमादरात्। वाचके वेतनं दत्त्वा भानोर्देवस्य मन्दिरे ॥

His wife too gained the merit of hearing this, O lovely hipped one. He gave wages to the reciter in the temple of the Sun.

BP 1.117.82

इत्थं राजा सपत्नीकः पूज्य भक्त्या दिवाकरम्। प्राप्तावुभौ परां प्रीतिं गतिं चानुत्तमां तथा ॥

Thus the king and his wife worshipped the Sun with devotion. Both attained supreme joy and the highest destination.
