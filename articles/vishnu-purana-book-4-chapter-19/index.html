VP 4.19.1

पूरोर्जनमेजयस्तस्यापि प्रचिन्वान् प्रचिन्वतः प्रवीरः प्रवीरान्मनस्युर्मनस्योश्चाभयदः तस्यापि सुद्युःसुद्योर्बहुगतः तस्यापि संयातिःसंयातेरहंयातिः ततो रौद्राश्वः

Sri Paraasharar - Puru had a son Janamejaya, whose son was Prachinvaan. His son was Praveera. Then Manasyu, Abhayada, then Sudyu, then Bahugata, then Samyaati, then Ahamyaati, then Roudraashva.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

pūrorjanamejayastasyāpi pracinvān pracinvataḥ pravīraḥ pravīrānmanasyurmanasyoścābhayadaḥ tasyāpi sudyuḥsudyorbahugataḥ tasyāpi saṃyātiḥsaṃyāterahaṃyātiḥ tato raudrāśvaḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.19.2

ऋतेषुकक्षेषुस्थण्डिलेषुकृतेषुजलेषुधर्मेधृतेषुस्थलेषुसन्नतेषुवनेषुनामानो रौद्राश्वस्य दश पुत्रा बभूवुः

Roudraashva had ten sons - Ruteshu, Kaksheshu, Sthandileshu, Kruteshu, Jaleshu, Dharmeshu, Dhruteshu, Sthaleshu, Sannaateshu, Vaneshu.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ṛteṣukakṣeṣusthaṇḍileṣukṛteṣujaleṣudharmedhṛteṣusthaleṣusannateṣuvaneṣunāmāno raudrāśvasya daśa putrā babhūvuḥ

Śrīdhara

ūnaviṃśe kramaprāptaḥ purorvaṃśī nirūpyate . duṣmantād yatra bharato yannāmnā bhāratāstvime . ṛteyurity-ādiśabdāḥ prathamaikavacanāntānukaraṇānyetāni nāmāni yeṣāmātmajānāṃ te ityete raudrāśvāsyātmajā iti pāṭhaḥ sugamaḥ ||२||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

ऊनविंशे क्रमप्राप्तः पुरोर्वंशी निरूप्यते । दुष्मन्ताद् यत्र भरतो यन्नाम्ना भारतास्त्विमे । ऋतेयुरित्य्-आदिशब्दाः प्रथमैकवचनान्तानुकरणान्येतानि नामानि येषामात्मजानां ते इत्येते रौद्राश्वास्यात्मजा इति पाठः सुगमः ॥२॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Roudraashva had ten sons - Ruteshu, Kaksheshu, Sthandileshu, Kruteshu, Jaleshu, Dharmeshu, Dhruteshu, Sthaleshu, Sannaateshu, Vaneshu.

VP 4.19.12

माता भस्त्रा पितुः पुत्रो येन जातः स एव सः । भरस्व पुत्रं दुष्यन्त मावमंस्थाः शकुन्तलाम्

Mother is like a sack made of skin. One who is born to a father, the son belongs to the father only. So, they told Dushyanta, do take care of him, you are the one responsible for him. You are the father, don't disrespect Shakuntalaa. , , If an object is kept in a sack, that object belongs to the person who kept it there. It does not belong to the sack. That is how the name Bharata got formed.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

mātā bhastrā pituḥ putro yena jātaḥ sa eva saḥ | bharasva putraṃ duṣyanta māvamaṃsthāḥ śakuntalām

Śrīdhara

duṣmanto mṛgayārtham araṇyaṃ gato viśvāmitrān menakāyāṃ jātāṃ śakuntalāṃ nāma kanyāṃ kaṇvāśrame dṛṣṭvā gāndharveṇa vivāhena saṅgamya garbham ādhāya sva-puraṃ gataḥ sarvaṃ visasmāra . yāṃ ca jāta-putraṃ kaṇva-prahitena śiṣyeṇānītāṃ smṛtvāpi lokāpavāda-bhayād asvīkurvantaṃ duṣmantaṃ prati devī-gīto’yaṃ ślokaṃ--māta iti . bhastrā carma-puṭakaṃ, tat-sthānīyā mātā vīryādhāra-mātraṃ, kintu pitur niṣektur eva putraḥ . kiṃ ca, yena pitrā jāto janitaḥ, sa eva putraḥ tad-aṃśa-bhūta-vīryopādānatvāt, ātmā vai jāyate putra iti vacanāc ca . ataḥ putraṃ bharasva bibhṛhi . śakuntalāṃ ca nirdoṣām avamaṃsthā iti bharata-nāma-niruktir api ||१२|| retādhāḥ putro nayati naradeva yama-kṣayāt .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

दुष्मन्तो मृगयार्थम् अरण्यं गतो विश्वामित्रान् मेनकायां जातां शकुन्तलां नाम कन्यां कण्वाश्रमे दृष्ट्वा गान्धर्वेण विवाहेन सङ्गम्य गर्भम् आधाय स्व-पुरं गतः सर्वं विसस्मार । यां च जात-पुत्रं कण्व-प्रहितेन शिष्येणानीतां स्मृत्वापि लोकापवाद-भयाद् अस्वीकुर्वन्तं दुष्मन्तं प्रति देवी-गीतोऽयं श्लोकं--मात इति ।

भस्त्रा चर्म-पुटकं, तत्-स्थानीया माता वीर्याधार-मात्रं, किन्तु पितुर् निषेक्तुर् एव पुत्रः । किं च, येन पित्रा जातो जनितः, स एव पुत्रः तद्-अंश-भूत-वीर्योपादानत्वात्, आत्मा वै जायते पुत्र इति वचनाच् च । अतः पुत्रं भरस्व बिभृहि । शकुन्तलां च निर्दोषाम् अवमंस्था इति भरत-नाम-निरुक्तिर् अपि ॥१२॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Mother is like a sack made of skin. One who is born to a father, the son belongs to the father only. So, they told Dushyanta, do take care of him, you are the one responsible for him. You are the father, don't disrespect Shakuntalaa. Sri Engal Aalwaan’s Commentary | | , | , | | | If an object is kept in a sack, that object belongs to the person who kept it there. It does not belong to the sack. That is how the name Bharata got formed.

VP 4.19.13

रेतोधाः पुत्रं नयति नरदेव यमक्षयात् । त्वं चास्य धाता गर्भस्य सत्यमाह शकुन्तला

The son helps the father not to go to naraka, O King. Son prevents father from falling into Yama's hands. Shakuntalaa is telling the truth - you are the one who is the father of the child that is in her womb. , , , There was a discussion regarding whom the son belongs to, the father or the mother. It is to one who gives the retas, the father. It does not belong to the mb of Shakuntalaa mother. You are the one who is the father of this the child in the womb.

Transliteration

Śrīdhara

kiṃ ca rotadhāḥ retasā dhīyate vidhīyate retodhāḥ sarmaṇyasun . aurasaḥ pitro’yamakṣayāt narakāt tatrasthaṃ pitaram unnayati ūrddhva svarga nayati yadvā retodhāḥ retaḥ sektā taha putram unnayati . tataḥ kim, ata āha, tvaṃ tvasya bharatasya retodhāḥ retasektā iti satyamevāha, ato bharasvety arthaḥ . nāmāniruktirūpaikakāryaparatvāt śloka ityekavacanamaviruddham ||१३||

Śrīdhara commentary.

The son helps the father not to go to naraka, O King. Son prevents father from falling into Yama's hands. VP 4.19.16

ततोस्य वितथे पुत्रजन्मनि पुत्रार्थिनो मरुत्सोमयाजिनो दीर्घतमसः पार्ष्ण्यपास्तबृहस्पतिवीर्यादुतथ्यपत्न्यां ममतायां समुत्पन्नो भरद्वाजाख्यः पुत्रो मरुद्भिर्दत्तः

When Bharata was not able to have a son, all his efforts got wasted, and he wanted to have a son, he performed a yaaga called Marutsoma, the Maruts gave him a son by name Bharadvaaja. , Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4 Bruhaspati's elder brother was Utatthya. Utatthya's wife was Mamataa. Mamataa was pregnant with Utatthya. The child in the womb of Mamataa was going to get the name Deerghatamas. Bruhaspati united with Mamataa, attracted by her pleasure, due to kaama. Utatthya's son was Outatthya, who was already in the womb of Mamataa, that child in the womb threw away Bruhaspati's veerya from the back of the foot, and that veerya fell on the earth. Then Bruhaspati gave him a curse that you become blind. That is why he was called Deerghatamas. That Bruhaspati veerya which fell down on the ground, became a child, on seeing that, that child which was born from two fathers - Bruhaspati and Utatthya, he tells Mamataa to take care of this child who was born from two fathers; she also tells Bruhaspati to bear him, and they both leave him. That is why he is called Bharadvaaja, Dvaajam bhara.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tatosya vitathe putrajanmani putrārthino marutsomayājino dīrghatamasaḥ pārṣṇyapāstabṛhaspativīryādutathyapatnyāṃ mamatāyāṃ samutpanno bharadvājākhyaḥ putro marudbhirdattaḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.19.17

तस्यापि नामनिर्वचनश्लोकः पठ्यते

How his name came into being is explained in this shloka.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tasyāpi nāmanirvacanaślokaḥ paṭhyate

Śrīdhara

vitartha vyarthe sati . dīrghatamaseti, atreyaṃ kathā, bṛhaspategrajasya utathyasya mamatākhyāyāṃ bhāryāyā bṛhaspatiḥ kāmābhibhūto reto’sṛjit . tacca garbha praviśad garbhasthitena sthānasaṅkocabhayāt pāṣṇaryāghātenāpāstaṃ bahiḥpātitamapi amogavīryatayā bharadvājanāmā putro’bhavat . garbhasthaśca bṛhaspatinā śapto’ndho dīrghatamā nāmnābhavat . sa bharadvājo devairdattaḥ . tatra ca bharadvājanāmaniruktiparastanmātāpitrorvivādarupaḥ śloko devaiḥ paṭhitaḥ ||17||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

वितर्थ व्यर्थे सति । दीर्घतमसेति, अत्रेयं कथा, बृहस्पतेग्रजस्य उतथ्यस्य ममताख्यायां भार्याया बृहस्पतिः कामाभिभूतो रेतोऽसृजित् । तच्च गर्भ प्रविशद् गर्भस्थितेन स्थानसङ्कोचभयात् पाष्णर्याघातेनापास्तं बहिःपातितमपि अमोगवीर्यतया भरद्वाजनामा पुत्रोऽभवत् । गर्भस्थश्च बृहस्पतिना शप्तोऽन्धो दीर्घतमा नाम्नाभवत् । स भरद्वाजो देवैर्दत्तः । तत्र च भरद्वाजनामनिरुक्तिपरस्तन्मातापित्रोर्विवादरुपः श्लोको देवैः पठितः ॥१७॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

How his name came into being is explained in this shloka.

VP 4.19.18

मूढे भर द्वाजमिमं भर द्वाजं बृहस्पते ।
यातौ यदुक्त्वा पितरौ भरद्वाजस्ततस्त्वयम्

“O foolish one, bear this Bharadvāja; O Bṛhaspati, bear this Bharadvāja.” Having said this, the two parents departed, and he himself thereafter was called Bharadvāja.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

भर (bhara) — bear/nourish
द्वाजम् इमम् (dvājam imam) — this Dvāja
भर द्वाजम् (bhara dvājam) — bear Dvāja

ततः स्वयम् (tataḥ svayam) — thereafter he himself [was so called].
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

mūḍhe bhara dvājamimaṃ bhara dvājaṃ bṛhaspate | yātau yaduktvā pitarau bharadvājastatastvayam

Śrīdhara

mūḍhe mamate dvājaṃ dvābhyām āvābhyāṃ jātaṃ imaṃ putraṃ bhara puṣāṇa ity evaṃ bruvantaṃ bṛhaspatiṃ mamatāha—he bṛhaspate tvam evemaṃ dvājaṃ bhara iti; rasam aparam uktvā parityajya pitarau mamatā-bṛhaspatī yat yasmāt yātau, tataḥ bharadvāja-śabdokter bharadvāja-saṃjño ’bhūd ity arthaḥ; yātau yad uktvā iti pāṭhe vivadamānāv ity adhyāhāryam

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

मूढे ममते द्वाजं द्वाभ्याम् आवाभ्यां जातम् इमं पुत्रं भर पुषाण इत्य् एवं ब्रुवन्तं बृहस्पतिं ममताह—हे बृहस्पते त्वम् एवेमं द्वाजं भर इति; रसम् अपरम् उक्त्वा परित्यज्य पितरौ ममता-बृहस्पती यत् यस्मात् यातौ, ततः भरद्वाज-शब्दोक्तेर् भरद्वाज-संज्ञोऽभूद् इत्य् अर्थः; यातौ यद् उक्त्वा इति पाठे विवादमानाव् इत्य् अध्याहार्यम् ॥१८॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

“O foolish Mamatā, bear and nourish this son born from both of us,” is what Bṛhaspati said. Mamatā replied, “O Bṛhaspati, you alone bear this Dvāja.” After saying the rest and abandoning the child, the two parents, Mamatā and Bṛhaspati, departed; therefore he became known by the name Bharadvāja. In the reading “having said this,” the words “while disputing” must be supplied.

tasmānmudgalasṛṃjayabṛhadiṣuyavīnarakāṃpilyasaṃjñāḥ pañcānāmaiva teṣāṃ viṣayāṇāṃ rakṣaṇāyālamete matputrā iti pitrābhihitāḥ pāñcālāḥ

Śrīdhara

nṛpāḥ kṣatriyāviśeṣasteṣāṃ kṣayaḥ kṛtaḥ . pañcānāṃ viṣacānāṃ madīyānāṃ deśānām ||५९||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

नृपाः क्षत्रियाविशेषस्तेषां क्षयः कृतः । पञ्चानां विषचानां मदीयानां देशानाम् ॥५९॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Haryashva had five sons - Mudgala, Srunjaya, Bruhadishu, Yaveenara, Kaampilya. When they were born, the father said that he had five countries, and these sons are capable of protecting the five countries, so they came to be known as Paanchaalaas.
