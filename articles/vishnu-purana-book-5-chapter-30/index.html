VP 5.30.1

Sri Paraasharar - Garuda carried the chatra of Varuna, and also the Maniparvata, and Krishna and also Satyabhaama. He carried all of them without any effort.

VP 5.30.6

नमस्ते पुण्डरीकाक्ष भक्तानामभयङ्कर । सनातनात्मन् सर्वात्मन् भूतात्मन् भूतभावन

VP 5.30.7

प्रणेतर्मनसो बुद्धेरिन्द्रियाणां गुणात्मक । त्रिगुणातीत निर्द्वद्व शुद्धसत्त्व हृदि स्थित

O Krishna, who is an abode to all the auspicious qualities. You are the one who controls the mind and buddhi, knowledge and all the senses. You are beyond the three qualities of prakruti, sattva, rajas, tamas. You are without any dualities of life, such as sukha, dukha, etc. You are antaryaami of everything, You have divine form.

You are immutable, so You do not have whiteness, blackness, long, short, etc. of mortals. You do not have any of these vikaaraas. You are untouched by birth, death, etc. You are untouched by the defects of the nature of dream, etc.

VP 5.30.9

संध्यारात्रिरहो भूमिर्गगनं वायुरंबु च । हुताशनो मनो बुद्धिर्भूतादिस्त्वं तथाऽच्युत

You are only the sandhyaa kaala, You are only the night, You are only the day. You are only the earth, and You are the sky also. You are the vaayu, the waters, fire, mind, jnyaana. You are the creator of all beings. O Achyuta. You are the inner controller of everything, and You are the creator. All of the beings are Your shareera only. You support and control everything.

VP 5.30.13

स्थूला मध्यास्तथा सूक्ष्माःसूक्ष्मात्सूक्ष्मतराश्च ये । देहभेदा भवान् सर्वे ये केचित्पुर्गलाश्रयाः

Those which are the gross, the subtle, those which are in between, those which are extremely subtle, all these differences in the forms that we see, all are You only. All those which are having a jeeva inside, You are only that. He is the inner self of everything. He is having everything as His shareera. He is the inner self of those gross bodies of whatever form. All the difference in the bodies that we see, which are having a jeevaatman inside. As per Koorma Puraana, purgala means jeeva. But as per Jainaas, purgala is paramaanu, because it fills and flows.

Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

* na vyākhyātam.* deha-bhedā bhavān sarve ye kecitpurgalāśrayāḥ619 || 13 || viṣṇu-cittaḥ — sthūlā iti | sthūlā ity ādinā purgalāśrayā ity antena tasyaiva tad-dehātmakatvam ucyate | ye kecit purgalāśrayāḥ deha-bhedāḥ, te sarve bhavān ity anvayaḥ | purgalo jīvaḥ, yathā kaurme— ahaṅkāro’bhimānaś ca kartā mantā ca sa smṛtaḥ | ātmā ca purgalo jīvo yataḥ sarvāḥ prasūtayaḥ || iti |

VP 5.30.14

माया तवेयमज्ञातपरमार्थाऽतिमोहिनी । अनात्मन्यात्मविज्ञानं यया मूढो निरुद्ध्यते

This prakruti is Your maayaa only. Because of that, we forget Brahma, as we are covered by the prakruti. We don't realize the truth. And this prakruti causes extreme moha. The effect of maaya is the having the idea of aatman in that which is not aatman, thinking of the body itself as aatman. Those who have their knowledge covered by prakruti, are bound in this prakruti.

Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

māyā taveyamajñātaparamārthā’timohinī | anātmanyātmavijñānaṃ yayā mūḍho niruddhyate

Śrīdhara

sthūlā iti | pūryanti galanti ceti purgalāḥ vṛddhy-apakṣaya-bhājo ye avayavāḥ, tad-āśrayā deha-bhedāḥ | yad āhuḥ—pūraṇād galanād dehe purgalāḥ paramāṇavaḥ iti || 13 || anātmany ātma-vijñānaṃ yayā mūḍhoniruddhyate621 || 14 || viṣṇu-cittaḥ — māyeti | ajñāta-paramārthā ajñātaḥ paramārtho brahma yasyā hetoḥ sā ajñāta-paramārthā atimohinī iyaṃ triguṇātmikā māyā tava tvadīyā | māyā-kāryam āha—anātmanīti asyārthasya pūrveṇānvayaḥ || 14 || śrīdharaḥ — atha māyā-svarūpānuvāda-pūrvakaṃ tan-nivṛtti prārthayate māyā taveyam ity ādinā nāśayety antena | māyeti | ajñāta-paramārthānāṃ jīvānām atimohinī | moham evāha—anātmanīti || 14 || asve svam iti bhāvo’tra yat puṃsām upajāyate |

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

स्थूला इति । पूर्यन्ति गलन्ति चेति पुर्गलाः वृद्ध्य्-अपक्षय-भाजो ये अवयवाः, तद्-आश्रया देह-भेदाः । यद् आहुः—पूरणाद् गलनाद् देहे पुर्गलाः परमाणवः इति ॥१३॥


अनात्मन्य् आत्म-विज्ञानं यया मूढोनिरुद्ध्यते६२१॥१४॥

विष्णु-चित्तः — मायेति । अज्ञात-परमार्था अज्ञातः परमार्थो ब्रह्म यस्या हेतोः सा अज्ञात-परमार्था अतिमोहिनी इयं त्रिगुणात्मिका माया तव त्वदीया । माया-कार्यम् आह—अनात्मनीति अस्यार्थस्य पूर्वेणान्वयः ॥१४॥

श्रीधरः — अथ माया-स्वरूपानुवाद-पूर्वकं तन्-निवृत्ति प्रार्थयते माया तवेयम् इत्य् आदिना नाशयेत्य् अन्तेन । मायेति । अज्ञात-परमार्थानां जीवानाम् अतिमोहिनी । मोहम् एवाह—अनात्मनीति ॥१४॥

अस्वे स्वम् इति भावोऽत्र यत् पुंसाम् उपजायते ।

Śrīdhara commentary.

No separate English commentary is supplied.

VP 5.30.15

अहं ममेति भावो यत्प्रायेणैवाभिजायते । संसारमातुर्मायायास्तवैतन्नाथ चेष्टितम्

That which does not belong to one, people think that it belongs to them only. People think that what is not theirs, is theirs. This kind of knowledge which is present in the humans, this kind of ahamkaara and mamakaara, that "I am the doer", which is present in the human beings here, the bound selves, all these which are caused by prakruti which is the cause of samsaara, are Your own sport only. These are all Your own leelaa only.

Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ahaṃ mameti bhāvo yatprāyeṇaivābhijāyate | saṃsāramāturmāyāyāstavaitannātha ceṣṭitam

Śrīdhara
