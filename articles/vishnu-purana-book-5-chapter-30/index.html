O director of mind, intellect, and senses, whose nature is the guṇas; beyond the three guṇas, free of duality, pure sattva, abiding in the heart.

“Praṇetā” means “director, agent.” By “whose nature is the guṇas”: tamas makes [you] the source of beings, sattva the director of mind, and rajas the director of intellect and senses.
