Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 83 — The Auspicious Observance

BP 1.83.1

ब्रह्मोवाच । मासि भाद्रपदे वीर शुक्ले पक्षे तु यो भवेत् । षष्ठ्यां गण-कुल-श्रेष्ठ स भद्रः परिकीर्तितः ॥ ॥

Brahmā said. O hero, the sixth day that falls in the bright fortnight of the month of Bhādrapada is proclaimed Auspicious, O chief of hosts.

brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said;
māsi — in the month;
bhādrapade — in Bhādrapada;
vīra — O hero;
śukle pakṣe — in the bright fortnight;
tu — indeed;
yaḥ — which;
bhavet — occurs;
ṣaṣṭhyām — on the sixth day;
gaṇa-kula-śreṣṭha — O best of the hosts;
saḥ — that;
bhadraḥ — Bhadra;
parikīrtitaḥ — proclaimed.

BP 1.83.2

तत्र नक्तं तु यः कुर्याद् उपवासम् अथापि वा । हंस-यान-समारूढो याति हंस-सलोकताम् ॥ ॥

Whoever observes the night fast there, or even a complete fast, mounts a swan vehicle and goes to the world of the swans.

tatra — there;
naktam — night observance;
tu — indeed;
yaḥ — whoever;
kuryāt — should perform;
upavāsam — fasting;
atha api vā — or else;
haṃsa-yāna-samārūḍhaḥ — mounted upon a swan vehicle;
yāti — goes;
haṃsa-salokatām — to the world of the swans.

BP 1.83.3

मालती-कुसुमानीह तथा श्वेतं च चन्दनम् । विजयं च तथा धूपं नैवेद्यं पायसं परम् ॥ ॥

Jasmine flowers, white sandalwood, victory incense, and excellent rice pudding are prescribed here as offerings.

mālatī-kusumāni — Mālatī flowers;
iha — here;
tathā — likewise;
śvetam — white;
ca — and;
candanam — sandalwood;
vijayam — Vijaya;
ca — and;
tathā — likewise;
dhūpam — incense;
naivedyam — food offering;
pāyasam — milk-rice;
param — excellent.

BP 1.83.4

पूजायां भास्करस्येह कुर्यात् त्रिपुर-सूदन । इत्थं सम्पूज्य देवेशं मध्याह्ने च दिनाधिपम् ॥ ॥

For the worship of the Sun, one should perform this, O destroyer of Tripura. Having thus worshipped the Lord of gods and the Lord of the day at midday.

pūjāyām — in worship;
bhāskarasya — of Bhāskara;
iha — here;
kuryāt — should perform;
tripura-sūdana — O destroyer of Tripura;
ittham — thus;
sampūjya — having fully worshiped;
deveśam — Lord of gods;
madhyāhne — at midday;
ca — and;
dinādhipam — Lord of the day.

BP 1.83.5

दत्त्वा तु दक्षिणां शक्त्या ततो भुञ्जीत वाग्यतः । पायसं गण-शार्दूल स-गुडं सर्पिषा सह ॥ ॥

Giving the offering according to his means, he should then eat in silence, O tiger among hosts, rice pudding with jaggery and ghee.

dattvā — having given;
tu — indeed;
dakṣiṇām — sacrificial fee;
śaktyā — according to ability;
tataḥ — then;
bhuñjīta — should eat;
vāk-yataḥ — silently, with speech restrained;
pāyasam — milk-rice;
gaṇa-śārdūla — O tiger among the hosts;
sa-guḍam — with jaggery;
sarpiṣā — with ghee;
saha — together.

BP 1.83.6

य एवं पूजयेद् भक्त्या मानवः तिमिरापहम् । सर्व-कामान् अवाप्नोति पुत्र-दार-धनादिकान् ॥ ॥

Whoever worships in this way with devotion obtains every desire, such as sons, a wife, and wealth, through the Sun, remover of darkness.

yaḥ — whoever;
evam — thus;
pūjayet — should worship;
bhaktyā — with devotion;
mānavaḥ — person;
timira-apaham — remover of darkness;
sarva-kāmān — every desire;
avāpnoti — obtains;
putra-dāra-dhana-ādikān — sons, wife, wealth, and the like.

BP 1.83.7

विमुक्तः सर्व-पापेभ्यो व्रजेद् भानु-सलोकताम् । एष भद्रा-विधिः प्रोक्तो मया यस् ते गणाधिप ॥ ॥

Freed from every sin, he goes to the world of the Sun. This Auspicious rite has been declared by me for you, O chief of hosts.

vimuktaḥ — freed;
sarva-pāpebhyaḥ — from every sin;
vrajet — should go;
bhānu-salokatām — to the world of Bhānu;
eṣaḥ — this;
bhadrā-vidhiḥ — Bhadra rite;
proktaḥ — declared;
mayā — by me;
yaḥ — which;
te — for you;
gaṇa-adhipa — O Lord of hosts.

BP 1.83.8

श्रुत्वा कृत्वा च यत् पापान् मुच्यते मानवो भुवि ॥ ॥

By hearing and performing it, a person on earth is freed from sins.

śrutvā — having heard;
kṛtvā — having performed;
ca — and;
yat — which;
pāpāt — from sin;
mucyate — is freed;
mānavaḥ — person;
bhuvi — on earth.
