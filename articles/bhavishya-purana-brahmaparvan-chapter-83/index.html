Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 83 — The Bhadra Observance

BP 1.83.1

ब्रह्मोवाच । मासि भाद्रपदे वीर शुक्ले पक्षे तु यो भवेत् । षष्ठ्यां गण-कुल-श्रेष्ठ स भद्रः परिकीर्तितः ॥ ॥

Brahmā said: O hero, that sixth day which occurs in the bright fortnight of the month of Bhādrapada is proclaimed as Bhadra, O best of the hosts.

Word-for-word
brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said;
māsi — in the month;
bhādrapade — in Bhādrapada;
vīra — O hero;
śukle pakṣe — in the bright fortnight;
tu — indeed;
yaḥ — which;
bhavet — occurs;
ṣaṣṭhyām — on the sixth day;
gaṇa-kula-śreṣṭha — O best of the hosts;
saḥ — that;
bhadraḥ — Bhadra;
parikīrtitaḥ — proclaimed.
Transliteration
brahmovāca | māsi bhādrapade vīra śukle pakṣe tu yo bhavet | ṣaṣṭhyāṃ gaṇa-kula-śreṣṭha sa bhadraḥ parikīrtitaḥ || 1 ||

BP 1.83.2

तत्र नक्तं तु यः कुर्याद् उपवासम् अथापि वा । हंस-यान-समारूढो याति हंस-सलोकताम् ॥ ॥

Whoever there observes the night fast, or even a complete fast, mounts a swan vehicle and goes to the world of the swans.

Word-for-word
tatra — there;
naktam — night observance;
tu — indeed;
yaḥ — whoever;
kuryāt — should perform;
upavāsam — fasting;
atha api vā — or else;
haṃsa-yāna-samārūḍhaḥ — mounted upon a swan vehicle;
yāti — goes;
haṃsa-salokatām — to the world of the swans.
Transliteration
tatra naktaṃ tu yaḥ kuryād upavāsam athāpi vā | haṃsa-yāna-samārūḍho yāti haṃsa-salokatām || 2 ||

BP 1.83.3

मालती-कुसुमानीह तथा श्वेतं च चन्दनम् । विजयं च तथा धूपं नैवेद्यं पायसं परम् ॥ ॥

Here the Mālatī flowers, white sandalwood, Vijaya incense, and excellent milk-rice are prescribed.

Word-for-word
mālatī-kusumāni — Mālatī flowers;
iha — here;
tathā — likewise;
śvetam — white;
ca — and;
candanam — sandalwood;
vijayam — Vijaya;
ca — and;
tathā — likewise;
dhūpam — incense;
naivedyam — food offering;
pāyasam — milk-rice;
param — excellent.
Transliteration
mālatī-kusumānīha tathā śvetaṃ ca candanam | vijayaṃ ca tathā dhūpaṃ naivedyaṃ pāyasaṃ param || 3 ||

BP 1.83.4

पूजायां भास्करस्येह कुर्यात् त्रिपुर-सूदन । इत्थं सम्पूज्य देवेशं मध्याह्ने च दिनाधिपम् ॥ ॥

In the worship of Bhāskara one should perform this, O destroyer of Tripura. Having fully worshiped the Lord of gods and the Lord of the day at midday,

Word-for-word
pūjāyām — in worship;
bhāskarasya — of Bhāskara;
iha — here;
kuryāt — should perform;
tripura-sūdana — O destroyer of Tripura;
ittham — thus;
sampūjya — having fully worshiped;
deveśam — Lord of gods;
madhyāhne — at midday;
ca — and;
dinādhipam — Lord of the day.
Transliteration
pūjāyāṃ bhāskarasyeha kuryāt tripura-sūdana | itthaṃ sampūjya deveśaṃ madhyāhne ca dinādhipam || 4 ||

BP 1.83.5

दत्त्वा तु दक्षिणां शक्त्या ततो भुञ्जीत वाग्यतः । पायसं गण-शार्दूल स-गुडं सर्पिषा सह ॥ ॥

having given the sacrificial fee according to ones means, one should then eat in silence, O tiger among the hosts, milk-rice together with jaggery and ghee.

Word-for-word
dattvā — having given;
tu — indeed;
dakṣiṇām — sacrificial fee;
śaktyā — according to ability;
tataḥ — then;
bhuñjīta — should eat;
vāk-yataḥ — silently, with speech restrained;
pāyasam — milk-rice;
gaṇa-śārdūla — O tiger among the hosts;
sa-guḍam — with jaggery;
sarpiṣā — with ghee;
saha — together.
Transliteration
dattvā tu dakṣiṇāṃ śaktyā tato bhuñjīta vāgyataḥ | pāyasaṃ gaṇa-śārdūla sa-guḍaṃ sarpiṣā saha || 5 ||

BP 1.83.6

य एवं पूजयेद् भक्त्या मानवः तिमिरापहम् । सर्व-कामान् अवाप्नोति पुत्र-दार-धनादिकान् ॥ ॥

The person who worships in this way with devotion obtains every desire, such as sons, wife, wealth, and the rest, through the remover of darkness.

Word-for-word
yaḥ — whoever;
evam — thus;
pūjayet — should worship;
bhaktyā — with devotion;
mānavaḥ — person;
timira-apaham — remover of darkness;
sarva-kāmān — every desire;
avāpnoti — obtains;
putra-dāra-dhana-ādikān — sons, wife, wealth, and the like.
Transliteration
ya evaṃ pūjayed bhaktyā mānavaḥ timirāpaham | sarva-kāmān avāpnoti putra-dāra-dhanādikān || 6 ||

BP 1.83.7

विमुक्तः सर्व-पापेभ्यो व्रजेद् भानु-सलोकताम् । एष भद्रा-विधिः प्रोक्तो मया यस् ते गणाधिप ॥ ॥

Freed from every sin, he goes to the world of Bhānu. This Bhadra rite has been declared by me for you, O Lord of hosts.

Word-for-word

proktaḥ — declared;
mayā — by me;
yaḥ — which;
te — for you;
gaṇa-adhipa — O Lord of hosts.
Transliteration
vimuktaḥ sarva-pāpebhyo vrajed bhānu-salokatām | eṣa bhadrā-vidhiḥ prokto mayā yas te gaṇādhipa || 7 ||

BP 1.83.8

श्रुत्वा कृत्वा च यत् पापान् मुच्यते मानवो भुवि ॥ ॥

By hearing and performing it, a person on earth is freed from sins.

Word-for-word

mānavaḥ — person;
bhuvi — on earth.
Transliteration
śrutvā kṛtvā ca yat pāpān mucyate mānavo bhuvi || 8 ||
