Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 83 — The Auspicious Observance

BP 1.83.1

ब्रह्मोवाच । मासि भाद्रपदे वीर शुक्ले पक्षे तु यो भवेत् । षष्ठ्यां गण-कुल-श्रेष्ठ स भद्रः परिकीर्तितः ॥ ॥

Brahmā said. O hero, the sixth day that falls in the bright fortnight of the month of Bhādrapada is proclaimed Auspicious, O chief of hosts.

BP 1.83.2

तत्र नक्तं तु यः कुर्याद् उपवासम् अथापि वा । हंस-यान-समारूढो याति हंस-सलोकताम् ॥ ॥

Whoever observes the night fast there, or even a complete fast, mounts a swan vehicle and goes to the world of the swans.

BP 1.83.3

मालती-कुसुमानीह तथा श्वेतं च चन्दनम् । विजयं च तथा धूपं नैवेद्यं पायसं परम् ॥ ॥

Jasmine flowers, white sandalwood, victory incense, and excellent rice pudding are prescribed here as offerings.

BP 1.83.4

पूजायां भास्करस्येह कुर्यात् त्रिपुर-सूदन । इत्थं सम्पूज्य देवेशं मध्याह्ने च दिनाधिपम् ॥ ॥

For the worship of the Sun, one should perform this, O destroyer of Tripura. Having thus worshipped the Lord of gods and the Lord of the day at midday.

BP 1.83.5

दत्त्वा तु दक्षिणां शक्त्या ततो भुञ्जीत वाग्यतः । पायसं गण-शार्दूल स-गुडं सर्पिषा सह ॥ ॥

Giving the offering according to his means, he should then eat in silence, O tiger among hosts, rice pudding with jaggery and ghee.

BP 1.83.6

य एवं पूजयेद् भक्त्या मानवः तिमिरापहम् । सर्व-कामान् अवाप्नोति पुत्र-दार-धनादिकान् ॥ ॥

Whoever worships in this way with devotion obtains every desire, such as sons, a wife, and wealth, through the Sun, remover of darkness.

BP 1.83.7

विमुक्तः सर्व-पापेभ्यो व्रजेद् भानु-सलोकताम् । एष भद्रा-विधिः प्रोक्तो मया यस् ते गणाधिप ॥ ॥

Freed from every sin, he goes to the world of the Sun. This Auspicious rite has been declared by me for you, O chief of hosts.

BP 1.83.8

श्रुत्वा कृत्वा च यत् पापान् मुच्यते मानवो भुवि ॥ ॥

By hearing and performing it, a person on earth is freed from sins.
