Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 191 — The Seven-Horsed Sun–Aruṇa Dialogue

BP 1.191.1

सप्ताश्वतिलक उवाच ।। ये गोब्राह्मणसस्यानां साधूनां तु तपस्विनाम् ।। दूषकाश्चैव वर्तंते नरा नरकगामिनः ।। १ ।।

The Seven-Horsed Sun said: Men who harm cows, Brahmins, crops, good people, and ascetics are bound for hell.

BP 1.191.2

परिश्रमेण तप्यन्ते येऽपरे तस्य सूचकाः ।। परदाररतानां च कन्याया दूषकाश्च ये ।। २ ।।

Those who report them are tormented by toil; so are those devoted to other men’s wives and those who defile maidens.

BP 1.191.3

गोष्ठाग्निजलरम्यासु तरुच्छायानगेषु च ।। त्यजंति ये पुरीषाणि आरामा यतनेषु च ।। ३ ।।

Those who leave feces in cow-pens, near fires, in pleasant waters, beneath tree shade, on mountains, in gardens, and in shrines.

BP 1.191.4

मद्यपानरता नित्यं गीतवाद्यरता नराः ।। कामक्रोधमदाविष्टा रंध्रान्वेषणतत्पराः ।। ४ ।।

Men always devoted to drinking liquor and to song and music, overcome by desire, anger, and pride, intent on seeking faults.

BP 1.191.5

पाखण्डमतसंयुक्ता वृथा संलाप कौतुकाः ।। ये मार्गानुपरुधंति परसीमां हरंति च ।। ५ ।।

Those joined to heretical doctrines, delighting in idle talk, who block roads and seize another’s boundary.

BP 1.191.6

कूटशासनकर्तारः कूटकर्मकृतो नराः ।। धनुषः शिल्पिशस्त्राणां यः कर्ता यश्च विक्रयी ।। ६ ।।

Men who make false decrees and false deeds; whoever makes and sells bows, crafts, and weapons.

BP 1.191.7

निर्दयोतीवभृत्येषु पशूनां दमकश्च यः ।। मिथ्या प्रवदतो वाचमाकर्णयति यः शनैः ।। स्वामिमित्रगुरुद्रोही मायावी चपलः शठः ।। ७ ।।

He who is exceedingly merciless toward servants and tames animals; he who quietly listens to false speech; he who betrays his master, friend, and teacher, deceitful, fickle, and treacherous.

BP 1.191.8

ये भार्यापुत्रमित्राणि बालवृद्धकुशातुरान् ।। भृत्यानतिथिबन्धूंश्च त्यजंति च बुभुक्षितान् ।। ८ ।।

Those who leave their wives, sons, friends, children, the aged, the weak and sick, servants, guests, and relatives hungry.

BP 1.191.9

यः स्वयं पक्वमश्नाति विप्रायान्नं न यच्छति ।। वृथा पाकः स विज्ञेयो ब्रह्मवादिषु गर्हितः ।। ९ ।।

Whoever eats food cooked by himself and does not give food to a Brahmin: that cooking is known to be fruitless, condemned among those who speak of Brahman.

BP 1.191.10

नियमं स्वयमादाय ये त्यजंत्यजितेंद्रियाः ।। प्रव्रज्यावसिता ये च रहस्यानां तु भेदकाः ।। 1.191.१० ।।

Those who take a rule upon themselves and then abandon it, with their senses unrestrained; those intent on renunciation, and those who reveal secrets.

BP 1.191.11

ये ताडयंति गां मूढास्त्रासयंति मुहुर्मुहुः ।। दुर्बलं न च पुष्णंति प्रनष्टान्नान्विषंति च ।। ११ ।।

Foolish men who beat a cow and frighten her again and again, do not feed the weak, and do not search for the lost.

BP 1.191.12

पीडयंत्यतिभारेण अक्षयं वाहयंति च ।। वृषाणां वृषणानन्ये पापिष्ठा गालयंति हि ।। वाहयंति च गां वंध्यां ते पापिष्ठा नराधमाः ।। १२ ।।

They oppress them with excessive loads and make them carry without respite; others, the worst sinners, castrate bulls; those worst men, lowest among men, make a barren cow carry loads.

BP 1.191.13

अनर्थविकलं हीनं बालवृद्धकृशानुगम् ।। नानुकंपंति ये मूढास्ते यांति नरकं नराः ।। १३ ।।

Those foolish men who show no compassion for the distressed, the disabled, the destitute, children, the aged, and the frail go to hell.

BP 1.191.14

अजाविका माहिषिकाः सवित्रीवृषलीपतिः ।। क्षत्रविट्शूद्रवृद्धाश्च स्वधर्मविहताः सदा ।। १४ ।।

Goat and sheep herders, buffalo herders, husbands of Śūdra women, and Kṣatriyas, Vaiśyas, Śūdras, and elders who always neglect their own duty.

BP 1.191.15

शिल्पिनः कारुका वेश्याः क्षेमकारनृपध्वजाः ।। नर्तक्यो ज्योतिषि हताः सर्वे नरकगामिनः ।। १५ ।।

Artisans, craftsmen, courtesans, those who secure well-being, royal standard-bearers, female dancers, and those ruined by astrology all go to hell.

BP 1.191.16

घृत तैलानुपानानि मधुमांसरसासवम् ।। गुडेक्षुक्षीरशाकान्हि दधिमूलफलानि च ।। १६ ।।

Ghee, oil, drinks taken after food, honey, meat, juices, and liquor; jaggery, sugarcane, milk, greens, curd, roots, and fruits.

BP 1.191.17

तृणानि काष्ठं पुष्पाणि बीजौषधिमनुत्तमाम् ।। उपानच्छत्र शकटमासनं शयनं मृदः ।।१७।।

Grass, wood, flowers, seeds, the finest medicinal herbs, footwear, umbrellas, carts, seats, beds, and clay.

BP 1.191.18

ताम्रं सीसं त्रपुं कांस्यं शंखाद्यं च जलोद्भवम् ।। वार्क्षं वा वैणवं वापि गृहोपकरणानि च ।।१८।।

Copper, lead, tin, bronze, conch and other things born from water, things made of wood or bamboo, and household utensils.

BP 1.191.19

और्णकार्पासकौशेयभंगपट्टोद्भवानि च ।। स्थूलसूक्ष्माणि संमूढा ये च लोका हरंति वै ।।१९।।

Wool, cotton, silk, hemp, and linen goods, coarse and fine things that foolish people steal.

BP 1.191.20

एवमादीनि चान्यानि द्रव्याणि विविधानि च।। नरकेषु ध्रुवं गच्छेद्यौ हरेत पुमान्बलात् ।। 1.191.२० ।।

Other various goods of this kind: whoever takes them by force surely goes to hell.

BP 1.191.21

यद्वा तद्वा तु पारोक्ष्यमपि सर्षपमात्रकम् ।। अपहृत्य नरो याति नरकं नात्र संशयः ।। २१ ।।

Whatever it may be, even a hidden thing, even a mustard-seed measure: having stolen it, a man goes to hell; there is no doubt.

BP 1.191.22

एवमाद्यैर्नरः पापैरुत्क्रांतेः समनंतरम् ।। शरीरयातनार्थं तत्पूर्वकायमवाप्नुयात् ।। २२ ।।

Immediately after death, because of sins such as these, a man obtains a former body for bodily torment.

BP 1.191.23

यमलोके व्रजेदेवं शरीरेण यमाज्ञया ।। यमदूतैर्महाघोरैर्नीयमानः सुदुःखितः ।। २३ ।।

By Yama’s command, he goes in a body to Yama’s world, led by Yama’s greatly dreadful messengers and greatly distressed.

BP 1.191.24

देवमानुषजीवानामधर्मनिरतात्मनाम् ।। धर्मराजः स्मृतः शास्ता सुघोरैर्विविधैर्वधैः ।। २४ ।।

For divine and human beings whose minds are devoted to unrighteousness, the King of Justice is regarded as the chastiser, with very dreadful and varied punishments.

BP 1.191.25

विनयाभावयुक्तानां प्रमादा त्स्वलितात्मनाम् ।। प्रायश्चित्तैर्बहुविधैः पातकं नष्टतामियात् ।। २५ ।।

For those without discipline, whose minds have strayed through carelessness, sin may be destroyed by many kinds of atonement.

BP 1.191.26

पारदारिकचोराणामन्यायव्यवहारिणाम् ।। शास्ता क्षितिपतिः प्रोक्तः प्रच्छन्नानां च धर्मराट् ।। २६ ।।

For adulterers, thieves, and those who deal unjustly, the earthly king is declared the chastiser; for those who act in secret, the King of Justice.

BP 1.191.27

तस्मात्कृतस्य पापस्य प्रायश्चित्तं समाचरेत् ।। नाभुक्तस्यान्यथा नाशः कल्पकोटिशतैरपि ।। २७ ।।

Therefore one should perform atonement for a sin committed; what has not been experienced is not destroyed, even in a hundred crores of aeons.

BP 1.191.28

यः करोति शुभं कर्म कारयेदनुमोदयेत् ।। कायेन मनसा वाचा स विंदेतोत्तमं सुखम् ।।२८।।

Whoever does a good deed, causes it to be done, or approves it by body, mind, and speech obtains the highest happiness.

BP 1.191.29

इति संक्षेपतः प्रोक्ता पापभेदात्त्रिधा गतिः ।। तथान्या गतयश्चित्राः कथ्यंते कर्मभेदतः ।। २९ ।।

Thus, briefly, the threefold course according to the kinds of sin has been told; other varied courses, too, are told according to the kinds of deeds.
