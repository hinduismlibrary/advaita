अहम् एवाक्षयो नित्यः परमात्मात्म-संश्रयः।
ब्रह्म-सञ्ज्ञोऽहम् एवाग्रे तथान्ते च परः पुमान्॥८६॥

I am undecayable, ever enduring, the receptacle of the spirit of the supreme. Brahma is my name; the supreme soul, that is before all things, that is after the end of all.
