Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 40 — Birth, Conduct and the Basis of Varṇa

BP 1.40.1

शतानीक उवाच
अहो व्रतं महत्कष्टं संशयो हृदि वर्तते ।
कार्तिकेयस्य माहात्म्यं श्रुत्वा जन्म तथा द्विज ॥

Śatānīka said: Ah, this vow is exceedingly difficult, and a doubt remains in my heart after hearing of Kārttikeyas greatness and birth, O brāhmaṇa.

Word-for-word
शतानीकः उवाच (śatānīkaḥ uvāca) — Śatānīka said;
अहो व्रतम् महत् कष्टम् (aho vratam mahat kaṣṭam) — ah, vow greatly difficult;
संशयः हृदि वर्तते (saṃśayaḥ hṛdi vartate) — doubt remains in heart;
कार्तिकेयस्य माहात्म्यम् (kārtikeyasya māhātmyam) — Kārttikeya’s greatness;
श्रुत्वा जन्म तथा (śrutvā janma tathā) — hearing also birth
Transliteration
śatānīka uvāca
aho vrataṃ mahatkaṣṭaṃ saṃśayo hṛdi vartate |
kārtikeyasya māhātmyaṃ śrutvā janma tathā dvija | | 1

BP 1.40.2

अनेकजनितस्येह कार्त्तिकेयस्य सुव्रत ।
माहात्म्यं सुमहद्विप्र कथमेतद्विभाव्यते ॥

Keeper of vows and brāhmaṇa, since Kārttikeya is described as born from many sources, how is his very great greatness to be understood?

Word-for-word
अनेक-जनितस्य (aneka-janitasya) — of one born from many;
कार्तिकेयस्य (kārtikeyasya) — Kārttikeya;
सुव्रत विप्र (suvrata vipra) — keeper of vows, brāhmaṇa;
माहात्म्यम् सु-महत् (māhātmyam su-mahat) — very great greatness;
कथम् एतत् विभाव्यते (katham etat vibhāvyate) — how is this understood
Transliteration
anekajanitasyeha kārttikeyasya suvrata |
māhātmyaṃ sumahadvipra kathametadvibhāvyate | | 2

BP 1.40.3

जातिः श्रेष्ठा भवेद्वीर उत कर्म भवेद्वरम् ।
संशयस्तु महानत्र दृष्ट्वा मे कृत्तिकासुतम् ॥

Is birth superior, O hero, or is action more excellent? Seeing the son of the Kṛttikās has raised this great doubt in me.

Word-for-word
जातिः श्रेष्ठा भवेत् (jātiḥ śreṣṭhā bhavet) — is birth superior;
उत कर्म भवेत् वरम् (uta karma bhavet varam) — or action more excellent;
संशयः महान् (saṃśayaḥ mahān) — great doubt;
दृष्ट्वा मे कृत्तिका-सुतम् (dṛṣṭvā me kṛttikā-sutam) — seeing son of Kṛttikās
Transliteration
jātiḥ śreṣṭhā bhavedvīra uta karma bhavedvaram |
saṃśayastu mahānatra dṛṣṭvā me kṛttikāsutam | | 3

BP 1.40.4

एतद्वद विनिश्चित्य न यथा संशयो भवेत् ।
जन्मतः कर्मणश्चैव यज्ज्वायस्तद् ब्रवीहि मे ॥

Resolve and explain this so no doubt remains: tell me which is greater, birth or action.

Word-for-word
एतत् वद विनिश्चित्य (etat vada viniścitya) — state this after deciding;
न यथा संशयः भवेत् (na yathā saṃśayaḥ bhavet) — so doubt does not remain;
जन्मतः कर्मणः च (janmataḥ karmaṇaḥ ca) — of birth and action;
यत् ज्यायः (yat jyāyaḥ) — whichever greater;
तत् ब्रूहि मे (tat brūhi me) — tell me that
Transliteration
etadvada viniścitya na yathā saṃśayo bhavet |
janmataḥ karmaṇaścaiva yajjvāyastad bravīhi me | | 4

BP 1.40.5

सुमन्तुरुवाच
इममर्थं पुरा पृष्टो ब्रह्मा शिष्यैर्मनीषिभिः ।
यदुक्तं तेन तेषां च तत्ते वच्मि निबोध मे ॥

Sumantu said: Long ago Brahmā was asked this very question by his thoughtful disciples. Understand as I relate to you what he told them.

Word-for-word
सुमन्तुः उवाच (sumantuḥ uvāca) — Sumantu said;
इमम् अर्थम् पुरा पृष्टः ब्रह्मा (imam artham purā pṛṣṭaḥ brahmā) — Brahmā formerly asked this matter;
शिष्यैः मनीषिभिः (śiṣyaiḥ manīṣibhiḥ) — by thoughtful disciples;
यत् उक्तम् तेन तेषाम् (yat uktam tena teṣām) — what he told them;
तत् ते वच्मि निबोध (tat te vacmi nibodha) — I tell you, understand
Transliteration
sumanturuvāca
imamarthaṃ purā pṛṣṭo brahmā śiṣyairmanīṣibhiḥ |
yaduktaṃ tena teṣāṃ ca tatte vacmi nibodha me | | 5

BP 1.40.8

भो ब्रह्मन्नादकल्पे हि ब्राह्मण्यं ब्रूहि किं भवेत् ।
जात्यध्ययनदेहात्मसंस्काराचारकर्मणाम् ॥

Word-for-word

आदि-कल्पे (ādi-kalpe) — at beginning of age;
ब्राह्मण्यम् किम् भवेत् (brāhmaṇyam kim bhavet) — what constitutes brāhmaṇa status;
जाति-अध्ययन-देह-आत्म-संस्कार-आचार-कर्मणाम् (jāti-adhyayana-deha-ātma-saṃskāra-ācāra-karmaṇām) — among birth study body self rites conduct action
Transliteration

BP 1.40.9

BP 1.40.10

सन्त्यक्तव्याः प्रसिद्धा ये जातिभेदविधायिनः ।
वस्तुभूताः परोक्षैर्वा प्रमाणैर्न विनिश्चिताः ॥

The familiar characteristics that institute birth-based divisions ought to be abandoned if they cannot be established as real entities by perceptual or other means of valid knowledge.

Word-for-word

वस्तु-भूताः (vastu-bhūtāḥ) — real entities;
प्रत्यक्षैः वा प्रमाणैः (pratyakṣaiḥ vā pramāṇaiḥ) — by perception or valid means;
न विनिश्चिताः (na viniścitāḥ) — not established
Transliteration
