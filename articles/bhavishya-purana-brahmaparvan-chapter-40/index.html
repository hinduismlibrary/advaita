Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 40 — Birth, Conduct and the Basis of Varṇa

BP 1.40.1

शतानीक उवाच
अहो व्रतं महत्कष्टं संशयो हृदि वर्तते ।
कार्तिकेयस्य माहात्म्यं श्रुत्वा जन्म तथा द्विज ॥

Śatānīka said. Ah, this vow is very difficult. A doubt remains in my heart after hearing of the greatness and birth of Kārttikeya, O Brahmin.

शतानीकः उवाच (śatānīkaḥ uvāca) — Śatānīka said;
अहो व्रतम् महत् कष्टम् (aho vratam mahat kaṣṭam) — ah, vow greatly difficult;
संशयः हृदि वर्तते (saṃśayaḥ hṛdi vartate) — doubt remains in heart;
कार्तिकेयस्य माहात्म्यम् (kārtikeyasya māhātmyam) — Kārttikeya’s greatness;
श्रुत्वा जन्म तथा (śrutvā janma tathā) — hearing also birth

BP 1.40.2

अनेकजनितस्येह कार्त्तिकेयस्य सुव्रत ।
माहात्म्यं सुमहद्विप्र कथमेतद्विभाव्यते ॥

O keeper of good vows, O Brahmin, how is the great majesty of Kārttikeya, born in many ways here, understood.

अनेक-जनितस्य (aneka-janitasya) — of one born from many;
कार्तिकेयस्य (kārtikeyasya) — Kārttikeya;
सुव्रत विप्र (suvrata vipra) — keeper of vows, brāhmaṇa;
माहात्म्यम् सु-महत् (māhātmyam su-mahat) — very great greatness;
कथम् एतत् विभाव्यते (katham etat vibhāvyate) — how is this understood

BP 1.40.3

जातिः श्रेष्ठा भवेद्वीर उत कर्म भवेद्वरम् ।
संशयस्तु महानत्र दृष्ट्वा मे कृत्तिकासुतम् ॥

Is birth superior, O hero, or is action better. This great doubt is in me after seeing the son of the Kṛttikās.

जातिः श्रेष्ठा भवेत् (jātiḥ śreṣṭhā bhavet) — is birth superior;
उत कर्म भवेत् वरम् (uta karma bhavet varam) — or action more excellent;
संशयः महान् (saṃśayaḥ mahān) — great doubt;
दृष्ट्वा मे कृत्तिका-सुतम् (dṛṣṭvā me kṛttikā-sutam) — seeing son of Kṛttikās

BP 1.40.4

एतद्वद विनिश्चित्य न यथा संशयो भवेत् ।
जन्मतः कर्मणश्चैव यज्ज्वायस्तद् ब्रवीहि मे ॥

Determine and tell me this, so that there may be no doubt. Tell me which is greater, birth or action.

एतत् वद विनिश्चित्य (etat vada viniścitya) — state this after deciding;
न यथा संशयः भवेत् (na yathā saṃśayaḥ bhavet) — so doubt does not remain;
जन्मतः कर्मणः च (janmataḥ karmaṇaḥ ca) — of birth and action;
यत् ज्यायः (yat jyāyaḥ) — whichever greater;
तत् ब्रूहि मे (tat brūhi me) — tell me that

BP 1.40.5

सुमन्तुरुवाच
इममर्थं पुरा पृष्टो ब्रह्मा शिष्यैर्मनीषिभिः ।
यदुक्तं तेन तेषां च तत्ते वच्मि निबोध मे ॥

Sumantu said. Brahmā was formerly asked this matter by wise disciples. What he said to them, that I tell you. Hear me.

सुमन्तुः उवाच (sumantuḥ uvāca) — Sumantu said;
इमम् अर्थम् पुरा पृष्टः ब्रह्मा (imam artham purā pṛṣṭaḥ brahmā) — Brahmā formerly asked this matter;
शिष्यैः मनीषिभिः (śiṣyaiḥ manīṣibhiḥ) — by thoughtful disciples;
यत् उक्तम् तेन तेषाम् (yat uktam tena teṣām) — what he told them;
तत् ते वच्मि निबोध (tat te vacmi nibodha) — I tell you, understand

BP 1.40.6

सुरज्येष्ठं सुखासीनमभिगम्य महर्षयः ।
प्रणम्य च महाबाहो विश्वामित्रस्य विप्रताम् ॥

The great sages approached the eldest of the gods, seated at ease, and bowed regarding the Brahminhood of Viśvāmitra, O mighty armed one.

सुर-ज्येष्ठम् सुख-आसीनम् (sura-jyeṣṭham sukha-āsīnam) — eldest god seated at ease;
अभिगम्य महा-ऋषयः (abhigamya mahā-ṛṣayaḥ) — great seers approaching;
प्रणम्य (praṇamya) — bowing;
विश्वामित्रस्य विप्रताम् (viśvāmitrasya vipratām) — Viśvāmitra’s brāhmaṇa status

BP 1.40.7

दृष्ट्वा विस्मयमागत्य कौतूहलसमन्विताः ।
भक्तिं श्रद्धां पुरोधाय प्रणम्यानतकन्धराः ॥

Having seen it, they became amazed and filled with curiosity, placing devotion and faith foremost, they bowed with lowered necks.

दृष्ट्वा विस्मयम् आगत्य (dṛṣṭvā vismayam āgatya) — seeing and becoming amazed;
कौतूहल-समन्विताः (kautūhala-samanvitāḥ) — filled with curiosity;
भक्तिम् श्रद्धाम् पुरस्कृत्य (bhaktim śraddhām puraskṛtya) — placing devotion and faith foremost;
प्रणम्य आनत-कन्धराः (praṇamya ānata-kandharāḥ) — bowing with lowered necks

BP 1.40.8

ऋषय ऊचुः
भो ब्रह्मन्नादकल्पे हि ब्राह्मण्यं ब्रूहि किं भवेत् ।
जात्यध्ययनदेहात्मसंस्काराचारकर्मणाम् ॥

The sages said. O Brahmā, at the beginning of the age, tell us what Brahminhood is among birth, study, body, self, rites, conduct, and action.

ऋषयः ऊचुः (ṛṣayaḥ ūcuḥ) — seers said;
भो ब्रह्मन् (bho brahman) — O Brahmā;
आदि-कल्पे (ādi-kalpe) — at beginning of age;
ब्राह्मण्यम् किम् भवेत् (brāhmaṇyam kim bhavet) — what constitutes brāhmaṇa status;
जाति-अध्ययन-देह-आत्म-संस्कार-आचार-कर्मणाम् (jāti-adhyayana-deha-ātma-saṃskāra-ācāra-karmaṇām) — among birth study body self rites conduct action

BP 1.40.9

बाह्याभ्यन्तरसामान्यविशेषा यदि कृत्रिमाः ।
मनोवाक्कर्मशारीरजातिद्रव्यगुणात्मकाः ॥

Are distinctions, external or internal, general or particular, artificial, consisting of mind, speech, action, body, birth, substance, and quality.

बाह्य-आभ्यन्तर (bāhya-ābhyantara) — outer and inner;
सामान्य-विशेषाः (sāmānya-viśeṣāḥ) — common and particular;
यदि कृत्रिमाः (yadi kṛtrimāḥ) — if artificial;
मनः-वाक्-कर्म-शारीर (manaḥ-vāk-karma-śārīra) — mind speech action body;
जाति-द्रव्य-गुण-आत्मकाः (jāti-dravya-guṇa-ātmakāḥ) — consisting of birth substance quality

BP 1.40.10

सन्त्यक्तव्याः प्रसिद्धा ये जातिभेदविधायिनः ।
वस्तुभूताः परोक्षैर्वा प्रमाणैर्न विनिश्चिताः ॥

The known grounds that establish distinctions by birth should be abandoned, whether they are real things or are not established by imperceptible means of knowledge.

सन्त्यक्तव्याः (santyaktavyāḥ) — should be abandoned;
प्रसिद्धाः ये (prasiddhāḥ ye) — those conventionally known;
जाति-भेद-विधायिनः (jāti-bheda-vidhāyinaḥ) — establishing birth distinctions;
वस्तु-भूताः (vastu-bhūtāḥ) — real entities;
प्रत्यक्षैः वा प्रमाणैः (pratyakṣaiḥ vā pramāṇaiḥ) — by perception or valid means;
न विनिश्चिताः (na viniścitāḥ) — not established

BP 1.40.11

अव्यक्तागमसिद्धश्चेज्जातिभेदविधिर्नृणाम् ।
विकल्पोऽयं न पुष्णाति भवतः शेमुषीबलम् ॥

If the rule establishing distinctions of birth among people rests on unseen tradition, this supposition does not strengthen your understanding.

अव्यक्त-आगम-सिद्धः चेत् (avyakta-āgama-siddhaḥ cet) — if established by unmanifest scripture;
जाति-भेद-विधिः नृणाम् (jāti-bheda-vidhiḥ nṛṇām) — rule of human birth distinctions;
विकल्पः अयम् (vikalpaḥ ayam) — this alternative;
न पुष्णाति (na puṣṇāti) — does not strengthen;
भवतः शेमुषी-बलम् (bhavataḥ śemuṣī-balam) — force of your intelligence

BP 1.40.12

ब्रह्मोवाच
एवमेतन्न सन्देहो यथा यूयं वदन्ति ह ।
शृणुध्वं योगिनो वाक्यं सतर्कं शिष्यश्रेयसे ॥

Brahmā said. It is so, without doubt, as you say. Hear the reasoned words of the yogi, for the welfare of disciples.

ब्रह्मा उवाच (brahmā uvāca) — Brahmā said;
एवम् एतत् न संदेहः (evam etat na saṃdehaḥ) — so it is, no doubt;
यथा यूयम् वदथ (yathā yūyam vadatha) — as you say;
शृणुध्वम् योगिनः (śṛṇudhvam yoginaḥ) — hear, yogins;
वाक्यम् स-तर्कम् (vākyam sa-tarkam) — statement with reasoning;
शिष्य-श्रेयसे (śiṣya-śreyase) — for disciples’ welfare

BP 1.40.13

योगेश्वर उवाच
प्रमाणे हि प्रसिद्धे तु भिन्नार्थविषये यतः ।
स्पष्टयोग्यार्थविषयं प्रत्यक्षं तावदीक्षते ॥

The Lord of Yoga said. When a means of knowledge is established for a distinct object, direct perception sees the object that is clear and fit for perception.

योगेश्वरः उवाच (yogeśvaraḥ uvāca) — Lord of Yoga said;
प्रमाणे प्रसिद्धे (pramāṇe prasiddhe) — when means of knowledge established;
भिन्न-अर्थ-विषये (bhinna-artha-viṣaye) — concerning distinct object;
स्पष्ट-योग्य-अर्थ-विषयम् (spaṣṭa-yogya-artha-viṣayam) — object with clear applicability;
प्रत्यक्षम् तावत् ईक्षते (pratyakṣam tāvat īkṣate) — perception first observes

BP 1.40.14

समान्यातीन्द्रियग्राही सिद्धान्तोऽभ्युपगम्यते ।
स एव भगवानेकं प्रमाणमिति चेन्न तत् ॥

A conclusion that grasps universals and what is beyond the senses is accepted. If you say that Bhagavān alone is the one means of knowledge, that is not so.

सामान्य-अतीन्द्रिय-ग्राही (sāmānya-atīndriya-grāhī) — grasping supersensible universal;
सिद्धान्तः अभ्युपगम्यते (siddhāntaḥ abhyupagamyate) — doctrine is admitted;
सः एव भगवान् एकम् प्रमाणम् इति चेत् (saḥ eva bhagavān ekam pramāṇam iti cet) — if that Lord alone one proof;
न तत् (na tat) — not so

BP 1.40.15

यस्माद्विविधमे तत्ते सङ्कटं भद्र वर्तते ।
वेदस्य पौरुषेयत्वं नित्यजातिसमर्थकम् ॥

For this difficulty stands before you in two ways, good one. The Veda is of human authorship and supports an eternal birth class.

यस्मात् द्वि-विधम् (yasmāt dvi-vidham) — because twofold;
एतत् ते सङ्कटम् (etat te saṅkaṭam) — this difficulty for you;
भद्र (bhadra) — good sir;
वेदस्य पौरुषेयत्वम् (vedasya pauruṣeyatvam) — Veda’s human authorship;
नित्य-जाति-समर्थकम् (nitya-jāti-samarthakam) — supporting eternal birth-class

BP 1.40.16

कार्या विशेषा वेदोक्ता न युक्तमकृतं वचः ।
ताल्वादिकरणानां च व्यापारानन्तरं श्रुतेः ॥

The particulars stated by the Veda are produced, an unproduced utterance is not reasonable, since hearing follows the activity of the palate and other organs.

कार्याः विशेषाः वेद-उक्ताः (kāryāḥ viśeṣāḥ veda-uktāḥ) — particular acts spoken by Veda;
न युक्तम् अकृतम् वचः (na yuktam akṛtam vacaḥ) — unproduced speech not reasonable;
तालु-आदि-करणानाम् व्यापार-अनन्तरम् (tālu-ādi-karaṇānām vyāpāra-anantaram) — following operation of palate and other organs;
श्रुतेः (śruteḥ) — of hearing

BP 1.40.17

व्यापारात्परतस्तस्य प्रागभावविशेषतः ।
तद्भावानुविधायित्वमन्वयव्यतिरकेतः ॥

It appears after that activity, having been absent before. Its existence follows that activity, as shown by agreement and difference.

व्यापारात् परतः (vyāpārāt parataḥ) — subsequent to operation;
तस्य प्राक्-अभाव-विशेषतः (tasya prāk-abhāva-viśeṣataḥ) — because of its prior absence;
तत्-भाव-अनुविधायित्वम् (tat-bhāva-anuvidhāyitvam) — following its presence;
अन्वय-व्यतिरेकतः (anvaya-vyatirekataḥ) — by positive and negative concomitance

BP 1.40.18

तस्माद्धूमाग्निवद्वार्थफलभावोऽवतिष्ठते ।
न च व्यापारवचसोरन्यथानुपपत्तितः ॥

Therefore, as with smoke and fire, the relation of cause and effect remains, for the activity of speech cannot be accounted for otherwise.

तस्मात् धूम-अग्नि-वत् (tasmāt dhūma-agni-vat) — therefore like smoke and fire;
अर्थ-फल-भावः अवतिष्ठते (artha-phala-bhāvaḥ avatiṣṭhate) — cause-result relation stands;
व्यापार-वचसोः (vyāpāra-vacasoḥ) — of operation and speech;
अन्यथा-अनुपपत्तितः (anyathā-anupapattitaḥ) — because otherwise inexplicable

BP 1.40.19

पुरुषानुगता जातिर्ब्राह्मणत्वादिकास्ति चेत् ।
द्विवर्णजातिभेदेन प्रत्याक्षार्थोपलक्षणात् ॥

If a birth class such as Brahminhood inheres in a person, its distinction would be marked by the visible difference between two varnas.

पुरुष-अनुगता जातिः (puruṣa-anugatā jātiḥ) — class inhering in person;
ब्राह्मणत्व-आदिका अस्ति चेत् (brāhmaṇatva-ādikā asti cet) — if brāhmaṇa status and others exist;
द्वि-वर्ण-जाति-भेदेन (dvi-varṇa-jāti-bhedena) — by distinction of two class forms;
प्रत्यक्ष-अर्थ-उपलक्षणात् (pratyakṣa-artha-upalakṣaṇāt) — from perceptual identification

BP 1.40.20

गोवर्गमध्यं च गतो यथाश्वो निर्धार्यते ज्ञैः सुविचक्षणत्वात् ।
मनुष्यभावादविशिष्यमाणस्तद्वद्द्विजः शूद्रगणान्न भिन्नः ॥

Just as the wise, by sharp discernment, distinguish a horse that has entered a herd of cows, so a twice born person, not distinguished by human nature, is not different from a group of Shudras.

गो-वर्ग-मध्यम् गतः अश्वः (go-varga-madhyam gataḥ aśvaḥ) — horse entered midst of cattle herd;
निर्धार्यते ज्ञैः (nirdhāryate jñaiḥ) — distinguished by experts;
सु-विचक्षणत्वात् (su-vicakṣaṇatvāt) — through discernment;
मनुष्य-भावात् अविशिष्यमाणः (manuṣya-bhāvāt aviśiṣyamāṇaḥ) — not differentiated in human nature;
द्विजः शूद्र-गणात् न भिन्नः (dvijaḥ śūdra-gaṇāt na bhinnaḥ) — twice-born not distinct from śūdra group

BP 1.40.21

मनुष्यजातेर्न परो विशेषो यः कल्प्यते सर्वनरानुयायी ।
संस्कारयुक्ता हि क्रियाविशिष्टा द्विजन्मनां शूद्रविवेकहेतुः ॥

There is no distinction beyond the human species that follows all people. Rites and particular actions are indeed the cause of distinguishing the twice born from Shudras.

मनुष्य-जातेः न परः विशेषः (manuṣya-jāteḥ na paraḥ viśeṣaḥ) — no further distinction beyond human species;
सर्व-नर-अनुयायी (sarva-nara-anuyāyī) — accompanying every person;
संस्कार-युक्ता क्रिया-विशिष्टा (saṃskāra-yuktā kriyā-viśiṣṭā) — distinguished action joined with rites;
द्विजन्मानाम् शूद्र-विवेक-हेतुः (dvijanmānām śūdra-viveka-hetuḥ) — basis distinguishing twice-born from śūdras

BP 1.40.22

जीवोऽपि ब्राह्मणः प्रोक्तो यैरतत्त्वज्ञमानवैः ।
प्रभ्रष्टब्राह्मणत्वास्ते जायन्ते विप्रसङ्गतः ॥

Those who do not know reality have even called the soul a Brahmin. They are born fallen from Brahminhood through connection with Brahmins.

जीवः अपि ब्राह्मणः प्रोक्तः (jīvaḥ api brāhmaṇaḥ proktaḥ) — even soul called brāhmaṇa;
यैः अतत्त्व-ज्ञ-मानवैः (yaiḥ atattva-jña-mānavaiḥ) — by people ignorant of reality;
प्रभ्रष्ट-ब्राह्मणत्वाः ते जायन्ते (prabhraṣṭa-brāhmaṇatvāḥ te jāyante) — they become fallen from brāhmaṇa status;
विप्र-सङ्गतः (vipra-saṅgataḥ) — through brāhmaṇa association

BP 1.40.23

जराजन्मान्तरक्लेशदुष्टग्राहकुलाकुलम् ।
नर तिर्यगसच्छूद्र योनिदुःखोर्मिसंकटम् ॥

Troubled by old age, the afflictions of repeated births, and evil family bonds, a man is caught in the peril of waves of suffering in human, animal, ignoble, and Shudra wombs.

जरा-जन्म-अन्तर-क्लेश (jarā-janma-antara-kleśa) — afflictions of age and rebirth;
दुष्ट-ग्राह-कुल-आकुलम् (duṣṭa-grāha-kula-ākulam) — surrounded by corrupt grasping families;
नर-तिर्यक्-असत्-शूद्र-योनि (nara-tiryak-asat-śūdra-yoni) — births as human animal unvirtuous śūdra;
दुःख-ऊर्मि-सङ्कटम् (duḥkha-ūrmi-saṅkaṭam) — distressed by waves of suffering

BP 1.40.24

दौस्थित्यरोगशोकार्तिजनावर्तसमन्वितम् ।
श्वानुशूकरचाण्डालकृमिकूर्मादिकायकम् ॥

Beset by hardship, disease, grief, distress, and the whirlpool of human life, it takes bodies such as dogs, pigs, outcastes, worms, tortoises, and others.

दौःस्थित्य-रोग-शोक-आर्ति (dauḥsthitya-roga-śoka-ārti) — misery disease grief pain;
जन-आवर्त-समन्वितम् (jana-āvarta-samanvitam) — joined with human whirlpool;
श्वन्-शूकर-चण्डाल (śvan-śūkara-caṇḍāla) — dog pig caṇḍāla;
कृमि-कूर्म-आदि-कायकम् (kṛmi-kūrma-ādi-kāyakam) — bodies of worm tortoise and others

BP 1.40.25

संसारसागरं घोरं मग्नः खलु परिप्लवन् ।
भूरिपापभराक्रान्तः स जीवो ब्राह्मणः कथम् ॥

Submerged and tossed about in the terrible ocean of the cycle of existence, overwhelmed by the burden of abundant sin, how can that soul be a Brahmin.

संसार-सागरम् घोरम् (saṃsāra-sāgaram ghoram) — terrible ocean of saṃsāra;
मग्नः परिप्लवन् (magnaḥ pariplavan) — immersed and tossed;
भूरि-पाप-भर-आक्रान्तः (bhūri-pāpa-bhara-ākrāntaḥ) — overcome by much sin;
सः जीवः ब्राह्मणः कथम् (saḥ jīvaḥ brāhmaṇaḥ katham) — how is that soul a brāhmaṇa

BP 1.40.26

ब्रह्मोवाच
सप्तव्याधकथा विप्रा मनुना परिकीर्तिताः ।
तं निशम्य यदुश्रेष्ठ नित्यं जातिपदं त्यजेत् ॥

Brahmā said. O Brahmins, Manu related the story of the seven hunters. Hearing it, O best of the Yadus, one should abandon the fixed status of birth.

ब्रह्मा उवाच (brahmā uvāca) — Brahmā said;
सप्त-व्याध-कथा (sapta-vyādha-kathā) — story of seven hunters;
मनुना परिकीर्तिता (manunā parikīrtitā) — related by Manu;
तम् निशम्य (tam niśamya) — hearing it;
यदु-श्रेष्ठ (yadu-śreṣṭha) — best of Yadus;
नित्यम् जाति-पदम् त्यजेत् (nityam jāti-padam tyajet) — abandon eternal birth-status claim

BP 1.40.27

सप्तव्याधा दशार्णेषु मृगाः कालञ्जरे गिरौ ।
चक्रवाकाः सरिद्द्वीपे हंसाः सरसि मानसे ॥

The seven hunters were in Daśārṇa, deer on Mount Kālañjara, ruddy geese on a river island, and swans in Lake Mānasa.

सप्त व्याधाः दशार्णेषु (sapta vyādhāḥ daśārṇeṣu) — seven hunters in Daśārṇa;
मृगाः कालञ्जरे गिरौ (mṛgāḥ kālañjare girau) — deer on Kālañjara mountain;
चक्रवाकाः सरित्-द्वीपे (cakravākāḥ sarit-dvīpe) — cakravāka birds on river island;
हंसाः सरसि मानसे (haṃsāḥ sarasi mānase) — swans on Mānasa lake

BP 1.40.28

तेऽपि जाताः कुरुक्षेत्रे ब्राह्मणा वेदपारगाः।
प्रस्थिता दीर्घमध्वानं मृयं किमवसीदथ ॥

They too were born in Kurukṣetra as Brahmins, versed in the Veda. Having set out on a long journey, why were you grieved at death.

ते अपि जाताः कुरुक्षेत्रे (te api jātāḥ kurukṣetre) — they also born in Kurukṣetra;
ब्राह्मणाः वेद-पारगाः (brāhmaṇāḥ veda-pāragāḥ) — brāhmaṇas versed in Veda;
प्रस्थिताः दीर्घम् अध्वानम् (prasthitāḥ dīrgham adhvānam) — departed on long road;
मृत्युम् किम् अवसीदथ (mṛtyum kim avasīdatha) — why despair at death

BP 1.40.29

तस्मान्न जीवे ब्राह्मण्यं पश्यामो हि कथञ्चन ॥

Therefore we do not see Brahminhood in the soul in any way.

तस्मात् (tasmāt) — therefore;
न जीवे ब्राह्मण्यम् (na jīve brāhmaṇyam) — no brāhmaṇa status in soul;
पश्यामः कथञ्चन (paśyāmaḥ kathañcana) — we perceive in any way

BP 1.40.30

शस्त्रादिमद्भार्गवजातियुक्तो गजाश्वगोजोष्ट्रखरादिकानाम् ।
शक्त्या कृतो ह्यङ्गजवर्णधर्मैर्भेदः स्फुटं लक्षणतोऽत्र यद्वत् ॥

Among elephants, horses, cows, camels, donkeys, and the like, distinctions are clearly marked by strength, bodily features, color, and characteristics, as among the weapon bearing Bhārgava classes.

शस्त्र-आदि-मत्-भार्गव-जाति-युक्तः (śastra-ādi-mat-bhārgava-jāti-yuktaḥ) — furnished with class designations of Bhārgava and others;
गज-अश्व-गो-ज-उष्ट्र-खर-आदिकानाम् (gaja-aśva-go-ja-uṣṭra-khara-ādikānām) — elephants horses cattle rams camels donkeys;
शक्त्या (śaktyā) — by capacity;
अङ्ग-ज-वर्ण-धर्मैः (aṅga-ja-varṇa-dharmaiḥ) — bodily colour traits;
भेदः स्फुटम् लक्षणतः (bhedaḥ sphuṭam lakṣaṇataḥ) — clear distinction by marks

BP 1.40.31

तदुत्तरान्नैव विकर्तनीया ब्राह्मण्यजातिनृषु नास्ति काचित् ।
नित्याकृतिर्नानुपभेदरूपा यथा हि भेदः परमोऽत्र सिध्येत् ॥
सिताद्यसाधारणतुल्यरूपाः सनातनोऽङ्गेषु न वर्णभेदः ॥

Beyond these, no Brahmin birth class is distinguishable among humans. There is no eternal form or distinct subtype by which a final difference could be established here, nor is there an enduring difference of color in the body, with forms alike except for white and the like.

न एव विकर्तनीया ब्राह्मण्य-जातिः नृषु (na eva vikartanīyā brāhmaṇya-jātiḥ nṛṣu) — no extractable brāhmaṇa class among humans;
नित्य-आकृतिः न (nitya-ākṛtiḥ na) — no eternal form;
अनुप-भेद-रूपा (anupa-bheda-rūpā) — possessing subtype form;
परमः भेदः सिध्येत् (paramaḥ bhedaḥ sidhyet) — ultimate difference established;
सित-आदि-असाधारण-तुल्य-रूपाः (sita-ādi-asādhāraṇa-tulya-rūpāḥ) — like exclusive whiteness and others;
अङ्गेषु न वर्ण-भेदः (aṅgeṣu na varṇa-bhedaḥ) — no bodily colour distinction

BP 1.40.32

ब्राह्मण्यमध्रुवमिदं किल कृत्रिमत्वादकृत्रिमं भवति सामयिकत्वयोगात् ।
साङ्केतिकं सुकृतलेशविशेषलब्धं वाणिज्यभेषजकृतामिव जातिभेदाः ॥

This Brahminhood is unstable because it is artificial and conventional. It is a designation gained through a particular measure of merit, like the class distinctions created by trade and medicine.

ब्राह्मण्यम् अध्रुवम् (brāhmaṇyam adhruvam) — brāhmaṇa status impermanent;
कृत्रिमत्वात् (kṛtrimatvāt) — because constructed;
अकृत्रिमम् न भवति (akṛtrimam na bhavati) — is not unconstructed;
सामयिकत्व-योगात् (sāmayikatva-yogāt) — because conventional;
साङ्केतिकम् (sāṅketikam) — designated;
सुकृत-लेश-विशेष-लब्धम् (sukṛta-leśa-viśeṣa-labdham) — gained by particular measure of merit;
वाणिज्य-भेषज-कृताम् इव जाति-भेदाः (vāṇijya-bheṣaja-kṛtām iva jāti-bhedāḥ) — like distinctions of merchants and physicians

BP 1.40.33

किं ब्राह्मणा ये सुकृतं त्यजन्ति किं क्षत्रिया लोकमपालयन्तः .।
स्वधर्महीना हि तथैव वैश्याः शूद्राः स्वमुख्यक्रियया विहीनाः ॥

Are those who abandon good deeds Brahmins. Are those who do not protect the people Kshatriyas. Likewise, are Vaishyas without their own duty, and Shudras without their principal activity.

किम् ब्राह्मणाः ये सुकृतम् त्यजन्ति (kim brāhmaṇāḥ ye sukṛtam tyajanti) — are those brāhmaṇas who abandon good action;
किम् क्षत्रियाः लोकम् अपालयन्तः (kim kṣatriyāḥ lokam apālayantaḥ) — are those kṣatriyas not protecting world;
स्व-धर्म-हीनाः वैश्याः (sva-dharma-hīnāḥ vaiśyāḥ) — vaiśyas without own duty;
शूद्राः स्व-मुख्य-क्रियया विहीनाः (śūdrāḥ sva-mukhya-kriyayā vihīnāḥ) — śūdras lacking principal action

BP 1.40.34

तस्मान्न गोश्ववत्कश्चिज्जातिभेदोऽस्ति देहिनाम् ।
कार्यशक्तिनिमित्तस्तु सङ्केतः कृत्रिमो भवेत् ॥

Therefore no birth distinction exists among embodied beings as between cows and horses. The designation is artificial, based on capacity for work.

तस्मात् (tasmāt) — therefore;
न गो-अश्व-वत् जाति-भेदः (na go-aśva-vat jāti-bhedaḥ) — no birth distinction like cow and horse;
देहिनाम् (dehinām) — among embodied beings;
कार्य-शक्ति-निमित्तः (kārya-śakti-nimittaḥ) — based on capacity for work;
सङ्केतः कृत्रिमः (saṅketaḥ kṛtrimaḥ) — artificial convention

BP 1.40.35

एवं प्रमाणैः प्रतिषिध्यमानां साङ्केतिकीं याति नरो व्यवस्थाम् ।
स्वकीयसिद्धां स्वमतैर्निषिद्धां न बुध्यते मूढमना वराकः ॥

Thus, though refuted by proofs, a man resorts to a conventional arrangement. The poor dull minded fool does not understand that what he establishes is refuted by his own opinions.

एवम् प्रमाणैः प्रतिषिध्यमानाम् (evam pramāṇaiḥ pratiṣidhyamānām) — thus being refuted by evidence;
साङ्केतिकीम् व्यवस्थाम् याति (sāṅketikīm vyavasthām yāti) — enters conventional arrangement;
स्वकीय-सिद्धाम् (svakīya-siddhām) — established by his own;
स्व-मतैः निषिद्धाम् (sva-mataiḥ niṣiddhām) — rejected by own views;
न बुध्यते मूढ-मनाः (na budhyate mūḍha-manāḥ) — dull-minded does not understand

BP 1.40.36

गोमहिष्यजवाज्युष्ट्रवानेयाविगजाधिपाः ।
प्रेष्यवार्धुषिकाकार्यकरणोद्यतमानसाः ॥

Those intent on keeping cows, buffaloes, goats, horses, camels, donkeys, sheep, and elephants, or on service, usury, and hired work.

गो-महिष-अज-वाजि-उष्ट्र (go-mahiṣa-aja-vāji-uṣṭra) — cattle buffalo goats horses camels;
वानेया-अवि-गज-अधिपाः (vāneyā-avi-gaja-adhipāḥ) — donkey sheep elephant keepers;
प्रेष्य-वार्धुषिक (preṣya-vārdhuṣika) — servants and usurers;
अकार्य-करण-उद्यत-मानसाः (akārya-karaṇa-udyata-mānasāḥ) — intent on improper work

BP 1.40.37

वणिक्कारुक्रियाविष्टा दिव्यास्तेऽपि च ये द्विजाः ।
विनष्टास्ते तु विज्ञेयाः क्रव्यादाश्च कुशीलवाः ॥

Those Brahmins devoted to trade, crafts, and gambling are to be known as fallen, as are flesh eaters and performers.

वणिक्-कारु-क्रिया-आविष्टाः (vaṇik-kāru-kriyā-āviṣṭāḥ) — absorbed in trade and craft;
ये द्विजाः (ye dvijāḥ) — those brāhmaṇas;
विनष्टाः विज्ञेयाः (vinaṣṭāḥ vijñeyāḥ) — known as fallen;
क्रव्यादाः (kravyādāḥ) — flesh eaters;
कुशीलवाः (kuśīlavāḥ) — performers

BP 1.40.38

पलाण्डुलशुनादाश्च मृग्युष्ट्रीक्षीरपायिनः ।
मांससर्वरसक्षीरक्रयविक्रयकारिणः ॥

Eaters of onions and garlic, drinkers of deer milk and she camel milk, and those who buy and sell meat, every kind of juice, and milk.

पलाण्डु-लशुन-अदाः (palāṇḍu-laśuna-adāḥ) — onion and garlic eaters;
मृगी-उष्ट्री-क्षीर-पायिनः (mṛgī-uṣṭrī-kṣīra-pāyinaḥ) — drinkers of deer and camel milk;
मांस-सर्व-रस-क्षीर (māṃsa-sarva-rasa-kṣīra) — meat all liquids milk;
क्रय-विक्रय-कारिणः (kraya-vikraya-kāriṇaḥ) — buyers and sellers

BP 1.40.39

पुनर्भूवृषलीवेश्याचाण्डालस्त्रीनिषेविणः ।
शूद्रान्नरसपुष्टाङ्गाः प्रेतवस्त्रान्नभोजनाः ॥

Those who consort with remarried women, low born women, prostitutes, and outcast women, whose bodies are nourished by the essence of Shudra food, and who eat the food and wear the clothes of the dead.

पुनर्भू-वृषली-वेश्या-चण्डाल-स्त्री-निषेविणः (punarbhū-vṛṣalī-veśyā-caṇḍāla-strī-niṣeviṇaḥ) — frequenters of remarried vṛṣalī prostitute caṇḍāla women;
शूद्र-अन्न-रस-पुष्ट-अङ्गाः (śūdra-anna-rasa-puṣṭa-aṅgāḥ) — bodies nourished by śūdra food;
प्रेत-वस्त्र-अन्न-भोजनाः (preta-vastra-anna-bhojanāḥ) — using dead person’s clothes and food

BP 1.40.40

मृतसूतकलब्धान्नपानाद्यभ्यवहारिणः ।
ब्रह्मदेवपितृभूतमनुष्येषु बहिष्कृताः ॥

Those who consume food, drink, and other things received during the impurity of birth or death are excluded from Brahmā, gods, ancestors, beings, and humans.

मृत-सूतक-लब्ध-अन्न-पान-आदि (mṛta-sūtaka-labdha-anna-pāna-ādi) — food and drink received during death or birth impurity;
अभ्यवहारिणः (abhyavahāriṇaḥ) — consumers;
ब्रह्म-देव-पितृ-भूत-मनुष्येषु (brahma-deva-pitṛ-bhūta-manuṣyeṣu) — in rites for Brahmā gods ancestors beings humans;
बहिष्कृताः (bahiṣkṛtāḥ) — excluded

BP 1.40.41

मात्सर्यमदविद्वेषतृष्णाकामतमोमयाः ।
हीनाचारा हि ये केचिदपरे पिशुना द्विजाः ॥
प्रकारैर्बहुभिः सर्वे ते प्रणश्यन्ति नान्यथा ॥

Those Brahmins who are filled with envy, pride, hostility, craving, desire, and darkness, who follow base conduct or slander others, all perish in many ways, and not otherwise.

मात्सर्य-मद-विद्वेष-तृष्णा-काम-तमो-मयाः (mātsarya-mada-vidveṣa-tṛṣṇā-kāma-tamo-mayāḥ) — filled with envy pride hostility craving desire darkness;
हीन-आचाराः (hīna-ācārāḥ) — of degraded conduct;
पिशुनाः द्विजाः (piśunāḥ dvijāḥ) — slanderous brāhmaṇas;
प्रकारैः बहुभिः प्रणश्यन्ति (prakāraiḥ bahubhiḥ praṇaśyanti) — perish in many ways;
न अन्यथा (na anyathā) — not otherwise

BP 1.40.42

एवं शास्त्रोदितन्यायमार्गभ्रष्टास्तु ये नराः ।
विशिष्टगोत्रसंस्कारकलापसकलात्मकाः ॥

Those men who stray from the path of reasoning set forth by shastra, though possessing the whole collection of distinguished lineage and rites.

शास्त्र-उदित-न्याय-मार्ग-भ्रष्टाः (śāstra-udita-nyāya-mārga-bhraṣṭāḥ) — fallen from scripture-taught reasoning path;
ये नराः (ye narāḥ) — those people;
विशिष्ट-गोत्र-संस्कार-कलाप (viśiṣṭa-gotra-saṃskāra-kalāpa) — collection of distinguished lineage and rites;
सकल-आत्मकाः (sakala-ātmakāḥ) — fully possessing

BP 1.40.43

वेदानध्यापयन्तोऽपि तेऽधीयानाः श्रुतिक्रमात् ।
ब्राह्मणत्वाद्विहीयन्ते दुराचारविघायिनः ॥

Though they teach the Vedas and study them in the received sequence, they lose Brahminhood, being practitioners of bad conduct.

वेदान् अध्यापयन्तः अपि (vedān adhyāpayantaḥ api) — even teaching Vedas;
अधीयानाः श्रुति-क्रमात् (adhīyānāḥ śruti-kramāt) — studying by revealed sequence;
ब्राह्मणत्वात् विहीयन्ते (brāhmaṇatvāt vihīyante) — fall from brāhmaṇa status;
दुराचार-विधायिनः (durācāra-vidhāyinaḥ) — practitioners of bad conduct

BP 1.40.44

तस्मान्न जातिरेकत्र भूतात्मास्त्यनपायिनी ।
नाशित्वादत्र च श्लोकान्मानवाः समधीयते ॥ .

Therefore no birth class is imperishable in any embodied being. Because it perishes, people study verses here.

तस्मात् (tasmāt) — therefore;
न जातिः एकत्र (na jātiḥ ekatra) — no class uniformly;
भूत-आत्मनि अनपायिनी (bhūta-ātmani anapāyinī) — imperishable in embodied self;
नाशित्वात् (nāśitvāt) — because destructible;
अत्र श्लोकान् मानवाः समधीयते (atra ślokān mānavāḥ samadhīyate) — people study verses here

BP 1.40.45

सद्यः पतति मांसेन लाक्षया लवणेन च ।
त्र्यहेण शूद्रो भवति ब्राह्मणः क्षीरविकयी ॥

A Brahmin falls at once through dealing in meat, lac, or salt. A Brahmin selling milk becomes a Shudra in three days.

सद्यः पतति (sadyaḥ patati) — falls immediately;
मांसेन लाक्षया लवणेन च (māṃsena lākṣayā lavaṇena ca) — through meat lac and salt;
त्रि-अहेन शूद्रः भवति (tri-ahena śūdraḥ bhavati) — becomes śūdra in three days;
ब्राह्मणः क्षीर-विक्रयी (brāhmaṇaḥ kṣīra-vikrayī) — brāhmaṇa selling milk

BP 1.40.46

गोरक्षकान्वाणिजिकांस्तथा कारुकुशीलवान् ।
प्रेष्यान्वार्धुषिकांश्चैव शूद्रास्तान्मनुरब्रवीत् ॥

Manu called cattle keepers, merchants, artisans, performers, servants, and usurers Shudras.

गो-रक्षकान् (go-rakṣakān) — cattle keepers;
वाणिजिकान् (vāṇijikān) — merchants;
कारु-कुशीलवान् (kāru-kuśīlavān) — artisans and performers;
प्रेष्यान् (preṣyān) — servants;
वार्धुषिकान् (vārdhuṣikān) — usurers;
शूद्रान् तान् मनुः अब्रवीत् (śūdrān tān manuḥ abravīt) — Manu called them śūdras

BP 1.40.47

शूद्रो ब्राह्मणतामेति ब्राह्मणश्चैति शूद्रताम् ।
क्षत्रियो याति विप्रत्वं विद्याद्वैश्यं तथैव च ॥

A Shudra attains Brahminhood, and a Brahmin attains Shudra status. A Kshatriya attains Brahminhood. Understand the same of a Vaishya.

शूद्रः ब्राह्मणताम् एति (śūdraḥ brāhmaṇatām eti) — śūdra attains brāhmaṇa status;
ब्राह्मणः च एति शूद्रताम् (brāhmaṇaḥ ca eti śūdratām) — brāhmaṇa reaches śūdra status;
क्षत्रियः याति विप्रत्वम् (kṣatriyaḥ yāti vipratvam) — kṣatriya attains brāhmaṇa status;
विद्यात् वैश्यम् तथा एव (vidyāt vaiśyam tathā eva) — know likewise of vaiśya
