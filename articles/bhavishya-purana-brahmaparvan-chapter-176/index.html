Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 176 — Description of Solar Dharma

BP 1.176.1

।। सुमंतुरुवाच ।। पद्मासनः पद्मवर्णः पद्मपत्रनिभेक्षणः ।। कमण्डलुधरः श्रीमान्देवगंधर्वपूजितः ।। १ ।।

Sumantu said: Seated on a lotus, lotus-hued, with eyes like lotus petals, bearing a water-pot, glorious, worshipped by gods and celestial musicians.

sumantuḥ — Sumantu
uvāca — said
padma-āsanaḥ — lotus-seated
padma-varṇaḥ — lotus-colored
padma-patra-nibha-īkṣaṇaḥ — with eyes resembling lotus petals
kamaṇḍalu-dharaḥ — bearing a water-pot
śrīmān — splendid
deva-gandharva-pūjitaḥ — worshipped by gods and Gandharvas

BP 1.176.2

चतुर्मुखो देवपतिः सूर्यार्चनपरः सदा ।। सुरज्येष्ठो महातेजाः सर्व लोकप्रजापतिः ।। ब्रह्मशब्देन दिव्येन ब्रह्मा शांतिं करोतु ते ।। २ ।।

Four-faced, lord of the gods, always intent on worship of the Sun, eldest among the gods, of great splendor, lord of creatures in all worlds; may Brahmā make peace for you by the divine word “Brahma.”

catur-mukhaḥ — four-faced
deva-patiḥ — lord of the gods
sūrya-arcana-paraḥ — devoted to Sun-worship
sadā — always
sura-jyeṣṭhaḥ — foremost of the gods
mahā-tejāḥ — of great splendor
sarva-loka-prajā-patiḥ — lord of the people of all worlds
brahma-śabdena — by the word “Brahma”
divyena — divine
brahmā — Brahmā
śāntim — peace
karotu — may he make
te — for you

BP 1.176.3

पीतांबरधरो देव आत्रेयीदयितः सदा ।। शंखचक्रगदापाणिः श्यामवर्णश्चतुर्भुजः ।। ३ ।।

The god wearing a yellow garment, ever dear to Ātreyī, holding conch, discus, and mace, dark in complexion, four-armed.

pīta-ambara-dharaḥ — wearing yellow garments
devaḥ — god
ātreyī-dayitaḥ — dear to Ātreyī
sadā — always
śaṅkha-cakra-gadā-pāṇiḥ — holding conch, discus, and mace
śyāma-varṇaḥ — dark-colored
catur-bhujaḥ — four-armed

BP 1.176.4

यज्ञदेहः क्रमो देव आत्रेयीदयितः सदा ।। शंखचक्रगदापाणिर्माधवो मधुसूदनः ।। ४ ।।

The sacrifice-bodied god, in sequence, ever dear to Ātreyī, holding conch, discus, and mace—Mādhava, Madhusūdana.

yajña-dehaḥ — whose body is sacrifice
kramaḥ — in sequence
devaḥ — god
ātreyī-dayitaḥ — dear to Ātreyī
sadā — always
śaṅkha-cakra-gadā-pāṇiḥ — holding conch, discus, and mace
mādhavaḥ — Mādhava
madhu-sūdanaḥ — Madhusūdana

BP 1.176.5

सूर्यभक्तान्वितो नित्यं विगतिर्विगतत्रयः ।। सूर्यध्यानपरो नित्यं विष्णुः शांतिं करोतु ते ।। ५ ।।

Ever accompanied by devotees of the Sun, without departure, beyond the three, ever intent on meditation on the Sun, may Viṣṇu make peace for you.

sūrya-bhakta-anvitaḥ — accompanied by Sun-devotees
nityam — always
vigatiḥ — vigati
vigata-trayaḥ — free from three
sūrya-dhyāna-paraḥ — devoted to meditation on the Sun
nityam — always
viṣṇuḥ — Viṣṇu
śāntim — peace
karotu — may he make
te — for you

BP 1.176.6

शशिकुंदेंदुसंकाशो विश्रुताभरणैरिह ।। चतुर्भुजो महातेजाः पुष्पार्धकृतशेखरः ।। ६ ।।

Resembling the moon, jasmine, and moon, adorned here with renowned ornaments, four-armed, of great splendor, with a crest made of half a flower.

śaśi — moon
kunda — jasmine
indu-saṃkāśaḥ — resembling the moon
viśruta-ābharaṇaiḥ — with renowned ornaments
iha — here
catur-bhujaḥ — four-armed
mahā-tejāḥ — of great splendor
puṣpa-ardha-kṛta-śekharaḥ — with a crest made of half a flower

BP 1.176.7

चतुर्मुखो भस्मधरः स्मशाननिलयः सदा ।। गोत्रारिर्विश्वनिलयस्तथा च क्रतुदूषणः ।। ७ ।।

Four-faced, bearing ash, always dwelling in the cremation ground, enemy of clans, abode of the universe, and destroyer of sacrifice.

catur-mukhaḥ — four-faced
bhasma-dharaḥ — bearing ash
śmaśāna-nilayaḥ — dwelling in the cremation ground
sadā — always
gotra-ariḥ — enemy of mountains
viśva-nilayaḥ — abode of the universe
tathā — and
kratu-dūṣaṇaḥ — destroyer of sacrifice

BP 1.176.8

वरो वरेण्यो वरदो देवदेवो महेश्वरः ।। आदित्यदेहसंभूतः स ते शांतिं करोतु वै ।। ८ ।।

Excellent, most worthy, giver of boons, god of gods, Maheśvara, born from the body of the Sun—may he indeed bring you peace.

vareṇyaḥ — most choice
vara-daḥ — giver of boons
deva-devaḥ — god of gods
maheśvaraḥ — Maheśvara
āditya-deha-sambhūtaḥ — born from the body of Āditya
saḥ — he
te — for you
śāntim — peace
karotu — may he make
vai — indeed
