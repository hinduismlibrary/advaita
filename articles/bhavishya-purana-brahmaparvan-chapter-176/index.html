Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 176 — Description of Solar Dharma

BP 1.176.1

।। सुमंतुरुवाच ।। पद्मासनः पद्मवर्णः पद्मपत्रनिभेक्षणः ।। कमण्डलुधरः श्रीमान्देवगंधर्वपूजितः ।। १ ।।

Sumantu said: Lotus-seated, lotus-colored, with eyes resembling lotus petals, bearing a water-pot, splendid, worshipped by gods and Gandharvas.

Word-for-word
sumantuḥ — Sumantu
uvāca — said
padma-āsanaḥ — lotus-seated
padma-varṇaḥ — lotus-colored
padma-patra-nibha-īkṣaṇaḥ — with eyes resembling lotus petals
kamaṇḍalu-dharaḥ — bearing a water-pot
śrīmān — splendid
deva-gandharva-pūjitaḥ — worshipped by gods and Gandharvas
Transliteration
| | sumaṃturuvāca | | padmāsanaḥ padmavarṇaḥ padmapatranibhekṣaṇaḥ | | kamaṇḍaludharaḥ śrīmāndevagaṃdharvapūjitaḥ | | १ | |

BP 1.176.2

चतुर्मुखो देवपतिः सूर्यार्चनपरः सदा ।। सुरज्येष्ठो महातेजाः सर्व लोकप्रजापतिः ।। ब्रह्मशब्देन दिव्येन ब्रह्मा शांतिं करोतु ते ।। २ ।।

Four-faced, lord of the gods, always devoted to Sun-worship, foremost of the gods, of great splendor, lord of all people: may Brahmā, by the divine word “Brahma,” make peace for you.

Word-for-word
catur-mukhaḥ — four-faced
deva-patiḥ — lord of the gods
sūrya-arcana-paraḥ — devoted to Sun-worship
sadā — always
sura-jyeṣṭhaḥ — foremost of the gods
mahā-tejāḥ — of great splendor
sarva-loka-prajā-patiḥ — lord of the people of all worlds
brahma-śabdena — by the word “Brahma”
divyena — divine
brahmā — Brahmā
śāntim — peace
karotu — may he make
te — for you
Transliteration
caturmukho devapatiḥ sūryārcanaparaḥ sadā | | surajyeṣṭho mahātejāḥ sarva lokaprajāpatiḥ | | brahmaśabdena divyena brahmā śāṃtiṃ karotu te | | २ | |

BP 1.176.3

पीतांबरधरो देव आत्रेयीदयितः सदा ।। शंखचक्रगदापाणिः श्यामवर्णश्चतुर्भुजः ।। ३ ।।

The god wearing yellow garments, always dear to Ātreyī, holding conch, discus, and mace, dark-colored, four-armed.

śyāma-varṇaḥ — dark-colored
catur-bhujaḥ — four-armed
Transliteration
pītāṃbaradharo deva ātreyīdayitaḥ sadā | | śaṃkhacakragadāpāṇiḥ śyāmavarṇaścaturbhujaḥ | | ३ | |

BP 1.176.4

यज्ञदेहः क्रमो देव आत्रेयीदयितः सदा ।। शंखचक्रगदापाणिर्माधवो मधुसूदनः ।। ४ ।।

The god whose body is sacrifice, in sequence, always dear to Ātreyī, holding conch, discus, and mace: Mādhava, Madhusūdana.

BP 1.176.5

सूर्यभक्तान्वितो नित्यं विगतिर्विगतत्रयः ।। सूर्यध्यानपरो नित्यं विष्णुः शांतिं करोतु ते ।। ५ ।।

Always accompanied by Sun-devotees, vigati, free from three, always devoted to meditation on the Sun, may Viṣṇu make peace for you.

Transliteration

BP 1.176.6

शशिकुंदेंदुसंकाशो विश्रुताभरणैरिह ।। चतुर्भुजो महातेजाः पुष्पार्धकृतशेखरः ।। ६ ।।

Resembling the moon, jasmine, and the moon, here with renowned ornaments, four-armed, of great splendor, with a crest made of half a flower.

BP 1.176.7

चतुर्मुखो भस्मधरः स्मशाननिलयः सदा ।। गोत्रारिर्विश्वनिलयस्तथा च क्रतुदूषणः ।। ७ ।।

Four-faced, bearing ash, always dwelling in the cremation ground, enemy of mountains, abode of the universe, and destroyer of sacrifice.

BP 1.176.8

वरो वरेण्यो वरदो देवदेवो महेश्वरः ।। आदित्यदेहसंभूतः स ते शांतिं करोतु वै ।। ८ ।।
