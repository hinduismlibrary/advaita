Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 173 — Description of Solar Dharma

BP 1.173.1

शतानीक उवाच ।। पुनर्मे ब्रूहि विप्रेन्द्र सौरं धर्ममनुत्तमम् ।। समासात्कथितं ब्रह्मन्विस्तरेण प्रकीर्तय ।। १ ।।

Śatānīka said: Tell me again, O foremost of Brahmins, the unsurpassed Solar Dharma; what was told in brief, O Brahman, proclaim at length.

BP 1.173.2

साधुसाधु महाबाहो साधु पृष्टोऽस्मि भारत ।। त्वत्समो नास्ति लोकेऽस्मिन्सौरः पार्थिवसत्तम ।। २ ।।

Well, well, O mighty-armed; well have I been asked, O Bhārata. There is no Sun-devotee equal to you in this world, O best of kings.

BP 1.173.3

कीर्तयाम्यथ तं पुण्यं संवादं पापनाशनम् ।। गरुडारुणयो राजन्पुरावृत्तं नराधिप ।। ३ ।।

Now I shall recount that meritorious, sin-destroying dialogue between Garuḍa and Aruṇa, which took place formerly, O king, ruler of men.

BP 1.173.4

सुखासीनं पुरा राजन्नरुणं सूर्यसारथिम् ।। उपगम्य महाबाहो गरुडो वाक्यमव्रवीत् ।। ४ ।।

Long ago, O king, Garuḍa approached Aruṇa, the Sun’s charioteer, seated at ease, and spoke these words, O mighty-armed.

BP 1.173.5

धर्माणामुत्तमं धर्मं सर्वपापप्रणाशनम् ।। सौरधर्मं खगश्रेष्ठ ब्रूहि मे कृत्स्नशोनघ ।। ५ ।।

Tell me fully, O best of birds, O sinless one, the highest dharma among dharmas, the Solar Dharma, destroyer of all sins.

BP 1.173.6

अरुण उवाच ।। साधु वत्स महात्मासि धन्यस्त्वं पापवर्जितः ।। श्रोतुकामोऽसि यत्पुत्र सौरधर्ममनुत्तमम् ।। ६ ।।

Aruṇa said: Well, my child. You are great-souled, fortunate, and free from sin, since you desire to hear the unsurpassed Solar Dharma, O son.

BP 1.173.7

शृणु त्वं कीर्तयाम्येष सुखोपायं महत्फलम् ।। परमं सर्वधर्माणां सौरधर्ममनुत्तमम् ।। ७ ।।

Hear; I shall proclaim this easy means, of great fruit, the highest among all dharmas, the unsurpassed Solar Dharma.

BP 1.173.8

अज्ञानार्णवमग्नानां सर्वेषां प्राणिनामयम् ।। सौरधर्मो ह्ययं श्रीमान्परतीरप्रदो यतः ।। ८ ।।

This splendid Solar Dharma is for all living beings sunk in the ocean of ignorance, since it gives the farther shore.

BP 1.173.9

ये स्मरंति रविं भक्त्या कीर्तयंति च ये खग ।। पूजयन्ति च ये नित्यं ते गताः परमं पदम् ।। ९ ।।

Those who remember Ravi with devotion, praise him, O bird, and worship him always have reached the highest state.

BP 1.173.10

आत्मद्रोहः कृतस्तेन जातेनेह खगाधिप ।। नार्चितो येन देवेशः सहस्रकिरणो रविः ।। १० ।।

O lord of birds, one born here who has not worshipped Ravi, Lord of the gods, thousand-rayed, has committed self-betrayal.

BP 1.173.11

सुचिरं संभ्रमत्यस्मिन्दुःखदे च भवार्णवे ।। जराभूतमहाग्राहे तृष्णावेलाकुलापरे ।। ११ ।।

For a long time he wanders in this ocean of becoming that gives sorrow, where old age is a great crocodile and the farther shore is crowded with waves of craving.

BP 1.173.12

मानुष्यं दुर्लभं प्राप्य येऽर्चयंति दिवाकरम् ।। तेषां हि सफलं जन्म कृतार्थास्ते नरोत्तमाः ।। १२ ।।

Having attained the rare human state, those who worship the Day-maker have a fruitful birth; they are fulfilled, O best of men.

BP 1.173.13

सूर्यभक्तिपरा ये च ये च तद्गतमानसाः ।। ये स्मरंति सदा सूर्यं न ते दुःखस्य भागिनः ।। १३ ।।

Those intent on devotion to the Sun, with minds fixed on him, who always remember the Sun, do not share in sorrow.

BP 1.173.14

विविधानि मनोज्ञानि विविधाभरणाः स्त्रियः ।। धनं वा दृष्टपर्यंतं सूर्यपूजाविधेः फलम् ।। १४ ।।

Varied pleasing things, women bearing varied ornaments, or wealth as far as the eye can see—these are fruits of the rite of Sun-worship.

BP 1.173.15

ये वांछन्ति महाभोगान्राज्यं वा त्रिदशा लये ।। सौभाग्यं कांतिमतुलां भोगं त्यागं यशः श्रियम् ।। १५ ।।

Those who desire great enjoyments, a kingdom in the realm of the thirty gods, good fortune, incomparable radiance, enjoyment, renunciation, fame, and prosperity;

BP 1.173.16

सौन्दर्यं जगतः ख्यातिः कीर्तिर्धर्मादयः स्मृताः ।। फलान्येतानि वै पुत्र सूर्य भक्तिविधेर्बुध ।। १६ ।।

Beauty, fame in the world, renown, righteousness, and the rest are remembered as these fruits of the rite of devotion to the Sun, O wise son.

BP 1.173.17

तस्मात्संपूजयेत्सूर्यं सर्वदेवगणार्चितम् ।। दुर्लभा भास्करे भक्तिर्दुर्लभं च तदर्चनम् ।। १७ ।।

Therefore one should thoroughly worship the Sun, honored by all hosts of gods. Devotion to Bhāskara is difficult to obtain, and worship of him is difficult to obtain.

BP 1.173.18

दानं च दुर्लभं तस्मै तद्धोमश्च सुदुर्लभः ।। दुर्लभं तस्य विज्ञानं तदभ्यासोऽपि दुर्लभः ।। १८ ।।

Giving to him is difficult to obtain, and his fire-offering is very difficult to obtain; knowledge of him is difficult to obtain, and its practice is also difficult to obtain.

BP 1.173.19

सुदुर्लभतरं ज्ञेयं तदाराधनमुत्तमम् ।। लोभस्तेषां मनुष्याणां ये रविं शरणं गताः ।। १९ ।।

That highest worship is known to be exceedingly difficult; the longing of those people who have taken refuge in Ravi.

BP 1.173.20

येषामिहेश्वरे भानौ नित्यं सूर्ये गतं मनः ।। नमस्कारादिसंयुक्तं रविरित्यक्षरद्वयम् ।। २० ।।

Those whose minds are always set on the Sun, the Lord Bhānu; the two syllables “Ravi,” joined with salutation and the rest,

BP 1.173.21

जिह्वाग्रे वर्तते यस्य सकलं तस्य जीवितम् ।। य एवं पूजयेद्भानुं श्रद्धया परयान्वितः ।। मुच्यते सर्वपापेभ्यः स नरो नात्र संशयः ।। २१ ।।

remain on whose tongue-tip—that is his whole life. Whoever worships Bhānu thus, endowed with supreme faith, is freed from all sins; of this there is no doubt.

BP 1.173.22

डाकिन्यो विविधाकारा राक्षसाः सपिशाचकाः ।। न तस्य पीडां कुर्वंति तथान्याश्च विभीषणाः ।। २२ ।।

Female spirits of many forms, demons together with ghosts, and other fearsome beings do not cause him affliction.

BP 1.173.23

शत्रवो नाशमायांति संग्रामे जयमाप्नुयात् ।। न रोगैः पीड्यते वीर आपदो न स्पृशंति तम् ।। २३ ।।

Enemies meet destruction; one attains victory in battle; the hero is not afflicted by diseases, and dangers do not touch him.

BP 1.173.24

धनमायुर्यशो विद्या प्रभावो ह्यतुलं तथा ।। शुभेनोपचयं यांति नित्यं पूर्णमनोरथाः ।।।२४ ।। ।।

Wealth, life, fame, learning, and incomparable power grow through auspiciousness; one’s desires are always fulfilled.
