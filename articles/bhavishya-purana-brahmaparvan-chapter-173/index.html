BP 1.173.24

धनमायुर्यशो विद्या प्रभावो ह्यतुलं तथा ।। शुभेनोपचयं यांति नित्यं पूर्णमनोरथाः ।।।२४ ।। ।।

Wealth, life, fame, knowledge, and incomparable power likewise go to increase with auspiciousness; one’s desires are always fulfilled.
