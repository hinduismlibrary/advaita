VP 3.15.1

और्व उवाच— ब्राह्मणान् भोजयेच् छ्राद्धे यद् गुणांस् तान् निबोध मे

O King, learn from me the qualifications of the Brāhmaṇas who are to be fed at a śrāddha.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
ब्राह्मणान् भोजयेत् श्राद्धे (brāhmaṇān bhojayet śrāddhe) — one should feed Brāhmaṇas at a śrāddha
यद्-गुणान् तान् निबोध मे (yad-guṇān tān nibodha me) — learn from me their qualifications
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

brāhmaṇān bhojayec chrāddhe yad guṇāṃs tān nibodha me

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

इति कृत्यान् विधीनत्र श्राद्धीयान् ब्राह्मणानपि । भोक्तुः कर्तृश्च वर्ज्यानि प्राह पञ्चदशे मुनिः । अथ पार्वणश्राद्धप्रयोगान् वक्ष्यन् प्रथमं श्राद्धीयान् ब्राह्मणानाह—ब्राह्मणान् इति त्रिभिः । यद्-गुणान् यैर् गुणैर् युक्तान् । द्वितीयकठिकस्थास्त्रयोऽनुवाकास्त्रिणाचिकेताः, तदध्यायी तदनुष्ठाता च त्रिणाचिकेतः । मधुवाता इति त्र्यृचाध्यायी तद्वृतश्च त्रिमधुः । ब्राह्मणेन्मान् इत्याद्यनुवाकत्रयाध्यायी तद्वृतश्च त्रिसुपर्णः । षट् अङ्गानि यस्य तं वेदमधीते वेत्ति वा षडङ्गवित्

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The sage describes here the prescribed acts and procedures, the Brāhmaṇas fit for śrāddha, and what the eater and performer must avoid. Now, while about to explain the procedure of the fortnightly śrāddha, he first describes the Brāhmaṇas fit for it, beginning with “Brāhmaṇas,” in three verses. “Their qualifications” means those endowed with these qualities. Triṇāciketa means one who has studied and practices the three Anuvākas of the second section of the Kāṭhaka. Trimadhu means one who has studied and practices the three ṛcs beginning “Madhuvātā”; alternatively, one devoted to the Madhu-vidyā taught in the Chāndogya. Trisuparṇa means one who has studied and practices the three Anuvākas beginning “Brahmametu mām”; alternatively, the three ṛcs. Ṣaḍaṅgavit is one who studies or knows the Veda together with its six limbs.

VP 3.15.2

त्रिणाचिकेतस् त्रिमधुस् त्रिसुपर्णः षडङ्गवित्।
वेदविच् छ्रोत्रियो योगी तथा वै ज्येष्ठ-सामगः

They are one who has mastered and practiced the Tri-Nāciketa teaching, one who has mastered and practiced the Tri-Madhu teaching, one versed in the Tri-Suparṇa passages, one who knows the six Vedāṅgas, a knower of the Veda, a Śrotriya, a yogin, and a singer of the Jyeṣṭha-sāman.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
त्रिणाचिकेतः (triṇāciketaḥ) — one versed in and practicing the three Nāciketa passages
त्रिमधुः (trimadhuḥ) — one versed in and practicing the Madhu teachings
त्रिसुपर्णः (trisuparṇaḥ) — one versed in and practicing the Tri-suparṇa passages
षड्-अङ्ग-वित् (ṣaḍ-aṅga-vit) — one who knows the six Vedāṅgas
वेदवित् श्रोत्रियः योगी ज्येष्ठ-सामगः (vedavit śrotriyaḥ yogī jyeṣṭha-sāmagaḥ) — a knower of the Veda, a Śrotriya, a yogin, and a singer of the Jyeṣṭha-sāman
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

triṇāciketas trimadhus trisuparṇaḥ ṣaḍaṅgavit|vedavic chrotriyo yogī tathā vai jyeṣṭha-sāmagaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

इति कृत्यान् विधीनत्र श्राद्धीयान् ब्राह्मणानपि । भोक्तुः कर्तृश्च वर्ज्यानि प्राह पञ्चदशे मुनिः । अथ पार्वणश्राद्धप्रयोगान् वक्ष्यन् प्रथमं श्राद्धीयान् ब्राह्मणानाह—ब्राह्मणान् इति त्रिभिः । यद्-गुणान् यैर् गुणैर् युक्तान् । द्वितीयकठिकस्थास्त्रयोऽनुवाकास्त्रिणाचिकेताः, तदध्यायी तदनुष्ठाता च त्रिणाचिकेतः । मधुवाता इति त्र्यृचाध्यायी तद्वृतश्च त्रिमधुः । ब्राह्मणेन्मान् इत्याद्यनुवाकत्रयाध्यायी तद्वृतश्च त्रिसुपर्णः । षट् अङ्गानि यस्य तं वेदमधीते वेत्ति वा षडङ्गवित्

वेदवित् वेदार्थविचारकः । श्रोत्रियस्तदर्थानुष्ठाता । योगी योगाभ्यासी । मूर्धानं दिव इत्याद्यृग्विशेषगीतं ज्येष्ठसाम तद्गाता ज्येष्ठसामगः । आहवनीयादित्रयः सज्यावसथ्यौ च द्वौ एते पञ्चाग्नयः, तेष्वभिरतस्तदुपासकः । यद्वा वेदान्तोक्तद्यु-पर्जन्य-पृथिवी-पुरुष-योषिद्रूपपञ्चाग्निविधोपासकः

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The sage describes here the prescribed acts and procedures, the Brāhmaṇas fit for śrāddha, and what the eater and performer must avoid. Now, while about to explain the procedure of the fortnightly śrāddha, he first describes the Brāhmaṇas fit for it, beginning with “Brāhmaṇas,” in three verses. “Their qualifications” means those endowed with these qualities. Triṇāciketa means one who has studied and practices the three Anuvākas of the second section of the Kāṭhaka. Trimadhu means one who has studied and practices the three ṛcs beginning “Madhuvātā”; alternatively, one devoted to the Madhu-vidyā taught in the Chāndogya. Trisuparṇa means one who has studied and practices the three Anuvākas beginning “Brahmametu mām”; alternatively, the three ṛcs. Ṣaḍaṅgavit is one who studies or knows the Veda together with its six limbs. Vedavit is an investigator of the meaning of the Veda; a Śrotriya practices that meaning. A yogin practices yoga. The Jyeṣṭha-sāman is the special ṛg-verse beginning “Mūrdhānaṃ diva” sung as a sāman; its singer is a Jyeṣṭha-sāmaga. The three fires beginning with Āhavanīya together with the two fires Sajya and Āvasathya are the five fires; one devoted to them is their worshipper. Alternatively, he is one who practices the five-fire meditation taught in the Vedānta, consisting of heaven, rain, earth, man, and woman.

VP 3.15.3

ऋत्विक्-स्वस्रेय-दौहित्र-जामातृ-श्वशुरास् तथा।
मातुलोऽथ तपो-निष्ठः पञ्चाग्न्यभिर् अतस् तथा।
शिष्याः सम्बन्धिनश् चैव माता-पितृ-रतश् च यः

There are also the ṛtvij, the sister’s son, the daughter’s son, the son-in-law, the father-in-law, the maternal uncle, one established in austerity, one devoted to the five fires, a disciple, a relative, and one devoted to his mother and father.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
ऋत्विक्-स्वस्त्रीय-दौहित्र-जामातृ-श्वशुराः तथा (ṛtvik-svastrīya-dauhitra-jāmātṛ-śvaśurāḥ tathā) — likewise a ṛtvik, sister’s son, daughter’s son, son-in-law, and father-in-law
मातुलः अथ तपो-निष्ठः (mātulaḥ atha tapo-niṣṭhaḥ) — a maternal uncle, or one established in austerity
पञ्च-अग्नि-अभिरतः तथा (pañca-agni-abhirataḥ tathā) — likewise one devoted to the five fires
शिष्याः सम्बन्धिनः च एव (śiṣyāḥ sambandhinaḥ ca eva) — disciples and relatives also
माता-पितृ-परः च यः (mātā-pitṛ-paraḥ ca yaḥ) — and one who is devoted to his mother and father
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ṛtvik-svasreya-dauhitra-jāmātṛ-śvaśurās tathā|mātulo'tha tapo-niṣṭhaḥ pañcāgnyabhir atas tathā|śiṣyāḥ sambandhinaś caiva mātā-pitṛ-rataś ca yaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

वेदवित् वेदार्थविचारकः । श्रोत्रियस्तदर्थानुष्ठाता । योगी योगाभ्यासी । मूर्धानं दिव इत्याद्यृग्विशेषगीतं ज्येष्ठसाम तद्गाता ज्येष्ठसामगः । आहवनीयादित्रयः सज्यावसथ्यौ च द्वौ एते पञ्चाग्नयः, तेष्वभिरतस्तदुपासकः । यद्वा वेदान्तोक्तद्यु-पर्जन्य-पृथिवी-पुरुष-योषिद्रूपपञ्चाग्निविधोपासकः

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Vedavit is one who investigates the meaning of the Veda; a Śrotriya practices that meaning. A yogin practices yoga. The special ṛg-verse beginning “Mūrdhānaṃ diva,” sung as a sāman, is the Jyeṣṭha-sāman, and its singer is a Jyeṣṭha-sāmaga. The three fires beginning with Āhavanīya together with Sajya and Āvasathya are the five fires; one devoted to them is their worshipper. Alternatively, he is one who practices the five-fire meditation taught in the Vedānta, consisting of heaven, rain, earth, man, and woman.

VP 3.15.4

एतान् नियोजयेच् छ्राद्धे पूर्वोक्तान् प्रथमे नृप।
ब्राह्मणान् पितृ-तुष्ट्य्-अर्थम् अनुकल्पेष्व् अनन्तरान्

O King, invite these previously mentioned Brāhmaṇas first for the satisfaction of the ancestors; thereafter invite the others as alternatives.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
एतान् नियोजयेत् श्राद्धे पूर्व-उक्तान् प्रथमे नृप (etān niyojayet śrāddhe pūrva-uktān prathame nṛpa) — O king, he should appoint these previously mentioned persons first at the śrāddha
ब्राह्मणान् पितृ-तुष्टि-अर्थम् (brāhmaṇān pitṛ-tuṣṭi-artham) — the Brāhmaṇas for the satisfaction of the ancestors
अनुकल्पेषु अनन्तरान् (anukalpeṣu anantarān) — and thereafter the others as alternatives
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

etān niyojayec chrāddhe pūrvoktān prathame nṛpa|brāhmaṇān pitṛ-tuṣṭy-artham anukalpeṣv anantarān

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

एतेषु मुख्यानुकल्पभेदम् आह—एतान् इति । एतान् प्रथमश्लोकोक्तान् ज्येष्ठसामगान् । प्रथमं मुख्यकल्पे निमन्त्रयेत् । अनन्तरान् ऋगादीन् अनुकल्पेषु

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

He explains the distinction between the principal and alternative choices: the persons stated in the first verse are the Jyeṣṭha-sāmaga and the others. Invite them first as the principal choice, and the Ṛg-vedins and others thereafter as alternatives.

VP 3.15.5

मित्र-ध्रुक्कुनखी क्लीबः श्यावदन्तस् तथा द्विजः।
कन्या-दूषयिता वह्नि-वेदोज्झः सोम-विक्रयी

A betrayer of a friend, one with malformed nails, an impotent man, one with dark teeth, one who violates a maiden, one who abandons the sacred fire or Veda without cause, and one who sells Soma are not fit for the śrāddha invitation.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
मित्र-ध्रुक् कुनखी कीवः श्याव-दन्तः तथा द्विजः (mitra-dhruk kunakhī kīvaḥ śyāva-dantaḥ tathā dvijaḥ) — a Brāhmaṇa who betrays a friend, has malformed nails, is impotent, or has dark teeth
कन्या-दूषयिता (kanyā-dūṣayitā) — one who violates a maiden
वह्नि-वेद-उज्झः (vahni-veda-ujjhaḥ) — one who abandons the fire or Veda
सोम-विक्रयी (soma-vikrayī) — one who sells Soma
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

mitra-dhrukkunakhī klībaḥ śyāvadantas tathā dvijaḥ|kanyā-dūṣayitā vahni-vedojjhaḥ soma-vikrayī

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

एतेषामसम्भवे निषिद्धग्रहणार्थं निषिद्धानाह—मित्रघ्रुग् इति सार्द्धैस्त्रिभिः । कुनखी निसर्गतः कुत्सितनखः । श्यावदन्तः सभावतः कृष्णदन्तः । अग्निवेदोज्झः अधिकारी शक्तश्च सन् अग्निहोत्रवेदाभ्यासत्यागी । सोमं सोमलतां विक्रीणीते स तथा

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

In the absence of these, he states the prohibited persons in order to prohibit their selection. A kunakhī is one whose nails are naturally deformed; one with dark teeth has naturally black teeth. A person who is capable but abandons the Agnihotra and Vedic study is a fire-and-Veda abandoner. One who sells the Soma creeper is a Soma seller.

VP 3.15.6

अभिशस्तस् तथा स्तेनः पिशुनो ग्राम-याजकः।
भृतकाध्यापकस् तद्वद् भृतकाध्यापितश् च यः

One accused of a grave sin, a thief, a backbiter, a village priest, a teacher who teaches for wages, and one who has been taught for wages are also unfit.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
अभिशस्तः तथा स्तेनः (abhiśastaḥ tathā stenaḥ) — one accused of a grave sin, and a thief
पिशुनः ग्राम-याजकः (piśunaḥ grāma-yājakaḥ) — a backbiter and a village priest
भृतक-अध्यापकः (bhṛtaka-adhyāpakaḥ) — a teacher who teaches for wages
तद्वत् भृतक-अध्यापितः च यः (tadvat bhṛtaka-adhyāpitaḥ ca yaḥ) — and one who has been taught for wages
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

abhiśastas tathā stenaḥ piśuno grāma-yājakaḥ|bhṛtakādhyāpakas tadvad bhṛtakādhyāpitaś ca yaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अभिशस्तः सत्येनासत्येन महापातकित्वेनाभियुक्तः स्तेनश्चौरः । पिशुनः निभृते परदोषवक्ता । ग्रामयाजकः साधारणानां याजयिता, ग्रामार्थपुष्टो वा । भृतकाध्यापकः वेतनेनाध्यापितश्च

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Abhiśasta means one accused of a great sin, whether truly or falsely; stena means a thief. Piśuna is one who secretly speaks of another’s faults. A grāmayājaka is one who officiates for ordinary people, or one maintained by a village. A hired teacher teaches for wages, and a hired student is taught for wages.

VP 3.15.7

पर-पूर्वापतिश् चैव माता-पित्रोस् तथोज्झकः।
वृषली-सूति-पोष्टा च वृसली-पतिर् एव च

One who marries a woman previously married to another, one who abandons his mother and father, one who raises the child of a Śūdra woman, and one who is married to a Śūdra woman are unfit.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
पर-पूर्व-अपतिः च एव (para-pūrva-apatiḥ ca eva) — one who marries a woman previously married to another
माता-पित्रोः तथा उज्झकः (mātā-pitroḥ tathā ujjhakaḥ) — and one who abandons his mother and father
वृषली-सूति-पोष्टा च (vṛṣalī-sūti-poṣṭā ca) — one who raises the child of a Śūdra woman
वृषली-पतिः एव च (vṛṣalī-patiḥ eva ca) — and one married to a Śūdra woman
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

para-pūrvāpatiś caiva mātā-pitros tathojjhakaḥ|vṛṣalī-sūti-poṣṭā ca vṛsalī-patir eva ca

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

परपूर्वा परस्मै या दत्ता तस्या अपरः पतिः । मातापित्रोरपातित्यादावपि उज्झकः उपेक्षकः । वृषलीसूतिपोष्टा शूद्रापत्यपोषकः । देवलकः— देवार्चनपरो विप्रो वित्तार्थी वत्सरत्रयम् । असौ देवलकी नाम हव्यकव्येषु गर्हितः ॥ इत्युक्तः । केतनं निमन्त्रणम्

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

A para-pūrvā is a woman previously given to another man; her later husband is a para-pūrvāpati. One who abandons or neglects his parents is a parent-abandoner. A vṛṣalī-sūti-poṣṭa raises the child of a Śūdra woman. A vṛṣalī-pati is married to a Śūdra woman.

VP 3.15.8

तथा देवलकश् चैव श्राद्धे नार्हति केतनम्

Likewise, a Devalaka is not fit for invitation at a śrāddha.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
तथा देवलकः च एव (tathā devalakaḥ ca eva) — likewise a Devalaka
श्राद्धे न अर्हन्ति केतनम् (śrāddhe na arhanti ketanam) — they are not fit to be invited to a śrāddha
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

दौहित्रो दुहितुः सुतः । कुतपोऽष्टमो मुहूर्तः । दौहित्रम् इति पाठे दौहित्रं घृतविशेषः । अमावास्यागते सोमे या च खादति गौरतृणम् । दौहित्री सा मता तस्या घृतं दौहित्रमुच्यते । इति । दौहित्रं खड्गपात्रम् इति केचित् । केचित्तु कुतपमपि छागलोमर्जं कम्बलमाहुः । रजतस्य कथासन्दर्शनादिकमपि । पवित्रं राजतं रजताक्तं वा पितॄणां पात्रमुच्यते । रजतस्य तथादानं दर्शनं नाम चेष्यते इति मत्स्यवायूक्तेः

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

A daughter’s son is a dauhitra; Kutapa is the eighth muhūrta. With the reading “dauhitra,” it means a special kind of ghee, explained by the verse about the cow that eats grass on the new moon. Some say dauhitra means a vessel shaped like a sword; some say Kutapa is a blanket made from goat hair. Speaking of silver, seeing it, and similar acts are also included. A silver or silver-coated vessel is called a pure vessel for the ancestors; the gift and sight of silver are praised in the Matsya and Vāyu texts.

VP 3.15.55

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

somādhāraḥ pitṛ-gaṇo yogādhāraś ca candramāḥ|śrāddhe yogi-niyogas tu tasmād bhūpāla śasyate

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

सोमाधारः पितृगणः अग्निष्वात्तादेराधिकारिको गणः सोमाधारः सोमोपजीवित्वात् । योगाधारश्चन्द्रमास्तेषामेवयोगबलेन पुनश्चन्द्रस्याप्यायनात् । तथा च हरिवंशे— एतेऽस्मत्पितरस्तात\! योगिनां योगवर्द्धनाः । आप्याययन्ति ये पूर्वं सोमं योगबलेन वै ॥ तथा वायुः— श्राद्धे प्रीताः पुनः सोमं पितरो योगमाश्रिताः । आप्याययन्ति योगेन त्रैलोक्यं येन जीवति ॥ तस्मात् श्राद्धे योगिनिमन्त्रणं शस्तम् इति

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The ancestral host has Soma as its support: it is the group including the Agniṣvāttas, who depend on Soma. The Moon has yoga as its support, because through the power of their yoga the Moon is replenished again. The Harivaṃśa says: “These are our ancestors, dear one, increasers of the yogins’ yoga; they first replenish Soma through the power of yoga.” The Vāyu likewise says: “The ancestors, pleased at the śrāddha and abiding in yoga, again replenish Soma through yoga, by which the three worlds live.” Therefore inviting a yogin at a śrāddha is praised.
