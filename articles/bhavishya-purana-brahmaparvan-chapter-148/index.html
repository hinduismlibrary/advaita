Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 148 — The Wheel of Time

BP 1.148.1

अथ साम्बो महातेजा दृष्ट्वा चक्रं पितुः करे । ज्वालामालाकरालं तु महता तेजसान्वितम् ॥

Then Sāmba, great in splendor, saw the wheel in his father’s hand, terrible with a garland of flames and endowed with great radiance.

BP 1.148.2

पप्रच्छ पितरं साम्बो भक्त्या श्रद्धासमन्वितः । कुतस्तात त्वया प्राप्तं चक्रमादित्यसन्निभम् ॥

Endowed with devotion and faith, Sāmba asked his father: From where did you obtain this wheel, resembling the Sun, O father?

BP 1.148.3

किमर्थं वहते देव दिव्यमायुधमुत्तमम् । एतदाख्याहि मे सर्वं श्रोतुकामस्य कौतुकात् ॥

For what purpose do you bear this divine, excellent weapon, O god? Tell me all this; I wish to hear, out of curiosity.

BP 1.148.4

वासुदेव उवाच । साधुसाधु महाबाहो साधु पृष्टोस्म्यहं त्वया । शृणुष्वैकमनाः पुत्र चक्रस्य विधिनिर्णयम् ॥

Vāsudeva said: Well, well, O mighty-armed one; well have I been asked by you. Listen with one mind, son, to the rule of the wheel.

BP 1.148.5

दिव्यं वर्षसहस्रं तु भानुमाराध्य श्रद्धया । प्राप्तं चक्रं मया तस्माद्भास्कराल्लोकपूजितात् ॥

Having worshipped Bhānu with faith for a thousand divine years, I obtained this wheel from Bhāskara, worshipped by the world.

BP 1.148.6

नभोगः पञ्चकारूढः स्थितः साक्षाद्दिवाकरः । ग्रहाः सोमादयो यस्य संस्थिता नाभिमण्डले ॥

The sky-goer, mounted upon the five, stands there as the Day-maker himself; the planets, beginning with Soma, are situated in his navel-disc.

BP 1.148.7

आदित्या द्वादश समा अरेषु क्रमशस्तथा । प्रोक्तं पथिषु तत्त्वानि पृथिव्यादीनि यानि वै ॥

The twelve Ādityas are arranged in order on the spokes. The elements, beginning with earth, are declared to be on the paths.

BP 1.148.8

एतैस्तत्त्वैः परिव्याप्तं चक्रं कालात्मकं परम् । संक्षेपात्ते मयाख्यातं दत्तं चक्रमिवापरम् ॥

Surrounded by these elements, the supreme wheel is of the nature of time. I have described it to you in brief, like another wheel that was given.

BP 1.148.9

साम्ब उवाच । कथं कालमयं देव चक्रं कमलमुच्यते । इदं तावन्ममाचक्ष्व ज्ञातुमिच्छामि तत्त्वतः ॥

Sāmba said: How, O god, is the wheel, made of time, called a lotus? Explain this to me; I wish to know it truly.

BP 1.148.10

वासुदेव उवाच । कमलं ह्यृतुभिः षड्भिः षट्दलं चाक्षयाश्रितम् । पुरुषाधिष्ठितं तद्धि तत्र साङ्गो रविः स्थितः ॥

Vāsudeva said: The lotus has six seasons and six petals, and rests on the imperishable. It is established by the Person; there the Sun, with all his limbs, is situated.

BP 1.148.11

यच्च कालत्रयं लोके तन्नाभित्रयमुच्यते । मासा अरा महाबाहो पक्षाश्च प्रधयः स्मृताः ॥

The three times in the world are called its three hubs. The months are its spokes, O mighty-armed one, and the fortnights are remembered as its rims.

BP 1.148.12

नेमी चैव परे प्रोक्ते अयने दक्षिणोत्तरे । पथिनाभिषु योगे च योगाख्यास्तपनादिभिः ॥

The two rims further declared are the northern and southern courses. In its paths, hubs, and conjunctions are the configurations called the Sun and the others.

BP 1.148.13

नक्षत्राणि ग्रहाश्चैव सदा चात्र स्थिताः स्मृताः । एतैर्व्याप्तमिदं चक्रं स्थूलसूक्ष्मप्रभेदतः ॥

The constellations and planets are always said to be situated here. This wheel is pervaded by them in its gross and subtle divisions.

BP 1.148.14

अत्रोद्दिष्टेषु कालेषु ये नोद्दिष्टा मया तव । युगादिकल्पपर्यन्तास्तेऽपि चात्र स्थिताः क्रमात् ॥

At the times indicated here, those I have not indicated to you, extending up to world-age and aeon, are also situated here in order.

BP 1.148.15

यत्ते कालात्मकं चक्रमिदं संक्षेपतो मया । कथितं तद्विनिष्क्रान्तं प्रदीप्तात्सूर्यमण्डलात् ॥

This wheel of the nature of time, which I described to you in brief, emerged from the blazing solar disc.

BP 1.148.16

असुराणां वधायेदं मया लब्धं दिवाकरात् । आराध्य तपसा सूर्यं पुरा कल्पे जगद्गुरुम् । अतः सम्पूज्ययाम्येनं ग्रहैस्तत्त्वैर्वृतं सदा ॥

I obtained this from the Day-maker to slay the demons, having formerly worshipped the Sun, teacher of the world, through austerity in an earlier aeon. Therefore I always worship it, surrounded by planets and elements.

BP 1.148.17

अर्कं भक्तो हि चक्रस्थं यः पूजयति भक्तिमान् । तेजसा रविसंकाशः पुष्पोत्तरपुरं व्रजेत् ॥

The devoted person who worships Arka, situated in the wheel, goes, radiant like Ravi, to the city of flowers.

BP 1.148.18

तस्मात्तं मत्कुलानन्दं मित्रं सम्पूजयाम्यहम् । ग्रहैस्तत्त्वैर्वृतं भक्त्या स्वमन्त्रैः सततं विभुम् ॥

Therefore I constantly worship that friend, the joy of my lineage, the all-pervading one, with devotion and his own sacred formulas, surrounded by planets and elements.

BP 1.148.19

सप्तम्यां चक्रमालिख्य ये यजन्ति दिवाकरम् । रक्तचन्दनपूर्णेन कुंकुमेन सुगंधिना ॥

Those who worship the Day-maker on the seventh lunar day, having drawn the wheel with fragrant saffron mixed with red sandalwood,

BP 1.148.20

पिष्टगन्धादिभिर्वापि रक्तवर्णकमिश्रकैः । रक्तैश्च कमलैः शुद्धैः करवीरैः सुगन्धिभिः ॥

or with ground perfumes and the like mixed with red color, with pure red lotuses and fragrant oleander flowers,

BP 1.148.21

अन्यैर्वा कुसुमैर्वन्यैः प्रत्यग्रैर्जन्तुवर्जितैः । अपर्युषितनिश्छिद्रैः शुभधन्यैर्दलैरपि ॥

or with other forest flowers, fresh, free from insects, not stale, unpierced, and with auspicious petals,

BP 1.148.22

फलैः पक्वैरोषधिभिस्तथा दूर्वाङ्कुरैः कुशैः । धूपैश्च विविधैर्गन्धैस्तथा वस्त्रैश्च भूषणैः ॥

with ripe fruits, herbs, shoots of sacred grass, incense and various fragrances, and likewise with garments and ornaments,

BP 1.148.23

भक्ष्यैर्भोज्यैश्च पेयैश्च चोष्यैर्लेह्यैश्च शक्तितः । वितानशोभासिकतैः पलाशैरुपशोभितैः ॥

with foods, solid foods, drinks, chewable foods, and lickable foods according to their means, and with canopies beautified with sand and adorned with palāśa leaves,

BP 1.148.24

छत्रचामरघण्टाभिर्भूषणैर्दर्पणादिभिः । नृत्यवादित्रगीतैश्च वेदैः पुण्यकथास्वनैः ॥

with umbrellas, fly-whisks, bells, ornaments, mirrors and the like, with dancing, instruments, songs, Vedic recitations, and sounds of sacred stories,

BP 1.148.25

सर्वत्र जयघोषैश्च सम्पूर्णे पूजयन्ति ये । सम्पूर्णान्विविधान्कामान्निर्विघ्नान्प्राप्नुवन्ति ते ॥

those who worship everywhere amid shouts of victory attain all their varied desires, complete and without obstacles.

BP 1.148.26

स्वचक्रं चापि निर्विघ्नं वृद्धिमायाति सुव्रत । हन्यते परचक्रं च यत्रेदं पूज्यते सकृत् ॥

Their own wheel too grows without obstacle, O steadfast one; the enemy’s wheel is destroyed wherever this worship is performed even once.

BP 1.148.27

सङ्क्रान्तौ ग्रहणे चापि लिखित्वा यो जपेदिदम् । भवन्ति नियताः साम्ब तस्य सानुग्रहा ग्रहाः ॥

At a solar transition or eclipse, whoever writes this and recites it, O Sāmba, will have the planets and their attendants become favorable.

BP 1.148.28

सर्वरोगविहीनस्तु सर्वदुःखविवर्जितः । चिरं जीवति धर्मात्मा सर्वैश्वर्यसमन्वितः ॥

Free from every disease and sorrow, the righteous person lives long, endowed with every prosperity.

BP 1.148.29

एष वै कथितो वत्स चक्रयोगो मया तव । अर्कस्य सर्वयज्ञानां श्रेष्ठं सिद्धिप्रदो भृशम् ॥

This wheel rite has indeed been told by me to you, child; it is excellent among all the rites of Arka and greatly bestows success.

BP 1.148.30

पुण्यो धर्मस्तथा पुष्ट्यः शत्रुघ्नश्च विशेषतः । श्वेतो रक्तोऽथ पीतश्च कृष्णश्चापि विभागशः ॥

It is meritorious, righteous, nourishing, and especially destroys enemies; white, red, yellow, and black, each in its division.
