BP 3.2.22.1

वैताल उवाच ।
भो राजन्बिल्वतीग्रामे गंगायामुनमध्यगे ।
अहं पूर्वभवे चासं क्षत्रसिंहो महीपतिः ॥ 1 ॥

Vetāla said: O king, in a former birth I was King Kṣatrasiṃha in Bilvatī, a lovely town between the Ganges and Yamunā.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
वैताल vaitāla the Vetāla
उवाच uvāca said
भो bho O friend
राजन्बिल्वतीग्रामे rājanbilvatīgrāme O king, in Bilvatī village
गंगायामुनमध्यगे gaṃgāyāmunamadhyage between the Ganges and Yamunā
अहं ahaṃ I
पूर्वभवे pūrvabhave in a former birth
चासं cāsaṃ I was
क्षत्रसिंहो kṣatrasiṃho Kṣatrasiṃha
महीपतिः mahīpatiḥ king
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

vaitāla uvāca | bho rājanbilvatīgrāme gaṃgāyāmunamadhyage | ahaṃ pūrvabhave cāsaṃ kṣatrasiṃho mahīpatiḥ || 1 ||

BP 3.2.22.3

उभकौ तनयौ तस्य सर्वविद्याविशारदौ ।
विष्णुभक्तः स्मृतो ज्येष्ठो नाम्ना लीलाधरो बली ।
शाक्तोऽभवत्तदनुजो मोहनो नाम श्रुतः ॥ 3 ॥

He had two sons, both skilled in every branch of knowledge. The strong elder, Līlādhara, was a devotee of Viṣṇu; the younger, Mohana, was a Śākta.

BP 3.2.22.4

कदाचित्क्षत्रसिंहस्तु यज्ञार्थी यज्ञहेतवे ।
शंभुदत्तं समाहूय ससुतं धर्मकोविदम् ।
स्वयं च कारयामास च्छागमेधं सुरप्रियम् ॥ 4 ॥

Once King Kṣatrasiṃha wished to perform a sacrifice. For that rite he summoned Śambhudatta, learned in dharma, with his son, and arranged a goat sacrifice pleasing to the gods.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

समाहूय samāhūya having summoned
ससुतं sasutaṃ with his son
धर्मकोविदम् dharmakovidam knower of dharma
स्वयं svayaṃ himself
च ca and
कारयामास kārayāmāsa caused; arranged
च्छागमेधं cchāgamedhaṃ a goat sacrifice
सुरप्रियम् surapriyam pleasing to the gods
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

BP 3.2.22.7

दयालुर्वेष्णवो धीमानब्रवीद्वचनं रुषा ॥ 7 ॥

Seeing the goat facing death, Līlādhara, a compassionate and wise Vaiṣṇava, spoke indignantly.

BP 3.2.22.8

दारुणं नरकं योग्यमनया जीवहिंसया ।
सर्वेशो भगवान्विष्णुहिंसायज्ञेन दुष्यति ॥ 8 ॥

This violent sacrifice is a path to terrible hell. The Lord of all, Bhagavān Viṣṇu, is displeased by a sacrifice involving violence.

Word-for-word

दारुणं dāruṇaṃ terrible

योग्यमनया yogyamanayā fit for this
जीवहिंसया jīvahiṃsayā through the killing of living beings
सर्वेशो sarveśo the Lord of all
भगवान्विष्णुहिंसायज्ञेन bhagavānviṣṇuhiṃsāyajñena Lord Viṣṇu by a violent sacrifice
दुष्यति duṣyati is displeased
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

dāruṇaṃ narakaṃ yogyamanayā jīvahiṃsayā | sarveśo bhagavānviṣṇuhiṃsāyajñena duṣyati || 8 ||

BP 3.2.22.9

मृदुपूर्वं mṛdupūrvaṃ first, gently
जहासोच्चैर्वचनं jahāsoccairvacanaṃ laughed aloud and spoke

BP 3.2.22.10

पुरा सत्ययुगे भ्रातर्ब्राह्मणा यज्ञतत्पराः ।
अजेनैव हि यष्टव्यमिति ज्ञात्वा परां श्रुतिम् ॥ 10 ॥

Formerly, in the Satya age, O brother, Brahmins intent on sacrifice knew from the highest scripture that only an aja should be sacrificed.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
पुरा purā formerly
सत्ययुगे satyayuge in the Satya Yuga
भ्रातर्ब्राह्मणा bhrātarbrāhmaṇā O brother, Brahmins
यज्ञतत्पराः yajñatatparāḥ intent on sacrifice
अजेनैव ajenaiva with aja alone
हि hi indeed
यष्टव्यमिति yaṣṭavyamiti that one should sacrifice

परां parāṃ the highest
श्रुतिम् śrutim scriptural revelation
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

purā satyayuge bhrātarbrāhmaṇā yajñatatparāḥ | ajenaiva hi yaṣṭavyamiti jñātvā parāṃ śrutim || 10 ||

BP 3.2.22.11

तदा शक्रादयो देवा वह्निमध्ये समागताः ॥ 11 ॥

ते te they

मनो mano their minds
दधुः dadhuḥ they placed

शक्रादयो śakrādayo Indra and the others
देवा devā the gods
वह्निमध्ये vahnimadhye in the midst of the fire
समागताः samāgatāḥ assembled
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

tilādhikamajaṃ matvā havye te tu mano dadhuḥ | tadā śakrādayo devā vahnimadhye samāgatāḥ || 11 ||

BP 3.2.22.12

अजच्छागः स्मृतो वेदैस्तेन यष्टव्यमन्तरम् ॥ 12 ॥

वाक्यं vākyaṃ words
त्वन्मतं tvanmataṃ your opinion
निष्फलप्रदम् niṣphalapradam producing no fruit
अजच्छागः ajacchāgaḥ the aja goat
स्मृतो smṛto is considered
वेदैस्तेन vedaistena therefore, by the Vedas
यष्टव्यमन्तरम् yaṣṭavyamantaram to be sacrificed at the proper interval
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

एतस्मिन्नंतरे etasminnaṃtare in the meantime

पितृयोनिरमावसुः pitṛyoniramāvasuḥ Amāvasu, born of the ancestral realm

Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

BP 3.2.22.17

रजोगुणमयो लोकस्त्रेतायां संबभूव ह ।
हिंसा सत्ययुगे नासीद्धर्मस्तत्र चतुष्पदः ॥ 17 ॥

In Treta the world came to be shaped by rajas. In the Satya age there was no violence; dharma stood on four feet.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

संबभूव saṃbabhūva came into being
ह ha indeed
हिंसा hiṃsā violence
सत्ययुगे satyayuge in the Satya Yuga
नासीद्धर्मस्तत्र nāsīddharmastatra then dharma was not
चतुष्पदः catuṣpadaḥ four-footed
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

rajoguṇamayo lokastretāyāṃ saṃbabhūva ha | hiṃsā satyayuge nāsīddharmastatra catuṣpadaḥ || 17 ||

BP 3.2.22.18

हव्येन तर्पिता देवा न मांसे रक्तसंभवैः ।
इति श्रुत्वा क्षत्रसिंहस्त्यक्त्वा छागं भयातुरम् ॥ 18 ॥

The gods are satisfied by oblations, not by meat arising from blood. Hearing this, King Kṣatrasiṃha released the frightened goat.

तर्पिता tarpitā satisfied
देवा devā the gods

मांसे māṃse with meat
रक्तसंभवैः raktasaṃbhavaiḥ arising from blood

भविष्यंति bhaviṣyaṃti will be
महाराज mahārāja O great king
पुराणश्रवणे purāṇaśravaṇe in hearing the Purāṇa
रताः ratāḥ engaged

Having said this, the Vetāla disappeared there. King Vikramāditya attained supreme bliss.
