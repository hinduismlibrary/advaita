Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 53 — Solar Motion and the Sun’s Worshippers

BP 1.53.1

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
तत्रारुणो मया पूर्वं सारथ्ये सन्नियोजितः ।
इन्द्रेण माठरो नाम वायुना कल्मषेण तु ॥

Brahmā spoke. Aruṇa was first appointed by me there as charioteer. Māṭhara was appointed by Indra, and Kalmaṣa by Vāyu.

ब्रह्मा उवाच (brahmā uvāca) — Brahmā said;
तत्र (tatra) — there; अरुणः (aruṇaḥ) — Aruṇa; मया (mayā) — by me; पूर्वम् (pūrvam) — formerly; सारथ्ये (sārathye) — to the charioteering; सन्नियोजितः (sanniyojitaḥ) — appointed;
इन्द्रेण (indreṇa) — by Indra; माठरः नाम (māṭharaḥ nāma) — Māṭhara by name; वायुना (vāyunā) — by Vāyu; कल्मषेण (kalmaṣeṇa) — Kalmaṣa; तु (tu) — indeed

BP 1.53.2

वैनतेयेन तार्क्ष्योपरि विमलो नखतुण्डप्रहरणः पुरोगामी नियुक्त इति ॥
कालेन दण्डो महादण्डायुधो भवता शेषा महागणाधिपः ॥

Vimala was appointed by Garuḍa to go in front upon Tārkṣya, armed with claws and beak. Kāla appointed Daṇḍa, bearer of a great staff weapon, and you appointed Śeṣa, lord of the great hosts.

वैनतेयेन (vainateyena) — by Vainateya; तार्क्ष्य-उपरि (tārkṣya-upari) — upon Tārkṣya; विमलः (vimalaḥ) — Vimala; नखतुण्डप्रहरणः (nakhtuṇḍapraharaṇaḥ) — weaponed with a nail-beak; पुरोगामी (purogāmī) — forerunner; नियुक्तः (niyuktaḥ) — appointed; इति (iti) — thus;
कालेन (kālena) — by Kāla; दण्डः (daṇḍaḥ) — Daṇḍa; महादण्डायुधः (mahādaṇḍāyudhaḥ) — bearer of a great staff weapon; भवता (bhavatā) — by you; शेषः (śeṣaḥ) — Śeṣa; महागणाधिपः (mahāgaṇādhipaḥ) — great lord of the hosts

BP 1.53.3

वैशाखेन राज्ञा वसुभिदायुधाङ्गारिकौ द्वौ अग्निना पिङ्गलः ।
संयन्ता महादण्डायुधो भवता शेषो महागणाधिपः ॥

King Vaiśākha appointed Vasubhid and Āyudhāṅgārika, the two. Agni appointed Piṅgala. You appointed the controller, the great staff bearer, and Śeṣa, lord of the great hosts.

वैशाखेन (vaiśākhena) — by Vaiśākha; राज्ञा (rājñā) — by the king; वसुभिदा (vasubhidā) — by Vasubhid; आयुधाङ्गारिकौ द्वौ (āyudhāṅgārikau dvau) — the two Āyudhāṅgārika; अग्निना (agninā) — by Agni; पिङ्गलः (piṅgalaḥ) — Piṅgala;
संयन्ता (saṃyantā) — controller; महादण्डायुधः (mahādaṇḍāyudhaḥ) — bearer of a great staff weapon; भवता (bhavatā) — by you; शेषः (śeṣaḥ) — Śeṣa; महागणाधिपः (mahāgaṇādhipaḥ) — great lord of the hosts

BP 1.53.4

हस्तो यमेन पाशहस्तोम्बुपतिना समिन्धनः ।
अलकाधिपतिना विष्णुः ॥

Hasta was appointed by Yama, the noose bearer by the lord of waters, Samindhana by the lord of Alakā, and Viṣṇu.

हस्तः (hastaḥ) — Hasta; यमेन (yamena) — by Yama; पाशहस्तः (pāśahastaḥ) — Pāśahasta; अम्बुपतिना (ambupatinā) — by the lord of waters; समिन्धनः (samindhanaḥ) — Samindhana;
अलकाधिपतिना (alakādhipatinā) — by the lord of Alakā; विष्णुः (viṣṇuḥ) — Viṣṇu

BP 1.53.5

अश्विभ्यां कालोपकालौ वाक्षप्रधानकौ ।
नरनारायणाभ्यां क्षारौ धारौ धिषणकृष्णौ ॥

Kāla and Upakāla were appointed by the Aśvins, with Vākṣa and Pradhānaka as their leaders. Kṣāra and Dhāra were appointed by Nara and Nārāyaṇa, and Dhīṣaṇa and Kṛṣṇa.

अश्विभ्याम् (aśvibhyām) — by the two Aśvins; कालोपकालौ (kālopakālau) — Kāla and Upakāla; वाक्षप्रधानकौ (vākṣapradhānakau) — Vākṣa and Pradhānaka;
नरनारायणाभ्याम् (naranārāyaṇābhyām) — by Nara-Nārāyaṇa; क्षारौ (kṣārau) — Kṣāra and Dhāra; धिषणकृष्णौ (dhiṣaṇakṛṣṇau) — Dhīṣaṇa and Kṛṣṇa

BP 1.53.6

वैराजशङ्खपालपर्जन्यरजसां दिशासु विदिशासु दिशां पालनं विश्वेदेवा ददुः ॥

The Viśvedevas gave the guardianship of the directions and intermediate directions to Vairāja, Śaṅkhapāla, Parjanya, and Rajas.

वैराज-शङ्खपाल-पर्जन्य-रजसाम् (vairāja-śaṅkhapāla-parjanyarajasām) — of Vairāja, Śaṅkhapāla, Parjanya, and Rajas; दिशासु (diśāsu) — in the directions; विदिशासु (vidiśāsu) — in the intermediate directions; दिशाम् पालनम् (diśām pālanam) — protection of the directions; विश्वेदेवाः (viśvedevāḥ) — the Viśvedevas; ददुः (daduḥ) — gave

BP 1.53.7

सप्तैता लोकमातरः सर्वमरुतोऽददन् ।
ओंकारो वषट्कारो वेदनिस्वनः पिनाकी विनायकः शेषोऽनन्तो वासुकिश्च नागसहस्रेणात्मतुल्येनादित्यस्य रथमनुयान्ति ॥

The seven mothers of the worlds and all the Maruts gave him their support. The syllable Om, the sacrificial utterance, the sound of the Vedas, the bow bearer, the obstacle remover, Śeṣa, Ananta, and Vāsuki, together with a thousand serpents equal to himself, follow the chariot of the Sun.

सप्त एताः (sapta etāḥ) — these seven; लोकमातरः (lokamātaraḥ) — mothers of the world; सर्वमरुतः (sarvamarutaḥ) — all the Maruts; अददन् (adadan) — gave;
ओंकारः (oṃkāraḥ) — Oṃkāra; वषट्कारः (vaṣaṭkāraḥ) — Vaṣaṭkāra; वेदनिस्वनः (vedanisvanaḥ) — Veda-resonance; पिनाकी (pinākī) — Pinākin; विनायकः (vināyakaḥ) — Vināyaka; शेषः (śeṣaḥ) — Śeṣa; अनन्तः (anantaḥ) — Ananta; वासुकिः च (vāsukiḥ ca) — and Vāsuki; नागसहस्रेण (nāgasahasreṇa) — with a thousand serpents; आत्मतुल्येन (ātmatulyena) — equal to himself; आदित्यस्य रथम् अनुयान्ति (ādityasya ratham anuyānti) — follow Āditya’s chariot; १ — editorial numeral retained from the source transcription

BP 1.53.8

गायत्री सावित्री रथे स्थिते उभे सन्ध्ये सदा सा देवता या रविमण्डलं नापैति ।
भगवन्तं सहस्रकिरणमवलम्बितुम् ॥

Gāyatrī and Sāvitrī, the two twilights, are always stationed in the chariot. That goddess does not leave the disk of the Sun, to support the Lord with a thousand rays.

गायत्री (gāyatrī) — Gāyatrī; सावित्री (sāvitrī) — Sāvitrī; रथे स्थिते उभे (rathe sthite ubhe) — both situated in the chariot; सन्ध्ये (sandhye) — the two twilights; सदा (sadā) — always; सा देवता (sā devatā) — that goddess; या (yā) — who; रविमण्डलम् (ravimaṇḍalam) — Ravi’s disk; न आपैति (na āpaiti) — does not depart; २ — editorial numeral retained from the source transcription;
भगवन्तम् (bhagavantam) — the blessed one; सहस्रकिरणम् (sahasrakiraṇam) — of a thousand rays; अवलम्बितुम् (avalambitum) — to support

BP 1.53.9

एतद्वै सर्वदैवत्यं मण्डलं ब्रह्मवादिनं ब्रह्मयज्ञवादिनीं यज्ञः ।
भगवद्भक्तानां परमादित्योयं विष्णुर्माहेश्वराणाम् ॥

This circle indeed belongs to all deities, to those who speak of Brahman, those who speak of the sacrifice to Brahman, and the sacrifice. For devotees of the Lord, this is the supreme Sun, and for the followers of Maheśvara, Viṣṇu.

एतत् वै (etat vai) — this indeed; सर्वदैवत्यं (sarvadaivatyaṃ) — all-divine; मण्डलम् (maṇḍalam) — disk; ब्रह्मवादिनम् (brahmavādinam) — for the Brahma-speaker; ब्रह्मयज्ञवादिनीम् (brahmayajñavādinīm) — Brahma-yajña-speaking; यज्ञः (yajñaḥ) — sacrifice;
भगवद्भक्तानाम् (bhagavadbhaktānām) — for the devotees of Bhagavat; परमादित्यः अयम् (paramādityaḥ ayam) — this supreme Āditya; विष्णुः (viṣṇuḥ) — Viṣṇu; माहेश्वराणाम् (māheśvarāṇām) — for the Māheśvaras

BP 1.53.10

स्थानाभिमानिनो ह्येते सदा वै वृषभध्वज ।
सूर्यमाप्याययन्त्येते तेजसां तेज उत्तमम् ॥

These guardians of places, O bull bannered one, always replenish the Sun, the highest splendor among splendors.

स्थानाभिमानिनः (sthānābhimāninaḥ) — place-proud ones; हि (hi) — indeed; एते (ete) — these; सदा वै (sadā vai) — always indeed; वृषभध्वज (vṛṣabhadhvaja) — O Bull-bannered;
सूर्यम् (sūryam) — Sūrya; आप्याययन्ति (āpyāyayanti) — replenish; एते (ete) — these; तेजसाम् (tejasām) — of splendors; तेजः उत्तमम् (tejaḥ uttamam) — highest splendor

BP 1.53.11

ग्रथितैः स्वैर्वचोभिस्तु स्तुवन्ते ऋषयो रविम् ।
गन्धर्वाप्सरसश्चैव गीतनृत्यैरुपासते ॥

The sages praise Ravi with their own composed words. Gandharvas and celestial nymphs serve him with songs and dances.

ग्रथितैः (grathitaiḥ) — woven; स्वैः वचोभिः (svaiḥ vacobhiḥ) — with their own words; तु (tu) — but; स्तुवन्ते (stuvante) — praise; ऋषयः (ṛṣayaḥ) — the ṛṣis; रविम् (ravim) — Ravi;
गन्धर्वाप्सरसः (gandharvāpsarasaḥ) — Gandharvas and Apsarases; च एव (ca eva) — and indeed; गीतनृत्यैः (gītanṛtyaiḥ) — with song and dance; उपासते (upāsate) — serve

BP 1.53.12

वियद्भ्रमणतो रक्षां कुर्वंति स्म इषुग्रहम् ।
सर्पा वहन्ति वै सूर्यं यातुधानास्तु यान्ति च ॥

As they travel through the sky, they protect the arrow bearer. Serpents carry the Sun, and the evil spirits also go along.

वियत्-भ्रमणतः (viyat-bhramaṇataḥ) — by roaming through the sky; रक्षाम् (rakṣām) — protection; कुर्वन्ति स्म (kurvanti sma) — they performed; इषुग्रहम् (iṣugraham) — arrow-grasp;
सर्पाः (sarpāḥ) — serpents; वहन्ति (vahanti) — carry; वै (vai) — indeed; सूर्यम् (sūryam) — Sūrya; यातुधानाः (yātudhānāḥ) — Yātudhānas; तु (tu) — but; यान्ति (yānti) — go; च (ca) — also

BP 1.53.13

वालखिल्या नमन्त्येतं परिचार्योदयाद्रविम् ।
दिवस्पतिः स्वभूश्चोभौ अग्रगौ योजनस्य तु ॥

The Vālakhilyas bow to him, attending upon the Sun from his rising. The lord of day and Svabhū are both at the front of the course.

वालखिल्याः (vālakhilyāḥ) — Vālakhilyas; १ — editorial numeral retained from the source transcription; नमन्ति (namanti) — bow; एतम् (etam) — to this; परिचार्य (paricārya) — attending; उदयात् रविम् (udayāt ravim) — Ravi from the rising;
दिवस्पतिः (divaspatiḥ) — lord of day; स्वभूः (svabhūḥ) — Svabhū; च उभौ (ca ubhau) — both; अग्रगौ (agragau) — foremost; योजनस्य (yojanasya) — of the yojana; तु (tu) — indeed

BP 1.53.14

भर्गोऽथ दक्षिणे पार्श्वे कञ्जजो वामतः स्थितः ।
सर्वे ते पृष्ठगा ज्ञेया ग्रहा लोकेषु पूजिताः. ॥

Bharga stands on the southern side, and the lotus born one on the left. All those going behind are to be known as planets, worshipped in the worlds.

भर्गः (bhargaḥ) — Bharga; अथ (atha) — then; दक्षिणे पार्श्वे (dakṣiṇe pārśve) — on the southern side; कञ्जजः (kañjajaḥ) — Kañjaja; वामतः (vāmataḥ) — on the left; स्थितः (sthitaḥ) — stands;
सर्वे ते (sarve te) — all those; पृष्ठगाः (pṛṣṭhagāḥ) — going behind; ज्ञेयाः (jñeyāḥ) — are to be known; ग्रहाः (grahāḥ) — planets; लोकेषु (lokeṣu) — in the worlds; पूजिताः (pūjitāḥ) — worshipped

BP 1.53.15

उपरागशिखी चोभावग्रतो नात्र संशयः ।
मनुष्यधर्मा दक्षिणत उत्तरेण प्रचेतसः ॥

The Eclipse and the Crested One are both in front, without doubt. Human duty is to the south, and Pracetasa to the north.

उपरागशिखी (uparāgaśikhī) — Uparāga and Śikhī; च उभौ (ca ubhau) — both; अग्रतः (agrataḥ) — in front; न अत्र संशयः (na atra saṃśayaḥ) — no doubt here;
मनुष्यधर्मा (manuṣyadharmā) — Manuṣyadharmā; दक्षिणतः (dakṣiṇataḥ) — on the south; उत्तरेण (uttareṇa) — on the north; प्रचेतसः (pracetasaḥ) — Pracetasa

BP 1.53.16

सम्भवन्ति तथा कृष्ण उभावेतौ सदाग्रगौ ।
वामेन वीतिहोत्रस्तु पृष्ठतस्तु हरिः सदा ॥

Thus Kṛṣṇa and the two others are always at the front. Vītihotra is on the left, and Hari is always at the rear.

सम्भवन्ति (sambhavanti) — arise; तथा (tathā) — thus; कृष्ण (kṛṣṇa) — O Kṛṣṇa; उभौ एतौ (ubhau etau) — these two; सदा अग्रगौ (sadā agragau) — always foremost;
वामेन (vāmena) — on the left; वीतिहोत्रः (vītihotraḥ) — Vītihotra; तु (tu) — but; पृष्ठतः (pṛṣṭhataḥ) — at the back; तु (tu) — but; हरिः (hariḥ) — Hari; सदा (sadā) — always

BP 1.53.17

रथपीठे क्षमा ज्ञेया अन्तराले नभस्तथा ।
आश्रित्य रथजां कान्तिं खं दिवः समयः स्थितः ॥

Earth is to be known on the chariot platform, and the sky in the space between. The vault of heaven, resting on the radiance born from the chariot, stands there.

रथपीठे (rathapīṭhe) — on the chariot-seat; क्षमा (kṣamā) — Kṣamā; ज्ञेया (jñeyā) — is to be known; अन्तराले (antarāle) — in the interval; नभः (nabhaḥ) — sky; तथा (tathā) — likewise;
आश्रित्य (āśritya) — relying on; रथजाम् कान्तिम् (rathajām kāntim) — chariot-born radiance; खम् (kham) — sky; दिवः (divaḥ) — of heaven; समयः (samayaḥ) — Samaya; स्थितः (sthitaḥ) — stands

BP 1.53.18

ध्वजो दण्डश्च विज्ञेयो ध्वजाग्रे वृष एव च ।
ऋद्धिर्वृद्धिस्तथा श्रीश्च पताका पार्वतीप्रिय ॥

The banner and staff are to be understood, and the bull is at the top of the banner. Prosperity, increase, and fortune are the pennants, O beloved of Pārvatī.

ध्वजः (dhvajaḥ) — banner; दण्डः (daṇḍaḥ) — staff; च (ca) — and; विज्ञेयः (vijñeyaḥ) — is to be known; ध्वजाग्रे (dhvajāgre) — at the top of the banner; वृषः (vṛṣaḥ) — bull; एव (eva) — indeed; च (ca) — and;
ऋद्धिः (ṛddhiḥ) — Ṛddhi; वृद्धिः (vṛddhiḥ) — Vṛddhi; तथा (tathā) — likewise; श्रीः (śrīḥ) — Śrī; च (ca) — and; पताका (patākā) — banner; पार्वतीप्रिय (pārvatīpriya) — Pārvatīpriya

BP 1.53.19

ध्वजदण्डाग्रे गरुड़स्तदग्रे वरुणालयः ।
मैनाकश्छत्रदण्डस्तु हिमवांश्छत्रमुच्यते ॥

At the top of the banner staff is Garuḍa, and beyond him is the abode of Varuṇa. Maināka is the parasol staff, and Himavān is called the parasol.

ध्वजदण्डाग्रे (dhvajadaṇḍāgre) — at the top of the banner-staff; गरुडः (garuḍaḥ) — Garuḍa; तत्-अग्रे (tad-agre) — at his top; वरुणालयः (varuṇālayaḥ) — Varuṇa’s abode;
मैनाकः (mainākaḥ) — Maināka; छत्रदण्डः (chatradaṇḍaḥ) — umbrella-staff; तु (tu) — but; हिमवान् (himavān) — Himavān; छत्रम् उच्यते (chatram ucyate) — is called the umbrella

BP 1.53.20

केचिदेवं वदन्तीह लोके चान्ये महामते ।
छत्रदण्डस्तथा क्लेशः क्लेशं छत्रं विदुर्बुधाः ॥

तपति (tapati) — burns; एकम् (ekam) — one; तु (tu) — but; मध्याह्ने (madhyāhne) — at midday; ताभिः एव (tābhiḥ eva) — with those very; गभस्तिभिः (gabhastibhiḥ) — rays

BP 1.53.42

उदितो वर्धमानाभिरामध्याह्नात्तपेद्रविः ।
ततः परं ह्रसन्तीभिर्गोभिरस्तं नियच्छति ॥

Having risen with rays that increase, Ravi heats until midday. After that, with rays that diminish, he brings on sunset.

उदितः (uditaḥ) — risen; वर्धमानाभिः (vardhamānābhiḥ) — with increasing; आमध्याह्नात् (āmadhyāhnāt) — until midday; तपेत् (tapet) — burns; रविः (raviḥ) — Ravi;
ततः परम् (tataḥ param) — thereafter; ह्रसन्तीभिः (hrasantībhiḥ) — with decreasing; गोभिः (gobhiḥ) — with rays; अस्तम् (astam) — to setting; नियच्छति (niyacchati) — brings

BP 1.53.43

यत्रोद्यन्दृश्यते सूर्यः स तेषामुदयः स्मृतः ।
प्रणाशं गच्छते यत्र स तेषामस्तमुच्यते ॥

Where the Sun is seen rising, that is called their sunrise. Where he disappears, that is called their sunset.

यत्र (yatra) — where; उद्यन् (udyan) — rising; दृश्यते (dṛśyate) — is seen; सूर्यः (sūryaḥ) — the Sun; सः (saḥ) — that; तेषाम् उदयः (teṣām udayaḥ) — their rising; स्मृतः (smṛtaḥ) — remembered;
प्रणाशम् (praṇāśam) — to destruction; गच्छते (gacchate) — goes; यत्र (yatra) — where; सः (saḥ) — that; तेषाम् अस्तम् (teṣām astam) — their setting; उच्यते (ucyate) — is called

BP 1.53.44

एवं पुष्करमध्येन तदा सर्पति भास्करः ।
त्रिंशद्भागं तु मेदिन्या मुहूर्तेन स गच्छति ॥

Thus Bhāskara moves through the middle of Puṣkara. He travels thirty parts of the earth in one time period.

एवम् (evam) — thus; पुष्करमध्येन (puṣkaramadhyena) — through the middle of Puṣkara; तदा (tadā) — then; सर्पति (sarpati) — moves; भास्करः (bhāskaraḥ) — Bhāskara;
त्रिंशद्भागम् (triṃśadbhāgam) — thirty parts; तु (tu) — but; मेदिन्याः (medinyāḥ) — of the earth; मुहूर्तेन (muhūrtena) — in a muhūrta; सः (saḥ) — he; गच्छति (gacchati) — goes

BP 1.53.45

योजनाग्रेण सङ्ख्यां तु मुहूर्तस्य निबोध मे ।
पूर्णं शतसहस्राशं सहस्रं तु त्रिलोचन ॥

Hear from me the measure in yojanas for one time period, a full hundred thousand and a thousand, O three eyed one.

योजनाग्रेण (yojanāgreṇa) — by yojana-measure; सङ्ख्याम् (saṅkhyām) — count; १ — editorial numeral retained from the source transcription; तु (tu) — but; मुहूर्तस्य (muhūrtasya) — of a muhūrta; निबोध (nibodha) — know; मे (me) — from me;
पूर्णम् (pūrṇam) — full; शतसहस्रांशम् (śatasahasrāṃśam) — hundred-thousand portion; सहस्रम् (sahasram) — thousand; तु (tu) — but; त्रिलोचन (trilocana) — O three-eyed one

BP 1.53.46

पञ्चाशच्च तथाल्पानि सहस्राण्यधिकानि तु ।
मौहूर्तिको गतिर्ह्येषा सूर्यस्य तु विधीयते ॥

Fifty thousand more, and further lesser thousands. This is prescribed as the speed of the Sun for one time period.

पञ्चाशत् च (pañcāśat ca) — and fifty; तथा (tathā) — likewise; अल्पानि (alpāni) — lesser; सहस्राणि अधिकानि (sahasrāṇi adhikāni) — additional thousands; तु (tu) — but;
मौहूर्तिकी गतिः (mauhūrtikī gatiḥ) — muhūrtika speed; हि एषा (hi eṣā) — this indeed; सूर्यस्य (sūryasya) — of Sūrya; तु (tu) — but; विधीयते (vidhīyate) — is ordained

BP 1.53.47

योजनानां सहस्रे द्वे द्वे शते द्वे च योजने ।
निमेषान्तरमात्रेण दिवि सूर्यः प्रसर्पति ॥

Two thousand two hundred and two yojanas, in only the interval of a blink, the Sun moves in heaven.

योजनानाम् (yojanānām) — of yojanas; सहस्रे द्वे (sahasre dve) — two thousands; द्वे शते (dve śate) — two hundreds; द्वे च योजने (dve ca yojane) — and two yojanas;
निमेषान्तरमात्रेण (nimeṣāntaramātreṇa) — in merely an interval of a nimeṣa; दिवि (divi) — in heaven; सूर्यः (sūryaḥ) — Sūrya; प्रसर्पति (prasarpati) — moves

BP 1.53.48

स शीघ्रमेव पर्येति भास्करोऽलातचक्रवत् ।
भ्रमन्वै भ्रममाणेषु ऋक्षेषु विचरत्यसौ ॥

He travels quickly like a circle made by a firebrand. Moving among the moving stars, he wanders.

सः (saḥ) — he; शीघ्रम् एव (śīghram eva) — quickly indeed; पर्येति (paryeti) — traverses; भास्करः (bhāskaraḥ) — Bhāskara; अलातचक्रवत् (alātacakravat) — like a firebrand-circle;
भ्रमन् वै (bhraman vai) — moving indeed; भ्रममाणेषु (bhramamāṇeṣu) — among moving; ऋक्षेषु (ṛkṣeṣu) — stars; विचरति (vicarati) — wanders; असौ (asau) — he

BP 1.53.49

इन्द्रः पूजयते सूर्यमुत्तिष्ठन्तं दिने दिने ।
मध्याह्ने च यमः पश्चादस्तं यान्तमपां पतिः ॥

Indra worships the Sun as he rises each day. At midday Yama worships him, and afterward the lord of waters worships him as he sets.

इन्द्रः (indraḥ) — Indra; पूजयते (pūjayate) — worships; सूर्यम् (sūryam) — Sūrya; उत्तिष्ठन्तम् (uttiṣṭhantam) — rising; दिने दिने (dine dine) — day by day;
मध्याह्ने (madhyāhne) — at midday; च (ca) — and; यमः (yamaḥ) — Yama; पश्चात् (paścāt) — afterward; अस्तम् यान्तम् (astam yāntam) — going to setting; अपाम् पतिः (apām patiḥ) — lord of waters

BP 1.53.50

सोमस्तथार्धरात्रे तु सदा पूजयते रविम् ।
विष्णुर्भवानहं रुद्रः पूजयाम निशाक्षये ॥

Likewise Soma always worships Ravi at midnight. Viṣṇu, Bhava, and I, Rudra, worship him at the end of night.

सोमः (somaḥ) — Soma; तथा (tathā) — likewise; अर्धरात्रे (ardharātre) — at half-night; तु (tu) — but; सदा (sadā) — always; पूजयते (pūjayate) — worships; रविम् (ravim) — Ravi;
विष्णुः (viṣṇuḥ) — Viṣṇu; भवः (bhavaḥ) — Bhava; अहम् (aham) — I; रुद्रः (rudraḥ) — Rudra; पूजयाम (pūjayāma) — worship; निशाक्षये (niśākṣaye) — at the end of night

BP 1.53.51

एवमग्निर्निर्ऋतिश्च वायुरीशान एव च ।
पूजयन्ति क्रमेणैव भ्रममाणं दिवाकरम् ॥
श्रेयोऽर्थं देवशार्दूल सर्वे ब्रह्मादयः सुराः ॥

Thus Fire, Nirṛti, Vāyu, and Īśāna worship the moving Sun in order. For welfare, O tiger among the gods, all the gods beginning with Brahmā worship him.

एवम् (evam) — thus; अग्निः (agniḥ) — Agni; निर्ऋतिः (nirṛtiḥ) — Nirṛti; च (ca) — and; वायुः (vāyuḥ) — Vāyu; ईशानः एव (īśānaḥ eva) — Īśāna likewise; च (ca) — and;
पूजयन्ति (pūjayanti) — worship; क्रमेण एव (krameṇa eva) — in order indeed; भ्रममाणम् (bhramamāṇam) — moving; दिवाकरम् (divākaram) — Divākara;
श्रेयोर्थम् (śreyortham) — for the sake of welfare; देवशार्दूल (devaśārdūla) — O tiger among the gods; सर्वे (sarve) — all; ब्रह्मादयः (brahmādayaḥ) — beginning with Brahmā; सुराः (surāḥ) — gods
