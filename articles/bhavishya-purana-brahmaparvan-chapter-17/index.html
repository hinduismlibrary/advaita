Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 17 — Worship of Brahmā on the First Lunar Day

BP 1.17.1

॥ शतानीक उवाच ॥ ॥
ब्रूहि मे विस्त राद्ब्रह्मन्प्रतिपत्कृत्यमादरात् ॥
ब्रह्मपूजाविधानं च पूजने यच्च वै फलम् ॥ ॥

Śatānīka said: O Brahmin, tell me carefully and fully the rites for the first lunar day, the procedure for worshipping Brahmā, and the fruit of worship.

BP 1.17.2

॥ सुमंतुरुवाच ॥ ॥
शृणुष्वैकमना राजन्कथयाम्येष शांतिदम् ॥
पूर्वमेकार्णवे घोरे नष्टे स्थावरजंगमे ॥ ॥

Sumantu said: Listen with undivided attention, O king; I shall tell you this peace-giving account. Formerly, when the terrible single ocean remained and moving and unmoving beings had perished—

BP 1.17.3

स्वयंभूरभवद्देवः सुरज्येष्ठश्चतुर्मुखः ॥
ससर्ज लोकान्देवांश्च भूतानि विविधानि च ॥ ॥

—the self-born god came to be, eldest of the gods and four-faced. He created the worlds, the gods, and beings of many kinds.

BP 1.17.4

कायेन मनसा वाचा जंगमस्थावराणि च ॥
पिता यः सर्वदेवानां भूतानां च पितामहः ॥ ॥

He is the father of all the gods and the grandfather of all beings, moving and unmoving, through body, mind, and speech.

BP 1.17.5

तस्मादेष सदा पूज्यो यतो लोकगुरुः परः ॥
सृजत्येष जगत्कृत्स्नं पाति संहरते तथा ॥ ॥

Therefore he is always to be worshipped, for he is the supreme teacher of the worlds. He creates this whole universe, preserves it, and likewise withdraws it.

BP 1.17.6

रुद्रोऽस्य मनसो जातो विष्णुर्जातोऽस्य वक्षसः ॥
मुखेभ्यश्चतुरो वेदा वेदांगानि च कृत्स्नशः ॥ ॥

Rudra was born from his mind, Viṣṇu from his chest; from his mouths came the four Vedas and all six auxiliary disciplines.

BP 1.17.7

देवाप्सरसगंधर्वाः सयक्षोरगराक्षसाः ॥
पूजयंति सदा वीर विरिंचिं सुरनायकम् ॥ ॥

Gods, heavenly nymphs, celestial musicians, spirits, serpents, and demons always worship Brahmā, leader of the gods, O hero.

BP 1.17.8

सर्वो ब्रह्ममयो लोकः सर्वं ब्रह्मणि संस्थितम् ॥
तस्मात्समर्चयेद्ब्रह्मन्य इच्छेच्छ्रेय आत्मनः ॥ ॥

The whole world is made of Brahman; everything is established in Brahman. Therefore one who desires his own good should worship Brahmā.

BP 1.17.9

यो न पूजयते भक्त्या सुरज्येष्ठं सुरेश्वरम् ॥
न स नाकस्य राज्यस्य न च मोक्षस्य भाजनम् ॥ ॥

One who does not worship with devotion the eldest of the gods, the lord of the gods, is fit neither for sovereignty in heaven nor for liberation.

BP 1.17.10

यस्तु पूजयते भक्त्या विरिंचिं भुवनेश्वरम् ॥
स नाकराज्यमोक्षेषु क्षिप्रं भवति भाजनम् ॥ ॥

But one who worships with devotion Brahmā, lord of the worlds, quickly becomes fit for sovereignty in heaven and for liberation.

BP 1.17.11

तस्मात्सौम्यमना भूत्वा यावज्जीवं प्रतिज्ञया ॥
अर्चयित्वा सदा देवमापन्नोपि नरो नृप ॥ ॥

Therefore, keeping a tranquil mind and a lifelong vow, a person should always worship the god, even when fallen into distress, O king.

BP 1.17.12

वरं देहपरित्यागो वरं नरकसम्भवः ॥
न त्वेवापूज्य भुंजंति देवं वै पद्मसंभवम् ॥ ॥

It is better to give up the body; it is better even to fall into hell; but one should never eat without first worshipping the lotus-born god.

BP 1.17.13

सदा पूजयते यस्तु वीर भक्त्या पितामहम् ॥
मनुष्यचर्मणा नद्धः स वेधा नात्र संशयः ॥ ॥

Whoever always worships the Grandfather with devotion, O hero, is Brahmā himself clothed in human skin; there is no doubt.

BP 1.17.14

न हि वेधोऽर्चनात्किंचित्पुण्यमभ्यधिकं भवेत् ॥
इति विज्ञाय यत्नेन पूजनी यः सदा विधिः ॥ ॥

No merit is greater than worship of the Creator. Knowing this, one should carefully follow the prescribed worship at all times.

BP 1.17.15

यो ब्रह्माणं द्वेष्टि मोहात्सर्वदेवनमस्कृतम् ॥
नरो नरकगामी स्यात्तस्य संभाषणादपि ॥ ॥

A man who through delusion hates Brahmā, whom all the gods revere, goes to hell even by speaking with him.

BP 1.17.16

ब्रह्मणोर्चां प्रतिष्ठाप्य सर्वयत्नैर्विधानतः ॥
यत्पुण्यं फलमाप्नोति तदेकाग्रमनाः शृणु ॥ ॥

Listen with undivided attention to the meritorious fruit a person gains by installing an image of Brahmā with every effort and according to rule.

BP 1.17.17

सर्वयज्ञतपोदानतीर्थवेदेषु यत्फलम् ॥
तत्फलं कोटिगुणितं लभेद्वेधः प्रतिष्ठया ॥ ॥

Whatever fruit there is in all sacrifices, austerities, gifts, pilgrimages, and the Vedas, one obtains that fruit multiplied ten million times by installing Brahmā.

BP 1.17.18

कञ्जजं स्थापयेद्यस्तु कृत्वा शालां मनोरमाम् ॥
सर्वांगमोदित पुण्यं कोटिकोटिगुणं लभेत् ॥ ॥

Whoever builds a beautiful shrine and installs the lotus-born god obtains merit complete in every way, multiplied ten million times.

BP 1.17.19

मातृजान्पितृजांश्चैव यां चैवोद्वहते स्त्रियम् ॥
कुलैकविंशमुत्तार्य ब्रह्मलोके महीयते ॥ ॥

He lifts up twenty-one family lines: those of his mother, his father, and the woman he marries; he is honoured in Brahmā’s world.

BP 1.17.20

भुक्त्वा तु विपुलान्भोगान्प्रलये समुपस्थिते ॥
ज्ञानयोगं समासाद्य स तत्रैव विमुच्यते ॥ ॥

After enjoying abundant pleasures, when dissolution arrives, he attains the yoga of knowledge and is liberated there.

BP 1.17.21

अथ वा राज्यमाकांक्षेज्जायते संभवांतरे ॥
सप्तद्वीपसमुद्रायाः क्षितेरधिपतिर्भवेत् ॥ ॥

Or, if he desires kingship, he is born again as lord of the earth with its seven islands and oceans.

BP 1.17.22

त्रिसंध्यं यो जपेद्ब्रह्म कृत्वाष्टदल पंकजम् ॥
पौर्णमास्यां प्रतिपदि तस्य पुण्यफलं शृणु ॥ ॥

Hear the fruit of one who recites Brahmā’s name at the three daily junctions after making an eight-petalled lotus on the full-moon and first lunar days.

BP 1.17.23

अनेनैव स देहेन ब्रह्मा संतिष्ठते क्षितौ ॥
पापहा सर्वमर्त्यानां दर्शनात्स्पर्शनादपि ॥ ॥ ॥

In this very body he stands on earth as Brahmā, destroying the sins of all mortals by sight and even by touch.

BP 1.17.24

उद्धृत्य दिवि संस्थाप्य कुलानामेकविंशतिम् ॥
तैः कुलैः सहितो नित्यं मोदते गोगतो नृप ॥ ॥

Having raised twenty-one family lines and established them in heaven, he rejoices always with those families there, O king.

BP 1.17.25

अप्येकवारं यो भक्त्या पूजयेत्पद्म संभवम् ॥
पद्मस्थं मूर्तिमंतं वा ब्रह्मलोकं स गच्छति ॥ ॥

Whoever worships the lotus-born god with devotion even once, whether set upon a lotus or made as an image, goes to Brahmā’s world.

BP 1.17.26

पुण्यक्षयात्क्षितिं प्राप्य भवेत्क्षितिपतिर्महान् ॥
वेदवेदांगतत्त्वज्ञो ब्राह्मणश्चापि जायते ॥ ॥ ॥

When that merit is exhausted and he returns to earth, he becomes a great ruler; he is also born a Brahmin who knows the truth of the Vedas and their auxiliary disciplines.

BP 1.17.27

न तत्तपोभिरत्युग्रैर्न च सर्वैर्महामखैः ॥
गच्छेद्ब्रह्मपुरं दिव्यं मुक्त्त्वा भक्तिपरात्मकान् ॥ ॥

Neither by extremely fierce austerities nor by all the great sacrifices does one reach Brahmā’s divine city, except through supreme devotion.

BP 1.17.28

मृद्दार्वेष्टकशैलैर्वा यः कुर्याद्ब्रह्मणो गृहम् ॥
त्रिःसप्तकुलसंयुक्तो ब्रह्मलोके महीयते ॥ ॥

Whoever builds a house for Brahmā of clay, wood, brick, or stone is honoured in Brahmā’s world together with twenty-one family lines.

BP 1.17.29

मृन्मयात्कोटिगुणितं फलं दार्विष्टकामये ॥
इष्टकाद्विगुण पुण्यं कृते शैलमये गृहे ॥ ॥

The merit of a wooden or brick house is ten million times that of a clay one; a stone house gives twice the merit of a brick one.

BP 1.17.30

क्रीडमानोपि यः कुर्याच्छालां वै ब्रह्मणो नृप ॥
ब्रह्मलोके स लभते विमानं सर्वकामिकम् ॥ ॥

Even whoever builds a shrine for Brahmā in play, O king, obtains in Brahmā’s world an aerial car that grants every desire.

BP 1.17.31

पुष्पमालापरिक्षिप्तं किंकिणीजालभूषितम् ॥
दोलाविक्षेपसंपन्नं घंटाचामरभूषितम् ॥ ॥

It is surrounded by flower garlands, adorned with nets of little bells, furnished with swinging seats, and decorated with large bells and yak-tail fans.

BP 1.17.32

मुक्तादामवितानेन शोभितं सूर्यसुप्रभम् ॥
अप्सरोगणसंकीर्णं सर्वकामसुखप्रदम् ॥ ॥

It shines like the sun, adorned with a canopy of pearl strings, filled with hosts of heavenly nymphs, and granting every desired pleasure.

BP 1.17.33

तत्रोषित्वा महाभोगी क्रीडमानः सदा सुरैः ॥
पुनरागत्य लोकेस्मिन्राजा भवति धार्मिकः ॥ ॥

After dwelling there, rich in pleasures and always sporting with the gods, he returns to this world and becomes a righteous king.

BP 1.17.34

पश्यन्परिहरञ्जन्तून्मृदुपूर्वं महीपते ॥
शनैः संमार्जनं कुर्वंश्चान्द्रायणफलं व्रजेत् ॥ ॥

O ruler of the earth, whoever gently and slowly sweeps while watching for and avoiding living creatures obtains the fruit of a lunar fast.

BP 1.17.35

वस्त्रपूतेन तोयेन यः कुर्यादुपलेपनम् ॥
पश्यन्परिहरञ्जतूंश्चांद्रायणफलं लभेत् ॥ ॥

Whoever plasters the floor with water strained through cloth, while watching for and avoiding living creatures, obtains the fruit of a lunar fast.

BP 1.17.36

नैरंतर्येण यः कुर्यात्पक्षं संमार्जनार्चनम् ॥
युगकोटिशतं साग्रं ब्रह्मलोके महीयते ॥ ॥

Whoever continuously performs sweeping and worship for a fortnight is honoured in Brahmā’s world for more than a hundred million ages.

BP 1.17.37

तस्यांते स चतुर्वेदः सुरूपः प्रियदर्शनः ॥
आढ्यः सर्वगुणोपेतो राजा भवति धार्मिकः ॥ ॥

At the end of that [reward], he becomes learned in the four Vedas, handsome and pleasing to see, wealthy, endowed with every virtue, and a righteous king.

BP 1.17.38

कपटेनापि यः कुर्याद्ब्रह्मशालां सुमानद ॥
संमार्जनादि वै कर्म सोऽपि प्राप्नोति तत्फलम् ॥ ॥

O giver of honour, even whoever builds a shrine of Brahmā by deceit and performs sweeping and the other acts obtains that same fruit.

BP 1.17.39

तावद्धमंति संसारे दुःखशोकभयप्लुताः ॥
न भवंति सुरश्रेष्ठे यावद्रक्ता महीपते ॥ ॥

Living beings wander in the cycle of rebirth, flooded with suffering, sorrow, and fear, only so long as they are not devoted to the highest god, O king.

BP 1.17.40

समासक्तं यथा चित्तं जन्तोर्विषयगोचरे ॥
यद्येवं ब्रह्मणि न्यस्तं को न मुच्येत बन्धनात् ॥ ॥

If a being’s mind were fixed on Brahman as completely as it is attached to sense-objects, who would not be released from bondage?

BP 1.17.41

खण्डस्फुटितसंस्कारं शालायां यः करोति वै ॥
अरामावसथाद्येषु लभते मौक्तिकं फलम् ॥ ॥

Whoever repairs broken or cracked parts of a shrine, garden, rest-house, or the like obtains a pearl-like reward.

BP 1.17.42

नास्ति ब्रह्मसमो देवो नास्ति ब्रह्मसमो गुरुः ॥
नास्ति ब्रह्मसमं ज्ञानं नास्ति वेधःसमं तपः ॥ ॥

There is no god equal to Brahmā, no teacher equal to Brahmā, no knowledge equal to Brahman, and no austerity equal to the Creator.

BP 1.17.43

प्रतिपद्यादिसर्वेषु दिवसेषूत्सवेषु च ॥
पर्वकालेषु पुण्ये पौर्णमास्यां विशेषतः ॥ ॥

On the first lunar day and all other days, at festivals and sacred occasions, and especially on the full moon—

BP 1.17.44

शंखभेर्यादिनिर्घोषैर्महद्भिगेंयसंयुतैः ॥
कुर्यान्नीराजनं देवे सुरज्येष्ठे चतुर्मुखे ॥ ॥

—one should wave lights before the four-faced god, eldest of the gods, to the great sounds of conches and kettledrums accompanied by songs.

BP 1.17.45

यावत्पर्वाणि विधिना कुर्या न्नीराजनं नृप ॥
तावद्युगसहस्राणि ब्रह्मलोके महीयते ॥ ॥

For as many festival occasions as one duly waves lights, O king, for that many thousands of ages one is honoured in Brahmā’s world.

BP 1.17.46

स्नानकाले त्रिसंध्यं तु यः कुर्यान्नृत्यवादनम् ॥
गीतं वा मुखवाद्यं वा तस्य पुण्य फलं शृणु ॥ ॥

Hear the meritorious fruit of whoever arranges dancing and music at the time of bathing and at the three daily junctions, whether song or music made with the mouth.

BP 1.17.47

यावन्त्यहानि कुरुते गेयनृत्यादिवादनम् ॥
तावद्युगसहस्राणि ब्रह्मलोके महीयते ॥ ॥

For as many days as one provides song, dance, and other music, for that many thousands of ages one is honoured in Brahmā’s world.

BP 1.17.48

कपिलापञ्चगव्येन कुशवारियुतेन च ॥
स्नापयेन्मंत्रपूतेन ब्राह्मं स्नानं हि तत्स्मृतम् ॥ ॥

One should bathe Brahmā with the five products of a tawny cow, together with water mixed with sacred grass and purified by mantra; this is called the Brahmā bath.

BP 1.17.49

कपिलापंचगव्येन दधिक्षीरघृतेन च ॥
स्नानं शतगुणं ज्ञेयमितरेषां नराधिप ॥ ॥

A bath with the five products of a tawny cow, together with curd, milk, and ghee, is to be understood as a hundredfold [more meritorious], O king.

BP 1.17.50

देवाग्निकार्यमुद्दिश्य कपिलामाहरेत्सदा ॥
ब्रह्मक्षत्रविशश्चैव न शूद्रस्तु कदाचन ॥ ॥

For divine and fire rites, a tawny cow should always be brought by Brahmins, Kṣatriyas, and Vaiśyas, but never by a Śūdra.

BP 1.17.51

कापिलं यः पिबेच्छूद्रो देवकार्यार्थनिर्मितम् ॥
स पच्येत महाघोरे सुचिरं नरकार्णवे ॥ ॥

A Śūdra who drinks the tawny cow’s offering prepared for a divine rite is cooked for a long time in the dreadful ocean of hell.

BP 1.17.52

वर्षकोटिसहस्रैस्तु यत्पापं समुपार्जितम् ॥
सुरज्येष्ठघृताभ्यंगाद्दहेत्सर्वं न संशयः ॥ ॥

An anointing of the eldest of the gods with ghee burns all the sin accumulated over thousands of crores of years; there is no doubt.

BP 1.17.53

कल्पकोटिसहस्रैस्तु यत्पापं समुपार्जितम् ॥
पितामहघृतस्नानं दहत्यग्निरिवेंधनम् ॥ ।

A ghee bath for the Grandfather burns sin accumulated over thousands of crores of cosmic ages, as fire burns fuel.

BP 1.17.54

घृतस्नानं प्रतिपदि सकृत्कृत्वा तु कांजजम् ॥
कुलैकविंशमुत्तार्य विष्णुलोके मही यते ॥ ॥

By bathing the lotus-born god with ghee once on the first lunar day, one raises up twenty-one family lines and is honoured in Viṣṇu’s world.

BP 1.17.55

अयुतं यो गवां दद्याद्भक्त्या वै वेदपारगे ॥
वस्त्रहेमादियुक्तानां क्षीरस्नानेन यत्फलम् ॥ ॥

The fruit of bathing with milk equals that of devotionally giving ten thousand cows, furnished with clothes, gold, and other things, to one learned in the Veda.

BP 1.17.56

सकृदाज्येन पयसा स्नपयेत्तु यः ॥
गांगेयेन स यानेन याति ब्रह्मसलोकताम् ॥ ॥

Whoever bathes him once with ghee and milk goes in a golden aerial car to Brahmā’s world.

BP 1.17.57

स्नाप्य दध्ना सकृद्वीर कञ्जजं विष्णुमाप्नुयात् ॥
मधुना स्नपयित्वा तु वीरलोके महीयते ॥ ॥

Having bathed the lotus-born god once with curd, O hero, one attains Viṣṇu; having bathed him with honey, one is honoured in the world of heroes.

BP 1.17.58

स्नानमिक्षुरसेनेह यो विरिंचेः समाचरेत् ॥
स याति लोकं सवितस्तु तेजसा भासयन्नभः ॥ ॥

Whoever here performs Brahmā’s bath with sugarcane juice goes to the world of the Sun, lighting the sky with radiance.

BP 1.17.59

शुद्धोदकेन यो भक्त्या स्नापयेत्पद्मसंभवम् ॥
उत्सृज्य पापकलिलं स यात्येव सलोकताम् ॥ ॥

Whoever devotionally bathes the lotus-born god with pure water casts off the mire of sin and surely reaches his world.

BP 1.17.60

वस्त्रपूतेन तोयेन स्नपयेद्यः सकृद्विभुम् ॥
स सर्वकालं तृप्तात्मा लोकवश्यत्वमा प्नुयात् ॥ ॥

Whoever bathes the mighty god once with water strained through cloth remains satisfied at all times and gains influence among people.

BP 1.17.61

सर्वौषधीभिर्यो भक्त्या स्नपयेत्पद्मसंभवम् ॥
कांचनेन विमानेन ब्रह्मलोके महीयते ॥ ॥

Whoever devotionally bathes the lotus-born god with all medicinal herbs is honoured in Brahmā’s world in a golden aerial car.

BP 1.17.62

गन्धचन्दनतोयेन स्नापयेद्योंबुजोद्भवम् ॥
रुद्रलोकमवाप्नोति तेजसा हेमसन्निभः ॥ ॥

Whoever bathes the lotus-born god with water scented with sandalwood reaches Rudra’s world, shining with a radiance like gold.

BP 1.17.63

पाटलोत्पलपद्मानि करवीराणि सर्वदा ॥
स्नानकाले प्रयोज्यानि स्थिराणि सुरभीणि च ॥ ॥

Trumpet flowers, blue lotuses, lotuses, and oleanders, firm and fragrant, should always be used at the time of bathing.

BP 1.17.64

एषामेकतमं स्नानं भक्त्या कृत्वा तु वेधसि ॥
विधूय पापकलिलं विधिलोके महीयते ॥ ॥

Having devoutly performed one of these baths for the Creator, one is cleansed of the mire of sin and honoured in Brahmā’s world.

BP 1.17.65

कर्पूरागरुतोयेन स्नपयेद्यस्तु कञ्जजम् ॥
सर्वपापविशुद्धात्मा ब्रह्मलोके महीयते ॥ ॥

Whoever bathes the lotus-born god with water scented with camphor and agarwood, pure of every sin, is honoured in Brahmā’s world.

BP 1.17.66

गायत्रीशतजप्तेन विमलेनांभसा विभुम् ॥
स्नपयित्वा सकृद्भक्त्या ब्रह्मलोकमवा प्नुयात् ॥ ॥

Having once bathed the mighty god with pure water sanctified by one hundred recitations of the Gāyatrī, one reaches Brahmā’s world.

BP 1.17.67

विभुं शीतांबुना स्नाप्य धारोष्णपयसा ततः ॥
ततः पश्चाद् घृतस्नानं कृत्वा पापैर्विमुच्यते ॥ ॥

Having bathed the mighty god with cool water, then with warm flowing milk, and afterward with ghee, one is freed from sins.

BP 1.17.68

एतत्स्नानत्रयं कृत्वा पूजयित्वा तु भक्तितः ॥
अश्वमेधसहस्रस्य फलं प्राप्नोति मानवः ॥ ॥

By performing these three baths and then worshipping with devotion, a person obtains the fruit of a thousand horse sacrifices.

BP 1.17.69

मृत्कुंभैस्ताम्रजैः कुंभैः स्नानं शतगुणं भवेत् ॥
रौप्ये लक्षोत्तरं प्रोक्तं हैमैः कोटिगुणं भवेत् ॥ ॥

Bathing from copper vessels is a hundredfold [more fruitful] than from clay pots; from silver it is said to be a hundred thousandfold, and from gold ten millionfold.

BP 1.17.70

ब्रह्मणो दर्शनं पुण्यं दर्शनात्स्पर्शनं परम् ॥
स्पर्शनादर्चनं श्रेष्ठं घृतस्नातमतः परम् ॥ ॥

Seeing Brahmā is meritorious; touching is greater than seeing; worship is greater than touching; and bathing him with ghee is greater still.

BP 1.17.71

वाचिकं मानसं पापं घृतस्नानेन देहिनाम् ॥
क्षिणुते पद्मजो यस्मात्तस्मात्स्नानं समाचरेत् ॥ ॥

Because the lotus-born god destroys the verbal and mental sins of embodied beings through the ghee bath, one should perform this bath.

BP 1.17.72

स्नपयित्वार्चयेद्भक्त्या यथा तच्छृणु भारत ॥
शुचिवस्त्रधरः स्नातः कृतन्यासश्च भारत ॥ ॥

Hear, O Bharata, how one should worship after bathing him with devotion: one should wear clean clothes, be bathed, and perform the ritual placements.

BP 1.17.73

चतुर्हस्तं लिखेत्पद्मं चतुर्भागविभागितम् ॥
मध्ये तस्य लिखेच्चक्रं दलैर्द्वादशभिश्चितम् ॥ ॥

One should draw a lotus four cubits across, divided into four parts, and in its middle draw a wheel marked with twelve petals.

BP 1.17.74

सरोजानि ततो न्यस्य अक्षराणि समन्ततः ॥
अक्षरं विहितं चान्यत्पत्रभागे प्रकीर्तितम् ॥ ॥

Then, having placed lotuses, one should place syllables all around; another syllable is prescribed for the section of each petal.

BP 1.17.75

नानावर्णकसंयोगाल्लिखेच्चैवानुपूर्वशः ॥
कृष्णोत्कटं तु मध्यं स्यात्पीतरक्तं तथा परम् ॥ ॥

One should draw them successively by combining various colours: the middle should be deep black, and the next [area] yellow and red.

BP 1.17.76

सितं शुद्धं तु कर्त्तव्यं मध्यभागे तु वर्तुलम् ॥
प्रभाकुण्डलकैर्बाह्यैर्वेष्टयेच्चक्रनायकम् ॥ ॥

The middle should be made a pure white circle; one should surround the lord of the wheel with outer circles of radiance.

BP 1.17.77

एवमालिख्य यत्नेन मूलमन्त्रं ततो न्यसेत् ॥
मूर्ध्नः पादतलं यावत्प्रणवं विन्यसेद्बुधः ॥ ॥

Having carefully drawn it thus, one should place the root mantra. The wise one should place Oṃ from the crown of the head down to the soles of the feet.

BP 1.17.78

नादरूपं न्यसेत्तावद्यावच्छब्दस्य शून्यता ॥
तत्कारं विन्यसेन्मूर्ध्नि सकारं मुखमण्डले ॥ ॥

One should place the form of sound up to where sound becomes void; place the syllable “tat” on the crown and “sa” on the circle of the face.

BP 1.17.79

विकारं कण्ठदेशे तु तुकारं सर्वसंधिषु ॥
वकारं हृदि मध्ये तु रेकारं पार्श्वयोर्द्वयोः ॥ ॥

Place the sound “vi” at the throat, “tu” at all the joints, “va” at the heart’s centre, and “re” on both sides.

BP 1.17.80

णकारं दक्षिणे कुक्षौ यकारं वामसंज्ञके ॥
भकारं कटिनाभिस्थं र्गोकारं जानुपर्वसु ॥ ॥

Place the sound “ṇa” on the right side of the abdomen, “ya” on the left, “bha” at the waist and navel, and “rgo” at the knee joints.

BP 1.17.81

देकारं जंघयोर्न्यस्य वकारं पादपद्मयोः ॥
स्यकारमंगुष्ठयोर्न्यस्य धीकारं चोरसि न्यसेत् ॥ ॥

Place “de” on the shanks, “va” on the lotus-feet, “sya” on the big toes, and “dhī” on the chest.

BP 1.17.82

मकारं जानुदेशे तु हिकारं गुह्यमाश्रितम् ॥
धिकारं हृदये न्यस्य योकारं चौष्ठयो र्न्यसेत् ॥ ॥

Place “ma” at the knees, “hi” in the private region, “dhi” at the heart, and “yo” on the lips.

BP 1.17.83

नकारं नासिकाग्रे तु प्रकारं नेत्रमाश्रितम् ॥
चोकारं तु भुवोर्मध्ये दकारं प्राणमाश्रितम् ॥ ॥

Place “na” at the tip of the nose, “pra” at the eyes, “co” between the eyebrows, and “da” at the breath.

BP 1.17.84

याकारं विन्यसेन्मूर्ध्नि तकारं केशमाश्रितम् ॥
न्यासं कृत्वात्मनो देहे देवे कुर्यात्तथा नृप ॥
सर्वोपचारसंपन्नं कृत्वा सम्यङ् निरीक्षयेत् ॥ ॥

Place “yā” on the crown and “ta” in the hair. Having made these placements on one’s own body, do likewise on the deity, O king, and then contemplate him furnished with every offering.

BP 1.17.85

कुंकुमागुरुकर्पूर चन्दनेन विमिश्रितम् ॥
गन्धतोयमुपस्कृत्य गायत्र्या प्रणवेन च ॥
प्रोक्षयेत्सर्वद्रव्याणि पश्चादर्चनमाचरेत् ॥ ॥

Prepare scented water mixed with saffron, agarwood, camphor, and sandalwood. Consecrate it with the Gāyatrī and Oṃ, sprinkle all the ritual articles, and then perform the worship.

BP 1.17.86

चक्रग्रन्थिषु सर्वासु प्रणवं विनिवेशयेत् ॥
भूयः प्लुतं समुच्चार्य प्रणवं सर्वतोमुखम् ॥ ॥

Place Oṃ at all the joints of the wheel; then again sound the prolonged, all-facing Oṃ.

BP 1.17.87

विन्यसेत्पद्ममध्ये तु पीठनिष्पत्तिहेतवे ॥
आसने पृथिवी ज्ञेया सर्वसत्त्वधरा मता ॥ ॥

Place it in the middle of the lotus to form the seat. The seat is to be understood as Earth, the bearer of all beings.

BP 1.17.88

ह्रस्वोंकारे मता सा तु दीर्घोंकारे तु देवराट् ॥
प्लुतस्तु व्यापयेद्भावं मोक्षदं चामृतात्मकम् ॥ ॥

She is held to be in the short Oṃ; the lord of the gods is in the long Oṃ. The prolonged Oṃ pervades the state that grants liberation and is of the nature of immortality.

BP 1.17.89

यत्नस्थो न निवर्तेत योगी प्राणपरायणः ॥
आवाहनं ततः कुर्यादक्षरेण परेण तु ॥ ॥

The yogin, intent on effort and wholly devoted to the breath, should not turn back. Then he should perform the invocation with the supreme syllable.

BP 1.17.90

आवाह्य तेजोरूपं तु न्यसेन्मन्त्रवरांस्ततः ॥
ततो विभावयेद्देवं पद्मस्थं चतुराननम् ॥ ॥

Having invoked the form of radiance, one should place the highest mantras and then visualize the four-faced god seated upon the lotus.

BP 1.17.91

स्रष्टारं सर्वजगतां विष्णुरुद्रविधानगम् ॥
संभाव्य गन्धपुष्पादि संभारान्क्रमात्सर्वान्प्रकल्पयेत् ॥

Having contemplated him as creator of all worlds and the cause of Viṣṇu’s and Rudra’s arising, one should arrange each requisite in order, beginning with fragrances and flowers.

BP 1.17.92

गायत्रीमुच्चरन्मन्त्रं सर्वकर्माणि कारयेत् ॥ ॥

Using the Gāyatrī as the governing mantra, one should perform all the rites.

BP 1.17.93

पुष्पं धूपं तथा दीपं नैवेद्यं सुमनोहरम् ॥
खंडलड्डुकश्रीवेष्टकासाराशोकवर्तिकाः ॥ ॥

One should offer flowers, incense, a lamp, and pleasing food: sugar pieces, round sweets, flour cakes, sweet rolls, and small cakes—

BP 1.17.94

स्वस्तिकोल्लोपिकादुग्धतिलावेष्टतिलाढिकाः ॥
फलानि चैव पक्वानि लग्नखण्डगुडानि च ॥ ॥

—together with swastika-shaped cakes, small cakes, milk pudding, sesame rolls, sesame sweets, ripe fruits, and pieces of solid molasses.

BP 1.17.95

अन्यांश्च विविधान्दद्यात्पूपानि विविधानि च ॥
एवमादीनि सर्वाणि दापयेचछक्तितो नृप ॥ ॥

One should also give other varied foods and many kinds of cakes. O king, one should provide all these things according to one’s ability.

BP 1.17.96

मूलमन्त्रेण देवस्य ततो देहं विभावयेत् ॥
पूजयेच्चापि विधिना येन तं ते ब्रवीम्यहम् ॥ ॥

One should then picture the deity’s body through the root mantra and worship him according to the procedure, which I shall now tell you.

BP 1.17.97

प्राणायामत्रयं कृत्वा देहसंशोधनाय वै ॥
आवाहयेत्ततोनन्तं धारयन्तं वचः सदा ॥ ॥

After performing breath-control three times to purify the body, one should invoke Ananta, who always bears and supports sacred speech.

BP 1.17.98

ध्यात्वानन्तं ततो रुद्रं पद्मकिंजल्कमध्यगम् ॥
ध्यायेद्विष्णुं ततो देवं न्यसेत्पद्मोदरोद्भवम् ॥ ॥

Having meditated on Ananta, one should meditate on Rudra in the centre of the lotus, then on Viṣṇu, and finally install the lotus-born god.

BP 1.17.99

एवं त्रिदेवतारूढं पद्ममध्येंबुजोद्भवम् ॥
पूजयेन्मूलमन्त्रेण पद्मोदरभवं नृप ॥ ॥

With the three gods thus installed, O king, one should worship through the root mantra the lotus-born Brahmā who stands in the centre of the lotus.

BP 1.17.100

ऋग्वेदं तु यजुर्वेदं सामवेदं च पूजयेत् ॥
ज्ञानवैराग्यमैश्वर्यं धर्मं संपूजयेद्बुधः ॥ ॥

The wise one should worship the Rigveda, Yajurveda, and Samaveda, and also knowledge, detachment, sovereignty, and righteousness.

BP 1.17.101

ईशानादिक्रमाद्राजन्विदिशासु समंततः ॥
शिक्षा कल्पो व्याकरणं निरुक्तं छन्द एव च ॥ ॥

Beginning with Īśāna and proceeding through the intermediate directions all around, O king, one should worship phonetics, ritual rules, grammar, etymology, and metre—

BP 1.17.102

ज्योतिषं च महाबाहो उपवेदाश्च कृत्स्नशः ॥
इतिहासपुराणानि यथायोग्यं यथाक्रमम् ॥ ॥

—as well as astronomy, O mighty-armed, all the subsidiary Vedas, and the histories and Purāṇas, each in its proper place and order.

BP 1.17.103

शिक्षा कल्पो व्याकरणं देवस्य पुरतः सदा ॥
कल्पादयस्ततश्चान्ये दिशासु विदिशासु च ॥ ॥

Phonetics, ritual rules, and grammar are always placed before the god; the others beginning with ritual rules are assigned among the directions and intermediate directions.

BP 1.17.104

महाव्याहृतयः सर्वाः प्रणवेन समन्विताः ॥
पूर्वादिक्रमयोगेन पूजयेद्विधिना नृप ॥ ॥

All the great sacred utterances, joined with Oṃ, should be worshipped according to rule, O king, proceeding in order from the east.

BP 1.17.105

शक्तयो ब्रह्मणस्त्वेता लोकरूपा व्यवस्थिताः ॥
पूजनीयाः प्रयत्नेन मंत्ररूपाः स्थिताः स्वयम् ॥ ॥

These are Brahmā’s powers, established as forms of the worlds and existing of themselves as mantras; they should be worshipped carefully.

BP 1.17.106

अरकांतरसंस्थांश्च षट् समुद्रान्समर्चयेत् ॥
नक्षत्राणि ग्रहाश्चैव राशयश्च विशेषतः ॥
पूज्याः सर्वे यथान्यायं सुराग्रेषु व्यवस्थिताः ॥ ॥

One should worship the six oceans between the wheel-spokes, and especially the lunar mansions, planets, and zodiac signs, each placed properly before the gods.

BP 1.17.107

नागाश्च गरुडश्चैव पूजनीयस्तथाग्रतः ॥
देवता ऋषयश्चैव सहिताः कुलपर्वताः ॥
तत्तेजोनिलयाः सर्वे पूजनीयाः प्रयत्नतः ॥ ॥

Serpents and Garuḍa should likewise be worshipped in front, together with the gods, seers, and ancestral mountains; all are abodes of that radiance and should be carefully honoured.

BP 1.17.108

आचम्य विधिवत्पूर्वं मंत्रपूतेन वारिणा ॥
हृदयादीन्न्यसेदंगान्हृदयादिषु कृत्स्नशः ॥ ॥

Having sipped water purified by mantra according to rule, one should place the heart and the other limb-mantras fully on the heart and the corresponding body parts.

BP 1.17.109

शिखा नेत्रं तथा चर्म अस्त्रं च भरतर्षभ ॥
महेंद्रादिदिशश्चैताः पूजयेद्विधिवन्नृप ॥ ॥

The crest, eyes, armour, and weapon are then placed, O bull among the Bharatas; the guardian deities of the directions, beginning with Indra, should be worshipped according to rule, O king.

BP 1.17.110

हृदयं पुरतः पूज्यं शिरो देवस्य पृष्ठतः ॥
पूर्वं संपूजयेद्देवं मूलमंत्रेण कृत्स्नशः ॥ ॥

The heart should be worshipped in front and the deity’s head behind; first one should worship the god completely with the root mantra.

BP 1.17.111

विसर्जयेद्दर्शयित्वा मुद्रां तु भरतर्षभ ॥
अंकुशं नरशार्दूल ह्याह्वाने कंजमादिशेत् ॥ ॥

At the end, O bull among the Bharatas, one should show the hand gesture of dismissal and release the deity. O tiger among men, the goad gesture is used for summoning, and the lotus gesture is prescribed.

BP 1.17.112

यस्त्वेवं पूजयेद्देवं प्रतिपन्नित्यमेव च ॥
उपोष्य पंचदश्यां तु स याति परमं पदम् ॥ ॥

Whoever worships the god thus on every first lunar day and fasts during the fortnight reaches the supreme state.

BP 1.17.113

॥ सुमंतु रुवाच ॥ ॥
आपो हिष्ठेति मंत्रोऽयं हृदयं परिकीर्तितम् ॥
ऋतं सत्यं शिखा प्रोक्ता उदुत्यं नेत्रमादिशेत् ॥ ॥

Sumantu said: The formula beginning “Waters, indeed, you are” is declared to be the heart; “Truth, reality” is prescribed as the crest, and the formula beginning “The sun has risen” is for the eyes.

BP 1.17.114

चित्रं देवानां मस्तमिति सर्वलोकेषु विश्रुतम् ॥
वर्मणा ते च्छादयामि कवचं समुदाहृतम् ॥ ॥

The formula beginning “A wonder among the gods,” famed throughout all worlds, is the head-mantra; “I cover you with armour” is declared to be the armour.

BP 1.17.115

भूर्भुवः स्वरिति तथा शिरसे परिकीर्तितम् ॥
गायत्रीमूलतंत्रस्तु साधकः सर्वकर्मणाम् ॥ ॥

“Earth, atmosphere, and heaven” is prescribed for the head. The Gāyatrī is the root ritual and the means for all rites.

BP 1.17.116

गायत्र्या पूजयेद्देवमोंकारेणाभिमंत्रितम् ॥
प्रणवेनापरान्सर्वानृग्वेदादीन्प्रपूजयेत् ॥ ॥

One should worship the god with the Gāyatrī after consecrating him with Oṃ; all the others, beginning with the Rigveda, should be worshipped through Oṃ.

BP 1.17.117

आह्वाने पूजने वीर विसर्गे ब्रह्मणस्तथा ॥
गायत्री परमो मंत्रो वेदमाता विभाविनी ॥ ।

In the invocation, worship, and dismissal of Brahmā, O hero, the Gāyatrī, the illuminating Mother of the Veda, is the supreme mantra.

BP 1.17.118

गायत्र्यक्षरतत्त्वैस्तु पूजयेद्यस्तु देवताम् ॥
स गच्छेद्ब्रह्मणः स्थानं दुर्लभं यद्दुरा सदम् ॥ ॥ ॥

Whoever worships the deity through the essential truth of the Gāyatrī’s syllables goes to Brahmā’s abode, difficult to attain and hard to reach.
