Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 17 — Worship of Brahmā on Pratipad

BP 1.17.1

॥ शतानीक उवाच ॥ ॥
ब्रूहि मे विस्त राद्ब्रह्मन्प्रतिपत्कृत्यमादरात् ॥
ब्रह्मपूजाविधानं च पूजने यच्च वै फलम् ॥ ॥

Śatānīka said: ‘O Brahmā-knowing sage, explain to me in detail and with care the duties of Pratipad, the procedure for worshipping Brahmā, and the fruit of that worship.

Word-for-word
शतानीकः उवाच (śatānīkaḥ uvāca) — Śatānīka said;
ब्रूहि मे विस्तरात् (brūhi me vistarāt) — explain to me in detail;
ब्रह्मन् (brahman) — O knower of Brahman;
प्रतिपत्-कृत्यम् (pratipat-kṛtyam) — duty of Pratipad;
आदरात् (ādarāt) — with care;
ब्रह्म-पूजा-विधानम् च (brahma-pūjā-vidhānam ca) — procedure of Brahmā worship;
पूजने यत् च फलम् (pūjane yat ca phalam) — and fruit of worship
Transliteration
|| śatānīka uvāca || ||
brūhi me vista rādbrahmanpratipatkṛtyamādarāt ||
brahmapūjāvidhānaṃ ca pūjane yacca vai phalam||1||

BP 1.17.2

॥ सुमंतुरुवाच ॥ ॥
शृणुष्वैकमना राजन्कथयाम्येष शांतिदम् ॥
पूर्वमेकार्णवे घोरे नष्टे स्थावरजंगमे ॥ ॥

Sumantu said: ‘Listen with one-pointed attention, O king, as I relate this peace-giving teaching. Formerly, when the terrible single ocean prevailed and moving and unmoving beings had perished—

Word-for-word
सुमन्तुः उवाच (sumantuḥ uvāca) — Sumantu said;
शृणुष्व एक-मनाः (śṛṇuṣva eka-manāḥ) — listen one-pointedly;
राजन् (rājan) — O king;
कथयामि (kathayāmi) — I relate;
एष शान्ति-दम् (eṣa śānti-dam) — this peace-giving teaching;
पूर्वम् (pūrvam) — formerly;
एक-अर्णवे घोरे (eka-arṇave ghore) — in terrible single ocean;
नष्टे स्थावर-जङ्गमे (naṣṭe sthāvara-jaṅgame) — when moving and unmoving beings perished
Transliteration
|| sumaṃturuvāca || ||
śṛṇuṣvaikamanā rājankathayāmyeṣa śāṃtidam ||
pūrvamekārṇave ghore naṣṭe sthāvarajaṃgame || 2 ||

BP 1.17.3

स्वयंभूरभवद्देवः सुरज्येष्ठश्चतुर्मुखः ॥
ससर्ज लोकान्देवांश्च भूतानि विविधानि च ॥ ॥

—the self-born god, eldest of the deities and four-faced, came into being. He created the worlds, the gods, and many kinds of living beings.

Word-for-word
स्वयम्भूः अभवत् देवः (svayambhūḥ abhavat devaḥ) — self-born god came into being;
सुर-ज्येष्ठः (sura-jyeṣṭhaḥ) — eldest of gods;
चतुर्मुखः (caturmukhaḥ) — four-faced;
ससर्ज (sasarja) — created;
लोकान् (lokān) — worlds;
देवान् च (devān ca) — and gods;
भूतानि विविधानि च (bhūtāni vividhāni ca) — and diverse beings
Transliteration
svayaṃbhūrabhavaddevaḥ surajyeṣṭhaścaturmukhaḥ ||
sasarja lokāndevāṃśca bhūtāni vividhāni ca ||3||

BP 1.17.4

कायेन मनसा वाचा जंगमस्थावराणि च ॥
पिता यः सर्वदेवानां भूतानां च पितामहः ॥ ॥

Through body, mind, and speech he produced both moving and unmoving things; he is the father of all the gods and the grandfather of all beings.

Word-for-word
कायेन (kāyena) — through body;
मनसा (manasā) — mind;
वाचा (vācā) — speech;
जङ्गम-स्थावराणि च (jaṅgama-sthāvarāṇi ca) — moving and unmoving things;
पिता यः सर्व-देवानाम् (pitā yaḥ sarva-devānām) — father of all gods;
भूतानाम् च पितामहः (bhūtānām ca pitāmahaḥ) — and grandfather of beings
Transliteration
kāyena manasā vācā jaṃgamasthāvarāṇi ca ||
pitā yaḥ sarvadevānāṃ bhūtānāṃ ca pitāmahaḥ || 4 ||

BP 1.17.5

तस्मादेष सदा पूज्यो यतो लोकगुरुः परः ॥
सृजत्येष जगत्कृत्स्नं पाति संहरते तथा ॥ ॥

Therefore he is always to be worshipped, since he is the supreme teacher of the worlds. He creates this entire universe, preserves it, and likewise withdraws it.

Word-for-word
तस्मात् एष सदा पूज्यः (tasmāt eṣa sadā pūjyaḥ) — therefore he always to be worshipped;
यतः लोक-गुरुः परः (yataḥ loka-guruḥ paraḥ) — because supreme world-teacher;
सृजति एष जगत् कृत्स्नम् (sṛjati eṣa jagat kṛtsnam) — creates entire universe;
पाति (pāti) — preserves;
संहरते तथा (saṃharate tathā) — and withdraws
Transliteration
tasmādeṣa sadā pūjyo yato lokaguruḥ paraḥ ||
sṛjatyeṣa jagatkṛtsnaṃ pāti saṃharate tathā ||5||

BP 1.17.6

रुद्रोऽस्य मनसो जातो विष्णुर्जातोऽस्य वक्षसः ॥
मुखेभ्यश्चतुरो वेदा वेदांगानि च कृत्स्नशः ॥ ॥

Rudra was born from his mind, Viṣṇu from his chest, and from his four mouths came the four Vedas together with all the Vedāṅgas.

Word-for-word
रुद्रः अस्य मनसः जातः (rudraḥ asya manasaḥ jātaḥ) — Rudra born from his mind;
विष्णुः जातः अस्य वक्षसः (viṣṇuḥ jātaḥ asya vakṣasaḥ) — Viṣṇu born from his chest;
मुखेभ्यः (mukhebhyaḥ) — from mouths;
चत्वारः वेदाः (catvāraḥ vedāḥ) — four Vedas;
वेदाङ्गानि च कृत्स्नशः (vedāṅgāni ca kṛtsnaśaḥ) — and all Vedāṅgas
Transliteration
rudro'sya manaso jāto viṣṇurjāto'sya vakṣasaḥ ||
mukhebhyaścaturo vedā vedāṃgāni ca kṛtsnaśaḥ || 6 ||

BP 1.17.7

देवाप्सरसगंधर्वाः सयक्षोरगराक्षसाः ॥
पूजयंति सदा वीर विरिंचिं सुरनायकम् ॥ ॥

Gods, Apsarases, Gandharvas, Yakṣas, serpents, and Rākṣasas continually worship Viriñci, the leader of the gods, O hero.

Word-for-word
देव (deva) — gods;
अप्सरसः (apsarasaḥ) — Apsarases;
गन्धर्वाः (gandharvāḥ) — Gandharvas;
स-यक्ष-उरग-राक्षसाः (sa-yakṣa-uraga-rākṣasāḥ) — with Yakṣas, serpents and Rākṣasas;
पूजयन्ति सदा (pūjayanti sadā) — continually worship;
वीर (vīra) — O hero;
विरिञ्चिम् (viriñcim) — Viriñci;
सुर-नायकम् (sura-nāyakam) — leader of gods
Transliteration
devāpsarasagaṃdharvāḥ sayakṣoragarākṣasāḥ ||
pūjayaṃti sadā vīra viriṃciṃ suranāyakam ||7||

BP 1.17.8

सर्वो ब्रह्ममयो लोकः सर्वं ब्रह्मणि संस्थितम् ॥
तस्मात्समर्चयेद्ब्रह्मन्य इच्छेच्छ्रेय आत्मनः ॥ ॥

The whole world is pervaded by Brahman, and everything rests in Brahman. Therefore one who desires one’s own highest welfare should worship Brahmā.

Word-for-word
सर्वः ब्रह्म-मयः लोकः (sarvaḥ brahma-mayaḥ lokaḥ) — whole world pervaded by Brahman;
सर्वम् ब्रह्मणि संस्थितम् (sarvam brahmaṇi saṃsthitam) — everything rests in Brahman;
तस्मात् समर्चयेत् ब्रह्माणम् (tasmāt samarcayet brahmāṇam) — therefore should worship Brahmā;
यः इच्छेत् श्रेयः आत्मनः (yaḥ icchet śreyaḥ ātmanaḥ) — whoever desires own welfare
Transliteration
sarvo brahmamayo lokaḥ sarvaṃ brahmaṇi saṃsthitam||
tasmātsamarcayedbrahmanya icchecchreya ātmanaḥ || 8 ||

BP 1.17.9

यो न पूजयते भक्त्या सुरज्येष्ठं सुरेश्वरम् ॥
न स नाकस्य राज्यस्य न च मोक्षस्य भाजनम् ॥ ॥

One who does not worship with devotion the eldest and lord of the gods becomes fit neither for sovereignty in heaven nor for liberation.

Word-for-word
यः न पूजयते भक्त्या (yaḥ na pūjayate bhaktyā) — one who does not worship with devotion;
सुर-ज्येष्ठम् (sura-jyeṣṭham) — eldest of gods;
सुर-ईश्वरम् (sura-īśvaram) — lord of gods;
न सः (na saḥ) — he is not;
नाकस्य राज्यस्य (nākasya rājyasya) — of heavenly sovereignty;
न च मोक्षस्य भाजनम् (na ca mokṣasya bhājanam) — nor recipient of liberation
Transliteration
yo na pūjayate bhaktyā surajyeṣṭhaṃ sureśvaram ||
na sa nākasya rājyasya na ca mokṣasya bhājanam || 9 ||

BP 1.17.10

यस्तु पूजयते भक्त्या विरिंचिं भुवनेश्वरम् ॥
स नाकराज्यमोक्षेषु क्षिप्रं भवति भाजनम् ॥ ॥

But one who worships with devotion Viriñci, lord of the worlds, quickly becomes qualified for both heavenly sovereignty and liberation.

Word-for-word
यः तु पूजयते भक्त्या (yaḥ tu pūjayate bhaktyā) — but one who worships with devotion;
विरिञ्चिम् (viriñcim) — Viriñci;
भुवन-ईश्वरम् (bhuvana-īśvaram) — lord of worlds;
सः (saḥ) — he;
नाक-राज्य-मोक्षेषु (nāka-rājya-mokṣeṣu) — for heavenly sovereignty and liberation;
क्षिप्रम् भवति भाजनम् (kṣipram bhavati bhājanam) — quickly becomes qualified
Transliteration
yastu pūjayate bhaktyā viriṃciṃ bhuvaneśvaram ||
sa nākarājyamokṣeṣu kṣipraṃ bhavati bhājanam || 1.17.10 ||

BP 1.17.11

तस्मात्सौम्यमना भूत्वा यावज्जीवं प्रतिज्ञया ॥
अर्चयित्वा सदा देवमापन्नोपि नरो नृप ॥ ॥

Therefore, becoming serene in mind and maintaining a lifelong vow, a person should always worship the god even when overtaken by adversity, O king.

Word-for-word
तस्मात् (tasmāt) — therefore;
सौम्य-मनाः भूत्वा (saumya-manāḥ bhūtvā) — becoming serene-minded;
यावत्-जीवम् प्रतिज्ञया (yāvat-jīvam pratijñayā) — by lifelong vow;
अर्चयित्वा सदा देवम् (arcayitvā sadā devam) — always worshipping god;
आपन्नः अपि नरः (āpannaḥ api naraḥ) — person even in adversity;
नृप (nṛpa) — O king
Transliteration
tasmātsaumyamanā bhūtvā yāvajjīvaṃ pratijñayā ||
arcayitvā sadā devamāpannopi naro nṛpa || 11 ||

BP 1.17.12

वरं देहपरित्यागो वरं नरकसम्भवः ॥
न त्वेवापूज्य भुंजंति देवं वै पद्मसंभवम् ॥ ॥

Better to abandon the body, and better even to fall into hell, than to eat without first worshipping the lotus-born god.

Word-for-word
वरम् देह-परित्यागः (varam deha-parityāgaḥ) — better abandonment of body;
वरम् नरक-सम्भवः (varam naraka-sambhavaḥ) — better birth in hell;
न तु एव (na tu eva) — but never;
अपूज्य भुञ्जन्ति (apūjya bhuñjanti) — eat without worshipping;
देवम् वै पद्म-सम्भवम् (devam vai padma-sambhavam) — lotus-born god
Transliteration
varaṃ dehaparityāgo varaṃ narakasambhavaḥ ||
na tvevāpūjya bhuṃjaṃti devaṃ vai padmasaṃbhavam || 12 ||

BP 1.17.13

सदा पूजयते यस्तु वीर भक्त्या पितामहम् ॥
मनुष्यचर्मणा नद्धः स वेधा नात्र संशयः ॥ ॥

O hero, one who always worships the Grandfather with devotion is Brahmā himself clothed in human skin—of this there is no doubt.

Word-for-word
सदा पूजयते यः (sadā pūjayate yaḥ) — one who always worships;
वीर (vīra) — O hero;
भक्त्या पितामहम् (bhaktyā pitāmaham) — Grandfather with devotion;
मनुष्य-चर्मणा नद्धः (manuṣya-carmaṇā naddhaḥ) — clothed in human skin;
सः वेधाः (saḥ vedhāḥ) — he is Brahmā;
न अत्र संशयः (na atra saṃśayaḥ) — no doubt
Transliteration
sadā pūjayate yastu vīra bhaktyā pitāmaham ||
manuṣyacarmaṇā naddhaḥ sa vedhā nātra saṃśayaḥ||13||

BP 1.17.14

न हि वेधोऽर्चनात्किंचित्पुण्यमभ्यधिकं भवेत् ॥
इति विज्ञाय यत्नेन पूजनी यः सदा विधिः ॥ ॥

No merit is greater than worship of Vedhas. Knowing this, one should carefully follow the prescribed procedure of worship at all times.

Word-for-word
न हि वेधः-अर्चनात् (na hi vedhaḥ-arcanāt) — than worship of Vedhas;
किञ्चित् पुण्यम् अभ्यधिकम् (kiñcit puṇyam abhyadhikam) — no merit is greater;
इति विज्ञाय (iti vijñāya) — knowing thus;
यत्नेन (yatnena) — carefully;
पूजनीयः सदा विधिः (pūjanīyaḥ sadā vidhiḥ) — procedure of worship always to be followed
Transliteration
na hi vedho'rcanātkiṃcitpuṇyamabhyadhikaṃ bhavet ||
iti vijñāya yatnena pūjanī yaḥ sadā vidhiḥ || 14 ||

BP 1.17.15

यो ब्रह्माणं द्वेष्टि मोहात्सर्वदेवनमस्कृतम् ॥
नरो नरकगामी स्यात्तस्य संभाषणादपि ॥ ॥

A person who through delusion hates Brahmā, whom all the gods revere, goes to hell; the source warns even against intimate association with such a person.

Word-for-word
यः ब्रह्माणम् द्वेष्टि (yaḥ brahmāṇam dveṣṭi) — one who hates Brahmā;
मोहात् (mohāt) — from delusion;
सर्व-देव-नमस्कृतम् (sarva-deva-namaskṛtam) — revered by all gods;
नरः नरक-गामी स्यात् (naraḥ naraka-gāmī syāt) — person goes to hell;
तस्य सम्भाषणात् अपि (tasya sambhāṣaṇāt api) — even from conversation with him
Transliteration
yo brahmāṇaṃ dveṣṭi mohātsarvadevanamaskṛtam ||
naro narakagāmī syāttasya saṃbhāṣaṇādapi || 15 ||

BP 1.17.16

ब्रह्मणोर्चां प्रतिष्ठाप्य सर्वयत्नैर्विधानतः ॥
यत्पुण्यं फलमाप्नोति तदेकाग्रमनाः शृणु ॥ ॥

Listen with one-pointed attention to the meritorious fruit obtained by installing an image of Brahmā with every effort and according to the prescribed procedure.

Word-for-word
ब्रह्मणः अर्चाम् प्रतिष्ठाप्य (brahmaṇaḥ arcām pratiṣṭhāpya) — installing image of Brahmā;
सर्व-यत्नैः (sarva-yatnaiḥ) — with every effort;
विधानतः (vidhānataḥ) — according to procedure;
यत् पुण्यम् फलम् आप्नोति (yat puṇyam phalam āpnoti) — what meritorious fruit one obtains;
तत् एक-अग्र-मनाः शृणु (tat eka-agra-manāḥ śṛṇu) — hear that one-pointedly
Transliteration
brahmaṇorcāṃ pratiṣṭhāpya sarvayatnairvidhānataḥ ||
yatpuṇyaṃ phalamāpnoti tadekāgramanāḥ śṛṇu || 16 ||

BP 1.17.17

सर्वयज्ञतपोदानतीर्थवेदेषु यत्फलम् ॥
तत्फलं कोटिगुणितं लभेद्वेधः प्रतिष्ठया ॥ ॥

Whatever fruit is found in all sacrifices, austerities, gifts, pilgrimages, and Vedic study is obtained multiplied ten millionfold through the installation of Vedhas.

Word-for-word
सर्व-यज्ञ-तपः-दान-तीर्थ-वेदेषु (sarva-yajña-tapaḥ-dāna-tīrtha-vedeṣu) — in all sacrifices, austerities, gifts, pilgrimages and Vedas;
यत् फलम् (yat phalam) — whatever fruit;
तत् फलम् कोटि-गुणितम् (tat phalam koṭi-guṇitam) — that fruit multiplied by ten million;
लभेत् (labhet) — one obtains;
वेधः-प्रतिष्ठया (vedhaḥ-pratiṣṭhayā) — by installing Vedhas
Transliteration
sarvayajñatapodānatīrthavedeṣu yatphalam ||
tatphalaṃ koṭiguṇitaṃ labhedvedhaḥ pratiṣṭhayā || 17 ||

BP 1.17.18

कञ्जजं स्थापयेद्यस्तु कृत्वा शालां मनोरमाम् ॥
सर्वांगमोदित पुण्यं कोटिकोटिगुणं लभेत् ॥ ॥

One who builds a beautiful shrine and installs the lotus-born god obtains the merit praised in all the scriptures multiplied by millions upon millions.

Word-for-word
कञ्ज-जम् स्थापयेत् यः (kañja-jam sthāpayet yaḥ) — one who installs lotus-born;
कृत्वा शालाम् मनोरमाम् (kṛtvā śālām manoramām) — building beautiful shrine;
सर्व-आगम-उदितम् पुण्यम् (sarva-āgama-uditam puṇyam) — merit praised in all scriptures;
कोटि-कोटि-गुणम् लभेत् (koṭi-koṭi-guṇam labhet) — obtains multiplied millions upon millions
Transliteration
kañjajaṃ sthāpayedyastu kṛtvā śālāṃ manoramām ||
sarvāṃgamodita puṇyaṃ koṭikoṭiguṇaṃ labhet || 18 ||

BP 1.17.19

मातृजान्पितृजांश्चैव यां चैवोद्वहते स्त्रियम् ॥
कुलैकविंशमुत्तार्य ब्रह्मलोके महीयते ॥ ॥

He delivers twenty-one generations from his mother’s line, his father’s line, and the line of the woman he marries, and is honoured in Brahmā’s world.

Word-for-word
मातृ-जान् (mātṛ-jān) — those born in mother’s line;
पितृ-जान् च एव (pitṛ-jān ca eva) — and father’s line;
याम् च एव उद्वहते स्त्रियम् (yām ca eva udvahate striyam) — and woman whom he marries;
कुल-एक-विंशम् उत्तार्य (kula-eka-viṃśam uttārya) — delivering twenty-one generations;
ब्रह्म-लोके महीयते (brahma-loke mahīyate) — is honoured in Brahmā’s world

गायत्र्या पूजयेद्देवमोंकारेणाभिमंत्रितम् ॥
प्रणवेनापरान्सर्वानृग्वेदादीन्प्रपूजयेत् ॥ ॥

One should worship the god through the Gāyatrī after consecrating him with Oṃ; all the others beginning with the Ṛgveda should be worshipped through the praṇava.

BP 1.17.117

आह्वाने पूजने वीर विसर्गे ब्रह्मणस्तथा ॥
गायत्री परमो मंत्रो वेदमाता विभाविनी ॥ ।

In the invocation, worship, and dismissal of Brahmā, O hero, the Gāyatrī—the illuminating Mother of the Veda—is the supreme mantra.

BP 1.17.118

गायत्र्यक्षरतत्त्वैस्तु पूजयेद्यस्तु देवताम् ॥
स गच्छेद्ब्रह्मणः स्थानं दुर्लभं यद्दुरा सदम् ॥ ॥ ॥

Whoever worships the deity through the essential reality of the Gāyatrī’s syllables goes to Brahmā’s abode, difficult to attain and hard to approach.
