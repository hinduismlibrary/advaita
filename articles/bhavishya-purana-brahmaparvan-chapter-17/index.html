Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 17 — Worship of Brahmā on Pratipad

BP 1.17.1

॥ शतानीक उवाच ॥ ॥
ब्रूहि मे विस्त राद्ब्रह्मन्प्रतिपत्कृत्यमादरात् ॥
ब्रह्मपूजाविधानं च पूजने यच्च वै फलम् ॥ ॥

Śatānīka said: ‘O Brahmā-knowing sage, explain to me in detail and with care the duties of Pratipad, the procedure for worshipping Brahmā, and the fruit of that worship.

BP 1.17.2

॥ सुमंतुरुवाच ॥ ॥
शृणुष्वैकमना राजन्कथयाम्येष शांतिदम् ॥
पूर्वमेकार्णवे घोरे नष्टे स्थावरजंगमे ॥ ॥

Sumantu said: ‘Listen with one-pointed attention, O king, as I relate this peace-giving teaching. Formerly, when the terrible single ocean prevailed and moving and unmoving beings had perished—

BP 1.17.3

स्वयंभूरभवद्देवः सुरज्येष्ठश्चतुर्मुखः ॥
ससर्ज लोकान्देवांश्च भूतानि विविधानि च ॥ ॥

—the self-born god, eldest of the deities and four-faced, came into being. He created the worlds, the gods, and many kinds of living beings.

BP 1.17.4

कायेन मनसा वाचा जंगमस्थावराणि च ॥
पिता यः सर्वदेवानां भूतानां च पितामहः ॥ ॥

Through body, mind, and speech he produced both moving and unmoving things; he is the father of all the gods and the grandfather of all beings.

BP 1.17.5

तस्मादेष सदा पूज्यो यतो लोकगुरुः परः ॥
सृजत्येष जगत्कृत्स्नं पाति संहरते तथा ॥ ॥

Therefore he is always to be worshipped, since he is the supreme teacher of the worlds. He creates this entire universe, preserves it, and likewise withdraws it.

BP 1.17.6

रुद्रोऽस्य मनसो जातो विष्णुर्जातोऽस्य वक्षसः ॥
मुखेभ्यश्चतुरो वेदा वेदांगानि च कृत्स्नशः ॥ ॥

Rudra was born from his mind, Viṣṇu from his chest, and from his four mouths came the four Vedas together with all the Vedāṅgas.

BP 1.17.7

देवाप्सरसगंधर्वाः सयक्षोरगराक्षसाः ॥
पूजयंति सदा वीर विरिंचिं सुरनायकम् ॥ ॥

Gods, Apsarases, Gandharvas, Yakṣas, serpents, and Rākṣasas continually worship Viriñci, the leader of the gods, O hero.

BP 1.17.8

सर्वो ब्रह्ममयो लोकः सर्वं ब्रह्मणि संस्थितम् ॥
तस्मात्समर्चयेद्ब्रह्मन्य इच्छेच्छ्रेय आत्मनः ॥ ॥

The whole world is pervaded by Brahman, and everything rests in Brahman. Therefore one who desires one’s own highest welfare should worship Brahmā.

BP 1.17.9

यो न पूजयते भक्त्या सुरज्येष्ठं सुरेश्वरम् ॥
न स नाकस्य राज्यस्य न च मोक्षस्य भाजनम् ॥ ॥

One who does not worship with devotion the eldest and lord of the gods becomes fit neither for sovereignty in heaven nor for liberation.

BP 1.17.10

यस्तु पूजयते भक्त्या विरिंचिं भुवनेश्वरम् ॥
स नाकराज्यमोक्षेषु क्षिप्रं भवति भाजनम् ॥ ॥

But one who worships with devotion Viriñci, lord of the worlds, quickly becomes qualified for both heavenly sovereignty and liberation.

BP 1.17.11

तस्मात्सौम्यमना भूत्वा यावज्जीवं प्रतिज्ञया ॥
अर्चयित्वा सदा देवमापन्नोपि नरो नृप ॥ ॥

Therefore, becoming serene in mind and maintaining a lifelong vow, a person should always worship the god even when overtaken by adversity, O king.

BP 1.17.12

वरं देहपरित्यागो वरं नरकसम्भवः ॥
न त्वेवापूज्य भुंजंति देवं वै पद्मसंभवम् ॥ ॥

Better to abandon the body, and better even to fall into hell, than to eat without first worshipping the lotus-born god.

BP 1.17.13

सदा पूजयते यस्तु वीर भक्त्या पितामहम् ॥
मनुष्यचर्मणा नद्धः स वेधा नात्र संशयः ॥ ॥

O hero, one who always worships the Grandfather with devotion is Brahmā himself clothed in human skin—of this there is no doubt.

BP 1.17.14

न हि वेधोऽर्चनात्किंचित्पुण्यमभ्यधिकं भवेत् ॥
इति विज्ञाय यत्नेन पूजनी यः सदा विधिः ॥ ॥

No merit is greater than worship of Vedhas. Knowing this, one should carefully follow the prescribed procedure of worship at all times.

BP 1.17.15

यो ब्रह्माणं द्वेष्टि मोहात्सर्वदेवनमस्कृतम् ॥
नरो नरकगामी स्यात्तस्य संभाषणादपि ॥ ॥

A person who through delusion hates Brahmā, whom all the gods revere, goes to hell; the source warns even against intimate association with such a person.

BP 1.17.16

ब्रह्मणोर्चां प्रतिष्ठाप्य सर्वयत्नैर्विधानतः ॥
यत्पुण्यं फलमाप्नोति तदेकाग्रमनाः शृणु ॥ ॥

Listen with one-pointed attention to the meritorious fruit obtained by installing an image of Brahmā with every effort and according to the prescribed procedure.

BP 1.17.17

सर्वयज्ञतपोदानतीर्थवेदेषु यत्फलम् ॥
तत्फलं कोटिगुणितं लभेद्वेधः प्रतिष्ठया ॥ ॥

Whatever fruit is found in all sacrifices, austerities, gifts, pilgrimages, and Vedic study is obtained multiplied ten millionfold through the installation of Vedhas.

BP 1.17.18

कञ्जजं स्थापयेद्यस्तु कृत्वा शालां मनोरमाम् ॥
सर्वांगमोदित पुण्यं कोटिकोटिगुणं लभेत् ॥ ॥

One who builds a beautiful shrine and installs the lotus-born god obtains the merit praised in all the scriptures multiplied by millions upon millions.

BP 1.17.19

मातृजान्पितृजांश्चैव यां चैवोद्वहते स्त्रियम् ॥
कुलैकविंशमुत्तार्य ब्रह्मलोके महीयते ॥ ॥

He delivers twenty-one generations from his mother’s line, his father’s line, and the line of the woman he marries, and is honoured in Brahmā’s world.

BP 1.17.20

भुक्त्वा तु विपुलान्भोगान्प्रलये समुपस्थिते ॥
ज्ञानयोगं समासाद्य स तत्रैव विमुच्यते ॥ ॥

After enjoying abundant celestial pleasures, when cosmic dissolution arrives he attains the yoga of knowledge and is liberated in that very realm.

BP 1.17.21

अथ वा राज्यमाकांक्षेज्जायते संभवांतरे ॥
सप्तद्वीपसमुद्रायाः क्षितेरधिपतिर्भवेत् ॥ ॥

Alternatively, if he desires sovereignty, in another birth he becomes lord of the earth with its seven continents and surrounding oceans.

BP 1.17.22

त्रिसंध्यं यो जपेद्ब्रह्म कृत्वाष्टदल पंकजम् ॥
पौर्णमास्यां प्रतिपदि तस्य पुण्यफलं शृणु ॥ ॥

Hear the meritorious fruit of one who, at the three junctions of the day, chants the Brahmā formula after drawing an eight-petalled lotus on the full moon and Pratipad observance.

BP 1.17.23

अनेनैव स देहेन ब्रह्मा संतिष्ठते क्षितौ ॥
पापहा सर्वमर्त्यानां दर्शनात्स्पर्शनादपि ॥ ॥ ॥

In this very body he becomes Brahmā present upon the earth, destroying the sins of all mortals merely through sight and even through touch.

BP 1.17.24

उद्धृत्य दिवि संस्थाप्य कुलानामेकविंशतिम् ॥
तैः कुलैः सहितो नित्यं मोदते गोगतो नृप ॥ ॥

Having raised twenty-one generations and established them in heaven, he continually rejoices together with those families in the celestial realm, O king.

BP 1.17.25

अप्येकवारं यो भक्त्या पूजयेत्पद्म संभवम् ॥
पद्मस्थं मूर्तिमंतं वा ब्रह्मलोकं स गच्छति ॥ ॥

Whoever worships the lotus-born god with devotion even once—whether represented upon a lotus or in an embodied image—goes to Brahmā’s world.

BP 1.17.26

पुण्यक्षयात्क्षितिं प्राप्य भवेत्क्षितिपतिर्महान् ॥
वेदवेदांगतत्त्वज्ञो ब्राह्मणश्चापि जायते ॥ ॥ ॥

When that merit is exhausted and he returns to earth, he becomes a great ruler; he is also born as a brāhmaṇa who knows the truth of the Vedas and Vedāṅgas.

BP 1.17.27

न तत्तपोभिरत्युग्रैर्न च सर्वैर्महामखैः ॥
गच्छेद्ब्रह्मपुरं दिव्यं मुक्त्त्वा भक्तिपरात्मकान् ॥ ॥

Neither through the fiercest austerities nor through all the great sacrifices does one reach the divine city of Brahmā apart from those whose inner nature is wholly devoted.

BP 1.17.28

मृद्दार्वेष्टकशैलैर्वा यः कुर्याद्ब्रह्मणो गृहम् ॥
त्रिःसप्तकुलसंयुक्तो ब्रह्मलोके महीयते ॥ ॥

One who builds a house for Brahmā from clay, wood, brick, or stone is honoured in Brahmā’s world together with twenty-one generations.

BP 1.17.29

मृन्मयात्कोटिगुणितं फलं दार्विष्टकामये ॥
इष्टकाद्विगुण पुण्यं कृते शैलमये गृहे ॥ ॥

A shrine of wood or brick yields fruit ten million times that of one made from clay; a stone shrine produces twice the merit of one made from brick.

BP 1.17.30

क्रीडमानोपि यः कुर्याच्छालां वै ब्रह्मणो नृप ॥
ब्रह्मलोके स लभते विमानं सर्वकामिकम् ॥ ॥

O king, even one who builds a shrine for Brahmā in play obtains in Brahmā’s world an aerial vehicle that fulfils every desire.

BP 1.17.31

पुष्पमालापरिक्षिप्तं किंकिणीजालभूषितम् ॥
दोलाविक्षेपसंपन्नं घंटाचामरभूषितम् ॥ ॥

It is encircled by flower garlands, adorned with networks of little bells, furnished with swinging seats, and decorated with large bells and yak-tail fans.

BP 1.17.32

मुक्तादामवितानेन शोभितं सूर्यसुप्रभम् ॥
अप्सरोगणसंकीर्णं सर्वकामसुखप्रदम् ॥ ॥

Brilliant as the sun beneath a canopy strung with pearl garlands, crowded with hosts of Apsarases, it bestows the pleasure of every desire.

BP 1.17.33

तत्रोषित्वा महाभोगी क्रीडमानः सदा सुरैः ॥
पुनरागत्य लोकेस्मिन्राजा भवति धार्मिकः ॥ ॥

After dwelling there as one of great enjoyment and continually sporting with the gods, he returns to this world and becomes a ruler devoted to dharma.

BP 1.17.34

पश्यन्परिहरञ्जन्तून्मृदुपूर्वं महीपते ॥
शनैः संमार्जनं कुर्वंश्चान्द्रायणफलं व्रजेत् ॥ ॥

O ruler of the earth, one who sweeps gently and slowly while watching for and avoiding living creatures obtains the fruit of a Cāndrāyaṇa observance.

BP 1.17.35

वस्त्रपूतेन तोयेन यः कुर्यादुपलेपनम् ॥
पश्यन्परिहरञ्जतूंश्चांद्रायणफलं लभेत् ॥ ॥

One who applies a cleansing coating with water strained through cloth, while watching for and avoiding living creatures, obtains the fruit of a Cāndrāyaṇa.

BP 1.17.36

नैरंतर्येण यः कुर्यात्पक्षं संमार्जनार्चनम् ॥
युगकोटिशतं साग्रं ब्रह्मलोके महीयते ॥ ॥

One who continuously performs sweeping and worship for a fortnight is honoured in Brahmā’s world for more than a hundred million ages.

BP 1.17.37

तस्यांते स चतुर्वेदः सुरूपः प्रियदर्शनः ॥
आढ्यः सर्वगुणोपेतो राजा भवति धार्मिकः ॥ ॥

At the end of that reward he becomes learned in the four Vedas, handsome and pleasing in appearance, wealthy, endowed with every virtue, and a ruler devoted to dharma.

BP 1.17.38

कपटेनापि यः कुर्याद्ब्रह्मशालां सुमानद ॥
संमार्जनादि वै कर्म सोऽपि प्राप्नोति तत्फलम् ॥ ॥

O giver of honour, even one who performs sweeping and related work in a shrine of Brahmā from an imperfect or pretended motive nevertheless obtains the stated fruit.

BP 1.17.39

तावद्धमंति संसारे दुःखशोकभयप्लुताः ॥
न भवंति सुरश्रेष्ठे यावद्रक्ता महीपते ॥ ॥

Living beings continue to wander in saṃsāra, overwhelmed by suffering, sorrow, and fear, only so long as they remain without loving attachment to the foremost god, O ruler of the earth.

BP 1.17.40

समासक्तं यथा चित्तं जन्तोर्विषयगोचरे ॥
यद्येवं ब्रह्मणि न्यस्तं को न मुच्येत बन्धनात् ॥ ॥

If a living being’s mind were fixed upon Brahman as completely as it is attached to the objects of the senses, who would not be released from bondage?

BP 1.17.41

खण्डस्फुटितसंस्कारं शालायां यः करोति वै ॥
अरामावसथाद्येषु लभते मौक्तिकं फलम् ॥ ॥

One who repairs broken or cracked parts of a shrine, garden, rest-house, or similar sacred facility obtains a precious, pearl-like fruit.

BP 1.17.42

नास्ति ब्रह्मसमो देवो नास्ति ब्रह्मसमो गुरुः ॥
नास्ति ब्रह्मसमं ज्ञानं नास्ति वेधःसमं तपः ॥ ॥

There is no god equal to Brahmā, no teacher equal to Brahmā, no knowledge equal to Brahman, and no austerity equal to devotion to Vedhas.

BP 1.17.43

प्रतिपद्यादिसर्वेषु दिवसेषूत्सवेषु च ॥
पर्वकालेषु पुण्ये पौर्णमास्यां विशेषतः ॥ ॥

On Pratipad and all other days, at festivals and sacred junctures, and especially on the full moon—

BP 1.17.44

शंखभेर्यादिनिर्घोषैर्महद्भिगेंयसंयुतैः ॥
कुर्यान्नीराजनं देवे सुरज्येष्ठे चतुर्मुखे ॥ ॥

—one should perform the waving-of-lights ceremony before the four-faced god, eldest of the deities, with the great sounds of conches and kettledrums accompanied by song.

BP 1.17.45

यावत्पर्वाणि विधिना कुर्या न्नीराजनं नृप ॥
तावद्युगसहस्राणि ब्रह्मलोके महीयते ॥ ॥

For as many festival occasions as one properly performs this waving-of-lights ceremony, O king, one is honoured in Brahmā’s world for that many thousands of ages.

BP 1.17.46

स्नानकाले त्रिसंध्यं तु यः कुर्यान्नृत्यवादनम् ॥
गीतं वा मुखवाद्यं वा तस्य पुण्य फलं शृणु ॥ ॥

Hear the meritorious fruit of one who, at the bathing rite and at the three daily junctions, arranges dance, instrumental music, song, or music made with the voice.

BP 1.17.47

यावन्त्यहानि कुरुते गेयनृत्यादिवादनम् ॥
तावद्युगसहस्राणि ब्रह्मलोके महीयते ॥ ॥

For as many days as one provides singing, dancing, and instrumental music, one is honoured in Brahmā’s world for that many thousands of ages.

BP 1.17.48

कपिलापञ्चगव्येन कुशवारियुतेन च ॥
स्नापयेन्मंत्रपूतेन ब्राह्मं स्नानं हि तत्स्मृतम् ॥ ॥

One should bathe the image with the five products of a tawny cow mixed with kuśa-water and consecrated by mantra; this is remembered as the Brahma bathing rite.

BP 1.17.49

कपिलापंचगव्येन दधिक्षीरघृतेन च ॥
स्नानं शतगुणं ज्ञेयमितरेषां नराधिप ॥ ॥

A bath using the five products of a tawny cow together with curd, milk, and clarified butter should be understood as producing a hundred times the merit of corresponding products from other cattle, O ruler of people.

BP 1.17.50

देवाग्निकार्यमुद्दिश्य कपिलामाहरेत्सदा ॥
ब्रह्मक्षत्रविशश्चैव न शूद्रस्तु कदाचन ॥ ॥

A tawny cow for divine or fire rites should always be procured by members of the brāhmaṇa, kṣatriya, or vaiśya classes; the source excludes a śūdra from doing so.

BP 1.17.51

कापिलं यः पिबेच्छूद्रो देवकार्यार्थनिर्मितम् ॥
स पच्येत महाघोरे सुचिरं नरकार्णवे ॥ ॥

a śūdra who drinks the tawny cow’s product prepared for a divine rite is cooked for a very long time in a dreadful ocean of hell.

BP 1.17.52

वर्षकोटिसहस्रैस्तु यत्पापं समुपार्जितम् ॥
सुरज्येष्ठघृताभ्यंगाद्दहेत्सर्वं न संशयः ॥ ॥

The sin acquired over thousands of crores of years is all burned by anointing Surajyeṣṭha with ghee; there is no doubt.

BP 1.17.53

कल्पकोटिसहस्रैस्तु यत्पापं समुपार्जितम् ॥
पितामहघृतस्नानं दहत्यग्निरिवेंधनम् ॥ ।

A clarified-butter bath for the Grandfather burns away sin accumulated over billions of cosmic ages just as fire burns fuel.

BP 1.17.54

घृतस्नानं प्रतिपदि सकृत्कृत्वा तु कांजजम् ॥
कुलैकविंशमुत्तार्य विष्णुलोके मही यते ॥ ॥

By giving the lotus-born god a clarified-butter bath once on Pratipad, one delivers twenty-one generations and is honoured in Viṣṇu’s world.

BP 1.17.55

अयुतं यो गवां दद्याद्भक्त्या वै वेदपारगे ॥
वस्त्रहेमादियुक्तानां क्षीरस्नानेन यत्फलम् ॥ ॥

The fruit of bathing the image with milk equals that obtained by devotionally giving ten thousand cows, furnished with garments, gold, and other gifts, to one learned in the Veda.

BP 1.17.56

सकृदाज्येन पयसा स्नपयेत्तु यः ॥
गांगेयेन स यानेन याति ब्रह्मसलोकताम् ॥ ॥

One who bathes the image even once with clarified butter and milk goes in a golden celestial vehicle to dwell in Brahmā’s world.

BP 1.17.57

स्नाप्य दध्ना सकृद्वीर कञ्जजं विष्णुमाप्नुयात् ॥
मधुना स्नपयित्वा तु वीरलोके महीयते ॥ ॥

O hero, one who bathes the lotus-born god once with curd attains Viṣṇu, while one who bathes him with honey is honoured in the realm of heroes.

BP 1.17.58

स्नानमिक्षुरसेनेह यो विरिंचेः समाचरेत् ॥
स याति लोकं सवितस्तु तेजसा भासयन्नभः ॥ ॥

One who here performs Viriñci’s bathing rite with sugarcane juice goes to the world of Savitṛ, illuminating the sky with radiance.

BP 1.17.59

शुद्धोदकेन यो भक्त्या स्नापयेत्पद्मसंभवम् ॥
उत्सृज्य पापकलिलं स यात्येव सलोकताम् ॥ ॥

One who devotionally bathes the lotus-born god with pure water casts off the mire of sin and certainly attains residence in his world.

BP 1.17.60

वस्त्रपूतेन तोयेन स्नपयेद्यः सकृद्विभुम् ॥
स सर्वकालं तृप्तात्मा लोकवश्यत्वमा प्नुयात् ॥ ॥

One who bathes the mighty god even once with water strained through cloth remains inwardly satisfied at all times and obtains influence among people.

BP 1.17.61

सर्वौषधीभिर्यो भक्त्या स्नपयेत्पद्मसंभवम् ॥
कांचनेन विमानेन ब्रह्मलोके महीयते ॥ ॥

One who devotionally bathes the lotus-born god with a mixture of all medicinal herbs is honoured in Brahmā’s world in a golden aerial vehicle.

BP 1.17.62

गन्धचन्दनतोयेन स्नापयेद्योंबुजोद्भवम् ॥
रुद्रलोकमवाप्नोति तेजसा हेमसन्निभः ॥ ॥

One who bathes the lotus-born god with fragrant sandalwood water attains Rudra’s world, shining with a radiance like gold.

BP 1.17.63

पाटलोत्पलपद्मानि करवीराणि सर्वदा ॥
स्नानकाले प्रयोज्यानि स्थिराणि सुरभीणि च ॥ ॥

Pāṭala flowers, blue lotuses, lotuses, and oleanders—firm and fragrant—should always be used during the bathing rite.

BP 1.17.64

एषामेकतमं स्नानं भक्त्या कृत्वा तु वेधसि ॥
विधूय पापकलिलं विधिलोके महीयते ॥ ॥

By devotionally performing even one of these bathing rites for Vedhas, a person shakes off the mire of sin and is honoured in Vedhas’s world.

BP 1.17.65

कर्पूरागरुतोयेन स्नपयेद्यस्तु कञ्जजम् ॥
सर्वपापविशुद्धात्मा ब्रह्मलोके महीयते ॥ ॥

One who bathes the lotus-born god with water scented by camphor and agarwood becomes purified from every sin and is honoured in Brahmā’s world.

BP 1.17.66

गायत्रीशतजप्तेन विमलेनांभसा विभुम् ॥
स्नपयित्वा सकृद्भक्त्या ब्रह्मलोकमवा प्नुयात् ॥ ॥

By bathing the mighty god once with devotion in clear water consecrated through one hundred recitations of the Gāyatrī, one attains Brahmā’s world.

BP 1.17.67

विभुं शीतांबुना स्नाप्य धारोष्णपयसा ततः ॥
ततः पश्चाद् घृतस्नानं कृत्वा पापैर्विमुच्यते ॥ ॥

Having bathed the mighty god with cool water, then with warm fresh milk, and afterward with clarified butter, a person is freed from sins.

BP 1.17.68

एतत्स्नानत्रयं कृत्वा पूजयित्वा तु भक्तितः ॥
अश्वमेधसहस्रस्य फलं प्राप्नोति मानवः ॥ ॥

A person who performs these three bathing rites and then worships with devotion obtains the fruit of one thousand Aśvamedha sacrifices.

BP 1.17.69

मृत्कुंभैस्ताम्रजैः कुंभैः स्नानं शतगुणं भवेत् ॥
रौप्ये लक्षोत्तरं प्रोक्तं हैमैः कोटिगुणं भवेत् ॥ ॥

Compared with bathing from clay pots, bathing from copper vessels is a hundredfold; from silver it is said to exceed a hundred thousandfold, and from gold it becomes ten millionfold.

BP 1.17.70

ब्रह्मणो दर्शनं पुण्यं दर्शनात्स्पर्शनं परम् ॥
स्पर्शनादर्चनं श्रेष्ठं घृतस्नातमतः परम् ॥ ॥

Seeing Brahmā is meritorious; touching is greater than seeing; worship is better than touching; and beyond worship stands bathing the image with clarified butter.

BP 1.17.71

वाचिकं मानसं पापं घृतस्नानेन देहिनाम् ॥
क्षिणुते पद्मजो यस्मात्तस्मात्स्नानं समाचरेत् ॥ ॥

Because the lotus-born god destroys the verbal and mental sins of embodied beings through the clarified-butter bath, one should perform this bathing rite.

BP 1.17.72

स्नपयित्वार्चयेद्भक्त्या यथा तच्छृणु भारत ॥
शुचिवस्त्रधरः स्नातः कृतन्यासश्च भारत ॥ ॥

Hear, O descendant of Bharata, how one should worship after bathing the image. The practitioner should have bathed, wear clean garments, and perform the prescribed placements upon the body.

BP 1.17.73

चतुर्हस्तं लिखेत्पद्मं चतुर्भागविभागितम् ॥
मध्ये तस्य लिखेच्चक्रं दलैर्द्वादशभिश्चितम् ॥ ॥

One should draw a lotus four cubits across, divided into four sections, and in its centre draw a wheel marked with twelve petals.

BP 1.17.74

सरोजानि ततो न्यस्य अक्षराणि समन्ततः ॥
अक्षरं विहितं चान्यत्पत्रभागे प्रकीर्तितम् ॥ ॥

Then one should place the lotus forms and arrange the sacred syllables all around; another prescribed letter is assigned to each division of the petals.

BP 1.17.75

नानावर्णकसंयोगाल्लिखेच्चैवानुपूर्वशः ॥
कृष्णोत्कटं तु मध्यं स्यात्पीतरक्तं तथा परम् ॥ ॥

One should draw them successively using combinations of various colours: the centre should be intensely black, and the next region yellow and red.

BP 1.17.76

सितं शुद्धं तु कर्त्तव्यं मध्यभागे तु वर्तुलम् ॥
प्रभाकुण्डलकैर्बाह्यैर्वेष्टयेच्चक्रनायकम् ॥ ॥

A pure white circular area should be made in the middle, and the lord of the wheel should be encircled by outer rings of radiance.

BP 1.17.77

एवमालिख्य यत्नेन मूलमन्त्रं ततो न्यसेत् ॥
मूर्ध्नः पादतलं यावत्प्रणवं विन्यसेद्बुधः ॥ ॥

Having drawn it carefully in this way, one should install the root mantra. The wise practitioner should place the praṇava from the crown of the head down to the soles of the feet.

BP 1.17.78

नादरूपं न्यसेत्तावद्यावच्छब्दस्य शून्यता ॥
तत्कारं विन्यसेन्मूर्ध्नि सकारं मुखमण्डले ॥ ॥

One should place the form of nāda as far as the point where sound becomes emptiness; the syllable tat is placed upon the crown and sa upon the circle of the face.

BP 1.17.79

विकारं कण्ठदेशे तु तुकारं सर्वसंधिषु ॥
वकारं हृदि मध्ये तु रेकारं पार्श्वयोर्द्वयोः ॥ ॥

The syllable vi is placed at the throat, tur at all the joints, va in the centre of the heart, and re upon both sides.

BP 1.17.80

णकारं दक्षिणे कुक्षौ यकारं वामसंज्ञके ॥
भकारं कटिनाभिस्थं र्गोकारं जानुपर्वसु ॥ ॥

The syllable ṇa is placed on the right abdomen, ya on the left, bha at the waist and navel, and rgo at the knee-joints.

BP 1.17.81

देकारं जंघयोर्न्यस्य वकारं पादपद्मयोः ॥
स्यकारमंगुष्ठयोर्न्यस्य धीकारं चोरसि न्यसेत् ॥ ॥

Having placed de upon the shanks, va upon the lotus-feet, and sya upon the great toes, one should place dhī upon the chest.

BP 1.17.82

मकारं जानुदेशे तु हिकारं गुह्यमाश्रितम् ॥
धिकारं हृदये न्यस्य योकारं चौष्ठयो र्न्यसेत् ॥ ॥

The syllable ma is placed at the knees, hi in the private region, dhi in the heart, and yo upon the lips.

BP 1.17.83

नकारं नासिकाग्रे तु प्रकारं नेत्रमाश्रितम् ॥
चोकारं तु भुवोर्मध्ये दकारं प्राणमाश्रितम् ॥ ॥

The syllable na is placed at the tip of the nose, pra upon the eyes, co between the eyebrows, and da upon the vital breath.

BP 1.17.84

याकारं विन्यसेन्मूर्ध्नि तकारं केशमाश्रितम् ॥
न्यासं कृत्वात्मनो देहे देवे कुर्यात्तथा नृप ॥
सर्वोपचारसंपन्नं कृत्वा सम्यङ् निरीक्षयेत् ॥ ॥

The syllable yā is placed upon the crown and ta in the hair. After performing the placement upon one’s own body, O king, one should do likewise upon the deity and then contemplate him as furnished with every offering.

BP 1.17.85

कुंकुमागुरुकर्पूर चन्दनेन विमिश्रितम् ॥
गन्धतोयमुपस्कृत्य गायत्र्या प्रणवेन च ॥
प्रोक्षयेत्सर्वद्रव्याणि पश्चादर्चनमाचरेत् ॥ ॥

One should prepare scented water mixed with saffron, agarwood, camphor, and sandalwood, consecrate it with the Gāyatrī and praṇava, sprinkle every ritual article, and afterward begin the worship.

BP 1.17.86

चक्रग्रन्थिषु सर्वासु प्रणवं विनिवेशयेत् ॥
भूयः प्लुतं समुच्चार्य प्रणवं सर्वतोमुखम् ॥ ॥

One should install the praṇava at every junction of the wheel, then again pronounce the lengthened, all-facing form of Oṃ.

BP 1.17.87

विन्यसेत्पद्ममध्ये तु पीठनिष्पत्तिहेतवे ॥
आसने पृथिवी ज्ञेया सर्वसत्त्वधरा मता ॥ ॥

It should be placed in the centre of the lotus to bring the ritual seat into being. The seat is understood as Earth, regarded as the bearer of all living beings.

BP 1.17.88

ह्रस्वोंकारे मता सा तु दीर्घोंकारे तु देवराट् ॥
प्लुतस्तु व्यापयेद्भावं मोक्षदं चामृतात्मकम् ॥ ॥

Earth is contemplated in the short form of Oṃ and the lord of the gods in its long form; the protracted form pervades the state that grants liberation and is of the nature of immortality.

BP 1.17.89

यत्नस्थो न निवर्तेत योगी प्राणपरायणः ॥
आवाहनं ततः कुर्यादक्षरेण परेण तु ॥ ॥

Established in effort and wholly intent upon the breath, the yogin should not turn back. He should then perform the invocation with the supreme syllable.

BP 1.17.90

आवाह्य तेजोरूपं तु न्यसेन्मन्त्रवरांस्ततः ॥
ततो विभावयेद्देवं पद्मस्थं चतुराननम् ॥ ॥

Having invoked the form of radiance, one should place the finest mantras and then visualize the four-faced god seated upon the lotus.

BP 1.17.91

स्रष्टारं सर्वजगतां विष्णुरुद्रविधानगम् ॥
संभाव्य गन्धपुष्पादि संभारान्क्रमात्सर्वान्प्रकल्पयेत् ॥

Having contemplated him as the creator of all worlds and the source from whom Viṣṇu and Rudra emerge, one should arrange in order every requisite, beginning with fragrances and flowers.

BP 1.17.92

गायत्रीमुच्चरन्मन्त्रं सर्वकर्माणि कारयेत् ॥ ॥

Reciting the Gāyatrī as the governing mantra, one should perform all the ritual actions.

BP 1.17.93

पुष्पं धूपं तथा दीपं नैवेद्यं सुमनोहरम् ॥
खंडलड्डुकश्रीवेष्टकासाराशोकवर्तिकाः ॥ ॥

One should offer flowers, incense, a lamp, and pleasing food: pieces of sugar, laḍḍukas, śrīveṣṭakas, sweet essence-preparations, and aśoka-shaped confections—

BP 1.17.94

स्वस्तिकोल्लोपिकादुग्धतिलावेष्टतिलाढिकाः ॥
फलानि चैव पक्वानि लग्नखण्डगुडानि च ॥ ॥

—together with svastika cakes, ullopikā preparations, milk dishes, sesame rolls, sesame-rich sweets, ripe fruits, and pieces of solid molasses.

BP 1.17.95

अन्यांश्च विविधान्दद्यात्पूपानि विविधानि च ॥
एवमादीनि सर्वाणि दापयेचछक्तितो नृप ॥ ॥

One should also offer other varied foods and many kinds of cakes. O king, all such offerings should be provided according to one’s ability.

BP 1.17.96

मूलमन्त्रेण देवस्य ततो देहं विभावयेत् ॥
पूजयेच्चापि विधिना येन तं ते ब्रवीम्यहम् ॥ ॥

One should then visualize the deity’s body through the root mantra and worship him according to the procedure, which I shall now explain to you.

BP 1.17.97

प्राणायामत्रयं कृत्वा देहसंशोधनाय वै ॥
आवाहयेत्ततोनन्तं धारयन्तं वचः सदा ॥ ॥

After performing three rounds of breath-regulation to purify the body, one should invoke Ananta, who continually bears and supports sacred speech.

BP 1.17.98

ध्यात्वानन्तं ततो रुद्रं पद्मकिंजल्कमध्यगम् ॥
ध्यायेद्विष्णुं ततो देवं न्यसेत्पद्मोदरोद्भवम् ॥ ॥

Having meditated upon Ananta, one should then meditate on Rudra in the centre of the lotus pericarp, contemplate the god Viṣṇu, and finally install the lotus-born deity.

BP 1.17.99

एवं त्रिदेवतारूढं पद्ममध्येंबुजोद्भवम् ॥
पूजयेन्मूलमन्त्रेण पद्मोदरभवं नृप ॥ ॥

With the three deities thus installed, O king, one should worship through the root mantra the lotus-born Brahmā situated in the centre of the lotus.

BP 1.17.100

ऋग्वेदं तु यजुर्वेदं सामवेदं च पूजयेत् ॥
ज्ञानवैराग्यमैश्वर्यं धर्मं संपूजयेद्बुधः ॥ ॥

The wise practitioner should worship the Ṛgveda, Yajurveda, and Sāmaveda, and also knowledge, detachment, sovereignty, and dharma.

BP 1.17.101

ईशानादिक्रमाद्राजन्विदिशासु समंततः ॥
शिक्षा कल्पो व्याकरणं निरुक्तं छन्द एव च ॥ ॥

Beginning with Īśāna and proceeding through the intermediate directions all around, O king, one should worship phonetics, ritual procedure, grammar, etymology, and metre—

BP 1.17.102

ज्योतिषं च महाबाहो उपवेदाश्च कृत्स्नशः ॥
इतिहासपुराणानि यथायोग्यं यथाक्रमम् ॥ ॥

—as well as astronomy, O mighty-armed one, all the subsidiary Vedas, and the Itihāsas and Purāṇas, each in its proper place and order.

BP 1.17.103

शिक्षा कल्पो व्याकरणं देवस्य पुरतः सदा ॥
कल्पादयस्ततश्चान्ये दिशासु विदिशासु च ॥ ॥

Phonetics, Kalpa, and grammar are always placed before the deity; the others beginning with Kalpa are then assigned among the directions and intermediate directions.

BP 1.17.104

महाव्याहृतयः सर्वाः प्रणवेन समन्विताः ॥
पूर्वादिक्रमयोगेन पूजयेद्विधिना नृप ॥ ॥

All the great Vyāhṛtis, joined with the praṇava, should be worshipped according to rule, O king, proceeding in order from the east.

BP 1.17.105

शक्तयो ब्रह्मणस्त्वेता लोकरूपा व्यवस्थिताः ॥
पूजनीयाः प्रयत्नेन मंत्ररूपाः स्थिताः स्वयम् ॥ ॥

These are Brahmā’s powers, established as forms of the worlds and existing of themselves in the form of mantras; they should be worshipped carefully.

BP 1.17.106

अरकांतरसंस्थांश्च षट् समुद्रान्समर्चयेत् ॥
नक्षत्राणि ग्रहाश्चैव राशयश्च विशेषतः ॥
पूज्याः सर्वे यथान्यायं सुराग्रेषु व्यवस्थिताः ॥ ॥

One should worship the six oceans situated between the spokes, and especially the lunar mansions, planets, and zodiacal signs, all placed properly before the gods.

BP 1.17.107

नागाश्च गरुडश्चैव पूजनीयस्तथाग्रतः ॥
देवता ऋषयश्चैव सहिताः कुलपर्वताः ॥
तत्तेजोनिलयाः सर्वे पूजनीयाः प्रयत्नतः ॥ ॥

Nāgas and Garuḍa should likewise be worshipped in front, together with the deities, seers, and ancestral mountains; all are abodes of that radiance and should be carefully honoured.

BP 1.17.108

आचम्य विधिवत्पूर्वं मंत्रपूतेन वारिणा ॥
हृदयादीन्न्यसेदंगान्हृदयादिषु कृत्स्नशः ॥ ॥

Having first sipped water according to rule, using water purified by mantra, one should place the heart and other limb-mantras completely upon the heart and corresponding parts.

BP 1.17.109

शिखा नेत्रं तथा चर्म अस्त्रं च भरतर्षभ ॥
महेंद्रादिदिशश्चैताः पूजयेद्विधिवन्नृप ॥ ॥

The crest, eyes, armour, and weapon are then installed, O bull among the Bharatas, and the directions beginning with that of Mahendra should be worshipped according to rule, O king.

BP 1.17.110

हृदयं पुरतः पूज्यं शिरो देवस्य पृष्ठतः ॥
पूर्वं संपूजयेद्देवं मूलमंत्रेण कृत्स्नशः ॥ ॥

The heart is to be worshipped in front and the deity’s head behind; first one should worship the god completely with the root mantra.

BP 1.17.111

विसर्जयेद्दर्शयित्वा मुद्रां तु भरतर्षभ ॥
अंकुशं नरशार्दूल ह्याह्वाने कंजमादिशेत् ॥ ॥

At the conclusion, O bull among the Bharatas, one should display the dismissal mudrā and release the deity. O tiger among men, the goad-sign is used for summoning, and the lotus-sign is prescribed.

BP 1.17.112

यस्त्वेवं पूजयेद्देवं प्रतिपन्नित्यमेव च ॥
उपोष्य पंचदश्यां तु स याति परमं पदम् ॥ ॥

One who worships the god in this way on every Pratipad and fasts on the fifteenth lunar date reaches the supreme state.

BP 1.17.113

॥ सुमंतु रुवाच ॥ ॥
आपो हिष्ठेति मंत्रोऽयं हृदयं परिकीर्तितम् ॥
ऋतं सत्यं शिखा प्रोक्ता उदुत्यं नेत्रमादिशेत् ॥ ॥

Sumantu said: ‘The mantra beginning āpo hi ṣṭhā is declared to be the heart; ṛtaṃ satyam is the crest, and the formula beginning ud utyam is prescribed for the eyes.

BP 1.17.114

चित्रं देवानां मस्तमिति सर्वलोकेषु विश्रुतम् ॥
वर्मणा ते च्छादयामि कवचं समुदाहृतम् ॥ ॥

The formula beginning citraṃ devānām, renowned in all the worlds, is the head-mantra; “I cover you with armour” is declared to be the protective casing.

BP 1.17.115

भूर्भुवः स्वरिति तथा शिरसे परिकीर्तितम् ॥
गायत्रीमूलतंत्रस्तु साधकः सर्वकर्मणाम् ॥ ॥

Bhūr bhuvaḥ svaḥ is proclaimed for the head. The Gāyatrī is the root ritual system and the effective instrument of every sacred action.

BP 1.17.116

गायत्र्या पूजयेद्देवमोंकारेणाभिमंत्रितम् ॥
प्रणवेनापरान्सर्वानृग्वेदादीन्प्रपूजयेत् ॥ ॥

One should worship the god through the Gāyatrī after consecrating him with Oṃ; all the others beginning with the Ṛgveda should be worshipped through the praṇava.

BP 1.17.117

आह्वाने पूजने वीर विसर्गे ब्रह्मणस्तथा ॥
गायत्री परमो मंत्रो वेदमाता विभाविनी ॥ ।

In the invocation, worship, and dismissal of Brahmā, O hero, the Gāyatrī—the illuminating Mother of the Veda—is the supreme mantra.

BP 1.17.118

गायत्र्यक्षरतत्त्वैस्तु पूजयेद्यस्तु देवताम् ॥
स गच्छेद्ब्रह्मणः स्थानं दुर्लभं यद्दुरा सदम् ॥ ॥ ॥

Whoever worships the deity through the essential reality of the Gāyatrī’s syllables goes to Brahmā’s abode, difficult to attain and hard to approach.
