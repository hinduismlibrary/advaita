VP 5.36.1

श्रीपराशर उवाच

मैत्रेयैतद्बलं तस्य बलस्य बलशालिनः
कृतं यदन्यत्तेनाभूत्तदपि श्रूयतां त्वया

Maitreya, hear what other deed was performed by that mighty Balabhadra.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
मैत्रेय (maitreya) — O Maitreya
एतत् बलम् (etat balam) — this deed
तस्य बलस्य (tasya balasya) — of that Bala
यत् अन्यत् कृतम् (yat anyat kṛtam) — what else was done
तत् अपि श्रूयताम् (tat api śrūyatām) — let that also be heard
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

maitreyaitadabalaṃ tasya balasya balaśālinaḥ | kṛtaṃ yadanyattenābhūttadapi śrūyatāṃ tvayā

Śrīdhara commentary.

No Śrīdhara commentary text is supplied here.

VP 5.36.2

नरकस्यासुरेन्द्रस्य देवपक्षविरोधिनः
सखाभवन्महावीर्यो द्विविदो वानरर्षभः

Dvivida, a great hero and bull among monkeys, became the friend of Naraka, the asura king hostile to the gods.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
नरकस्य (narakasya) — of Naraka
असुरेन्द्रस्य (asurendrasya) — of the asura king
देव-पक्ष-विरोधिनः (deva-pakṣa-virodhinaḥ) — hostile to the gods
सखा अभवत् (sakhā abhavat) — became a friend
महा-वीर्यः (mahā-vīryaḥ) — great hero
द्विविदः (dvividaḥ) — Dvivida
वानर-ऋषभः (vānara-ṛṣabhaḥ) — bull among monkeys
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

narakasyāsurendrasya devapakṣavirodhinaḥ | sakhābhavanmahāvīryo dvivido vānararṣabhaḥ

Śrīdhara

maitreya śrūyatām iti yad uktaṃ tad eva vivṛṇoti—narakasyeti || 2 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

मैत्रेय श्रूयताम् इति यद् उक्तं तद् एव विवृणोति—नरकस्येति ॥२॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Śrīdhara says that the words “Maitreya, hear” introduce the explanation of what was just promised; he now explains the verse beginning “of Naraka.”

VP 5.36.11

एकदा रैवतोद्याने पपौ पानं हलायुधः
रेवती च महाभागा तथैवान्या वरस्त्रियः

One day Halāyudha drank at the Raivataka garden, together with Revati and other excellent women.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
एकदा (ekadā) — one day
रैवतोद्याने (raivatodyāne) — in the Raivataka garden
पपौ पानम् (papau pānam) — drank wine
हलायुधः (halāyudhaḥ) — Halāyudha
रेवती च (revatī ca) — and Revati
अन्याः वर-स्त्रियः (anyāḥ vara-striyaḥ) — other excellent women
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ekadā raivatodyāne papau pānaṃ halāyudhaḥ | revatī ca mahābhāgā tathaivānyā varastriyaḥ

Śrīdhara

ekadeti | raivatako dvārakāyāḥ pūrvato giriḥ tat-sambandhiny udyāne || 11 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

एकदेति । रैवतको द्वारकायाः पूर्वतो गिरिः तत्-सम्बन्धिन्य् उद्याने ॥११॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Śrīdhara explains that Raivataka is a mountain east of Dvārakā, and that this was the garden associated with it.

VP 5.36.12

उद्गीयमानो विलसल्ललनामौलिमध्यगः
रेमे यदुकुलश्रेष्ठः कुबेर इव मन्दरे

The best of the Yadus sported like Kubera in Nandana, singing while situated among the crowns of the beautiful women.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
उद्गीयमानः (udgīyamānaḥ) — singing
विलसत्-ललना-मौलि-मध्यगः (vilasat-lalanā-mauli-madhyagaḥ) — among the crowns of beautiful women
रेमे (reme) — sported
यदु-कुल-श्रेष्ठः (yadu-kula-śreṣṭhaḥ) — best of the Yadus
कुबेर इव नन्दने (kubera iva nandane) — like Kubera in Nandana
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

udagīyamāno vilasallalanāmaulimadhyagaḥ | reme yadukulaśreṣṭhaḥ kubera iva mandare

Śrīdhara

udgīyamāna iti | vilasal-lalanā vilāsinyaḥ tāsāṃ maulayaḥ śreṣṭhāḥ, tāsāṃ madhyagaḥ || 12 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

उद्गीयमान इति । विलसल्-ललना विलासिन्यः तासां मौलयः श्रेष्ठाः, तासां मध्यगः ॥१२॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Śrīdhara explains that the beautiful women are the playful women, that their crowns mean the foremost women, and that Baladeva was situated among them.

VP 5.36.13

Transliteration (IAST)

Śrīdhara

tata iti | viḍambanaṃ bhrū-vikṣepa-danta-darśanānetrādi-vikriyām || 13 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तत इति । विडम्बनं भ्रू-विक्षेप-दन्त-दर्शनानेत्रादि-विक्रियाम् ॥१३॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Śrīdhara explains that the monkey’s mockery consisted of movements of the eyebrows, showing the teeth, movements of the eyes, and similar gestures.

VP 5.36.20

पतता तच्छरीरेण गिरेश्शृङ्गमशीर्यत
मैत्रेय शतधा वज्रिवज्रेणेव विदारितम्

As his body fell, the mountain peak was shattered into a hundred pieces, as if split by Indra’s thunderbolt.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
पतता तच् छरीरेण (patatā tac charīreṇa) — as that body fell
गिरेः शृङ्गम् (gireḥ śṛṅgam) — the mountain peak
अशीर्यत (aśīryata) — was shattered
मैत्रेय (maitreya) — O Maitreya
शतधा (śatadhā) — into a hundred pieces
वज्रि-वज्रेणेव (vajri-vajreṇeva) — as by Indra’s thunderbolt
विदारितम् (vidāritam) — split
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

patatā taccharīreṇa gireśśṛṅgamaśīryata | maitreya śatadhā vajrivajreṇeva vidāritam

Śrīdhara

patateti | aśīryata viśīrṇam abhavat || 20 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

पततेति । अशीर्यत विशीर्णम् अभवत् ॥२०॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Śrīdhara explains that aśīryata means that the mountain peak became shattered.
