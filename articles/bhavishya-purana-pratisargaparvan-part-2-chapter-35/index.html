PP 2.35.1

सूत उवाच ।
चित्रकूटे गिरौ रम्ये नानाधातुविचित्रिते ।
तत्रावसन्महाप्राज्ञ उपाध्यायः पतञ्जलिः ॥ १ ॥

Sūta said: On the lovely mountain Citrakūṭa, adorned with many kinds of minerals, lived the very wise teacher Patañjali.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
सूत (sūta) — Sūta;
उवाच (uvāca) — said;
चित्रकूटे (citrakūṭe) — at Citrakūṭa;
गिरौ रम्ये (girau ramye) — on the lovely mountain;
नानाधातुविचित्रिते (nānādhātuvicitrite) — adorned with many kinds of minerals;
तत्र (tatra) — there;
अवसत् (avasat) — lived;
महाप्राज्ञः (mahāprājñaḥ) — very wise;
उपाध्यायः (upādhyāyaḥ) — teacher;
पतञ्जलिः (patañjaliḥ) — Patañjali;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

sūta uvāca | citrakūṭe girau ramye nānādhātuvicitrite | tatrāvasanmahāprājña upādhyāyaḥ patañjaliḥ || 1 ||

PP 2.35.2

वेदवेदांगतत्त्वज्ञो गीताशास्त्रपरायणः ।
विष्णुभक्तः सत्यसन्धो भाष्यशास्त्रविशारदः ॥ २ ॥

He knew the true principles of the Vedas and their ancillary disciplines, was devoted to the teaching of the Gītā, was a devotee of Viṣṇu, steadfast in truth, and skilled in the science of commentary.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
वेद-वेदाङ्ग-तत्त्वज्ञः (veda-vedāṅga-tattvajñaḥ) — knower of the principles of the Vedas and Vedāṅgas;
गीता-शास्त्र-परायणः (gītā-śāstra-parāyaṇaḥ) — devoted to the teaching of the Gītā;
विष्णुभक्तः (viṣṇubhaktaḥ) — a devotee of Viṣṇu;
सत्यसन्धः (satyasandhaḥ) — steadfast in truth;
भाष्यशास्त्रविशारदः (bhāṣyaśāstraviśāradaḥ) — skilled in the science of commentary;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

vedavedāṃgatattvajño gītāśāstraparāyaṇaḥ | viṣṇubhaktaḥ satyasandho bhāṣyaśāstra viśāradaḥ || 2 ||

PP 2.35.3

कदाचित्स तु शुद्धात्मा गतस्तीर्थान्तरं प्रति ।
काश्यां कात्यायनेनैव तस्य वादो महानभूत् ॥ ३ ॥

Once that pure-souled man went to another place of pilgrimage. In Kāśī he had a great debate with Kātyāyana.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
कदाचित् (kadācit) — once;
सः तु (saḥ tu) — that man;
शुद्धात्मा (śuddhātmā) — pure-souled;
गतः (gataḥ) — went;
तीर्थान्तरं प्रति (tīrthāntaraṃ prati) — to another place of pilgrimage;
काश्याम् (kāśyām) — in Kāśī;
कात्यायनेन एव (kātyāyanena eva) — with Kātyāyana himself;
तस्य वादः (tasya vādaḥ) — his debate;
महान् अभूत् (mahān abhūt) — became great;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

kadācitsa tu śuddhātmā gatastīrthāṃtaraṃ prati | kāśyāṃ kātyāyanenaiva tasya vādo mahānabhūt || 3 ||

PP 2.35.4

वर्षान्ते च तदा विप्रो देवीभक्तेन निर्जितः ।
लज्जितः स तु धर्मात्मा संतुष्टाव सरस्वतीम् ॥ ४ ॥

At the end of the year, the brahmin was defeated by a devotee of the Goddess. Ashamed, that righteous man praised Sarasvatī.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

विप्रः (vipraḥ) — the brahmin;
देवीभक्तेन (devībhaktena) — by a devotee of the Goddess;
निर्जितः (nirjitaḥ) — defeated;
लज्जितः (lajjitaḥ) — ashamed;
सः तु (saḥ tu) — that man;
धर्मात्मा (dharmātmā) — righteous;

Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

varṣānte ca tadā vipro devībhaktena nirjitaḥ | lajjitaḥ sa tu dharmātmā saṃtuṣṭāva sarasvatīm || 4 ||

PP 2.35.5

पतञ्जलिरुवाच ।
नमो देव्यै महामूर्त्यै सर्वमूर्त्यै नमो नम ।
शिवायै सर्वमांगल्यै विष्णुमाये च ते नमः ॥ ५ ॥

Patañjali said: Salutations again and again to the Goddess of great form, who is all forms. Salutations to you, auspicious one, source of every blessing, O Viṣṇumāyā.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
पतञ्जलिः उवाच (patañjaliḥ uvāca) — Patañjali said;
नमः देव्यै (namaḥ devyai) — salutation to the Goddess;
महामूर्त्यै (mahāmūrtyai) — to her of great form;
सर्वमूर्त्यै (sarvamūrtyai) — to her who is all forms;
नमो नम (namo nama) — salutations again and again;
शिवायै (śivāyai) — to the auspicious one;
सर्वमाङ्गल्यै (sarvamāṃgalyai) — to her who is all auspiciousness;
विष्णुमाये (viṣṇumāye) — O Viṣṇumāyā;
च ते (ca te) — and to you;
नमः (namaḥ) — salutation;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

patañjaliruvāca | namo devyai mahāmūrtyai sarvamūrtyai namo nama | śivāyai sarvamāṃgalyai viṣṇumāye ca te namaḥ || 5 ||

PP 2.35.6

त्वमेव श्रद्धा बुद्धिस्त्वं मेधा विद्या शिवंकरी ।
शांतिर् वाणी त्वमेवासि नारायणी नमोनमः ॥ ६ ॥

You alone are faith and discernment; you are intelligence, knowledge, and the giver of good. You are peace and speech. O Nārāyaṇī, salutations to you again and again.

श्रद्धा (śraddhā) — faith;

त्वम् (tvam) — you;
मेधा (medhā) — intelligence;
विद्या (vidyā) — knowledge;
शिवंकरी (śivaṃkarī) — giver of good;
शान्तिः (śāntiḥ) — peace;
वाणी (vāṇī) — speech;
असि (asi) — you are;
नारायणी (nārāyaṇi) — O Nārāyaṇī;
नमः नमः (namaḥ namaḥ) — salutations again and again;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

tvameva śraddhā buddhistvaṃ medhā vidyā śivaṃkarī | śāṃtirvāṇī tvamevāsi nārāyaṇi namonamaḥ || 6 ||

PP 2.35.8

तच्चरित्रप्रभावेण सत्यं ज्ञानमवाप्स्यसि ।
कात्यायनस्य विप्रस्य राजसंज्ञानमुद्धतम् ।
मद्भक्त्या तेन संप्राप्तं पराजय पतञ्जले ॥ ८ ॥

Through the power of that account you will attain true knowledge. The brahmin Kātyāyana has gained a lofty royal standing; through devotion to me he has brought about your defeat, Patañjali.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
तत्-चरित्र-प्रभावेण (tat-caritra-prabhāveṇa) — through the power of that account;
सत्यम् ज्ञानम् (satyaṃ jñānam) — true knowledge;
अवाप्स्यसि (avāpsyasi) — you will attain;
कात्यायनस्य विप्रस्य (kātyāyanasya viprasya) — of the brahmin Kātyāyana;
राजसंज्ञानम् (rājasaṃjñānam) — royal designation or recognition; the compound is uncertain;
उद्धतम् (uddhatam) — raised high, lofty;
मद्भक्त्या (madbhaktyā) — through devotion to me;
तेन (tena) — by him;
संप्राप्तम् (saṃprāptam) — attained;
पराजय (parājaya) — defeat;
पतञ्जले (patañjale) — O Patañjali;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

taccaritraprabhāveṇa satyaṃ jñānamavāpsyasi | kātyāyanasya viprasya rājasaṃjñānamuddhatam | madbhaktyā tena saṃprāptaṃ parājaya patañjale || 8 ||

PP 2.35.9

PP 2.35.10

ज्ञानं प्रसादजं विप्रः प्राप्य विष्णुपरायणम् ।
कात्यायनं पराजित्य परां मुदमवाप ह ॥ १० ॥

The brahmin received knowledge born of grace and became devoted to Viṣṇu. Having defeated Kātyāyana, he attained great joy.

Word-for-word

पराजित्य (parājitya) — having defeated;

PP 2.35.11

PP 2.35.16

शुचिर्यो हि नरो नित्यमितिहाससमुच्चयम् ।

śuciryo hi naro nityamitihāsasamuccayam | śṛṇuyāddharmakāmārthī sa yāti paramāṃ gatim || 16 ||

इति श्रीभविष्ये महापुराणे प्रतिसर्गपर्वणि चतुर्युगखण्डापरपर्याये कलियुगीयेतिहाससमुच्चये उत्तमचरितमाहात्म्ये पञ्चत्रिंशोऽध्यायः ॥ ३५ ॥
समाप्तोऽयं द्वितीयः खण्डः ॥

Thus ends the thirty-fifth chapter, in the Pratisargaparvan, in the section also called the Caturyuga-khaṇḍa and the compendium of Kali-age histories, on the glory of the excellent account. The second section ends here.
