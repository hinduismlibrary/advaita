Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 181 — Kinds of Sacred Tradition

BP 1.181.1

।। शतानीक उवाच।। पंचप्रकारं धर्मं मे वद स्मार्तं यथाक्रमम् ।। कौतुकं पृच्छ ते ब्रह्मन्समासव्यासयोगतः ।। १ ।।

Śatānīka said: Tell me, in order, the fivefold dharma of Smṛti. Out of curiosity, O Brahmin, I ask for its concise and full account.

BP 1.181.2

।। सुमंतुरुवाच ।। पंचधा वर्णितं धर्मं शृणु राजन्समासतः ।। यथोक्तं भास्करेणेह अरुणस्य महात्मनः ।। २ ।।

Sumantu said: Hear briefly the dharma described in five ways, O king, as Bhāskara spoke here to the great-souled Aruṇa.

BP 1.181.3

सहस्रकिरणं भानुमुदयस्थं दिवाकरम् ।। प्रणम्य शिरसा देवमुवाच गरुडाग्रजः ।। ३ ।।

Having bowed his head to Bhānu, the thousand-rayed, risen Day-maker, Aruṇa, elder brother of Garuḍa, spoke to the god.

BP 1.181.4

भगवन्देवदेवेश सहस्रकिरणोज्ज्वल ।। स्मृतिधर्मान्यथातत्त्वं वक्तुमर्हसि पृच्छतः ।। ४ ।।

O Lord, Lord of the gods, radiant with a thousand rays, you should tell me truly the dharmas of Smṛti, as I ask.

BP 1.181.5

एवं पृष्टस्तु भगवानरुणेन खगाधिपः ।। उवाच परया प्रीत्या पूजयित्वा महीपते ।। ५ ।।

Thus asked by Aruṇa, the Lord of birds spoke with great affection after worshipping him, O king.

BP 1.181.6

।। भास्कर उवाच ।। स्मृतिधर्मं वेदमूलं शृणु त्वं गरुडाग्रज ।। पूर्वानुभूतं यद्ध्यानमथ तत्स्मरणं स्मृतिः ।। ६ ।।

Bhāskara said: Hear the dharma of Smṛti, rooted in the Veda, O elder brother of Garuḍa. That which was experienced before as meditation—its recollection afterward is Smṛti.

BP 1.181.7

धर्मः क्रियात्मा निर्दिष्टः श्रेयोभ्युदयलक्षणः ।। स च पञ्चविधः प्रोक्तो वेदमूलः सनातनः ।। ७ ।।

Dharma is declared to have action as its nature, marked by welfare and prosperity. It is said to be fivefold, eternal, rooted in the Veda.

BP 1.181.8

अस्य शब्दस्यानुष्ठानात्स्वर्गो मोक्षश्च जायते ।। इह लोके सुखैश्वर्यमलं यच्च खगाधिप ।। ८ ।।

By the practice of this word, heaven and liberation arise, as do abundant happiness and sovereignty in this world, O lord of birds.

BP 1.181.9

।। अनूरुरुवाच ।। कथं पंचविधो ह्येष प्रोक्तो धर्मः सनातनः ।। कस्य भेदास्तु ते पंच ब्रूहि मे देवसत्तम ।। ९ ।।

Anūru said: How is this eternal dharma said to be fivefold? What are its five divisions? Tell me, O best of gods.

BP 1.181.10

।। भास्कर उवाच ।। वेदधर्मः स्मृतस्त्वेक आश्रमाणां स तत्परः ।। वर्णाश्रमस्तृतीयस्तु गुणनैमित्तिको यथा ।। १० ।।

Bhāskara said: Veda-based dharma is remembered as one, and duty of the life-stages follows it; duty of varṇa and life-stage is the third, then quality-based and occasion-based duty, in that order.

BP 1.181.11

वर्णत्वमेकमाश्रित्य अधिकारे प्रवर्तते ।। सवर्णाश्रमदंडस्तु भिक्षा दंडादिको यथा ।।११।।

Relying on varṇa alone, duty proceeds according to eligibility; the rules of varṇa and life-stage include alms, the staff, and the like.

BP 1.181.12

वर्णाश्रमाश्रमत्वं च योधिकृत्य प्रवर्तते ।। स वर्णाश्रमधर्मस्तु दंडाद्या मेखला यथा ।।१२।।

That which proceeds by taking varṇa, life-stage, and the status of a life-stage as authoritative is duty of varṇa and life-stage, such as the staff, belt, and the rest.

BP 1.181.13

यो गुणेन प्रवर्तेत स गुणो धर्म उच्यते ।। यथा मूर्धाभिषिक्तस्य प्रजानां पालनं परम् ।।१३।।

Duty based on a quality is called quality-based duty, as in the supreme protection of subjects by one who has been crowned.

BP 1.181.14

निमित्तमेकमाश्रित्य यो धर्मः संप्रवर्तते ।। नैमित्तिकः स विज्ञेयो जातिद्रव्यगुणाश्रयः ।।१४।।

Duty that proceeds by relying on a single occasion is to be known as occasion-based, resting on birth, substance, and quality.

BP 1.181.15

एष तु द्विविधः प्रोक्तः समासादविशेषतः ।। नैमित्तिकः स विज्ञेयः प्रायश्चित्तविधिर्यथा ।। १५ ।।

This is said to be twofold in brief, without distinction; occasion-based duty is known, for example, as the rule of expiation.

BP 1.181.16

स चतुर्धा निरूप्यस्तु स्वरूपफलसाधनैः ।। प्रमाणतस्तु प्रत्येकसमस्तैश्च यथाक्रमम् ।। १६ ।।

It is to be described fourfold by form, fruit, and means: according to authority, each separately and all together, in due order.

BP 1.181.17

श्रुत्या सह विरोधे तु बाध्यते विषयं विना ।। व्यवस्थया विरोधेन कार्यो यत्नः परीक्ष्यते ।।१७।।

In conflict with revealed text, it is overridden, except where the subject differs; when arrangements conflict, effort should be made to examine the action.

BP 1.181.18

स्मृत्या सह विरोधेन चार्थशास्त्रस्य साधनम् ।। परस्परविरोधे तु अर्थशास्त्रस्य साधनम् ।।१८।।

When it conflicts with Smṛti, the treatise on practical affairs is the authority; when they conflict with one another, the treatise on practical affairs is the authority.

BP 1.181.19

अदृष्टार्थे विकल्पस्तु व्यवस्थासंभवे सति ।। स्मृतिशास्त्रविकल्पस्तु आकांक्षापूरणे सति ।। १९ ।।

For an unseen matter, there is an alternative when a settled arrangement is possible; in a Smṛti treatise, an alternative is used when the requirement is fulfilled.

BP 1.181.20

वेदमूले स्थितं त्वेतदनुष्ठानं क्रिया सती ।। एवं शक्यविधानं तु न्यायो ह्येवं व्यवस्थितः ।। २० ।।

This observance, rooted in the Veda, is true action. Thus a feasible prescription is justice, established in this way.

BP 1.181.21

निषेधविधिरूपं तु द्विधा शास्त्रं खगाधिप ।। एकरूपं वदंत्यन्ये बहुरूपमथा परे ।। २१ ।।

Scripture has two forms, prohibition and injunction, O lord of birds. Some say it has one form; others say it has many forms.

BP 1.181.22

पंचप्रकाराः स्मृतय एवं शिष्यव्यवस्थिताः ।। त्रिधा चतुर्धा द्वेधा वा एकधा बहुधा खग ।। २२ ।।

The Smṛtis are fivefold, thus arranged for disciples: in three ways, four ways, two ways, one way, and many ways, O bird.

BP 1.181.23

दृष्टार्था तु स्मृतिः काचिददृष्टार्था तथापरा ।। अनुवादस्मृतिस्त्वन्या दृष्टादृष्टा तु पंचमी ।। सर्वा एता वेदमूलाः स्मृता वै ऋषिभिः स्वयम् ।। २३ ।।

One Smṛti has a visible result, another an unseen result, another is a restatement, and the fifth has both visible and unseen results. The sages themselves remember all these as rooted in the Veda.

BP 1.181.24

।। अरुण उवाच ।। या एता भवता प्रोक्ताः स्मृतयः पर्वगोपने ।। एतासां लक्षणं ब्रूहि समासादेव सत्तम ।। २४ ।।

Aruṇa said: These Smṛtis that you have declared in the hidden account, tell their characteristics briefly, O best one.

BP 1.181.25

दृष्टार्था का मता देव अदृष्टार्था च का भवेत् ।। दृष्टादृष्टस्वरूपा का न्यायमूला च का भवेत् ।। अनुवादस्मृतिः का स्यादृष्टादृष्टा तु का भवेत् ।। २५ ।।

Which is held to have a visible result, O god, and which would have an unseen result? Which has both visible and unseen results, and which is rooted in reasoning? What is restatement-Smṛti, and which would have visible and unseen results?

BP 1.181.26

एवमुक्तो महातेजा भास्करो वारितस्करः ।। उवाच तं खगं वीरं प्रणतं विनयान्वितम् ।। २६ ।।

Thus addressed, the greatly radiant Bhāskara, the water-stealing Sun, spoke to that heroic bird, bowed and endowed with humility.

BP 1.181.27

।। आदित्य उवाच ।। षड्गुणस्य स्वरूपं तु प्रयोगा त्कार्यगौरवात् ।। समयानामुपायानां योगो व्याससमासतः ।। २७ ।।

Āditya said: The nature of the six qualities, their application according to the importance of the task, and the joining of times and means, in detail and in brief—

BP 1.181.28

अध्यक्षाणां च निक्षेपः करणानां निरूपणम् ।। दृष्टार्थेयं स्मृतिः प्रोक्ता ऋषिभिर्गरुडाग्रज ।। २८ ।।

the appointment of superintendents and the description of instruments: the sages declare this Smṛti to have visible results, O elder brother of Garuḍa.

BP 1.181.29

संध्योपास्तिस्तथा कार्या शुनो मांसं न भक्षयेत् ।। अदृष्टार्था स्मृतिः प्रोक्ता मनुना विनतात्मज ।। २९ ।।

Twilight worship should likewise be performed; one should not eat dog’s meat. Manu declared this to be Smṛti with an unseen result, O son of Vinatā.

BP 1.181.30

पालाशं धारयेद्दंडमुभयार्थं विदुर्बुधाः ।। विरोधे तु विकल्पः स्याद्यागो होमस्ततो यथा ।। ३० ।।

One should carry a Palāśa staff; the wise know it to have both results. In a conflict there should be an alternative, as in sacrifice and fire-offering.

BP 1.181.31

श्रुतौ दृष्टं यथा कार्यं स्मृतो तत्तादृशं यदि ।। उक्तानुवादिनी सा तु पारिव्राज्यं तथा गृहात् ।। ३१ ।।

If what is stated in revealed text is likewise in Smṛti, that Smṛti repeats what was stated, as with renunciation from the household.

BP 1.181.32

उक्तो धर्मश्च संक्षेपात्परिभाषा च तद्गता ।। तत्साधनं च देशादि इत्थमित्यब्रवीद्रविः ।। ३२ ।।

Dharma was stated briefly, and its definition contained therein, along with its means beginning with place; thus spoke Ravi.

BP 1.181.33

ब्रह्मावर्तः परो देश ऋषिशस्तस्त्वनंतरः ।। मध्यदेशस्ततोऽप्यन्य आर्यावर्तस्त्वनंतरः ।। ३३ ।।

Brahmāvarta is the foremost region; next is the region praised by sages; then Madhyadeśa, and after that Āryāvarta.

BP 1.181.34

कृष्णसारस्तु विचरेन्मृगो यत्र स्वभावतः ।। यज्ञियः स तु देशः स्यान्म्लेच्छदेशस्ततः परः ।। ३४ ।।

Where the black antelope wanders naturally, that region is fit for sacrifice; beyond it is the foreign region.

BP 1.181.35

ब्रह्मादीनां च देवानां ब्राह्मणादेस्तथैव च ।। भूतग्रामस्य कृत्स्नस्य त्रयं कृत्स्नस्य खेचर ।। ३५ ।।

For Brahmā and the other gods, for Brahmins and the rest, and for the entire community of beings—the whole threefold [matter], O bird—

BP 1.181.36

साधनत्वं मनुः प्राह वेदमूलं सनातनम् ।। प्रकाशयज्ञसंसिद्ध्यै यदशब्दस्य एव तु ।। ३६ ।।

Manu declares this to be the means, eternal and rooted in the Veda, for completing the spoken sacrifice and the soundless one.

BP 1.181.37

उपलभ्य यथातत्त्वं स च दर्शितवानृषिः ।। सम्यक्संसाधनं धर्मः कर्तव्यस्त्वधिकारिणा ।। ३७ ।।

Having understood it according to its true nature, that sage showed it. Dharma is right accomplishment, to be performed by one who is qualified.

BP 1.181.38

निष्कामेन सदा वीर काम्यं रूपान्वितेन च ।। आचारयुक्तः श्रद्धालुर्वेदज्ञोऽध्यात्मचिंतकः ।। कर्मणां फलमाप्नोति न्यायार्जितधनश्च यः ।। ३८ ।।

The fruit of actions is obtained by one who is always without desire, O hero, and by one whose purpose is fulfilled, who is disciplined in conduct, faithful, knows the Veda, contemplates the self, and has earned wealth justly.

BP 1.181.39

।। अरुण उवाच ।। ब्रह्मावर्तादिदेशानां समस्तानां विभावसो ।। विभागं ब्रूहि देवेंद्र संमानय ग्रहाधिप ।। ३९ ।।

Aruṇa said: O Vibhāvasu, tell me the divisions of all the regions beginning with Brahmāvarta, O lord of gods; honor the lord of the planets.

BP 1.181.40

।। आदित्य उवाच ।। सरस्वतीदृषद्वत्योर्देवनद्योर्यदंतरम् ।। तं देवनिर्मितं देशं ब्रह्मावर्तं प्रचक्षते ।। ४० ।।

Āditya said: The region between the divine rivers Sarasvatī and Dṛṣadvatī, made by the gods, is called Brahmāvarta.

BP 1.181.41

हिमवद्विंध्यधरयोर्यदंतरमुदाहृतम् ।। प्रत्यगेव प्रयागाच्च मध्यदेशः प्रकीर्तितः ।। आसमुद्रात्तु वै पूर्वादासमुद्रात्तु पश्चिमात् ।। ४१ ।।

The region between the Himālaya and Vindhya is declared; westward from Prayāga it is called Madhyadeśa, from the eastern ocean to the western ocean.

BP 1.181.42

तयोरेवांतरं गिर्योरार्यावर्तं विदुर्बुधाः ।। आर्यावर्त एतान्विज्ञाय सद्देशान्त्संश्रयेत प्रयत्नतः ।। ४२ ।।

The wise know the region between those two mountains as Āryāvarta. Having recognized these good regions, one should carefully resort to them.

BP 1.181.43

शूद्रस्तु यस्मिंस्तस्मिन्वा निवसेद्वृत्तिकर्शितः ।। एषा धर्मस्य वै ज्योतिः समासात्कथिता तव ।। ४३ ।।

A Śūdra, distressed for livelihood, may dwell wherever. This light of dharma has been told to you in brief.
