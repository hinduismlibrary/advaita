VP 5.25.1

वने विहरतस् तस्य सह गोपैर् महात्मनः ।
मानुष-च्छद्म-रूपस्य शेषस्य धरणी-भृतः

Sri Paraasharar - Balaraama was wandering around in Gokula with all the gopaas. He was taking the form of a human being, He is actually the one who is holding the entire earth, He is Shesha, Aadishesha.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
वने विचरतः तस्य (vane vicarataḥ tasya) — while he wandered in the forest
सह गोपैः महात्मनः (saha gopaiḥ mahātmanaḥ) — with the great-souled cowherds
मानुष-छद्म-रूपस्य (mānuṣa-chadma-rūpasya) — in human-disguise form
शेषस्य धरणी-धृतः (śeṣasya dharaṇī-dhṛtaḥ) — of Śeṣa, bearer of the earth
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vane viharatas tasya saha gopair mahātmanaḥ | mānuṣa-cchadma-rūpasya śeṣasya dharaṇī-bhṛtaḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

VP 5.25.2

निष्पादितोरुकार्यस्य कार्येणोर्वीप्रचारिणः ।
उपभोगार्थमत्यर्थं वरुणः प्राह वारुणीम्

With His great act, Balaraama, had executed all the things in order to help the gods. He had incarnated on earth for the sake of the gods, and had done some great acts here. For His enjoyment, Varuna called Vaaruni, Madiraa and told her thus.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

उपभोगार्थम् अत्यर्थम् (upabhogārtham atyartham) — for abundant enjoyment
वरुणः प्राह वारुणीम् (varuṇaḥ prāha vāruṇīm) — Varuṇa addressed Vāruṇī

VP 5.25.3

अभीष्टां सर्वदा ह्य् अस्य मदिरे त्वं महौजसः ।
अनन्तस्योपभोगाय तस्य गच्छ मुदे शुभे

O Madire, go and give Him all the enjoyments and pleasures, for the enjoyment of Ananta, go happily and please Him. Let Him be pleased.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

अनन्तस्य उपभोगाय (anantasya upabhogāya) — for Ananta’s enjoyment
तस्य गच्छ (tasya gaccha) — go to him
मुदे शुभे (mude śubhe) — for auspicious joy
Transliteration (IAST)
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

abhīṣṭāṃ sarvadā hy asya madire tvaṃ mahaujasaḥ | anantasyopabhogāya tasya gaccha mude śubhe

VP 5.25.9

नाजगाम ततः क्त्रुद्धो हलं जग्राह लाङ्गली

The Yamunaa river saw that He was intoxicated, and not in His proper senses, so she just neglected what He said. Balaraama got very angry, and took His halaayudha, His great plough.

रेवतीं नाम तनयां रैवतस्य महीपतेः ।
उपयेमे बलस्तस्यां जज्ञाते निशितोल्मुकौ

He then married Revati, who was daughter of King Raivata. Through her, he got two sons, Nishita and Ulmuka. This completed Chapter Twenty Five. The story of Krishna and Rukmini will be told very briefly in the next chapter. We just concluded Chapter 25 of Amsha 5.
