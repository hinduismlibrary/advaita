  80. Colophon

Sūta narrates the Śukla lineage and the rulers associated with it. Several passages use historical, religious, and community labels that reflect the source’s own polemics; the translation presents them as claims made by the text, not as modern facts or classifications. English translation and lexical glosses are original to this reader.

।। सूत उवाच ।। — Sūta said.

BP 3.4.3.1

शुक्लवंशं प्रवक्ष्यामि शृणु विप्रवरादितः ।।
यदा कृष्णः स्वयं ब्रह्म त्यक्त्वा भूमिं स्वकं पदम् ।। १ ।।

I will narrate the Śukla lineage; listen from the beginning, best of brahmins. When Kṛṣṇa himself, the Supreme Brahman, left the earth, he went to his own abode.

Transliteration (IAST)

śuklavaṃśaṃ pravakṣyāmi śṛṇu vipravarāditaḥ || yadā kṛṣṇaḥ svayaṃ brahma tyaktvā bhūmiṃ svakaṃ padam || 1 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
śuklavaṃśam — the Śukla lineage
pravakṣyāmi — I shall narrate
śṛṇu vipravara — listen, best of brahmins
kṛṣṇaḥ svayaṃ brahma — Kṛṣṇa himself, Brahman
svakaṃ padam — his own abode

BP 3.4.3.2

दिव्यं वृन्दावनं रम्यं प्रययौ भूतले तदा ।।
कलेरागमनं ज्ञात्वा म्लेच्छपा द्वीपमध्यगे ।। २ ।।

That abode was the lovely divine Vṛndāvana on earth. In the island’s midst, the mleccha rulers learned that Kali was coming.

Transliteration (IAST)

Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

mlecchapāḥ dvīpamadhyage — mleccha rulers in the island’s midst

BP 3.4.3.3

स्थिता द्वीपेषु वै नाना मनुजा वेदतत्पराः ।।
कलिना मित्रधर्मेण दूषितास्ते बभूविरे ।। ३ ।।

Many kinds of people dwelt on the islands, devoted to Vedic teaching; through Kali’s policy of alliance, they became corrupted.

Transliteration (IAST)

अष्टषष्टिसहस्राणां वर्षाणां मुनिसत्तम ।।

Transliteration (IAST)

tatpaścādbhārate varṣe mlecchayā kalirāyayau || dṛṣṭvā tadbhārataṃ varṣaṃ lokapālaiśca pālitam || 6 ||

Transliteration (IAST)

bhayabhītastvarāviṣṭo gandharvāṇāṃ yaśaskaraḥ || sa kaliḥ sūryamārādhya samādhistho babhūva ha ||7 ||

BP 3.4.3.8

सोंशुभिर्लोकमातप्य महावृष्टिमकारयत् ।। ८ ।।

At the end of a hundred years, the pleased Sun appeared. With his rays he scorched the world and caused a great rain.

Transliteration (IAST)

BP 3.4.3.10

न्यूहाख्यो यवनो नाम तेन वै पूरितं जगत् ।। १० ।।

Transliteration (IAST)

BP 3.4.3.11

सहस्राब्दकलौ प्राप्ते महेन्द्रो देवराट् स्वयम् ।।

स्वदेशं पुनरागत्य शिष्यांस्तान्स चकार ह ।। १३ ।।

Transliteration (IAST)

BP 3.4.3.14

नष्टायां सप्तपुर्यां च ब्रह्मावर्तं महोत्तमम् ।।
सरस्वतीदृषद्वत्योर्मध्यगं तत्र चावसत् ।। १४ ।।

When the seven cities had been lost, he dwelt in splendid Brahmāvarta, between the Sarasvatī and Dṛṣadvatī rivers.

BP 3.4.3.15

स्वपुत्रं शुक्लमाहूय द्विजश्रेष्ठं तपोधनम् ।।
