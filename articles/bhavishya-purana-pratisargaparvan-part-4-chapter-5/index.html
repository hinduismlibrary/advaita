  35. Colophon

Sūta narrates cosmic dissolution, the ordering of the ages, and the divine forms associated with them. Translation and lexical glosses present the source’s mythic account.

।। सूत उवाच ।। — Sūta said.

BP 3.4.5.1

मध्याह्नकाले संप्राप्ते ब्रह्मणोऽव्यक्तजन्मनः ।।
चाक्षुषांतरमेवापि महावायुर्बभूव ह ।। १ ।।

When midday arrived in the age of Brahmā’s unmanifest birth, a mighty wind arose, even during the Cākṣuṣa interval.

Transliteration (IAST)

madhyāhnakāle saṃprāpte brahmaṇo'vyaktajanmanaḥ || cākṣuṣāṃtaramevāpi mahāvāyurbabhūva ha || 1 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
madhyāhnakāle saṃprāpte — when midday arrived
brahmaṇo'vyaktajanmanaḥ — of Brahmā’s unmanifest birth
cākṣuṣāntare — within the Cākṣuṣa interval
mahāvāyuḥ babhūva — a great wind arose

BP 3.4.5.2

तत्प्रभावेन हेमाद्रिः कंपमानः पुनःपुनः ।।
यथा वृक्षस्तथैवासौ तत्कंपादेव मंडलः ।।२ ।।

By its force Mount Hemādri trembled repeatedly; its surrounding region shook like a tree.

Transliteration (IAST)

tatprabhāvena hemādriḥ kaṃpamānaḥ punaḥpunaḥ || yathā vṛkṣastathaivāsau tatkaṃpādeva maṃḍalaḥ ||2 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
tatprabhāvena — by its force
hemādriḥ kaṃpamānaḥ — Hemādri trembling
tatkaṃpāt — from that shaking
maṃḍalaḥ — the region

BP 3.4.5.3

नभसो भूतले प्राप्तस्तदा भूमिः प्रकंपिता ।।
बभूव मुनिशार्दूल सर्वलोकविनाशिनी ।। ३ ।।

The sky fell to the earth; then the earth trembled, O tiger among sages, bringing destruction to all the worlds.

Transliteration (IAST)

nabhaso bhūtale prāptastadā bhūmiḥ prakaṃpitā || babhūva muniśārdūla sarvalokavināśinī || 3 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
nabhasaḥ bhūtale — from sky to earth
tadā bhūmiḥ prakaṃpitā — then the earth shook
muniśārdūla — O tiger among sages
sarvalokavināśinī — bringing destruction to all worlds

BP 3.4.5.4

सप्तद्वीपाः समुद्राश्च जलभूता बभूविरे ।।
लोकालोकस्तदा शेषोऽभवत्सोत्तरपर्वतः ।। ४ ।।

The seven islands and oceans became water; only Lokāloka and the northern mountain remained.

Transliteration (IAST)

saptadvīpāḥ samudrāśca jalabhūtā babhūvire || lokālokastadā śeṣo'bhavatsottaraparvataḥ || 4 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
saptadvīpāḥ samudrāḥ — the seven islands and oceans
jalabhūtāḥ — became water
lokālokaḥ — Lokāloka
sottaraparvataḥ — with the northern mountain
śeṣaḥ abhavat — alone remained

BP 3.4.5.5

शेषा भूमिर्लयं प्राप्ता मन्वंतरे लये ।।
सहस्राब्दांतरे भूमिर्बभूव जलमध्यगा।। ५ ।।

The rest of the earth was dissolved in the dissolution of the Manvantara. After a thousand years, the earth arose amid the waters.

Transliteration (IAST)

śeṣā bhūmirlayaṃ prāptā manvaṃtare laye || sahasrābdāṃtare bhūmirbabhūva jalamadhyagā|| 5 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
śeṣā bhūmiḥ — the remaining earth
layaṃ prāptā — entered dissolution
manvantare laye — in the Manvantara dissolution
sahasrābdāntare — after a thousand years
jalamadhyagā — amid water

BP 3.4.5.6

तदा स भगवान्विष्णुर्भवेन विधिना सह ।।
शैशुमारं शुभं चक्रं चकार नभसि स्थितम् ।। ६ ।।

Then Lord Viṣṇu, together with Bhava and following the proper rite, set the auspicious Śiśumāra wheel in the sky.

Transliteration (IAST)

tadā sa bhagavānviṣṇurbhavena vidhinā saha || śaiśumāraṃ śubhaṃ cakraṃ cakāra nabhasi sthitam || 6 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
bhagavān viṣṇuḥ — Lord Viṣṇu
bhavena vidhinā saha — with Bhava and proper rite
śubhaṃ cakram — auspicious wheel
nabhasi sthitam — placed in the sky

BP 3.4.5.7

गृहीत्वा सकलास्तारा ग्रहान्सर्वान्यथाविधि ।।
स्थापयामास भगवान्यथायोग्यं पितामहः ।।७ ।।

Taking all the stars and planets in due order, the Lord, the grandsire, placed each where it belonged.

Transliteration (IAST)

gṛhītvā sakalāstārā grahānsarvānyathāvidhi || sthāpayāmāsa bhagavānyathāyogyaṃ pitāmahaḥ ||7 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
sakalāḥ tārāḥ grahāḥ — all stars and planets
yathāvidhi — in due order
sthāpayāmāsa — placed
yathāyogyam — where fitting
pitāmahaḥ — the grandsire

BP 3.4.5.8

पुनर्वै ज्योतिषां चक्रैः शोषिता सकला मही ।।
स्थलीभूयायुताब्दान्ते दृश्यमाना बभूव ह ।। ८ ।।

The whole earth was dried by the celestial orbits; after ten thousand years it appeared again as solid land.

Transliteration (IAST)

punarvai jyotiṣāṃ cakraiḥ śoṣitā sakalā mahī || sthalībhūyāyutābdānte dṛśyamānā babhūva ha || 8 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
jyotiṣāṃ cakraiḥ — by celestial orbits
sakalā mahī — all the earth
śoṣitā — dried
sthalībhūya — becoming solid land
āyutābdānte — after ten thousand years
dṛśyamānā babhūva — became visible

BP 3.4.5.9

तदा स भगवान्ब्रह्मा मुखात्सोमं चकार ह ।।
द्विजराजं महाप्राज्ञं सर्ववेदविशारदम् ।। ९ ।।

Then Lord Brahmā created Soma from his mouth: the lord of brahmins, greatly wise, and learned in all the Vedas.

Transliteration (IAST)

tadā sa bhagavānbrahmā mukhātsomaṃ cakāra ha || dvijarājaṃ mahāprājñaṃ sarvavedaviśāradam || 9 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
bhagavān brahmā — Lord Brahmā
mukhāt somam cakāra — created Soma from his mouth
dvijarājam — lord of the twice-born
sarvavedaviśāradam — learned in all Vedas

BP 3.4.5.10

भुजाभ्यां भगवान्ब्रह्मा क्षत्रराजं महाबलम् ।।
सूर्यं च जनयामास राजनीतिपरायणम् ।। १० ।।

From his arms Brahmā generated the mighty ruler of the Kṣatriyas: Sūrya, devoted to statecraft.

Transliteration (IAST)

bhujābhyāṃ bhagavānbrahmā kṣatrarājaṃ mahābalam || sūryaṃ ca janayāmāsa rājanītiparāyaṇam || 10 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
bhujābhyām — from the arms
kṣatrarājam mahābalam — the mighty Kṣatriya ruler
sūryam janayāmāsa — generated Sūrya
rājanītiparāyaṇam — devoted to statecraft

BP 3.4.5.11

ऊरुभ्यां वैश्यराजं च समुद्रं सरितां पतिम् ।।
रत्नाकरं च कृतवान्परमेष्ठी पितामहः ।।११ ।।

From his thighs he generated the Vaiśya ruler, the Ocean, lord of rivers; the grandsire also made him the jewel-bearing sea.

Transliteration (IAST)

ūrubhyāṃ vaiśyarājaṃ ca samudraṃ saritāṃ patim || ratnākaraṃ ca kṛtavānparameṣṭhī pitāmahaḥ ||11 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
ūrubhyām — from the thighs
vaiśyarājam — the Vaiśya ruler
samudram saritāṃ patim — the Ocean, lord of rivers
ratnākaram ca — and the jewel-bearing sea

BP 3.4.5.12

पद्भ्यां च जनयामास विश्वकर्माणमुत्तमम् ।।
दक्षं नाम कलाभिज्ञं शूद्रराजं सुकृत्यकम् ।।१२ ।।

From his feet he generated the excellent Viśvakarman, and Dakṣa, skilled in the arts, as the Śūdra ruler devoted to good works.

Transliteration (IAST)

padbhyāṃ ca janayāmāsa viśvakarmāṇamuttamam || dakṣaṃ nāma kalābhijñaṃ śūdrarājaṃ sukṛtyakam ||12 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
padbhyām — from the feet
viśvakarmāṇam uttamam — excellent Viśvakarman
dakṣam kalābhijñam — Dakṣa skilled in arts
śūdrarājam sukṛtyakam — Śūdra ruler devoted to good works

BP 3.4.5.13

सोमाद्वै ब्राह्मणा जाताः सूर्याद्राजन्यवंशजाः ।।
समुद्रात्सकला वैश्या दक्षाच्छूद्रा बभूविरे ।।१३ ।।

Brahmins were born from Soma, royal lineages from Sūrya, all Vaiśyas from the Ocean, and Śūdras from Dakṣa.

Transliteration (IAST)

somādvai brāhmaṇā jātāḥ sūryādrājanyavaṃśajāḥ || samudrātsakalā vaiśyā dakṣācchūdrā babhūvire ||13 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
somāt brāhmaṇāḥ jātāḥ — brahmins were born from Soma
sūryād rājanyavaṃśajāḥ — royal lineages from Sūrya
samudrāt vaiśyā — Vaiśyas from the Ocean
dakṣāt śūdrāḥ — Śūdras from Dakṣa

BP 3.4.5.14

सूर्यमंडलतो जातो मनुर्वैवस्वतः स्वयम् ।।
तस्य राज्यमभूत्सर्वं प्राणिनां लोकवासिनाम् ।।१४ ।।

From the solar orb Manu Vaivasvata himself was born. His rule extended over all living beings dwelling in the worlds.

Transliteration (IAST)

sūryamaṃḍalato jāto manurvaivasvataḥ svayam || tasya rājyamabhūtsarvaṃ prāṇināṃ lokavāsinām ||14 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
sūryamaṇḍalataḥ — from the solar orb
manuḥ vaivasvataḥ — Manu Vaivasvata
svayam jātaḥ — was himself born
sarvaṃ rājyam — all dominion
prāṇinām lokavāsinām — of living beings dwelling in worlds

BP 3.4.5.15

दिव्यानां च युगानां च तज्ज्ञेयं चैकसप्ततिः ।।
तदा स भगवान्विष्णुर्विश्वरूपाऽवतारकः ।। १५ ।।

The span of the divine ages is known as seventy-one [cycles]. Then Lord Viṣṇu became the avatāra called Viśvarūpa.

Transliteration (IAST)

divyānāṃ ca yugānāṃ ca tajjñeyaṃ caikasaptatiḥ || tadā sa bhagavānviṣṇurviśvarūpā'vatārakaḥ || 15 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
divyānāṃ yugānām — of the divine ages
ekasaptatiḥ — seventy-one
tajjñeyam — that is to be known
bhagavān viṣṇuḥ — Lord Viṣṇu
viśvarūpāvatārakaḥ — became the Viśvarūpa avatāra

BP 3.4.5.16

विष्णुः पूर्वार्द्धतो जातः परार्द्धाद्वामनः स्वयम् ।।
बालः सत्ययुगे देवो विश्वरूपः सनातनः ।।१६ ।।

Viṣṇu was born from the first half, and Vāmana himself from the latter half. In the Satya age the eternal deity appeared as a child.

Transliteration (IAST)

viṣṇuḥ pūrvārddhato jātaḥ parārddhādvāmanaḥ svayam || bālaḥ satyayuge devo viśvarūpaḥ sanātanaḥ ||16 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
viṣṇuḥ pūrvārddhataḥ jātaḥ — Viṣṇu born from the first half
parārddhāt vāmanaḥ — Vāmana from the latter half
bālaḥ satyayuge — child in Satya age
viśvarūpaḥ sanātanaḥ — eternal Viśvarūpa

BP 3.4.5.17

चतुश्शतानि वर्षाणि परमायुर्नृणां तदा ।।
त्रेतायां यौवनं प्राप्तः पूर्वार्द्धात्संभवो हरेः ।। १७ ।।

Then the maximum human lifespan was four hundred years. In Tretā, Hari, born from the first half, reached youth.

Transliteration (IAST)

catuśśatāni varṣāṇi paramāyurnṛṇāṃ tadā || tretāyāṃ yauvanaṃ prāptaḥ pūrvārddhātsaṃbhavo hareḥ || 17 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
catuśśatāni varṣāṇi — four hundred years
paramāyuḥ nṛṇām — human maximum lifespan
tretāyām yauvanam — young adulthood in Tretā
hareḥ sambhavaḥ — Hari’s appearance

BP 3.4.5.18

वर्षाणां त्रिशतानां च नृणामायुः प्रकीर्तितम् ।।
द्वापरे वार्द्धिको देवो नृणामायुः शतद्वयम् ।। १८ ।।

Human life in Tretā was said to be three hundred years; in Dvāpara the deity was mature, and human life was two hundred years.

Transliteration (IAST)

varṣāṇāṃ triśatānāṃ ca nṛṇāmāyuḥ prakīrtitam || dvāpare vārddhiko devo nṛṇāmāyuḥ śatadvayam || 18 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

āyuḥ śatadvayam — lifespan two hundred years

BP 3.4.5.19

कलौ तु मरणं प्राप्तो विश्वरूपो हरिः स्वयम् ।।
नृणामायुः शताब्दं च केषांचिद्धर्मशालिनाम् ।। १९ ।।

In Kali, Viśvarūpa Hari himself met death. Human life was a hundred years, for some who lived righteously.

Transliteration (IAST)

kalau tu maraṇaṃ prāpto viśvarūpo hariḥ svayam || nṛṇāmāyuḥ śatābdaṃ ca keṣāṃciddharmaśālinām || 19 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

BP 3.4.5.20

परार्द्धाद्वामनो देवो महेन्द्रावरजो हरिः ।।
चतुर्भुजो महाश्यामो गरुडोपरि संस्थितः ।। २० ।।

From the latter half came Vāmana, Hari, four-armed and very dark, the younger brother of Mahendra, mounted upon Garuḍa.

Transliteration (IAST)

parārddhādvāmano devo mahendrāvarajo hariḥ || caturbhujo mahāśyāmo garuḍopari saṃsthitaḥ || 20 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

BP 3.4.5.21

विश्वरूपहितार्थाय त्रियुगी संबभूव ह ।।
वामनार्द्धाच्च त्रियुगी जातो नारायणः स्वयम्।।२१ ।।

For Viśvarūpa’s benefit he became Triyugī; from Vāmana’s latter half Nārāyaṇa himself was born as Triyugī.

Transliteration (IAST)

viśvarūpahitārthāya triyugī saṃbabhūva ha || vāmanārddhācca triyugī jāto nārāyaṇaḥ svayam||21 ||

BP 3.4.5.22

श्वेतरूपो हरिः सत्ये हंसाख्यो भगवान्स्वयम् ।।
त्रेतायां रक्तरूपश्च यज्ञाख्यो भगवान्स्वयम् ।।
द्वापरे पीतरूपश्च स्वर्णगर्भो हरिः स्वयम् ।।२२ ।।

In Satya, Hari was white and called Haṃsa; in Tretā he was red and called Yajña; in Dvāpara he was yellow and called Svarṇagarbha.

Transliteration (IAST)

śvetarūpo hariḥ satye haṃsākhyo bhagavānsvayam || tretāyāṃ raktarūpaśca yajñākhyo bhagavānsvayam || dvāpare pītarūpaśca svarṇagarbho hariḥ svayam ||22 ||

Word-for-word

BP 3.4.5.23

कलिकाले तु संप्राप्ते संध्यायां द्वापरे युगे ।।
कला तु सकला विष्णोर्वामनस्य तथा कला ।।
एकीभूता च देवक्यां जातो विष्णुस्तदा स्वयम् ।। २३ ।।

When Kali arrived at the twilight of Dvāpara, all the portions of Viṣṇu, including Vāmana’s portion, united; Viṣṇu himself was born in Devakī.

kalikāle tu saṃprāpte saṃdhyāyāṃ dvāpare yuge || kalā tu sakalā viṣṇorvāmanasya tathā kalā || ekībhūtā ca devakyāṃ jāto viṣṇustadā svayam || 23 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

BP 3.4.5.24

वसुदेवगृहे रम्ये मथुरायां च देवताः ।।
ब्रह्माद्यास्तुष्टुवुर्देवं परं ब्रह्म सनातनम् ।। २४ ।।

In the lovely house of Vasudeva at Mathurā, the gods led by Brahmā praised the eternal Supreme Brahman.

Transliteration (IAST)

vasudevagṛhe ramye mathurāyāṃ ca devatāḥ || brahmādyāstuṣṭuvurdevaṃ paraṃ brahma sanātanam || 24 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

mathurāyām — in Mathurā
