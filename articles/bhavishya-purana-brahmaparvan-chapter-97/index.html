Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 97 — Jayantī Saptamī

BP 1.97.1

ब्रह्मोवाच। माघस्य शुक्लपक्षे तु सप्तमी या त्रिलोचन। जयन्ती नाम सा प्रोक्ता पुण्या पापहरा शिवा ॥

Brahmā said: O Three-eyed One, the Saptamī that occurs in the bright fortnight of Māgha is called Jayantī; it is meritorious, sin-destroying, and auspicious.

BP 1.97.8
