Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 97 — The Victorious Seventh Day Observance

BP 1.97.1

ब्रह्मोवाच। माघस्य शुक्लपक्षे तु सप्तमी या त्रिलोचन। जयन्ती नाम सा प्रोक्ता पुण्या पापहरा शिवा ॥

Brahma spoke. The seventh day in the bright fortnight of Māgha, O Three Eyed One, is called Jayantī, meritorious, sin removing, and auspicious.

brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said;
māghasya — of Māgha;
śukla-pakṣe — in the bright fortnight;
tu — indeed;
saptamī — the seventh lunar day;
yā — which;
trilocana — O Three-eyed One;
jayantī nāma — named Jayantī;
sā — that;
proktā — is called;
puṇyā — meritorious;
pāpa-harā — destroying sin;
śivā — auspicious.

BP 1.97.2

सोपोष्या येन विधिना शृणु तं पार्वतीप्रिय। पारणानि तु चत्वारि कथितान्यत्र पण्डितैः ॥

Hear the procedure by which it is observed with fasting, O beloved of Pārvatī. The learned have declared four fast breakings here.

pāraṇāni — breakings of the fast;
tu — indeed;
catvāri — four;
kathitāni — are taught;
atra — here;
paṇḍitaiḥ — by the learned.

BP 1.97.3

पञ्चम्यामेकभक्तं तु षष्ठ्यां नक्तं प्रकीर्तितम्। उपवासस्तु सप्तम्यामष्टम्यां पारणं भवेत् ॥

One meal is prescribed on the fifth day. Eating at night is proclaimed on the sixth. Fasting is on the seventh, and fast breaking should be on the eighth.

pañcamyām — on the fifth day;
eka-bhaktam — one meal;
tu — indeed;

tu — and;
saptamyām — on the seventh day;
aṣṭamyām — on the eighth day;
pāraṇam — breaking the fast;
bhavet — should be.

BP 1.97.4

माघे च फाल्गुने मासि तथा चैत्रे च सुव्रत। बकपुष्पाणि रम्याणि कुङ्कुमं च विलेपनम् ॥

In Māgha, Phālguna, and Caitra, O steadfast one, are beautiful agati flowers and saffron for anointing.

māghe — in Māgha;
ca — and;

tathā — likewise;
caitre — in Caitra;
ca — and;
suvrata — O steadfast one;

ramyāṇi — beautiful;
kuṅkumam — saffron;
ca — and;
vilepanam — anointing.

BP 1.97.5

नैवेद्यं मोदकांश्चात्र धूप आज्यमुदाहृतः। प्राशनं पञ्चगव्यं तु पवित्रीकरणं परम् ॥

Sweet dumplings are the offering here, and ghee is declared to be the incense. Partaking of the five cow products is the highest purification.

naivedyam — offering;
modakān — modakas;
ca — and;
atra — here;
dhūpaḥ — incense;
ājya — ghee;
udāhṛtaḥ — is prescribed;
prāśanam — partaking;
pañca-gavyam — the five products of the cow;
tu — indeed;
pavitrī-karaṇam — purification;
param — supreme.

BP 1.97.6

मोदकैर्भोजयेद्विप्रान्यथाशक्त्या गणाधिप। शाल्योदनं च भूतेश दद्याच्छक्त्या द्विजेषु वै ॥

One should feed the Brahmins with sweet dumplings according to ability, O lord of the hosts. One should give cooked fine rice to the twice born according to ability, O Lord of Beings.

modakaiḥ — with modakas;
bhojayet — should feed;
viprān — the Brāhmaṇas;
yathā-śaktyā — according to ability;
gaṇa-adhipa — O lord of the Gaṇas;
śāli-odanam — cooked śāli rice;
ca — and;
bhūta-īśa — O Lord of beings;
dadyāt — should give;
śaktyā — according to means;
dvijeṣu — to the twice-born;
vai — indeed.

BP 1.97.7

इत्थं सम्पूजयेद्यस्तु भास्करं लोकपूजितम्। सर्वस्मिन्पारणे वीर सोऽश्वमेधफलं लभेत् ॥

Whoever thus worships Bhāskara, worshipped by the world, obtains the fruit of the horse sacrifice at every fast breaking, O hero.

ittham — thus;
sampūjayet — fully worships;
yaḥ tu — whoever;
bhāskaram — Bhāskara;
loka-pūjitam — worshiped by the world;
sarvasmin pāraṇe — at every breaking of the fast;
vīra — O hero;
saḥ — he;
aśvamedha-phalam — the fruit of the Aśvamedha;
labhet — obtains.

BP 1.97.8

द्वितीये पारणे पूज्य राजसूयफलं लभेत्। वैशाखाषाढज्येष्ठेषु श्रावणे मासि सुव्रत। पूजार्थमथ भानोर्वै शतपत्राणि सुव्रत ॥

Having worshipped at the second fast breaking, one obtains the fruit of the royal consecration. In Vaiśākha, Āṣāḍha, Jyeṣṭha, and Śrāvaṇa, O steadfast one, hundred petaled lotuses are for the worship of the Sun.

dvitīye pāraṇe — at the second breaking;
pūjya — having worshiped;
rājasūya-phalam — fruit of the Rājasūya;
labhet — one obtains;
vaiśākha-āṣāḍha-jyeṣṭheṣu — in Vaiśākha, Āṣāḍha, and Jyeṣṭha;
śrāvaṇe māsi — in the month of Śrāvaṇa;
suvrata — O steadfast one;
pūjā-artham — for worship;
atha — then;
bhānoḥ — of Bhānu;
vai — indeed;
śata-patrāṇi — hundred-petaled flowers.

BP 1.97.9

श्वेतं च चन्दनं भीम धूपो गुग्गुलुरुच्यते। नैवेद्यं गुडपूपास्तु प्राशनं गोमयस्य तु। भोजने चापि विप्राणां गुडपूपाः प्रकीर्तिताः ॥

White sandalwood, O Bhīma. Guggul resin is called the incense. Jaggery cakes are the offering, and cow dung is to be eaten. Jaggery cakes are also prescribed for the Brahmins meal.

śvetam — white;
ca — and;
candanam — sandalwood;
bhīma — O Bhīma;
dhūpaḥ — incense;
gugguluḥ — guggulu;
ucyate — is said;
naivedyam — offering;
guḍapūpāḥ — guḍapūpas;
tu — indeed;
prāśanam — tasting;
gomayasya — of cow dung;
bhojane — in the meal;
ca api — and also;
viprāṇām — of the Brāhmaṇas;
prakīrtitāḥ — are prescribed.

BP 1.97.10

द्वितीयमिदमाख्यातं पारणं पापनाशनम्। राजसूयाश्वमेधाभ्यां फलदं भास्करप्रियम् ॥

This second fast breaking is declared to destroy sin, to give the fruits of the royal consecration and horse sacrifice, and to be dear to Bhāskara.

dvitīyam — second;
idam — this;
ākhyātam — is declared;
pāraṇam — breaking of the fast;
pāpa-nāśanam — destroyer of sin;
rājasūya-aśvamedhābhyām — with the fruits of the Rājasūya and Aśvamedha;
phaladam — fruit-giving;
bhāskara-priyam — dear to Bhāskara.

BP 1.97.11

तृतीयं शृणु देवस्य पूजार्थे भास्करस्य तु। मासि भाद्रपदे वीर तथा चाश्वयुजे विभो ॥

Hear the third observance for the worship of Bhāskara, O hero. It is in the month of Bhādrapada and likewise in Āśvayuja, O Lord.

tṛtīyam — third;
śṛṇu — hear;
devasya — of the deity;
pūjā-arthe — for worship;
bhāskarasya — of Bhāskara;
tu — indeed;
māsi — in the month;
bhādrapade — in Bhādrapada;
vīra — O hero;
tathā — likewise;
ca — and;
āśvayuje — in Āśvayuja;
vibho — O Lord.

BP 1.97.12

कार्त्तिके चापि मासे तु रक्तचन्दनमादिशेत्। मालतीकुसुमानीह धूपो विजय उच्यते ॥

In Kārttika month too one should prescribe red sandalwood. Jasmine flowers are here. Victory is said to be the incense.

kārttike māse — in the month of Kārttika;
ca api — also;
tu — indeed;
rakta-candanam — red sandalwood;
ādiśet — one should prescribe;
mālatī-kusumāni — mālatī flowers;
iha — here;
dhūpaḥ — incense;
vijayaḥ — vijaya;
ucyate — is said.

BP 1.97.13

नैवेद्यं घृतपूपास्तु भोजनं च द्विजन्मनाम्। कुशोदकप्राशनं तु कायशुद्धिकरं परम् ॥

Ghee cakes are the offering and the food of the twice born. Partaking of water with kusha grass is the highest purification of the body.

ghṛta-pūpāḥ — ghṛtapūpas;

tu — and;
kāya-śuddhi-karam — making the body pure;
param — supreme.

BP 1.97.14

तृतीयमपि चाख्यातं पारणं पापनाशनम्। राजसूयाश्वमेधाभ्यां फलदं भास्करप्रियम् ॥

The third fast breaking is also declared to destroy sin, to give the fruits of the royal consecration and horse sacrifice, and to be dear to Bhāskara.

BP 1.97.15

चतुर्थमपि ते वच्मि पारणं पापनाशनम्। राजसूयाश्वमेधाभ्यां फलदं भास्करप्रियम् ॥

BP 1.97.16

तदद्य देवशार्दूल पारणं श्रेयसे शृणु। मासि मार्गशिरे वीर पौषे मासि तथा शिव ॥

Hear now the fast breaking for welfare, O tiger among the gods. It is in the month of Mārgaśīrṣa, O hero, and likewise in Pauṣa, O auspicious one.

BP 1.97.17

माघे च देवशार्दूल शृणु पुण्यान्यशेषतः। करवीराणि रक्तानि तथा रक्तं च चन्दनम् ॥

And in Māgha, O tiger among the gods, hear all the meritorious details. Red oleander flowers and red sandalwood likewise.

BP 1.97.18

अमृताख्यस्तथा धूपो नैवेद्यं पायसं परम्। आर्जनीयं तथा तक्रं प्राशनं परमं स्मृतम् ॥

The incense is called Nectar. The excellent offering is milk pudding. The procurable thing and buttermilk are remembered as the highest intake.

BP 1.97.19

अगरुं चन्दनं मुस्तं सिह्लकं त्र्यूषणं तथा। समभागैस्तु कर्तव्यमिदं चामृतमुच्यते ॥

Agarwood, sandalwood, nutgrass, olibanum, and the three pungent spices are to be prepared in equal parts. This is called nectar.

BP 1.97.20

नामानि कथितान्यत्र भास्करस्य महात्मनः। चित्रभानुस्तथा भानुरादित्यो भास्करस्तथा ॥

BP 1.97.21

प्रीयतामिति सर्वस्मिन्पारणे विधिमादिशेत्। अनेन विधिना यस्तु कुर्यात्पूजां विभावसोः ॥

BP 1.97.22

BP 1.97.23

पुत्रार्थी लभते पुत्रान्धनार्थी लभते धनम्। सरोगो मुच्यते रोगैः शुभमाप्नोति पुष्कलम् ॥

BP 1.97.24

पूर्णे संवत्सरे भीम कार्या पूजा दिवाकरे। गन्धपुष्पोपहारैस्तु ब्राह्मणानां च तर्पणैः। नानाविधैः प्रेक्षणकैः पूजया वाचकस्य तु ॥

BP 1.97.26

धर्मकार्येषु मे देव अर्थकार्येषु नित्यशः। कामकार्येषु सर्वेषु जयो भवतु सर्वदा ॥

BP 1.97.27

Then one should dismiss the Brahmins and the excellent twice born reciter. Whoever does this thus obtains the fruit of victory. With his self purified from all sins, he goes to the world of the Sun.

BP 1.97.28

विमानवरमारुढः कञ्जजोद्भवमुत्तमम्। तेजसा रविसंकाशः प्रभया पतगोपमः ॥

Mounted on an excellent celestial vehicle born from the Lotus Born, he is like the Sun in splendor and like a bird in radiance.
