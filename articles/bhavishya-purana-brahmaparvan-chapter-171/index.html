Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 171 — Observance of Feeding Sun-Priests

BP 1.171.1

शतानीक उवाच ।। मगानां ब्रूहि मे धर्मं समासव्यासयोगतः ।। फलं च किं भवेद्ब्रह्मन्मगधर्मनिषेवणात् ।। १ ।।

Śatānīka said: Tell me the dharma of the Magas, in brief and in full, and the fruit of following Maga-dharma, O Brahmin.

BP 1.171.2

सुमंतुरुवाच ।। य एष धर्मः सूर्येति तवाख्यातो मयानघ ।। मगधर्मः स एवोक्तः सर्वपापभयापहः ।। २ ।।

Sumantu said: O sinless one, that dharma which I told you as the Sun’s is itself declared Maga-dharma, remover of all sin and fear.

BP 1.171.3

सर्वेषामेव वर्णानां मगधर्मनिषेवणम् ।। मगधर्मश्च संप्रोक्त एतेषां भवमुक्तये ।। ३ ।।

The practice of Maga-dharma is for all varṇas; Maga-dharma is declared for their release from existence.

BP 1.171.4

ब्राह्मणाः क्षत्रिया वैश्याः स्त्री शूद्रो वा मगाश्रमी ।। यः पूजयति मार्तण्डं स याति परमां गतिम् ।। ४ ।।

A Brahmin, Kṣatriya, Vaiśya, woman, Śūdra, or resident of a Maga hermitage—whoever worships the Sun reaches the highest state.

BP 1.171.5

त्रिसन्ध्यमर्चयेद्भानुमग्निकार्यं च शक्तितः ।। कुर्यान्मगो महाबाहो मुखमावृत्य यत्नतः ।। ५ ।।

A Maga should worship the Sun at the three twilight periods and perform the fire-rite according to his ability; O mighty-armed one, he should do this carefully with his mouth covered.

BP 1.171.6

त्रिसन्ध्यमेककालं वा पूजयेत्श्रद्धया रविम् ।। असंपूज्य रविं मोहान्न भुंजीत कदाचन ।। ६ ।।

One should worship the Sun with faith at the three twilight periods or once; never, through delusion, should one eat without worshipping the Sun.

BP 1.171.7

एष धर्मः परो ज्ञेयः शेषो भवति मानवः ।। अपूजयित्वा भुंजानो विष्ठां भुङ्क्ते च वै मगः ।। ७ ।।

This dharma is to be known as highest; the rest is merely human. A Maga who eats without worshipping indeed eats excrement.

BP 1.171.8

देवं समाश्रितैः पूजा कर्तव्येयं त्रिभिः सदा ।। मनसा पूजयेद्योगी पुष्पैश्चारण्यसंभवैः ।। ८ ।।

Those who have taken refuge in the god should always perform this worship in three ways; the yogi should worship with the mind and with flowers grown in the forest.

BP 1.171.9

देवार्थपुष्पहिंसायां न भवेत्तस्य हिंसकः ।। यद्यल्पमपि चात्मार्थं निहन्याद्धिंसकस्तदा ।। ९ ।।

By harming flowers for the god, one does not become a killer; but if one kills even a little for oneself, then one is a killer.

BP 1.171.10

मगश्चाग्निपरो नित्यं तद्भक्तोऽतिथिपूजकः ।। मगी मैथुनवर्ज्यः स्यात्श्रीमान्गृहमगाश्रमी ।। १० ।।

A Maga should always be devoted to fire and a devotee of it, worshipping the guest; a Maga woman should abstain from sexual union; the prosperous Maga lives in the householder’s āśrama.

BP 1.171.11

देवाग्निस्वतिथौ भक्तं पचते चात्मकारणात् ।। आत्मार्थे यः पचेन्मोहात्स मगो नरकं व्रजेत् ।। ११ ।।

He cooks food for the god, fire, guest, and himself; whoever through delusion cooks for himself alone goes to hell.

BP 1.171.12

देवार्थे पचनं येषां सन्तानार्थं तु मैथुनम् ।। अर्थो दानार्थ उद्दिष्टो नरकं हि विपर्ययात् ।। १२ ।।

For them, cooking is for the god, sexual union is for offspring, and wealth is appointed for giving; the reverse indeed leads to hell.

BP 1.171.13

जीवतृतीयभागेऽपि न प्रकुर्वीत वार्चनम् ।। वित्तार्जने तदर्धेन यतो नित्यं हि जीवितम् ।। १३ ।।

One should not devote even a third part of life to worship; half of that should be spent earning wealth, for livelihood is always necessary.

BP 1.171.14

न्यायोपार्जितवित्तः स्यादन्यायं परिवर्जयेत् ।। अन्यायार्जितवित्तैस्तु कुर्वन्नरकमाप्नुयात् ।। १४ ।।

One should have wealth earned justly and shun injustice; whoever acts with wealth earned unjustly goes to hell.

BP 1.171.15

वाचोर्थे ब्रह्मचारी यः सूर्यपूजाग्नितत्परः ।। भवेज्जितेन्द्रियः शान्तो नैष्ठिको भौतिकोऽपि वा ।। १५ ।।

He who is celibate in speech, devoted to Sun-worship and fire, becomes self-controlled and peaceful, whether a lifelong celibate or a householder.

BP 1.171.16

सर्वगन्धविनिर्मुक्तः कन्दमूलफलाशनः ।। मम वैखानसो ज्ञेयः सूर्यपूजाग्नितत्परः ।। १६ ।।

Free from all fragrances, eating tubers, roots, and fruit, my forest hermit is known as devoted to Sun-worship and fire.

BP 1.171.17

निवृत्तः सङ्गमेभ्यस्तु सूर्यध्यानरतः सदा ।। ज्ञेयः सौरयतीन्द्राय पूजानिष्ठो जितेन्द्रियः ।। १७ ।।

Withdrawn from unions, always intent on meditation on the Sun, devoted to worship and master of the senses, he is known as a solar ascetic.

BP 1.171.18

मुण्डोपनयनो व्यङ्गी शुक्लवासः समन्वितः ।। ज्ञेयं तदर्चनस्थानमेतत्कार्यं प्रयत्नतः ।। १८ ।।

Shaven-headed, invested with the sacred thread, marked, and clothed in white: this is to be known as the place of worship, and is to be done with care.

BP 1.171.19

अथाव्यङ्गो महाराज धारयेद्यस्तु भोजकः ।। अगम्यं सर्वसत्त्वानां सूर्यलोकं स गच्छति ।। १९ ।।

Then, O great king, the unblemished Sun-priest who bears this reaches the Sun’s world, inaccessible to all beings.

BP 1.171.20

ध्वंसनं सर्वदुष्टानां सर्वपापभयापहम् ।। भावशुद्धेन सततमर्चनीयो दिवाकरः ।। २० ।।

The Sun, destroyer of all the wicked, remover of all sin and fear, should always be worshipped with purity of heart.

BP 1.171.21

गन्धलेपविहीनोऽपि भावशुद्धो न दुष्यति ।। भावेषु च चरेच्छौचं वस्त्रपूतं जलं पिबेत् ।। २१ ।।

Though without fragrant unguent, one pure in heart is not tainted; one should maintain purity of thought and drink water strained through cloth.

BP 1.171.22

दृष्टिपूतं न्यसेत्पादं सत्यपूतं वचो वदेत् ।। सौरध्यानरताः शान्ताः सौरधर्मपरायणाः ।। २२ ।।

Look before placing the foot, and speak words purified by truth. Those intent on meditation on the Sun are peaceful and devoted to the Sun’s dharma.

BP 1.171.23

सर्व एवाश्रमा ज्ञेया भास्कराङ्गसमुद्भवाः ।। भोजकाष्टव्रतं धार्यं रविणोक्तमनौपमम् ।। २३ ।।

All the stages of life are to be known as arising from the limbs of the Sun; one should uphold the incomparable eight vows of a Sun-priest, taught by the Sun.

BP 1.171.24

सर्वव्रतानां परमं धर्मालयमनुत्तमम् ।। सौरभक्ते सदा क्षान्तिरहिंसा सर्वदा शमः ।। २४ ।।

The highest among all vows, the unsurpassed abode of dharma: for the Sun-devotee, patience always, non-violence at all times, and calmness.

BP 1.171.25

सन्तोषः सत्यं स्तेयं ब्रह्मचर्यं तथाष्टमम् ।। यथासम्भवपूजाभिः कर्मणा मनसा गिरा ।। २५ ।।

Contentment, truth, non-stealing, and celibacy as the eighth; worship as possible, by action, mind, and speech.

BP 1.171.26

सौरभक्तिः सदा कार्या भोजकेषु विशेषतः ।। स्वदेहान्निर्विशेषं हि भोजकान्पालयेद्बुधः ।। २६ ।।

Devotion to the Sun should always be practiced, especially toward Sun-priests; the wise should protect them as their own body, without distinction.

BP 1.171.27

भयदारिद्र्यरोगेभ्यस्तेषां कुर्यात्प्रियाणि वै ।। सूर्यस्य परिपूर्णस्य किं नाम क्रियते नरैः ।। २७ ।।

One should do for them what wards off fear, poverty, and disease; what indeed can people do for the complete Sun?

BP 1.171.28

यत्कृतं भोजकानां वै तत्कृतं स्याद्रवेर्नृप ।। सुदूरमपि गन्तव्यं मगानां यत्र वै गणः ।। २८ ।।

Whatever is done for Sun-priests is indeed done for the Sun, O king; one should go even a great distance to wherever there is a company of Magas.

BP 1.171.29

स च प्रयत्नाद्द्रष्टव्यस्तत्र सन्निहितो रविः ।। भोजकस्य तु भक्तस्य सूर्यपूजारतस्य च ।। २९ ।।

He should be seen there with care; there the Sun is present with the devoted Sun-priest who is intent on Sun-worship.

BP 1.171.30

आज्ञां कृत्वा यथान्यायमश्वमेधफलं लभेत् ।। देवाश्रमगतो भक्त्या देवार्चां पूजयेनृप ।। ३० ।।

By carrying out the command according to rule, one gains the fruit of the horse sacrifice; having gone to a divine hermitage, one should worship the deity-image with devotion, O king.

BP 1.171.31

स्वागतासनपाद्यार्घ्यमधुपर्काद्यनुक्रमात् ।। भोजयित्वा यथान्यायं सूर्यलोके महीयते ।। ३१ ।।

In due order, with welcome, a seat, water for the feet, a water-offering, a honey-mixture, and the rest, having fed him properly, one is honored in the Sun’s world.

BP 1.171.32

प्रतिश्रयप्रदानेन राजा भवति भारत ।। दत्त्वा स्थानं तथा शौचं वारुणं लोकमाप्नुयात् ।। ३२ ।।

By giving shelter one becomes a king, O Bharata; having given a place and cleanliness, one reaches Varuṇa’s world.

BP 1.171.33

श्वेतबिन्दुपरीताङ्गं ध्यानश्रमविकर्शितम् ।। संविज्य तालवृन्तेन वायुलोके महीयते ।। ३३ ।।

Having fanned with a palm-leaf fan the one whose body is covered with white drops and worn by the toil of meditation, one is honored in the world of Vāyu.

BP 1.171.34

क्षुत्पिपासातुरं श्रान्तं मलिनं रोगिणं तथा ।। पालयित्वा यथाशक्त्या सर्वान्कामानवाप्नुयात् ।। ३४ ।।

Having cared for one distressed by hunger and thirst, weary, dirty, and ill, according to one’s ability, one obtains all desires.

BP 1.171.35

पतिताशस्तसङ्कीर्णचण्डालादीनां पक्षिणाम् ।। कारुण्यात्सर्वभूतानां देयमन्नं स्वशक्तितः ।। ३५ ।।

Out of compassion for all beings, food should be given according to one’s ability to the fallen, the despised, people of mixed status, outcastes, birds, and others.

BP 1.171.36

अत्यल्पमपि कारुण्याद्दत्तं भवति चाक्षयम् ।। तस्मात्सर्वेषु भूतेषु देवकारुण्यमुच्यते ।। ३६ ।।

Even the very little given out of compassion becomes imperishable; therefore compassion toward all beings is called divine compassion.

BP 1.171.37

अभावे तृणभूम्यन्नं पत्रं धनफलानि च ।। दत्त्वाऽऽगताय प्रणतः स्वर्गं याति प्रियेण वा ।। ३७ ।।

When means are lacking, one should give grass, a place, food, leaves, wealth, and fruits to the arriving guest; having bowed, one goes to heaven by pleasing him.

BP 1.171.38

न हीदृक्स्वर्गयानाय यथा लोके प्रियं वचः ।। इहामुत्र सुखं तेषां वाग्येषां मधुरा भवेत् ।। ३८ ।।

No vehicle to heaven in this world is like kind speech; those whose words are sweet have happiness here and hereafter.

BP 1.171.39

अमृतस्यन्दिनीं वाचं चन्दनस्पर्शशीतलाम् ।। धर्माविरोधिनीमुक्त्वा सुखमक्षय्यमाप्नुयात् ।। ३९ ।।

Having spoken words that flow like nectar, cool as the touch of sandalwood, and not opposed to dharma, one obtains imperishable happiness.

BP 1.171.40

अलं दानेन राजेन्द्र पूजयाध्यापनेन वा ।। इदं स्वर्गस्य सोपानमचलं यत्प्रियं वचः ।। ४० ।।

Enough of giving, worship, or teaching, O king of kings; kind speech is the firm stairway to heaven.

BP 1.171.41

पूजाभिभाषणं दृष्टिः प्रत्येकं स्वर्गहेतवः ।। सम्पृच्छेदागतं भक्त्या कुशलं प्रश्नमादरात् ।। ४१ ।।

Worship, address, and sight are each causes of heaven; with devotion and respect, one should ask the welfare of the one who has arrived.

BP 1.171.42

गमने तस्य वक्तव्यं पन्थानः सन्तु ते शिवाः ।। सुखं भवतु ते नित्यं सर्वकार्यकरं भृशम् ।। ४२ ।।

When he departs, one should say to him, “May your paths be auspicious; may happiness always be yours, accomplishing all your tasks.”

BP 1.171.43

आशीर्वादमिदं वाक्यं सर्वकालेषु सर्वदा ।। नमस्कारादिवाक्येषु स्वस्ति मङ्गलवादने ।। ४३ ।।

This speech is a blessing at every time, always: salutations and such words, the utterance of welfare and auspiciousness.

BP 1.171.44

शिवं भवतु ते नित्यं तं ब्रूयात्सर्वकर्मसु ।। एवमादि च वाचारमनुष्ठाय सदाश्रमी ।। ४४ ।।

In all actions, one should say to him, “May well-being be yours always.” One devoted to the stages of life should practice such and like speech.

BP 1.171.45

अशेषपापनिर्मुक्तः सूर्यलोके महीयते ।। सूर्यभक्ते तु या भक्तिः सद्भक्तैः क्रियते नरैः ।। सूर्ये भक्तिसमा नित्यं भक्ते भक्तिरनुष्ठिता ।। ४५ ।।

Freed from every sin, one is honored in the Sun’s world. The devotion that good men show to a Sun-devotee is always equal to devotion to the Sun; devotion is practiced toward the devotee.

BP 1.171.46

आक्रुष्टे ताडिते वापि यो नाक्रोशेन्न ताडयेत् ।। वाक्यादधिकृतः स्वस्थः स दुःखात्परिमुच्यते ।। ४६ ।।

Whether insulted or struck, whoever neither insults nor strikes back, self-controlled in speech and composed, is released from suffering.

BP 1.171.47

सर्वेषामेव तीर्थानां क्षान्तिः परमपूजिता ।। तस्मात्पूर्वं प्रयत्नेन क्षान्तिः कार्या क्रियासु वै ।। ४७ ।।

Of all sacred fords, patience is most honored; therefore patience should first be practiced with effort in actions.

BP 1.171.48

ज्ञानयोगतपो यस्य यज्ञदानानि सत्क्रिया ।। क्रोधनस्य वृथा यस्मात्तस्मात्क्रोधं विवर्जयेत् ।। ४८ ।।

For an angry person, knowledge, yoga, austerity, sacrifices, gifts, and good actions are fruitless; therefore one should abandon anger.

BP 1.171.49

मर्मास्थिप्राणहृदयं निर्दहेदप्रियंवचः ।। न वचो ह्यप्रियं तस्माद्भोजकेषु विशेषतः ।। ४९ ।।

Unpleasant speech burns the vital parts, bones, breath, and heart; therefore one should speak no unpleasant words, especially to Sun-priests.

BP 1.171.50

क्षमा दानं त्विषः सत्यं क्षमाहिंसार्कसम्भवाः ।। न शक्या विस्तराद्वक्तुमपि वर्षशतैरपि ।। ५० ।।

Forbearance, giving, splendor, truth, patience, and non-violence born of the Sun cannot be described at length even in a hundred years.
