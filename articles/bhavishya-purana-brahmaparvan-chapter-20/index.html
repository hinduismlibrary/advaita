Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 20 — The Aśūnya-śayana or Phala-dvitīyā Vow

BP 1.20.1

शतानीक उवाच
ब्रूहि मे द्विजशार्दूल द्वितीयां फलसंज्ञिताम् ।
यामुपोष्य नरो योषिद्वियोगं नेह गच्छति ॥

Śatānīka said: ‘Tiger among brāhmaṇas, tell me of the second lunar day called Phala, by fasting on which neither man nor woman suffers separation here.

BP 1.20.2

पत्न्या नरो मुनिश्रेष्ठ भार्या च पतिना सह ।
तामहं श्रोतुमिच्छामि विधवा स्त्री न जायते ॥
उपोषितेन येनार्य पत्न्या च सहितो नरः ॥

O best sage, a man remains with his wife and a wife with her husband; a woman does not become widowed when she and her husband observe this fast. I wish to hear of it.

BP 1.20.3

तन्मे ब्रूहि द्विजश्रेष्ठ श्रेयोऽर्थं नरयोषिताम् ।
येन मे कौतुकं ब्रह्मञ्छ्रुत्वापूर्वं प्रसर्पति ॥

Best of brāhmaṇas, explain it for the welfare of men and women; hearing of this unprecedented observance has awakened my curiosity.

BP 1.20.4

सुमन्तुरुवाच
अशून्यशयनां नाम द्वितीयां शृणु भारत ।
यामुपोष्य न वैधव्यं स्त्री प्रयाति नराधिप ॥
पत्नीवियुक्तश्च नरो न कदाचित्प्रजायते ॥

Sumantu said: ‘O Bharata, hear of the second day named Aśūnya-śayana. A woman who fasts on it does not become widowed, and a man is never separated from his wife.

BP 1.20.5

शेते जगत्पतिः कृष्णः श्रिया सार्धं यदा नृप ।
अशून्यशयना नाम तदा ग्राह्या हि सा तिथिः ॥

When Kṛṣṇa, Lord of the world, lies down together with Śrī, O king, that lunar date should be observed as Aśūnya-śayana, the “bed not left empty.”

BP 1.20.6

कृष्णपक्षे द्वितीयायां श्रावणे मासि भारत ।
इदमुच्चारयेत्स्नातः प्रणम्य जगतः पतिम् ॥
श्रीवत्सधारिणं देवं भक्त्याभ्यर्च्य श्रिया सह ॥

On the second day of the dark fortnight in Śrāvaṇa, one should bathe, bow to the Lord of the world, worship with devotion the god bearing Śrīvatsa together with Śrī, and recite this prayer.

BP 1.20.7

श्रीवत्सधारिञ्छ्रीकान्त श्रीवत्स श्रीपतेऽव्यय ।
गार्हस्थ्यं मा प्रणाशं मे यातु धर्मार्थकामदम् ॥

“Bearer of Śrīvatsa, beloved of Śrī, imperishable Lord of Śrī: may my household life, which bestows righteousness, prosperity, and pleasure, never perish.”

BP 1.20.8

गावश्च मा प्रणश्यन्तु मा प्रणश्यन्तु मे जनाः ॥

“May my cattle not perish, and may my people not perish.”

BP 1.20.9

जामयो मा प्रणश्यन्तु मत्तो दाम्पत्यभेदतः ।
लक्ष्म्या वियुज्येऽहं देव न कदाचिद्यथा भवान् ॥

“May my family relations not be destroyed through marital division arising from me. God, just as you are never separated from Lakṣmī, may I never be separated from prosperity.”

BP 1.20.10

तथा कलत्रसम्बन्धो देव मा मे वियुज्यताम् ।
लक्ष्म्या न शून्यं वरद यथा ते शयनं सदा ॥

“God, may my marital bond likewise never be severed. Bestower of boons, as your bed is never empty of Lakṣmī—”

BP 1.20.11

शय्या ममाप्यशून्यास्तु तथा तु मधुसूदन ।
एवं प्रमाद्य पूजां च कृत्वा लक्ष्म्यास्तथा हरेः ॥

“—so, Madhusūdana, may my bed also never be empty.” Having prayed in this manner, one should worship Lakṣmī and Hari.

BP 1.20.12

फलानि दद्याच्छायायामभीष्टानि जगत्पतिम् ।
नक्तं प्रणम्यायतने हविर्भुञ्जीत वाग्यतः ॥

One should offer the desired fruits in the shade to the Lord of the world. At night, after bowing in the shrine, one should silently eat sacrificial food.

BP 1.20.13

ब्राह्मणाय द्वितीयेऽह्नि शक्त्या दद्याच्च दक्षिणाम् ॥

On the second day one should give a brāhmaṇa a sacrificial fee according to one’s means.

BP 1.20.14

शतानीक उवाच
कानि तानि अभीष्टानि केशवस्य फलानि तु ।
योज्यानि शयने विप्र देवदेवस्य कथ्यताम् ॥

Śatānīka said: ‘Brāhmaṇa, tell me which fruits are dear to Keśava and should be placed upon the bed of the God of gods.

BP 1.20.15

किं च दानं द्वितीयेऽह्नि दातव्यं ब्राह्मणस्य तु ।
भक्तैर्नरैर्द्विजश्रेष्ठ देवदेवस्य शक्तितः ॥

Best of brāhmaṇas, what gift should devotees give the brāhmaṇa on the second day according to their means in honour of the God of gods?

BP 1.20.16

सुमन्तुरुवाच
यानि तत्र महाबाहो काले सन्ति फलानि तु।
मधुराणि सुतीव्राणि न चापि कटुकानि तु ॥

Sumantu said: ‘Mighty-armed king, whatever fruits are then in season—sweet and not excessively sharp or bitter—

BP 1.20.17

दातव्यानि नृपश्रेष्ठ स्वशक्त्या शयने नृप ।
मधुराणि प्रदत्तानि नरो वल्लभतां यजेत् ॥

—should be placed upon the bed according to one’s means. O excellent king, by giving sweet fruits a man becomes beloved.

BP 1.20.18

योषिच्च कुरुशार्दूल भर्तुर्वल्लभतामियात् ।
तस्मात्कटुकतीव्राणि स्त्रीलिङ्गानि विवर्जयेत् ॥

O tiger among the Kurus, a woman likewise becomes beloved of her husband. Therefore one should avoid sharp and bitter fruits and those grammatically feminine in name.

BP 1.20.19

खर्जूरमातुलिङ्गानि श्वेतेन शिरसा सह ।
फलानि शयने राजन्यज्ञभागहरस्य तु ॥

O king, dates, citrons, and white-headed fruits should be placed upon the bed of the receiver of sacrificial portions.

BP 1.20.20

देयानि कुरुशार्दूल स्वशक्त्या मुञ्जकेशिने ।
एतान्येव तु विप्रस्य गाङ्गेयसहितानि तु ॥

O tiger among the Kurus, they should be offered according to one’s means to Muñjakeśin. These same fruits, together with a silver gift, should be given to a brāhmaṇa.

BP 1.20.21

द्वितीयेऽह्नि प्रदेयानि भक्त्या शक्त्या च भारत ।
वासोदानं तथा धान्यफलदानसमन्वितम् ॥
गाङ्गेयस्य विशेषेण धान्यदानं प्रचक्षते ॥

O Bharata, on the second day they should be given with devotion and according to one’s means, together with gifts of clothing, grain, and fruit; a gift of grain accompanied especially by silver is prescribed.

BP 1.20.22

एवं करोति यः सम्यङ्नरो मासचतुष्टयम् ।
ततो जन्मत्रयं वीर गृहभङ्गो न जायते ॥

For one who properly performs this observance for four months, O hero, the household is not broken for three successive births.

BP 1.20.23

अशून्यशयनश्चासौ धर्मकामार्थसाधनः ।
भवत्यव्याहतैश्वर्यः पुरुषो नात्र संशयः ॥

That person’s bed remains occupied; he accomplishes righteousness, pleasure, and prosperity and possesses unobstructed sovereignty—of this there is no doubt.

BP 1.20.24

नारी च राजन्धर्मज्ञा व्रतमेतद्यथाविधि ।
या करोति न सा शोच्या बन्धुवर्गस्य जायते ॥

O king, a woman who understands dharma and performs this vow according to rule never becomes an object of sorrow to her circle of relatives.

BP 1.20.25

वैधव्यं दुर्भगत्वं च भर्तृत्यागं च सत्तम ।
नाप्नोति जन्म त्रियतमेतच्चीर्त्वा महाव्रतम् ॥

Excellent one, after performing this great vow, she does not experience widowhood, misfortune, or abandonment by her husband for three births.

BP 1.20.26

अदत्त्वा कटुकानीह फलानि कुरुनन्दन ।
खर्जूरमातुलिङ्गानि बृहत्फलशिरांसि च ॥

O delight of the Kurus, one should refrain from giving bitter fruits here, including dates, citrons, and large fruits with prominent tops.

BP 1.20.27

दत्त्वा द्विजेभ्यो राजेन्द्र मधुराणि पराणि च ।
तस्मात्स्वशक्त्या यत्तेन देयानि मधुराणि च ॥

O king of kings, one should give brāhmaṇas excellent sweet fruits. Therefore sweet fruits should be carefully offered according to one’s means.

BP 1.20.28

इत्येषा कथिता कृष्णद्वितीया तिथिरुत्तमा ।
यामुपोष्य नरो राजन्नृद्धिं वृद्धिं तथा व्रजेत् ॥

Thus has been related the excellent dark-fortnight second lunar day. By fasting upon it, O king, a person attains prosperity and increase.

BP 1.20.29

शतानीक उवाच
भवता कथितेयं वै द्वितीया तिथिरुत्तमा ।
अश्विभ्यां द्विजशार्दूल कथमस्यां जनार्दनः ॥

Śatānīka said: ‘You have described this excellent second lunar day as belonging to the Aśvins. Tiger among brāhmaṇas, how then is Janārdana—

BP 1.20.30

सम्पूज्यः फलसंज्ञायां कथितः पद्मया सह ।
तदत्र कौतुकं मह्यं सुमहज्जायते द्विज ॥

—said to be worshipped together with Padmā on the day named Phala? This raises a very great curiosity in me, brāhmaṇa.

BP 1.20.31

सुमन्तुरुवाच
एवमेतन्न सन्देहो तथा वदसि भारत ।
अश्विनोर्वै तिथिरियं किं तु वाक्यं निबोध मे ॥

Sumantu said: ‘It is just as you say, O Bharata; there is no doubt. This date belongs to the Aśvins, but now understand my explanation.

BP 1.20.32

अशून्यशयना दत्ता विष्णोरमिततेजसः ।
अश्विभ्यां कुरु शार्दूल प्रीतये मुञ्जकेशिनः ॥

O tiger among the Kurus, the Aśūnya-śayana observance of immeasurably radiant Viṣṇu was granted to the Aśvins for the pleasure of Muñjakeśin.

BP 1.20.33

तावेव कुरुशार्दूल पूज्येतेऽत्र महीपते ।
नासत्यौ भगवान्विष्णुर्दत्तश्च श्रीर्विभाव्यते ॥

Therefore, O tiger among the Kurus and lord of earth, those very two are worshipped here: Nāsatya is contemplated as Lord Viṣṇu, and Dasra as Śrī.
