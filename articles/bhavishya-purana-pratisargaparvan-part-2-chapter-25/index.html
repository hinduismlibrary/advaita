BP 3.2.25.1

सूत उवाच ।
कृपया ब्राह्मणद्वारा प्रकटीकृतवान्स्वकम् ।
इतिहासमिमं वक्ष्ये संवादं हरिविप्रयोः ॥ 1 ॥

Sūta said: With compassion, through a Brahmin, I shall reveal this history of the dialogue between Hari and the Brahmin.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
सूत sūta Sūta
उवाच uvāca said
कृपया kṛpayā kindly
ब्राह्मणद्वारा brāhmaṇadvārā through a Brahmin
प्रकटीकृतवान्स्वकम् prakaṭīkṛtavānsvakam made his own account manifest
इतिहासमिमं itihāsamimaṃ this history
वक्ष्ये vakṣye I shall tell
संवादं saṃvādaṃ the dialogue
हरिविप्रयोः hariviprayoḥ between Hari and the Brahmin
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

sūta uvāca | kṛpayā brāhmaṇadvārā prakaṭīkṛtavānsvakam | itihāsamimaṃ vakṣye saṃvādaṃ hariviprayoḥ || 1 ||

BP 3.2.25.2

काशीपुरीति विख्याता तत्रासीद्ब्राह्मणो वरः ।

In the famed city of Kāśī there lived an excellent Brahmin, poor, a householder and mendicant, with a wife and children.

वरः varaḥ excellent
दीनो dīno poor
गृहाश्रमी gṛhāśramī a householder
नित्यं nityaṃ always
भिक्षुः bhikṣuḥ a mendicant

Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

BP 3.2.25.3

शतानंद इति ख्यातो विष्णुव्रतपरायणः ।
एकदा पथि भिक्षार्थं गच्छतस्तस्य श्रीपतिः ॥ 3 ॥

He was known as Śatānanda, devoted to Viṣṇu's vow. One day as he went along the road to seek alms, the Lord of Lakṣmī came into view.

Word-for-word

इति iti thus

एकदा ekadā once
पथि pathi on the road
भिक्षार्थं bhikṣārthaṃ for alms
गच्छतस्तस्य gacchatastasya as he was going, before him
श्रीपतिः śrīpatiḥ the Lord of Lakṣmī
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

śatānaṃda iti khyāto viṣṇuvrataparāyaṇaḥ | ekadā pathi bhikṣārthaṃ gacchatastasya śrīpatiḥ || 3 ||

BP 3.2.25.4

विनीतस्यातिशांतस्य स बभूवाक्षिगोचरः ।

क्व यासीति द्विजश्रेष्ठ वृत्तिः कामेन कथ्यताम् ॥ 4 ॥

Appearing as an old Brahmin before the humble and peaceful man, Hari asked: Where are you going, best of Brahmins? Tell me what you do for a living.

BP 3.2.25.5

BP 3.2.25.6

तद्वारक उपायोयं विशेषेण कलौ किल ॥ 6 ॥

BP 3.2.25.7

ममोपदेशतो विप्र सत्यनारायणं भज ।
दारिद्र्यशोकशमनं संतापहरणं हरेः ।
चरणं शरणं याहि मोक्षदं पद्मलोचनम् ॥ 7 ॥

By my instruction, worship Satyanārāyaṇa. Take refuge in the lotus-eyed Hari, whose feet remove poverty, sorrow, and distress and grant liberation.

हरेः hareḥ of Hari
चरणं caraṇaṃ the feet
शरणं śaraṇaṃ refuge

Transliteration

BP 3.2.25.9

इदानीं विप्ररूपेण तव प्रत्यक्षमागतः ॥ 9 ॥
