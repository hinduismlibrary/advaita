Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 12 — Women’s Duties

BP 1.12.1

ब्रह्मोवाच
व्रीहीणां कोद्रवाणां च सारधर्ममुदारकः ।
कङ्गुकोद्रवयोर्ज्ञेयो वरटः पञ्चभागकः ॥

For rice and kodo millet, the principal grain portion is reckoned; for foxtail and kodo millet, the husk is one fifth.

BP 1.12.2

पञ्चभागान्प्रियङ्गूनां शालीनां च त्रयोऽष्ट च ।
चणकानां तृतीयांशः समक्षुण्णं त्रयं विदुः ॥

Five parts are reckoned for millet, three eighths for rice, and one third for chickpeas; for unbroken grain, three parts are recognized.

BP 1.12.3

पानीययवगोधूमं पिष्टधान्यचतुष्टयम् ।
तुल्यमेवावगन्तव्यं मुद्गा माषास्तिला यवाः ॥

The flours of green gram, black gram, sesame, and barley are to be taken in equal measure, as with barley and wheat mixed with water.

BP 1.12.4

पञ्चभागादिका घृष्टा गोधूमाः सक्तवस्तथा ।
कुल्माषाः पिष्टमांसं च सम्यगर्धादिकं भवेत् ॥

Ground wheat and roasted barley meal are reckoned with a fifth part and the like; cooked pulses and minced meat are properly reckoned from a half and the like.

BP 1.12.5

सिद्धं तदेव द्विगुणं पुन्नाको यावकस्तथा ।
कङ्गुकोद्रवयोरन्नं चणकोदारकस्य च ॥

When cooked, that same amount doubles; so do grain cakes and barley gruel, and foods made from foxtail millet, kodo millet, chickpeas, and other grains.

BP 1.12.6

द्विगुणं चीनकानां च व्रीहीणां च चतुर्गुणम् ।
शालेः पञ्चगुणं विद्यात्पुराणे त्वतिरिच्यते ॥

For small millet it is double, for rice fourfold, and for fine rice fivefold; with old grain it is greater.

BP 1.12.7

क्रियापाकविशेषास्तु वृद्धिरेवोपदिश्यते ।
निमित्तस्य वरान्नस्य तद्वृद्धिर्द्विगुणा भवेत् ॥

Differences of preparation and cooking are taught as increase; from excellent grain the increase should be twofold.

BP 1.12.8

तस्माद्भूयो विरूढस्य चतुर्भागो विवर्धते ।
लाजा धानाः कलायाश्च भृष्टाद्द्विगुणवृद्धयः ॥

Sprouted grain increases by another quarter; puffed grain, parched grain, and peas increase twofold when roasted.

BP 1.12.9

भ्रष्टव्यानामतोऽन्येषां पञ्चभागोऽधिको मतः ।
चापकानां च पिष्टानां पादहीनाः कलायजाः ॥

For other things to be roasted, one fifth more is reckoned; pea cakes are a quarter less.

BP 1.12.10

मुद्गमाषमसूराणामर्धपादावरोभवेत् ।
क्लिन्नशुष्कवरान्नानां हानिर्वृद्धिर्विशिष्यते ॥

For green gram, black gram, and lentils, the loss is half or a quarter; for soaked and dried foods of good quality, loss and increase differ.

BP 1.12.11

तयार्धेन तु शोध्यानामाढक्या मुद्गमाषयोः ।
मसूराणां च जानीयात्क्षयं पञ्चमभागकम् ॥

For cleaned green gram and black gram, reckon half from the measure; for lentils, know the loss to be one fifth.

BP 1.12.12

षड्भागेनातसीतैलं सिद्धार्थककपित्थयोः ।
तथा निम्बकदम्बादौ विद्यात्पञ्चमभागकम् ॥

Linseed oil is one sixth; oil from mustard and wood apple, and from neem, kadamba, and the like, is one fifth.

BP 1.12.13

तिलेङ्गुदीमधूकानां नक्तमालकुसुम्भयोः ।
जानीयात्पादकं तैलं खलमन्यत्प्रचक्षते ॥

From sesame, the oil-bearing tree, mahua, karanja, and safflower, reckon oil at one quarter; what remains is called oil-cake.

BP 1.12.14

क्षेत्रकालक्रियादिभ्यः क्षयादेर्व्यभिचारतः ।
प्रत्यक्षीकृत्य तान्सम्यगनुमित्यावधारयेत् ॥

Because loss and the like vary according to field, season, process, and the rest, observe them directly, infer, and determine them properly.

BP 1.12.15

क्षीरदोषे गवां प्रस्थं महिषीणां च सर्पिषः ।
पादाधिकमजावीनामुत्पादं तद्विदो विदुः ॥

Those who know reckon one measure of cow's milk and buffalo ghee; the yield from goats and sheep is a quarter more.

BP 1.12.16

सुभूमितृणकालेभ्यो वृद्धिर्वा क्षीरसर्पिषाम् ।
अतस्तेषां विधातव्यो ह्यर्थादेव विनिश्चयः ॥

Milk and ghee increase with good ground, grass, and seasons; their amount must therefore be determined from actual conditions.

BP 1.12.17

प्रत्यक्षीकृत्य यत्नेन पक्षमासान्तरे तथा ।
पयोर्वृत्तैर्गवादीनां कुर्यात्सम्भवनिर्णयम् ॥

By careful direct observation at intervals of a fortnight or a month, determine the expected milk yield of cows and other animals.

BP 1.12.18

कार्पास कृमिकोशौमौर्णक क्षौमादिकर्तनम् ।
कुणिपङ्ग्वन्धयोषाभिर्विधवाभिश्च कारयेत् ॥

Have the processing of cotton, silkworm cocoons, wool, flax, and the like done by hunchbacked, lame, and blind women, and widows.

BP 1.12.19

बालवृद्धान्धकार्पण्ये यत्कर्तव्यमवश्यतः ।
विनियोगं नयेत्सर्वं प्रियोपग्रहपूर्वकम् ॥

Whatever work must necessarily be done by children, the elderly, blind people, and the destitute, assign all of it with kind support beforehand.

BP 1.12.20

कर्मणामन्तरालेषु प्रोषिते चापि भर्तरि ।
स्वयं वै तदनुष्ठेयं नित्यानां चाविरोधतः ॥

Between tasks, and also when her husband is away, she herself should carry it out without hindering daily duties.

BP 1.12.21

शूद्राणां स्थूलसूक्ष्मत्वं बहुत्वं च व्ययाव्ययौ ।
मत्वा विशेषं कुर्वीत चेतनप्रतिपत्तिषु ॥

Considering the coarse and fine work of Śūdras, its quantity, and expenditure and saving, make distinctions in their wages.

BP 1.12.22

कारयेद्वस्त्रधान्यादि स्वाप्तवृद्धैरधिष्ठितम् ।
शूद्राणां क्षयवृद्ध्यादि मन्तव्यं वेतनानि च ॥

Have cloth, grain, and the like produced under the supervision of trusted elders; consider the loss, gain, and wages of Śūdras.

BP 1.12.23

क्षौमकार्पासयोर्विद्यात्सूत्रं पञ्चमभागकम् ।
देशकालादिभागात्तु प्रत्यक्षादेव निर्णयः ॥

For flax and cotton, reckon yarn at one fifth; because of differences of place, season, and the like, determine it by direct observation.

BP 1.12.24

अवघातेन तूलस्य क्षयो विंशतिभागकः ।
छन्नां व्याप्तां तु वातेन तद्वदूर्णां प्रचक्षते ॥

Beating cotton causes a loss of one twentieth; the same is said of wool spread out and scattered by the wind.

BP 1.12.25

पञ्चाशद्भागिकीं हानिं सूत्रे कुर्वीत लक्षणात् ।
वृद्धिस्तु मण्डसम्पर्काद्दशकादशिका भवेत् ॥

By its condition, reckon a loss of one fiftieth in yarn; through contact with rice starch, the increase is one tenth.

BP 1.12.26

श्लक्ष्णमध्यमसूत्राणामर्धाधिकसमं भवेत् ।
स्थूलानां तु पुनर्मूल्यात्पादोनं बालचेतनम् ॥

For fine and medium yarn, the amount should be equal with a half added; for coarse work, the children's wage should be a quarter less than the value.

BP 1.12.27

कर्मणो भूरिभेदत्वाद्देशकालप्रभेदतः ।
तद्विद्भ्य एव बोद्धव्यो बालचेतननिश्चयः ॥

Because work varies greatly by place, season, and the like, children's wages should be determined by those who know it.

BP 1.12.28

स्थूलं दिनत्रयं देयं मध्यमं च त्रिरात्रिकम् ।
सूक्ष्ममापक्षतो मृष्टं मासात्तत्परिकर्मकम् ॥
यदत्र क्षत्रवृद्ध्यादि तदुत्सर्गात्प्रदर्शितम् ॥

Pay three days for coarse work and three nights for medium work; fine and polished work for half a month, its finishing for a month. The increase and similar allowances given here are stated as general rules.

BP 1.12.29

कालकर्त्रादिभेदेन व्यभिचारोऽपि दृश्यते ।
शय्यासनान्यनेकानि कम्बलाश्चतुराश्रिकाः ॥

Variation also occurs according to differences in time, worker, and the like. There should be many beds and seats, and square blankets.

BP 1.12.30

कम्बुकाश्चावकोषाश्च मध्या रक्ताश्च भूरिशः ।
गुरुबालादिवृद्धानामभ्यागतजनस्य च ॥

There should be many kinds of coverings, including medium-grade and red ones, for elders, children, aged people, and arriving guests.

BP 1.12.31

भोगायानुगतो भर्त्ता कुर्याद्विविधमात्रकम् ।
यदस्य श्वशुरादीनां कल्पितं शयनादिकम् ॥

The husband, intent on comfort, should provide varied amounts, including the bedding and other things arranged for his father-in-law and others.

BP 1.12.32

भर्तुश्चैव विशेषण तदन्येन न कारयेत् ।
वस्त्रं माल्यमलङ्कारं विधृतं देवरादिभिः ॥

The husband's particular things should not be entrusted to another. The garments, garlands, and ornaments worn by younger brothers and others—

BP 1.12.33

न धारयेन्न चैतेषामाक्रमेच्छयनानि वा ।
पिण्याकनककुट्टाश्च कालरूक्षाणि यानि च ॥

One should not wear them, nor occupy their beds. Oil-cake, bran, husks, and whatever is coarse and dry—

BP 1.12.34

हेयं पर्युषिताद्यन्नं गोभक्तेनोपयोजयेत् ।
कुलानां बहुधेनूनां गोध्यक्षव्रजजीविनाम् ॥

Discarded and stale food should be used as cattle feed in households with many cows and among cattle overseers and herd keepers.

BP 1.12.35

किलाटगविकादीनां भक्तार्थमुपयोजनम् ।
दध्नः समाहरेत्सर्पिर्दुहेद्वत्सान्न पीडयेत् ॥

Curd preparations and the like should be used as food; collect ghee from curds and milk without distressing the calves.

BP 1.12.36

वर्षाशरद्वसन्तेषु द्वौ कालावन्यदा सकृत् ।
तक्रं वाप्युपयुञ्जीत श्ववराहादिपोषणे ॥

Twice in the rainy season, autumn, and spring; once at other times. Buttermilk may also be used to feed dogs, pigs, and the like.

BP 1.12.37

पिण्याकक्लेदनार्थं वा विक्रेयं वा तदर्हयेत् ।
वृत्तिं धान्यहिरण्येन गोपादीनां प्रकल्पयेत् ॥

Let it be made fit for sale or for moistening oil-cake; establish the livelihood of cowherds and others in grain or gold.

BP 1.12.38

ते हि क्षीरव्रता लोभादुपहन्युस्तदन्वयान् ।
दोहकालं गवां दोग्धानीतिवर्तेत वै द्विजाः ॥

For, devoted to milk, they may through greed harm their young. O twice-born, one should not go beyond the time for milking the cows.

BP 1.12.39

प्रसरोदकयोर्गोपा मन्थकस्य च मन्थकाः ।
मासमेकं यथा स्तन्यं मासमेकं स्तनद्वयम् ॥

Cowherds receive one measure and water; churners receive their share of churning. For one month there is milk from one teat, for one month from two teats.

BP 1.12.40

सततं पाययेदूर्ध्वं स्तनमेकं स्तनद्वयम् ।
तिलपिष्टाभिः पिण्डाभिस्तृणेन लवणेन च ॥
वारिणा च यथाकालं पुष्णीयादिति वत्सकान् ॥

Afterward, let calves constantly suckle from one teat or two; nourish them at the proper time with balls of sesame flour, grass, salt, and water.

BP 1.12.41

जरद्गुर्गर्भिणी धेनुर्वत्सा वत्सतरी तथा ।
पञ्चानां समभागेन घासं यूथे प्रकल्पयेत् ॥

Let equal shares of grass in the herd be allotted to the old cow, pregnant cow, milch cow, calf, and heifer.

BP 1.12.42

एको गोपालकस्तस्य त्रयाणामथ वा द्वयम् ।
पञ्चानां वत्सकश्चैकः प्रवरास्तु पृथक्पृथक् ॥

One cowherd for two or three; one calf-keeper for five; keep the best animals separately.

BP 1.12.43

गोचरस्यानयनार्थं व्यालानां त्रासनाय च ।
घण्टा कर्णेषु बध्नीयुः शोभारक्षार्थमेव च ॥

Tie bells to their ears to bring them from pasture, frighten wild animals, and for ornament and protection.

BP 1.12.44

पशव्ये व्यालनिर्मुक्ते देशे भूरितृणोदके ।
अभूतदुष्टे वारण्ये सदा कुर्वीत गोकुलम् ॥

Always keep the cattle herd in a place fit for cattle, free of wild animals, rich in grass and water, undamaged, or in the forest.

BP 1.12.45

सगुप्तमटवीवासं नित्यं कुर्यादजाविकम् ।
ऊर्णां वर्षे द्विरादद्याच्चैत्राश्वयुजमासयोः ॥

Always keep goats and sheep in a protected forest dwelling; take wool twice in the year, in the months of Caitra and Aśvayuja.

BP 1.12.46

यूथे मृषा दशैतासां चत्वारः पञ्च वा गवाम् ।
अश्वोष्ट्रमहिषाणां च यथा स्युः सुखसेविताः ॥

In the flock, one ram for ten ewes; four or five bulls for cows. Horses, camels, and buffaloes should be well cared for.

BP 1.12.47

विद्यात्कृषीवलादीना योगं कृषिककर्मसु ।
भक्तवेतनलाभं च कर्मकालानुरूपतः ॥

Know the arrangement of farmers and others for agricultural work, and their food, wages, and earnings according to the work and its season.

BP 1.12.48

क्षेत्रकेदारवाटेषु भृत्यानां कर्म कुर्वताम् ।
खलेषु च विजानीयात्क्रियायोगं प्रतिक्षणम् ॥

Continually know the work arrangement of servants laboring in fields, irrigated plots, gardens, and threshing floors.

BP 1.12.49

योग्यतातिशयं मत्वा कर्मयोगेषु कस्यचित् ।
ग्रासाच्छदशिरोभ्यङ्गैर्विशेषं तस्य कारयेत् ॥

Recognizing someone's greater skill in the work, give that person distinction with food, clothing, and anointing of the head.

BP 1.12.50

पद्मशाकादिवापानां कन्दबीजादिजन्मनाम् ।
सङ्ग्रहः सर्वबीजानां काले वापः सुभूमिषु ॥

Gather all seeds of lotus shoots, vegetables, and those produced from roots and seeds; sow them in good soil at the proper time.

BP 1.12.51

जातानां रक्षणं सम्यग्रक्षितानां च संग्रहः ।
तेषां च संगृहीतानां यथावन्निवपक्रिया ॥

Carefully protect what has sprouted; gather what has been protected; sow properly what has been gathered.

BP 1.12.52

गृहमूलं स्त्रियश्चैव धान्यमूलो गृहाश्रमः ।
तस्माद्धान्येषु भक्तेषु न कुर्यान्मुक्तहस्तताम् ॥

Women are the foundation of the house, and grain the foundation of the household; therefore do not be open-handed with grain and food.

BP 1.12.53

धान्यं तु सञ्चितं नित्यं मितो भक्तपरिव्ययः ।
न चान्ते मुक्तहस्तत्वं गृहिणीनां प्रशस्यते ॥

Grain should always be stored, and expenditure on food measured; liberality at the end is not praised in housewives.

BP 1.12.54

अल्पमित्येव नावज्ञां चरेदन्नेषु वै द्विजाः ।
मधुवल्मीकयोर्वृद्धिं क्षयं दृष्ट्वांजनस्य च ॥

O twice-born, do not despise food as little; observe the increase of honey and anthills, and the decrease of collyrium.

BP 1.12.55

ये केचिदिह निर्दिष्टा व्यापाराः पुरुषोचिताः ।
दाम्पत्योरैक्यमास्थाय तद्धिदानप्रसङ्गतः ॥

Whatever occupations here are declared proper for men, relying on the unity of the couple, should be carried out when the occasion for assigning them arises.

BP 1.12.56

सन्त्येव पुरुषा लोके स्त्रीप्रधानाः सहस्रशः ।
तेषु तासां प्रयोक्तृत्वाददोष इति गृह्यताम् ॥

There are indeed thousands of men in the world whose households are directed by women; because women exercise authority among them, understand that this is no fault.

BP 1.12.57

एवं योग्यतया युक्ता सौभाग्येनोद्यमेन च ।
सम्यगाराध्य भर्तारं तत्रैनं वशमानयेत् ॥

Endowed with competence, good fortune, and diligence, having duly attended to her husband, she should bring him under her control.
