Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 12 — Women’s Duties

BP 1.12.1

ब्रह्मोवाच
व्रीहीणां कोद्रवाणां च सारधर्ममुदारकः ।
कङ्गुकोद्रवयोर्ज्ञेयो वरटः पञ्चभागकः ॥

Brahmā spoke: The essential yield of vrīhi rice and kodrava is to be determined; in kaṅgu and kodrava, the husk is known as one fifth.

BP 1.12.2

पञ्चभागान्प्रियङ्गूनां शालीनां च त्रयोऽष्ट च ।
चणकानां तृतीयांशः समक्षुण्णं त्रयं विदुः ॥

Five parts for priyaṅgu, three eighths for śāli rice, and a third part for chickpeas; when unbroken, three parts are recognized.

BP 1.12.3

पानीययवगोधूमं पिष्टधान्यचतुष्टयम् ।
तुल्यमेवावगन्तव्यं मुद्गा माषास्तिला यवाः ॥

The flour of mudga, māṣa, tila, and yava is to be understood as equal to that of barley and wheat prepared with water.

BP 1.12.4

पञ्चभागादिका घृष्टा गोधूमाः सक्तवस्तथा ।
कुल्माषाः पिष्टमांसं च सम्यगर्धादिकं भवेत् ॥

Ground wheat and saktu are reckoned with an additional fifth; kulmāṣa and minced meat are properly reckoned from a half and the like.

BP 1.12.5

सिद्धं तदेव द्विगुणं पुन्नाको यावकस्तथा ।
कङ्गुकोद्रवयोरन्नं चणकोदारकस्य च ॥

Cooked, that same amount is doubled; likewise punnāka and yavaka, and food of kaṅgu, kodrava, chickpea, and udāraka.

BP 1.12.6

द्विगुणं चीनकानां च व्रीहीणां च चतुर्गुणम् ।
शालेः पञ्चगुणं विद्यात्पुराणे त्वतिरिच्यते ॥

Know the increase as twofold for cīnaka, fourfold for vrīhi, and fivefold for śāli; with old grain it is greater.

BP 1.12.7

क्रियापाकविशेषास्तु वृद्धिरेवोपदिश्यते ।
निमित्तस्य वरान्नस्य तद्वृद्धिर्द्विगुणा भवेत् ॥

Differences of preparation and cooking are taught as increase; when the grain used is excellent, its increase is twofold.

BP 1.12.8

तस्माद्भूयो विरूढस्य चतुर्भागो विवर्धते ।
लाजा धानाः कलायाश्च भृष्टाद्द्विगुणवृद्धयः ॥

Sprouted grain increases by a further quarter; laja, parched grain, and kalāya peas increase twofold when roasted.

BP 1.12.9

भ्रष्टव्यानामतोऽन्येषां पञ्चभागोऽधिको मतः ।
चापकानां च पिष्टानां पादहीनाः कलायजाः ॥

For other things to be roasted, one fifth more is held; cakes made from kalāya flour are a quarter less.

BP 1.12.10

मुद्गमाषमसूराणामर्धपादावरोभवेत् ।
क्लिन्नशुष्कवरान्नानां हानिर्वृद्धिर्विशिष्यते ॥

For mudga, māṣa, and masūra, the reduction is half or a quarter; for soaked and dried excellent foods, loss and increase vary.

BP 1.12.11

तयार्धेन तु शोध्यानामाढक्या मुद्गमाषयोः ।
मसूराणां च जानीयात्क्षयं पञ्चमभागकम् ॥

For mudga and māṣa that require cleaning, reckon half from an āḍhaka measure; for masūra, know the loss as one fifth.

BP 1.12.12

षड्भागेनातसीतैलं सिद्धार्थककपित्थयोः ।
तथा निम्बकदम्बादौ विद्यात्पञ्चमभागकम् ॥

Linseed oil is one sixth; from mustard and kapittha, and from neem, kadamba, and the like, know it as one fifth.

BP 1.12.13

तिलेङ्गुदीमधूकानां नक्तमालकुसुम्भयोः ।
जानीयात्पादकं तैलं खलमन्यत्प्रचक्षते ॥

From sesame, iṅgudī, madhūka, naktamāla, and safflower, know the oil as one quarter; the remainder is called oil-cake.

BP 1.12.14

क्षेत्रकालक्रियादिभ्यः क्षयादेर्व्यभिचारतः ।
प्रत्यक्षीकृत्य तान्सम्यगनुमित्यावधारयेत् ॥

Because loss and the like vary with field, season, method, and the rest, inspect them directly, infer, and determine them properly.

BP 1.12.15

क्षीरदोषे गवां प्रस्थं महिषीणां च सर्पिषः ।
पादाधिकमजावीनामुत्पादं तद्विदो विदुः ॥

Those who know the matter reckon a prastha for cows’ milk and buffaloes’ ghee; the yield of goats and sheep is a quarter more.

BP 1.12.16

सुभूमितृणकालेभ्यो वृद्धिर्वा क्षीरसर्पिषाम् ।
अतस्तेषां विधातव्यो ह्यर्थादेव विनिश्चयः ॥

Milk and ghee increase with good ground, grass, and seasons; therefore determine their amounts from the actual circumstances.

BP 1.12.17

प्रत्यक्षीकृत्य यत्नेन पक्षमासान्तरे तथा ।
पयोर्वृत्तैर्गवादीनां कुर्यात्सम्भवनिर्णयम् ॥

By careful direct observation at intervals of a fortnight or a month, determine the expected yield from the milk of cows and other animals.

BP 1.12.18

कार्पास कृमिकोशौमौर्णक क्षौमादिकर्तनम् ।
कुणिपङ्ग्वन्धयोषाभिर्विधवाभिश्च कारयेत् ॥

The cutting and working of cotton, silkworm cocoons, wool, flax, and the like should be assigned to infirm, lame, blind women, and widows.

BP 1.12.19

बालवृद्धान्धकार्पण्ये यत्कर्तव्यमवश्यतः ।
विनियोगं नयेत्सर्वं प्रियोपग्रहपूर्वकम् ॥

Whatever work must necessarily be done by children, the old, the blind, and the destitute should all be assigned with kind assistance beforehand.

BP 1.12.20

कर्मणामन्तरालेषु प्रोषिते चापि भर्तरि ।
स्वयं वै तदनुष्ठेयं नित्यानां चाविरोधतः ॥

In the intervals between tasks, and when her husband is away, she herself should perform it, without conflict with daily duties.

BP 1.12.21

शूद्राणां स्थूलसूक्ष्मत्वं बहुत्वं च व्ययाव्ययौ ।
मत्वा विशेषं कुर्वीत चेतनप्रतिपत्तिषु ॥

Considering the coarse and fine work of Śūdras, its quantity, and expenditure and saving, one should make distinctions in their wages.

BP 1.12.22

कारयेद्वस्त्रधान्यादि स्वाप्तवृद्धैरधिष्ठितम् ।
शूद्राणां क्षयवृद्ध्यादि मन्तव्यं वेतनानि च ॥

Cloth, grain, and the like should be produced under the supervision of trusted elders; the loss, increase, and wages of the Śūdras should be considered.

BP 1.12.23

क्षौमकार्पासयोर्विद्यात्सूत्रं पञ्चमभागकम् ।
देशकालादिभागात्तु प्रत्यक्षादेव निर्णयः ॥

For flax and cotton, know the thread as one fifth; the determination by region, season, and the like is made by direct observation.

BP 1.12.24

अवघातेन तूलस्य क्षयो विंशतिभागकः ।
छन्नां व्याप्तां तु वातेन तद्वदूर्णां प्रचक्षते ॥

The loss of cotton by beating is one twentieth; the same is declared for wool covered or spread by the wind.

BP 1.12.25

पञ्चाशद्भागिकीं हानिं सूत्रे कुर्वीत लक्षणात् ।
वृद्धिस्तु मण्डसम्पर्काद्दशकादशिका भवेत् ॥

One fiftieth loss should be reckoned for thread by its condition; through contact with maṇḍa, the increase may be one tenth.

BP 1.12.26

श्लक्ष्णमध्यमसूत्राणामर्धाधिकसमं भवेत् ।
स्थूलानां तु पुनर्मूल्यात्पादोनं बालचेतनम् ॥

For fine and medium thread, the amount is equal with a half added; for coarse work, and for children’s work, it is a quarter less than the value.

BP 1.12.27

कर्मणो भूरिभेदत्वाद्देशकालप्रभेदतः ।
तद्विद्भ्य एव बोद्धव्यो बालचेतननिश्चयः ॥

Because work differs greatly according to region and season, the determination of children’s wages should be learned from those who know the work.

BP 1.12.28

स्थूलं दिनत्रयं देयं मध्यमं च त्रिरात्रिकम् ।
सूक्ष्ममापक्षतो मृष्टं मासात्तत्परिकर्मकम् ॥
यदत्र क्षत्रवृद्ध्यादि तदुत्सर्गात्प्रदर्शितम् ॥

Three days’ pay should be given for coarse work and three nights’ pay for medium work; fine, polished work for half a month, its finishing for a month. The allowances for increase and the like are stated here as general rules.

BP 1.12.29

कालकर्त्रादिभेदेन व्यभिचारोऽपि दृश्यते ।
शय्यासनान्यनेकानि कम्बलाश्चतुराश्रिकाः ॥

Variation is also seen according to differences of time, worker, and the like. There should be many beds and seats, and square blankets.

BP 1.12.30

कम्बुकाश्चावकोषाश्च मध्या रक्ताश्च भूरिशः ।
गुरुबालादिवृद्धानामभ्यागतजनस्य च ॥

There should also be kambuka and avakośa coverings, and many medium red cloths, for elders, children, and arriving guests.

BP 1.12.31

भोगायानुगतो भर्त्ता कुर्याद्विविधमात्रकम् ।
यदस्य श्वशुरादीनां कल्पितं शयनादिकम् ॥

The husband, intent on comfort, should provide various quantities; the bedding and the like arranged for his father-in-law and others [should be kept].

BP 1.12.32

भर्तुश्चैव विशेषण तदन्येन न कारयेत् ।
वस्त्रं माल्यमलङ्कारं विधृतं देवरादिभिः ॥

And especially the husband’s [things] he should not have another use. Garments, garlands, and ornaments worn by brothers-in-law and others—

BP 1.12.33

न धारयेन्न चैतेषामाक्रमेच्छयनानि वा ।
पिण्याकनककुट्टाश्च कालरूक्षाणि यानि च ॥

She should not wear them, nor should she occupy their beds. Oil-cake, bran, husks, and whatever is coarse or dry by season—

BP 1.12.34

हेयं पर्युषिताद्यन्नं गोभक्तेनोपयोजयेत् ।
कुलानां बहुधेनूनां गोध्यक्षव्रजजीविनाम् ॥

Discarded, stale food and the like she should use as cattle-feed, in households with many cows and among those who live by herds.

BP 1.12.35

किलाटगविकादीनां भक्तार्थमुपयोजनम् ।
दध्नः समाहरेत्सर्पिर्दुहेद्वत्सान्न पीडयेत् ॥

Kilāṭa, gavikā, and the like should be used for food; she should collect ghee from curds and milk without distressing the calves.

BP 1.12.36

वर्षाशरद्वसन्तेषु द्वौ कालावन्यदा सकृत् ।
तक्रं वाप्युपयुञ्जीत श्ववराहादिपोषणे ॥

In the rainy season, autumn, and spring, [milk them] twice; at other times, once. Buttermilk may also be used to feed dogs, pigs, and the like.

BP 1.12.37

पिण्याकक्लेदनार्थं वा विक्रेयं वा तदर्हयेत् ।
वृत्तिं धान्यहिरण्येन गोपादीनां प्रकल्पयेत् ॥

Let it be valued for moistening oil-cake or for sale; establish the livelihood of cowherds and the like in grain or gold.

BP 1.12.38

ते हि क्षीरव्रता लोभादुपहन्युस्तदन्वयान् ।
दोहकालं गवां दोग्धानीतिवर्तेत वै द्विजाः ॥

For, devoted to milk, they may through greed harm their offspring. O twice-born ones, they should not exceed the time for milking the cows.

BP 1.12.39

प्रसरोदकयोर्गोपा मन्थकस्य च मन्थकाः ।
मासमेकं यथा स्तन्यं मासमेकं स्तनद्वयम् ॥

The cowherds [receive] prasara and water, and the churners [their share] from churning. For one month [the calf has] milk, for one month two teats.

BP 1.12.40

सततं पाययेदूर्ध्वं स्तनमेकं स्तनद्वयम् ।
तिलपिष्टाभिः पिण्डाभिस्तृणेन लवणेन च ॥
वारिणा च यथाकालं पुष्णीयादिति वत्सकान् ॥

After that, continually give [the calf] one teat or two; at the proper time nourish the calves with balls of sesame flour, grass, salt, and water.

BP 1.12.41

जरद्गुर्गर्भिणी धेनुर्वत्सा वत्सतरी तथा ।
पञ्चानां समभागेन घासं यूथे प्रकल्पयेत् ॥

An old cow, a pregnant cow, a calf, and a heifer: in the herd, allot the grass in equal shares among five.

BP 1.12.42

एको गोपालकस्तस्य त्रयाणामथ वा द्वयम् ।
पञ्चानां वत्सकश्चैकः प्रवरास्तु पृथक्पृथक् ॥

One cowherd for three, or for two; one calf-tender for five; the superior [animals] separately.

BP 1.12.43

गोचरस्यानयनार्थं व्यालानां त्रासनाय च ।
घण्टा कर्णेषु बध्नीयुः शोभारक्षार्थमेव च ॥

Bells should be fastened to their ears to bring them from pasture, to frighten wild beasts, and for ornament and protection.

BP 1.12.44

पशव्ये व्यालनिर्मुक्ते देशे भूरितृणोदके ।
अभूतदुष्टे वारण्ये सदा कुर्वीत गोकुलम् ॥

Always keep the cattle herd in a livestock-friendly place, free of wild beasts, rich in grass and water, unspoiled, or in the forest.

BP 1.12.45

सगुप्तमटवीवासं नित्यं कुर्यादजाविकम् ।
ऊर्णां वर्षे द्विरादद्याच्चैत्राश्वयुजमासयोः ॥

Goats and sheep should always be kept in a protected forest dwelling; wool should be taken twice a year, in Caitra and Āśvayuja.

BP 1.12.46

यूथे मृषा दशैतासां चत्वारः पञ्च वा गवाम् ।
अश्वोष्ट्रमहिषाणां च यथा स्युः सुखसेविताः ॥

In a herd, one male for ten of these, four or five for cows; horses, camels, and buffaloes should be tended comfortably.

BP 1.12.47

विद्यात्कृषीवलादीना योगं कृषिककर्मसु ।
भक्तवेतनलाभं च कर्मकालानुरूपतः ॥

She should know the arrangement of cultivators and others for agricultural work, and the food, wages, and gain in keeping with the work and season.

BP 1.12.48

क्षेत्रकेदारवाटेषु भृत्यानां कर्म कुर्वताम् ।
खलेषु च विजानीयात्क्रियायोगं प्रतिक्षणम् ॥

She should continually know the work arrangement of servants laboring in fields, irrigated plots, gardens, and threshing floors.

BP 1.12.49

योग्यतातिशयं मत्वा कर्मयोगेषु कस्यचित् ।
ग्रासाच्छदशिरोभ्यङ्गैर्विशेषं तस्य कारयेत् ॥

Recognizing someone’s superior skill in the tasks, she should make a distinction for that person with food, clothing, and anointing of the head.

BP 1.12.50

पद्मशाकादिवापानां कन्दबीजादिजन्मनाम् ।
सङ्ग्रहः सर्वबीजानां काले वापः सुभूमिषु ॥

Seeds of lotus stalks, vegetables, and those arising from tubers and seeds should all be gathered and sown at the proper time in good soil.

BP 1.12.51

जातानां रक्षणं सम्यग्रक्षितानां च संग्रहः ।
तेषां च संगृहीतानां यथावन्निवपक्रिया ॥

Those that have sprouted should be well protected; what has been protected should be gathered; what has been gathered should be sown properly.

BP 1.12.52

गृहमूलं स्त्रियश्चैव धान्यमूलो गृहाश्रमः ।
तस्माद्धान्येषु भक्तेषु न कुर्यान्मुक्तहस्तताम् ॥

Women are the foundation of the house, and grain is the foundation of the householder’s life; therefore one should not be liberal with grain and food.

BP 1.12.53

धान्यं तु सञ्चितं नित्यं मितो भक्तपरिव्ययः ।
न चान्ते मुक्तहस्तत्वं गृहिणीनां प्रशस्यते ॥

Grain should always be stored, and expenditure on food measured; liberality at the end is not praised in housewives.

BP 1.12.54

अल्पमित्येव नावज्ञां चरेदन्नेषु वै द्विजाः ।
मधुवल्मीकयोर्वृद्धिं क्षयं दृष्ट्वांजनस्य च ॥

O twice-born ones, food should not be despised as merely little; see the growth of honey and anthills, and the loss of collyrium.

BP 1.12.55

ये केचिदिह निर्दिष्टा व्यापाराः पुरुषोचिताः ।
दाम्पत्योरैक्यमास्थाय तद्धिदानप्रसङ्गतः ॥

Whatever occupations here are declared proper for men, relying on the unity of the couple, may be carried out when their performance is required.

BP 1.12.56

सन्त्येव पुरुषा लोके स्त्रीप्रधानाः सहस्रशः ।
तेषु तासां प्रयोक्तृत्वाददोष इति गृह्यताम् ॥

There are indeed thousands of men in the world whose households are directed by women; because the women act in those households, understand that it is no fault.

BP 1.12.57

एवं योग्यतया युक्ता सौभाग्येनोद्यमेन च ।
सम्यगाराध्य भर्तारं तत्रैनं वशमानयेत् ॥

Endowed with such competence, good fortune, and diligence, having duly attended to her husband, she should bring him under her control.
