Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 56 — The Solar Chariot Procession and Planetary Pacification

BP 1.56.1

श्रीरुद्र उवाच ।
कथं प्रचालयेद्ब्रह्मन् रथस्थं तमनाशनम् ।
अनुगाश्च कथं चास्य के च ते अनुगा क्रमात् ॥

Rudra spoke, asking how one should set the imperishable one seated on the chariot in motion, O Brahman, how his attendants should follow, and who those attendants are in order.

श्रीरुद्रः उवाच (śrīrudraḥ uvāca) — Śrī Rudra said
कथम् (katham) — how; प्रचालयेत् (pracālayet) — should set in motion; ब्रह्मन् (brahman) — O Brahman; रथस्थम् (rathastham) — seated on the chariot; तम् (tam) — him; अनाशनम् (anāśanam) — imperishable
अनुगाः (anugāḥ) — attendants; च (ca) — and; कथम् (katham) — how; च अस्य (ca asya) — and of him; के (ke) — who; च ते (ca te) — and those; अनुगाḥ (anugāḥ) — attendants; क्रमात् (kramāt) — in order

BP 1.56.2

भूयोभूयः सुरश्रेष्ठ विस्तरान्मम श्रेयसे ।
वद सर्वं जगन्नाथ परं कौतूहलं हि मे ॥

Again and again, O best of gods, tell me fully for my welfare. Tell everything, O lord of the world, for my curiosity is indeed great.

भूयः भूयः (bhūyaḥ bhūyaḥ) — again and again; सुरश्रेष्ठ (suraśreṣṭha) — O best of the gods; विस्तरात् (vistarāt) — in detail; मम (mama) — for me; श्रेयसे (śreyase) — for welfare
वद (vada) — tell; सर्वम् (sarvam) — everything; जगन्नाथ (jagannātha) — O lord of the world; परम् (param) — great; कौतूहम् (kautūhalam) — curiosity; हि मे (hi me) — indeed my

BP 1.56.3

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
शनैर्नयेद्रथं रुद्र वर्त्मना सु समेन तु ।
यथा पर्यटनं तु स्याद्विषमे पथि गच्छतः ॥

Brahmā spoke. One should lead the chariot slowly, O Rudra, along a very level road, so that it may travel on an uneven path.

ब्रह्मा उवाच (brahmā uvāca) — Brahmā said
शनैः (śanaiḥ) — slowly; नयेत् (nayet) — one should lead; रथम् (ratham) — the chariot; रुद्र (rudra) — O Rudra; वर्त्मना (vartmanā) — by the road; सु-समेन (su-samena) — very even; तु (tu) — indeed
यथा (yathā) — so that; पर्यटनम् (paryaṭanam) — journey; स्यात् (syāt) — may be; विषमे पथि (viṣame pathi) — on a difficult/uneven road; गच्छतः (gacchataḥ) — of one going

BP 1.56.4

प्रतीहाररथं पूर्वं नयेन्मार्गविशुद्धये ।
तस्मादनन्तरं रुद्र दण्डनायकमादरात् ॥

First one should lead the chariot of the gatekeeper to clear the road. After that, one should lead the commander with respect, O Rudra.

प्रतीहार-रथम् (pratīhāra-ratham) — the chariot of Pratīhāra; पूर्वम् (pūrvam) — first; नयेत् (nayet) — one should lead; मार्ग-विशुद्धये (mārga-viśuddhaye) — for purification of the road
तस्मात् अनन्तरम् (tasmāt anantaram) — after that; रुद्र (rudra) — O Rudra; दण्डनायकम् (daṇḍanāyakam) — Daṇḍanāyaka; आदरात् (ādarāt) — with respect

BP 1.56.5

पिङ्गलं च ततस्तस्य पृष्ठगं चादरान्नयेत् ।
रक्षको द्वारको यस्माद्रथारूढौ तु पृष्ठतः ॥

Then one should respectfully lead Piṅgala, who follows behind it. For the protector and the doorkeeper are mounted on the chariot at the rear.

पिङ्गलम् (piṅgalam) — Piṅgala; च (ca) — and; ततः (tataḥ) — then; तस्य पृष्ठगम् (tasya pṛṣṭhagam) — going behind that; आदरात् (ādarāt) — with care; नयेत् (nayet) — one should lead
रक्षकः द्वारकः (rakṣakaḥ dvārakaḥ) — Rakṣaka and Dvāraka; यस्मात् (yasmāt) — because; रथारूढौ (rathārūḍhau) — mounted on the chariot; तु (tu) — indeed; पृष्ठतः (pṛṣṭhataḥ) — behind

BP 1.56.6

रथारूढस्तथा दिण्डी देवस्य पुरतः स्थितः ।
तस्मादपि तथा रुद्र लेखको भास्करप्रियः ॥

Likewise, Diṇḍī, mounted on the chariot, stands before the god. Likewise, O Rudra, the writer dear to Bhāskara should be led.

रथारूढः (rathārūḍhaḥ) — mounted on the chariot; तथा (tathā) — likewise; दिण्डी (diṇḍī) — Dindī; देवस्य पुरतः (devasya purataḥ) — before the god; स्थितः (sthitaḥ) — standing
तस्मात् अपि (tasmāt api) — also in that arrangement; तथा (tathā) — likewise; रुद्र (rudra) — O Rudra; लेखकः (lekhakaḥ) — the scribe; भास्कर-प्रियः (bhāskara-priyaḥ) — dear to Bhāskara

BP 1.56.7

शनैःशनैर्नयेद्रुद्र रथं देवस्य यत्नतः ।
युगाक्षचक्रभङ्गो वा यथा न स्यात्त्रिलोचन ॥

One should lead the chariot of the god slowly and carefully, O Rudra, so that the yoke, axle, or wheel does not break, O three eyed one.

शनैः शनैः (śanaiḥ śanaiḥ) — slowly, slowly; नयेत् (nayet) — one should lead; रथम् (ratham) — the chariot; देवस्य (devasya) — of the god; यत्नतः (yatnataḥ) — carefully
युग-अक्ष-चक्र-भङ्गः (yuga-akṣa-cakra-bhaṅgaḥ) — breaking of the yoke, axle, or wheel; वा (vā) — or; यथा न स्यात् (yathā na syāt) — so that it may not be; त्रिलोचन (trilocana) — O three-eyed one

BP 1.56.8

ईषाभङ्गे द्विजभयं भग्नेऽक्षे क्षत्रियक्षयः ।
तुलाभङ्गे तु वैश्यानां शय्याशूद्रक्षयो भवेत् ॥

When the pole breaks, fear arises for the Brahmins. When the axle breaks, the Kṣatriyas are destroyed. When the crossbar breaks, the Vaiśyas are destroyed, and when the bed of the chariot breaks, the Śūdras are destroyed.

ईषा-भङ्गे (īṣā-bhaṅge) — when the pole/shaft breaks; द्विज-भयम् (dvija-bhayam) — danger for the twice-born
भग्ने अक्षे (bhagne akṣe) — when the axle is broken; क्षत्रिय-क्षयः (kṣatriya-kṣayaḥ) — destruction/decline of Kṣatriyas
तुला-भङ्गे (tulā-bhaṅge) — when the balance beam breaks; तु (tu) — indeed; वैश्यानाम् (vaiśyānām) — of Vaiśyas; शय्या-शूद्र-क्षयः (śayyā-śūdra-kṣayaḥ) — a textually difficult compound, concerning bed/Śūdra decline; भवेत् (bhavet) — may be

BP 1.56.9

युगभङ्गे त्वनावृष्टिः पीठभङ्गे प्रजाभयम् ।
परचक्रागमं विद्याच्चक्रभङ्गे रथस्य तु ॥

When the yoke breaks, drought follows, and when the platform breaks, fear for the people arises. When the chariot wheel breaks, one should know that an enemy army is coming.

युग-भङ्गे (yuga-bhaṅge) — when the yoke breaks; तु (tu) — indeed; अनावृष्टिः (anāvṛṣṭiḥ) — lack of rain/drought
पीठ-भङ्गे (pīṭha-bhaṅge) — when the platform breaks; प्रजा-भयम् (prajā-bhayam) — fear among the people
पर-चक्र-आगमम् (para-cakra-āgamam) — arrival of a hostile army; विद्यात् (vidyāt) — one should know; चक्र-भङ्गे (cakra-bhaṅge) — when the wheel breaks; रथस्य (rathasya) — of the chariot; तु (tu) — but

BP 1.56.10

ध्वजस्य पतने चापि नृपभङ्गं विनिर्दिशेत् ।
व्यङ्गितप्रतिमायां तु राज्ञो मरणमादिशेत् ॥

When the banner falls, one should declare the downfall of the king. When the image is damaged, one should predict the death of the king.

ध्वजस्य पतने (dhvajasya patane) — at the falling of the flag; च अपि (ca api) — and also; नृप-भङ्गम् (nṛpa-bhaṅgam) — the king’s downfall; विनिर्दिशेत् (vinirdiśet) — one should declare
व्यङ्गित-प्रतिमायाम् (vyaṅgita-pratimāyām) — when the image is disfigured; तु (tu) — but; राज्ञः (rājñaḥ) — of the king; मरणम् (maraṇam) — death; आदिशेत् (ādiśet) — one should foretell

BP 1.56.11

छत्रभङ्गाद्भयं रुद्र युवराज्ञो विनिर्दिशेत् ।
उत्पन्नेष्वेवमाद्येषु उत्पातेष्वशुभेषु च ॥

When these and other inauspicious omens arise, one should declare danger for the crown prince from the breaking of the umbrella, O Rudra.

छत्र-भङ्गात् (chatra-bhaṅgāt) — from the breaking of the parasol; भयम् (bhayam) — fear; रुद्र (rudra) — O Rudra; युवराज्ञः (yuvarājñaḥ) — for the crown prince; विनिर्दिशेत् (vinirdiśet) — one should predict
उत्पन्नेषु (utpanneṣu) — when they arise; एवम्-आद्येषु (evam-ādyeṣu) — such and the like; उत्पातेषु अशुभेषु (utpāteṣu aśubheṣu) — inauspicious portents; च (ca) — and

BP 1.56.12

बलिकर्म पुनः कुर्याच्छांतिहोमं तथैव च ।
ब्राह्मणान्वाचयेद्भूयो दद्याद्दानानि चैव हि ॥

One should perform the offering rite again, and likewise the peace fire offering. One should again have the Brahmins recite and give gifts as well.

बलि-कर्म (bali-karma) — the bali rite; पुनः (punaḥ) — again; कुर्यात् (kuryāt) — one should perform; शान्ति-होमम् (śānti-homam) — the peace-offering; तथा एव च (tathā eva ca) — likewise
ब्राह्मणान् वाचयेत् (brāhmaṇān vācayet) — one should have Brāhmaṇas recite; भूयः (bhūyaḥ) — again; दद्यात् (dadyāt) — one should give; दानानि (dānāni) — gifts; च एव हि (ca eva hi) — indeed also

BP 1.56.13

पूर्वोत्तरे च दिग्भागे रथस्याग्निं प्रकल्पयेत् ।
समिद्भिस्तु घृताक्ताभिर्होमयेज्जातवेदसम् ॥

One should set the fire in the northeast part of the chariot. With fuel sticks smeared with clarified butter, one should offer to Fire.

पूर्वोत्तरे दिग्भागे (pūrvottare digbhāge) — in the north-eastern quarter; च (ca) — and; रथस्य (rathasya) — of the chariot; अग्निम् (agnim) — fire; प्रकल्पयेत् (prakalpayet) — one should prepare
समिद्भिः (samidbhiḥ) — with kindling sticks; तु (tu) — indeed; घृत-आक्ताभिः (ghṛta-āktābhiḥ) — anointed with ghee; होमयेत् (homayet) — one should offer; जातवेदसम् (jātavedasam) — to Jātavedas, the all-knowing fire

BP 1.56.14

स्वाहाकारान्वदन्सम्यग्दैवतेभ्यस्त्वनुक्रमात् ।
ग्रहेभ्यश्च प्रजाभ्यश्च नामान्युद्दिश्य होमयेत् ॥

One should utter the offering formulas properly to the deities in order. One should make offerings for the planets and the people, naming them.

स्वाहा-कारान् (svāhākārān) — svāhā formulas; वदन् (vadan) — speaking; सम्यक् (samyak) — correctly; दैवतेभ्यः (daivatebhyaḥ) — for the deities; तु (tu) — indeed; अनुक्रमात् (anukramāt) — in sequence
ग्रहेभ्यः (grahebhyaḥ) — for the planets; च (ca) — and; प्रजाभ्यः (prajābhyaḥ) — for beings/subjects; च (ca) — and; नामानि उद्दिश्य (nāmāni uddiśya) — naming; होमयेत् (homayet) — one should offer

BP 1.56.15

प्रथमं चाग्नये स्वाहा स्वाहा सोमाय चैव हि ।
स्वाहा प्रजापतये च देया आहुतयः क्रमात् ॥

First, hail to Fire, and likewise hail to Soma. Hail to Prajāpati, and the oblations should be given in order.

प्रथमम् (prathamam) — first; च (ca) — and; अग्नये (agnaye) — to Agni; स्वाहा (svāhā) — svāhā
सोमाय (somāya) — to Soma; च एव हि (ca eva hi) — likewise indeed
प्रजापतये (prajāpataye) — to Prajāpati; देयाः (deyāḥ) — to be given; आहुतयः (āhutayaḥ) — oblations; क्रमात् (kramāt) — in order

BP 1.56.16

स्वस्त्यस्त्विह च विप्रेभ्यः स्वस्ति राज्ञे तथैव च ।
गोभ्यः स्वस्ति प्रजाभ्यश्च जगतः शान्तिरस्तु वै ॥

May there be well being here for the Brahmins, and well being for the king likewise. May there be well being for cows and people, and may there indeed be peace for the world.

स्वस्ति अस्तु (svasti astu) — may there be welfare; इह (iha) — here; च (ca) — and; विप्रेभ्यः (viprebhyaḥ) — for Brāhmaṇas
स्वस्ति राज्ञे (svasti rājñe) — welfare for the king; तथा एव च (tathā eva ca) — likewise and
गोभ्यः (gobhyaḥ) — for cows; स्वस्ति (svasti) — welfare; प्रजाभ्यः (prajābhyaḥ) — for living beings/subjects; च (ca) — and; जगतः (jagataḥ) — for the world; शान्तिः अस्तु (śāntiḥ astu) — may there be peace; वै (vai) — truly

BP 1.56.17

शं नोऽस्तु द्विपदे नित्यं शान्तिरस्तु चतुष्पदे ।
शं प्रजाभ्यस्तथैवास्तु शं सदात्मनि चास्तु वै ॥

May there always be well being for the two footed, and peace for the four footed. May there likewise be well being for the people, and may there always be well being in the self.

शम् (śam) — blessing/peace; नः (naḥ) — for us; अस्तु (astu) — may there be; द्विपदे (dvipade) — for two-footed beings; नित्यम् (nityam) — always
शान्तिः अस्तु (śāntiḥ astu) — may there be peace; चतुष्पदे (catuṣpade) — for four-footed beings
शम् (śam) — welfare; प्रजाभ्यः (prajābhyaḥ) — for the people; तथा एव अस्तु (tathā eva astu) — may it likewise be; सदात्मनि (sadātmani) — in the enduring self; च अस्तु वै (ca astu vai) — and may it truly be

BP 1.56.18

भूः शान्तिरस्तु देवेश भुवः शान्तिस्तथैव च ।
स्वश्चैवास्तु तथा शान्तिः सर्वत्रास्तु तथा रवेः ॥

May there be peace on earth, O lord of gods, and likewise peace in the atmosphere. May there also be peace in heaven, and may there be peace everywhere for the Sun.

भूः (bhūḥ) — Bhūḥ; शान्तिः अस्तु (śāntiḥ astu) — may there be peace; देवेश (deveśa) — O lord of the gods
भुवः (bhuvaḥ) — Bhuvaḥ; शान्तिः (śāntiḥ) — peace; तथा एव च (tathā eva ca) — likewise and
स्वः (svaḥ) — Svaḥ; च एव अस्तु (ca eva astu) — may there also be; तथा शान्तिः (tathā śāntiḥ) — thus peace; सर्वत्र अस्तु (sarvatra astu) — may there be everywhere; तथा रवेः (tathā raveḥ) — likewise for Ravi

BP 1.56.19

त्वं देव जगतः स्रष्टा पोष्टा चैव त्वमेव हि ।
प्रजापाल ग्रहेषान शान्तिं कुरु दिवस्पते ॥

You alone are the creator and nourisher of the world, O god. O protector of people, lord of planets, make peace, O lord of day.

त्वम् (tvam) — you; देव (deva) — O god; जगतः (jagataḥ) — of the world; स्रष्टा (sraṣṭā) — creator; पोष्टा (poṣṭā) — nourisher; च एव (ca eva) — and indeed; त्वम् एव (tvam eva) — you alone; हि (hi) — surely
प्रजा-पाल (prajā-pāla) — O protector of beings; ग्रह-ईशान (graha-īśāna) — O lord of the planets; शान्तिम् कुरु (śāntiṃ kuru) — grant peace; दिवस्-पते (divas-pate) — O lord of heaven/day

BP 1.56.20

इदमَन्यच्च वक्ष्यामि शान्त्या परमकारणम् ।
यात्राकारणभूतस्य पुरुषस्य स्वजन्मनः ॥

I shall also tell the highest means of peace for a person undertaking a journey, according to his own birth.

इदम् अन्यत् च (idam anyat ca) — this other thing also; वक्ष्यामि (vakṣyāmi) — I shall tell; शान्त्या (śāntyā) — of/for pacification; परम-कारणम् (parama-kāraṇam) — the supreme cause
यात्रा-कारण-भूतस्य (yātrā-kāraṇa-bhūtasya) — having become the cause of the journey; पुरुषस्य (puruṣasya) — of the person; स्व-जन्मनः (sva-janmanaḥ) — of his own birth

BP 1.56.21

दुःस्थान्ग्रहांश्च विज्ञाय ग्रहशान्तिं समाचरेत् ।
प्रादेशमात्राः कर्त्तव्याः समिधोऽथ प्रमाणतः ॥

Having discerned the planets that are ill placed, one should perform the pacification of the planets. The kindling sticks should be made one handspan in length according to measure.

दुःस्थान् ग्रहांस् च (duḥsthān grahāṃś ca) — planets in evil positions; विज्ञाय (vijñāya) — having recognized; ग्रह-शान्तिम् (graha-śāntim) — planetary pacification; समाचरेत् (samācaret) — one should perform
प्रादेश-मात्राः (prādeśa-mātrāḥ) — of a pradeśa measure; कर्तव्याः (kartavyāḥ) — to be made; समिधः (samidhaḥ) — kindling sticks; अथ (atha) — then; प्रमाणतः (pramāṇataḥ) — according to measure

BP 1.56.22

अर्कमय्यो रवेः कार्या पालाश्यः शशिनः स्मृताः ।

Fuel sticks from the giant milkweed are prescribed for the Sun, and those from the flame of the forest for the Moon. Those from the catechu tree are prescribed for Mars, and those from the prickly chaff flower for the son of the Moon.

होतव्याः (hotavyāḥ) — should be offered; मधु-सर्पिभ्याम् (madhu-sarpibhyām) — with honey and ghee; दध्ना (dadhnā) — with curds; च एव (ca eva) — also; समन्विताः (samanvitāḥ) — accompanied

BP 1.56.44

पूर्वोक्तभोजनं यद्धि ब्राह्मणेभ्यो निवेदयेत् ।
शक्तितो वा यथालाभं दक्षिणा तु विधानतः ॥

One should present the food stated before to the Brahmins, with a sacrificial gift according to capacity or according to what is obtained.

पूर्व-उक्त-भोजनम् (pūrva-ukta-bhojanam) — the food mentioned earlier; यत् हि (yat hi) — which indeed; ब्राह्मणेभ्यः (brāhmaṇebhyaḥ) — to Brāhmaṇas; निवेदयेत् (nivedayet) — one should offer
शक्तितः (śaktitaḥ) — according to capacity; वा (vā) — or; यथा-लाभम् (yathā-lābham) — according to what is obtained; दक्षिणा (dakṣiṇā) — honorarium/gift; तु (tu) — indeed; विधानतः (vidhānataḥ) — according to rule

BP 1.56.45

यश्च यस्य यदा दुःस्थः स तं यत्नेन पूजयेत् ।
मयैषा हि वरो दत्तः पूजिताः पूजयिष्यथ ॥

When any planet is ill placed for someone, that planet should be worshipped carefully. This boon was indeed given by me, when worshipped, you shall worship in return.

यः च (yaḥ ca) — whichever; यस्य (yasya) — for whom; यदा (yadā) — when; दुःस्थः (duḥsthaḥ) — ill-placed; सः तम् (saḥ tam) — he, that one; यत्नेन (yatnena) — carefully; पूजयेत् (pūjayet) — should worship
मया (mayā) — by me; एषः वरः (eṣaḥ varaḥ) — this boon; दत्तः (dattaḥ) — was given; पूजिताः (pūjitāḥ) — when worshipped; पूजयिष्यथ (pūjayiṣyatha) — you will worship / they will show favor

BP 1.56.46

ग्रहाधीना नरेन्द्राणामुच्छ्रयाः पतनानि च ।
भावाभावौ च जगतस्तस्मात्पूज्यतमा ग्रहाः ॥

The rises and falls of kings are under the planets, and the existence and nonexistence of the world. Therefore, the planets are most worthy of worship.

ग्रह-अधीनाः (graha-adhīnāḥ) — dependent on the planets; नरेन्द्राणाम् (narendrāṇām) — of kings; उच्च्रयाः (ucchrayāḥ) — rises; पतनानि (patanāni) — falls; च (ca) — and
भाव-अभावौ (bhāva-abhāvau) — prosperity and absence/lack; च (ca) — and; जगतः (jagataḥ) — of the world; तस्मात् (tasmāt) — therefore; पूज्यतमाः (pūjyatamāḥ) — most worthy of worship; ग्रहाः (grahāḥ) — the planets

BP 1.56.47

ग्रहा गावो नरेन्द्राश्च गुरवो ब्राह्मणास्तथा ।
पूजिताः पूजयन्त्येते निर्दहन्त्यपमानिताः ॥

Planets, cows, kings, teachers, and Brahmins, when worshipped, worship in return, and when dishonored, they burn.

ग्रहाः (grahāḥ) — planets; गावः (gāvaḥ) — cows; नरेन्द्राः (narendrāḥ) — kings; च (ca) — and; गुरवः (guravaḥ) — teachers; ब्राह्मणाः (brāhmaṇāḥ) — Brāhmaṇas; तथा (tathā) — likewise

BP 1.56.48

यथा समुत्थितं यन्त्रं यन्त्रेणैव प्रहन्यते ।
तथा समुत्थितां पीडां ग्रहशान्त्या प्रशामयेत् ॥

As a machine that has arisen is struck down by a machine, so one should calm an arisen affliction by the pacification of the planets.

यथा (yathā) — just as; समुत्थितम् (samutthitam) — arisen; यन्त्रम् (yantram) — weapon/device; यन्त्रेण एव (yantreṇa eva) — by another device alone; प्रहन्यते (pra hanyate) — is struck down
तथा (tathā) — so; समुत्थिताम् पीडाम् (samutthitām pīḍām) — arisen affliction; ग्रह-शान्त्या (graha-śāntyā) — by planetary appeasement; प्रशामयेत् (praśāmayet) — one should pacify

BP 1.56.49

यज्वनां सत्यवाक्यानां तथा नित्योपवासिनाम् ।
जपहोमपराणां च सर्वं दुष्टं प्रशाम्यति ॥

For those who perform sacrifices, speak truth, always fast, and are intent on recitation and fire offerings, all evil is calmed.

यज्वनाम् (yajvanām) — of sacrificers; सत्य-वाक्यानाम् (satya-vākyānām) — of truth-speakers; तथा (tathā) — likewise; नित्य-उपवासिनाम् (nitya-upavāsinām) — of those always fasting

BP 1.56.50

एवं कृत्वा प्रजाशान्तिं कृत्वा च स्वस्तिवाचनम् ।
पुनः सज्जं रथं कृत्वा कुर्यात्प्रक्रमणं हर ॥

BP 1.56.51

मार्गं शेषं नयित्वा तु नयेद्देवालयं रविम् ।
पूजयित्वा ततः पूर्वा याः प्रोक्ता रथदेवताः ॥

BP 1.56.52

यथा पूज्या ग्रहाः सर्वे उत्पातेṣu त्रिलोचन ।
रथदेवास्तथा पूज्या याः स्थिता रथमाश्रिताः ॥
