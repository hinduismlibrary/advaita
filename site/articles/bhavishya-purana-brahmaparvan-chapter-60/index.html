Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 60 — The Description of the Chariot Journey

BP 1.60.1

इत्युक्त्वा स जगामाशु सुरज्येष्ठं त्रिलोचनम् ।
रथयात्रा महाबाहो सूर्यस्येत्यमितौजसः ॥ १ ॥

Sumantu spoke. Having said this, he quickly went to the eldest among the gods, the three eyed one. O mighty armed, the chariot journey of the Sun of immeasurable splendor.

iti uktvā — having said this; saḥ jagāma āśu — he went quickly; sura-jyeṣṭham — the eldest of the gods; trilocanam — the three-eyed one; ratha-yātrā — chariot-procession; mahābāho — O mighty-armed one; sūryasya — of the Sun; amita-ojas — of immeasurable splendor

BP 1.60.2

यमाराध्य जगन्नाथं मम पूर्वपितामहाः ।
तुष्ट्यर्थं ब्राह्मणानां त्वन्नमापुश्चतुर्विधम् ॥ २ ॥

Śatānīka spoke. My forefathers obtained four kinds of food for the satisfaction of the Brahmins by worshiping the Lord of the universe.

yam ārādhya — having worshiped whom; jagannātham — Lord of the world; mama pūrva-pitāmahāḥ — my forefathers; tuṣṭyartham — for satisfaction; brāhmaṇānām — of Brāhmaṇas; annam āpuḥ caturvidham — obtained four kinds of food

BP 1.60.2

तस्य देवस्य माहात्म्यं श्रुतं च बहुशो मया ।
देवर्षिसिद्धमनुजैः स्तुतस्य हि दिने दिने ॥ २ ॥

I have often heard the greatness of that god, who is praised day after day by divine sages, perfected beings, and humans.

tasya devasya — of that god; māhātmyam — greatness; śrutam — heard; bahuśaḥ — many times; mayā — by me; deva-ṛṣi-siddha-manujaiḥ — by divine sages, Siddhas, and men; stutasya — of the praised one; dine dine — day after day

BP 1.60.4

कः स्तोतुमीशस्तमजं यस्यैतत्सचराचरम् ।
अव्ययस्याप्रमेयस्य विबुध्येतोदयाज्जगत् ॥ ४ ॥

Who can praise that unborn one, the imperishable and immeasurable one, whose moving and unmoving world this is, by whose rising the world awakens.

kaḥ — who; stotum īśaḥ — able to praise; tam ajam — that unborn one; yasya etat sacarācaram — whose is this moving and unmoving; avyayasya aprameyasya — of the imperishable, immeasurable one; udayāt jagat vibudhyeta — the world awakens from whose rising

BP 1.60.5

कराभ्यां यस्य देवेशौ कविष्णू लोकपूजितौ ।
उत्पन्नौ द्विजशार्दूल ललाटात्त्रिपुरान्तकः ॥ ५ ॥

From whose two hands were born the two lords of the gods, Brahmā and Viṣṇu, worshiped by the world, and from whose forehead came Śiva, the destroyer of Tripura, O tiger among Brahmins.

karābhyām — from the two hands; yasya — of whom; deveśau kaviṣṇū — the two divine lords, Brahmā and Viṣṇu; lokapūjitau — worshiped by the world; utpannau — arose; dvijaśārdūla — O tiger among Brāhmaṇas; lalāṭāt — from the forehead; tripurāntakaḥ — the destroyer of Tripura

BP 1.60.6

तस्य देवस्य कैः शक्या वक्तुं सर्वा विभूतयः ।
सोऽहमिच्छामि देवस्य तस्य सर्वात्मना द्विज ॥ ६ ॥

Who can tell all the powers of that god. O Brahmin, I wish to hear of his worship with my whole being.

tasya devasya — of that god; kaiḥ śakyāḥ — by whom are possible; vaktum — to tell; sarvāḥ vibhūtayaḥ — all glories; saḥ aham — therefore I; icchāmi — wish; sarvātmanā — with all my being; dvija — O twice-born one

BP 1.60.7

श्रोतुमाराधनं येन निस्तरेयं भवार्णवम् ।
केनोपायेन मन्त्रैर्वा रहस्यैः परिचर्यया ॥ ७ ॥

By which I may cross the ocean of existence, through what means, through sacred formulas, secret teachings, or service.

śrotum — to hear; ārādhanam — worship; yena — by which; nistareyam — I might cross; bhava-arṇavam — the ocean of worldly existence; kena upāyena — by what means; mantraiḥ vā — by mantras or; rahasyaiḥ — by secret teachings; paricaryayā — by service

BP 1.60.8

दानैर्वृतोपवासैर्वा होमैर्जाप्यैरथापि वा ।
आराधितः समस्तानां क्लेशानां हानिदो भवेत् ॥ ८ ॥

By gifts, vows, and fasts, by fire offerings and recitations, when worshiped, may he remove all afflictions.

dānaiḥ — by gifts; vrata-upavāsaiḥ — by vows and fasts; homaiḥ — by oblations; jāpyaiḥ — by recitations; atha api vā — or by any; ārādhitaḥ — worshiped; samastānām kleśānām — of all afflictions; hānidaḥ bhavet — may become the remover

BP 1.60.9

सैका विद्या हि विद्यानां यया तुष्यति सर्वकृत् ।
श्रुतानामपि तत्पुण्यं यत्र भानोः प्रकीर्तनम् ॥ ९ ॥

That alone is the knowledge among all knowledges by which the maker of all is pleased. There is merit also in what is heard where praise of the Sun is proclaimed.

sā ekā vidyā — that one knowledge; vidyānām — among knowledges; yayā — by which; tuṣyati — is pleased; sarva-kṛt — the doer of all; śrutānām api — even of what is heard; tat puṇyam — that merit; yatra — where; bhānoḥ prakīrtanam — praise of Bhānu

BP 1.60.10

रहस्यानां रहस्यं तद्येन हंसः प्रसीदति ।
एकः श्रेष्ठतमो मंत्रस्तदेकं परमं व्रतम् ॥ १० ॥

That is the secret of secrets by which the Swan is pleased. That sacred formula is the most excellent, that is the supreme vow.

rahasyānām rahasyam — secret of secrets; yena — by which; haṃsaḥ prasīdati — Haṃsa is pleased; ekaḥ śreṣṭhatamaḥ mantraḥ — one most excellent mantra; tat ekam paramam vratam — that one supreme vow

BP 1.60.11

उपोषितं च तच्छ्रेष्ठं येन भानुः प्रसीदति ।
सा चैका रसना धन्या मार्तण्डं स्तौति या सदा ॥ ११ ॥

That fast is most excellent by which the Sun is pleased. That tongue alone is blessed which always praises the Sun.

upoṣitam — fasted; tat śreṣṭham — that most excellent; yena — by which; bhānuḥ prasīdati — Bhānu is pleased; sā ekā rasanā — that one tongue; dhanyā — blessed; yā stauti — which praises; sadā — always

BP 1.60.12

तदेकं निर्मलं चित्तं यद्गतं सततं रवौ ।
श्लाघ्यानामपि तौ श्लाघ्याविह लोके परत्र च ॥ १२ ॥

That alone is a pure mind which is always fixed on the Sun. Those two are praiseworthy among the praiseworthy, here in this world and beyond.

tat ekam nirmalam cittam — that one pure mind; yat gatam — which has gone; satatam ravau — always to Ravi; ślāghyānām api — even among the praiseworthy; tau ślāghyau — those two praiseworthy; iha loke paratra ca — here and in the next world

BP 1.60.13

यो सदा द्विजशार्दूल भानोः पूजाकरौ करौ ।
तदेकं केवलं धन्यं शरीरं सर्वजन्तुषु ॥ १३ ॥

O tiger among Brahmins, that body alone is blessed among all beings whose two hands always worship the Sun.

yaḥ — whoever; sadā — always; dvijaśārdūla — O tiger among Brāhmaṇas; bhānoḥ pūjā-karau karau — two hands making worship of Bhānu; tat ekam kevalam — that alone; dhanyam śarīram — blessed body; sarva-jantuṣu — among all creatures

BP 1.60.14

यदेव पुलकोद्भासि भानोर्नामानुकीर्तने ।
सा जिह्वा कण्ठतालूकमथ वा प्रतिजिह्विका ॥ १४ ॥

During recitation of the name of the Sun, hairs stand on end on the tongue, the palate of the throat, or the uvula.

yat eva — that indeed; pulaka-udbhāsi — shining with horripilation; bhānoḥ nāma-anukīrtane — during recitation of Bhānu’s name; sā jihvā — that is the tongue; kaṇṭha-tālūkam — throat-palate; atha vā — or; prati-jihvikā — secondary tongue

BP 1.60.15

अथ वा सापरो रोगो या न वक्ति रवेर्गुणम् ।
नवद्वाराणि सन्त्यस्मिन्पुरे पुरुषसत्तम ॥ १५ ॥

Or the failure to speak of the virtues of the Sun is another disease. Nine gates are in this city, O best of men.

atha vā — or else; sā — that; aparaḥ rogaḥ — another disease; yā na vakti — which does not speak; raveḥ guṇam — Ravi’s virtues; nava-dvārāṇi — nine gates; santi asmin pure — are in this city; puruṣasattama — O best of men

BP 1.60.16

प्राकारैस्त्वावृते विष्वग्वृथा तानि विदुर्बुधाः ।
दत्त्वावधानं यच्छब्दे विनैव रविसंस्तुतिम् ॥ १६ ॥

The wise know those gates, surrounded on all sides by walls, to be useless when attention is given to sound without praise of the Sun.

prākāraiḥ āvṛte viṣvak — surrounded everywhere by ramparts; vṛthā tāni viduḥ budhāḥ — the wise know them useless; dattvā avadhānam — giving attention; yat śabde — to sound; vinā eva — without; ravi-saṃstutim — praise of Ravi

BP 1.60.17

श्रेयसां न हि सम्प्राप्तौ पुरुषाणां विचेष्टितम् ।
जन्मन्यविफला सेवा कृता याश्रित्य भास्करम् ॥ १७ ॥

The efforts of people toward attaining the highest good are indeed not fruitless. Service done in a birth by taking refuge in the Sun is not fruitless.

śreyasām samprāptau — in attaining blessedness; na hi — indeed not; puruṣāṇām viceṣṭitam — human effort; janmani — in a birth; aviphalā sevā — fruitless service; kṛtā — performed; āśritya bhāskaram — having taken refuge in Bhāskara

BP 1.60.18

दुर्गसंसारकान्तारमपारमभिधावताम् ।
एको भानुनमस्कारः संसारार्णवतारकः ॥ १८ ॥

For those who rush through the difficult, boundless wilderness of worldly existence, one salutation to the Sun carries them across the ocean of worldly existence.

durga-saṃsāra-kāntāram — difficult wilderness of saṃsāra; apāram — boundless; abhidhāvatām — for those running through; ekaḥ — one; bhānu-namaskāraḥ — salutation to Bhānu; saṃsāra-arṇava-tārakaḥ — ferry across the ocean of saṃsāra

BP 1.60.19

रत्नानामाकरो मेरुः सर्वाश्चर्यमयं नभः ।
तीर्थानामाश्रयो गङ्गा देवानामाश्रयो रविः ॥ १९ ॥

Meru is a mine of jewels, the sky is full of every wonder. The Ganges is the refuge of holy fords, the Sun is the refuge of the gods.

ratnānām ākaraḥ — mine of jewels; meruḥ — Meru; sarva-āścarya-mayam nabhaḥ — sky made of every wonder; tīrthānām āśrayaḥ — refuge of sacred fords; gaṅgā — Gaṅgā; devānām āśrayaḥ — refuge of the gods; raviḥ — Ravi

BP 1.60.20

एवमादिगुणो भोगो भानोरमिततेजसः ।
श्रुतो मे बहुशः सिद्धैर्गीयमानैस्तथामरैः ॥ २० ॥

I have often heard these and similar qualities and powers of the Sun of immeasurable splendor, sung by perfected beings and immortals.

evam-ādi-guṇaḥ bhogaḥ — such and other qualities and enjoyments; bhānoḥ amita-tejasaḥ — of Bhānu of immeasurable radiance; śrutaḥ me — heard by me; bahuśaḥ — many times; siddhaiḥ — from Siddhas; gīyamānaiḥ amaraiḥ — from singing immortals

BP 1.60.21

सोऽहमिच्छामि तं देवं सप्तलोकपरायणम् ।
दिवाकरमशेषस्य जगतो हृद्यवस्थितम् ॥ २१ ॥

I wish to worship that god, the refuge of the seven worlds, the Sun abiding in the heart of the whole universe.

saḥ aham — therefore I; icchāmi — wish; tam devam — that god; sapta-loka-parāyaṇam — refuge of the seven worlds; divākaram — Divākara; aśeṣasya jagataḥ — of the entire universe; hṛdi avasthitam — abiding in the heart

BP 1.60.22

आराधयितुमीशेशं भास्करं चामितौजसम् ।
मार्तण्डं भुवनाधारं स्मृतमात्राघदारिणम् ॥ २२ ॥

He is the lord of lords, the Sun of immeasurable splendor, the supporter of the worlds, the destroyer of sin by the mere act of being remembered.

ārādhayitum — to worship; īśeśam — lord of lords; bhāskaram — Bhāskara; amita-ojasam — of immeasurable splendor; mārtaṇḍam — Mārtaṇḍa; bhuvana-ādhāram — support of the worlds; smṛta-mātra-aghadāriṇam — destroying sin by mere remembrance
