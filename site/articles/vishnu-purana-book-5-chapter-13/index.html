VP 5.13.1

गते शक्रे तु गोपालाः कृष्णम् अक्लिष्ट-कारिणम्।
ऊचुः प्रीत्या धृतं दृष्ट्वा तेन गोवर्धनाचलम् ॥१॥

When Śakra had gone, the cowherds spoke lovingly to Kṛṣṇa, the effortless performer, having seen Govardhana mountain held by him.

Śrīdhara translation

The Rāsa-sport of the great Kṛṣṇa with the Gopīs, his disappearance, and then his appearance again are described here.

VP 5.13.61

तद्-भर्तृषु तथा तासु सर्व-भूतेषु चेश्वरः ।
आत्म-स्वरूप-रूपोऽसौव्यापी वायुर् इव स्थितः ॥६१॥

In their husbands, in those women, and in all beings, that Lord, whose form is his own Self, stands pervading like the wind.

Śrīdhara translation

Now, if Bhagavān descended to establish dharma, how did he himself commit sin by engaging the cowherd women in wrongdoing? This is answered by “in their husbands.” The Gopīs have no sin, since he is able to destroy the fault in them, being the destroyer of harm to his devotees.
