VP 4.7.1

तस्याप्य् आयुर् धीमान् अमावसुर् विश्वावसुर्ः श्रुतायुः शतायुर् अयुतायुर् इति सञ्ज्ञाः षट् पुत्रा अभवन् ॥१॥

VP 4.7.14

गाधिर् अप्य् अतिरोषणायातिवृद्धाय ब्राह्मणाय दातुम् अनिच्छन्न् एकतः श्याम-कर्णानाम् इन्दु-वर्चसाम् अनिल-रंहसाम् अश्वानां सहस्रं कन्या-शुल्कम् अयाचत ॥१४॥

Gaadhi asked for a kanyaa shulka to give his daughter in marriage. He did not want to give Satyavati to Rucheeka, seeing him, who was with beard and moustache grown, looking very fierce, and was also looking very old. Gaadhi was not interested in giving Satyavati to such a rishi. He could not say no, but puta condition to give a kanyaa shulka, &quot;I want you to give me 1000 horses, all of them should have one ear black, and these horses should all be white, and they should have the speed of wind&quot;.
