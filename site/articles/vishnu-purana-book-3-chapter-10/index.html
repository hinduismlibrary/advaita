VP 3.10.1

कथितं चातुराश्रम्यं चातुर्वर्ण्यक्रियास् तथा पुंसः क्रियाम् अहं श्रोतुम् इच्छामि द्विजसत्तम ॥१॥

The duties of the four āśramas and the actions of the four varṇas have been related; I wish to hear the acts of a person, O best of the twice-born.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
कथितं चातुराश्रम्यं (kathitaṃ cāturāśramyaṃ) — the duties of the four āśramas have been related
चातुर्वर्ण्यक्रियास् तथा (cāturvarṇyakriyās tathā) — and likewise the actions of the four varṇas
पुंसः क्रियाम् अहं श्रोतुम् इच्छामि (puṃsaḥ kriyām ahaṃ śrotum icchāmi) — the acts of a person I wish to hear
द्विजसत्तम (dvijasattama) — O best of the twice-born
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kathitaṃ cāturāśramyaṃ cāturvarṇyakriyās tathā puṃsaḥ kriyām ahaṃ śrotum icchāmi dvijasattama || 1 ||

VP 3.10.2

नित्यां नैमित्तिकीं काम्यां क्रियां पुंसाम् अशेषतः समाख्याहि भृगुश्रेष्ठ सर्वज्ञो ह्य् असि मे मतः ॥२॥

Explain fully the acts of people that are obligatory, occasional, and desire-based, O best of Bhṛgus; I consider you indeed all-knowing.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
नित्यां नैमित्तिकीं काम्यां क्रियां (nityāṃ naimittikīṃ kāmyāṃ kriyāṃ) — obligatory, occasional, and desire-based acts
पुंसाम् अशेषतः (puṃsām aśeṣataḥ) — of people, completely
समाख्याहि (samākhyāhi) — explain
भृगुश्रेष्ठ सर्वज्ञो ह्य् असि मे मतः (bhṛguśreṣṭha sarvajño hy asi me mataḥ) — O best of Bhṛgus; you are indeed considered by me all-knowing
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nityāṃ naimittikīṃ kāmyāṃ kriyāṃ puṃsām aśeṣataḥ samākhyāhi bhṛguśreṣṭha sarvajño hy asi me mataḥ || 2 ||

VP 3.10.3

यद् एतद् उक्तं भवता नित्यनैमित्तिकाश्रितम् तद् अहं कथयिष्यामि शृणुष्वैकमना नृप ॥३॥

What you have stated, grounded in obligatory and occasional acts, I shall explain. Listen with a single mind, O king.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
यद् एतद् उक्तं भवता (yad etad uktaṃ bhavatā) — what this has been said by you
नित्यनैमित्तिकाश्रितम् (nityanaimittikāśritam) — grounded in obligatory and occasional acts
तद् अहं कथयिष्यामि (tad ahaṃ kathayiṣyāmi) — that I shall explain
शृणुष्वैकमना नृप (śṛṇuṣvaikamanā nṛpa) — listen with a single mind, O king
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yad etad uktaṃ bhavatā nityanaimittikāśritam tad ahaṃ kathayiṣyāmi śṛṇuṣvaikamanā nṛpa || 3 ||

VP 3.10.4

जातस्य जातकर्मादि क्रियाकाण्डम् अशेषतः पुत्रस्य कुर्वीत पिता श्राद्धं चाभ्युदयात्मकम् ॥४॥

For a son who has been born, the father should perform completely the rites beginning with the birth rite, and also the śrāddha connected with prosperity.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
जातस्य जातकर्मादि (jātasya jātakarmādi) — for the born one, rites beginning with birth
क्रियाकाण्डम् अशेषतः (kriyākāṇḍam aśeṣataḥ) — the full sequence of rites
पुत्रस्य कुर्वीत पिता (putrasya kurvīta pitā) — for his son the father should perform
श्राद्धं चाभ्युदयात्मकम् (śrāddhaṃ cābhyudayātmakam) — and śrāddha connected with prosperity
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

jātasya jātakarmādi kriyākāṇḍam aśeṣataḥ putrasya kurvīta pitā śrāddhaṃ cābhyudayātmakam || 4 ||

VP 3.10.5

युग्मांस् तु प्राङ्मुखान् विप्रान् भोजयेन् मनुजेश्वर यथावृत्ति तथा कुर्याद् दैवं पित्र्यं द्विजन्मनाम् ॥५॥

He should feed an even number of brāhmaṇas facing east, O lord of men; according to his means, he should perform the divine and ancestral rites for the twice-born.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
युग्मांस् तु प्राङ्मुखान् विप्रान् (yugmāṃs tu prāṅmukhān viprān) — even-numbered brāhmaṇas facing east
भोजयेन् मनुजेश्वर (bhojayen manujeśvara) — he should feed, O lord of men
यथावृत्ति तथा कुर्याद् (yathāvṛtti tathā kuryād) — according to means, thus he should perform
दैवं पित्र्यं द्विजन्मनाम् (daivaṃ pitryaṃ dvijanmanām) — the divine and ancestral rites of the twice-born
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yugmāṃs tu prāṅmukhān viprān bhojayen manujeśvara yathāvṛtti tathā kuryād daivaṃ pitryaṃ dvijanmanām || 5 ||

VP 3.10.6

दध्ना यवैः सबदरैर् मिश्रान् पिण्डान् मुदा युतः नान्दीमुखेभ्यस् तीर्थेन दद्याद् दैवेन पार्थिव ॥६॥

With joy, he should give the Nandīmukhas piṇḍas mixed with curds, barley, and badara fruits, by the tīrtha, in the divine rite, O king.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
दध्ना यवैः सबदरैर् मिश्रान् पिण्डान् (dadhnā yavaiḥ sabadarair miśrān piṇḍān) — piṇḍas mixed with curds, barley, and badara fruits
मुदा युतः (mudā yutaḥ) — with joy
नान्दीमुखेभ्यस् तीर्थेन दद्याद् (nāndīmukhebhyas tīrthena dadyād) — to the Nandīmukhas, by the tīrtha, he should give
दैवेन पार्थिव (daivena pārthiva) — in the divine rite, O king
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dadhnā yavaiḥ sabadarair miśrān piṇḍān mudā yutaḥ nāndīmukhebhyas tīrthena dadyād daivena pārthiva || 6 ||

VP 3.10.7

प्राजापत्येन वा सर्वम् उपचारं प्रदक्षिणम् कुर्वीत तत् तथाशेषवृद्धिकालेषु भूपते ॥७॥

Or, at all occasions of increase, he should perform the whole observance with circumambulation according to the Prājāpatya rite, O lord of earth.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
प्राजापत्येन वा (prājāpatyena vā) — or by the Prājāpatya rite
सर्वम् उपचारं प्रदक्षिणम् (sarvam upacāraṃ pradakṣiṇam) — the whole observance with circumambulation
कुर्वीत तत् (kurvīta tat) — he should perform that
तथाशेषवृद्धिकालेषु भूपते (tathāśeṣavṛddhikāleṣu bhūpate) — at all occasions of increase, O lord of earth
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

prājāpatyena vā sarvam upacāraṃ pradakṣiṇam kurvīta tat tathāśeṣavṛddhikāleṣu bhūpate || 7 ||

VP 3.10.8

ततश् च नाम कुर्वीत पितैव दशमे ऽहनि देवपूर्वं नराख्यं हि शर्मवर्मादिसंयुतम् ॥८॥

Then the father himself should give a name on the tenth day: a human name preceded by a deity’s name and joined with Śarman, Varman, and the like.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
ततश् च नाम कुर्वीत (tataś ca nāma kurvīta) — then he should give a name
पितैव दशमे ऽहनि (pitaiva daśame 'hani) — the father himself on the tenth day
देवपूर्वं नराख्यं हि (devapūrvaṃ narākhyaṃ hi) — indeed, a human name preceded by a deity
शर्मवर्मादिसंयुतम् (śarmavarmādisaṃyutam) — joined with Śarman, Varman, and the like
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tataś ca nāma kurvīta pitaiva daśame 'hani devapūrvaṃ narākhyaṃ hi śarmavarmādisaṃyutam || 8 ||

VP 3.10.9

शर्मेति ब्राह्मणस्योक्तं वर्मेति क्षत्रसंश्रयम् गुप्तदासात्मकं नाम प्रशस्तं वैश्यशूद्रयोः ॥९॥

Śarman is prescribed for a brāhmaṇa; Varman is for a kṣatriya. A name of the form Gupta or Dāsa is praised for Vaiśyas and Śūdras.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
शर्मेति ब्राह्मणस्योक्तं (śarmeti brāhmaṇasyoktaṃ) — Śarman is prescribed for a brāhmaṇa
वर्मेति क्षत्रसंश्रयम् (varmeti kṣatrasaṃśrayam) — Varman is for a kṣatriya
गुप्तदासात्मकं नाम (guptadāsātmakaṃ nāma) — a name of the form Gupta or Dāsa
प्रशस्तं वैश्यशूद्रयोः (praśastaṃ vaiśyaśūdrayoḥ) — is praised for Vaiśyas and Śūdras
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

śarmeti brāhmaṇasyoktaṃ varmeti kṣatrasaṃśrayam guptadāsātmakaṃ nāma praśastaṃ vaiśyaśūdrayoḥ || 9 ||

VP 3.10.10

नार्थहीनं न चाशस्तं नापशब्दयुतं तथा नामङ्गल्यं जुगुप्स्यं वा नाम कुर्यात् समाक्षरम् ॥१०॥

He should not make a name that is devoid of meaning, unpraised, joined with a bad word, inauspicious, or reprehensible, or one with an even number of syllables.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
नार्थहीनं न चाशस्तं (nārthahīnaṃ na cāśastaṃ) — not without meaning, nor unpraised
नापशब्दयुतं तथा (nāpaśabdayutaṃ tathā) — nor likewise joined with a bad word
नामङ्गल्यं जुगुप्स्यं वा (nāmaṅgalyaṃ jugupsyaṃ vā) — nor inauspicious or reprehensible
नाम कुर्यात् समाक्षरम् (nāma kuryāt samākṣaram) — he should make no name with an even number of syllables
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nārthahīnaṃ na cāśastaṃ nāpaśabdayutaṃ tathā nāmaṅgalyaṃ jugupsyaṃ vā nāma kuryāt samākṣaram || 10 ||

VP 3.10.11

नातिदीर्घं न ह्रस्वं वा नातिगुर्वक्षरान्वितम् सुखोच्चार्यं तु तन् नाम कुर्याद् यत् प्रवणाक्षरम् ॥११॥

He should make a name that is not too long or too short, not furnished with too many heavy syllables, easy to pronounce, and with agreeable syllables.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
नातिदीर्घं न ह्रस्वं वा (nātidīrghaṃ na hrasvaṃ vā) — neither too long nor short
नातिगुर्वक्षरान्वितम् (nātigurvakṣarānvitam) — not furnished with too many heavy syllables
सुखोच्चार्यं तु तन् नाम कुर्याद् (sukhoccāryaṃ tu tan nāma kuryād) — but he should make that name easy to pronounce
यत् प्रवणाक्षरम् (yat pravaṇākṣaram) — which has agreeable syllables
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nātidīrghaṃ na hrasvaṃ vā nātigurvakṣarānvitam sukhoccāryaṃ tu tan nāma kuryād yat pravaṇākṣaram || 11 ||

VP 3.10.12

ततो ऽनन्तरसंस्कारसंस्कृतो गुरुवेश्मनि यथोक्तं विधिम् आश्रित्य कुर्याद् विद्यापरिग्रहम् ॥१२॥

Then, purified by the subsequent saṃskāras, he should, following the prescribed procedure, acquire learning in the guru’s house.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
ततो ऽनन्तरसंस्कारसंस्कृतो (tato 'nantarasaṃskārasaṃskṛto) — then, purified by subsequent saṃskāras
गुरुवेश्मनि (guruveśmani) — in the guru’s house
यथोक्तं विधिम् आश्रित्य (yathoktaṃ vidhim āśritya) — adopting the prescribed procedure
कुर्याद् विद्यापरिग्रहम् (kuryād vidyāparigraham) — he should acquire learning
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tato 'nantarasaṃskārasaṃskṛto guruveśmani yathoktaṃ vidhim āśritya kuryād vidyāparigraham || 12 ||

VP 3.10.13

गृहीतविद्यो गुरवे दत्त्वा च गुरुदक्षिणाम् गार्हस्थ्यम् इच्छन् भूपाल कुर्याद् दारपरिग्रहम् ॥१३॥

Having acquired learning and given the guru his dakṣiṇā, wishing for household life, O king, he should take a wife.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
गृहीतविद्यो (gṛhītavidyo) — having acquired learning
गुरवे दत्त्वा च गुरुदक्षिणाम् (gurave dattvā ca gurudakṣiṇām) — and having given the guru his dakṣiṇā
गार्हस्थ्यम् इच्छन् भूपाल (gārhasthyam icchan bhūpāla) — wishing for household life, O king
कुर्याद् दारपरिग्रहम् (kuryād dāraparigraham) — he should take a wife
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

gṛhītavidyo gurave dattvā ca gurudakṣiṇām gārhasthyam icchan bhūpāla kuryād dāraparigraham || 13 ||

VP 3.10.14

ब्रह्मचर्येण वा कालं कुर्यात् संकल्पपूर्वकम् गुरोः शुश्रूषणं कुर्यात् तत्पुत्रादेर् अथापि वा ॥१४॥

Or he should spend the time in brahmacarya, having made a resolve; he should serve the guru, or his son and the others.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
ब्रह्मचर्येण वा कालं (brahmacaryeṇa vā kālaṃ) — or the time in brahmacarya
कुर्यात् संकल्पपूर्वकम् (kuryāt saṃkalpapūrvakam) — he should spend, having made a resolve
गुरोः शुश्रूषणं कुर्यात् (guroḥ śuśrūṣaṇaṃ kuryāt) — he should serve the guru
तत्पुत्रादेर् अथापि वा (tatputrāder athāpi vā) — or his son and the others
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

brahmacaryeṇa vā kālaṃ kuryāt saṃkalpapūrvakam guroḥ śuśrūṣaṇaṃ kuryāt tatputrāder athāpi vā || 14 ||

VP 3.10.15

वैखानसो वापि भवेत् परिव्राड् अथवेच्छया पूर्वसंकल्पितं यादृक् तादृक् कुर्यान् महीपते ॥१५॥

Or he may become a Vaikhānasa or a wandering mendicant, as he wishes; whatever he had resolved before, that he should do, O king.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
वैखानसो वापि भवेत् (vaikhānaso vāpi bhavet) — or he may become a Vaikhānasa
परिव्राड् अथवेच्छया (parivrāḍ athavecchayā) — or a mendicant, as he wishes
पूर्वसंकल्पितं यादृक् (pūrvasaṃkalpitaṃ yādṛk) — whatever was resolved before
तादृक् कुर्यान् महीपते (tādṛk kuryān mahīpate) — that he should do, O king
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vaikhānaso vāpi bhavet parivrāḍ athavecchayā pūrvasaṃkalpitaṃ yādṛk tādṛk kuryān mahīpate || 15 ||

VP 3.10.16

वर्षैर् एकगुणां भार्याम् उद्वहेत् त्रिगुणः स्वयम् नातिकेशाम् अकेशां वा नातिकृष्णां न पिङ्गलाम् ॥१६॥

He should marry a wife one-third his age in years; he himself threefold; not one with too much hair or no hair, not too dark or tawny.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
वर्षैर् एकगुणां भार्याम् (varṣair ekaguṇāṃ bhāryām) — a wife one share in years
उद्वहेत् त्रिगुणः स्वयम् (udvahet triguṇaḥ svayam) — he should marry, himself threefold
नातिकेशाम् अकेशां वा (nātikeśām akeśāṃ vā) — not one with too much hair or no hair
नातिकृष्णां न पिङ्गलाम् (nātikṛṣṇāṃ na piṅgalām) — not too dark nor tawny
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

varṣair ekaguṇāṃ bhāryām udvahet triguṇaḥ svayam nātikeśām akeśāṃ vā nātikṛṣṇāṃ na piṅgalām || 16 ||

VP 3.10.17

निसर्गतो विकलाङ्गीम् अधिकाङ्गीं च नोद्वहेत् नाविशुद्धां सरोमां वा ऽकुलजां वातिरोगिणीम् ॥१७॥

He should not marry one malformed by nature, one with an extra limb, impure, hairy, of low birth, or very diseased.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
निसर्गतो विकलाङ्गीम् (nisargato vikalāṅgīm) — one malformed by nature
अधिकाङ्गीं च नोद्वहेत् (adhikāṅgīṃ ca nodvahet) — and one with an extra limb he should not marry
नाविशुद्धां सरोमां वा (nāviśuddhāṃ saromāṃ vā) — not one impure or hairy
ऽकुलजां वातिरोगिणीम् ('kulajāṃ vātirogiṇīm) — of low birth or very diseased
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nisargato vikalāṅgīm adhikāṅgīṃ ca nodvahet nāviśuddhāṃ saromāṃ vā 'kulajāṃ vātirogiṇīm || 17 ||

VP 3.10.18

न दुष्टां दुष्टवाचालां व्यङ्गिनीं पितृमातृतः न श्मश्रुव्यञ्जनवतीं न चैव पुरुषाकृतिम् ॥१८॥

He should not marry one wicked, foul-spoken, deformed on the father’s or mother’s side, marked with a beard, or having a man’s form.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
