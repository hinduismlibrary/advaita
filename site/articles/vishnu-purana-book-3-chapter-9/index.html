VP 3.9.1

बालः कृतोपनयनो वेदाहरणतत्परः गुरुगेहे वसेद् भूप ब्रह्मचारी समाहितः ॥१॥

A boy who has undergone upanayana, intent on learning the Veda, should dwell in the guru’s house, composed as a brahmacārin, O king.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
बालः कृतोपनयनो (bālaḥ kṛtopanayano) — a boy whose upanayana has been performed
वेदाहरणतत्परः (vedāharaṇatatparaḥ) — intent on learning the Veda
गुरुगेहे वसेद् (gurugehe vased) — should dwell in the guru’s house
भूप ब्रह्मचारी समाहितः (bhūpa brahmacārī samāhitaḥ) — O king; a brahmacārin; composed
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bālaḥ kṛtopanayano vedāharaṇatatparaḥ gurugehe vased bhūpa brahmacārī samāhitaḥ || 1 ||

VP 3.9.2

शौचाचारवता तत्र कार्यं शुश्रूषणं गुरोः व्रतानि चरता ग्राह्यो वेदश् च कृतबुद्धिना ॥२॥

There, service of the guru is to be performed by one of pure conduct; the Veda too is to be learned by one observing the vows, with a steady mind.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
शौचाचारवता (śaucācāravatā) — by one possessing purity and conduct
तत्र कार्यं शुश्रूषणं गुरोः (tatra kāryaṃ śuśrūṣaṇaṃ guroḥ) — there, service of the guru is to be performed
व्रतानि चरता ग्राह्यो वेदश् च (vratāni caratā grāhyo vedaś ca) — by one observing vows, the Veda too is to be learned
कृतबुद्धिना (kṛtabuddhinā) — by one steady in mind
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

śaucācāravatā tatra kāryaṃ śuśrūṣaṇaṃ guroḥ vratāni caratā grāhyo vedaś ca kṛtabuddhinā || 2 ||

VP 3.9.3

उभे संध्ये रविं भूप तथैवाग्निं समाहितः उपतिष्ठेत् तथा कुर्याद् गुरोर् अप्य् अभिवादनम् ॥३॥

At both twilights, O king, he should attentively attend upon the Sun and likewise the fire; he should also salute the guru.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
उभे संध्ये (ubhe saṃdhye) — at both twilights
रविं भूप तथैवाग्निं (raviṃ bhūpa tathaivāgniṃ) — the Sun, O king, and likewise the fire
समाहितः उपतिष्ठेत् (samāhitaḥ upatiṣṭhet) — attentively, he should attend upon
तथा कुर्याद् गुरोर् अप्य् अभिवादनम् (tathā kuryād guror apy abhivādanam) — likewise he should perform salutation to the guru
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ubhe saṃdhye raviṃ bhūpa tathaivāgniṃ samāhitaḥ upatiṣṭhet tathā kuryād guror apy abhivādanam || 3 ||

VP 3.9.4

स्थिते तिष्ठेद् व्रजेद् याते नीचैर् आसीत चासति शिष्यो गुरौ नृपश्रेष्ठ प्रतिकूलं न संचरेत् ॥४॥

When the guru stands, he should stand; when the guru goes, he should go; when the guru sits, he should sit lower. A disciple, O best of kings, should not act contrary to the guru.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
स्थिते तिष्ठेद् व्रजेद् याते (sthite tiṣṭhed vrajed yāte) — when [the guru] stands, he should stand; when [he] goes, he should go
नीचैर् आसीत चासति (nīcair āsīta cāsati) — when [he] sits, he should sit lower
शिष्यो गुरौ नृपश्रेष्ठ (śiṣyo gurau nṛpaśreṣṭha) — the disciple toward the guru, O best of kings
प्रतिकूलं न संचरेत् (pratikūlaṃ na saṃcaret) — should not act contrary
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sthite tiṣṭhed vrajed yāte nīcair āsīta cāsati śiṣyo gurau nṛpaśreṣṭha pratikūlaṃ na saṃcaret || 4 ||

VP 3.9.5

तेनैवोक्तः पठेद् वेदं नान्यचित्तः पुरः स्थितः अनुज्ञातश् च भिक्षान्नम् अश्नीयाद् गुरुणा ततः ॥५॥

Told by him, he should recite the Veda, with mind on nothing else, standing before him; then, permitted by the guru, he should eat alms-food.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
तेनैवोक्तः पठेद् वेदं (tenaivoktaḥ paṭhed vedaṃ) — told by him, he should recite the Veda
नान्यचित्तः पुरः स्थितः (nānyacittaḥ puraḥ sthitaḥ) — with mind on nothing else, standing before him
अनुज्ञातश् च भिक्षान्नम् अश्नीयाद् (anujñātaś ca bhikṣānnam aśnīyād) — and, permitted, he should eat alms-food
गुरुणा ततः (guruṇā tataḥ) — by the guru, then
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tenaivoktaḥ paṭhed vedaṃ nānyacittaḥ puraḥ sthitaḥ anujñātaś ca bhikṣānnam aśnīyād guruṇā tataḥ || 5 ||

VP 3.9.6

अवगाहेद् अपः पूर्वम् आचार्येणावगाहिताः समिज्जलादिकं चास्य काल्यं काल्यम् उपानयेत् ॥६॥

He should bathe in the waters first entered by the ācārya; he should bring firewood, water, and the rest for him day after day.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
अवगाहेद् अपः पूर्वम् (avagāhed apaḥ pūrvam) — he should first bathe in the waters
आचार्येणावगाहिताः (ācāryeṇāvagāhitāḥ) — entered by the teacher
समिज्जलादिकं चास्य (samijjalādikaṃ cāsya) — firewood, water, and the rest for him
काल्यं काल्यम् उपानयेत् (kālyaṃ kālyam upānayet) — day after day he should bring
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

avagāhed apaḥ pūrvam ācāryeṇāvagāhitāḥ samijjalādikaṃ cāsya kālyaṃ kālyam upānayet || 6 ||

VP 3.9.7

गृहीतग्राह्यवेदश् च ततो ऽनुज्ञाम् अवाप्य वै गार्हस्थ्यम् आवसेत् प्राज्ञो निष्पन्नगुरुनिष्कृतिः ॥७॥

Having learned the Veda that is to be learned and then obtained permission, the wise one, having discharged his debt to the guru, should enter household life.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
गृहीतग्राह्यवेदश् च (gṛhītagrāhyavedaś ca) — and having learned the Veda to be learned
ततो ऽनुज्ञाम् अवाप्य वै (tato 'nujñām avāpya vai) — then, having obtained permission indeed
गार्हस्थ्यम् आवसेत् प्राज्ञो (gārhasthyam āvaset prājño) — the wise one should enter household life
निष्पन्नगुरुनिष्कृतिः (niṣpannaguruniṣkṛtiḥ) — having discharged his debt to the guru
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

gṛhītagrāhyavedaś ca tato 'nujñām avāpya vai gārhasthyam āvaset prājño niṣpannaguruniṣkṛtiḥ || 7 ||

VP 3.9.8

विधिनावाप्तदारस् तु धनं प्राप्य स्वकर्मणा गृहस्थकार्यम् अखिलं कुर्याद् भूपाल शक्तितः ॥८॥

Having obtained a wife according to rule and acquired wealth by his own work, he should perform all the duties of a householder according to his ability, O king.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
विधिनावाप्तदारस् तु (vidhināvāptadāras tu) — having obtained a wife according to rule
धनं प्राप्य स्वकर्मणा (dhanaṃ prāpya svakarmaṇā) — having acquired wealth through his own work
गृहस्थकार्यम् अखिलं कुर्याद् (gṛhasthakāryam akhilaṃ kuryād) — he should perform all householder duties
भूपाल शक्तितः (bhūpāla śaktitaḥ) — O king, according to ability
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vidhināvāptadāras tu dhanaṃ prāpya svakarmaṇā gṛhasthakāryam akhilaṃ kuryād bhūpāla śaktitaḥ || 8 ||

VP 3.9.9

निवापेन पितॄन् अर्चेद् यज्ञैर् देवांस् तथातिथीन् अन्नैर् मुनींश् च स्वाध्यायैर् अपत्येन प्रजापतिम् ॥९॥

With oblations he should honor the Pitṛs, with sacrifices the gods and guests, with food the sages, with Veda study, and with offspring Prajāpati.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
निवापेन पितॄन् अर्चेद् (nivāpena pitṝn arced) — he should honor the Pitṛs with oblations
यज्ञैर् देवांस् तथातिथीन् (yajñair devāṃs tathātithīn) — the gods and guests with sacrifices
अन्नैर् मुनींश् च स्वाध्यायैर् (annair munīṃś ca svādhyāyair) — the sages with food, and with Veda study
अपत्येन प्रजापतिम् (apatyena prajāpatim) — Prajāpati with offspring
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nivāpena pitṝn arced yajñair devāṃs tathātithīn annair munīṃś ca svādhyāyair apatyena prajāpatim || 9 ||

VP 3.9.10

बलिकर्मणा च भूतानि वात्सल्येनाखिलं जगत् प्राप्नोति लोकान् पुरुषो निजकर्मसमार्जितान् ॥१०॥

By the bali offering he honors beings, and by affection the entire world; a person obtains the worlds earned by his own actions.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
बलिकर्मणा च भूतानि (balikarmaṇā ca bhūtāni) — and beings by the bali offering
वात्सल्येनाखिलं जगत् (vātsalyenākhilaṃ jagat) — the entire world with affection
प्राप्नोति लोकान् पुरुषो (prāpnoti lokān puruṣo) — a person obtains worlds
निजकर्मसमार्जितान् (nijakarmasamārjitān) — earned by his own actions
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

balikarmaṇā ca bhūtāni vātsalyenākhilaṃ jagat prāpnoti lokān puruṣo nijakarmasamārjitān || 10 ||

VP 3.9.11

भिक्षाभुजश् च ये केचित् परिव्राड्ब्रह्मचारिणः ते ऽप्य् अत्रैव प्रतिष्ठन्ते गार्हस्थ्यं तेन वै परम् ॥११॥

All those who eat alms, wandering ascetics and brahmacārins, also remain established in this [household] stage; therefore household life is indeed supreme.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
भिक्षाभुजश् च ये केचित् (bhikṣābhujaś ca ye kecit) — and whoever eat alms
परिव्राड्ब्रह्मचारिणः (parivrāḍbrahmacāriṇaḥ) — wandering mendicants and brahmacārins
ते ऽप्य् अत्रैव प्रतिष्ठन्ते (te 'py atraiva pratiṣṭhante) — they too remain established right here
गार्हस्थ्यं तेन वै परम् (gārhasthyaṃ tena vai param) — householder life is therefore indeed supreme
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bhikṣābhujaś ca ye kecit parivrāḍbrahmacāriṇaḥ te 'py atraiva pratiṣṭhante gārhasthyaṃ tena vai param || 11 ||

VP 3.9.12

वेदाहरणकार्येण तीर्थस्नानाय च प्रभो अटन्ति वसुधां विप्राः पृथिवीदर्शनाय च ॥१२॥

For the work of learning the Veda, for bathing at tīrthas, O lord, and for seeing the earth, brāhmaṇas wander over the land.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
वेदाहरणकार्येण (vedāharaṇakāryeṇa) — for the work of learning the Veda
तीर्थस्नानाय च प्रभो (tīrthasnānāya ca prabho) — and for bathing at tīrthas, O lord
अटन्ति वसुधां विप्राः (aṭanti vasudhāṃ viprāḥ) — brāhmaṇas wander over the earth
पृथिवीदर्शनाय च (pṛthivīdarśanāya ca) — and for seeing the land
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vedāharaṇakāryeṇa tīrthasnānāya ca prabho aṭanti vasudhāṃ viprāḥ pṛthivīdarśanāya ca || 12 ||

VP 3.9.13

अनिकेता ह्य् अनाहारा यत्रसायंगृहास् तु ये तेषां गृहस्थः सर्वेषां प्रतिष्ठा योनिर् एव च ॥१३॥

Those homeless and without food, whose home is wherever evening is: for all of them the householder is indeed the support and source.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
अनिकेता ह्य् अनाहारा (aniketā hy anāhārā) — homeless and indeed without food
यत्रसायंगृहास् तु ये (yatrasāyaṃgṛhās tu ye) — those whose home is wherever evening finds them
तेषां गृहस्थः सर्वेषां (teṣāṃ gṛhasthaḥ sarveṣāṃ) — for all of them, the householder
प्रतिष्ठा योनिर् एव च (pratiṣṭhā yonir eva ca) — is indeed the support and source
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

aniketā hy anāhārā yatrasāyaṃgṛhās tu ye teṣāṃ gṛhasthaḥ sarveṣāṃ pratiṣṭhā yonir eva ca || 13 ||

VP 3.9.14

तेषां स्वागतदानादि वक्तव्यं मधुरं नृप गृहागतानां दद्याच् च शयनासनभोजनम् ॥१४॥

Welcome, gifts, and the like should be spoken to them kindly, O king; he should give those who come to the house a bed, a seat, and food.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
तेषां स्वागतदानादि (teṣāṃ svāgatadānādi) — welcome, gifts, and the like to them
वक्तव्यं मधुरं नृप (vaktavyaṃ madhuraṃ nṛpa) — should be spoken kindly, O king
गृहागतानां दद्याच् च (gṛhāgatānāṃ dadyāc ca) — and to those who have come to the house he should give
शयनासनभोजनम् (śayanāsanabhojanam) — a bed, a seat, and food
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

teṣāṃ svāgatadānādi vaktavyaṃ madhuraṃ nṛpa gṛhāgatānāṃ dadyāc ca śayanāsanabhojanam || 14 ||

VP 3.9.15

अतिथिर् यस्य भग्नाशो गृहात् प्रतिनिवर्तते स दत्त्वा दुष्कृतं तस्मै पुण्यम् आदाय गच्छति ॥१५॥

The guest whose hope is broken turns back from a house; giving his demerit to that householder, he takes merit and goes.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
अतिथिर् यस्य भग्नाशो (atithir yasya bhagnāśo) — the guest whose hope is broken
गृहात् प्रतिनिवर्तते (gṛhāt pratinivartate) — turns back from the house
स दत्त्वा दुष्कृतं तस्मै (sa dattvā duṣkṛtaṃ tasmai) — he, having given demerit to that one
पुण्यम् आदाय गच्छति (puṇyam ādāya gacchati) — takes merit and goes
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

atithir yasya bhagnāśo gṛhāt pratinivartate sa dattvā duṣkṛtaṃ tasmai puṇyam ādāya gacchati || 15 ||

VP 3.9.16

अवज्ञानम् अहंकारो दम्भश् चैव गृहे सतः परितापोपघातौ च पारुष्यं च न शस्यते ॥१६॥

Disregard, ego, and arrogance, and also distress, injury, and harshness are not praised for one who dwells in a house.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
अवज्ञानम् अहंकारो दम्भश् चैव (avajñānam ahaṃkāro dambhaś caiva) — disregard, ego, and arrogance indeed
गृहे सतः (gṛhe sataḥ) — for one dwelling in a house
परितापोपघातौ च (paritāpopaghātau ca) — and distress and injury
पारुष्यं च न शस्यते (pāruṣyaṃ ca na śasyate) — and harshness are not praised
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

avajñānam ahaṃkāro dambhaś caiva gṛhe sataḥ paritāpopaghātau ca pāruṣyaṃ ca na śasyate || 16 ||

VP 3.9.17

यस् तु सम्यक् करोत्य् एवं गृहस्थः परमं विधिम् सर्वबन्धविमुक्तो ऽसौ लोकान् आप्नोत्य् अनुत्तमान् ॥१७॥

The householder who thus properly performs the highest rule is freed from all bonds and obtains unsurpassed worlds.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
यस् तु सम्यक् करोत्य् एवं (yas tu samyak karoty evaṃ) — but whoever thus properly performs
गृहस्थः परमं विधिम् (gṛhasthaḥ paramaṃ vidhim) — the householder’s highest rule
सर्वबन्धविमुक्तो ऽसौ (sarvabandhavimukto 'sau) — he, freed from all bonds
लोकान् आप्नोत्य् अनुत्तमान् (lokān āpnoty anuttamān) — obtains unsurpassed worlds
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yas tu samyak karoty evaṃ gṛhasthaḥ paramaṃ vidhim sarvabandhavimukto 'sau lokān āpnoty anuttamān || 17 ||

VP 3.9.18

वयःपरिणतौ राजन् कृतकृत्यो गृहाश्रमी पुत्रेषु भार्यां निक्षिप्य वनं गच्छेत् सहैव वा ॥१८॥

When age has advanced, O king, the householder who has accomplished his duties should entrust his wife to his sons and go to the forest, or go together with her.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
वयःपरिणतौ राजन् (vayaḥpariṇatau rājan) — when age has advanced, O king
कृतकृत्यो गृहाश्रमी (kṛtakṛtyo gṛhāśramī) — the householder who has accomplished his duties
पुत्रेषु भार्यां निक्षिप्य (putreṣu bhāryāṃ nikṣipya) — having entrusted his wife to his sons
वनं गच्छेत् सहैव वा (vanaṃ gacchet sahaiva vā) — should go to the forest, or together indeed
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
