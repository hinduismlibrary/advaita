VP 1.18.1

तस्यैतां दानवाश् चेष्टां दृष्ट्वा दैत्यपतेर् भयात् । आचचक्षुः स चोवाच सूदान् आहूय सत्वरः ॥१॥

Seeing this conduct of his, the Dānavas, through fear of the Daitya lord, reported it; he quickly summoned the cooks and spoke.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
तस्यैवं — his conduct thus;
दानवाः — the Dānavas;
चेष्टाम — conduct;
दृष्ट्वा — having seen;
दैत्य-पतेः — of the Daitya king;
भयात — from fear;
आचचक्षुः — they reported;
सः च उवाच — and he said;
सूदान — the cooks;
आहूय — having summoned;
सत्वरः — quickly.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

VP 1.18.2

O cooks, this son of mine is ill-minded toward others too, a wicked guide to a bad path; let him be killed without delay.

VP 1.18.3

Then the cooks, terrified, seeing the great poison digested, approached the Daitya lord, bowed down, and spoke this.

Daitya king, the extremely dreadful poison was given by us, and it was digested with the food by your son Prahlāda.

Then the priests came near Prahlāda and first spoke conciliatory words to Prahlāda, who was endowed with humility.

Long-lived one, you were born famous in the three worlds in the family of Brahmā; you are the son of Hiraṇyakaśipu, the Daitya king.

किं दैवैः किम् अनन्तेन किम् अन्येन तवाश्रयः । पिता समस्तलोकानां त्वं तथैव भविष्यसि ॥१२॥

What refuge do you have in the gods? What in Ananta? What in another? Your father is the father of all worlds; you too will become just so.

Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Yes, great-souled ones, this is so; this great lineage is praiseworthy. Who in all these three worlds would speak otherwise of Marīci?

Viṣṇu Purāṇa

My father too has the most excellent conduct in the whole world. I understand this also as true; there is no untruth in this.

Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Devanagari
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
