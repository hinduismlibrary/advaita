VP 3.16.1

हविष्यमत्स्यमांसैस् तु शशस्य शकुनस्य च । सौकरच्छागलैणेयरौरवैर् गवयेन च ॥१॥

Havis, fish, and meat of the hare, bird, boar, goat, eṇeya, raurava, and gavaya,

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
हविष्यमत्स्यमांसैस् तु शशस्य शकुनस्य (haviṣyamatsyamāṃsais tu śaśasya śakunasya) — havis, fish, and meat of the hare and bird
च सौकरच्छागलैणेयरौरवैर् गवयेन च (ca saukaracchāgalaiṇeyarauravair gavayena ca) — and of boar, goat, eṇeya, raurava, and gavaya
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

haviṣyamatsyamāṃsais tu śaśasya śakunasya ca saukaracchāgalaiṇeyarauravair gavayena ca || 1 ||

VP 3.16.2

औरभ्रगव्यैश् च तथा मासवृद्ध्या पितामहाः । प्रयान्ति तृप्तिं मांसैस् तु नित्यं वार्ध्रीणसाम् इषैः ॥२॥

and of sheep and cattle, the Pitāmahas attain satisfaction for an increasing number of months; but through the meat of the Vārdhrīṇasa they are satisfied always.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
औरभ्रगव्यैश् च तथा मासवृद्ध्या पितामहाः प्रयान्ति (aurabhragavyaiś ca tathā māsavṛddhyā pitāmahāḥ prayānti) — likewise sheep and cattle, with months increasing the Pitāmahas attain
तृप्तिं मांसैस् तु नित्यं वार्ध्रीणसाम् इषैः (tṛptiṃ māṃsais tu nityaṃ vārdhrīṇasām iṣaiḥ) — satisfaction; but by Vārdhrīṇasa meat they are satisfied always
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

aurabhragavyaiś ca tathā māsavṛddhyā pitāmahāḥ prayānti tṛptiṃ māṃsais tu nityaṃ vārdhrīṇasām iṣaiḥ || 2 ||

VP 3.16.3

खड्गमांसम् अतीवात्र कालशाकं तथा मधु । शस्तानि कर्मण्य् अत्यन्ततृप्तिदानि नरेश्वर ॥३॥

Rhinoceros meat, Kāla greens, and honey are praised in this rite, giving great satisfaction, O king.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
खड्गमांसम् अतीवात्र कालशाकं तथा (khaḍgamāṃsam atīvātra kālaśākaṃ tathā) — rhinoceros meat, here especially, Kāla greens likewise
मधु शस्तानि कर्मण्य् अत्यन्ततृप्तिदानि नरेश्वर (madhu śastāni karmaṇy atyantatṛptidāni nareśvara) — and honey are praised in the rite, giving great satisfaction, O king
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

khaḍgamāṃsam atīvātra kālaśākaṃ tathā madhu śastāni karmaṇy atyantatṛptidāni nareśvara || 3 ||

VP 3.16.4

गयाम् उपेत्य यः श्राद्धं करोति पृथिवीपते । सफलं तस्य तज् जन्म जायते पितृतुष्टिदम् ॥४॥

Whoever performs śrāddha after going to Gayā, O king, has a fruitful birth, giving satisfaction to the Pitṛs.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
गयाम् उपेत्य यः श्राद्धं करोति पृथिवीपते (gayām upetya yaḥ śrāddhaṃ karoti pṛthivīpate) — having gone to Gayā, whoever performs śrāddha, O king
सफलं तस्य तज् जन्म जायते पितृतुष्टिदम् (saphalaṃ tasya taj janma jāyate pitṛtuṣṭidam) — his birth is fruitful, giving satisfaction to the Pitṛs
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

gayām upetya yaḥ śrāddhaṃ karoti pṛthivīpate saphalaṃ tasya taj janma jāyate pitṛtuṣṭidam || 4 ||

VP 3.16.5

प्रसातिकाः सनीवाराः श्यामाका द्विविधास् तथा । वन्यौषधीप्रधानास् तु श्राद्धार्हाः पुरुषर्षभ ॥५॥

Prasātikā, Sanīvāra, and two kinds of Śyāmāka, being chiefly wild plants, are fit for śrāddha, O best of men.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
प्रसातिकाः सनीवाराः श्यामाका द्विविधास् (prasātikāḥ sanīvārāḥ śyāmākā dvividhās) — Prasātikā, Sanīvāra, two kinds of Śyāmāka
तथा वन्यौषधीप्रधानास् तु श्राद्धार्हाः पुरुषर्षभ (tathā vanyauṣadhīpradhānās tu śrāddhārhāḥ puruṣarṣabha) — and chiefly wild plants are fit for śrāddha, O best of men
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

prasātikāḥ sanīvārāḥ śyāmākā dvividhās tathā vanyauṣadhīpradhānās tu śrāddhārhāḥ puruṣarṣabha || 5 ||

VP 3.16.6

यवाः प्रियंगवो मुद्गा गोधूमा व्रीहयस् तिलाः । निष्पावाः कोविदाराश् च सर्षपाश् चात्र शोभनाः ॥६॥

Barley, Priyaṅgu, mung beans, wheat, rice, sesame, niṣpāva beans, Kovidāra, and mustard are good here.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
यवाः प्रियंगवो मुद्गा गोधूमा व्रीहयस् तिलाः (yavāḥ priyaṃgavo mudgā godhūmā vrīhayas tilāḥ) — barley, Priyaṅgu, mung beans, wheat, rice, sesame
निष्पावाः कोविदाराश् च सर्षपाश् चात्र शोभनाः (niṣpāvāḥ kovidārāś ca sarṣapāś cātra śobhanāḥ) — niṣpāva, Kovidāra, and mustard are good here
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yavāḥ priyaṃgavo mudgā godhūmā vrīhayas tilāḥ niṣpāvāḥ kovidārāś ca sarṣapāś cātra śobhanāḥ || 6 ||

VP 3.16.7

अकृताग्रयणं यच् च धान्यजातं नरेश्वर । राजमाषान् अणूंश् चैव मसूरांश् च विवर्जयेत् ॥७॥

Whatever grain has not had the first-grain offering performed, O king, and Rājamāṣa, Aṇu, and lentils he should avoid.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
अकृताग्रयणं यच् च धान्यजातं नरेश्वर (akṛtāgrayaṇaṃ yac ca dhānyajātaṃ nareśvara) — whatever grain has not had the first-grain offering, O king
राजमाषान् अणूंश् चैव मसूरांश् च विवर्जयेत् (rājamāṣān aṇūṃś caiva masūrāṃś ca vivarjayet) — Rājamāṣa, Aṇu, and lentils also he should avoid
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

akṛtāgrayaṇaṃ yac ca dhānyajātaṃ nareśvara rājamāṣān aṇūṃś caiva masūrāṃś ca vivarjayet || 7 ||

VP 3.16.8

अलाबुं गृञ्जनं चैव पलाण्डुं पिण्डमूलकम् । गन्धारकं करम्भाणि लवणान्य् औषराणि च ॥८॥

Bottle gourd, gṛñjana, onion, piṇḍamūlaka, Gandhāraka, karambha, salts, and alkalines,

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
अलाबुं गृञ्जनं चैव पलाण्डुं पिण्डमूलकम् (alābuṃ gṛñjanaṃ caiva palāṇḍuṃ piṇḍamūlakam) — bottle gourd, gṛñjana, onion, piṇḍamūlaka
गन्धारकं करम्भाणि लवणान्य् औषराणि च (gandhārakaṃ karambhāṇi lavaṇāny auṣarāṇi ca) — Gandhāraka, karambha, salts, and alkalines
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

alābuṃ gṛñjanaṃ caiva palāṇḍuṃ piṇḍamūlakam gandhārakaṃ karambhāṇi lavaṇāny auṣarāṇi ca || 8 ||

VP 3.16.9

आरक्ताश् चैव निर्यासाः प्रत्यक्षलवणानि च । वर्ज्यान्य् एतानि वै श्राद्धे यच् च वाचा न शस्यते ॥९॥

red resins and conspicuous salts, and whatever is not praised in speech: these indeed are to be avoided at śrāddha.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
आरक्ताश् चैव निर्यासाः प्रत्यक्षलवणानि च वर्ज्यान्य् एतानि (āraktāś caiva niryāsāḥ pratyakṣalavaṇāni ca varjyāny etāni) — red resins, conspicuous salts, these indeed
वै श्राद्धे यच् च वाचा न शस्यते (vai śrāddhe yac ca vācā na śasyate) — at śrāddha are to be avoided, and whatever is not praised in speech
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

āraktāś caiva niryāsāḥ pratyakṣalavaṇāni ca varjyāny etāni vai śrāddhe yac ca vācā na śasyate || 9 ||

VP 3.16.10

नक्ताहृतम् अनुत्सृष्टं तृप्यते न च यत्र गौः । दुर्गन्धि फेनिलं चाम्बु श्राद्धयोग्यं न पार्थिव ॥१०॥

Water brought at night, unreleased, and water where a cow is not satisfied; foul-smelling, foamy water is not fit for śrāddha, O king.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
नक्ताहृतम् अनुत्सृष्टं तृप्यते न च यत्र (naktāhṛtam anutsṛṣṭaṃ tṛpyate na ca yatra) — brought at night, unreleased, where a cow
गौः दुर्गन्धि फेनिलं चाम्बु श्राद्धयोग्यं न पार्थिव (gauḥ durgandhi phenilaṃ cāmbu śrāddhayogyaṃ na pārthiva) — is not satisfied; foul-smelling, foamy water is not fit, O king
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

naktāhṛtam anutsṛṣṭaṃ tṛpyate na ca yatra gauḥ durgandhi phenilaṃ cāmbu śrāddhayogyaṃ na pārthiva || 10 ||

VP 3.16.11

क्षीरम् एकशफानां यद् औष्ट्रम् आविकम् एव च । मार्गं च माहिषं चैव वर्जयेच् छ्राद्धकर्मणि ॥११॥

Milk of one-hoofed animals, and camel’s, ewe’s, deer’s, and buffalo’s, he should avoid in the śrāddha rite.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
क्षीरम् एकशफानां यद् औष्ट्रम् आविकम् एव (kṣīram ekaśaphānāṃ yad auṣṭram āvikam eva) — milk of one-hoofed animals, camel’s, ewe’s also
च मार्गं च माहिषं चैव वर्जयेच् छ्राद्धकर्मणि (ca mārgaṃ ca māhiṣaṃ caiva varjayec chrāddhakarmaṇi) — deer’s and buffalo’s he should avoid in śrāddha
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kṣīram ekaśaphānāṃ yad auṣṭram āvikam eva ca mārgaṃ ca māhiṣaṃ caiva varjayec chrāddhakarmaṇi || 11 ||

VP 3.16.12

षण्डापविद्धचण्डालपाषण्ड्युन्मत्तरोगिभिः । कृकवाकुश्वनग्नैश् च वानरग्रामसूकरैः ॥१२॥

By eunuchs, the expelled, caṇḍālas, pāṣaṇḍins, mad and sick persons, cocks, dogs, naked persons, monkeys, and village pigs,

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
षण्डापविद्धचण्डालपाषण्ड्युन्मत्तरोगिभिः कृकवाकुश्वनग्नैश् (ṣaṇḍāpaviddhacaṇḍālapāṣaṇḍyunmattarogibhiḥ kṛkavākuśvanagnaiś) — eunuchs, outcasts, caṇḍālas, pāṣaṇḍins, mad, sick, cocks, dogs, naked persons
च वानरग्रामसूकरैः (ca vānaragrāmasūkaraiḥ) — and monkeys, village pigs
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ṣaṇḍāpaviddhacaṇḍālapāṣaṇḍyunmattarogibhiḥ kṛkavākuśvanagnaiś ca vānaragrāmasūkaraiḥ || 12 ||

VP 3.16.13

उदक्यासूतिकाशौचिमृतहारैश् च वीक्षिते । श्राद्धे सुरा न पितरो भुञ्जते पुरुषर्षभ ॥१३॥

when the śrāddha is seen by menstruant women, women in childbirth, impure persons, and corpse-carriers, the Suras eat, not the Pitṛs, O best of men.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
उदक्यासूतिकाशौचिमृतहारैश् च वीक्षिते श्राद्धे (udakyāsūtikāśaucimṛtahāraiś ca vīkṣite śrāddhe) — when the śrāddha is seen by menstruants, women after birth, the impure, corpse-carriers
सुरा न पितरो भुञ्जते पुरुषर्षभ (surā na pitaro bhuñjate puruṣarṣabha) — Suras, not Pitṛs, eat, O best of men
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

udakyāsūtikāśaucimṛtahāraiś ca vīkṣite śrāddhe surā na pitaro bhuñjate puruṣarṣabha || 13 ||

VP 3.16.14

तस्मात् परिश्रिते कुर्याच् श्राद्धं श्रद्धासमन्वितः । उर्व्यां च तिलविक्षेपाद् यातुधानान् निवारयेत् ॥१४॥

Therefore he should perform śrāddha in an enclosed place, with faith; by scattering sesame on the earth, he should ward off yātudhānas.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
तस्मात् परिश्रिते कुर्याच् श्राद्धं श्रद्धासमन्वितः (tasmāt pariśrite kuryāc śrāddhaṃ śraddhāsamanvitaḥ) — therefore in an enclosed place he should perform śrāddha with faith
उर्व्यां च तिलविक्षेपाद् यातुधानान् निवारयेत् (urvyāṃ ca tilavikṣepād yātudhānān nivārayet) — and by scattering sesame on earth ward off yātudhānas
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tasmāt pariśrite kuryāc śrāddhaṃ śraddhāsamanvitaḥ urvyāṃ ca tilavikṣepād yātudhānān nivārayet || 14 ||

VP 3.16.15

न पूति नैवोपपन्नं केशकीटादिभिर् नृप । न चैवाभिषवैर् मिश्रम् अन्नं पर्युषितं तथा ॥१५॥

Not foul, nor contaminated by hair, insects, and the like, O king; nor food mixed with abhiṣava, nor likewise stale.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
न पूति नैवोपपन्नं केशकीटादिभिर् नृप (na pūti naivopapannaṃ keśakīṭādibhir nṛpa) — not foul, not contaminated by hair, insects, and the like, O king
न चैवाभिषवैर् मिश्रम् अन्नं पर्युषितं तथा (na caivābhiṣavair miśram annaṃ paryuṣitaṃ tathā) — nor food mixed with abhiṣava, nor likewise stale
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

na pūti naivopapannaṃ keśakīṭādibhir nṛpa na caivābhiṣavair miśram annaṃ paryuṣitaṃ tathā || 15 ||

VP 3.16.16

श्रद्धासमन्वितैर् दत्तं पितृभ्यो नामगोत्रतः । यदाहारास् तु ते जातास् तदाहारत्वम् एति तत् ॥१६॥

Whatever is given to the Pitṛs with faith, by name and gotra, takes the form of the food of whatever kind they have become.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
श्रद्धासमन्वितैर् दत्तं पितृभ्यो नामगोत्रतः यदाहारास् (śraddhāsamanvitair dattaṃ pitṛbhyo nāmagotrataḥ yadāhārās) — given with faith to the Pitṛs, by name and gotra, whatever
तु ते जातास् तदाहारत्वम् एति तत् (tu te jātās tadāhāratvam eti tat) — food-form they have become, that takes the form of that food
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

śraddhāsamanvitair dattaṃ pitṛbhyo nāmagotrataḥ yadāhārās tu te jātās tadāhāratvam eti tat || 16 ||

VP 3.16.17

श्रूयन्ते चापि पितृभिर् गीता गाथा महीपते । इक्ष्वाकोर् मनुपुत्रस्य कलापोपवने पुरा ॥१७॥

A gāthā sung by the Pitṛs is also heard, O king, concerning Ikṣvāku, Manu’s son, long ago in the grove of Kalāpa.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
श्रूयन्ते चापि पितृभिर् गीता गाथा (śrūyante cāpi pitṛbhir gītā gāthā) — also heard, the gāthā sung by the Pitṛs, O king
महीपते इक्ष्वाकोर् मनुपुत्रस्य कलापोपवने पुरा (mahīpate ikṣvākor manuputrasya kalāpopavane purā) — concerning Ikṣvāku, Manu’s son, long ago in the Kalāpa grove
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

śrūyante cāpi pitṛbhir gītā gāthā mahīpate ikṣvākor manuputrasya kalāpopavane purā || 17 ||

VP 3.16.18

अपि नस् ते भविष्यन्ति कुले सन्मार्गशीलिनः । गयाम् उपेत्य ये पिण्डान् दास्यन्त्य् अस्माकम् आदरात् ॥१८॥

May there be in our lineage those of righteous conduct who, having gone to Gayā, will give piṇḍas to us with respect.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
अपि नस् ते भविष्यन्ति कुले सन्मार्गशीलिनः (api nas te bhaviṣyanti kule sanmārgaśīlinaḥ) — may these be in our lineage, of righteous conduct
गयाम् उपेत्य ये पिण्डान् दास्यन्त्य् अस्माकम् आदरात् (gayām upetya ye piṇḍān dāsyanty asmākam ādarāt) — who, going to Gayā, will give piṇḍas to us with respect
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

api nas te bhaviṣyanti kule sanmārgaśīlinaḥ gayām upetya ye piṇḍān dāsyanty asmākam ādarāt || 18 ||

VP 3.16.19

अपि नः स कुले जायाद् यो नो दद्यात् त्रयोदशीम् । पायसं मधुसर्पिभ्यां वर्षासु च मघासु च ॥१९॥

May such a one be born in our lineage who will give us pāyasa with honey and ghee on the thirteenth, in the rains and in Māgha.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
अपि नः स कुले जायाद् यो नो (api naḥ sa kule jāyād yo no) — may such a one be born in our lineage who will give to us
दद्यात् त्रयोदशीम् पायसं मधुसर्पिभ्यां वर्षासु च मघासु च (dadyāt trayodaśīm pāyasaṃ madhusarpibhyāṃ varṣāsu ca maghāsu ca) — on the thirteenth, pāyasa with honey and ghee, in rains and Māgha
Transliteration

VP 3.16.20

गौरीं वाप्य् उद्वहेत् कन्यां नीलं वा वृषम् उत्सृजेत् । यजेत वाश्वमेधेन विधिवद् दक्षिणावता ॥२०॥

Or marry a fair maiden, or release a blue bull, or perform the Aśvamedha according to rule, with a dakṣiṇā.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

वृषम् उत्सृजेत् यजेत वाश्वमेधेन विधिवद् दक्षिणावता (vṛṣam utsṛjet yajeta vāśvamedhena vidhivad dakṣiṇāvatā) — bull he may release, or perform Aśvamedha by rule with dakṣiṇā
Transliteration
