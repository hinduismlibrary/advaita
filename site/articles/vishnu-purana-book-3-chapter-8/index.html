VP 3.8.1

भगवन् भगवान् देवः संसारविजिगीषुभिः समाख्याहि जगन्नाथो विष्णुर् आराध्यते यथा ॥१॥

O Lord, O divine Lord, tell me how Viṣṇu, the Lord of the universe, is worshipped by those who seek to overcome saṃsāra.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
भगवन् भगवान् देवः (bhagavan bhagavān devaḥ) — O Lord, O divine Lord
संसारविजिगीषुभिः (saṃsāravijigīṣubhiḥ) — by those who wish to overcome saṃsāra
समाख्याहि जगन्नाथो (samākhyāhi jagannātho) — tell how the Lord of the universe
विष्णुर् आराध्यते यथा (viṣṇur ārādhyate yathā) — Viṣṇu is worshipped
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bhagavan bhagavān devaḥ saṃsāravijigīṣubhiḥ samākhyāhi jagannātho viṣṇur ārādhyate yathā ||१||

VP 3.8.2

आराधिताच् च गोविन्दाद् आराधनपरैर् नरैः यत् प्राप्यते फलं श्रोतुं तच् चेच्छामि महामुने ॥२॥

O great sage, I wish to hear the fruit obtained by people devoted to worship when they worship Govinda.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
आराधिताच् च गोविन्दात् (ārādhitāc ca govindāt) — and from Govinda when worshipped
आराधनपरैर् नरैः (ārādhanaparair naraiḥ) — by people devoted to worship
यत् प्राप्यते फलं श्रोतुं (yat prāpyate phalaṃ śrotuṃ) — the fruit that is obtained, to hear
तच् चेच्छामि महामुने (tac cecchāmi mahāmune) — that I wish, O great sage
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ārādhitāc ca govindād ārādhanaparair naraiḥ yat prāpyate phalaṃ śrotuṃ tac cecchāmi mahāmune ||२||

VP 3.8.3

यत् पृच्छति भवान् एतत् सगरेण महात्मना और्वः प्राह यथा पृष्टस् तन् मे कथयतः शृणु ॥३॥

This is what the great-souled Sagara asked. Aurva answered as he was asked; hear that from me as I tell it.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
यत् पृच्छति भवान् एतत् (yat pṛcchati bhavān etat) — this which you ask
सगरेण महात्मना (sagareṇa mahātmanā) — by the great-souled Sagara
और्वः प्राह यथा पृष्टस् (aurvaḥ prāha yathā pṛṣṭas) — Aurva spoke as he was asked
तन् मे कथयतः शृणु (tan me kathayataḥ śṛṇu) — hear that from me as I tell it
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yat pṛcchati bhavān etat sagareṇa mahātmanā aurvaḥ prāha yathā pṛṣṭas tan me kathayataḥ śṛṇu ||३||

VP 3.8.4

सगरः प्रणिपत्येदम् और्वं पप्रच्छ भार्गवम् विष्णोर् आराधनोपायसंबन्धं मुनिसत्तम ॥४॥

Sagara bowed to Aurva the Bhārgava and asked about the means of worshipping Viṣṇu, O best of sages.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
सगरः प्रणिपत्येदम् (sagaraḥ praṇipatyedam) — Sagara, having bowed to him
और्वं पप्रच्छ भार्गवम् (aurvaṃ papraccha bhārgavam) — asked Aurva the Bhārgava
विष्णोर् आराधनोपायसंबन्धं (viṣṇor ārādhanopāyasaṃbandhaṃ) — about the means of worshipping Viṣṇu
मुनिसत्तम (munisattama) — O best of sages
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sagaraḥ praṇipatyedam aurvaṃ papraccha bhārgavam viṣṇor ārādhanopāyasaṃbandhaṃ munisattama ||४||

VP 3.8.5

फलं चाराधिते विष्णौ यत् पुंसाम् अभिजायते स चाह पृष्टो यत् तेन तन् मैत्रेयाखिलं शृणु ॥५॥

Asked carefully about the fruit that arises for people when Viṣṇu is worshipped, he spoke to him. Hear all that from me, O Maitreya.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
फलं चाराधिते विष्णौ (phalaṃ cārādhite viṣṇau) — the fruit when Viṣṇu is worshipped
यत् पुंसाम् अभिजायते (yat puṃsām abhijāyate) — that arises for people
स चाह पृष्टो यत् तेन (sa cāha pṛṣṭo yat tena) — he spoke, asked by him
तन् मैत्रेयाखिलं शृणु (tan maitreyākhilaṃ śṛṇu) — hear all that, O Maitreya
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

phalaṃ cārādhite viṣṇau yat puṃsām abhijāyate sa cāha pṛṣṭo yat tena tan maitreyākhilaṃ śṛṇu ||५||

VP 3.8.6

भौमान् मनोरथान् स्वर्गं स्वर्गवन्द्यं तथास्पदम् प्राप्नोत्य् आराधिते विष्णौ निर्वाणम् अपि चोत्तमम् ॥६॥

When Viṣṇu is worshipped, [one] obtains earthly desires, heaven, the abode revered in heaven, and the highest nirvāṇa.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
भौमान् मनोरथान् (bhaumān manorathān) — earthly desires
स्वर्गं स्वर्गवन्द्यं तथास्पदम् (svargaṃ svargavandyaṃ tathāspadam) — heaven and the abode revered by heaven
प्राप्नोत्य् आराधिते विष्णौ (prāpnoty ārādhite viṣṇau) — one obtains when Viṣṇu is worshipped
निर्वाणम् अपि चोत्तमम् (nirvāṇam api cottamam) — and the highest nirvāṇa
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bhaumān manorathān svargaṃ svargavandyaṃ tathāspadam prāpnoty ārādhite viṣṇau nirvāṇam api cottamam ||६||

VP 3.8.7

यद् यद् इच्छति यावच् च फलम् आराधितेऽच्युते तत् तद् आप्नोति राजेन्द्र भूरि स्वल्पम् अथापि वा ॥७॥

Whatever result a person desires, in whatever measure, worshipping Acyuta, that result he obtains, O king, abundant or small.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
यद् यद् इच्छति यावच् च (yad yad icchati yāvac ca) — whatever one desires, in whatever measure
फलम् आराधितेऽच्युते (phalam ārādhite 'cyute) — result when Acyuta is worshipped
तत् तद् आप्नोति राजेन्द्र (tat tad āpnoti rājendra) — that he obtains, O king
भूरि स्वल्पम् अथापि वा (bhūri svalpam athāpi vā) — much, little, or even
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yad yad icchati yāvac ca phalam ārādhite 'cyute tat tad āpnoti rājendra bhūri svalpam athāpi vā ||७||

VP 3.8.8

यत् तु पृच्छसि भूपाल कथम् आराध्यते हि सः तद् अहं सकलं तुभ्यं कथयामि निबोध मे ॥८॥

You ask how he is worshipped, O king; I shall tell all that to you. Listen to me.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
यत् तु पृच्छसि भूपाल (yat tu pṛcchasi bhūpāla) — that which you ask, O king
कथम् आराध्यते हि सः (katham ārādhyate hi saḥ) — how he is indeed worshipped
तद् अहं सकलं तुभ्यं कथयामि (tad ahaṃ sakalaṃ tubhyaṃ kathayāmi) — that all I tell to you
निबोध मे (nibodha me) — listen to me
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yat tu pṛcchasi bhūpāla katham ārādhyate hi saḥ tad ahaṃ sakalaṃ tubhyaṃ kathayāmi nibodha me ||८||

VP 3.8.9

वर्णाश्रमाचारवता पुरुषेण परः पुमान् विष्णुर् आराध्यते पन्था नान्यस् तत्तोषकारणम् ॥९॥

The Supreme Person, Viṣṇu, is worshipped by the person who follows varṇa and āśrama conduct; there is no other path for pleasing him.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
वर्णाश्रमाचारवता पुरुषेण (varṇāśramācāravatā puruṣeṇa) — by a person following varṇa-āśrama conduct
परः पुमान् (paraḥ pumān) — the Supreme Person
विष्णुर् आराध्यते पन्था (viṣṇur ārādhyate panthā) — Viṣṇu is worshipped; the path
नान्यस् तत्तोषकारणम् (nānyas tattoṣakāraṇam) — no other is a cause of pleasing him
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

varṇāśramācāravatā puruṣeṇa paraḥ pumān viṣṇur ārādhyate panthā nānyas tattoṣakāraṇam ||९||

VP 3.8.10

यजन् यज्ञान् यजत्य् एनं जपत्य् एनं जपन् नृप घ्नंस् तथान्यान् हिनस्त्य् एनं सर्वभूतो यतो हरिः ॥१०॥

Offering sacrifices, a person worships him through sacrifices; reciting, he recites him; harming others, he harms him, O king, because Hari is in every being.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
यजन् यज्ञान् यजत्य् एनं (yajan yajñān yajaty enaṃ) — offering sacrifices, he worships him
जपत्य् एनं जपन् नृप (japaty enaṃ japan nṛpa) — reciting, he recites him, O king
घ्नंस् तथान्यान् हिनस्त्य् एनं (ghnaṃs tathānyān hinasty enaṃ) — harming others, he harms him likewise
सर्वभूतो यतो हरिः (sarvabhūto yato hariḥ) — for Hari is in every being
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yajan yajñān yajaty enaṃ japaty enaṃ japan nṛpa ghnaṃs tathānyān hinasty enaṃ sarvabhūto yato hariḥ ||१०||

VP 3.8.11

तस्मात् सदाचारवता पुरुषेण जनार्दनः आराध्यते स्ववर्णोक्तधर्मानुष्ठानकारिणा ॥११॥

Therefore Janārdana is worshipped by a man of good conduct who performs the duties stated for his own varṇa.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
तस्मात् सदाचारवता पुरुषेण (tasmāt sadācāravatā puruṣeṇa) — therefore by a man of good conduct
जनार्दनः आराध्यते (janārdanaḥ ārādhyate) — Janārdana is worshipped
स्ववर्णोक्तधर्मानुष्ठानकारिणा (svavarṇoktadharmānuṣṭhānakāriṇā) — by one who performs the dharma stated for his varṇa
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tasmāt sadācāravatā puruṣeṇa janārdanaḥ ārādhyate svavarṇoktadharmānuṣṭhānakāriṇā ||११||

VP 3.8.12

ब्राह्मणः क्षत्रियो वैश्यः शूद्रश् च पृथिवीपते स्वधर्मतत्परो विष्णुम् आराधयति नान्यथा ॥१२॥

A Brāhmaṇa, Kṣatriya, Vaiśya, or Śūdra, O lord of earth, devoted to his own dharma, worships Viṣṇu, not otherwise.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
ब्राह्मणः क्षत्रियो वैश्यः (brāhmaṇaḥ kṣatriyo vaiśyaḥ) — Brāhmaṇa, Kṣatriya, Vaiśya
शूद्रश् च पृथिवीपते (śūdraś ca pṛthivīpate) — and Śūdra, O lord of earth
स्वधर्मतत्परो विष्णुम् आराधयति (svadharmatatparo viṣṇum ārādhayati) — devoted to his own dharma worships Viṣṇu
नान्यथा (nānyathā) — not otherwise
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

brāhmaṇaḥ kṣatriyo vaiśyaḥ śūdraś ca pṛthivīpate svadharmatatparo viṣṇum ārādhayati nānyathā ||१२||

VP 3.8.13

परापवादं पैशुन्यम् अनृतं च न भाषते अन्योद्वेगकरं वापि तोष्यते तेन केशवः ॥१३॥

He does not speak ill of others, malicious gossip, or untruth, or anything that causes distress to another; by him Keśava is pleased.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
परापवादं पैशुन्यम् (parāpavādaṃ paiśunyam) — slander and malicious gossip
अनृतं च न भाषते (anṛtaṃ ca na bhāṣate) — and does not speak untruth
अन्योद्वेगकरं वापि (anyodvegakaraṃ vāpi) — or anything that causes distress to another
तोष्यते तेन केशवः (toṣyate tena keśavaḥ) — by him Keśava is pleased
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

parāpavādaṃ paiśunyam anṛtaṃ ca na bhāṣate anyodvegakaraṃ vāpi toṣyate tena keśavaḥ ||१३||

VP 3.8.14

परपत्नीपरद्रव्यपरहिंसासु यो मतिम् न करोति पुमान् भूप तोष्यते तेन केशवः ॥१४॥

The man who does not set his mind on another’s wife, another’s wealth, or harming another pleases Keśava, O king.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
परपत्नीपरद्रव्यपरहिंसासु (parapatnīparadravyaparahiṃsāsu) — in another’s wife, another’s wealth, or harming another
यो मतिं न करोति पुमान् (yo matiṃ na karoti pumān) — the man who does not set his mind
भूप (bhūpa) — O king
तोष्यते तेन केशवः (toṣyate tena keśavaḥ) — by him Keśava is pleased
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

parapatnīparadravyaparahiṃsāsu yo matim na karoti pumān bhūpa toṣyate tena keśavaḥ ||१४||

VP 3.8.15

न ताडयति नो हन्ति प्राणिनोऽन्यांश् च देहिनः यो मनुष्यो मनुष्येन्द्र तोष्यते तेन केशवः ॥१५॥

The man who does not beat or kill other living beings, O lord of men, pleases Keśava.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
न ताडयति नो हन्ति (na tāḍayati no hanti) — does not beat nor kill
प्राणिनोऽन्यांश् च देहिनः (prāṇino 'nyāṃś ca dehinaḥ) — other living beings and embodied ones
यो मनुष्यो मनुष्येन्द्र (yo manuṣyo manuṣyendra) — the man who, O lord of men
तोष्यते तेन केशवः (toṣyate tena keśavaḥ) — by him Keśava is pleased
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

na tāḍayati no hanti prāṇino 'nyāṃś ca dehinaḥ yo manuṣyo manuṣyendra toṣyate tena keśavaḥ ||१५||

VP 3.8.16

देवद्विजगुरूणां यः शुश्रूषासु सदोद्यतः तोष्यते तेन गोविन्दः पुरुषेण नरेश्वर ॥१६॥

The person who is always devoted to serving the gods, twice-born, and gurus pleases Govinda, O lord of men.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
देवद्विजगुरूणां यः (devadvijagurūṇāṃ yaḥ) — he who, toward gods, twice-born, and gurus
शुश्रूषासु सदोद्यतः (śuśrūṣāsu sadodyataḥ) — is always eager for service
तोष्यते तेन गोविन्दः (toṣyate tena govindaḥ) — by him Govinda is pleased
पुरुषेण नरेश्वर (puruṣeṇa nareśvara) — by that person, O lord of men
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

devadvijagurūṇāṃ yaḥ śuśrūṣāsu sadodyataḥ toṣyate tena govindaḥ puruṣeṇa nareśvara ||१६||

VP 3.8.17

यथात्मनि च पुत्रे च सर्वभूतेषु यस् तथा हितकामो हरिस् तेन तोष्यते सर्वदा सुखम् ॥१७॥

Hari is always pleased with the one whose wish for good is equal toward himself, his son, and all beings.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
यथात्मनि च पुत्रे च (yathātmani ca putre ca) — as toward himself and son
सर्वभूतेषु यस् तथा (sarvabhūteṣu yas tathā) — so toward all beings
हितकामो हरिस् तेन (hitakāmo haris tena) — by him, well-wishing in that way, Hari
तोष्यते सर्वदा सुखम् (toṣyate sarvadā sukham) — is always pleased, gladly
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yathātmani ca putre ca sarvabhūteṣu yas tathā hitakāmo haris tena toṣyate sarvadā sukham ||१७||

VP 3.8.18

यस्य रागादिदोषेण न दुष्टं नृप मानसम् विशुद्धचेतसा विष्णुस् तोष्यते तेन सर्वदा ॥१८॥

Viṣṇu is always pleased with the one whose mind is not polluted by passion and other faults and whose consciousness is pure, O king.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
यस्य रागादिदोषेण (yasya rāgādidoṣeṇa) — whose [mind] by passion and other faults
न दुष्टं नृप मानसम् (na duṣṭaṃ nṛpa mānasam) — is not polluted, O king
विशुद्धचेतसा विष्णुः (viśuddhacetasā viṣṇuḥ) — Viṣṇu, by the pure-minded
तोष्यते तेन सर्वदा (toṣyate tena sarvadā) — is always pleased by him
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yasya rāgādidoṣeṇa na duṣṭaṃ nṛpa mānasam viśuddhacetasā viṣṇus toṣyate tena sarvadā ||१८||

VP 3.8.19

वर्णाश्रमेषु ये धर्माः शास्त्रोक्ता नृपसत्तम तेषु तिष्ठन् नरो विष्णुम् आराधयति नान्यथा ॥१९॥

VP 3.8.38

क्षात्रं कर्म द्विजस्योक्तं वैश्यकर्म तथापदि राजन्यस्य च वैश्योक्तं शूद्रकर्म न चैतयोः ॥३८॥

Kṣatriya work is stated for a twice-born man; in adversity, Vaiśya work is stated for the Brāhmaṇa and the Kṣatriya; Śūdra work is not [stated] for these two.

VP 3.8.39

सामर्थ्ये सति तत् त्याज्यम् उभाभ्याम् अपि पार्थिव तद् एवापदि कर्तव्यं न कुर्यात् कर्मसंकरम् ॥३९॥

When able, both should abandon that [emergency duty], O king; in adversity that same duty must be performed. One should not mix occupations.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

तद् एवापदि कर्तव्यं (tad evāpadi kartavyaṃ) — that same in adversity must be done
न कुर्यात् कर्मसंकरम् (na kuryāt karmasaṃkaram) — one should not mix occupations

VP 3.8.40

इत्य् एते कथिता राजन् वर्णधर्मा मया तव धर्मम् आश्रमिणां सम्यग् ब्रुवतो मे निशामय ॥४०॥

These varṇa-dharmas have been told by me to you, O king. Listen as I properly speak of the dharmas of the āśramas.
