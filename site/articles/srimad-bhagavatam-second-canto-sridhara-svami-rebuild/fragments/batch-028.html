ŚB 2.10.39

कूष्माण्डोन्मादवेतालान् यातुधानान् ग्रहानपि ।
खगान्मृगान् पशून् वृक्षान् गिरीन्नृप सरीसृपान् ॥ ३९ ॥

He also establishes Kūṣmāṇḍas, Unmādas, Vetālas, Yātudhānas and other possessing beings, as well as birds, wild animals, domestic animals, trees, mountains and reptiles, O King.

Word-for-word

kūṣmāṇḍa — Kūṣmāṇḍas; unmāda — beings associated with madness; vetālān — Vetālas; yātudhānān — Yātudhānas; grahān — possessing influences; khagān — birds; mṛgān — wild animals; paśūn — animals; vṛkṣān — trees; girīn — mountains; nṛpa — O King; sarīsṛpān — reptiles.

Transliteration
kūṣmāṇḍonmāda-vetālān
yātudhānān grahān api
khagān mṛgān paśūn vṛkṣān
girīn nṛpa sarīsṛpān
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

गिरीन् इत्य् अस्यानन्तरं नृपेति सम्बोधनम् । द्वि-विधाः स्थावर-जङ्गम-रूपेण । चतुर्-विधाः जरायुजाण्डज-स्वेदजोद्भिज्ज-रूपेण । जल-स्थल-नभांस्य् ओकांसि येषां तान् अपि धत्त इति पूर्वेणान्वयः ॥३९॥

girīn ity asyānantaraṁ nṛpeti sambodhanam | dvi-vidhāḥ sthāvara-jaṅgama-rūpeṇa | catur-vidhāḥ jarāyujāṇḍaja-svedajodbhijja-rūpeṇa | jala-sthala-nabhāṁsy okāṁsi yeṣāṁ tān api dhatta iti pūrveṇānvayaḥ ||39||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. nṛpa — vocative, “O King”; dvi-vidhāḥ — twofold as stationary and moving; catur-vidhāḥ — fourfold as womb-born, egg-born, moisture-born and sprouting; jala-sthala-nabhas — water, land and sky as habitats; dhatte — the verb from the preceding construction continues.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. The list leads naturally into broader classifications: beings are stationary or moving, arise in four principal modes, and inhabit water, land or sky.

ŚB 2.10.40

द्विविधाश्चतुर्विधा येऽन्ये जलस्थलनभौकसः ।
कुशलाकुशला मिश्राः कर्मणां गतयस्त्विमाः ॥ ४० ॥

These and other beings are classified in two ways and four ways, and according to whether they dwell in water, on land or in the sky. Their destinations through karma are auspicious, inauspicious or mixed.

Word-for-word

dvi-vidhāḥ — twofold; catur-vidhāḥ — fourfold; anye — others; jala-sthala-nabha-okasaḥ — dwelling in water, land or sky; kuśala — auspicious; akuśala — inauspicious; miśrāḥ — mixed; karmaṇām gatayaḥ — destinations/results of karma; imāḥ — these.

Transliteration
dvi-vidhāś catur-vidhā ye ’nye
jala-sthala-nabhaukasaḥ
kuśalākuśalā miśrāḥ
karmaṇāṁ gatayas tv imāḥ
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

यच् च राज्ञा पृष्टं यावत्यः कर्म-गतयो यादृशीर् द्विज-सत्तम इति तस्यापि प्रसङ्गाद् अनेनैवोत्तरम् आह । कुशला उत्तमाः, अकुशला नीचाः । मिश्रा इति पदान्तरम् । मिश्रा मध्यमाः । कर्मणां पुण्य-पाप-मिश्राणां गतयः फलानि । सत्त्वादि-भेदेन कर्म-गति-वैचित्र्यं प्रपञ्चयति—सत्त्वम् इति सार्धेन ॥४०-४१॥

yac ca rājñā pṛṣṭaṁ yāvatyaḥ karma-gatayo yādṛśīr dvija-sattama iti tasyāpi prasaṅgād anenaivottaram āha | kuśalā uttamāḥ, akuśalā nīcāḥ | miśrā iti padāntaram | miśrā madhyamāḥ | karmaṇāṁ puṇya-pāpa-miśrāṇāṁ gatayaḥ phalāni | sattvādi-bhedena karma-gati-vaicitryaṁ prapañcayati—sattvam iti sārdhena ||40-41||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. kuśalāḥ — higher/auspicious; akuśalāḥ — lower/inauspicious; miśrāḥ — intermediate/mixed; karma-gatayaḥ — results or destinations of acts; puṇya-pāpa-miśrāṇām — of merit, demerit and mixed karma.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. Śrīdhara treats verses 40–41 jointly as the answer to Parīkṣit’s earlier question about karmic destinations: actions bear higher, lower or mixed results, with further variety according to the guṇas.

ŚB 2.10.41

सत्त्वं रजस्तम इति तिस्रः सुरनृनारकाः ।
तत्राप्येकैकशो राजन् भिद्यन्ते गतयस्त्रिधा ।
यदैकैकतरोऽन्याभ्यां स्वभाव उपहन्यते ॥ ४१ ॥

The three guṇas—sattva, rajas and tamas—correspond broadly to divine, human and lower destinations. Yet each of these again becomes threefold, O King, whenever one natural tendency is modified by the influence of the other two.

Word-for-word

sattvam — goodness; rajaḥ — passion; tamaḥ — darkness; tisraḥ — three; sura — divine; nṛ — human; nārakāḥ — lower/hellish; ekai-kaśaḥ — each individually; bhidyante — are differentiated; gatayaḥ — destinations; tridhā — threefold; svabhāvaḥ — inherent disposition; upahanyate — is modified or affected.

Transliteration
sattvaṁ rajas tama iti
tisraḥ sura-nṛ-nārakāḥ
tatrāpy ekaikaśo rājan
bhidyante gatayas tridhā
yadaikaitaro ’nyābhyāṁ
svabhāva upahanyate
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

सत्त्वादयस् तिस्रो गतयः । सुरादि-शब्दा ऋस्य्-आदीनाम् उपलक्षणार्थाः । अन्याभ्यां गुणाभ्यां स्वभावो गुण उपहन्यतेऽनुविध्यते ॥४०-४१॥

sattvādayas tisro gatayaḥ | surādi-śabdā ṛṣy-ādīnām upalakṣaṇārthāḥ | anyābhyāṁ guṇābhyāṁ svabhāvo guṇa upahanyate ’nuvidhyate ||40-41||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. sattvādayaḥ — sattva and the other guṇas; tisraḥ gatayaḥ — three broad destinations; upalakṣaṇa — representative terms, not exhaustive categories; anyābhyām guṇābhyām — by the other two guṇas; upahanyate — is colored or modified.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. The labels “divine,” “human,” and “lower” are representative. Because the guṇas continually mix, each broad karmic destination itself admits further threefold differentiation.
