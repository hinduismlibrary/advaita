ŚB 2.9.13

भ्राजिष्णुभिर्यः परितो विराजते
लसद्विमानावलिभिर्महात्मनाम् ।
विद्योतमानः प्रमदोत्तमाद्युभिः
सविद्युदभ्रावलिभिर्यथा नभः ॥ १३ ॥

That realm shines on every side with brilliant rows of aerial palaces belonging to the great souls. Illuminated by the radiance of exalted women, it resembles a sky filled with banks of clouds flashing with lightning.

Word-for-word

bhrājiṣṇubhiḥ — with brilliant; yaḥ — which realm; paritaḥ virājate — shines all around; lasat-vimāna-āvalibhiḥ — with rows of splendid aerial palaces; mahātmanām — of the great souls; vidyotamānaḥ — illuminated; pramada-uttama-dyubhiḥ — by the brilliance of the finest women; sa-vidyut — with lightning; abhra-āvalibhiḥ — with rows of clouds; yathā nabhaḥ — like the sky.

Transliteration
bhrājiṣṇubhir yaḥ parito virājate
lasad-vimānāvalibhir mahātmanām
vidyotamānaḥ pramadottamādyubhiḥ
savidyud-abhrāvalibhir yathā nabhaḥ
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

पार्षदान् अनुवर्ण्य पुनर् अपि लोकम् अनुवर्णयति । भ्राजिष्णुभिर् देदीप्यमानाभिः प्रमदोत्तमानां दिवः कान्तयस् ताभिर् विद्योतमानः । सह विद्युद्भिर् वर्तमानाः स-विद्युतस् ताभिर् अभ्रावलिभिः । तत्र विद्युत इव स्त्रियः, अभ्रपङ्क्तय इव विमानानि, नभ इव लोकः ॥१२॥

pārṣadān anuvarṇya punar api lokam anuvarṇayati | bhrājiṣṇubhir dedīpyamānābhiḥ pramadottamānāṁ divaḥ kāntayas tābhir vidyotamānaḥ | saha vidyudbhir vartamānāḥ sa-vidyutas tābhir abhrāvalibhiḥ | tatra vidyuta iva striyaḥ, abhrapaṅktaya iva vimānāni, nabha iva lokaḥ ||12||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. pārṣadān anuvarṇya — having described the attendants; punar api lokam anuvarṇayati — he again describes the realm; vidyut iva striyaḥ — the women are like lightning; abhra-paṅktaya iva vimānāni — the aerial palaces are like rows of clouds; nabha iva lokaḥ — the realm is like the sky.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. After describing the Lord’s attendants, Śrīdhara returns to Vaikuṇṭha itself. The visual analogy is exact: its women are the lightning, the radiant vimānas the clouds, and the realm itself the vast sky. Source-numbering note: this is Vishvasa 2.9.12, corresponding to Vedabase 2.9.13 after the earlier grouped verse.

ŚB 2.9.14

श्रीर्यत्र रूपिण्युरुगायपादयोः
करोति मानं बहुधा विभूतिभिः ।
प्रेङ्खं श्रिता या कुसुमाकरानुगै-
र्विगीयमाना प्रियकर्म गायती ॥ १४ ॥

There Śrī herself, manifest in personal form, worships the feet of the greatly praised Lord through many opulences. Seated upon a swing and celebrated by bees that follow the flowering spring, she sings the deeds of her beloved Hari.

Word-for-word

śrīḥ — Śrī, the goddess of fortune; yatra — where; rūpiṇī — personally manifest; urugāya-pādayoḥ — at the feet of the greatly praised Lord; karoti mānam — offers honor and worship; bahudhā — in many ways; vibhūtibhiḥ — with opulences; preṅkham śritā — seated on a swing; kusumākara-anugaiḥ — by followers of spring, the bees; vigīyamānā — being sung about; priya-karma — deeds of her beloved; gāyatī — she sings.

Transliteration
śrīr yatra rūpiṇy urugāya-pādayoḥ
karoti mānaṁ bahudhā vibhūtibhiḥ
preṅkhaṁ śritā yā kusumākarānugair
vigīyamānā priya-karma gāyatī
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

श्रीः सम्पत् । रूपिणी मूर्तिमती । मानं पूजाम् । विभूतिभिर् नाना-विभवैः प्रेङ्खम् आन्दोलं संश्रिता । कुसुमाकरो वसन्तः, तस्यानुगा भ्रमराः, तैर् विविधं गीयमाना । स्वयं तु प्रियस्य हरेः कर्म गायन्तीव भवति ॥१३॥

śrīḥ sampat | rūpiṇī mūrtimatī | mānaṁ pūjām | vibhūtibhir nānā-vibhavaiḥ preṅkham āndolaṁ saṁśritā | kusumākaro vasantaḥ, tasyānugā bhramarāḥ, tair vividhaṁ gīyamānā | svayaṁ tu priyasya hareḥ karma gāyantīva bhavati ||13||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. śrīḥ sampat — Śrī means divine fortune; rūpiṇī mūrtimatī — embodied in personal form; mānam pūjām — honor means worship; preṅkham āndolam — a swing; kusumākaraḥ vasantaḥ — the flowering season is spring; tasyānugā bhramarāḥ — its followers are bees; priyasya hareḥ karma — the deeds of her beloved Hari.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. Śrī is not an abstraction here: she is personally manifest. Amid the opulences of Vaikuṇṭha she worships Hari, while the humming bees form a natural chorus and she herself sings His beloved deeds.

ŚB 2.9.16

भृत्यप्रसादाभिमुखं दृगासवं
प्रसन्नहासारुणलोचनाननम् ।
किरीटिनं कुण्डलिनं चतुर्भुजं

His face was turned toward blessing His servant; His very glance was intoxicating with joy. His face bore a gracious smile and reddish eyes. Crowned and adorned with earrings, four-armed and dressed in yellow, His chest was marked by Śrī.

Word-for-word

bhṛtya-prasāda-abhimukham — turned toward bestowing grace on His servant; dṛk-āsavam — whose glance is like a joyful nectar or wine; prasanna-hāsa — graciously smiling; aruṇa-locana-ānanam — with reddish eyes upon His face; kirīṭinam — crowned; kuṇḍalinam — wearing earrings; catuḥ-bhujam — four-armed; pīta-aṁśukam — dressed in yellow; vakṣasi — on the chest; lakṣitam śriyā — marked and beautified by Śrī.

Transliteration
bhṛtya-prasādābhimukhaṁ dṛg-āsavaṁ
prasanna-hāsāruṇa-locanānanam
kirīṭinaṁ kuṇḍalinaṁ catur-bhujaṁ
pītāṁśukaṁ vakṣasi lakṣitaṁ śriyā
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

भृत्यानां प्रसादोऽभिमुखम्, दृग् एव आसव इव द्रष्टॄणां हर्ष-करी यस्य तम्, प्रसन्न-हासम् अरुण-लोचनं चाननं यस्य, वक्षसि स्थितया च श्रिया लक्षितम्, अलंकृतम् इत्य् अर्थः ॥१५॥

bhṛtyānāṁ prasādo ’bhimukham, dṛg eva āsava iva draṣṭṝṇāṁ harṣa-karī yasya tam, prasanna-hāsam aruṇa-locanaṁ cānanaṁ yasya, vakṣasi sthitayā ca śriyā lakṣitam, alaṅkṛtam ity arthaḥ ||15||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. bhṛtyānām prasādaḥ abhimukham — oriented toward favoring His servants; dṛk eva āsava iva — His glance itself is like intoxicating nectar; draṣṭṝṇām harṣa-karī — producing joy in those who see Him; prasanna-hāsam — graciously smiling; śriyā lakṣitam alaṅkṛtam — marked and adorned by Śrī upon His chest.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. The Lord’s beauty is described in relation to the devotee: His face is turned toward grace, and His glance itself produces joy in the beholder. Śrī upon His chest is an ornament and identifying mark of that divine form.

वृतं चतुःषोडशपञ्चशक्तिभिः ।
युक्तं भगैः स्वैरितरत्र चाध्रुवैः
स्व एव धामन् रममाणमीश्वरम् ॥ १७ ॥

The Supreme Lord sat upon the most exalted throne, surrounded by four, sixteen and five potencies. He possessed His own intrinsic opulences—qualities that elsewhere appear only temporarily—and delighted eternally in His own abode and essential nature.

Word-for-word

adhyarhaṇīya-āsanam āsthitam — seated on the most worshipful throne; param — the Supreme; vṛtam — surrounded; catuḥ-ṣoḍaśa-pañca-śaktibhiḥ — by four, sixteen and five potencies; yuktam — endowed; bhagaiḥ svaiḥ — with His own opulences; itaratra — elsewhere; adhruvaiḥ — temporary; sva eva dhāman — in His own abode/essential glory; ramamāṇam — delighting; īśvaram — the Lord.

Transliteration
adhyarhaṇīyāsanam āsthitaṁ paraṁ
vṛtaṁ catuḥ-ṣoḍaśa-pañca-śaktibhiḥ

अध्यर्हणीयं वरिष्ठं सिंहासनम् । चतस्रः प्रकृति-पुरुष-महद्-अहंकार-रूपाः, षोडश एकादशेन्द्रिय-पञ्च-महाभूताख्याः पञ्च-तन्मात्र-रूपा याः शक्तयस् ताभिर् वृतम् । स्वैर् भगैः स्वाभाविकैर् ऐश्वर्यादिभिः इतरत्र योगिश्व् अध्रुवैर् आगन्तुकैः । साधारणैर् इत्य् अर्थः । एवं सत्य् अपि स्व एव धामन् स्व-स्वरूप एव रममाणम् । अत एवेश्वरम् ॥१६॥

adhyarhaṇīyaṁ variṣṭhaṁ siṁhāsanam | catasraḥ prakṛti-puruṣa-mahad-ahaṅkāra-rūpāḥ, ṣoḍaśa ekādaśendriya-pañca-mahābhūtākhyāḥ pañca-tanmātra-rūpā yāḥ śaktayas tābhir vṛtam | svair bhagaiḥ svābhāvikair aiśvaryādibhiḥ itaratra yogiṣv adhruvair āgantukaiḥ | sādhāraṇair ity arthaḥ | evaṁ saty api sva eva dhāman sva-svarūpa eva ramamāṇam | ata eveśvaram ||16||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. variṣṭham siṁhāsanam — the most excellent throne; catasraḥ — four potencies; ṣoḍaśa — sixteen; pañca-tanmātra-rūpāḥ — five subtle-element potencies; svābhāvikaiḥ aiśvaryādibhiḥ — with intrinsic opulences such as sovereignty; itaratra yogiṣu adhruvaiḥ — temporary when found in yogins elsewhere; sva-svarūpa eva ramamāṇam — delighting in His own very nature.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. Śrīdhara distinguishes the Lord’s opulences from powers temporarily acquired by yogins: His are natural and intrinsic. Even while surrounded by all cosmic potencies, He remains established in and delights in His own svarūpa—hence He is truly Īśvara.

puṁsāṁ mad-darśanāvadhiḥ
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

मा इति माम् । श्रेयसां फलानां परि-श्रामः परिश्रमः साधन-प्रयासः मम दर्शनम् अवधिर् यस्य स तथा । अतोऽधिकं तु पुनः फलं नास्तीत्य् अर्थः ॥२०॥

mā iti mām | śreyasāṁ phalānāṁ pari-śrāmaḥ pariśramaḥ sādhana-prayāsaḥ mama darśanam avadhir yasya sa tathā | ato ’dhikaṁ tu punaḥ phalaṁ nāstīty arthaḥ ||20||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. mā iti mām — mā means “Me”; śreyasām phalānām — of auspicious and highest results; sādhana-prayāsaḥ — the effort of spiritual practice; mama darśanam avadhiḥ — has seeing Me as its endpoint; ataḥ adhikam phalam na asti — there is no higher fruit beyond this.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. All effort aimed at the supreme good terminates in the Lord’s direct realization. Once that is reached, Śrīdhara says, there is no further or higher fruit still to be sought.

ŚB 2.9.22

मनीषितानुभावोऽयं मम लोकावलोकनम् ।
यदुपश्रुत्य रहसि चकर्थ परमं तपः ॥ २२ ॥

Your vision of My own realm is the effect of My intention and grace. Having heard My instruction in solitude, you performed the highest tapas; even that impulse toward tapas arose from Me.

Word-for-word

manīṣita-anubhāvaḥ — effect or manifestation of intention; ayam — this; mama — My; loka-avalokanam — seeing My abode; yat — because / that which; upaśrutya — having heard nearby; rahasi — in solitude; cakartha — you performed; paramam tapaḥ — the highest austerity.

Transliteration
manīṣitānubhāvo ’yaṁ
mama lokāvalokanam
yad upaśrutya rahasi
cakartha paramaṁ tapaḥ
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

एतच् च मत्-कृपयैव त्वया प्राप्तम् इत्य् आह । मनीषितम् इच्छा तुभ्यम् इदं दातव्यम् इति या ममेच्छा तस्यानुभावोऽयम् । कोऽसौ तम् आह । मम लोकस्यावलोकनं यत् । न चेदं मयैव तपो-बलेन प्राप्तम् इति स्वातन्त्र्यं मन्यस्व तत्-प्रवृत्तेर् अपि मत्-कृतत्वाद् इत्य् आह । रहसि तप तपेति यद् वच उपश्रुत्य परमं तपश् चकर्थ कृतवान् असि ॥२१॥

etac ca mat-kṛpayaiva tvayā prāptam ity āha | manīṣitam icchā tubhyam idaṁ dātavyam iti yā mamecchā tasyānubhāvo ’yam | ko ’sau tam āha | mama lokasyāvalokanaṁ yat | na cedaṁ mayaiva tapo-balena prāptam iti svātantryaṁ manyasva tat-pravṛtter api mat-kṛtatvād ity āha | rahasi tapa tapeti yad vaca upaśrutya paramaṁ tapaś cakartha kṛtavān asi ||21||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. mat-kṛpayā eva — by My grace alone; mama icchā — My intention; tasya anubhāvaḥ ayam — this is its manifestation; mama lokasya avalokanam — vision of My realm; svātantryam mā manyasva — do not regard it as independent achievement; tat-pravṛtteḥ api mat-kṛtatvāt — even the impulse toward that practice came from Me; tapa tapa — the command “perform tapas.”

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. Brahmā is not to interpret the vision as something forced by his independent ascetic power. The Lord’s grace gives both the revelation and, earlier, the very instruction and inclination by which Brahmā undertook tapas.
