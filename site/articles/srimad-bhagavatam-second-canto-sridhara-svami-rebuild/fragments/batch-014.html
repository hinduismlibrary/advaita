ŚB 2.8.22

यो वानुशायिनां सर्गः पाषण्डस्य च सम्भवः ।
आत्मनो बन्धमोक्षौ च व्यवस्थानं स्वरूपतः ॥ २२ ॥

Please explain the creation of those living beings whose subtle conditions remain merged, the origin of heterodox paths, the bondage and liberation of the jīva, and its abiding in its own intrinsic nature beyond both.

Word-for-word

yaḥ — that which; vā — or, also; anuśāyinām — of beings remaining latent with their subtle conditions; sargaḥ — creation; pāṣaṇḍasya — of heterodox or faithless doctrine; ca sambhavaḥ — and origin; ātmanaḥ — of the jīva; bandha-mokṣau — bondage and liberation; ca — and; vyavasthānam — abiding, established condition; sva-rūpataḥ — in its own intrinsic nature.

Transliteration
yo vānuśāyināṁ sargaḥ
pāṣaṇḍasya ca sambhavaḥ
ātmano bandha-mokṣau ca
vyavasthānaṁ sva-rūpataḥ
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अनुशायिनां लीनोपाधीनां जीवानाम् । आत्मनो जीवस्य । व्यवस्थानं बन्ध-मोक्षातिरिक्त-स्वरूपेणावस्थानम् ॥२२॥

anuśāyināṁ līnopādhīnāṁ jīvānām | ātmano jīvasya | vyavasthānaṁ bandha-mokṣātirikta-svarūpeṇāvasthānam ||22||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. anuśāyinām — of the jīvas whose limiting conditions are merged; līna-upādhīnām jīvānām — living beings with latent upādhis; ātmanaḥ — of the jīva; vyavasthānam — abiding; bandha-mokṣa-atirikta — apart from bondage and liberation; svarūpeṇa avasthānam — remaining in its own nature.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. Śrīdhara takes anuśāyin as the jīva whose limiting adjuncts have become latent, and the last question as asking about the self’s own condition apart from the temporary categories of bondage and release.

ŚB 2.8.23

यथात्मतन्त्रो भगवान् विक्रीडत्यात्ममायया ।
विसृज्य वा यथा मायामुदास्ते साक्षिवद् विभुः ॥ २३ ॥

Please explain how the independent Bhagavān sports through His own potency and how, having projected or set aside Māyā, the all-pervading Lord remains aloof like a witness.

yathā — how; ātma-tantraḥ — completely independent; bhagavān — Bhagavān; vikrīḍati — sports, performs pastimes; ātma-māyayā — by His own potency; visṛjya — projecting, sending forth or relinquishing; vā — or; yathā — how; māyām — Māyā; udāste — remains aloof; sākṣivat — like a witness; vibhuḥ — the all-pervading Lord.

Transliteration
yathātma-tantro bhagavān
vikrīḍaty ātma-māyayā
visṛjya vā yathā māyām
udāste sākṣivad vibhuḥ
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

[विष्वास-प्रस्तुतौ अस्मिन् श्लोके पृथक् श्रीधर-स्वामी-व्याख्या न प्रदत्ता।]

[viśvāsa-prastutau asmin śloke pṛthak śrīdhara-svāmī-vyākhyā na pradattā.]

Śrīdhara word-for-word. No separate Śrīdhara gloss is supplied for this verse in the Vishvasa Gauḍīya-prastuti source; none has been invented.

Textual note. This final doctrinal question contrasts the Lord’s complete independence with His employment of potency: He acts through it without becoming bound by it.

ŚB 2.8.24

सर्वमेतच्च भगवन् पृच्छतो मेऽनुपूर्वशः ।
तत्त्वतोऽर्हस्युदाहर्तुं प्रपन्नाय महामुने ॥ २४ ॥

O venerable great sage, please explain all these matters in their proper sequence and according to truth for me, who have approached you in surrender—and also what I have failed to ask.

Word-for-word

sarvam etat — all this; ca — and also more; bhagavan — O venerable sage; pṛcchataḥ me — for me who am asking; anupūrvaśaḥ — in due order; tattvataḥ — according to truth; arhasi — you ought, please be so kind; udāhartum — to explain; prapannāya — to one surrendered; mahā-mune — O great sage.

Transliteration
sarvam etac ca bhagavan
pṛcchato me ’nupūrvaśaḥ
tattvato ’rhasy udāhartuṁ
prapannāya mahā-mune
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

च-शब्दाद् अपृष्टम् अपि ॥२४॥

ca-śabdād apṛṣṭam api ||24||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. ca-śabdāt — from the word “and”; apṛṣṭam api — even what has not been asked.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. The small conjunction ca broadens Parīkṣit’s request: he wants Śukadeva to teach not only everything explicitly asked, but also anything important that his questions have omitted.

atra pramāṇaṁ hi bhavān

[viśvāsa-prastutau asmin śloke pṛthak śrīdhara-svāmī-vyākhyā na pradattā.]

Śrīdhara word-for-word. No separate Śrīdhara gloss is supplied in the Vishvasa source for this verse.

ŚB 2.8.26

न मेऽसवः परायन्ति ब्रह्मन्ननशनादमी ।
पिबतोऽच्युतपीयूषं तद्वाक्याब्धिविनिःसृतम् ॥ २६ ॥

O learned brāhmaṇa, my vital strength is not disturbed by fasting, for I am drinking the nectar of Acyuta flowing from the ocean of your words.

Word-for-word

na — not; me — my; asavaḥ — vital breaths, life forces; parāyanti — depart or become agitated; brahman — O learned brāhmaṇa; anaśanāt — because of fasting; amī — these; pibataḥ — of me who am drinking; acyuta-pīyūṣam — the nectar concerning Acyuta; tat-vākya-abdhi-viniḥsṛtam — flowing forth from the ocean of your speech.

Transliteration
na me ’savaḥ parāyanti
brahmann anaśanād amī
pibato ’cyuta-pīyūṣaṁ
tad-vākyābdhi-viniḥsṛtam
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

ननु तवानशन-द्विज-कोपाभ्यां व्याकुलस्य कुतः श्रवणम् ? तत्राह—नेति । न परायन्ति नापगच्छन्ति । न व्याकुलीभवन्तीत्य् अर्थः । अच्युत-कीर्ति-पीयूषम् ॥२६॥

nanu tavānaśana-dvija-kopābhyāṁ vyākulasya kutaḥ śravaṇam? tatrāha—neti | na parāyanti nāpagacchanti | na vyākulībhavantīty arthaḥ | acyuta-kīrti-pīyūṣam ||26||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. anaśana-dvija-kopābhyām — from fasting and the brāhmaṇa’s anger; vyākulasya — for one supposedly distressed; kutaḥ śravaṇam — how could hearing be possible?; na parāyanti — they do not depart; na apagacchanti — they do not go away; na vyākulībhavanti — they do not become disturbed; acyuta-kīrti-pīyūṣam — the nectar of Acyuta’s glories.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. The anticipated objection is that hunger and the curse should make concentrated hearing impossible. Parīkṣit replies that his life forces are not disturbed at all because the glories of Acyuta are themselves nectar.

ŚB 2.8.27

sūta uvāca

2.9 Answers by Citing the Lord’s Version

ŚB 2.9.1

श्रीशुक उवाच
आत्ममायामृते राजन् परस्यानुभवात्मनः ।
न घटेतार्थसम्बन्धः स्वप्नद्रष्टुरिवाञ्जसा ॥ १ ॥

Śrī Śuka said: O King, apart from the Lord’s Māyā, a real relation between the conscious self—distinct from material objects—and the body and other objects could not arise, just as a dreamer’s relation with a dream-body has no ultimate reality.

Word-for-word

śrī-śukaḥ uvāca — Śrī Śukadeva said; ātma-māyām ṛte — apart from the Lord’s Māyā; rājan — O King; parasya — of the self that is distinct; anubhava-ātmanaḥ — whose nature is consciousness; na ghaṭeta — could not properly occur; artha-sambandhaḥ — relation with objects such as the body; svapna-draṣṭuḥ iva — like that of the dreamer; añjasā — truly, in reality.

Transliteration
śrī-śuka uvāca
ātma-māyām ṛte rājan
parasyānubhavātmanaḥ
na ghaṭetārtha-sambandhaḥ
svapna-draṣṭur ivāñjasā
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

राज-प्रश्नोत्तरं वक्ष्यन् ब्रह्मणे हरिणोदितम् । कथयाम् आस नवमे शुको भागवतं पुनः ॥ तत्र यत् तावद् उक्तं यद् अधातुमतः इत्य् अनेन जीवस्य कथं देह-सम्बन्ध इति तत्रोत्तरम् आह । आत्मनो हरेर् मायाम् अन्तरेणानुभव-रूपस्यात्मनोरथेन दृश्येन देहादिना सम्बन्धोऽञ्जसा तत्त्वतो न घटेत । अत्र हेतुः—परस्येति । स्वप्न-द्रष्टुर् यथा स्वप्न-देह-सम्बन्धो न घटते तद्वत् ॥१॥

rāja-praśnottaraṁ vakṣyan brahmaṇe hariṇoditam | kathayām āsa navame śuko bhāgavataṁ punaḥ || tatra yat tāvad uktaṁ yad adhātumata ity anena jīvasya kathaṁ deha-sambandha iti tatrottaram āha | ātmano harer māyām antareṇānubhava-rūpasyātmano ’rthena dṛśyena dehādinā sambandho ’ñjasā tattvato na ghaṭeta | atra hetuḥ—parasyeti | svapna-draṣṭur yathā svapna-deha-sambandho na ghaṭate tadvat ||1||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. rāja-praśna-uttaram vakṣyan — intending to answer the king’s questions; brahmaṇe hariṇā uditam — what Hari spoke to Brahmā; jīvasya katham deha-sambandhaḥ — how does the jīva become related to a body?; ātmano hareḥ māyām antareṇa — apart from Hari’s Māyā; anubhava-rūpasya ātmanaḥ — of the self whose nature is consciousness; dehādinā sambandhaḥ — connection with body and the like; tattvataḥ na ghaṭeta — does not occur in ultimate truth; svapna-draṣṭuḥ — like a dreamer.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. Chapter Nine begins the answers to Parīkṣit by recounting what Hari taught Brahmā. Śrīdhara answers the embodiment question first: the jīva is consciousness and intrinsically distinct from the seen body; their apparently real bond is produced through Māyā, like the dreamer’s identification with a dream-body.

ŚB 2.9.2

बहुरूप इवाभाति मायया बहुरूपया ।
रममाणो गुणेष्वस्या ममाहमिति मन्यते ॥ २ ॥

Through multiform Māyā the jīva appears as though itself multiform. Taking enjoyment in her guṇas and in the bodies produced from them, it comes to think in terms of “mine” and “I.”

Word-for-word

bahu-rūpaḥ iva — as though having many forms; ābhāti — appears; māyayā — by Māyā; bahu-rūpayā — which is multiform; ramamāṇaḥ — taking enjoyment; guṇeṣu — in the qualities/modes; asyāḥ — of her, Māyā; mama — mine; aham — I; iti manyate — thus thinks.

Transliteration
bahu-rūpa ivābhāti
māyayā bahu-rūpayā
ramamāṇo guṇeṣv asyā
mamāham iti manyate
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

संसारोऽपि माययैवेत्य् आह । बहु-रूपो बाल-युवादि-रूपो देव-नरादि-रूपश् चाभाति । गुणेषु देहादिषु ॥२॥

saṁsāro ’pi māyayaivety āha | bahu-rūpo bāla-yuvādi-rūpo deva-narādi-rūpaś cābhāti | guṇeṣu dehādiṣu ||2||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. saṁsāraḥ api māyayā eva — saṁsāra too is through Māyā alone; bahu-rūpaḥ — apparently many-formed; bāla-yuvādi-rūpaḥ — child, youth and other stages; deva-narādi-rūpaḥ — deva, human and other embodiments; ābhāti — appears; guṇeṣu — among the qualities; dehādiṣu — in bodies and the like.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. Saṁsāra itself is a work of Māyā. The one jīva seems to become child, youth, deva, human and countless other forms because it identifies with bodies and their qualities, and from that identification arise “I” and “mine.”
