VP 3.12.1

देवगोब्राह्मणान् सिद्धवृद्धाचार्यांस् तथार्चयेत् द्विकालं च नमेत् संध्याम् अग्नीन् उपचरेत् तथा ॥१॥

He should honor the gods, cows, brāhmaṇas, Siddhas, elders, and teachers; twice daily he should bow at twilight and attend to the fires.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
देवगोब्राह्मणान् सिद्धवृद्धाचार्यांस् तथार्चयेत् द्विकालं च (devagobrāhmaṇān siddhavṛddhācāryāṃs tathārcayet dvikālaṃ ca) — He should honor the gods, cows, brāhmaṇas, Siddhas, elders, and teachers
नमेत् संध्याम् अग्नीन् उपचरेत् तथा (namet saṃdhyām agnīn upacaret tathā) — twice daily he should bow at twilight and attend to the fires.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

devagobrāhmaṇān siddhavṛddhācāryāṃs tathārcayet dvikālaṃ ca namet saṃdhyām agnīn upacaret tathā || 1 ||

VP 3.12.2

सदानुपहते वस्त्रे प्रशस्ताश् च तथौषधीः गारुडानि च रत्नानि बिभृयात् प्रयतो नरः ॥२॥

A careful man should wear garments that are always clean and unspoiled, approved medicines, and Garuḍa gems.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
सदानुपहते वस्त्रे प्रशस्ताश् च तथौषधीः गारुडानि (sadānupahate vastre praśastāś ca tathauṣadhīḥ gāruḍāni) — A careful man should wear garments that are always clean
च रत्नानि बिभृयात् प्रयतो नरः (ca ratnāni bibhṛyāt prayato naraḥ) — unspoiled, approved medicines, and Garuḍa gems.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sadānupahate vastre praśastāś ca tathauṣadhīḥ gāruḍāni ca ratnāni bibhṛyāt prayato naraḥ || 2 ||

VP 3.12.3

प्रस्निग्धामलकेशश् च सुगन्धश् चारुवेषधृक् सिताः सुमनसो हृद्या बिभृयाच् च नरः सदा ॥३॥

A man should always keep his hair well-oiled and clean, fragrant, wearing fine clothing, and bear white, fragrant, pleasing flowers.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
प्रस्निग्धामलकेशश् च सुगन्धश् चारुवेषधृक् सिताः सुमनसो (prasnigdhāmalakeśaś ca sugandhaś cāruveṣadhṛk sitāḥ sumanaso) — A man should always keep his hair well-oiled and clean, fragrant
हृद्या बिभृयाच् च नरः सदा (hṛdyā bibhṛyāc ca naraḥ sadā) — wearing fine clothing, and bear white, fragrant, pleasing flowers.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

prasnigdhāmalakeśaś ca sugandhaś cāruveṣadhṛk sitāḥ sumanaso hṛdyā bibhṛyāc ca naraḥ sadā || 3 ||

VP 3.12.4

किंचित् परस्वं न हरेन् नाल्पम् अप्य् अप्रियं वदेत् प्रियं च नानृतं ब्रूयान् नान्यदोषान् उदीरयेत् ॥४॥

He should take nothing, even a little, that belongs to another; he should not speak even a little unpleasantly; he should speak what is pleasant, not falsehood, and not speak of others’ faults.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
किंचित् परस्वं न हरेन् नाल्पम् अप्य् अप्रियं (kiṃcit parasvaṃ na haren nālpam apy apriyaṃ) — He should take nothing, even a little, that belongs to another; he should not speak even a little unpleasantly
वदेत् प्रियं च नानृतं ब्रूयान् नान्यदोषान् उदीरयेत् (vadet priyaṃ ca nānṛtaṃ brūyān nānyadoṣān udīrayet) — he should speak what is pleasant, not falsehood, and not speak of others’ faults.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kiṃcit parasvaṃ na haren nālpam apy apriyaṃ vadet priyaṃ ca nānṛtaṃ brūyān nānyadoṣān udīrayet || 4 ||

VP 3.12.5

नान्यस्त्रियं तथा वैरं रोचयेत् पुरुषेश्वर न दुष्टयानम् आरोहेत् कूलच्छायां न संश्रयेत् ॥५॥

He should not cultivate another’s wife or enmity, O lord of men; he should not mount a bad vehicle or take shelter beneath a riverbank’s shade.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

दुष्टयानम् आरोहेत् कूलच्छायां न संश्रयेत् (duṣṭayānam ārohet kūlacchāyāṃ na saṃśrayet) — he should not mount a bad vehicle or take shelter beneath a riverbank’s shade.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nānyastriyaṃ tathā vairaṃ rocayet puruṣeśvara na duṣṭayānam ārohet kūlacchāyāṃ na saṃśrayet || 5 ||

VP 3.12.6

विद्विष्टपतितोन्मत्तबहुवैरादिकीटकैः बन्धकीबन्धकीभर्तृक्षुद्रानृतकथैः सह ॥६॥

He should not associate with the hated, the fallen, the mad, people of many enmities, pests, prostitutes, the husbands of prostitutes, petty people, or liars;

VP 3.12.7

तथातिव्ययशीलैश् च परिवादरतैः शठैः बुधो मैत्रीं न कुर्वीत नैकः पन्थानम् आश्रयेत् ॥७॥

nor with spendthrifts, slanderers, or deceivers. The wise should not make friendship with them or travel a road alone.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
तथातिव्ययशीलैश् च परिवादरतैः शठैः बुधो मैत्रीं (tathātivyayaśīlaiś ca parivādarataiḥ śaṭhaiḥ budho maitrīṃ) — nor with spendthrifts, slanderers, or deceivers
न कुर्वीत नैकः पन्थानम् आश्रयेत् (na kurvīta naikaḥ panthānam āśrayet) — The wise should not make friendship with them or travel a road alone.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tathātivyayaśīlaiś ca parivādarataiḥ śaṭhaiḥ budho maitrīṃ na kurvīta naikaḥ panthānam āśrayet || 7 ||

VP 3.12.8

नावगाहेज् जलौघस्य वेगम् अग्रे नरेश्वर प्रदीप्तं वेश्म न विशेन् नारोहेच् छिखरं तरोः ॥८॥

He should not plunge into the force of a flood from the front, O lord of men; he should not enter a burning house or climb to a tree’s top.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
नावगाहेज् जलौघस्य वेगम् अग्रे नरेश्वर प्रदीप्तं (nāvagāhej jalaughasya vegam agre nareśvara pradīptaṃ) — He should not plunge into the force of a flood from the front, O lord of men
वेश्म न विशेन् नारोहेच् छिखरं तरोः (veśma na viśen nārohec chikharaṃ taroḥ) — he should not enter a burning house or climb to a tree’s top.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nāvagāhej jalaughasya vegam agre nareśvara pradīptaṃ veśma na viśen nārohec chikharaṃ taroḥ || 8 ||

VP 3.12.9

न कुर्याद् दन्तसंघर्षं न कुष्णीयाच् च नासिकाम् नासंवृतमुखो जृम्भेच् श्वासकासौ च वर्जयेत् ॥९॥

He should not grind his teeth or pick his nose; he should not yawn with his mouth uncovered; he should avoid heavy breathing and coughing.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
न कुर्याद् दन्तसंघर्षं न कुष्णीयाच् च (na kuryād dantasaṃgharṣaṃ na kuṣṇīyāc ca) — He should not grind his teeth or pick his nose; he should not yawn with his mouth uncovered
नासिकाम् नासंवृतमुखो जृम्भेच् श्वासकासौ च वर्जयेत् (nāsikām nāsaṃvṛtamukho jṛmbhec śvāsakāsau ca varjayet) — he should avoid heavy breathing and coughing.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

na kuryād dantasaṃgharṣaṃ na kuṣṇīyāc ca nāsikām nāsaṃvṛtamukho jṛmbhec śvāsakāsau ca varjayet || 9 ||

VP 3.12.10

नोच्चैर् हसेत् सशब्दं च न मुञ्चेत् पवनं बुधः नखान् न खादयेच् छिन्द्यान् न तृणं न महीं लिखेत् ॥१०॥

He should not bite nails; he should not cut grass or scratch the earth.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
नोच्चैर् हसेत् सशब्दं च न मुञ्चेत् पवनं बुधः नखान् (noccair haset saśabdaṃ ca na muñcet pavanaṃ budhaḥ nakhān) — He should not bite nails
न खादयेच् छिन्द्यान् न तृणं न महीं लिखेत् (na khādayec chindyān na tṛṇaṃ na mahīṃ likhet) — he should not cut grass or scratch the earth.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

noccair haset saśabdaṃ ca na muñcet pavanaṃ budhaḥ nakhān na khādayec chindyān na tṛṇaṃ na mahīṃ likhet || 10 ||

VP 3.12.11

न श्मश्रु भक्षयेल् लोष्टं न मृद् नीयाद् विचक्षणः ज्योतींष्य् अमेध्यः शस्तानि नाभिवीक्षेत च प्रभो ॥११॥

The discerning man should not eat beard or clod, nor carry earth; while impure he should not gaze at auspicious stars, O lord.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
न श्मश्रु भक्षयेल् लोष्टं न मृद् नीयाद् (na śmaśru bhakṣayel loṣṭaṃ na mṛd nīyād) — The discerning man should not eat beard or clod, nor carry earth
विचक्षणः ज्योतींष्य् अमेध्यः शस्तानि नाभिवीक्षेत च प्रभो (vicakṣaṇaḥ jyotīṃṣy amedhyaḥ śastāni nābhivīkṣeta ca prabho) — while impure he should not gaze at auspicious stars, O lord.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

na śmaśru bhakṣayel loṣṭaṃ na mṛd nīyād vicakṣaṇaḥ jyotīṃṣy amedhyaḥ śastāni nābhivīkṣeta ca prabho || 11 ||

VP 3.12.12

नग्नां परस्त्रियं चैव सूर्यं चास्तमनोदये न हुंकुर्याच् छवं चैव शवगन्धो हि सोमजः ॥१२॥

He should not look at a naked woman who is not his, or at the Sun at its rising or setting; he should not cry out at a corpse, for the scent of a corpse is born of Soma.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
नग्नां परस्त्रियं चैव सूर्यं चास्तमनोदये न (nagnāṃ parastriyaṃ caiva sūryaṃ cāstamanodaye na) — He should not look at a naked woman who is not his, or at the Sun at its rising or setting
हुंकुर्याच् छवं चैव शवगन्धो हि सोमजः (huṃkuryāc chavaṃ caiva śavagandho hi somajaḥ) — he should not cry out at a corpse, for the scent of a corpse is born of Soma.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nagnāṃ parastriyaṃ caiva sūryaṃ cāstamanodaye na huṃkuryāc chavaṃ caiva śavagandho hi somajaḥ || 12 ||

VP 3.12.13

चतुष्पथं चैत्यतरुं श्मशानोपवनानि च दुष्टस्त्रीसंनिकर्षं च वर्जयेन् निशि सर्वदा ॥१३॥

He should always avoid crossroads, caitya trees, cremation-ground groves, and at night the company of wicked women.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

च वर्जयेन् निशि सर्वदा (ca varjayen niśi sarvadā) — cremation-ground groves, and at night the company of wicked women.
Transliteration

catuṣpathaṃ caityataruṃ śmaśānopavanāni ca duṣṭastrīsaṃnikarṣaṃ ca varjayen niśi sarvadā || 13 ||

VP 3.12.14

पूज्यदेवद्विजज्योतिश्छायां नातिक्रमेद् बुधः नैकः शून्याटवीं गच्छेन् न च शून्यगृहे वसेत् ॥१४॥

The wise man should not cross the shadows of worshipful gods, brāhmaṇas, and luminaries; he should not go alone to an empty forest or dwell in an empty house.

Viṣṇu Purāṇa

गच्छेन् न च शून्यगृहे वसेत् (gacchen na ca śūnyagṛhe vaset) — luminaries; he should not go alone to an empty forest or dwell in an empty house.
Transliteration

VP 3.12.15

केशास्थिकण्टकामेध्यबलिभस्मतुषांस् तथा स्नानार्द्रधरणीं चैव दूरतः परिवर्जयेत् ॥१५॥

चैव दूरतः परिवर्जयेत् (caiva dūrataḥ parivarjayet) — bali, ashes, and chaff, and also from ground wet after bathing.

VP 3.12.16

नानार्यान् आश्रयेत् कांश्चिन् न जिह्मं रोचयेद् बुधः उपसर्पेत न व्यालांश् चिरं तिष्ठेन् न चोत्थितः ॥१६॥

The wise should not resort to ignoble people or delight in crookedness; he should not approach wild beasts, stand for a long time, or remain upright.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

उपसर्पेत न व्यालांश् चिरं तिष्ठेन् न चोत्थितः (upasarpeta na vyālāṃś ciraṃ tiṣṭhen na cotthitaḥ) — he should not approach wild beasts, stand for a long time, or remain upright.

VP 3.12.17

अतीव जागरस्वप्ने तद्वत् स्थानासने बुधः न सेवेत तथा शय्यां व्यायामं च नरेश्वर ॥१७॥

The wise should not indulge in excessive waking or sleep, standing or sitting, a bed, or exercise, O king.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
अतीव जागरस्वप्ने तद्वत् स्थानासने बुधः न (atīva jāgarasvapne tadvat sthānāsane budhaḥ na) — The wise should not indulge in excessive waking or sleep
सेवेत तथा शय्यां व्यायामं च नरेश्वर (seveta tathā śayyāṃ vyāyāmaṃ ca nareśvara) — standing or sitting, a bed, or exercise, O king.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

VP 3.12.18

दंष्ट्रिणः शृङ्गिणश् चैव प्राज्ञो दूरेण वर्जयेत् अवश्यायं च राजेन्द्र पुरो वातातपौ तथा ॥१८॥

A discerning man should avoid fanged and horned creatures from afar; he should also avoid dew, wind, and sunlight directly before him, O king.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
दंष्ट्रिणः शृङ्गिणश् चैव प्राज्ञो दूरेण वर्जयेत् (daṃṣṭriṇaḥ śṛṅgiṇaś caiva prājño dūreṇa varjayet) — A discerning man should avoid fanged and horned creatures from afar
अवश्यायं च राजेन्द्र पुरो वातातपौ तथा (avaśyāyaṃ ca rājendra puro vātātapau tathā) — he should also avoid dew, wind, and sunlight directly before him, O king.

वर्षातपादिके छत्री दण्डी रात्र्यटवीषु च शरीरत्राणकामो वै सोपानत्कः सदा व्रजेत् ॥३८॥

For protection of the body, he should always travel with an umbrella in rain or sun, with a staff, and with shoes at night and in forests.

VP 3.12.39

VP 3.12.44

VP 3.12.45

प्राणिनाम् उपकाराय यद् एवेह परत्र च कर्मणा मनसा वाचा तद् एव मतिमान् भजेत् ॥४५॥

Whatever action, by body, mind, or speech, is for the benefit of living beings here and hereafter, the wise man should pursue.

मनसा वाचा तद् एव मतिमान् भजेत् (manasā vācā tad eva matimān bhajet) — hereafter, the wise man should pursue.
