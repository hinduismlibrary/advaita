Pūrvapakṣa

Pūrvapakṣa / पूर्वपक्ष

It is said that Varna is directly correlated and in fact predicated upon by Jati. One born as a brahmin-yoni belongs to the Brahmin jati and Brahmin varna and likewise for the other varnas. However one simply is not a Brahmin entirely by jati alone but also by the Vedic karmas pertaining to initiation , vedic study etc. In short birth/jati determines initial varna status and eligibility whereas samskaras and vedic karmas complete and actualise the status required for certain adhikaras. In other words one without a Brahmin-Kshatriya-Vaishya yoni can never attain dvija-hood and one should follow his jati dharma. Let us provide some pramana for the Purvapaksha:

Manusmṛti 10.4–5

Show Sanskrit
ब्राह्मणः क्षत्रियो वैश्यस्त्रयो वर्णा द्विजातयः। चतुर्थ एकजातिस्तु शूद्रो नास्ति तु पञ्चमः॥ ४॥ सर्ववर्णेषु तुल्यासु पत्नीष्वक्षतयोनिषु। आनुलोम्येन सम्भूता जात्या ज्ञेयास्त एव ते॥ ५॥

The brāhmaṇa, kṣatriya, and vaiśya are the three twice-born varṇas; the fourth, the śūdra, is once-born, and there is no fifth. Among all varṇas, those born in the natural order from wives of equal status are to be regarded as belonging to the same jāti as their fathers.

Padma Purāṇa 7.47.129

Show Sanskrit
जन्मना ब्राह्मणो ज्ञेयः संस्कारैर्द्विज उच्यते॥ १२९॥

By birth he is known as a brāhmaṇa, and through the initiatory rites he is called a dvija, or twice-born

Chāndogya Upaniṣad 5.10.7

Show Sanskrit
तद्य इह रमणीयचरणा अभ्याशो ह यत्ते रमणीयां योनिमापद्येरन् ब्राह्मणयोनिं वा क्षत्रिययोनिं वा वैश्ययोनिं वा। अथ य इह कपूयचरणा अभ्याशो ह यत्ते कपूयां योनिमापद्येरन् श्वयोनिं वा सूकरयोनिं वा चण्डालयोनिं वा॥

Those whose conduct in this world was good quickly attain a good birth: birth as a brāhmaṇa, kṣatriya, or vaiśya. Those whose conduct was evil quickly attain an evil birth: birth as a dog, a pig, or a caṇḍāla.

Show Remark 3

However being born as a Brahmin will provide certain benefits no matter how indulged in sin you are. Such as:

Show Sanskrit
न जातु ब्राह्मणं हन्यात् सर्वपापेष्वपि स्थितम् । राष्ट्रादेनं बहिः कुर्यात् समग्रधनमक्षतम् ॥ ३८० ॥

Verily he shall not kill the Brāhmaṇa, even though he be steeped in all crimes; he should banish him from the kingdom, with all his property and unhurt.—(380).

Show Sanskrit
जातिमात्रोपजीवी वा कामं स्याद् ब्राह्मणब्रुवः । धर्मप्रवक्ता नृपतेर्न शूद्रः कथं चन ?? ॥ २० ॥

Even a so-called Brāhmaṇa, who makes a living by his caste only, may, at pleasure be the propounder of the Law for the king,—but not a Śūdra under any circumtsances.—(20)

Show Sanskrit
विस्रब्धं ब्राह्मणः शूद्राद् द्रव्योपादानमाचरेत् । न हि तस्यास्ति किं चित् स्वं भर्तृहार्यधनो हि सः ॥ ४१७ ॥

The Brāhmaṇa may confidently have recourse to seizing the goods of the Śūdra; as the latter has no property, and his property is meant to be seized by the master.—(417)
