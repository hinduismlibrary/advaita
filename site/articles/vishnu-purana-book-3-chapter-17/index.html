VP 3.17.1

इत्य् आह भगवान् और्वः सगराय महात्मने । सदाचारान् पुरा सम्यङ् मैत्रेय परिपृच्छते ॥१॥

Thus Bhagavān Aurva spoke to the great-souled Sagara, when he formerly asked properly about good conduct, O Maitreya.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
इत्य् आह भगवान् और्वः सगराय (ity āha bhagavān aurvaḥ sagarāya) — thus Bhagavān Aurva spoke to Sagara
महात्मने सदाचारान् पुरा सम्यङ् मैत्रेय परिपृच्छते (mahātmane sadācārān purā samyaṅ maitreya paripṛcchate) — great-souled, formerly properly asking about good conduct, O Maitreya
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ity āha bhagavān aurvaḥ sagarāya mahātmane sadācārān purā samyaṅ maitreya paripṛcchate || 1 ||

VP 3.17.2

मयाप्य् एतद् अशेषेण कथितं भवतो द्विज । समुल्लङ्घ्य सदाचारं कश्चिन् नाप्नोति शोभनम् ॥२॥

I too have told you all this completely, O dvija; no one who transgresses good conduct attains the good.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
मयाप्य् एतद् अशेषेण कथितं भवतो (mayāpy etad aśeṣeṇa kathitaṃ bhavato) — I too have told you all this completely
द्विज समुल्लङ्घ्य सदाचारं कश्चिन् नाप्नोति शोभनम् (dvija samullaṅghya sadācāraṃ kaścin nāpnoti śobhanam) — O dvija, transgressing good conduct, no one attains the good
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

mayāpy etad aśeṣeṇa kathitaṃ bhavato dvija samullaṅghya sadācāraṃ kaścin nāpnoti śobhanam || 2 ||

VP 3.17.3

षण्डापविद्धप्रमुखा विदिता भगवन् मया । उदक्याद्याश् च ये सर्वे नग्नम् इच्छामि वेदितुम् ॥३॥

The eunuch, the expelled, the caṇḍāla, and the rest are known by me, O Bhagavan; and all those menstruating and the like. I wish to know nagna.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
षण्डापविद्धप्रमुखा विदिता भगवन् मया उदक्याद्याश् (ṣaṇḍāpaviddhapramukhā viditā bhagavan mayā udakyādyāś) — eunuch, expelled, caṇḍāla and foremost ones are known by me, O Bhagavan; all those menstruating and the rest
च ये सर्वे नग्नम् इच्छामि वेदितुम् (ca ye sarve nagnam icchāmi veditum) — and all who are so; nagna I wish to know
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ṣaṇḍāpaviddhapramukhā viditā bhagavan mayā udakyādyāś ca ye sarve nagnam icchāmi veditum || 3 ||

VP 3.17.4

को नग्नः किंसमाचारो नग्नसंज्ञां नरो लभेत् । नग्नस्वरूपम् इच्छामि यथावत् गदितं त्वया ॥४॥

Who is nagna, and of what conduct? How does a person attain the designation nagna? I wish to hear the nature of nagna exactly as you have told it.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
को नग्नः किंसमाचारो नग्नसंज्ञां नरो (ko nagnaḥ kiṃsamācāro nagnasaṃjñāṃ naro) — who is nagna, of what conduct, how a man attains the name nagna
लभेत् नग्नस्वरूपम् इच्छामि यथावत् गदितं त्वया (labhet nagnasvarūpam icchāmi yathāvat gaditaṃ tvayā) — I wish to hear the nature of nagna as you have told it
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ko nagnaḥ kiṃsamācāro nagnasaṃjñāṃ naro labhet nagnasvarūpam icchāmi yathāvat gaditaṃ tvayā || 4 ||

VP 3.17.5

ऋग्यजुःसामसंज्ञेयं त्रयी वर्णावृतिर् द्विज । एताम् उज्झति यो मोहात् स नग्नः पातकी स्मृतः ॥५॥

The threefold [Veda], known as Ṛc, Yajus, and Sāman, is the covering of the varṇas, O dvija. Whoever abandons it through delusion is remembered as nagna, sinful.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
ऋग्यजुःसामसंज्ञेयं त्रयी वर्णावृतिर् द्विज एताम् उज्झति (ṛgyajuḥsāmasaṃjñeyaṃ trayī varṇāvṛtir dvija etām ujjhati) — the three Vedas named Ṛc, Yajus, Sāman are the covering of varṇas, O dvija; this one who abandons
यो मोहात् स नग्नः पातकी स्मृतः (yo mohāt sa nagnaḥ pātakī smṛtaḥ) — through delusion he is remembered as nagna, sinful
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ṛgyajuḥsāmasaṃjñeyaṃ trayī varṇāvṛtir dvija etām ujjhati yo mohāt sa nagnaḥ pātakī smṛtaḥ || 5 ||

VP 3.17.6

त्रयी समस्तवर्णानां द्विज संवरणं यतः । नग्नो भवत्य् उज्झितायाम् अतस् तस्याम् असंशयम् ॥६॥

Because the threefold Veda is the covering of all varṇas, when it is abandoned one becomes nagna; of this there is no doubt.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
त्रयी समस्तवर्णानां द्विज संवरणं यतः (trayī samastavarṇānāṃ dvija saṃvaraṇaṃ yataḥ) — the threefold Veda, O dvija, is the covering of all varṇas, because
नग्नो भवत्य् उज्झितायाम् अतस् तस्याम् असंशयम् (nagno bhavaty ujjhitāyām atas tasyām asaṃśayam) — when it is abandoned one becomes nagna, of that no doubt
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

trayī samastavarṇānāṃ dvija saṃvaraṇaṃ yataḥ nagno bhavaty ujjhitāyām atas tasyām asaṃśayam || 6 ||

VP 3.17.7

इदं च श्रूयताम् अन्यद् भीष्माय सुमहात्मने । कथयाम् आस धर्मज्ञो वसिष्ठो ऽस्मत्पितामहः ॥७॥

Hear this other account too: our grandfather Vasiṣṭha, the knower of dharma, formerly told it to the great-souled Bhīṣma.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
इदं च श्रूयताम् अन्यद् भीष्माय (idaṃ ca śrūyatām anyad bhīṣmāya) — hear this other account, to Bhīṣma, the great-souled
सुमहात्मने कथयाम् आस धर्मज्ञो वसिष्ठो ऽस्मत्पितामहः (sumahātmane kathayām āsa dharmajño vasiṣṭho 'smatpitāmahaḥ) — our grandfather Vasiṣṭha, knower of dharma, told it formerly
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

idaṃ ca śrūyatām anyad bhīṣmāya sumahātmane kathayām āsa dharmajño vasiṣṭho 'smatpitāmahaḥ || 7 ||

VP 3.17.8

मयापि तस्य गदतः श्रुतम् एतन् महात्मनः । नग्नसंबन्धि मैत्रेय यत् पृष्टो ऽहम् इह त्वया ॥८॥

I too heard this from that great-souled one as he spoke, concerning nagna, which you asked me about here, O Maitreya.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
मयापि तस्य गदतः श्रुतम् एतन् महात्मनः (mayāpi tasya gadataḥ śrutam etan mahātmanaḥ) — I too heard this from him as he spoke, the great-souled
नग्नसंबन्धि मैत्रेय यत् पृष्टो ऽहम् इह त्वया (nagnasaṃbandhi maitreya yat pṛṣṭo 'ham iha tvayā) — about nagna, O Maitreya, which you asked me about here
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

mayāpi tasya gadataḥ śrutam etan mahātmanaḥ nagnasaṃbandhi maitreya yat pṛṣṭo 'ham iha tvayā || 8 ||

VP 3.17.9

देवासुरम् अभूद् युद्धं दिव्यम् अब्दं पुरा द्विज । तस्मिन् पराजिता देवा दैत्यैर् ह्रादपुरोगमैः ॥९॥

Long ago a divine war between the Devas and Asuras lasted a divine year, O dvija; in it the Devas were defeated by the Daityas led by Hrāda.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
देवासुरम् अभूद् युद्धं दिव्यम् अब्दं पुरा (devāsuram abhūd yuddhaṃ divyam abdaṃ purā) — a divine war between Devas and Asuras occurred long ago, a divine year
द्विज तस्मिन् पराजिता देवा दैत्यैर् ह्रादपुरोगमैः (dvija tasmin parājitā devā daityair hrādapurogamaiḥ) — O dvija, in it the Devas were defeated by Daityas led by Hrāda
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

devāsuram abhūd yuddhaṃ divyam abdaṃ purā dvija tasmin parājitā devā daityair hrādapurogamaiḥ || 9 ||

VP 3.17.10

क्षीरोदस्योत्तरं कूलं गत्वातप्यन्त वै तपः । विष्णोर् आराधनार्थाय जगुश् चेमं स्तवं तदा ॥१०॥

They went to the northern shore of the Milk Ocean and practiced austerity for the worship of Viṣṇu; then they sang this hymn.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
क्षीरोदस्योत्तरं कूलं गत्वातप्यन्त वै तपः (kṣīrodasyottaraṃ kūlaṃ gatvātapyanta vai tapaḥ) — the northern shore of the Milk Ocean having gone to, they practiced austerity
विष्णोर् आराधनार्थाय जगुश् चेमं स्तवं तदा (viṣṇor ārādhanārthāya jaguś cemaṃ stavaṃ tadā) — for worship of Viṣṇu, then they sang this hymn
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kṣīrodasyottaraṃ kūlaṃ gatvātapyanta vai tapaḥ viṣṇor ārādhanārthāya jaguś cemaṃ stavaṃ tadā || 10 ||

VP 3.17.11

आराधनाय लोकानां विष्णोर् ईशस्य यां गिरम् । वक्ष्यामो भगवान् आद्यस् तया विष्णुः प्रसीदतु ॥११॥

For the worship of the worlds’ Lord Viṣṇu, may the primordial Bhagavān Viṣṇu be pleased by the speech we shall utter.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
आराधनाय लोकानां विष्णोर् ईशस्य यां गिरम् (ārādhanāya lokānāṃ viṣṇor īśasya yāṃ giram) — for worship of the worlds, the Lord Viṣṇu’s speech which
वक्ष्यामो भगवान् आद्यस् तया विष्णुः प्रसीदतु (vakṣyāmo bhagavān ādyas tayā viṣṇuḥ prasīdatu) — we shall speak; by it may primordial Bhagavān Viṣṇu be pleased
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ārādhanāya lokānāṃ viṣṇor īśasya yāṃ giram vakṣyāmo bhagavān ādyas tayā viṣṇuḥ prasīdatu || 11 ||

VP 3.17.12

यतो भूतान्य् अशेषाणि प्रसूतानि महात्मनः । यस्मिंश् च लयम् एष्यन्ति कस् तं संस्तोतुम् ईश्वरः ॥१२॥

From whom all beings have been born, and into whom they will dissolve, who can praise that Lord?

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
यतो भूतान्य् अशेषाणि प्रसूतानि महात्मनः यस्मिंश् (yato bhūtāny aśeṣāṇi prasūtāni mahātmanaḥ yasmiṃś) — from whom all beings were born, the great-souled one, into whom
च लयम् एष्यन्ति कस् तं संस्तोतुम् ईश्वरः (ca layam eṣyanti kas taṃ saṃstotum īśvaraḥ) — they will dissolve; who can praise that Lord?
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yato bhūtāny aśeṣāṇi prasūtāni mahātmanaḥ yasmiṃś ca layam eṣyanti kas taṃ saṃstotum īśvaraḥ || 12 ||

VP 3.17.13

तथाप्य् अरातिविध्वंसध्वस्तवीर्या भवार्थिनः । त्वां स्तोष्यामस् तवोक्तीनां याथार्थ्यं नैव गोचरे ॥१३॥

Nevertheless, with our strength destroyed by our enemies and seeking well-being, we will praise you, though the truth of your words is beyond reach.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
तथाप्य् अरातिविध्वंसध्वस्तवीर्या भवार्थिनः त्वां (tathāpy arātividhvaṃsadhvastavīryā bhavārthinaḥ tvāṃ) — nevertheless, with strength destroyed by enemies, seeking well-being, you
स्तोष्यामस् तवोक्तीनां याथार्थ्यं नैव गोचरे (stoṣyāmas tavoktīnāṃ yāthārthyaṃ naiva gocare) — we will praise, though the truth of your words is beyond reach
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tathāpy arātividhvaṃsadhvastavīryā bhavārthinaḥ tvāṃ stoṣyāmas tavoktīnāṃ yāthārthyaṃ naiva gocare || 13 ||

VP 3.17.14

त्वम् उर्वी सलिलं वह्निर् वायुर् आकाशम् एव च । समस्तम् अन्तःकरणं प्रधानं तत्परः पुमान् ॥१४॥

You are earth, water, fire, air, and ether; the entire inner organ, Pradhāna, and the Supreme Person.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
त्वम् उर्वी सलिलं वह्निर् वायुर् आकाशम् (tvam urvī salilaṃ vahnir vāyur ākāśam) — you are earth, water, fire, air, ether
एव च समस्तम् अन्तःकरणं प्रधानं तत्परः पुमान् (eva ca samastam antaḥkaraṇaṃ pradhānaṃ tatparaḥ pumān) — and the whole inner organ, Pradhāna, and the Supreme Person
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tvam urvī salilaṃ vahnir vāyur ākāśam eva ca samastam antaḥkaraṇaṃ pradhānaṃ tatparaḥ pumān || 14 ||

VP 3.17.15

एकं तवैतद् भूतात्मन् मूर्तामूर्तमयं वपुः । आब्रह्मस्तम्बपर्यन्तं स्थानकालविभेदवत् ॥१५॥

O Self of beings, this one body of yours, made of the manifest and unmanifest, extends from Brahmā to a blade of grass, differentiated by place and time.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
एकं तवैतद् भूतात्मन् (ekaṃ tavaitad bhūtātman) — one, this body of yours, O Self of beings
मूर्तामूर्तमयं वपुः आब्रह्मस्तम्बपर्यन्तं स्थानकालविभेदवत् (mūrtāmūrtamayaṃ vapuḥ ābrahmastambaparyantaṃ sthānakālavibhedavat) — made of manifest and unmanifest, reaching from Brahmā to a blade of grass, differentiated by place and time
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ekaṃ tavaitad bhūtātman mūrtāmūrtamayaṃ vapuḥ ābrahmastambaparyantaṃ sthānakālavibhedavat || 15 ||

VP 3.17.16

तत्रेश तव यत् पूर्वं त्वन्नाभिकमलोद्भवम् । रूपं सर्गोपकाराय तस्मै ब्रह्मात्मने नमः ॥१६॥

That earlier form of yours, O Lord, arising from the lotus of your navel for the aid of creation—to that Brahman-Self, homage.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
तत्रेश तव यत् पूर्वं त्वन्नाभिकमलोद्भवम् (tatreśa tava yat pūrvaṃ tvannābhikamalodbhavam) — there, O Lord, your former form arising from your navel lotus
रूपं सर्गोपकाराय तस्मै ब्रह्मात्मने नमः (rūpaṃ sargopakārāya tasmai brahmātmane namaḥ) — for the aid of creation; to that Brahman-Self, homage
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tatreśa tava yat pūrvaṃ tvannābhikamalodbhavam rūpaṃ sargopakārāya tasmai brahmātmane namaḥ || 16 ||

VP 3.17.17

शक्रार्करुद्रवस्वश्विमरुत्सोमादिभेदवत् । वयम् एवं स्वरूपं ते तस्मै देवात्मने नमः ॥१७॥

We are this form of yours, distinguished as Indra, the Sun, Rudra, the Vasus, the Aśvins, the Maruts, Soma, and others; homage to that divine Self.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
शक्रार्करुद्रवस्वश्विमरुत्सोमादिभेदवत् वयम् एवं स्वरूपं (śakrārkarudravasvaśvimarutsomādibhedavat vayam evaṃ svarūpaṃ) — Indra, Sun, Rudra, Vasus, Aśvins, Maruts, Soma, etc., we are thus your form
ते तस्मै देवात्मने नमः (te tasmai devātmane namaḥ) — to that divine Self, homage
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

śakrārkarudravasvaśvimarutsomādibhedavat vayam evaṃ svarūpaṃ te tasmai devātmane namaḥ || 17 ||

VP 3.17.18

दम्भप्रायम् असंबोधि तितिक्षादमवर्जितम् । यद् रूपं तव गोविन्द तस्मै दैत्यात्मने नमः ॥१८॥

That form of yours, O Govinda, mostly hypocrisy, without awareness, devoid of forbearance and self-control—to that Daitya-Self, homage.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
दम्भप्रायम् असंबोधि तितिक्षादमवर्जितम् यद् रूपं (dambhaprāyam asaṃbodhi titikṣādamavarjitam yad rūpaṃ) — mostly hypocrisy, without awareness, without patience and self-control, that form
तव गोविन्द तस्मै दैत्यात्मने नमः (tava govinda tasmai daityātmane namaḥ) — of yours, O Govinda, to that Daitya-Self, homage
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dambhaprāyam asaṃbodhi titikṣādamavarjitam yad rūpaṃ tava govinda tasmai daityātmane namaḥ || 18 ||
