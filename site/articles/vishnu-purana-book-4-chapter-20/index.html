अश्वत्थाम-प्रयुक्त-ब्रह्मास्त्रेण (aśvatthāma-prayukta-brahmāstreṇa) — by the Brahmāstra launched by Aśvatthāman
गर्भे एव भस्मीकृतः (garbhe eva bhasmīkṛtaḥ) — burnt in the womb
भगवतः सकल-सुर-असुर-वन्दित-चरण-युगलस्य आत्म-इच्छया कारण-मानुष-रूप-धारिणः अनुभावात् (bhagavataḥ sakala-sura-asura-vandita-caraṇa-yugalasya ātma-icchayā kāraṇa-mānuṣa-rūpa-dhāriṇaḥ anubhāvāt) — by the power of Bhagavān, whose feet are worshipped by all gods and asuras, and who by his own will bears a human form for a purpose
पुनः जीवितम् अवाप्य (punaḥ jīvitam avāpya) — having regained life
परीक्षित् जज्ञे (parīkṣit jajñe) — Parīkṣit was born

He now rules this earth with dharma maintained without interruption.
