VP 2.7.41

तद् ब्रह्म तत् परं धाम सदसत्परमं पदम् । यस्य सर्वम् अभेदेन यतश् चैतच् चराचरम् ॥४१॥

That is Brahman, that supreme abode, the highest state beyond being and nonbeing; all is without distinction in him, and from him is this moving and unmoving [world].
