VP 4.9.1

VP 4.9.8

न वयम् अन्यथा वदिष्यामोऽन्यथा करिष्यामोऽस्माकम् इन्द्रः प्रह्लादस् तद्-अर्थम् एवायम् उद्यम इत्य् उक्त्वा गतेष्व् असुरेषु देवैर् अप्य् असाव् अवनि-पतिर् एवम् एवोक्तस् तेनापि च तथैवोक्ते देवैर् इन्द्रस् त्वं भविष्यसीति सम न्वप्सितम् ॥८॥

“We will not say one thing and do another. Prahlāda is our Indra; for that very purpose this undertaking is made.” When the Asuras had departed after saying this, the gods too spoke in the same way to the king; when he had likewise answered them, the gods agreed, “You will become Indra.”

Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Prahlāda is our Indra; for that very purpose this undertaking is made.” When the Asuras had departed after saying this, the gods too spoke in the same way to the king; when he had likewise answered them, the gods agreed, “You will become Indra.”
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
