VP 2.10.1

त्र्यशीतिमण्डलशतं काष्ठयोर् अन्तरं द्वयोः । आरोहणावरोहाभ्यां भानोर् अब्देन या गतिः ॥१॥

The interval between the two limits is eighty-three hundred maṇḍalas, which is the Sun’s course by ascent and descent through the year.
