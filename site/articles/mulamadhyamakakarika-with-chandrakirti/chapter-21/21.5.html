MMK 21.5

Investigation of Rising and Passing

MMK 21.5

saṃbhavaḥ = arising; vibhavena = with dissolution; eva = indeed; katham = how; saha = together; bhaviṣyati = will be; na = not; janma = birth; maraṇam = death; ca = and; eva = indeed; tulya-kālam = at the same time; hi = indeed; vidyate = exists

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

saṃbhavo vibhavenaiva kathaṃ saha bhaviṣyati | na janmamaraṇaṃ caiva tulyakālaṃ hi vidyate || 5 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

If arising occurred together with passing, birth and death would happen at the same time. Candrakīrti rejects that as a contradiction, like light and darkness occupying the same place at once.

Arising therefore cannot be established either together with or apart from passing.
