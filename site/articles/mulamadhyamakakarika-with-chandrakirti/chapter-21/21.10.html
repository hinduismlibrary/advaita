MMK 21.10

saṃbhavo vibhavaścaiva naika ityupapadyate
saṃbhavo vibhavaścaiva na nānetyupapadyate || 10 ||

Arising and passing away are not reasonable as one; arising and passing away are not reasonable as different.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

saṃbhavo vibhavaścaiva naika ityupapadyate | saṃbhavo vibhavaścaiva na nānetyupapadyate || 10 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

And further, if arising and passing away existed, would they exist as one or as different? Neither way is reasonable, he says:

“Arising and passing away are not reasonable as one; arising and passing away are not reasonable as different.” || 10 ||

Their oneness is not possible, as with mutually opposed light and darkness. Nor are they reasonable as different, because neither deviates from the other. For passing away without arising is not seen, nor is arising without passing away seen. In this way, because the two do not deviate from one another, arising and passing away are not reasonable as different. || 10 ||
