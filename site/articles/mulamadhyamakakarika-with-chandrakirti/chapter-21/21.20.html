MMK 21.20

na cennirudhyamānaśca jāyamānaśca yujyate
sārdhaṃ ca mriyate yeṣu teṣu skandheṣu jāyate || 20 ||

And if neither ceasing nor arising is fitting, then one is born among the aggregates in which one dies.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

na cennirudhyamānaśca jāyamānaśca yujyate | sārdhaṃ ca mriyate yeṣu teṣu skandheṣu jāyate || 20 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

Thus, by the sequence of examination stated,

“And if neither ceasing nor arising is fitting, then one is born among the aggregates in which one dies.” || 20 ||

The particle “and” has the meaning of combination; it indicates “separately as well.” When, by the reasoning stated, the first existence does not arise when the final existence has ceased, nor when the final existence has not ceased, and does not arise together at the same time with the final existence either, then would it be the case that one is born among the very aggregates among which one dies? “One is born among those very aggregates among which one, abiding, dies”—this is utterly contradictory. For it is not seen that one who is dying is born. || 20 ||
