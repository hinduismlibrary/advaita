MMK 21.18

carame na niruddhe ca prathamo yujyate bhavaḥ
carame nāniruddhe ca prathamo yujyate bhavaḥ || 18 ||

The first existence is not fitting when the final existence has ceased; the first existence is not fitting when the final existence has not ceased.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

carame na niruddhe ca prathamo yujyate bhavaḥ | carame nāniruddhe ca prathamo yujyate bhavaḥ || 18 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

Here, this is examined: does the first existence, which is established as having the form of a fruit, arise when the final existence has ceased, when it has not ceased, or when it is ceasing? For there to be the sequence of cause and fruit, none of these is possible when examined in every way, he says:

“The first existence is not fitting when the final existence has ceased; the first existence is not fitting when the final existence has not ceased.” || 18 ||

Here, if it is imagined that the first existence arises when the final existence has ceased, then it would be without a cause. A sprout would arise even from a seed burned by fire. But this is not accepted. Therefore the first existence is not fitting when the final existence has ceased.

Now the first existence is not fitting when the final existence has not ceased either. If it were, it would be without a cause; one living being would have a double nature; an entirely new being would appear; the former would be permanent; and a sprout would arise while the seed was not destroyed. But this too is not accepted. Therefore it is established: “The first existence is not fitting when the final existence has not ceased.” || 18 ||
