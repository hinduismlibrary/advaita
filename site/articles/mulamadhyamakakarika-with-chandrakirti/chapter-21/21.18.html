MMK 21.18

If the end stops, it is unreasonable for there to be a beginning of becoming. When the end does not stop, it is unreasonable for there to be a beginning of becoming.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

carame na niruddhe ca prathamo yujyate bhavaḥ | carame nāniruddhe ca prathamo yujyate bhavaḥ || 18 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

Candrakīrti tests how a first life could arise in relation to a final life. If the final life has already ceased, the first would arise without a cause, like a sprout from a burned seed.

If the final life has not ceased, the new life would arise while the old one remains, implying an unbroken, duplicated being. Neither relation accounts for rebirth as an intrinsically existing sequence.
