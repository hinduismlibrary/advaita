MMK 17.17

sarveṣāṃ viṣabhāgānāṃ sabhāgānāṃ ca karmaṇām
pratisaṃdhau sadhātūnām eka utpadyate tu saḥ || 17 ||

At the linking of rebirth, one arises for all actions, those differing and those alike, for those of the same realm.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

sarveṣāṃ viṣabhāgānāṃ sabhāgānāṃ ca karmaṇām | pratisaṃdhau sadhātūnām eka utpadyate tu saḥ || 17 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

“At the linking of rebirth, one arises for all actions, those differing and those alike, for those of the same realm.”

Actions of different kinds are differing; similar ones are alike. At the linking of rebirth in the desire, form, and formless realms, one non-destruction, which suppresses all actions, arises for all actions, both those alike and those differing. And that too arises only for those of the same realm, those belonging to the same realm, not those belonging to a different realm.
