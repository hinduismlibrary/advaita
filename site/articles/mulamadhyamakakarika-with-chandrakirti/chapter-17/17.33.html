MMK 17.33

kleśāḥ karmāṇi dehāś ca kartāraś ca phalāni ca
gandharvanagarākārā marīcisvapnasaṃnibhāḥ || 33 ||

Afflictions, actions, bodies, agents, and results are like a city of gandharvas, a mirage, or a dream.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

kleśāḥ karmāṇi dehāś ca kartāraś ca phalāni ca | gandharvanagarākārā marīcisvapnasaṃnibhāḥ || 33 ||

Commentary note on Candrakīrti

Afflictions, actions, bodies, agents, and fruits are like a city of celestial musicians, a mirage, or a dream. They appear and function through conditions, while no intrinsic essence is found. This is how karma and its results can be taught without falling into permanence or annihilation.
