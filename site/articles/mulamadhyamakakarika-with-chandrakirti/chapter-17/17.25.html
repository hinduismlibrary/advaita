MMK 17.25

tat-vipakva-vipākam = ripening of what is ripened; ca = and; punaḥ = again; eva = indeed; vipakṣyati = will ripen; karma = action; vyavasthitam = established; yasmāt = because; tasmāt = therefore; svābhāvikam = natural; yadi = if

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

tadvipakvavipākaṃ ca punar eva vipakṣyati | karma vyavasthitaṃ yasmāt tasmāt svābhāvikaṃ yadi || 25 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

If karma remained fixed by its own nature, a fruit that had already ripened could ripen again, since the action would still be unchanged. This absurdity confirms that action does not endure as a self-existing thing. Its causal efficacy is dependent, not permanent.
