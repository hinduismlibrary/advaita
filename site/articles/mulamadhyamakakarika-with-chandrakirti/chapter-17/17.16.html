MMK 17.16

Investigation of Actions and Fruits

MMK 17.16

prahāṇataḥ praheyaḥ syāt karmaṇaḥ saṃkrameṇa vā
yadi doṣāḥ prasajyeran tatra karmavadhādayaḥ || 16 ||

It would be abandoned by abandonment or by the transition of action; if so, faults such as the destruction of action would follow.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

prahāṇataḥ praheyaḥ syāt karmaṇaḥ saṃkrameṇa vā | yadi doṣāḥ prasajyeran tatra karmavadhādayaḥ || 16 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

If, again, this non-destruction of action were to be abandoned by abandonment, through abandonment, or were to be destroyed through the transition of action, through the destruction of one action and its becoming manifest as another action, what fault would there be? The response:

“It would be abandoned by abandonment or by the transition of action; if so, faults such as the destruction of action would follow.”

If non-destruction were abandoned by the path of seeing, like the actions of ordinary persons, then there would be only destruction of action. And because of the destruction of action, the ripening of desired and undesired fruits of action for noble ones, with former action as their cause, would not exist. Fruit would arise from action that was not done. And from the view that action and fruit do not exist, a wrong view would follow. Thus the faults beginning with destruction of action follow, if it is accepted that non-destruction is to be abandoned by abandonment. It is to be applied in the same way to the transition of action.
