MMK 17.11

dharmasya sādhanopāyāḥ śuklāḥ karmapathā daśa
phalaṃ kāmaguṇāḥ pañca dharmasya pretya ceha ca || 11 ||

The ten wholesome paths of action are means to Dharma; the five strands of sensual pleasure are its fruit, both in future lives and in this one.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

dharmasya sādhanopāyāḥ śuklāḥ karmapathā daśa | phalaṃ kāmaguṇāḥ pañca dharmasya pretya ceha ca || 11 ||

Commentary note on Candrakīrti

The ten wholesome paths of action are taught as means for practicing dharma, with the five kinds of sense pleasure as their results in this life and the next. Candrakīrti explains that bodily and verbal conduct, along with mental qualities, support the cultivation of restraint, kindness, and benefit to others.
