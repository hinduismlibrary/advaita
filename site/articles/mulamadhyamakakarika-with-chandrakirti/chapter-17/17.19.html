MMK 17.19

phala-vyatikramāt = from passing beyond the fruit; vā = or; saḥ = it; maraṇāt = at death; vā = or; nirudhyate = ceases; anāsravam = without outflows; sāsravam = with outflows; ca = and; vibhāgam = division; tatra = there; lakṣayet = one should mark

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

phalavyatikramād vā sa maraṇād vā nirudhyate | anāsravaṃ sāsravaṃ ca vibhāgaṃ tatra lakṣayet || 19 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

The factor ceases when its result has been fully passed through, or at death when a new rebirth link forms. One should distinguish the contaminated kind, connected with afflictions, from the uncontaminated kind, which is associated with the path beyond them.
