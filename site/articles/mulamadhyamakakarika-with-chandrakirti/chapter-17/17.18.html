MMK 17.18

karmaṇaḥ karmaṇo dṛṣṭe dharma utpadyate tu saḥ
dviprakārasya sarvasya vipakke 'pi ca tiṣṭhati || 18 ||

For each action that dharma arises here in this life; it remains even when ripened, for all of the two kinds.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

karmaṇaḥ karmaṇo dṛṣṭe dharma utpadyate tu saḥ | dviprakārasya sarvasya vipakke 'pi ca tiṣṭhati || 18 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

“For each action that dharma arises here in this life; it remains even when ripened, for all of the two kinds.”

This dharma called “non-destruction” arises here in this very life, one for each action, for every action of the nature of volition and of what is done after intending, and also for every action divided into two kinds as contaminated and uncontaminated. And this non-destruction does not necessarily cease even when there is ripening. Although it exists, like a note that has been discharged, it is not able to ripen again.
