MMK 17.4

Whatever (1) speech and (2) movements and (3) "unconscious not-letting-go," (4) other kinds of unconscious letting-go are also regarded like that.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

vāgviṣpando 'viratayo yāś cāvijñaptisaṃjñitāḥ | avijñaptaya evānyāḥ smṛtā viratayas tathā || 4 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

The chapter further distinguishes spoken words, bodily movements, and forms of restraint or non-restraint that are not directly communicated to others. These include patterns established by undertaking harmful conduct or by vows to refrain from it, which continue to shape later behavior even when no visible action is occurring.
