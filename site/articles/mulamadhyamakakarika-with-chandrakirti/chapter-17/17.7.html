MMK 17.7

yo 'ṅkuraprabhṛtir bījāt saṃtāno 'bhipravartate
tataḥ phalam ṛte bījāt sa ca nābhipravartate || 7 ||

A continuum beginning with the sprout proceeds from the seed; apart from the seed, that continuum and its fruit do not arise.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

yo 'ṅkuraprabhṛtir bījāt saṃtāno 'bhipravartate | tataḥ phalam ṛte bījāt sa ca nābhipravartate || 7 ||

Commentary note on Candrakīrti

A seed gives rise to a series of moments—sprout, stalk, leaves, and so on—and from that series a fruit develops. Although the seed itself ceases, it initiates a continuum with the capacity to produce its characteristic result. Without the seed, that continuum would not occur; conditions shape the result as the series unfolds.
