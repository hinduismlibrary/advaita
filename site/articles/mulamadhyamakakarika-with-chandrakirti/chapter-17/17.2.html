MMK 17.2

The great sage has taught all actions to be intention and what is intended. The specifics of those actions are well known to be of many kinds.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

cetanā cetayitvā ca karmoktaṃ paramarṣiṇā | tasyānekavidho bhedaḥ karmaṇaḥ parikīrtitaḥ || 2 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

The Buddha classifies action as intention and what is intended. Candrakīrti explains that “intention” is mental karma, while what one does after intending is bodily or verbal karma. This is the starting point for distinguishing the forms of action without treating them as permanent substances.
