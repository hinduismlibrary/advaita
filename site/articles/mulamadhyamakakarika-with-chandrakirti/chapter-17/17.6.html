MMK 17.6

tiṣṭhati = remains; ā = until; pāka-kālāt = the time of ripening; cet = if; karma = action; tat = that; nityatām = permanence; iyāt = would reach; niruddham = ceased; cet = if; niruddham = ceased; sat = being; kim = what; phalam = fruit; janayiṣyati = will produce

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

tiṣṭhaty ā pākakālāc cet karma tannityatām iyāt | niruddhaṃ cen niruddhaṃ sat kiṃ phalaṃ janayiṣyati || 6 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

An opponent asks how a momentary action can produce a later result. If the action remains until its fruit ripens, it would be permanent. If it ceases immediately, how can something that no longer exists produce a fruit? Candrakīrti introduces a dependent account in which causal continuity is maintained without making the action eternal.
