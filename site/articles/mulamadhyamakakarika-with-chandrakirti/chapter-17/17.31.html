MMK 17.31

yathā = as; nirmitakam = a created form; śāstā = teacher; nirmimīta = creates; ṛddhi-sampadā = by power of marvel; nirmitāḥ = created; nirmimīta = creates; anyam = another; saḥ = that; ca = and; nirmitakaḥ = created form; punaḥ = again

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

yathā nirmitakaṃ śāstā nirmimītarddhisaṃpadā | nirmito nirmimītānyaṃ sa ca nirmitakaḥ punaḥ || 31 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

A teacher can create an illusory figure through miraculous power, and that created figure can in turn create another. The example shows how an apparent agent and result can function without an intrinsic nature. Conventional causal activity does not require an independently existing creator.
