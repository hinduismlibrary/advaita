MMK 7.29

yadaivaṃ sarvadharmāṇāmutpādo nopapadyate
tadaivaṃ sarvadharmāṇāṃ nirodho nopapadyate || 29 ||

Just as the arising of all dharmas is not possible, so the cessation of all dharmas is not possible.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

yadaivaṃ sarvadharmāṇāmutpādo nopapadyate | tadaivaṃ sarvadharmāṇāṃ nirodho nopapadyate || 29 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

Someone says: “Although one state does not cease by that very state, nor does one state cease by another state, nevertheless the milk-state has cessation; therefore there could also be arising.” Reply: “Alas, you display your extreme dullness! Surely, by the reasoning stated earlier, when it was said:

‘Just as the arising of all dharmas is not possible,’

then, in the same way, it has been stated even more clearly that:

‘So the cessation of all dharmas is not possible.’”

This is the meaning.
