MMK 7.29

yadaivaṃ sarvadharmāṇāmutpādo nopapadyate
tadaivaṃ sarvadharmāṇāṃ nirodho nopapadyate || 29 ||

When the arising of all phenomena is not possible in this way, their cessation is not possible either.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

yadaivaṃ sarvadharmāṇāmutpādo nopapadyate | tadaivaṃ sarvadharmāṇāṃ nirodho nopapadyate || 29 ||

Commentary note on Candrakīrti

Since arising of all phenomena has already been shown to be impossible, their cessation is impossible by the same reasoning. The two claims stand or fall together in the analysis.
