MMK 7.22

na sthitabhāvastiṣṭhatyasthitabhāvo na tiṣṭhati
na tiṣṭhati tiṣṭhamānaḥ ko 'nutpannaśca tiṣṭhati || 22 ||

An existing thing does not remain; a nonexistent thing does not remain. What thing remains while remaining, and what unborn thing remains?

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

na sthitabhāvastiṣṭhatyasthitabhāvo na tiṣṭhati | na tiṣṭhati tiṣṭhamānaḥ ko 'nutpannaśca tiṣṭhati || 22 ||

Commentary note on Candrakīrti

What has already remained does not remain, since its act of remaining has ended; what has not remained does not remain, since it lacks that activity. What is “remaining” cannot be a third state distinct from these two; otherwise there would be two acts of remaining. And since arising has been refuted, what unarisen thing could remain? Abiding is not established in any of these cases.
