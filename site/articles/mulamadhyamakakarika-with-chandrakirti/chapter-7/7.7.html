MMK 7.7

ayamutpadyamānaste kāmamutpādayedimam
yadīmamutpādayitumajātaḥ śaknuyādayam || 7 ||

This arising, while arising, might indeed produce this one for you, if this one, unborn, were able to produce it.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

ayamutpadyamānaste kāmamutpādayedimam | yadīmamutpādayitumajātaḥ śaknuyādayam || 7 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

Someone says: “The root arising itself, while arising, produces the arising-of-arising; that very arising-of-arising will produce the root arising.” Reply:

“This arising, while arising, might indeed produce this one for you, if this one, unborn, were able to produce it.”

The root arising, while arising, might indeed produce the arising, if this very one, unborn, were able to produce another unborn thing. For what is called ‘while arising’ is future; and how will that, unborn, produce? This is certainly not reasonable, is the meaning. The same is to be said about the arising-of-arising.
