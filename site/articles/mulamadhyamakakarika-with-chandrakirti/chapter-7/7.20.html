MMK 7.20

sataḥ = of the existent; ca = and; tāvat = first; utpattiḥ = arising; asataḥ = of the non-existent; ca = and; na yujyate = is not fitting; na = not; sataḥ = of the existent; ca = and; asataḥ = of the non-existent; ca = and; iti = thus; pūrvam = before; eva = indeed; upapāditam = explained

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

sataśca tāvadutpattirasataśca na yujyate | na sataścāsataśceti pūrvamevopapāditam || 20 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

The production of an existent thing, a nonexistent thing, or something both existent and nonexistent has already been refuted. Nāgārjuna established earlier that a thing is not produced from itself, from something else, from both, or without a cause. So neither the thing said to be arising nor arising itself can be established by this proposal.
