MMK 23.6

svabhāvato na vidyante śubhāśubhaviparyayāḥ
pratītya katamān kleśāḥ śubhāśubhaviparyayān || 6 ||

The good, the bad, and mistaken views do not exist by own-nature. Which afflictions depend on the good, the bad, and mistaken views?

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

svabhāvato na vidyante śubhāśubhaviparyayāḥ | pratītya katamān kleśāḥ śubhāśubhaviparyayān || 6 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

Here an opponent says: “Although you have refuted the afflictions, the good, the bad, and mistaken views that are their causes at least exist. Since they exist, the afflictions exist.” We reply: the afflictions would exist if the good, the bad, and mistaken views existed. But these too have arisen in dependence and will be refuted below. And when they do not exist by own-nature, then:

“The good, the bad, and mistaken views do not exist by own-nature. Which afflictions depend on the good, the bad, and mistaken views?”

The meaning is: the afflictions do not exist, because the good, the bad, and mistaken views that would be their causes do not exist.
