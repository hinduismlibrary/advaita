MMK 23.19

anutpannāḥ = unarisen; katham = how; nāma = indeed; bhaviṣyanti = will be; viparyayāḥ = inversions; viparyayeṣu = when inversions; ajāteṣu = are unborn; viparyaya-gataḥ = gone into inversion; kutaḥ = whence

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

anutpannāḥ kathaṃ nāma bhaviṣyanti viparyayāḥ | viparyayeṣv ajāteṣu viparyayagataḥ kutaḥ || 19 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

If the inversions have not arisen by their own nature, how could there be an intrinsically deluded person who undergoes them? The verse challenges the claim that the presence of an afflicted person proves that afflictions exist inherently.

The conclusion follows from the absence of an intrinsic arising: a deluded person cannot be independently grounded in afflictions that are themselves unestablished.
