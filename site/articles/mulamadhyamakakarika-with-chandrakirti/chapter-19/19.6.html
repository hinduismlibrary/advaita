MMK 19.6

If time depended on things, where would time which is a non-thing exist? If there were no things at all, where would a view of time exist?

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

bhāvaṃ pratītya kālaścetkālo bhāvādṛte kutaḥ | na ca kaścana bhāvo 'sti kutaḥ kālo bhaviṣyati || 6 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

The opponent now says that time is designated in dependence on things. Candrakīrti replies: if time depends on things, where could it be without them? And since things themselves are not established with their own nature, what could support an inherently real time?

Without independently existing things there is no independent time or fixed sequence of moments. Candrakīrti’s conclusion is that both are dependently designated; the analysis rejects their intrinsic existence, not their ordinary use.
