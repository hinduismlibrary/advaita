MMK 3.2

svamātmānaṃ darśanaṃ hi tattameva na paśyati
na paśyati yadātmānaṃ kathaṃ drakṣyati tatparān || 2 ||

Seeing does not see itself. How could it see something else, when it does not see itself?

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

svamātmānaṃ darśanaṃ hi tattameva na paśyati | na paśyati yadātmānaṃ kathaṃ drakṣyati tatparān || 2 ||

Commentary note on Candrakīrti

Seeing cannot see itself: an action cannot operate upon itself in the same respect. Since seeing does not see itself, Candrakīrti argues, it does not see blue or other forms either—just as hearing does not hear itself. Seeing is therefore not established as an independently existing faculty.
