MMK 6.3

sahaiva punarudbhūtirna yuktā rāgaraktayoḥ
bhavetāṃ rāgaraktau hi nirapekṣau parasparam || 3 ||

The arising of passion and the passionate person together is not reasonable; for passion and the passionate person would be independent of one another.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

sahaiva punarudbhūtirna yuktā rāgaraktayoḥ | bhavetāṃ rāgaraktau hi nirapekṣau parasparam || 3 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

Someone says: “Passion and the passionate person do not come about in sequence, which is why this refutation would apply. Rather, passion and the passionate person arise together. For the mind is colored by passion that coexists with the mind, and it is passionate. Therefore passion and the passionate person certainly exist.” Reply: Even so:

“The arising of passion and the passionate person together is not reasonable, because passion and the passionate person would be independent of one another,”

through their coexistence, like the left and right horns of a cow. This is the meaning.
