MMK 6.9

pṛthagbhāvāprasiddheśca sahabhāvo na sidhyati
katamasmin pṛthagbhāve sahabhāvaṃ satīcchasi || 9 ||

Because separate existence is not established, coexistence is not established. Which separate existence could be present for you to claim that they coexist?

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

pṛthagbhāvāprasiddheśca sahabhāvo na sidhyati | katamasmin pṛthagbhāve sahabhāvaṃ satīcchasi || 9 ||

Commentary note on Candrakīrti

Since their separate existence is not established, co-existence is not established either. If you insist that the two are separate, in what independently established difference do you want them to co-exist? No such difference is found.
