MMK 6.8

pṛthaṅ na sidhyatītyevaṃ sahabhāvaṃ vikāṅkṣasi
sahabhāvaprasiddhyartha pṛthaktvaṃ bhūya icchasi || 8 ||

Thinking they are not established separately, if you desire their coexistence, you again desire separateness for establishing their coexistence.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

pṛthaṅ na sidhyatītyevaṃ sahabhāvaṃ vikāṅkṣasi | sahabhāvaprasiddhyartha pṛthaktvaṃ bhūya icchasi || 8 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

But:

“If, thinking ‘they are not established separately,’ you desire their coexistence, you again desire separateness for the establishment of their coexistence.”

Because you think that the separate establishment of passion and the passionate person does not exist, if you desire their coexistence, and that does not exist for things not established separately,

“you again desire separateness for the establishment of their coexistence.”

In that case, since establishment rests on mutual dependence, when one is established, of what is the establishment of what to be?
