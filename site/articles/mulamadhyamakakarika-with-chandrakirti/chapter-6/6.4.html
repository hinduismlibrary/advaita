MMK 6.4

naikatve sahabhāvo 'sti na tenaiva hi tatsaha
pṛthaktve sahabhāvo 'tha kuta eva bhaviṣyati || 4 ||

There is no coexistence in oneness, for it is not together with itself. And how could there be coexistence in separateness?

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

naikatve sahabhāvo 'sti na tenaiva hi tatsaha | pṛthaktve sahabhāvo 'tha kuta eva bhaviṣyati || 4 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

Further, is the coexistence of these two, passion and the passionate person, posited in oneness or in separateness? If in oneness, this is not reasonable, because:

“There is no coexistence in oneness.”

Why is there not? He says:

“For it is not together with itself.”

For the nature of passion, being not different from passion, is not described as being together with passion.

Now he states that there is no coexistence in separateness either:

“And how could there be coexistence in separateness?”

For coexistence is not seen between things that are separate, such as light and darkness, or saṃsāra and nirvāṇa.
