MMK 6.6

Investigation of Desire and the Desirous One

MMK 6.6

pṛthaktve = in difference; sahabhāvaḥ = coexistence; ca = and; yadi = if; kim = what; rāga-raktayoḥ = of desire and desirous one; siddhaḥ = established; pṛthak-pṛthak-bhāvaḥ = separate existence; sahabhāvaḥ = coexistence; yataḥ = from which; tayoḥ = of those two

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

pṛthaktve sahabhāvaśca yadi kiṃ rāgaraktayoḥ | siddhaḥ pṛthakpṛthagbhāvaḥ sahabhāvo yatastayoḥ || 6 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

If desire and the desirous person are said to be different, their difference must already be established before their co-existence can be asserted. Co-existence is observed between separately established things, such as a cow and a horse; but desire and the desirous person are not established independently in that way. Their supposed difference therefore cannot establish their co-existence.
