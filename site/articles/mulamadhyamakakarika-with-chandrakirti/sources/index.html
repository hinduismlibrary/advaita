Text sources

Nāgārjuna’s verses

All 448 root verses are presented in Sanskrit across the 27 chapters. The English translations for Chapters 2–27 were prepared directly from the GRETIL Sanskrit text; Chapter 1 retains its adapted English verse translations from Stephen Batchelor’s Verses from the Center (1994), under CC BY-NC-SA 3.0. No word-by-word glosses are included. The root text is checked against GRETIL’s standalone Sanskrit transcription (input by Douglas Bachman; revised by Richard Mahoney). That file is marked for reference purposes, with usage terms governed by the source file; consult it for reuse terms.

Candrakīrti’s Prasannapadā

Chapter 1 is presented as one page per root verse, plus a separate introduction page. Each verse page includes the root verse, an English translation, and a full independent translation of Candrakīrti’s commentary. For Chapters 2–27, each verse page includes the root verse and a concise English reading note on Candrakīrti’s commentary. These notes are explanatory summaries, not full translations of his Sanskrit prose. The verse sidebar highlights the current page. Read Candrakīrti’s introduction.

For comparison, an independently published full English translation (open-access, copyrighted): Anne MacDonald, In Clear Words: The Prasannapadā, Chapter One, Vol. II (2015). MacDonald’s two-volume edition presents a critical Sanskrit text and annotated English translation; the published translation is linked for reference, not reproduced. Primary Sanskrit commentary text: GRETIL, Candrakīrti’s Prasannapadā, based on P. L. Vaidya’s 1960 edition (electronic transcription distributed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). The volumes state that they underwent anonymous international peer review. For bibliographic details, see Anne MacDonald, In Clear Words: The Prasannapadā, Chapter One (2015).

Further English reference: Mervyn Sprung, Lucid Exposition of the Middle Way: The Essential Chapters from the Prasannapadā of Candrakīrti (1979), translated with T. R. V. Murti and U. S. Vyas. This is a copyrighted, selectively translated volume; it is a reference for comparison rather than the text reproduced on this site.

The root verses and English translations were checked against GRETIL’s standalone Mūlamadhyamakakārikā transcription (input by Douglas Bachman; revised by Richard Mahoney) and Candrakīrti’s Prasannapadā. The site’s translations and reading notes have not undergone external peer review.
