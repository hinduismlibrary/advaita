Text sources

Nāgārjuna’s verses

The English root verses are adapted from Stephen Batchelor’s translation in Verses from the Center (1994), under CC BY-NC-SA 3.0. Selected wording has been revised for terminological accuracy and clarity; MMK 2.24 is translated from the GRETIL Sanskrit to restore its full three-part form.

Candrakīrti’s original Sanskrit and English translations

Chapter 1 is presented as one continuous reader: Candrakīrti’s introduction, all fourteen root verses, and the complete translated commentary in order. The on-page contents follow the reader as it scrolls and keep one section highlighted at a time. Original Sanskrit is available in collapsed panels. Chapters 2–21 include translated commentary passages for every root verse; Chapters 22–27 still need translation from the Sanskrit. The GRETIL Sanskrit transcription is distributed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; the site’s independent English translation of Candrakīrti’s introduction and commentary is shared under the same license. It is not the published translation of Anne MacDonald or Mervyn Sprung.

For comparison, an independently published full English translation (open-access, copyrighted): Anne MacDonald, In Clear Words: The Prasannapadā, Chapter One, Vol. II (2015). MacDonald’s two-volume edition presents a critical Sanskrit text and annotated English translation; the published translation is linked for reference, not reproduced. Primary Sanskrit text: GRETIL, Candrakīrti’s Prasannapadā, based on P. L. Vaidya’s 1960 edition (electronic transcription distributed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). The volumes state that they underwent anonymous international peer review. For bibliographic details, see Anne MacDonald, In Clear Words: The Prasannapadā, Chapter One (2015).

Further English reference: Mervyn Sprung, Lucid Exposition of the Middle Way: The Essential Chapters from the Prasannapadā of Candrakīrti (1979), translated with T. R. V. Murti and U. S. Vyas. This is a copyrighted, selectively translated volume; it is a reference for comparison rather than the text reproduced on this site.
