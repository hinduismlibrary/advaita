MMK 4.4

rūpe satyeva rūpasya kāraṇaṃ nopapadyate
rūpe 'satyeva rūpasya kāraṇaṃ nopapadyate || 4 ||

While form exists, a cause of form is not possible; while form does not exist, a cause of form is not possible.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

rūpe satyeva rūpasya kāraṇaṃ nopapadyate | rūpe 'satyeva rūpasya kāraṇaṃ nopapadyate || 4 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

Now, is this cause of form, which is accepted, accepted as a cause when form exists or when it does not exist? Neither way is reasonable, he says:

“While form exists, a cause of form is not possible; while form does not exist, a cause of form is not possible.”

If form exists, what use is there for a cause of form? If form does not exist, what use is there for a cause of form, or of what is it posited as the cause? Therefore, even when form does not exist, a cause of form is not possible.
