MMK 26.4

cakṣuḥ pratītya rūpaṃ ca samanvāhāram eva ca
nāmarūpaṃ pratītyaivaṃ vijñānaṃ saṃpravartate || 4 ||

Depending on the eye, forms, and attention, consciousness proceeds; in this way it also proceeds in dependence on name-and-form.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

cakṣuḥ pratītya rūpaṃ ca samanvāhāram eva ca | nāmarūpaṃ pratītyaivaṃ vijñānaṃ saṃpravartate || 4 ||

Commentary note on Candrakīrti

Visual consciousness arises in dependence on the eye, visible form, and attention; the other kinds of consciousness arise in comparable ways. Their coming together is called contact.

The verse highlights that consciousness depends on a sense faculty, an object, and attention. Contact is not an independent event but the meeting of these conditions.
