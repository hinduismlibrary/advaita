MMK 26.12

tasya tasya nirodhena tat tan nābhipravartate
duḥkhaskandhaḥ kevalo 'yam evaṃ samyag nirudhyate || 12 ||

With the cessation of each factor, the next does not proceed. In this way this entire mass of suffering is rightly brought to cessation.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

tasya tasya nirodhena tat tan nābhipravartate | duḥkhaskandhaḥ kevalo 'yam evaṃ samyag nirudhyate || 12 ||

Commentary note on Candrakīrti

When each link ceases, the next link does not proceed. In this way the whole mass of suffering is completely brought to cessation.

The chain is reversed link by link: with ignorance ended, formations cease, and the later conditions do not follow. The chapter presents dependent arising as the way both suffering and its cessation work.
