MMK 26.11

When ignorance stops, formative impulses too do not occur. The stopping of ignorance [comes] through practising that with understanding.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

avidyāyāṃ niruddhāyāṃ saṃskārāṇām asaṃbhavaḥ | avidyāyā nirodhas tu jñānasyāsyaiva bhāvanāt || 11 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

When ignorance ceases, formations do not arise. Ignorance is ended through cultivating knowledge of this very dependent arising.

Candrakīrti explains that one who sees the twelve links accurately sees their lack of intrinsic nature. Without something to grasp as real, confusion and the karma it drives are brought to an end.
