MMK 12.9

syād ubhābhyāṃ kṛtaṃ duḥkhaṃ syād ekaikakṛtaṃ yadi
parākārāsvayaṃkāraṃ duḥkham ahetukaṃ kutaḥ || 9 ||

If suffering were made by both, it would follow that it was made by each separately. How could suffering be made by another, by oneself, or without a cause?

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

syād ubhābhyāṃ kṛtaṃ duḥkhaṃ syād ekaikakṛtaṃ yadi | parākārāsvayaṃkāraṃ duḥkham ahetukaṃ kutaḥ || 9 ||

Commentary note on Candrakīrti

Suffering could be made by both only if each of the two had made it individually. But neither self-production nor production by another has been established. We do not ordinarily call an act jointly done merely because each person separately performs an act. Thus the combined account fails along with its two parts.
