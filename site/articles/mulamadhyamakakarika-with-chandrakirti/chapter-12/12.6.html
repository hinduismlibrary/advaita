MMK 12.6

If anguish arose [from] another person, who would that other person be who, having made it, gives it to someone else, but is not included in the anguish?

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

parapudgalajaṃ duḥkhaṃ yadi kaḥ parapudgalaḥ | vinā duḥkhena yaḥ kṛtvā parasmai prahiṇoti tat || 6 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

Nor is there an established giver. What person, apart from the aggregates by which a human is designated, could make divine suffering and send it to a divine person? Since no such independent maker can be found, suffering is not established as made by another.
