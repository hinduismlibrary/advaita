MMK 12.7

svayaṃkṛtasyāprasiddher duḥkhaṃ parakṛtaṃ kutaḥ
paro hi duḥkhaṃ yat kuryāt tat tasya syāt svayaṃkṛtam || 7 ||

If suffering made by oneself is unestablished, how could suffering be made by another? For whatever suffering another makes would be made by oneself for that one.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

svayaṃkṛtasyāprasiddher duḥkhaṃ parakṛtaṃ kutaḥ | paro hi duḥkhaṃ yat kuryāt tat tasya syāt svayaṃkṛtam || 7 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

And therefore:

If the suffering of the god-person is made by the human person, then it would be made by another because it was made by the human person. Is it not then made by itself for the human person? And this has been said not to exist. Therefore, when that suffering is not made by the human person himself, where could that suffering of the god-person, called other, be made by another? Therefore suffering made by another also does not occur.
