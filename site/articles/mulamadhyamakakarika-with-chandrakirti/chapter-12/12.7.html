MMK 12.7

svayam-kṛtasya = of what is made by oneself; aprasiddheḥ = because of non-establishment; duḥkham = suffering; para-kṛtam = made by another; kutaḥ = from where; paraḥ = another; hi = indeed; duḥkham = suffering; yat = which; kuryāt = would make; tat = that; tasya = for him; syāt = would be; svayam-kṛtam = made by himself

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

svayaṃkṛtasyāprasiddher duḥkhaṃ parakṛtaṃ kutaḥ | paro hi duḥkhaṃ yat kuryāt tat tasya syāt svayaṃkṛtam || 7 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

If a human person makes the suffering later experienced by a divine person, then that suffering would have to be self-made for the human person. But self-made suffering has already been shown to be impossible. Since the supposed maker did not make it for himself, there is no basis for calling it made by another.
