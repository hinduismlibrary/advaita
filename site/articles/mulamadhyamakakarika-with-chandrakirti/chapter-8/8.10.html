MMK 8.10

nāsadbhūto 'pi sadbhūtaṃ sadasadbhūtameva vā
karoti kārakaḥ karma pūrvoktaireva hetubhiḥ || 10 ||

Nor does a nonexistent actor perform an existent or both existent and nonexistent action, by the reasons stated earlier.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

nāsadbhūto 'pi sadbhūtaṃ sadasadbhūtameva vā | karoti kārakaḥ karma pūrvoktaireva hetubhiḥ || 10 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

Now he says that a nonexistent actor too does not perform an existent action or an action that is both existent and nonexistent:

“Nor does a nonexistent actor perform an existent or both existent and nonexistent action, by the reasons stated earlier.”

A nonexistent actor would be without a cause, because of the fault stated with “when there is no cause, neither effect nor cause exists.” Because of the consequence “For an existing action there is no activity, and the agent would be without an action,” an existent action is not performed. Nor is an action that is both existent and nonexistent performed, because:

“For how could what is mutually opposed, existent and nonexistent, be one?”
