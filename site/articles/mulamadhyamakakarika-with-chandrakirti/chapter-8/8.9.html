MMK 8.9

Investigation of Act and Actor

MMK 8.9

na asadbhūtam = not non-existent; na = not; sadbhūtaḥ = existent; sat-asadbhūtam = existent-and-non-existent; eva = indeed; vā = or; karoti = does; kārakaḥ = agent; karma = action; pūrvoktaiḥ = by previously-stated; eva = indeed; hetubhiḥ = reasons

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

nāsadbhūtaṃ na sadbhūtaḥ sadasadbhūtameva vā | karoti kārakaḥ karma pūrvoktaireva hetubhiḥ || 9 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

An existent actor cannot perform a nonexistent action, nor an action said to be both existent and nonexistent. The former has no activity or cause; the latter combines opposed natures. The earlier arguments apply directly.
