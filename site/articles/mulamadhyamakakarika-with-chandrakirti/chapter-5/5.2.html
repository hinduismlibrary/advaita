MMK 5.2

alakṣaṇo na kaścicca bhāvaḥ saṃvidyate kvacit
asatyalakṣaṇe bhāve kramatāṃ kuha lakṣaṇam || 2 ||

No entity without a characteristic is found anywhere. When an entity without a characteristic does not exist, where could the characteristic proceed?

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

alakṣaṇo na kaścicca bhāvaḥ saṃvidyate kvacit | asatyalakṣaṇe bhāve kramatāṃ kuha lakṣaṇam || 2 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

“Let the uncharacterized thing be there; let the characteristic operate. In the absence of that, space does not exist, like a sky-flower,” he says:

“No entity without a characteristic is found anywhere.”

Someone says: “The characteristic would operate on the characterized thing; because that exists, the characterized thing too exists.” This too is not so, because:

“When an entity without a characteristic does not exist, where could the characteristic proceed?”

It has been said that an uncharacterized entity does not exist before the characteristic. Therefore, when an entity without a characteristic, not existing, is absent, where now could the characteristic operate? There is no operation of a characteristic.
