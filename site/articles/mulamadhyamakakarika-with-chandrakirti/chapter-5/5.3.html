MMK 5.3

nālakṣaṇe lakṣaṇasya pravṛttirna salakṣaṇe
salakṣaṇālakṣaṇābhyāṃ nāpyanyatra pravartate || 3 ||

A characteristic does not operate on what is without a characteristic, nor on what has a characteristic; it does not operate elsewhere than on what has a characteristic and what is without a characteristic.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

nālakṣaṇe lakṣaṇasya pravṛttirna salakṣaṇe | salakṣaṇālakṣaṇābhyāṃ nāpyanyatra pravartate || 3 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

Further, would this characteristic, as it operates, operate on something that has a characteristic or on something without a characteristic? Neither way is reasonable, he says:

“A characteristic does not operate on what is without a characteristic, nor on what has a characteristic; it does not operate elsewhere than on what has a characteristic and what is without a characteristic.”

Here, a characteristic does not operate on what is without a characteristic, like a donkey’s horn. Nor is the operation of a characteristic on an entity that has a characteristic reasonable, because there is no purpose. For what purpose would there be for a characteristic again with regard to an entity already established as possessing a characteristic? In that case there would be an infinite regress and excessive consequence, since it could never be without a characteristic; therefore the operation of a characteristic would always follow. But this is not desired. Therefore the operation of a characteristic on an entity that has a characteristic is also not reasonable, because there is no purpose. Someone might say: “Then it will operate somewhere other than what has a characteristic and what is without a characteristic.” Reply:

“It does not operate elsewhere than on what has a characteristic and what is without a characteristic.”

For what reason? Because such a thing does not exist. If something has a characteristic, it is not without a characteristic; if it is without a characteristic, it does not have a characteristic. Therefore, that it both has a characteristic and is without a characteristic is contradictory. And what is contradictory cannot occur. Therefore, because it is impossible, the operation of a characteristic on what has a characteristic and what is without a characteristic is not possible.
