MMK 5.7

tasmānna bhāvo nābhāvo na lakṣyaṃ nāpi lakṣaṇam
ākāśasamā dhātavaḥ pañca ye pare || 7 ||

Therefore there is neither existence nor nonexistence, neither characterized thing nor characteristic; the five other elements are like space.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

tasmānna bhāvo nābhāvo na lakṣyaṃ nāpi lakṣaṇam | ākāśasamā dhātavaḥ pañca ye pare || 7 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

Now, summarizing the meaning that has been explained, he says:

“Therefore there is neither entity nor non-entity, neither characterized thing nor characteristic; the five other elements are like space.”

And just as space is, so the five other elements that remain, named earth and the rest, are also to be understood as devoid of the nature of the conceptions of entity and non-entity, characterized thing and characteristic, just as space is. This is the meaning.
