MMK 10.5

anyo na prāpsyate 'prāpto na dhakṣyatyadahan punaḥ
na nirvāsyatyanirvāṇaḥ sthāsyate vā svaliṅgavān || 5 ||

If fire were something other than fuel, it would not reach the fuel; not reaching it, it would not burn. Not burning, it would not be extinguished, but would remain with its own defining mark.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

anyo na prāpsyate 'prāpto na dhakṣyatyadahan punaḥ | na nirvāsyatyanirvāṇaḥ sthāsyate vā svaliṅgavān || 5 ||

Commentary note on Candrakīrti

If fire were different from fuel, it would not reach the fuel. What it does not reach cannot be burned; what is not burned cannot be extinguished; and what is not extinguished would remain with its own defining character. The proposed separate fire would not be the fire that appears and goes out in dependence on fuel.
