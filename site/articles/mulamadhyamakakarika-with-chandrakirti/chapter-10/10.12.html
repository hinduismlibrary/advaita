MMK 10.12

apekṣyendhanamagnirna nānapekṣyāgnirindhanam
apekṣyendhanamagniṃ na nānapekṣyāgnimindhanam || 12 ||

Fire is not established in dependence on fuel, nor is fuel established without dependence on fire. Fire is not established without fuel, nor fuel without fire.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

apekṣyendhanamagnirna nānapekṣyāgnirindhanam | apekṣyendhanamagniṃ na nānapekṣyāgnimindhanam || 12 ||

Commentary note on Candrakīrti

Fire does not exist in dependence on fuel, but neither does it exist without dependence on fuel. Likewise, fuel exists neither in dependence on fire nor independently of fire. The alternatives of dependent and independent existence have both failed.
