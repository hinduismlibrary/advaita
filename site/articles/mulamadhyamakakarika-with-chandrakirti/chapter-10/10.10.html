MMK 10.10

yo 'pekṣya sidhyate bhāvastamevāpekṣya sidhyati
yadi yo 'pekṣitavyaḥ sa sidhyatāṃ kamapekṣya kaḥ || 10 ||

A thing is established depending on that very thing on which it is to depend. If that which is to be depended on is established, let it be said: depending on what is which established?

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

yo 'pekṣya sidhyate bhāvastamevāpekṣya sidhyati | yadi yo 'pekṣitavyaḥ sa sidhyatāṃ kamapekṣya kaḥ || 10 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

Then he may think: The establishment of fuel occurs simultaneously with the establishment of fire, and the establishment of fire with the establishment of fuel. And because the prior establishment of either one is not accepted, what was said there—“Which is established first, on which fire or fuel depends?”—is not correct. The response: Even when it is held thus, neither is established, because:

Here, if the entity called fire is established depending on the entity called fuel, and the entity called fuel, which must be depended on by fire for its own establishment, is established depending on that very entity called fire, then let it now be stated: depending on what is which established? And when fire is absent, there is no establishment of fuel; then, because fuel without a cause is absent, from where will fire, having that as its cause, be established? Likewise, the entity called fuel, which is established depending on the entity called fire, and the entity called fire, which must be depended on by fuel for its own establishment, if it is established depending on that very entity called fuel, then let it be stated: depending on what is which established? For when fuel is absent, there is no establishment of fire; then, because fire without a cause is absent, from where is fuel, having that as its cause?
