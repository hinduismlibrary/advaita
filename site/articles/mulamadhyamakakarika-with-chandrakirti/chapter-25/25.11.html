MMK 25.11

bhaved abhāvo bhāvaś ca nirvāṇam ubhayaṃ yadi
bhaved abhāvo bhāvaś ca mokṣas tac ca na yujyate || 11 ||

If nirvāṇa were both non-existence and existence, liberation would be both non-existence and existence; and that is not reasonable.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

bhaved abhāvo bhāvaś ca nirvāṇam ubhayaṃ yadi | bhaved abhāvo bhāvaś ca mokṣas tac ca na yujyate || 11 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

For those who say that nirvāṇa is both: because afflictions and birth do not exist there, it is of the nature of non-existence; and because nirvāṇa itself is of the nature of an existent thing, it is of the nature of existence. To show that nirvāṇa does not obtain as both, he says:

“If nirvāṇa were both non-existence and existence, liberation would be both non-existence and existence; and that is not reasonable.”

If nirvāṇa were of the nature of both existence and non-existence, then liberation would be existence and non-existence. And then the arising into being of formations and the disappearance of those very formations would itself be liberation. But it is not reasonable that formations themselves are liberation. For this very reason he says: “And that is not reasonable.”
