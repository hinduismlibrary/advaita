MMK 16.6

Investigation of Bondage and Freedom

MMK 16.6

bandhanam = binding; cet = if; upādānam = appropriation; sa-upādānaḥ = with appropriation; na badhyate = is not bound; badhyate = is bound; na = not; anupādānaḥ = without appropriation; kim = what; avasthaḥ = state; atha = then; badhyate = is bound

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

bandhanaṃ ced upādānaṃ sopādāno na badhyate | badhyate nānupādānaḥ kim avastho 'tha badhyate || 6 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

The opponent proposes that clinging itself is the bond. But someone who has clinging is already bound, so the bond cannot bind that person again. Someone without clinging is already unbound and cannot be bound either. No third state exists in which a person is neither with nor without clinging and can then be bound. Since clinging binds no one, it is not established as an intrinsic bond; without a bond, no intrinsically bound person is found.
