MMK 16.5

Investigation of Bondage and Freedom

MMK 16.5

na badhyante = are not bound; na mucyante = are not released; udaya-vyaya-dharmiṇaḥ = having the nature of arising and passing away; saṃskārāḥ = formations; pūrvavat = as before; sattvaḥ = being; badhyate = is bound; na = not; na mucyate = is not released

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

na badhyante na mucyante udayavyayadharmiṇaḥ | saṃskārāḥ pūrvavat sattvo badhyate na na mucyate || 5 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

An opponent says that even if saṃsāra and nirvāṇa are not established, beings are still bound and freed. Candrakīrti replies that the passions called bondage arise and cease moment by moment. By the time a passion has arisen, it has already ceased; it cannot continue binding a formation that is no longer present. Liberation, understood as cutting off those passions, also cannot be a real event involving vanished formations. The same analysis applies to a person: neither binding nor release is ultimately established.
