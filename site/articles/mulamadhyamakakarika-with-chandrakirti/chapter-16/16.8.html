MMK 16.8

baddho na mucyate tāvad abaddho naiva mucyate
syātāṃ baddhe mucyamāne yugapad bandhamokṣaṇe || 8 ||

The bound one is not liberated, and the unbound one is not liberated. If one were liberated while bound, bondage and release would occur at once.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

baddho na mucyate tāvad abaddho naiva mucyate | syātāṃ baddhe mucyamāne yugapad bandhamokṣaṇe || 8 ||

Commentary note on Candrakīrti

A bound person cannot be freed while still bound. If the bound person is said to be freed later, the claim should be that the person will be freed, not that the bound person is already being freed; no independently existing moment of release is found. An unbound person is already free and has nothing further to attain. If someone were bound and being freed at once, bondage and release would occur simultaneously, like darkness and light. Since liberation cannot be assigned to either a bound or unbound person, bondage too is not established.
