MMK 22.13

yena grāho gṛhītastu ghano 'stīti tathāgataḥ
nāstīti sa vikalpayan nirvṛtasyāpi kalpayet || 13 ||

By whomsoever the grasping “The Tathāgata exists” has been firmly grasped, he, thinking “He does not exist,” would conceive even of the one who has passed into nirvāṇa.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

yena grāho gṛhītastu ghano 'stīti tathāgataḥ | nāstīti sa vikalpayan nirvṛtasyāpi kalpayet || 13 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

By whom the grasping, “The Tathāgata exists,” has been taken up with a very firm and great adherence, he will certainly conceive, when the Tathāgata has passed completely into nirvāṇa, “The Tathāgata does not exist after death; after death he does not exist; the Tathāgata is cut off; he is not found.” For him who thus conceptualizes, the result is a view.
