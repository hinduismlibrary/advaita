MMK 22.10

evam = thus; śūnyam = empty; upādānam = appropriation; upādātā = appropriator; ca = and; sarvaśaḥ = entirely; prajñapyate = is designated; ca = and; śūnyena = by the empty; katham = how; śūnyaḥ = empty; tathāgataḥ = Tathāgata

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

evaṃ śūnyamupādānamupādātā ca sarvaśaḥ | prajñapyate ca śūnyena kathaṃ śūnyastathāgataḥ || 10 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

The aggregates and the appropriator have both been shown to be empty of intrinsic nature. The opponent asks how an empty basis could designate an empty Tathāgata.

Candrakīrti accepts the question only if designation is being treated as an ultimate production of one independently real thing by another. He is not denying ordinary designation; he is rejecting a Tathāgata established from his own side.
