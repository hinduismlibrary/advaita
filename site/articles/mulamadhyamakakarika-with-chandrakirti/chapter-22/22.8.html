MMK 22.8

tattvānyatvena yo nāsti mṛgyamāṇaśca pañcadhā
upādānena sa kathaṃ prajñapyeta tathāgataḥ || 8 ||

Since the Tathāgata is not found as identical with or different from the aggregates, when sought in five ways, how could he be designated through appropriation?

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

tattvānyatvena yo nāsti mṛgyamāṇaśca pañcadhā | upādānena sa kathaṃ prajñapyeta tathāgataḥ || 8 ||

Commentary note on Candrakīrti

The Tathāgata has not been found through identity or difference, nor in any of the five relations to the aggregates. If he is absent under each mode of analysis, how could the aggregates establish him as an intrinsically real person?

The conclusion concerns ultimate analysis: designation through the aggregates does not disclose a separate, self-grounding Tathāgata.
