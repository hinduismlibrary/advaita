MMK 15.10

astīti śāśvatagrāho nāstīty ucchedadarśanam
tasmād astitvanāstitve nāśrīyeta vicakṣaṇaḥ || 10 ||

‘It exists’ is the view of permanence; ‘it does not exist’ is the view of annihilation. The wise therefore rely on neither existence nor nonexistence.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

astīti śāśvatagrāho nāstīty ucchedadarśanam | tasmād astitvanāstitve nāśrīyeta vicakṣaṇaḥ || 10 ||

Commentary note on Candrakīrti

The wise do not rely on either existence or non-existence. “It exists” becomes a grasping at permanence; “it does not exist” becomes the view of annihilation. Both follow when one first assumes that things have own-nature and then either preserves that assumption forever or declares the same things destroyed. The middle way avoids both views.
