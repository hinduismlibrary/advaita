MMK 15.6

svabhāvaṃ parabhāvaṃ ca bhāvaṃ cābhāvam eva ca
ye paśyanti na paśyanti te tattvaṃ buddhaśāsane || 6 ||

Those who see own-being, other-being, being, and non-being do not see the truth in the Buddha’s teaching.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

svabhāvaṃ parabhāvaṃ ca bhāvaṃ cābhāvam eva ca | ye paśyanti na paśyanti te tattvaṃ buddhaśāsane || 6 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

Thus, when own-being, other-being, being, and non-being are in every way not established, those who, because the eye of understanding is afflicted by the darkness of ignorance, see perversely:

Those who, while claiming to interpret the Tathāgata’s teaching without error, describe own-being of entities thus: “Hardness is the own-being of earth; experience of an object is the own-being of feeling; awareness of an object is the own-being of consciousness”; who describe other-being thus: “Consciousness is one thing, form is another, feeling is distinct”; and who describe the present state of consciousness and the like as being, and that very consciousness and the like when it has become past as non-being, do not describe the reality of the most profound dependent arising. For the existence of own-being, other-being, and the like is contrary to the reasoning stated. And the Tathāgatas do not describe the own-being of things contrary to reasoning, because they themselves have directly awakened to the reality of all entities without error. For this very reason, discerning ones set forth the word of the Buddhas, the Blessed Ones alone, as authoritative, because it is accompanied by reasoning and does not deceive. For this very reason, because it has come from trustworthy ones whose faults have all been abandoned, and because it makes known, or leads everywhere to, reality, or because the world goes toward nirvāṇa by going directly to it, only the word of the Fully Awakened One is established as authoritative scripture. The views of others, however, are not authoritative because they are devoid of reasoning; they are established as only a semblance of scripture.
