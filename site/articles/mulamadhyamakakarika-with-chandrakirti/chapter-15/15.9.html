MMK 15.9

prakṛtau = when nature; kasya-cit = of anything; asatyām = absent; anyathātvam = change; bhaviṣyati = will be; prakṛtau = when nature; kasya = of what; ca = and; satyām = present; anyathātvam = change; bhaviṣyati = will be

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

prakṛtau kasya cid asatyām anyathātvaṃ bhaviṣyati | prakṛtau kasya ca satyām anyathātvaṃ bhaviṣyati || 9 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

An opponent asks: if no nature exists, what could change? Candrakīrti turns the question around: if nature does exist, what could change? A thing without nature has no intrinsically established basis for transformation; a thing with an unchanging nature could not transform. Therefore ultimate change is not established in either case. Change is described in ordinary convention, while analysis finds no fixed entity beneath it.
