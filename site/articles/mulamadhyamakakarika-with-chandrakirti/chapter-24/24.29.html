MMK 24.29

phalābhāve phalasthā no na santi pratipannakāḥ
saṃgho nāsti na cetsanti te 'ṣṭau puruṣapudgalāḥ || 29 ||

If there are no results, there are no people abiding in or practicing toward the results. If the eight kinds of noble person do not exist, there is no Sangha.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

phalābhāve phalasthā no na santi pratipannakāḥ | saṃgho nāsti na cetsanti te 'ṣṭau puruṣapudgalāḥ || 29 ||

Commentary note on Candrakīrti

If the fruits could not exist, no one could abide in them or practice toward them; the eight noble persons and the Sangha would likewise be impossible.

These consequences follow from the opponent’s insistence on fixed realities, not from emptiness. The path and its stages are dependently designated.
