MMK 24.33

na = not; ca = and; dharmam = dharma; adharmaṃ = non-dharma; vā = or; kaścit = anyone; jātu = ever; kariṣyati = will do; kim = what; aśūnyasya = of the nonempty; kartavyam = to be done; sva-bhāvaḥ = own-nature; kriyate = is made; na = not; hi = indeed

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

na ca dharmam adharmaṃ vā kaścij jātu kariṣyati | kim aśūnyasya kartavyaṃ svabhāvaḥ kriyate na hi || 33 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

If things were intrinsically established, no one could perform good or harmful actions, because an inherent nature is not made or altered.

The verse asks what could be done to something already fixed by its own nature. Action and ethical responsibility depend on conditions and change.
