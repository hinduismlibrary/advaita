MMK 24.31

apratītyāpi bodhiṃ ca tava buddhaḥ prasajyate
apratītyāpi buddhaṃ ca tava bodhiḥ prasajyate || 31 ||

And it follows for you that the Buddha exists even without awakening; and awakening exists for you even without the Buddha.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

apratītyāpi bodhiṃ ca tava buddhaḥ prasajyate | apratītyāpi buddhaṃ ca tava bodhiḥ prasajyate || 31 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

For if some existent called “Buddha” were by own-being, it would exist without depending on awakening, that is, omniscient knowledge, without requiring it.

For it is said: “For own-being is unmade and independent of another.”

Likewise, awakening would exist without the Buddha; awakening, without depending on the Buddha, would be without a basis.
