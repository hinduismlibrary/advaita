MMK 24.25

yadā duḥkhaṃ samudayo nirodhaś ca na vidyate
mārgo duḥkhanirodhatvāt katamaḥ prāpayiṣyati || 25 ||

If suffering, its origin, and its cessation do not exist, what path could lead to the cessation of suffering?

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

yadā duḥkhaṃ samudayo nirodhaś ca na vidyate | mārgo duḥkhanirodhatvāt katamaḥ prāpayiṣyati || 25 ||

Commentary note on Candrakīrti

If suffering, its origin, and its cessation are all fixed by intrinsic nature, what path could lead to the cessation of suffering?

This is the contradiction in the opponent’s position: an unchanging reality cannot be transformed through a path.
