MMK 24.2

parijñā = full understanding; ca = and; prahāṇam = abandonment; ca = and; bhāvanā = cultivation; sākṣi-karma = making-manifest; ca = and; caturṇām = of four; ārya-satyānām = noble truths; abhāvāt = from nonexistence; na = not; upapadyate = is possible

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

parijñā ca prahāṇaṃ ca bhāvanā sākṣikarma ca | caturṇām āryasatyānām abhāvān nopapadyate || 2 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

If there were no Four Noble Truths, the four tasks of the path would make no sense: understanding suffering, abandoning its origin, realizing cessation, and cultivating the path.

The opponent treats these tasks as impossible because the truths are empty. Candrakīrti will show that a fixed, self-existing reality would make change and practice impossible instead.
