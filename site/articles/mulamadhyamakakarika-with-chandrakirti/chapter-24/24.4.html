MMK 24.4

saṃgho nāsti na cet santi te 'ṣṭau puruṣapudgalāḥ
abhāvāc cāryasatyānāṃ saddharmo 'pi na vidyate || 4 ||

If those eight persons are not present, the Sangha is not present. Because the noble truths are absent, the true Dharma too is not present.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

saṃgho nāsti na cet santi te 'ṣṭau puruṣapudgalāḥ | abhāvāc cāryasatyānāṃ saddharmo 'pi na vidyate || 4 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

If the four noble truths and their full knowledges do not exist, then, because the fruits obtained through seeing and cultivating the truths do not exist, the persons proceeding toward the fruits and established in the fruits do not exist. Therefore the Sangha does not exist. In that case, through the Dharma of realization, the direct presence of Dharma, and the arising of confidence from knowing that it cannot be shaken even by all the Māras, the Sangha would not exist. And if those eight person-individuals do not exist, there is no Sangha.

And because the noble truths do not exist, the true Dharma too is not possible. The Dharma of the noble ones is the true Dharma. There, the truth of cessation is the Dharma that is the fruit; the truth of the path is the Dharma that leads into the fruit. This is the Dharma of realization. The teaching that makes that clear is the Dharma of scripture. All this does not exist when the noble truths do not exist.
