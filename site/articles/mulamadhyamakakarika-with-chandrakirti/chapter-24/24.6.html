MMK 24.6

śūnyatām = emptiness; phala-sad-bhāvam = existence of fruits; adharmam = non-dharma; dharmam = dharma; eva = indeed; ca = and; sarva = all; saṃvyavahārān = conventions; ca = and; laukikān = worldly; pratibādhase = you contradict

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

śūnyatāṃ phalasadbhāvam adharmaṃ dharmam eva ca | sarvasaṃvyavahārāṃś ca laukikān pratibādhase || 6 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

The opponent adds that emptiness would undermine the results of practice, moral and immoral action, and ordinary transactions in the world.

Candrakīrti answers that the argument presupposes emptiness means nothing exists at all. The actual point is that outcomes, conduct, and everyday activity arise through conditions.
