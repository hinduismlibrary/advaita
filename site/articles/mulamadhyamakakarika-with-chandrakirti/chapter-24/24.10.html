MMK 24.10

Without relying on conventions, the sublime meaning cannot be taught. Without understanding the sublime meaning, one will not attain nirvana.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

vyavahāram anāśritya paramārtho na deśyate | paramārtham anāgamya nirvāṇaṃ nādhigamyate || 10 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

The ultimate cannot be taught without ordinary conventions, and nirvāṇa cannot be attained without realizing the ultimate.

Words and concepts are necessary as a path, though they do not capture the final realization. Conventional teaching functions as a vessel that carries the listener toward understanding.
