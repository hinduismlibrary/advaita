MMK 24.19

apratītya = without depending; samutpannaḥ = arisen; dharmaḥ = thing; kaścit = any; na = not; vidyate = exists; yasmāt = because; tasmāt = therefore; aśūnyaḥ = nonempty; hi = indeed; dharmaḥ = thing; kaścit = any; na = not; vidyate = exists

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

apratītya samutpanno dharmaḥ kaścin na vidyate | yasmāt tasmād aśūnyo hi dharmaḥ kaścin na vidyate || 19 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

No phenomenon exists without depending on something else. Therefore no phenomenon is nonempty or established by its own nature.

This is the direct link between dependent arising and emptiness. The opponent’s demand for intrinsically real things has no example to support it.
