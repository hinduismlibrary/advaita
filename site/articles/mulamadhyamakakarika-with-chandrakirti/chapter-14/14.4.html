MMK 14.4

na = not; ca = and; kevalam = only; anyatvam = otherness; draṣṭavya-ādeḥ = of what is to be seen and the rest; na vidyate = is not found; kasyacit = of anything; kenacit = with anything; sārdham = together; na = not; anyatvam = otherness; upapadyate = is possible

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

na ca kevalam anyatvaṃ draṣṭavyāder na vidyate | kasyacit kenacit sārdhaṃ nānyatvam upapadyate || 4 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

The absence of otherness is not limited to the seen and the other elements of perception. No thing is ultimately other than any other thing. Even where things are spoken of as causes and effects, their identities depend on one another. When examined in themselves, they cannot be established as independently different.
