MMK 20.19

hetoḥ = of cause; phalasya = and of fruit; ca = and; ekatvam = identity; na hi = indeed not; jātu = ever; upapadyate = is possible; hetoḥ = of cause; phalasya = and of fruit; ca = and; anyatvam = difference; na hi = indeed not; jātu = ever; upapadyate = is possible

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

hetoḥ phalasya caikatvaṃ na hi jātūpapadyate | hetoḥ phalasya cānyatvaṃ na hi jātūpapadyate || 19 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

The cause and fruit cannot be established as one, nor can they be established as wholly different. Candrakīrti considers these two possibilities because they are the basic ways to claim an intrinsic relation between them.

If they are one, there is no distinction between what produces and what is produced. If they are wholly separate, the fruit no longer depends on the cause.
