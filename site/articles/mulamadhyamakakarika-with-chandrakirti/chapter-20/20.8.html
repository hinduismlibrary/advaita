MMK 20.8

pūrvam = before; eva = indeed; ca = and; sāmagryāḥ = the assemblage; phalam = fruit; prādurbhavet = would arise; yadi = if; hetu-pratyaya-nirmuktam = apart from cause and condition; phalam = fruit; ahetukam = without cause; bhavet = would be

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

pūrvameva ca sāmagryāḥ phalaṃ prādurbhavedyadi | hetupratyayanirmuktaṃ phalamāhetukaṃ bhavet || 8 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

Some argue that the fruit exists in an unmanifest future state before the conditions bring it into its present form. Candrakīrti says that a fruit already established before the causes and conditions would be independent of them.

What is independent of its supposed conditions is causeless. If the fruit is already complete, its later production is also redundant.
