MMK 20.3

If the fruit exists in the combination of cause and conditions, it would be correct for it to be apprehendable in the combination but it is not apprehendable in the combination.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

hetośca pratyayānāṃ ca sāmagryāmasti cetphalam | gṛhyeta nanu sāmagryāṃ sāmagryāṃ ca na gṛhyate || 3 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

If the fruit truly existed in the combination, it should be found there. Candrakīrti points out that it is not apprehended among the causes and conditions.

The opponent cannot establish a pre-existing effect simply by saying that it is subtle or hidden. A result that is not found in the combination is not thereby proven to exist there by its own nature.
