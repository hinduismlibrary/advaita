MMK 20.13

na = not; jātasya = of the arisen; hi = indeed; ajātena = with the unborn; phalasya = fruit; saha = together; hetunā = with cause; na = not; atītena = with the past; na = not; jātena = with the arisen; saṃgatiḥ = conjunction; jātu = ever; vidyate = exists

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

na jātasya hyajātena phalasya saha hetunā | nātītena na jātena saṃgatirjātu vidyate || 13 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

A fruit that has arisen cannot meet a cause that is still future or already past. Nor is a meeting between a present cause and present fruit useful: if both are already present, their connection does not explain production.

The temporal alternatives exhaust the proposed ways cause and effect might come together. In none is a self-existing connection established.
