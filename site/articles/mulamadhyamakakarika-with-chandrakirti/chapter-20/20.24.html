MMK 20.24

asti = exists; pratyaya-sāmagri = assemblage of conditions; kutaḥ = from where; eva = indeed; phalam = fruit; vinā = without

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

asti pratyayasāmagrī kuta eva phalaṃ vinā || 24 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

The final challenge turns the argument around: without a fruit, how could there be a collection called its causes and conditions? Conditions are identified as conditions because a result is said to arise in dependence on them.

Cause and fruit are therefore mutually designated. Candrakīrti concludes that neither exists by its own nature, while their conventional relation remains available for explaining experience and action.
