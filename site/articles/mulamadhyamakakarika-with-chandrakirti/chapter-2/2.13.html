MMK 2.13

Investigation of Coming and Going

MMK 2.13

na = not; pūrvam = before; gamana-ārambhāt = the beginning of going; gamyamānam = being-gone-over; na = not; vā = or; gatam = gone; yatra = where; ārabhyeta = going would begin; gamanam = going; agate = in the not-gone; gamanam = going; kutaḥ = from where

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

na pūrvaṃ gamanārambhād gamyamānaṃ na vā gatam | yatrārabhyeta gamanam agate gamanaṃ kutaḥ || 13 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

Before movement begins, there is no stretch already crossed or presently being crossed in which it could begin. While Devadatta is still sitting, neither category is present. The opponent may suggest that movement begins in what has not yet been gone over; but that is precisely where the action has not arisen. To place a beginning there is incoherent. Thus movement cannot be established by appeal to its beginning.
