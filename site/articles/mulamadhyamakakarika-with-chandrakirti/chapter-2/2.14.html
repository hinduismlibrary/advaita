MMK 2.14

gataṃ kiṃ gamyamānaṃ kim agataṃ kiṃ vikalpyate
adṛśyamāna ārambhe gamanasyaiva sarvathā || 14 ||

What is traversed? What is being traversed? What is not traversed? These are distinctions proposed when the beginning of going cannot be seen in any way.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

gataṃ kiṃ gamyamānaṃ kim agataṃ kiṃ vikalpyate | adṛśyamāna ārambhe gamanasyaiva sarvathā || 14 ||

Commentary note on Candrakīrti

Even if no beginning of movement is found in any of the three cases, one might say that the categories “gone,” “being gone over,” and “not yet gone” still exist. Candrakīrti replies that these categories depend on the start of movement: where the action has ceased one would designate “gone,” where it is occurring one would designate “being gone over,” and where it has not arisen one would designate “not yet gone.” If no beginning of the action can be found, what could these three labels refer to, and what could ground their use?
