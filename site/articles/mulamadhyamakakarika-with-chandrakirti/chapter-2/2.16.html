MMK 2.16

gantā tāvat tiṣṭhatīti kathamevopapatsyate
gamanena vinā gantā yadā naivopapadyate || 16 ||

How can it be possible that the goer stands, since a goer without going is not possible?

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

gantā tāvat tiṣṭhatīti kathamevopapatsyate | gamanena vinā gantā yadā naivopapadyate || 16 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

Here someone says: “The non-goer does not stand, nor does anyone other than the goer and non-goer; rather, the goer himself stands.” This is not so, because:

“How can it be possible that the goer stands, since a goer without going is not possible?”

When he is said to stand, the going that is opposed to standing does not belong to him; and without going, the designation “goer” does not exist. Therefore, it is not reasonable that the goer stands.
