MMK 2.11

gamane dve prasajyete gantā yadyuta gacchati
ganteti cocyate yena gantā san yacca gacchati || 11 ||

If a goer goes, two acts of going follow: the act by which one is called a goer, and the act performed by the goer.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

gamane dve prasajyete gantā yadyuta gacchati | ganteti cocyate yena gantā san yacca gacchati || 11 ||

Commentary note on Candrakīrti

If movement is attached to both parts of “the goer goes,” two movements follow: one by which the person is called a goer, and another which that person performs as he goes. This again entails the rejected double action and double-agent consequence. Ordinary statements such as “Devadatta goes” do not establish movement when examined in this way.
