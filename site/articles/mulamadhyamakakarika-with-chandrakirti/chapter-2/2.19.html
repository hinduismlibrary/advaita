MMK 2.19

yadeva gamanaṃ gantā sa eva hi bhaved yadi
ekībhāvaḥ prasajyeta kartuḥ karmaṇa eva ca || 19 ||

If that very going were the goer himself, the agent and the action would necessarily be one.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

yadeva gamanaṃ gantā sa eva hi bhaved yadi | ekībhāvaḥ prasajyeta kartuḥ karmaṇa eva ca || 19 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

How, then, is it not reasonable? He says:

“If that very going were the goer himself, the agent and the action would necessarily be one.”

If this act of going were not different from the goer, and were not something other than him, then the agent and the action would be one. And then there would be no distinction between “this is the action” and “this is the agent.” But the action of cutting and the cutter are not one. Therefore, it is not reasonable that the very going is the goer.
