MMK 2.18

yadeva gamanaṃ gantā sa eveti na yujyate
anya eva punargantā gateriti na yujyate || 18 ||

It is not tenable that the going itself is the goer; nor is it tenable that the goer is something other than going.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

yadeva gamanaṃ gantā sa eveti na yujyate | anya eva punargantā gateriti na yujyate || 18 ||

Commentary note on Candrakīrti

Candrakīrti now asks how a goer and the action of going could be related. If movement exists, the goer and going must either be the same or different. He first shows why neither relation can be established.
