MMK 11.7

kāryam = effect; ca = and; kāraṇam = cause; ca = and; eva = indeed; lakṣyam = characterized; lakṣaṇam = characteristic; eva = indeed; ca = and; vedanā = feeling; vedakaḥ = experiencer; ca = and; eva = indeed; santi = are; arthāḥ = entities; ye = which; ca = and; kecana = any

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

kāryaṃ ca kāraṇaṃ caiva lakṣyaṃ lakṣaṇameva ca | vedanā vedakaścaiva santyarthā ye ca kecana || 7 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

The lack of a first limit applies not only to saṃsāra but also to cause and effect, a mark and what it marks, feeling and the one who feels. If the cause comes first, it is not yet a cause of an effect; if the effect comes first, it is causeless; if they are simultaneous, neither causes the other. The same reasoning applies to each pair.
