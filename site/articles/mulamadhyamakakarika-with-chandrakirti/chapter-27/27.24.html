MMK 27.24

pūrve yadi na bhajyerann utpadyeran na cāpy amī
skandhāḥ skandhān pratītyemān atha loko 'nanto bhaved atha || 24 ||

If earlier aggregates were not destroyed before these arose in dependence on them, then the world would be infinite.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

pūrve yadi na bhajyerann utpadyeran na cāpy amī | skandhāḥ skandhān pratītyemān atha loko 'nanto bhaved atha || 24 ||

Commentary note on Candrakīrti

If the earlier aggregates did not perish and later aggregates did not arise dependent on them, the world would be endless and unchanging, since it would never depart from its own nature. But earlier aggregates cease and later ones arise because of them. Since the earlier aggregates do not remain forever, the world is not endless either.
