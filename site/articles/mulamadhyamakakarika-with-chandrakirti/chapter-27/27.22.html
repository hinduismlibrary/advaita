MMK 27.22

skandhānām = of aggregates; eṣaḥ = this; saṃtānaḥ = continuity; yasmāt = because; dīpa-arciṣām = of lamp-flames; iva = like; tasmāt = therefore; na = not; anantavattvam = boundlessness; ca = and; na = not; antavattvam = boundedness; ca = and; yujyate = is fitting

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

skandhānām eṣa saṃtāno yasmād dīpārciṣām iva | tasmān nānantavattvaṃ ca nāntavattvaṃ ca yujyate || 22 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

The continuum of the aggregates proceeds like the succession of a lamp’s flames: each moment ceases, while later moments arise through preceding causes and effects in an unbroken sequence. Because this continuum both changes and continues through causal succession, neither the view that the world has an end nor the view that it is endless is reasonable.
