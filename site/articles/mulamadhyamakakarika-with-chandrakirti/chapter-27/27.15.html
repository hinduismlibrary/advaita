MMK 27.15

sa devaḥ sa manuṣyaś ced evaṃ bhavati śāśvatam
anutpannaś ca devaḥ syāj jāyate na hi śāśvatam || 15 ||

If the god were the same as the human, permanence would follow; and a permanent god could not be born.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

sa devaḥ sa manuṣyaś ced evaṃ bhavati śāśvatam | anutpannaś ca devaḥ syāj jāyate na hi śāśvatam || 15 ||

Commentary note on Candrakīrti

If a human being were exactly the same as a god into whose realm that person is later born, the self would be permanent. But a human being does not remain exactly the same while becoming a god. Moreover, if the god were permanent, the god would never be born: what already exists eternally does not arise. Yet a god is said to be born through the result of action. Thus permanence is not established.
