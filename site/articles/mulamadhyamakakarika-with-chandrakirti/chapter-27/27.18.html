MMK 27.18

aśāśvatam = non-eternal; śāśvatam = eternal; ca = and; prasiddham = established; ubhayam = both; yadi = if; siddhe = when established; na = not; śāśvatam = eternal; kāmam = certainly; na = not; eva = indeed; aśāśvatam = non-eternal; iti = thus; api = also

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

aśāśvataṃ śāśvataṃ ca prasiddham ubhayaṃ yadi | siddhe na śāśvataṃ kāmaṃ naivāśāśvatam ity api || 18 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

If both permanence and non-permanence were established, one might then deny both and claim that the world is neither permanent nor non-permanent. But neither of the first two has been established. Without an established thing to negate, the “neither” view cannot be established either.
