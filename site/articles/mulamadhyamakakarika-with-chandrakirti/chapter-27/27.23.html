MMK 27.23

pūrve = former; yadi = if; ca = and; bhajyeran = would be destroyed; utpadyeran = would arise; na = not; ca = and; api = also; amī = these; skandhāḥ = aggregates; skandhān = aggregates; pratītya = depending on; imān = these; atha = then; lokaḥ = world; antavān = bounded

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

pūrve yadi ca bhajyerann utpadyeran na cāpy amī | skandhāḥ skandhān pratītyemān atha loko 'ntavān bhavet || 23 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

If the earlier aggregates were destroyed and the later aggregates did not arise in dependence on them, the world would have an end, like a lamp that goes out when its oil and wick are spent. But later aggregates do arise dependent on earlier ones. Their arising rules out the claim that the world simply ends.
