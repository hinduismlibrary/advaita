MMK 18.12

saṃbuddhānām = of fully awakened buddhas; anutpāde = when arising is absent; śrāvakāṇām = of hearers; punaḥ-kṣaye = when they are exhausted; jñānam = knowledge; pratyekabuddhānām = of solitary buddhas; asaṃsargāt = without conjunction; pravartate = proceeds

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

saṃbuddhānāmanutpāde śrāvakāṇāṃ punaḥkṣaye | jñānaṃ pratyekabuddhānāmasaṃsargātpravartate || 12 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

Even when no fully awakened Buddha appears and the Buddha’s disciples have died out, the knowledge of pratyekabuddhas can arise independently. Candrakīrti explains this independence as solitary practice without a present teacher; it still draws on seeds planted by hearing and practice in earlier lives.

The path therefore bears fruit beyond a single lifetime and outside a period when the teaching is publicly available. Candrakīrti closes by praising the serious pursuit of the Dharma, recalling the bodhisattva Sadāprarudita’s unwavering search for wisdom.
