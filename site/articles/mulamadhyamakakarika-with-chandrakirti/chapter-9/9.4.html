MMK 9.4

vināpi darśanādīni yadi cāsau vyavasthitaḥ
amūnyapi bhaviṣyanti vinā tena na saṃśayaḥ || 4 ||

If that one is established even without seeing and the rest, then surely these too will exist without that one.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

vināpi darśanādīni yadi cāsau vyavasthitaḥ | amūnyapi bhaviṣyanti vinā tena na saṃśayaḥ || 4 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

Further:

“If that one is established even without seeing and the rest, then surely these too will exist without that one.”

If you think: “A person exists before seeing and the rest; he takes up seeing and the rest as appropriations,” then, in that case, without doubt seeing and the rest too will exist without the person. For example, Devadatta, established as distinct from wealth before his relation to wealth, takes up wealth, which is a separate thing. In the same way, seeing and the rest would be appropriations that are other than the appropriator’s self and established separately from it.
