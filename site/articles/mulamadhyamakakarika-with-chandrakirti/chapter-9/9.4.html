MMK 9.4

Investigation of the Presence of Something Prior

← MMK 9.3Chapter 9MMK 9.5 →

MMK 9.4

vinā = without; api = even; darśana-ādīni = seeing and the rest; yadi = if; ca = and; asau = that; vyavasthitaḥ = established; amūni = these; api = also; bhaviṣyanti = will be; vinā = without; tena = that; na saṃśayaḥ = without doubt

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

vināpi darśanādīni yadi cāsau vyavasthitaḥ | amūnyapi bhaviṣyanti vinā tena na saṃśayaḥ || 4 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

If a person existed even without seeing, hearing and the other experiences, then those experiences should also exist without that person. The opponent’s analogy makes them separate things: as Devadatta may be considered apart from his wealth, the senses would be established apart from the person who appropriates them. But the appropriator and what is appropriated are not independently established in that way.
