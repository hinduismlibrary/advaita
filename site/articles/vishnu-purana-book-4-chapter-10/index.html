VP 4.10.1

यति-ययाति-संयात्यायाति-वियाति-कृति-साञ्ज्ञा नहुषस्य षट् पुत्रा महाबल-परिक्रमा बभूवुः ॥१॥

Nahuṣa had six sons of great strength and valor, named Yati, Yayāti, Saṃyāti, Āyāti, Viyāti, and Kṛti.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
यति-ययाति-संयात्यायाति-वियाति-कृति-साञ्ज्ञा नहुषस्य षट् पुत्रा महाबल-परिक्रमा बभूवुः (yati-yayāti-saṃyātyāyāti-viyāti-kṛti-sāñjñā nahuṣasya ṣaṭ putrā mahābala-parikramā babhūvuḥ) — Nahuṣa had six sons of great strength and valor, named Yati, Yayāti, Saṃyāti, Āyāti, Viyāti, and Kṛti.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yati-yayāti-saṃyātyāyāti-viyāti-kṛti-sāñjñā nahuṣasya ṣaṭ putrā mahābala-parikramā babhūvuḥ || 1 ||

VP 4.10.6

यदुं च दुर्वसुं चैव देवयानी व्यजायत । / द्रुह्यं चानुं च पूरुं च शर्मिष्ठा वार्षपर्वणी ॥६॥

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

VP 4.10.9

वत्स त्वन्-मातामह-शापाद् इयम् अकालेअनिव जरा ममोपस्थिता ताम् अहं तस्यैवानुग्रहाद् भवतः सञ्चारयामि ॥९॥

“Child, because of the curse of your maternal grandfather, old age has come upon me before its time. By your favor I shall transfer it to you.”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

By your favor I shall transfer it to you.”

vatsa tvan-mātāmaha-śāpād iyam akāleaniva jarā mamopasthitā tām ahaṃ tasyaivānugrahād bhavataḥ sañcārayāmi || 9 ||

VP 4.10.10

एकं वर्ष-सहस्रम् अतृप्तोऽस्मि विषयेषु त्वद्-वयसा विषयान् अहं भोक्तुम् इच्छामि ॥१०॥

“Though a thousand years have passed, I am not satiated with sense-objects; with your youth I wish to enjoy the sense-objects.”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

VP 4.10.11

“You must not refuse this.” Thus addressed, Yadu did not wish to take that old age.

Transliteration
