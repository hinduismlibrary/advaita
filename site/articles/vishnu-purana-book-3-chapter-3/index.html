VP 3.3.1

ज्ञातमेतन्मया त्वत्तो यथा सर्वमिदं जगत्विष्णुर्विष्णौ विष्णुतश्च न परं विद्यते ततः ॥१॥

I have understood from you how this whole world is Viṣṇu, exists in Viṣṇu, and comes from Viṣṇu. Nothing exists beyond that.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
ज्ञातमेतन्मया (jñātam etan mayā) — this has been understood by me
त्वत्तो (tvatto) — from you
यथा (yathā) — how
सर्वमिदं जगत् (sarvam idaṃ jagat) — this entire world
विष्णुर् (viṣṇur) — Viṣṇu
विष्णौ (viṣṇau) — in Viṣṇu
विष्णुतश्च (viṣṇutaś ca) — and from Viṣṇu
न परं विद्यते (na paraṃ vidyate) — nothing exists beyond that
ततः (tataḥ) — beyond that.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

jñātam etan mayā tvatto yathā sarvam idaṃ jagat viṣṇur viṣṇau viṣṇutaś ca na paraṃ vidyate tataḥ ||1||

VP 3.3.2

एतत्तु श्रोतुमिच्छामि व्यस्ता वेदा महात्मना वेदव्यासस्वरूपेण यथा तेन युगे युगे ॥२॥

Now I want to hear how the great one divided the Vedas, age after age, in the form of Veda-vyāsa.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
एतत्तु श्रोतुमिच्छामि (etat tu śrotum icchāmi) — this I desire to hear
व्यस्ता वेदा (vyastā vedā) — the Vedas divided
महात्मना (mahātmanā) — by the great one
वेदव्यासस्वरूपेण (vedavyāsasvarūpeṇa) — in the form of Veda-vyāsa
यथा (yathā) — in that way
तेन (tena) — by him
युगे युगे (yuge yuge) — age after age.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

etat tu śrotum icchāmi vyastā vedā mahātmanā vedavyāsasvarūpeṇa yathā tena yuge yuge ||2||

VP 3.3.3

यस्मिन्यस्मिन्युगे व्यासो यो य आसीन्महामुने तं तमाचक्ष्व भगवञ्शाखाभेदांश्च मे वद ॥३॥

Tell me who was Vyāsa in each age, O great sage. Tell me each of them and the divisions of the branches.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
यस्मिन्यस्मिन्युगे (yasmin yasmin yuge) — in each age
व्यासो (vyāso) — Vyāsa
यो य आसीन् (yo ya āsīn) — whoever was
महामुने तं (mahāmune taṃ) — O great sage
तमाचक्ष्व (tam ācakṣva) — tell each one
भगवञ् (bhagavañ) — O venerable one
शाखाभेदांश् (śākhābhedāṃś) — branch divisions
च (ca) — and
मे वद (me vada) — tell me.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yasmin yasmin yuge vyāso yo ya āsīn mahāmune taṃ tam ācakṣva bhagavañ śākhābhedāṃś ca me vada ||3||

VP 3.3.4

वेदद्रुमस्य मैत्रेय शाखाभेदैः सहस्रशः न शक्यो विस्तरो वक्तुं संक्षेपेण शृणुष्व तम् ॥४॥

Maitreya, the branch divisions of the Veda-tree are in the thousands. They cannot be described at length, so listen to a concise account.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
वेदद्रुमस्य (vedadrumasya) — of the Veda-tree
मैत्रेय (maitreya) — O Maitreya
शाखाभेदैः (śākhābhedaiḥ) — branch divisions
सहस्रशः (sahasraśaḥ) — by thousands
न शक्यो (na śakyo) — cannot be
विस्तरो वक्तुं (vistaro vaktuṃ) — described at length
संक्षेपेण (saṃkṣepeṇa) — in summary
शृणुष्व (śṛṇuṣva) — listen
तम् (tam) — you.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vedadrumasya maitreya śākhābhedaiḥ sahasraśaḥ na śakyo vistaro vaktuṃ saṃkṣepeṇa śṛṇuṣva tam ||4||

VP 3.3.5

द्वापरे द्वापरे विष्णुर्व्यासरूपी महामुने वेदमेकं स बहुधा कुरुते जगतो हितः ॥५॥

In every Dvāpara, Viṣṇu, in the form of Vyāsa, divides the one Veda into many for the welfare of the world, O great sage.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
द्वापरे द्वापरे (dvāpare dvāpare) — in each Dvāpara
विष्णुर् (viṣṇur) — Viṣṇu
व्यासरूपी (vyāsarūpī) — in the form of Vyāsa
महामुने (mahāmune) — O great sage
वेदमेकं (vedam ekaṃ) — the one Veda
स बहुधा (sa bahudhā) — in many ways
कुरुते (kurute) — makes
जगतो हितः (jagato hitaḥ) — for the welfare of the world.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dvāpare dvāpare viṣṇur vyāsarūpī mahāmune vedam ekaṃ sa bahudhā kurute jagato hitaḥ ||5||

VP 3.3.6

वीर्यं तेजो बलं चाल्पं मनुष्याणामवेक्ष्य च हिताय सर्वभूतानां वेदभेदान्करोति सः ॥६॥

Seeing that human beings have little vigor, radiance, and strength, Viṣṇu divides the Veda for the welfare of all beings.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
वीर्यं (vīryaṃ) — vigor
तेजो (tejo) — radiance
बलं (balaṃ) — strength
चाल्पं (cālpaṃ) — and little
मनुष्याणाम् (manuṣyāṇām) — of human beings
अवेक्ष्य (avekṣya) — observing
च हिताय (ca hitāya) — for the welfare
सर्वभूतानां (sarvabhūtānāṃ) — of all beings
वेदभेदान्करोति (vedabhedān karoti) — makes divisions of the Veda
सः (saḥ) — the Lord.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vīryaṃ tejo balaṃ cālpaṃ manuṣyāṇām avekṣya ca hitāya sarvabhūtānāṃ vedabhedān karoti saḥ ||6||

VP 3.3.7

यया स कुरुते तन्वा वेदमेकं पृथक्प्रभुः वेदव्यासाभिधाना तु सा मूर्तिर्मधुविद्विषः ॥७॥

The body by which that Lord divides the one Veda into separate parts is called Veda-vyāsa. That is the form of the slayer of Madhu.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

प्रभुः (prabhuḥ) — the Lord
वेदव्यासाभिधाना तु सा (vedavyāsābhidhānā tu sā) — called Veda-vyāsa
मूर्तिर् (mūrtir) — that form
मधुविद्विषः (madhuvidviṣaḥ) — of the slayer of Madhu.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yayā sa kurute tanvā vedam ekaṃ pṛthak prabhuḥ vedavyāsābhidhānā tu sā mūrtir madhuvidviṣaḥ ||7||

VP 3.3.8

यस्मिन्मन्वन्तरे ये ये व्यासास्तांस्तान्निबोध मे यथा च भेदः शाखानां व्यासेन क्रियते मुने ॥८॥

Learn from me which Vyāsas there were in each Manvantara, and how Vyāsa makes the branch divisions, O sage.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
यस्मिन्मन्वन्तरे (yasmin manvantare) — in which Manvantara

व्यासास्तांस् (vyāsās tāṃs) — whoever existed
तान्निबोध (tān nibodha) — learn them
मे (me) — from me
यथा (yathā) — how
च (ca) — and
भेदः शाखानां (bhedaḥ śākhānāṃ) — division of branches
व्यासेन (vyāsena) — by Vyāsa
क्रियते (kriyate) — is made
मुने (mune) — O sage.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yasmin manvantare ye ye vyāsās tāṃs tān nibodha me yathā ca bhedaḥ śākhānāṃ vyāsena kriyate mune ||8||

VP 3.3.9

अष्टाविंशतिकृत्वो वै वेदो व्यस्तो महर्षिभिः वैवस्वतेऽन्तरे तस्मिन्द्वापरेषु पुनः पुनः ॥९॥

In this Vaivasvata Manvantara, the great seers have divided the Veda twenty-eight times, again and again in its Dvāpara ages.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
अष्टाविंशतिकृत्वो (aṣṭāviṃśatikṛtvo) — twenty-eight times

व्यस्तो (vyasto) — divided
महर्षिभिः (maharṣibhiḥ) — by great seers
वैवस्वतेऽन्तरे (vaivasvate 'ntare) — in the Vaivasvata Manvantara
तस्मिन् (tasmin) — in that
द्वापरेषु (dvāpareṣu) — in Dvāpara ages
पुनः पुनः (punaḥ punaḥ) — again and again.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

aṣṭāviṃśatikṛtvo vai vedo vyasto maharṣibhiḥ vaivasvate 'ntare tasmin dvāpareṣu punaḥ punaḥ ||9||

VP 3.3.10

वेदव्यासा व्यतीता ये अष्टाविंशति सत्तम चतुर्धा यैः कृतो वेदो द्वापरेषु पुनः पुनः ॥१०॥

Twenty-eight Veda-vyāsas have passed from office, O excellent one. They divided the Veda fourfold again and again in the Dvāpara ages.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
वेदव्यासा (vedavyāsā) — Veda-vyāsas

Transliteration

VP 3.3.11

द्वापरे प्रथमे व्यस्ताः स्वयं वेदाः स्वयंभुवा द्वितीये द्वापरे चैव वेदव्यासः प्रजापतिः ॥११॥

In the first Dvāpara, the Veda itself was divided by Svayambhū. In the second Dvāpara, Prajāpati was the Veda-vyāsa.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
द्वापरे प्रथमे (dvāpare prathame) — in the first Dvāpara
व्यस्ताः (vyastāḥ) — was divided

वेदव्यासः (vedavyāsaḥ) — Veda-vyāsa
प्रजापतिः (prajāpatiḥ) — Prajāpati.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dvāpare prathame vyastāḥ svayaṃ vedāḥ svayaṃbhuvā dvitīye dvāpare caiva vedavyāsaḥ prajāpatiḥ ||11||

VP 3.3.12

तृतीये चोशना व्यासश्चतुर्थे च बृहस्पतिः सविता पञ्चमे व्यासो मृत्युः षष्ठे स्मृतः प्रभुः ॥१२॥

In the third, Uśanas was Vyāsa. In the fourth, Bṛhaspati was Vyāsa. Savitṛ was Vyāsa in the fifth, and in the sixth the Lord is remembered as Mṛtyu.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

पञ्चमे व्यासो (pañcame vyāso) — in the fifth
मृत्युः (mṛtyuḥ) — in the sixth
षष्ठे स्मृतः (ṣaṣṭhe smṛtaḥ) — remembered as Mṛtyu
प्रभुः (prabhuḥ) — the Lord.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tṛtīye cośanā vyāsaś caturthe ca bṛhaspatiḥ savitā pañcame vyāso mṛtyuḥ ṣaṣṭhe smṛtaḥ prabhuḥ ||12||

VP 3.3.13

सप्तमे च तथैवेन्द्रो वसिष्ठश्चाष्टमे स्मृतः सारस्वतश्च नवमे त्रिधामा दशमे स्मृतः ॥१३॥

VP 3.3.14

एकादशे तु त्रिवृषा भारद्वाजस्ततः परम्त्रयोदशे चान्तरिक्षो वर्णी चापि चतुर्दशे ॥१४॥

VP 3.3.16

ततो व्यासो भरद्वाजो भरद्वाजात्तु गौतमः गौतमादुत्तमो व्यासो हर्यात्मा योऽभिधीयते ॥१६॥

VP 3.3.17
