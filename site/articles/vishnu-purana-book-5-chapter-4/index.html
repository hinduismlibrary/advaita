VP 5.4.1

कंसस् ततोद्विग्न-मनाः प्राह सर्वान् महासुरान् ।
प्रलम्ब-केशि-प्रमुखान् आहूयासुर-पुङ्गवान् ॥१॥

Then Kamsa, distressed in mind, spoke to all the great demons, after summoning the demon leaders headed by Pralamba and Keśin.

Śrīdhara translation

“Distressed in mind after that”: “tata” means spread, extended; “udvigna” means agitation—a mind filled with widespread agitation. The reading “tadā” (“then”) is easier.
