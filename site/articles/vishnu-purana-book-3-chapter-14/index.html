VP 3.14.1

ब्रह्मेन्द्ररुद्रनासत्यसूर्याग्निवसुमारुतान् । विश्वेदेवान् ऋषिगणान् वयांसि मनुजान् पशून् ॥१॥

Brahmā, Indra, Rudra, the Nāsatyas, the Sun, Fire, the Vasus, the Maruts, the Viśvedevas, groups of sages, birds, humans, and animals,

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
ब्रह्मेन्द्ररुद्रनासत्यसूर्याग्निवसुमारुतान् विश्वेदेवान् ऋषिगणान् वयांसि (brahmendrarudranāsatyasūryāgnivasumārutān viśvedevān ṛṣigaṇān vayāṃsi) — Brahmā, Indra, Rudra, Nāsatyas, Sun, Fire, Vasus, Maruts, Viśvedevas,
मनुजान् पशून् (manujān paśūn) — groups of sages, birds, humans, animals
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

brahmendrarudranāsatyasūryāgnivasumārutān viśvedevān ṛṣigaṇān vayāṃsi manujān paśūn || 1 ||

VP 3.14.2

सरीसृपान् पितृगणान् यच् चान्यद् भूतसंज्ञितम् । श्राद्धं श्रद्धान्वितः कुर्वन् तर्पयत्य् अखिलं जगत् ॥२॥

Reptiles, groups of Pitṛs, and whatever else is called a being: performing śrāddha with faith, one satisfies the whole world.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
सरीसृपान् पितृगणान् यच् चान्यद् भूतसंज्ञितम् (sarīsṛpān pitṛgaṇān yac cānyad bhūtasaṃjñitam) — reptiles, groups of Pitṛs, and whatever else is called a being
श्राद्धं श्रद्धान्वितः कुर्वन् तर्पयत्य् अखिलं जगत् (śrāddhaṃ śraddhānvitaḥ kurvan tarpayaty akhilaṃ jagat) — performing śrāddha with faith, one satisfies the whole world
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sarīsṛpān pitṛgaṇān yac cānyad bhūtasaṃjñitam śrāddhaṃ śraddhānvitaḥ kurvan tarpayaty akhilaṃ jagat || 2 ||

VP 3.14.3

मासि मास्य् असिते पक्षे पञ्चदश्यां नरेश्वर । तथाष्टकासु कुर्वीत काम्यान् कालाञ् छृणुष्व मे ॥३॥

Month by month, in the dark fortnight, on the fifteenth, and likewise at the aṣṭakās, one should perform desired rites; hear the times from me, O king.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
मासि मास्य् असिते पक्षे पञ्चदश्यां (māsi māsy asite pakṣe pañcadaśyāṃ) — month by month, in the dark fortnight, on the fifteenth, O king
नरेश्वर तथाष्टकासु कुर्वीत काम्यान् कालाञ् छृणुष्व मे (nareśvara tathāṣṭakāsu kurvīta kāmyān kālāñ chṛṇuṣva me) — likewise at the aṣṭakās one should perform desired rites; hear the times from me
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

māsi māsy asite pakṣe pañcadaśyāṃ nareśvara tathāṣṭakāsu kurvīta kāmyān kālāñ chṛṇuṣva me || 3 ||

VP 3.14.4

श्राद्धार्हम् आगतं द्रव्यं विशिष्टम् अथवा द्विजम् । श्राद्धं कुर्वीत विज्ञाय व्यतीपाते ऽयने तथा ॥४॥

When suitable material for śrāddha, or a distinguished dvija fit for śrāddha, has arrived, one should perform śrāddha with discernment at Vyatīpāta and at the solstice.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
श्राद्धार्हम् आगतं द्रव्यं विशिष्टम् अथवा द्विजम् (śrāddhārham āgataṃ dravyaṃ viśiṣṭam athavā dvijam) — material fit for śrāddha that has arrived, or a distinguished dvija fit for śrāddha
श्राद्धं कुर्वीत विज्ञाय व्यतीपाते ऽयने तथा (śrāddhaṃ kurvīta vijñāya vyatīpāte 'yane tathā) — one should perform śrāddha, discerning [the occasion], at Vyatīpāta and at the solstice
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

śrāddhārham āgataṃ dravyaṃ viśiṣṭam athavā dvijam śrāddhaṃ kurvīta vijñāya vyatīpāte 'yane tathā || 4 ||

VP 3.14.5

विषुवे चापि संप्राप्ते ग्रहणे शशिसूर्ययोः । समस्तेष्व् एव भूपाल राशिष्व् अर्के च गच्छति ॥५॥

When the equinox has arrived, at an eclipse of the Moon or Sun, O king, and whenever the Sun enters any of the zodiac signs,

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
विषुवे चापि संप्राप्ते ग्रहणे शशिसूर्ययोः समस्तेष्व् (viṣuve cāpi saṃprāpte grahaṇe śaśisūryayoḥ samasteṣv) — when the equinox has arrived, at an eclipse of Moon or Sun
एव भूपाल राशिष्व् अर्के च गच्छति (eva bhūpāla rāśiṣv arke ca gacchati) — O king, when the Sun enters all the zodiac signs
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

viṣuve cāpi saṃprāpte grahaṇe śaśisūryayoḥ samasteṣv eva bhūpāla rāśiṣv arke ca gacchati || 5 ||

VP 3.14.6

नक्षत्रग्रहपीडासु दुष्टस्वप्नावलोकने । इच्छाश्राद्धानि कुर्वीत नवसस्यागमे तथा ॥६॥

when the stars or planets are afflicted, when bad dreams are seen, one should perform desired śrāddhas, and likewise when the new grain arrives.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
नक्षत्रग्रहपीडासु दुष्टस्वप्नावलोकने इच्छाश्राद्धानि (nakṣatragrahapīḍāsu duṣṭasvapnāvalokane icchāśrāddhāni) — at afflictions of stars and planets, at the sight of bad dreams
कुर्वीत नवसस्यागमे तथा (kurvīta navasasyāgame tathā) — one should perform desired śrāddhas, likewise when new grain arrives
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nakṣatragrahapīḍāsu duṣṭasvapnāvalokane icchāśrāddhāni kurvīta navasasyāgame tathā || 6 ||

VP 3.14.7

अमावास्या यदा मैत्रविशाखास्वातियोगिनी । श्राद्धैः पितृगणस् तृप्तिं तथाप्नोत्य् अष्टवार्षिकीम् ॥७॥

When the new moon is conjoined with Maitra, Viśākhā, and Svāti, the group of Pitṛs obtains satisfaction for eight years through śrāddhas.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
अमावास्या यदा मैत्रविशाखास्वातियोगिनी श्राद्धैः (amāvāsyā yadā maitraviśākhāsvātiyoginī śrāddhaiḥ) — when the new moon is conjoined with Maitra, Viśākhā, and Svāti
पितृगणस् तृप्तिं तथाप्नोत्य् अष्टवार्षिकीम् (pitṛgaṇas tṛptiṃ tathāpnoty aṣṭavārṣikīm) — through śrāddhas the group of Pitṛs obtains eight-year satisfaction
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

amāvāsyā yadā maitraviśākhāsvātiyoginī śrāddhaiḥ pitṛgaṇas tṛptiṃ tathāpnoty aṣṭavārṣikīm || 7 ||

VP 3.14.8

अमावास्या यदा पुष्ये रौद्रे चर्क्षे पुनर्वसौ । द्वादशाब्दं तदा तृप्तिं प्रयान्ति पितरो ऽर्चिताः ॥८॥

When the new moon is in Puṣya, in the Raudra asterism, or in Punarvasu, the worshipped Pitṛs then attain satisfaction for twelve years.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
अमावास्या यदा पुष्ये रौद्रे चर्क्षे पुनर्वसौ (amāvāsyā yadā puṣye raudre carkṣe punarvasau) — when the new moon is in Puṣya, Raudra, and Punarvasu
द्वादशाब्दं तदा तृप्तिं प्रयान्ति पितरो ऽर्चिताः (dvādaśābdaṃ tadā tṛptiṃ prayānti pitaro 'rcitāḥ) — the worshipped Pitṛs then attain satisfaction for twelve years
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

amāvāsyā yadā puṣye raudre carkṣe punarvasau dvādaśābdaṃ tadā tṛptiṃ prayānti pitaro 'rcitāḥ || 8 ||

VP 3.14.9

वासवाजैकपादर्क्षे पितॄणां तृप्तिम् इच्छताम् । वारुणे चाप्य् अमावास्या देवानाम् अपि दुर्लभा ॥९॥

For those desiring satisfaction of the Pitṛs, a new moon in the Vāsava, Ajaikapāda, or Varuṇa asterism is difficult to obtain even for the gods.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
वासवाजैकपादर्क्षे पितॄणां तृप्तिम् इच्छताम् (vāsavājaikapādarkṣe pitṝṇāṃ tṛptim icchatām) — in the Vāsava-Ajaikapāda asterism, for those desiring satisfaction of the Pitṛs
वारुणे चाप्य् अमावास्या देवानाम् अपि दुर्लभा (vāruṇe cāpy amāvāsyā devānām api durlabhā) — and in Varuṇa too, a new moon is difficult even for the gods
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vāsavājaikapādarkṣe pitṝṇāṃ tṛptim icchatām vāruṇe cāpy amāvāsyā devānām api durlabhā || 9 ||

VP 3.14.10

नवस्व् ऋक्षेष्व् अमावास्या यदैतेष्व् अवनीपते । तदा तृप्तिप्रदं श्राद्धं पितॄणां शृणु चापरम् ॥१०॥

When the new moon occurs in these nine asterisms, O king, hear another śrāddha that gives satisfaction to the Pitṛs.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
नवस्व् ऋक्षेष्व् अमावास्या यदैतेष्व् अवनीपते (navasv ṛkṣeṣv amāvāsyā yadaiteṣv avanīpate) — when the new moon is in these nine asterisms, O king
तदा तृप्तिप्रदं श्राद्धं पितॄणां शृणु चापरम् (tadā tṛptipradaṃ śrāddhaṃ pitṝṇāṃ śṛṇu cāparam) — then hear another śrāddha that gives satisfaction to the Pitṛs
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

navasv ṛkṣeṣv amāvāsyā yadaiteṣv avanīpate tadā tṛptipradaṃ śrāddhaṃ pitṝṇāṃ śṛṇu cāparam || 10 ||

VP 3.14.11

गीतं सनत्कुमारेण यदऽइलाय महात्मने । पृच्छते पितृभक्ताय श्रद्धयावनताय च ॥११॥

This was sung by Sanatkumāra to the great-souled Ilā, who asked, devoted to the Pitṛs, and bowed with faith.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
गीतं सनत्कुमारेण यद् इलाय महात्मने (gītaṃ sanatkumāreṇa yad ilāya mahātmane) — sung by Sanatkumāra to the great-souled Ilā
पृच्छते पितृभक्ताय श्रद्धयावनताय च (pṛcchate pitṛbhaktāya śraddhayāvanatāya ca) — who asked, devoted to the Pitṛs, and bowed with faith
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

gītaṃ sanatkumāreṇa yad ilāya mahātmane pṛcchate pitṛbhaktāya śraddhayāvanatāya ca || 11 ||

VP 3.14.12

वैशाखमासस्य च या तृतीया नवम्य् असौ कार्त्तिकशुक्लपक्षे । नभस्यमासस्य तु कृष्णपक्षे त्रयोदशी पञ्चदशी च माघे ॥१२॥

The third of Vaiśākha, and that ninth of the bright fortnight of Kārttika; in the month of Nabhas, the thirteenth of the dark fortnight, and the fifteenth in Māgha.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
वैशाखमासस्य च या तृतीया नवम्य् असौ कार्त्तिकशुक्लपक्षे नभस्यमासस्य (vaiśākhamāsasya ca yā tṛtīyā navamy asau kārttikaśuklapakṣe nabhasyamāsasya) — the third of Vaiśākha, and the ninth of Kārttika’s bright fortnight
तु कृष्णपक्षे त्रयोदशी पञ्चदशी च माघे (tu kṛṣṇapakṣe trayodaśī pañcadaśī ca māghe) — in Nabhas’s dark fortnight the thirteenth, and in Māgha the fifteenth
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vaiśākhamāsasya ca yā tṛtīyā navamy asau kārttikaśuklapakṣe nabhasyamāsasya tu kṛṣṇapakṣe trayodaśī pañcadaśī ca māghe || 12 ||

VP 3.14.13

एता युगाद्यास् तिथयश् चतस्रो ऽप्य् अनन्तपुण्या नृप संप्रदिष्टाः । उपप्लवे चन्द्रमसो रवेश् च त्रिष्व् अष्टकास्व् अप्य् अयनद्वये च ॥१३॥

These four tithis at the beginning of the yugas are declared to be of endless merit, O king; at an eclipse of the Moon or Sun, at the three aṣṭakās, and at the two solstices.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
एता युगाद्यास् तिथयश् चतस्रो ऽप्य् अनन्तपुण्या नृप संप्रदिष्टाः (etā yugādyās tithayaś catasro 'py anantapuṇyā nṛpa saṃpradiṣṭāḥ) — these four tithis at the beginning of the yugas
उपप्लवे चन्द्रमसो रवेश् च त्रिष्व् अष्टकास्व् अप्य् अयनद्वये च (upaplave candramaso raveś ca triṣv aṣṭakāsv apy ayanadvaye ca) — are declared to be of endless merit, O king; at Moon or Sun eclipses, the three aṣṭakās, and two solstices
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

etā yugādyās tithayaś catasro 'py anantapuṇyā nṛpa saṃpradiṣṭāḥ upaplave candramaso raveś ca triṣv aṣṭakāsv apy ayanadvaye ca || 13 ||

VP 3.14.14

पानीयम् अप्य् अत्र तिलैर् विमिश्रं दद्यात् पितृभ्यः प्रयतो मनुष्यः । श्राद्धं कृतं तेन समाः सहस्रं रहस्यम् एतत् पितरो वदन्ति ॥१४॥

Here, too, an attentive man should give water mixed with sesame to the Pitṛs. By him a śrāddha is performed for a thousand years; this is a secret, the Pitṛs say.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
पानीयम् अप्य् अत्र तिलैर् विमिश्रं दद्यात् पितृभ्यः प्रयतो मनुष्यः (pānīyam apy atra tilair vimiśraṃ dadyāt pitṛbhyaḥ prayato manuṣyaḥ) — water mixed with sesame here, an attentive man should give to the Pitṛs
श्राद्धं कृतं तेन समाः सहस्रं रहस्यम् एतत् पितरो वदन्ति (śrāddhaṃ kṛtaṃ tena samāḥ sahasraṃ rahasyam etat pitaro vadanti) — by him śrāddha is performed for a thousand years; a secret, the Pitṛs say
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

pānīyam apy atra tilair vimiśraṃ dadyāt pitṛbhyaḥ prayato manuṣyaḥ śrāddhaṃ kṛtaṃ tena samāḥ sahasraṃ rahasyam etat pitaro vadanti || 14 ||

VP 3.14.15

माघासिते पञ्चदशी कदाचिद् उपैति योगं यदि वारुणेन । ऋक्षेण कालः स परः पितॄणां न ह्य् अल्पपुण्यैर् नृप लभ्यते ऽसौ ॥१५॥

If the fifteenth of Māgha’s dark fortnight sometimes comes into conjunction with the Varuṇa asterism, that is the highest time for the Pitṛs, O king; indeed, it is not attained by those of little merit.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
माघासिते पञ्चदशी कदाचिद् उपैति योगं यदि वारुणेन ऋक्षेण कालः स परः (māghāsite pañcadaśī kadācid upaiti yogaṃ yadi vāruṇena ṛkṣeṇa kālaḥ sa paraḥ) — the fifteenth of Māgha’s dark fortnight sometimes comes into conjunction with Varuṇa’s asterism; that time
पितॄणां न ह्य् अल्पपुण्यैर् नृप लभ्यते ऽसौ (pitṝṇāṃ na hy alpapuṇyair nṛpa labhyate 'sau) — is highest for the Pitṛs, O king; it is not attained by those of little merit
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

māghāsite pañcadaśī kadācid upaiti yogaṃ yadi vāruṇena ṛkṣeṇa kālaḥ sa paraḥ pitṝṇāṃ na hy alpapuṇyair nṛpa labhyate 'sau || 15 ||

VP 3.14.16

काले धनिष्ठा यदि नाम तस्मिन् भवन्ति भूपाल तदा पितृभ्यः । दत्तं जलान्नं प्रददाति तृप्तिं वर्षायुतं तत्कुलजैर् मनुष्यैः ॥१६॥

If Dhaniṣṭhā is present at that time, O king, then water and food given to the Pitṛs by people of that family give satisfaction for ten thousand years.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
काले धनिष्ठा यदि नाम तस्मिन् भवन्ति भूपाल तदा (kāle dhaniṣṭhā yadi nāma tasmin bhavanti bhūpāla tadā) — if Dhaniṣṭhā is present at that time, O king
पितृभ्यः दत्तं जलान्नं प्रददाति तृप्तिं वर्षायुतं तत्कुलजैर् मनुष्यैः (pitṛbhyaḥ dattaṃ jalānnaṃ pradadāti tṛptiṃ varṣāyutaṃ tatkulajair manuṣyaiḥ) — then water and food given to the Pitṛs by people of that family give ten-thousand-year satisfaction
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
