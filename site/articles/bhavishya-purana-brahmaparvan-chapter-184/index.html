A Brahmin who eats stale food, honey, and meat should fast for three days and remain in water for one day.

mṛta-annam — food from the dead
madhu — honey
māṃsam — meat
ca — and
yaḥ — whoever
tu — indeed
bhuñjīta — eats
brāhmaṇaḥ — Brāhmaṇa
saḥ — he
trīṇi ahāni — three days
upavaset — should fast
eka-aham — one day
ca — and
udake — in water
vaset — should dwell
