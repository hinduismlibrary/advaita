VP 2.8.1

व्याख्यातम् एतद् ब्रह्माण्डसंस्थानं तव सुव्रत । ततः प्रमाणसंस्थाने सूर्यादीनां शृणुष्व मे ॥१॥

This arrangement of the brahmāṇḍa has been explained to you, O Suvrata. Now hear from me the measures and positions of the Sun and the others.
