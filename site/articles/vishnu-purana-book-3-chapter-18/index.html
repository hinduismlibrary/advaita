VP 3.18.1

तपस्य् अभिरतान् सोऽथ मायामोहो महासुरान् । मैत्रेय ददृशे गत्वा नर्मदातीरसंश्रयान् ॥१॥

Māyāmoha went to the great asuras devoted to austerity and saw them dwelling on the bank of the Narmadā, O Maitreya.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
तपस्य् अभिरतान् सोऽथ मायामोहो महासुरान् (tapasy abhiratān so 'tha māyāmoho mahāsurān) — devoted to austerity; then Māyāmoha the great asuras
मैत्रेय ददृशे गत्वा नर्मदातीरसंश्रयान् (maitreya dadṛśe gatvā narmadātīrasaṃśrayān) — O Maitreya, he saw; having gone to those dwelling on the bank of the Narmadā
Transliteration

VP 3.18.2

ततो दिगम्बरो मुण्डो बर्हिपत्रधरो द्विज । मायामोहोऽसुरान् श्लक्ष्णम् इदं वचनम् अब्रवीत् ॥२॥

Then Māyāmoha, naked, shaven-headed, wearing peacock feathers, addressed the asuras with this smooth speech, O twice-born.

मायामोहोऽसुरान् श्लक्ष्णम् इदं वचनम् अब्रवीत् (māyāmoho 'surān ślakṣṇam idaṃ vacanam abravīt) — Māyāmoha addressed the asuras in these smooth words

VP 3.18.3

भो दैत्यपतयो ब्रूत यदर्थं तप्यते तपः । ऐहिकं वाथ पारत्र्यं तपसः फलम् इच्छथ ॥३॥

O lords of the Daityas, tell me: for what purpose is this austerity performed? Do you desire a worldly fruit or one beyond this world?

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
भो दैत्यपतयो ब्रूत यदर्थं तप्यते तपः (bho daityapatayo brūta yadarthaṃ tapyate tapaḥ) — O lords of the Daityas, tell me for what purpose austerity is performed
ऐहिकं वाथ पारत्र्यं तपसः फलम् इच्छथ (aihikaṃ vātha pāratryaṃ tapasaḥ phalam icchatha) — do you seek a worldly result or an other-worldly result from austerity
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bho daityapatayo brūta yadarthaṃ tapyate tapaḥ | aihikaṃ vātha pāratryaṃ tapasaḥ phalam icchatha || 3 ||

VP 3.18.4

पारत्र्यफललाभाय तपश्चर्या महामते । अस्माभिर् इयम् आरब्धा किं वा तेऽत्र विवक्षितम् ॥४॥

For the sake of attaining a fruit beyond this world, O great-minded one, we began this austerity. What is your intention here?

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
पारत्र्यफललाभाय तपश्चर्या महामते (pāratryaphalalābhāya tapaścaryā mahāmate) — for attaining an other-worldly result, O great-minded one, this austerity is undertaken by us
अस्माभिर् इयम् आरब्धा किं वा तेऽत्र विवक्षितम् (asmābhir iyam ārabdhā kiṃ vā te 'tra vivakṣitam) — what is intended by you here
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

pāratryaphalalābhāya tapaścaryā mahāmate | asmābhir iyam ārabdhā kiṃ vā te 'tra vivakṣitam || 4 ||

VP 3.18.5

कुरुध्वं मम वाक्यानि यदि मुक्तिम् अभीप्सथ । अर्हध्वं धर्मम् एतं च मुक्तिद्वारम् असंवृतम् ॥५॥

Follow my words if you desire liberation. Make yourselves worthy of this dharma; its gate to liberation is open.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
कुरुध्वं मम वाक्यानि यदि मुक्तिम् अभीप्सथ (kurudhvaṃ mama vākyāni yadi muktim abhīpsatha) — perform my words if you desire liberation
अर्हध्वं धर्मम् एतं च मुक्तिद्वारम् असंवृतम् (arhadhvaṃ dharmam etaṃ ca muktidvāram asaṃvṛtam) — make yourselves worthy of this dharma; the gate of liberation is open
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kurudhvaṃ mama vākyāni yadi muktim abhīpsatha | arhadhvaṃ dharmam etaṃ ca muktidvāram asaṃvṛtam || 5 ||

VP 3.18.6

धर्मो विमुक्तेर् अर्होऽयं नैतस्माद् अपरः परः । अत्रैवावस्थिताः स्वर्गं विमुक्तिं वा गमिष्यथ ॥६॥

This dharma is worthy of liberation; nothing is higher than it. Established in it, you will attain heaven or liberation.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
धर्मो विमुक्तेर् अर्होऽयं नैतस्माद् अपरः परः (dharmo vimukter arho 'yaṃ naitasmād aparaḥ paraḥ) — this dharma is worthy of liberation; no other is higher than it
अत्रैवावस्थिताः स्वर्गं विमुक्तिं वा गमिष्यथ (atraivāvasthitāḥ svargaṃ vimuktiṃ vā gamiṣyatha) — established in this alone, you will attain heaven or liberation
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dharmo vimukter arho 'yaṃ naitasmād aparaḥ paraḥ | atraivāvasthitāḥ svargaṃ vimuktiṃ vā gamiṣyatha || 6 ||

VP 3.18.7

अर्हध्वं धर्मम् एतं च । सर्वे यूयं महाबलाः ॥७॥

All of you, O mighty ones, become worthy of this dharma.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
अर्हध्वं धर्मम् एतं च (arhadhvaṃ dharmam etaṃ ca) — become worthy; of this dharma also
सर्वे यूयं महाबलाः (sarve yūyaṃ mahābalāḥ) — all of you, O mighty ones
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

arhadhvaṃ dharmam etaṃ ca | sarve yūyaṃ mahābalāḥ || 7 ||

VP 3.18.8

एवंप्रकारैर् बहुभिर् युक्तिदर्शनवर्धितैः । मायामोहेन दैत्यास् ते वेदमार्गाद् अपाकृताः ॥८॥

Through many such arguments and discussions of reasoning, Māyāmoha turned the Daityas away from the Vedic path.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
एवंप्रकारैर् बहुभिर् युक्तिदर्शनवर्धितैः (evaṃprakārair bahubhir yuktidarśanavardhitaiḥ) — with many such methods, discussed through reasoning and doctrine
मायामोहेन दैत्यास् ते वेदमार्गाद् अपाकृताः (māyāmohena daityās te vedamārgād apākṛtāḥ) — by Māyāmoha, those Daityas were turned away from the Vedic path
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

evaṃprakārair bahubhir yuktidarśanavardhitaiḥ | māyāmohena daityās te vedamārgād apākṛtāḥ || 8 ||

VP 3.18.9

धर्मायैतद् अधर्माय सद् एतन् न सद् इत्य् अपि । विमुक्तये त्व् इदं नैतद् विमुक्तिं संप्रयच्छति ॥९॥

This is for dharma and for adharma; it is existent and non-existent. This is for liberation, yet it does not grant liberation.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

विमुक्तये त्व् इदं नैतद् विमुक्तिं संप्रयच्छति (vimuktaye tv idaṃ naitad vimuktiṃ saṃprayacchati) — for liberation, yet this does not grant liberation
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dharmāyaitad adharmāya sad etan na sad ity api | vimuktaye tv idaṃ naitad vimuktiṃ saṃprayacchati || 9 ||

VP 3.18.10

परमार्थोऽयम् अत्यर्थं । परमार्थो न चाप्य् अयम् ॥१०॥

This is the highest truth, and it is not the highest truth; it should be done, and it should not be done; this is not so, yet this is clearly so.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
परमार्थोऽयम् अत्यर्थं (paramārtho 'yam atyarthaṃ) — this is the highest truth, very much; it is not the highest truth also
परमार्थो न चाप्य् अयम् (paramārtho na cāpy ayam) — this is to be done, this is not to be done; this is not so, yet this is clearly so
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

paramārtho 'yam atyarthaṃ | paramārtho na cāpy ayam || 10 ||

VP 3.18.11

कार्यम् एतद् अकार्यं च नैतद् एवं स्फुटं त्व् इदम् । दिग्वाससाम् अयं धर्मो धर्मोऽयं बहुवाससाम् ॥११॥

This is the dharma of those clothed in the directions, and this is the dharma of those with many garments.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
कार्यम् एतद् अकार्यं च नैतद् एवं स्फुटं त्व् इदम् (kāryam etad akāryaṃ ca naitad evaṃ sphuṭaṃ tv idam) — this is the dharma of those clothed in the directions
दिग्वाससाम् अयं धर्मो धर्मोऽयं बहुवाससाम् (digvāsasām ayaṃ dharmo dharmo 'yaṃ bahuvāsasām) — this is the dharma of those with many garments
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kāryam etad akāryaṃ ca naitad evaṃ sphuṭaṃ tv idam | digvāsasām ayaṃ dharmo dharmo 'yaṃ bahuvāsasām || 11 ||

VP 3.18.12

इत्य् अनेकान्तवादं च मायामोहेन नैकधा । तेन दर्शयता दैत्याः स्वधर्मांस् त्याजिता द्विज ॥१२॥

By presenting anekāntavāda in many forms, Māyāmoha made the Daityas abandon their own dharma, O twice-born.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
इत्य् अनेकान्तवादं च मायामोहेन नैकधा (ity anekāntavādaṃ ca māyāmohena naikadhā) — and this anekāntavāda, in many ways, by Māyāmoha
तेन दर्शयता दैत्याः स्वधर्मांस् त्याजिता द्विज (tena darśayatā daityāḥ svadharmāṃs tyājitā dvija) — by displaying it, the Daityas were made to abandon their own dharma, O twice-born
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ity anekāntavādaṃ ca māyāmohena naikadhā | tena darśayatā daityāḥ svadharmāṃs tyājitā dvija || 12 ||

VP 3.18.13

अर्हथेमं महाधर्मं मायामोहेन ते यतः । प्रोक्तास् तम् आश्रिता धर्मम् अर्हतास् तेन तेऽभवन् ॥१३॥

Because Māyāmoha taught them this great dharma, they became Ārhatas by taking refuge in it.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
अर्हथेमं महाधर्मं मायामोहेन ते यतः (arhathemaṃ mahādharmaṃ māyāmohena te yataḥ) — because of Māyāmoha, they embraced this great dharma, the Ārhata doctrine
प्रोक्तास् तम् आश्रिता धर्मम् अर्हतास् तेन तेऽभवन् (proktās tam āśritā dharmam arhatās tena te 'bhavan) — having been taught it and taking refuge in that dharma, they became Ārhatas
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

arhathemaṃ mahādharmaṃ māyāmohena te yataḥ | proktās tam āśritā dharmam arhatās tena te 'bhavan || 13 ||

VP 3.18.14

त्रयीधर्मसमुत्सर्गं मायामोहेन तेऽसुराः । कारितास् तन्मया ह्य् आसंस् तथान्ये तत्प्रबोधिताः ॥१४॥

Māyāmoha caused those asuras to abandon the threefold Vedic dharma; influenced by them, others were impelled in the same way.

त्रयीधर्मसमुत्सर्गं मायामोहेन तेऽसुराः (trayīdharmasamutsargaṃ māyāmohena te 'surāḥ) — the abandonment of the threefold dharma, by Māyāmoha, those asuras
कारितास् तन्मया ह्य् आसंस् तथान्ये तत्प्रबोधिताः (kāritās tanmayā hy āsaṃs tathānye tatprabodhitāḥ) — made to do this by me, they became so; thereafter others were impelled by them
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

trayīdharmasamutsargaṃ māyāmohena te 'surāḥ | kāritās tanmayā hy āsaṃs tathānye tatprabodhitāḥ || 14 ||

VP 3.18.15

तैर् अप्य् अन्ये परे तैश् च तैर् अप्य् अन्ये परे च तैः । अल्पैर् अहोभिः संत्यक्ता तैर् दैत्यैः प्रायशस् त्रयी ॥१५॥

Those taught by them taught others, and those others taught still others; within a few days the Daityas had mostly abandoned the threefold Veda.

अल्पैर् अहोभिः संत्यक्ता तैर् दैत्यैः प्रायशस् त्रयी (alpair ahobhiḥ saṃtyaktā tair daityaiḥ prāyaśas trayī) — in a few days, the threefold Veda was mostly abandoned by those Daityas
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tair apy anye pare taiś ca tair apy anye pare ca taiḥ | alpair ahobhiḥ saṃtyaktā tair daityaiḥ prāyaśas trayī || 15 ||

VP 3.18.16

पुनश् च रक्ताम्बरधृङ् मायामोहोऽजितेक्षणः । अन्यान् आहासुरान् गत्वा मृद्वल्पमधुराक्षरम् ॥१६॥

Then Māyāmoha, wearing red garments and having mastered his senses, went to other asuras and addressed them in a few gentle, sweet words.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
पुनश् च रक्ताम्बरधृङ् मायामोहोऽजितेक्षणः (punaś ca raktāmbaradhṛṅ māyāmoho 'jitekṣaṇaḥ) — then again, Māyāmoha wearing red garments, self-controlled
अन्यान् आहासुरान् गत्वा मृद्वल्पमधुराक्षरम् (anyān āhāsurān gatvā mṛdvalpamadhurākṣaram) — he went and addressed other asuras in soft, few, sweet words
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

punaś ca raktāmbaradhṛṅ māyāmoho 'jitekṣaṇaḥ | anyān āhāsurān gatvā mṛdvalpamadhurākṣaram || 16 ||

VP 3.18.17

स्वर्गार्थं यदि वो वाञ्छा निर्वाणार्थम् अथासुराः । तद् अलं पशुघातादिदुष्टधर्मं निबोधत ॥१७॥

If you desire heaven or nirvāṇa, O asuras, understand this: enough of wicked dharmas such as killing animals.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
स्वर्गार्थं यदि वो वाञ्छा निर्वाणार्थम् अथासुराः (svargārthaṃ yadi vo vāñchā nirvāṇārtham athāsurāḥ) — if your desire is for heaven, or for nirvāṇa, O asuras
तद् अलं पशुघातादिदुष्टधर्मं निबोधत (tad alaṃ paśughātādiduṣṭadharmaṃ nibodhata) — then enough of wicked dharmas beginning with animal killing; understand
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

svargārthaṃ yadi vo vāñchā nirvāṇārtham athāsurāḥ | tad alaṃ paśughātādiduṣṭadharmaṃ nibodhata || 17 ||

VP 3.18.18

विज्ञानमयम् एवैतद् अशेषम् अवगच्छत । बुध्यध्वं मे वचः सम्यग् बुधैर् एवम् उदीरितम् ॥१८॥

Know all this to be made of consciousness; understand my words correctly, as the wise have taught here.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
विज्ञानमयम् एवैतद् अशेषम् अवगच्छत (vijñānamayam evaitad aśeṣam avagacchata) — know all this to be entirely made of consciousness
बुध्यध्वं मे वचः सम्यग् बुधैर् एवम् उदीरितम् (budhyadhvaṃ me vacaḥ samyag budhair evam udīritam) — understand my word rightly, spoken here by the awakened
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
