VP 4.14.4

Word-for-word

Śrīdhara

kramāgatā vṛṣṇivaṃśāḥ śaineyādyāścaturdaṃśa| varṇyante śiśupālasya sāyujyañcātra sūcyate | satyatasya saptamaḥ putro vṛṣṇistataputrau anamitraśinī | tatrānamitrasya nighnataḥ tasya prasenasatrāditāvityeko vaṃsa uktaḥ | tatra prasaṅgāgatasyamantakodāraṇamuktam, idānīmanamitrānujasyānyasya śinervaśyāḥ śaineyāḥ ||4||

Devanagari
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

इत्य् एते शैनेयाः ॥४॥

Śrīdhara
