VP 3.13.1

सचैलस्य पितुः स्नानं जाते पुत्रे विधीयते जातकर्म तथा कुर्याच् श्राद्धम् अभ्युदये च यत् ॥१॥

When a son is born, a bath with clothes on is prescribed for the father; he should also perform the birth rite and the śrāddha for prosperity.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
सचैलस्य पितुः स्नानं जाते पुत्रे विधीयते जातकर्म (sacailasya pituḥ snānaṃ jāte putre vidhīyate jātakarma) — When a son is born, a bath with clothes on is prescribed for the father
तथा कुर्याच् श्राद्धम् अभ्युदये च यत् (tathā kuryāc śrāddham abhyudaye ca yat) — he should also perform the birth rite and the śrāddha for prosperity.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sacailasya pituḥ snānaṃ jāte putre vidhīyate jātakarma tathā kuryāc śrāddham abhyudaye ca yat || 1 ||

VP 3.13.2

युग्मान् देवांश् च पित्र्यांश् च सम्यक् सव्यक्रमाद् द्विजान् पूजयेद् भोजयेच् चैव तन्मना नान्यमानसः ॥२॥

He should properly honor and feed an even number of twice-born for the divine and ancestral rites, in the proper order, attentive in mind, not thinking of another thing.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
युग्मान् देवांश् च पित्र्यांश् च सम्यक् सव्यक्रमाद् (yugmān devāṃś ca pitryāṃś ca samyak savyakramād) — He should properly honor and feed an even number of twice-born for the divine
द्विजान् पूजयेद् भोजयेच् चैव तन्मना नान्यमानसः (dvijān pūjayed bhojayec caiva tanmanā nānyamānasaḥ) — ancestral rites, in the proper order, attentive in mind, not thinking of another thing.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yugmān devāṃś ca pitryāṃś ca samyak savyakramād dvijān pūjayed bhojayec caiva tanmanā nānyamānasaḥ || 2 ||

VP 3.13.3

दध्यक्षतैः सबदरैः प्राङ्मुखोदङ्मुखोऽपि वा देवतीर्थेन वै पिण्डान् दद्यात् कायेन वा नृप ॥३॥

He should give piṇḍas with curds, unbroken rice, and jujube fruits, facing east or north, through the deva-tīrtha, or by the body, O king.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
दध्यक्षतैः सबदरैः प्राङ्मुखोदङ्मुखोऽपि वा देवतीर्थेन (dadhyakṣataiḥ sabadaraiḥ prāṅmukhodaṅmukho 'pi vā devatīrthena) — He should give piṇḍas with curds, unbroken rice, and jujube fruits
वै पिण्डान् दद्यात् कायेन वा नृप (vai piṇḍān dadyāt kāyena vā nṛpa) — facing east or north, through the deva-tīrtha, or by the body, O king.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dadhyakṣataiḥ sabadaraiḥ prāṅmukhodaṅmukho 'pi vā devatīrthena vai piṇḍān dadyāt kāyena vā nṛpa || 3 ||

VP 3.13.4

नान्दीमुखः पितृगणस् तेन श्राद्धेन पार्थिव प्रीयते तत् तु कर्तव्यं पुरुषैः सर्ववृद्धिषु ॥४॥

The Nandīmukha group of Pitṛs is pleased by that śrāddha, O king; people should perform it at every occasion of increase.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
नान्दीमुखः पितृगणस् तेन श्राद्धेन पार्थिव प्रीयते (nāndīmukhaḥ pitṛgaṇas tena śrāddhena pārthiva prīyate) — The Nandīmukha group of Pitṛs is pleased by that śrāddha, O king
तत् तु कर्तव्यं पुरुषैः सर्ववृद्धिषु (tat tu kartavyaṃ puruṣaiḥ sarvavṛddhiṣu) — people should perform it at every occasion of increase.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nāndīmukhaḥ pitṛgaṇas tena śrāddhena pārthiva prīyate tat tu kartavyaṃ puruṣaiḥ sarvavṛddhiṣu || 4 ||

VP 3.13.5

कन्यापुत्रविवाहेषु प्रवेशे नववेश्मनः नामकर्मणि बालानां चूडाकर्मादिके तथा ॥५॥

बालानां चूडाकर्मादिके तथा (bālānāṃ cūḍākarmādike tathā) — the naming rite for children, the cūḍā rite, and the like,

VP 3.13.6

सीमन्तोन्नयने चैव पुत्रादिमुखदर्शने नान्दीमुखं पितृगणं पूजयेत् प्रयतो गृही ॥६॥

and at sīmantonnayana and the first sight of a son’s face, the householder, intent, should honor the Nandīmukha group of Pitṛs.

Viṣṇu Purāṇa

पितृगणं पूजयेत् प्रयतो गृही (pitṛgaṇaṃ pūjayet prayato gṛhī) — the householder, intent, should honor the Nandīmukha group of Pitṛs.
Transliteration

VP 3.13.7

पितृपूजाक्रमः प्रोक्तो वृद्धाव् एष समासतः श्रूयताम् अवनीपाल प्रेतकर्मक्रियाविधिः ॥७॥

The order of Pitṛ worship for increase has been stated in brief; hear, O king, the rule for the rites for the preta.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
पितृपूजाक्रमः प्रोक्तो वृद्धाव् एष (pitṛpūjākramaḥ prokto vṛddhāv eṣa) — The order of Pitṛ worship for increase has been stated in brief
समासतः श्रूयताम् अवनीपाल प्रेतकर्मक्रियाविधिः (samāsataḥ śrūyatām avanīpāla pretakarmakriyāvidhiḥ) — hear, O king, the rule for the rites for the preta.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

pitṛpūjākramaḥ prokto vṛddhāv eṣa samāsataḥ śrūyatām avanīpāla pretakarmakriyāvidhiḥ || 7 ||

VP 3.13.8

प्रेतदेहं शुभैः स्नानैः स्नापितं स्रग्विभूषितम् दग्ध्वा ग्रामाद् बहिः स्नात्वा सचैलाः सलिलाशये ॥८॥

Having bathed the preta’s body with auspicious baths and adorned it with a garland, they should burn it outside the village; clothed, they should bathe in a body of water.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
प्रेतदेहं शुभैः स्नानैः स्नापितं स्रग्विभूषितम् दग्ध्वा (pretadehaṃ śubhaiḥ snānaiḥ snāpitaṃ sragvibhūṣitam dagdhvā) — Having bathed the preta’s body with auspicious baths and adorned it with a garland
ग्रामाद् बहिः स्नात्वा सचैलाः सलिलाशये (grāmād bahiḥ snātvā sacailāḥ salilāśaye) — they should burn it outside the village; clothed, they should bathe in a body of water.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

pretadehaṃ śubhaiḥ snānaiḥ snāpitaṃ sragvibhūṣitam dagdhvā grāmād bahiḥ snātvā sacailāḥ salilāśaye || 8 ||

VP 3.13.9

यत्र तत्र स्थितायैतद् अमुकायेति वादिनः दक्षिणाभिमुखा दद्युर् बान्धवाः सलिलाञ्जलिम् ॥९॥

Wherever he stood, his relatives should face south and offer handfuls of water, saying, “This is for so-and-so.”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
यत्र तत्र स्थितायैतद् अमुकायेति वादिनः (yatra tatra sthitāyaitad amukāyeti vādinaḥ) — Wherever he stood, his relatives should face south
दक्षिणाभिमुखा दद्युर् बान्धवाः सलिलाञ्जलिम् (dakṣiṇābhimukhā dadyur bāndhavāḥ salilāñjalim) — offer handfuls of water, saying, “This is for so-and-so.”
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yatra tatra sthitāyaitad amukāyeti vādinaḥ dakṣiṇābhimukhā dadyur bāndhavāḥ salilāñjalim || 9 ||

VP 3.13.10

प्रविष्टाश् च समं गोभिर् ग्रामं नक्षत्रदर्शने कटधर्मांस् ततः कुर्युर् भूमौ प्रस्तरशायिनः ॥१०॥

At the sight of stars, they should enter the village together with the cows; then they should perform the kaṭa rites and lie on a spread upon the ground.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
प्रविष्टाश् च समं गोभिर् ग्रामं नक्षत्रदर्शने (praviṣṭāś ca samaṃ gobhir grāmaṃ nakṣatradarśane) — At the sight of stars, they should enter the village together with the cows
कटधर्मांस् ततः कुर्युर् भूमौ प्रस्तरशायिनः (kaṭadharmāṃs tataḥ kuryur bhūmau prastaraśāyinaḥ) — then they should perform the kaṭa rites and lie on a spread upon the ground.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

praviṣṭāś ca samaṃ gobhir grāmaṃ nakṣatradarśane kaṭadharmāṃs tataḥ kuryur bhūmau prastaraśāyinaḥ || 10 ||

VP 3.13.11

दातव्योऽनुदिनं पिण्डः प्रेताय भुवि पार्थिव दिवा च भक्तं भोक्तव्यम् अमांसं मनुजर्षभ ॥११॥

A piṇḍa should be given to the preta every day on the ground, O king; by day the meal should be eaten without meat, O best of men.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
दातव्योऽनुदिनं पिण्डः प्रेताय भुवि पार्थिव (dātavyo 'nudinaṃ piṇḍaḥ pretāya bhuvi pārthiva) — A piṇḍa should be given to the preta every day on the ground, O king
दिवा च भक्तं भोक्तव्यम् अमांसं मनुजर्षभ (divā ca bhaktaṃ bhoktavyam amāṃsaṃ manujarṣabha) — by day the meal should be eaten without meat, O best of men.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dātavyo 'nudinaṃ piṇḍaḥ pretāya bhuvi pārthiva divā ca bhaktaṃ bhoktavyam amāṃsaṃ manujarṣabha || 11 ||

VP 3.13.12

दिनानि तानि चेच्छातः कर्तव्यं विप्रभोजनम् प्रेतस् तृप्तिं तथा याति बन्धुवर्गेण भुञ्जता ॥१२॥

On those days, feeding brāhmaṇas is to be done if desired; the preta also reaches satisfaction when the group of relatives eats.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
दिनानि तानि चेच्छातः कर्तव्यं विप्रभोजनम् प्रेतस् (dināni tāni cecchātaḥ kartavyaṃ viprabhojanam pretas) — On those days, feeding brāhmaṇas is to be done if desired
तृप्तिं तथा याति बन्धुवर्गेण भुञ्जता (tṛptiṃ tathā yāti bandhuvargeṇa bhuñjatā) — the preta also reaches satisfaction when the group of relatives eats.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dināni tāni cecchātaḥ kartavyaṃ viprabhojanam pretas tṛptiṃ tathā yāti bandhuvargeṇa bhuñjatā || 12 ||

VP 3.13.13

प्रथमेऽह्नि तृतीये च सप्तमे नवमे तथा वस्त्रत्यागं बहिःस्नानं कृत्वा दद्यात् तिलोदकम् ॥१३॥

On the first, third, seventh, and ninth days, having cast off the garment and bathed outside, one should give sesame-water.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
प्रथमेऽह्नि तृतीये च सप्तमे नवमे (prathame 'hni tṛtīye ca saptame navame) — On the first, third, seventh, and ninth days, having cast off the garment
तथा वस्त्रत्यागं बहिःस्नानं कृत्वा दद्यात् तिलोदकम् (tathā vastratyāgaṃ bahiḥsnānaṃ kṛtvā dadyāt tilodakam) — bathed outside, one should give sesame-water.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

prathame 'hni tṛtīye ca saptame navame tathā vastratyāgaṃ bahiḥsnānaṃ kṛtvā dadyāt tilodakam || 13 ||

VP 3.13.14

चतुर्थेऽह्नि च कर्तव्यं भस्मास्थिचयनं नृप तदूर्ध्वम् अङ्गस्पर्शश् च सपिण्डानाम् अपीष्यते ॥१४॥

On the fourth day the collection of ashes and bones should be performed, O king; after that, bodily contact with the Sapindas is permitted.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

तदूर्ध्वम् अङ्गस्पर्शश् च सपिण्डानाम् अपीष्यते (tadūrdhvam aṅgasparśaś ca sapiṇḍānām apīṣyate) — O king; after that, bodily contact with the Sapindas is permitted.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

caturthe 'hni ca kartavyaṃ bhasmāsthicayanaṃ nṛpa tadūrdhvam aṅgasparśaś ca sapiṇḍānām apīṣyate || 14 ||

VP 3.13.15

योग्याः सर्वक्रियाणां तु समानसलिलास् तथा अनुलेपनपुष्पादिभोगाद् अन्यत्र पार्थिव ॥१५॥

Viṣṇu Purāṇa

तथा अनुलेपनपुष्पादिभोगाद् अन्यत्र पार्थिव (tathā anulepanapuṣpādibhogād anyatra pārthiva) — except the use of unguents, flowers, and the like, O king.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

VP 3.13.16

शय्यासनोपभोगश् च सपिण्डानाम् अपीष्यते भस्मास्थिचयनाद् ऊर्ध्वं संयोगो न तु योषिताम् ॥१६॥

The use of beds and seats is permitted for Sapindas after the collection of ashes and bones; union with women is not.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
शय्यासनोपभोगश् च सपिण्डानाम् अपीष्यते भस्मास्थिचयनाद् (śayyāsanopabhogaś ca sapiṇḍānām apīṣyate bhasmāsthicayanād) — The use of beds and seats is permitted for Sapindas after the collection of ashes
ऊर्ध्वं संयोगो न तु योषिताम् (ūrdhvaṃ saṃyogo na tu yoṣitām) — bones; union with women is not.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

śayyāsanopabhogaś ca sapiṇḍānām apīṣyate bhasmāsthicayanād ūrdhvaṃ saṃyogo na tu yoṣitām || 16 ||

VP 3.13.17

बाले देशान्तरस्थे च पतिते च मुनौ मृते सद्यः शौचं तथेच्छातो जलाग्न्युद्बन्धनादिषु ॥१७॥

When a child, one living in another land, a fallen person, or a sage dies, purification is immediate; likewise by choice in drowning, fire, hanging, and the like.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
बाले देशान्तरस्थे च पतिते च मुनौ (bāle deśāntarasthe ca patite ca munau) — When a child, one living in another land, a fallen person, or a sage dies
मृते सद्यः शौचं तथेच्छातो जलाग्न्युद्बन्धनादिषु (mṛte sadyaḥ śaucaṃ tathecchāto jalāgnyudbandhanādiṣu) — purification is immediate; likewise by choice in drowning, fire, hanging, and the like.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bāle deśāntarasthe ca patite ca munau mṛte sadyaḥ śaucaṃ tathecchāto jalāgnyudbandhanādiṣu || 17 ||

VP 3.13.18

मृतबन्धोर् दशाहानि कुलस्यान्नं न भुज्यते दानं प्रतिग्रहो यज्ञः स्वाध्यायश् च निवर्तते ॥१८॥

For ten days after a relative’s death, the family’s food is not eaten; giving, receiving, sacrifice, and Veda study cease.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
मृतबन्धोर् दशाहानि कुलस्यान्नं न भुज्यते दानं (mṛtabandhor daśāhāni kulasyānnaṃ na bhujyate dānaṃ) — For ten days after a relative’s death, the family’s food is not eaten
प्रतिग्रहो यज्ञः स्वाध्यायश् च निवर्तते (pratigraho yajñaḥ svādhyāyaś ca nivartate) — giving, receiving, sacrifice, and Veda study cease.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

mṛtabandhor daśāhāni kulasyānnaṃ na bhujyate dānaṃ pratigraho yajñaḥ svādhyāyaś ca nivartate || 18 ||

VP 3.13.37

पूर्वाः क्रियास् तु कर्तव्याः पुत्राद्यैर् एव चोत्तराः दौहित्रैर् वा नरश्रेष्ठ कार्यास् तत्तनयैस् तथा ॥३७॥

The former rites should be performed by the son and the others; the later by daughters’ sons, O best of men, and likewise by their descendants.

VP 3.13.38

मृताहनि च कर्तव्याः स्त्रीणाम् अप्य् उत्तराः क्रियाः प्रतिसंवत्सरं राजन्न् एकोद्दिष्टविधानतः ॥३८॥

The later rites for women too should be performed on the day of death, every year, according to the Ekoddiṣṭa procedure, O king.

VP 3.13.39

तस्माद् उत्तरसंज्ञा याः क्रियास् ताः शृणु पार्थिव यदा यदा च कर्तव्या विधिना येन चानघ ॥३९॥

Therefore hear the rites called the later rites, O king, and when and by which rule they are to be performed, O sinless one.
