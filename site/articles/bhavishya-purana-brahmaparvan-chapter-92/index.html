Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 92 — The Great White Observance

BP 1.92.1

ब्रह्मोवाच । यस्त्वादित्यग्रहस्यास्य वारो देवस्य सुव्रत । शस्यः प्रोक्तो प्रियो लोके ख्यातो गोश्रुतिभूषणः ॥ ॥

Brahmā said. The day of this solar observance, O good vowed one, is declared praiseworthy, dear in the world, and famed as an ornament of Gośruti.

brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said;
yaḥ tu — whoever, indeed;
āditya-grahasya asya — of this Āditya observance;
vāraḥ — day;
devasya — of the deity;
suvrata — O well-disciplined one;
śasyaḥ — auspicious, praiseworthy;
proktaḥ — proclaimed;
priyaḥ — dear;
loke — in the world;
khyātaḥ — famed;
gośruti-bhūṣaṇaḥ — Gośruti-bhūṣaṇa.

BP 1.92.2

यस्तु पूजयते तस्मिन् पतङ्गं पतगप्रियम् । गन्धपुष्पोपहारैस्तु सूर्यलोकं स गच्छति ॥ ॥

Whoever worships the Sun there, the winged one beloved of birds, with perfumes, flowers, and offerings goes to the world of the Sun.

yaḥ tu — whoever;
pūjayate — worships;
tasmin — on that [day];
pataṅgam — Pataṅga, the Sun;
pataga-priyam — beloved of birds;
gandha-puṣpa-upahāraiḥ — with fragrances, flowers, and offerings;
tu — indeed;
sūrya-lokam — to the world of Sūrya;
saḥ — he;
gacchati — goes.

BP 1.92.3

सोपवासो गणश्रेष्ठ आदित्यग्रहणे शुचिः । जपमानो महाश्वेतां खपोषमथ वा शिवम् ॥ ॥

Fasting and pure during the solar eclipse, O chief of hosts, he should recite the Great White One, the Sky Nourisher, or Śiva.

sa-upavāsaḥ — with fasting;
gaṇa-śreṣṭha — O best among the hosts;
āditya-grahaṇe — at the Āditya observance;
śuciḥ — pure;
japamānaḥ — reciting;
mahāśvetām — Mahāśvetā;
khapoṣam — Khapoṣa;
atha vā — or else;
śivam — Śiva.

BP 1.92.4

पूजयेज्जगतामीशं तमोनाशनमाशुगम् । पूजयित्वा खपोषं तु महाश्वेतां ततो जपेत् ॥ ॥

He should worship the Lord of the worlds, destroyer of darkness, swift moving. Having worshipped the Sky Nourisher, he should then recite the Great White One.

pūjayet — should worship;
jagatām īśam — Lord of the worlds;
tamaḥ-nāśanam — destroyer of darkness;
āśu-gam — swift-going;
pūjayitvā — having worshiped;
khapoṣam — Khapoṣa;
tu — indeed;
mahāśvetām — Mahāśvetā;
tataḥ — then;
japet — should recite.

BP 1.92.5

पूजयित्वा महाश्वेतां रविं देवं समर्चयेत् । महाश्वेतां प्रतिष्ठाप्य गन्धपुष्पैः सुपूजिताम् ॥ ॥

He should fully worship the god Sun after worshipping the Great White One. He should install the Great White One, well worshipped with perfumes and flowers.

pūjayitvā — having worshiped;
mahāśvetām — Mahāśvetā;
ravim — Ravi;
devam — the deity;
samarcayet — should worship fully;
mahāśvetām — Mahāśvetā;
pratiṣṭhāpya — having installed;
gandha-puṣpaiḥ — with fragrances and flowers;
su-pūjitām — well worshiped.

BP 1.92.6

तस्या एव बहिः कार्यं स्थण्डिलं सुसमाहितः । शुचौ भूमिविभागे तु वीरं संस्थाप्य यत्नतः ॥ ॥

Outside it, with collected mind, he should carefully make a square altar. On a clean part of the ground he should carefully install the hero.

tasyāḥ eva — of that very one;
bahiḥ — outside;
kāryam — to be made;
sthaṇḍilam — an altar;
su-samāhitaḥ — with a well-collected mind;
śucau — in a clean;
bhūmi-vibhāge — portion of ground;
tu — indeed;
vīram — the hero;
saṃsthāpya — having established;
yatnataḥ — carefully.

BP 1.92.7

कुर्याद्धोमं तिलैः स्नातः सर्पिषा च विशेषतः । आदित्यग्रहवेलायां जपेञ्छ्वेतां महामते ॥ ॥

Having bathed, he should perform a fire offering with sesame, especially with ghee. At the time of the solar eclipse, O great minded one, he should recite the White One.

kuryāt — should perform;
homam — fire offering;
tilaiḥ — with sesame;
snātaḥ — having bathed;
sarpiṣā — with ghee;
ca — and;
viśeṣataḥ — especially;
āditya-graha-velāyām — at the time of the Āditya observance;
japet — should recite;
śvetām — Śvetā;
mahāmate — O great-minded one.

BP 1.92.8

मुक्ते दिनकरे पश्चात्स्नानं कृत्वा समाहितः । पूजयित्वा महाश्वेतां खगोल्कं च ग्रहाधिपम् ॥ ॥

After the Sun has been released from the eclipse, having bathed and composed himself, he should worship the Great White One, the celestial sphere, and the lord of planets.

mukte — when released;
dinakare — Dīnakara, the Sun;
paścāt — afterward;
snānam — bathing;
kṛtvā — having performed;
samāhitaḥ — composed, concentrated;
pūjayitvā — having worshiped;
mahāśvetām — Mahāśvetā;
khagolkam — Khagolka;
ca — and;
graha-adhipam — lord of the planets.

BP 1.92.9

ब्राह्मणान् वाचयित्वा च ततो भुञ्जीत वाग्यतः । आदित्यग्रहयुक्तेऽस्मिन् वारे त्रिपुरसूदन ॥ ॥

Having had the Brahmins recite, he should then eat with speech restrained on this day joined with the solar eclipse, O destroyer of Tripura.

brāhmaṇān — the Brāhmaṇas;
vācayitvā — having caused [them] to recite;
ca — and;
tataḥ — then;
bhuñjīta — should eat;
vāk-yataḥ — with speech restrained;
āditya-graha-yukte — joined with the Āditya observance;
asmin vāre — on this day;
tripura-sūdana — O destroyer of Tripura.

BP 1.92.10

यत्कर्म क्रियते पुण्यं तत्सर्वं शुभदं भवेत् । स्नानदानजपादीनां कर्मणां गोवृषध्वज ॥ ॥

Whatever meritorious act is done becomes auspicious, including bathing, giving, recitation, and the other acts, O Bull Bannered One.

yat — whatever;
karma — act;
kriyate — is performed;
puṇyam — meritorious;
tat sarvam — all that;
śubha-dam — giving auspiciousness;
bhavet — becomes;
snāna-dāna-japa-ādīnām — of bathing, giving, recitation, and other acts;
karmaṇām — of actions;
go-vṛṣa-dhvaja — O bull-bannered one.

BP 1.92.11

अनन्तं हि फलं तेषां भवत्यस्मिन्न संशयः । कृतानां तु गणश्रेष्ठ भास्करस्य वचो यथा ॥ ॥

The fruit of those acts performed becomes endless here, without doubt, O chief of hosts, according to the word of Bhāskara.

anantam — endless;
hi — indeed;
phalam — fruit;
teṣām — of those;
bhavati — becomes;
asmin — in this [observance];
na saṃśayaḥ — there is no doubt;
kṛtānām — of acts performed;
tu — indeed;
gaṇa-śreṣṭha — O best among the hosts;
bhāskarasya vacaḥ — Bhāskara’s word;
yathā — according to.

BP 1.92.12

तस्माद्द्विजगणैः कार्यं पुण्यकर्मविचक्षणैः । एकभक्तं च नक्तं च उपवासं गणाधिप ॥ ॥

Therefore the groups of Brahmins, discerning in meritorious acts, should observe one meal, a night meal, and a fast, O lord of hosts.

tasmāt — therefore;
dvija-gaṇaiḥ — by groups of the twice-born;
kāryam — to be performed;
puṇya-karma-vicakṣaṇaiḥ — discerning in meritorious acts;
eka-bhaktam — one meal;
ca — and;
naktam — night observance;
ca — and;
upavāsam — fasting;
gaṇādhipa — O lord of hosts.

BP 1.92.13

ये वादित्यदिने कुर्युस् ते यान्ति परमं पदम् । धर्म्यं पुण्यं यशस्यं च पुत्रीयं कामदं तथा ॥ ॥

Those who perform these on the day of the Sun attain the highest state, righteousness, merit, fame, sons, and fulfillment of desires.

ye — those who;
vā — indeed;
āditya-dine — on Āditya’s day;
kuryuḥ — perform;
te — they;
yānti — go, attain;
paramam padam — the supreme state;
dharmyam — righteousness;
puṇyam — merit;
yaśasyam — fame;
ca — and;
putrīyam — producing sons;
kāma-dam — granting desires;
tathā — likewise.

BP 1.92.14

तस्मिन्दानमपूपस्य गोदानेन समं भवेत् । द्वादशैते महाबाहो वीरभानोर्महात्मनः ॥ ॥

In this observance, the gift of a cake should be equal to the gift of a cow. These twelve belong to the great souled Vīrabhānu, O mighty armed one.

tasmin — in that;
dānam — gift;
apūpasya — of an apūpa cake;
go-dānena — with the gift of a cow;
samam — equal;
bhavet — becomes;
dvādaśa — twelve;
ete — these;
mahābāho — O mighty-armed one;
vīra-bhānoḥ — of Vīrabhānu;
mahātmanaḥ — of the great-souled one.

BP 1.92.15

तुष्टिदाः कथितास् तुभ्यं सर्वपापभयापहाः । पठतां शृण्वतां तात कुर्वतां च विशेषतः ॥ ॥

They are said to give satisfaction and remove all sin and fear for those who read, hear, and especially perform them, dear son.

tuṣṭi-dāḥ — givers of satisfaction;
kathitāḥ — declared;
tubhyam — to you;
sarva-pāpa-bhaya-apahāḥ — removers of every sin and fear;
paṭhatām — of those reading;
śṛṇvatām — of those hearing;
tāta — O dear one;
kurvatām — of those performing;
ca — and;
viśeṣataḥ — especially.

BP 1.92.16

कृत्वैकमेषां विधिवद् वारं वृषभवाहन । वृषादित्रितयं प्राप्य चात्रिजामचलां तथा ॥ ॥

Having properly performed one of these on its appointed day, O Bull Rider, one attains the triad beginning with the Bull and also the immovable state born of Atri.

kṛtvā — having performed;
ekam — one;
eṣām — of these;
vidhi-vat — according to the rule;
vāram — observance, day;
vṛṣabha-vāhana — O bull-bannered one;
vṛṣa-ādi-tritayam — the triad beginning with Vṛṣa;
prāpya — having attained;
ca — and;
ātrijām — born of Atri;
acalām — immovable [state];
tathā — likewise.

BP 1.92.17

ततो याति परं लोकं वृषकेतो महात्मनः । तेजसाम्बुजसंकाशः प्रभयाण्डजसन्निभः ॥ ॥

Then he goes to the highest world of the great souled Bull Bannered One, radiant as a lotus and bright like one born from an egg.

tataḥ — then;
yāti — goes;
param lokam — to the supreme world;
vṛṣa-ketoḥ — of Vṛṣaketu;
mahātmanaḥ — of the great-souled one;
tejasā — in radiance;
ambuja-saṃkāśaḥ — resembling a lotus;
prabhayā — in splendor;
aṇḍaja-sannibhaḥ — resembling an egg-born being.

BP 1.92.18

पत्रिहेतिसमो वीर्ये कान्त्या चन्द्रसमप्रभः ॥ ॥

In strength he is equal to the weapon of the winged one. In radiance he shines like the moon.

patri-heti-samaḥ — equal to the weapon of the winged one;
vīrye — in valor;
kāntyā — in radiance;
candra-sama-prabhaḥ — shining like the moon.
