VP 3.4.1

आद्यो वेदश्चतुष्पादः शतसाहस्रसंमितः ततो दशगुणः कृत्स्नो यज्ञोऽयं सर्वकामधुक् ॥१॥

The first Veda is four-footed, measured at one hundred thousand. This entire yajña is tenfold and fulfills every desire.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

सर्वकामधुक् (sarvakāmadhuk) — fulfiller of every desire
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ādyo vedaś catuṣpādaḥ śatasāhasrasaṃmitaḥ tato daśaguṇaḥ kṛtsno yajño 'yaṃ sarvakāmadhuk ||1||

VP 3.4.2

VP 3.4.5
