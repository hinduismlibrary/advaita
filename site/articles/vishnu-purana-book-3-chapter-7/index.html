VP 3.7.1

यथावत् कथितं सर्वं यत् पृष्टोऽसि मया गुरो श्रोतुम् इच्छाम्य् अहं त्व् एकं तद् भवान् प्रब्रवीतु मे ॥१॥

You have told me everything that I asked, just as it is, O guru. I wish to hear one thing; may you tell it to me.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
यथावत् कथितं सर्वं (yathāvat kathitaṃ sarvaṃ) — everything was told just as it is
यत् पृष्टोऽसि मया गुरो (yat pṛṣṭo 'si mayā guro) — what you were asked by me, O guru
श्रोतुम् इच्छाम्य् अहं त्व् एकं (śrotum icchāmy ahaṃ tv ekaṃ) — I wish to hear one thing
तद् भवान् प्रब्रवीतु मे (tad bhavān prabravītu me) — that, may you tell to me
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yathāvat kathitaṃ sarvaṃ yat pṛṣṭo 'si mayā guro śrotum icchāmy ahaṃ tv ekaṃ tad bhavān prabravītu me ||१||

VP 3.7.2

सप्तद्वीपानि पातालवीथ्यश् च सुमहामुने सप्त लोकाश् च येऽन्तःस्था ब्रह्माण्डस्यास्य सर्वतः ॥२॥

The seven islands, the paths of Pātāla, and the seven worlds situated everywhere within this cosmic egg, O great sage.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
सप्तद्वीपानि पातालवीथ्यश् च सुमहामुने (saptadvīpāni pātālavīthyaś ca sumahāmune) — seven islands and paths of Pātāla, O great sage
सप्त लोकाश् च येऽन्तःस्था (sapta lokāś ca ye 'ntaḥsthā) — and seven worlds that are situated within
ब्रह्माण्डस्यास्य सर्वतः (brahmāṇḍasyāsya sarvataḥ) — everywhere in this cosmic egg
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

saptadvīpāni pātālavīthyaś ca sumahāmune sapta lokāś ca ye 'ntaḥsthā brahmāṇḍasyāsya sarvataḥ ||२||

VP 3.7.3

स्थूलैः सूक्ष्मैस् तथासूक्ष्मैः सूक्ष्मात् सूक्ष्मतरैस् तथा स्थूलैः स्थूलतरैश् चैतत् सर्वं प्राणिभिर् आवृतम् ॥३॥

All this is covered by beings gross, subtle, very subtle, subtler than subtle, and grosser than gross.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
स्थूलैः सूक्ष्मैस् तथासूक्ष्मैः (sthūlaiḥ sūkṣmais tathāsūkṣmaiḥ) — by gross, subtle, and very subtle
सूक्ष्मात् सूक्ष्मतरैस् तथा (sūkṣmāt sūkṣmatarais tathā) — from subtle, subtler likewise
स्थूलैः स्थूलतरैश् चैतत् सर्वं प्राणिभिर् आवृतम् (sthūlaiḥ sthūlataraiś caitat sarvaṃ prāṇibhir āvṛtam) — all this is covered by beings gross and still grosser
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sthūlaiḥ sūkṣmais tathāsūkṣmaiḥ sūkṣmāt sūkṣmatarais tathā sthūlaiḥ sthūlataraiś caitat sarvaṃ prāṇibhir āvṛtam ||३||

VP 3.7.4

अङ्गुलस्याष्टभागोऽपि न सोऽस्ति मुनिसत्तम न सन्ति प्राणिनो यत्र कर्मबन्धनिबन्धनाः ॥४॥

There is no place even an eighth part of a finger where beings bound by the bonds of karma are absent, O best of sages.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
अङ्गुलस्याष्टभागोऽपि (aṅgulasyāṣṭabhāgo 'pi) — even an eighth part of a finger
न सोऽस्ति मुनिसत्तम (na so 'sti munisattama) — does not exist, O best of sages
न सन्ति प्राणिनो यत्र कर्मबन्धनिबन्धनाः (na santi prāṇino yatra karmabandhanibandhanāḥ) — where beings bound by the bonds of karma are not
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

aṅgulasyāṣṭabhāgo 'pi na so 'sti munisattama na santi prāṇino yatra karmabandhanibandhanāḥ ||४||

VP 3.7.5

सर्वे चैते वशं यान्ति यमस्य भगवन् किल आयुषोऽन्ते ततो यान्ति यातनास् तत्प्रचोदिताः ॥५॥

All these indeed go under Yama’s control, O Lord. At the end of their lives they then go to torments, impelled by him.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
सर्वे चैते वशं यान्ति यमस्य (sarve caite vaśaṃ yānti yamasya) — all these go under Yama’s control
भगवन् किल (bhagavan kila) — O Lord, indeed
आयुषोऽन्ते ततो यान्ति (āyuṣo 'nte tato yānti) — at the end of life they then go
यातनास् तत्प्रचोदिताः (yātanās tatpracoditāḥ) — to torments impelled by him
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sarve caite vaśaṃ yānti yamasya bhagavan kila āyuṣo 'nte tato yānti yātanās tatpracoditāḥ ||५||

VP 3.7.6

यातनाभ्यः परिभ्रष्टा देवाद्यास्व् अथ योनिषु जन्तवः परिवर्तन्ते शास्त्राणाम् एष निर्णयः ॥६॥

Having fallen from the torments, beings then pass among wombs beginning with those of the gods; this is the determination of the śāstras.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
यातनाभ्यः परिभ्रष्टा (yātanābhyaḥ paribhraṣṭā) — having fallen from the torments
देवाद्यास्व् अथ योनिषु (devādyāsv atha yoniṣu) — then among wombs beginning with those of gods
जन्तवः परिवर्तन्ते (jantavaḥ parivartante) — beings pass around
शास्त्राणाम् एष निर्णयः (śāstrāṇām eṣa nirṇayaḥ) — this is the decision of the śāstras
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yātanābhyaḥ paribhraṣṭā devādyāsv atha yoniṣu jantavaḥ parivartante śāstrāṇām eṣa nirṇayaḥ ||६||

VP 3.7.7

सोऽहम् इच्छामि तच् छ्रोतुं यमस्य वशवर्तिनः न भवन्ति नरा येन तत् कर्म कथयामलम् ॥७॥

So I wish to hear that action by which people do not become subject to Yama. Tell it fully to me.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
सोऽहम् इच्छामि तच् छ्रोतुं (so 'ham icchāmi tac chrotuṃ) — so I wish to hear that
यमस्य वशवर्तिनः न भवन्ति नरा येन (yamasya vaśavartinaḥ na bhavanti narā yena) — by which people do not become subject to Yama
तत् कर्म कथयामलम् (tat karma kathayāmalam) — tell that action fully to me
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

so 'ham icchāmi tac chrotuṃ yamasya vaśavartinaḥ na bhavanti narā yena tat karma kathayāmalam ||७||

VP 3.7.8

अयम् एव मुने प्रश्नो नकुलेन महात्मना पृष्टः पितामहः प्राह भीष्मो यत् तच् छृणुष्व मे ॥८॥

This very question was asked by the great-souled Nakula; grandfather Bhīṣma spoke [the answer]. Hear from me what he said.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
अयम् एव मुने प्रश्नो नकुलेन महात्मना (ayam eva mune praśno nakulena mahātmanā) — this very question by the great-souled Nakula
पृष्टः पितामहः प्राह भीष्मो (pṛṣṭaḥ pitāmahaḥ prāha bhīṣmo) — grandfather Bhīṣma, asked, spoke
यत् तच् छृणुष्व मे (yat tac chṛṇuṣva me) — what he said, hear from me
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ayam eva mune praśno nakulena mahātmanā pṛṣṭaḥ pitāmahaḥ prāha bhīṣmo yat tac chṛṇuṣva me ||८||

VP 3.7.9

पुरा ममागतो वत्स सखा कालिङ्गको द्विजः माम् उवाच स पृष्टो वै मया जातिस्मरो मुनिः ॥९॥

Long ago my friend, a twice-born man from Kaliṅga, came to me, dear child. He said to me: “I indeed questioned a sage who remembers former births.”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
पुरा ममागतो वत्स सखा कालिङ्गको द्विजः (purā mamāgato vatsa sakhā kāliṅgako dvijaḥ) — formerly my friend, a twice-born man from Kaliṅga, came to me, dear child
माम् उवाच स (mām uvāca sa) — he said to me
पृष्टो वै मया जातिस्मरो मुनिः (pṛṣṭo vai mayā jātismaro muniḥ) — “I indeed questioned a sage who remembers former births”
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

purā mamāgato vatsa sakhā kāliṅgako dvijaḥ mām uvāca sa pṛṣṭo vai mayā jātismaro muniḥ ||९||

VP 3.7.10

तेनाख्यातम् इदं चेदम् इत्थं चैतद् भविष्यति तथा च तद् अभूद् वत्स यथोक्तं तेन धीमता ॥१०॥

He told me this: “This will happen in this way.” And so it happened, dear child, as that wise one had said.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
तेनाख्यातम् इदं चेदम् (tenākhyātam idaṃ cedam) — by him this was told to me
इत्थं चैतद् भविष्यति (itthaṃ caitad bhaviṣyati) — “and this will happen in this way”
तथा च तद् अभूद् वत्स (tathā ca tad abhūd vatsa) — and so it happened, dear child
यथोक्तं तेन धीमता (yathoktaṃ tena dhīmatā) — as it was said by that wise one
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tenākhyātam idaṃ cedam itthaṃ caitad bhaviṣyati tathā ca tad abhūd vatsa yathoktaṃ tena dhīmatā ||१०||

VP 3.7.11

स पृष्टश् च मया भूयः श्रद्दधानेन वै द्विजः यद् यद् आह न तद् दृष्टम् अन्यथा हि मया क्वचित् ॥११॥

I questioned him again, O twice-born, trusting him; whatever he said, I never saw it otherwise anywhere.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
स पृष्टश् च मया भूयः (sa pṛṣṭaś ca mayā bhūyaḥ) — he was questioned again by me
श्रद्दधानेन वै द्विजः (śraddadhānena vai dvijaḥ) — with trust, O twice-born
यद् यद् आह (yad yad āha) — whatever he said
न तद् दृष्टम् अन्यथा हि मया क्वचित् (na tad dṛṣṭam anyathā hi mayā kvacit) — I never saw it otherwise anywhere
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sa pṛṣṭaś ca mayā bhūyaḥ śraddadhānena vai dvijaḥ yad yad āha na tad dṛṣṭam anyathā hi mayā kvacit ||११||

VP 3.7.12

एकदा तु मया पृष्टम् यद् एतद् भवतोदितम् प्राह कालिङ्गको विप्रः स्मृत्वा तस्य मुनेर् वचः ॥१२॥

Once I asked about this very thing that you have said; the Brahmin from Kaliṅga spoke, remembering the words of that sage.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
एकदा तु मया पृष्टम् (ekadā tu mayā pṛṣṭam) — once, when I asked
यद् एतद् भवतोदितम् (yad etad bhavatoditam) — about this which you have said
प्राह कालिङ्गको विप्रः (prāha kāliṅgako vipraḥ) — the Brahmin from Kaliṅga spoke
स्मृत्वा तस्य मुनेर् वचः (smṛtvā tasya muner vacaḥ) — remembering the words of that sage
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ekadā tu mayā pṛṣṭam yad etad bhavatoditam prāha kāliṅgako vipraḥ smṛtvā tasya muner vacaḥ ||१२||

VP 3.7.13

जातिस्मरेण कथितं रहस्यं परमं मम यमकिंकरयोर् योऽभूत् संवादस् तं ब्रवीमि ते ॥१३॥

The sage who remembers former births told me the supreme secret: the dialogue between Yama and his attendant. I tell it to you.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
जातिस्मरेण कथितं (jātismareṇa kathitaṃ) — told by the rememberer of former births
रहस्यं परमं मम (rahasyaṃ paramaṃ mama) — my supreme secret
यमकिंकरयोर् योऽभूत् संवादस् (yamakiṃkarayor yo 'bhūt saṃvādas) — the dialogue that was between Yama and his attendant
तं ब्रवीमि ते (taṃ bravīmi te) — that I tell to you
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

jātismareṇa kathitaṃ rahasyaṃ paramaṃ mama yamakiṃkarayor yo 'bhūt saṃvādas taṃ bravīmi te ||१३||

VP 3.7.14

स्वपुरुषम् अभिवीक्ष्य पाशहस्तं वदति यमः किल तस्य कर्णमूले परिहर मधुसूदनप्रपन्नान् प्रभुर् अहम् अन्यनृणां न वैष्णवानाम् ॥१४॥

Seeing his own man holding a noose, Yama indeed spoke in his ear: “Keep away from those surrendered to Madhusūdana. I am the lord of other men, not of Vaiṣṇavas.”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
स्वपुरुषम् अभिवीक्ष्य पाशहस्तं (svapuruṣam abhivīkṣya pāśahastaṃ) — seeing his own man holding a noose
वदति यमः किल तस्य कर्णमूले (vadati yamaḥ kila tasya karṇamūle) — Yama indeed speaks into his ear
परिहर मधुसूदनप्रपन्नान् (parihara madhusūdanaprapannān) — keep away from those surrendered to Madhusūdana
प्रभुर् अहम् अन्यनृणां न वैष्णवानाम् (prabhur aham anyanṛṇāṃ na vaiṣṇavānām) — I am lord of other men, not of Vaiṣṇavas
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

svapuruṣam abhivīkṣya pāśahastaṃ vadati yamaḥ kila tasya karṇamūle parihara madhusūdanaprapannān prabhur aham anyanṛṇāṃ na vaiṣṇavānām ||१४||

VP 3.7.15

अहम् अमरगणार्चितेन धात्रा यम इति लोकहिताहिते नियुक्तः हरिगुरुवशगोऽस्मि न स्वतन्त्रः प्रभवति संयमने ममापि विष्णुः ॥१५॥

I was appointed by the Creator, worshipped by the hosts of immortals, as Yama, over the welfare and harm of the world. I am under the power of Hari, my guru; I am not independent. Viṣṇu has power to restrain even me.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
अहम् अमरगणार्चितेन धात्रा यम इति (aham amaragaṇārcitena dhātrā yama iti) — I, by the Creator worshipped by hosts of immortals, as Yama
लोकहिताहिते नियुक्तः (lokahitāhite niyuktaḥ) — appointed over the welfare and harm of the world
हरिगुरुवशगोऽस्मि न स्वतन्त्रः (hariguruvaśago 'smi na svatantraḥ) — I am under Hari my guru’s power, not independent
प्रभवति संयमने ममापि विष्णुः (prabhavati saṃyamane mamāpi viṣṇuḥ) — Viṣṇu has power to restrain even me
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

aham amaragaṇārcitena dhātrā yama iti lokahitāhite niyuktaḥ hariguruvaśago 'smi na svatantraḥ prabhavati saṃyamane mamāpi viṣṇuḥ ||१५||

VP 3.7.16

कटकमुकुटकर्णिकादिभेदैः कनकम् अभेदम् अपीष्यते यथैकम् सुरपशुमनुजादिकल्पनाभिर् हरिर् अखिलाभिर् उदीर्यते तथैकः ॥१६॥

Just as gold, though undivided, is considered one through distinctions such as bracelet, crown, earring, and the like, so Hari, though one, is spoken of through all the forms of gods, animals, humans, and the rest.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
कटकमुकुटकर्णिकादिभेदैः (kaṭakamukuṭakarṇikādibhedaiḥ) — by distinctions of bracelets, crowns, earrings, and the like
कनकम् अभेदम् अपीष्यते यथैकम् (kanakam abhedam apīṣyate yathaikam) — gold, though undivided, is regarded as one
सुरपशुमनुजादिकल्पनाभिर् (surapaśumanujādikalpanābhir) — through conceptions of gods, animals, humans, and the rest
हरिर् अखिलाभिर् उदीर्यते तथैकः (harir akhilābhir udīryate tathaikaḥ) — so Hari, one, is spoken of through all
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kaṭakamukuṭakarṇikādibhedaiḥ kanakam abhedam apīṣyate yathaikam surapaśumanujādikalpanābhir harir akhilābhir udīryate tathaikaḥ ||१६||

VP 3.7.17

क्षितिजलपरमाणवोऽनिलान्ते पुनर् अपि यान्ति यथैकतां धरित्र्याः सुरपशुमनुजादयस् तथान्ते गुणकलुषेण सनातनेन तेन ॥१७॥

Just as the atoms of earth again reach oneness with earth when the wind ends, so at the end gods, animals, humans, and the rest [become one] with that eternal one, sullied by the guṇas.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
क्षितिजलपरमाणवोऽनिलान्ते (kṣitijalaparamāṇavo 'nilānte) — atoms of earth when the wind ends
पुनर् अपि यान्ति यथैकतां धरित्र्याः (punar api yānti yathaikatāṃ dharitryāḥ) — again reach oneness with earth
सुरपशुमनुजादयस् तथान्ते (surapaśumanujādayas tathānte) — so gods, animals, humans, and the rest at the end
गुणकलुषेण सनातनेन तेन (guṇakaluṣeṇa sanātanena tena) — with that eternal one sullied by the guṇas
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
