VP 1.15.1

तपश् चरत्सु पृथिवीं प्रचेतःसु महीरुहाः । अरक्ष्यमाणाम् आबब्रुर् बभूवाथ प्रजाक्षयः ॥१॥

While the Pracetases were performing tapas on earth, trees covered the unprotected earth; then the people perished.
