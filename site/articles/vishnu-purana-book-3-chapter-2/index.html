VP 3.2.1

प्रोक्तान्येतानि भवता सप्त मन्वन्तराणि वै भविष्याण्यपि विप्रर्षे ममाख्यातुं त्वमर्हसि ॥१॥

These seven Manvantaras have been told by you, O Brahmin seer. Tell me also the future ones.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
प्रोक्तान्येतानि भवता (proktāny etāni bhavatā) — these have been told by you
सप्त मन्वन्तराणि वै (sapta manvantarāṇi vai) — the seven Manvantaras indeed
भविष्याण्यपि विप्रर्षे (bhaviṣyāṇy api viprarṣe) — future ones too, O Brahmin seer
ममाख्यातुं त्वमर्हसि (mamākhyātuṃ tvam arhasi) — you should tell me
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

proktāny etāni bhavatā sapta manvantarāṇi vai bhaviṣyāṇy api viprarṣe mamākhyātuṃ tvam arhasi ||1||

VP 3.2.2

सूर्यस्य पत्नी संज्ञाभूत्तनया विश्वकर्मणः मनुर्यमो यमी चैव तदपत्यानि वै मुने ॥२॥

Saṃjñā, daughter of Viśvakarman, was the Sun's wife. Manu, Yama, and Yamī were her children, O sage.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

संज्ञाभूत्तनया विश्वकर्मणः (saṃjñābhūt tanayā viśvakarmaṇaḥ) — Saṃjñā was, daughter of Viśvakarman
मनुर्यमो यमी चैव (manur yamo yamī caiva) — Manu, Yama, and Yamī
तदपत्यानि वै मुने (tadapatyāni vai mune) — are indeed her children, O sage
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sūryasya patnī saṃjñābhūt tanayā viśvakarmaṇaḥ manur yamo yamī caiva tadapatyāni vai mune ||2||

VP 3.2.3

असहन्ती तु सा भर्तुस्तेजश्छायां युयोज वै भर्तुः शुश्रूषणेऽरण्यं स्वयं च तपसे ययौ ॥३॥

She, unable to bear her husband's radiance, placed a shadow in her husband's service; she herself went to the forest for tapas.

Word-for-word

भर्तुः शुश्रूषणेऽरण्यं (bhartuḥ śuśrūṣaṇe 'raṇyaṃ) — for the husband's service, to the forest
स्वयं च तपसे ययौ (svayaṃ ca tapase yayau) — she herself went for austerity
Transliteration

VP 3.2.5

तदान्येयमसौ बुद्धिरित्यासीद् (tadānyeyam asau buddhir ity āsīd) — then the thought arose that she was another woman

Transliteration

VP 3.2.9

भ्रममारोप्य सूर्यं तु तस्य तेजोविशातनम्कृतवानष्टमं भागं न व्यशातयताव्ययम् ॥९॥

Word-for-word

Transliteration

यत्सूर्याद्वैष्णवं तेजः शातितं विश्वकर्मणा जाज्वल्यमानमपतत्तद्भूमौ मुनिसत्तम ॥१०॥

VP 3.2.13

VP 3.2.18

विष्णुप्रसादादनघः पातालान्तरगोचरः विरोचनसुतस्तेषां बलिरिन्द्रो भविष्यति ॥१८॥

VP 3.2.61

The past, the future, and what is to come among all beings belong to the great-souled one. That reality, here or elsewhere, has been told to you, O Brahmin.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
