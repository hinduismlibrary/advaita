VP 3.11.1

गृहस्थस्य सद्आचारं श्रोतुम् इच्छाम्य् अहं मुने । लोकाद् अस्मात् परस्माच् च यम् आतिष्ठन् न हीयते ॥१॥

I wish to hear, O sage, the conduct of a householder by which one does not decline from this world or the next.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
गृहस्थस्य (gṛhasthasya) — of the householder
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

gṛhasthasya sadācāraṃ śrotum icchāmy ahaṃ mune | lokād asmāt parasmāc ca yam ātiṣṭhan na hīyate || 1 ||

VP 3.11.2

श्रूयतां पृथिवीपाल सदाचारस्य लक्षणम् । सद्आचारवता पुंसा जितौ लोकाव् उभाव् अपि ॥२॥

Hear, O king, the definition of right conduct; the man who follows it conquers both worlds.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
श्रूयतां पृथिवीपाल सदाचारस्य लक्षणम् (śrūyatāṃ pṛthivīpāla sadācārasya lakṣaṇam) — Hear, O king, the definition of right conduct
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

śrūyatāṃ pṛthivīpāla sadācārasya lakṣaṇam | sadācāravatā puṃsā jitau lokāv ubhāv api || 2 ||

VP 3.11.3

साधवः क्षीणदोषास् तु सच्छब्दः साधुवाचकः । तेषाम् आचरणं यत् तु सद्आचारः स उच्यते ॥३॥

The sādhus are those whose faults are worn away; sat denotes a sādhu, and their conduct is called sadācāra.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
साधवः क्षीणदोषास्तु सच्छब्दः साधुवाचकः (sādhavaḥ kṣīṇadoṣāstu sacchabdaḥ sādhuvācakaḥ) — The sādhus are those whose faults are worn away
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sādhavaḥ kṣīṇadoṣās tu sacchabdaḥ sādhuvācakaḥ | teṣām ācaraṇaṃ yat tu sadācāraḥ sa ucyate || 3 ||

VP 3.11.4

सप्तर्षयोऽथ मनवः प्रजानां पतयस् तथा । सदाचारस्य वक्तारः कर्तारश् च महीपते ॥४॥

The Saptarṣis, Manus, and lords of beings are speakers and doers of sadācāra, O king.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
सप्तर्षयोऽध मनवः प्रजानां पतयस्तथा (saptarṣayo'dha manavaḥ prajānāṃ patayastathā) — Saptarshis
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

saptarṣayo’tha manavaḥ prajānāṃ patayas tathā | sadācārasya vaktāraḥ kartāraś ca mahīpate || 4 ||

VP 3.11.5

ब्राह्मे मुहूर्ते चोत्थाय मनसा मतिमान् नृप । प्रबुद्धश् चिन्तयेद् धर्मम् अर्थं च्प्य् अविरोधिनम् ॥५॥

One should wake up in Braahma muhoorta, and should think at that time, about dharma and artha which are not opposed to each other.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
ब्राझे मुहूर्ते चोत्थाय मनसा मतिमान्नृप (brājhe muhūrte cotthāya manasā matimānnṛpa) — One should wake up in Braahma muhoorta
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

brāhme muhūrte cotthāya manasā matimān nṛpa | prabuddhaś cintayed dharmam arthaṃ cpy avirodhinam || 5 ||

VP 3.11.6

अपीडया तयोः कामम् उभयोर् अपि चिन्तयेत् । दृष्टादृष्टविनाशाय त्रिवर्गे समदर्शिता ॥६॥

Without injuring those two, he should also consider kāma. Equal regard toward the three aims is for the destruction of what is seen and unseen.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
अपीडया तयोः काममुभयोरपि चिन्तयेत् (apīḍayā tayoḥ kāmamubhayorapi cintayet) — And kaama which is also a purushaartha, which is not opposed to dharma and artha.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

apīḍayā tayoḥ kāmam ubhayor api cintayet | dṛṣṭādṛṣṭavināśāya trivarge samadarśitā || 6 ||

VP 3.11.7

परित्यजेद् अर्थकामौ धर्मपीडाकरौ नृप । धर्मम् अप्य् असुखोदर्कं लोकविद् विष्टम् एव च ॥७॥

He should abandon artha and kāma that injure dharma, O king, and also dharma that has an unpleasant result or is condemned by the world.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
परित्यजेदर्थकामौ धर्मपीडाकरौ नृप (parityajedarthakāmau dharmapīḍākarau nṛpa) — Artha and kaama if they are coming in the way of following dharma, One should completely reject them.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

parityajed arthakāmau dharmapīḍākarau nṛpa | dharmam apy asukhodarkaṃ lokavid viṣṭam eva ca || 7 ||

VP 3.11.8

ततः कल्यं समुत्थाय कुर्यान् मूत्रं नरेश्वर ॥८॥

Clearing of the urine, excretion and others, he should do all that.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
ततः काल्यम् समुत्थाय (tataḥ kālyam samutthāya) — then rising early
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tataḥ kalyaṃ samutthāya kuryān mūtraṃ nareśvara || 8 ||

VP 3.11.9

नैरृत्याम् इषुविक्षेपम् अतीत्याभ्यधिकं भुवः । दूरादाव् असथान् मूत्रं पुरीषं च विसर्जयेत् ॥९॥

He should relieve himself to the southwest, beyond an arrow's throw from the dwelling, and farther away.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
नैरृत्यामिषुविक्षेपमतीत्याभ्यधिकं भुवः (nairṛtyāmiṣuvikṣepamatītyābhyadhikaṃ bhuvaḥ) — He should relieve himself to the southwest
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nairṛtyām iṣuvikṣepam atītyābhyadhikaṃ bhuvaḥ | dūrādāv asathān mūtraṃ purīṣaṃ ca visarjayet || 9 ||

VP 3.11.10

पादाव् अनेजनोच्छिष्टे प्रक्षिपेन् न गृहाङ्गणे ॥१०॥

After having washed, the left over water - he should not throw it in front of the house.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
पाद-अवनिजन-उच्छिष्टे (pāda-avanijana-ucchiṣṭe) — the leftover water from washing the feet
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

pādāv anejanocchiṣṭe prakṣipen na gṛhāṅgaṇe || 10 ||

VP 3.11.11

आत्मच्छायां तरुच्छायां गोसूर्याग्न्य्अनिलांस् तथा । गुरुद्विजादींस् तु बुधो नाधिमेहेत् कदाचन ॥११॥

A wise person should never urinate beneath his own shadow, a tree’s shadow, or the shadow or presence of a cow, the sun, fire, wind, a teacher, or a twice-born person.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
आत्मच्छायां तरुच्छायां गोसूर्याग्न्यनिलांस्तथा (ātmacchāyāṃ tarucchāyāṃ gosūryāgnyanilāṃstathā) — A wise person should never urinate beneath his own shadow
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ātmacchāyāṃ tarucchāyāṃ gosūryāgnyanilāṃs tathā | gurudvijādīṃs tu budho nādhimehet kadācana || 11 ||

VP 3.11.12

न कृष्टे सस्यमध्ये वा गोव्रजे जनसंसदि । न वर्त्मनि न नद्यादितीर्थेषु पुरुषर्षभ ॥१२॥

He should not do so in a ploughed field, among crops, in a cowshed or crowd, on a road, or at a river or other sacred water, O best of men.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
न कष्टे सस्यमध्ये वा गोव्रजे जनसंसदि (na kaṣṭe sasyamadhye vā govraje janasaṃsadi) — He should not do so in a ploughed field
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

na kṛṣṭe sasyamadhye vā govraje janasaṃsadi | na vartmani na nadyāditīrtheṣu puruṣarṣabha || 12 ||

VP 3.11.13

नाप्सु नैवाम्भसस् तीरे श्मशाने न समाचरेत् । उत्सर्गं वै पुरीषस्य मूत्रस्य च विसर्जनम् ॥१३॥

Not in water, not in the banks of a river, not in the smashaana. One should not go an urinate or remove excreta there.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
नाप्सु नैवांभसस्तीरे श्मशाने न समाचरेत् (nāpsu naivāṃbhasastīre śmaśāne na samācaret) — Not in water, not in the banks of a river, not in the smashaana.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nāpsu naivāmbhasas tīre śmaśāne na samācaret | utsargaṃ vai purīṣasya mūtrasya ca visarjanam || 13 ||

VP 3.11.14

उदङ्मुखो दिवा मूत्रं विपरीतमुखो निशि । कुर्वीतानापदि प्राज्ञो मूत्रोत्सर्गं च पार्थिव ॥१४॥

During daytime, one should be facing north. During night, one should be facing opposite of this, south. When there is no obstruction, at that time, he should follow this.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
उदङ्मुखो दिवा मूत्रं विपरीतमुखो निशि (udaṅmukho divā mūtraṃ viparītamukho niśi) — During daytime, one should be facing north.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

udaṅmukho divā mūtraṃ viparītamukho niśi | kurvītānāpadi prājño mūtrotsargaṃ ca pārthiva || 14 ||

VP 3.11.15

तृणैर् आस्तीर्य वसुधां वस्त्रप्रावृतमस्तकः । तिष्ठेन् नातिचिरं तत्र नैव किञ्चिद् उदीरयेत् ॥१५॥

He should cover his head with cloth. On the ground He should spread grass. He should not speak during that time. He should not stay for too long.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
तृणैरास्तीर्य वसुधां वस्त्रप्रावृतमस्तकः (tṛṇairāstīrya vasudhāṃ vastraprāvṛtamastakaḥ) — He should cover his head with cloth.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tṛṇair āstīrya vasudhāṃ vastraprāvṛtamastakaḥ | tiṣṭhen nāticiraṃ tatra naiva kiñcid udīrayet || 15 ||

VP 3.11.16

वल्मीकमूषिकोद्भूतां मृदं नान्तर्जलां तथा । शौचावशिष्टां गेहाच् च नादद्याल् लेपसम्भवाम् ॥१६॥

He should not take earth dug up by anthills or mice, earth under water, earth left after cleaning, or earth from a house suitable for plaster.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
वल्मीकमूषिकोत्खातां मृदं नान्तर्जलात्तथा (valmīkamūṣikotkhātāṃ mṛdaṃ nāntarjalāttathā) — The shoucha which is told in the shaastra is cleaning with mud.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

valmīkamūṣikodbhūtāṃ mṛdaṃ nāntarjalāṃ tathā | śaucāvaśiṣṭāṃ gehāc ca nādadyāl lepasambhavām || 16 ||

VP 3.11.17

अन्तःप्राण्यवपन्नां च हलोत्खातां च पार्थिव । परित्यजेन् मृदश् चैताः सकलाः शौचसाधने ॥१७॥

He should reject these kinds of earth for cleansing: earth fallen upon by animals within, and earth dug up by a plough, O king.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
अणुप्राप्युपपन्नां च हलोत्खातां च पार्थिव (aṇuprāpyupapannāṃ ca halotkhātāṃ ca pārthiva) — That which is taken out using the plough, or that which has a lot of insects, this kind of mud one should not collect for cleaning.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

antaḥprāṇyavapannāṃ ca halotkhātāṃ ca pārthiva | parityajen mṛdaś caitāḥ sakalāḥ śaucasādhane || 17 ||

VP 3.11.18

एका लिङ्गे गुदे तिस्रस् दश वामकरे नृप । हस्तद्वये सप्तान्या मृदः शौचोपपादिकाः ॥१८॥

At the place of urination, he should clean once. At the rectum, three times. Left hand, ten times, one should clean with mud. Then, both the hands, One should clean seven times with mud.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
एका लिङ्गे गुदे तिस्रो दश वामकरे नृप (ekā liṅge gude tisro daśa vāmakare nṛpa) — At the place of urination, he should clean once.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ekā liṅge gude tisras daśa vāmakare nṛpa | hastadvaye saptānyā mṛdaḥ śaucopapādikāḥ || 18 ||

VP 3.11.19

अच्छेनागन्धफेनेन जलेनाबुद्बुदेन च । आचामेत मृदं भूयस् तथा दद्यात् समाहितः ॥१९॥

He should do aachamana after that. He should not take water which is having foam, or which has got dirty smell. With clean water, One should do aachamana. Again, He should collect a lot of mud and keep it with him, for later usage.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
अच्छेनागंधफेनेन जलेनाबुद्बुदेन च (acchenāgaṃdhaphenena jalenābudbudena ca) — He should do aachamana after that.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

acchenāgandhaphenena jalenābudbudena ca | ācāmeta mṛdaṃ bhūyas tathā dadyāt samāhitaḥ || 19 ||

VP 3.11.20

निष्पादिताङ्घ्रिशौचस् तु पापाव् अभ्युक्ष्य वै पुनः । त्रिः पिबेत् सलिलं तेन तथा द्विः परिमार्जयेत् ॥२०॥

Having cleaned up his feet, He should wash his feet again. He should do gargling three times, and throw the water. He should wash his face two times.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
निष्पादिताङ्घ्रिशौचस्तु पादावभ्युक्ष्य वै पुनः (niṣpāditāṅghriśaucastu pādāvabhyukṣya vai punaḥ) — Having cleaned up his feet, He should wash his feet again.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

niṣpāditāṅghriśaucas tu pāpāv abhyukṣya vai punaḥ | triḥ pibet salilaṃ tena tathā dviḥ parimārjayet || 20 ||

VP 3.11.21

शीर्षण्यानि ततः खानि मूर्धानं च मृदा लभेत् । बाहू नाभिं च तोयेन हृदयं चापि संस्पृशेत् ॥२१॥

He should clean all the openings (sense organs) in his head - two eyes, two ears, two nostrils, mouth. And He should wash the face. He should touch baahu, naabhi, hrudaya with water. As in doing the aachamana.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
शीर्षण्यानि ततः स्वानि मूर्धानं च समालभेत् (śīrṣaṇyāni tataḥ svāni mūrdhānaṃ ca samālabhet) — He should clean all the openings (sense organs) in his head - two eyes, two ears, two nostrils, mouth.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

śīrṣaṇyāni tataḥ khāni mūrdhānaṃ ca mṛdā labhet | bāhū nābhiṃ ca toyena hṛdayaṃ cāpi saṃspṛśet || 21 ||

VP 3.11.22

स्वाचान्तस् तु ततः कुर्यात् पुमान् केशप्रसाधनम् । आदर्शञ्जनमङ्गल्यं दूर्वाद्य्आलम्भनानि च ॥२२॥

Then He should do aachamana, and decorate himself. He should comb his hair. He can apply mangala dravyaas, scent, gandha. He should decorate himself.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
स्वाचान्तश्च ततः कुर्यात्पुमान्केशप्रसाधनम् (svācāntaśca tataḥ kuryātpumānkeśaprasādhanam) — Then He should do aachamana, and decorate himself.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

A person who on all these days uses oil, has intercourse, or eats meat goes after death to a hell called “food of excrement and urine.”

VP 3.11.119

अशेषपर्वस्व् एतेषु तस्मात् संयमिभिर् बुधैः । भाव्यं सच्छास्त्रदेवेज्याध्यानजप्यपरैर् नरैः ॥११९॥

VP 3.11.120

VP 3.11.121

One should not engage in intercourse at a shrine, public square, or pilgrimage place, nor in a cowshed, at a crossroads, in a cremation ground, in a garden, or in water.

VP 3.11.122

प्रोक्तपर्वस्वशेषेषु नैव भूपाल सन्ध्ययोः । गच्छेद् व्यवायं मतिमान् न मूत्रोच्चारपीडितः ॥१२२॥

Intercourse should not be undertaken on the stated festival days, at either twilight, or while one is distressed by the need to urinate or defecate.

VP 3.11.123

पर्वस्व् अभिगमोऽधन्यो दिवा पापप्रदो नृप । भुवि रोगावहो नॄणाम् अप्रशस्तो जलाशये ॥१२३॥

VP 3.11.124
