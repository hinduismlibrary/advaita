Verse 1

1

अविनयमपनय विष्णो दमय मनः शमय विषयमृगतृष्णाम् । भूतदयां विस्तारय तारय संसारसागरात् ॥

Remove my arrogance, Viṣṇu; discipline my mind; quiet the mirage-thirst for sense objects. Expand my compassion for living beings and carry me across the ocean of worldly existence.

Word-for-word
अविनयम् arrogance, lack of humility
अपनय remove
विष्णो O Viṣṇu
दमय discipline, subdue
मनः the mind
शमय quiet, calm
विषय sense objects
मृगतृष्णाम् mirage-thirst
भूत-दयाम् compassion for living beings
विस्तारय expand
तारय carry across, deliver
संसार-सागरात् from the ocean of worldly existence

Verse 2

2

दिव्यधुनीमकरन्दे परिमलपरिभोगसच्चिदानन्दे । श्रीपतिपदारविन्दे भवभयखेदच्छिदे वन्दे ॥

I bow to the lotus feet of the Lord of Śrī: nectar from the celestial river, fragrant, and the enjoyment of Being, Consciousness, and Bliss; they cut away the fear and distress of worldly existence.

Word-for-word
दिव्य divine, celestial
धुनी river
मकरन्दे in the honey, nectar
परिमल fragrance
परिभोग enjoyment, experience
सत् Being
चित् Consciousness
आनन्दे in Bliss
श्री-पति Lord of Śrī, Viṣṇu
पद-अरविन्दे at the lotus feet
भव worldly existence
भय fear
खेद distress
छिदे to the cutter, remover
वन्दे I bow

Verse 3

3

सत्यपि भेदापगमे नाथ तवाहं न मामकीनस्त्वम् । सामुद्रो हि तरङ्गः क्वचन समुद्रो न तारङ्गः ॥

Even when difference has vanished, Lord, I am yours; you are not mine. A wave belongs to the sea, indeed; the sea is never the wave’s.

Word-for-word
सति अपि even when there is
भेद-अपगमे the disappearance of difference
नाथ O Lord
तव अहम् I am yours
न not
मामकीनः mine, belonging to me
त्वम् you
सामुद्रः belonging to the sea
हि indeed
तरङ्गः a wave
क्वचन anywhere, at all
समुद्रः the sea
न तारङ्गः not belonging to a wave

Verse 4

4

उद्धृतनग नगभिदनुज दनुजकुलामित्र मित्रशशिदृष्टे । दृष्टे भवति प्रभवति न भवति किं भवतिरस्कारः ॥

O lifter of the mountain, younger brother of Indra, enemy of the demon clans, whose eyes are the sun and moon! When you are seen, does turning away from worldly existence not prevail? What cannot then happen?

Word-for-word
उद्धृत-नग lifter of the mountain [Govardhana]
नग-भिद् mountain-breaker [Indra]
अनुज younger brother
दनुज-कुल the demon clans
अमित्र enemy
मित्र • शशि • दृष्टे sun • moon • eyed
दृष्टे when seen
भवति comes to be
प्रभवति prevails, takes effect
न भवति किम् what cannot happen?
भव-तिरस्कारः rejection of worldly existence

Verse 5

5

मत्स्यादिभिरवतारैरवतारवतावता सदा वसुधाम् । परमेश्वर परिपाल्यो भवता भवतापभीतोऽहम् ॥

With incarnations beginning with the Fish, you have always protected the earth. Supreme Lord, I am frightened by the fever of worldly existence; I should be protected by you.

Word-for-word
मत्स्य-आदिभिः by those beginning with the Fish
अवतारैः by incarnations
अवतार-वता by the one endowed with incarnations
अवता protected
सदा always
वसुधाम् the earth
परम-ईश्वर O Supreme Lord
परिपाल्यः to be protected
भवता by you
भव-ताप the heat, anguish of worldly existence
भीतः frightened
अहम् I

Verse 6

6

दामोदर गुणमन्दिर सुन्दरवदनारविन्द गोविन्द । भवजलधिमथनमन्दर परमं दरमपनय त्वं मे ॥

O Dāmodara, abode of virtues, lotus-faced and beautiful, Govinda, Mandara Mountain for churning the ocean of worldly existence—remove my great fear, I pray.

Word-for-word
दामोदर O Dāmodara
गुण-मन्दिर abode of virtues
सुन्दर beautiful
वदन-अरविन्द lotus face
गोविन्द O Govinda
भव-जलधि ocean of worldly existence
मथन churning
मन्दर Mandara Mountain
परमम् great, supreme
दरम् fear
अपनय remove
त्वम् you
मे from me, for me

Verse 7

7

नारायण करुणामय शरणं करवाणि तावकौ चरणौ । इति षट्पदी मदीये वदनसरोजे सदा वसतु ॥

O Nārāyaṇa, full of compassion, I make your two feet my refuge. May this Ṣaṭpadī always dwell in the lotus of my mouth.

Word-for-word
नारायण O Nārāyaṇa
करुणा-मय full of compassion
शरणम् refuge
करवाणि may I make
तावकौ your two
चरणौ feet
इति thus
षट्पदी the six-footed one; this Ṣaṭpadī hymn
मदीये in my
वदन-सरोजे lotus-like mouth
सदा always
वसतु may it dwell

Thus is the Ṣaṭpadī Stotram complete.
