Opening invocation

Invocation

चिन्मुद्रितकरकमलं चिन्तितभक्तेष्टदं विमलम । गुरुवरमाद्यं कञ्चन निरवधिकानन्दनिर्भरं वन्दे ॥

I bow to the spotless, primordial revered teacher, whose lotus hand bears the seal of awareness, who grants devotees their cherished wishes, and who is filled with limitless bliss.

Word-for-word
चिन्मुद्रित-कर-कमलम whose lotus hand bears the seal of awareness
चिन्तित-भक्त-इष्ट-दम granting devotees their cherished wishes
विमलम spotless
गुरुवरम-आद्यम the primordial, revered teacher
निरवधि-आनन्द-निर्भरम filled with limitless bliss
वन्दे I bow

Verse 1

1

वटतरुनिकटनिवासं पटुतरविज्ञानमुद्रितकराब्जम । कञ्चन देशिकमाद्यं कैवल्यानन्दकन्दलं वन्दे ॥1॥

I bow to a certain primordial teacher who dwells beside the banyan tree, whose lotus hand is sealed in keen knowledge, and who is the very sprout of liberation's bliss.

Word-for-word
वट-तरु-निकट-निवासम dwelling beside the banyan tree
पटु-तर-विज्ञान-मुद्रित-कर-अब्जम whose lotus hand is sealed in keen knowledge
कञ्चन देशिकम-आद्यम a certain primordial teacher
कैवल्य-आनन्द-कन्दलम the sprout of liberation's bliss
वन्दे I bow

Verse 2

2

निरवधिसंसृतिनीरधिनिपतितजनतारणस्फुरन्नौकाम । परमतभेदनघुटिकां परमशिवेन्द्रार्यपादुकां नौमि ॥2॥

I salute the noble sandals of Paramasivendra: the shining boat that rescues people fallen into the boundless ocean of transmigration, and the small pickaxe that cuts through rival doctrines.

Word-for-word
निरवधि-संसृति-नीरधि of the boundless ocean of transmigration
निपतित-जनता the people who have fallen in
तारण-स्फुरत-नौकाम the shining rescue-boat
परमत-भेदन-घुटिकाम the pickaxe that cuts through rival doctrines
परमशिवेन्द्र-आर्य-पादुकाम the noble sandals of Paramasivendra
नौमि I salute

Verse 3

3

देशिकपरमशिवेन्द्रादेशवशोद्बुद्धदिव्यमहिमाऽहम । स्वात्मनि विश्रान्तिकृते सरसं प्रस्तौमि किञ्चिदिदम ॥3॥

Awakened to divine greatness by the command of the teacher Paramasivendra, I offer this small, gracious composition so that I may rest in my own Self.

सरसम sweet and gracious

Verse 4

4

निरुपमनित्यनिरीहो निष्कलनिर्मायनिर्गुणाकारः । विगलितसर्वविकल्पः शुद्धो बुद्धश्चकास्ति परमात्मा ॥4॥

The supreme Self shines without a peer: eternal, desireless, partless, free from māyā and qualities, beyond every thought, pure and awake.

Word-for-word
निरुपम without a peer
नित्य eternal
निरीह desireless
निष्कल partless
निर्माय free from māyā
निर्गुण-आकारः without qualities in its nature
विगलित-सर्व-विकल्पः free of every thought
शुद्धः बुद्धः pure and awake

Verse 5

5

स्वाविद्यैकनिबद्धः कुर्वन्कर्माणि मुह्यमानः सन । दैवाद्विधूतबन्धः स्वात्मज्ञानान्मुनिर्जयति ॥5॥

The sage, bound only by his own ignorance, performs actions while deluded; by good fortune his bondage is shaken off, and through knowledge of his own Self he prevails.

Word-for-word

दैवात by good fortune
विधूत-बन्धः with bondage shaken off
स्वात्म-ज्ञानात through knowledge of his own Self
मुनिः जयति the sage prevails

Verse 6

6

मायावशेन सुप्तो मध्ये पश्यन्सहस्रशः स्वप्नान । देशिकवचःप्रबुद्धो दीव्यत्यानन्दवारिधौ कोऽपि ॥6॥

Someone, asleep under māyā and seeing thousands of dreams within, is awakened by the teacher's words and shines in the ocean of bliss.

आनन्द-वारिधौ in the ocean of bliss
कश्चित someone

Verse 7

7

प्राकृतभावमपास्य स्वीकृतनिजरूपसच्चिदानन्दः । गुरुवरकरुणापाङ्गाद्गौरवमासाद्य माद्यति प्राज्ञः ॥7॥

Setting aside the ordinary state, the wise one takes up his own nature as being-consciousness-bliss; touched by the great teacher's compassionate glance, he rejoices in that dignity.

श्रीगुरुकृपया सच्चित्सुखनिजरूपे निमग्नधीर्मौनी । विहरति कश्चन विबुधः शान्ताहन्तो नितान्तमुदितान्तः ॥8॥

By the revered guru's grace, a silent sage with mind immersed in his own nature of being, consciousness, and bliss lives in deep peace, inwardly glad.

Word-for-word
श्री-गुरु-कृपया by the revered guru's grace

निमग्न-धीः with mind immersed
मौनी silent
विहरति dwells
कश्चन विबुधः some wise one
शान्त-अहंतः with ego quiet
नितान्त-मुदित-अन्तः with an inward heart wholly glad

Cooled within by the full force of the great teacher's waves of compassion, the best ascetic freely delights on the shore of incomparable bliss.

श्रीदेशिकवरकरुणारविकरसमपोहितान्तरध्वान्तः । विहरन्मस्करिवर्यो निरवधिकानन्दनीरधावास्ते ॥10॥

Verse 11

11

जनिविपरीतक्रमतो बुद्ध्या प्रविलाप्य पञ्चभूतानि । परिशिष्टमात्मतत्त्वं पश्यन्नास्ते मुनिः शान्तः ॥11॥

Through understanding, dissolving the five elements in reverse order, the tranquil sage sees what remains, the truth of the Self, and rests there.

Word-for-word
जनि-विपरीत-क्रमतः in the reverse order of birth
बुद्ध्या प्रविलाप्य having dissolved through understanding
पञ्च-भूतानि the five elements
परिशिष्टम what remains
आत्म-तत्त्वम the truth of the Self
पश्यन seeing
आस्ते rests
मुनिः शान्तः the tranquil sage

Verse 12

12

जगदखिलमिदमसारं मायिकमेवेति मनसि मन्वानः । पर्यटति पाटिताशः प्रगलितमदमानमत्सरः कोऽपि ॥12॥

Thinking that this entire world is insubstantial, merely māyā, someone wanders, casting off desire, pride, conceit, and envy.

प्रगलित-मद-मान-मत्सरः with pride, conceit, and envy dissolved
कश्चित someone

Verse 13

13

नात्मनि किञ्चिन्माया तत्कार्यं वाऽस्ति वस्तुतो विमले । इति निश्चयवानन्तः हृष्यत्यानन्दनिर्भरो योगी ॥13॥

The yogin is certain within: in the stainless Self there is in truth nothing of māyā or its effects. Filled with bliss, he rejoices.

Word-for-word

तत-कार्यम वा or of its effects

अन्तः within
हृष्यति rejoices
आनन्द-निर्भरः filled with bliss

17

Verse 18

18

सवितर्यपि शीतरुचौ चन्द्रे तीक्ष्णेऽप्यधो वहत्यग्नौ । मायिकमिदमिति जानन्जीवन्मुक्तो न विस्मयी भवति ॥18॥

Even if the sun grew cool like the moon, the moon burned fiercely, or fire flowed downward, one liberated while living would not be surprised, knowing it to be māyā.

Word-for-word

Verse 19

19
