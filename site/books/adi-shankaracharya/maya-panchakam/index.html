1

निरुपमनित्यनिरंशकेऽप्यखण्डे मयि चिति सर्वविकल्पनादिशून्ये । घटयति जगदीशजीवभेदं त्वघटितघटनापटीयसी माया ॥

In me, consciousness—unmatched, eternal, partless, undivided, and free of every imagined distinction—māyā brings about a difference between the Lord of the world and the individual soul. Māyā is indeed skilled at making the impossible happen.

Word-for-word
निरुपम (nirupama) — matchless
नित्य • निरंश (nitya • niraṃśa) — eternal • partless
अपि • अखण्ड (api • akhaṇḍa) — even • undivided
मयि • चिति (mayi • citi) — in me • in consciousness
सर्व-विकल्पना-आदि-शून्ये (sarva-vikalpanā-ādi-śūnye) — free of every imagined distinction and the like
घटयति (ghaṭayati) — brings about
जगत्-ईश • जीव-भेद (jagat-īśa • jīva-bheda) — world • Lord • individual-soul • distinction
तु (tu) — indeed
अघटित-घटना-पटीयसी (aghaṭita-ghaṭanā-paṭīyasī) — skilled at making the impossible happen
माया (māyā) — Māyā
Transliteration

nirupamanityaniraṃśake’pyakhaṇḍe mayi citi sarvavikalpanādiśūnye | ghaṭayati jagadīśajīvabhedaṃ tvaghaṭitaghaṭanāpaṭīyasī māyā ||

2

श्रुतिशतनिगमान्तशोधकानप्यहह धनादिनिदर्शनेन सद्यः । कलुषयति चतुष्पदाद्यभिन्नानघटितघटनापटीयसी माया ॥

Alas, using lures such as wealth, māyā quickly stains even those who investigate hundreds of revealed texts and Upaniṣads, making them no different from beasts and the like. She is skilled at making the impossible happen.

Word-for-word
श्रुति-शत (śruti-śata) — hundreds of revealed texts
निगम-अन्त (nigama-anta) — the end, or Upaniṣads, of the Vedas
शोधकान् • अपि (śodhakān • api) — investigators • even
अहह (ahaha) — alas
धन-आदि-निदर्शनेन (dhana-ādi-nidarśanena) — by means of lures such as wealth
सद्यः (sadyaḥ) — immediately
कलुषयति (kaluṣayati) — stains, corrupts
चतुष्पद-आदि (catuṣpada-ādi) — quadrupeds and the like
अभिन्नान् (abhinnān) — not different from
अघटित-घटना-पटीयसी (aghaṭita-ghaṭanā-paṭīyasī) — skilled at making the impossible happen
माया (māyā) — Māyā
Transliteration

śrutiśatanigamāntaśodhakānapyahaha dhanādinidarśanena sadyaḥ | kaluṣayati catuṣpadādyabhinnānaghaṭitaghaṭanāpaṭīyasī māyā ||

3

सुखचिदखण्डविबोधमद्वितीयं वियदनलादिविनिर्मिते नियोज्य । भ्रमयति भवसागरे नितान्तं त्वघटितघटनापटीयसी माया ॥

Having placed undivided awareness-bliss, non-dual consciousness, within a world fashioned from space, fire, and the other elements, māyā utterly confuses it in the ocean of becoming. She is indeed skilled at making the impossible happen.

Word-for-word
सुख • चित् (sukha • cit) — bliss • consciousness
अखण्ड-विबोध (akhaṇḍa-vibodha) — undivided awareness
अद्वितीय (advitīya) — non-dual
वियत् • अनल • आदि (viyat • anala • ādi) — space • fire • and the rest
विनिर्मिते (vinirmite) — in what is fashioned
नियोज्य (niyojya) — having placed, having assigned
भ्रमयति (bhramayati) — leads astray, confuses
भव-सागर (bhava-sāgara) — ocean of becoming
नितान्तम् (nitāntam) — utterly
तु (tu) — indeed
अघटित-घटना-पटीयसी (aghaṭita-ghaṭanā-paṭīyasī) — skilled at making the impossible happen
माया (māyā) — Māyā
Transliteration

sukhacidakhaṇḍavibodhamadvitīyaṃ viyadanalādivinirmite niyojya | bhramayati bhavasāgare nitāntaṃ tvaghaṭitaghaṭanāpaṭīyasī māyā ||

4

अपगतगुणवर्णजातिभेदे सुखचिति विप्रविडाद्यहंकृतिं च । स्फुटयति सुतदारगेहमोहं त्वघटितघटनापटीयसी माया ॥

In blissful consciousness, beyond distinctions of qualities, color, and birth, māyā makes the ego notion—‘I am a priest, a merchant, and so on’—plainly appear; she also produces attachment to children, spouse, and home. Māyā is indeed skilled at making the impossible happen.

Word-for-word
अपगत (apagata) — gone beyond, free from
गुण • वर्ण • जाति-भेद (guṇa • varṇa • jāti-bheda) — quality • color • birth • distinctions
सुख-चिति (sukha-citi) — in blissful consciousness
विप्र • विट् • आदि (vipra • viṭ • ādi) — priest • merchant • and the like
अहंकृति (ahaṃkṛti) — ego-making, I-notion
च (ca) — and
स्फुटयति (sphuṭayati) — makes manifest
सुत • दार • गेह (suta • dāra • geha) — child • spouse • home
मोह (moha) — delusion, attachment
तु (tu) — indeed
अघटित-घटना-पटीयसी (aghaṭita-ghaṭanā-paṭīyasī) — skilled at making the impossible happen
माया (māyā) — Māyā
Transliteration

apagataguṇavarṇajātibhede sukhaciti vipraviḍādyahaṃkṛtiṃ ca | sphuṭayati sutadāragehamohaṃ tvaghaṭitaghaṭanāpaṭīyasī māyā ||

5

विधिहरिहरविभेदमप्यखण्डे बत विरचय्य बुधानपि प्रकामम् । भ्रमयति हरिहरभेदभावानघटितघटनापटीयसी माया ॥

Alas, fashioning even the distinction among Brahmā, Hari, and Hara within the undivided, māyā thoroughly bewilders even the wise with the idea that Hari and Hara are separate. Māyā is skilled at making the impossible happen.

Word-for-word
विधि • हरि • हर (vidhi • hari • hara) — Brahmā • Viṣṇu • Śiva
विभेदम् • अपि (vibhedam • api) — distinction • even
अखण्डे (akhaṇḍe) — in the undivided [reality]
बत (bata) — alas
विरचय्य (viracayya) — having fashioned
बुधान् • अपि (budhān • api) — the wise • even
प्रकामम् (prakāmam) — fully, greatly
भ्रमयति (bhramayati) — bewilders
हरि-हर-भेद-भावान् (hari-hara-bheda-bhāvān) — the notion of a difference between Hari and Hara
अघटित-घटना-पटीयसी (aghaṭita-ghaṭanā-paṭīyasī) — skilled at making the impossible happen
माया (māyā) — Māyā
Transliteration

vidhihariharavibhedamapyakhaṇḍe bata viracayya budhānapi prakāmam | bhramayati hariharabhedabhāvānaghaṭitaghaṭanāpaṭīyasī māyā ||

Colophon

इति श्रीमत्परमहंसपरिव्राजकाचार्यस्य श्रीगोविन्दभगवत्पूज्यपादशिष्यस्य श्रीमच्छङ्करभगवतः कृतौ मायापञ्चकं संपूर्णम् ॥

Thus is completed the Māyāpañcakam composed by the revered Śaṅkara Bhagavat, disciple of the venerable Govinda Bhagavat, the revered teacher who has renounced all, and supreme among wandering ascetics.

Word-for-word
इति (iti) — thus
श्रीमत् • परमहंस (śrīmat • paramahaṃsa) — revered • supreme renunciant
परिव्राजक-आचार्य (parivrājaka-ācārya) — teacher among wandering ascetics
श्री-गोविन्द-भगवत् (śrī-govinda-bhagavat) — revered Govinda Bhagavat
पूज्य-पाद (pūjya-pāda) — whose feet are worthy of worship
शिष्य (śiṣya) — disciple
श्रीमत्-शङ्कर-भगवत् (śrīmat-śaṅkara-bhagavat) — the revered Śaṅkara Bhagavat
कृतौ (kṛtau) — in the composition
माया-पञ्चकम् (māyā-pañcakam) — the five verses on māyā
संपूर्णम् (saṃpūrṇam) — complete
Transliteration

iti śrīmatparamahaṃsaparivrājakācāryasya śrīgovindabhagavatpūjyapādaśiṣyasya śrīmacchaṅkarabhagavataḥ kṛtau māyāpañcakaṃ saṃpūrṇam ||
