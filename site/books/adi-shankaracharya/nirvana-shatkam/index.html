Verse 1

1

मनोबुद्ध्यहंकारचित्तानि नाहं न कर्णे न जिह्वा न च घ्राणनेत्रे । न च व्योम भूमिर्न तेजो न वायुश्चिदानन्दरूपः शिवोऽहं शिवोऽहम् ॥

I am not mind, intellect, ego, or the mental field; nor ear, tongue, nose, or eyes; nor space, earth, fire, or air. I am Śiva, whose nature is consciousness and bliss; I am Śiva.

Verse 2

2

न च प्राणसंज्ञो न वै पञ्चवायुर्न वा सप्तधातुर्न वा पञ्चकोशः । न वाक्पाणिपादौ न चोपस्थपायू चिदानन्दरूपः शिवोऽहं शिवोऽहम् ॥

I am not what is called the vital force, nor the five winds, nor the seven bodily constituents, nor the five sheaths; nor speech, hands, feet, the generative organ, or the excretory organ. I am Śiva, whose nature is consciousness and bliss; I am Śiva.

Verse 3

3

न मे द्वेषरागौ न मे लोभमोहौ मदो नैव मे नैव मात्सर्यभावः । न धर्मो न चार्थो न कामो न मोक्षश्चिदानन्दरूपः शिवोऽहं शिवोऽहम् ॥

I have neither aversion nor attachment, neither greed nor delusion; I have no pride and no trace of jealousy. I am not duty, wealth, desire, or liberation. I am Śiva, whose nature is consciousness and bliss; I am Śiva.

Verse 4

4

न पुण्यं न पापं न सौख्यं न दुःखं न मन्त्रो न तीर्थं न वेदा न यज्ञाः । अहं भोजनं नैव भोज्यं न भोक्ता चिदानन्दरूपः शिवोऽहं शिवोऽहम् ॥

I am neither merit nor sin, neither pleasure nor pain; neither mantra, pilgrimage place, Veda, nor sacrifice. I am not the act of eating, the food, or the eater. I am Śiva, whose nature is consciousness and bliss; I am Śiva.

Verse 5

5

न मृत्युर्न शङ्का न मे जातिभेदः पिता नैव मे नैव माता च जन्म । न बन्धुर्न मित्रं गुरुर्नैव शिष्यश्चिदानन्दरूपः शिवोऽहं शिवोऽहम् ॥

I have no death or fear, and for me there is no distinction of birth or class. I have no father, no mother, and no birth; no relative, friend, teacher, or disciple. I am Śiva, whose nature is consciousness and bliss; I am Śiva.

Verse 6

6

अहं निर्विकल्पो निराकाररूपो विभुत्वाच्च सर्वत्र सर्वेन्द्रियाणाम् । न चासंगतं नैव मुक्तिर्न बन्धश्चिदानन्दरूपः शिवोऽहं शिवोऽहम् ॥

I am free from distinctions and formless. Because I pervade everywhere, I am present through all the senses. I am not the state of being unattached; I am not even liberation or bondage. I am Śiva, whose nature is consciousness and bliss; I am Śiva.

Closing colophon

Colophon

इति श्रीमत्परमहंसपरिव्राजकाचार्यस्य श्रीगोविन्दभगवत्पूज्यपादशिष्यस्य श्रीमच्छङ्करभगवतः कृतौ निर्वाणषट्कं संपूर्णम् ॥

Thus is completed the Nirvāṇaṣaṭkam composed by the revered Śaṅkara Bhagavat, disciple of the venerable Govinda Bhagavat, the revered teacher who has renounced all, and supreme among wandering ascetics.
