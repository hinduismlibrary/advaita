Verse 1

1

जाग्रत्स्वप्नसुषुप्तिषु स्फुटतरा या संविदुज्जृम्भते या ब्रह्मादिपिपीलिकान्ततनुषु प्रोता जगत्साक्षिणी । सैवाहं न च दृश्यवस्त्विति दृढप्रज्ञापि यस्यास्ति चेच्चाण्डालोऽस्तु स तु द्विजोऽस्तु गुरुरित्येषा मनीषा मम ॥

That consciousness which shines clearly in waking, dream, and deep sleep, and which is woven through bodies from Brahmā down to the tiniest ant as the witness of the world: whoever firmly knows “I am that, not an object seen”—whether an outcaste or a twice-born—is my teacher. This is my conviction.

Verse 2

2

ब्रह्मैवाहमिदं जगच्च सकलं चिन्मात्रविस्तारितं सर्वं चैतदविद्यया त्रिगुणयाऽशेषं मया कल्पितम् । इत्थं यस्य दृढा मतिः सुखतरे नित्ये परे निर्मले चाण्डालोऽस्तु स तु द्विजोऽस्तु गुरुरित्येषा मनीषा मम ॥

“I alone am Brahman; this entire world is unfolded by pure consciousness, and all this, in its entirety, is imagined by me through ignorance composed of the three qualities.” Whoever has this firm understanding of the eternal, supreme, most blissful, stainless reality—outcaste or twice-born—is my teacher. This is my conviction.

Verse 3

3

शश्वन्नश्वरमेव विश्वमखिलं निश्चित्य वाचा गुरोर्नित्यं ब्रह्म निरन्तरं विमृशता निर्व्याजशान्तात्मना । भूतं भावि च दुष्कृतं प्रदहता संविन्मये पावके प्रारब्धाय समर्पितं स्ववपुरित्येषा मनीषा मम ॥

Knowing the entire world to be ever perishing, reflecting constantly on Brahman through the teacher’s words with an utterly calm and guileless mind, burning past and future misdeeds in the fire that is consciousness, and surrendering one’s own body to the karma already begun—this is my conviction.

मम my

Verse 4

4

या तिर्यङ्नरदेवताभिरहमित्यन्तः स्फुटा गृह्यते यद्भासा हृदयाक्षदेहविषया भान्ति स्वतोऽचेतनाः । तां भास्यैः पिहितार्कमण्डलनिभां स्फूर्तिं सदा भावयन् योगी निर्वृतमानसो हि गुरुरित्येषा मनीषा मम ॥

The yogi whose mind is at peace and who continually contemplates that inner awareness—clearly grasped as “I” by animals, humans, and gods, by whose light the otherwise insentient mind, eyes, body, and objects shine, and which is like the sun’s radiance veiled by the things it illumines—is indeed a teacher. This is my conviction.

Verse 5

5

यत्सौख्याम्बुधिलेशलेशत इमे शक्रादयो निर्वृता यच्चित्ते नितरां प्रशान्तकलने लब्ध्वा मुनिर्निर्वृतः । यस्मिन्नित्यसुखाम्बुधौ गलितधीर्ब्रह्मैव न ब्रह्मविद् यः कश्चित्सुरेन्द्रवन्दितपदो नूनं मनीषा मम ॥

From even the smallest fraction of whose ocean of bliss, Indra and the others are fulfilled; whose peace the sage attains and becomes content; in whose eternal ocean of happiness the mind dissolves—whoever is Brahman itself, not merely a knower of Brahman, and whose feet are revered by Indra, is surely my teacher. This is my conviction.

Colophon

॥

इति श्रीमत्परमहंसपरिव्राजकाचार्यस्य श्रीगोविन्दभगवत्पूज्यपादशिष्यस्य श्रीमच्छङ्करभगवतः कृतौ मनीषापञ्चकं संपूर्णम् ॥

Thus ends the Manīṣāpañcakam, composed by the revered Śaṅkara, disciple of the revered Govinda Bhagavatpāda, the eminent teacher and wandering paramahaṃsa.
