Verse 1

1

मनो निवृत्तिः परमोपशान्तिः सा तीर्थवर्या मणिकर्णिका च । ज्ञानप्रवाहा विमलादिगङ्गा सा काशिकाहं निजबोधरूपा ॥

The mind’s withdrawal is supreme peace; that is the finest sacred bathing place, Maṇikarṇikā. The stream of knowledge is the pure, primordial Ganges; that Kāśī am I, of the nature of self-knowledge.

Verse 2

2

यस्यामिदं कल्पितमिन्द्रजालं चराचरं भाति मनोविलासम् । सच्चित्सुखैका परमात्मरूपा सा काशिकाहं निजबोधरूपा ॥

In that Kāśī, this conjurer’s show—the moving and unmoving world—shines as a play of mind. She is one, of the nature of being, consciousness, and bliss, and is the supreme Self. That Kāśī am I, of the nature of self-knowledge.

Verse 3

3

कोशेषु पञ्चस्वधिराजमाना बुद्धिर्भवानी प्रतिदेहगेहम् । साक्षी शिवः सर्वगतोऽन्तरात्मा सा काशिकाहं निजबोधरूपा ॥

The intellect, reigning in the five sheaths in every bodily dwelling, is Bhavānī; Śiva is the witness, the all-pervading inner Self. That Kāśī am I, of the nature of self-knowledge.

Verse 4

4

काश्यां हि काशते काशी काशी सर्वप्रकाशिका । सा काशी विदिता येन तेन प्राप्ता हि काशिका ॥

In Kāśī, Kāśī indeed shines; Kāśī illumines everything. Whoever knows that Kāśī has truly attained Kāśī.

Verse 5

5

काशीक्षेत्रं शरीरं त्रिभुवनजननी व्यापिनी ज्ञानगङ्गा भक्तिः श्रद्धा गयेयं निजगुरुचरणध्यानयोगः प्रयागः । विश्वेशोऽयं तुरीयं सकलजनमनःसाक्षिभूतोऽन्तरात्मा देहे सर्वं मदीये यदि वसति पुनस्तीर्थमन्यत्किमस्ति ॥

The body is the sacred field of Kāśī; the all-pervading mother of the three worlds is the Ganges of knowledge. Devotion and faith are Gayā; meditation on the feet of one’s own teacher is Prayāga. The Lord of the universe is this fourth state, the inner Self who witnesses the minds of all people. If all this abides in my body, what other pilgrimage place is there?

Closing colophon

Colophon

इति श्रीमत्परमहंसपरिव्राजकाचार्यस्य श्रीगोविन्दभगवत्पूज्यपादशिष्यस्य श्रीमच्छङ्करभगवतः कृतौ काशीपञ्चकं संपूर्णम् ॥

Thus is completed the Kāśīpañcakam composed by the revered Śaṅkara Bhagavat, disciple of the venerable Govinda Bhagavat, the revered teacher who has renounced all, and supreme among wandering ascetics.

संपूर्णम् complete
