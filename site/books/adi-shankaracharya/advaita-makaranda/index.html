Verse 1

1

कटाक्षकिरणाचान्तनमन्मोहाब्धये नमः । अनन्तानन्दकृष्णाय जगन्मङ्गलमूर्तये ॥1॥

Salutation to Krishna, whose bliss is boundless and whose form blesses the world; the rays of his glance drink up the ocean of delusion in those who bow.

Word-for-word
कटाक्ष-किरण-आचान्त drunk dry by the rays of a glance
नमत्-मोह-अब्धये for the ocean of delusion of those who bow
नमः salutation
अनन्त-आनन्द-कृष्णाय to Krishna, whose bliss is boundless
जगत्-मङ्गल-मूर्तये to the auspicious form that blesses the world

Verse 2

2

अहमस्मि सदा भामि कदाचिन्नाहमप्रियः । ब्रह्मैवाऽहमतः सिद्धं सच्चिदानन्दलक्षणम् ॥2॥

I always exist and shine; I am never unwelcome. Therefore I am Brahman itself, whose nature is being, consciousness, and bliss.

Word-for-word
अहम् अस्मि I am
सदा भामि I always shine
कदाचित् न अहम् अप्रियः I am never unlovely
ब्रह्म एव अहम् I am Brahman alone
अतः सिद्धम् therefore established
सत्-चित्-आनन्द-लक्षणम् defined by being, consciousness, and bliss

Verse 3

3

मय्येवोदेति चिद्व्योम्नि जगद्गन्धर्वपत्तनम् । अतोऽहं न कथं ब्रह्म सर्वज्ञं सर्वकारणम् ॥3॥

The world, a phantom city in the sky, arises in me alone, the sky of consciousness. How, then, am I not Brahman, all-knowing and the cause of all?

Word-for-word
मयि एव उदेति arises in me alone
चित्-व्योम्नि in the sky of consciousness
जगत्-गन्धर्व-पत्तनम् the world, a phantom city
अतः therefore
अहम् I
न कथम् ब्रह्म how am I not Brahman?
सर्वज्ञम् सर्वकारणम् all-knowing and cause of all

Verse 4

4

न स्वतः प्रत्यभिज्ञानान्निरंशत्वान्न चान्यतः । न चाश्रयविनाशान्मे विनाशः स्यादनाश्रयात् ॥4॥

I cannot be destroyed by myself, since I recognize my continuity; nor by another, since I am partless; nor by the destruction of a support, for I depend on none.

Word-for-word
न स्वतः not by itself
प्रत्यभिज्ञानात् because of recognition
निरंशत्वात् because of being partless
न च अन्यतः nor by another
न च आश्रय-विनाशात् nor by destruction of a support
मे विनाशः स्यात् would my destruction occur
अनाश्रयात् because I have no support

Verse 5

5

न शोषप्लोषविक्लेदच्छेदाश्चिन्नभसो मम । सत्यैरप्यनिलाग्न्यम्भः शस्त्रैः किमुत कल्पितैः ॥5॥

The sky of consciousness that I am cannot be dried, burned, soaked, or cut by real wind, fire, water, or weapons—how much less by imagined ones.

Word-for-word
न शोष-प्लोष-विक्लेद-च्छेदाः no drying, burning, soaking, or cutting
चित्-नभसः मम of me, the sky of consciousness
सत्यैः अपि even by real
अनिल-अग्नि-अम्भः-शस्त्रैः wind, fire, water, and weapons
किम् उत कल्पितैः how much less by imagined ones

Verse 6

6

आभारूपस्य विश्वस्य भानं भासन्निधेर्विना । कदाचिन्नावकल्पेत भा चाहं तेन सर्वगः ॥6॥

This world of appearances could never shine without the presence of my light; and since I am that light, I am all-pervading.

Word-for-word
आभा-रूपस्य विश्वस्य of the world whose nature is appearance
भानम् shining
भास्-सन्निधेः विना without the presence of light
कदाचित् न अवकल्पेत could never occur
भा च अहम् and I am the light
तेन सर्वगः therefore all-pervading

Verse 7

7

न हि भानादृते सत्त्वं नर्ते भानं चितोऽचितः । चित्सम्भेदोऽपि नाध्यासादृते तेनाहमद्वयः ॥7॥

Nothing exists apart from manifestation, and the inert cannot shine apart from consciousness. Even their apparent connection comes only from superimposition; therefore I am nondual.

Word-for-word
न हि भानात् ऋते for not apart from manifestation
सत्त्वम् existence
न ऋते भानम् no shining apart from
चितः consciousness
अचितः of the inert
चित्-सम्भेदः अपि even the connection with consciousness
न अध्यासात् ऋते not apart from superimposition
तेन अहम् अद्वयः therefore I am nondual

Verse 8

8

न देहो नेन्द्रियं चाहं न प्राणो न मनो न धीः । ममतापरिरब्धत्वादाक्रीडत्वादिदं धियः ॥8॥

I am not the body, the senses, the vital force, the mind, or the intellect: they are held as “mine” and serve as objects in which the intellect plays.

Word-for-word
न देहः not the body
न इन्द्रियम् not the senses
च अहम् न प्राणः and I am not the vital force
न मनः न धीः not the mind, not the intellect
ममता-परिरब्धत्वात् because they are embraced by the notion “mine”
आक्रीडत्वात् because they are a playfield
इदम् धियः objects of the intellect

Verse 9

9

साक्षी सर्वान्वितः प्रेयानहं नाहं कदाचन । परिणामपरिच्छेदपरितापैरुपप्लवात् ॥9॥

I am the witness, present in all, and most dear; I am never the ego, which is beset by change, limitation, and suffering.

Word-for-word
साक्षी the witness
सर्व-अन्वितः present in all
प्रेयान् most dear
अहम् न अहम् I am not the ego
कदाचन at any time
परिणाम-परिच्छेद-परितापैः by change, limitation, and affliction
उपप्लवात् because it is troubled

Verse 10

10

सुप्तेऽहमि न दृश्यन्ते दुःखदोषप्रवृत्तयः । अतस्तस्यैव संसारो न मे संसर्तृसाक्षिणः ॥10॥

When the ego is asleep, suffering, faults, and their activities are not seen. Saṃsāra belongs to that ego alone, not to me, its witnessing awareness.

Word-for-word
सुप्ते अहम् when the ego is asleep
न दृश्यन्ते are not seen
दुःख-दोष-प्रवृत्तयः suffering, faults, and activities
अतः therefore
तस्य एव संसारः saṃsāra belongs to that alone
न मे not to me
संसर्तृ-साक्षिणः the witness of the one who transmigrates

Verse 11

11

सुप्तः सुप्तिं न जनाति नाऽसुप्ते स्वप्नजागरौ । जाग्रत्स्वप्नसुषुप्तीनां साक्ष्यतोऽहमतदृशः ॥11॥

In deep sleep, the sleeper does not know sleep; when not asleep, dream and waking are absent. Since I witness waking, dream, and deep sleep, I am distinct from all three.

Word-for-word
सुप्तः the sleeper
सुप्तिम् न जनाति does not know sleep
न असुप्ते when not asleep, not present
स्वप्न-जागरौ dream and waking
जाग्रत्-स्वप्न-सुषुप्तीनाम् of waking, dream, and deep sleep
साक्षी अतः because I am the witness
अहम् अतदृशः I am of a different nature

Verse 12

12

विज्ञानविरतिः सुप्तिस्तज्जन्म स्वप्नजागरौ । तत्साक्षिणः कथं मे स्युर्नित्यज्ञानस्य ते त्रयः ॥12॥

Sleep is the cessation of specific cognition; dream and waking are its arising. How could those three states belong to me, the witness whose awareness is eternal?

Word-for-word
विज्ञान-विरतिः cessation of particular cognition
सुप्तिः is sleep
तत्-जन्म its arising
स्वप्न-जागरौ are dream and waking
तत्-साक्षिणः of the witness of those
कथम् मे स्युः how could they be mine?
नित्य-ज्ञानस्य whose awareness is eternal
ते त्रयः those three

Verse 13

13

षड्विकारवतां वेत्ता निर्विकारोऽहमन्यथा । तद्विकारानुसन्धानं सर्वथा नावकल्पते ॥13॥

I know things subject to six changes; I am therefore changeless. Otherwise, no awareness of their changes could ever be possible.

Word-for-word
षड्-विकारवताम् of those having six changes
वेत्ता the knower
निर्विकारः अहम् I am changeless
अन्यथा otherwise
तत्-विकार-अनुसन्धानम् recognition of their changes
सर्वथा in any way
न अवकल्पते could not occur

Verse 14

14

तेन तेन हि रूपेण जायते लीयते मुहुः । विकारि वस्तु तस्यैषामनुसन्धातृता कुतः ॥14॥

A changing thing is born and dissolves again and again in each successive form. How could it be the knower that traces all those changes?

Word-for-word
तेन तेन रूपेण in each successive form
हि indeed
जायते is born
लीयते dissolves
मुहुः again and again
विकारि वस्तु the changing thing
तस्य एषाम् of it, these
अनुसन्धातृता कुतः how could there be the knowing continuity?

Verse 15

15

न च स्वजन्म नाशं वा द्रष्टुमर्हति कश्चन । तौ हि प्रागुत्तराभावचरमप्रथमक्षणौ ॥15॥

No one can witness their own birth or death, for these are respectively the first moment after prior nonexistence and the last before what follows is absent.

Word-for-word
न च and not
स्व-जन्म one's own birth
नाशम् वा or death
द्रष्टुम् अर्हति is able to see
कश्चन anyone
तौ हि for those two
प्राक्-उत्तर-अभाव the absence before and after
चरम-प्रथम-क्षणौ the final and initial moments

Verse 16

16

न प्रकाशेऽहमित्युक्तिर्यत्प्रकाशैकबन्धना । स्वप्रकाशं तमात्मानमप्रकाशः कथं स्पृशेत् ॥16॥

The statement “I do not shine” depends wholly on light. How could darkness touch the Self, which is self-luminous?

Word-for-word
न प्रकाशे अहम् इति उक्तिः the statement “I do not shine”
यत् which
प्रकाश-एक-बन्धना depends solely on light
स्व-प्रकाशम् self-luminous
तम् आत्मानम् that Self
अप्रकाशः darkness
कथम् स्पृशेत् how could it touch?

Verse 17

17

तथाप्याभाति कोऽप्येष विचाराभावजीवनः । अवश्यायश्चिदाकाशे विचारार्कोदयावधिः ॥17॥

Even so, a certain mist appears in the sky of consciousness, sustained by lack of inquiry; it lasts only until the sun of inquiry rises.

Word-for-word
तथा अपि even so
आभाति appears
कः अपि एषः this certain
विचार-अभाव-जीवनः sustained by absence of inquiry
अवश्यायः mist
चित्-आकाशे in the sky of consciousness
विचार-अर्क-उदय-अवधिः lasting until the sun of inquiry rises

Verse 18

18

आत्माज्ञानमहानिद्राजृम्भितेऽस्मिन् जगन्मये । दीर्घस्वप्ने स्फुरन्त्येते स्वर्गमोक्षादिविभ्रमाः ॥18॥

In this world-dream, expanded by the great sleep of ignorance of the Self, such appearances as heaven and liberation shine.

Word-for-word
आत्म-अज्ञान-महा-निद्रा the great sleep of ignorance of the Self
जृम्भिते unfolded
अस्मिन् जगत्-मये in this world-filled
दीर्घ-स्वप्ने in the long dream
स्फुरन्ति shine
एते these
स्वर्ग-मोक्ष-आदि-विभ्रमाः appearances such as heaven and liberation

Verse 19

19

जडाजडविभागोऽयमजडे मयि कल्पितः । भित्तिभागे समे चित्रचराचरविभागवत् ॥19॥

This division between inert and conscious is imagined in me, who am never inert, as figures of moving and unmoving things are divided on a single flat wall.

Word-for-word
जड-अजड-विभागः अयम् this distinction between inert and conscious
अजडे मयि in me, who am not inert
कल्पितः is imagined
भित्ति-भागे समे on a single even wall
चित्र-चर-अचर-विभाग-वत् like the division of moving and unmoving figures in a picture

Verse 20

20

चेत्योपरागरूपा मे साक्षितापि न तात्त्विकी । उपलक्षणमेवेयं निस्तरङ्गचिदम्बुधेः ॥20॥

Even my witnesshood, shaped by association with what is known, is not ultimately real; it merely indicates the wave-free ocean of consciousness.

Word-for-word
चेत्य-उपराग-रूपा taking form through association with the known
मे साक्षिता अपि even my witnesshood
न तात्त्विकी is not ultimately real
उपलक्षणम् एव इयम् this is only an indication
निस्तरङ्ग-चित्-अम्बुधेः of the wave-free ocean of consciousness

Verse 21

21

अमृताब्धेर्न मे जीर्णिर्मृषा डिण्डीरजन्मभिः । स्फटिकार्द्रो मे रागः स्वाप्नसंध्याभ्रविभ्रमैः ॥21॥

The ocean of immortality that I am is not diminished by the false birth of foam; dreamlike twilight-cloud illusions do not stain me, clear as crystal.

Word-for-word
अमृत-अब्धेः of the ocean of immortality
न मे जीर्णिः no wearing away for me
मृषा डिण्डीर-जन्मभिः through the false birth of foam
स्फटिक-अद्रः मे for me, clear as crystal
रागः stain or coloring
स्वप्न-सन्ध्या-अभ्र-विभ्रमैः from dreamlike twilight-cloud illusions

Verse 22

22

स्वरूपमेव मे सत्त्वं न तु धर्मो नभस्त्ववत् । मदन्यस्य सतोऽभावान्न हि सा जातिरिष्यते ॥22॥

Existence is my very nature, not an attribute such as “being sky.” Since no existent thing is other than me, existence is not a separate class inhering in me.

Word-for-word
स्वरूपम् एव the very nature
मे सत्त्वम् of me is existence
न तु धर्मः not an attribute
नभस्त्व-वत् like skyhood
मत्-अन्यस्य सतः अभावात् because no existent thing is other than me
न हि सा जातिः इष्यते that universal class is not accepted

Verse 23

23

स्वरूपमेव मे ज्ञानं न गुणः स गुणो यदि । अनात्मत्वमसत्त्वं वा ज्ञेयाज्ञेयत्वयोः पतेत् ॥23॥

Consciousness is my very nature, not a quality. If it were a quality, I would be either non-self as something knowable, or nonexistent as something unknowable.

Word-for-word
स्वरूपम् एव the very nature
मे ज्ञानम् of me is awareness
न गुणः not a quality
सः गुणः यदि if it were a quality
अनात्मत्वम् non-selfhood
असत्त्वम् वा or nonexistence
ज्ञेय-अज्ञेयत्वयोः in the alternatives of being knowable or unknowable

Verse 24

24

अहमेव सुखं नान्यदन्यच्चेन्नैव तत् सुखम् । अमदर्थं न हि प्रेयो मदर्थं न स्वतः प्रियम् ॥24॥

I alone am happiness; nothing else is. If something were other than me, it could not be happiness: nothing is dear apart from me, and nothing is dear in itself.

Word-for-word
अहम् एव सुखम् I alone am happiness

न एव तत् सुखम् that would not be happiness
अमत्-अर्थम् न not apart from me
हि प्रेयः is anything dear
मत्-अर्थम् for my sake
न स्वतः प्रियम् nothing is dear by itself

Verse 25

25

न हि नानास्वरूपं स्यादेकं वस्तु कदाचन । तस्मादखण्ड एवास्मि विजहज्जागतीं भिदाम् ॥25॥

A single reality can never have many natures. Therefore I am indivisible, having left behind the distinctions of the world.

एकम् वस्तु one reality
कदाचन ever
तस्मात् therefore
अखण्डः एव अस्मि I am indeed indivisible

Verse 26

26

परोक्षतापरिच्छेदशाबल्यापोहनिर्मलम् । तदसीति गिरा लक्ष्यमहमेकरसं महः ॥26॥

The radiant, undivided essence indicated by “That thou art” is me, purified of indirectness, finitude, and the defects that obscure identity.

अहम् एक-रसम् महः I, the one-essence radiance

Verse 27

27

उपशान्तजगज्जीवशिष्याचार्येश्वरभ्रमम् । स्वतः सिद्धमनाद्यन्तं परिपूर्णमहं महः ॥27॥

I am the self-established, beginningless, endless, all-complete radiance in which the mistaken notions of world, individual, student, teacher, and Lord have come to rest.

Verse 28

28
