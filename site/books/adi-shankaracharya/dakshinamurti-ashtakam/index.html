Verse 1

1

विश्वं दर्पणदृश्यमाननगरीतुल्यं निजान्तर्गतं पश्यन्नात्मनि मायया बहिरिवोद्भूतं यथा निद्रया । यः साक्षात्कुरुते प्रबोधसमये स्वात्मानमेवाद्वयं तस्मै श्रीगुरुमूर्तये नम इदं श्रीदक्षिणामूर्तये ॥

The universe, like a city seen in a mirror, is within oneself, yet appears by māyā as though brought forth outside, as in a dream through sleep. Whoever, at awakening, directly realizes the non-dual Self within, to that auspicious Dakṣiṇāmūrti, embodied as the teacher, this salutation is offered.

Verse 2

2

बीजस्यान्तरिवाङ्कुरो जगदिदं प्राङ्निर्विकल्पं पुनर्मायाकल्पितदेशकालकलनावैचित्र्यचित्रीकृतम् । मायावीव विजृम्भयत्यपि महायोगीव यः स्वेच्छया तस्मै श्रीगुरुमूर्तये नम इदं श्रीदक्षिणामूर्तये ॥

This world, like a sprout within a seed, was at first undifferentiated; later it is made variegated through māyā’s imagined distinctions of place, time, and measure. Whoever unfolds it at will, like a magician or a great yogin—to that auspicious Dakṣiṇāmūrti, embodied as the teacher, this salutation is offered.

Verse 3

3

यस्यैव स्फुरणं सदात्मकमसत्कल्पार्थगं भासते साक्षात्तत्त्वमसीति वेदवचसा यो बोधयत्याश्रितान् । यत्साक्षात्करणाद्भवेन्न पुनरावृत्तिर्भवाम्भोनिधौ तस्मै श्रीगुरुमूर्तये नम इदं श्रीदक्षिणामूर्तये ॥

By whose very light, whose nature is Being, even imagined unreal objects shine; who instructs those who take refuge in him through the Vedic sentence “That thou art”; by directly realizing whom there is no return to the ocean of becoming—to that auspicious Dakṣiṇāmūrti, embodied as the teacher, this salutation is offered.

साक्षात् directly

नमः इदम् this salutation
श्री-दक्षिणामूर्तये to the revered Dakṣiṇāmūrti

Verse 4

4

नानाच्छिद्रघटोदरस्थितमहादीपप्रभाभास्वरं ज्ञानं यस्य तु चक्षुरादिकरणद्वारा बहिः स्पन्दते । जानामीति तमेव भान्तमनुभात्येतत्समस्तं जगत्तस्मै श्रीगुरुमूर्तये नम इदं श्रीदक्षिणामूर्तये ॥

The knowledge that shines like the light of a great lamp placed inside a pot pierced with many holes shines outward through the instruments beginning with the eyes. “I know,” one says; this entire world shines after that very luminous One—to that auspicious Dakṣiṇāmūrti, embodied as the teacher, this salutation is offered.

Verse 5

5

देहं प्राणमपीन्द्रियाण्यपि चलां बुद्धिं च शून्यं विदुः स्त्रीबालान्धजडोपमास्त्वहमिति भ्रान्ता भृशं वादिनः । मायाशक्तिविलासकल्पितमहाव्यामोहसंहारिणे तस्मै श्रीगुरुमूर्तये नम इदं श्रीदक्षिणामूर्तये ॥

Those who argue in deep delusion, “I am the body, the vital breath, the senses, the restless intellect, or the void,” are like women, children, the blind, or the dull. To the destroyer of the great bewilderment fashioned by māyā’s power and play—to that auspicious Dakṣiṇāmūrti, embodied as the teacher, this salutation is offered.

Verse 6

6

राहुग्रस्तदिवाकरेन्दुसदृशो मायासमाच्छादनात्सन्मात्रः करणोपसंहरणतो योऽभूत्सुषुप्तः पुमान् । प्रागस्वाप्समिति प्रबोधसमये यः प्रत्यभिज्ञायते तस्मै श्रीगुरुमूर्तये नम इदं श्रीदक्षिणामूर्तये ॥

Like the sun or moon seized by Rāhu, the person obscured by māyā becomes mere Being when the instruments are withdrawn in deep sleep. At waking, that same person is recognized as saying, “I slept before.” To that auspicious Dakṣiṇāmūrti, embodied as the teacher, this salutation is offered.

प्रबोध-समये at the time of waking
यः who
प्रत्यभिज्ञायते is recognized again

Verse 7

7

बाल्यादिष्वपि जाग्रदादिषु तथा सर्वास्ववस्थास्वपि व्यावृत्तास्वनुवर्तमानमहमित्यन्तः स्फुरन्तं सदा । स्वात्मानं प्रकटीकरोति भजतां यो मुद्रया भद्रया तस्मै श्रीगुरुमूर्तये नम इदं श्रीदक्षिणामूर्तये ॥

Across childhood and the other stages of life, across waking and the other states, through all changing conditions, the one who ever continues to shine within as “I” makes the Self manifest to those who worship him by the auspicious hand gesture. To that Dakṣiṇāmūrti, embodied as the teacher, this salutation is offered.

Verse 8

8

विश्वं पश्यति कार्यकारणतया स्वस्वामिसम्बन्धतः शिष्याचार्यतया तथैव पितृपुत्राद्यात्मना भेदतः । स्वप्ने जाग्रति वा य एष पुरुषो मायापरिभ्रामितस्तस्मै श्रीगुरुमूर्तये नम इदं श्रीदक्षिणामूर्तये ॥

The person bewildered by māyā sees the world as cause and effect, as the relation of owner and owned, as teacher and student, and likewise as father, son, and other distinctions, whether in dream or waking. To that auspicious Dakṣiṇāmūrti, embodied as the teacher, this salutation is offered.

Verse 9

9

भूरम्भांस्यनलोऽनिलोऽम्बरमहर्नाथो हिमांशुः पुमानित्याभाति चराचरात्मकमिदं यस्यैव मूर्त्यष्टकम् । नान्यत्किंचन विद्यते विमृशतां यस्मात्परस्माद्विभोस्तस्मै श्रीगुरुमूर्तये नम इदं श्रीदक्षिणामूर्तये ॥

Earth, waters, fire, air, ether, the sun, the moon, and the conscious Person shine as this eightfold form of the One whose nature is all that moves and does not move. For those who discern, nothing whatever exists apart from that supreme, all-pervading One. To that Dakṣiṇāmūrti, embodied as the teacher, this salutation is offered.

Verse 10

10

सर्वात्मत्वमिति स्फुटीकृतमिदं यस्मादमुष्मिंस्तवे तेनास्य श्रवणात्तदर्थमननाद्ध्यानाच्च संकीर्तनात् । सर्वात्मत्वमहाविभूतिसहितं स्यादीश्वरत्वं स्वतःसिद्ध्येत्तत्पुनरष्टधा परिणतं चैश्वर्यमव्याहृतम् ॥

This hymn clearly sets forth the state of being the Self of all. By hearing it, reflecting on its meaning, meditating on it, and reciting it, sovereignty as Lord arises of itself, together with the great power of universal Selfhood; that unobstructed sovereignty is again expressed in eight forms.

Closing colophon

Colophon

इति श्रीमत्परमहंसपरिव्राजकाचार्यस्य श्रीगोविन्दभगवत्पूज्यपादशिष्यस्य श्रीमच्छङ्करभगवतः कृतौ श्रीदक्षिणामूर्त्यष्टकं संपूर्णम् ॥

Thus is completed the Śrī Dakṣiṇāmūrtyaṣṭakam, composed by the revered Śaṅkara Bhagavat, disciple of the venerable Govinda Bhagavat, the revered teacher and supreme among wandering ascetics.
