Contents

  1. Verses 1–10Turn from greed and delusion; face life’s brevity; seek wise company and truth.
  2. Verses 11–20See the pull of time and desire; weigh renunciation, knowledge, and devotion.
  3. Verses 21–30Seek release from rebirth through inquiry, even-mindedness, discipline, and meditation.
  4. Verse 31 · Closing instructionRely on the guru; steady the senses and mind; see the Lord within.
