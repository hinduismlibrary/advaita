Verse 1

1

नाहं देहो नेन्द्रियाण्यन्तरङ्गो नाहंकारः प्राणवर्गो न बुद्धिः । दारापत्यक्षेत्रवित्तादिदूरः साक्षी नित्यः प्रत्यगात्मा शिवोऽहम् ॥

I am not the body, the senses, the inner faculty, the ego, the vital breaths, or the intellect. I am far removed from spouse, children, land, wealth, and the rest: I am the eternal witness, the inward Self, Śiva.

प्रत्यक्-आत्मन् inward Self
शिवः अहम् I am Śiva, the auspicious Self

Verse 2

2

रज्ज्वज्ञानाद्भाति रज्जौ यथाहिः स्वात्माज्ञानादात्मनो जीवभावः । आप्तोक्त्याहिभ्रान्तिनाशे स रज्जुर्जीवो नाहं देशिकोक्त्या शिवोऽहम् ॥

Just as, through ignorance of the rope, a snake appears on the rope, so from ignorance of the Self the Self seems to be an individual soul. When the snake-delusion is dispelled by a trusted statement, what remains is the rope; by the teacher’s statement, I am not the limited soul: I am Śiva.

Word-for-word
रज्जु-अज्ञानात् from ignorance of the rope
भाति appears
रज्जौ on the rope
यथा • अहि just as • snake
स्व-आत्म-अज्ञानात् from ignorance of one’s own Self
आत्मनः • जीव-भाव of the Self • individual-soul state
आप्त-उक्ति trusted statement
अहि-भ्रान्ति-नाशे when the snake-delusion is destroyed
सः • रज्जु that • [is] the rope
जीवः • न अहम् limited individual • am not I
देशिक-उक्त्या by the teacher’s statement
शिवः अहम् I am Śiva, the auspicious Self

Verse 3

3

आभातीदं विश्वमात्मन्यसत्यं सत्यज्ञानानन्दरूपे विमोहात् । निद्रामोहात्स्वप्नवत्तन्न सत्यं शुद्धः पूर्णो नित्य एकः शिवोऽहम् ॥

Through delusion, this unreal universe appears in the Self, whose nature is reality, knowledge, and bliss. Like a dream produced by sleep-delusion, it is not ultimately real. I am pure, complete, eternal, one—Śiva.

तत् • न सत्यम् that • is not real

Verse 4

4

नाहं जातो न प्रवृद्धो न नष्टो देहस्योक्ताः प्राकृताः सर्वधर्माः । कर्तृत्वादिश्चिन्मयस्यास्ति नाहंकारस्यैव ह्यात्मनो मे शिवोऽहम् ॥

I was not born, did not grow, and was not destroyed; all these natural conditions are declared to belong to the body. Agency and the rest belong to the ego, not to my conscious Self. I am Śiva.

Word-for-word

कर्तृत्व • आदि agency • and the rest
चित्-मयस्य of the consciousness-natured [Self]
अहंकारस्य • एव of the ego • alone
हि • आत्मनः • मे indeed • of the Self • my
शिवः अहम् I am Śiva, the auspicious Self

Verse 5

5

मत्तो नान्यत्किंचिदत्रास्ति विश्वं सत्यं बाह्यं वस्तु मायोपकॢप्तम् । आदर्शान्तर्भासमानस्य तुल्यं मय्यद्वैते भाति तस्माच्छिवोऽहम् ॥

There is nothing here apart from me. The universe, taken as a real external object, is fashioned by māyā. It appears in non-dual me like an image shining within a mirror; therefore I am Śiva.

शिवः अहम् I am Śiva, the auspicious Self

Closing colophon

Colophon

इति श्रीमत्परमहंसपरिव्राजकाचार्यस्य श्रीगोविन्दभगवत्पूज्यपादशिष्यस्य श्रीमच्छङ्करभगवतः कृतौ अद्वैतपञ्चरत्नं संपूर्णम् ॥

Thus is completed the Advaitapañcaratnam composed by the revered Śaṅkara Bhagavat, disciple of the venerable Govinda Bhagavat, the revered teacher who has renounced all, and supreme among wandering ascetics.

Word-for-word
इति thus

श्री-गोविन्द-भगवत् revered Govinda Bhagavat
पूज्य-पाद whose feet are worthy of worship
शिष्य disciple
श्रीमत्-शङ्कर-भगवत् the revered Śaṅkara Bhagavat
कृतौ in the composition
अद्वैत-पञ्चरत्नम् the five jewels of non-duality
संपूर्णम् complete
