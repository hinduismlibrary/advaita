## Text 1

### Devanagari

सूत उवाच—
यं ब्रह्मा वरुणेन्द्र-रुद्र-मरुतः स्तुन्वन्ति दिव्यैः स्तवैः
वेदैः साङ्ग-पद-क्रमोपनिषदैर् गायन्ति यं सामगाः ।
ध्यानावस्थित-तद्गतेन मनसा पश्यन्ति यं योगिनः
यस्यान्तं न विदुः सुरासुर-गणाः देवाय तस्मै नमः ॥ १ ॥

### Verse text

sūta uvāca—
yaṃ brahmā varuṇendra-rudra-marutaḥ stunvanti divyaiḥ stavaiḥ
vedaiḥ sāṅga-pada-kramopaniṣadair gāyanti yaṃ sāmagāḥ |
dhyānāvasthita-tadgatena manasā paśyanti yaṃ yoginaḥ
yasyāntaṃ na viduḥ surāsura-gaṇāḥ devāya tasmai namaḥ

### Synonyms

सूतः — Sūta; उवाच — said; यम् — whom; ब्रह्मा — Brahmā; वरुण-इन्द्र-रुद्र-मरुतः — Varuṇa, Indra, Rudra and the Maruts; स्तुन्वन्ति — praise; दिव्यैः — with divine; स्तवैः — hymns; वेदैः — with the Vedas; स-अङ्ग-पद-क्रम-उपनिषदैः — with their branches, word-sequences and Upaniṣads; गायन्ति — sing; यम् — whom; साम-गाः — Sāma singers; ध्यान-अवस्थित-तद्-गतेन — fixed in meditation on Him; मनसा — with the mind; पश्यन्ति — see; यम् — whom; योगिनः — yogīs; यस्य — whose; अन्तम् — end; न — not; विदुः — know; सुर-असुर-गणाः — hosts of gods and demons; देवाय — to the God; तस्मै — to Him; नमः — obeisance

### Translation

Sūta said: Those whom Brahmā, Varuṇa, Indra, Rudra and the Maruts praise with divine hymns, whom the Vedas with their branches, word-sequences and Upaniṣads sing, whom yogīs see with minds fixed in meditation on Him, whose end the hosts of gods and demons do not know—to that God, obeisance.

## Text 2

### Verse text

yat-saṃskāra-kalā-anuvartana-vaśād velā-nibhenāmbhasāṃ

### Synonyms

पृष्ठे — on the back; भ्राम्यत् — revolving; अमन्द — heavy; मन्दर-गिरि — Mandara Mountain; ग्राव-अग्र — stone-points; कण्डूयनात् — from scratching; निद्रालोः — of the sleepy one; कमठ-आकृतेः — of tortoise form; भगवतः — of the Lord; श्वास-अनिलाः — breath-winds; पान्तु — may they protect; वः — you; यत् — whose; संस्कार — impression; कला — trace; अनुवर्तन-वशात् — by following the effect; वेला-निभेन — resembling a tide; अम्भसाम् — of waters; यातायातम् — coming and going; अतन्द्रितम् — ceaseless; जल-निधेः — of the ocean; न — not; अद्य — today; अपि — even; विश्राम्यति — ceases

### Translation

Śrīmad-Bhāgavata, the spotless Purāṇa dear to the Vaiṣṇavas, in which the one spotless supreme knowledge of paramahaṁsas is sung, reveals there freedom from action joined with knowledge, renunciation and devotion; a person hearing it, reciting it well, intent on inquiry, is liberated by devotion.
