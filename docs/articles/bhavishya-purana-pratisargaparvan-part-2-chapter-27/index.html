BP 3.2.27.1

सूत उवाच ।
अथेतिहासं शृणुत यथा भिल्लाः कृतार्थिनः ।
विचरंतो वने नित्यं निषादाः काष्ठवाहिनः ॥ 1 ॥

Sūta said: Now listen to the history of the Bhillas, the wood-carrying Niṣādas who found fulfillment while wandering in the forest.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
सूत sūta Sūta
उवाच uvāca said
अथेतिहासं athetihāsaṃ now this history
शृणुत śṛṇuta listen
यथा yathā as
भिल्लाः bhillāḥ the Bhillas
कृतार्थिनः kṛtārthinaḥ made successful
विचरंतो vicaraṃto wandering
वने vane in the forest
नित्यं nityaṃ always
निषादाः niṣādāḥ Nishadas
काष्ठवाहिनः kāṣṭhavāhinaḥ wood-carriers
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

sūta uvāca | athetihāsaṃ śṛṇuta yathā bhillāḥ kṛtārthinaḥ | vicaraṃto vane nityaṃ niṣādāḥ kāṣṭhavāhinaḥ || 1 ||
