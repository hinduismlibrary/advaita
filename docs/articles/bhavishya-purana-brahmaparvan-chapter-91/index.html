Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 91 — The Observance That Removes Disease

BP 1.91.1

ब्रह्मोवाच । पूष्णो भवेद् यदा ऋक्षं भवेच् च भगदैवतम् । वासरः स महान् प्रोक्तः सर्वरोगभयापहः ॥ ॥

Brahmā said. When the asterism of Pūṣan occurs and Bhaga is its presiding deity, that day is declared great and removes the fear of every disease.

brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said;
pūṣṇaḥ — of Pūṣan;
bhavet — may be, occurs;
yadā — when;
ṛkṣam — asterism;
bhavet — may be, is;
ca — and;
bhaga-daivatam — having Bhaga as its presiding deity;
vāsaraḥ — day;
saḥ — that;
mahān — great;
proktaḥ — proclaimed;
sarva-roga-bhaya-apahaḥ — remover of the fear of every disease.

BP 1.91.2

योऽत्र पूजयते भानुं शुभगन्धविलेपनैः । सर्वरोगविनिर्मुक्तो याति भानुसमोकताम् ॥ ॥

Whoever worships the Sun here with auspicious perfumes and ointments, freed from every disease, goes to the world of the Sun.

yaḥ — whoever;
atra — here;
pūjayate — worships;
bhānum — Bhānu;
śubha-gandha-vilepanaiḥ — with auspicious fragrances and unguents;
sarva-roga-vinirmuktaḥ — freed from every disease;
yāti — goes;
bhānu-salokataṃ — to the world of Bhānu.

BP 1.91.3

अर्कपत्रपुटे कृत्या पुष्पाण्यर्कस्य सुव्रत । देवस्य पुरतो रात्रौ भक्त्या यः स्थापयेत् बुधः ॥ ॥

Making a cup from milkweed leaves and milkweed flowers, the wise one should place it before the god at night with devotion, O good vowed one.

arka-patra-puṭe — in a cup made of arka leaves;
kṛtyā — having made;
puṣpāṇi — flowers;
arkasya — of arka;
suvrata — O well-disciplined one;
devasya — of the deity;
purataḥ — before;
rātrau — at night;
bhaktyā — with devotion;
yaḥ — whoever;
sthāpayet — should place;
budhaḥ — the wise person.

BP 1.91.4

पूजयित्वार्कपुष्पैस्तु अर्कमर्कप्रियं सदा । प्राशयित्वार्कपुष्पं तु दत्त्वा विप्राय दक्षिणाम् ॥ ॥

Having worshipped the Sun, ever dear to milkweed, with milkweed flowers, and having consumed a milkweed flower, he should give a gift to a Brahmin.

pūjayitvā — having worshiped;
arka-puṣpaiḥ — with arka flowers;
tu — indeed;
arkam — Arka, the deity or arka tree;
arka-priyam — dear to Arka;
sadā — always;
prāśayitvā — having consumed;
arka-puṣpam — an arka flower;
tu — indeed;
dattvā — having given;
viprāya — to a Brāhmaṇa;
dakṣiṇām — a gift, sacrificial fee.

BP 1.91.5

भक्त्या च पायसं वीर रात्रौ स्वपिति भूतले । अनेन विधिना यस्तु पूजयेतत्र वै रविम् ॥ ॥

With devotion he eats rice pudding and sleeps on the ground at night, O hero. Whoever worships the Sun here according to this procedure.

bhaktyā — with devotion;
ca — and;
pāyasam — payasa, milk-rice;
vīra — O hero;
rātrau — at night;
svapiti — sleeps;
bhū-tale — upon the ground;
anena vidhinā — by this procedure;
yaḥ tu — whoever;
pūjayet — should worship;
atra — here;
vai — indeed;
ravim — Ravi.

BP 1.91.6

स मुक्तः सर्वरोगैंस्तु गच्छेद्दिनकरालयम् । तस्मादपि व्रजेल्लोकं फुंकाररवहेतिनः ॥ ॥

Freed from all diseases, he goes to the abode of the Sun. From there he goes to the world produced by the utterance of the syllable Phuṃ.

saḥ — he;
muktaḥ — freed;
sarva-rogaiḥ — from every disease;
tu — indeed;
gacchet — goes;
dinakara-ālayam — to the abode of Dīnakara;
tasmāt — from there;
api — also;
vrajet — proceeds;
lokam — to the world;
phuṃkāra-rava-hetinaḥ — caused by the sound of the phuṃkāra.
