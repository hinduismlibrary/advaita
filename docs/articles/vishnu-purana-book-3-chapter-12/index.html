VP 3.12.1

देवगोब्राह्मणान् सिद्धान् वृद्धाचार्यांस् तथार्चयेत् । द्विकालं च नमेत् सन्ध्याम् अग्नीन् उपचरेत् तथा ॥१॥

One should honour the gods, cows, Brāhmaṇas, perfected beings, elders, and teachers; one should salute the Sandhyā twice daily and likewise tend the sacred fires.

Śrīdhara translation

Śrīdhara glosses brāhma-muhūrta-ārabhya niśītha-antam nirūpitāḥ as “described from Brahma-muhūrta through midnight”. He glosses sad-ācārāḥ dvādaśe tu te eva aniyama-īritāḥ as “in the twelfth, those same good practices are stated without fixed rule”. He glosses prāyeṇa tu gṛhasthānām tri-varga-para-cetasām as “mostly for householders whose minds are devoted to the three aims”. He glosses dṛṣṭa-adṛṣṭa-pradhānānām sad-dharmāṇām iha uktayaḥ as “the teachings here concern good duties focused on seen and unseen results”.
