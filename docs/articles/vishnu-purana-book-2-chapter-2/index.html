VP 2.2.1

कथितो भवता ब्रह्मन् सर्गः स्वायम्भुवश् च मे । श्रोतुम् इच्छाम्य् अहं त्वत्तः सकलं मण्डलं भुवः ॥१॥

Maitreya said—You have told me of the creation of Svāyambhuva, O Brahman; I wish to hear from you the whole expanse of the earth.

Śrīdhara translation

After repeating what was related, Maitreya asks, wishing to know the cosmography in detail: “You have told [me].” Since Manu is an aṃśa of Svayambhu, Manu is called Svayambhu here. His creation, beginning with Priyavrata, has been described. Through that account, with the divisions of islands, varṣas, and the rest stated, I wish to hear about the earth and the rest.

Śrīdhara translation

Now he states the boundary mountains of the eastern and western varṣas, Bhadrāśva east of Meru and Ketumāla west: “Ānīla,” and so on. Nīla is to the north and Niṣadha to the south; the two whose length extends between these limits are called Ānīla-Niṣadha-length. Mālyavat is to the west and Gandhamādana to the east. These two extend north to south between Nīla and Niṣadha, on the two sides of Ilāvṛta, as Śuka states. Some, however, give their positions in reverse. “Meru, together with Ilāvṛta, lies between them”—this is to be understood, since Ilāvṛta has been said to extend nine thousand yojanas in each of the four directions around Meru. This implies that the two mountains are thirty-four thousand yojanas long.
