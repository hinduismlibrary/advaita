BP 3.2.21.1

सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा स तु वैतालो राजानं प्राह नम्रधीः ।
जयस्थलपुरे रम्ये वर्धमानो नृपोभवत् ॥ 1 ॥

Sūta said: Having heard this, the Vetāla again told King Vikrama a story: In the lovely city of Jayasthala there lived a king.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

उवाच uvāca said
इत्युक्त्वा ityuktvā having said this
स sa that one
तु tu but
वैतालो vaitālo Vetāla
राजानं rājānaṃ the king
प्राह prāha said
नम्रधीः namradhīḥ humble-minded
जयस्थलपुरे jayasthalapure in the city of Jayasthala
रम्ये ramye lovely
वर्धमानो vardhamāno prospering; dwelling
नृपोभवत् nṛpobhavat was a king
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

sūta uvāca | ityuktvā sa tu vaitālo rājānaṃ prāha namradhīḥ | jayasthalapure ramye vardhamāno nṛpobhavat || 1 ||

राजन्मूर्खो हि कस्तेषां श्रुत्वा राजाब्रवीदिदम् ॥ 19 ॥

Sūta said: Having told this, the Vetāla asked King Vikrama, O king, who among them was the fool? The king answered:

बोधितो येन स व्याघ्रः स मूर्खस्त्वधिको मतः ।

The one who awakened the tiger is considered the greatest fool. Hearing this, the excellent Brahmin Vetāla spoke again.
