Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 96 — The Seventh Day of Victory

BP 1.96.1

दिण्डिरुवाच। यच्चैताः सप्त सप्तम्यो भवता कथिता मम। तासां या प्रथमा देव कथिता सा सविस्तरा ॥

BP 1.96.3

BP 1.96.10

प्राप्येह देवशार्दुल सूर्यलोकं स गच्छति। तस्मादेत्य पुनर्भूमौ राजराजो भवेद्बुधः ॥

Obtaining these here, O tiger among the gods, he goes to the world of the Sun. Coming from there again to earth, the wise one becomes a king of kings.

BP 1.96.32

Thus, O hero, this seventh day called Jaya has been described. Performed, remembered, or heard, it bestows the world of the Sun.
