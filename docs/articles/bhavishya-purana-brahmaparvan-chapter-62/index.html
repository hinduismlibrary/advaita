Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 62 — The Dialogue of Diṇḍi and the Sun

BP 1.62.1

सुमन्तुरुवाच ।
अथान्यं सरहस्यं तु संवादं वच्मि तेऽखिलम् ।
सूर्यस्य दिण्डिना सार्धं सर्वपापप्रणाशनम् ॥ १ ॥

Sumantu said. I shall now tell you the whole of another secret dialogue between the Sun and Diṇḍi, which destroys all sins.

sumantuḥ uvāca — Sumantu said; atha — now; anyam — another; sa-rahasyam — secret; tu — indeed; saṃvādam — dialogue; vacmi — I shall tell; te akhilam — to you completely; sūryasya — of Sūrya; diṇḍinā sārdham — together with Diṇḍi; sarva-pāpa-praṇāśanam — destroying every sin

BP 1.62.2

ब्रह्महत्याभिभूतस्तु परा दिंडिर्महातपाः ।
आराधनाय देवस्य स्तोत्रं चक्रे महात्मनः ॥ २ ॥

The great ascetic Diṇḍi, overcome by the sin of killing a Brahmin, composed a hymn for worship of the great souled god.

brahma-hatyā-abhibhūtaḥ — overcome by brahmahatyā; tu — indeed; parāḥ — great; diṇḍiḥ — Diṇḍi; mahā-tapāḥ — a great ascetic; ārādhanāya — for worship; devasya — of the god; stotram cakre — made a hymn; mahātmanaḥ — of the great-souled one

BP 1.62.3

श्रुत्वा तस्यार्थतः स्तोत्रं तुतोष भगवान् रविः ।
उवाच देवदेवस्तं दिण्डिनं गणनायकम् ॥ ३ ॥

Having heard the hymn and its meaning, the Blessed Sun was pleased. The God of gods spoke to Diṇḍi, leader of the hosts.

śrutvā — having heard; tasya stotram — his hymn; arthataḥ — according to its meaning; tutoṣa — was pleased; bhagavān raviḥ — the Blessed Ravi; uvāca — spoke; deva-devaḥ — God of gods; tam diṇḍinam — to that Diṇḍi; gaṇa-nāyakam — leader of the hosts

BP 1.62.4

आदित्य उवाच ।
हन्त दिण्डे प्रसन्नोऽस्मि भक्त्या स्तोत्रेण तेऽनघ ।
वरं वृणीष्व धर्मज्ञ यत्ते मनसि वर्तते ॥ ४ ॥

The Sun said. Well, Diṇḍi, sinless one, I am pleased by your devotion and hymn. Choose a boon, knower of righteousness, whatever is in your mind.

āditya uvāca — Āditya said; hanta — well then; diṇḍe — O Diṇḍi; prasannaḥ asmi — I am pleased; bhaktyā — by devotion; stotreṇa — by the hymn; te — your; anagha — O sinless one; varam vṛṇīṣva — choose a boon; dharma-jña — O knower of dharma; yat — whatever; te manasi vartate — is present in your mind

BP 1.62.5

दिण्डिरुवाच ।
एष एव वरः श्लाघ्यो यत्प्राप्तोऽसि ममांतकम् ।
त्वद्दर्शनमपुण्यानां स्वप्नेष्वपि च दुर्लभम् ॥ ५ ॥

Diṇḍi said. This very thing is a praiseworthy boon, that you have come near me. Your sight is difficult for the unmeritorious to obtain even in dreams.

diṇḍiḥ uvāca — Diṇḍi said; eṣa eva varaḥ — this itself is the boon; ślāghyaḥ — praiseworthy; yat — that; prāptaḥ asi — you have come; mama antikam — near me; tvat-darśanam — sight of you; apuṇyānām — for the unmeritorious; svapneṣu api — even in dreams; durlabham — difficult to obtain

BP 1.62.6

यथैषा ब्रह्महत्या मे आगता लोकगर्हिता ।
भवान् जानाति सर्वेशो हृदिस्थः सर्वदेहिनाम् ॥ ६ ॥

You know how this sin of killing a Brahmin, condemned by the world, came upon me, O Lord of all, dwelling in the hearts of all embodied beings.

yathā — how; eṣā brahma-hatyā — this brahmahatyā; me āgatā — came upon me; loka-garhitā — condemned by the world; bhavān jānāti — you know; sarva-īśaḥ — Lord of all; hṛdi-sthaḥ — dwelling in the heart; sarva-dehinām — of all embodied beings

BP 1.62.7

त्वत्प्रसादान्ममेशान नाशमाशु प्रयातु वै ।
तथा च दुरितं सर्वं यच्चान्यल्लोकगर्हितम् ॥ ७ ॥

Through your grace, O Lord, may it quickly perish, and likewise all evil and anything else condemned by the world.

tvat-prasādāt — by your grace; mama — my; īśāna — O Lord; nāśam prayātu — may it go to destruction; āśu — quickly; vai — indeed; tathā ca — and likewise; duritam sarvam — every sin; yat ca anyat — whatever else; loka-garhitam — condemned by the world

BP 1.62.8

यद्यदिच्छाम्यहं तत्तत्सर्वमस्तु दिवस्पते ।
एतेनैवानुमानेन प्रसन्नो भगवन्निति ॥ ८ ॥

May everything I desire come to pass, O lord of the day. By this very sign, I know, Blessed One, that you are pleased.

yat yat — whatever; icchāmi — I desire; aham — I; tat tat sarvam — all that; astu — let it be; divas-pate — O lord of the day; etena eva anumānena — by this very inference; prasannaḥ — pleased; bhagavan — O Blessed One; iti — thus

BP 1.62.9

ज्ञातं मया हि मार्तण्डे नाप्रसन्ने विभूतयः ।
एवं सर्वसुखाह्लादमध्यस्थोऽपि हि भानुमान् ॥ ९ ॥

I know, O Sun, that without your favor there are no blessings. Even amid every happiness and delight, the Sun stands at the center.

jñātam mayā — it is known by me; hi — indeed; mārtaṇḍe — when Mārtaṇḍa; na aprasanne — is not pleased; vibhūtayaḥ — powers and blessings; evam — thus; sarva-sukha-āhlāda — of every happiness and delight; madhya-sthaḥ — standing in the midst; api — even; hi — indeed; bhānumān — Bhānumān

BP 1.62.10

त्वं मामगाधे संसारे मग्नमुद्धर्तुमर्हसि ।
सुखानि तानि चैवान्ते तेषां दुःखं न तत्सुखम् ॥ १० ॥

You should lift me, sunk in the bottomless cycle of existence. Those pleasures become suffering in the end, they are not happiness.

tvam — you; mām — me; agādhe saṃsāre — in the unfathomable saṃsāra; magnam — sunk; uddhartum arhasi — ought to raise up; sukhāni tāni — those pleasures; ca eva ante — and indeed at the end; teṣām — of them; duḥkham — suffering; na tat sukham — that is not happiness

BP 1.62.11

यदा तु दुःखमागामि किं वा कस्यैव भक्षणात् ।
तत्प्रसादं कुरु विभो जगतां त्वं जगत्पते ॥ ११ ॥

When suffering is to come, or from the eating of what, grant me your grace, O all pervading Lord of the worlds.

yadā tu — but when; duḥkham āgāmi — suffering is coming; kim vā — or what; kasya eva bhakṣaṇāt — from whose very consumption; tat — that; prasādam kuru — grant grace; vibho — O all-pervading one; jagatām pate — O Lord of the worlds

BP 1.62.12

ज्ञानदानेन येनैवमुत्तरेयं भवर्णवम् ।
इत्युक्तस्तेन मार्तण्डः कथयामास योगवित् ॥ १२ ॥

I ask for the gift of knowledge by which I may cross the ocean of existence. Thus addressed, the Sun, knower of the discipline, spoke.

jñāna-dānena — by the giving of knowledge; yena — by which; evam — thus; uttareyam — I may cross; bhava-arṇavam — the ocean of becoming; iti uktaḥ — thus addressed; tena — by him; mārtaṇḍaḥ — Mārtaṇḍa; kathayām āsa — spoke; yoga-vit — knower of yoga

BP 1.62.13

योगं निर्बीजमत्यन्तं दुःखसंयोगभेषजम् ।
श्रुत्वा योगं तु तं दिण्डिर्निर्बीजं निष्कलं बभौ ॥ १३ ॥

He taught the seedless, highest discipline, the remedy for union with suffering. Having heard it, Diṇḍi became seedless and partless.

yogam — yoga; nir-bījam — without seed; atyantam — supreme; duḥkha-saṃyoga-bheṣajam — medicine for union with suffering; śrutvā — having heard; tam yogam — that yoga; diṇḍiḥ — Diṇḍi; nir-bījam — seedless; niṣkalam — without parts; babhau — became

BP 1.62.14

प्रणिपत्य महातेजा इदं वचनमब्रवीत् ।
देवदेव त्वया योगो यः प्रोक्तो ध्वान्तनाशन ।
नैष प्राप्यो मया नान्यैर्मानवैरजितेन्द्रियैः ॥ १४ ॥

Having bowed, the greatly radiant one spoke these words. O God of gods, the discipline you taught, destroyer of darkness, cannot be attained by me or by other humans whose senses are unconquered.

praṇipatya — having bowed; mahā-tejāḥ — greatly radiant; idam vacanam abravīt — spoke these words; deva-deva — O God of gods; tvayā proktaḥ — taught by you; yogaḥ — yoga; dhvānta-nāśana — O destroyer of darkness; na eṣaḥ prāpyaḥ — this is not attainable; mayā — by me; na anyaiḥ mānavaiḥ — nor by other humans; ajita-indriyaiḥ — whose senses are unconquered

BP 1.62.15

विषया दुर्जयाः पुंभिरिन्द्रियाकर्षिणः सदा ।
इन्द्रियाणां जयो युक्तः कः शक्तानां करिष्यति ॥ १५ ॥

The objects of the senses are always hard for people to conquer, for they draw the senses away. Even the capable struggle to conquer the senses.

viṣayāḥ — sense-objects; durjayāḥ — difficult to conquer; puṃbhiḥ — by men; indriya-ākarṣiṇaḥ — drawing away the senses; sadā — always; indriyāṇām jayaḥ — conquest of the senses; yuktaḥ — joined/undertaken; kaḥ — who; śaktānām — of the capable; kariṣyati — will accomplish

BP 1.62.16

अहंममेतिविख्यातिर्दुर्जयं चञ्चलं मनः ।
रागादयस्तथा त्यक्तुं शक्या जन्मान्तरेर्यदि ॥ १६ ॥

The notion of I and mine is well known, and the restless mind is difficult to conquer. If attachment and the rest can be abandoned, it is only over other births.

aham-mama iti — “I and mine”; vikhyātiḥ — the prevailing notion; durjayam — difficult to conquer; cañcalam manaḥ — the restless mind; rāga-ādayaḥ — attachment and the rest; tathā — likewise; tyaktum — to abandon; śakyāḥ — possible; janma-antaraiḥ — through other births; yadi — if

BP 1.62.17

सोऽहमिच्छामि देवेश त्वत्प्रसादादनिर्जितैः ।
रागादिभिरमर्त्यत्वं प्रापुः प्रक्षीणकल्मषाः ॥ १७ ॥

I wish, O Lord of the gods, to attain immortality by your grace, even with attachment and the rest unconquered and my impurities exhausted.

saḥ aham — therefore I; icchāmi — wish; deveśa — O Lord of gods; tvat-prasādāt — by your grace; anirjitaiḥ — with the unconquered; rāga-ādibhiḥ — attachment and the rest; amartyatvam — immortality; prāpuḥ — may attain; prakṣīṇa-kalmaṣāḥ — those whose impurities are exhausted

BP 1.62.18

आदित्य उवाच ।
यद्येवं मुक्तिकामस्त्वं गणनाथ शृणुष्व तम् ।
क्रियायोगं समस्तानां क्लेशानां हानिकारकम् ॥ १८ ॥

The Sun said. If you desire liberation, O leader of the hosts, listen to the ritual discipline that removes every affliction.

āditya uvāca — Āditya said; yadi evam — if thus; mukti-kāmaḥ tvam — you are desirous of liberation; gaṇa-nātha — O lord of the hosts; śṛṇuṣva — listen; tam — to that; kriyā-yogam — Kriyāyoga; samastānām kleśānām — of all afflictions; hāni-kārakam — causing destruction

BP 1.62.19

मन्मना भव मद्भक्तो मद्याजी मां नमस्कुरु ।
मामेवैष्यसि युक्त्वैवमात्मानं मत्परायणः ॥ १९ ॥

Keep your mind on me, be devoted to me, worship me, and bow to me. Thus uniting yourself with me and taking me as your supreme refuge, you will come to me alone.

mat-manāḥ — with mind on me; bhava — become; mat-bhaktaḥ — my devotee; mat-yājī — my worshiper; mām namaskuru — bow to me; mām eva eṣyasi — you will come to me alone; yuktvā — having joined; evam — thus; ātmānam — yourself; mat-parāyaṇaḥ — taking me as supreme refuge

BP 1.62.20

मद्भावना मद्यजना मद्भक्ता मत्परायणाः ।
मम पूजाकराश्चैव मयि यान्ति लयं नराः ॥ २० ॥

People who think of me, worship me, are devoted to me, take me as their refuge, and perform my worship enter into me.

mat-bhāvanāḥ — meditating upon me; mat-yajanāḥ — sacrificing to me; mat-bhaktāḥ — devoted to me; mat-parāyaṇāḥ — taking me as supreme refuge; mama pūjā-karāḥ — performers of my worship; ca eva — and indeed; mayi yānti layam — enter into dissolution in me; narāḥ — people

BP 1.62.21

सर्वभूतेषु मां पश्यन्मामवस्थितमीश्वरम् ।
कर्त्तासि केन चैव त्वमेवं दोषान् प्रहास्यसि ॥ २१ ॥

See me, the Lord, abiding in all beings. Who is then the doer, and how can you cast away faults.

sarva-bhūteṣu — in all beings; mām paśyan — seeing me; mām avasthitam īśvaram — me, the Lord, abiding; karttā asi — are you a doer; kena — by whom; ca eva — and indeed; tvam — you; evam — thus; doṣān — faults; prahāsyasi — you will abandon

BP 1.62.22

जङ्गमाजङ्गमे ज्ञाते मय्यासक्ते समन्ततः ।
रागलोभादिनाशेन भवित्री कृतकृत्यता ॥ २२ ॥

When the moving and unmoving are known to rest in me, with attachment to me on every side, fulfillment comes through the destruction of passion, greed, and the rest.

jaṅgama-ajaṅgame — in the moving and unmoving; jñāte — when known; mayi āsakte — attached to me; samantataḥ — everywhere; rāga-lobha-ādi-nāśena — through destruction of attachment, greed, and the rest; bhavitrī — will become; kṛta-kṛtyatā — accomplishment of what must be done

BP 1.62.23

भक्त्यातिप्रणयस्यापि चञ्चलत्वान्मनो यदि ।
मय्यावेशं दधद्भूयः कुरु मद्रूपिणीं तनुम् ॥ २३ ॥

Even if the mind is restless from intense devotion and love, fix it in me again and make the body take my form.

bhaktyā ati-praṇayasya api — even through excessive love of devotion; cañcalatvāt — because of restlessness; manaḥ — the mind; yadi — if; mayi āveśam dadhat — placing absorption in me; bhūyaḥ — again; kuru — make; mat-rūpiṇīm tanum — the body having my form

BP 1.62.24

सुवर्णरजताद्यैस्त्वं शैलमृद्दारुलेखनम् ।
पूजोपहारैर्विविधैः सम्पूजय त्रिलोचनम् ॥ २४ ॥

With gold, silver, and the like, make an image carved in stone, clay, or wood, and worship the Three Eyed One with various offerings.

suvarṇa-rajata-ādyaiḥ — with gold, silver, and the like; tvam — you; śaila-mṛt-dāru-lekhanam — an image carved in stone, clay, or wood; pūjā-upahāraiḥ vividhaiḥ — with various offerings of worship; sampūjaya — worship completely; tri-locanam — the Three-Eyed One

BP 1.62.25

तस्याश्रितं समाविश्य सर्वभावेन सर्वदा ।
पूजिता सैव ते भक्त्या ध्याता चैवोपकारिणी ॥ २५ ॥

Always enter that image, which is his refuge, with your whole being. Worship that form with devotion and meditate on it as your benefactor.

tasya āśritam — that which is his refuge; samāviśya — entering; sarva-bhāvena — with the whole being; sarvadā — always; pūjitā — worshiped; sā eva — that very one; te — by you; bhaktyā — with devotion; dhyātā — meditated upon; ca eva — and indeed; upakāriṇī — beneficent

BP 1.62.26

गच्छंस्तिष्ठन् स्वपन् भुञ्जस्तामेवाग्रे च पृष्ठतः ।
उपर्यधस्तथा पार्श्वे चिन्तयन् तन्मयश्च वै ॥ २६ ॥

As you go, stand, sleep, and eat, think of that very one before and behind you, above, below, and beside you, and become one with that very one.

gacchan — going; tiṣṭhan — standing; svapan — sleeping; bhuñjan — eating; tām eva — that very one; agre — in front; ca — and; pṛṣṭhataḥ — behind; upari — above; adhaḥ — below; tathā pārśve — likewise at the side; cintayan — thinking; tat-mayaḥ — filled with that; ca vai — indeed

BP 1.62.27

स्नानैस्तीर्थोदकैर्हद्यैः पुष्पैर्गन्धानुलेपनैः ।
वासोभिर्भूषणैर्भक्ष्यैर्गीतवाद्यैर्मनोरमैः ॥ २७ ॥

With bathing in pleasing sacred waters, flowers, fragrances and ointments, garments, ornaments, foods, delightful songs, and instruments.

snānaiḥ — with baths; tīrtha-udakaiḥ — with sacred waters; hṛdyaiḥ — pleasing; puṣpaiḥ — with flowers; gandha-anulepanaiḥ — with fragrant unguents; vāsobhiḥ — with garments; bhūṣaṇaiḥ — with ornaments; bhakṣyaiḥ — with food; gīta-vādyaiḥ manoramaiḥ — with delightful song and instruments

BP 1.62.28

यच्च यच्च तवेष्टं वै किञ्चिद्भोज्यादिकं तव ।
भक्तिनम्रो गणश्रेष्ठ प्रीणयस्व कृतिं मम ॥ २८ ॥

Whatever you desire, and whatever food or other thing is yours, bowing with devotion, O best of the hosts, use it to please my image.

yat ca yat ca — whatever and whatever; tava iṣṭam — desired by you; vai — indeed; kiñcit bhojya-ādikam — anything beginning with food; tava — yours; bhakti-namraḥ — bowed through devotion; gaṇa-śreṣṭha — O best of the hosts; prīṇayasva — please; kṛtim mama — my image/work

BP 1.62.29

रागेणाकृष्यते तात गन्धर्वाभिमुखं यदि ।
मयि बुद्धिं समावेश्य गायेत्था याः कथाः मम ॥ २९ ॥

If you are drawn by passion toward the celestial musicians, fix your mind on me and sing the stories about me.

rāgeṇa — by passion; ākṛṣyate — is drawn; tāta — O child; gandharva-abhimukham — toward the Gandharvas; yadi — if; mayi buddhiṃ samāveśya — placing the understanding in me; gāyethāḥ — you should sing; yāḥ kathāḥ mama — the stories that are mine

BP 1.62.30

कथया रमते चेतो यदि तद्भवतो मम ।
श्रोतव्याः प्रीतियोगेन मत्स्वरूपोदयाḥ कथाः ॥ ३० ॥

If your mind delights in stories, hear with loving attention the stories that reveal my nature.

kathayā — by narration; ramate — delights; cetaḥ — the mind; yadi — if; tat — then; bhavataḥ — of you; mama — my; śrotavyāḥ — should be heard; prīti-yogena — with loving attention; mat-svarūpa-udayāḥ kathāḥ — stories in which my form arises

BP 1.62.31

एवं समर्पितमनाश्चेतसो येऽथ आश्रयाः ।
हेयास्तान्निखिलान् दिण्डे परित्यज्य सुखी भव ॥ ३१ ॥

Thus with your mind surrendered, abandon all the things that the mind should reject, O Diṇḍi, and be happy.

evam — thus; samarpita-manāḥ — with mind surrendered; cetasaḥ āśrayāḥ — supports of the mind; ye — whatever; atha — then; heyāḥ — to be abandoned; tān nikhilān — all those; diṇḍe — O Diṇḍi; parityajya — abandoning; sukhī bhava — be happy

BP 1.62.32

अक्षीणरागद्वेषोऽपि मत्प्रियः परमः परम् ।
पदमाप्नोषि मा भैषीर्मय्यर्पितमना भव ॥ ३२ ॥

Even if attachment and aversion are not destroyed, you are most dear to me and will attain the highest state. Do not fear, let your mind be offered to me.

akṣīṇa-rāga-dveṣaḥ api — even with attachment and aversion undiminished; mat-priyaḥ — dear to me; paramaḥ param — supremely highest; padam āpnoṣi — you attain the state; mā bhaiḥ — do not fear; mayi arpita-manāḥ bhava — let your mind be offered to me

BP 1.62.33

मयि संन्यस्य सर्वं त्वमात्मानं यत्नवान् भव ।
मदर्थं कुरु कर्माणि मा च धर्मव्यतिक्रमम् ॥ ३३ ॥

Surrender everything to me, and be diligent regarding yourself. Perform actions for my sake, and do not transgress righteousness.

mayi saṃnyasya — having renounced into me; sarvam — everything; tvam — you; ātmānam — yourself; yatnavān bhava — be diligent; mat-artham — for my sake; kuru — perform; karmāṇi — actions; mā — do not; dharma-vyatikramam — transgression of dharma

BP 1.62.34

एवं व्यपोह्य हत्यास्त्वं ब्राह्मण मोक्ष्यसे भवात् ।
एतेनैवोपदेशेन व्याख्यातमखिलं तव ॥ ३४ ॥

In this way, having cast aside acts of killing, O Brahmin, you will be freed from worldly existence. All this has been explained to you through this instruction.

evam — thus; vyapohya — casting away; hatyāḥ — the sin of killing; tvam — you; brāhmaṇa — O Brāhmaṇa; mokṣyase — you will be freed; bhavāt — from worldly existence; etena eva upadeśena — by this very teaching; vyākhyātam akhilam — everything has been explained; tava — to you

BP 1.62.35

क्रियायोगं समास्थाय मदर्पितमना भव ॥ ३५ ॥

Take up the ritual discipline and keep your mind offered to me.

kriyā-yogam — Kriyāyoga; samāsthāya — taking up; mat-arpita-manāḥ bhava — let the mind be offered to me

BP 1.62.36

दिण्डिरुवाच ।
मद्धिताय जगन्नाथ क्रियायोगामृतं मम ।
विस्तरेण समाख्याहि प्रसन्नस्त्वं हि दुःखहा ॥ ३६ ॥

Diṇḍi said. For my welfare, O Lord of the universe, explain in detail the nectar of ritual discipline. You are gracious and destroyer of sorrow.

diṇḍiḥ uvāca — Diṇḍi said; mat-hitāya — for my welfare; jagannātha — O Lord of the universe; kriyā-yoga-amṛtam — nectar of Kriyāyoga; mama — for me; vistareṇa — in detail; samākhyāhi — explain; prasannaḥ — gracious; tvam — you; duḥkha-hā — destroyer of sorrow

BP 1.62.37

त्वामृते न हि तद्वक्तुं समर्थोऽन्यो जगद्गुरो ।
गुह्यमेतत्पवित्रं च तदाचक्ष्व प्रसीद मे ॥ ३७ ॥

No one other than you is able to explain this, O teacher of the world. It is secret and pure. Tell it, and be gracious to me.

tvām ṛte — without you; na hi — indeed not; tat vaktum — to speak of that; samarthaḥ anyaḥ — another is capable; jagad-guro — O teacher of the world; guhyam — secret; etat — this; pavitram — pure; ca — and; tat ācakṣva — explain that; prasīda me — be gracious to me

BP 1.62.38

आदित्य उवाच ।
आख्यास्यते तदखिलं निर्विकल्पं गणाधिप ।
इत्युक्त्वान्तर्दधे देवः सर्वलोकप्रदीपकः ॥ ३८ ॥

The Sun said. The whole of it will be told completely and without qualification, O leader of the hosts. Having said this, the god, lamp of all worlds, disappeared.

āditya uvāca — Āditya said; ākhyāsyate — shall be explained; tat akhilam — all that completely; nirvikalpam — without remainder/without alternatives; gaṇa-adhipa — O lord of the hosts; iti uktvā — having said this; antardadhe — disappeared; devaḥ — the god; sarva-loka-pradīpakaḥ — illuminer of all worlds

BP 1.62.39

स च दिण्डिर्महातेजा जगामाशु नभोगतिम् ॥ ३९ ॥

And the greatly radiant Diṇḍi quickly went along the path of the sky.
