VP 5.25.10

गृहीत्वा तां हलान्तेन चकर्ष मद-विह्वलः ।

Taking hold of her with the end of his plough, he dragged her, bewildered by intoxication: “Wicked one, you do not come; you do not come! Go elsewhere as you wish!”

“Having taken” recalls her offense as he is angry. “You do not come, you do not come” is repetition. “Go as you wish” means: if you are able, then go.

रेवतीम् (revatīm) — Revati
इति (iti) — thus
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

revatīṃ nāma tanayāṃ raivatasya mahī-pateḥ ।
upayeme balas tasyāṃ jajñāte niśitolmukī ॥19॥

Śrīdhara

atha śrī-kṛṣṇasya vivāhān putrāṃś ca vakṣyan ādau tāvaj jyeṣṭhasya rāmasya pūrvokta-vivāham anusmarati | revatīm iti ||19||

Devanagari
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

रेवतीं नाम तनयां रैवतस्य मही-पतेः ।
उपयेमे बलस् तस्यां जज्ञाते निशितोल्मुकी ॥१९॥
