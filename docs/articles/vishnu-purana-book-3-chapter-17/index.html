VP 3.17.1

इत्य् आह भगवान् और्व सगराय महात्मने । सदाचारान् पुरा सम्यङ् मैत्रेय परिपृच्छते ॥१॥

Thus the blessed Aurva spoke to the great Sagara, who had formerly asked properly about right conduct, O Maitreya.

Śrīdhara translation

Through nine chapters the duties of the threefold Veda have been elaborated. Their opposite, and the attainment of misfortune that follows it, is explained here in two [sets of] verses. In the seventeenth chapter Viṣṇu, praised by the gods, generated Māyā-moha; through him the Daityas became deluded and were born as nagna.

VP 3.17.6

त्रयी समस्तवर्णानां द्विज संवरणं यतः । नग्नो भवत्य् उज्जितायाम् अतस् तस्यां न संशयः ॥६॥

Since the threefold Veda is the covering and protection of all classes, one becomes nagna when it is abandoned; of this there is no doubt.

Śrīdhara translation

The threefold Veda is not merely a covering like cloth; it is saṃvaraṇa, that which removes all suffering. Therefore one who abandons it is called nagna; this does not mean merely a digambara.
