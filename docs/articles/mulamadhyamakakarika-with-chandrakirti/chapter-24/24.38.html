MMK 24.38

The world would be unarisen, unceasing, and fixed, with no varied states, if it had an intrinsic nature.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

ajātaṃ aniruddhaṃ ca kūṭasthaṃ ca bhaviṣyati | vicitrābhir avasthābhiḥ svabhāve rahitaṃ jagat || 38 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

If the world had intrinsic nature, it would be unarisen, unceasing, and fixed, with no genuinely varied states.

The changing world we experience depends on conditions. A fixed essence would rule out the transformations that ordinary life displays.
