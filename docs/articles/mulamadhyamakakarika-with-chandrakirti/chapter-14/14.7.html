MMK 14.7

Otherness does not exist in the other. Nor does it exist in what is not other. If otherness does not exist, neither the other nor that itself exists.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

nānyasmin vidyate 'nyatvam ananyasmin na vidyate | avidyamāne cānyatve nāsty anyad vā tadeva vā || 7 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

Otherness cannot be located in what is already called other, because the designation would already be established without it. Nor can it be located in what is not other, since non-otherness and otherness are opposed. There is no further kind of thing beyond these two possibilities. Thus, when otherness is absent, neither an intrinsically other thing nor an intrinsically identical thing can be established.
