MMK 11.6

Why fixate on that birth, that aging / dying, for which the phases of before, after, simultaneity are impossible?

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

yatra na prabhavantyete pūrvāparasahakramāḥ | prapañcayanti tāṃ jātiṃ tajjarāmaraṇaṃ ca kim || 6 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

For a birth and an aging-and-death whose before, after and simultaneous relations cannot be established, what is there to elaborate? Noble ones do not construct such a birth and death, because neither is found as an independent thing. Ordinary people elaborate them through confusion, though their account has no basis in an intrinsically existing sequence.
