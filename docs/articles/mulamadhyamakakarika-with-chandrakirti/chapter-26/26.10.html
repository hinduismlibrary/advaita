MMK 26.10

The root of life is formative impulses. Therefore, the wise do not form impulses. Therefore, the unwise are formers, but not the wise since they see reality.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

saṃsāramūlaṃ saṃskārān avidvān saṃskaroty ataḥ | avidvān kārakas tasmān na vidvāṃs tattvadarśanāt || 10 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

Ignorance is the root of saṃsāra. The ignorant form actions that condition further existence; those who see reality do not generate these formations in the same way.

Candrakīrti quotes the Buddha’s description of actions formed under ignorance. The wise do not grasp phenomena as intrinsically real, so they do not continue the karmic process on that basis.
