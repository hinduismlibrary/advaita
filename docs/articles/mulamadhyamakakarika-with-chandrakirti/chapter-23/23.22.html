MMK 23.22

If self and purity and permanence and happiness were non-existent, selflessness, impurity, impermanence and anguish would not exist.

Original Sanskrit (GRETIL)

nātmā ca śuci nityaṃ ca sukhaṃ ca yadi vidyate | anātmā 'śucy anityaṃ ca naiva duḥkhaṃ ca vidyate || 22 ||

Candrakīrti’s Commentary

If self, purity, permanence, and happiness are not established, their opposites—non-self, impurity, impermanence, and suffering—cannot be fixed as independent realities either. One set is framed against the other.

Candrakīrti concludes that the eight positions should be relinquished as intrinsic descriptions. This is not a denial of conventional teaching; it is a rejection of reifying either side.
