Sūta narrates a royal genealogy. The closing verses preserve the source’s own community and caste labels as textual claims; their wording is historical language, not a modern classification. Sanskrit follows the Wikisource transcription. IAST is transliterated from that text; the English translation and lexical glosses are original to this reader.

BP 3.4.2.1

वयहानिर्महीपालो मध्यदेशे स्वकं पदम् ।।

तामुद्वाह्य विधानेन तस्यां पुत्रानजीजनत् ।। २३ ।।

Anaṅgapāla’s son married Kīrtimālinī, the eldest, according to proper custom, and begot sons by her.

Transliteration (IAST)

anaṃgapālasya suto jyeṣṭhāṃ vai kīrtimālinīm || tāmudvāhya vidhānena tasyāṃ putrānajījanat || 23 ||

BP 3.4.2.24

BP 3.4.2.27

म्लेच्छैश्च भुक्तवत्यस्ता बभूबुर्वर्णसंकराः ।।

BP 3.4.2.28

मेहना म्लेच्छजातीया जट्टा आर्यमयाः स्मृताः ।।

BP 3.4.2 · Colophon

इति श्रीभविष्ये महापुराणे प्रतिसर्गपर्वणि कलियुगीयेतिहास समुच्चये प्रमरवंशवर्णनं नाम द्वितीयोऽध्यायः ।। २ ।।
