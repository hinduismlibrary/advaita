।। बृहस्पतिरुवाच ।। — Bṛhaspati said.

BP 3.4.13.1

षोडशाब्दे च सम्प्राप्ते ब्रह्मणोऽव्यक्तजन्मनः ।
स उषित्वा च कमले स्थितः सृष्ट्यर्थमुद्यतः ।। १ ।।

When the sixteenth year of Brahmā, born from the Unmanifest, arrived, he had rested on the lotus and was ready to create.

Transliteration (IAST)

ṣoḍaśābde ca samprāpte brahmaṇo’vyaktajanmanaḥ || sa uṣitvā ca kamale sthitaḥ sṛṣṭyarthamudyataḥ || 1 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa · literal pāda gloss
ṣoḍaśābde ca samprāpte brahmaṇo’vyaktajanmanaḥ — When the sixteenth year of Brahmā, born from the Unmanifest, arrived,
sa uṣitvā ca kamale sthitaḥ sṛṣṭyarthamudyataḥ — he had rested on the lotus and was ready to create.

BP 3.4.13.2

द्विव्याङ्गं सुन्दरं तस्या दृष्ट्वा ब्रह्मा स्मरातुरः ।। २ ।।

At that time Śāradā arose from his mouth. Seeing her divine, beautiful form, Brahmā was overcome by desire.

Transliteration (IAST)

balād gṛhītvā tāṃ kanyāmuvāca smarapīḍitaḥ || ratiṃ dehi madāghūrṇe rakṣa māṃ kāmavihvalam || 3 ||

BP 3.4.13.6

BP 3.4.13.21

मन्दोदरी मयसुता त्रिपुराधिपतेः स्वसा ।
त्रिपुरे तु तदा नष्टे महाविष्णुं सनातनम् ।
भक्त्या तुष्टाव सा देवी प्रत्यहं गुप्तभाविनी ।। २१ ।।
