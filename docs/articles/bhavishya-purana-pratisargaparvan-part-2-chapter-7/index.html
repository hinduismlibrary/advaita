BP 3.2.7.1

सूत उवाच ।
तस्मिन्काले स वैतालो भृगुवर्यः प्रसन्नधीः ।
राजानमुत्तमां गाथां वर्णयामास विश्रुताम् ॥ 1 ॥

Sūta said: At that time, the composed Vetāla narrated a famous and excellent tale to the king.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
सूत (sūta) — Sūta;
उवाच (uvāca) — said;
तस्मिन्काले (tasminkāle) — at that time;
स (sa) — he;
वैतालो (vaitālo) — Vetāla;
भृगुवर्यः (bhṛguvaryaḥ) — O excellent Bhṛgu;
प्रसन्नधीः (prasannadhīḥ) — clear-minded;
राजानमुत्तमां (rājānamuttamāṃ) — to the king the excellent;
गाथां (gāthāṃ) — tale; verse;
वर्णयामास (varṇayāmāsa) — described; conveyed;
विश्रुताम् (viśrutām) — renowned;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

sūta uvāca | tasminkāle sa vaitālo bhṛguvaryaḥ prasannadhīḥ | rājānamuttamāṃ gāthāṃ varṇayāmāsa viśrutām || 1 ||

BP 3.2.7.18

कलाज्ञः स तु शूद्रो हि धनुर्वेदी स भूपतिः ।
सवर्णाय च वैताल सदा योग्या हि कन्यका ।
अतो विवाहिता बाला धर्मदत्ताय शीलिने ॥ 18 ॥

He knew the arts and was a Śūdra; he was skilled in archery and was a king. The maiden was always suited to a man of her own varṇa; therefore, she was married to virtuous Dharmadatta.
