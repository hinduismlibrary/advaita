BP 3.2.24.1

व्यास उवाच ।
एकदा नैमिषारण्ये ऋषयः शौनकादयः ।
पृच्छन्ति विनयेनैव सूतं पौराणिकं खलु ॥ 1 ॥

Vyāsa said: Once in Naimiṣāraṇya, the sages led by Śaunaka humbly asked Sūta, the Purāṇic narrator:

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
व्यास vyāsa Vyāsa
उवाच uvāca said
एकदा ekadā once
नैमिषारण्ये naimiṣāraṇye in Naimiṣāraṇya
ऋषयः ṛṣayaḥ the sages
शौनकादयः śaunakādayaḥ headed by Śaunaka
पृच्छन्ति pṛcchanti ask
विनयेनैव vinayenaiva with humility
सूतं sūtaṃ to Sūta
पौराणिकं paurāṇikaṃ the Purāṇic narrator
खलु khalu indeed
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

vyāsa uvāca | ekadā naimiṣāraṇye ṛṣayaḥ śaunakādayaḥ | pṛcchanti vinayenaiva sūtaṃ paurāṇikaṃ khalu || 1 ||

BP 3.2.24.2

भगवन्ब्रूहि लोकानां हितार्थाय चतुर्युगे ।
कः पूज्यः सेवितव्यश्च वांच्छितार्थप्रदायकः ॥ 2 ॥

Revered one, for the benefit of the worlds in the four ages, who is worthy of worship and service, and grants the things people desire?

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

चतुर्युगे caturyuge in the four ages
कः kaḥ who
पूज्यः pūjyaḥ worthy of worship
सेवितव्यश्च sevitavyaśca and to be served
वांच्छितार्थप्रदायकः vāṃcchitārthapradāyakaḥ giver of desired aims
Transliteration

BP 3.2.24.3

विनायासेन वै कामं प्राप्नुयुर्मानवाः शुभम् ।
सत्यं ब्रह्मन्वदोपायं नराणां कीर्तिकारकम् ॥ 3 ॥

How may people attain their good wishes without hardship? Tell us the true means, O Brahmin, praised among humankind.

Word-for-word

वै vai indeed

शुभम् śubham auspicious
सत्यं satyaṃ the truth
ब्रह्मन्वदोपायं brahmanvadopāyaṃ O Brahmin, tell the means
नराणां narāṇāṃ for people
कीर्तिकारकम् kīrtikārakam making them renowned

BP 3.2.24.4

सूत उवाच ।
नवांभोजनेत्रं रमाकेलिपात्रं चतुर्बाहुचामीकराचारुगात्रम् ।
जगत्त्राणहेतुं रिपौ धूम्रकेतुं सदा सत्यनारायणं स्तौमि देवम् ॥ 4 ॥

Sūta said: I always praise the god Satyanārāyaṇa, lotus-eyed, delight of Lakṣmī, four-armed with a beautiful golden form, protector of the world and a comet-like terror to his foes.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

उवाच uvāca said

रमाकेलिपात्रं ramākelipātraṃ the delight of Lakṣmī
चतुर्बाहुचामीकराचारुगात्रम् caturbāhucāmīkarācārugātram four-armed, with a beautiful golden form
जगत्त्राणहेतुं jagattrāṇahetuṃ the protector of the world
रिपौ ripau to enemies
धूम्रकेतुं dhūmraketuṃ a comet-like terror
सदा sadā always
सत्यनारायणं satyanārāyaṇaṃ Satyanārāyaṇa
स्तौमि staumi I praise
देवम् devam the god

BP 3.2.24.5

श्रीरामं सहलक्ष्मणं सकरुणं सीतान्वितं सात्त्विकं वैदेहीमुखपद्मलुब्धमधुपं पौलस्त्यसंहारकम् ।
वन्दे वन्द्यपदांबुजं सुरवरं भक्तानुकम्पाकरं शत्रुघ्नेन हनूमता च भरतेनासेवितं राघवम् ॥ 5 ॥

I bow to Rāghava with Lakṣmaṇa and Sītā, compassionate and pure, the bee enchanted by Vaidehī's lotus face, destroyer of Rāvaṇa, whose lotus feet are revered, who is merciful to devotees, and served by Śatrughna, Hanumān, and Bharata.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
श्रीरामं śrīrāmaṃ Śrī Rāma
सहलक्ष्मणं sahalakṣmaṇaṃ together with Lakṣmaṇa
सकरुणं sakaruṇaṃ compassionate
सीतान्वितं sītānvitaṃ accompanied by Sītā
सात्त्विकं sāttvikaṃ of pure nature
वैदेहीमुखपद्मलुब्धमधुपं vaidehīmukhapadmalubdhamadhupaṃ the bee enchanted by Vaidehī's lotus face
पौलस्त्यसंहारकम् paulastyasaṃhārakam destroyer of the son of Pulastya
वन्दे vande I bow
वन्द्यपदांबुजं vandyapadāṃbujaṃ whose lotus feet are worthy of reverence
सुरवरं suravaraṃ the best of the gods
भक्तानुकम्पाकरं bhaktānukampākaraṃ merciful to devotees
शत्रुघ्नेन śatrughnena by Śatrughna
हनूमता hanūmatā by Hanumān
च ca and
भरतेनासेवितं bharatenāsevitaṃ served by Bharata
राघवम् rāghavam Rāghava
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

śrīrāmaṃ sahalakṣmaṇaṃ sakaruṇaṃ sītānvitaṃ | sāttvikaṃ vaidehīmukhapadmalubdhamadhupaṃ paulastyasaṃhārakam | vande vandyapadāṃbujaṃ suravaraṃ bhaktānukampākaraṃ | śatrughnena hanūmatā ca bharatenāsevitaṃ rāghavam || 5 ||

परजन्मकृतं कर्म भोक्तव्यं सर्वदा नरैः ॥ 36 ॥

Actions performed in this life come to fruition in another birth; actions from a former birth must always be experienced.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
इह iha in this life
जन्मकृतं janmakṛtaṃ done in this birth
कर्म karma deed
परजन्मनि parajanmani in another birth
पद्यते padyate comes to fruition
परजन्मकृतं parajanmakṛtaṃ done in a former birth
कर्म karma deed
भोक्तव्यं bhoktavyaṃ must be borne
सर्वदा sarvadā always
नरैः naraiḥ men
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

iha janmakṛtaṃ karma parajanmani padyate | parajanmakṛtaṃ karma bhoktavyaṃ sarvadā naraiḥ || 36 ||

BP 3.2.24.37

सत्यनारायण व्रतमिह सर्वान्कामान्ददाति हि ।
अद्यैव जगतीमध्ये स्थापयामि त्वदाज्ञया ॥ 37 ॥

The Satyanārāyaṇa vow here grants every desire. At your command, I establish it today in the world.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
सत्यनारायण satyanārāyaṇa Satyanārāyaṇa
व्रतमिह vratamiha the vow here
सर्वान्कामान्ददाति sarvānkāmāndadāti grants all desires
हि hi indeed
अद्यैव adyaiva even today
जगतीमध्ये jagatīmadhye in the midst of the world
स्थापयामि sthāpayāmi I establish
त्वदाज्ञया tvadājñayā with your permission
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

satyanārāyaṇa vratamiha sarvānkāmāndadāti hi | adyaiva jagatīmadhye sthāpayāmi tvadājñayā || 37 ||

BP 3.2.24.38

इत्युक्त्वाऽन्तर्दधे देवो नारदः स्वर्गतिं ययौ ।
स्वयं नारायणो देवः काश्यां पुर्यां समागतः ॥ 38 ॥

Having said this, the god disappeared. Nārada went to heaven, and Lord Nārāyaṇa himself came to the city of Kāśī.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
इत्युक्त्वाऽन्तर्दधे ityuktvā'ntardadhe having said this, disappeared
देवो devo the god
नारदः nāradaḥ Nārada
स्वर्गतिं svargatiṃ to heaven
ययौ yayau went
स्वयं svayaṃ himself
नारायणो nārāyaṇo Nārāyaṇa
देवः devaḥ god
काश्यां kāśyāṃ in Kāśī
पुर्यां puryāṃ in the city
समागतः samāgataḥ returned
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

ityuktvā'ntardadhe devo nāradaḥ svargatiṃ yayau | svayaṃ nārāyaṇo devaḥ kāśyāṃ puryāṃ samāgataḥ || 38 ||
