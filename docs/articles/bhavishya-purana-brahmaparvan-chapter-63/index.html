Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 63 — The Glory and Worship of the Sun

BP 1.63.1

सुमन्तुरुवाच ।
प्रणम्य शिरसा देवं सुरज्येष्ठं चतुर्मुखम् ।
उवाच स महातेजा दिण्डिर्लोकेशमादरात् ॥ १ ॥

Sumantu said. Having bowed his head to the four faced god, the eldest of the gods, the greatly radiant Diṇḍi respectfully spoke to the Lord of the worlds.

sumantuḥ uvāca — Sumantu said; praṇamya — having bowed; śirasā — with the head; devam — the god; sura-jyeṣṭham — eldest of the gods; catur-mukham — four-faced; uvāca — spoke; saḥ — he; mahā-tejāḥ — greatly radiant; diṇḍiḥ — Diṇḍi; loka-īśam — to the Lord of the worlds; ādarāt — respectfully

BP 1.63.2

देवदेवेन भवतादिष्टोऽस्मि च महात्मना ।
क्रियायोगामृतं सर्वमाख्यास्यति भवान् किल ॥ २ ॥

I was instructed by the great God of gods, and you will explain the whole nectar of ritual practice.

deva-devena — by the God of gods; bhavatā — by you; ādiṣṭaḥ asmi — I have been instructed; ca — and; mahātmanā — by the great-souled one; kriyā-yoga-amṛtam — nectar of Kriyāyoga; sarvam — all; ākhyāsyati — will explain; bhavān — you; kila — indeed/it is said

BP 1.63.3

स त्वां पृच्छाम्यहं ब्रह्मन् क्रियायोगं निरन्तरम् ।
सन्तोषयितुमीशेहं यथावद्वक्तुमर्हसि ॥ ३ ॥

So I ask you, O Brahman, about ritual practice without interruption. You should explain it properly so that I may satisfy the Lord.

saḥ — therefore; tvām pṛcchāmi — I ask you; aham — I; brahman — O Brahman; kriyā-yogam — Kriyāyoga; nirantaram — continuously/fully; santoṣayitum — to satisfy; īśeham — the Lord here; yathāvat — properly; vaktum arhasi — you ought to speak

BP 1.63.4

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
एह्येहि मत्सकाशं च मत्समीपे गणाधिप ।
ब्रह्महत्या प्रणष्टा ते दर्शनादेव तस्य तु ॥ ४ ॥

Brahmā said. Come, come near me, O leader of the hosts, come into my presence. The sin of killing a Brahmin has vanished for you merely by seeing him.

brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said; ehi ehi — come, come; mat-sakāśam — near me; ca — and; mat-samīpe — in my presence; gaṇa-adhipa — O lord of the hosts; brahma-hatyā — brahmahatyā; praṇaṣṭā — has vanished; te — your; darśanāt eva — merely by the sight; tasya — of him; tu — indeed

BP 1.63.5

अनुग्राह्योऽसि भूतेश भास्करस्यामितौजसः ।
आराधनाय भूतेश यदीशे प्रवणं मनः ॥ ५ ॥

You are worthy of the grace of the Sun of immeasurable splendor, O Lord of beings, because your mind is inclined toward worship of the Lord.

anugrāhyaḥ asi — you are worthy of grace; bhūta-īśa — O lord of beings; bhāskarasya — of Bhāskara; amita-ojasaḥ — of immeasurable splendor; ārādhanāya — toward worship; bhūta-īśa — O lord of beings; yat — because; īśe — toward the Lord; pravaṇam manaḥ — the mind is inclined

BP 1.63.6

यदि देवपतिं भानुमाराधयितुमिच्छसि ।
भगवन्तमनाद्यन्तं भव दीक्षागुणान्वितः ॥ ६ ॥

If you wish to worship the Sun, Lord of the gods, the Blessed One without beginning or end, become endowed with the qualities of initiation.

yadi — if; deva-patim — Lord of the gods; bhānum — Bhānu; ārādhayitum icchasi — you wish to worship; bhagavantam — the Blessed One; anādi-antam — without beginning or end; bhava — become; dīkṣā-guṇa-anvitaḥ — endowed with the qualities of initiation

BP 1.63.7

न ह्यदीक्षान्वितैर्भानुर्ज्ञातुं स्तोतुं च तत्त्वतः ।
द्रष्टुं वा शक्यते मूढैः प्रवेष्टुं कुत एव हि ॥ ७ ॥

Those without initiation cannot truly know or praise the Sun, or even see him, much less enter him.

na hi — indeed not; adīkṣā-anvitaiḥ — by those endowed with no initiation; bhānuḥ — Bhānu; jñātum — to know; stotum — to praise; ca — and; tattvataḥ — truly; draṣṭum — to see; vā — or; śakyate — is possible; mūḍhaiḥ — by the deluded; praveṣṭum — to enter; kutaḥ eva — how indeed

BP 1.63.8

जन्मभिर्बहुभिः पूता नरास्तद्गतचेतसः ।
भवन्ति भगवन् सौरास्तदा दीक्षागुणान्विताः ॥ ८ ॥

When people are purified through many births and their minds are fixed upon him, they become worshipers of the Sun, endowed with the qualities of initiation, O Blessed One.

janmabhiḥ bahubhiḥ — through many births; pūtāḥ narāḥ — purified people; tat-gata-cetasaḥ — whose minds have gone to him; bhavanti — become; bhagavan — O Blessed One; saurāḥ — Sauras; tadā — then; dīkṣā-guṇa-anvitāḥ — endowed with initiation’s qualities

BP 1.63.9

अनेकजन्मसंसारचिते पापसमुच्चये ।
नाक्षीणे जायते पुंसां मार्तण्डाभिमुखी मतिः ॥ ९ ॥

When the mass of sin accumulated through many births in the cycle of existence has not been exhausted, the minds of people do not turn toward the Sun.

aneka-janma-saṃsāra-cite — accumulated through saṃsāra of many births; pāpa-samuccaye — mass of sin; na akṣīṇe — when not exhausted; jāyate — arises; puṃsām — of people; mārtaṇḍa-abhimukhī matiḥ — a mind turned toward Mārtaṇḍa

BP 1.63.10

प्रद्वेषं याति मार्तण्डे द्विजान् वेदांश्च निन्दति ।
यो नरस्तं विजानीयात् पापबीजसमुद्भवम् ॥ १० ॥

A man who is hostile to the Sun and condemns Brahmins and the Vedas should be known as born from the seed of sin.

pradveṣam yāti — goes to hatred; mārtaṇḍe — toward Mārtaṇḍa; dvijān — the twice-born; vedān ca — and the Vedas; nindati — condemns; yaḥ naraḥ — the man who; tam — him; vijānīyāt — should recognize; pāpa-bīja-samudbhavam — arisen from the seed of sin

BP 1.63.11

पाखण्डेषु रतिः पुंसां हेतुवादानुकूलता ।
जायते विष्णुमायाम्भः पतितानां दुरात्मनाम् ॥ ११ ॥

For fallen and wicked people, delight in false doctrines and a bent toward disputation arise from the waters of the illusion of Viṣṇu.

pākhaṇḍeṣu — in heresies; ratiḥ — delight; puṃsām — of people; hetu-vāda-anukūlatā — fondness for reasoning doctrines; jāyate — arises; viṣṇu-māyā-ambhaḥ — the water of Viṣṇu’s māyā; patitānām — of the fallen; durātmanām — of the wicked

BP 1.63.12

यदा पापक्षयः पुंसां तदा वेदद्विजादिषु ।
रवौ च देवदेवेशे श्रद्धां भवति निश्चला ॥ १२ ॥

When the sins of people are destroyed, steadfast faith arises in the Vedas, the Brahmins, and the rest, and in the Sun, the Lord of the gods.

yadā — when; pāpa-kṣayaḥ — destruction of sins; puṃsām — of people; tadā — then; veda-dvija-ādiṣu — in the Veda, twice-born, and others; ravau — in Ravi; ca — and; deva-deva-īśe — Lord of the gods; śraddhā — faith; bhavati — becomes; niścalā — unwavering

BP 1.63.13

यदा स्वल्पावशेषस्तु नराणां पापसञ्चयः ।
तदा दीक्षागुणान् सर्वे भजन्ते नात्र संशयः ॥ १३ ॥

When only a little of the accumulated sin of people remains, they all pursue the qualities of initiation, there is no doubt.

yadā — when; svalpa-avaśeṣaḥ — a small remainder; tu — indeed; narāṇām — of people; pāpa-sañcayaḥ — accumulation of sin; tadā — then; dīkṣā-guṇān — the qualities of initiation; sarve — all; bhajante — pursue/possess; na atra saṃśayaḥ — there is no doubt here

BP 1.63.14

भ्रमतामत्र संसारे नराणां पापदुर्गमे ।
हस्तावलम्बदोप्येको भक्तिप्रीतो दिवाकरः ॥ १४ ॥

As people wander here in this difficult and sinful cycle of existence, the Sun, pleased by devotion, is their single handhold.

bhramatām — for those wandering; atra — here; saṃsāre — in saṃsāra; narāṇām — of people; pāpa-durgame — difficult through sin; hasta-avalambadaḥ — giver of a handhold; api — even; ekaḥ — alone; bhakti-prītaḥ — pleased by devotion; divākaraḥ — Divākara

BP 1.63.15

सर्वभागवतो भूत्वा सर्वपापहरं रविम् ।
आराधयेह तं भक्त्या प्रीतिमेष्यति भास्करः ॥ १५ ॥

Having become wholly devoted, worship here with devotion the Sun, remover of all sins, and the Radiant One will be pleased.

sarva-bhāgavataḥ bhūtvā — becoming wholly devoted; sarva-pāpa-haram — remover of every sin; ravim — Ravi; ārādhaya — worship; iha — here; tam — him; bhaktyā — with devotion; prītim eṣyati — will become pleased; bhāskaraḥ — Bhāskara

BP 1.63.16

दिण्डिरुवाच ।
किं लक्षणा नरा दीक्षामर्हन्ति पद्मसम्भव ।
यच्च दीक्षान्वितैः कार्यं तन्मे कथय पद्मज ॥ १६ ॥

Diṇḍi said. O Lotus born, what marks qualify people for initiation, and what should those endowed with initiation do. Tell me, O Lotus born.

diṇḍiḥ uvāca — Diṇḍi said; kim lakṣaṇāḥ — of what marks; narāḥ — people; dīkṣām arhanti — deserve initiation; padma-saṃbhava — O Lotus-born; yat ca — and what; dīkṣā-anvitaiḥ — by those endowed with initiation; kāryam — must be done; tat me kathaya — tell that to me; padma-ja — O Lotus-born

BP 1.63.17

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
कर्मणा मनसा वाचा प्राणिनां यो न हिंसकः ।
भावभक्तश्च मार्तण्डे तस्य दीक्षा गुणान्विता ॥ १७ ॥

Brahmā said. Whoever does not injure living beings by deed, thought, or speech, and is inwardly devoted to the Sun, has the qualities of initiation.

brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said; karmaṇā manasā vācā — by action, mind, and speech; prāṇinām — living beings; yaḥ — whoever; na hiṃsakaḥ — is not harmful; bhāva-bhaktaḥ — inwardly devoted; ca — and; mārtaṇḍe — to Mārtaṇḍa; tasya — his; dīkṣā — initiation; guṇa-anvitā — endowed with qualities

BP 1.63.18

ब्राह्मणांश्चैव देवांश्च नित्यमेव नमस्यति ।
न च द्रोघ्दा परं वादे स मार्तण्डं समर्चति ॥ १८ ॥

He always bows to Brahmins and the gods, does not attack another in debate, and worships the Sun.

brāhmaṇān — Brāhmaṇas; ca eva — and indeed; devān — gods; ca — and; nityam eva — always; namasyati — bows; na ca — and not; droghdā — with hostility; param vāde — in argument; saḥ — he; mārtaṇḍam samarcati — worships Mārtaṇḍa

BP 1.63.19

सर्वान् देवान् रविं वेत्ति सर्वलोकांश्च भास्करम् ।
तेभ्यश्च नान्यमात्मानं स नरः सौरतां व्रजेत् ॥ १९ ॥

He knows all the gods as the Sun and all the worlds as the Radiant One. He knows no self apart from them, and such a person attains the state of a Sun worshiper.

sarvān devān — all gods; ravim vetti — knows as Ravi; sarva-lokān ca — and all worlds; bhāskaram — as Bhāskara; tebhyaḥ ca — from them; na anyam ātmānam — no other self; saḥ naraḥ — that person; sauratām vrajet — attains Saurahood

BP 1.63.20

देवं मनुष्यमन्यं वा पशुपक्षिपिपीलिकान् ।
तरुपाषाणकाष्ठानि भूम्यम्भो गगनं दिशः ॥ २० ॥

He does not know himself as separate from gods, humans, animals, birds, ants, trees, stones, wood, the earth, water, the sky, or the directions.

devam — a god; manuṣyam — a human; anyam vā — or another; paśu-pakṣi-pipīlikān — animals, birds, and ants; taru-pāṣāṇa-kāṣṭhāni — trees, stones, and wood; bhūmi — earth; ambhaḥ — water; gaganam — sky; diśaḥ — directions

BP 1.63.21

आत्मानं चापि देवेशाद् व्यतिरिक्तं दिवाकरात् ।
यो न जानाति यतिषु स वै दीक्षागुणान् भजेत् ॥ २१ ॥

He does not know himself as separate from the Lord of the gods, the Sun, or the ascetics. Such a person possesses the qualities of initiation.

ātmānam — the self; ca api — also; deva-īśāt — from the Lord of gods; vyatiktam — separate; divākarāt — from Divākara; yaḥ na jānāti — whoever does not know; yatiṣu — among these; saḥ vai — he indeed; dīkṣā-guṇān bhajet — possesses the qualities of initiation

BP 1.63.22

भावं न कुरुते यस्तु सर्वभूतेषु पापकम् ।
कर्मणा मनसा वाचा स तु दीक्षां समर्हति ॥ २२ ॥

He who forms no sinful intention toward any living being by deed, thought, or speech is indeed worthy of initiation.

bhāvam — thought or attitude; na kurute — does not make; yaḥ tu — whoever; sarva-bhūteṣu — toward all beings; pāpakam — sinful; karmaṇā manasā vācā — by action, mind, and speech; saḥ tu — he indeed; dīkṣām samarhati — is worthy of initiation

BP 1.63.23

सुतप्तेनेह तपसा यज्ञैर्वा बहुदक्षिणैः ।
तां गतिं न नरा यान्ति यां गताः सूर्यमाश्रिताः ॥ २३ ॥

By intense austerity here, or by sacrifices with many gifts, people do not attain the state reached by those who take refuge in the Sun.

su-taptena tapasā — by intensely practiced austerity; yajñaiḥ vā — or by sacrifices; bahu-dakṣiṇaiḥ — with abundant gifts; tām gatim — that state; na narāḥ yānti — people do not reach; yām gatāḥ — which have reached; sūryam āśritāḥ — those who have taken refuge in the Sun

BP 1.63.24

येन सर्वात्मना भानौ भक्त्या भावो निवेशितः ।
गणेश्वर कृतार्थत्वाच्छ्लाघ्यः सौरः स मानवः ॥ २४ ॥

The person whose whole being is fixed in devotion to the Sun, O lord of the hosts, is fulfilled and worthy of praise as a worshiper of the Sun.

yena — by whom; sarva-ātmanā — with the whole self; bhānau — in Bhānu; bhaktyā — with devotion; bhāvaḥ — feeling; niveśitaḥ — placed; gaṇa-īśvara — O lord of the hosts; kṛtārthatvāt — because of fulfillment; ślāghyaḥ — praiseworthy; saḥ mānavaḥ — that human; sauraḥ — a Saura

BP 1.63.25

अपि नः स कुले धन्यो जायते कुलपावनः ।
भगवान् भक्तिभावेन येन भानुरुपासितः ॥ २५ ॥

Even in our family, a blessed person is born, a purifier of the family, who worships the Sun with devotion.

api — even; naḥ kule — in our family; saḥ — he; dhanyaḥ — blessed; jāyate — is born; kula-pāvanaḥ — purifier of the family; bhagavān bhānuḥ — the Blessed Bhānu; bhakti-bhāvena — with devotional feeling; yena — by whom; upāsitaḥ — worshiped

BP 1.63.26

यः कारयति देवार्चां हृदयालम्बनं रवेः ।
स नरो भानुसालोक्यमाप्नोति धुतकल्मषः ॥ २६ ॥

A person who has an image of the Sun made as a support for the heart, his impurities shaken off, attains the world of the Sun.

yaḥ — whoever; kārayati — causes to be made; deva-arcām — divine image; hṛdaya-ālambanam — support for the heart; raveḥ — of Ravi; saḥ naraḥ — that person; bhānu-sālokyam — the world of Bhānu; āpnoti — reaches; dhuta-kalmaṣaḥ — with impurities washed away

BP 1.63.27

यस्तु देवालयं भानोर्भक्त्या कारयति ध्रुवम् ।
स सप्त पुरुषान् लोकं भानोर्नयति मानवः ॥ २७ ॥

The person who firmly builds a temple of the Sun through devotion leads seven generations to the world of the Sun.

yaḥ tu — whoever; deva-ālayam — temple; bhānoḥ — of Bhānu; bhaktyā — with devotion; kārayati — causes to be made; dhruvam — firmly; saḥ mānavaḥ — that human; sapta puruṣān — seven generations/persons; lokam bhānoḥ — to the world of Bhānu; nayati — leads

BP 1.63.28

यावन्त्यब्दानि देवार्चा रवेस्तिष्ठति मन्दिरे ।
तावद्वर्षसहस्राणि सूर्यलोके महीयते ॥ २८ ॥

For as many years as the image of the god Ravi remains in the temple, for that many thousand years its maker is honored in the world of the Sun.

yāvanti abdāni — for as many years; deva-arcā — the divine image; raveḥ — of Ravi; tiṣṭhati — remains; mandire — in the temple; tāvat varṣa-sahasrāṇi — that many thousands of years; sūrya-loke — in the world of the Sun; mahīyate — is honoured

BP 1.63.29

देवार्चा लक्षणोपेता तद्गृहे सन्ततो विधिः ।
निष्कामं च मनो यस्य स यात्यक्षरसाम्यताम् ॥ २९ ॥

If the divine image has its proper marks and worship is constant in that house, a person whose mind is desireless attains likeness to the Imperishable.

deva-arcā — divine image; lakṣaṇa-upetā — furnished with marks; tat-gṛhe — in that house; santataḥ vidhiḥ — constant rite; niṣkāmam — desireless; ca — and; manaḥ — mind; yasya — whose; saḥ yāti — he goes/attains; akṣara-sāmyatām — likeness with the Imperishable

BP 1.63.30

पुष्पाणि च सुगन्धीनि मनोज्ञानि च यः पुमान् ।
प्रयच्छति सहस्रांशोः सदा प्रयतमानसः ॥ ३० ॥

A person with a disciplined mind always offers fragrant and pleasing flowers to the thousand rayed Sun.

puṣpāṇi — flowers; ca — and; su-gandhīni — fragrant; manojñāni — beautiful/delightful; yaḥ pumān — the man who; prayacchati — offers; sahasra-aṃśoḥ — to the Thousand-rayed One; sadā — always; prayatamānasaḥ — with a disciplined mind

BP 1.63.31

धूपांश्च तांस्तान् विविधान् गन्धाढ्यं चानुलेपनम् ।
नरः सोऽनुदिनं यज्ञं करोत्याराधनं रवेः ॥ ३१ ॥

With various incenses and fragrant ointment, that person performs a daily sacrifice, the worship of the Sun.

dhūpān — incenses; ca — and; tān tān vividhān — those various; gandha-āḍhyam — rich in fragrance; ca — and; anulepanam — unguent; naraḥ saḥ — that person; anudinam — daily; yajñam karoti — performs a sacrifice; ārādhanam raveḥ — worship of Ravi

BP 1.63.32

यज्ञेशो भगवान् पूषा सदा क्रतुभिरिज्यते ।
बहूपकरणा यज्ञा नानासम्भारविस्तराः ॥ ३२ ॥

The Blessed Nourisher, Lord of sacrifice, is always worshiped with rites. These sacrifices require many implements and an abundance of varied materials.

yajña-īśaḥ — Lord of sacrifice; bhagavān pūṣā — the Blessed Pūṣā; sadā — always; kratubhiḥ ijyate — is worshiped with rituals; bahu-upakaraṇāḥ — having many implements; yajñāḥ — sacrifices; nānā-sambhāra-vistarāḥ — extensive in varied materials

BP 1.63.33

न ते दिण्डिन्नवाप्यन्ते मनुष्यैरल्पसञ्चयैः ।
भक्त्या तु पुरुषैः पूजा कृता दूर्वाङ्कुरैरपि ।
भानोर्ददाति हि फलं सर्वयज्ञैः सुदुर्लभम् ॥ ३३ ॥

Those sacrifices are not attained by people with little means, O Diṇḍi. But worship performed with devotion, even with shoots of Bermuda grass, causes the Sun to grant a result very difficult to obtain through all sacrifices.

na te — not these; diṇḍin — O Diṇḍi; avāpyante — are attained; manuṣyaiḥ — by people; alpa-saṃcayaiḥ — with little accumulation; bhaktyā — with devotion; tu — but; puruṣaiḥ — by people; pūjā kṛtā — worship performed; dūrvā-aṅkuraiḥ api — even with shoots of dūrvā; bhānoḥ — of Bhānu; dadāti phalam — gives the fruit; sarva-yajñaiḥ sudurlabham — very difficult through all sacrifices

BP 1.63.34

यानि पुष्पाणि हृद्यानि धूपगन्धानुलेपनम् ।
दयितं भूषणं यच्च प्रीतये चैव वाससी ॥ ३४ ॥

Offer pleasing flowers, incense, fragrance and ointment, beloved ornaments, and garments for delight.

yāni — whatever; puṣpāṇi — flowers; hṛdyāni — pleasing to the heart; dhūpa-gandha-anulepanam — incense, fragrance, and unguent; dayitam bhūṣaṇam — beloved ornament; yat ca — whatever; prītaye — for delight; ca eva — and indeed; vāsasī — garments

BP 1.63.35

यानि चाभ्यवहार्याणि भक्ष्याणि च फलानि वै ।
प्रयच्छ तानि मार्तण्डे भवेष्ठाश्चैव तन्मनाः ॥ ३५ ॥

Offer to the Sun whatever foods are fit to eat and whatever fruits there are, and remain devoted to him alone with your mind set on him.

BP 1.63.36

आद्यं तं भुवनाधारं यथाशक्त्या प्रसादय ।
आराध्य याति तं देवं तस्मिन्नेव नरो लयम् ॥ ३६ ॥

Please that primordial one, the support of the worlds, according to your ability. Having worshiped that god, a person goes to him and dissolves in him alone.

BP 1.63.37

पुष्पैस्तीर्थोदकैर्गन्धैर्मधुना सर्पिषा तथा ।
क्षीरेण स्नापयेद्भानुं ग्रहेशं गोपतिं खगम् ॥ ३७ ॥

puṣpaiḥ — with flowers; tīrtha-udakaiḥ — sacred waters; gandhaiḥ — fragrances; madhunā — with honey; sarpiṣā — with ghee; tathā — likewise; kṣīreṇa — with milk; snāpayet — should bathe; bhānum — Bhānu; graha-īśam — Lord of the planets; go-patim — Lord of the cows; khagam — the sky-going one

BP 1.63.38

दधिक्षीरह्रदान् पुण्यांस्ततो लोकान् मधुच्युतः ।
प्रयास्यति गणश्रेष्ठ निर्वृत्तिं च विलक्षणाम् ॥ ३८ ॥

Then, O best of the hosts, he will go to holy lakes of curd and milk, to worlds flowing with honey, and to an extraordinary release.

BP 1.63.39

स्तोत्रैर्गीतैस्तथा वाद्यैर्ब्राह्मणानां च तर्पणैः ।
मनसश्चैव योगेन आराधय दिवाकरम् ॥ ३९ ॥

BP 1.63.40

आराध्य तं जगन्नाथं मया सर्गः प्रवर्तितः ।
विष्णुश्च पालयेल्लोकांस्तमाराध्य दिवाकरम् ॥ ४० ॥

BP 1.63.41

रुद्रश्च प्राप्तवान् देवीं भवानीं तत्प्रसादतः ।
दीप्यन्ते ऋषयश्चापि तमाराध्य दिवाकरम् ॥ ४१ ॥

BP 1.63.42

स त्वमेभिः प्रकारैस्तमुपवासैश्च भास्करम् ।
तोषयाब्दं हि तुष्टोऽसौ भानुर्द्वन्द्वप्रशान्तिदः ॥ ४२ ॥
