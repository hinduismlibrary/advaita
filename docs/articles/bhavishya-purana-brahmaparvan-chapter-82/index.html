Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 82 — The Nanda Rite

BP 1.82.1

दिण्डिर् उवाच । ये त्व् आदित्य-दिने ब्रह्मन् पूजयन्ति दिवाकरम् । स्नान-दानादिकं तेषां किं फलं स्याद् ब्रवीतु मे ॥ ॥

Diṇḍi said, O Brahman, tell me what fruit those who worship the Sun on Sunday obtain through bathing, giving, and the like.

diṇḍiḥ uvāca — Diṇḍin said;
ye — those who;
tu — indeed;
āditya-dine — on the day of Āditya;
brahman — O Brahman;
pūjayanti — worship;
divākaram — Divākara;
snāna-dāna-ādikam — bathing, giving, and the rest;
teṣām — of them;
kim phalam — what fruit;
syāt — may be;
bravītu — let it be told;
me — to me.

BP 1.82.2

पुण्या सा सप्तमी प्रोक्ता युक्ता तेन पितामह । विजयेति तथा नाम वर्ण्यताम् अस्य पुण्यता ॥ ॥

That seventh day joined with it is declared meritorious, O Grandfather. It is also named Victory. Its merit should be described.

sā — that;
saptamī — Saptamī;

yuktā — joined;

pitāmaha — O Grandfather;

tathā — likewise;
nāma — named;
varṇyatām — let it be described;
asya — of this;
puṇyatā — holiness, merit.

BP 1.82.3

ब्रह्मोवाच । ये त्व् आदित्य-दिने ब्रह्मञ् छ्राद्धं कुर्वन्ति मानवाः । सप्त-जन्मसु ते जाताः सम्भवन्ति विरोगिणः ॥ ॥

Brahmā said. O Brahman, people who perform the ancestral rite on Sunday are born free from disease for seven births.

brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said;
ye — those who;
tu — indeed;
āditya-dine — on the day of Āditya;
brahman — O Brahman;
śrāddham — ancestral rite;
kurvanti — perform;
mānavāḥ — people;
sapta-janmasu — for seven births;
te — they;
jātāḥ — born;
sambhavanti — become;
virogiṇaḥ — free from disease.

BP 1.82.4

नक्तं कुर्वन्ति ये तत्र मानवाः स्थैर्यम् आश्रिताः । जपमानाः परं जाप्यं आदित्य-हृदयं परम् ॥ ॥

Those people who keep the night fast there with firm resolve, reciting the supreme recitation, the supreme Heart of the Sun.

naktam — at night;
kurvanti — perform;
ye — those who;
tatra — there;
mānavāḥ — people;
sthairyam āśritāḥ — resorted to firmness;
japamānāḥ — reciting;
param — supreme;
jāpyam — recitation;
āditya-hṛdayam — Āditya Hṛdaya;
param — supreme.

BP 1.82.5

आरोग्यम् इह वै प्राप्य सूर्य-लोकं व्रजन्ति ते । उपवासं च ये कुर्युर् आदित्यस्य दिने सदा ॥ ॥

Having obtained health here, they go to the world of the Sun. Those who always fast on the day of the Sun

ārogyam — health;
iha — here;
vai — indeed;
prāpya — having attained;
sūrya-lokam — world of Sūrya;
vrajanti — go;
te — they;
upavāsam — fasting;
ca — and;
ye — those who;
kuryuḥ — perform;
ādityasya dine — on Āditya’s day;
sadā — always.

BP 1.82.6

जपन्ति च महा-श्वेतां ते लभन्ते यथेप्सितम् । अहोरात्रेण नक्तेन त्रि-रात्र-नियमेन वा ॥ ॥

And recite the Great White One obtain what they desire, whether by fasting for a day and night, by night, or for three nights.

japanti — recite;
ca — and;
mahā-śvetām — Mahāśvetā;
te — they;
labhante — obtain;
yathepsitam — what is desired;
ahorātreṇa — through day and night;
naktena — through the night observance;
tri-rātra-niyamena — through a three-night discipline;
vā — or.

BP 1.82.7

जपमानो महा-श्वेताम् ईप्सितं लभते फलम् । विशेषतः सूर्य-दिने जपमानो गणाधिप ॥ ॥

One who recites the Great White One obtains the desired fruit, especially by reciting it on Sunday, O Lord of the hosts.

japamānaḥ — reciting;
mahā-śvetām — Mahāśvetā;
īpsitam — desired;
labhate — obtains;
phalam — fruit;
viśeṣataḥ — especially;
sūrya-dine — on Sūrya’s day;
japamānaḥ — reciting;
gaṇa-adhipa — O Lord of hosts.

BP 1.82.8

षड्-अक्षरं तथा श्वेतां गच्छेद् वैरोचनं पदम् । द्वादशेह स्मृता वारा आदित्यस्य महात्मनः ॥ ॥

By reciting the six syllables and likewise the White One, one should reach the abode of Vairocana. Twelve days are remembered for the great Sun.

ṣaḍ-akṣaram — six-syllabled mantra;
tathā — likewise;
śvetām — white one;
gacchet — should go;
vairocanam padam — the abode of Virocana;
dvādaśa — twelve;
iha — here;
smṛtāḥ — remembered;
vārāḥ — days;
ādityasya — of Āditya;
mahātmanaḥ — the great one.

BP 1.82.9

नन्दो भद्रस् तथा सौम्यः कामदः पुत्रदस् तथा । जयो जयन्तो विजय आदित्याभिमुख-स्थिताः ॥ ॥

Joy, auspiciousness, gentleness, wish giving, son giving, victory, conquest, and triumph stand facing the Sun.

bhadraḥ — Bhadra;
tathā — likewise;
saumyaḥ — Saumya;
kāma-daḥ — giver of desires;
putra-daḥ — giver of sons;

jayaḥ — Jaya;
jayantaḥ — Jayanta;
vijayaḥ — Vijaya;
āditya-abhi-mukha-sthitāḥ — standing facing Āditya.

BP 1.82.10

हृदयो रोग-हा चैव महा-श्वेत-प्रियो ऽपरः । शुक्ल-पक्षस्य षष्ठ्यां तु माघे मासि गणाधिप ॥ ॥

The Heart, the Disease Remover, and another dear to the Great White One, on the sixth day of the bright fortnight in Māgha, O Lord of the hosts.

roga-hā — remover of disease;
ca eva — and indeed;
mahā-śveta-priyaḥ — dear to Mahāśvetā;
aparaḥ — another;
śukla-pakṣasya — of the bright fortnight;
ṣaṣṭhyām — on the sixth day;
tu — indeed;
māghe māsi — in the month of Māgha;
gaṇa-adhipa — O Lord of hosts.

BP 1.82.11

यः कुर्यात् स भवेद् भूपः सर्व-पाप-भयापहः । अत्र नक्तं स्मृतं पुण्यं घृतेन स्नपनं रवेः ॥ ॥

Whoever performs it becomes a king, a remover of all sins and fear. Here the night fast is held sacred, and Ravi is bathed with ghee.

kuryāt — should perform;
saḥ — he;
bhavet — becomes;
bhūpaḥ — king;
sarva-pāpa-bhaya-apahaḥ — remover of the fear of every sin;

naktam — night observance;
smṛtam — remembered;
puṇyam — holy;
ghṛtena — with ghee;
snapanam — bathing;
raveḥ — of Ravi.

BP 1.82.12

अगस्त्य-कुसुमानीह भानोस् तुष्टि-कराणि तु । विलेपनं सु-गन्धस् तु श्वेत-चन्दनम् उत्तमम् ॥ ॥

Agastya flowers here please Bhānu. The fragrant anointing is excellent white sandalwood.

BP 1.82.13

धूपस् तु गुग्गुलः श्रेष्ठो नैवेद्यं पूपम् एव हि । दत्त्वा पूपं तु विप्रस्य ततो भुञ्जीत वाग्यतः ॥ ॥

Guggul resin is the finest incense, and the cake itself is the food offering. Having given a cake to a Brahmin, one should then eat in silence.

tataḥ — then;
bhuñjīta — should eat;
vāk-yataḥ — restrained in speech, silently.

BP 1.82.14

नक्षत्र-दर्शनान् नक्तं केचिद् इच्छन्ति मानद । मुहूर्तो ऽनं दिनं केचित् प्रवदन्ति मनीषिणः ॥ ॥

Some desire the night fast to last until the stars appear, O giver of honor. Some wise men declare it a day less one muhūrta.

naktam — night observance;

BP 1.82.15

नक्षत्र-दर्शनान् नक्तं अहम् मन्ये गणाधिप । प्रस्थ-मात्रं भवेत् पूपं गोधूम-मयम् उत्तमम् ॥ ॥

I think the night fast lasts until the stars appear, O Lord of the hosts. The excellent cake should be made of wheat and measure one prastha.

BP 1.82.16

यव-उद्भवं वा कुर्वीत स-गुडं सर्पिषा-अन्वितम् । स-हिरण्यं च दातव्यं ब्राह्मणे स-इतिहासके ॥ ॥

BP 1.82.17

भौमे दिव्ये ऽथ वा देयं न्यसेद् वा पुरतो रवेः । दातव्यो मन्त्रतस् चायं मण्डको ग्राह्य एव हि ॥ ॥

BP 1.82.18

भूत्वादित्येन वै भक्त्या आदित्यं तु नमस्य च । आदित्य-तेजस-उत्पन्नं राज्ञी-कर-विनिर्मितम् । श्रेयसे मम विप्र त्वं प्रतीच्छ अपूपम् उत्तमम् ॥ ॥

Having become devoted to the Sun and bowed to the Sun, O Brahmin, accept the excellent cake for my welfare, born from the radiance of the Sun and made by the hand of the queen.

BP 1.82.19

कामदं सुखदं धर्म्यं धनदं पुत्रदं तथा । सदास्तु ते प्रतीच्छामि मण्डकं भास्कर-प्रियम् ॥ ॥

BP 1.82.20

एतौ चैव महा-मन्त्रौ दानादाने रवि-प्रियौ । अपूपस्य गण-श्रेष्ठ श्रेयसे नात्र संशयः ॥ ॥

BP 1.82.21

एष नन्द-विधिः प्रोक्तो नराणां श्रेयसे विभो । अनेन विधिना यस् तु नरो पूजयते रविम् । सर्व-पाप-विनिर्मुक्तः सूर्य-लोके महीयते ॥ ॥
