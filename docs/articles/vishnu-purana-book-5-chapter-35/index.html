The heroic Sāmba, son of Jāmbavatī, forcibly took the daughter of Suyodhana when the occasion for her svayaṃvara had arrived.

VP 5.35.38

ततस् तु कौरवाः साम्बं सम्पूज्य हलिना सह ।
प्रेषयाम् आसुर् उद्वाह-धन-भार्या-समन्वितम् ॥३८॥

Then the Kauravas honored Sāmba and sent him away with the plough-bearer, supplied with the wealth and wife required for the marriage.

Śrīdhara translation

They sent [him] away, supplied with the marriage wealth and with his wife.
