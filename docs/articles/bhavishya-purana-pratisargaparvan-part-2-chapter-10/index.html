BP 3.2.10.1

सूत उवाच ।
वैतालस्तु महाभाग राजानमिदमब्रवीत् ।
गौडदेशे महाराज वर्धनं नाम वै पुरम् ॥ 1 ॥

Sūta said: The Vetāla addressed the king: “O great king, there is a city called Vardhana in Gauḍa country.”

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
सूत (sūta) — Sūta (the narrator);
उवाच (uvāca) — said;
वैतालस्तु (vaitālastu) — the Vetāla, however;
महाभाग (mahābhāga) — O greatly fortunate one;
राजानमिदमब्रवीत् (rājānamidamabravīt) — said this to the king;
गौडदेशे (gauḍadeśe) — in Gauḍa country;
महाराज (mahārāja) — O great king;
वर्धनं (vardhanaṃ) — Vardhana;
नाम (nāma) — named; by name;
वै (vai) — indeed;
पुरम् (puram) — city;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

sūta uvāca | vaitālastu mahābhāga rājānamidamabravīt | gauḍadeśe mahārāja vardhanaṃ nāma vai puram || 1 ||

BP 3.2.10.2

गुणशेखर आख्यातो भूपालस्तत्र धर्मवान् ।
तन्मन्त्री निर्भयानन्दो जैनधर्मपरा यणः ॥ 2 ॥

There ruled the righteous King Guṇaśekhara. His minister Nirbhayānanda was devoted to Jain dharma.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
गुणशेखर (guṇaśekhara) — Guṇaśekhara (the king's name);
आख्यातो (ākhyāto) — known as;
भूपालस्तत्र (bhūpālastatra) — the king there;
धर्मवान् (dharmavān) — righteous;
तन्मन्त्री (tanmantrī) — his minister;
निर्भयानन्दो (nirbhayānando) — Nirbhayānanda;
जैनधर्मपरा (jainadharmaparā) — devoted to Jain dharma;
यणः (yaṇaḥ) — (ending of the compound) devoted;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

guṇaśekhara ākhyāto bhūpālastatra dharmavān | tanmantrī nirbhayānando jainadharmaparā yaṇaḥ || 2 ||

BP 3.2.10.3

कदाचिद्भूपतिर्यातो मन्दिरे गिरिजापतेः ।
पूजयामास तं देवं सर्वव्यापिनमीश्वरम् ॥ 3 ॥

One day the king went to the temple of Girijāpati and worshipped that all-pervading Lord.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

पूजयामास (pūjayāmāsa) — worshipped;
तं (taṃ) — him;
देवं (devaṃ) — the god;
सर्वव्यापिनमीश्वरम् (sarvavyāpinamīśvaram) — the all-pervading Lord;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

kadācidbhūpatiryāto mandire girijāpateḥ | pūjayāmāsa taṃ devaṃ sarvavyāpinamīśvaram || 3 ||

BP 3.2.10.4

वृश्चिकस्तत्र संप्राप्य ददंश नृपतिं रुषा ।
तत्कष्टेन स भूपालो मूर्छितः पतितो भुवि ॥ 4 ॥

A scorpion arrived there and angrily stung the king. From the pain, the ruler fainted and fell to the ground.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
वृश्चिकस्तत्र (vṛścikastatra) — a scorpion there;

नृपतिं (nṛpatiṃ) — the king;

तत्कष्टेन (tatkaṣṭena) — from that pain;

भूपालो (bhūpālo) — the king;

Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

vṛścikastatra saṃprāpya dadaṃśa nṛpatiṃ ruṣā | tatkaṣṭena sa bhūpālo mūrchitaḥ patito bhuvi || 4 ||

BP 3.2.10.5

तदा तु निर्भयानन्दो विषमुत्तार्य तस्य वै ।
भूपतिं वर्णयामास जैनधर्म परायणः ॥ 5 ॥

Nirbhayānanda drew out the poison and spoke to the king. He was devoted to Jain dharma.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
तदा (tadā) — then;
तु (tu) — indeed; then;
निर्भयानन्दो (nirbhayānando) — Nirbhayānanda;

तस्य (tasya) — of him;
वै (vai) — indeed;
भूपतिं (bhūpatiṃ) — to the king;
वर्णयामास (varṇayāmāsa) — described; conveyed;
जैनधर्म (jainadharma) — Jain dharma;
परायणः (parāyaṇaḥ) — devoted;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

tadā tu nirbhayānando viṣamuttārya tasya vai | bhūpatiṃ varṇayāmāsa jainadharma parāyaṇaḥ || 5 ||

BP 3.2.10.6

शृणु राजन्महाभाग शत्रून्षण्मानसाधमान् ।
कामः क्रोधस्तथा लोभो रतिर्हिंसा च तृष्णिका ।
रजोगुणाच्च ते जातास्तेषां भेदाः पृथक्पृथक् ॥ 6 ॥

“Listen, greatly fortunate king, to the six base enemies of the mind: desire, anger, greed, passion, violence, and craving. They arise from rajas and have many separate forms.”

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
शृणु (śṛṇu) — hear;
राजन्महाभाग (rājanmahābhāga) — O greatly fortunate king;
शत्रून्षण्मानसाधमान् (śatrūnṣaṇmānasādhamān) — the six base enemies of the mind;
कामः (kāmaḥ) — desire;
क्रोधस्तथा (krodhastathā) — and anger;
लोभो (lobho) — greed;
रतिर्हिंसा (ratirhiṃsā) — passion and violence;
च (ca) — and;
तृष्णिका (tṛṣṇikā) — craving;
रजोगुणाच्च (rajoguṇācca) — and from the quality of rajas;
ते (te) — you; for you;
जातास्तेषां (jātāsteṣāṃ) — they were born; their;
भेदाः (bhedāḥ) — divisions;
पृथक्पृथक् (pṛthakpṛthak) — each separately;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

śṛṇu rājanmahābhāga śatrūnṣaṇmānasādhamān | kāmaḥ krodhastathā lobho ratirhiṃsā ca tṛṣṇikā | rajoguṇācca te jātāsteṣāṃ bhedāḥ pṛthakpṛthak || 6 ||

BP 3.2.10.7

मोहो दंभो मदश्चैव ममताशा च गर्हणा ।
तमोगुणाच्च ते जातास्तैरिदं पूरितं जगत् ॥ 7 ॥

“Delusion, hypocrisy, pride, possessiveness, desire, and condemnation arise from tamas; by them this world is filled.”

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
मोहो (moho) — delusion;
दंभो (daṃbho) — hypocrisy;
मदश्चैव (madaścaiva) — and pride;
ममताशा (mamatāśā) — possessiveness and desire;
च (ca) — and;
गर्हणा (garhaṇā) — condemnation;
तमोगुणाच्च (tamoguṇācca) — and from the quality of tamas;
ते (te) — you; for you;
जातास्तैरिदं (jātāstairidaṃ) — born from them, this;
पूरितं (pūritaṃ) — was filled;
जगत् (jagat) — the world;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

moho daṃbho madaścaiva mamatāśā ca garhaṇā | tamoguṇācca te jātāstairidaṃ pūritaṃ jagat || 7 ||

BP 3.2.10.8

कामी विष्णुस्तथा रुद्रः क्रोधी लोभी विधिस्तथा ।
दंभी शक्रो यमो मोही मदी यक्षपतिः स्वयम् ॥ 8 ॥

“Viṣṇu is passionate, Rudra wrathful, Brahmā greedy; Indra is proud, Yama deluded, and Kubera himself conceited.”

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
कामी (kāmī) — lustful;
विष्णुस्तथा (viṣṇustathā) — and Viṣṇu;
रुद्रः (rudraḥ) — Rudra;
क्रोधी (krodhī) — wrathful;
लोभी (lobhī) — greedy;
विधिस्तथा (vidhistathā) — and Brahmā likewise;
दंभी (daṃbhī) — hypocritical;
शक्रो (śakro) — Śakra (Indra);
यमो (yamo) — Yama;
मोही (mohī) — deluded;
मदी (madī) — proud;
यक्षपतिः (yakṣapatiḥ) — the lord of the Yakṣas (Kubera);
स्वयम् (svayam) — himself;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

kāmī viṣṇustathā rudraḥ krodhī lobhī vidhistathā | daṃbhī śakro yamo mohī madī yakṣapatiḥ svayam || 8 ||

BP 3.2.10.9

माया वश्याश्च ते सर्वे तर्हि तत्पूजनेन किम् ।
षट्शत्रुभिर्जितो यो वै स जिनो मुनिभिः स्मृतः ॥ 9 ॥

“All of them are subject to Māyā, so why worship them? The sages call a person who conquers the six enemies a Jina.”

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
माया (māyā) — Māyā, illusion;
वश्याश्च (vaśyāśca) — and are controlled;
ते (te) — you; for you;
सर्वे (sarve) — all;
तर्हि (tarhi) — then;
तत्पूजनेन (tatpūjanena) — by worshipping them;
किम् (kim) — what;
षट्शत्रुभिर्जितो (ṣaṭśatrubhirjito) — one who has conquered the six enemies;
यो (yo) — whoever;
वै (vai) — indeed;
स (sa) — he;
जिनो (jino) — a Jina, conqueror;
मुनिभिः (munibhiḥ) — by sages;
स्मृतः (smṛtaḥ) — remembered;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

māyā vaśyāśca te sarve tarhi tatpūjanena kim | ṣaṭśatrubhirjito yo vai sa jino munibhiḥ smṛtaḥ || 9 ||

BP 3.2.10.10

न जितः स जिनो ज्ञेयोऽद्वैतवादी निरंजनः ।
तस्य ध्यानेन भावेन मोक्षवंतो नराः सदा ॥ 10 ॥

“One who has not conquered them is no Jina; the untainted nondual teacher is to be known. Through meditation on him, people attain liberation.”

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
न (na) — not;
जितः (jitaḥ) — conquered;
स (sa) — he;
जिनो (jino) — a Jina, conqueror;
ज्ञेयोऽद्वैतवादी (jñeyo'dvaitavādī) — to be known as a nondual teacher;
निरंजनः (niraṃjanaḥ) — the untainted one;
तस्य (tasya) — of him;
ध्यानेन (dhyānena) — through meditation;
भावेन (bhāvena) — with devoted thought;
मोक्षवंतो (mokṣavaṃto) — liberated;
नराः (narāḥ) — people;
सदा (sadā) — always;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

na jitaḥ sa jino jñeyo'dvaitavādī niraṃjanaḥ | tasya dhyānena bhāvena mokṣavaṃto narāḥ sadā || 10 ||

BP 3.2.10.11

तत्प्रसादाय यो धर्मः शृणु मे वसुधाधिप ।
गोपूजनेन ते देवास्तुष्टिं यान्ति सदैव हि ॥ 11 ॥

“Hear how to gain his favor, O lord of the earth: the gods are always pleased by the worship of cows.”

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
तत्प्रसादाय (tatprasādāya) — for his favor;
यो (yo) — whoever;
धर्मः (dharmaḥ) — dharma;
शृणु (śṛṇu) — hear;
मे (me) — to me;
वसुधाधिप (vasudhādhipa) — O lord of the earth;
गोपूजनेन (gopūjanena) — through cow worship;
ते (te) — you; for you;
देवास्तुष्टिं (devāstuṣṭiṃ) — the gods to satisfaction;
यान्ति (yānti) — attain;
सदैव (sadaiva) — always;
हि (hi) — indeed;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

tatprasādāya yo dharmaḥ śṛṇu me vasudhādhipa | gopūjanena te devāstuṣṭiṃ yānti sadaiva hi || 11 ||

BP 3.2.10.12

अतो गोपूजनं शुद्धं हिंसा सर्वत्र वर्जिता ।
मदपानेन सर्वात्मा जिनः क्लेशं समाप्नुयात् ॥ 12 ॥

“Therefore cow worship is pure, and violence is everywhere to be avoided. By drinking liquor, the Jina, the universal self, would suffer.”

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
अतो (ato) — therefore;
गोपूजनं (gopūjanaṃ) — cow worship;
शुद्धं (śuddhaṃ) — pure;
हिंसा (hiṃsā) — violence;
सर्वत्र (sarvatra) — everywhere;
वर्जिता (varjitā) — to be avoided;
मदपानेन (madapānena) — through drinking liquor;
सर्वात्मा (sarvātmā) — the universal self;
जिनः (jinaḥ) — the Jina;
क्लेशं (kleśaṃ) — affliction;
समाप्नुयात् (samāpnuyāt) — would experience;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

ato gopūjanaṃ śuddhaṃ hiṃsā sarvatra varjitā | madapānena sarvātmā jinaḥ kleśaṃ samāpnuyāt || 12 ||

BP 3.2.10.13

तस्मान्मांसं च पानं च वर्जितं सर्वदैव हि ।
न्यायेनोपार्जितं वित्तं भोजयेच्च बुभुक्षितान् ॥ 13 ॥

च (ca) — and;
पानं (pānaṃ) — drink;
च (ca) — and;
वर्जितं (varjitaṃ) — is to be avoided;
सर्वदैव (sarvadaiva) — at all times;
हि (hi) — indeed;
न्यायेनोपार्जितं (nyāyenopārjitaṃ) — earned justly;
वित्तं (vittaṃ) — wealth;
भोजयेच्च (bhojayecca) — and one should feed;
बुभुक्षितान् (bubhukṣitān) — the hungry;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

tasmānmāṃsaṃ ca pānaṃ ca varjitaṃ sarvadaiva hi | nyāyenopārjitaṃ vittaṃ bhojayecca bubhukṣitān || 13 ||

BP 3.2.10.14

रविरात्मा जिनस्यैव तत्प्रकाशे हि भोजयेत् ।
इत्येवं वर्णयित्वैनं मंत्री गेहमुपाययौ ॥ 14 ॥

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

जिनस्यैव (jinasyaiva) — of the Jina;

हि (hi) — indeed;

गेहमुपाययौ (gehamupāyayau) — returned home;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

ravirātmā jinasyaiva tatprakāśe hi bhojayet | ityevaṃ varṇayitvainaṃ maṃtrī gehamupāyayau || 14 ||

BP 3.2.10.15

तथैव मत्वा स नृपो जिनधर्मं गृहीतवान् ।
कियता चैव कालेन वेदमार्गो हि लंघितः ॥ 15 ॥

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

लंघितः (laṃghitaḥ) — was transgressed;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

tathaiva matvā sa nṛpo jinadharmaṃ gṛhītavān | kiyatā caiva kālena vedamārgo hi laṃghitaḥ || 15 ||

BP 3.2.10.16

तदा तु दुःखिता राज्ञी शिवस्य शरणं ययौ ॥ 16 ॥

Word-for-word

Transliteration

BP 3.2.10.17

वरदानेन रुद्रस्य पुत्रो जातो महोत्तमः ।
धर्मराज इति ख्यातो वेदव्रतपरायणः ॥ 17 ॥

By Rudra’s boon an excellent son was born. He was named Dharmarāja and devoted to Vedic observances.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
वरदानेन (varadānena) — by the boon;
रुद्रस्य (rudrasya) — of Rudra;
पुत्रो (putro) — a son;
जातो (jāto) — was born;
महोत्तमः (mahottamaḥ) — most excellent;
धर्मराज (dharmarāja) — Dharmarāja (the prince's name);
इति (iti) — thus;
ख्यातो (khyāto) — renowned;
वेदव्रतपरायणः (vedavrataparāyaṇaḥ) — devoted to Vedic vows;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

varadānena rudrasya putro jāto mahottamaḥ | dharmarāja iti khyāto vedavrataparāyaṇaḥ || 17 ||
