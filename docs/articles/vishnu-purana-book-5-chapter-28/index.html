VP 5.28.4

देवी जाम्बवती चापि सदा तुष्टा तु रोहिणी ।
मद्रराज-सुता चान्या सुशीला शील-मण्डला ॥४॥

The goddess Jāmbavatī too—Rohiṇī, of beautiful form—and another, Suśīlā, daughter of the king of Madra, adorned with good character.

Śrīdhara translation

“Jāmbavatī” herself bears the name Rohiṇī; therefore, the number [of wives] does not increase. For this reason, since she is of bear birth, the epithet “assuming a desired form” is used. Some say Rohiṇī is another woman, equal to a chief queen; otherwise, the later statement about offspring from the two would not be possible.
