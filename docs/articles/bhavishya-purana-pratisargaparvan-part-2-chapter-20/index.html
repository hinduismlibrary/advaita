BP 3.2.20.1

सूत उवाच ।
इति श्रुत्वा स वैतालो नृपं प्राह पुनः कथाम् ।
विशालनगरे रम्ये विपुलेशो महीपतिः ॥ 1 ॥

Sūta said: Hearing this, the Vetāla again told King Vikrama a story: In the beautiful city of Viśāla ruled King Vipuleśa.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
सूत sūta Sūta
उवाच uvāca said
इति iti thus
श्रुत्वा śrutvā having heard
स sa he
वैतालो vaitālo Vetāla
नृपं nṛpaṃ the king
प्राह prāha said
पुनः punaḥ again
कथाम् kathām the story
विशालनगरे viśālanagare in the beautiful city of Viśāla
रम्ये ramye lovely
विपुलेशो vipuleśo King Vipuleśa
महीपतिः mahīpatiḥ king
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

sūta uvāca | iti śrutvā sa vaitālo nṛpaṃ prāha punaḥ kathām | viśālanagare ramye vipuleśo mahīpatiḥ || 1 ||

BP 3.2.20.2

तस्य ग्रामेवसद्वैश्योर्थदत्तोविपणे रतः ।
अनंगमंजरी कन्या तस्य जाता मनोरमा ॥ 2 ॥

In the king's village lived a Vaiśya named Arthadatta, engaged in trade. His delightful daughter was named Anaṅgamañjarī.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
तस्य tasya of him
ग्रामेवसद्वैश्योर्थदत्तोविपणे grāmevasadvaiśyorthadattovipaṇe in the village lived the Vaiśya Arthadatta, engaged in trade
रतः rataḥ engaged
अनंगमंजरी anaṃgamaṃjarī Anaṅgamañjarī
कन्या kanyā the maiden
तस्य tasya of him
जाता jātā born
मनोरमा manoramā delightful
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

tasya grāmevasadvaiśyorthadattovipaṇe rataḥ | anaṃgamaṃjarī kanyā tasya jātā manoramā || 2 ||

BP 3.2.20.3

सुवर्णनाम्ने वैश्याय पिता वै दत्तवान्स्वयम् ।
कदाचित्कमलग्रामात्सुवर्णो द्वीपमागमत् ।
द्रव्यलाभाय न्यवसच्चिरं कालं स लुब्धवान् ॥ 3 ॥

Her father gave her in marriage to a Vaiśya named Suvarṇa. One day Suvarṇa traveled from Kamalagrāma to an island to gain wealth, and remained there a long time in his greed.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
सुवर्णनाम्ने suvarṇanāmne named Suvarṇa
वैश्याय vaiśyāya to the Vaiśya
पिता pitā father
वै vai indeed
दत्तवान्स्वयम् dattavānsvayam he himself gave
कदाचित्कमलग्रामात्सुवर्णो kadācitkamalagrāmātsuvarṇo once Suvarṇa from Kamalagrāma
द्वीपमागमत् dvīpamāgamat went to an island
द्रव्यलाभाय dravyalābhāya to gain wealth
न्यवसच्चिरं nyavasacciraṃ dwelt for a long time
कालं kālaṃ time
स sa he
लुब्धवान् lubdhavān greedy
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

suvarṇanāmne vaiśyāya pitā vai dattavānsvayam | kadācitkamalagrāmātsuvarṇo dvīpamāgamat | dravyalābhāya nyavasacciraṃ kālaṃ sa lubdhavān || 3 ||

BP 3.2.20.4

अनंगमंजरीगेहे दैवयोगाद्द्विजोत्तमः ।
कमलाकरनामासौ कृत्ययोगात्समागतः ॥ 4 ॥

By fate, an excellent Brahmin named Kamalākara came to Anaṅgamañjarī's house on ritual business.

दैवयोगाद्द्विजोत्तमः daivayogāddvijottamaḥ by fate, an excellent Brahmin
कमलाकरनामासौ kamalākaranāmāsau he, named Kamalākara
कृत्ययोगात्समागतः kṛtyayogātsamāgataḥ arrived for ritual work
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

anaṃgamaṃjarīgehe daivayogāddvijottamaḥ | kamalākaranāmāsau kṛtyayogātsamāgataḥ || 4 ||

BP 3.2.20.5

होमांते सुन्दरी नारी मार्जनार्थे सुता गता ।
दृष्ट्वा तां कामकलिकां मुमोह द्विजसत्तमः ॥ 5 ॥

After the fire offering, the beautiful young woman went to clean the place. Seeing her, the excellent Brahmin was overcome with desire.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
होमांते homāṃte after the fire offering
सुन्दरी sundarī beautiful
नारी nārī woman
मार्जनार्थे mārjanārthe for cleaning
सुता sutā daughter
गता gatā went
दृष्ट्वा dṛṣṭvā having seen
तां tāṃ her
कामकलिकां kāmakalikāṃ the lovely maiden, a bud of desire
मुमोह mumoha became bewildered
द्विजसत्तमः dvijasattamaḥ the excellent Brahmin
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

homāṃte sundarī nārī mārjanārthe sutā gatā | dṛṣṭvā tāṃ kāmakalikāṃ mumoha dvijasattamaḥ || 5 ||

BP 3.2.20.6

सुतापि मदघूर्णाक्षी विप्राय समयं ददौ ।
निशीथे तमउद्भूते त्वं मां प्राप्य सुखी भव ॥ 6 ॥

The daughter too, her eyes rolling with passion, made an appointment with the Brahmin: When deep darkness falls at midnight, come to me and be happy.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
सुतापि sutāpi the daughter too
मदघूर्णाक्षी madaghūrṇākṣī with eyes rolling from intoxication
विप्राय viprāya to the Brahmin
समयं samayaṃ the matter; the question
ददौ dadau gave
निशीथे niśīthe at midnight
तमउद्भूते tamaudbhūte when darkness arose
त्वं tvaṃ you
मां māṃ me
प्राप्य prāpya having reached
सुखी sukhī happy
भव bhava be
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

sutāpi madaghūrṇākṣī viprāya samayaṃ dadau | niśīthe tamaudbhūte tvaṃ māṃ prāpya sukhī bhava || 6 ||

BP 3.2.20.7

इति श्रुत्वा द्विजो वाक्यं तस्या ध्यानं तदाकरोत् ।
कामाग्निना चिरं तप्तः सुष्वाप परमासने ॥ 7 ॥

Hearing her words, the Brahmin meditated on her. Long burned by the fire of desire, he fell asleep on his fine seat.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
इति iti thus
श्रुत्वा śrutvā having heard
द्विजो dvijo a Brahmin
वाक्यं vākyaṃ words
तस्या tasyā of her
ध्यानं dhyānaṃ meditation
तदाकरोत् tadākarot then performed
कामाग्निना kāmāgninā by the fire of desire
चिरं ciraṃ for a long time
तप्तः taptaḥ burned
सुष्वाप suṣvāpa fell asleep
परमासने paramāsane on an excellent seat
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

iti śrutvā dvijo vākyaṃ tasyā dhyānaṃ tadākarot | kāmāgninā ciraṃ taptaḥ suṣvāpa paramāsane || 7 ||

BP 3.2.20.8

अर्द्धरात्रे तु सा नारी द्विजागमनतत्परा ।
मार्गमन्वेषमाणा सा प्रियस्य स्मरपीडिता ॥ 8 ॥

At midnight the woman, intent on the Brahmin's arrival, searched for the path, tormented by longing for her beloved.

द्विजागमनतत्परा dvijāgamanatatparā intent on the Brahmin's arrival
मार्गमन्वेषमाणा mārgamanveṣamāṇā searching for the path
सा sā she
प्रियस्य priyasya of her beloved
स्मरपीडिता smarapīḍitā tormented by love
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

arddharātre tu sā nārī dvijāgamanatatparā | mārgamanveṣamāṇā sā priyasya smarapīḍitā || 8 ||

BP 3.2.20.9

नागतः स द्विजो दैवात्तदा सा मरणं गता ।
कमलाकर एवाशु समयान्ते समाययौ ॥ 9 ॥

By fate the Brahmin did not come, and she died. Kamalākara arrived quickly, but only after the appointed time.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

सा sā she
मरणं maraṇaṃ death
गता gatā went
कमलाकर kamalākara Kamalākara
एवाशु evāśu indeed, quickly
समयान्ते samayānte at the end of the appointed time
समाययौ samāyayau she arrived
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

nāgataḥ sa dvijo daivāttadā sā maraṇaṃ gatā | kamalākara evāśu samayānte samāyayau || 9 ||

BP 3.2.20.10

दृष्ट्वा मृत्युवशां सुभ्रूं स्वयं मरणमागतः ।
प्रभाते चार्थदत्तो वै दाहयामास तां शुचा ॥ 10 ॥

Seeing the lovely woman overcome by death, he too approached death. In the morning Arthadatta had her cremated in grief.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
दृष्ट्वा dṛṣṭvā having seen
मृत्युवशां mṛtyuvaśāṃ in death's power
सुभ्रूं subhrūṃ lovely-browed
स्वयं svayaṃ himself
मरणमागतः maraṇamāgataḥ came to death
प्रभाते prabhāte at dawn
चार्थदत्तो cārthadatto and Arthadatta
वै vai indeed
दाहयामास dāhayāmāsa caused to be cremated
तां tāṃ her
शुचा śucā in grief
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

dṛṣṭvā mṛtyuvaśāṃ subhrūṃ svayaṃ maraṇamāgataḥ | prabhāte cārthadatto vai dāhayāmāsa tāṃ śucā || 10 ||

BP 3.2.20.11

सुवर्णश्च तदागत्य विललाप प्रियां प्रति ।
चितायां भस्मसाद्भूत्वा स्वर्गलोके तु सा ययौ ॥ 11 ॥

Then Suvarṇa arrived and lamented for his beloved. She became ashes on the pyre and went to heaven.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
सुवर्णश्च suvarṇaśca and Suvarṇa
तदागत्य tadāgatya arriving then
विललाप vilalāpa cried out
प्रियां priyāṃ beloved
प्रति prati toward
चितायां citāyāṃ at the pyre
भस्मसाद्भूत्वा bhasmasādbhūtvā having become ashes
स्वर्गलोके svargaloke to the heavenly world
तु tu but
सा sā she
ययौ yayau went
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

suvarṇaśca tadāgatya vilalāpa priyāṃ prati | citāyāṃ bhasmasādbhūtvā svargaloke tu sā yayau || 11 ||

BP 3.2.20.12

इत्युक्त्वा स तु वैतालो नृपतिं प्राह विक्रमम् ।
कस्य स्नेहोऽधिकस्तेषां कुतः स्वर्गपुरं ययौ ॥ 12 ॥

Having said this, the Vetāla asked King Vikrama: Whose affection among them was greatest, and why did she go to heaven?

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
इत्युक्त्वा ityuktvā having said this
स sa he
तु tu but
वैतालो vaitālo Vetāla
नृपतिं nṛpatiṃ the king
प्राह prāha said
विक्रमम् vikramam Vikrama
कस्य kasya whose
स्नेहोऽधिकस्तेषां sneho'dhikasteṣāṃ whose love among them is greater
कुतः kutaḥ why; by what cause
स्वर्गपुरं svargapuraṃ to the city of heaven
ययौ yayau went
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

ityuktvā sa tu vaitālo nṛpatiṃ prāha vikramam | kasya sneho'dhikasteṣāṃ kutaḥ svargapuraṃ yayau || 12 ||

BP 3.2.20.13

राजोवाच ।
पति स्नेहोधिकस्तेषां मध्यमौ नारिविप्रकौ ।
द्विजस्नेहेन सा नारी मृता स्वर्गपुरं ययौ ॥ 13 ॥

The king replied: The husband's affection is greatest among them; the woman and the Brahmin are the middle pair. Through love for the Brahmin, the woman died and went to heaven.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

स्नेहोधिकस्तेषां snehodhikasteṣāṃ his affection is greatest among them
मध्यमौ madhyamau the middle pair
नारिविप्रकौ nāriviprakau the woman and the Brahmin
द्विजस्नेहेन dvijasnehena through love for the Brahmin
सा sā she
नारी nārī woman
मृता mṛtā dead
स्वर्गपुरं svargapuraṃ to the city of heaven
ययौ yayau went
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

rājovāca | pati snehodhikasteṣāṃ madhyamau nāriviprakau | dvijasnehena sā nārī mṛtā svargapuraṃ yayau || 13 ||

BP 3.2.20.15

The Brahmin too did not obtain the woman. But placing Hari in his heart and dying, he reached heaven from this world.

BP 3.2.20.16

सुवर्णो हृदि संज्ञाय मत्प्रिया ब्रह्मवत्सला ।
