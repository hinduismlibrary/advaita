Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 67 — The Inner Self of the Sun and the One Reality

BP 1.67.1

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
प्रभाते संस्तुतो देवो मूर्तिहेतोर्मया पुरा ।
यजन्तं चापरं देवं भक्तिनम्रं महामतिः ॥ 1 ॥

At dawn long ago, I praised the god for the sake of his form. I, the great-minded one, saw another god worshipping with devotion.

brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said; prabhāte — at dawn; saṃstutaḥ — praised; devaḥ — the Deity; mūrti-hetor — for the sake of a form; mayā — by me; purā — formerly; yajantam — worshiping; ca — and; aparam devam — another deity; bhakti-namram — bent through devotion; mahā-matiḥ — great-minded

BP 1.67.2

प्रत्यक्षत्वमथो गत्वा रहस्यं प्रोक्तवान्मम ।
अहं च कृतवान्प्रश्नं दृष्ट्वा प्रत्यक्षतो रविम् ॥ 2 ॥

Then he became visible and told me a secret. I asked him a question after seeing the Sun face to face.

pratyakṣatvam — manifestness; athaḥ — then; gatvā — having attained; rahasyam — secret; proktavān — spoke; mama — to me; aham — I; ca — and; kṛtavān — made; praśnam — a question; dṛṣṭvā — having seen; pratyakṣataḥ — directly; ravim — Ravi

BP 1.67.3

वेदेषु च पुराणेषु साङ्गोपाङ्गेषु गीयसे ।
त्वमजः शाश्वतो धाता महाभूतमनुत्तमम् ॥ 3 ॥

You are sung in the Vedas and Purāṇas, together with their auxiliaries and subsidiary parts. You are unborn, eternal, the supporter, and the unsurpassed great element.

vedeṣu — in the Vedas; ca — and; purāṇeṣu — in the Purāṇas; sa-aṅga-upāṅgeṣu — with limbs and subsidiary limbs; gīyase — you are sung; tvam — you; ajaḥ — unborn; śāśvataḥ — eternal; dhātā — supporter; mahā-bhūtam — great element; anuttamam — unsurpassed

BP 1.67.4

प्रतिष्ठितं भूतभव्यं त्वयि सर्वमिदं जगत् ।
चत्वारो ह्याश्रमा देव सर्वे गार्हस्थ्यमूलकाः ॥ 4 ॥

All this universe, past and future, is established in you. O god, the four stages of life all have household life as their root.

pratiṣṭhitam — established; bhūta-bhavyam — past and future; tvayi — in you; sarvam idam jagat — all this universe; catvāraḥ — four; hi — indeed; āśramāḥ — stages of life; deva — O Deity; sarve — all; gārhasthya-mūlakāḥ — rooted in household life

BP 1.67.5

यजन्ते त्वामहरहर्नानामूर्तिसमाश्रिताः ।
पिता माता हि सर्वस्य दैवतं त्वं हि शाश्वतम् ॥ 5 ॥

They worship you day after day, taking refuge in many forms. You are the eternal deity, the father and mother of all.

yajante — worship; tvām — you; ahar-ahaḥ — day after day; nānā-mūrti-samāśritāḥ — taking refuge in many forms; pitā — father; mātā — mother; hi — indeed; sarvasya — of all; daivatam — deity; tvam — you; śāśvatam — eternal

BP 1.67.6

यजसे चैव कं देवमेव चापि न विद्महे ।
कथ्यतां मम देवेश परं कौतूहलं हि मे ॥ 6 ॥

We do not know which god you worship. Tell me, lord of the gods, for my curiosity is great.

yajase — you worship; ca eva — and indeed; kam devam — which deity; eva ca api — even; na vidmahe — we do not know; kathyatām — let it be told; mama — to me; deva-īśa — O Lord of the gods; param — great; kautūhalam — curiosity; hi — indeed; me — my

BP 1.67.7

इत्थं मयोक्तो भगवानिदं वचनमब्रवीत् ।
अवाच्यमेतद्वक्तव्यमात्मगुह्यं सनातनम् ॥ 7 ॥

Thus addressed by me, the blessed one spoke these words. This is unspeakable, yet must be spoken, the eternal secret of the Self.

ittham — thus; mayā uktaḥ — addressed by me; bhagavān — Blessed Lord; idam vacanam — these words; abravīt — spoke; avācyam — unutterable; etat — this; vaktavyam — must be spoken; ātma-guhyam — secret of the Self; sanātanam — eternal

BP 1.67.8

तव भक्तिमतो ब्रह्मन्वक्ष्याम्यहं यथातथम् ।
यतः सूक्ष्ममविज्ञेयमव्यक्तमचलंध्रुवम् ॥ 8 ॥

O Brahmā, because you are devoted, I shall tell you exactly as it is. It is subtle, unknowable, unmanifest, unmoving, and constant.

tava — your; bhakti-mataḥ — devoted; brahman — O Brahman; vakṣyāmi — I shall speak; yathā-tatham — exactly as it is; yataḥ — which; sūkṣmam — subtle; avijñeyam — unknowable; avyaktam — unmanifest; acalam — unmoving; dhruvam — fixed

BP 1.67.9

इन्द्रियैरिन्द्रियार्थैश्च सर्वभूतैश्च वर्जितम् ।
स ह्यन्तरात्मा भूतानां क्षेत्रज्ञश्चेति कथ्यते ॥ 9 ॥

It is free from the senses, their objects, and all beings. It is called the inner Self of beings and the knower of the field.

indriyaiḥ — from senses; indriya-arthaiḥ — from sense objects; ca — and; sarva-bhūtaiḥ — from all beings; varjitam — free; saḥ — he; hi — indeed; antar-ātmā — inner Self; bhūtānām — of beings; kṣetrajñaḥ — knower of the field; ca — and; iti — thus; kathyate — is called

BP 1.67.10

त्रिगुणव्यतिरिक्तोऽसौ पुरुषश्चेति कथ्यते ।
हिरण्यगर्भो भगवान्सैव बुद्धिरिति स्मृता ॥ 10 ॥

It is called the Person beyond the three qualities. The blessed Hiraṇyagarbha is remembered as that very intelligence.

tri-guṇa-vyatiriktaḥ — beyond the three guṇas; asau — he; puruṣaḥ — Puruṣa; iti — thus; kathyate — is called; hiraṇya-garbhaḥ — Hiraṇyagarbha; bhagavān — Blessed; sa eva — that same; buddhiḥ — intellect; iti — thus; smṛtā — remembered

BP 1.67.11

महानिति च योगेषु प्रधानश्चेति कथ्यते ।
साङ्ख्ये च पठ्यते शास्त्रे नामभिर्बहुभिः सदा ॥ 11 ॥

In yoga teachings it is called the Great. In the Sāṅkhya scripture it is called the Primary, and is always read under many names.

mahān — the Great; iti — thus; ca — and; yogeṣu — in the teachings of yoga; pradhānaḥ — Pradhāna; ca — and; iti — thus; kathyate — is called; sāṅkhye — in Sāṅkhya; paṭhyate — is read; śāstre — in scripture; nāmabhiḥ — by names; bahubhiḥ — many; sadā — always

BP 1.67.12

विश्वगो विश्वभूतश्च विश्वात्मा विश्वसम्भवः ।
धृतं चैवात्मकं येन इदं त्रैलोक्यमात्मना ॥ 12 ॥

He goes everywhere, is all beings, the Self of the universe, and the source of the universe. By his own Self this threefold world is held.

viśvagaḥ — all-pervading; viśva-bhūtaḥ — source of all beings; viśva-ātmā — Self of the universe; viśva-sambhavaḥ — source of the universe; dhṛtam — held; ca eva — and indeed; ātmakam — as Self; yena — by which; idam trailokyam — this threefold world; ātmanā — by the Self

BP 1.67.13

अशरीरः शरीरेषु लिप्यते न च कर्मभिः ।
मम अन्तरात्मा तव च ये चान्ये देहसंज्ञकाः ॥ 13 ॥

Bodiless among bodies, it is not stained by actions. It is the inner Self of me, of you, and of others who are called embodied.

aśarīraḥ — bodiless; śarīreṣu — among bodies; lipyate — is stained; na — not; ca — and; karmabhiḥ — by actions; mama — my; antar-ātmā — inner Self; tava — your; ca — and; ye anye — and others who; deha-saṃjñakāḥ — known as embodied

BP 1.67.14

सर्वेषां साक्षिभूतोऽसौ न करोति न लिप्यते ।
सगुणो निर्गुणो विष्णुर्ज्ञानगम्यो ह्यसौ स्मृतः ॥ 14 ॥

It is the witness of all. It does not act and is not stained. Viṣṇu, with and without qualities, is said to be reached through knowledge.

sarveṣām — of all; sākṣibhūtaḥ — having become the witness; asau — he; na karoti — does not act; na lipyate — is not stained; saguṇaḥ — with attributes; nirguṇaḥ — without attributes; viṣṇuḥ — Viṣṇu; jñāna-gamyaḥ — attainable through knowledge; hi — indeed; asau — he; smṛtaḥ — remembered

BP 1.67.15

सर्वतः पाणिपादोऽसौ सर्वतोऽक्षिशिरोमुखः ।
सर्वतः श्रुतियुक्तोऽसौ सर्वमावृत्य तिष्ठति ॥ 15 ॥

He has hands and feet everywhere, eyes, heads, and mouths everywhere. He has ears everywhere and stands pervading everything.

sarvataḥ — everywhere; pāṇi-pādaḥ — hands and feet; asau — he; sarvataḥ — everywhere; akṣi-śiras-mukhaḥ — eyes, heads, and faces; sarvataḥ — everywhere; śruti-yuktaḥ — possessed of ears; sarvam āvṛtya — pervading everything; tiṣṭhati — stands

BP 1.67.16

विश्वमूर्धा विश्वभुजो विश्वपादाक्षिनासिकः ।
एकश्चरति क्षेत्रेषु स्वैरचारी यथासुखम् ॥ 16 ॥

He is the head and arms of the universe, with feet, eyes, and nose throughout the universe. He moves alone through the fields, freely as he pleases.

viśva-mūrdhā — whose head is the universe; viśva-bhujaḥ — whose arms are the universe; viśva-pāda-akṣi-nāsikaḥ — whose feet, eyes, and noses are the universe; ekaḥ — alone; carati — moves; kṣetreṣu — through fields; svaira-cārī — freely moving; yathā-sukham — as he pleases

BP 1.67.17

क्षेत्राण्यस्य शरीराणि बीजं चापि शुभाशुभम् ।
तानि वेत्ति स योगात्मा अतः क्षेत्रज्ञ उच्यते ।
अव्यक्तके पुरे शेते तेनासौ पुरुषः स्मृतः ॥ 17 ॥

The fields are his bodies, and the seed is both good and evil. The self united with yoga knows them, and so is called the knower of the field. He lies in the unmanifest city, therefore he is called the Person.

kṣetrāṇi — fields; asya — his; śarīrāṇi — bodies; bījam — seed; ca api — and also; śubha-aśubham — good and evil; tāni vetti — knows them; saḥ — he; yoga-ātmā — Self joined with yoga; ataḥ — therefore; kṣetrajñaḥ — knower of the field; ucyate — is called; avyakta-ke pure — in the unmanifest city; śete — lies; tena — therefore; asau — he; puruṣaḥ — Puruṣa; smṛtaḥ — remembered

BP 1.67.18

विश्वं बहुविधं ज्ञेयं स च सर्वत्र विद्यते ।
तस्मात्स बहुरूपत्वाद्विश्वरूप इति स्मृतः ॥ 18 ॥

The universe is manifold, and he is present everywhere. Because he has many forms, he is remembered as the Universal Form.

viśvam — universe; bahu-vidham — manifold; jñeyam — to be known; saḥ — he; ca — and; sarvatra — everywhere; vidyate — is present; tasmāt — therefore; bahu-rūpatvāt — because of many forms; viśva-rūpaḥ — universal-formed; iti — thus; smṛtaḥ — remembered

BP 1.67.19

महापुरुषशब्दं हि बिभर्त्येष सनातनः ।
स तु वै विक्रियापन्नः सृजत्यात्मानमात्मना ॥ 19 ॥

This eternal one bears the name Great Person. Having undergone transformation, he creates himself by himself.

mahā-puruṣa-śabdam — the word Mahāpuruṣa; hi — indeed; bibharti — bears; eṣaḥ — he; sanātanaḥ — eternal; saḥ — he; tu — indeed; vai — truly; vikriyā-pannaḥ — having undergone transformation; sṛjati — creates; ātmānam — himself; ātmanā — by himself

BP 1.67.20

आकाशात्पतितं तोयं याति स्वाद्वन्तरं यथा ।
भूमे रसविशेषेण तथा गुणवशात्तु सः ॥ 20 ॥

As water fallen from the sky acquires a different taste through the particular flavor of the earth, so he appears according to the power of the qualities.

ākāśāt patitam — fallen from the sky; toyam — water; yāti — goes; svādu-antaram — to a different taste; yathā — as; bhūmeḥ rasa-viśeṣeṇa — through the special flavor of the earth; tathā — so; guṇa-vaśāt — according to the power of the guṇas; tu — indeed; saḥ — he

BP 1.67.21

एक एव यथा वायुर्देहे तिष्ठति पञ्चधा ।
एकत्वं च पृथक्त्वं च तथा तस्य न संशयः ॥ 21 ॥

As one wind remains in the body in five forms, so oneness and separateness are in him. Of this there is no doubt.

ekaḥ eva — one alone; yathā — as; vāyuḥ — wind; dehe — in the body; tiṣṭhati — remains; pañca-dhā — fivefold; ekatvam — unity; ca — and; pṛthaktvam — separateness; ca — and; tathā — so; tasya — of him; na saṃśayaḥ — there is no doubt

BP 1.67.22

स्थानान्तरविशेषेण यथाग्निर्लभते पराम् ।
संज्ञां दावाग्निराद्येषु तथा देवो ह्यसौ स्मृतः ॥ 22 ॥

As fire receives a different name according to the place, such as forest fire and the rest, so this god is remembered.

sthāna-antara-viśeṣeṇa — through difference of place; yathā — as; agniḥ — fire; labhate — receives; parām saṃjñām — a distinct name; dāvāgni-ādiṣu — in forest fire and the rest; tathā — so; devaḥ — deity; hi — indeed; asau — this; smṛtaḥ — remembered

BP 1.67.23

यथा दीपसहस्राणि दीप एकः प्रसूयते ।
तथा रूपसहस्राणि स एवैकः प्रसूयते ॥ 23 ॥

As one lamp gives rise to a thousand lamps, so that one alone gives rise to a thousand forms.

yathā — as; dīpa-sahasrāṇi — a thousand lamps; dīpaḥ ekaḥ — one lamp; prasūyate — gives rise; tathā — so; rūpa-sahasrāṇi — a thousand forms; saḥ eva ekaḥ — that one alone; prasūyate — gives rise

BP 1.67.24

स यदा बुध्यतेत्मानं तदा भवति केवलः ।
एकत्वं प्रलये चास्य बहुत्वं स्यात्प्रवर्तने ॥ 24 ॥

When he knows himself, he becomes alone. At dissolution he is one, and at manifestation he becomes many.

saḥ — he; yadā — when; budhyate — knows; ātmānam — the Self; tadā — then; bhavati — becomes; kevalaḥ — alone; ekatvam — oneness; pralaye — at dissolution; ca — and; asya — for him; bahutvam — multiplicity; syāt — is; pravartane — at manifestation

BP 1.67.25

नित्यं हि नास्ति जगति भूतं स्थावरजङ्गमम् । ऋते तमेकमीशानं पुरुषं बीजसंज्ञितम् ॥ 25 ॥

There is indeed no moving or unmoving being in the world apart from that one Lord, the Person called the seed.

BP 1.67.26

अक्षयश् चाप्रमेयश् च सर्वगश् च स उच्यते । तस्मादव्यक्तमुत्पन्नं त्रिगुणं सर्वकारणम् ॥ 26 ॥

He is called imperishable, immeasurable, and all-pervading. From him the unmanifest, threefold, cause of all, arose.

BP 1.67.27

अव्यक्ताव्यक्तभावस्था या सा प्रकृतिरुच्यते । तां योनिं ब्रह्मणो विद्धि योऽसौ सदसदात्मकः ॥ 27 ॥

That which is in the state of the unmanifest and manifest is called Nature. Know it as the womb of Brahmā, composed of being and non-being.

BP 1.67.28

नास्ति तस्मात्परो ह्यन्यः स पिता स प्रजापतिः । आत्मा मम स विज्ञेयस्ततस्तं पूजयाम्यहम् ॥ 28 ॥

There is no other beyond him. He is the father and lord of creatures. He is to be known as my Self, therefore I worship him.

BP 1.67.29

स्वर्गताश्चापि ये केचित्तं नमस्यन्ति देहिनः । ते तत्प्रसादाद्गच्छन्ति तेनादिष्टाः परां गतिम् ॥ 29 ॥

The embodied beings who have gone to heaven and bow to him attain the highest state, directed by him through his grace.

BP 1.67.30

तं देवाश्चासुराश्चैव नानामतसमाश्रिताः । भक्त्या सम्पूजयन्त्याद्यं गतिं चैषां ददाति सः ॥ 30 ॥

Gods and Asuras, resorting to various doctrines, worship the Primal One with devotion, and he grants them their destination.

BP 1.67.31

स हि सर्वगतश्चैव निर्गुणश्चापि कथ्यते । एवं ज्ञात्वा तमात्मानं पूजयामि सनातनम् । भास्करं देवदेवेशं सर्वभूतेशमच्युतम् ॥ 31 ॥

He is called all-pervading and without qualities. Knowing him, the eternal Self, thus, I worship the Sun, lord of the gods, lord of all beings, the unfailing one.

BP 1.67.32

ब्रह्मोवाच । इत्युक्तवान्पुरा पृष्टो मया देवो दिवाकरः । पूजय त्वं महात्मानं तपन्तं विपुलं तपः ॥ 32 ॥

Brahmā said. When asked by me long ago, the god Sun spoke thus. Worship the great-souled one who performs vast austerities.
