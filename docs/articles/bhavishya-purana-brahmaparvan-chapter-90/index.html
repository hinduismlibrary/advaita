Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 90 — The Heart of the Sun Observance

BP 1.90.1

ब्रह्मोवाच । रविसङ्क्रमणे यः स्याद्रवेर्वारो गणाधिप । स ज्ञेयो हृदयो नाम आदित्यहृदयप्रियः ॥ ॥

Brahmā said. When the Sun is in transit, the day, O chief of hosts, is declared great, known as the Heart, and dear to the Heart of the Sun.

brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said;
ravi-saṅkramaṇe — at the Sun’s transition;
yaḥ — which;
syāt — may occur;
raveḥ — of Ravi;
vāraḥ — day;
gaṇādhipa — O lord of hosts;
saḥ — that;
jñeyaḥ — is to be known;
hṛdayaḥ — Hṛdaya;
nāma — by name;
āditya-hṛdaya-priyaḥ — dear to Āditya’s heart.

BP 1.90.2

तत्र नक्तं समाश्रित्य देवं सम्पूज्य भक्तितः । गत्वा च सदने भानोरादित्याभिमुखस्थितः ॥ ॥

Undertaking the night observance there and worshipping the god with devotion, he should go to the abode of the Sun and stand facing the Sun.

tatra — there;
naktam — the night observance;
samāśritya — undertaking, resorting to;
devam — the deity;
sampūjya — having fully worshiped;
bhaktitaḥ — with devotion;
gatvā — having gone;
ca — and;
sadane — at the abode;
bhānoḥ — of Bhānu;
āditya-abhimukha-sthitaḥ — standing facing Āditya.

BP 1.90.3

जपेदादित्यहृदयं सङ्ख्ययाष्टशतं बुधः । अथ वास्तमनं यावद्भास्करं चिंतयेद्धृदि ॥ ॥

The wise person should recite the Heart of the Sun one hundred and eight times, or meditate on the Sun in his heart until sunset.

japet — should recite;
āditya-hṛdayam — the Āditya-hṛdaya;
saṅkhyayā — in number;
aṣṭa-śatam — eight hundred;
budhaḥ — the wise person;
atha vā — or else;
astamanam — sunset;
yāvat — until;
bhāskaram — Bhāskara;
cintayet — should contemplate;
hṛdi — in the heart.

BP 1.90.4

गृहमेत्य ततो विप्रान्भोजयेच्छक्तितः शिव । भुक्त्वा तु पायसं वीर ततो भूमौ स्वपेद्बुधः ॥ ॥

Then, returning home, he should feed Brahmins according to his ability, O auspicious one. Having eaten rice pudding, O hero, the wise person should then sleep on the ground.

gṛham etya — having come home;
tataḥ — then;
viprān — Brāhmaṇas;
bhojayet — should feed;
śaktitaḥ — according to ability;
śiva — O auspicious one;
bhuktvā — having eaten;
tu — indeed;
pāyasam — milk-rice, payasa;
vīra — O hero;
tataḥ — then;
bhūmau — upon the ground;
svapet — should sleep;
budhaḥ — the wise person.

BP 1.90.5

योऽत्र सम्पूजयेत्भानुं भक्त्या श्रद्धासमन्वितः । स कामांल्लभते सर्वान्भास्कराद्धृदयस्थितान् ॥ ॥

Whoever worships the Sun here with devotion and faith obtains all desires that dwell in the heart of the Sun.

yaḥ — whoever;
atra — here;
sampūjayet — should fully worship;
bhānum — Bhānu;
bhaktyā — with devotion;
śraddhā-samanvitaḥ — endowed with faith;
saḥ — he;
kāmān — desires;
labhate — obtains;
sarvān — all;
bhāskarāt — from Bhāskara;
hṛdaya-sthitān — abiding in the heart.

BP 1.90.6

तेजसा यशसा तुल्यः प्रभयैषां महात्मनः । शक्रगोपाण्डजानां तु गोपतेर्गोवृषेक्षण ॥ ॥

He becomes equal in brilliance, fame, and splendor to those great ones, Indra, the cowherd, and the egg born bird, O Lord of cows with bull like eyes.

tejasā — in radiance;
yaśasā — in fame;
tulyaḥ — equal;
prabhayā — in splendor;
eṣām — of these;
mahātmanām — great beings;
śakra-gopa-aṇḍajānām — of Śakra, gopa, and the egg-born;
tu — indeed;
gopateḥ — of the Lord of cows;
go-vṛṣa-īkṣaṇa — O one whose eyes are like a bull’s.
