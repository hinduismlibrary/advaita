Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 55 — The Solar Chariot Journey

BP 1.55.1

रुद्र उवाच
रथयात्रा कथं कार्या भास्करस्येह मानवैः ।
फलं च किं भवेत्तेषां यात्रां कुर्वन्ति ये रवेः ॥

Rudra spoke. He asked how people should perform the chariot procession of Bhāskara, and what fruit comes to those who make the journey of the Sun.

रुद्र उवाच (rudra uvāca) — Rudra said
रथयात्रा कथं कार्या भास्करस्येह मानवैः । (rathayātrā kathaṃ kāryā bhāskarasyeha mānavaiḥ |) — How should the chariot journey of Bhāskara be performed here by humans?
फलं च किं भवेत्तेषां यात्रां कुर्वन्ति ये रवेः १। । १ (phalaṃ ca kiṃ bhavetteṣāṃ yātrāṃ kurvanti ye raveḥ 1 | | 1 | |) — And what fruit is theirs who perform the journey of Ravi?

BP 1.55.2

विधिना केन कर्तव्या कस्मिन्काले सुरोत्तम ।
कथं च भ्रामयेद्देवं रथारूढं दिवाकरम् ॥

He asked by what rule and at what time it should be performed, O best of gods, and how one should make the Sun god riding on the chariot travel around.

विधिना केन कर्तव्या कस्मिन्काले सुरोत्तम । (vidhinā kena kartavyā kasminkāle surottama |) — By what rule is it to be performed, at what time, O best of gods?
कथं च भ्रामयेद्देवं रथारूढं२ दिवाकरम् । । २ (kathaṃ ca bhrāmayeddevaṃ rathārūḍhaṃ2 divākaram | | 2 | |) — And how should one make the god, mounted on the chariot, revolve, O Divākara?

BP 1.55.3

देवस्य ये रथं भक्त्या भ्रामयन्ति वहन्ति च ।
तेषां च किं फलं प्रोक्तं ये च नृत्यकरा नराः ॥

He asked what fruit is declared for those who devotionally make the chariot of the god circle and carry it, and for the men who dance.

देवस्य ये रथं भक्त्या भ्रामयन्ति वहन्ति च । (devasya ye rathaṃ bhaktyā bhrāmayanti vahanti ca |) — Those who by devotion make the god’s chariot revolve and carry it
तेषां च किं फलं प्रोक्तं ये च नृत्यकरा नराः । । ३ (teṣāṃ ca kiṃ phalaṃ proktaṃ ye ca nṛtyakarā narāḥ | | 3 | |) — What fruit is declared for them, and for those men who are dancers?

BP 1.55.4

भ्रमन्ति ये न च देवेन नृत्यगीतपरायणाः ।
प्रजागरं च कुर्वन्ति भक्त्या श्रद्धासमन्विताः ॥

He asked what fruit is declared for those who go about with the god, devoted to dancing and singing, and keep vigil with faith and devotion.

भ्रमन्ति ये न च देवेन नृत्यगीतपरायणाः । (bhramanti ye na ca devena nṛtyagītaparāyaṇāḥ |) — Those who move about with the god, devoted to dance and song
प्रजागरं च कुर्वन्ति भक्त्या श्रद्धासमन्विताः । । ४ (prajāgaraṃ ca kurvanti bhaktyā śraddhāsamanvitāḥ | | 4 | |) — And who keep vigil with devotion, endowed with faith

BP 1.55.5

तेषां च किं फलं प्रोक्तं. रथं यच्छन्ति ये रवेः ।
बलिं भक्तं च ये भक्त्या दिशन्त्याहि(?)कभोजनम् ॥

He also asked what fruit is declared for those who give a chariot to the Sun, offer a ritual offering and food with devotion, and give meals daily.

तेषां च किं फलं प्रोक्तं. रथं यच्छन्ति ३ ये रवेः । (teṣāṃ ca kiṃ phalaṃ proktaṃ. rathaṃ yacchanti 3 ye raveḥ |) — What fruit is declared for them who give the chariot of Ravi
बलिं भक्तं च ये भक्त्या दिशन्त्याहि(?)कभोजनम् । । ५ (baliṃ bhaktaṃ ca ye bhaktyā diśantyāhi kabhojanam | | 5 | |) — And for those who with devotion give bali and food

BP 1.55.6

एतन्मे ब्रूहि निखिलं सुरज्येष्ठ सविस्तरम् ।
लोकानां श्रेयसे देव परं कौतूहलं हि मे ॥

Tell me all this completely and in detail, O eldest of gods. For the welfare of the worlds, O god, my curiosity is indeed great.

एतन्मे ब्रूहि निखिलं सुरज्येष्ठ सविस्तरम् । (etanme brūhi nikhilaṃ surajyeṣṭha savistaram |) — Tell me all this completely, O eldest of the gods, in detail
लोकानां श्रेयसे देव परं कौतूहलं हि मे । । ६ (lokānāṃ śreyase deva paraṃ kautūhalaṃ hi me | | 6 | |) — For the welfare of the worlds, O god; for I have great curiosity

BP 1.55.7

ब्रह्मोवाच
साधु पृष्टोऽस्मि भूतेश गणेशोऽसि त्रिलोचन ।
शृणुष्वैकमना वच्मि यथाप्रश्नं सविस्तरम् ॥

Brahmā spoke. Well have you asked, O lord of beings. You are lord of hosts, O three eyed one. Listen with a single mind, I shall speak in detail according to your question.

ब्रह्मोवाच (brahmovāca) — Brahmā said
साधु पृष्टोऽस्मि भूतेश गणेशोऽसि त्रिलोचन । (sādhu pṛṣṭo'smi bhūteśa gaṇeśo'si trilocana |) — Well asked, O Bhūteśa; you are Gaṇeśa, O three-eyed one
शृणुष्वैकमना वच्मि यथाप्रश्नं सविस्तरम् । । ७ (śṛṇuṣvaikamanā vacmi yathāpraśnaṃ savistaram | | 7 | |) — Listen with one mind; I speak in detail according to the question

BP 1.55.8

देवस्य रथयात्रेयं भास्करस्य महात्मनः ।
इन्द्रोत्सवस्तथा रुद्र मया ह्येतौ प्रकीर्तितौ ॥

This chariot procession of the great Bhāskara, and the festival of Indra, O Rudra, these two have indeed been proclaimed by me.

देवस्य रथयात्रेयं भास्करस्य महात्मनः । (devasya rathayātreyaṃ bhāskarasya mahātmanaḥ |) — This chariot journey of the great-souled Bhāskara
इन्द्रोत्सवस्तथा रुद्र मया ह्येतौ प्रकीर्तितौ । । ८ (indrotsavastathā rudra mayā hyetau prakīrtitau | | 8 | |) — And the Indra festival, O Rudra—these two have been proclaimed by me

BP 1.55.9

मर्त्यलोके शान्तिहेतोर्लोकानां लोकपूजित ।
प्रवर्तितावुभौ यस्मिन्देशे देवमहोत्सवौ ॥

Both these great divine festivals are established in whatever land they are held, for the peace of people in the mortal world, O worshipped by the worlds.

मर्त्यलोके शान्तिहेतोर्लोकानां लोकपूजित । (martyaloke śāntihetorlokānāṃ lokapūjita |) — For the sake of peace in the mortal world of the worlds, O worshipped of worlds
प्रवर्तितावुभौ यस्मिन्देशे देवमहोत्सवौ । । ९ (pravartitāvubhau yasmindeśe devamahotsavau | | 9 | |) — In whichever country these two divine festivals have been commenced

BP 1.55.10

न तत्रोपद्रवाः सन्ति राजतस्करसम्भवाः ।
तस्मात्कार्याविमौ भक्त्या दुर्भिक्षस्येह शान्तये ॥

There calamities arising from kings and thieves do not occur. Therefore, these two should be performed with devotion here for the pacification of famine.

न तत्रोपद्रवाः सन्ति राजतस्करसम्भवाः । (na tatropadravāḥ santi rājataskarasambhavāḥ |) — There are no disturbances there arising from kings and thieves
तस्मात्कार्याविमौ भक्त्या दुर्भिक्षस्येह शान्तये । । 1.55.१० (tasmātkāryāvimau bhaktyā durbhikṣasyeha śāntaye | | 10 | |) — Therefore these two should be performed with devotion here for the appeasement of famine

BP 1.55.11

शुक्लपक्षे तु सप्तम्यां मासि भाद्रपदे हर ।
घृतेनाभ्यङ्गयेद्देवं पञ्चपूताङ्गजेन वै ॥

On the seventh of the bright fortnight in the month of Bhādrapada, O Hara, one should anoint the god with clarified butter and a substance made from five purified ingredients.

शुक्लपक्षे तु सप्तम्यां मासि भाद्रपदे हर । (śuklapakṣe tu saptamyāṃ māsi bhādrapade hara |) — On the seventh of the bright fortnight, in the month Bhādrapada, O Hara
घृतेनाभ्यङ्गयेद्देवं पञ्चपूताङ्गजेन वै । । ११ (ghṛtenābhyaṅgayeddevaṃ pañcapūtāṅgajena vai | | 11 | |) — One should anoint the god with ghee and with the pañcapūta-aṅgaja

BP 1.55.12

अभ्यङ्गयेद्महेशं यः सर्षपैः श्रद्धयान्वितः ।
दिने दिने जगन्नाथं प्रविष्टं वर्णके रविम् ॥

Whoever anoints Maheśa with mustard seeds, filled with faith, anoints the lord of the world, the Sun, placed in the colored design day after day.

अभ्यङ्गयेद्महेशं यः सर्षपैः श्रद्धयान्वितः । (abhyaṅgayedmaheśaṃ yaḥ sarṣapaiḥ śraddhayānvitaḥ |) — He who anoints Maheśa with mustard seeds, endowed with faith
दिने दिने जगन्नाथं प्रविष्टं वर्णके रविम् । । १२ (dine dine jagannāthaṃ praviṣṭaṃ varṇake ravim | | 12 | |) — The Lord of the world, the Sun, entered in varṇaka, day by day

BP 1.55.13

स गच्छेद्यानमारूढो गैरिकं किङ्किणीकृतम् ।
वैश्वानरपुरं दिव्यं गन्धर्वाप्सरशोभितम् ॥

Mounted on a vehicle decorated with ochre and made to tinkle with little bells, he goes to the divine city of Fire, adorned with Gandharvas and celestial nymphs.

स गच्छेद्यानमारूढो गैरिकं किङ्किणीकृतम् । (sa gacchedyānamārūḍho gairikaṃ kiṅkiṇīkṛtam |) — He goes, mounted on a vehicle made tinkling with gairika
वैश्वानरपुरं दिव्यं गन्धर्वाप्सरशोभितम् । । १३ (vaiśvānarapuraṃ divyaṃ gandharvāpsaraśobhitam | | 13 | |) — To the divine city of Vaiśvānara, adorned with Gandharvas and Apsarases

BP 1.55.14

शाल्योदनं खण्डमिश्रं वज्रं वज्रसमन्वितम् ।
वर्णभक्तं प्रयच्छेद्यो भास्कराय दिने दिने ॥

One should offer daily to Bhāskara fine rice mixed with sugar, a diamond, a diamond mixture, and colored rice.

शाल्योदनं खण्डमिश्रं वज्रं वज्रसमन्वितम् । (śālyodanaṃ khaṇḍamiśraṃ vajraṃ vajrasamanvitam |) — Śālyodana mixed with khaṇḍa, vajra endowed with vajra
वर्णभक्तं प्रयच्छेद्यो भास्कराय दिने दिने । । १४ (varṇabhaktaṃ prayacchedyo bhāskarāya dine dine | | 14 | |) — He who gives varṇabhakta to Bhāskara day by day

BP 1.55.15

आरूढः स विमानं तु ज्वालामालाकुलं शुभम् ।
गच्छेन्मम पुरं देव स्तूयमानो महर्षिभिः ॥

Having mounted an auspicious aerial car crowded with garlands of flames, he goes to my city, O god, praised by great sages.

आरूढः स विमानं तु ज्वालामालाकुलं शुभम् । (ārūḍhaḥ sa vimānaṃ tu jvālāmālākulaṃ śubham |) — Having mounted an auspicious vimāna filled with garlands of flames
गच्छेन्मम पुरं देव स्तूयमानो महर्षिभिः । । १५ (gacchenmama puraṃ deva stūyamāno maharṣibhiḥ | | 15 | |) — Let him go to my city, O god, praised by great sages

BP 1.55.16

तस्मात्सर्वप्रयत्नेन भास्कराय नरः शिव ।
वर्णभक्तं प्रदातव्यं प्रविष्टस्येह वर्णकम् ॥

Therefore, with every effort, O Śiva, the man should give colored rice to Bhāskara who has entered the colored design.

तस्मात्सर्वप्रयत्नेन भास्कराय नरः शिव । (tasmātsarvaprayatnena bhāskarāya naraḥ śiva |) — Therefore with every effort, O auspicious man, for Bhāskara
वर्णभक्तं प्रदातव्यं प्रविष्टस्येह वर्णकम् । । १६ (varṇabhaktaṃ pradātavyaṃ praviṣṭasyeha varṇakam | | 16 | |) — Varṇabhakta should be given, varṇaka having entered here

BP 1.55.17

घृतपूर्णं खण्डवेष्टं कासारं मोदकं पयः ।
दध्योदनं पायसं च संयावं गुडपूपकान् ॥

Ghee filled sweets, sugar covered cakes, a rice and pulse dish, sweet dumplings, milk, curd rice, rice pudding, flour cakes, and jaggery cakes.

घृतपूर्णं खण्डवेष्टं कासारं मोदकं पयः । (ghṛtapūrṇaṃ khaṇḍaveṣṭaṃ kāsāraṃ modakaṃ payaḥ |) — Ghee-filled, khaṇḍaveṣṭa, kāsāra, modaka, milk
दध्योदनं पायसं च संयावं गुडपूपकान् । । १७ (dadhyodanaṃ pāyasaṃ ca saṃyāvaṃ guḍapūpakān | | 17 | |) — Curd-rice, pāyasa, saṃyāva, and jaggery cakes

BP 1.55.18

ये प्रयच्छन्ति देवस्य भास्करस्येह वर्णकम् ।
ते गच्छन्ति न सन्देहो नरा वै मन्दिरं मम ॥

Those who offer this colored food to the god Bhāskara here go to my temple, there is no doubt.

ये प्रयच्छन्ति देवस्य भास्करस्येह वर्णकम् । (ye prayacchanti devasya bhāskarasyeha varṇakam |) — Those who give here the varṇaka of the god Bhāskara
ते गच्छन्ति न सन्देहो नरा वै मन्दिरं मम । । १८ (te gacchanti na sandeho narā vai mandiraṃ mama | | 18 | |) — Those men go, without doubt, to my temple

BP 1.55.19

अहन्यहनि यो भक्त्या भास्कराय प्रयच्छति ।
अभ्यङ्गाय घृतं देयं स याति परमां गतिम् ॥

Whoever offers to Bhāskara with devotion every day should give clarified butter for anointing. He goes to the highest state.

अहन्यहनि यो भक्त्या भास्कराय प्रयच्छति । (ahanyahani yo bhaktyā bhāskarāya prayacchati |) — He who gives daily with devotion to Bhāskara
अभ्यङ्गाय घृतं देयं स याति परमां गतिम् । । १९ (abhyaṅgāya ghṛtaṃ deyaṃ sa yāti paramāṃ gatim | | 19 | |) — Ghee should be given for anointing; he goes to the highest state

BP 1.55.20

तथा यो वर्णभक्तं च अहन्यहनि भक्तितः ।
स प्राप्येह शुभान्कामान्गच्छेत्स भवसालयम् ॥

Likewise, whoever offers colored rice with devotion every day obtains auspicious desires here and goes to the abode of Bhava.

तथा यो वर्णभक्तं च अहन्यहनि भक्तितः । (tathā yo varṇabhaktaṃ ca ahanyahani bhaktitaḥ |) — Likewise he who gives varṇabhakta every day with devotion
स प्राप्येह शुभान्कामान्गच्छेत्स भवसालयम् । । 1.55.२० (sa prāpyeha śubhānkāmāngacchetsa bhavasālayam | | 20 | |) — Having obtained auspicious desires here, let him go to Bhavasālaya

BP 1.55.21

चूर्णमुद्वर्तनायेह यः प्रयच्छेच्छुभं रवेः ।
स याति परमं स्थानं यत्र देवो दिवाकरः ॥

Whoever gives fine powder here for rubbing on the Sun goes to the highest place where the Sun god is.

चूर्णमुद्वर्तनायेह यः प्रयच्छेच्छुभं रवेः । (cūrṇamudvartanāyeha yaḥ prayacchecchubhaṃ raveḥ |) — He who gives auspicious powder here for udvartana to Ravi
स याति परमं स्थानं यत्र देवो दिवाकरः । । २१ (sa yāti paramaṃ sthānaṃ yatra devo divākaraḥ | | 21 | |) — He goes to the supreme place where the god Divākara is

BP 1.55.22

ततस्तं स्नापयेद्देवं पौषे मासि विधानतः ।
सप्तम्यां शुक्लपक्षस्य शृणुष्वैकमनास्तथा ॥

Then one should bathe that god according to rule in the month of Pauṣa, on the seventh day of the bright fortnight. Listen with a single mind.

ततस्तं स्नापयेद्देवं पौषे मासि विधानतः । (tatastaṃ snāpayeddevaṃ pauṣe māsi vidhānataḥ |) — Then he should bathe the god according to rule in the month Pauṣa
सप्तम्यां शुक्लपक्षस्य शृणुष्वैकमनास्तथा । । २२ (saptamyāṃ śuklapakṣasya śṛṇuṣvaikamanāstathā | | 22 | |) — On the seventh of the bright fortnight; listen likewise with one mind

BP 1.55.23

तीर्थोदकमुपानीय अन्यद्वाथ जलं शुभम् ।
वेदोक्तेन विधानेन प्रतिमां स्थापयेद्बुधः ॥

Taking water from a sacred ford or other pure water, the wise one should install the image according to the procedure stated in the Veda.

तीर्थोदकमुपानीय अन्यद्वाथ जलं शुभम् । (tīrthodakamupānīya anyadvātha jalaṃ śubham |) — Bringing water from a tīrtha, or other auspicious water
वेदोक्तेन विधानेन प्रतिमां स्थापयेद्बुधः । । २३ (vedoktena vidhānena pratimāṃ sthāpayedbudhaḥ | | 23 | |) — The wise man should install the image according to the rule spoken in the Veda

BP 1.55.24

यजेद्धि तीर्थनामानि मनसा संस्मरन्बुधः ।
प्रयागं पुष्करं देवं कुरुक्षेत्रं च नैमिषम् ॥

ब्रह्मघोषैश्च विविधैः समन्ताद्दीपकैः शुभैः ॥

With sounds of conches and instruments, excellent spectacles, various recitations, and auspicious lamps all around.

शंखवादित्रनिर्घोषैस्तथा प्रेक्षणकैर्वरैः । (śaṃkhavāditranirghoṣaistathā prekṣaṇakairvaraiḥ |) — From there, O Rudra, it should move and go to the middle of the city
ब्रह्मघोषैश्च विविधैः समन्ताद्दीपकैः शुभैः । । ९३ (brahmaghoṣaiśca vividhaiḥ samantāddīpakaiḥ śubhaiḥ | | 93 | |) — There standing, Brahmins worship it endowed with faith

BP 1.55.94

नानाविधैर्वित्तदानैर्ब्राह्मणानां च तर्पणैः ।
दीनान्धकृपणानां च तर्पणैस्त्रिपुरान्तक ॥

With various gifts of wealth, offerings to the Brahmins, and offerings to the poor, blind, and needy, O destroyer of Tripura.

नानाविधैर्वित्तदानैर्ब्राह्मणानां च तर्पणैः । (nānāvidhairvittadānairbrāhmaṇānāṃ ca tarpaṇaiḥ |) — With the roar of conches and instruments and with excellent spectacles
दीनान्धकृपणानां च तर्पणैस्त्रिपुरान्तक । । ९४ (dīnāndhakṛpaṇānāṃ ca tarpaṇaistripurāntaka | | 94 | |) — With various Brahmanical recitations and auspicious lamps all around

BP 1.55.95

पुरमध्यात्तु चलितस्तिष्ठेत्प्राप्य स्वमंदिरम् ।
इत्थं प्राप्य स्थितो देवः पुरतो मंदिरस्य तु ॥

Having departed from the middle of the city, it should stop upon reaching its own temple. Thus having arrived, the god stands before the temple.

पुरमध्यात्तु चलितस्तिष्ठेत्प्राप्य स्वमंदिरम् । (puramadhyāttu calitastiṣṭhetprāpya svamaṃdiram |) — With various gifts of wealth and with satisfactions of Brahmins
इत्थं प्राप्य स्थितो देवः पुरतो मंदिरस्य तु । । ९५ (itthaṃ prāpya sthito devaḥ purato maṃdirasya tu | | 95 | |) — And with satisfactions of the poor and miserly, O Tripurāntaka

BP 1.55.96

तत्र स्थितः पूजनीयो भवेत्पौरेण कृत्स्नशः ।
पूज्यमानस्त्वहोरात्रं रथारूढस्तु तिष्ठति ॥

Standing there, he should be worshipped fully by the townspeople. Being worshipped day and night, he remains mounted on the chariot.

तत्र स्थितः पूजनीयो भवेत्पौरेण कृत्स्नशः । (tatra sthitaḥ pūjanīyo bhavetpaureṇa kṛtsnaśaḥ |) — Having moved from the middle of the city, it should stay after reaching its own temple
पूज्यमानस्त्वहोरात्रं रथारूढस्तु तिष्ठति । । ९६ (pūjyamānastvahorātraṃ rathārūḍhastu tiṣṭhati | | 96 | |) — Thus having reached, the god stands before the temple

BP 1.55.97

अपरे दिने व्रजेत्स्थानं तच्चिरन्तनमादरात् ।
त्रयोदश्यां व्यतीतायां चतुर्दश्यां त्रिलोचन ॥

On the next day, it should reverently go to its ancient place, on the fourteenth day after the thirteenth has passed, O three eyed one.

अपरे दिने व्रजेत्स्थानं तच्चिरन्तनमादरात् । (apare dine vrajetsthānaṃ taccirantanamādarāt |) — Standing there, he should be worshipped entirely by the townspeople
त्रयोदश्यां व्यतीतायां चतुर्दश्यां त्रिलोचन । । ९७ (trayodaśyāṃ vyatītāyāṃ caturdaśyāṃ trilocana | | 97 | |) — Being worshipped day and night, mounted on the chariot, he remains

BP 1.55.98

सदैवं भ्रामयेद्देवं ग्रहेशं दुरितापहम् ।
परिवारयुतं रुद्र सानुगं परमेश्वरम् ॥

One should always cause the god, the lord of the planets, the remover of evil, to circle in this way, O Rudra, together with his retinue and attendants.

सदैवं भ्रामयेद्देवं ग्रहेशं दुरितापहम् । (sadaivaṃ bhrāmayeddevaṃ graheśaṃ duritāpaham |) — On the next day he should go respectfully to the eternal place
