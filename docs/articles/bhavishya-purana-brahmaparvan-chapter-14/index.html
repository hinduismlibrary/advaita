Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 14 — Conduct During Absence and Relations Among Co-Wives

BP 1.14.1

ब्रह्मोवाच
प्रेषिते मण्डनं स्त्रीणां पत्यौ मङ्गलमात्रकम् ।
निष्पादनं च यत्नेन तदारब्धस्य कर्मणः ॥

When her husband is away, a woman’s adornment should be only auspicious; she should carefully complete the work he began.

ब्रह्मा (brahmā) — Brahmā; उवाच (uvāca) — spoke; प्रेषिते (preṣite) — when [he is] away; मण्डनम् (maṇḍanam) — adornment; स्त्रीणाम् (strīṇām) — of women; पत्यौ (patyau) — when the husband; मङ्गलमात्रकम् (maṅgalamātrakam) — only auspicious; निष्पादनम् (niṣpādanam) — completion; च (ca) — and; यत्नेन (yatnena) — carefully; तत् (tat) — that; आरब्धस्य (ārabdhasya) — begun; कर्मणः (karmaṇaḥ) — of the work

BP 1.14.2

शय्यामुपार्जनमर्थानां व्ययानां परिहापणम् ॥

She should prepare a bed, acquire resources, and reduce expenses.

शय्याम् (śayyām) — a bed; उपार्जनम् (upārjanam) — acquiring; अर्थानाम् (arthānām) — of resources; व्ययानाम् (vyayānām) — of expenses; परिहापणम् (parihāpaṇam) — reduction

BP 1.14.3

व्रतोपवासतात्पर्यं तद्वार्तापरिमार्गणम् ।
दैवज्ञेक्षणिकप्रश्नो देवानामुपयाचनम् ॥

She should be devoted to vows and fasts, seek news of him, question astrologers and diviners, and supplicate the gods.

व्रत (vrata) — vow; उपवास (upavāsa) — fast; तात्पर्यम् (tātparyam) — devotion; तद्वार्ता (tadvārtā) — news of him; परिमार्गणम् (parimārgaṇam) — seeking; दैवज्ञ (daivajña) — astrologer; ईक्षणिक (īkṣaṇika) — diviner; प्रश्नः (praśnaḥ) — questioning; देवानाम् (devānām) — of the gods; उपयाचनम् (upayācanam) — supplication

BP 1.14.4

नित्यं तस्यागमाशंसा क्षेमार्थं देवपूजनम् ।
न चात्युज्ज्वलवेषत्वं न सदा तैलधारणम् ॥

She should always hope for his arrival and worship the gods for his welfare; she should not wear excessively bright clothing or apply oil every day.

नित्यम् (nityam) — always; तस्य (tasya) — his; आगम (āgama) — arrival; आशंसा (āśaṃsā) — hope; क्षेमार्थम् (kṣemārtham) — for his welfare; देवपूजनम् (devapūjanam) — worship of the gods; न (na) — not; च (ca) — and; अत्युज्ज्वलवेषत्वम् (atyujjvalaveṣatvam) — excessively bright attire; न (na) — not; सदा (sadā) — always; तैलधारणम् (tailadhāraṇam) — application of oil

BP 1.14.5

ज्ञातिवेश्म न गन्तव्यं सकामगमनेन च ।
गुरूणामाज्ञया यावद्भर्तुराप्तजनैः सह ॥

She should not visit a relative’s house at her own desire; she should go with the elders’ permission and accompanied by people trusted by her husband.

ज्ञाति (jñāti) — relative; वेश्म (veśma) — house; न (na) — not; गन्तव्यम् (gantavyam) — should be visited; सकाम (sakāma) — at her own desire; गमनेन (gamanena) — by going; च (ca) — and; गुरूणाम् (gurūṇām) — of the elders; आज्ञया (ājñayā) — by permission; यावत् (yāvat) — as far as; भर्तुः (bhartuḥ) — of the husband; आप्तजनैः (āptajanaiḥ) — with trusted people; सह (saha) — together

BP 1.14.6

तत्रापि न चिरं तिष्ठेत्स्नानादीन्वापि नाचरेत् ।
यावदर्थं क्षणं स्थित्वा ततः शीघ्रं समाचरेत् ॥

Even there she should not stay long or perform bathing and the like; after staying only as long as needed, she should return quickly.

तत्र (tatra) — there; अपि (api) — even; न (na) — not; चिरम् (ciram) — for long; तिष्ठेत् (tiṣṭhet) — she should stay; स्नान (snāna) — bathing; आदीन् (ādīn) — and the like; वा (vā) — or; अपि (api) — even; न (na) — not; आचरेत् (ācaret) — she should perform; यावदर्थम् (yāvadartham) — as long as needed; क्षणम् (kṣaṇam) — a moment; स्थित्वा (sthitvā) — having stayed; ततः (tataḥ) — then; शीघ्रम् (śīghram) — quickly; समाचरेत् (samācaret) — she should return

BP 1.14.7

आगते प्रकृतिस्थैव कृत्वा तात्कालिकं विधिम् ।
मुक्तप्रवासने पथ्ये स्नाते भुक्तवति प्रिये ॥

When he has arrived, she should remain composed and perform the rite appropriate to that time; after her beloved has recovered from travel, taken suitable food, bathed, and eaten—

आगते (āgate) — when he has arrived; प्रकृतिस्था (prakṛtisthā) — composed; एव (eva) — indeed; कृत्वा (kṛtvā) — having performed; तात्कालिकम् (tātkālikam) — appropriate to that time; विधिम् (vidhim) — rite; मुक्त (mukta) — released; प्रवासने (pravāsane) — from travel; पथ्ये (pathye) — suitable food; स्नाते (snāte) — having bathed; भुक्तवति (bhuktavati) — having eaten; प्रिये (priye) — beloved

BP 1.14.8

आत्मानं समलङ्कृत्य सविशेषं मुदान्विता ।
देवपूजोपहारादीन्दद्यात्प्रागुपपादितान् ॥

—she should especially adorn herself, filled with joy, and give the offerings for worship of the gods and the like that were prepared beforehand.

आत्मानम् (ātmānam) — herself; समलङ्कृत्य (samalaṅkṛtya) — having adorned; सविशेषम् (saviśeṣam) — especially; मुदान्विता (mudānvitā) — filled with joy; देवपूजा (devapūjā) — worship of the gods; उपहार (upahāra) — offering; आदीन् (ādīn) — and the like; दद्यात् (dadyāt) — she should give; प्राक् (prāk) — beforehand; उपपादितान् (upapāditān) — prepared

BP 1.14.9

कनिष्ठामातृवज्ज्येष्ठां तदपत्यानि चात्मवत् ।
पश्येत्तत्परिवर्गं तु नित्यं स्वपरिवर्गवत् ॥

The younger wife should regard the elder wife as a mother, the elder wife’s children as her own, and the entire circle always as her own family.

कनिष्ठाम् (kaniṣṭhām) — the younger wife; मातृवत् (mātṛvat) — like a mother; ज्येष्ठाम् (jyeṣṭhām) — the elder wife; तदपत्यानि (tadapatyāni) — her children; च (ca) — and; आत्मवत् (ātmavat) — as her own; पश्येत् (paśyet) — she should regard; तत्परिवर्गम् (tatparivargam) — her whole circle; तु (tu) — indeed; नित्यम् (nityam) — always; स्वपरिवर्गवत् (svaparivargavat) — like her own family

BP 1.14.10

तत्पुरोनासने तिष्ठेत्पतिं नामंत्रयेत च ।
तदभिप्रायतः कुर्यात्प्रवृत्तिं सर्वकर्मसु ॥

She should not sit on a seat before the elder wife or summon her husband; she should conduct all activities according to the elder wife’s intention.

तत्पुरः (tatpuraḥ) — before her; न (na) — not; आसने (āsane) — on a seat; तिष्ठेत् (tiṣṭhet) — she should sit; पतिम् (patim) — her husband; न (na) — not; आमन्त्रयेत् (āmantrayet) — she should address; च (ca) — and; तदभिप्रायतः (tadabhiprāyataḥ) — according to her intention; कुर्यात् (kuryāt) — she should perform; प्रवृत्तिम् (pravṛttim) — conduct; सर्वकर्मसु (sarvakarmasu) — in all activities

BP 1.14.11

न संसृजेत तद्द्विष्टैः सख्यं कुर्वीत तत्प्रियैः ।
जनमाप्ततमं तस्य सदाभर्तुश्च मानयेत् ॥

She should not associate with those disliked by the elder wife; she should befriend those dear to her and always honor her husband’s most trusted person.

न (na) — not; संसृजेत (saṃsṛjeta) — she should associate; तद्द्विष्टैः (taddviṣṭaiḥ) — with those disliked by her; सख्यम् (sakhyam) — friendship; कुर्वीत (kurvīta) — she should make; तत्प्रियैः (tatpriyaiḥ) — with those dear to her; जनम् (janam) — a person; आप्ततमम् (āptatamam) — most trusted; तस्य (tasya) — of him; सदा (sadā) — always; भर्तुः (bhartuḥ) — of her husband; च (ca) — and; मानयेत् (mānayet) — she should honor

BP 1.14.12

पैतृकात्समुपानीतं वसुसौगंधिकादिकम् ।
तस्मै निवेद्यात्मतया तदा तदुपयोजयेत् ॥

Whatever wealth, perfumes, and the like she has brought from her father’s house, she should disclose to the elder wife and then use as her own.

पैतृकात् (paitṛkāt) — from her father’s house; समुपानीतम् (samupānītam) — brought together; वसु (vasu) — wealth; सौगन्धिक (saugandhika) — perfumed things; आदिकम् (ādikam) — and the like; तस्मै (tasmai) — to her; निवेद्य (nivedya) — having disclosed; आत्मतया (ātmatayā) — as her own; तदा (tadā) — then; तत् (tat) — that; उपयोजयेत् (upayojayet) — she should use

BP 1.14.13

सोपि तत्प्रीतये किंचिदादद्यादल्पमूल्यकम् ।
संगोप्य मातृवत्स्थेयं तत्तथैवोपयोजयेत् ॥

The elder wife too should accept something of little value for her pleasure; the younger wife should remain with her like a mother and use it accordingly.

सा (sā) — she; अपि (api) — also; तत्प्रीतये (tatprītaye) — for her pleasure; किञ्चित् (kiñcit) — something; आदद्यात् (ādadyāt) — she should accept; अल्पमूल्यकम् (alpamūlyakam) — of little value; संगोप्य (saṃgopya) — having kept it; मातृवत् (mātṛvat) — like a mother; स्थेयम् (stheyam) — one should remain; तत् (tat) — that; तथा (tathā) — so; एव (eva) — indeed; उपयोजयेत् (upayojayet) — she should use

BP 1.14.14

तत्प्रीत्यर्थं गृहीतं यद्वैलक्ष्यादिनिवृत्तये ।
सविशेषं प्रसंगेन तस्यैतत्प्रतिपादयेत् ॥

Whatever was received for her pleasure or to remove embarrassment and the like, she should present to her with special care when an occasion arises.

तत्प्रीत्यर्थम् (tatprītyartham) — for her pleasure; गृहीतम् (gṛhītam) — received; यत् (yat) — whatever; वैलक्ष्य (vailakṣya) — embarrassment; आदि (ādi) — and the like; निवृत्तये (nivṛttaye) — for removal; सविशेषम् (saviśeṣam) — especially; प्रसंगेन (prasaṃgena) — when an occasion arises; तस्य (tasya) — to her; एतत् (etat) — this; प्रतिपादयेत् (pratipādayet) — she should present

BP 1.14.15

स्त्रीणां यदेतत्सापत्न्यं परं मात्सर्यकारणम् ।
तस्मात्तत्परिहर्तव्यं परमोदारचर्यया ॥

Because co-wifehood is a great cause of jealousy among women, it should be avoided through very generous conduct.

स्त्रीणाम् (strīṇām) — among women; यत् (yat) — that which; एतत् (etat) — this; सापत्न्यम् (sāpatnyam) — co-wifehood; परम् (param) — great; मात्सर्यकारणम् (mātsaryakāraṇam) — cause of jealousy; तस्मात् (tasmāt) — therefore; तत् (tat) — that; परिहर्तव्यम् (parihartavyam) — should be avoided; परमोदारचर्यया (paramodāracaryayā) — by very generous conduct

BP 1.14.16

तथा कल्पितनेपथ्या भर्तुः पर्यायवासरे ।
ह्रियमादयमानेव पतिं गच्छेद्विसर्जिता ॥

Thus attired for her turn with her husband, when sent for she should go to him as if assuming modesty.

तथा (tathā) — thus; कल्पितनेपथ्या (kalpitanepathyā) — attired; भर्तुः (bhartuḥ) — of her husband; पर्यायवासरे (paryāyavāsare) — on her turn; ह्रियम् (hriyam) — modesty; आद्यमाना (ādyamānā) — assuming; इव (iva) — as if; पतिम् (patim) — to her husband; गच्छेत् (gacchet) — she should go; विसर्जिता (visarjitā) — when sent for

BP 1.14.17

गत्वा रहसि भर्तारं तत्कालोचितसंभ्रमैः ।
तद्भावानुगतैस्तैस्तैः सविशेषमुपाचरेत् ॥

Having gone to her husband in private, she should attend to him especially with attentiveness suited to that time and following his disposition.

गत्वा (gatvā) — having gone; रहसि (rahasi) — in private; भर्तारम् (bhartāram) — to her husband; तत्कालोचित (tatkālocita) — appropriate to that time; संभ्रमैः (saṃbhramaiḥ) — with attentiveness; तद्भावानुगतैः (tadbhāvānugataiḥ) — following his disposition; तैः (taiḥ) — with those; तैः (taiḥ) — with those; सविशेषम् (saviśeṣam) — especially; उपाचरेत् (upācaret) — she should attend

BP 1.14.18

प्रतिबुध्य ततः काले सविशेषं त्रपान्विता ।
ज्येष्ठाय वसतिं गच्छेद्विशेषेण तथा पुनः ॥

Having awakened at the time, filled especially with modesty, she should go again to the elder wife’s dwelling.

प्रतिबुध्य (pratibudhya) — having awakened; ततः (tataḥ) — then; काले (kāle) — at the time; सविशेषम् (saviśeṣam) — especially; त्रपान्विता (trapānvitā) — filled with modesty; ज्येष्ठाय (jyeṣṭhāya) — to the elder wife; वसतिम् (vasatim) — dwelling; गच्छेत् (gacchet) — she should go; विशेषेण (viśeṣeṇa) — particularly; तथा (tathā) — thus; पुनः (punaḥ) — again

BP 1.14.19

अप्रातिकूल्यं ज्येष्ठाया हितमन्यत्र योषितः ।
ततः शनैस्त्ववच्छिद्य पतिं स्ववशमानयेत् ॥

Without opposition to the elder wife, seeking the good of the other woman, she should then gradually draw her husband under her control.

अप्रातिकूल्यम् (aprātikūlyam) — without opposition; ज्येष्ठायाः (jyeṣṭhāyāḥ) — to the elder wife; हितम् (hitam) — the good; अन्यत्र (anyatra) — for the other; योषितः (yoṣitaḥ) — woman; ततः (tataḥ) — then; शनैः (śanaiḥ) — slowly; तु (tu) — but; अवच्छिद्य (avacchidya) — drawing away; पतिम् (patim) — the husband; स्ववशम् (svavaśam) — under her own control; आनयेत् (ānayet) — she should bring

BP 1.14.20

बहिष्पाकादियोगेन चतुःषष्ट्या रहोगतम् ।
ज्येष्ठामतिशयानेव भर्तारमुपरञ्जयेत् ॥

By skill in cooking separately and the like, and by the sixty-four arts in private, she should please her husband, indeed surpassing the elder wife.

बहिः (bahiḥ) — outside; पाक (pāka) — cooking; आदि (ādi) — and the like; योगेन (yogena) — by skill; चतुःषष्ट्या (catuḥṣaṣṭyā) — by the sixty-four arts; रहोगतम् (rahogatam) — in private; ज्येष्ठाम् (jyeṣṭhām) — the elder wife; अतिशयाना (atiśayānā) — surpassing; एव (eva) — indeed; भर्तारम् (bhartāram) — the husband; उपरञ्जयेत् (uparañjayet) — she should please

BP 1.14.21

प्रागल्भ्यं रहसि स्त्रीणां लज्जाधिक्यं ततोऽन्यदा ।
चित्तज्ञानानुवृत्तिश्च पत्यौ संसेवनं परम् ॥

For women, boldness in private, greater modesty at other times, understanding and following the husband’s mind, and serving him are supreme.

प्रागल्भ्यम् (prāgalbhyam) — boldness; रहसि (rahasi) — in private; स्त्रीणाम् (strīṇām) — for women; लज्जाधिक्यम् (lajjādhikyam) — greater modesty; ततः (tataḥ) — then; अन्यदा (anyadā) — at other times; चित्तज्ञान (cittajñāna) — understanding the mind; अनुवृत्तिः (anuvṛttiḥ) — following; च (ca) — and; पत्यौ (patyau) — toward the husband; संसेवनम् (saṃsevanam) — service; परम् (param) — supreme

BP 1.14.22

एवमाराध्य भर्तारं गृहमाक्रम्य च क्रमात् ।
गौरवं प्रतिपत्तिं वा ज्येष्ठादिषु न हापयेत् ॥

Having thus pleased her husband and gradually entered the household, she should not diminish respect or deference toward the elder wife and the others.

एवम् (evam) — thus; आराध्य (ārādhya) — having pleased; भर्तारम् (bhartāram) — the husband; गृहम् (gṛham) — the household; आक्रम्य (ākramya) — having entered; च (ca) — and; क्रमात् (kramāt) — gradually; गौरवम् (gauravam) — respect; प्रतिपत्तिम् (pratipattim) — deference; वा (vā) — or; ज्येष्ठादिषु (jyeṣṭhādiṣu) — toward the elder wife and others; न (na) — not; हापयेत् (hāpayet) — she should diminish

BP 1.14.23

गृहव्यापारदानेषु पतिं गूढं तथा वदेत् ।
अधिकुर्यादनिच्छन्ती ज्येष्ठैवेनां यथा बलात् ॥

In household affairs and assignments she should speak privately to her husband; the elder wife should place her in charge, though unwilling, as if by force.

गृहव्यापार (gṛhavyāpāra) — household affairs; दानेषु (dāneṣu) — in assignments; पतिम् (patim) — to the husband; गूढम् (gūḍham) — privately; तथा (tathā) — thus; वदेत् (vadet) — she should speak; अधिकुर्यात् (adhikuryāt) — she should place in charge; अनिच्छन्ती (anicchantī) — though unwilling; ज्येष्ठा (jyeṣṭhā) — the elder wife; इव (iva) — as if; एनाम् (enām) — her; यथा (yathā) — as; बलात् (balāt) — by force

BP 1.14.24

सापि विज्ञाय भर्तारं कनिष्ठाकृष्टमानसम् ।
विश्रामं प्रार्थयेदेनामधिकुर्यात्सुतामिव ॥

She too, understanding that her husband’s mind is drawn to the younger wife, should ask her for relief and appoint her as a daughter.

सा (sā) — she; अपि (api) — also; विज्ञाय (vijñāya) — having understood; भर्तारम् (bhartāram) — the husband; कनिष्ठा (kaniṣṭhā) — by the younger wife; आकृष्ट (ākṛṣṭa) — drawn; मानसम् (mānasam) — mind; विश्रामम् (viśrāmam) — relief; प्रार्थयेत् (prārthayet) — she should request; एनाम् (enām) — from her; अधिकुर्यात् (adhikuryāt) — she should appoint; सुताम् (sutām) — a daughter; इव (iva) — like

BP 1.14.25

मत्वा भर्तुरभिप्रेतं रक्षन्ती निजगौरवम् ।
कृतं भर्त्रनुकूलं स्यात्तदिष्टायानुमोदयेत् ॥

Understanding her husband’s wish and preserving her own dignity, the elder wife should approve what has been done to please him.

मत्वा (matvā) — having understood; भर्तुः (bhartuḥ) — of the husband; अभिप्रेतम् (abhipretam) — the intention; रक्षन्ती (rakṣantī) — preserving; निज (nija) — her own; गौरवम् (gauravam) — dignity; कृतम् (kṛtam) — what is done; भर्त्रनुकूलम् (bhartranukūlam) — pleasing to the husband; स्यात् (syāt) — may be; तत् (tat) — that; इष्टायाः (iṣṭāyāḥ) — of the beloved; अनुमोदयेत् (anumodayet) — she should approve

BP 1.14.26

स्थानिनो यदभिप्रेतं भृत्यैः किं क्रियतेऽन्यथा ।
क्लिश्यते तत्र मूढात्मा परतन्त्रो वृथा जनः ॥

What can servants do otherwise than what the master wishes? There the foolish, dependent person suffers in vain.

स्थानिनः (sthāninaḥ) — of the master; यत् (yat) — whatever; अभिप्रेतम् (abhipretam) — is desired; भृत्यैः (bhṛtyaiḥ) — by servants; किम् (kim) — what; क्रियते (kriyate) — is done; अन्यथा (anyathā) — otherwise; क्लिश्यते (kliśyate) — suffers; तत्र (tatra) — there; मूढात्मा (mūḍhātmā) — the foolish person; परतन्त्रः (paratantraḥ) — dependent; वृथा (vṛthā) — in vain; जनः (janaḥ) — a person

BP 1.14.27

तस्मात्सर्वास्ववस्थासु मनोवाक्कायकर्मभिः ।
हितं स्वाम्यनुकूलत्वं नारीणां तु विशेषतः ॥

Therefore, in every circumstance, accord with the master through mind, speech, body, and actions is beneficial, especially for women.

तस्मात् (tasmāt) — therefore; सर्वासु (sarvāsu) — in all; अवस्थासु (avasthāsu) — circumstances; मनः (manaḥ) — mind; वाक् (vāk) — speech; काय (kāya) — body; कर्मभिः (karmabhiḥ) — by actions; हितम् (hitam) — beneficial; स्वाम्यनुकूलत्वम् (svāmyanukūlatvam) — accord with the master; नारीणाम् (nārīṇām) — for women; तु (tu) — indeed; विशेषतः (viśeṣataḥ) — especially

BP 1.14.28

सापि ज्येष्ठापतिं चैव गृहतन्त्रं च सर्वदा ।
समावर्ज्य गुणैर्धीरा प्रागवस्थां न विस्मरेत् ॥

She too, steadfast, should always win over the elder wife, her husband, and the household order through her virtues, and should not forget her former position.

सा (sā) — she; अपि (api) — also; ज्येष्ठा (jyeṣṭhā) — the elder wife; पतिम् (patim) — the husband; च (ca) — and; एव (eva) — indeed; गृहतन्त्रम् (gṛhatantram) — household order; च (ca) — and; सर्वदा (sarvadā) — always; समावर्ज्य (samāvarjya) — having won over; गुणैः (guṇaiḥ) — through virtues; धीरा (dhīrā) — steadfast; प्रागवस्थाम् (prāgavasthām) — her former position; न (na) — not; विस्मरेत् (vismaret) — she should forget

BP 1.14.29

न सौभाग्यमदं कुर्यान्न चौद्धत्यादिविक्रियाम् ।
नितरामानतिं गच्छेत्सदानार्यभयादिव ॥

She should not display pride in good fortune or arrogance and the like; she should especially remain humble, as if always fearing ignobility.

न (na) — not; सौभाग्यमदम् (saubhāgyamadam) — pride in good fortune; कुर्यात् (kuryāt) — she should display; न (na) — not; च (ca) — and; औद्धत्यादि (auddhatyādi) — arrogance and the like; विक्रियाम् (vikriyām) — behavior; नितराम् (nitarām) — especially; आनतिम् (ānatim) — humility; गच्छेत् (gacchet) — she should adopt; सदा (sadā) — always; अनार्यभयात् (anāryabhayāt) — from fear of ignobility; इव (iva) — as if

BP 1.14.30

यथा योग्यतया पत्यौ सौभाग्यमभिवर्धते ।
स्पर्धयेच्य कुलस्त्रीणां प्रश्रयोपाधिकं तथा ॥

As good fortune with the husband increases through suitability, so the rivalry of women of good families is qualified by deference.

यथा (yathā) — as; योग्यतया (yogyatayā) — through suitability; पत्यौ (patyau) — toward the husband; सौभाग्यम् (saubhāgyam) — good fortune; अभिवर्धते (abhivardhate) — increases; स्पर्धयेच्य (spardhayecya) — rivalry; कुलस्त्रीणाम् (kulastrīṇām) — of women of good families; प्रश्रय (praśraya) — deference; उपाधिकम् (upādhikam) — qualified by; तथा (tathā) — so

BP 1.14.31

एवमाराध्य भर्तारं तत्कार्येष्वप्रमादिनी ।
पूज्यानां पूजने नित्यं भृत्यानां भरणेषु च ॥

Having thus pleased her husband and remained attentive to his affairs, she should always honor those worthy of honor and maintain the servants—

एवम् (evam) — thus; आराध्य (ārādhya) — having pleased; भर्तारम् (bhartāram) — the husband; तत्कार्येषु (tatkāryeṣu) — in his affairs; अप्रमादिनी (apramādinī) — attentive; पूज्यानाम् (pūjyānām) — of those worthy of honor; पूजने (pūjane) — in honoring; नित्यम् (nityam) — always; भृत्यानाम् (bhṛtyānām) — of servants; भरणेषु (bharaṇeṣu) — in maintaining; च (ca) — and

BP 1.14.32

गुणानामर्जने नित्यं शीलवत्परिरक्षणे ।
प्रेत्य चेह च निर्द्वन्द्वं सुखमाप्नोत्यनुत्तमम् ॥

—always acquiring virtues and carefully preserving good conduct, she attains unsurpassed happiness, free from conflict, both here and after death.

गुणानाम् (guṇānām) — of virtues; अर्जने (arjane) — in acquiring; नित्यम् (nityam) — always; शीलवत् (śīlavat) — good conduct; परिरक्षणे (parirakṣaṇe) — in preserving; प्रेत्य (pretya) — after death; च (ca) — and; इह (iha) — here; च (ca) — and; निर्द्वन्द्वम् (nirdvandvam) — free from conflict; सुखम् (sukham) — happiness; आप्नोति (āpnoti) — she attains; अनुत्तमम् (anuttamam) — unsurpassed
