VP 5.27.5

पातितं तत्र चैवैको मत्स्यो जग्राह बालकम् ।
न ममार च तस्यापि जठराग्नि-प्रदीपितः ॥५॥

There a fish swallowed the child that had been cast in; yet the child did not die, though the fish’s digestive fire was burning.

Śrīdhara translation

“Cast down” means: he did not die because he was born from the Lord’s power and was a portion of Kāma; Kāma is not destroyed by water and the like.

VP 5.27.32

चिरं नष्टेन पुत्रेण सङ्गतां प्रेक्ष्य रुक्मिणीम् ।
अवाप विस्मयं सर्वो द्वारवत्यां जनस् तदा ॥३२॥

Seeing Rukmiṇī reunited with her son, who had been lost for a long time, everyone in Dvāravatī was filled with wonder.
