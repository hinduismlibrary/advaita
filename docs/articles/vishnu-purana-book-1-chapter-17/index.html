VP 1.17.1

मैत्रेय श्रूयतां सम्यक् चरितं तस्य धीमतः । प्रह्लादस्य सदोदारचरितस्य महात्मनः ॥१॥

Listen, Maitreya, to the story of the wise and magnanimous Prahlāda, whose adventures are ever interesting and instructive.

Śrīdhara translation

Since hatred of Bhagavān is itself the cause of hatred even toward his own son, who is a devotee, he introduces Hiraṇyakaśipu’s story with the words “Diti’s son,” and so on.

तस्मिन् प्रसन्ने किम् इहास्त्य् अलभ्यं धर्मार्थकामैर् अलम् अल्पकास् ते । समाश्रिताद् ब्रह्मतरोर् अनन्तान् निःसंशयं प्राप्स्यथ वै महत् फलम् ॥९१॥

“What here is difficult of attainment, when he is pleased? Wealth, pleasure, virtue, are things of little moment. Precious is the fruit that you shall gather, be assured, from the exhaustless store of the tree of true wisdom.”
