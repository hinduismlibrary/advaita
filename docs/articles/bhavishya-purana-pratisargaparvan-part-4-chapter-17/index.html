Part IV, Chapter 17

The Maruts, Kabīra, and the planetary gods

BP 3.4.17.1

बृहस्पतिरुवाच । दितिपुत्रौ महाघोरौ विष्णुना प्रभविष्णुना । संहतौ तु दितिर्ज्ञात्वा कश्यपं समपूजयत् ॥ १ ॥

Bṛhaspati said: Diti, learning that her two very terrible sons had been slain by powerful Viṣṇu, worshipped Kaśyapa.

Transliteration (IAST)

bṛhaspatiruvāca | ditiputrau mahāghorau viṣṇunā prabhaviṣṇunā | saṃhatau tu ditirjñātvā kaśyapaṃ samapūjayat || 1 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa · literal gloss

BP 3.4.17.2

द्वादशाब्दांतरे स्वामी कश्यपो भगवानृषिः । उवाच पत्नीं स हि तां वरं ब्रूहि वरानने । सा तु श्रुत्वा नमस्कृत्य वचनं प्राह हर्षिता ॥ २ ॥

After twelve years, the lordly sage Kaśyapa said to his wife, “Ask for a boon, fair-faced one.” Hearing him, she bowed and gladly spoke.

Transliteration (IAST)

dvādaśābdāṃtare svāmī kaśyapo bhagavānṛṣiḥ | uvāca patnīṃ sa hi tāṃ varaṃ brūhi varānane | sā tu śrutvā namaskṛtya vacanaṃ prāha harṣitā || 2 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa · literal gloss

BP 3.4.17.4

“Those two terrible sons of mine were destroyed by Viṣṇu, protector of the gods; therefore I am deeply grieved.”

Transliteration (IAST)

BP 3.4.17.5

देहि मे तनयं स्वामिन्द्वादशादित्यनाशनम् । इति श्रुत्वा वचो घोरं दितिं प्राह सुदुःखितः ॥ ५ ॥

“Give me, lord, a son who will destroy the twelve Ādityas.” Hearing these terrible words, Kaśyapa spoke to Diti with sorrow.

Transliteration (IAST)

BP 3.4.17.6

ब्रह्मणा निर्मितौ लोके धर्माधर्मौ परापरौ । धर्मपक्षास्तु ये लोके नरास्ते ब्रह्मणः प्रियाः ॥ ६ ॥

“In the world, Brahmā created dharma and adharma, the higher and the lower. Those people who stand with dharma are dear to Brahmā.”

Transliteration (IAST)

BP 3.4.17.7

अधर्मपक्षास्तु नरा वैरिणस्तस्य धीमतः । अधर्मपक्षौ तनयौ तस्मान्मृत्युमुपागतौ ॥ ७ ॥

“Those who stand with adharma are enemies of that wise one. Your two sons stood with adharma; therefore they met death.”

Transliteration (IAST)

BP 3.4.17.8

अतो धर्मप्रिये शुद्धं कुरु तस्मान्महाबलः । भविष्यति सुतो धीमांश्चिरजीवी तव प्रियः ॥ ८ ॥

“Therefore, lover of dharma, purify yourself. A mighty, wise, long-lived son, dear to you, will be born.”

Transliteration (IAST)

BP 3.4.17.9

इति श्रुत्वा दितिर्देवी कश्यपाद्गर्भमुत्तमम् । संप्राप्य सा शुभाचारा बभूव व्रतधारिणी ॥ ९ ॥

Hearing this, goddess Diti received an excellent embryo from Kaśyapa; she of good conduct became an observer of vows.

Transliteration (IAST)

BP 3.4.17.10

तस्या गर्भगते पुत्रे महेन्द्रश्च भयान्वितः । दासभूतः स्थितो गेहे स दितेराज्ञया गुरोः ॥ १० ॥

सप्तमासि स्थिते गर्भे शक्रमायाविमोहिता । अशुचिश्च दितिर्देवी सुष्वाप निजमंदिरे ॥ ११ ॥

BP 3.4.17.12

अंगुष्ठमात्रो भगवान्महेन्द्रो वज्रसंयुतः । कुक्षिमध्ये समागम्य चक्रे गर्भं स सप्तधा ॥ १२ ॥

जीवभूतानतिबलान्दृष्ट्वा सप्त महारिपून् । एकैकः सप्तधा तेन महेन्द्रेण तदा कृतः ॥ १३ ॥

नम्रीभूतश्च तान्दृष्ट्वा महेन्द्रस्तैः समन्वितः । योनि द्वारेण चागम्य प्रणनाम तदा दितिम् ॥ १४ ॥

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa · literal gloss
grahabhūtaḥ sthitastatra nabhodevyāṃ tadudbhavaḥ | rāhurnāma tathā ghoro mahāgraha upagrahaḥ — There, born from the sky-goddess, stood the planetary being named Rāhu: a terrible great planet, a secondary planet.

Primary Sanskrit source: Bhaviṣya Purāṇa, Sanskrit edition, Pratisargaparvan 3.4.17. The verse text and transliteration follow that edition; the English translation and literal gloss are translated from the Sanskrit.
