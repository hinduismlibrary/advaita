VP 5.3.2

ततोऽखिल-जगत्-पद्मः बोधायाच्युत-भानुना ।
देवकी-पूर्व-सन्ध्यायाम् आविर्भूतं महात्मना ॥२॥

Then the great-souled Acyuta, the sun, appeared in Devakī, the dawn, to awaken the lotus of the whole world.

Śrīdhara translation

‘Thus’ refers to this: the whole world itself is the lotus; for its awakening, the sun in the form of Acyuta appeared in Devakī, who herself was the prior dawn.

VP 5.3.3

तज्-जन्म-दिनम् अत्यर्थम् आह्लाद्य् अमल-दिङ्-मुखम् ।

That day of his birth was exceedingly delightful, its horizons stainless; for the whole world it became like moonlight from the moon.

Śrīdhara translation

‘On that birth’ etc.: there was delight. ‘Stainless’ means that the faces of the directions were stainless. ‘Kaumudī’ means moonlight. There is also the reading, ‘the day of that birth.’
