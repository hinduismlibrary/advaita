BP 3.1.3.1

शौनक उवाच ।
संवर्णश्च महीपालः कस्मिन्काले समागतः ।
लोमहर्षण मे ब्रूहि द्वापरस्य नृपांस्तथा ॥ १ ॥

Śaunaka said: O Lomaharṣaṇa, tell me when King Saṃvaraṇa arrived, and tell me about the kings of the Dvāpara age.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
शौनक (śaunaka) — Śaunaka, the questioner;
उवाच (uvāca) — said;
संवर्णश्च (saṃvarṇaśca) — Saṃvaraṇa and;
महीपालः (mahīpālaḥ) — king; lord of the earth;
कस्मिन्काले (kasminkāle) — at what time;
समागतः (samāgataḥ) — arrived;
लोमहर्षण (lomaharṣaṇa) — proper name in the royal genealogy;
मे (me) — to me;
ब्रूहि (brūhi) — tell; speak;
द्वापरस्य (dvāparasya) — of Dvāpara;
नृपांस्तथा (nṛpāṃstathā) — the kings likewise;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

śaunaka uvāca | saṃvarṇaśca mahīpālaḥ kasminkāle samāgataḥ | lomaharṣaṇa me brūhi dvāparasya nṛpāṃstathā || 1 ||
