Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 46 — The Sixth Day of Bhādrapada, Sacred to Skanda

BP 1.46.1

सुमन्तुरुवाच
येयं भाद्रपदे मासि षष्ठी च भरतर्षभ ।
सुपुण्येयं पापहरा शिवा शान्ता गुहप्रिया ॥

Sumantu said, This sixth day in Bhādrapada, O best of Bharatas, is very meritorious, remover of sins, auspicious, peaceful, and dear to Guha.

सुमन्तुरुवाच (sumanturuvāca) — Sumantu said;
येयं भाद्रपदे मासि षष्ठी च भरतर्षभ (yeyaṃ bhādrapade māsi ṣaṣṭhī ca bharatarṣabha) — This is the sixth day of the month of Bhādrapada, O best of the Bharatas;
सुपुण्येयं पापहरा शिवा शान्ता गुहप्रिया (supuṇyeyaṃ pāpaharā śivā śāntā guhapriyā) — This is very pious, destroyer of sins, auspicious, peaceful and dear to Guha (Skanda)

BP 1.46.2

स्नानदानादिकं सर्वं यस्यामक्षय्यमुच्यते ।
येऽस्यां पश्यन्ति गाङ्गेयं दक्षिणापथमाश्रितम् ॥

All rites beginning with bathing and giving are said to be inexhaustible on it. Those who see Gāṅgeya dwelling in the southern region

स्नानदानादिकं सर्वं यस्यामक्षय्यमुच्यते (snānadānādikaṃ sarvaṃ yasyāmakṣayyamucyate) — All that is said to be inexhaustible, such as bathing and giving;
येऽस्यां पश्यन्ति गाङ्गेयं दक्षिणापथमाश्रितम् (ye'syāṃ paśyanti gāṅgeyaṃ dakṣiṇāpathamāśritam) — Those who see this Gāṅgeya (Kārttikeya) taking refuge in the southern path

BP 1.46.3

ब्रह्महत्यादिभिः पापैर्मुच्यन्ते नात्र संशयः ।
तस्मादस्यां सदा पश्येत्कार्त्तिकेयं नृपोत्तम ॥

are freed from sins such as killing a Brahmin, without doubt. Therefore one should always see Kārttikeya on this day, O best of kings.

ब्रह्महत्यादिभिः पापैर्मुच्यन्ते नात्र संशयः (brahmahatyādibhiḥ pāpairmucyante nātra saṃśayaḥ) — They are undoubtedly freed from sins like brahma-hatya;
तस्मादस्यां सदा पश्येत्कार्त्तिकेयं नृपोत्तम (tasmādasyāṃ sadā paśyetkārttikeyaṃ nṛpottama) — Therefore, O best of kings, one should always see this Kārttikeya

BP 1.46.4

पूजयन्ति गुहं येऽस्यां नरा भक्तिसमन्विताः ।
प्राप्येह ते सुखान्कामान्गच्छन्तीन्द्रसलोकताम् ॥

The men who worship Guha with devotion on this day attain desired joys here and go to the world of Indra.

पूजयन्ति गुहं येऽस्यां नरा भक्तिसमन्विताः (pūjayanti guhaṃ ye'syāṃ narā bhaktisamanvitāḥ) — Those men who worship Guha’s shrine with devotion;
प्राप्येह ते सुखान्कामान्गच्छन्तीन्द्रसलोकताम् (prāpyeha te sukhānkāmāngacchantīndrasalokatām) — Having attained happiness and desires here they attain the world of Indra

BP 1.46.5

यस्तु कारयते वेश्म सुदृढं सुप्रतिष्ठितम् ।
दार्वं शैलमयं चापि भक्तया श्रद्धासमन्वितः ॥
गाङ्गेयं यानमारुह्य गच्छेद्गाङ्गेयसद्म वै ॥

Whoever has a firm, well established temple of wood or stone built with devotion and faith will mount the vehicle of Skanda and go to the abode of Skanda.

यस्तु कारयते वेश्म सुदृढं सुप्रतिष्ठितम् (yastu kārayate veśma sudṛḍhaṃ supratiṣṭhitam) — But he who builds a house strong and well established;
दार्वं शैलमयं चापि भक्तया श्रद्धासमन्वितः (dārvaṃ śailamayaṃ cāpi bhaktayā śraddhāsamanvitaḥ) — Even wood and rock with devotion and faith;
गाङ्गेयं यानमारुह्य गच्छेद्गाङ्गेयसद्म वै (gāṅgeyaṃ yānamāruhya gacchedgāṅgeyasadma vai) — Ascend Gāṅgeya (Kārttikeya) chariot and go to Gāṅgeya (Kārttikeya) lodge

BP 1.46.6

सम्मार्जनादि यः कर्म कुर्याद्गुहगृहे नरः ।
ध्वजस्यारोपणं राजन्स गच्छेद्रुद्रसद्म वै ॥

Whoever performs works such as sweeping in the temple of Guha and raises a banner, O king, goes to the abode of Rudra.

सम्मार्जनादि यः कर्म कुर्याद्गुहगृहे नरः (sammārjanādi yaḥ karma kuryādguhagṛhe naraḥ) — A man who performs sweeping and other duties in Guha’s shrine;
ध्वजस्यारोपणं राजन्स गच्छेद्रुद्रसद्म वै (dhvajasyāropaṇaṃ rājansa gacchedrudrasadma vai) — O king the flag should be hoisted at the abode of Rudra

BP 1.46.7

चन्दनागरुकर्पूरैर्यश्च पूजयते गुहम् ।
गजाश्वरथयानाढ्यं सैनापत्यमवाप्नुते ॥

Whoever worships Guha with sandalwood, agarwood, and camphor obtains military command with a host of elephants, horses, and chariots.

चन्दनागरुकर्पूरैर्यश्च पूजयते गुहम् (candanāgarukarpūrairyaśca pūjayate guham) — And he who worships Guha (Skanda) with sandalwood, agaru and camphor;
गजाश्वरथयानाढ्यं सैनापत्यमवाप्नुते (gajāśvarathayānāḍhyaṃ saināpatyamavāpnute) — He acquires an army rich in elephants horses chariots

BP 1.46.8

राज्ञां पूज्यः सदा प्रोक्तः कार्तिकेयो महीपते ।
कार्त्तिकेयमृते नान्यं राज्ञां पूज्यं प्रचक्षते ॥

Kārttikeya is always declared worthy of worship by kings, O lord of the earth. They proclaim no other worthy of worship by kings apart from Kārttikeya.

राज्ञां पूज्यः सदा प्रोक्तः कार्तिकेयो महीपते (rājñāṃ pūjyaḥ sadā proktaḥ kārtikeyo mahīpate) — O king Kārttikeya is always said to be worshiped by kings;
कार्त्तिकेयमृते नान्यं राज्ञां पूज्यं प्रचक्षते (kārttikeyamṛte nānyaṃ rājñāṃ pūjyaṃ pracakṣate) — No other is said to be worshiped by kings except Kārttikeya

BP 1.46.9

सङ्ग्रामं गच्छमानो यः पूजयेत्कृत्तिकासुतम् ।
स शत्रुं जयते वीर यथेन्द्रो दानवान्रणे ॥

Whoever worships the son of Kṛttikā when going to battle conquers the enemy, O hero, as Indra conquers the demons in war.

सङ्ग्रामं गच्छमानो यः पूजयेत्कृत्तिकासुतम् (saṅgrāmaṃ gacchamāno yaḥ pūjayetkṛttikāsutam) — He who worships the son of Krittika while going to battle;
स शत्रुं जयते वीर यथेन्द्रो दानवान्रणे (sa śatruṃ jayate vīra yathendro dānavānraṇe) — O valiant one he conquers the enemy just as Indra conquers the demons in battle

BP 1.46.10

तस्मात्सर्वप्रयत्नेन पूजयेच्छङ्कारात्मजम् ।
पूजमानस्तु तं भक्त्या चम्पकैर्विविधैर्नृप ॥
मुच्यते सर्वपापेभ्यस्तदा गच्छेच्छिवालयम् ॥

Therefore one should worship the son of Śaṅkara with every effort. O king, whoever worships him with devotion using various champaka flowers is freed from all sins and then goes to the abode of Śiva.

तस्मात्सर्वप्रयत्नेन पूजयेच्छङ्कारात्मजम् (tasmātsarvaprayatnena pūjayecchaṅkārātmajam) — Therefore one should worship the son of Sankara with all effort;
पूजमानस्तु तं भक्त्या चम्पकैर्विविधैर्नृप (pūjamānastu taṃ bhaktyā campakairvividhairnṛpa) — O King worshiping him with devotion with various champak flowers;
मुच्यते सर्वपापेभ्यस्तदा गच्छेच्छिवालयम् (mucyate sarvapāpebhyastadā gacchecchivālayam) — He is freed from all sins and then he goes to the temple of Shiva

BP 1.46.11

तैलं षष्ठ्यां न भुञ्जीत न दिवा कुरुनन्दन ।
यस्तु षष्ठ्यां नरो नक्तं कुर्याद्धि भरतर्षभ ॥
सर्वपापैः स निर्मुक्तो गाङ्गेयस्य सदो व्रजेत् ॥

One should not consume oil on the sixth day, nor eat during daytime, O delight of the Kurus. Whoever fasts by night on the sixth day, O best of Bharatas, is freed from all sins and goes to the abode of Skanda.

तैलं षष्ठ्यां न भुञ्जीत न दिवा कुरुनन्दन (tailaṃ ṣaṣṭhyāṃ na bhuñjīta na divā kurunandana) — O son of the Kurus one should not eat oil on the sixth day nor during the day;
यस्तु षष्ठ्यां नरो नक्तं कुर्याद्धि भरतर्षभ (yastu ṣaṣṭhyāṃ naro naktaṃ kuryāddhi bharatarṣabha) — But whoever, O best of the Bharatas, performs the night on the sixth day;
सर्वपापैः स निर्मुक्तो गाङ्गेयस्य सदो व्रजेत् (sarvapāpaiḥ sa nirmukto gāṅgeyasya sado vrajet) — He who is freed from all sins should always go to Gāṅgeya (Kārttikeya)

BP 1.46.12

त्रिकृत्वो दक्षिणामाशां गत्वा यः श्रद्धयान्वितः ।
पूजयेद्देवदेवेशं स गच्छेच्छान्तिमन्दिरम् ॥

One who, endowed with faith, goes three times toward the southern direction and worships the Lord of the gods goes to the abode of peace.

त्रिकृत्वो दक्षिणामाशां गत्वा यः श्रद्धयान्वितः (trikṛtvo dakṣiṇāmāśāṃ gatvā yaḥ śraddhayānvitaḥ) — He who goes to the southern hope three times with faith;
पूजयेद्देवदेवेशं स गच्छेच्छान्तिमन्दिरम् (pūjayeddevadeveśaṃ sa gacchecchāntimandiram) — He who worships Lord of the gods and goddesses should go to the temple of peace
