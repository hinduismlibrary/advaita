ŚB 2.10.36

स वाच्यवाचकतया भगवान् ब्रह्मरूपधृक् ।
नामरूपक्रिया धत्ते सकर्माकर्मकः परः ॥ ३६ ॥

The Supreme Lord, assuming the form of Brahmā, establishes names, forms and activities through the relation of word and meaning. Thus He appears engaged in cosmic action, although in His own transcendental nature He is untouched by material action.

Word-for-word

saḥ — He; vācya-vācakatayā — through the relation of the denoted and the denoting word; bhagavān — the Supreme Lord; brahma-rūpa-dhṛk — assuming Brahmā’s form; nāma-rūpa-kriyāḥ — names, forms and activities; dhatte — establishes; sa-karma — appearing engaged in action; akarmakaḥ — intrinsically beyond material action; paraḥ — the Supreme.

Transliteration
sa vācya-vācakatayā
bhagavān brahma-rūpa-dhṛk
nāma-rūpa-kriyā dhatte
sa-karmākarma-kaḥ paraḥ
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

यद् रूप-गुण-कर्मकः इति यद् उक्तं भगवता ब्रह्माणं प्रति तत् प्रपञ्चयितुं ब्रह्मादि-रूपाणि तत्-कर्माणि चाह—स इत्य्-आदिना । स भगवान् ब्रह्म-रूप-धृक् सन् वाच्य-वाचकतया नामानि वाच्यतया रूपाणि क्रियाश् च धत्ते, सृजतीत्य् अर्थः । मायया स-कर्मा स-व्यापारः सन् वस्तुतस् त्व् अकर्मकः ॥३६॥

yad rūpa-guṇa-karmakaḥ iti yad uktaṁ bhagavatā brahmāṇaṁ prati tat prapañcayituṁ brahmādi-rūpāṇi tat-karmāṇi cāha—sa ity-ādinā | sa bhagavān brahma-rūpa-dhṛk san vācya-vācakatayā nāmāni vācyatayā rūpāṇi kriyāś ca dhatte, sṛjatīty arthaḥ | māyayā sa-karmā sa-vyāpāraḥ san vastutas tv akarmakaḥ ||36||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. brahma-rūpa-dhṛk — taking Brahmā’s form; vācya-vācakatayā — through designation and what is designated; nāma-rūpa-kriyāḥ — names, forms and acts; māyayā sa-karmā — active through māyā; vastutaḥ akarmakaḥ — in reality untouched by material action.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. Cosmic naming, form and action are attributed to the Lord through His assumption of Brahmā’s creative function. His apparent activity belongs to the realm of māyā; it does not compromise His transcendence.

ŚB 2.10.37

प्रजापतीन्मनून् देवानृषीन् पितृगणान् पृथक् ।
सिद्धचारणगन्धर्वान् विद्याध्रासुरगुह्यकान् ॥ ३७ ॥

He manifests, each in their particular divisions, the Prajāpatis, Manus, devas, sages and hosts of ancestors, as well as Siddhas, Cāraṇas, Gandharvas, Vidyādharas, Asuras and Guhyakas.

Word-for-word

prajāpatīn — progenitors; manūn — Manus; devān — devas; ṛṣīn — sages; pitṛ-gaṇān — hosts of ancestors; pṛthak — distinctly; siddha — Siddhas; cāraṇa — Cāraṇas; gandharvān — Gandharvas; vidyādhara — Vidyādharas; asura — Asuras; guhyakān — Guhyakas.

Transliteration
prajā-patīn manūn devān
ṛṣīn pitṛ-gaṇān pṛthak
siddha-cāraṇa-gandharvān
vidyādhrāsura-guhyakān
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

एतत् प्रपञ्चयति—प्रजापतीन् इति सार्धैस् त्रिभिः । द्वितीयान्तानां धत्त इत्य् अनेनान्वयः । तत्र प्रजा-पत्य्-आदीनि नामानि तत्-तद्-वाच्यानि रूपाणि तत्-तत्-कर्माणि च ज्ञेयानि पृथक् तत्-तद्-अवान्तर-भेदेन ॥३७-३८॥

etat prapañcayati—prajāpatīn iti sārdhais tribhiḥ | dvitīyāntānāṁ dhatte ity anenānvayaḥ | tatra prajā-paty-ādīni nāmāni tat-tad-vācyāni rūpāṇi tat-tat-karmāṇi ca jñeyāni pṛthak tat-tad-avāntara-bhedena ||37-38||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. etat prapañcayati — he expands the preceding statement; dhatte iti anvayaḥ — all these accusatives connect with “He establishes”; nāmāni — names; rūpāṇi — corresponding forms; karmāṇi — corresponding functions; pṛthak — in their particular subdivisions.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. Śrīdhara reads verses 37–38 as one grammatical continuation of verse 36: the Lord establishes the names, forms and distinctive functions of all these classes of beings.

ŚB 2.10.38

किन्नराप्सरसो नागान् सर्पान् किम्पुरुषान्नरान् ।
मातृरक्षःपिशाचांश्च प्रेतभूतविनायकान् ॥ ३८ ॥

He likewise establishes Kinnaras, Apsarās, Nāgas, serpents, Kimpuruṣas and human beings, together with the Mothers, Rākṣasas, Piśācas, Pretas, Bhūtas and Vināyakas.

Word-for-word

kinnara — Kinnaras; apsarasaḥ — Apsarās; nāgān — Nāgas; sarpān — serpents; kimpuruṣān — Kimpuruṣas; narān — humans; mātṝḥ — the Mothers; rakṣaḥ — Rākṣasas; piśācān — Piśācas; pretān — Pretas; bhūtān — Bhūtas; vināyakān — Vināyakas.

Transliteration
kinnarāpsaraso nāgān
sarpān kimpuruṣān narān
mātṝ rakṣaḥ-piśācāṁś ca
preta-bhūta-vināyakān
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

एतत् प्रपञ्चयति—प्रजापतीन् इति सार्धैस् त्रिभिः । द्वितीयान्तानां धत्त इत्य् अनेनान्वयः । तत्र प्रजा-पत्य्-आदीनि नामानि तत्-तद्-वाच्यानि रूपाणि तत्-तत्-कर्माणि च ज्ञेयानि पृथक् तत्-तद्-अवान्तर-भेदेन ॥३७-३८॥

etat prapañcayati—prajāpatīn iti sārdhais tribhiḥ | dvitīyāntānāṁ dhatte ity anenānvayaḥ | tatra prajā-paty-ādīni nāmāni tat-tad-vācyāni rūpāṇi tat-tat-karmāṇi ca jñeyāni pṛthak tat-tad-avāntara-bhedena ||37-38||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. Śrīdhara treats 37–38 jointly: every listed class receives its own nāma — designation, rūpa — form, and karma — characteristic activity, with further internal distinctions.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. This continues the same list and the same construction as verse 37. The displayed Bhagavatam reading follows the Vedabase verse numbering/text while Śrīdhara’s joint gloss is preserved.
