ŚB 2.9.23

प्रत्यादिष्टं मया तत्र त्वयि कर्मविमोहिते ।
तपो मे हृदयं साक्षादात्माहं तपसोऽनघ ॥ २३ ॥

O sinless Brahmā, when you were bewildered about the work before you, it was I who gave that instruction. Tapas is directly My inner potency—indeed I am the very self and foundation of such tapas.

Word-for-word

pratyādiṣṭam — instructed, commanded; mayā — by Me; tatra — there, at that time; tvayi — when you; karma-vimohite — were bewildered about your work; tapaḥ — tapas; me hṛdayam — My heart, inner potency; sākṣāt — directly; ātmā aham — I am its self; tapasaḥ — of tapas; anagha — O sinless one.

Transliteration
pratyādiṣṭaṁ mayā tatra
tvayi karma-vimohite
tapo me hṛdayaṁ sākṣād
ātmāhaṁ tapaso ’nagha
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तद् अपि त्वं प्रति मयैवादिष्टम् उपदिष्टम् । कदा ? तत्र तदा सृष्ट्य्-आरम्भे त्वयि कर्मणि कार्येऽर्थे विमोहिते सति । किं च, तपो नाम ममैव शक्तिर् इत्य् आह—तपो मे हृदयम् अन्तरङ्गा शक्तिः, आत्मा स्वरूपम् । यस्य ज्ञानमयं तपः इति श्रुतेः ॥२२॥

tad api tvaṁ prati mayaivādiṣṭam upadiṣṭam | kadā? tatra tadā sṛṣṭy-ārambhe tvayi karmaṇi kārye ’rthe vimohite sati | kiṁ ca, tapo nāma mamaiva śaktir ity āha—tapo me hṛdayam antaraṅgā śaktiḥ, ātmā svarūpam | yasya jñānamayaṁ tapaḥ iti śruteḥ ||22||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. mayā eva ādiṣṭam — instructed by Me alone; sṛṣṭi-ārambhe — at the beginning of creation; vimohite — when bewildered; tapo me hṛdayam — tapas is My heart; antaraṅgā śaktiḥ — the internal potency; ātmā svarūpam — the very essential nature.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. The command to perform tapas was itself the Lord’s gift. Śrīdhara interprets the Lord’s “heart” as His internal potency and connects true tapas with spiritual knowledge rather than mere bodily hardship.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. Brahmā asks to understand both the subtle/transcendent and gross/cosmic manifestations of the Lord without confusing either with an ordinary material embodiment.

ŚB 2.9.27

यथात्ममायायोगेन नानाशक्त्युपबृंहितम् ।
विलुम्पन् विसृजन् गृह्णन् बिभ्रदात्मानमात्मना ॥ २७ ॥

Please let me understand how, through the operation of Your own Māyā and manifold potencies, You withdraw, project, assume and sustain the universe, while manifesting Yourself by Yourself.

vilumpan visṛjan gṛhṇan
bibhrad ātmānam ātmanā
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

यथा च त्वं क्रीडसि, तथा तद्-विषयां मनीषां मयि धेहि इत्य् उत्तरेणान्वयः। नाना-शक्त्य्-उपबृंहितं विश्वं विलुम्पन् संहरन्, विविधं सृजन्, बिभ्रत् पालयन्, आत्मना स्वयम् एव आत्मानं ब्रह्मादि-रूपं गृह्णन् क्रीडसि । ऊर्णुते तन्तुभिर् आत्मानम् आच्छादयति ॥२६-२७॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. Śrīdhara treats 2.9.27–28 as a connected pair. The Lord’s play includes creation, maintenance and withdrawal through many powers, while He Himself remains the source of the functional forms by which these activities occur.

astu te mad-anugrahāt
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

ननु त्वद्-दर्शनेऽप्य् असमर्थोऽहं कथं ज्ञानाधिकारी स्याम् ? तत्राह—यावान् इति । यावान् स्वरूपतः । यथाभावो यादृक्-सत्तावान् । यानि रूपाणि गुणाः कर्माणि च यस्य ॥३१॥

nanu tvad-darśane ’py asamartho ’haṁ kathaṁ jñānādhikārī syām? tatrāha—yāvān iti | yāvān svarūpataḥ | yathābhāvo yādṛk-sattāvān | yāni rūpāṇi guṇāḥ karmāṇi ca yasya ||31||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. katham jñāna-adhikārī syām — how could I become qualified for this knowledge?; yāvān svarūpataḥ — as I truly am in My own nature; yathābhāvaḥ — of what mode of existence; rūpāṇi guṇāḥ karmāṇi — forms, qualities and activities.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. The qualification Brahmā lacks by his own capacity is supplied by grace. What is revealed is not an abstraction but the Lord in His own reality—with form, qualities and activity fully included.
