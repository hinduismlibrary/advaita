BP 3.2.6.1

सूत उवाच ।
पुनराह स वैतालः शृणु राजन्कथामिमाम् ।
ग्रामे धर्मपुरं रम्ये नानाजननिषेविते ॥ 1 ॥

Sūta said: “The Vetāla spoke again: ‘Listen, O king, to this tale. In the lovely village of Dharmapura, served by many people…’”

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
सूत (sūta) — Sūta;
उवाच (uvāca) — said;
पुनराह (punarāha) — spoke again;
स (sa) — he;
वैतालः (vaitālaḥ) — the Vetāla;
शृणु (śṛṇu) — hear;
राजन्कथामिमाम् (rājankathāmimām) — O king, this tale;
ग्रामे (grāme) — in the village;
धर्मपुरं (dharmapuraṃ) — Dharmapura;
रम्ये (ramye) — lovely;
नानाजननिषेविते (nānājananiṣevite) — served by many people;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

sūta uvāca | punarāha sa vaitālaḥ śṛṇu rājankathāmimām | grāme dharmapuraṃ ramye nānājananiṣevite || 1 ||

BP 3.2.6.2

स्तुतिं चकार नम्रात्मा येन दुर्गा प्रसीदति ॥ 5 ॥

BP 3.2.6.6
