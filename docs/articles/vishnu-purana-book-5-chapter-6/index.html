VP 5.6.2

तस्य पाद-प्रहारेण शकटं परिवर्तितम् ।

By the blow of his feet the cart was overturned; its pots and vessels shattered, and it fell upside down.

Śrīdhara translation

“Broken pot-and-vessel” means broken pots and other vessels. With the reading “metal vessel,” the meaning is a vessel made of material other than gold or silver.

VP 5.6.8

गर्गश् च गोकुले तत्र वसुदेव-प्रचोदितः ।
प्रच्छन्न एव गोपानां संस्कारान् अकरोत् तयोः ॥८॥

And Garga, impelled by Vasudeva, came there to Gokula and secretly performed the rites for the two, concealed from the cowherds.

Śrīdhara translation

Garga, priest of the Yadus, came to Gokula. When Nanda asked him to name the two boys, he said: “I am well known as the teacher of the Yadu lineage, and you are Vasudeva’s dear friend. From the words of the bodiless voice and the girl, Kaṃsa thinks that Devakī’s son, his enemy, has been born somewhere. If Kaṃsa were to learn that I, the priest of the Yadus, performed the naming rites and other ceremonies, and became suspicious that Devakī’s son had somehow reached Gokula and was cherished by Nanda, a great calamity would follow. Nanda then said: ‘Perform only the rites proper for a twice-born, secretly, without my cowherds knowing.’ This is the meaning of ‘he secretly performed the rites for the two.’ The later word ‘greatly wise’ refers to Garga.”
