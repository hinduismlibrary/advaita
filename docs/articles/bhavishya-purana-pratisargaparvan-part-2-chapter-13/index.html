BP 3.2.13.1

सूत उवाच ।
कृतकृत्यः प्रसन्नात्मा वैतालो नृपमब्रवीत् ।
नगरे चन्द्रहृदये रणधीरो नृपोऽभवत् ॥ 1 ॥

Sūta said: Having fulfilled his task and become composed, the Vetāla spoke to the king: “In the city of Candrahṛdaya there ruled the king Raṇadhīra.”

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
सूत (sūta) — Sūta (the narrator);
उवाच (uvāca) — said;
कृतकृत्यः (kṛtakṛtyaḥ) — having fulfilled his task;
प्रसन्नात्मा (prasannātmā) — with a serene heart;
वैतालो (vaitālo) — Vetāla;
नृपमब्रवीत् (nṛpamabravīt) — spoke to the king;
नगरे (nagare) — in the city;
चन्द्रहृदये (candrahṛdaye) — in Candrahṛdaya;
रणधीरो (raṇadhīro) — Raṇadhīra;
नृपोऽभवत् (nṛpo'bhavat) — there was a king;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

sūta uvāca | kṛtakṛtyaḥ prasannātmā vaitālo nṛpamabravīt | nagare candrahṛdaye raṇadhīro nṛpo'bhavat || 1 ||

BP 3.2.13.2

तत्र वैश्योऽवसद्धर्मी नाम्ना धर्मध्वजो धनी ।
तस्य पुत्री समायाता सुन्दरी सुखभाविनी ॥ 2 ॥

A wealthy, righteous Vaiśya named Dharmadhvaja lived there. His daughter was beautiful and joyful.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
तत्र (tatra) — there;
वैश्योऽवसद्धर्मी (vaiśyo'vasaddharmī) — a virtuous Vaiśya lived;
नाम्ना (nāmnā) — by name;
धर्मध्वजो (dharmadhvajo) — Dharmadhvaja;
धनी (dhanī) — wealthy;
तस्य (tasya) — of him;
पुत्री (putrī) — daughter;
समायाता (samāyātā) — came;
सुन्दरी (sundarī) — beautiful;
सुखभाविनी (sukhabhāvinī) — Sukhabhāvinī (Dharmadhvaja’s daughter);
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

tatra vaiśyo'vasaddharmī nāmnā dharmadhvajo dhanī | tasya putrī samāyātā sundarī sukhabhāvinī || 2 ||

BP 3.2.13.3

एकदा नगरे तस्मिन्यातुभक्तो नरोऽभवत् ।
द्यूतविद्यापरो नित्यं मद्यमांसपरायणः ॥ 3 ॥

Once, a man devoted to sorcery lived in that city. He was constantly given to gambling, liquor, and meat.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
एकदा (ekadā) — once;
नगरे (nagare) — in the city;
तस्मिन्यातुभक्तो (tasminyātubhakto) — a sorcery-devotee in that city;
नरोऽभवत् (naro'bhavat) — there was a man;
द्यूतविद्यापरो (dyūtavidyāparo) — devoted to gambling;
नित्यं (nityaṃ) — always;
मद्यमांसपरायणः (madyamāṃsaparāyaṇaḥ) — devoted to liquor and meat;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

ekadā nagare tasminyātubhakto naro'bhavat | dyūtavidyāparo nityaṃ madyamāṃsaparāyaṇaḥ || 3 ||

BP 3.2.13.4

वाशरो नाम तत्रासीद्राक्षसः पुरुषादनः ।
तस्मै मद्यं च मांसं च प्रत्यहं स च दत्तवान् ॥ 4 ॥

A man-eating demon named Vāśara lived there. The sorcery-devotee gave him liquor and meat every day.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
वाशरो (vāśaro) — Vāśara;
नाम (nāma) — named; by name;

पुरुषादनः (puruṣādanaḥ) — man-eater;
तस्मै (tasmai) — for him;
मद्यं (madyaṃ) — liquor;
च (ca) — and;
मांसं (māṃsaṃ) — meat;
च (ca) — and;
प्रत्यहं (pratyahaṃ) — each day;

च (ca) — and;

Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

vāśaro nāma tatrāsīdrākṣasaḥ puruṣādanaḥ | tasmai madyaṃ ca māṃsaṃ ca pratyahaṃ sa ca dattavān || 4 ||

BP 3.2.13.5

प्रसन्नो राक्षसो भूत्वा यातुभक्तं तमब्रवीत् ।
वरं वरय यो योग्यो मत्तः सर्वमवाप्स्यसि ॥ 5 ॥

Pleased, the demon said to him: “Ask for any boon you wish; you will obtain everything from me.”

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
प्रसन्नो (prasanno) — pleased;
राक्षसो (rākṣaso) — a demon;
भूत्वा (bhūtvā) — having become;
यातुभक्तं (yātubhaktaṃ) — the sorcery-devotee;
तमब्रवीत् (tamabravīt) — said to him;
वरं (varaṃ) — boon;
वरय (varaya) — choose;
यो (yo) — whoever;
योग्यो (yogyo) — suitable;

सर्वमवाप्स्यसि (sarvamavāpsyasi) — you will obtain everything;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

prasanno rākṣaso bhūtvā yātubhaktaṃ tamabravīt | varaṃ varaya yo yogyo mattaḥ sarvamavāpsyasi || 5 ||

BP 3.2.13.6

स होवाच वरो मह्यं देयस्ते पुरुषादक ।
गुप्तगर्तं च भूमध्ये कुरु चौरनिरूपणम् ॥ 6 ॥

He said: “Man-eater, grant me this boon: make a hidden pit beneath the earth, a trap that confounds thieves.”

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
स (sa) — he;
होवाच (hovāca) — he said;
वरो (varo) — a bridegroom;
मह्यं (mahyaṃ) — for me;
देयस्ते (deyaste) — must be given by you;
पुरुषादक (puruṣādaka) — O man-eater;
गुप्तगर्तं (guptagartaṃ) — a hidden pit;
च (ca) — and;
भूमध्ये (bhūmadhye) — beneath the ground;
कुरु (kuru) — do; make;
चौरनिरूपणम् (cauranirūpaṇam) — a trap for thieves;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

sa hovāca varo mahyaṃ deyaste puruṣādaka | guptagartaṃ ca bhūmadhye kuru cauranirūpaṇam || 6 ||

BP 3.2.13.7

इति श्रुत्वाकरोद्गर्तं नर बुद्धिविमोहनम् ।
स्वयं तत्र स्थितो देवः स्वभक्तेन समन्वितः ॥ 7 ॥

Hearing this, the demon made a pit that bewildered human reason. He stayed there himself with his devotee.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
इति (iti) — thus;
श्रुत्वाकरोद्गर्तं (śrutvākarodgartaṃ) — hearing this, he made a pit;
नर (nara) — the man;
बुद्धिविमोहनम् (buddhivimohanam) — bewildering human reason;
स्वयं (svayaṃ) — himself;
तत्र (tatra) — there;
स्थितो (sthito) — having stayed;
देवः (devaḥ) — god;
स्वभक्तेन (svabhaktena) — with his devotee;
समन्वितः (samanvitaḥ) — accompanied;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

iti śrutvākarodgartaṃ nara buddhivimohanam | svayaṃ tatra sthito devaḥ svabhaktena samanvitaḥ || 7 ||

BP 3.2.13.8

तेन रात्रौ तु चौर्येण नृपदासी वरांगना ।
हृता संस्थापिता गर्ते बहुद्रव्यं तथा हृतम् ॥ 8 ॥

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
तेन (tena) — by him;
रात्रौ (rātrau) — at night;

संस्थापिता (saṃsthāpitā) — was placed;
गर्ते (garte) — in the pit;
बहुद्रव्यं (bahudravyaṃ) — much wealth;
तथा (tathā) — so;
हृतम् (hṛtam) — was taken;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

tena rātrau tu cauryeṇa nṛpadāsī varāṃganā | hṛtā saṃsthāpitā garte bahudravyaṃ tathā hṛtam || 8 ||

BP 3.2.13.9

सप्तपत्न्योऽभवंस्तस्य चतुर्वर्णस्य योषितः ।
तासां मध्ये भूपदासी तस्य चौरस्य वल्लभा ॥ 9 ॥

The thief had seven wives, women of the four social classes. Among them, the king’s maidservant was his beloved.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
सप्तपत्न्योऽभवंस्तस्य (saptapatnyo'bhavaṃstasya) — he had seven wives;

भूपदासी (bhūpadāsī) — the king's maidservant;
तस्य (tasya) — of him;
चौरस्य (caurasya) — of the thief;

Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

saptapatnyo'bhavaṃstasya caturvarṇasya yoṣitaḥ | tāsāṃ madhye bhūpadāsī tasya caurasya vallabhā || 9 ||

BP 3.2.13.10

नृपदुर्गसमं गेहं रचितं तेन रक्षसा ।
भूतले गुप्तरूपं च नरबुद्धिविमोहनम् ॥ 10 ॥

The demon made a hidden dwelling on the ground, like the king’s fortress, bewildering people who tried to understand it.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
नृपदुर्गसमं (nṛpadurgasamaṃ) — like the king's fortress;
गेहं (gehaṃ) — house;
रचितं (racitaṃ) — built;
तेन (tena) — by him;
रक्षसा (rakṣasā) — by the demon;
भूतले (bhūtale) — on the ground;
गुप्तरूपं (guptarūpaṃ) — concealed in form;
च (ca) — and;
नरबुद्धिविमोहनम् (narabuddhivimohanam) — confusing human understanding;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

nṛpadurgasamaṃ gehaṃ racitaṃ tena rakṣasā | bhūtale guptarūpaṃ ca narabuddhivimohanam || 10 ||

BP 3.2.13.11

चोरितं बहुधा द्रव्यं गर्ते संस्थापितं बलात् ।
तदा ते व्याकुला राजञ्जना राजानमब्रु वन् ॥ 11 ॥

Much stolen wealth was forcibly stored in the pit. Then, O king, the people became distressed and told the king.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
चोरितं (coritaṃ) — stolen;
बहुधा (bahudhā) — greatly; in many ways;
द्रव्यं (dravyaṃ) — wealth; goods;
गर्ते (garte) — in the pit;
संस्थापितं (saṃsthāpitaṃ) — stored;

तदा (tadā) — then;
ते (te) — you; for you;
व्याकुला (vyākulā) — distressed;
राजञ्जना (rājañjanā) — the people, O king;
राजानमब्रु (rājānamabru) — told the king;
वन् (van) — (split ending of the word);
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

coritaṃ bahudhā dravyaṃ garte saṃsthāpitaṃ balāt | tadā te vyākulā rājañjanā rājānamabru van || 11 ||

BP 3.2.13.12

त्यजाम नगरीं भूप चौरविघ्नकरीं तव ।
इति श्रुत्वाथ भूपेन रक्षिणः शस्त्रसंयुताः ।
स्थापिता नगरे तस्मिश्चौरहिंसापराय णाः ॥ 12 ॥

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
त्यजाम (tyajāma) — we shall abandon;
नगरीं (nagarīṃ) — the city;
भूप (bhūpa) — king;
चौरविघ्नकरीं (cauravighnakarīṃ) — made troublesome by thieves;

श्रुत्वाथ (śrutvātha) — then, hearing this;
भूपेन (bhūpena) — by the king;
रक्षिणः (rakṣiṇaḥ) — guards;
शस्त्रसंयुताः (śastrasaṃyutāḥ) — armed;
स्थापिता (sthāpitā) — were stationed;
नगरे (nagare) — in the city;
तस्मिश्चौरहिंसापराय (tasmiścaurahiṃsāparāya) — devoted to violence against the thief;
णाः (ṇāḥ) — (ending of the compound);
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

tyajāma nagarīṃ bhūpa cauravighnakarīṃ tava | iti śrutvātha bhūpena rakṣiṇaḥ śastrasaṃyutāḥ | sthāpitā nagare tasmiścaurahiṃsāparāya ṇāḥ || 12 ||

BP 3.2.13.15

पुनस्ते प्रययुर्भूपं रणधीरं समेरयन् ।
श्रुत्वा तु विस्मितो राजा स्वयं नगरमागतः ॥ 15 ॥

BP 3.2.13.16

अर्धरात्रे तमोभूते स चौरो नृपमागतम् ।
ज्ञात्वाब्रवीच्च राजानं को भवानत्र चाप्तवान् ॥ 16 ॥

When midnight had fallen and darkness filled the place, the thief realized that the king had arrived and asked him: “Who are you, and why have you come here?”

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

चौरो (cauro) — the thief;

BP 3.2.13.17

नृपोऽब्रवीदहं चौरश्चोदितार्थः समागतः ।
यास्यामि धनिनां गेहे भवान्मे वचनं कुरु ॥ 17 ॥

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

Transliteration
