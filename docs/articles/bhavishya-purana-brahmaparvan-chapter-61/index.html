Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 61 — The Discipline of Sun Worship

BP 1.61.1

सुमन्तुरुवाच ।
तमेकमक्षरं धाम परं सदसतोर् महत् ।
भेदाभेदस्वरूपस्थं प्रणिपत्य रविं नृप ॥ १ ॥

Sumantu said. Having bowed to the Sun, the one imperishable abode, supreme and great, beyond being and nonbeing, established in the nature of difference and non difference, O king,

sumantuḥ uvāca — Sumantu said; tam ekam akṣaram dhāma — that one imperishable abode; param — supreme; sat-asatoḥ — beyond being and non-being; mahat — great; bhedābheda-svarūpa-stham — abiding in the nature of difference and non-difference; praṇipatya — having bowed; ravim — to Ravi; nṛpa — O king

BP 1.61.2

प्रवक्ष्यामि यथापूर्वं विरिञ्चेन महात्मना ।
ऋषीणां कथितं पूर्वं तं निबोध नराधिप ॥ २ ॥

I shall explain as before what Brahmā formerly told the sages. Understand that, O king.

pravakṣyāmi — I shall explain; yathā-pūrvam — as before; viriñcena mahātmanā — by the great Viriñci; ṛṣīṇām — to the ṛṣis; kathitam pūrvam — spoken formerly; tam nibodha — understand that; narādhipa — O lord of men

BP 1.61.3

आराधनाय सवितुर्महात्मा पद्मसंभवः ।
योगं ब्रह्मपरं प्राह महर्षीणां यथा प्रभुः ॥ ३ ॥

For worship of the Sun, the great Lotus born taught the great sages the highest discipline directed toward Brahman, as the Lord had taught it.

ārādhanāya — for worship; savituḥ — of Savitṛ; mahātmā padmasaṃbhavaḥ — the great Padmasaṃbhava; yogam — yoga; brahma-param — directed to Brahman; prāha — spoke; maharṣīṇām — to the great sages; yathā — as; prabhuḥ — the Lord

BP 1.61.4

समस्तवृत्तिसंरोधात् कैवल्यप्रतिपादकम् ।
तदा जगत्पतिर्ब्रह्मा प्रणिपत्य महर्षिभिः ॥ ४ ॥

It teaches liberation by restraining all mental movements. Then Brahmā, Lord of the world, bowed together with the great sages.

samasta-vṛtti-saṃrodhāt — through restraint of all movements; kaivalya-pratipādakam — revealing liberation; tadā — then; jagat-patiḥ brahmā — Brahmā, lord of the world; praṇipatya — having bowed; maharṣibhiḥ — together with the great sages

BP 1.61.5

सर्वैः किलोक्तो भगवानात्मयोनिः प्रजाहितम् ।
योऽयं योगो भगवता प्रोक्तो वृत्तिनिरोधजः ॥ ५ ॥

All of them, seeking the welfare of beings, addressed the self born Blessed One. This discipline taught by the Blessed One arises from restraint of mental movement.

sarvaiḥ — by all; kila uktaḥ — indeed addressed; bhagavān ātma-yoniḥ — the Blessed self-born Lord; prajā-hitam — for the welfare of beings; yaḥ ayam yogaḥ — this yoga which; bhagavatā proktaḥ — was taught by the Blessed Lord; vṛtti-nirodha-jaḥ — arising from restraint of movements

BP 1.61.6

प्राप्तुं शक्यः स त्वनेकैर्जन्मभिर्जगतः पते ।
विषया दुर्जया नॄणामिन्द्रियाकर्षिणः प्रभो ॥ ६ ॥

That can be attained after many births, O lord of the world. Sense objects are difficult for people to conquer, for they draw away the senses, O Lord.

prāptum śakyaḥ — able to be attained; saḥ — that; tu — indeed; anekaiḥ janmabhiḥ — through many births; jagataḥ pate — O lord of the world; viṣayāḥ — sense-objects; durjayāḥ — difficult to conquer; nṝṇām — for human beings; indriya-ākarṣiṇaḥ — drawing away the senses; prabho — O Lord

BP 1.61.7

वृत्तयश्चेतसश्चापि चञ्चलस्यापि दुर्धराः ।
रागादयः कथं जेतुं शक्या वर्षशतैरपि ॥ ७ ॥

The movements of even a restless mind are hard to restrain. Passion and the rest are difficult to conquer even in a hundred years.

vṛttayaḥ — movements; cetasaḥ — of the mind; ca api — and also; cañcalasya — of the restless one; durdharāḥ — difficult to restrain; rāga-ādayaḥ — attachment and the rest; katham — how; jetum — to conquer; śakyāḥ — are possible; varṣa-śataiḥ api — even in hundreds of years

BP 1.61.8

न योगयोग्यं भवति मन एभिरनिर्जितैः ।
अल्पायुषश्च पुरुषा ब्रह्मन् कृतयुगेऽप्यमी ॥ ८ ॥

The mind is not fit for discipline while these remain unconquered. People are short lived even in the age of truth, O Brahman.

na — not; yoga-yogyam — fit for yoga; bhavati — becomes; manaḥ — the mind; ebhiḥ anirjitaiḥ — with these unconquered; alpa-āyuṣaḥ — short-lived; ca — and; puruṣāḥ — people; brahman — O Brahman; kṛta-yuge api — even in the Kṛta age; amī — these

BP 1.61.9

त्रेतायां द्वापरे चैव किमु प्राप्ते कलौ युगे ।
भगवंस्त्वामुपासीनान् प्रसन्नो वक्तुमर्हसि ॥ ९ ॥

How much more so in the Treta and Dvapara ages, and when the Kali age arrives. O Blessed One, you should speak kindly to us who sit before you.

tretāyām — in Tretā; dvāpare ca eva — and in Dvāpara; kim u — how much more; prāpte kalau yuge — when the Kali age has arrived; bhagavan — O Blessed One; tvām upāsīnān — us seated near you; prasannaḥ — gracious; vaktum arhasi — you ought to speak

BP 1.61.10

अनायासेन येनैव उत्तरेम भवर्णवम् ।
दुःखाम्बुमग्नाः पुरुषाः प्राप्य ब्रह्मन् महाप्लवम् ॥ १० ॥

O Brahman, people sunk in the waters of sorrow could cross the ocean of existence without difficulty by finding the great raft.

anāyāsena — without hardship; yena eva — by which indeed; uttarema — we may cross; bhava-arṇavam — the ocean of becoming; duḥkha-ambu-magnāḥ — sunk in the waters of sorrow; puruṣāḥ — people; prāpya — having obtained; brahman — O Brahman; mahā-plavam — the great raft

BP 1.61.11

उत्तरेम भवाम्भोधिं तथा त्वमनुचिन्तय ।
एवमुक्तस्तदा ब्रह्मा क्रियायोगं महात्मनाम् ॥ ११ ॥

Think on a way that we may cross the ocean of existence.

uttarema — we may cross; bhava-ambhodhim — the ocean of becoming; tathā — thus; tvam anucintaya — reflect; evam uktaḥ — thus addressed; tadā — then; brahmā — Brahmā; kriyā-yogam — Kriyāyoga; mahātmanām — of the great-souled

BP 1.61.12

तेषामृषीणामाचष्ट नराणां हितकाम्यया ।
आराधयत विश्वेशं दिवाकरमतन्द्रिताः ॥ १२ ॥

Thus addressed, Brahmā explained the ritual discipline of the great souls to those sages, seeking the welfare of people. Worship the Lord of all, the Sun, without weariness.

teṣām ṛṣīṇām — to those ṛṣis; ācaṣṭa — he explained; narāṇām hitakāmyayā — wishing the welfare of human beings; ārādhayata — worship; viśveśam — the Lord of the universe; divākaram — Divākara; atandritāḥ — without sloth

BP 1.61.13

बाह्यालम्बनसापेक्षास्तमजं जगतः पतिम् ।
इज्यापूजानमस्कारशुश्रूषाभिरहर्निशम् ॥ १३ ॥

Relying on external supports, worship that unborn Lord of the world day and night with sacrifice, worship, bows, and service.

bāhya-ālambana-sāpekṣāḥ — depending upon external supports; tam ajam — that unborn one; jagataḥ patim — Lord of the world; ijyā-pūjā-namaskāra-śuśrūṣābhiḥ — with sacrifice, worship, prostration, and service; ahar-niśam — day and night

BP 1.61.14

व्रतोपवासैर्विविधैर्ब्राह्मणानां च तर्पणम् ।
तैस्तैश्चाभिमतैः कामैर्ये च चेतसि तुष्टिदाः ॥ १४ ॥

Serve the Brahmins with various vows and fasts, and offer the desired things that gladden the mind.

vrata-upavāsaiḥ vividhaiḥ — with various vows and fasts; brāhmaṇānām tarpaṇam — satisfaction of the Brāhmaṇas; tais taiḥ — with those various; ca abhimataiḥ kāmaiḥ — desired objects; ye — which; cetasi tuṣṭidāḥ — give contentment to the mind

BP 1.61.15

अपरिच्छेद्यमाहात्म्यमाराधयत भास्करम् ।
तन्निष्ठास्तद्गतधियस्तत्कर्माणस्तदाश्रयाः ॥ १५ ॥

Worship the Sun whose greatness cannot be measured. Be steadfast in him, your minds fixed on him, your actions for him, and your refuge in him.

aparicchedya-māhātmyam — whose greatness is immeasurable; ārādhayata — worship; bhāskaram — Bhāskara; tat-niṣṭhāḥ — devoted to him; tat-gata-dhiyaḥ — minds directed to him; tat-karmāṇaḥ — actions belonging to him; tat-āśrayāḥ — taking refuge in him

BP 1.61.16

तद्दृष्टयस्तन्मनसः सर्वस्मिन् स इति स्थिताः ।
समस्तान्यथ कर्माणि तत्र सर्वात्मनात्मनि ॥ १६ ॥

Keep your eyes on him and your minds on him, established in the thought that he is in all things. Then place all your actions in him, the Self who is the Self of all.

tat-dṛṣṭayaḥ — seeing him; tat-manasaḥ — minds fixed on him; sarvasmin — in everything; saḥ iti — “he is,” thus; sthitāḥ — established; samastāni karmāṇi — all actions; atha — then; tatra — there; sarva-ātmanā — with the whole self; ātmani — to the Self

BP 1.61.17

संन्यस्यध्वं स वो कर्त्ता समस्तावरणक्षयम् ।
एतत्तदक्षरं ब्रह्म प्रधानपुरुषावुभौ ॥ १७ ॥

Surrender everything to him, your doer and the destroyer of every covering. This is the imperishable Brahman, both primordial matter and the Person.

saṃnyasyadhvam — renounce; saḥ — he; vaḥ — your; karttā — doer; samasta-āvaraṇa-kṣayam — destruction of every covering; etat tat akṣaram brahma — this is that imperishable Brahman; pradhāna-puruṣau ubhau — both Pradhāna and Puruṣa

BP 1.61.18

यतो यस्मिन् यथा चोभौ सर्वव्यापिन्यवस्थितौ ।
परः पराणां परमः स एकः सुमनसां परः ॥ १८ ॥

Both all pervading principles abide from him, in him, and according to his nature. He is higher than the highest, supreme, the One, and beyond the good minded.

yataḥ — from whom; yasmin — in whom; yathā — in the manner in which; ca ubhau — both; sarva-vyāpini — in the all-pervading one; avasthitau — are established; paraḥ — higher; parāṇām — than the high; paramaḥ — supreme; saḥ ekaḥ — he is one; sumanasām paraḥ — higher than the benevolent

BP 1.61.19

यस्माद्भिन्नमिदं सर्वं यच्चेदं यच्च नेङ्गति ।
मोक्षकारणमव्यक्तमचिन्त्यमपरिग्रहम् ।
समाराध्य जगन्नाथं क्रियायोगेन मुच्यते ॥ १९ ॥

From him all this differs, whatever moves and whatever does not move. The unmanifest, inconceivable, unattached one is the cause of liberation. By worshiping the Lord of the world through ritual discipline, one is freed.

yasmāt bhinnam — from whom it is distinct; idam sarvam — all this; yat ca idam — whatever is this; yat ca na iṅgati — and whatever does not move; mokṣa-kāraṇam — cause of liberation; avyaktam — unmanifest; acintyam — inconceivable; aparigraham — without attachment; samārādhya — having worshiped completely; jagannātham — Lord of the universe; kriyā-yogena — through Kriyāyoga; mucyate — one is freed

BP 1.61.20

इति ते ब्रह्मणः श्रुत्वा रहस्यमृषिसत्तमाः ॥ २० ॥

Having heard this secret from Brahmā, the foremost sages,

iti — thus; te — this; brahmaṇaḥ — from Brahmā; śrutvā — having heard; rahasyam — secret; ṛṣi-sattamāḥ — O best of sages

BP 1.61.21

नराणामुपकाराय योगशास्त्राणि चक्रिरे ।
क्रियायोगपराणीह मुक्तिकारीण्यनेकशः ॥ २१ ॥

composed teachings on discipline for the welfare of people, many of them devoted to ritual worship and granting liberation.

narāṇām upakārāya — for the benefit of human beings; yoga-śāstrāṇi — treatises on yoga; cakrire — they made; kriyā-yoga-parāṇi — devoted to Kriyāyoga; iha — here; mukti-kārīṇi — bestowing liberation; anekaśaḥ — many times/in many forms

BP 1.61.22

आराध्यते जगन्नाथस्तदनुष्ठानतत्परैः ।
परमात्मा स मार्तण्डः सर्वेशः सर्वभावनः ॥ २२ ॥

The Lord of the universe is worshiped by those devoted to that practice. He, the Sun, is the Supreme Self, Lord of all, and source of all beings.

ārādhyate — is worshiped; jagannāthaḥ — Lord of the universe; tat-anuṣṭhāna-tat-paraiḥ — by those devoted to its practice; paramātmā — Supreme Self; saḥ — he; mārtaṇḍaḥ — Mārtaṇḍa; sarveśaḥ — Lord of all; sarva-bhāvanaḥ — source and nourisher of all

BP 1.61.23

यान्युक्तानि पुरा तेन ब्रह्मणा कुरुनन्दन ।
तानि ते कुरुशार्दूल सर्वपापहराण्यहम् ॥ २३ ॥

Whatever Brahmā said long ago, I shall tell you, O joy of the Kurus, O tiger among the Kurus. These things remove all sins.

yāni uktāni — whatever was spoken; purā — formerly; tena brahmaṇā — by that Brahmā; kuru-nandana — O delight of the Kurus; tāni — those; te — to you; kuru-śārdūla — O tiger of the Kurus; sarva-pāpa-harāṇi — removing every sin; aham — I

BP 1.61.24

वक्ष्यामि श्रूयतामद्य रहस्यमिदमुत्तमम् ।
संसारार्णवमग्नानां विषयाक्रान्तचेतसाम् ॥ २४ ॥

I shall now speak, hear this highest secret, concerning those sunk in the ocean of worldly existence, with minds overcome by sense objects.

vakṣyāmi — I shall speak; śrūyatām — let it be heard; adya — today/now; rahasyam idam uttamam — this supreme secret; saṃsāra-arṇava-magnānām — of those sunk in the ocean of saṃsāra; viṣaya-ākrānta-cetasām — whose minds are overwhelmed by sense-objects

BP 1.61.25

हंसपोतं विना नान्यत्किञ्चिदस्ति परायणम् ।
उत्तिष्ठंश्चिन्तय रविं व्रजंश्चिन्तय गोपतिम् ॥ २५ ॥

There is no refuge other than the swan boat. As you rise, think of the Sun. As you go, think of the Lord of cows.

haṃsa-potam — the swan-boat; vinā — except; na anyat kiṃcit — nothing else; asti — exists; parāyaṇam — refuge; uttiṣṭhan — rising; cintaya — think upon; ravim — Ravi; vrajan — going; cintaya — think upon; go-patim — Lord of the cows

BP 1.61.26

भुञ्जंश्चिन्तय मार्तण्डं स्वपंश्चिन्तय भास्करम् ।
एवमेकाग्रचित्तस्त्वं संशृतः सततं रविम् ॥ २६ ॥

As you eat, think of the Sun, and as you sleep, think of the Sun. In this way, with a one pointed mind, always take refuge in the Sun.

bhuñjan — eating; cintaya — think upon; mārtaṇḍam — Mārtaṇḍa; svapan — sleeping; cintaya — think upon; bhāskaram — Bhāskara; evam — thus; ekāgra-cittaḥ — one-pointed in mind; tvam — you; saṃśritaḥ — taking refuge; satatam — constantly; ravim — in Ravi

BP 1.61.27

जन्ममृत्युमहाग्राहं संसाराम्भस्तरिष्यसि ॥ २७ ॥

You will cross the waters of worldly existence, whose great crocodile is birth and death.

janma-mṛtyu-mahā-grāham — whose great crocodile is birth and death; saṃsāra-ambhas — the waters of saṃsāra; tariṣyasi — you will cross

BP 1.61.28

ग्रहेशमीशं वरदं पुराणं जगद्विधातारमजं च नित्यम् ।
समाश्रिता ये रविमीशितारं तेषां भवो नास्ति विमुक्तिभाजाम् ॥ २८ ॥

Those who take refuge in the Sun, lord of planets, supreme Lord, giver of boons, ancient, creator of the world, unborn and eternal, have no further worldly existence, having attained liberation.

graha-īśam — Lord of the planets; īśam — supreme Lord; vara-dam — giver of boons; purāṇam — ancient; jagat-vidhātāram — creator of the world; ajam — unborn; nityam — eternal; samāśritāḥ — having taken refuge; ye — those who; ravim — in Ravi; īśitāram — the ruler; teṣām — for them; bhavaḥ nāsti — worldly becoming does not exist; vimukti-bhājām — for the participants in liberation
