BP 3.1.5.1

शौनक उवाच ।
सांप्रतं वर्तते यो वै प्रलयांते मुनीश्वर ।
दिव्यदृष्टिप्रभावेन ज्ञातं ब्रूहि ततः परम् ॥ १ ॥

Śaunaka said: O sage, what is happening now, after the dissolution? Through your divine sight, tell me what came next.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
शौनक (śaunaka) — Śaunaka, the questioner;
उवाच (uvāca) — said;
सांप्रतं (sāṃprataṃ) — at present;
वर्तते (vartate) — rules;
यो (yo) — who;
वै (vai) — indeed;
प्रलयांते (pralayāṃte) — at the end of dissolution;
मुनीश्वर (munīśvara) — O great sage;
दिव्यदृष्टिप्रभावेन (divyadṛṣṭiprabhāvena) — through the power of divine sight;
ज्ञातं (jñātaṃ) — known;
ब्रूहि (brūhi) — tell;
ततः (tataḥ) — then; after that;
परम् (param) — what follows;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

śaunaka uvāca | sāṃprataṃ vartate yo vai pralayāṃte munīśvara | divyadṛṣṭiprabhāvena jñātaṃ brūhi tataḥ param || 1 ||

BP 3.1.5.2

सूत उवाच ।
न्यूहो नाम स्मृतो म्लेच्छो विष्णुमोहं तदाकरोत् ।
तदा प्रसन्नो भगवांस्तस्य वंशः प्रवर्द्धितः ॥ २ ॥

Sūta said: Nyuha is remembered as a Mleccha; he brought about Viṣṇu’s delusion. Then the Blessed Lord was pleased and his lineage grew.

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
सूत (sūta) — Sūta, the narrator;
उवाच (uvāca) — said;
न्यूहो (nyūho) — Nyuha;
नाम (nāma) — names;
स्मृतो (smṛto) — is remembered;
म्लेच्छो (mleccho) — a Mleccha;
विष्णुमोहं (viṣṇumohaṃ) — Viṣṇu’s delusion;
तदाकरोत् (tadākarot) — then caused;
तदा (tadā) — then;
प्रसन्नो (prasanno) — pleased;
भगवांस्तस्य (bhagavāṃstasya) — the Blessed Lord; his;
वंशः (vaṃśaḥ) — lineage;
प्रवर्द्धितः (pravarddhitaḥ) — increased;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa

sūta uvāca | nyūho nāma smṛto mleccho viṣṇumohaṃ tadākarot | tadā prasanno bhagavāṃstasya vaṃśaḥ pravarddhitaḥ || 2 ||

BP 3.1.5.3

म्लेच्छभाषा कृता तेन वेदवाक्यपराङ्मुखा ।
कलेश्च वृद्धये ब्राह्मीं भाषां कृत्वाऽपशब्दगाम् ॥ ३ ॥

He made a Mleccha language, averse to the words of the Veda; to increase Kali, he made the Brahmī language a path of corrupt words.

कृता (kṛtā) — made;

वेदवाक्यपराङ्मुखा (vedavākyaparāṅmukhā) — turned away from Vedic speech;

वृद्धये (vṛddhaye) — for the increase;
ब्राह्मीं (brāhmīṃ) — Brahmī speech;
भाषां (bhāṣāṃ) — language;
कृत्वाऽपशब्दगाम् (kṛtvā'paśabdagām) — making it a path of corrupt words;
Transliteration
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa
