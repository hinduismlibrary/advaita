Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 113 — The Merit of Building a Sun Temple

BP 1.113.1

विष्णुरुवाच।
सुरज्येष्ठ पुनर्ब्रूहि यत्पृच्छाम्यहमादितः।
यत्फलं समवाप्नोति कारयित्वा रवेर्गृहम् ॥

Viṣṇu said. O eldest among the gods, tell me again what I ask at the outset. What fruit does a person obtain by having a temple built for the Sun.

विष्णुः(viṣṇur) — Viṣṇu said;
सुर-ज्येष्ठ(sura-jyeṣṭha) — O eldest among the gods;
पुनः ब्रूहि(punar brūhi) — tell again;
यत् पृच्छामि अहम् आदितः(yat pṛcchāmy aham āditaḥ) — what I ask at the outset;
यत् फलम् समवाप्नोति(yat phalaṃ samavāpnoti) — what fruit one obtains;
कारयित्वा(kārayitvā) — having caused to be made;
रवेः गृहम्(raver gṛham) — a house for Ravi;

BP 1.113.2

देवार्चां कारयित्वा तु यत्पुण्यं पुरुषोऽश्नुते।
पूजयित्वा च विधिवदनुलिप्य च यत्फलम् ॥

What merit does a person gain by having an image of the god made, worshipping it according to rule, and anointing it.

देव-अर्चाम्(devārcām) — the divine image;
कारयित्वा(kārayitvā) — having caused to be made;
तु(tu) — indeed;
यत् पुण्यम् पुरुषः अश्नुते(yat puṇyaṃ puruṣo'śnute) — what merit a person enjoys;
पूजयित्वा च(pūjayitvā ca) — having worshipped, and;
विधिवत्(vidhivat) — according to rule;
अनुलिप्य च(anulipya ca) — having plastered or anointed, and;
यत् फलम्(yat phalam) — what fruit;

BP 1.113.3

कानि माल्यानि शस्तानि कानि नार्हति भास्करः।
के धूपा भानुदयिताः के वर्ज्याश्च जगत्पतेः ॥

Which garlands are auspicious for the Sun, and which are not fit for him. Which incenses are dear to the Sun, and which are to be avoided for the Lord of the world.

कानि माल्यानि(kāni mālyāni) — which garlands;
शस्तानि(śastāni) — auspicious;
कानि न अर्हति(kāni nārhati) — which are not fit;
भास्करः(bhāskaraḥ) — Bhāskara;
के धूपाः(ke dhūpāḥ) — which incenses;
भानु-दयिताः(bhānu-dayitāḥ) — dear to Bhānu;
के वर्ज्याः च(ke varjyāś ca) — which are to be avoided, and;
जगत्-पतेः(jagat-pateḥ) — for the Lord of the world;

BP 1.113.4

उपचारफलं किं स्यात्किं फलं गीतवादिते।
घृतक्षीरादिना यत्तु स्नापिते भास्करे फलम् ॥

What is the fruit of the offerings, and what is the fruit of singing and instrumental music. What fruit comes when the Sun is bathed with ghee, milk, and the like.

उपचार-फलम्(upacāra-phalam) — the fruit of offerings;
किम् स्यात्(kiṃ syāt) — what may it be;
किम् फलम्(kiṃ phalam) — what fruit;
गीत-वादिते(gīta-vādite) — in singing and instrumental music;
घृत-क्षीर-आदिना(ghṛta-kṣīrādinā) — with ghee, milk, and the like;
यत् तु(yat tu) — that which indeed;
स्नापिते भास्करे(snāpite bhāskare) — when Bhāskara is bathed;
फलम्(phalam) — the fruit;

BP 1.113.5

यथोपलेपनादौ च फलमभ्युक्षितेन तु।
दिवाकरगृहे तात तदशेषं वदस्व मे ॥

What is the fruit of plastering, the other acts, and sprinkling in the temple of the Sun, dear one. Tell me all of it without remainder.

यथा उपलेपन-आदौ च(yathopalepanādau ca) — and concerning plastering and the like;
फलम्(phalam) — the fruit;
अभ्युक्षितेन तु(abhyukṣitena tu) — and of sprinkling;
दिवाकर-गृहे(divākara-gṛhe) — in the house of Divākara;
तात(tāta) — O dear one;
तत् अशेषम्(tad aśeṣam) — that entirely;
वदस्व मे(vadasva me) — tell me;

BP 1.113.6

ब्रह्मोवाच।
साधु वत्स यदेतत्त्वं मार्तण्डस्येह पृच्छसि।
शुश्रूषणे विधिं पुण्यं तदिहैकमनाः शृणु ॥

Brahmā said. Well asked, dear child. Since you ask here about the Sun, hear with one pointed attention the meritorious rule for his service.

ब्रह्मा उवाच(brahmovāca) — Brahmā said;
साधु(sādhu) — good;
वत्स(vatsa) — dear child;
यत् एतत् त्वम्(yad etat tvam) — since you this;
मार्तण्डस्य इह पृच्छसि(mārtaṇḍasya iha pṛcchasi) — ask here about Mārtaṇḍa;
शुश्रूषणे विधिम् पुण्यम्(śuśrūṣaṇe vidhiṃ puṇyam) — the meritorious rule in service;
तत् इह एक-मनाः शृणु(tad iha eka-manāḥ śṛṇu) — hear that here with one-pointed mind;

BP 1.113.7

यस्तु देवालयं भानोर्दार्वं शैलमथापि वा।
कारयेन्मृन्मयं चापि तस्य पुण्यफलं शृणु ॥

Hear the meritorious fruit of the person who causes a temple of the Sun to be made of wood, stone, or even clay.

यः तु(yaḥ tu) — who indeed;
देवालयम्(devālayam) — a temple;
भानोः(bhānoḥ) — of Bhānu;
दार्वम्(dārvaṃ) — wooden;
शैलम् अथ अपि वा(śailam athāpi vā) — or stone;
कारयेत्(kārayet) — causes to be made;
मृन्मयम् च अपि(mṛnmayaṃ cāpi) — also of clay;
तस्य पुण्य-फलम् शृणु(tasya puṇya-phalaṃ śṛṇu) — hear his meritorious fruit;

BP 1.113.8

अहन्यहनि यज्ञेन यजतो यन्महाफलम्।
प्राप्नोति तत्फलं भानोर्यः कारयति मन्दिरम् ॥

The person who causes a temple to be built for the Sun obtains the great fruit that one who worships day after day through sacrificial rites obtains.

अहनि अहनि(ahany ahani) — day after day;
यज्ञेन यजतः(yajñena yajataḥ) — of one who worships with sacrifice;
यत् महा-फलम्(yan mahā-phalam) — whatever great fruit;
प्राप्नोति(prāpnoti) — he obtains;
तत् फलम्(tat phalam) — that fruit;
भानोः(bhānoḥ) — of Bhānu;
यः कारयति(yaḥ kārayati) — who causes to be made;
मन्दिरम्(mandiram) — a temple;

BP 1.113.9

कुलानां शतमागामि समतीतं कुलं शतम्।
कारयेद्भगवद्धाम स नयेदर्कलोकताम् ॥

Having caused the divine abode to be made, he leads one hundred future families and one hundred families already gone to the world of the Sun.

कुलानाम् शतम् आगामि(kulānāṃ śatam āgāmi) — one hundred future families;
समतीतम् कुलम् शतम्(samatītaṃ kulaṃ śatam) — one hundred families already gone;
कारयेत् भगवत्-धाम(kārayet bhagavad-dhāma) — having caused the divine abode to be made;
सः नयेत्(sa nayet) — he leads;
अर्क-लोकताम्(arka-lokatām) — to the state of the Sun's world;

BP 1.113.10

सप्तजन्मकृतं पापं स्वल्पं वा यदि वा बहु।
भानोरालयविन्यासप्रारम्भादेव नश्यति ॥

Sin committed over seven births, whether little or much, perishes from the very beginning of laying out the Sun temple.

सप्त-जन्म-कृतम् पापम्(sapta-janma-kṛtaṃ pāpam) — sin committed in seven births;
स्वल्पम् वा यदि वा बहु(svalpaṃ vā yadi vā bahu) — little or much;
भानोः आलय-विन्यास-प्रारम्भात् एव(bhānor ālaya-vinyāsa-prārambhād eva) — from the very beginning of arranging Bhānu's temple;
नश्यति(naśyati) — perishes;

BP 1.113.11

सप्तलोकमयो भानुस्तस्य यः कुरुते गृहम्।
प्रतिष्ठां समवाप्नोति स नरः साप्तलौकिकीम् ॥

The Sun consists of the seven worlds. The person who makes his temple obtains the sevenfold station among the worlds.

सप्त-लोक-मयः(sapta-loka-mayaḥ) — consisting of the seven worlds;
भानुः(bhānuḥ) — Bhānu;
तस्य यः कुरुते गृहम्(tasya yaḥ kurute gṛham) — who makes his house;
प्रतिष्ठाम् समवाप्नोति(pratiṣṭhāṃ samavāpnoti) — obtains establishment;
सः नरः(sa naraḥ) — that man;
साप्त-लौकिकीम्(sāpta-laukikīm) — the seven-world status;

BP 1.113.12

प्रशस्तदेशभूमिभागे प्रशस्तं भवनं रवेः।
कारयेदक्षयांल्लोकान्स नरः प्रतिपद्यते ॥

A person who causes an excellent dwelling for the Sun to be made on a fine tract of land attains imperishable worlds.

प्रशस्त-देश-भूमि-भागे(praśasta-deśa-bhūmi-bhāge) — in an excellent region of land;
प्रशस्तम् भवनम्(praśastaṃ bhavanam) — an excellent dwelling;
रवेः(raveḥ) — of Ravi;
कारयेत्(kārayet) — causes to be made;
अक्षयान् लोकान्(akṣayāṃl lokān) — imperishable worlds;
सः नरः प्रतिपद्यते(sa naraḥ pratipadyate) — that man attains;

BP 1.113.13

इष्टकाचयविन्यासो यावद्वर्षाणि तिष्ठति।
तावद्वर्षसहस्राणि तत्कर्तुर्दिवि संस्थितिः ॥

For as many years as the arrangement of its bricks endures, for so many thousands of years its maker remains in heaven.

इष्टका-चय-विन्यासः(iṣṭakā-caya-vinyāsaḥ) — the arrangement of a pile of bricks;
यावत् वर्षाणि तिष्ठति(yāvad varṣāṇi tiṣṭhati) — for as many years as it endures;
तावत् वर्ष-सहस्राणि(tāvad varṣa-sahasrāṇi) — so many thousand years;
तत् कर्तुः(tat kartuḥ) — of its maker;
दिवि संस्थितिः(divi saṃsthitiḥ) — abiding in heaven;

BP 1.113.14

प्रतिमां लक्षणवतीं यः कारयति मानवः।
दिवाकरस्य तल्लोकमक्षयं प्रतिपद्यते ॥

The person who causes an image bearing the proper marks to be made attains the imperishable world of the Sun.

प्रतिमाम् लक्षण-वतीम्(pratimāṃ lakṣaṇa-vatīm) — an image possessing the proper marks;
यः कारयति(yaḥ kārayati) — who causes to be made;
मानवः(mānavaḥ) — a person;
दिवाकरस्य तत् लोकम्(divākarasya tal lokam) — that world of Divākara;
अक्षयम्(akṣayam) — imperishable;
प्रतिपद्यते(pratipadyate) — attains;

BP 1.113.15

षष्टिर्वर्षसहस्राणां सहस्राणि स मोदते।
लोके सुमनसां वीर प्रत्येकं मधुसूदन ॥

He rejoices for sixty million years in the world of the benevolent, O hero, O Kṛṣṇa.

षष्टिः वर्ष-सहस्राणाम्(ṣaṣṭir varṣa-sahasrāṇām) — sixty thousand years;
सहस्राणि(sahasrāṇi) — thousands;
सः मोदते(sa modate) — he rejoices;
लोके(loke) — in the world;
सुमनसाम्(sumanasām) — of the benevolent;
वीर(vīra) — O hero;
प्रत्येकम्(pratyekam) — each / fully;
मधुसूदन(madhusūdana) — O Madhusūdana;

BP 1.113.16

प्रतिष्ठाप्य रवेर्चां सुप्रशस्ते निवेशने।
पुरुषः कृतकृत्योऽस्ति न दोषफलमश्नुते ॥

Having installed an image of the Sun in an excellent dwelling, a person has fulfilled his duty and does not suffer the fruit of any fault.

प्रतिष्ठाप्य(pratiṣṭhāpya) — having installed;
रवेः अर्चाम्(raver arcām) — Ravi's image;
सुप्रशस्ते निवेशने(supraśaste niveśane) — in an excellent dwelling;
पुरुषः(puruṣaḥ) — a person;
कृत-कृत्यः अस्ति(kṛta-kṛtyo'sti) — has fulfilled his duty;
न दोष-फलम् अश्नुते(na doṣa-phalam aśnute) — does not suffer the fruit of fault;

BP 1.113.17

ये भविष्यन्ति येऽतीता आकल्पं पुरुषाः कुले।
तांस्तारयति संस्थाप्य देवस्य प्रतिमां रवेः ॥

Having installed the image of the Sun, he saves all the people who will be born and those who have passed away in his family, for the duration of the age.

ये भविष्यन्ति(ye bhaviṣyanti) — who will be;
ये अतीताः(ye'tītāḥ) — who have passed away;
आकल्पम्(ākalpam) — until the end of the age;
पुरुषाः कुले(puruṣāḥ kule) — the persons in the family;
तान् तारयति(tāṃs tārayati) — he saves them;
संस्थाप्य(saṃsthāpya) — having installed;
देवस्य प्रतिमाम् रवेः(devasya pratimāṃ raveḥ) — the image of the god Ravi;

BP 1.113.18

अनुशिष्टाः किल पुरा यमेन यमकिङ्कराः।
पाशदण्डकराः कृष्ण प्रजासंयमनोद्यताः ॥

The servants of Yama, bearing nooses and staffs and intent on restraining beings, had formerly been instructed by Yama, O Kṛṣṇa.

अनुशिष्टाः किल पुरा(anuśiṣṭāḥ kila purā) — had formerly been instructed;
यमेन(yamena) — by Yama;
यम-किङ्कराः(yama-kiṅkarāḥ) — the servants of Yama;
पाश-दण्ड-कराः(pāśa-daṇḍa-karāḥ) — holding nooses and staffs;
कृष्ण(kṛṣṇa) — O Kṛṣṇa;
प्रजा-संयमन-उद्यताः(prajā-saṃyamana-udyatāḥ) — intent on restraining beings;

BP 1.113.19

यम उवाच।
विहरन्तु यथान्यायं नियोगो मेऽनुपाल्यताम्।
नाज्ञाभङ्गं करिष्यन्ति भवतां जन्तवः क्वचित् ॥

Yama said. Let them move according to the proper rule, and let my command be carried out. Your beings shall never disobey the command.

यमः उवाच(yama uvāca) — Yama said;
विहरन्तु(viharantu) — let them move;
यथा-न्यायम्(yathā-nyāyam) — according to the proper rule;
नियोगः मे अनुपाल्यताम्(niyogo me'nupālyatām) — let my command be carried out;
न आज्ञा-भङ्गम् करिष्यन्ति(nājñā-bhaṅgaṃ kariṣyanti) — they shall not disobey the command;
भवताम् जन्तवः क्वचित्(bhavatāṃ jantavaḥ kvacit) — your beings ever;

BP 1.113.20

केवलं ये जगन्मूलं विवस्वन्तमुपाश्रिताः।
भवद्भिः परिहर्तव्यास्तेषां नैवेह संस्थितिः ॥

Only those who have taken refuge in the Sun, the root of the world, must be avoided by you. They have no place here.

केवलम्(kevalam) — only;
ये जगत्-मूलम्(ye jagan-mūlam) — who are the root of the world;
विवस्वन्तम् उपाश्रिताः(vivasvantam upāśritāḥ) — who have taken refuge in Vivasvant;
भवद्भिः परिहर्तव्याः(bhavadbhiḥ parihartavyāḥ) — must be avoided by you;
तेषाम् न एव इह संस्थितिः(teṣāṃ naiva iha saṃsthitiḥ) — there is no place here for them;

BP 1.113.21

ये तु वैवस्वता लोके तच्चित्तास्तत्परायणाः।
पूजयन्ति सदा भानुं ते च त्याज्याः सुदूरतः ॥

Those in the world who belong to the Sun, whose minds are fixed on him and who are devoted to him, always worship the Sun. They too are to be left far away.

ये तु वैवस्वताः लोके(ye tu vaivasvatā loke) — those in the world who belong to Vaivasvata;
तत्-चित्ताः(tat-cittāḥ) — whose minds are fixed on him;
तत्-परायणाः(tat-parāyaṇāḥ) — devoted to him;
पूजयन्ति सदा भानुम्(pūjayanti sadā bhānum) — always worship Bhānu;
ते च त्याज्याः(te ca tyājyāḥ) — they too are to be left;
सुदूरतः(sudūrataḥ) — far away;

BP 1.113.22

तिष्ठंश्च प्रस्वपन्गच्छन्नुत्तिष्ठन्स्खलिते क्षुते।
सङ्कीर्तयति देवं यः स नस्त्याज्यः सुदूरतः ॥

Whoever praises the god while standing, sleeping, walking, rising, stumbling, or sneezing is not to be abandoned and must be kept far away.

तिष्ठन् च(tiṣṭhaṃś ca) — standing, and;
प्रस्वपन् गच्छन्(prasvapan gacchan) — sleeping and walking;
उत्तिष्ठन्(uttiṣṭhan) — rising;
स्खलिते क्षुते(skhalite kṣute) — in stumbling and sneezing;
सङ्कीर्तयति देवम् यः(saṅkīrtayati devaṃ yaḥ) — who praises the god;
सः न स्त्याज्यः(sa nastyājyaḥ) — he is not to be abandoned;
सुदूरतः(sudūrataḥ) — far away;

BP 1.113.23

नित्यनैमित्तिकैर्देवं ये यजन्ति तु भास्करम्।
न चालोक्या भवद्भिस्ते यद्ध्यानं हन्ति वो गतिम् ॥

Those who worship the Sun through daily and occasional rites are not to be looked upon by you, for their meditation frustrates your course.

नित्य-नैमित्तिकैः(nitya-naimittikaiḥ) — with daily and occasional rites;
देवम्(devam) — the god;
ये यजन्ति तु भास्करम्(ye yajanti tu bhāskaram) — who worship Bhāskara;
न च आलोक्याः(na cālokyāḥ) — are not to be looked upon;
भवद्भिः ते(bhavadbhis te) — by you, those;
यत् ध्यानम्(yad dhyānam) — whose meditation;
हन्ति वः गतिम्(hanti vo gatim) — destroys your advance;

BP 1.113.24

ये पुष्पधूपवासोभिर्भूषणैश्चापि वल्लभैः।
अर्चयन्ति न ते ग्राह्या मत्पितुस्ते परिग्रहाः ॥

Those who worship with flowers, incense, garments, and beloved ornaments are not to be seized. They are under the protection of my father.

ये(ye) — those who;
पुष्प-धूप-वासोभिः(puṣpa-dhūpa-vāsobhiḥ) — with flowers, incense, and garments;
भूषणैः च अपि वल्लभैः(bhūṣaṇaiś cāpi vallabhaiḥ) — and also with beloved ornaments;
अर्चयन्ति(arcayanti) — worship;
न ते ग्राह्याः(na te grāhyāḥ) — they are not to be seized;
मत्-पितुः ते परिग्रहाः(mat-pitus te parigrahāḥ) — they are the dependents of my father;

BP 1.113.25

उपलेपनकर्तारः कर्तारो मार्जनस्य ये।
अर्कालये परित्याज्यं तेषां त्रिपुरुषं कुलम् ॥

Those who plaster and those who clean the temple of the Sun, their families for three generations are to be left alone.

उपलेपन-कर्तारः(upalepana-kartāraḥ) — those who perform plastering;
कर्तारः मार्जनस्य ये(kartāro mārjanasya ye) — those who perform cleaning;
अर्क-आलये(arkālaye) — in the temple of Arka;
परित्याज्यम्(parityājyam) — must be left alone;
तेषाम् त्रि-पुरुषम् कुलम्(teṣāṃ tri-puruṣaṃ kulam) — their three-generation family;

BP 1.113.26

ये वायतनं भानोः कारितं तत्कुलोद्भवः।
पुमान्न नावलोक्यो वै भवद्भिर्दुष्टचक्षुषा ॥

A man born in the family of one who caused a temple of the Sun to be made must not be looked upon by you with an evil eye.

ये वा आयतनम् भानोः(ye vāyatanam bhānoḥ) — or the temple of Bhānu;
कारितम्(kāritam) — caused to be made;
तत्-कुल-उद्भवः(tat-kulodbhavaḥ) — born from that family;
पुमान्(pumān) — a man;
न नावलोक्यः वै(na nāvalokyo vai) — must not be looked upon indeed;
भवद्भिः दुष्ट-चक्षुषा(bhavadbhir duṣṭa-cakṣuṣā) — by you with an evil eye;

BP 1.113.27

येनार्चा भगवद्भक्त्या मत्पितुः कारिता शुभा।
नराणां तत्कुलं वीराः सदा त्याज्यं सुदूरतः ॥

The family of a person who made the auspicious image of my father with devotion to the Lord is always to be left far away, O heroes.

येन अर्चा(yenārcā) — by whom the image;
भगवत्-भक्त्या(bhagavad-bhaktyā) — with devotion to the Lord;
मत्-पितुः(mat-pituḥ) — of my father;
कारिता शुभा(kāritā śubhā) — was caused to be made auspicious;
नराणाम् तत्-कुलम्(narāṇāṃ tat-kulam) — the family of that man;
वीराः(vīrāḥ) — O heroes;
सदा त्याज्यम्(sadā tyājyam) — is always to be left;
सुदूरतः(sudūrataḥ) — far away;

BP 1.113.28

भवतां भ्रमतां यत्र भानुसंश्रयमुद्रया।
न चाज्ञाभङ्गकृत्कश्चिद्भविष्यति नरः क्वचित् ॥

Wherever you wander marked by refuge in the Sun, no person there will violate your command.

भवताम् भ्रमताम्(bhavatāṃ bhramatām) — of you who wander;
यत्र(yatra) — wherever;
भानु-संश्रय-मुद्रया(bhānu-saṃśraya-mudrayā) — under the sign of refuge in Bhānu;
न च(na ca) — and not;
आज्ञा-भङ्ग-कृत् कश्चित्(ājñā-bhaṅga-kṛt kaścit) — any violator of the command;
भविष्यति नरः क्वचित्(bhaviṣyati naraḥ kvacit) — will ever be a person;

BP 1.113.29

इत्युक्ताः किङ्करास्तेन यमेन सुमहात्मना।
अनाश्रित्य वचः कृष्ण सत्राजितमथो गताः ॥

Thus addressed by the great souled Yama, the servants, not relying on his words, then went to Satrājit, O Kṛṣṇa.

इति उक्ताः(ity uktāḥ) — thus addressed;
किङ्कराः(kiṅkarāḥ) — the servants;
तेन यमेन(tena yamena) — by that Yama;
सुमहात्मना(sumahātmanā) — the great-souled;
अनाश्रित्य वचः(anāśritya vacaḥ) — not disregarding the words;
कृष्ण(kṛṣṇa) — O Kṛṣṇa;
सत्राजितम् अथो गताः(satrājitam atho gatāḥ) — then went to Satrājit;

BP 1.113.30

तस्य ते तेजसा सर्वे भानोर्भक्तस्य सुव्रत।
मोहिताः पतिता भूमौ यथा च विहगा नगात् ॥

All of them were overcome by the radiance of that devotee of the Sun, O steadfast one, and fell to the ground like birds from a mountain.

तस्य ते सर्वे(tasya te sarve) — all of them;
तेजसा(tejasā) — by the radiance;
भानोः भक्तस्य(bhānor bhaktasya) — of the devotee of Bhānu;
सुव्रत(suvrata) — O steadfast one;
मोहिताः(mohitāḥ) — overcome;
पतिताः भूमौ(patitā bhūmau) — fell to the ground;
यथा च विहगाः नगात्(yathā ca vihagā nagāt) — like birds from a mountain;

BP 1.113.31

एतां महाफलां चार्चां भानोः कारयते नरः।
तवाख्यानं महाबाहो गृहं कारयितुश्च यत् ॥

The man who causes this highly fruitful image of the Sun to be made, this is the account, O mighty armed one, of the fruit of building his temple.

एताम् महा-फलाम् च अर्चाम्(etāṃ mahā-phalāṃ cārcām) — this highly fruitful image;
भानोः(bhānoḥ) — of Bhānu;
कारयते नरः(kārayate naraḥ) — the man causes to be made;
तव आख्यानम्(tavākhyānam) — the account for you;
महाबाहो(mahābāho) — O mighty-armed;
गृहम् कारयितुः च यत्(gṛhaṃ kārayituś ca yat) — and whatever belongs to the maker of the house;

BP 1.113.32

यज्ञा नराणां पापौघनाशकाः सर्वकामदाः।
तथैवेष्टो जगद्भानुः सर्वयज्ञमयो रविः ॥

Sacrifices destroy the mass of human sins and grant every desire. Likewise the world bright Sun, who consists of all sacrifices, is worshipped.

यज्ञाः नराणाम्(yajñā narāṇām) — sacrifices of human beings;
पाप-ओघ-नाशकाः(pāpaugha-nāśakāḥ) — destroyers of the flood of sins;
सर्व-काम-दाः(sarva-kāmadāḥ) — granting every desire;
तथा एव इष्टः(tathaiveṣṭaḥ) — so likewise worshipped;
जगत्-भानुः(jagad-bhānuḥ) — the Sun of the world;
सर्व-यज्ञ-मयः(sarva-yajña-mayaḥ) — formed of every sacrifice;
रविः(raviḥ) — Ravi;
