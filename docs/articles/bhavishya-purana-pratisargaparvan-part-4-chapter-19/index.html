Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa is the blessed, complete, eternal Brahman. Therefore Kṛṣṇa alone is not the Lord; Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa is the supreme ruler.

Transliteration (IAST)

rādhākṛṣṇassa bhagavānpūrṇabrahma sanātanaḥ | ataḥ kṛṣṇo na bhagavānrādhākṛṣṇaḥ paraḥ prabhuḥ || 65 ||

Bhaviṣya Purāṇa · literal gloss

BP 3.4.19.66

इति श्रुत्वा वचस्तस्य मध्वाचार्यो हरिप्रियः । शिष्यो भूत्वा स्थितस्तत्र कृष्णचैतन्यपूजकः ॥ ६६ ॥

Hearing his words, Madhvācārya, dear to Hari, became his disciple, remained there, and worshipped Kṛṣṇa Caitanya.

Transliteration (IAST)

iti śrutvā vacastasya madhvācāryo haripriyaḥ | śiṣyo bhūtvā sthitastatra kṛṣṇacaitanyapūjakaḥ || 66 ||

Word-for-word
Bhaviṣya Purāṇa · literal gloss
iti śrutvā vacastasya madhvācāryo haripriyaḥ | śiṣyo bhūtvā sthitastatra kṛṣṇacaitanyapūjakaḥ — Hearing his words, Madhvācārya, dear to Hari, became his disciple, remained there, and worshipped Kṛṣṇa Caitanya.
