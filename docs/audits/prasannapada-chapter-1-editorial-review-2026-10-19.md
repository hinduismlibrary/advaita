# Prasannapadā, Chapter 1: editorial review

## Scope

This review covers Candrakīrti’s *Prasannapadā* Chapter 1 and Nāgārjuna’s fourteen root verses (MMK 1.1–1.14). The reader now presents the introduction, verses, and complete commentary in one continuous page. Verse links remain available as anchors; prior per-verse URLs redirect to those anchors.

## Textual basis

- GRETIL’s Sanskrit transcription of Candrakīrti’s *Prasannapadā*, identified by GRETIL as based on P. L. Vaidya’s 1960 edition.
- Anne MacDonald, *In Clear Words: The Prasannapadā, Chapter One* (2015), Vol. I (critical Sanskrit edition) and Vol. II (annotated translation). The publisher states that the publication underwent anonymous international peer review.
- Stephen Batchelor, *Verses from the Center* (1994), as the attributed base for the English root verses; selected wording revisions are disclosed on the source page.

The GRETIL stanza markers for the fourteen root verses run from 3 through 16; the reader uses conventional MMK Chapter 1 numbering, 1.1–1.14.

## Corrections recorded

- Rendered *kṣaṇabhaṅga* as “momentary destruction,” correcting “the passing of an instant.”
- Distinguished *svabhāva* (“intrinsic nature”) from *parabhāva* (“other-nature”) in MMK 1.3.
- Rendered *asvayaṃmaya* in MMK 1.13 as “not made of themselves,” preserving the verse’s self-made/condition-made argument.
- Clarified the subjects and parallel clauses in MMK 1.7, 1.8, 1.10, 1.12, and 1.14.
- Fixed a missing space before the quotation in MMK 1.10.
