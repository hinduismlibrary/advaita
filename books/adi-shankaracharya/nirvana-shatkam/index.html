Verse 1

1

मनोबुद्ध्यहंकारचित्तानि नाहं न कर्णे न जिह्वा न च घ्राणनेत्रे । न च व्योम भूमिर्न तेजो न वायुश्चिदानन्दरूपः शिवोऽहं शिवोऽहम् ॥

I am not mind, intellect, ego, or the mental field; nor ear, tongue, nose, or eyes; nor space, earth, fire, or air. I am Śiva, whose nature is consciousness and bliss; I am Śiva.

Transliteration

manobuddhyahaṃkāracittāni nāhaṃ na karṇe na jihvā na ca ghrāṇanetre | na ca vyoma bhūmirna tejo na vāyuścidānandarūpaḥ śivo’haṃ śivo’ham ||

Word-for-word
मनस् (manas)mind
बुद्धि (buddhi)intellect
अहंकार (ahaṃkāra)ego, I-making
चित्त (citta)mental field, memory
न अहम् (na aham)not I
कर्ण (karṇa)ear
जिह्वा (jihvā)tongue
घ्राण • नेत्र (ghrāṇa • netra)nose • eyes
व्योम • भूमि (vyoma • bhūmi)space • earth
तेजस् • वायु (tejas • vāyu)fire/light • air
चित् • आनन्द (cit • ānanda)consciousness • bliss
रूप (rūpa)nature, form
शिवः अहम् (śivaḥ aham)Śiva am I; I am the auspicious Self
च (ca)and; joins the following list of senses and elements

Verse 2

2

न च प्राणसंज्ञो न वै पञ्चवायुर्न वा सप्तधातुर्न वा पञ्चकोशः । न वाक्पाणिपादौ न चोपस्थपायू चिदानन्दरूपः शिवोऽहं शिवोऽहम् ॥

I am not what is called the vital force, nor the five winds, nor the seven bodily constituents, nor the five sheaths; nor speech, hands, feet, the generative organ, or the excretory organ. I am Śiva, whose nature is consciousness and bliss; I am Śiva.

Transliteration

na ca prāṇasaṃjño na vai pañcavāyurna vā saptadhāturna vā pañcakośaḥ | na vākpāṇipādau na copasthapāyū cidānandarūpaḥ śivo’haṃ śivo’ham ||

Word-for-word
न • च (na • ca)not • and
प्राण-संज्ञः (prāṇa-saṃjñaḥ)called vital breath
पञ्च-वायु (pañca-vāyu)five vital winds
सप्त-धातु (sapta-dhātu)seven bodily constituents
पञ्च-कोश (pañca-kośa)five sheaths
वाक् (vāk)speech
पाणि • पाद (pāṇi • pāda)hand • foot
उपस्थ • पायु (upastha • pāyu)generative organ • excretory organ
चित् • आनन्द-रूप (cit • ānanda-rūpa)of the nature of consciousness • bliss
शिवः अहम् (śivaḥ aham)Śiva am I; I am the auspicious Self
वै (vai)indeed
वा (vā)or; repeated with the following lists

Verse 3

3

न मे द्वेषरागौ न मे लोभमोहौ मदो नैव मे नैव मात्सर्यभावः । न धर्मो न चार्थो न कामो न मोक्षश्चिदानन्दरूपः शिवोऽहं शिवोऽहम् ॥

I have neither aversion nor attachment, neither greed nor delusion; I have no pride and no trace of jealousy. I am not duty, wealth, desire, or liberation. I am Śiva, whose nature is consciousness and bliss; I am Śiva.

Transliteration

na me dveṣarāgau na me lobhamohau mado naiva me naiva mātsaryabhāvaḥ | na dharmo na cārtho na kāmo na mokṣaścidānandarūpaḥ śivo’haṃ śivo’ham ||

Word-for-word
न • मे (na • me)not • for me
द्वेष • राग (dveṣa • rāga)aversion • attachment
लोभ • मोह (lobha • moha)greed • delusion
मद (mada)pride
न (na)not
एव (eva)indeed; emphatic particle
मात्सर्य-भाव (mātsarya-bhāva)state of jealousy
धर्म • अर्थ (dharma • artha)duty • wealth
काम • मोक्ष (kāma • mokṣa)desire • liberation
चित्-आनन्द-रूप (cit-ānanda-rūpa)of the nature of consciousness-bliss
शिवः अहम् (śivaḥ aham)Śiva am I; I am the auspicious Self

Verse 4

4

न पुण्यं न पापं न सौख्यं न दुःखं न मन्त्रो न तीर्थं न वेदा न यज्ञाः । अहं भोजनं नैव भोज्यं न भोक्ता चिदानन्दरूपः शिवोऽहं शिवोऽहम् ॥

I am neither merit nor sin, neither pleasure nor pain; neither mantra, pilgrimage place, Veda, nor sacrifice. I am not the act of eating, the food, or the eater. I am Śiva, whose nature is consciousness and bliss; I am Śiva.

Transliteration

na puṇyaṃ na pāpaṃ na saukhyaṃ na duḥkhaṃ na mantro na tīrthaṃ na vedā na yajñāḥ | ahaṃ bhojanaṃ naiva bhojyaṃ na bhoktā cidānandarūpaḥ śivo’haṃ śivo’ham ||

Word-for-word
पुण्य • पाप (puṇya • pāpa)merit • sin
सौख्य • दुःख (saukhya • duḥkha)ease, happiness • suffering
मन्त्र • तीर्थ (mantra • tīrtha)sacred formula • pilgrimage place
वेद • यज्ञ (veda • yajña)Veda • sacrifice
अहम् (aham)I
भोजन (bhojana)eating, meal
भोज्य (bhojya)what is eaten
भोक्तृ (bhoktṛ)eater, experiencer
चित्-आनन्द-रूप (cit-ānanda-rūpa)of the nature of consciousness-bliss
शिवः अहम् (śivaḥ aham)Śiva am I; I am the auspicious Self
न (na)not; repeated before each listed item
एव (eva)indeed; with न, forms नैव, “not indeed”

Verse 5

5

न मृत्युर्न शङ्का न मे जातिभेदः पिता नैव मे नैव माता च जन्म । न बन्धुर्न मित्रं गुरुर्नैव शिष्यश्चिदानन्दरूपः शिवोऽहं शिवोऽहम् ॥

I have no death or fear, and for me there is no distinction of birth or class. I have no father, no mother, and no birth; no relative, friend, teacher, or disciple. I am Śiva, whose nature is consciousness and bliss; I am Śiva.

Transliteration

na mṛtyurna śaṅkā na me jātibhedaḥ pitā naiva me naiva mātā ca janma | na bandhurna mitraṃ gururnaiva śiṣyaścidānandarūpaḥ śivo’haṃ śivo’ham ||

Word-for-word
मृत्यु • शङ्का (mṛtyu • śaṅkā)death • fear, doubt
न • मे (na • me)not • for me
जाति-भेद (jāti-bheda)distinction of birth or class
पितृ • मातृ (pitṛ • mātṛ)father • mother
जन्म (janma)birth
बन्धु • मित्र (bandhu • mitra)relative • friend
गुरु • शिष्य (guru • śiṣya)teacher • disciple
चित्-आनन्द-रूप (cit-ānanda-rūpa)of the nature of consciousness-bliss

Verse 6

6

अहं निर्विकल्पो निराकाररूपो विभुत्वाच्च सर्वत्र सर्वेन्द्रियाणाम् । न चासंगतं नैव मुक्तिर्न बन्धश्चिदानन्दरूपः शिवोऽहं शिवोऽहम् ॥

I am free from distinctions and of formless nature; as the all-pervading Self, I am present everywhere, beyond the senses. I have no association; there is neither liberation nor bondage. I am Śiva, whose nature is consciousness and bliss; I am Śiva.

Transliteration

ahaṃ nirvikalpo nirākārarūpo vibhutvācca sarvatra sarvendriyāṇām | na cāsaṃgataṃ naiva muktirna bandhaścidānandarūpaḥ śivo’haṃ śivo’ham ||

Word-for-word
अहम् (aham)I
निर्विकल्प (nirvikalpa)free from distinctions, thought-free
निराकार-रूप (nirākāra-rūpa)of formless nature
विभुत्वात् (vibhutvāt)because of all-pervasiveness
सर्वत्र (sarvatra)everywhere
सर्व-इन्द्रियाणाम् (sarva-indriyāṇām)of all the senses
असङ्गत (asaṅgata)unassociated
मुक्ति • बन्ध (mukti • bandha)liberation • bondage
चित्-आनन्द-रूप (cit-ānanda-rūpa)of the nature of consciousness-bliss
शिवः अहम् (śivaḥ aham)Śiva am I; I am the auspicious Self
न (na)not; repeated in the closing denials
च (ca)and
एव (eva)indeed; with न, forms नैव

Closing colophon

Colophon

इति श्रीमत्परमहंसपरिव्राजकाचार्यस्य श्रीगोविन्दभगवत्पूज्यपादशिष्यस्य श्रीमच्छङ्करभगवतः कृतौ निर्वाणषट्कं संपूर्णम् ॥

Thus is completed the Nirvāṇaṣaṭkam composed by the revered Śaṅkara Bhagavat, disciple of the venerable Govinda Bhagavat, the revered teacher who has renounced all, and supreme among wandering ascetics.

Transliteration

iti śrīmatparamahaṃsaparivrājakācāryasya śrīgovindabhagavatpūjyapādaśiṣyasya śrīmacchaṅkarabhagavataḥ kṛtau nirvāṇaṣaṭkaṃ saṃpūrṇam ||

Word-for-word
इति (iti)thus
श्रीमत् • परमहंस (śrīmat • paramahaṃsa)revered • supreme renunciant
परिव्राजक-आचार्य (parivrājaka-ācārya)teacher among wandering ascetics
श्री-गोविन्द-भगवत् (śrī-govinda-bhagavat)revered Govinda Bhagavat
पूज्य-पाद (pūjya-pāda)whose feet are worthy of worship
शिष्य (śiṣya)disciple
श्रीमत्-शङ्कर-भगवत् (śrīmat-śaṅkara-bhagavat)the revered Śaṅkara Bhagavat
कृतौ (kṛtau)in the composition
निर्वाण-षट्कम् (nirvāṇa-ṣaṭkam)the six verses on liberation
संपूर्णम् (saṃpūrṇam)complete
