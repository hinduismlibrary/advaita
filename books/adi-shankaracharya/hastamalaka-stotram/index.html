Verse 1

1

निमित्तं मनश्चक्षुरादिप्रवृत्तौ निरस्ताखिलोपाधिराकाशकल्पः । रविर्लोकचेष्टानिमित्तं यथा यः स नित्योपलब्धिस्वरूपोऽहमात्मा ॥१॥

I am the Self, whose nature is ever-present awareness and who is the occasion for the activity of mind, eye, and the rest, as the sun is for the world's activity. Free of every limiting adjunct, I am like space.

Transliteration

nimittaṃ manaścakṣurādipravṛttau nirastākhilopādhirākāśakalpaḥ | ravirlokaceṣṭānimittaṃ yathā yaḥ sa nityopalabdhisvarūpo’hamātmā ||1||

Word-for-word
निमित्तम् (nimittam)the occasion or cause
मनः (manaḥ)mind
चक्षुः-आदि (cakṣuḥ-ādi)the eye and the rest
प्रवृत्तौ (pravṛttau)in their activity
निरस्त-अखिल-उपाधिः (nirasta-akhila-upādhiḥ)free from every limiting adjunct
आकाश-कल्पः (ākāśa-kalpaḥ)like space
रविः (raviḥ)the sun
लोक-चेष्टा-निमित्तम् (loka-ceṣṭā-nimittam)the cause of the world's activity
यथा (yathā)as
यः (yaḥ)that which
नित्य-उपलब्धि-स्वरूपः (nitya-upalabdhi-svarūpaḥ)whose nature is ever-present awareness
अहम् आत्मा (aham ātmā)I am the Self

Verse 2

2

यमग्न्युष्णवन्नित्यबोधस्वरूपं मनश्चक्षुरादीन्यबोधात्मकानि । प्रवर्तन्त आश्रित्य निष्कम्पमेकं स नित्योपलब्धिस्वरूपोऽहमात्मा ॥२॥

I am the Self, the one unmoving ground of ever-present awareness, like fire's heat in being inseparable from its nature. Mind, eye, and the other faculties, though themselves insentient, function by depending on it.

Transliteration

yamagnyuṣṇavannityabodhasvarūpaṃ manaścakṣurādīnyabodhātmakāni | pravartanta āśritya niṣkampamekaṃ sa nityopalabdhisvarūpo’hamātmā ||2||

Word-for-word
यम् (yam)that which
अग्नि-उष्णवत् (agni-uṣṇavat)like heat in fire
नित्य-बोध-स्वरूपम् (nitya-bodha-svarūpam)whose nature is eternal awareness
मनः-चक्षुः-आदीनि (manaḥ-cakṣuḥ-ādīni)mind, eye, and the rest
अबोध-आत्मकानि (abodha-ātmakāni)insentient in nature
आश्रित्य (āśritya)depending upon
निष्कम्पम् (niṣkampam)unmoving
एकम् (ekam)the one
प्रवर्तन्ते (pravartante)they function
नित्य-उपलब्धि-स्वरूपः (nitya-upalabdhi-svarūpaḥ)of the nature of ever-present awareness
अहम् आत्मा (aham ātmā)I am the Self

Verse 3

3

मुखाभासको दर्पणे दृश्यमानो मुखत्वात् पृथक्त्वेन नैवास्ति वस्तु । चिदाभासको धीषु जीवोऽपि तद्वत् स नित्योपलब्धिस्वरूपोऽहमात्मा ॥३॥

A face seen in a mirror as a reflection is no real thing separate from the face. Likewise, the individual appears in intellects as a reflection of consciousness, yet is not separate from it. I am that Self, ever-present awareness.

Transliteration

mukhābhāsako darpaṇe dṛśyamāno mukhatvāt pṛthaktvena naivāsti vastu | cidābhāsako dhīṣu jīvo’pi tadvat sa nityopalabdhisvarūpo’hamātmā ||3||

Word-for-word
मुख-आभासकः (mukha-ābhāsakaḥ)the reflection of a face
दर्पणे (darpaṇe)in a mirror
दृश्यमानः (dṛśyamānaḥ)being seen
मुखत्वात् (mukhatvāt)because it is the face itself
पृथक्त्वेन (pṛthaktvena)as something separate
न एव अस्ति वस्तु (na eva asti vastu)is not a real thing
चित्-आभासकः (cit-ābhāsakaḥ)a reflection of consciousness
धीषु (dhīṣu)in intellects
जीवः अपि (jīvaḥ api)the individual self too
तद्वत् (tadvat)in the same way
सः (saḥ)that
नित्योपलब्धि-स्वरूपः (nityopalabdhi-svarūpaḥ)the Self of ever-present awareness
अहम् आत्मा (aham ātmā)I am

Verse 4

4

यथा दर्पणाभाव आभासहानौ मुखं विद्यते कल्पनाहीनमेकम् । तथा धीवियोगे निराभासको यः स नित्योपलब्धिस्वरूपोऽहमात्मा ॥४॥

When the mirror is gone and the reflection disappears, the single face remains, free from imagined doubling. So, when the intellect is absent, the one without reflection remains. I am that ever-present Self.

Transliteration

yathā darpaṇābhāva ābhāsahānau mukhaṃ vidyate kalpanāhīnamekam | tathā dhīviyoge nirābhāsako yaḥ sa nityopalabdhisvarūpo’hamātmā ||4||

Word-for-word
यथा (yathā)just as
दर्पण-अभावे (darpaṇa-abhāve)when the mirror is absent
आभास-हानौ (ābhāsa-hānau)when the reflection has vanished
मुखम् (mukham)the face
विद्यते (vidyate)remains
कल्पना-हीनम् (kalpanā-hīnam)free of imagined appearance
एकम् (ekam)one
तथा (tathā)so
धी-वियोगे (dhī-viyoge)when the intellect is absent
निराभासकः (nirābhāsakaḥ)without a reflection
यः (yaḥ)that which
सः (saḥ)that
नित्योपलब्धि-स्वरूपः (nityopalabdhi-svarūpaḥ)the Self of ever-present awareness
अहम् आत्मा (aham ātmā)I am

Verse 5

5

मनश्चक्षुरादेर्वियुक्तः स्वयं यो मनश्चक्षुरादेर्मनश्चक्षुरादिः । मनश्चक्षुरादेरगम्यस्वरूपः स नित्योपलब्धिस्वरूपोऽहमात्मा ॥५॥

I am the Self, distinct from mind, eye, and the rest, yet the awareness that makes mind, eye, and the rest possible. My nature cannot be reached by those faculties; I am ever-present awareness.

Transliteration

manaścakṣurāderviyuktaḥ svayaṃ yo manaścakṣurādermanaścakṣurādiḥ | manaścakṣurāderagamyasvarūpaḥ sa nityopalabdhisvarūpo’hamātmā ||5||

Word-for-word
मनः-चक्षुः-आदेः (manaḥ-cakṣuḥ-ādeḥ)from mind, eye, and the rest
वियुक्तः (viyuktaḥ)distinct
स्वयम् (svayam)itself
यः (yaḥ)that which
मनः-चक्षुः-आदेः (manaḥ-cakṣuḥ-ādeḥ)of mind, eye, and the rest
मनः-चक्षुः-आदिः (manaḥ-cakṣuḥ-ādiḥ)the mind of mind, eye of eye, and so on
अगम्य-स्वरूपः (agamya-svarūpaḥ)whose nature cannot be reached
सः (saḥ)that
नित्योपलब्धि-स्वरूपः (nityopalabdhi-svarūpaḥ)the Self of ever-present awareness
अहम् आत्मा (aham ātmā)I am

Verse 6

6

य एको विभाति स्वतः शुद्धचेताः प्रकाशस्वरूपोऽपि नानेव धीषु । शरावोदकस्थो यथा भानुरेकः स नित्योपलब्धिस्वरूपोऽहमात्मा ॥६॥

I am the one Self, pure awareness, shining by itself. Though its nature is light, it seems manifold in different intellects, as one sun appears in the water held in many vessels.

Transliteration

ya eko vibhāti svataḥ śuddhacetāḥ prakāśasvarūpo’pi nāneva dhīṣu | śarāvodakastho yathā bhānurekaḥ sa nityopalabdhisvarūpo’hamātmā ||6||

Word-for-word
यः (yaḥ)that which
एकः (ekaḥ)one
विभाति (vibhāti)shines
स्वतः (svataḥ)by itself
शुद्ध-चेताः (śuddha-cetāḥ)pure awareness
प्रकाश-स्वरूपः अपि (prakāśa-svarūpaḥ api)though of the nature of light
नाना इव (nānā iva)as if manifold
धीषु (dhīṣu)in intellects
शराव-उदक-स्थः (śarāva-udaka-sthaḥ)seen in water held in vessels
यथा (yathā)as
भानुः एकः (bhānuḥ ekaḥ)one sun
नित्योपलब्धि-स्वरूपः (nityopalabdhi-svarūpaḥ)of the nature of ever-present awareness
अहम् आत्मा (aham ātmā)I am the Self

Verse 7

7

यथानेकचक्षुःप्रकाशो रविर्न क्रमेण प्रकाशीकरोति प्रकाश्यम् । अनेका धियो यस्तथैकप्रबोधः स नित्योपलब्धिस्वरूपोऽहमात्मा ॥७॥

As the sun makes visible what is before many eyes all at once, not one after another, so I am the single awareness that illumines many intellects at the same time.

yathānekacakṣuḥprakāśo ravirna krameṇa prakāśīkaroti prakāśyam | anekā dhiyo yastathaikaprabodhaḥ sa nityopalabdhisvarūpo’hamātmā ||7||

यथा (yathā)just as

न (na)not
क्रमेण (krameṇa)in sequence
प्रकाशी-करोति (prakāśī-karoti)makes visible
प्रकाश्यम् (prakāśyam)what is to be seen
अनेकाः धियः (anekāḥ dhiyaḥ)many intellects

अहम् आत्मा (aham ātmā)I am the Self

Verse 8

8

विवस्वत्प्रभातं यथारूपमक्षं प्रगृह्णाति नाभातमेवं विवस्वान् । यदाभात आभासयत्यक्षमेकः स नित्योपलब्धिस्वरूपोऽहमात्मा ॥८॥

As the eye apprehends a form revealed by the sun, but not one left unlit, so the single Self illumines the eye itself. I am that ever-present awareness by which even the sun is known.

Transliteration

vivasvatprabhātaṃ yathārūpamakṣaṃ pragṛhṇāti nābhātamevaṃ vivasvān | yadābhāta ābhāsayatyakṣamekaḥ sa nityopalabdhisvarūpo’hamātmā ||8||

अक्षम् (akṣam)the eye
प्रगृह्णाति (pragṛhṇāti)apprehends
न आभातम् (na ābhātam)not what is unlit
एवम् (evam)so
विवस्वान् (vivasvān)the sun
यत् आभातः (yat ābhātaḥ)by which it is illumined

अक्षम् (akṣam)the eye
एकः (ekaḥ)the one

अहम् आत्मा (aham ātmā)I am

Verse 9

9

यथा सूर्य एकोऽप्स्वनेकश्चलासु स्थिरास्वप्यनन्वग्विभाव्यस्वरूपः । चलासु प्रभिन्नासु धीष्वेवमेकः स नित्योपलब्धिस्वरूपोऽहमात्मा ॥९॥

One sun appears as many in waters that move or stand still, yet it does not move with them. So the one Self appears among distinct, changing intellects without itself becoming divided. I am that ever-present awareness.

Transliteration

yathā sūrya eko’psvanekaścalāsu sthirāsvapyananvagvibhāvyasvarūpaḥ | calāsu prabhinnāsu dhīṣvevamekaḥ sa nityopalabdhisvarūpo’hamātmā ||9||

Word-for-word
यथा (yathā)just as

अप्सु (apsu)in waters

अनन्वक्-विभाव्य-स्वरूपः (ananvak-vibhāvya-svarūpaḥ)not following their movement in its own nature
चलासु (calāsu)in changing

एवम् (evam)so

Verse 10

10

घनच्छन्नदृष्टिर्घनच्छन्नमर्कं यथा निष्प्रभं मन्यते चातिमूढः । तथा बद्धवद्भाति यो मूढदृष्टेः स नित्योपलब्धिस्वरूपोऽहमात्मा ॥१०॥

As a person whose sight is clouded foolishly thinks the cloud-covered sun has lost its light, so to the deluded the Self appears bound. I am that Self, ever-present awareness.

Verse 11

11

समस्तेषु वस्तुष्वनुस्यूतमेकं समस्तानि वस्तूनि यं न स्पृशन्ति । वियद्वत्सदा शुद्धमच्छस्वरूपं स नित्योपलब्धिस्वरूपोऽहमात्मा ॥११॥

I am the one Self threaded through all things, yet untouched by anything. Ever pure like space, clear in nature, I am the Self of ever-present awareness.

Verse 12

12

उपाधौ यथा भेदता सन्मणीनां तथा भेदता बुद्धिभेदेषु तेऽपि । यथा चन्द्रिकाणां जले चञ्चलत्वं तथा चञ्चलत्वं तवापीह विष्णो ॥१२॥
