Verse 1

1

निमित्तं मनश्चक्षुरादिप्रवृत्तौ निरस्ताखिलोपाधिराकाशकल्पः । रविर्लोकचेष्टानिमित्तं यथा यः स नित्योपलब्धिस्वरूपोऽहमात्मा ॥१॥

I am the Self, whose nature is ever-present awareness and who is the occasion for the activity of mind, eye, and the rest, as the sun is for the world's activity. Free of every limiting adjunct, I am like space.

Word-for-word
निमित्तम् the occasion or cause
मनः mind
चक्षुः-आदि the eye and the rest
प्रवृत्तौ in their activity
निरस्त-अखिल-उपाधिः free from every limiting adjunct
आकाश-कल्पः like space
रविः the sun
लोक-चेष्टा-निमित्तम् the cause of the world's activity
यथा as
यः that which
नित्य-उपलब्धि-स्वरूपः whose nature is ever-present awareness
अहम् आत्मा I am the Self

Verse 2

2

यमग्न्युष्णवन्नित्यबोधस्वरूपं मनश्चक्षुरादीन्यबोधात्मकानि । प्रवर्तन्त आश्रित्य निष्कम्पमेकं स नित्योपलब्धिस्वरूपोऽहमात्मा ॥२॥

I am the Self, the one unmoving ground of ever-present awareness, like fire's heat in being inseparable from its nature. Mind, eye, and the other faculties, though themselves insentient, function by depending on it.

Word-for-word
यम् that which
अग्नि-उष्णवत् like heat in fire
नित्य-बोध-स्वरूपम् whose nature is eternal awareness
मनः-चक्षुः-आदीनि mind, eye, and the rest
अबोध-आत्मकानि insentient in nature
आश्रित्य depending upon
निष्कम्पम् unmoving
एकम् the one
प्रवर्तन्ते they function
नित्य-उपलब्धि-स्वरूपः of the nature of ever-present awareness
अहम् आत्मा I am the Self

Verse 3

3

मुखाभासको दर्पणे दृश्यमानो मुखत्वात् पृथक्त्वेन नैवास्ति वस्तु । चिदाभासको धीषु जीवोऽपि तद्वत् स नित्योपलब्धिस्वरूपोऽहमात्मा ॥३॥

A face seen in a mirror as a reflection is no real thing separate from the face. Likewise, the individual appears in intellects as a reflection of consciousness, yet is not separate from it. I am that Self, ever-present awareness.

Word-for-word
मुख-आभासकः the reflection of a face
दर्पणे in a mirror
दृश्यमानः being seen
मुखत्वात् because it is the face itself
पृथक्त्वेन as something separate
न एव अस्ति वस्तु is not a real thing
चित्-आभासकः a reflection of consciousness
धीषु in intellects
जीवः अपि the individual self too
तद्वत् in the same way
सः that
नित्योपलब्धि-स्वरूपः the Self of ever-present awareness
अहम् आत्मा I am

Verse 4

4

यथा दर्पणाभाव आभासहानौ मुखं विद्यते कल्पनाहीनमेकम् । तथा धीवियोगे निराभासको यः स नित्योपलब्धिस्वरूपोऽहमात्मा ॥४॥

When the mirror is gone and the reflection disappears, the single face remains, free from imagined doubling. So, when the intellect is absent, the one without reflection remains. I am that ever-present Self.

Word-for-word
यथा just as
दर्पण-अभावे when the mirror is absent
आभास-हानौ when the reflection has vanished
मुखम् the face
विद्यते remains
कल्पना-हीनम् free of imagined appearance
एकम् one
तथा so
धी-वियोगे when the intellect is absent
निराभासकः without a reflection
यः that which
सः that
नित्योपलब्धि-स्वरूपः the Self of ever-present awareness
अहम् आत्मा I am

Verse 5

5

मनश्चक्षुरादेर्वियुक्तः स्वयं यो मनश्चक्षुरादेर्मनश्चक्षुरादिः । मनश्चक्षुरादेरगम्यस्वरूपः स नित्योपलब्धिस्वरूपोऽहमात्मा ॥५॥

I am the Self, distinct from mind, eye, and the rest, yet the awareness that makes mind, eye, and the rest possible. My nature cannot be reached by those faculties; I am ever-present awareness.

Word-for-word
मनः-चक्षुः-आदेः from mind, eye, and the rest
वियुक्तः distinct
स्वयम् itself
यः that which
मनः-चक्षुः-आदेः of mind, eye, and the rest
मनः-चक्षुः-आदिः the mind of mind, eye of eye, and so on
अगम्य-स्वरूपः whose nature cannot be reached
सः that
नित्योपलब्धि-स्वरूपः the Self of ever-present awareness
अहम् आत्मा I am

Verse 6

6

य एको विभाति स्वतः शुद्धचेताः प्रकाशस्वरूपोऽपि नानेव धीषु । शरावोदकस्थो यथा भानुरेकः स नित्योपलब्धिस्वरूपोऽहमात्मा ॥६॥

I am the one Self, pure awareness, shining by itself. Though its nature is light, it seems manifold in different intellects, as one sun appears in the water held in many vessels.

Word-for-word
यः that which
एकः one
विभाति shines
स्वतः by itself
शुद्ध-चेताः pure awareness
प्रकाश-स्वरूपः अपि though of the nature of light
नाना इव as if manifold
धीषु in intellects
शराव-उदक-स्थः seen in water held in vessels
यथा as
भानुः एकः one sun
नित्योपलब्धि-स्वरूपः of the nature of ever-present awareness
अहम् आत्मा I am the Self

Verse 7

7

यथानेकचक्षुःप्रकाशो रविर्न क्रमेण प्रकाशीकरोति प्रकाश्यम् । अनेका धियो यस्तथैकप्रबोधः स नित्योपलब्धिस्वरूपोऽहमात्मा ॥७॥

As the sun makes visible what is before many eyes all at once, not one after another, so I am the single awareness that illumines many intellects at the same time.

Word-for-word
यथा just as
अनेक-चक्षुः-प्रकाशः the illuminator of many eyes
रविः the sun
न not
क्रमेण in sequence
प्रकाशी-करोति makes visible
प्रकाश्यम् what is to be seen
अनेकाः धियः many intellects
यः that which
तथा so
एक-प्रबोधः one awareness
नित्योपलब्धि-स्वरूपः of the nature of ever-present awareness
अहम् आत्मा I am the Self

Verse 8

8

विवस्वत्प्रभातं यथारूपमक्षं प्रगृह्णाति नाभातमेवं विवस्वान् । यदाभात आभासयत्यक्षमेकः स नित्योपलब्धिस्वरूपोऽहमात्मा ॥८॥

As the eye apprehends a form revealed by the sun, but not one left unlit, so the single Self illumines the eye itself. I am that ever-present awareness by which even the sun is known.

Word-for-word
विवस्वत्-प्रभातम् made bright by the sun
यथा as
रूपम् a form
अक्षम् the eye
प्रगृह्णाति apprehends
न आभातम् not what is unlit
एवम् so
विवस्वान् the sun
यत् आभातः by which it is illumined
आभासयति makes shine
अक्षम् the eye
एकः the one
नित्योपलब्धि-स्वरूपः the Self of ever-present awareness
अहम् आत्मा I am

Verse 9

9

यथा सूर्य एकोऽप्स्वनेकश्चलासु स्थिरास्वप्यनन्वग्विभाव्यस्वरूपः । चलासु प्रभिन्नासु धीष्वेवमेकः स नित्योपलब्धिस्वरूपोऽहमात्मा ॥९॥

One sun appears as many in waters that move or stand still, yet it does not move with them. So the one Self appears among distinct, changing intellects without itself becoming divided. I am that ever-present awareness.

Word-for-word
यथा just as
सूर्यः एकः one sun
अप्सु in waters
अनेकः as many
चलासु स्थिरासु अपि in moving and still waters alike
अनन्वक्-विभाव्य-स्वरूपः not following their movement in its own nature
चलासु in changing
प्रभिन्नासु धीषु distinct intellects
एवम् so
एकः one
नित्योपलब्धि-स्वरूपः of the nature of ever-present awareness
अहम् आत्मा I am the Self

Verse 10

10

घनच्छन्नदृष्टिर्घनच्छन्नमर्कं यथा निष्प्रभं मन्यते चातिमूढः । तथा बद्धवद्भाति यो मूढदृष्टेः स नित्योपलब्धिस्वरूपोऽहमात्मा ॥१०॥

As a person whose sight is clouded foolishly thinks the cloud-covered sun has lost its light, so to the deluded the Self appears bound. I am that Self, ever-present awareness.

Word-for-word
घन-छन्न-दृष्टिः one whose sight is covered by a cloud
घन-छन्नम् अर्कम् the cloud-covered sun
यथा as
निष्प्रभम् without brilliance
मन्यते thinks
च अतिमूढः and is greatly deluded
तथा so
बद्धवत् भाति appears as if bound
यः that which
मूढ-दृष्टेः to a deluded vision
सः that
नित्योपलब्धि-स्वरूपः the Self of ever-present awareness
अहम् आत्मा I am

Verse 11

11

समस्तेषु वस्तुष्वनुस्यूतमेकं समस्तानि वस्तूनि यं न स्पृशन्ति । वियद्वत्सदा शुद्धमच्छस्वरूपं स नित्योपलब्धिस्वरूपोऽहमात्मा ॥११॥

I am the one Self threaded through all things, yet untouched by anything. Ever pure like space, clear in nature, I am the Self of ever-present awareness.

Word-for-word
समस्तेषु वस्तुषु in all things
अनुस्यूतम् threaded through
एकम् the one
समस्तानि वस्तूनि all things
यम् which
न स्पृशन्ति do not touch
वियत्-वत् like space
सदा always
शुद्धम् pure
अच्छ-स्वरूपम् clear in nature
सः that
नित्योपलब्धि-स्वरूपः the Self of ever-present awareness
अहम् आत्मा I am

Verse 12

12

उपाधौ यथा भेदता सन्मणीनां तथा भेदता बुद्धिभेदेषु तेऽपि । यथा चन्द्रिकाणां जले चञ्चलत्वं तथा चञ्चलत्वं तवापीह विष्णो ॥१२॥

Just as clear gems seem different under differing adjuncts, so do you seem varied in the distinct intellects. As moonlight appears to waver in water, so you too seem to waver here, O Viṣṇu.

Word-for-word
उपाधौ in a limiting adjunct
यथा just as
भेदता difference appears
सत्-मणीनाम् among clear gems
तथा so
भेदता difference appears
बुद्धि-भेदेषु in distinct intellects
ते अपि in you too
यथा as
चन्द्रिकाणाम् of moonbeams
जले in water
चञ्चलत्वम् wavering
तथा so
चञ्चलत्वम् wavering
तव अपि इह in you here as well
विष्णो O Viṣṇu
