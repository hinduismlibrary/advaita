Advaita Treatises

Vivekacūḍāmaṇi

By Ādi Śaṅkara

Info

A teaching poem on discrimination, the qualifications of a seeker, and liberation, with all 580 numbered verses.

English translation by Swami Madhavananda, Himalayan Series No. XLIII (1921). Sanskrit electronic witness from GRETIL, marked not proofread, normalized from IAST and converted to Devanāgarī for this reader. The local text includes all 580 numbered verses; English translations that cover adjacent verses are labeled as ranges. Editorial notes and index are omitted. No separate word-for-word lexical gloss is supplied.
