# Source texts and attribution

The Sanskrit text of most readers is based on Ambuda electronic editions, which dedicate the transcriptions to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal. The listed print sources are editions of *The Works of Sri Sankaracharya*, published by Sri Vani Vilas Press (1910). Ambuda credits Arun Prasad for the machine-readable versions; that credit is retained here. The Upadeśapañcakam follows the text in Advaita Śāradā, Sringeri. The English translations and word-for-word glosses on this site are original.

## Proofreading references

These were consulted to check the Sanskrit readings: [Advaita Śāradā, Sringeri](https://www.advaitasharada.sringeri.net/read/pancharatna-stotrani/6/) for Advaitapañcaratnam; [the Sanskrit Documents text of Māyāpañcakam](https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_z_misc_shankara/mAyA5.html), proofread by Sunder Hattangadi; [Sanskrit Documents](https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_shiva/dakShiNAmUrtyaShTakam.html) and [Stotra Nidhi](https://stotranidhi.com/en/dakshinamurthy-ashtakam-in-english) for Śrī Dakṣiṇāmūrtyaṣṭakam; and [Stotra Nidhi](https://stotranidhi.com/hi/vishnu-shatpadi-stotram-in-sanskrit/) for Viṣṇu Ṣaṭpadī; [Advaita Śāradā, Sringeri](https://advaitasharada.sringeri.net/read/pancharatna-stotrani/11/) for Manīṣāpañcakam; and [Sanskrit Documents](https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_z_misc_shankara/dashashl.html) for Daśaślokī; and [Sanskrit Documents](https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_z_misc_shankara/aatmabodha.html) for Ātmabodha. These references are recorded for collation only; their website transcriptions are not reproduced here.

Dṛg-dṛśya-viveka uses the 1931 Sri Ramakrishna Asrama edition as its Sanskrit witness. Its English translation and word-level gloss are newly prepared for this site. The attribution is described as traditional rather than certain.
