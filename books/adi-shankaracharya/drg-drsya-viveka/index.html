Verse 1

1

रूपं दृश्यं लोचनं दृक् तद् दृश्यं दृक् तु मानसम्। दृश्या धीवृत्तयः साक्षी दृगेव न तु दृश्यते॥

Forms are seen; the eye is their seer. The eye in turn is seen; the mind is its seer. The mind’s thoughts are seen; the Witness alone is their seer, and is never itself seen.

Transliteration

rūpaṃ dṛśyaṃ locanaṃ dṛk tad dṛśyaṃ dṛk tu mānasam| dṛśyā dhīvṛttayaḥ sākṣī dṛgeva na tu dṛśyate||

Word-for-word
रूपम् (rūpam)form
दृश्यम् (dṛśyam)is seen
लोचनम् (locanam)the eye
दृक् (dṛk)is the seer
मानसम् (mānasam)the mind
धी-वृत्तयः (dhī-vṛttayaḥ)mental thoughts
साक्षी (sākṣī)the Witness
न तु दृश्यते (na tu dṛśyate)is not seen

Verse 2

2

नीलपीतस्थूलसूक्ष्मह्रस्वदीर्घादिभेदतः। नानाविधानि रूपाणि पश्येल्लोचनमेकधा॥

Though one, the eye sees forms of many kinds, differing as blue or yellow, gross or subtle, short or long, and so forth.

Transliteration

nīlapītasthūlasūkṣmahrasvadīrghādibhedataḥ| nānāvidhāni rūpāṇi paśyellocanamekadhā||

Word-for-word
नील-पीत-स्थूल-सूक्ष्म-ह्रस्व-दीर्घ-आदि-भेदतः (nīla-pīta-sthūla-sūkṣma-hrasva-dīrgha-ādi-bhedataḥ)through differences such as blue/yellow, gross/subtle, short/long
नाना-विधानि रूपाणि (nānā-vidhāni rūpāṇi)many kinds of forms
लोचनम् (locanam)the eye
एकधा (ekadhā)as one
पश्येत् (paśyet)sees

Verse 3

3

आन्ध्यमान्द्यपटुत्वेषु नेत्रधर्मेषु चैकधा। सङ्कल्पयेन्मनः श्रोत्रत्वगादौ योज्यतामिदम्॥

The one mind recognizes blindness, dullness, and acuity as conditions of the eye. Apply this same inquiry to the ear, skin, and the other senses.

Transliteration

āndhyamāndyapaṭutveṣu netradharmeṣu caikadhā| saṅkalpayenmanaḥ śrotratvagādau yojyatāmidam||

एकधा (ekadhā)as one
मनः (manaḥ)the mind
सङ्कल्पयेत् (saṅkalpayet)recognizes / conceives
श्रोत्र-त्वक्-आदौ (śrotra-tvak-ādau)the ear, skin, and others
इदम् (idam)this [inquiry]

Verse 4

4

कामः सङ्कल्पसन्देहौ श्रद्धाश्रद्धे धृतीतरे। ह्रीर्धीर्भीरित्येवमादीन् भासयत्येकधा चितिः॥

Consciousness, one and the same, illumines desire, resolve and doubt, faith and its absence, steadiness and its opposite, shame, understanding, fear, and the rest.

Transliteration

kāmaḥ saṅkalpasandehau śraddhāśraddhe dhṛtītare| hrīrdhīrbhīrityevamādīn bhāsayatyekadhā citiḥ||

Word-for-word
कामः (kāmaḥ)desire
सङ्कल्प-सन्देहौ (saṅkalpa-sandehau)resolve and doubt
श्रद्धा-अश्रद्धे (śraddhā-aśraddhe)faith and lack of faith
धृति-इतरे (dhṛti-itare)steadiness and its opposite
ह्रीः-धीः-भीः (hrīḥ-dhīḥ-bhīḥ)shame, understanding, fear
इति एवम्-आदीन् (iti evam-ādīn)and these others
चितिः (citiḥ)consciousness
एकधा भासयति (ekadhā bhāsayati)illumines uniformly

Verse 5

5

नोदेति नास्तमेत्येषा न वृद्धिं याति न क्षयम्। स्वयं विभात्यथान्यानि भासयेत्साधनं विना॥

This awareness neither rises nor sets; it neither increases nor diminishes. It shines by itself and, without another instrument, illumines all other things.

Transliteration

nodeti nāstametyeṣā na vṛddhiṃ yāti na kṣayam| svayaṃ vibhātyathānyāni bhāsayetsādhanaṃ vinā||

Word-for-word
एषा (eṣā)this [awareness]

न वृद्धिम् याति (na vṛddhim yāti)does not increase
न क्षयम् (na kṣayam)nor decline
स्वयम् विभाति (svayam vibhāti)shines by itself
अथ (atha)and then
अन्यानि (anyāni)other things
साधनम् विना (sādhanam vinā)without an instrument
भासयेत् (bhāsayet)makes known / illumines

Verse 6

6

चिच्छायावेशतो बुद्धौ भानं धीस्तु द्विधा स्थिता। एकाहङ्कृतिरन्या स्यादन्तःकरणरूपिणी॥

Because consciousness is reflected in the intellect, knowing appears there. The intellect has two aspects: one is the ego-sense, and the other is the inner instrument of thought.

Transliteration

cicchāyāveśato buddhau bhānaṃ dhīstu dvidhā sthitā| ekāhaṅkṛtiranyā syādantaḥkaraṇarūpiṇī||

चित्-छाया-आवेशतः (cit-chāyā-āveśataḥ)through the presence of consciousness’s reflection
बुद्धौ (buddhau)in the intellect
भानम् (bhānam)appearance / illumination
धीः (dhīḥ)the intellect
द्विधा स्थिता (dvidhā sthitā)stands in two forms
एका अहङ्कृतिः (ekā ahaṅkṛtiḥ)one is the ego-sense
अन्या (anyā)the other
अन्तःकरण-रूपिणी (antaḥkaraṇa-rūpiṇī)has the form of the inner instrument

Verse 7

7

छायाहङ्कारयोरैक्यं तप्तायःपिण्डवन्मतम्। तदहङ्कारतादात्म्याद्देहश्चेतनतामगात्॥

The reflection of consciousness and the ego are regarded as united, like a red-hot iron ball. Through identification with that ego, the body appears to acquire sentience.

मतम् (matam)is held
तत्-अहङ्कार-तादात्म्यात् (tat-ahaṅkāra-tādātmyāt)through identification with that ego
देहः (dehaḥ)the body

Verse 8

8

अहङ्कारस्य तादात्म्यं चिच्छायादेहसाक्षिभिः। सहजं कर्मजं भ्रान्तिजन्यञ्च त्रिविधं क्रमात्॥

The ego’s identification with reflected consciousness, the body, and the Witness is threefold: innate, produced by karma, and born of delusion, respectively.

Transliteration

ahaṅkārasya tādātmyaṃ cicchāyādehasākṣibhiḥ| sahajaṃ karmajaṃ bhrāntijanyañca trividhaṃ kramāt||

Verse 9

9

सम्बन्धिनोः सतोर्नास्ति निवृत्तिः सहजात्तु। कर्मक्षयात्प्रबोधाच्च निवर्तेते क्रमादुभे॥

While the reflection and ego remain connected, their innate identification does not cease. The other two cease in sequence: one through the exhaustion of karma, the other through awakening.

Transliteration

sambandhinoḥ satornāsti nivṛttiḥ sahajāttu| karmakṣayātprabodhācca nivartete kramādubhe||

Word-for-word

निवृत्तिः न अस्ति (nivṛttiḥ na asti)does not cease
कर्म-क्षयात् (karma-kṣayāt)through exhaustion of karma
प्रबोधात् च (prabodhāt ca)and through awakening
उभे (ubhe)both [remaining kinds]
क्रमात् (kramāt)in sequence
निवर्तेते (nivartete)cease

Verse 10

10

अहङ्कारलये सुप्तौ भवेद्देहोऽप्यचेतनः। अहङ्कारविकासार्धः स्वप्नः सर्वस्तु जागरः॥

When the ego subsides in deep sleep, the body too is insentient. The partial manifestation of the ego is dream; its full manifestation is waking.

Transliteration

Word-for-word

अचेतनः भवेत् (acetanaḥ bhavet)is insentient
अहङ्कार-विकास-अर्धः (ahaṅkāra-vikāsa-ardhaḥ)the partial unfolding of the ego
स्वप्नः (svapnaḥ)is dream
सर्वः तु (sarvaḥ tu)whereas the full [unfolding]
जागरः (jāgaraḥ)is waking

Verse 11

11

Verse 12

12

Verse 13

13

Verse 14

14

Verse 17
