Verse 1

1

तपोभिः क्षीणपापानां शान्तानां वीतरागिणाम् । मुमुक्षूणामपेक्ष्योऽयमात्मबोधो विधीयते ॥

This teaching on the Self is set forth for those who, through austerity, have exhausted their demerit, are peaceful and free from attachment, and seek liberation.

4

As though bounded because of ignorance, the Self shines by itself as the One alone when that ignorance is destroyed, like the sun when the clouds disperse.

5

अज्ञानकलुषं जीवं ज्ञानाभ्यासाद्विनिर्मलम् । कृत्वा ज्ञानं स्वयं नश्येज्जलं कतकरेणुवत् ॥

Having purified the mind-clouded individual through the practice of knowledge, knowledge itself subsides, like the cleansing powder settling out of water.

Verse 6

The cycle of worldly existence, crowded with attachment, aversion, and the rest, is indeed like a dream: it seems real while it lasts, but on awakening is seen as unreal.
