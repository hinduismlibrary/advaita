Advaita Texts

Stotrams

  • Advaitapañcaratnam by Ādi Śaṅkara 5 verses
    i

    Five verses expressing the non-dual nature of the Self.

  • Bhajagovindam (Mohamudgara) by Ādi Śaṅkara 31 verses
    i

    Verses on discernment, devotion to Govinda, and freedom from attachment.

  • Kāśīpañcakam by Ādi Śaṅkara 5 verses
    i

    Five verses on the inner meaning of Kāśī and the Self.

  • Manīṣāpañcakam by Ādi Śaṅkara 5 verses
    i

    Five teachings on the Self and the knowledge of Brahman.

  • Māyāpañcakam by Ādi Śaṅkara 5 verses
    i

    Five verses on how Māyā makes non-duality appear as plurality.

  • Nirvāṇaṣaṭkam by Ādi Śaṅkara 6 verses
    i

    Six verses describing the Self beyond body, mind, and personal identity.

  • Śrī Dakṣiṇāmūrtyaṣṭakam by Ādi Śaṅkara 8 verses
    i

    A hymn to Dakṣiṇāmūrti as the silent teacher of non-dual knowledge.

  • Viṣṇu Ṣaṭpadī Stotram by Ādi Śaṅkara 6 verses
    i

    A devotional prayer to Viṣṇu seeking refuge and freedom from saṃsāra.

  • Yatipañcakam by Ādi Śaṅkara 5 verses
    i

    Five verses on the realization and life of a renunciate.

  • Hastāmalaka-stotram by Hastāmalaka 12 verses
    i

    Twelve verses describing the Self as pure awareness.

Short Texts

  • Brahmajñānāvalīmālā by Ādi Śaṅkara 21 verses
    i

    Twenty-one verses summarizing the knowledge of Brahman.

  • Daśaślokī by Ādi Śaṅkara 10 verses
    i

    Ten verses answering the question of the nature of the Self.

  • Ekaślokī by Ādi Śaṅkara 1 verse
    i

    A brief question and answer on the identity of the Self and Brahman.

  • Pañcīkaraṇa-prakaraṇa by Ādi Śaṅkara 7 verses
    i

    Seven verses explaining the five-fold combination of the elements.

  • Tattvabodha by Ādi Śaṅkara Prose
    i

    An introductory prose text defining key Advaita terms and stages of knowledge.

  • Upadeśapañcakam (Sādhanapañcakam) by Ādi Śaṅkara 5 verses
    i

    Five verses of guidance on spiritual practice and discipline.

  • Advaitamakaranda by Lakṣmīdhara Kavi 28 verses
    i

    Twenty-eight verses presenting the non-dual nature of the Self.

Treatises

  • Aparokṣānubhūti by Ādi Śaṅkara 144 verses
    i

    A verse treatise on direct knowledge of the Self.

  • Ātmabodha by Ādi Śaṅkara 68 verses
    i

    A verse text explaining the path to Self-knowledge and liberation.

  • Sarvavedāntasiddhāntasārasaṅgraha by Ādi Śaṅkara 1,006 verses
    i

    A compendium of central conclusions drawn from Vedānta.

  • Upadeśasāhasrī by Ādi Śaṅkara 675 verses
    i

    A systematic work on Self-knowledge in prose and verse.

  • Vivekacūḍāmaṇi by Ādi Śaṅkara 580 verses
    i

    A verse treatise on discrimination, Self-inquiry, and liberation.

  • Dṛg-dṛśya-viveka by Bhāratī Tīrtha 46 verses
    i

    Forty-six verses examining the seer and the seen to establish the witnessing Self.

  • Māṇḍūkyakārikā by Gauḍapāda 215 verses
    i

    Two hundred fifteen verses interpreting the Māṇḍūkya Upaniṣad.

  • Prasthānabheda by Madhusūdana Sarasvatī Prose
    i

    A concise classification of the principal scriptural sources of Vedānta.

  • Brahmasiddhi by Maṇḍana Miśra Prose
    i

    A major philosophical work on Brahman and knowledge.

  • Vedāntasāra by Sadānanda Yogīndra 227 verses
    i

    An introductory manual presenting the main teachings and terms of Advaita Vedānta.

  • Ātmavidyāvilāsa by Sadāśiva Brahmendra 65 verses
    i

    Sixty-six verses on Self-knowledge and the life of a renunciate.

  • Naiṣkarmyasiddhi by Sureśvara 423 verses
    i

    A philosophical treatise on knowledge of the Self and the meaning of actionlessness.

  • Jīvanmuktiviveka by Vidyāraṇya Prose
    i

    A work on liberation while living and the discipline associated with it.

  • Pañcadaśī by Vidyāraṇya 1,571 verses
    i

    A fifteen-chapter exposition of Advaita Vedānta.
