Chapter 1 · Verse 1

1.1

नमः श्री-शङ्करानन्द-गुरु-पादाम्बु-जन्मने। सविलास-महा-मोह-ग्राह-ग्रासैक-कर्मणे॥

Salutation to the lotus-like feet of my guru, Śrī Śaṅkarānanda, whose only job is to swallow the crocodile that is this grand delusion along with its effects.

Transliteration

namaḥ śrī-śaṅkarānanda-guru-pādāmbu-janmane| savilāsa-mahā-moha-grāha-grāsaika-karmaṇe||

Word-for-word
नमः (namaḥ)salutation
श्री-शङ्करानन्द-गुरु-पादाम्बु-जन्मने (śrī-śaṅkarānanda-guru-pādāmbu-janmane)to the lotus feet of Guru Śaṅkarānanda
सविलास-महा-मोह-ग्राह-ग्रासैक-कर्मणे (savilāsa-mahā-moha-grāha-grāsaika-karmaṇe)whose sole work is to swallow great delusion with its play

Chapter 1 · Verse 2

1.2

तत्-पादाम्बु-रुह-द्वन्द्व-सेवा-निर्मल-चेतसाम्। सुख-बोधाय तत्त्वस्य विवेदोऽयं विधीयते॥

This discussion regarding reality and truth is presented for the easy understanding of those whose minds are least defiled via attendance at the two lotus-like feet of their.

Transliteration

tat-pādāmbu-ruha-dvandva-sevā-nirmala-cetasām| sukha-bodhāya tattvasya vivedo’yaṃ vidhīyate||

Word-for-word
तत्-पादाम्बु-रुह-द्वन्द्व-सेवा-निर्मल-चेतसाम् (tat-pādāmbu-ruha-dvandva-sevā-nirmala-cetasām)for those whose minds are purified by service to those two lotus feet
सुख-बोधाय (sukha-bodhāya)for easy understanding
तत्त्वस्य (tattvasya)of reality
विवेकः अयम् विधीयते (vivekaḥ ayam vidhīyate)this discernment is set forth

Chapter 1 · Verse 3

1.3

शब्द-स्पर्शादयो वेद्या वैचित्र्याज् जागरे पृथक्। ततो विभक्ता तत्-संविद् ऐक-रूप्यान् न भिद्यते॥

The knowable beginning with sound, touch, etc. are separate in waking state due to their distinguishing characteristics, whereas the knower of these which is distinct from all of them does not differ because of its singular nature.

Transliteration

śabda-sparśādayo vedyā vaicitryāj jāgare pṛthak| tato vibhaktā tat-saṃvid aika-rūpyān na bhidyate||

Word-for-word
शब्द-स्पर्श-आदयः (śabda-sparśa-ādayaḥ)sound, touch, and the rest
वेद्याः (vedyāḥ)objects of knowledge
वैचित्र्यात् (vaicitryāt)because of their variety
जागरे पृथक् (jāgare pṛthak)separate in waking
ततः विभक्ता (tataḥ vibhaktā)distinct from them
तत्-संवित् (tat-saṃvit)the consciousness that knows them
ऐक-रूप्यात् (aika-rūpyāt)because it is one in nature
न भिद्यते (na bhidyate)does not differ

Chapter 1 · Verse 4

1.4

तथा स्वप्नेऽत्र वेद्यं तु न स्थिरं जागरे स्थिरम्। तद्-भेदोऽतस् तयोः संविद् एकरूपा न भिद्यते॥

Similarly in the dream state. Whereas a known object was stable in waking, here it is unstable. Thus there is a difference between them. the knower having the same nature does not differ.

Transliteration

tathā svapne’tra vedyaṃ tu na sthiraṃ jāgare sthiram| tad-bhedo’tas tayoḥ saṃvid ekarūpā na bhidyate||

Word-for-word
तथा स्वप्ने (tathā svapne)likewise in dream
अत्र वेद्यम् न स्थिरम् (atra vedyam na sthiram)here the known is not stable
जागरे स्थिरम् (jāgare sthiram)stable in waking
तत्-भेदः अतः (tat-bhedaḥ ataḥ)therefore the objects differ
तयोः संवित् (tayoḥ saṃvit)the consciousness in both
एकरूपा न भिद्यते (ekarūpā na bhidyate)one in nature, does not differ

Chapter 1 · Verse 5

1.5

सुप्तोत्थितस्य सौषुप्त-तमो-बोधो भवेत् स्मृतिः। सा चावबुद्ध-विषयाऽवबुद्धं तत् तदा तमः॥

For the one waking from deep sleep, there is remembrance that there was cognition of ignorance in deep sleep. A has as its object a cognition. At that time, that cognition is tamas.

Transliteration

suptotthitasya sauṣupta-tamo-bodho bhavet smṛtiḥ| sā cāvabuddha-viṣayā’vabuddhaṃ tat tadā tamaḥ||

Word-for-word
सुप्त-उत्थितस्य (supta-utthitasya)of one risen from sleep
सौषुप्त-तमः-बोधः (sauṣupta-tamaḥ-bodhaḥ)awareness of the darkness of deep sleep
भवेत् स्मृतिः (bhavet smṛtiḥ)is remembered
सा च (sā ca)and that memory
अवबुद्ध-विषया (avabuddha-viṣayā)has what was known as its object
अवबुद्धम् तत् तदा तमः (avabuddham tat tadā tamaḥ)what was known then was ignorance

Chapter 1 · Verse 6

1.6

स बोधो विषयाद् भिन्नो न बोधात् स्वप्न-बोधवत्। एवं स्थान-त्रयेऽप्येका संवित् तत्वद् दिनान्तरे॥

That cognition is distinct from its object, but, like in dream, does not differ from itself. Thus the knower remains one only in the three states in that same way throughout the day.

Transliteration

sa bodho viṣayād bhinno na bodhāt svapna-bodhavat| evaṃ sthāna-traye’pyekā saṃvit tatvad dināntare||

Word-for-word
सः बोधः (saḥ bodhaḥ)that awareness
विषयात् भिन्नः (viṣayāt bhinnaḥ)is distinct from its object
न बोधात् (na bodhāt)not from awareness itself
स्वप्न-बोधवत् (svapna-bodhavat)as in dream cognition
एवं स्थान-त्रये अपि (evaṃ sthāna-traye api)thus in all three states
एका संवित् (ekā saṃvit)consciousness is one
तत्त्वत् दिनान्तरे (tattvat dināntare)likewise through the day

Chapter 1 · Verse 7

1.7

मासाब्द-युग-कल्पेषु गतागम्येष्वनेकधा। नोदेति नास्तम् एत्येका संविद् एषा स्वयं-प्रभा॥

Throughout the countless months, years, ages and world cycles, both past and future, it neither rises nor sets. This one consciousness is self shining.

Transliteration

māsābda-yuga-kalpeṣu gatāgamyeṣvanekadhā| nodeti nāstam etyekā saṃvid eṣā svayaṃ-prabhā||

Word-for-word
मास-अब्द-युग-कल्पेषु (māsa-abda-yuga-kalpeṣu)through months, years, ages, and cosmic cycles
गत-आगम्येषु अनेका (gata-āgamyeṣu anekā)through past and future in many ways
न उदेति न अस्तम् एति (na udeti na astam eti)neither rises nor sets
एका संवित् एषा (ekā saṃvit eṣā)this one consciousness
स्वयं-प्रभा (svayaṃ-prabhā)self-luminous

Chapter 1 · Verse 8

1.8

इयम् आत्मा परानन्दः पर-प्रेमास्पदं यतः। मा न भूवं हि भूयासम् इति प्रेमात्मनीक्ष्यते॥

This is oneself. It is ultimate fullness, because it is the natural abode of our greatest love. Love is seen in the self as, “May I indeed never cease to be,” “May I always be.”

Transliteration

iyam ātmā parānandaḥ para-premāspadaṃ yataḥ| mā na bhūvaṃ hi bhūyāsam iti premātmanīkṣyate||

Word-for-word
इयम् आत्मा (iyam ātmā)this is the Self
परानन्दः (parānandaḥ)supreme bliss
पर-प्रेम-आस्पदम् (para-prema-āspadam)the ground of supreme love
यतः (yataḥ)because
मा न भूवम् (mā na bhūvam)may I never cease to be
हि भूयासम् (hi bhūyāsam)may I always be
इति प्रेम आत्मनि ईक्ष्यते (iti prema ātmani īkṣyate)thus love is seen directed toward oneself

Chapter 1 · Verse 9

1.9

तत् प्रेमात्मार्थम् अन्यत्र नैवम् अन्यार्थम् आत्मनि। अतस् तत् परमं तेन परमानन्दतात्मनः॥

Only for the sake of oneself is that love directed towards another, thus love towards oneself is never for the sake of another. Therefore, that is the highest. Hence the nature of the ultimate fullness belongs only to the self.

Transliteration

tat premātmārtham anyatra naivam anyārtham ātmani| atas tat paramaṃ tena paramānandatātmanaḥ||

Word-for-word
तत् प्रेम (tat prema)that love
आत्म-अर्थम् (ātma-artham)is for the sake of the Self
अन्यत्र (anyatra)for another
न एवम् (na evam)not so
अन्य-अर्थम् (anya-artham)for the sake of another
आत्मनि (ātmani)in the Self
अतः (ataḥ)therefore
तत् परमम् (tat paramam)that is highest
तेन (tena)therefore
परमानन्दता आत्मनः (paramānandatā ātmanaḥ)the Self is supreme bliss by nature

Chapter 1 · Verse 10

1.10

इत्थं सच्-चित्-परानन्द आत्मा युक्त्या तथाविधम्। परं ब्रह्म तयोश् चैक्यं श्रुत्यन्तेषूपदिश्यते॥

In this way, by this logic, existence-consciousness-fullness is the nature of oneself. Similarly is the ultimate brahman taught in the śrutis as the complete identity of these two.

Transliteration

itthaṃ sac-cit-parānanda ātmā yuktyā tathāvidham| paraṃ brahma tayoś caikyaṃ śrutyanteṣūpadiśyate||

Word-for-word
इत्थम् (ittham)thus
सत्-चित्-परानन्द आत्मा (sat-cit-parānanda ātmā)the Self is existence, consciousness, and supreme bliss
युक्त्या (yuktyā)by reasoning
तथाविधम् परं ब्रह्म (tathāvidham paraṃ brahma)the supreme Brahman is of the same nature
तयोः च ऐक्यम् (tayoḥ ca aikyam)the identity of the two
श्रुत्यन्तेषु उपदिश्यते (śrutyanteṣu upadiśyate)is taught in the Upaniṣads

Chapter 1 · Verse 11

1.11

अभाने न परं प्रेम भाने न विषये स्पृहा। अतो भानेऽप्यभाताऽसौ परमानन्दतात्मनः॥

If the ultimate fullness nature of the self was not evident, there would be no ultimate love. if it was evident, there would be no attachment towards the objects of experience. Therefore, even though evident, it is not evident.

Transliteration

abhāne na paraṃ prema bhāne na viṣaye spṛhā| ato bhāne’pyabhātā’sau paramānandatātmanaḥ||

Word-for-word
अभाने (abhāne)if not manifest
न परं प्रेम (na paraṃ prema)there is no supreme love
भाने (bhāne)if manifest
न विषये स्पृहा (na viṣaye spṛhā)there is no longing for objects
अतः (ataḥ)therefore
भाने अपि अभाता असौ (bhāne api abhātā asau)though shining, it appears unshining
परमानन्दता आत्मनः (paramānandatā ātmanaḥ)the Self’s nature as supreme bliss

Chapter 1 · Verse 12

1.12

अध्येतृ-वर्ग-मध्यस्थ-पुत्राध्ययन-शब्दवत्। भानेऽप्यभानं भानस्य प्रतिबन्धेन युज्यते॥

Like the chanting voice of one’s child amongst a group of students, even though is evident, in regard to being evident, it is proper to take it as not evident, because of obstruction.

Transliteration

adhyetṛ-varga-madhyastha-putrādhyayana-śabdavat| bhāne’pyabhānaṃ bhānasya pratibandhena yujyate||

Word-for-word
अध्येतृ-वर्ग-मध्यस्थ (adhyetṛ-varga-madhyastha)among a group of students
पुत्र-अध्ययन-शब्दवत् (putra-adhyayana-śabdavat)like the sound of one’s child reciting
भाने अपि अभानम् (bhāne api abhānam)non-recognition despite being present
भानस्य प्रतिबन्धेन (bhānasya pratibandhena)through an obstruction to recognition
युज्यते (yujyate)is explained

Chapter 1 · Verse 13

1.13

प्रतिबन्धोऽस्ति-भातीति-व्यवहारार्ह-वस्तुनि। तन्-निरस्य विरुद्धस्य तस्योत्पादनम् उच्यते॥

Toward any object is applicable the experience, “It is” and it is evident”, there is an obstruction said to be the rejecting of this and imposing its opposite.

Transliteration

pratibandho’sti-bhātīti-vyavahārārha-vastuni| tan-nirasya viruddhasya tasyotpādanam ucyate||

Word-for-word
प्रतिबन्धः (pratibandhaḥ)obstruction
अस्ति-भाति इति (asti-bhāti iti)‘exists and shines’
व्यवहार-अर्ह-वस्तुनि (vyavahāra-arha-vastuni)for an object fit for ordinary experience
तत् निरस्य (tat nirasya)rejecting that
विरुद्धस्य तस्य (viruddhasya tasya)its opposite
उत्पादनम् (utpādanam)the superimposition
उच्यते (ucyate)is called

Chapter 1 · Verse 14

1.14

तस्य हेतुः समानाभिहारः पुत्र-ध्वनि-श्रुतौ। इहानादिर् अविद्यैव व्यामोहैक-निबन्धनम्॥

The reason for that in regard to hearing the voice of one’s child is the mingling together as the same. Here, is beginningless ignorance. That alone is the obstruction leading to confusion.

Transliteration

tasya hetuḥ samānābhihāraḥ putra-dhvani-śrutau| ihānādir avidyaiva vyāmohaika-nibandhanam||

Word-for-word
तस्य हेतुः (tasya hetuḥ)its cause
समान-अभिहारः (samāna-abhihāraḥ)confusion through mingling with similar sounds
पुत्र-ध्वनि-श्रुतौ (putra-dhvani-śrutau)when hearing one’s child’s voice
इह (iha)here
अनादिः अविद्या एव (anādiḥ avidyā eva)beginningless ignorance alone
व्यामोह-एक-निबन्धनम् (vyāmoha-eka-nibandhanam)is the sole basis of delusion

Chapter 1 · Verse 15

1.15

चिद्-आनन्दमय-ब्रह्म-प्रतिबिम्ब-समन्विता। तमो-रजस्-सत्व-गुणा प्रकृतिर् द्विविधा च सा॥

Prakṛti consists of the three guṇas: tamas, rajas, and sattva. It is imbued with a reflection of brahman consisting of consciousness-fullness. This prakṛti has two forms.

Transliteration

cid-ānandamaya-brahma-pratibimba-samanvitā| tamo-rajas-satva-guṇā prakṛtir dvividhā ca sā||

Word-for-word
चित्-आनन्द-मय-ब्रह्म-प्रतिबिम्ब-समन्विता (cit-ānanda-maya-brahma-pratibimba-samanvitā)joined with a reflection of Brahman, whose nature is consciousness and bliss
तमस्-रजस्-सत्त्व-गुणा (tamas-rajas-sattva-guṇā)having the qualities of tamas, rajas, and sattva
प्रकृतिः द्विविधा च सा (prakṛtiḥ dvividhā ca sā)that prakṛti is twofold

Chapter 1 · Verse 16

1.16

सत्व-शुद्ध्यविशुद्धिभ्यां मायाऽविद्ये च ते मते। माया-बिम्बो वशी-कृत्य तां स्यात् सर्वज्ञ ईश्वरः॥

From the perspective of its pure sattva alone, it is called ‘māyā’. From the perspective of its impurity, it is called ‘a-vidyā’. ‘Īśvara’ is the presence in that māyā, controlling that, and is all-knowing.

Transliteration

satva-śuddhyaviśuddhibhyāṃ māyā’vidye ca te mate| māyā-bimbo vaśī-kṛtya tāṃ syāt sarvajña īśvaraḥ||

Word-for-word
सत्त्व-शुद्धि-अविशुद्धिभ्याम् (sattva-śuddhi-aviśuddhibhyām)by purity and impurity of sattva
माया-अविद्ये (māyā-avidye)called māyā and avidyā
च ते मते (ca te mate)these are the two
माया-बिम्बः (māyā-bimbaḥ)the reflection in māyā
वशी-कृत्य ताम् (vaśī-kṛtya tām)having brought it under control
स्यात् सर्वज्ञः ईश्वरः (syāt sarvajñaḥ īśvaraḥ)becomes the all-knowing Lord

Chapter 1 · Verse 17

1.17

अविद्या-वशगस् त्वन्यस् तद्-वैचित्र्याद् अनेकधा। सा कारण-शरीरं स्यात् प्राज्ञस् तत्राभिमानवान्॥

But the other is under the spell of a-vidyā, and, because of the variety in that is taken as many. This is the causal body, and is the one who is identified in that. That is, it is called the ‘prājña’.*

Transliteration

avidyā-vaśagas tvanyas tad-vaicitryād anekadhā| sā kāraṇa-śarīraṃ syāt prājñas tatrābhimānavān||

Word-for-word
अविद्या-वशगः तु अन्यः (avidyā-vaśagaḥ tu anyaḥ)the other is subject to ignorance
तद्-वैचित्र्यात् (tad-vaicitryāt)because of its variety
अनेकधा (anekadhā)in many forms
सा कारण-शरीरम् (sā kāraṇa-śarīram)that is the causal body
प्राज्ञः तत्र अभिमानवान् (prājñaḥ tatra abhimānavān)the one identified with it is prājña

Chapter 1 · Verse 18

1.18

तमः-प्रधान-प्रकृतेस् तद्-भोगायेश्वराज्ञया। वियत्-पवन-तेजो-ऽम्बु-भुवो भूतानि जज्ञिरे॥
