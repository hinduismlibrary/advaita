Verse 1

1

अविनयमपनय विष्णो दमय मनः शमय विषयमृगतृष्णाम् । भूतदयां विस्तारय तारय संसारसागरात् ॥

Remove my arrogance, Viṣṇu; discipline my mind; quiet the mirage-thirst for sense objects. Expand my compassion for living beings and carry me across the ocean of worldly existence.

Transliteration

avinayamapanaya viṣṇo damaya manaḥ śamaya viṣayamṛgatṛṣṇām | bhūtadayāṃ vistāraya tāraya saṃsārasāgarāt ||

Word-for-word
अविनयम् (avinayam)arrogance, lack of humility
अपनय (apanaya)remove
विष्णो (viṣṇo)O Viṣṇu
दमय (damaya)discipline, subdue
मनः (manaḥ)the mind
शमय (śamaya)quiet, calm
विषय (viṣaya)sense objects
मृगतृष्णाम् (mṛgatṛṣṇām)mirage-thirst
भूत-दयाम् (bhūta-dayām)compassion for living beings
विस्तारय (vistāraya)expand
तारय (tāraya)carry across, deliver
संसार-सागरात् (saṃsāra-sāgarāt)from the ocean of worldly existence

Verse 2

2

दिव्यधुनीमकरन्दे परिमलपरिभोगसच्चिदानन्दे । श्रीपतिपदारविन्दे भवभयखेदच्छिदे वन्दे ॥

I bow to the lotus feet of the Lord of Śrī: nectar from the celestial river, fragrant, and the enjoyment of Being, Consciousness, and Bliss; they cut away the fear and distress of worldly existence.

Transliteration

divyadhunīmakarande parimalaparibhogasaccidānande | śrīpatipadāravinde bhavabhayakhedacchide vande ||

Word-for-word
दिव्य (divya)divine, celestial
धुनी (dhunī)river
मकरन्दे (makarande)in the honey, nectar
परिमल (parimala)fragrance
परिभोग (paribhoga)enjoyment, experience
सत् (sat)Being
चित् (cit)Consciousness
आनन्दे (ānande)in Bliss
श्री-पति (śrī-pati)Lord of Śrī, Viṣṇu
पद-अरविन्दे (pada-aravinde)at the lotus feet
भव (bhava)worldly existence
भय (bhaya)fear
खेद (kheda)distress
छिदे (chide)to the cutter, remover
वन्दे (vande)I bow

Verse 3

3

सत्यपि भेदापगमे नाथ तवाहं न मामकीनस्त्वम् । सामुद्रो हि तरङ्गः क्वचन समुद्रो न तारङ्गः ॥

Even when difference has vanished, Lord, I am yours; you are not mine. A wave belongs to the sea, indeed; the sea is never the wave’s.

Transliteration

satyapi bhedāpagame nātha tavāhaṃ na māmakīnastvam | sāmudro hi taraṅgaḥ kvacana samudro na tāraṅgaḥ ||

Word-for-word
सति अपि (sati api)even when there is
भेद-अपगमे (bheda-apagame)the disappearance of difference
नाथ (nātha)O Lord
तव अहम् (tava aham)I am yours
न (na)not
मामकीनः (māmakīnaḥ)mine, belonging to me
त्वम् (tvam)you
सामुद्रः (sāmudraḥ)belonging to the sea
हि (hi)indeed
तरङ्गः (taraṅgaḥ)a wave
क्वचन (kvacana)anywhere, at all
समुद्रः (samudraḥ)the sea
न तारङ्गः (na tāraṅgaḥ)not belonging to a wave

Verse 4

4

उद्धृतनग नगभिदनुज दनुजकुलामित्र मित्रशशिदृष्टे । दृष्टे भवति प्रभवति न भवति किं भवतिरस्कारः ॥

O lifter of the mountain, younger brother of Indra, foe of the demon clans, friend of all, moon-eyed Lord! When you are seen, does not the rejection of worldly becoming come to pass? What can then continue to arise?

Transliteration

uddhṛtanaga nagabhidanuja danujakulāmitra mitraśaśidṛṣṭe | dṛṣṭe bhavati prabhavati na bhavati kiṃ bhavatiraskāraḥ ||

Word-for-word
उद्धृत-नग (uddhṛta-naga)lifter of the mountain [Govardhana]
नग-भिद् (naga-bhid)mountain-breaker [Indra]
अनुज (anuja)younger brother
दनुज-कुल (danuja-kula)the demon clans
अमित्र (amitra)enemy
मित्र (mitra)the sun [Mitra]
शशि (śaśi)the moon
दृष्टे (dṛṣṭe)O one whose eyes are
भवति (bhavati)it comes to be
प्रभवति (prabhavati)becomes possible, prevails
न भवति किम् (na bhavati kim)does it not come to pass?
भव-तिरस्कारः (bhava-tiraskāraḥ)rejection, ending of worldly becoming

Verse 5

5

मत्स्यादिभिरवतारैरवतारवतावता सदा वसुधाम् । परमेश्वर परिपाल्यो भवता भवतापभीतोऽहम् ॥

With incarnations beginning with the Fish, you have always protected the earth. Supreme Lord, I am frightened by the fever of worldly existence; I should be protected by you.

Transliteration

matsyādibhiravatārairavatāravatāvatā sadā vasudhām | parameśvara paripālyo bhavatā bhavatāpabhīto’ham ||

Word-for-word
मत्स्य-आदिभिः (matsya-ādibhiḥ)by those beginning with the Fish
अवतारैः (avatāraiḥ)by incarnations
अवतार-वता (avatāra-vatā)by the one endowed with incarnations
अवता (avatā)protected
सदा (sadā)always
वसुधाम् (vasudhām)the earth
परम-ईश्वर (parama-īśvara)O Supreme Lord
परिपाल्यः (paripālyaḥ)to be protected
भवता (bhavatā)by you
भव-ताप (bhava-tāpa)the heat, anguish of worldly existence
भीतः (bhītaḥ)frightened
अहम् (aham)I

Verse 6

6

दामोदर गुणमन्दिर सुन्दरवदनारविन्द गोविन्द । भवजलधिमथनमन्दर परमं दरमपनय त्वं मे ॥

O Dāmodara, abode of virtues, lotus-faced and beautiful, Govinda, Mandara Mountain for churning the ocean of worldly existence—remove my great fear, I pray.

Transliteration

dāmodara guṇamandira sundaravadanāravinda govinda | bhavajaladhimathanamandara paramaṃ daramapanaya tvaṃ me ||

Word-for-word
दामोदर (dāmodara)O Dāmodara
गुण-मन्दिर (guṇa-mandira)abode of virtues
सुन्दर (sundara)beautiful
वदन-अरविन्द (vadana-aravinda)lotus face
गोविन्द (govinda)O Govinda
भव-जलधि (bhava-jaladhi)ocean of worldly existence
मथन (mathana)churning
मन्दर (mandara)Mandara Mountain
परमम् (paramam)great, supreme
दरम् (daram)fear
अपनय (apanaya)remove
त्वम् (tvam)you
मे (me)from me, for me

Verse 7

7

नारायण करुणामय शरणं करवाणि तावकौ चरणौ । इति षट्पदी मदीये वदनसरोजे सदा वसतु ॥

O Nārāyaṇa, full of compassion, I make your two feet my refuge. May this Ṣaṭpadī always dwell in the lotus of my mouth.

Transliteration

nārāyaṇa karuṇāmaya śaraṇaṃ karavāṇi tāvakau caraṇau | iti ṣaṭpadī madīye vadanasaroje sadā vasatu ||

Word-for-word
नारायण (nārāyaṇa)O Nārāyaṇa
करुणा-मय (karuṇā-maya)full of compassion
शरणम् (śaraṇam)refuge
करवाणि (karavāṇi)may I make
तावकौ (tāvakau)your two
चरणौ (caraṇau)feet
इति (iti)thus
षट्पदी (ṣaṭpadī)the six-footed one; this Ṣaṭpadī hymn
मदीये (madīye)in my
वदन-सरोजे (vadana-saroje)lotus-like mouth
सदा (sadā)always
वसतु (vasatu)may it dwell

Closing colophon

Colophon

इति षट्पदीस्तोत्रं संपूर्णम् ॥

Thus is the Ṣaṭpadī Stotram complete.

Transliteration

iti ṣaṭpadīstotraṃ saṃpūrṇam ||

Word-for-word
इति (iti)thus
षट्पदी-स्तोत्रम् (ṣaṭpadī-stotram)the Ṣaṭpadī hymn
संपूर्णम् (saṃpūrṇam)complete
