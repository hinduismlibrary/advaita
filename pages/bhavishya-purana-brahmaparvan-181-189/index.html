Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan 181–189

Context block surrounding Chapter 184. Chapters 181–189 belong to one continuing Smṛti-dharma discourse. Reading them together is important because Chapter 184 contains both a rule favouring a qualified recipient and a later local-priority rule telling the householder not to bypass a nearby Brahmin merely for a more qualified distant one.
